一宵无话，第二天一早胡雪岩起身，阿珠服侍他漱口洗脸，由于急着要上岸办事，连点心都顾不得吃，就起身去了。临走留下话，中午约在盐桥一家叫“纯号”的酒店见面，又说，如果阿珠和她娘有兴致，也一道来逛逛。母女俩的兴致自然极好。盐桥大街多的是布店和估衣店，阿珠跟她娘商量：“爹要做老板了，总不能再穿‘短打’，先到估衣店去买件长衫，再自己剪布来做。”“好啊！”她娘欣然同意，“我们早点去！”她们母女俩高高兴兴在收拾头面，预备出门。老张一个人坐在船头上闷闷不乐，心里在想，中午一见了面，胡雪岩当然会把银子交过来，只要一接上手，以后再有什么话说，就显得不够味道了。要说，说在前面，或者今天先不接银子，等商量停当了再说。他要跟他妻子商量，无奈有阿珠在，不便开口，心里踌躇无计，而一妻一女倒已经头光面滑，穿上“出客”的衣服，预备动身了。

The night passed without incident. Early next morning Hu rose, and A’zhu helped him wash and rinse his mouth. In his haste to go ashore on business, he could not even stay for breakfast. Before leaving, he arranged to meet them at midday at a tavern called Chunhao, near Yan Bridge, and said that A’zhu and her mother might come along for an outing if they liked. Naturally, both were delighted. Yan Bridge Street had plenty of cloth shops and shops selling second-hand clothes. A’zhu consulted her mother. “Father's going to be a proprietor. He can't go on wearing a boatman's short jacket. Let's buy him a long gown at a second-hand shop first, then get some cloth and make him more clothes ourselves.” “Good!” her mother agreed happily. “Let's go early!” While they cheerfully dressed their hair and prepared to go out, Old Zhang sat alone in the bow, sunk in gloom. When they met at noon, Hu would surely hand him the money. Once he accepted it, anything he might say afterwards would come too late and look bad. He must speak first—or decline to take the money today and wait until everything had been settled. He wanted to discuss it with his wife, but with A’zhu there he could not open his mouth. While he hesitated helplessly, mother and daughter finished making themselves immaculate, put on their visiting clothes, and stood ready to leave.

“该走了吧！”阿珠的娘催促老张。“爹！”阿珠又嫌她爹土气，“你把蓝布小衫换一换，好不好，寿头寿脑的，真把人的台都坍光的！”由于宠女儿的缘故，老张一向把她这些没规没矩的话，当作耳边风。但话虽不理，该有行动，而他望着她们母女，怔怔地好象灵魂出窍了似的，好半天不开口。“呀！”他妻子不胜讶异地：“怎的？”老张摇摇头，接着说了句：“你们娘儿俩去好了。我不去了。”“咦！为啥？”老张想了想说：“我要帮阿四把船摇回万安桥去。”这是不成理由的理由，阿珠和她娘的脸上，顿时象眼前的天气一样，阴睛不定了。“你在想什么古里古怪的心思？”阿珠娘脸板得一丝笑容都没有，眼圈都有些红了，“生来是吃苦的命！好日子还没有过一天，就要‘作’了！”“作”是杭州话，通常只用来骂横也不是，竖也不是，不讨人喜欢的孩子，用来责备老张，便有“自作孽、不可活”的意思，话重而怨深，他不能不做个比较明白的表示了。

“Time to go!” his wife urged. A’zhu, too, found fault with her father's rustic appearance. “Father, will you change that little blue cotton jacket? You look such a bumpkin! You'll disgrace us completely.” Old Zhang spoiled his daughter and generally let her impudent remarks pass as if he had not heard them. Still, even if he ignored her words, he ought to have done something. Instead he stared at the two of them as though his soul had left his body, remaining silent for a long time. “Why!” his wife exclaimed in astonishment. “What's the matter?” He shook his head, then said, “You two go. I'm staying here.” “Why ever not?” After a moment's thought, he answered, “I have to help A’si take the boat back to Wan'an Bridge.” It was no excuse at all. The expressions on mother and daughter changed as quickly as the uncertain weather outside. “What strange notions have you got into your head now?” His wife's face lost every trace of a smile, and her eyes reddened. “You were born to toil! You haven't enjoyed a single comfortable day yet, and already you're making trouble for yourself!” The Hangzhou expression she used normally described a tiresome child who was dissatisfied whichever way things went. Applied to Old Zhang, it carried the sharper implication that he was bringing his own ruin upon himself. Her words were harsh and her resentment deep. He had to make his position somewhat clearer.

“你不要一门心里只想自己！”他说，“人家白花花一千两银子，不是小数目，把它蚀光了怎么办？”“你啊，‘树叶儿掉下来怕打开头’，生意还没有做，开口闭口蚀本！照我这样子说，一辈子摇船好了，摇到七老八十，一口气不来，棺材都用不着买，往河里一推，喂鱼拉倒！“爹娘吵架，遇到紧要关头，阿珠总是站在她爹这面，这时便埋怨着说：“娘！何苦说这些话？爹不肯去，让他不去好了。”“对！”阿珠的娘真的生气了，“枉为他是一家之主。我们敬他，他不受敬，随他去，我们走！”听得这负气的话，阿珠又觉得不安，想了想只好这样说：“怎么走？路好远到那里。”路不但好远，而且郊野小径，泥泞不堪，就能走进城，一又脚上的鞋袜亦已不成样子，不过，这也难不倒她娘，高声喊道：“阿四，阿四！”“阿四到万安桥去了。”老张说。亏得他接了这句口，局面才不致僵持，他妻子气消了些，声音却依旧很大，“我们今天把话说说清楚，你到底是怎么个意思？”

“Don't think of nobody but yourself,” he said. “A thousand taels of good silver is no trifling sum. What happens if we lose it all?” “You! You'd fear a falling leaf might split your head! You haven't begun trading, and all you can talk about is losses. If that's the way you look at it, you might as well spend your whole life rowing. Row till you're seventy or eighty, and when you draw your last breath we needn't even buy a coffin. Just push you into the river and let the fish have you!” Whenever her parents quarrelled, A’zhu sided with her father at the critical moment. Now she protested, “Mother! Why say such things? If Father doesn't want to go, let him stay.” “Very well!” Her mother was truly angry. “Some head of the family he is! We give him his due and he won't accept it. Let him please himself. We're going!” This angry declaration troubled A’zhu in turn. After thinking for a moment, she could only ask, “How are we to get there? It's such a long walk.” Besides the distance, the country paths were deep in mud. Even if they managed to walk into town, their shoes and stockings would be a sorry sight. This did not defeat her mother, however. “A’si! A’si!” she shouted. “A’si's gone to Wan'an Bridge,” Old Zhang answered. Fortunately, this reply prevented a complete deadlock. His wife's anger subsided a little, though her voice remained loud. “We'll have this out today. Just what do you mean?”

“等下再说。”老张这样回答，一面看了阿珠一眼。这一下她们母女俩都懂了他的意思，阿珠有些羞，有些恼，更有些焦忧，看爹这神气，事情怕要变卦。“阿珠！你到后面去看看，炖在炉子上的蹄筋，怕要加水了。”借这个因由把她支使了开去，夫妻俩凑在一起谈私话。老张第一句话就问：“人家姓胡的，对阿珠到底是怎么个主意？你倒说说看！”“何用我说？你还看不出来？”“我怎么看不出？不过昨天看得出，今天看不出了。”“这叫什么话？”“我问你，”老张想了想说，“他到底是要做丝生意，是要我们阿珠，还是两样都要？”“自然两样都要。”“他要两样，我只好做一样，他要我们阿珠，开丝行请他去请教别人，要我替他做伙计来出面，娶阿珠的事就免谈。”“这为啥？”他妻子睁大了眼问，“你倒说个道理我听听看。”他的道理就是不愿意让人笑他，靠裙带上拖出一个老板来做，“一句话，”他很认真地说，“我贫虽贫，还不肯担个卖女儿的名声！”人人要脸，树树要皮！他妻子在想，也不能说他的话没有道理。但事难两全，只好劝他委屈些。

“We'll talk about it later,” Old Zhang replied, with a glance at A’zhu. Both women now understood. A’zhu felt embarrassed, annoyed, and, above all, anxious. Judging by her father's expression, their plans might fall through. “A’zhu! Go and look at the tendons stewing on the stove. They may need more water.” Having sent her daughter away on this pretext, his wife drew close to him for a private conversation. His very first question was, “Tell me this: what exactly does that fellow Hu intend to do about A’zhu?” “Why ask me? Can't you see for yourself?” “Of course I can. Or I could yesterday. Today I'm not so sure.” “What do you mean by that?” He considered, then asked, “Does he want to trade in silk, does he want our A’zhu, or does he want both?” “Both, naturally.” “He may want both, but I'll agree to only one. If he wants our A’zhu, let him find someone else to run his silk firm. If he wants me to work for him and act as its proprietor, there can be no question of his marrying her.” “Why?” she demanded, her eyes widening. “Give me one good reason.” His reason was that he would not let people laugh at him for becoming a proprietor through his daughter's skirts. “In a word,” he said earnestly, “poor I may be, but I won't have a name for selling my daughter!” Every person had a reputation to protect, just as every tree had its bark. His wife could not say he had no grounds for his objection. But since they could not have everything, she could only urge him to swallow his pride a little.

“你脾气也不要这么倔，各人自扫门前雪，没有哪家来管我们的闲事。”“没有？”老张使劲摇着头，“你女人家，难得到茶坊酒肆，听不到。我外头要跑跑的，叫人家背后指指点点，我还好过日子？好了，好了，“他越想越不妥，大声说道：”我主意打定了。你如果一定不肯依我，我也有我的办法。““什么办法？”她不安地问。“丝行你去开，算老板也好，算老板娘也好，我不管。我还是去做我的老本行，做一天吃一天，有生意到了湖州，我来看你们娘儿两个。”

“Don't be so stubborn. Everyone has enough to do sweeping the snow from his own doorstep. Nobody will meddle in our affairs.” “Nobody?” Old Zhang shook his head vigorously. “You're a woman. You seldom visit teahouses or taverns, so you don't hear what people say. I have to go about outside. How am I to live with people pointing at me behind my back? Enough, enough!” The more he thought about it, the more intolerable it seemed. He raised his voice. “I've made up my mind. If you absolutely refuse to listen, I have a way of dealing with it.” “What way?” she asked uneasily. “You run the silk firm. Call yourself the proprietor or the proprietor's wife, as you please. I shan't interfere. I'll go back to my old trade, working each day for my daily bread. Whenever business takes me to Huzhou, I'll come and see you and our daughter.”

听他这番异想天开的话，居然说得象煞有介事，她失笑了，便故意这样问：“那么，你算是来做客人？”“是啊！做客人。”“照这样说，你是没良心把我休掉了？”虽是半带玩笑，这“没良心”三个字，在老张听来就是劈脸一个耳光，顿时觉得脸上火辣辣地，极力分辩着：“怎么说我没良心？你不好冤枉我！”“我没有冤枉你！如果你有良心，就算为我受委屈，好不好呢？”他不作声了，她看得出，自己真的要这么做，也可以做得到，但是他嘴上不说，心里不愿，到底是夫归的情分，何苦如此？想想还是要把他说得心甘情愿，这件事才算“落胃”。

He spoke of this fantastic scheme so seriously that she could not help laughing. “So you'll be coming as a visitor?” she asked deliberately. “Yes. As a visitor.” “In that case, you've no conscience: you're casting me off?” She was half joking, but the accusation struck him like a slap across the face. His cheeks burned as he protested, “How can you say I've no conscience? You mustn't accuse me unjustly!” “I'm not accusing you unjustly. If you have a conscience, can't you put up with a little humiliation for my sake?” He fell silent. She could see that, if she insisted, she could have her way. But he would submit without agreeing in his heart, and they were husband and wife, after all. Why make him suffer? Better to persuade him to accept willingly. Only then would the matter sit comfortably with them both.

于是她想着想着，跟她女儿想到一条路上去了，“这样行不行呢？”她说，“你无非怕人家背后说闲话，如果人家在湖州照样请过客，见过礼，算是他在湖州的一房家小，这总没有话说了吧？”见他妻子让步，他自然也要让步，点点头：“照这样子还差不多。”“那好了，我来想法子。萝卜吃一截剥一截，眼前的要紧事先做。你换换衣裳，我们也好走了。“老张换好一套出客穿的短衣，黑鞋白袜扎脚裤，上身一件直贡呢的夹袄。正好阿四划了一只小船，买菜回来，留他看船，老张自己把他妻儿划到盐桥上岸，从河下走上熙熙攘攘的盐桥大街。

As she thought it over, she reached the same conclusion as her daughter. “Would this do?” she asked. “All you're afraid of is gossip behind your back. Suppose he gives a proper wedding feast in Huzhou, observes the ceremonies, and establishes her as his wife there. Surely there could be no objection then?” Since his wife had yielded a little, he must do the same. He nodded. “That would be more like it.” “Good. I'll find a way. Peel a radish a piece at a time as you eat it; let's deal with what's urgent first. Change your clothes so that we can go.” Old Zhang changed into a short outfit he kept for visits: black shoes, white stockings, trousers tied at the ankles, and a lined jacket of fine woollen cloth. Just then A’si returned in a small boat with the provisions he had bought. Leaving him to watch the larger vessel, Old Zhang rowed his wife and daughter to Yan Bridge himself. They landed and climbed from the waterside to the crowded street.

水上生涯的人家，难得到这条肩摩毂击的大街上来，阿珠颇有目迷五色之感，顾上不顾下，高一脚，低一脚地不小心踩着了一块活动的青石板，泥浆迸溅，弄脏了新上身的一条雪青百褶裙，于是失声而喊，顿时引得路人侧目而视。“唷，唷，走路要当心！”有个二十来岁的油头光棍，仿佛好意来扶她，趁势在她膀子捏了一把。阿珠涨红了脸，使劲把膀子一甩，用力过猛，一甩上去，正好打了他一个反手耳光，其声清脆无比。“唷，好凶！”有人吃惊，也有人发笑。这一下使得被误打了的人，面子上越发下不来，一手捂着脸，跳脚大骂。阿珠和她娘吓得面色发白。老张一看闯了祸，赶紧上前陪笑道歉：“对不起，对不起，无心的！”

Families who lived on the water seldom visited this busy thoroughfare, where shoulders brushed and carriage wheels almost touched. A’zhu was dazzled by everything around her. Looking up without watching her feet, she stumbled on an uneven stretch and stepped on a loose stone slab. Mud splashed up and stained the new mauve pleated skirt she was wearing. Her involuntary cry made the passers-by turn to look. “Careful, careful! Watch where you're going!” A sleek-haired loafer in his twenties pretended to help her, taking the opportunity to pinch her arm. Her face flamed. She flung her arm away so violently that the back of her hand caught his face with a resounding slap. “Oh! What a fierce one!” Some bystanders exclaimed in surprise; others laughed. This made it still harder for the man to save face. Clutching his cheek, he stamped and cursed. A’zhu and her mother turned pale. Seeing trouble, Old Zhang hurried forward, smiling apologetically. “I'm sorry, I'm sorry! It was an accident!”

杭州人以掴脸为奇耻大辱，特别是让妇女打了，认为是“晦气”，而那个油头光混又是杭州人所谓“撩鬼儿”的小流氓，事态使越发严重了，立刻便有五六个同党围了上来。其中一个一面口沫横飞地辱骂，一面劈胸一把将老张的衣服抓住，伸出拳来就要打。“打不得，打不得！有话好讲。”阿珠的娘大喊。“讲你娘的……”一拳伸了过来，老张接住，下面一腿又到，老张又避开，他打过几个月的拳，也练过“仙人担”，抛过“石锁”，两条膀子上有一两百斤力气，这五六个人还应付得了，不过一则是自己的理屈，再则为人忠厚，不愿打架，所以只是躲避告饶。拉拉扯扯，身上已经着了两下，还是趁火打劫的，挨挨蹭蹭来轻薄阿珠，就在这她眼泪都快要掉来来的当儿，来了个救星。

In Hangzhou a slap on the face was considered a terrible disgrace, and to be struck by a woman was thought particularly unlucky. The sleek-haired fellow was also one of the petty street ruffians known locally as liaoguier, which made matters worse. Five or six companions immediately surrounded them. One seized Old Zhang's clothing at the chest, showering him with abuse, and raised his fist. “Don't hit him! Don't hit him! We can talk this over!” A’zhu's mother cried. “Talk to your bloody mother—” A fist shot out. Old Zhang caught it. A kick followed, and he dodged. He had trained in boxing for several months, practised with a weighted carrying pole, and tossed stone exercise weights. His arms had the strength to lift a hundred or two hundred jin, and he could have dealt with these five or six men. But he felt that his side was in the wrong, and his honest, peaceable nature made him unwilling to fight. He merely ducked and pleaded. In the scuffle he took two blows, while others used the confusion to crowd against A’zhu and molest her. She was on the verge of tears when a rescuer appeared.

“三和尚！啥事体？”叫得出名字就好办了，那人手上的劲，立刻就松。阿珠的娘如逢大赦，赶紧抢上来说：“张老板，张老板，请你来说一句！本来没事……”“没事？”被打的那人也要抢着来做原告，指着阿珠说：“张老板，请你老人家评评理看，我看她要惯倒，好意扶她一把，哪晓得她撩起一个嘴巴！端午脚边，晦气不晦气？“张胖子肚里雪亮，自然是调戏人家，有取打之道，而心里却有些好笑，故意问道：“阿珠，你怎么出手就打人？”一听他叫得出阿珠的名字，原是熟人，抓住老张的那个人，不自觉地就把手松开了。又羞又窘，脸色象块红布样的阿珠，这才算放了心，得理不让人，挺起了胸说，“我也不是存心打他，是他自己不好。”“好了，好了！”她娘赶紧拦她，“你也少说一句。”“看我面子！是我侄女儿。”张老板对被打的那人说，“等下我请你们吃老酒。”一场看来不可开交的纠纷，就此片言而决。老张夫妇向张胖子谢了又谢，阿珠心里却是连自己都辨不出的滋味，仿佛觉得扫兴，又仿佛觉得安慰，站在旁边不开口。“这里不是说话之处。”张胖子说，“你们不是约了在‘纯号’碰头？喏，那里就是。“

“Third Monk! What's going on?” The fact that the newcomer knew a name made all the difference: the man immediately relaxed his grip. A’zhu's mother felt as though she had been reprieved. She hurried forward. “Mr Zhang! Mr Zhang! Please say a word for us! There was nothing to begin with—” “Nothing?” The man who had been slapped also rushed to present his complaint, pointing at A’zhu. “Mr Zhang, you judge for yourself! I thought she was about to fall and kindly tried to help her. Who'd have expected her to slap me across the mouth? With the Dragon Boat Festival so close, isn't that a fine piece of bad luck?” Fat Zhang understood perfectly: the fellow had been pestering the girl and had brought it on himself. He was rather amused, however, and deliberately asked, “A’zhu, why did you hit him?” Hearing him address her by name, the man holding Old Zhang realised that they knew one another and instinctively let go. A’zhu, blushing scarlet with embarrassment, felt safe at last. Sure of her ground, she straightened up. “I didn't mean to hit him. It was his own fault.” “All right, all right!” her mother hastily intervened. “You needn't say any more.” “Let it pass for my sake. She's my niece,” Zhang told the injured party. “I'll buy you all a drink later.” A quarrel that had seemed impossible to resolve was settled in a few words. Old Zhang and his wife thanked him again and again. A’zhu stood silent beside them, unable to define her own feelings: she seemed both disappointed and reassured. “This is no place to talk,” Fat Zhang said. “Aren't you meeting at Chunhao? Look, there it is.”

纯号这家酒店，出名的是绍烧。双开间门面，一半为一座曲尺形的柜台所隔断，柜台很高，上面放着许多直径一尺多的大瓷盘，盛着客种下酒菜，从最起码的发芽豆到时鲜海货，有十来样之多。这时已有好些人在吃“柜台酒”，菜市上的小贩，盐桥河下的脚伕，早市已毕，到这里来寻些乐趣，一碗绍烧、一碟小菜，倚柜而立，吃完走路，其中不少是老张的熟人，看到他穿得整整齐齐，带着妻子女儿在一起，不免有一番问询。等他应付完了，张胖子和两个“堂客”，已经在里面落座了。里面是雅座，八仙桌子只坐了两面，阿珠和她母亲合坐一张条凳。老张来了，又占一面，留着上首的座位给胡雪岩。“真碰得巧！”张胖子说，“我也是雪岩约我在这里，他一早到我店里来过了，现在回局里有事，等一下就来，我们一面吃，一面等。”于是呼酒叫菜，喝着谈着。“堂客”上酒店是不大有的事，阿珠又长得惹眼，所以里里外外都不免要探头张望一番，她又局促又有些得意，但心里只盼望着胡雪岩。

Chunhao was famous for its Shaoxing spirits. Its shopfront was two bays wide, half the space taken up by a high, L-shaped counter. On this stood large porcelain dishes more than a foot across, holding a dozen or so foods to accompany a drink, from humble sprouted beans to fresh seafood in season. A number of customers were already drinking at the counter. The market stallholders and the porters from the Yan Bridge waterfront had finished their morning's work and come to enjoy themselves: a bowl of spirits, a small dish, a brief rest leaning against the counter, and away again. Several knew Old Zhang. Seeing him neatly dressed with his wife and daughter, they naturally had questions to ask. By the time he had dealt with them, Fat Zhang and the two ladies were seated inside. This inner room had more comfortable seating. Only two sides of their square table were occupied, A’zhu and her mother sharing a bench. Old Zhang took a third side, leaving the place of honour for Hu. “What a lucky encounter!” Fat Zhang said. “Xueyan asked me to meet him here too. He came to the bank first thing this morning. He's gone back to the bureau on business and will be here shortly. Let's eat while we wait.” They ordered food and wine and talked over their drinks. Respectable women seldom entered taverns, and A’zhu was striking enough to attract still more attention. People kept craning their necks from both inside and outside. She felt awkward and a little pleased, but all she really wanted was for Hu to arrive.

胡雪岩终于来了。等他一入座，张胖子便谈阿珠误打了“撩鬼儿”的趣事，因为排解了这场纠纷，他显得很得意地。“阿珠！”胡雪岩听完了笑道：“我们还不知道你这么厉害。”听他的口气，当她是“雌老虎”，阿珠便红着脸分辩：“他是有心的，大街上动手动脚象啥样子？我一急一甩，打到他脸上，什么厉害不厉害？厉害也不会让人欺侮了！”胡雪岩笑笑不响。张胖子听她对胡雪岩说话的态度，心里明白，两个人已到了不需客气、无话不谈的地步，不妨开个玩笑。

At last he did. As soon as he sat down, Fat Zhang related the entertaining story of her accidental blow to the street ruffian, plainly proud of having settled the dispute. “A’zhu!” Hu laughed when he had heard it. “We never knew you were such a terror.” Feeling that he was calling her a tigress, she flushed and protested, “He did it on purpose. What business had he putting his hands on me in the street? I panicked and flung my arm away, and it caught his face. What has that to do with being a terror? If I were such a terror, nobody would bully me!” Hu merely smiled. From the way she spoke to him, Fat Zhang saw that they had reached a stage where ceremony was unnecessary and nothing need go unsaid. A joke would do no harm.

“老张，”他把视线落在阿珠和她娘脸上，“什么时候请我吃喜酒？”老张无从置答，阿珠羞得低下了头，她娘却正要拜托张胖子，随即笑滋滋地答道：“这要看张老板！”“咦！关我什么事？”阿珠的娘话到口边，又改了一句：“张老板府上在哪里？我做两样菜请张老板、张太太尝尝。”在座的人只有胡雪岩懂她的意思，是要托张胖子出来做媒，心想透过熟人来谈这件事也好，便提醒张胖子：“只怕有事情托你！”“喔！喔！”张胖子会意了“我住在‘石塔儿头’到底，碰鼻头转弯，‘塞然弄堂’，坐北朝南倒数第二家。”这个地址一口气说下来，仿佛说绕口令似地，阿珠忍不住“扑哧”一声笑了出来。

“Old Zhang,” he began, looking at A’zhu and her mother, “when are you inviting me to the wedding feast?” Old Zhang had no answer. A’zhu lowered her head in embarrassment, but her mother had been hoping to ask Fat Zhang for help and answered with a broad smile, “That depends on you, Mr Zhang!” “Why! What have I to do with it?” She checked the words on her lips and said instead, “Where do you live, Mr Zhang? I'd like to prepare a couple of dishes for you and Mrs Zhang.” Hu alone understood that she meant to ask Fat Zhang to act as matchmaker. It would be a good thing to discuss the matter through someone they knew, he thought, and prompted him: “Perhaps she has something to ask of you.” “Oh! Oh!” Understanding at last, Fat Zhang replied, “I live right at the end of Stone Pagoda Street. When you can't go any farther, turn into Sairan Lane. It's the second house from the end, facing south.” Rattled off in a single breath, the address sounded so like a tongue-twister that A’zhu burst out laughing.

张胖子又逗着阿珠说了些笑话，适可而止，然后把话锋一转。看着胡雪岩说：“我们谈正经吧！”一听他用“我们”二字，便知湖州的丝生意，张胖子也有份。胡雪岩已经跟他谈妥当了，目前先由信和在湖州的联号恒利钱店放款买丝，除了照市拆息以外，答应将来在盈余中提两成作为张胖子个人的好处。他愿意出这样优厚的条件，一则是为了融通资金方便，其次是他自己怕照顾不到，希望张胖子能替他分劳，再有一层就是交情了，信和钱庄虽然做着了海运局的生意，但张胖子自己没有什么利益，胡雪岩借这个机会“ 挑”他赚几文。

He teased her a little more, stopped before the jokes went too far, and then changed the subject. Looking at Hu, he said, “Let's get down to our business.” That word “our” showed that he, too, had a share in the Huzhou silk venture. Hu had already settled matters with him. For the present, Hengli Bank, Xinhe's affiliated establishment in Huzhou, would lend the money to buy silk. Besides paying interest at the prevailing market rate, Hu had promised Fat Zhang personally twenty per cent of the eventual profits. He offered such generous terms partly to make financing easy, partly because he feared he could not supervise everything himself and hoped Zhang would share the burden, and partly out of friendship. Xinhe Bank had gained the Sea Transport Bureau's business, but Fat Zhang had made nothing personally from it. Hu was using this opportunity to give him a chance to earn something.

“老张！我今天有两件事交代你，第一，一千两银子在这里，你收好。”说着，胡雪岩取出一个毛巾包来，打开来看，里面是五百两一张的两张银票，“张老板那里出的票子，在湖州恒利照兑。”“恒利在城隍庙前。”张胖子说，“老张，你在那里立个折子好了，随用随提，方便得很。”“是的。”老张很吃力地回答。“第二件，张老板荐了个朋友替你做帮手……”“噢！”老张很高兴地抢着说，“那就好！我就怕一个人‘没脚蟹’似地，摆布不开。”

“Old Zhang! There are two things I want to settle with you today. First, here are the thousand taels. Keep them safe.” Hu produced a bundle wrapped in a towel and opened it. Inside were two banknotes for five hundred taels each. “Mr Zhang's bank issued these. Hengli in Huzhou will honour them.” “Hengli is in front of the City God Temple,” Fat Zhang added. “Open a passbook account there, Old Zhang. You can withdraw whatever you need as you need it. Very convenient.” “Yes,” Old Zhang answered with difficulty. “Second, Mr Zhang has recommended someone to help you—” “Oh!” Old Zhang interrupted gladly. “That's excellent! I was afraid I'd be like a crab without legs, unable to manage on my own.”

“不过，老张，有一层你一定要弄清楚。”胡雪岩看一看张胖子，很郑得地说：“丝行是你开，主意要你自己拿，荐来的人给你做伙计，凡事他听你，不是你听他。这话我今天要当着张老板交代清楚。”“不错，不错。”张胖子接口说道：“那个小伙子姓李，是我的晚辈亲戚，人是蛮能干的，丝行生意也懂，不过年轻贪玩，要托你多管管他。”老张把他们两个人的话体味了一遍，点点头说：“生意归生意，朋友归朋友，我晓得了。”“对啊！”胡雪岩很欣慰地说，“老张，你说得出这一句话，生意一定会做得好。尽管放手去做！还有一句话，你一到湖州，马上就要寻个内行，眼光要好，人要靠得住，薪水不妨多送，一分价钱一分货，用人也是一样的。”老张受了鼓舞，大有领会，不断点头，“那么，这位姓李的朋友，我们什么时候见见面？”他问。“吃完了到我店里去。”张胖子答道，“我派人把他去叫了来见你。”

“But there's one thing you must understand clearly.” Hu glanced at Fat Zhang and spoke with great seriousness. “It's your silk firm, and you must make the decisions. The person recommended to you will be your employee. He takes his orders from you, not the other way round. I want to make that clear today in Mr Zhang's presence.” “Quite right, quite right,” Fat Zhang agreed. “The young fellow's surname is Li. He's a younger relative of mine, quite capable, and he understands the silk trade. But he's young and fond of amusement. I'll rely on you to keep him in order.” Old Zhang considered what both men had said, then nodded. “Business is business, friendship is friendship. I understand.” “Exactly!” Hu said, greatly pleased. “If you can say that, Old Zhang, you're bound to do well. Go ahead with confidence! And one more thing: as soon as you reach Huzhou, find an experienced man with a good eye whom you can trust. Don't begrudge him a generous salary. You get what you pay for, and that's as true of hiring people as of anything else.” Encouraged, Old Zhang nodded repeatedly, understanding much that had been unclear. “When shall we meet this Mr Li?” he asked. “Come to the bank after we've eaten,” Fat Zhang answered. “I'll send for him to meet you.”

因为有许多正经事要办，这一顿酒草草终场，出了纯号，分成两拨，张胖子带着老张到信和，阿珠和她娘到估衣铺去替老张办“行头”。剩下胡雪岩一个，阿珠总以为他一定也到信和，谁知他愿意跟她们做一路。这是求之不得的事，阿珠心里十分高兴，不过在大街上不肯跟他走在一起，搀扶着她娘故意远远地落在后面。胡雪岩却是有心要计阿珠的好，走到一家大布庄门口，站住了脚等她们。“这里我很熟，包定不会吃亏。要剪些什么料子，尽量挑，难得上街一趟，用不着委屈自己。”越是他这么说，她们母女俩越不青让他破费，略略点缀了一下，便算了事。胡雪岩要替她们多剪，口口声声：“干娘这块料子好”、“这块颜色阿珠可以穿”，但那母女俩无论如何不要，为了不肯直说“舍不得你多花钱”这句话，阿珠便故意挑剔那些衣料，不是颜色不好，就是花样过时，不然就是“门面”太狭，下水会缩之类的“欲加之罪”，味着良心胡说，把布店里的伙计，气得半天不开口。

With so much serious business to attend to, they brought the meal to a hurried end. Outside Chunhao the party divided. Fat Zhang took Old Zhang to Xinhe, while A’zhu and her mother went to a second-hand clothes shop to equip him for his new role. That left Hu. A’zhu had assumed that he would go to the bank too, but to her surprise he preferred to accompany the women. Nothing could have pleased her more. Yet she would not walk beside him in the street. Taking her mother's arm, she deliberately lagged well behind. Hu, intent on pleasing her, stopped outside a large cloth shop to wait for them. “I know this place well. I guarantee you won't be cheated. Choose whatever cloth you want. You don't often come shopping, so don't stint yourselves.” The more he urged them, the less willing they were to put him to expense. They selected only a few modest additions and would have stopped there. Hu wanted them to buy more. “Auntie, this is a lovely piece,” he kept saying. “That colour would suit A’zhu.” But neither would agree. Rather than say plainly that she could not bear to let him spend so much, A’zhu found fault with every fabric: the colour was wrong, the pattern old-fashioned, the width too narrow, or it would shrink in water. Her baseless accusations, made against her own better judgement, left the shop assistant speechless with anger.

布店隔壁就是估衣店，到替老张买衣服，胡雪岩当仁不让了，“这要我来作主！”他说，“现在做生意不象从前了，打抢得越老实越好，上海的‘十里夷场’你们见过的，哪一行走出来不是穿得挺挺括括？佛要金装，人要衣装，你看我把老张打份起来，包他象个大老板。”听他说得头头是道，阿珠抿着嘴笑了，推一推她娘小声说道：“你也要打扮打扮，不然不象个老板娘！”真的要做老板娘了！阿珠的娘心里在想，昨天还只是一句话，到底不知如何？这现在可是踏踏实实再无可疑，别样不说，那一千两银子总是真的。这样一想，就想得远了，只是想着怎样做老板娘和做老板娘的滋味，忘掉了自己身在何处？

The second-hand clothes shop was next door. When it came to buying Old Zhang's clothes, Hu firmly took charge. “You must leave this to me,” he said. “Business isn't what it used to be. A man's appearance has to look thoroughly respectable. You've seen Shanghai's miles of foreign settlement. Wherever men come out from, don't they all look trim and smart? A Buddha needs gilding, and a man needs clothes. Watch me dress Old Zhang: I guarantee he'll look like a great merchant.” A’zhu smiled at his persuasive speech, then nudged her mother and whispered, “You'll have to dress up too. Otherwise you won't look like a proprietor's wife!” A proprietor's wife—she really was going to become one! Only yesterday it had been so much talk, with no certainty of what would happen. Now it was solid fact beyond doubt. Whatever else might be uncertain, those thousand taels were certainly real. Her thoughts wandered far ahead. Absorbed in imagining how she would conduct herself and what it would feel like, she forgot where she was.

等她惊醒过来，胡雪岩已经替老张挑了一大堆衣服，长袍短套，棉夹俱备。胡雪岩还要替老张买件“紫羔”的皮袍子，阿珠的娘不肯，说是：“将来挣了钱做新的！”才算罢手。结了帐，一共二十多两银子，胡雪岩掏出一大把银票，拣了一张三十两的，交了过去，找来的零头，他从阿珠手里取了手巾包过来，把它包在里面。“这算啥？”她故意这样问。“对面就是‘戴春林’分号，”胡雪岩说：“胭脂花粉我不会买，要你自己去挑。”阿珠果然去挑了许多，而且很舍得花钱，尽拣好的买，除了“鹅蛋粉”之类的本地货以外，还买了上海来的“水粉”、花露水、“洋肥皂”。要用这些东西打扮出来，博得胡雪岩赞一声“好”！

By the time she came to herself, Hu had chosen a great pile of clothes for Old Zhang: long gowns, short outer jackets, padded garments, and lined ones. He even wanted to buy a dark lambskin robe, but she stopped him. “When we've made some money, we'll have a new one made.” The bill came to more than twenty taels. Hu pulled out a large handful of banknotes, selected one for thirty taels, and paid. When the change came, he took A’zhu's handkerchief bundle and wrapped the money inside it. “What's this for?” she asked, though she knew perfectly well. “There's a branch of Dai Chunlin across the street. I know nothing about rouge and powder. You'll have to choose those yourself.” She did choose a great many things, spending freely and buying only the best. Besides local products such as egg-shaped powder cakes, she bought liquid face powder, scented toilet water, and foreign soap brought from Shanghai. She would use them to dress herself up and earn a word of praise from Hu.

* * *在老张动身到湖州的第二天，阿珠的娘弄了几样极精致的菜，起个大早，雇了顶小轿到石塔儿头去看张胖子。见了张太太，少不得有阵寒暄，很快地便由她所送的那四样菜上，转入正题，张太太在表示过意不去，张胖子却笑了，“ ‘十三只半鸡’，着实还有得吃！”他说。据说做媒的男女两家跑，从“问名”开始到“六礼”将成，媒人至少要走十三趟。主人家每一趟都要杀鸡款待，到“好日子”那天还有一只鸡好吃。不过新娘子要上轿，不能从容大嚼，至多只能吃半只，合起来便是十三只半，这是贫嘴的话，久而久之便成了做媒的意思。张太太一听这话，便极感兴趣地问他丈夫：“我们这位阿嫂是男家还是女家？”

The day after Old Zhang left for Huzhou, A’zhu's mother prepared several exquisite dishes, rose early, and hired a small sedan chair to take her to Fat Zhang's house on Stone Pagoda Street. After the customary greetings to Mrs Zhang, conversation soon turned from the four dishes she had brought to the purpose of her visit. Mrs Zhang protested at the trouble she had taken, but her husband laughed. “Thirteen and a half chickens! I've a good deal of eating ahead of me yet.” It was said that a matchmaker had to go between the two families at least thirteen times, from the first exchange of names to the completion of the six marriage rites. At every visit, the host would kill a chicken for the guest. There was another chicken on the wedding day, but with the bride due to enter her sedan chair, there was no time to linger over it: at most, half could be eaten. The total was thirteen and a half. Originally a facetious remark, the phrase had gradually become a synonym for matchmaking. Mrs Zhang immediately took an interest. “Does our visitor represent the bride or the groom?” she asked.

“女家。”“喔，恭喜，恭喜！”张太太向客人笑着道贺，然后又问她丈夫：“那么男家呢？”“你倒猜猜看！”张胖子道，“你也很熟的。”于是张太太从信和钱庄几个得力而未曾成家的伙计猜起，猜到至亲好友的少年郎君，说了七八个人，张胖子便摇了七八次头。“好了，好了！你猜到明天天亮都猜不着的。”他将他妻子往里面推，“闲话少说，你好到厨房里去了，今天有好菜，我在家早早吃了中饭，再到店里，等下我再跟你说。”一面推着，一面向他妻子使了个眼色。意思是关照她一进去便不必再出来了。

“The bride.” “Oh, congratulations, congratulations!” She smiled at her guest, then asked her husband, “And who is the groom?” “Guess! You know him very well.” She began with the capable, unmarried employees at Xinhe and went on to the young sons of their close friends and relations. She offered seven or eight names, and seven or eight times Fat Zhang shook his head. “Enough, enough! You could guess till dawn tomorrow and never get it.” He steered her towards the inner rooms. “Never mind the talk. Off to the kitchen with you. With such good food today, I'll have an early lunch at home before going to the bank. I'll tell you later.” As he ushered her away, he gave her a meaningful glance, telling her not to come out again.

这就是张胖子老练圆滑之外，因为，第一，胡雪岩跟阿珠的这头姻缘，究还不知结果如何？也不知胡雪岩是不是要瞒着家里？此时需要保守秘密，他妻子最近常到胡家去作客，万一不小心漏了口风，影响到他跟胡雪岩的交情，而胡雪岩现在是他最好、最要紧的一个朋友，决不能失掉的。其次他是为阿珠的娘设想。女儿给人作妾，谈起来不是什么光彩之事，怕她有初见面的人在座，难于启齿。这一层意思，阿珠的娘自然了解，越觉得张胖子细心老到，自己是找对了人。

This was where Fat Zhang's experience and tact showed. First, the outcome of the proposed match between Hu and A’zhu remained uncertain, and he did not know whether Hu intended to keep it from his family. For the moment, secrecy was necessary. His wife had lately been visiting the Hu household often. An inadvertent word might damage his friendship with Hu, now his closest and most important friend, whom he could not afford to lose. Second, he was thinking of A’zhu's mother. Giving one's daughter to a man as a concubine was hardly a glorious subject, and he feared she might find it difficult to discuss before someone she had only just met. She naturally understood this consideration and felt all the more certain that she had chosen the right man: he was thoughtful and experienced.

“张老板，”她说，“我的来意，你已经晓得了。这头亲事，能不能成功，全要靠你张老板费心。”“那何消说得？”张胖子很诚恳地答道，“雪岩是我的好朋友，就是你们两家不托我，我也要讨这杯喜酒来吃。”“噢！”阿珠的娘异常关切地问，“胡老爷也托过你了，他怎么说？”“他没有托我。我说‘两家’的意思是，随便他们男女两家哪一家。不都一样的吗？”“不一样，不一样。”阿珠的娘摇着头说，“胡老爷是你的好朋友，不错！不过今天我来求张老板，你张老板答应了，就是我们女家的大媒，总要帮我们阿珠说话才对。你想是不是呢？”张胖子笑了，“阿嫂！我服你。”他说，“到底是书香人家出身，说出话来，一下子就扎在道理上。好，好，你说，我总尽心就是了。”

“Mr Zhang,” she said, “you know why I've come. Whether this marriage can be arranged depends entirely on your kindness.” “There's no need to say that,” he answered earnestly. “Xueyan is a good friend of mine. Even if neither family asked me, I'd still want to help arrange the wedding.” “Oh!” she asked eagerly. “Has Mr Hu asked you too? What did he say?” “He hasn't asked me. By ‘neither family’ I meant either side, the bride's or the groom's. Isn't it all the same?” “No, no.” She shook her head. “Mr Hu is your good friend, certainly. But I'm the one who has come to ask you today. If you agree, you're the matchmaker for our side and ought to speak up for A’zhu. Don't you think so?” Fat Zhang laughed. “My good sister, I take my hat off to you. Your scholarly upbringing shows: the moment you speak, you go straight to the heart of the matter. Very well, tell me what you want. I'll do all I can.”

“多谢大媒老爷！”她想了想说，“我也不怕你笑话，说句老实话，我们阿珠一片心都在胡老爷身上，完全是感情，决不是贪图富贵。”“这我知道。”“大家爱亲结亲，财礼、嫁妆都不必去谈它。胡老爷看样子也喜欢我们阿珠，想来总也不肯委屈她的。”张胖子心里有些嘀咕了，既非贪图将来的富贵，又不是贪图眼前的财礼，那么所谓“不肯委屈”阿珠，要怎么样办呢？“我实话直说。这名分上头……要请张老板你给阿珠争一争。”这怎么争法？张胖子心想，总不能叫胡雪岩再娶！“莫非，”他忽然想到了，“莫非‘两头大’？”阿珠的娘反问一句：“张老板，你看这个办法行得通，行不通？”张胖子不愿作肯定的答复，笑一笑说：“如果换了是我，自然行得通。”

“Thank you, my honoured matchmaker!” She paused to think. “I won't be afraid of your laughing at me. To tell the truth, our A’zhu has set her whole heart on Mr Hu. It's entirely a matter of affection, with no thought of wealth or position.” “I know that.” “Let affection bring the families together. There's no need to discuss betrothal gifts or a dowry. Mr Hu seems fond of our A’zhu too. Surely he wouldn't wish to slight her.” Fat Zhang began to feel uneasy. If she sought neither future wealth nor immediate marriage gifts, what exactly did she mean by not slighting A’zhu? “I'll speak plainly. As to her standing in the family... I want you to press her claim, Mr Zhang.” How was he to do that? Surely Hu could not be expected to marry again as though he had no wife! Suddenly he understood. “Do you mean... two wives of equal standing?” “Mr Zhang, do you think that arrangement could be made?” she asked in return. Unwilling to give a definite answer, he smiled. “If it were up to me, of course it could.”

这表示在胡雪岩就不大可能。原因何在？阿珠的娘当然要打听。张胖子却又说不上来，他只是怕好事不谐，预留后步。其实他也不了解胡雪岩的家庭，不知道这桩好事，会有些什么障碍？不过，他向她保证，一定尽力去做这头媒，不论如何，最短期间内，必有确实的答复。同时他也劝她要耐心，事缓则圆，心太急反倒生出意外的障碍。他说象阿珠这样的人才，好比奇货可居，最好要让胡雪岩万般难舍，自己先开口来求婚，那样事情就好办了。阿珠的娘先有些失望，听到最后几句话，觉得很在道理。心里在想，阿珠也不可太迁就胡雪岩，这些事上面，真象做生意一样，太迁就顾客，反显得自己的“货色”不灵光似地，因而深深受教，但依旧重重拜托，能够早日谈成，早了一件心事，总是好的。

This implied that, with Hu, it might be difficult. Naturally she asked why. He could offer no reason: he was merely afraid the match might fail and was leaving himself room to retreat. In truth, he knew too little about Hu's household to foresee the obstacles. Nevertheless, he promised to do his utmost and, whatever happened, to obtain a definite answer as soon as possible. He also urged patience. Matters settled better when allowed time; haste could create unexpected difficulties. A girl like A’zhu, he said, was like a rare article whose owner could afford to wait. Best to make Hu feel he could not bear to lose her, until he proposed of his own accord. Then everything would be easier. Her initial disappointment faded as she heard these final words. They made excellent sense. A’zhu must not be too accommodating to Hu. In such matters it was just like business: indulge a customer too much and he began to suspect that the goods were inferior. She took the lesson deeply to heart, though she still pressed Zhang to help. The sooner things were settled, the sooner she would have one less worry.

于是张胖子一到店里，立刻打发一个小徒弟到胡家去说，请胡雪岩这天晚上到信和来吃饭，有要紧事要谈，不论迟早，务心劳驾。快到天黑，张胖子备了酒菜专诚等候。直到八点钟左右，胡雪岩才到，见面连声道歉，说王有龄那里有许多公事。“不是我的事情，是你的，这件事要一面吃酒一面谈，才有味道。”张胖子肃客入座，关照他店里的人，不喊不要进来，然后，把杯说媒，将阿珠的娘这天早晨的来意，原原本本告诉了胡雪岩。

As soon as Fat Zhang reached the bank, he sent a young apprentice to the Hu house with an invitation to dine at Xinhe that evening. There was something important to discuss, and Hu must please come, however late. Towards dusk Zhang had food and wine prepared and waited especially for him. Hu did not arrive until about eight, apologising repeatedly and explaining that there had been a great deal of official business at Wang's office. “This isn't my affair; it's yours. And it's a matter that will be all the pleasanter discussed over a drink.” Zhang courteously showed him to his seat and instructed the bank's employees not to come in unless called. Then, cup in hand, he took up his duties as matchmaker and related A’zhu's mother's request in full.

“事情当然要办的，不过我没有想到她这么心急。”“我也这么劝她。”张胖子说到这里，忽然露出极诡秘的笑容，凑近了低声问道：“雪岩，我倒要问你句话，到底他把阿珠弄上手了没有？”“干干净净，什么也没有。”“那她娘为什么这么急？”张胖子是替他宽慰的神气，“我还当生米已成熟饭，非逼你吃了下去不可呢！”“要吃也吃得下。不过现在这个当口，我还不想吃，实在也是没有工夫去吃，生意刚刚起头，全副精神去对付还不够，哪里有闲心电来享艳福？”张胖子心里明白，胡雪岩逢场作戏，寻些乐趣则可，要让他立一个门户，添上一个累，尚非其时，彼此休戚相关，他当然赞成胡雪岩把精力放在生意上面，所双这时候忘掉女家的重找，反倒站在胡雪岩这面了。

“Of course the matter must be dealt with,” Hu said. “I hadn't expected her to be in such a hurry.” “I advised her against hurrying too.” Suddenly Zhang gave a knowing smile, leaned closer, and lowered his voice. “Xueyan, let me ask you something. Have you and A’zhu been intimate?” “Nothing of the kind. Nothing has happened.” “Then why is her mother so anxious?” Zhang looked relieved on his behalf. “I thought the rice was already cooked and they meant to make you eat it!” “I could eat it if I had to. But just now I don't want to, and I really haven't the time. My business is only getting started. Even devoting all my energy to it scarcely seems enough. Where am I to find the leisure for romance?” Fat Zhang understood. Hu was willing to amuse himself as occasion offered, but the time had not come to establish another household and take on another burden. Their fortunes were linked, so Zhang naturally preferred him to concentrate on business. Forgetting the bride's mother's solemn commission, he now took Hu's side instead.

“那么，你说，你是怎么个意思？我来帮你应付。”胡雪岩有些踌躇了，阿珠的一颦一笑，此时都映现在脑子里，实在不忍心让她失望。“照我看，只有一个字：拖！”张胖子为他设谋。“拖下去不是个了局！”胡雪岩不以为然，“话要把它说清楚。”“怎么说法？”胡雪岩又踌躇了：“这话说出来，怕有人会伤心。”那当然是指阿珠，“你先说来听听，是怎么句话？”张胖子说，“我是站在旁边的，事情看得比较清楚。”“我在想，生意归生意，感情归感情，两件事不能混在一起。”“对啊！”张胖子鼓掌称善，“你的脑筋真清楚。不过我倒要问你，你在湖州开丝行，既然不是为了安顿阿珠，又何必找到老张？他又不是内行。”“他虽不是内行，但是老实、勤恳，这就够了。”胡雪岩问：“难道你我生来就会在‘铜钱眼里翻跟头’的？”

“Then tell me what you want to do. I'll help you manage it.” Hu hesitated. A’zhu's every smile and frown came into his mind, and he could not bear to disappoint her. “In my view, there's only one thing to do: delay,” Zhang advised. “Dragging it out solves nothing,” Hu objected. “We must make things clear.” “How?” Hu hesitated again. “I'm afraid someone will be hurt if I say what I think.” He meant A’zhu, of course. “Tell me first,” Zhang said. “Standing outside the situation, I can see it more clearly.” “I think business is business and affection is affection. The two shouldn't be mixed.” “Exactly!” Fat Zhang applauded. “What a clear head you have! But let me ask you this: if you aren't opening the Huzhou silk firm to provide for A’zhu, why choose Old Zhang? He knows nothing of the trade.” “He may not know the trade, but he's honest and hardworking. That's enough. Were you and I born knowing how to turn somersaults through the hole in a copper coin?”

“这话也不错，只是现在已经有感情夹在里面，事情就麻烦了。”“麻烦虽麻烦，有感情到底也是好的。有了感情，老张夫妇才会全心全意去做生意。”“话又兜回来了。”张胖子笑说，“我们在商量的，就是怎么才能够不把感情搞坏，可又不叫感情分你的心？”“正就是这话，所以不宜拖。拖在那里，老张夫妇心思不定，生意哪里还做得好？而且拖到后来，因情生恨，一定搞得彼此翻脸，那又何苦？”张胖子心想，翻来覆去都是胡雪岩一个人的话，自己脑筋也算清楚，嘴也不笨，就是说不过他，倒不如听他自己拿定了主意，该怎么办怎么办，自己只听他的好了。

“True enough. But now affection has got mixed up in it, and that makes things difficult.” “Difficult, perhaps, but affection is still a good thing. It's because of that bond that Old Zhang and his wife will give their whole hearts to the business.” “And now we've come round in a circle,” Zhang laughed. “What we're discussing is how to preserve that affection without letting it distract you.” “Precisely. That's why we mustn't let it drag on. With nothing settled, how can Old Zhang and his wife concentrate on business? And if it drags on long enough, affection will turn to resentment, and we'll surely fall out. What would be the point?” Fat Zhang reflected that, whichever way the argument turned, Hu had an answer. He considered himself clear-headed and no poor speaker, yet he could not get the better of him. He might as well let Hu decide what he wanted and then do as he was told.

“张先生，”胡雪岩看他闷声不响，只管端杯夹菜，便即问道：“你是不是觉得这个媒不做成功，在阿珠的娘面上，不好交代？”“这倒也不是。”张胖子答道：“能够做成功了，总是件高兴的事。”“做是一这做得成功的，不过媒人吃十三只半鸡，没有一趟头就说成功的。”胡雪岩笑说：“阿珠的娘拿手菜好得很，你一趟说成功，以后就没有好东西吃了。”张胖子也笑了，觉得胡雪岩的话，也颇有些滋味好辨，“那么，我这样子去说，你看行不行？”他说，“我告诉阿珠的娘，既然是‘两头大’，不能马马虎虎，先把八字合一合，看看有没有什么冲克？然后再跟老太太说明白，原配太太那里也要打个招呼。这两关过去，再排日子。这一来就是年把过去了，还是我说的话，一个‘拖’字。”“这一拖跟你所说的‘拖’不同。你的拖是没有一句准话，心思不定，我的拖是照规矩一定要拖，就算将来不成功，譬如八字犯冲之类，那是命该如此，大家没话好说。”张胖子想一想果然，“雪岩！”他举杯相敬，“随便你做啥，总是先想到退步。这一点我最佩服你，也是人家放心，愿意跟你打伙的道理。”

Seeing Zhang silently occupied with his cup and chopsticks, Hu asked, “Mr Zhang, do you feel that if the match doesn't come off, you won't know how to face A’zhu's mother?” “Not exactly,” Zhang replied. “But it would be a happy thing if it could be arranged.” “It certainly can be arranged. But the matchmaker eats thirteen and a half chickens; nobody settles everything on the first visit.” Hu smiled. “A’zhu's mother is a wonderful cook. Get it all settled at once, and you won't have any more of her good dishes.” Zhang laughed too, feeling that Hu's remark contained something worth considering. “Then what if I put it this way?” he asked. “I'll tell her mother that, since the two wives are to have equal standing, nothing can be done carelessly. First we'll compare the birth horoscopes for any clash. Then we'll explain matters to your mother, and your first wife must be consulted too. Once those two obstacles are past, we can choose a date. All that will take a year or so. It still comes back to what I said: delay.” “This delay is different from yours. Yours offers no definite word and leaves everyone uncertain. Mine follows the formalities, which necessarily take time. Even if it fails later—because the birth horoscopes clash, for instance—that will be fate, and nobody will have grounds for complaint.” Zhang thought about it and saw the distinction. “Xueyan!” He raised his cup in a toast. “Whatever you undertake, you always think first of a way to retreat. That's what I admire most about you. It's why people feel secure and are willing to join you in business.”

胡雪岩笑笑不答，只这样问道：“你什么时候去回报女家？”“我看她明天来不来？不来也不要紧，她在后天总见得着面。”后天就是王有龄荣行上任的日子，胡雪岩和张胖子要坐张家的船送到临平，阿珠的娘得预备一桌好菜，一点空都抽不出来，所以她心里虽急着想听回音，却跟张胖子的打算一样，只能等到他们上船的那天再说。

Hu smiled without answering, asking only, “When will you take your answer back to the bride's family?” “I'll see whether she comes tomorrow. If she doesn't, it won't matter. I'm bound to see her the day after.” That was the day Wang would leave to take up his new post. Hu and Fat Zhang were to accompany him as far as Linping aboard the Zhang family's boat. A’zhu's mother had to prepare an excellent banquet and could not spare a moment. Anxious though she was for news, she could only do as Fat Zhang had planned and wait until they came aboard.

那天王有龄在运司河下船，胡雪岩和张胖子在万安桥下船，约在拱宸桥的北新关前相会。两人一到船上，只见阿珠打扮得艳光照人，笑嘻嘻地把他们迎入舱中。胡雪岩和张胖子都注意到她的脸色，毫无忸怩不自然的神态，心里便都有数，她还不知道她娘在提亲，胡雪岩即时对张胖子使了个眼色，示意他不必说破。“胡老爷，张老板！”阿珠的娘出来打招呼，“你们请宽坐，我不陪你们。”打招呼是表面文章，实际上是来观望气色，不过胡、张两人都是很深沉的人，自然不会在脸上让她看出什么来，张胖子只是这样回答：“你尽管去忙，回头等你闲一闲再谈。”有了这句话，阿珠的娘便回到船梢去忙着整治筵席，船也解缆往北面去。张胖子乘胡雪岩跟阿珠谈笑得起劲的那一刻，托辞要去看看准备了些什么菜，一溜溜到船梢上。

On the appointed day, Wang boarded his vessel at the wharf by the salt administration, while Hu and Zhang embarked at Wan'an Bridge. They were to meet outside the Beixin customs barrier by Gongchen Bridge. As the two men stepped aboard, A’zhu, beautifully dressed and radiant, welcomed them into the cabin with a smile. Both noticed her expression. There was no embarrassment or awkwardness, so they knew she had heard nothing of her mother's marriage negotiations. Hu immediately signalled to Zhang not to mention them. “Mr Hu, Mr Zhang!” A’zhu's mother came out to greet them. “Please make yourselves comfortable. You'll excuse me if I can't sit with you.” The greeting was merely a pretext for studying their faces, but both men were too practised to reveal anything. Fat Zhang simply said, “Attend to whatever you must. We'll talk when you have a moment to spare.” Reassured by this, she went back to the stern to prepare the feast, and the boat cast off and headed north. When Hu and A’zhu were absorbed in laughing and talking, Fat Zhang slipped aft on the pretext of seeing what dishes were being prepared.

“阿嫂，恭喜你！”张胖子轻声说着，拱拱手道贺。就这一句话，把阿珠的娘高兴得眉开眼笑，除却连声“多谢”以外，竟不知道说什么好。“一切照你的意思。”张胖子紧接着说，“不过这不比讨偏房，要规规矩矩，按部就班来做，你们肯马虎，我媒人也不肯。阿嫂，这话是不是？”“是啊，一点不错。张老板，请你吩咐。”“那么我先讨个生辰八，阿珠今年十几？”“道光个八年戊戌生的，今年十六。”“那是属狗，雪岩属羊，羊同狗倒可以打伙，不犯冲的。”张胖子又问，“阿珠几月里生日？”

“My good sister, congratulations!” he said softly, clasping his hands. That single sentence made her beam. She could only repeat her thanks, unable to think of anything else to say. “Everything will be as you wish,” he continued. “But this isn't simply taking a concubine. It must be done properly, step by step. Even if you were willing to dispense with formalities, I, as matchmaker, would not. Isn't that right?” “Yes, absolutely. Tell me what to do, Mr Zhang.” “First, then, I'll need her birth particulars. How old is A’zhu this year?” “She was born in the eighteenth year of Daoguang, the wuxu year. She's sixteen this year.” “So she was born in the Year of the Dog. Xueyan was born in the Year of the Sheep. Sheep and Dog can get along; there's no clash there. What month is her birthday?”

犯冲不犯冲这句话提醒了她。媒人讨了八字去，自然要去请教算命的，拿胡雪岩的八字合在一起来排一排，倘或有何冲克，胡雪岩自己或许不在乎，但他堂上还有老亲，不能不顾忌。最好预先能够把胡雪岩的八字打听清楚，自己先请人看一看，如果有什么合不拢的地方，可以把阿珠生日的月份、日子、时辰改一改，叫乾坤两造合得拢。这样打定了主意，她便不肯先透露了，“张老板，准定这样办！”她说，“等我回到杭州，请人写好了送到府上去。”“好，好，就这样。”就这样三言两语，张胖子对女家的重托，算是圆满地交了差，走回中舱，避开阿珠的视线，向胡雪岩笑一笑，表示事情办得很顺利。

This reference to a clash alerted her. Naturally, the matchmaker would take A’zhu's birth particulars to a fortune-teller to compare with Hu's. If anything proved incompatible, Hu himself might not care, but with an elderly mother still living he could not disregard it. Better to find out Hu's birth particulars first and have someone examine them privately. If anything failed to agree, she could alter A’zhu's birth month, day, or hour to make the two horoscopes compatible. Having decided this, she would reveal nothing further yet. “We'll certainly do that, Mr Zhang,” she said. “When I return to Hangzhou, I'll have the particulars written out and sent to your house.” “Good, good. That's settled.” In those few exchanges, Fat Zhang had satisfactorily discharged the bride's family's commission. He returned to the middle cabin and, out of A’zhu's sight, smiled at Hu to show that everything had gone smoothly.

于是到了北新关前，等候王有龄的官船一到，讨关过闸，把王有龄和秦寿门、杨用之一起请到张家的船上，一面在水波不兴的运河中，缓缓行去，一面由阿珠伺候着，开怀畅饮。因为有秦、杨两师爷在座，既不能一无顾忌，畅抒肺腑，也不便放浪形骸，大谈风月，所以终席只是娓娓清谈。这席酒从拱宸桥吃到临平，也就是从中午吃到晚上。宴罢又移到王有龄船上去品茗闲话，到了起更时分，秦、杨二人告辞回自己的船，张胖子跟着也走了，只有胡雪岩为王有龄留了下来话别。

At the Beixin barrier they waited for Wang's official boat. When it arrived, they obtained clearance and passed through the lock, then invited Wang, Qin Shoumen, and Yang Yongzhi aboard the Zhang family's vessel. As it moved slowly over the still waters of the canal, A’zhu served them and they drank with pleasure. With the two secretaries, Qin and Yang, present, however, they could neither speak their hearts without reserve nor abandon propriety to discuss romantic adventures. The entire meal therefore passed in gentle, agreeable conversation. It lasted from Gongchen Bridge to Linping—from noon until evening. Afterwards they moved to Wang's boat for tea and more talk. At the first night watch, Qin and Yang took leave and returned to their own vessel. Fat Zhang followed them away. Wang detained only Hu for a private farewell.

虽只有几个月的相聚，而且也只是一水可航，两无可达的腰隔，但王有龄的离愁无限，除了感情以外，他还有着近乎孤立无倚的恐惧，因为这些日子来，倚胡雪岩如左右手，已养成“一日不可无此君”的习惯了。不过他也知道，要胡雪岩舍却自己的事业，到他衙门中去当遇事可以随时商议的客卿，不但办不到，就算办到了，又置秦、杨三人于何地？因此，这条心他是死了，退而求其次，唯有希望常见见面。于是他问：“雪岩，你什么时候到湖州来？”“不会太远。”他算了算日子，等阜康开了张，立即就要到湖州支看老张这方面的情形，“快则半个月，迟则月底。”他说。

They had been together for merely a few months, and only two days' journey along a navigable waterway would separate them, yet Wang felt an immense sorrow at parting. Besides affection, he felt something close to the fear of being left alone and helpless. In recent months he had relied on Hu as on his own hands and had grown so accustomed to him that he could scarcely do without him for a day. Yet he knew that he could not ask Hu to abandon his business and become a resident adviser at the yamen, available for consultation whenever anything arose. Even if it were possible, where would that leave Qin and Yang? He had therefore given up that hope. His next best wish was that they should meet often. “Xueyan, when are you coming to Huzhou?” he asked. “Before long.” Hu calculated the days. As soon as Fukang opened, he would go to Huzhou to see how Old Zhang was getting on. “In a fortnight at the earliest; by the end of the month at the latest.”

“我倒起起来了。”王有龄说，“ 前两天忙得不可开交，没有工夫问们。你要在湖州开丝行，是怎么回事？““这件事，我本来想到了湖州再跟你谈。此刻不妨就说给你听。”他把前后经过，细细讲了一遍，包括阿珠的亲事在内。事情相当复杂，王有龄一时还抓不着头绪，只是深感兴味地说：“你搞的花样真热闹。”“雪公，热闹都从你身上来的。”胡雪岩放低了声音说：“丝行当然有你一份。”“这不必，怕外面知道了，名声不好听。反正你我之间，无事不可商量，这些话现在都不必去谈它。倒是杨用之那里，你得想办法下些功夫。不然，他有他的主张，在公款的调度上，不无麻烦。”“我早已想到了。不过，我仍旧要用雪公你的名义来办。”“怎么办？”王有龄问。“秦、杨两家的眷属，住在哪里，我都打听清楚了。我会派人照应，到时候该送东西送东西，该送钱送钱，他们家里自会写信到湖州，秦、杨两位知道了，当然会见你的情。那时候一切都好办了。”

“That reminds me,” Wang said. “I've been so busy these last two days that I haven't had time to ask you. What's this about your opening a silk firm in Huzhou?” “I meant to discuss it with you when I got there. But I may as well tell you now.” Hu explained the whole story in detail, including the proposed marriage to A’zhu. It was rather complicated, and Wang could not immediately grasp all its threads. Deeply interested, he merely remarked, “You certainly keep things lively.” “Mr Xuexuan, all this activity comes from you.” Hu lowered his voice. “Naturally, there's a share in the silk firm for you.” “No need for that. If word got out, it wouldn't do my reputation any good. There's nothing you and I can't discuss in any case; we needn't talk about these things now. But you must find some way to work on Yang Yongzhi. Otherwise, with his own views, he may make it difficult to arrange the movement of public funds.” “I've already thought of that. But again, I intend to act in your name.” “How?” Wang asked. “I've found out where both Qin's and Yang's families live. I'll send people to look after them, providing gifts when gifts are called for and money when money is called for. Their families will naturally write to Huzhou. When Qin and Yang hear of it, they'll feel grateful to you. Then everything will be easy.”

“对，对！”王有龄欣然嘉许，“这样最好！我也不必先说破，等他们来跟我道谢时，我自会把交情卖到你身上。”胡雪岩笑着说了句杭州的俗语：“花花轿儿人抬人！”“那么，”王有龄突然露出顽皮的笑容，“你什么时候让阿珠坐花轿？”“现在还谈不到。走到哪里算哪里。”“你太太知道这件事不？”胡雪岩摇摇头：“最好不要让她知道。”“这一点我不赞成。”王有龄说，“你是绝顶聪明的人，总该晓得这两句话：”糟糠之妻不下堂，贫贱之交不可忘。‘如今虽非停妻再娶，也得跟你太太商量一下才好。“胡雪岩默然，觉得王有龄的话，有点打官腔的味道。

“Yes, yes!” Wang approved delightedly. “That's the best way. I needn't explain beforehand. When they come to thank me, I'll make sure you get the credit.” Hu smiled and quoted a Hangzhou saying: “We take turns carrying one another's flowered sedan chairs!” “Speaking of which,” Wang said, suddenly smiling mischievously, “when will you put A’zhu in a bridal sedan chair?” “It's too soon to talk of that. We'll take things as they come.” “Does your wife know?” Hu shook his head. “I'd rather she didn't.” “There I disagree,” Wang said. “A man as clever as you should surely know those two sayings: ‘Do not turn out the wife who shared your coarse meals and hardship; do not forget the friends you made in poverty.’ You're not dismissing your wife to marry another, but you ought to discuss the matter with her all the same.” Hu said nothing. He felt that Wang's words had something of an official sermon about them.

附：文本说明与本批精读方向（译者说明，非小说原文）

本批为第七章下半，从胡雪岩约船家在盐桥会面，译至王有龄赴湖州前与胡雪岩话别。本批包含老张对名声的顾虑、街头纠纷、丝行出资和用人安排、张胖子居中说媒，以及胡、王商议如何联络幕友家眷。正文按完整语义段落对照，不显示编号或语言标签。

文本处理

中文沿用输入文件。英文依上下文处理明显识别错误，如“惯倒”理解为摔倒，“所双”“重找”在说媒段落中分别按所以、重托理解。“道光个八年戊戌”按道光十八年戊戌处理；年龄仍保留人物所说的十六岁，不擅自换算周岁。“两无可达的腰隔”依相隔水路的上下文理解为两天可达的间隔。原文“秦、杨三人”只列出秦、杨两位，英文直接称 Qin and Yang，不增添第三个人。

“打抢得越老实越好”一句用字有疑，英文结合随后强调衣着挺括的语境，保守处理为外表须体面可靠，不臆造完整中文校文。

“下船”在赴任当日的叙述中是到河下登船，英文据行程译作 boarded / embarked，避免误解成抵达后离船。“十三只半鸡”的笑谈保留完整解释；婚礼当天因新娘须上轿而不能慢慢吃鸡，英文保留这一时间限制，不额外断定半只鸡是谁所吃。分场星号由新的叙事段落承接。

人物把婚事比作做生意、货色待价而沽的说法均保留为人物的言语或想法。张胖子关于生肖是否相冲的判断也按他的说法译出，不将其改写成译者对婚配的事实判断。亲密关系的问答使用非露骨措辞，保留双方所说“什么也没有”的信息，没有删去婚事讨论或增添情色细节。

新增名称与术语

纯号：Chunhao，盐桥附近的酒店；盐桥：Yan Bridge。
恒利钱店：Hengli Bank，信和在湖州的联号；“立个折子”在此按开设存取款账户译为 open a passbook account。
石塔儿头：Stone Pagoda Street；塞然弄堂：Sairan Lane。后者沿原文音译，不据猜测换成其他地名。
戴春林：Dai Chunlin，香粉店号；“鹅蛋粉”按其形状描述为 egg-shaped powder cakes。
三和尚：Third Monk，街头人物绰号；“撩鬼儿”以 liaoguier 配合 petty street ruffian 说明。
“两头大”：two wives of equal standing，指本段商议的两处家室中地位相等的安排；“偏房”“作妾”按具体句子译为 concubine，不抹去人物争取名分这一情节。
“八字”：birth particulars 或 birth horoscopes，分别突出出生年月日时的资料，以及用这些资料比较婚配的做法。
“仙人担”“石锁”按锻炼动作的语境描述为 weighted carrying pole、stone exercise weights；不擅自补充器材规格。

精读方向

阅读：分别追踪老张、阿珠母亲、张胖子、胡雪岩的目标。老张最在意名声，母亲争取女儿的名分，张胖子的立场会随着利益和谈话对象变化，胡雪岩则要兼顾生意、感情与退路。注意区分人物直接说出的理由与叙述者交代的真实顾虑。
完形：比较 but、yet、nevertheless、otherwise、even if 等连接方式。胡雪岩先说“感情与生意不能混在一起”，又说感情有利于尽心做事；要根据完整对话识别这并非单纯的同义重复，而是在调整论证角度。
段落排序：把说媒过程概括为“提出条件—保留答复—转述条件—内部商议—设计程序—向女方回复”。用 this、that、the matter、the arrangement 等回指寻找先后关系，再检查人物此刻知道什么、不知道什么。
翻译：选老张担忧名声或张胖子支开妻子的段落，先梳理 because、if、whether 所连接的因果与假设，再还原省略的主语。对“花花轿儿人抬人”等比喻，先译出互相帮衬的意思，再观察保留轿子意象如何连接下一句关于婚事的玩笑。
写作：可练习“明确职责有助于合作”“给予明确预期能减少焦虑”“沟通应回应对方真正的顾虑”等主题。用一两句概括小说事例，再改写为学习或团队合作的例子；注意把人物有效的说服手段与值得赞成的行为区分开来。
