“你一个人去，陌陌生生，怎么走法？”郁四把沂园的伙计喊了来说：“你到轿行里去喊顶轿子，说是我要的。”很快地，簇新的一顶轿子抬到，三个年轻力壮的轿伕，态度非常谦恭，这自然是郁四吩咐过了的缘故，胡雪岩说了地址，上轿就走。张家住在城外，就在码头旁边一条小巷子里，轿子一抬进去就塞住了，这条巷子，实在也难得有轿子经过，所以路人不但侧身而让，并且侧目而视，其中一个就是阿珠。他没有看见，她却发现了，“喂，喂！”她望着抬过门的轿子喊：“你们要抬到哪里去？”轿伕不理她，胡雪岩却听出是阿珠的声音，急忙拍拍扶手板，示意停轿。“怎么到这时候才来？”一见面就是埋怨的口气，显见得是“一家人”，让左邻右舍发觉了，会引起诧异。阿珠自觉失言，立刻红晕上脸，强笑道：“我们这条巷子里，难得有坐轿来的贵客！请进来，请进来。”“你先进去。”胡雪岩心细，看轿子停在门口，妨碍行人会挨骂，所以先关照轿伕，把轿子停在巷口，然后进门。

“How will you find your way alone in a place you don't know?” Yu called over an attendant at Yiyuan. “Go to the chair agency and have them send a chair. Tell them it's for me.” A brand-new sedan chair soon arrived, carried by three strong young men whose manner was exceptionally deferential—naturally, because Yu had sent for them. Hu gave the address, got in, and set off. The Zhang family lived outside the city, in a narrow lane beside the wharf. The chair almost blocked the lane as it entered. Sedan chairs seldom came this way, so passers-by not only turned sideways to let it pass but stared at it. Among them was A’zhu. Hu did not see her, but she saw him. “Hey! Hey!” she called as the chair went past the door. “Where are you taking him?” The bearers ignored her, but Hu recognised her voice and hurriedly tapped the armrest to signal a halt. “Why have you come so late?” she complained the instant they met. Her manner was plainly that of someone addressing a member of the family, which would surprise any neighbours who noticed. Realising her slip, she blushed and forced a smile. “We rarely have distinguished guests arrive by chair in this lane! Please come in.” “You go in first.” Hu had noticed that the chair would obstruct pedestrians and invite abuse if left at the door. He told the bearers to wait at the mouth of the lane, then entered.

进门就是客堂。里面说话，大门外的人都听得见，自然不便，阿珠把他领到后面，隔着一个小小的天井，东面两问，看样子是卧室，西面也是两间，一间厨房，炖肉的香味四溢，一间堆着什物。“只有到我房间里坐了！”阿珠有些踌躇，“实在不大方便。”不方便是因为她父母都不在家，“到哪里去了？”胡雪岩问。“ 还不是伺候你胡老爷！”阿珠微带怨怼地答道，“爹到衙门看你去了，娘在河滩上，看有什么新鲜鱼买一条，好等你来吃。”“那么，你呢？你在门口等我？”“哪个要等你？我在等我娘。”“闲话少说。”胡雪岩说，“要去通知你爹一声，不要叫他空等了。”“不用，说好了的，等不到就回来，也快到家了。”

The front door opened straight into the reception room. Anyone outside could hear what was said there, making conversation awkward. A’zhu led him to the rear. Across a tiny courtyard stood two rooms on the east, apparently bedrooms. There were two on the west as well: a kitchen, from which the smell of stewing meat spread, and a room full of miscellaneous belongings. “We'll have to sit in my room,” A’zhu said hesitantly. “It really isn't very convenient.” The difficulty was that neither parent was home. “Where have they gone?” Hu asked. “Where else? Seeing to your needs, Mr Hu!” she answered with mild resentment. “Father's gone to the yamen to look for you. Mother's at the riverbank, hoping to buy a fresh fish for your meal.” “And you? Were you waiting for me at the door?” “Who'd wait for you? I was waiting for Mother.” “Enough of that,” he said. “Someone must tell your father. We can't leave him waiting for nothing.” “There's no need. We agreed he'd come back if he couldn't find you. He should be home soon.”

说着，阿珠推开房门。屋中刚刚裱糊过，四白落地，十分明亮。一张床，一张梳头桌，收拾得很洁净，桌上还有只花瓶，插着几朵荷花。“地方太小了！”阿珠不好意思地说。胡雪岩开了一句玩笑，她白了他一眼：“说说就没有好话了。”“来，来，坐下来再说。”两人坐下后，他刚要开口，阿珠像突然想起了什么，跳起身跑到客堂里，转了一圈又回来。“你做什么去了？”“闩门。”她说，“大门不关上，客堂里的东西叫人偷光了都不晓得。”胡雪岩明白这只是托词，她是不想让父母突然进来，便笑着答道：“现在你可以放心了。”

As she spoke, A’zhu pushed open her door. The walls had just been repapered, white all the way to the floor, making the room bright. A bed and dressing table were kept scrupulously clean, and a few lotus flowers stood in a vase on the table. “It's so small!” she said apologetically. Hu made a teasing remark, and she gave him a reproachful glance. “You can't speak without saying something improper.” “Come, sit down and talk.” They sat together, but just as he was about to speak, A’zhu seemed to remember something. She jumped up, ran into the reception room, and returned. “What did you go to do?” “Bolt the door. If we leave it open, someone could steal everything in the reception room without our knowing.” Hu understood that this was an excuse: she did not want her parents to walk in unexpectedly. “Now you can stop worrying,” he answered with a smile.

胡雪岩有了过分亲近的举动。阿珠拒绝了他的一次举动后，他没有继续坚持，转而说起绣花，问她为什么不给自己绣件衣物。“懒得动。”“你好好绣一个。绣好了，我有奖赏。”“奖赏！”阿珠笑道，“奖什么？”“奖你一条金链条。你看好不好？”这怎么不好？阿珠直望着他：“这样子讲究？”“这算得了什么？将来有得你讲究。”“好！一言为定。”阿珠很起劲地说，“我好好绣一件红的。你看，绣什么花样？”“自然是鸳鸯戏水。”阿珠脸红了，低着头不作声。“怎么样？”他催问，“这个花样好不好？”她点点头，又看了他一眼。两人的私下相处这时被门外的两声敲门打断，接着有人喊：“阿珠，阿珠！”

Hu behaved too familiarly. When A’zhu refused one of his advances, he did not press it and turned the conversation to embroidery, asking why she had not embroidered a garment for herself. “I can't be bothered.” “Make a really fine piece. When you've finished, I'll give you a reward.” “A reward?” A’zhu laughed. “What sort?” “A gold chain. How would you like that?” Naturally she liked the idea. Looking straight at him, she asked, “Something as fine as that?” “That's nothing. You'll have plenty of fine things in future.” “Very well, it's a promise!” she said eagerly. “I'll embroider a red piece. What pattern should I use?” “Mandarin ducks playing in the water, of course.” A’zhu blushed and lowered her head without speaking. “Well?” he urged. “Would that be a good pattern?” She nodded and glanced at him again. Their private exchange was then interrupted by two knocks and a voice calling, “A’zhu! A’zhu!”

“我娘回来了！”阿珠慌忙起身，诸事不做，先照镜子，镜子里一张面泛桃花的脸，鬓边也有些乱，她着急他说：“都是你害人！这样子怎么走得出去？”“白天不做虚心事，夜半敲门心不惊！怕什么？我去开门，你把心定下来。”胡雪岩倒真沉得住气，把长衫抹一抹，泰然自若地走了出去，开开门来，笑嘻嘻地叫了一声，“干娘！”“咦！”阿珠的娘惊喜地问，“什么时候来的？”“刚来不多一息。”“阿珠呢？”“在后面。”胡雪岩知道阿珠红晕未退，有心救她一救，便问这样，问那样，绊住了阿珠的娘，容不得她抽身。

“Mother's back!” A’zhu jumped up and looked in the mirror before doing anything else. Seeing her flushed face and disordered hair, she said anxiously, “It's all your fault! How can I go out looking like this?” “If you've done nothing wrong by day, a knock at midnight won't frighten you. What's there to fear? I'll open the door. Take a moment to calm down.” Hu was indeed perfectly composed. He smoothed his robe, walked out calmly, opened the door, and greeted her mother with a cheerful “Auntie!” “Why!” she exclaimed, surprised and pleased. “When did you arrive?” “Only a little while ago.” “Where's A’zhu?” “At the back.” Knowing that A’zhu was still blushing, he tried to help her by asking one question after another, keeping her mother occupied and preventing her from going inside.

而她记挂着拎在手里的一条活鳜鱼，“桃花流水鳜鱼肥”，春天不希罕，夏天却难得，而且鳜鱼往往出水就死，这却是一条活的，更为名贵，急于想去“活杀”，偏偏胡雪岩絮絮不休，只好找个空隙，向里大喊：“阿珠阿！”阿珠已经心定神闲，把发鬓梳得整整齐齐的走了出来。她娘便吩咐她去剖鱼，剖她了等她来动手，又问胡雪岩喜欢清蒸，还是红烧呢？“活鳜鱼不容易买到，自然是清蒸。”阿珠替他作了主。胡雪岩还有许多事要办，只待见老张一面，交代几句话就要走，现在看样子，这顿饭是非吃不可了！这就索性在这里，跟老张把事情都商量好了再说。“干娘！”他说，“吃饭是小事，越简单越好、等老张回来，我有许多话说。市面要弄得很热闹，大家都有得忙，工夫不能白糟蹋！”阿珠的娘知道他是实话，好在她手下快，好老张从县衙门回家，饭菜都已齐备，四个人团团坐下，边吃边谈。

Her mother, however, was worried about the live mandarin fish in her hand. As the verse said, “Peach blossoms drift on flowing waters, and the mandarin fish grow fat.” They were common enough in spring but hard to find in summer. Moreover, they often died as soon as they were taken out of the water. This one was still alive, making it especially valuable. She wanted to prepare it while it was fresh, but Hu would not stop talking. Finding a pause at last, she called into the house, “A’zhu!” A’zhu emerged, calm now and with her hair neatly combed. Her mother told her to clean the fish and leave it ready for cooking, then asked whether Hu preferred it steamed or braised in soy sauce. “A live mandarin fish is hard to come by. Steamed, of course,” A’zhu decided for him. Hu still had much to do. He had intended only to see Old Zhang, give him a few instructions, and leave. Now it seemed he could not escape this meal. He might as well stay and settle everything with Old Zhang. “Auntie,” he said, “the meal isn't important. The simpler the better. I've a great deal to say when Old Zhang comes back. We're going to make business lively here. Everyone will be busy, and we can't waste time!” She knew he meant it. Fortunately, she worked quickly. By the time Old Zhang returned from the county yamen, the food was ready. The four sat round the table, eating and talking.

“一家人，我先要说句老实话。”高踞上座的胡雪岩说：“明天一早，第一件事就是搬家！不管什么地方，搬了再说，这里实在太小了。”老张夫妇，面面相觑，他们的感想一样，搬家是件大事，要看房子，拣黄道吉日，家具什物虽不多，收拾起来也得两三天。胡雪岩一看他们的脸色就知通他们的心思，数着手指说。“第一，房子明天一大早去看，象个样子就可以，先租下来住了再说，好在自己要买房子，不过一个短局，她歹都无所谓。第二，这些家具将来也用不着，不如送了左邻右舍，做个人情，另外买新的。第三，拣日不如撞日，说搬就搬，明天一天把它都弄舒齐。”

“We're family, so let me speak frankly.” Seated in the place of honour, Hu began, “The first thing tomorrow morning is to move house! Anywhere will do for now. This place is much too small.” Old Zhang and his wife exchanged glances. Both were thinking the same thing. Moving was a major undertaking. They must find a house and choose an auspicious day. Though they had little furniture and few possessions, packing would still take two or three days. Hu read their thoughts in their faces and counted off the points on his fingers. “First, look at houses early tomorrow. Anything reasonably presentable will do. Rent it and move in. Since you'll be buying a house eventually, this is only temporary, and its quality doesn't matter much. Second, you won't need this furniture later. Give it to the neighbours, earn their goodwill, and buy new things. Third, taking the day that comes is better than choosing one. Once you decide to move, move! Get everything settled tomorrow.”

“明天一天怕来不及。”阿珠的娘踌躇着说。“那就两天。”胡雪岩很“慷慨”地放宽了限期，但又重重地叮嘱了一句，“后天晚上，我到你们新搬的地方来吃饭。”“哪有这么快？”阿珠提出抗议，“你只管你自己说得高兴，不想想人家。”“来得及，来得及！”阿珠的娘不愿违拗胡雪岩的意思，但只有一点顾虑，叫阿珠去拿皇历来看。刚好，第二天、第三天都是宜于迁居的好日子，那就连最后一点顾虑都消除了，决定吃完晚饭，连夜去找房产经纪觅新居。

“I'm afraid we couldn't manage it all tomorrow,” A’zhu's mother said hesitantly. “Then take two days.” Hu extended the deadline with great “generosity,” but added emphatically, “The evening after tomorrow, I'll come to supper at your new house.” “How can it be done so fast?” A’zhu protested. “You enjoy giving orders without thinking what they mean for other people.” “We can manage, we can!” Her mother did not want to oppose him. She had only one remaining concern and asked A’zhu to bring the almanac. Fortunately, both of the next two days were favourable for moving. With that last anxiety removed, they decided to find a property broker that very evening after supper and begin looking for a new home.

“不要怕花钱！”胡雪岩取出一张二百两的银票，放在她面前：“先拿这个去用。我在湖州还要开钱庄，另外也还有些些生意要做，只怕事情做不完，不怕没有钱用。他们照我的话做，没有错！”这句话为他们带来了满怀的兴奋，但都矜持着，只睁大了眼，迷惘地看着这位“娇客”。喝了几杯的胡雪岩，回想这两无的经历，也是满心愉悦，得意非凡，因而谈兴大发，“说句实请，我也没有想到，今年脱运交运，会走到这样一步！”他说，“哪个说‘福无双至’？机会来起来，接二连三，推都推不开。我现在最苦的是，人手不足，一个人当两个人，一天当两天，都还不够，实实在在要三头六臂才好。”“这就是所谓‘能者多劳’！”阿珠的娘到底是大小姐出身，这样掉了一句话。“说到‘能’，那倒不必假客气，我自己晓得我的本事，不过光是我一个人有本事也不行，‘牡丹虽好，绿叶扶持’。干娘，你说是不是？”“是啊！不过你也不是‘光杆儿牡丹，，我们大家齐心合力，帮你来做。”

“Don't be afraid to spend money!” Hu took out a banknote for two hundred taels and placed it before her. “Use this to begin with. I'm going to open a bank in Huzhou, and I've other business plans too. My worry is getting everything done, not finding money. Follow my advice and you won't go wrong!” His words filled them with excitement, though they kept it to themselves, merely staring wide-eyed in bewilderment at this honoured prospective son-in-law. After several cups of wine, Hu was also delighted and immensely pleased with himself as he recalled the past two days. He became expansive. “Honestly, I never imagined that this year's change of fortune would bring me so far! Who says blessings never come in pairs? Opportunities keep arriving one after another. I couldn't turn them away if I tried. My greatest difficulty now is a shortage of people. Even doing two men's work and fitting two days into one isn't enough. I really need three heads and six arms!” “That's what they mean by ‘the capable must labour more’!” A’zhu's mother, after all, had grown up as a young lady and could produce a polished phrase. “As for ability, there's no need for false modesty. I know what I can do. But ability alone isn't enough. Even the finest peony needs green leaves to support it. Isn't that so, Auntie?” “Indeed. But you aren't a peony on a bare stalk. We'll all work together to help you.”

“就是这话。大家帮我来做！再说名实话，帮我就是帮自己。”胡雪岩看着老张说，“县衙门的户书郁四，你总晓得？”“晓得！”老张答道，“码头上就凭他一句话。”“那么我告诉你、郁四要眼我联手做丝生意。老张，你想想看，我在湖州，上有王大老爷，下有郁四，要钱有钱，要路子有路子，如果说不好好做一番市面出来，自己都对不起自己了。“老张老实，越是他这样说，越觉得不安，生意做得太大，自己才具不胜，所以踌躇着说：“只怕我挑不动这副担子！”“这话也是，”阿珠的娘也有些惴惴然，“市面太大，他应付不来。再说，郁四手下有的是人，未见得……”“未见得什么？”胡雪岩抢讨她的话来说，“郁四是怎么样的人，你们总也晓得。光棍做事，只要是朋友，只有拉人家一把，没有踹人家一脚的道理。他也晓得我们的交情不同，怎么好说不要老张？你们老夫妇俩放心，丝行开起来，你们只要把店里管好，坐在那里就有进帐。总而言之一句话，要勤、要快，事情只管多做，做错了不要紧！有我在错不到哪里去的。”

“Exactly. Everyone must help. And to speak frankly again, helping me means helping yourselves.” Hu looked at Old Zhang. “You know Yu Si, the county revenue clerk?” “Certainly,” Old Zhang answered. “A word from him settles everything at the wharf.” “Then let me tell you: Yu is joining me in the silk business. Think of it, Old Zhang. In Huzhou I have Mr Wang above and Yu below, all the money and connections I need. If I didn't build a flourishing business with that, I'd be letting myself down.” Simple and honest as he was, Old Zhang grew more uneasy the more Hu said. A business on such a scale was beyond his abilities. “I'm afraid the burden may be too much for me,” he said hesitantly. “That's true,” A’zhu's mother added, also anxious. “He couldn't manage anything too large. Besides, Yu has plenty of men. He may not...” “May not what?” Hu interrupted. “You know what sort of man Yu is. A man of standing helps his friends up; he doesn't kick them down. He knows our relationship is special. How could he suggest leaving Old Zhang out? Set your minds at rest. Once the silk firm is open, all you need do is manage the shop properly, and the money will come in. In a word, work hard and act quickly. Do as much as you can. Mistakes don't matter—with me here, you won't go far wrong.”

老张一面听，一面点头，脸上慢慢不同了，是那种有了把握的神气。等扒完一碗饭，他拿筷子指一指胡雪岩说：“你慢慢吃！我出去一趟。”“这么晚了！”阿珠接口问道：“到哪里去？”“我去看房子。我想起有个地方，前后两进，好象大了点，不管它，先租下来再说。”“对啊！”胡雪岩大力高兴，“你请，你请！如果回来得快，我还好在这里等你听回音。”等老张一走，阿珠下逐客令了：“我看你也早点吃完饭走吧，一则你忙，二则，你走了，我们好收拾。不然明天怎么搬？”“这倒是老实话。”她娘也这样说。胡雪岩深感安慰，这一家三个人，就这一顿饭的工夫，脑筋都换过来了。如果手下每个有都是这样子勤快，何愁生意不发达？

Old Zhang nodded as he listened, and his expression gradually changed into one of confidence. When he had finished a bowl of rice, he gestured towards Hu with his chopsticks. “Take your time over the meal. I'm going out.” “At this hour?” A’zhu asked. “Where?” “To look at a house. I've remembered one with two courtyards, front and back. Perhaps it's rather large, but never mind. I'll rent it first.” “That's the spirit!” Hu was delighted. “Go, go! If you're quick, I can wait here and hear the news.” Once Old Zhang had left, A’zhu began sending Hu away too. “You'd better finish eating and go early. First, you're busy. Second, when you've gone, we can pack. Otherwise, how are we to move tomorrow?” “That's plain truth,” her mother agreed. Hu was deeply reassured. In the space of a single meal, the whole family had changed its way of thinking. If everyone under him worked with such energy, how could his business fail to prosper?

到第二天，大家都忙，老张夫妇忙着搬定，胡雪岩忙着筹划设立阜康分号，跟杨用之商量了一上午。到了日中，依旧到水晶阿七家去访郁四。谈完正事，谈到小和尚，却是阿七先提起来的，“胡老板，”她问，“你想把小和尚带到杭州去？”“是啊，还不知道他自己的意思怎么样？”“你自然肯的。”阿七又问，“我倒不懂胡老板为啥要把他带到杭州？”这话在郁四问，不足为奇，出于阿七之口，就得好好想一想，或许她已经疑心是郁四的指使，先得想办法替他解释这可能已有的误会。“老实跟四嫂说，我看人最有把握。”他从从容容地答道：“小和尚人最活络，能到大地方去历练历练，将来是一把好手。我不但要带他到杭州，还想带他到上海。”“上海十里夷场，他一去，更不得了。”阿七以一种做姐姐的口吻拜托：“胡老板要好好管一管他。”“是啊！”胡雪岩趁机说道，“郁四哥劝我，还是把小和尚放在湖州，多几个‘管头’，好叫他不敢调皮。调皮不要紧，只上‘上路’，我有办法管他。”这一说，阿七释然，郁四欣然，事实上阿七确有些疑心，让胡雪岩把小和尚带到杭州，是郁四的授意、现在才知道自己的疑心是多余的。

Everyone was busy the next day. Old Zhang and his wife dealt with the move, while Hu planned the Fukang branch, discussing it with Yang Yongzhi throughout the morning. At noon he again called on Yu at Crystal A’qi's house. When their business was finished, the conversation turned to Little Monk. A’qi herself raised the subject. “Mr Hu, are you thinking of taking Little Monk to Hangzhou?” “Yes, though I don't yet know what he thinks of it.” “He's bound to agree. But I don't understand why you want to take him there.” From Yu, the question would have been unremarkable. From A’qi, it required thought. Perhaps she already suspected that Yu had arranged it. Hu must first remove any such misunderstanding on his behalf. “To be frank, Sister-in-law, I have great confidence in my judgement of people,” he answered calmly. “Little Monk is quick and adaptable. If he gains experience in a larger place, he'll become a very capable man. I mean to take him not only to Hangzhou but to Shanghai as well.” “Once he's in Shanghai's miles of foreign settlements, there'll be no holding him.” Speaking like an elder sister, A’qi urged, “You must keep him under proper control.” “Certainly!” Hu seized the opportunity. “Brother Yu advised me to leave him in Huzhou, where more people could keep an eye on him and stop him getting into mischief. Mischief doesn't worry me. As long as he understands how to behave, I have ways of managing him.” A’qi was reassured and Yu pleased. She had indeed suspected that Yu was sending Little Monk away through Hu. Now she believed her suspicions had been unnecessary.

“小和尚是我从小的邻居。”阿七显然也想到了，自己对小和尚这么关心，须有解释，“他姐姐是我顶顶好的朋友，死了好几年了。小和尚就当我是他的姐姐，他人最聪明，就是不务正业，好赌，赌输了总来跟我要。所以，”她愤然作色，“有些喜欢嚼舌头的，说我跟他怎么长，怎么短，真气人！说句难听的话，我是……““好了，好了！”郁四真怕她口没遮拦，自道“身分”，因而赶紧拦住她说：“ ‘只要我没嚼你的舌头就好了，旁人的闲话，管他呢？”“你也敢！”阿七戟手指着，放出泼妇的神态，但随即又笑了，笑得极其妩媚。胡雪岩倒是欣赏她这样爽朗的性情，但郁四的禁脔，唯有收摄心神，视如不见。转念想到小和尚，既然话已说明，便无须有所顾忌。此刻正在用人之际，应该谈定了，马上拿他来派用场。于是他说，“郁四哥，此刻能不能跟小和尚见个面？”“怎么不能？”郁四站起身说：“走！”

“Little Monk has been my neighbour since childhood.” A’qi evidently realised that her concern for him needed explaining too. “His elder sister was my closest friend. She died several years ago. He treats me as his sister. He's terribly clever, but he won't settle to honest work. He loves gambling, and when he loses he always comes to me for money. So...” Her expression became indignant. “Some people who love gossip make up all sorts of stories about us. It infuriates me! To put it bluntly, I'm a...” “There, there!” Yu was truly afraid she would speak without restraint and name her profession. He hurriedly stopped her. “As long as I'm not gossiping about you, why bother about anyone else?” “You wouldn't dare!” She pointed at him fiercely, looking for a moment like a shrew, then immediately broke into a charming smile. Hu admired her frank, spirited nature. But she was Yu's exclusive companion, so he collected himself and pretended not to notice. Returning his thoughts to Little Monk, he decided that, now the situation had been explained, there was no reason to hesitate. He urgently needed men. He should settle the matter and put the fellow to work immediately. “Brother Yu, could we see Little Monk now?” “Why not?” Yu rose. “Let's go.”

两个人又到了沂园。郁四派人把小和尚去找了来，招呼过后，他问：“四叔寻我有话说？”郁四先不答他的话，只问：“你的赌，戒得掉戒不掉？”小和尚一愣，笑着说道：“四叔要我戒赌？”“我是为你好。你这样子天天滥赌，哪一天才得出头？”郁四又说：“靠赌吃饭没出息，你晓不晓得？”小和尚不答，只看看胡雪岩，仿佛已知道郁四的意思了。于是郁四又问：“你想不想出去闯闯码头呢？”一听这话，小和尚显得很注意，而眼中看得出来，是憧憬大地方热闹，就象小孩听说能跟大人去看戏的那种神色。

They returned to Yiyuan. Yu sent for Little Monk, who asked after greeting them, “You wanted to speak to me, Fourth Uncle?” Instead of answering, Yu asked, “Can you give up gambling or can't you?” Little Monk paused in surprise, then smiled. “You want me to stop gambling, Fourth Uncle?” “It's for your own good. If you go on gambling recklessly every day, when will you ever get anywhere? There's no future in living by gambling. Do you understand?” Little Monk said nothing, only glanced at Hu as though he already grasped Yu's intention. Yu asked another question. “Would you like to go out and try your luck in a new place?” Little Monk immediately became attentive. His eyes showed his longing for the excitement of a larger city, like a child's at the prospect of going to the theatre with the grown-ups.

“胡老板想带你到杭州去。”郁四说道，“我已经答应胡老板了，要问问你自己的意思。”“四叔已经答应了，我不愿意也要办得到呀！”“小鬼！”郁四笑着骂道：“我不见你这个空头人情。你自己说一句，到底愿意不愿意呢？胡老板的脾气，不喜欢人家勉强。”“愿意！”小和尚很清楚的表示，同时向胡雪岩点点头。“那好了。你现在就跟胡老板去办事，胡老板的事，就是我的事。”有这句话交代，什么都在里头了。胡雪岩辞别郁四，找了个清静酒店，先要了解了解小和尚的一切。

“Mr Hu wants to take you to Hangzhou,” Yu continued. “I've already agreed to Mr Hu's proposal. Now I want to hear what you think.” “Since you've already promised, Fourth Uncle, I must manage it even if I don't want to!” “You little rascal!” Yu laughed as he scolded him. “I don't need that empty show of doing me a favour. Tell us plainly: do you want to go or not? Mr Hu doesn't like people acting against their will.” “I do!” Little Monk stated clearly, nodding to Hu. “Then that's settled. Go and work with him now. His business is my business.” Those words said everything necessary. Hu took his leave of Yu and found a quiet wine shop where he could first learn all he needed to know about Little Monk.

小和尚名叫陈世龙，孑然一身，身无恒业，学过刻字店的生意，因为没有终日伏案的耐性，所以半途而废。“这样说，你认得字？”“认得几个。”小和尚——陈世龙说，“ ‘百家姓’最熟。”“你说话倒有趣。”胡雪岩答道，“会不会打算盘？”“会。不过不大精。我在牙行帮过忙。”“牙行”是最难做的一种生意，就凭手里一把秤，要把不相识的买卖双方，撮合成交易，赚取佣金。陈世龙在牙行帮过忙，可知能干，胡雪岩越发中意了。

Little Monk's name was Chen Shilong. He had no family and no regular occupation. He had once apprenticed in an engraver's shop but lacked the patience to sit at a bench all day and had given up halfway through. “Then you can read?” “A few characters,” Little Monk—Chen Shilong—replied. “I know the Hundred Family Surnames best.” “You have an amusing way of talking,” Hu said. “Can you use an abacus?” “Yes, though not particularly well. I've helped in a brokerage.” Brokerage was among the most difficult trades. Armed with little more than a set of scales, a broker had to bring together buyers and sellers who had never met and earn a commission by making their transaction possible. Chen's experience there showed ability, and Hu liked him still better.

“听说你喜欢赌，是不是？”“赚两个外快用。”陈世龙说，“世界上好玩的花样多得很，不一定要赌。”“说得对！你这算是想通了。你去过上海没有？”“没有。”“你去过上海就知道了。光是见见世在就很好玩，世界上的享，没有一样不好玩，只看你怎么样想？譬如说，我想跟你交朋友，交到了，心里很舒服，不就很好玩吗？“这话是陈世龙从未听过的，有些不懂，却似乎又有些领悟，所以只是看着他发愣。

“I hear you're fond of gambling. Is that right?” “Just to make a little extra money,” Chen said. “There are plenty of enjoyable things in the world. Gambling isn't essential.” “Quite right! You've thought that through. Have you ever been to Shanghai?” “No.” “Once you've been, you'll understand. Simply seeing more of the world is enjoyable. There isn't a thing in life that can't be interesting; it all depends on how you look at it. For instance, suppose I want to make friends with you. When I've succeeded, I feel pleased. Isn't that enjoyable?” Chen had never heard such an argument. He did not entirely understand it, yet seemed to glimpse its meaning. He could only stare at Hu in wonder.

“世龙，我再问你一句话……”看他不说下去了，陈世龙不由得奇怪，刚喊得一声：“胡老板……”胡雪岩打断了他的话。“你叫我胡先生。”这就有点收他做学生的味道在内，陈世龙对他很服帖，便改口说道：“胡先生，你要问我句什么话？”“我这句话，如果问得不对，你不要摆在心上，也不必跟人说起。我问你，阿七到底对你有意思没有？”“这我哪里晓得。”“你难道看不出来？”“我看不出来。我只晓得我自己，郁四叔疑心病重，我哪里会对阿七动什么脑筋？”陈世龙停了一下又说：“赌输了跟她伸伸手是有的。别的没有。”胡雪岩用他，别的都不在乎，唯一顾虑的就是他跟阿七的关系，这一点非弄得清清楚楚不可。因而又向下追问：“你动不动歪脑筋是一口事，动不动心又是一回事。你说，你心里喜欢不喜欢阿七？”陈世龙到底资格还嫩，不免受窘，犹豫了一会答道：“男人总是男人嘛！”这句话说很明白了，胡雪岩对他的答复很满意，因为他说了实话。不过，接下来的却是告诫。

“Shilong, let me ask you something else...” When Hu stopped, Chen was puzzled. He had just begun, “Boss Hu...” when Hu interrupted him. “Call me Mr Hu.” There was a suggestion in this of taking him as a pupil. Chen was ready to follow his lead and changed his form of address. “Mr Hu, what did you want to ask?” “If the question is improper, don't take offence, and don't repeat it to anyone. Tell me: does A’qi have feelings for you?” “How would I know?” “Can't you tell?” “I can't. I only know my own mind. Fourth Uncle Yu is suspicious by nature. How could I think of making advances to A’qi? I do ask her for money after losing at gambling,” he added after a pause. “Nothing else.” In employing Chen, Hu was unconcerned about anything except his relationship with A’qi. He had to make this perfectly clear. He pressed further. “Having designs on her is one thing; feeling attracted is another. Tell me, are you fond of her?” Chen was still inexperienced and could not help feeling embarrassed. After hesitating, he answered, “A man's a man, after all!” That was plain enough. Hu was satisfied, because it was an honest answer. His next words, however, were a warning.

“你也怨不得你四叔疑心病重。有道是‘麻布筋多，光棍心多’，你年轻力壮，跟阿七又是从小就认识的，常来常往，人家自然要说闲话。”胡雪岩停了一下又说：“照我看，你郁四叔少不得阿七，你就做得格外漂亮些。”“怎么做法？”“从此不跟阿七见面。”“这做得到。我答应胡先生。”陈世龙放出很豁达的神态，扬着脸说，“天下漂亮女人多得是！”“这话说得好！”胡雪岩心想得要试一试他，从身上取出来五十两一张银票，“这点钱，你先拿去用。”陈世龙迟疑了一下，接过银票道了谢。

“You can't blame your Fourth Uncle for being suspicious. As the saying goes, coarse cloth has many fibres, and a man of the world has many thoughts. You're young and strong, and you've known A’qi since childhood. If you're constantly visiting, naturally people will gossip.” Hu paused, then added, “It seems to me that your Uncle Yu can't do without her. You should behave with particular generosity.” “How?” “Don't see A’qi again.” “I can do that. I promise you, Mr Hu.” Chen lifted his head with an air of broad-mindedness. “There are plenty of beautiful women in the world!” “Well said!” Hu decided to test him. He took out a fifty-tael banknote. “Take this for your expenses.” Chen hesitated, accepted it, and thanked him.

“再有件事，你替我去办一办，我在沂园等你回话。”他说了老张的地方，要陈世龙去看，搬了家没有？搬在何处？陈世龙答应着走了，胡雪岩也重新回到沂园，把他们谈话的情形，略略说了些给郁四听。很快地，陈世龙有了回话，说老张正在搬家，也说了新址所在，然后问道，“胡先生，今天还有什么事交代我做？”“没有了。你去做你自己的事。明天早晨，我在碧浪春吃茶。”“那么明天一早，我到碧浪春去碰头。”等陈世龙一走，胡雪岩才跟郁四说，给了他五十两银子，“你要他戒赌，他自己也跟我说，不一定要赌。”胡雪岩说，“喜欢赌的人，有钱在身上，手就会痒。你倒不妨派人去打听一下看。”“不错！倒要看看这个小鬼，是不是口不应心？”于是郁四找了个人来，秘密叮嘱了几句，去打听陈世龙的影踪，约辰明天上午回话。

“There's another errand I want you to do. I'll wait at Yiyuan for your report.” Hu gave him Old Zhang's address and told him to find out whether the family had moved and where they were now living. Chen agreed and left. Hu returned to Yiyuan and gave Yu a brief account of their conversation. Chen soon came back to report that Old Zhang was in the process of moving, and supplied the new address. “Is there anything else you want me to do today, Mr Hu?” “Nothing. Go and attend to your own affairs. Tomorrow morning I'll be taking tea at Bilangchun.” “Then I'll meet you there first thing.” Only after Chen had left did Hu tell Yu about the fifty taels. “You told him to stop gambling, and he told me he could do without it. A gambler with money in his pocket gets itchy fingers. You might send someone to find out what he does.” “Quite right! Let's see whether the rascal means what he says.” Yu summoned a man, gave him a few private instructions to investigate Chen's movements, and arranged for a report the next morning.

当夜郁四请了两个南浸镇上的朋友跟胡雪岩见面。这两个人都懂洋文，跟外国商人打过交道，谈起销洋庄的丝生意，认为应以慎重为是，因为上海有“小刀会”活动，市面不太平静。将来夷场上会不会涉及，尚不可知，最好看看风色再说。席间胡雪岩不多开口，只是静静听着。当夜无话，第二天一早到碧浪春，陈世龙已经等在那里了。胡雪岩心想，他光棍一条，有了五十两银子在身上，如果不是送在赌场里，一定会买两身好衣服，新鞋新帽，打扮得十分光鲜，而此刻看他，依旧是昨天那一身衣服，心里便嘀咕：只怕靠不住，口不应心了！不过他口中不作声，只叫他到老张新搬的地方去看一看，可曾搬定？

That evening Yu invited two friends from Nanxun to meet Hu. Both knew a foreign language and had dealt with foreign merchants. Discussing silk exports, they advised caution. The Small Swords Society was active in Shanghai, and business conditions were unsettled. No one yet knew whether the disturbances would spread to the foreign settlements. It would be better to watch developments before acting. Hu said little during the meal and listened quietly. Nothing further happened that night. When he reached Bilangchun early the next morning, Chen was already waiting. Hu reasoned that a young man without dependants, given fifty taels, would surely buy a couple of good outfits and new shoes and a hat, unless he had lost the money at the gambling tables. Yet Chen was wearing the same clothes as yesterday. Perhaps he could not be trusted after all; perhaps his words had not matched his intentions. Hu said nothing about this, however. He merely sent Chen to the Zhang family's new house to see whether they had finished moving.

接着郁四也到了，依旧在当门的“马头桌子”上一坐。同时把胡雪岩请了来，在左首第一位上坐下。少不得又有一阵忙乱，等清静下来，才见郁四昨天派去访查陈世龙行动的那个人，悄悄走了过来。“小和尚真难得！”他根本不知道胡雪岩给了陈世龙一笔钱，而陈世龙应诺戒赌的情形，所以一开口就这样说：“居然不出手。”郁四跟胡雪岩对看了一眼，彼此会意，虽然不曾出手，赌场还是去了。“他昨天身上的钱很多，不晓得什么道理？看了半天，不曾下注，后来就走了。”“是不是到别家赌场去了？”郁四问。“没有，”那人答道，“后来跟几个小弟兄去听书。听完书吃酒，吃到半夜才散，睡在家里的。”“好！”郁四点点头，“辛苦你！你不必跟小和尚说起。”“晓得了。”等他一走，胡雪岩便笑道：“我没有料中。看起来他倒是说话算话。”“还好。”郁四也表示满意：“没有坍我的台。”

Yu arrived soon afterwards and took his usual place at the horse-head table by the entrance, inviting Hu to sit in the first seat on his left. Another flurry of activity followed. Only when things had settled down did the man sent to investigate Chen quietly approach. “Little Monk really is remarkable!” He knew nothing of Hu's gift or Chen's promise to give up gambling, and began, “He actually kept his hands off the stakes.” Yu and Hu exchanged an understanding glance. Even if he had not played, he had still visited the gambling house. “He had plenty of money yesterday. I don't know why, but he watched for ages without placing a bet, then left.” “Did he go to another gambling house?” Yu asked. “No. He went to hear a storyteller with a few young fellows. Afterwards they drank together until midnight, then separated. He slept at home.” “Good.” Yu nodded. “Thank you for your trouble. You needn't mention any of this to Little Monk.” “Understood.” When the man had gone, Hu smiled. “I guessed wrong. It seems he does keep his word.” “Not bad,” Yu agreed with satisfaction. “He hasn't let me lose face.”

“郁四哥，我昨天晚上想了一夜，”胡雪岩说，“销洋庄的生意，还是可以做，大家怕小刀会闹事，不敢做，我们偏偏要做，这就与众不同，变成独门生意了。”“嗯！”郁四想了想，不断颔首，“你的想法，总比别人来得深一层。你再说下去看。““凡事就是起头难，有人领头，大家就跟着来了。做洋庄的那些人，生意不动，就得吃老本，心里何尝不想做？只是肚子小，不敢动，现在我们想个风险不大的办法出来，让大家跟着我们走。”胡雪岩问道，“郁四哥，那时候，你想一想，我们在这一行之中，是什么地位？”“对！”郁四拍案激赏，“人家根深蒂固多少年，我们只要一上手就是头儿、脑儿！这种好事情，天下哪里去找？”“我就是这个意思。‘胆大做王’！再说，别人看来危险，照我看，风险不大。第一，夷场上，人家外国人要保护他自己的人，有大兵船停在黄浦江，小刀会也要看看风色，小刀子到底比不得洋枪洋炮。”

“Brother Yu, I thought about it all night,” Hu said. “We can still do the export business. Everyone else is afraid of disturbances by the Small Swords and daren't trade. If we do precisely what they won't, we'll stand apart and have the business to ourselves.” “Hmm.” Yu considered, nodding repeatedly. “You always think a step further than other people. Go on.” “The beginning is always the difficult part. Once someone leads, others follow. Those export merchants have to live on their capital while trade is idle. Of course they want to do business. They simply lack the nerve. Now suppose we find a way that carries little risk and have them follow us. Think, Brother Yu: what position would that give us in the trade?” “Exactly!” Yu slapped the table in admiration. “Other people have built up their standing over years. We'd become the leaders the moment we began! Where else could you find an opportunity like that?” “That's just what I mean. The bold become kings! Besides, what looks dangerous to others doesn't seem very risky to me. First, the foreigners in the settlements will protect their own people. They have large warships on the Huangpu. The Small Swords must consider the situation too. Little knives are no match for foreign guns and cannon.”

“这话也不错。”郁四看看四周，凑过头去低声说道，“我现在还不大清楚上海的情形，不过照我想，小刀会里，一定有尤老五的弟兄，不妨打听打听看。”“我正就是这个意思。”胡雪岩也低声答道：“我们也不是跟小刀会走到一条线上，他们造反，我们是安分老百姓，打听消息，就是要避开他们，省得走到一条线上。”郁四深深点头：“你们闹事，我们不动，他们不动，我们抢空档把货色运到上海去。”“郁四哥，”胡雪岩笑道，“不是我恭维你，你这两句话，真正是在刀口上。”“好了！”郁四抬起头来，从容说道，“回头我们到阿七那里细谈。”

“That's true.” Yu glanced round, leaned towards him, and lowered his voice. “I don't yet know much about the situation in Shanghai. But I imagine some of You Laowu's brethren must belong to the Small Swords. We could make inquiries.” “Exactly my thought,” Hu answered quietly. “We aren't joining forces with the Small Swords. They're rebels; we're law-abiding citizens. We're seeking information precisely so that we can keep clear of them and avoid becoming entangled.” Yu nodded deeply. “When they make trouble, we stay still. When they're quiet, we seize the interval to send our goods to Shanghai.” “Brother Yu,” Hu said with a smile, “this isn't flattery: those two sentences cut straight to the heart of it.” “Very well.” Yu raised his head and spoke calmly again. “We'll discuss the details at A’qi's later.”

接着便谈到陈世龙。胡雪岩的意思，看他年轻聪明，口齿伶俐，打算止他去学洋文，因为将来销洋庄，须直接跟洋人交往，如果没有一个亲信的人做“ ‘通事”，请教他人传译，也许在语言隔阂之中，为人从中做了手脚，自己还象蒙在鼓里似地，丝毫不知，这关系太重大了。“这个主意很好。”郁四说道，“不过学洋文要精通，不是一年半载的事，眼前得先寻一个人，”“我也是这么想。这个人，第一，要靠得住，第二，要有本事，第三，脾气要好。就叫世龙跟他学。不晓得郁四哥有没有这样的人呢？”“当然有。还不止一个。”“好极了。”胡雪岩很高兴的说，“那就请来谈谈。”“我托人去约。今天晚上或者明天中午碰头好了。”

They next discussed Chen Shilong. Seeing that he was young, intelligent, and quick with words, Hu wanted him to learn a foreign language. Export business would eventually require direct dealings with foreigners. Without a trusted interpreter of his own, he would have to rely on someone else to translate. Behind the language barrier, that person might manipulate matters without Hu ever knowing. Too much depended on it. “An excellent idea,” Yu said. “But mastering a foreign language takes more than a few months or a year. You must first find someone for the immediate work.” “I agree. He must first be trustworthy, second capable, and third good-tempered. Shilong can learn from him. Do you know anyone like that?” “Certainly. More than one.” “Excellent!” Hu was delighted. “Then invite them to talk.” “I'll send someone to arrange it. We can meet this evening or tomorrow at noon.”

这天晚上，胡雪岩在老张的新居吃饭，座间还有陈世龙。陈世龙跟老张也认识。平常“老张、老张”叫惯的，但这时不能不改改口，他是极机警的人，两次到张家，把胡雪岩和老张的关系，看出了一半，等看到了阿珠对胡雪岩，在眉梢眼角，无时不是关切的样子，更料中了十之八九。既然自己叫他为“胡先生”，对老张就不能不客气些。改口叫他“张老板”，阿珠的娘便成了“张太大”，而阿珠是“张小姐”。阿珠还是第一次被人叫做“小姐”，心里有种说不出的喜悦，因而对陈世龙也便另眼相看了。“世龙！”阿珠的娘——张大太则是看在胡雪岩的分上，而且也希望这个年轻力壮的小伙子，能帮丈夫的忙，所以加意笼络：“都是一家人，你不必客气。我这里就当你自己家里一样，你每天来吃饭，有啥衣服换洗，你也拿了来，千万不要见外。”“是啊！”胡雪岩也说，“这不是客气话。”“我懂，我懂。”陈世龙连连点头，“我要客气，做事就不方便了。”于是一面吃，一面谈生意。有陈世龙在座，事情就顺利了。因为老张所讲的情形，他差不多都知道，可以为胡雪岩作补充，象老张所说的那两个懂丝行生意的朋友，陈世龙就指出姓黄的那个比姓王的好，后者曾有欺骗东定，侵吞货款的劣迹，是老张所不知道的。

That evening Hu ate at Old Zhang's new house, with Chen Shilong among the company. Chen knew Old Zhang and had always called him simply “Old Zhang,” but now he had to change his manner. He was exceptionally alert. Two visits to the Zhang home had given him half an understanding of Hu's relationship with the family. Once he saw the constant concern for Hu in A’zhu's every glance and expression, he was almost certain. Since he addressed Hu as his respected teacher, he must show Old Zhang greater courtesy. He began calling him “Mr Zhang,” making A’zhu's mother “Mrs Zhang” and A’zhu “Miss Zhang.” This was the first time anyone had addressed A’zhu as a young lady. It gave her an indescribable pleasure and caused her to regard Chen with greater favour. “Shilong!” Her mother—Mrs Zhang—was attentive for Hu's sake and also hoped that this strong young man could help her husband. She took special pains to win him over. “We're all family. Don't stand on ceremony. Treat this as your own home. Come here for your meals every day, and bring any clothes that need washing. Never feel you're imposing.” “Quite right,” Hu added. “These aren't empty courtesies.” “I understand.” Chen nodded repeatedly. “If I stood on ceremony, it would only make working together harder.” They discussed business as they ate. With Chen there, matters proceeded smoothly. He knew almost everything Old Zhang described and could supply Hu with additional information. For example, of the two friends Old Zhang had mentioned as knowing the silk trade, Chen judged the man named Huang better than the man named Wang. The latter had cheated an employer and misappropriated payment for goods, something Old Zhang had not known.

“世龙！”胡雪岩对在湖州的一切安排，大致都已作了决定，“明夭我们就动手，把阜康分号和丝行开起来。到事情差不多了，你要替我跑一趟松江。”“松江？”陈世龙颇感意外，“我还没有去过。”“没有去过不要紧，去闯一闯。”胡雪岩一件事没有谈定规，又谈第二件，“我再问你一句话，你肯不肯学洋文？”陈世龙更觉意外，“胡先生，”他嗫嚅着说，“我还弄不懂是怎么回事？”“那自然是要你做‘丝通事’。”阿珠接口说道。“连她都懂了！”胡雪岩又对陈世龙说：“将来我不止于丝生意，还有别样生意也想销洋庄。你想，没有一个懂洋文的人，怎么行？”

“Shilong!” Hu had now broadly settled his arrangements in Huzhou. “Tomorrow we'll start getting the Fukang branch and the silk firm open. When things are nearly ready, I want you to go to Songjiang for me.” “Songjiang?” Chen was surprised. “I've never been there.” “That doesn't matter. Go and see something new.” Before finishing one subject, Hu moved to another. “Let me ask you something else: would you be willing to learn a foreign language?” Chen was still more surprised. “Mr Hu,” he faltered, “I don't yet understand what this is about.” “Naturally, he wants you to become an interpreter in the silk trade,” A’zhu put in. “Even she understands!” Hu turned back to Chen. “I don't intend to stop at silk. There are other goods I want to sell abroad. Think: how could I manage without someone who knows a foreign language?”

陈世龙的脑筋也很快，根据他这一句话，立刻就能力自己的将来，画出许多景象，不管丝生意还是别样生意，在上海必是他“坐庄”，凡跟洋人打交道，都是自己一手主持。南浔的那些“丝通事”，他也知道，一个个坐收佣金，附带做些洋货生意，无不大发其财。起居饮食的阔绰，自然不在话下，最令人羡慕的是，有许多新奇精巧的洋货可用。如果自己懂了洋文，当然也有那样的一天。转念到此，他毫不犹豫地答道：“胡先生叫我学洋文，我就学。我一定要把它学好！”“有志气！”胡雪岩把大拇指一翘，很高兴他说：“学一样东西就要这样子，不学拉倒，要学就要精。世龙，你跟我跟长了就知道了，我不喜欢‘三脚猫’的人。”一知半解叫做“三脚猫”，年轻好胜的人，最讨厌这句话，所以陈世龙立刻答道：“胡先生放心，我不会做‘三脚猫’。”

Chen's mind was quick too. From that single remark, he immediately pictured many possibilities for his future. Whether the business concerned silk or other goods, he would surely be the resident representative in Shanghai, personally handling every transaction with foreigners. He knew about the silk interpreters in Nanxun. They all collected commissions and traded in foreign goods on the side, making substantial fortunes. Naturally they lived and ate extravagantly, but what he envied most was their possession of so many ingenious and novel imported articles. If he learnt a foreign language, his turn would come too. Having reached that thought, he answered without hesitation. “If you tell me to learn, Mr Hu, I will. And I'll make certain I learn it properly!” “That's ambition!” Hu raised his thumb approvingly. “That's the way to study anything. Either leave it alone or master it. When you've been with me longer, Shilong, you'll discover that I have no use for ‘three-legged cats.’” A three-legged cat meant someone with only a smattering of knowledge. To a competitive young man, few descriptions were more unwelcome. Chen replied at once, “Don't worry, Mr Hu. I won't be a three-legged cat.”

“我想你也不会。”胡雪岩又说，“我再问你一句话，松江有个尤五，你知道不知道？”漕帮里的大亨，陈世龙如何不知道？不过照规矩，在这方面他不能跟“空子”多说，即使“胡先生”这个“空子”比“门槛里”的还要“落门落槛”也不行，所以他只点点头作为答复。胡雪岩却不管这些，率直问道：“你跟他的辈分怎么排？应该叫他爷叔？”“是的。”“尤五管我叫‘小爷叔，。”胡雪岩有意在陈世龙面前炫耀一番，好叫这个小伙子服帖，“为什么呢？因为他老头子看得起我，尤五敬重他老头子，所以也敬重我。他本人跟我的交情，也就象你郁四叔跟我的交情一样。你说松江没有去过，不要紧，有我的信，你尽管去，没有人敢拿你当’洋盘‘。”“我晓得，我晓得。”陈世龙一叠连声他说，显得异常兴奋。他也真没有想到，胡雪岩这样一个“空子”，有这么大的来头！顿时眼中看出来的“胡先生”，便如丈六金身的四大金刚一般高大了。

“I don't suppose you will.” Hu continued, “Now let me ask you this: do you know You Wu in Songjiang?” How could Chen fail to know a leading figure in the Grain Transport Brotherhood? But its rules forbade him to say much about such matters to an outsider. Even if this outsider, Mr Hu, understood the brotherhood's ways better than many initiates, the rule still applied. He merely nodded. Hu ignored these considerations and asked directly, “How do you stand in relation to him by generation? Would you call him Uncle?” “Yes.” “You Wu calls me ‘Young Uncle.’” Hu deliberately displayed his standing to make the young man more obedient. “Why? Because his old master thinks well of me. You respects his master, so he respects me too. His personal friendship with me is much like your Uncle Yu's. You say you've never been to Songjiang. That doesn't matter. Take my letter and go without concern. Nobody will dare treat you as a gullible newcomer.” “I understand, I understand!” Chen answered repeatedly, visibly excited. He had never imagined that an outsider like Hu possessed such powerful connections. Suddenly Mr Hu seemed to tower before him like one of the great gilded guardian figures in a temple.

“现在我再告诉你，你到了松江，先到一家通裕米行去寻他们的老板，寻到了他自会带你去见尤五。你把我的信当面交给他，千万记住，要当面交给他本人，这封信不能落到外人手里。”很显然的这是封极机密的信，陈世龙深深点着头问：“要不要等回信？”“当然要。回信也是紧要的，千万不能失落。”胡雪岩又说，“或许他不会写回信，只是带回来口信，他跟你说什么，你都记住，说什么你记住什么，不要多问！”“也不要跟旁人说。”陈世龙这样接了一句。“对！”胡雪岩放心了，“你懂我的道理了。”

“Now listen. When you reach Songjiang, go first to a rice firm called Tongyu and find its proprietor. He'll take you to You Wu. Give him my letter in person. Remember: hand it directly to him yourself. It must not fall into anyone else's hands.” Clearly it was a highly confidential letter. Chen nodded solemnly. “Should I wait for a reply?” “Of course. The reply will be important too. You mustn't lose it. Perhaps he won't write, though,” Hu added. “He may send a spoken message. Remember everything he says—whatever he says—and don't ask unnecessary questions.” “And don't tell anyone else,” Chen supplied. “Exactly!” Hu was reassured. “You understand what I mean.”

陈世龙这里倒交代清楚了，但写这封信却成了难题，胡雪岩的文墨不甚高明，而这封信又要写得含蓄，表面没有破绽，暗中看得明白，他没有这一份本事，只好去请教郁四。郁四是衙门里的人，对于“一字入公门，九牛拔不转”这句话，特持警惕，认为这样的事，不宜在信中明言，万一中途失落了这封信，会惹出极大的麻烦。“你我都无所谓，说句老实话，上上下下都是人，总可以洗刷干净。”郁四很诚恳的说，“不过，你无论如何也要替王大老爷想想，事情弄到他头上，就很讨厌了！”这个警告，胡雪岩十分重视，翻然变计，决定让陈世龙当面跟尤五去谈。

The instructions to Chen were now clear enough, but writing the letter proved difficult. Hu's literary abilities were limited. This letter had to be discreet: nothing incriminating on its face, yet its hidden meaning perfectly clear. He lacked the skill, so he consulted Yu. As a man of the yamen, Yu was particularly mindful of the saying, “Once a word enters an official office, nine oxen cannot drag it back.” He thought a matter like this should not be stated openly in a letter. If the letter were lost on the way, it could cause enormous trouble. “You and I would be all right. Frankly, we have friends at every level and could always clear ourselves,” Yu said earnestly. “But you must think of Mr Wang. If he were implicated, it would be extremely awkward.” Hu took the warning very seriously. He immediately changed his plan and decided that Chen should speak to You Wu in person instead.

附：文本说明与本批精读方向（译者说明，非小说原文）

本批为第九章下半部分，从郁四替胡雪岩安排轿子前往张家，译至胡雪岩决定让陈世龙当面向尤五传达消息。正文依场景、对话目的及推理层次分段；商业、用人、搬家、查验守信和传话安排均依原文展开。

文本处理

阿珠在前文已明确为未成年人。以下改写对应原稿第7439—7457、7459—7493行。本批从进入她的房间至闩门的一处性暗示，以及其后包含性接触、解衣的片段，采用非露骨改写。保留房间陈设、闩门、她的拒绝、绣花、金链礼物、鸳鸯花样和母亲归来的情节；不复现性接触及衣物暴露细节。对应中文与英文使用相互匹配的安全改写。这两处不应视作逐字完整翻译，其余非性正文没有以概述代替翻译。

除上述明确说明的改写外，中文保留输入文件用字。英文将明显识别错误按上下文理解，例如“两问”为两间、“剖她了”为剖好了、“搬定”在搬家语境中照原意处理、“两无”为两天、“跟我联手”对应“眼我联手”、“南浸”为南浔、“东定”为东家、“止他去学”为让他去学、“张太大／张大太”为张太太。“你自然肯的”在谈小和尚是否愿赴杭州的语境中按“他自然肯的”理解。

“老头子”在尤五所处的帮会辈分语境中指其师父，与前文魏老爷子一段衔接，不译成尤五的亲生父亲。“小和尚”是陈世龙的绰号；本批没有给出他的具体年龄，不据绰号将他当成儿童，也不将他写成真正的僧人。

谈到“洋文”时，原文未明确是哪一种外语，译为 a foreign language，没有直接替换为 English。胡雪岩对上海局势、外国兵船及运输风险的推断作为人物的判断译出，不把这些判断改写成已经证实的结果。

精读方向

阅读：陈世龙衣着未变，并不能证明他赌输了钱。比较胡雪岩先前的推测、探访者的事实报告和胡雪岩随后承认判断有误，区分推断、证据和结论。阿七相信郁四不想把小和尚送走，则来自胡雪岩有意安排的说法，不应直接当作真实动机。

完形：关注 even if、unless、instead、therefore、as long as 所连接的条件和结果。“去过赌场”与“下注赌博”是不同事实；“有吸引力”与“打算采取行动”也被胡雪岩明确区分。先辨析逻辑层次，再判断转折或递进。

段落排序：招用陈世龙的段落可以按“征求意愿—了解经历—澄清关系—交付小事—观察行为—决定培养”排列。出口贸易部分则由外部风险引出逆向判断，再落实为获取消息、选择运输时机及培养通事。注意问题怎样在下一段转为具体办法。

翻译：选“胡老板／胡先生”“张老板／张太太／张小姐”两处，观察称谓怎样表示身份变化；再选书信改口信一段，先拆出“信息必须保密”“胡雪岩写作能力有限”“可能牵连王有龄”三个不同原因，避免把最终改变计划归因于单一原因。

写作：可练习“判断应根据证据修正”和“培养人才需要机会、要求与检验”两个主题。借用具体情节解释观点，再换成学习或团队协作中的例子。可积累 keep one's word、stand on ceremony、act against one’s will、speak without restraint、take a warning seriously 等表达；写作时要明确它们适用的语境，不把它们机械堆入每篇文章。
