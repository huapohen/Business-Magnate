第十一章｜CHAPTER ELEVEN

王有龄的船到杭州，仍旧泊在万安桥。来时风光，与去时又不大相同。去时上任，仪制未备，不过两号官船，数面旗牌，这一次回省，共有五只大号官船，隶役侍应，旗帜鲜明。未到码头，仁和、钱塘两县已派了差役在岸上照应，驱散闲人，静等泊岸，坐上大轿，径回公馆。胡雪岩却不忙回家，一乘小轿直接来到阜康，他事先并无消息，所以这一到，刘庆生颇感意外。胡雪岩原是故意如此，叫他猝不及防，才好看出刘庆生一手经理之下的阜康，是怎么个样子。因此，他一面谈路上和湖州的情形，一面很自然地把视线扫来扫去，店堂里的情形，大致都看清楚了，伙计接待顾客，也还客气，兑换银钱的生意，也还不少，所以对刘庆生觉得满意。

Wang Youling’s boats reached Hangzhou and moored once more at Wan’an Bridge. His return was rather more splendid than his departure had been. When he left to take up his appointment, his official establishment was not yet complete: he had only two government boats and a few flags and insignia. Now he returned to the provincial capital with five large official boats, attendants and servants in attendance, and bright banners flying. Before he reached the landing, runners sent by the counties of Renhe and Qiantang were already on shore, clearing away onlookers and waiting to receive him. He stepped into a large sedan chair and went directly to his residence. Hu Xueyan, however, was in no hurry to go home. A small sedan carried him straight to Fukang. He had sent no advance notice, so Liu Qingsheng was rather surprised to see him. That was deliberate: by arriving unexpectedly, Hu could see how the bank was being run under Liu’s sole management. As he talked about the journey and affairs in Huzhou, he let his gaze move naturally about the shop. He soon had a fair impression of everything. The assistants were courteous to customers, and the exchange business in silver and copper cash was reasonably brisk. He was pleased with Liu.

“麟藩台的两万银子，已经还了五千……”刘庆生把这些日子以来的业务情形，作了个简略的报告。然后请胡雪岩看帐。“不必看了。”胡雪岩问道：“帐上应该结存的现银有多少？”“总帐在这里，”刘庆生翻看帐簿，说结存的现银，包括立刻可以兑现的票子，一共七万五千多银子。“三天以内要付出去的有多少？”“三万不到。”“明天呢？”胡雪岩又问。“明天没有要付的。”“那好！”胡雪岩说，“我提七万银子，只要用一天好了。”说着拿笔写了一张提银七万两的条子，递了过去。他这是一个试探，要看看刘庆生的帐目与结存是不是相符？如果叫他拿库存出来看，显得对人不相信，所以玩了这么一记小小的花样。等刘庆生毫不迟疑地开了保险箱，点齐七万两的客票送到他手里，他又说了：“今天用出去，明天就可以收回来。你放心，不会耽误后天的用途。说不定用不到七万，我是多备些。“

“Of the twenty thousand taels lent to Treasurer Lin, five thousand have already been repaid...” Liu gave a brief report of business during Hu’s absence, then invited him to examine the accounts. “There is no need,” Hu replied. “What balance of ready money should the books show?” “Here is the general ledger.” Liu consulted it. Including notes that could be cashed immediately, the available balance was a little over seventy-five thousand taels. “How much must be paid out within the next three days?” “Less than thirty thousand.” “And tomorrow?” “Nothing is due tomorrow.” “Good. I shall take seventy thousand taels. I need it for only one day.” Hu took up a brush, wrote an order for the withdrawal, and handed it over. This was a test of whether Liu’s actual holdings matched his accounts. To demand to see the reserves would suggest distrust, so Hu had devised this little stratagem. Without the slightest hesitation, Liu opened the strongbox, counted out seventy thousand taels in customers’ bank drafts, and placed them in his hands. “I shall use these today and get the money back tomorrow,” Hu said. “Do not worry; it will not interfere with the payments due the day after. I may not need all seventy thousand. I am merely allowing a margin.”

就这么片刻的工夫，他已经神不知、鬼不觉地把刘庆生的操守和才干，考察了一番。回家拜见了老母，正在跟妻子谈此行的成就，王有龄派人来请，说有要紧事商量，请他即刻到王家见面。到得王家，已经晚上九点钟了。王有龄正在书房里踱方步，一见胡雪岩就皱着眉说：“搞了件意想不到的差使，要到新城去一趟。”新城又称新登，是杭州府属的一县，在富阳与桐庐之间，那一条富春江以严子陵的钓台得名，风光明媚，是骚人墨客歌咏留连的胜区，但新城却是个小小的山城。湖州府署理知府，跑到那儿去干什么？“莫非奉委审案子？”胡雪岩问。“案子倒是有件案子，不是去审问。”王有龄答道：“新城有个和尚，聚众抗粮，黄抚台要我带兵去剿办。”

In those few moments, without anyone suspecting it, Hu had tested both Liu’s integrity and his ability. He went home to pay his respects to his mother and was telling his wife of the results of his journey when a messenger arrived from Wang. There was an urgent matter to discuss; would he come at once? It was already nine in the evening when Hu reached Wang’s house. Wang was pacing his study. At the sight of him, he frowned. “I have been given a quite unexpected assignment. I must go to Xincheng.” Xincheng, also known as Xindeng, was a county under Hangzhou Prefecture, between Fuyang and Tonglu. That stretch of the Fuchun River, famous for Yan Ziling’s fishing terrace, was a place of beautiful scenery where poets and writers loved to linger. Xincheng itself, however, was only a small mountain town. What could the acting prefect of Huzhou have to do there? “Have you been commissioned to try a case?” Hu asked. “There is a case, but I am not being sent to hear it. A monk in Xincheng has gathered people to resist the grain tax. Governor Huang wants me to take troops and suppress them.”

听得这话，胡雪岩大吃一惊，“这不是当耍的事。”他问，“雪公，你带过兵没有？”“这倒不关紧要，我从前随老太爷在云南任上，带亲兵抓过作乱的苗子。不过这情形是不同的，听说新城的民风强悍得很。“凡是山城的百姓，总以强悍的居多。新城这地方，尤其与众不同，那里在五代钱武肃王的时候，出过一个名人，叫做罗隐，在两浙和江西，福建的民间，“罗隐秀才”的名气甚大，据说出语成谶，言必有中，而他本人亦多奇行异事。新城的民风，继承了他的那股傲岸倔强之气，所以很不容易对付。

Hu was greatly alarmed. “This is no matter to trifle with. Mr Xuexuan, have you ever commanded troops?” “That is not the main difficulty. When I was with my father during his appointment in Yunnan, I led his bodyguard to arrest Miao tribesmen who had rebelled. But this is a different situation. I hear that the people of Xincheng are exceptionally fierce.” The inhabitants of mountain towns generally tended to be tough and combative, and Xincheng was particularly so. During the Five Dynasties, in the time of King Qian Wusu, it had produced a celebrated man named Luo Yin. Among ordinary people in the two Zhe regions, Jiangxi, and Fujian, “Scholar Luo Yin” enjoyed great fame. His words were said to foretell events without fail, and many strange deeds and stories were associated with him. The people of Xincheng had inherited something of his proud, stubborn spirit and were therefore far from easy to handle.

“是啊！”胡雪岩答道：“这很麻烦。和尚聚众抗粮，可知是个不安分的人。如果带了兵去，说不定激成民变。雪公，你要慎重。”“我所怕的正就是这一点。再说，一带兵去，那情形……”王有龄大摇其头，“越发糟糕！”这话胡雪岩懂。绿营兵丁，已到了不可救药的地步，真正是“兵不如匪”，一带队下去，地方老百姓行就遭殃。想到这一天，胡雪岩觉得事有可为。“雪公！随便什么地方，总有明事理的人。照我看。兵以不动为妙，你不妨单枪匹马，到新城找着地方上有声望的绅士，把利害关系说明白。此事自然能够化解。”“话是不错。”王有龄放低了声音说，“为难的是，大事化小，小事化无还不够。上头的意思是，现在各地风声都很紧，怕刁民学样捣乱，非要严办祸首不可。”“不管是严是宽，那是第二步的事！”“对！”王有龄一下领悟了，不管怎么样，要眼前先把局势平服了下来，才能谈得到第二步。他想了想，站起身来说，“我要去拜个客，先作一番部署。”

“Yes,” Hu said. “This is troublesome. A monk who gathers a crowd to resist taxes is plainly a restless fellow. If you take troops there, you may provoke a popular uprising. You must be careful.” “That is precisely what I fear. Besides, once soldiers are sent in...” Wang shook his head vigorously. “It will be worse still.” Hu understood. The Green Standard troops had deteriorated beyond remedy; the soldiers were truly worse than bandits. Wherever a detachment was sent, the local people suffered. Thinking of this, Hu saw a possible way forward. “Mr Xuexuan, wherever you go, there are always reasonable people. In my opinion, it would be best to keep the troops where they are. You might go to Xincheng yourself, find respected local gentlemen, and explain the consequences clearly. The trouble should then be capable of settlement.” “That is sound advice.” Wang lowered his voice. “The difficulty is that reducing a large disturbance to a small one, and then making it disappear altogether, will not be enough. The authorities fear that, with unrest everywhere, other troublemakers will imitate this example. They insist on severe punishment for the ringleaders.” “Whether to be severe or lenient is a matter for the second stage.” “Exactly!” Wang understood at once. Whatever else was to be done, the immediate situation must first be brought under control. Only then could they consider the next step. He thought for a moment and rose. “I must pay someone a visit and make a preliminary arrangement.”

“拜哪个？”“魁参将。他原来驻防嘉兴，现在调到省城。黄抚台派他带兵跟我到新城，我得跟他商量一下。”“雪公，你预备怎么跟他说？”“我把以安抚为先的宗旨告诉他，请他听我的招呼出队，不能胡来。”“叫他不出队，怕办不到。”胡雪岩说，“绿营兵一听见这种差使，都当发财的机会到了。哪里肯听你的话？”“那么照你说，该怎么办呢？”“总要许他点好处。”胡雪岩说，“现在不是求他出队，是求他不要出队。”“万一安抚不下来，还是要靠他。”王有龄点点头，下了个转语：“不过，你的话确是‘一针见血’，我先许了他的好处，那就收发由心，都听我的指挥了。”

“Whom are you going to see?” “Commander Kui. He was formerly stationed at Jiaxing and has now been transferred to the provincial capital. Governor Huang has ordered him to lead the troops accompanying me to Xincheng. I must discuss matters with him.” “What do you intend to say?” “I shall explain that conciliation comes first and ask him to wait for my instructions before marching. He must not act recklessly.” “It may be difficult to keep him from marching,” Hu said. “Whenever Green Standard soldiers hear of such an assignment, they think their chance to make money has come. Why should they listen to you?” “Then what do you suggest?” “You must offer him some advantage. What you need from him now is not that he march, but that he refrain from marching.” “If conciliation fails, I shall still have to rely on him.” Wang nodded, then reconsidered. “Still, you have put your finger on the matter. If I promise him a reward first, I can then move him forward or hold him back as I choose. He will obey my direction.”

当夜王有龄去拜访了魁参将，答应为他在黄抚台那里请饷，将来事情平定以后，“保案”中一定把他列为首功。但希望他听自己的话，实在是要他听自己的指挥。魁参将见王有龄很知趣，很爽快地答应照办。由于王有龄遭遇了这么一件意外的差使，把他原来的计划都打乱了，该办的事无法分身，只有胡雪岩帮他的忙。首先是藩司衙门的公事要紧，胡雪岩用他从阜康取来的客票，解入藩库，把湖州带来，由郁四调来的五万银票，连同多下的两万，一起还了给刘庄生。此外还有许多王有龄个人的应酬，何处该送礼，何处该送钱，胡雪岩找着刘庆生帮忙，两个人整整奔走了一天，算是都办妥了。

That night Wang visited Commander Kui. He promised to apply to Governor Huang for funds for the troops and, once the affair was settled, to place Kui first on the list recommended for rewards. In return, he hoped Kui would listen to him—which really meant submit to his command. Seeing that Wang understood how things should be managed, Kui agreed readily. This unexpected assignment overturned all Wang’s original plans. He could not divide himself among his other duties and had to rely on Hu’s help. Business with the provincial treasurer’s yamen came first. Hu used fifty thousand taels of the customers’ drafts withdrawn from Fukang to make the payment into the provincial treasury. He then returned to Liu Qingsheng the fifty thousand taels in banknotes brought from Huzhou and supplied by Yu Si, together with the twenty thousand left from his withdrawal. There were also many personal obligations of Wang’s to attend to: gifts to be delivered here, money to be sent there. Hu enlisted Liu’s help, and the two spent an entire day rushing about before everything was finally arranged.

“这就该忙我自己的事了。”胡雪岩把经手的事项，一一向王有龄交代过后，这样对他说，“我赤手空拳做出来的市面，现在都该要有个着落。命脉都在这几船丝上面，一点大意不得。”王有龄哑然。他此刻到新城，也等于赤手空拳，至少要有个心腹在身边，遇到疑难危急的时候，也有个人可以商量。但胡雪岩既已做了这样的表示，而且也知道这一次的丝生意，对他的关系极大，所以原想留他帮忙的话，这时候就无论如何说不出口了。他的失望无奈的神色，胡雪岩自然看得出来。心里在想：这真叫爱莫能助！第一，实在抽不出空，第二，新城地方不熟，第三，带兵出队，动刀动枪的事，也真有点“吓势势”，还是不必多事为妙。因为如此，他就不去打听这件事了。管自己跟张胖子和刘庆生去碰头，把他到上海这个把月中，需要料理或者联络的事，都作了妥帖的安排。三天工夫过去，丝船到了杭州，陈世龙陪着老张到阜康来报到。

“Now I must attend to my own affairs,” Hu said after reporting each item to Wang. “I have built up this business from nothing, and it must now begin to produce results. Everything depends on those boatloads of silk. I cannot afford the slightest carelessness.” Wang fell silent. He, too, would be going to Xincheng with little to rely on. At the very least, he wanted a trusted man beside him, someone with whom he could discuss a difficulty or an emergency. But Hu had made his position clear, and Wang knew how much this silk venture meant to him. He could no longer bring himself to ask Hu to stay and help. Hu naturally saw his disappointment and helplessness. He thought, with genuine regret, that there was nothing he could do. First, he truly had no time to spare. Second, he knew nothing of Xincheng. Third, leading troops and dealing with swords and guns was a distinctly alarming business. Better not to involve himself unnecessarily. He therefore asked no more about the matter. Instead, he met Fat Zhang and Liu and made careful arrangements for everything that would need attention or communication during his month or so in Shanghai. Three days passed. The silk boats reached Hangzhou, and Chen Shilong accompanied Old Zhang to Fukang to report their arrival.

问起路上的情形，陈世龙说一路都很顺利，不过听到许多消息，各地聚众抗粮的纠纷，层出不穷，谣言极盛，都非好兆。因此，他劝胡雪岩当夜就下船，第二天一早动身，早早赶到松江地界，有尤五“保镖”就可以放心了。“世龙兄这话很实在。胡先生早到早好。今天晚上我做个小东，给胡先生送行。”刘庆生又面邀老张和陈世龙说：“也是替你们两位送行。”“既如此，你就再多请一位‘堂客’。”“是，是。”刘庆生知道胡雪岩指的是阿珠，“今天夜里的月亮还很好，我请大家到西湖上去逛逛。”“一天到晚坐船也坐厌了。”胡雪岩笑道，“还是去逛城隍山的好。”“就是城隍山！主从客便。”刘庆生问老张：“令媛在船上？”“是的，我去接她。”“何必你自己去？”胡雪岩说，“叫世龙走一趟，先接她到这里来再说。”

Asked about the journey, Chen said that everything had gone smoothly. They had, however, heard a great deal of news. Disturbances involving organized resistance to grain taxes were breaking out everywhere, and rumours were rife. None of it was a good sign. He urged Hu to board that very night and set off early next morning, reaching the Songjiang district as soon as possible. With You Laowu there to protect them, they could feel secure. “Brother Shilong’s advice is sound,” Liu said. “The sooner Mr Hu arrives, the better. Let me give a modest farewell dinner tonight.” He turned to Zhang and Chen. “And for you two as well.” “In that case, invite one more guest—a lady.” “Certainly, certainly.” Liu knew that Hu meant A’zhu. “The moon will still be lovely tonight. I shall take everyone for an outing on West Lake.” “One grows tired of boats after sitting in them all day,” Hu said with a smile. “Let us go to City God Hill instead.” “City God Hill it shall be. The host follows his guests’ wishes.” Liu asked Zhang, “Is your daughter on the boat?” “Yes. I shall fetch her.” “Why go yourself?” Hu said. “Let Shilong make the trip and bring her here first.”

听得这话，陈世龙连声答应着，站起来就走。等了有个把时辰，两乘小轿，抬到门前，阿珠走下轿来，只见她破例着条绸裙子，但盈尺莲船，露在裙幅外面。走起路来，裙幅摆动得很厉害，别人还不曾摇头，她自己先不好意思地笑了：“这条断命的裙子，我真正着不惯！”“那你何必自己跟自己过不去，找罪来受？”胡雪岩这样笑着问。“喏！都是他。”他是指陈世龙。阿珠一面说，一面拿手指着，眼风自然而然地瞟了过去。话中虽带着埋怨，脸色和声音却并无责怪之意，倒象是陈世龙怎么说，她就该怎么听似地。这微妙的神情，老张看不出来，刘庆生更是如蒙在鼓里，甚至连阿珠自己都没有觉察有什么异样，但胡雪岩心里明白，向陈世龙笑了一下，没有再说下去。“我们商量商量，到哪里去吃饭？”刘庆生还把阿珠当做胡雪岩的心上人，特地征询她的意见：“ ‘皇饭儿’好不好？”最好的一家本地馆子，就在城隍山脚下，吃完逛山，正好顺路，自然一致同意。于是刘庆生作东，吃了一顿丰盛的晚饭，上城隍山去品茗纳凉。

Chen agreed at once and hurried off. About two hours later, two small sedan chairs arrived outside. A’zhu stepped down, wearing, unusually, a silk skirt. Her large, unbound feet showed beneath its hem, and the skirt swayed awkwardly as she walked. Before anyone else could shake his head, she laughed in embarrassment. “This wretched skirt! I simply cannot get used to it.” “Then why make life difficult for yourself?” Hu asked with a smile. “Why suffer needlessly?” “There! It is all his doing.” She meant Chen Shilong, pointing at him and naturally glancing in his direction. Though her words sounded like a complaint, neither her voice nor her expression conveyed blame. It was almost as though whatever Chen said was something she ought to obey. Old Zhang did not notice this subtle change; Liu was entirely in the dark; even A’zhu herself was unaware of anything unusual. Hu understood, however. He smiled at Chen and said no more. “Let us decide where to eat,” Liu said. Still regarding A’zhu as Hu’s sweetheart, he made a point of consulting her. “Would Huangfan’er do?” This was the finest restaurant serving local dishes, conveniently situated at the foot of City God Hill. They could eat and then go up the hill, so everyone agreed. Liu gave them a generous dinner, after which they climbed the hill to drink tea and enjoy the cool air.

这夜月明如昼，游客甚多，树下纳凉，胡雪岩跟老张和刘庆生在谈近来的市面，阿珠和陈世龙便小声闲话。杭州的一切，他不如她熟，所以尽是她的话，指点着山下的万家灯火，为他介绍杭州的风物。到得二更将近，老张打个哈欠说：“回去吧！明天一早就要动身。”阿珠有些恋恋不舍，但终于还是站了起来。陈世龙却是一言不发，抢先下山。胡雪岩心里奇怪，不知道他去干什么？这个疑团直到下山才打破，原来他是雇轿子去了。“只得两顶轿子。”陈世龙说：“胡先生坐一顶。”还有一顶呢？不用说，当然是阿珠坐。胡雪岩心想，自己想是沾了她的光，其实可以不必，我家甚近，不妨安步当车。阿珠父女回船的路相当远，不如让他们坐了去。“我要托世龙帮我收拾行李，我们先走，轿子你们坐了去。”胡雪岩又对刘庆生拱拱手说：“你也请回去吧！”“好的。明天一早我来送行。”

The moon made the night almost as bright as day, and many people were out enjoying themselves. Under the trees, Hu discussed the recent state of business with Old Zhang and Liu, while A’zhu and Chen talked quietly together. She knew Hangzhou far better than he did, so she did most of the talking, pointing towards the myriad lights below and describing the city’s sights and customs. As the second watch approached, Zhang yawned. “Time to go back. We must leave early tomorrow.” A’zhu was reluctant, but finally rose. Chen said nothing and hurried down the hill ahead of them. Hu wondered what he was doing; the puzzle was solved only when they reached the bottom. He had gone to hire sedan chairs. “There were only two,” Chen said. “Mr Hu can take one.” The other was naturally for A’zhu. Hu thought that he was benefiting from the young man’s attentions to her. There was really no need: his own house was close by, and he could comfortably walk. A’zhu and her father had much farther to go to reach their boat; better to let them take the chairs. “I want Shilong to help me with my luggage, so we shall go on ahead. You two take the chairs.” He clasped his hands to Liu. “You should go home too.” “Very well. I shall come early tomorrow to see you off.”

于是五个人分做三路。胡雪岩把陈世龙带到家。胡家大非昔比了，胡太太很能干，在丈夫到湖州去的一个月中，收拾得门庭焕然，还用了一个老妈子，一个打杂的男工，这时还都在等候“老爷”回家。“行李都收拾好了。”打杂的男工阿福，向“老爷”交代：“约了两个挑夫在那里，行李是不是今天晚上就发下船，还是明天一早挑了去。”胡雪岩觉得阿福很会办事，十分满意，但他还未接口，陈世龙就先说了：“今天晚上下船！回头我带了挑伕去，也省得你走一趟。这样说停当，阿福立刻去找挑伕，趁这片刻闲空，胡雪岩问道：“一路上，阿珠怎么样？”这话让陈世龙很难回答，虽已取得默契，却不便自道如何向阿珠献殷勤？想了想答道：“我都照胡先生的话做。”“好！”胡雪岩说，“你就照这样子做好了。不过生意上也要当心。”

The five separated into three parties. Hu took Chen home. The household was very different from what it had once been. His wife was a capable woman. During the month he had spent in Huzhou, she had transformed the appearance of the place and hired an older maid and a man to do odd jobs. Both were still waiting for “the master” to return. “Your luggage is packed,” the manservant, A’fu, reported. “I have arranged for two porters. Should they take it down to the boat tonight or first thing tomorrow?” Hu was very pleased with his efficiency. Before he could answer, however, Chen spoke. “Tonight. I shall go with the porters presently and save you the journey.” With that settled, A’fu went to fetch the men. Taking advantage of the brief interval, Hu asked, “How was A’zhu on the journey?” Chen found this difficult to answer. They had an understanding, but he could hardly describe his attentions to her. After a moment’s thought, he said, “I did everything as you instructed.” “Good. Carry on in that way. But you must pay attention to the business as well.”

这是警告他，不要陷溺在阿珠的巧笑娇语之中。这言外之意，陈世龙当然懂，到底年纪还轻，脸有些红了。但此刻不能装糊涂，事实上他也一直在找这样一个可以表示忠心的机会，所以用极诚恳坦率的声音答道：“胡先生，你尽管请放心，江湖上我虽少跑，江湖义气总晓得的，胡先生这样子待我，我拆烂污对不起胡先生，将来在外面还要混不要混？”“对！”胡雪岩颇为嘉许，“你能看到这一点，就见得你脑子清楚。我劝你在生意上巴结，不光是为我，是为你自己。你最多拆我两次烂污，第一次我原谅你，第二次对不起，要请你卷铺盖了，如果烂污拆碍太过，连我都收不了场，那时候该杀该剐，也是你去。不过你要晓得，也有人连一次烂污都不准人拆的，只要有这么一次，你就吃不开了。”他这番话，等于定了个规约，让陈世龙清清楚楚地明白了他对待手下的态度。不过陈世龙，绝没有半点因为可容许拆一次烂污而有恃无恐的心思，相反地，这时候暗暗下了决心，在生意上非要规规矩矩地做个样子来给胡雪岩看不可。“胡先生如果没有别的吩咐，我就走了。”他又问：“明天一早，要不要来接？”“不必，不必！我自己会去的。”

This was a warning not to lose himself in A’zhu’s smiles and conversation. Chen understood the implication. He was still young and blushed slightly, but this was no time to pretend ignorance. In fact, he had been looking for just such an opportunity to express his loyalty. He answered with complete sincerity and openness. “You may be entirely at ease, Mr Hu. I have not travelled much in the world, but I understand loyalty between men. After the way you have treated me, if I let you down by making a mess of things, how could I ever show my face again?” “Exactly.” Hu was approving. “The fact that you can see that shows you have a clear head. I urge you to work hard at the business for your own sake, not merely for mine. You could let me down at most twice. The first time I would forgive you; the second, I am sorry, you would have to pack your bedding and go. If you made such a mess that even I could not put it right, whatever punishment followed would be yours to bear. But remember that some people will not forgive even one failure. Let them down once, and you are finished.” This amounted to a set of rules, making clear how Hu treated those who worked for him. Far from feeling safe because one failure might be forgiven, Chen privately resolved to show Hu that he could conduct himself properly in business. “If there are no further instructions, I shall leave. Shall I come to fetch you early tomorrow?” “No, no. I shall find my own way.”

等陈世龙一走，胡雪岩也就睡了。临别前夕，夫妇俩自然有许多话要说，谈到半夜，人是倦了，却不能安心入梦，心绪零乱，一直在想王有龄，担心他到新城，生命有没有危险，公事会不会顺利？“怎么这时候才来？太阳都好高了！”阿珠一见胡雪岩上船，就这样埋怨地问。“一夜没有睡着。”胡雪岩答道：“我在担心王大老爷。”“王大老爷怎么样？”“这时候没有工夫谈。开了船再说。”解缆开船，也得要些工夫，胡雪岩一个人坐在船舱里喝茶，懒得开口，自从与王有龄重逢以来，他的情绪从没有象这样恶劣过。“到底啥事情？”阿珠问道：“这样子愁眉不展，害得大家都不开心。”听这话胡雪岩感到歉然，心情便越发沉重，“嗐！”他突然站起身来，“我今天不走了！王大老爷的公事有麻烦，我走了对不起朋友。阿珠，你叫他们停船。”

Once Chen had gone, Hu went to bed. On the eve of his departure, husband and wife naturally had much to say. They talked until midnight. By then he was tired, but could not settle to sleep. His thoughts were confused and kept returning to Wang: would his life be in danger at Xincheng? Would the assignment go smoothly? “Why are you so late? The sun is already high!” A’zhu complained as soon as he stepped aboard the next morning. “I did not sleep all night. I have been worrying about Prefect Wang.” “What has happened to him?” “There is no time to explain now. I shall tell you once we are under way.” Even casting off and getting the boat moving took some time. Hu sat alone in the cabin, drinking tea and reluctant to speak. Since his reunion with Wang, his spirits had never been so low. “What is the matter?” A’zhu asked. “You look so miserable that you are making everyone else unhappy.” Hu felt sorry at this, which only weighed on him further. “Ah!” He suddenly stood up. “I am not leaving today. Prefect Wang has a difficult official assignment. If I go, I shall be failing a friend. A’zhu, tell them to stop the boat.”

等船一停，老张和陈世龙不约而同的搭了跳板，都来到胡雪岩舱里，查问原因。这时候他的心情轻松了，把王有龄奉令赴新城办案的经过说了一遍，表示非跟他在一起不可。“我事情一办好，就赶了上来，行李也不必卸了。”“如果事情没有办完，赶不到呢？”陈世龙针对这个疑问作了建议：“我们在松江等你，有尤五照应，船上的货色决不会少。”胡雪岩觉得这办法十分妥贴，欣然同意，随即单身上岸，雇了乘小轿，直接来到王家。

Once the boat had stopped, Old Zhang and Chen, without consulting each other, crossed the gangplank and came into Hu’s cabin to ask why. Hu felt lighter now. He explained how Wang had been ordered to deal with the Xincheng affair and said that he must be at his side. “As soon as the matter is settled, I shall catch up with you. There is no need to unload my luggage.” “What if it is not settled in time and you cannot catch us?” Chen proposed an answer to his own question. “We shall wait for you at Songjiang. With You Laowu looking after us, none of the cargo will go missing.” Hu thought this an excellent arrangement and readily agreed. He went ashore alone, hired a small sedan chair, and headed straight to Wang’s house.

王有龄家高朋满座，个个都穿着官服，看样子都是“州县班子”，自然是“听鼓辕门”的候补知县。胡雪岩自己虽也是捐班的“大老爷”，但从未穿过补褂、戴过大帽，与这班官儿们见面，先得一个个请教了，才好定称呼，麻烦甚大，所以踏人院子，不进大厅，由廊下绕列厅房一间小客厅去休息等候。等听差的捧了茶来，他悄悄问道：“你家老爷在谈什么？”“还不是新城的事！听说那和尚厉害得很，把新城的县官都杀掉了。为此，我们太太愁得觉都睡不着。”胡雪岩大吃一惊！这一来，事情越闹越大，必不能善罢干休，王有龄真是“湿手捏了干燥面”，怕一时料理不清楚了。于是他侧耳静听着，不久就弄清楚了，那些候补州县，奉了抚台的委札，到王有龄这里来听候差委，此刻他正召集他们在会议，商量处理的办法。

Wang’s house was full of distinguished visitors, all wearing official dress. They appeared to be the usual pool of expectant magistrates—men awaiting county appointments and answering the summons at the governor’s gates. Although Hu himself had purchased official rank and was therefore also entitled to be addressed as an official, he had never worn an embroidered rank badge or an official hat. To meet these men would mean asking each his name and position before deciding how to address him, an exceedingly troublesome business. On entering the courtyard, he therefore bypassed the main hall and followed the corridor to a small side parlour, where he could rest and wait. When an attendant brought tea, he quietly asked, “What is your master discussing?” “The Xincheng affair, what else? They say that monk is a terrible fellow. He has even killed the county magistrate. Our mistress is so worried that she cannot sleep.” Hu was shocked. The trouble was growing far more serious; it could no longer end with a simple settlement. Wang had indeed grasped dry flour with wet hands and might not get clear of the mess for some time. Hu listened carefully. Before long he understood that these officials awaiting appointments had been sent by the governor to serve under Wang’s direction. Wang had called them together to discuss how to proceed.

你一言，他一言，聚讼纷坛了半天，只听有个人说道：“现在是抗粮事小，戕宫事大，首要各犯，朝廷决不会放松。我看，第一步，要派兵分守要隘，第二步，才谈得到是剿、是抚，还是剿抚兼施？”胡雪岩暗暗点头，只有这个人说话还有条理，外面的王有龄大概也是这样的想法，只听他说：“高明之至。我还要请教鹤翁，你看是剿呢？还是抚呢？”“先抚后剿。”那个被称做“鹤翁”的人，答得极其爽脆。“先抚后剿，先抚后剿，这四个字的宗旨，确切不移。”王有龄很快地说：“我索性再请教鹤翁，能就抚自然不必出队进剿，所以能抚还是要抚。应该如何着手？想来必有高见。““倒是有点看法，说出来请王大人指教……”

They argued back and forth, one speaking over another. At last Hu heard someone say, “Resistance to the grain tax is now the lesser offence; the killing of an official is the greater. The court will certainly not spare the principal offenders. In my view, the first step is to station troops at the strategic approaches. Only then should we decide whether to suppress the disturbance, conciliate the people, or combine both methods.” Hu nodded to himself. Here, at least, was someone who spoke coherently. Wang evidently thought the same. “An excellent judgment. May I ask further, Master He: would you recommend force or conciliation?” “Conciliation first, force afterwards,” the man addressed as Master He answered crisply. “Conciliation first, force afterwards—that principle is exactly right,” Wang said quickly. “Let me pursue the point. If conciliation succeeds, there is naturally no need to send troops in. Wherever conciliation is possible, we must attempt it. How should we begin? I imagine you have a proposal.” “I do have a few thoughts. Let me put them before you for your consideration...”

胡雪岩正听到紧要地方，谁知听差奉命来请，说是王太太吩咐，请他到里面去坐。彼此的关系，已超过“通家之好”的程度，内眷不避，胡雪岩便到内厅去见了王太太。“你看，好端端在湖州，上省一趟，就派了这么件差使！”王太太愁眉苦脸地说，“省城里谣言很多，都说新城这件事，跟‘长毛’是有勾结的。那地方又在山里，雪轩一去，万一陷在里面，叫天天不应，叫地地不灵，那时候怎么办？““不要紧，不要紧！”胡雪岩为了安慰她，只好硬起头皮拍胸脯，“有我在！我来想办法，包你平安。”“是啊！”王太太有惊喜之色，“雪轩常说，什么事都靠你。你们象弟兄一样，你总要帮帮你哥哥的忙。”“那还用说。你先请放宽了心，等他回头开完了会，我们再来商量。”于是胡雪岩便大谈王有龄在湖州的情形，公事如何顺利，地方如何爱戴，尽是些好听的话，让王太太好忘掉新城的案子。

Hu had just reached the crucial point in his listening when the attendant came to fetch him. Mrs Wang had asked him to come into the inner rooms. The intimacy between the two households went beyond ordinary family friendship; the women did not need to keep out of his sight. He therefore went to greet her in the inner hall. “Just think—he was perfectly well in Huzhou, and one visit to the provincial capital brings him an assignment like this!” she said miserably. “There are so many rumours in the city. They say the Xincheng trouble is connected with the Longhairs. The place is in the mountains too. If Xuexuan goes there and is trapped, with no one to answer his cries for help, what are we to do?” “Do not worry, do not worry.” To comfort her, Hu could only steel himself and offer a firm assurance. “I am here. I shall think of something. I promise he will be safe.” “Yes!” Her face showed both relief and surprise. “Xuexuan is always saying that he relies on you in everything. You are like brothers. You must help your elder brother.” “Of course. Set your mind at ease for now. When his meeting is over, we shall discuss it.” Hu then talked at length about Wang’s life in Huzhou: how smoothly his duties were proceeding, how warmly the local people regarded him. Everything he said was reassuring, intended to make Mrs Wang forget the Xincheng case for a while.

谈到日中要开饭了，王太太派人到外面去催请，把王有龄催了进来，他一见胡雪岩便问：“你怎么没有走？”“把你一个人先在这里，我在船上提心吊胆，雪公，你想想那是什么滋味？”王有龄不知道那是什么滋味，但他知道自己的感觉跟胡雪岩做朋友，实在够味得很！“雪岩，”他眼睛都有些润湿了，“这才是生死患难之交！说实话，一见你的面，精神就是一振。事情是很棘手，不过你来了，我倒也不怎么怕了。”玉太太听他们这一番对答，对胡雪岩的看法越发不同，而且她也跟她丈夫一样，愁怀一政，这几天以来，第一次出现了从容的神色。“有话慢慢谈，先吃饭！”她对王有龄说，“一直觉也睡不好，饭也吃不香。今天可以舒舒服服吃餐饭了，你们弟兄俩先吃洒，我做个‘红糟鸡’替你们下饭。”王有龄欣然赞许，对胡雪岩夸耀他太太的手艺：“你尝尝内人的手段！跟外面福州馆子里的菜，大不相同。“

They talked until it was time for the midday meal. Mrs Wang sent someone to hurry her husband in. On seeing Hu, he immediately asked, “Why have you not left?” “How could I leave you here alone and sit on the boat worrying myself sick? Mr Xuexuan, imagine what that would feel like.” Wang could not say exactly what it would feel like, but he knew how deeply satisfying it was to have Hu for a friend. His eyes grew moist. “Xueyan, this is friendship through life and death, through hardship and danger. To tell you the truth, the very sight of you lifts my spirits. The business is terribly difficult, but now that you are here, I am not nearly so afraid.” Listening to them, Mrs Wang regarded Hu with still greater esteem. Like her husband, she felt her anxieties lift; for the first time in days, she looked composed. “You can talk at leisure later. Eat first,” she said to Wang. “You have been unable to sleep properly or enjoy your food. Today you can have a comfortable meal. You two brothers begin with some wine. I shall make chicken with red rice-wine lees to go with your rice.” Wang approved delightedly and praised his wife’s cooking to Hu. “You must try her skill. It is quite different from what you get in the Fuzhou restaurants.”

于是都变得好整以暇了，王有龄擎着酒杯为胡雪岩细述新城一案的来龙去脉，以及眼前的处理办法。果然如胡雪岩所想象的，那些奉派听候王有龄差委的候补州县中，管用的只有那个“鹤翁”。“此人名叫嵇鹤龄，真正是个人才！”王有龄说，“足智多谋，能言善道，如果他肯帮我的忙，虽不能高枕无忧，事情已成功了一半。”“喔！”胡雪岩问，“他的忙怎么帮法？”“去安抚！”王有龄说，“新城在省的绅士，我已经碰过头了，那几位异口同声表示，有个得力的人到新城就地办事，事半而功倍。本来也是，遇到这种情形，一定是‘不入虎穴，焉得虎子’！无奈能干的，胆小不敢去，胆大敢去的，又多是庸才，成事不足，败事有余。除非我自己去，我不能去就得找嵇鹤龄这样的人。”

They were all at ease now. Wine cup in hand, Wang described the origins and development of the Xincheng affair and the measures currently proposed. As Hu had suspected, among all the officials assigned to serve under Wang, the only useful one was the man addressed as Master He. “His name is Ji Heling, and he is truly able,” Wang said. “Resourceful, full of ideas, and an excellent speaker. If he would help me, I could not exactly sleep without a care, but half the task would already be accomplished.” “What help do you want from him?” “To go and conciliate the people. I have already met the Xincheng gentlemen who are in the provincial capital. They all say that an effective man working on the spot could achieve twice as much with half the effort. It stands to reason. In a situation like this, one cannot catch a tiger’s cub without entering its lair. Unfortunately, the capable men are too timid to go, while most of those bold enough to venture there are mediocrities who would do more harm than good. Either I must go myself, or I must find someone like Ji Heling.”

“我明白了。嵇鹤龄不肯去的原因何在？也是胆小？”“哪里？”王有龄说，“此人有谋有勇，没有把那班扰民，放在眼里。他只是不肯去……“不肯去的原因是他觉得不合算。王有龄谈嵇鹤龄的为人，吃亏在恃才做物，所以虽有才干，历任大僚都不肯或者不敢用他，在浙江候补了七八年，派不上几回差使，因而牢骚极多。“他跟人家表示：”三年派不上一趟差，有了差使，好的轮不着，要送命的让我去。我为何这么傻？老实说，都为王某某还是个肯办事、脑筋清楚的，我才说几句。不然，我连口都懒得开。‘“王有龄说：”今天这一会，其实毫无影响，我一直在动脑筋的是，设法说动嵇鹤龄，谁知劳而无功！“

“I see. Why does Ji refuse? Is he afraid too?” “Not at all. He has both courage and judgment and thinks little of that crowd of troublemakers. He simply will not go...” The reason was that he did not consider it worth his while. Wang explained that Ji’s weakness lay in being proud of his abilities and disdainful of others. Talented though he was, successive high officials had been unwilling, or afraid, to employ him. He had spent seven or eight years in Zhejiang waiting for an appointment and had received very few assignments. He was consequently full of grievances. “He told someone, ‘Three years can pass without my being sent on a single errand. When there is an assignment, the good ones never come to me; the one that may cost a man his life is mine. Why should I be such a fool? To be frank, I spoke only because this Wang fellow is willing to get things done and has a clear head. Otherwise I should not even have bothered opening my mouth.’” Wang continued, “Today’s meeting has achieved nothing. All the time I was trying to think of some way to persuade Ji Heling, but my efforts came to nothing.”

“重赏之下，必有勇夫！雪公，你的条件开得不够吧？”“根本谈不上！嵇鹤龄穷得你们杭州人说的‘嗒嗒嘀’，但就是不肯哭穷，不谈钱，你拿他有什么办法？”工有龄停了一下又说体谅的话，“想想也难怪，八月半就要到了，要付的帐还没有着落，转眼秋风一起，冬天的衣服还在长生库里。听说他最近悼亡，留下一大堆孩子要照应。心境既不好，又分不开身，也实在难怪他不肯帮忙。”“那就只有我去了。”胡雪岩说。“你我是一样的。”王有龄说：“我不能去，当然也不能让你去。”“既如此，雪公，你要我做点什么？”胡雪岩已有所领会，特意这样问一句。“你看，雪岩，怎么想个办法，能让嵇鹤龄欣然应请，到新城去走一趟？”

“A generous reward will always find a brave man. Perhaps you have not offered enough?” “We never get as far as that. Ji is as poor as you Hangzhou people mean when you say a man has nothing whatever. Yet he refuses to plead poverty or discuss money. What can one do?” Wang paused, then spoke more sympathetically. “On reflection, I can hardly blame him. The Mid-Autumn Festival is almost here, and he has no means of paying the bills that fall due. Soon the autumn winds will rise, and his winter clothes are still in the pawnshop. I hear he has recently lost his wife and has a whole brood of children to look after. His spirits are low and he cannot get away. It is really no wonder that he will not help.” “Then I shall have to go,” Hu said. “You and I are the same in this. If I cannot go, I certainly cannot send you.” Hu had begun to understand what Wang wanted, but deliberately asked, “In that case, what would you like me to do?” “Can you think of some way, Xueyan, to make Ji Heling willingly accept the invitation and go to Xincheng?”

胡雪岩不即作答，慢慢喝着酒盘算。这个征兆不好，在王有龄的印象中，任何难题，一跟他提出来，就会有办法，没有办法也有答复，一两句话，直抉症结的根源，商量下去，总能解决。象这样不开口，看起来真是把他难倒了。难是有点难，却还不至于把胡雪岩难倒，他现在所想的还不是事而是人，嵇鹤龄这样的人，胡雪岩最倾倒，有本事也还要有骨气。王有龄所说的“恃才傲物”四个字，里面有好多学问，傲是傲他所看不起的人，如果明明比他高明不肯承认，眼睛长在额角上，目空一切，这样的人不是“傲”是“狂”，不但不值得佩服，而且要替他担心，因为狂下去就要疯了。嵇鹤龄心里是丘壑分明的，只听他说王有龄“还肯办事，脑筋清楚”，他才肯有所建言，就知道他的为人。这样的人，只要摸着他的脾气，很容易对付，话不投机，他睬都不睬你。

Hu did not answer immediately. He drank slowly and considered. This was not a promising sign. In Wang’s experience, whenever he put a difficulty before Hu, there was always a solution—or at least a response. A sentence or two would strike straight at the root of the problem; further discussion would resolve it. Such silence seemed to mean that this time Hu was truly at a loss. The problem was difficult, but by no means beyond him. At present he was considering the man rather than the task. Ji was precisely the sort of person Hu admired most: ability ought to be matched by backbone. There was much to understand beneath Wang’s description of a man “proud of his talents and disdainful of others.” Pride meant looking down on those one considered unworthy. A man who refused to acknowledge someone manifestly superior, who set his eyes above everyone else and saw no merit anywhere, was not proud but conceited. Such a man deserved not admiration but concern, for conceit carried far enough would become madness. Ji, however, clearly had a well-ordered judgment. His remark that Wang was “willing to get things done and has a clear head,” and that this was why he had offered advice, revealed his character. Understand his temper, and he would be easy enough to deal with; speak on the wrong terms, and he would not even acknowledge you.

“可惜事情太急，没有辰光了，不然，我跟他个把月交下来，一定可以叫他听我的话。”“是啊！我是不容你下水磨功夫。难就难这日子上头。”“他有没有什么好朋友？”“怎么没有？”王有龄说，“也是个候补知县。会画画，好酒量，此人最佩服嵇鹤龄，但虽无话不谈，却做不得他的主。我就是托他去疏通的。”“喔，‘无话不谈’？胡雪岩很注意地问。“是的。此人姓裘，裘、酒谐音，所以外号叫‘酒糊涂’，其实不糊涂。我介绍他跟你见见面？““不忙！”

“The pity is that the business is so urgent and there is no time,” Hu said. “Otherwise, give me a month or so to make friends with him, and I could certainly persuade him to listen to me.” “Exactly. I cannot give you time to work on him gradually. That is the difficulty.” “Has he any close friends?” “Of course. Another man awaiting a county appointment, who paints and has an excellent capacity for wine. He admires Ji enormously. They discuss everything, but he cannot make Ji’s decisions for him. It was through him that I tried to arrange matters.” “They discuss everything?” Hu asked with particular attention. “Yes. His surname is Qiu, which sounds rather like the word for wine, so they call him ‘Wine Muddlehead.’ He is not actually muddleheaded. Shall I introduce you?” “No hurry.”

胡雪岩说了这一句，却又不开口了，尽白夹着王太太精心烹调的红糟鸡，大块往嘴里送。还要腾出工夫来向她讨教做法，越发不来理会王有龄。吃完饭、洗过脸，胡雪岩叼着根象牙“剔牙杖”，手里捏一把紫砂小茶壶，走来走去踱方步，踱了半天，站往脚说：“要他‘欣然’，只怕办不到！”等了好久的王有龄，听得这一说，赶紧接口：“不管了！嵇鹤龄欣然也好，不高兴也好，反正只要肯去，就一定会尽心。公事完了，我替他磕个头道谢都无所谓。”“好，我来办！雪公，把你的袍褂借我一套。”“什么借？”王有龄转身喊道：“太太，你捡一身袍褂，还有，全副的七品服色，捡齐了叫高升送到雪岩那里去。““对了，顺便托高升跟我家说一声，我上海暂时不去了。”王太太答应首，自去料理。王有龄便问：“你忽然想起要套公服，作何用处？”

After that, Hu fell silent again. He concentrated on the chicken Mrs Wang had so carefully prepared, putting generous pieces into his mouth. He even found time to ask her how it was made, paying Wang less attention than ever. After the meal, he washed his face and began pacing about with an ivory toothpick between his lips and a small purple-clay teapot in his hand. At last he stopped. “To make him go willingly may be beyond me.” Wang had been waiting a long time. He seized on the remark. “Never mind that. Willingly or unwillingly, once Ji agrees to go, he will do his utmost. When the business is over, I would willingly kneel and kowtow to thank him.” “Very well. I shall arrange it. Lend me a set of your official robes.” “Lend you?” Wang turned and called to his wife. “Choose a robe and jacket for Xueyan, and a complete set of insignia for the seventh rank. Have Gao Sheng take them to his house.” “Yes, and ask Gao Sheng to tell my family that I am not going to Shanghai just yet.” Mrs Wang agreed and went to make the arrangements. Wang asked, “Why have you suddenly thought of official dress? What do you need it for?”

“我要唱出戏。”胡雪岩又说，“闲话不必提，你发个帖子，晚上请‘酒糊涂’来喝酒，我有事要问他。”王有龄依言照办，立刻发了帖子，同时预备酒筵，因为宾主一共只有三个人，菜备得不多，却特地觅了一罐十五年陈的“竹叶青”，打算让“酒糊涂”喝个痛快。到晚来，客人欣然应约，胡雪岩跟他请教了“台甫”，略略寒暄，随即入席。姓裘的名叫丰言，名如其人，十分健谈，谈的自然是嵇鹤龄。这一顿酒吃完，已经二更过后。王有龄厚犒裘丰言的跟班、轿伕，并且派高升把有了六七分酒意的客人送了回去。然后跟胡雪岩商量如何说服嵇鹤龄？“雪公，”也有了酒意的胡雪岩笑道，“山人自有道理，你就不必问了。明天我得先部署部署，后天一早去拜嵇鹤龄，必有好音。我这出戏得有个好配角，请你关照高升到舍间来，我用他做配角儿。““好！好！”王有龄也笑道：“我等着看你这出戏。”

“I am going to stage a little play. But let us say no more of that. Send an invitation asking Wine Muddlehead to dinner tonight. There is something I want to ask him.” Wang did as requested, dispatching the invitation at once and arranging a banquet. Since there would be only three of them, he ordered few dishes, but made a special effort to obtain a jar of fifteen-year-old Bamboo Leaf Green wine, intending to let his guest drink to his heart’s content. The visitor accepted gladly and arrived that evening. Hu asked his name, exchanged a few civilities, and they sat down to dinner. His full name was Qiu Fengyan—abundant speech—and it suited him, for he was extraordinarily talkative. Naturally, their subject was Ji Heling. By the time the meal ended, the second watch had passed. Wang gave generous tips to Qiu’s attendant and sedan bearers and sent Gao Sheng to escort the decidedly tipsy guest home. Then he turned to Hu to discuss how Ji might be persuaded. Hu, who had also been drinking, smiled. “This humble strategist has his own method, Mr Xuexuan. You need not ask. Tomorrow I must make my preparations; early the following morning I shall call on Ji Heling. There will be good news. My play requires an able supporting actor. Send Gao Sheng to my house; I shall give him the part.” “Excellent, excellent!” Wang laughed. “I look forward to your performance.”

第三天一早，胡雪岩穿起补子的袍褂，戴上水晶顶子的大帽，坐上轿子，由高升“执帖”，径自来拜嵇鹤龄。他住的是租来的房子，式微的巨族，房屋破旧，但格局甚大，里面住着六、七户人家，屋主连门房都租了出去，黯旧的粉墙上写着“陈记苏广成衣”六个大字。高升便上去问讯，“陈老板，请问嵇老爷可是住在这里？”“嵇老爷还是纪老爷？”姓陈的裁缝问，嵇跟纪念不清楚，听来是一个音。“嵇鹤龄嵇老爷。”“我不晓得他们的名字。可是喜欢骂人的那位嵇老爷？”“这我就不晓得了。”高升把一手所持的清香素烛拿给他看，“刚刚死了太太的那位嵇老爷。”“不错，就是喜欢骂人的那个。他住在三厅东面那个院子。”“多谢，多谢！”高升向胡雪岩使个眼色，接着取根带来的纸煤，在裁缝案板上的熨斗里点燃了，往里就走。

On the third morning, Hu put on his official robe and jacket with their rank badge, donned a formal hat with a crystal button, and set out in a sedan chair to call on Ji. Gao Sheng accompanied him, carrying the visiting card. Ji lived in rented quarters in a house belonging to a once-prominent family now in decline. The building was dilapidated but spacious, with six or seven households lodged inside. Even the gatehouse had been let. On its dingy plaster wall were painted the words “Chen’s Suzhou and Guangdong Ready-Made Clothes.” Gao Sheng went up to inquire. “Mr Chen, does Master Ji live here?” “Which Ji?” the tailor asked. The two surnames he mentioned sounded much alike. “Master Ji Heling.” “I do not know their personal names. Do you mean the Master Ji who likes scolding people?” “That I cannot say.” Gao Sheng showed him the incense and plain mourning candles he carried. “The Master Ji whose wife has just died.” “That is him—the one who likes scolding. He lives in the courtyard east of the third hall.” “Thank you, thank you.” Gao Sheng gave Hu a look, then took a paper spill he had brought and lit it from the iron on the tailor’s worktable. With that, he went inside.

胡雪岩穿官服，还是破题儿第一遭，踱不来方楞折角的四方步，加以高升走得又快，他不能不紧紧跟着，所以顾不得官派，捞起下摆，大踏步赶了上去。穿过大厅，沿着夹弄，走到三厅，东面一座院落，门上钉着麻，一看不错，高升便开始唱戏了，拉长了调子喊一声：“胡老爷拜！”一路高唱，一路往里直闯，到了灵堂里，吹旺纸煤，先点蜡烛后燃香。这个突如其来的动作，把嵇家弄得莫名其妙，有个跟班模样的老者问道：“老哥，贵上是哪一位？”“敝上姓胡，特来拜嵇老爷！拜托你递一递帖子。”说道，高升从拜匣里取出一张“教愚弟胡光塘拜”的名帖递了过去。他们在里头在打交道，胡雪岩只在院子门口等，过了一会，听见嵇家的跟班在说：“不敢当，不敢当！敝上说，跟胡老爷素味平生，不敢请见，连帖子亦不敢领。“

This was the first time in his life that Hu had worn official dress. He had not mastered the measured, angular gait expected of an official, and Gao Sheng was walking so fast that he had to hurry after him. Abandoning dignity, he gathered up the hem of his robe and strode forward. They crossed the main hall, followed a narrow passage, and reached the third hall. Mourning hemp fixed to the doorway of the eastern courtyard confirmed that they had found the right place. Gao Sheng began his performance. In a long, ringing voice he announced, “Master Hu has come to call!” Still proclaiming the visitor’s arrival, he went straight into the mourning hall, blew his paper spill into flame, lit the candles, and then the incense. This unexpected intrusion bewildered the household. An elderly man who looked like an attendant asked, “Sir, who is your master?” “His surname is Hu. He has come specially to call on Master Ji. Would you kindly present his card?” From the card box, Gao Sheng produced one bearing the formal inscription, “Your humble younger brother, Hu Guangyong, respectfully calls.” While the two men dealt with each other inside, Hu waited at the courtyard entrance. Presently he heard the attendant saying, “We could not presume, we could not presume! My master says he has never had the honour of meeting Master Hu and cannot venture to receive him. He cannot even presume to accept his card.”

这拒人于千里以外的态度，是胡雪岩早就料到了的。他的步骤是，如果投帖而获嵇鹤龄延见，自然最好，否则就还有一步棋。此刻便是走这步棋的时候了，他不慌不忙地往里走去，直入灵堂，一言不发，从高升手里接过已点燃的线香，在灵前肃穆地往上一举，然后亲自去上香。等嵇家的跟班会过意来，连忙喊道：“真不敢当，真不敢当！”胡雪岩不理他，管自己恭恭敬敬地跪在拜垫上行札。嵇家的跟班慌了手脚，顺手拉过一个在看热闹的、胖胖的小姑娘，把她的头一掀，硬捺着跪下。“快磕头回礼！”这时把嵇家上下都惊却了，等胡雪岩站起身来，只见五、六个孩子，有男有女，小到三、四岁，大到十四五岁，都围在四周，用好奇的眼光，注视着这位从未见过的客人。“大官！”嵇家的跟班，招呼年龄最大的那个男孩，“来给胡老爷磕头道谢。”就这时候嵇鹤龄出现了，“是哪位？”他一面掀起门帘，一面问。“这位想来就是嵇大哥了！”胡雪岩兜头一揖。嵇鹤龄还了礼，冷冷地问道：“我与足下素昧平生，何劳吊唁？”

Hu had expected this forbidding reception. His plan was simple: if his card secured an interview, so much the better; otherwise he had another move to make. Now was the time for it. Unhurriedly, he walked straight into the mourning hall. Without speaking, he took the lighted incense from Gao Sheng, raised it solemnly before the spirit tablet, and placed it in position himself. When Ji’s attendant understood what was happening, he hastily protested, “You do us too much honour! Far too much!” Hu ignored him and knelt reverently on the cushion to perform the mourning rites. Flustered, the attendant seized a plump little girl who had been watching and pressed her down into a kneeling position. “Quickly! Kowtow in return!” By now the whole household had been stirred. When Hu rose, five or six children surrounded him, boys and girls ranging from three or four to fourteen or fifteen, all gazing curiously at this stranger. “Young master!” the attendant called to the eldest boy. “Come and kowtow to thank Master Hu.” At that moment Ji himself appeared. “Who is it?” he asked, lifting the curtain. “You must be Brother Ji.” Hu greeted him with a deep bow. Ji returned it and asked coldly, “You and I have never met. Why trouble yourself to offer condolences?”

“草草不恭！我是奉王太守的委托，专诚来行个礼。”胡雪岩张开两臂，看看自己身上，不好意思地笑道：“不瞒嵇大哥说，从捐了官以来，这套袍褂还是第一次穿。只因为初次拜访，不敢不具公服。”“言重，言重！不知足下光降，有何见教？”话是很客气，却不肯肃客人座，意思是立谈数语便要送客出门。不过他虽崖岸自同，他那跟班却很懂礼数，端了盖碗茶来，说一声：“请坐，用茶！”这一下嵇鹤龄不能不尽主人的道理了。等一坐下来，胡雪岩便是一顿恭维，兼道王有龄是如何仰慕。他的口才本就来得，这时又是刻意敷衍，俗语道得好：“千穿万穿，马屁不穿”，就怕拍得肉麻，因而几句恰到好处的恭维，胡雪岩就把嵇鹤龄的傲气减消了一半。

“My poor preparations are hardly respectful enough. Prefect Wang asked me to come specially and pay my respects.” Hu spread his arms, looked down at his clothes, and smiled with apparent embarrassment. “To tell you the truth, Brother Ji, although I purchased my rank some time ago, this is the first time I have worn these robes. Since this is my first visit, I did not dare come without formal dress.” “You are too kind. May I ask what brings you here?” The words were courteous, but he did not invite his visitor to sit; plainly he meant to exchange a few standing remarks and send him away. Though Ji held himself aloof, his attendant understood the courtesies. Bringing covered bowls of tea, he said, “Please sit and take some tea.” Ji could no longer avoid doing his duty as host. Once seated, Hu offered a stream of compliments and spoke of Wang’s admiration. He was naturally eloquent and was now taking special pains to please. As the saying went, anything might fail to get through, but flattery never did—provided it was not offensively excessive. A few well-judged compliments reduced Ji’s hauteur by half.

“嵇大哥，还有点东西，王太守托我面交，完全是一点点敬意。”说着，他从靴页子里掏出来一个信封，隔着茶几递了过去。嵇鹤龄不肯接，“内中何物呢？”他问。“不是银票。”胡雪岩爽爽快快的把他心中的疑惑揭破，接下来又加了一句：“几张无用的废纸。”这句话引起了嵇鹤龄的好奇心，撕开封套一看，里面一叠借据，有向钱庄借的，有裘丰言经手为他代借的，上面或者盖着“注销”的戳子，或者写着“作废”二字。不是“废纸”是什么呢？“这、这、这怎么说呢？”嵇鹤龄的枪法大乱，而尤其令他困惑的是，有人抬进来两只皮箱，他认得那是自己的东西，但不应该在这里，应该在当铺里。于是嵇鹤龄急急喊他那跟在箱子后面的跟班：“张贵！怎么回事？”上当铺的勾当，都归张贵经手，但是他也不明白是怎么回事，一出戏他不过看到前台的演出，后台的花样他看不见。

“Brother Ji, there is something else Prefect Wang asked me to place in your hands—a very small token of his regard.” Hu drew an envelope from the side of his boot and passed it across the tea table. Ji would not take it. “What is inside?” “Not banknotes,” Hu said briskly, bringing the unspoken suspicion into the open. “Just a few useless scraps of paper.” This aroused Ji’s curiosity. He tore open the envelope and found a bundle of promissory notes: some for loans from banks, others for money Qiu Fengyan had borrowed on his behalf. Each had been stamped “Cancelled” or marked “Void.” What were they now but waste paper? “What—what is the meaning of this?” Ji’s defences were thrown into confusion. More bewildering still, two leather trunks were carried in. He recognized them as his own, but they should not have been here; they should have been in the pawnshop. “Zhang Gui!” he called urgently to the attendant following the trunks. “What is happening?” Zhang Gui handled all the household’s pawnshop business, but he too did not understand the whole affair. He had seen only the performance on stage, not the devices behind the scenes.

线索是裘丰言那里来的，知道了嵇家常去求教的那家当铺就好办了。钱庄与当铺素有往来，刘庆生就认识那家当铺的徽州朝奉，一说替嵇老爷赎当，自然万分欢迎。但赎当要有当票，因而作了一个约定，由刘庆生将全部本息付讫，“当头”送到嵇家，凭票收货，否则原货取回。这是万无一失的安排，当铺里自然乐从。因此，在胡雪岩跟嵇鹤龄打交道时，作为“配角”的高升也在“唱戏”，他把张贵悄悄拉到一边，先请教了“贵姓”，然后说道：“张老哥，有点东西在门外，请你去看看。”门外是指定时间送到的两口皮箱。高升告诉他，本息部已付过，只凭当票就可取回箱子。张贵跟了嵇鹤龄十几年，知道主人的脾气，但也因为跟得太久，不但感情上已泯没了主仆的界限，而且嵇鹤龄的日常家用，都由他调度，等于是个“当家人”，别的都还好办，六个孩子的嘴非喂不可，所以对这两箱子衣服，决定自作主张把它领了下来，至多受主人几句埋怨，实惠总是实惠。

The information had come from Qiu Fengyan. Once Hu knew which pawnshop the Ji household habitually used, the rest was easy. Banks and pawnshops regularly dealt with each other, and Liu knew the Huizhou appraiser at that establishment. The proposal to redeem Master Ji’s pledges was naturally most welcome. Redemption, however, required the pawn tickets. An arrangement was therefore made: Liu would pay the entire principal and interest, and the pledged goods would be delivered to Ji’s house and handed over against the tickets. If the tickets were not produced, the goods would be taken back. It was a perfectly secure arrangement, and the pawnshop readily agreed. Thus, while Hu was dealing with Ji, Gao Sheng was playing his supporting role. He quietly drew Zhang Gui aside, asked his surname, and said, “Brother Zhang, there are a few things outside. Please come and look.” The two trunks had arrived at the appointed time. Gao Sheng explained that principal and interest were paid; Zhang needed only to produce the tickets to recover them. Zhang had served Ji for more than ten years and knew his temper. Yet long service had also softened the division between master and servant, while the management of the household’s everyday expenses rested on Zhang. He was virtually the head of its domestic affairs. Other things might be managed somehow, but six children had to be fed. He decided on his own authority to accept the two trunks of clothing. At worst, his master would reproach him. A practical benefit was still a practical benefit.

“唉！”被请到一边，悄悄听完经过的嵇鹤龄，微顿着足叹气：“我从来没有遇见过这种事。现在怎么办呢？”张贵不作声，心里在想：有钱，把赎当的本息归还人家，没有钱，那就只好领受人家的好意。不然，难道把东西丢掉？““好了，好了！”嵇鹤龄横一横心，另作处置，挥手说道：“你不用管了。”“老爷！张贵交代了一句：”本息一共是二百三十二两六钱银子。“嵇鹤龄点点头，又去陪客，“仁兄大人，”他略带点气愤地说，“这是哪位的主意？高明之至！”“哪里，哪里！”胡雪岩用不安的声音说，“无非王太守敬仰老兄，略表敬意，你不必介怀！”“我如何能不介怀？”嵇鹤龄把声音提得高，“你们做这个圈套，硬叫我领这个情，拒之不可，受之不甘。真正是……”他总算把话到口边的“岂有此理”四个字咽了回去。

Called aside and quietly told what had happened, Ji stamped his foot lightly and sighed. “I have never encountered anything like this. What am I to do now?” Zhang said nothing. He thought: if there was money, they could repay the principal and interest to the man who had redeemed the goods. If there was none, they could only accept his kindness. What else could they do—throw the things away? “Very well, very well!” Ji hardened his resolve to deal with the matter in another way and waved him off. “Leave it to me.” “Sir,” Zhang added, “the principal and interest came to two hundred and thirty-two taels and six qian.” Ji nodded and returned to his guest. “My honoured friend,” he said with a trace of anger, “whose idea was this? Exceedingly clever!” “Not at all, not at all.” Hu sounded uneasy. “Prefect Wang admires you and wished only to show a little respect. Please do not take it to heart.” “How can I do otherwise?” Ji raised his voice. “You have laid a trap and forced this obligation upon me. I cannot refuse it, yet I have no wish to accept it. It is really...” He managed to swallow the words “utterly outrageous” before they escaped.

他要发脾气，也在胡雪岩意料之中，笑嘻嘻地站起身来又作揖：“老兄，我领罪！是我出的主意，与王太守无干！说句实话，我倒不是为老兄，是为王太守，他深知老兄的耿介，想有所致意而不敢，为此愁眉不展，我蒙王太守不弃，视为患难之交，不能不替他分忧，因而想了这么一条唐突大贤的计策。总之，是我荒唐，我跟老兄请罪！”说到这里又是长揖到地。嵇鹤龄不知道这番措词雅驯的话，是经王有龄斟酌过的“戏辙儿”，只觉得他谈吐不俗，行事更不俗，象是熟读《战国策》的，倒不可小看了这个“铜钱眼里翻跟斗”的陌生人。于是他的态度和缓了，还了礼拉着胡雪岩的手说：“来，来，我们好好谈一谈。”

His anger, too, was part of what Hu had anticipated. Smiling, Hu rose and bowed again. “Brother, I accept the blame. The idea was mine; Prefect Wang had nothing to do with it. To speak frankly, I acted less for your sake than for his. He knows your upright and independent nature and dared not offer the consideration he wished to show you. It troubled him greatly. He has been kind enough to treat me as a friend through hardship and danger; I could not fail to relieve his anxiety. That is how I came to devise this scheme, so presumptuous towards a man of your worth. In short, I have behaved outrageously, and I ask your pardon.” He bowed deeply again. Ji did not know that this polished speech was a carefully prepared piece of the performance, revised with Wang’s help. He merely felt that the stranger spoke with distinction and acted with still greater originality. He seemed a man who had studied the Stratagems of the Warring States. This fellow who turned somersaults through the hole in a copper coin was not to be underestimated. Ji’s manner softened. Returning the bow, he took Hu’s hand. “Come, let us have a proper talk.”

一看这情形，胡雪岩自觉嵇鹤龄已入掌握，不过此刻有两种不同的应付办法，如果只要他就范，替工有龄作一趟新城之行，事毕即了，彼此漠不相关，那很好办，就地敷衍他一番就行了。倘或想跟他做个朋友，也是为王有龄在官场中找个得力帮手，还须好好下一番功夫。转念之间，就有了抉择，他实在也很欣赏嵇鹤龄这样的人，所以提了个建议，并且改了称呼，不称“老兄”称“鹤龄兄”。“我看这样，”他说，“鹤龄兄，我奉屈小酌，找个清凉的地方”摆一碗‘，你看怎么样？“日已将午，对这样一位来“示惠”的客人，嵇鹤龄原就想到，应该留客便饭，只是中馈乏人，孩子又多，家里实在不方便，不想胡雪岩有此提议，恰中下怀，因而欣然表示同意。“这身公服，可以不穿了！”胡雪岩看着身上，故意说道：“等我先回家换了衣服再来。”“那何心呢？”嵇鹤龄马上接口，“天气还热得很，随便找件纱衫穿就行了。”接着就叫他的儿子：“大毛，把我挂在门背后的那件长衫拿来。”于是胡雪岩换了公服，芽上嵇鹤龄的一件实地纱长衫。到了这样可以“共衣”的程度，交情也就显得不同了。两个人都没有穿马褂，一袭轻衫，潇潇洒洒的出了嵇家的院子。

Seeing this, Hu felt that he had gained the upper hand. There were now two possible courses. If all he wanted was Ji’s compliance—one journey to Xincheng for Wang, after which the affair would be finished and the two men need have nothing more to do with one another—he could easily manage it by saying what was needed on the spot. But if he wanted a friendship, and thereby a capable ally for Wang in official life, more effort would be required. He made his choice almost at once. He truly admired men like Ji. Changing his form of address from the general “Brother” to the more personal “Brother Heling,” he proposed a meal. “Let me invite you for a modest drink somewhere cool. What do you say?” It was nearly noon. Ji had already thought that he ought to keep a visitor who had come to do him such a kindness for lunch. But with no wife to manage the kitchen and so many children, his household was hardly convenient for entertaining. Hu’s suggestion suited him perfectly, and he gladly agreed. “I can take off this official dress now!” Hu deliberately looked down at himself. “Let me go home, change, and come back.” “Why trouble yourself? It is still very hot. Any light gauze gown will do.” Ji called to his son, “Da Mao, bring me the gown hanging behind the door.” Hu changed into one of Ji’s fine gauze gowns. Once they had reached the point of sharing clothes, their friendship plainly stood on a different footing. Neither wore a riding jacket; lightly dressed and at ease, they left the courtyard together.

“鹤龄兄，你请先走一步，我跟他说几句话。”他是指高升，胡雪岩先夸奖了他几句，然后让他回去，转告王有龄，事情一定可以成功，请王有龄即刻到嵇家来拜访。“胡老爷！”高升低声问道，“你跟嵇老爷吃酒去了，我们老爷一来，不是扑个空吗？”“ ‘孔子拜阳货’，就是要扑空。”胡雪岩点破其中的奥妙：“你们老爷来拜了，嵇老爷当然要去回拜，这下有事不就可以长谈了吗？”“是的，胡老爷的脑筋真好！”高升笑着说，“我懂了，你懂了请。”

“Brother Heling, go on a few steps. I should like a word with him.” Hu meant Gao Sheng. He first praised his performance, then told him to return and inform Wang that success was certain. Wang should come to call on Ji immediately. “Master Hu,” Gao Sheng asked quietly, “if you and Master Ji are going out to drink, will our master not find him away?” “Like Confucius calling on Yang Huo—that is precisely the point.” Hu explained the device. “Once your master has called, Master Ji must return the visit. Then, if there is business to discuss, they can have a long conversation.” “Yes, indeed. What a mind you have, Master Hu!” Gao Sheng smiled. “I understand. Please go ahead.”

文本处理

中文保留输入文件用字。英文对明显识别错误按上下文作保守理解，包括“刘庄生”按刘庆生、“玉太太”按王太太、“胡光塘”按已确立的正式姓名胡光墉处理；“把你一个人先在这里”按把你一个人留在这里理解。“行札”“素味平生”“崖岸自同”“部已付过”“芽上”“何心”等分别依行礼、素昧平生、崖岸自高、都已付过、穿上、何必的语境译出。

七万两提款中五万用于缴入藩库，随后还回阜康的是郁四调来的五万两票款加上余下两万两；英文区分“原已准备的票款”“暂提的可兑现票据”和“还回银行的款项”，不将一段中的所有数字都解释为新增借款。客户票据用 customers’ bank drafts，现银结存则依原文包含随时可兑现的票据。前章第6293行筹借给麟桂的款项为两万五千两，而本章第8385行称两万两；此处照录本章数字，不把原稿差异暗自消除。

“个把时辰”在此按约两个小时表达；“二更”保留 second watch，不强行给出钟表上的精确分钟。六个孩子、七八年候补、两百三十二两六钱等数目照原文保留。对当时的官制、地方传说和地方民风，英文均呈现小说及人物的说法，不另作史实背书。

“孔子拜阳货”保留典故名，并接着译出本段已经说明的策略：有意趁对方不在时拜访，促成后续回拜。没有额外编入典故故事。“象是熟读《战国策》的”是嵇鹤龄对胡雪岩的印象，并不表示叙述者已证明胡实际熟读此书。

精读方向

阅读：比较胡雪岩决定离开、夜间难安、停船返回三个阶段。注意行为变化是由对朋友的责任感推动，不要把开头“爱莫能助”的念头误作他最终的态度。对嵇鹤龄，则应区分傲慢的表象、对不公待遇的不满、经济与家务的实际约束。

完形：可遮住 first、otherwise、yet、therefore、once、even if 等连接表达，分别还原军事处置、资金周转、拜访与赎当三条因果链。尤其注意“先安抚后进剿”中的先后关系，以及“想提供帮助但对方拒收”中的让步与条件。

段落排序：嵇家一场拜访先写客人看到的变化，后倒叙解释借据和皮箱如何准备。不能只按事件实际发生时间排序；先辨认叙述者是在展示结果还是补述原因。“那两只皮箱”“这一步棋”等回指可帮助定位。

翻译：尝试处理“不是傲而是狂”一段的概念区分，保留判断的条件，避免将人物的性格一概归入一个负面词。再读王、胡关于求助的对话，识别明说的请求与含蓄表达的需要。

写作：可以围绕“理解人的具体困难有助于解决问题”“信任需要能力与可靠行为共同支持”“计划应区分当前步骤和后续目标”练习论证。注意把小说中有效的策略与值得推广的道德原则分开。可积累 put one’s finger on the matter、take someone at their word、under someone’s direction、bring a situation under control 等相关表达；使用时核对具体含义，勿把成语机械堆叠。
