第十二章｜CHAPTER TWELVE

到新城先到富阳，走钱塘江这条水路。等送行的王有龄一走，嵇鹤龄把胡雪岩留了下来，说还有几句话要谈。到船舱中坐定，他从拜匣里取出一张梅红单帖，放在胡雪岩面前，上面写的是“嵇鹤龄，以字行。湖北罗田人，嘉庆二十一年十月初四午时生。”“喔！”胡雪岩笑道：“你倒真巴结，应该我先去讨瑞云的八字来给你。其实，这也可以不必。““不是，不是！”嵇鹤龄摇着手说，“这张帖子是交给你的。雪岩兄，我想高攀，我们拜个把子。”“这……”胡雪岩愣了一下，接着喜逐颜开地说：“那是我高攀了！不过，此刻来不及备帖子，但是也要磕个头。”“这都好办，等我新城回来再行礼。”嵇鹤龄说：“相知贵相知心。如果你不嫌弃，此刻我们就改称呼。你今年贵庚？”

To reach Xincheng, one first travelled to Fuyang by water along the Qiantang River. Once Wang, who had come to see him off, had left, Ji asked Hu to stay a little longer. There were still a few things he wished to say. Seated in the cabin, he took a plum-red card from his visiting-card box and placed it before Hu. It read: “Ji Heling, known by his courtesy name. Native of Luotian, Hubei. Born at the noon hour on the fourth day of the tenth month, in the twenty-first year of Jiaqing.” “Oh!” Hu laughed. “You certainly don't waste time. I ought to have obtained Ruiyun's birth particulars for you first. Though really, we could dispense with this.” “No, no!” Ji waved his hand. “The card is for you. Brother Xueyan, I hope you will grant me the honour of becoming my sworn brother.” “This...” Hu paused in surprise, then beamed. “The honour would be mine! I haven't time to prepare a card now, but we must at least bow.” “That can all be arranged. We can perform the ceremony when I return from Xincheng. What matters in friendship is understanding each other's hearts. If you accept me, let's change our forms of address now. How old are you?”

“我小得多。”胡雪岩改了称呼，叫一声：“大哥！”接着便给“大哥”磕头。嵇鹤龄急忙也跪下还礼，自然称他“二弟”。两人对拜了一拜，连“撮土为香”都用不着，就结成了异姓手足。拜罢起身，彼此肩上的感觉便都不同了，嵇鹤龄是减轻而胡雪岩是加重，“大哥！”他说，“你尽管放心到新城去，专心一致办事，家里一点都不用记挂，一切都有我！”“那自然要托你。”嵇鹤龄又说，“不过眼前有瑞云在，也没有什么不放心的，我走了，你也赶紧动身到上海去吧！早去早回，我们换帖子请客。““好的，我晓得，一路顺风。”

“Much younger than you.” Hu adopted the new form at once. “Elder Brother!” Then he knelt to bow to him. Ji hastily knelt in return, naturally calling him “Second Brother.” They exchanged a bow and became brothers of different surnames without even taking a pinch of earth to serve as incense. When they rose, each felt a change in the weight on his shoulders. Ji's had lightened; Hu's had grown heavier. “Elder Brother,” Hu said, “go to Xincheng with your mind at ease and devote yourself entirely to the work. Don't spend a moment worrying about things at home. I'll attend to everything.” “Naturally I must rely on you. But with Ruiyun there now, I have little cause for anxiety. Once I've gone, hurry to Shanghai yourself. Go early and return early, and we'll exchange cards and give a feast.” “Very well. I understand. A safe journey!”

胡雪岩离船登岸，坐轿进城，等王有龄到家，他接着也到了他那里，脸上是掩抑不住的笑容，王有龄夫妇都觉得奇怪，问他什么事这么高兴。“你们两位再也想下到的，就雪公上了岸那一刻工夫，我跟鹤龄拜成把弟兄了。”“太好了！恭喜，恭喜！”王有龄对他妻子说：“太太，这一来我们跟鹤龄的情分也不同了。”“真成了一家人，至亲好友，原是越多越好。”“说到这一层，我倒想起来了。”胡雪岩从马褂口袋里摸出个红封套递向王太太。她不肯接，“这是什么？”“瑞云的聘金……”话没有完，王有龄先就乱喊：“不行，不行！这怎么好收他的？你还给他。”

Hu left the boat, went ashore, and took a chair into the city. He reached Wang's house just after Wang himself, unable to suppress his smile. Both husband and wife wondered what had made him so happy. “You'd never guess! In the few moments after Mr Xuexuan went ashore, Heling and I became sworn brothers.” “Splendid! Congratulations!” Wang turned to his wife. “My dear, this changes our relationship with Heling too.” “Now we really are one family. The more close relations and good friends, the better.” “That reminds me.” Hu took a red envelope from his jacket pocket and offered it to Mrs Wang. She would not accept it. “What is this?” “Ruiyun's betrothal money...” Before he could finish, Wang protested loudly. “No, no! How could we take his money? Give it back.”

“慢慢，你不要吵！”玉太太挥挥手说：“我先要问问清楚，瑞云怎么样？她自己答应了没有？”“看样子是千肯万肯的了。”“哪有这么快？”王太太不信，“她到底怎么说的？”“这也用不着明说。”胡雪岩把昨晚上的情形讲了一遍。这些眉目传情，灵犀暗通的事，本来就是最好的话题，胡雪岩又有意刻画入微，所以把王有龄夫妇听得津津有味，都是微张着嘴，耸起两面唇角，随时准备放声大笑的神态。“差也差不多了。”等他讲完，王有龄点点头说。“到底不是什么‘千肯万肯’，总还要我来说两句，她才会松口。”

“Wait. Don't make such a noise!” Mrs Wang waved a hand. “First I want to be clear about Ruiyun. Has she agreed herself?” “She seems willing a thousand times over.” “How could it happen so quickly?” Mrs Wang was unconvinced. “What exactly did she say?” “It hardly needs to be said outright.” Hu recounted the previous evening. Affection conveyed in looks and unspoken understanding always made an engaging story, and he deliberately described every nuance. The couple listened with relish, mouths slightly open and the corners lifted, ready to burst out laughing at any moment. “It seems nearly settled,” Wang said with a nod when Hu finished. “Nearly isn't ‘willing a thousand times over,’” Mrs Wang replied. “I shall still have to say a few words before she gives her consent.”

“拜托，拜托！”胡雪岩拱一拱手，趁势又把红封套递了过去。王太太已经接到手里，王有龄一把夺了回来，塞回胡雪岩：“这不能收的。”“没有什么不能收。”王太太接口， “我们瑞云是人家聘了去的，不是不值钱白送的。兄弟，你把聘金交给我，我另有用处。”“你有什么用处？”王有龄大为不悦，几乎要跟太太吵架了。“我说给你听！”王太太的声音也很大，“瑞云一份嫁妆归我们预备。这一千两银了，我另外交给她，是她的私房钱。请问王大老爷，可以不可以？“王有龄的表情立刻改变了，歉意地笑着，却用埋怨的语气回答：“太太，你何不早说？”“现在说也不晚。”王太太拿着红封套，得意地走了。

“Then I entrust it to you!” Hu clasped his hands and used the opportunity to offer the red envelope again. Mrs Wang had just taken it when her husband snatched it back and thrust it at Hu. “We can't accept this.” “Of course we can,” she answered. “Ruiyun is being honourably betrothed, not given away as though she were worth nothing. Brother, give me the money. I have another use for it.” “What use?” Wang was thoroughly displeased and on the verge of quarrelling with her. “I'll tell you!” Her voice rose too. “We shall provide Ruiyun's dowry ourselves. These thousand taels will go to her separately, as money of her own. May I ask whether Your Honour Wang permits that?” His expression changed at once. He smiled apologetically, though his reply sounded reproachful. “My dear, why didn't you say so earlier?” “It isn't too late to say it now.” Mrs Wang carried off the envelope in triumph.

“雪岩！”王有龄略有忧色，“我们先商量一下，万一嵇鹤龄此去无功，下一步该如何？”“先抚后剿”的宗旨是早已定好了的，抚既不成，自然是派兵进剿，何需问得？但胡雪岩了解他的内心，便不肯这么回答，只说：“你不必过虑！鹤龄跟我说过，无论如何，自保之策，总是有的，可见得他极有把握。而且，人逢喜事精神爽，他此去没有后顾之虑，专心一致对付公事，当然无往不利。“听他侃侃而谈，声音中极具自信，王有龄不知不觉受了鼓舞，愁怀一放，连连点头。“还有，雪公，”胡雪岩又说，“你正鸿运当头，瑞云也要托你的福，她又是一副福相，看起来必有帮夫运，所以鹤龄一定马到成功。瑞云迟早是个‘掌印夫人’！”这一说，王有龄越发高兴，“不错，不错！我也觉得，这无论如何不是倒霉的时候。”他又说：“等鹤龄功成回省，我一定力保他接归安县。这个缺，一年起码有五万银子进帐。”

“Xueyan.” Wang looked a little worried. “We ought to discuss what comes next if Ji fails.” Their principle had long been settled: conciliation first, force afterwards. If conciliation failed, naturally troops would be sent. Why ask? But Hu understood what was troubling him and would not answer so literally. “Don't worry too much. Heling told me that whatever happened, he had ways to protect himself. He must be very confident. Besides, happy events lift a man's spirits. He has no domestic worries now and can concentrate entirely on the public business. Of course he'll succeed.” Hu spoke fluently and with such assurance that Wang was unconsciously encouraged. His worries eased, and he nodded repeatedly. “And another thing, Mr Xuexuan. Your fortune is at its height. Ruiyun will share your good luck, and she has a fortunate face herself. She looks certain to bring prosperity to her husband. Heling is bound to succeed at once. Sooner or later Ruiyun will be an official's lady!” Wang was still more pleased. “Quite right! I too feel this cannot possibly be an unlucky time. When Heling returns successfully to the provincial capital, I'll strongly recommend him for Gui'an County. That post brings in at least fifty thousand taels a year.”

胡雪岩心想，归安县现在由王有龄兼署，保了嵇鹤龄，就等于从他自己荷包里挖五万银子出来。一时慷慨，终必失悔，却又是说不出的苦。朋友相交，到了这地步一定不能善始善终，倒要劝一劝他。“归安是一等大县，只怕上头不肯。如果碰个钉子，彼此不好，我倒有个想法。”“噢！你说，一定是好主意。”“你看是不是好主意？”胡雪岩说，“海运局的差使，你又兼顾不到，何不保鹤龄接替？”“啊！”王有龄恍然大悟，“对了！这才是一举数得。”胡雪岩懂他这句话的意思，这一举数得就包括了他的便利在内，嵇鹤龄接替海运局的差使，他经手的几笔垫款、借款，料理起来就顺利了。

Hu reflected that Wang was currently acting magistrate of Gui'an himself. Recommending Ji would be like taking fifty thousand taels out of his own pocket. A moment's generosity would eventually bring regret, and regret he could not express. A friendship brought to that point could hardly end as well as it had begun. Hu ought to dissuade him. “Gui'an is a large county of the first class. The authorities might not agree. A refusal would be awkward for both of you. I have another idea.” “Tell me! It's sure to be a good one.” “See what you think. You can't give proper attention to the Sea Transport Bureau as well. Why not recommend Heling to succeed you there?” “Ah!” Wang understood. “Exactly! That would achieve several things at once.” Hu knew what he meant. The benefits would include Hu's own convenience: with Ji in charge of the bureau, the advances and loans Hu had arranged would be easier to settle.

“准定这么办，”王有龄又问，“你哪天走？”“至迟后天一走要走了。”“那好，你办完了事就回来。”王有龄放低了声音说，“我托你带笔钱去。”带给谁？心照不宣，胡雪岩只问：“带多少？”“给她二三百两银子吧！”“知道了，我替你垫付二百两，回来再算。”于是胡雪岩回家重整行装。第二天抽出工夫来，亲自上街买了好些茶食，去探望嵇鹤龄的子女，只见瑞云把那六个孩子料理得干干净净，心里大为宽慰。他跟嵇鹤龄拜把子的事，没有跟他的儿女说，却跟瑞云说了。正在谈着，来了位意想不到的“堂客”，是王太太。她的来意，胡雪岩明白，他没有理由妨碍她们谈正事，便笑笑走了。

“That's what we'll do,” Wang said. “When are you leaving?” “The day after tomorrow at the latest. I really must go.” “Good. Come back as soon as your business is finished.” Wang lowered his voice. “I'd like you to take some money for me.” For whom? Neither needed to say. Hu asked only, “How much?” “Give her two or three hundred taels.” “Understood. I'll advance two hundred and settle with you when I return.” Hu went home to repack. The next day he found time to buy a variety of tea snacks himself and visit Ji's children. Seeing all six clean and well cared for by Ruiyun greatly reassured him. He did not tell the children about his sworn brotherhood with their father, but he told Ruiyun. As they talked, an unexpected lady visitor arrived: Mrs Wang. Hu understood why she had come. He had no reason to obstruct their serious conversation, so he smiled and left.

一到松江，仍旧在出四鳃鲈的秀野桥上岸，胡雪岩没有带跟班，却有许多零零碎碎的行李，多是些杭州的土产，但他不怕照应不了。叫船家找了轿子和挑伕来，关照到通裕米行，那就连价钱都不用讲。因为“车、船、店、脚、牙”虽然难惹，却也十分开窍，通裕米行的后台是谁？码头上没有一个人不晓得，也没有一个人不买帐。到了通裕，却好遇见陈世龙在门口，一见面就说，“胡先生，我天天在盼望，为啥到今天才到？”“说来话长。”胡雪岩问道，“尤五哥在不在松江？”“昨天晚上刚从上海回来。”“好，进去再说。”

On reaching Songjiang, Hu again went ashore at Xiuye Bridge, famed for its four-gilled perch. He had brought no attendant, but carried many small pieces of luggage, mostly local products from Hangzhou. He had no fear of being unable to manage them. He asked the boatman to fetch a chair and porters and told them to go to Tongyu Rice Firm. There was no need even to bargain over the price. Carters, boatmen, innkeepers, porters, and brokers might be difficult people, but they understood how the world worked. Everyone at the wharf knew who stood behind Tongyu, and everyone respected his influence. Chen Shilong happened to be at the door when Hu arrived. “Mr Hu, I've been waiting for you every day! Why have you only just come?” “It's a long story. Is Brother You in Songjiang?” “He came back from Shanghai only last night.” “Good. Let's go inside and talk.”

通裕的人听见声音也迎了出来，代为开发轿子挑伕，把他奉为上宾，同时赶紧派人去通知尤五。“不必，不必！”明雪岩拦着他们说，“我去看尤五哥，跟他一起到老太爷那里请安。”说着，便检点土仪，叫陈世龙拿着跟了去。尤五家住得不远，不必再用轿马。陈世龙一面走，一面把到了松江以后的情形。扼要地报告，人是分开来往，陈世龙住在通裕，老张住在船上，阿珠就住在尤五家。胡雪岩心里明白，尤五仍旧当阿珠是他的心上人，所以特加礼遇，这且不去管她，他关心的是货色。“货色进上海丝栈了。”陈世龙说道，“是尤五叔作的主。堆在上海二洋径桥北大街的裕记丝栈，栈单在尤五叔那里，他要交给我，我不肯收。不过一张记数的单子，还在我手里。”陈世龙算是机警的，栈单在人家那里，他自己留着一张计数的单子，多少算个字佯。其实无用！粑栈单收了下来，原是正办，否则就索性大方到底。捏一张记数单子算是啥名堂？这是陈世龙做事不够老到，也正是自己要教导他的地方，但此时此地，不便多说，点点头就算了。

Hearing them, Tongyu's staff came out to welcome Hu. They settled with the chair-bearers and porters, treated him as an honoured guest, and hurried to send word to You. “No need!” Hu stopped them. “I'll call on Brother You and go with him to pay my respects to the old master.” He checked the gifts and told Chen to carry them and follow. You's house was near enough to require neither chair nor horse. As they walked, Chen briefly reported what had happened since their arrival in Songjiang. The party was staying separately: Chen at Tongyu, Old Zhang aboard the boat, and A’zhu at You's house. Hu understood that You still regarded her as his beloved and was therefore treating her with special courtesy. He put her aside for the moment; his concern was the goods. “The silk is in a warehouse in Shanghai,” Chen said. “Uncle You arranged it. It's at Yuji Silk Warehouse, on North Street by Eryangjing Bridge. He has the warehouse receipt. He offered it to me, but I wouldn't take it. I do have a tally of the quantities.” Chen showed some caution: while another man held the receipt, he kept a written tally as a form of evidence. Yet it was really useless. The proper course was to accept the warehouse receipt. Otherwise, he might as well show complete trust. What was the point of clinging to a tally? This showed Chen's inexperience, precisely the sort of thing Hu must teach him. But the time and place were unsuitable for a lecture, so he merely nodded.

到了尤五那里，只见高朋满座，胡雪岩方在踌躇，尤五已迎了出来，神情显得异常亲热。两个人拱拱手打过招呼，尤五拉着他的手问道：“我以为你还有几天才来。王大老爷的公事有了头绪没有？”他怎么知道王有龄的公事？看一看陈世龙，神态自如，显然不是他告诉尤五的。然则消息何以如此灵通？胡雪岩飞快地在心里转念头，同时口中答道：“有头绪了！不然我也抽不出身本。”“好的！回头我们细谈。”尤五把他拉到一边，低声说道：“厅里那班‘神道’，我不替你引见了。你懂？”胡雪岩一想就明白，很爽脆地签了一个字：“懂！”“那好。你先请到通裕去，等我‘送鬼出门，马上就来。”“不要紧，不要紧！我们在老太爷那里碰头好了。”“老太爷倒常提到你。我派人领了你去。”尤五又拍拍陈世龙的肩膀说：“这位小老弟也见过老太爷，蛮喜欢他的。”听得这句话，陈世龙脸上象飞了金一样：“那还不是看胡先生的面子。”他一半谦虚，一半说的也是实话。

They found You's house full of important visitors. Hu was hesitating when You came out with exceptional warmth. They exchanged clasped-hand greetings, and You took his hand. “I thought you wouldn't arrive for several more days. Has Mr Wang's official business begun to take shape?” How did he know about Wang's business? Hu glanced at Chen, whose expression was quite natural. Evidently Chen had not told him. How, then, was You so well informed? The question flashed through Hu's mind while he answered, “Yes. Otherwise I couldn't have got away.” “Good. We'll discuss it later.” You drew him aside and lowered his voice. “I won't introduce you to those ‘divinities’ in the hall. Do you understand?” Hu understood after a moment's thought and answered crisply, “Yes.” “Then go back to Tongyu first. I'll come as soon as I've seen these ‘ghosts’ out.” “No hurry. Let's meet at the old master's instead.” “He often speaks of you. I'll send someone to take you.” You patted Chen's shoulder. “This young fellow has met him too. He's quite fond of him.” Chen's face shone as though gilded. “Only out of regard for Mr Hu.” It was partly modesty and partly the truth.

于是由尤五派了人，陪看到他老头子那里。“老太爷”已经退隐，除了有关一般的大计以外，别的事都已不问，每天空下来的工夫，都在徒子徒孙陪侍闲谈中打发。最近兴致不佳，但见了胡雪岩却是十分高兴，这有许多原因，最主要的一点是，他觉得胡雪岩顶对劲。问过安，献上土仪，老太爷叫都打了开来，大部分是茶食之类的东西，他每佯都尝了些，不断说好。这样乱过一阵，算是坐定了，老太爷吩咐：“你们都到外头坐坐！我跟胡先生有话说。”摒人密谈的事。除非是对尤五，现在对一位远来的“空子”也是如此，大家不免诧异。不过也没有入敢问。一屋中十来个人，都静悄悄地退了出去。“雪岩！”老太爷扶着他说道，“最近我兴致很不好。兵荒马乱，着实有些担心，老五呢，能干倒能干，运气不好，轮着他挑这副担子，一天好日子都没有过过，我做老头子的，觉得对不起他。”“儿孙自有儿孙福！老太爷，你实在可以想开些，船到桥门自会直，凭五哥在外头的面子，无往不利，老太爷何必替小辈担心？”

A man sent by You accompanied them to Old Master Wei's house. The old man had retired and concerned himself only with matters affecting the brotherhood as a whole. Most of his leisure passed in conversation with disciples of various generations. He had been out of spirits lately, but Hu's arrival delighted him. There were several reasons, the chief one being that Hu suited him perfectly. After receiving Hu's greetings and gifts, he ordered everything opened. Most were tea snacks and similar delicacies. He tried a little of each and repeatedly praised them. Once the bustle had subsided and they were seated, he instructed the others, “Go outside for a while. I have something to discuss with Mr Hu.” Usually only You was admitted to such private conversations. To dismiss everyone for a visiting outsider surprised them, but no one dared ask why. The dozen or so people in the room withdrew silently. “Xueyan,” the old man said, resting a hand on him, “I've been very low lately. With war and disorder everywhere, there's much to worry about. Old Five is capable enough, but his luck is poor. Since it became his turn to bear this burden, he hasn't had a single easy day. As his old master, I feel I've failed him.” “The younger generation has its own good fortune! You can afford to take an easier view. A boat straightens as it reaches the bridge. With Brother Five's standing outside, he can manage anything. Why worry so much about the younger men?”

“江湖上总还好说，官面上事，再是朝廷的圣旨，叫他有啥法子？雪岩，你倒想想我们的处境！”胡雪岩明白，这是指漕米改为海运，漕帮有解体之危。这件事，他当初也想过，打算尽点心，部为接二连三地有所发展，忙得连想这件事的工夫都没有。所以这时一听老太爷的话，内心立即泛起浓重的歉仄。“现在做官的人，不是我说句看不起他们的话，‘ 江西人补碗，自顾自’，妻财子禄最要紧！不然，不会弄成今天这样子的局面……”老太爷大发了一顿牢骚，说的却是实话。这胡雪岩心里也很明白，是对漕米海运有所不满，或者说，不替漕帮谋善后之策有所不满。不过他觉得这件事也不能完全怪官府，但这话此时不便说，说也无益，所以保持着沉默，要等弄清了他的意思再作道理。

“Among men of the waterways, perhaps. But when it comes to officials—and an imperial edict at that—what can he do? Xueyan, consider our position!” Hu understood. The transfer of grain transport to the sea route threatened the brotherhood's very existence. He had thought about this before and meant to help, but one development after another had kept him too busy even to consider it. The old man's words immediately filled him with remorse. “Officials nowadays—I don't mean to disparage them—are like the Jiangxi bowl-mender in the saying: each looks after himself. Wife, wealth, children, and salary come first! Otherwise things would never have reached this state...” The old man poured out his grievances, but there was truth in what he said. Hu understood that his anger concerned the sea transport of grain, or rather the failure to make provision for the brotherhood afterwards. Yet Hu felt the authorities could not bear all the blame. This was neither the moment to say so nor likely to do any good. He remained silent until he could discover what the old man wanted, then decide how to respond.

“现在能替朝廷和老百姓办事的人，不是我恭维你，实在只有象你老弟这样的人！”老太爷又说，“王大老爷的官声，我也有点晓得，算是明白事理，肯做事的官。为此，我有句话想跟老弟你说！”“是的，老太爷尽管吩咐，漕帮都是我的好朋友，效得上劳的地方，我当我自己的事一样。”“所以我要跟你谈，除了你够朋友、重义气以外，还有一层，你见得事明，决不会弄错我的意思。老弟，”老太爷凑过头来，低声说道：“一个人总要放他条路走，狗急跳墙，人急悬梁，何况我们漕帮的情形，你是晓得的，好说话很好说话，不好说话也着实难弄。事情总要预先铺排，等抓破了脸，再想来摆平，交关吃力，雪岩，王大老爷还兼着海运局差使，请你劝劝他，不要顾前不顾后，替我们漕帮弟兄也要想一想。”

“I'm not flattering you, but the people who can actually serve the court and the common people now are men like you,” the old master continued. “I know something of Mr Wang's official reputation too. He's a reasonable man, willing to work. That's why there's something I want to say to you.” “Please give me your instructions. The brotherhood's men are all my good friends. Wherever I can help, I'll treat their business as my own.” “That's why I want to speak to you. Besides being a good friend who values loyalty, you see things clearly and won't misunderstand me.” He leaned closer and lowered his voice. “A man must always be left a way out. A desperate dog leaps a wall; a desperate man may hang himself. And you know our brotherhood: when we're amenable, we're very amenable; when we're not, we're hard to manage. Arrangements must be made beforehand. Once relations have broken down, restoring peace is painfully difficult. Xueyan, Mr Wang still holds the Sea Transport Bureau appointment. Ask him not to look only at what lies ahead and forget what follows behind. He must spare a thought for our brethren too.”

这番话听得胡雪岩暗暗心惊，看样子漕帮内部怨气冲天，一旦纸包不住火，烧开来会成燎原之势。局势已经够乱了，听说太平天国跟洪门有关，如果再加上“安庆”一起起事，越发不得了。做生意总要市面平靖，而市面的平靖，不能光靠官府，全需大家同心协力。胡雪岩一向有此想法，所以听了老太爷的话，细想一想其中的利害关系，自觉义不容辞，有替漕帮好好出番力的必要。于是他很郑重地说道：“你老人家的话，也不光是顾自己，是为地方着想。一条运河，从南到北，没有什么省界好分，只要我用得上力，一定效劳。”“对呀！”老太爷拍拍他的背说，“所以我说你‘见得事明’，晓得休戚相关，不分彼此，事情就好办了。”

Hu was inwardly alarmed. Resentment within the brotherhood must be running high. Once the paper could no longer contain the fire, it might spread across the plains. The situation was chaotic enough already. He had heard that the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom had links with the Hongmen society. If the Anqing brotherhood rose as well, matters would be still more terrible. Trade required peaceful conditions, and peace could not depend on the authorities alone: everyone must work together. Hu had always thought so. Considering the interests involved, he now felt duty-bound to make a serious effort for the brotherhood. “Your words aren't merely for your own benefit,” he said solemnly. “You're thinking of the whole region. The canal runs from south to north; provincial boundaries cannot divide its concerns. Wherever I can be useful, I shall help.” “Exactly!” The old man patted his back. “That's why I say you see clearly. Once we understand that our fortunes are linked and cease dividing ourselves from others, matters become easier.”

“那么，老太爷，你请吩咐，要我回去怎么说？”老太爷略想一想答道：“第一，时世不同了，海运当然也有好处，不过河运也不是一点用处都没有。请你跟王大老爷说，河运能维持还要维持。”这意思是漕米不必尽改海运，要求也不算过分。胡雪岩点点头说：“这应该办得到的。”“第二，”老太爷又说，“漕帮的运丁，总该有个安置的办法。王大老爷也该替我们说说话。”这更是义不容辞的事，“一定，一定！”胡雪岩满口答应，“一定会说。”“我晓得你老弟是有肩胛的。”老太爷拱拱手说，“做官的不大晓得底下的苦楚，难得有你老弟承上启下，可以替我们通条路子，拜托，拜托！我替我们一帮磕头。”

“Then tell me what I should say when I return.” The old master thought briefly. “First, times have changed. Sea transport certainly has advantages, but canal transport isn't entirely useless. Ask Mr Wang to preserve it wherever possible.” He meant that not all grain shipments need be transferred to the sea route. The request was not excessive. Hu nodded. “That ought to be possible.” “Second, some provision must be made for the brotherhood's transport workers. Mr Wang should speak for us on that point too.” This was still more clearly a duty. “Certainly!” Hu agreed readily. “He certainly will.” “I know you can shoulder responsibility.” The old master clasped his hands. “Officials understand little of the hardships below. How fortunate that someone like you can connect those above and those below and open a path for us. I entrust this to you! I bow in thanks on behalf of our whole association.”

“老太爷这后言重了！”胡雪岩又说，“不过，我倒有句话，怕不中听。”“你尽管说。”“我在想，漕帮自己也该寻条生路，譬如‘屯田’可以整顿整顿。”“老弟这话，自然在道理上。不过，说到‘屯田’，真正是一言难尽，多少年下来，‘私卖’、‘私典’的不知道多少？松江独多‘挂户田’，所以成了‘疲帮’。““挂户田”这个名目，胡雪岩还是初次听到，因而老太爷替他作了一番解释。“屯田”原是官产，“屯丁”领来耕种。算是皇家的佃户，因此“屯丁”便有双重负担，一是向公家完纳正赋，再是论亩出银、津贴运了，名为“津银”，每亩银子一分到三、四分不等。所以名为“屯田”，其实比民田的负担还要重。这一来就有许多弊病出现，一种是“丁逃地荒”，一种是为土豪劣绅，或者卫所衙门的书办等类的人霸占，再有一种是私卖或者私典屯田——照律法讲，以“私典军田例”，买卖双方均须治罪，因此有了“挂户田”这个名目，就是买或典的人，仍旧在屯丁或运丁名下挂户，完粮纳税，成了有名无实。

“You do me too much honour! But there's something I'd like to say, though you may not welcome it.” “Go ahead.” “I think the brotherhood should seek a livelihood for itself too. For instance, could the garrison lands be put in order?” “You're right in principle. But those lands are a long story. Over the years, who knows how many have been privately sold or pledged? Songjiang has an exceptional amount of land held under other men's registered names. That's why ours has become an impoverished association.” Hu had never heard this term, so the old master explained. Garrison farmland had originally been public property assigned to registered cultivators, who were in effect the emperor's tenants. They therefore bore a double burden: first, the regular land tax payable to the authorities; second, a silver payment per mu to subsidise the transport workers. This was called transport-support silver and ranged from one fen to three or four fen of silver per mu. Thus, despite being called garrison land, it carried heavier obligations than ordinary private farmland. Many abuses followed. Some cultivators fled, leaving fields untended. Other land was seized by local bullies, unscrupulous gentry, or clerks in the garrison offices. Still more was privately sold or pledged. Under the law governing the unauthorised pledging of military land, both parties to such a transaction were punishable. Hence the practice of holding land under another's registered name: the purchaser or pledge-holder kept it entered under the original cultivator or transport worker and paid the taxes through that name, leaving the registered tenure a mere fiction.

“从雍正十三年到道光十八年，屯田清查过七次，其中什么毛病，上头都晓得，始终整顿不出一个名堂来。老弟，”老太爷双手一摊，“请你想想，朝廷都没法办的事，叫我们自己如何整顿？”“我懂了！”胡雪岩说，“屯田既成为漕帮一家，这事情反倒好办。”这话听来费解，还需胡雪岩补充说明。他认为田地是样“绊手绊脚的东西”，不知道多少人安土重迁，只为家乡有块田地舍不得丢下，不肯挺起胸来，去闯市面。松江漕帮的屯田如果有好处，屯丁、运丁或者会在本乡本土，你争我夺，事情就麻烦了。既然是个累，丢掉就丢掉，只要公家筹得了办法，改行就行，无所瞻顾争执，岂非反而省事？““老弟，真正要佩服你！”老太爷大为感叹，“英雄出少年，你的见解，实在高人一等。”

“Between the thirteenth year of Yongzheng and the eighteenth year of Daoguang, these lands were investigated seven times. The authorities know every abuse, yet they've never managed to put things in order.” The old man spread his hands. “Consider it: if the court can't deal with the problem, how are we to reform it ourselves?” “I understand!” Hu said. “Since the land has become a burden to the brotherhood, that actually makes things easier.” This sounded puzzling and required explanation. Hu regarded land as something that could tie a man's hands and feet. Countless people remained rooted in their native places, unwilling to leave a plot behind and venture boldly into business. If the Songjiang brotherhood's lands offered real benefits, cultivators and transport workers might fight over them at home, making matters troublesome. Since they were a burden, why not let them go? Provided the authorities could devise an arrangement, the men could change occupations without hesitation or disputes over what they were leaving. Wouldn't that make things simpler? “I truly admire you!” the old master exclaimed. “Heroes arise among the young. Your insight is far above the ordinary.”

说到这里，尤五闯了进来。老太爷便把刚才与胡雪岩的谈话，扼要地告诉了他。尤五很仔细地听着，但这只是表示“孝顺”，心里觉得这件事虽然重要，但有力无处使，只有听其自然，至少在眼前来说是不急之务。因而答了句：“我跟小爷叔慢慢商量。”就把话扯开去了。扯的是闲话，说阿珠在他家作客，跟他家内眷如何投缘。胡雪岩自然要客气几句。他从话锋中听出来，尤五似乎有事要跟他老头子谈，说闲话便有碍着自己在座的意思在内，因而很知趣地站起身来，说先回通裕休息，等尤五来一起吃饭，商量生意。话还没有完，尤五就拉住他说：“小爷叔，你等一等。我跟老太爷稍为说两句话，一起走。”“好的，那么我在外面坐一坐。”“不必！”老太爷对尤五说，“你小爷叔不是外人，有话不必避他。”“不是我避小爷叔。我们是无法，人家找到头上，不能把耳朵遮起来。小爷叔不相干的人，何必让他也晓得？眼不见，心不烦，多好呢！““这话也是。那么，雪岩，你就到外面坐一坐！”老太爷提高了声音说：“来个人啊！陪客人去看看我的兰花。”

At this point You came in. The old master briefly recounted his conversation with Hu. You listened very attentively, but this was chiefly a display of filial respect. He thought the matter important yet impossible to influence; they could only let events take their course. At least for the present, it was not urgent. “I'll discuss it with Young Uncle in due course,” he answered, then changed the subject. He spoke casually of A’zhu's stay and how well she got on with the women of his household. Hu naturally expressed his thanks. From the turn of the conversation, he sensed that You had something to discuss with his old master and that the small talk concealed an awkwardness about Hu's presence. Taking the hint, Hu rose and said he would rest at Tongyu until You joined him for a meal and a discussion of business. Before he had finished, You caught his arm. “Wait a moment, Young Uncle. I only need a few words with the old master. Then we'll go together.” “Very well. I'll sit outside.” “No need,” the old master told You. “Your Young Uncle is no stranger. You needn't keep anything from him.” “It isn't that I wish to keep it from him. We have no choice: when people bring things to us, we can't stop our ears. But he has no part in this. Why make him know about it? What he doesn't see needn't trouble him.” “That's true. Then sit outside for a while, Xueyan.” The old man raised his voice. “Someone come and show our guest my orchids!”

老太爷养了好几百盆“建兰”，有专人替他照料，就由这个人陪着胡雪岩去看兰花。一花一叶，都能谈出好些名堂来。胡雪岩没有那么雅，敷衍着混辰光，心里只在想，是什么机密而又麻烦的大事，尤五看得如此郑重？想到尤五在他自己家所说的“送鬼出门”这句话，胡雪岩恍然了。那班“神道”大概是“小刀会”的，不然亦必与刘丽川有关。一想到此，又惊又喜。惊的是这要“造反”，尤五和他老头子不要被牵涉了进去，喜的是小刀会的情形，尤五都知道，避凶趋吉，对自己的生意，大有益处。只要益处，不要坏处！他在心里说，这件事倒要跟尤五好好商量一下。好久，尤五才跟老太爷谈完话出来，于是招呼了陈世龙一起出门。“小爷叔，”他问，“你是到我那里，还是到通裕？通裕比较静，谈天方便。”话中的意思是，到他家便可以先跟阿珠见面。在这时来说，无此必要，所以毫不迟疑地答道，“到通裕好了。我有好些话要跟你一个人谈。”因为有这样的暗示，所以到了通裕，只有他们两个人把杯密谈。

The old master kept several hundred pots of jianlan orchids, tended by a specialist. This man escorted Hu round them, finding much to say about every flower and leaf. Hu had no such refined interest. He responded politely to pass the time, thinking only of the confidential and troublesome business You regarded so gravely. Then he remembered You's phrase about seeing the “ghosts” out and understood. Those “divinities” were probably members of the Small Swords Society, or at least connected with Liu Lichuan. The thought both alarmed and pleased him. Alarmed him because this meant rebellion: he hoped neither You nor the old master would be drawn in. Pleased him because You knew what the Small Swords were doing. Information that helped him avoid danger and seize favourable chances could greatly benefit his trade. Benefits without harm! He must discuss that carefully with You. Only after a long while did You emerge. They called Chen and left together. “Young Uncle, shall we go to my house or Tongyu? Tongyu is quieter and better for talking.” Going to You's house would mean seeing A’zhu first. Hu felt no need for that just now. “Tongyu,” he answered without hesitation. “I have much to discuss with you alone.” Taking the hint, You arranged matters so that only the two of them sat drinking and talking privately there.

“你的货色，我代为作主进丝栈。栈单交了给你！”尤五首先交代这件事。栈单在胡雪岩手里有许多花佯好耍，起码也可以作为表示实力和信用的凭证，因而他不必作不必要的客气，接过来放在一边。“这家丝栈跟我也熟。栈租特别克己。不过你能早脱手，还是早脱手的好，丝摆下去会变黄，价钱上就要吃亏了。”“五哥说得不错。不过，”胡雪岩停了一下说：“我现在又有了新主意，要跟你商量。”“这上面我不大懂，且不管它，你先讲出来再说。”“五哥跟洋行里很熟？”“是的。是不是要我介绍洋商？”“还不止这一层。另外。我有句话，不知道该不该问？如果不该问，五哥老实不客气告诉我。自己弟兄，千万不要存丝毫不好意思的心。”

“I took the decision to put your goods in a silk warehouse. Here is the receipt.” You began by settling that matter. In Hu's hands, the receipt could serve many purposes. At the least it was evidence of his resources and credit. There was no need for empty politeness: he accepted it and laid it aside. “I know the warehouse well,” You continued. “They're charging a specially low rate. But if you can sell early, do so. Silk yellows when it lies in store, and the price suffers.” “You're right, Brother Five. But...” Hu paused. “I have a new idea to discuss with you.” “I don't understand this business particularly well, but never mind. Tell me first.” “You know the foreign trading houses well?” “Yes. Do you want introductions to foreign merchants?” “More than that. There's another question I'm not sure I ought to ask. If it's improper, tell me plainly. We're brothers: there must be no awkwardness between us.”

“我晓得了！‘光棍心多，麻布筋多’，有时候，我不能不顾忌。不过对你不同。”尤五这时对胡雪岩的看法，跟刚才又不同了，“老头子跟我说，说你的见解，着实高明，有许多事，是江湖道上的人见不到的。”多谢他老人家的夸奖，说句实话，我别的长处没有，第一自觉从未做过对不起朋友的事，第二，事情轻重出入，我极清楚。所以我那句也许不该问的话，五哥你大可放心。“他这是一再表示不会泄密，尤五“光棍玲珑心”，自然会意，心想何必等你问出来？我先告诉你，不显得漂亮些吗？于是他说：“你要问的，就是你今天在我那里看见的那班‘神道’？”“对了。”胡雪岩很严肃地点着头，“你是为我好，叫我‘眼不见，心不烦’。而我呢，另有生意上的打算。”尤五不即回答，慢慢喝了口酒，夹了一块鱼干在嘴里嚼了半天，然后吐掉了渣滓说话。“我不晓得你在生意上有什么打算。这件事，我老实告诉你好了，小刀会就这几天要起事，他们来请我‘入伙’，我决定随他们自己去搞。”

“I understand. ‘A man of the world has many thoughts, as coarse cloth has many fibres.’ Sometimes I can't avoid being cautious. But you're different.” You now thought more highly of Hu than even a little earlier. “The old master said your insight was exceptional. You see things that men of our world can't.” “I thank him for the compliment. Frankly, I claim no other virtues, but first, I've never knowingly failed a friend; second, I understand very clearly the weight and implications of a matter. So you needn't worry about this question I perhaps ought not to ask.” Hu was repeatedly assuring him that he would keep a confidence. You's worldly intelligence understood. Why wait to be asked? Wouldn't volunteering the information be more generous? “You want to ask about those ‘divinities’ you saw at my house today?” “Exactly.” Hu nodded gravely. “For my sake you wanted to spare me the trouble of knowing. But I have a business plan in mind.” You did not reply immediately. He slowly drank some wine, chewed a piece of dried fish for a long while, spat out the residue, and then spoke. “I don't know what your business plan is. But I'll tell you plainly about this. The Small Swords will rise within the next few days. They came to invite me to join them. I've decided to let them do it on their own.”

果然是这么回事！“五哥，”胡雪岩先敬一杯酒，“你这个主意捏得好！跟他们一起浑水，实在犯不着。““主意是容易捏，做起来不容易，浑水要泼到你身上，要躲掉也蛮难的。”这表示尤五虽未“入伙”，但也不便反对他们。胡雪岩了解他的难处，不了解的是小刀会的作为，“那么，五哥，我还有句话请问。”他说，你看那班会不会成气候？““这很难说。有外国人夹在里头，事情就难弄了。”“怎么？”胡雪岩一惊，“还有外国人插手？”“那是刘丽川的关系。”“照这样说，夷场里是一定不会乱的？”“外国人跟刘丽川打交道，就是为了保夷场的平安。”尤五答道：“不然我为什么要把你的丝送进夷场的丝栈？”胡雪岩不作声，默默地把他的话细想了一遍，觉得又是一个绝好的机会到了。

Just as Hu had thought! He first offered You a toast. “You've made the right decision, Brother Five. It isn't worth entering those muddy waters with them.” “Deciding is easy. Acting on it isn't. When muddy water is splashed at you, it's hard to keep clear.” This meant that although You had not joined them, he could scarcely oppose them either. Hu understood his difficulty. What he did not understand was the Small Swords' potential. “Then let me ask one more thing. Do you think they can become a real force?” “Hard to say. With foreigners mixed up in it, things become complicated.” “What?” Hu was startled. “Foreigners are involved too?” “That's through Liu Lichuan.” “Then the foreign settlements are certain to remain peaceful?” “The foreigners deal with Liu precisely to preserve order in the settlements,” You replied. “Why else would I have sent your silk to a warehouse there?” Hu said nothing. He carefully considered these words and felt that another excellent opportunity had arrived.

这个好机会自然要与尤五分享，而且事实上也不能不靠他的力量。因此，胡雪岩这佯说道：“五哥，照我的看法，小刀会一起事，不是三、五个月可以了事的，丝的来路会断，洋庄价钱看好，我们可以趁此赚它一票。”“我倒真想赚它一票。”尤五答说，“帮里越来越穷，我肩上这副担子，越来越吃力。就不知道怎么赚怯？你说买丝囤在那里，等洋庄价钱好了再卖，这我也懂。不过，你倒说说看，本钱呢？”最大的困难，就是本钱。胡雪岩已经有了成算，但需要先打听一下尤五这方面的情形，“你能调多少？”他问，“先说个有把握的数目，代们再来商量。”“ ‘三大’的十万银子，我已经转了一期，不能再转了！眼前我先要凑这笔款子，哪里还谈得到别的？”“那么，这笔借款上，你已经凑到了多少？”“还只有一半。”“一半就是五万。”胡雪岩问：“三天之内你还能调多少？”“最多再调两万。”“那就是七万。好了，你只管去调，‘三大’转期，归我来想办法。”

Naturally he must share the opportunity with You. In fact, he could not proceed without You's influence. “Brother Five, as I see it, once the Small Swords rise, the affair won't be over in three or five months. Silk supplies will be cut off, and export prices will strengthen. We can make a good profit.” “I'd certainly like to,” You answered. “The association grows poorer, and the burden on my shoulders grows heavier. I only wish I knew how. Buying silk, holding it, and selling when the export price rises—I understand that too. But tell me, where's the capital?” Capital was the chief difficulty. Hu already had a plan, but first he needed to know You's position. “How much can you raise? Give me a figure you're sure of, and we'll discuss it.” “I've already renewed the hundred-thousand-tael loan from the Big Three once. I can't renew it again. My immediate task is to raise the repayment. How could I think of anything else?” “How much have you raised towards it?” “Only half.” “Half means fifty thousand. How much more could you find within three days?” “Twenty thousand at most.” “Then that's seventy thousand. Good. Go ahead and raise it. Leave the renewal with the Big Three to me.”

胡雪岩接着又问：“有件事我不大明白，洋行里可能做押款？”“这倒没有听说过。”“那么请五哥去打听一下。”胡雪岩说，“我们本钱虽少，生意还是可以做得很热闹，这有两个办法。”他的两个办法是这样：第一，他预备把存在裕记丝栈的货色作抵押，向洋行借款，把“栈革”化成现银，在上海就地收货。如果洋行借不到，再向钱庄去接头。“慢慢！”尤五打断他的话说，“你的服筋倒动得不错，不过我就不明白，为啥不直接向钱庄做押款呢？”胡雪岩笑了，略有些不好意思地说，“五哥，我要拿那张栈单变个戏法。”他低声说道：“ ‘三大，那面的款子转期，要有个说法，就说我有笔款子划给你，不过要等我的丝脱手，才能料理清楚。栈单给他们瞧一瞧，货色又在丝栈里不曾动，他们自然放心。哪晓得我的栈单已经抵押了出去？”尤五也笑了：“你真厉害！做生意哪个都弄不过你。”他说，“我懂了！反正栈单不能流入钱庄，戏法才不会揭穿。如果洋行那方面不行，只要有东西，我在私人方面亦可以商量。“

Hu went on to ask, “There's something I don't quite understand. Do foreign trading houses lend money against goods?” “I've never heard of it.” “Then please make inquiries, Brother Five. Even with little capital, we can do a very lively business. There are two ways.” His first proposal was to pledge the goods in the Yuji Silk Warehouse for a loan from a foreign firm, turn the warehouse receipt into ready silver, and buy silk locally in Shanghai. If no foreign firm would lend, he would approach the banks. “Wait!” You interrupted. “That's a clever idea, but why not go directly to a bank for the loan?” Hu smiled, a little sheepishly. “Brother Five, I want to perform a conjuring trick with that receipt.” Lowering his voice, he explained, “We need a reason for asking the Big Three to renew your loan. We can say I have a sum to transfer to you, but can't settle it until my silk is sold. Show them the receipt, with the goods still untouched in the warehouse, and they'll naturally feel reassured. How are they to know I've already pledged the receipt elsewhere?” You laughed too. “You're formidable! No one can beat you at business. I understand. The receipt must stay out of banking circles if the trick is to remain hidden. If the foreign firms won't do it, I can try private lenders, so long as there's something to pledge.”

“那就再好不过了。我再说第二个办法。”第二办法，一直是胡雪岩的理想，丝商联合起来跟洋行打交道，然后可以制人而非制于人，这个理想当然不是一蹴可就，而眼前不妨试办，胡雪岩的打算是用尤五的关系和他自己的口才，说服在上海的同行——预备销洋庄的“丝客人”，彼此台作。“这又有两个办法，第一个，我们先付定金，或者四分之一，或者三分之一，货色就归我们，等半年以后付款提货。价钱上通扯起来，当然要比他现在就脱手来得划算，人家才会点头。”第二个办法是联络所有的丝客人，相约不卖，由他们去向洋人接头讲价，成交以后，抽取佣金。胡雪岩讲得很仔细，尤五也听得很用心。耳中在听，心里在算，照胡雪岩的办法，十万银子就可以做五十万银子的生意，赚来的钱对分，每人有五万银子，加上已经在手里的五万，恰好可以还“三大”的借款。他不能不动心。“小爷叔！”他说，“你的算盘真精明，我准定跟你搭伙。我们啥时候动身到上海？”“你看呢？”胡雪岩答道。“在我是越快越好。”“最快也得明天。”“就是明天。一言为定。”

“Nothing could be better. Now for the second way.” Hu had long cherished the idea of uniting silk merchants to deal with foreign trading houses, so that they could dictate terms instead of having terms dictated to them. Such an ambition could not be achieved at a stroke, but they might begin on a small scale. With You's connections and his own powers of persuasion, he meant to win the cooperation of fellow silk dealers in Shanghai whose goods were intended for export. “Here again, there are two possible arrangements. First, we pay a deposit of a quarter or a third, and the goods are reserved for us. Six months later we pay the balance and collect them. Taking the price as a whole, the seller must of course get a better bargain than by selling now, or he won't agree.” The other arrangement was to bring all the dealers together and secure their agreement to withhold their silk from sale. Hu and You would then negotiate prices with the foreigners and take a commission on concluded deals. Hu explained carefully, and You listened closely, calculating as he listened. By Hu's method, a hundred thousand taels could finance trade worth five hundred thousand. If they divided the profits equally, each would receive fifty thousand. Added to the fifty thousand already in You's hands, that would exactly repay his loan from the Big Three. He could hardly remain indifferent. “Young Uncle, you calculate brilliantly. I'll certainly join you. When shall we leave for Shanghai?” “What do you think? For me, the sooner the better.” “Tomorrow at the earliest.” “Tomorrow it is. Agreed.”

谈完正事谈闲天。尤五提到阿珠，笑着问他何时纳宠，预备送礼。“你弄错了！”胡雪岩答了这一句，又觉得话没有说对，“也不是你弄错。实在是哪个也不晓得我的心思。五哥，我倒要先问你一句话，你看阿珠为人如何？自己人，不必说客气话。”“人是好的，脾气好象很刚。说句实话，这种小姐要嫁给肯闯市面的小伙子，倒是好帮手，嫁了给你，”尤五忽然问道：“嫂夫人的脾气怎么样？”“内人的脾气，说好也不好，说坏也不坏。这也不去管她，反正跟阿珠不相干的了。”“小爷叔，你这话奇怪了！”尤五诧异地，“听你的口气，不预备把她讨回去。可是她跟内人无话不谈，说你已经答应她在湖州另立门户。这不是两面的话对不上榫头吗？”“是的。这件事我不知道做得对不对呢？我说出来，五哥，你倒替我想一想。”于是他把准备移花接木，有劝阿珠嫁陈世龙的打算，细细说了给尤五听。

Business settled, they turned to lighter talk. You mentioned A’zhu and asked with a smile when Hu would take her as a concubine, so that he could prepare a present. “You've misunderstood!” Hu answered, then felt that this was not quite right. “No, it isn't that you've misunderstood. The truth is that no one knows what's in my mind. First let me ask you something, Brother Five. What do you think of A’zhu's character? We're among ourselves; there's no need for polite phrases.” “She's a good girl, though she seems rather strong-tempered. Frankly, a girl like that would be a fine helpmate for a young fellow willing to make his way in the world. Married to you...” You suddenly asked, “What is your wife's temper like?” “I couldn't call it good, but I couldn't call it bad either. Never mind her. In any case, she won't concern A’zhu.” “Young Uncle, that's a strange thing to say!” You exclaimed. “You sound as if you don't intend to marry the girl. Yet she tells my wife everything and says you've promised to establish a separate household for her in Huzhou. Those two accounts don't fit together.” “True. I don't know whether I'm doing the right thing. Let me tell you, Brother Five, and you can help me think it through.” He then explained in detail his plan to redirect A’zhu's affections and persuade her to marry Chen Shilong.

“原来如此！”尤五笑道，“小爷叔，你不但银钱上算盘精明，做人的算盘也精明。不错！陈世龙这位小老弟是有出息的。我赞成你的主意。”“那好！我一起想找个人谈谈，不知道我的想法，是不是‘一厢情愿’？既然你赞成，那就准定这么做了。“尤五一时高兴，随即自告奋勇：“这件事虽好，做起来不容易，她一心一意在你身上，忽然要叫她抛掉，难得很。要不要我来帮忙？”这是好意，胡雪岩没有拒绝的道理，“当然要的。”他问，“就不知道怎么想法？”“我不是跟你说过，她跟内人无话不谈，要不要内人来做个媒呢？”“这再好都没有。不过……”胡雪岩说，“这件事急不得。”尤五一听懂了，这是变相的辞谢，所以点点头说：“好的！那么等一等再看，只要用得着，随时效劳。”

“So that's it!” You said with a laugh. “Young Uncle, you're as shrewd at managing people as you are at managing money. Yes, young Chen Shilong has a future. I approve of your idea.” “Good! I've wanted someone to talk it over with, to discover whether it was only wishful thinking on my part. Since you approve, I've decided to go ahead.” In his enthusiasm, You immediately volunteered his services. “It's a good idea, but it won't be easy. Her whole heart is set on you. Getting her to give you up all of a sudden will be very difficult. Would you like my help?” The offer was well meant; Hu had no reason to refuse. “Of course. But how would you go about it?” “Haven't I told you she confides everything to my wife? Shall I ask my wife to act as matchmaker?” “That would be excellent. Only...” Hu added, “this is something we mustn't rush.” You understood at once: this was a tactful refusal. He nodded. “Very well. We'll wait and see. Whenever you need me, I'm at your service.”

“言重了！”胡雪岩忽然又改了主意，“我想请嫂夫人先探探她口气，一路上觉得陈世龙怎么样？如果她认为他不错，那就请嫂夫人进一步劝一劝。看她是何话说？”“不是这样说法！”尤五摇摇头。这下，胡雪岩倒有些不大服帖了，难道以自己对阿珠的了解，还会不知道该如何着手？于是他问：“那么，该怎么说呢？”“第一步就要让她晓得，她给人做小是委屈的，第二步要让她晓得，给你做小，将来未见得舒服。”想想不错，胡雪岩服帖了，“我是当局者迷。”他拱拱手说，“完全拜托，这件事我就要丢开了。”丢开了这件事，他才能专心一意去做他的丝生意，尤五心想，此事非把它办成不可，不然会分他的心，彼此的利害，都有关系。

“You're too kind!” Hu suddenly changed his mind again. “I'd like your wife to sound her out first. What did she think of Chen Shilong on the journey? If she thinks well of him, your wife could go a little further and try persuading her. Then we'll see what she says.” “That isn't the way to put it!” You shook his head. Hu felt somewhat unconvinced. Surely, knowing A’zhu as he did, he knew how to approach her? “Then how should it be put?” he asked. “First, she must understand that becoming anyone's concubine would be unfair to her. Next, she must understand that becoming yours might not give her a comfortable life.” On reflection, Hu saw that he was right. “I'm too involved to see clearly.” He clasped his hands respectfully. “I leave it entirely to you. I shall put the matter out of my mind.” Only when that concern was off his mind could he devote himself wholly to the silk trade. You thought that they simply must bring the affair to a successful conclusion. Otherwise it would distract Hu, and both their interests were at stake.

于是当天回家，就跟他妻子作了一番密商。话刚说完，看见阿珠从窗外经过，便喊住她说：“张小姐，我有句话告诉你。”阿珠自以为胡雪岩的人，所以跟他用一样的称呼，叫一声“五哥！”接着便走了进来，挨着“五嫂”一起坐下。在她面前，尤五却不叫胡雪岩为“小爷叔”，他说：“雪岩托我告诉你一声，他今天不来看你了，因为晚上还有一件事要料理。”阿珠自然失望，不过心里在想：他事情多，应该原谅他。所以点点头，“我晓得了。”“他明天动身，我跟他一起走。走以前，恐惶也没有工夫跟你见面。”这话就奇怪了：“我们不是一起到上海吗？”“不！”尤五答道：“他的意思，让你住在我这里。”“你就住在我们这里。”尤太太拉一拉她的手，接着她丈夫的话说，“过几天我也要到上海，你跟我去，我们去玩我们的。”阿珠一泡泪，忍住在眼眶里。越是居停情重，越觉得胡雪岩可恶。看起来他有些变心了！

Returning home that day, You held a private consultation with his wife. They had just finished when A’zhu passed the window. He called her in. “Miss Zhang, I have something to tell you.” Considering herself Hu's woman, A’zhu used his form of address. “Brother Five!” she said, and entered to sit beside her Fifth Sister-in-Law. In her presence, however, You did not refer to Hu as “Young Uncle.” “Xueyan asked me to tell you that he won't be visiting today. He has something to attend to this evening.” A’zhu was naturally disappointed, but she told herself that he had many things to do and ought to be forgiven. She nodded. “I understand.” “He's leaving tomorrow, and I'm going with him. I'm afraid he won't have time to see you before we leave, either.” This was strange. “Aren't we all going to Shanghai together?” “No,” You replied. “He means you to stay here with us.” “Stay here,” Mrs You added, taking her hand. “In a few days I'm going to Shanghai too. You can come with me, and we'll enjoy ourselves.” A’zhu held back the tears welling in her eyes. The kinder her hosts were, the more hateful Hu seemed. It looked as though his feelings for her were changing!

“张小姐，明天一早，我就要跟他碰头，你有什么话要跟他说？我替你转到。”“没有！”阿珠因为负气，语气很硬，说出口来，自己觉得很不应该这样子对尤五，因而赶紧又用很漫柔的声音说：“谢谢你，五哥！我没有什么话想跟他说。”“好！我就把你这句话说给他听。”这下，阿珠又有些不安了，她自己负气，甚至于见着胡雪岩的面，想骂他几句，但不愿旁人把她的气话传来传去。不过她也弄不懂尤五的意思，不便再有所表示，只问：“我爹和陈世龙呢？他们是不是一起走。”“当然。上海有许多事情在那里，人手不够，他们怎好不去。”“好的。那我明天到船上去看我爹。”她已打定了主意，明天到了船上，总可以遇见胡雪岩，一定要拿点颜色给他看，是怎样的颜色，她却还不知道，得要慢慢去想了再说。

“Miss Zhang, I'm meeting him early tomorrow. Is there anything you'd like me to tell him? I'll pass it on.” “No!” A’zhu's anger made her answer sharply. As soon as she spoke, she felt it was wrong to treat You that way. She hastily softened her voice. “Thank you, Brother Five. There's nothing I want to say to him.” “Very well. I'll tell him exactly that.” Now she felt uneasy again. She was angry and even wanted to scold Hu to his face, but she did not want other people carrying her angry words back and forth. Nor could she make out what You meant, so she could hardly say more. She merely asked, “What about my father and Chen Shilong? Are they leaving too?” “Of course. There's a great deal to do in Shanghai, and we're short of people. How could they stay behind?” “All right. Then I'll go to the boat tomorrow to see my father.” Her mind was made up. Once aboard, she would surely meet Hu, and she would teach him a lesson. What sort of lesson she had not yet decided. She would have to think it over.

“天气真热！”尤太太拉着她的手站了起来，“我们到亭子里乘凉去。”尤家后园，小有花木之胜，还有一座假山，山上一座亭子，题名甚怪，叫做“不买亭”，大概是取“清凤明月不费一文钱买”的意思，但题名虽怪，亭子倒构筑得相当古朴，而且地势极好，登高远眺，绿野遥山，颇能赏心悦目。园子的围墙不高，假山上望得见行人，行人只望得见亭子里的鬓丝丽影。在谨饬的人家，这座亭子是不宜女眷登临的，但尤五家与众不同，女眷向不避人，而外人也不敢订尤家女眷什么主意，所以从阿珠来了以后，几乎每天晚上都随着尤太太在“不买亭”纳凉。

“What hot weather!” Mrs You rose, still holding her hand. “Let's cool off in the pavilion.” The You family's back garden had its share of pleasant flowers and trees, as well as an artificial hill with a pavilion on top. Its name was odd: the Bumai, or “No Purchase,” Pavilion, presumably because a clear breeze and a bright moon cost nothing to enjoy. Oddly named though it was, the pavilion had a pleasingly simple, old-fashioned design and an excellent position. From its height, the green fields and distant mountains made a delightful prospect. The garden wall was low. Those on the hill could see passers-by, while those passing below could glimpse only the women's hair and graceful silhouettes inside the pavilion. In a strictly proper household, the women would not have been allowed up there. You's family was different. Its women never hid themselves from view, and no outsider dared entertain designs on them. Since her arrival, A’zhu had therefore gone almost every evening with Mrs You to enjoy the cool air in the Bumai Pavilion.

经常在一起的，还有尤五的一个妹妹，行七，尤家都叫她“七姑奶奶”。七站奶奶早年居孀，与翁姑不和，住在娘家，三十岁左右，长得极艳，但坐在那里不讲话，是个绝色美人，一开口出来，会把胆小的男人吓走，因为她伉爽有须眉气概，而且江湖气极重，不独言词犀利，表情丰富，横眉瞪眼，杀气腾腾，最让男人吃不消的是，口没遮拦，骂人也是如此，什么“蠢话”都说得出口，所以她嫂子叫她“女张飞”。

They were often joined by You's younger sister, seventh among the children, whom the household called the Seventh Lady. Widowed young, she had quarrelled with her parents-in-law and returned to her own family. She was about thirty and strikingly beautiful. Sitting in silence, she was a woman of exceptional loveliness; once she opened her mouth, she could frighten timid men away. She had a man's bold, forthright manner and a strong flavour of the rougher world beyond respectable society. Her speech was sharp and her face expressive: she could lower her brows, glare, and look positively murderous. What men found hardest to bear was her utterly unguarded tongue, especially when she was abusing someone. There was no coarse expression she would not use. Her sister-in-law therefore called her “the female Zhang Fei,” after the famously fierce and outspoken warrior.

嵇鹤龄与胡雪岩结拜后，直接称呼使用 Elder Brother、Second Brother；“以字行”译为 known by his courtesy name，保留原帖载明的籍贯和嘉庆纪年，不据人物年龄擅改生日。尤五为 You Laowu，通常叙述中简称 You；五哥为 Brother Five，小爷叔为 Young Uncle。尤五所称“老头子”“老太爷”在帮会师承关系中处理为 old master，不误作他的生父。七姑奶奶为尤五的妹妹、家中排行第七，叙述用 the Seventh Lady；其嫂叙述为 Mrs You。“女张飞”译为 the female Zhang Fei，并简释人物豪爽粗直、说话无所顾忌的比喻。本段并未把七姑奶奶的身世或与尤五的关系另行推断成其他关系。

屯田、屯丁和运丁分别译作 garrison land、registered cultivators、transport workers，按本段解释区分土地、承种人及运输人员。“津银”译为 transport-support silver；mu、fen 和 tael 保留传统计量单位。文中一分至三四分为每亩银款，不折算现代币值。土地私典及挂户的叙述保留其名义登记与实际权益分离的逻辑。“安庆”出现在洪门与漕帮的组织语境，按原字保留为 Anqing brotherhood，不自行改作其他名称或解释成安庆城。相关制度与帮会判断均为小说叙述，不据外部史料校改。

裕记丝栈为 Yuji Silk Warehouse，栈单为 warehouse receipt；地点“二洋径桥北大街”按所给文字译为 North Street by Eryangjing Bridge，未擅自规范历史地名。“不买亭”译为 the Bumai, or “No Purchase,” Pavilion，保留清风明月不花钱的取名趣味。沪上“三大”沿用 the Big Three；小刀会为 the Small Swords Society，刘丽川为 Liu Lichuan。

原文识读

以下只影响英译理解，不改动所给中文原件：
“屯田既成为漕帮一家”结合下文“既然是个累”，按屯田已经成为帮会负担理解；字面显有脱讹，未强行补造具体原字。
“栈革”按同段反复出现的“栈单”理解；“服筋”按脑筋、想法理解；“彼此台作”按彼此合作理解；“我一起想找个人谈谈”按一直想找人谈谈理解。
“恐惶也没有工夫”按恐怕理解；“漫柔”按温柔理解；“清凤明月”按清风明月理解；“订尤家女眷什么主意”按打什么主意理解；“七站奶奶”按七姑奶奶理解。“蠢话”结合骂人、口无遮拦的上下文，译为 coarse expression，表达粗话而不新增具体辱骂。
第9299行“家里一点都不用记挂”指嵇鹤龄不必牵挂家中事务，胡雪岩承诺代为照顾，并非要求嵇鹤龄不要让家人担忧。
其他如“每佯”“没有入”“这后”等明显字形讹误按上下文顺读。原文关于十万本钱、五十万生意、各分五万利润及原有五万还款的数字全部保留；前段另称还能筹两万，不据此擅改后段人物的心算。筹资方案和预期盈利不等于叙事中已经完成的事实。

综合精读方向

阅读：比较王太太追问瑞云是否亲口同意，与胡雪岩根据神情推定她愿意的差别；区分明说、推断和人物自信。商业段可分别标出已掌握的信息、关于小刀会的消息、对丝价的预测和仍待落实的融资。

完形：留意 if、otherwise、provided、since、yet 等词怎样连接借款条件、商品抵押、贷款展期和销货预期。尤其注意“拿栈单作信用展示”和“栈单已经另作抵押”之间的隐含转折。

段落排序：本段有两层“两个办法”：先是抵押融资与联合丝商；联合丝商之下又分订金包货和统一议价抽佣。先画出层级，再根据 this、the other arrangement 和 first 等指代、次序信号复原段落，避免把不同层级混排。

翻译：练习把屯田双重负担、土地挂户和栈单流转等长句分解为“主体—行为—条件—结果”，再恢复自然英文。准确区分 loan、deposit、balance、commission、pledge、renew a loan 和 warehouse receipt；同时保留人物称谓、传统计量与数字关系。

写作：可借鉴产业变化时安置受影响群体、考虑他人处境、区分猜测与明确同意等论述主题；学习从具体观察推到判断，再说明条件与后果的句段结构。小说人物的自利计算、隐瞒信息等行为需要按人物立场阅读，不宜直接当作作文中应当赞许的原则。
