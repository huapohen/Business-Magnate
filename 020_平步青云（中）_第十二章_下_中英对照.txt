“我问你，”陈世龙又说，“你预备哪天到上海去？”“我哪里晓得，要看尤太太和七姑奶奶的意思。”“尤太太是靠不住的。他们家天天高朋满座，都靠尤太太招呼，又有孩子，哪里抽得出空来陪你到上海去？”“七姑奶奶有空。不过……”“不过你不大愿意跟她在一起！是不是？”“她人是好人，心直口快，可惜稍为过分了些。”阿珠苦笑着摇头，“真有些吃她不消。”陈世龙颇有同感，他也吃不消七姑奶奶。说起来也是好意，总拿他当兄弟看，但大庭广众之间，过于亲热，看起来仿佛情有所钟似地。陈世龙虽有些浪子的气质，因为身在客边，辈分又矮，怕惹出许多话，所以总避着她，这也就是他少到尤家去的原因。但以前可以少去，现在要在阿珠身上下功夫，不能不多去。去了又吃不消七姑奶奶，而且说不定会引起阿珠的误会，这倒是个难题。

“Tell me,” Chen Shilong continued, “when do you mean to go to Shanghai?” “How should I know? That depends on Mrs You and the Seventh Lady.” “You can't count on Mrs You. Their house is full of distinguished guests every day, and she has to look after them all. With the children as well, how could she find time to take you to Shanghai?” “The Seventh Lady has time. But...” “But you don't much care to be with her. Is that it?” “She's a good person, and she speaks her mind. Unfortunately, she carries it a little too far.” A’zhu shook her head with a rueful smile. “I really find her rather too much.” Chen sympathised. He too found the Seventh Lady overwhelming. Her intentions were kindly enough, and she always treated him as a younger brother, but in company she was so familiar that she seemed to have a special affection for him. Although Chen had something of the roving gallant in his nature, he was a guest here and junior in standing. Afraid of provoking gossip, he generally avoided her; that was why he seldom visited the You household. Previously, he could stay away. Now that he was courting A’zhu, he must visit more often. Yet the Seventh Lady would be difficult to contend with and might even cause A’zhu to misunderstand. This was a problem.

看他不说话，她觉得再坐下去也没有意思，便站起身来，把衣襟和下摆扯一扯平整，又掠一掠发鬓说道，“该回去了吧？”“再坐一下，我还有话说。”阿珠不即回答，心里在想，这一坐下来再谈，就决不是谈什么可有可无的闲天，他是在自己身上打主意，当然有些紧要的话要说。目己跟胡雪岩就是这样好起来的，前车不远，应当警惕，如果自己根据不容他打什么主意，那就不如趁早躲开。然而心里想得很明，那双脚却似钉住在地上，动弹不得。最后，终于糊里糊涂坐回原处。“我看你不必等尤太太和七姑奶奶了。过两天，我来接你。你看，好不好？”这也没有什么不好。只是一走容易，到了上海，不能好好玩一玩，反倒无趣，那得先问一问清楚。

When he said nothing, A’zhu felt there was little point in sitting there any longer. She rose, straightened the front and hem of her clothes, and smoothed her hair at the temples. “Shouldn't we be going back?” “Sit a little longer. I still have something to say.” She did not answer at once. If she sat down to talk again, it would certainly be more than idle conversation. He had designs on her and must have something consequential to say. Her closeness with Hu Xueyan had begun just this way. With that warning so recent, she ought to be careful. If she did not mean to encourage Chen at all, she had better leave at once. Yet although her thoughts were perfectly clear, her feet seemed nailed to the ground. In the end, scarcely knowing how it happened, she sat down again. “I don't think you need wait for Mrs You or the Seventh Lady. I'll come for you in a few days. Would that suit you?” There was nothing wrong with the idea. But leaving was easy; if she could not enjoy herself properly once in Shanghai, the trip would only disappoint her. She must ask about that first.

“到了上海以后怎么样呢？”“玩嘛！”陈世龙说：“夷场上很开通的，洋人和洋婆子都是手搀手上街……”阿珠很敏感，大声打断他的话说，“哪个要跟你手搀手上街？”“我没有这样说。”陈世龙觉得好笑，“不过拿洋人作个比方，我的意思是，你要在上海逛一逛，也不必一定要七姑奶奶作件。我就好陪你。”话倒说得轻松，实际上决不会这么简单，“偶不陪一趟可以，天天陪我上街……”阿珠很吃力地说：“成什么样子？”“人家不晓得我们是怎么回事？说是兄妹，难道不可以？”“这哪里好冒充？亲兄妹到底亲兄妹，一看就看出来了。”“不见得。”陈世龙说，“这也可以装得象的。”

“What happens when we get to Shanghai?” “We enjoy ourselves!” Chen replied. “People are very broad-minded in the foreign settlement. Foreign men and women walk through the streets hand in hand...” A’zhu was quick to take his meaning. She interrupted loudly. “Who said I wanted to walk hand in hand with you?” “I didn't say that.” Chen was amused. “I was only using the foreigners as an example. What I mean is that, if you want to look around Shanghai, you needn't insist on having the Seventh Lady with you. I can take you.” He made it sound easy, but in practice it could never be so simple. “Once in a while would be all right. But taking me out every day...” She struggled to express herself. “What would people think?” “They don't know what we are to each other. Couldn't we say we're brother and sister?” “How could we pretend that? A real brother and sister are different. People can tell at a glance.” “Not necessarily,” Chen said. “We could make it convincing.”

“怎么装法？”“第一，要亲热……”“啐！”阿珠脸红了，“哪个要跟你亲热？”动辄是“哪个要跟你”怎么样，“哪个要跟你”怎么样，陈世龙注意到了这种语气，蓬门碧玉他见多了，了解这种语气后面的真意，完全是“对人不对事”，意思是“手搀手上街”也可以，“亲热”也可以，只不过不愿“跟你”如此而已。当然，这也算是句反话，有点故意“搭架子”的意味，仿佛暗示着，只要情分够了，无事不可商量。这就是无意间流露的真情，陈世龙越觉得有把握，也就越不肯放松，“你不肯跟我亲热也不要紧，”他说，“好在我装得象，叫人家看起来，一定当我是你的亲哥哥。那一来，你还怕什么？”

“How would we do that?” “First, we'd have to be affectionate...” “Shame on you!” A’zhu blushed. “Who wants to be affectionate with you?” Whatever he proposed, she answered, “Who wants to do that with you?” Chen noticed the form of words. He had known plenty of pretty girls from modest homes and understood what lay behind it. Her objection concerned the person, not the action. Walking hand in hand would be acceptable, as would affectionate behaviour; she merely did not want to do such things “with you.” Of course, even that was a kind of contrary answer, a deliberate show of reserve, as though hinting that, once their affection was deep enough, everything could be discussed. To Chen, this was an involuntary revelation of her true feelings. More confident than ever, he would not let the matter drop. “It doesn't matter if you won't be affectionate towards me. I can act the part well enough to make everyone take me for your real brother. What would you have to fear then?”

阿珠想了一会，决定依他的话，但还要约法三章：“我话先说在前面：第一，不准你嬉皮笑脸，第二，不准你噜哩噜苏，第三，“她略顿一顿，板着脸说：”不准你动手动脚！你答应了，我跟你去。“陈世龙笑道：“还有第四没有？”“你看你，”阿珠斜着白眼看他：“刚刚说过，不准你嬉皮笑脸，你马上就现形了。”这是真的有点生气，陈成龙起了戒心，正一正脸色答道：“好，你不喜欢这样子，我懂了。我决不讨你的厌！”这倒提醒了阿珠。她一直弄不清自己对陈世龙是怎么样的一种感觉？现在“找”到了：这个人不讨厌，而且应该说是蛮讨人喜欢的，这样恩着，忍不住抬头看了他一眼。大大方方地看，原也不妨，她却偏要偷偷摸摸去看，一瞥之下，迅即回避。越是如此，越使陈世龙动心，几乎当时就想违反她的约法第三章，抓住她那白白、软软的手握一握。

After some thought, A’zhu decided to agree, but she must first lay down three rules. “Let me make this clear beforehand. First, no grinning and joking. Second, no pestering me. Third...” She paused briefly and said with a stern face, “Keep your hands to yourself! If you agree, I'll go with you.” Chen smiled. “Is there a fourth?” “Look at you!” She gave him a sideways, reproachful glance. “I've only just said no grinning and joking, and already you're showing your true colours.” She was genuinely a little annoyed. Chen took warning and composed his face. “Very well. Now I understand that you don't like it. I certainly won't make myself unpleasant to you.” This gave A’zhu an insight. She had never quite understood how she felt about Chen. Now she had found the answer: he was not unpleasant. Indeed, she ought to admit that he was rather likeable. At the thought, she could not help looking up at him. There would have been nothing wrong with an open look, but she chose to steal one instead, then swiftly averted her eyes. This only stirred Chen's feelings further. For a moment, he almost wanted to break her third rule and take her soft, fair hand in his.

“嗨！”突然有个在戏水的顽重大喊：“你们来看，一男一女吊膀子！”这一下把阿珠羞得脸如红布，顾不得陈世龙，拔脚就走，走得象逃。河里的顽童，还在哗笑大喊：“吊膀子！吊膀子！”阿珠急得要哭了。“小鬼！”陈世龙恨不得抓住他，狠狠揍一顿，只是顾阿珠要紧，便也拔脚追了上去。追是很快地追上了，阿珠不理他，特意避到对面檐下去走。陈世龙很机警，知道她这时的心境，不敢再跟过去。尤家快到了，只见她忽然站住脚，微微回头望着，这自然是有话要说。陈世龙加快几步，到了她身边。不忙开口，先看脸色、红晕尚未消退，怒气更其明显。他心里有些着慌，不知道该怎么说？

“Hey!” A mischievous boy playing in the water suddenly shouted. “Come and look! A man and a woman flirting!” A’zhu's face turned scarlet. Forgetting Chen, she hurried off as though fleeing. The boys in the river laughed and shouted after her, “Flirting! Flirting!” She was so distressed that she nearly cried. “Little devils!” Chen longed to catch them and give them a sound beating, but A’zhu came first. He hurried after her. He soon caught up, but she ignored him and deliberately crossed to walk beneath the eaves on the other side of the street. Understanding her mood, he dared not follow her over. They were almost at the You house when she suddenly stopped and glanced back. Plainly, she had something to say. Chen quickened his pace and came to her side. Before speaking, he studied her expression. The flush had not faded, and her anger was even more apparent. He became anxious, not knowing what to say.

“都是你！”阿珠咬牙瞪眼地埋怨。迁怒是可想而知的，他唯有解劝：“那些淘气的小鬼，犯不着为他们生气！”“你脸皮厚，自然不在乎！那些难听的话……”阿珠深感屈辱，眼圈一红，要掉眼泪。“不要哭！”陈世龙轻声说道，“七姑奶奶喜欢管闲事，当心她会打破沙锅问到底。”这下提醒了阿珠，她的原意就是要告诫他，不准把刚才这件事当笑话去讲，所以此时用指抹一抹眼角答道，“只要你不说就好了！”说完，阿珠转身就走。陈世龙心里很不是味道，好好一件事，不想叫那几个“小鬼”搞得糟不可言，这是从何说起？细想一想，也要怪自己太大意，如果能够谨慎小心些，不是在那人来人往的河边，大诉衷曲，岂不是就不会有这样扫兴的事了？

“It's all your fault!” A’zhu reproached him through clenched teeth, glaring. It was natural that she should turn her anger on him. He could only try to soothe her. “Those naughty little devils aren't worth getting upset over.” “You're thick-skinned. Naturally you don't care! But those dreadful things they said...” She felt deeply humiliated, and her eyes reddened and filled with tears. “Don't cry,” Chen said softly. “The Seventh Lady likes to meddle. Be careful, or she'll question you until she gets to the bottom of it.” This reminded A’zhu of her purpose. She had stopped to warn him not to turn the incident into an amusing story. Wiping the corner of her eye with a finger, she said, “It will be all right as long as you say nothing.” She turned and left. Chen felt thoroughly miserable. A perfectly promising encounter had been ruined by those little devils. How had it come to this? On reflection, he must blame his own carelessness too. Had he been more discreet, instead of declaring his feelings on a busy riverbank, nothing would have happened to spoil their pleasure.

徒悔无益，为今之计，必须全力挽回局面。因此，陈世龙经过仔细考虑之后，还是跟了进去。他在尤家没有象阿珠那样熟，而且尤家虽说江湖上人，比较开通，男女之防，还是很着重的，尽管七姑奶奶不大在乎，他却不便穿房入户，闯入后厅。到尤家，只是存下个见机行事的打算，就算不能见着阿珠，无论如何要让她知道，为了她恋恋不忍遽去。他不知道，这天的情形跟昨天已大不相同，不同的原因，就在尤家姑嫂对他已“另眼相看”，所以当他正在厅上与尤五手底下的人闲谈时，尤太太打发一个丫头来请，说有话跟他谈。这真是“宠召”了！陈世龙精神抖擞地到了后厅，恭敬而亲热地招呼：“尤太太，七姑奶奶！”“不要用这样客气的称呼了。”七姑奶奶说道：“你跟我们张家妹子一样，也叫‘五嫂’、‘七姐’好了。”陈世龙越有受宠若惊之感，而且福至心灵，想起一句很“文”的话：“恭敬不如从命！”他垂着手喊：“五嫂！七姐！”一面喊，一面眼风顺便扫过阿珠，她把脸转了过去，不知是有意不理，还是别有缘故？“

Regret alone was useless. He must now do everything he could to retrieve the situation. After careful consideration, Chen followed her into the house. He was not as familiar with the You household as A’zhu was. Although these were worldly people with relatively open minds, they still attached considerable importance to the separation of men and women. The Seventh Lady might disregard it, but he could hardly walk through their private rooms and enter the rear hall uninvited. He came merely to await an opportunity. Even if he could not see A’zhu, he must at least let her know how reluctant he was to leave her. What he did not know was that things were very different from the day before: Mrs You and her sister-in-law now viewed him in a new light. While he was talking in the hall to some of You Laowu's men, Mrs You sent a maid to say she wished to speak with him. This was a flattering summons indeed. Chen went eagerly to the rear hall and greeted them with warmth and respect. “Mrs You! Seventh Lady!” “No need for such formal titles,” the Seventh Lady said. “Call us Fifth Sister-in-Law and Seventh Sister, as our young Zhang sister does.” Feeling still more favoured, Chen found himself inspired with an elegant phrase. “Obedience is better than ceremony!” Standing with his hands at his sides, he said, “Fifth Sister-in-Law! Seventh Sister!” As he spoke, he glanced towards A’zhu. She turned her face away. Was she deliberately ignoring him, or was there some other reason?

“世龙！”陈太太开口了，语气平静自然，“你今天下午要走了？”“是的。下午走。”“我托你点事，可以不可以？”“五嫂怎么说这话？有事尽管吩咐！”“我托你在上海买点东西。”尤太太接下来解释，“不要看我这里，差不多天天有人到上海，关照他们买点东西，总是不称心，不是样子不对，就是多了少了的，真气人！我晓得你能干，这一趟特为托你。”“五嫂说得好。”陈世龙笑道，“只怕我买回来，一样也要挨骂。”“不会的。”尤太太问道：“东西很多，要开个单子，你会不会写字？”陈世龙学过刻字生意，字认得不多，却写得很好，便即答道：“会！”他一说会，七姑奶奶已把笔砚捧了过来，在红木方桌上放下，拉开凳子，还拿手拍了一下：“来！坐下写。”他坐在东首顺光的那一边，七姑奶奶坐在他对面，左手方是尤太太。还空着上首一个座位，七姑奶奶把阿珠硬拉了来坐下，三双眼睛灼然地看着陈世龙手中的那支笔。他忽然意会了，“这哪里是开买东西的单子？简直是考自己的文墨嘛！”心里不安而又兴奋，打起精神，希望在三位“考官”面前交一本好卷子。

“Shilong,” Mrs You began in an even, natural voice, “are you leaving this afternoon?” “Yes, this afternoon.” “May I ask you to do something for me?” “Why put it like that, Fifth Sister-in-Law? Just tell me what you need.” “I'd like you to buy some things in Shanghai. You might think it easy, with people going there from this house almost every day. But whenever I ask them to buy something, it's never right. Either the style is wrong or there's too much or too little. It really is exasperating! I know how capable you are, so this time I'm asking you especially.” “You are very kind,” Chen said with a smile. “I'm only afraid you'll scold me just the same when I bring the things back.” “No, I shan't. There are quite a few things, so we need a list. Can you write?” Chen had once trained in engraving. He did not recognise many characters, but wrote a fine hand. “Yes,” he answered. At once the Seventh Lady brought brush and inkstone, set them on the square rosewood table, drew out a stool, and patted it. “Come! Sit down and write.” He took the seat on the eastern side, where the light fell favourably. The Seventh Lady sat opposite him, with Mrs You to his left. The seat at the head remained empty until the Seventh Lady insisted that A’zhu take it. Three pairs of eyes fixed intently on his brush. Suddenly he understood. This was hardly a shopping list: it was an examination of his learning! Uneasy and excited, he rallied himself, hoping to produce a fine paper for his three examiners.

真如“说书先生”常用来表白那句话：“磨得墨浓，舐得笔饱”，陈世龙执笔在手，看着尤太太，静候吩咐。“男人的袍子要一丈四。一丈四、一丈四、两丈八；再加八尺，就剪四丈八好了。”尤太太念念有词地盘算了一会，抬头看着陈世龙，“哆罗呢四丈。”第一遭就遇着难题。哆罗呢这种衣料听说过，是外国来的呢子，却不知怎么写法？不过陈世龙的脑筋也很快，他想，外国名字大多加个“口”字旁，譬如“■咭唎”之类，那就不妨如法炮制。这一下倒是写对了。他也很细心，写完又问：“什么颜色？”“玄色。”“玄”字不会写，却也不算错，他在“哆罗呢”三字下，注了个“黑”字。就这样尤太太口述，陈世龙笔录，许多洋货的名字，他“以意为之”，只译写声首，反正自己知道。尤太太她们也不来管他，实在是不知道他写对了没有？不过阿珠看他那笔字，写得端端正正，心里也不知是安慰：还是得意，只觉得脸上很有光彩。

As the storytellers liked to put it, he ground the ink thick and filled the brush well. Then, brush in hand, he looked at Mrs You and respectfully awaited her instructions. “A man's gown takes one zhang and four chi. One zhang and four, one zhang and four: two zhang and eight chi. Add another eight chi, and we may as well have four zhang and eight chi cut.” After muttering these calculations, Mrs You looked up. “Four zhang of duoluo cloth.” His very first item presented a difficulty. Chen had heard of this imported woollen cloth but did not know how to write its name. He thought quickly. Foreign names were often written with characters containing the mouth radical, as in the name for England. He might try the same method. As it happened, he got it right. He was careful enough to ask, “What colour?” “Deep black—xuan.” He could not write xuan, but his solution was no mistake either: beneath the cloth's name, he wrote the ordinary character for black. Mrs You continued dictating and Chen writing. He improvised many names of foreign goods, using characters for their sounds; he himself would know what they meant. The women did not interfere, since they really could not tell whether he had written them correctly. A’zhu, however, saw his neat, upright handwriting and felt something between reassurance and pride, as though it reflected credit on her.

女人家办这些琐碎事最麻烦，尤太太跟她小姑又商议、又争辩，阿珠也不时参加些意见，越发耗费辰光。陈世龙很耐心地等着。等那单子写完，已经误了中饭时间，一桌子的菜都摆得凉了。“吃饭，吃饭！”七姑奶奶对陈世龙的称呼，也众不同，比较亲昵：“阿龙，你不必到外头吃，同我们一桌好了。”如果是在平常日子，陈世龙一定会辞谢她的好意，而这天不同，欣然落座，坐下来就吃。一面吃，一面闲谈，不过“手挥五弦，目送飞鸿”，视线不断缭绕在阿珠脸上，她除掉偶尔低下头来，很快地眨着眼，仿佛有些事在想以外，脸色大致是恬静的，大可叫人放心。吃完饭，尤太太进去取出一张一百两银子的银票，交了给陈世龙。这就该走了！他却还不肯告辞，总觉得没有机会跟阿珠再说两句话，于心不甘。谁知有个意想不到的机会，“我还要到船上去一趟。”阿珠起身说道，“有两句要紧话，刚才忘了跟我爹说了。”用不着陈世龙自告奋勇，有意为他们撮合的七姑奶奶，当然会顺理成章地建议，仍旧由陈世龙陪着她到船上。

Women could make such small household purchases extraordinarily complicated. Mrs You and her sister-in-law consulted and argued, while A’zhu offered suggestions from time to time, consuming still more time. Chen waited patiently. By the time the list was finished, lunchtime had passed and the dishes on the table had grown cold. “Let's eat, let's eat!” The Seventh Lady had a particularly familiar form of address for him. “A’long, you needn't eat out front. Join our table.” On an ordinary day, Chen would certainly have declined the kindness. Today was different: he gladly sat down and began to eat. He chatted over the meal, but, like the man whose hands played his lute while his eyes followed a wild goose in flight, his attention was elsewhere. His gaze kept returning to A’zhu's face. Apart from occasionally lowering her head and blinking quickly, as though thinking something over, she looked generally serene. He could feel reassured. After the meal, Mrs You went inside and returned with a hundred-tael banknote, which she handed to him. He ought now to leave, but could not bring himself to say goodbye. He still had not spoken to A’zhu again and was unwilling to let the opportunity go. Then, unexpectedly, an opportunity appeared. “I have to go to the boat once more,” A’zhu said, rising. “I forgot to tell my father something important.” Chen had no need to volunteer. The Seventh Lady, intent on bringing them together, naturally suggested that he accompany her again.

“不要走那条路了。”一出尤家后门，阿珠就嘟着嘴说。“总归要到河边。”陈世龙答道，“那些小鬼再淘气，我一定捉牢他们敲屁股。”“你少替我多事！”其实，阿珠并不要到船上，只是有件事要跟陈世龙说，所以当先领路，走到僻静之处站住了脚。“我请你办点事。”她说，“在尤家叨扰了他们许多日子，应该有点意思，我想送他们一份礼，请你在上海办一办。”说着，她从手巾里取出一张银票，递了过去：“尽二十两银子办，要办两份，送五嫂的那份，是伢儿用的东西就可以了。”“我晓得了。等我办好了，回来再跟你算。”“那样我就不要。”阿珠把银票塞到他手里。不接不行，陈世龙也就不再多说什么，只另外问了一句要紧话：“我先前说来接你的话，怎么样？”

“Don't take that road again,” A’zhu said with a pout as soon as they left the back gate. “We have to reach the river somehow,” Chen replied. “If those little devils misbehave again, I'll catch them and smack their bottoms.” “Don't you go making trouble on my account!” In fact, she did not need to visit the boat. She merely had something to say to Chen. Taking the lead, she stopped in a quiet place. “I'd like you to do something for me. We've imposed on the You family for so many days that I ought to show my appreciation. I want to give them some presents. Could you buy them in Shanghai?” She took a banknote from her handkerchief and offered it to him. “Spend up to twenty taels. There must be two sets of presents. Things for the children will do for Fifth Sister-in-Law's share.” “I understand. I'll buy them, and we can settle the account when I come back.” “Then I won't have them.” She pressed the note into his hand. He had to accept it and said no more, but asked another important question. “What about my proposal to come and fetch you?”

阿珠知道，这象走路一样，又到了一处三叉路口，一条路渺渺茫茫，走到哪里算哪里，路虽平坦不会摔跟斗，但没有什么景致，也不知走到头来是何光景？另一条路已可以看得出来，崎岖难行，但必有山光水色、奇石怪木，堪以流连，而走到头来，若有归宿必是个很好的归宿，就怕中途失足，葬送一生。陈世龙见她久无回答，心急催问：“怎么样呢？你倒是说一句呀！”“让我想一想也不要紧……”“好，好！”陈世龙是怕她听而不闻，在转别的念头，只要是想这件事，时间再长，他也能等待，所以这样抢着说：“你尽管慢慢想！”想了半天，委决不下，心里是愿意走第二条路，却又有些胆怯。她这时候才感觉到，一个人不能没有一个可以商量心事的亲人或者朋友，如果有七姑奶奶在旁边就好了。这样一转念，她越不肯作肯定的答复，不过这一来，反倒有话可说了：“到时候再看！”这句话，如果他一开口她就这么回答，必是敷衍，经过好一阵考虑才说，那是打不定主意。陈世龙虽有些扫兴，不过因为一时得不到一句准话，细想一想，正见得她重视此行，不仅仅是为了玩一趟。至于她为何打不定主意？这倒该设法在她心里查一查。

A’zhu knew that she had reached another fork in the road. One way stretched away uncertainly; she could simply go wherever it led. The road was level and she would not stumble, but there was little to see and no knowing what awaited her at its end. The other was already visibly rough and difficult, yet there would surely be mountain scenery, bright waters, strange rocks, and remarkable trees to linger over. If it led to a destination, that destination would be a very good one. The danger was a false step along the way that might ruin her whole life. After waiting without an answer, Chen urged her impatiently, “Well? Do say something.” “Surely you can let me think...” “Of course, of course!” He had feared that she was not listening and had turned her thoughts elsewhere. So long as she was considering his proposal, he could wait however long she needed. “Take all the time you like.” She thought for a long while but could not decide. In her heart she wanted the second road, yet she was afraid. Only now did she realise how necessary it was to have a relative or friend with whom one could discuss private worries. If only the Seventh Lady were beside her! That thought made her still less willing to give a definite answer, though it did supply something to say. “We'll see when the time comes.” Had she said this immediately, she would have been putting him off. After so much thought, it meant that she could not decide. Chen was disappointed at receiving no definite promise, but reflection showed that she took the journey seriously: it meant more to her than a pleasure trip. Why, then, was she unable to decide? He ought to find out.

于是他问：“你是不是还顾忌着胡先生？”“顾忌他点啥？”阿珠把脸绷得极紧，才好说出她那一句不大好意思出口的话：“我跟他清清白白，干干净净，有啥好顾忌的？”不但已可以把胡雪岩抛开，而且在表明心迹了，其中的意味，着实深厚。陈世龙心满意足，“自说自话”地放下诺言：“我五天以后来接你。”阿珠差一点又要说：“哪个要你来接？我又没有答应你一起走。”只是毕竟未曾出口，而且心里觉得好笑，此人比胡雪岩还要不讲理。“好了，好了。我要回去了。”阿珠挥挥手说。“要不要我送？”“不要！”阿珠又说，“你也该早点到船上去，人家在等你。正经事也要紧，不要尽转不相干的念头。”陈世龙笑笑走了，走了几步，转脸去看，恰好阿珠也回身在望，视线一触便离，扭转身去，沿着路边很快地走了。这一个望着苗条的背影，回想她临别之际的那两句叮咛，觉得有咀嚼不尽的余味，心里是说不出的好过。

“Are you still worried about Mr Hu?” he asked. “Why should I be worried about him?” A’zhu held her face stiffly, so that she could say the words she found embarrassing. “I've behaved quite properly with him. Nothing has happened between us. What have I to worry about?” She was not merely setting Hu aside; she was revealing her own position. There was considerable meaning in that. Entirely satisfied, Chen made a promise on his own authority. “I'll come for you in five days.” A’zhu almost said, “Who asked you to? I haven't agreed to go with you.” But she did not say it. Indeed, she was inwardly amused. This man was even more unreasonable than Hu. “Very well, very well. I'm going back.” She waved a hand. “Shall I see you home?” “No. You ought to get to the boat early too. People are waiting. Your proper business matters as well. Don't spend all your time thinking about other things.” Chen smiled and went. After a few steps he looked back, just as she too turned to look. Their eyes met and immediately parted. She turned away and hurried along the side of the road. Watching her slender figure recede, he recalled her parting admonitions and found endless pleasure in their implications. His contentment was beyond words.

阿珠却跟他不同，心里乱糟糟的，不辨是何滋味？却又无法静下来想一想，因为一回去就让七姑奶奶缠住了。“你怎么这么快就回来了？”这第一句话就让她不容易回答，她嘴上不大肯让人，其实说不来假话，自己算一算，到船上来回一趟，这点辰光是不够的，因而疑心七姑奶奶已发觉她根本没有去见她父亲，只是借故溜出去跟陈世龙“讲私话”。于是象被人捉住了短处似地，她一张脸涨得退红，半晌说不出话来。七姑奶奶等于一个女光棍，那双眼睛看阿珠这样的人，表里俱澈。恍然大悟之余，心中好笑，真正是做贼心虚。但她虽口沿遮拦，对这句后到底还有顾忌，怕阿珠脸皮薄，一个挂不住，会伤了彼此情分，因此笑笑不响。这一笑在心思也极灵敏的阿珠，当然亦猜到了她的心理。掩饰不可，只有解释，索性把话说明了，倒也无所谓。

A’zhu felt very differently. Her thoughts were in turmoil, and she could not tell what she felt. Nor could she sit quietly to consider it, for the Seventh Lady caught her the moment she returned. “How did you get back so quickly?” The very first question was hard to answer. A’zhu seldom conceded anything in an argument, but she was a poor liar. A quick calculation showed that she had not been away long enough to go to the boat and back. She suspected the Seventh Lady had discovered that she had never visited her father at all, merely used him as an excuse to slip out for a private conversation with Chen. Feeling as though she had been caught doing something wrong, she flushed deeply and could not speak for some time. The Seventh Lady was as worldly as any hardened rogue. She could see straight through someone like A’zhu. Understanding suddenly, she was amused: a guilty conscience indeed! Yet, free as she was with her tongue, she hesitated to say this aloud. If she embarrassed the thin-skinned girl beyond endurance, she might damage their affection. She merely smiled. A’zhu was perceptive too and naturally guessed what lay behind the smile. Concealment was impossible; she could only explain. There was no harm in stating the matter openly.

“老实告诉你，”她的脸色反转为平静，“我也要托陈世龙买点东西，不好当着你们的面说。”“为啥？”“在府上打扰了好些日子，哪怕送点不值钱的东两，也是我一点心。我如果当了你们的面说，你们一定不肯，所以我要避开你们托他。”“原来这佯。你何必又破费。”“是不是？”阿珠理直气壮似地说，“我就晓得你们一定会拦住我。”“好了。我就不客气了。自己姐妹，老说客气话也没有意思。”七姑奶奶看一看桌上的自鸣钟说：“我要到书场去了。你去不去？”七姑奶奶喜欢听书。一部书听上了瘾，天天要听。阿珠总觉得女人抛头露面上书场，不象样子。而且有些“先生”，说到男女间事、看有“堂客”在座，比较含蓄，有些就毫无顾忌了，绘声绘影，春情十足，七姑奶奶不在乎，阿珠却窘不可言。她“上过一回当”，颇存戒心，七姑奶奶也不便勉强，只是每天去总要问她一声。她有时去，有时不去，要看那天说的是哪一回书。

“To tell you the truth,” A’zhu said, her expression becoming calm, “I wanted to ask Chen to buy some things too, and I couldn't say so in front of you.” “Why not?” “I've been troubling your household for so many days. Even a small present would show my gratitude. If I'd mentioned it before you, you would certainly have objected. That was why I had to ask him privately.” “So that was it. Why put yourself to more expense?” “You see?” A’zhu spoke as though fully vindicated. “I knew you would try to stop me.” “Very well. I won't stand on ceremony. We're sisters; there's no point in endlessly exchanging polite speeches.” The Seventh Lady glanced at the striking clock on the table. “I'm going to hear the storyteller. Are you coming?” She loved storytelling. Once absorbed in a tale, she had to hear it every day. A’zhu, however, felt that women made a poor show of themselves by appearing in public at storytelling houses. Besides, when describing affairs between men and women, some performers restrained themselves if ladies were present, while others had no scruples at all and told their amorous scenes with vivid abandon. The Seventh Lady did not mind; A’zhu found it unbearably embarrassing. Having been caught out once, she remained wary. The Seventh Lady could hardly insist, but always asked before going. Sometimes A’zhu went and sometimes she stayed, depending on which episode was to be told.

阿珠知道，她听上瘾的那部书是《玉晴蜓》，随即问道：“今天说到哪里？”“快要‘庵堂产子’了。”“庵堂产子”只有怀孕足月的小尼姑志贞，没有造孽缘的申贵升，听这回书不会受窘，阿珠便答应同去。有人做伴，七姑奶奶的兴致格外好，一面涂脂抹粉，细细打扮，一面把“庵堂产子”的情节和昨天的“关子”说到什么地方，都讲了给阿珠听。“到底是‘申大爷’，还是‘金大爷’？”“应该是‘申大爷’，说书先生都称‘金大爷’，因为苏州申家势力大，不敢得罪他们，这部书，从前是禁的。”“这样说来，真的有这回事了？”“那就不晓得了。不过，”七姑奶奶说：“申家上代出过状元，倒是真的。有年到苏州，走过一家人家，门口下马石、旗杆，有块匾‘状元及第’，气派大得很，别人说是申状元家。”“这个状元，就是小尼姑志贞的儿子？”“照《玉蜻蜓》说，志贞的儿子叫申元宰，后来中了状元，‘庵堂认母’，把她接回家里。”

Knowing that the tale which had captivated her was The Jade Dragonfly, A’zhu asked, “Where have they got to today?” “Almost to ‘The Birth in the Nunnery.’” In that episode, the young nun Zhizhen was at the end of her pregnancy, but Shen Guisheng, the man with whom she had formed the ill-fated attachment, did not appear. There would be nothing to embarrass her, so A’zhu agreed to go. Pleased to have company, the Seventh Lady carefully applied her rouge and powder and dressed, all the while explaining the episode and the point of suspense at which yesterday's performance had ended. “Is he really Master Shen or Master Jin?” A’zhu asked. “It ought to be Master Shen. The storytellers all call him Master Jin because the Shen family is powerful in Suzhou and they dare not offend them. This tale used to be forbidden.” “Then did it really happen?” “That I don't know. But it's true that an ancestor of the Shen family came first in the palace examination. One year in Suzhou, I passed a house with dismounting stones and flagpoles outside, and a plaque proclaiming its owner's first place in the examination. It was very grand. Someone said it was the home of the great scholar Shen.” “Was that scholar the nun Zhizhen's son?” “According to The Jade Dragonfly, her son's name was Shen Yuanzai. He later came first in the palace examination, acknowledged his mother at the nunnery, and took her home.”

“那么，”阿珠问道：“ ‘申大娘娘’呢？怎么说？”“这还有啥话说？儿子虽不是她生的、诰封总要先归她，再说申大爷老早痨病死在庵里，为死人吃醋也没有这个道理。”“这一下，志贞总算苦出头了。”阿珠感叹着说，“大概她做梦也不曾想到，儿子会中了状元。”“照我想想犯不着。”七姑奶奶很平静的说：“苦守苦熬多少年，才熬得儿子出了头，头发白了，眼睛花了，牙齿掉了，就算有福好享，也是枉然。倒不如觅个知心合意的，趁少年辰光，过几天写意日子。“这话不知是不是有意讽劝？反正阿珠的印象极深。等听了“庵堂产子”回来，感触越深，而且由志贞的伶仃无告，勾起她想家的念头，渴望着回到湖州，觉得只有在自己娘身边，这颗心才能定下来。

“What about Madam Shen?” A’zhu asked. “What did she say?” “What could she say? Although she hadn't given birth to him, any imperial title granted to his mother would go to her first, as the lawful wife. Besides, Master Shen had long since died of consumption in the nunnery. There was no sense in being jealous over a dead man.” “Then Zhizhen finally came to the end of her troubles.” A’zhu sighed. “She probably never dreamed her son would come first in the examination.” “To my mind, it wasn't worth it,” the Seventh Lady said calmly. “Years and years of suffering and waiting, until her son finally made his way. By then her hair would be white, her eyes dim, and her teeth gone. Even if there were good fortune to enjoy, it would be wasted. Better to find someone who understands you and suits you, and have a few happy days while you're young.” Whether or not this was intended as indirect advice, it left a deep impression on A’zhu. After hearing “The Birth in the Nunnery,” she felt it more keenly still. Zhizhen's loneliness and helplessness made her homesick. She longed to return to Huzhou and felt that only beside her own mother could her heart find peace.

乡思造成失眠，一直到四更天还不曾睡着。七姑奶奶跟她住东西两厢房、一觉睡醒，发觉对面还有灯光，心里有些不放心，便起床来敲她的房门。阿珠知道是七姑奶奶，除了她不会有第二个人。于是开门问道：“你怎么还不睡？”“我已经睡过一大觉了，看见你这里灯光亮着，过来看看。”她走进门来，发觉阿珠的两面帐门都未放下，便奇怪的问：“你一直都不曾睡吗？在做什么？”“什么都没有做，就是睡不着。”“在想哪个？”阿珠脸一红，“会想哪个？”她说，“自然是想娘。”“怪不得！”七姑奶奶捏着她的手臂问：“冷不冷？”“还好。”阿珠见她只穿着一件对襟短袖的褂子，胸前钮扣，不曾扣好，露出雪白的一块肉，褂子又小了些，鼓蓬蓬的凸出两大块。心里便想，七姑奶奶象花开到盛时，却形单影只的守了寡，似乎也可怜。这样想着，不由得伸手捏住了她的丰腴的手臂，“七姐，”她说，“这里来坐！”她拉着她并坐在床沿上，怔怔地看着她，眼中有些迷惘和优郁，把七姑奶奶看得莫名其妙，便即问道，“怎么回事？你有话说嘛！”

Homesickness kept her awake. Even during the fourth watch, she had not slept. She and the Seventh Lady occupied opposite wings of the house. Waking after a good sleep, the Seventh Lady noticed light across the way and became uneasy. She rose and knocked on A’zhu's door. A’zhu knew who it must be; nobody else would come. Opening the door, she asked, “Why aren't you asleep yet?” “I've already had a good sleep. I saw your lamp burning and came to look in.” Entering, she noticed that the bed curtains on both sides were still raised. “Haven't you slept at all? What have you been doing?” “Nothing. I simply can't sleep.” “Who are you thinking of?” A’zhu blushed. “Who should I be thinking of? My mother, naturally.” “No wonder!” The Seventh Lady held her arm. “Are you cold?” “Not very.” A’zhu saw that she wore only a short-sleeved, front-fastening blouse. The buttons had not all been done up, exposing a patch of pale skin, and the blouse was rather tight across her full bosom. A’zhu thought that the Seventh Lady was like a flower in full bloom, yet lived alone as a widow. It seemed pitiable. Almost involuntarily, she took the woman's full arm in turn. “Seventh Sister, come and sit here.” She drew her onto the edge of the bed and gazed at her with bewilderment and melancholy in her eyes. The Seventh Lady could not understand it. “What's the matter? If you have something to say, say it.”

“我在想，”阿珠缓慢而低沉地说，“俗语说‘家家有本难念的经’，这话还不对，实在是‘人人有本难念的经’。譬如七姐你，别人看起来，一天到晚，嘻嘻哈哈，好象没啥心事，仔细想一想，你一个人的日子也难过。”这两句话听来平淡无奇，谁知恰好触着了七姑奶奶的隐痛，连她兄嫂在内，从来没有人说过这话。午夜梦回，凄凉万状，那时的心境，只有自己知道。如今总算还有个人了解她的苦楚！七姑奶奶顿有知遇之感，那么刚强的人，竟忍不住眼圈一红，快要掉眼泪了。但是刚强的人总是刚强的，就在这时候，也不愿让人觉得她可怜，“你说得不对！”所以她装得很豁达地，“我倒不觉得日子难过。”“叫我，”阿珠摇摇头，“这种日子就过不下去。”“所以罗！”七姑奶奶为人的心又热了，接口劝她，“你过不惯这种一个人孤孤单单的日子，要趁早打主意。跟胡老板断了，这着棋走得一点不错，他是个做大生意的人，一会儿湖州，一会儿上海，说走就走，丢下你独守空房，这味道不大好受的。”“嗳！”阿珠皱眉摇手，“不要去讲他了。讲讲别人吧！”

“I was thinking,” A’zhu said slowly and quietly, “people say every household has a difficult scripture to recite. That isn't quite right. Really, every person has one. Take you, Seventh Sister. Others see you laughing and joking all day, as though you had no worries. But when I think about it, living alone must be hard for you.” The words sounded ordinary enough, yet they touched the Seventh Lady's hidden pain. No one, not even her brother or his wife, had ever said such a thing. Only she knew the desolation of waking from a dream in the middle of the night. At last someone understood her suffering. She felt profoundly recognised. Strong as she was, her eyes reddened and tears almost came. Yet a strong person remained strong: even now, she would not allow herself to be pitied. “You're wrong!” she said, affecting an easy spirit. “I don't find life hard at all.” “I couldn't bear it,” A’zhu said, shaking her head. “Precisely!” Her concern for others revived, and the Seventh Lady seized the opportunity to advise her. “If you can't endure a life of loneliness, you must decide what to do early. You were quite right to break with Mr Hu. He's a man with a great business: Huzhou one moment, Shanghai the next. He would leave at a moment's notice and let you keep watch over an empty room. That isn't a pleasant life.” “Oh, don't talk about him!” A’zhu frowned and waved a hand. “Let's talk about someone else.”

她是无心的一句话，七姑奶奶却大为兴奋，“来！”她拉着她倒下，“今天我陪你。我们姐妹也说说私话。”阿珠也是精神亢奋，毫无睡意，刚过了立秋的天气，后半夜非常舒服，她也愿意作个长夜之谈。不过七姑奶奶如不羁的野马，她买在有些怕她，便得要有句话“言明在先”。“说私话可以。”她笑道，“就是你哇啦、哇啦吃不消。”“傻妹子！”七姑奶奶捧着她的红馥馥的脸香了一下，“说到私话，怎么会哇啦、哇啦？自然只有你我两个人才听得见。”“这样才好，”阿珠问道，“你饿不饿？我有杭州带来的‘绍兴香糕’，要不要吃？”“ ‘绍兴香糕’哪有你们‘湖州酥糖’好吃。有没有‘沙核桃糖’？”“有，有！我倒忘记掉了。”阿珠从置放茶食用的可以收燥的石灰坛里，摸出一大包沙核桃糖，带到床上，两个人并头共枕，盖着一条薄薄的紫罗被，一面吃糖，一面谈私话。

She spoke without any particular intention, but the Seventh Lady became excited. “Come!” She drew A’zhu down beside her. “I'll stay with you tonight. We sisters can have a private talk.” A’zhu was wide awake too. The season had just passed the Beginning of Autumn, and the later hours of the night were very comfortable. She was willing to talk until dawn. But the Seventh Lady was like an unbridled horse, and A’zhu was rather afraid of her. A condition must be stated first. “A private talk is all right,” she said with a smile. “It's your shouting and carrying on that I can't stand.” “Silly girl!” The Seventh Lady took her flushed face in her hands and kissed her cheek. “How could I shout when we're talking privately? Naturally, only you and I will hear.” “That's all right, then. Are you hungry? I've some Shaoxing fragrant cakes I brought from Hangzhou. Would you like some?” “Those aren't as good as your Huzhou flaky sweets. Have you any walnut sweets?” “Yes, yes! I'd quite forgotten them.” From the jar in which snacks were kept dry with lime, A’zhu took a large packet of walnut sweets and brought it to bed. With their heads beside each other on the pillow and a thin purple gauze coverlet over them, the two ate sweets and talked confidentially.

“七姐，你守寡守了几年了？”“四年。”这四年的味道如何呢？阿珠很想问，又觉得碍口，只好扯些不相干的话，“想来你那婆婆很凶。”“凭良心说，倒也还好。就是脾气合不来，一天到晚罗嗦，实在也是好意，譬如说，天气热胃口总有不好的时候，只要一顿不吃，她老人家就问长问短，一刻不停了。一会儿是不是病了？要不要看医生？一会儿又说受凉了，晚上睡觉要小心。如果我不理她，她就哭儿子，我都想哭在那里，听见她哭，你想烦不烦？”“那么，回娘家来住，是哪个的意思呢？”“自然是我自己的意思，”七姑奶奶说，“哪个都做不得我的主。”“难道，”阿珠很谨慎地问： “在娘家住一辈子？”“住一辈子也不要紧。我五哥、五嫂，跟别家的兄嫂不同。”“这我看得出来的，说句良心话，五哥、五嫂待你是再也没话可说了。”“当然，自己同胞手足嘛！不过，”七姑奶奶又说，“其中还有个道理，说给你听听也不要紧。“

“Seventh Sister, how long have you been widowed?” “Four years.” What had those four years been like? A’zhu wanted to ask, but felt awkward. Instead, she ventured something less direct. “I suppose your mother-in-law was very fierce.” “To be fair, she was quite decent. We simply didn't get on. She fussed from morning to night, though she really meant well. In hot weather, for instance, everyone loses their appetite sometimes. But if I missed a single meal, she would ask questions without stopping. Was I ill? Did I need a doctor? Then she would say I'd caught cold and must be careful at night. If I ignored her, she began mourning her son. I felt like crying myself already. Imagine having to listen to her too! Wasn't that exasperating?” “Whose idea was it for you to come back to your own family?” “Mine, naturally. Nobody else can decide for me.” “But surely...” A’zhu asked cautiously, “you won't stay here for the rest of your life?” “It wouldn't matter if I did. My fifth brother and his wife aren't like other brothers and sisters-in-law.” “I can see that. Honestly, nobody could say anything against the way they treat you.” “Of course. We're flesh and blood. But there's another reason too. I may as well tell you.”

原来尤五在十几年前，是倔强到底，宁折不弯的脾气，有一次跟松江府知府的大少爷，在妓院里打架，被抓到了“班房”里。那知府倒也还明理，预备训斥一顿，放他走路。但尤五自觉道理上站得住，所以言语顶撞，不受责备，这一下知府动了真气，非办他个“目无官长”的罪名不可。“老太爷”托出许多人来求情，那知府是个书呆子，说什么也不行。“这时漕粮要起运了，船上不是我五哥，就吃不住，老太爷十分着急。后来是我出面去见知府。“七姑奶奶回忆着得意的往事，那双眼睛格外亮，格外显得一汪水似的，”我说：大老爷，我哥得罪了大少爷，又得罪大老爷，理当吃三年六个月的官司。不过现在他有公事，好不好我来做押头？把我关起来，放我哥哥出去当差，等漕船回空，他进监牢，我再出去。“

More than ten years earlier, You Laowu had been stubborn to the last degree: he would sooner break than bend. Once, after fighting in a brothel with the eldest son of the prefect of Songjiang, he had been taken into custody. The prefect was reasonably fair-minded and meant merely to reprimand him and let him go. But You believed himself in the right and answered back, refusing to accept blame. The prefect became genuinely angry and insisted on charging him with contempt for official authority. The old master enlisted many people to intercede, but the prefect was a bookish man who would listen to nothing. “The grain tribute was just about to sail,” the Seventh Lady continued. “Without my fifth brother, nobody could keep the boats under control. The old master was terribly worried. In the end, I went to see the prefect myself.” Recalling this proud episode, her eyes grew unusually bright and lustrous. “I said, ‘Your Honour, my brother has offended both your son and yourself. He deserves three years and six months in prison. But he has public duties to perform just now. Could I stand as security for him? Lock me up and let him go about his work. When the grain boats return empty, he can go to prison and I can leave.’”

“你倒想得出。”阿珠听得津津有味的笑道：“那知府大老爷，怎么说法？”“大家都说知府大老爷是书呆子，其实不呆。”七姑奶奶答道：“当时他跟我说：”你哥哥不讲道理。世界上只有老百姓怕官，照他这样子，莫非官要怕他？那不是没有王法了吗？我本来不但要重办，而且还要申详到上头，革他尖丁的差使。现在看你倒还讲道理，不过你也不要看得太容易，监狱里的罪不是好受的。‘我说：“我晓得。不过不是这样子，大老爷不能消气，说不得只好我咬咬牙关来受罪。’大老爷听我这一说，摇摇手：”罢了，罢了！看你这样子，我也不气了。你具个结，把你哥哥领了回去。‘“

“What an idea!” A’zhu listened with delighted interest. “What did the prefect say?” “Everyone called him a pedant, but he wasn't stupid. He told me, ‘Your brother is unreasonable. In this world, ordinary people fear officials. Does he expect officials to fear him? Where would the law be then? I meant not only to punish him severely but to report the matter to my superiors and have him dismissed from his post as a grain-transport headman. You seem more reasonable. But don't think this is easy. Prison is no pleasant place.’ I said, ‘I know. But unless I do this, Your Honour's anger cannot be appeased. I'll just have to grit my teeth and bear it.’ He waved his hand. ‘Enough, enough! Seeing you like this has taken away my anger. Sign a bond and take your brother home.’”

“这真正是新闻。”阿珠笑道：“还要你具结？”“是啊！硬是我盖手模具结。具了结，知府大老爷把五哥叫了去说‘你要改过自新！再是这样子横行霸道，我不办你，办具结的人。你要想想，倘或你连累你妹子吃官司，对不对得起你父母？’”“啊！这一着厉害。”阿珠倒懂得那知府的用意，“就算五哥自己天不怕，地怕，总要顾到你。这一来，脾气无论如何要改改了。”“就是这话罗！所以我说知府大老爷一点不呆。”七姑奶奶又说，“等堂上下来，老太爷亲自来接我，接到他家，摆开了十桌酒席，帮里弟兄都到了，老太爷叫我坐首座。他说：阿七可惜是女的，如果是男的，我要收了‘他’才‘关山门’。”“七姐！”阿珠听得出了神， “我倒没有想到，你出过这么大的风头？”“唉！”七姑奶奶长叹一声：“就是那次风头出坏了。”

“Now that is something new!” A’zhu laughed. “He actually made you sign a bond?” “Yes, and put my handprint on it. Afterwards he summoned my fifth brother and said, ‘You must mend your ways. If you behave like this again, I shan't punish you. I shall punish the person who stood surety. Think about it: if you land your sister in prison, how can you face your parents?’” “Ah! That was a powerful move.” A’zhu understood the prefect's purpose. “Even if your brother feared neither heaven nor earth, he'd have to think of you. Whatever happened, he'd have to improve his temper.” “Exactly! That is why I say the prefect wasn't stupid at all.” The Seventh Lady continued, “When we left the court, the old master came to fetch me himself. At his house he had laid on ten tables of food, and all the brothers in the association were there. He made me take the seat of honour. ‘It's a pity A’qi is a woman,’ he said. ‘If she were a man, I would take her as my disciple before closing my doors to any more pupils.’” “Seventh Sister!” A’zhu was absorbed. “I never imagined you had made such a name for yourself.” “Ah!” The Seventh Lady gave a long sigh. “It was making that name that ruined things.”

“怎么呢？”阿珠诧异地问。是老于世故的，就不会觉得诧异。以七姑奶奶的性情，出了这样一回风头，自不免得意非凡，从此以后，也象男子汉一样，伸手管事，“吃讲茶”常有她一份。豪情胜概，自然会把女孩儿家的温柔、消折殆尽。“女人总是女人。”七姑奶奶不胜悔怨地说：“女人不象女人，要女人做啥？象我这样子，弄到头来，吃亏的是自己。”这句话说得极深。七姑奶奶以过来人的资格，才有此“见道之言”。阿珠既警惕，又感动。警惕的是女人争强好胜，使得男人敬神而远之，实在欠聪明。感动的是七姑奶奶的这些话，真正是肺腑之言，对旁人是决不肯说的。“七姐！”阿珠也还报以真情，“你不说，我不敢说，你既然说了，我倒要劝你。你不开口坐在那里，真正是一尊观音菩萨，一开口就比申大娘娘还要厉害。如果申大娘娘不是雌老虎，申大爷不会迷上那几个‘师太’，一条命也不会送掉。我劝你，也要象五哥一样，把脾气好好改一改。““我何尝不想改？”七姑奶奶摇摇头，不说下去了。这是说改不掉？阿珠在想，改不掉就不会有男人敢要她。真的守一辈子寡？想守出一座贞节牌坊来？她疑心七姑奶奶守不住。但这话说出来会得罪人，所以几次想开口，终于还是忍住了。

“How?” A’zhu asked in surprise. Someone more experienced in the world would not have been surprised. Given the Seventh Lady's temperament, such a success was bound to make her exceedingly proud. From then on, she intervened in affairs like a man and often took part in the gatherings at tea houses where disputes were settled. Her boldness and high spirits gradually wore away the gentleness expected of a young woman. “A woman is still a woman,” she said regretfully. “If she doesn't behave like one, what is the use of being a woman? In the end, someone like me is the one who suffers.” There was deep meaning in this. Only experience had brought the Seventh Lady such insight. A’zhu felt both warned and moved. It was foolish, she thought, for a woman to be so fiercely competitive that men kept a respectful distance. Yet she was moved that the Seventh Lady should speak from her heart as she would never do to others. “Seventh Sister, I wouldn't have dared say it if you hadn't. But now you have, I must offer some advice. When you sit without speaking, you look just like the Goddess of Mercy. Once you open your mouth, you're more formidable than Madam Shen. If Madam Shen hadn't been such a tigress, Master Shen would never have become infatuated with those nuns and lost his life. I think you should do as your fifth brother did and try to change your temper.” “Do you suppose I haven't wanted to?” The Seventh Lady shook her head and said no more. Did she mean she could not change? A’zhu wondered. If she could not, no man would dare marry her. Would she truly remain a widow all her life, hoping to earn a memorial arch for chastity? A’zhu doubted that she could endure it. But saying so would offend her. Several times she was about to speak; each time she held back.

“我问你，”七姑奶奶突如其来地说：“你看阿龙这个人怎么样？”“又要提到他了。”阿珠想拦住她，因而持意装出不悦的神情，“你为啥这么关心他？”七姑奶奶笑了，略带些忸怩的神色，这样的神色，阿珠几乎还是第一次看见，在她的印象中，七姑奶奶从不知什么难为情，因而这一丝忸怩之色，便特别引人注意。阿珠想起她平日对陈世龙的殷勤，深悔失言，自己的这句话，可能在七姑奶奶听来刺耳。正想有所弥补时，七姑奶奶说出一番令人大吃一惊的话来：“不错，我关心他。老实跟你说了吧，我也想过好几回，要么不嫁，要嫁，现成有在那里！”“现成有在那里”的，自然是陈世龙。话说得如此赤裸裸，阿珠简直不能相信自己的耳朵，回忆一遍，并未听错。这一来，心里的滋味，便不好受了，脸上的神色，也不好看了，勉强笑着问了声：“你是说哪个？陈世龙？”“是啊，陈世龙。”七姑奶奶看了看她的脸色，又问，“你看我嫁他配不配？”

“Tell me,” the Seventh Lady asked abruptly, “what do you think of A’long?” “Back to him again!” Hoping to stop her, A’zhu deliberately looked displeased. “Why are you so interested in him?” The Seventh Lady smiled with a trace of shyness. A’zhu could hardly remember seeing such an expression on her before. In her experience, the woman never knew embarrassment, so even this hint of it was striking. Recalling the attention she usually paid Chen, A’zhu regretted her question. It might have sounded offensive. Just as she was trying to make amends, the Seventh Lady said something astonishing. “You're right. I am interested in him. I'll tell you honestly: I've thought several times that, if I ever marry again, there's someone ready to hand.” The man ready to hand was, of course, Chen Shilong. So blunt a declaration seemed incredible. A’zhu went over it in her mind, but she had heard correctly. Her feelings became unpleasant, and so did her expression. Forcing a smile, she asked, “Who do you mean? Chen Shilong?” “Yes, Chen Shilong.” The Seventh Lady studied her face. “Do you think he and I would make a suitable match?”

真正脸皮厚，居然问得出来！阿珠心想：你不怕难为情，我就胡胡你的调。因而点点头说：“配！怎么不配？”“你倒说说看，我跟他怎么样的相配？”“这话就奇怪了。”阿珠依然是很勉强的笑容，“怎么样的相配，你自己总想过，何用来问我？”“我跟你开开玩笑的。”七姑奶奶在她脸上轻轻拧了一把，“我怎么会跟他相配？第一，年纪不对，第二，身分不配，他没讨过亲，要娶自然娶个黄花闺女，第三，脾气不配，他的性子也是好胜的，两个人在一起，他不让我，我不让他，非天天吵架不可。”阿珠不知怎么，颇有如释重负之感，但因为她言语闪烁，一会儿象熬有介事，一会儿又说“开玩笑”，所以大起戒心，不敢轻易答话，只微笑着作出不甚关心的样子，同时很仔细地观察她的脸色。“你说，我的话对不对？”“也不见得对！”阿珠很谨慎地回答，反过来试探她：“七姐，陈世龙娶了你，也有很多好处。象你这样的人才，打了灯笼都没处去寻的，又漂亮，又能干，而且还有五哥的照应。再好都没有了。”“真的？”七姑奶奶有意相问。语气中听得出来，有说她作违心之论的意味在内。阿珠有些发窘，但不容不答，更不容改口，硬着头皮答道：“自然是真的。”

What nerve, to ask such a question! A’zhu thought that, if the Seventh Lady felt no embarrassment, she might as well humour her. She nodded. “Certainly. Why wouldn't you?” “Then tell me what makes us suitable.” “That's a strange question.” A’zhu's smile was still forced. “You must have considered that yourself. Why ask me?” “I'm only teasing you.” The Seventh Lady gently pinched her cheek. “How could we be suitable? First, our ages are wrong. Second, our circumstances don't match. He's never married. Naturally, he'll want a maiden. Third, our temperaments don't suit each other. He likes to have his own way too. If we lived together, he wouldn't yield to me and I wouldn't yield to him. We'd quarrel every day.” A’zhu felt unaccountably relieved. Yet the Seventh Lady's shifting words—serious one moment, a joke the next—made her wary. She dared not answer readily and merely smiled as though little concerned, while carefully studying the woman's expression. “Am I right?” “Not necessarily,” A’zhu replied cautiously, now testing her in turn. “There would be plenty of advantages for Chen if he married you. You could search with a lantern and not find another woman like you: pretty, capable, and with Brother Five to support you. He couldn't do better.” “Really?” The Seventh Lady asked with deliberate emphasis. Her tone suggested that A’zhu did not mean what she said. Embarrassed, A’zhu nevertheless had to answer and could not retract her words. She braced herself. “Of course.”

七姑奶奶笑一笑不答。随后又说：“话再拉回来，你看阿龙这个人怎么样？”第二次再问，如果依旧避而不答，便显得“有心”了。阿珠想了想说：“我跟他认识的日子也不久，只晓得他人很能干的。”“心呢？”七姑奶奶问，“你看他的心好不好？”“我看不出来。”阿珠说：“有道人心难测。”“别人的心思难测，阿龙的心，你总晓得的。”“又来说疯话了！”阿珠一半害羞、一半赌气，翻个身脸朝里，以背向人。过了一会，没有动静，她当七姑奶奶有些动气了，想回过身来敷衍两句，但外床的人比她快了一步，已经起身下床。“嗨！”她提高了声音喊，“你到哪里去？”

The Seventh Lady smiled without replying. Then she returned to her question. “But tell me, what do you think of A’long?” Asked a second time, A’zhu could not evade it again without revealing that she had something to conceal. After some thought, she said, “I haven't known him long. All I know is that he's very capable.” “And his heart? Do you think he has a good heart?” “I can't tell. As they say, the human heart is hard to fathom.” “Other people's hearts may be hard to fathom. Surely you know A’long's.” “There you go talking nonsense again!” Half embarrassed and half annoyed, A’zhu turned towards the wall, presenting her back. For a while there was no response. Thinking the Seventh Lady had taken offence, she meant to turn back and say something conciliatory. But the woman beside her moved first and got out of bed. “Hey!” A’zhu called. “Where are you going?”

“哪里也不去。”七姑奶奶吹灭灯，仍旧回到床上。她过分亲昵的举动让阿珠感到不自在。阿珠轻声抗议，要她让开一些，她才稍稍松开。“现在你用不着怕难为情了，”她说，“有话尽管讲。”“我没有什么话好讲。”七姑奶奶仍把话引向陈世龙，要阿珠想一想自己对他的感情。一再的逗弄使阿珠越发不好意思。“真不得了，从没有遇见过你这样的人！”七姑奶奶说，想象一下自己盼望的幸福又有什么要紧。阿珠回答，越想只怕越盼望。“这倒是真的。”七姑奶奶承认，却仍想从阿珠的陪伴中得到些安慰。

“Nowhere.” The Seventh Lady blew out the lamp and returned to bed. Her excessive familiarity made A’zhu uneasy. “Give me some room,” A’zhu protested softly, and the Seventh Lady eased back a little. “Now you needn't be embarrassed,” she said. “Say whatever you like.” “I have nothing to say.” The Seventh Lady kept steering the conversation towards Chen and urging A’zhu to consider her feelings for him. The persistent teasing only deepened her embarrassment. “Really! I've never met anyone like you!” The Seventh Lady suggested that there could be no harm in imagining the happiness one wanted. A’zhu answered that imagining it might only make the longing worse. “That's true,” the Seventh Lady admitted, though she continued to seek comfort in A’zhu's company.

阿珠不肯顺着她一再的要求。七姑奶奶随后吐露，自己心里想的是死去的丈夫。阿珠很意外，也生出不忍，便表示体谅，又拿明天谁来服侍谁、谁替谁洗脚开了个玩笑，想使气氛轻松些。“你正好说反了，从前是我们那口子服侍我洗脚。”“我不相信！男子汉大丈夫，做这种没体面的事，真正气数！”“你不懂。夫妻相处有许多意想不到的习惯，将来等你嫁了阿龙就知道了。”又是阿龙！阿珠不作声，争辩也无用，而且觉得越争辩似乎越认真，不如随她说去。她对夫妻间这些意想不到的相处方式感到好奇，却问不出口，只希望七姑奶奶自己说下去。七姑奶奶没有猜到她的心思，见她不响，自己也不开口，沉在自己的回忆里，渐渐困倦，一觉睡到天亮。

A’zhu resisted her insistence. The Seventh Lady then confided that she was thinking of her dead husband. This admission surprised A’zhu and softened her. She responded sympathetically, then tried to lighten the mood with a joke about being waited on and having her feet washed the next day. “You've got it the wrong way round,” the Seventh Lady answered. “My husband used to wash my feet.” “I don't believe it! A grown man doing such an undignified thing! What a sorry state of affairs.” “You don't understand. Married couples have all sorts of unexpected ways. You'll know when you marry A’long.” A’long again! A’zhu remained silent. Arguing would achieve nothing and might even make her seem too concerned. Better to let the woman say what she pleased. She was curious about these unexpected ways of married life, but could not bring herself to ask and hoped the Seventh Lady would explain without prompting. The Seventh Lady did not guess what she was thinking. Since A’zhu said nothing, she too fell silent. Absorbed in her own memories, she grew drowsy and slept until morning.

是阿珠先惊醒，只听见有人叫门：“阿七，阿七！”是尤五嫂的声音：“张家妹子！你醒醒！”“来了！”阿珠听得尤嫂的声音有异，急忙推醒七姑奶奶：“你听，五嫂在叫你，好象出了什么事似地。”七姑奶奶定定神，一骨碌下床，拔开门闩，只见尤五嫂的脸色有些惊惶。“怎么搞的！都叫不醒。”尤五嫂一脚跨进门来，拉住七姑奶奶的手，连摇撼：“小刀会造反，上海昨天失守了。”“喔！”七姑奶奶回身看了看阿珠，“不要把她吓一跳！到我房里去说。”这句话反而说坏了，阿珠的耳朵尖，已经听得清清楚楚，急急赶过来问道：“七姐，出了什么事？”“你慌啥？”七姑奶奶很沉着地指着她嫂子说：“我也是刚听她说，说上海失守了！”

A’zhu woke first to someone calling at the door. “A’qi! A’qi!” It was Mrs You. “Young Zhang sister, wake up!” “Coming!” Something unusual in her voice alarmed A’zhu. She hurriedly woke the Seventh Lady. “Listen, Fifth Sister-in-Law is calling you. It sounds as though something has happened.” Collecting herself, the Seventh Lady sprang from bed and unbolted the door. Mrs You stood outside looking frightened. “What's the matter with you? I couldn't wake either of you!” She stepped inside, seized the Seventh Lady's hand, and shook it repeatedly. “The Small Swords Society has risen. Shanghai fell yesterday.” “Oh!” The Seventh Lady glanced back at A’zhu. “Don't frighten her. Come to my room and tell me.” That only made matters worse. A’zhu's hearing was sharp, and she had already caught every word. Hurrying over, she asked, “Seventh Sister, what's happened?” “Why panic?” Calmly, the Seventh Lady pointed to her sister-in-law. “I've only just heard it from her myself. She says Shanghai has fallen.”

阿珠何能不慌？小刀会要起事的消息，事先她毫无所闻，只想到上海失守，她父亲便要陷在里面，还有陈世龙，还有胡雪岩，都是有关系的人，如今一起都有危险，因而急得快要哭了。“你怎么想不穿！”这些时候，就看出七姑奶奶的“本事”来了，说出话来，明白有力：“我五哥也在上海，难道我倒不急？”想想不错，尤五嫂似乎也不怎么着急，可见得事情不要紧，再想到尤五的手面，越发心宽。当然，关切还是关切，不过看她们姑嫂有正事要谈，只得暂时忍耐，回头再来打听。尤五嫂没有工夫来管她，拉着七姑奶奶的手说：“你快去穿衣服。嘉定有人来了，你去跟他见个面。”听她这一说，七姑奶奶拉着尤五嫂就走，到了她自己房里匆匆漱洗，拢一拢头发，穿裙着衫，走来走会地忙着。尤五嫂便跟来跟去，把嘉定来客的话，告诉了她。

How could A’zhu not panic? She had heard nothing beforehand about the Small Swords Society's intended rising. All she could think was that, with Shanghai lost, her father would be trapped there. Chen Shilong too, and Hu Xueyan—all of them mattered to her, and now all were in danger together. She was almost in tears. “Why can't you think it through?” At such moments, the Seventh Lady's ability showed itself. Her words were clear and forceful. “My fifth brother is in Shanghai too. Do you suppose I don't care?” On reflection, that was true. Mrs You did not seem excessively anxious either, so perhaps things were not very serious. Remembering You Laowu's influence and connections reassured A’zhu further. Naturally, she remained concerned. But the two women had urgent business to discuss, and she could only be patient for the moment and ask more later. Mrs You had no time to attend to her. Taking the Seventh Lady's hand, she said, “Quickly, go and dress. Someone has come from Jiading. You must see him.” At once the Seventh Lady drew her sister-in-law away to her own room, where she hurriedly washed, smoothed her hair, and put on her skirt and blouse, moving busily back and forth. Mrs You followed her about, repeating what the visitor from Jiading had said.
