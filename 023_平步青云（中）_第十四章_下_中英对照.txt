就这样谈着夷场风月，不知不觉到了恰情院。一进门就见相帮、娘姨、大姐聚在一起，指指点点在小声说笑，似乎遇见了什么神秘而有趣的事，胡雪岩便好奇地问道：“你们在讲啥？”“胡老爷，有位堂客在里面，跟二小姐谈得好亲热。”“堂客！”胡雪岩诧异：“堂子里只住官客，哪来的堂客？”说着便站往了脚，因为有堂客在里面，虽未“放门帘”，也不便乱闯。“不要紧！胡老爷你请进去看了，就晓得了。”古应春比胡雪岩更好奇，听得“不要紧”三字，首先就拔脚进门，只觉眼前一亮，那位堂客如雪山皑皑，令人不可逼视。这位丰腴丰皙、艳光照人的少妇，正是七姑奶奶。看见闯来的那个陌生男子，长身如鹤，英气勃勃，不觉心中一动，五百年风流冤家，就此在不该相遇的地方遇到了。

Talking of romance in the foreign settlement, they reached Yiqing House almost without noticing. Inside the entrance, the male and female attendants and maids were gathered together, pointing and laughing quietly as though something mysterious and amusing had occurred. Curious, Hu asked, “What are you all talking about?” “Mr Hu, there's a lady inside, having a very friendly conversation with the Second Miss.” “A lady!” Hu was astonished. “A courtesan house receives gentlemen. Where has a lady come from?” He stopped. With a woman visiting, it would be improper to walk straight in, even though the curtain had not been lowered. “It's all right, Mr Hu. Go in and see, and you'll understand.” Gu Yingchun was even more curious. At the words “It's all right,” he stepped through the door ahead of Hu. His eyes were dazzled: the visitor was as radiantly fair as a snow-covered mountain, almost too brilliant to look upon. The full-figured, fair-skinned young woman, radiant with beauty, was the Seventh Lady. Seeing the stranger—tall as a crane and full of spirit—she felt an involuntary stirring. A pair of lovers destined for each other five hundred years before had met where they ought never to have met.

一半是不知如何招呼，一半是目炫心迷，正当他们错愕无语，而怡情老二也觉得为难之际，胡雪岩跟了进来，一看亦大感意外：“咦，七姐！是你。”有人搭腔，事情便好办了，七姑奶奶向来说话粗声大气，不堪领教，这时不知是受了恰情老二一口吴侬软语的感染，还是因为有古应春这个一见便生好感的陌生男客在，心存顾忌，居然斯斯文文地喊一声：“小爷叔，你想不到我在这里吧？”自然想不到，胡雪岩心想，兄弟一起逛堂子的事，听说过，兄妹一起逛堂子，却是天大的新闻。便点点头说：“我道是哪位堂客？怎么样也想不到是你。”“请坐，请坐！”怡情老二看古应春和七姑奶奶偷眼相望，随即说道：“胡老爷，你来引见吧！”于是胡雪岩为古应春及七姑奶奶作了介绍，一个盈盈含笑，把双手放在左腰上，福了一福，一个抱拳作揖说道：“原来是七姐！真正伉爽不让须眉。”七姑奶奶懂了他那句语，虽是恭维，却也有惊诧的意味在内，想想一个良家妇女，独闯娼门，说起来是有些不守妇道，所以很难得地害了羞，红着脸报以微笑。她的笑容最甜，虽是窘笑，依然妩媚。古应春心里在想：倒不曾料到，尤五有这样漂亮的一个妹妹！

Neither knew quite how to greet the other, and both were dazzled and bewildered. They stood in startled silence, while Yiqing's Second Miss wondered what to do. Then Hu came in and was equally surprised. “Why, Seventh Sister! It's you.” With someone to begin the conversation, things became easier. The Seventh Lady ordinarily spoke so loudly and roughly that she was difficult to endure. Now, whether influenced by the Second Miss's soft Suzhou speech or restrained by the presence of a stranger who had pleased her at first sight, she spoke with remarkable gentleness. “Young Uncle, you didn't expect to find me here, did you?” Certainly not. Hu had heard of brothers visiting courtesans together, but a brother and sister doing so was extraordinary news. He nodded. “I was wondering who the lady could be. I'd never have thought of you.” “Please sit down, both of you!” Noticing Gu and the Seventh Lady stealing glances at one another, the Second Miss added, “Mr Hu, do introduce them.” Hu did so. Smiling, the Seventh Lady placed both hands at her left waist and curtsied. Gu returned a clasped-hands bow. “So you are Seventh Sister! Your spirit is the equal of any man's.” She understood the compliment, but heard surprise in it too. A respectable woman coming alone into a courtesan house could hardly be called proper conduct. Unusually for her, she felt shy and answered with a blush and a smile. Her smile was exceptionally sweet; even an embarrassed one remained charming. Gu thought that he had never expected You Laowu to have such a beautiful sister.

等怡情老二招呼着坐定，胡雪岩自然要问来意，七姑奶奶坦率相告，因为尤五一夜不曾回家，而她回松江之前还有许多话要问他，心里焦急，所以找上门来。“你一个人来的？”“是啊！”七姑奶奶顽皮而得意地笑道，“我那位妹子不许我来，阿龙也不肯带路，我只好借故溜了出来，自己雇一顶小轿到这里。不曾遇着五哥，倒跟二小姐谈得好投机，”“啊呀！七姑奶奶，”怡情老二不安地笑着：“真正不敢当你这么的称呼，叫我老二好了。”“或者叫小五嫂。”胡雪岩打着趣问：“那么，人呢？”这是指尤五，怡情老二答道：“有朋友约了出去了。说八点钟一定回来，请胡老爷、古老爷务必等他。”“自然要等。”胡雪岩问七姑奶奶，“想来你也还没有吃饭，我们是上馆子，还是就在这里吃。”“自然是在这里吃。”怡情老二急忙接口，“我请七姑奶奶吃便饭，请你们两位作陪客。”“理当奉陪。”

Once the Second Miss had settled them in their seats, Hu naturally asked what had brought the Seventh Lady there. She answered frankly: her brother had stayed out all night, and she had much to ask him before returning to Songjiang. Growing anxious, she had come to find him. “Did you come alone?” “Yes!” She smiled with mischievous satisfaction. “Our young sister wouldn't let me come, and A’long wouldn't show me the way. I had to slip out on an excuse and hire a small sedan chair myself. I haven't found Brother Five, but I've had a splendid talk with the Second Miss.” “Oh, Seventh Lady!” Their hostess smiled uneasily. “Such a formal title is far too much for me. Just call me Second.” “Or Little Fifth Sister-in-Law,” Hu teased. “Well, where is he?” He meant You Laowu. “A friend invited him out,” the Second Miss replied. “He said he'd certainly be back at eight and asked both of you to wait.” “Of course we shall.” Hu turned to the Seventh Lady. “I suppose you haven't eaten either. Shall we go to a restaurant or eat here?” “Here, naturally!” the Second Miss interrupted eagerly. “I'm inviting the Seventh Lady to a simple meal, with you two gentlemen to keep her company.” “We shall be honoured to join you,” Gu replied.

古应春都答应了，胡雪岩还有什么话说？七姑奶奶却是外场人物，招招手把他叫到一边，悄悄问道：“小爷叔，这里的规矩，我不大懂。你看，这顿饭该不该吃？”“来都来了，还讲什么规矩？”七姑奶奶脸一红，“本来是没有这种规矩的，我大着胆子乱闯。只怕叫人笑死了！”说着，悄伶伶一双眼睛瞟了过去。胡雪岩顺着她的眼光看过去，恍炊大悟，怪不得“女张飞”这般斯文！当时只有一个念头，要成人之美。于是他轻轻一道：“七姐，你请过来，我有句话说。“怡情院的那个“大房间”甚大，西面用个“多宝格”隔开，他领着她到里面，在窗下红木太师椅上坐下，两人的脸都朝外，透过多宝格，只见古应春和怡情老二也正谈到起劲，不会注意到他们的谈后，于是胡雪岩才出言规劝。“七姐”他用兄妹般，极恳切的声音说，“你不开口，是尊观音，开出口来，说句实话，别人吃你不消！今天总算难得，替五哥做了面子。回头你自己再做忌些，那样子，人家就不会笑你了。”在平日，七姑奶奶对他这话，一定不服帖，这时却是窘笑着点一点头说：“我晓得了。就是这句话吗？”“就是这句话。”胡雪岩说：“你是玲珑七窍心，自己有数就是，何必还要我多说呢？”这话有言外之意，七姑奶奶想再问些什么，到底还不好意思出口，只很妩媚地笑着道谢：“谢谢你，小爷叔！”

Since Gu had accepted, what could Hu say? The Seventh Lady, however, was accustomed to dealing with people. She beckoned Hu aside and asked quietly, “Young Uncle, I don't know the customs here. Do you think I ought to stay for this meal?” “Now that you've come, why worry about customs?” She blushed. “There certainly isn't any custom of this kind. I simply took my courage in both hands and came. I'm afraid people will laugh themselves to death at me.” As she spoke, her lively eyes glanced across the room. Hu followed her gaze and understood. No wonder the female Zhang Fei had become so refined! His immediate impulse was to help a happy match along. “Seventh Sister, come over here,” he said softly. “There's something I want to tell you.” The large room at Yiqing House was spacious indeed. Open display shelves divided off the western side. He led her behind them, and they sat in rosewood armchairs beneath the window, both facing outwards. Through the shelves, they could see Gu and the Second Miss deep in conversation, unlikely to notice their own talk. Only then did Hu offer his advice. “Seventh Sister,” he said earnestly, as a brother might, “when you say nothing, you look like the Goddess of Mercy. But once you begin to speak, people frankly find you too much. Today, for once, you've done Brother Five credit. Keep a little more restraint as the evening goes on, and nobody will laugh at you.” Ordinarily, she would have resented this. Now she merely smiled awkwardly and nodded. “I understand. Is that all?” “That is all. You're clever enough to grasp the slightest hint. Once you understand, why should I say more?” There was meaning beneath the words. She wanted to ask further, but could not quite bring herself to do so. With a charming smile, she simply said, “Thank you, Young Uncle.”

两人走到外面，怡情老二迎上来说：“古老爷的话不错，这里大嘈杂，请到我‘小房了’去吃吧！”姑娘与恩客另营不虑人干扰的双宿双飞之处，叫做“小房子”。抬情老二的小房子就在这条弄堂的末尾，也是尤五每个月贴开销，但尤五的朋友多，在怡情院会客比较方便，所以难得到小房子去。想不到这时候倒派上了用场。胡雪岩自然赞成，回头对七姑奶奶说道：“那是老二住家的地方，比较清静，走吧！”于是怡情老二关照相帮，凡有“局票”来，只说病了，不能出“堂差”，又关照，等尤五一来，请到小房子去。这一下倒提醒七姑奶奶了，依然是把胡雪岩喊到一边，悄悄说道：“我是溜出来的。不见我的人，他们会发急。”这是指阿珠和陈世龙而言，“那好办！”他说，“叫人去通知一声就是了。”当时写了个便条，说七姑奶奶与尤五在一起，到时自回，不必着急。胡雪岩掏了个银角子做力钱，叫怡情院的相帮，立刻送交陈世龙。

When they emerged, the Second Miss came to meet them. “Mr Gu is right. It's too noisy here. Let's eat at my little house.” A courtesan's “little house” was a separate retreat where she and a favoured patron could be together undisturbed. Hers stood at the far end of the lane. You Laowu paid its monthly expenses too, but with so many friends to entertain, he found Yiqing House more convenient and seldom used the retreat. Unexpectedly, it now had a purpose. Hu naturally approved. Turning to the Seventh Lady, he said, “It's where she lives. Much quieter. Let's go.” The Second Miss instructed the male attendant that, if any invitations arrived, he must say she was ill and could not attend outside engagements. When You arrived, he was to be directed to the little house. This reminded the Seventh Lady of something. Calling Hu aside again, she said softly, “I slipped out. If they can't find me, they'll worry.” She meant A’zhu and Chen. “That's easily managed. We'll send someone to tell them.” He wrote a note saying that the Seventh Lady was with You Laowu and would return in due course; there was no need for anxiety. Taking out a small silver coin as a tip, he told the attendant to deliver it to Chen immediately.

办妥了这一切，一起走到怡情老二的小房子，是一楼一底的石库门房子，楼下是另一家，她住楼上，布置得楚楚有致，看上去是很舒服的地方。刚刚坐定，怡情院里自己做的酒菜。已经送到。怡情老二和古应春都要推七姑奶奶上座，她则一定不肯，结果是古应春首座，她和胡雪岩两对面，主人未座，正好各据一方。款客的是红葡萄酒，古应春送的洋酒。据说那是补血的，连宫里都经常饮用。怡情老二把它看得很珍贵，殷殷相劝。七姑奶奶的酒量，也还不坏，但一心只记着胡雪岩的忠告，强持着不肯多喝，也不多说话。席面上只听古应春在谈胡雪岩上外国酒馆的经过，七姑奶奶和怡情老二都听得只是笑。古应春这天的兴致很好，谈笑风生，滔滔不绝，一直到尤五出现，话锋才被打断。

With everything settled, they went to the Second Miss's little house. It was a two-storey building with a stone-framed gateway. Another household occupied the ground floor; she lived upstairs, where everything was arranged with taste and care. It looked very comfortable. They had barely sat down when the food and wine prepared at Yiqing House arrived. Both their hostess and Gu urged the Seventh Lady to take the seat of honour, but she firmly refused. In the end, Gu sat at the head, she and Hu faced one another, and their hostess took the lowest place, one person on each side of the table. The drink was a foreign red grape wine, a gift from Gu. It was said to nourish the blood and to be drunk regularly even at the imperial court. The Second Miss prized it highly and pressed it on her guests. The Seventh Lady could hold her liquor, but with Hu's advice firmly in mind, she made herself drink little and speak little. Gu did most of the talking, recounting Hu's visit to the foreign tavern and keeping both women laughing. He was in excellent spirits that day, witty and fluent, and his stream of conversation continued until You Laowu appeared.

兄妹相见，都有些不自然的表情，尤五的不悦，还可以想象得到，但对七姑奶奶的微现俱惮，胡雪岩却有些意外，在他的印象中，七姑奶奶行事任性，从不知什么叫害怕？平日只见尤五有些怕她，此刻为何她怕尤五？这就是为了有古应春在座的缘故。胡雪岩很快的想通了，她怕她哥哥责备她几句，当着古应春下不得台。既然如此，倒要小心防护她，因此，他首先就替她解释不能不来的缘故。接着便谈与哈德逊会面的经过，算是让尤五忘掉了对七姑奶奶的不快。自此开始，就没有工夫说笑了，许多正事要商量，头绪纷繁，一件事没有办妥，又扯到第二件。直到午夜，还未安排停当。“怎么办呢？我非早早赶回杭州不可。”胡雪岩有些着急，“一直都觉得人不够用，此刻越觉得摆布不开。”半天未曾开口的七七姑奶奶开口了：“也没有什么摆布不开！小爷叔你明天尽管动身，路上没有人送，我送，保你到了嘉兴，我再回松江。”“这倒也是个办法！”尤五点点头，“好在一路上，阿七都熟。就这样吧！你到了杭州，赶快派世龙拿了公事到松江来接洋枪。”他们兄妹这一番对答，使得古应春大为惊奇，“原来七姐是这么能干！”他自愧不如以外，也为她担忧，“这条路上，这几天很不好走，要当心！”“谢谢你！”她报以矜持的微笑，“不要紧的。”

Brother and sister greeted each other awkwardly. You's displeasure was understandable, but the slight fear in the Seventh Lady's manner surprised Hu. In his experience, she did exactly as she pleased and knew nothing of fear. Usually her brother seemed somewhat afraid of her. Why was she afraid of him now? Hu soon understood: Gu was present. She feared a reprimand from her brother that would embarrass her before him. Hu must protect her. He began by explaining why she had had to come, then recounted their meeting with Hudson, successfully diverting You from his displeasure. After that there was no time for jokes. Many practical matters needed discussion, so entangled that they passed to a second before resolving the first. By midnight, everything was still not arranged. “What are we to do? I must get back to Hangzhou soon.” Hu was anxious. “I've always felt short of people, but now I can hardly manage at all.” The Seventh Lady, silent for a long time, spoke up. “There's nothing so unmanageable about it. Go tomorrow, Young Uncle. If there's nobody to escort you, I'll do it. I'll see you safely to Jiaxing and then return to Songjiang.” “That would be a solution,” You said, nodding. “A’qi knows the whole route well. Let's settle it like that. Once you reach Hangzhou, send Shilong to Songjiang as quickly as possible with the official papers to collect the foreign guns.” Gu was astonished by the exchange. “So Seventh Sister is capable of all this!” He felt outdone, but worried for her too. “That route has been difficult these last few days. You must be careful.” “Thank you.” She gave him a restrained smile. “It will be quite all right.”

“真的不要紧！”到这时候，尤五总算找到机会，可以说她一句了，“我们家这位姑奶奶，一个人乱闯闯惯了的。”“也不是什么乱闯。”七姑奶奶觉得必须分辩，“有把握的地方我才敢去，摸不清路道的地方，我也不敢乱闯。象这里，我就晓得是不要紧的。”“对啊！”怡情老二接口说道，“要是不嫌弃，常常请过来，这里就跟自己家一样。”“听见没有，五哥！”七姑奶奶碍意地，“就跟自己家一样！”“只有一件，”古应春也凑趣说笑，“回去在五嫂面前瞒着点。”“这倒不碍事。我五嫂最贤慧，不管他这笔帐。”“好了，好了！”尤五看看钟说，“该走了。”

“Quite all right indeed!” At last You had found an opportunity to reprimand her. “Our lady here is used to rushing off anywhere by herself.” “I don't simply rush off anywhere.” She felt obliged to defend herself. “I go where I'm confident of things. I wouldn't venture where I didn't know my way. Here, for instance, I knew there was nothing to worry about.” “Exactly!” the Second Miss said. “If you don't think it beneath you, please come often. Treat this place as your own home.” “Did you hear, Brother Five?” the Seventh Lady asked triumphantly. “My own home!” “Only one thing,” Gu added playfully. “Keep it quiet before Fifth Sister-in-Law when you return.” “There's no need. She's exceedingly good-natured. She doesn't hold this sort of thing against him.” “Enough, enough!” You looked at the clock. “It's time to go.”

于是古应春首先告辞，却悄悄拉了胡雪岩一把。知道是有话说。胡雪岩便跟着古应春下楼出门，站定了脚笑道：“你可是要跟我打听一个人？”“咦！”古应春诧异：“你怎么知道？”“你别管！说吧，可是要问七姑奶奶？”“是的。”古应春说，“我听老二告诉我，她似乎居孀多年。可有这话？”“有的。不过也不算多年。”“倒守得住？”这是指七姑奶奶守节为何守不住，胡雪岩觉得他的话问得好笑，而且难以回答，只好半开玩笑地答道：“你何不自己去问她？”

Gu was the first to take leave, quietly giving Hu a tug. Understanding that he wished to speak, Hu followed him downstairs and outside. Stopping, he asked with a smile, “Do you want to inquire about someone?” “Why!” Gu was surprised. “How did you know?” “Never mind that. Tell me—is it the Seventh Lady?” “Yes. The Second Miss told me she has apparently been widowed for many years. Is that true?” “Yes, though it hasn't been so many years.” “And she's managed to remain a chaste widow?” He meant her fidelity to her late husband. Why should she not have managed it? Hu found the question amusing and difficult to answer. Half joking, he replied, “Why don't you ask her yourself?”

古应春也发觉自己失言，只好报以苦笑。就这时候看到尤五兄妹和怡情老二，已经走下楼来，古应春心想，明天胡雪岩就要走了，此一去又有多日暌隔，而自己有一番心事非要跟他商量不可，因而便向尤五说道：“五哥，你们先请。我跟胡雪岩还有些事要商量。”尤五还不曾开口，怡情老二便说：“何不请到我那里去谈？”这就是胡雪岩机警了，不等古应春开口，他先就搭话：“实在是我有点私事托应春兄，就在这里谈一谈好了，你们先请过去，我们马上就到。”“那么，快点来。”怡情老二说：“等你们来吃消夜。等他们走远了，胡雪岩便问：“应春兄，是在这里谈，还是找个地方坐坐呢？我看你要谈的事，不是三言两语所能变得清楚的。”“你大概也猜到了。”古应春说，“七姑奶奶的相貌、风度，很对我的劲。我托你做个媒。“

Gu realised that he had said the wrong thing and could only offer a rueful smile. Just then, he saw You, the Seventh Lady, and the Second Miss coming downstairs. Hu was leaving tomorrow and would be away for many days, yet Gu had something on his mind that he must discuss with him. “Brother Five, please go ahead,” he said. “Hu Xueyan and I have something to talk over.” Before You could answer, the Second Miss said, “Why not come and talk at my place?” Hu was quick enough to intervene before Gu spoke. “Actually, I have a little private business to ask Brother Yingchun about. We'll discuss it here. Please go ahead. We'll join you shortly.” “Then don't be long. We'll wait for you to have supper.” Once they were out of earshot, Hu asked, “Shall we talk here, Brother Yingchun, or find somewhere to sit? I suspect this isn't something that can be settled in a few words.” “You've probably guessed. The Seventh Lady's looks and manner suit me very well. I'd like you to act as matchmaker.”

胡雪岩想不到他这么开门见山，就说了出来，一时倒有些无从答复，愣在那里，半晌无声。“怎么样？”古应春很关切的问，“是不是有难处？”“有没有难处，还不知道。”胡雪岩说，“你总先把你的情形跟我说一说。”“对，对！这是我的疏忽……”古应春说了他的家庭，父母都在广东，也娶过亲，只是妻子已经过世，有个女儿，今年十六岁，随祖父母在乡，如此而已。“那倒好，没有什么罗嗦。”胡雪岩说：“七姑奶奶就因为跟她婆太太合不来，才回的娘家，照你府上这情形，如果不回广东，大概她也愿意。”“那……”古应春反倒迟疑了，“不回广东是办不到的。无论如何要回去见一见家父、家母。”“那自然。我是说不回广东乡下去住，你们夫妇在上海自立门户。这都是以后的事……”胡雪岩沉吟着说：“看样子，七姑奶奶对你，倒也还中意。不过，我有句话，一定要说在前面。““是，是。你说！我总尽力照办。”“不是要你什么‘照办’！是要你忍耐。你晓不晓得七姑奶奶有个外号，叫做‘女张飞’！”“是不是说她脾气暴躁？”古应春摇摇头，“我看倒不象‘女张飞’！”

Hu had not expected him to come straight to the point. For a while he could find no answer and stood silent. “Well?” Gu asked anxiously. “Is there some difficulty?” “I don't know yet whether there is. First, tell me about your own circumstances.” “Of course. How thoughtless of me.” Gu described his family. Both parents were in Guangdong. He had married, but his wife was dead. His daughter, now sixteen, lived with her grandparents in their home district. That was all. “Then there should be little complication,” Hu said. “The Seventh Lady returned to her own family because she couldn't get on with her mother-in-law. Given your circumstances, I imagine she might agree, provided she didn't have to return to Guangdong.” “But...” Now Gu hesitated. “Never going back to Guangdong would be impossible. Whatever happens, we must visit my father and mother.” “Naturally. I meant not settling in the countryside there. You and your wife would establish your own household in Shanghai. Those are matters for later.” Hu considered. “The Seventh Lady seems to like you too. But there's something I must say beforehand.” “Please do. I'll do my best to comply.” “It's patience you need, not compliance. Do you know that the Seventh Lady has a nickname—the female Zhang Fei?” “Do you mean she has a violent temper?” Gu shook his head. “She doesn't seem like a female Zhang Fei to me.”

这一半是“情人眼里出西施”，一半也是七姑奶奶特意收敛，看样子好事可谐，但情愿还是先把话说得深些，劝他慎重的好。“应春兄，”他说，“日子太浅，相知不深，好在以后见面的时候有得是，你何不看一看再说？”听语气是七姑奶奶有着不便说破的缺点，自己去看，当然最好。但古应春鳏居十年，一下子动了心，有如古井重波，心澜难平，急于要问个明白，所以接下来又说：“看归看，听归听！你多告诉我些。”胡雪岩不知该告诉他些什么？七姑奶奶的情形，他耳闻目见的很多，但不能一昧说好话，更不能一味说坏话。如果是寻常女子，品貌过得去，他一定尽说好话，促成美事，因为那可以断定，决不会成为怨偶。而七姑奶奶与众不同，做媒的责任甚重，真仿佛一言可兴邦，也可丧邦，谁能受得了她的脾气，她便一定是个贤内助，否则，感情会搞得极坏，媒人挨骂一辈子，于心何安？

Partly, beauty lay in the lover's eyes; partly, the Seventh Lady had deliberately restrained herself. A happy outcome seemed possible, but Hu preferred to speak frankly and urge caution. “Brother Yingchun, you've known each other too briefly. There will be plenty of opportunities to meet. Why not observe her for a while before deciding?” His tone suggested some fault that could not conveniently be stated. Seeing for himself would of course be best. But after ten years as a widower, he had suddenly been moved, like a long-still well stirred into waves. His heart would not settle, and he urgently wanted to understand. “I shall observe her, but I should like to hear about her too. Tell me more.” Hu did not know what to tell him. He had seen and heard a good deal about the Seventh Lady, yet he could neither praise her without reserve nor speak only of her faults. Had she been an ordinary woman of acceptable looks and character, he would certainly have praised her to bring about a happy match, confident that the pair would not become enemies. But the Seventh Lady was different. A matchmaker's responsibility was heavy: one word seemed capable of either bringing prosperity to a kingdom or destroying it. To a man who could bear her temper, she would be an excellent helpmate. Otherwise, the marriage would turn bitter, and the matchmaker would be cursed for life. How could he be easy in his conscience?

“说实话，你们都是一见钟情，瞒不过我，我也用不着你说，就已经想来做这个媒。应春兄，胡雪岩非常恳切的说，”你知道我的，我做事一向性子急，但这件事，实在急不得！为啥呢？七姑奶奶的好处，是别人没有的，她的叫人啼笑皆非的脾气，也是别人没有的，所以你要我说，我实在说不象。要你自己看，反正我总一定帮你的忙，做你的参赞。再透个信息给你，七姑奶奶的愿守不愿守，她兄嫂都做不得她的主。现在她似乎也看中你了，那你就请放心，好事迟早必成。“这番话对古应春是颗定心丸，而且启发甚多，大致七姑奶奶是个巾帼须眉，个性极强，遇事敢当。这样性格刚强的人，要看自己能不能驾驭得住她？驾驭得住，百炼钢化为绕指柔，闺房中仍有画盾之乐，驾驭不住，一辈子是她系在裤腰带上的裙下之囚。“多谢，多谢！就你这几句话，我已受惠非浅。走吧！”

“Honestly, you were both taken with each other at first sight. You couldn't conceal it from me. Before you said anything, I was already thinking of arranging the match. Brother Yingchun,” Hu said with great earnestness, “you know how impatient I usually am to get things done. But this is something that really cannot be hurried. Why? Because the Seventh Lady has good qualities nobody else possesses, and a temperament, unlike anyone else's, that can leave you unsure whether to laugh or cry. If you ask me to describe her, I simply can't do her justice. You must see for yourself. I shall certainly help and advise you. And here's something else: whether she remains a widow is entirely her own decision. Neither her brother nor his wife can decide for her. Now she seems to have taken a fancy to you, so you may rest assured. Sooner or later, the match is bound to succeed.” These words reassured Gu and gave him much to consider. Evidently, the Seventh Lady had the spirit of a man, a powerful will, and the courage to take responsibility. With such a strong character, the question was whether he could manage her. If he could, tempered steel might become soft enough to wind about his finger, and married life would still offer its tender pleasures, such as painting her brows. If he could not, he would be her prisoner for life, tied to her apron strings. “Thank you, thank you. Those few words have helped me greatly. Let's go.”

两个人一起回到怡情院，只见七姑奶奶跟怡情老二，并坐在床边，喁喁细语，亲热得象姐妹。尤五显然对此感到欣慰，含笑坐在一旁，神态显得很恬静。“来了，来了！”他站起来，兴致勃勃地：“有人送了我一篓蟹，刚才忘了拿到那里去吃了，尝一尝！”于是怡情老二急忙站起来招呼，七姑奶奶自己也要下手帮忙，做主人的一定不准她动手，这是堂子里，七姑奶奶是客，下手帮忙变得也成了主人，那不象话，但她想不到此，最后是胡雪岩递了个眼色，她才会过意来。这使得古应春又得了个极深的印象，他觉得她只是凡事热心。所以显得有些鲁莽。好在她也肯听人教导，绝不是那种蛮不讲理，死不认错的泼妇。这就没有可怕了。

They returned to Yiqing House and found the Seventh Lady and the Second Miss sitting together on the edge of the bed, talking confidentially, as intimate as sisters. You was plainly gratified and sat smiling beside them, looking very peaceful. “Here they are!” He rose with enthusiasm. “Someone sent me a basket of crabs. I forgot to take them over for our meal. Come and try them.” The Second Miss immediately rose to make arrangements. The Seventh Lady wanted to help, but her hostess firmly refused. This was a courtesan house, and the Seventh Lady was a guest. Helping would make her seem another hostess, which would be quite improper. This did not occur to her until Hu gave her a meaningful glance. Gu took a strong impression from the incident. He felt that she was simply eager to help in everything and therefore seemed somewhat rash. Fortunately, she was willing to listen to advice. She was certainly not one of those unreasonable shrews who would never admit a fault. So, he concluded, there was nothing to fear.

摆好桌子，娘姨端出两大盘热气腾腾，加紫苏蒸的阳澄湖大蟹，此是文人墨客笔下的天下第一名物，阳澄湖的尤其出名。特征是“金毛紫背”，通常每只八两，两只一斤，所以称为“对蟹”。七姑奶奶嗜蟹如命，但这时却很斯文，先挑了一只团脐送到尤五面前。“先敬客嘛！”尤五完全是做哥哥教导弟妹的派头。客是两位，论客气应该是古应春，七姑奶奶不知不觉地又有些着急，便拿那只蟹送到胡雪岩面前。“七姐，我们自己人。我自己来！”胡雪岩有些促狭，不但话里挤对她非把那只蟹送给古应春不可，而且还用手往外推谢。“那就你来！”七姑奶奶被逼到差不多的地步。“冲劲”就来了，大大方方地对古应春说，并且还把一小碗姜醋推到他面前。“谢谢！”古应春含着笑说，同时深深看了她一眼。七姑奶奶装作不见，只拿一只蟹在手，看胡雪岩已经自己动手，“便拿向她哥哥面前，然后自己也取一只，同时转眼去看怡情老二。

Once the table was ready, a female attendant brought two large plates of steaming Yangcheng Lake crabs, cooked with perilla. Crabs were celebrated by men of letters as the finest delicacy under heaven, and those from Yangcheng Lake were especially renowned. Their distinguishing features were golden hairs and purplish backs. They generally weighed eight liang apiece, so that two made a jin; hence the name “pair crabs.” The Seventh Lady adored them, but now behaved with great refinement, first choosing a female crab and placing it before her brother. “Guests first!” You spoke like an elder brother instructing his younger siblings. There were two guests; courtesy plainly required her to offer it to Gu. Growing flustered despite herself, she put the crab before Hu instead. “Seventh Sister, we're old friends. I'll help myself.” Mischievously, Hu not only used his words to press her into giving it to Gu, but pushed it away with his hand. “Then you take it!” Driven far enough, she recovered her boldness and addressed Gu openly, also placing a little bowl of ginger vinegar before him. “Thank you,” Gu said with a smile, giving her a long look. Pretending not to notice, she picked up another crab. Seeing that Hu had already helped himself, she gave it to her brother, took one for herself, and glanced towards their hostess.

怡情老二正取了一副吃蟹的家伙出来，纯银打造，小钳小锤子的，看来十分精巧。七姑奶奶觉得好玩，取过小锤子来，一下打在蟹螯上。在她自觉未曾用力，但那只蟹赘已被砸得甲碎肉烂，一塌糊涂了。大家都笑，七姑奶奶也笑，“这东西不是我用的。”她说，“还是用手方便。”她的那只手仿佛生来就是为剥蟹用的，手法熟练非凡，只用一根牙筷帮忙，须臾之间，把一只蟹吃得干干净净，蟹赘、蟹脚和那个“盖”拼凑在一起，看来仍旧是一只蟹。“这倒着实要点本事。”古应春颇为惊异，“我还是第一次见！”广东人的古应春，吃蟹自然没有苏锡嘉湖一带出蟹地方的人来得内行，表里不分，胡嚼一气，吐了一桌子的渣滓，七姑奶奶直性子，实在看不过去，便打趣他说：“你真是猪八戒吃人参果。看我来！”她取了一只蟹，依然只用一根筷子，很快地剥了一盖子的蟹肉，黄白杂陈，倒上姜醋，却不是自己享用，一推推到了古应春面前。这真叫古应春受宠若惊了，但也知不宜显示心中的感觉，所以只是接连说了两声：“多谢，多谢！”

The Second Miss was bringing out a set of crab-eating implements: tiny pincers and hammers made entirely of silver, exquisitely fashioned. Amused by them, the Seventh Lady took a little hammer and struck a claw. She felt that she had hardly used any force, yet the shell shattered and the flesh was mashed into a hopeless mess. Everyone laughed, and she joined them. “These things aren't meant for me. My hands are easier.” Her hands seemed made for shelling crabs. With astonishing skill and only one ivory chopstick to help her, she emptied a crab in moments. Claws, legs, and upper shell could be fitted back together so that it still looked whole. “That certainly takes skill,” Gu said in surprise. “I've never seen it before.” Coming from Guangdong, he naturally knew less about eating crabs than people from the crab-producing districts of Suzhou, Wuxi, Jiaxing, and Huzhou. He failed to distinguish shell from flesh, chewed indiscriminately, and spat debris all over the table. The forthright Seventh Lady could not bear to watch. “You're just like Zhu Bajie swallowing the ginseng fruit without tasting it. Watch me!” Taking another crab and again using only one chopstick, she quickly filled its shell with white flesh and yellow roe. She added ginger vinegar, then pushed the filled shell towards Gu instead of eating it herself. He was overwhelmed by her kindness, but knew better than to display his feelings. He merely said, “Thank you, thank you.”

巧得很，怡情老二正好也用小钳小锤子，敲敲打打，外带嘴咬手剥，也弄了一盖子蟹肉，送给尤五。于是胡雪岩笑道：“你们都有人代劳，只有我没有这份福气！“古应春知道他在打趣七姑奶奶，怕她脸上下不来，有意要把“美人之贻”这回事，看作无所谓，便将那蟹盖推过去说：“你来，你来！”“你舍得？”胡雪岩抓住题目，越发要开玩笑。这话很难回答，要说“舍得”，马上就会惹七姑奶奶在心里骂一句：没良心！想了想这样答道：“在别人，自然舍不得，你老兄又当别论。”“承情之至。不过，只怕你舍得，人家舍不得。”胡雪岩说，“人家辛辛苦苦剥了给你吃的，让我吃掉了，一定会心痛！”话还不曾完，七姑奶奶发急了，“小爷叔！”她用笑容掩饰窘态， “罚酒！你的话真正说得气人。”“是啊！”怡情老二在一旁帮腔，平她的气：“胡老爷话里有骨头，应该罚酒。”“好，好！”胡雪岩原是为古应春试探，看七姑奶奶虽然羞窘，并无温色，觉得试探的结果，大可满意，便欣然引杯，一饮而尽。

As it happened, the Second Miss had also been working with pincers and hammer, helped by her teeth and fingers, and had filled a shell with crabmeat for You. Hu laughed. “You both have someone to do the work for you. I'm the only one without such good fortune.” Gu knew he was teasing the Seventh Lady. Afraid she would be embarrassed, he tried to treat this gift from a beauty as nothing exceptional. Pushing the shell towards Hu, he said, “Here, you have it.” “Could you bear to part with it?” Hu seized the opening for more mischief. It was a difficult question. If Gu said yes, the Seventh Lady might immediately think him ungrateful. After a moment, he answered, “Not for anyone else, naturally. But you are another matter.” “I'm deeply obliged. Only, you may be willing to part with it when someone else is not. After all her hard work shelling it for you, she would surely be heartbroken if I ate it.” Before he finished, the Seventh Lady protested. “Young Uncle!” She hid her embarrassment behind a smile. “You must drink a forfeit. Your words are really exasperating.” “Quite right!” the Second Miss supported her, trying to soothe her feelings. “Mr Hu's words have sharp edges. He must drink.” “Very well, very well!” Hu had been testing the Seventh Lady's feelings on Gu's behalf. Seeing her embarrassed but not offended, he was thoroughly satisfied. He gladly raised his cup and emptied it.

一直坐在那里不说话的尤五，到这时才恍然大悟，他是做哥哥的想法，觉得七姑奶奶不些放浪形骸，心里便不大舒服。胡雪岩鉴貌辨色，看出风向不对，很知趣地把话题引了开去，同时也不肯再多作流连，找个机会，提议散席。时近午夜，而怡情院所在地的那条弄堂，却还热闹得很，卖熟食的小贩，往来如梭，吆喝不停，弄口停着许多小轿，流苏轿帘，玻璃小窗，十分精致，专做深宵寻芳倦客的生意，唯有这天抬着一位堂客——七姑奶奶。回到裕记丝栈，她第一个下轿，往后直奔，刚上楼梯，便扯开喉咙大喊：“张家妹子，你睡了没有？”阿珠还没有睡，先是不放心七姑奶奶，要为她等门，后来是跟陈世龙吃零食闲谈，谈上了劲，倒把要等的人忘掉了。这时听得楼下一喊，方始惊觉，赶紧起身迎了出去。两人在楼涕口相遇，只见七姑奶奶双颊如霞，眼波如水，一片春色，不觉大声而问：“你在哪里吃得这么醉醺醺地回来？”

Only now did You, who had been silent all this time, understand. Thinking as an elder brother, he felt that the Seventh Lady was behaving too freely and became uncomfortable. Reading his expression, Hu saw the change in the air and tactfully turned the conversation elsewhere. Nor did he wish to linger. At a suitable moment, he suggested ending the gathering. It was nearly midnight, but the lane outside Yiqing House was still lively. Food hawkers passed continually, calling their wares. At the entrance stood numerous little sedan chairs, finely made, with tasselled curtains and small glass windows. They catered especially to weary late-night seekers of pleasure. On this occasion alone, one carried a lady—the Seventh Lady. Back at the Yuji Silk Warehouse, she was first out of her chair and hurried towards the rear. Scarcely on the stairs, she called at the top of her voice, “Sister Zhang, are you asleep?” A’zhu was still awake. At first she had stayed up to let the Seventh Lady in, worried about her. Later, chatting and eating snacks with Chen, she had become so absorbed that she forgot whom she was waiting for. The call from below startled her, and she hurried out. They met at the head of the stairs. Seeing the Seventh Lady's rosy cheeks, sparkling eyes, and exuberant glow, A’zhu exclaimed, “Where have you been drinking to come back so tipsy?”

“你看，我带了什么好东西来给你吃！”七姑奶奶把一只细竹篮递了过去。这时胡雪岩和尤五亦已上楼，加上阿龙和闻声起床的老张，挤得满满的一屋子，却只听得七姑奶奶一个人的声音，大讲在怡情院消磨了这一晚上的经过。在老张父女是闻所未闻的奇事，就连陈世尤也觉得这位七姑奶奶胆大得惊人。“你们吃嘛！”最后她揭开了篮盖，里面是六只阳澄湖大蟹。她粗中有细，特别周到，连姜醋都是现成带着的。一则情不可却，再则那蟹也实在诱人，老张父女和陈世龙，便一面剥蟹，一面听七姑奶奶谈怡情院的风光。尤五却向胡雪岩使个眼色，两人避到里面谈心去了。

“Look what I've brought you!” The Seventh Lady handed her a finely woven bamboo basket. Hu and You had now come upstairs too. With Chen and Old Zhang, who had risen at the noise, the room was crowded, yet only the Seventh Lady's voice could be heard, recounting how she had spent the evening at Yiqing House. To Old Zhang and his daughter, it was an unheard-of adventure. Even Chen marvelled at her audacity. “Have some!” At last she lifted the basket lid, revealing six large Yangcheng Lake crabs. Beneath her rough manner, she was remarkably thoughtful: even the ginger vinegar was ready to hand. Her kindness could not be refused, and the crabs themselves were tempting. Old Zhang, A’zhu, and Chen began shelling them as they listened to her description of life at Yiqing House. You, meanwhile, signalled to Hu, and the two withdrew into the inner room for a private conversation.

“小爷叔，”尤五皱着眉头说：“你看我这个妹子越来越不象样，怎么得了？”“不要这么说！”胡雪岩笑嘻嘻地答道，“五哥，我要讨喜酒吃了。你晓得老古跟我怎么说？他要托我做媒！”尤五大为诧异，愣了好一会才问：“是想娶我们阿七。”“对！这才叫一见倾心。姻缘，姻缘，真正是缘分。”“什么缘分？”尤五的双眉皱得更深，“说起来是在堂子里见过面，那有多难听！”这个回答大出胡雪岩的意料，一时不知如何为他和七姑奶奶譬解？愣在那里，好半晌作声不得。“我倒不懂了，老古怎么会知道阿七此刻住在娘家？”尤五又问，“他当阿七还是大小姐？”“不”他晓得七姐居孀。是老二告诉他的，不对！是他跟老二打听的。“接着，胡雪岩便把古应春家里的情形说了一遍。

“Young Uncle,” You said with a frown, “my sister's behaviour grows more improper every day. What are we to do?” “Don't say that!” Hu replied cheerfully. “Brother Five, I shall be asking for wedding wine soon. Do you know what Gu said to me? He wants me to arrange a marriage.” You was astonished. After a long silence, he asked, “He wants to marry our A’qi?” “Exactly. Love at first sight! Marriage really is a matter of destiny.” “What destiny?” You frowned still more deeply. “Think how it would sound: they met in a courtesan house!” This response surprised Hu. He could not immediately think how to defend Gu and the Seventh Lady, and stood speechless for some time. “There's something I don't understand,” You continued. “How did Gu know that A’qi is living with her own family now? Does he think she's still an unmarried young lady?” “No. He knows she's a widow. The Second Miss told him—no, he asked her.” Hu then described Gu's family circumstances.

“那么，小爷叔，你怎么回答他的呢？”“我说，要他自己看。我看……他们有缘，这杯喜酒吃得成功的。”尤五不以为然，大摇其头：“算了，我看不要害人！”“你倒也不必把我们这位姑奶奶贬得太厉害！”胡雪岩以不平的语气说：“象她这样的人才，嫁给老古，照我看还是委屈的。至于说她脾气不好，这话要说回来，女人家心思最怪不过，只要她自己愿意，自然会改。看今天的样子，斯斯文文，大大方方，可见已经在改了！”话虽说得动听，却无结论，事实上婚姻大事，一时也不可能有什么结论，只有摆着再说，先料理第二天动身的事。

“Well, Young Uncle, what did you tell him?” “I told him to see for himself. I think they are destined for each other. We should be able to drink that wedding wine.” You shook his head emphatically. “Let it be. We shouldn't ruin a man's life.” “There's no need to put our lady down so harshly!” Hu spoke with indignation. “With her looks and abilities, I think even marrying Gu would scarcely do her justice. As for her temper, that cuts both ways. A woman's mind is a strange thing: if she herself wishes to, she will naturally change. Look at her today—refined and perfectly at ease. Clearly, she's changing already.” Persuasive though the words were, they led to no conclusion. A marriage could hardly be settled immediately in any case. They must leave it for later and first arrange the following day's departure.

下船是在中午，胡雪岩“师弟”，老张父女，加上七姑奶奶一共五个人，除去老张，各自只可促膝密谈，未便公然表露的心事，加以路上不太平，风吹草动，需要随时当心，所以就连七姑奶奶这样爱说话的人，也是保持沉默的时候居多。第二天快到松江了，胡雪岩该当作个决定，要不要七姑奶奶送到嘉兴？如果认为不需要，把她留在松江，扬帆而走，至多停泊半日，将他自己和阿珠寄在尤家的行李搬上船，否则，至少得在松江停一天，让七姑奶奶先打听消息，或者带个把可供奔走的人同行。“小爷叔！”等胡雪岩刚一提及，七姑奶奶便抢着说，“不管我送不送你，无论如何在我们那里住一天再走。”“杭州等得很急……”“急也不急在一天，我五嫂有话跟你说。”这倒奇了，尤五嫂会有什么话？就有话要说，七姑奶奶怎么会知道？凡是遇到艰难，胡雪岩总要先通前彻后想一遍，等自己想不通时再发问。

They went aboard at noon: Hu and his pupil, Old Zhang and his daughter, and the Seventh Lady—five in all. Apart from Old Zhang, each had private concerns that could be discussed only in close confidence, never openly. Moreover, the route was unsettled, and every disturbance required vigilance. Even the talkative Seventh Lady remained silent much of the time. As they approached Songjiang the following day, Hu had to decide whether she should escort them as far as Jiaxing. If unnecessary, they could leave her in Songjiang and sail on after stopping no more than half a day to collect the luggage he and A’zhu had left with the You family. Otherwise, they must stay at least a day while she gathered news and perhaps brought along someone to run errands. “Young Uncle!” As soon as he raised the matter, she interrupted. “Whether I escort you or not, you must stay a day at our house before leaving.” “They're waiting urgently in Hangzhou...” “One day won't make all the difference. Fifth Sister-in-Law has something to say to you.” That was strange. What could Mrs You want to say? And if she did have something to say, how could the Seventh Lady know? Whenever faced with a difficulty, Hu tried to reason it through from beginning to end before asking questions.

他的脑筋特别快，察言辨包，觉得只有一个可能，“七姐，”他问，“是不是你自己有话不便说，要请五嫂来问我？”七姑奶奶笑了，带些顽皮，也有些忸怩，“小爷叔，”她说：“你顶聪明。”“既然如此，你何不直接告诉我？”“还是等五嫂自己来问你的好。”这话倒象是关于尤五夫妇的事，胡雪岩有些困惑，细想一想，莫非是有关怡情老二的话？也许七姑奶奶多事，要到她嫂子那里去“告密”，所以尤五嫂会有些话要问。或者七姑奶奶倒是好心，与怡情老二投缘，在她嫂子面前下说词，劝她为夫纳妾，这样尤五嫂就更会有些话要问。同样是问，有的话可说，有的话不可说。到底是怎样的一问？先得把方向弄清楚，临事才不致窘迫。于是他问：“七姐，你晓不晓得五嫂要问我的话，是好事还是啥？”“自然是好事。”

His mind worked exceptionally quickly. Watching her expression, he could see only one possibility. “Seventh Sister, is there something you find awkward to say yourself and want Fifth Sister-in-Law to ask me?” She smiled, partly mischievous and partly shy. “Young Uncle, you are very clever.” “Then why not tell me directly?” “It would still be better to let her ask you herself.” That sounded as though it concerned You and his wife. Puzzled, Hu considered it further. Could it be about the Second Miss? Perhaps the Seventh Lady meant to meddle and report her brother's activities, giving Mrs You questions to ask. Or perhaps she intended kindness. Having taken to the Second Miss, she might persuade her sister-in-law to let her husband take the courtesan as a concubine. In that case, Mrs You would have even more to ask. Questions might be alike in form, yet some could be answered freely and others could not. What sort of inquiry awaited him? He must establish its direction beforehand, or risk being caught at a disadvantage. “Seventh Sister, do you know whether what she wants to ask concerns something good?” “Naturally.”

这下胡雪岩放心了。船抵松江，上岸直到尤家，歇一歇脚。他趁空去拜访了“老太爷”，在他那里吃了饭。再到尤家，谈不到三五句话，尤五嫂起身说道：“小爷叔，我有件事拜托你。”是拜托胡雪岩做媒，却不是为尤五娶怡情老二进门，是替七姑奶奶促成良缘。尤五嫂告诉他说，当他在裕记丝浅跟尤五密谈古应春时，七姑奶奶在外屋趁老张父女和陈世龙吃蟹吃得起劲时，悄悄在“听壁脚”，古应春的意思她已经知道了，表示非古应春不嫁。因为听出尤五似乎不赞成这头亲事，所以特为来跟嫂子谈。听完经过，胡雪岩失笑了。笑自己误解了七姑奶奶的语气，上了自己的当，如果是跟人做一笔出入甚巨的生意，也是这样子胡思乱猜，自以为是，那就非大蚀其本不可。

Hu was reassured. At Songjiang they went ashore and directly to the You house for a rest. Taking the opportunity, Hu paid a visit to the old master and dined with him. Back at the house, he had scarcely exchanged a few words when Mrs You rose. “Young Uncle, I have a favour to ask.” She wanted him to act as matchmaker. It was not, however, to bring the Second Miss into You's household, but to secure a happy marriage for the Seventh Lady. Mrs You explained that, while Hu and You had been discussing Gu privately at the Yuji Silk Warehouse, the Seventh Lady had taken advantage of the others' absorption in their crabs to listen at the wall. She knew Gu's intentions and had declared that she would marry nobody else. Having heard that her brother seemed opposed, she had come especially to discuss it with his wife. Hu laughed at himself. He had misread the Seventh Lady's tone and fallen victim to his own cleverness. If he approached a major business transaction with such wild guesses and self-assurance, he would surely lose heavily.

“小爷叔，”尤五嫂问道，“阿七怎么会认识那姓古的，好象是第一次见面，在哪里？”这一问就不易回答了，尤其是对她。诚然如尤五所说的，在堂子里见的面，这话提起来难听。再问下去：她怎么跑到了那种地方去？那又要牵涉到怡情老二，尤五这样的人，在花街柳巷走走，尤五嫂自然不会干涉，但如说是怡情老二的恩客，在外面置了“小房子”，就难保尤五嫂会不吃醋。于是他说：“在裕记丝栈。老古现在跟五哥，跟我，三个人合伙。这头亲事说起来倒也是顺理成章的事，郎有意姐有情，哪还有啥话说？至于做媒的话，不但义不容辞，而且是所谓非我莫属。不过，五嫂，我们有这样一个想法，说出来你看，对不对！”“你的话没有错的，小爷叔，你说。”

“Young Uncle,” Mrs You asked, “how did A’qi come to know this Mr Gu? It seems they had only just met. Where was it?” That was not easy to answer, especially to her. As You had said, meeting in a courtesan house sounded disgraceful. Her next question would be why the Seventh Lady had gone there, which would bring in the Second Miss. Mrs You would naturally not interfere with a man like her husband occasionally visiting the pleasure quarters. But knowing that he was the Second Miss's favoured patron and maintained a separate little house might well make her jealous. Hu therefore said, “At the Yuji Silk Warehouse. Gu, Brother Five, and I are now partners. The marriage would be quite natural. He is willing and so is she. What more is there to say? As for arranging it, I am not only bound to help; you might say nobody else is so well placed. But, Fifth Sister-in-Law, we have an idea. Let me put it to you and see what you think.” “Your judgement is sound, Young Uncle. Tell me.”

“我们杭州说媒人‘吃十三只半鸡’，意思是说要媒人一遍遍传话，事情极慢。别的亲事嫌慢，这头亲事嫌快，我看还是慢一点的好。”“我懂小爷叔的意思，是怕太快了，彼此都看不清楚，将来会懊悔？”“对了，就是这个意思。”“意思是好的。不过，你晓得的，我们家这位姑奶奶是急性子。”“这就要你劝她了。”胡雪岩放低了声音说：“还有一层，听七姐的意思，好象有点跟五哥怄气，你不大赞成，我偏要嫁他。婚姻大事，怄气就不对了。”尤五嫂想了想。深深点头，“小爷叔，你的话不错的。我倒没有想到。”

But you know how impatient our lady is.” “Then you must persuade her.” Hu lowered his voice. “There's something else. From what Seventh Sister says, she seems a little determined to spite Brother Five: if he doesn't quite approve, she'll marry Gu all the more. Spite is no basis for such an important decision.” Mrs You considered, then nodded deeply. “You're right, Young Uncle. That hadn't occurred to me.”

That should take up half a month.” “I understand. I'll find a way to delay things. But let me ask one more question. What sort of man is this Mr Gu, really?” “You had better see for yourself.” That was hardly a matchmaker's answer. In fact, Hu was beginning to regret his involvement. The Seventh Lady was too impatient and was acting partly out of defiance, while You had objections. Hu vaguely foresaw complications. His enthusiasm suddenly cooled. For that reason, he wanted to avoid the Seventh Lady and firmly declined her kind offer to escort him to Jiaxing. The next day he boarded the boat and continued down the canal. Fortunately, the journey went smoothly. They entered Zhejiang without incident, and from there to Hangzhou there would be no further danger.

阅读：重点区分事实、人物推断、社交场合的表现与刻意隐瞒。七姑奶奶在古应春面前自我约束，古据此认为她并不暴躁；胡了解她的平常性情，因而要求观察。结尾胡对尤太太询问内容的猜测全部落空，适合练习辨认推测性词语及反转信息。
完形：关注provided、otherwise、yet、though、partly...partly、before、once等连接条件、让步与时间的方式。胡先愿促成、继而告诫慎重、最后热情冷却，这一变化由人物了解程度、家人态度和赌气动机推动，不能只凭单句褒贬选择态度词。
段落排序：先画场所顺序：怡情院初见—小房子吃饭谈事—门外私议婚事—回怡情院吃蟹—裕记丝栈内外两处谈话—返程船上—松江尤家。尤其注意“里面／外面”、同一时间胡与尤五私谈、七姑奶奶偷听的关系，结尾的解释会回指此前场景。
翻译：可选11655—11661，拆解“观察虽好，但动心后急问—媒人不能一味褒贬—性格相合与不合会产生相反后果”的层次。另练11811—11815，把尤太太可能追问的链条与胡最终给出的回答分开；保留是谁知道、谁猜测、谁故意隐瞒，不将间接心理描写误归说话人。
写作：可练“重大决定需要充分了解”“善意不能替代审慎判断”“应当核实猜测后再采取行动”“自我约束能够改善沟通”。语言可积累see for oneself、take responsibility、come straight to the point、in due course、be bound to、read someone’s expression等表达。小说中支配伴侣、评价寡妇及隐瞒婚姻信息的观念属于人物和时代语境，不应不加辨别地套入现代论证。
