* * *一到杭州，胡雪岩回家坐得一坐，立刻便到阜康，陈世龙已押了行李先在那里等候。行李虽多，尽是些送人的礼物，由刘庆生帮着料理，一份份分配停当，派了一个“出店”陪着陈世龙一家家去分送。胡雪岩则趁此刻工夫，听取刘庆生的报告。“胡先生，请你先看帐。”刘庆生捧着一叠帐簿，很郑重地说。“不忙，不忙！你先跟我说说大概情形。”“请你看了帐再说。”听他如此坚持，料知帐傅中就可以看出生意好坏，于是他点点头先看存款。一看不由得诧异了，存户中颇多“张得标”、“李德胜”、“王占魁”、“赵虎臣”之类的名字，存银自几百到上万不等，而名下什九注着这么四个小字，“长期无息。”“唷，唷！”胡雪岩大为惊异，“阜康真的要发财了！怎么会有这么多的户头？”“胡先生！”刘庆生矜持着说：“你再看这一笔帐。”他翻到的一笔帐是支出，上面写着：“八月二十五日付罗尚德名下本银一万一千两。息免。”“喔，原来罗尚德的那笔款子，提回去了？”“不是！”刘庆生说，“罗尚德阵亡了，银子等于是我送还的。我不知道这件事做得对不对……”

On reaching Hangzhou, Hu Xueyan stopped briefly at home, then went straight to Fukang. Chen Shilong had already brought the luggage there and was waiting. There was a great deal of it, but everything was a present for someone. With Liu Qingsheng's help, the gifts were sorted, and one of the bank's errand clerks was sent with Chen to deliver them from house to house. Hu used the interval to hear Liu's report. “Mr Hu, please examine the accounts first,” Liu said gravely, bringing a pile of ledgers. “No hurry, no hurry. Give me a general account first.” “Please look at the books before we talk.” His insistence suggested that the books themselves would show how business had gone. Hu nodded and began with the deposits. What he saw astonished him. There were many names such as Zhang Debiao, Li Desheng, Wang Zhankui, and Zhao Huchen, with sums ranging from a few hundred to more than ten thousand taels. Nine out of ten entries bore the brief annotation: “Long-term; no interest.” “Well, well!” Hu exclaimed. “Fukang really is going to prosper! How did we acquire so many accounts?” With restrained pride, Liu said, “Please look at this entry too.” He turned to a payment: “Twenty-fifth day of the eighth month: paid out eleven thousand taels, principal standing in Luo Shangde's name. Interest waived.” “Oh! So Luo has withdrawn his deposit?” “No. Luo Shangde was killed in action. In effect, I returned the money. I don't know whether I did the right thing...”

刘庆生细谈这件事的经过，是八月二十五那天，有两个军官到阜康来问，说是听闻罗尚德曾有一笔款子存在阜康，可有其事？又说罗尚德已经阵亡，但他在四川还有亲属，如果有这笔款子，要提出来寄回去。罗尚德的存折在刘庆生手里，倘或否认其事，别无人证。但他不肯这样做，一口承认，同时立即取出存折，验明银数，但他表示，不能凭他们两个人的片面之词就付这笔存款。“那么该怎么办呢？”“我知道罗老爷跟抚台衙门的刘二爷是朋友，要刘二爷跟你们营官一起出面，出条子给阜廉。”刘庆生说：“只要罗老爷是真的阵亡，你们各位肯担责任，阜康立刻照付。“于是那两个军官，当天便我了刘二爷来，公同具了领条，刘庆生立即捧出一万一千两银子，还要算利息，人家自然不肯再要。这样到了第二天，张得标、李德胜等等，便都上门来了。

Liu explained what had happened. On the twenty-fifth of the eighth month, two officers had come to ask whether it was true that Luo had deposited money at Fukang. They said he had been killed in action but still had relatives in Sichuan. If the deposit existed, they wished to withdraw it and send it home. Luo's passbook was in Liu's keeping. Had he denied the account's existence, there would have been no other witness. But he refused to act that way. He acknowledged it at once, produced the passbook, and verified the amount. He made clear, however, that he could not pay out the deposit on the two men's unsupported statement. “What should we do, then?” “I know that Mr Luo was a friend of Mr Liu Er at the governor's office. Mr Liu Er and your commanding officer must jointly give Fukang written authorisation,” Liu replied. “Provided Luo really has been killed in action and you gentlemen accept responsibility, Fukang will pay immediately.” The two officers brought Liu Er that same day, and a joint receipt was signed. Liu immediately produced eleven thousand taels and offered to calculate the interest as well, but they naturally refused anything more. The following day, Zhang Debiao, Li Desheng, and the others began coming to the bank.

胡雪岩听他讲完，异常满意，“庆生，”他说，“阜康的牌子打响了！你做得高明之极。““老实说，”刘庆生自己也觉得很安慰，“我是从胡先生你这里学来的窍门。做生意诚实不欺，只要自己一颗心把得定就可以了，诚实不欺要叫主顾晓得，到处去讲，那得要花点心思，我总算灵机一动，把机会抓住了。”“对！做生意把握机会，是第一等的学问。你能够做到这一点，我非常高兴。庆生，我现在帮手不够，你还是替我享管点事，以后钱庄的生意都归你。”胡雪岩说：“我一切不管，都归你调度。”“这……”刘庆生兴奋之余，反有恐惧不胜之感，“这副扭子我怕挑不下。”“不要紧！你只要多用心思，凡事想停当了去做，就冒点风险也不要紧。不冒风险的生意，人人会做，如问能够比头？只要值得，你尽管放手去做。“

Hu was exceedingly pleased by the story. “Qingsheng, Fukang's reputation is made! You handled it brilliantly.” “To be honest,” Liu said, feeling much comforted himself, “I learned the method from you. To deal honestly, one need only hold firm to one's principles. But to make customers know of that honesty and speak of it everywhere requires some thought. I had a sudden idea and managed to seize the opportunity.” “Exactly. Seizing opportunities is the highest art in business. I'm delighted that you can do it. Qingsheng, I haven't enough assistants. You must take more off my hands. From now on, the bank's business is all yours to manage. I shall leave everything to your direction.” Alongside his excitement, Liu felt overwhelming apprehension. “I'm afraid I may not be able to carry such a burden.” “Don't worry. Put more thought into things and work them out before acting. A little risk doesn't matter. Everyone can do business that involves no risk. How could that put you ahead? As long as it's worthwhile, go ahead boldly.”

“这话就很难说了，怎么叫值得，怎么叫不值得？各人看法不同。”“人生在世，不为利，就为名。做生意也是一样，冒险值得不值得，就看你两佯当中能不能占一样？”胡雪岩停了一下指着帐簿说，“譬如这笔放款，我知道此人是个米商，借了钱去做生意，你就要弄弄清楚，他的米是运到什么地方？运到不曾失守的地方，不要紧，运到长毛那里，这笔放款就不能做！为啥呢，万一这笔帐放倒了，外面说起来是：哪个要你去帮长毛？倒帐活该！这一来名利两失，自然犯不着冒险。”“我懂了！”刘庆生深深点头，“凡事总要有个退步。即使出了事，也能够在台面上说得过去。”“对啊！庆生，”胡雪岩拍着他的肩说，“你完全懂了！我们的生意，不管是啥，都是这个宗旨，万一失手，有话好说。这样子，别人能够原谅你，就还有从头来起的机会，虽败不倒！”“虽败不倒！”刘庆生把这句话在心里念了好几遍，颇有领悟。接着便谈了些业务扩充的计划，胡雪岩因为自己在杭州只有几天耽搁，一拖便无结果，所以或可或否，当时便要作出决定。

“That's difficult to judge. What counts as worthwhile and what doesn't? People see things differently.” “In life, people seek either profit or a good name. Business is the same. Whether a risk is worth taking depends on whether you can gain one of the two.” Hu paused and pointed to the ledger. “Take this loan. I know the borrower is a rice merchant, using the money for trade. You must establish where he's sending his rice. If it's going somewhere that hasn't fallen, that's all right. If it's going to the Longhairs, you mustn't lend. Why? If the loan goes bad, people will say, ‘Who asked you to help the Longhairs? You deserved to lose your money.’ You lose both profit and reputation. Naturally, that risk isn't worth taking.” Liu nodded firmly. “I understand. There must always be a way to fall back. Even if something goes wrong, we must be able to justify ourselves openly.” “Exactly, Qingsheng!” Hu patted his shoulder. “You understand completely. Whatever business we do, that is our principle: if we fail, we must have an explanation. If others can forgive us, we still have a chance to begin again. Defeated, but not destroyed!” Liu repeated the phrase inwardly several times and grasped much from it. They then discussed plans for expanding the business. Since Hu could remain in Hangzhou only a few days, delay might leave matters unsettled. He therefore gave an immediate decision, yes or no, on each proposal.

正在从长计议时，只听有人一路喊了进来：“二弟，二弟！”听这称呼便知是嵇鹤龄，胡雪岩急忙迎了出去，只见他红光满面，梳一条又黑又亮的辫子，身上穿一件极挺括的紫酱色线春夹袍，外面套一件黑缎“巴图鲁”坎肩，平肩一排珊瑚套扣，卷着袖子，露出雪白纺绸的袖头，左手盘一对核桃，右手拿昔支湘妃竹镶翠的短烟袋，十足一副纨袴公子的打扮，以前的那副不修边幅的名士派头，连影子都找不到了。“大哥！”胡雪岩笑道：“你年轻了十几岁，差点都认不得了。”“都是瑞云啊！”嵇鹤龄有着掩抑不往的喜色，“打扮了几个孩子，还要打扮我。不作无益之事，何以遣有生之涯？这且不去说它。我是奉命来邀客，瑞云叫我来说，晚上为你接风，没有什么菜吃，但一定要到。”“一定到。只是时候不会太早。”“你是要先去记雪公？”嵇鹤龄说，“那就不必了。我已约了雪公，他到舍间来会你，吃完饭，你们一起走好了。”“那好，省了我多少事。”胡雪岩笑着问道，“瑞姑娘怎么样？”“那是尽在不言中了。总而言之一句话，承情不尽。”

In the midst of their deliberations, someone came in calling, “Second Brother! Second Brother!” The form of address could belong only to Ji Heling. Hu hurried out to greet him. His face glowed, and his queue was black and glossy. He wore a crisp, lined robe of purplish-brown xianchun cloth beneath a black satin baturu waistcoat, with a row of coral fastening buttons level with the shoulders. His rolled sleeves revealed snowy-white silk cuffs. In his left hand he turned a pair of walnuts; in his right he held a short pipe of spotted bamboo mounted with jade. He looked every inch the fashionable young gentleman. Not a trace remained of his former careless scholar's appearance. “Elder Brother!” Hu laughed. “You look more than ten years younger. I hardly recognised you.” “It's all Ruiyun!” Ji could not suppress his delight. “After dressing up the children, she insists on dressing me up too. If we never did anything useless, how would we pass our allotted years? But never mind that. I've been ordered to invite you. Ruiyun says we're to give you a welcome-home dinner tonight. There won't be much to eat, but you must come.” “Certainly. Only I may not be very early.” “You mean to call on Mr Xuexuan first? There's no need. I've invited him too. He'll meet you at my house, and you can leave together after dinner.” “Excellent. That saves me a great deal of trouble.” Hu smiled. “How is Miss Rui?” “That goes without saying. In a word, I'm endlessly grateful.”

“新城的案子，雪公已经写信告诉我了，说得语焉不详，我在上海记挂得很。”胡雪岩问道，“对你总有个安排？”“是的，我正要跟你详细谈。”嵇鹤龄略一踌躇，接着又说，“话太长，一说开头，就无法收场了。这样吧，我还要去办点事，瑞云要我去买儿盆菊花，我把轿子留在这里，回头你坐了来。最好早些到，雪公未来之前，我们先可以好好谈一谈。”看他春风满面，服饰华丽，此时又知道养了“轿班”，可知情况很不坏，胡雪岩先忧放心了，点点头答应，尽快赴约。在阜康把几件紧要的事处置完毕，胡雪岩坐了轿子径到嵇家。嵇鹤龄也刚回来不久，正穿着短衣在指挥花匠陈设菊花，一见他来，便说一声：“你到里面坐，我洗了手就来。”这时张贵已来肃客，看见胡雪岩异常恭敬，也格外亲热，一面伛偻着身子引路，一面殷殷问讯，直接领到后厅，迎面遇着瑞云。

“Mr Xuexuan wrote to tell me about the Xincheng affair, but without much detail. I've been concerned about it in Shanghai. Has an arrangement been made for you?” Hu asked. “Yes. I was just going to tell you all about it.” Ji hesitated. “But it's a long story. Once I begin, there'll be no end. Let's do this: I still have an errand to run. Ruiyun wants me to buy a few pots of chrysanthemums. I'll leave my chair here for you to come in later. Try to arrive early, so that we can talk properly before Mr Xuexuan comes.” Seeing his cheerful expression and fine clothes, and now discovering that he maintained his own chair-bearers, Hu could tell that matters were going reasonably well. Reassured, he nodded and promised to come as soon as possible. After dealing with several urgent matters at Fukang, he took the chair straight to Ji's house. Ji had only recently returned and was wearing short working clothes while directing a gardener in arranging the chrysanthemums. “Go inside and sit down,” he said on seeing Hu. “I'll wash my hands and join you.” Zhang Gui came to receive the guest, exceptionally respectful and warmly attentive. Bowing as he led the way and inquiring after Hu's well-being, he took him directly to the rear hall, where they met Ruiyun.

“二老爷！”因为胡雪岩与嵇鹤龄拜了把子，所以她这样含笑称呼，略一凝视，接着又说，“清瘦了些，想来路上辛苦了！不过精神气色都还是老样子。”“你象是发福了。”胡雪岩笑着问，“日子过得还称心吧！”“托二老爷的福。”瑞云向里喊道：“荷官，领了弟弟、妹妹来见二叔！”“噢！”里面娇滴滴地答应一声，只见丹荷领头，带着一群小家伙，摇摇摆摆走了来，一个个都穿得很干净，等丹荷一站定，便也都站住了。“叫啊！二叔。”瑞云看着丹荷说。于是丹荷先叫，她叫过了再叫弟、妹们叫。胡雪岩一看这情形，对瑞云佩服得不得了。她是用的“擒贼擒王”的手段，不知怎么一来，把最调皮的丹荷笼络得服服帖帖！那一群小家伙便也都安分了。“老大呢？”他问。“我送他‘附馆’去了。”嵇鹤龄进门接口，两个小的立刻便都扑了过去。胡雪岩心里着实羡慕嵇鹤龄，自然也深感安慰，拉着丹荷的手问长问短，好半天不放。“好了好了！”瑞云大声说道，“都跟着二姐到里头去，不要来烦你们二叔！”

“Second Master!” she said with a smile, using the title because Hu and Ji were sworn brothers. After studying him briefly, she added, “You've grown a little thinner. The journey must have been tiring. But your spirits and complexion are just the same.” “You seem to have filled out.” Hu smiled. “Are you happy with life here?” “Thanks to you, Second Master.” She called inside, “Heguan, bring your younger brothers and sisters to greet Second Uncle!” “Yes!” came a sweet voice. Danhe led the little troop out. They toddled forward in clean clothes and stopped as soon as she did. “Greet him—Second Uncle,” Ruiyun told her. Danhe spoke first, then instructed the others to follow. Seeing this, Hu admired Ruiyun enormously. She had followed the principle of catching the bandits' chief first. Somehow, she had won over the most mischievous child, Danhe, so completely that the rest had settled down too. “Where's the eldest?” he asked. “I've sent him to join another household's school,” Ji answered as he entered. The two youngest immediately rushed to him. Hu truly envied Ji, and felt deeply comforted as well. He took Danhe's hand, asked her all manner of questions, and did not let go for a long time. “Enough, enough!” Ruiyun said loudly. “Follow your second sister inside. Don't bother Second Uncle.”

遣走了孩子们，瑞云也告个便回到厨下。于是嵇鹤龄跟胡雪岩谈起别后的光景。新城之行，先抚后剿的宗旨定得不错，当地士绅对嵇鹤龄革枪匹马，深入危城，都佩服他的胆气，也了解他的诚意，因此都愿意跟他合作，设法把为首的“ 强盗和尚”意心，引诱到县自首。蛇无头而不行，乌合之众，一下子散得光光。前后不过费了半个月的工夫。功成回来，王有龄自然敬礼有加，万分亲热，私人先送了五百两银子，作为谢礼。嵇鹤龄不肯收，王有龄则非送不可，“到后来简直要吵架了。”他说，“我想你跟他的交情不同，我跟你又是弟兄，就看在这一层间接的渊源上，收了下来。”“你真是取与舍之间，一丝不苟。”胡雪岩点点头说，“用他几个也不要紧。这且不去说他，你补缺的事呢？雪公说过，补实缺的事，包在他身上。现在怎么样了？“

After sending the children away, Ruiyun excused herself and returned to the kitchen. Ji then described what had happened since they parted. The policy for Xincheng—conciliation first, force afterwards if needed—had been correct. The local gentry admired his courage in going alone into the endangered county town and recognised his sincerity. They willingly cooperated, finding a way to persuade the leader, the “bandit monk” Huixin, to come to the county office and surrender. A snake cannot move without its head: the disorganised followers scattered completely. The whole affair had taken no more than half a month. When Ji returned successful, Wang naturally treated him with special respect and warmth, offering him five hundred taels from his own purse in gratitude. Ji refused, and Wang insisted. “In the end, we almost quarrelled,” Ji said. “Then I thought of your close friendship with him and our own brotherhood. On the strength of that indirect connection, I accepted.” Hu nodded. “You are scrupulous indeed about what to accept and what to decline. There's no harm in using a little of his money. But leave that aside. What about a substantive appointment? Mr Xuexuan said he would take responsibility for securing you a permanent post. Where does that stand now?”

“这件事说起来，有点气人，”嵇鹤龄急忙又加了一句：“不过，雪公对我是没有什么好说的，他保我署理归安县，黄抚台不肯，又保我接海运局，他也不肯，说等‘保案’下来再说。”地方上一件大案子，或则兵剿，或则河工，或则如漕运由何运改为海运等等大事曲张的案子，办妥出奏，照例可以为出力人员请奖，称为“保案”，保有“明保”、“密保”之分，自然是密保值钱。“黄抚台给了我一个明保，反是雪公倒是密保……”“这太不公平了。”胡雪岩打断他的话说：“莫非其中有鬼？”“嗨！”嵇鹤龄一拍大腿，“真正机灵不过你！黄抚台手下一个文案委员，要我两千银子，我也不知道这两千银子是他自己，还是他替黄抚台要？反正别说我拿不出，就拿得出来，也不能塞这个狗洞。““那么，雪公怎么说呢？”“雪公根本不知道。我没有告诉他。”嵇鹤龄说，“我跟他说了，他一定为我出这两千银子。我何必再欠他一个人情？”

“It's rather exasperating.” Ji hastened to add, “Though I have nothing to complain of in Mr Xuexuan. He recommended me as acting magistrate of Gui'an, but Governor Huang refused. Then he proposed that I take over the Sea Transport Bureau, and Huang refused that too, saying we must wait for the decision on the recommendations for reward.” When a major local undertaking—suppressing disorder, repairing waterways, or changing grain transport from the canal to the sea—had been completed and reported to the throne, it was customary to recommend rewards for those who had contributed. Such recommendations might be public or confidential. Naturally, the confidential kind was more valuable. “Governor Huang has put me forward publicly, whereas Mr Xuexuan has received a confidential recommendation...” “That's most unfair,” Hu interrupted. “Could there be something underhand behind it?” “Ah!” Ji slapped his thigh. “Nobody is sharper than you. One of the governor's clerical commissioners asked me for two thousand taels. I don't know whether he wants it for himself or on Huang's behalf. In any case, even if I had the money—which I don't—I wouldn't pour it down that dog's hole.” “What does Mr Xuexuan say?” “He knows nothing of it. I haven't told him. If I did, he'd certainly pay the two thousand for me. Why should I put myself under yet another obligation to him?”

There were few men of such unbending integrity left in official life. Hu felt a deep respect for him. Yet although Ji might take that view, Hu himself ought to explain matters to Wang. They must secure the Sea Transport Bureau post somehow, even if the money had to be poured down the dog's hole after all. “Elder Brother, leave this alone,” he said. “Mr Xuexuan will have an answer. Let me speak to him.” “There's really no need to force matters.” Ji shook his head. “I'm tired of the changing warmth and coldness of official society. I'm quite comfortable as I am. We can think again when the five hundred taels are spent. Surely nobody need starve in this world.” “A true scholar's spirit!” Hu laughed. “Without boasting, I could afford to keep you in your present style. But you certainly wouldn't agree, and I wouldn't want you idle either. A comfortable life is not enough to satisfy a man.” Ji nodded deeply. “Exactly. I have plans of my own, of course. If I can't make my way in Zhejiang, I mean to return to Hubei and organise local militia.” “There's no need. We have ample scope here in Zhejiang. When Mr Xuexuan comes, we'll discuss it properly.”

As the saying went, speak of Cao Cao and Cao Cao arrives. Wang was now a familiar visitor. Zhang Gui had barely announced him before he came through the door, calling as he walked, “Xueyan! Xueyan!” “Mr Xuexuan!” Hu went to meet him and greeted him with clasped hands. “I've been waiting for you every day. Now you're here, I can finally fix a day to return to Huzhou.” “Master!” Ruiyun called, still using the form of address she had used in Wang's household. “Please come inside. Let's have dinner. Mr Hu must be hungry too.” “Yes, yes! Let's eat and drink,” Wang replied happily. “Today we must have a few good cups.” They went into the rear hall, where the table was already covered with dishes. There was Ruiyun's speciality, chicken in red fermented-rice lees, and an ingenious rich hotpot combining fish offal and bean curd in the style of Ji's home region with Hangzhou's fish-head-and-bean-curd dish. It was just the thing for an evening of west wind and falling leaves.

“胡老爷送的洋酒。”瑞云拿着一瓶白兰地笑道，“我竟不知道怎么开法？”“我来，我来！”嵇鹤龄接过酒来，很自然把双手抚在她肩上说，“喝这酒省事，不必烫。你请到厨房里去吧！菜慢一点好了。回头你也来敬酒。“他这样款款而言，一点都不觉得有什么不合适，瑞云却很不好意思，微微窘笑着白了他一眼，然后低声埋怨：“你真罗嗦！”王有龄向胡雪岩看了一眼，等瑞云的背影一失，忍不往哈哈大笑，“雪岩！”他说，“我现在才知道你的乐趣，看天下有情人都成眷属，实在是件赏心乐事。”“愿天下有情人终成眷属”，是西湖月老祠的对联，嵇鹤龄随即笑道：“这一字改得好！雪公有此襟怀，自然常乐。”“好说，好说！”都亏你们两位帮了我的大忙。今天先借花献佛，聊表寸心。“于是三个人先干了一杯。白兰地不比绍兴酒，嵇鹤龄喝得大猛了些，呛了嗓子，咳得面红脖子粗，连瑞云在厨房里都听到了，赶了出来一看，便一面问原因。一面替他捶背。王、胡两人看在眼里又相视而笑了。

“This is the foreign liquor Mr Hu sent.” Holding a bottle of brandy, Ruiyun smiled. “I don't even know how to open it.” “Give it to me.” Ji took the bottle, then quite naturally laid both hands on her shoulders. “This saves trouble. It doesn't need warming. Go back to the kitchen. There's no hurry with the dishes. Come and drink a toast later.” He spoke with gentle affection, finding nothing improper in it, but Ruiyun was embarrassed. With a shy smile and a reproachful glance, she murmured, “You do talk too much!” Wang looked at Hu. As soon as she disappeared, he burst out laughing. “Xueyan, now I understand your pleasure. To see all lovers united in marriage is a delightful thing indeed.” The couplet at the Temple of the Old Man under the Moon by West Lake expressed the wish that all lovers should be united. Ji laughed. “An excellent change of one word—from wishing to seeing! With that spirit, Mr Xuexuan, you will always find joy.” “You are too kind. Both of you have helped me greatly. Today I'll borrow another's flowers to offer to the Buddha and express a little of my gratitude.” The three emptied their cups. Brandy was unlike Shaoxing wine. Ji drank too quickly, choked, and coughed until his face and neck were red. Hearing him from the kitchen, Ruiyun hurried out, asking what had happened while patting his back. Wang and Hu exchanged another smile.

“你那位珠小姐呢？”王有龄问胡雪岩，“现在是要看你的了！”“那也是件赏心乐事……”“怎么？”王有龄很关切地抢着问，“莫非好事不谐？”“在阿珠仍旧是件好事，这也不去谈她了。倒是畹香，”胡雪岩说，“我在上海叫人去看过她，还住在梅家弄，不曾受到什么惊吓。她有意思来玩一趟，雪公，你看如何？”“看看再说吧！”王有龄的神色很冷淡，是不大愿意谈及此事的神情。嵇鹤龄本来想问畹香是何许人？看见他这样的神色，见机不言。胡雪岩当然更不会再提，话题一扯，谈到他自己在上海的交游及生意。

“And your Miss Zhu?” Wang asked Hu. “Now it's your turn.” “That too is something to be pleased about...” “What?” Wang interrupted with concern. “Has the match failed?” “It's still a happy outcome for A’zhu. But leave that aside. As for Wanxiang, I sent someone to see her in Shanghai. She's still in Meijia Lane and hasn't suffered any serious fright. She'd like to come for a visit. What do you think?” “We'll see later.” Wang's expression was cool; he plainly did not wish to discuss it. Ji had meant to ask who Wanxiang was, but took the hint and remained silent. Naturally, Hu did not pursue the matter either. He turned the conversation towards his own acquaintances and business in Shanghai.

此刻有两件事要谈，一件是代买的洋枪，一件是海运由浏河出口，交尤五驳运，后者又跟嵇鹤龄的出处有关，胡雪岩灵机一动，认为可以当作嵇鹤哈的见解提出来，显得他在这方面也有过人的才干，因而决定先谈洋枪。“雪公！”他问，“湖州的团练怎么样了？”一问到此，王有龄大力兴奋，“很好哇！全省各地的团练，就数我湖州顺利。平心而论，都是赵景贤的功劳。”他对嵇鹤龄说，“此人的才具，不逊干老兄。几时我介绍你跟他交个朋友。”“我亦听说此君既贤且能，很想交这个朋友。若蒙雪公引见，真是快事！”说着，他陶然引杯，一仰脖子干了酒。

There were two matters to discuss: the foreign guns he had bought on Wang's behalf, and the plan for the sea-going grain shipments to leave by Liuhe, with You Laowu handling the lighterage. The latter was connected with Ji's future employment. An idea came to Hu: he could present the proposal as Ji's own, demonstrating that he possessed exceptional ability in this field too. He therefore began with the guns. “Mr Xuexuan, how is the Huzhou militia getting on?” The question delighted Wang. “Very well! Of all the militia organisations in the province, Huzhou's is proceeding most smoothly. To be fair, the credit belongs to Zhao Jingxian.” He turned to Ji. “His abilities are no less than yours. I must introduce you sometime.” “I've heard that he is both worthy and capable, and have long wanted to know him. An introduction from you would be a great pleasure.” Contentedly, Ji raised his cup and drained it.

“雪公！”胡雪岩把话题拉丁回来，“我替你买了一批洋枪。”他把整个经过说了一遍。“我也要浮一大白！”王有龄极高兴地说，“雪岩你这件事，办得好极了！前两天，抚台还跟我谈起，兵在精而不在多，又说欲善其事，先利其器。龚振麟父子，对造炮虽有经验，无奈不会造枪，现在能够买到洋枪，对防务大有裨益。我明天就‘上院’去见抚台，筹个通盘的办法出来，洋枪多多益善。““那是以后的事。目前这批枪呢？”“这一批枪，当然是我们湖州买！有了这批洋枪，将来的效用如何，且不去说它，起码眼前就可以激励团练的士气，关系甚重。”王有龄又说，“赵景贤知道了这个消息不知道会高兴成什么样子！”

Tomorrow I'll call on the governor and work out a comprehensive arrangement. The more foreign guns, the better.” “That's for later. What about this consignment?” “Huzhou will buy it, naturally. “雪公！”嵇鹤龄插进来说，“既然湖州志在心得，事情就不是这么个做法。明天要防黄抚台截留这批枪，还是暂时不说的好。”“那么到什么时候再说？”“我看要用这么个步骤，”嵇鹤龄慢条斯理地答道，“先跟藩司请一张洋枪的运照，接着了这批枪，送到湖州，然后再跟黄抚台去说。那时枪支已经发了下去，莫非黄抚台倒说，通通收了回来，给他的亲兵用？”“对，对！”王有龄说，“有你们两个人替我画策，真正是万元一失！来，吃酒！“

Ji listened silently, thinking of Hu's interests. The militia is under official supervision but organised locally. The locality raises its own funds and keeps charge of them. Huzhou is wealthy, its people are enthusiastic, and the militia has ample money. Zhao will be grateful to you, and I can speak easily. As for the price, whatever you say will be accepted. The rebate is yours, naturally.” Hu's greatest difficulties were a shortage of assistants and insufficient ready funds. A large deposit from the Huzhou militia would be like water to a fish: nothing could be better. With substantial business in prospect, he would not chase a small profit. “No,” he said. “I want this done handsomely. Let the rebate go into the public funds or be divided among Jingxian's staff. I'll perform the service for nothing.” “There's no need to work for nothing,” Wang replied. “Leave this to me. I'll speak to Zhao Jingxian.”

王有龄私赠嵇鹤龄500两与文案委员索要2,000两是两笔不同性质的钱。王不知道索贿一事，因为嵇不愿让王再替他支付，不能误解为王已拒绝帮助。
湖州团练经费由地方筹集、保管，王有龄只是拟请赵景贤存到阜康，此前因怕被婉拒一直未开口。购枪使该提议更易被接受，尚未写成资金已经到账。胡愿放弃小额回扣，以公共收入或分给赵手下人的方式处理；王最后表示无需白做，由他与赵谈，并未在本批宣布最终分配数额。Hu的预期利益是扩大存款、缓解头寸，不是这次回扣。

英语一综合精读方向

阅读：可比较两条“声誉与金钱”的链条。刘诚实返还已故军官的存款，且通过核验和共同领条使行为被人知道，获得新存户；胡预期因购枪获得团练存款，主动让出回扣。区分已经发生的存款增加与尚待落实的新业务。另注意人物信息差：嵇知道索贿而王不知，胡决定代为说明；王仍不清楚阿珠的婚事转向。
完形：重点观察provided、had...would have...、even if、whereas、otherwise、only afterwards等表达。归还存款一段是“承认存款—提出核验条件—共同具条—立即付款—口碑带来存款”；购枪一段是“王欲先报抚台—嵇预见截留—先领运照并运到湖州—后报抚台”。遮去连接词后，依行为的前提和后果恢复顺序。
段落排序：银行汇报由账目异常引出罗尚德死亡的回叙，再回到当前授权与扩张；嵇来邀宴中断银行谈话，家中孩子的变化引入新城功劳与任用受阻；王到来后以宴席接续购枪。先识别当前动作与往事说明，尤其留意this entry、the following day、since they parted、that consignment的回指。
翻译：可选12357—12363，处理“名与利—风险值不值得—例证—舆论反应—再起机会”的层次，保证胡的原则归属于人物。另选12525—12531，画出团练资金所有与保管、银行存款期待、枪款与回扣四条关系，避免把deposit、payment、rebate混译。人物称谓变化也适合练习指代题：Master、Second Master与Second Uncle所指虽可能同场出现，关系各不相同。
写作：可从中练习“诚信需要在行动中得到证明”“授权应与能力和责任相匹配”“决策应考虑声誉与长期利益”“充分准备有助于实现计划”。可积累hold firm to one’s principles、seize an opportunity、take something off someone’s hands、come to an immediate decision、take responsibility for等表达。战争立场、官场索贿和军火利益分配属于小说情境，不把人物所有手段都作为现代议论文可直接倡导的做法。
