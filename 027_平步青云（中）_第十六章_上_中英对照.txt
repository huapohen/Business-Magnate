第十六章｜CHAPTER SIXTEEN

当天回家，胡雪岩叫阿福把住在附近客栈里的陈世龙去找了来，他是要告诉他一个好消息，到松江接枪，已经用不着他了。眼前在杭州也没有什么事，可以先回湖州一趟，去见一见“丈母娘”。“不必！”陈世龙说，“接枪的事情，也很麻烦，我跟了裘老爷去好了。”“为什么呢？”胡雪岩倒有些诧异，心想这是求之不得的“美差”，陈世龙不该不领情。他何尝不领情，心里也巴不得去看一看小别数日，便如数年的阿珠，只是为了感恩图报，自愿出力。而这话他又不愿说，觉得说了便没意思了，因而沉默不答。胡雪岩是察言观色，只需稍力用点心，便可以看透他的腑肺，心里暗暗欣慰，也不说破，只这样告诉他：“叫你去看丈母娘是‘顺带公文一角’，湖州我一时去不了，有好些事，要你替我去办。你不必到松江去了！”最后一句话，完全是长辈的口气，没有讨价还价的余地，陈世龙只好点点头。

When Hu returned home that day, he sent A’fu to fetch Chen Shilong from the nearby inn. He had good news for him: Chen was no longer needed to collect the guns at Songjiang. There was nothing for him to do in Hangzhou just now either, so he could go back to Huzhou first and visit his “mother-in-law.” “There’s no need,” Chen said. “Collecting the guns will be troublesome too. I had better go with Mr Qiu.” “Why?” Hu was surprised. Surely this was a delightful errand anyone would welcome. Why should Chen refuse the kindness? Chen was far from ungrateful. He longed to see A’zhu; a few days apart had seemed like years. But he wanted to repay Hu by doing whatever work he could. To explain that would take the meaning out of it, so he remained silent. Hu needed only a little attention to read his expression and see into his heart. Secretly pleased, he did not expose the reason. “Your visit to your mother-in-law is merely a dispatch carried on the way. I cannot go to Huzhou just yet, and there are several things you must do for me. You are not going to Songjiang.” The final sentence had all the authority of an elder’s command. There was no room for bargaining. Chen could only nod.

“第一件，你跟你郁四叔去说，如果有多余的头寸，我要用，请他汇到阜康来，期限最好长一点，利息我特别加厚。第二件……”说到第二件，他沉吟了，意思是想把黄仪调开，但丝行才开始做，总得把这一“季”做出个起落来，净赚多少，该分多少花红，有个实实惠惠的交代，则宾主尽欢而散，才是正办。照目前这样子，仿佛有些过河拆桥，传出去于自己的名声有损。“世龙，”他问：“你看黄仪这个人怎么样？”“本事是有的，不大合得来群。”陈世龙直抒观感。“对！你说到了他的短处。”胡雪岩说，“你丈人自己说过，‘吃不住他’，我要想个办法，把他调开，不过目前还不到时候，你跟你丈人说，好歹先敷衍敷衍他，到明年我自有妥当办法。”“我晓得了。”陈世龙又说，“郁四叔那里，最好请胡先生写封信。”“信我是要写的，还有东西带去。啊！”胡雪岩突然喊了起来，“我倒想起来了，老黄文墨很不错，我想请他来帮忙，专门替我写写信，你倒探探他的口气看！ 送他的酬劳，一定够他用，你看他的意思如何？写信来告诉我。”

“First, tell your Uncle Four that, if he has spare funds, I need them. Ask him to remit them to Fukang, preferably for a fairly long term. I will pay him an especially generous rate of interest. Second...” Hu paused. He wanted to move Huang Yi elsewhere, but the silk firm had only just begun operations. They ought at least to finish this season and settle how much net profit had been made and what bonus Huang should receive. A tangible settlement that let employer and employee part happily was the proper course. To move him now would look like destroying the bridge after crossing it, and word of that would damage Hu’s reputation. “Shilong, what do you think of Huang Yi?” “He has ability, but doesn’t get on well with others,” Chen replied frankly. “Exactly. You’ve put your finger on his weakness. Your father-in-law himself says he can’t manage him. I must find a way to move him elsewhere, but the time isn’t right yet. Tell your father-in-law to get along with him as best he can for now. I’ll have a suitable arrangement next year.” “I understand. It would be best if you wrote Uncle Four a letter too.” “I shall write, and there are things for you to take. Ah!” Hu suddenly exclaimed. “That reminds me. Old Huang writes very well. I’d like him to help me by attending solely to my correspondence. Sound him out. I’ll pay enough to meet his needs. Find out what he thinks and write to tell me.”

“这倒也不错。老黄这个人也只有胡先生能收服，他做事最好自己做自己的，不跟人联手，一定做得好。”这样商量定了，陈世龙便整整忙了两天，把胡雪岩要带到湖州送人的土仪什物，以及他自己“孝敬”丈人丈母娘的衣料与食物，向阿珠献殷勤的胭脂花粉，一起采办齐全，再下一天就下了航船，直放湖州。一上岸先到大经丝行，迎面就遇见阿珠的娘，心里没有预备，顿时搞得手足无措。首先称呼就为难，自然不能再叫“张太太”，但又老不出面皮喊声，“娘！”阿珠的娘，却是又惊又喜，“你怎么回来了？”她说，“来，先坐了再说，你丈人也在里头。”说着，她自己先转身走了进去。陈世龙定定神，心里在想，看这样子，丈母娘对自己是中意的，他唯一的顾虑，是怕阿珠的娘，觉得受胡雪岩的好处太多，不一定以这头亲事为然，或者口中不说，心里起了个疙瘩。现在，这个疑虑似乎是多余的了。

“That’s a good idea too. You’re the only person who can bring Old Huang round. He works best on his own, without having to cooperate with anyone. He would certainly do it well.” With this agreed, Chen spent two full days buying the local delicacies and other gifts Hu wanted taken to Huzhou, together with cloth and food for his own offerings to his future parents-in-law, and rouge and scented face powder to please A’zhu. The following day he boarded a passenger boat and went straight to Huzhou. His first call ashore was at Dajing Silk Firm. He met A’zhu’s mother coming towards him and, wholly unprepared, became awkward and flustered. Even a greeting was difficult. He could hardly call her Mrs Zhang any longer, yet could not bring himself to say “Mother.” She, however, was surprised and delighted. “What brings you back? Come and sit down first. Your father-in-law is inside too.” She turned and went ahead. Chen collected himself. Evidently his future mother-in-law approved of him. His one fear had been that, having received so much from Hu, she might dislike this marriage or silently harbour a grievance about it. Now that worry seemed unnecessary.

由店堂绕过屏风，走人第二进就是客帚，这时不是收丝的季节，空荡荡地一个客人都没有，但旁边厢房却有人，是黄仪，在窗子里望见了便喊：“啊呀，新贵人上门了！”一路喊，一路抢了出来，笑脸迎人。陈世龙有些发窘，站定了脚招呼一声：“黄先生，你好！”“你发福了！”黄仪歪着头，从上到下把陈世龙端详了一遍，“上海住了几个月，样子变过了！”这一说引起了阿珠的娘的注意，也是退后两步，直盯着陈世龙看。夷场上的衣饰总要漂亮些，又是“丈母娘看女婿”，所以她脸上的笑意越堆越浓，这样就更要惹得黄仪开玩笑。“张太太，”他笑着说，“回去慢慢看！新贵人脸嫩，看得他不好意思了。”“晓得他脸嫩，你就少说一两句！”阿珠的娘已经在卫护女婿，这样笑着说，“都到里头来坐！”“对！”黄仪兴味盎然地，“我到里头来看你们‘见礼’。”阿珠的娘心里一动，立刻有了个主意，她是体恤女婿，看陈世龙有点发窘，心里便想，“毛脚女婿”第一次上门，总要有个媒人，或者男女两家都熟悉的亲友陪着，彼此才不致尴尬。现在陈世龙象个“没脚蟹”似地，要请黄仪来帮忙，媒人照规矩是两位，有了一个胡雪岩，另一个不是现成在眼前？

Beyond the shopfront and its screen, the second courtyard led into the reception hall. It was not the season for buying silk, and there was not a customer in the empty hall. Huang Yi was in a side room, however. Catching sight of Chen through the window, he called, “Why, the honoured new son-in-law has arrived!” Still calling, he hurried out with a welcoming smile. Chen stopped, somewhat embarrassed. “How are you, Mr Huang?” “You’ve filled out!” Tilting his head, Huang looked him over from head to foot. “A few months in Shanghai have changed your appearance.” This drew the mother’s attention. She too stepped back and stared. Clothes from the foreign settlement were always finer; and with a mother-in-law looking at her prospective son-in-law, her smile naturally grew broader and broader. Huang could not resist more teasing. “Mrs Zhang, you can study him at leisure when you get home. Our new son-in-law is shy. You’re embarrassing him.” Already taking his side, she laughed. “If you know he’s shy, say a little less! Come inside, both of you.” “Yes!” Huang said enthusiastically. “I’ll come and watch you exchange the formal greetings.” This gave her an idea. A young man’s first visit to his future wife’s family ought to be made with a matchmaker or someone who knew both families, to spare everyone embarrassment. Chen had come like a crab without legs. Why not enlist Huang? Custom required two matchmakers. Hu was one; wasn’t the other standing ready before her?

于是她说：“黄先生，我们女家的大媒是胡先生，男家的大媒老爷，拜托了你好不好？”“怎么不好？现成的媒人，求之不得。”陈世龙也听出丈母娘意存体恤，这样安排，再好不过，便向黄仪拱手作揖：“黄先生，我重重拜托！”“好说，好说！”黄仪很高兴地，“那么，张太太，我要叫你亲家太太了！”就这样说笑着，一起进了胡雪岩以前所住的那个院子，老张闻声迎了出来，也有意外的惊喜，陈世龙喊一声：“爹！”有了爹自然有娘，黄仪以媒人的身份，从中牵引，陈世龙便又替老张夫妇磕了头，正式见过礼，改了口，把阿珠的娘笑得合不扰口。这时大经丝行里用的伙计，出店、烧饭司务，还有两三个缫丝的女工，都跑了来看热闹，因为陈世龙平常人缘极好，所以都替他高兴，但也多要开几句玩笑。陈世龙觉得最艰难的是见丈母娘这一关，这一关一过就不在乎，脸皮也厚了，随他们去说，只报以矜持的微笑。

“Mr Huang, Mr Hu is the matchmaker on our daughter’s side. Would you be so kind as to act for the bridegroom?” “Gladly! A ready-made match to arrange—what could be better?” Chen understood her consideration. Nothing could suit him better. He clasped his hands and bowed to Huang. “I entrust it to you, Mr Huang.” “Of course, of course!” Huang was delighted. “Then, Mrs Zhang, I must greet you as our new relation by marriage.” Talking and laughing, they entered the courtyard Hu had formerly occupied. Old Zhang came out at the sound, equally surprised and pleased. “Father!” Chen called. With a father, there was naturally a mother too. Guided by Huang in his new capacity as matchmaker, Chen kowtowed to the couple, formally paid his respects and adopted his new forms of address. A’zhu’s mother could scarcely stop smiling. The firm’s clerks, outside errand man, cook and two or three women who reeled silk all came to watch. Chen had always been well liked, so everyone was happy for him, though most had a joke to make. He had considered meeting his mother-in-law the hardest ordeal. Once through it, he no longer minded. His skin had grown thicker; he let them say what they pleased and answered only with a restrained smile.

然而另一个难关又来了，这一关不是他自己难过，是替阿珠担心，说巧不巧，阿珠从家里到丝行，一路走进来，就看见大家想笑不笑，已在怀疑，等踏入院子，第一眼就看见陈世龙，心里一慌，赶紧想溜，已来不及。“阿珠！”老张在里头喊。阿珠不理，依旧往外走，有个缫丝的女工叫阿翠，生性最好事，偏偏就在她身后，堵着门不让她出去。“走开！”她低声怒喝。“你不要逃嘛！”阿翠笑道，“又不是不认识。”于是里面也笑，外面也笑，终于让阿珠夺门逃走，陈世龙才算松了一口气。阿珠的娘记挂着女儿，同时为女婿设想，料知他一颗心也早就飞了出去，因而看一看天色，提议回家，顺便邀黄仪一起去吃晚饭。黄仪大喜。他不喜欢赌钱，也不会花花草草在外头搞女人，甚至连旱烟都不抽，唯一的嗜好，是口腹之欲，这位“老板娘”的烹调手段，他是领教过的，只是在老张父女到上海去的那些日子，只有阿珠的娘带着个使女爱珍在家，他不便上门去叨扰。从老张回来以后，才又去吃过两次饭，家常肴馔、精洁有余，丰腆不足，未能大嚼，今天又是款待“毛脚女婿”，又是请媒人，自然有一顿称心满意的晚饭好吃了。

Another ordeal now arrived, though it was A’zhu, rather than himself, for whom Chen was anxious. By an awkward coincidence she had just come from home to the firm. The half-suppressed smiles she encountered on her way in had already made her suspicious. As soon as she entered the courtyard, she saw Chen. In sudden confusion she tried to slip away, but it was too late. “A’zhu!” her father called from inside. Ignoring him, she kept going out. A silk-reeling woman named A’cui, who loved to meddle, happened to be behind her and blocked the doorway. “Get out of the way!” A’zhu whispered angrily. “Don’t run away!” A’cui laughed. “It isn’t as though you don’t know him.” Laughter broke out inside and outside. Only when A’zhu finally escaped through the door did Chen breathe freely. Her mother was concerned for her daughter and considerate of Chen, whose heart, she guessed, had already flown after her. Looking at the hour, she proposed returning home and invited Huang to supper. He was delighted. He disliked gambling, did not chase women, and did not even smoke a dry pipe. Food was his one indulgence, and he knew his employer’s wife could cook. While Zhang and A’zhu were in Shanghai, she had been alone at home with her maid Aizhen, and he could hardly impose on them. Since Zhang’s return he had dined there twice, but the everyday dishes, though excellent and beautifully prepared, had not been plentiful enough for him to eat his fill. Today they were entertaining both a prospective son-in-law on his first visit and a matchmaker. A satisfying dinner was assured.

“你先去！”老张对他妻子说，“胡先生带来送人的东西，我跟世龙先料理料理，弄好了就回来。”“今天也晚了，留到明天再说。”阿珠的娘这样嘱咐：“世龙就住在店里好了，要茶要水也方便。要住哪一问自己挑，挑好了叫他们打扫，铺盖到家里去拿。”这番体贴，完全是父母之心，陈世龙极其感动，但也很不安，就此刻他已觉得岳家的恩情太重，不知何以报答？加上胡雪岩的一手提拔，越有恐惧不胜之情，于是不由得又想到阿珠的那番激励：“ ‘好女不穿嫁时衣’，这些首饰，可惜不是你买给我的！”同时也记了胡雪岩对阿珠说过的那句话：“等世龙将来发达了，给你买金刚钻。”两下凑在一起，陈世龙死心塌地了！“爹！”等阿珠的娘一走，陈世龙这样对老张说：“你先陪了黄先生回去。我把胡先生交代的事，办完了就来。今天我仍旧回家去住，省得麻烦。”“何必？”黄仪劝他：“明天一早来料理也一样。”“不！”陈世龙固执地：“今日事，今日毕，明天有明天的事，积在一起，拖到后天，那就永远料理不清楚了！”听这一说，已入中年的黄仪不断点头，“老张！”他说，“你这个女婿，人又变过了，不但聪明勤快，而且老成扎实！真正是乘尤快婿，恭喜，恭喜！”老张是忠厚老实到了家的，自然更欣赏陈世龙的作风。要这样，后半世才有依靠！照他的想法，当时就想下手帮忙，但既邀了黄仪回家吃饭，也不便让他空等。就这踌躇之间，有了个主意，正不妨趁此机会跟黄仪先谈一谈如何办喜事。

“Go ahead,” Old Zhang told his wife. “Shilong and I will sort out the presents Mr Hu has sent. We’ll come when we’ve finished.” “It’s late already. Leave them until tomorrow. Shilong had better stay at the shop, where tea and water will be handy. Let him choose whichever room he likes, have them clean it and fetch bedding from home.” Her care was entirely a parent’s. Chen was deeply touched, but uneasy too. Already he felt his debt to his future wife’s family was too great to repay. Added to Hu’s efforts to advance him, it almost frightened him. Inevitably he remembered A’zhu’s encouragement: “A good wife doesn’t depend on the clothes she brought at marriage. It’s a pity you didn’t buy these jewels for me yourself!” He also remembered Hu telling her, “When Shilong prospers, he’ll buy you diamonds.” The two remarks together fixed his determination. Once her mother had gone, he told Old Zhang, “Father, take Mr Huang home first. I’ll come when I’ve finished Mr Hu’s business. I’ll sleep at my own place tonight to save trouble.” “Why?” Huang urged. “You can see to it early tomorrow.” “No,” Chen insisted. “Today’s work must be finished today. Tomorrow has its own business. Let them pile up and carry over to the day after, and they’ll never be cleared.” Huang, already middle-aged, nodded repeatedly. “Old Zhang, your son-in-law has changed again. Clever and diligent, and now mature and dependable too! An excellent match for your daughter. Congratulations!” Thoroughly honest and conscientious himself, Zhang valued this attitude still more. Here was someone he could rely on in later life. He wanted to help at once, but having invited Huang to dinner, could hardly keep him waiting. While hesitating, he saw an opportunity: they could discuss the wedding first.

陪他到家，刚一进门，里面阿珠便躲了开去，爱珍来开了门，第一个先寻陈世龙，看看不见，便失望地问了出来：“咦！姑少爷呢？”骤然改口，老张倒是一愣，想一想才明白，随即答道：“在收拾东西，要等下才来。”听这一说，爱珍便急忙到厨房里去报告消息。阿珠跟她一样失望，但似乎又觉得轻松。不过，还有个黄仪，这时一走出去，必定受窘，因而又有些上心事。她娘看不出她的心事，正忙得不可开交，要在个把钟头以内，弄出一桌象样的菜来，着实要费一番手脚。而且不但手脚忙，口中也不闭，一面调理咸酸，一面不厌其详地讲解，让阿珠都听得有些烦了。“娘！”她说，“这时候哪里有工夫讲空话？”“你当是空话？”做母亲的大为不悦。“马上要自己做人家了，我教得你一样是一样，你还不肯学！”阿珠的娘埋怨女儿，“虽然上头没有婆婆，旁人要说闲话。一把锅铲刀上没有点功夫，你想想，男人怎么会在家里耽得住？”话是不中听，但看娘忙成这个样子，阿珠不肯再跟她争辩，只是一向撒娇惯了的，不顶句嘴办不到，便笑着说道：“随你，随你！你老太太喜欢罗嗦，尽管去罗嗦好了！”阿珠的娘，实在也没有工夫“罗嗦”了，却又惦记着外面，你去听听！“她说，“黄先生跟你爹讲些什么？”这句话正中下怀，阿珠随即出了厨房，躲在窗下，用发簪在窗纸上戳出个小孔，悄悄向外窥探。

As they entered the house, A’zhu withdrew from sight and Aizhen came to open the door. Her eyes searched first for Chen. Not seeing him, she asked in disappointment, “Where is the young son-in-law?” The sudden change of title startled Zhang before he understood. “Sorting things out. He’ll come later.” Aizhen hurried to the kitchen with the news. A’zhu was just as disappointed, though also somewhat relieved. Huang was still there, however; if she went out now, he would certainly embarrass her. This gave her another worry. Her mother was too busy to notice. Preparing a respectable dinner within an hour or so required considerable effort. Her tongue was as busy as her hands: while adjusting the seasoning, she explained every detail until A’zhu grew impatient. “Mother, how have you time for idle talk now?” “You call it idle talk?” Her mother was displeased. “You’ll soon have a household of your own. Every little thing I can teach you will help, yet you won’t learn! You may have no mother-in-law above you, but other people will gossip. Without some skill with a knife and spatula, how do you expect to keep a man at home?” The words were unwelcome, but seeing her mother so busy, A’zhu would not argue. Still, she was too accustomed to having her own way to offer no retort at all. “As you please! If you enjoy lecturing, go on!” she said with a smile. In fact, her mother had no time for more lecturing. Remembering the men outside, she said, “Go and listen. What are Mr Huang and your father discussing?” This was precisely what A’zhu wanted. She left the kitchen, concealed herself beneath a window, pricked a little hole in the paper with her hairpin and peered through.

外面一主一宾，神态各别，老张正襟危坐，显得极为郑重，黄仪却是翘着着“二郎腿”，很随便的样子，这时正是他在说话。“换个庚帖，方便得很，回头叫你们大小姐去买全帖来，我马上就写，男女两家，归我一手包办。还有啥？”“还有，‘送日子’归男家。”老张停了一下又说：“世龙预备啥时候办喜事，拜托你问他一声。”“这何必还要我问？”黄仪笑道，“你们翁婿这么熟的人，用得着我这个现成的媒人传话？”“这也是规矩。总要请大媒老爷……”“老张！”黄仪突然打断他的话说，“所谓六礼：纳采、问名、纳吉、纳征、请期、亲迎，只有一项，我该替女家效劳的。‘纳征’怎么说？”“六礼”二字，老张倒听见过，“纳征”他就不懂了。后面的阿珠也在纳闷，听语气是不知出了什么花样？所以越发侧耳细听。“纳征就是聘礼。这个上头，你们自己不好开口，我倒可以替你去问。”“原来是聘礼，这个已经有了。想来你还不晓得，应该请你过目。”

Host and guest presented different pictures. Zhang sat upright and looked extremely serious; Huang sat casually with one leg crossed over the other. He was speaking. “Exchanging birth cards is easy enough. Send your young lady to buy the full set of formal sheets later, and I’ll write them at once. I can handle both families’ copies. What else?” “The bridegroom’s family must send the date.” Zhang paused. “Please ask Shilong when he intends to hold the wedding.” “Why ask through me?” Huang laughed. “You two know each other so well. Do you need this matchmaker, who has had the whole affair ready-made for him, to carry messages?” “That is the custom too. We must ask our honoured matchmaker...” “Old Zhang!” Huang suddenly interrupted. “Of the six rites—sending the proposal, asking the bride’s name, announcing the auspicious divination result, presenting the betrothal gifts, requesting a date and fetching the bride—there is only one in which I ought to serve the bride’s family. What about the betrothal offerings?” Zhang had heard of the “six rites,” but the formal term Huang used for the offerings meant nothing to him. Behind the window, A’zhu was puzzled too. What new complication did his tone suggest? She listened still more closely. “I mean the betrothal gifts. You can hardly raise that question yourselves, but I can ask for you.” “Oh, those! They have already arrived. You may not know. You ought to inspect them.”

于是老张亲自入内，小心翼翼地捧了个朱漆描金的拜盒出来，打开一看，是这么四件首饰，黄仪大出意外。“是胡先生代世龙送的。”这句话使黄仪更感意外。他对胡雪岩的接触不算多，但却听见过许多说他慷慨的话，于今一看，果不其然。这位“东家”本性着实宽厚，就跟他一辈子亦何妨。“好极，好极！”黄仪也替阿珠高兴，“将来新娘子珠围翠绕，打扮出来，格外出色。我看老张，现在凡事有胡先生替世龙作主，啥事情你不必问我，问他好了。”这一句话，确是要言不烦，老张爽然若失，问了半天，原是白问，照现在这样子看，只怕陈世龙也做不得自己的主。说不定胡雪岩已有话交代，等下倒不妨问问他。又闲谈了好一会，黄仪肚子饿得咕咕叫，正想开口先向主人家要些什么点心来吃，总算还好，陈世龙到了。一路上他是想好了来的，虽说结成至亲，不过多了一重名分，在岳家他仍旧应该象从前一样，才显得亲切自然，而且也为自己减除了许多窘相。所以招呼过后，一直就往厨房里走去。

Zhang went inside himself and carefully brought out a red lacquer presentation box decorated in gold. The four kinds of jewellery it contained greatly surprised Huang. “Mr Hu sent them on Shilong’s behalf.” This surprised him still more. Though his dealings with Hu had been limited, he had heard many accounts of his generosity. Now he saw that they were true. His employer was thoroughly generous by nature. Why should he not remain with such a man all his life? “Excellent, excellent!” Huang was happy for A’zhu too. “Decked in pearls and jade, the bride will look splendid. Old Zhang, since Mr Hu is arranging everything for Shilong, there’s no need to ask me about any of it. Ask him.” Those few words went straight to the point. Zhang felt suddenly at a loss. All his questions had been wasted. Apparently Chen might not be free to decide for himself either. Perhaps Hu had already given instructions; Zhang could ask Chen when he arrived. They talked idly for a while longer. Huang’s stomach was rumbling, and he was about to ask his host for a snack when Chen finally came. On the way, Chen had thought matters out. Becoming close relations merely added a new formal bond. He should behave in his future wife’s home as he always had. That would be affectionate and natural, and would spare him much embarrassment. After greeting the men, therefore, he headed straight for the kitchen.

一踏到后面，顶头就遇见阿珠，双方都以猝不及防而微吃一惊，但亦随即都在心头浮现了莫可言喻的喜悦。陈世龙只叫得一声：“阿珠！”便把一双眼睛瞪住在她身上不放。“你有几天耽搁？”她很快地说，声音也很轻。不问来，先问走，便已见得她的不舍之意，就这样一句平淡的话，已使得陈世龙回肠荡气，真想终老家乡，一辈子厮守着阿珠。然而他也马上自谴，觉得起这种念头就是没出息，因而放出那种无所谓的神态说：“要看胡先生的意思，他差遣我到哪里，就到哪里，信一来就走。”阿珠不响，心里有许多话要说，而此时此地不是细诉衷曲的时候，便侧着身子努一努嘴，意思是让他到厨房里去跟她娘招呼。陈世龙会意，微笑着点一点头，走过她身边时，在暗头里捏住了她的手，柔荑一握，入手心荡，倒又舍不得走了。阿珠不赞成他这样的行为，只是不忍拒绝，倚恃母亲的宽容，就看见了也不会责备，便尽着由他握着。偏偏不识相的爱珍一头冲了出来，阿珠眼尖，夺手便走。陈世龙也有些吃惊，搭讪着说：“爱珍，我有两样东西从上海带来送你。一样是象牙蓖箕，一样是一个五颜六色的木头，镶嵌得很好看的盒子，不晓得你喜欢不喜欢？”“喜欢的！”爱珍很高兴地说，“谢谢姑少爷！”“少爷”这个称呼在陈世龙已觉得很新鲜，何况是“ 姑少爷”？他自己把这三个字，默默念了两遍，忽然发觉，他和张家的身分，都在无形中提高了！这自是受了胡雪岩的惠，但自己和张家的身分，是不是真的提高了呢？这一点他却有些不大明白。

At the back of the house he met A’zhu coming towards him. Both were startled by the unexpected encounter, but in the next instant an indescribable joy rose in both their hearts. Chen could only say, “A’zhu!” and fix his eyes on her. “How many days can you stay?” she asked quickly and softly. Asking about his departure before his arrival already showed how unwilling she was to part. That simple question stirred him deeply. He wanted to spend the rest of his days in his native town, always beside her. At once he reproached himself: such thoughts showed a lack of ambition. Assuming an unconcerned manner, he answered, “That depends on Mr Hu. Wherever he sends me, I go. I shall leave as soon as his letter comes.” She said nothing. There was much in her heart, but this was no time or place to express it. Moving aside, she gestured with her lips towards the kitchen, telling him to go and greet her mother. He smiled and nodded. As he passed her in the dim passage, he took her hand. At its touch his heart stirred, and he could not bear to move on. A’zhu did not approve of this, but could not bear to refuse him either. Trusting that her mother would be indulgent even if she saw, she let him hold her hand. Just then, the tactless Aizhen came rushing out. A’zhu saw her first, pulled her hand free and hurried away. Startled too, Chen tried to cover his confusion. “Aizhen, I’ve brought you two things from Shanghai: an ivory comb and a little box beautifully inlaid with different-coloured woods. I hope you’ll like them.” “I will! Thank you, young son-in-law!” Even being called “young master” was new to Chen, let alone this title. Repeating it silently twice, he suddenly realised that both his standing and the Zhang family’s had risen without their noticing. This was Hu’s gift to them. Yet had their standing really risen? On that point he was uncertain.

这些念头如电闪一般在心头划过，一时也不暇去细思，因为人已到了厨房，先喊一声，“娘！”然后去到他丈母娘身边去看她做菜。“厨房里脏！”阿珠的娘一面煎鱼，一面大声说道：“你外头坐。”“不要紧！”陈世龙不肯走。这时是一条尺把长的鲫鱼，刚刚下锅，油锅正“哗哗”地响，阿珠的娘全神贯注着，没有工夫跟他说话，等下了作料，放了清汤，盖上锅盖以后，才用围裙擦一擦手，笑嘻嘻地问：“东西都料理好了？”“都料理好了，请出店一份份连夜去送，也挑他挣几个脚力钱。娘。”陈世龙又说，“我给你剪了两件衣服。天气快冷了，我又替你买了个白铜手炉。”“我哪里有闲下来烘手炉的辰光？”做丈母娘的说，“下次不要买，啥也不要买，何必去花这些钱？再说，你现在也挣不到多少钱，一切总要俭朴。”话是好话，陈世龙不大听得进去。不过他也了解，天下父母心都是如此。所以不答这句腔，把话题扯了开去。就这样，他绕着丈母娘的身子转，谈到在上海、在松江的情形，絮絮不断地，真有那种依依膝下的意绪。阿珠的娘，一面忙着做菜，一面也兴味盎然地听他讲话，有些事已听阿珠讲过，但再听一遍，仍然觉得有趣。

The thoughts flashed through him too quickly to examine. He was already in the kitchen. “Mother!” he called, then went over to watch her cook. “It’s dirty in the kitchen!” she said loudly, busy frying a fish. “Go and sit outside.” “That doesn’t matter.” Chen would not go. A crucian carp about a foot long had just gone into the pan, and the oil was hissing. She was too intent on it to talk. Only after adding the seasonings and clear stock and covering the pan did she wipe her hands on her apron and ask with a smile, “Have you sorted everything out?” “Everything. I’ve asked the outside errand man to deliver the parcels tonight. It will let him earn a little in delivery fees too. Mother, I also bought cloth for two outfits for you. The weather will soon be cold, so I bought you a white-copper hand-warmer as well.” “When do I ever have time to sit holding a hand-warmer? Don’t buy anything next time. Nothing at all. Why spend money on these things? Besides, you don’t earn much yet. You must be economical in everything.” It was good advice, though Chen did not much take it in. Still, he understood that parents everywhere felt this way. Rather than answer that remark, he changed the subject. Thus he stayed close beside his mother-in-law, talking endlessly about Shanghai and Songjiang, with all the affection of a child at a parent’s knee. While she cooked, she listened with keen interest. A’zhu had already told her some of the stories, but hearing them again was still a pleasure.

等厨房里整备停当，人座时又有一番谦让，结果当然是黄仪上座。阿珠和她母亲，原可入席，而这天是例外，母女俩等前面吃完了，方始将残肴撤下来，叫爱珍一起坐下，将就着吃了一顿。吃完收拾，洗碗熄火，请事皆毕，而前面却还谈得很热闹。老张回来多日，上海的情形他也很清楚，但一向不善同令也不喜说话，所以黄仪从他嘴里听不到什么。跟陈世龙在一起就不同了，他说话本有条理，记性又好，形容十里夷场的风光，以及各式各样的人物，把个足不出里门的黄仪，听得神往不止。这种不自觉流露的表情，不要说陈世龙，就连老张都看出来了，因此当谈话告一段落时，他向黄仪说道，“上海倒是不可不去，几时你也去走一趟？”“那一定要的。”黄仪也是个不甘雌伏的人，此时听了陈世龙的话，对胡雪岩有了一种新的想法，觉得跟了这个人去闯市面，是件很够劲的事，不过这番意思却不知如何表达，只问了声：“胡先生啥时光到湖州来？”“他一时伯没有到湖州来的工夫。”陈世龙说，“上海、杭州方面的事，怕生了四只手都忙不过来。”“其实，我们在这里也是闲坐。”陈世龙听出因头，当时不响。辞出张家时，表示要送黄仪回店，那一个谈兴未央，欣然表示欢迎。于是回到大经丝行，泡了壶茶，剔亮了灯，继续再谈。陈世龙依照胡雪岩的指示，以话套话，把黄仪所希望的“进帐”，探听清楚，然后说道：“胡先生很佩服你的文墨，他现在就少一个能够替他代代笔的人。胡先生经手的事，官私西面都很多，‘有些事情是不便叫第三者晓得的，只有心腹知己才可以代劳。这一个人很难找。”

When the food was ready, there was another exchange of polite refusals over the places at table. Huang naturally took the seat of honour. Ordinarily A’zhu and her mother could have joined them, but that evening was an exception. They waited until the men had finished, then brought back the remaining dishes and ate a makeshift meal with Aizhen. Afterwards they cleared up, washed the dishes and put out the fire. Everything was done, but the talk in the front room was still lively. Zhang had been back for days and knew Shanghai well enough, yet he had never been good at conversation or fond of it. Huang had learned little from him. With Chen it was different. Chen spoke clearly and remembered well. His descriptions of the foreign settlement and its varied inhabitants filled Huang, who scarcely ventured outside his own neighbourhood, with longing. His expression betrayed him so plainly that even Zhang noticed, let alone Chen. At a pause, Zhang said, “Shanghai is somewhere you really must see. When will you go?” “I certainly shall.” Huang was not a man content to remain in obscurity. Chen’s account had given him a new view of Hu: following such a man into the wider world of business would be exhilarating. He did not know how to express this and merely asked when Hu would next come to Huzhou. “He probably won’t have time just yet,” Chen answered. “With all his affairs in Shanghai and Hangzhou, even four hands wouldn’t be enough.” “We’re only sitting idle here, really.” Chen recognised an opening but said nothing then. When they left the Zhang house, he offered to walk Huang back to the firm. Still eager to talk, Huang gladly accepted. There they made tea, trimmed the lamp and resumed their conversation. Following Hu’s instructions, Chen drew Huang out until he knew what income Huang hoped for. “Mr Hu admires your writing,” he then said. “He needs someone to take care of his correspondence. He handles a great deal of public and private business, some of which cannot be known to a third person. Only a trusted intimate can help with it. Such a man is hard to find.”

“怎么样？”黄仪很注意地问，“胡先生是不是想叫我去？”“他没有跟我说。”陈世龙本来想说：如果你有意思，我可以写信给胡先生。转念一想，这样说法，即表示自己在胡雪岩面前的关系比他深，怕黄仪多心，因而改口说道：“如果胡先生有这个意思，当然直接会跟你商量的。”“嗯，嗯！”黄仪忽然想到，大经丝行的事也不坏，不必亟亟乎改弦易辙，便即答道：“一动不如一静，看看再说。”陈世龙一听话锋不对，知道是因为自己话太多了的缘故，心里深为澳悔。同时再也不肯多说，告辞回到自己住处。多日不曾归家，灰尘积得甚厚，又忙了大半夜，草草睡下，这一天实在太累了，头一着枕，便已入梦。睡梦头里仿佛听得屋里有脚步声，但双眼倦涩，懒得去问。翻个身想再寻好梦时，只觉双眼刺痛，用手遮着，睁眼看时，但见红日满窗，阳光中一条女人的影子，急切问，辨不出是什么人？只是睡意却完全为这条俏拔的影子所驱除，坐起来掀开帐门，细看，不由得诧异：“是你！”“是我！你想不到吧？”“真是不曾想到。”陈世龙不曾想到水晶阿七会突然出现。梦意犹在，而又遇见梦想不到的情况，他的脑子被搅得乱七八糟，茫然不知所措，只是看看窗外，又看看阿七，先要把到底是不是在做梦这个疑问，作个澄清。

“What?” Huang asked with close attention. “Does Mr Hu want me to go?” “He didn’t tell me.” Chen had meant to add, “If you’re interested, I can write to him.” Then he reflected that this would suggest he stood closer to Hu than Huang did, which might make Huang suspicious. Instead he said, “If Mr Hu has that in mind, naturally he will discuss it with you himself.” “Mm, mm.” It suddenly occurred to Huang that Dajing Silk Firm was a good place too; there was no need to hurry into a change. “Better to stay than move. We’ll wait and see.” Hearing the altered tone, Chen knew he had talked too much and deeply regretted it. He would say no more. Taking his leave, he went back to his own lodging. Dust lay thick after his long absence. He spent another half-night putting things right, then hastily went to bed. The day had exhausted him; his head scarcely touched the pillow before he slept. In his dreams he seemed to hear footsteps in the room, but his eyes were too weary to open and he could not be bothered to ask. Turning over to seek another pleasant dream, he felt a sudden stab of light in his eyes. He shaded them with a hand and looked. Bright sunlight filled the window, with a woman’s figure outlined against it. At first he could not tell who she was, but the slender silhouette drove away his sleepiness. Sitting up and pulling aside the bed-curtains, he looked more closely. “You!” “Yes, me. You didn’t expect me, did you?” “Certainly not.” He had never expected Crystal A’qi to appear. Still half in a dream, confronted by something beyond his imagining, he was utterly confused. He looked from her to the window and back again, first trying to establish whether he was awake at all.

“我盼望你好几天了！”阿七幽幽地说，同时走了过来，由暗处到亮处站住脚，拿一双水汪汪的眼睛，在陈世龙脸上瞟来瞟去。这下陈世龙才把她看清楚，脂粉未施，鬓发蓬松，但不假膏沐，却越显她的“真本钱”，白的雪白，黑的漆黑，一张嘴唇不知是不是上火的关系，红得象榴花。身上穿一件紧身黑缎夹袄，胸前鼓蓬蓬，大概连肚兜都未带。这触目惊心的一番打量，把他残余的睡意，驱除得干干净净，跳起身来，先把所有的窗子打开，然后大声说道：“你请外面坐！”“为啥？”“不方便！”“怕什么！”阿七答道，“我们规规矩矩说话，又没有做啥坏事。”“话不是这么说……”陈世龙心里十分着急，就无法跟她好好讲了，紧皱着眉，连连挥手，“你最好请回去！我这个地方你不要来。”这一说，阿七脸色大变，但愤怒多于羞惭，同时也不能期望她能够为这么一句话气走，不但不走，反倒坐了下来，冷笑说道：“小和尚，我晓得你已讨厌我了。”看样子，她要撒泼。如果换了几个月以前，他倒也不在乎她，对骂就对骂，对打就对打，如果她要哭、自己就甩手一走，反正没有她占的便宜。但现在情形不同了，这中间关碍着身分，脸面，而最要紧的是嫌疑，在郁四面前分辩不清楚，固然麻烦，若是风声传入阿珠耳中，更是件不得了的事，因而只好想办法敷衍。

“I’ve been waiting for you for days,” A’qi said plaintively. She came closer, stepping out of the shadow into the light, and let her liquid eyes move over his face. Now he could see her clearly. She wore no powder or rouge, and her hair was loose, but the lack of grooming only revealed her natural attractions more fully. The white of her skin was snowy, the black of her hair jet-black. Perhaps from excess inner heat, her lips were as red as pomegranate flowers. Her close-fitting black satin jacket swelled over her breasts; apparently she wore no bodice beneath it. This startling sight banished every remnant of sleep. He sprang up, opened all the windows and said loudly, “Please sit outside.” “Why?” “It isn’t convenient.” “What is there to fear? We’re talking properly. We haven’t done anything wrong.” “That isn’t the point...” In his anxiety, Chen could no longer explain patiently. Frowning, he waved his hand repeatedly. “You had better go home. Don’t come here.” Her face changed sharply, with more anger than shame. Nor could he expect one remark to drive her off. Instead of leaving, she sat down and sneered. “Little Monk, I know you’re tired of me now.” It looked as though she meant to make a scene. A few months earlier he would not have cared: he could answer abuse with abuse and blows with blows. If she cried, he could simply walk away. She would have gained nothing. Now matters were different. His standing and reputation were involved, and above all there was the danger of suspicion. Explaining himself to Yu Si would be troublesome enough; if word reached A’zhu, it would be disastrous. He must find some way to manage A’qi.

“不是讨厌你，是不敢惹你。”陈世龙这样答道，“你不想想你现在啥身分？我啥身分？”“你啥身分我不晓得！不过吃饭不要忘记种田人，不是我在胡老板面前替你说好话，你哪有今天？这话不是我丑表功，要你见我的情。我不过表表心，让你晓得，你老早把我抛到九霄云外，我总是时时刻刻想着你。”这番话叫陈世龙无以为答，唯有报以苦笑：“谢谢你！闲话少说，你有啥事情，灶王爷上天，直奏好了。”“不作兴来看看你，一定要有事才来？”“好了，好了！”陈世龙又不耐烦了，“你晓得郁四叔的脾气的。而且我……”他是要说，答应过胡雪岩，从此不跟她见面。但这话说出来，没意思，所以顿住了口，而阿七却毫不放松：“男了汉、大丈夫，该说就说！你有什么话说不出口。”“跟你不相干！总而言之，你来看我，我谢谢你。现在看过了，你好走了！”阿七一听这话，霍地站起身来，把脚顿两顿才骂道：“你死没良心！”她咬牙切齿的，“我偏偏不走！”

“It isn’t that I’m tired of you. I dare not get involved with you,” Chen replied. “Think of your position now, and mine.” “I don’t know what your position is! But when you eat, don’t forget the man who grew the rice. Where would you be today if I hadn’t spoken for you to Mr Hu? I’m not boasting of my services or demanding gratitude. I only want you to know what is in my heart. You forgot me long ago, but I’ve never stopped thinking of you.” Chen had no answer beyond a rueful smile. “Thank you. But enough of that. If you have business, be like the Kitchen God going up to Heaven: make your report directly.” “Can’t I come to see you? Must I have business?” “Enough!” His patience was failing again. “You know Uncle Four’s temper. Besides, I...” He had meant to say that he had promised Hu never to see her again. But there was no point in saying that, and he stopped. A’qi gave him no respite. “Be a man and say what you have to say! What can’t you bring yourself to tell me?” “Nothing that concerns you. In any case, thank you for coming to see me. Now you’ve seen me, you can leave.” At this she sprang up and stamped twice. “You heartless wretch!” she said through clenched teeth. “I won’t go!”

“你不走，我走！”陈世龙摘下衣架上的夹袍，往身上一披，低头拔鞋，连正眼都不看她。“好了，好了！”阿七软语赔罪，“何必生这么大的气？”陈世龙啼笑皆非，同时也不能再走了，因为这样要甩手一走，就会有人批评：第一欺侮女人，不算好汉，第二，说他连水晶阿七这样一个女人都应付不了。不走就得另打主意，陈世龙发过一阵脾气，此时冷静下来，觉得麻烦要找了来，推不掉就只有挺身应付，且看她说些什么？反正抱定宗旨，不理她，等她走后，再到郁四那里和盘托出，原来就要去看郁四，转达胡雪岩的口信，正好“烧香看和尚，一事两勾当”。于是他拔上鞋子再扣衣纽，阿七还来帮他的忙，低着头替他扣腋下的扣子，露出雪白了这一段头颈，正在陈世龙眼下，他把视线移了开去，但“元宝领”中的散发出来的甜甜、暖暖的香味，却叫他躲避不了。好在这只是片刻工夫，等把衣纽扣好，随即走到窗前一张凳子上坐下，预备好好应付麻烦。“我昨天刚刚到，胡先生有好些要紧的事情，叫我替他去办。县衙门里杨师爷在等我，”陈世龙先表白一段，然后提出要求说：“你有话，爽爽快快说！我实在没有工夫陪你。”

“If you won’t, I will!” Chen took his lined gown from the clothes stand and slipped it on, then bent to pull on his shoes without looking at her. “All right, all right!” She softened her voice and tried to make peace. “Why be so angry?” He did not know whether to laugh or cry. Nor could he now walk away. If he did, people would say, first, that bullying a woman was unworthy of a man, and second, that he could not even cope with Crystal A’qi. If he stayed, he must devise another approach. His fit of temper had passed. More calmly, he reflected that trouble had sought him out: if he could not push it away, he must face it and hear what she had to say. He would hold firmly to his resolve and give her no encouragement. When she left, he would tell Yu Si everything. He had to see him anyway with Hu’s message. As the saying went, burn incense and visit the monk—two errands accomplished together. Chen pulled on his shoes and began fastening his gown. A’qi came to help, lowering her head to fasten the buttons beneath his arm. A stretch of white neck lay directly under his eyes. He turned his gaze away, but could not escape the warm, sweet scent rising from her high, ingot-shaped collar. Fortunately it lasted only a moment. As soon as the buttons were fastened, he sat on a stool by the window, ready to deal with the difficulty. “I only arrived yesterday. Mr Hu has several urgent matters for me to attend to, and Secretary Yang at the county yamen is waiting for me.” Having stated his position, he made his request. “Say what you have to say plainly. I really haven’t time to keep you company.”

水晶阿七不即回答，想了好一会才说：“本来有一肚皮的话，要细细的告诉你，所以特为起个早来。既然你没有工夫，要我爽爽快快地说，我就说一句：三年前头，你跟我说过的那句话，算不算数？”提到三年前，陈世龙就知道麻烦不小，那时阿七还没有跟郁四，跟陈世尤有过一段情。情热如火时，什么话都说出来，陈世龙不知道她指的是哪句话？不过也可以想象得到，这句话在这时候来说，一定对自己不利。因此他先就来个“金钟罩”，概不认帐：“那时的话哪里好作数？”“什么？”阿七咄咄逼人地，“亏你说得出口，说了话不算数？难道你小和尚是这种没肩胛的人？”“肩胛要看摆在什么地方？”陈世龙说，“我也不知道你指的是啥？如果说，我答应过你什么，譬如买衣料、打镯子什么的，我自然有肩胛，倘或有些事情，当时做得到，现在做不到，再有肩胛的也没有办法。”“你自然做得到。”阿七说道：“你倒再想想看，你答应过我一句什么话？”“我想不起，你说好了。”“你说过，要我跟你。就是这句话！”

A’qi did not answer at once. She thought for a good while before saying, “I had a bellyful of things to tell you, every detail of them. That was why I got up specially early to come. Since you have no time, and want me to be quick and plain, I’ll ask just one thing. Three years ago you made me a promise. Does it still count?” At the mention of three years ago, Chen knew he was in serious trouble. Before A’qi had taken up with Yu Si, she and Chen had had a romance. When their passion burned hot, he had said all sorts of things. He had no idea which promise she meant, but any promise she brought up now was sure to tell against him. He therefore put on an armour of blanket denial. “How can anything said then still count?” “What?” she demanded fiercely. “Aren’t you ashamed to say that? Your word doesn’t count? Is Little Monk the sort of man who refuses to take responsibility?” “That depends on what I’m supposed to take responsibility for,” Chen said. “I don’t even know what you mean. If I promised to buy you cloth, say, or have bracelets made, of course I’ll stand by it. But if something was possible then and is impossible now, all the responsibility in the world won’t help.” “Of course it’s possible. Think again. What did you promise me?” “I can’t remember. Tell me.” “You said you wanted me to come and live with you. That was the promise!”

这句话却把陈世龙搞糊涂了，原来以为她只是想瞒着郁四来偷情，不道是这样一句话！“那怎么行！”他脱口答道，“你是郁四叔的人，怎么谈得到此？”这是陈世龙失言，他没有细想一想，如果她还是跟着郁四，怎么能说这话？阿七相当机警，捉住他这个漏洞，逼紧了问：“你是说，碍着郁老头？如果没有这重关碍，你当然还是有肩胛，说话一定算话！是不是？“话外有话，陈世龙再不敢造次，先把她前后两句话的意思细想了一遍问道：“是不是你跟郁四叔散伙了？”“对！我跟郁老头散伙了。”果有其事，陈世龙不免诧异，照他知道，郁四是一天都离不开阿七的，何以竟会散伙？莫非阿七做下什么不规矩的事，为郁四所不能容忍，赶出门去？“你奇怪是不是？”阿七神色泰然地说，“我先说一句，好叫你放心，我跟郁老头是好来好散的。”这就越发不能理解了！“是怎么回事？”他说，“我有点不大相信。”“不要说你不相信，连我自己都不大相信。不过，这也该当你我要走到这一步，真正运气来了，城墙都挡不住。”看她那种兴高采烈、一厢情愿的神气，陈世龙又好笑，又好气，本来想拦着不让她说，但这一来马上又要吵架，她如何跟郁四散伙的经过，就听不到了。因而很沉着地听她讲完，催促着说：“你闲话少说！就讲郁四叔为啥跟你散伙好了。”

This left Chen bewildered. He had assumed that she merely wanted a secret affair behind Yu Si’s back. He had never expected this. “How could that be?” he blurted out. “You’re Uncle Four’s woman. How can you even speak of it?” He had made a false step. He had not stopped to reflect that if she were still living with Yu Si she could hardly make such a proposal. A’qi was quick enough to seize the opening. “You mean Old Yu is the obstacle? Without that obstacle, of course you’d be a responsible man and keep your word. Isn’t that so?” There was something behind her words. Chen dared not answer rashly again. He considered her two remarks together, then asked, “Have you and Uncle Four parted company?” “Exactly. Old Yu and I have parted company.” So it was true. Chen was astonished. As far as he knew, Yu Si could not do without A’qi for a single day. Why should they have parted? Had she behaved so badly that he could not tolerate her and had driven her out? “You’re surprised, aren’t you?” she said calmly. “Let me put your mind at rest. Old Yu and I parted on good terms.” This was more incomprehensible still. “What happened? I find it rather hard to believe.” “Never mind you—I can hardly believe it myself. But it must have been fated that you and I should come to this. When good fortune arrives, even a city wall can’t keep it out.” Her cheerful certainty that everything would turn out as she wished both amused and irritated Chen. He wanted to cut her short, but that would start another quarrel, and he would never learn how she and Yu Si had separated. He listened patiently to the end, then urged, “Never mind all that. Just tell me why Uncle Four parted with you.”

“嗨！提起来，真是说书先生的口头禅：”六月里冻杀一只老绵羊，说来话长！‘“说到这里，阿七的神色忽显哀伤，”你晓不晓得，阿虎死掉了？“陈世龙大惊：“什么？阿虎死掉了，怎么死的？”“绞肠痧！可怜，八月十四下半天得的病，一夜工夫就‘翘’掉了，连个节都过不过！”陈世龙听得傻了，眼中慢慢流出两滴眼泪。郁四生一子一女，阿虎就是他的独子，今年才二十二岁，去年娶的亲。为人忠厚，极重义气，跟陈世龙也算是要好弟兄，尤其因为他父亲不准陈世龙上门，他似乎倒怀着歉意，所以对陈世龙格外另眼相看，三天两头不是来邀他听书、吃酒，就是来问问要不要铜钿用？这样一个好朋友，一别竟成永诀，陈世龙自然要伤心。但是，他的这两滴眼泪，在阿七看来，却别有会心，越觉得好事可成，因为这可以看出，陈世龙是有良心，重感情的。

“Ah! As the storytellers say, ‘An old ram froze to death in the sixth month—a long tale lies behind it!’” Her expression suddenly turned sorrowful. “Do you know that A’hu is dead?” Chen was horrified. “What? A’hu dead? How did he die?” “A sudden intestinal illness. Poor fellow! He fell ill on the afternoon of the fourteenth day of the eighth month and was gone overnight. He didn’t even live to see the festival.” Chen sat stunned, and slowly two tears rolled from his eyes. Yu Si had a daughter and one son. A’hu, his only boy, was just twenty-two and had married the year before. Honest, kind and deeply loyal to his friends, he and Chen had been close as brothers. Because his father forbade Chen to visit their house, A’hu had seemed almost apologetic and had treated him with particular consideration. Every few days he came to invite him to a storytelling performance or out for a drink, or simply to ask whether he needed spending money. To think that when they had last parted, he had been saying farewell for ever to such a friend! Naturally Chen grieved. A’qi, however, found another meaning in those two tears. They showed that he had a conscience and deep feelings, and made her all the more hopeful that her own wish would come true.

“你也不要难过。死了，死了，死啦就了掉了！”阿七停一下说，“我跟郁老头散伙，就是因为阿虎死了，才起的因头。阿虎不死，将来他老子的家当，归他独得，哪个也不能说话，阿虎一死，又没有留下一儿半女，你想想看，自然有人要动脑筋了。你晓得是哪个动脑筋？”陈世龙摇摇头，方在哀伤之际，懒得去想，也懒得说话。“一说破，你就不会奇怪了，是阿兰姐夫妇！”阿兰姐是郁四的大女儿，今年快三十了，是个极厉害的角色，年前，郁四跟他的同事，一个姓邢的刑房书办结了亲家。老书办是世袭的行当，老邢去世，小邢进衙门当差，比他老了干得还出色，又可知是如何厉害的角色呢？这对夫妇凑在一起，图谋回娘家来夺产，自是不足为奇之事。陈世龙因为跟阿虎的交情，此时便想到阿虎嫂的将来，不由得愤愤说道：“阿兰姐是嫁出去的人，她凭啥来动脑筋呢？”“就是这话罗！‘嫁出去的女儿，泼出去的水’，本来没啥脑筋好动，说来说去，是阿兰姐和她男人厉害，没事找事，脑筋动到了我头上。”“怎么呢？”陈世龙有些想不通，“跟你啥相干？”“怎么不相干？如果我替郁老头养个儿子，他们还有啥脑筋好动，所以把我看成眼中钉。你懂了吧？”“懂是懂了！”陈世龙摇摇头，“我就不懂郁四叔，怎么肯放你走？”

“Don’t take it so hard. Dead is dead, and once you’re dead, it’s all over.” A’qi paused. “It was A’hu’s death that led to my parting with Old Yu. If A’hu had lived, he alone would have inherited everything, and nobody could have said a word. But he died without leaving a child. Think of it! Naturally somebody began to scheme. Do you know who?” Chen shook his head. In his grief he had no wish either to think or to talk. “Once I tell you, you won’t be surprised. Sister A’lan and her husband!” A’lan was Yu Si’s eldest daughter, nearly thirty and a formidable woman. Earlier, Yu Si had married her to the son of a colleague named Xing, a clerk in the criminal office. The old clerks passed their occupation from father to son. When Old Xing died, Young Xing entered the yamen and proved still more capable than his father. One could imagine what a formidable character he was too. It was hardly surprising that this pair should scheme to seize the property of her family. Remembering his friendship with A’hu, Chen began to worry about the young widow’s future. “Sister A’lan has married out of the family,” he said indignantly. “What right has she to scheme for it?” “Exactly! ‘A married daughter is like water poured away.’ There was nothing for her to get. But she and her husband are clever. They invented a problem and turned their schemes on me.” “How?” Chen could not understand. “What had it to do with you?” “How could it have nothing to do with me? If I bore Old Yu a son, what could they hope to get? That made me a thorn in their eyes. Do you see?” “I see that,” Chen said, shaking his head. “What I don’t see is why Uncle Four was willing to let you go.”

“哼！”阿七冷笑道，“你当郁老头是什么有良心的人？年纪一大把，‘色’得比哪个都厉害。你道他那宝贝女儿怎么跟他说？”“我想不出。总归是郁四叔听得进去的话。”“自然罗！说给他另外买人，又年轻、又漂亮，老色鬼还有啥听不进去。”照阿七打听来的消息是如此：阿兰姐劝她父亲，说阿七过了两三年，没有喜信，就不会有喜信了，风尘出身的，“凉药”吃得多，根本不能生育。没有儿子，只能在族中替阿虎嫂过继一个，偌大家产，将来白白便宜了别人。最好的办法，莫如买两个宜男之相的年轻女人做侍妾，必有得子之望。讲到这里，陈世龙插了一句嘴：“什么，还要买两个？”“是啊，怕一个不保险，多弄一个。”阿七用讥嘲的口风说：“有这样孝顺的女儿，做老子的，当然艳福不浅！”“我懂了。买这两个人，一定归阿兰姐经手，他们夫妇就从这上头一步一步踏进来，把持一切。不过，”陈世龙说，“又何必把你看成眼中钉？”“他们怕我坏她的事。在郁老头面前说，我会吃醋，搅得家宅不安。最最气不过的是，”阿七咬牙切齿地说，“自己做贼，赖人做贼，说我一定会勾引了外面的野汉子，来谋他郁家的财产，小和尚你想想，这种女人，心毒不毒？”

“Hmph!” A’qi sneered. “Do you imagine Old Yu has a conscience? At his age he’s more lustful than anyone. Do you know what his precious daughter told him?” “I can’t imagine. Something he wanted to hear, no doubt.” “Of course! She would buy him other women, young and pretty. What was there in that for the lecherous old man to object to?” According to what A’qi had learned, A’lan had told her father that, since A’qi had failed to become pregnant in two or three years, she never would. Women from the pleasure quarters took so much ‘cooling medicine’ that they could not bear children. Without a son, they would have to adopt a boy from the clan as an heir for A’hu’s widow, and all that property would eventually enrich someone else. The best course was to buy two young concubines whose appearance promised sons. Then there would surely be hope of an heir. At this point Chen interrupted. “What? Two of them?” “Yes. One might not be enough, so he must have another for insurance.” Her voice was mocking. “With such a dutiful daughter, a father can certainly expect plenty of female favours!” “I understand. Sister A’lan would choose and buy them, of course. From there, she and her husband could move in step by step and take control of everything. But why did they have to make an enemy of you?” “They were afraid I’d spoil their plans. They told Old Yu I’d be jealous and turn the household upside down. And the most infuriating thing of all,” she added through clenched teeth, “was that the thieves called someone else a thief! They said I’d be sure to seduce some lover outside and bring him in to steal the Yu family fortune. Little Monk, tell me—could any woman be more vicious?”

话说到这里，全盘情况，皆已了解，郁四听了女儿的话，决定跟阿七散伙。既说“好来好散”自然有一笔钱可拿，照郁四的手面，这笔钱还不会少，没有五千，也有三千。只不知道阿七自郁家下堂以后，是不是重张艳帜？不过，他心里虽然存疑，而且好奇心驱使，得问个明白，却终于不曾开口，因为他要表示出事不干已，不闻不问的态度，好让阿七自己识趣，知难而退。阿七却决不会如他的愿，“现在谈到正事上头来了。”她说：“小和尚，我随郁老头唱了半出‘乌龙院’，他走他的清秋大路，我也没有什么麻烦好找他的。走的时候，总算客客气气，房子是他买的，早已过户到我名下，所以该他搬出，另外给了我一个他钱庄里的折子，数目是五千两，只能取息，不能动本，这以后再说了，是我名下的铜钿，我当然要提出来。他识相的，拉倒，不识相我要打官司，好在王大老爷跟胡老板是好朋友……““慢慢！”陈世龙当头泼她的冷水：“你不要做梦！人家胡老板跟郁四叔等于弟兄一样，打到官司，一定帮他不帮你！”“那就不要他帮！”阿七答得极爽利，“我自己到堂上去告，说他那爿钱庄要‘倒灶’了，我不相信他，可以不可以？”陈世龙为她那种自说自话的神态逗得笑了，“都随你！”他说，“你跟阿兰姐一样，都算是厉害角色！”

The whole picture was now clear. Yu Si had listened to his daughter and decided to part with A’qi. Since they had parted on good terms, she must have received a settlement, and, given his generous habits, it could hardly have been small: if not five thousand taels, then at least three thousand. Chen wondered whether, after leaving Yu, she intended to return to her former profession. Curious as he was, he refrained from asking. He wanted to appear wholly unconcerned, neither asking nor listening, so that she would take the hint and withdraw. But A’qi had no intention of obliging him. “Now we come to the real business,” she said. “Little Monk, Old Yu and I have acted out half of The Black Dragon Courtyard. He can go his own open road, and I have no quarrel to pursue with him. We parted quite civilly. He bought the house, but he had already transferred it to my name, so he was the one who had to move out. He also gave me a passbook for five thousand taels at his bank. I can draw the interest, but not the principal. We’ll see about that later. It’s money in my name, and naturally I mean to withdraw it. If he sees sense, well and good. If not, I’ll sue. Luckily, Prefect Wang and Mr Hu are close friends...” “Wait!” Chen threw cold water over this at once. “Don’t dream of it! Mr Hu and Uncle Four are practically brothers. If you sue, he’ll help him, not you.” “Then I don’t need his help!” she answered readily. “I’ll go into court myself and say his bank is about to fail and I don’t trust him. Can I withdraw it then or can’t I?” Her confidence that everything would happen just as she wished made Chen laugh. “Have it your own way! You and Sister A’lan are both formidable women.”

“我啥厉害？做人全靠心好！象阿兰姐，哼，也是到现在没有儿子，将来有苦头吃。这都不去说它了。”话到此处，阿七的神情变得郑重而兴奋，“小和尚，从我跟郁老头分手，就有好些上门来打我的主意，都叫我回绝掉了，不识相的，我就爽爽快快的把他骂了出去。我平日都不出门，出门就是去打听你的消息。我一直在守你，今天总算守到了。你先搬到我那里去住，有话我们慢慢再说，”长篇大套，自说自话完了，一只手就搭了过来，按在陈世龙肩膀上，同时一双俏伶伶的眼睛瞟着，是恨不得弄碗水来，把他一口吞了下去的神气。陈世龙并不觉得好笑，是着急，没有想到她一厢情愿到痴的程度！照此看来，只怕她跟郁四过了两三年日子，心里是对他想了两三年，牵丝攀藤这么多日子下来，要想好好摆脱是无论如何办不到的事。那么怎么办呢？“说嘛！”她又催促，“啥辰光搬？我那里通通现成，不象你这里，一早起来，要茶要水，什么都没。洗个脸都要到茶店里去。这种光棍打流的日子，你自己想想看，苦不苦？”不对了！就这片刻工夫，又是结结实实的一根藤缠了上来，这样下去，非让她捆得动弹不得不可。陈世龙心想，只有快刀一挥，才能斩断纠葛，这在她自己受不了，但为了自保，不能不下辣手。“阿七！我骗你我天诛地灭！”他先罚个咒，让她知道决非设词推托：“小和尚老早有小厄姑了！”

“What’s formidable about me? All that matters is having a good heart. As for A’lan—hmph! She has no son herself yet. She’ll have trouble enough in years to come. But never mind all that.” A’qi grew earnest and excited. “Little Monk, since Old Yu and I parted, plenty of men have come with designs on me. I turned them all away. If they wouldn’t take the hint, I simply cursed them out of the house. I hardly ever go out. When I do, it’s to ask for news of you. I’ve been waiting and waiting, and today I’ve finally found you. Move in with me first. We can talk everything over at leisure.” Having settled the matter to her own satisfaction in this long speech, she reached out and laid a hand on Chen’s shoulder. Her lively eyes played over him, as though she longed for a bowl of water to wash him down whole. Chen found nothing amusing in this. He was alarmed. He had never imagined her wishful thinking could reach such a pitch of obsession. Perhaps throughout her two or three years with Yu Si she had been thinking of Chen all the time. If she had been winding these tendrils around him for so long, there could be no gentle way to break free. What was he to do? “Tell me!” she urged. “When will you move? Everything’s ready at my place. Not like here, where you get up in the morning and there’s neither tea nor water. You even have to go to a teashop to wash your face. Think what a miserable life it is, drifting about as a bachelor!” This would never do. In those few moments another stout vine had wrapped itself around him. At this rate she would bind him so tightly he could not move. Only one swift cut could sever the tangle. It would hurt her badly, but to save himself he must be ruthless. “A’qi, may Heaven destroy me if I’m lying!” He began with an oath, to show that this was no excuse. “Little Monk already has his Little Nun!”

阿七的脸色大变，眼猜倒还是水汪汪的，不过象含了两泡泪水，脸上一阵青、一阵白，摇摇头说：“我不相信！是哪个？”“张家的阿珠。”“哪个张家的阿珠？”“原来摇船，现在开大经丝行的……”“你在说啥！”阿七打断了他的话，显得十分困惑地，愣了好半天才说：“我还是不相信，摇船老张的女儿，不是胡老板的人吗？”“你完全弄错了！人家是把阿珠当女儿看，哪里有啥别的意思？”陈世龙又说，“就是这趟到上海，胡老板替我定下的亲事。聘礼都送过去，四样首饰，也是胡老板买的。总在今年年底，就要请大家吃喜酒。”言之凿凿，不象撒谎，把阿七听得目瞪口呆，背脊上一阵阵发凉，颓然坐倒，只是喃喃地说，“有这种事情？想都想不到的！”“就是罗！”陈世龙此时如释重负，“就象你跟郁四叔散伙一样，也是想都想不到的。”“不过……”阿七霍地站了起来，仿佛犹不死心，最后还想跟阿珠争夺一番似地，但是力不从心，终于气馁。

A’qi’s face changed completely. Her eyes were still liquid, but now they seemed full of tears. Her face turned pale and ashen by turns. She shook her head. “I don’t believe it. Who?” “A’zhu, from the Zhang family.” “Which Zhang family?” “The one who used to work a boat and now runs the Dajing Silk Firm...” “What are you saying?” She broke in, utterly bewildered, and stared for a long moment before continuing. “I still don’t believe it. Isn’t the old boatman Zhang’s daughter Mr Hu’s woman?” “You’ve got it all wrong. He treats A’zhu as a daughter. He never meant anything else.” Chen went on, “He arranged the match for me on this trip to Shanghai. The betrothal gifts have already been sent—four kinds of jewellery, bought by Mr Hu himself. We’ll be inviting everyone to the wedding feast before the year is out.” He spoke with such assurance that it did not sound like a lie. A’qi was dumbfounded. Chills ran down her back, and she sank into a chair, muttering, “Such a thing! I could never have imagined it.” “Exactly.” Chen felt an enormous weight lift from him. “Just like you and Uncle Four parting. Nobody could have imagined that either.” “But...” A’qi suddenly rose, as though she still refused to admit defeat and meant to make one last attempt to wrest him from A’zhu. Yet she had no strength for it, and her courage failed.

“阿七！”陈世龙安慰她说，“人都是缘分。我们缘分不到，没有话说。你也不要难过，象你这样的人，不怕没人要。“他又说：”你的心好，好心自有好报。你请回去吧，我送你回去。“阿七象斗败了的公鸡似地，垂头不语，慢慢站起身来，脸上浑不似初来时那种芍药带露、艳光逼人的神采，气色灰暗，倒象一下子老了十年。陈世龙瞻念旧情，不能无动于衷，但怜念一生，马上又感到双肩都有沉重的压力，一只肩上是与阿珠偕老的盟约，想到在船上跪在她面前求婚所许下的诺言，一只肩膀上是胡雪岩的情分，想到他提携爱护，待自己嫡亲的子弟，亦不过如此，自己何能去找这种一沾上便摆不开的麻烦，以致耗神废业，辜负了他的期望？这样一转念，他的心肠便又硬了。对阿七的神情，视如不见，走出巷，招手喊过一顶小轿来，同时早就拈了块只多不少的碎银子在手里，等轿子抬到，他把碎银子递了过去，交代了阿七的住处，使往旁边一站，意思是等她上轿。“小和尚！”阿七这样喊了一声，欲言又止，只拿忧郁而惶惑的眼色看着他。“你回去吧！”陈世龙觉得要有句话，哪怕是敷衍的话，也得说一句，才能叫她上轿，因而顺口又说：“有空我来看你！”阿七点点头，脸上有着感激的意味，移步从放倒的轿杠上跨了进去，回身倒退着进轿时，又是深深地一瞥，为陈世龙留下来无数幽怨。

“A’qi,” Chen said soothingly, “everything depends on fate. We weren’t meant for each other, and there’s nothing to be done. Don’t be unhappy. A woman like you need never fear that nobody will want her. You have a good heart, and a good heart gets its reward. Go home now. I’ll see you off.” She hung her head like a defeated fighting cock and slowly rose without a word. None of the dazzling freshness of her arrival, like a peony wet with dew, remained. Her face was dull and grey; she seemed to have aged ten years in an instant. Remembering their old affection, Chen could not remain unmoved. But as soon as pity stirred, he felt a heavy weight on each shoulder. On one rested his pledge to grow old with A’zhu, the promises he had made as he knelt before her on the boat and asked her to marry him. On the other rested his debt to Hu Xueyan, who had encouraged and protected him as generously as if he had been his own flesh and blood. How could he seek out a trouble that, once entangled in it, he would never shake off, wasting his energies, neglecting his work and disappointing Hu’s hopes? At this thought his heart hardened again. Ignoring A’qi’s dejection, he went out of the alley and hailed a small sedan chair. He already had a piece of silver in his hand, more than enough for the fare. When the chair arrived, he gave it to the bearers, told them her address and stood aside, waiting for her to get in. “Little Monk!” she said, then stopped, looking at him in melancholy bewilderment. “Go home.” He felt that he must say something, however empty, to persuade her to enter, and added without thinking, “I’ll come and see you when I have time.” She nodded gratefully and stepped over the lowered poles. Turning to back into the chair, she gave him one last deep look, leaving him burdened with all her unspoken sorrow.

这时太阳已经很高了，十月小阳春，阳光明亮，照得人有些目，陈世龙觉得有些晕淘淘，信步踏进一爿小茶店，洗脸喝茶点心，静静坐了一会，脑子才算完全清醒。想想这天该做的事，第一件就是到阿虎灵前一拜，同时把胡雪岩的话交代了郁四。于是他取钱托茶博士办来一份素烛清香，往北门郁四的老家走了去。进门就淌眼泪，一路淌到灵前，焚烛上香，拜罢起身，只见阿兰头上簪一朵白花，手扶在一个小丫头的肩上，袅袅婷婷地走了出来。一见了面少不得又是“流泪眼观流眼泪”，阿兰姐一面抹眼泪，一面为陈世龙说阿虎得病的经过。接着又说她父亲晚年丧子，家门如何不幸，然后再谈阿七，指她不安于室，又说阿七日夜吵着要进郁家的门，不但进门，还要做阿虎嫂的婆婆，要给她磕头。“小和尚，你想想看！这是做不做得到的事情？”阿兰姐说，“明晓得做不列，天天又哭又闹，她打的是什么主意？还不是一想就明白！所以大家都劝爹，放她走路算了，这件事提来鸭屎臭，你见了我爹，不必说起。免得他老人家心里不舒服。”照她说来，是阿七不对。不过陈世龙也不尽相信她的话，只觉得事不关己。不必多问，所以点点头说：“我晓得了。四叔是不是在茶店里？”“是啊！”阿兰说，“你昨天叫人送了胡老板的礼来，他才晓得你回来了。一早就要到碧浪春去等你。你就到那里去看他吧！”

The sun was high by now. In this brief return of spring in the tenth month, its bright light was almost dazzling, and Chen felt rather dazed. He wandered into a little teashop, washed his face and had tea and something to eat. Only after sitting quietly for a while did his mind become fully clear. He considered the day’s business. First he must pay his respects before A’hu’s spirit tablet and deliver Hu’s message to Yu Si. He gave the tea attendant money to buy plain candles and incense, then walked to Yu Si’s family home near the north gate. Tears began to flow as he entered, and continued all the way to the mourning altar. He lit the candles and incense and bowed. As he rose, A’lan appeared, a white flower pinned in her hair, walking gracefully with one hand resting on a little maid’s shoulder. At their meeting, each looked through tears at the other’s tearful face. Wiping her eyes, A’lan told him how A’hu had fallen ill. She spoke of their family’s misfortune, and of her father losing his son in old age. Then she turned to A’qi, accusing her of being restless and unfaithful. A’qi, she said, had clamoured day and night to enter the Yu family household. Nor was that all: she wanted to become A’hu’s widow’s mother-in-law and have the young woman kowtow to her. “Little Monk, think! Was such a thing possible? She knew perfectly well it wasn’t, yet every day she cried and made scenes. What was she after? One moment’s thought makes it plain. We all urged Father to let her go. The whole affair stinks of duck dung. Don’t mention it when you see him. It will only upset him.” In her account, A’qi was to blame. Chen did not entirely believe her, but it was no affair of his and there was no need to ask further. He nodded. “I understand. Is Uncle Four at the teahouse?” “Yes. When you sent over Mr Hu’s gifts yesterday, he learned you were back. He went to Bilangchun early this morning to wait for you. Go and see him there.”

到了碧浪春，只见郁四仍旧坐在马头桌子上，人瘦了不少。陈世龙叫过一声：“四叔”，相顾黯然。“你昨天到的？”郁四有气没力地说。“是的。昨天下半天到的。”说了这一句话，陈世龙忽然转到一个念头，在“家门”里，他的“前人”跟郁四是“同参”，师父一死，郁四就算嫡亲的长辈，为了阿七不准自己上门，并不是不照应自己，起码胡雪岩这条路子就是从这位长辈身上来的，“家门”里讲究饮水思源，“引见”之恩不可忘。照此说来，昨天一到，应该先去看他，自己是走错了一步，尤其这天早晨，阿七又来密访，“光棍心多，麻布筋多”，如果郁四把这两件事摆在一起想一想，搞出什么误会来，那就“跳到黄河洗不清”了！所以正好趁此刻先作一个不着痕迹的解释。于是他说：“四叔！昨天一到，我就先要给你老人家来请安的，哪晓得一到了老丈人那里，硬给他们留住了。”这段话有两层用意，一是解释他所以昨天一到未去看郁四的原因，二是表示他已经定了亲，决不会再跟阿七搅七念三。然而郁四却有些莫名其妙，“你说啥？”他问“啥个老丈人？你几时定的亲，怎么我不晓得？”“湖州还没有人晓得，是这趟胡先生作主替我定下的。”“噢！”郁四显然自这喜讯中，受到了鼓舞，失神的双眼，有了闪闪的亮光，“好极！是哪一家的姑娘？”“这话说来很长，也很有趣，四叔万万想不到的。”陈世龙先宕开一句：“ 胡先生还有他自己的事情，要我跟四叔谈。”这话郁四明白，自然是头寸上的事，于是他站起身来说：“这里人来人往，静不下来。走，到聚成去！”

At Bilangchun, Yu Si sat at his usual horse-head table. He had grown much thinner. Chen greeted him as “Uncle Four,” and they looked at one another sadly. “You arrived yesterday?” Yu asked listlessly. “Yes. Yesterday afternoon.” As he said this, a thought struck Chen. Within their brotherhood, his initiating master had been Yu Si’s fellow initiate. Since the master’s death, Yu counted as his closest elder. Forbidding him to visit because of A’qi did not mean that Yu had ceased to look after him. At the very least, it was through this elder that he had come to know Hu Xueyan. In the brotherhood, a man must remember the spring from which he drank: the favour of an introduction must never be forgotten. He should therefore have called on Yu as soon as he arrived yesterday. That had been a mistake. Worse still, A’qi had paid him a secret visit this morning. ‘A crafty fellow has as many suspicions as hemp has fibres.’ If Yu put the two things together and misunderstood, Chen could never wash himself clean, even in the Yellow River. Here was his chance to explain without appearing to do so. “Uncle Four, the moment I arrived yesterday I meant to come and pay my respects. But once I reached my father-in-law’s, they insisted on keeping me.” This served two purposes: it explained his failure to visit the previous day, and announced that he was engaged and would no longer become entangled with A’qi. Yu, however, was bewildered. “What are you saying? What father-in-law? When did you get engaged? Why didn’t I know?” “Nobody in Huzhou knows yet. Mr Hu arranged it for me on this trip.” “Oh!” The good news visibly heartened Yu. His dull eyes began to shine. “Splendid! Whose daughter is she?” “It’s a long story, and an interesting one. You’d never guess, Uncle Four.” Chen put him off for the moment. “Mr Hu also has some business of his own for me to discuss with you.” Yu understood: naturally, it concerned funds. He stood up. “People are always coming and going here. There’s no peace. Come along to Jucheng.”
