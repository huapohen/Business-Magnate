湖州之行，三天之内，胡雪岩替自己办了两件要紧事。第一件是约妥了黄仪，随他到杭州去办笔墨。黄仪改变了心意，一则想到外面去闯闯，二则是觉得跟了胡雪岩这样的东家，十分够味，当然也知道这位东家不会薄待，所以薪水酬劳等等，根本不谈。第二件是进一步赢得了郁四的友谊。郁四自从跟阿七言归于好，他的颓唐老态，一扫而空，不再谈衙门里辞差的话，家务也不劳胡雪岩再费心，表示自己可以打点精神来料理。胡雪岩要头寸周转，除了已经拨付的那一笔以外，另外又调动了五万两银子，让他带走。“你的事就是我的事。为你这样的朋友，倾家荡产也值得。况且，我相信你一定有办法。”他这样对胡雪岩说：“你要头寸，只要早点告诉我，我一定替你调齐。”有了郁四的十万银子和他的那句话，胡雪岩又是雄心万丈了。他目前最困难的，就是头寸，在上海堆栈里的丝，搁煞了他的大部分本钱，阜康钱庄的生意，做得极其热闹，已成“大同行”中的“金字招牌”之一，但唯其如此，决不能露丝毫捉襟见时的窘态，而海运局方面，正当新旧交替之际，亏空只能补，不能拉。在这青黄不接的当口，萌雪岩一度想把那批丝，杀价卖掉，虽仍有盈余，但已有限，费心费力的结果，变成几乎白忙一场，自是于心不甘，同时也不肯错过这个机会。左右为难之下，有郁四的这一臂之力，帮忙帮得大了。

During three days in Huzhou, Hu Xueyan accomplished two important things for himself. First, he secured Huang Yi’s agreement to accompany him to Hangzhou as his secretary. Huang had changed his mind partly because he wanted to try his fortune elsewhere, and partly because serving an employer like Hu promised to be thoroughly satisfying. He also knew that Hu would not treat him poorly, so salary and remuneration were never even discussed. Second, Hu strengthened his friendship with Yu Si. Since making peace with A’qi, Yu had lost all his dejection and signs of age. He no longer spoke of resigning his post at the yamen and told Hu not to trouble himself further about his family affairs: he could now summon the energy to manage them himself. As Hu needed working funds, Yu arranged another fifty thousand taels for him to take away, in addition to the sum already transferred. “Your business is mine. For a friend like you, even losing my entire fortune would be worthwhile. Besides, I am sure you will find a way. Whenever you need funds, give me a little notice, and I will have them ready.” With Yu’s hundred thousand taels and that assurance, Hu’s ambitions soared again. His greatest difficulty was liquidity. Most of his capital was tied up in the silk stored in Shanghai. Fukang was flourishing and had become one of the most respected names among the leading banks; for that very reason, it could not afford the slightest appearance of financial strain. Meanwhile, the Sea Transport Bureau was changing hands: its deficit must be reduced, not increased. In this interval before fresh funds became available, Hu had considered selling the silk at a heavy discount. There would still be a profit, but a small one, making all his effort almost unrewarded. He could not reconcile himself to that, nor did he wish to abandon the opportunity. Caught between these difficulties, he found Yu’s help invaluable.

“四哥！”他兴奋地说，“只要你相信我，我包你这笔款子的利息，比放给哪个都来得划算。我已经看准了，这十万银子，我还要‘扑’到洋庄上去。前两天我在杭州得到消息，两江总督怡大人，要对洋人不客气了，这是个难得的机会，一抓住必发大财。不过，机会来了，别人不晓得，我晓得，别人看不准，我看得准。这就是人家做生意，做不过我的地方。“说了半天是什么机会呢？两江总督怡良，郁四倒是晓得的，他是当权的恭亲王的老丈人，也算是皇亲国戚，如果有什么大举措，朝廷一定会支持他，然而对洋人是如何不客气？“莫非，”他迟疑地问，“又要跟洋人开仗？”“那是不会的……”胡雪岩说，他听到的消息是，因为两件事，两江总督怡良对洋人深为不满，第一，小刀会的刘丽川，有洋人自租界接济军火粮食，这是“助逆”而不是“助顺”，就算实际上对刘丽川没有什么帮助，朝廷亦难容忍，而况对刘丽川确为一大助力。第二是从上海失守以后，“夷税”也就是按值百抽五计算的关税，洋人借口虞乱影响，商务停顿，至今不肯缴纳。商务受影响自是难免，如说完全停顿，则是欺人之谈。洋商缴纳关税，全靠各国领事代为约束，现在有意不缴，无奈其何！那就只有一个办法：不跟洋人做生意。

“Brother Yu,” he said excitedly, “if you trust me, I guarantee that the return on this money will be better than you could obtain from lending it to anyone else. I have made up my mind to commit these hundred thousand taels to the foreign trade too. A few days ago in Hangzhou, I learned that Governor-General Yiliang intends to take a hard line with the foreigners. It is a rare opportunity, and whoever seizes it will make a fortune. When an opportunity comes, others do not know of it, but I do; others cannot judge it clearly, but I can. That is why they cannot match me in business.” What opportunity did he mean? Yu knew who Yiliang was: the father-in-law of the influential Prince Gong, and therefore a relative of the imperial house. If he undertook any major action, the court would certainly support him. But what did taking a hard line with the foreigners mean? “Surely,” Yu asked hesitantly, “we are not going to war with them again?” “No, not that.” Hu explained that, according to his information, Yiliang was deeply displeased with the foreigners for two reasons. First, foreigners were sending arms and provisions from the settlement to Liu Lichuan of the Small Swords Society. This was supporting rebellion instead of lawful authority. The court would scarcely tolerate it even if the supplies did Liu little practical good; in fact, they were a considerable help. Second, since Shanghai had fallen, the foreigners had refused to pay the so-called foreign tax, the customs duty levied at five percent of value. They claimed that the disturbances had brought commerce to a halt. Trade was inevitably affected, but to say it had stopped entirely was deception. Payment of customs duties by foreign merchants depended on their consuls enforcing the obligation. If they deliberately refused, what could be done? There was only one answer: cease trading with them.

“租界上的事，官府管不到，再说不跟洋商做生意，难道把销洋庄的货色，抛到黄浦江里？这自然是办不到的，所以，再退一步说，只有一个办法，这个办法也很厉害，内地的丝茶两项，不准运入租界。这是官府办得到的事。”“我懂了！还是你原来的办法，”郁四点点头说，“那样子一来，丝茶两项存货的行情，一定大涨。这倒是好主意！”“自然是好生意。”胡雪岩说，“丝我有了，而且现在也不是时候，收不到货，茶叶上面，大有脑筋可动，官府做事慢，趁告示没有出来以前，我还来得及办货。此外，我还想开一爿当铺，开一家药店，阜康也想在上海设分号……”“老胡，”郁四打断他的话，“我说一句，怕不中听，不过我声明在先，决不是我有啥别的心思，无非提醒你，事情还是你去做，你说怎么办就怎么办。”“四哥，我们的交情，你这番表白是多余的。”话虽多余，不能不先交代，这就是江湖上的“过节”，其实就是郁四以下要说的话，也近乎多余，他劝胡雪岩说，一个人本事再大，精力有限，头绪太多，必有照顾不到的地方。而且他的生意，互相关联，牵一发而动全身，一垮下来，不可收拾。不如暂时收敛，稳扎稳打。这番话语重心长，见得郁四的关切，但胡雪岩自己何尝不知道？其间的利害关系，他远比郁四了解得更透彻，不过他自己足以应付得了，哪一处出了毛病，该如何急救？也曾细细策划过，有恃无恐，所以我行我素。只是郁四说到这样的话，休戚相关，虽不能听，亦不宜辩，因而不断点头，表示接受。

“The authorities cannot control affairs inside the settlement. And even if we say we will stop trading with foreign merchants, are we to throw all our export goods into the Huangpu River? Obviously not. So there is only one practicable step left, and it is a powerful one: prohibit silk and tea from the interior from entering the settlement. That is something the authorities can enforce.” “I see!” Yu nodded. “Your original plan again. The price of silk and tea already held there would certainly soar. A fine idea!” “A fine business, certainly. I already have silk, and this is not the season to buy more. But there is plenty to be done with tea. Officials move slowly. Before the proclamation appears, I still have time to acquire stock. Besides, I want to open a pawnshop and a pharmacy, and establish a Shanghai branch of Fukang.” “Old Hu,” Yu interrupted, “I want to say something you may not like. But let me make it clear first: I have no ulterior motive. It is only a reminder. You will still conduct the business, and whatever you decide will be done.” “With our friendship, Brother Yu, such an explanation is unnecessary.” Unnecessary though it was, it had to be made. This was one of the formal observances of the brotherhood world. Indeed, what Yu went on to say was almost unnecessary too. However capable a man might be, his energy was limited. With too many undertakings, something would escape his attention. Moreover, Hu’s businesses were interconnected: pull one thread and the whole fabric moved. A collapse could become irretrievable. Better to restrain himself for the present and advance steadily. The earnest advice showed Yu’s concern. But how could Hu be unaware of these dangers? He understood the interests involved far better than Yu did. He believed he could manage them and had carefully planned emergency measures for trouble in each quarter. Confident of his resources, he intended to follow his own course. Yet when a friend whose fortunes were bound to his offered such advice, he ought not argue even if he could not follow it. He therefore nodded repeatedly, as though accepting it.

接受不是一句空话可以敷衍的，而郁四有大批本钱投在自己名下，也得替他顾虑。胡雪岩的思考向来宽阔而周密，心里在想郁四的话，可有言外之意？却是不能不问清楚的。“四哥，你的话十分实在。当铺、药店，我决定死了心，暂且丢下。不过，我要请问一句，四哥一定要跟我说实话。”“你这话也是多余的。”郁四答道，“我几时跟你说过假话？”“是的，是的，我晓得。”胡雪岩连连点头，“不过，我怕我或者有啥看不列的地方，要请四哥指点。你看，我们在上海的那批丝，是不是现在脱手比较好？““嗐！”郁四的神色和声音，大似遗憾，“你完全弄错我的意思了！你当我不放心我投在你那里的本钱，决不是！我早就说过了，我相信你，生意你去做，我不过问。”

Acceptance could not be reduced to an empty assurance. Yu had invested a large amount with him, and Hu had to consider his interests. Hu’s thoughts were always wide-ranging and thorough. Was there something behind Yu’s advice? He must find out. “Brother Yu, what you say is entirely sound. I have decided to put the pawnshop and pharmacy aside for now. But there is something I must ask, and you must answer frankly.” “That remark is unnecessary too. When have I ever lied to you?” “Of course, of course. I know.” Hu nodded repeatedly. “But I may have overlooked something and need your guidance. Do you think we should sell our Shanghai silk now?” “Ah!” Yu’s expression and voice showed deep regret. “You have completely misunderstood me. You think I am worried about the capital I have entrusted to you. Not at all! I have told you before: I trust you. You conduct the business; I do not interfere.”

“四哥是相信我，结果弄得‘鸭屎臭’，叫我怎么对四哥交代？”“不要交代！要啥交代？做生意有亏有蚀，没话可说！只有‘开口自己人，独吃自己人’的才是‘鸭屎臭’，你不是那种人。再说一句，就算你要存心吃我，我也情愿，这话不是我现在说，你问阿七。”说着便连声喊着：“阿七，阿七！胡老板有话问你。”阿七在打点送胡雪岩的土仪，正忙得不可开支，但听说是胡雪岩有话问，还是抽出身子来了。“我昨天晚上跟你谈到上海的那批丝，我是怎么跟你说的？”郁四问。“你说，那批丝上的本钱，你只当赌铜钿输掉了。赚了，你不结帐，蚀了，你也睡得着觉。”听这样一说，胡雪岩既感激，又不安，听郁四的口气，大有把那笔本钱奉送之意，这无论如何是受之有愧的。但此时无需急着表白，朋友相交不在一日，郁四果有此心，自己倒要争个面子，将来叫他大大地出个意外。于是他说：“四哥你这样说，我的胆就大了。人生难得遇着知己，趁这时候我不好好去闯一闯，也太对不起自己了。”

“You trust me, Brother Yu, but if the result is a foul mess, how could I answer to you?” “You needn’t answer for it! Business has losses as well as gains. There is nothing to complain of. The only foul men are those who call you one of their own and then swallow all the profit themselves. You are not such a man. I’ll say more: even if you deliberately took advantage of me, I would accept it. I am not saying this only now. Ask A’qi.” He called repeatedly, “A’qi! A’qi! Boss Hu has something to ask you.” A’qi was extremely busy preparing local gifts for Hu to take away, but at the mention of his request she came out. “What did I tell you last night about the silk in Shanghai?” Yu asked. “You said you treated the capital in it as money lost at gambling. If it made a profit, you would not call for a settlement; if it made a loss, you would still sleep soundly.” Hu felt both grateful and uneasy. Yu almost seemed to intend the capital as a gift, which Hu could not accept with an easy conscience. But there was no need to protest at once. Friendship was not measured in a single day. If that was indeed Yu’s intention, Hu would make a special effort and give him a great surprise in the future. “Brother Yu, that gives me courage,” he said. “It is rare to find a friend who understands one. If I don’t venture out while I have the chance, I shall scarcely be doing myself justice.”

在这一刻，胡雪岩又有了新的主意，但决定等那批丝脱手以后，把郁四名下应得的一份，替他在上海买租界上的地皮。这是一个突如其来的念头，细细想去，第一，不受炮火的影响，各地逃难到上海租界的人，一定会越来越多，市面当然要兴旺，第二，朝廷对洋人不欢迎，但既然订了商约，洋人要来，不欢迎也办不到。“五口通商”只有上海这个码头最热闹，一旦洪杨战败，逃难的人会相携还乡，但做生意的人，是不会走的。所以，趁现在把上海租界里那些无甚入息，地价便宜的苇塘空地买下来，将来一定会大发其财。不过，这是五年、十年以后，如果有闲钱无甚用处，不妨买了摆在那里，象自己现在这样，急需头寸周转，就不必去打这个主意。“老胡！”郁四见他沉吟不语，便即问道：“你在想啥？”“还不是动生意上的脑筋。”说了这一句，胡雪岩才想起郁四劝他的话，自然不宜再出花样，因而自己摇着手说：“不谈，不谈。是空想！”“不要去多想了！我们吃酒，谈点有趣的事。”

At that moment Hu had a new idea. Once the silk was sold, he would use Yu’s rightful share to buy land for him in the Shanghai settlement. The thought came suddenly, but it bore examination. First, the settlement was protected from bombardment. More and more people would flee there from troubled districts, and its commerce would flourish. Second, the court might dislike foreigners, but having signed commercial treaties, it could not prevent them from coming. Of the five treaty ports, Shanghai was the busiest. Once Hong and Yang were defeated, refugees would return home together, but businessmen would remain. Buying cheap, unproductive reed beds and vacant land in the settlement now would therefore bring a great fortune later. That, however, lay five or ten years ahead. A man with idle money could buy land and leave it. A man like Hu, urgently needing working funds, ought not to consider it for himself. “Old Hu,” Yu asked, noticing his silence, “what are you thinking about?” “Business again.” Then Hu remembered Yu’s warning. He should not propose another venture now. Waving his hand, he added, “Never mind. It is only a fancy.” “Stop thinking so much. Let’s have a drink and talk of something amusing.”

趣事甚多，胡雪岩讲了七姑奶奶逛堂子的笑话，把阿七听得出了神。郁四也觉得新奇，表示很想会一会这样一个“奇女子”。“那容易得很！”胡雪岩说，“只要你抽得出空，我陪你走一遭，尤家兄妹一定也会觉得你很对劲。”“真的，”阿七接口向郁四说，“你也该到外头走走，见见世面。年纪一大把，乐得看开些，吃吃喝喝，四处八方去逛逛，让我也开开眼界。”这番怂恿把郁四说动了心，平生足迹不出里门，外面是怎么样的一个花花世界，只听人说，未曾目睹，到底是桩憾事，如果能带着阿七去走一走，会一会江湖上的朋友，也是暮年一大乐事。只是怎么能抽得出身。因此，他又想到衙门里的差使，要找个替手这件大事，“老胡，”他毫不考虑地问了出来，“上次我跟你谈过的，想叫小和尚来当差，你可曾问过他？”“还不曾问。”胡雪岩心想，陈世龙大概不会愿意，而且有阿七在，陈世龙也实在不宜过分接近郁家，再为自己打算，也难放手，所以索性再加一句：“我想不问也罢。我看他十之八九不肯！”“那就算了。”郁四偶惘地说，“我另外物色。”

There was plenty to amuse them. Hu told the story of the Seventh Lady’s visit to a courtesan house, and A’qi listened spellbound. Yu too found it remarkable and said he would like to meet such an extraordinary woman. “Easily arranged,” Hu replied. “If you can find the time, I’ll go with you. Both Brother You and his sister would certainly take to you.” “Indeed,” A’qi urged Yu, “you ought to go out and see something of the world. At your age, why not take things easily, enjoy good food and drink, and travel about? Let me broaden my horizons too.” This tempted Yu. He had never traveled beyond his home district. All he knew of the colorful world outside came from other people’s stories, which was a real regret. Traveling with A’qi and meeting friends from the brotherhood would be a great pleasure in his later years. But how could he free himself? He thought again of finding a replacement for his work at the yamen. “Old Hu,” he asked without hesitation, “did you ask Little Monk about taking the post I mentioned last time?” “Not yet.” Hu thought Chen would probably be unwilling. Moreover, with A’qi there, it would be unwise for Chen to become too closely involved with the Yu household. Hu’s own affairs would also make it hard to spare him. He therefore added, “I think there is no need to ask. I am almost certain he would refuse.” “Then let it drop,” Yu said disconsolately. “I’ll look for someone else.”

这两句对答，使得阿七深为注意，在过去，如果谈到陈世龙，她立刻会插嘴来问，但自从有了那两番私晤，倾诉心曲的经历，变得“做贼心虚”，在郁四面前，处处要避嫌疑，所以当时不敢搭腔，过后才找个机会，悄悄问胡雪岩是怎么回事？胡雪岩也正要这样一个单独相处的机会，好问她一个明白，因而说明其事以后，紧接着便是这样一句：“郁四嫂，我有句话，不晓得能不能问？问了伯你不高兴，不问，我心里总不安稳。真正不知道该怎么办？”阿七是很聪明、也很爽荡的人，微微红着脸说：“我晓得你要问的是啥？那件事我做错了。不过当时并不晓得做错。““这话怎么说？”胡雪岩觉得她的话，很有意味，“是你跟郁四哥讲和以后，才晓得自己错了？”“是的！”阿七羞涩地一笑，别具妩媚之姿，“想想还是老头子好，样样依我，换了别人，要我样样依他，这在我，也是办不到的。”

A’qi listened closely to this exchange. In the past, she would immediately have asked questions whenever Chen was mentioned. But since their two private meetings, when she had poured out her feelings, she had developed a guilty conscience and took care to avoid suspicion in Yu’s presence. She dared not join in then. Only later did she find an opportunity to ask Hu privately what it was about. Hu had wanted just such a chance to question her. After explaining, he went straight on: “Sister-in-Law Yu, there is something I do not know whether I may ask. If I ask, I fear you will be displeased; if I don’t, I cannot feel at ease. I really do not know what to do.” A’qi was intelligent and candid. Blushing slightly, she said, “I know what you want to ask. I was wrong about that. But at the time I did not know it was wrong.” “What do you mean?” Hu found this interesting. “Did you realize you were wrong only after making peace with Brother Yu?” “Yes.” She gave a shy, charming smile. “When I think it over, the old man is best after all. He gives way to me in everything. With someone else, I would have to give way to him in everything, and I could never do that.”

胡雪岩觉得以她的脾气和出身，还有句话提出来也不算太唐突，所以接着又问：“那么你去看世龙之前，是怎么个想法？”一听这话，阿七有些紧张：“小和尚把我的话，都告诉你了？”这下胡雪岩倒要考虑了，看阿七的神气，是不愿意让第三者晓得她的秘密，如果为了叫她心里好过，大可否认。只是这一来，就不会了解她对陈世龙到底是怎么一种感情？想一想，还是要说实话。于是他点一点头，清清楚楚地答道：“源源本本地告诉我了。”阿七大为忸怩，“这个死东西！”她不满地骂，“跟他闹着玩的，他竟当真的了！真不要脸！”这是掩饰之词，胡雪岩打破沙锅问到底，又刺她一句：“你说闹着玩，也闹得太厉害了，居然还寻上门去，如果让阿珠晓得了，吃起醋来，你岂不是造孽？”“那也要怪他自己不好。”阿七不肯承认自己的错处，“无论如何香火之情总有的。那时候我心里一天到晚发慌，静不下来，只望有个人陪我谈谈。他连这一点都不肯，我气不过，特为跟他罗嗦，叫他的日子也不好过！“说着，她得意地笑了。这翻话照胡雪岩的判断，有十分之七可靠，不可靠的是她始终不承认对陈世龙动过心！然而事过境迁可以不去管它，只谈以后好了。“以后呢？”他问，“你怎么样看待陈世龙？”“有啥怎么样？”阿七说得很坦率，“我死心塌地跟了老头子，他也要讨亲了，还有啥话说？”于是胡雪岩也没话说了，神色轻松，大可放心。

Given her temperament and background, Hu felt that another question would not be too presumptuous. “Then what were you thinking before you went to see Shilong?” A’qi grew tense. “Did Little Monk tell you everything I said?” Now Hu had to consider. Her expression showed that she did not want a third person to know her secret. To spare her feelings, he could deny it. But then he would never discover exactly what she felt for Chen. After reflecting, he decided to tell the truth. He nodded and said clearly, “Everything, from beginning to end.” She was deeply embarrassed. “The wretch!” she complained. “I was only teasing him, and he took it seriously. How shameless!” It was an excuse. Determined to get to the bottom of it, Hu pressed her again. “Even if you were teasing, you went very far—actually calling on him! If A’zhu had found out and become jealous, wouldn’t you have done harm?” “Then he would have been partly to blame himself.” A’qi would not admit fault. “Whatever else, there was still a bond between us. At that time I was anxious all day and could not settle down. All I wanted was someone to talk to. He wouldn’t grant me even that. I was angry, so I deliberately bothered him to make his life uncomfortable too.” She smiled with satisfaction. Hu judged her account about seven-tenths credible. The doubtful part was her continued refusal to admit having felt an attraction to Chen. But circumstances had changed; there was no need to pursue the past. They need only discuss the future. “What about now?” he asked. “How do you regard Shilong?” “What is there to regard?” she answered frankly. “I have settled my heart on the old man, and Shilong is going to marry. What more is there to say?” Hu had nothing further to ask. His expression relaxed; he could be at ease.

“胡老板，”阿七出了难题给他来回答，“张家阿珠这样的人品，你怎么舍得放手？”“这话，”胡雪岩想了想答道，“说来你不会相信，只当我卖膏药、说大话。不过我自己晓得，我做这件事就象我劝郁四哥把你接回来一样，是蛮得意的。““得意点啥？”阿七有意报复，“刚开的一朵鲜花，便宜了小和尚。你倒不懊悔！”“要说懊悔，”胡雪岩也有意跟她开玩笑，“我懊悔不该劝郁四哥把你接回来，我自己要了你好了，大不了象黄仪一样，至多讨一场没趣。”阿七笑了，“好样不学，学他！”接着，神色一正，“胡老板，我规规矩矩问你一句话。”“好！我规规矩矩听。”“你太太凶不凶？”“你问她作啥？”胡雪岩笑道：“是不是要替我做媒？”“对！不然何必问？”

“Boss Hu,” A’qi said, posing a difficult question in return, “how could you let a girl like the Zhang family’s A’zhu go?” Hu considered. “If I explain, you won’t believe me. You will think I am a quack selling his remedies and boasting. But I know for myself that I am as pleased with arranging that affair as I am with persuading Brother Yu to bring you back.” “What is there to be pleased about?” she asked, deliberately retaliating. “A flower just coming into bloom, and Little Monk gets the benefit. Aren’t you sorry?” “If I have any regrets,” Hu replied, teasing in turn, “it is that I persuaded Brother Yu to take you back. I ought to have tried for you myself. At worst I would have been rebuffed like Huang Yi.” A’qi laughed. “Of all the examples you might follow, you choose his!” Then she grew serious. “Boss Hu, let me ask you a proper question.” “Very well. I shall listen properly.” “Is your wife fierce?” “Why do you ask?” Hu laughed. “Are you going to find me a match?” “Exactly. Why else would I ask?”

“那么，你打说来听听，是怎么样一个人？”“人是比我胜过十倍，不过命也比我苦。”阿七说道，“是个小孤孀。”接着，阿七便夸赞这个“小孤孀”的品貌，胡雪岩被她说得心思有些活动了，试探着问道：“她家里怎么样？守不住改嫁，夫家娘家都要答应，麻烦很多。”“麻烦是有一点，不过也没有料理不好的。”阿七说道，“她夫家没有人。倒是娘家，有个不成材的叔叔，还有个小兄弟，如果娶了她，这个小兄弟要带在身边。”“那倒也无所谓。”胡雪岩沉吟着，好半天不作声。“胡老板，”阿七怂恿着说，“你湖州也常要来的，有个门口在这里，一切方便，而且，说人品真正是又漂亮、又贤惠！要不要看看？”“那好啊！怎么个看法，总不是媒婆领了来吧？”“当然不能这么青。”阿七想了想说，“这样吧，明天一早我邀她到北门天圣寺烧香，你在那里等，见了装作不认识我，不要打招呼。我也不跟她说破，这样子没有顾忌，你就看得清楚了。”“也好！准定这么办。”

“Then tell me about her. What sort of person is she?” “Ten times better than I am, though her life has been harder. She is a young widow.” A’qi went on to praise the widow’s beauty and character. Hu began to feel interested and asked cautiously, “What is her family situation? If she chooses to remarry instead of remaining a widow, both her husband’s family and her own must agree. There could be a great deal of trouble.” “Some trouble, but nothing that cannot be managed. There is no one in her husband’s family. On her own side, there is a worthless uncle and a younger brother. Whoever marries her must take the boy into the household too.” “That would not matter.” Hu pondered in silence. “Boss Hu,” she urged, “you often come to Huzhou. Having a household here would make everything convenient. And she really is both beautiful and good. Would you like to see her?” “Certainly. How would we arrange it? Surely not by having a matchmaker bring her round?” “Of course we cannot be so blunt.” A’qi thought for a moment. “Tomorrow morning I’ll invite her to burn incense at Tiansheng Temple by the North Gate. Wait there. When you see me, pretend not to know me and don’t greet me. I won’t tell her the purpose either. With no reason for self-consciousness, you will be able to see her as she is.” “Very well. Let us do that.”

到了第二天，胡雪岩找陈世龙陪着，到了北门天圣寺，先烧香，后求签，签上是这样一首诗：暮云千里乱吴峰，落叶微闻远寺钟；目尽长江秋草外，美人何处采芙蓉？胡雪岩看不懂这首诗，只看签是“中平”，解释也不见得高明，便一笑置之，跟阵世龙寺前寺后，闲步随喜。陈世龙却有些奇怪，只听胡雪岩说要到天圣寺走走，未说是何用意？他这样的一个大忙人，力何忽发雅兴，来游古刹。先是心里打算，他既不说，自己也不必问，但等到了天圣寺，自然明白，这时看不出名堂，就忍不住要问了。“胡先生，你是不是等什么人？还是……”“对！我正是等人。跟你说了吧！”一说经过，陈世龙笑道：“幄。我晓得了！”他说，“一定是何家的那个小孤孀，不错！阿七的眼光不错，不过，这个媒做得成，做不成，就很难说了。”“原来你也晓得。”胡雪岩颇有意外之感，“来，我们到那里坐一坐。”

The next day Hu took Chen Shilong with him to Tiansheng Temple. He first burned incense, then drew a fortune slip. It bore a poem: “Evening clouds for a thousand li blur the peaks of Wu; through falling leaves, a distant temple bell is faintly heard. Beyond the autumn grass, the gaze follows the Yangtze as far as sight can reach; where does the beauty gather lotus blooms?” Hu did not understand the verse. He saw only that the fortune was rated middling, and its explanation seemed no more illuminating. He dismissed it with a smile and strolled around the temple with Chen. Chen was puzzled. Hu had said that he wanted to visit Tiansheng Temple but had given no reason. Why had such a busy man suddenly developed a taste for ancient temples? At first Chen thought that, since Hu had not explained, he need not ask; once they arrived, the purpose would become clear. Now, seeing no sign of it, he could not help inquiring. “Mr Hu, are you waiting for someone, or…” “Exactly. I am waiting for someone. I may as well tell you.” When Hu explained, Chen laughed. “Ah, I understand! It must be the young widow from the He family. A’qi has a good eye. Whether the match can be made is another question.” Hu was rather surprised. “So you know about her too. Come, let us sit over there.”

两人在庙门口一家点心摊子上坐了下来，一面吃汤圆，一面谈何家的小孤孀。据陈世龙说，此人颇有艳名，自从居孀以后，很有些人打她的主意，但夫家还好说话，娘家有个胞叔，十分难，所以好事一直不谐。“无非是多要几两银子。”胡雪岩问，“有什么难的？”“那家伙嫖赌吃着，一应俱全，哪个跟他做了亲戚，三天两头上门来罗嗦，就吃不消了。”“这倒不必怕他。”胡雪岩又问，“她娘家姓啥？”“娘家姓刘。他叔叔叫刘三才，人家把他的名字改了一个字，叫做刘不才。由这上头，胡先生就可以晓得他的为人了。”“总有点用处吧！”“用处是有点的。不过没有人敢用他。这个人太滑、太靠不住。”“不管它！你倒说来我听听，刘不才有何用处？”“他能说会道，风花雪月，无不精通，是做篾片的好材料。”陈世龙接着又用警告的语气说，“就是银钱不能经他的手。说句笑话，他老子死了，如果买棺材的钱经他的手，他都会先用了再说。”胡雪岩笑了，“有这样的人？”是不甚相信的语气。“就有这样的人！”陈世尤特为举证：“我跟他在赌场里常常碰头，诸如此类的事，见得多了。”

They sat at a refreshment stall by the temple gate and talked over bowls of sweet rice dumplings. According to Chen, the widow was celebrated for her beauty. Many men had been interested since her husband’s death. His family was reasonably accommodating, but her father’s brother was extremely difficult. That was why no match had yet succeeded. “He can only want a little more money,” Hu said. “What is difficult about that?” “Every indulgence—women, gambling, fine food and clothes—he enjoys them all. Anyone who becomes his relative will have him at the door every few days making trouble. It would be intolerable.” “There is no need to fear him. What was her maiden surname?” “Liu. Her uncle is Liu Sancai. People change a character of his name and call him Liu Bucai—Liu the Good-for-Nothing. That tells you what he is like.” “Surely he is useful in some way?” “In some ways, yes. But nobody dares employ him. He is too slippery and unreliable.” “Never mind that. Tell me what he is good at.” “He is an eloquent talker, thoroughly versed in every romantic and fashionable amusement—the very material for a rich man’s hanger-on.” Chen added a warning. “Only never let money pass through his hands. To put it as a joke, if his father died and he were given money for the coffin, he would spend it on himself first.” Hu laughed incredulously. “Can anyone be like that?” “He is exactly like that. I often met him in gambling houses and saw plenty of such behavior.”

胡雪岩点点头，抛开陈世龙的话，管自己转念头。他心里在想，篾片有篾片的用处，帮闲的人，官场中叫清客，遇着纨袴子弟便叫篾片，好似竹篓子一样，没有竹筐片，就拧不起空架子。自己也要几个篾片，帮着交际应酬。如果刘不才本心还不坏，只是好拆烂污，倒不妨动动脑筋，收服了他做个帮手。“来了，来了！”陈世龙突然拉着他的衣服，轻轻说道。胡雪岩定定神，抬头望去，这一望，心里立刻便是异样的味道。何家的小寡妇是个“黑里俏”，除了皮肤以外，无可批评。腰肢极细，走几步路，如凤摆杨柳，却又不象风尘中人的有意做作，而是天然袅娜。她下了轿子，扶着个十一二岁的小丫头。一步一步的走过点心摊子。胡雪岩的脸便随着她转，一直转到背脊朝陈世尤为止。陈世龙已会过了帐，悄悄的拉了他一把，两个人跟着又进了山门。阿七是早就看到了他们的，此时落后一步，微微转近身来摇一摇手。

Hu nodded, put Chen’s words aside and pursued his own thoughts. A hanger-on had his uses. Among officials, such a companion was called a cultivated guest; among wealthy young idlers, he was a miepian, a strip of bamboo. Just as a basket’s framework could not hold together without bamboo strips, a grand social establishment needed such men. Hu too could use a few to help with entertaining and social obligations. If Liu Bucai was not bad at heart, merely prone to making a mess of things, it might be worth finding a way to win him over as an assistant. “Here they are!” Chen suddenly tugged at Hu’s clothes and spoke softly. Hu collected himself and looked up. At once he felt something unusual. The young widow was a dark-complexioned beauty. Apart from her complexion, he could find nothing to criticize. Her waist was extremely slender, and as she walked she swayed like a willow in the breeze. Yet there was none of the calculated display of a courtesan; her grace was natural. Alighting from her chair, she rested a hand on a maid of eleven or twelve and walked past the stall. Hu’s face followed her until his back was turned to Chen. Chen had already paid. He gave Hu a quiet tug, and they followed the women through the temple gate. A’qi had noticed them long before. Now she dropped back a step, turned slightly and made a small warning gesture with her hand.

“她什么意思？”胡雪岩问。“大概是关照不是靠得太近。”听这一说，胡雪岩便站住了脚，尽自盯着她的背影看。从头到脚，一身玄色，头上簪一朵穿孝的白绒花，显得格外触目。“胡先生，”陈世龙轻声问道：“怎么样？”“就是皮肤黑一点。”“有名的‘黑芙蓉’嘛！”陈世龙说。“怎么叫黑芙蓉？只听说过黑牡丹。”“她的名字就叫芙蓉。”“芙蓉！”胡雪岩偏着头，皱着眉想，“好象什么地方听说过个名字？”就这样不断念着“芙蓉、芙蓉”，皱眉苦思，到底起起来了。“原来在这里！”他把刚才求的那张签，拿给陈世龙看。“巧了！”陈世龙极感兴趣的笑着，“看起来是前世注定的姻缘。”“不见得！‘美人何处采芙蓉’，是采不到的意思。”胡雪岩摇摇头，大为快怏之意。陈世龙从未见过他有这样患得患失、近乎沮丧的神情，心里有些好笑。但随即想到，胡雪岩对芙蓉，可说是一见钟情，无论如何得把她“采”来供养，才是报答之道。

“What does she mean?” Hu asked. “Probably that we should not follow too closely.” Hu stopped and stared after the widow. She wore black from head to foot, with a soft white mourning flower in her hair that stood out strikingly. “What do you think, Mr Hu?” Chen asked softly. “Only that her complexion is a little dark.” “She is famous as Black Lotus.” “Why Black Lotus? I have only heard of Black Peony.” “Her name is Furong—Lotus.” “Furong!” Hu tilted his head and frowned. “Where have I heard that name?” He repeated it, thinking hard, until at last he remembered. “Here!” He showed Chen the fortune slip. “What a coincidence!” Chen laughed with interest. “It looks like a marriage destined in a former life.” “Not necessarily. ‘Where does the beauty gather lotus blooms?’ means that the lotus cannot be gathered.” Hu shook his head in disappointment. Chen had never seen him so anxious about gaining or losing something, almost dejected, and was secretly amused. Then he reflected that Hu had plainly fallen in love at first sight. Whatever happened, he must help gather this lotus for Hu. That would be a way to repay his kindness.

“再进去看看！”胡雪岩说。“胡先生，你一个人去好了，她有点认识我的，见面不大方便，我先避开为妙。”等陈世龙一走，胡雪岩一个在大殿前面那只高可及人的大香炉旁边，七上八下想心里，又想闯进殿去细看一看，又怕不依阿七的暗示，会把好事搞坏，左思右想只是打不定主意，自己都觉得有些好笑，几万银子上落的生意，都是当机立断，毫无悔尤，偏偏这么点事会大为作难！辰光就这样空耗着，耗到阿七和芙蓉出殿，他不能再没行动了，“嗐！”他自己对自己不满，这有什么大不了的！成也罢，不成也罢，何必看得那样认真？这一转念，犹豫和怯意一扫而空，同时也把阿七的约定和暗示，都抛到九霄云外，踏着从容潇洒的步子迎了上去，清清朗朗地喊一声：“郁四嫂！”

“Let us go farther in and have another look,” Hu said. “You had better go alone, Mr Hu. She knows me a little, and meeting her might be awkward. I should keep out of the way.” Once Chen left, Hu stood beside the great incense burner, as tall as a man, in front of the main hall. His thoughts rose and fell. He wanted to go inside for a closer look, yet feared that disregarding A’qi’s warning would spoil the affair. He could not make up his mind. It seemed ridiculous even to him: in transactions involving tens of thousands of taels, he decided instantly and felt no regret. Yet such a small matter left him at a loss. Time slipped away until A’qi and Furong emerged from the hall. He could not remain inactive. “Bah!” he said to himself in annoyance. What was so important? Whether it succeeded or failed, why take it so seriously? The thought swept away his hesitation and timidity. It also sent A’qi’s agreement and warning clean out of his mind. With an easy, confident step he went forward and called clearly, “Sister-in-Law Yu!”

既然叫出来了，阿七不能不理，装出略如惊喜的神态说道：“啊，胡老板，是你！怎么有空？来烧香，还是啥？”“偶然路过，进来逛一逛。”胡雪岩一面说，一面打量芙蓉。她那双眼睛很活，但也很静，在初见胡雪岩，视线飞快地一绕之后，一直垂着眼皮，看着地下。阿七心想，一不做，二不休，既然胡雪岩自己要出头，索性彰明较著替他们拉拢，让他自己来显显本事，倒省了许多心。于是她说：“胡老板，我要敲你的竹杠，好好请一请我们……”一说到“我们”两字，芙蓉便推一推她的手埋怨：“你这个人！哪里有这样子的？”“怕啥！”阿七一副理直气壮的态度，“胡老板又不是外人，是我们老头子的要好弟兄！”“正是这话。这位……”胡雪岩微笑着说：“这位小姐，不必见外！”“喔，”阿七趁机说道，“胡老板，我来引见，这是我的小姐妹，娘家姓刘，夫家姓何，小名叫芙蓉。你叫她名字好了。”听这番介绍，芙蓉只是皱眉，胡雪岩不知道她因何不满，不敢鲁莽，“没有这个道理！至少该尊称一声小姐。”说着作了个揖，“芙蓉小姐！”“不敢当。”芙蓉带着羞意，还了礼，接着转脸对阿七说：“我先走一步了！”

Since he had called, A’qi had to answer. She assumed an expression of mild surprise and pleasure. “Ah, Boss Hu! How do you find the time? Have you come to burn incense, or what?” “I happened to be passing and came in for a stroll.” As he spoke, Hu studied Furong. Her eyes were lively yet composed. On first seeing him, she had glanced swiftly at him; ever since, she had kept them lowered toward the ground. A’qi decided that, since Hu had chosen to show himself, she might as well bring them together openly and let him display his own abilities. It would save her a great deal of trouble. “Boss Hu, I mean to take advantage of you. You must give us a proper treat.” At the word “us,” Furong nudged her hand and protested. “Really! How can you behave like that?” “What is there to fear?” A’qi answered as though her conduct were perfectly justified. “Boss Hu is no stranger. He is my old man’s close friend.” “Exactly,” Hu said with a smile. “This young lady need not stand on ceremony.” “Oh, let me introduce you,” A’qi went on. “This is a young friend of mine. Her maiden surname is Liu, and she married into the He family. Her personal name is Furong. You may call her that.” Furong frowned at the introduction. Hu did not know what displeased her and dared not be rash. “Certainly not! I must at least address her respectfully as Miss.” He bowed. “Miss Furong.” “You are too kind.” Blushing, she returned the greeting, then turned to A’qi. “I must leave you now.”

“你不要扫我的兴！”阿七一把拉住她，“我老早想到白衣庵去吃素斋，难得今天凑巧，又有人做东道，又有人陪我。”芙蓉不响，自是默许了。胡雪岩便一叠连声地说：“好，好！我做个小东。不过白衣奄在哪里？在它那里吃素斋是怎么个规矩？我都不知道。”“我知道！”阿七接口答说，“不过，胡老板，这个东道倒不是小东道！白衣奄的素菜，湖州有名的，吃一顿斋，缘簿上总要写五两银子才够面子。““只要你吃得中意，五两银子算啥？”胡雪岩避开一步问道：“轿子可是在山门外？”“已经打发走了。胡老板，拜托你到山门口去雇两顶，白衣庵在西门城脚下，轿伕都知道的。“胡雪岩答应着，抢步先行，等阿七和芙蓉一出山门口，轿子已经倾倒轿杠在等着了。但事情起了变化，芙蓉原已默许了的，突然变卦，说她的小兄弟在发烧，甚不放心，一定要回家。阿七自然不肯，无奈芙蓉的主意也很坚决。众目睽睽之下，不便拖拖拉拉地争持，于是胡雪岩反帮着她阿七，说不必勉强，改天还有相叙的机会。“哪里还有相叙的机会？”等芙蓉坐上轿子回家，阿七这样埋怨胡雪岩，“我关照你不要叫我，你不听！好好一头姻缘，让你自己搅散了！”此时此地，不宜细谈此事，胡雪岩自己认错：“都怪我不好。回家去说。”

“Don’t spoil my pleasure!” A’qi caught hold of her. “I have long wanted a vegetarian meal at Baiyi Nunnery. Today, by good fortune, I have someone to treat me and someone to keep me company.” Furong said nothing, which amounted to assent. Hu eagerly agreed. “Very well! Let me offer a modest meal. But where is Baiyi Nunnery, and how does one arrange to eat there? I know nothing about it.” “I know. But this will be no modest treat, Boss Hu! Its vegetarian dishes are famous in Huzhou. To keep up appearances, a guest must enter at least five taels in the donation book.” “If the food pleases you, what are five taels?” Hu stepped aside and asked, “Are your chairs outside the gate?” “We sent them away. Please hire two for us at the gate. Baiyi Nunnery is at the foot of the city wall by the West Gate. Every bearer knows it.” Hu agreed and hurried ahead. By the time the women came out, the chairs were waiting with their shafts lowered. But matters had changed. Furong, who had tacitly agreed, suddenly said that her younger brother had a fever. She was too worried to stay and must go home. A’qi resisted, but Furong was firm. With so many eyes upon them, they could not argue or pull one another about. Hu therefore helped persuade A’qi not to press her: there would be another occasion to meet. Once Furong had departed in her chair, A’qi reproached him. “What other occasion? I told you not to greet me, and you wouldn’t listen. You have spoiled a perfectly good match yourself!” This was no place for a detailed discussion. Hu admitted fault. “It was all my doing. Let us go home and talk.”

一回到家，说郁四到沂园“孵混堂”去了。好在通家之好，不避形迹，阿七便留胡雪岩吃午饭，谈芙蓉的事。“我已经露口风给她了，虽然没有指出人来，不过你一露面，也就很清楚了。”阿七又说：“她跟我的交情很够，等我慢慢来说，一定可以成功。哪晓得你心这么急？现在事情弄僵了！““也不见得。”胡雪岩说，“也许是她心里有数，所以不好意思。你不妨去探探她的口气看！”“当然！总不能就此算数。不过，很难！”阿七摇摇头说，“我懂她的脾气。”“她的脾气怎么样？”“她也是很爽快的人，一肯就肯，说不肯就不肯。”“我倒不相信！”胡雪岩心想，本来也还无所谓，照现在看，非要把芙蓉弄到手不可！不然传出去便成了一个话柄。不过这一趟是无论如何来不及了！且等年下有空，好好来动一番脑筋。心里存了这么个主意，便暂且抛开了芙蓉，自去知府衙门访杨、秦两位老夫子辞行，准备再住一天就带着黄仪回杭州。

Back at the house, they learned that Yu had gone to spend a leisurely spell at Yiyuan Bathhouse. As Hu was a close family friend, there was no need for formal reserve. A’qi kept him to lunch and discussed Furong. “I had already hinted at the matter to her. I didn’t name the man, but once you appeared it was obvious. She and I are very close. If I had been allowed to work on her gradually, it would certainly have succeeded. Who would have thought you were in such a hurry? Now things are at an impasse.” “Not necessarily,” Hu replied. “Perhaps she understood and felt embarrassed. You might sound her out.” “Of course. We cannot simply abandon it. But it will be difficult.” A’qi shook her head. “I know her temperament.” “What is it like?” “She is straightforward too. When she agrees, she agrees; when she refuses, she refuses.” “I don’t believe it.” Hu had initially been indifferent enough, but now felt he must win Furong. Otherwise, if the story spread, it would become a subject of gossip. There was certainly no time on this visit. He would wait until he was free near the year’s end and give the matter serious thought. Having settled on that, he put Furong aside for the moment and went to the prefectural yamen to call on the two secretaries, Yang and Qin, and take his leave. He intended to stay one more day and then return to Hangzhou with Huang Yi.

“来一趟不容易，何妨多住几天。”郁四挽留他说，“你不是要在上海打局面，我有几个南得的朋友，不可不文。”这一说胡雪岩心思活动了。他一直想到南浔去一趟，因为做洋庄的丝商，南浔最多，一则应该联络一气，以便对付洋人，再则洋庄方面还有许多奥妙，非局外人所知，他们也不肯随便透露，现在有郁四介绍，正好叨教。于是他欣然答道：“好的！我就多留两天。”“两天？”郁四慢吞吞地答道：“也够了。不过，我这两天衙门里有事，不能陪你，我另外找个人陪你去，就同我去一样。”“好的。什么时候动身？”“随便你。明天一早动身好了。晚上我把陪你去的人找来，你们先见一见面。”那人是郁四手下的一个帮手，沉默寡言，但人头极熟，交游极广。他姓刘，单名一个权字，原是南浔人。南浔刘家是大族，刘权以同族的关系，包收南得刘家的钱粮。以这样的关系，陪着胡雪岩同行引路，可说是最适当的人选。

“You seldom come. Why not stay a few more days?” Yu urged. “You want to establish yourself in Shanghai. I have several friends in Nanxun whom you ought to know.” This interested Hu. He had long wanted to visit Nanxun, which had more silk merchants engaged in the export trade than anywhere else. They ought to build connections and act together in dealing with foreigners. Besides, there were many intricacies of the export business that outsiders did not know and insiders would not readily disclose. With Yu’s introduction, he could learn from them. “Very well,” he answered gladly. “I’ll stay another two days.” “Two days?” Yu replied slowly. “That should be enough. But I have business at the yamen and cannot accompany you. I’ll find someone else to go with you. It will be just as good as going with me.” “When do we leave?” “Whenever you like. Tomorrow morning would do. I’ll bring your companion here tonight so that you can meet.” The man was one of Yu’s assistants, quiet and sparing of words but widely acquainted and well connected. His surname was Liu and his given name Quan. He was a native of Nanxun, where the Liu clan was a great family. Through his clan connections, he had contracted to collect its taxes. This made him an ideal companion and guide for Hu.

“你哪一天回湖州？”郁四问道，“我们把它说定规！”“我想两天工夫总够了。”“明天，后天，好！你准定大后天回来，我有事要请个客，你一定要赶到。““一定！”胡雪岩毫不迟疑地应承。“那就拜托你了。”郁四向刘权说，“老刘，你晓得的，胡老板是王大老爷的好朋友。”这是指点刘权，要把胡雪岩的这种特殊关系说出去，好增加声势，果然，“不怕官，只怕管”，就因为王有龄的关系，胡雪岩在南浔的两天，极受优礼，到第三天东道主还挽留，胡雪岩因为郁四有事请客，不能失约，坚辞而回。早晨上船，过午到湖州，陈世龙在码头迎接，告诉他说，郁四在沂园等他。“好，我正要淴个浴。”“我也晓得胡先生一定要淴浴。”陈世龙把手里的包裹一扬，“我把胡先生的干净小褂裤、袜子都带来了。”这虽是一件小事，显得陈世龙肯在自己身上用心，胡雪岩相当高兴。一路谈着南浔的情形，走到沂园。跟郁四见面招呼过，随即解衣磅礴，一洗征尘，顿觉满身轻快，加以此行极其顺利，所以精神抖擞，特别显得有劲。

“Which day will you return to Huzhou?” Yu asked. “Let us fix it.” “I should think two days will be enough.” “Tomorrow and the next day. Good. Then be sure to return the day after that. I am giving a dinner for a particular occasion, and you must be there.” “Certainly,” Hu agreed without hesitation. “Then I leave it to you,” Yu told Liu Quan. “Old Liu, you know that Boss Hu is Prefect Wang’s good friend.” He meant Liu to make this special relationship known and add weight to Hu’s standing. As the saying went, people feared not officials in general but the official who had authority over them. Through his connection with Wang, Hu received exceptional courtesy during his two days in Nanxun. On the third day his hosts still urged him to remain, but he could not break his promise to Yu and firmly took his leave. Boarding in the morning, he reached Huzhou after noon. Chen met him at the landing and said that Yu was waiting at Yiyuan. “Good. I was just wanting a bath.” “I knew you would, Mr Hu.” Chen raised the bundle in his hand. “I have brought your clean underclothes and socks.” It was a small matter, but it showed Chen’s thoughtful attention to him, and Hu was pleased. They walked to Yiyuan, talking of Nanxun on the way. After greeting Yu, Hu undressed and washed away the dust of travel, feeling light and refreshed. The success of his journey added to his spirits, and he seemed especially energetic.

谈了好些在南浔的经过，看看天色将晚，胡雪岩便问：“四哥，你今天请哪个？是啥事？”“很客气的一位客人。”郁四说着，便向放在软榻前面的胡雪岩的那双鞋子，看了一眼。胡雪岩是极机警的人，立刻便说：“我这双鞋子走过长路，不大干净，恐怕在生客面前，不大好看吧！”“自己人说老实话，是不大光鲜。不要紧，”郁四叫过跑堂来说，“你到我那里去一趟，跟四奶奶说，把我新做的那件宁绸衬绒袍子，直贡呢马褂拿来。另外再带一双新鞋子。”“何必？”胡雪岩说，“你新做的袍子怎么拿来我穿？我的这身衣服也还有八成新，叫他们刷刷干净，也还可以将就。鞋子也不必去拿，回头走出去现买一双好了。”郁四没有理他，挥挥手示意跑堂照办，然后才说：“你也太见外了，套把衣服算得了什么？还要客气！”听这一说，胡雪岩还能有何表示？丢开此事，谈到他预备第二天就回杭州。郁四还要留他，胡雪岩不肯，两人翻覆争执，没有结果，而跑堂的已把衣服取来了。“走吧！”郁四说，“时间不早了。你到底哪天动身，回头再说。”

After much talk of Nanxun, Hu noticed that evening was approaching. “Brother Yu, whom are you entertaining today? What is the occasion?” “A guest who requires special courtesy,” Yu replied, glancing at the shoes in front of Hu’s couch. Hu was quick to understand. “These shoes have traveled a long way and are rather dirty. They might not make a good impression on a guest I haven’t met.” “Between ourselves, they are not very presentable. Never mind.” Yu called an attendant. “Go to my house and ask Fourth Mistress for my new ning-silk robe with its plush lining and my gong-cloth riding jacket. Bring a pair of new shoes too.” “Why should I wear your new robe?” Hu protested. “My clothes are still almost new. Have them brushed, and they will do. There is no need to fetch shoes either. I can buy a pair when we go out.” Yu ignored him and waved the attendant away to carry out the order. Then he said, “You are standing far too much on ceremony. What is a suit of clothes between friends?” Hu could hardly object further. He changed the subject to his plan to return to Hangzhou the following day. Yu urged him to stay; Hu refused. They argued back and forth without reaching a decision, until the attendant returned with the clothes. “Let us go,” Yu said. “It is getting late. We can discuss your departure later.”

“慢点！”胡雪岩看着那双双梁缎鞋和一身新衣服，摸着脸说，“要剃个头才好，不知道辰光够不够？”“够，够！你尽管剃！”于是唤了个剃头担子来，胡雪岩剃头修脸，重新打过辫子，才穿上新袍新鞋，里里外外，焕然一新，跑堂的打趣说道：“胡老爷象个新郎官！”“我呢？”郁四接口问道：“你看我象不象个‘大冰老爷’？”郁四也是上下簇新，喜气洋洋，很象个吃喜酒的冰人。跑堂的还不曾接口，又出现了一个衣帽鲜洁，象个贺客佯的人，那是陈世龙。胡雪岩不觉诧异，“你怎么又来了？”他问，“是找我有话说？”陈世龙笑笑不响，只看着郁四。于是郁四说道：“我请客也有他一个。走吧！“

“Wait.” Looking at the double-ridged satin shoes and the new clothes, Hu touched his face. “I ought to have a shave. Is there time?” “Plenty. Go ahead.” A traveling barber was called. Hu had his head and face shaved and his queue redressed before putting on the new robe and shoes. He was transformed from head to foot. The attendant joked, “Master Hu looks like a bridegroom!” “And I?” Yu asked. “Do I look like an honored matchmaker?” He too was dressed in new clothes and beaming, very much like a matchmaker attending a wedding feast. Before the attendant could answer, another man appeared in fresh clothes and a clean hat, looking like a guest come to offer congratulations. It was Chen Shilong. Hu was surprised. “Why have you come back? Is there something you need to tell me?” Chen only smiled and looked at Yu. “He is invited too,” Yu said. “Let us go.”

译者说明与综合精读提示（第十七章后段）

本批覆盖所提供文本第14341—14714行，至第十七章结束。正文按完整语义段落逐段对应；以下说明与练习提示不属于小说原文。

人物、术语与文本识读

本批阿七是郁四的成年伴侣 Crystal A’qi，不是尤五之妹七姑奶奶。叙述中简称 A’qi，胡雪岩称其“郁四嫂”时用 Sister-in-Law Yu。黄仪 Huang Yi 此行随胡到杭州办笔墨，依职责译为秘书；不把薪酬未谈误写成无薪服务。芙蓉 Furong 是何家年轻寡妇，娘家姓刘；为保留签诗与名字的照应，初次解释其名时补出 Lotus 这一词义。

刘三才为 Liu Sancai，讥称刘不才为 Liu Bucai／Liu the Good-for-Nothing。另一个人物刘权 Liu Quan 是郁四的助手与南浔向导，不是刘不才。篾片 miepian 保留音译，并随原文解释为供人应酬解闷的帮闲者及竹篾比喻。沂园用 Yiyuan，天圣寺用 Tiansheng Temple，白衣庵用 Baiyi Nunnery；南浔用 Nanxun。

“萌雪岩”“阵世龙”“陈世尤”等按胡雪岩、陈世龙识读；“风摆杨柳”语境中的“凤”按风理解。“南得”依前后文统一识读为南浔；“不可不文”按不可不交识读。“白衣奄”与白衣庵为同一地点。“宁绸”“直贡呢”采用 ning silk、gong cloth 等保守名称，不据字面擅自补定产地或具体纤维；“双梁缎鞋”保留双道凸棱的形制描述。

金额与叙述限度

第十六章第13431行明确说聚成可以调拨三万两，本章第14343行另调五万两，随后第14347、14349行又称郁四的十万两。本译分别保留30,000、50,000、100,000，不为凑数擅改某一笔。相邻叙述没有完整列明累计口径，不能仅凭上述两笔推出十万；第十六章范围内未发现另调两万的明文。

外商关税按原文为货值的5%；白衣庵斋饭为捐缘簿上至少五两。上海土地五年、十年后的收益是胡雪岩在小说中的判断，保留其人物视角，不当作译者对土地收益的断言。怡良的亲属关系、外国租界的地位及各项时局判断均依所给小说呈现，不以译者推断替换原稿。

签诗

诗中“芙蓉”与女子名字形成情节线索，英文以 lotus blooms 呼应后文 Furong—Lotus。签评“中平”译为 middling；胡雪岩把问句解作“采不到”，陈世龙却解作宿缘，是两个人物的不同读法，不能在译文中合并成已经应验的预言。

阅读理解

可比较三层声音：郁四真诚地劝收缩经营；胡雪岩表面接受，同时判断自己可以控制风险；胡又试探郁四是否担心本金。阅读推断题必须分别标明是谁知道、谁猜测、谁在解释动机，尤其留意“有言外之意吗”尚未获得确认时的语气。

完形填空

本段有多组条件与让步：即便亏本仍能接受，虽有信任仍需顾及出资者，对外贸易虽受影响却未完全停顿。先判断正反关系，再选 even if、although、yet、therefore 等词。不要把事实上的矛盾和人物态度的转变混为一谈。

段落排序与衔接

资金解困引出扩张计划，朋友劝告引出风险与信任，信任引出替朋友置地的打算；阿七的谈话再从陈世龙转到替胡说媒。后半段“签诗—听名字—回忆签诗”是明确的前后照应。排序时先找重复对象，再判断哪个段落提供了后文理解所必需的前提。

翻译训练

可练“头寸”“本钱”“存货”“收益”“结账”等在不同句子中的准确区分。对“虽不能听，亦不宜辩”一类省略主语的句式，先补出人物与动作，再翻译；不要把点头表示接受直接译成实际照办。人物说“不相信”，也不意味着叙述保证其判断正确。

表达积累

可积累 working funds、capital tied up in stock、take a long-term view、leave room for maneuver、sound someone out、keep a promise 等。讨论决策时可借鉴“机会—约束—风险—备选安排”的组织次序；小说人物对风险和人际关系的看法应先辨析，再选择适合规范写作的表达。
