第二天胡雪岩借了王有龄家请客，依旧是“小玩玩”。两天下来，刘不才赢了一万多银子，大为兴奋。胡雪岩却提醒他，不可因此改变初衷，赌上绝不能成功立业，同时也喜一次拜托，务必把庞二笼络得服服帖帖，然后好相机进言。“看样子我们很投缘。”刘不才说，“长线放远鹞，‘火到猪头烂’……”“不！”胡雪岩不容如此闲豫，“我要托他的事，很急！三叔，你无论如何，趁明天这个机会，就要把他收服。象昨天那样子就很好，连我都佩服。不过你今天就不大对了，全副心思放在赌上，误了正事。““今天的机会很好，我先弄它几个，好做赌本。”刘不才不好意思地笑一笑，“以后没有机会了，你就先放我一马！”“赌本你不必愁。有机会能赢几个，我自然也没有反对你，非要你输的道理，只是你要顾到你去赌的原意。”胡雪岩又重重地说：“做生意就是这样！处处地方不要忘记自己是为的什么！”刘不才想了一会，点头答道：“好！我明天全副精神对付庞二。”

The next day, Hu Xueyan borrowed Wang Youling's house to entertain the guests, again for “a little play.” Over the two days, Liu Bucai won more than ten thousand taels and was greatly excited. Hu reminded him, however, not to let this alter his original intentions: gambling could never establish a man's career. Once more, he urged Liu to win Pang over completely, then find an opportunity to put their proposal to him. “We seem to get on very well,” Liu said. “A long line flies the kite far; enough fire will soften the pig's head...” “No!” Hu would not allow such leisurely patience. “What I need from him is urgent. Third Uncle, you must use tomorrow's opportunity to win him over. Yesterday you did wonderfully. Even I admired you. But today you went wrong. You put your whole mind into gambling and neglected the real business.” “The opportunity was good today. I wanted to win something first, to use as gambling capital.” Liu smiled sheepishly. “There may be no chance later. Let me off this once.” “You needn't worry about capital. Naturally, if there's a chance to win something, I've no reason to object and insist on your losing. But remember why you went there to gamble.” Hu emphasised his point. “That's how business works. Wherever you are, never forget what you're there for.” Liu considered, then nodded. “Very well. Tomorrow I'll give all my attention to Pang Er.”

庞二请客的场面很阔，他家在西湖葛岭山脚下有一所别墅，请客就请在那里。十一月的天气，外面西北风刮得人重裘不暖，但在庞二的别墅中，却是温暖如春，在那间背山面湖的温室中开筵，一共三桌客，身分极杂，但都穿的便衣，也就不容易分得出来了。宴是午宴，吃完已经下午两点，除了少数几个人以外其余都是知名的赌客，一散席便商量如何赌法？“做主人的摇场摊吧！”这个提议，立刻有人附和。庞二喜欢摇摊是出名的，而在这个场合中，最有资格做庄的，自然也是庞二。在他虽有当仁不让之心，却不免踌躇，因为缺少一个帮手。但转眼看到刘不才，立即欣然答应：“好的！各位有兴致，我就先遥儿十摊。”于是除了一桌麻将以外，近二十个人都预备打摊。听差的准备桌子、座位、赌具，庞二却把刘不才找到一边有话说。

Pang's entertainment was on a grand scale. His family had a villa at the foot of Geling Hill beside West Lake, and he received his guests there. Outside, the north-west wind of the eleventh month penetrated even heavy furs. Inside the villa, it was warm as spring. The luncheon was served in a heated room with the hill behind it and the lake in front. There were three tables of guests, drawn from widely differing ranks, though their informal clothes made those distinctions difficult to see. By the time they finished eating, it was already two in the afternoon. Apart from a few exceptions, they were all well-known gamblers. As soon as they left the tables, they discussed what to play. “Let our host bank a dice-shaking game!” Others immediately agreed. Pang was famous for his fondness for the game, and of everyone present he was naturally the best qualified to act as banker. Willing though he was, he hesitated: he lacked an assistant. Then he caught sight of Liu and readily agreed. “Very well! Since you're in the mood, I'll shake a few dozen rounds first.” Apart from one table of mahjong, nearly twenty people prepared to play. While servants arranged the table, seats, and equipment, Pang drew Liu aside.

“老刘！我们合伙。我六成，你四成，你看如何？”“当然好罗！不过，我先要‘灵一灵’市面，我只带了三万银子在身上，场面太大，我要派人回去拿钱。”“不必，不必，钱我有。你也不要先拿本钱，等场头散了再算。只有一件事，请你替我做‘开配’。”庞二又说，“我摇摊有个臭脾气，开配不灵光，我摇起来就没劲。那天在周五家摇摊，临时请了位朋友帮忙，我不过出了五个‘老宝’，输不到两万银子，那位开配朋友的手就有些发抖了。不是人家帮我的忙，我不见情，还要说人家，象那位朋友开配，真把我的脸面都丢完了！”“我没有替你做过开配，不过，你的事，自然没话说。就怕我应付不下来，”“你别客气了。”庞二拱拱手，“捧我小弟的场！承情，承情。”

“Old Liu, let's be partners. Sixty per cent for me, forty for you. How does that sound?” “Excellent, of course. But let me first feel out the scale of the game. I've only thirty thousand taels on me. If the stakes are too large, I'll have to send home for more.” “No need. I have the money. You needn't put up any capital beforehand; we'll settle when the game breaks up. There's just one thing: I'd like you to handle the collection and payment of bets.” Pang continued, “I've a bad habit at this game. If the man settling the bets isn't up to the mark, I lose all enthusiasm. The other day, at Zhou's, I asked a friend to help at short notice. I'd only thrown five Old Treasures and lost less than twenty thousand taels when his hands began to tremble. It's not that I fail to appreciate someone helping me and still complain about him, but the way that fellow handled the settlements made me lose all face!” “I've never done it for you before. Naturally, if you ask, there's nothing more to say. I'm only afraid I won't be able to manage.” “Don't be modest.” Pang clasped his hands. “Help me put on a good show. I'll be grateful indeed.”

于是刘不才到场执行开配的任务。只见台面已经布置好了，那张台子，是专为摇摊用的，紫檀桌子，黄杨木的桌面，比平常方桌大一号，四角用象牙嵌出界线，每一方又用象牙嵌出茶杯大的圆点，庄家一点，对门三点，右方是二，左方是四，左青龙，右白虎，开配照例站立在左上角的三与四之间，那是吉利的“青龙角”。等他在青龙角上站定，随即便有听差送过一盒筹码来，筹码是四寸长的牙筹！上面刻着金字“世载堂庞”四字，作为标识，筹码共分五种，分别刻着骨牌中“天、地、人、和”的点子，另外还有一种只刻堂名的白筹，自然是最小的码子。刘不才把筹码定为五等，一千、五百、一百、五十、十两，等赌客买好筹码，才是“皇帝”庞二落座，拿起一个明朝成化窑的青花摇缸，“察浪浪，察浪浪”地摇了三下，打开摇缸来看，十二点是四。“不错！‘开青龙’！”庞二说着又摇。

Liu took his place to settle the bets. The table was already prepared. Made specially for the game, it had a zitan-wood frame and a boxwood top and was one size larger than an ordinary square table. Ivory inlay marked the boundaries at its four corners. Each side also had ivory dots the size of teacups: one on the banker's side, three opposite, two to his right, and four to his left. The left was the Green Dragon, the right the White Tiger. The settling clerk customarily stood at the upper-left corner between three and four, the auspicious Green Dragon Corner. Once Liu stood there, a servant brought him a box of counters. They were ivory tallies four cun long, engraved in gold with the identifying words “Pang of Shizai Hall.” There were five kinds. Four bore the Heaven, Earth, Man, and Harmony markings from domino tiles; the fifth bore only the hall's name and was naturally the lowest denomination. Liu assigned them five values: one thousand, five hundred, one hundred, fifty, and ten taels. Only when the players had bought their counters did Pang, the “emperor,” sit down. He picked up a blue-and-white shaking bowl from the Chenghua kilns of the Ming dynasty and gave it three rattling shakes. Opening it, he found twelve points, which counted as four. “Good! An opening Green Dragon!” he said, and shook again.

前三下，名为“亮摊”，好供赌客“画路”，摊路的名堂甚多，大路、小路、荤路、素路，各人相信各人的。到第四下摇过，那才正式开始下注，场面极其热闹，刘不才的本事也就要拿出来了。摇摊在赌里面最公平，做下手的一点亏都不吃，而下手押注的花样也最多，跟牌九一洋，打“角”、打“横堂”以外，还可以打“大头”。角与横堂，下手与庄家各占两门，所以是一赌一，“大头”就不同了，虽也是各占两门，但赢法有差别，二带么的大头，开出“白虎”赢两倍，开出“进门”算和气。此外还有“放鹞子”，下手打三门，赢了吃二配三，在钱上是以三赌一，大本钱卜小利，好象吃亏，但在骰子上，下手占了便宜，赢三门输一门，当然，偏开不下注的一门，也是有的，那一下三注都吃，全军皆墨，就变成“放鹞子断线”了。

The first three throws were called “showing the game,” allowing the players to chart its course. There were many kinds of patterns: main patterns, minor patterns, “meat” patterns, and “vegetable” patterns. Each player believed in his own. Only after the fourth shake did betting formally begin. The scene grew lively, and Liu had to display his ability. Of all gambling games, this was the fairest: the players suffered no disadvantage, and they had the widest variety of wagers. As in pai gow, besides betting a “corner” or “across the hall,” they could bet a “big head.” With corner and across-the-hall bets, player and banker each held two of the four numbers, so the wager was one against one. A big-head bet also gave each side two numbers, but the method of winning differed. With a two-led big head carrying one, a White Tiger paid double, while an Entrance was a draw. Then there was “flying a kite”: the player backed three numbers, and on a win the settlement was “take two, pay three.” In money terms, this meant staking three to win one: large capital for a small return, seemingly a disadvantage. With the dice, however, the player held the advantage, winning on three numbers and losing on only one. Of course, the unbacked number could still come up. Then all three stakes were taken, the whole force was wiped out, and the flying kite's string had snapped.

“放鹞子”还是“孤丁”，照吃照配，不伤脑筋，伤脑筋的是改注码，有的大头改为孤丁，有的把这门注码移到另一门，注码不动，只凭口说，都要开配记住。不该配的配了，自然没有人说话，不该吃的吃了，便有人提出抗议。赔钱是小事，出了错便是不够格，会替庞二丢面子，所以刘不才不敢轻忽，每一注都得注意。暗中用心，表面却很悠闲，等摇缸亮出，该吃的吃进，该配的配多少倍，一一计算清楚，没有下手说闲话，更不曾起争执。刘不才不但计算得清楚，而且计算得特别快，庄家不会等得无聊，所以摇起来格外起劲。不多时候，二十摊已经摇完，做庄做了一半，庞二才看一看面前的银票。开配手边，只存筹码和不足一万的银票，满了一万，就得摆到庄家面前，名为讨口彩的“进庄”，其实是防范开配落入自己荷包。刘不才与庞二初交，兼以负有争取信任的责任，对这些细节，自然特别当心。庞二这时略略点了下，共有十四五叠之多，自己是十万银子的本钱，算来赢得也不能说少。

Whether the wager was a kite or a single-number bet, collecting and paying it was straightforward enough. The difficult part was a changed wager. One player might turn a big head into a single-number bet; another might transfer his stake from one number to another without moving the counters, merely announcing the change. The settling clerk had to remember it all. Nobody complained if he paid a bet that did not deserve payment, but if he collected a stake that should not have been taken, someone would protest. Losing money was a small matter. Making a mistake showed incompetence and would embarrass Pang. Liu dared neglect nothing; he watched every wager. Inwardly attentive but outwardly at ease, he waited for the bowl to be opened, then collected the losing bets and calculated each payment and its proper multiple. There were no complaints and no disputes. He worked not only accurately but exceptionally fast, so the banker never grew bored waiting and shook with increasing enthusiasm. Before long, twenty rounds were over. Halfway through his turn as banker, Pang finally looked at the banknotes in front of him. The settling clerk kept only counters and less than ten thousand taels in notes beside him. Once the notes reached ten thousand, he had to put them before the banker. This was auspiciously called “bringing money into the bank,” but its real purpose was to prevent the clerk from slipping money into his own pocket. Newly acquainted with Pang and charged with winning his trust, Liu was especially careful over such details. Pang made a quick count: there were fourteen or fifteen stacks. His own capital had been a hundred thousand taels. The winnings were by no means small.

但后半场的手风就不如前半场了，只见刘不才不断伸手到他面前取钱，转眼间，只剩下七叠。而摊路更坏，一缸青龙，一缸白虎，来回地甩，这名为“摇路”，又称“摇橹”，周五看准了，一下就在白虎上打了两万孤丁，另外在这一门上还有万把银子，假如庄家开个二，便得配九万银子，虽有三门可吃，为数极微，庄家面前的钱是不够输的。这是开配的责任，得要提醒庄家，但也有些庄家不爱听这罄其所有还不够配的话，所以刘不才有些踌躇。一抬眼恰好看到胡雪岩，不自觉略一皱眉，胡雪岩立刻便抛过一个阻止的眼色来。刘不才警觉了，嘴向庄家面前一努，随即恢复常态。“老刘！”庞二自己当然有个计算，问道：“怎么样？”这一问当然是问本钱够不够？刘不才不能给他泄气，但也不便大包大揽，说得太肯定，只这样含含糊糊地说：“开吧！”开开来是三，刘不才松了口气，等吃配完毕，只见庞家的听差，取了两张银票，悄悄往庞二面前一放。他看了看，略有诧异之色，欲言又止地点一点头，不知是表示会意，还是嘉许。“老五！”庞二看着周五说，“你打吧！我添本钱了，再添十万。”说也奇怪，一添本钱，手风便又不同，摊路变幻莫恻，专开注码少的那门。等四十摊摇完，结帐赢了七万银子。

The second half went less well. Liu kept reaching towards Pang for money, and before long only seven stacks remained. The sequence was worse still: a Green Dragon followed by a White Tiger, swinging back and forth. This was called a “swaying course,” or “rowing.” Zhou judged it ripe and placed twenty thousand taels on White Tiger as a single-number bet. Another ten thousand or so was already on that number. If the banker threw a two, he would have to pay ninety thousand taels. Although he could collect the stakes on the other three numbers, they were negligible. The money before him would not cover the loss. It was the settling clerk's responsibility to warn him, but some bankers disliked being told that even everything they had would not cover the payouts. Liu hesitated. Looking up, he happened to catch Hu's eye and frowned involuntarily. Hu immediately gave him a warning glance. Liu took the hint, indicated the money before Pang with his lips, and resumed his usual expression. “Old Liu, how is it?” Pang naturally had his own reckoning. He meant: was the capital enough? Liu must not discourage him, yet could not confidently undertake to cover everything. He merely answered, “Open it.” The result was three. Liu breathed freely. When collection and payment were complete, a servant of the Pang household quietly laid two banknotes before Pang. He glanced at them, looked slightly surprised, and nodded without speaking. Whether in understanding or approval, it was hard to tell. “Old Five,” he told Zhou, “go on betting. I've added more capital—another hundred thousand.” Strangely enough, the extra capital seemed to change his luck. The sequence became unpredictable, repeatedly favouring whichever number carried the smallest stake. When the forty rounds were over, the accounts showed a profit of seventy thousand taels.

接下来是周五做庄，也要求刘不才替他做开配，二十摊终了，看钟已是晚上八点，暂停吃饭。趁这空隙，庞二把刘不才找到书房里，打开抽屉，取出两个信纣，递了给他。刘不才不肯接，“庞二哥！”他问，“这是啥？”“你打开来看。”打开第一只信封，里面是三张银票，两张由阜康钱庄所出，每张五万，另外还有一张别家钱庄的，数目是五千。“老胡很够朋友，叫我听差送了十万银子约我添本钱，我用不着，不过盛情可感。五千银子算是彩，请你转交给他。”“雪岩不肯收的……”“你别管。”庞二打断他的话说，“只托你转交就是了。”刘不才也是大少爷出身，知道替胡雪岩辞谢，反拂他的意，便收了下来。

Zhou took the bank next and also asked Liu to settle the bets. After twenty rounds, they saw that it was eight in the evening and stopped for dinner. Pang used the interval to take Liu into his study. Opening a drawer, he brought out two envelopes and offered them to him. Liu would not take them. “Brother Pang, what are these?” “Open them and see.” The first held three banknotes. Two, issued by Fukang Bank, were for fifty thousand taels each; the third, from another bank, was for five thousand. “Old Hu is a true friend. He had my servant bring me a hundred thousand taels to add to my capital. I didn't need it, but I appreciate the kindness. The five thousand is a share of the luck. Please take it to him.” “Xueyan won't accept...” “Never mind that,” Pang interrupted. “I'm only asking you to deliver it.” Liu too had been brought up as a wealthy young master. He knew that refusing on Hu's behalf would offend Pang, so he accepted the envelope.

看第二只信封，里面是三万二千多两银子。“这是你的一份。”庞二解释，“原说四六成，我想还是‘南北开’的好。”刘不才当年豪赌的时候，也很少有一场赌三万银子进出的手面，而此时糊里糊涂的赢了这么一笔钱，有些不大能信其为真实，因而愣在那里，说不出话来。庞二不免觉得奇怪。他在想，莫非他意有不足？这个疑惑的念头，一起即灭，那是绝不会有的事！然则必是在想一句什么交代的话。这交代，并非道一声谢，就可以了事的，三万二千银子，不是小数目，庞二对自己能给人带来这么大的好处，已觉得很得意。当然还想再听两句“过瘾”的话，大少爷的脾气，就是这样。

The second envelope held more than thirty-two thousand taels in notes. “This is your share,” Pang explained. “We agreed on forty and sixty per cent, but I think an even division would be better.” Even in his former days of extravagant gambling, Liu had seldom played on a scale that involved thirty thousand taels in a single session. Now, almost without understanding how, he had won such a sum. He could scarcely believe it and stood speechless. Pang found this puzzling. Could Liu be dissatisfied? The suspicion vanished as soon as it arose. That was impossible. He must be considering what to say. A mere thank-you would hardly suffice: thirty-two thousand taels was no small sum. Pang already felt pleased with himself for being able to confer so great a benefit. Naturally, he wanted a few words that would give him further satisfaction. Such was the temperament of a wealthy young master.

刘不才的感动，不言可知，不过他倒也没有让这笔倘来之财，冲昏了头脑，心想，胡雪岩的意思，是要自己争取庞二的信任，最好还能叫他见自己的情。现在分到了这笔巨数，就得见人家的情了。再说，赌场里讲究的就是“现钱”两个字，当时讲好四六成比例合伙，就该先出本钱，把身上的三万银票交了过去，到此刻来分红，就毫无愧作了。虽然庞二是有名的阔少，不在乎此，但人家漂亮，自己也要漂亮，这才是平等相交的朋友，不然就成了抱粗腿的篾片，说话的分量，大不相同。道理是想通了，要交庞二这个朋友，要替胡雪岩办事，这笔钱就不能收。不收呢，到底是三万二千银子，加上前一天赢的一万多，要把“敬德堂”恢复起来，本钱也够了。因为出入关系太大，决心可真难下，但此时不容他从容考虑，咬一咬牙在心里说：铜钱银子用得光，要想交胡雪岩和庞二这样的朋友，今后未见得再有机会。

Liu was deeply moved, but the unexpected windfall had not turned his head. Hu wanted him to win Pang's confidence and, if possible, place Pang under some obligation to him. Accepting this enormous sum would put the obligation the other way round. Besides, ready money was what counted at a gambling table. Having agreed to a partnership on a forty-sixty basis, Liu should have supplied his capital first and handed over the thirty thousand taels in notes he carried. Then he could take his profit with a clear conscience. Pang was famously wealthy and might not care, but if the other man behaved generously, Liu must conduct himself handsomely too. Only then could they be friends on equal terms. Otherwise, he would become a hanger-on clinging to a powerful patron, and his words would carry quite a different weight. The principle was clear. To gain Pang's friendship and carry out Hu's business, he must refuse the money. Yet it was thirty-two thousand taels. Added to the ten thousand and more he had won the previous day, it would provide enough capital to restore Liu Jingde Hall. So much was at stake that the decision was agonising. There was no time for leisurely reflection. Gritting his teeth, he told himself that silver and copper could be spent to the last coin; another chance to make friends like Hu and Pang might never come.

于是他做出为难而歉然的神色，笑一笑说道：“庞二哥，你出手之阔是有名的，这等于送了我三万二千银子。我不收是不识抬举，收了心里实在不安。我想这样，做朋友不在一日。以后无论是在一起玩，还是干啥正经，总还有合伙的机会。这笔钱，我存在你这里。”说着，把那个信封放回庞二面前。“你……”庞二搔搔头皮，“没有这个道理！我们一笔了一笔，以后再说，无论一起玩，还是干啥正经，总有你一份就是了。”刘不才急忙拱手：“庞二哥说到这话，当我一个朋友，这就尽够了！来来，吃饭去！”一面说，一面走了出去。庞二无可奈何，只好在那个信封上写了“刘存”二字，藏入抽斗。

With an awkward, apologetic expression, Liu smiled. “Brother Pang, everyone knows how generous you are. This is as good as giving me thirty-two thousand taels. To refuse would be ungrateful, but to accept would leave me very uneasy. Let me suggest this. Friendship isn't a matter of one day. Whether we play together again or undertake some proper business, we'll have other chances to be partners. I'll leave this money on deposit with you.” He put the envelope back before Pang. “You...” Pang scratched his head. “That won't do! Let's settle one account at a time. Whatever we do later, gambling or business, there'll always be a share for you.” Liu hastily clasped his hands. “If you say that and regard me as a friend, Brother Pang, that's more than enough. Come, let's eat.” Speaking as he went, he walked out. Pang could only write “Liu's deposit” on the envelope and put it in the drawer.

等吃了饭再赌，刘不才觉得刚才那样做法，对胡雪岩的委托来说，已经做到，所以心无牵挂，全副精神摆在赌上，用“冷、准、狠”的三字诀，在周五所摇的二十摊中，只下了三次注，看准了“老宝”打两千银子的孤丁，赢了六千，连本带利再扑一记，变成一万八。第三记收起一万打八千，如果赢了，就是两千变成三万四，除去本钱，恰好是那辞谢未受的三万二千银子。结果吃掉了，周五的庄也做完了，刘不才赢了八千银子。以后换了推牌九，赌到天亮，没有什么进出，而刘不才觉得三四天工夫就赢了两万银子，大可知足。

When play resumed after dinner, Liu felt that his conduct had fulfilled Hu's commission. With that concern removed, he gave his whole mind to gambling, following the three rules of coolness, precision, and ruthlessness. During Zhou's twenty rounds, he bet only three times. Judging Old Treasure to be the right number, he staked two thousand taels on it alone and won six thousand. He put down capital and winnings together once more, and the sum became eighteen thousand. On the third bet, he set aside ten thousand and wagered eight thousand. If he won, his original two thousand would have become thirty-four thousand; after deducting the capital, the profit would exactly equal the thirty-two thousand he had declined. Instead, the stake was taken, and Zhou's turn as banker ended. Liu had won eight thousand taels. They changed to pai gow and played until dawn without any substantial change in his fortunes. Having won twenty thousand taels in three or four days, Liu felt he had every reason to be satisfied.

伸个懒腰，离开牌桌，走到窗前把窗帘拉开，顿觉强光炫目，闭一闭眼，再从那难得几家有的外国玻璃窗望出去，不由得讶然失声：“好大的雪！”“真是！赌得昏天黑地，”高四也说，“外面下这么大的雪都不知道。”“雪景倒真不坏！”刘不才望着弥望皆白的西湖说，“庞二哥这个庄子的地势真好，真正是洞天福地。”“你说好就不要走。”周五赌兴未已，“多的是客房，睡一觉起来，我们再盘肠大战。”刘不才遇到赌是从不推辞的，但此时想到胡雪岩的正事，而他本人又早已回城，必得跟他碰个头才谈得到其他，所以推说有个紧要约会，宁可回了城再来。“再来就不必了。”庞二说道，“今天歇一天吧！如果有兴，倒不妨逛一逛西湖，我派船到涌金门码头去等你们。”一听这话，周五先就将脖子一缩，“我可没有这个雅兴，”他说，“不如到我那里去吃火锅，吃完再赌一场。”“不行！”庞二笑道，“我这个地方，就是赏雪最好，我也学一学高人雅士，今天不想进城。”高四也说有事，还有几位客，都不开口，周五的提议，就此打消。在庞家吃了丰盛的早饭，各自坐轿进城。刘不才不回钱庄，直接到一家招牌叫“华清池”的澡堂，在滚烫的“大汤”中泡了一会，躺在软榻上叫人捶着腿便睡着了。

Stretching, he left the card table and drew back a window curtain. The sudden brightness dazzled him. He closed his eyes for a moment, then looked through the foreign glass, a luxury few households possessed. “What a snowfall!” he exclaimed. “Indeed!” Gao said. “We've gambled ourselves blind to the world. We didn't even know it was snowing so heavily.” “The view is magnificent.” Liu looked over West Lake, white as far as he could see. “Brother Pang's villa is wonderfully situated—a paradise.” “Then don't leave,” said Zhou, whose appetite for gambling remained unsatisfied. “There are plenty of guest rooms. Sleep a while, and we'll return to battle.” Ordinarily, Liu never refused a game. But now he remembered Hu's business. Hu had long since returned to the city, and he must meet him before considering anything else. He therefore pleaded an important appointment: he would rather return to town and come out again. “There's no need to come again,” Pang said. “Let's rest today. If you're interested, we could tour West Lake. I'll send a boat to wait at the Yongjin Gate wharf.” Zhou drew in his neck at once. “I haven't that refined taste. Better come to my place for hotpot, then another game.” “No!” Pang laughed. “This is the best place to admire the snow. I'll imitate the elegant scholars for once and stay out of town today.” Gao too said he had business, and several other guests said nothing. Zhou's proposal came to nothing. After a plentiful breakfast, they each took a sedan chair into the city. Liu went directly to a bathhouse called Huaqing Pool instead of returning to the bank. He soaked in its scalding communal bath, then lay on a padded couch and fell asleep while an attendant pounded his legs.

这一觉睡到下午两点才醒，还不想离开澡堂子，喊来一名跑堂，到馆子里，叫菜来吃饭，同时写了张条子，吩咐送到胡雪岩家，说明行踪，请来相会。等他说着一只十景生片火锅，喝完四两白干，正在吃饭时，胡雪岩到了，一见他便很注意的说：“ 你今天的气色特别好。想来得意？”“还不错。一切都很顺利。等我吃完这碗饭，再细谈。”刘不才说，“天气太冷，你先到池子里泡一泡。”于是胡雪岩解衣入池，等他回到座位，刘不才已很悠闲的在喝着茶等。炕几上摆着个信封，看上面写着两行字：“拜烦袖致雪岩老哥。”

He did not wake until two in the afternoon and still felt no wish to leave the bathhouse. He called an attendant to order dishes from a restaurant and, at the same time, wrote a note for delivery to Hu's house, giving his whereabouts and asking Hu to meet him. By the time Hu arrived, Liu had been enjoying a hotpot of ten varieties of sliced raw ingredients, had drunk four liang of strong clear spirits, and was eating his rice. Looking at him closely, Hu said, “You look particularly well today. Things must have gone your way.” “Quite well. Everything went smoothly. Let me finish this bowl of rice, then I'll tell you all about it. It's bitterly cold. Go and have a soak first.” Hu undressed and entered the pool. When he returned to his place, Liu was waiting at ease over his tea. An envelope lay on the low table, bearing two lines of writing: “Kindly deliver by hand to my elder friend Xueyan.”

“你昨天怎么不等庞二把摊摇完，就走了？”“我自然要先走，不然，到晚上‘叫城门’就麻烦了。”胡雪岩说，“我开了两张票子，带在身上，交是交了给庞二，号子里有没有这么多存款，还不知道，必得赶进城来布置好。”“亏得庞二不曾输掉，否则就麻烦了。”刘不才这时倒有不寒而栗之感，“你想，我说了跟他四六成合伙，倘或连你这十万一起输光，就是二十万。我派四成，得要八万，划个帐，找两万银子。十万剩了两万，险呀！这种事下次做不得了。““你也知道做不得！”胡雪岩笑道，“你在场上赌，等于我在场外赌。不过我这场外赌，无论输赢，都是合算的。““赢了是格外合算。你看！”刘不才把信封推了给他，说明经过。

“Why did you leave yesterday before Pang finished his turn?” Liu asked. “Naturally, I had to leave early. Getting the city gate opened at night would have been troublesome. I had two notes drawn up and carried them with me. I'd handed them to Pang, but I didn't know whether the bank had enough deposits to cover them. I had to hurry back into town and make the arrangements.” “Fortunately, Pang didn't lose the money. Otherwise, there would have been trouble.” Liu shuddered at the thought. “I'd agreed to a forty-sixty partnership. If he had lost everything, including your hundred thousand, that would have been two hundred thousand. My forty per cent would be eighty thousand. After offsetting the accounts, there would be only twenty thousand to give back. A hundred thousand reduced to twenty thousand! What a risk. We must never do that again.” “So you know it shouldn't be done!” Hu laughed. “You were gambling at the table, and I was gambling outside it. But my gamble would have been worthwhile whether you won or lost.” “Winning made it especially worthwhile. Look!” Liu pushed the envelope towards him and explained what had happened.

胡雪岩这时才打开信封，把他自己的两张银票收了起来，扬着庞二的那张五千两的银票说：“我当然不能要他这五千银子，但也不便退回。只有一个办法，用他的名义，捐给善堂。昨天夜里一场大雪，起码有二三十具‘倒路尸’，我钱庄里已经舍了四口棺材了。”“ ‘做好事’应该！我也捐一千银子。”“算了，算了！”胡雪岩不便说他有了钱，“大少爷脾气”就会发作，只这样阻止：“你要做好事，也该到湖州去做！杭州有我，不劳你费心。”刘不才有些发觉了，略显窘色地笑道：“其实我也要别人来做好事，自己哪里有这个资格。”“闲话少说。”胡雪岩说，“这里不是说话的地方，到舍间去谈。”

Only then did Hu open the envelope. He put away his own two banknotes and held up Pang's note for five thousand taels. “Naturally, I can't take this five thousand. But it would be awkward to return it. There's only one course: donate it to a charitable institution in his name. After last night's heavy snow, there must be at least twenty or thirty bodies lying in the roads. My bank has already given four coffins.” “Good works are only right. I'll give a thousand taels too.” “No, no!” Hu could hardly say that, once Liu had money, his wealthy young master's habits would return. Instead he checked him with, “If you want to do good works, you should do them in Huzhou. Hangzhou has me; you needn't trouble yourself.” Liu sensed something of his meaning and smiled a little awkwardly. “In truth, I need other people to do good works for me. What standing have I to do them myself?” “Enough of that. This isn't the place to talk. Let's go to my house.”

于是两个人穿衣起身。刘不才是第一次到胡家，想到他侄女儿，有些心事重重的模样，他不知道胡雪岩在湖州另立门户，胡太太是不是知道？倘或知道，自己的身分不免尴尬，因而便有畏缩之意。但转念又觉得这是机会，可以看看胡太太为人如何？将来跟芙蓉是不是相处得来？就这样踌躇着，走出华清池时，脚步就懒了。胡雪岩回身一望，从他的脸色，猜到他的心里，觉得必须交代一句。“三叔，”他说，“在湖州的事，见了内人，不必提起。”这句话解消了刘不才心里的一个疙瘩，脑筋就变得灵活了。“那么，”他提醒他说：“你也不能叫我三叔！脱口出来，就露了马脚。”“不要紧。倘或内人问起来，我只说我先认识你侄儿，跟着小辈叫，也是有的。”“算了，你叫我别样。我也不想做你的长辈，宁愿做朋友。”“是的！刘三爷。”这是“官称”，刘不才欣然同意。一起坐轿到了胡家，拜见胡雪岩的母亲和妻子，刘不才口称“伯母”、“大嫂”。看这位“胡大嫂”人虽精明，极顾“外场”，不是那种蛮不讲理的悍泼妇人，刘不才替芙蓉放了一半心。

They dressed and got up to leave. This would be Liu's first visit to Hu's home, and the thought of his niece made him uneasy. Did Mrs Hu know that her husband had established another household in Huzhou? If she did, Liu's position would be awkward. He felt apprehensive. Then he reflected that this was an opportunity to see what sort of woman she was and whether she and Furong might get on in future. Torn between these thoughts, he lagged as they left Huaqing Pool. Hu looked back, read his expression, and realised that he must say something. “Third Uncle, when you meet my wife, there's no need to mention Huzhou.” This removed one obstacle from Liu's mind, and his thoughts began to move freely again. “Then you mustn't call me Third Uncle either. If it slips out, you'll give yourself away.” “That doesn't matter. If she asks, I'll say I knew your nephew first and followed the younger generation's form of address. That happens often enough.” “Never mind. Call me something else. I don't want to be your elder anyway. I'd rather be your friend.” “Certainly, Third Master Liu.” This was his formal social title, and he readily accepted it. They took sedan chairs to Hu's house and paid their respects to his mother and wife. Liu addressed them as Auntie and Sister-in-Law. He found that Mrs Hu, though shrewd, had a proper regard for social appearances and was no unreasonable shrew. Half his anxiety on Furong's behalf disappeared.

于是围炉把酒，胡雪岩开始谈到庞二，“你晓得的，我现在顶要紧的一笔生意，是上海的丝。”他说，“我既然托了你，以后也还要共事，我不必瞒你，年关快到了，各处的帐目要结，应该开销的要开销，上海那批丝，非脱手不可。““嗯，嗯！”刘不才生长在湖州，耳濡目染，对销洋庄的丝，自然也颇了解，“现在价钱不错呀！不如早早脱手。摆到明年，丝一变黄，再加新丝上市，你就要吃大亏了。”“是的，眼前的价钱虽不错，不过还可以卖得好，说句你不相信的话，价钱可以由我开。”“有这样的好事！”刘不才真的有些不信，反问一句，“那你还在这里做啥？赶紧到上海去呀！”“对！就这几天，我一定要动身。现在只等庞二的一句话。”这一句话就是要取得庞二的承诺，他在上海跟洋商做丝的交易，跟胡雪岩采取同样的步骤，胡雪岩已经得到极机密的消息，江苏的督抚，已经联衔出奏，因为在上海租界中的洋人，不断以军械粮食接济刘丽川，决定采取封锁的措施，断绝内地也洋人的贸易，迫使其转向“助顺”。这一来，丝茶两项，来源都会断绝，在上海的存货，洋人一定会尽量搜购，只要能够“垄断”，自然可以“居奇”。

Sitting round the brazier with wine, Hu began to discuss Pang. “As you know, the most important business before me is the silk in Shanghai. Since I've entrusted this matter to you and we shall be working together, there's no need to conceal anything. The year's end is approaching. Accounts must be settled everywhere, and expenses must be met. That silk in Shanghai has to be sold.” “Yes, yes.” Growing up in Huzhou had naturally given Liu considerable knowledge of silk for the foreign market. “Prices are good now. Better sell early. If you keep it until next year, the silk will yellow, and the new crop will come on the market. You'll lose heavily.” “Exactly. Prices are good now, but they could be better. You may not believe this, but I can name my own price.” “As good as that?” Liu was indeed doubtful. “Then what are you doing here? Get to Shanghai at once!” “Quite right. I must leave within the next few days. I'm only waiting for Pang's word.” That word was a promise that, in selling silk to foreign merchants in Shanghai, Pang would follow the same course as Hu. Hu had received highly confidential news: Jiangsu's governor-general and governor had submitted a joint memorial. Because foreigners in the Shanghai settlement were continually supplying Liu Lichuan with arms and provisions, they had decided on a blockade, cutting off trade between the interior and the foreigners to force them to support the imperial side. Supplies of both silk and tea would then cease. Foreigners would buy as much of the stock in Shanghai as possible. Whoever could control the supply could naturally hold it for an exceptional price.

“原来如此！”刘不才很有把握地说，“这庞二一定会答应的，挑他赚钱，何乐而不为？”“话不是这么说。”胡雪岩大摇其头，“你不要把事情看得太容易！”刘不才是不大肯买帐的性格，“我倒不相信！”他说，“宠二没有不答应的道理。”“凭交情，自然会答应。交情不够就难说了。你要晓得。第一，他跟洋人做了多年的交易，自然也有交情，有时不能不迁就，第二，在商场上，这有面子的关系，说起来庞二做丝生意，要听我胡某人的指挥。象他这样的身分，这句话怎么肯受？”想想果然！刘不才又服帖了，笑着说道：“你的脑筋是与众不同。这样一说，我倒还真得小心才好。”“对了！话有个说法。”胡雪岩接下来便教了他一套话。

“So that's it!” Liu said confidently. “Pang will certainly agree. You're helping him make money. Why wouldn't he?” “It isn't as simple as that.” Hu shook his head emphatically. “Don't take it too lightly.” Liu was not easily convinced. “I don't believe it. There's no reason for him to refuse.” “With enough friendship, naturally he'll agree. Without it, one can't be sure. First, he's traded with foreigners for years and has friendships among them. Sometimes he must accommodate them. Second, there's his standing in business. People would say Pang has to take instructions from me about his silk. How could a man in his position accept that?” On reflection, this was true. Liu yielded again and smiled. “Your mind works differently from other people's. Put like that, I really must be careful.” “Exactly. There's a way of saying things.” Hu then taught him how to put the proposal.

刘不才心领神会的点头，因为休戚相关的缘故，不免又问：“万一你倒扳价不放，洋人看看不划算，做不成交易，岂非枉做恶人？而且对庞二也不好交代！”“不会的！”胡雪岩答道，“外国的丝，本来出在叫做意大利的一个国度，法兰西也有。前个六八年，这两个国度里的蚕，起了蚕瘟，蚕种死了一大半，所以全要靠中国运丝去。原料不够，外国的丝厂、机坊都要关门，多少人的生计在那里！他们非买我们的丝不可，羊毛出在羊身上，水涨船高，又不亏洋丝商的本，怕什么！”“你连外国的行情都晓得！”刘不才颇有闻所未闻之感，“怪不得人家的生意做不过你。”“好了，好了！你不要恭维我了。”胡雪岩笑道，“这些话留着跟庞二去说。”

Liu nodded with full understanding. Since their fortunes were linked, however, he could not help asking, “What if you hold firmly to your price and the foreigners decide it isn't worth their while? If no sale takes place, won't you have made yourself unpleasant for nothing? And how would you explain it to Pang?” “That won't happen. Foreign silk originally came from a country called Italy, and from France too. Six or eight years ago, a disease struck their silkworms and killed more than half their stock. So they depend entirely on silk shipped from China. Without enough raw material, their silk mills and weaving workshops must close. Think how many people's livelihoods depend on them! They have to buy our silk. The wool comes from the sheep, and boats rise with the water: the foreign silk merchants won't lose their own capital. What is there to fear?” “You even know the foreign market!” Much of this was entirely new to Liu. “No wonder other people can't compete with you.” “Enough! Stop flattering me,” Hu laughed. “Save those words for Pang.”

刘不才如言受教，第二夭专诚去访庞二，一见面先拿他恭维一顿，说他做生意有魄力，手段厉害。接着便谈到胡雪岩愿意拥护他做个“头脑”的话。“雪岩的意思是，洋人这几年越来越精明，越来越刁，看准有些户头急于脱货求现，故意杀价。一家价钱做低了，别家要想抬价不容易，所以，想请你出来登高一呼，号召同行，齐心来对付洋人！”“是啊！我也想到过，就是心不齐。原是为大家好，哪晓得人家倒象是求他似地。”庞二摇摇头，叹口气。“唉！我何苦舒服日子不过，要吃力不讨好，自己给自己找气来受！”“你是大少爷出身，从出娘胎，也没有受过气，自然做不来这种仰面求人的事。雪岩也知道，他只请你出面为头，靠你的地位号召，事情归他去做。”“这也不敢当！”庞二答道，“老胡这样捧我，实在当不起。”这话就要辨辨味道了，可能是真心话，也可能是推托。如果是推托，原因何在？刘不才这样想着，一面口中恭维，一面在细察庞二的脸色。这是刘不才有阅历的地方！庞二果然是假客气的话，他对胡雪岩虽颇欣赏，但相知不深，对于胡雪岩一下子如跳龙门似地，由穷小子闯出这样的手面，其间的传奇，也听人约略谈过，认为他实力毕竟有限，深恐他弄什么玄虚，存着戒心。

Following Hu's advice, Liu made a special call on Pang the next day. He began by praising Pang's boldness and ability in business, then explained that Hu wished to support him as their leader. “Xueyan thinks the foreigners have grown shrewder and more difficult these last few years. They see that some dealers need to sell quickly for cash and deliberately force down prices. Once one firm's price falls, it's hard for the others to raise theirs. So he'd like you to give the call and bring the trade together to deal with the foreigners.” “Yes, I've thought of that myself. The trouble is that people won't act together. I mean it for everyone's benefit, but they behave as though I were begging a favour.” Pang shook his head and sighed. “Why should I give up a comfortable life for thankless work and invite vexation?” “You were born a young master and have never had to put up with an insult. Naturally, you can't go humbly begging others. Xueyan understands that. He asks only that you stand at the head and lend the movement your prestige. He'll do the work.” “I couldn't accept such an honour,” Pang answered. “Old Hu gives me far too much credit.” The words needed careful interpretation. They might be sincere, or merely an excuse. If an excuse, why? As he continued to flatter Pang, Liu studied his expression closely. Here his experience proved useful. Pang's modesty was indeed a pretence. He admired Hu, but they did not know each other well. He had heard something of the remarkable story of Hu's rise, like a leap through the Dragon Gate, from a poor young fellow to a man who could operate on such a scale. Yet he thought Hu's real resources must still be limited. Afraid there might be some trick behind the proposal, he remained on his guard.

说到后来，刘不才有些着急了，“庞二哥，承蒙你看得起我，一见如故，所以雪岩托我这件事，我一口答应。现在你一再谦虚，似乎当我外人看待。”说到这里，发觉自己的态度，有些过分，便笑一笑说，“好了，好了！庞二哥，我不管这桩闲事了，我请你到‘江山船’上吃花酒去。”最后这一转很好，庞二觉得刘不才很够朋友，自己虽存着猜疑之心，他却依旧当自己好朋友，这很难得。就一转念之间，心便软了，觉得无论如何要有个交代，于是这样笑道：“老刘，你不要气急！不看僧面看佛面，你第一趟跟我谈正经事，又是为彼此的利益，我怎么能不买你的帐？不过，我也说句实话，象这样的事，做好了没有人感激，做坏了，同行的闲话很多。中国人的脑筋比外国人好，就是私心太重，所以我不敢冒昧出头。现在这样，我跟老胡先谈一谈再说，能做我一定做，决不会狗皮倒灶。你看好不好？”“哪还有不好的道理？你说，你们在哪里谈？”“今天我还有一个约，没有空了，就明天吧。”庞二又说，“你不是要请我吃花酒吗？我们就在江山船上谈好了。”“一言为定。明天请你江山船上吃花酒，我发帖子来。”

As the conversation continued, Liu grew anxious. “Brother Pang, you've been kind enough to take to me as an old friend from the moment we met. That's why I immediately agreed when Xueyan asked me to undertake this. Now you keep declining so modestly that you seem to regard me as an outsider.” Realising that he had gone a little too far, he smiled. “Never mind! I won't meddle in this any longer. Let me invite you to drink with the courtesans on a Jiangshan pleasure boat.” The final turn was well judged. Pang felt that Liu was a true friend. Though Pang had remained suspicious, Liu still treated him warmly. That was rare. The thought softened him, and he felt he must give some definite answer. “Old Liu, don't be impatient. If not for the monk, then for the Buddha: this is the first time you've brought me a business proposal, and it benefits both sides. How could I refuse you consideration? But let me speak frankly. If such a scheme succeeds, nobody thanks you. If it fails, the trade will have plenty to say. Chinese minds are sharper than foreign ones; the trouble is excessive self-interest. That's why I dare not rush to take the lead. Let me talk to Old Hu first. If it can be done, I'll certainly do it. I won't be a tiresome obstruction. Will that suit you?” “How could it not? Where shall you meet?” “I've another appointment today and no time. Tomorrow, then. Didn't you offer me a courtesans' drinking party? We can talk on the Jiangshan boat.” “Agreed. Tomorrow I'll entertain you there. I'll send an invitation.”

“这不必了。你是用哪家的船？”庞二对此道也很熟悉，“顶好的是小金桂的船，只怕定出去了。其次就是‘何仙姑’的船。”“好，不是小金桂，就是何仙姑。事不宜迟，我马上去办。定好了船，还是发帖子来。”“好，好，我听你招呼。”庞二又说，“人不宜太多，略微清静些，好谈正事。”刘不才答应着告辞而去。进城直接去找胡雪岩，细说了经过，表示佩服胡雪岩有先见之明，果然事情不那么容易，又说他未能圆满达成任务，深感歉疚。“这是哪里的话！”胡雪岩安慰他说，“有这样一个结果，依我看，已经非常好了。”“那么，预备怎么跟他谈呢？”“那自然要临机应变。看样子，他是跟我初次共事，还不大能够相信。”胡雪岩又说，“这件事即使做不成功，我以后跟他合作的日子还有。所以，三爷，倘或事情谈不拢，你不必摆在心上，好象觉得对不起我，他不够朋友。你要一切照常，一点不在乎。你懂我意思不懂？““当然懂！”刘不才深深点头，“这个朋友是长朋友。”“对了！”胡雪岩极欣慰的，“说这话，你是真的懂了。”

“No need for that. Whose boat will you use?” Pang knew this world well too. “Xiao Jingui's is the best, but it may already be booked. Next best is He Xiangu's.” “Then one of those two. There's no time to lose. I'll arrange it now. Once I've secured the boat, I'll send an invitation all the same.” “Very well. I'll await your word. But don't invite too many people. We want some quiet to discuss business.” Liu agreed and took his leave. Returning to the city, he went directly to Hu and recounted the conversation. He admired Hu's foresight: the business had indeed proved difficult. He also apologised deeply for failing to complete his mission satisfactorily. “What are you saying?” Hu reassured him. “To my mind, this is already an excellent result.” “How will you approach him, then?” “Naturally, I'll adapt as we talk. It seems he doesn't quite trust me yet, since this would be our first undertaking together. Even if this one doesn't succeed, there will be other opportunities for us to work together. So, Third Master, if we can't agree, don't take it to heart as though you'd failed me or he weren't a good friend. Treat everything as usual and think nothing of it. Do you understand?” “Of course.” Liu nodded earnestly. “This is a friendship for the long term.” “Exactly!” Hu was deeply pleased. “That shows you truly understand.”

于是，刘不才告辞回去，托刘庆生派人定了小金桂的船，又发帖子，整整忙了一下午，才算诸事就绪。哪知到了夜里，突然接到庞二的信，说他接到家报，第二天必须赶回甫浔，花酒之约，只得辞谢，胡雪岩的事，希望即晚谈一谈，在何处见面，立等回音。信是由庞家的听差送来的，刘不才打听了一下，才知道庞二闹家务，看起来他的心境不会好，对胡雪岩的事，自然也不会感觉兴趣，谈与不谈已经无关宏旨了。不过想到“长朋友”这句话，刘不才觉得对庞二应有一番慰问之意，因此告诉庞家的听差，说他马上约了胡雪岩去拜访。等庞家的听差一走，刘不才接着也赶到了胡家，相见之下，说了经过，胡雪岩大为皱眉，沉吟了好半晌，倏地起身，成竹在胸似地说：“走吧！船到桥头自然直。”坐轿出城，见着了庞二，胡雪岩发觉他眉宇之间，隐然有忧色，便不谈自己的事，只问庞二有何急事，要赶回家去？“我叫人告到官里了！”庞二很坦率地回答，“这一趟回去，说不定要对簿公堂。”“不幸之至。”胡雪岩问道，“到底为了什么？”“ 这话说来太长，总之，族中有人见我境遇还过得去，无理取闹。花几个钱倒不在乎，这口气忍不下去。”

Liu took his leave and asked Liu Qingsheng to send someone to book Xiao Jingui's boat. He also sent the invitations. The preparations occupied the whole afternoon. That night, unexpectedly, a letter arrived from Pang. He had received news from home and must return to Nanxun the following day, so he could no longer attend the party. He hoped to discuss Hu's business that very evening and was waiting for an immediate reply about where they should meet. The letter came by Pang's servant. After a few inquiries, Liu learned that there was trouble within Pang's family. He would hardly be in good spirits or interested in Hu's affairs; whether they discussed the proposal now seemed to matter little. But remembering their “friendship for the long term,” Liu felt he should offer some sympathy. He told the servant that he would fetch Hu and call on Pang immediately. As soon as the servant left, Liu hurried to Hu's house and explained. Hu frowned deeply and considered for some time, then suddenly rose as though his plan were settled. “Let's go. The boat will straighten itself when it reaches the bridge.” They took chairs out of the city. On seeing Pang, Hu detected worry in his expression. Instead of raising his own business, he asked what urgent matter required Pang to return home. “Someone has brought a case against me,” Pang answered frankly. “I may have to appear in court.” “Most unfortunate. What is it about?” “That's a long story. In short, some members of my clan see that I'm fairly comfortably off and are making unreasonable trouble. I don't mind spending money. It's the insult I can't swallow.”

一听这话，就知道无非族人夺产，事由不明，无法为他出什么主意，只好这样相劝：“庞二哥，讼则终凶，惟和为贵。”“和也要和得下来。”庞二摇摇头，“唉！不必谈了。”庞二不谈，胡雪岩却不能不谈，也不可不谈，因为他可以帮庞二的忙，“如果你愿意和，我包你和得下来。”胡雪岩说，“庞二哥，打官司你不必担心！只要理直，包赢不输，不过俗话说得好：富不跟穷斗。你的官司就打赢了也没有什么意思。”“啊！”庞二突然双眼发亮，“对了，你跟王大老爷是好朋友。这个忙可以帮我。”“当然。”胡雪岩说，“我先陪你走一趟。你的事要紧，我上海的事只好摆着再说了。”这是以退为进的说法，庞二被提醒了，他是阔少的作风，遇到这些地方，最拿得出决断，“老胡！”他说，“你上海的事不要紧，都在我身上。你说，要我怎么样？”

Hu understood at once that the clansmen were after Pang's property. Without the particulars, he could offer no specific advice. He could only urge, “Brother Pang, lawsuits end badly. Peace is best.” “Peace must be possible first.” Pang shook his head. “Never mind. Let's not discuss it.” But Hu could not let the subject drop, nor should he: he could help. “If you're willing to settle, I guarantee a settlement. You needn't fear a lawsuit, Brother Pang. If right is on your side, I guarantee you'll win. But the saying is sound: the rich shouldn't fight the poor. Even winning your case would bring little satisfaction.” “Ah!” Pang's eyes suddenly brightened. “Of course! You're a close friend of Prefect Wang. You can help me.” “Naturally. I'll go with you first. Your affairs are urgent. My business in Shanghai will have to wait.” This was a retreat intended to gain ground. Reminded of Hu's business, Pang showed the decisiveness of a wealthy young master. “Old Hu, don't worry about Shanghai. Leave it all to me. Tell me what you want me to do.”

“刘三爷跟你大致已经谈过了。我就是想庞二哥来出面，我劝同行齐心一致，由我陪你去跟洋人谈判。”“我是没有空来办这件事了。”庞二问道，“你在上海有多少丝？”“我有两万包。”“那就行了。我跟你加在一起，已经占到百分之七十，实力尽够了。你跟洋人会谈，我把我的栈单交了给你，委托你代我去做交易，你说怎么就怎么。这样总行了吧？”得到这样一个结果，胡雪岩喜出望外。有庞二的全权委托，不但对洋商的交易，可以顺利达成，而且自己的声望，立刻就会升高。但好事来得太容易，反令人有不安之感，他不敢有得意的神色，“庞二哥，你这个委任重了！”他戒慎恐惧的说：“我怕万一搞得灰头土脸，对你不好交代。”“不会的！”庞二答道：“我听老刘谈过了，你对丝不外行。就请你记住一句话，‘顺风旗不要扯得太足’，自然万无一失。”“是的，”胡雪岩衷心受教，“我照你的话去做。价钱方面，我总还要跟你商量的，不会独断独行。”

“Third Master Liu has told you the general idea. I'd like you to take the lead. I'll persuade the trade to act together, then accompany you to negotiate with the foreigners.” “I won't have time to handle it myself. How much silk have you in Shanghai?” “Twenty thousand bales.” “Then that's enough. Yours and mine together already account for seventy per cent. We have ample strength. You negotiate with the foreigners. I'll hand you my warehouse receipts and authorise you to sell on my behalf. Whatever you decide goes. Surely that will do?” The result surpassed Hu's hopes. With Pang's full authority, he could not only bring the transaction with the foreign merchants to a successful conclusion but immediately raise his own standing. Yet such easily won good fortune made him uneasy. He dared show no triumph. “Brother Pang, you're placing a heavy responsibility on me. I'm afraid that if I make a mess of it, I shan't know how to face you.” “You won't. Old Liu has told me about you. You know the silk trade. Remember just one thing: don't stretch your banner too far in a favourable wind. Then nothing can go wrong.” “Yes,” Hu answered with sincere respect. “I'll follow your advice. As for the price, I shall still consult you. I won't decide everything on my own.”

“不必，你看着办好了。至于回扣……”“不，不！”胡雪岩急忙摇手，“你这么捧我，我决不能再要回扣。原是你自己可以谈的事，怎么好损失回扣？我晓得你为人大方，不过你手下也有一般‘朋友’，叫他们背后说你的闲后，变得我对不起你了。”听这一说，庞二越觉得胡雪岩“落门落槛”，是做生意可以倾心合作的人。别人漂亮，他更不肯马虎，坚持一定要送，胡雪岩也作了很肯定的表示，倘或庞二一定要送，他不能不收，只是除了必要的开支以外，余数他要送庞二手下的“朋友”。“那随你，我就不管了。”庞二又说，“今天晚上我就写信通知上海，把栈单给你送去，送到哪里？”“不是这么做法，只请你写封委托信给我，同时请你通知宝号的档手，说明经过。栈单不必交给我。”

“No need. Use your own judgement. As for your commission...” “No, no!” Hu hastily waved his hands. “After such support, I couldn't take a commission as well. You could have handled the transaction yourself. Why should you lose that amount? I know you're generous, but you have ‘friends’ working under you. If they criticised you behind your back, I should have done you a disservice.” Pang felt still more strongly that Hu understood how things should be done and was a man with whom he could cooperate wholeheartedly. If the other man behaved handsomely, he would not be careless in return. He insisted on paying. Hu was equally definite: if Pang insisted, he could not refuse, but after deducting necessary expenses, he would give the balance to Pang's “friends.” “That's up to you. I won't interfere. I'll write to Shanghai tonight and have the warehouse receipts sent to you. Where should they go?” “Not that way. Please just give me a letter of authorisation and notify your firm's manager of the arrangement. The receipts needn't be handed to me.”

这样做，亦无不可。谈完胡雪岩的事，庞二谈他自己的事。照胡雪岩的想法，上海那方面的生意，他可以托人代办，自己该陪着庞二到湖州，去替他料理官司。刘不才也在旁边帮腔，说胡雪岩对这种徘难解纷的事，最为擅长，此行少不得他。但唯其如此，庞二反倒顾虑了。“老胡！有你出大力帮忙，这件事，我现在就可以放心，至多惹几天麻烦，花几吊银子，没有什么大不了的。不过，我不愿意落个仗势欺人的名声。你陪了我去，好是好，就只一样不妥，湖州好些人都知道你跟王大老爷是知交，看你出面，明明王大老爷秉公办理，别人说起来，总是我走了门路。“庞二停了一下又说，“这一来不但我不愿意，对王大老爷的官声也不好。”听了这番话，胡雪岩心想，谁说庞二是不懂事的纨袴，谁就是有眼无珠的草包，因而心悦诚服的答说：“庞二哥看事情，真正透彻！既然如此，我全听吩咐。”“不敢当！”庞二说道：“我只请你切切实实的替我写封信，我也是备而不用。”“好的。我的信要写两封，一封给王雪公，一封给刑幕秦老夫子，此人我也是有交情的，庞二哥有什么难处，尽管跟他商量。”

That arrangement was equally acceptable. With Hu's business settled, they returned to Pang's. Hu thought he could entrust the Shanghai transaction to someone else and accompany Pang to Huzhou to help with the lawsuit. Liu supported him, saying that Hu excelled at resolving disputes and was indispensable to the journey. Yet this very argument made Pang hesitate. “Old Hu, with your full help, I can already set my mind at rest. At worst there'll be a few troublesome days and a few strings of cash to spend. Nothing very serious. But I don't want a reputation for using influence to bully people. It would be good to have you with me, except for one thing. Many people in Huzhou know you're a close friend of Prefect Wang. If you appear, they'll say I used connections even if he handles the case with complete impartiality.” After a pause, Pang added, “I wouldn't like that, and it would harm his official reputation too.” Hearing this, Hu thought that anyone who called Pang an ignorant rich idler was a blind fool himself. With heartfelt admiration, he replied, “You see matters very clearly indeed, Brother Pang. In that case, I'll do as you wish.” “You flatter me. All I ask is that you write me a proper, substantial letter, to keep in reserve rather than use.” “Certainly. I'll write two: one to Prefect Wang and one to Secretary Qin, his legal secretary. I'm friendly with him too. If you have any difficulty, consult him freely.”

“这是文的一面，还有武的一面。”刘不才插嘴问庞二：“郁四，你认不认识？”“认是认得，交情不深。”庞二答道：“说句实话，这些江湖朋友，我不大敢惹。”“这个人也是‘备而不用’好了。”胡雪岩说，“信我也是照写，其实不写也不要紧，郁四听见是庞二哥的事，不敢不尽心。”这是胡雪岩拿高帽子往庞二头上戴，意思是以庞家的名望，郁四自然要巴结。只是恭维得不肉麻，庞二听了非常舒服，心里在想，他们杭州人的俗语，“花花轿儿人抬人”，胡雪岩越是如此说，就越要买他的面子。“老胡，听你这一说，郁四跟你的交情一定不错。你的朋友就是我的朋友，我这趟回湖州，倒要交他一交，请你替我写介绍信。”“一句话！”胡雪岩起身告辞，“你就要走了，总还有些事要料理，我不耽搁你的工夫，明天一早，我把信送来。”这天晚上胡雪岩备下三封极其切实的信，第二天一早带到庞二那里。投桃报李，他交给胡雪岩的两封信也很实在，一封是委托书，一封是写给他在上海的管事的，特意不封口，请胡雪岩代发，意思是让他过了目，好放心。这使得胡雪岩对庞二又有深一层的了解，做事不但豪爽，而且过节上的交代，一丝不苟，十分漂亮。

“That's the formal side. There's the forceful side too.” Liu asked Pang, “Do you know Yu Si?” “I know him, but not well. Frankly, I don't much care to get involved with such men of the streets and waterways.” “Keep him in reserve too,” Hu said. “I'll write him a letter as well, though it scarcely matters. Once Yu hears that it's your business, Brother Pang, he won't dare spare any effort.” Hu was flattering him, implying that the Pang family's prestige would naturally make Yu eager to oblige. The compliment was subtle enough to give Pang great pleasure. He thought of the Hangzhou saying: people take turns carrying one another's decorated chairs. The more Hu spoke like this, the more Pang ought to honour him. “Old Hu, from what you say, you and Yu must be good friends. Your friends are mine. When I return to Huzhou, I should like to get to know him. Please write me an introduction.” “Consider it done.” Hu rose to leave. “You're going tomorrow and must still have things to arrange. I won't take up your time. I'll bring the letters early in the morning.” That night, Hu prepared three letters, all giving firm and practical support, and took them to Pang the next morning. Returning favour for favour, Pang gave him two equally substantial letters: one was an authorisation; the other was addressed to his manager in Shanghai. He had deliberately left the latter unsealed and asked Hu to send it, meaning that Hu should read it first and be reassured. This gave Hu a deeper understanding of Pang. He was not only generous and forthright; in attending to every detail of an arrangement, he was scrupulous and admirable.

称谓与场景

刘不才 Liu Bucai，叙述简称 Liu；庞二 Pang Er，承接前批已介绍的人物，在本批简称 Pang，直呼庞二哥为 Brother Pang。周五与高四的排行前批已说明，本批简称 Zhou、Gao；庞二直呼周五“老五”时用 Old Five。胡雪岩称刘“三叔”为 Third Uncle；为不在妻子面前暴露湖州另立门户的关系，改称刘三爷 Third Master Liu，保留这一特意换称的情节。“刘庆生”写 Liu Qingsheng，不与刘不才混同。
敬德堂沿用 Liu Jingde Hall；芙蓉 Furong；华清池 Huaqing Pool；葛岭 Geling Hill；涌金门 Yongjin Gate；南浔 Nanxun。江山船按此处花酒场景写 Jiangshan pleasure boat；小金桂 Xiao Jingui、何仙姑 He Xiangu 为船主/应酬场景中的名称，不把后者改写成神仙亲自出现。刑幕秦老夫子为 Secretary Qin，身份是 legal secretary；“王雪公”在此沿用 Prefect Wang，并非另一个姓王人物。
warehouse receipt 为栈单，manager 为档手/管事；letter of authorisation 为本段委托信/委托书。回扣在这里是交易代理报酬，英文用 commission，不误写成官员受贿。庞二手下的“朋友”保留引号，指其属下相关人员，而非无关的社交朋友。

牌局叙述与不明处

牌九沿用 pai gow，麻将 mahjong，庄为 bank/banker；本批“摇摊”用 dice-shaking game。开配用 settling clerk，指负责记录下注、收取输注和赔付的助手，不与坐庄者混同。“孤丁”为 single-number bet；角为 corner，横堂为 across the hall，大头为 big head，放鹞子为 flying a kite。只按源文给出的范围和规则说明，不补未出现的玩法。
桌面一、二、三、四的位置、青龙角、两色木材、象牙镶嵌、牙筹四寸、堂号和五等筹值都保留。四寸用 four cun，未换成四英寸。“世载堂庞”译为 Pang of Shizai Hall；天、地、人、和为对应骨牌标记，未额外列其点数。前三次亮摊、第四次后正式下注，以及“十二点是四”的叙述均照录；不自行补骰子数量或取余公式。
青龙在该桌面对应四，白虎对应二；“二带么”的大头里，白虎赢两倍、进门算和气，按原文译出。老宝在本批没有明确解释对应哪门，保留 Old Treasure，不猜成某一具体点数。大路、小路、荤路、素路是原文列出的路数称呼，分别保留 main、minor、meat、vegetable 的词面差异；小说未进一步解释其画法，译文也不杜撰。
“放鹞子”押三门、“吃二配三”、钱上以三赌一、骰面三赢一输及断线的形容全部保留。赔付词语是否包含原注，小说未统一明说，因此英文未另加一套计算公式。改注码只口头宣布而不搬筹码、开配必须记住、不足万两留手边、满万摆到庄前以及防开配私吞的用意，均保留。
15959滚注数字照录：初注二千，“赢了六千”；连本带利再押后“变成一万八”；第三次留一万、押八千；若赢则称总额三万四、除本净得三万二，实际该注输了，最后净赢八千。文本关于“赢”“配”及含本口径不足以支持另定算法；本译不把六千擅改为八千，也不补写第二注的确切下注数，更不据假定赔率重算作者数字。

金额、单位与承诺

刘最初约占四成、庞占六成，刘虽带三万银票，却未先交本。庞原有十万本钱，胡后送两张阜康五万两银票，共十万，帮助扩充牌局资本。刘拿到的第一封信封为胡的两张本金票加另外五千彩钱；第二封是三万二千多的分红。庞后来改为对半分，但正文没有列出完整结算式，未自行把“多”精确填成五百两。刘不收这份分红，将其暂存在庞处，并非当天拿走用于重开药铺。
15995—15997是刘假设总输二十万、自己四成承担八万、抵账后十万剩二万的担忧，不是已发生亏损。胡解释无论牌局输赢都能取得人情上的收益，是他的商业判断。五千彩钱拟以庞名义捐善堂；刘另提一千捐款被胡阻止，不能把这一千也算成已经捐出。
“四两白干”用 four liang，表示原有酒量单位，不写成四两银子。“几吊银子”在16171原稿混用了吊与银，正文 a few strings of cash 取吊钱的口语语境；这是为使英语自然所作的有限处理，并非换算出确定银两，更未擅加具体金额。
胡在上海有两万包丝，庞说双方合计占七成；保留这两个数字，不倒推出小说未交代的庞方数量或整个市场库存。最初方案是庞出面领头、胡联络同行并陪同谈判，最后庞因家事授权胡全权代理。胡提出仍要商量价格，庞随后明确让他自行酌办，两次发言顺序及差别均保留。
庞最初提出交栈单，胡最终只要委托信及通知档手，明确栈单不必交给自己，不能写成仓单已经移交。胡为庞准备三封信，分别通往王、秦、郁；庞给胡两封，一封委托书，一封致上海管事。特意不封口、让胡先阅的是后一封，未误写成所有信都公开。

人物判断与原稿识读

胡对外国买方需求、蚕瘟、封锁后行情及可自定价的说法，按小说及人物原话翻译，不拿外部史料改正文；“前个六八年”保留 six or eight years ago 的不精确时间。刘以为庞一定会同意，胡指出旧客户交情与身份面子的障碍；庞先辞谢、后来愿谈，最后委任，不合并成一开始已有合作承诺。
庞担心胡实际资本有限、可能弄玄虚，属于小说写明的顾虑；其对诉讼的说明只是族人告他，没有已经证明对方侵产的裁判。胡据此判断族人谋产，及承诺“只要理直，包赢不输”，保留各自的推断与条件。庞不让胡同行，是顾虑自己与王的名誉，不是拒绝所有帮助；备信仍可不用，未写成已干预案件。
“温室”按宴客的暖房处理为 heated room，不擅改为培植花木的玻璃温室；外国玻璃窗的稀有性另随原文保留。“十景生片火锅”按十样生片食材的菜名词面处理，不列未写出的食材。“冷、准、狠”是小说人物总结的赌桌方式，完整翻译，不另作教学。
明显录入问题仅用于理解，不改中文输入：“也喜一次拜托”按又一次；“遥儿十摊”按摇几十摊；“一洋”按一样；“大本钱卜小利”按大本钱搏小利；“莫恻”按莫测；“信纣”按信封；“愧作”按愧怍；“他说着一只火锅”据正在吃饭的语境按吃着；“内地也洋人”按内地与洋人；“宠二”按庞二；“第二夭”按第二天；“甫浔”据全书地点按南浔；“劝同行”语义保留；“闲后”按闲话；“徘难解纷”按排难解纷。

英语一综合精读方向

阅读：辨别目的与手段：胡关心的是争取合作，刘一度专注赢钱；刘拒收分红，意在维持平等交往；胡借出资本，关注的是牌局之外的人情收益。再分别标出行情信息、人物预测、谈判承诺和最终落实的文件，避免将预期读成已实现事实。

完形：留意 yet、otherwise、even if、whether...or、once、only when、rather than 等如何推进“有利但不宜接受”“赢牌却可能误事”“先拒绝后转圜”的关系。还可比较 no need、must、would、might、if 等对行为必要性与判断强度的不同限制。

段落排序：前半抓住“目的提醒—邀赌—合伙收赔—缺本补资—结算辞红—回城复命”；后半抓住“争取庞领头—刘试谈—预约花酒—家事临时打断—帮助诉讼—取得委托—双方交换信件”。注意胡先离席赶回城安排资金，刘后来才知道原委，不能把后置解释误当成后来发生的动作。

翻译：可练15893—15899关于口头改注与收赔责任的句群，关注条件、数量和主语；再练16169—16181关于公平办理却仍可能被认为走关系的让步句。文化比喻如退一步以求进、抬轿、顺风旗先辨对话功能，再选择自然英文，避免不加辨别地逐词堆砌。

写作：可积累 never forget what you're there for、a friendship for the long term、consult someone before deciding、retain one's independence、attend to every detail 等表达，练习关于长期合作、独立判断、责任与信誉的论述。小说中的赌博、市场控制及利用关系情节属于分析材料，不宜直接写成应普遍倡导的处事规范；本批语言练习仍需与英语一真题和限时训练配合。
