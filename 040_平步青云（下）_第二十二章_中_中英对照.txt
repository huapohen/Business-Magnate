到了怡情院，已经灯火阑珊，只有楼上前厢房还有一台酒在闹。到了怡情老二的大房间略坐一坐，古应春首先告辞，接着是尤五道声“明朝会”，怡情老二诡秘地一笑，相偕离去。阿巧姐却始终不曾露面，一个小大姐名叫阿翠的，替胡雪岩铺衾安枕，接着端了热水来，服侍他洗脚。杂事已毕，掩上房门，管自己走了。胡雪岩有些心神不安，不知怡情老二是怎么一个安排？只凝神静听房门外面，脚步声倒有，都是由远而近再由近而远，不曾见有人推门进来，而自鸣钟已经打了数下，自笑是“痴汉等老婆”，懒洋洋地上了床。这一天相当累，心里有事，眼皮却酸涩得很，蒙蒙胧胧地睡了去。也不知过了多少时候，突然发觉被中伸进一口冰冷的手来，“啊！地一声，不等他开口，又有一只冰冷的手，掩在他嘴上。胡雪岩会意，身子往里面一缩，腾出地方来容纳阿巧姐。她钻进被窝，牙齿冻得“格格”发抖，同时一把抱往了他，前胸紧贴着他的后背，意在取暖。“怎么冻得这样子？”胡雪岩转过脸悄悄问说。“前厢房断命客人，到三点钟才走。”阿巧姐说，“今天轮着我值夜，风又在，冻得我来！”说着吸了口气，把他抱得更紧了。胡雪岩好生怜惜，翻个身伸手把被掖一掖，阿巧索性把头钻在他胸前，他的一双手自然也就不老实了。

By the time they reached Yiqing House, most of the lights had dimmed. Only a drinking party in the front room upstairs was still making a noise. They sat briefly in Second Miss's large room. Gu Yingchun left first; then You Laowu said, “See you tomorrow.” Second Miss gave a mysterious smile and went away with him. A’qiao had not appeared at all. A young maid called A’cui arranged Hu's bedding, brought hot water, and helped him wash his feet. Her work finished, she shut the door and went off. Hu felt uneasy, wondering what Second Miss had arranged. He listened intently outside. There were footsteps, approaching and receding, but nobody opened the door. The striking clock had sounded several times. Laughing at himself as a fool waiting for a wife, he climbed languidly into bed. The day had been tiring. Though his mind was occupied, his eyelids ached, and he drifted into sleep. He did not know how much later it was when an icy hand suddenly entered the bedding. Before his startled exclamation could become a word, another cold hand covered his mouth. Understanding, he moved farther into the bed to make room for A’qiao. She slipped under the quilt, her teeth chattering, and clasped him, pressing her chest against his back for warmth. “How did you get so cold?” he whispered, turning his head. “Those wretched guests in the front room didn't leave until three. It was my turn on night duty, and the wind was fierce. I'm frozen!” She drew a breath and held him tighter. Filled with tenderness, Hu turned over and tucked the quilt around her. She buried her head against his chest, and his hands naturally began to wander.

一面膜索着，他一面问：“阿巧，你今年几岁？”“猜猜看呢？”“二十三。”胡雪岩说，“至多二十四。”“二十四是要来生了。”“那么多少呢？”“我属羊的。”“属羊？”胡雪岩在多底拿起阿巧姐的纤纤五指，扳数着说，“今年咸丰四年甲寅，道光二十七年丁未，十五年乙未，正好二十岁。”“越算越好了！”阿巧姐当然知道他是有意这样算法，但心里总是高兴的。“阿巧，”胡雪岩做了反面文章，又做正面，“你真正看不出三十二岁。”“大家都说胡老爷一双眼睛厉害，会看不出？”“真的看不出！”胡雪岩问道：“象你这样的人才，为啥不自己铺房间，要帮人家？”“吃这碗饭，三十二岁就是老太婆了！人老珠黄不值钱，啥人要？”“我要，”胡雪岩不假思索的回答。阿巧姐见多识广，当然不会拿他的话当真，接口答道：“既然有人要，我还要铺啥房间？”“这话倒也不错。”胡雪岩又问：“你家里有些什么人？”问到这话，近乎多余，而偏偏客人常喜欢问这句话，阿巧姐都腻烦回答了，“问它作啥！”她说，“总不见得是千金小姐出身。”言语简峭，胡雪岩又多一层好感，不由得想起了尤五的话，认真地开始考虑。

As he caressed her, he asked, “A’qiao, how old are you?” “Guess.” “Twenty-three. Twenty-four at most.” “I'll have to wait for another life to be twenty-four.” “How old, then?” “I was born in the Year of the Goat.” “The Goat?” Taking her slender fingers beneath the quilt, he counted on them. “This is the fourth year of Xianfeng, jiayin. The twenty-seventh of Daoguang was dingwei, and the fifteenth was yiwei. That makes you exactly twenty.” “Your reckoning gets better and better!” She knew he was doing it deliberately, but it pleased her all the same. Having taken the playful approach, Hu now spoke seriously. “You really don't look thirty-two.” “Everyone says Master Hu has such sharp eyes. Could you really fail to see?” “I truly couldn't. A woman of your qualities—why don't you set up a room of your own instead of working for someone else?” “In this trade, thirty-two makes you an old woman. Old age and faded looks are worth nothing. Who would want me?” “I would,” Hu answered without thinking. A’qiao had seen too much of the world to take that seriously. “If someone wants me, why would I need to set up my own room?” “True enough. Who is there in your family?” It was an unnecessary question, yet one customers often asked. She was tired of answering. “What do you want to know that for? I can hardly have been brought up as a young heiress.” Her crisp reply increased his liking for her. Remembering You's suggestion, he began to consider it seriously.

此时此地，忽然既不动口，又不动手，那是大为反常的事，阿巧狙不由得有些奇怪，伸一只手去摸在他的胸前，左一按，右一按，这使得胡雪岩也奇怪了。“做什么？”“看看可能摸得出你的心事？”“心事怎么摸得出？只能猜。你倒猜猜我的心事看。”“我不用猜，我摸得出。”阿巧姐说，“你不喜欢我。”“奇了！哪有这话？你倒讲个道理给我听听。”“你喜欢我就会心跳。现在心一点不跳，是‘当伊煞介事’。”“妙！”胡雪岩笑道，“还有这么一套说法？不晓得你这样子摸过几个男人？”这句话说得失于检点，阿巧姐恼怒伤心，兼而有之，慢慢抽开手，背脸向外。胡雪岩这才发觉，说了句极无趣的话，深为失悔，扳她身子不动，仰头去看，梳妆台上一只洋灯的残焰映照，阿巧姐两粒泪珠，晶莹可见。“生气了是不是？”胡雪岩尴尬地说，“说说笑话，何苦当真！”说着，拿手指替她拭去眼泪，顺势就亲着她的脸。阿巧姐不作声，但也没有再作何不快的表示，她只是尽力为自己譬解，敷衍怡情老二和尤五的面子，好歹应付了这一夜。胡雪岩却是由于这个言语上的波折，失去了兴趣，同时也累得懒于说话，一合上眼，便觉双目酸涩，真的借了一夜“干铺”。

At such a time and in such a place, suddenly to cease both talking and touching was decidedly unusual. Puzzled, A’qiao put a hand on his chest, pressing here and there until he too was puzzled. “What are you doing?” “Seeing whether I can feel what's on your mind.” “You can't feel a thought. You can only guess it. Go on, guess mine.” “I needn't guess. I can feel it. You don't like me.” “How extraordinary! What makes you say that? Give me a reason.” “If you liked me, your heart would beat faster. It isn't stirring at all. You're merely going through the motions.” “Wonderful!” Hu laughed. “What a theory! I wonder how many men's chests you've tested like that.” It was a thoughtless remark. Hurt and angry, she slowly withdrew her hand and turned her face away. Only then did Hu realise how unpleasantly he had spoken. He regretted it deeply. When he tried to turn her towards him, she would not move. He raised his head. By the dying flame of the foreign lamp on the dressing table, he could see two shining tears. “You're angry, aren't you?” he said awkwardly. “It was a joke. Why take it seriously?” He wiped her tears with his fingers and kissed her face. She said nothing, but showed no further displeasure. She merely did her best to reason herself out of her distress and, for Second Miss's and You's sake, get through the night somehow. The awkward exchange had dampened Hu's interest too. He was tired and disinclined to talk. As soon as he closed his eyes, they ached with fatigue. In the end, he really did spend the night in a “dry bed.”

到第二天一觉醒来，时已近午，侧身一望，阿巧姐自然不在，枕边却遗下一根长长的头发，拈到手里，想起宵来的光景，倒有无端的怅惆，同时也觉得有些歉疚，心想阿巧姐一定很不高兴，并且也辜负了尤五和怡情老二玉成的美意。这样转着念头，便打算要跟阿巧姐先谈一谈，披衣起床，咳嗽一声，房门随即“呀”地推开，进来的正是阿巧姐，梳一个极光极亮的头，脸却是不施脂粉的清水脸，新象牙似的皮肤，淡红的嘴唇，颊上有几点茶叶未似的雀斑，徐娘丰韵，别有动人之处。“起来了！”她说，眼睛一瞟，撮两个手指放在嘴唇，示意禁声。看她这个姿态，明雪岩自然什么话都不敢说，而实在有些困惑，不知道要顾忌的是哪些话？“夜里的事，不要漏出来！”原来如此！胡雪岩不知是不是因为她来相伴，不合于“长三”。的规矩，所以有所忌讳。只觉得这样子倒有偷情的趣味，越发觉得昨夜的机会可惜。

When he awoke, it was nearly noon. Turning over, he found A’qiao gone, as he had expected, but a long hair remained beside the pillow. He picked it up. The memory of the night brought an unaccountable wistfulness and a sense of guilt. She must be very unhappy; he had also disappointed You and Second Miss in their kindly attempt to bring them together. He decided to speak to her first. Putting on his clothes, he rose and coughed. The door immediately creaked open, and A’qiao herself entered. Her hair was dressed smooth and glossy, but her face was bare of powder or rouge. With skin like new ivory, pale red lips, and a few freckles like specks of tea on her cheeks, she had the distinctive charm of a mature woman. “You're up.” She glanced at him, then put two fingers to her lips to enjoin silence. He dared say nothing, though he was puzzled about which words required caution. “Don't let last night's business slip out.” So that was it! He wondered whether her coming to him had broken some rule of the changsan establishment. In any case, the secrecy gave the affair the flavour of a clandestine romance and made him regret the lost opportunity all the more.

要再找这样一个机会也不难。等小大姐打了脸水进来，阿巧姐理好了床，来替他打辫子时，胡雪岩便说：“今天晚上我仍旧要借干铺。”“随便你。”阿巧姐淡淡地应声。“还跟昨天一样。”“啥个一样？”他不知她是真不明白，还是有意装傻？想了想笑道，“来摸摸我的心跳不跳？”阿巧姐不响，把眼垂了下去，似乎专心一致在他那条辫子上。“还在生我的气？”“哪有这话？我们什么人，敢生贵客的气？”阿巧姐正色说道：“胡老爷，你千万不能说这话，传到二小姐耳朵里，一定会说我。”“不会，不会！”胡雪岩灵机一动，“你能不能请一天假？”“为啥？”“我带你到一个地方去玩。”停了一会，见她不作声，便知不是不能请假的，因而又加了一句：“我来跟老二说，放你一天假。”“不！”阿巧姐说，“我自己跟二小姐讲。不过，胡老爷，你要带我到啥地方去玩？”“玩就是玩。看戏，吃大菜，再到外国洋行看看，有什么新样子的首饰？”这一说，阿巧姐不由得露了笑容，昨夜那一言之夫所引起的不愉快，至此才算消除。“胡老爷！”小大姐走了来说：“尤五少说，请胡老爷到小房子去吃中饭。”“好。我就去。”胡雪岩暗示阿巧姐说，“我吃完饭就要走了。”

Another opportunity would not be hard to arrange. Once the maid had brought washing water and A’qiao had straightened the bed and begun to braid his queue, Hu said, “Tonight I shall borrow a dry bed again.” “As you please,” she replied indifferently. “Just like last night.” “What is to be the same?” Was she genuinely puzzled or pretending not to understand? After a moment, he smiled. “Come and feel whether my heart is beating.” She lowered her eyes without answering, apparently intent on his braid. “Still angry with me?” “How could you say that? What are we, to dare be angry with distinguished guests?” She spoke seriously. “Mr Hu, you must never say that. If Second Miss heard, she would blame me.” “She won't!” An idea came to him. “Could you take a day off?” “Why?” “I'd like to take you out.” When she remained silent, he concluded that leave was possible and added, “I'll ask Second Miss to give you the day.” “No. I'll ask her myself. But where would you take me?” “Just out for pleasure: a play, a Western meal, then a look at a foreign shop to see what new jewellery they have.” At that, she could not help smiling. The unhappiness caused by his thoughtless words was finally dispelled. The maid came in. “Mr Hu, Fifth Master You asks you to lunch at his little house.” “Very well. I'll go now.” Hu gave A’qiao a hint. “I'll be leaving as soon as lunch is over.”

等胡雪岩一到，只见古应春也在那里，踉尤五和怡情老二的脸上一样，都挂着愉悦的笑容，仿佛正在谈一件很有趣的事，看到胡雪岩出现，笑容更浓了，显然的，所谈的这件趣事，与他有关。“昨晚我竟蒙在鼓里。”古应春迎着他说，“这也算‘小登科’，恭喜，恭喜！”“怎么样？”尤五问了这一句，又说：“老二说，她在床上……”“瞎三话四！”怡情老二赶紧拦住，同时又给了尤五一个白眼，“胡老爷自己不知道，要你来说？”“是啊！阿巧姐好在哪里，小爷叔身历其境，最清楚不过，何用旁人告诉他？”古应春这一说，胡雪岩才完全懂得，急于求得补偿的心也更热了，然而口中却不知道该怎么说才好？唯有笑而不答。“先吃饭，还是先谈事？”古应春一面问，一面从怀里掏出两张纸来。“先谈事吧！”胡雪岩望着一窗的好太阳，兴致勃勃地问：“老古，你的马车坐了来没有？”“在弄堂口。你要到哪里去？”“难得有空，又是好天气，我想好好去逛半天。”那三个人互相望了望，仍旧是古应春开口动问：“你预备怎么逛法？我来替你安排。”“回头再说。”胡雪岩指着他手中的纸问：“这是什么？”“两通信稿子。你看吧！”一通是致王有龄的，请他出信给何桂清，介绍古应春去谒见，一通是致刘不才的，要他到上海来。胡雪岩看完，仍旧交了回去，请古应春誉正发出。

When Hu arrived, Gu was there too. He, You, and Second Miss all wore delighted smiles, as though discussing something amusing. At Hu's appearance their smiles broadened: clearly he was the subject. “I was completely in the dark last night,” Gu greeted him. “A little wedding celebration, eh? Congratulations!” “How was it?” You asked. “Second Miss says that in bed she...” “Nonsense!” Second Miss hurriedly stopped him and gave him a sidelong glare. “Doesn't Mr Hu know for himself? Must you tell him?” “Exactly,” Gu said. “Little Uncle has experienced A’qiao's merits first-hand. Who could know better? He hardly needs anyone else to explain.” Only now did Hu fully understand, and his desire to make up for the lost opportunity grew stronger. Yet he did not know what to say. He could only smile. “Shall we eat first or discuss business?” Gu drew two sheets of paper from his clothes. “Business first.” Looking at the sunshine filling the window, Hu asked eagerly, “Did you bring your carriage, Old Gu?” “It's at the lane entrance. Where do you want to go?” “Such good weather, and a rare free afternoon! I'd like a proper outing.” The three exchanged glances. Gu spoke again. “What have you in mind? I'll arrange it.” “We can discuss that later.” Hu pointed to the papers. “What are those?” “Two draft letters. Read them.” One asked Wang Youling to write to He Guiqing, introducing Gu and securing him an audience; the other summoned Liu Bucai to Shanghai. Hu read them and handed them back, asking Gu to make fair copies and send them.

要谈的事，就是这些。开出饭来，正在喝酒，阿巧姐到了，大大方方的一招手，最后向怡情老二抛了个眼色，两人走到后房会谈心。“真不错！”古应春望着阿巧姐的苗条背影说，“是扬州‘瘦马’的样子。““什么‘瘦马’？活马！”尤五笑道：“小爷叔，你怎么谢媒？”“谢你，还是谢老二？”“我当差应该，自然是谢老二。”“那容易。回头我要到洋行里去，挑点首饰，老二一起去好了，她喜欢什么，我就买什么送她。”“说说笑话的，何用你如此破费？不过，”尤五向后房望了一眼，放低了声音说：“你买首饰给哪个？阿巧是厉害角色，你不要做‘洋盘’！”“如果她是厉害角色，就不会当我洋盘。”“对！”古应春击节称赏，“小爷叔这句话，真是一针见血，深极了。”“也好！”尤五笑着对胡雪岩说，“你也难得做一回洋盘，就带着她去好了。老二就不必了。”“一起去，一起去！”胡雪岩说。“打搅老二的地方很多，我本来想送她点东西，表示表示我的意思。”“回来再说吧！”尤五不置可否。

That completed their business. Lunch was served. While they were drinking, A’qiao arrived, greeted them with an easy gesture, and finally gave Second Miss a glance. The two went into a back room to talk privately. “Very fine indeed!” Gu said, watching A’qiao's slender retreating figure. “She has the look of one of Yangzhou's ‘slender horses.’” “Slender horse? A lively horse!” You laughed. “Little Uncle, how will you thank your matchmaker?” “You, or Second Miss?” “It's my duty to serve. Second Miss, naturally.” “Easy enough. I'm going to the foreign shop to choose jewellery. She should come too. Whatever she likes, I'll buy her.” “We were joking. Why put you to such expense? Still...” You glanced towards the back room and lowered his voice. “Whom are you buying jewellery for? A’qiao is a shrewd one. Don't let yourself be taken for a gullible customer.” “If she's truly shrewd, she won't take me for one.” “Exactly!” Gu applauded. “That goes straight to the heart of it. Very profound!” “Very well,” You laughed. “It's rare enough for you to play the gullible customer. Take her along. Second Miss needn't go.” “Let her come too! I've troubled her often enough. I already meant to give her something as a token of appreciation.” “We'll see later,” You said, committing himself neither way.

于是喝着酒谈些夷场趣事。不久，看见怡情老二和阿巧姐一前一后走了出来，一个是春风满面，一个是故作矜持，反正神色之间，都显得不平常。“都坐下来吃吧！”怡情老二坐下来当女主人，阿巧则无论如何不肯，说“没有这个规矩”，侍立在旁，递菜热酒，三个男的主客，视线都断断续续地跟着她转，倒把她看得不好意思了。“二小姐！”她说，“没有事情我就转去了。”“不要走，不要走！”尤五首先就喊。“让她走吧！”怡情老二向尤五抛过去一个眼色。等阿巧姐走了，才便于说话，她说，阿巧姐把昨夜的事都告诉她了。阿巧姐不知道胡雪岩是打的什么主意？如果真的喜欢她，她愿意陪着一起玩，倘或以为是尤五和怡情老二的面子，不能不对她敷衍敷衍，那就大可不必了。

They drank and discussed amusing incidents in the foreign settlement. Before long, Second Miss and A’qiao emerged, one beaming, the other affecting reserve, both looking noticeably different. “Sit down and eat.” Second Miss took her place as hostess, but A’qiao refused. “It isn't the rule.” She stood beside them, passing dishes and warming wine. The three men's eyes kept following her until she grew embarrassed. “Second Miss, if there's nothing else, I'll go back.” “Don't go!” You called first. “Let her,” Second Miss said, signalling to him with a glance. Only once A’qiao had gone could she speak freely. A’qiao had told her what happened the night before. She could not understand Hu's intentions. If he really liked her, she was willing to keep him company. But if he merely felt obliged, for You's and Second Miss's sake, to be agreeable to her, there was no need.

“人在这里”，尤五指着胡雪岩对怡情老二说，“你自己问他。”“胡老爷，”怡情老二笑嘻嘻地问道：“昨天夜里是怎么想了想，不愿意理她了？”“我没有什么不愿意，我是怕她不愿，心想不必勉强。”“怎么？”尤五大为诧异，“昨夜你没有理她？真的是‘干铺’？”胡雪岩点点头说：“这也是常事！”“叫我就煞不住车。”尤五看一看怡情老二说，“我是怕她‘三礼拜、六点钟’，不然我早就动脑筋了。”“你不要扯到我身上！”怡情老二讥嘲地说：“你动得上脑筋，尽管去动。阿巧姐眼界高得很，不见得看得上你，现在有胡老爷一比，你更加‘鼻头上挂盐鱼——嗅鲞’！”她这样一说，古应春和尤五都笑了，胡雪岩却有点不明白，“什么叫‘三礼拜、六点钟’？”他问。“这是夷场上兴出来的一句俗话，”古应春为他解释， “三礼拜‘廿一日’，六点钟‘酉’正，合起来是个什么字？你自己去想。”“原来是说老二会吃醋！”胡雪岩说：“老二不是那种人，再说，尤五哥也不会让老二吃醋，不然，我们在旁边的人也不服。”由这两句话，怡情老二对胡雪岩更有好感，决心要促成他与阿巧姐的姻缘，便趁尤五和古应春谈他们都相识的一个熟人，谈得起劲时，招招手把胡雪岩找到一边，探问他的意思。

“The man is here,” You said, pointing at Hu. “Ask him yourself.” “Mr Hu,” Second Miss asked smilingly, “what made you change your mind last night and leave her alone?” “I had no objection. I was afraid she did. I thought there was no need to force things.” You was astonished. “What? You left her alone? It really was a dry bed?” Hu nodded. “These things happen.” “I couldn't have put on the brakes.” You glanced at Second Miss. “If I weren't afraid of her ‘three weeks, six o'clock,’ I'd have made my plans long ago.” “Don't drag me into it!” she retorted. “Make all the plans you like, if you've a chance. A’qiao sets her sights high. She might not fancy you. Now she has Mr Hu to compare you with, you're like a man with salted fish hanging under his nose—sniff away, but forget your hopes!” Gu and You laughed, but Hu was puzzled. “What's ‘three weeks, six o'clock’?” “A new saying in the settlement,” Gu explained. “Three weeks give twenty-one days; six in the evening is the middle of the you hour. Combine the written components for ‘twenty-one days’ with the character for you, and what character do you get? Think it out.” “Vinegar! You mean Second Miss gets jealous. She isn't that sort of woman. Besides, Brother You wouldn't give her cause. The rest of us would object if he did.” Those two remarks made Second Miss like Hu still more. Determined to bring him and A’qiao together, she waited until You and Gu were absorbed in talking about a mutual acquaintance, then beckoned Hu aside to ask his intentions.

“胡老爷，你是预备长局，还是短局？”“长局如何，短局又如何？”“短局呢？我另外用人，你借一处小房子，或者就在楼下，那家房客就要搬了，大家住在一起热闹些。长局呢？事情比较麻烦，阿巧姐是有男人的，在木渎种田，不过也不要紧，包在我身上，花个二三百两银子：就可了结。阿巧姐身上没有什么亏空，胡老爷，“怡情老二很热心的说，”这件事，只要胡太太那里没有麻烦，你大可做得。“胡雪岩一时无从回答，事情倒是好事，但窒碍甚多，必须好好打算，但直说了怕扫了怡情老二的兴，所以考虑了好半天这样答道：“长也好，短也好，总要成局。你的好意，我十分领情，哪一天空了，我们好好谈一谈。眼前请你放在心里好了。”“我晓得。”怡情老二连连点头，“这件事本来也是急不得的。不过，胡老爷，我还有句话。你不要多花冤枉钱。”这话与尤五的忠告，如出一辙，可见得大家都拿他当自己人看待，这一点是胡雪岩最感到安慰的。

“Mr Hu, are you thinking of a permanent arrangement or a temporary one?” “What would each involve?” “For a temporary one, I'd employ someone else. You could rent a little house—or the rooms downstairs. The present tenant is leaving, and we'd have company living together. A permanent arrangement would be more troublesome. A’qiao has a husband, farming in Mudu. Still, that needn't matter. Leave it to me. Two or three hundred taels would settle it. She has no substantial debts. So long as Mrs Hu raises no difficulty, you could quite well do it.” Hu could not answer at once. It was an attractive prospect, but there were many obstacles, and he must think carefully. To say so plainly might dampen Second Miss's enthusiasm. After long consideration, he replied, “Permanent or temporary, I certainly want an arrangement. I deeply appreciate your kindness. When there's time, we must discuss it properly. For now, please keep it in mind.” “I understand.” She nodded repeatedly. “It can't be hurried. But one more thing, Mr Hu: don't spend money needlessly.” Her advice matched You's exactly. Clearly, they all regarded him as one of themselves. Nothing pleased Hu more.

因此，他的兴致越发好了，“今天的天气实在不坏。”他怂恿着怡情老二说，“一起出去兜兜风，痛痛快快玩它半天。”“到哪里去呢？总要想好一个地方。”这时他们说话的声音响了，古应春已经听到，便插嘴提议：“到龙华去看桃花如何？”“龙华？”胡雪岩对上海还不熟，便即问道：“那里地方安静不安静？”“怎么不安静？离着县城还有十八里路呢！再说，有五哥在，怕什么。”“好吧！”尤五接口，“你们有兴，我就保驾。”这一说，大家的兴致都提了起来，古应春亲自到弄堂口去雇好马车，怡情老二则派人去找阿巧姐来，就在她那里梳妆换衣服，都是素雅的淡妆，但天然丰韵，已是出人头地，胡雪岩颇为得意。

His spirits rose still further. “The weather really is fine. Come out for a drive and enjoy half a day properly.” “Where? We must choose somewhere first.” Their voices had grown louder, and Gu heard them. “How about Longhua, to see the peach blossoms?” Hu did not yet know Shanghai well. “Longhua? Is it peaceful there?” “Why wouldn't it be? It's eighteen li from the county town. Besides, with Brother Five along, what is there to fear?” “Very well,” You said. “If you're all keen, I'll be your escort.” Everyone's enthusiasm mounted. Gu went himself to the lane entrance to hire a carriage, while Second Miss sent for A’qiao to dress and change clothes at her rooms. Both women dressed with simple elegance, but their natural grace already set them above the crowd. Hu felt well pleased.

马车一共是两部，古应春自己的那部亨斯美，载了胡雪岩和阿巧姐，出了弄堂，向南疾驰，经斜桥、高昌庙，一条官道，相当宽广。这个天气，都愿郊游，一路轿马纷纷，极其热闹，但象这两部马车，敞着篷，俪影双双，招摇而过的，却不多见，因此轮声鞭影中，不断有人指指点点。阿巧姐视而不见，只是稳稳地坐着，不轻言笑，怎么也看不出风尘气息。等望见了龙华寺的塔影，同时也望见了一道长桥。这道桥也是上海的一胜，称为百步桥，长二十四丈，阔二丈有余，马蹄得得，轮声辘辘，过了百步桥不远，便是龙华寺。这座古刹，以一座七级浮屠著名，是上海唯一的古塔。马车就在塔前停下，怡情老二和阿巧姐先忙着请香烛烧香。胡雪岩想起在湖州与芙蓉初见，也是在佛像之前，当时还求了一张签，“江上采芙蓉”成为姻缘前定的佳签，此时也不妨如法炮制一番。不过，自己不必再求，“阿巧姐，”他说，“你无妨求张签看。”“问啥呢？”阿巧姐想了想说，“好，我来求它一张。”于是烧了香求签，签条拿到她手里，不肯给胡雪岩看，她不识多少字，只知道这张签，是“下下”，当然不是好签，怕扫了胡雪岩的兴，所以不愿公开。怡情老二也求了一张，倒是“上上”，说得妻财子禄，无一不好，如果是妇人求得这张签，主得贵子，古应春便向尤五道贺，而实际上是拿怡情老二开玩笑。就这样说笑着，闲步桃林，随意浏览，五个人分做两起，古应春不知是有意，还是无意，引着尤五和怡情老二，越走越远，留下胡雪岩和阿巧姐在后面，正好谈话。

There were two carriages. Gu's own hansom carried Hu and A’qiao. Leaving the lane, they sped south, past Xieqiao and Gaochang Temple along a broad public road. The fine weather had brought everyone into the countryside, and the route was busy with chairs and horses. But two open carriages displaying their couples so conspicuously were an unusual sight. Amid rattling wheels and flashing whips, people continually pointed and commented. A’qiao appeared not to notice. Sitting steadily, with no ready chatter or laughter, she showed nothing of the woman of the pleasure quarters. When Longhua Temple's pagoda came into view, so did a long bridge. This too was a Shanghai landmark: Hundred-Pace Bridge, twenty-four zhang long and over two wide. Hooves clattered and wheels rumbled across it. The temple lay a short distance beyond. The ancient monastery was famous for its seven-storey pagoda, Shanghai's only old tower. The carriages stopped before it, and the women first bought incense and candles to make their offerings. Hu remembered meeting Furong before a Buddha image in Huzhou. The fortune he had drawn then, “Gathering lotus blooms upon the river,” had become an auspicious sign of their destined union. Why not do the same here? He need not draw one himself this time. “Sister A’qiao, why don't you try a fortune?” “What should I ask?” She considered. “Very well, I'll draw one.” After burning incense, she drew a slip but refused to show Hu. She knew few written characters, yet could recognise its rating: “lowest of the low.” It was plainly unlucky. Afraid of spoiling his pleasure, she kept it to herself. Second Miss drew one too, rated “highest of the high”: wife, wealth, children, and official advancement were all favourable. For a woman, it predicted a distinguished son. Gu congratulated You, really teasing Second Miss. Talking and laughing, they strolled among the peach trees. The five divided into two groups. Whether deliberately or by chance, Gu led You and Second Miss farther and farther ahead, leaving Hu and A’qiao free to talk.

“累了吧！”胡雪岩看她双足纤纤，不免怜惜，便指着一处茶座说：“喝碗茶再走！”白布棚子下的茶座，几乎都是官客，有一两桌有女眷，也是坐在僻隐之处，而且背朝着外，不肯以面目示人。阿巧姐却无此顾忌，拣了张干净桌子坐下来，正在通道旁边，人来人往，无不注以一瞥，也有已走过去了，又借故回头，好再看一眼的。而阿巧姐是视如不见，等茶博士拿了茶来要斟时，她赶紫摇手阻止：“谢谢你，我们自己来。”茶博士住了手，阿巧姐才用茶涮了茶碗，抽出一条来路货的雪白麻纱手绢，将杯口里外擦净，然后斟得八分满，双手捧到胡雪岩面前，到她自己喝时，也是这样一丝不苟，极讲究洁净。“我在想，人生在世，实在奇妙难测。我敢说，没有一个人，今天能晓得明天的事。”胡雪岩对景生情，发了这么一段感慨，阿巧姐目然莫名其妙，一双俏伶伶的眼睛看着他不断眨动，示意他说下去。“譬如昨天，我做梦也想不到今天会在龙华看桃花，更想不到会跟你在一起。”“我算啥！”阿巧姐说，“名字生得不好，说破了不值钱，不会有啥‘巧’事落到我头上。这段话令人有突兀之感，胡雪岩细辨了辨，觉得意味深长，可能也是在试探，便先不追究，只问：“你是七月初七生的？”

“Tired?” The sight of her delicate little feet stirred Hu's tenderness. He pointed towards some tea tables. “Let's have tea before going on.” Beneath the white cloth awning, almost all the customers were men. At the one or two tables with women, the women sat out of the way, their backs turned towards the public, unwilling to display their faces. A’qiao had no such scruples. She chose a clean table beside the passage. Everyone passing glanced at her; some who had gone by found an excuse to turn back for a second look. She took no apparent notice. When the waiter brought the tea and was about to pour, she stopped him. “Thank you. We'll do it ourselves.” Once he withdrew his hand, she rinsed the cups with tea, took out a spotless imported linen handkerchief, and wiped the rims inside and out. She filled one cup four-fifths full and offered it to Hu with both hands. She showed the same scrupulous care for cleanliness with her own. “Life is truly strange and unpredictable,” Hu said, moved by the scene. “I'll wager nobody today can know what tomorrow will bring.” Naturally, she could not make out his meaning. Her bright eyes blinked at him, inviting him to continue. “Yesterday, for instance, I never dreamed that today I'd be admiring peach blossoms at Longhua—still less with you.” “What am I?” she replied. “My name is ill chosen. Once it's explained, there's nothing much to it. No happy coincidence is likely to come my way.” The remark seemed abrupt. Considering it, Hu detected deeper meaning, perhaps even a test. He did not pursue that yet. “Were you born on the seventh day of the seventh month?”

“不然怎么叫这个名字？”“好！你的生日好记得很。今年我替你做生日。”“啊唷唷！”阿巧姐有些受宠若惊，“真正不敢当，折煞我了。”“日子过来快得很，桃花开过开荷花，七月初七转眼就到。”胡雪岩问：“那时候我接你到杭州去逛西湖、看荷花，好不好？”“怎么不好！”阿巧姐双眼凝望着茶碗，口中不断在吹着茶水，茶已经不烫，可以上得口了，何需再吹？可见碍她是在想心事。当然，胡雪岩自己也知道，这话可以解释为一种暗示，有把她娶回杭州的意思，阿巧姐所想的必也是这一点。自己是无心的一句话，如果她真有此误会，未免言之过早，转念到此，微生悔意，同时也更留心她的脸色和言语了。“胡老爷这一趟有多少日子耽搁？”她问。“说不定，少则半个月，多则二十天，一定得回杭州。”“我晓得了。跟胡太太说好了来的，不能误卯。”胡雪岩笑而不答，他的笑容是经过做作的，特意要显得令人莫测高深。阿巧姐很能观察，见此光景，便不再多说，只望着悠悠的塔影，慢慢地品茗，样子十分闲适。胡雪岩看她的态度，倒有些不明究竟，心里七上八下的放不下。但转念却又自笑，自己没有应付不了的人，也很少心浮气躁过，此刻是怎么回事？这样一想，硬生生的把杂念抛开，也是抱着“偷得浮生半日闲”的心情，品茗看花，只求自适，阿巧姐看他这样，当然更不便多说什么。两个人等于都在肚子里做功夫。

“How else would I have got this name?” “Good! Your birthday is easy to remember. This year I'll celebrate it for you.” “Oh, you honour me too much! I could never deserve it.” “Time passes quickly. After peach blossoms come lotuses. The seventh day of the seventh month will soon be here. Shall I take you to Hangzhou then, to enjoy West Lake and the lotuses?” “Of course!” She gazed into her cup, continually blowing on the tea. It was already cool enough to drink. Plainly she was thinking of something else. Hu himself knew that his words could be taken as a hint that he meant to marry her and bring her to Hangzhou. That must be what she was considering. His remark had been unpremeditated. If she misunderstood it that way, he had spoken too soon. He felt a little regret and watched her expression and words more closely. “How long will you be staying this time?” she asked. “I can't say. At least half a month, twenty days at most. Then I must return to Hangzhou.” “I see. You've agreed it with Mrs Hu and mustn't miss your roll call.” He smiled without answering, deliberately making the smile inscrutable. A’qiao was observant. She said no more, but gazed towards the distant pagoda and sipped her tea with a leisurely air. Her attitude puzzled him. He felt unsettled. Then he laughed at himself inwardly. There was nobody he could not handle, and he seldom lost his composure. What was the matter with him now? He forcibly dismissed these distractions and adopted the spirit of stealing half a day of leisure from life's bustle. He drank tea and admired the blossoms, seeking only his own contentment. Seeing this, she naturally refrained from further comment. Both were, in effect, conducting their manoeuvres inwardly.

看看日色偏西，桃林中潋滟红霞，如火如荼，真叫“夕阳无限好，只是近黄昏”，再流连不走，天一黑，路上就不好了，于是仍旧照原来的样子，坐着马车，疾驰而回。胡雪岩兴犹未央，同时要“守信用”，说了带阿巧姐去挑首饰，也要送怡情老二“做媒”的谢礼，一定要做到，所以特意关照古应春，先到黄浦滩禅臣洋行。尤五记起胡雪岩的话，便特别注意阿巧姐，可是拿客人当“洋盘”？只见她初入店内，望着成排的玻璃柜和闪闪生光的珠宝首饰，颇有目迷五色之概，但很快的恢复了常态，看看古应春说道：“古大少爷，请你问问洋人，有没有男用的表链？”“男人用的？”“是呀！”阿巧姐笑着问，“怎么了？”“没有什么。我只当我没有听清楚。”于是古应春跟洋人一说，立刻便捧出一只皮盒子来，打开来一看，里面有十几副表链，金银粗细，各式俱备。阿巧姐伸出手去，一条一条挑，最后挑了一根十八开金的，链子一端坠着一只铸得很玲珑的小金羊。“这东西不错！”胡雪岩在一旁说，“再挑！”“不挑了。”阿巧姐走开两步，同时招招手把古应春邀了过去，悄悄说道：“这是我自己买的东西，千万不好叫胡老爷惠钞。请你替我付一付。”说着，手一伸，一张折得小小的银票，塞到了古应春手里。古应春明白了，这是阿巧姐买给她乡下的丈夫的，自然不便让胡雪岩出钱，便点点头说：“我知道了。”胡雪岩还在坚持着，要阿巧姐再挑一两年首饰，她只是袖手不动。又再三问怡情老二喜欢什么？她却不过情，挑了一瓶法国香水。

The sun was sinking westward. Among the peach trees, rippling crimson radiance blazed like fire: truly, “The setting sun is infinitely lovely, only evening is near.” If they lingered until dark, the journey would be less safe. They took their original places in the carriages and drove swiftly back. Hu's pleasure was not yet exhausted, and he meant to keep his word. He had promised jewellery shopping for A’qiao and a matchmaker's gift for Second Miss. He specially asked Gu to stop first at Chanchen Trading House on the Huangpu waterfront. Remembering Hu's remark, You watched A’qiao closely. Would she treat her customer as a gullible spender? On entering, she seemed dazzled by the rows of glass cases and glittering jewellery, but soon recovered. “Young Master Gu, please ask the foreigner whether they have watch chains for men.” “For men?” “Yes.” She smiled. “What's the matter?” “Nothing. I only wondered whether I'd heard correctly.” Gu spoke to the shopkeeper, who brought out a leather case containing more than a dozen chains, gold and silver, thick and thin. She examined them one by one and finally chose an eighteen-carat gold chain with a delicately cast little goat hanging from one end. “Very nice!” Hu said. “Choose something else.” “No more.” Moving a few steps away, she beckoned Gu and whispered, “I'm buying this myself. You mustn't let Mr Hu pay. Please settle it for me.” She slipped a tightly folded banknote into his hand. Gu thought he understood: the chain was for her husband in the country, so she naturally could not let Hu pay. “I understand.” Hu still insisted that she choose a piece or two of jewellery, but she kept her hands tucked in her sleeves. He repeatedly asked Second Miss what she wanted. Unable to refuse his kindness, she chose a bottle of French perfume.

“算帐吧！”胡雪岩取了一百两的银票，交给古应春。接到手里，古应春也不作声，到帐台上跟洋女人结了帐，上车回到怡情老二的小房子，古应春才把他的银票交了回去，“你还阿巧姐六块洋钱。”他说，“表链子阿巧姐自己买，不叫你惠钞。”“岂有此理。”“日子长了，何争一时？”尤五这样说，心里也有替他们作撮合的打算了。胡雪岩听得这么一说，也就一笑置之。在那里吃了饭，怡情老二拉着尤五到一边说了几句，尤五又转达给胡雪岩：阿巧姐今天既然休息，就不想回怡情院，问胡雪岩的意思如何？“那好办！”他说，“跟我走好了。”“要走就早走！不必在这里泡了。”“时候还早，”胡雪岩踌躇着说：“我们一起看戏去？”这个提议没有人接受，古应春说明天要动身到苏州去见何桂清投信，尤五表示倦了，不想出门。其实都是托同，目的是要让胡雪岩踉阿巧姐早圆好梦。

“Settle the bill.” Hu handed Gu a hundred-tael note. Gu took it without comment and paid the foreign woman at the counter. Only after they had driven back to Second Miss's little house did he return Hu's note. “You owe A’qiao six foreign silver dollars. She bought the chain herself and won't let you pay for it.” “That's absurd!” “There are many days ahead. Why dispute this moment?” You said, now intending to help bring them together himself. Hu smiled and let it pass. They dined there. Second Miss drew You aside for a few words, which he then conveyed to Hu: since A’qiao had the day off, she did not want to return to Yiqing House. What did Hu think? “Easy enough. She can come with me.” “Then go early. Don't linger here.” Hu hesitated. “It's still early. Why don't we all go to the theatre?” Nobody accepted. Gu said he was leaving for Suzhou the next day to deliver the letter to He Guiqing, and You said he was tired and did not want to go out. Both were making excuses to let Hu and A’qiao fulfil their hopes sooner.

这当然不宜在裕记丝栈双宿双飞。他由于尤五的推荐，住进一家新开的“仕宦行台”大兴客栈，是个小小的跨院，一明两暗三间房。阿巧姐认为太大了用不着，胡雪岩认为房间一定要多，会客才方便，有时客人来访，只为说一句知心话，稠人广众，大家都憋在肚子里不便说，结果高朋满座，尽是空谈，如果多一间空屋子作为退步，就方便得多了。“照这个样子说，胡老爷，你是预备长住？”“是啊！”胡雪岩说，“丝栈里诸多不便，我想在这里长住，比较舒服。”“你不是说，”阿巧姐指出他的前言不符后语，“半个月、二十天就要回杭州吗？”“ 不错！”胡雪岩很从容地答道，“去了马上要来的，房间留着也不要紧，不过多花几个房钱，有限的。”阿巧姐不作声，心里在盘算，既然如此，不妨备办一些动用什物，于是喊进茶房来，有条不紊地吩咐他去买办风炉锅碗等等，吃的、用的一大堆。胡雪岩心想，照此看来，已不用多说，至少一个“短局”已经存在了。

Naturally, they could not spend the night together at Yuji Silk Warehouse. On You's recommendation, Hu took rooms at the newly opened Daxing Inn, an establishment catering to travelling officials. He had a small side courtyard with three rooms, a central reception room and two private rooms. A’qiao thought it unnecessarily large. Hu replied that ample rooms made receiving visitors easier. Sometimes a guest came only to exchange a word in confidence. In a crowd, everyone kept such thoughts to himself, and a gathering of distinguished friends produced nothing but empty conversation. An extra room into which one could withdraw made all the difference. “Then you're planning to stay a long time, Mr Hu?” “Yes. The warehouse has many inconveniences. A longer stay here would be more comfortable.” “Didn't you say you'd return to Hangzhou in half a month or twenty days?” she asked, pointing out the contradiction. “Quite right,” he answered calmly. “But I'll soon come back. Keeping the rooms costs only a little more, not much.” She said nothing. Thinking that they might as well acquire some household necessities, she called the attendant and gave orderly instructions to buy a stove, pots, bowls, and a host of other things to eat and use. Watching, Hu thought there was no need to say more. At least a temporary arrangement was already in existence.

阿巧姐也真是“做人家”的样子，为他打开行李，将日用杂件，布置妥贴，然后铺好了床，请胡雪岩安置。等胡雪岩上床，她却不睡，将一盏洋灯移到窗前方桌上，背着身子，不知在做些什么？胡雪岩等得不耐烦，便即催问：“你怎么不来睡？我有好些话跟你说。”“来了，来了！”于是阿巧姐移灯到梳妆台前，洗脸卸妆，又检点了门窗，才披了一件夹袄，掀开帐子，跟胡雪岩并头睡下。“你晓得我刚才在做啥？”“我怎么晓得？”“你看！”她伸手从夹袄口袋中掏出一个金表交到胡雪岩手里。表是他的，却多了一条金链子，正就是她在禅臣洋行自己花钱买的那一条。“我送你的。”“你送我的？”胡雪岩大感意外，接着浮起满怀的喜悦和感动，把表链子上坠着的那只小金羊，凑近眼前，仔细观玩，才领悟她特为挑选这一条链子的深意，她是属羊的，这只玲珑的小金羊，就是她的化身，怀中相伴，片刻不离，这番深情，有如食蜜，中边皆甜。“喏！”她又塞过来一个纸包，“大概是胡太太替你打的丝绦子，好好带回去，不然胡太太问起来，设法交帐。”她猜得一点不错，原来系表的一条黑丝绦，是胡太太亲手所织，难为她想得这么周到。“这条丝绦子，龌龊是龌龊得来！”阿巧姐皱着眉说，“本来我想拿它洗洗清爽，深怕你太太会问，是哪个洗的？就露了马脚了。男人决不会想到，拿这条丝绦子洗洗干净！”心细如发，人情透切，胡雪岩对阿巧姐刮目相看了。

A’qiao truly behaved as though setting up a household. She unpacked his luggage, put the everyday articles in their places, made the bed, and invited him to retire. Once he was in bed, however, she did not join him. She moved a foreign lamp to the square table by the window and sat with her back towards him, doing something he could not see. Impatient, he called, “Why don't you come to bed? I've a great deal to say to you.” “Coming!” She moved the lamp to the dressing table, washed off her make-up, checked the doors and windows, and at last, wearing a lined jacket, lifted the bed curtains and lay beside him. “Do you know what I was doing?” “How should I?” “Look.” From her jacket pocket she took a gold watch and placed it in his hand. It was his own, now fitted with the gold chain she had bought with her own money at Chanchen Trading House. “My gift to you.” “To me?” Astonishment gave way to delight and gratitude. Bringing the little gold goat close to his eyes, he studied it and understood her purpose. Born in the Goat year, she had chosen this delicate animal as her own likeness, to remain close to him in his pocket, never parted for a moment. The tenderness was like honey, sweet from edge to centre. “Here.” She passed him a paper packet. “I expect Mrs Hu braided that silk cord for you. Take it home carefully, or you'll have no answer if she asks.” She was exactly right. His wife had made the black silk cord that had held the watch. How thoughtful of A’qiao to consider it! “It's filthy,” she said, frowning. “I thought of washing it, but your wife might ask who had done it, and then the secret would be out. A man would never think of washing his watch cord!” Her meticulous care and understanding of people made him regard her with new respect.

一手把玩着“小金羊”，一手轻抚着活的“白羊”，胡雪岩才真的领略到了温柔乡中的滋味，“阿巧，”他忽然问道：“你把我当作什么人？”这话的意思欠明确，阿巧姐只有这样答道：“好人。”“是相好的好，还是好坏的好？”“好坏的好。”“那种好人我不要做。”胡雪岩说，“我是说，你把我当作你的什么人？”这话就更难回答了，如果说是客人，则私赠表记，变作笼络客人的虚情假意，即有此意，阿巧姐也不肯承认，若说是心上人，又觉得肉麻碍口，想了想有个说法：“你是胡老爷，我自然当你老爷！”“老爷”的意思是双关，下人称男主人为老爷，妻妾称男主人亦是老爷。阿巧姐这样回答，要自己去体会，才有意味，胡雪岩当然懂，但为了逗乐，有怠误解。“你骂我‘赤佬’？”上海话称“鬼”为“赤化”，苏州人则对邪魔外道的鬼祟，如“五通神”之类，为了忌讳，有时亦称“老爷”，意义与上海话的“赤佬”相近，所以胡雪岩这样歪缠。“啥人骂你？”阿巧姐真的骂了，“你自己下作，好的人不要做，要做赤佬。”“赤佬自然不想做，老爷也不必。”胡雪岩涎着关脸道，“阿巧，我做你的‘姘头’好不好？”“要死快哉！”阿巧姐打了他一下，用道地的苏州话娇嗔着，“闲话阿要难听！”

With one hand he played with the little gold goat; with the other he gently caressed the living “white goat.” Only now did he fully appreciate the pleasures of tenderness. “A’qiao,” he asked suddenly, “what sort of man do you take me for?” The meaning was unclear. She could only answer, “A good man.” “Good as in a man you're fond of, or good as opposed to bad?” “Good as opposed to bad.” “I don't want to be that sort of good man. I mean, what am I to you?” That was harder still. To call him a customer would make her personal gift seem a calculated attempt to win favour. Even if that had been her intention, she would not admit it. To call him her beloved felt embarrassingly sentimental. After a moment, she found a way. “You're Master Hu. Naturally, I regard you as my master.” The word had two meanings: servants used it of the head of a household, and wives and concubines used it of their husband. The charm of her answer lay in leaving him to understand it himself. Of course he did, but for amusement he deliberately misunderstood. “Are you calling me a devil?” In Shanghai speech, a ghost was called chilao. In Suzhou, people sometimes respectfully called sinister spirits, such as the Five Powers, “masters” to avoid offending them. The sense was close to chilao, and Hu was twisting her words accordingly. “Who's calling you names?” Now she really scolded him. “You're the shameless one. You won't be a decent man; you insist on being a devil.” “I don't want to be a devil, and I needn't be a master.” With an ingratiating smile, he asked, “A’qiao, may I be your lover?” “You wicked thing!” She struck him lightly, scolding in broad Suzhou speech. “Must you say such improper things?”

越是如此，胡雪岩越觉得乐不可支，调笑闲话，几乎闹了一整夜。睡到第二天上午十一点，阿巧姐才起身，胡雪岩则还在呼呼大睡。也不过是她刚刚漱洗好，有人来敲门，开开一看，是尤五和古应春。“怎么？”尤五探头一望，脱口问道：“小爷叔到此刻还不起来！你们一夜在干什么？”阿巧姐脸一红，强笑道：“我是老早起来了，哪个晓得他这么好困？”古应春走了过来，摸一摸那只洋瓷脸盆，余温犹在，笑一笑说道：“对！阿巧姐老早起来了。“谎话拆穿，阿巧姐更窘，不过她到底经验丰富，不至于手足无措，依旧口中敷衍，手头张罗，把客人招待到外面坐下，然后去叫醒胡雪岩。睡眼惺松的胡雪岩，还恋着宵来的温馨，一伸手就拉住了她往怀里抱，急得阿巧姐恨恨地骂：“人家已经在笑了，你脸皮厚，我可吃不消！”“谁，谁在笑？”“尤五少、古大少都来了，坐在外头，你快起来吧！”阿巧姐又说，“说话当心些。”一面说，一面服侍他起床，胡雪岩只是回忆着昨夜的光景又发愣、又发笑、傻兮兮的样子，惹得阿巧姐更着急。“求求你好不好！越是这样，人家越会跟你开玩笑。”“怕什么！”胡雪岩说，“你不理他们就是了。”见了面还是有一番调笑，甚至可说是谑，尤五和古应春这一双未来的郎舅，象逼问犯人口供似地，要胡雪岩“招供”衾底风情。急得里屋的阿巧姐，暗地里大骂“杀千刀”！幸好胡雪岩一问三不知，只报以满脸笑容，阿巧姐总算不至于太受窘，当然，对胡雪岩这样的态度是满意的，同时也对他有了深一层的认识，嘴上尽管不听她的劝，做出事来，深可人意，是要这样的男人才靠得住。

The more she protested, the more Hu enjoyed himself. They teased and laughed almost the whole night. It was eleven the next morning before A’qiao rose; Hu still slept soundly. She had scarcely finished washing when someone knocked. Opening the door, she found You and Gu. You peered in. “What? Little Uncle still isn't up? What were you two doing all night?” Blushing, she forced a smile. “I've been up ages. Who knew he'd sleep so long?” Gu came over and touched the enamel basin, which was still warm. He smiled. “Yes, Sister A’qiao has been up ages.” Exposed, she grew more embarrassed, but she was too experienced to become flustered. Still exchanging civilities, she settled the visitors in the outer room, then went to wake Hu. Sleepy-eyed and still relishing the night's warmth, he reached out and drew her towards him. She scolded him anxiously. “They're laughing already! You may have a thick skin, but I can't bear it.” “Who? Who's laughing?” “Fifth Master You and Young Master Gu. They're outside. Get up quickly—and mind what you say.” As she helped him dress, he kept remembering the night, staring and smiling foolishly, which only increased her anxiety. “Please! The more you behave like that, the more they'll tease you.” “What is there to fear? Just ignore them.” When they met, there was indeed more teasing, almost mockery. You and Gu, the prospective brothers-in-law, questioned him like interrogators demanding a prisoner's confession, insisting he reveal what had passed beneath the quilt. In the inner room, A’qiao silently cursed the pair. Fortunately, Hu answered every question with a smile and professed to know nothing. She was spared the worst embarrassment. Naturally, she approved, and understood him a little better. He might refuse her advice in words, yet his actions were deeply considerate. That was the kind of man one could rely on.
