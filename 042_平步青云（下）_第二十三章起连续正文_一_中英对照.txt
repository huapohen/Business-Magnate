第二十三章｜CHAPTER TWENTY-THREE

回到大兴客栈，阿巧姐一面收拾随身动用什物，一面问起胡雪岩此行的目的，这没有什么隐瞒的必要，而且也深知她不是那种无知无识，不懂轻重的妇女，所以他把实话都告诉了她。“学台是个啥个官？”“专管考秀才的。”“有没有外快？”“这我倒不大懂了。”胡雪岩说，“听说四川学台、广东学台是肥缺。江苏就不晓得了。照我想，现在兵荒马乱，好些地方连去都不能去。地盘一小，就有外快也有限。““如果是这样子，要请何学台去谋干一个好地方的官，只怕不成功。”“怎么呢？”“要钱呀！”阿巧姐笑一笑又说，“我是不懂啥！有一次一个候补道台汪老爷在怡情院请客，大讲官场的生意经，说是京里的大老倌那里，都要送钱的。钱越多，越容易升官。”“嗯，嗯！”胡雪岩被提醒了，暗地里打了主意，却不愿说破，因为其中出入关系甚大，即令是对阿巧姐这样的人，也是不说的好。“总还要送点礼啊！”阿巧姐又说。“那有了，备了四色洋货。”“何学台哪里人？”“云南。”“那不如送云南东西……”“啊，对！”胡雪岩大力赞赏：“阿巧，你的脑筋真不错。”

Back at Daxing Inn, A’qiao packed the things they would need and asked Hu Xueyan about the purpose of the journey. There was no reason to conceal it. He knew, moreover, that she was no ignorant woman without judgment or a sense of what mattered, so he told her the truth. “What sort of official is an education commissioner?” “He oversees the examinations for licentiates.” “Does he have any extra income?” “That I don't really understand. I hear that Sichuan and Guangdong are lucrative appointments. I don't know about Jiangsu. But with war and disorder everywhere, there are many places he can't even visit. With his territory reduced, any extra income must be limited.” “If that's so, asking Commissioner He to seek office in a good province may not work.” “Why?” “It takes money!” She smiled. “Not that I understand these things. But once, at Yiqing House, a Mr Wang awaiting a circuit appointment gave a party and talked at length about the business side of official life. You have to send money to the great gentlemen in the capital, he said. The more money, the easier promotion becomes.” “Mm, mm.” Reminded of this, Hu privately formed a plan, but did not explain it. Too much depended on it; even to a woman like A’qiao, it was better left unsaid. “You must at least take some gifts,” she added. “I have those—four kinds of foreign goods.” “Where is Commissioner He from?” “Yunnan.” “Then wouldn't things from Yunnan be better?” “Ah, exactly!” Hu praised her warmly. “A’qiao, you have a very good head.”

于是第二天一早，胡雪岩便去寻古应春，要觅云南土产，结果找着一个解铜到江苏藩司衙门的云南候补州判，在他那里转让了四佯云南土产。这四样土产是宣威火腿、紫大头菜、鸡踪菌和咸牛肉干，可惜数量不多，但也正因为数量不多，便显得物以稀为贵了。中午在怡情老二那里吃了饭，彼此约定，互不相送。等古应春替他安排护送的那个人一到，胡雪岩很客气地请教了“尊姓台甫”，然后一起上船，船是小火轮拖带的一条“无锡快”，胡雪岩带着阿巧姐住后舱，前舱止给护送的那个人住。

Early the next morning, Hu went to Gu Yingchun for help in finding Yunnan specialities. They eventually found a Yunnan official awaiting appointment as an assistant departmental magistrate, who had escorted copper to the Jiangsu provincial treasurer's yamen. From him they obtained four local products: Xuanwei ham, purple datoucai, jizong mushrooms and dried salted beef. Unfortunately, there was not much of any of them, but their scarcity made them all the more precious. After lunch at Second Miss's, the party agreed not to see one another off. When the man Gu had arranged to escort Hu arrived, Hu courteously asked his name, and they boarded together. Their vessel was a ‘Wuxi fast boat’ towed by a small steam launch. Hu and A’qiao occupied the aft cabin, while the forward cabin was left to their escort.

此人名叫周一鸣，湖南人，原在江南水师中当哨官，因为喜欢喝酒闹事，一次打伤了长官的小舅子，被责了二十军棍，开革除名。但同一鸣的酒德虽不好，为人倒极豪爽重义气，由于在水师当差，认识的船户颇不少，所以起先是跑码头、打秋风，大家也乐予周济，有时托他带个把口信，他倒也“食人之禄，忠人之事”，一定确确实实做到，慢慢地有了信用，便在上海船户的“茶会”上帮忙。各行各业的茶会，犹如同业公所，或者按头生意，或者与官场打交道，或者同业中有纠纷“吃讲茶”，都在茶会上商谈，周一鸣就成了船户茶会上的一名要角，特别是“抓船”、“派差”等等官面上硬压下来的公事，都由周一鸣出面去接头。这次也是有公事到苏州，古应春跟他相熟，正好把胡雪岩托了他，连雇船带护送，都归他包办，讲好送二十两银子。胡雪岩的出手大方是出名的，一上船就找了个红封套，装了一张三十两银子的银票，当面双手奉上。周一鸣还要客气，禁不住胡雪岩言词恳切，他千恩万谢地收了下来。这一路招呼得自是格外周到。

His name was Zhou Yiming, and he came from Hunan. He had once served as a detachment officer in the Jiangnan naval force, but was fond of drinking and making trouble. After injuring his superior's younger brother-in-law, he had received twenty strokes of the military cane and been dismissed. Though badly behaved in drink, Zhou was generous and loyal to his friends. His naval service had acquainted him with many boatmen, so at first he went round the wharves seeking handouts, which people willingly gave. Sometimes they asked him to carry a message. Having accepted their support, he faithfully discharged their business, making certain that every errand was properly done. Gradually he earned a reputation and began helping at the Shanghai boatmen's tea association. Such associations existed in every trade and functioned much like guildhalls. Whether to arrange business, deal with officials or settle disputes over tea, members discussed their affairs there. Zhou became an important figure in the boatmen's association. In particular, when officials imposed requisitions for boats or other compulsory service, he handled the negotiations. He had official business in Suzhou on this occasion. Gu, who knew him, therefore entrusted Hu to his care. He would hire the boat and provide the escort, for an agreed twenty taels. Hu was famous for his generosity. As soon as they boarded, he put a thirty-tael note into a red envelope and presented it with both hands. Zhou tried to decline, but Hu was so earnest that he accepted with repeated thanks. His attentions throughout the journey were naturally all the more thorough.

胡雪岩出门一向不喜欢带听差，于是周一鸣自告奋勇，到了苏州雇轿子，提行李，下客栈，都由他一手经理。客栈在阎门外，字号就叫“金阎”，等安置停当，周一鸣要告辞了。“胡大老爷！”因为胡雪岩是捐班候补知县，所以他这要称呼他，“我在苏州有个‘门口’，现在回去看一看。明天上半天到水师衙门去投文办事，中午过来伺候。你老看，行不行？”“我有个不情之请。”胡雪岩说，“有四件东西，一封信，想拜托你此刻就送一送。”“是了。”周一鸣问，“送到哪里？”“送给何学台。还得先打听一下，何学台公馆在哪里？”“这容易，都交给我好了。”于是胡雪岩托金间栈的帐房，写了个手本，下注：“寓阊门外金阎栈第三进西头”，连同四样云南土仪和一封王有龄的信，都交了给周一鸣。信是胡雪岩密封了的，内中附着一张五千两的银票，作为王有龄送何桂清的，这封信当然重要，所以胡雪岩特别叮嘱：“老周，还要麻烦你，务必跟何公馆的门上说明白，讨一张有何学台亲笔的回片。”“是！”周一鸣问，“今天要不要把回片送来？”胡雪岩心想，疑人莫用，用人莫疑，而且周一鸣人既重义气，又是有来历的，因而很快地答道：“如果回片上只写收到，那就不必来了，明天再说。”

Hu never liked taking a personal attendant on his travels, so Zhou volunteered to manage everything on arrival in Suzhou: hiring chairs, carrying luggage and finding an inn. The inn stood outside Chang Gate and was called Jinchang. Once they were settled, Zhou prepared to leave. “Your Honour”—he addressed Hu this way because Hu had purchased eligibility for appointment as a county magistrate—“I have a place here in Suzhou and shall go and look in. Tomorrow morning I must deliver documents and attend to business at the naval yamen. I'll come to wait on you at midday. Would that suit you?” “I have a rather troublesome favour to ask. Four gifts and a letter—could you deliver them now?” “Certainly. Where to?” “To Education Commissioner He. First we must find out where he's staying.” “That's easy. Leave it to me.” Hu had Jinchang Inn's bookkeeper prepare a formal visiting card, with the address below: “Lodging outside Chang Gate, Jinchang Inn, west side of the third courtyard.” He gave this to Zhou, together with the four Yunnan gifts and Wang Youling's letter. Hu had sealed the letter himself, enclosing a five-thousand-tael note as a gift from Wang to He Guiqing. The letter was important, so he gave particular instructions. “Old Zhou, one more trouble: you must explain to the doorkeeper that I need an acknowledgement in Commissioner He's own hand.” “Yes. Shall I bring it back today?” Hu reflected that one should not employ a man one distrusted, or distrust a man one employed. Zhou valued loyalty and had an established background. He answered at once, “If the card merely acknowledges receipt, you needn't come again. Tomorrow will do.”

等周一鸣一走，胡雪岩的迫不及待的想跟阿巧姐去观光。苏州不比上海，虽然妇女喜欢小庙烧香，凡有出会报赛等等人声鼎沸的场面，都要去轧个热闹，但一男一女不论是出现在玄妙观，还是虎丘山塘，总是招摇过市、惹人物议的一件事，而且阿巧姐是本乡本土，难免遇见熟人，尤须顾忌，因此，她更觉为难。就在这软语相磨，未定行止之际，只见周一鸣把顶红缨帽捏在手里当扇子扇，跑得满头大汗，却是笑容满面，胡雪岩当是何桂清有什么话交代，赶紧迎了出去。“送到了！”周一鸣说，“回贴在这里。”接过回贴来一看，只见上面写着一行字：“王太守函一件，收讫。外隆仪四色，敬领谢谢。”贴尾又有一行字，“敬使面致。”“胡大老爷，真要谢谢你挑我。”周一鸣垂着手打个千说：“何学台出手很阔，赏了我二十两银子。”听这一说，胡雪岩觉得很有面子，便说：“很好，你收下好了。”“我特为跟你老来说一声，何学台住在苏州府学。”“喔，你见着何学台没有？”“见是没有见着。不过听他们二爷出来说，学台很高兴。”高兴的是收到五千两银子，还是四色云南土产，或则两者兼而有之？胡雪岩就不知道了。不过不管怎么样，都算是得阿巧姐的力。

Once Zhou had gone, Hu was eager to go sightseeing with A’qiao. Suzhou was unlike Shanghai. Women there liked visiting temples to burn incense and joining the crowds at festivals and processions, but for a man and woman to appear together, whether at Xuanmiao Temple or along Shantang by Tiger Hill, was to parade themselves before the public and invite gossip. A’qiao was a native of the district and might meet acquaintances, which required still greater caution. She was consequently reluctant. While they were gently arguing the point, with no decision reached, Zhou returned, holding his red-tasselled cap as a fan. He had run himself into a sweat, yet his face was all smiles. Thinking He had sent some instructions, Hu hurried out. “Delivered! Here is the reply.” Hu took the card. It read, “One letter from Prefect Wang received. The four generous gifts are also accepted with thanks.” A further line at the foot stated, “Your messenger has been duly thanked in person.” Zhou made a formal greeting, hands lowered. “Your Honour, I must thank you for giving me this opportunity. Commissioner He is most generous. He gave me twenty taels.” This enhanced Hu's own standing, and he was pleased. “Excellent. Keep it.” “I came specially to tell you that the commissioner is staying at the Suzhou Prefectural Academy.” “Oh. Did you see him?” “No. But his steward came out and said he was delighted.” Whether He was delighted by the five thousand taels, the four Yunnan gifts, or both, Hu could not tell. In any event, he owed it to A’qiao.

因为如此，他便依从了她的意思，不勉强她一起出游。但打算一个人出去逛逛，这得先跟阿巧姐请教，正在谈着苏州城里的名园古刹，突然发现金阎栈的掌拒，行色匆匆，直奔了进来。“胡大老爷，胡大老爷！”掌柜说道：“何学台来拜，已经下轿了。”听这一说，胡雪岩倒有些着慌，第一，没有听差“接贴”，第二，自己该穿公服肃迎，时间上来不及了。所以一时有手足无措之感。还是阿巧姐比较沉着，“何学台穿啥衣服来的？”她问。“穿的便服。”“这还好！”胡雪岩接口说道：“来不及了，我也只好便服相迎。”说着，他便走了出去，阿巧姐也赶紧将屋里刚刚倒散未曾归理的行李，略略收拾了一下，在窗口张望，只等何桂清一到，便要回避。

For that reason, Hu accepted her wishes and did not press her to go out with him. He still intended to take a walk alone, but first sought her advice. They were discussing Suzhou's famous gardens and old temples when the innkeeper hurried straight in. “Your Honour! Education Commissioner He has come to call. He's already out of his chair.” Hu was alarmed. First, he had no attendant to receive the visiting card. Second, he ought to put on official dress to welcome his visitor, and there was no time. For a moment he did not know what to do. A’qiao was more composed. “What is the commissioner wearing?” “Informal clothes.” “That's a relief,” Hu said. “There's no time. I shall have to receive him informally too.” He went out. A’qiao hastily tidied the luggage they had unpacked but not yet put away, then watched at the window, ready to withdraw as soon as He arrived.

何桂清是走到第二进中门遇着胡雪岩的。虽然穿的便衣，但跟着两名青衣小帽的听差，便能认出他的身分，胡雪岩却还下敢造次，站住脚一青，这位来客年纪与自己用仿，生得极白净的一张脸，这模样与王有龄所形容的何桂清的仪表，完全相符，便知再不得错了。“何大人！”他迎面请个安说：“真不敢当。”“请起，请起！”何桂清拱拱手说：“想来足下就是雪岩兄了？”“不敢当此称呼！我是胡雪岩。”“幸会之至。”说着，何桂清又移动了脚步。于是胡雪岩引路，将何桂清引到自己屋里。就这几步路，做主人的转了好些念头，他发觉情况很尴尬，二品大员拜访一个初交，地点又是在客栈里，既没有象佯的堂奥可以容纳贵客，又没有听差可以供奔走之役。这样子就很难讲官场的仪节了。索性当他自己人！胡雪岩断然作了这样一个决定，首先就改了称呼，何桂清字根云，便仿照“雪公”的例，称他“云公”。接入客座，他这样说道：“公云，礼不可废，请上坐，让我这个候补知县参见！”这是打的一个“过门”，既是便服，又是这样的称呼，根本就没有以官场礼节参见的打算，何桂清是绝顶聪明的人，一听就懂，再替他设身处地想一想，倒又佩服他这别出一格的处置，因而笑道：“雪岩兄，不要说杀风景的话。我听雪轩谈过老兄，神交已久，要脱略形迹才好！”“是！恭敬不如从命！”胡雪岩一捐到地，站起身来说：“ 请里面坐吧！”

He Guiqing met Hu at the middle gateway of the second courtyard. Though informally dressed, he was followed by two attendants in dark gowns and small caps, which revealed his standing. Hu nevertheless dared not take anything for granted. He stopped for a look. The visitor was about his own age, with an exceptionally fair, clean-cut face, exactly as Wang had described. There could be no mistake. He stepped forward and made a formal bow of greeting. “Your Excellency! I don't deserve such an honour.” “Please rise, please rise.” He returned a clasped-hands salute. “You must be Brother Xueyan?” “I don't deserve that form of address. I am Hu Xueyan.” “A great pleasure to meet you.” He began walking again, and Hu led him towards his rooms. In those few steps, many thoughts passed through the host's mind. The situation was awkward. An official of the second rank was calling on a new acquaintance at an inn, with neither a proper reception hall for such a guest nor servants to attend them. The ceremonies of official life would be difficult to observe. He would simply treat He as an intimate. Having decided, he first changed his form of address. He's courtesy name was Genyun; following the familiar respectful form he used for Wang as Mr Xuexuan, Hu addressed He as Mr Genyun. “Mr Genyun,” he said on bringing him into the reception area, “we mustn't neglect propriety. Please take the place of honour and let this county magistrate awaiting appointment pay his respects.” This was merely an opening flourish. Informal dress and so familiar an address showed that he had no intention of observing full official ceremony. He, a man of exceptional intelligence, understood at once. Putting himself in Hu's place, he rather admired this unconventional solution. He smiled. “Brother Xueyan, don't spoil the occasion. I've heard Xuexuan speak of you and have long felt I knew you. Let's dispense with formalities.” “Certainly. Obedience is better than ceremony.” Hu made a deep bow, then straightened. “Please come inside.”

这才真的是脱略形迹，一见面就延入内室，何桂清略一踌躇，也就走了进去。一进门却又赶紧退了出来，因为看到一具闺阁中用的镜箱，还有两件女衣。“宝眷大此，不好唐突！”“不妨，不妨。”胡雪岩一面说，一面便喊：“阿巧，你出来见见何老爷。”何桂清还在迟疑之际，突然眼前一亮，就不肯再退出去了，望着走几步路如风摆杨柳似的阿巧姐，向胡雪岩问道：“怎么称呼？是如嫂夫人？”“不是！”胡雪岩说：“云公叫她小名阿巧好了。”就这对答间，阿巧姐已经含笑叫一声：“何老爷！”同时盈盈下拜。“不敢当，不敢当！请起来。”男女授受不亲，不便动手去扶，到底让阿巧姐跪了一跪，她站起来说一声：“何老爷请坐！”然后翩然走了出去，听她在喊客栈里的伙计泡盖碗茶。

This was truly dispensing with formality: at their first meeting, Hu invited him straight into the inner room. He hesitated briefly, then entered. But he immediately stepped out again on seeing a woman's mirror case and two articles of female clothing. “Your lady is here. I mustn't intrude.” “Not at all, not at all.” Hu called, “A’qiao, come and meet Mr He.” While He was still hesitating, his eyes suddenly brightened, and he no longer wished to withdraw. Watching A’qiao approach, swaying gracefully like a willow in the breeze, he asked Hu, “How should I address her? Is she your concubine?” “No. Just call her by her own name, A’qiao.” As they spoke, she smiled and greeted him. “Mr He.” Then she gracefully sank to her knees. “I don't deserve this. Please rise!” Propriety forbade physical contact between men and women, so he could not help her up and had to accept the kneeling salutation. She rose, invited him to sit and went lightly out. They heard her calling to the inn's waiter for tea in lidded cups.

真是当做自己人看待，何桂清也就不再拘束，坐在窗前上首一张椅子上，首先向胡雪岩道谢：“多蒙专程下顾，隆仪尤其心感。天南万里，何况烽火，居然得尝家乡风味，太难得了。”“说实话，是阿巧姐的主意。”“可人，可人！”何桂清的视线又落在正在装果碟子的阿巧姐身上。“没有好东西请何老爷吃，意思意思。”阿巧姐捧了四个果碟子走过来说，四个果碟子是她带在路上的闲食，一碟洋糖、一碟蜜枣、一碟杭州的香榧、一碟是昆山附近的黄埭瓜子。“谢谢！”何桂清目光随着她那一双雪白的手转，蓦然警觉，这忘形的神态是失礼的，便收拢眼光，看着胡雪岩说：“雪岩兄是哪天到的？”“今天刚到。”“从杭州来？”“不，到上海有几天了。”胡雪岩说，“本想请个人来送信。因为久慕云公，很想见一见，所以专诚来一趟。”“盛情可感之至。”何桂清拱拱手，“不知道雪岩兄有儿日勾留？”不说耽搁说勾留，这些文绉绉的话，胡雪岩是跟嵇鹤龄相处得有了些日子，才能听懂，因而也用很雅饬的修辞答道：“此来专为奉谒。顺道访一访灵岩、虎丘，总有三、五日盘桓。”“老兄真是福气人！”何桂清指着阿巧姐说：“隽侣又携，载酒看山，不要说是这种乱世，就是承平时节，也是人生难得之事。”

Treated genuinely as one of the household, He dropped his reserve. Taking the chair in the place of honour by the window, he first thanked Hu. “You've honoured me with a special journey, and I'm especially touched by the gifts. My home is so far away in the south, with war between us as well. To taste its flavours here is a rare pleasure.” “To tell the truth, it was A’qiao's idea.” “What a delightful woman!” His eyes returned to her as she arranged dishes of snacks. “There's nothing very fine for you, Mr He, only a little token,” she said, bringing over four dishes. These were the provisions she had brought to eat on the journey: foreign sweets, candied dates, Hangzhou torreya nuts and Huangdai melon seeds from near Kunshan. “Thank you.” His gaze followed her pair of snowy-white hands. Suddenly realizing that such an unguarded stare was discourteous, he drew his eyes back to Hu. “When did you arrive, Brother Xueyan?” “Only today.” “From Hangzhou?” “No. I've been in Shanghai for several days. I had meant to send someone with the letter, but I've long admired you and wanted to meet you, so I made the journey myself.” “I'm most grateful for your kindness.” He clasped his hands. “How many days will you linger here?” It was his time with Ji Heling that enabled Hu to understand such elegantly literary wording as ‘linger’ instead of the plain ‘stay’. He answered with equal refinement. “My visit is expressly to pay my respects to you. I shall also take the opportunity to visit Lingyan and Tiger Hill, so I expect to sojourn here for three to five days.” “You are a fortunate man!” He indicated A’qiao. “A charming companion at your side, wine aboard and hills to admire—rare pleasures in life, even in peaceful times, let alone these troubled days.”

阿巧姐听不懂他说的什么，但估量必是在说自己，而且料定是好话。再看这位“何老爷”，是“白面书生”的模样，不道已经戴上了红顶子，说来有些叫人不能相信，转念又想，“说书先生”常常讲的，落难公子中状元，放作“七省巡按”，随带上方宝剑，有恩报恩，有仇报仇，怕正就是象眼前“何老爷”这样子的人。心里如此七颠八倒的在想，一双勾魂摄魄的眼睛，便不住看着何桂清。那位阿巧姐眼中的“白面书生”，心里也是说不出的滋味。同时不断在想：她是什么路数，与胡雪岩是怎么回事？因为如此，口中便不知道跟胡雪岩在讲些什么？直到阿巧姐悄悄起去，倩影消失，他才警觉，既不安、又好笑，想想不能再坐下去了，否则神魂颠倒，不知会有什么笑话闹出来？“我告辞！”他说，“今晚上奉屈小酌，我要好好请教。”“不敢当。”“雪岩兄！”何桂清很认真地说，“我不是客套。雪轩跟你的交情，我是知道的，他信中也提起，说你‘足智多谋，可共肝胆’，我有好些话，要跟老兄商议。”“既如此，我就遵命了。”“这才好。”何桂清欣然又说，“我不约别人，就是我们两个。回头我具柬贴来。”

A’qiao did not understand his words, but guessed that they concerned her and were complimentary. Looking at this Mr He, so like a fair-faced young scholar, she could scarcely believe he already wore the red button of high rank. Then she remembered the storytellers' tales: a young gentleman in distress came first in the examinations and was sent out as imperial inspector of seven provinces, carrying the sovereign's sword, rewarding kindness and avenging wrongs. Such a man must look just like the Mr He before her. As these thoughts tumbled through her mind, her enchanting eyes kept returning to him. The ‘fair-faced scholar’ she was watching had feelings of his own that he could not put into words. Who was she? What was her relationship with Hu? So absorbed was he in these questions that he scarcely knew what he was saying to his host. Only when A’qiao quietly rose and her lovely figure disappeared did he come to himself, embarrassed and amused. He had better leave. If he stayed, who knew what foolishness his distraction might produce? “I must take my leave,” he said. “Please honour me at a little dinner tonight. I have much to ask you.” “You do me too much honour.” “Brother Xueyan, this is no empty courtesy,” He said earnestly. “I know your friendship with Xuexuan. In his letter he calls you ‘resourceful and worthy of complete trust’. There are many things I want to discuss with you.” “In that case, I shall obey.” “Excellent. I shall invite no one else, only the two of us. I'll send a formal invitation presently.”

于是胡雪岩将何桂清送了出门，等他上了轿，回到自己屋里，看见阿巧姐在收拾果盘，想起她刚才跟何桂清眉来眼去的光景，心里便有些酸溜溜地，不大得劲。“这位何老爷，”阿巧姐说，“看上去年纪比你还轻。”“是啊！”胡雪岩说，“我看他不过比你大两三岁，正好配得上你。”“瞎三话四！”阿巧姐白了他一眼。她不再说话，胡雪岩也懒得开口，一个人歪在床上想心思，想东想西，百无聊赖。看看天快黑下来了，外面又有掌柜的声间，急促地在喊：“胡大老爷，胡大老爷！”这声音喊得人心慌，赶紧一骨碌起身，迎了出去，只见前面是掌柜，后面跟着个戴红缨帽的听差，手里夹一个“护书”，见了胡雪岩，抢上两步打个千说：“小的何福，给胡大老爷请安。敞上特地叫小的来迎接，轿子在门口，请胡大老爷就动身吧！”说着递了一份贴子上来。贴子写的是：“即夕申刻奉迓便酌。”下款具名：“教愚弟何桂清谨订。”“喔！好，我就走。”胡雪岩回到屋里，只见阿巧姐已取了一件马褂，作势等他来穿。

Hu saw him out to his chair and returned to his room. A’qiao was clearing away the dishes. Remembering the glances she and He had exchanged, Hu felt a sour touch of jealousy and discomfort. “That Mr He looks younger than you,” she said. “Yes. I'd say he's only two or three years older than you. Just the right match.” “What nonsense!” She gave him a reproachful look. She said no more, and Hu had no inclination to speak either. He lay on the bed turning one thought after another over in his mind, bored and restless. Dusk was approaching when the innkeeper's hurried voice sounded outside again. “Your Honour! Your Honour!” The cries were alarming. Hu sprang up and went out. Behind the innkeeper stood an attendant in a red-tasselled cap, carrying a document case. He stepped forward and made a formal greeting. “Your servant He Fu pays his respects. My master has sent me specially to fetch you. The chair is at the door. Please set out now.” He presented a card: “I respectfully invite you to an informal dinner this evening at the shen hour.” It was signed, “Your junior and humble pupil, He Guiqing.” “Ah, good. I'll come at once.” Hu returned inside and found A’qiao holding a riding jacket ready for him to put on.

“留你一个人在客栈里了！”胡雪岩说了这一句，忽起试探的念头，“等我到了那里，请何老爷派人来接你好不好？”这应该算作绝顶荒唐的念头，主客初会，身分不同，离通家之好还有十万八千里，就算一见如故，脱略形迹，而她是“妾身未分明”，怎能入官宦之家？再迟一步而论，算是有了名分，胡家的姨太太，也得何家的内亲眷派人来接，怎么样也不能说由“何老爷”来邀堂客！因此，阿巧姐的表情应该是惊异，或者笑一笑，照苏州人的说法：“亏你想得出！”甚至，置之下理，表示无可与言，亦在意中。而她什么都不是，只这样答说：“不好意思的！”是怎么样的不好意思，就颇耐人寻味了。胡雪岩便报以一笑，不再说下去了。等坐上轿子，心里还一直在研究阿巧姐的态度，他很冷静，就当估量一笔有暴利可图，但亦可能大蚀其本的大生意那样，不动感情，纯从利害去考虑。考虑到轿子将停，他大致已经有了主见，暂且搁下，抖擞精神来对付这个新交的贵人。

“I must leave you alone at the inn.” As he said this, a desire to test her suddenly occurred to him. “When I get there, shall I ask Mr He to send someone to fetch you?” The idea was absurd in the extreme. Host and guest were newly acquainted and of different standing, far from the intimacy that allowed their families to visit freely. Even if they felt like old friends and dispensed with ceremony, A’qiao's position was still undefined. How could she enter an official household? Even supposing she had acquired a recognized position as Hu's concubine, an invitation must come from the women of the He family. Mr He could not possibly invite a female guest himself. Her answer ought therefore to have been astonishment, or a smile and the Suzhou expression, ‘What an idea to come up with!’ She might even ignore it as unworthy of an answer. Instead, she merely said, “I couldn't presume.” What kind of hesitation did that express? It invited speculation. Hu smiled and pursued the matter no further. Once in the chair, he continued to examine her response. Calmly, without emotion, he weighed advantage and disadvantage as though considering a business venture that might yield enormous profit or swallow its entire capital. By the time the chair was about to stop, he had broadly made up his mind. He put the matter aside for now and rallied himself to deal with his new acquaintance of high rank.

何桂清是借住在苏州府学的西花厅，厅中用屏风隔成三间，最外一间，当作“签押房”，接见是在第二间，书房的格局，布置得雅洁有效。胡雪岩到时，他正在写大字，放下未写成的对联，欢然待客。但见他穿一件枣红宁绸的夹袍，外套一字襟的玄色软缎坎肩，戴一顶六角形的折帽，一种象扇子样，可以折起来，置入衣袋中的爪皮小帽，这副打扮，哪里象个考秀才的学台？倒象洋场中的纨袴.“雪岩兄！”何桂清潇洒的将手一摆，“你看，就你我俩，无话不可谈。”作此表示，非同寻常，胡雪岩相当感动，但也格外慎重，“云公，”他以端然的神色说，“雪公把信交给我的时候，特别叮嘱，云公如果有什么吩咐，务必照办。这句话，我亦不肯随便出口，因为怕力量有限办不到。如今我不妨跟云公说，即使办不到，我觉得云公一定也会体谅，所以有话尽请吩咐。”这话已经说到头了，何桂清也就无所顾虑，很坦率他说：“黄寿臣是我的同年，他如果不走，我不便有所表示，现在听说他有调动的消息，论资格，我接他的缺，也不算意外，所以雪轩为我设谋，倒也不妨计议计议。不过，费了好大的劲，所得的如果是‘鸡肋’，那就不上算了。你看，浙江的情形，到底怎么样？”胡雪岩不懂“爵之无味，弃之可惜”的“鸡肋”作何解？不过整段话的意思，大致可以明白，是问浙江巡抚这个缺分的好坏。“浙江当然不如江苏，不过，有一点比江苏好！到底还不曾打仗。”“虽未打仗，替江南大营办粮台，还有安徽的防务，也得帮忙，为人作嫁，颇不上算。”“这也不见得。”胡雪岩答道，“如果是个清闲无事的缺，只怕云公亦未必肯屈就。“

He Guiqing was lodging in the west reception hall of the Suzhou Prefectural Academy. Screens divided it into three rooms. The outermost served as an office for handling documents, and he received visitors in the second, furnished as a study with tasteful simplicity. When Hu arrived, He was practising large calligraphy. He laid aside an unfinished pair of couplets and welcomed his guest warmly. He wore a lined robe of jujube-red Ning silk beneath a black soft-satin waistcoat with a straight fastening, and a little six-sided cap that folded like a fan and could be slipped into a pocket. In this attire, he looked less like an education commissioner examining licentiates than a fashionable young man-about-town in the foreign settlement. “Brother Xueyan!” He waved a hand with easy grace. “As you see, there are only the two of us. Nothing need be left unsaid.” This was no ordinary declaration. Hu was touched, but all the more cautious. “Mr Genyun, when Mr Xuexuan gave me the letter, he particularly instructed me to carry out any request you might make. I didn't care to repeat that lightly, for fear my abilities might fall short. Now I can say it, because I feel certain you would understand even if I failed. Please tell me freely what you wish.” This was as far as such an assurance could go. He put aside his remaining doubts and spoke openly. “Huang Shouchen passed in the same examination year as I did. While he stays, I can't make any move. Now I hear he may be transferred. By seniority, there would be nothing surprising in my succeeding him, so Xuexuan's plan for me is worth considering. But after a great deal of effort, if all I get is a chicken rib, it won't be worthwhile. What exactly is the situation in Zhejiang?” Hu did not understand the allusion to a chicken rib, tasteless to chew yet regrettable to discard. But he understood the general question: how desirable was the governorship of Zhejiang? “Naturally, Zhejiang cannot compare with Jiangsu. But in one respect it's better: there has been no fighting there yet.” “True, but it must run a supply office for the Jiangnan Grand Camp and help with Anhui's defence. Preparing a bride's trousseau for someone else is hardly worthwhile.” “Not necessarily,” Hu answered. “If it were an idle, uneventful post, you might not care to accept it.”

“这倒是真话。”何桂清颇有深获我心之感，“我这个江苏学政，照承平时候来说，也就仅仅次于‘提督顺天学政’，这是因为京畿之地，论人才，又何尝及得上贵处江南？所以江苏学政的是否得人，关乎国家的气运，人才的消长。谁知两百年来，我适逢其会，遇上这么个用兵的时候，如今是只讲战备，不修文治，加以地方沦陷的很多，我原可躲躲懒，但此时不讲培育，战乱一年，人才中断，那就是我的误国之罪了。所以借地科考，辗转跋涉，自觉也对得起皇上，对得起江苏百姓了。”胡雪岩也曾听说过，何桂清这个江苏学政做得相当起劲，本职以外，常有奏疏论军务，本意以为他越俎代庖，迹近多事，现在听他谈到“借地科考，辗转跋涉”，才知道未乔所职，心里不觉浮起敬意。但这方面他无可赞一词，唯有凝神倾听，不断点头而已。“老爷！”有个丫头走来说，“请客人入席吧。”“请吧！真正是小酌，”何桂清说，“而且是借花献佛。”

“That's true.” He felt that Hu had understood him very well. “In peaceful times, my appointment as education commissioner of Jiangsu would rank just below the education commissionership of Shuntian. That is because Shuntian embraces the capital. In talent, how could it compare with your Jiangnan? Whether the right man holds Jiangsu's educational office therefore affects the fortunes of the state and the rise or decline of its talent. Who could have expected that, after two hundred years, I should happen to hold it at a time of war? Everyone speaks of military preparations and neglects civil learning. Much of the territory has fallen, so I could have taken things easily. But if we fail to cultivate scholars now, a year of war could interrupt the succession of talent. I should be guilty of failing the country. I've therefore borrowed premises elsewhere to hold the examinations, travelling from place to place. I feel I've done my duty to the emperor and the people of Jiangsu.” Hu had heard that He pursued his work with considerable energy. Besides his regular duties, he often submitted memorials on military matters. Hu had thought he was interfering in others' business and almost making unnecessary trouble. Now, hearing how he held examinations in borrowed premises and travelled about, he realized that He had not neglected his own office. Respect rose in him, though he knew too little of the subject to offer a word of appreciation. He could only listen closely and keep nodding. A maid approached. “Master, please invite your guest to the table.” “Come. It really is only a little dinner,” He said, “and I'm offering you flowers borrowed from yourself.”

果然，六样菜倒有四样的材料，出自胡雪岩送的那四色云南土产，当中一个一品锅，揭开来看，形式与众不同，中间“朝天一柱”，多出个嘴了，里面是一锅鸡块，汤汁极清，微带糟香，不觉就在喉间咽了一口唾沫。“这大概就是‘汽锅鸡’了。”胡雪岩说，“久闻其名，还是初次见识。”“这鸡也就是喝点汤。做法并不麻烦。难得的是家伙，这汽锅，我曾托人到宜兴仿制，怎么样也不合适。”何桂清说到这里，忽然问道：“雪岩兄到敝处去过没有？”“没有。不过我久慕昆明是侗夭福地，四季如春，山明水秀。”胡雪岩又说，“俗语道得好，人杰地灵，有这样的好地方，才能出云公这样的人物。”“过奖，过奖！”何桂清说，“你总听雪轩说过，我不是云南土著。”肯提到这一点，也就表示不讳他的身世，胡雪岩转念到此，便理解到何桂清真的是拿自己当知心朋友看待。不过，自己却不便透露已尽知他的底细，所以这样答道：“略知一二。雪公也是很佩服云公的。”“我跟他的交情不同，你跟他的交情也不同。所以今后你不要见外才好。”“是！是！承蒙云公不弃，我敬云公亦象敬雪公一样。”“敬则不敢，但愿你不分彼此。来‘相见欢’，请干了这一杯。”

Indeed, four of the six dishes were made from Hu's four Yunnan gifts. In the middle stood a covered casserole. When the lid was lifted, Hu saw an unusual construction: a spout rose like a pillar through its centre. Inside were pieces of chicken in a very clear broth, faintly fragrant with fermented grain. He swallowed involuntarily. “This must be steam-pot chicken. I've long heard of it, but never seen it before.” “The broth is really the point. It's not difficult to make; the rare thing is the vessel. I've had people try to reproduce this steam pot at Yixing, but it never came out right.” Suddenly He asked, “Have you ever visited my part of the country?” “No. But I've long admired Kunming as a blessed place, with spring throughout the year, beautiful hills and clear waters. As they say, fine land produces outstanding men. Only such a place could produce someone like you.” “You flatter me. Surely Xuexuan has told you I'm not a native of Yunnan?” His willingness to mention this showed that he did not conceal his origins. Hu realized that He truly regarded him as a confidential friend. Yet it would be awkward to reveal that he already knew the whole story, so he answered, “A little. Mr Xuexuan greatly admires you too.” “My friendship with him is unusual, and so is yours. You mustn't stand on ceremony with me from now on.” “Certainly. Since you're willing to accept me, I shall respect you just as I respect Mr Xuexuan.” “I don't ask for respect, only that you make no distinction between us. Come, a cup to the joy of meeting. Let us drain it.”

两个人都干了照杯。然后低斟慢饮，继续谈浙江的情形。胡雪岩认为已不需怂恿他作何打算，只就浙江的吏治、民生、人情、风土，尽其所知地细细陈述。何桂清听得很仔细，偶尔也发一两句问，问的都是地方的形势，胡雪岩听得出来，他的兴趣是在军务上，倘或防守没有把握，他对浙江巡抚这个缺，就不见得会有兴趣。谈到最后，何桂清对他的出处，作了透露：“我这个学政是一定不干了。以后于什么，却还打不定主意。“官场上的花样，胡雪岩所了解的，只到府县为止，省里的事，还可以猜得出来。至于京官以后许多特殊的缺分，他就不懂了，所以对何桂清的话，无可置答。“你知道，我们那一榜，道光十五年乙未，现在算是最得意了。这是因为当年穆相国的提拔，穆相国你知道吧？”“说来惭愧。我还不大清楚。”“这也怪你不来，你不是我们这一路上的人……”

They drained their cups and showed them empty. Refilling their cups sparingly and drinking slowly, they continued to discuss Zhejiang. Hu thought there was no need to urge any particular course upon him now. He simply described all he knew of the province's administration, people's livelihoods, customs and local conditions. He Guiqing listened carefully and occasionally asked a question, always about the situation on the ground. Hu could tell that his interest lay in military affairs. If defence seemed uncertain, the Zhejiang governorship might not appeal to him. At last He revealed something of his career plans. “I certainly shan't remain education commissioner. What I'll do afterwards is still undecided.” Hu's understanding of official life extended only as far as prefectures and counties. He could guess at provincial affairs, but appointments in the capital and the many special offices were beyond him. He therefore had no answer. “You know, our examination class—the yiwei year, the fifteenth of Daoguang—is the most successful now. That is because Grand Secretary Mu advanced us all those years ago. You know who he is?” “I'm ashamed to say I don't really.” “You can hardly be blamed. You aren't one of our kind of people...”

何桂清接下来更为胡雪岩“穆相国”——道光朝的权相穆彰阿。乙未科会试，是他的大主考，十五年工夫，尽是提拔门生，内而军机部院，外而巡抚藩桌，遍布要津，所以穆彰阿虽在当今咸丰皇帝接位的第二年垮了下来，但乙未科同榜，羽翼已经丰满，个个可以振翅高飞，不但不受老师垮台的影响，而且老师反因门生的力量，仅仅得了个革职的处分，不曾象当年“和坤跌倒”那样，搞成抄家送命的悲惨结局。“所以，”何桂清话锋一转，谈到自己，“我不能轻弃机会，动是总要动的，现在不是承平之世，学政没有干头。如果说想到浙江去，变成控黄寿臣的根，同年相好，说不过去。叫我回去当礼部侍郎的本缺，亦实在没有意思。我在想，象仓场侍郎之类的缺分，倒不妨过个渡。”“仓场侍郎”这个官称，胡雪岩倒是知道，因为与漕运有关，听王有龄和嵇鹤龄都谈过。仓场侍郎驻通州，专管漕粮的接收、存贮，下面有十一个仓监督，是个肥缺，做两三年下来，外放巡抚，便有了做清官的资格，因为宦囊已丰，不必再括地皮。

He went on to explain: Grand Secretary Mu was Muzhang’a, the powerful minister of the Daoguang reign. As chief examiner at the metropolitan examination in the yiwei year, he had spent the next fifteen years promoting his pupils. They filled key posts everywhere: the Grand Council, ministries and courts at the centre, and governorships, treasurerships and judicial commissions in the provinces. Thus, although Muzhang’a had fallen in the second year after the present Xianfeng Emperor's accession, his yiwei pupils had fully grown wings and could each fly high. Their teacher's fall had not harmed them. On the contrary, their influence had saved him from anything worse than dismissal. He had not suffered Heshen's fate of confiscation and death. “So I can't lightly throw away an opportunity,” He continued, turning to his own affairs. “I must make a move. These aren't peaceful times, and there is little future in an education commissionership. If I said I wanted Zhejiang, it would amount to undermining Huang Shouchen. We're friends from the same examination class; I couldn't justify that. Returning to my substantive post as vice-minister of rites holds no interest either. I've been thinking that a post such as granary vice-minister might serve as an intermediate step.” Hu knew that title because it concerned the grain tribute and both Wang and Ji had mentioned it. The granary vice-minister was stationed at Tongzhou and supervised the receipt and storage of tribute grain, with eleven granary supervisors below him. It was a lucrative office. After two or three years, a man could be appointed a provincial governor with the means to behave honestly: his official purse would already be full, and he need no longer squeeze the people.

胡雪岩的脑筋快，一下子想到浙江的海运，从王有龄到嵇鹤龄，海运局的麻烦还很多，有许多核销的帐目，要靠通州方面的帮忙，如果何洼清能够去掌管其事，一切都方便了。于是他说：“云公，你这个打算，真正不错！说到这上头，我倒有微劳可效。天下的漕粮重在江浙，浙江方面的海运，只要云公坐镇通州，说什么便是什么，一定遵照云公的意思办理。““喔，”何桂清问：“浙江的海运，雪轩已经交卸了，你何以有这样的握握？”“雪公虽已交卸，现在的坐办嵇鹤龄，跟雪公仍旧有极深的渊源。嵇某人是我拜把的兄弟。”“原来如此！”何桂清欣喜中有惊异，觉得事情真有这么凑巧，倒是意想不到。“至于江苏方面的海运，云公想必比我还清楚，而且由江苏调过去，不论谁来办，必都是熟人，自然一切容易说话。”说到这里，胡雪岩作了一个结论：“总而言之，云公去干这个缺，是人地个宜。”“能人地相宜，就可以政通人和。”何桂清停了一下，又说，“我本来只是随便起的一个念头，不想跟你一谈，倒谈出名堂来了。我已写了信到京里，想进京去一趟，‘陛见’的上谕，大概快下来了，准定设法调仓场。”

Hu's quick mind turned at once to Zhejiang's sea transport. From Wang to Ji, the Sea Transport Bureau had faced many difficulties. Numerous accounts still needed Tongzhou's help to obtain approval. If He could take charge there, everything would become easier. “Mr Genyun, that is an excellent plan! On this subject, I can be of some small service. The grain tribute depends chiefly on Jiangsu and Zhejiang. As far as Zhejiang's sea transport is concerned, once you are established at Tongzhou, your word will be law. Everything will be done as you wish.” “Oh?” He asked. “Xuexuan has handed over Zhejiang's sea transport. How can you be so certain?” “He has, but the present executive director, Ji Heling, still has very close ties with him. Ji is my sworn brother.” “So that's how it is!” He was delighted and surprised. Such a fortunate connection was quite unexpected. “As for Jiangsu's sea transport, you must know more than I do. And since you'll be transferring from Jiangsu, whoever handles it will be someone you know. Naturally, arrangements will be easy. In short, you and the post are perfectly suited.” “With the right man in the right place, administration runs smoothly and people live in harmony.” He paused. “It was only a passing thought at first. Talking with you has given it substance. I've already written to the capital and mean to go there. The imperial order summoning me to an audience should come soon. I'll definitely seek a transfer to the granaries.”

何桂清肯说到这样的话，便见得已拿胡雪岩当作无话不谈的心腹。听话的人了解，人与人之间，交情跟关系的建立与进展，全靠在这种地方有个扎实的表示。这一步跨越不了，密友亦会变成泛泛之交。因此，胡雪岩当然不会轻易放过。“云公！我敢说，你的打算，不能再好了。事不宜迟，就该放手进行。不过，有句话，我不知道说得冒昧不冒昧？““你不曾说，我怎么知道？”何桂情剥着指甲，眼睛望着他自己的手，是准备接受他那句“冒昧”话的神气。“听说藩司进一趟京，起码得花两万银子，可是有这话？”“这也不能一概而论，中等省份够了，象江苏这样一等一的大省就不够。仅仅陛见述职够了，如果有公事接头，或者请款，或者报销，那‘部费’就没得底，两万银子哪里够？““照这样说，有所谋干，就更不够了。”“这也要看缺分、看圣眷、看朝里有人无人而定。象我这趟去，就花不了多少钱。”“那么，”胡雪岩敛眉正视，一个字、一个字很清楚地问：“到底要多少呢？”何桂清不即回答，乱眨着眼，念念有词地数着指头，好久才说：“若有一万五千银子，尽足敷用。”

For He to speak so openly meant he now regarded Hu as a confidant with whom he could discuss anything. Hu understood that friendships and connections were established and strengthened by some concrete expression of commitment at just such a moment. Fail to take that step, and even close friends could become mere acquaintances. He would not let this opportunity slip. “Mr Genyun, I can say that your plan could not be better. There is no time to lose. You should go ahead. But there's something I don't know whether I may venture to say.” “How can I know until you've said it?” He picked at his nails, looking down at his hands as though preparing to receive the presumptuous remark. “I hear that a provincial treasurer's journey to the capital costs at least twenty thousand taels. Is that true?” “One can't generalize. For a middling province it would be enough; for a first-rank province such as Jiangsu, it wouldn't. It would cover merely attending an imperial audience and reporting on one's duties. But if there is official business to transact—requesting funds or getting accounts approved—there is no limit to the departmental fees. How could twenty thousand suffice?” “Then it would be still less adequate if one were seeking an appointment.” “That too depends on the office, imperial favour and whether one has people at court. My own journey this time won't cost very much.” Hu looked at him directly, his brows drawn, and asked with deliberate clarity, “How much, exactly?” He did not reply at once. Blinking repeatedly and murmuring, he counted on his fingers. After some time he said, “Fifteen thousand taels would be amply sufficient.”

“云公，”胡雪岩一笑，又放正了脸色，“你老知道的，我做钱庄，我们这行生意，最怕‘烂头寸’，你老这趟进京，总要用我一点才好。”这一说，何桂清的表情便很复杂了，惊喜而兼困惑，仿佛还不十分懂他的话似地，是有点不懂，细想一想才算弄明白，但亦不知道自己的解释对不对，所以话说得不很利落。“雪岩兄，你的意思是想放一笔款子给我？”“是的。”胡雪岩很率直，也很清楚地回答：“我想放一万五千银子的帐给云公。利息特别克己，因为我的头寸多，总比烂在那里好。”“期限呢？”“云公自己说。”何桂清又答不上来了，他要好好盘算一下，却又无从算起，因为只知道仓场侍郎的缺不错，一年到底有多少进帐并不知道。看他迟疑，胡雪岩便说，“我替云公出个主意，在京城里，我替云公介绍一家票号，云公的款子都存在他那里，看情形办，钱多多还，钱少少还，期限不定，你老看如何？”“好，好，就是这么办。不过我不必用那么多，只要一万就可以了。”

Hu smiled, then became serious again. “Mr Genyun, you know I run a bank. What we dread most in our trade is idle money. You must use some of mine for this journey.” He's expression became complicated: delighted surprise mixed with uncertainty, as though he had not quite understood. In fact, he had not. Only after reflection did he grasp the meaning, and even then he doubted his interpretation. He spoke hesitantly. “Brother Xueyan, do you mean you wish to lend me money?” “Yes.” Hu answered directly and clearly. “I'd like to lend you fifteen thousand taels. The interest would be especially modest. I have plenty of funds, and that is better than leaving them idle.” “And the term?” “Whatever you say.” Again the commissioner had no answer. He needed to calculate, but had no basis for doing so. He knew the granary vice-minister's post was a good one, but not what it yielded in a year. Seeing his hesitation, Hu offered a suggestion. “I'll introduce you to a remittance bank in the capital. Keep your funds there, and repay according to circumstances: more when you have more, less when you have less, with no fixed term. How would that be?” “Good, good. Let us do that. But I don't need so much. Ten thousand will be enough.”

胡雪岩知道，五千已有着落，还是自己听了阿巧姐的话，亲手封进去的银票，但不便说破，怎么呢？不还差五千吗？他故意这样问。何桂清也不肯说破，王有龄在信中，已附了五千银子，只是这样答道：“不敷之数，我另外找人凑一凑，也就差不多了。”胡雪岩肚子里雪亮，便点点头说：“那么，请云公的示，我那一万银子，送到哪里？”这平平常常的一句话，应该是极容易回答的，而何桂清竞开不得口！因为这件事说起来未免令人觉得突兀而骤难相信。一万银子不是小数，初次见面，三言两语便大把捧出来借与人，不要中，不要保，还不必讲利息和期限，这不太少见？这样茫然想着，忽有领悟，胡雪岩这样做法，固可解释为王有龄的交情使然，但他本人是否有所图谋呢？生意人的算盘，无论如何是精明的，还是先问一问清楚的好。“雪岩兄”，他很吃力他说，“你真的是所谓‘烂头寸’？”问到这话，胡雪岩觉得不必再说假话，因而这样模棱地答道：“就算头寸不烂，云公的大事，我亦不能不勉力效劳。”

Hu knew the other five thousand was already provided for: he himself had sealed the note inside the letter after listening to A’qiao. But he could not say so openly. He deliberately asked, “Why? Aren't you still five thousand short?” The commissioner likewise would not reveal that Wang's letter had enclosed five thousand. He merely said, “I can ask someone else to make up the shortfall. That should be enough.” Understanding perfectly, Hu nodded. “Then please tell me where I should send the ten thousand.” It was an ordinary question, apparently easy to answer, yet the commissioner could not speak. The whole affair was so sudden as to seem scarcely believable. Ten thousand taels was no small sum. At a first meeting, after a few words, a man offered it as a loan, without an intermediary or guarantor, without even settling the interest or the term. Was that not extraordinary? While he wondered, a thought struck him. Hu's conduct might be explained by his friendship with Wang, but did he also have some purpose of his own? A businessman's calculations were certain to be shrewd. Better ask first. “Brother Xueyan,” he said with difficulty, “is this money really idle, as you say?” At this question, Hu felt there was no need to maintain the fiction. He answered ambiguously, “Even if it weren't, I could hardly fail to do my utmost to help with your important affairs.”

“感激得很。只是我受你此惠，不知何以为报？”话是一句普通见情的话，但他的眼神不同，双目的的地望着胡雪岩，是等候回话的神态。这一下，玲珑剔透的胡雪岩就了然了，这句活不仅是内心感激的表示，还带着“问条件”的意味。条件自然有，但决不能说，说了就是草包。同时明雪岩也觉得他的这一问，未免看轻了他自己跟王有龄的交情，所以意中微有不满。“大公说的是哪里的话？我不曾读过书，不过《史记》上的《货殖列传》、《游侠列传》也听人讲过。区区万金，莫非有所企图，才肯出手？”“是，是！”何桂清大为不安，连连拱手：“是我失言了。雪岩兄，我真还想不列。你是读书有得的人。”胡雪岩心里好笑，自然也得意，听嵇鹤龄讲过几个汉朝的故事，居然把翰林出身的学台大人都唬住了，将来跟玉有龄、嵇鹤龄他们谈起来，倒是一件值得夸耀之事。“哪里，哪里，云公这话，等于骂我。”他一半实话，一半谦虚的话。而何桂清却真的刮目相看了，“怪不得雪轩佩服你。”他说，“雪轩以前虽不得意，却也是眼高于顶的人，平日月旦人物，少所许可，独独对你不同，原来你果然不同。”

“I'm deeply grateful. Only, if I accept such a favour, how am I to repay it?” The words were an ordinary expression of gratitude, but his eyes were different. They fixed directly upon Hu, waiting for an answer. Hu's acute understanding caught the meaning at once: this was not merely thanks, but an inquiry about his conditions. Naturally, there were conditions, but to state them would mark him as a fool. Moreover, he felt slightly displeased that He should rate his friendship with Wang so lightly. “Mr Genyun, what are you saying? I've had no schooling, but I've heard people explain the ‘Biographies of the Money-Makers’ and ‘Biographies of the Knight-Errants’ in the Records of the Grand Historian. Must I have some ulterior purpose before I can part with a mere ten thousand taels?” “Yes, yes!” He was greatly embarrassed and repeatedly clasped his hands. “I've spoken wrongly. Brother Xueyan, I never expected it. You are a man who has truly profited from learning.” Hu was inwardly amused and, naturally, pleased with himself. A few Han-dynasty stories heard from Ji had actually overawed an education commissioner of Hanlin background. It would make a fine story to boast of later to Wang and Ji. “Not at all. To say that is almost to mock me.” It was half truth, half modesty. But He really did regard him with fresh respect. “No wonder Xuexuan admires you. Even in his unsuccessful days, he was proud and exacting. He praised few of the people he judged, yet he always spoke differently of you. Now I see that you really are different.”

胡雪岩报以矜持谦虚的微笑，拿话题又拉回到借款上：“我那一万银子，一到上海就可以备妥，是寄了来，还是怎么样？”“不必寄来。”何桂清想了想说，“等我进京，自然是先到上海，由海道北上，一则路上比较平靖，再则也看看海运的情形。到了上海，我们见面再说。那时少不得还有麻烦你的地方。”“好，好……”胡雪岩自告奋勇：“云公什么时候进京，先给我一封信，在上海备公馆，定船舱都归我办差。”“ ‘办差’两个字请收回。”何桂清又踌躇着说：“倒是有一件，我动身至快也得端午前后，那时候，恐怕你已回杭州了。”“我从杭州赶回上海。”胡雪岩答得极其爽利，“而且，我上海也有人，一切不需云公费心。”谈话到此，酒也够了，胡雪岩请主人“赏饭”，吃完略坐一坐，随即起身告辞，何桂清仍旧用轿子将他送回金阊栈。阿巧姐正灯下独坐，在守候他回来。

Hu answered with a reserved, modest smile and returned the conversation to the loan. “I can have the ten thousand ready as soon as I reach Shanghai. Shall I send it here, or would you prefer another arrangement?” “There is no need to send it.” He considered. “When I go to the capital, I'll first come to Shanghai and travel north by sea. The route is more peaceful, and I can also inspect the sea transport arrangements. We can discuss it when we meet in Shanghai. I shall probably have other troubles for you then.” “Excellent.” Hu volunteered eagerly. “Let me know beforehand when you are going. I'll attend to everything in Shanghai—finding you a residence and booking your cabin.” “Please don't speak as though you were attending to official duties.” He hesitated. “There is one thing. At the earliest, I cannot leave until about the Dragon Boat Festival. By then you may have returned to Hangzhou.” “Then I'll come back to Shanghai from Hangzhou,” Hu answered readily. “Besides, I have people in Shanghai. You needn't concern yourself with any of it.” By now, they had talked and drunk enough. Hu asked his host to favour him with rice. After the meal he sat a little longer, then took his leave. He again sent him by chair to Jinchang Inn, where A’qiao was sitting alone by lamplight, waiting for his return.

胡说利息特别克己，但未议定数值，不是明确免息。还款拟经京中票号，钱多多还、钱少少还，无固定到期日；没有中人及保人。款要等胡回上海才备妥，何说无需寄苏州，待自己先到上海再谈。本批仅作承诺与安排，不能说贷款已经交付。何最快端午前后动身，不是当晚或三五天后立即赴京；胡在苏州预计逗留三五天是另一时间。
藩司入京20,000两为胡所听的概数，何认为是否足够取决于省份与业务，不能误作他的固定报价。部费按语境为衙门环节费用；不补出具体索贿人或收费标准。收入较丰、可不再搜刮百姓的仓场仕途叙述是小说中的官场逻辑，不作外部政治史或财务建议。

叙事立场与歧义处理
阿巧说升迁要送钱是她在怡情院听候补道台谈过的内容。胡对何与自己年龄相仿、何比阿巧大两三岁的说法是观察及带醋意的戏言，不据此编造何的确切年龄。
何登门是亲自来访；周并未见到何，只听何家管事说主人高兴。胡不知何究竟为钱、土产还是两者而喜，保留这种不确定性。阿巧对状元及“七省巡按”的联想来自说书故事，不把故事中的官衔剑令写成何的实际经历。
19181–19185的来往礼节和妾身份界限属于小说语境。阿巧只答“不好意思的”，胡据此分析；译成I couldn't presume以保留犹疑而不提前写成应允或拒绝。胡如算生意般衡量她的态度，本批没有说明他最终如何安排，译文不提前揭示后续。
何反复考虑官途，先谈若黄调走可接浙江，后谈回礼部不愿意，再决定谋仓场；不是已获得仓场任命。胡从何的提问推知其重视防务，保留判断层次。何说已致信京里、召见上谕大概快下，译为预计，不能写成已奉召。穆彰阿倒台年次、门生关系和两百年说法均照人物及小说叙述，不据外部资料擅改。
胡所谓头寸多、最怕闲钱起初是他的说辞；19317叙述明确此刻不必再说假话，英文保留maintain the fiction。他心中有条件，却认定不能说破；外面引用《史记》来维护豪爽形象，不能把公开说法当成无条件赠款的客观结论。史记篇名为Biographies of the Money-Makers与Biographies of the Knight-Errants；胡自称无书面教育，只听嵇讲过故事，何误以为其读书有得，前后都保留。

英语一综合精读
阅读：重点追踪5000赠款、15000总需求、10000拟借款三层关系，并区分已送达、口头提议、未付、预计任职与胡的推断。可用一句英文概括每段信息变化，再核对人物是谁、是否确定发生。
完形：观察therefore、nevertheless、even if、in any event、on the contrary、if等表达在礼节与利害论证中的作用；积累take something for granted、stand on ceremony、put aside doubts、part with money、make up the shortfall、fall short等搭配，以正文实际语境为准。
段落排序：按提出需求—礼物与信—送达确认—亲访—私下夜谈—职位选择—资金缺口—贷款条件—交付安排追踪信息链。尤其不要因为钱的数额相同而把第一笔信中款和后一笔贷款的段落错接。
翻译：练习He姓与he代词的区分、长转述的主语追踪，以及might／would／should／must等情态；先找每句判断由谁作出，再译条件、结果与保留语气。对礼称、官名和风俗不强套现代机构，先根据文内职掌理解。
写作：可练“充分了解背景后提出具体帮助”的短论证，借鉴There is no reason to...、Whether...or...、Even if...等结构。将原段改写成简洁论点与证据，避免照搬含蓄的官场说辞。小说精读须与真题、限时阅读／完形／排序／翻译及作文反馈结合，不作分数保证。
