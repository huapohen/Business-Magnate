等把这个难题想通了，胡雪岩觉得心情相当轻松，盘算了一下，古应春这天一定在忙着眼洋人接头，不必去打扰他，只有找刘不才一起盘桓，不妨一面出去游逛，一面看看可有合适的地皮，为潘叔雅买下来建新居。想停当了才起身下床，芙蓉晨妆已毕，侍候他漱洗早餐，同时间起这天要办些什么事？“等你三叔来了再谈。”胡雪岩说，“我想带你去逛逛。”“我不去。抛头露面象啥样子？”“那么你做点啥呢？”“我还是到七姑奶奶那里去。”芙蓉答道，“跟她在一起，永远是热闹的。”“就你们两个人，怎么热闹得起来？我看不如约了七姑奶奶一起去玩。”“她不肯的。”芙蓉忽然问道，“你说了她什么？她好象有点赌气的样子，古老爷常常劝她出去走走，不要在家闷出病来，她说什么也不肯。”这话胡雪岩在前一天也听见过，当时不以为意，现在听芙蓉提到，才知道七姑奶奶真的发愤了！倒是一件令人感动的事。“我不过劝她，要象个大家闺秀的样子，哪知道她这样认真。”胡雪岩说，“赌气是决不会有的事，她最佩服我，还有大事要我帮忙，赌什么气？”“这倒是真的，”芙蓉点点头，“提起你来总是小爷叔长，小爷叔短。我看，“芙蓉笑道，”只有一个人不佩服你。““哪个？”“梅玉的娘。”昨天是为了阿巧姐生醋意，这时候又提到他妻子，胡雪岩心里不免有些厌烦，所以默不作声。芙蓉也是很知趣的人，见他是这样的态度，便不再往下说，聊些别的闲天，等着刘不才。

Having thought his way through this difficulty, Hu felt considerably relieved. Gu Yingchun would certainly be busy dealing with the foreigners that day, so there was no need to disturb him. He would find Liu Bucai instead. They could stroll about together and look for a suitable piece of land to buy for Pan Shuya's new home. Only after settling this in his mind did he get out of bed. Furong had finished dressing and attended to him as he washed and ate breakfast, asking what he meant to do that day. “We'll discuss it when your Third Uncle comes. I thought of taking you out for a look around.” “I'm not going. What would people think of me showing myself in public?” “What will you do, then?” “Go to the Seventh Lady again. It's always lively with her.” “How lively can it be with only the two of you? Why not ask her to come out with us?” “She won't.” Furong suddenly asked, “What did you say to her? She seems a little out of temper. Mr Gu keeps urging her to go out before staying at home makes her ill, but she refuses.” Hu had heard this the day before without taking it seriously. Now he realized that the Seventh Lady was making a determined effort. It was rather touching. “I only advised her to behave like a lady from a distinguished family. I never imagined she'd take it so seriously. She certainly isn't sulking. She admires me more than anyone, and needs my help with an important matter. Why would she be angry?” “That's true.” Furong nodded. “It's ‘Little Uncle’ this and ‘Little Uncle’ that whenever she speaks of you. I think there's only one person who doesn't admire you.” She smiled. “Who?” “Meiyu's mother.” Yesterday she had been jealous over A’qiao; now she was bringing up his wife. Hu felt a little irritated and said nothing. Furong knew when to stop. Seeing his response, she dropped the subject and chatted about other things while they waited for Liu.

结果刘不才不曾来，来了个古应春，带了由丝栈里转来的两封人，一封是尤五的，由陈世龙代笔，说杭州漕帮闹事，经过调处，已经平息。只是新交了好些朋友，饮宴酬醉无虚日，所以还得几天才能回上海。再有一封是王有龄的，这封信就长了。工有龄校到胡雪岩初到上海的信，又接到何桂清从苏州写给他的信，加上陈世龙带去的口信，都要在这纣信中答复，所以足足写了七张纸，认得出是他的亲笔。这样一个浙江官场中的红人及能员，每天忙得不可开交，居然能抽出工夫来写这么一封洋洋洒洒的信，就显得交情确是与众不同了。信上自然先提到尤五，说是“既感且愧”，因为尤五会同郁四，将浙江漕帮的纠纷，顺顺利利地处置停当，感情已是可感，而且还承他送了许多礼物，实在受之有愧。至于认七姑奶奶作义妹一节，君子成人之美，而况又是旧雨新知双重的交情，自然乐从。问七姑奶奶什么时候到浙江，他好派专差来迎接。“你看！”胡雪岩将前面两张信递了给古应春，接着又往下看。下面提到何桂清，说是接到他从苏州寄会的信，才知道胡雪岩的行踪。何桂清认为能结识胡雪岩，是“平生一大快事”，也提到了那一万银子，这下是王有龄来赞扬胡雪岩了，说他的处置“高明之至”，这一万两银子，请胡雪岩替他记入帐下，将来一起结算。此外还有许多琐碎的事，其中比较重要的是，催促裘丰言早日回杭州，因为现在有个“优差”的机会，他可以设法谋取，“迟则为他人捷足先登，未免可惜。”

Liu did not come. Instead, Gu arrived with two letters forwarded from the silk warehouse. One was from You Laowu, written for him by Chen Shilong. The disturbance in the Hangzhou grain-transport brotherhood had been settled through mediation. But You had made many new friends, and not a day passed without a banquet or some social obligation, so it would be several days before he could return to Shanghai. The other letter, from Wang Youling, was a long one. Wang had received the letter Hu wrote on first reaching Shanghai, another from He Guiqing in Suzhou, and the oral message Chen had brought. Replying to all of these had taken seven sheets, unmistakably in Wang's own hand. For a man so favoured and capable in Zhejiang official circles, busy from morning to night, to find time for such an expansive letter showed how unusual their friendship was. He began, naturally, with You, saying that he was “both grateful and ashamed”. You and Yu Si had settled the Zhejiang brotherhood's dispute smoothly, a kindness that deserved gratitude in itself; to receive so many gifts as well made him feel unworthy. As for recognizing the Seventh Lady as his sworn younger sister, a gentleman should help others fulfil their wishes. With old friendships and new ones both involved, he would gladly agree. When was she coming to Zhejiang? He would send a special attendant to welcome her. “Look at this.” Hu handed Gu the first two sheets and read on. Wang next mentioned He: only from He's letter from Suzhou had he learned where Hu was. He considered meeting Hu “one of the greatest pleasures of my life” and had mentioned the ten thousand taels. Now it was Wang's turn to praise Hu, calling his handling of the matter “admirable in the highest degree”. Hu was to charge those ten thousand taels to Wang's account, to be settled with the rest later. There were many smaller matters too. The most important was a request that Qiu Fengyan return to Hangzhou promptly. A desirable appointment was available, and Wang could try to secure it for him. “It would be a pity if delay allowed someone else to get there first.”

“对了！”胡雪岩放下信问道，“ ‘酒糊涂，住在哪里？他的事办得怎么样了？昨天我倒忘了问你。”“都弄好了，就因为五哥不在这里，略上没有交代好，不敢启运。”古应春又说，“刘三爷知道你要跟他碰头，去约他了。等一下就到。”“那这样吧，我们先去吃饭，然后到七姐那里去，留下口信请他们来。”“那又何必在外头吃？还是到我们那里去。”于是古应春和胡雪岩坐马车，芙蓉不肯跟胡雪岩同车招摇过市，另雇一顶小轿走。轿慢车快，等她到时，只见七姑奶奶正笑容满面地在跟胡雪岩商量到湖州的行程。“怎么？”芙蓉惊喜地问道，“你也要到湖州去？”“是啊！”七姑奶奶洋洋得意他说，“我哥哥在做知府，我为啥不去。”这一节，也就象阿巧姐那件事一样，是无话不谈的七姑奶奶所不曾跟她谈到的少数“秘密”之一。不谈阿巧姐是为了怕替胡雪岩惹麻烦，不谈胡雪岩屠间拉拢，认王有龄作义兄，是七姑奶奶自觉身分悬殊，不相信现任知府的王大老爷肯降尊纤贵，认此义妹。事情不成，徒落话柄，所以她不愿告诉芙蓉。谁知王大老爷居然答应了，而且仿佛认此义妹，是件极可高兴的事，当然喜出望外，加以芙蓉一见投缘，不算外人，所以有那得意忘形的神态。听她自己约略说明缘由，芙蓉也替她高兴，“恭喜，恭喜！”她笑着说，“从今以后，不叫你七姑奶奶，要叫你王大小姐了。”“好了，好了！自己人，不作兴笑我的。我是沾了小爷叔的光。来！”七姑奶奶一把拉着她走，“到厨房里帮帮我的忙。”

“That reminds me.” Hu put down the letter. “Where is Wine Muddlehead staying? How is his business going? I forgot to ask yesterday.” “It's all arranged. But with Brother Five away, arrangements along the route haven't been settled, so he daren't set off. Third Master Liu knew you wanted to see him and has gone to fetch him. They'll be here shortly.” “Then let's have something to eat and go to Seventh Sister's. We can leave word for them to join us.” “Why eat out? Come straight to our place.” Gu and Hu took a carriage. Furong refused to parade through the streets in the same carriage as Hu and hired a small sedan chair. The chair was slower; by the time she arrived, the Seventh Lady was already discussing the journey to Huzhou with Hu, all smiles. “What? Are you going to Huzhou too?” Furong asked in delighted surprise. “Yes, of course. My brother is the prefect. Why shouldn't I?” Like the affair of A’qiao, this was one of the few secrets the otherwise talkative Seventh Lady had kept from Furong. She had withheld A’qiao's story for fear of causing Hu trouble. As for Hu's efforts to arrange for Wang to become her sworn elder brother, she had felt the difference in their standing too keenly to believe that a serving prefect would condescend to accept her as his sister. If it came to nothing, she would only expose herself to ridicule. Now he had actually agreed and seemed delighted at the prospect. She was overjoyed; and since she and Furong had taken to one another at once, she felt no need to restrain her delight. After hearing a brief explanation, Furong was happy for her too. “Congratulations! From now on, we must stop calling you the Seventh Lady and call you Miss Wang instead.” “Enough! We're friends; you mustn't make fun of me. It's all thanks to Little Uncle. Come!” She caught Furong by the hand. “Help me in the kitchen.”

古应春是广东人，讲究饮馔，七姑奶奶闲着无事，也就在烹调上消磨辰光，所以家里没有客来，饭菜也很丰腆，厨房里早已预备得差不多了，还有一个娘姨，一个小大姐，四个人一起动手，很快地把饭开了出来。主客四人一面吃饭，一面还是谈湖州之行。刚刚只谈了一半，胡雪岩决定亲自送七姑奶奶去，现在要谈的是动身的日期。这是个难题，胡雪岩的事情太多，不容易抽出工夫来，“五月初七以后就不行了，苏州的人要来。再等下去，天气太热，又不相宜。”他踌躇着说，“而且一去一来至少要半个月的工夫”小爷叔抽不出工夫，只好等秋凉以后再说。“七姑奶奶不愿强人所难，这样很爽快地表示了态度。“那不行。耽误了你们的好事。”胡雪岩又说，“再者，陈世龙也要做亲了。这杯喜酒一定也要去吃的，事情总有办法，等我慢慢来想。”

Gu was Cantonese and particular about food. With little else to occupy her, the Seventh Lady had taken to passing the time in cookery, so their meals were plentiful even without guests. Most of the preparations were already made. With an older maid and a young maid helping the two women, the food was soon on the table. The four of them continued discussing Huzhou as they ate. Their earlier conversation had gone only halfway: Hu had decided to escort the Seventh Lady himself, and now they must settle when to leave. This was difficult. He had so much business that it was hard to spare the time. “After the seventh of the fifth lunar month is impossible—the people from Suzhou are coming. If we wait longer, the weather will be too hot. That won't do either. Besides, the journey there and back will take at least a fortnight.” He hesitated. “If you can't spare the time, Little Uncle, we'll wait until the autumn cools things down.” She was unwilling to impose and said so readily. “No. That would delay your marriage. Besides, Chen Shilong is getting married too, and I must attend his wedding feast. There must be a way. Let me think.”

话题中断，接下来是古应春谈他上午跟洋人见面的情形，谈到一半又被打断了，刘不才和裘丰言连翩而至，两个人脸上红着，是喝了酒来的，但也不妨再来几杯。“事情都弄好了。”裘丰言说，“只等尤五哥来就动身。”“他还有些日子才能回来。”胡雪岩说，“或者你先回去一趟。”“不必，不必！”裘丰言指着刘不才说，“我跟刘三哥在一起，写意得很，每天吃吃酒，到处逛逛，这种逍遥自在的日子，难得遇到，尤五哥尽管慢点回来好了。”胡雪岩又好气，又好笑，“你真正‘酒糊涂’！一则要早早交差，人家等着洋枪在用，采运军火的事，哪容得你逍遥自在？真是‘急惊风遇着慢郎中’！再则，”他把王有龄的信拿给他看，“雪公一番热心，你不要错过机会。”等把信看完，裘丰言点点头说，“雪公的盛意，着买可感。不过，尤五哥不来，我也没办法走。空手回去，算啥名堂？只好让人家捷足先登了！”这话也不错，于是胡雪岩又遇到一个难题。七姑奶奶看他们愁颜相向，忍不住要问：“小爷叔！到底为了啥？”“ 老裘要运洋枪回去，路上怕不安靖，要五哥先替他沿路安排好。只要一进浙江地界就不要紧了。”胡雪岩说，“上次也是这样。一定要等五哥来，说妥当了才敢走。”

The subject was dropped, and Gu began describing his morning meeting with the foreigners. Halfway through, he too was interrupted. Liu Bucai and Qiu Fengyan arrived together, their faces red from drinking, though they had no objection to a few more cups. “Everything is ready,” Qiu said. “As soon as Brother You comes, I'll leave.” “He won't be back for some days. Perhaps you should go home first.” “No need!” Qiu pointed to Liu. “I'm very comfortable with Brother Liu. We drink every day and go about seeing the sights. Such carefree days are rare. Brother You needn't hurry back.” Hu was half annoyed, half amused. “You really are Wine Muddlehead! First, you must complete your commission promptly. They're waiting to use those foreign guns. How can the transport of military supplies allow you such leisure? A patient in convulsions has found himself a leisurely doctor! Second”—he handed him Wang's letter—“Mr Xuexuan has gone to some trouble. Don't miss the opportunity.” Having read it, Qiu nodded. “His kindness is deeply appreciated. But until Brother You comes, I can't leave. What would be the point of returning empty-handed? I'll just have to let someone else get there first.” There was truth in that, and Hu faced another difficulty. Seeing their worried faces, the Seventh Lady could not help asking, “Little Uncle, what exactly is the trouble?” “Old Qiu has to take the foreign guns back. The route may be unsafe, so Brother Five must make arrangements along the way. Once they're inside Zhejiang, all will be well. It was the same last time. We must wait until he returns and everything is settled before setting off.”

“是这样一桩事情！为啥早不跟我说？”一听这话，胡雪岩和裘丰言精神一振，齐声说道：“七姐！你有办法？”“这不是什么大不了的事。”七姑奶奶又怪古应春，“你知道这件事，也放在肚里不说，真正气数。“一时疏忽，也是有的。”古应春笑道，“闲话少说，你有办法就拿出来！”七姑奶奶的办法很简单。尤五手下几个得力的人，她无不相熟，只要找到其中之一个，一切迎刃而解。但十分不凑巧的是，古应春亲自去跑了一遍，竟一个也不曾找到。“不要紧！”七姑奶奶真有男子汉的气概，毫不迟疑地说，“这段路上有头有脸的人物，也都晓得我。我送了裘老爷去。”这真是语惊四座了！首先古应春就担心，“一船军人，不是好玩的事！”他说，“千斤重担你挑不挑得下来，自己要想一想。”“我想过了。不要紧的。”语气虽平静，而胡雪岩却听得出，愈平静愈显得倔强，他是深知她的脾气的，发现美蓉也想说话，急忙抛过去一个阻止的眼色，然后装出欢然的神情好：“好极，好极！有七姐出马，一定一路顺风。老裘，就让七姐送你去好了。”裘丰言知道胡雪岩这样说法，必有道理，自然桴鼓相应地也装出兴奋和感激的神态，拱拱手说：“多谢七姑奶奶，只是劳动玉步，于心不安。”

“So that's all it is! Why didn't you tell me sooner?” Hu and Qiu brightened at once. “Seventh Sister, can you manage it?” “It's no great matter.” She turned reproachfully to Gu. “You knew about this and kept it to yourself. Really, you're infuriating!” “People do overlook things,” he replied with a smile. “Never mind the talk. If you have a way, tell us.” Her plan was simple. She knew all You's trusted men, and finding just one of them would settle everything. Unfortunately, Gu went round in person and failed to find a single one. “Never mind.” With a man's boldness, she spoke without hesitation. “All the important people along that stretch know me too. I'll escort Mr Qiu myself.” This astonished everyone, and Gu was the first to express concern. “A boatload of arms is no joke. Think whether you can carry such a heavy responsibility.” “I have thought. There'll be no trouble.” Her voice was calm, but Hu knew her temper: the calmer she sounded, the more stubbornly she meant it. Seeing Furong about to speak, he stopped her with a glance, then assumed a delighted expression. “Excellent! With Seventh Sister taking charge, the journey is bound to go smoothly. Old Qiu, let her escort you.” Qiu knew Hu must have his reasons. Taking his cue, he too put on a show of excitement and gratitude and bowed with clasped hands. “Thank you, Seventh Lady. I only feel uneasy at putting you to the trouble of travelling yourself.”

“没有多少路，只当到嘉兴去玩一趟。”“慢点！”胡雪岩灵机一动，“我倒有个办法。七姐，你索性到杭州，把那件大事办了它。”“那……”事出突兀，七姑奶奶一时还想不通，“那么，小爷叔你呢？”“我是对不起，这趟不能陪你了……”胡雪岩的打算是，七姑奶奶认义兄，尤五一定要到场，来了又去，徒劳跋涉，而自己算来抽不出工夫，那就不如趁此机会，早早办了这件大事，以便向古家老族长去说媒。至于尤家兄妹与王有龄之间，要有个人从中传话照料，他也想好了，可以拜托裘丰言。裘丰言当然乐意效劳。七姑奶奶和古应春也觉得这样安排十分妥帖。只是一船军火，真个托付七姑奶奶保险，这件事除了她自己有信心以外，谁也觉得大不妥当。找个机会，古应春将胡雪岩和裘丰言拉到一边说道：“小爷叔，你真的信任我们那口子？她是‘女张飞’，你是诸葛亮，莫非有啥妙算？”“妙算不敢说，打算是有的。要我亲自跑一趟松江，我到‘老太爷’那里去搬救兵。”“妙，妙！”古应春大喜，“真正是妙算！”“轻点！轻点！”胡雪岩急忙阻止，“七姐的脾气你晓得的，这件事不能让她知道。我悄悄去，悄悄来，有一昼夜的工夫就够了。”

“It's no great distance. I'll treat it as an excursion to Jiaxing.” “Wait!” An idea struck Hu. “Why not go all the way to Hangzhou and settle that important matter?” “Then...” The suggestion was so sudden that she could not immediately take it in. “What about you, Little Uncle?” “I'm sorry. I shan't be able to accompany you this time.” His reasoning was that You Laowu must be present when his sister acquired her sworn elder brother. It would be a needless journey for him to come back and then leave again. Since Hu himself could spare no time, they might as well use this opportunity to settle the matter promptly and then approach the Gu family's old clan head about the marriage. Someone would have to carry messages and make arrangements between Wang and the You brother and sister. Hu had thought of that too: he could ask Qiu. Qiu was naturally willing, and the Seventh Lady and Gu found the arrangement excellent. The only difficulty was entrusting a whole boatload of arms to her protection. Everyone but the lady herself thought that most unwise. Finding a chance, Gu drew Hu and Qiu aside. “Little Uncle, do you really trust my woman with this? She's the female Zhang Fei, but you're Zhuge Liang. Have you some brilliant stratagem?” “I wouldn't call it brilliant, but I do have a plan. I must go to Songjiang myself and ask the Old Master for reinforcements.” “Excellent! A brilliant plan indeed!” “Quiet!” Hu stopped him hastily. “You know Seventh Sister's temper. She mustn't learn of this. I'll go quietly and come back quietly. A day and a night will be enough.”

“那么，你预备啥时候走？”“今天就走。”“我陪你去。”裘丰言说，“我也久慕‘老太爷’的名，想见见他。”“也好！不过水路不平靖，我想走陆路，为了赶辰光我骑马去，你行不行？”裘丰言不会骑马，无法同行，只得快快而罢。及至回到屋里，只见刘不才正为七姑奶奶在开备办礼物的单子，芙蓉则是七姑奶奶的参赞，两人商量着说一样，刘不才便提笔写一样。开完长长的一张单子，七姑奶奶接到手里看了一遍，自言自语他说：“备齐总得六七百丙银子。”接着便叫一声：“小爷叔！”“怎么样？”“你有没有空？”她问，“我是说能不能抽出两天的工夫来？”胡雪岩面有难色，便先问一句：“你要我替你办什么事，说来商量。”“我想请你陪我回一趟松江。”这一说，古应春不由得就要问：“回松江干什么？”“要去拿东西，天气热了，我的单衣夹服还在家里，还有些首饰，到杭州去也要用的。”

“When do you mean to leave?” “Today.” “I'll go with you,” Qiu offered. “I've long admired the Old Master's reputation and would like to meet him.” “Very well. But the waterways are unsafe, and I mean to go overland. To save time, I'll ride. Can you?” Qiu could not ride, so he had to abandon the idea in disappointment. When they returned to the room, Liu was drawing up a list of gifts for the Seventh Lady. Furong acted as her adviser: as the two women discussed each item, Liu wrote it down. Once the long list was finished, the Seventh Lady took it and read it through. “Getting all this together will cost six or seven hundred taels,” she murmured. Then she called, “Little Uncle!” “What is it?” “Have you any time? I mean, could you spare two days?” Hu looked troubled and asked first, “What do you want me to do? Tell me, and we'll discuss it.” “I'd like you to accompany me home to Songjiang.” Gu could not help asking, “What do you want to go back for?” “To collect some things. The weather's growing warm, and my unlined and lined clothes are still at home. There's jewellery too that I'll need in Hangzhou.”

“那也用不着小爷叔陪你去啊？”“这件大事，我总要跟老太爷说一声，还有，你的那件事。”“我的？”古应春诧异地，“我自己倒不晓得！”“你真是木头人！” 七姑奶奶恨恨地说，“小爷叔是不是你的大媒老爷？”“原来是这件事！”古应春笑着答道：“你不说是我们两个人的事，我怎么知道？”谈到这里，裘丰言大为高兴地说了句：“这一下，我也去得成了。”七姑奶奶自然不懂他的话，胡雪岩便一半解释，一半掩饰地说：“老裘跟我提过好几次，想去见见老太爷，一直没有机会。现在可以一起去了。”“喔，那太好了！”七姑奶奶也问道：“小爷叔，那么你呢？”胡雪岩还不曾开口，古应春和裘丰言相视而笑，神态诡秘。使得七姑奶奶大感困惑，睁圆了一双眼，直瞟着古应春。“说实话吧！”胡雪岩深伯引起误会，揭破了真相，“我原来就想去见老太爷，跟他要两个人，送老等到杭州。七姐，不是我不相信你有办法，是因为我觉得千斤重担，何必放在你肩膀上？万一出了事，五哥一定要怪我，说：”老七是心热，做事为了朋友，不计后果。你们怎么也不仔细想一想。‘这话我就没法交代了。七姐，你是明白人，一定体谅我跟老裘的处境！““那没有什么！只要把事情办通就是。小爷叔用不着这样子来解释的。”听她如此谅解，胡雪岩深感欣慰，“说你是明白人，真是明白人！”他转脸去问芙蓉：“你呢？”“我们说好了。”七姑奶奶抢着答道：“一起到松江去玩一趟。现在就挑日子好走！”

“You don't need Little Uncle to accompany you for that.” “I must tell the Old Master about this important business. And there's that matter of yours.” “Mine?” Gu looked astonished. “I don't know of any.” “You blockhead!” she said indignantly. “Isn't Little Uncle your matchmaker?” “Oh, that!” Gu laughed. “How could I know when you didn't say it concerned both of us?” Qiu was delighted. “Now I can go after all!” Naturally, the Seventh Lady did not understand. Hu explained, while partly concealing the truth. “Old Qiu has told me several times that he'd like to meet the Old Master, but has never had a chance. Now he can come with us.” “Oh, excellent! And you, Little Uncle?” Before Hu could answer, Gu and Qiu exchanged conspiratorial smiles. Puzzled, she opened her eyes wide and stared at Gu. Afraid of causing a misunderstanding, Hu revealed the truth. “I'll be frank. I was already planning to see the Old Master and ask him for two men to escort Old Qiu to Hangzhou. Seventh Sister, it isn't that I doubt your ability. But why should we put this heavy burden on your shoulders? If anything went wrong, Brother Five would certainly blame me. He'd say, ‘Seven is warm-hearted and thinks nothing of the consequences when helping friends. Why didn't the rest of you consider it more carefully?’ I should have no answer. You're a sensible woman; you must understand the position Old Qiu and I are in.” “There's no difficulty. So long as the business gets done, that's what matters. You needn't explain yourself like this.” Hu was deeply relieved by her understanding. “I said you were sensible, and so you are!” He turned to Furong. “What about you?” The Seventh Lady answered first. “We've agreed. We'll all go to Songjiang for a visit. Now we must choose the day.”

芙蓉取了皇历来看，第二天就是宜于长行的好日子，时间是太局促了些，但以芙蓉在这些上头很迷信，明天不走，就得再等五天，为了迁就她，只好大家赶一赶。“你没事，替我们去雇船，要大，要好！”七姑奶奶这样吩咐古应春。听得七姑奶奶这一声，古应春赛如奉了将军令，答应着转身就走。“等等，等等！”刘不才慌不迭地站起来，“我跟你一起走。”这下芙蓉开了口，“三叔！”她也是极匆遽的语气，“你不要走！这里有好多事，要请你办。”刘不才无可奈何地站定脚，转身答道：“你快说！我有要紧事。”“咦！”芙蓉倒奇怪了，“忽然有要紧事，三叔，你倒说！”“哎呀！”他着急地，“姑奶奶，你就少问了，只说要我办什么事就是。”“我也要买点零碎东西带走，不是三言两语说得完的。”“那就这样。你请雪岩开单子，我一下就回来，替你去买。夷场上市面迟，都买得到。买不齐的，明天上午再补。”芙蓉见他行踪诡秘，还要留住他说个究竟。倒是胡雪岩看不过，阻住了芙蓉，于是刘不才如逢皇恩大赦似地，跟着古应春匆匆走了。

Furong consulted the almanac. The following day was auspicious for a long journey. It left rather little time, but she was deeply superstitious about such matters: if they did not go tomorrow, they would have to wait five days. To accommodate her, everyone must hurry. “You've nothing to do. Hire us a boat—a large, good one,” the Seventh Lady told Gu. He responded as though he had received a general's command and turned to go at once. “Wait!” Liu rose hastily. “I'll come with you.” Furong spoke just as urgently. “Third Uncle, don't go! There are lots of things I need you to do.” Helplessly, Liu stopped and turned. “Tell me quickly. I have important business.” “Why, what's this sudden important business? Tell me.” “Oh, my dear lady!” he said impatiently. “Ask fewer questions and tell me what you want done.” “I need to buy some odds and ends to take along. I can't explain it in two words.” “Then ask Xueyan to make a list. I'll be back in a moment and buy them for you. The settlement's shops stay open late; everything will still be available. Whatever I can't get, I'll buy tomorrow morning.” His mysterious behaviour made Furong want to keep him there and find out why. Hu, however, could bear it no longer and stopped her. Liu hurried off after Gu like a man released by an imperial pardon.

“奇怪！”芙蓉咕哝着说，“我这三叔，尽做些别人不懂的事。我看不是好花样。”“算了，算了！”胡雪岩说，“我要去看两个钱庄朋友，你要买点啥，我替你带来。其实你不说我也晓得，无非胭脂花粉、衣料吃食，新奇实用的洋货。”“对！我要送人的。不过，千万不要太贵，贵的你买来我也不要。”“你看你，”胡雪岩笑道，“七姐是自己人。客气一点的，听了你的话会怎么想？送人的礼，不要贵的，原来是弄些不值钱的东西送人！”“话不是这么说，”七姑奶奶向着芙蓉，“东西贵不一定好，贱的也不一定不好。送礼全在合用，要看人会不会买？”胡雪岩笑了，“七姐，你现在真的很会说话了。”他说，“老古是好口才，总算在这上头你拜着个好师傅。”“哪个要拜他师傅？除非你小爷叔，还差不多。”“好了，好了，不要恭维我了。”胡雪岩一笑出门。

“How strange!” Furong muttered. “Third Uncle is always doing things nobody understands. I don't think he's up to any good.” “Never mind,” Hu said. “I'm going to see two friends in banking. Tell me what you want, and I'll bring it back. In fact, I know without being told: rouge and face powder, dress materials and food, and novel, useful foreign goods.” “Yes, for presents. But they mustn't be too expensive. If you buy costly things, I won't have them.” “Listen to yourself!” Hu laughed. “Seventh Sister is one of us, but what would someone less familiar think? Presents mustn't be expensive—so you mean to give people a lot of worthless things!” The Seventh Lady took Furong's side. “That's not what she means. Expensive things aren't necessarily good, and cheap things aren't necessarily bad. A present must suit its purpose. It depends on knowing what to buy.” Hu smiled. “Seventh Sister, you're really becoming an accomplished speaker. Old Gu has a ready tongue; at least in that respect you've found a good teacher.” “Who wants him for a teacher? If it were you, Little Uncle, that would be different.” “Enough. Don't flatter me.” With a smile, Hu went out.

等他走了不久，刘不才笑嘻嘻地走了进来，是极得意的神情，自道是赌“花会”去了，赢了二百多两银子。什么叫“花会”，芙蓉还是第一次听见这两个字。七姑奶奶却是懂的，不但懂，而且迷过，因而便为芙蓉解释，“花会”跟广东的“白鸽票”相仿，上海设局赌花会的，亦以广东省城和潮州两地的人居多。赌法是三十六门开一门，其中两门永远不开，所以实际上是三十四门猜一门，猜中的一赔二十八。“这种赌不公平，要公平就要一赔三十三，一赔二十八，等于多占五门。”七姑奶奶说，“后来我是想穿了，所以不赌。这种赌不知道害了多少人！尤其是没有知识的女人！”“本来嘛！”芙蓉这样说，“好好的良家妇女到花会里去赌钱，象什么样子？输了钱，自然吵得家宅不安。”“还不光是输钱，为了‘祈梦’，败坏名节的都不知道多少。”“什么？”芙蓉大为不解，“与‘祈梦’啥相干？”

Soon after he left, Liu came back grinning, looking very pleased with himself. He had been gambling at huahui, he announced, and had won more than two hundred taels. Furong had never heard of this “flower lottery”. The Seventh Lady knew it well; indeed, she had once been addicted to it. She explained that it resembled Guangdong's “white-pigeon lottery”, and that most people running flower lotteries in Shanghai came from the provincial capital or Chaozhou. One winning entry was drawn from thirty-six, though two were always excluded, leaving thirty-four possible choices. A correct guess paid twenty-eight to one. “That's unfair. To be fair, it should pay thirty-three to one. Paying twenty-eight gives them the advantage of five extra entries,” she said. “Eventually I saw through it and stopped. Who knows how many people it has ruined, especially ignorant women?” “Exactly,” Furong agreed. “What business have respectable women going to a flower lottery to gamble? Naturally, losing money will throw their homes into uproar.” “It's worse than losing money. Who knows how many have lost their honour through seeking dreams?” “What?” Furong was bewildered. “What have dreams to do with it?”

芙蓉也是迷信这些花佯的，七姑奶奶觉得正好借此讽劝，便从头讲起：“花会的总机关叫‘总筒’，各地方设‘筒’，也有上门来兜揽的，叫做‘航船’。赌法是每天早晚各开一次，称为‘早筒’、‘晚筒’。向例前面两筒开过的围不开，所以三十六门实际上只开三十四门。“三十六门是三十六个人，据说最初就是梁山泊的三十六响马巨头，但久而久之，宋江、吴用等等名字，完全改过了。三十六个人的身分，各个不同，另外每个人有座‘本命星’，天上飞的、陆上爬的、水中游的都有，象第二十五，名叫林太平，身分是皇帝，本命星就是一条龙。“三十四门只能挑一门，怎么挑法？这样也好，那样也好，心里七上八下拿不定主意，那就只好祈梦了。梦见龙，当然押林太平，梦见黑狗，就要押第二十八罗必得。”七姑奶奶停了一下问，“你晓得祈梦到哪里去祈？”“自然是庙里。”芙蓉答说。“不是！荒山野地的坟头上。”芙蓉大骇，“是晚上？”她问。“当然是晚上，哪有白天祈梦的？”“晚上睡在坟头上？”芙蓉不断摇头，“不吓死人！”“为了钱，胆就大了，不但是坟头上，而且越是新坟越好……”这是由于“新鬼大，故鬼小”的说法，新坟则墓中人新死不久，魂灵易聚，招魂的方法是用一口空铁锅，拿锅铲空铲一阵，据说鬼魂就会闻声而至。然后根据梦兆去押，百不失一。

Furong herself believed in such superstitions. Seeing a chance to warn her, the Seventh Lady began at the beginning. “The central office is called the ‘main tube’. There are local ‘tubes’ everywhere, and agents who come to your door are called ‘ferryboats’. A draw is held twice a day, morning and evening—the ‘morning tube’ and ‘evening tube’. By custom, the winners of the previous two draws are excluded, which is why only thirty-four of the thirty-six entries can win. The thirty-six stand for thirty-six men, said to have been the thirty-six bandit chiefs of Liangshan Marsh originally. Over time, the names of Song Jiang, Wu Yong and the others were all changed. Each man has a different status and a ‘guardian star’, which may be something that flies in the sky, crawls on land or swims in the water. Number twenty-five, for instance, is Lin Taiping, an emperor, whose guardian star is a dragon. You can choose only one of the thirty-four. But which? This seems as good as that, and you're too anxious and uncertain to decide, so you seek an answer in a dream. Dream of a dragon, and naturally you bet on Lin Taiping; dream of a black dog, and you bet on number twenty-eight, Luo Bide.” She paused. “Do you know where people go to seek these dreams?” “A temple, of course.” “No. A grave in some lonely wilderness.” Furong was horrified. “At night?” “Of course. Who seeks such dreams in daylight?” “Sleeping on a grave at night?” Furong shook her head repeatedly. “Wouldn't you die of fright?” “Money makes people brave. And not just any grave—the newer, the better.” This followed the belief that “a new ghost is strong, an old ghost weak”. The occupant of a fresh grave had died recently, so the spirit was more easily summoned. One scraped a spatula about in an empty iron pot, and the ghost was said to come at the sound. A bet based on the ensuing dream, they claimed, would never fail.

“那么，灵不灵呢？”“怎么会灵？”七姑奶奶说。“譬如你梦见黄狗，我梦见黑狗，各押各的，总有一个不灵。各人有各人的心境，各人做各人的梦，个个要灵，除非三十四门全开。哪有这个道理？”“讲得透彻！”对赌之一道三折肽的刘不才，击案称赏，“赌钱全靠算！‘触机’不足为据。“芙蓉也深有所思地点点头，接着又问：“那么，怎么说是败坏名节呢？”“你想想，一个女人独自睡在荒郊野外，还有个不被人糟踏的？”“啊！”芙蓉悚然，“这花会说起来真是害人无穷！三叔，你也少去！”“你放心，这种赌是不会赌的人玩的。迷不到我！我不过喜欢赌，要会见识见识而已。”刘不才又说，“今天赢了二百多两银子，不足为奇。遇见一桩妙事，说起来，倒着实叫我佩服。”听这一说，七姑奶奶首先就高兴了，“快说，快说！”她捧杯茶给刘不才，“你说的妙事一定妙！”

“Does it really work?” “How could it?” the Seventh Lady replied. “Suppose you dream of a yellow dog and I dream of a black one. We each bet accordingly; at least one dream must be wrong. Everyone has a different state of mind and dreams their own dreams. For all of them to come true, all thirty-four entries would have to win. Where's the sense in that?” “Perfectly explained!” Liu, whose repeated reverses had given him plenty of gambling experience, slapped the table in admiration. “Gambling depends on calculation. Sudden inspirations are no guide.” Furong nodded thoughtfully, then asked, “But how does it destroy a woman's honour?” “Think of a woman sleeping alone in the wilderness. How could she escape being violated?” “Oh!” Furong shuddered. “This flower lottery really does cause endless harm. Third Uncle, you should go less often too.” “Don't worry. That game is for people who don't know how to gamble. It won't catch me. I simply enjoy gambling and wanted to see what it was like. Winning over two hundred taels today was nothing remarkable. But I saw something extraordinary that truly earned my admiration.” The Seventh Lady brightened at once. “Tell us!” She brought him a cup of tea. “If you call it extraordinary, it must be.”

刘不才所讲的，是他在一处“分筒”中亲眼得见的一位人物。这处分筒，规模极大，赌客中颇多殷实富户，下的赌码极重，其中有个富孀，夫家姓梁，行四，所以都叫她“梁四太太”。梁四太太打花会与众不同，专打一门，这一门在三十六门中，名列十六，叫做李汉云。奇的是她专打这一门。总筒中偏偏不开这一门。这样一年多下来，已经输了上万的银子。这天下午，她照例坐轿到了那里，因为是大户，自然殷勤接待，一盏茶罢，分筒执事便赔笑相问：“四太太，把条子交下来吧！”花会打那一门的那张“条子”照例是封缄的，要等总筒开出来才能揭晓。不如此则总筒可以统计每一门下注的数目，避重就轻拣注码最少的一门开。话虽如此，弊端还是有的。梁四太太这时听执事问到，便愤愤地说：“钱输了，还是小事，我就不相信一次都不会中。我总要着一次才服气。”“我劝四太太换一门的好！”分筒执事说，“赌上面真是有鬼的，不开起来一定不开。”

Liu described someone he had seen at a local branch. It was a large establishment frequented by many prosperous households, and the stakes were heavy. Among its customers was a rich widow whose husband's surname was Liang and who was known by her family's rank as Fourth Madam Liang. Unlike others, she always bet on a single entry: number sixteen, Li Hanyun. Curiously, while she persisted in backing that one, the main office never drew it. In a little over a year she had lost more than ten thousand taels. That afternoon she arrived by sedan chair as usual. Such an important customer was naturally welcomed with every attention. After a cup of tea, the branch's clerk asked with a courteous smile, “Fourth Madam, will you give me your slips?” The slip naming one's choice was customarily sealed and opened only after the main office announced the winner. Otherwise, the main office could total the stakes on each entry and choose the one carrying the smallest sum. Yet fraud remained possible. At the clerk's question, Fourth Madam Liang said angrily, “Losing money is a small matter. What I don't believe is that I can never win once. I must win at least once before I'm satisfied.” “I'd advise you to change your choice. There's something uncanny about gambling. When a number won't come up, it simply won't.”

“今天开出来，我一定会中。你看，”梁四太太便从手巾包里取出一把纸条来，“今天我打三十四门，莫非还不中？”“哪有这种赌法的？”分筒执事笑道，“四太太你不想想，三十四门，只中一门，赔了你二十八，还要输四门。这叫什么算盘？”“当然下注有多少。开出来是我的重门，我就赢了。”梁四太太说：“总要中一回，我才能死心歇手。”分筒执事，听她的口风，这是最后一回来赌花会，平白失去这么一个大户，未免可惜。但此时亦不便相劝，只拿笔来记每一门所下的注码。一注注写完，却只有三十三门，梁四太太奇怪，凝神细想一想说道：“下轿的时候我还数过的，是三十四张条子，大概是数弄掉了一张，你们替我去找一找看？”那分筒执事，工于心计，而且日夕从事，对于这上面的舞弊，精到极点，当时心里就打算好，这张条子就寻着了，也不能够给她。果然在门槛下面找到了，但回复梁四太太却是如此：“到处找过，没有！”“没有，就算了！ 莫非偏偏就开那一门？我想，世界上没有那么巧的事！”分筒一则要“统吃”梁四太太，再则怕她今日一中，明日不来，于是便革开那一门，打开捡到的那张条子，看是第三十五门张九官，当即通知总筒，开出张九官来。

“I shall certainly win today. Look.” She took a handful of slips from her handkerchief bundle. “I've backed all thirty-four entries. Surely I must win now?” “What a way to gamble!” the clerk laughed. “Think, Fourth Madam. Only one of the thirty-four can win. Even with twenty-eight paid back, you'll still lose four stakes. What sort of reckoning is that?” “Naturally, I've staked different amounts. If one of my heavy bets wins, I'll come out ahead. I must win once before I can make up my mind to stop.” The clerk gathered that this would be her last visit. It was a pity to lose such an important customer for nothing, but he could hardly try to dissuade her now. He took up his brush to record the stake on each entry. When all were listed, there were only thirty-three. Puzzled, she thought carefully. “I counted them when I got out of the chair. There were thirty-four slips. I must have dropped one. Will you look for it?” Cunning and practised daily in every trick of the business, the clerk immediately decided that even if the slip was found, he must not give it back. Sure enough, it lay beneath the threshold, but the report to her was, “We've looked everywhere. Nothing.” “Then never mind. Surely that won't be the very entry drawn? I don't believe the world contains such coincidences.” The branch wanted both to take all her stakes and to prevent a win from putting an end to her visits. They would therefore have that very entry drawn. Opening the recovered slip, they found number thirty-five, Zhang Jiuguan, and immediately told the main office to draw him.

“我跟这位梁四太太前后脚到。”刘不才说，“眼看她的三十三张条子拆封，第一封拆开来就是张九官……”七姑奶奶心急，打断他问：“这是啥道理？好奇怪！”“怪事还多呢！你不要心急，听我说！”刘不才又说：“拆开第二封，还是张九官。““第三封呢？”七姑奶奶问，“莫非也是张九官？”“这还用说！一直拆到第三十三封，都是张九官，梁四太太一共赢了一万两千多银子，一年多输下去的，一下子扳本反赢钱！”这个故事的谜底揭开来，将芙蓉听得目瞪口呆，不信地说：“真想得出这种恶刻的法子？”“这梁四太太的脑筋，可以跟小爷叔比了！”七姑奶奶不胜向往他说，“我们真想结识结识她！”“那也容易，”刘不才说，“只要到那处分筒去几回，一定遇得见她。”

“I arrived at almost the same time as she did,” Liu said, “and watched her thirty-three slips being opened. The very first was Zhang Jiuguan.” Impatient, the Seventh Lady broke in. “How could that be? How strange!” “There are stranger things yet. Don't hurry me. Listen. The second was Zhang Jiuguan too.” “And the third? Surely not Zhang Jiuguan again?” “Of course! All the way to the thirty-third, every one was Zhang Jiuguan. Fourth Madam Liang won more than twelve thousand taels. In one stroke, she recovered everything she had lost over the past year and made a profit besides.” At the revelation, Furong stared open-mouthed. “Who could think of such a devilish trick?” she exclaimed incredulously. “Her brains can compare with Little Uncle's!” the Seventh Lady said longingly. “I'd love to make her acquaintance.” “Easy enough,” Liu replied. “Go to that branch a few times, and you're sure to meet her.”

“省省吧！”芙蓉赶紧劝阻，“这种花会，害人不浅，这样子猜心思，寿命都要短几年，你既然已经戒掉了，千万别去。”“这话也是！”刘不才大有忏悔之意，“赌这样东西，不赌心思没有趣味，要赌心思，真叫‘强中自有强中手’，永远不会有啥把握。想想真没意思！”“照这样子说，刘三爷，你也要洗手戒赌了？”“你听他的！”芙蓉撇撇嘴，对七姑奶奶说，“我们三叔说要戒赌，总有十七八回了。”刘不才不好意思地笑了一下，七姑奶奶便为他解嘲：“虽然没有戒掉，总常常想着在戒，这就蛮难得的了！”“怎么难得？”门外有人在搭腔，大家转脸看时，是不知道什么时候溜了出去，如今又溜了回来的裘丰言。于是七姑奶奶将刚刚听来的故事，又讲了一遍。裘丰言也对梁四太太赞叹不止，这样谈到十点多钟，古应春和胡雪岩陆续归来，船已雇好，胡雪岩所买的东西，已直接送回客栈。约定第二天中午，仍在七姑奶奶那里会齐，一起下船。

“Spare yourself!” Furong intervened at once. “This lottery does terrible harm. All that guessing at other people's schemes would shorten your life by years. You've already given it up. Whatever you do, don't go back.” “That's true,” Liu said, sounding deeply repentant. “Gambling is no fun without a battle of wits. Yet once you pit your wits against others, however clever you are, there's always someone cleverer. You can never be sure of anything. When I think about it, there's really no point.” “Does that mean you'll give up gambling too, Third Master Liu?” “Listen to him!” Furong curled her lip and told the Seventh Lady, “Third Uncle has promised to stop seventeen or eighteen times already.” Liu smiled in embarrassment. The Seventh Lady came to his rescue. “Even if he hasn't stopped, he's always thinking of stopping. That's something to admire.” “What's admirable?” someone asked from the doorway. They turned to find Qiu, who had slipped out unnoticed and now slipped back in. The Seventh Lady retold the story she had just heard. Qiu too was full of admiration for Fourth Madam Liang. Their conversation continued until after ten, when Gu and Hu returned one after the other. The boat had been hired, and Hu's purchases sent directly to the inn. They agreed to meet at the Seventh Lady's again at noon the next day and go aboard together.
