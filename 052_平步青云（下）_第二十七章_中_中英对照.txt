“真正不敢当！胡老爷、裘老爷这么隆重的礼数，又赏了那么贵重的东西，叫我老婆子真不知道怎么说才好。”俞三婆婆说到这里，又转脸对七姑奶奶说，“我的耳朵不好，回头两位有什么吩咐，你替我仔细听着！”这就显得俞三婆婆是个角色了！她明朗耳聪目明，却偏这样子交代，为的是留下一个退步，等胡雪岩有所干求而无法办到时，便好装聋作哑，得有闪转腾挪的余地。因为如此，胡雪岩越发不敢大意，要盲不烦地叙明来意，一方面表示不愿使松江漕帮为难，开脱了老太爷的窘境，一方面又表示不愿请兵护运，怕跟俞武成发生冲突，伤了江湖的义气。这番话真如俗语所说“ 绵里针”，表面极软，骨子里大有讲究。俞三婆婆到底老于江湖，熟悉世面，听胡雪岩说到“不愿请兵护运”这句话，暗地里着实吃惊。话中等于指责俞武成抢劫军械，这是比强盗还重的罪名，认起真来，灭门有余。

“Really, this is more than I deserve! Such elaborate courtesies from you, Mr Hu and Mr Qiu, and such valuable gifts as well! You leave an old woman quite at a loss for words.” Third Granny Yu turned to the Seventh Lady. “My hearing is poor. If these gentlemen have any instructions for us, listen carefully on my behalf.” This revealed what a formidable woman she was. Her hearing and sight were perfectly clear, yet she deliberately said this to leave herself a way out: if Hu Xueyan asked for something she could not provide, she could pretend not to have heard or understood, and still have room to manoeuvre. Hu therefore took even greater care. He explained his purpose briefly and plainly. On the one hand, he wished to spare the Songjiang grain-transport brotherhood any embarrassment, thus relieving the Old Master of his predicament; on the other, he was reluctant to request a military escort, for fear of a clash with Yu Wucheng that would damage their bonds of loyalty. His words were a needle wrapped in cotton: soft on the surface, but with a sharp point within. Third Granny Yu knew the world and its ways. When Hu spoke of his reluctance to request a military escort, she was secretly alarmed. His words amounted to an accusation that Yu Wucheng intended to seize military arms, a crime more serious than ordinary robbery. If pursued in earnest, it could destroy his entire household.

“胡老爷，裘老爷！”俞三婆婆装出气得不得了的样子，“我这个儿子，真正无法无天！活到六十多，实在还不及我这个孙子懂事。两位看我老婆子的面上，千万不必生气，等我找了他来问。”她回头拄一拄拐杖，厉声吩咐俞少武：“赶快多派人，把你那个糊涂老子找回来！”不管她是真的动气，还是有意做作，来客都大感不安，“三婆婆！”胡雪岩急忙相劝，“这件事怪不得俞大哥！我们也是道听途说，事情还不知道真假，我想俞大哥亦不至于敌友不分。我们的来意，是想请三婆婆做主，就算没有这回事，少不得也要仰仗俞大哥的威名，保一保我们。”听得这一说，俞三婆婆的脸色和缓了，转眼对七姑奶奶说：“这倒还罢了！我想你师叔也不至于这么糊涂！”略停一下，她又对客人说道：“既承两位看得起我，武成理当效劳。他心直口快，外面得罪的人多，每每有人造他的谣言，亏得两位贤明，决不会误听人言。事情好办，请两位在苏州玩个两三日，我一定叫两位高高兴兴回杭州。”胡雪岩将她的话，一字一句，听得明明白白，心里着实佩服俞三婆婆，就这么轻描淡写地，将俞武成意图劫械的一行罪嫌，洗刷掉了。话是从自己口里说出去的：“道听途说”、“不知真假”，即使将来翻脸，要想改口，已是不能。真正姜是老的辣！自己竟糊里糊涂被她骗了一句话去、可以说是这一年多一帆风顺的境遇中，唯一的一次栽跟斗。然而，这个跟斗栽得不能不服输。

“Mr Hu! Mr Qiu!” Third Granny Yu put on a show of furious indignation. “That son of mine has no respect for law or heaven! Over sixty years old, and still he has less sense than his own son. For my sake, gentlemen, please do not be angry. Let me have him brought here and question him.” She turned, struck the floor with her stick, and ordered Yu Shaowu sternly, “Send more men at once. Find that muddleheaded father of yours and bring him home!” Whether her anger was real or assumed, her guests were deeply uncomfortable. “Granny!” Hu hastened to soothe her. “We must not blame Brother Yu. We have only heard rumours, and do not yet know whether there is any truth in them. I cannot believe Brother Yu would confuse friend and foe. We came to ask you to decide what should be done. Even if there is nothing in the story, we shall still need the protection of Brother Yu’s great name.” Her expression softened. Turning to the Seventh Lady, she said, “That is better! I did not think your Uncle Yu could be quite so foolish.” After a pause she addressed her guests again. “Since you have honoured me with your confidence, Wucheng ought to serve you. He is a straightforward man who speaks his mind and has offended many people; they are forever spreading stories about him. Fortunately, you gentlemen are too discerning to be misled by gossip. This will be easily settled. Spend two or three days enjoying Suzhou, and I promise to send you back to Hangzhou happy.” Hu took in every word, and could not help admiring her. With a few casual remarks she had washed away the entire suspicion that Yu Wucheng intended to seize the arms. “Rumours”; “we do not yet know whether there is any truth in them”: those were Hu’s own words. Even if they later became enemies, he could hardly retract them. Old ginger was indeed the hottest. Without realizing it, he had been tricked into making a concession. In more than a year of uninterrupted success, this was the one occasion on which he had stumbled—and he had to acknowledge himself fairly beaten.

“多谢三婆婆，我们不敢打搅了。静听好音！”胡雪岩站起身说：“不过，我们还有句话，实在想交一交俞大哥。等他来了，务必请三婆婆派人给我们个信，我们好当面跟俞大哥解释。”“都是好朋友，一切心照，何用解释？”俞三婆婆说，“两位抬举武成，我们母子祖孙三代都是感激的。等武成一回来，我马上叫他给两位去请安。”这几句交代，漂亮之至。胡雪岩和裘丰言，心满意足，但要告辞，却被留住了。“无论如何，要让我们租孙，尽一点意思，吃了便饭再请回去！”俞三婆婆又说：“看见两位，我倒想起有件心事，还要重托。”俞三婆婆的话，其实是留客的托词。筵席是早就预备好的，俞家还请了陪客，有些是俞少武的同僚，有些是俞武成的师兄弟。不管是何身分，对胡、裘二人的礼数，都极恭敬。好在胡雪岩长于词令，裘丰言为人风趣，所以很快地都消除了拘束的感觉，快谈豪饮，颇为酣畅。

“Thank you, Granny. We must not trouble you further, and shall await your good news.” Hu rose. “There is just one more thing. We would very much like to make Brother Yu’s acquaintance. When he comes, please be sure to send word, so that we may explain things to him in person.” “We are all good friends and understand one another. What need is there for explanations?” she replied. “Your kindness to Wucheng makes all three generations of our family grateful. As soon as he returns, I shall send him to pay his respects.” Nothing could have been more gracefully said. Hu and Qiu were entirely satisfied, but when they tried to leave, she detained them. “You must at least allow my grandson and me to show a little hospitality. Have a simple meal before you go. Besides, seeing you has reminded me of something that has been weighing on my mind. I have an important favour to ask.” This was really an excuse to keep them. A banquet had been prepared beforehand, and the family had invited other guests to help entertain them: some were Yu Shaowu’s colleagues, others fellow disciples of Yu Wucheng. Whatever their standing, all treated Hu and Qiu with the utmost respect. Fortunately, Hu’s easy eloquence and Qiu’s wit soon dispelled the stiffness. They talked freely and drank heartily, and the occasion became thoroughly enjoyable.

酒到一半，俞少武告个罪，回到二厅，那里也有一桌丰盛筵席，是俞三婆婆亲自做主人，款待芙蓉和七姑奶奶。这一桌就不如外面那样轻松自如了，主要的原因是，芙蓉被奉为首席，深感不安，过于矜持。俞少武一进来，先敬堂客的酒。照官称叫芙蓉是“胡姨太太”，他也学了京里的规矩，将“姨”字念成“亦”子，表示“亦是一位太太”。敬了“胡亦太太”，再敬七姑奶奶，她跟俞少武是青梅竹马之交，一个叫“七姐”，一个叫“大弟弟”。这一番周旋过后，俞少武才搀着祖母到大厅向官客来敬酒。在座的陪客都是她的晚辈，胡、裘二人亦以晚辈自居，所以一齐起身离座，再三谦辞。结果由俞三婆婆总敬一杯，然后向他孙子说道：“少武，你要向胡老爷、裘老爷磕头道谢。这两位真正够义气！”俞少武也已知道他父亲的所作所为，倘或认真，是件不得了的事，所以连声答应着，要来行礼。胡雪岩和裘丰言，自然不肯受这个头。逊席相避，于是俞三婆婆又说话了。

Halfway through the drinking, Yu Shaowu excused himself and returned to the second hall. Another sumptuous table had been laid there, where Third Granny Yu was entertaining Furong and the Seventh Lady herself. This gathering was less relaxed than the one outside, chiefly because Furong, installed in the seat of honour, felt uncomfortable and was excessively reserved. Shaowu first offered the ladies a toast. In official usage he addressed Furong as “Madam Hu,” with the title given to a concubine, but followed the polite Beijing custom of altering the tone of the word yi so that it suggested “also”: she was “also a wife.” After toasting her, he drank to the Seventh Lady. They had known one another since childhood: he called her Seventh Sister, and she affectionately called him her eldest little brother. When these courtesies were over, he helped his grandmother into the main hall to toast the gentlemen. All the guests there belonged to a younger generation, and Hu and Qiu likewise treated her as their elder. They rose from their seats together and repeatedly protested that she was too kind. In the end she drank one toast to the whole company, then told her grandson, “Shaowu, you must kowtow to Mr Hu and Mr Qiu and thank them. These are men who truly understand loyalty.” Shaowu now knew what his father had been doing, and understood how grave the consequences would be if the matter were pursued. He readily agreed and stepped forward to perform the ceremony. Hu and Qiu naturally refused to accept it, moving away from their seats to avoid receiving his bow. Third Granny Yu spoke again.

“两位请听我说。我就是这个孙子，如今大小也是朝廷的命官，在我们这种人家，也算荣宗耀祖了。不过，江湖上的家世，跟官场难免合不拢，这是我一直不放心的一件事，总想托个人照应，说实话，官场中也认识几位，不是人家看不起我们，就是自己觉得高攀不上。难得两位赏面子，再说句放肆的话，我也看得两位跟官场中人不同，真正是重情分，讲义气。所以，今天当着大家的面，我把我这个孙子，托付给两位，要让少武磕了头，我才放心。”这一套长篇大论，旁人只觉得俞三婆婆是特别看重两位贵客，在胡雪岩却听出弦外之音，拜托照应俞少武，实在是拜托回护俞武成。照此看来，俞三婆婆用的心思极深，处处在防备自己这方面会动用官面上的力量来对付她的儿子。有此疑忌存在，总不是件妙事。为了消释可能会有的误会，胡雪岩不肯说谦辞的话，“既然三婆婆如此吩咐。我们倒不能不老着脸受少武一个头。”他说，“三婆姿，从今天起，少武的事，就等于我自己兄弟的事一样。”“胡老爷，你的话错了！”俞三婆婆平静地说：“是你侄儿的事。”“侄儿也罢，兄弟也罢，只当我自己的事！”“少武！”俞三婆婆极欣慰地说：“你听见没有？还不快磕头！你说想调回来，跟在我身边，胡老爷一定会替你想法子。”这一说，俞少武更是心甘情愿地跪了下来，胡雪岩也就坦然受了他的大礼。江湖上重然诺，经此当筵一拜，俞少武的穷通富贵，便与胡雪岩息息相关了。而父子的安危祸福是不可分的，所以俞武成如果遇到了什么难题，胡雪岩由于对俞少武有责任，自然也不能袖手。俞三婆婆这着棋，实在高明，然而也只有胡雪岩喻得其中的深意。

“Gentlemen, hear me out. This is my only grandson. He now holds an imperial appointment, however modest, and in a family such as ours that brings honour to our ancestors. But a background in the brotherhoods does not sit easily with official life. That has always worried me, and I have long wanted someone to look after him. To tell you the truth, we do know a few officials, but either they look down on us or we feel we are reaching above ourselves. You gentlemen have been exceptionally kind. If I may speak freely, I also find you different from other officials: you value affection and honour your obligations. So today, before everyone here, I entrust my grandson to you. I shall not feel at ease until Shaowu has kowtowed to you.” To the others, this lengthy speech showed only how highly she valued her distinguished guests. Hu heard the meaning beneath it. In asking them to protect Yu Shaowu, she was really asking them to shield Yu Wucheng. Her calculations went deep: at every turn she was guarding against their using official power against her son. Such suspicions could do no good. To dispel any possible misunderstanding, Hu abandoned polite refusals. “Since those are your instructions, Granny, we must have the effrontery to accept Shaowu’s bow. From today, I shall treat his affairs as I would my own brother’s.” “You have that wrong, Mr Hu,” she said calmly. “Your nephew’s.” “Nephew or brother, I shall treat them as my own affairs.” “Shaowu!” she said, deeply pleased. “Did you hear that? Hurry and bow! You say you want a transfer home so that you can stay with me. Mr Hu is sure to find a way for you.” Shaowu now knelt with even greater willingness, and Hu accepted his solemn obeisance without further demur. In the brotherhoods, a pledge was binding. After this bow before the assembled guests, Shaowu’s future fortunes were intimately linked with Hu’s. A father’s and a son’s safety and prosperity could not be separated; if Wucheng encountered trouble, Hu’s obligation to Shaowu would prevent him from standing aside. Third Granny Yu had played a masterly move, though Hu alone understood its full significance.

因此，他对松江的消息，特感关心。为了不愿让裘丰言担心，他只好独任其忧，在肚子里默默做功夫，将俞武成的情况，重新作一番深入的估计。想得越多，疑虑越深，到了第二天早晨，尚无消息，他觉得不能再因循株守，坐失时机了。于是约了俞少武在吴苑茶馆见面，找个僻静之处，悄悄问道：“你晓不晓得令尊此刻在哪里？”“大概是在青浦叉袋角。”俞少武说，“不瞒老世叔说，家父在那里有一房家眷，叉袋角又有几家大赌场，是家父喜欢去的地方。我昨天就请人分头去找了，到今天晚上一定会有消息的。”“我倒要问问你，令尊跟赖某人到底是啥交情？他想动那票‘货色’，你知道不知道？“这一问，俞少武的脸色显得异常认真，用一种近乎要赌咒的语气答道：“在老世叔面前，我不敢说一个字的假话，我一点都不晓得。家父不会跟我说，我也不便去问。而且我一直在京城里，回来还下到半个月，一共见过家父两面，谈不了几句话。如果我晓得有这件事，无论如何要想法子，劝家父打消了它！”

This made Hu particularly anxious for news from Songjiang. Unwilling to alarm Qiu, he bore the worry alone, silently working through a fresh and more searching assessment of Yu Wucheng’s position. The more he thought, the deeper his misgivings grew. When the following morning came without news, he felt that he must stop waiting passively and letting his opportunities slip away. He arranged to meet Yu Shaowu at the Wuyuan teahouse, found a secluded place, and asked quietly, “Do you know where your father is at present?” “Probably at Chadaijiao, near Qingpu. To be frank with you, Uncle, my father keeps another household there. There are also several large gambling houses at Chadaijiao, and he likes to visit them. Yesterday I sent people off in different directions to look for him. There should certainly be news by tonight.” “Tell me, then: what exactly is your father’s relationship with this Lai? Did you know he intended to lay hands on that consignment of ‘goods’?” Shaowu’s face became intensely serious. He answered in a tone that came close to swearing an oath. “I would not dare tell you a single falsehood, Uncle. I knew absolutely nothing. My father would not tell me, and it would hardly be proper for me to question him. Besides, I have been in the capital all this time and returned less than a fortnight ago. I have seen him only twice, and we exchanged very few words. If I had known of any such plan, I would have done everything I could to persuade him to abandon it.”

话说得很诚恳，也相当坦率，胡雪岩觉得跟他谈论，不必象对他祖母那样，要加几分小心，便直抒所感，“这件事，照我看有麻烦。令尊客居异地，手下的弟兄都不在这里，虽然出头来主持，无非因人成事。上山容易下山难，不是凭一句话就可以罢手的。如果脱不得身，怎么办？”俞少武是现任的武官，当然能够领会胡雪岩所说的话，想一想果然，截掠军械，是件非同小可的事，调兵遣将，如何下手，得手以后，如何将这批枪械运交赖汉英？官军派出大队拦截剿办，又如何应付？自然得有一番布置，而入不是自己的人，中途变卦，想凭一句话就撤消原有的布置，哪有这么容易的事？这样一层一层想下来，脸上顿现愁云：“事不宜迟！”他说，“及早劝阻，还容易着手。我马上就到青浦去一趟。”见他如此果断，胡雪岩深感安慰，不过他的计算到底比俞少武深得多，按着他的手说：“你不宜去！因为虽是父子，到底是朝廷的五品武官，去了容易让人起疑。而且，只要令尊是在青浦，这时候就一定到了松江，你去了也是扑空。”“那么，老世叔说怎么办，我听命。”“我想我马上赶回松江去看看。你派个得力的人跟了我去。”胡雪岩紧接着说，“令祖母有什么话交代，最好也由这个人带了去，那就更省事了。”“是！”俞少武说，“我马上回去告诉我奶奶。老世叔是不是一起到舍下坐坐？”“不必！”胡雪岩答道：“我先回金阊栈料理，在那里等你的信息。再托你转告七姑奶奶，小妾烦她照应。”“是，是！我奶奶跟姨太太极谈得来，就请她在舍下玩两天，一切我们都会伺候，老世叔请放心！”“打搅不安。只有等我回来，再给三婆婆道谢了。”

His sincerity and frankness convinced Hu that he need not be as guarded with him as with his grandmother. He spoke his mind. “I foresee trouble here. Your father is living away from his own territory, and his men are not here. Even if he takes the lead, he must depend on others to carry it through. It is easier to climb the mountain than to come down. He cannot necessarily call the whole thing off with a word. What if he cannot extricate himself?” As a serving military officer, Shaowu readily grasped the point. Seizing a shipment of arms was no small undertaking. Men had to be deployed, the attack planned, the captured weapons delivered to Lai Hanying. And what would they do if government troops came in force to intercept and suppress them? Arrangements must already have been made. If the men were not his father’s own, how could he change his mind halfway through and cancel everything with a word? As Shaowu followed the implications, anxiety clouded his face. “There is no time to lose. The sooner we dissuade him, the easier it will be. I shall go to Qingpu at once.” Hu was reassured by his decisiveness, but his own calculations went further. He laid a hand on Shaowu’s. “You should not go. He may be your father, but you are an imperial military officer of the fifth rank. Your presence could arouse suspicion. Besides, if your father was at Qingpu, he will certainly have reached Songjiang by now. You would miss him.” “Then tell me what to do, Uncle. I shall obey.” “I think I had better hurry back to Songjiang and see for myself. Send a capable man with me. And if your grandmother has any instructions, it would be best for him to carry them too. That will save trouble.” “Certainly. I shall go straight home and tell her. Would you like to come with me?” “No. I shall return to the Jinchang Inn to make arrangements and wait there for your message. Please also tell the Seventh Lady that I must trouble her to look after Furong.” “Of course. My grandmother and Madam Hu get on wonderfully. Let her spend a couple of days at our house; we shall attend to everything. Please set your mind at rest.” “I am sorry to impose on you. I shall thank your grandmother when I return.”

于是就在吴苑分手，各奔东西。胡雪岩轿去如飞，到了金阊栈，只见裘丰言一个人在那里独酌。裘丰言见他进来，便站起身来说，“你到哪里去了？刘三爷和老同又不在，我一个人又不敢走开，无聊之极，只有借酒遣闷。“胡雪岩虽有事在心，但天生是什么忧烦都不肯现于词色的人，便笑笑调侃他说：“没有哪个不准你吃早酒，何必还要想套话来说？”刚说到这里，只见刘不才脚步轻飘飘地走了进来，裘丰言一见，便趁着酒兴向他这位谐谑惯了的好朋友取笑，“三爷，春风得意？”他说，“我真羡慕，老胡委派了你那么好一个差使。说说看，温柔乡中是何风光？”胡雪岩昨天派他的差使，是去寻芳问艳，刘不才不辱所命，连走数家，到底访着了一处极出色的妆阁，主政是金阊的一朵名葩。“你先说，芳名叫啥？”“你看！”

They parted at the Wuyuan and went their separate ways. Hu’s bearers carried him swiftly to the Jinchang Inn, where he found Qiu drinking alone. Qiu rose when he entered. “Where have you been? Third Master Liu and Old Zhou are both out, and I did not dare leave the place unattended. I was bored to death and could only drink to pass the time.” Hu had much on his mind, but it was in his nature never to let worry show in his face or voice. He smiled and teased him. “Nobody has forbidden you your morning wine. Why bother inventing an excuse?” Just then Liu Bucai floated in on light feet. Warmed by the wine, Qiu turned his jokes upon this old partner in banter. “Third Master, enjoying the spring breeze of success? I envy you. What a delightful commission Old Hu gave you! Tell us what the land of tender pleasures is like.” Hu had sent Liu out the previous day to seek attractive women. Liu had done his duty, visiting house after house until he found a particularly fine establishment, presided over by one of Jinchang’s celebrated beauties. “First, tell us her charming name.” “Look!”

刘不才从口袋里掏出一张“局票”，黄笺纸印着一个银元宝，只字皆无。连胡雪岩那样的人，都猜不透他是什么用意？“我是问那个姑娘的花名，你弄这张纸头给我们看干什么？”裘丰言把局票翻过来、翻过去看了两遍，交还刘不才。刘不才不接，“你再仔细看看，”他说，“这张局票上就隐着她的名字。”这一指点，胡雪岩马上就猜到了一半：“姓黄？”“对！叫做黄银宝。”“妙！说穿了一点不错。”裘丰言仔细欣赏那张局票，角上有“北京琉璃厂荣宝斋精制”的字样，不由得又夸一声：“似俗而雅，倒也难得。”“一点不错！似俗而雅。”刘不才抚掌说道，“名字俗气，人倒雅得很，象朵菊花似地。

Liu drew an invitation card from his pocket. On yellow paper was printed a silver ingot, without a word of writing. Even Hu could not fathom his meaning. “I asked for the lady’s professional name. What are you showing us a piece of paper for?” Qiu turned the card over twice and handed it back. Liu would not take it. “Look more closely. Her name is hidden in the card.” With that hint Hu immediately guessed half the answer. “Her surname is Huang—yellow?” “Exactly! Huang Yinbao: Silver Ingot.” “Excellent! Once you explain it, there can be no doubt.” Qiu examined the card appreciatively. In one corner were the words “Finely made by Rongbaozhai, Liulichang, Beijing.” He praised it again. “Refinement beneath apparent vulgarity. Quite unusual.” “Precisely! Refinement beneath apparent vulgarity.” Liu clapped his hands. “Her name is common enough, but the woman herself is wonderfully elegant. Like a chrysanthemum.”

“那么你就是陶渊明了！‘采菊东篱下，悠然见南山’。”裘丰言笑道，“昨天晚上采了花没有？”“哪有这么容易的事，你看得她们太不值钱了。”“那么昨天一夜不回是借的干铺？”胡雪岩说，“刚刚头一夭肯借干铺，也就不错的了。”“照这样说，你今天就该‘报效’了！”裘丰言兴致勃勃地说，“今天晚上吃你的‘镶边酒’！我替你看看客人看，老胡一个，俞少武一个……”“慢点，慢点！”胡雪岩打断他的话，“不要算上我，我马上要到松江……”这下是裘丰言打断了他的话：“何出此言？”“是真的。吃花酒的事，摆在一边再说。”胡雪岩略顿一下，毅然说道：“我们先商量正经。”先是不愿他人分忧，到此地步，已非胡雪岩一个人的力量所能消弭可能有的祸患，因此，他唯有直言心中的顾虑。裘丰言已有先见，经验也多，倒还不怎么样，刘不才从前是纨袴，此刻成了清客的材料，酒阵拳仗，一往无前，但听得这种隐伏杀机的勾当，顿时脸色大变，连黄银宝都置诸脑后了。胡雪岩一见他这样子，赶紧加以安慰，拍拍他的背说：“没有你的事，你跟老裘坐守苏州。”“就没有我的事，我也不放心你去啊！”“这话不错。”裘丰言接口：“是我的事，我没有袖手闲坐的道理。”“算了，算了！”胡雪岩急忙拦在前头，“我没工夫跟你们争论，现在办事要紧，你们要听我的，不要乱了阵脚。”

“Then you must be Tao Yuanming! ‘Picking chrysanthemums by the eastern fence, at leisure I see the southern hills.’” Qiu laughed. “Did you pick the flower last night?” “Do you think it is so easy? You value these women far too cheaply.” “So you stayed out all night on a ‘dry bed’?” Hu asked. “Being allowed even that on the first night is not bad.” “In that case you ought to show your gratitude today!” Qiu said eagerly. “Tonight we shall drink your ‘fringed banquet’ wine. Let me draw up the guest list: Old Hu for one, Yu Shaowu for another—” “Wait, wait!” Hu interrupted. “Leave me out. I have to go to Songjiang at once—” Now it was Qiu who interrupted. “Whatever makes you say that?” “It is true. Leave the courtesans’ banquet for later.” Hu paused, then said decisively, “We must discuss serious business first.” He had initially wished to spare the others his worries, but the possible danger had grown beyond his power to avert alone. He could only tell them frankly what he feared. Qiu had already foreseen trouble and, with his greater experience, was not especially shaken. Liu, once a rich young idler and now well suited to life as a gentleman’s entertaining companion, could charge fearlessly through any battle of wine and drinking games; but at the hint of a venture concealing deadly danger, his face changed completely. He even forgot Huang Yinbao. Hu hastened to reassure him, patting him on the back. “You have no part in this. Stay here in Suzhou with Old Qiu.” “Even if I have no part in it, how can I be easy about your going?” “Quite right,” Qiu put in. “This is my business. I cannot sit here doing nothing.” “Enough, enough!” Hu stopped them before they could go further. “I have no time to argue. What matters now is getting things done. Listen to me, and do not throw our arrangements into confusion.”

这是所谓徒乱人意，裘丰言和刘不才不敢再开口。于是胡雪岩又估计情势，分析出三种情况，三种难处。三种情形是：第一，俞武成跟洪杨合作，调兵遣将，已经布置就绪，而且身不由己，无形中受了挟制。其次，虽已布置就绪，但收发由心，仍可化干戈为玉帛，只是一笔遣散的费用，相当可观。最后一种情况，也正就是大家所希望的，俞武成可以说不干就不干，至多将已收的酬金退还给对方而已。“凡事总要作最坏的打算。算它是第一种情形，我倒也是个逢盘。”裘丰言略一踌躇，“老胡，你先说，是哪三种难处？”“第一是俞家的交情。俞三婆婆实在厉害，如今这件‘湿布衫’好象糊里糊涂套到我身上了，投鼠忌器，处处要顾着俞武成，这是最大的难处。”“是的。”裘丰言深深点头，“又不光是俞家的交情，牵涉到松江漕帮，无论如何这份交情要保全。”“我也是这么想。所以我初步有这么个打算，倘或是第一种情形，至少要想法让俞武成退出局外，哪面也不管。“

They would only unsettle him by going on, so Qiu and Liu fell silent. Hu reassessed the situation, distinguishing three possibilities and three difficulties. First, Yu Wucheng might have joined forces with the Taipings, completed all the deployments, and then found himself constrained by circumstances and no longer free to act. Second, the arrangements might be complete but still under his control, so that fighting could be averted, though disbanding the men would cost a considerable sum. The third possibility, and the one they all hoped for, was that Yu could simply withdraw, having at most to return the payment he had received. “We must always allow for the worst,” Qiu said. “Suppose it is the first case. I do have a plan.” He hesitated. “But tell us your three difficulties first, Old Hu.” “The first is our obligation to the Yu family. Third Granny Yu is formidable. Somehow this wet shirt has been slipped over my head, and now it clings to me. I cannot strike at the rat for fear of breaking the vessel: at every turn I must consider Yu Wucheng’s safety. That is the greatest difficulty.” “Yes.” Qiu nodded gravely. “And it is not only our friendship with the Yu family. The Songjiang brotherhood is involved too. Whatever happens, those ties must be preserved.” “That is what I think. So my first idea is this: if the worst case proves true, we must at least find a way to get Yu Wucheng out of the affair, helping neither side.”

“你的意思是，如果赖汉英一定要蛮干，就是我们自己来对付？”“对！我们要替俞武成找个理由，让那方面非许他抽身不可。”“这容易想。难的是我们自己如何对付？”裘丰言说，“照我看到那时候，非请兵护运不可。”“难就难在这里，目前请兵不容易，就请到了，绿营的那班大爷，也难伺候，开拔要钱，安营要钱，出队要钱，阵亡抚恤，得胜犒赏更要钱……”“算了，算了！”裘丰言连连摇手：“此路不通！不必谈了。”“那么谈第三种难处。譬如能够和平了结，他们的人或者撤回，或者遣散，我们当然要筹笔钱送过去。钱在其次，万一有人告我们一状，说我们‘通匪’，这个罪名，不是好开玩笑的！”裘丰言瞿然而惊，“我倒没有想到这一层。”他是那种做了噩梦而惊醒的欣慰：“亏得你想得深！”

“You mean that if Lai Hanying insists on forcing the issue, we must deal with him ourselves?” “Exactly. We must find Yu Wucheng a reason for withdrawing that the other side cannot refuse to accept.” “That should be easy enough. The difficulty is how we deal with them ourselves. At that point, I imagine we should have to request a military escort.” “That is precisely the trouble. Troops are not easy to obtain just now. Even if we secured them, those grand gentlemen of the Green Standard Army would be hard to satisfy. They need money to march, money to pitch camp, money to send out a detachment; compensation for the dead and rewards for victory cost still more—” “Enough, enough!” Qiu waved both hands. “That road is blocked. No point discussing it.” “Then take the third difficulty. Suppose we can settle it peacefully, and their men either withdraw or disband. Naturally, we shall have to raise a sum to send them. The money itself matters less than this: what if someone accuses us of ‘colluding with rebels’? That is no charge to trifle with.” Qiu started. “I had not thought of that.” His relief was like that of a man waking from a nightmare. “Thank goodness you look so far ahead!”

在旁边半天不曾开口的刘不才，听得满腹忧烦，忍不住插了句口：“只听你们说难！莫非真的一筹莫展？”“你倒说，有什么好办法？事情是真难！”裘丰言看着胡雪岩，“老胡，我看只有照我的办法，一了百了。”他故意不说，留下时间好让人去猜。可是连胡雪岩那样的脑筋，亦不得不知难而退：“老裘，你说吧！看看你在死棋肚里出了什么仙着？”“依我说，这票货色，拿它退掉！”他撇眷京腔说，“大爷不玩儿了！看他们还有辙没有？““这，这叫什么话。”刘不才是跟他开惯玩笑的，便尖刻地讥嘲：“天气还没有热，你的主意倒有点馊了！”“三爷，话不是这么说！出的主意能够出其不意，就是高着。真的如此，叫他们自费心思一场空，倒也不错。不过，为了明哲保身，不求有功，但求无过，不妨这么办。现在，我们是在打开下，就决不能这么退缩。面子要紧！“这个面子关乎胡雪岩的信誉，裘丰言的前程，还有王有龄的声望。非绷了起来不可。说来说去还是得照胡雪岩的办法，初步找个理由让俞武成脱身事外，第二步看情形再作道理。“这个理由太容易找了！”裘丰言说：“俞武成是孝子，江湖上尽人皆知。如今者太太说不行，就叫不行！俞武成母命难违，不是很好的理由吗？”

Liu, who had been silent for some time, grew increasingly anxious and could not resist breaking in. “All I hear is how difficult it is! Surely we are not completely helpless?” “Then suggest a good solution. It really is difficult!” Qiu turned to Hu. “Old Hu, I think we shall have to do as I propose and settle the whole thing once and for all.” He deliberately withheld his plan to let them guess, but even Hu’s quick mind had to admit defeat. “Tell us, Old Qiu. What miraculous move have you found inside this dead position?” “I say we cancel that consignment!” He affected a Beijing accent. “Your lordship isn’t playing any more! Let us see what they can do then.” “What sort of suggestion is that?” Long accustomed to joking with Qiu, Liu jeered sharply. “The weather is not even hot yet, and your ideas are already going sour!” “You must not put it that way, Third Master,” Hu said. “An unexpected move can be a brilliant one. If we really did that, all their scheming would come to nothing, which would be no bad thing. If all we wanted was to keep ourselves safe, seeking no achievement and merely avoiding blame, we could do it. But we are trying to build something. We cannot retreat like that. Our standing matters!” That standing involved Hu’s credit, Qiu’s prospects, and Wang Youling’s reputation. They had to uphold it. After all their discussion, they returned to Hu’s plan: first find a reason to release Yu Wucheng from the affair, then decide the next step according to circumstances. “The reason is easy enough,” Qiu said. “Everyone in the brotherhoods knows that Yu Wucheng is a dutiful son. If his mother says no, then no it must be. He cannot disobey her. What better reason could there be?”

胡雪岩还未及答言，只见又是四名马弁出现，随后便见俞少武陪着一个人进来，这个人的形象生得极其奇特，一张圆脸上眉眼鼻子凑得极近，年纪有六十了，一张瘪嘴缩了上去，越显得五官不分，令人忍不住好笑。“老世叔，我替你引见一个人，是我大师兄杨凤毛。”看杨凤毛年纪一大把，胡雪岩总当他是俞少武的父执辈，如今听说是“大师兄”，知是俞武成的“开山门了的徒弟，大概代师掌帮，是极有分量的人物，所以赶紧走上去拉着他的手说：”幸会，幸会！“哪知杨凤毛年纪虽大，腰脚极其轻健，一面口中连称“不敢”，一面已跪了下去磕头。胡雪岩谦谢不遑，而杨凤毛“再接再励”，对裘丰言和刘不才都行了大礼。“这是怎么说？”胡雪岩很不安地，“这样子客气，叫我们倒难说话了。”“是我们三婆婆交代的，见了胡老爷跟胡老爷的令友，就跟见了师父一样。“杨凤毛垂手说道：”胡老爷，三婆婆派我跟了你老到松江去。“接着张目四顾，显得很踟蹰似地。

Before Hu could answer, four mounted attendants appeared again, followed by Yu Shaowu escorting a man of singular appearance. His eyebrows, eyes, and nose were crowded close together in a round face. He was about sixty, and his sunken mouth drew upwards, making his features seem even more tightly clustered. It was difficult not to laugh. “Uncle, let me introduce Yang Fengmao, my senior fellow disciple.” Seeing Yang’s age, Hu had assumed he belonged to the generation of Shaowu’s father. On hearing “senior fellow disciple,” he realized that this must be Yu Wucheng’s first disciple, probably a man who managed the brotherhood on his master’s behalf and carried considerable weight. Hu hurried forward and took his hand. “A great pleasure to meet you!” But despite his years, Yang was extraordinarily supple and nimble. Repeatedly protesting that the honour was undeserved, he had already dropped to his knees and kowtowed before Hu could prevent him. While Hu was still protesting, Yang pressed on with his courtesies and performed the same full ceremony before Qiu and Liu. “What is all this?” Hu asked uneasily. “With such elaborate courtesies, you make it difficult for us even to talk.” Yang stood with his hands respectfully at his sides. “Those were Third Granny’s instructions. I am to treat Mr Hu and his friends as I would my own master. Mr Hu, she has sent me to accompany you to Songjiang.” He looked about him, apparently hesitating.

胡雪岩懂得他的意思，江湖上最重秘密，有些话是连家人父子都不能相告的、虽然裘、刘在座共闻，决不会泄漏，不过“麻布筋多，光棍心多”，杨凤毛既然有所顾忌，不如单独密谈的好。于是他招招手说：“杨兄，我们借一步说话！”“告罪，告罪！”杨凤毛又向裘丰言、刘不才作了两个大揖，才跟着胡雪岩走到套间，地方大小，两个人就坐在床沿上说话。“胡老爷！三婆婆跟我说，胡老爷虽在‘门槛’外头，跟自己人一样，关照我说话不必叙客套，有什么说什么。所以，我有句老实话，不晓得该不该说？”这样招呼打在前头，可知那句“老实话”，不会怎么动听。只是胡雪岩不是那么喜欢听甜言蜜语的人，便点点头说：“没有关系！你尽管说好了。”“我也打听过，胡老爷是了不起的人物。不过隔道门槛就象隔重山，有些事情，胡老爷怕没有经过。”杨凤毛略停一下又说：“江湖上的事，最好不沾上手，一沾上就象唱戏那样，出了上场门就不容你再缩回去了。”“我知道。这出戏不容我不唱，哪怕台下唱倒彩，我也要把它唱完。”“现在这出戏不容易唱，‘九更天带滚钉板’！”杨凤毛满脸诚恳地说，“能不唱最好不唱。”

Hu understood. Secrecy mattered greatly in the brotherhoods; there were things that could not be told even within a family, between father and son. Qiu and Liu would never reveal what they heard, but as the saying went, hemp has many fibres and a worldly man has many precautions. Since Yang had reservations, a private conversation would be best. Hu beckoned. “Brother Yang, let us step aside for a word.” “Pray excuse us.” Yang bowed deeply to Qiu and Liu, then followed Hu into the inner room. It was small, and they sat on the edge of the bed. “Mr Hu, Third Granny tells me that although you stand outside our threshold, you are one of our own. She says I need not trouble with polite speeches, but should tell you plainly whatever there is to say. There is something frank I should like to tell you, though I do not know whether I ought.” Such a preamble suggested that his frankness would not be particularly agreeable. Hu, however, was not a man who wanted only sweet words. He nodded. “Never mind that. Say whatever you wish.” “I have made inquiries too, and know you are an exceptional man. But a threshold can divide people as completely as a mountain. There may be things you have never experienced.” Yang paused. “It is best not to become involved in the affairs of the brotherhoods. Once you do, it is like going onstage: after you pass through the entrance, you cannot turn back.” “I know. This is a play I have no choice but to perform. Even if the audience boos, I must finish it.” “And this particular play is no easy performance: Nine Watches of the Night, with a turn rolling on a bed of nails!” Yang’s face was earnest. “If you can avoid performing it, that would be best.”

一听这话，胡雪岩起了戒心。俞武成想动那批洋枪，显然的，杨凤毛也是参预其事的一个，而且以他们的关系来说，必还是一个重要角色。虽然三婆婆极其漂亮，俞少武相当坦率，然而都算是局外人，只有眼前的这个杨凤毛，才是对自己此行成败，大有关系的人物，而照彼此的立场来说，是敌是友，还不分明，倒要好好应付。因此，他很谨慎地答道：“多谢老兄的好意。事出无奈，不要说是‘九更天’，就是‘游十殿’我也只好去。不过，‘花花轿儿人抬人’，承三婆婆看得起我，我唱这出戏，总要处处顾得到她老人家。”这番表白，似软实硬，意思是不着三婆婆的面子，就要硬碰硬干个明白。至于“花花轿儿人抬人”这句俗话是反着说：“我是如此尊敬三婆婆，莫非你们就好意思让我下不去？”杨凤毛是俞武成最得力的帮手，见多识广，而且颇读过几句书，此来原是先要试探试探胡雪岩，看他是不是够分量、能经得起大风大浪的人？如果窝窝囊囊不中用，或者虽中用是个半吊子，便另有打算。现在试探下来，相当佩服，这才倾心相待。“胡大叔！”他将称呼都改过了，“既然你老能体谅我们这方面，愿意担当，那么我就掏心窝子说实话。事情相当麻烦。”

These words put Hu on his guard. Clearly Yang was involved in Yu Wucheng’s plan to seize the foreign guns, and their relationship suggested that his role must be important. Third Granny had handled matters admirably, and Shaowu had been quite frank, but both stood outside the actual undertaking. The man before him was directly concerned with the success or failure of his mission. Given their respective positions, it was not yet clear whether they were friends or enemies. Hu would have to handle him carefully. “Thank you for your kindness, brother,” he answered cautiously. “But I have no choice. Never mind Nine Watches of the Night—even if the play were A Tour of the Ten Courts of Hell, I should have to go on. Still, people carry one another in the flowered bridal chair: respect must be mutual. Since Third Granny thinks so well of me, I must consider her at every turn in this performance.” The words sounded soft but were firm beneath. Without his regard for Third Granny, he implied, he would meet force with force and settle the matter outright. As for people carrying one another in the bridal chair, he meant the saying in reverse: “I show her such respect—can you have the heart to leave me stranded?” Yang, Yu Wucheng’s ablest assistant, was widely experienced and had some learning as well. He had come intending first to test Hu: was this a man of sufficient weight, able to weather great storms? If he proved feeble and useless, or capable but lacking sound judgment, Yang would make other plans. The test now left him impressed, and he offered his confidence. “Uncle Hu!” Even his form of address had changed. “Since you understand our position and are willing to shoulder the responsibility, I shall speak from the heart. We are in considerable trouble.”

果然，是胡雪岩所估计的第一种情形。这当然也要怪俞武成沉不住气，自觉失去了镇江一带的地盘，寄人篱下，不是滋味，同时漕帮弟兄的生计甚艰，他也必须得想办法，为了急谋打开困难，以致身不由己，受到挟制。“胡大叔，”杨凤毛说，“我师父现在身不由己。人是他们的一切布置也是他们的，不过抬出我师父这块招牌，挡住他们的真面目而已。”“那我就不懂了，莫非他们从镇江、扬州那方面派人过来？不怕官军晓得了围剿？”“这就要靠我师父帮他们遮盖了。”杨凤毛答道，“镇江、杨州派来的人倒还不多，一大半是小刀会方面的。周立春的人本来已经打散，现在又聚了拢来了。““如果你师父不替他们遮盖呢？”胡雪岩问：“那会变成啥样子？”“变得在这一带存不住身。”这就是对方非要绊住俞武成不可的道理。事情很明显了，俞武成是骑虎难下，纵能从背上跳下来，亦难免落个出卖自己人的名声。江湖上最着重这一点，所以俞三婆婆的话，有没有效力，俞武成是不是始终能做个百依百顺的孝子，都大成疑问。

It was indeed the first of the possibilities Hu had foreseen. Yu Wucheng was partly to blame for his own impatience. Having lost his territory around Zhenjiang, he found it galling to live dependent on others. The men of the grain-transport brotherhood were also struggling to survive, and he had to find some means of supporting them. In his haste to break through these difficulties, he had placed himself under constraints and lost his freedom of action. “Uncle Hu, my master is no longer his own master,” Yang said. “The men are theirs, and so are all the arrangements. They merely put my master’s name out in front to conceal who they really are.” “Then I do not understand. Have they sent men over from Zhenjiang and Yangzhou? Are they not afraid the government troops will discover them and surround them?” “That is where they depend on my master to provide cover. There are not actually many men from Zhenjiang and Yangzhou. Most belong to the Small Swords Society. Zhou Lichun’s men were scattered, but now they have gathered again.” “And if your master stopped providing cover? What would happen then?” “They would be unable to remain in this district.” So that was why the other side had to keep Yu Wucheng tied to them. The position was plain. He was riding a tiger and could not easily dismount. Even if he managed to jump down, he could scarcely escape the name of a man who had betrayed his own people. Nothing mattered more in the brotherhoods. Whether his mother’s instructions would have any effect, and whether he could remain an invariably obedient son, were now very doubtful.

想是这样想，话不妨先说出来：“ ‘萝卜吃一截剥一截’，我想第一步只有让你师父跳出是非之地，哪一方面都不帮。这总可以办得到吧？”“那也要做起来看。”“怎么呢？”“那方面如果不放，势必至于就要翻了脸。”杨凤毛说，“翻了脸能够一了百了，倒也罢了，是非还在！胡大叔，请问你怎么对付？除非搬动官军，那一来是非更大了。”这就是说，跳下了虎背，老虎依然张牙舞爪，如何打虎，仍旧是个难题。就这处处荆棘之际，胡雪岩灵机一动，不自觉地说出来一句话。“做个伏虎罗汉，收服了它！”杨凤毛不懂他的话，愕然问道，“胡大叔！你说点啥？”胡雪岩这才醒悟，自己忘形自语，“喔，”他笑道，“我想我心里的事。有条路或许走得通，我觉得这条路，恐怕是唯一的一条路。““只要走得通，我们一定拼命去走。胡大叔，你说！”胡雪岩定定神答道：“我是‘空子’，说话作兴触犯忌讳，不过……”“唉，胡大叔！”杨凤毛有些不耐，“我们没有拿你老当空子看。胡大叔，你何需表白。”

Those were Hu’s thoughts, but he could still put his proposal into words. “You peel a radish one section at a time as you eat it. I think our first step must be to get your master out of the trouble, helping neither side. Surely that can be done?” “We shall have to try it before we know.” “Why?” “If the other side refuses to release him, there is bound to be a breach. If a breach settled everything, well and good, but the trouble would remain. How would you deal with it, Uncle Hu? You would have to bring in government troops, and that would make matters worse.” In other words, even if the rider dismounted, the tiger would still bare its teeth and claws. How to deal with it remained a problem. With thorns obstructing every path, an idea suddenly flashed into Hu’s mind, and he spoke without realizing it. “Become the tiger-taming arhat and bring it to heel!” Yang stared in puzzlement. “What was that, Uncle Hu?” Hu realized that he had been thinking aloud. “Oh,” he said, smiling, “I was considering something. There may be a way through. I rather think it is the only way.” “If it leads anywhere, we shall follow it with all our strength. Tell me!” Hu collected himself. “I am an outsider, and what I say may offend against your rules. Still—” “Uncle Hu!” Yang was growing impatient. “We do not regard you as an outsider. There is no need to explain yourself.”

“好！那我就实说。”胡雪岩回忆着老太爷的话，从容发言：“你们漕帮的起源，我也有些晓得，洪杨初起，你们都很看重的，哪晓得长毛做出来的事，不伦不类，跟圣经贤传上所说的大道理，全不对头，简直可以说是逆天行事，决计成不了气候。既然如此，无需跟他们客气。再说，你们镇江、扬州的地盘，就失在他们手里。有朝一日光复了，你们才有生路。你说我这话是不是？”“是的！”杨凤毛深深点头，忧郁地说：“我师父这一次是做得莽撞了些。”“歪打可以正着！老兄，”胡雪岩抚着他的背说，“我替你们师弟想条路子！小刀会这方面的情形，我也有点晓得，周立春他们那班人，亦不过一时鬼摸头，心里何尝不懊悔？只不过摸不到一条改邪归正的路子。如今要靠你们师弟两个。我的意思是，周立春下面那批打散了的人，既然已经聚拢，何不拿他们拉过来？”一听这话，杨凤毛那张瘪嘴闭得越紧，以至于下巴都翘了起来，一双眼睛眨得很厉害，不过眼中发亮，是既困惑又欣喜的神情。“胡大叔，你是说‘招安’这批人？”“是啊！”胡雪岩说，“赖汉英那里来的长毛，如果肯一起过来最好，不然就滚他娘的蛋，也算对得起他们了！”杨凤毛觉得胡雪岩的做法很平和。再往深处去想，就算俞武成能退出来成为局外人，也只是表面如此看法，实际上是决不能置身事外的，倘或官军围剿，事情闹大了，江湖上还会批评他不够朋友。所以唯有这样子才是正办，退一步说，招安不成，他总算为朋友尽过心力，对江湖上也有了交代了。

“Very well. I shall speak plainly.” Recalling the Old Master’s words, Hu continued calmly. “I know something of the origins of your grain-transport brotherhoods. When Hong and Yang first rose, you all thought highly of them. Who could have known that the Long-haired rebels would behave so perversely, in complete defiance of the great principles taught by the sages in the Classics? They are acting against Heaven itself and can never amount to anything. Since that is so, there is no need to stand on ceremony with them. Besides, your territory in Zhenjiang and Yangzhou was lost to them. Only when it is recovered will you have a way to make a living. Am I right?” “You are.” Yang nodded deeply. “My master acted a little rashly this time,” he added gloomily. “A crooked shot may still hit the mark.” Hu patted his back. “Brother, let me suggest a way out for you and your master. I know something of the Small Swords Society too. Zhou Lichun and his men merely lost their heads for a time. How could they not regret it? They simply cannot find a path back to an honest life. Now it must depend on you two. Since Zhou Lichun’s scattered followers have gathered again, why not bring them over to our side?” Yang’s sunken mouth tightened until his chin tilted upwards. His eyes blinked rapidly, but shone with a mixture of bewilderment and delight. “Uncle Hu, you mean to secure an amnesty and bring these men into government service?” “Yes. If the Long-haired men sent by Lai Hanying are willing to come over too, so much the better. Otherwise, let them get the hell out. That would be treating them fairly enough!” Yang found Hu’s approach remarkably conciliatory. Thinking further, he saw that even if Yu Wucheng withdrew, his neutrality would be only apparent. In reality he could never stand entirely apart. If government troops attacked and matters grew serious, the brotherhoods would still accuse him of failing his friends. This was therefore the only proper course. Even if the attempt to obtain an amnesty failed, he would at least have done his best for his friends and could account honourably for his conduct.

想通了这些道理，顿时将胡雪岩敬如天神，站起来便磕了个头。胡雪岩大惊，急忙避开，拉着他的胳膊说：“怎么，怎么，无缘无故来这一套！”“胡大叔，你算是救了我师父一家，你老怕还不晓得，三婆婆几十年没有为难过，这一趟她老人家，急得睡不着觉，在苏州，我们是客地，这件事要闹开来，充军杀头都有分！再说，她老人家又疼孙子，少武是朝廷的武官，我师父做这件事，传出去不断送了少武的前程？如今好了！不过，”杨凤毛又赔笑说：“你老送佛到西天，我晓得你老跟何学台有交情，招安的事，还要仰仗鼎力。”说着，又作了个大揖。胡雪岩倒不曾想到何桂清。如今听杨凤毛一提醒，立刻在心里喊一声：妙！何桂清纸上谈兵的套折，上了不少，现在能办成这事，是大功一件，对于他进京活动，大有帮助。这样看来，自己的这个主意，凭心而论，着实不坏。于是他很爽快地答道：“一句话！这样好的事情不做，还做啥！”“多谢胡大叔！”杨凤毛的脸色转为严肃，“我听你老的差遣。”胡雪岩最会听话，听出这是句表示谦虚的反话，实际上是杨凤毛有一套话要说，所以这样答道：“事情是你们师弟为头，我只要能尽力，决不偷半分的懒。不必客气，该怎么办请你分派。”“那我就放肆了！我想，第一，这话只有你老跟我两人晓得。”“当然！”胡雪岩说，“你们杨家的堂名叫‘四知’，天知、地知、你知、我知。”“是。第二，我想我先去一趟，请胡大叔听我的消息，再去见何学台。”“那也是一定的。总要那方面点了头，才好进一步谈条件。”

Once he saw all this, Yang regarded Hu as nothing less than a saviour. He rose and immediately dropped to his knees in a kowtow. Startled, Hu hurried aside and caught his arms. “What are you doing? Why this ceremony all of a sudden?” “Uncle Hu, you have saved my master’s whole family. You may not realize it, but Third Granny has not faced such trouble for decades. This time she has been too anxious to sleep. We are strangers in Suzhou. If this affair comes to light, penal military exile or execution could await us. And she dotes on her grandson. Shaowu is an imperial military officer. If what my master has done became known, would it not ruin his career? Now things will be all right! But”—Yang gave an ingratiating smile—“since you have begun this good work, please carry it through to the end. I know you are on friendly terms with Education Commissioner He. We shall still need your wholehearted assistance over the amnesty.” He bowed deeply again. Hu had not thought of He Guiqing. At Yang’s reminder, he exclaimed inwardly, “Excellent!” He Guiqing had submitted plenty of memorials discussing warfare on paper. If he could accomplish this, it would be a substantial achievement and a great help to his efforts in the capital. In fairness to himself, Hu felt that his own idea was really quite good. He answered readily, “You have my word. If I would not do such a worthwhile thing, what would I do?” “Thank you, Uncle Hu.” Yang’s face became solemn. “I await your instructions.” Hu was an expert listener. He understood that this was a modest way of saying the reverse: Yang actually had proposals of his own. “You and your master must take the lead. Wherever I can help, I shall spare no effort. Let us dispense with courtesies. Tell me what is to be done.” “Then I shall take the liberty. First, I think this should remain between the two of us.” “Certainly. Your Yang clan’s hall is called the Hall of the Four Knowings: Heaven knows, Earth knows, you know, and I know.” “Yes. Second, I should like to go there first. Please wait for my news before approaching Commissioner He.” “Of course. The other side must agree before we can go on to discuss terms.”

“你老最明白不过，那我就不必多说了。”杨凤毛说，“我马上赶去见我师父，最多一昼夜的工夫，一定赶回来。”“你师父怕是在松江，我们一起去也可以。”“不！不在松江。”不在松江在哪里呢？他不说，胡雪岩也不便问，不过心里已经雪亮，俞武成的行踪，杨凤毛一定清楚。说是最多一昼夜定能赶回来，则隐藏之地亦决不会远。“事不宜迟。我现在就走。”杨凤毛郑重叮嘱：“胡大叔！明天上午，请你无论如何不要走开，我人不到一定有信到。”等杨凤毛告辞，裘丰言自然要问起谈话的情形。胡雪岩谨守约定，只字不吐，只笑着说：“你陪刘三爷去捧那个‘银元宝’好了。几台花酒吃下来，就有好消息了。”裘丰言宽心大放，喜滋滋地跟着刘不才走了。胡雪岩一个人静了下来，将前后经过情形细想了一遍，觉得自己的路子走对了，走得通，走不通，明日此时，可见分晓，且不去管它。眼前有一整天的工夫，光阴如金，不该虚耗，正好将潘家所托，以及阿巧姐的终身，办出个头绪来。这就得找周一鸣了。奇怪的是一早不见他的面，只好留下话，如果来了，让他在金阊栈等候，然后坐轿进城，先去拜访何桂清。

“You understand perfectly. I need say no more,” Yang said. “I shall leave at once to see my master, and return within a day and a night at most.” “Your master is probably in Songjiang. We could go together.” “No. He is not in Songjiang.” Where, then? Yang did not say, and Hu could hardly ask. But one thing was now perfectly clear to him: Yang knew exactly where Yu Wucheng was. And if he could promise to return within a day and a night, the hiding place could not be far away. “There is no time to lose. I shall leave now.” Yang gave a solemn reminder. “Uncle Hu, whatever happens, please stay here tomorrow morning. If I do not come myself, a message certainly will.” After he left, Qiu naturally asked about their conversation. Hu kept his promise and revealed nothing. He merely smiled. “Go with Third Master Liu and pay court to his ‘Silver Ingot.’ By the time you have enjoyed a few courtesans’ banquets, there will be good news.” Greatly relieved, Qiu went off happily with Liu. Left alone, Hu quietly reviewed everything that had happened and felt that he had chosen the right course. Whether it would work would be clear by this time tomorrow; he could leave it alone for now. He had a whole day before him. Time was gold and should not be wasted. He could use it to make some headway with the Pan family’s commission and Sister A’qiao’s future. For that he needed Zhou Yiming. Strangely, Zhou had not appeared all morning. Hu could only leave instructions that, if he came, he should wait at the Jinchang Inn. Then he took a chair into the city to call first on He Guiqing.

名帖一投进去，立刻延见，何桂清将他请到书斋，执手寒暄，极其殷勤，自然要问起如何又到了苏州？“有几件事，必得来一趟，才能料理清楚。其中是一件是云公吩咐的，办得差不多了。”“喔！”何桂清很高兴地问：“是怎样一个人？”“德是中上，貌是上中，才是上上，将来体贴殷勤，一定没话可说。”胡雪岩因为阿巧姐自己看中过何桂清，料想进了何家的门，必然驯顺非凡，所以此时夸下这样的海口。何桂清当然相信他的话，喜心翻倒，忍不住搓着手说：“能不能见一面？”“请云公稍安毋躁。”胡雪岩笑道：“几时到了上海，立刻就能见面。”到底身分是二品大员，不便做出猴急相，何桂清只得强自按捺着那颗痒痒的心，定一定神答道：“天气快热了。炎暑长行，一大苦事，我想早一点走。算日子，也就在这几天必有旨意。”“这样说起来，总在五月中就可以动身了。”“对了。”“那我跟云公暂且作个约定，以五月十五为期，如何？”“好的。我也照这个日子去作安排。”何桂清又说：“你托我的事，我替你办了。潘叔雅人倒不俗，我们现在常有往来。承他的情，常有馈遗，想辞谢吧，是你老兄面上的朋友，似乎不恭，只好愧受了。”

The moment his visiting card went in, he was admitted. He Guiqing invited him into the study, clasped his hand, and greeted him most cordially, naturally asking what had brought him back to Suzhou. “There are several matters I could only settle by coming in person. One is the commission you gave me, Mr Genyun. That is nearly arranged.” “Oh!” He asked delightedly, “What sort of woman is she?” “For virtue, the upper of the three middle grades; for looks, the middle of the three upper grades; for talent, the highest grade of all. As for thoughtfulness and attentive care, I am certain you will have nothing to complain of.” Since A’qiao had already taken a fancy to He, Hu assumed that she would be exceptionally compliant once she entered his household, and so made this sweeping promise. He naturally believed him. Overjoyed, he could not help rubbing his hands. “Could I meet her?” “A little patience, Mr Genyun,” Hu said with a smile. “As soon as you reach Shanghai, you can see her.” After all, He was a senior official of the second rank and could not appear indecently eager. He forced himself to restrain his itching desire and composed himself. “The weather will soon be hot. A long journey in the height of summer is a great hardship, so I should like to leave a little earlier. By my reckoning, the imperial instructions must arrive within the next few days.” “Then you should be able to set off by the middle of the fifth month.” “Yes.” “Shall we make a provisional arrangement for the fifteenth day of the fifth month?” “Very well. I shall make my plans accordingly.” He continued, “I have attended to the matter you entrusted to me. Pan Shuya is a cultivated fellow, and we now see one another often. He is kind enough to send frequent gifts. I have thought of declining them, but since he is a friend of yours, that might seem discourteous. I could only accept them with some embarrassment.”

话中是很愿屈尊交潘叔雅这样一个朋友，而后叔雅对他的尊敬，则从“常有往来，常有馈遗”这些话中，表现得明明白白。胡雪岩的愿意，就是要替他们拉拢，所以听得何桂清的话，当然感到欣慰。照规矩，他亦还需有所表示，“云公爱屋及乌，真是感同身受。”他拱拱手说。“哪里，哪里！”何桂清心里在想，真叫“三日不见，刮目相看”，相隔没有多少日子，不想他也会掉文了！虽是尺牍上的套话，总算难能可贵，这样想着，便又笑道：“雪岩兄，曾几何时，你的谈吐大不相同，可喜之至。”胡雪岩略有窘色，“叫云公见笑！”他急转直下地说：“有件事，想跟云公请教。”说着，他看了看站在门口的听差。这是有要紧话说，何桂清便吩咐听差回避，然后由对面换到胡雪岩下首，侧过头来，等他发话。

His words showed that he was quite willing to condescend to a friendship with Pan Shuya. Pan’s respect for him was equally plain in those frequent visits and gifts. Bringing the two together had been Hu’s intention, and he was naturally pleased. Courtesy required an acknowledgment. “Your kindness extends from me to my friends, Mr Genyun, and I feel it as though it were shown to myself,” he said, clasping his hands. “Not at all, not at all!” He thought of the saying that after only three days apart one must look at a man with fresh eyes. They had been separated for so short a time, yet Hu had learnt to turn a literary phrase! Even if it was merely a conventional expression from letter-writing, it was a creditable accomplishment. Smiling, he said, “Brother Xueyan, in no time at all your conversation has become quite different. How pleasing!” Hu looked a little embarrassed. “You flatter me, Mr Genyun.” He abruptly changed the subject. “There is something on which I should like your advice.” He glanced at the attendant by the door. Understanding that an important confidence was coming, He dismissed the man, moved from the seat opposite Hu to the place beside him on the less honoured side, and inclined his head to listen.

“我想请教云公一件事，”胡雪岩低声说道，“现在有一批人，一时糊涂，误犯官军，很想改过，不知道朝廷能不能给他们一条自新之路？”“怎么不能？这是件绝好之事！” 何桂清大为兴奋，“这批人是哪里的？”问到这话，胡雪岩当然不肯泄底，“我亦是辗转受人之托，来手做事很慎重，详情还不肯说。不过，托我的那人，是我相信得过的。我也觉得这是件好事，心想云公是有魄力、肯做事的人，所以特地来请教。”他略停一下又说：“如今我要讨云公一句话，此事可行与否？朝廷可有什么安抚奖励的章程？”“一般都是朝廷的子民，如能悔过自新，朝廷自然优容，所以安抚奖励，都责成疆吏，相机处理。”何桂清又说，“我为什么要问这批人在哪里，就是要看看归谁管，如果是苏州以西，常州、镇、扬一带，归江南、江北两大营，怡制台都难过问。倘或是苏州以东，许中丞是我同年，我可以跟他说，诸事都好办。”听得这话，胡雪岩暗暗心喜，“那么，等我问清了再回报云公。不过，”胡雪岩试探着问：“我想，招抚总不外有官做、有饷领，云公，你说是不是呢？““给官做是一走的，看那方面人数多少，枪械如何，改编为官军，要下委札派相当的官职。饷呢，至多只能过来的时候，关一次恩饷，以后看是归谁节制，自有‘粮台’统筹发放。”

“I should like to ask you about a certain matter, Mr Genyun,” Hu began in a low voice. “There is a group of men who lost their heads for a time and wrongly took up arms against the government. They are anxious to mend their ways. Could the court give them a chance to make a fresh start?” “Why ever not? That would be an excellent thing!” He grew highly excited. “Where are these men?” Hu would certainly not disclose that. “The request reached me through intermediaries. The man handling it is very cautious and will not yet give the details. But the person who approached me is someone I trust. I too thought it a good thing, and knowing that you have both courage and a willingness to act, I came expressly for your advice.” He paused. “What I need now is your word: can it be done? Does the court have any regulations for granting assurances or rewards?” “They are all subjects of the court. If they repent and reform, the court will naturally treat them generously. Such assurances and rewards are therefore left to the provincial authorities to arrange as circumstances require. I asked where they were because we must see whose jurisdiction they come under. West of Suzhou, around Changzhou, Zhenjiang, and Yangzhou, they would fall under the Jiangnan and Jiangbei Grand Camps. Even Governor-General Yi would find it difficult to intervene. But east of Suzhou, Governor Xu is a fellow graduate of my year. I can speak to him, and everything will be easier.” Hu was secretly delighted. “Then I shall ascertain the facts and report back to you. But I imagine,” he ventured, “that winning men over generally comes down to giving them appointments and paying them. Would you agree?” “Appointments are certain. According to their numbers and their weapons, they would be reorganized as government troops and commissions issued for suitable posts. As for pay, at most they could receive a special gratuity when they first came over. Afterwards, it would depend on whose command they entered; the military finance office would arrange their regular pay.”

胡雪岩所想象的，亦是如此。只是授官给饷，都还在第二步争取，首先有句话，关系极重，不能不问清楚。“云公，”他特意摆出担忧的沉重脸色，“我听说有些地方弃械就抚的，结果上了大当，悔之莫及。不知可有这话？”“你是说‘杀降’？”何桂清大摇其头，“杀降不祥，古有明训。这件事你托到我，就是你不说，我也一定要当心。你想想，我无缘无故来造这个孽干什么？再说，我对你又怎么交代？”“是！是！”胡雪岩急忙站起来作了个揖：“云公厚爱，我自然知道，只不过提醒云公而已。”“是你的事，我无有不好说的。不过，这件事要快，迟了我就管不到了。”“我明白，就在这两三天内，此事必有个起落。不过还有句话，我要先求云公体谅。”胡雪岩说：“人家来托我，只是说有这件事，详情如何，一概不知。也许别有变化，作为罢论，到那时候，我求云公不要追究。”“当然。我不会多事的。”“还要求云公不必跟人谈起。”“我知道，我知道。如果此事作为罢论，我就当根本没有听你说过。总而言之，我决不会给你惹麻烦。”

This was what Hu had expected. Appointments and pay, however, belonged to the second stage of negotiation. First there was something of far greater importance that he had to establish. “Mr Genyun,” he said, deliberately assuming a grave, anxious expression, “I have heard that in some places men who laid down their arms and accepted pacification were badly deceived, and regretted it when it was too late. Is there any truth in that?” “You mean the killing of men who surrender?” He shook his head emphatically. “Since ancient times we have been warned that killing those who surrender brings ill fortune. If you entrust this affair to me, I shall take care over that even without your asking. Why should I bring such evil upon myself for no reason? And how could I answer to you?” “Of course, of course!” Hu rose hastily and bowed. “I know your kindness to me. I merely wished to remind you.” “Since it is your affair, I am ready to accommodate you in every way. But it must be done quickly. If it is delayed, it will be beyond my reach.” “I understand. Within the next two or three days it will certainly be settled one way or the other. There is just one more thing for which I must ask your understanding. The people who approached me told me only that such a matter existed. I know none of the details. Circumstances may change and the whole thing be dropped. If that happens, I beg you not to pursue it.” “Naturally. I shall not make trouble.” “And please do not mention it to anyone.” “I understand. If it is dropped, I shall behave as though you had never told me. In any event, I shall certainly cause you no difficulties.”

“云公如此体恤，以后我效劳的地方就多了！”这句话中有深意，意思是说，只要何桂清肯言听计从，不是自作主张，他就会有许多办法拿出来，帮何桂清升官发财。“正要倚重。”何桂清说：“老兄阛阓奇才，佩服之至。前几天又接得雪轩的长函，说老兄帮了他许多忙。我跟雪轩的交情，不同泛泛，以后要请老兄以待雪轩者待我！”于是由此又开始叙旧，一谈就谈得无休无止。许多客来拜访，何桂清都吩咐听差，请在花厅里坐，却迟迟不肯出见，尽自应酬胡雪岩。这让客人很不安，同时也因为还有许多事要料理，所以一再告辞，而主人一再挽留，最后还要留着吃晚饭，胡雪岩无论如何不肯。等到脱身辞了出来，太阳已快下山了。

“With such consideration from you, Mr Genyun, there will be many more ways in which I can be of service.” The words carried a deeper meaning. If He would follow Hu’s advice instead of acting on his own initiative, Hu could devise any number of ways to help him gain promotion and wealth. “That is exactly the assistance I hope to rely on,” He replied. “I have the greatest admiration for your extraordinary commercial gifts. A few days ago I received another long letter from Xuexuan, telling me how much you had done for him. My friendship with him is no ordinary one. In future, I hope you will treat me as you treat him.” This led them into reminiscence, and the talk went on and on. Many other visitors arrived; He instructed his attendant to seat them in the reception pavilion, yet repeatedly put off seeing them while he continued to entertain Hu. This made Hu uncomfortable, and he also had many matters still to attend to. He repeatedly tried to leave, but his host repeatedly detained him, finally insisting that he stay for dinner. Hu would not agree. By the time he managed to take his leave, the sun was almost down.
