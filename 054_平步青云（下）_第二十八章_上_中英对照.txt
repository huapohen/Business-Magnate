第二十八章｜CHAPTER TWENTY-EIGHT

安下了这支伏兵，胡雪岩才算放下心来。第二天一早起身，漱洗穿戴，刚刚停当，杨凤毛就到了，一起吃了早饭上船。船就停在阊门码头，双桨如飞，穿过吴江有名的垂虹桥，中午时分就到了同里。船是停在一人家后门口，踏上埠头，就算到了目的地。在船上，胡雪岩就听杨凤毛谈过，这家人家做米行生意，姓朱，朱家老大是俞武成的徒弟，也就是杨凤毛的后弟。俞武成只要一到同里，就住他家，朱老大待师父极其恭敬，所以胡雪岩、刘不才不妨亦以朱家为居停。胡雪岩此来一切听从杨凤毛的安排，虽觉得住在素昧平生的朱家，可能会十分不便，但亦不便表示异议，幸好朱老大殷勤随和，一见之下，颇觉投缘，把那嫌拘束的感觉，消除了许多。引见寒暄以后，朱老大随即向杨凤毛说道：“大哥，师父到青浦去了，今天晚上如果不回来，明天早晨一定到。临走留下话，请大哥代为向贵客道歉，失迎不安。又说，请贵客一定住在这里。”说到这里，面向胡雪岩和刘不才：“舍间太小，只怕款待不周，让两位委屈。”于是胡雪岩少不得也有几句谦谢的门面话，一面应酬，一面在心里转念头，觉得这半天的工夫，白耗费了可惜，应该如何想法子的好好利用。

Only after arranging for this reserve force did Hu Xueyan feel at ease. Early the next morning, he had just finished washing and dressing when Yang Fengmao arrived. They breakfasted together and boarded the boat waiting at the Chang Gate landing. The two oars flew. They passed beneath Wujiang’s famous Chuihong Bridge and reached Tongli at noon. The boat drew up at the back door of a house; once they stepped onto the landing, they had arrived. On the journey Yang had explained that their hosts, the Zhu family, were rice merchants. The eldest Zhu brother was a disciple of Yu Wucheng, and therefore Yang’s junior fellow disciple. Whenever Yu came to Tongli, he stayed here. Eldest Brother Zhu treated his master with the greatest respect, so Hu and Liu Bucai could also make the house their quarters. Hu had left all the arrangements to Yang. Although he thought it might be inconvenient to stay with a family he had never met, he could hardly object. Fortunately, Zhu was attentive and easy-going. They took to one another at first sight, and much of Hu’s anticipated discomfort disappeared. After introductions and greetings, Zhu said to Yang, “Elder Brother, Master has gone to Qingpu. If he does not return tonight, he will certainly be back tomorrow morning. He asked you to apologise to our honoured guests for his absence, and said they must stay here.” He turned to Hu and Liu. “Our house is small. I fear we cannot entertain you properly and shall put you to some inconvenience.” Hu naturally replied with the customary thanks and modest assurances. As he did so, he considered how to make good use of the half-day ahead rather than let it go to waste.

念头还没有转定，朱家的佣工来请吃饭，鱼米之乡，饮食丰美，虽是便饭，亦如盛筵，朱老大还说：“简慢不恭，到晚上替贵客接风。”同席的除了宾主四人，另外还有三个人作陪，朱家的老三、帐户和教书先生。席间谈谈吴江的风物，轻松得很。饭罢，杨凤毛征询胡雪岩的意见，是在朱家客房中睡个午觉起来，再作道理，还是出去走走。“久闻同里是个福地，去瞻仰瞻仰吧！”于是由杨凤毛、朱老大陪着，出去走走，后门进来，前门出去。一条长街，铺得极平整的青石板，放眼望去，鳞次栉比的楼房，相当整齐。街上行人，十九穿的绸衫，哪怕是穿草鞋的乡下人，都是干干净净的一身细蓝布短衫裤，手中多半持一支湘妃竹的早烟袋，有的套一个白玉扳指，有的腰上拴一挂玉石佩件。吴中人物的俊雅，光看这些乡下人，就不难想见了。走到一家挂灯结彩的人家，朱老大站住脚说：“两位要不要进去玩玩？”从大门中望进去，里面有好几桌赌，胡雪岩便问：“不认识的也可以进去吗？”“可以，可以，敝处的风俗是如此。”于是进去看了看，有牌九、有摇摊。胡雪岩入境问俗，志在观光，不肯出手，刘不才则守着“冷、等、狠”三字诀，不愿出手，这样连闯了几家，都是转个圈子就走，由南到北，一条长街快到尽头了。

Before he had decided, a servant came to announce lunch. In this land of fish and rice, food was plentiful and delicious. An ordinary meal resembled a banquet, yet Zhu still said, “Forgive this poor reception. Tonight we shall give you a proper welcome.” Besides the four hosts and guests, three others joined them: the third Zhu brother, the accountant and the family tutor. They talked pleasantly of Wujiang’s scenery and local life. After lunch Yang asked whether Hu would like to take a nap in the guest room before deciding what to do, or go for a walk. “I have long heard that Tongli is a blessed place. Let us go and admire it.” Accompanied by Yang and Zhu, they went out through the front door, having arrived by the back. Before them stretched a long street paved with exceptionally even blue-grey stone slabs, its rows of buildings presenting a remarkably orderly appearance. Nine out of ten passers-by wore silk robes. Even country people in straw sandals were dressed in spotless short jackets and trousers of fine blue cotton. Most carried a long pipe of mottled bamboo. Some wore white-jade thumb rings; others had jade ornaments at their waists. These villagers alone gave some idea of the grace and refinement of the people of the Wu region. Outside a house hung with lanterns and festive decorations, Zhu stopped. “Would you two like to go in and amuse yourselves?” Several gambling tables were visible through the gate. “May strangers go in?” Hu asked. “Certainly. That is our custom here.” They went in and looked about. There was pai gow and the dice-shaking game. Hu was interested in local customs and wanted only to observe, so he declined to play. Liu, following his three rules—keep cool, wait, strike hard—was not yet willing to play either. They entered several more houses, making a circuit of each before leaving, until they had almost reached the northern end of the long street.

因为胡雪岩和刘不才都有些鼓不起兴致来的样子，朱老大颇感不安，悄悄向杨凤毛问道：“到小金秀那里去坐坐，怎么样？”杨凤毛略有些踌躇，胡雪岩耳朵尖，心思快，听出来小金秀必是当地的一朵“名花”，勾栏人家要熟朋友同去，才有点意思，否则就会索然寡味，所以赶紧接口：“不必费心，就这样走走很好。”说着话，又到了一处热闹的人家，这家的情形与众不同，石库门开得笔直，许多卖熟食的小贩，由门外延入门内，似乎二门院子里都有。进出的人物，也不象别家衣冠楚楚地相当整齐，三教九流，龙蛇混杂，胡雪岩摸不清它是什么路道？刘不才却一望而知，别家是“书房赌”，这一家是真正的赌场。“如果要玩，就要在这种地方，”他说，“ ‘开了饭店不怕大肚汉’，赌起来爽气。”“刘三爷眼力真好！”朱老大听懂了他的话，由衷地佩服，“真正的赌场，在同里就这一家。要不要进去看看？只有这一家赌‘白星宝’。”听说是“白星宝”，刘不才技痒了，“这是赌心思！”他问，“这种赌在浙东很流行，怎么也传到了贵处？”“原是从浙东传过来的……”有个绍兴人姓章的，到同里来开酒作坊，生性好赌，先是聚集友好，关起门来玩，不久有人闻风而至，场面便大了，正好驻同里的巡检换人，新任的吴巡检是章老板的同乡，因势利用，包庇他正式开赌场，而巡检老爷则坐抽头钱，日进斗金，两年下来，已经腰缠十万了。听朱老大说明了来历，刘不才认为一定赌得很硬，不妨进去看看。

Neither guest seemed particularly entertained, and Zhu felt uneasy. Quietly he asked Yang, “What about calling on Little Jinxiu?” Yang hesitated. Hu, sharp of hearing and quick of mind, understood that Little Jinxiu must be a celebrated local beauty. A pleasure house was enjoyable only when one went with friends familiar with it; otherwise it could be thoroughly dull. He promptly intervened. “Please don’t trouble yourselves. A walk like this is quite enough.” As they spoke, they came to another lively house, different from the others. Its stone-framed gate stood wide open. Vendors of cooked food stretched from outside the entrance into the house, apparently even into the courtyard beyond the second gate. The people coming and going were not uniformly well dressed as elsewhere, but a mixture of every sort and station. Hu could not make out what kind of establishment it was. Liu recognised it at once. The others offered private gambling in gentlemen’s studies; this was a true gambling house. “If we mean to play, this is the sort of place,” he said. “‘A man who opens a restaurant cannot be afraid of big appetites.’ Here one can gamble freely.” Zhu understood and admired his discernment. “Third Master Liu has a good eye! This is the only proper gambling house in Tongli. Would you like to look inside? It is the only place that plays White Star Treasure.” At the name, Liu’s fingers began to itch. “That is a contest of minds. It is popular in eastern Zhejiang. How did it reach your town?” “It came from eastern Zhejiang originally.” A Shaoxing man named Zhang had opened a distillery in Tongli. An inveterate gambler, he had first gathered his friends to play behind closed doors. Before long others heard of it and joined them, and the operation grew. Just then a new police inspector, Mr Wu, arrived in Tongli. As Zhang’s fellow townsman, he took advantage of the situation, protected him in opening a regular gambling house, and collected a share of the gaming fees without lifting a finger. Money poured in daily; in two years he had amassed a hundred thousand. After hearing this account, Liu judged that the play must be substantial and worth a look.

到了大厅上一看，有牌九，有摇摊，赌客却并不多，从夹弄穿到二厅，情况就大不相同了，一张大方桌，三面是人，人有三排，第一排坐，第二排立，第三排则站在条凳上，肩叠着肩，头并着头，挤得水泄不通，好在朱老大也是当地有面子的人物，找着熟人情商，才腾出空位，让他们挤了进去。不管是江南用骰子摇的摇摊，广东抓棋子数的番摆，都在未知之数，只有白星宝是庄家可以操纵的“做宝”，所以刘不才说“这是赌心思”，赌客跟一个不在场的人赌心思。这个人名为“做手”，住在楼上，为了防止弊端，也为了不以场上的胜负得失影响他的冷静思考，所以楼梯是封闭的，只在板壁上开一个小孔，用一只吊篮传递宝盒。楼下有个小童专司奔走之役，铃声一响，将篮子吊了上去，拿着那个铜制的宝盒，送给在烟榻上吞云吐雾的做手，做好了室，再用铃声通知，将篮子吊了下来，等宝盒上桌，赌客方才下注。赌注跟摇摊完全一样，只是前朱雀、后玄武、左青龙、右白虎是用天、地、人，和四张牌九来表示。而且，虽是“做宝”，一样也有“路”。刘不才借了旁人所画的“路”来一看，认为这个做手是高手，做的宝变幻莫测，哪一条路都是，其实哪一条都不是，因而决定等着看一看再说。

In the main hall they found pai gow and the dice-shaking game, but relatively few players. When they passed through a side passage into the second hall, the scene was quite different. Three sides of a large square table were packed three rows deep: the first row seated, the second standing, the third standing on benches. Shoulders touched shoulders and heads pressed against heads, leaving scarcely a gap. Fortunately Zhu had some local standing. He found acquaintances willing to make room, and the party squeezed in. Whether it was the dice-shaking game of Jiangnan or Cantonese fan-tan, in which counters were gathered and counted, the result was an unknown quantity. White Star Treasure alone was a game whose result could be deliberately set by the house. That was why Liu called it a contest of minds: the players were matching wits with someone who was not in the room. This man, the setter, stayed upstairs. To prevent cheating and keep the fortunes of the table from disturbing his cool judgement, the staircase was closed off. A small opening in the wooden partition allowed the treasure box to travel in a basket. A boy upstairs attended to the errands. When the bell rang, the basket was hauled up, and he took the copper box to the setter, who reclined on an opium couch amid clouds of smoke. Once the result had been set, another bell signalled that the basket should be lowered. Only when the box reached the table did the players place their bets. The betting was exactly as in the dice-shaking game, except that four pai gow tiles—Heaven, Earth, Man and Harmony—represented the Vermilion Bird in front, the Dark Warrior behind, the Green Dragon on the left and the White Tiger on the right. Though the result was deliberately set, it still had a “pattern.” Liu borrowed another player’s chart of the results and studied it. The setter, he decided, was an expert: his choices were so unpredictable that they seemed to fit every pattern and in fact fitted none. Liu resolved to watch a little longer.

这时候已经连开了三记“老宝”，都是地牌，第四宝开出来还是老宝。到了第五宝，楼上的铃声还不响，宝官沉得住气，赌客却不耐烦了，连声催促，于是宝官叫人去拉铃，催上面快将宝盒送下来。催管催，上面只是毫无动静，催到第三遍，才听见铃响。但是赌客望着宝盒，却都踌躇着不知如何下注，因为连开了四记老宝，第五宝又拖延了这么多时候，料想楼上的做手，殚精竭虑算无遗策，这一宝十分难猜。“我照路打，应该这一门！”有人把赌往放在无牌那一门上。“不能照路了！一定是老宝。”另一个人说，随即在“老宝”上下注。“有理，有理！”又一个赌客连连点头，“拖延了这许多工夫，就为的要狠得下心来做老宝。”由于这两个人一搭一档，认定是老宝，别的赌客在不知不觉中受了影响，纷纷跟着下注，开出宝来，哗然欢呼，果然又是一张地牌，庄家赔了个大重门。

The same result, or “Old Treasure,” had already appeared three times in succession, each time the Earth tile. When the fourth box was opened, it too was Old Treasure. For the fifth round, the bell upstairs failed to ring. The croupier remained composed, but the players grew impatient and urged him to hurry. He sent someone to ring the bell and ask for the box. They rang and rang without any response. Only on the third attempt did a bell answer. Yet when the players looked at the box, they hesitated over their bets. After four repetitions of Old Treasure and such a long delay before the fifth, they imagined the setter upstairs must have exhausted his ingenuity to devise an infallible choice. This round would be especially hard to guess. “If I follow the pattern, it ought to be this gate,” someone said, placing his stake on Heaven. “You can’t follow the pattern now. It must be Old Treasure,” another declared, betting accordingly. “Quite right,” a third agreed. “All that delay was to work up the nerve to set Old Treasure again.” These two reinforced one another’s conviction, unconsciously influencing the others, who followed them with their stakes. When the box was opened, cheers broke out. It was indeed another Earth tile, and the house had to pay heavily on the most popular gate.

到第六宝越发慢了，等把室盒子催了下来，打老宝的人就少了，但是开出来的，居然又是老宝。这一次是惊异多于一切，而越到后来越惊异，连开六记地牌。“出赌鬼了！”有人向宝官说：“弄串长锭去烧烧！”“笑话！哪里有这种事？”宝官因为打地宝的越来越少，吃重赔轻，得其所哉，所以拒绝了那人的提议。到第九记再开出老宝来，赌客相顾歇手，没有一个人相信还会出老宝。于是道有赌鬼的那人便谈掌故，说乾隆年间有家赌场摇摊，曾经一晚上一连出过十九记的“四”，后来被人识破玄机，在场赌客都押“四孤丁”，逼得赌场只好封宝关门。“什么玄机？”“那晚上，乾隆皇帝南巡的龙船在同里过夜。真龙出现，还会不出四？”“对，对！”四是青龙，问的那人领悟了，但对眼前却又不免迷惑，“那么此刻又是什么花样？皇帝在京城，同里不会出现真龙，而且地牌是‘进门’！”“所以我说有赌鬼。”“照你这样说，还要出老宝？”“不晓得！”那人摇摇头：“就明晓得是老宝，也打不下手，照我看，这一记决不会‘两眼笔直’了！”“两眼笔直”是形容地牌。别的赌客都以其人之言为是，一直冷静在听，在看的刘不才，却独具机抒，他认为如果是讲“路”，则怪路怪打，还该追老宝，若是讲赌心思，则此人做老宝做得别人不敢下注，这才是一等一的好心思！照此推论，着实还有几记老宝好开。

The sixth round took even longer. When the box was at last brought down, fewer people bet on Old Treasure. Yet Old Treasure appeared again. Astonishment now outweighed every other response: six Earth tiles in succession, and the surprise only grew as the run continued. “There’s a gambling ghost about!” someone told the croupier. “Burn a string of paper ingots for it.” “Nonsense! There’s no such thing.” With fewer and fewer players backing Earth, the house was collecting large stakes and making small payouts. The croupier was well satisfied and rejected the suggestion. When the ninth round produced Old Treasure again, the gamblers looked at one another and held back. No one believed it could appear yet another time. The man who had spoken of a gambling ghost now told a story. During the Qianlong reign, a gambling house had once thrown four nineteen times running in a single night. At last the players discovered the secret and all placed single-number bets on four, forcing the establishment to close its box and shut its doors. “What was the secret?” “The Qianlong Emperor’s dragon boat was spending the night in Tongli on his southern tour. With the true dragon present, how could anything but four appear?” “Of course!” Four was the Green Dragon. The questioner understood, but the present case still puzzled him. “What is happening now, then? The Emperor is in the capital. There can be no true dragon in Tongli. And the Earth tile is Entrance!” “That is why I say there is a gambling ghost.” “Then you think Old Treasure will come up again?” “I don’t know.” The man shook his head. “Even if I knew it would, I couldn’t bring myself to bet on it. To my mind, this round certainly won’t be ‘two eyes in a straight line.’” That expression described the Earth tile. The other gamblers agreed with him. Liu, who had been listening and watching coolly throughout, had an idea of his own. If one was following patterns, a strange pattern demanded a strange response: one should keep backing Old Treasure. If it was a contest of minds, then setting Old Treasure until no one dared bet on it was the cleverest strategy of all. On that reasoning, there might well be several more repetitions to come.

“冷、等”两字做到了，现在所要的是个“狠”字，正当宝官要揭宝盒子时，他轻喝一声：“请等一等！”“可以。”宝官缩住手说：“等足输赢。”“请问，多少‘封门’？”“一千两。”“一千两！”“刘不才从身上掏出一卷银票来，取一张，摆在地牌那一门上。“这一下便令全场侧目。由于刘不才是生客，而且看他气度安闲，将千把两银子，看得如一吊铜钱似的不在乎，越发觉得此人神秘莫恻，因而也越增好奇的兴趣。百多只眼睛注视之下，开来居然又是“两眼笔直”！于是场中象沸了似的，诧异的、羡慕的、气愤的、懊恼的，众声并作，诸态毕陈。刘不才却是声色不动，只回头向朱老大轻声说了句：“侥幸！”这一下大家才知道这个生面孔的大赌客是未老大的朋友，纷纷投以仰慕的眼光。江湖中人最爱的是面子，朱老大自然以有这样一个“一赌惊人”的朋友为得意，脸上象飞了金，心上象拿熨斗烫过，舒坦异常。宝官笼络赌客，也凑兴表示佩服，而且关照站在“青龙角”上的“开赔”，免抽头钱，行话叫做“水子”，三厘、五厘不等。当然，刘不才也是很漂亮的，等开赔将三千两的筹码赔到，他取了根一百两的牙筹，往青龙角上抛了过去。

He had observed the first two rules, coolness and patience. Now it was time to strike hard. Just as the croupier was about to lift the lid, Liu called softly, “One moment, please.” “Certainly.” The croupier withdrew his hand. “We wait for every wager.” “What is the limit on a gate?” “A thousand taels.” “A thousand it is.” Liu took a roll of banknotes from his clothes, selected one and laid it on Earth. Every eye turned towards him. He was a stranger, and his unhurried manner, treating a thousand taels as casually as a string of copper cash, made him seem all the more mysterious and intriguing. Under more than a hundred watching eyes, the box was opened. Once again it was “two eyes in a straight line.” The room boiled over. Astonishment, envy, anger and regret found expression in a confusion of voices and gestures. Liu’s face remained unchanged. He merely turned and said quietly to Zhu, “A lucky chance.” Now everyone knew that this unfamiliar high-stakes gambler was Zhu’s friend, and admiring looks followed. To men of this world, reputation mattered above all. Zhu was naturally proud of a friend who had won such acclaim with a single wager. His face seemed gilded with delight, his heart as pleasantly warmed as though smoothed by an iron. Eager to cultivate his customers, the croupier added his compliments and told the settling clerk at the Green Dragon corner to waive the house commission, known in the trade as “water money,” normally three or five li. Liu, for his part, knew how to make a handsome gesture. When the clerk paid him three thousand taels in counters, he took an ivory counter worth a hundred taels and tossed it towards the Green Dragon corner.

等宝盒子再放到赌台上时，大家都要看刘不才如何下手？再定主意。这也有句红话，叫做“灯笼”。灯笼照“路”，有红有黑，赌场里讲究避黑趋红，如果刚才一直有人在追老宝，而有人错过了好几宝不出手，到“年三十看皇历，好日子过完了”再来下注，则其人之黑可知！善于趋避的人，就会抽回注码，改押别处，但刘不才这盏灯笼是红灯笼，别人对老宝不敢再押，就他敢，而且居然追到了，这是多旺的手气？所以都要跟着他下注。于是等刘不才将一千两银子一押在地牌上，赌注如雨，纷纷跟进。开出盒子来，宝官与开赔，相顾失色，而赌客则皆大欢喜，庄家在这一记者宝赔了两万多银子。这一下，全场鼎沸，连大厅上的赌客都赶了进来，刘不才则被奉若神明，他左右的两个赌客，都尽量将身子往外缩，怕挤得他不舒服。而就在这时候，发觉有人拍一拍他的肩，回头看时，是胡雪岩在向他使眼色，接着努一努嘴，示意他离去。刘不才实在舍不得起身，但又不敢不听胡雪岩的指挥，终于装模作样地掏出金表来看了看，点点头，表示约会的时间到了，然后一把抓起银票，站起身来。赌场里专有班在混的人，一看刘不才赢了六千银子，便包围上来献殷勤，刘不才自然懂“规矩”，到帐房里去兑现时，顺便买了一百两的小筹码，一人一根，来者不拒。

When the box was placed on the table again, everyone waited to see how Liu would bet before deciding for themselves. There was a term for this too: a “lantern.” A lantern illuminated the path, and it might be red or black. Gamblers avoided black luck and sought red. Suppose people had been backing Old Treasure throughout, while one man had missed several rounds and only joined in when, as the saying went, he consulted the almanac on New Year’s Eve and found all the auspicious days already gone. That man was plainly unlucky. A player skilled at reading such signs would withdraw his stake and move it elsewhere. Liu, however, was a red lantern. He had dared to back Old Treasure when no one else would, and had won. What magnificent luck! Everyone wanted to follow him. As soon as he placed another thousand taels on Earth, wagers rained down beside it. The box was opened. The croupier and settling clerk exchanged stricken looks, while the players rejoiced. On this round of Old Treasure the house paid out more than twenty thousand taels. The whole room erupted. Even gamblers from the front hall came rushing in. Liu was treated like a god; the men on either side pressed themselves away, afraid of crowding him. At that moment someone tapped his shoulder. He turned to see Hu signalling with his eyes, then pursing his lips towards the door to indicate that they should leave. Liu was intensely reluctant to rise, but he did not dare disregard Hu. At last he ostentatiously took out his gold watch, glanced at it and nodded, as though the hour of an appointment had arrived. He gathered up his banknotes and stood. The hangers-on who made their living around gambling houses saw that he had won six thousand taels and crowded round with attentions. Liu understood the customary courtesies. When he went to the cashier to exchange his winnings, he bought a hundred taels’ worth of small counters and gave one to everyone who approached, refusing no one.

一面“分红”，一面便有怨言，“你不该催我，”他向胡雪岩说，“做手的路子，让我摸到了，起码还有三记老宝。”“就因为你摸到了，我才催你走。大家都跟着你打，再有两下，就可以把赌场打坍。何苦一到同里，就害得人家栽跟斗？”“胡大叔！”朱老大跟着杨凤毛这样称呼，“你老人家真正是老江湖，够义气。”刘不才心里不服，“赌场无父子”，讲情面义气，自己倒霉，但当着主人，又见朱老大是那样尊重胡雪岩，只好隐忍不言。再退一步想想，片刻工夫，赢进六千银子，真正“赌能不输，天下营生第一”！不由得便有了笑意。“刘三爷赌得好，胡大叔不赌则更好！”杨凤毛对朱老大说：“怪不得胡大叔有那么好的人缘，你我都要学他老人家。”“言重，言重！胡雪岩摸着脸笑道：”你们两位说得我脸红了。““闲话收起。”杨凤毛问道：“再到哪里去坐坐？”“恐怕胡大叔、刘三爷也倦了，回到舍间息一息，吃酒吧！”

Even as he distributed these shares of his good fortune, he complained to Hu. “You shouldn’t have hurried me away. I had worked out the setter’s pattern. There were at least three more Old Treasures to come.” “It was precisely because you had worked it out that I hurried you away. Everyone was following your bets. A couple more rounds and you could have ruined the place. Why should we arrive in Tongli and immediately bring someone else to grief?” “Uncle Hu,” Zhu said, adopting Yang’s form of address, “you really understand the ways of the world, and you have a proper sense of honour.” Liu did not agree. At the gambling table there were no fathers and sons; considering friendship and loyalty only meant losing oneself. But in front of their host, and seeing Zhu’s respect for Hu, he could only keep his thoughts to himself. On reflection, he had won six thousand taels in a matter of moments. Truly, if only one could gamble without losing, it would be the finest occupation in the world. A smile came to his face. “Third Master Liu plays well, but Uncle Hu does still better by not playing,” Yang told Zhu. “No wonder he is so well liked. We ought both to learn from him.” “You are far too kind!” Hu rubbed his face with a smile. “You two are making me blush.” “Enough compliments,” Yang said. “Where shall we go next?” “Uncle Hu and Third Master Liu must be tired,” Zhu replied. “Let us return to my house, rest a little and have a drink.”

于是安步当车，仍旧回到朱家。他家最好的一处房子，是座水阁，在嘉宾莅止时，正好有朱家亲戚女客住在那里，这时已腾了出来，朱老大便将胡雪岩等人，延入水阁休息。刚刚坐定，朱家老仆，在门外轻叫一声“大少爷！”使个眼色把他请了出去，悄悄说道：“赌场里的章老板来了，说要看我们家一位客人，还带了四样礼，请大少爷先出去看看。”这真是不速之客了！朱老大不知他要看哪个？想想哪个也跟他没有渊源，这件事倒着实猜它不透。于是匆匆出厅接见，彼此熟人，见面不用寒暄，直问来意。一问才知道他要看的是胡雪岩。章老板是从那些向刘不才讨彩的闲汉口中，得知胡雪岩用心仁厚，特意将刘不才那盏“灯笼”拿走，解了赌场的一个大厄。因而专诚拜访，一则道谢，二则想交个朋友。“这位胡大叔，是我师父的朋友，还有点干亲，为人四海得很，道谢不必，交朋友一定可以。不过，“朱老大说：”你这四样礼，大可省省。““我也晓得，几样吃食东西，不成敬意，不过空手上门，不好意思。”章老板也觉得这四样水礼送得不妥，如果说是谢礼，反倒象轻看胡雪岩的一番意思，所以踌躇了一下说：“这样吧，你不必跟胡先生说起。不过，东西带都带来了，再拿回去也麻烦，你就丢在厨房里好了。”“这倒也是句话。来，来，我带你进去。”

They walked leisurely back to the Zhu house. Its finest accommodation was a pavilion overlooking the water. When the guests arrived, some women relatives of the family had been staying there, but it had now been vacated, and Zhu invited Hu and the others inside to rest. They had scarcely sat down when an old servant called softly from the doorway, “Eldest Young Master!” With a glance he drew Zhu outside and whispered, “Mr Zhang from the gambling house is here. He says he wants to see one of our guests, and he has brought four kinds of gifts. Will you come and see him?” This was an unexpected visitor indeed. Zhu could not imagine whom he wanted to see: none of them had any previous connection with him. Puzzled, he hurried to the reception hall. Since the two knew each other, there was no need for preliminaries, and Zhu asked his purpose at once. It emerged that Zhang wanted to see Hu. From the idlers who had solicited gifts from Liu, he had learnt that Hu, out of kindness, had deliberately removed the “lantern” and saved the gambling house from a grave disaster. He had come expressly to offer thanks and make a friend. “Uncle Hu is my master’s friend and related to him by an adopted family tie,” Zhu said. “He is generous to everyone. Thanks are unnecessary, but he will certainly be willing to make a friend. As for these four gifts, however, you really needn’t have brought them.” “I know. A few things to eat are hardly an adequate token, but I felt awkward coming empty-handed.” Zhang too realised that these modest gifts were inappropriate. Offered as payment for a kindness, they might seem to belittle Hu’s intentions. He hesitated, then said, “Do this: don’t mention them to Mr Hu. But since they are here, taking them back would be a nuisance. Just leave them in the kitchen.” “That is sensible. Come along, and I shall take you in.”

一直带到水阁，引见以后，朱老大代为道明来意，胡雪岩对此不虞之誉，谦谢不受。章老板却是一脸诚意，一揖到地，差点就要跪了来。“胡先生，你帮我这个忙帮大了。说实话，”他指着刘不才说：“这位刘三爷也是我在赌上混了二三十年，头一遭遇见的人物。如果刘三爷再玩一会，大家跟着他‘一条边’打‘进门’，我今天非倾家荡产不可！”“怎么呢？”胡雪岩问道：“下面还是出老宝？”“一共出了十六记。说起来，也是一桩新闻。幸好，”章老板仿佛提起来仍有余悸的神情，“只有刘三爷一个人看得透。刘三爷一走，大家都不敢押老宝，通扯起来，庄家还是赢面。”刘不才听见这话，自然面有得色，于是特地笑道：“我也不过怪路怪打，瞎碰瞎撞而已。”“赌就是赌个机会，千载一时的机会，只有刘三爷一个人抓得住。说起来叫人不相信，做手只做了四记老宝，但开出来的是十六记，毛病出在第五记上……”“啊，我想起来了。”刘不才插嘴说，“第五记上，宝盒子老不下来，拉铃拉了三遍才催到。出了什么毛病？”是做手得了暴疾，昏迷在烟榻上。传递宝盒子的小童，不知就里，拼命推他椎不醒，下面铃声催得心慌，便不问青红皂白，将原盒子送了下来。做到十六记上，隐隐听得楼上有哭声，拿钥匙开了楼门，上去一看，那小童因为上下隔绝，呼援无门，越想越害怕，已是面无人色。再看那做手，连身子都凉了。

Zhu led him straight to the waterside pavilion, introduced him and explained why he had come. Hu modestly disclaimed the unexpected praise, but Zhang was utterly sincere. He bowed to the ground and nearly went down on his knees. “Mr Hu, you have done me an enormous service. To tell you the truth”—he pointed to Liu—“in twenty or thirty years of gambling I have never met anyone like Third Master Liu. If he had played a little longer, with everyone following him and backing Entrance on the same side, I would have been ruined today.” “How so?” Hu asked. “Did Old Treasure keep coming up?” “Sixteen times in all. It really is something extraordinary. Fortunately”—Zhang still seemed shaken at the thought—“Third Master Liu was the only one who saw through it. Once he left, no one dared back Old Treasure. Taking the whole session together, the house still came out ahead.” Liu naturally looked pleased. With a deliberate smile he said, “I merely answered a strange pattern with a strange wager and happened to strike lucky.” “Gambling is about seizing an opportunity. When a chance like that comes once in a thousand years, Third Master Liu alone can take it. It sounds incredible, but the setter prepared only four Old Treasures, while sixteen appeared. The trouble began with the fifth.” “Ah, I remember,” Liu interrupted. “The fifth box wouldn’t come down. They rang three times before they got it. What happened?” The setter had been struck by a sudden illness and lost consciousness on his opium couch. The boy who carried the box did not understand. He pushed and shook him but could not wake him. Panicked by the insistent bell below, he simply sent the same box down again. After the sixteenth round, faint crying was heard upstairs. Someone unlocked the staircase door and went up. Cut off from those below and unable to summon help, the boy had grown more and more terrified until his face was drained of colour. As for the setter, his body was already cold.

这是闻所未闻的怪事，连在赌场里混过半辈子的刘不才，都觉得不可思议，在那烽火不惊、平静富足的同里，连张家的母狗哺育了李家的小猫，都会成为谈来津津有味的新闻，对这样一件“死人做宝”的怪事，自然会轰动。所以，就在章老板访胡雪岩的那时刻，茶坊酒肆便到处在谈论。于是朱老大家的两个客人，立即成了同里的风头人物。这件新闻，下午刚到，在酒店里小酌自劳的裘丰言和周一鸣也听到了，两人相视而笑，十分兴奋，裘丰言倒还持重，周一鸣却忍不住了，同时他跟胡雪岩这许多日子，也懂了很多扬名创招牌的花样，于是将胡雪岩和刘不才的身分揭露了出来，道是并非朱老大的朋友，是朱老大的师父，俞武成的朋友。这一下。在大家的心目中，俞武成这个名字，似乎也很响亮了。

It was an unheard-of occurrence. Even Liu, who had spent half his life in gambling houses, found it scarcely believable. In peaceful, prosperous Tongli, untouched by the alarms of war, even the Zhang family’s dog nursing the Li family’s kitten could become an endlessly relished piece of news. A marvel like “a dead man setting the treasure” was bound to cause a sensation. At the very moment Zhang was visiting Hu, teahouses and taverns everywhere were discussing it. The two guests at the Zhu house instantly became the talk of Tongli. Qiu Fengyan and Zhou Yiming, who had arrived that afternoon and were rewarding themselves with a quiet drink in a tavern, heard the story too. They exchanged delighted smiles. Qiu remained restrained, but Zhou could not contain himself. During his time with Hu he had learnt something of the art of building a name. He revealed who Hu and Liu were, explaining that they were not simply Zhu’s friends but friends of Zhu’s master, Yu Wucheng. In everyone’s mind, Yu’s name at once acquired a new resonance as well.
