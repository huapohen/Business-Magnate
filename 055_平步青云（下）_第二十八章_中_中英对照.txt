消息传播得真快，第二天一早，俞武成从青浦回同里，中途在一处村镇歇脚吃茶，便有人向他打听胡雪岩和刘不才。因此，在朱老大家的水阁初见面，他向胡雪岩说的第一句话就是：“老兄一到，名气就响。我们在江湖上混了几十年的，真要甘拜下风了！”这话不是句好话，胡雪岩自然听得出来，只好这样答道：“我们是仰仗大哥的声光。这种毫无道理的风头，不出为妙，所以今天步门不敢出，专诚等候大哥，一切听大哥的吩咐。”宾主之间，一见面便有些格格不入的模样，杨凤毛大为不安，赶紧将俞武成的袖子一拉：“师父！”他轻声说道：“你老请到这面来！”将俞武成拉到一边，杨凤毛将三婆婆如何看重这门干亲，一一细陈，最后极郑重地说：“临走之前，三婆婆特为拿我喊到一边，叫我告诉师父：这位胡大叔是极能干、极讲义气的人。她老人家说：几十年工夫当中，看过的也不少，狠的有，忠厚的也有，象胡大叔这样又狠又忠厚的人，还是第一趟见……”

News travelled remarkably fast. Early the next morning, as Yu Wucheng returned from Qingpu to Tongli, he stopped for tea in a village along the way and was already being asked about Hu Xueyan and Liu Bucai. Thus, when he first met Hu in Eldest Brother Zhu’s waterside pavilion, his opening words were, “You have only just arrived, brother, and already your name is on everyone’s lips. Those of us who have spent decades in the brotherhoods must acknowledge ourselves outdone!” There was an unpleasant edge to this, which Hu naturally detected. He could only reply, “We owe it all to your reputation, Brother Yu. Such pointless attention is better avoided. That is why I have not ventured outside today, but have waited here expressly for you, ready to follow your instructions in everything.” Their first encounter had scarcely begun before host and guest seemed at odds. Alarmed, Yang Fengmao tugged at Yu’s sleeve. “Master,” he said softly, “please come this way.” Drawing him aside, Yang explained in detail how highly Third Granny valued this new bond of adopted kinship. Finally, with particular gravity, he said, “Before I left, Third Granny called me aside especially to give you this message: Uncle Hu is an exceptionally capable and loyal man. She says that in all her decades of experience she has seen plenty of ruthless men and plenty of kindly, honest ones, but this is the first time she has met a man who is both ruthless and kindly.”

“什么？”俞武成说，“我倒不懂她老人家的话，怎么叫又狠又忠厚？”“忠厚是说他的本性，狠是说他办事的手段。”杨凤毛又说：“我倒觉得三婆婆的眼光到底厉害，这‘又狠又忠厚’五个字，别人说不出。”“那么，你说对不对呢？”“自然说得对！”杨凤毛接下来又转述“慈训”：“三婆婆说，我们在这里，寄人篱下，受人的气，也不是办法。想要打开局面，都在胡大叔身上。师父要格外尊敬他！““昨天章老板赌场里又是怎么回事？”“ ”这件事，“杨凤毛的神色显得很兴奋，”师父也有面子！“接着，他将当时的情形，细说了一遍。“这倒难得！说他忠厚不错。”俞武成又说，“那姓刘的，看起来也是‘老白相’，居然对他服服帖帖，这就看得出来，有点本事的。”“本事不止一点点。师父，你老跟他一谈就知道了。”

“What?” Yu asked. “I do not understand her. How can a man be both ruthless and kindly?” “Kindly in his nature, ruthless in his methods. I must say Third Granny’s judgment is extraordinary. No one else could have found those words.” “And do you think she is right?” “Of course she is!” Yang continued to relay the maternal instructions. “Third Granny says it is no way to go on, living here dependent on others and having to swallow their slights. Our chance to improve matters rests entirely with Uncle Hu. You must treat him with particular respect.” “And what happened at Boss Zhang’s gambling house yesterday?” “That,” Yang replied with an excited expression, “brought you credit too, Master!” He recounted the whole affair. “That is unusual. She was right to call him kindly,” Yu said. “That fellow Liu looks like an old hand at the pleasures of life, yet he obeys Hu meekly enough. You can see from that that Hu has some ability.” “More than a little. Once you talk to him, Master, you will see for yourself.”

于是俞武成再跟胡雪岩交谈时，态度就大不相同了，他很客气，一定要让胡雪岩和刘不才“升炕”，而叙起礼节来，刘不才是芙蓉的叔叔，长了一辈，所以称谓亦自各别，俞武成叫胡雪岩“老胡”，叫刘不才则是官称“刘三爷”，刘三爷却又尊称他“俞老”，跟胡雪岩所叫的“大哥”一比，仿佛又矮了一辈。反正江湖上各叙各的，称呼虽乱，其实都是一律平等的朋友。俞武成的门规甚严，杨凤毛、朱老大都是站着服劳，他自己则坐在水阁临窗的一张太师椅上相陪，跟胡雪岩大谈松江漕帮。他称“老太爷”为“松江老大”，说起许多他们年轻时一起闯荡江湖的故事，感叹着日子不如从前好过。刘不才在这场合，只有静听的份儿。一面听，一面打量俞武成，年纪六十开外，打扮得却如纨袴子弟，缎鞋、缎袍、雪白的袖头，不时卷上翻下，等袖子翻下来时，已经盖过手面，所以必得翘起一只大拇指来，将袖口挡住，才便于行动，这原是江湖上人特有的一种姿态，只是俞武成身材魁梧，服装华丽，大拇指一翘起来，那只通体碧绿的“玻璃翠”扳指，异常耀眼，所以格外显得有派头。

When Yu resumed his conversation with Hu, his manner was quite different. He was exceedingly courteous and insisted that Hu and Liu take the places of honour on the raised couch. In reckoning their relationships, Liu, as Furong’s uncle, belonged to the senior generation, so their forms of address differed accordingly. Yu called Hu “Old Hu,” but addressed Liu formally as “Third Master Liu.” Liu in turn respectfully called him “Elder Yu,” which, compared with Hu’s “Brother Yu,” seemed to put Liu a generation below him again. In the brotherhoods everyone reckoned such relationships separately. The titles might be confused, but in practice they were all friends on an equal footing. Yu enforced strict rules among his disciples. Yang and Zhu remained standing to attend to their needs, while he sat in a grand armchair beside the pavilion window, talking at length with Hu about the Songjiang grain-transport brotherhood. He referred to the Old Master as the “Songjiang Chief” and told many stories of their adventures together in their youth, lamenting that life was harder now than it had been. Liu could only sit and listen. As he did so, he studied Yu. Although over sixty, the man dressed like a wealthy young idler, in satin shoes and a satin robe, with snow-white cuffs that he kept turning up and down. When lowered, the cuffs covered his hands, so he had to raise a thumb to hold the cuff back before he could move freely. This was a characteristic gesture among men of the brotherhoods, but Yu’s imposing build and splendid clothes gave it a particular flourish. Whenever his thumb rose, the ring of glass-clear green jade upon it flashed brilliantly, adding still further to his air of distinction.

然而刘不才感觉兴趣，也感到困惑的是，俞武成那件在斜阳里闪闪发光的缎袍，无风自动，不时东面凸起一块，西面蠕动片刻，不知是何缘故！目不转睛地看了半天，总想不透，心便痒得厉害，正忍不住要动问时，谜底揭晓了。朱老大捧了一大冰盘出于太湖中洞庭东山的樱桃来款客，但见俞武成抓了一串在手里，平伸手掌，很快地，袖子里钻出一只毛茸茸的小松鼠来，一对极大、极明亮的眼睛，灵活地转了转，然后拱起两只前爪，就俞武成掌中捧着樱桃咬。刘不才嘻开了嘴笑，“俞老，你真会玩！”他问：“怎么养只松鼠在身上？不觉得累赘？”“养熟了就好了。”“整天在身上？”“嗯！”俞武成点点头，“几乎片刻不离。”“一天到晚，在你身上爬来爬去，不嫌烦吗？”“自然也有睡觉的时候；只要拿它一放到口袋里，它就不闹了。”俞武成又说：“刘三爷喜欢，拿了去玩！”“不，不！”刘不才播着手说：“君子不夺人所好。而且，说实话，在我身上爬来爬去，也嫌肉麻！”

What both fascinated and puzzled Liu, however, was the way Yu’s satin robe, glittering in the slanting sunlight, moved without any breeze. A bulge would appear here, something would wriggle there. What could cause it? He watched intently for a long time without solving the mystery, until his curiosity became almost unbearable. Just as he was about to ask, the answer appeared. Zhu brought in a large platter of cherries from Dongting East Mountain in Lake Tai. Yu took a cluster and held out his open palm. A furry little squirrel promptly emerged from his sleeve. Its large, bright eyes darted about; then it raised its forepaws, grasped a cherry in Yu’s palm, and began nibbling. Liu broke into a broad smile. “Elder Yu, you certainly know how to amuse yourself! Why keep a squirrel on your person? Is it not a nuisance?” “Once it is tame, there is no trouble.” “You keep it there all day?” “Yes.” Yu nodded. “We hardly ever part.” “Does it not bother you, crawling all over you from morning to night?” “It sleeps sometimes, naturally. Put it in my pocket and it settles down. If you like it, Third Master Liu, take it and amuse yourself.” “No, no!” Liu waved his hands. “A gentleman does not deprive another man of something he loves. Besides, to tell the truth, I should find it rather creepy having it crawl all over me.”

俞武成笑笑不响，回头问朱老大：“快开饭了吧？”“听胡大叔跟师父的意思。”朱老大答道，“如果不怎么饿，不妨稍等一等，火腿煨鱼翅，火功还不大够。”“那就等一下。先弄些点心来给胡大叔点饥，等我们谈好了正事，痛痛快快吃酒。”这段话中要紧的是“谈正事”这一句，胡雪岩怕他不愿刘不才与闻机密，便不经意地使个眼色，刘不才会意，站起身来说：“你们谈吧！我趁这会儿工夫，上街去看个朋友。““那么，”朱老大自告奋勇，“我陪着刘三爷一起去。”刘不才是想去看周一鸣，这是暗中埋伏的援兵，不便让俞武成这方面的人知道，所以拱拱手说：“不敢，不敢！你做主人，要留在府上，而且，同里我也熟，绝不致迷路。”这是假话，他也是第一次到同里，只是不如此说，朱老大还会派上引路。果然，做主人的不再客气，放他一个人走了。

Yu smiled without answering, then turned to Zhu. “Will the meal be ready soon?” “Whenever you and Uncle Hu wish, Master. If you are not particularly hungry, it would be better to wait a little. The shark’s fin stewed with ham needs more time over the fire.” “Then let us wait. Bring Uncle Hu some refreshments to take the edge off his hunger. Once we have discussed our business, we can enjoy our wine properly.” The significant words were “discussed our business.” Hu feared that Yu might not wish Liu to hear their secrets, and gave him a casual-looking glance. Liu understood and rose. “You talk. I shall use the time to go into town and see a friend.” “Then I shall accompany you,” Zhu volunteered. Liu intended to see Zhou Yiming, their reserve force concealed from view. He could hardly let Yu’s people know about him. Clasping his hands, he said, “Please do not trouble yourself. You are our host and must stay here. Besides, I know Tongli well; there is no danger of my getting lost.” This was untrue: it was his first visit to Tongli too. Unless he said so, however, Zhu would send someone to guide him. Sure enough, his host pressed the matter no further and let him go alone.

于是，俞武成跟胡雪岩，还有杨凤毛在一起密认。俞武成表示愿意听从胡雪岩的安排，老实相告，原来准备动那船洋枪的人马，都由周立春手下一个得力的头目“跷脚长根”安排。所要借重俞武成的，是因为这条水路，是松江漕帮的势力范围，必须请他出面，来打通“松江老大”的路子。现在松江方面，由于守着“两方面都是朋友，只好袖手中立”的立场，所以“跷脚长根”也踌躇着不敢下手。如今得有这样一条出路，深符所愿，但条件如何？必得跟胡雪岩谈一谈。“那当然。”胡雪岩问道，“怎么样跟这位朋友碰头？”“那还得再联络。老胡，我是直心直肚肠，”俞武成很郑重地说：“有句话我想先请教你，你是一家人了，而且我老娘的眼光是不会错的，我当然相信。不过，那批做官的，我吃过他们的苦头，实在不大相信。当初我儿子要去考武举，我就跟他说：”做官也没啥意思，不要去考。‘也是我老娘’望孙成龙‘亲自料理，亲自送考。至于招抚这一节，我是无所谓的，办成功了，帮里弟兄，可以去吃一份粮，也算是糊口，再说，拿他们拉过来，也总算是替朝廷出了力。就怕那批做官的老爷，口是心非，等出了毛病，我怪你也无用，那时候，我就不是在江湖上好混不好混的事了！“

Yu, Hu, and Yang then conferred in private. Yu said that he was willing to follow Hu’s arrangements and explained the position frankly. The men who had been preparing to seize the boatload of foreign guns were all under the direction of Lame Changgen, one of Zhou Lichun’s capable subordinate leaders. They needed Yu because this water route lay within the Songjiang brotherhood’s sphere of influence: he had to approach the Songjiang Chief and clear the way. Now Songjiang had adopted the position that, since both sides were friends, it could only stand aside and remain neutral. Changgen therefore hesitated to act. The proposed way out suited him very well, but he would have to discuss the terms with Hu. “Naturally,” Hu said. “How can I meet this friend?” “That still has to be arranged. Old Hu, I am a plain-spoken man.” Yu’s tone became solemn. “There is something I want to ask you first. You are family now, and my mother’s judgment is never wrong, so of course I trust you. But I have suffered at the hands of those officials, and cannot put much faith in them. When my son first wanted to take the provincial military examination, I told him, ‘There is nothing so wonderful about being an official. Do not bother with it.’ But my mother wanted her grandson to make a name for himself; she arranged everything and escorted him to the examination herself. As for this amnesty, it makes no difference to me personally. If it succeeds, the men can draw military pay and at least feed themselves. Bringing them over would also be a service to the court. What I fear is that those grand officials will say one thing and mean another. If something goes wrong, blaming you will be no use. And for me it will then be more than a question of whether I can still get along in the brotherhoods!”

听他这夹枪带棒一大顿，胡雪岩相当困惑，不知他说的什么？只是抓住“出了毛病”这四个字极力思考，慢慢悟出道理来了。“你是说，人过去以后，当官儿的，翻脸不认人，是不是？”“对了！”俞武成说，“光是翻脸不认人，还好办，就怕……”他摇摇头，“真的有那么一下子，那就惨了。”“你是说……”胡雪岩很吃力地问：“会‘杀降’？”“保不定的。”“不会！”这时候胡雪岩才用斩钉截铁的声音：“我包你不会，大哥，我跟你实说吧，我接头的是何学使的路子，他马上要放好缺了。京里大军机是他们同年，各省巡抚也有许多是他同年。这一榜红得很，说出话来有分量的。”“那么，何学使跟你的交情呢？”“何学使托我替他置妾。交情如此而已！”“那就没话说了。”俞武成欣然问道，“何学使可曾谈起，给点啥好处？”他赶紧又补了一句，“不是说我。是说对跷脚长根他们。”

This long speech, with its barbed insinuations, left Hu rather puzzled. What exactly did Yu mean? He seized on the phrase “something goes wrong” and thought hard until the meaning gradually became clear. “You mean that once the men have gone over, the officials might turn on them and disown their promises?” “Exactly! If they merely turned their backs on them, we could still manage. What I fear is—” Yu shook his head. “If they really did that, it would be terrible.” “Do you mean”—Hu found the question difficult to utter—“that they might kill the men who surrender?” “There is no telling.” “They will not!” Only now did Hu speak with absolute firmness. “I guarantee they will not. Brother, let me tell you plainly: my contact is Education Commissioner He. He is about to receive an excellent appointment. The leading grand councillor in the capital graduated in the same examination year as he did, and so did many provincial governors. His year is very much in favour, and their words carry weight.” “And how close are you to Commissioner He?” “He has asked me to find him a concubine. That is all there is to our friendship!” “Then there is nothing more to say.” Yu looked pleased. “Did he mention what advantages he could offer?” He hurriedly added, “I do not mean for me. I mean for Changgen and his men.”

“提到这一层，就我不说，大哥也想象得到：弃暗投明，朝廷自然有一番奖励，官是一定有得做的。”接下来，胡雪岩便根据何桂清的指示说道：“弟兄们总可以关一个月恩饷，作为犒赏。以后看拔到哪里，归哪里的粮台发饷。本来，一个月的恩饷好象少了点，不过也实在叫没法子，地方失得太多，钱粮少收不少，这些情形，大哥你当然清楚。”俞武成当然清楚，他自己和这一帮无事可做，便是朝廷岁入减少的明证，所以点点头表示领会，“恩晌不恩饷，倒不在话下，照跷脚长根的意思，将来投过去，变成官兵，驻扎的地方要随他挑，说老实话，也就是仍；日想驻扎在这一带。这一点，”俞武成很难出口似地，“总要把它做到！”胡雪岩对这方面虽不在行，但照情理而论，觉得不容易做到，他略想一想问道：“那么我倒请问大哥，如果叫他去打小刀会，他肯不肯？”“还不肯的。原来是一条跳板上的人，怎么好意思？”“这样子就难了！”胡雪岩说，“这一带驻了兵，都是要打小刀会的。军情紧急，一道命令下来，就要开拔，如果不肯出队，就是不服调度。大哥，你想想看，你做了长官，会怎么样处置？““我倒没有想到这一层……”俞武成搔搔头皮，显得很为难似的。

“On that point, brother, you can imagine the answer without my telling you. If they abandon darkness for the light, the court will naturally reward them. Official appointments will certainly be available.” Hu went on, following He Guiqing’s instructions. “The men should at least receive a special gratuity equal to a month’s pay. After that, their pay will come from the military finance office wherever they are assigned. A single month’s gratuity may seem a little meagre, but there is really no help for it. So much territory has been lost that tax receipts have fallen greatly. You know the circumstances as well as anyone.” Yu certainly did: the fact that he and his followers had nothing to do was itself evidence of the decline in imperial revenue. He nodded. “The gratuity is not the main thing. Changgen wants to choose where his men are stationed after they become government troops. To be frank, he wants to remain in this district. That is something”—he seemed to find it difficult to say—“we really must arrange.” Hu was no expert in such matters, but common sense told him this would be difficult. After a moment’s thought he asked, “Then let me ask you, brother: if he were ordered to fight the Small Swords Society, would he agree?” “No. They have all stood on the same gangplank. How could he bring himself to do it?” “Then we have a difficulty. The troops stationed here are all intended to fight the Small Swords Society. If the military situation became urgent, a single order would require them to march. Refusing to go would be disobedience. Imagine you were their commanding officer. How would you deal with it?” “I had not thought of that.” Yu scratched his head, looking deeply troubled.

胡雪岩看得出来，俞武成大概已拍了胸脯，满口应承，必可做到，所以才有此着急的神情。正在替他伤脑筋时，杨凤毛已先开了口。“师父只有这样回复他，还是调得远些的好，本乡本土，如果小刀会不体谅他的处境，或者事急相投，拒而不纳，就伤了感情，要帮忙呢，窝藏叛逆的罪名，非同小可。何不远离了左右为难的窘境？”“这话说得透彻。”胡雪岩趁机劝道：“大哥，你就照此回复，跷脚长根如果明道理、讲道理，一定不会再提什么人家做不到的要求。”这两个人一说，俞武成释然了，“今天就谈到这里。”他站起身来，“我想，大致可以谈得拢了。我们吃饭吧！”

Hu could see that Yu had probably thumped his chest and promised without reservation that this could be arranged; hence his present anxiety. While Hu was still puzzling over a way to help him, Yang spoke. “Master, you must tell him it would be better to accept a posting farther away. If he remains in his home district, the Small Swords men may fail to understand his position. Or they may come to him for refuge in an emergency. If he turns them away, he will hurt their feelings; if he helps them, harbouring rebels is no trifling charge. Why not move away and escape that dilemma?” “That puts it very clearly,” Hu said, taking the opportunity to persuade him. “Give him that answer, brother. If Changgen understands reason and will listen to it, he is sure to stop making demands that others cannot fulfil.” Their arguments relieved Yu. “That will do for today.” He rose. “I think we should be able to reach agreement on the main points. Let us eat.”

开席要等刘不才，而刘不才迟迟不回，于是一面先用些点心，一面闲谈坐等。等到天黑净了，才见刘不才赶回来，进门向主人道歉，却偷空向胡雪岩使了个眼色，暗示着周一鸣那里有了什么花样。胡雪岩声色不动。席间谈笑风生，跟俞武成无所不谈，散了席又喝茶，有意无意打个呵欠，朱老大便提议让客人休息，送入客房，各道安置。胡雪岩和刘不才各住一间屋，但有门相通，为了慎重，他先看清了没有朱家的人住在临近，才招招手将刘不才邀了过来，细问究竟。“老周在这一带很熟，水路上到处有朋友，据他听到的消息，俞老头的处境，相当窘迫。不知道他自己跟你谈了没有？”“略为谈了些。却不是什么‘窘迫’。”胡雪岩问：“老周怎么说？”“老周是这么说，他听人谈起，这一带是松江漕帮的势力，也很有人知道你跟尤五的交情，所以‘松江老大’一说退出，名为中立，在旁人看，就是不管俞老头的事了。江湖上虽重义气，但也要是熟人才行，俞老头的地盘都丢掉了，在这里是靠松江老大的牌头，松江老大一不管，就没有人买他的帐了。“

They had to wait for Liu before beginning the banquet, but he was slow to return. They took some refreshments and sat talking until it was quite dark, when he finally hurried in. He apologized to his host, but found a moment to signal to Hu that something had developed with Zhou. Hu’s face and manner betrayed nothing. At dinner he talked and laughed freely with Yu about every imaginable subject. Afterwards they drank tea. Hu gave a yawn that might or might not have been deliberate, and Zhu suggested that the guests rest. He escorted them to their rooms, and they exchanged goodnights. Hu and Liu had separate rooms connected by a door. As a precaution, Hu first made sure that no member of Zhu’s household was staying nearby, then beckoned Liu in and asked for the details. “Old Zhou knows this district well and has friends everywhere along the waterways. From what he has heard, old Yu is in a very awkward position. Has he said anything to you about it?” “A little, but nothing about being in an ‘awkward position.’ What does Zhou say?” “He has heard that this is the Songjiang brotherhood’s territory. Many people know about your friendship with You Laowu. So when the Songjiang Chief announced that he was withdrawing, it might have been called neutrality, but everyone else took it to mean that he was no longer backing old Yu. Loyalty matters in the brotherhoods, but it depends on personal acquaintance. Yu has lost all his own territory and relies here on the Songjiang Chief’s name. Once the Chief stops backing him, nobody pays him any heed.”

胡雪岩拿这些话跟俞武成自己的情形，合作一起来想，觉得周一鸣所得到的消息，相当可靠。照目前的情形看，俞武成确在窘境之中，成事不能，败事不足，变成无足轻重的人物，如果说他还有什么作用，无非是他身上，还维系着跷脚长根这条线索而已！“我看，你也犯不着这么敷衍俞老头。”刘不才说，“我看他跟药渣子一样，过气无用了。”“话不是这么说。既然交了朋友，也不便太过于势利。”“朋友是朋友，办正事是办正事。他已经没得用了，你还跟他搅在一起做什么？”“不！”胡雪岩还不想跟他说跷脚长根的事，只这样答道：“我要从他身上牵出一个要紧人来！所以还要跟他合作。”“你跟他合作是你的事，不过，你要想想人家会不会跟他合作呢？”这句话提醒了胡雪岩，心里在想：是啊！跷脚长根当然也已晓得，俞武成的行情大跌，然则是不是会象自己一样，跟他推心置腹，就大成疑问。说不定周一鸣所说的“没有人买他的帐”，正就是跷脚长根那面的人。念头转到这里，觉得自己布下周一鸣这支伏兵的做法，还真是一步少不得的棋。于是他将俞武成跟他密谈商定，要与跷脚长根见一次面的话，都悄悄说了给刘不才听，然后嘱咐他第二天一早，再去看周一鸣，托他找水路上的朋友，好好去摸一摸跷脚长根的底，看看俞武成跟他的关系如何？

Comparing this account with what he had seen of Yu’s circumstances, Hu found Zhou’s information quite credible. Yu was indeed in a predicament. He lacked the power either to bring anything about or to prevent it, and had become a man of little consequence. If he retained any usefulness, it lay in the thread that still connected him with Changgen. “I do not see why you need to humour old Yu so much,” Liu said. “He is like the dregs left after brewing medicine: his strength is spent and he is no use.” “That is not the way to put it. Once we have made a friend, we cannot be too calculating.” “Friendship is one thing; serious business is another. If he is no use any more, why keep yourself entangled with him?” “No.” Hu was not yet ready to tell him about Changgen. “I need him to lead me to someone important. That is why I must still work with him.” “Whether you work with him is your affair. But have you considered whether the other man will?” That brought Hu up short. Yes: Changgen must also know how sharply Yu’s standing had fallen. Whether he would trust Yu as openly as Hu did was therefore very doubtful. Perhaps the people Zhou meant when he said that nobody paid Yu any heed were precisely Changgen’s men. The thought convinced Hu that placing Zhou in reserve had been an indispensable move. He quietly told Liu about his private discussion with Yu and their agreement to arrange a meeting with Changgen. Then he instructed Liu to see Zhou again early the next morning and ask him to use his friends along the waterways to investigate Changgen thoroughly, particularly his relationship with Yu.

到了第二天早晨，刘不才依旧托词看朋友，一个人溜了出去，胡雪岩则由杨凤毛和朱老大相陪吃早茶，说俞武成一清早有事出去了，到午后才能回来。胡雪岩心里有数，是安排他跟跷脚长根的约会去了。到得吃过午饭，胡雪岩深感无聊，正想利用这段闲工夫，去打听打听丝市，刘不才匆匆赶了回来，一见胡雪岩便悄悄招手，拉到僻处，压低声音问道：“俞老头回来了没有？”“你怎么知道俞老头出去了？”“你先不必问。”“还没有回来！”“还好，还好，真是命中该救。”“咦！”胡雪岩大吃一惊，“你怎么说？”“周一鸣真得力。打听来的消息，说出来要吓你一跳。跷脚长根摆下了‘鸿门宴’，不但你，连俞老头都要陷在里面。”“这……”胡雪岩定定神先想一想，然后沉着地问：“你慢慢儿说，是怎么回事？”

The next morning Liu again slipped out alone on the pretext of visiting a friend. Hu took morning tea with Yang and Zhu. They said that Yu had gone out early on business and would not return until the afternoon. Hu understood that he had gone to arrange the meeting with Changgen. After lunch, thoroughly bored, Hu was thinking of using the spare time to inquire into the silk market when Liu hurried back. The moment he saw Hu, he beckoned discreetly, drew him into a secluded spot, and asked in a low voice, “Has old Yu returned?” “How did you know he had gone out?” “Never mind that for the moment.” “He has not come back yet.” “Good, good! You were clearly destined to be saved.” “What?” Hu was badly startled. “What do you mean?” “Zhou Yiming is a capable fellow. What he has discovered will give you a shock. Changgen is preparing a Hongmen banquet—a feast that conceals a trap. It is meant to catch not only you, but old Yu as well.” “This—” Hu steadied himself and thought for a moment, then asked calmly, “Take your time. What has happened?”

据周一鸣打听来的消息是如此，跷脚长根听说“松江老大”变了卦，俞武成又谈什么招安，疑心他要出卖朋友，因而一不做，二不休，决定连俞武成一起下手，预备绑架勒索，条件就是那一船洋枪。跷脚长很的打算是，请俞武成跟胡雪岩到他家会面，一入牢笼，移换密处，等所欲既偿，便带着那船洋枪，投奔洪杨。而且还怕胡雪岩不敢深入虎穴，预备了第二处地方，是同里闹市中的一家“私门头”，内中有一双坠溷的姊妹花，妹妹叫妙珠，姐姐叫妙珍，是跷脚长根的禁脔。她家跟朱老大家一样，开出后门，就是河埠，半夜里绑架落船，人不知，鬼不觉。这消息太可惊了，但也太可疑了，胡雪岩实在不能相信，因为这样做法，在江湖上来说，是异常“伤道”的，跷脚长根纠有此心，部署一定异常机密，如何轻易能让周一鸣打听得到？“我也是这么想。”听胡雪岩提出疑问以后，刘不才这样答道，“但老周说得斩钉截铁，消息万分可靠。他又说，这也是无意中遇到一个知道内幕的人，他承认事情太巧，说是你鸿运当头，才有这种逢凶化吉的机遇。”“那好！这一试就试出来了。你说，那私门头姐妹叫什么名字？”“妙珍，妙珠。”

According to Zhou’s information, Changgen had heard that the Songjiang Chief had changed his mind and that Yu was talking about an amnesty. Suspecting Yu of intending to betray his friends, he had decided to carry matters through without half measures and seize Yu as well. He planned to abduct them and demand the boatload of foreign guns as ransom. His scheme was to invite Yu and Hu to his house for a meeting, trap them there, and move them to a secret place. Once he had obtained what he wanted, he would take the guns and join the Taipings. Fearing that Hu might refuse to venture so far into the tiger’s den, he had also prepared an alternative venue: a private brothel in the busy part of Tongli. Two sisters who had fallen into prostitution lived there, the younger named Miaozhu and the elder Miaozhen. Miaozhen was Changgen’s exclusive mistress. Like Zhu’s house, theirs had a landing just outside the back door. The captives could be bundled into a boat in the middle of the night without anyone knowing. The report was astonishing, but also highly doubtful. Hu could scarcely believe it. Such conduct would be a grave violation of the brotherhoods’ code. If Changgen intended it, his preparations must be extremely secret. How could Zhou have discovered them so easily? “I thought the same,” Liu replied when Hu raised the question. “But Old Zhou is adamant that the information is completely reliable. He says he happened to meet someone who knew the inside story. He admits it was an extraordinary coincidence, but says your luck is running high: that is why you have this chance to escape disaster.” “Very well. We can test it easily enough. What did you say the sisters were called?” “Miaozhen and Miaozhu.”

胡雪岩点点头，四面一望，窗前就是书桌，有副笔砚，砚台尘封，墨剩了半段，拔出笔架上的笔来看，笔锋已秃，这都只得将就了，他亲自倒了点茶汁在砚台中，一面磨墨，一面招手将刘不才唤到跟前，低声说过：“你随便找张纸，替我写下来，写一句话好了：不在长根家，就在妙珍家。”说着，他走到门外去替刘不才“望风”。急切间就是找不到纸，情急智生，刘不才将一方雪白的杭纺手绢，铺在桌上，提笔写了那十个字，然后折了起来，交到胡雪岩手里，他很慎重地藏在贴肉小褂子的口袋里。这一来，胡雪岩就改了主意，托词想睡午觉，把自己关在屋子里，筹划应付可能会有的这一番意外变化，刘不才则在主人的安排下，上了牌桌。到了四点多钟吃点心的时候，俞武成回来了，一来便问胡雪岩。他倒是真的睡着了，为朱老大唤醒，请到水阁跟俞武成见面。

Hu nodded and looked around. A writing desk stood by the window, with a brush and inkstone. The inkstone was covered in dust, only half an inkstick remained, and the brush he took from its rack had a worn-out point. They would have to make do. He poured a little tea into the inkstone himself, began grinding the ink, and beckoned Liu closer. “Find any scrap of paper,” he said softly, “and write this for me. Just one sentence: ‘If not at Changgen’s, then at Miaozhen’s.’” He went outside to keep watch. In his haste Liu could find no paper. Necessity supplied an idea: he spread a snow-white Hangzhou silk handkerchief on the table, wrote the ten characters, folded it, and handed it to Hu, who carefully hid it in the pocket of his underjacket next to his skin. Hu now changed his plans. Claiming that he wanted an afternoon nap, he shut himself in his room to consider how to meet this possible and unexpected turn of events. Liu meanwhile joined the gaming table arranged by their host. Shortly after four, when refreshments were being served, Yu returned and immediately asked for Hu. By then Hu had actually fallen asleep. Zhu woke him and invited him to the waterside pavilion to meet Yu.

“我去看了跷脚长根，他听说你来了，很高兴，明天晚上替你接风，详谈一切。”俞武成说，“我把你的话都告诉了他，他也很体谅，藩库已不比从前，一个月的恩饷，对弟兄也总算有了交代。”俞武成说得很起劲，胡雪岩却显得相当冷淡，平静地问道：“他预备请我在哪里吃饭？”“主随客便！”俞武成说，“如果你不嫌路远，就到他那里，他住在平望，说远也不远。不然，就在同里，他有个老相好是这里出名的私门头，名叫？”他敲敲自己的额角，“这两年的记性坏了，怎么一下子就想不起？”“是不是叫妙珍？”“妙珍，妙珍！”俞武成一叠连声地：“老胡，你怎么知道？”“大哥！”胡雪岩用极冷静的声音答道：“我给你看样东西。”不用说，就是刘不才的那块杭纺手绢，展开来铺在桌上，潦潦草草十个大字：“不在长根家，就在妙珍家。”“老胡，”俞武成疑云满面，“这，这是啥讲究？”胡雪岩不答他的话，只顾自己说：“大哥，今天我们同船合命，有哈话你无论如何不能瞒我！”看他面色凝重，俞武成便知内中大有文章，而且事机可能非常急迫，于是拉着他的膀子说：“来，来！到我房间里去谈。”

“I have seen Changgen. He was delighted to hear you had come, and wants to give you a welcoming dinner tomorrow evening, when you can discuss everything in detail,” Yu said. “I told him all you said. He quite understands that the provincial treasury is not what it once was. A month’s gratuity will at least give the men something to show for it.” Yu spoke enthusiastically, but Hu was noticeably cool. He asked calmly, “Where does he intend to give me dinner?” “Wherever suits the guest. If you do not mind the journey, you can go to his house. He lives at Pingwang, which is not really so far away. Otherwise, here in Tongli. He has a long-standing mistress here, a well-known private courtesan. Her name is—” He tapped his forehead. “My memory has grown poor these last two years. Why has it suddenly escaped me?” “Is it Miaozhen?” “Miaozhen, yes, Miaozhen! Old Hu, how did you know?” “Brother,” Hu answered in an exceptionally cool voice, “I shall show you something.” It was, of course, Liu’s silk handkerchief. Hu spread it on the table, revealing the ten large, hastily written characters: “If not at Changgen’s, then at Miaozhen’s.” Yu’s face clouded with suspicion. “Old Hu, what does this mean?” Hu did not answer, but continued with his own point. “Brother, we are in the same boat now, with our lives bound together. Whatever you know, you must not conceal it from me.” His grave expression told Yu that there was much behind this, and that the situation might be urgent. Taking Hu’s arm, he said, “Come. Let us talk in my room.”

朱老大为他师父预备的住处，不但讲究，而且严密，是个花木扶疏的小院落，北面三间平房，俞武成往在最里面那一间，引客入内，在一张临窗的红木小圆桌旁边坐下，脸朝着外，窗外若是有人经过，绝逃不脱他的视、其实这是顾虑，从开始筹划要动那票洋枪开始，这三间精舍，便成了禁地，除却朱老大和杨凤毛以外，什么人都不敢擅自入内的。“老胡，我想你一定另外有路子！”俞武成说，“既然你说同船合命，你那边如果另有打算，也不要瞒我。”真是“光棍眼，赛夹剪”，一下就看出端倪来了，胡雪岩自然不肯再隐瞒，“另外打算是没有，另外有路子，倒是真的。不过这条路，来得也意外，回头我当然一五一十都要告诉大哥你听。”他停了一下说：“我先请问大哥一句话，跷脚长根为人怎么样？跟大哥的交情够不够？”“要说他为人，向来是有心计的，外号‘赛吴用’，至于跟我的交情，那就难说了。”“怎么呢？”“我跟他本人交情不算深，不过，他的‘前人’跟我一辈，叫做‘金毛狗炳奎’。我救过金毛狗的性命，这话一时也说不清楚。”俞武成紧接着说：“长根是金毛狗最喜欢的一个徒弟，金毛狗临死的时候，关照徒弟：俞某人的恩，我今生是无法报答了！将来你们见了他，就当见了我一样。等他的徒弟点头答应了，金毛狗才咽的气。所以他的徒弟都叫我俞师父，长根也就是为此，才来找我帮忙。”

The quarters Zhu had prepared for his master were not only elegant but secure: a little courtyard thick with flowers and trees, with three single-storey rooms along its northern side. Yu occupied the innermost room. He led Hu inside and sat beside a small round rosewood table at the window, facing outwards so that nobody passing could escape his notice. The precaution was really unnecessary. Ever since the plot to seize the foreign guns had begun, these three fine rooms had been forbidden ground. Apart from Zhu and Yang, nobody dared enter without permission. “Old Hu, I imagine you must have another channel of information,” Yu said. “Since you say our lives are bound together, do not keep it from me if your side has other plans.” A worldly man’s eyes were sharper than shears: he had seen the clue at once. Hu naturally concealed it no longer. “We have no other plans. Another channel of information, yes. But that too came about unexpectedly. I shall tell you every detail presently.” He paused. “First let me ask you something. What sort of man is Changgen? Are his ties with you strong enough?” “He has always been a schemer. His nickname is ‘Outdoing Wu Yong.’ As for his ties with me, that is harder to say.” “Why?” “I am not particularly close to him personally, but his master belonged to my generation. He was called Bingkui, the Golden-haired Dog. I once saved his life; that is too long a story to tell now. Changgen was one of his favourite disciples. On his deathbed, the Golden-haired Dog told his disciples, ‘I cannot repay my debt to Yu in this life. Whenever you see him in future, you must treat him as you would me.’ Only after they nodded their assent did he breathe his last. That is why his disciples all call me Master Yu, and why Changgen came to me for help.”

“这样说，此人就是‘欺师灭祖’了！”听这一说，俞武成骇然，这四个字是他们帮中极严重的恶行，犯者“三刀六洞”，决不容情，所以俞武成神情紧张，一时竟无法开口了。“大哥，你大概不大相信？”“是的。”俞武成慢慢点着头，“跷脚长根脚一跷就是一个主意，我也不相信他是什么好人。不过，老胡，江湖上不讲义气，也要讲利害，他做了‘初一’，不怕我做‘初二’？”“你做初一，我做初二”，是与“君子报仇，三年不晚”大同小异的说法。大同者有仇必报，小异者时间不同，一个是“三年不晚”，一个是初一吃了亏，初二就要找场。俞武成的话问得自然有道理，不过胡雪岩也可以解释，诚如他自己所说的，“不讲义气，讲利害”，跷脚长根认为俞武成已经失势，“虎落平阳被犬欺”，无足为奇，只是这知不便直说，怕俞武成听了伤心。“大哥的话是不错。”他这样答道：“跷脚长根已经预备逃到那方面去了，当然不怕大哥做初二。”“逃得了和尚，逃不了庙……”“跟他算帐是以后的事。”胡雪岩有些着急，抢着开口，将话题拉了回来，“我们先谈眼前，这消息宁可信其有，不可信其无。”

“Then this man is betraying his master and dishonouring his forebears!” Yu was appalled. Within their brotherhood, that was an accusation of the gravest misconduct. An offender faced the punishment of “three knife thrusts, six holes,” without mercy. Yu grew so tense that for a moment he could not speak. “You probably find it hard to believe, brother?” “Yes.” Yu nodded slowly. “Every time Changgen lifts his lame leg, he has another scheme. I do not suppose he is a good man. But even if a man ignores loyalty, he must consider the consequences. If he acts on the first day, does he not fear what I may do on the second?” “You act on the first, and I shall act on the second” was much like “Three years is not too late for a gentleman to take revenge.” Both promised retaliation; the difference lay in the timing. One allowed three years, while the other demanded redress the very day after an injury. Yu’s question was reasonable, but Hu could answer it. As Yu himself had said, a man who ignored loyalty would still consider advantage and danger. Changgen believed that Yu had lost his power. A tiger brought down to the plains might be bullied by dogs; there was nothing surprising about it. Hu could not put that bluntly, however, for fear of hurting him. “You are right, brother,” he replied. “But Changgen is already preparing to flee to the other side. Naturally, he is not afraid of your acting on the second day.” “The monk may escape, but his temple cannot—” “Settling accounts with him comes later.” Growing impatient, Hu interrupted and brought him back to the point. “First we must deal with what is before us. It is safer to believe this warning than to dismiss it.”

俞武成摇摇头，“不是什么信不信！要弄清楚，这个消息真不真？”他抬头逼视着胡雪岩问：“你这个消息哪里来的？”“有个姓周的湖南人，从前在水师衙门做过事，水路上的情形很熟悉，是他得来的消息。”“能不能请来见个面？”“当然可以。我托刘三爷去找他。”于是将刘水才从牌桌子上拉了下来，胡雪岩当着俞武成的面，把任务告诉了他，特意说明是俞武成要跟周一鸣见面。这是个暗示，周一鸣一定会想得到是怎么回事，该当如何答复，便好早作准备。在等待的工夫中，俞武成将杨凤毛、朱老大都找了来，关门密议，宣布了周一鸣所得来的消息，杨凤毛跟朱老大的看法不同，一个信以为真，一个说靠不住。说靠不住的是朱老大，他的理由是，妙珍、妙珠这双姐妹的香巢每日户限为穿，人来人在不知有多少，众目昭彰之下，根本不能干那种绑架的事。而且，她家后门那段河面，离码头不远，整夜有船只来往，要想悄悄将俞武成、胡雪岩弄上船，运出水关，也不是轻而易举的。

Yu shook his head. “It is not a question of belief. We must establish whether the information is true.” He looked up and fixed Hu with a searching gaze. “Where did you hear it?” “From a Hunan man named Zhou. He used to work at a naval yamen and knows the waterways well. He obtained the information.” “Can you bring him here for us to meet?” “Of course. I shall ask Third Master Liu to fetch him.” Liu was summoned from the gaming table. In Yu’s presence Hu explained the errand, taking particular care to say that it was Yu who wanted to meet Zhou. This was a hint. Zhou would surely understand what had happened and could prepare his answers in advance. While they waited, Yu sent for Yang and Zhu, closed the door, and told them what Zhou had discovered. They disagreed: Yang believed the report, while Zhu thought it unreliable. Zhu argued that so many people visited the sisters Miaozhen and Miaozhu every day that their threshold was almost worn through. With all those eyes about, such an abduction would be impossible. Besides, the river behind their house was close to the wharf, with boats coming and going throughout the night. Quietly putting Yu and Hu aboard a boat and carrying them out through the water gate would be no easy matter.

“你是小开出身，没有经过这种花样。”杨凤毛平静地驳他，“只要他起了这种心思，办法多得很。说实话，跷脚长根这个人，照我看就是魏延，脑有反骨。事情有七、八分是真的，幸亏周朋友的消息得来得早，我们还好想法子防备，不过，也难！”“怎么呢？”俞武成说，“你说出来，向胡大叔讨教。”“胡大叔！”杨凤毛问道：“你老看，是软做，还是硬做？”“怎么叫软做？”“软做是当场戳穿他的把戏，劝他不要这样子做！”“不好，不好！”俞武成大摇其头，“这样子软法，越让他看得我们不值钱。而且他真的敢这样做，就是生了一副狼心狗肺，你跟他说人话，他哪里会听？”“这话说得是。软做怕没有用。”胡雪岩又说，“不过硬做要做得漂亮。最要紧的是，先把证据抓在手里。““着啊！”杨凤毛拍看大腿说，“胡大叔的话，一滴水落在油瓶里，再准不过。硬做的办法很多，就是要看证据说话。”“怎么样抓证据，我们回头再说。”俞武成问：“你先说，硬做有几个做法？”杨凤毛很奇怪地，却又踌躇不语，他师父连连催问，才将他的话逼出来：“我的办法不妥当！”为来为去是为了证据，照杨凤毛的设计，俞武成和胡雪岩要先入牢宠再设法跳出来，才可以抓得住跷脚长根犯罪的真凭实据。万一配合得不凑手，跳不出来，反激起长根的杀机，那就神仙都难救了。

“You were brought up as a rich man’s son. You have never experienced this sort of trick,” Yang countered calmly. “Once he sets his mind on it, there are any number of ways. To tell the truth, I see Changgen as another Wei Yan, with the traitor’s bone in his skull. The story is seven or eight parts true out of ten. Fortunately, our friend Zhou brought us the news early, so we still have time to take precautions. Even so, it will be difficult.” “Why?” Yu asked. “Say what you mean and ask Uncle Hu’s advice.” “Uncle Hu, do you think we should take a gentle approach or a forceful one?” “What do you mean by a gentle approach?” “Expose his trick when we meet and persuade him not to proceed.” “No, no!” Yu shook his head vigorously. “That sort of softness will only make him despise us. If he really dares do this, he has the heart of a wolf and the guts of a dog. Why would he listen to human reason?” “That is true. A gentle approach may be useless,” Hu said. “But if we use force, it must be done properly. The first and most important thing is to get the evidence into our hands.” “Exactly!” Yang slapped his thigh. “Uncle Hu has put the drop straight into the oil bottle. Nothing could be more precise. There are plenty of ways to act forcefully, but everything depends on the evidence.” “We can discuss how to obtain evidence later,” Yu said. “First tell us what forceful measures you propose.” Strangely, Yang hesitated and fell silent. Only after repeated urging from his master did he admit, “My plan will not do.” It all came back to evidence. His scheme required Yu and Hu to enter the trap first and then find a way out, thereby obtaining solid proof of Changgen’s crime. If their coordination failed, they might be unable to escape and might instead provoke Changgen into killing them. Then even an immortal could not save them.

相谈尚无结论，刘不才却陪着周一鸣到了，他在胡雪岩面前，身分低一等，但对俞武成师弟而言，却同样是朋友，而且有了那个消息，等于已嘉惠俞武成，所以他们师弟对他很客气，着买敷衍了一阵，才谈到正题。话当然要由胡雪岩来问：“老周，你那个消息，很有点道理。不过其中也不能说没有疑问。这件事关系太大，非要弄清楚不可。这消息是怎么来的，你能不能讲出来听听？”如果光是胡雪岩一个人私下问他，他自然据实而言，但有初会面的俞武成师徒在，不免有所顾忌。俞武成看出端倪，但作了很诚恳的表示：“周老兄，你尽管说，我们这面，决不会泄漏半个字。你如果不相信，我拿我老娘来罚咒……”周一鸣倏然动容，连连摇手：“这怎么可以？”他想了想问：“我想请问俞大爷，跷脚长根做的那些坏事，你是不是都晓得？”“晓得一点，不能说完全晓得。”“他欺侮过一个寡妇，这件事你听说过没有？”“听说过。”俞武成点点头，“他先搭上了一个寡妇，赌输了就去伸手，那寡妇的一点私房跟首饰，都让他逼光了。长根要她卖祭祀田，她不肯，就吓她，要撕她的面皮。那寡妇想想左右做不来人，一索子上吊死了，是不是这么回事？““是的，那寡妇姓魏，有个兄弟在长根手下，长根大意，不在乎他……”“我懂了。”俞武成不需他再说下去，“姓魏的，是你老兄的好朋友？”“不是，我跟他初交。我有个换帖弟兄，跟他是好朋友，这趟跟我换帖弟兄谈起长根，他才找了小魏来跟我见面。消息是决不假，可惜详细情形他还不清楚。”

Before they reached any conclusion, Liu arrived with Zhou. Zhou stood below Hu in status, but with Yu and his disciples he was a friend on equal terms. Moreover, by bringing the warning he had already done Yu a great service. They therefore treated him with marked courtesy, and some time passed in polite conversation before they came to the point. Naturally, Hu had to put the question. “Old Zhou, your information makes a good deal of sense. Still, there are questions about it. So much depends on this that we must establish the facts. Can you tell us how you obtained it?” Had Hu asked him alone, Zhou would readily have told the truth. With Yu and his disciples, whom he was meeting for the first time, present, he could not help feeling wary. Yu detected his hesitation and spoke with great sincerity. “Brother Zhou, speak freely. Not half a word will escape anyone here. If you do not believe me, I shall swear an oath upon my mother—” Zhou’s expression changed at once, and he waved his hands repeatedly. “You must not do that!” After a moment’s thought he asked, “May I ask, Master Yu, whether you know all the wicked things Changgen has done?” “Some, but I cannot claim to know everything.” “Have you heard how he mistreated a widow?” “Yes.” Yu nodded. “He began an affair with her and then demanded money whenever he lost at gambling. He forced her to give up all her private savings and jewellery. Then he wanted her to sell the land set aside to support ancestral rites. When she refused, he threatened to expose and disgrace her. She felt that whatever she did, she could no longer face the world, and hanged herself. Is that the affair?” “Yes. Her surname was Wei. She had a brother serving under Changgen, but Changgen was careless and paid him no attention—” “I understand.” Yu needed no more explanation. “Is this Wei a close friend of yours?” “No, I have only just met him. One of my sworn brothers is his close friend. When I mentioned Changgen to my sworn brother this time, he brought Young Wei to meet me. There is no doubt that the information is true. Unfortunately, Wei does not yet know the details.”

“这已经够了。”俞武成问道：“不知道小魏肯不肯出面做见证？”“不会肯的。”胡雪岩接口，“就肯出面，口说无凭，长根也可以赖掉的。”“那么，”俞武成断然决然地说，“就我一个人去会他！”“不！”胡雪岩说，“大哥，你一个人去无用，他一定按兵不动。我看此事只好作罢。那一船洋枪，承大哥情让，我另有补报……”“嗐！”俞武成抢着打断，“老胡，你这不成话了。事情弄到这步田地，糟糕得很，窝窝囊囊，叫我以后怎么再在场面上混？这样，你先请回去，我跟松江老大去商量，一定把你这一船洋枪，运到杭州。跷脚长根，当然也饶不过他，不要看我借地安营，我照样要跟他拼个明白。”看到俞武成有些闹意气的模样，胡雪岩认为这件事不宜再谈下去，先要让他冷一冷，消一消气，所以一面向刘不才使个眼色，一面摆摆手说：“‘性急吃不得热粥’，回头再谈吧！反正有大哥在这里，没有什么办不通的事。”“对了！”刘不才领受默喻，附和着说：“我陪俞老先玩一场牌九，换换脑筋！”说着，他将俞武成硬拖了走。朱家吃闲饭的人很多，等场面摆开，自有人聚拢来，很快地凑起一桌小牌九。刘不才有意推让俞武成做庄，绊住了他的身子，以便胡雪岩与杨凤毛好从容筹计。

“That is enough already,” Yu said. “Would Young Wei be willing to come forward as a witness?” “He would not,” Hu answered. “And even if he did, a statement without proof could simply be denied by Changgen.” “Then I shall go and meet him alone!” Yu declared. “No. Going alone would achieve nothing, brother. He would certainly make no move. I think we must abandon the matter. I am grateful that you have agreed to leave that boatload of guns alone, and I shall repay your kindness in some other way—” “Oh, come!” Yu interrupted. “Old Hu, you cannot say that. For things to reach this miserable state! If I submit to such humiliation, how can I ever show my face again? Here is what we shall do: you go back first. I shall talk to the Songjiang Chief and make certain your guns reach Hangzhou. As for Changgen, of course I shall not let him off. I may be camped on borrowed ground, but I shall still fight this out with him.” Seeing that Yu was letting wounded pride govern him, Hu decided they should not continue the discussion. He must first cool down and lose some of his anger. Hu signalled to Liu and waved his hand. “You cannot swallow hot porridge in a hurry. Let us discuss it later. With you here, brother, there is nothing that cannot be managed.” “Exactly!” Liu understood the unspoken request and chimed in. “I shall play a round of pai gow with Elder Yu first, to give his mind a change.” He practically dragged Yu away. There were plenty of idle dependants in Zhu’s household; as soon as the game was set out, people gathered, and they soon had enough players for the small version of pai gow. Liu deliberately insisted that Yu take the bank, keeping him occupied so that Hu and Yang could devise a plan at leisure.
