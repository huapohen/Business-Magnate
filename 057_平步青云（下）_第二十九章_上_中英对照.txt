第二十九章｜CHAPTER TWENTY-NINE

这轻飘飘的一封八行，在胡雪岩感觉中，仿佛肩上压下一副沉重的担子。地方的安危，跷脚长根的祸福，以及何桂清的前程，都系于他的一句话中。说一声：是预备点验，不是别有用心，则清军自然撤围，但万一跷脚长根乘机作乱，则追究责任，岂仅何桂清不得了，自己亦有脑袋搬家的可能。倘或答说：情况不明，难作判断，则清军便可能围剿，有如杀降，自己在场面上如何交代，还在其次，身上等于背了一笔血债，以后的日子怎么过得下去？跟俞武成商量的结果，只有这样答复：已经遵谕开始调查，真相未明之前，请何桂清转告营务处，按兵不动，加意防范。这是搪塞眼前，究竟真相如何，亟待澄清，周一鸣却又不知到哪里去了？胡雪岩心想，形势象炉子上烘着一罐火药，随时可以爆发，这罐火药不早早设法拿开，令人片刻难安。因而当机立断，决定了一个开门见山的办法。

That flimsy letter seemed to lay a heavy burden on Hu's shoulders. The safety of the district, Lame Changgen's fate and He Guiqing's future all depended on a word from him. If he said that the men were assembling for inspection and had no hidden purpose, the Qing troops would withdraw their encirclement. But if Changgen seized the chance to rebel, He would face ruin, and Hu himself might lose his head. If, on the other hand, he said the situation was unclear and impossible to judge, the troops might attack. It would be like killing men who had surrendered. How he could explain himself was a secondary matter; he would carry a debt of blood. How could he live afterwards? After consulting Yu Wucheng, he replied that inquiries had begun as instructed, and asked He to tell the military affairs office to hold its position and exercise particular vigilance until the truth was clear. This put off the immediate difficulty, but the facts urgently needed establishing. Where had Zhou Yiming gone? Hu felt as though a jar of gunpowder were heating on a stove, ready to explode at any moment. Until it was removed, he could have no peace. He therefore decided to tackle the matter directly.

这天晚上打听到，跷脚长根歇在妙珍那里，胡雪岩请朱老大派了个人引导，径造妙珍香阁。这是不速之客，跷脚长根深感意外。内心紧张，表面却甚闲豫，胡雪岩先打量妙珍，貌不甚美，但长身玉立，身段极好，而且花信年华，正是风尘女子中最妙的那段年岁。至于谈吐应酬，更见得气度不凡，配了跷脚长根那样一个草莽英雄，他倒替她觉得可惜。等摆出碟子来小酌，胡雪岩才看一看妙珍问跷脚长根：“有封信，想给你看。”“喔，”跷脚长根会意了，“请到这边来，”一引引入妙珍的卧室，请胡雪岩坐在妆台边，跷脚长根自己坐在床沿上，俯身相就，静候问话。“我听你一句话，你说怎么样，我就怎么样答复前途。”胡雪岩一面说，一面把信递了过去。看完了信，跷脚长根的脸色显得很不安，静静想了一会答道：“老兄，你看我是什么意思？”这话问得很有分量，胡雪岩很慎重地答道：“如果我不相信，我就不拿这封信给你看了。”跷脚长根点点头，表示满意：“好的！我晓得你为难。该怎么办，请你吩咐。”“言重，言重！”胡雪岩想了想答道：“也难怪官军！实在时世太乱，不能不防，弄出误会来，说句实话，总是我们吃亏。所以，我想不如等一等，到有了点验的日子，大家再来，官军就不会疑心了。”“是！”跷脚长根说：“吃酒去！”

That evening he learned that Changgen was staying with Miaozhen. He asked Eldest Brother Zhu for a guide and went straight to her rooms. His unexpected arrival greatly surprised Changgen. Though tense within, Hu appeared perfectly at ease. He first took stock of Miaozhen. Her face was not especially beautiful, but she was tall and graceful, with an excellent figure, and at the flowering age considered the finest in a courtesan's life. Her conversation and social manner showed unusual distinction. Hu felt it a pity that she should be paired with a rough adventurer like Changgen. Only after small dishes had been set out for a drink did he glance at her and tell Changgen, “There's a letter I'd like you to see.” “Oh.” Understanding, Changgen said, “Come this way.” He led Hu into Miaozhen's bedroom and seated him by the dressing table. Changgen sat on the edge of the bed, leaning towards him, and waited. “I'll take your word. Whatever you tell me, that is what I'll report to the authorities.” Hu handed him the letter. After reading it, Changgen looked troubled. He considered silently for a moment. “Brother, what do you think my intentions are?” It was a weighty question. Hu answered carefully. “If I didn't trust you, I wouldn't show you this letter.” Changgen nodded, satisfied. “Good. I know your difficulty. Tell me what I should do.” “You honour me.” Hu thought for a moment. “We can hardly blame the troops. These times are so unsettled that they must take precautions. If a misunderstanding arises, frankly, we shall be the ones to suffer. Why not wait? Once an inspection date is fixed, everyone can assemble, and the troops will have no reason for suspicion.” “Very well. Let's have a drink.”

走到外间，他立刻找了贵生来，嘱咐他连夜派人，分头通知部下，各回原处。这样明快的处置，胡雪岩也深感满意。喝酒闲谈之际，由于撤除了内心的戒备，两个人越谈越投机，胡雪岩不待周一鸣来回报，就已知道了跷脚长根改变态度，愿意就抚的原因，当然，这是出于他的自叙。一言以蔽之，是为了胡雪岩的态度。那副牌九上的“高抬贵手”，当然是促成跷脚长根改变态度的主要原因，但不是唯一原因。他认为胡雪岩讲江湖义气讲得“上路”，固然心服，而真正使他能够信任的，还在胡雪岩的才干。讲义气也要有个讲法，同生共死算得是最义气的，但同年同月同日的同死，究竟不如一起吃酒吃肉的同生来得有味道。跷脚长根很坦白地表示，他就是相信胡雪岩有让他吃酒吃肉的本事。这番推心置腹的话，自然令胡雪岩有着意外的感动，不过他向来的处世之道是，大家越尊敬他，他越替人着想，所以一再谦虚，认为跷脚长根“够朋友”，给他这么一个面子。同时又极力推崇俞武成，让跷脚长根清楚地感觉到，能尊敬俞武成，则比尊敬他更能使他高兴。这一番小酌，吃到深更半夜，俞武成却有些不放心，特为派朱老大来探问，托词苏州有连夜送到的信，要请他回去看。到家相见，彼此说明经过，俞武成便越发对他刮目相看了。第二天一早，周一鸣带来的消息，与跷脚长根自己所说的，大致相仿，而他，此刻又有了新的任务。在苏州那方面，胡雪岩的布置是七分防备，三分招抚，现在防备不需要了，关卡上所设的暗桩，应该撤回，而招抚的准备工作，只做了三分是不够的，必得立刻替跷脚长根去安排，特意先派周一鸣去见何桂清，报个信息，他自己打算在这晚上赴宴以后，连夜回苏州去料理。

Back in the outer room, Changgen immediately summoned Guisheng and ordered him to send men out that night in every direction, telling his followers to return to their original quarters. Hu was deeply satisfied by this prompt decision. With his inner guard lowered, their conversation over wine grew increasingly congenial. Before Zhou returned to report, Hu learned why Changgen had changed his attitude and decided to accept the amnesty—at least, according to Changgen's own account. In a word, it was Hu's conduct. His generosity over that pai gow hand had been the chief cause, but not the only one. Changgen admired the way Hu understood and practised loyalty among friends. What truly won his trust, however, was Hu's ability. Even loyalty needed the right means of expression. Living and dying together might be its highest form, but dying together on the same day of the same month and year was less enjoyable than living together over wine and meat. Changgen frankly admitted that he trusted Hu to provide the wine and meat. Such confidence moved Hu unexpectedly. Yet his usual rule was that the more respect people showed him, the more consideration he owed them. He repeatedly disclaimed the credit, saying that Changgen was a true friend to grant him such favour. He also praised Yu Wucheng warmly, making Changgen understand that respect shown to Yu would please him even more than respect shown to himself. They drank far into the night. Yu grew anxious and sent Zhu to inquire, using the excuse that a letter had arrived from Suzhou and Hu must return to read it. Once home, they explained what had happened, and Yu's esteem for him rose still higher. Early next morning, Zhou brought information broadly matching Changgen's account. Zhou now had another task. Hu's arrangements in Suzhou had been seven parts precaution and three parts amnesty. The precautions were no longer needed; the concealed men at the checkpoints should be withdrawn. Three parts preparation for the amnesty was insufficient, however, and arrangements for Changgen must be made at once. Hu sent Zhou ahead to inform He, intending to return to Suzhou himself overnight after that evening's banquet.

一场“鸿门宴”，变成了庆功宴，在妙珍姐妹殷勤侍奉，以及跷脚长根的不断相劝之下，胡雪岩跟俞武成一样喝得酪酊大醉。等酒醒过来，忽切间不辨身在何处？一只手无意间一伸，触摸到极软、极滑的肌肤，于是接着闻到了脂香，看到了粉光，昏昏罗帐中有个妙年女子陪他睡着，只是脸朝外面，一时看不出是谁？定定神细想，除了猜拳闹酒的情形，再也想不起酒阑人散的光景。于是摇摇他身边那段藕也似的手臂，摇醒了一看，是妙珍的妹妹，颜色远胜于她姐姐的妙珠。“喔，胡老爷，你醒了！”和衣而睡的妙珠，急忙坐了起来，“要不要喝茶？”“要的。”胡雪岩觉得嗓子干涩，说话都很吃力，“要冷茶，大大来一杯！”“酒吃得忒多了。俞大爷也醉得人事不知。”说着，她掀帐下床，剔亮了灯，倒了一大杯半温的茶，挂起帐子，拿茶杯送到胡雪岩唇边。他一饮而尽，喘口气问道：“什么时候了？”“快四点钟了。”“只怕害你半夜不曾好睡，真正过意不去。”

The banquet meant as a trap became a celebration of success. Attended devotedly by Miaozhen and her sister, and urged on continually by Changgen, Hu became as thoroughly drunk as Yu. When he woke, he could not for a moment tell where he was. An unthinking movement of his hand brought it against skin exquisitely soft and smooth. Then came the scent of cosmetics and the pale gleam of a powdered face. A young woman lay sleeping beside him in the dim bed curtains, but her face was turned away, and he could not see who she was. Collecting himself, he remembered the drinking games, but nothing of the party's end. He shook the arm beside him, pale as a lotus root, and woke her. It was Miaozhen's younger sister, Miaozhu, far more beautiful than her elder sister. “Oh, Master Hu, you're awake!” Still fully dressed, she sat up quickly. “Would you like tea?” “Yes. Cold tea—a great cupful!” His throat was so dry that speech was difficult. “You drank far too much. Master Yu was insensible too.” She lifted the curtains, got out of bed, brightened the lamp and poured a large cup of lukewarm tea. Hooking up the curtains, she brought it to his lips. He drank it all, caught his breath and asked, “What time is it?” “Almost four.” “I'm afraid I've kept you from sleeping half the night. I'm very sorry.”

“胡老爷为啥这样子说？你是李七爷的朋友。”李七爷是指跷脚长根，胡雪岩便问：“他醉了没有？”“李七爷从不醉的。”“喔！”胡雪岩很诧异，“他的酒量这么大？”“李七爷的酒量并不大，不过，他会得吃酒。”“你这话倒有趣！”胡雪岩讪笑地说，“又说他会吃酒，又说他酒量并不大。”“喔唷！胡老爷，你不作兴‘扳差头’的！”妙珠的神态，声音都嗲得令人发腻，“我是说李七爷吃酒上会变把戏。”“我不是扳你的差头，你说话真的有趣。”胡雪岩捧着她的脸说：“吃酒还会变把戏，你自己想想，话可有趣！”“真的！不作兴瞎说。”妙珠问道：“胡老爷，你跟李七爷熟不熟？”“也算熟，也算不熟。”“你自己呢？”妙珠反唇相讥，“说话也是一脚进、一脚出。”“这有个说法，相交的日子不久，不能算熟，不过交情已很深了，所以也可以说是很熟。”“熟了你就知道了，豁拳敬酒，你要当心李七爷，明明看他已经灌进嘴，实在是倒在地上，或者袖子里。他晓得自己酒量的深浅，永远喝到七分数就不喝了。不过，他不肯说一句话吃不下了，那时候……”妙珠笑笑不再说下去，意思是到那时候，就有“把戏”看了。

“Why say that? You're Seventh Master Li's friend.” By Seventh Master Li she meant Changgen. “Was he drunk?” Hu asked. “Seventh Master Li never gets drunk.” “Oh!” Hu was surprised. “Can he hold so much?” “He can't hold much, but he knows how to drink.” Hu laughed teasingly. “That's an amusing way to put it. You say he knows how to drink, and then that he can't drink much.” “Oh, Master Hu, don't pick holes in what I say!” Her voice and manner were cloyingly coy. “I mean he can play tricks when he drinks.” “I'm not picking holes. You really do say amusing things.” Hu cupped her face in his hands. “Think of it yourself: playing tricks with drinking!” “It's true. You mustn't talk nonsense. Do you know Seventh Master Li well?” “In one way, yes; in another, no.” “And what about you?” she retorted. “One foot in and one foot out whenever you speak!” “There's an explanation. We haven't known each other long, so I can't say I know him well. But our friendship is already deep, so in that sense I do.” “Once you know him, you'll understand. Watch him during drinking games and toasts. You may see him pouring wine into his mouth when it's really going onto the floor or into his sleeve. He knows his own capacity and always stops at seven-tenths of it. But he'll never say he can't drink any more. At that point...” She smiled and left the rest unsaid. That was when the tricks began.

这句毫不相干的闲谈，在胡雪岩觉得极其有用，喝酒赌钱，最可以看出性情，照跷脚长根这种喝酒的情形来看，显然是个极能自制的人，但也是极难惹的人，到他不说做这件事，而逼着他非做不可时，他就出花样了。因此，胡雪岩对他仍不免引起了一两分戒心。妙珠极其机敏，从他眼睛里看出他神思不属，随即问道：“胡老爷你在想点啥？”“我在想李七爷吃酒的把戏，以后遇到这种情形，要防备他，不叫他变把戏。”“不容易，李七爷花样多得很，你防不住的。”“喔！”胡雪岩的戒心更深了，“你们看，李七爷这个人怎么样？”妙珠想了想答道：“极能干的。”“他的脾气呢？”“一个人总有脾气的。李七爷有佯好，脾气不乱发。我姐姐就欢喜他这一点。”“你呢？你跟你姐姐是不是一样？”“是啊！”妙珠做出那种娇柔不胜的神态：“喔唷，碰着有种脾气丑的客人，那么，我们吃这碗饭，真是叫作孽，什么伤人心的话都说得出来！”“照这样说，你也跟你姐姐欢喜李七爷那样，会得欢喜我。”胡雪岩说：“我是从不发脾气的。”“真的？”“自然是真的。”

Hu found this apparently irrelevant chatter extremely useful. Drinking and gambling were the best ways to reveal a man's character. Changgen's way of drinking showed exceptional self-control, but also that he was difficult to push. When he would not agree to do something and others insisted, he resorted to tricks. A little caution therefore returned to Hu's mind. Miaozhu was perceptive. Seeing the distraction in his eyes, she asked what he was thinking. “About Seventh Master Li's drinking tricks. Next time I must watch him and stop him playing them.” “That won't be easy. He has too many tricks for you to guard against.” “Oh!” Hu grew more wary. “What do you think of him?” She considered. “Extremely capable.” “And his temper?” “Everyone has a temper. His good point is that he doesn't lose it without reason. That's what my sister likes about him.” “And you? Do you feel the same as your sister?” “Of course.” Her manner grew languishing. “Oh, when we meet one of those foul-tempered customers, earning our living this way is a real torment. There's no hurtful thing they won't say.” “Then you'll like me as your sister likes Seventh Master Li. I never lose my temper.” “Really?” “Of course.”

“那我欢喜。”说着，妙珠一把抱住胡雪岩，而且深深吸气，仿佛无端兴奋得不克自持似地。胡雪岩静静享受着那种温馨的滋味，同时想到以前亲近过的几位女子，觉得妙珠别有动人之处。芙蓉沉静，阿巧姐老练，而妙珠娇柔灵动，自有一番魅力。他沉浸在眼前的亲密之中。一番缱绻，万种风情，胡雪岩心满意足地沉沉睡去。一觉醒来，红日满窗，第一件事，就是想到要上苏州，但不知如何，一念及此，那颗心便往下一沉，就象小时候新年里正玩得高高兴兴，忽然听说蒙馆里开学那样，真是一万个不情愿。

“Then I like you.” Miaozhu threw her arms round him and drew a deep breath, suddenly seeming unable to contain her delight. Hu quietly enjoyed the warmth of the embrace. Comparing the sensation with the women he had known, he found something especially appealing in Miaozhu. Furong was reserved, A’qiao experienced, while Miaozhu had a tender, lively charm of her own. He gave himself over to their present intimacy. After loving embraces and countless endearments, he fell into a deep, contented sleep. When he woke again, sunlight flooded the window. His first thought was that he must go to Suzhou. Somehow, the thought made his heart sink, like a child enjoying the New Year holidays who suddenly hears that lessons are beginning again. He was utterly unwilling to go.

算了！他将心一横，决定偷一天懒。于是翻个身又睡，只是枕上衾底，香泽犹存，缭绕鼻端，荡漾心头，怎么样也睡不着了。辗转反侧之际，惊动了在后房理妆的妙珠，轻轻走了出来，探望动静。胡雪岩从簇新的珠罗纱帐子中望出去，只见妙珠淡妆犹如浓抹，因为天生来唇格外红，皮肤格外白，朝阳映照，犹如一株带露的芍药，而隔青帐子，又如雾里看花，逗得他格外心痒，渴望着再亲一亲。因此，等妙珠刚一掀帐子，他就伸子去拉，突如其来，动作又太猛了些，妙珠真的吓一大跳，“啐！啐！”她拍着自己的胸说：“吓得我来！”“对不起，对不起！”胡雪岩歉意的赔笑，同时将身子往里缩了一下，示意她坐下。“真正是‘猛门’老爷！”妙珠还在拍胸，“到现在我心还在跳！”“哪里就吓得这样了？”胡雪岩不满地说，“我不相信。”“不相信你摸摸看。”胡雪岩便伸手摸到她胸前，一面摸，一面得意地笑了，这才让妙珠发觉上了当，将腰一扭，捉住他的手，“啪”地打了一下，然后白着眼，将他的手塞到被头里。

Never mind! He hardened his resolve and decided to steal a day of leisure. Turning over, he tried to sleep again. But the lingering fragrance in the pillows and quilts drifted round his nose and stirred his heart; sleep would not come. His tossing disturbed Miaozhu, who was dressing in the back room. She came quietly to see what was happening. Through the new gauze bed curtains, Hu watched her. Her light makeup seemed rich, for her lips were naturally red and her skin exceptionally fair. In the morning sun she looked like a dew-covered peony. Through the blue-green curtains she was a blossom in mist, exciting his desire to kiss her again. As soon as she raised the curtain, he reached out and pulled her towards him. The movement was so sudden and forceful that she was genuinely startled. “Oh!” She patted her chest. “You frightened me!” “I'm sorry.” He smiled apologetically and shifted farther into the bed to make room for her to sit. “What a fierce gentleman you are!” She was still patting her chest. “My heart is pounding even now.” “Surely you weren't that frightened? I don't believe it.” “Feel it, then.” Hu put his hand to her breast and smiled triumphantly as he touched her. Only then did she realize she had been tricked. Twisting away, she caught his hand, slapped it and gave him a reproachful look before thrusting it beneath the quilt.

“妙珠！”胡雪岩涎着脸说，“再陪我睡一会！”“啐！不作兴的。”说着站起来要走。“别走，别走！”胡雪岩软化了，连声喊道：“我不跟你罗嗦，陪我说说话总可以吧！”妙珠嫣然一笑，又坐了下来，“时候还早，你再睡一息。”她问，“今天想吃点啥？鲥鱼，好不好？”“好！”“那么，我要早点去关照大司务。”妙珠按着他的被头，不让他将手伸出来，“我马上就来！”果然，言而有信，一去即回，一面收拾房间，一面有一搭没一搭地与胡雪岩说闲话。这一来，越发使得胡雪岩无法再睡，但他深知那种地方的规矩，午饭之前，除了厨子和打杂男工以外，娘姨、大姐都还在床上，非到中午不起市面，自己如果起身，则按规矩要有人来伺候，岂不是扰了人家的好梦？胡雪岩最肯体恤下人，为此便依旧“赖”在床上，口中闲话，心里盘算着事，倒也难得悠闲。

“Miaozhu,” Hu pleaded with an unabashed smile, “lie with me a little longer.” “No. You mustn't.” She rose to go. “Don't go!” He yielded. “I won't bother you. Surely you can stay and talk?” She smiled and sat down again. “It's still early. Sleep a little more. What would you like to eat today? Shad?” “Good.” “Then I must tell the cook early.” She held down the quilt to keep his hands beneath it. “I'll be straight back.” She kept her word. As she tidied the room, she chatted with him intermittently. This made it still more impossible to sleep, but Hu knew the customs of such houses. Before lunch, everyone except the cook and male odd-job servants was still in bed. The maids and attendants did not begin their day until noon. If he got up, someone would have to attend him. Why disturb their pleasant dreams? Hu was always considerate towards servants. He therefore continued to linger in bed, talking while turning business over in his mind, enjoying a rare spell of leisure.

就这佯挨到近午时分，方始起身。漱洗完毕，正想去跟跷脚长根见面，忽然来了个不速之客，是朱老大，带来了一个意外的消息，说尤五和古应春都到了，俞武成请他立刻去见面。“好！”胡雪岩十分高兴，“我跟主人说一声，马上就走。”到得后进妙珍的香巢，才知道跷脚长根一早就走了，因为胡雪岩那时好梦正酣，不便惊扰，临走留下话，留胡雪岩住一天，晚上依然在这里宴叙。为了报答珠珠，同时，既还跷脚长根的席，又替尤、古二人接风，胡雪岩使用妙珠的称呼，对妙珍说：“珍姐，今天应该我‘做花头’，请你备个‘双台’。菜跟酒都要好！”说着，取了张五十两的银票，放在桌上。妙珍无论如何不肯收，又说用不了这么多钱，推让再四，胡雪岩只能收回，另外给了二十两银子的赏钱，娘姨、大姐、相帮一齐来谢赏，个个笑逐颜开。于是，“胡老爷是第一号好客人”这句话，马上传开去了。

He remained there until nearly noon, then rose. After washing, he was about to seek Changgen when an unexpected visitor arrived: Zhu, with surprising news. You Laowu and Gu Yingchun had both come, and Yu wanted him to meet them at once. “Excellent!” Hu was delighted. “I'll tell our host and come immediately.” In Miaozhen's rooms at the rear, he learned that Changgen had left early. Hu had been sound asleep, so Changgen had not disturbed him. He had left word inviting Hu to stay another day and dine there again that evening. Hu wished to repay Miaozhu's attentions, return Changgen's hospitality and welcome You and Gu. Using Miaozhu's form of address for her sister, he told Miaozhen, “Sister Zhen, today I should provide the entertainment. Arrange two tables, please. Both the food and wine must be good.” He laid a fifty-tael note on the table. Miaozhen refused it firmly, saying so much money was unnecessary. After repeated offers and refusals, Hu could only take it back. Instead he gave twenty taels in gratuities. The maids, attendants and male servants came to thank him, every face beaming. The word that “Master Hu is the finest guest of all” soon spread.

* * *到得朱家，胡雪岩就感到不寻常，不请自来的不止尤五和古应春，另外还有五个人，都是中年，个个衣冠楚楚，但神态间总掩不住江湖豪气，倒叫他识不透是何路数。等尤五一一引见，才约略听出来，都是苏、松、太一带提得起名头的第一等人物。其中有个人管胡雪岩叫“小爷叔”，不用说，是尤五的师兄弟。有了这个“底子”在心里，胡雪岩应酬寒暄就很投机了。然而此辈来意如何，煞费猜疑，因而找个机会，将尤五邀到一边，细问究竟。“我们白来一趟，不过倒是白来的好，要用得着我们的力量，事情就不妙了！”尤五微笑着说了这几句没头没脑的话，然后表明来意，他是前天回松江的，王有龄托办的事，此刻无暇细说，一到松江就得到消息，说跷脚长根将有不利于胡雪岩和俞武成的举动，松江老大颇为关心，与尤五商议，邀了这批人，赶来徘解，如果排解不成，说不定就要“动手”，因此，松江老大亲自在调兵遣将，还有大批人马在待命。“老大爷这么待我，真正感激不尽。”胡雪岩是真的感动，“事情弄好了！”“我也是一到就听说了。小爷叔，你真行！跷脚长根是有名疙瘩难弄的人，居然让你摆平。不过，我想，我们此来，替你助助阵也是好的。”“一点都不错。老实说，我打听过跷脚长根的为人，十分之中，还有两三分不大靠得住，有你们几位的面子压一压，那就十足保险了！”“好的！我出面来请客。”“今天晚上是我的，大家吃花酒。明天中午算你出面，你看在这里好不好？”“也只有借朱老大的地方才合适。不过……”尤五迟疑着，仿佛有句话不便出口似地。“五哥，有话你尽管说。”胡雪岩倒真想不出尤五跟自己的关系，还有什么话碍口，因而充满了好奇心，“我们的交情，还有什么话不能说的。”“小爷叔，我先告个罪。说来说去，你总在‘门槛’外头……”原来为此！胡雪岩抢过来说，“你不用说了。我知道。我理当回避。”能谅解最好。尤五觉得交情已够，无需解释，便又提到另外一件事：“老古是昨天到我那里的，他也有许多话要跟你说，听说洋人已经服帖了。我去陪客人，把他调出来跟你来谈。”

At the Zhu house, Hu at once sensed something unusual. You and Gu were not the only uninvited arrivals. Five others were there, all middle-aged and well dressed, yet with an unmistakable air of boldness belonging to men of the brotherhood. He could not place them. As You introduced them, he learned that they were leading figures in the Suzhou, Songjiang and Taicang region. One addressed him as Little Uncle, plainly marking him as one of You's fellow disciples. With that foundation established, Hu found the greetings easy enough. Their purpose remained puzzling, however, and he took You aside to ask. “We've come for nothing—but it's better that way. If our strength had been needed, matters would have been serious.” You smiled at this cryptic opening, then explained. He had returned to Songjiang the day before yesterday. There was no time yet to discuss Wang's commission in detail. On arriving, he had heard that Changgen intended to harm Hu and Yu. The Old Master was deeply concerned. After consulting You, he had invited these men to come and mediate. If mediation failed, they might have to use force. The Old Master was personally making arrangements, with a large body of men awaiting orders. “I can never thank him enough for treating me like this.” Hu was genuinely moved. “The business is settled now.” “So I heard as soon as I arrived. Little Uncle, you're remarkable. Changgen is notoriously difficult, yet you've brought him round. Still, I think our presence can lend you useful support.” “Exactly. Frankly, from what I've learned of him, I still have two or three parts in ten of doubt. With your standing brought to bear, we can be completely secure.” “Good. I'll give a dinner.” “Tonight is mine. We'll dine with the courtesans. You can give yours tomorrow at noon. Would this house do?” “Zhu's is the only suitable place. But...” You hesitated, as though something were difficult to say. “Brother Five, speak freely. What could our friendship prevent you from saying?” Hu was truly curious. “First forgive me, Little Uncle. However close we are, you're still outside our threshold.” Hu understood at once. “Say no more. I know. Naturally, I should stay away.” Their friendship was close enough to need no further explanation. You changed the subject. “Old Gu reached my place yesterday. He has much to tell you too. Apparently the foreigners have yielded. I'll attend to the guests and send him out to speak with you.”

古应春带来了极好的消息，洋人终于软化了，决定出高价买丝。照古应春的算法，这一笔生意，可以赚十八万银子，问胡雪岩卖不卖？“怎么不卖？”胡雪岩很高兴地说，“不要说十八万银子，就是赚八万银子，我也要卖了！生意要慢慢做，长线放远鹞。而且，说老实话，我手上的事情太多，不清理不得了！”“卖是卖，洋人有个条件，要订三年的约，以后的丝都归他一个人买。”“这也可以，就是价钱上，年年不同，怎么算法？”“这当然到时候再议。他保证我们有钱赚。”古应春说，“大致是照外洋报价，扣除他的赚头，就是实价。”“这恐怕不妥当吧！这样变成包他有钱赚了。”胡雪岩说，“你想想看，如果外洋丝价一落，扣除了他的赚头，不够我们的成本，怎么办？”“是的。我也想到了。不过，说来说去，‘千来万来，赔本不来’，中外都是一样的。如果外洋丝价落，他不收，别人当然也不收。我再说一句，洋人做生意，跟我们不同，他们做生意，讲究培养来源，所以亦决不会要求过分。我想，我们这方面的顾虑，亦可以跟他谈。总而言之，守住互利两个字，合约一定谈得拢。不晓得你什么时候到上海去？”

Gu brought excellent news. The foreigners had finally softened and agreed to pay a high price for the silk. By his calculation, the transaction would yield a profit of a hundred and eighty thousand taels. Would Hu sell? “Of course!” Hu replied gladly. “Never mind a hundred and eighty thousand—even eighty thousand would make me sell. Business must be built gradually, flying the kite far out on a long line. Besides, I have too many affairs on my hands. I must clear some of them.” “We can sell, but the foreigner has a condition: a three-year contract, with all our future silk sold exclusively to him.” “That would be acceptable too. But prices change every year. How would they be fixed?” “We would negotiate them at the time, of course. He guarantees us a profit. Broadly, the price would be the overseas quotation less his margin.” “That may not be sound. It would guarantee his profit. Suppose the overseas silk price fell and, after his margin was deducted, what remained didn't cover our costs?” “Yes, I've thought of that. But in the end, whatever else a man will do, he won't trade at a loss. That's true of Chinese and foreigners alike. If the overseas price falls and he won't buy, neither will anyone else. Besides, foreigners conduct business differently from us. They take care to develop their sources of supply and won't make excessive demands. We can discuss our concerns with him. So long as we hold to mutual benefit, we can agree a contract. When can you reach Shanghai?”

“我的事，大部分要在上海办，不过，杭州不能不去，七姐的事也要紧。”“喔！”古应春问，“五哥没有限你谈过？”“谈什么？没有！”“五哥跟王雪公老实说了，结这门干亲，是借重他的名望，好叫我们那位老族长服帖。王雪公很体谅，他说，既然如此，不妨先提亲事，现在天气也热，不必劳动七姐。秋凉办喜事，他抽空来吃喜酒，再补认亲的礼节。如呆他不能来，就让我送七姐去，回门带认亲，一事两便。”“好极了！雪公既有这话，恭敬不如从命，我暂时不必回杭州，办完了跷脚长根的事，由苏州回上海。”胡雪岩又问：“老裘怎么办？”“预定今天从上海动身。俞老的那位少君，我也见着了，少年老成，人很妥当。松江一带，五哥已经关照过了，必定一路顺风，你放心好了。”由于这一连串诸事顺利的好消息，胡雪岩的心境开朗，兴致大好，决定大大地请一次客。另外挑日子已不可能，就拿这晚上的宴会扩大，这件事交给刘不才去办，他跟杨凤毛、朱老大商议，将当地与漕帮有渊源的人，统统请到。又顾虑到跷脚长根当着尤五他们这班远客，不便高踞首座，而又不宜委屈他做个陪客，特地向胡雪岩说明，将跷脚长根也当作主人，发帖子拿他列在前面，这样也就算很捧他了。

“Most of my business needs doing in Shanghai. But I must go to Hangzhou too. Seventh Sister's affairs are important.” “Oh! Didn't Brother Five tell you?” “Tell me what? No.” “He explained frankly to Mr Xuexuan that we wanted the sworn family connection for the weight of his name, to persuade our old clan head. Mr Xuexuan was very understanding. Since that was the purpose, he said, we might as well propose the marriage first. The weather is growing hot; there's no need to trouble Seventh Sister with a journey. When we marry in the cool of autumn, he'll find time to attend the feast and complete the sworn-sibling ceremony then. If he can't come, I can take her to him afterwards, combining her customary post-wedding family visit with the ceremony.” “Excellent! Since he says so, obedience is the best respect. I needn't return to Hangzhou for now. Once Changgen's business is finished, I'll go from Suzhou to Shanghai. What about Old Qiu?” “He was due to leave Shanghai today. I've met Yu's son too: mature beyond his years and thoroughly dependable. Brother Five has made arrangements throughout Songjiang. The passage is sure to go smoothly. Set your mind at rest.” This succession of good news lifted Hu's spirits and put him in an expansive mood. He decided to entertain on a grand scale. There was no time to choose another date; that evening's banquet must be enlarged. He put Liu Bucai in charge. After consulting Yang Fengmao and Zhu, Liu invited everyone locally connected with the Grain Transport Brotherhood. Another difficulty was Changgen's position: before visiting dignitaries like You, he could hardly occupy the chief seat, yet making him merely another guest would slight him. Liu explained this to Hu and proposed listing Changgen as a fellow host, with his name first on the invitations. That would honour him handsomely.

尴尬的是到了傍晚，嘉宾云集，总数不下四十，主人之一的跷脚长根始终不曾露面。胡雪岩一个人八面周旋，未免吃力，而心里犹自不断嘀咕，更觉得不是滋味。“珍姐！” 胡雪岩悄悄问妙珍，“长根到底到哪里去了？你总有点数吧？”“我也猜不透。一早有他一个弟兄来叫，背人谈了一会就走了，临走什么话都没有留下。我看，”妙珍倒很有决断，“不便让客人久等，就开席吧！”于是筵开四席，推让多时，方始坐定。刘不才早就有了准备，将同里的“名花”列成一张单子，在席间传观，有熟识愿意招呼的，便拿笔做个记号，然后飞笺催花，莺莺燕燕，陆续而至，有熟客的自然去就熟客，没有熟客的，由刘不才看情形撮合。一时丝竹歌喉，接踵而起，前门轿马后门船，热闹非凡。这番豪举，吸引了无数路人，驻足探望，纷纷探询，是哪位阔客有此手面，等听说是跷脚长根做主人，便有人诧异，不知道他何以忽然有此阔绰的场面。还有个诧异的人，就是跷脚长根自己，一见妙珍那里如此热闹，倒有些不便乱闯，进门拉住一个相帮问道：“是什么人在这里请客？”“咦！李七爷，你这话问得可要叫人好笑？不是我自己跟胡老爷一起请客吗？”跷脚长根明白了，是胡雪岩替他做面子，于是先不进大厅，由备弄绕到后面，把妙珍找了来，细细一问，才知究竟。“对不起，对不起！”跷脚长根走到厅上，握拳作了个罗圈揖，“我做主人的迟到，失礼之至。没有什么说，罚我三杯。”说着，便端起胡雪岩面前的酒杯，连着干了三杯，然后看行辈大小，到席前一一招呼。那番应酬，相当漂亮周到。

By evening the distinguished guests had gathered, at least forty in all, but Changgen, one of the hosts, had still not appeared. Hu was hard pressed to attend to everyone alone, and his private misgivings made it more unpleasant still. “Sister Zhen, where has Changgen gone?” he asked quietly. “Surely you have some idea?” “I can't guess. One of his men came for him early this morning. They spoke privately for a while, and he left without saying anything further. I think we should serve the meal. We can't keep the guests waiting.” Four tables were laid. After much polite yielding over places, everyone sat down. Liu had prepared a list of Tongli's celebrated courtesans and passed it round. Those wishing to summon an acquaintance marked her name. Notes went flying out, and the beauties arrived one after another. Those with familiar customers joined them; Liu matched the others as he thought suitable. Strings, flutes and singing followed in succession, with chairs and horses at the front and boats at the back. The excitement was extraordinary. Passers-by stopped to stare and ask which wealthy guest could put on such a display. When told that Changgen was the host, some wondered how he had suddenly come to such magnificence. Changgen himself was equally surprised. Finding Miaozhen's house so crowded, he hesitated to push his way in and stopped an attendant. “Who's giving this party?” “Why, Seventh Master Li, that's a funny question! Aren't you hosting it with Master Hu?” Changgen understood: Hu was giving him face. Rather than enter the hall at once, he went round a side passage to the back, found Miaozhen and learned the details. Then he entered the hall, clasped his hands and bowed all round. “Forgive me! For a host to arrive late is a grave discourtesy. There is nothing to say. I'll drink three penalty cups.” He took Hu's cup and drained it three times, then greeted the guests in order of seniority. His manners were both graceful and attentive.

盛筵已毕，接着便拉开台子豪赌，安排好了客人，跷脚长根将胡雪岩拉到一边，用埋怨的口气，说道：“老胡，有件事你做得不对了。差点出大乱子！”“怎么？”“你从上海起运洋枪，也该先跟我说一声！”“喔！喔！”胡雪岩急忙认锗：“这是我疏忽。对不起，对不起！”“我今天一早才晓得，忙到下午才算摆平。”于是，跷脚长根透露了他部下的情形，两千七百多人，并非个个都肯听他的指挥，有一批人态势不稳，只是他以大压小，暂时制服着。及至跷脚长根翻然变计，化干戈为玉帛，那一批人便有反他的意思，而且预备依照原定计划硬夺裘丰言所押运的那一船洋枪。幸好，事机不密，为跷脚长根的一个心腹探明究竟，星夜赶来同里，这天一清早将他从妙珍的香衾中唤了起来，赶到青浦与嘉定交界之处，才算截住了那批人。“截是截住了，费了好大的手脚。那船洋枪，已过金山卫，有松江老大的人在，不要紧了。不过……”跷脚长根摇摇头，不愿再说下去。

When the banquet ended, the tables were cleared for serious gambling. Once the guests were settled, Changgen drew Hu aside and spoke reproachfully. “Old Hu, you handled something badly. There was nearly serious trouble.” “What?” “You ought to have told me before shipping the guns out of Shanghai.” “Oh!” Hu hastened to admit his fault. “An oversight. I'm sorry.” “I only learned this morning. It took me until the afternoon to settle things.” He then disclosed the state of his forces. Not all his more than twenty-seven hundred men obeyed him willingly. One group was unreliable, and he had held it down for the moment by the weight of his seniority. When he abruptly changed course and made peace, they turned against him and prepared to follow the original plan, seizing the boatload of guns under Qiu's escort. Fortunately, the plot leaked. One of Changgen's trusted men learned the details, hurried to Tongli through the night and roused him from Miaozhen's bed at dawn. He went to the border between Qingpu and Jiading and managed to intercept them. “I stopped them, but it took a great deal of effort. The guns have passed Jinshanwei now. With the Old Master's men there, they're safe. But...” He shook his head and would say no more.

胡雪岩感激而不安，“李七哥，”他改了称呼，“你帮了我这个大忙，现在你自己有为难之处，该我出力。你说，只要我力量用得上，无不从命。”跷脚长根想了好一会，毅然说道：“你老兄与众不同，我就跟你说实话吧，那批人为头的是我一个‘同参’的徒弟，让我‘做’掉了……”胡雪岩什么事都敢做，什么事都不在乎，只有听见这话，脸色一变，不由得抢着问道：“怎么？你拿他杀掉了？”跷脚长根脸色凝重地点点头。“那么，”胡雪岩失声而言：“他家不要找你算帐？”“照江湖上的规矩，我做得不算错，他不听话，而且这件事关系太大，事情又紧急，我这样做，没有人可以说我不对。不过，公是公，私是私，为了家门的规矩，我不能不做掉他，论到私情，他的后事我不能不料理。”“喔，喔，我懂了，我懂了！好比诸葛亮斩马谡，他‘家有八旬老母’，你不能不管。”胡雪岩略停一下，直截了当地问道：“李七哥，你是不是要铜钱用？”“是的。一面是抚恤，一面有些人嘴里不敢说，心里不肯跟我，我想不如打发掉的好。”“对！这样做倒也干净。”胡雪岩问道：“你要多少？万把银子我现成，再多也有，不过要隔个两三天。”“够了，够了！两千银子抚恤，打发走路的十两银子一个，大概有三百多人，你借我五千银子好了。”

Hu felt both grateful and uneasy. Changing his form of address, he said, “Seventh Brother Li, you've done me a great service. If you're in difficulty now, it's my turn to help. Tell me. Whatever lies within my power, I'll do.” Changgen thought for a long time, then spoke firmly. “You're different from other men. I'll tell you the truth. Their leader was a disciple of one of my fellow initiates. I had him done away with.” Hu would dare almost anything and cared little for most dangers, but these words changed his expression. “What? You killed him?” Changgen nodded gravely. “Then won't his family come to settle accounts?” Hu exclaimed. “By the rules of our world, I wasn't wrong. He disobeyed; the business was too important and too urgent. No one can say I acted improperly. But public duty and private feeling are separate. The rules obliged me to put him to death; personal feeling obliges me to attend to what follows his death.” “I understand. Like Zhuge Liang executing Ma Su, whose family had an eighty-year-old mother—you can't ignore her.” Hu paused, then asked directly, “Do you need money?” “Yes. Compensation for his family, for one thing. Then some men won't speak openly but don't want to stay with me. Better let them go.” “Quite right. That makes a clean settlement. How much? I have ten thousand taels ready. More can be found, but it would take two or three days.” “That's enough. Two thousand for the family, ten taels apiece to those leaving—there are a little over three hundred of them. Lend me five thousand.”

说着，他一跷一拐地走到窗前，取出写局票用的笔砚，很吃力地写了一张借据，字迹歪歪斜斜，措词却很得体：“今借到胡雪岩兄名下纹银五千两整。彼此至好，无保无息，约期三个月归清。特立笔据存照。”下面具名是“李长根”。他在写借据的当儿，胡雪岩已去寻着刘不才，准备好了银数，等回进来，跷脚长根递过那张借据，胡雪岩看都不看，就在蜡烛火上点燃烧掉，“李七哥，我那个合伙做生意的好朋友古应春告诉我，我在丝上赚了一票。自己人有难同当，有福同享，”他将一叠银票递了过去：“你分一万银子的红。”“这，这……”一向精明强干长于词令的跷脚长根不知道怎么说才好。“李七哥！交朋友的日子长得很。”胡雪岩拍拍他的背，微笑着走了。

He limped to the window, took the brush and ink used for writing invitations to courtesans, and laboriously drew up a promissory note. The handwriting was crooked, but the wording entirely proper: “Received on loan from my friend Hu Xueyan the sum of five thousand taels of standard silver. As we are close friends, no guarantor or interest is required. Repayment is agreed within three months. This note is made as evidence.” He signed it “Li Changgen”. While he wrote, Hu found Liu and prepared the money. When he returned and Changgen handed him the note, he did not even look at it, but held it to the candle and burned it. “Seventh Brother Li, my business partner Gu Yingchun tells me I've made a good profit on silk. Our own people should share misfortunes and blessings alike.” He handed over a bundle of banknotes. “Here is your share of the profit: ten thousand taels.” “But...but...” Changgen, normally so shrewd, capable and eloquent, could not find words. “We have many years of friendship ahead.” Hu patted his back and walked away smiling.

这一夜尽欢而散。送走了客人，胡雪岩要用现银开销，妙珍不肯收，因为跷脚长根已有话关照，都归他算。妙珍又说，头钱打了两百多两银子，她亦不好意思再要客人有何花费。胡雪岩只得由她。于是摆上消夜，团团一桌，胡雪岩扶起筷子，先就说了一句：“早点散吧！”“散？”跷脚长根问道：“今天不住在这里？”于是妙珍也劝他留宿，而胡雪岩因有事要连夜赶办，执意不从。妙珠的脸色便不好看了，托词头痛，告个罪离席而去。“这未免煞风景了！”古应春说，“老胡，何苦？”胡雪岩不响，站起身来，去看妙珠，进房就发现她一个人坐要梳妆台前面抹眼泪。“怎么样？”他走过去，扶着她的肩，用服软的声音说道：“是生我的气？”“没有！”妙珠摇摇头。“那么，好端端，淌什么眼泪？”“是我自己心里有感触。”妙珠不胜幽怨地，“生来命苦，吃这碗断命饭！”

The gathering ended in complete enjoyment. After the guests had gone, Hu offered to settle the account in cash, but Miaozhen refused. Changgen had told her to put everything on his account. Besides, she had collected more than two hundred taels in gaming commissions and felt embarrassed to charge her guests anything further. Hu had to let her have her way. A late supper was set out, and they gathered round one table. Taking up his chopsticks, Hu said at once, “Let's finish early.” “Finish?” Changgen asked. “Aren't you staying here tonight?” Miaozhen urged him to stay too, but he insisted that urgent work must be done that night. Miaozhu's face fell. Pleading a headache, she excused herself and left the table. “This rather spoils the occasion,” Gu said. “Old Hu, why do it?” Hu said nothing. He rose and went to see Miaozhu. In her room he found her sitting alone at the dressing table, wiping away tears. “What's wrong?” He rested his hands on her shoulders and spoke gently. “Are you angry with me?” She shook her head. “No.” “Then why these sudden tears?” “My own feelings. I was born to a bitter fate, earning my living in this miserable trade.” Her voice was full of sorrow and resentment.

胡雪岩觉得有些搭不上话，想了想，取出二百两银票塞到她手里说：“明天下午我就回苏州了。这给你买点东西吃。”“我不要！”妙珠将银票往外一推，冷冷答道：“我卖笑不卖眼泪。”这句气话的情分就深了，胡雪岩愣在那里，好半天作声不得。“你请吧！不是说半夜里还有要紧事要办？”“我不骗你。”他改变了办法：“这样，我就在你这里办。你这里有信纸没有？”“间壁就是笺纸店，敲开门来也不要紧。”“那就是了。你叫人去买点顶好的信笺、信封，再沏一壶浓茶，我跟古老爷要商量写信。”胡雪岩又郑重地告诫：“是机密信，所以我先要回家写，此刻在你这里写，你听见了什么，千万不可以说出去。”“你放心！我听都不听。”

Hu could think of no suitable reply. After a moment he took out a two-hundred-tael note and pressed it into her hand. “I return to Suzhou tomorrow afternoon. Buy yourself something to eat with this.” “I don't want it.” She pushed it away. “I sell smiles, not tears.” Beneath the angry words lay a deeper feeling. Hu stood stunned, unable to speak for a long while. “Please go. Didn't you say you had important work to do in the middle of the night?” “I'm not deceiving you.” He changed his approach. “Then I'll do it here. Have you any writing paper?” “There's a stationer's next door. We can knock them up even now.” “Good. Send someone for the best letter paper and envelopes, and make a pot of strong tea. Mr Gu and I must discuss some letters.” He warned her solemnly, “They are confidential. That's why I meant to go home to write them. Since I'm staying here, you must never repeat anything you hear.” “Set your mind at rest. I won't even listen.”
