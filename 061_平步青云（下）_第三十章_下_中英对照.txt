Hu therefore grew grave and warned him, “Brother Pang, you must not act against him yet. You still do not know what tricks he has been playing. You are in the open while he is in the dark; that puts you at a disadvantage. So long as neither of you breaks openly with the other, he will not dare cause flagrant trouble. He knows better than anyone who owes you money and whom you owe. If something goes wrong in the accounts, it may take you a month or two to discover it. I shall merely ask you to take charge of my accounts and examine them carefully for any wrongdoing.” “I cannot oblige you in that. It would not work.” Pang was deeply disappointed. “I know. You are generous to people and do not like to offend them.” “That is not the point. For your sake, Brother Pang, I would willingly offend someone. But it must achieve something. Why offend a man for no benefit? Hear me out.”

胡雪岩有三点理由，第一，怕打草惊蛇，反逼得朱福年去舞弊使坏，第二，庞二手下用的人很多，就算要换朱福年，也该从伙计当中去挑选替手，徐图整顿，此刻弄个不相干的人去查帐，仿佛看大家都靠不住，是跟朱福年走在一条路上，通同作弊，岂不令人寒心？第三，胡雪岩也实在抽不出那许多工夫替他专办这件事。“而况，我对你那方面的情形又不清楚，贸贸然下手，一年半载不能完事，在我有没有工夫，且不去说它，就怕一年半载下来，查不出名堂，那时你做东家的，对伙计如何交代？”“这没有什么！我现在可以断定，朱福年一定有毛病。”“毛病可以弥补的……”“对啊！”庞二抢着说道：“只要你一去，他看见厉害的人来了，赶紧想法子把他的毛病弥补起来，你不就帮了我的大忙了吗？”这话倒也驳他不倒。胡雪岩想了一会，总觉得庞二的做法，不甚妥当，就算将朱福年的毛病查出来了，甚至于照庞二的如意算盘，把胡雪岩三个字抬了出去，就能叫朱福年敛迹，弥补弊病，然而以后还用不用他呢？这样想着，便问出口来：“庞二哥，这朱某人的本事到底怎么样？”“本事是有的。”“如果他肯改过，实实在在替你办事，你还用不用他？”“如果是这佯，当然可以用。不过……”他摇摇头，觉得说下去就没有味道了。

Hu had three reasons. First, he feared that raising the alarm would drive Zhu Funian into fraud or deliberate mischief. Second, Pang employed many men. Even if Zhu were to be replaced, a successor should be chosen from among the staff and improvements made gradually. To bring in an outsider to inspect the accounts would suggest that everyone was untrustworthy and in league with Zhu. Would that not dishearten them? Third, Hu really could not spare the time to devote himself to the task. “Besides, I know nothing of the circumstances of your business. If I plunge in unprepared, it might take six months or a year. Leave aside whether I can spare the time. Suppose that after all those months I find nothing definite. How would you, as their employer, explain yourself to the staff?” “That does not matter. I am now certain there is something wrong with Zhu.” “Irregularities can be made good—” “Exactly!” Pang interrupted. “Once you arrive, he will see that a formidable man has come and hurry to put his irregularities right. Would that not be a great service to me?” It was difficult to refute this. Hu thought for a while, but still felt that Pang’s approach was unsatisfactory. Even if he uncovered Zhu’s misconduct—or if, as Pang hoped, merely invoking Hu Xueyan’s name made Zhu mend his ways and repair the damage—would Pang continue to employ him afterwards? He put the question aloud. “Brother Pang, how capable is this man Zhu?” “He has ability.” “If he were willing to reform and serve you honestly, would you still employ him?” “In that case, of course. But—” Pang shook his head, feeling that there was little point in continuing.

“我懂你的意思。”胡雪岩停了一下说，“人不对，请他走路。这是普通人的做法，你庞二哥要么不出马，一出马就要叫人晓得厉害，佩服你确是有一套。”这两句话，最配争强好胜的纨绔脾气，所以庞二精神一振，有了笑容。“老胡，你这两句话我交关听得进。你倒再说说看，应该怎么做法？”“要象诸葛亮‘七擒孟获’那样，‘火烧藤甲兵’不足为奇，要烧得他服帖，死心塌地替你出力，才算本事。”“话是一点都不错，不过，”庞二踌躇着说，“我实在没有这份本事。”说到这里，突然眼睛一亮，拍着自己的后脑勺：“我真糊涂了！现成的诸葛亮在这里。老胡，”他停了一下，喜逐颜开地又说，“我送你股份，你算是跟我台伙，也是老板的身分，名正言顺来管事，不就可以收服朱福年了吗？”胡雪岩的打算就是如此，不过自己说不出口，难得庞二的想法相同。光就是这一点，使值得替他出一番力了。胡雪岩有项过人的长处，能在心血来潮之际，作出重要而正确的决定，思路快不足为奇，能快又能细致深刻，就只有他有此本事。

“I understand what you mean.” Hu paused. “If a man is no good, dismiss him: that is the ordinary way. But you, Brother Pang, should either stay out of the field or, once you enter it, make everyone recognize your strength and admire your methods.” This appealed perfectly to the competitive pride of a wealthy young gentleman. Pang brightened and smiled. “Old Hu, that is exactly the sort of thing I like to hear. Tell me more. What should I do?” “Be like Zhuge Liang, capturing Meng Huo seven times. Burning the troops in rattan armour was nothing remarkable. The real achievement was to make him submit wholeheartedly and serve with complete devotion.” “You are quite right. But I really have no such ability.” Suddenly Pang’s eyes lit up. He slapped the back of his head. “What a fool I am! Here is Zhuge Liang, ready to hand. Old Hu”—he paused, then continued with a delighted smile—“I shall give you shares. You will be my partner, an owner yourself, with every right to take charge. Then you can bring Zhu Funian to heel!” This was precisely what Hu had intended, though he could hardly propose it himself. That Pang had reached the same idea was reason enough to make a considerable effort on his behalf. Hu possessed an exceptional gift: on the spur of the moment, he could make important decisions and make them correctly. Quick thinking alone was nothing extraordinary. To think quickly while remaining thorough and penetrating was his particular strength.

此刻便是这样。因为庞二先作提议，就是个极好的机会，他抓住了题目的精义，立即使有一篇好文章交卷。“庞二哥，”他正色说道，“生意是生意！分花红彼此礼让，是交朋友的情分、义气，不可一概而论。我是不赞成吃干股这一套花佯的，如果你看得起我，愿意让我搭点股份，我交现银出来。““好啊！”庞二欣然同意，因为这一来，胡雪岩就更加出力。他问：“你想要多少股子？”“我的实力比你差得远，只能来个两成。”“一句话！我们重新盘过，你十万，我四十万，我们五十万银子下手，上海的市面，可以捏在手里了。”“准定如此，庞二哥，”胡雪岩带点兴奋的神色，“我的钱庄，你也来点股子。索性大家滚在一起，有福同享，有难同当，人看好不好？”“怎么不好！礼尚往来，再好不过！而且便宜不落外方，你在上海立一爿分号起来，我们自己的款了存在自己的钱庄里，岂不方便？”胡雪岩的打算就是如此，他还有进一步的打算，此刻却不宜先露，只是连连称“是”。接着又说定庞二的股份，真个礼尚往来，他也是十万，彼此只要立个合伙的合同，划一笔帐，都不必另拨现银。

So it was now. Pang’s own proposal had presented an excellent opportunity. Hu grasped its essential point and immediately had his response ready. “Brother Pang, business is business,” he said seriously. “Giving one another a share of profits is a matter of friendship and loyalty. The two must not be confused. I do not approve of holding shares without putting in capital. If you think well enough of me to let me take a stake, I shall pay for it in silver.” “Excellent!” Pang readily agreed: this would give Hu still more reason to work hard. “How large a stake would you like?” “My means are far below yours. I could manage only two tenths.” “Agreed! We shall make a fresh reckoning. You put in a hundred thousand taels and I four hundred thousand. Starting with half a million, we can hold the Shanghai market in our hands.” “Then that is settled.” Hu looked a little excited. “Brother Pang, you must take a stake in my bank too. Let us join our interests completely, sharing good fortune and hardship alike. What do you say?” “Why not? Reciprocity could not be better. And the benefit will stay among ourselves. Establish a branch in Shanghai, and we can keep our own money in our own bank. How convenient!” This was exactly Hu’s plan. He had something further in mind, but it was too early to reveal it, so he merely agreed repeatedly. They then settled Pang’s contribution: in true reciprocity, it would also be a hundred thousand taels. All they needed was a partnership agreement and a transfer in the books; neither would have to send over additional cash.

他们谈得津津有味，外面却等得心急了，酒已经回烫过两遍，再烫就要走味，怡情老二推门望到第三遍，看他们还没有住口的样子，忍不住便轻轻咳嗽了一声。这下才惊醒了庞二，歉然说道：“对不起，对不起，害他们久等了，我们出去吧！”等坐定下来，第一件事是叫局。怡情老二亲自捧过一只长方红木托盘，里面是笔砚局票，拈笔在手，先问庞二。“我好久没有到上海来了，市面不灵。”他想了想说：“叫宝琴老三吧？”“是怡红院的宝琴老三吗？”怡情老二问。“对了。怡红院。”“这一节不做了。”怡情老二说，“节前嫁了个道台，做官太太去了。”于是庞二又想了两个人，非常不巧，不是从良，便是开了码头，他不免怅惘，说一声：“随便找好了！”“你替庞二少做个媒吧。”尤五对怡情老二说了这一句，便又转脸问庞二：“喜欢啥样子的？”“脾气爽快的好。”“有了！”怡情老二喜滋滋他说，“我替庞二少保荐一个，包管中意。”这个人叫怡云老七，就在怡情院“铺房间”，她怕庞二以为她有意照应小姐妹，不管好歹，硬塞给他，所以只说名字，不说地方。刘不才会意，也不多问，将一叠局票写好，交给“相帮”发了出去。

They were absorbed in their talk, but the people outside were growing impatient. The wine had already been warmed twice; another warming would spoil its flavour. When Second Miss opened the door for the third time and saw no sign of their stopping, she could not resist a discreet cough. Only then did Pang come to himself. “My apologies! We have kept everyone waiting. Let us go out.” Once they were seated, the first business was to summon the courtesans. Second Miss personally brought a long rosewood tray holding brush, inkstone, and invitation cards. Taking up the brush, she asked Pang first. “I have not been to Shanghai for a long time and am out of touch.” He thought for a moment. “Shall we ask for Third Miss Baoqin?” “Third Miss Baoqin of Yihong House?” “Yes, Yihong House.” “She is no longer working this season. Before the festival she married a circuit intendant and became an official’s wife.” Pang thought of two more women, but his luck was poor: they had either married out of the profession or moved elsewhere to work. A little wistfully, he said, “Find anyone you like.” “Make a match for Second Master Pang,” You Laowu told Second Miss, then turned to Pang. “What sort do you like?” “Someone with a frank, easy temper.” “I have just the one!” Second Miss said happily. “Let me recommend someone. I guarantee you will like her.” The woman was called Seventh Miss Yiyun and had rooms at Yiqing House itself. Second Miss feared Pang might suppose she was pushing a sister of the house on him merely to help her, regardless of suitability. She therefore gave only the name, not the address. Liu understood and asked no questions. He filled out a stack of invitations and gave them to an attendant to dispatch.

隔不多久，莲步姗姗进来一个丽人，鹅蛋脸，高身材，长眉入鬓，神采飞扬，是那种一见便能令人目眩神移的尤物。在座的人都没有见过她，她却全认得，含笑一一招呼，最后才在庞二身后坐下，未曾开口，先抛媚眼，然后轻声说道：“二少，长远不见了！”“原来你们是老相好！”刘不才起哄，“庞二哥怎不早说？罚酒，罚酒。”“你看！”庞二对怡云老七说，“你一来就害我罚酒。我们啥地方见过？我怎么想不起来？““在怡红院。二少，你自然想不起了，一则贵人多忘事，二则也看我不上眼。”庞二将牙一龇，故意说道：“好酸！”“庞二哥，你不要假惺惺装不认识。这杯酒非罚不可！”刘不才将一杯酒端了过来。庞二顺手就端向怡云老七，意思是要她代酒，怡云老七毫无难色，一仰脸干了那杯酒。“谢谢！”庞二开始有了笑容。于是怡云老七执壶敬酒，酒量很好，一个个都照了杯，最后是自己喝了半杯酒，剩下的半杯敬庞二，却又温柔地问：“嫌不嫌脏？”杯沿脂痕宛然，美人余泽，脏之何有？庞二笑嘻嘻地干了酒，大家也都相视而笑，笑庞二是如此容易地掉入怡云老七的罗网中。

Before long a beauty entered with graceful steps: tall, with an oval face, long eyebrows sweeping towards her temples, and a radiant liveliness that could dazzle a man at first sight. None of those present had seen her before, yet she knew them all and greeted each with a smile. Finally she sat behind Pang. Before speaking, she cast him an enticing glance, then said softly, “Second Master, it has been such a long time!” “So you are old sweethearts!” Liu raised a clamour. “Why did you not tell us, Brother Pang? A penalty cup!” “You see?” Pang told Yiyun. “You have only just arrived, and already you have landed me with a drinking penalty. Where have we met? Why can I not remember?” “At Yihong House. Naturally you would not remember. Important men have short memories, and besides, you never thought me worth a glance.” Pang bared his teeth deliberately. “How sour!” “Brother Pang, stop pretending not to recognize her. You must drink this cup.” Liu brought him the wine. Pang promptly held it out to Yiyun, asking her to drink on his behalf. Without the least reluctance, she tipped back her head and emptied it. “Thank you!” Pang began to smile. She then took the wine pot and toasted the company. Her capacity was excellent; she drank with each man in turn, showing her empty cup. Finally she drank half a cup herself and offered the remaining half to Pang, asking tenderly, “Do you mind its being soiled?” Rouge marked the rim: what could be dirty about a beauty’s lingering traces? Pang drank it with a delighted smile. The others exchanged smiles too, amused at how easily he had fallen into her net.

“你住在哪里？”庞二悄然相问。“ 等下告诉你。”他还想说什么，只听门帘响动，胡雪岩和刘不才叫的局，陆续到了。为求热闹，叫得不少，片刻之间，莺莺燕燕，翩然群集，猜拳的猜拳，唱戏的唱戏，因为庞二是主客，自然都应酬他，左顾右应，忙得不可开交。叫的局来了又去，川流不息，怡云老七却始终不动，娘姨拿进一叠局票，悄悄塞了过来，她看都不看、就交了回去，只说得一声：“随它去！”这一下反倒使得庞二过意不去了，“你管你出局去！”他说，“回头我们‘翻台，过来。你住得远不远？”“是真的要翻台过来？”“这，我骗你干什么？”怡云老七笑一笑不响，却依然坐着不动。“你先回去，预备预备，我们就过去。”“叫我回哪里去？”怡云老七用手一指，“喏，前厢房就是我的房间。”“原来你也在这里！”庞二顿觉意外，“为啥早不说？”“现在说也不晚。”怡云老七越发坐近了，手扳着他的肩，低声说道：“翻来翻去，都在一处地方。尤五少的面子，你就在这里多坐一会。回头到我那里去消夜好不好？”这便是一种暗示，有身分的“红棺人”，通常是不肯作此露骨的表示的，所以庞二颇为高兴。

“Where do you live?” Pang asked quietly. “I shall tell you later.” He wanted to say more, but the door curtain stirred. The women summoned by Hu and Liu began arriving one after another. To make the party lively, they had invited quite a number. Soon the room was filled with graceful, chattering beauties: some played drinking games, others sang opera. Pang was the guest of honour, so all attended to him, and he scarcely knew which way to turn. The summoned women came and went in an endless stream, but Yiyun remained. A maid slipped her a stack of invitation cards. Without looking at them, she handed them back. “Never mind them.” This made Pang feel guilty. “Do go to your engagements,” he said. “We shall move the party to your place afterwards. Is it far away?” “Do you really mean to come?” “Why should I deceive you?” She smiled without answering and remained seated. “Go back and make your preparations. We shall follow.” “Where do you want me to go?” She pointed. “Those rooms in the front wing are mine.” “So you live here too!” Pang was surprised. “Why did you not say so?” “It is not too late to say it now.” She moved closer, rested a hand on his shoulder, and lowered her voice. “Wherever we move, it is all the same house. For Fifth Master You’s sake, stay here a little longer. Afterwards, will you come to my rooms for supper?” This was a hint of the kind that a sought-after courtesan with a reputation to maintain would not ordinarily make so openly. Pang was therefore particularly pleased.

他们低眉垂眼，款款深谈的神情，都落入旁人眼中，也猜得到他们已有密约，所以为了予人方便，作主人的竟一反常例，提议早早散席，理由是因为怕庞二在路上辛苦了，需要早早休息。“多谢关切！”庞二指着怡云老七说，“我答应到她那里宵夜。大家一起过去坐一息。”怡云老七唯恐客人推辞，抢着先拜托怡情老二：“二阿姐，你替我讲一声，请各位老爷，赏我个面子。”直待大家都答应了，怡云老七方始匆匆赶回自己房间去准备。等庞二陪春客人一到，已经准备停当，虽是消夜，依然丰盛，还特地用了一副“银家伙”，开了一小坛十年陈的“竹叶青”，此外果盘茶烟，无不精美，这又合了庞二的脾胃，脸上飞了金似地，相当得意。“明天原班人马在这里，我不发帖子了。”“好的。”刘不才说，“后天该我……”“不行！刘三哥！你再让我两天，后天、大后天仍旧应该是我的，还是在这里。”阔客捧场，也要有个规矩，所以刘不才问道：“明天算是庞二哥还席，后天、大后天算是啥名堂？““我跟老胡的交情，还席可以摆在后头……”照庞二的说法，明天是他诚意结交新朋友，专请尤五和古应春，后天则是酬谢刘不才，在南浔替他照料宾客，大后天才是不胡雪岩的席，花丛哄饮，能够说得出道理，没有不凑兴的道理，因而大家都答应了，然后又徘定次序，接下来是刘、古、尤三人做主人。庞二的兴致极好，还要叫局，只是大家都说良朋良夜，清谈最好，只把抬情老二找了来，浅斟低酌，又消磨了一个时辰，方始兴尽而散。当然，这一夜的庞二是不会再回一品香了。

Their lowered eyes and intimate conversation had not escaped the others, who could guess that they had made a private arrangement. To oblige them, their hosts departed from the usual practice and proposed ending the banquet early, on the grounds that Pang must be tired from his journey and needed rest. “Thank you for your concern!” Pang pointed to Yiyun. “I have promised to take supper at her place. Come over with me and sit a while.” Afraid that the guests might decline, Yiyun quickly appealed to Second Miss. “Second Sister, please speak for me. Ask the gentlemen to honour me.” Only when all had agreed did she hurry back to her rooms to prepare. By the time Pang arrived with his friends, everything was ready. Though it was only supper, the spread was lavish. She had brought out a set of silverware and opened a little jar of ten-year-old Zhuyeqing wine. The fruit, tea, and tobacco were all of the finest quality. This suited Pang perfectly. His face seemed to shine with gold, so pleased was he. “The same company here tomorrow. I shall send no formal invitations.” “Very well,” Liu said. “The day after tomorrow is my turn—” “No, Brother Liu! Give me another two days. The day after tomorrow and the following day must still be mine, and here again.” Even a wealthy patron’s generosity needed a proper form, so Liu asked, “Tomorrow can be your return banquet. But what are the next two occasions?” “With Old Hu and me, returning his hospitality can wait.” Pang’s explanation was that tomorrow he wished sincerely to cultivate new friends, so You and Gu would be his particular guests. The next day he would thank Liu for entertaining his visitors at Nanxun. Only on the third day would he return Hu’s hospitality. At a drinking party among the flowers, any plausible reason deserved support. Everyone agreed, then fixed the order afterwards: Liu, Gu, and You would host in turn. Pang was in excellent spirits and wanted to summon more women, but the others said that with good friends on such a fine night, quiet conversation was best. They brought in only Second Miss, sipped their wine, and passed another two hours before dispersing at last. Naturally, Pang would not return to the Yipinxiang Inn that night.

第二天午后，刘不才听从胡雪岩的指挥，特地去陪伴庞二。胡雪岩则与古庆春和尤五在裕记丝栈谈了一下午，听说了庞二与他昨天所谈的话，尤、古二人大为兴奋。能够与庞二合作，无论讲声势、讲实力，都是十分有利的事，尤其是在上海设一爿钱庄，现成有五十万银子这么个大户头作往来，这个局面的开展，是件非同小可的事。不过障碍也不是没有，“朱福年多年耕耘，视庞二的事业如禁膏，肯拱手上让人吗？”古应春怀着浓重的疑惑。“小爷叔，”尤五也说，“你在庞二面前已夸下口了，要‘七擒孟获’，我倒要问问，怎么个擒法？”“用不着七擒！”胡雪岩说：“昨天我在床上就想好了办法，要下一着狠棋。五哥，同兴的档手你熟不熟？”“你是说同兴钱庄？”尤五答道：“档手姓邵，镇江人，我不熟，不过我可以托朋友去说话。”“说要我自己来说，不有让第三者知道。你能不能托人介绍，大家见一面？”“这不难。你想要啥时候见面？”“越快越好。”“今天晚上就可以。应春，”尤五转脸说道，“你替我写封信给华佩卿。”

The following afternoon, at Hu’s direction, Liu went specially to keep Pang company. Hu spent the afternoon at Yuji Silk Warehouse with Gu and You. Both were greatly excited to hear what Pang and Hu had discussed. Cooperation with Pang would bring substantial advantages in influence and resources alike. In particular, opening a bank in Shanghai with a half-million-tael business ready to become a major customer would create an opportunity of immense consequence. There were obstacles, however. “Zhu Funian has worked at this for years and regards Pang’s business as his private preserve. Will he hand it over meekly?” Gu was deeply doubtful. “Little Uncle,” You added, “you have already promised Pang that you will capture Meng Huo seven times. How do you mean to do it?” “There is no need for seven captures. I worked out a method in bed last night. We must make a ruthless move. Brother Five, do you know the manager at Tongxing?” “You mean Tongxing Bank? His name is Shao, and he comes from Zhenjiang. I do not know him, but I can ask friends to approach him.” “I must speak to him myself. No third person can know about it. Can you get someone to introduce us?” “That is easy enough. When would you like to meet?” “The sooner the better.” “This evening would be possible.” You turned to Gu. “Yingchun, write a letter to Hua Peiqing for me.”

古应春也认识华佩卿，他是个书贾，跟北京的琉璃厂有联络，以前在江南旧家收买了善本古书，总是搭松江帮的漕船进京，所以跟尤五颇有交情。古应春跟他相识，就是从尤五的关系上来的。“今天晚上要应酬庞二。请他约一约，明天中午见面如何？”“随便你。”于是古应春用尤五的名义给华佩卿写了信，立即派“出店”送去。信上注明：“即晚候玉”，而回信在他们到怡情院赴约以前就收到了。华佩卿很热心，回信中说，接到信他立即照办，找到了同兴的档手邵仲甫，说明经过。邵仲甫也知道有胡雪岩这么一位同业，仰慕已久，乐于相交。不过他明天中午有个“非践不可之约”，所以华佩卿已经跟他约好，第二天上午吃早茶，由华佩卿作东。介绍认识以后，胡雪岩要跟邵仲甫单独相谈，“自行面约可也”。

Gu knew Hua Peiqing too. He was a bookseller with connections in Beijing’s Liulichang district. In the past, when he bought rare old editions from established Jiangnan families, he had always sent them to the capital on the Songjiang brotherhood’s grain boats. He was therefore on friendly terms with You, through whom Gu had met him. “We have to entertain Pang this evening. Could you ask Hua to arrange a meeting for tomorrow at noon?” “As you please.” Gu wrote in You’s name, and a messenger was sent off at once. The letter said, “Your reply is awaited this evening,” and it arrived before they left for the banquet at Yiqing House. Hua had been most obliging. On receiving the letter he had immediately sought out Tongxing’s manager, Shao Zhongfu, and explained the request. Shao already knew of Hu as a fellow banker and had long admired him; he would gladly make his acquaintance. Unfortunately, he had an engagement at noon the next day that he could not fail to keep. Hua had therefore arranged to host morning tea instead. Once they had been introduced, Hu could make his own appointment for a private conversation with Shao.

* * *名为“吃早茶”，其实是约在一家扬帮馆子里。扬州人早晨这一顿很讲究，先拿肴肉、干丝来吃酒，然后点过桥面，“浇头”也先炒出来下酒。主客一共四个人，胡雪岩是由尤五陪着去的，四碗面两样花色，炒出来两大盘浇头，一盘虾腰，一盘“马鞍桥”，华佩卿不断劝客，十分殷勤。彼此都是“外场人物”，做生意又讲究和气亲热，不似官场中人矜持，所以胡雪岩跟邵仲甫第一遭相见，就很熟了。尤五看华佩卿健谈而又健啖，这顿早酒，着实要消磨些工夫，便向胡雪岩使个眼色：“你跟邵先生有话，就这里借个地方谈谈，岂不省事？”“对，对！你们两位尽管请便，我跟尤五哥好久不见，也要叙叙。”于是一桌化做两桌，胡雪岩跟邵仲甫另外在僻静角落坐定，喝茶密谈。在这一顿点心的工夫中，胡雪岩对邵仲甫的性情，已有了解，不善言词而是心有丘壑的人，这路人物比较讲实际，动以利害则自能分辨，所以他决定开门见山，实话直说。

Though called “morning tea,” the meeting was at a restaurant serving Yangzhou food. Yangzhou people took considerable care over their morning meal. They began by drinking wine with jellied meat and finely shredded bean curd, then ordered guoqiao noodles. The toppings were cooked first and also served to accompany the wine. There were four men in all, for You had come with Hu. Their four bowls of noodles had two different toppings, brought on two large platters: shrimps and kidneys on one, and a dish called “Saddle Bridges” on the other. Hua urged them to eat with great hospitality. They were all men accustomed to dealing with the world, and business encouraged warmth and friendliness rather than the reserve of officials. Hu and Shao therefore felt at ease together at this first meeting. Seeing that Hua was as hearty an eater as he was a talker, You realized that the morning drinking would occupy some time. He signalled to Hu. “You have something to discuss with Mr Shao. Why not find a place here? It would save trouble.” “Yes, by all means!” Hua agreed. “Brother You and I have not met for ages. We have plenty to talk about too.” One table thus became two. Hu and Shao settled in a quiet corner to talk privately over tea. During the meal Hu had already formed an idea of Shao’s character. He was no eloquent talker, but a man with well-considered plans of his own. Such men were practical and could judge their interests clearly. Hu decided to come straight to the point.

“仲甫兄，”他问，“宝号跟宠家的‘恒记，有往来？”“是的。”邵仲甫答道，“我们做往来，不是一年了。”“那以后还要请你多帮忙。”胡雪岩说，“庞家二少爷已经到了上海，你总见过面了。”“还没有。约了今天中午见面。”胡雪岩心里明白，所谓“非践不可之约”，就是跟庞二见面。照此看来，他对庞二的重视，又不言可知，然则自己动以利害的打算，越显得不错，不过，胡雪岩灵机一动，改变了主意，“这样说，我们中午还要见面。”他说，“我有几句话，不妨明后天再谈。”邵仲甫跟恒记有多年的关系，所以跟恒记有往来的客户，大致也都了解，就没有听说过有胡雪岩在内。然而照他此刻的话来看，似乎跟庞二很熟，与恒记在生意上有密切的牵连，岂不费解？既为了生意上的关切，也为了好奇，邵仲甫何能置而不问，“雪岩兄，我们一见如故，有话尽说不妨！”他用套交情的方式来套话，“何必等到明后天？”在胡雪岩原是盘马弯弓，有意要引起邵仲甫的注意，见他这副神情，便知已经入彀，不妨略为透露，于是很快地答道：“原是一见如故，我才跟仲甫兄谈到深处。庞二哥是我的好朋友，最近进一步谈到彼此合伙。当然，恒记是以他为主，听他跟你老兄是怎么说，我们再细谈。彼此同业，要讲义气，没有不好谈的。”

“Brother Zhongfu,” he asked, “does your bank do business with the Pang family’s Hengji?” “Yes. We have dealt with them for years.” “Then I shall need your help in future too. Second Master Pang has reached Shanghai. I imagine you have seen him?” “Not yet. I am meeting him at noon today.” Hu understood: this was the engagement that could not be missed. The importance Shao attached to Pang was plain, confirming Hu’s judgment that an appeal to his interests would be effective. Then an idea struck him, and he changed his approach. “In that case we shall meet again at noon. What I have to say can wait until tomorrow or the next day.” Through his long connection with Hengji, Shao knew most of the people with whom it did business, but had never heard of Hu among them. Yet Hu now spoke as though he were close to Pang and intimately concerned with Hengji’s affairs. It was puzzling. Both his business interests and his curiosity made it impossible to let the matter rest. “Brother Xueyan, we feel like old friends at our first meeting. Speak freely.” He used a profession of friendship to draw Hu out. “Why wait until tomorrow or the next day?” Hu had deliberately held his bow drawn to attract Shao’s attention. Seeing his expression, he knew that the bait had taken and could reveal a little. “It is precisely because we feel like old friends that I am speaking to you so intimately. Brother Pang is a good friend of mine, and recently we have gone a step further and discussed a partnership. Naturally, he remains the principal man in Hengji. Once I know what he has said to you, we can talk in detail. As fellow bankers, we must honour our obligations to one another. There is nothing we cannot discuss.”

这几句话闪闪烁烁，越引人关切，邵仲甫拿他的话，一个字一个字地体味了一遍，有些明白了，既然他们合伙，则庞二跟钱城有银钱往来，自然要问问做钱庄的胡雪岩的意见，最后讲的两句话，就是这个意思。恒记是同兴的大户，也是一根台柱，如果这根台柱一抽走，后果不堪设想。虽然胡雪岩的话，靠得住靠不住，尚待求证，但宁可信其有，不可信其无，难得他有讲同业义气的善意表示，不正好拉近了交情？“好极了！庞二少有你搭档，将来做出来的市面不得了，雪岩兄，”他急转直下他说，“我是久仰大才，也久仰阜康的信誉，大树底下好乘凉，想沾你老兄一点光，不晓得肯不肯照应照应我们？”“好说，好说，请吩咐！只要力量够得上，决不推辞。”“我是想，同兴跟阜康做个联号，不晓是高攀得上，高攀不上？”对这个提议，胡雪岩倒有些意外之感，暗暗佩服邵仲甫的手腕也不坏，做成联号，则恒记跟同兴的往来，也就等于跟阜康往来，他考虑了一下答道：“只怕阜康高攀不上。仲甫兄，我说句实话，现在丝生意是我自己管，钱庄都托了一个刘姓朋友，你老兄晓得的，东家未见得都了解，全盘情形，都在档手肚子里。彼此联手，我完全赞成，不过先要问一问我那个刘朋友，我写信叫他上来，大家一起谈好不好？““是的。做事情是应该如此。”“就这样说了。”胡雪岩假意掏出表来看了一下：“我还有个约会，先走一步，中午再碰头。”

These elusive remarks made Shao still more attentive. He weighed every word and began to understand. If Hu and Pang became partners, Pang would naturally consult Hu, himself a banker, about Hengji’s banking arrangements. That was the meaning of the last two sentences. Hengji was a major customer and one of Tongxing’s main supports. Losing it would have unthinkable consequences. Whether Hu’s claims could be trusted still needed confirmation, but it was safer to believe them than not. And since he had expressed goodwill towards a fellow banker, was this not a chance to draw closer? “Excellent! With you as his partner, Second Master Pang will build an extraordinary business. Brother Xueyan”—Shao abruptly changed direction—“I have long admired your ability and Fukang’s reputation. It is pleasant to sit in the shade of a great tree. I should like to share a little of your good fortune. Might you be willing to look after us?” “You are too kind. Tell me what you wish. If it is within my power, I shall certainly not refuse.” “I wondered whether Tongxing might become an associate bank of Fukang. I do not know whether we could aspire to such a connection.” The proposal took Hu somewhat by surprise, and he privately admired Shao’s skill. If the banks became associates, Hengji’s dealings with Tongxing would effectively be dealings with Fukang too. After considering it, he answered, “It is Fukang that may not be worthy of the connection. To tell you frankly, brother, I manage the silk business myself, but have entrusted the bank to a friend named Liu. You know how it is: an owner may not understand everything. The manager has the whole business in his head. I thoroughly approve of our joining forces, but I must first consult my friend Liu. Shall I write and ask him to come up, so that we can all discuss it?” “Certainly. That is the proper way to conduct business.” “Then we shall leave it at that.” Hu made a show of taking out his watch. “I have another appointment and must go. We shall meet again at noon.”

于是胡雪岩站起身来，向华佩卿道了谢，与尤五告辞出门，一起赶到怡情院，庞二刚穿好衣服，预备到一品香去会见约好了的人。“二哥！”胡雪岩将他拉到一边，悄然问道：“你今天中午是不是约了同兴的邵仲甫见面？”“是啊！你怎么知道？”“我跟他刚见了面。”胡雪岩以郑重的神色，低声说道：“恒记跟同兴的往来，都由朱福年经手，我先要拿同兴方面稳往，以防万一。”“不错，不错！你的心思真细。”庞二说道：“谈得怎么样？”“没有深谈，因为恒记到底是你的事业，要你作主。我告诉他，要先听你怎么说，我才能跟他进一步谈。”这两句话中，一方面表示尊重庞二，一方面也是为他自己表白，并无喧宾夺主的意思。同时也在暗示，需将双方的关系，公开向邵仲甫说明。措词相当巧妙，而丝毫不着痕迹。庞二深为满意，不知不党中便由胡雪岩牵着鼻子走了。

Hu rose, thanked Hua, and took his leave with You. Together they hurried to Yiqing House. Pang had just dressed and was preparing to go to the Yipinxiang Inn to meet the people he had invited. “Brother Pang!” Hu drew him aside. “Are you meeting Shao Zhongfu of Tongxing at noon?” “Yes. How did you know?” “I have just seen him.” Hu lowered his voice and grew serious. “All Hengji’s dealings with Tongxing pass through Zhu Funian. I must first make sure of Tongxing’s position, as a precaution.” “Quite right! You think of everything. How did the talk go?” “We did not go into detail. Hengji is your business, after all, and you must decide. I told him I had to hear what you said before I could discuss it further.” These two sentences both showed respect for Pang and assured him that Hu had no intention of usurping his authority. They also hinted that Pang needed to make their new relationship clear to Shao. The wording was skilful without showing any trace of artifice. Pang was delighted and, without realizing it, allowed Hu to lead him by the nose.

“好的。回头我们一起吃饭，我当面跟邵仲甫说。时候不早了，一起走吧。”到了一品香，已有好些人在等。包括朱福年在内，一见胡雪岩跟庞二在一起，他的脸色一变。庞二不曾发觉，胡雪岩是见如不见，神色不动地跟他寒暄，说前天请他作陪，未见赏光，深为遗憾。朱福年当然也有几句致歉的话，只是神色之间，不免忸怩。由这一番周旋，便看出朱福年其实不是什么厉害角色，因而越有自信必可将他收服。“福年！”庞二打发走了一些不相干的访客，招招手说：“你请过来，我有件事告诉你。”庞二住的是一进五间屋子，将朱福年找到最东面那一间，谈了好半天，才见朱福年出来，脸上的气色越发难看了，但对胡雪岩却又不能不敷衍。“胡先生，刚才二少爷跟我说了，说胡先生有大股份加到恒记来。”他极力装出欣幸的神情，“好极，好极！以后要请胡先生多教导。”“不敢当，不敢当。”胡雪岩很恳切地，但说话已有老板的味道：“老兄在恒记多年，将来着实还要借重。”听得这一说，朱福年的脸色好看了些，赔着笑敷衍了一会。胡雪岩以话套话，将庞二跟他说的话，都打听了出来，果然说的是“大股份”。显然的，这是为了让他好受恒记的同人着重，有意这么说，庞二真的很够交情。

“Very well. We shall have lunch together, and I shall tell Shao myself. It is getting late. Let us go.” At the Yipinxiang Inn, several people were already waiting, Zhu among them. At the sight of Hu beside Pang, his expression changed. Pang did not notice; Hu noticed but behaved as though he had not. He greeted Zhu calmly and said how sorry he had been that Zhu could not join them at dinner the day before yesterday. Zhu naturally offered apologies, though he could not conceal his awkwardness. This exchange showed Hu that Zhu was not really such a formidable man after all, strengthening his confidence that he could bring him to heel. After dismissing some unrelated visitors, Pang beckoned. “Funian, come here. I have something to tell you.” Pang occupied a suite of five rooms. He took Zhu into the easternmost one, and they talked for a considerable time. When Zhu emerged, his colour was worse than ever, though he still had to be polite to Hu. “Mr Hu, Second Master has just told me that you are bringing a substantial stake into Hengji.” He strained to look delighted. “Excellent! In future I shall depend on your guidance.” “You are too kind.” Hu sounded sincere, but already spoke as an owner. “You have been at Hengji for years, brother. We shall have much to rely on you for.” Zhu’s expression eased a little, and he continued the courtesies with a conciliatory smile. Drawing one remark out of another, Hu learnt what Pang had said. He had indeed spoken of a “substantial stake.” Clearly Pang had deliberately put it that way so that Hengji’s staff would give Hu due weight. He was a loyal friend indeed.

* * *由邵仲甫作东，吃了一顿丰盛的“番菜”，庞二要陪怡云老七到洋行里去买首饰衣料，匆匆走了，主人留胡雪岩在原处喝“英国红茶”，有话要谈。在邵仲甫面前，庞二也说胡雪岩在恒记有大股份，因而他的神态也显得跟第一次见面不同，连称呼也改过了，不是称兄道弟，而是叫“胡先生”。“胡先生！”他说，“我有句话请教，刚刚庞二少爷关照，以后恒记跟同兴往来，归胡先生你经手，那么，朱福年来说的话，算不算数？“一下子问到要害上，胡雪岩不敢轻率回答，先反问一句：“是什么话？”“恒记跟同兴的往来，本来都归朱福年一个人接头，上十万银子的出入，或者调拨户头，都听他一句话。以后，我们听不听呢？”这“调拨户头”四个字，正就是胡雪岩要弄明白的，当然往下追问：“恒记在宝号有几个户头？”“三个。”邵仲甫答道：“恒记、继嘉堂、福记。”“继嘉堂”是庞家的堂名，“福记”当然是朱福年，这个都算是私人户头，但恒记与继嘉堂不可分，福记的私人户头如何可以跟恒记混在一起？这其间，不言可知有了弊病。于是胡雪岩不但不答邵仲甫的询问，而且提出要求：“请同兴先将福记历年进出的数目，抄个单子给我。”

Shao hosted a sumptuous Western meal. Pang, who was to accompany Yiyun to the foreign shops to buy jewellery and dress fabrics, left in haste. Their host detained Hu over “English black tea”; he had something to discuss. Pang had told Shao too that Hu held a substantial stake in Hengji. Shao’s manner had consequently changed since their first meeting, as had his form of address. Instead of calling him brother, he now called him “Mr Hu.” “Mr Hu, there is something I must ask. Second Master Pang has just instructed us that you will henceforth handle Hengji’s dealings with Tongxing. Does that mean we should still act on Zhu Funian’s instructions?” The question went straight to the heart of the matter. Hu would not answer hastily. “What sort of instructions?” “Until now, Zhu alone has dealt with us for Hengji. Payments and receipts of over a hundred thousand taels, or transfers between accounts—we have acted on his word. Should we continue to do so?” “Transfers between accounts” was exactly what Hu wished to understand. He followed it up. “How many accounts does Hengji have with your bank?” “Three: Hengji, Jijia Hall, and Fuji.” Jijia Hall was the Pang family’s hall name, while Fuji was, of course, Zhu Funian’s account. The latter two were private accounts. Hengji and Jijia Hall could not be separated, but how could Zhu’s own account properly be mixed with Hengji’s? The existence of irregularities was plain. Instead of answering Shao, Hu made a request. “First have Tongxing copy out a statement of all Fuji’s receipts and payments over the past years for me.”

邵仲甫一听吓一跳。这是钱庄的大忌，有钱的人，守着“财不露白”的古训，在钱庄里存款是决不肯告诉人的，用堂名或用个什么“记”的户名，就是为了隐藏真相，而钱庄里也有义务为客户守机密，如今将福记存款进出的数目，泄漏给第三者，这话一传出去，信用一失，人人白危，都来提存，岂不把同兴挤垮。“胡先生，你是内行。”他哭丧着脸说：“这件事实在不敢从命。”他的难处，胡雪岩完全了解，所以早就想好了的，这时便即问道，“仲甫兄，我跟你有没有仇？”“哪里来的仇？”“那不就是了！我跟你无冤无仇，何必来害你？福记是纯粹的私人户头，我没有资格查他的帐，既然跟恒记混在一起，当然我要弄弄清楚。就是在同兴来说，也有义务拿福记的进出开给我看。”胡雪岩又说：“你放心好了！我不会坏同业的规矩的。这件事，无知地知，你知我知，连庞老二我都不告诉他，你还怕什么？“邵仲甫想了想问道：“胡先生，你要这张单子做啥用场，是不是跟朱福年去算帐？”“不是！”胡雪岩说：“朱福年也不会晓得有这件事，我是根据你开的单子，盘恒记的帐。”邵仲甫真的为难了，“英国红茶”喝了一杯又一杯，只是答不出来。

Shao was startled. This was a grave taboo in banking. Wealthy people followed the old rule of never displaying their riches and would not tell others what they had deposited. They used a family hall name or an account name ending in ji precisely to conceal the truth. Banks, in turn, were obliged to keep their customers’ affairs secret. To disclose Fuji’s deposits and withdrawals to a third person—if word of it spread, confidence would collapse. Every customer would fear for himself and withdraw his money. Tongxing would be ruined by a run. “Mr Hu, you know our business,” Shao said miserably. “I really cannot comply.” Hu understood his difficulty perfectly and had already considered it. “Brother Zhongfu, is there any enmity between us?” “Why should there be?” “Exactly. Since we have no grievance against one another, why should I wish to harm you? If Fuji were a purely private account, I should have no right to inspect it. But since it has been mixed with Hengji’s, naturally I must find out what has happened. Tongxing too has an obligation to show me its transactions. Set your mind at rest. I shall not break the rules of our profession. Heaven knows, Earth knows, you know, and I know. I shall not tell even Pang. What is there to fear?” Shao considered this. “Mr Hu, what will you use the statement for? To settle accounts with Zhu?” “No. He will not even know it exists. I shall use the figures you give me to examine Hengji’s books.” Shao was in a genuine quandary. He drank cup after cup of “English black tea,” unable to give an answer.

胡雪岩也知道这是件极严重的事，不加点压力，邵仲甫决不肯就范，所以用相当冷峻的声音说道：“庞老二本有意叫我在上海立阜康的分号，我因为你老兄有言在先，没有答应他。现在在看来，只有自己有钱庄，帐目才能弄得清楚。”说着，便有起身告辞的模样。阜康一设分号，同兴当然再也做不成恒记的生意，这一着棋是“将”邵仲甫的“军”，他不能不着急。“胡先生，胡先生，有话好商量。你能不能让我明天答你的话。”“那自然可以。不过有一层，仲甫兄你千万记住，无论你答应也好，不答应也好，这件事只有你我两个人晓得。”意思是不可泄露其事给朱福年。邵仲甫当然意会得到，连连答说：“我知道，我知道。”

Hu knew that this was a matter of the utmost seriousness. Without pressure, Shao would never agree. He therefore said in a rather cold voice, “Pang wanted me to establish a Fukang branch in Shanghai. Because of what you had already said to me, I did not agree. Now it seems that only by having a bank of my own can I get a clear view of the accounts.” He made as though to rise and leave. If Fukang opened a branch, Tongxing would naturally lose Hengji’s business. The move put Shao in check, and he could not help growing anxious. “Mr Hu, let us discuss this. Could you allow me until tomorrow to answer?” “Certainly. But remember one thing, brother. Whether you agree or refuse, this must remain known only to the two of us.” He meant that Zhu must hear nothing of it. Shao understood perfectly and repeatedly assured him, “I know.”

到了第二天一早，同兴钱庄派人送了信来，邵仲甫约胡雪岩，中午仍旧在那家番菜馆见面。准时赴约，点好了菜，等“仆欧”迟了出去，做主人的取出一个信封，摆在面前，跟他先有番话要交代。邵仲甫提出了“约法三章”：第一，这份清单不得泄漏给任何人，第二，不得以此作为对付朱福年的根据，第三，不管胡雪岩是不是在上海设阜康的分号，恒记不能与同兴断绝往来。第三点其实是请求，只是邵仲甫的措词不甚恰当，有些近乎要挟的意味。胡雪岩颇为不悦，“仲甫兄，”他这样答道：“第一、第二两点，我谨遵台命，第三点，我只能这么说，我一定讲同业的义气。恒记如果是我一个人的事业，老兄吩咐，闲话一句，无奈大老板是庞老二，他又是大少爷脾气，如果恼了他，翻脸不认人，我说的话，他也未见得听。所以这一点，完全要看你自己的做法，我在旁边总替同兴说好话就是。”这是暗示邵仲甫，如果同兴是这种近乎要侠的做法，庞二首先就会着恼，邵仲甫也是极老到的人，一听他这话，自知失态，很见机地道歉。“胡先生，我不会说话，请你不要见怪。将来仰仗的地方还多，一切心照。我也不多说了，总而言之，听你的吩咐就是。”

Early the next morning a messenger came from Tongxing with a letter. Shao asked Hu to meet him at noon at the same Western restaurant. Hu arrived on time. Once they had ordered and the waiter had withdrawn, Shao took out an envelope, laid it before him, and said that there were some points he must first make clear. He proposed three conditions. First, the statement must not be disclosed to anyone. Second, it must not be used as evidence against Zhu. Third, whether or not Hu opened a Fukang branch in Shanghai, Hengji must not break off its dealings with Tongxing. The third point was really a request, but Shao’s unfortunate wording made it sound almost like coercion. Hu was displeased. “Brother Zhongfu, I shall observe the first two conditions. On the third, I can only say that I shall honour my obligations to a fellow banker. If Hengji were mine alone, a word from you would settle it. Unfortunately, the principal owner is Pang, and he has a young gentleman’s temper. Anger him and he may turn his back on you; he will not necessarily listen to me. So that depends entirely on your own conduct. I shall always speak well of Tongxing at his side.” The implication was that if Tongxing adopted this almost coercive manner, Pang would be the first to take offence. Shao was experienced enough to recognize his mistake at once and tactfully apologized. “Mr Hu, I have expressed myself badly. Please do not hold it against me. I shall have much to depend on you for in future, and we understand one another. There is no need to say more. In short, I shall follow your instructions.”

胡雪岩的度量宽，有他这两句话，不满之意，随即消失。等邵仲甫将他面前的信封移了过来，便即抽出里面的单子来看，只见开头写的是“福记名下收付清单”，后面盖着“同兴协记钱庄”的书柬图章。他不暇细看内容，将前后折起，用桌上现成的餐，裁下“福记”字样及同兴图章，各约一指宽的两张纸条，交回邵仲甫。这个小小的动作，使得邵仲甫大为服帖，一则见得胡雪岩的诚意，不会拿这张清单作为对付朱福年的把柄，二则也见得他心细，邵仲甫发觉自己做错了，本来就不必写明“福记”字样，更不必盖上书柬图章，纵然胡雪岩无他，万一遗失了这张清单，落入旁人手中，依然是件极不妥的事。幸好，他的这个错误，为胡雪岩及时纠正了。“胡先生，”他由衷地表示佩服，“有魄力的人，粗枝大叶，心细的人，手面放不开。只有你胡先生，这两样长处都有，实在是没话可说了。”“谬奖，谬奖！”胡雪岩亦颇欣慰，因为邵仲甫言出至诚，看起来自己是在事业上结交了一个很有用的朋友。

Hu was generous in temper, and those words dispelled his displeasure. When Shao pushed the envelope across the table, he drew out the statement. At the top it read “Statement of Receipts and Payments under the Fuji Account”; at the end was the correspondence seal of “Tongxing Xieji Bank.” Without pausing to examine the figures, Hu folded back the top and bottom, took the table knife lying ready to hand, and cut off the account name “Fuji” and Tongxing’s seal. He returned the two strips, each about a finger’s width, to Shao. This small act won Shao over completely. First, it demonstrated Hu’s sincerity: he would not use the statement as evidence against Zhu. Second, it revealed his care for detail. Shao realized that he himself had made a mistake. There had been no need to write the name Fuji, still less to stamp the paper with the bank’s seal. Even if Hu intended no harm, the statement might be lost and fall into someone else’s hands, which would be highly undesirable. Fortunately, Hu had corrected the mistake in time. “Mr Hu,” Shao said with heartfelt admiration, “bold men tend to overlook details, while careful men lack breadth of action. You alone possess both qualities. There is really nothing more to be said.” “You praise me too highly!” Hu too was pleased. Shao spoke with complete sincerity, and it seemed that Hu had gained a very useful friend in business.
