第三十一章｜CHAPTER THIRTY-ONE

朱福年的“把柄”虽已入手，胡雪岩却反丢开了，他做事一向往好的方面走，眼前的唯一大事是与庞二谈判合伙的细节。由于彼此都具诚意，谈判相当顺利，胡雪岩在恒记不居任何名义，但先要为恒记作一番整顿，等到有了头绪，再进行筹设阜康钱庄上海分号。对这方面，庞二表示概不过问，又说，如果胡雪岩资金不足，他可以拉一批长期存款的户头来，变相地为阜康增添资本。于是，双方找了见证人来写台伙的契约，胡雪岩请的是尤五，庞二找了一个他的父执，专做桐油出口的孙大存，合同签押好了，庞二大张筵席，请见证人，也请恒记管事的人，包括朱福年在内，即席宣布，赋胡雪岩以盘查银钱货色、考查同人、重新改组的大权。胡雪岩接着又站起来说了话，表示决不轻易更动，请大家照常办事，不必三心两意，话不多而扼要，每人都象服了颗定心丸。当然，只有朱福年是例外。到了第二天，朱福年来请胡雪岩到恒记去“视事”。他早就打好了主意，到了恒记在帐户中坐定，管事的人一个个来见过，他问了问各人的经历，随即起身辞别，朱福年请他看帐，他回说：“不忙。慢慢儿来好了。”这一半是放朱福年一马，看他是不是自己去弥补他的“花帐”，一半也是实话，因为眼前先有件与他切身利害有关的大事要办。

Although Hu now held evidence against Zhu Funian, he put it aside. He always preferred to turn affairs towards a favourable outcome, and his one immediate concern was to settle the details of his partnership with Pang Er. Since both were sincere, their negotiations went smoothly. Hu would hold no formal title at Hengji, but would first put its affairs in order. Once that work had taken shape, he would proceed with preparations for a Shanghai branch of Fukang Bank. Pang said he would leave that entirely to Hu. If Hu lacked funds, he could introduce customers willing to make long-term deposits, thereby increasing Fukang's working capital indirectly. They then invited witnesses to draw up the partnership agreement. Hu asked You Laowu; Pang chose Sun Dacun, a friend of his father's who specialized in exporting tung oil. Once the agreement had been signed and sealed, Pang gave a grand banquet for the witnesses and Hengji's managers, Zhu included. There he announced that Hu had full authority to examine the firm's money and merchandise, assess its staff and reorganize its operations. Hu rose in turn and assured everyone that he would make no changes lightly. They should carry on as usual, without doubts or divided minds. His few concise words reassured them all—except Zhu. Next day Zhu came to invite Hu to Hengji to assume his duties. Hu had already decided how to proceed. Once seated in the countinghouse, he received the managers one by one, asked about their experience, then rose to leave. Zhu invited him to examine the accounts. “There is no hurry. We can take our time.” Partly he was giving Zhu a chance to repair his irregular accounts himself; partly he meant exactly what he said. Another urgent matter directly affecting his own interests came first.

恒记人事上的变动，朱福年已经告诉了怡和洋行的大班吉伯特。这个意外的变化，自然是一大打击，但朱福年还不服气，怂恿吉伯特说：胡雪岩实力不足，只要吉伯特坚持原议，必可迫他杀价脱手。因此，当古应春跟吉伯恃再度会面，说明恒记的丝亦归他经手，要求照最初的议价成交时，吉伯特断然拒绝，依旧以欧洲丝价大跌为托词，只肯照八五折收买。事情成了僵局，胡雪岩相当为难，如果坚持原价，万一不能成交，不但自己的本钱搁不起，丝也会变质，而且对庞二这方面也难以交代，倘或委曲，则更不能求全，不但为宠二所笑，在商场上辛辛苦苦建立起来的名声，亦会大打折扣。同时还有一层顾虑，也许朱福年已经跟庞二说过，他那里的货色，可以照原定的价钱卖给吉伯特，由自己来经手，反打了个八五折，即或庞二了解其中的苦衷，为了划一步骤，以后易于控制全局，眼前不能不吃点亏，但心里总不会舒服，那就要影响彼此合伙的关系了。

Zhu had told Gilbert, the head of Jardine Matheson, about the changes at Hengji. This unexpected development was naturally a serious blow. But Zhu remained defiant. He urged Gilbert to remember that Hu lacked sufficient resources: by holding to his proposal, Gilbert could force Hu to sell at a reduced price. When Gu Yingchun met Gilbert again, explained that Hengji's silk was now also in his hands, and requested a sale at the price originally negotiated, Gilbert therefore refused outright. Still pleading a sharp fall in European silk prices, he would offer only eighty-five per cent of that price. The business was deadlocked, and Hu faced a real dilemma. If he held to the original price and no sale resulted, he could not afford to leave his capital tied up, the silk would deteriorate, and he would have difficulty explaining matters to Pang. Yet yielding would bring no satisfactory solution either. Pang would laugh at him, and the commercial reputation he had built with such effort would suffer badly. He had another concern. Perhaps Zhu had already told Pang that he could sell Hengji's goods to Gilbert at the agreed price. If Hu took over and obtained only eighty-five per cent, Pang might understand the difficulty—that a unified course of action and future control of the whole business required an immediate sacrifice—but he could hardly feel pleased. Their partnership would suffer.

“我在想，吉伯特恐怕也是‘嘴硬骨头酥’，莫非他买不成我们中国的丝，外国那些绸厂就拿织机停下来，不同绸缎？我想总没有这样的道理吧？”这一说，触发了古应春的灵感，“有了，”他喜滋滋地说，“我有个办法，打听他的虚实！”“那太好了。”胡雪岩精神一振，“我就是想要晓得他手里的牌，看样子‘三副落地’，到底是不是清一色呢？如果不是，我们死扣着那张牌，不是自己害自己？”“就是这话。我马上去打听”““慢来！”胡雪岩拉住他说，“你怎么样下手，先说来我听听！”“吉伯特听了朱福年的话，自然以为千稳万妥，买不成我们的货色，至少可以买恒记的，有了货色，当然要定轮船舱位装货。我就从轮船公司方面去打听，看他定了舱位没有？“古应春又说，”货色不在少数，一两条船还装不下，非先预定不可。所以一定打听得出来的。“

“I wonder whether Gilbert is all hard words and soft bones. If he cannot buy our Chinese silk, will the foreign silk mills stop their looms and cease weaving? Surely that cannot be so?” This gave Gu an inspiration. “I have it!” he said delightedly. “I know a way to discover how he really stands.” “Excellent!” Hu brightened. “That is just what I want: to see his hand. He seems to have three sets exposed already, but is he really playing for a hand all of one suit? If he isn't, aren't we hurting ourselves by holding back that one tile?” “Exactly. I shall find out at once.” “Wait.” Hu caught his arm. “Tell me how you mean to go about it.” “After listening to Zhu, Gilbert must think himself perfectly secure. Even if he cannot buy our goods, he can at least buy Hengji's. With goods in prospect, he will naturally have booked space aboard steamships. I shall ask the shipping company whether he has made a reservation. This is a substantial shipment. One or two ships cannot take it all; space must be booked in advance. We are certain to find something out.”

“对！这个办法好。”胡雪岩的脑筋极快，当时便说：“除非他真的不想做这票生意，要做这票生意，不但要他照我们的价钱，额外还要他破费。”古应春笑了。由于心情由沉重转为轻松，所以戏谑地挖苦胡雪岩：“小爷叔，你也真是，得着风就是雨！给不得你三分颜色，就要开大红染坊了。”“我说个道理你听，你就晓得我不是胡言乱说。”照他的判断，吉伯特以为自己这方面迟早总会就范，所以轮船的舱位定好了不会退掉，如果能够跟轮船公司接洽，以高价将吉伯特所定的舱位抢过来，则洋人买下了丝运不出去，又会来跟自己这方面情商转让，岂不又可以赚他一笔。“这是如意算盘。”古应春说，“不过也不妨试试。”说到这里，他触类旁通，仍旧觉得胡雪岩的话极有用，“小爷叔，你说的办法，恐怕行不通，不过我倒想到了，大可借这个说法，逼他一逼。”“嗯，嗯！”胡雪岩意会了，点点头说：“你请吧！我等你的回音。”

“Good! An excellent idea.” Hu's mind moved quickly. “Unless he truly means to abandon this transaction, we shall make him pay our price—and something extra besides.” Gu laughed. Relieved of his earlier heaviness, he teased Hu. “Little Uncle, you hear a breeze and call it rain! Give you a little colour and you open a great scarlet dyehouse.” “Let me explain my reasoning, and you will see that I am not talking nonsense.” Hu thought Gilbert believed they would eventually submit, so he would not cancel the shipping space he had booked. If they approached the shipping company and offered a higher price to take that space themselves, the foreigner might buy the silk only to find he could not ship it. He would then have to negotiate with them for the space, allowing them to make another profit. “That is a very hopeful calculation,” Gu said. “Still, we might try.” The suggestion gave him another idea, and he again found Hu's words useful. “Your plan may not work, Little Uncle, but we could certainly use the threat of it to put pressure on him.” Hu understood and nodded. “Go, then. I shall wait for your news.”

于是古应春去寻一个名叫陈顺生的朋友，此人是他的同乡，在太古轮船公司做买办，专门负责招揽客货承运。太古也是英国人的资本，怡和有货色交运，当然委托太古。一问果然，“不错，有这回事。”陈顺生答道：“先是定了两班轮船的舱位，到期说货色还不齐，要延到下两班，贴了四百两银子的损失。”“那么下两班什么时候到？”“一班十天以后，还有一班要半个月。到埠卸货装货，要十天工夫。”陈顺生问，“你打听它是为什么？”托人办事，当然要相见以诚，而且是同乡好友，也不必顾虑他会“泄底”，所以古应春将跟吉伯特斗法的经过，源源本本说了一遍，接着便托陈顺生去“逼他一逼”。“延过一次期，话就更好说了。”古应春低声说道：“我拜托你问一问吉伯特，货色齐了没有？到时候能不能装船？如果不能，要趁早说，好让太古另外去招揽客户。”“懂了。这个忙我可以帮你。”“多谢，多谢。今天晚上我请你吃花酒，顺便听你的消息。”“这么急？”“拜托，拜托！”古应春长揖恳求，“务必请你就跑一趟。”

Gu went to see a friend named Chen Shunsheng, a fellow townsman who worked as a comprador for the Swire Steamship Company, responsible for securing passenger and freight business. Swire was also British-owned, so Jardine Matheson naturally entrusted its shipments to it. His inquiry confirmed the guess. “Yes, that is right,” Chen said. “He first booked space on two sailings. When the time came, he said the goods were not ready and asked to postpone to the next two. He paid four hundred taels to cover the loss.” “When do the next two arrive?” “One in ten days, the other in half a month. Unloading and loading in port will take ten days. Why are you asking?” When requesting help, one ought to speak frankly. Chen was both a fellow townsman and a close friend, so Gu had no reason to fear that he would disclose their position. He explained the contest with Gilbert from beginning to end, then asked Chen to put some pressure on him. “Since he has already postponed once, you have all the more reason to ask,” Gu said quietly. “Please find out whether his goods are ready and can be loaded on time. If not, he should say so early, allowing Swire to seek other customers.” “Understood. I can help you with that.” “Thank you. I shall entertain you at a courtesan house tonight and hear your news.” “So urgent?” Gu bowed deeply. “I beg you to go at once.”

情面难却，陈顺生真的丢下了自己的事，去为古应春奔走。到了晚上在估情院见面，他带来了吉伯特的消息。“他说等三夭看。如果三天当中没有回话再谈。”“怎么叫‘再谈，？”古应春问，“是谈班期顺延，还是根本就不要舱位了？”“怎么不要？当然要的！”古应春听得这个回音，十分满意。足见怡和洋和非买丝不可，而且在三天以内就会来谈判。这个看法，胡雪岩也认为不错，但主张再逼一逼。这就是请陈顺生再跟吉伯特去说，有客户求货运舱位甚急，请他在三天以内，必须提出确实答复，否则，吉伯特就得照约履行，即使放弃不用，亦要照全价收费。

Unable to refuse his friend's appeal, Chen set aside his own affairs and went on Gu's errand. When they met at Yiqing House that evening, he brought Gilbert's answer. “He asks us to wait three days. If he has not replied by then, we can discuss it again.” “Discuss what?” Gu asked. “Postponing to another sailing, or giving up the space altogether?” “Why would he give it up? Of course he still wants it.” Gu was thoroughly satisfied. This showed, he thought, that Jardine Matheson had to buy the silk and would come to negotiate within three days. Hu agreed with this assessment, but favoured applying still more pressure. Chen should tell Gilbert that another customer urgently wanted freight space. Gilbert must give a definite answer within three days; otherwise, he would have to fulfil the contract and pay the full charge even if he chose not to use the space.

“这一逼还不够。”胡雪岩又说，“我们还要想个办法，让吉伯特以为我们不愿意跟他再做生意，他才会着慌，你看，我们是不是能够另外找洋人接头，虚张声势一番？”“不行！洋人比我们团结，彼此都通声气的，而且哪个洋行做哪项买卖，完全听他们国内指挥，不会突然之间改做别项生意。虚张声势瞒不过吉伯特。”古应春又说：“倒是有个办法，我们放个风声出去，预备立一间号子，专做洋庄，直接写信给外国厂家交涉。看吉伯特怎么说？”“这也是一个办法。不过，”胡雪岩沉吟了一会说：“俗语说得好：”前半夜想想自己，后半夜想想人家。‘吉伯特就算愿意回头，总也要有个’落场话‘。大家的话都很硬，自己转不来弯，我们要替吉伯特开条路子出来。你说是不是？““我也想到过。就怕我们想转圜，他以为我们软弱，越发搭架子，岂非僵上加僵？”对这个顾虑，胡雪岩无法作判断了，因为洋人做生意的规矩，以及吉伯特的性情，他都不太了解。只是将心比心，自己不肯低头，谅来吉伯特也是如此，如果从中有个穿针引线的人，搭一搭桥，事情使容易办通了。“小爷叔！”古应春看他犹豫的神色，提醒他说：“洋人做生意，讲利益，也讲道理，只要我们道理站得住，态度坚决，洋人倒是不讲面子的，自会笑嘻嘻来跟你说好话。所以你不要三心二意，让洋人看穿了，事情格外难办。”

“That is still not enough,” Hu added. “We must make Gilbert think we no longer wish to do business with him. Then he will become anxious. Could we approach another foreigner and make a show of it?” “No. The foreigners are more united than we are and exchange information. Besides, each firm's line of business is determined by instructions from home. They do not suddenly change to something else. Such a display would not deceive Gilbert. But there is another possibility: spread word that we intend to establish a firm dealing directly with foreign markets and write to manufacturers overseas ourselves. Then see what Gilbert says.” “That is one way. Still...” Hu reflected. “As the saying goes, think of yourself for the first half of the night, and of others for the second. Even if Gilbert is willing to reconsider, he needs a way to retreat with dignity. Both sides have spoken so firmly that neither can turn back easily. We must leave him a way out. Don't you agree?” “I have thought of that too. But if we seek a compromise, he may mistake it for weakness and grow more overbearing. Wouldn't that only deepen the deadlock?” Hu could not judge this concern. He knew too little about foreign business practices or Gilbert's character. Putting himself in the other man's place, he merely felt that since he was unwilling to bow first, Gilbert probably felt the same. An intermediary to bring them together would make matters easier. Seeing his hesitation, Gu reminded him, “Little Uncle, foreigners care about profit, but also about reason. If our case is sound and our position firm, they will not stand on dignity; they will come smiling to speak pleasantly to us. So do not waver. If they see through you, matters will become still harder.”

胡雪岩最尊重行家的意见，古应春跟洋人的交道打得多，自然听他的，“那好！”他说，“我们就做一番态度坚决的表示给他看，请尤五哥弄两条船，我们拿货色装上去。”“这，这表示，绝不卖给他了？”“对了！对外头说，我们的丝改内销了，预备卖给杭州织造衙门。”“那么，恒记的货色呢？”“这我会跟庞二说，让庞二关照朱福年，也是雇船运杭州。”古应春闭着嘴，脸色郑重地考虑好一会，毅然决然地答道：“可以！我们就这么做。不过，庞二对朱福年说的话很要紧。”“那当然！我知道。”胡雪岩说，“朱福年自然要劝他，不必受我们这方面的牵累拿丝卖给吉伯特。庞二只要说一句：”胡某人怎么样，我们怎么样，吉伯特要买丝跟胡某人去接头。‘那就成功了。“照胡雪岩的估计，朱福年当然会将庞二的态度告诉吉伯特，吉伯特一定会回头。如果不理，那么僵局就真的不能化解了。自己这方面固然损失惨重，怡和洋行从此也就不用再想在中国买丝。

Hu always respected expert opinion. Gu had dealt extensively with foreigners, so he naturally followed his advice. “Very well. Let us show Gilbert how firm we are. Ask Brother You to find two boats; we shall load our goods aboard.” “To show that we absolutely will not sell to him?” “Exactly. Say we are turning to the domestic market and intend to sell the silk to the Hangzhou Imperial Weaving Office.” “And Hengji's goods?” “I shall speak to Pang and have him tell Zhu to hire boats for Hangzhou too.” Gu considered this silently for some time, his expression grave. Then he answered decisively, “Yes. Let us do it. But what Pang says to Zhu will be crucial.” “Of course. I understand. Zhu will advise him to avoid being dragged into our difficulties and sell his silk to Gilbert. Pang need only say, ‘We shall do whatever Hu does. If Gilbert wants silk, let him approach Hu.’ That will settle it.” Hu reckoned that Zhu would certainly report Pang's attitude to Gilbert, who would then reconsider. If he ignored it, the deadlock would indeed become insoluble. Their own losses would be heavy, but Jardine Matheson need never again expect to buy silk in China.

想到就做，而且象煞有介事，裕记线栈开了仓，一包包的丝，用板车送到内河码头上去装船。另一方面，庞二听了胡雪岩的话，照计行事。他做生意多少有点公子哥儿的脾气，喜欢发发“骠劲”，把朱福年找了来，叫他雇船装丝运杭州，一言不合，拿朱福年训了一顿。“二少爷！”朱福年问，“这是为啥？”“丝不卖给洋人了！可以不可以？”“那也不用运杭州。运到杭州卖给哪个？”“卖给织造衙门。”“二少爷，这不对吧！”他说，“从一闹长毛，京里就有圣旨。各织造衙门的贡品都减少了。怎么会买我们的丝？这点道理，难道二少爷都不懂？”“我不懂你懂！”庞二的声音粗了，“除非有人吃里扒外，不然洋人怎么会晓得我们的情形？你跟洋人去说，他有洋钱是他的，我不希罕。他到中国来做生意，三翻四覆，处处想占便宜，当我们中国人好欺负？滚他娘的蛋！”

They acted at once, with every appearance of earnest purpose. Yuji Silk Warehouse opened its store, and carts carried bundle after bundle of silk to the inland-waterway wharf for loading. Meanwhile, Pang followed Hu's plan. In business he retained something of a rich young gentleman's temperament and enjoyed an occasional display of defiance. Summoning Zhu, he ordered him to hire boats and load the silk for Hangzhou. At the first disagreement, he gave Zhu a scolding. “Second Master,” Zhu asked, “why are we doing this?” “Because we are not selling to the foreigners! May we not choose?” “But there is no need to send it to Hangzhou. Who will buy it there?” “The Imperial Weaving Office.” “That cannot be right, Second Master. Ever since the Taiping troubles began, imperial orders from the capital have reduced the tribute goods required of all the weaving offices. Why would they buy our silk? Surely you understand that much?” “You understand everything, and I understand nothing!” Pang's voice grew harsh. “Unless someone was betraying us from within, how would the foreigners know our circumstances? Tell the foreigner that his silver is his own; I care nothing for it. He comes to China to trade, changes his position again and again, and tries to take advantage at every turn. Does he think we Chinese are easily bullied? To hell with him!”

这种情形，遇到过不止一次，朱福年也知道他不过一时之气，做伙计的遇上有脾气的东家，当不得真，否则不如早早卷铺盖走路。而况，庞二虽有脾气，御下相当宽厚，象恒记这种职位是“金饭碗”，丢掉了不易再找。所以想一想，宁可挨骂，该说的话还是要说，才显得自己是“忠心耿耿”。“二少爷，难怪你发脾气，洋人是不大对，不过，他既然是来做生意，当然没有空手而回的道理，我看，丝是一定要买的，就是价钱上有上落……”“免谈。少一个‘沙壳子’都办不到。就算现在照我的价钱，卖不卖也要看我的高兴。”“二少爷，生意到底是生意。”他试探着说：“要不要我再跟洋人去谈谈？如果肯依我们的价钱，不如早早脱手，钱也赚了，麻烦也没有。”“我不管。你跟胡先生去谈，看他怎么说就怎么说。”听得这一句话，朱福年只觉得酸味直味脑顶，顿时改了主意，回到帐房里，自己在咕哝：“他娘的，随他去。看他这票货色能摆到啥辰光？”这话是什对胡雪岩而说的，原来是“忠心耿耿”对东家，此时决定牺牲东家的利益，变相打击胡雪岩，真的雇了船，连夜装货，预备直驶杭州。但是，吉伯特却沉不住气了，一面是陈顺生来催，一面是对方的丝真有改为内销的迹象，不由得便软化了，急于想找个人来转圜。

Zhu had encountered such scenes before. He knew this was merely a passing fit of temper. An employee with a hot-tempered master could not take everything seriously; otherwise, he might as well pack up and leave. Besides, for all his temper, Pang treated his staff generously. A post at Hengji was a golden rice bowl, hard to replace once lost. Zhu therefore decided to endure the abuse and say what needed saying, demonstrating his “unswerving loyalty.” “You have reason to be angry, Second Master. The foreigner has behaved badly. But he came to do business and can hardly return empty-handed. I believe he must buy the silk. The only question is the price.” “No discussion. I will not take a single copper less. Even if he offers my price now, whether I sell depends on my mood.” “Business is business, after all,” Zhu ventured. “Shall I speak to him again? If he accepts our price, we might as well sell quickly, make our profit and end the trouble.” “I am not dealing with it. Speak to Mr Hu and follow whatever he says.” At these words, bitterness seemed to rise to the top of Zhu's head. He changed his mind at once. Back in the countinghouse, he muttered, “Damn him, let him do as he likes. We shall see how long he can sit on that silk.” He meant Hu. His earlier “loyalty” to his employer gave way to a decision to sacrifice that employer's interests in order to strike indirectly at Hu. He actually hired boats, loaded them through the night and prepared to send them straight to Hangzhou. Gilbert, however, was losing his composure. With Chen pressing him on one side, and real signs that the silk might be diverted to the domestic market on the other, he began to yield and urgently sought someone to arrange a compromise.

* * *这些情形胡雪岩不知道，他只听庞二说过，朱福年自告奋勇，愿跟吉伯特去重开谈判。又说已告诉朱福年，一切都听自己作主。既如此，则朱福年不论谈判得如何，都该跟自己来接头。何以不见他的踪影，反倒真的雇船装货？显见得其中起了变化。“如果朱福年肯去说，倒是最适当的人选。”古应春也说，“不过现在对他弄僵了，我们不便在他面前示弱，只有再请庞二去问他。”胡雪岩沉吟未答，古应春看的是一面，他要看两面，一面容易找出办法，要兼顾两面，就煞费周章了。“庞二以东家的身分，问他一声，这件事办得怎么了，有何不可。”“自无不可，不过那是不得已的办法，套句你们文绉绉的话，是下策。”“怎么样才是上策呢？”胡雪岩有些答非所问地：“象猪八戒这种样子，我们杭州话，叫做‘不入调’。现在好比唱出戏，我跟庞二唱的是‘乙字调’，他唱的是”扒字调‘，根本搭配不拢。我们调门高的，唱到半路拉不低，就算拉低了来迁就他，这出戏也好听不到哪里去了。“古应春把他这个比方，体味了一会，恍然大悟，“我懂了！”他说，“上策是叫朱福年将调门提高，让它入调！”“一点都不错。”“想倒想得不错。”古应春看一看胡雪岩的脸色，猜不透他葫芦里卖的什么药，只好老实问道：“计将安出？”

Hu knew none of this. Pang had merely told him that Zhu had volunteered to reopen negotiations with Gilbert, and that he had instructed Zhu to leave all decisions to Hu. In that case, whatever the result, Zhu should have approached him. Why had he vanished instead, actually hiring boats and loading the goods? Something had plainly changed. “If Zhu were willing to speak to him, he would be the best intermediary,” Gu said. “But now that our relations with him are strained, we cannot appear weak before him. We can only ask Pang to inquire.” Hu reflected without answering. Gu was considering one side; he had to consider two. Finding a remedy for one side was easy. Providing for both required much more care. “As his employer, Pang can surely ask how the business is progressing,” Gu continued. “Of course. But that is a last resort—the inferior strategy, to borrow your scholarly language.” “What would be the superior strategy?” Hu answered somewhat indirectly. “A man like Pigsy is what we in Hangzhou call ‘out of tune.’ Imagine that we are performing an opera. Pang and I are singing in a high key, and he in a low one. We cannot fit together. Halfway through, we cannot lower our key; even if we did, the performance would not be worth hearing.” Gu considered the comparison and suddenly understood. “I see! The superior strategy is to make Zhu raise his key and sing in tune.” “Exactly.” “A fine idea.” Gu studied Hu's expression, unable to guess his concealed purpose. He could only ask plainly, “But how?”

“喏！就靠这个。”他从身上掏出一张纸来一扬，古应春认出是同兴抄来的那张“福记”收付清单。“你倒看看，这里面有啥毛病？”古应春仔细看了一遍，实在找不出毛病，“我看不出。”他摇摇头，“钱庄生意，我是外行。”“用不着行家，照普通清理，就可以看得出来的。他一个做伙计的人，就算在恒记是头脑，进出数目，充其量万把银子，至矣尽矣。所以，”胡雪岩指着单子说：“这几笔大数目，都有毛病，尤其是这一笔，收五万、付五万，收的哪一个的，付的哪一个的？如果说是恒记的生意，头寸一时兜不转，他有款子，先代垫五万，这倒也说得过去。现在明明是转一个手，我可以断定收的五万是从恒记来的。如果恒记要付偿款，直接支付好了，为啥在要福记的户头里打个转？”他这样一说，古应春也觉得大有疑问，“那么，”他问，“小爷叔，你就当面拆穿他，让他不能不买你的帐？”要当面拆穿，我早就动手了，为的是要顾他的面子。我自有道理，明天上午你在这里等我消息。“

“With this.” Hu produced a sheet of paper and waved it. Gu recognized the statement of receipts and payments for the Fuji account copied from Tongxing's records. “Look at it. What is wrong here?” Gu examined it carefully but could find nothing. “I cannot see it. Banking is outside my trade.” “You need no special knowledge. Ordinary reasoning will do. He is an employee, even if he is Hengji's chief manager. At the very most, his own transactions could amount to ten thousand taels or so. These large entries must therefore be wrong. Especially this one: fifty thousand received, fifty thousand paid out. From whom, and to whom? If Hengji were temporarily short of funds and he had fifty thousand to advance, that might make sense. But here the money simply passes through his hands. I am certain the fifty thousand came from Hengji. If Hengji had a payment to make, why not make it directly? Why pass it through the Fuji account?” Gu now found it highly suspicious too. “Then will you expose him to his face and force him to respect your wishes?” “If I meant to expose him, I would have acted long ago. I am considering his dignity. I have my reasons. Wait here for word from me tomorrow morning.”

* * *第二天上午，胡雪岩到恒记说要看看帐，朱福年自然无话可说，硬着头皮，亲自开锁，从柜子里捧出一大叠总帐来。“总帐不必看，我看看流水。你的帐不会错的，我随便挑几天看看好了。”接着，胡雪岩便说，“请你拿咸丰三年七月、十月、十一月的流水帐给我。”听这样交代，朱福年大放其心，以为他真的不过随便抽查，便依言将这三个月的流水帐找了出来，捧到他的面前。胡雪岩翻到七月初八那一天细看，果然，有一笔五万两银子的现款，送于同兴。“福年兄。”他说，“请你拿‘恒记’户头的存折我看看。”朱福年的一颗心，陡地提了起来：“是不是现在在用的那一个？”这句话便是个老大的漏洞。按常理而论，应该就是目前在用的那一个，何消问得？问到这话，便表示他是“哑子吃馄饨，肚里有数”，胡雪岩的不是这一个。这见得朱福年不是什么老奸巨滑，只因为庞二到底是大少爷，只要对了他的脾气，什么都好说话。意会到此，胡雪岩越发打定了将朱福年收为己用的主意，因而在表面上越对他尊重，和颜悦色地说：“不晓得找起来方便不方便？我想拿这两年的存折，大略看一遍。”越是这样，越使朱福年有莫测高深之感，喏喏连声地说：“方便，方便。”

Next morning Hu went to Hengji and asked to examine the accounts. Zhu had no grounds to refuse. Bracing himself, he unlocked a cabinet and brought out a great stack of general ledgers. “No need for the general ledgers. Let me see the daybooks. Your accounts will be correct; I shall merely choose a few days at random.” Hu then requested the daybooks for the seventh, tenth and eleventh lunar months of the third year of Xianfeng's reign. Greatly relieved, Zhu assumed that Hu really intended only a casual spot check and brought him the three months' books. Hu turned to the eighth day of the seventh month and examined it closely. Sure enough, fifty thousand taels in cash had been sent to Tongxing. “Brother Funian, let me see the passbook for the Hengji account.” Zhu's heart suddenly rose into his throat. “The one currently in use?” The question exposed a serious weakness. Ordinarily, that would naturally be the book required; why ask? His question showed that, like a mute man eating wontons, he knew the count in his own stomach. He knew Hu wanted a different book. This suggested that Zhu was no hardened master of deception. Pang was, after all, a pampered young gentleman: humour him properly and everything was easy. Recognizing this, Hu became still more determined to win Zhu over for his own use. Outwardly, he treated him with greater respect and asked pleasantly, “Would they be easy to find? I should like to glance through the passbooks for the last two years.” The more considerate he was, the less Zhu could fathom him. “Quite easy, quite easy,” he answered repeatedly.

一把存折送了过来，胡雪岩慢条斯理地随意浏览，一面说着闲话，根不不象查帐的样子。朱福年却没有他那份闲豫情致，惴惴然坐在帐桌对面，表面是准备接受询问，其实一双眼只瞪在存折上。“朱先生！”小徒弟走来通报，“船老大有事来接头。”这“船老大”就是承揽装丝运杭州的船家。朱福年不能不去接头。趁这空档，胡雪岩在存折上翻到咸丰三年七月初八那一天。那里有同兴收银五万两的记载。胆子倒真大！胡雪岩心里在想，莫非硬吞五万银子？这盘帐倒要细看了。他是这一行的好手，如今虽因不大管帐打算盘，但要算起帐来，还是眼明手快，帐薄与存折一对，再看一看总帐，便弄清楚了，朱福年硬吞五万银子还不敢，只是挪用了公款，以后在半个月中，分四次归还了。然而这已是做伙计的大忌。胡雪岩认为不必细看，将翻开的帐簿、存折都收好，静等朱福年来答话。

A bundle of passbooks was brought over. Hu browsed through them at leisure, chatting as he did so, with nothing in his manner to suggest an audit. Zhu had no such ease. He sat anxiously across the desk, ostensibly ready to answer questions, while his eyes remained fixed on the books. “Mr Zhu,” a young apprentice came to announce, “the boatmaster has come on business.” This was the man engaged to carry the silk to Hangzhou, and Zhu had to see him. During his absence, Hu turned in the passbook to the eighth day of the seventh month in Xianfeng's third year. There was a record of Tongxing receiving fifty thousand taels. What nerve! Had he actually pocketed fifty thousand? Hu must examine this transaction closely. He was an expert in the trade. Though he seldom kept accounts or worked an abacus now, his eyes and hands were still quick. Comparing the account book with the passbook, then consulting the general ledger, he soon understood. Zhu had not dared to steal fifty thousand outright. He had diverted the firm's money to his own use, then repaid it in four instalments over the following half month. Even this, however, was a grave offence for an employee. Hu felt no need to examine further. He closed the books and passbooks he had opened and waited quietly for Zhu's return.

“船老大来问，货都装齐了，问啥时候开船？”朱福年说，“我告诉他，跟胡先生的货色搭帮走，比较有照应。不晓得胡先生的丝船，啥时候开？”很显然地，就这样一查帐，还未有何结果，就已让他感到威胁，不能不来周旋示好。胡雪岩便将计就计地说：“我们那票货色，是我的朋友古应春在料理。如果福年兄有空，中午我们一起吃饭，当面谈一谈这件事。你看好不好。”“好，好！”朱福年急忙答应，“我做个小东，请胡先生吃徽馆。”“哪个做东都一样。请你拿帐薄、存折收一收，我们就走吧。”看样子太平无事了，朱福年顿觉步履轻快，浑身是劲，收拾一切，陪着胡雪岩出了恒记的大门。

“The boatmaster says everything is loaded and asks when to sail,” Zhu reported. “I told him to travel with your shipment so that the boats could look after one another. When will your silk boats leave?” Plainly, the mere examination of the accounts, before any result had been announced, had threatened him enough to make him seek friendly relations. Hu took advantage of the opening. “My friend Gu Yingchun is handling our shipment. If you are free, Brother Funian, why not lunch together and discuss it with him? What do you say?” “Certainly!” Zhu agreed eagerly. “Allow me to offer a modest lunch at a Huizhou restaurant.” “It makes no difference who is host. Put away the books and passbooks, and let us go.” Everything seemed to have passed without incident. Zhu suddenly felt light on his feet and full of energy. After putting everything away, he accompanied Hu out of Hengji.

“就是后马路，有家徽馆，叫做福源楼，做几样我们家乡菜，着实道地。请胡先生尝尝看。““原来你是徽州人，口音倒听不出。”“我原籍徽州。”朱福年说，“在外多年，口音变过了。”“既是徽州，对典当自然熟悉？”“怎么不熟悉？我也劝过二少爷开典当。他说，穷人的钱不忍心赚。怎么也不肯。”“开典当是为了方便穷人，穷人出点利息，也是心甘情愿的。”“我也是这样说，二少爷听不进去，也是枉然。”就这样一路谈着典当，不知不觉地走到了福源楼。坐定下来，胡雪岩先写张条子，交柜上派人送到裕记丝栈去请古应春，然后点了菜，趁这等客等菜的工夫，他跟朱福年谈到了帐务。

“There is a Huizhou restaurant on Houmalu, called Fuyuanlou. Some of its dishes taste exactly as they do at home. Do try them.” “So you are from Huizhou? I could not tell from your accent.” “My family comes from there. After so many years away, my accent has changed.” “Then you must know the pawnshop business?” “Of course. I have urged Second Master to open a pawnshop. He says he cannot bear to make money from the poor and refuses.” “Pawnshops provide a service to the poor. They pay a little interest willingly.” “That is what I told him, but it is useless if he will not listen.” Talking of pawnshops, they reached Fuyuanlou almost without noticing. Once seated, Hu wrote a note and asked the restaurant to send someone with it to Yuji Silk Warehouse to invite Gu. He ordered the dishes, then, while waiting for their guest and the food, returned to the accounts.

“福年兄，刚才我看的那笔五万银子的帐，恐怕有点错了。”“喔。”因为胡雪岩语气缓和，所以朱福年也能沉得住气，平静地问道：“我倒还不清楚。日子久了，不大记得起来。”“帐上有送存同兴的一笔帐，存折上没有。”“是说恒记这个折子？”朱福年答道，“恒记在同兴有三个折子。”“我知道。”胡雪岩接着便问，“福记是你老兄的户头吧？”这就是所谓作贼心虚了，朱福年脸上的颜色，立刻就不大自然，勉强答说，“是的。”“我做钱庄也多年了，这种情形，倒还少见。”“各处地方不一样。”朱福年说，“为了调度方便，二少爷叫我也立一个户头。”“喔，”胡雪岩抓住他“调度方便”这四个字追问：“是不是说，有时候要向外头调动头寸，恒记不便出面，用你福记的名义？”这话，朱福年就答不出来了，因为庞二财大势雄，从不向外面调动头雨，如果应声“是”，胡雪岩跟庞二一谈，西洋镜马上拆穿，金饭碗也就要不翼而飞了。因此，他只能含含糊糊地答说：“不是这意思。”“那么是什么意思呢？”胡雪岩若无其事地问，声音中不带丝毫诘质的意味。而朱福年却已急得满头大汗，结结巴巴地不知道说些什么。

“Brother Funian, there may be a slight error in that fifty-thousand-tael transaction I looked at.” “Oh?” Hu's mild tone allowed Zhu to remain composed. “I am not quite sure. It was some time ago; I cannot remember it clearly.” “The account book records a deposit at Tongxing, but the passbook does not.” “You mean the Hengji passbook? Hengji has three passbooks at Tongxing.” “I know. Fuji is your own account, is it not?” A guilty conscience betrayed Zhu at once. His expression changed, and he forced out a “Yes.” “I have been in banking for years, but have seldom seen this arrangement.” “Customs differ from place to place. Second Master told me to open an account too, for convenience in managing funds.” Hu seized on those words. “Do you mean that when funds must be raised outside and Hengji cannot conveniently appear in the transaction, you use the name of Fuji?” Zhu could not answer. Pang was immensely wealthy and influential and never needed to raise funds outside. If he said yes and Hu mentioned it to Pang, the deception would be exposed at once, and his golden rice bowl would vanish. He could only answer vaguely, “That is not what I mean.” “What do you mean, then?” Hu asked casually, without the slightest interrogatory edge. Zhu, however, was sweating with anxiety and could only stammer incoherently.

“那也不必说它了！”胡雪岩不再侧面相逼，正面指出他的错，“那五万银子，细看前后帐，分毫不少……”“是啊！”朱福年急忙抢着辩白，“帐是决不会错的。”“错不错，要看怎么个看法，什么人来看？”胡雪岩答得极快，“我看是不错，因为以前的帐目，跟我到底没有啥关系，叫你们二少爷来看，就错了。你说是不是呢？”最后这一问，使得朱福年又大受其窘，只得先虚晃一枪：“我倒还不明白胡先生你的话？”“再明白都没有，五万银子说存恒记，结果存入福记，福记再分四次归还。前后数目不错，起码拆息上，恒记吃亏了。不过，这在我看，是小事，你倒拿我前后的话，仔细想一想！”他以前说过什么话？朱福年茫然不辨，定定心细想，才意会到他有句话，大有深意。这句话就是：“我看是不错，因为以前的帐目，跟我到底没有啥关系！”这就是暗示，以前的帐目他不会顶真，但以后他是恒记的股东，帐目便不能说无关，当然也就要认真了。意会到此，朱福年才知道自己不是“猪八戒”，倒是“孙悟空”，跳不出胡雪岩这尊“如来佛”的手掌心，乖乖儿认输，表示服帖，是上上大吉。

“Let us leave that aside.” Abandoning indirect pressure, Hu pointed out his fault plainly. “Looking carefully at the accounts before and after that fifty thousand, not a fraction is missing.” “Exactly!” Zhu hastened to defend himself. “The figures cannot be wrong.” “Whether they are wrong depends on how—and by whom—they are examined,” Hu answered at once. “To my eyes they are correct, since the earlier accounts have nothing to do with me. To Second Master's eyes, they would be wrong. Don't you agree?” Again cornered by the final question, Zhu tried an evasion. “I still do not quite understand you.” “It could not be clearer. Fifty thousand was said to have been deposited under Hengji, but went into Fuji. Fuji then repaid it in four instalments. The totals agree, but at the very least Hengji lost the interest. Still, I regard it as a small matter. Think carefully about everything I have said.” What had he said? Zhu was confused. Collecting himself, he finally grasped the significance of one sentence: “To my eyes they are correct, since the earlier accounts have nothing to do with me.” Hu was indicating that he would not insist on pursuing the old accounts. From now on, however, he would be a partner in Hengji. Its accounts would concern him, and he would examine them seriously. Zhu realized that he was not Pigsy after all, but Sun Wukong, unable to escape from Hu Xueyan’s palm, as though Hu were the Buddha himself. His best course was to admit defeat and submit.

“胡先生，我在恒记年数久了，手续上难免有疏忽的地方，一切要请胡先生包涵指教。将来怎么个做法，请胡先生吩咐，我无不遵办。”这是递了“降表”。到此地步，胡雪岩无需用旁敲侧击的办法，更用不着假客气，直接提出他的意见：“福年兄，受人之托，忠人之事，你们二少爷既然请我来看看帐，我当然对他要有个交代。你是抓总的，我只要跟你谈就是了，下面各人的帐目，你自己去查，用不着我插手。”“是。”朱福年说，“我从明天就清查各处的帐目，日夜赶办，有半个月的工夫，一定可以盘清楚。”“好的。你经手的总帐，我暂时也不看，等半个月以后再说。”“是！”“这半个月之中，你也不妨自己检点一下，如果还有疏忽的地方，想法子自己弥补。我将来也不过看几笔帐，”接着，胡雪岩清清楚楚他说了几个日子，这是从同兴送来的福记收支清单中挑出来的，都是有疑问的日子。朱福年暗暗心惊，自己的毛病自己知道，却不明白胡雪岩何以了如指掌，莫非他在恒记中已经埋伏了眼线？照此看来，此人高深莫测，真要步步小心才是。

“Mr Hu, I have been at Hengji for many years, and there may have been oversights in procedure. I ask your understanding and guidance. Tell me how to proceed in future, and I shall follow every instruction.” This was his declaration of surrender. Hu no longer needed hints or false politeness. He stated his wishes directly. “Brother Funian, when entrusted with a man's affairs, one must serve him faithfully. Since Second Master has asked me to examine the accounts, I must give him an explanation. You are in overall charge, so I need speak only to you. Examine the accounts of those beneath you yourself. I need not interfere.” “Yes. I shall begin checking every department tomorrow and work day and night. Within half a month, I shall have everything reconciled.” “Good. For the moment, I shall leave the general ledger in your hands unexamined too. We can discuss it in half a month.” “Yes.” “Meanwhile, review your own work. If there are any further oversights, find a way to put them right yourself. Later, I shall look at only a few transactions.” He then named several dates clearly and precisely, chosen from the statement of Fuji's receipts and payments supplied by Tongxing. Every one was questionable. Zhu was secretly alarmed. He knew his own irregularities, but could not understand how Hu knew them so thoroughly. Had he already planted informants at Hengji? The man was unfathomable. He must be careful at every step.

他的疑惧都流露在脸上，胡雪岩使索性开诚布公地说：“福年兄，你我相交的日子还浅，恐怕你还不大晓得我的为人。我一向的宗旨是：有饭大家吃，不但吃得饱，还要吃得好。所以，我决不肯敲碎人家的饭碗。不过做生意跟打仗一样，总要同心协力，人人肯拼命，才会成功。过去的都不必说了，以后看你自己，你只要肯尽心尽力，不管心血花在明处还是暗处？说句我自负的话，我一定看得到，也一定不会抹煞你的功劳，在你们二少爷面前会帮你说话。或者，你倒看得起我，将来愿意跟我一道来打天下，只要你们二少爷肯放你，我欢迎之至。““胡先生，胡先生！”朱福年激动不已，“你说到这样的金玉良言，我朱某人再不肯尽心尽力，就不是人了。胡先生，我敬一杯，表表我的心。”说罢，满斟一杯，仰脸饮尽。胡雪岩当然高兴，陪了一满杯，然后笑道：“福年兄，从此我们是一家人了，有啥说啥，不要见外。”

His fear and suspicion showed on his face, so Hu decided to speak openly. “Brother Funian, we have known each other only a short time. Perhaps you do not yet understand my character. My principle has always been that everyone should eat—not merely enough, but well. I would never willingly smash another man's rice bowl. Yet business is like war: success requires everyone to work together and give all their strength. Let us say no more about the past. The future depends on you. If you serve wholeheartedly, whether your efforts are visible or hidden, I venture to say that I shall see them. I shall never deny you the credit, and I shall speak for you before Second Master. Or, if you think well enough of me to wish to build a business at my side one day, I shall welcome you warmly, provided Second Master is willing to release you.” “Mr Hu!” Zhu was deeply moved. “After such precious words, I should scarcely deserve to be called a man if I failed to give my best. Let me drink to show my feelings.” He filled a cup and drained it. Hu gladly drank a full cup with him, then smiled. “Brother Funian, from now on we belong to one family. Speak freely. There is no need for ceremony.”

“是的。”朱福年想一想说，“胡先生，以后恒记的跟同兴的往来，只用两个户头，公款用恒记，二少爷私人收支用继嘉堂。我在同兴的户头，决定结了它。”“结了它也不必。”胡雪岩说，“不必让外头人猜测，以为我们内部生了啥意见。”这更见得胡雪岩的体恤，顾到自己的面子，当然乐受这番好意，“是！”他很恭敬地回答：“我懂胡先生的意思，找机会，我要告诉下面的‘朋友’们，恒记是一家，总要让外头人看得我们上下一心，不敢来动我们的歪脑筋才好。”“就是这话！‘打落牙齿往肚里咽’，方算好汉。”

“Yes.” Zhu considered. “Mr Hu, in future our dealings with Tongxing should use only two accounts: Hengji for the firm's money, Jijia Hall for Second Master's personal receipts and payments. I have decided to close my own account there.” “There is no need,” Hu replied. “We need not set outsiders wondering whether there has been a disagreement within the firm.” This showed still greater consideration for Zhu's dignity, and he gladly accepted it. “Yes, I understand,” he answered respectfully. “I shall find an opportunity to tell the men under me that Hengji is one family. Outsiders must see that we are united from top to bottom, so that they dare not plot against us.” “Exactly. A man must be able to swallow his broken teeth without a word.”

说到这里，只见古应春步履安详地踏了进来，朱福年起身让坐。极其殷勤。在右应春的心目中，此人自视甚高，加以东家“弹硬”，所以平日总在无意间流露出“架子大”“的味道，此刻一反常态，不用说，是对胡雪岩服帖了，才有这番连带尊敬的表示。意会到此，他的神情越发从容，说着闲话，不提正事。倒是朱福年忍不住了，“胡先生，应春兄来了，我们拿丝上的事说个定规。”他略停了一下又说：“ 照我看，‘只拉弓，不放箭’也就够了。”胡、古二人，目视而笑。然后是胡雪岩回答他的话，反问一句：“我们在‘打弓’，吉伯特晓不晓得？”“我想他是晓得的。我们真的‘放箭’他也会着急。”“当然罗！”古应春接口，极有信心地说：“他万里迢迢跑了来为啥？不是为了生意？生意做不成，他的盘缠开销哪里来？““话虽如此，事情有点弄僵！”胡雪岩问古应春：“你肯不肯向他去低头？”“我不去了！洋人是‘蜡烛脾气’，越迁就他，他越摆架子。”“为来为去，只为了我是当事人。如果这票货色不是我的，替双方拉场，话就好说了。而且双方也都一定感激此人。”

At this point Gu entered with an unhurried step. Zhu rose and offered him a seat with great attentiveness. Gu had always thought Zhu full of self-importance. Backed by a powerful employer, he habitually revealed an overbearing air. His changed manner could only mean that he had submitted to Hu and was extending the respect to Hu's friend. Understanding this, Gu grew still more composed. He chatted without mentioning business. Zhu finally could bear it no longer. “Mr Hu, now Brother Yingchun is here, let us settle what to do about the silk.” After a pause he added, “In my opinion, drawing the bow without loosing the arrow will be enough.” Hu and Gu exchanged a smile. “We are drawing the bow,” Hu replied, “but does Gilbert know it?” “I think so. If we really loose the arrow, he will be anxious.” “Of course!” Gu said confidently. “Why did he travel all these thousands of miles? For business. If no business is done, where will the money for his expenses come from?” “True, but things have become rather strained.” Hu turned to Gu. “Are you willing to bow to him?” “I shall not go again. Foreigners have a candle's temper: the more you indulge them, the more airs they put on.” “The difficulty is simply that I am a party to the transaction. If the silk were not mine, it would be easy to mediate—and both sides would surely be grateful to whoever did.”

“这个人很难。”古应春会意，故意不去看朱福年，尽自摇头：“不容易找！”他们这样一拉一唱。 暗中拉住了朱福年，他终于忍不住：“胡先生！你看，我跟吉伯特去谈一谈，是不是有用？”“噢！”胡雪岩一拍前额，做出茅塞顿开的姿态，“有你老兄出面，再好都没有了。有用，有用，一定有用。”受了鼓励的朱福年，越发兴致勃勃，自告奋勇：“吃完饭，我就去看他。我要吓他一吓，他不照原议买我们的这票货色，劝他趁早回国，他在这里永远买不到我们的丝！““对。就这么说。这倒也不完全是吓他，反正这票生意做不到，我们就斗气不斗财了！”朱福年倒真是赤胆忠心，即时就要去办事。胡雪岩当然要留住他，劝他从容些，把话想停当了再说。接着便设想吉伯特可能会有反响，他这么说便那么回答，那么说便这么回答，一一商量妥帖，还要先约个时间，从容不迫地谈，才能收效。

“Such a man would be hard to find.” Understanding Hu's meaning, Gu deliberately avoided looking at Zhu and kept shaking his head. “Very hard.” Between them they drew Zhu in. At last he could not resist. “Mr Hu, do you think it would help if I spoke to Gilbert?” “Ah!” Hu slapped his forehead as though suddenly enlightened. “Nothing could be better than having you act for us. It would help enormously.” Encouraged, Zhu grew more eager. “I shall see him after lunch. I shall give him a fright: if he will not buy our silk on the original terms, he had better go home. He will never buy our silk here.” “Yes. Say that. Nor would it be entirely an empty threat. If this transaction falls through, we shall fight for our pride instead of our profit.” Zhu was now wholly devoted to the cause and wanted to leave immediately. Hu naturally detained him, urging him to take his time and prepare what to say. They considered Gilbert's possible responses, deciding how to answer each one. They should also arrange an appointment first. Only a calm, unhurried discussion would achieve the desired result.
