胡雪岩｜中英对照版
原著：高阳

红顶商人｜The Red-Topped Merchant

第一章（本章第2部分，共6部分）

阅读说明：每段中文后接对应英文；两版英文正文一致。下载站水印已清除，原始疑字在文末说明。

一大碗浆糊吃得光光，实在意有未尽；便用无可奈何的声音说道∶『七姐，五脏庙还在造反。』『小爷叔，』古应春劝他，『等下再吃！『喔！』胡雪岩点点头，但脸上是异常失望的神色。七姑奶奶大为不忍，但也不能不顾他的肠胃，随即说道∶『这样吧，弄点吃不坏的东西来吃。』于是装了几盘零食，松子、杏仁、蜜枣、金橘饼之类，为他『煞馋』；而就在这个时候，伤科医生到了，检视伤口，认为相当严重，总要半个月才能行动。

He finished the great bowl of gruel without leaving a trace, yet it was nowhere near enough. “Seventh Sister,” he said helplessly, “the temple of my five organs is still in revolt.” “Little Uncle,” Gu urged, “wait a little before you eat again!” “Oh!” Hu nodded, but his disappointment was plain. The Seventh Lady could scarcely bear it, though she could not disregard the condition of his stomach. “I know. Let us find something that will do you no harm.” She brought out several plates of small delicacies—pine nuts, almonds, honeyed dates and candied kumquats—to appease his craving. Just then the doctor who treated injuries arrived. After examining the wound, he pronounced it serious: Hu would need at least a fortnight before he could move about.

『这，这办不到，』胡雪岩很着急地说，『至多三、五天，我一定要回去。』『什么？』七姑奶奶急急问道，『小爷叔，你还要回去？回杭州？』『是啊！杭州城里，多少张嘴都朝天张大了在等我。』『小爷叔是受王抚台的重托，特为到上海来买米的。』古应春向七姑奶奶解释∶『这是救命的事，小爷叔确是不便耽搁；我已经派人去请五哥来商量了。不过，』他转脸向伤科医生问道∶『先生，无论如何要请你费心；不管用什么贵重药，总要请你想个法子，让我们这位小爷叔，三五天以内，就能走动。』『真的。』这时的七姑奶奶也帮着恳求，『郎中先生，你要做做好事；我们这位小爷叔早到一天，杭州城里就要多活好些人。这是阴功积德的大好事；郎中先生，你一生看过的病人，没有比这位再要紧的。』最后这句话很有力量，伤科医生大为动容，将他的伤口左看右看，攒眉咂嘴了好半天，说出一句话来。『办法是有，只怕病人吃不起痛苦。』『不要紧！』胡雪岩咬一咬牙说，『什么痛我都不在乎，只要早好！』

“That—that is impossible,” Hu said anxiously. “I must go back in three to five days at the very most.” “What?” the Seventh Lady asked urgently. “Little Uncle, you are going back? Back to Hangzhou?” “Yes. Think how many mouths are open there, waiting for me.” “Governor Wang has entrusted Little Uncle with a special mission to buy rice in Shanghai,” Gu explained to her. “Lives depend on it. He really cannot afford to linger. I have already sent someone to ask Brother Five to come and discuss matters. But—” He turned to the doctor. “Sir, whatever happens, I must beg you to do your utmost. No medicine is too costly. Please find some way of getting Little Uncle on his feet within three to five days.” “Indeed!” The Seventh Lady joined her pleas to his. “Doctor, you must perform this act of kindness. Every day sooner that Little Uncle gets back means many more lives saved in Hangzhou. You will earn untold merit for such a deed. In all your life, you have never treated a patient more important than this one.” That last sentence had great force. Deeply moved, the doctor examined the wound from every angle, frowning and clicking his tongue for a long time. At last he spoke. “There is a way. I fear only that the patient could not bear the pain.” “Never mind that!” Hu clenched his teeth. “I care nothing for pain, so long as I recover quickly.”

『说说容易。』伤科医生大摇其头，『看你的样子，人是虚弱到了极点；痛得厉害，人会昏过去。等我想想。』他转脸问道∶『古先生，你不是认识外国医生？』这一说，提醒了古应春；悔恨不迭——只为胡雪岩的模样，令人震惊；一时昏瞀，竟想不起请西医，如今倒不便『另请高明了』了。『是！』他只好先回答了再说。『外国医生的看法来得慢∶不过他们有两样药很管用；你能不能去要点止痛药来。』『这，』古应春面有难色，他知道西医跟中医不同，不曾诊视过病人，不肯随便给药；而且止痛的药也不止一种，有外敷、有内服，『要哪一种止痛药，总得有个药名才好。』

“Easy enough to say.” The doctor shook his head. “You are exhausted to the very limit. Severe pain could make you faint. Let me think.” He turned to Gu. “Mr Gu, do you not know a foreign doctor?” The question reminded Gu of something he bitterly regretted forgetting. Hu’s appearance had so shocked him that he had been too bewildered to think of summoning a Western physician. Now it would be awkward to seek another doctor’s services. “Yes,” he answered, having little choice. “Foreign doctors take their time over a case, but they have a couple of very useful medicines. Could you obtain some painkiller from them?” “Well—” Gu looked troubled. He knew that Western physicians differed from Chinese practitioners: they would not readily prescribe for a patient they had not examined. Moreover, there was more than one kind of painkiller, some applied externally and others taken internally. “Which kind would you want? I should at least need its name.”

『药名就说不出来了；叽哩咕噜的洋文，弄不清楚。』伤科医生略停一下，下了决心，『算了！耽误时候，也不是一回事，我先动手。』于是他从药箱里取出一个布包，一打开来，雪亮耀眼，是几把大小不同的刀钳；然后用新棉花擦拭伤口，运刀剜去腐肉，疼得胡雪岩满头大汗。古应春和七姑奶奶心惊肉跳，也陪着他淌汗；同时还得胡作镇静，想出话来安慰病人，七姑奶奶象哄小孩似地，不断地说∶『不疼、不疼，马上就好了。』毕竟好了，敷上止血定痛的『降香散』包扎妥当；伤科医生自己也大大地舒了口气，『总算还好，没有变成破伤风。』他说，『「金疮出血太多，其脉虚细者生。」如今千万要好好照料，疏忽不得。』接着他又说了许多禁忌，不能劳动，不能生气，不能大说大笑；还要『忌口』，咸、酸、辣和热酒、热汤都不能喝，连热粥也在禁忌之列。『糟了！』七姑奶奶说，『刚喝了一大碗热鸡汤。』『喝也喝过了，提它干什么？』古应春说，『以后小心就是了。』

“I cannot tell you its name. I cannot make sense of that foreign gibberish.” The doctor paused, then made up his mind. “Never mind! We cannot keep wasting time. I shall begin.” From his medicine chest he took a cloth bundle. Unfolded, it revealed several knives and forceps of different sizes, gleaming brightly. He cleaned the wound with fresh cotton, then cut away the dead flesh. The pain brought sweat pouring from Hu’s head. Gu and the Seventh Lady were trembling with alarm and sweating with him, yet they had to pretend to be calm and find something to say to comfort him. Like someone coaxing a child, she kept repeating, “It does not hurt, it does not hurt. It will soon be over.” At last it was over. The doctor applied Jiangxiang Powder to stop the bleeding and ease the pain, then bandaged the wound carefully. He too breathed a great sigh of relief. “Fortunately it has not developed into tetanus,” he said. “‘When a blade wound has bled heavily, a weak and fine pulse foretells survival.’ He must now be looked after with the utmost care. There must be no neglect.” He went on to enumerate many prohibitions: no exertion, no anger, no loud talk or laughter. Hu must also restrict his diet—nothing salty, sour or spicy, no hot wine or soup, not even hot congee. “Oh dear!” the Seventh Lady exclaimed. “He has just had a great bowl of hot chicken soup.” “He has already drunk it. What is the use of mentioning it now?” Gu said. “We must be careful from now on.”

等伤科医生一走，古应春要改请西医来看；七姑奶奶不赞成，胡雪岩也表示不必，因为他自觉痛楚已经减轻，证明这位伤科医生有些手段，自不宜更换医生。『我精神好多了。』胡雪岩说，『办大事要紧。五哥怎么还不来？』『今天是他一徒弟续弦，在吃喜酒，我已经派人去追了。小爷叔，』古应春说∶『有事你先分派我。』『好！』他探手入怀，掏摸了好半天，才掏出一个油纸包；递了给古应春。

Once the doctor had left, Gu proposed bringing in a Western physician instead. The Seventh Lady objected, and Hu too said it was unnecessary. He felt the pain had already diminished, which proved that the doctor had some skill; they ought not to replace him. “I feel much stronger,” Hu said. “We must attend to the important business. Why is Brother Five still not here?” “One of his pupils is marrying again today, and he is at the wedding feast. I have sent someone after him. Little Uncle, give me your instructions first.” “Very well.” Hu put a hand inside his coat and felt about for a long time before bringing out an oilpaper packet. He handed it to Gu.

打开油纸包，里面是惊心动魄的王有龄的两通血书，一通致闽浙总督庆端，乞援以外，更望设法督催一直逗留在衡州的李元度，带领所募的湘勇，往杭州这方面打，好牵制长毛，减轻杭州的压力。还有一通是给江苏巡抚薛焕的，要求筹饷筹粮，同时附着一件奏稿，托薛焕代缮拜发。

Inside were two appalling letters written in Wang Youling’s blood. One was addressed to Qingduan, Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang. Besides begging for reinforcements, Wang hoped he would press Li Yuandu, who was still lingering at Hengzhou, to lead the Hunan recruits he had raised towards Hangzhou. This would hold the Longhairs in check and ease the pressure on the city. The other was for Xue Huan, Governor of Jiangsu, asking him to raise funds and provisions. Enclosed was the draft of a memorial, which Wang asked Xue to have copied out and forwarded to the throne on his behalf.

其中详叙杭州被围绝粮，归咎于驻在绍兴的团练大臣王履谦，勾结劣绅，把持地方，视省城的危急，如秦人之视越；更骇人听闻的是，居然唆使莠民戕害命官——九月廿四，长毛窜陷钱塘江南岸，与杭州隔水相望的萧山，如兴知府廖宗元派炮船，迎头拦击；寡不敌众，官军败退。王履谦和萧绍一带的百姓，平时就与官军不和，猜忌甚深；这时以为炮船通敌，回来是替长毛带路，王履谦便下令包围活捉，格杀不论。廖宗元得报，知道这纵非诬陷，也是极严重的误会，赶紧亲自出城弹压。暴民一声呼啸，将廖宗元从马上拉下来痛殴，王履谦袖手旁观，默赞其事。由这一番内讧，替敌人制造了机会；长毛长驱猛扑，兵不血刃而陷绍兴。长毛进城的前一天，王履谦携带家眷辎重，由绍兴逃到宁波，经海道逃到福建；而杭州的粮道，也就此断了。王有龄自然要参劾王履谦，措词极其严厉；甚至有『臣死不瞑目』的话，可以想见他对王履谦怨恨入骨。

The memorial described in detail how Hangzhou had been besieged and deprived of food. It laid the blame on Wang Lüqian, the militia commissioner stationed in Shaoxing, who had colluded with corrupt local gentry to monopolise power and regarded the provincial capital’s peril as indifferently as the people of distant Qin might regard the troubles of Yue. Still more shocking, he had actually incited ruffians to assault an imperial official. On the twenty-fourth of the ninth month, the Longhairs had overrun Xiaoshan, across the Qiantang from Hangzhou. Liao Zongyuan, prefect of Shaoxing, sent gunboats to intercept them. Hopelessly outnumbered, the imperial troops were forced to retreat. Wang Lüqian and the people of Xiaoshan and Shaoxing had long been on bad terms with the government forces and distrusted them deeply. Now they supposed the gunboats had gone over to the enemy and were returning to guide the Longhairs. Wang ordered the boats surrounded and their crews captured, with no penalty for killing them. When Liao heard the report, he knew that, even if the accusation was not deliberately false, it was a grave misunderstanding. He hurried outside the city in person to restore order. With a roar the mob dragged him from his horse and beat him savagely, while Wang stood by, silently approving. This internal strife gave the enemy their opportunity. The Longhairs swept forward and took Shaoxing without shedding a drop of blood. The day before they entered, Wang had fled with his family and baggage from Shaoxing to Ningbo, and thence by sea to Fujian. Hangzhou’s supply route had thus been severed. Naturally, Wang Youling wished to impeach him. His language was exceptionally severe, even declaring, “Your servant could not close his eyes in death.” One could imagine the depth of his hatred.

『这两封血书，』古应春问道，『怎么样处置？』『都送薛抚台——。』『好。』古应春不等他话完，就要起身，『我连夜送去。』『这倒不必。明天一早送去好了；我还有话。』『是！你说。』『我要托你面见薛抚台。』胡雪岩虽然气弱，但低微的语声中，仍然显得很有决断∶『米，我自己想办法；运米的船，回头要问五哥，能够不麻烦官府最好。不过，他要替我派兵护运。』『这条路通吗？』

“What shall I do with these two letters?” Gu asked. “Send them both to Governor Xue—” “Very well.” Before Hu had finished, Gu was already making to rise. “I shall take them tonight.” “There is no need for that. First thing tomorrow will do. I have more to say.” “Of course. Tell me.” “I want you to see Governor Xue in person.” Weak though Hu was, his low voice still carried great resolution. “I shall find the rice myself. As for boats to carry it, I must ask Brother Five when he comes. It would be best if we could manage without troubling the authorities. But the governor must detail troops to escort us.” “Is there a route open?”

『有一条路好走，你不明白；五哥知道，等他来了再说。』胡雪岩又说∶『还有几首诗，也请你送给薛抚台；你说我因为腿伤，不能当面去见他，要问杭州惨状到什么样子？请他看这几首诗就知道了。』一面说，一面又在衣襟中摸索半天，才掏出几张极皱的纸。古应春摆在桌上抹平了细看，标题叫《辛酉杭城纪事诗》，作者名叫张荫榘。一共是十二首七绝；每首都有注解，看到第五首，古应春念道∶雍容铃阁集簪裾，九月秋清气象舒；无数妖氛惊乍逼，十门从此断军书。诗下的注解是∶『九月二十六日，贼以数十万众围城，十门紧闭，文报从此不通，居民如笼中鸟，釜中鱼。』古应春念到这里，屈指数了一下∶『今天十一月初五，围了四十天了。』『四十天不算多，无奈缺粮已久；围到第十天就人心大乱了。』胡雪岩叹口气说∶『你再看下去。』接下去看，写的是∶十面城门十面围，大臣谁是识兵机？国人望岁君胡胄，传说张巡整队师。

“There is one way through. You would not know it, but Brother Five does. We shall discuss it when he comes.” Hu continued, “There are some poems for Governor Xue as well. Tell him that my injured leg prevents me from calling on him. If he wishes to know how terrible things are in Hangzhou, he need only read them.” Again he felt inside his coat for a long time before producing several badly crumpled sheets. Gu spread them on the table, smoothed them out and read carefully. They were entitled Poems Recording Events in Hangzhou in the Xinyou Year, by Zhang Yinju: twelve quatrains of seven-character lines, each with explanatory notes. At the fifth, Gu read aloud: “Dignified officials gather in the commander’s hall; / The ninth month’s clear autumn breathes an air of ease. / Suddenly countless evil mists press close; / Henceforth no military dispatch passes the ten gates.” The note beneath read: “On the twenty-sixth of the ninth month, several hundred thousand rebels surrounded the city. All ten gates were shut, and official communications ceased. The inhabitants were like birds in a cage or fish in a cauldron.” Gu counted on his fingers. “Today is the fifth of the eleventh month. They have been under siege for forty days.” “Forty days is not so long, but the shortage of food began much earlier. By the tenth day of the siege, the people were already in a panic.” Hu sighed. “Read on.” Next came: “Ten city gates besieged on every side; / Which minister understands the art of war? / Your people await you like the harvest—why the helmet, lord? / Word comes that Zhang Xun is marshalling his men.”

注是∶『十月初六日，张军门玉良援到，大获胜仗；即派况副将文榜于下午入城见王中丞有龄，请城内连夜移兵出扎，便可与张军门联络，以通粮道。饶军门从旁阻之云∶』明日总来得及。「不料伪逆李秀成连夜筑成木城，于是饷道与张营隔绝。而十城隔濠，亦遍筑土城。当张军门令况副将入城见中丞，以灭贼自任，百姓延颈觇伺，均言贼必扑灭。『看完这首诗和原注，古应春问道∶』饶军门是谁？『』饶廷选。这个人因为救过广信府，靠沈夫人出了大名，其实没用。『胡雪岩叹口气说∶』我劝过王雪公多少次，说他因人成事，自己胆子小得很。王雪公不听我的话。救杭州就靠这个机会；错过这个机会，神仙来都没救了。『』张玉良呢？『古应春又问，』这个人大家都说他不行，到底怎么样？『『你再往下看。下面有交代。』

The note explained: “On the sixth of the tenth month, General Zhang Yuliang arrived to relieve the city and won a great victory. That afternoon he sent Deputy Commander Kuang Wenbang inside to see Governor Wang Youling, requesting that troops move out during the night and establish a camp outside. They could then link up with General Zhang and open a route for provisions. General Rao, who was present, opposed the proposal, saying, ‘There will still be time tomorrow.’ Unexpectedly, the rebel Li Xiucheng erected a wooden fortification during the night, cutting off the supply route from Zhang’s camp. Earthen works were also built across the moat all around the city’s ten gates. When General Zhang sent Deputy Commander Kuang to see the governor and pledged to destroy the rebels, the people had craned their necks in eager expectation, all declaring that the enemy would surely be crushed.” After reading the poem and its original note, Gu asked, “Who is General Rao?” “Rao Tingxuan. He became famous through Madam Shen when he relieved Guangxin Prefecture. In truth, he is useless.” Hu sighed. “How many times have I told Governor Wang that Rao’s achievements depended on others, and that the man himself is a coward! Governor Wang would not listen. That was the one chance of saving Hangzhou. Once it was lost, not even an immortal could save the place.” “And Zhang Yuliang?” Gu asked. “Everyone says he is no good. What is the truth about him?” “Read on. The next poem explains.”

诗中是这样交代∶桓侯勇健世无双，飞炮当前岂肯降？万马不嘶军尽泣，将星如斗落长江。『怎么？阵亡了？』『阵亡了。』胡雪岩摇摇头，『这个人也耽误了大事，嘉兴一败，金华兰溪又守不住，杭州就危险了。不过，总算亏他。』『诗里拿他比做张飞，说得他很好。』『他是阵亡殉国的，自然要说得他好。』胡雪岩黯然说道∶『我劝王雪公暂且避一避。好比推牌九摇摊一样，这一庄手气不顺；歇一歇手，重新来过。王雪公不肯，他说他当初劝何根云，守土有责，决不可轻离常州；现在自己倒言行不符；怎么交代得过去？『『看起来王雪公倒是忠臣。』

The poem explained it thus: “Marquis Huan, unrivalled in courage and strength, / Would never yield before the flying cannon shot. / Ten thousand horses silent, the whole army weeps; / A commander’s star, huge as a grain measure, falls into the Yangtze.” “What? Was he killed?” “Killed in battle.” Hu shook his head. “He too did enormous harm through his failures. After his defeat at Jiaxing, he could not hold Jinhua or Lanxi either, and Hangzhou was placed in danger. Still, we owed something to him in the end.” “The poem compares him to Zhang Fei. It praises him highly.” “He died fighting for his country. Naturally they must speak well of him.” Hu’s face darkened. “I urged Governor Wang to withdraw for a while. It is like banking at pai gow or playing the dice-shaking game: if luck runs against you, stop for a spell and begin again. But he refused. He said that he had once told He Genyun that an official was bound to defend the territory entrusted to him and must on no account abandon Changzhou lightly. If he now acted contrary to his own words, how could he justify himself?” “It seems Governor Wang is a loyal servant of the throne after all.”

『忠臣？』胡雪岩冷笑∶『忠臣几个钱一斤？我看他——。』语声哽咽欲绝。古应春从未听胡雪岩说过什么愤激的话，而居然将『忠臣』说得一文不值，可以想见他内心的沉痛悲愤。只是苦于没有话可以安慰他。『先吃饭吧！』七姑奶奶说，『天大的事，总也得吃饱了才好打主意。而且小爷叔真的也饿了。』『提到杭州，我哪里还吃得下饭？』胡雪岩泪汪汪地抬眼，『你看最后那两首诗。』古应春细细看了下，颜色大变；七姑奶奶不免奇怪，『怎么了？』她问，『说什么？』『你听我念！』古应春一个字一个字地念。

“A loyal servant?” Hu gave a bitter laugh. “How many coins a catty are loyal servants worth? I think he—” His voice choked and nearly failed. Gu had never heard him speak with such angry bitterness. That he should now dismiss loyalty as utterly worthless revealed the pain and indignation within him. Yet Gu could think of nothing to offer him in comfort. “Let us eat first,” the Seventh Lady said. “However great the trouble, we must fill our stomachs before we can think what to do. And Little Uncle really is hungry.” “How can I eat when we are talking of Hangzhou?” Hu looked up, his eyes full of tears. “Read the last two poems.” Gu read them closely and his face changed. The Seventh Lady could not understand it. “What is it?” she asked. “What do they say?” “Listen.” He read aloud, pronouncing every word distinctly.

剜肉人来非补疮，饥民争啖事堪伤；一腔热血三升血，强作龙肝凤脯尝。『什么？』七姑奶奶大惊问道『人吃人？』古应春不即回答，一个字一个字地念着注解∶『兵勇肆掠，居民鸣锣捕获，解送凤山门王中丞常驻之处。中丞询实，请王命尽斩之；尸积道旁，兵士争取心肝下酒，饥民亦争脔食之。「食人肉」，平日见诸史乘者，至此身亲见之。』就这一段话，将厅前厅后的人，听得一个个面无人色，七姑奶奶连摇摇头∶『世界变了！有这样的事！』『我也不大相信。小爷叔真有其事？』『不但真有其事，简直叫无足为奇。』胡雪岩容颜惨淡地喘着气说∶『人饿极了，什么东西都会吃。』他接下来，便讲杭州绝粮的情形——这年浙西大熟，但正当收割之际，长毛如潮水般涌到；官军节节败退，现成的稻谷，反而资敌，得以作长围久困之计。否则，数十万长毛无以支持；杭州之围也就不解而自解了。

“Men come to carve flesh, not to heal a wound; / The starving vie to eat it—grief beyond words. / A breastful of hot blood, three measures of blood, / Forced down as though it were dragon’s liver or phoenix breast.” “What?” the Seventh Lady cried in horror. “People eating people?” Gu did not answer at once, but read out the note word by word. “Soldiers pillaged at will. The inhabitants sounded gongs, seized them and brought them to Fengshan Gate, where Governor Wang was usually stationed. The governor established the facts and, invoking the emperor’s authority, had them all beheaded. Their bodies lay heaped by the roadside. Soldiers fought for their hearts and livers to eat with wine, and starving civilians likewise competed for morsels of their flesh. ‘Eating human flesh’, something formerly encountered only in histories, I now witnessed with my own eyes.” Everyone within hearing, before the hall and behind it, turned pale. The Seventh Lady shook her head again and again. “The world has changed! That such things should happen!” “I can scarcely believe it myself,” Gu said. “Little Uncle, is it really true?” “Not only true; there is scarcely anything surprising in it.” Hu’s face was bleak as he struggled for breath. “People will eat anything when they are starving.” He went on to describe the famine in Hangzhou. Western Zhejiang had enjoyed a bumper harvest that year, but just as the crop was being gathered, the Longhairs had swept in like a tide. The imperial troops retreated at every turn, and the grain ready for harvesting supplied the enemy instead, enabling them to maintain a long blockade. Otherwise their hundreds of thousands of men could not have sustained themselves, and Hangzhou’s siege would have collapsed of its own accord.

杭州城里的小康之家，自然有些存粮；升斗小民，却立刻就感到了威胁，米店在闭城之前，就已歇业。于是胡雪岩发起开办施粥厂，上中下三城共设四十七处，每日辰、申两次，每次煮米一石，粥少人多，老羽妇孺挤不到前面，有去了三、四次空手而回的。没有多久，粥厂就不能不关闭。但官米还在计口平卖，米卖完了卖豆子，豆卖完了卖麦子。有钱的人家，另有买米的地方，是拿黄金跟鸦片向旗营的八旗兵私下交换军粮。又不久，米麦杂粮都吃得光光，便吃药材南货，熟地、米仁、黄精，都可以代饭；枣栗之类，视如珍品，而海参，鱼翅等等席上之珍，反倒是穷人的食料。

The comfortably off households in Hangzhou naturally held some grain in reserve, but people who lived from one small measure of rice to the next felt the danger immediately. The rice shops had closed even before the gates were shut. Hu therefore took the lead in establishing soup kitchens: forty-seven throughout the upper, middle and lower divisions of the city. They served twice daily, in the morning and late afternoon, cooking one shi of rice at each serving. There was too little congee and too great a crowd. The old and infirm, women and children could not force their way to the front; some went three or four times and came away empty-handed. Before long, the kitchens had to close. Government grain was still sold at controlled prices in quantities allotted by household size. When the rice was gone, they sold beans; when the beans were gone, wheat. The rich had another source of rice: they secretly exchanged gold and opium with the Eight Banner troops for their military rations. Soon the rice, wheat and other grains were all exhausted, and people turned to medicinal roots and dried provisions. Prepared rehmannia, Job’s tears and Solomon’s seal could all serve in place of meals. Dates and chestnuts became treasures, while sea cucumbers, shark’s fins and other delicacies of the banquet table became the food of the poor.

再后来就是吃糠、吃皮箱、吃钉鞋——钉鞋是牛皮做的；吃浮萍，吃草根树皮。杭州人好佛，有钱人家的老太太，最喜欢『放生』；有处地方叫小云楼，专养放生的牛羊猪鸭，自然一扫而空了。『杭州城里的人，不是人，是鬼；一个个骨头瘦得成了一把，望过去脸上三个洞，两个洞是眼睛，一个洞是嘴巴。走在路上，好比「风吹鸭蛋壳」，飘飘荡荡，站不住脚。』胡雪岩喘口气，很吃力地说∶『好比两个人在路上遇着，有气无力在谈话；说着，说着，有一个就会无缘无故倒了下去。另一个要去扶他；不扶还好，一扶头昏眼花，自己也一跟头栽了下去，爬不起来了。象这样子的，「倒路尸」，不晓得有多少？幸亏是冬天，如果是夏天，老早就生瘟疫了。』『那末，』七姑奶奶急急问道∶『府上呢？』

After that they ate bran, leather trunks and hobnailed shoes—the shoes were made of oxhide—then duckweed, grass roots and tree bark. The people of Hangzhou were devoted to Buddhism, and old ladies of wealthy families particularly loved to purchase animals and spare their lives. At a place called Little Cloud Tower, cattle, sheep, pigs and ducks were kept for this purpose. Naturally, they were all swept away. “The people in Hangzhou are no longer people. They are ghosts, every one of them a bundle of bones. Look at a face and you see three holes: two for the eyes and one for the mouth. In the streets they are like empty eggshells blown by the wind, swaying and drifting, unable to keep their feet.” Hu paused for breath, then continued with great effort. “Two people might meet in the road and talk, both too weak to raise their voices. As they speak, one suddenly falls down for no apparent reason. The other tries to help him. It would be better if he did not: the moment he bends to lift him, his own head swims, his eyes blur, and down he goes too, unable to rise. Who can count the bodies left in the roads like that? It is fortunate that this is winter. In summer, plague would have broken out long ago.” “Then what of your own family?” the Seventh Lady asked urgently.

『生死不明。』胡雪岩垂泪说道∶『早在八月里，我老娘说是避到乡下好；全家大小送到北高峰下的上天竺，城一关，就此消息不知。』『一定不要紧的。』七姑奶奶说，『府上是积善之家，老太太又喜欢行善做好事，吉人天相，一定平安无事。』『唉！』古应春叹口气，『浩劫！』这时已经钟打八点，一串大蟹，蒸而又冷，但得知素称佛地的杭州，竟有人吃人的惨状，上上下下，谁都吃不下饭。七姑奶奶做主人的，自不能不劝；但草草终席，塞责而已。吃饱了的，只有一个闻信赶来的尤五，吃他徒弟的喜酒，自然奉为上宾；席间听得胡雪岩已到的消息，急于脱身，但仍旧被灌了好些酒，方得离席。此时一见之下，酒意去了七八分，只望着胡雪岩发愣。

“I do not know whether they are alive or dead.” Tears fell as Hu spoke. “Back in the eighth month, my mother thought we would be safer taking refuge in the country. I sent the whole household, young and old, to Upper Tianzhu below the Northern Peak. Once the city gates shut, all news of them ceased.” “They will be all right,” the Seventh Lady said. “Yours is a family that has accumulated merit. Your mother is always doing good and helping people. Heaven protects the virtuous. They will certainly be safe.” “Ah!” Gu sighed. “What a catastrophe!” By now the clock was striking eight. The great crabs had been steamed and had grown cold, but after learning that Hangzhou, so often called a Buddhist sanctuary, had been reduced to the horror of people eating one another, no one in the household could swallow a meal. As hostess, the Seventh Lady was obliged to urge them to eat; they merely went through the motions and soon left the table. The only man who had eaten his fill was You Wu, who arrived after hearing the news. At his pupil’s wedding feast he had naturally been treated as an honoured guest. When he learnt that Hu had come, he was eager to leave, but still had to drink a good deal more before he could get away. Now the sight of Hu drove most of the wine from his head. He could only stare.

『小爷叔，怎么弄成这个样子？』『五哥，你不要问他了。真正人间地狱，九死一生，现在商量正事吧！』『请到里头来。』七姑奶奶说，『我替小爷铺排好了。』她将胡雪岩的卧室安排在古应春书斋旁边的一间小屋；裱糊得雪白的窗子，生着极大的火盆，一张西洋铜床铺得极厚的被褥。同时又预备了『独参汤』和滋养而易于消化的食物；让他一面吃、一面谈。

“Little Uncle, how have you come to this?” “Brother Five, do not ask him about it. It was truly hell on earth; he escaped by the narrowest margin. Let us discuss what must be done.” “Please come inside,” the Seventh Lady said. “I have everything ready for Little Uncle.” She had prepared a small room beside Gu’s study as Hu’s bedroom. The windows were papered a spotless white, a great brazier was burning, and a Western brass bed had been furnished with thick quilts and bedding. She had also prepared a decoction of ginseng alone, together with nourishing, easily digested food, so that he could eat while they talked.

实际上是由古应春替他发言，『五哥，』他说，『杭州的百姓都要活活饿死了，小爷叔是受王抚台的重托，到上海来办米的；越多越好，越快越好。』『浙江藩库发了两万银子；现银没法带，我是空手来的。』胡雪岩说，『我钱庄里也不知道怎么样？五哥，这笔帐只好以后再算了。』『钱小事，』古应春接口说道，『我垫。』『也用不着你垫，』尤五接口说道，『通裕庄一千石米在仓里；另外随时可以弄一千石，如果不够；再想办法。米总好办，就是怎么样运法？』

In fact it was Gu who spoke for him. “Brother Five, the people of Hangzhou are starving to death. Governor Wang has entrusted Little Uncle with the task of buying rice in Shanghai. The more the better, and the sooner the better.” “The Zhejiang provincial treasury allocated twenty thousand taels,” Hu said. “But I could not bring silver with me. I have come empty-handed, and I do not know what has happened to my bank either. Brother Five, this account will have to be settled later.” “Money is a small matter,” Gu put in. “I shall advance it.” “There is no need even for that,” You replied. “Tongyu has a thousand shi of rice in its warehouse. I can get another thousand at any time. If that is not enough, we shall find more. The rice itself is easy enough. The question is how to transport it.”

译文判断与原文疑点：
1. 第165行“国人望岁君胡胄”初看疑误，后查明是《左传·哀公十六年》叶公入楚的压缩用典：一人先劝他戴盔以防受伤，另一人又以百姓盼望见其面容、如盼丰收为由问“何为胄”，劝其摘盔振奋民心。故胡=何/为何、胄=头盔，原字可以讲通，不应擅改成“胡青”等字。本译保留盼丰收与头盔两个意象，不在小说正文插入大段注释。“you”按这一典故显出百姓期盼将领的对象。依据：教育部《重编国语辞典》“望岁”条 https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=162991&la=0&powerMode=0 ；《成大历史学报》第39期论文引《左传》叶公段原文（文内第31页）https://nckur.lib.ncku.edu.tw/bitstream/987654321/130928/1/1010315005-000087.pdf 。
2. 第189行“一腔热血三升血”疑有复字/讹字，未擅改为人肉或其他字，按原文保留两次血的意象；三升保留 three measures，不强换现代公升。诗的各行以 / 分隔，仍属同一阅读段落。
3. 第151行“如兴知府”据同段绍兴事件及上下文按“绍兴知府”理解；第197行“老羽妇孺”据情境按“老弱妇孺”理解；第215前“小爷”按反复使用的小爷叔处理。引号错乱及跨行断句依据说话者恢复，但未改源文件。
4. 第131行“外国医生的看法来得慢”按人物谈论诊治速度译为 take their time over a case，不替人物添加现代医学解释。伤科医嘱、脉象判断和饮食禁忌为小说人物观点，译文照叙事保留。
5. 王雪公即王有龄，本批称 Governor Wang；第177行何根云为既有 He Genyun。桓侯与张飞的对应由紧接着的人物对话确认；军星坠“长江”按原文保留 Yangtze，未代作者作地理校改。
6. 金额及粮数：浙江藩库拨20,000两，但胡未带到上海；古应春提出垫款，尤五随即称无需垫款；通裕庄已有1,000石，另1,000石可随时筹得，不把拟筹粮数写成已运到杭州。全城47处粥厂，每日两次，每次各一石。辰、申概括为早间与午后，不误当两天。
7. 原文只写朗读“最后两首”的其中一首，译文仅译实际提供的诗，不补写缺失的另一首。小说说九月二十六至十一月初五围城40天，照录人物计算，未自行历法校正。
8. 饥荒食人及清创为历史小说中的非性叙事，完整翻译，未进行安全删改。
