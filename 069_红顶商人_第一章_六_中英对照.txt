胡雪岩｜中英对照版
原著：高阳

红顶商人｜The Red-Topped Merchant

第一章（本章第6部分，共6部分）

阅读说明：每段中文后接对应英文；两版英文正文一致。下载站水印已清除，原始疑字在文末说明。

『没有测量过。但是在岸上用长枪射击，就能打到船上也没有力量了。』华尔摇摇头∶『我不怕步枪。』他接着又问∶『有没有炮台？』『决没有。』古应春在旁边接口。『即使没有炮台，也一定有临时安置的炮位。如果是我，一定在这里部署炮兵阵地。』『你不要将洪杨部队，估计得太高。』古应春又说，『他们不可能了解你们的兵法。』这一点，华尔认为说得不错；他跟长毛接过许多次仗，对此颇有了解，他们连用洋枪都不十分熟练，当然不会懂得用火力扼守要隘的战法。要进一步看，即使懂得，亦用不着防守这个隘口，因为在这一带的清军，兵力薄弱，更无水师会通过这个隘口，增援杭州；那末，布炮防守，岂不是置利器于无用之地。

“It has never been measured. But even if a shot fired from the shore reached a vessel, it would have lost its force.” Ward shook his head. “Rifles do not worry me.” Then he asked, “Are there any batteries?” “Certainly not,” Gu answered from beside him. “Even without permanent batteries, there must be temporary gun positions. If I were there, I should certainly deploy artillery at this point.” “You must not rate the Taiping forces too highly,” Gu said. “They cannot understand your methods of warfare.” Ward thought there was truth in this. He had fought the rebels many times and knew something of their abilities. They were not even fully proficient with foreign firearms; naturally, they would not understand the tactics of holding a strategic passage by concentrated fire. Moreover, even if they did understand, there would be no reason to defend this particular passage. The Qing forces in the area were weak, and there was no naval force likely to pass through it to relieve Hangzhou. To station guns there would be to put valuable weapons where they served no purpose.

但是，『多算胜』的道理，中外兵法都是一样的；华尔觉得还是要采用比较安全的办法，所以又问∶『这个隘口，是不是很长？』『不会。』古应春估计着说∶『至多十里八里路。』『那末，用什么船呢？』『用海船。』所谓海船就是沙船。华尔学的是陆军，对船舶是外行；不过风向顺逆之理总知道的，指着地图说道∶『现在是西北风的季节，由东向西行驶；风向很不利。』『这一点，』古应春很谨慎地答道∶『我想你不必过虑，除了用帆以外，总还有其它辅助航行的办法。海船坚固高大，船身就具备相当的防御力；照我想，是相当安全的。』『这方面，我还要研究；我要跟船队的指挥者研究。最好，我们能在黑夜之间，偷渡这个隘口，避免跟洪杨部队发生正面的冲突。』这样的口气，已经是答应派兵护航了，杨坊便很高兴地说∶『谢谢上校！我们今天就作个决定，将人数以及你所希望补助的饷银，定规下来，你看如何？』

Still, the principle that victory favoured the side which planned more carefully was common to Chinese and foreign military thinking. Ward preferred to take the safer course. “Is this narrow passage very long?” he asked. “No,” Gu estimated. “Eight or ten li at most.” “And what sort of vessels will you use?” “Sea-going junks.” These were the flat-bottomed trading vessels known as shachuan. Ward was trained for land warfare and knew little about ships, but he understood the effect of a favourable or contrary wind. Pointing to the map, he said, “This is the season of northwesterly winds. Sailing from east to west, you will have the wind against you.” “On that point,” Gu answered cautiously, “I do not think you need be too anxious. Besides sails, there must be other ways of helping the vessels along. These sea-going junks are large and strongly built. Their hulls alone provide considerable protection. I should think them fairly safe.” “I must look into that further. I shall have to discuss it with the commander of the flotilla. Ideally, we should slip through the passage at night and avoid a direct engagement with the Taiping forces.” His tone already suggested agreement to provide an escort. Yang spoke with delight. “Thank you, Colonel! Shall we settle the matter today—the number of men and the contribution you would like towards their pay?”

『你们要五十个人，我照数派给你们。其他的细节，请你们明天跟我的军需官商量。』『好的！』杨坊欣然答道∶『完全遵照你的意思。』于是『化干戈为玉帛』，古应春亦含笑道谢，告辞上车。『老古，』在车中，杨坊表示钦佩∶『你倒是真有一套。以后我们多多合作。』『侥幸！亏得高人指点。』古应春说∶『也是胡道台一句话∶请将不如激将。果然把华尔激成功了。』『原来胡道台也是办洋务的好手。』『他倒不十分懂洋务，只是人情熟透熟透！』『几时我倒要见见他。』杨坊又说∶『华尔的「军需官」，也是我们中国人；我极熟的。明天晚上我约他出来吃花酒，一切都好谈。』『那好极了。应该我做东。明天早晨，我就行帖子送到你那里，请你代劳。』

“You asked for fifty men. I shall give you fifty. Discuss the other details with my quartermaster tomorrow.” “Certainly!” Yang replied gladly. “We shall do exactly as you wish.” Hostility had given way to friendship. Gu too smiled, offered his thanks, and took his leave. “Gu,” Yang said admiringly in the carriage, “you really know how to handle a situation. We must work together more often.” “It was good luck, with some guidance from a wiser man. Intendant Hu gave me one piece of advice: a challenge will move a commander more readily than a request. And it did move Ward.” “So Intendant Hu is skilled in dealing with foreigners as well.” “He knows little of foreign affairs. What he understands through and through is human nature.” “I must meet him sometime,” Yang said. “Ward’s quartermaster is Chinese, and I know him extremely well. Tomorrow evening I shall invite him to dine with courtesans. Everything will be easy to discuss.” “Excellent. I ought to be the host. Tomorrow morning I shall send the invitations to your house and ask you to arrange it.”

『你做东，还是我做东，都一样。这就不去说它了，倒是有句话，我要请教∶杭州不是被围了吗？粮船到了那里，怎么运进城。』这句话让古应春一楞，『啊，』他如梦初醒似地，『这倒是！我还没有想到。等我回去问了，再答复你。』『可以不可以今天就给我一个确实回音？』到了杭州的事，此刻言之过早；而且米能不能运进杭州城，与杨坊无干，何以他这么急着要答复？看起来，别有作用，倒不能不弄个明白。这样想着，便即问道∶『为什么这么急？』『我另外有个想法。如果能运进杭州城，那就不必谈了；否则——。』杨坊忽然问道∶『能不能此刻就替我引见，我想跟胡道台当面谈一谈。』『这有什么不可以？』

“It makes no difference whether you give the dinner or I do. Let us leave that aside. There is something I should like to ask you. Is Hangzhou not under siege? Once the grain boats get there, how will the rice enter the city?” Gu was taken aback. “Ah!” he said, as though waking from a dream. “Of course. I had not thought of that. I shall ask when I get back and let you know.” “Could you give me a definite answer tonight?” It seemed premature to worry about what would happen once the boats reached Hangzhou. Besides, whether the rice could enter the city was no concern of Yang’s. Why did he want an answer so urgently? There seemed to be another purpose behind it, and Gu felt he must find out. “Why the hurry?” “I have another idea. If the rice can enter Hangzhou, there is nothing more to say. Otherwise—” Yang broke off to ask, “Could you introduce me to him now? I should like to discuss it with Intendant Hu himself.” “Why not?”

于是马车转向，直驶古家；车一停，萧家骥首先奔了进去通知。胡雪岩很讲究礼节，要起床在客厅里迎接会面；七姑奶奶坚决反对，结果折衷办法，起床而不出房门，就在卧室里接见客人。女眷自然回避。等古应春将杨坊迎了进来，胡雪岩已经穿上长袍马褂，扶着萧家骥的肩，等在门口了。彼此都闻名已久，所以见礼以后，非常亲热，互相仰慕，话题久久不断。古应春找个机会，插进话去，将与华尔交涉的经过，略略说了一遍；胡雪岩原已从萧家骥口中，得知梗概，此刻少不得要向杨坊殷殷致谢。『都是为家乡的事，应当出力。不过，』杨坊急转直下的转入本题∶『粮船到了杭州，不晓得怎么运进杭州？』

The carriage changed direction and drove straight to Gu’s house. As soon as it stopped, Xiao ran inside to announce the visitor. A great stickler for courtesy, Hu wanted to get out of bed and receive him in the reception room. The Seventh Lady firmly opposed this. They finally compromised: he might get up, but must remain in his bedroom and receive the guest there. Naturally, the women withdrew. By the time Gu ushered Yang inside, Hu had put on a long gown and riding jacket and was waiting at the doorway, leaning on Xiao’s shoulder. Each had long known the other by reputation. Once the formal greetings were over, they spoke with great warmth and mutual admiration, their conversation continuing for some time. Gu found an opening to give a brief account of the negotiations with Ward. Hu had already heard the outline from Xiao, but now he naturally thanked Yang most earnestly. “It concerns our home province. I ought to do what I can. But”—Yang turned abruptly to the business that concerned him—“once the grain boats reach Hangzhou, how is the rice to enter the city?”

提到这一层，胡雪岩的脸色，马上转为忧郁了；叹口气说∶『唉！这件事也是失策。关城之先，省城里的大员，意见就不一，有的说十个城门统通要关；有的说应该留一两个不关。结果是统统关了。这里一关，长毛马上在城外掘壕沟，做木墙。围困得实腾腾。』他一口气说到这里，喘息了一下又说∶『当初还有人提议，从城上筑一道斜坡，直到江边，作为粮道。这个主意听起来出奇∶大家都笑。而且工程也浩大，所以就没有办。其实，此刻想来，实在是一条好计；如果能够这么做，虽费点事，可是粮道不断，杭州就能守得住！』接着，又是一声长叹。听得这样说法，古应春先就大为着急∶『小爷叔，』他问∶『照你这么说，我们不是劳而无功？』『这也不见得。』胡雪岩说∶『只要粮船一到，城里自然拼死命杀开一条血路，护粮进城。』杨坊点点头，看一看古应春，欲语不语地；胡雪岩察言观色，便知其中有话。

At this, Hu’s face immediately clouded. He sighed. “That was another mistake. Before the city was closed, the high officials disagreed. Some wanted all ten gates shut; others thought one or two should be left open. In the end, they shut them all. As soon as that happened, the rebels dug trenches and put up wooden barriers outside. They sealed us in completely.” Having said this in one breath, he paused for air, then continued. “Someone had proposed building a sloping causeway from the city wall right down to the river, to serve as a supply route. It sounded so extraordinary that everyone laughed. The work would also have been enormous, so nothing was done. Looking back now, it was an excellent idea. It would have taken some trouble, but with the supply route open, Hangzhou could have held out.” He gave another long sigh. Gu was the first to show alarm. “Little Uncle, if that is so, will all our efforts be wasted?” “Not necessarily. Once the grain boats arrive, the people inside will fight their way out at all costs and escort the grain into the city.” Yang nodded, glanced at Gu, and seemed about to speak, then stopped. Watching his expression, Hu knew he had something to say.

『杨兄，』他说，『你我一见如故，有话尽请直说。』『是这样的，我当然也希望杭州的同乡，有一口活命的饭吃。不过，凡事要从最坏的地方去打算∶万一千辛万苦将粮船开到杭州，城里城外交通断绝，到时候，胡先生，你怎么办？』『我请问杨兄，依你看，应该怎么办？』『在商言商，这许多米，总不能送给长毛，更不能丢在江里。』杨坊说道∶『如果运不进杭州城，可以不可以请胡先生改运宁波？』原来他急于要见胡雪岩，是为了这句话。古应春心想∶此人倒也是厉害脚色，『门槛』精得很，不可小觑了他。因此，很注意地要听胡雪岩如何回答。『杨兄的话很实在。如果米运不进杭州城，我当然改运别处，只要不落在长毛手里，运到什么地方都可以。』说到这里，胡雪岩下了一个转语∶『不过，杨兄的话，我倒一时答应不下。为什么呢？因为宁波的情形，我还不晓得；许了杨兄，倘或办不到，岂不是我变成失信用。』『宁波的情形，跟上海差不多——。』

“Brother Yang, we have met as though we were old friends. Please speak freely.” “The situation is this. Naturally, I too want our fellow provincials in Hangzhou to have enough food to stay alive. But one must allow for the worst. Suppose you get the grain boats to Hangzhou after endless hardships, only to find all communication between the city and the outside world cut off. What will you do then, Mr Hu?” “What would you advise?” “Speaking as a businessman, you can hardly hand all that rice to the rebels or throw it into the river. If it cannot enter Hangzhou, could I ask you to divert it to Ningbo?” So this was why he had been so eager to meet Hu. Gu thought to himself that Yang was a formidable fellow, shrewd in every calculation, and not to be underestimated. He listened closely for Hu’s reply. “That is a practical suggestion. If the rice cannot enter Hangzhou, of course I shall send it elsewhere. So long as it does not fall into rebel hands, any destination will do.” Then Hu qualified his answer. “But I cannot promise what you ask just yet. Why? Because I do not know the situation in Ningbo. If I gave you my word and then could not fulfil it, I should be breaking faith.” “The situation in Ningbo is much like Shanghai’s—”

因为宁波也有租界。江苏的富室逃到上海，浙东的大户，则以宁波租界为避难之地；早在夏天，宁波的士绅就条陈地方官，愿集资五十万两银子，雇英法兵船代守宁波，及至萧绍失守，太平军一路向东，势如破竹，攻余姚、下慈溪、陷奉化，宁波旦夕不保；于是英、法、美三国领事，会商以后，决定派人到奉化会晤太平军守将范汝增，劝他暂缓进攻宁波。范汝增对这个请求，不作正面答复，但应允保护洋人，因此三国领事已经会衔了布告，保护租界；但陆路交通，近乎断绝，商旅裹足，也在大闹粮荒。杨坊的打算，一方面固然是为桑梓尽力；另一方面亦有善价而沽，趁此机会做一笔生意的想法。

Ningbo too had a foreign settlement. Just as wealthy Jiangsu families had fled to Shanghai, the rich households of eastern Zhejiang had taken refuge in the Ningbo settlement. As early as the summer, Ningbo’s gentry had submitted proposals to the local authorities, offering to raise five hundred thousand taels to hire British and French warships to defend the city. After Xiaoshan and Shaoxing fell, the Taiping army swept eastward, taking Yuyao, Cixi, and Fenghua in rapid succession. Ningbo might fall at any moment. The British, French, and American consuls therefore conferred and decided to send representatives to Fenghua to meet the Taiping commander, Fan Ruzeng, and urge him to postpone an attack on Ningbo. Fan gave no direct answer to the request, but promised to protect foreigners. The three consuls had consequently issued a joint proclamation guaranteeing the safety of the settlement. Yet land communications were virtually severed, travelling merchants dared not move, and the city was suffering a severe grain shortage. Yang’s purpose was partly to help his native place. It was also to obtain a good price and make a profitable transaction out of the opportunity.

不过杨坊的私心，自然不肯透露，『胡先生，』他说，『据我晓得，逃在宁波的杭州人也不少。所以你拿粮食改运宁波，实在是不得已而求其次的唯一出路。』『那末，到了宁波呢？如果不能上岸，又怎么办？』『不会的。英、法、美三国领事，哪一位都可以出面保护你，到那时候，我当然会从中联络。』『既然如此——。』胡雪岩矍然而起——想好了主意，一时兴奋，忘却腿伤，一下子摔倒在地，疼得额上沁出黄豆大的汗珠。萧家骥动作敏捷，赶紧上前扶起；古应春也吃了一惊，为他检视伤势。乱过一阵，胡雪岩方能接着他自己的话说下去。『杨兄，既然如此，我们做一笔交易。杭州缺粮，宁波也缺粮，我们来合作；宁波，我负责运一批米过去，米、船，都归我想办法。杭州这方面，可以不可以请你托洋人出面，借个做善事的名义，将我这一批米护送进城？』

His private interest, of course, was something Yang had no intention of revealing. “Mr Hu, I understand that many Hangzhou people have taken refuge in Ningbo as well. If you cannot achieve your first object, sending the grain there is really the only alternative.” “And when it reaches Ningbo? What if it cannot be landed?” “That will not happen. Any one of the three consuls—British, French, or American—can arrange protection for you. Naturally, I shall make the necessary contacts.” “In that case—” Hu sprang to his feet. A plan had occurred to him, and in his excitement he forgot his injured leg. He fell to the floor, pain bringing beads of sweat as large as soybeans to his forehead. Xiao quickly stepped forward to help him up. Gu, badly startled, examined the injury. Only after the commotion subsided could Hu finish what he had been saying. “Brother Yang, in that case, let us make a bargain. Hangzhou is short of grain; so is Ningbo. We can work together. I shall undertake to send a consignment of rice to Ningbo and find both the rice and the vessels. In return, could you ask the foreigners to intervene on charitable grounds and escort this consignment of mine into Hangzhou?”

『这个办法——。』杨坊看着古应春，颇有为难的神情。『小爷叔，做生意，动脑筋，不能不当你诸葛亮。』古应春很委婉地说，『可惜，洋务上，小爷叔你略为有点外行，这件事行不通。』『怎么呢？』『因为外国领事，出面干预，要有个名目；运粮到宁波，可以「护侨」为名，为的洋人不能没有食物接济。但杭州的情形就不同了，并无英法美三国侨民，需要救济；而救济中国百姓，要看地方，在交战区域，民食军粮是无从区分的。』等古应春解释完了，杨坊接着补充∶『八月里，英国京城有一道命令给他们的公使，叫做「严守中立」；这就是说，哪一面也不帮。所以胡先生的这个打算，好倒是好，可惜办不通。』胡雪岩当然失望，但不愿形诸颜色；将话题回到杨坊的要求上，慨然说道∶『那就一言为定了。这批米如果运不进杭州城，就转运宁波。不过，这话要跟郁老大先说明白；到时候，沙船不肯改地方卸货，就要费口舌了。』『这一层，我当然会请应春兄替我打招呼；我要请胡先生吩咐的是粮价——。』

“That proposal…” Yang looked at Gu, visibly uneasy. “Little Uncle, when it comes to business and ingenuity, we must acknowledge you as a Zhuge Liang,” Gu said tactfully. “Unfortunately, you are something of a novice in foreign affairs. This cannot be done.” “Why not?” “Foreign consuls need grounds for intervening. Grain can be sent to Ningbo in the name of protecting their nationals, since foreigners must have food. Hangzhou is different. There are no British, French, or American residents there in need of relief. As for feeding Chinese civilians, it depends on the place. In a war zone, there is no way to distinguish food for civilians from provisions for troops.” When Gu had finished, Yang added, “In the eighth month, the British government sent its minister orders to maintain strict neutrality. That means helping neither side. Your proposal is excellent, Mr Hu, but unfortunately it is impracticable.” Hu was disappointed, of course, but did not want to show it. Returning to Yang’s request, he declared generously, “Then it is settled. If this rice cannot enter Hangzhou, we shall divert it to Ningbo. But Chief Yu must be told beforehand. If the junk owners refuse to discharge at a different destination, we shall have an argument on our hands.” “Of course I shall ask Brother Yingchun to make that clear. What I should like your instructions on is the price of the rice—”

『这不要紧！』胡雪岩有力地打断他的话，『怎么样说都可以。如果是做生意，当然一分一厘都要算清楚；现在不是做生意。』『是，是！』杨坊不免内惭；自语似地说∶『原是做好事。』谈话到此告一段落，古应春怕胡雪岩过于劳累，于伤势不宜，邀了杨坊到客厅里去坐；连萧家骥在一起，商定了跟华尔这方面联络的细节，直到深夜方散。

“That is no difficulty!” Hu cut in firmly. “We can settle it however you wish. If this were business, every fraction of a tael would have to be reckoned. But this is not business.” “Yes, yes.” Yang felt an inward pang of shame. Almost to himself, he said, “Of course, we are doing a good deed.” With that, the discussion reached a conclusion. Afraid that Hu was tiring himself and delaying his recovery, Gu invited Yang into the reception room. There, with Xiao, they settled the details of their further dealings with Ward. The party did not break up until late at night.

第二天大家分头办事，只有胡雪岩在古家养伤，反觉清闲无事；行动不便，不能出房门，一个人觉得很气闷，特为将七姑奶奶请了来，不免有些微怨言。『我是不敢来打扰小爷叔；让你好好养伤。』七姑奶奶解释她的好意，『说话也费精神的。』『唉！七姐，你哪晓我的心事。一个人思前想后，连觉都睡不着；有人谈谈，辰光还好打发。』谈亦不能深谈，胡雪岩一家，消息全无，谈起来正触及他的痛处。因此，平日健谈的七姑奶奶，竟变得笨嘴拙舌，不知道说什么好？『七姐，』胡雪岩问道∶『这一阵，你跟何姨太太有没有往来？』何姨太太就是阿巧姐。从那年经胡雪岩撮合，随着何桂清到通州；不久，何桂清果然出仓场侍郎，外放浙江巡抚；升任两江总督，一路扶摇直上。阿巧姐着实风光过一阵子。『好久没有见到她了。』七姑奶奶不胜感慨地，『那时候哪个不说她福气好？何大人在常州的时候，我去过一次；她特为派官船到松江来接我，还有一百个兵保护，让我也大大出了一次风光。到了常州，何大人也很客气。何太太多病，都是姨太太管事，走到哪里，丫头老妈子一大群跟着，那份气派还了得！人也长得越漂亮了，满头珠翠，看上去真象一品夫人。哪晓得何大人坏了事！前一晌听人说，人都老得认不得了。伍子胥过昭关，一夜工夫急白了头发；看起来真有这样的事。』『这样说起来，她倒还是有良心的。』

Next day everyone went off on his separate business. Hu alone remained at Gu’s house to recover, with nothing to occupy him. Unable to move freely or leave his room, he found solitude oppressive. He sent especially for the Seventh Lady and could not help voicing a mild complaint. “I did not dare disturb you, Little Uncle. I wanted you to rest and let the wound heal,” she explained. “Talking takes strength too.” “Ah, Sister Seven, you do not know what is on my mind. Left alone, I keep turning things over until I cannot even sleep. Someone to talk to would help the time pass.” Yet they could not talk too deeply. There was no news of any member of Hu’s family; to speak of them was to touch his sorest wound. The Seventh Lady, ordinarily so talkative, found herself tongue-tied, at a loss for anything to say. “Sister Seven,” Hu asked, “have you had any dealings with Madam He lately?” Madam He was A’qiao. After Hu had arranged her match with He Guiqing, she had accompanied him to Tongzhou. Before long, He had indeed left his post as granary vice-minister to become governor of Zhejiang, then governor-general of Liangjiang, rising steadily in the world. For a time A’qiao had enjoyed considerable splendour. “I have not seen her for a long while,” the Seventh Lady said with deep feeling. “Who did not call her fortunate then? When Mr He was in Changzhou, I visited once. She sent an official boat to fetch me from Songjiang, with a hundred soldiers for protection. I enjoyed a grand occasion myself! Mr He was very courteous when we arrived. His wife was often ill, and A’qiao managed everything. Wherever she went, a whole crowd of maids and serving women followed. What airs and grandeur! She had grown prettier too, her hair full of pearls and jade. She looked every inch the wife of a first-rank official. Who could have imagined that Mr He would come to grief? Not long ago someone told me she had aged beyond recognition. They say Wu Zixu’s hair turned white in a single night as he struggled to get through Zhaoguan Pass. It seems such things really do happen.” “Then she has a conscience after all.”

『小爷叔是说她为何制台急成这个样子？』『是啊！』胡雪岩说，『我听王雪公说，何制台的罪名不得了。』『怎样不得了？莫非还要杀头？』胡雪岩看着她，慢慢点头，意思是说∶你不要不信，确有可能。『这样大的官儿，也会杀头？』七姑奶奶困惑地，大有不可思议之感。『当然要杀！』胡雪岩忽然出现了罕见的激动，『不借一两个人头做榜样，国家搞不好的。平常作威作福，要粮要饷，说起来是为了朝廷、为了百姓；到真正该他出力的时候，收拾细软，一溜了之。象这样的人，可以安安稳稳拿刮来的钱过舒服日子；尽心出力，打仗阵亡的人，不是太冤枉了吗？』七姑奶奶从未见过朝雪岩有这样气急败坏的愤激之态，因而所感受的冲击极大。同时也想到了他的境况；心里有着说不出的难过。

“You mean she has worried herself into this state over Governor-General He?” “Yes. Governor Wang told me the charges against him are extremely grave.” “How grave? Surely they would not behead him?” Hu looked at her and slowly nodded, as if to say that, unbelievable though it might seem, it was quite possible. “They would execute an official as high as that?” The Seventh Lady found it almost inconceivable. “Of course they should!” Hu suddenly became agitated in a way she seldom saw. “Unless one or two heads are taken as an example, the country will never be put right. In ordinary times they lord it over everyone, demanding grain and money, claiming it is all for the court and the people. When the moment comes for them to do their duty, they gather up their valuables and run. If men like that can live comfortably on the money they have squeezed out of others, what justice is there for the men who do their utmost and die fighting?” She had never seen him so fiercely indignant, and the outburst affected her deeply. Thinking too of his own position, she felt a grief she could not express.

『小爷叔，』她不由自主地说∶『我看，你也用不着到杭州去了；粮船叫五哥的学生子跟家骥押了去，你在上海养养伤，想办法去寻着了老太太，拿一家人都接到上海来，岂不甚好？』『七姐，谢谢你！你是替我打算，不过办不到。』『这有什么办不到？』七姑奶奶振振有词地说话∶『这一路去，有你无你都一样。船归李得隆跟沙船帮的人料理；洋将派来保护的兵，归家骥接头。你一个受了伤的人，自己还要有人照应，去了有帮什么忙？越帮越忙，反而是累赘。』『话不错。不过到了杭州，没有我在从中联络，跟王雪公接不上头，岂不误了大事？』想一想这话也不错；七姑奶奶便又问道∶『只要跟王抚台接上头，城里派兵出来运粮进城；小爷叔，就没有你的事了。』『对。』『那就这样，小爷叔，你不要进城，原船回上海；我们再商量下一步，怎么样想法子去寻老太太。』七姑奶奶又说，『其实，小爷叔你就在杭州城外访查也可以；总而言之，已经出来了，决没有自投罗网的道理。』『这话也说得是——。』

“Little Uncle,” she said involuntarily, “I do not think you need return to Hangzhou. Let Brother Five’s disciple and Jiaji escort the grain boats. Stay in Shanghai and let your wound heal. Find a way to locate your mother and bring the whole family here. Would that not be better?” “Thank you, Sister Seven. You are thinking of my interests. But it cannot be done.” “Why not?” she argued vigorously. “On the voyage it makes no difference whether you are there or not. Li Delong and the Sea-Junk Guild will handle the vessels. Jiaji will deal with the troops sent by the foreign commander. You are wounded and need looking after yourself. What help could you give? You would only make more trouble and become a burden.” “That is true. But once they reach Hangzhou, without me to make contact with Governor Wang, the whole undertaking might be ruined.” On reflection, she could see the force of that. “Once you have reached Governor Wang, and the city sends troops out to escort the grain inside, there is nothing more you need do?” “That is so.” “Then do this. Do not enter the city. Return to Shanghai on the same boat. We can discuss the next step and how to find your mother. You could even make enquiries outside Hangzhou yourself. In any case, having got out, you must not walk back into the trap.” “There is truth in that too—”

听他的语气，下面还有转语；七姑奶奶不容他出口，抢着说道∶『本来就是嘛，小爷叔，你是做生意的大老板；捐班的道台，跟何制台不同，没有啥守土的责任。』『不尽是为公，为的是交情。』胡雪岩说∶『我有今天，都是王抚台的提拔，他现在这样子为难，真正是在十八层地狱里受熬煎，我不跟他共患难，良心上说不过去。』『这自然是义气，不过这份义气，没啥用处。』七姑奶奶说，『倒不如你在外头打接应，还有用些。』这话说得很有道理，但胡雪岩总觉得不能这么做。他做事一向有决断，不容易为感情所左右——其实，就是为感情所左右，也总在自己的算盘上先要打得通；道穿了，不妨说是利用感情。而对王有龄，又当别论了。

His tone suggested that an objection was coming. Before he could voice it, the Seventh Lady broke in. “Of course there is! You are a great businessman, Little Uncle. You purchased your rank as intendant. You are not like Governor-General He; you have no responsibility for defending territory.” “It is not entirely a matter of public duty. It is friendship,” Hu replied. “Everything I am today is due to Governor Wang’s support. He is in such desperate straits now, suffering as though he were in the deepest hell. If I will not share his danger, my conscience will never be clear.” “That is loyalty, certainly. But loyalty of that sort serves no purpose. You could do him more good by helping from outside.” Her argument was sound, yet Hu still felt he could not act on it. Ordinarily he was decisive and not easily swayed by sentiment. Even when sentiment did move him, the course of action usually had to make sense in his own calculations first. To put it plainly, he might be said to make use of sentiment. With Wang Youling, however, it was different.

『唉！』他叹口气，『七姐，我何尝不知道你是一句好话；不但对我一个人好，而且对王雪公也好。不过，我实在办不到。』『这就奇怪了！既然对你好，对他也好，又为什么不这么做？小爷叔，你平日为人不是这样的。』『是的。我平日为人不是这样；唯独这件事，不知道怎么，想来想去想不通。第一、我怕王雪公心里会说；胡某人不够朋友，到要紧关头，他一个人丢下我不管了。第二、我怕旁人说我，只晓得富贵，不知道啥叫生死交情？』『嗳！』七姑奶奶有些着急了，因此口不择言∶『小爷叔，你真是死脑筋，旁人的话，哪里听得那么多，要说王抚台，既然你们是这样深的交情，他也应该晓得你的心。而况，你又并没有丢下他不管；还是替他在外办事。』说到这里，她觉得有一肚子的议论要发∶『为人总要通情达理。三纲五常，总也要合道理，才有用处。我最讨厌那些伪道学，或者不明事理的说法∶什么「君要臣死，不能不死∶父要子亡，不得不亡」！你倒想想看，忠臣死了，哪个替皇帝办事？儿子死了，这一家断宗绝代，孝心又在哪里？』

He sighed. “Sister Seven, do you think I cannot see that your advice is good? Good not only for me, but for Governor Wang as well. Yet I simply cannot do it.” “That is strange! If it is good for you and good for him, why not do it? This is not like you.” “No, it is not. But in this one matter, however much I think, I cannot find a way through. First, I fear Governor Wang will say to himself: ‘Hu is no true friend. At the critical moment, he abandoned me and saved himself.’ Second, I fear other people will say I understand wealth and honour but know nothing of friendship that stands the test of life and death.” “Oh!” Growing impatient, she spoke without choosing her words. “Little Uncle, you are being stubborn. How can you listen to everything other people say? As for Governor Wang, if your friendship runs so deep, he ought to understand your heart. Besides, you would not be abandoning him. You would still be working for him outside.” Now she felt she had a great deal more to say. “People must be reasonable. Even the Three Bonds and Five Constant Virtues are useful only when they make sense. I cannot bear those moralizing hypocrites and their unreasonable doctrines: ‘If the ruler orders his minister to die, he must die; if the father orders his son to perish, he must perish.’ Just think! If the loyal minister is dead, who will serve the emperor? If the son dies and the family line ends, where is the filial devotion in that?”

胡雪岩笑了，『七姐，』他说，『听你讲道理，真是我们杭州人说的∶』刮拉松脆「。好痛快！『』小爷叔，你不要恭维我；你如果觉得我的话，还有点道理，那就要听我的劝！『七姑奶奶讲完君臣、父子；又谈』第五伦『朋友∶』我听说大书的说「三国」，桃园结义，刘关张不愿同年同月同日生，但愿同年同月同日死，这话就不通！如果讲义气的好朋友，死了一个，别的都跟着他一起去死，这世界上，不就没有君子，只剩小人了？『『这话倒是。』胡雪岩兴味盎然，『凡事不能寻根问底，追究到底好些话都不通。』

Hu laughed. “Sister Seven, listening to you reason is what we Hangzhou people call crisp and clear. How refreshing!” “Do not flatter me. If you think my words have any sense in them, take my advice!” Having dealt with ruler and minister, father and son, she turned to friendship, the fifth of the human relationships. “I have heard storytellers recite the Three Kingdoms. When Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei swear brotherhood in the Peach Garden, they say they do not ask to have been born in the same year, month, and day, but wish to die in the same year, month, and day. That makes no sense either! If one loyal friend dies and all the others follow him to the grave, will there not be a world without decent men, with only scoundrels left?” “True enough,” Hu said, thoroughly interested. “One must not examine everything too closely. Follow things to their foundations, and a great many sayings cease to make sense.”

『原是如此！小爷叔，这天把，我夜里总在想你的情形；想你，当然也要想到王抚台。我从前听你说过，他曾劝过何制台不要从常州逃走；说一逃就身败名裂了！这话现在让他说中；想来杭州如果不保，王抚台是决不会逃走；做个大大的忠臣。不过，你要替他想一想，他还有什么好朋友替他料理后事？不就是小爷叔你吗？『这话说得胡雪岩矍然动容，『七姐，』他不安地，『你倒提醒我了。』『谢天谢地！』七姑奶奶合掌当胸，长长地舒了口气∶『小爷叔，你总算想通了。』『想是还没有想通。不过，这件事倒真的要好好想一想。』

“Exactly! Little Uncle, these last nights I have been thinking about your situation. And if I think of you, naturally I must think of Governor Wang. You once told me that he urged Governor-General He not to flee Changzhou, warning that flight would ruin his name and standing. That prediction has come true. If Hangzhou cannot be saved, I am sure Governor Wang will never flee. He will be a great loyal servant of the dynasty. But consider this for his sake: what friend has he to take care of his affairs after his death? Who but you?” Hu looked up, visibly moved. “Sister Seven,” he said uneasily, “you have reminded me of something important.” “Thank Heaven!” She pressed her palms together at her breast and let out a long breath. “At last you have seen reason.” “Not quite. But this really is something I must think about carefully.”

于是他一面跟七姑奶奶闲谈，一面在心里盘算。看样子七姑奶奶的话丝毫不错，王有龄这个忠臣是做定了！杭州的情形，要从外面看，才知道危险；被围在城里的，心心念念只有一个想法∶救兵一到，便可解围。其实，就是李元度在衢州的新军能够打到杭州，亦未见得能击退重重包围的长毛。破城是迟早间事；王有龄殉节，亦是迟早间事。且不说一城的眼光，都注视在他身上，容不得他逃；就有机会也不能逃走，因为一逃，不但所有的苦头都算白吃，而且象何桂清这样子，就能活又有什么味道？『我想通了。』胡雪岩说∶『王雪公是死定了！我要让他死得值。』

He continued talking with her while turning the matter over in his mind. It appeared that she was entirely right: Wang Youling was determined to fulfil the role of a loyal minister. Only from outside could one see how perilous Hangzhou’s position was. Those shut inside the besieged city had only one thought: once reinforcements arrived, the siege would be lifted. Yet even if Li Yuandu’s newly raised army at Quzhou could fight its way to Hangzhou, it might not be able to drive off the many surrounding rebel forces. The city’s fall was only a matter of time; so was Wang’s death in the cause of loyalty. Quite apart from the eyes of the whole city, which were fixed upon him and would not permit him to flee, he could not run even if an opportunity presented itself. Escape would make all his suffering meaningless. And if he lived on as He Guiqing now did, what pleasure would there be in life? “I have thought it through,” Hu said. “Governor Wang is bound to die. I must make his death count.”

『是嘛！』七姑奶奶异常欣慰，『原说小爷叔是绝顶聪明的人，哪里会连这点道理都想不通？常言的道的是「生死交情」，一个人死了，有人照他生前那样子待他；这个人就算有福气了。』『是啊！他殉了节，一切都在我身上；就怕——。』他虽没有说出口来，也等于说明白了一样——。这倒不是他自己嫌忌讳；是怕七姑奶奶伤心。然而，在这样的情形之下，以七姑奶奶的性情，自然也会有句痛快话。『小爷叔，这一层你请放心。万一有个三长两短，一切都在我们兄妹夫妻身上。』『是了！』胡雪岩大大地喘了口气，『有七姐你这句话，我什么地方都敢去闯。』这话又说得不中听了，七姑奶奶有些不安∶『小爷叔，』她惴惴然地问∶『你是怎么闯法？』『我当然不会闯到死路上去。我说的闯是，遇到难关，壮起胆子来闯。』胡雪岩说，『不瞒你说，这一路来，我遇见长毛，实在有点怕；现在我不怕了，越怕越误事，索性大胆去闯，反倒没事。』

“Exactly!” The Seventh Lady was immensely relieved. “I always said you were an exceptionally clever man. How could you fail to grasp so simple a truth? People speak of friendship through life and death. If, after a man dies, someone still treats him as faithfully as when he was alive, he is a fortunate man.” “Yes. Once he dies for his duty, everything will fall to me. My only fear is—” Though he stopped, his meaning was as plain as if he had finished. It was not that he himself shrank from mentioning an ill omen; he feared distressing her. But in such circumstances a woman of her temperament would naturally have something direct to say. “Little Uncle, set your mind at ease on that score. If anything happens to you, my brother, my husband, and I will take everything upon ourselves.” “Good!” Hu drew a great breath. “With that assurance from you, Sister Seven, there is nowhere I would fear to venture.” This again was unwelcome language. Uneasily, she asked, “Little Uncle, what sort of venture do you mean?” “I certainly do not mean rushing down a road to death. I mean facing a difficult passage with courage. To tell you the truth, I was rather frightened whenever I encountered the rebels on my way here. Now I am not afraid. The more frightened one is, the more likely one is to make a mistake. Facing things boldly may prove the safer course.”

译者说明
本段承接第068批护航谈判。华尔同意派五十人，薪饷及其他细节仍待与军需官商谈；粮船尚未启程。宁波五十万两是士绅提出筹集的数额，不是已付出的费用。胡雪岩承诺的改运宁波，以粮食无法进杭州为前提；他提出两地合作换取外交护粮的方案遭否定，不能混为已经实施的安排。
原文“王雪公”“王抚台”均指王有龄，英文按上下文统一Governor Wang。何姨太太是阿巧姐，非何桂清正妻。何桂清可能被处死和王有龄必将殉节，此处分别是谈话中的判断及人物的预期，并非本段已经发生的事件。
原文第607行含下载站水印，属非小说杂质，正文不译，排版时从中文对照段删除并保留清理记录。朝雪岩显然指胡雪岩；引号倒置及跨行断句按明晰语义整理，不添情节。
