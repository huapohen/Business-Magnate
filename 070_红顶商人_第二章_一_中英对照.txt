第二章｜CHAPTER TWO

由济河出长江，经崇明岛南面入海；一共是十八号沙船，保护的洋兵——最后商量定规，一共是一百十二个人，一百士兵，大多是『吕宋人』；十二个官长，七个吕宋人，三个美国人，还有两个中国人算是联络官。分坐两号沙船，插在船队中间。胡雪岩是在第一条船上。同船的有萧家骥、李得隆、郁馥华派来的『船老大』李庆山；还有一个姓孔的联络官。一切进退行止，都由这五个人在这条船上商量停当，发号施令。一上船，胡雪岩就接到警告，沙船行在海里，忌讳甚多，舵楼上所设，内供天后神牌的小神龛，尤其不比等闲。想起『是非只为多开口』这句话，胡雪岩在船上便不大说话，闲下来只躺在铺位上想心事。但是，别人不同，萧家骥虽惯于水上生活，但轮船上并无这些忌讳；姓孔的更不在乎；李庆山和李得隆识得忌讳，不该说虽不说，该说的还是照常要说。相形之下，就显得平日谈笑风生的胡雪岩仿佛心事重重，神情万分抑郁似的。

They sailed out of the Ji River into the Yangtze, then passed south of Chongming Island and entered the sea. There were eighteen sea junks in all. The foreign escort, as finally agreed, numbered a hundred and twelve: a hundred soldiers, most of them men from Luzon, and twelve officers—seven from Luzon, three Americans and two Chinese serving as liaison officers. They occupied two junks in the middle of the convoy. Hu Xueyan travelled aboard the leading vessel, together with Xiao Jiaji, Li Delong, the skipper Li Qingshan whom Yu Fuhua had sent, and a liaison officer surnamed Kong. These five settled every question of movement and action aboard that vessel and issued the orders. As soon as he came aboard, Hu was warned of the many taboos observed when sea junks sailed at sea. The little shrine on the steering deck, containing a spirit tablet of the Queen of Heaven, was especially sacred. Remembering the saying that trouble comes from speaking too much, Hu said little. In his spare time he merely lay on his bunk, absorbed in thought. The others behaved differently. Xiao was accustomed to life afloat, but steamships had none of these taboos, and Kong cared even less about them. Li Qingshan and Li Delong knew which subjects to avoid; they refrained from saying what ought not to be said but otherwise talked as usual. Beside them, Hu, normally so lively and entertaining, seemed weighed down by cares and sunk in the deepest gloom.

于是姓孔的提议打麻将，萧家骥为了替胡雪岩解除寂寞，特地去请他入局。『五个人怎么打。除非一个人做——。』说到『做』字，胡雪岩缩住了口；他记起坐过『水路班子』的船，『梦』是忌讳的，要说『黄粱子』，便接下去∶『除非一个人做黄粱子。』萧家骥一楞，想了一下才明白，『用不着。』他说，『我不想打。胡先生你来，解解厌气。』于是胡雪岩无可无不可地入了局。打到一半，风浪大作，被迫终止；胡雪岩又回到铺上去睡觉，心里不免忐忑不安，加以不惯风涛之险，大呕大吐，心里那份不宁帖，真有求生不得，求死不能之感。『胡先生，不要紧的！』萧家骥一遍一遍地来安慰他。不光是语言安慰，还有起居上的照料，对待胡雪岩真象对待古应春一样，尊敬而亲热。胡雪岩十分感动，心里有许多话，只是精神不佳，懒得去说。

Kong suggested mahjong. Hoping to relieve Hu’s loneliness, Xiao went specially to invite him to join them. “How can five of us play? Unless one man has a—” Hu stopped short. He remembered travelling on a floating pleasure boat, where the word “dream” was taboo and had to be replaced by “golden millet”. “Unless one man has his golden millet,” he finished. Xiao stared, then understood. “There is no need,” he said. “I do not wish to play. Come, Mr Hu. It will relieve the tedium.” Indifferent either way, Hu joined the game. Halfway through, a gale and heavy seas forced them to stop. He returned to his bunk to sleep. He was anxious enough already; now, unused to the violence of the wind and waves, he began vomiting miserably. His whole being was so unsettled that life seemed intolerable, while death offered no escape. “There is nothing to worry about, Mr Hu!” Xiao came again and again to comfort him. Nor did he offer words alone: he attended to all Hu’s needs, with the same affectionate respect he showed Gu Yingchun. Hu was deeply touched. There was much he wanted to say, but he felt too ill to make the effort.

入夜风平浪静，海上涌出一轮明月，胡雪岩晕船的毛病，不药而愈，只是腹饥难忍，记得七姑奶奶曾亲手放了一盒外国饼干在网篮，起床摸索，惊醒了熟睡中的萧家骥。『是我！』他歉然说道∶『想寻点干点心吃。』『胡先生人舒服了！』萧家骥欣然说道∶『尾舱原留了粥在那里，我替你去拿来。』于是萧家骥点上了盏马灯，到尾舱去端了粥米，另外是一碟盐鱼，一个盐蛋；胡雪岩吃得一干二净，抹一抹嘴笑道∶『世乱年荒，做人就讲究不到哪里去了。』

By nightfall the wind and waves had subsided. A bright moon rose out of the sea, and Hu’s seasickness vanished without medicine. Now, however, hunger tormented him. He remembered the box of foreign biscuits that the Seventh Lady had put into his mesh basket with her own hands. He got up to search for it and woke Xiao, who had been sleeping soundly. “It is only me,” he said apologetically. “I was looking for something dry to eat.” “You feel better, Mr Hu!” Xiao said delightedly. “They kept some congee for you in the stern cabin. I shall fetch it.” He lit a storm lantern and went aft, returning with the congee, a plate of salted fish and a salted egg. Hu ate everything, wiped his mouth and smiled. “In times of war and famine, one cannot be too particular about how one lives.”

『做人不在这上面，讲究的是心。』萧家骥说，『王抚台交胡先生这样的朋友，总算是有眼光的。』『没有用！』胡雪岩黯然，『尽人事，听天命。就算到了杭州，也还不知道怎么个情形；说不定就在这一刻，杭州城已经破了。』『不会的。』萧家骥安慰他说∶『我们总要朝好的地方去想。』『对！』胡雪岩很容易受鼓舞，『人，就活在希望里面。家骥，我倒问你，你将来有什么打算？』这话使萧家骥有如逢知音之感。连古应春都没有问过他这句话。所以满腹大志，无从诉说；不想这时候倒有了倾诉的机会。『我将来要跟外国人一较短长。我总是在想，他们能做的，我们为什么不能做？中国人的脑筋，不比外国人差，就是不团结；所以我要找几个志同道合的人，联合起来，跟外国人比一比。』『有志气！』胡雪岩脱口赞道∶『我算一个。你倒说说看，怎么样跟他们比？』『自然是做生意。他到我们这里来做生意，我们也可以到他那里去做生意。在眼前来说，中国人的生意应该中国人做；中国人的钱也要中国人来赚。只要便宜不落外方，不必一定要我发达。』胡雪岩将他的话细想了一会，赞叹着说∶『你的胸襟了不起。我一定要帮你，你看，眼前有啥要从外国人那里抢过来的生意——。』『第一个就是轮船——。』

“What matters in a man’s life is not that sort of thing, but his heart,” Xiao said. “Governor Wang showed sound judgement in choosing a friend like you.” “It is no use!” Hu’s face darkened. “We do what we can and leave the rest to Heaven. Even if we reach Hangzhou, who knows what we shall find? The city may be falling at this very moment.” “No,” Xiao said consolingly. “We must always look towards the brighter possibility.” “True!” Hu was easily encouraged. “Hope is what keeps a man alive. Jiaji, tell me: what do you mean to do in the future?” Xiao felt that at last he had found someone who understood him. Not even Gu had ever asked him that question. All his great ambitions had remained unspoken; now, unexpectedly, he had a chance to pour them out. “One day I mean to compete with the foreigners. I am always asking myself: why should we not do what they can do? Chinese minds are no worse than theirs. We simply fail to work together. I want to find a few men who share my aims, join forces with them and see how we measure up against the foreigners.” “That is ambition!” Hu exclaimed in admiration. “Count me in. Tell me how you propose to compete.” “In business, naturally. They come here to trade; we can go to their countries and do the same. For the present, Chinese business ought to be conducted by Chinese, and Chinese money earned by Chinese. So long as the advantage stays in our hands, I need not be the one to prosper.” Hu considered this for a moment. “What breadth of spirit! I shall certainly help you. What business could we begin taking from foreign hands now?” “Steamships, first of all—”

于是，从这天起，胡雪岩就跟萧家骥谈开办轮船公司的计划；直到沙船将进鳖子门，方台停了下来。依照预定的计划，黑夜偷渡，越过狭处，便算脱险，沿钱塘江往西南方向走；正遇着东北风，很快地到了杭州；停泊在江心。但是，胡雪岩却不知道如何跟城里取得联络；从江心遥望，凤山门外，长毛猬集，仿佛数十里连绵不断，谁也不敢贸然上岸。『原来约定，是王雪公派人来跟我联络；关照我千万不要上岸。』胡雪岩说∶『我只有等、等、等！』王有龄预计胡雪岩的粮船，也快到了，此时全力所谋求的，就是打通一线之路，直通江边，可以运粮入城。无奈十城紧围，战守俱穷，因而忧愤成疾，肝火上升；不时吐血，一吐就是一碗，失血太多，头昏目眩，脸如金纸，然而他不肯下城休息，因为休息亦归于无用，倒不如勉力支撑，反倒可收激励士气的效用。

From that day, Hu and Xiao discussed plans for establishing a steamship company. Only when the junks were about to enter Biezimen did their discussions cease. According to their plan, they slipped through under cover of darkness. Once through the narrows, they were out of danger and sailed southwest along the Qiantang. A favourable northeasterly wind soon brought them to Hangzhou, where they anchored in midstream. But Hu did not know how to communicate with the city. Looking from the river towards Fengshan Gate, they saw the Longhairs massed outside it, apparently stretching unbroken for tens of li. No one dared go ashore rashly. “The arrangement was that Governor Wang would send someone to contact me. He warned me on no account to land,” Hu said. “All I can do is wait, and wait, and wait!” Wang Youling expected the grain boats to arrive shortly. His whole effort now went into opening a passage to the riverbank through which the provisions could enter the city. But all ten gates were tightly besieged, and the defenders had exhausted their resources for both attack and defence. Anxiety and anger made him ill. His liver fire rose, and he repeatedly vomited blood, a bowlful at a time. The loss left him dizzy and faint, his face as yellow as gold paper. Yet he would not leave the wall to rest. Rest would do no good; better to force himself to hold on, and at least encourage the troops by his presence.

哀兵的士气，倒还不坏；但俗语道得好∶『皇帝不差饿兵』；打仗是费气力的事，枵腹操戈，连跑都跑不动，哪谈得到杀敌？所以每天出城攻击，长毛一退，官军亦随即鸣金收兵。这样僵持了好久，一无成就，而城里饿死的人，却是越来越多了；先还有做好事的人，不忍见尸骨骨露，掘地掩埋，到后来埋不胜埋，只好听其自然；大街小巷『路倒尸』不计其数，幸好时值冬天，还不致发生疫疠，但一城的尸臭，也熏得人够受的了。到了十月底，城外官军的营盘，都为长毛攻破；硕果仅存的，只有候潮门外，副将曾得胜一营，屹然不动。这一营的不倒，是个奇迹；但说穿了不希奇，城外比较容易找粮食，真的找不到了，到长毛营盘里去找。反正打仗阵亡也是死，绝粮坐毙也是死；既然如此，不如去夺长毛的粮食，反倒是死中求活的一条生路。因此，曾军打起仗来，真有视死如归之概。说也奇怪，长毛望见『曾』字旗帜，先就心慌，往往不战而遁；但是，这一营也只能自保，要想进击破敌，实力悬殊过甚。到底无能为力。

The defenders’ spirit, born of desperation, was not bad. Yet the saying was sound: even an emperor does not send hungry soldiers on a mission. Fighting requires strength. With empty bellies and weapons in their hands, the men could scarcely run, let alone kill an enemy. Each day they sallied out, but the moment the Longhairs drew back, the imperial troops sounded the gong to recall their own men. This stalemate continued for a long time without achieving anything, while more and more people starved inside the city. At first, charitable souls who could not bear to see the bodies exposed dug graves and buried them. Eventually the dead were too numerous to bury, and they had to leave them where they lay. Bodies beyond counting filled the streets and alleys. Fortunately it was winter, so no epidemic had yet broken out, but the stench throughout the city was torment enough. By the end of the tenth month, the Longhairs had overrun all the imperial camps outside the walls except one: Deputy Commander Zeng Desheng’s battalion stood firm outside Houchao Gate. Its survival seemed a miracle, though the explanation was simple. Food was easier to find outside the city; when none could be found elsewhere, the men sought it in the Longhairs’ camps. Death in battle and death from starvation were equally death. Better, then, to seize the enemy’s provisions and seek a chance of life in mortal danger. Zeng’s men accordingly fought with a courage that made them appear to welcome death as a homecoming. Strangely enough, the Longhairs lost heart at the sight of the flag bearing the character “Zeng” and often fled without fighting. Yet this single battalion could do no more than preserve itself. The disparity in strength was too great for it to launch an offensive and break the enemy. It was powerless to do more.

只是王有龄却对这一营寄以莫大的期望，特别下令仁和知县吴保丰，将安置在城隍山上的一尊三千斤重的大炮，费尽力量，移运到曾得胜营里，对准长毛的壁垒，大轰特轰。这一带长毛倒是绝迹了，但仍无法直通江边，因为大炮射程以外，长毛仍如牛毛，重重隔阻，处处填塞，始终杀不开重围。就在这时候，抓住一名奸细——奸细极易分别，因为城里的人，不是面目浮肿，就是骨瘦如柴，走路挪不了三寸，说话有气无力；如果遇到一个气色正常，行动舒徐，说话不必侧耳就可以听得清楚的，必是从城外混进来的；这样一座人间地狱，还有人跳了进来，其意何居？不问可知。果然，抓住了一顿打，立刻打出了实话，此人自道是长毛所派，送一封信来给饶廷选部下的一外营官，约定里应外合的日期。同时也从他口中得到一个消息，说钱塘江中，停泊了十几号大船，满装粮食。这不问可知，是胡雪岩的粮船到了；王有龄陡觉精神一振，当即去看杭州将军瑞昌，商量如何杀开一条血路，能让江中的粮食运入城内？不须多作商量，便有了结果，决定请副都统杰纯，当此重任。事实上怕也只有此人堪当重任——杰纯是蒙古人，他祖先驻防杭州，早有好几代；杰纯本人是正六品骁骑校出身，武艺娴熟，深得军心，积功升到正四品的协领，颇为瑞昌所倚重。

Wang nonetheless placed enormous hopes in it. He specially ordered Wu Baofeng, magistrate of Renhe, to move a cannon weighing three thousand jin from City God Hill to Zeng’s camp, at immense effort. Trained on the Longhairs’ fortifications, it thundered away until no enemy remained in that immediate area. Still they could not reach the riverbank. Beyond the cannon’s range the Longhairs remained as numerous as hairs on an ox, blocking every approach in layer upon layer. The encirclement could not be broken. Just then a spy was caught. Spies were easy to recognise: the people inside the city were either bloated in the face or wasted to skin and bone; they could scarcely shuffle a few inches and spoke in voices too weak to hear. Anyone of normal complexion who walked easily and could be heard without straining one’s ears must have slipped in from outside. What purpose could draw a man into such a hell on earth? There was no need to ask. Sure enough, a beating soon brought out the truth. The man admitted that the Longhairs had sent him with a letter for a battalion commander under Rao Tingxuan, fixing a date for a coordinated attack from within and without. He also revealed that more than a dozen large vessels, laden with provisions, lay in the Qiantang. Plainly Hu’s grain boats had arrived. Wang’s spirits revived at once. He went immediately to Ruichang, the garrison general of Hangzhou, to discuss how to force a bloody passage through which the grain might be brought into the city. Little discussion was needed. They decided to entrust the task to Deputy Lieutenant-General Jiechun. Indeed, there was probably no one else equal to it. Jiechun was a Mongol whose ancestors had served in the Hangzhou garrison for generations. He had begun as a cavalry officer of the senior sixth rank. Highly skilled in arms and deeply trusted by his men, he had risen through service to the senior fourth rank as a banner commander, and Ruichang relied heavily upon him.

咸丰十年春天，杭州城第一次为长毛轰破，瑞昌预备自刎殉国；杰纯劝他不必轻生，认为安徽广德来的敌军，轻骑疾进，未有后继，不足为忧，不妨固守待援。瑞昌听了他的话，退守满营；营盘在西湖边上，实际是一座子城，俗称满城。因为防御得法，长毛连攻六天，劳而无功；杰纯的长子守城阵亡，杰纯殓而不哭，认为长子死得其所，死得其时。到了第七天，张玉良的援兵到了；杰纯怒马突出，当者披靡，配合援军，大举反攻，将长毛逐出城外十几里。以此功劳，赏戴花翎，升任为宁夏副都统，但仍旧留在杭州，成了瑞昌的左右手。这次杭州再度吃紧，杰纯战功卓著，赐号巴图鲁，调任乍浦副都统，这是海防上的一个要缺；但乍浦已落入长毛手中，所以仍旧留防省城。杭州十城，最关紧要的就是北面的武林门和南门的凤山门；凤山门原由王有龄亲自坐镇，这一阵因为呕血过多，气衰力竭，才改由杰纯防守——胡雪岩的粮船，就泊在凤山门外的江面；让杰纯去杀开一条血路，亦正是人和地理，两皆相合的顺理成章 之事。

In the spring of the tenth year of Xianfeng, when the Longhairs first breached Hangzhou, Ruichang had prepared to cut his throat and die for his country. Jiechun urged him not to throw away his life. The enemy had advanced rapidly with light cavalry from Guangde in Anhui and had no reinforcements behind them; there was no cause for despair. They should hold their ground and await relief. Ruichang accepted his advice and withdrew into the Manchu garrison beside West Lake. In effect it was a city within the city, commonly called the Manchu City. Its defences were so skilfully managed that six days of Longhair attacks achieved nothing. Jiechun’s eldest son died defending the walls. His father laid out the body without tears, believing that his son had died as a man should, at the proper moment. On the seventh day Zhang Yuliang’s reinforcements arrived. Jiechun burst forth on a charging horse, sweeping aside all opposition. In concert with the relieving army, he launched a great counterattack and drove the Longhairs more than ten li beyond the city. For this service he received the right to wear a peacock feather and was promoted to deputy lieutenant-general of Ningxia, though he remained in Hangzhou as Ruichang’s right-hand man. During the present crisis his distinguished services earned him the title Baturu, “the Brave”, and a transfer to the deputy lieutenant-generalship of Zhapu, an important coastal-defence post. But Zhapu had fallen to the Longhairs, so he stayed to defend the provincial capital. Of Hangzhou’s ten gates, Wulin in the north and Fengshan in the south were the most important. Wang had originally commanded at Fengshan in person. Only recently, exhausted by vomiting blood, had he handed its defence to Jiechun. Hu’s grain ships lay in the river outside that very gate. To send Jiechun to force a passage was therefore the obvious choice, both for the man and for the position he held.

围凤山门的长毛主将叫做陈炳文，照太平天国的爵位，封号称为『朗天义』。他本来要走了——长毛的军粮，亦渐感不敷；李秀成已经拟定行定计划，回苏州度岁，预备明年春天，卷土重来。但陈炳文已从城里逃出来的难民口中，得知城内绝粮，已到了人吃人的地步；所以翻然变计，坚持不走；同时也知道城内防守，以凤山门为重点，因而又厚集兵力，一层夹一层，直到江边，弹丸之地，集结了四万人之多。等到粮船一到，遥遥望见，陈炳文越发眼红，一方面防备城内会冲出来接粮；一方面千方百计想攻夺粮船，无奈江面辽阔，而华尔的部下防守严密，小划子只要稍稍接近，便是一排抢过来，就算船打不沉，人却非打死打伤不可。一连三日，无以为计；最后有人献策，依照赤壁鏖兵，大破曹军的办法，用小船满载茅柴，浇上油脂，从上游顺流而下，火攻粮船。陈炳文认为此计可行。但上游不是自己的战区，需要派人联络；又要禀报忠王裁夺，不是一两天所能安排停当的。同时天气回暖，风向不定，江面上有自己的许多小划子；万一弄巧成拙，惹火烧身，岂不糟糕？因而迟疑未发。就在这时候，粮船上却等不得了。

The Longhair commander besieging Fengshan Gate was Chen Bingwen, whose title in the Taiping hierarchy was Langtianyi. He had been about to leave: the Longhairs too were beginning to run short of provisions. Li Xiucheng had already drawn up plans to return to Suzhou for the New Year and resume the campaign the following spring. But Chen learnt from refugees escaping the city that its food was gone and its inhabitants had begun eating one another. He abruptly changed his mind and insisted on staying. Knowing also that Fengshan Gate was the key to the city’s defence, he massed his forces there in layer after layer all the way to the river, concentrating as many as forty thousand men in that tiny area. When he saw the grain boats in the distance, his eagerness to seize them grew fiercer. He prepared against a sortie from the city to collect the provisions while trying every means of capturing the vessels. But the river was broad and Ward’s men guarded them closely. Whenever a small boat drew near, a volley met it. Even if the boat did not sink, its occupants could scarcely escape death or injury. Three days passed without success. Finally someone proposed copying the method by which Cao Cao’s army had been destroyed at Red Cliffs: load small boats with brushwood, soak it in oil and send them downstream from above to burn the grain ships. Chen thought the scheme feasible. But the upper reaches were outside his sector, so he had to make contact with the commanders there and submit the proposal to the Loyal King for a decision. Such arrangements could not be completed in a day or two. Moreover, the weather had grown warmer, the wind was changeable, and many of their own small boats were on the river. What if the clever plan backfired and they burned themselves instead? He hesitated to act. Meanwhile, the men aboard the grain ships could wait no longer.

因为一连三天的等待，胡雪岩度日如年，眠食俱废。而护航洋兵的孔联络官，认为身处危地，如果不速作鼾，后果不堪设想，不断催促胡雪岩，倘或粮食无法运上陆地，就应依照原说，改航宁波。沙船帮的李庆山口中不言，神色之间亦颇为焦急，这使得胡雪岩越发集躁，双眼发红，终日喃喃自语，不知说些什么，看样子快要发疯了。『得隆哥，』萧家骥对胡雪岩劝慰无效，只好跟李得隆商议，『我看，事情不能不想办法了。这样「屏」下去要出事。』『是啊！我也是这样在想。不过有啥办法呢？困在江心动弹不得。』李得隆指着岸上说∶『长毛象蚂蚁一样；将一座杭州城，围得铁桶似的，城里的人，怎么出得来？』『就是为了这一点。我想，城里的人出不来，只有我们想法子进城去，讨个确实口信；行就行，不行的话，胡先生也好早作打算。这样痴汉等老婆一船，等到哪一天为止？』李得隆也是年轻性急，而且敢冒险的人，当然赞成萧家骥的办法；而且自告奋勇，愿意泅水上岸，进城去通消息。『得隆哥，』萧家骥很平静地说∶『这件事倒不是讲义气，更不是讲客气的。事情要办得通；你去我去都一样，只看哪个去合适？你水性比我好，人比我灵活，手上的功夫，更不是我比得了的——。』『好了，好了！』李得隆笑道，『你少捧我！前面捧得越高，后面的话越加难所；你老实说，我能不能去？』『不是我有意绕弯子说话，这种时候，杂不得一点感情意气，自己好弟兄，为啥不平心静气把话说清楚。我现在先请问你，得隆哥，你杭州去过没有？你晓得我们前面的那个城门叫啥？』

For Hu, the three days had felt like years. He could neither eat nor sleep. Kong, liaison officer with the foreign escort, believed they were in a dangerous position and that failure to decide quickly might have disastrous consequences. He kept urging Hu to sail for Ningbo, as originally agreed, if the provisions could not be landed. Li Qingshan of the Sea-Junk Guild said nothing, but his face betrayed considerable anxiety. This made Hu still more frantic. His eyes reddened, and he muttered unintelligibly to himself all day. He looked close to madness. Unable to comfort him, Xiao turned to Li Delong. “Brother Delong, we must find some way out of this. Something will go wrong if we remain bottled up like this.” “Yes, I have been thinking the same thing. But what can we do? We are trapped in the middle of the river.” Li pointed ashore. “The Longhairs are like ants. They have sealed Hangzhou as tight as an iron barrel. How can anyone get out?” “That is precisely the point. If the people inside cannot get out, we must find a way in and obtain a definite answer. If it can be done, good; if it cannot, Mr Hu can make other plans at once. We are waiting like a fool for a wife. How long can this go on?” Li too was young, impatient and willing to take risks. He naturally approved of Xiao’s proposal and volunteered to swim ashore and carry a message into the city. “Brother Delong,” Xiao said calmly, “this is not a question of proving our loyalty, still less of making polite offers to one another. We have to get the task done. Whether you go or I go makes no difference; what matters is which of us is better suited. You swim better than I do, you are more agile, and your skill in a fight is far beyond mine—” “Enough, enough!” Li laughed. “Stop flattering me. The higher you praise me now, the harsher the words that follow. Tell me plainly: can I go or not?” “I am not trying to be roundabout. At a time like this we must not allow feeling or pride to interfere. We are good brothers; why should we not discuss it calmly and frankly? Let me first ask you this, Brother Delong: have you ever been to Hangzhou? Do you know the name of the gate in front of us?”

『不晓得。我杭州没有去过。』『这就不大相宜了。杭州做过宋朝的京城，城里地方也蛮大的。不熟，寻不着；这还在其次，最要紧的一点是，你不是听胡先生说过，杭州城里盘查奸细严得很；而且因为饿火中烧，不讲道理。得隆哥，』萧家骥停了一下说∶『我说实话，你不动气。你的脾气暴躁；口才不如我。你去不大相宜！』李得隆性子直爽，服善而肯讲道理，听萧家骥说得不错，例即答道∶『好！你去。』于是两个人又商量了如何上岸；如何混过长毛的阵地；到了城下，如何联络进城，种种细了，大致妥当，才跟胡雪岩去说明其事。『胡先生！』是由李得隆开口，『有件事禀告你老人家，事情我们都商量好了，辰光也不容我们再拖下去了，我说了，请你老人家照办，不要驳回。请你写封信给王抚台，由家骥进城去送。』李得隆其实是将胡雪岩看错了。他早就想过，自己必须坐守，免得城里千辛万苦派出人来，接不上头，造成无可挽救的错失；此外，只要可能，任何人都不妨进城通消息。所以一听这话，神态马上变过了。

“No. I have never been to Hangzhou.” “Then you are not quite the right man. Hangzhou was the capital of the Song dynasty. It is a large city, and without knowing it you will have trouble finding your way. But that is the lesser difficulty. The chief point is this: you heard Mr Hu say how strictly they are hunting spies inside the walls. Hunger has inflamed people’s tempers, and they will not listen to reason.” Xiao paused. “Brother Delong, do not be angry if I speak frankly. You have a quick temper, and you are not as ready with words as I am. It would not be wise to send you.” Li was straightforward, willing to listen to reason and accept sound advice. Recognising the truth of Xiao’s words, he answered at once, “Very well. You go.” The two then discussed how Xiao should land, pass through the Longhairs’ positions and make contact beneath the walls to enter the city. Only when these details were largely settled did they take their proposal to Hu. “Mr Hu,” Li began, “there is something we must report. We have worked it all out, and we cannot afford any more delay. Once I have told you, I beg you to agree and not refuse. Please write a letter to Governor Wang. Jiaji will take it into the city.” In fact, Li had misjudged Hu. Hu had long understood that he himself must stay where he was, lest someone from the city should reach the boats after terrible hardships only to miss him, with irreparable consequences. Apart from that, anyone who could possibly carry a message into the city ought to go. At Li’s words, his whole manner changed.

『慢慢来！』他又恢复了临大事从容不乱的态度；比起他这两天的坐卧不宁来，判若两人，『你先说给我听听，怎么去法？』『泅水上去——。』『不是，不是！』第一句话就让他大摇其头，『湿淋淋一身，就不冻出病来，上了岸怎么办？难道还有客栈好投，让你烤干衣服？』『原是要见机行事。』『这时候做事，不能说碰运气了。要想停当再动手。』胡雪岩说，『你听我告诉你。』他也实在没有什么腹案，不过一向机变快，一路想，一路说，居然就有了一套办法——整套办法中，最主要的一点是，遇到长毛，如何应付？胡雪岩教了他一条计策∶冒充上海英商的代表，向长毛兜售军火。

“Steady!” He had recovered his customary calm in the face of great affairs, utterly different from the restless man of the past two days. “First tell me how you intend to get there.” “Swim ashore—” “No, no!” Hu shook his head at the very first sentence. “What will you do once you land, soaked to the skin, even if you do not fall ill from the cold? Do you imagine there is an inn where you can dry your clothes by a fire?” “We meant to act as circumstances required.” “At a time like this we cannot simply trust to luck. We must think everything through before acting,” Hu said. “Listen to me.” He had no prepared plan himself. But he had always been quick-witted; thinking as he spoke, he soon worked out a complete scheme. Its most important point was how to deal with any Longhairs Xiao encountered. Hu suggested a stratagem: pose as the representative of a British merchant in Shanghai and offer to sell them arms.

译者说明与学习提示（不属于小说正文）

本批为《红顶商人》第二章开头，覆盖 source_red.txt 第661—751行全部非空行。

译文判断与原文疑点：
1. 本批船队18艘；护兵最终人数112=100士兵+12官长；12官长=7吕宋人+3美国人+2中国联络官。与此前讨论中的人数有区别，现译以“最终议定”保留阶段变化，未把全部官长称外国人。
2. “黄粱子”是船上忌说“梦”的替代语。对话保留欲说又止以及 golden millet 的替换；这里与五人不能同时打四人麻将相连。黄粱梦不是客观真有一人睡着。
3. “吕宋人”按地名译 men from Luzon，不额外推断各人的国籍或民族。鳖子门沿最近068采用 Biezimen；066的 Biezimen 指同地，可在总审时统一。
4. 王有龄吐血的“肝火上升”保留为人物叙事中的 liver fire，不把小说中的传统病因描述擅改为现代诊断。曾得胜一营能自保不等于已打通粮道；杰纯早年战绩、当前拟派任务与胡粮船到达分清。
5. 骁骑校以 cavalry officer（并保留正六品）表义；协领为 banner commander（并保留正四品）；副都统采用 deputy lieutenant-general。巴图鲁作为封号 Baturu, “the Brave”，不是新姓名。
6. 旱路重围和江中射击、拟用火攻保持为文学历史叙事。陈炳文仅拟火攻并迟疑，译文未写成已经放火；粮船已等三天，胡尚未有确定运输方案。
7. 明显讹字按语境理解：697“方台”作方才；703“尸骨骨露”作尸骨暴露；721“行定计划”作行动/行程计划；723“排抢”作排枪；727“速作鼾”作速作决断、“集躁”作焦躁；731“难所”作难听、“杂不得”作掺不得；735“例即”作立即；737“细了”作细节。未改源文件。
8. 本批没有需安全改写的范围。

综合学习提示：
— whether you go or I go... what matters...：先消解人物之争，再引出适任性标准，适合识别论证主轴。
— even if / lest / once：分别标示让步、预防性目的和时间条件，注意不能互换。
— thinking as he spoke / act as circumstances required：表现即时应变；结尾与 think everything through before acting 构成情节中的具体取舍。
