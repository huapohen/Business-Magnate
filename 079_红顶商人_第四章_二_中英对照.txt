胡雪岩｜中英对照版
原著：高阳

红顶商人｜The Red-Topped Merchant

第四章（本章第2部分，共2部分）

阅读说明：每段中文后接对应英文；两版英文正文一致。下载站水印已清除，原始疑字在文末说明。

这时还有个怪现象，就是『卖钱』；钱重不便携带，要换银子或者银洋，一串一串的铜钱，公然插上草标出卖，当然银贵钱贱。这是预作逃亡之计，军心如此，民心更加恐慌，这时相顾谈论的，只有一个话题∶长毛会在哪天破城？到了十一月廿七，守城的官军，决定死中求活，第二天黎明冲出艮山门，杀开一条血路，接引可能会有的外援。这虽是妄想，但无论如何是奋发自救的作为，可以激励民心士气，有益无害。不想到了夜里，情况起了变化，士兵三三两两，缒城而下；这就变做军心涣散，各奔前程的『开小差』了。据说，这个变化是有人从中煽动的结果。煽动的人还是浙江的大员∶藩司林福祥。林福祥带领的一支军队，名为『定武军』，军纪最坏，而作战最不力。而林福祥则颇善于做作，专干些毫无用处的花样；又喜欢出奇计，但到头来往往『赔了夫人又折兵』，因此颇有人怀疑他已与长毛暗通了款曲。说他曾与一个姓甘的候补知府，到长毛营盘里议过事。

Another strange phenomenon had appeared: people were “selling money.” Copper cash was heavy and awkward to carry, so they exchanged it for silver bullion or silver dollars. Strings of coins were openly offered for sale with wisps of straw stuck into them as signs. Naturally, silver commanded a high price and copper fell cheap. These were preparations for flight. With the soldiers in such a state of mind, the civilians were more frightened still. Whenever they met, there was only one subject of conversation: on what day would the Longhairs take the city? On the twenty-seventh of the eleventh month, the defenders decided to seek life in the midst of death. At dawn the next day they would charge out through Genshan Gate, force a passage, and meet whatever relief forces might conceivably be outside. It was a desperate fantasy, yet at least it represented a determined attempt to save themselves, capable of heartening troops and people alike. It could do good and no harm. During the night, however, matters changed. Soldiers began lowering themselves from the wall in twos and threes. The planned assault had become desertion, each man seeking his own escape. It was said that someone had incited this change, and that the instigator was no less a person than Zhejiang’s provincial treasurer, Lin Fuxiang. Lin commanded a force called the Dingwu Army, whose discipline was the worst and whose fighting was the least effective. He was skilled at empty display, forever devising useless flourishes. He also liked ingenious stratagems, which usually cost him both the prize he sought and the men he risked. Many consequently suspected that he was secretly in league with the Longhairs. It was said that he and an expectant prefect surnamed Gan had visited the enemy camp to discuss matters.

这些传闻虽莫可究诘，但有件事却实在可疑；王有龄抓到过一个奸细名为徐宗鳌，就是林福洋保举在定武军当差的营官。王有龄与张玉良在城内城外互通消息，约期会合的『战书』，都由定武军转送，先后不下十余通之多，都为徐宗鳌转送到了长毛那里；后来经人密告，逮捕审问属实，徐宗鳌全家，除了留下三岁的一个小儿子以外，尽数斩决。可是只办了这样一个罪魁祸首；王有龄虽然对幕后的林福祥已大具戒心，却因投鼠忌器，不愿在强敌包围之下，还有自乱阵脚的内讧出现，只好隐忍不言。

Those rumors could not be verified, but one incident did give substantial grounds for suspicion. Wang Youling had captured a spy named Xu Zong’ao, a battalion commander whose appointment to the Dingwu Army Lin Fuxiang had recommended. The messages exchanged between Wang inside the city and Zhang Yuliang outside, fixing dates for coordinated operations, had all passed through the Dingwu Army. Xu had forwarded more than ten of these military dispatches to the Longhairs. After an informer exposed him, he was arrested, interrogated, and proved guilty. His entire family was beheaded, apart from a three-year-old son who was spared. Yet only this principal offender was dealt with. Though Wang had become deeply suspicious of Lin behind the scenes, he feared smashing the vessel while striking at the rat. With a powerful enemy surrounding them, he would not risk an internal conflict that might throw their own ranks into disorder. He could only suppress his anger and remain silent.

而林福祥却确确实实跟长毛已取得了默契，虽不肯公然投降，却答应在暗底下帮着『拆墙脚』，这天晚上煽动艮山门守军潜逃，就是要拆杭州这座将倒的危墙。夜里的逃兵，长毛不曾发觉；到了天明，发现踪迹，长毛认为这是杭州城内守军溃散的迹象，于是发功攻势，凤山、候潮、清波三门，首先被破。报到王有龄那里，知道大势去矣！自道∶『不负朝廷，只负了杭州城内数十万忠义士民。』殉节之志早决，这是时候了！回到巡抚衙门，穿戴衣冠，望阙谢恩，留下遗书，然后吞金，唯恐不死，又服鸦片烟；而这时衙门内的哭声和衙门外人声相应和，长毛已经迫近，为怕受辱，王有龄上吊而死。同时殉难的有学政员锡庚、处州镇总兵文瑞、仁和知县吴保丰。盐运使庄焕文所带的是晓勇善战的福建泉州籍的『泉勇』，奋战突围，不幸兵败，庄焕文投水自尽。

Lin, however, had indeed reached a secret understanding with the Longhairs. Unwilling to surrender openly, he had promised to help undermine the defenses from within. His incitement of the Genshan Gate troops to desert that night was meant to bring down Hangzhou’s tottering wall. The Longhairs did not notice the deserters during the darkness. At dawn they discovered the traces and took them as evidence that the garrison was disintegrating. They launched an attack, and Fengshan, Houchao, and Qingbo gates were the first to fall. When the news reached Wang, he knew that all was lost. “I have not failed the court,” he said, “only the hundreds of thousands of loyal and righteous people of Hangzhou.” He had long resolved to die for his duty. The moment had come. Returning to the governor’s yamen, he put on his formal robes and cap, bowed toward the imperial palace in gratitude for the emperor’s favors, and left a final letter. He swallowed gold; fearing that it would not kill him, he also took opium. By then the cries of the household mingled with the uproar outside. The Longhairs were approaching, and, rather than suffer humiliation, Wang hanged himself. Among those who died for their duty at the same time were Education Commissioner Yun Xigeng, Wenrui, brigade commander of Chuzhou, and Wu Baofeng, magistrate of Renhe. Salt Commissioner Zhuang Huanwen led the Quanzhou troops, fierce and experienced fighters recruited in that district of Fujian. They fought desperately to break out but were defeated, and Zhuang drowned himself.

林福祥却果然得到长毛的破格优遇，被安置在藩司衙门的西花厅；好酒好肉款待，而且答应听凭林福祥自己决定，要到哪里便护送到哪里。林福祥选择的是上海，据说此来还有一项任务，是护送王有龄的灵柩及家眷，由上海转回福建原籍。听到这里，古应不能不打断话问了。因为王有龄的灵柩到上海，且不说胡雪岩凭棺一恸，决不可免；就是他在情分上亦不能不吊祭一番。尤其是想到刚听妻子听说，颇以对这位『干亲』生前，未能稍尽心意而引为莫大憾事；那就不但灵前叩拜，还须对遗属有所慰恤，才能稍舟弥补歉疚的心情。问到王有龄灵柩到上海的日期，谁也不知道。然而也不碍；到时候必有迎灵、路祭等等仪式，不管哪个衙门都会知道，不难打听。

Lin Fuxiang, by contrast, received exceptional favor from the Longhairs. He was lodged in the western reception hall of the provincial treasurer’s yamen, entertained with fine food and wine, and promised an escort to any destination he chose. He chose Shanghai. It was said that he would also escort Wang Youling’s coffin and surviving family there, for onward travel to their native Fujian. At this, Gu had to interrupt with questions. If Wang’s coffin came to Shanghai, Hu would inevitably insist on mourning before it. Quite apart from that, Gu’s own relationship with the dead man obliged him to pay his respects. He remembered his wife’s bitter regret that they had done nothing for their adopted kinsman while he lived. Bowing before the coffin would not be enough; they must also offer comfort and assistance to the surviving family if they were to ease their sense of guilt. No one could tell him when the coffin would reach Shanghai. That did not matter greatly. There would be a formal reception and roadside offerings when it arrived; any yamen would know, and the date would be easy to discover.

一顿花酒吃到半夜。古应春看张医生对艳春老四有些着迷的模样，有心作个『红娘』；将外号『金大块头』的『本家』唤到一边，探问是否可以让张医生『借干铺』？『古大少！』金大块头笑道，『你是「老白相」，想想看可有这规矩？』『规矩是人兴出来的。』古应春说，『我跟你说老实话，这位医生朋友我欠他的情，你自帮我的忙，不要讲规矩好不好？再说，他是外路来的，又住不到多少日子，也不能跟你慢慢讲规矩。』古应春是花丛阔客，金大块头要拉拢他，听他一开口，心里便已允许，但答应得太爽快，未免自贬身价，也不是让古应春见情，所以说了些什么『小姐名声要紧』∶『头一天叫的局，什么「花头」都没有做过，就借干铺，会教人笑话』之类的言语；而到头来是『古大少的面子，不肯也要肯。』这面肯了，那面反倒不肯；张医生到了洋场，算『乡下人』，在宁波也是场面上的人物，不肯留个『头一天到上海就住在堂子里』的话柄，所以坚持要回家。

The courtesan banquet continued until midnight. Seeing how fascinated the doctor was with the Fourth Miss of Yanchun House, Gu thought of playing matchmaker. He drew aside the proprietor, a man nicknamed Big Jin, and asked whether the doctor might “borrow a bed for the night.” “Young Master Gu!” Big Jin laughed. “You are an old hand at pleasure. Think for yourself: is that the way things are done?” “Rules are made by people. Let me be frank. I owe this doctor a favor. Help me out, and leave the rules aside, will you? Besides, he is from out of town and will not be staying long. He cannot spend weeks observing all your formalities.” Gu was a lavish patron, and Big Jin wanted to cultivate him. He had inwardly consented as soon as the request was made. But agreeing too readily would cheapen his concession and prevent Gu from appreciating the favor. He therefore spoke of the young lady’s reputation, of how people would laugh if a man who had summoned her for the first time that day and observed none of the customary preliminaries were allowed to stay overnight. In the end, naturally, “For Young Master Gu’s sake, even if I were unwilling, I must agree.” Once that side had consented, however, the other refused. In Shanghai’s foreign settlement, Dr Zhang might be a countryman, but in Ningbo he was a person of standing. He would not give people grounds to say that he had spent his very first night in Shanghai in a courtesan house. He insisted on going home.

一到家，又替胡雪岩看了一回病，『望闻问切』四个字都做到，很高兴地告诉古应春夫妇，说病人十天一定可以起床。『那末，张先生，』七姑奶奶说，『我留张先生住十天，肯不肯赏我一个面子？』『言重，言重！』张医生面有难色∶『再住十天，就到了送灶的日子了。』古应春也觉得急景凋年，硬留人羁栖异乡，不但强人所难，也不近人情，所以折衷提议∶『再住五天吧』『好，就住五天。』张医生略有些忸怩地说，『我还有件事，恐怕要重托贤伉俪。』这话正好为要掀门帘进屋的阿巧姐听见，扭头就走；古应春不明白是怎么回事，想开口相问；七姑奶奶机警，抢着悄悄拉了他一把衣服，才将他的话挡了回去。『张先生，不要这么说。』七姑奶奶答道∶『只要我们办得到的事，你尽管吩咐。今天怕累了，吃了粥，请安置吧！』『粥是不吃了；累倒真有些累了。』张医生略有些怏怏然。

On returning, he examined Hu again, carrying out all four stages of a physician’s inquiry: inspection, listening and smelling, questioning, and taking the pulse. He then told Gu and his wife, with great satisfaction, that the patient would certainly be able to get out of bed in ten days. “Then, Dr Zhang,” the Seventh Lady said, “will you do me the honor of staying with us for those ten days?” “You are too kind, too kind!” He looked troubled. “Another ten days would take us to the festival for sending off the Kitchen God.” Gu too felt that, with the year drawing so rapidly to its close, forcing a man to remain far from home would be unreasonable and unkind. He proposed a compromise. “Stay five more days, then.” “Very well, five days.” The doctor looked a little bashful. “There is another matter in which I may have to ask a considerable favor of you both.” A’qiao, just about to lift the curtain and enter, heard this and turned away at once. Gu did not understand and was about to ask, but his wife quickly tugged at his clothes to silence him. “Please do not put it that way, Dr Zhang. If there is anything within our power, you have only to tell us. You must be tired today. Have some congee, then go to bed.” “I shall not have any congee. But yes, I am rather tired.” He sounded somewhat disappointed.

七姑奶奶向来待客殷勤诚恳，煮了一锅极道地的鱼生粥，定要请客人试试她的手段；又说还有话要谈。张医生自然没有坚拒之理；于是一面吃宵夜，一面谈正事。第一件大事，就是古应春谈杭州的情形。这些话张医生已经在艳春院听过一遍，所以古应春不便再详细复述，顶要紧的是证实王有龄殉节，以及由林福祥护送灵柩到上海的话，要告诉七姑奶奶。『那就对了！我的想法不错。』她转脸对张医生说∶『张先生大概还不十分清楚。我们这位小爷，跟王抚台是生死之交；现在听说王抚台死得这么惨，病中当然更受刺激。不过我在想，我这位小爷叔，为人最明道理，最看得开；而且王抚台非死不可，他也早已看到了的，所以这个消息也不算意外。现在王抚台的灵柩到上海，马上要回福建，如果他不能到灵前去哭一场，将来反倒会怪我们。所以我想，不如就在这一两天告诉他。张先生，你看可以不可以？』

The Seventh Lady was always sincerely attentive to guests. She had cooked an excellent pot of congee with fresh sliced fish and insisted that he taste her handiwork. Besides, she said, there were still things to discuss. He could hardly persist in refusing, so they talked over the late supper. The first important subject was Hangzhou. The doctor had already heard the details at Yanchun House, so Gu could not properly repeat them at length. The essential points to tell his wife were that Wang’s death had been confirmed and that Lin Fuxiang was escorting the coffin to Shanghai. “Then I was right. My idea was sound.” She turned to the doctor. “Perhaps you do not quite understand the situation yet. Little Uncle and Governor Wang were friends bound to one another in life and death. To hear that the governor died so tragically must naturally upset him all the more while he is ill. But Little Uncle is exceptionally reasonable and knows how to accept things. He had already foreseen that the governor could hardly escape death, so the news will not be entirely unexpected. Now the coffin is coming to Shanghai and will soon leave for Fujian. If he cannot go and weep before it, he will blame us afterward. I therefore think we should tell him within the next day or two. What do you say, Dr Zhang?”

『这就很难说了。』张医生答道∶『病人最怕遇到伤心的事；不过照你所说，似乎又不要紧。』『应春，』七姑奶奶转脸问道∶『你看呢？』古应春最了解妻子，知道她已经拿定了主意，问这一句，是当着客人的面，表示尊重他做丈夫的身份。自己应该知趣。知趣就要凑趣∶『张先生自然要慎重。以小爷叔的性情来说，索性告诉了他，让他死了心，也是一个办法。』『对！』张医生觉得这话有见地，『胡道台心心念念记挂杭州，于他养病也是不宜的。不过告诉他这话，要一步一步来，不要说得太急。』『是的。』七姑奶奶这时便要提出请求了，『我在想，告诉了他，难免有一场伤心；只怕他一时会受震动，要请张先生格外费心。张先生，我虽是女流之辈，做事不喜欢扭扭捏捏，话先说在前面，万一病势反复，我可要硬留张先生在上海过年了。』此时此地，张医生还能说什么？只好报以苦笑，含含糊糊地先答应下来。

“It is very difficult to judge,” the doctor replied. “Nothing is worse for a sick man than grief. Yet from what you say, perhaps it would do no harm.” “Yingchun, what do you think?” She turned to her husband. Gu knew that she had already made up her mind. She was asking only to show respect for his position as her husband in front of their guest. He ought to take the hint—and taking it meant playing his part. “Dr Zhang is naturally cautious. But knowing Little Uncle’s temperament, one way might be to tell him plainly and put an end to his hopes.” “Exactly!” The doctor thought this perceptive. “His constant preoccupation with Hangzhou is not good for his recovery either. But you must prepare him gradually. Do not give him the news too abruptly.” “Certainly.” The Seventh Lady now came to her request. “I imagine that once he knows, there must be an outpouring of grief. He may suffer a shock, and we shall need you to take particular care of him. I may be a woman, Dr Zhang, but I do not like to beat about the bush. Let me say it beforehand: if his illness returns, I shall insist on keeping you in Shanghai for the New Year.” What could the doctor say in such circumstances? He could only offer a wry smile and give a vague assent for the moment.

等吃完粥，古应春亲送张医生到客房；是七姑奶奶亲自料理的，大铜床，全新被褥，还特为张了一顶灰鼠皮帐子，以示待客的隆重，害得张医生倒大为不安。又说了些闲话，谈谈第二天逛些什么地方？然后道声『明天见』，古应春回到卧室，七姑奶奶已经卸了妆在等他了。『今天张医生高兴不高兴？』『有个艳春老四，他看了很中意，我本来想替他拉拢，就住在那里。都已经说好了，张医生一定不肯，只好由他。』古应春又问，『你这样子热心，总有道理在内吧？我一直在想，想不通。』『说起来有趣。你晓得张医生这趟，怎么来的？』

When they had finished the congee, Gu personally escorted the doctor to his room. The Seventh Lady had arranged it herself: a large brass bed, entirely new bedding, and, as a special mark of honor, a canopy of grey squirrel fur. The lavish attention made the doctor quite uneasy. They chatted a little longer about where to go sightseeing the next day. Then, with “Until tomorrow,” Gu returned to the bedroom, where his wife had removed her makeup and was waiting for him. “Did Dr Zhang enjoy himself tonight?” “There was a Fourth Miss at Yanchun House whom he liked very much. I meant to arrange things so that he could stay there. Everything was settled, but he absolutely refused, so I had to let him have his way. What is behind all this eagerness of yours? I have been turning it over in my mind and cannot work it out.” “It is an amusing story. Do you know what brought him on this journey?”

这一问自然有文章 ，古应春用右手掩着他妻子的嘴说∶『你不要开口，让我想一想。』聪明人一点就透。古应春只要从女人身上去思索，立刻就想到方才阿巧姐帘前惊鸿一瞥的情；于是张医生刚到时对阿巧姐处处殷勤的景象，亦都浮现脑际，恍然大悟，原来如此！『是为了这个？』他缩回右手，屈起两指。做了个『七』的手势；暗扣着一个『巧』字。七姑奶奶似乎有些扫兴，『真无趣！』她说，『怎么会让你猜到？』

Clearly the question had a meaning behind it. Gu covered his wife’s mouth with his right hand. “Do not tell me. Let me think.” An intelligent man needs only a hint. Once he began to think in terms of a woman, he immediately remembered A’qiao’s fleeting appearance at the curtain a moment earlier. The doctor’s attentive behavior toward her on his arrival also came back to him. Now he understood. “Was that the reason?” He withdrew his hand and bent two fingers to make the sign for seven, hinting at the word qiao, “cleverness.” The Seventh Lady looked rather disappointed. “How tiresome! How did you manage to guess?”

『猜到这一点没有用处。来，来，』他拉着妻子并肩坐下，『你讲这段新闻来听听。』这段新闻讲得有头有尾，纤细无遗，比身历其境的人还清楚；因为他们都只知道自己在场或者听说过的一部分，萧家骥有些话不便出口；阿巧姐跟胡雪岩的想法，亦颇多保留，唯有在七姑奶奶面前倾囊而出，反能了解全盘真相。『家骥这个小鬼头！』古应春骂着，有些忧虑，却也有些得意，『本来人就活动，再跟小爷叔在一起，越发学得花样百出。这样下去，只怕他会走火入魔，专动些歪脑筋。』『他不是那种人。』七姑奶奶答道，『闲话少说，有件事，我还要告诉你∶小爷叔的脾气你晓得的，出手本来就大方；又觉得欠了张郎中很重的一个情，所以我的办法——。』『慢来，慢来！』古应春打断他的话问，『你是什么办法，还没有告诉我；是不是李代桃僵？』『是啊！不然真要弄僵。』七姑奶奶说，『小爷叔也觉得只有我这件办法。而且他想最好年内办成，让张郎中高高兴兴回家；花个千把银子，把归他去。』虽说长三的身价高，千金赎身，也算很阔绰了；但这样身价的『红倌人』，给张郎中作妾，就有些『齐大非偶』的意味了。

“Guessing that much tells me nothing. Come.” He drew her down to sit beside him. “Tell me the whole story.” She told it from beginning to end, omitting no detail, more clearly than anyone who had been present could have done. Each participant knew only what he or she had witnessed or been told. There were things Xiao was reluctant to say, while A’qiao and Hu had kept much of their thinking to themselves. Only before the Seventh Lady had they emptied their hearts, allowing her to grasp the whole truth. “That young rogue Jiaji!” Gu scolded, a little worried but also rather proud. “He was resourceful enough already. Put him with Little Uncle, and he learns ever more tricks. If he goes on like this, he may become so carried away that he thinks of nothing but crooked schemes.” “He is not that sort of person,” she replied. “Enough of that. There is something else you must know. You know how generous Little Uncle is, and he feels deeply indebted to the doctor. So my plan—” “Wait! You have not told me your plan yet. Do you mean to sacrifice the plum tree to save the peach—to find a substitute?” “Of course. Otherwise, things really will turn ugly. Little Uncle agrees that my plan is the only way. He would like it settled before the New Year so that the doctor can go home happy. He says he will pay a thousand taels or so and let him have the woman.” Even among the expensive changsan courtesans, a thousand taels was a generous redemption price. But to make so celebrated and costly a woman the doctor’s concubine would be to give him a match altogether too grand for him.

『这样做法不妥。你再行，到底外场的事情懂得太少——。』『这我又不服了。』七姑奶奶性急的毛病发作了，『就算我一窍不通，难道小爷叔的话也不对？』『自然不对，刚刚一场大病，脑筋自然不够用。再说，小爷叔对堂子里的情形，到底也没有我懂得多。象这种「红倌人」，一句话，叫做不甘寂寞！平日穿得好，吃得好，且不去说它；光是夜夜笙歌的热闹，已经养成习惯，你想想，跟了张郎中，怎么会称心如意？』

“That would not do. Capable as you are, you know too little of affairs outside the home—” “I will not accept that!” Her impatience flared. “Even if I knew nothing, are you saying Little Uncle is wrong too?” “Of course he is. He has just had a serious illness; naturally his mind is not yet working at its best. Besides, even he knows less about the pleasure quarters than I do. A popular courtesan of that sort can be described in a word: she cannot endure a quiet life. Never mind the fine clothes and good food. She has become accustomed to music, company, and revelry every night. How could she be content with Dr Zhang?”

『照你说，那里头就没有一个能从良的？』『十室之内，必有芳草。要说出淤泥而不染的，自然也有，不过可遇而不可求，一下子哪里打了灯笼去找？就算找到了，也要看彼此有没有缘分；光是一头热，有啥用处？』古应春又说，『看在银子分上，勉强跟回家也会过日子，也会生儿子，就是没有笑脸；要笑也是装出来的。如果是这样的情形，哪怕她天仙化人，我也敬谢不敏。』话是不能说没有道理，只是有些言过其实。但是不这么做，『难道就此罢手不成？』她怔怔地问她丈夫。『最后罢手，花了钱挨骂；岂不冤枉？』

“According to you, then, not one of them can leave the profession and marry?” “Even among ten houses, fragrant herbs may be found. Of course there are women who emerge unstained from the mud. But you encounter them by chance; you cannot summon them to order. Where could we find one at a moment’s notice, even with a lantern? And if we did, we should still have to see whether the two were suited. What use is affection on only one side? For money’s sake, a woman might reluctantly go home with a man, keep his house, and bear him sons. But she would never smile; or, if she did, the smile would be forced. In those circumstances, I would respectfully decline even a fairy descended from Heaven.” There was sense in what he said, though he overstated it. Yet if they did not follow the plan, what were they to do? “Must we simply abandon the whole thing?” she asked, staring at him. “In the end you would have to abandon it anyway, after spending the money and being cursed for it. What a waste that would be!”

这句话，七姑奶奶大为不服，『奇了！』她说，『这种事也多得是。你不是自己说过，上个月，什么办厘金的朱老爷，就花三千银子弄了个「活宝」送上司。』『献活宝巴结上司，又当别论——。』古应春另有一番议论——官场中巴结上司，物色美人进献，原是自古已然的事；但取悦一时，不必计及后果。而且名妓为达官贵人作妾，即令家规森严，行动不自由；然而锦衣玉食，排场阔绰，总也有贪图。风尘中受慕虚荣的多；珠围翠绕，婢仆簇拥，夸耀于旧日小姊妹，听得啧啧称羡之声的那一刻，也还是很『过瘾』的。

She objected strongly. “How strange! Such things happen all the time. Did you not tell me yourself that last month some Mr Zhu who collected likin spent three thousand taels on a ‘living treasure’ and presented her to his superior?” “Presenting a living treasure to curry favor with a superior is another matter.” Gu had further arguments. Finding beauties to offer to men above one had been a practice of official life since ancient times. Its purpose was to please for the moment; consequences need not be considered. Moreover, if a famous courtesan became the concubine of a high official, even a strict household and restrictions on her movements were compensated by rich food, fine clothes, and splendid display. Many women of the pleasure quarters loved vanity. Surrounded by jewels and attendants, showing off to former companions and hearing their admiring exclamations—that moment could be deeply satisfying.

『张郎中能够有什么给艳春老四？』古应春说，『就算他殷实，做生意人家总是生意人家的规矩，讲究实惠；不见得经常替她做衣服，打首饰。日常饮食，更不会象做大官的人家，天天鸡鱼鸭肉。内地又不比上海，过惯了繁华日子的，你想想她心里是何滋味？少不得三天两头生闲气，这就叫不安于室。张郎中哪里还有艳福好享？』七姑奶奶想起一句成语∶『爱之适足以害之』；也觉得不妥，然而又何致于挨骂？她心里这样在想，还未问出口，古应春却已有了解释∶『做人情也是一门学问。象这样的情形，懂道理的人，一定批评小爷叔，简直就是以怨报德，这倒还在其次；张郎中家里的人，一定骂死了小爷叔。你想是不是呢？』

“What could Dr Zhang give the Fourth Miss of Yanchun House?” Gu asked. “Even if he is comfortably off, a business household follows business habits. It values practical things. He is unlikely to keep having clothes and jewelry made for her. Nor will the daily table resemble a great official’s, with chicken, fish, duck, and meat every day. An inland town is not Shanghai. Think how a woman accustomed to all this splendor would feel. She would be finding something to be angry about every few days. That is what it means to be restless at home. How would Dr Zhang enjoy his good fortune then?” The Seventh Lady remembered the saying that love may do precisely the harm it wishes to prevent. She too began to think the plan unsuitable. But why should it bring curses upon them? Before she could ask, Gu supplied the answer. “Doing a favor is an art in itself. In such a case, anyone with sense would accuse Little Uncle of repaying kindness with injury. And that is the least of it. The doctor’s family would curse him to death. Would they not?”

设身处地想一想，自己也会如此；不但要骂出钱的人，还会骂出主意的人。七姑奶奶这样想着，深为不安。可是，阿巧姐又如何？『事情总要有个了结。』七姑奶奶说，『当然，这件事要两厢情愿，这面不肯，那面也没有话说；不过当初那样做法，显得有点有意用「美人计」骗人上当，倘或就此记恨，说出去的话一定难听；不要说阿巧姐，就是小爷叔也一定不开心。』古应春沉吟了一会，从从容容地答道∶『没有别的办法，只有多送银子，作为补偿。』『也只好如此。』七姑奶奶说，『到时候再说，此刻不必去伤脑筋了！』

Putting herself in their place, she realized that she would do the same. She would curse not only the person who paid, but the person who proposed the scheme. The thought troubled her deeply. Yet what was to become of A’qiao’s predicament? “There must be some conclusion to this. Naturally, it requires willingness on both sides. If one refuses, the other has nothing to say. But the way it began makes it look as though we deliberately used a beauty to lure him into a trap. If he resents it, what he says afterward will be unpleasant. Quite apart from A’qiao, Little Uncle will certainly be unhappy too.” Gu considered for a while, then answered calmly, “There is no other way. We must give him more money as compensation.” “Then that is what we must do,” she said. “We can discuss it when the time comes. There is no need to rack our brains over it now.”
