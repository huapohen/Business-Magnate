红顶商人｜The Red-Topped Merchant

第六章（本章第3部分，共5部分）

第一步实在是试探。如果阿巧姐不信她只信胡雪岩∶拿她批评胡雪岩用情不专，迹近薄幸的种种『背后之言』，付之一笑，听过丢开；这出戏就很难唱得下去了。或者，胡雪岩对阿巧姐迷恋已深，极力辩白，决无其事，取得阿巧姐的谅解；这出戏就更难唱得下去了。谁知阿巧姐疑心她的话，出于胡雪岩的授意；而胡雪岩居然是默认的模样，这个机会若是轻轻放过，岂不大负本心？于是，她正一正脸色，显得极郑重地相劝∶『小爷叔！阿巧姐你不能要了。旁观者清，我替你想过，如果你一定不肯撒手，受累无穷——。』照七姑奶奶的说法，胡雪岩对阿巧姐有『四不可要』∶第一、阿巧姐如果一定要在外面『立门户』，坏了胡太太的家法，会搞得夫妇反目。第二、即令阿巧姐肯『回去』，亦是很勉强的事，心中有了芥蒂，妻妾之间会失和。第三、阿巧姐既由何家下堂，而且当初是由胡雪岩撮合，如今就该避嫌疑；不然，保不定会有人说他当初不过『献美求荣』，这是个极丑的名声。第四、阿巧姐出身青楼，又在总督衙门见过大世面；这样的人，是不是能够跟着胡雪岩从良到底，实在大成疑问。『小爷叔！』最后七姑奶奶又恳切地劝说，『杭州一失守，王雪公一殉难；你的老根断掉了，靠山倒掉了。以后等于要重起炉灶，着实得下一番功夫，才能恢复从前那种场面。如果说，你是象张胖子那样肯守的，只要一家吃饱穿暖就心满意足，那我没有话说；想要创一番事业，小爷叔，你这个时候千万闹不得家务。不但闹不得家务，还要婶娘切切实实助你一臂之力才行。这当中的利害关系，你倒仔细想一想！』前面的『四不可要』，胡雪岩觉得也不过『想当然耳』的危言耸听；最后一句『这个时候千万闹不得家务』，却真的让他悚然心惊了。『七姐，你晓得的，我不是张胖子那种人，我不但要重起炉灶创一番事业；而且要大大创它一番事业。你提醒了我，这个时候心无二用，哪里有功夫来闹家务——。』『是啊！』七姑奶奶抢着说∶『你不想闹家务；家务会闹到你头上来！推不开，摔不掉，那才叫苦恼。『』我就是怕这个！看样子，非听你的不可了。『』这才是！谢天谢地，小爷叔，你总算想通了。『七姑奶奶高兴地说，』阿巧姐自然是好的；不过也不是天下独一无二就是她！将来有的是。『』将来！『胡雪岩顿一顿足∶』就看在将来上面。七姐，我们好好来谈一谈。『

Her first step had really been a test. If A’qiao had trusted Hu alone, laughed off the Seventh Lady’s remarks behind his back about his inconstancy and apparent heartlessness, and forgotten them, the play would have been difficult to continue. If Hu’s infatuation had been so deep that he strenuously denied every charge and won A’qiao’s understanding, it would have been harder still. Instead, A’qiao suspected that Hu had authorised the remarks, while Hu seemed to accept the accusation in silence. To let such a chance slip would betray the Seventh Lady’s own purpose. She therefore composed her face and spoke with great earnestness. “Little Uncle, you must give A’qiao up. An onlooker sees clearly. I have considered it for you: if you refuse to let go, there will be no end to the trouble.” She advanced four reasons. First, if A’qiao insisted on establishing a separate household, breaking Mrs Hu’s rule would set husband and wife against each other. Second, even if she agreed to join the family, she would do so unwillingly; the grievance in her heart would destroy harmony between wife and concubine. Third, since she had left He Guiqing, and Hu himself had arranged that union, he ought now to avoid suspicion. Otherwise people might say he had originally offered a beauty in exchange for advancement—a profoundly ugly reputation. Fourth, she had come from the pleasure quarters and had seen the great world in a governor-general’s residence. Whether such a woman could settle permanently into respectable life with Hu was very doubtful. “Little Uncle,” she urged finally, “when Hangzhou fell and Governor Wang died, your roots were cut and your support collapsed. You must begin again. It will take real effort to recover your former position. If you were like Fatty Zhang, content to hold what you have and keep the family fed and clothed, I should have nothing to say. But if you mean to build a career, this is the one time when you must not have domestic trouble. More than that, you need your wife’s solid support. Think carefully what you stand to gain or lose.” Hu thought the first four reasons little more than alarming suppositions. But the last warning—that this was no time for domestic trouble—truly startled him. “Seventh Sister, you know I am not like Fatty Zhang. I mean not merely to begin again and build a career, but to build a great one. You have reminded me: my mind cannot be in two places at once. Where have I the time for family quarrels?” “Exactly!” she broke in. “You may not want domestic trouble, but domestic trouble will come looking for you. You cannot push it away or throw it off. That is when you will suffer.” “That is just what I fear. It seems I must listen to you.” “At last! Thank Heaven, Little Uncle, you have come to see it!” she said joyfully. “A’qiao is a fine woman, of course, but she is not the only one in the world. There will be others in the future.” “The future!” Hu stamped his foot. “Let it be for the sake of the future. Seventh Sister, let us discuss this properly.”

要谈的是如何处置阿巧姐。提到这一层，七姑奶奶不免踌躇∶『说实话，』她说，『我还要动脑筋！』『七姐，』胡雪岩似乎很不放心，『我现在有句话，你一定要答应我。你动出啥脑筋来，要先跟我说明白。』这话使得七姑奶奶微觉不安，也微有反感∶『哟！哟！你这样子说法，倒象我会瞒着你，拿她推到火炕里去似的。』她很费劲地分辩，『我跟阿巧姐一向处得很好，现在为了你小爷叔，抹熬良心做事；你好象反倒埋怨我独断独行——。』『七姐，七姐！』胡雪岩不容她再往下说，兜头长揖，『我不能「狗咬吕洞宾，不识好人心」，无非我自己觉得对不起她，要想好好补报她一番而已。』『我还不是这样？你放心好了，我决不会动她的坏脑筋。』说到这里，七姑奶奶的眼睛突然发亮；同时绽开笑靥，望空出神。这是动到了极好的脑筋。胡雪岩不敢打搅她；但心里却急得很！渴望她揭开谜底。七姑奶奶却似有意报复∶『我想得差不多了。不过，小爷对不起，我现在不没有动手，到开始做的时候，一定跟你说明白；你也一定会赞成。』『七姐！』胡雪岩陪笑说道∶『你何妨先跟我说说？』『不行，起码要等我想妥当，才能告诉你。』七姑奶奶又说，『不是我故意卖关子，实在是还没有把握，不如暂且不说的好。』听她言词闪烁，竟不知她葫芦里卖的什么药？以她的性情，再问亦无用，胡雪岩只好叹口气算了。

They needed to decide what to do for A’qiao. Here the Seventh Lady hesitated. “To be honest, I must still think of a way.” Hu seemed uneasy. “There is one thing you must promise me. Whatever plan you devise, explain it to me first.” This made her a little uncomfortable and a little resentful. “Oh, listen to you! You make it sound as though I might secretly push her into some dreadful fate.” She defended herself with considerable feeling. “A’qiao and I have always got on well. Now, for your sake, I am acting against my conscience, and you seem to blame me for taking too much upon myself—” “Seventh Sister, Seventh Sister!” Hu stopped her with a deep bow. “I must not be the dog that bites Lü Dongbin and fails to recognise a benefactor. I merely feel I have wronged her and want to compensate her properly.” “Do I feel otherwise? Rest assured. I shall never devise anything to harm her.” Suddenly her eyes brightened. A smile broke over her face as she gazed into space. A very good idea had occurred to her. Hu dared not interrupt, though he was impatient to hear it. She, however, seemed determined to take a little revenge. “I have most of it worked out. But forgive me, Little Uncle: I have not begun yet. When I do, I shall explain it clearly, and you will certainly approve.” “Why not tell me first?” he asked with an ingratiating smile. “No. At least let me think it through before I tell you. I am not deliberately keeping you in suspense. I am simply not yet sure of it, so I had better say nothing for the moment.” Her evasions left him entirely uncertain what she was planning. Knowing her temperament, he realised further questions were useless and gave up with a sigh.

到了第二天，胡雪岩又去看七姑奶奶，恰好古应春也在，谈起家眷将到，另外要找房子，置家具，备办日用物品，本来可以关照阿巧姐动手的，此刻似乎不便麻烦她了。『不要紧！』七姑奶奶在这些事上最热心，也最有兴趣，慨然应承∶『都交给我好了。』在一旁静听的古应春，不免困惑，『为啥不能请阿巧姐帮忙？』他问。『其中自然有道理。』七姑奶奶抢着说∶『回头告诉你。』『又是什么花样？』古应春跟他妻子提忠告∶『你可不要替小爷叔乱出主意。现在这个辰光，顶要紧的就是安静二字。』『正是为了安静两个字。』七姑奶奶不愿丈夫打搅，催着他说∶『不是说，有人请你吃花酒；可以走了。』『吃花酒要等人来催请，哪有这么早，自己赶了去的？』古应春看出妻子的意思，觉得还是顺从为妙；所以又自己搭讪着说∶『也好！我先去看个朋友。』『慢点！』七姑奶奶说，『我想起来了，有次秦先生说起，他的亲戚有幢房子在三马路，或卖或典都可以，你不妨替小爷叔去问一问。』秦先生是她家号子里的帐房。古应春恪遵阃令，答应立刻去看秦先生细问；请胡雪岩第二天来听消息。『这样吧，』七姑奶奶说，『你索性请秦先生明天一早来一趟。』『大概又是请他写信。』古应春说，『如果今天晚上有空，我就叫他来。』

The next day Hu called again. Gu was there too. They discussed the approaching arrival of Hu’s family and the need to find a house, furnish it and obtain everyday necessities. Normally A’qiao could have been asked to see to these things, but now it seemed awkward to trouble her. “Never mind!” The Seventh Lady was always most enthusiastic about such matters and enjoyed them greatly. “Leave it all to me.” Listening beside them, Gu was puzzled. “Why can A’qiao not help?” “There is a reason,” his wife interrupted. “I shall tell you later.” “What new device is this?” He warned her, “Do not give Little Uncle ill-considered advice. At this moment, peace is what matters most.” “Peace is precisely the purpose.” She did not want her husband interfering and hurried him along. “Were you not invited to a courtesan banquet? You can go now.” “One waits for a reminder before going to such a banquet. Who hurries off on his own at this hour?” Gu saw what she meant and thought it best to comply. Finding his own way out, he added, “Still, I can visit a friend first.” “Wait!” she said. “I remember Mr Qin once mentioning that a relative of his had a house on Third Road, available either for sale or under a redeemable property pledge. Ask about it for Little Uncle.” Mr Qin was the accountant at their firm. Obedient to his wife’s command, Gu agreed to question him at once and asked Hu to return for news the following day. “Better still,” she said, “ask Mr Qin to come here early tomorrow.” “You probably want him to write another letter,” Gu observed. “If he is free tonight, I shall send him over then.”

于是七姑奶奶等丈夫一走，便又跟胡雪岩谈阿巧姐，『小爷叔，』他问∶『你的主意打定了？将来不会懊悔，背后埋怨我棒打鸳鸯两分离？』『哪有这样的事？七姐在现在还不明白我的脾气？』『我晓得，小爷叔是说到做到、做了不悔的脾气。不过，我还是问一声的好，既然小爷叔主意打定，明天我就要动手了。你只装不知道，看出什么异样，放在肚子里就是。』『我懂！』胡雪岩问∶『她如果要逼着我问，我怎么样？』『不会逼着你问的，一切照旧，毫无变动，她问什么？』『好的！那就是我们杭州人说的那句话∶』城隍山上看火烧！「我只等着看热闹了。『如果不是极深的交情，这句话就有讽刺意味的语病了。不过七姑奶奶还是提醒他，不可自以为已经置身事外；一旦火烧了起来，也许会惊心动魄，身不由主，那时一定要有定方，视如不见，切忌临时沉不住气，横身插入，那一来，她说∶『就会引火烧身；我也要受连累，总而言之一句话，不管阿巧姐说什么，你不要理她！』原来七姑奶奶由胡雪岩要买房子，想到一个主意，决定借这个机会刺激阿巧姐，能把她气走了，一了百了。但也可能会发生极大的风波，所以特意提出警告。

Once her husband had gone, the Seventh Lady returned to A’qiao’s affairs. “Little Uncle, is your mind made up? You will not regret it later and complain behind my back that I struck apart a pair of loving mandarin ducks?” “How could I? Do you still not know my temper?” “I know: you do what you say, and do not regret what you have done. Still, I had better ask. Since you are determined, I shall begin tomorrow. You must merely pretend to know nothing. Whatever unusual thing you notice, keep it to yourself.” “I understand. But what if she presses me with questions?” “She will not. Everything will continue as before, without a change. What would she ask?” “Very well. As we say in Hangzhou, I shall watch the fire from City God Hill. I shall wait and enjoy the spectacle.” Between less intimate friends, this might have sounded offensive or sarcastic. Even so, she warned him not to suppose that he had removed himself from the affair. Once the fire began, events might alarm him and draw him in despite himself. He must remain firm, act as though he saw nothing and above all avoid losing his composure and intervening. “Otherwise you will bring the fire upon yourself, and I shall suffer too. In a word, whatever A’qiao says, pay no attention.” Hu’s need to buy a house had given her an idea. She would use it to provoke A’qiao into leaving of her own accord and settle the matter once for all. But there might also be a great storm, hence her special warning.

购屋之事，相当顺利；秦先生所介绍的那幢房子，在三马路靠近有名的画锦里，虽是闹事，但屋宇宏深，关紧大门，就可以隔绝市嚣，等于闹中取静。胡雪岩深为中意，问价钱也不贵，只有鹰洋两千五百元；所以当天就成交了。七姑奶奶奶非常热心，『小爷叔，』她说，『你再拿一千块钱给我；一切都归我包办。这三天你去干你的事；到第四天你来看，是啥样子？』『这还有啥好说的？不过，七姐，太费你的心了！』胡雪岩知道她的脾气，这样说句客气话就行了。如果觉得她过于劳累，于心不安，要派人去为她分劳，反使得她不高兴，所以交了一千银洋给她，不闻不问。趁这三天功夫，在自己钱庄里盘一盘帐，问一问业务，倒是切切实实做了些事。第三天从集贤里阜康钱庄回家，只见阿巧姐头光面滑，点唇涂脂，是打扮过了；但身上却穿的是家常衣衫，不知是正要出门，还是从外面回来？『我刚回来。我去看七姑奶奶了。』阿巧姐说，『三马路的房子，弄得很漂亮啊！』

The purchase went smoothly. The house Mr Qin recommended stood on Third Road near the famous Huajin Lane. Though the neighbourhood was busy, the house was spacious and deep; closing its great door shut out the clamour and created quiet amid the bustle. Hu liked it very much. The price was modest too—only two thousand five hundred eagle silver dollars—so he completed the purchase that day. The Seventh Lady was full of zeal. “Little Uncle, give me another thousand dollars and leave everything to me. Attend to your own affairs for three days. On the fourth, come and see what I have made of it.” “What more could I ask? But it is so much trouble for you, Seventh Sister.” He knew her ways. That polite acknowledgement was enough. If he worried that she was overworking and sent someone to share the burden, he would only displease her. He gave her a thousand silver dollars and neither asked nor interfered. During the three days he examined the accounts at his own bank and enquired into its business, accomplishing some solid work. On the third day he came home from Fukang Bank in Jixian Lane and found A’qiao with her hair dressed, her face carefully powdered and her lips coloured. Yet she wore informal clothes. Was she about to go out or had she just returned? “I have only just come back. I went to see the Seventh Lady,” she said. “The house on Third Road has been made very handsome.”

语气很平静，但在胡雪岩听来，似有怨责他瞒着她的味道；因而讪讪地有些无从接口。『七姑奶奶问我∶房子好不好？我自然说好。她又问我想不想去住；你道我怎么回答她？我说∶我没有这份福气。』胡雪岩本来想答一句∶只怕是我没有这份福气。话到口边，忽又缩住；用漫不经意的口吻答道∶『住这种夷场上的所谓「弄堂房子」，算啥福气？将来杭州光复，在西湖上好好造一座庄子；住那种洞天福地，可真就要前世修一修了。』阿巧姐不作声，坐到梳妆台前去卸头面首饰；胡雪岩便由丫头伺候着，脱掉马褂，换上便鞋，坐在窗前喝茶。『我看，』阿巧姐突然说道∶『我修修来世吧！』『来世我们做夫妻。』胡雪岩脱口相答。阿巧姐颜色大变——在胡雪岩的意思，既然她今生不肯嫁胡家的偏房；那就只好期望来世一夫一妻，白头到老。而阿巧姐误会了！『我原在奇怪，七姑奶奶为啥说那些话？果不其然，你是变心了！有话你很可以自己说，何必转弯抹角去托人？』

Her tone was calm, but Hu thought he heard a reproach for keeping it from her. Embarrassed, he hardly knew how to answer. “She asked whether I liked the house. Naturally I said yes. Then she asked if I wanted to live there. Do you know what I told her? That I was not so fortunate.” Hu almost replied, “I fear I am the one without that good fortune,” but checked the words. Casually he said, “What good fortune is there in one of these so-called lane houses in the foreign settlement? When Hangzhou is recovered, I shall build a proper estate beside West Lake. To live in such a paradise would indeed require merit accumulated in a former life.” A’qiao said nothing. She went to the dressing table to remove her ornaments, while a maid helped Hu take off his riding jacket and change into slippers. He sat by the window with his tea. “I think I had better accumulate merit for the next life,” she said suddenly. “In the next life we shall be husband and wife,” he answered without thinking. Her face changed dramatically. He meant that, since she would not become his concubine in this life, he could only hope that in the next they might be one husband and one wife, growing old together. But she misunderstood. “I wondered why the Seventh Lady said those things. So your heart really has changed! If you have something to say, say it yourself. Why go round corners and get someone else to do it?”

胡雪岩知道自己失言了。然而也实在不能怪自己；那天原就问过七姑奶奶，如果阿巧姐逼着要问她的归宿？如何作答。七姑奶奶认为『一切照旧，毫无变动』，她不会问。照现在看，情形不同了！新居既已为她所见，『变动』便已开始，以后她不断会问；总不能每次一问，便象此刻一样，惹得她怨气冲天。看来还是要靠自己动脑筋应付！他这样对自己说；而且马上很用心地去体察她的态度。为什么她不自己想一想，她这样不肯与大妇同住，悖乎常情，强人所准；而偏偏一再要指责他变心？莫非她自己有下堂求去之意，只是说不出口，有意这样诿过，这样逼迫；想把决裂的责任，加在他头上？这是个看来近乎荒诞的想法。胡雪岩自问∶果真自己是小人之心？不见得！阿巧姐当初对何桂清亦曾倾心过，到后来不管怎么说，总是负心；而且是在何桂清倒霉的时候负心。这样看起来，将她看成一个『君子』，似乎也太天真了些。就这一念之间，他自己觉得心肠硬了；用不大带感情的、平静得近乎冷漠的声音说∶『我没有什么话好说。你愿意修修来世，我当然也只好希望来世再做夫妻。』『你的意思是，今生今世不要我了？』阿巧姐转过脸过来，逼视着他问。他将视线避了开去，『我没有说这话，不过——。』他没有再说下去。

Hu knew he had spoken wrongly. Yet he could hardly be blamed. He had asked the Seventh Lady what to say if A’qiao pressed him about her future. She had assured him that, with everything unchanged, no questions would arise. But things were different now. A’qiao had seen the new house; the change had begun. She would keep asking, and he could not have her fly into a rage every time, as she had now. It seemed he must rely on his own ingenuity after all. He began studying her attitude closely. Why did she not consider her own refusal to live with his principal wife? She was defying accepted practice and asking the impossible, yet repeatedly accused him of a change of heart. Could it be that she herself wished to leave but could not say so, and was pressing him and shifting blame in order to make him responsible for the break? The thought seemed almost absurd. Was he judging her with a petty mind? Not necessarily. She had once given her heart to He Guiqing too. Whatever might be said, in the end she had failed him—and when he was in misfortune. Was it not rather naive to regard her as a paragon? In that moment Hu felt his own heart harden. In a quiet voice, almost cold, he said, “I have nothing to say. If you wish to cultivate good fortune for another life, I can only hope that we shall be husband and wife then.” She turned and looked directly at him. “You mean you do not want me in this life?” He avoided her eyes. “I did not say that. Only—” He stopped.

『说啊！男子汉大丈夫，说话不要吞吞吐吐！』遇到他这种口吻语气，如果她是愿意委屈息事的，至多流泪，不会追问，既然追问，便有不惜破脸的打算。胡雪岩觉得了解她的态度就够了；此时犯不着跟她破脸——最好永不破脸，好来好散！于是他笑笑说道∶『我们都不是三岁两岁的小孩，这个样子教底下人笑话，何必呢？』『哼！』阿巧姐冷笑了一下，依然回过脸去，对镜卸妆。胡雪岩觉得无聊得很。这种感觉是以前所从不曾有过的；他在家的时候不多，所以一回到家，只要看见阿巧姐的影子，便觉得世界上只有这个家最舒服，非万不得已，不肯再出门。而此刻，却想到哪里去走走；哪怕就在街上逛逛也好。此念一动，不可抑制；站起身来说∶『我还要出去一趟。』说了这话，又觉歉然，因而问道∶『你想吃点啥？我替你带回来。』阿巧姐只摇摇头，似乎连话也懒得说。胡雪岩觉得背上一阵一阵发冷；拔步就走，就穿着那双便鞋，也不着马褂，径自下楼而去。

“Say it! A grown man should not mumble and hesitate.” If she had been willing to yield for the sake of peace, she might have wept at his tone, but would not have pursued the question. By pressing him, she showed that she was prepared for an open break. Hu felt it was enough to understand her position. There was no need to quarrel now—better never to quarrel, but to part amicably. He smiled. “Neither of us is a two- or three-year-old child. Why make ourselves ridiculous before the servants?” “Hmph!” She gave a cold laugh and turned back to the mirror to remove her make-up. Hu felt thoroughly weary of the place, a feeling he had never known before. He spent little time at home, but whenever he returned, the mere sight of A’qiao had made it seem the most comfortable place in the world. Only absolute necessity could draw him out again. Now he wanted to go somewhere—anywhere, even for a walk in the street. The impulse grew irresistible. Rising, he said, “I must go out again.” Then, feeling apologetic, he asked, “Is there anything you would like to eat? I shall bring it back.” She merely shook her head, as though even speech were too much trouble. A chill passed repeatedly down his back. He left at once, going straight downstairs in his slippers, without putting on his riding jacket.

走出大门，不免茫然∶『轿班』阿福赶来问道∶『老爷要到哪里去？我去叫人。』轿班一共四个人；因为胡雪岩回家时曾经说过，这夜不再出门，所以那三个住在阜康钱庄的都已走了，只剩下阿福在家。『不必！』胡雪岩摆一摆手，径自出弄堂而去。茫然闲步，意兴阑珊；心里要想些有趣的事，偏偏抛不开的是阿巧姐。美目盼兮，巧笑倩兮，那些影子都在眼前；其美如莺的吴枕软语亦清清楚楚地响在耳际。突然间，胡雪岩有着浓重的悔意；掉头就走，而且脚步极快。

Outside the door he felt at a loss. A’fu, one of his sedan-chair bearers, hurried after him. “Where are you going, sir? I shall fetch the men.” There were four bearers. Hu had said on returning that he would not go out again that evening, so the other three, who lodged at Fukang Bank, had already left. Only A’fu remained. “No need!” Hu waved a hand and went out of the lane alone. He wandered without purpose, dispirited. He tried to think of something pleasant, but could not put A’qiao out of his mind. Her lovely, expressive eyes and enchanting smile remained before him; her soft Wu voice, sweet as an oriole’s, sounded clearly in his ears. Suddenly he was seized by overwhelming regret. He turned and walked home at great speed.

到家只见石库墙门已经关上了，叩了几下铜环，来开门的仍是阿福；胡雪岩踏进门便上楼，一眼望去，心先凉了！『奶奶呢？』他指着漆黑的卧室；向从另一间屋里迎出来的丫头素香问说。『奶奶出去了。』『到哪里？』『没有说。』『什么时候走的？』『老爷一走，奶奶就说要出去。』素香答说∶『我问了一声，奶奶骂我∶少管闲事。』『那，怎么走的呢？』胡雪岩问∶『为什么没有要你跟去？』『奶奶不要我跟去；说是等一息就回来。我说∶要不要雇顶轿子？她说，她自己到弄堂口会雇的。』胡雪岩大为失望，而且疑虑重重，原来想跟阿巧姐来说∶『一切照旧，毫无变动』；不管胡太太怎么说，他决意维持这个外室。除非阿巧姐愿意另外择人而事，他是决不会变心的。这一番热念，此刻全都沉入深渊。而且觉得阿巧姐的行踪，深为可疑；素香是她贴身的丫头，出门总是伴随的，而竟撇下不带，可知所去的这个地方，是素香去不得的，或者说，是她连素香都要瞒住的。

The stone-framed outer door was already shut. He knocked several times with the bronze ring. A’fu opened it, and Hu went straight upstairs. One glance chilled his heart. “Where is your mistress?” Pointing to the dark bedroom, he questioned Suxiang, the maid who had come out of another room to meet him. “She has gone out.” “Where?” “She did not say.” “When did she leave?” “As soon as you had gone, she said she was going out. I asked her a question and she scolded me: ‘Mind your own business.’” “How did she go? Why did she not take you?” “She would not have me with her. She said she would be back shortly. I asked if I should hire a chair, and she said she could get one herself at the entrance to the lane.” Hu was deeply disappointed and full of misgivings. He had returned to tell her that everything would remain as before, without a change. Whatever his wife might say, he had resolved to maintain this separate household. Unless A’qiao herself wished to choose another man, he would never change his heart. Now all that warmth sank into an abyss. Her movements seemed deeply suspicious. Suxiang was her personal maid and always accompanied her outside. If she had left the girl behind, she must have gone somewhere the maid could not go—or somewhere she wished to conceal even from Suxiang.

意会到此，心中泛起难以言宣的酸苦抑郁；站在客堂中，久久无语。这使得素香有些害怕，怯怯地问道∶『老爷！是不是在家吃饭？我去关照厨房。』『我不饿！』胡雪岩问∶『阿祥呢？』『阿祥，出去了。』『出去了！到哪里？』『要——，』素香吞吞吐吐地说∶『要问阿福。』

With that thought, an inexpressible bitterness and despondency filled him. He stood in the sitting room without speaking for a long time. Suxiang grew frightened. “Sir,” she asked timidly, “will you dine at home? I shall tell the kitchen.” “I am not hungry. Where is A’xiang?” “A’xiang has gone out.” “Out? Where?” “You would have to—” Suxiang faltered. “You would have to ask A’fu.”
