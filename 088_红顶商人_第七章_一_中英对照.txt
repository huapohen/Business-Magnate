第七章｜CHAPTER SEVEN

一早起身，张胖子还保持着多年的习惯，提着鸟笼上茶店；有时候经过魏老板那里，因为同行的缘故，也打个招呼。魏老板克勤克俭，从来不上茶店；但张胖子这天非邀他去吃茶不可，因为做媒的事，当着阿巧不便谈。踏进店堂，开门见山道明来意，魏老板颇有突然之感；因而便有辞谢之意。就在这时候，阿巧替她父亲来送早点，一碗豆腐浆，一团粢米饭，看到张老板甜甜地招呼∶『张伯伯早！点心吃过没有？』

On rising early, Fatty Zhang followed the habit of many years and took his birdcage to the teahouse. Sometimes, passing Mr Wei’s shop, he would stop to greet a fellow tradesman. Wei was industrious and frugal and never went to teahouses, but that morning Zhang was determined to take him out for tea: the proposed marriage could hardly be discussed in A’qiao’s presence. Entering the shop, he stated his immediate purpose plainly. Wei was taken by surprise and inclined to decline. Just then A’qiao brought her father breakfast, a bowl of soy milk and a ball of glutinous rice. Seeing their visitor, she greeted him sweetly. “Good morning, Uncle Zhang! Have you had breakfast?”

张胖子不即回答，将她从头看到脚，真有点相亲的味道；看得阿巧有些发窘。但客人还未答话，不便掉身而去；只有将头扭了开去，避开张胖子那双盯住了看的眼睛。『阿巧！』张胖子问道，『你今年几岁？』『十七。』『生日当然是七月初七。时辰呢？』这下惊了阿巧！一早上门，来问时辰八字，不是替自己做媒是做啥？这样转着念头，立刻想到阿祥；也立刻就着慌了！『那个要你来做啥断命的媒？』她在心中自语；急急地奔到后面，寻着她母亲问道∶『张胖子一早跑来为啥？』『哪个张胖子？』

Zhang did not answer at once. He looked her over from head to foot, very much as though appraising a prospective bride, until she grew embarrassed. Since he had not yet answered her greeting, however, she could hardly turn and leave. She merely averted her face from his fixed gaze. “A’qiao,” he asked, “how old are you this year?” “Seventeen.” “Your birthday is naturally the seventh day of the seventh month. At what hour were you born?” That startled her. What could a man mean by calling first thing in the morning and asking for her birth details except that he had come to arrange a match? Her thoughts flew at once to A’xiang, and she panicked. “Who asked you to come matchmaking and ruin my life?” she thought. Hurrying to the back, she found her mother. “What has Fatty Zhang come for so early?” “Which Fatty Zhang?”

『还有哪个？不就是同行冤家的张胖子！』『他来了？我不晓得啊！』『娘！』阿巧扯着她的衣服说∶『张胖子不晓得啥心思，又问生日，又问时辰。我——，』她顿一顿足说∶『我是不嫁的！用不着啥人来罗嗦。』这一说，做母亲的倒是精神一振；不晓得张胖子替女儿做的媒，是个何等样人？当时便说∶『你先不要乱！等我来问问看。』

“Who else? The one in the same trade as Father—his rival!” “Has he come? I did not know.” “Mother!” A’qiao clutched her clothes. “He is up to something. First he asks my birthday, then my birth hour. I—” She stamped her foot. “I am not getting married! No one need come meddling.” Her mother, however, became interested at once. What sort of young man had Zhang found for her daughter? “Do not make a fuss yet. Let me ask.”

发觉母亲是颇感兴趣的神气，阿巧非常失望，也很着急。她心里在想，此身已有所属，母亲是知道的，平时对阿祥的言语态度，隐隐然视之为『半子』；那就不但知道自己属意于什么人，而且这个人也是她所中意的。既然如此，何必又去『问问看』？岂不是不明事理的老糊涂了？苦的是心里这番话说不出口；也无法用任何暗示提醒她。情急之下，只有撒娇；拉住她母亲的衣服不放。『不要去问！狗嘴里吐不出象牙，没有啥好问的。』『问问也不要紧。你这样子做啥？』母女俩拉拉扯扯，僵持着，也因循着；而魏老板却因为情面难却，接受了张胖子的邀请，在外面提高了声音喊∶『阿巧娘！你出来看店；我跟张老板吃茶去了。』这一下阿巧更为着急。原意是想母亲拿父亲叫进来，关照一句∶如果张胖子来做媒，不要理他。不想要紧话未曾说清楚，白白耽误了功夫。如今一起去吃茶，当然是说媒；婚事虽说父母之命，而父亲可以做七分主，如果在茶店里糊里糊涂听信了张胖子的花言巧语，那就是一辈子不甘心的恨事。念头风驰电掣般快，转到此处，阿巧脱口喊道∶『爹，你请进来，娘有要紧话说。』魏老板听这一说，便回了进来；他妻子问他∶『张胖子是不是来替阿巧做媒？』

Her mother’s interest disappointed and alarmed A’qiao. Surely she knew that her daughter’s heart was already given. Her ordinary manner toward A’xiang had long hinted that she regarded him as half a son. She therefore knew whom A’qiao wanted and approved of him herself. Why, then, should she still want to “ask”? Was she growing foolish and unreasonable in her old age? The trouble was that A’qiao could say none of this aloud, nor could she think of any hint that would remind her. In desperation she could only behave like a spoiled child, clinging to her mother’s clothes. “Do not ask! No ivory comes from a dog’s mouth. There is nothing worth asking him.” “What harm can it do? Why are you behaving like this?” Mother and daughter tugged at each other, delaying and reaching no conclusion. Meanwhile, Wei had found it impossible to refuse Zhang’s invitation. From the front he raised his voice. “A’qiao’s mother! Come and mind the shop. I am going out for tea with Mr Zhang.” Now A’qiao was more anxious than ever. She had meant her mother to call her father in and warn him not to listen if Zhang proposed a match. Instead, they had wasted precious time without making the important point clear. Naturally, if the men went out for tea, they would discuss the marriage. Marriage was said to be decided by both parents, but seven-tenths of the authority lay with her father. If he carelessly swallowed Zhang’s honeyed words at the teahouse, she would have cause for resentment all her life. Her thoughts raced like lightning. “Father, come inside!” she blurted out. “Mother has something important to say.” Wei returned, and his wife asked, “Has Fatty Zhang come to arrange a match for A’qiao?”

魏老板还未答话，阿巧接口∶『哪个要他来做啥媒？我是不嫁的。』『咦！』魏老板看看妻子，又看看女儿，真有些莫名其妙了，『你们怎么想到这上头去了？』阿巧耳朵灵，心思快，立刻喜孜孜地问道∶『那末，他来做啥呢？』『他说要跟我谈一笔生意。』『谈生意？』他妻子问道∶『店里不好谈？』『我也是这么说。他说他一早起来一定要吃茶，不然没有精神。我就陪他去吃一回也不要紧。』『好，好！』阿巧推一推她父亲，『你老人家请！不过，只好谈主意，不好谈别的。』

Before he could answer, A’qiao broke in. “Who asked him to arrange any match? I am not getting married.” “Eh?” Wei looked from his wife to his daughter in genuine bewilderment. “How did you come to think of that?” Quick-eared and quick-witted, A’qiao brightened at once. “Then what has he come for?” “He says he wants to discuss a piece of business.” “Business?” his wife asked. “Can he not discuss it in the shop?” “That is what I said. But he says he must have his morning tea or he has no energy. There is no harm in my joining him for once.” “Very well!” A’qiao gave her father a little push. “Off you go, then. But you may discuss business only—nothing else.”

这一去去了两个钟头还不回来；阿巧心里有嘀咕，叫小徒弟到张胖子每天必到的那家茶店里去悄悄探望。须臾回转，张胖子跟魏老板都不在那里。这就显得可疑了。等到日中，依然不见魏老板的影子，母女俩等了好半天等不回来，只有先吃午饭。刚扶起筷子，魏老板回来了，满脸红光，也满脸的笑容。阿巧又是欣慰又是怨∶『到哪里去了？』她埋怨着∶『吃饭也不回来！』『张胖子请我吃酒；这顿酒吃得开心。』『啥开心？生意谈成功了？』阿巧问∶『是啥生意？』『不但谈生意，还谈了别样。是件大事！』魏老板坐下来笑道∶『你们猜得不错，张胖子是来替我们女儿做媒的。』听到这里，阿巧手足发冷；一下扑到母亲肩上，浑身抖个不住。

Two hours passed, and he did not return. Uneasy, A’qiao sent the little apprentice to make a discreet inquiry at the teahouse Zhang visited every day. The boy soon came back: neither man was there. This looked suspicious. By noon there was still no sign of Wei. Mother and daughter waited a long time, then decided to eat without him. They had just picked up their chopsticks when he appeared, his face flushed and wreathed in smiles. A’qiao was relieved but reproachful. “Where have you been? You did not even come home for lunch.” “Fatty Zhang invited me for a drink. A thoroughly enjoyable drink it was too.” “What was so enjoyable? Did you settle the business? What business was it?” “We discussed more than business. There was something else—something important.” Wei sat down, smiling. “You guessed correctly. Zhang did come to arrange our daughter’s marriage.” A’qiao’s hands and feet turned cold. She threw herself against her mother’s shoulder and shook from head to foot.

魏老板夫妇俩无不既惊且毫！问她是怎么回事？却又似不肯明说；只勉强坐了下来，怔怔地望着她父亲。到底知女莫若母，毕竟猜中了她的心事；急急向丈夫说∶『张胖子做媒，你不要乱答应人家。』『为啥不答应？』『你答应人家了！是怎么样的人家；新郎倌什么样子？』『新郎倌什么样子，何用我说？你们天天看见的。』

Both parents were startled and alarmed. They asked what was wrong, but she seemed unwilling to explain. With an effort she sat down again and stared at her father. No one knew a daughter better than her mother, who at last guessed what was troubling her. “You must not agree carelessly to a match Fatty Zhang proposes,” she told her husband urgently. “Why should I not agree?” “You have agreed already? What sort of family is it? What is the bridegroom like?” “Why should I tell you what he is like? You see him every day.”

提到每天看到的人，第一个想起的是间壁水果店的小伙计润生，做事巴结，生得也还体面；他有一手『绝技』，客人上门买只生梨要扦皮，润生手舞两把平头薄背的水果刀，旋转如飞，眼睛一霎的功夫，扦得干干净净，梨皮成一长条。陈巧最爱看他这手功夫；他也最爱看阿巧含笑凝视的神情。有一次看得出神失了手，自己削掉一小节指头；一条街上传为笑谈。以此话柄为嫌，阿巧从此总是避着他；但彼此紧邻，无法不天天见面，润生颇得东家的器重，当然是可能来求婚的。第二个想起的是对面香蜡店的小开，生得倒是一表人才，而且门当户对，可惜终年揭不得帽子；因为是个癞痢。阿巧想起来就腻味，赶紧抛开再想。

The first person A’qiao thought of was Runsheng, the young assistant at the fruit shop next door. He worked hard and was quite presentable. He also had a remarkable skill: if a customer bought a pear and wanted it peeled, Runsheng would set two thin-backed, flat-tipped fruit knives whirling in his hands. In the blink of an eye the pear was bare, its skin removed in one long strip. A’qiao loved to watch him do it, and he loved to see her gazing at him with a smile. Once he had become so absorbed in looking at her that his hand slipped and he sliced off a small piece of his finger. The whole street had made a joke of it. To avoid further gossip, she had kept out of his way ever since. But the shops were neighbors, so they could not help seeing each other daily, and his employer thought highly of him. It was quite possible that he had asked for her. The second person she thought of was the son of the incense-and-candle shop owner opposite. He was handsome, and their families were well matched, but he could never remove his cap: his scalp was diseased and patchy. The thought repelled her, and she hurriedly cast him from her mind.

这一想就想到阿祥了；顿时面红心跳。要问问不出口，好在有她母亲，『是哪个？』她问她丈夫。『还有哪个，自然是阿祥！』『祥』字刚刚出口，阿巧便霍地起身，躲了进去；脚步轻盈无比。魏老板楞了一会，哈哈大笑。『笑啥？快说！阿祥怎么会托张胖子来做媒？他怎么说？你怎么答复他？从头讲给我们听。』这一讲，连『听壁脚』的阿巧在内，无不心满意足；喜极欲涕，心里都有句话∶『阿祥命中有贵人；遇见胡道台这样的东家！』然而胡道台此时却还管不到阿祥的事；正为另一个阿巧在伤脑筋。

Then she thought of A’xiang. Her face flushed and her heart beat wildly. She could not bring herself to ask, but fortunately her mother could. “Who is it?” she asked her husband. “Who else? A’xiang, naturally.” The name had scarcely left his lips when A’qiao sprang up and fled into the back, her feet light as air. Wei stared for a moment, then burst into laughter. “What are you laughing at? Tell us! How did A’xiang come to ask Fatty Zhang to act as matchmaker? What did he say, and what did you answer? Begin at the beginning.” As the story unfolded, everyone—including A’qiao, listening from the other side of the wall—was entirely satisfied, so happy that tears seemed near. All shared the same thought: A’xiang had found a benefactor in an employer like Circuit Intendant Hu. Hu himself, however, could not attend to A’xiang’s affairs just then. Another A’qiao was giving him a headache.

阿巧姐昨夜通宵不归；一直到这天早晨九点钟才回家。问起她的行踪，她说心中气闷，昨天在一个小姊妹家谈了一夜。她的『小姊妹』也都三十开外了，不是从良；便是做了本家——老鸨。如是从了良的『人家人』，不会容留她只身一个人过夜；一定在头天夜里就派人送了她回来。这样看来，行踪就很有疑问了。于是胡雪岩不动声色地派阿祥去打听。阿巧姐昨天出门虽不坐家里轿子，但料想她也不会步行；所以阿祥承命去向弄堂口待雇的轿夫去探问。果然问到了；阿巧姐昨天是去了宝善街北的兆荣里，那轿夫还记得她是在倒数第二家，一座石库门前下的轿。所谓『有里兆荣并兆富，近接公兴，都是平康路』，那一带的兆荣里、兆富里、公兴里是有名的纸醉金迷之地；阿巧姐摒绝从人，私访平康，其意何居？着实可疑。

Sister A’qiao had been out all night and had not returned until nine that morning. Asked where she had been, she said she had felt depressed and spent the night talking at the home of an old friend. These “young companions” of hers were all over thirty now. They had either left the profession to marry or become proprietresses—in other words, madams. If she had visited a woman who had married into a respectable household, the family would hardly have kept an unaccompanied visitor overnight; someone would have escorted her home the evening before. Her movements therefore looked suspicious. Without betraying his thoughts, Hu sent A’xiang to investigate. She had not used the household sedan chair, but he did not imagine she had walked. A’xiang accordingly questioned the bearers waiting for hire at the mouth of the lane. He found what he wanted. She had gone to Zhaorong Lane, north of Baoshan Street. The bearer remembered setting her down at a stone-framed doorway, the second house from the far end. As the saying went, “Zhaorong and Zhaofu, with Gongxing close beside—all are roads to the pleasure quarters.” Those lanes were famous haunts of luxurious dissipation. Why had A’qiao dismissed her attendants and visited them secretly? It was deeply suspicious.

要破这个疑团，除却七姑奶奶更无别人。胡雪岩算了一下，这天正是她代为布置新居，约定去看的第四天；因而坐轿不到古家，直往画锦里而去。果然，屋子已粉刷得焕然一新；七姑奶奶正亲自指挥下人，在安放簇新的红木家具。三月底的天气，艳阳满院，相当燠热，七姑奶奶一张脸如中了酒似的，而且额上见汗，头发起毛，足见劳累。胡雪岩大不过意，兜头一揖，深深致谢；七姑奶奶答得漂亮∶『小爷叔用不着谢我，老太太，婶娘要来了；我们做小辈的，该当尽点孝心。』说着，她便带领胡雪岩一间屋子、一间屋子去看；不但上房布置得井井有条，连下房也不疏忽，应有尽有。费心如此，作主人的除了没口夸赞以外，再不能置一词。一个圈子兜下来，回到客厅喝茶休息，这时候胡雪岩方始开口，细诉阿巧姐一夜的芳踪；向七姑奶奶讨主意。

Only the Seventh Lady could solve the mystery. Hu counted the days: this was the fourth day since she had undertaken to furnish his new home, the day agreed upon for him to inspect it. He therefore took his chair directly to Huajin Lane instead of Gu’s house. Sure enough, the rooms had been repainted and looked entirely new. The Seventh Lady herself was directing the servants as they arranged brand-new rosewood furniture. It was late in the third month; brilliant sunshine filled the courtyard, and the weather was quite warm. Her face was flushed as if she had been drinking, her forehead beaded with sweat, and her hair coming loose. Plainly she had worked hard. Hu felt deeply indebted and made a profound bow of thanks. Her answer was graceful. “There is no need to thank me, Little Uncle. Your honored mother and my aunt—your wife—are coming. We of the younger generation ought to show our filial respect.” She led him through the house, room by room. Not only were the family apartments arranged in perfect order; even the servants’ quarters had been supplied with everything necessary. Such care left the owner nothing to do but praise it without reservation. After making the circuit, they returned to the drawing room to rest over tea. Only then did Hu explain A’qiao’s movements the previous night and ask for advice.

事出突兀，她一时哪里有主意？将胡雪岩所说的话，前前后后细想了一遍，觉得有几件事先要弄清楚。『小爷叔，』她问∶『阿巧姐回来以后，对你是啥样子？有没有发牢骚？』『没有，样子很冷淡。』『有没有啥收拾细软衣服，仿佛要搬出去的样子？』『也没有。』胡雪岩答说，『坐在那里剥指甲想心事，好象根本没有看到我在那里似的。』就问这两句话便够了。七姑奶奶慢慢点着头，自言自语似地说∶『这就对了！她一定是那么个主意！』由于刚才一问一答印证了回忆，胡雪岩亦已有所意会；然而他宁愿自己猜得不对，『七姐，』他很痛苦地问∶『莫非她跟她小姊妹商量好了，还要抛头露面，自己去「铺房间」？』『贱货！』脱口骂了一句。

The matter was so unexpected that the Seventh Lady had no ready answer. She considered his account from beginning to end and decided that a few points must first be clarified. “Little Uncle, how did she behave toward you when she came back? Did she complain?” “No. She was very cold.” “Did she pack any clothes or valuables, as though she meant to move out?” “No. She just sat there picking at her nails and thinking, as if I were not in the room.” Those two questions were enough. The Seventh Lady nodded slowly, almost talking to herself. “That fits. That must be her plan.” The exchange had revived Hu’s own recollections, and he too had begun to understand, though he would rather have been wrong. “Seventh Sister,” he asked painfully, “can she have arranged with her old companions to appear in public again—to open a courtesan establishment of her own?” A curse escaped him. “The shameless woman!”

『小爷叔！这，我要替阿巧姐不服。』七姑奶奶的本性露出来了，义形于色地说∶『一个人总要寻个归宿。她宁愿做低服小，只为觉得自己出身不是良家，一向自由惯了的，受不得大宅门的拘束，要在外头住；说起来也不算过分。这一层既然办不到，只有另觅出路；哪里来的还到哪里去，不也是顺理成间的事？就算是从良，总亦不能喊个媒波来说∶』我要嫁人了，你替我寻个老公来！「她」铺房间「自己不下水；遇见个知心合意的，自订终身，倒是正办。『听她一顿排揎，胡雪岩反倒心平气和了，笑笑说道∶』其实她要这样子做，倒应该先跟七姐来商量。『』跟我没商量！我心里不反对她这样子做；口里没有赞成她再落火炕的道理。阿巧姐是聪明人，怎么会露口风？我现在倒担心一件事；怕她心里恨你，将来会有意塌你的台。『』怎么塌法？『胡雪岩苦笑着，』只要她再落水，我的台就让她坍足了。『

“Little Uncle, there I must take her side.” The Seventh Lady’s nature asserted itself; righteous indignation showed in her face. “A person must find some settled place in life. She is willing to accept a subordinate position, but she feels that, having come from outside respectable society and been accustomed to freedom, she could not bear the restrictions of a great household. She wants to live separately. That is not an excessive request. If it cannot be granted, she must find another way. Is it not natural to return to the place she came from? Even if she wishes to marry, she can hardly summon a matchmaker and say, ‘I want a husband; find me one.’ She could open an establishment without taking clients herself. If she meets a suitable man who understands her, they can decide their own future. That would be a proper course.” After this scolding, Hu grew calmer. He smiled. “If that is what she means to do, she ought to have consulted you first.” “She has not consulted me. In my heart I might not oppose it, but I could hardly say aloud that I approved of her returning to that pit of fire. She is a clever woman. Why should she give me a hint? What worries me now is that she may resent you and deliberately damage your reputation afterward.” “How?” Hu smiled bitterly. “If she goes back into that life at all, my reputation will be damaged enough.”

『那还不算坍足。明天她挂上一块「杭州胡寓」的牌子，那才好看呢！』一句话说得胡雪岩发楞。他也听人说过，这一两年夷场『花市』，繁盛异常，堂子里兴起一种专宰冤大头的花样，找个初涉花丛，目炫于珠围翠绕；鼻醉于粉腻脂香；耳溺于嗷嘈弦管的土财主，筵前衾底，做足了宛转绸缪的柔态痴情；到两情浓时，论及嫁娶，总说孤苦伶仃一个人，早已厌倦风生，只为『身背浪向』有几多债务，只要替她完了债，她就是他家的人，除此别无要求。

“Not as badly as it could be. Suppose tomorrow she puts up a sign saying ‘The Hu Residence of Hangzhou.’ Then you will have something to look at!” Hu was stunned. He too had heard how, during the past year or two, the flourishing pleasure trade in the foreign settlement had developed a new device for fleecing wealthy fools. A provincial rich man, newly ventured among the flowers, would find his eyes dazzled by jewels and beauty, his nose intoxicated by powder and perfume, and his ears drowned in music. At the banquet and beneath the quilts, the woman would lavish tender, clinging affection upon him. When their feelings had ripened and marriage was mentioned, she would explain that she was lonely and without family, had long been weary of her life, and was held back only by debts incurred in the profession. If he paid those debts, she would belong to his household. She asked for nothing else.

于是冤大头替她还债『卸牌子』，自此从良。到一做了良家妇女，渐渐不安于室；百般需索，贪壑难填，稍不如意，就会变脸，三天一小吵，五天一大吵，吵得这家人家的上上下下，六神不安。冤大头这才知道上了恶当；然而悔之晚矣！少不得再花一笔钱，才能请她走路。这个花样名为『氵忽浴』。如果洗清了一身债务，下堂求去，两不相干，还算是有良心的；有些积年妖狐，心狠手辣，嫁而复出，还放不过冤大头，顶着他的姓接纳生张熟魏；甚至当筵诉说她的嫁后光阴如何如何？或者这家人家的阴私家丑，少不得又要花钱，才能无事。

The gullible patron would pay, have her signboard taken down, and bring her out of the profession. Once installed as a respectable wife, however, she would gradually become restless at home, making endless demands. Nothing could fill the gulf of her greed. Any slight disappointment brought a change of face; minor quarrels every three days and major ones every five left the entire household distraught. Only then would the victim realize how thoroughly he had been duped. But regret came too late. He would have to pay another sum to be rid of her. The trick was known as “taking a cleansing bath.” If, having washed away her debts, she left his household and broke off all dealings, she still had some conscience. Other hardened foxes were more ruthless. Returning to the profession after marriage, they refused to release their victim. They received new and familiar patrons alike under his surname, even recounting over dinner what married life in his house had been like, or exposing its private secrets and scandals. More money would then be needed to purchase peace.

不过，阿巧姐总不致于如此绝情。胡雪岩问道∶『她这样子做，于她有什么好处？她是理路极清楚的人；为啥要做这种损人不利己的事？』『小爷叔这句话说得很实在；阿巧姐应该不是这种人。事情到了这步田地，反倒好办了。小爷叔，你交给我，包你妥当。』七姑奶奶接着又说∶『小爷叔，你这两天不要回去！住在我这里，还是住在钱庄里；随你的便，就是不要跟阿巧姐见面。『胡雪岩实在猜不透她葫芦里卖的什么药？料知问亦无用；为今之计，只有丢开不管，听凭她去料理了。于是他说∶『我住在钱庄里好了。我请了张胖子做档手；趁这两天功夫陪他在店里谈谈以后的生意。』『张胖子为人倒靠得住的。就这样好了！你去忙你的生意；有事我会到阜康来接头。』

Surely A’qiao would not be so heartless. “What good would it do her?” Hu asked. “She is an exceptionally reasonable woman. Why would she harm another person without benefiting herself?” “That is a very fair point. She ought not to be that sort of person. Now that matters have reached this stage, they are easier to settle. Leave it to me, Little Uncle. I guarantee that I shall arrange everything properly. And do not go back for the next couple of days. Stay at my house or at the bank, whichever you please. Only do not see A’qiao.” Hu could not fathom her plan. He knew that questions would be useless; the only course was to put the matter aside and let her manage it. “I shall stay at the bank. I have engaged Fatty Zhang as a senior clerk. These two days will give me time to sit with him at the shop and discuss future business.” “Zhang is a dependable man. Very well. Attend to your business. If anything arises, I shall find you at Fukang.”

当天下午，七姑奶奶就去看一个人；是尤五的旧相知怡情老二。当年因为松江漕帮正在倒霉的时候，弟兄们生计艰难；身为一帮当家的尤五，岂可金屋藏娇？因而尽管怡情老二说之再三，尤五始终不肯为她『卸牌子』；怡情老二一气之下，择人而事，嫁的是个破落的世家子弟，体弱多病，不到两年呜呼哀哉。怡情老二没有替他守节的必要；事实上也不容于大妇，因而重张艳帜。先是做『先生』；后来做『本家』，跟尤五藕继丝连，至今不绝。

That afternoon the Seventh Lady went to see an old intimate of Brother You’s, the Second Miss of Yiqing House. Years earlier, when the Songjiang Grain-Boat Guild had fallen on hard times and its members could scarcely make a living, how could its leader establish a gilded nest for a favorite? However often the Second Miss urged him, Brother You had refused to redeem her from the profession. Angry, she had chosen another man, a sickly son of an impoverished distinguished family. He died within two years. She felt no obligation to remain a faithful widow; indeed, his principal wife would not tolerate her. She therefore returned to the pleasure quarters, first as a courtesan and later as a proprietress. Her ties with Brother You had never quite been severed and continued to this day.

阿巧姐原是怡情老二房间里的人；七姑奶奶去看怡情老二，一则是要打听打听阿巧姐预备复出，到底是怎么回事？再则也是要利用她跟阿巧姐旧日的情分，从中斡旋。不过自己一个良家妇女，为了古应春的声名，不便踏入妓家；特意到相熟的一家番菜馆落脚，托西崽去请怡情老二来相会。两个人有大半年不曾见面了。由于彼此的感情，一向很好，所以执手殷勤，叙不尽的寒温。怡情老二问讯了七姑奶奶全家，与尤五以外，也问起胡雪岩；这恰好给了她一个诉说的机会。『我今天就是为我们这位小爷叔的事，要跟你商量。』七姑奶奶说∶『阿巧姐跟胡老爷要分手了。』『为啥？』怡情老二讶然相问∶『为啥合不来？』『其实也没有啥合不来——。』七姑奶奶将家眷属脱困，将到上海；谈到阿巧姐的本心。语气中一直强调，脱辐已成定局，姻缘无可挽救。怡情老二凝神听完，面现困惑，『阿巧姐跟我，一两个月总要见一次面；这样的大事，她怎么不来跟我谈？』她问∶『她跟胡老爷分手以后怎么办？苏州又回不去；而且乡下她也住不惯的。』『是啊！』七姑奶奶接口说道∶『不管她怎么样，我们大家的情分总在的；就是胡老爷也很关心她。一个女流之辈，孤零零地，总要有个妥当的安顿之处才好。她自己好象打定了主意；不过，这个主意照我看不大高明。二阿姐，你晓不晓得她在兆富里有没有要好的小姊妹？』

A’qiao had once worked in the Second Miss’s establishment. The Seventh Lady sought her out partly to learn the truth about A’qiao’s supposed return to the trade and partly to enlist her old friendship as a means of mediation. But as a respectable married woman, she could not enter a courtesan house without compromising Gu’s reputation. She went instead to a familiar Western restaurant and asked a waiter to invite the Second Miss to meet her there. They had not seen each other for more than half a year. Their affection had always been strong, and they clasped hands warmly, with endless inquiries and greetings. After asking about the Seventh Lady’s family and Brother You, the Second Miss also asked after Hu, giving her visitor just the opening she needed. “It is on Little Uncle’s account that I want to consult you today. A’qiao and Mr Hu are going to part.” “Why?” the Second Miss asked in surprise. “Why can they not get along?” “There is really no quarrel between them—” The Seventh Lady explained that Hu’s family had escaped and was coming to Shanghai, then described A’qiao’s wishes. Throughout, she emphasized that the separation was settled and the relationship could not be saved. The Second Miss listened attentively, looking puzzled. “A’qiao and I meet every month or two. Why has she not discussed something so important with me? What will she do after leaving Mr Hu? She cannot return to Suzhou, and she is not used to country life.” “Exactly,” the Seventh Lady agreed. “Whatever she does, our friendship remains. Even Mr Hu is concerned for her. A woman alone must have somewhere suitable to settle. She seems to have made up her mind, but I do not think her plan is wise. Second Sister, do you know whether she has any close friends in Zhaofu Lane?”
