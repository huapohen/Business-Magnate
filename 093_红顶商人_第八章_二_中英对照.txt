胡雪岩｜中英对照版

红顶商人｜The Red-Topped Merchant

第八章（本章第2部分，共9部分）

阅读说明：每段中文后接对应英文；两版英文正文一致。下载站水印已清除，原始疑字在文末说明。

小张怎么不知道？阿狗嫂是有名的一个老鸨；主持一家极大的『私门头』，凡是富春江上『江山船』中投怀送抱的船娘，一上了岸都以阿狗嫂为居停。小张跟她，亦很相熟；只是杭州被围，花事阑珊，乱后却还不曾见过。因而小张又惊又喜地问∶『阿狗嫂倒不曾饿杀！』『她那里又热闹了。不过我住在她后面，很清静。』『好！明天下午我一定来。』

How could Zhang fail to know her? Madam A’gou was a celebrated procuress who ran a very large private brothel. The boatwomen who offered their favours aboard the Jiangshan boats on the Fuchun River all lodged with her when they came ashore. Zhang knew her well. But the siege of Hangzhou had ruined the pleasure trade, and he had not seen her since the upheaval. “So Madam A’gou did not starve to death!” he exclaimed, surprised and delighted. “Her place is lively again. But I am staying in the rear, where it is very quiet.” “Good! I shall certainly come tomorrow afternoon.”

刘不才的住处是阿狗嫂特地替他预备的，就在后面，单成院落，有一道腰门，闩上门便与前面隔绝；另有出入的门户。『』张兄，『刘不才改了称呼，』阜康的票子你要不要？『』喔，我倒忘记了。『小张从身上掏出一个棉纸小包，递了过去，』东西在这里，你看一看！『『不必看。』刘不才交了五十两一张庄票；银货两讫以后，拉开橱门说道∶『张兄，我有几样小意思送你。我们交个朋友。』那些『小意思』长短大小不一，长的是一枝『司的克』；小的是一个金表；大的是一副吕宋烟；还有短不及五寸，方楞折角的一包东西，就看不出来了——样子象书；小张却不相信他会送自己一部书。而且给好赌的人送书，也嫌『触霉头』。

Madam A’gou had prepared the rooms specially for Liu. They occupied a separate courtyard at the back, with a dividing gate that could be bolted to shut them off from the front and an entrance of their own. When Zhang arrived, Liu changed his form of address. “Brother Zhang, will you accept Fukang notes?” “Oh, I nearly forgot.” Zhang took a small tissue-paper parcel from his pocket and handed it over. “Here is your property. Have a look.” “No need.” Liu gave him a fifty-tael banknote. With the transaction settled, he opened a cupboard. “Brother Zhang, I have a few little things for you. Let us be friends.” The little things varied in size and shape: a long walking stick, a small gold watch, a large box of Manila cigars, and an angular rectangular parcel less than five cun long that was harder to identify. It looked like books, but Zhang could not believe that Liu would give him books. Besides, to give books to a gambler was to invite bad luck—the word sounded like “lose.”

『你看这枝「司的克」，防身的好东西。』刘不才举起来喝一声∶『当心！』接着便当头砸了下来。小张当然拿手一格，捏住了尾端。也不知刘不才怎么一下，那根『司的克』分成两截，握在刘不才手里的，是一枝雪亮的短剑。『怎么搞的？』小张大感兴趣，『我看看，我看看。』看那短剑，形制与中国的剑完全不同；三角形；尖端如针；剑身三面血槽，确是可以致人于死的利器。『你看，这中间有机关。』原来司的克中间有榫头，做得严丝合缝，极其精细；遇到有人袭击，拿司的克砸过去，对方不抓不过挨一下打；若是想夺它就上当了，正好借势一扭，抽出短剑刺过去，突出不意，必定得手。了解了妙用，小张越发喜爱；防身固然得力；无事拿来献献宝，夸耀于人，更是一乐。

“Look at this stick. An excellent thing for protecting yourself.” Liu raised it. “Watch out!” Then he brought it down towards Zhang’s head. Zhang naturally threw up a hand to block it and caught the far end. With some movement of Liu’s, the stick parted in two. What remained in his hand was a gleaming short sword. “How does it work?” Zhang asked eagerly. “Let me see!” The blade was quite unlike a Chinese sword: triangular, with a needle-sharp point and a groove along each of its three faces. It was unquestionably a deadly weapon. “There is a mechanism in the middle,” Liu explained. The join in the stick fitted with remarkable precision. If someone attacked, its owner could strike with it. An opponent who did not catch it would merely suffer a blow; one who tried to seize it would fall into the trap. A twist, using the other man’s grip, released the short sword for an unexpected thrust. Once Zhang understood its ingenuity, he liked it all the more. It would protect him, certainly; better still, he could display it and boast of it to others when nothing threatened him.

所以笑得嘴都合不拢了。『这里是几本洋书。』果然是书！这就送得不对路了，小张拱拱手说∶『老刘！好朋友说实话∶中国书我都不大看得懂；洋书更加「赵大人看榜」，莫名其妙。』『你看得懂的。』刘不才将交到他手里，『带回去一个人慢慢看。』这句话中，奥妙无穷，小张就非当时拆开来看不可了。打开来一翻，顿觉血脉贲张——是一部『洋春宫』。这一下就目不旁观了。刘不才悄悄端了张椅子扶他坐下；自己远远坐在一边，冷眼旁观，看他眼珠凸出，不断咽口水的穷形极相，心里越发泰然。好不容易，小张才看完，『过瘾！』他略带些窘地笑道∶『老刘，你哪里觅来的？』

He was still grinning with delight when Liu said, “Here are some foreign books.” So they were books after all! That gift was wide of the mark. Zhang clasped his hands. “Old Liu, between friends, let me be frank. I can barely understand Chinese books. Foreign ones would leave me completely at sea.” “You will understand these.” Liu put them in his hands. “Take them home and look through them slowly, by yourself.” The suggestion was too intriguing: Zhang had to open the parcel immediately. A glance made his blood race. It was a foreign erotic picture book. Nothing else could hold his attention now. Liu quietly brought a chair and helped him sit, then took a seat some distance away. He watched coolly as Zhang stared and swallowed, every expression betraying his absorption. Liu’s confidence grew. At last Zhang finished. “What a treat!” he said with a somewhat embarrassed smile. “Old Liu, where did you find this?”

『自然是上海夷场上。』『去过上海的也很多，从没有看着他们带过这些东西回来。』小张不胜钦服地说，『老刘，你真有办法！』『我也没办法。这些东西，我也不知道哪里去觅？是一个亲戚那里顺手牵来的。这话回头再说；你先看看这两样东西。』这就是一大一小两个盒子；小张倒都仔细看了。一面看，一面想，凭空受人家这份礼，实在不好意思；不受呢，那支司的克和那部『洋书』真有些舍不得放手。想了半天，委决不下，只有说老实话∶『老刘，我们初交，你这样够朋友，我也不晓得怎么说才好？不过，我真的不大好意思。』『这你就见外了。老弟台，朋友不是交一天；要这样分彼此，以后我就不敢高攀了。』『我不分，我不分。』小张极力辩白，不过，『你总也要让我尽点心意才好。』

“In the foreign settlement at Shanghai, of course.” “Plenty of people have been to Shanghai, but I have never seen them bring back anything like these.” Zhang was full of admiration. “Old Liu, you certainly know how to manage!” “Not at all. I would not know where to find them myself. I picked them up from a relative. But we can discuss that later. Look at these other two things first.” These were the large box and the small one. Zhang examined both carefully. As he did so, he felt ashamed to receive such presents for nothing. Yet he could not bear to give up the stick and the foreign books. After prolonged indecision, he could only speak honestly. “Old Liu, we have only just met. You are so generous that I do not know what to say. But I really feel rather embarrassed.” “Then you are treating me as a stranger, my dear fellow. Friendship is not for a single day. If you insist on drawing such distinctions between us, I shall scarcely dare presume upon your acquaintance again.” “I make no distinctions!” Zhang protested earnestly. “Only you must let me do something in return.”

看样子是收服了，那就不必多费功夫，打铁趁热，『我也说老实话，这些东西，不是我的；是我一个亲威托我带来的。』他接着又说∶『你家老太爷，对我这个亲戚有点误会；不但误会，简直有点冤枉。』『喔，』小张问道∶「令亲是哪一个？『『阜康钱庄的胡雪岩。』小张失声说道∶『是他啊！』『是他。怎么说你家老太爷对他的误会是冤枉的呢？话不说不明，我倒晓得一点。』小张很注意地在等他说下去，而刘不才却迟疑着不大愿意开口的样子；这就令人奇怪了，『老刘！』小张问道∶『你不是说晓得其中的内情吗？』

He appeared won over. There was no need to labour the point; Liu would strike while the iron was hot. “I shall be frank too. These things do not belong to me. A relative asked me to bring them.” He added, “Your father has misunderstood this relative of mine. More than misunderstood him—he has really done him an injustice.” “Who is your relative?” “Hu Xueyan of Fukang Bank.” “Him!” Zhang exclaimed. “Yes. Why do I say your father has wronged him through this misunderstanding? Such things must be explained. I happen to know something about it.” Zhang waited attentively, but Liu seemed reluctant to proceed. This was puzzling. “Old Liu, did you not say that you knew the inside story?”

『是的，我完全晓得。王抚台由湖州府调杭州的时候，我是从湖州跟了他来的，在他衙门里办庶务，所以十分清楚。不过，这件事谈起来若论是非；你家老太爷也是我长辈。我不便说他。』『那有什么关系？自己人讲讲不要紧。我们家「老的」，名气大得很，不晓得多少人说过他，我也听得多了，又何在乎你批评他？』『我倒不是批评他老人家，是怪他太大意，太心急了。「新官上任三把火」，该当避他一避；偏偏「吃盐水」让他撞见。告示就贴在那里浆糊都还没有干，就有人拿他的话不当话，好比一巴掌打在他脸上——人家到底是杭州一府之首，管着好几县上百万的老百生；这一来他那个印把子怎么捏得牢？老弟，「前半夜想想人家。后半夜想想自己。」换了你是王抚台，要不要光火？』小张默然。倒不仅因为刘不才的话说得透彻；主要的还是因为有交情在那里，就什么话都容易听得进去了。『不错，雪岩当时没有能保得住你家老太爷的秀才。不过，外头只知其一，不知其二；王抚台动公事给学里老师，革掉了秀才还要办人出气。这个上头，雪岩一定不答应，先软后硬，王抚台才算勉强卖了个面子。』『喔，』小张乱眨着眼说∶『这我倒不晓。怎么叫「先软后硬？」』

“Yes, I know it thoroughly. When Governor Wang was transferred from Huzhou to Hangzhou, I came with him and managed miscellaneous affairs in his office. I know exactly what happened. But if we discuss the rights and wrongs, your father is my elder too. I ought not to criticize him.” “What does that matter? We are among friends. The old man at home is famous enough. Heaven knows how many people have criticized him. I have heard plenty; why should I mind hearing you?” “It is not criticism, really. I only think he was too careless and impatient. A newly appointed official is eager to make his authority felt; he ought to have kept out of the way for a while. Instead, he was caught collecting ‘salt-water’ fees. The proclamation was still posted there—the paste was scarcely dry—yet someone was already treating the prefect’s words as nothing. It was like a slap in the face. After all, he was the head of Hangzhou prefecture, responsible for several counties and more than a million people. How could he hold his authority securely after that? My dear fellow, spend the first half of the night considering the other man, and the second half considering yourself. If you had been Governor Wang, would you not have been furious?” Zhang said nothing. Liu’s reasoning was clear enough, but it was their new friendship that principally disposed him to listen. “True, Xueyan failed to save your father’s degree. But outsiders know only half the story. Governor Wang not only wrote to the educational authorities to have it revoked; he intended to punish your father afterwards to satisfy his anger. Xueyan would not consent to that. He tried entreaty first and then defiance, until the governor reluctantly yielded.” “Oh?” Zhang blinked repeatedly. “I did not know that. What do you mean by entreaty and defiance?”

『软是下跪，硬是吵架。雪岩为了你家老太爷，要跟王抚台绝交；以后倒反说他不够朋友不帮忙，你说冤枉不冤枉？』『照你这么说，倒真的是冤枉了他？』小张紧接着说∶『那末，他又为啥要送我这些东西。好人好到这样子，也就出奇了。』『一点不奇。他自然有事拜托你。』『可以！』小张慨然答道∶『胡老板我不熟，不过你够朋友。只要我做得到，你说了我一定帮助。』『说起来，不是我捧自己亲戚，胡雪岩实在是够朋友的；你家老太爷对他虽有误会，他倒替你家老太爷伸好后脚，留好余地在那里了。』这两句话没头没脑，小张不明所以；但话是好话，却总听得出来，『这倒是谢谢他了。』他问，『不知道伸好一只什么后脚？』『我先给你看样东西。』

“Entreaty meant kneeling; defiance meant a quarrel. Xueyan was ready to break with Governor Wang for your father’s sake. Afterwards they said he had failed as a friend and would not help. Was that not unjust?” “As you explain it, he certainly seems to have been wronged. But why send me these things? It would be extraordinary to be so kind.” “Nothing extraordinary. Naturally, he has a favour to ask.” “Then ask!” Zhang replied generously. “I do not know Mr Hu well, but you have treated me as a friend. If it is something I can do, I shall certainly help.” “I am not praising my own relative without cause. Hu Xueyan really does stand by his friends. Although your father has misunderstood him, he has already made provision for your father’s future and left him room to retreat.” This was too obscure for Zhang to follow, though he could tell it meant something favourable. “Then I must thank him. What sort of provision?” “Let me show you something.”

刘不才从床底下拖出皮箱来，开了锁，取出一本『护书』，抽了一通公文，送到小张手里。小张肚子里的墨水有限，不过江苏巡抚部堂的紫泥大印，是看得懂的；他父亲的名字也是认识的，此外由于公文套子转来转去，一时就弄不明白是说些什么了。『这件公事，千万不能说出去。一说出去，让长毛知道了不得了。』刘不才故作郑重地嘱咐；然后换了副轻快的神情说∶「你带回去，请老太爷密密收藏；有一天官军克复杭州，拿出公文来看，不但没有助逆反叛之罪，还有维持地方之功。你说，胡雪岩帮你家老太爷这个忙，帮得大不大。『这一说，小张方始有点明白；不解的是∶』那末眼前呢？眼前做点啥？『

Liu dragged a leather case from beneath the bed, unlocked it, took out a document folder, and handed Zhang an official communication. Zhang’s learning was limited. He could recognize the great purple seal of the governor of Jiangsu and his father’s name, but the elaborate official phrasing left him unable to make out the meaning. “You must never speak of this dispatch outside. If word reaches the rebels, the consequences will be terrible.” Liu assumed an air of solemn warning, then lightened his manner. “Take it home and ask your father to keep it secretly. When the government troops recover Hangzhou, he can produce it. Far from being guilty of aiding rebellion, he will be credited with preserving order. Tell me, has Hu Xueyan done your father a great service or not?” Zhang began to understand. One point still puzzled him. “What about now? What are we to do now?”

『眼前，当然该做啥就做啥。不是维持地方吗，照常维持好了。』『喔，喔！』小张终于恍然大悟，『这就是脚踏两头船。』『对！脚踏两头船。不过，现在所踏的这只船，早晚要翻身的；还是那只船要紧。』『我懂。我懂。』『你们老太爷呢？』『我去跟他说，他一定很高兴。』小张答说∶『明天就有回话。时候不早，我也要去了。』第二天一早，小张上门，邀刘不才到家。张秀才早就煮酒在等了。

“For the present, do whatever needs doing. Are you not preserving order? Continue as before.” “Ah!” Zhang finally saw. “A foot in each boat.” “Exactly. But the boat you are standing in now will capsize sooner or later. The other boat matters more.” “I understand.” “And your father?” “I shall tell him. He will be delighted. You shall have an answer tomorrow. It is getting late; I must go.” Early the next morning, Zhang came to invite Liu to the family home. The licentiate was already waiting with wine warming.

为了套交情，刘不才不但口称『老伯』；而且行了大礼，将张秀才喜得有些受宠若惊的模样。『不敢当，不敢当！刘三哥，』他指着小张说，『我这个畜生从来不交正经朋友；想不到交上了你刘三哥。真正我家门之幸。』『老伯说得我不曾吃酒，脸就要红了。』『对了，吃酒，吃酒！朋友交情，吃酒越吃越厚，赌钱越赌越薄。』他又骂儿子，『这个畜生，就是喜欢赌；我到赌场里去，十次倒有九次遇见他。』『你也不要说人家。』小张反唇相讥，『你去十次，九次遇见我；总还比你少一次！』『你看看，你看看！』张秀才气得两撇黄胡子乱动，『这个畜生说的话，强词夺理。』

To cultivate their intimacy, Liu addressed him as “Uncle” and performed a full ceremonial bow. Zhang was almost overwhelmed by the honour. “You are too kind, much too kind! Brother Liu,” he said, pointing to his son, “this wretch never makes respectable friends. Who would have thought he would meet a man like you? A blessing for our family indeed!” “Uncle, you will make my face red before I have even drunk any wine.” “Quite right! Let us drink. Drinking makes friendship stronger; gambling makes it weaker.” He scolded his son again. “This wretch loves gambling. Nine times out of ten, when I go to a gambling house, I find him there.” “You need not complain about others,” the son retorted. “If you go ten times and find me there nine, I still go once less than you!” “Listen to him!” Zhang’s two wisps of yellow moustache quivered with anger. “The wretch twists every argument!”

刘不才看他们父不父，子不子，实在好笑∶『老伯膝下，大概就是我这位老弟一个。』他说，『从小宠惯了！』「『都是他娘宠的。家门不幸，叫你刘三哥见笑。』『说哪里话！我倒看我这位老弟，着实能干、漂亮。绝好的外场人物。『一句话说到张秀才得意的地方，敛容答道∶』刘三哥，玉不琢，不成器；我这个畜生，鬼聪明是有的，不过要好好跟人去靡炼。回头我们细谈，先吃酒。『于是宾主三人，围炉小炊；少不得先有些不着边际的闲话。

The sight of a father and son each so far from the proper dignity of his role amused Liu greatly. “I suppose my young friend is your only son,” he said. “Spoiled from childhood!” “His mother spoiled him. A misfortune for the family, Brother Liu. We make ourselves ridiculous before you.” “Not at all. I find him most capable and personable—admirably suited to dealing with the world.” This touched the father’s pride. Composing his face, he replied, “Brother Liu, jade must be polished before it becomes useful. The wretch has a sort of cleverness, but he needs to follow someone and gain experience. We can discuss that later. First, let us drink.” The three sat around the brazier, eating and drinking, with the usual preliminary talk of inconsequential matters.

谈到差不多，张秀才向他儿子呶一呶嘴；小张便起身出堂屋，四面看了一下，大声吩咐他家的男仆∶『贵生，你去告诉门上；老爷今天身子不舒服，不见客。问到我，说不在家。如果有公事，下午到局子里去说。『这便是摒绝闲杂，倾心谈秘密的先声，刘不才心里就有了预备，只待张秀才发话。『刘三哥，你跟雪岩至亲？』话是泛泛之词，称呼却颇具意味；不叫『胡道台』而直呼其号，这就是表示∶一则很熟；二则平起平坐的朋友。刘不才再往深入细想一想，是张秀才仿佛在暗示∶他不念前嫌，有紧要话，尽说不妨。如果自己猜得不错，那就是好征兆；不过知人知面不知心，又想起胡雪岩的叮嘱∶『逢人只说三分话』，所以很谨慎地答道∶『是的，我们是亲戚？』

When enough had been said, the father signalled with his mouth. The son rose, went out of the hall, looked around, and called loudly to a servant. “Guisheng, tell the gatekeeper that the master is unwell today and will receive no visitors. If anyone asks for me, say I am out. Official business can be brought to the bureau this afternoon.” This exclusion of outsiders announced that a confidential discussion was about to begin. Liu prepared himself and waited. “Brother Liu, are you a close relative of Xueyan?” The question itself was ordinary, but the form of address was significant. Instead of calling him Intendant Hu, Zhang used his personal name. This suggested both familiarity and equality. Thinking further, Liu felt that Zhang might be implying that old resentments were forgotten and important matters could now be discussed freely. If that reading was correct, it was a good sign. Yet one could know a man’s face without knowing his heart. Remembering Hu’s warning to reveal only a third of what he knew, Liu answered cautiously. “Yes, we are related.”

『怎么称呼？』『雪岩算是比我晚一辈。』『啊呀呀，你是雪岩的长亲，我该称你老世叔才是。』张秀才说，『你又跟小儿叙朋友，这样算起来，辈分排不清楚了。刘三哥。我们大家平叙最好！』『不敢！不敢！我叫张大爷吧。』刘不才不愿在礼节上头，多费功夫，急转直下地说∶『雪岩也跟我提过，说有张大爷这么一位患难之交；嘱咐我这趟回杭州，一定要来看看张大爷，替他说声好。』『说患难之交，倒是一点不错。当初雪岩不曾得发的时候，我们在茶店里是每天见面的。后来他有跟王抚台这番遇合，平步青云，眼孔就高了。一班穷朋友不大在他眼里；我们也高攀不上。患难之交，变成了「点头朋友」。』这是一番牢骚，刘不才静静听他发完，自然要作解释∶『雪岩后来忙了，礼节疏漏的地方难免；不过说到待朋友，我不是回护亲戚，雪岩无论如何「不伤道」这三个字，总还做到了的。』『是啊！他外场是漂亮的。』张秀才说∶『承蒙他不弃，时世又是这个样子，过去有啥难过，也该一笔勾销，大家重新做个朋友。』『是！』刘不才答说，『雪岩也是这个意思。说来说去，大家都是本乡本土的人，叶落归根，将来总要在一起。雪岩现在就是处处在留相见的余地。』这番话说得很动听，是劝张秀才留个相见的余地，却一点不着痕迹；使得内心原为帮长毛做事而惶惑不安的张秀才，越发觉得该跟胡雪岩『重新做个朋友』了。

“How do you address one another?” “Xueyan belongs to the generation below mine.” “Ah! Then, as his elder, you ought to be my honoured uncle. Yet you and my son call each other friends. We shall never straighten out the generations. Brother Liu, it is best if we all meet as equals.” “You are too kind. I shall call you Master Zhang.” Liu had no wish to spend more time on ceremonial niceties. He went straight on. “Xueyan mentioned you too, as a friend from difficult times. He particularly asked me to call on you when I returned to Hangzhou and convey his good wishes.” “Friends from difficult times—that is perfectly true. Before Xueyan prospered, we saw each other in the teahouse every day. Then came his fortunate connection with Governor Wang, and he rose straight into the clouds. His eyes rose with him. He scarcely noticed his poor friends any more, and we could no longer presume on his acquaintance. A friendship forged in hardship became a mere nodding acquaintance.” Liu listened quietly to this complaint, then naturally offered an explanation. “He became busy, and some lapses in courtesy were inevitable. But as to friendship, I am not shielding my own relative when I say that Xueyan has always managed at least to keep faith with others.” “Yes, he knows how to conduct himself in the world,” Zhang conceded. “Since he is good enough to remember me, and times are what they are, whatever was unpleasant in the past should be wiped away. Let us be friends afresh.” “That is exactly his wish,” Liu replied. “After all, we belong to the same place. Leaves return to their roots; one day we shall all be together again. Xueyan is taking care everywhere to leave room for future meetings.” It was beautifully put. Without openly admonishing Zhang, Liu had urged him to leave just such room himself. Already anxious about his service to the rebels, Zhang felt all the more strongly that he ought to renew his friendship with Hu.

『我也是这么想，年纪也都差不多了；时世又是如此。说真的，现在大家都是再世做人；想想过去，看看将来，不能再糊涂了。我有几句话！』张秀才毅然说了出来∶『要跟刘三哥请教。听这一说，刘不才将自己的椅子拉一拉，凑近了张秀才；两眼紧紧望着，是极其郑重、也极其诚恳的倾听之态。『明人不说暗话，雪岩的靠山是王抚台；如今已不在人世。另外一座靠山是何制军，听说「泥菩萨过江，自身难保」。既然这样子，我倒要请教刘三哥，雪岩还凭啥来混？』这话问在要害上，刘不才不敢随便，心里第一个念头是∶宁慢勿错。所以一面点头，一面细想；如果随意编上一段关系，说胡雪岩跟京里某大老如何如何；跟某省督抚又如何如何？话也可以编得很圆，无奈张秀才决不会相信；所以这是个很笨的法子。刘不才认为话说得超脱些，反而动听，因而这样答道∶『靠山都是假的，本事跟朋友才是真。有本事、有朋友，自然寻得着靠山。』他又补上一句∶『张大爷，我这两句话说得很狂。你老不要见气。』『好！』张秀才倒是颇为倾心，『刘三哥，听你这两句话，也是好脚色！』『不敢，我乱说。』

“I think the same. None of us is young any longer, and with times like these—truly, we have all lived a second life. Looking back on the past and ahead to the future, we cannot afford to remain foolish. There are some things,” Zhang said resolutely, “on which I should like your advice.” Liu drew his chair nearer, fixed his eyes on Zhang, and assumed a manner of grave, sincere attention. “Let us speak plainly. Xueyan’s patron was Governor Wang, who is now dead. His other support was Governor-General He, who, they say, is hard put to save himself. In that case, Brother Liu, what has Xueyan left to rely on?” The question went to the heart of the matter. Liu dared not answer carelessly. Better slow than wrong, he thought. He nodded while considering. He could invent a plausible tale about Hu’s relations with some great man in the capital or some provincial governor, but Zhang would never believe it. That would be a foolish course. A more independent answer would be more persuasive. “Patrons are unreliable. Ability and friends are what count. With ability and friends, a man will naturally find a patron.” He added, “Master Zhang, those are bold words. Please do not take offence.” “Good!” Zhang was plainly impressed. “Those two sentences show that you are a man of substance yourself.” “You flatter me. I am merely talking.”

『刘三哥，我再请教你，』张秀才将声音放得极低∶『你看大局怎么样？』这话就不好轻易回答了；刘不才拿眼看一看小张——小张会意，重重点头；表示但说不妨。『我从前也跟张大爷一样，人好象闷在坛子里，黑漆一团；这趟在上海住了几天，夷场上五方杂处，消息灵通。稍微听到些，大家都在说∶』这个「不长的！『一面说，一面做了个手势，指一指头发，意示『这个』是指长毛。张秀才听罢不响，拿起水烟袋，噗噜噜、噗噜噜，抽了好一会方始开口。『你倒说说看，为啥不长？』『这不是三言两语说得尽的——。』

“Let me ask you something else, Brother Liu.” Zhang lowered his voice almost to a whisper. “What do you think of the general situation?” This could not be answered lightly. Liu glanced at the son, who understood and nodded emphatically: he could speak freely. “I used to be like you, Master Zhang, shut in a jar, with everything dark around me. This time I spent a few days in Shanghai. People from every quarter gather in the foreign settlement, and news travels quickly. I heard a little. Everyone is saying that this will not last.” As he spoke, he pointed to his hair, indicating the Long-haired Rebels. Zhang said nothing. He took up his water pipe and smoked, bubbling away for some time before asking, “Tell me why it will not last.” “That cannot be explained in a few words—”

刘不才的口才很好，何况官军又实在打得很好；两好并一好，刘不才分析局势，将张秀才说得死心塌地。他也知道他们父子的名声不好，必得做一件惊世骇俗，大有功于乡邦的奇行伟举，才能遮掩得许多劣迹，令人刮目相看。现在有胡雪岩这条路子，岂可轻易放过？『刘三哥，我想明白了，拜托你回复雪岩，等官军一到，撵走长毛，光复杭州，我做内应。到那时候，雪岩要帮我洗刷。』『岂止于洗刷！』刘不才答说，『那时朝廷褒奖，授官补缺，这个从军功上得来的官，比捐班还漂亮些！』果然，等杭州克复，张秀才父子因为开城迎接藩司蒋益澧之功，使小张获得了一张七品奖札，并被派为善后局委员。张秀才趁机进言，杭州的善后，非把胡雪岩请回来主持不可。

Liu was an excellent speaker, and the government troops were in fact fighting very well. With both advantages on his side, he analysed the situation so persuasively that Zhang became entirely convinced. Zhang also knew that he and his son had bad reputations. They needed some extraordinary deed, a conspicuous service to their native place, to cover their many misdeeds and make people see them anew. With this opportunity through Hu before him, how could he let it pass? “Brother Liu, I have made up my mind. Tell Xueyan that, when the government troops arrive to drive out the rebels and recover Hangzhou, I shall assist them from within. When that day comes, he must help clear my name.” “Clear it? Much more than that!” Liu replied. “The court will reward you and appoint you to office. An office earned through military service is more honourable than one obtained by purchase.” And so it proved. When Hangzhou was recovered, father and son were credited with opening the gates to welcome Provincial Treasurer Jiang Yili. The son received a certificate granting him the seventh rank and was appointed to the reconstruction bureau. The father took the opportunity to insist that Hu must be invited back to direct Hangzhou’s reconstruction.

译者说明与学习提示（不属于小说正文）

译文说明
1. “司的克”为 stick 的旧音译。正文使用 walking stick；所藏短剑、榫口及其用途均按小说既有描写翻译，没有增添制造或使用步骤。“寸”保留为 cun，不换算成精确现代单位。
2. “送书”触赌客霉头源于“书／输”同音，英文简短补明双关。“洋春宫”按文中性质译为 foreign erotic picture book，没有增写画面或具体行为。小张只被称作少年、少爷或儿子，正文未给确切年龄，译作 young man/son，不自行写成未成年人或指定成人年龄。
3. 礼物实际来自胡雪岩，刘先称从亲戚处拿来、后明说受托携来，保留揭示顺序。五十两庄票是归还前批借款、取回表坠，非本批额外借款。
4. 刘不才关于“下跪、吵架、绝交”的话是人物为劝说所作陈述，不把它提升为新补的全知叙事事实。公文仍须秘密保存，张氏此时对未来免责的理解不等于朝廷已作终局裁决。
5. 最后一段以“果然”跳到杭州克复后的结果：小张确实得到七品奖札及善后局职位。译文在此转换为既成事实，与前面的承诺和预期区分。

阅读、完型与衔接
“The question itself was ordinary, but the form of address was significant”提示称谓携带关系信息：直称 Xueyan 而不称 Intendant Hu，是刘推测对方释怀的重要线索。
“Better slow than wrong”概括刘回答前的心理取舍；后续摒弃编造权贵关系、改谈本事与朋友，与这一原则衔接。
同一事件中的前景用 would、将来承诺用 will，结尾实际结果用过去时；阅读时应根据叙事层级判断，不能只看单个时态词。

翻译与表达
“keep faith with others”传达“不伤道”的守信、不亏待朋友；“a mere nodding acquaintance”对应“点头朋友”；“wipe away”在此搭配过去的芥蒂。注意这些表达在具体语境里的语气与关系含义。
