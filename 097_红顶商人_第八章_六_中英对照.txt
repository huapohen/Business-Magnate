胡雪岩｜中英对照版
原著：高阳

红顶商人｜The Red-Topped Merchant

第八章（本章第6部分，共9部分）

『领了两万两银子。如今面缴大人。』说着，从身上掏出一个红封袋来，当面奉上。左宗棠不肯接红封袋，『这是公款，不便私相授受。』他说，『请你跟粮台打交道。』当时便唤了粮台上管出纳的委员前来，收取了胡雪岩的粮票，开收据，盖上大印，看来是了却了一件公事，却不知胡雪岩还有话说。『大人，我还要交代。当初奉令采办的是米，不能拿米办到，就不能算交差。』『这——？』左宗棠相当困扰；对他的话，颇有不知所云之感，因而也就无法作何表示。『说实话，这一批米不能办到，我就是对不起王中丞的在天之灵。现在，总算可以真正有交代了！』胡雪岩平静地说，『我有一万石米，就在杭州城外江面上，请大人派员验收。』此言一出，左宗棠越发困惑，『你说的什么？』他问∶『有一万石米在？』『是！』『就在杭州城外江面上？』『是！』胡雪岩答说，『已有几百石，先拨了给蒋方伯，充作军粮了。』

“Twenty thousand taels. I now return them to Your Excellency in person.” Hu took a red envelope from his clothing and offered it. Zuo refused to accept it. “This is public money. It cannot be handed over privately. Please deal with the military supply office.” He summoned the officer responsible for receipts and payments. The officer took Hu’s note, issued a receipt and affixed the great seal. One official matter seemed settled, but Hu had more to say. “Your Excellency, there is still my commission to account for. I was ordered to procure rice. Until the rice has arrived, I cannot consider the task discharged.” “But—?” Zuo was puzzled, uncertain what Hu meant or how to respond. “To speak frankly, until I delivered that rice, I could not face Governor Wang’s spirit in Heaven. Now, at last, I can truly render an account.” Hu spoke calmly. “I have ten thousand shi of rice on the river outside Hangzhou. Please send an officer to inspect and receive it.” Zuo grew still more bewildered. “What are you saying? There are ten thousand shi of rice?” “Yes.” “On the river outside Hangzhou?” “Yes. Several hundred shi have already been assigned to Treasurer Jiang as military provisions.”

左宗棠听得这话便左右问道∶『护送胡大人来的是谁？』『是何都司』。于是找了何都司来，左宗棠第一句话便是∶『你知道不知道，有几百石军粮从钱塘江上运到城里？』『回大帅的话，有的。』何都司手一指∶『是胡大人从上海运来的。』『好！你先下去吧。』左宗棠向听差吩咐∶『请胡大人升炕！』礼数顿时不同了！由不令落座到升炕对坐，片刻之间，荣枯大不相同；胡雪岩既感慨，又得意，当然对应付左宗棠也更有把握了。等听差将盖碗茶移到炕几上，胡雪岩道谢坐下；左宗棠徐徐说道∶『有这一万石米，不但杭州的百姓得救；肃清浙江全境，我也有把握了。老兄此举，出人意表，功德无量。感激的，不止我左某一个人。』『大人言重了。』

Zuo turned to those beside him. “Who escorted Mr Hu here?” “Officer He.” He was summoned, and Zuo’s first question was, “Do you know whether several hundred shi of military grain have been brought into the city from the Qiantang River?” “They have, Commander-in-Chief.” He pointed towards Hu. “Mr Hu brought them from Shanghai.” “Good. You may withdraw.” Zuo instructed the attendant, “Invite Mr Hu to the kang.” The courtesies changed at once. From being denied a seat to sitting opposite Zuo on the raised platform—how completely fortune could turn in a moment! Hu felt both reflective and triumphant, and naturally more confident of handling him. The attendant moved the covered teacup to the small table on the kang. Hu thanked his host and sat down. Zuo spoke slowly. “With these ten thousand shi of rice, not only can the people of Hangzhou be saved; I can be confident of clearing all Zhejiang. What you have done is beyond expectation, a service of immeasurable merit. I shall not be the only man grateful to you.” “Your Excellency is too generous.”

『这是实话。不过我也要说实话。』左宗棠说，『一万石米，时价要值五六万银子；粮台上一时还付不起那么多。因为刚打了一个大胜仗，犒赏弟兄是现银子。我想，你先把你缴来的那笔款子领了回去；余数我们倒商量一下，怎么样个付法？』『大人不必操心了。这一万石米，完全由光墉报。』『报效？』左宗棠怕自己是听错了。『是！光墉报效。』『这，未免太破费了。』左宗棠问道∶『老兄有什么企图，不妨实说。』『毫无企图。第一，为了王中丞；第二，为了杭州百姓；第三，为了大人。』『承情之至！』左宗棠拱拱手说，『我马上出奏，请朝廷褒奖。』『大人栽培，光墉自然感激，不过，有句不识抬举的话，好比骨鲠在喉；吐出来请大人不要动气。』『言重，言重！』左宗棠一叠连声地说，『尽管请说。』『我的报效这批米，决不是为朝廷褒奖。光墉是生意人，只会做事，不会做官。』『好一个只会做事，不会做官！』这一句话碰到左宗棠的心坎上，拍着炕几，大声地说；赞赏之意，真个溢于言表了。『我在想，大人也是只晓得做事，从不把功名富贵放在心上的人。』胡雪岩说，『照我看，跟现在一位大人物，性情正好相反。』前半段话，恭维得恰到好处；对于后面一句话，左宗棠自然特感关切，探身说道∶『请教！』『大人跟江苏李中丞正好相反。李中丞会做官；大人会做事。』胡雪岩又说∶『大人也不是不会做官，只不过不屑于做官而已。』『啊，痛快，痛快！』左宗棠仰着脸，摇着头说；是一副遇见了知音的神情。

“I speak the truth. But there is something else I must say frankly. At current prices, ten thousand shi of rice must be worth fifty or sixty thousand taels. The supply office cannot pay so much at once. We have just won a great victory, and the troops must be rewarded in ready silver. I suggest that you first take back the money you have just returned. Then we can discuss how to pay the balance.” “Your Excellency need not trouble yourself. I am donating all ten thousand shi.” “Donating them?” Zuo feared he had misheard. “Yes. They are my contribution.” “That is a great expense. What do you hope to obtain, my friend? You may speak plainly.” “Nothing for myself. First, I do it for Governor Wang; second, for the people of Hangzhou; third, for Your Excellency.” “I am deeply obliged.” Zuo clasped his hands. “I shall memorialise the throne at once and request a commendation.” “I am naturally grateful for your favour. But there is something I must say, though it may seem unappreciative. It is like a bone in my throat. Please do not be angry if I speak.” “Not at all, not at all!” Zuo answered repeatedly. “Speak freely.” “I am certainly not giving this rice to obtain an imperial commendation. I am a businessman. I know how to get things done, not how to make a career in office.” “How well said! ‘Get things done, not make a career in office’!” The words went straight to Zuo’s heart. He slapped the kang table and spoke loudly, his admiration unmistakable. “I believe Your Excellency too thinks only of getting things done, never of honours and wealth,” Hu continued. “In my view, your temperament is exactly the opposite of that of a certain great man today.” The first part was perfectly judged flattery. The last naturally aroused Zuo’s particular interest. He leaned forward. “Whom do you mean?” “Governor Li of Jiangsu. He knows how to make a career; Your Excellency knows how to do the work.” Hu added, “It is not that you do not know how to make a career. You merely disdain it.” “Ah, how refreshing! How refreshing!” Zuo tilted back his face and shook his head, looking like a man who had found someone who understood him.

胡雪岩见好即收，不再奉上高帽子；反而谦虚一句∶『我是信口胡说。在大人面前放肆。』『老兄，』左宗棠正色说道，『你不要妄自菲薄，在我看满朝朱紫贵，及得上老兄识见的。实在不多。你大号是哪两个字？』『草字雪岩。风雪的雪，岩壑的岩。』『雪岩兄，』左宗棠说，『你这几年想必一直在上海，李少荃的作为，必然深知；你倒拿我跟他比一比看。』『这，』胡雪岩问道，『比哪一方面？』『比比我们的成就。』『是！』胡雪岩想了一下答道∶『李中丞克复苏州，当然是一大功；不过，因人成事；比不上大人孤军奋战，来得难能可贵。』『这，总算是一句公道话。』左宗棠说，『我吃亏的有两种，第一是地方不如他好；第二、是人才不如他多。』『是的。』胡雪岩深深点头，『李中丞也算会用人的。』『那末，我有句很冒昧的话请教，以你的大才，以你在王中丞那里的业绩，他倒没有起延揽之意？』

Hu stopped while his flattery was well received and offered no more extravagant compliments. Instead he said modestly, “I have been speaking at random, taking liberties before Your Excellency.” “My friend,” Zuo said seriously, “you must not undervalue yourself. Among all the grandees in purple and scarlet at court, few possess your insight. What are the characters of your courtesy name?” “Xueyan: xue for snow, yan for rocky crags.” “Brother Xueyan, you have presumably spent these past few years in Shanghai. You must know Li Shaoquan’s conduct well. Compare him with me.” “In what respect?” Hu asked. “Our achievements.” “Very well.” Hu thought for a moment. “Governor Li’s recovery of Suzhou was naturally a great achievement. But he succeeded through the efforts of others. It cannot compare with your struggle alone, which was far more difficult and therefore more admirable.” “That, at least, is a fair statement,” Zuo said. “I have suffered two disadvantages: my territory is less favourable, and I have fewer able men.” “Yes.” Hu nodded gravely. “Governor Li does know how to employ people.” “Then may I ask a rather presumptuous question? With your great abilities and your record under Governor Wang, did he never think of recruiting you?”

『有过的。我不能去！』『为什么？』『第一、李中丞对王公有成见，我还为他所用，也太没有志气了。』『好！』左宗棠接着问∶『第二呢？』『第二、我是浙江人，我要为浙江出力；何况我还有王中丞委托我未了的公事，就是这笔买米的款子，总要有个交代。』『难得，难得，雪岩兄，你真有信用。』左宗棠说到这里，喊一声∶『来呀！留胡大人吃便饭。』照官场中的规矩，长官对属下有这样的表示，听差便得做两件事，第一件是请客人更换便衣；第二件是准备将客人移到花厅甚至『上房』中去。在正常的情况之下，胡雪岩去拜客，自然带着跟班；跟班手中捧着衣包，视需要随时伺候主人更换。但此时只有胡雪岩一个人，当然亦不会有便衣；左宗棠便吩咐听差，取他自己的薄棉袍来为『胡大人』更换。左宗棠矮胖；胡雪岩瘦长，这件棉袍穿上身，大袖郎当，下摆吊起一大截，露出一大截沾满了黄泥的靴帮子，形容不但不雅，而且有些可笑。但这份情意是可感的。所以胡雪岩觉得穿在身上很舒服。至于移向花厅，当然也办不到了。一座小关帝庙里，哪里来的空闲房屋，闽浙总督的官厅，签押房与卧室，都在那里了。不过，庙后倒有一座土山，山上有座茅亭，亦算可供登临眺望的一景；左宗棠为了避免将领请谒的纷扰，吩咐就在茅亭中置酒。

“He did. I could not go.” “Why?” “First, Governor Li was prejudiced against Governor Wang. To serve him despite that would show too little spirit.” “Good! And second?” “Second, I am a Zhejiang man and wish to work for Zhejiang. Besides, Governor Wang left me unfinished public business: this money for buying rice. I had to account for it.” “Admirable, admirable! Brother Xueyan, you are truly trustworthy.” Zuo called out, “Attend me! Mr Hu will stay for a simple meal.” According to official custom, when a superior made such an invitation, the attendants had two duties: to ask the guest to change into informal clothing, and to arrange his move to a garden reception room or even the private apartments. Ordinarily Hu would bring a servant on a visit, carrying a bundle of clothes and ready to help him change. Today he had come alone and naturally had nothing informal to wear. Zuo therefore ordered the attendant to bring one of his own lightly padded gowns for “Mr Hu”. Zuo was short and stout, Hu tall and lean. The sleeves hung loosely, while the hem stopped far too high, exposing a long stretch of boots caked with yellow mud. The effect was not merely inelegant but rather comical. Yet the kindness behind it was touching, and Hu felt thoroughly comfortable in it. Moving to a garden reception room was equally impossible. In this little temple, where could spare rooms be found? The governor-general’s audience room, office and bedroom were all confined here. Behind the temple, however, was a small earthen hill with a thatched pavilion, a modest place from which to enjoy a view. To escape interruptions from officers seeking audiences, Zuo ordered the meal served there.

酒当然是好酒。绍兴早经克复，供应一省长官的，自然是历经兵燹而无恙的窑藏陈酿；菜是湖南口味，虽只两个人对酌，依然大盘长筷，最后厨子戴着红缨帽，亲自来上菜，打开食盒，只是一小盘湖南腊肉。不知何以郑重如此？『这是内子亲手调制的，间关万里，从湖南送到这里，已经不中吃了。只不过我自己提醒我，不要忘记内子当年委曲绸缪的一番苦心而已。』胡雪岩也听说过，左宗棠的周夫人，是富室之女；初嫔左家时，夫婿是个寒士。但是周夫人却深知『身无半亩，心忧天下』的左宗棠，才气纵横，虽然会试屡屡落弟，终有破壁飞去的一日；所以鼓励慰藉，无怕不至。以后左宗棠移居岳家，而周家大族，不会看得起这个脾气的穷姑爷。周夫人一方面怕夫婿一怒而去，一方面又要为夫家做面，左右调停，心力交痤，如今到底也有扬眉吐气的一天了。这对胡雪岩又是一种启示。左宗棠如今尊重周夫人，报恩的成分，多于一切，足见得是不会负人，不肯负人而深具性情者，这比起李鸿章 以利禄权术驾驭部下来，宁愿倾心结交此人。因此，当左宗棠有所询问时，他越发不作保留，从杭州的善后谈到筹饷，他都有一套办法拿出来，滔滔不绝，言无不尽。宾主之间，很快地已接近脱略形迹，无所不谈的境地了。

The wine was naturally excellent. Shaoxing had long since been recovered, and the province’s highest official was supplied with aged wine that had survived the war undamaged in its cellars. The food was Hunan style. Though only two men were dining, there were great dishes and long chopsticks. Finally the cook, wearing a hat with a red tassel, personally brought a food box. He opened it to reveal nothing more than a small plate of Hunan cured pork. Why such ceremony? “My wife prepared this herself,” Zuo explained. “It came all the way from Hunan through countless difficulties. It is no longer good to eat. I keep it simply to remind myself of all the pains she once took to manage our affairs in trying circumstances.” Hu had heard something of Madam Zhou. She came from a wealthy family, but when she married Zuo he was a poor scholar. She understood that this man, who “owned not half a mu yet worried for all under heaven”, possessed extraordinary talent. Though he repeatedly failed the metropolitan examination, one day he would burst through the wall and soar. She encouraged and consoled him with unceasing care. Later he moved into her family’s home. The large Zhou clan could hardly admire a poor son-in-law with such a temper. Afraid that her husband might storm away in anger, yet anxious to uphold his family’s dignity, she mediated on every side until mind and strength were exhausted. Now, at last, her day of vindication had come. Hu drew another lesson from this. Gratitude, more than anything else, lay behind Zuo’s present respect for his wife. Clearly he was a man of deep feeling, unwilling to fail those who had helped him. Compared with Li Hongzhang, who controlled his subordinates through rewards, advancement and political manoeuvring, this was the man Hu would rather befriend wholeheartedly. Accordingly, when Zuo asked questions, he held back still less. From Hangzhou’s reconstruction to raising military funds, he had a method for everything and spoke without reserve. Host and guest soon approached the point where all formality could be discarded and nothing was beyond discussion.

一顿酒喝了两个时辰方罢。左宗棠忽然叹口气说∶『雪岩兄，我倒有些发愁了。不知应该借重你在哪方面给我帮忙？当务之急是地方善后，可是每个月二十五、六万的饷银，尚无的款，又必得仰仗大力。只恨足下分身无术！雪岩兄，请你自己说一说，愿意做些什么？』『筹饷是件大事，不过只要有办法，凡是操守靠得住的人，都可以干得。』胡雪岩歉然地说，『光墉稍微存一点私心，想为本乡本土尽几力。』『这哪里是私心！正见得你一副侠心义肠。军兴以来，杭州被祸最惨，善后事宜，经纬万端，我兼摄无篆，责无旁贷，有你老兄这样大才，而且肯任劳任怨，又是为桑梓效力的人帮我的忙，实在太好了。』左宗棠说到这里，问道∶『跟蒋芗泉想来见面了？』『是！』『你觉得他为人如何？』『很直爽的人。我们谈得很投机。』『好极，好极！』左宗棠欣然问道∶『地方上的一切善后，总也谈过了？』『还不曾深谈。不过承蒋方伯看得起，委托我的一个小小钱庄，为他代理藩库；眼前急需的支出，我总尽力维持。』『那更好了。万事莫如赈济急；如今有一万石米，在军需民食，能维持一两个月，后援就接得上了。再有宝号代为支应藩库的一切开销，扶伤恤死，亦不愁无款可垫。然则杭州的赈济事宜，应当马上动手。我想，设一善后局，雪岩兄，请你当总办，如何？』『是！』胡雪岩肃然答说∶『于公于私，义不容辞。』『我就代杭州百姓致谢了。』左宗棠拱拱手说，『公事我马上叫他们预备，交蒋芗泉转送。』

Their meal lasted two double-hours. At last Zuo sighed. “Brother Xueyan, now I am troubled. In what capacity should I ask your help? Reconstruction is the immediate need, yet the monthly military payroll of two hundred and fifty or sixty thousand taels still has no assured source, and there too I must rely on you. If only you could be in two places at once! Tell me yourself: what would you like to do?” “Raising funds is important, but once a method is established, anyone whose integrity can be trusted can carry it out.” Hu sounded apologetic. “I have a small selfish wish: to do something for my native place.” “How is that selfish? It shows your generous and honourable heart. Since the war began, Hangzhou has suffered most grievously. Its reconstruction has countless threads to draw together. As acting governor, I cannot evade responsibility. To have a man of your ability, willing to labour and bear complaints, helping me serve his homeland—that is excellent indeed.” Zuo asked, “You have met Jiang Xiangquan, I suppose?” “Yes.” “What do you think of him?” “He is very straightforward. We got on extremely well.” “Excellent, excellent!” Zuo asked gladly, “Then you must have discussed the work of reconstruction?” “Not in depth. But Treasurer Jiang has honoured my little bank by entrusting it with the business of the provincial treasury. I shall do my utmost to meet the immediate expenses.” “Better still. Nothing is more urgent than relief. With ten thousand shi of rice, the soldiers and people can be fed for a month or two, until further supplies arrive. And with your bank meeting the treasury’s expenses, there will be advances available for helping the wounded and caring for the dead. Relief in Hangzhou should therefore begin at once. I propose establishing a reconstruction bureau. Brother Xueyan, will you serve as its director?” “Yes.” Hu answered solemnly. “Both public duty and private feeling forbid me to decline.” “Then I thank you on behalf of Hangzhou’s people.” Zuo clasped his hands. “I shall have the papers prepared at once and send them through Jiang Xiangquan.”

这样处置，正符合胡雪岩的希望。因为他为人处世，一向奉『不招忌』三字为座右铭；自己的身分与蒋益澧差不多，但在左宗棠手下，到底只算一个客卿，如果形迹太密甚至越过蒋益澧这一关，直接听命于左宗棠，设身处地为人想一想，心里也会不舒服。现在当着本人在此，而委任的札子却要交由蒋益澧转发，便是尊重藩司的职权；也是无形中为他拉拢蒋益澧，仅不过公事上小小的一道手续，便有许多讲究；只见得做官用人，不是件容易的事。这样想着，他对左宗棠又加了几分钦佩之心；因而厚意替他多做一点事，至少也得为他多策划几个好主意。心念刚动，左宗棠正好又谈起筹饷，他决定献上一条妙计。这一计，他筹之已熟；本来的打算是『货卖识家』，不妨『待价而沽』。这也就是说，如果没有相当的酬庸，他是不肯轻易吐露的；此刻对左宗棠，多少有知遇之感，因而就倾囊而出了。

This arrangement was precisely what Hu wanted. He had always taken “arouse no jealousy” as his guiding rule. His status was much the same as Jiang Yili’s, but under Zuo he was, after all, only an invited adviser. If he appeared too intimate with Zuo, or even bypassed Jiang to take orders directly, Jiang could hardly feel comfortable. Hu had only to put himself in the other man’s place to see that. Though Hu was present, the appointment would be sent through Jiang. This respected the provincial treasurer’s authority and quietly helped Hu draw closer to him. Such a small official procedure, yet so much lay behind it! Holding office and employing men were no simple matters. His admiration for Zuo increased, and he wanted to do a little more for him—or at least devise a few more useful ideas. Just then Zuo returned to military finance, and Hu decided to offer an ingenious scheme. He had long thought it through. Originally he had meant to sell his wares to a buyer who knew their worth, waiting for the proper price: without sufficient reward, he would not readily have disclosed it. Now, feeling that Zuo had recognised his worth, he emptied his store of ideas before him.

『筹饷之道多端，大致不外两途，第一是办厘金，这要靠市面兴旺，无法强求；第二是劝捐，这几年捐得起的都捐过了，「劝」起来也很吃力。如今我想到有一路人，他们捐得起，而且一定肯捐；不妨在这一路人头上，打个主意。』『捐得起，又肯捐，那不太妙了吗？』左宗棠急急问道∶『是哪一路人？』『是长毛！』胡雪岩说，『长毛盘踞东南十几年，搜括得很不少；现在要他们捐几文，不是天经地义？』这一说，左宗棠恍然大悟，连连点头∶『对，对，请你再说下去。』于是胡雪岩为他指出，这十几年中，颇有些见机而作的长毛，发了财退藏于密；洪杨一旦平定，从逆的当然要依国法治罪。可是叛逆虽罪在不赦，而被裹胁从逆的人很多，办不胜办。株连过众，扰攘不安，亦非大乱之后的休养生息之道；所以最好的处置办法是，网开一面，予人自新之路。只是一概既往不咎，亦未免太便宜了此辈；应该略施薄惩。愿打愿罚，各听其便。『大人晓得的，人之常情，总是愿罚不愿打；除非罚不起。』胡雪岩说，『据我知道，罚得起的人很多。他们大都躲在夷场上，倚仗洋人的势力，官府一时无奈其何，可是终究是个出不了头的「黑人」，如果动以利害，晓以大义；反正手头也是不义之财，舍了一笔，换个重新做人的机会，何乐不为？』『说得是。』左宗棠笑道，『此辈不甘寂寞，不但要爬起来做人，只怕还要站出来做官。』『正是这话。』胡雪岩撮起两指一伸，『象这种人，要捐他两笔。』

“There are many ways of raising military funds, but broadly they fall into two kinds. One is the likin tax. That depends on a thriving market and cannot be forced. The other is soliciting contributions. In recent years, everyone able to give has already given, and persuasion is difficult. But I have thought of a class of people who can afford to contribute and will certainly be willing. We might turn our attention to them.” “They can afford it and are willing? What could be better?” Zuo asked eagerly. “Who are they?” “The Longhairs. They have held the southeast for more than ten years and amassed a great deal by plunder. Is it not only right that they should now contribute something?” Understanding dawned on Zuo. He nodded repeatedly. “Yes, yes. Go on.” Hu explained that during those years, quite a number of rebels had recognised the right moment, made their fortunes and retired into hiding. Once the Hong-Yang rebellion was suppressed, those who had joined it would naturally be punished by law. Yet though rebellion was an unpardonable crime, many had been forced into it—too many to prosecute. To implicate large numbers and keep society in turmoil would not allow a country exhausted by great disorder to recover. The best course was to leave an opening in the net, a way for people to begin anew. Still, to overlook everything would let them off too cheaply. There should be a mild punishment, with each person free to choose between a beating and a fine. “Your Excellency knows human nature,” Hu said. “People prefer a fine to a beating, unless they cannot afford it. I know there are many who can. Most hide in the foreign settlements, relying on foreign protection, and for the moment the authorities can do little about them. Yet they remain men without a lawful identity, unable to emerge openly. Explain the advantages and risks, appeal to their sense of right: their money was ill-gotten anyway. Why should they not part with some of it in exchange for a fresh start?” “Quite so,” Zuo said with a smile. “Such men will not be content with obscurity. They may want not only to become respectable again, but to take office.” “Exactly.” Hu raised two fingers. “Men of that kind must contribute twice.”

『怎么呢？』『一笔是做人；另外一笔是做官。做官不要捐吗？』左宗棠失笑了，『我倒弄糊涂了！』他说，『照此看来，我得赶快向部里领几千张空白捐照来。』『是！大人尽管动公事去领。』『领是领了。雪岩兄，』左宗棠故意问道∶『交给谁去用呢？』胡雪岩不作声，停了一会方说∶『容我慢慢物色好了，向大人保荐。』『我看你也不用物色了，就是你自己勉为其难吧！』『这怕——。』『不，不！』左宗棠挥手打断了他的话，『你不必推辞了！雪岩兄，你遇见我，就容不得你再作主张。这话好象蛮不讲理；不是的！足下才大如海，我已深知。不要说就这两件事，再多兼几个差使，你也能够应付裕如。我想，你手下总有一班得力的人；你尽管开单子来，我关照蒋芗泉，一律照委。你往来沪杭两地，出出主意就行了。』如此看重，不由得使胡雪岩想起王有龄在围城中常说的两句话说∶『鞠躬尽痤，死而后已。』便慨然答道∶『既然大人认为我干得了，我就试一试看。』『不用试，包你成功！』左宗棠说，『我希望你两件事兼筹并顾。浙江的军务，正在紧要关头上，千万不能有「闹饷」的活把戏弄出来。』『是。我尽力而为。』胡雪岩说，『如今要请示的是，这个捐的名目。我想叫「罚捐」。』『罚捐倒也名副其实。不过——。』他沉吟着，好久未说下去。

“How so?” “Once to become respectable, and once to become officials. Does entering office not require a contribution?” Zuo laughed. “I had confused myself. In that case, I had better request several thousand blank contribution certificates from the ministry at once.” “Yes. Please send the official request.” “Suppose I obtain them, Brother Xueyan,” Zuo asked deliberately. “Whom shall I entrust with them?” Hu was silent for a while. “Allow me time to find a suitable man and recommend him.” “I do not think you need look. You must undertake the difficulty yourself.” “I fear that—” “No, no!” Zuo waved aside his objection. “Do not decline. Once you have met me, Brother Xueyan, you can no longer insist on your own wishes. That may sound unreasonable, but it is not. I know now that your abilities are as vast as the sea. Never mind these two tasks: you could handle several more with ease. You must have capable men under you. Give me their names, and I shall instruct Jiang Xiangquan to appoint them all. You need only travel between Shanghai and Hangzhou and provide the ideas.” Such confidence reminded Hu of two lines Wang Youling had often repeated during the siege: “Bend every effort to the task; cease only in death.” He answered with spirit. “If Your Excellency believes I can do it, I shall try.” “No need to try. I guarantee you will succeed,” Zuo said. “I want you to attend to both matters together. Military affairs in Zhejiang are at a crucial stage. We must on no account have a disturbance over unpaid wages.” “I shall do my utmost. What I need your instructions on now is the name of this contribution. I suggest ‘penalty contribution’.” “That describes it accurately. But—” He pondered for a long time without finishing.

这当然是有顾忌；胡雪岩也可以想象得到，开办『罚捐』可能会惹起浮议，指作『包庇逆党』。这是很重的一个罪名。然而是否『包庇』，要看情节而定；与予人自新之路，是似是而非的两回事。他心里这样在想，口头却保持沉默；而且很注意左宗棠的表情，要看他是不是有担当？左宗棠自然是有担当的；而且这正也是他平时自负之处。他所考虑的改换名目；想了好一会，竟找不出适当的字眼，便次定暂进先用了再说。

He plainly had a misgiving, and Hu could imagine it. A scheme of “penalty contributions” might provoke criticism and the charge of “sheltering rebels”, a very serious offence. Yet whether it amounted to sheltering them depended on the circumstances. That and allowing people to reform were superficially similar but different things. Hu kept these thoughts to himself, watching Zuo’s expression closely to see whether he would take responsibility. Zuo naturally would; indeed, that was one of the qualities on which he prided himself. What troubled him was finding a different name. He thought for some time without discovering a suitable term and finally decided to use this one for the present.
