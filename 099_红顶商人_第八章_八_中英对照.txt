胡雪岩｜中英对照版
原著：高阳

红顶商人｜The Red-Topped Merchant

第八章（本章第8部分，共9部分）

阅读说明：每段中文后接对应英文；两版英文正文一致。下载站水印已清除，原始疑字在文末说明。

『只怕我想得不对。』『不会错！』左宗棠叹口气，『我一直也是这么在想，不过不肯承认我自己的想法；我总觉得李少荃总算也是个翰林，肚子里的货色，虽只不过温熟了一部诗经，忠君爱国的道理总也懂的，而况受恩深重，又何忍辜负君父灭此大盗，以安四海的至意？如今你跟我的看法不约而同，就见得彼此的想法都不错。论少荃的为人，倒还不致巴结曾九；只为他老师节制五省军务，圣眷正隆，不免功名心热，屈己从人。至于他对曾九，虽不便明助，睹底下却要帮忙，助饷助械，尽力而为；所以金陵克复的日子，仍旧不会远。』『是的。这是明摆在那里的事；江宁合围，外援断绝，城里的存粮一完，长毛也就完了。照我看，总在夏秋之交，一定可以成功。』『那时候就有麻烦了。你先看着这个——。』

“Perhaps I am mistaken.” “You are not!” Zuo sighed. “I have thought the same all along, but refused to admit it to myself. Li Shaoquan is, after all, a Hanlin scholar. His learning may amount only to a well-rehearsed Book of Songs, but he must understand loyalty to his sovereign and love of country. Having received such great favours, how could he disappoint his sovereign’s earnest wish to destroy this great bandit and bring peace to the empire? Now you have reached the same conclusion independently, which shows that we are both right. Shaoquan is not the sort to flatter Zeng the Ninth merely for his own sake. But his teacher commands military affairs in five provinces and stands high in imperial favour. His own eagerness for advancement makes him yield and accommodate others. As for Zeng the Ninth, though he cannot help openly, he will do all he can in secret, supplying money and arms. The recovery of Jinling cannot be far away.” “Yes. That is plain to see. Jiangning is surrounded and outside help cut off. Once the food in the city is exhausted, the Longhairs are finished. In my view, success must come around the turn of summer and autumn.” “Then the trouble will begin. Look at this first.”

说着左宗棠从怀中掏出一封信来，厚甸甸地，总有十来张信笺；他检视了一下，抽出其中的两张，递了给胡雪岩。这两张信笺中，谈的是一件事；也就是报告一个消息。说兵部与户部的书办，眼看洪杨肃清在即；军务告峻，要办军费报销，无不额手相庆。但以湘淮两军，起自田间，将领不谙规制，必不知军费应如何报销？因而有人出头，邀约户兵两部的书办，商定了包揽的办法，多雇书手，备办笔墨纸张；专程南下，就地为湘淮两军代办报销。一切不用费心，只照例奉送『部费』即可。在他们看，这是利人利己的两全之计，必为湘淮两军乐予接纳，所以不但已有成议，而且已经筹集了两万银子，作为『本钱』，光是办购置造报销的连史纸，就将琉璃几家纸店的存货都搜空了。『这个花样倒不错！』胡雪岩有意出以轻松的姿态，『不过这笔「部费」可观。我替殉节的王中丞经手过，至少要百分之二。』『就是这话罗！』左宗棠说，『我要跟你商量的就是这件事。我前后用过七千万的银子，如果照例致送，就得二十万银子。哪里来这笔闲钱，且不去说它；就有这笔闲钱，我也不愿意塞狗洞。你倒想个法子看，怎么样打消了它！』『打消是容易，放句话出去挡驾就是。可是以后呢？恐怕不胜其烦了！军费报销是最噜苏的事，一案核销，有几年不结的。大人倒仔细想一想，宝贵的精神，犯得着犯不着花在跟这些人打交道上头？』

Zuo took a thick letter from inside his robe, perhaps ten or more sheets, examined it and selected two to hand to Hu. The two sheets concerned a single piece of news. The clerks in the Ministries of War and Revenue saw that the Hong-Yang rebellion would soon be suppressed. When military operations ended, the war accounts would have to be submitted for audit, and they were already congratulating themselves. The Xiang and Huai armies had risen from the countryside; their commanders knew little of official regulations and could hardly know how military expenditure should be accounted for. Someone had therefore organised clerks from both ministries to undertake the work as a package. They would hire extra copyists, prepare brushes, ink and paper, and travel south specially to compile the armies’ accounts on the spot. The commanders need trouble themselves with nothing, merely paying the customary “ministry fees”. The clerks considered this mutually beneficial and were certain both armies would welcome it. They had not only agreed on a plan but raised twenty thousand taels as capital. Their purchases of fine lianshi paper for the accounts alone had emptied the stocks of several paper shops in Liuli. “An ingenious arrangement,” Hu said, deliberately keeping his manner light. “But the ministry fees will be substantial. I dealt with them for the late Governor Wang. They amount to at least two per cent.” “Exactly!” Zuo said. “That is what I wish to discuss. I have spent seventy million taels in all. At the customary rate, I should have to pay two hundred thousand. Leave aside where I could find such spare money: even if I had it, I would not stuff it down a dog’s hole. Think of a way to put an end to this.” “Putting an end to it is easy. Send word declining their services. But afterwards? The trouble may be endless. Auditing military expenditure is the most tedious business. A single case can remain unsettled for years. Consider carefully, Your Excellency: is dealing with these men worth your precious time and energy?”

『不！』左宗棠大不以为然，『我的意思是，根本不要办报销。军费报销，在乾隆年间最认真；部里书办的花样也最多。不过此一时，彼一时，那时是「在人檐下过，不敢不低头」；如今我又何必低头？户部也没有资格跟我要帐！』这话说得太霸道了些。诚然，湘军和淮军的军费，都是在地方自筹，户部并没有支付过；但在地方自筹，不管是厘金、捐募，总是公款，何致于户部连要个帐都没有资格？胡雪岩不以左宗棠的话为然，因而沉默未答。『雪翁，』左宗棠催问着，『有何高见，请指教！』这就不能不回答了，胡雪岩想了一下答道∶『那不是大人一个人的事。』『是啊！不过事情来了，我可是脱不了麻烦。』『就有麻烦，也不致于比两江来得大。』这一说，左宗棠明白了，『你的意思是，策动曾相去顶？』他问。这是指曾国藩，他以协办大学士兼领两江总督，也算入阁拜相，所以称之为『曾相』；胡雪岩正是此意，点点头答说∶『似乎以曾相出面去争，比较容易见效。』『我也想到过，没有用。曾相忧谗畏讥，胆小如鼠；最近还有密折，请朝廷另简亲信大臣，分任重责。你想，他怎么肯不避嫌疑，奏请免办报销？何况时机亦还未到可以上折的时候？』『难处就在这里。』胡雪岩说，『军务究竟尚未告竣，贸然奏请免办报销，反会节外生枝，惹起无谓的麻烦。』『可是消弭隐患，此刻就得着手。倘或部里书办勾结司员；然后说动堂官；再进而由军机奏闻两宫，一经定案，要打消就难了。』胡雪岩觉得这番顾虑，决不能说是多余；而且由他的『书办勾结司员』这句话，触机而有灵感，不暇思索地答说∶『既然如此，不妨在第一关上就拿书办挡了回去。』『喂，喂！』左宗棠一面想，一面说，『你这话很有意味。然而，是如何个挡法呢？』

“No!” Zuo disagreed emphatically. “I mean that there should be no audit at all. Military accounts were scrutinised most strictly in the Qianlong reign, when the ministry clerks had the most tricks. But those were different times. Then, under another man’s eaves, one had to bow one’s head. Why should I bow mine now? The Ministry of Revenue has no right to demand my accounts.” That was rather too high-handed. The Xiang and Huai armies had indeed raised their own funds locally; the ministry had paid none of their expenses. Yet whether the money came from likin or contributions, it was still public money. How could the ministry have no right even to ask for an account? Hu disagreed and remained silent. “Mr Xueyan,” Zuo pressed, “if you have an opinion, please enlighten me.” Hu had to answer. After a moment, he said, “This does not concern Your Excellency alone.” “True. But when the trouble comes, I cannot escape it.” “Even so, it will hardly be greater than the trouble facing the governor-general at Liangjiang.” Zuo understood. “You mean we should prompt Grand Secretary Zeng to resist?” He meant Zeng Guofan, whose concurrent appointments as assistant grand secretary and governor-general of Liangjiang entitled him to be addressed as a chief minister. Hu nodded. “An argument presented by him seems more likely to succeed.” “I have thought of that. It is useless. Zeng fears slander and criticism; he is as timid as a mouse. He recently sent a confidential memorial asking the court to appoint another trusted minister to share his responsibilities. How could he disregard suspicion and request exemption from an audit? Besides, the time is not yet ripe for such a memorial.” “That is the difficulty,” Hu said. “The campaign is not over. A premature request could only create needless complications.” “But we must remove the hidden danger now. Suppose the clerks win over the ministry’s departmental officers, then persuade its senior officials, and finally have the Grand Council present the matter to the two empress dowagers. Once a decision is made, it will be hard to undo.” Hu could not dismiss that concern. The words about clerks conspiring with departmental officers gave him an idea, and he answered without hesitation, “Then we might block the clerks at the first stage.” “Ah, ah!” Zuo pondered. “There is something in that. But how?”

『这等大事，书办不能做主；就如大人所说的，得要勾结司官。司官给他们来盆冷水，迎头一浇；或者表面上敷衍，到紧要关头，挺身出来讲话，只要有理，户部堂官亦不能不听。』『话是有理。难在哪里去找这么一位明大体、有胆识的户部司官？』『不一定要明大体、有胆识。』胡雪岩答说，『只要这位司官，觉得这么做于他有利；自然就会挺身而出。』『着！』左宗棠又是猛拍自己的大腿，『雪翁，你的看法，确是高人一筹，足以破惑。』略停一下，他又说道∶『听你的口气，似乎胸有成竹；已经想到有这么一个人了。』『是的。就是杭州人。』『杭州人，』左宗棠偏着头想，『在户部当司官的是谁？我倒想不起来了。』『这个人是咸丰二年的进士，分发户部，由主事做起，现在是掌印郎中了。他叫王文韶；大人听说过此人没有？』左宗棠凝神了一会，想起来了∶『似乎听人提起过。』他问，『他的号，是叫夔石吗？』『正是。王夔石。』『此人怎么样？很能干吧？』『很能干，也很圆滑；人缘不错。加以户部左侍郎沈桂芬是他乡试的座师，很照应这个门生，所以王夔石在户部很红。』『既然人很圆滑，只怕不肯出头去争！』左宗棠说，『这种事，只有性情比较耿直的人才肯做。』『大人见得是。不过，我的意思不是鼓动王夔石出头去力争，是托他暗底下疏通。我想，为了他自己的前程，他是肯效劳的。』『何以见得？雷翁，请道其详。』

“The clerks cannot decide so great a matter themselves. As you say, they must secure departmental support. An officer could throw cold water on the scheme from the outset, or seem to acquiesce and then speak out at the crucial moment. If his argument is sound, the ministry’s senior officials cannot ignore it.” “True. But where shall we find a Revenue officer with the breadth of view and courage to do it?” “He need not possess either. He need only see an advantage to himself. Then he will step forward.” “Exactly!” Zuo again struck his thigh. “Your insight is a cut above others’, Mr Xueyan. It clears away confusion.” After a pause, he added, “You sound as though you have someone in mind already.” “I do. A man from Hangzhou.” “A Hangzhou man?” Zuo tilted his head. “Who is serving as a departmental officer in Revenue? I cannot recall.” “He gained his metropolitan degree in the second year of Xianfeng and was assigned to Revenue. He began as a secretary and is now a director in charge of a departmental seal. His name is Wang Wenshao. Have you heard of him?” Zuo concentrated for a while, then remembered. “I seem to have heard him mentioned. Is his courtesy name Kuishi?” “Yes. Wang Kuishi.” “What sort of man is he? Capable?” “Very capable, and very smooth. He is well liked. Moreover, Shen Guifen, the ministry’s left vice-minister, was his examiner in the provincial examination and looks after this pupil. Wang is consequently well placed at Revenue.” “If he is so smooth, he may not be willing to lead an argument. Only a rather upright, outspoken man would do that.” “You are right. But I do not propose urging him to argue openly. I would ask him to work quietly behind the scenes. For the sake of his own prospects, I think he would be willing.” “Why? Explain in detail, Mr Xueyan.”

照胡雪岩的看法，做京官若说不靠关系靠自己，所可凭借者，不是学问，便是才干。当翰林靠学问；当司官就要靠才干。这才干是干济之才，不在乎腹有经纶，而是在政务上遇到难题，能有切切实实的办法拿出来。至少也要能搪塞得过去。王文韶之所长，正就是在此。可是，做京官凭才干，实在不如凭学问。因为凭学问做京官，循资推转，处处得以显其所长；翰林做到兼日讲起注官，进而『开坊』升任京堂，都可以专折言事，更是卖弄学问的时候。也许一道奏疏，上结天知，就此飞黄腾达，三数年间便能戴上红顶子。而凭才干做官。就没有这样便宜了！『为啥呢？因为英雄要有用武之地。做部里司官，每天公事经手，该准该驳，权柄很大；准有准的道理，驳有驳的缘故，只要说得对，自然显的的才干。可是司官不能做一辈子；象王夔石，郎中做了好多年了，如果升做四品京堂，那些鸿胪寺、通政司，都是「聋子的耳朵」，没有它不象样子，有了它毫无用处。王夔石就有天大的本事，无奈冷衙门无事可做，也是枉然。』胡雪岩略停一下又说∶『司官推转，还有一条出路就是考御史；当御史更是只要做文章 的差使，王夔石搞不来。而且他也不是什么铁面无情的人；平时惟恐跟人结怨，哪里好当什么都老爷？』『我懂了！』左宗棠说，『王夔石是不愿做京官，只想外放？』『是的。外放做知府；做得好，三两年就可以升道员。』胡雪岩笑笑说道∶『做外官，就要靠督抚了！』这一下，左宗棠一心领神会，彻底明了。因为做外官靠督抚，没有比他更清楚的。清朝的督抚权重，京官外转府道；督抚如果不喜此人，从前可以『才不胜任』的理由，奏请『请京任用』，等于推翻朝旨。乾隆初年，虽曾下诏切责，不准再有这样的事例；可是督抚仍旧有办法可以不使此人到任，或者奏请调职。至于未经指明缺分，只分省候补任用的，补缺的迟早；缺分的优瘠，其权更操之督抚。

In Hu’s view, a capital official who relied on himself rather than connections had two resources: learning or practical ability. Hanlin scholars depended on learning, departmental officers on ability. That meant the capacity to get things done, not some grand theory of government: when a practical difficulty arose, a man must produce a concrete solution, or at least find a way to get past it. This was precisely Wang Wenshao’s strength. Yet in the capital, ability was a less useful resource than learning. A learned official advancing through the usual stages found constant opportunities to display his gifts. A Hanlin scholar could serve as a daily lecturer and recorder of the emperor’s activities, then enter the offices of the heir apparent and rise to senior metropolitan rank. Such officials could submit memorials directly—another opportunity to show their learning. One memorial might catch the emperor’s attention and bring rapid advancement, even a red hat-button within three or four years. Practical ability offered no such convenient path. “Why?” Hu asked. “Because a hero needs a field on which to use his weapons. Departmental officers handle official matters daily and wield considerable power to approve or reject. There is a reason for approval and a reason for rejection. Put the case correctly, and your ability shows. But you cannot remain a departmental officer all your life. Wang Kuishi has been a director for years. If he rises to a fourth-rank metropolitan post, he may go to the Court of State Ceremonial or the Office of Transmission. Those offices are like a deaf man’s ears: without them, the appearance is wrong; with them, they serve no purpose. However great Wang’s abilities, he cannot use them in an idle office with nothing to do.” Hu paused. “There is another route for departmental officers: examination for a censor’s post. But a censor’s work requires literary composition above all, and Wang cannot manage it. Nor is he a stern, impartial man. He always tries to avoid making enemies. How could he be a censor?” “I understand!” Zuo said. “He does not want to remain in the capital. He wants a provincial appointment?” “Yes, as a prefect. If he does well, he can become a circuit intendant in two or three years.” Hu smiled. “A provincial official must rely on governors and governors-general.” Zuo now understood completely. No one knew better than he how dependent provincial officials were on their superiors. Under the Qing, governors and governors-general held great power. If they disliked a capital official appointed as a prefect or intendant, they had formerly been able to declare him unequal to his duties and request his return to the capital—effectively overturning the imperial appointment. Early in the Qianlong reign, an edict had sharply rebuked this practice and forbidden its repetition. Even so, provincial chiefs still had ways to prevent a man from taking up his post or to request his transfer. For officials sent to a province merely to await vacancies, with no specific appointment, the timing and the richness or poverty of the eventual post lay still more completely in their hands.

因此可以想象得到，王文韶如果志在外官，就必得与督抚结缘；而能够设法搞成免办平洪杨的军费报销，正是可遇而不可求的良机。因为这一条，湘港将领，无不感戴；而天下督抚，就眼前来说，两江曾国藩、闽浙是左宗棠自己、江苏李鸿章 、直隶刘长佑、四川路秉章 、湖广官文、河南张之万、江西沈荷桢、湖北严树森、广东郭嵩焘，哪一个都花过大把银子的军费；能够免办报销，个人要见王文韶的情，等他分发到省，岂有不格外照应之理？想到这里，左宗棠心头的一个疙瘩，消减了一半，『王夔石果然是能干的，就得好好抓住这个机会，普结天下督抚之缘。』他又回想了一下胡雪岩的话，发现有件事令人惊异，便即问道∶『雪翁，你到京里去过没有？』

If Wang Wenshao wished to serve outside the capital, he must therefore cultivate them. Securing exemption from the audit of expenditure against the Hong-Yang rebellion was an opportunity that might never come again. Every Xiang and Huai commander would be grateful. Consider the provincial chiefs then in office: Zeng Guofan at Liangjiang, Zuo himself in Fujian and Zhejiang, Li Hongzhang in Jiangsu, Liu Changyou in Zhili, Luo Bingzhang in Sichuan, Guanwen in Huguang, Zhang Zhiwan in Henan, Shen Baozhen in Jiangxi, Yan Shusen in Hubei, and Guo Songtao in Guangdong. Every one had spent great sums on the war. Exemption from an audit would leave each personally obliged to Wang. If he were assigned to their province, how could they fail to favour him? Half the knot in Zuo’s mind loosened. “If Wang is truly capable, he should seize this chance to make friends with governors throughout the empire.” Then he recalled something surprising in Hu’s account. “Have you ever been to Beijing, Mr Xueyan?”

『还不曾过去。』『那就怪了！你没有上过京，又是半官半商，何以倒对京官的推迁升转，如此熟悉？』『我本来也不懂。前年跟王夔石在上海见面，长谈了好几夜；都是听他说的。』『原来如此！不过能说得清源流，也很难得的了。』左宗棠又问∶『你跟王夔石很熟？』『是的。』胡雪岩又说，『不过并无深交。』『看你们谈得倒很深。』『有利害关系，谈得就深了；交情又另是一回事。王夔石没有什么才气，也没有什么大志，做人太圆滑，未免欠诚恳。我不喜欢这个人。』左宗棠觉得胡雪岩这几句话，颇对自己的胃口；同时对他的本性，也更为了解，确是个可以论大事、共患难的人。因而不断点头，表示心许。

“Not yet.” “That is strange. You have never visited the capital and are half official, half merchant. How do you know so much about promotion through the metropolitan service?” “Originally I knew nothing. I met Wang Kuishi in Shanghai the year before last, and we talked for several nights. I heard it all from him.” “So that is it. Still, to explain the whole course of it so clearly is no small achievement. Do you know him well?” “Yes, though we are not close friends.” “Your conversations seem to have gone deeply into things.” “Common interests can make a discussion profound. Friendship is another matter. Wang has no great brilliance or ambition. He is so smooth in dealing with people that he lacks sincerity. I do not like him.” These words suited Zuo’s taste and gave him a clearer understanding of Hu’s nature. Here was a man with whom he could discuss great matters and share adversity. He nodded repeatedly in silent approval.

『大人的意思是，』胡雪岩问道∶『让我写封信给王夔石，请他从中尽力？』『是的。我有这个意思。不过，我怕他一个人的力量不够；四处去瞎撞木钟，搞得满城风雨，无益有害。』『他一个的力量，诚然不够；不过事情的轻重，他是识得的。他的本性也是谨慎小心一路，决不致于飞扬浮躁，到处瞎说。大人这样说，我信上格外关照，叫他秘密就是。』『能这样最好。』说到这里，左宗棠向左右吩咐∶『拿「缙绅」来！』缙绅是京师书坊刻的一部职官录，全名叫做『大清缙绅全书』。由『宗人府』开始，一直到各省的佐杂官儿，从亲王到未入流，凡是有职衔的，无不有简历记载。左宗棠索取缙绅，是要查户部的职官。翻到『户部衙门』这一栏，头一行是『文渊阁大学士管理户部事务倭仁』。左宗棠顿时喜孜孜地说∶『行了！此事可望有成。』

“Then Your Excellency would like me to write to Wang and ask him to do what he can?” “Yes. But I fear he cannot accomplish it alone. If he goes knocking at every door and stirs up a public commotion, he will do harm rather than good.” “His own strength is indeed insufficient, but he knows what is at stake. He is cautious by nature, not a flighty man who will talk recklessly everywhere. Since you mention it, I shall particularly stress secrecy.” “That would be best.” Zuo turned to his attendants. “Bring the Official Register.” This was a directory printed by Beijing booksellers, its full title the Complete Register of Officials of the Great Qing. Beginning with the Imperial Clan Court and continuing down to the subordinate and miscellaneous officials in every province, it gave brief records of every holder of an official title, from princes to men below the ranked service. Zuo wanted to examine the Ministry of Revenue. At the head of that section he found: “Woren, Grand Secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion, supervising the affairs of the Ministry of Revenue.” His face brightened immediately. “Good! There is hope of success.”

『喔，』胡雪岩问道∶『大人参透了什么消息？』『这倭相辊蒙古人。他家一直驻防开封；所以跟河南人没有什么两样。河南是讲理学的地方，这倭相国规行矩步，虽然有点迂，倒是不折不扣的道学先生；先帝对此人颇为看重，所以两宫太后亦很尊敬他，能得此老出头说话，事无不成之理。』『那末，』胡雪岩问道∶『这话可以不可以跟王夔石说？』『这些情形，王夔石比我们清楚得多。说亦可、不说亦可。』左宗棠又说，『这倭相国与曾相会试同榜；想来他亦肯帮帮老同年的忙的。』『既然如此，何不由大人写封信给曾相；结结实实托一托倭中堂？』『这也是一法。我怕曾相亦在道学气，未见得肯写这样的信。』『是！』胡雪岩口里答应着，心中另有盘算。兹事体大，而不与自己相干。甚至左宗棠亦不必太关切；天塌下来有长人顶，曾氏弟兄所支销的军费比左宗棠所经手的，多过好几倍；要办军费报销，曾氏弟兄，首当其冲，自然会设法疏通化解。如今自己替左宗棠出主意，不须太起劲；不求有功，先求无过，最为上策。

“Oh?” Hu asked. “What have you discovered?” “Grand Secretary Woren is a Mongol, but his family has long served in the Kaifeng garrison, so he is much like a Henan man. Henan is a home of Neo-Confucian learning. He follows every rule and convention; a little pedantic, perhaps, but an orthodox moralist through and through. The late emperor valued him highly, and both empress dowagers respect him. If we can get the old gentleman to speak, the matter cannot fail.” “May I tell Wang Kuishi this?” “He knows these circumstances far better than we do. You may tell him or leave it unsaid.” Zuo added, “Woren and Zeng passed the metropolitan examination in the same year. Presumably he would help an old examination colleague.” “Then why not write to Grand Secretary Zeng and ask him to make a firm appeal to Woren?” “That is one method. But I fear Zeng may be too full of moral propriety to write such a letter.” “Yes,” Hu said, while thinking differently. The affair was important, but did not concern him personally. Even Zuo need not be so anxious. If the sky fell, the tallest men would hold it up. The Zeng brothers had spent several times as much on the war as Zuo. An audit would strike them first, and they would naturally seek a way to resolve the difficulty. In advising Zuo, Hu need not be overzealous. Better to avoid mistakes before trying to win credit.

这样转念，步子便踏得更稳了，『为求妥当，我看莫如这么办，先写信透露给王夔石，问问他的意思，看看能不能做得到？要做，如何着手；请他写个节略来！』『这样做再好都没有。可是，』左宗棠怀疑地问，『他肯吗？』『一定肯！我有交情放给他。』『你不是说∶你们没有深交吗？』『放交情』是句江湖上的话，与深交有别，左宗棠不懂这句话，胡雪岩便只好解释∶『我是说，王夔石欠下我一个人情在那里；所以我托他点事，他一定不会怕麻烦。』『那就是了。此事能办成功，与你也有好处；曾相、李少荃都要见你的情。』说罢，左宗棠哈哈一笑。这一笑便有些莫测高深了。胡雪岩心想，大家都说此公好作英雄欺人之谈；当然也喜欢用权术。他说这话，又打这么一个莫名其妙的哈哈，莫非有什么试探之意在内？继而转念，不管他是不是试探？自己正不妨借此机会，表明心迹，因而正色说道∶『大人！我跟王夔石不同，王夔石是想做官上头飞黄腾达；我是想做大生意。因为自己照照镜子，不象做官的材料。所以曾相跟李中函见不见我的情，我毫不在乎；他们见我的情，我亦不会去巴结他们的。如今，我倒是只巴结一个人！』谈到这里，他有意停了下来，要看左宗棠是何反应？左宗棠当然要问；而且是很关切地问∶『巴结谁？』『还有谁？自然是大人。』胡雪岩说，『我巴结大人，不是想做官，是报答。第一、大人是我们浙江的救星，尤其是克复了杭州；饮水思源，想到我今天能回家乡；王雪公地下有知，可以瞑目，不能不感激大人。第二、承蒙大人看得起我，一见就赏识，所谓「士为知己者死」不巴结大人巴结谁？』『言重，言重！你老哥太捧我了。』左宗棠笑容满面地回答。

This reflection made him more cautious. “For safety’s sake, we might first write to Wang, tell him the idea and ask his opinion. Can it be done? If so, how should we begin? Let him send a brief outline.” “Nothing could be better. But will he agree?” “Certainly. I have a favour on account with him.” “Did you not say you were not close friends?” Having a “favour on account” was an expression from the world outside official circles, and did not mean close friendship. Zuo did not understand it, so Hu explained. “I mean that Wang owes me a favour. If I ask him to do something, he will not begrudge the trouble.” “Then that settles it. Success will benefit you as well. Zeng and Li Shaoquan will both be obliged to you.” Zuo laughed heartily. There was something unfathomable in the laugh. People said this man liked to assert his own heroic superiority; naturally, he liked political manoeuvring too. Was he testing Hu with that remark and inexplicable laughter? On reflection, whether it was a test or not, Hu could use the opportunity to declare his intentions. He answered seriously. “Your Excellency, I am different from Wang Kuishi. He seeks rapid advancement in office. I want to do business on a grand scale. When I look at myself in the mirror, I do not see the makings of an official. I therefore care nothing whether Grand Secretary Zeng or Governor Li feels obliged to me. Even if they do, I shall not seek their favour. There is only one man whose favour I seek.” He paused deliberately to see Zuo’s response. Zuo naturally asked, with great interest, “Whose?” “Whose else but Your Excellency’s? I seek your favour not to obtain office, but out of gratitude. First, you have saved Zhejiang, above all by recovering Hangzhou. When drinking water, one remembers its source. I can return home today, and Governor Wang’s spirit can rest in peace. How could I not be grateful? Second, you have thought well of me and recognised my worth at our first meeting. ‘A man will die for one who understands him.’ Whose favour should I seek if not yours?” “Too much, too much! My dear fellow, you flatter me excessively,” Zuo replied, his face wreathed in smiles.

翻译与核验：
1. 军费矛盾忠实保留：部费至少2%；左称经手70,000,000两，随后称按例需200,000两。按2%实际应为1,400,000两，源文显有数额矛盾，不能静默改为10,000,000或1,400,000。另20,000两是书办筹集的启动本钱，与上述不混。
2. 军费报销译 accounting/audit，指支出凭证造册核销，并非财政部事后把全军款报销返还。部费为小说官场陋规，不写成正式合法费率。
3. 左称地方自筹所以户部无权要账，叙述者马上反驳仍属公款；明确保留批评，未把左观点当成译者判断。
4. 王文韶与王夔石同人；曾相=曾国藩，曾九=曾国荃，李少荃=李鸿章。王需外放而要结督抚缘、先问可行性再索节略、胡仅在人情上有债而非深交，三层区别保留。
5. 3753单源长段包含对话与清朝督抚用人制度，英文整段均归3749–3753范围，不能把制度说明错配3755。所有篇章转接已逐行核验。
6. 3755人名明显讹字“路秉章”“沈荷桢”按骆秉章 Luo Bingzhang、沈葆桢 Shen Baozhen读；“湘港将领”按湘淮。其余名单完整：Zeng Guofan、Zuo Zongtang、Li Hongzhang、Liu Changyou、Guanwen、Zhang Zhiwan、Yan Shusen、Guo Songtao。
7. “琉璃几家纸店”似琉璃厂脱字，保守译 in Liuli，不擅增厂字。“壹/辊/雷翁/李中函”等明显讹字分别依上下文处理为相应连词、系、雪翁、李中丞。
8. 皇权与官场评价、对李让曾独功的揣测、对洪杨败亡时点推断均保留为人物观点；本批无安全改写。
