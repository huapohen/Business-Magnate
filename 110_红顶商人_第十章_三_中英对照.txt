胡雪岩｜中英对照版
原著：高阳

红顶商人｜The Red-Topped Merchant

第十章（本章第3部分，共3部分）

阅读说明：每段中文后接对应英文；两版英文正文一致。下载站水印已清除，原始疑字在文末说明。

加官自是胡雪岩所希望的；不过，使他特别兴奋的，还不在布政使这个衔头，而加了布政使衔，便可改换顶戴。原衔按察使——臬司是正三品，戴的是亮蓝顶子；布政使——藩司是从二品，便可以戴红顶子了。捐班出身的官儿，戴到红顶子，极不容易；买卖人戴红顶子，更是绝无仅有的事；除非象乾隆年间的盐商那样出自特恩，但亦只有一两个人。是故饮水思源，想起将有得戴的红顶子，虽出自左宗棠的保荐；但没有王有龄，何有今日？因而又特地到王有龄的老家去了一趟——赡恤王氏遗属，是胡雪岩逢年过节的第一件大事；这次登门，完全是感念旧情，哭奠一番。本来还想亲谒墓门，无奈有件大事在办，忙得不可开交；只好等公事完了再说。

Advancement was naturally what Hu desired. Yet what particularly excited him was not the title of provincial treasurer itself, but the change of hat insignia it permitted. His existing honorary rank, judicial commissioner, belonged to the upper third grade and carried a bright blue finial. Provincial treasurer belonged to the lower second grade: he could wear a red one. For a man who had entered officialdom by purchase, attaining a red finial was extremely difficult. For a merchant, it was almost unheard of, apart from one or two salt merchants specially favoured in the Qianlong reign. Remembering the source of his good fortune, Hu reflected that, although Zuo had recommended the honour he was about to receive, without Wang Youling there could have been no present success. He therefore made a special visit to Wang’s ancestral home. Providing for Wang’s surviving family was his first concern at every festival and New Year; this visit was solely to remember the old friendship, weep, and offer sacrifice. He had hoped to visit the grave as well, but an important undertaking kept him desperately busy. That would have to wait until the official business was finished.

这件大事就是打算自己造轮船。左宗棠的意志强毅，蓄志之事，非见诸实行，不能甘心。当时奉命入闽督师，不能躬亲料理，却并未搁下，委托了一个他最信任的人，就是胡雪岩。有关跟洋人打交道的事，胡雪岩必求救于古应春；他的路子很广，认为造轮船不必找日意格、德克碑。方今泰西各国，讲到轮船、铁路、火器的粮良，美国有后来居上之势。同时美国人不似英国人的狡猾、法国人的蛮横、德国人的顽固、日本人的阴险，比较易于相处。可是胡雪岩另有看法，外国在华势力，英国最大，法国其次。要制抑英国的势力，只有利用法国；美国与英国同种，所以与美国合作，等于帮助英国扩张势力。同时，日意格与德克碑是原始创议之人，无故背弃，道义有亏。

The undertaking was the plan to build steamships themselves. Zuo possessed an iron determination: once he had set his mind on something, he could not rest until it was put into practice. His orders to direct the Fujian campaign had prevented personal attention to the project, but he had not abandoned it. He entrusted it to the man he most trusted, Hu. In dealings with foreigners, Hu invariably sought Gu’s help. Gu had wide connections and saw no need to employ Giquel and d’Aiguebelle. In steamships, railways, and firearms, he thought, the United States was catching up with and surpassing the older Western powers. Americans also seemed easier to deal with: in his view, they lacked the cunning of the British, the arrogance of the French, the stubbornness of the Germans, and the deviousness of the Japanese. Hu thought differently. Britain possessed the greatest foreign influence in China, France the next. Only France could be used to check Britain. Americans and British were of the same stock, he reasoned, so cooperation with Americans would in effect help British expansion. Moreover, Giquel and d’Aiguebelle had originated the proposal. To abandon them without cause would be dishonourable.

其实胡雪岩还有一层没有说出来的意思；古应春与他多年相处，亦能揣摩得到——左宗棠与李鸿章 争权夺利，几已成不两立之势，李鸿章 办洋务，倚总税务局英国人赫德为重；然则左宗棠如果再请教英国人，将会逃不了仍由赫德经手。而赫德与李鸿章 互为表里，说不定会向总洋务的恭王与文建建议，制造轮船事务以由两江经办为宜。那一来岂不是给李鸿章 开了路？因此，古应春不再有何主张，只实心实力地作胡雪岩跟日意格、德克碑打交道的助手——实际上只跟日意格一个人接头；因为德克碑已经退伍回国了。一切建船厂的计划、图样及瞀，都由德克碑在法国托人办理，寄给日意格，再找胡雪岩、古应春洽谈；一年多下来，已经策划得很周祥了。到得左宗棠由广东班师，胡雪岩立即陪着日意格到了福州；左宗棠一看图说详明，非常高兴，亲自去视察日意格所建议的设厂之地；地在福建海口、马尾罗星塔一带，水清土实，宜于开槽建坞。兼以密迩省城，稽察方便，所以一看便即中意。

There was another reason Hu did not mention, though Gu, after their years together, could infer it. Zuo’s rivalry with Li over power and advantage had almost become irreconcilable. Li’s dealings with foreigners relied heavily upon the British Inspector General of Customs, Hart. If Zuo consulted the British too, the matter would probably pass through Hart’s hands. Hart and Li supported one another; Hart might well advise Prince Gong and Wenxiang, who directed foreign affairs, that steamship construction should be entrusted to Liangjiang. Would that not open the way for Li? Gu therefore offered no further proposal and devoted himself to assisting Hu in negotiations with Giquel and d’Aiguebelle. In practice, they dealt only with Giquel, since d’Aiguebelle had left military service and returned to France. He arranged there for the shipyard plans, drawings, and associated material to be prepared and sent to Giquel, who discussed them with Hu and Gu. After more than a year, the scheme had been worked out in considerable detail. When Zuo returned from Guangdong, Hu immediately accompanied Giquel to Fuzhou. The clarity of the drawings and explanations delighted Zuo. He personally inspected the proposed site near Luoxing Pagoda at Mawei, by the approach to Fujian’s coastal waters. Clear water and firm ground made it suitable for slipways and docks, while proximity to the provincial capital made supervision convenient. He approved it as soon as he saw it.

剩下来的事，就是筹划经费。造厂买机器、雇募师匠，瞀开办费要三十多万银子，厂成开工，材料薪水，每月须银五六万两，一年就是六、七十万，预计两年以后造出第一艘船，要花下去一百五十万银子。不过以后就可以省了，五年通计，不过三百多万。这三百多万银子，从何筹集？当然煞费周章 ；左宗棠的意思是先办起来再说，只要有一百万银子，能应付得了头一年，此后欲罢不能，不愁朝廷拿不出办法，好在有胡雪岩，一定可以想出一条维持得下的路子来。因而粗粗计算，福建海关及本省厘税，提用之权在自己手里；浙江分属自己管辖，不会袖手；广东蒋益澧是自己一手提拔，更当效劳。有此三处财源，尽可放手办事了。

The remaining problem was finance. Building the yard, purchasing machinery, and hiring foreign specialists would require more than three hundred thousand taels in initial expenditure. Once production began, materials and wages would cost fifty or sixty thousand a month, or six to seven hundred thousand a year. The first ship was expected after two years, by which time one and a half million taels would have been spent. Thereafter costs would fall: the total for five years was estimated at a little over three million. Raising that sum would naturally be troublesome. Zuo’s idea was to begin first. A million taels would carry the project through its first year. Once it had gone too far to abandon, the court would have to find a means of continuing it. And with Hu at hand, some way to sustain it could surely be devised. Zuo made a rough calculation. He controlled the allocation of Fujian Customs revenue and provincial likin. Zhejiang lay within his jurisdiction and would not stand aside. Jiang in Guangdong owed his advancement entirely to Zuo and should contribute all the more readily. With those three sources, the undertaking could proceed boldly.

因此，左宗棠在五月中旬，便先奏陈『拟购机器，雇洋匠，试造轮船大概情形』。同时应诏陈言，以为剿捻宜用车战；平回则千里馈粮，转运艰难，应该采用屯田之策。复旨对车战、屯田之议，不见得欣赏；试造轮船则以为『实系当今应办急务』，所需经费，准予在闽海关关税中酌量提用；如果不够，准再提用福建厘金。同时指示∶『所陈各条，均着照议办理；一切未尽事宜，仍着详悉议奏。』有此一旨，左宗棠便密锣紧鼓地干了起来，一面关照胡雪岩通过已调汉口江汉关税务司的日意格，与在安南的德克碑，商酌一切细节。日意格是七月初，冒暑到达福州的。第一件事是勘察船厂地址，择定马尾山下，潮平之时水深亦达十二丈的地方设厂；然后议土木、议工匠、议经费，大致妥协，订立草约，担保人照胡雪岩的建议，由法国驻上海的总领事白来尼担保。当然，这个差使必然又落在胡雪岩肩上。

In the middle of the fifth month, Zuo submitted an initial account of the proposed purchase of machinery, recruitment of foreign artisans, and experimental construction of steamships. At the same time, responding to a request for opinions, he recommended wagon warfare against the Nian rebels and military agricultural colonies for the campaign against the Hui, where provisions otherwise had to be transported over immense distances. The court’s reply showed little enthusiasm for the wagons or the agricultural proposal. Steamship construction, however, was declared an urgent necessity of the day. Funds could be drawn in suitable amounts from Fujian Customs, with provincial likin available if those proved insufficient. The proposed measures were approved, and Zuo was instructed to submit detailed further memorials on matters not yet settled. With that authority, he set to work vigorously. He instructed Hu to negotiate the details with d’Aiguebelle, now in Annam, through Giquel, who had been transferred to the Jianghan Customs at Hankou. Giquel travelled through the summer heat and reached Fuzhou early in the seventh month. His first task was to survey the site. He selected ground below Mount Mawei, where the water reached twelve zhang in depth when the tide stood level. They then discussed construction, workmen, and finance. With broad agreement reached, they drew up a preliminary contract. At Hu’s suggestion, the French consul-general in Shanghai, Brenier, was to act as guarantor. Arranging that naturally fell to Hu as well.

到了八月下旬德克碑直接由安南到达福州，与左宗棠晤见之下，对于所订草约，并无异词，但对所选定的建厂地点，却有意见，认为马尾山下是淤沙积成的一块陆地，基址不够坚固。因而左宗棠决定邀请白来尼、日意格到福州作客，作一个最后的，也是全面的商议，作成定案，正式出奏。主意既定，先写信找胡雪岩到福州来谈。正在起劲的时候，忽然奉到调督陕甘的上谕；在左宗棠虽觉突兀，但稍一细想，便知事所必然，势所必至，并非全出意外。同时想起历史上许多平定西域的史实，雄心陡起，跃跃欲试，相当兴奋。在胡雪岩却是件非常扫兴的事，而且忧心忡忡，颇有手足无措之感。因此，到总督衙门向左宗棠道贺时，虽然表面从容，一切如常；但逃不过相知较深的人的眼光。其中有一个是他的小同乡吴观礼。此人字子儒号圭庵，本来是一名举人；才气纵横，做得极好的诗。由于胡雪岩的推荐，入左宗棠幕府，深得信任，担任总理营务处的职司，是闽浙总督衙门唯一参赞军务，可说是运筹帷幄的一位幕友。

Late in the eighth month, d’Aiguebelle came directly from Annam to Fuzhou. On meeting Zuo, he raised no objection to the preliminary contract, but disputed the site. The ground below Mount Mawei, he said, had formed from deposited silt and was not firm enough for foundations. Zuo decided to invite Brenier and Giquel to Fuzhou for a final, comprehensive discussion, after which the plan could be settled and formally submitted. He first wrote asking Hu to come. Just as his enthusiasm was at its height, the edict transferring him to Shaanxi and Gansu arrived. He found it abrupt, but a little reflection showed it to be a natural outcome of events, not wholly unexpected. Recalling the many historical campaigns that had pacified the Western Regions, he felt his ambition rise and was eager for the challenge. Hu, however, was deeply disappointed and anxious, almost at a loss. When he called at the governor-general’s office to offer congratulations, his outward composure seemed unchanged, but those who knew him well could see beneath it. One was his fellow townsman Wu Guanli, courtesy name Ziru and literary name Gui’an. A provincial examination graduate, Wu was brilliantly gifted and wrote excellent poetry. Hu had recommended him for Zuo’s staff, where he had won profound trust and become head of the military affairs office. He was the one secretary in the governor-general’s establishment who participated in military strategy, a counsellor directing events from behind the scenes.

吴观礼对左宗棠所了解的，是胡雪岩所不能了解的，这就因为是读书多少的缘故。看到胡雪岩的眉宇之间有落寞之色，当然也就猜想得到他内心的想法。『雪岩，』吴观礼问道，『你是不是怕左公一去西北，你失掉靠山？』话问得很率直，胡雪岩也就老实答道∶『是的！以后无论公私，我都难了！』『不然！不然！』吴观礼大为摇头。照吴观礼的看法，出关西征，总得三年五载，才能见功；这当然是一次大征伐，但情势与剿捻不同。捻匪窜扰中原，威胁京畿，在朝廷看，纵非心腹之患，但患在肘腋，不除不能安心；所以督兵大臣，必得克日收功。事势急迫，不容延误。西征则在边陲用兵，天高皇帝远，不致于朝夕关怀，其势较缓，公事自然比较好办。至于私事，无非胡雪岩个人的事业，有近在东南的左宗棠，可资荫庇，处处圆通。一旦靠山领兵出关，远在西陲，鞭长莫及；缓急之际呼应为难。吴观礼认为亦是过虑。『你要晓得，从来经营西北，全靠东南支持；此后你在上海的差使，会更加吃重，地位也就更非昔比。事在人为。』吴观礼拍拍胡雪岩的肩说，『你没有读过「圣武记」，不知道乾隆年间的「十大武功」。经营边疆，从前都是派亲贵或者满洲重臣挂帅；如今派了我们左公，是件非同小可的事。洪杨以来的元戎勋臣，曾相高高在上；左李两位两次；从此以后，只怕曾左要并称了。』最后一句话，点醒了胡雪岩；满腔忧烦，顿时一扫而空。靠山虽远，却更高大稳固；了解到这一层，就不必发什么愁了。

Wu understood aspects of Zuo that Hu could not, a difference arising from the extent of their reading. Seeing dejection in Hu’s face, he readily guessed his thoughts. “Xueyan, are you afraid that, once Lord Zuo goes northwest, you will lose your patron?” The question was direct, and Hu answered honestly. “Yes. Both public and private business will be difficult for me.” Wu shook his head vigorously. “Not at all!” In his view, a western expedition beyond the passes would take three to five years to show results. It was a major campaign, certainly, but differed from the struggle against the Nian, who roamed the central plains and threatened the approaches to the capital. Even if not a disease at the heart, they were a danger close at hand. The court could not rest until they were removed, and commanders had to achieve success within a fixed time. No delay could be tolerated. The western war, by contrast, would be fought on a distant frontier, beyond the court’s anxious attention morning and evening. With less immediate pressure, official business would be easier. As for private matters, these meant Hu’s own enterprises. A patron nearby in the southeast could protect him and smooth his path; one far beyond the passes might be difficult to reach in an emergency. But Wu thought that fear excessive too. “You must understand that campaigns in the northwest have always depended upon support from the southeast. Your duties in Shanghai will grow more important, and your standing with them. What happens depends on what a man makes of it.” He patted Hu’s shoulder. “You have not read the Record of Imperial Military Achievements and do not know Qianlong’s Ten Great Campaigns. In the past, frontier enterprises were entrusted to imperial relatives or great Manchu officials. Giving one to our Lord Zuo is no ordinary matter. Among the great commanders distinguished since the Taiping uprising, Lord Zeng has stood above the rest, with Zuo and Li next. From now on, Zeng and Zuo may well be spoken of together.” That last sentence awakened Hu. His anxiety vanished. His patron would be farther away, but greater and more firmly established. Once he understood that, there was no reason to fret.

『多承指点。』胡雪岩很高兴地说，『索性还要费你的心，西北是怎么个情形，请你细细谈一谈。』『我们先谈谈造轮船。』左宗棠极坚决地说∶『不管朝廷催得怎么紧，要我赶出关；这件事非在我手里先定了局，我不会离开福建。』『是的。』胡雪岩问道∶『定局以后，交给哪位？』『着！你问在要害上了。我蓄志三年，辛苦数月，才能有此结果，倘或付托非人，半途而废，我是不甘心的。这一层，我还在考虑；眼前还要请你多偏劳。』『那何消说得。不过，我亦只能管到大人离福建为止。』『不然。我离开福建，你还是要管。』左宗棠说，『管的是船厂。这件事我决不能半途而废，为李少荃所笑。而且我不知道盘算过多少次，这件事办成，比李少荃所办的洋务，不知道要好过多少倍。』这就很明白的了，左宗棠是出于争胜之心。他的好胜心是决不因任何的规劝而稍减的；胡雪岩知道自己难卸仔肩，非『顶石臼做戏』不可了。不过，刚才那句『问在要害』上的话，并无答复，还得追问。『大人这么说，我当然只有遵命。』胡雪岩说，『就不知道将来在福建还要伺候哪位？』『不要说什么伺候的话。雪岩，你最聪明不过；没有什么你不能相处的。惟其我付托了这个人，更得借重你——。』左宗棠没有再说下去∶胡雪岩却完全懂了他的意思，他所付托的，是个很难「伺候」的人。这就更急着要问∶『是哪位？』『沈幼丹。』

“Thank you for enlightening me,” Hu said happily. “Let me trouble you further. Please explain the situation in the northwest in detail.” “First let us discuss the steamships,” Zuo said firmly. “However urgently the court presses me to go beyond the passes, I shall not leave Fujian until this undertaking has been settled under my own hand.” “Yes, sir,” Hu replied. “Once it is settled, to whom will you entrust it?” “Exactly! You have reached the crucial point. I have cherished this ambition for three years and worked hard for months to bring it this far. If I entrust it to the wrong man and it is abandoned halfway, I shall never be reconciled to it. I am still considering that. For the present, I must ask you to take more trouble.” “That goes without saying. But I can only attend to it until you leave Fujian.” “No. You must continue after I leave,” Zuo said. “The shipyard will remain your concern. I will never abandon this halfway and give Li Shaoquan occasion to laugh. I have considered it countless times: if it succeeds, it will surpass his foreign undertakings many times over.” His competitive motive was plain. No advice could diminish his desire to excel. Hu knew that he could not escape the burden; he must perform his part, however crushing the weight upon his head. Yet the crucial question remained unanswered, and he had to ask again. “Then I can only obey. But whom shall I have to serve in Fujian?” “Do not speak of serving. Xueyan, you are exceptionally clever. There is no one you cannot get on with. It is precisely because I shall entrust the work to this man that I need you still more—” He broke off. Hu understood perfectly: the chosen man would be difficult to serve. He was all the more eager to know. “Who is it?” “Shen Youdan.”

原来是丁忧回籍守制的前任江西巡抚沈葆桢。这在胡雪岩却真有意外之感。细想一想，付托倒也得人；不过以本省人做本省官，而且必是大官，为法例所不许。兼以丁忧，更成窒碍。不知左宗棠是怎么想来的？他只有付之默然了。『我知道你的想法，我给你看个奏稿。』奏向洋洋千言，畅论造船之利；最后谈到主题∶臣维轮船一事，势在必行，岂可以去闽在迩，忽为搁置？且设局制造，一切繁难事宜，均臣与洋员议定，若不趁臣在闽定局，不但头绪纷繁，接办之人无从咨防；且恐要约不明，后多民议，臣尤无可诿咎。臣之不能不稍留三旬，以待此局之定者，此也！惟此事固须择接办之人；尤必接办之人能久于其事，然后一气贯注，众志定而成功可期；亦研求深而事理愈熟。再四思维，惟丁忧在籍前江西抚臣沈荷桢，在官在籍，久负清望，为中外所仰。其虑事详审精密，早在圣有洞鉴之中。现在里居侍养，爱日方长，非若宦辙靡常，时有量移更替之事；又乡评素重，更可坚乐事赴功之心。若令主持此事，必期就绪。商之英桂、徐宗余亦以为然。臣曾三次造庐商情，沈荷桢始终逊谢不遑。可否仰恳皇上天恩，俯念事关至要，局在垂成，温谕沈葆桢，勉轻大义，特命总理船政，由部颁发关防，凡事涉船政，由其专奏请旨，以防牵制。

It was Shen Baozhen, the former governor of Jiangxi, now at home observing mourning. Hu was genuinely surprised. On reflection, the choice was sound, but the regulations forbade a man to serve as an official—particularly a high official—in his native province. Mourning created a further obstacle. What was Zuo’s intention? Hu remained silent. “I know what you are thinking. Let me show you a draft memorial.” The lengthy document argued the advantages of shipbuilding before reaching its principal point. The undertaking was imperative, it said; Zuo’s approaching departure from Fujian could not justify setting it aside. He and the foreign officers had negotiated the many complicated arrangements for establishing the works. Unless these were settled while he remained, his successor would face tangled affairs without anyone to consult; unclear agreements might also cause later controversy for which Zuo could not evade responsibility. He therefore had to remain another thirty days to bring the arrangements to completion. A successor must be chosen, but above all he must be able to remain at the work for a long time. Continuity would steady everyone’s purpose, make success attainable, and allow deeper study to bring greater mastery. After repeated consideration, Zuo could think only of Shen Baozhen. In office and at home, Shen had long enjoyed a reputation for integrity admired throughout the realm. His careful, exact judgment was already known to the emperor. At present he was at home attending to his family and might remain there for many years, unlike officials liable to frequent transfer. His high local reputation would strengthen his commitment to the enterprise. If given charge, he would certainly see it established. Yinggui and Xu Zonggan agreed. Zuo had visited him three times to discuss the matter, but Shen had repeatedly declined. Might the emperor, considering the project’s importance and near completion, graciously urge him to accept the public duty and appoint him director-general of shipbuilding? An official seal should be issued by the appropriate board, and he should have authority to memorialize directly on all shipbuilding matters, preventing obstruction by others.

其经费一切，会商将军督抚随时调取；责成署藩司周开锡，不得稍有延误。一切工料及延洋匠、雇华工、开艺局，责成胡光墉一手经理。缘胡光墉才长心细，熟谙洋务，为船局断不可少之人，且为洋人所素信也。『好！我就交给你了！』左宗棠站起身，一面走向书案，一面说道∶『现在要跟你谈第一件大事了！』

The necessary funds would be obtained as required in consultation with the general, governor-general, and governor. Acting Provincial Treasurer Zhou Kaixi was to ensure that there was no delay. Hu Guangyong was to manage all materials, the engagement of foreign artisans and Chinese workers, and the opening of the technical school. Hu, the memorial explained, was capable, meticulous, experienced in foreign affairs, indispensable to the shipbuilding establishment, and already trusted by the foreigners. “Good! I entrust it to you.” Zuo rose and moved towards his writing table. “Now we must discuss the first great task.”

译文说明
1. 按察使正三品、亮蓝顶；布政使从二品、红顶。均为授衔及服饰资格，不等于胡已实际出任布政使。对未来红顶仍保留 about to receive/可以取得的表述。
2. 日意格采用 Giquel；外交部门资料明确作 Prosper Giquel：https://fr.china-embassy.gov.cn/chn/sgxw/202407/t20240705_11448787.htm 。德克碑沿用 d’Aiguebelle；白来尼作 Brenier，上海总领事身份依本文，未把个人担保夸大为法国政府已全面承保项目。
3. Gu、Hu对各国人的评价是人物当时的观念，英文保留 he thought/in his view/he reasoned 等归属，不作译者观点。赫德、李鸿章可能干预是胡未明说、古推知的顾虑，未写成已经发生。
4. 开办费三十多万、月费五六万、年费六七十万、两年出第一艘时累计一百五十万、五年合计三百多万均是预估，保留原文约数。先有一百万撑第一年是左的筹划，不是钱已到位。
5. “潮平”不擅定现代测潮标准，按原文作 when the tide stood level，水深单位保留 zhang。“图样及瞀”的末字不明，以 associated material 表示不明类别的附属材料，未编出具体设备清单。
6. 原4539行由胡与吴观礼交谈，突然转成左宗棠说话，底本未交代场景过渡；译文保持这一跳接，不虚构“次日”“另赴书房”等情节。“沈荷桢／徐宗余／文建”依明确上下文统一为 Shen Baozhen、Xu Zonggan、Wenxiang。
7. 沈幼丹即沈葆桢。三访、婉拒与奏请任命依原文顺序保留；奏稿中的将来安排不写成已正式获准。

阅读与衔接
“There was another reason Hu did not mention”引出未公开动机。读者须区分对话中可见理由与叙述者补充的政治考虑。
“His patron would be farther away, but greater and more firmly established”概括吴的论证如何改变胡的看法：地理距离与政治支持强度是两个不同维度。

翻译与表达
“entrust something to someone”用于托付；“continuity”概括久任和一气贯注；“see an undertaking through”可表达将事情办到底。章节中的限定、转折与因果关系比单独记住生词更影响理解。
