胡雪岩｜中英对照版
原著：高阳

红顶商人｜The Red-Topped Merchant

第十一章（本章第2部分，共2部分）

阅读说明：每段中文后接对应英文；两版英文正文一致。下载站水印已清除，原始疑字在文末说明。

所苦的是眼前还脱不得身，因为日意格、德克碑与中国官场打交道，大至船厂计划，小至个人生活，都要找他接头。在左宗棠，对洋人疑信参半；而有些话怕一说出来，洋人憨直，当场驳回，未免伤他的身分与威望，因而亦少不得胡雪岩这样一个居间曲曲转达的人。这就难了！左思右想，一时竟无以为答；坐在那里大大发楞。这是左宗棠从未见过的样子，不免诧异；却又不好问得。主宾二人，默然相答；使得侍立堂下的戈什哈亦惊愕不止，因为平日总见左宗棠与胡雪岩见了面，谈笑风生，滔滔不绝，何以此刻对坐发呆？于是，有个左宗棠亲信的戈什哈上前问道∶『可是留胡大人在这里便饭？』

The difficulty was that Hu could not yet get away. In their dealings with Chinese officials, Giquel and d’Aiguebelle came to him about everything, from the shipyard scheme to the smallest details of their daily lives. Zuo, for his part, was divided between trust and suspicion of foreigners. He also feared that, if he said certain things himself, their bluntness might lead them to contradict him outright, injuring his dignity and authority. An intermediary such as Hu, who could convey his meaning tactfully, was indispensable. It was a predicament. Hu turned it over in his mind without finding an answer and sat lost in thought. Zuo had never seen him like this. He was surprised, but could hardly ask what was wrong. Host and guest sat facing each other in silence. Even the guards below the hall were astonished: normally these two would be laughing and talking without pause. Why were they now staring blankly at each other? At last a trusted guard approached. “Will His Excellency Hu be staying for a simple meal?”

这下使胡雪岩惊醒了，『不，不，多谢！』他首先辞谢，『我还要到码头去送客。』『送什么人？』左宗棠问。『福州税务局布浪。』『喔，他到上海去。』『是的。』胡雪岩答说，『是驻上海的法国总领事白来尼找他谈公事。』『谈什么公事？』左宗棠问道∶『莫非与船厂有关？』胡雪岩灵机一动，点点头答说∶『也许。』

Hu came to himself. “No, no, thank you,” he said at once. “I must go to the wharf to see someone off.” “Who is it?” Zuo asked. “Brown, the Fuzhou Customs Commissioner.” “Ah, he is going to Shanghai.” “Yes. Brenier, the French Consul General in Shanghai, has asked to see him on official business.” “What business? Could it concern the shipyard?” A thought flashed through Hu’s mind. He nodded. “It might.”

『那可得当心。』左宗棠说，『洋人花样多。日意格、德克碑办理此事，起先越过他们总领事，直接回国接头；白来尼当然不高兴。而此刻一切合同，又非白来尼画押不可；恐怕他会阻挠。』『大人深谋远虑，见得很是。我看——，』胡雪岩故意踌躇着，『办不到的事。算了！』『怎么？』左宗棠问∶『什么事办不到？』『我想最好我也走一趟；钉住布浪。只是这里不容我分身。』左宗棠摸着花白短髭，沉吟了一会，徐徐说道∶『速去速回，亦自不碍。』

“Then we must be careful,” Zuo said. “Foreigners have plenty of tricks. When Giquel and d’Aiguebelle first took up this project, they bypassed their consul general and approached people in France directly. Naturally Brenier was displeased. Now every contract requires his signature. He may obstruct us.” “Your Excellency has considered the matter with great foresight. You are quite right. I think—” Hu hesitated deliberately. “But it cannot be done. Never mind.” “What cannot be done?” Zuo asked. “It would be best if I went too and kept close to Brown. Only I cannot leave my work here.” Stroking his short, greying moustache, Zuo considered this for a while. “If you went and came back quickly,” he said slowly, “it need not interfere.”

听得这话，胡雪岩精神一振，『是！』他立即答说『我遵大人吩咐，速去速回。如果布浪谈的公事与轮船无关，不过三、五天功夫，就可以回福州。』『好！』左宗棠说，『你就请吧！我还有好些大事，跟你商量；尤其是那一百二十万银子，一天没有着落，我一天心不安。』胡雪岩这一次不敢再说满话了，只答应尽速赶回。至于在福州，唯一不放心的日意格与德克碑已萌退之意，深恐事生周折，斡旋无人，以致决裂；而左宗棠却劝他不必过虑，同时拍胸担保，必定好言相劝，善为抚慰。如果有什么意见不能相合之处，自会暂且搁下，等胡雪岩回到福州以后再说。得此保证，胡雪岩才算放心；回到寓处，匆匆收拾行装，赶到码头，与布浪同船，直航上海。到上海第一件事是访古应春密谈。

Hu brightened immediately. “Yes! I shall follow Your Excellency’s instructions and return as quickly as possible. If Brown’s business has nothing to do with the steamships, I can be back in Fuzhou within three to five days.” “Very well. Go, then. I have many important matters still to discuss with you. Above all, until that million two hundred thousand taels is secured, I shall not have a day’s peace.” This time Hu dared make no sweeping promises. He merely agreed to return at the earliest opportunity. His only anxiety in Fuzhou concerned Giquel and d’Aiguebelle, who were already thinking of withdrawing. If difficulties arose without anyone to mediate, he feared an outright breach. Zuo told him not to worry and gave him his personal assurance that he would reason with them kindly and do his best to reassure them. Any differences they could not resolve would be set aside until Hu returned. Only with that promise could Hu feel at ease. He hurried back to his lodgings, packed, and reached the wharf in time to sail directly for Shanghai on Brown’s ship. His first business on arrival was a confidential visit to Gu Yingchun.

古应春近年又有新的发展，是英商汇丰银行的买办；照英文译名，俗称『康白度』，在银行中是华籍职员的首脑；名义上只是管理帐目及一切杂务，其实凡与中国人的一切交涉，大至交接官场，小至雇用苦力，无不唯买办是问。而中国人上外国银行有业务接头，更非找买办不可。因此，古应春在汇丰银行权柄很大；他又能干而勤快，极得洋东信任，言听计从，这就是胡雪岩所以首先要找他的缘故。『我要请几家外国银行的「档手」吃饭。』他一开口就说∶『你倒替我开个单子看！』『小爷叔，』古应春问道∶『是不是为船厂的事？』『不是！我要跟他们借钱。』

Gu’s career had taken another turn in recent years: he had become comprador of the British firm HSBC. Known in Chinese by a phonetic rendering of the foreign term, the comprador was the head of the bank’s Chinese staff. Nominally he managed accounts and miscellaneous business; in practice, every dealing with Chinese people, from relations with officials to the hiring of labourers, came under his charge. Chinese customers likewise had to go through him when doing business with a foreign bank. Gu consequently wielded considerable authority at HSBC. Able and industrious, he had won his foreign employers’ complete confidence; they listened to his advice. That was why Hu came to him first. “I want to invite the managers of several foreign banks to dinner,” he began. “Make me a list, will you?” “Little Uncle,” Gu asked, “is this about the shipyard?” “No. I want to borrow money from them.”

平时向外国银行借钱，十万廿万银子，只凭胡雪岩一句话就可以借到。如今特为要请洋人吃饭，可见得数目不小。古应春想了一下，拿出一本同治四年的洋商行名簿，翻到『银行』这一栏问道∶『是不是十家都请？？胡雪岩看这十家外国银行∶一、阿加剌银行二、利中银行三、利商银行四、汇泉银行五、麦加利银行六、汇隆银行七、有利银行八、法兰西银行九、汇丰银行十、丽如银行这一着，他倒踌躇了。因为通称外国银行，而国籍不同；尤其英法两国，一向钩心斗角，各自扩张势力，如今为了左宗棠设厂造船，更加不和。如果请在一起，彼此猜忌，不肯开诚布公相见，岂不是白费功夫？于是他问∶』分开来请如何？『『当然可以。不过，小爷叔，照我看，只请有用的好了。一次弄妥当了，其余的就不必理了。』『那末，你说，哪些是有用的呢？』古应春提笔在手，毫不考虑地在五、七、九三家银行上面一钩。这也是胡雪岩意中，因为汇丰银行在古应春是必不会少的；既有汇丰，便有麦加利与有利两家，因为这两家是英国银行，与汇丰的渊源较深。

Ordinarily Hu could borrow a hundred or two hundred thousand taels from a foreign bank on his word alone. A special dinner invitation suggested a much larger sum. Gu thought for a moment, took out a directory of foreign firms for the fourth year of Tongzhi, and turned to the banks. “Shall we invite all ten?” Hu looked down the list: 1. Agra Bank; 2. Lizhong Bank; 3. Lishang Bank; 4. Huiquan Bank; 5. the Chartered Bank; 6. Huilong Bank; 7. the Mercantile Bank; 8. the French Bank; 9. HSBC; 10. the Oriental Bank. Now he hesitated. They were all called foreign banks, but their nationalities differed. Britain and France in particular were constantly manoeuvring against each other to extend their influence; Zuo’s shipbuilding project had sharpened their rivalry. Invited together, they might become suspicious and refuse to speak openly. Would that not waste everyone’s time? “What about inviting them separately?” he asked. “Certainly. But, Little Uncle, I would invite only those who can help. If we settle it with them at one sitting, we need not bother with the others.” “Which ones would be useful, then?” Without hesitation Gu marked numbers five, seven, and nine. Hu had expected as much. Gu would naturally include HSBC; with HSBC came the Chartered and Mercantile Banks, both British institutions with close connections to it.

但是，汇丰银行却并非纯然英国银行。它原名『香港上海银行有限公司』，同治三年创设总行于香港，资本定为港币五百万元，由英国的怡和洋行、仁记洋行；美国的旗昌洋行，以及德国、中东的商人投资。华商亦有股份加入；古应春即是其中之一，而且以此渊源，得以充任上海分行的买办。香港上海银行的上海分行，较总行迟一年成立，派来的总经理名叫麦林，是英国人；与古应春是旧识，久知他干练可靠，且又是本行的股东，因而延揽他出任买办。古应春接事后第一个建议是『正名』；香港上海银行的名称，照英文原名直译，固无错误，但照中国的习惯，开店不管大小，总要取个吉利的名字；用地名，而且用两个地名作为银行的名称，令人有莫名其妙之感。如果『香港上海银行』之下，再赘以『上海分行』四字，更觉不伦不类，文理不协，难望成为一块『金字招牌』。

HSBC, however, was not an exclusively British bank. Originally named the Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Company Limited, it had established its head office in Hong Kong in the third year of Tongzhi, with a capital of five million Hong Kong dollars. Investors included the British firms Jardine Matheson and Renji, the American firm Qichang, and merchants from Germany and the Middle East. Chinese merchants also held shares. Gu was among them, and this connection had helped him obtain the comprador’s position at the Shanghai branch. That branch opened a year after the head office. Its manager, a Briton named McLean, had known Gu for years and considered him capable and trustworthy. Gu’s position as a shareholder provided a further reason to engage him. His first suggestion after taking office concerned the bank’s name. A literal rendering of “Hongkong and Shanghai Bank” was accurate enough, but Chinese custom required a business, however small, to have an auspicious name. To name a bank after a place—indeed, two places—struck people as puzzling. Add “Shanghai Branch” after “Hongkong and Shanghai Bank,” and the result became more awkward still. Such an ungainly name could hardly become a celebrated commercial signboard.

麦林从善如流，接纳了古应春的意见，依照中国『讨口采』的习俗，取名香港上海汇丰银行；简称汇丰银行或汇丰，无论南北口音，喊起来都很响亮。而且南北口音，都无甚区别；不比麦加利银行的麦加二字，在上海人口中便与北方人并不一致。古应春的第二个建议是，股东的国籍不同，彼此立场不同，就会意见分歧，形成相互掣肘，无可展布的不利情况。所以主张以英国为主体，逐渐收买他国股份；同时联络友行，厚集势力，相互支援。亦为麦林所欣然接纳。

McLean readily accepted the suggestion. Following the Chinese preference for auspicious associations, they adopted the name Hongkong and Shanghai Huifeng Bank, shortened to Huifeng Bank or simply Huifeng: “abundant remittances.” It sounded clear and resonant in both northern and southern speech, with little variation in pronunciation. The Chinese name Maijiali, used for the Chartered Bank, was less fortunate: its first two syllables sounded different in Shanghai from the way northerners pronounced them. Gu’s second proposal concerned the shareholders. Different nationalities brought different interests, producing disagreements and mutual obstruction that could prevent the bank from acting effectively. He therefore urged that British interests should predominate, gradually buying out shareholders of other nationalities, while alliances with friendly banks would strengthen their collective resources and provide mutual support. McLean welcomed this advice too.

汇丰所联络的两家友行，当然是英国银行，亦就是麦加利与有利两行。有利是上海资格最老的外国银行，创设于咸丰四年。它是英国的海外银行之一，总行设在伦敦；在印度孟买及上海都有分行。麦加利银行是英皇发布敕令，特许在印度、澳洲、上海设立分行的股份有限公司。总行设在伦敦；咸丰七年在上海开设分行，广东人称它为『喳打银行』；喳打的是英文『特许』一词的音译；可是上海人却赚喳打二字拗口，索性以它第一任总经理麦加利为名，叫它麦加利银行。麦加利银行完全是为了便利英商在印度、澳洲、上海的贸易而设，所以跟胡雪岩在阜康钱庄的同行关系以外，还有『销洋庄』生意上的往来。

The two banks HSBC cultivated were naturally British: the Chartered and the Mercantile. The Mercantile, established in the fourth year of Xianfeng, was Shanghai’s oldest foreign bank. It was one of Britain’s overseas banks, with its head office in London and branches in Bombay and Shanghai. The Chartered Bank was a joint-stock company authorised by royal charter to establish branches in India, Australia, and Shanghai. Its head office was in London; its Shanghai branch opened in the seventh year of Xianfeng. Cantonese speakers called it “Ja Da,” a phonetic rendering of “chartered.” Shanghai people found that name awkward to pronounce and instead called it Maijiali, after its first manager. It had been established specifically to facilitate British trade with India, Australia, and Shanghai. Thus, besides its dealings with Fukang as a fellow banking concern, it also did business with Hu through his sales to foreign trading houses.

『这三家银行当然有用。』胡雪岩踌躇说，『只怕还不够。』『还不够？』古应春这时才发觉，谈了半天，是怎么回事，还没有弄明白；只凭彼此相知既久，默契已深，猜测着谈论，毕竟是件可笑的事，因而扼要问道∶『小爷叔，你要借多少银子？』『至少一百二十万。』『这是银行从来没有贷放过的一笔大数目。』古应春又问，『是替谁借？当然是左大人？』『当然！』『造轮船？』『不是！西征的军饷。』即令是通晓中外，见多识广的古应春，也不由得楞住了，『向外国人借了钱来打仗，似乎没有听说过。』他很坦率地说∶『小爷叔，这件事恐怕难。』『我也知道难。不过一定要办成功。』古应春不再劝阻了。胡雪岩从不畏难，徒劝无效；他知道自己唯一所能采取的态度，便是不问成败利钝，尽力帮胡雪岩去克服困难。于是他问∶『小爷叔，你总想好了一个章 程，如何借，如何还；出多少利息，定多少期限？且先说出来，看看行得通行不通？』

“Those three banks would certainly be useful,” Hu said doubtfully. “I am afraid they may not be enough.” “Not enough?” Only now did Gu realise that, after all this discussion, he still did not know what the matter involved. They had known each other so long and understood each other so well that they had been talking on assumptions. It was rather absurd. He came to the point. “Little Uncle, how much do you want to borrow?” “At least a million two hundred thousand.” “The banks have never advanced such a large sum. For whom? His Excellency Zuo, of course?” “Of course.” “To build steamships?” “No. To pay for the western campaign.” Even Gu, for all his experience and knowledge of Chinese and foreign affairs, was taken aback. “I do not think I have heard of borrowing from foreigners to fight a war,” he said frankly. “Little Uncle, I fear this will be difficult.” “I know it will. But it must be done.” Gu did not argue further. Hu had never shrunk from difficulties; persuasion would be wasted. The only course open to him was to help Hu overcome them, without counting the chances of success or failure. “You must have worked out a scheme,” he said. “How will you borrow and repay? What interest, and for how long? Tell me first, and we can see whether it will work.”

『借一百二十万，利息不妨稍为高些。期限一年，前半年只行息；下半年拔月按本，分六期拔还。』『到时候拿什么来还？』『各省的西征协饷。』胡雪岩屈指算道∶『福建四万、广东四万、浙江七万；这就是十五万，只差五万了。江海关打它三万的主意，还差两万，无论如何好想法子。』『小爷叔，你打的如意算盘。各省协饷是靠不住的！万一拖欠呢？』『我阜康钱庄担保。』『不然！』古应春大摇其头，『犯不着这么做！而且洋人做事，讲究直接了当；如果说到阜康担保的话，洋人一定会说∶』钱借给你阜康钱庄好了。只要你提供担保，我们不管你的用途。「那一来，小爷叔，你不但风险担得太大，而且也太招摇。不妥，不妥！『想想果然不妥，很能服善的胡雪岩深深点头，』外国银行的规矩，外国人的脾气，你比我精通得多；你看，是怎么个办法？『他说，』只要事情办通，什么条件我都接受。『』洋人办事跟我们有点不同。我们是讲信义通商，只凭一句话就算数；不大去想后果。洋人呢，虽然也讲信义，不过更讲法理；而且有点」小人之心「，不算好，先算坏，拿借钱来说，第一件想到的事是，对方将来还不还得起？如果还不起又怎么办？这两点，小爷叔，你先要盘算妥当；不然还是不开口的好。『』我明白了。第一点，一定还得起，因为各省的协饷，规定了数目，自然要奏明朝廷；西征大事，哪一省不解，贻误戎机，罪名不轻。再说，福建、广东、浙江三省，都有左大人的人在那里，一定买账。这三省就有十五万；四股有其三，不必担心。『』好，这话我可以跟洋人说。担保呢？『

“A million two hundred thousand. The interest can be a little high. A term of one year: interest only for the first six months, then repayment of the principal in six monthly instalments.” “What will you use to repay it?” “The provincial contributions for the western campaign.” Hu counted on his fingers. “Forty thousand from Fujian, forty thousand from Guangdong, seventy thousand from Zhejiang. That makes a hundred and fifty thousand, leaving fifty thousand. We can look to Jianghaiguan Customs for thirty thousand; the remaining twenty thousand can surely be found somehow.” “Little Uncle, that is a calculation of everything going your way. Provincial contributions are unreliable. What if they fall into arrears?” “Fukang will guarantee them.” “No!” Gu shook his head vigorously. “You have no reason to do that. Besides, foreigners like a straightforward arrangement. If Fukang provides the guarantee, they will say, ‘We may as well lend directly to Fukang. So long as you give us security, we do not care what you use the money for.’ You would be taking an enormous risk and attracting far too much attention. It will not do.” Hu considered this and nodded. He was always willing to accept sound advice. “You understand foreign banks and foreign habits far better than I do. What do you suggest? Provided the business can be arranged, I will accept any conditions.” Gu explained, “They handle things somewhat differently from us. We trade on good faith: a spoken promise is enough, and we seldom think very far about the consequences. They value good faith too, but attach greater importance to legal principles. They are also rather suspicious: instead of calculating how things will go well, they first consider how they might go wrong. In a loan, their first questions are whether the borrower will be able to repay and what happens if he cannot. You must settle those two points in your own mind before approaching them.” “I understand. First, repayment is certain. Each province will have a fixed contribution, formally reported to the court. The western campaign is a great undertaking. A province that withholds its remittance and obstructs military operations will incur a serious charge. Besides, His Excellency Zuo has his own men in Fujian, Guangdong, and Zhejiang. They will certainly cooperate. Those three provinces provide a hundred and fifty thousand—three quarters of what we need. There is no cause for concern.” “Very well. I can tell the foreigners that. What about security?”

『阜康既然不便担保，那就只有请左大人自己出面了。』『左大人只能出面来借，不能做保人。』『这就难了！』胡雪岩灵机一动，『请协饷的各省督抚做保，先出印票，到期向各少藩司衙门收兑。这样总可以了吧？』『不见得！不过总是一个说法。』古应春又说，『照我看，各省督抚亦未见得肯。』『这一层你不必担心，左大人自然做得到。「挟天子以令诸侯」的花样，他最擅长。』『好的。只要有把握，就可以谈了。』古应春说∶『我想，请吃饭不妨摆在后面；我先拿汇丰的大板约出来跟小爷叔见个面，怎么样？』『大板』是『大老板』的简称；洋行的华籍职员，都是这样称他们的『洋东』。汇丰的『大板』麦林，胡雪岩也曾会过，人很精明，但如上海人所说的很『上路』，凡事只要在理路上，总可以谈得成功。所以胡雪岩欣然表示同意。不过还有些话要交代明白。

“If Fukang cannot conveniently guarantee it, His Excellency Zuo must come forward himself.” “He can come forward as the borrower, not as his own guarantor.” “That makes it difficult.” An idea occurred to Hu. “Have the governors and governors-general of the contributing provinces guarantee it. They can issue sealed notes in advance, redeemable at the provincial treasuries when due. Surely that would do?” “Not necessarily. Still, it is a proposal. I doubt the provincial authorities would agree.” “You need not worry about that. His Excellency Zuo can manage it. Nobody is better at invoking the Son of Heaven’s authority to command the regional lords.” “If you are confident, we can begin discussions,” Gu said. “I suggest leaving the dinner until later. Let me first arrange a meeting with HSBC’s chief. How would that be?” Chinese employees commonly called the head of a foreign firm the “big boss,” shortened simply to “boss.” Hu had met McLean before. He was shrewd, but also, as Shanghai people said, a man who understood how things worked: if a proposal made sense, one could usually reach an agreement with him. Hu readily accepted. There were, however, a few more things he needed to explain.

『老古，』他说，『我的情形本来瞒不过你；这年把你兼了汇丰的差使，对我个人的情形有些隔膜了。我如今是个「空心大老倌」，场面扯得太大，而且有苦难言。福建这面，现银接济跟买军火的垫款，通扯要亏我二三十万；浙江这面，代理藩库的帐，到现在没有结算清楚。有些帐不好报销，也不好争，因为碍着左大人的面子；善后局的垫款，更是只好摆在那里再说。这样扯算下来，又是二三十万，总共有五十万银子的宕帐在那里，你说，怎么吃得消？』『有这么多宕帐！』古应春吃了一惊，『转眼开春，丝茶两市都要热闹；先得大把银子垫下去。那时候，小爷叔，阜康倘或周转不灵，岂不难看？』『岂但难看？简直要命！』胡雪岩紧接着又说，『说到难看，年内有件事铺排不好，就要显原形。我是分发福建的道员，本不该管浙北的盐务；不过浙江总算闽浙总督管辖，勉强说得过去。如今我改归陕甘总督差遣了，将来必是长驻上海，办西北军火粮饷的转运；浙北盐务，非交卸不可。要交卸呢，扯了十几万的亏空，怎好不归清？』『这就是说，年内就要十几万才能过门。』

“Old Gu, you know my affairs well enough. But during this past year, with your duties at HSBC, you have lost touch with some of my personal circumstances. I am now a grand gentleman with an empty purse. I have spread myself too far, and some of my troubles cannot be spoken of. In Fujian, cash assistance and advances for arms purchases together leave me two or three hundred thousand taels out of pocket. In Zhejiang, the accounts for handling the provincial treasury’s business are still unsettled. Some items cannot easily be claimed, and I cannot press them without embarrassing His Excellency Zuo. The advances to the Reconstruction Bureau must simply remain outstanding. That is another two or three hundred thousand. Altogether I have about half a million tied up in unpaid accounts. How can I stand it?” “That much outstanding!” Gu was startled. “Spring is almost upon us. Both the silk and tea markets will be busy, and you must advance large sums first. If Fukang cannot turn its funds over then, it would look very bad.” “Look bad? It would kill me! And speaking of appearances, unless I arrange one matter properly before the New Year, the truth will already be exposed. I am a circuit intendant assigned to Fujian. Strictly speaking, I ought never to have administered the northern Zhejiang salt business; it was only defensible because Zhejiang lay under the Fujian–Zhejiang Governor General. Now I am being transferred to serve the Shaanxi–Gansu Governor General. I shall probably be stationed permanently in Shanghai to forward arms, provisions, and military funds to the northwest. I must hand over the northern Zhejiang salt administration. But there is a deficit of over a hundred thousand taels. How can I hand it over without making that good?” “So you need over a hundred thousand before the year ends merely to clear that hurdle.”

『还只是这一处；其他还有。一等开了年，阜康总要五十万银子才周转得过来。如果这笔借款成功，分批汇解，我可以先用一用；一到明年夏天，丝茶两市结束，货款源源而来，我就活络了。』古应春松了口气。『好！』他毅然决然地说，『我一定想法子，拿这笔借款弄成功。』『有你，一定可以成功。老古，我还有点意思，说给你听，第一，这件事要做得秘密，千万漏不得一点风声，不然，京里的「都老爷」奏上一本，坏事有余。我告诉你吧，这个做法连左大人自己都还不知道——。』此言一出，古应春大为诧异，『那末，』他忧虑地说，『到谈成功了，如果左大人说「不行」，那不是笑话！』『你放心！决不会闹笑话，我有十足的把握，他会照我的话做。』『好！再说第二件。』

“And that is only one item. There are others. Once the New Year begins, Fukang will need half a million just to keep its funds circulating. If this loan goes through and the money is remitted in batches, I can use some temporarily. By next summer, when the silk and tea seasons are over, payment for the goods will come pouring in and I shall have room to move again.” Gu breathed more freely. “Very well,” he said decisively. “I will find a way to make this loan succeed.” “With you helping, it certainly can. Old Gu, I have two more points. First, everything must be kept secret. Not a whisper must escape. One memorial from those censors in the capital could wreck it. Let me tell you: even His Excellency Zuo does not yet know of this method.” Gu was astonished. “Then suppose we negotiate successfully and he says no? Would that not make us ridiculous?” “Do not worry. There will be no such embarrassment. I am absolutely certain he will follow my advice.” “Very well. What is the second point?”

『第二件，我想托名洋商；其实，有人愿意放款，也不妨搭些份头，多赚几个利息。』『这要看情形，如今还言之过早。』『只要你心里有数就是。』胡雪岩说，『左大人的功名，我的事业，都寄托在这笔借款上了。』为了保持机密，古应春将麦林约在新成立的『德国总会』与胡雪岩见面，一坐下来便开门见山地谈到正题。麦林相当深沉，听完究竟，未置可否，先发出一连串的询问。『贵国朝廷对此事的意见如何？』

“The second is that, although the loan will be made in the name of foreign merchants, Chinese people willing to lend could take shares in it and earn some additional interest.” “That will depend on circumstances. It is too early to say.” “Just keep it in mind. His Excellency Zuo’s achievements and my own business both depend on this loan.” To preserve secrecy, Gu arranged for McLean to meet Hu at the newly established German Club. They went straight to the subject as soon as they sat down. McLean was a reserved man. Having heard the whole proposal, he neither accepted nor rejected it, but began a series of questions. “What is your court’s view of the matter?”

『平定回乱在中国视为头等大事。』胡雪岩透过古应春的解释答说∶『能够由带兵大臣自己筹措到足够的军费，朝廷当然全力支持。』『据我所知，中国的带兵大臣，各有势力范围。左爵爷的势力范围，似乎只有陕西甘肃两省，那是最贫瘠的地方。』『不然。』胡雪岩不肯承认地盘之说，『朝廷的威信，及于所有行省；只要朝廷同意这笔借款，以及由各省分摊归还的办法，令出必行，请你不必顾虑。』『那末，这笔借款，为什么不请你们的政府出面来借？』『左爵爷出面，即是代表中国政府。』胡雪岩说，『一切交涉，要讲对等的地位；如果由中国政府出面，应该向你们的「户部」商谈，不应该是我们在这里计议。』麦林深深点头；但紧接着又问∶『左爵爷代表中国政府，而你代表左爵爷；那就等于你代表中国政府。是这样吗？』这话很难回答。因为此事，正在发动之初，甚至连左宗棠都还不知道有此借款办法；更谈不到朝廷授权。如果以讹传讹，胡雪岩便是窃冒名义，招摇辱国，罪名不轻。但如不敢承认，便就失去凭借，根本谈不下去了。

“Suppressing the Muslim rebellion is regarded in China as a matter of the first importance,” Hu replied through Gu. “If the commander can raise sufficient military funds himself, the court will naturally give him its full support.” “As I understand it, your military commanders each have their own sphere of influence. Lord Zuo’s appears to comprise only Shaanxi and Gansu, which are very poor provinces.” “That is not so.” Hu would not accept this division into personal domains. “The court’s authority extends to every province. Once it approves the loan and the arrangement for the provinces to share repayment, its orders will be obeyed. You need have no anxiety.” “Then why does your government not take out the loan itself?” “When Lord Zuo comes forward, he represents the Chinese government. Negotiations must be conducted between parties of equivalent standing. If our government acted directly, it should discuss the matter with your Treasury. It would not be for us to settle it here.” McLean nodded thoughtfully, then pressed the point. “Lord Zuo represents the Chinese government, and you represent Lord Zuo. Does that mean you represent the Chinese government?” It was difficult to answer. The scheme had barely begun; even Zuo did not yet know of it, much less had the court authorised it. If a mistaken account spread, Hu could be charged with usurping official authority and bringing disgrace upon the country—a serious offence. Yet to deny it would deprive him of his standing and make further negotiation impossible.

想了一会，含含糊糊地答道∶『谈得成功，我是代表中国政府；谈不成功，我只代表我自己。』『胡先生的词令很精彩，也很玄妙，可是也很实在。好的，我就当你中国政府的代表看待。这笔借款，原则是我可以同意；不过，我必须声明，在我们的谈判未曾有结论以前，你们不可以跟任何另一家银行去谈。』『可以，我愿意信任你。』胡雪岩说，『不过我们应该规定一个谈判的限期；同时我也有一个要求，在谈判没有结果以前，你必须保守秘密。』『那是彼此都应该接受的约束。至于限期，很难定规，因为细节的商谈，往往需要长时间的磋商。』『好！我们现在就谈细节。』这等于确定麦林是作了借款的承诺；连古应春都笑了，『小爷叔，』他说，『我看交涉是你自己办的好；我只管传译。麦林很精明；也只有精明的人才能让他佩服。『于是即时展开了秘密而冗长的谈判；前后三天，反复商议，几于废寝忘食。麦林原来就佩服精明的人，此时更为胡雪岩的旺盛企图心所感动；更为胡雪岩的过人的精力所压倒，终于建成了协议。

After a moment’s thought, he gave an ambiguous answer. “If the negotiations succeed, I represent the Chinese government. If they fail, I represent only myself.” “Your wording is ingenious, Mr Hu: subtle, but very practical. Very well, I shall regard you as the representative of the Chinese government. I can agree to the loan in principle. But I must make one condition: until our negotiations reach a conclusion, you must not approach any other bank.” “Agreed. I am willing to place my trust in you. But we should set a time limit. And I have a condition of my own: until we reach a conclusion, you must keep the matter secret.” “Confidentiality is an obligation we should both accept. As for a deadline, that is difficult. The details often require prolonged discussion.” “Very well. Let us discuss the details now.” This amounted to treating McLean’s words as a commitment to lend. Even Gu smiled. “Little Uncle, you had better handle the negotiations yourself. I shall confine myself to interpreting. McLean is a shrewd man; only someone equally shrewd can win his respect.” Secret and lengthy negotiations began at once. For three days they discussed and reconsidered the terms, almost forgetting food and sleep. McLean already admired intelligence; now he was moved by Hu’s powerful ambition and overwhelmed by his extraordinary energy. At last they reached agreement.

这一协议并未订成草约，亦未写下笔录，但彼此保证，口头协定，亦具有道义上的约束力量，决无翻悔。商定的办法与条件是∶第一、借款总数，关于一百二十万两；由汇丰银行组成财团承贷。

They drew up no draft contract and made no written record. Both sides nevertheless pledged that their oral agreement would bind them in honour, and that neither would go back on it. The arrangements and conditions were these. First, the total loan would be one million two hundred thousand taels, to be provided by a syndicate organised by HSBC.

第二、月息八厘，付款先扣。

Second, interest would be 0.8 per cent a month, deducted in advance when the funds were disbursed.

第三、由胡雪岩、古应春介绍华商向汇丰银行存款，月息明盘四厘、暗盘六厘。

Third, Hu and Gu would introduce Chinese merchants as depositors at HSBC, with a publicly quoted monthly rate of 0.4 per cent and a private rate of 0.6 per cent.

第四、各海关每月有常数收入，各税务局多为洋人，因此，借款笔据，应由各海关出印票，并由各省督抚加印，到期向各海关兑取。

Fourth, since the customs houses had regular monthly receipts and their commissioners were mostly foreigners, the loan instruments would be sealed notes issued by the customs houses, bearing the additional seals of the provincial governors or governors-general. They would be redeemable at the customs houses when due.

第五、自同治六年七月起，每月拔本二十万两，半年清偿。

Fifth, beginning in the seventh month of the sixth year of Tongzhi, two hundred thousand taels of principal would be repaid each month, discharging the loan within six months.

这五条办法中，第三条是洋商与胡雪岩、古应春合得的好处，明盘四厘，暗盘六厘，即是中间人得二厘的佣金；这也就是说，洋商向中国人借了钱，转借与中国官场，四厘入，八厘出，所得四厘好处，各半均分。

Of these five provisions, the third gave the foreign bankers, Hu, and Gu a shared benefit. The difference between the public rate of 0.4 per cent and the private rate of 0.6 per cent was a commission of 0.2 per cent for the intermediaries. In other words, the foreign bankers would borrow Chinese money and lend it on to Chinese officials, paying 0.4 per cent and receiving 0.8. The resulting margin of 0.4 per cent would be divided equally between bankers and intermediaries.

至于印票必出自海关，是麦林坚决的主张。因为他虽相信胡雪岩与左宗棠，却不相信有关各省的督抚，到时候印票如废纸，无可奈何；而海关由洋人担任税务局，一经承诺，没有理由不守信用。这在胡雪岩却是个难题，因为除江海关每月协解三万两，可以情商上海道先出印票以外，其余各海关并无协饷之责，就不见得肯出印票。想来想去只有一个办法，就是奏明朝廷，每月由各省藩司负责将应解甘饷，解交本省海关归垫。

McLean insisted that the sealed notes must come from the customs houses. Though he trusted Hu and Zuo, he did not trust the governors and governors-general of the provinces concerned. Their notes might prove worthless when due, leaving him helpless. The customs commissioners, however, were foreigners; once they had given their word, he saw no reason why they should fail to keep it. This presented Hu with a difficulty. Jianghaiguan was already obliged to contribute thirty thousand taels a month, so he could ask the Shanghai circuit intendant to arrange for its notes to be issued in advance. The other customs houses had no corresponding obligation and might refuse. After much thought, he saw only one solution: obtain imperial approval for each provincial treasurer to remit the province’s monthly contribution for Gansu to its own customs house, reimbursing the sums advanced there.

幸好协饷各省都有海关，每月闽粤两海关各代借二十四万；浙海关代借四十二万两；加上江海关本身应解的十八万两，共计一百零八万两，所缺只有十二万。胡雪岩建议左宗棠要求湖北每月协饷两万，由江汉关出十二万两的印票，合成一百二十万整数。

Fortunately, all the contributing provinces had customs houses. Fujian and Guangdong Customs could each provide notes for two hundred and forty thousand taels, Zhejiang Customs for four hundred and twenty thousand, and Jianghaiguan for the hundred and eighty thousand it was itself due to contribute. Together these made one million eighty thousand, leaving only a hundred and twenty thousand short. Hu suggested that Zuo ask Hubei to contribute twenty thousand a month, with Jianghan Customs issuing notes for a hundred and twenty thousand. This would bring the total to exactly one million two hundred thousand.

这些办法，左宗棠完全同意；但等奏准，已在开春，丝茶两市方兴，正须放款，因而利息提高到一分三厘。这是从未有过的高利贷，于是流言四起，说胡雪岩从中渔利；尤其是李鸿章一派的人，不但展开口头的攻击，而且亦有实际的破坏行动。这个行动很简单，却很有效，就是策动江海关税务司拒绝出具印票。一关如此，他关皆然，几于功败垂成。经过胡雪岩的巧妙斡旋，这笔大借款还是做成功了。是为中国借外债的开始；而左宗棠的勋业，以及胡雪岩个人的事业，亦因此而有了一个新的开始。但福者祸所倚，『红顶商人』胡雪岩的结局，相当凄惨；种因亦在于此！

Zuo approved all these arrangements. By the time imperial permission arrived, however, spring had begun. The silk and tea markets were gathering momentum, demand for loans was strong, and the interest rate rose to 1.3 per cent a month. Such a high lending rate was unprecedented. Rumours spread that Hu was profiting from it. Li Hongzhang’s faction in particular went beyond verbal attacks and took practical steps to sabotage the loan. The measure was simple but effective: they induced the Jianghaiguan Customs Commissioner to refuse to issue sealed notes. Once one customs house refused, the others followed. Success was almost lost at the last moment. Through Hu’s skilful mediation, the great loan was nevertheless completed. This was the beginning of China’s foreign borrowing; it also marked a new beginning for Zuo’s achievements and Hu’s own career. Yet misfortune lies hidden in good fortune. The Red-Topped Merchant would meet a tragic end, and the seed of it was planted here.

1. 第4697–4707行的银行沿革按小说叙述保留，不能当作已核实的银行史。汇丰官方年表记香港开业为1865年3月3日、上海同年4月开业，与小说“同治三年总行、分行迟一年”不合；小说称古应春参与取名、股权建议，亦仅作为小说情节翻译。官方参考：https://history.hsbc.com/history/timeline 。港币五百万元与本章其他银两不是同一币种，已区分。
2. 麦林作 McLean；汇丰作 HSBC，麦加利作 Chartered Bank，有利作 Mercantile Bank，丽如作 Oriental Bank。目录中的利中、利商、汇泉、汇隆以及仁记保留中文名的拼音形式，法兰西暂作 French Bank，未未经查证猜成某一家现代同名银行。旗昌沿用前文 Qichang。买办对应 comprador，是华人业务与人事的中介主管，不等于董事长。英国银行并非全部股东都是英国人，原文已作区别。
3. 第4727行初案是一年期，前半年付息、后半年每月还本二十万；后面的实际谈判条款注明扣息在先。八厘按月为0.8%，四厘0.4%，六厘0.6%，二厘0.2%，一分三厘1.3%。明盘/暗盘的差额是中间人佣金，不是直接断言存款人实际领到0.6%。这些是小说中的历史交易叙述，不是今日利率报价。
4. 第4785行“每月闽粤两海关各代借二十四万”存在时间单位矛盾。上下文月额四万、四万、七万、三万，分别乘六才得到24万、24万、42万、18万；合计108万，再加湖北每月两万、六个月12万，才是120万。因此英文按六个月所需票据总额叙述，未把24万误写成每月额外借款，也未另造本金；原字保留于中英对照供核对。
5. 官场应收约五十万、盐务亏空十余万、新年周转需求五十万分别属于不同口径，不能直接相加就当成已确认负债。胡打算在分批汇解期间暂用借款，是他向古坦言的安排；本段最初左尚不知“向外国银行借款”这个办法，朝廷更未授权，谈判成功后才报批。阜康个人担保被古明确否定，未写成最后采用的担保方式。

阅读、完型与衔接
“in principle”只表示原则同意；“provided / once / if”分别带出条件，不能把商议、承诺、批准和实际完成混为一谈。结尾 nevertheless 标示虽遭阻挠仍办成借款，yet 将事业新起点转向悲剧伏笔。
指代题可追踪“their notes”与“the customs houses”：麦林不信任各省督抚单独出具的票据，才坚持海关出票，并非他对所有中国人一概不信任。

翻译与表达
“tied up in unpaid accounts”指资金压在未收款项上；“redeemable ... when due”表示到期可兑付；“bring the total to”交代合计结果。借款金额、每月现金流、六个月票据总额要分开读，段落排序可据“方案—质疑—替代担保—谈判—报批—阻挠—完成”的因果顺序判断。
