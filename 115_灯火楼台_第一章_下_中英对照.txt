贤良寺本来是雍正朝抬贤亲王的故居，屋宇精洁、花木扶疏，而且离东华门很近，上朝方便，所以封疆大吏入觐述职，都爱住在这里。左宗棠下榻之处，是其中最大的一个院落，另外开门出入，门口站着七八名壮汉，服饰随便，举止粗率，形似斯养卒，但古应春却丝毫不敢怠慢。原来左宗棠镇压洪杨、捻回，二十年指挥过无数战役，麾下将校，百战余生，从军功上保到总兵，提督的，不知凡几？但武人诚朴，颇有不愿赴任，而宁愿跟着左杀棠当差官，出入相从，不说破不知道他们都有红顶子，黄马褂，甚至双眼花翎。

Xianliang Temple had originally been the residence of Prince Yixian in the Yongzheng reign. Its buildings were clean and elegant, its grounds thick with flowers and trees, and its proximity to Donghua Gate made attendance at court convenient. Provincial governors and governors-general therefore liked to stay there when summoned to report to the emperor. Zuo Zongtang occupied the largest courtyard, with a separate entrance. Seven or eight burly men stood at the gate, casually dressed and rough in manner, looking like ordinary menials or soldiers. Gu Yingchun nevertheless dared not treat them lightly. Over twenty years of suppressing the Taipings, the Nian, and the Muslim rebellions, Zuo had commanded countless battles. Many officers who survived those campaigns had won promotion through military merit to brigade commander or provincial military commander. But soldiers were straightforward and loyal. A good number preferred not to take up their appointments, choosing instead to remain with Zuo as officers on special duty, accompanying him wherever he went. Unless someone told you, you would never guess that they were entitled to red hat buttons, yellow riding jackets, and even double-eyed peacock feathers.

一次，有个何总兵奉左宗棠之命，去见陕西藩司谈公事。这个藩司是满洲的世家子，架子极大，平时视部属如仆从，呼来喝去，视作当然，因而都敬鬼神而远之。此人本来对外事不大明白，加以部下疏远，对各方面的情形，更加隔膜，不知道何总兵的来头，不过看在左宗棠的分上，接见时以平礼相待。只是心里有个想法：我是敬其上而重其下，你就该守着你的规矩，要谦虚客气才是。不道何总兵全不理会，“升炕”就升炕！“上坐”就上坐，而且翘起二郎腿，高谈阔论旁若无人。藩司心里已很讨厌了，及至“端茶”送客，何总兵昂然直出中门，将藩司抛任身后，竟似以长官自居了。是可忍，孰不可忍？藩司震怒之余，第二天谒见左宗棠时，谈及此事，惯愤不平之意，还现于词色。左宗棠笑一笑，将何总兵传了来训斥，他说：“你们自以为都出生入死，立过战功，在我面前随意坐卧淡笑，固无不可。藩台大人是朝廷大员，体制何等尊贵，你怎么可以放肆，当是在我面前一样，何以这样不自量。你现在赶快给藩台磕头陪罪，不然藩台发了脾气，我亦没有这张脸替你再求情。何总兵答应一声，跪倒在地，磕头请罪。过了一会，左宗棠送客，藩司一出中门就看到十几个红顶花翎黄马褂的武官手扶腰刀在那里站班，其中有一个就是何总兵。这一下，头上蓝顶子，脑后只有一条辫子的藩司，大惊失色，手足无措。还算见机，定定神伛偻着身子，一一请安招呼，步行到辕门外，方始上轿，但己汗透重棉了。

Once, Zuo sent a brigade commander named He to discuss official business with the provincial treasurer of Shaanxi. The treasurer came from an established Manchu family and put on tremendous airs. He treated his subordinates as servants, ordering them about as a matter of course, so they kept a respectful distance. He already knew little of affairs outside his office, and their remoteness made him still more ignorant. Unaware of Commander He’s standing, he received him as an equal only out of regard for Zuo. Inwardly he thought: I honor the subordinate out of respect for his superior; you should know your place and show some humility in return. He, however, paid no heed to any of this. Invited onto the raised couch, he took his seat; offered the place of honor, he accepted it. Crossing his legs, he talked freely, paying no heed to anyone else. The treasurer was already deeply annoyed. When he lifted his teacup to signal the end of the visit, He strode grandly out through the central doorway, leaving his host behind as though he were the senior official. This was intolerable. The next day, when the treasurer called on Zuo, indignation still showed in his voice and face as he recounted the affair. Zuo smiled, summoned He, and rebuked him. “You men think that because you have risked your lives and earned distinction in battle, you may sit, lounge, talk, and laugh freely in my presence. That is all very well with me. But His Excellency the Treasurer is a great officer of the court, whose position commands the highest respect. How dare you behave as freely with him as you do with me? You have no sense of your place. Kowtow and apologize at once. Otherwise, if His Excellency loses his temper, I shall have no standing to plead for you.” He obeyed, kneeling and kowtowing. Presently, when Zuo saw the treasurer out, the man passed through the central doorway and found a dozen or so military officers standing in attendance, hands on their sword hilts, all wearing red buttons, peacock feathers, and yellow jackets. Commander He was among them. The treasurer, whose own hat button was blue and who wore no peacock feather, turned pale in alarm and scarcely knew what to do. Recovering enough presence of mind, he bent deferentially and saluted each in turn. He walked all the way beyond the outer gate before entering his chair, by which time sweat had soaked his thick padded clothes.

古应春从听说这个笑话以后，就不敢小看这些“老粗”们，当时陪笑问道：“大人回来了？”其时有个差官认识古应春，上前接话，“我们大人刚回来。”他说：“胡大先生陪着洋人早就到了，派人出来问过你两次，赶快请进去吧！”到得花厅，见了胡雪岩，还来不及叙话，只见角门已开，闪出来两名差官，知道左宗棠要来了，当即招呼两名洋人站起来迎接。左宗棠自然是便衣，一件旧薄棉袍，头上是兰州织呢厂所出的一顶鼻烟色的毡帽。胡雪岩跟古应春自然磕头请安，洋人则是一鞠躬，然后又跟左宗某拉手。于是左宗棠独坐，问了些“哪天到的”、“路上如何”、“江南有什么新闻”之类的话，胡雪岩一一照答，一阵寒暄过后，谈入正题。

Ever since hearing that amusing story, Gu had known better than to look down on these rough soldiers. He therefore asked with a smile, “Has His Excellency returned?” One of the officers recognized him and came forward. “He has just returned. Mr Hu arrived with the foreigners some time ago and has sent out twice to ask for you. Please hurry in.” Gu reached the reception hall and found Hu, but before they could speak, a side door opened and two officers appeared. Knowing that Zuo was coming, Gu at once asked the two foreigners to rise. Zuo was naturally in informal dress: an old, lightly padded robe and a snuff-colored felt hat made by the Lanzhou woolen mill. Hu and Gu kowtowed, while the foreigners bowed and then shook hands with him. Zuo alone took a seat. He asked when they had arrived, how the journey had gone, and what news there was from Jiangnan. Hu answered each question, and after these preliminary courtesies they turned to business.

正题是借洋债。胡雪岩自同治五年至光绪四年，为左宗棠借过四次外债，以充“西饷”。西睡用兵，需由各省补助军饷，称为“协响”。但协饷分年解送，而打仗不能说今年饷银用完，不打了，明年有了饷再打。时而胡雪岩想出一个借洋债的办法，最大的“银主”是英商汇丰银行。还款的方式是由江海关开出期票，而由协饷省分，主要的是江苏、浙江、广东、福建四省的督抚、盖上大印，表示承诺在到期以前，将协饷解交江海关，偿还洋商。 年限总在六年上下，半年一期，付息拔本。方式是由胡雪岩秉承左宗棠的意思，找洋商谈妥细节，然后由左宗棠出奏。奏准后，以上谕饬协饷冬省出具印票，并江海关，同时由总理衙门照会英闰公使，转知贷款的汇丰银行照付。

The business was a foreign loan. Between the fifth year of Tongzhi and the fourth year of Guangxu, Hu had arranged four foreign loans for Zuo to finance the western campaigns. Military operations on the western frontier depended on contributions from other provinces, known as supplementary military funds. But those contributions arrived year by year. A war could not be stopped merely because the year’s money had run out, to be resumed when next year’s allocation arrived. Hu had therefore devised a system of foreign borrowing, with HSBC as the principal lender. Repayment was arranged through dated bills issued by Jianghaiguan Customs. The governors or governors-general of the contributing provinces—chiefly Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Guangdong, and Fujian—affixed their official seals, undertaking to remit their contributions to Jianghaiguan before the bills fell due so that the foreign lenders could be repaid. The term was generally about six years, with interest and principal paid in installments every six months. Hu first negotiated the details with the foreigners in accordance with Zuo’s wishes; Zuo then submitted a memorial. Once imperial approval had been obtained, an edict directed the contributing provinces to furnish sealed bills through Jianghaiguan. At the same time, the Zongli Yamen notified the British minister, who instructed the lending bank to release the money.

这套手续很繁琐，其中还有两道关口，一道是总税务司赫德。根据中英条约，关税是用来赔偿鸦片战争失败军费的保证，因此英国人要求控制中国新开各口岩，称为“洋关”的海关，职称是税务司，都归总税务司赫德管辖，赫德不下命令，江海关税务司不肯出票，钱就借不成了。再一道关口是英国驻华公使，没有他的核准，汇丰银行不能拔款，有他批准了，即等于英国政府担保汇丰银行不会吃倒帐。赫德还好，因为他毕竟是中国的客卿，不能不买总理衙门的帐，而且有回佣好分，亦愿乐观其成。但英国公使这一关很罗嗦，哪怕上谕批准了，各省的印票也备齐了，总理衙门跟赫德也说好了，没有英国公使点头，饯仍旧借不到。

This procedure was cumbersome, and two further barriers stood in the way. One was Hart, the Inspector-General of Customs. Under the Sino-British treaties, customs revenue served as security for the indemnity arising from China’s defeat in the Opium War. The British therefore demanded control of the customs offices at the newly opened ports, known as the foreign customs. Their commissioners were all under Hart. Without his orders, the commissioner at Jianghaiguan would not issue the bills, and the loan could not proceed. The other barrier was the British minister in China. Without his approval, the bank could not release the funds. His approval effectively amounted to a British government guarantee that the bank would not suffer a default. Hart was comparatively easy to manage. After all, he was a foreigner in Chinese official service and could not disregard the Zongli Yamen. There was also commission to be shared, so he was willing to see the business succeed. The British minister was far more troublesome. Even with an imperial edict, all the provinces’ sealed documents, and an understanding between the Zongli Yamen and Hart, the money remained unavailable until he gave his assent.

以左宗棠天马行空的性格，这当然是件不能容忍的事，中国人借洋债，要做中国官的英国人赫德同意，更起反感。因此当德国泰来洋行的经理福克，向左宗棠表示，有钱可错，手续可以节减许多，左宗棠自然是欢迎的。福克之得以谒见左宗棠，出于胡雪岩的推荐，那是一年前的话，西陲已经平定，左宗棠准备在陕甘大兴实业，关照胡雪岩招聘技师，胡雪岩找上了福克。在哈密行营一席之谈，左宗棠认为福克“切实而有条理”，颇为欣赏，福克便抓住机会，为德国资本找出路，当然，要谈这笔借款，仍旧需要胡雪岩。当时正是崇厚擅自订约，被捕下狱，中俄关系搞得剑拔弩张之时，左宗棠接到一个情报，说俄国举了一笔“国债”达五千二百万两之巨，用来扩充军备，认为中俄难免一战，将来兵连祸结，其势难以停止，亦须未雨绸缪，如果能惜二三千万银子，分数十年偿还，则饷源一广，练兵必精，写信给胡雪岩，要他跟泰来洋行谈判，而且约他在开年灯节以后，进京面谈。不久，这件事打消了，因为由于曾纪泽斡旋，中俄形势已趋缓和，没有再大举外债的理由。

To a man of Zuo’s unfettered temperament, this was intolerable. He especially resented the idea that Chinese borrowing required the consent of Hart, an Englishman serving as a Chinese official. Naturally, therefore, he welcomed an offer from Focke, manager of the German firm Tailai Trading House, who said that money was available with much less formality. Hu had introduced Focke to Zuo a year earlier. The western frontier had been pacified, and Zuo was preparing to develop industry in Shaanxi and Gansu. Asked to recruit technicians, Hu had approached Focke. During a conversation at the Hami field headquarters, Zuo found him practical and methodical and formed a favorable impression. Focke seized the opportunity to find an outlet for German capital. Hu’s participation, of course, was still necessary to negotiate the loan. At that time Chonghou had been arrested and imprisoned for signing a treaty without authorization, and Sino-Russian relations were dangerously tense. Zuo received intelligence that Russia had raised a national loan equivalent to fifty-two million taels to expand its armaments. Believing war between China and Russia almost inevitable, and fearing that once begun it might continue indefinitely, he felt obliged to prepare. A loan of twenty or thirty million taels, repayable over several decades, would provide abundant funds and allow the troops to be thoroughly trained. He wrote to Hu, asking him to negotiate with Tailai and to come to Beijing after the Lantern Festival early in the new year. Before long the proposal was dropped. Through Zeng Jize’s diplomacy, relations with Russia had eased, and there was no longer any reason for such extensive foreign borrowing.

这是第一遍。第二遍旧事重提，又要借了。原来左宗棠内召入关进军机时，奉旨将他的一差一缺，分别交卸，一差是“钦差大臣督办新疆军务”，交由刘锦棠接替，一缺是“陕甘总督”交由杨昌浚署理。刘、杨都是左宗棠麾下的大将，但资望不足，难当重任，陕甘贫瘠，全靠各省协饷，各省如果不买帐，刘、杨就一筹奠展，因此，左宗棠必须为刘锦棠、杨昌浚筹好了饷，西征的功绩，才算有了着落。照左宗棠的盘算，新疆与陕甘以玉门关为界，每年关外军饷要三百七十万，关内二百一十万，全年为五百八十万两。光绪五年起，上谕各省协饷，必须解足五百万两，相差八十万，前后套搭，总还可敷衍得过，哪知上谕归上谕，协饷归协饷，两年之间，各省协饷欠解竟达四百二十万两之巨。为此，刘锦棠忧心忡忡，左宗棠为他出奏陈情说：“不虞兵机之迟钝，而忧饷事之难难，深惧仔肩难卸，掣时堪虞，将来恼不应手，必致上负圣恩，悔已无及。”这也是实在情形，即令宝鋆表示：“西饷可缓，洋款不必着急。”朝廷仍旧许他再借一笔外债，弥补协饷之不足。胡雪岩与福克，就是为这件事来的。

That was the first proposal. Then the matter was revived, and borrowing was again required. When Zuo was recalled within the passes to join the Grand Council, he was ordered to hand over both his special commission and his substantive office. Liu Jintang would succeed him as Imperial Commissioner Supervising Military Affairs in Xinjiang; Yang Changjun would act as Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu. Both were senior commanders under Zuo, but neither had sufficient standing and prestige to bear these responsibilities easily. Shaanxi and Gansu were poor and depended entirely on supplementary funds from the other provinces. If those provinces refused to cooperate, Liu and Yang would be helpless. Only by securing their military funding could Zuo make the achievements of his western campaigns lasting. He calculated the requirements on either side of Yumen Pass, which divided Xinjiang from Shaanxi and Gansu. The army beyond the pass needed 3.7 million taels a year; the forces within it needed 2.1 million, making 5.8 million in all. From the fifth year of Guangxu, imperial edicts had required the provinces to remit a full five million annually. The gap of eight hundred thousand could still be bridged by shifting funds between payments. But the edicts were one thing and the remittances another. In two years, provincial arrears had reached 4.2 million taels. Liu was deeply troubled. Zuo memorialized on his behalf: “His concern is not that military operations will falter, but that funding will prove difficult. He fears that the burden of responsibility cannot be discharged while his efforts are continually hampered. Should his plans prove impossible to carry out, he will fail the imperial trust, and regret will come too late.” These were real difficulties. Although Grand Secretary Bao Yun argued that “the western army’s funding can wait; there is no need to hurry over a foreign loan,” the court nevertheless authorized further borrowing to make up the shortfall. It was this business that had brought Hu and Focke to Beijing.

胡雪岩在左宗棠面前的信用，大不如前了。一则是借洋债及商款的利息过重，人言藉藉，连左宗棠都没面子，二则是采买军火有浮报情事。但左宗棠仍旧少不了胡雪岩，而胡雪岩亦想力盖前愆，对这趟借洋债，格外尽心尽力，希望左宗棠能对他的成绩满意。“雪岩，你信上说票要出给汇丰，怎么又是汇丰呢？”左宗棠指着福克说：“不是他们泰来洋行吗？”“是。一大半是泰来的款子，不过要由汇丰出面。”“这是什么讲究？”“汇丰是洋商的领袖。 要它出面，款子调度起来才容易。这好有一比。好比刘饮差、杨制台筹饷筹不动，只要大人登高一呼，马上万山响应，是一样的道理。“左宗棠平生一癖，是喜欢人恭维，听胡雪岩这一说，心里很舒服，“雪岩，”他说：“你这一阵子倚红偎翠之余，想来还读读书吧？”这话，想来是指着“登高一呼”、“万山响应”这两句话而说的。胡雪岩笑着答道：“大人太夸奖我了，哪里谈得到读书？无非上次大人教导我，闲下来看看，《唐诗三百首》，现在总算平仄也有点懂了，王黄也分得清了。”

Hu’s standing with Zuo was much lower than before. For one thing, the heavy interest on foreign loans and funds borrowed from merchants had provoked widespread criticism and embarrassed Zuo himself. For another, there had been inflated charges in the purchase of arms. Yet Zuo still could not dispense with Hu, and Hu wished to make amends. He was taking exceptional pains over this loan, hoping that the result would satisfy his patron. “Xueyan, your letter says that the bills must be made out to HSBC. Why that bank again?” Zuo pointed to Focke. “Isn’t the money coming from their Tailai firm?” “Yes. More than half comes from Tailai, but the bank must act as intermediary.” “Why is that?” “The bank is the leader among the foreign firms. With it taking the lead, the funds can be assembled more easily. Let me give you a comparison. Commissioner Liu and Governor-General Yang may have difficulty raising military funds, but if Your Excellency calls from the heights, ten thousand mountains answer. It is the same principle.” Zuo had a lifelong weakness for flattery, and this pleased him greatly. “Xueyan, between your embraces of the fair sex, I take it you have been doing a little reading?” Hu supposed that the phrases about calling from the heights and the answering mountains had prompted the remark. He smiled. “Your Excellency is too generous. I could hardly call it reading. Following your advice last time, I look through Three Hundred Tang Poems when I am free. I now understand a little about level and oblique tones, and can even tell wang and huang apart.”

“居然平仄也懂了，难得，难得。”左宗棠转脸看着福克说：“我本来打算借三百万，你一定要我多借一百万，我也许了你了，你利息上头，应该格外克己才是。”古应春司翻译之责，福克与凯密伦各有所言，及至他再翻给左宗棠听时，已非洋人原来的话了。福克的回答是：“不早就谈好了吗？”经古应春翻给左宗棠听是：“一钱一分。”“还是高了。”左宗棠的话刚完，胡雪岩便即接口：“是不是？”他向古应春说：“我早说大人不会答应的。你跟他说，无论如何不能超过一钱。”于是古应春便要求福克，就谈好的利率再减若干，福克自然不悦，便有了争执的模样。其间当然也牵涉到汇丰的利益，所以凯密伦亦有意见发表。最后，古应春说了句：“好吧！就照原议。”洋人都不响了。

“You even understand the tones now? Remarkable, remarkable!” Zuo turned to Focke. “I originally intended to borrow three million. You insisted on my borrowing another million, and I agreed. You ought to be particularly accommodating over the interest.” Gu served as interpreter. Focke and Cameron each had something to say, but what Gu translated back to Zuo was no longer what the foreigners had actually said. Focke’s answer was, “Wasn’t that agreed long ago?” What Gu rendered for Zuo was, “Eleven percent.” “Still too high.” No sooner had Zuo spoken than Hu put in, “There! Didn’t I say His Excellency would never agree?” He turned to Gu. “Tell him that under no circumstances can it exceed ten percent.” Gu accordingly asked Focke to reduce the rate they had already agreed upon. Focke was naturally displeased, and the conversation took on the appearance of an argument. Since the bank’s interests were involved too, Cameron also had his say. Finally Gu said, “Very well. We shall keep to the original terms.” The foreigners fell silent.

“怎么样？”胡雪岩问：“肯不肯减？”“福克跟凯密伦说：以前是一钱二分五。这回一钱一分已经减了。我跟他们说：你不能让胡先生没面子。总算勉强答应在一钱以内，九分七厘五。”“是年息？”“当然是年息。”于是胡雪岩转眼看着左宗棠，一面掐指甲，一面说道：“年息九分七厘五，合着月息只有八厘一毫二丝五。四百万两一个月的息钱是三万两千五，六个月也不过二十万银子。头两年只付息，不还本，第三年起始，每年拔还一百万，四年还清。大人看，这个章程行不行？”“一共是六年。”“是。”胡雪岩答说：“头两年只付息，不还本，我是磨了好久才磨下来的。这一两年各省关有余力还以前的洋款，就宽裕得多了。”

“Well?” Hu asked. “Will they reduce it?” “Focke and Cameron say the previous rate was twelve and a half percent, and eleven percent is already a reduction. I told them they could not make Mr Hu lose face. At last, though reluctantly, they agreed to come below ten percent: nine and three-quarters.” “That is annual interest?” “Of course.” Hu turned to Zuo, counting on his fingers. “Nine and three-quarters percent a year is only 0.8125 percent a month. On four million taels, that comes to thirty-two thousand five hundred a month, and no more than two hundred thousand for six months. During the first two years we pay interest only, without repaying principal. From the third year, we repay a million a year and clear the principal in four years. Does Your Excellency find that arrangement acceptable?” “Six years in all.” “Yes. I argued for a long time before securing those first two years without principal repayments. It will leave the provinces and customs offices much more room over the next year or two to pay off the earlier foreign loans.”

“好，好！”左宗棠连赞两声，然后俯身向前，很关切地问：“要不要海关出票？”“不要！”胡雪岩响亮地回答。“只要陕甘出票？”“是。只凭‘陕甘总督部堂’的关防就足够了。”左宗棠连连点头，表示满意，但也不免感慨系之，“陕甘总督的关防，总算也值钱了！”接着还叹口气：“唉！”“事在人为。”胡雪岩说：“陕西。甘肃是最穷最苦最偏僻的省份。除了俄国以外，哪怕是久住中国的外国人，也不晓得陕甘在哪里？如今不同了，都晓得陕甘有位左爵爷，洋人敬重大人的威名，连带陕甘总督的关防，比直隶两江还管用。“说到这里，他转脸关照古应春：”你问他们，如果李合肥要借洋款，他们要不要直隶总督衙门的印票。“古应春跟福克、凯密伦各说了一句不知什么话，等他们回答以后才说：“都说还是要关票。”听得这一句，左宗棠笑逐颜开，他一直自以为勋业过于李鸿章，如今则连办洋务都凌驾其上了。这份得意，自是非同小可。“好！我们就这样说定了。三两天后就出奏，这回宝中堂应该不会有后言了。”

“Good, good!” Zuo said approvingly. Then he leaned forward and asked with particular concern, “Will the customs have to issue bills?” “No!” Hu answered emphatically. “Only Shaanxi and Gansu?” “Yes. The official seal of the Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu will be sufficient.” Zuo nodded repeatedly in satisfaction, though the thought also stirred his feelings. “So the governor-general’s seal is worth something at last!” He sighed. “It all depends on the man,” Hu replied. “Shaanxi and Gansu are the poorest, most afflicted, and most remote provinces. Apart from the Russians, even foreigners who have lived in China for years scarcely knew where they were. Now it is different. They all know that Marquis Zuo is there. The foreigners respect Your Excellency’s great name, and consequently the seal of the Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu carries more weight than those of Zhili or Liangjiang.” He turned to Gu. “Ask them whether, if Li of Hefei wanted a foreign loan, they would accept bills bearing the seal of the Governor-General of Zhili.” Gu said something or other to Focke and Cameron. After hearing their replies, he announced, “Both say they would still require customs bills.” Zuo’s face lit up. He had always considered his achievements greater than Li Hongzhang’s; now he had surpassed him even in foreign affairs. His satisfaction was extraordinary. “Good! Then it is settled. I shall submit the memorial in two or three days. Grand Secretary Bao should have no grounds for criticism this time.”

胡雪岩不懂“后言”二字，不过意思可以猜得出来，而且他也有把握能使得宝釜服帖。因而提出最要紧的一句话。“有一层要先跟大人回明白，如今既然仍旧要汇丰来领头调度，那就仍旧要总理衙门给英国公使一个照会。”“这是一定的道理。我知道。”“还有一层，要请大人的示，是不是仍旧请大人给我一道札子。”下行公事叫“札子”，指令如何办理？左宗棠答说：“这不行！不在其位，不谋其政，你是陕西驻上海转运局的委员，应该杨制军下札子给你。”“是！不过，我有句话，不知道该不该说？”“你说，不要紧。”“同样是陕甘总督衙门下的札子，分量不一样。如果是大人的札子，我办事就方便得多了。”“呃，呃！我明白了。”左宗棠心想，杨昌浚的威望不够，胡雪岩即不能见重于人，为他办事顺利起见，这个障碍得替他消除。盘算了好一会，有个变通办法，“这样”，他说，“只要是牵涉到洋人，总署都管得到的，我在奏折上特为你叙一笔，请旨下总理衙门札饬道员胡某某遵照办理。你看如何？”胡雪岩喜出望外，因为这一来就是受命于恭亲王，声价又抬高了。不过，表面上却不敢有何形色，而用微感无奈的神情说：“如果大人不便下札子给我，那也就只好请总理衙门下了。”

Hu did not understand Zuo’s particular expression for criticism, though he could guess its meaning. He was also confident that he could bring Bao Yun into line. He therefore raised the most important point. “There is one thing I must make clear, Your Excellency. Since the bank must still take the lead in assembling the funds, the Zongli Yamen must still send a formal note to the British minister.” “Naturally. I understand.” “And there is one further matter on which I need your instructions. May I ask Your Excellency to issue me an official directive as before?” A directive was an official communication to a subordinate, specifying how he should proceed. “That will not do,” Zuo answered. “One should not direct affairs outside one’s office. You are an officer of the Shaanxi Transport Bureau in Shanghai. Governor-General Yang should issue your instructions.” “Yes. But there is something I am not sure I ought to say.” “Say it. There is no harm.” “Though both would come from the Governor-General’s office, they would not carry the same weight. With a directive from Your Excellency, my work would be much easier.” “Ah, I see.” Zuo reflected that Yang’s prestige was insufficient. Hu would therefore not command the necessary respect. To let him conduct the business smoothly, this obstacle must be removed. After considering for some time, he found a way around it. “The Zongli Yamen has authority over anything involving foreigners. I shall mention you specifically in the memorial and request an imperial order directing the Yamen to instruct Circuit Intendant Hu to carry out the arrangements. How would that be?” Hu was delighted. He would then be acting under Prince Gong, which would raise his standing still further. Outwardly, however, he showed none of this. With an air of slight resignation, he said, “If Your Excellency cannot conveniently issue the directive yourself, I suppose we must ask the Zongli Yamen to do so.”

“好！这就说定了。”左宗棠接着又说：“雪岩，我们打个商量，西边境况很窘，刘毅斋又要撤勇，打发的盘川还不知道在哪里？你能不能先凑一百万，尽快解到杨石泉那里。”毅斋、石泉分别是刘锦棠、杨昌浚的别号。胡雪岩责无旁贷，很爽快地答应了。这时有一名听差，悄然到左宗棠身边说了句话，他便问道：“这两个洋朋友，会不会用筷子？”左宗棠是打算留福克与凯密伦吃饭。胡雪岩觉得大可不必，便即答说：“大人不必费心了。”“那么，你留下来陪我谈谈。”“是。”见此光景，古应春便向洋人表示，公事已经谈妥，应该告辞了。接着便站起来请了个安，洋人亦起立鞠躬。左宗棠要送客，胡雪岩劝住，说是由他代送，乘此机会可跟古应春说几句话。

“Good. That is settled.” Zuo went on, “Xueyan, let us discuss something else. Conditions in the west are desperate. Liu Yizhai must disband some troops, and does not yet know where to find their journey money. Can you raise a million first and send it to Yang Shiquan as quickly as possible?” Yizhai and Shiquan were the respective alternate names of Liu Jintang and Yang Changjun. Hu could not evade the responsibility and readily agreed. At that moment an attendant approached Zuo and whispered something. Zuo asked, “Can these two foreign friends use chopsticks?” He intended to keep Focke and Cameron for a meal. Hu saw no need for that and answered, “Your Excellency need not trouble yourself.” “Then you stay and talk with me.” “Yes.” Gu understood that the official business was finished and told the foreigners they should take their leave. He rose and made his formal salutation; the foreigners stood and bowed. Zuo meant to see them out, but Hu persuaded him to remain, offering to do it for him. That would also give him a chance to speak to Gu.

“应春，你把他们送回去了，交代给陪他们的人，空出身体来办两件事。”胡雪岩交代，一件是跟汪惟贤去谈，能不能在京里与天津两处地方，筹划出一百万现银？“这件事马上要有回音。”胡雪岩轻声说道：“左大人一开了话匣子，先讲西征功劳，再骂曾文正，这顿饭吃下来，起码三个钟头，你三点钟以前来，我一定还在这里。”“好！还有一件呢？”“还有一件，你倒问问福克，王府井大街的德国洋行里，有没有望远镜、挂表。如果有，你问他有多少，先把它定下来。”“喔，”古应春明白了，是左宗棠应醇王之邀，到神机营“看操”，作犒赏用的，便即问说：“有是一定有的。不知道要多少？”现在还不知道。你先问了再说。“古应春答应着，陪着洋人回阜康福。下午三点钟复又回到贤良寺，果然，那顿午饭尚未结束，他在花厅外面等待时，听得左宗棠正在谈“湖湘子弟满天山”的盛况，中气十足，毫无倦容，看来还得有些时候才会散。古应春心想，胡雪岩急于要知道交办两事的结果，无非是即席可以向左宗棠报告。既然如此，就不必等着面谈，写个条于通知他好了。打定主意，便从怀中掏出一个洋纸笔记本来，撕一张纸，抽出本子上所附的铅笔，蘸一点口水，写道：“现银此间有三十万，天津约十余万。镜表各约百余具，已付定。惟大小参差不齐。”

“Yingchun, take them back and leave them with the men attending them. Then free yourself to do two things.” First, he was to speak to Wang Weixian and discover whether a million taels in ready silver could be raised between Beijing and Tianjin. “I need an answer immediately,” Hu said quietly. “Once His Excellency starts talking, he first recounts his western victories and then abuses Zeng Wenzheng. This meal will last at least three hours. Come before three, and I am sure to be here.” “Very well. What is the other thing?” “Ask Focke whether the German trading house in Wangfujing Street has telescopes and pocket watches. If it does, find out how many and reserve them.” Gu understood: these were rewards for the Shenji Camp when Zuo accepted Prince Chun’s invitation to review its drill. “They certainly have them. How many do you need?” “I do not know yet. Ask first.” Gu agreed and accompanied the foreigners back to Fukangfu Bank. At three that afternoon he returned to Xianliang Temple. Just as Hu had predicted, lunch was not over. Waiting outside the reception hall, he heard Zuo describing the grand sight of “the sons of Hunan filling the Tianshan.” His voice was powerful, with no sign of weariness; the meal might continue for some time. Gu thought that Hu wanted the answers urgently only so that he could report to Zuo there and then. There was no need to wait for a conversation; he could send a note. He took out a notebook of foreign paper, tore off a sheet, and moistened the point of its attached pencil with his tongue. He wrote: “Three hundred thousand in ready silver available here, somewhat over a hundred thousand in Tianjin. A little over a hundred each of telescopes and watches; deposits paid. Sizes vary.”

这张字条传至席面时，为左宗棠发现问起，胡雪岩正好开口，“回大人，”他说：“京里现银可以凑五十万，一两日内就解出去，另外一半，等我回上海以后，马上去想法子。不知道来得及，来不及？”“能有一半先解，其余慢一点不要紧。”“是。”胡雪岩又问：“听说醇亲王要请大人到神机营去看操？”“有这回事”。“一提到此，左宗棠的精神又来了，”神机营是八旗劲旅中的精华。醇王现在以皇上本身父的身分，别样政务都不能管，只管神机营，上头对神机营的看重，可想而知。李少荃在北洋好几年了，醇王从未请他去看过操，我一到京，头一回见面，他就约我，要我定日子，他好下令会操。我心里想，人家敬重我，我不能不替醇王做面子。想等你来了商量，应该怎么样犒赏？““大人的意思呢？”“我想每人犒赏五两银子，按人数照算。”“神机营的士兵，不过万把人，五、六万银子的事，我替大人预备好了。”胡雪岩又说：“不过现银只能犒赏士兵，对官长似乎不大妥当。”

Zuo noticed the note being passed to the table and asked about it, giving Hu his opportunity. “Your Excellency, we can assemble five hundred thousand in ready silver in Beijing and send it off within a day or two. I shall find the other half as soon as I return to Shanghai. Will that be soon enough?” “If half can be sent first, a little delay over the rest does not matter.” “Yes. I also hear that Prince Chun wishes to invite Your Excellency to review the Shenji Camp.” The mention revived Zuo’s spirits. “Indeed. The Shenji Camp is the flower of the Eight Banners’ best troops. As the emperor’s natural father, Prince Chun cannot concern himself with other affairs of government; he has charge only of the Shenji Camp. You can imagine how highly it is regarded at court. Li Shaoquan has been in charge of the Beiyang for years, yet the prince has never invited him to a review. At our very first meeting after I reached Beijing, he invited me and asked me to name a day so that he could order a combined drill. When someone honors me, I must return the honor and give the prince face. I meant to wait for you and discuss suitable rewards.” “What did Your Excellency have in mind?” “Five taels for every man, calculated according to their numbers.” “There are only about ten thousand soldiers in the Shenji Camp. Fifty or sixty thousand taels would cover it. I have made provision for that on your behalf. But silver seems suitable only for the soldiers, not quite appropriate for their officers.”

“是啊！我也是这么想。”“我看送东西好了。送当然也要实用，而且是军用。我有个主意，大人看能不能用。”“你说。”“每人送一架望远镜、一个挂表。”话刚完，左宗棠便击案称赞，“这两佯东西好！很切实用。”他说：“神机营的官长一百多，要一百多份，不知道备得齐，备不齐？”“大人定了主意，我马上写信到上海，尽快送来。我想日子上一定来得及。“胡雪岩紧接着说：”大人去看操的日子，最好等借洋款的事办妥了再定。不然，恐怕有人会说闲话，说大人很阔，西饷一定很宽裕，洋款缓一缓不要紧。“不等他话完，左宗棠便连连点着头说：“你倒提醒了我。此事虽小，足以影响大局。我准定照你的话办。”“是！”胡雪岩问：“大人还有什么交代？”“一时倒想不起，想起来再跟你谈。”左宗棠说：“借洋款的章程，你马上写个节略来，我尽明天一天办好奏稿递上去，倘或顺利的话，大概三五大就定局了。”“是！”胡雪岩说道：“明天我想跟大人告一天假，办办私事。后来来伺侯。”“后天如果没事也不必来。有事我会随时派人来招呼你，你尽管办你自己的事去好了。”

“Exactly. I thought so too.” “We should give the officers presents—useful things, naturally, and useful for military purposes. I have an idea, if Your Excellency approves.” “Tell me.” “A telescope and a pocket watch for each.” As soon as Hu finished speaking, Zuo struck the table in approval. “Excellent choices! Very practical. There are more than a hundred officers. Can you obtain enough sets?” “Once Your Excellency decides, I shall write to Shanghai and have them sent as quickly as possible. I am sure they can arrive in time.” Hu added at once, “It would be best to fix the day of the review only after the foreign loan is settled. Otherwise someone may gossip that Your Excellency is so wealthy that the western army must have ample funds, and the loan can wait.” Before he had finished, Zuo was nodding repeatedly. “You have reminded me of something important. A small matter, but enough to affect the whole situation. I shall certainly do as you say.” “Does Your Excellency have any further instructions?” “Nothing occurs to me just now. I shall tell you when it does. Write me a summary of the loan terms at once. I shall have the memorial ready and submit it by tomorrow. If all goes smoothly, the matter should be settled within three to five days.” “I should like to ask for tomorrow off to attend to some private business. I shall return the day after.” “If there is nothing to do, you need not come the day after either. I shall send for you whenever necessary. Attend to your own affairs.”

于是胡雪岩告辞回阜康福，先请杨师爷将借洋款的条件写成一个节略，即刻派人送到贤良寺。然后向古应春细问到海岳山房接头的经过。“应春，你知道的，为了去年买水雷的价钱，福德多嘴泄了底，左大人对我已经起疑心了。这件事我心里很难过，所以这趟借洋款，除了大家该得的好处以外，我不但分文不要，而且预备贴几万银子，一定要把这件事办成功。办成功不算，还要办得漂亮，要叫左大人心里舒服，倘或宝中堂罗嗦，就算办成功，他也不会高兴，所以宝中堂那里，一定要摆平，能听他说一句：这笔洋款借得划算，我这几万银子，花得就值了。”“小爷叔的心思，我是早看出来了。不过，我想也不必把钱花在宝中堂一个人身上，他手下的人也是要紧的。”古应春问道：“小爷叔预备花多少。”“这个数。”胡雪岩将手一伸。“那么，送四万，留一万作开销。”“好的。你跟徐筱云去商量，看这条路子应该怎么样走通？”

Hu took his leave and returned to Fukangfu Bank. He first had Secretary Yang prepare a summary of the loan conditions and immediately sent it to Xianliang Temple. Then he asked Gu for a detailed account of his dealings at Haiyue Shanfang. “Yingchun, you know that Fude talked too freely about the price of last year’s naval mines and gave the game away. His Excellency has been suspicious of me ever since. That troubles me deeply. This time, apart from the benefits properly due to everyone else, I shall take nothing myself. I am even prepared to spend several tens of thousands of taels of my own money. I must make the loan succeed, and success alone is not enough: it must be done handsomely, so that His Excellency feels pleased. If Grand Secretary Bao makes difficulties, even a successful loan will give him no pleasure. Bao must therefore be brought round. If I can hear him say, ‘This loan was obtained on favorable terms,’ my money will have been well spent.” “I understood your intention long ago, Little Uncle. But I do not think all the money should go to Bao himself. His subordinates matter too. How much do you intend to spend?” “This much.” Hu held out his hand. “Then forty thousand for him, and ten thousand for expenses.” “Very well. Discuss it with Xu Xiaoyun and see how this approach can be made to work.”

第二天三月初九，徐彼云不待去请，自己来访，胡雪岩不在，由古应春接待，他告诉古应春说，左宗棠的奏槁是他办的，已经誊正呈递。不过，三五天内，决不会有结果，因为恭亲王为福晋安葬，请了七天假，而这件大事，非恭亲王来议不可。“这样说，宝中堂也不能起作用？”“不，不！有作用的，恭王听他的话，而且凡是到了这个地位，不管怎么样，败事总是有余的。”“筱翁，这么说，胡大先生要重重拜托你，海岳山房我去过了，跟老朱谈得很好。胡大先生要我跟筱翁商量，这条路子一定要走通，你看该送多少？”“借洋款的条件比过去都好，我的奏稿上写得很切实，事情一定可成，不送亦可，要送，有这差不多了。”说着，徐用仪示以一指。“筱翁，‘差不多’不够，要势在必成。”“多送当然更保险，不过钱要用在刀口上。”徐用仪问道：“明天你会去贤良寺不会？”“会去，明天我带洋人给左大人去辞行。”“那么，我们明天中午在贤良寺见，到时候我再跟你谈。”

The next day, the ninth day of the third lunar month, Xu came without waiting to be invited. Hu was out, so Gu received him. Xu explained that he had drafted Zuo’s memorial, which had already been copied and submitted. There could be no result within three to five days, however. Prince Gong had taken seven days’ leave for his consort’s burial, and such an important matter could not be discussed without him. “Then Grand Secretary Bao can have no effect either?” “On the contrary, he can. Prince Gong listens to him. Besides, anyone in such a position always has more than enough power to spoil things.” “Mr Xiaoyun, in that case Mr Hu must ask a great favor of you. I have been to Haiyue Shanfang and had an excellent talk with Zhu. Mr Hu asked me to consult you. We must make this approach succeed. How much do you think we should send?” “The loan conditions are better than before, and my memorial sets them out very clearly. The business is sure to succeed. You need send nothing, but if you do, about this much should be enough.” Xu held up one finger. “Mr Xiaoyun, ‘about enough’ is not enough. We need certainty.” “More money will naturally make it safer. But it should be used where it counts. Will you be at Xianliang Temple tomorrow?” “Yes. I shall take the foreigners to say goodbye to His Excellency.” “Then we shall meet there at noon. I shall speak to you again.”

第二天中午胡雪岩、古应春带着两个洋人，都到了贤良寺，静等左宗棠自军机处散值回寓，以便辞行。哪知一等等到下午三点半钟，还不见人影，亦无消息。宫门申正下钥，申正就是四点钟，通常军机处自大臣至章京人，最迟未正二刻，也就是两点半钟，一定已走得光光，而左宗棠到此时尚未出宫，是件令人百思不得其解的事。“只怕宫里出事了。”胡雪岩悄悄跟古应春耳语：“莫非西太后的病，起了变化？”一语未终，只见徐用仪匆匆而来，他也顾不得行礼，一把将胡雪岩拉到僻处，低声说道：“左大人叫我来送个信，洋人慢点走，事情或许会有波折。”“怎么？”胡雪岩又问：“左大人何以到现在还不出宫。”“宫里出了件意想不到的怪事。”徐用仪的声音越发低了，“今天军机没有叫起，说太后受了寒，人不舒服。大家都当是感冒，到内奏事处看药方，管事太监说没有发下来。后来听内务府的人说，是昨天下午发的病，突然之间，口吐白沫，象发羊癫疯。今天到现在为止，已经请了三次脉，早晨一次，午时一次，未时一次，人只怕不中用了。”“慢慢，筱翁，”胡雪岩问道：“你说是东太后，还是西太后？”“是东太后。”“东太后？”胡雪岩越发诧异。“自然是东太后。西太后好久不视朝，因为东太后违和，军机才没有叫起。”“喔。”胡雪岩点点头说：“我知道了。我来把洋人留下来。”

At noon the following day, Hu and Gu took the two foreigners to Xianliang Temple. They waited for Zuo to return from duty at the Grand Council so that they could take their leave. Yet half past three came with no sign of him and no message. The palace gates were locked at four. Ordinarily, everyone from the grand councillors to their secretaries had left by half past two at the latest. Why Zuo should still be inside the palace was utterly baffling. “Something must have happened in the palace,” Hu whispered to Gu. “Could the Western Empress Dowager’s illness have taken a turn?” Before he had finished, Xu hurried in. Disregarding formal greetings, he pulled Hu aside and lowered his voice. “His Excellency sent me with a message. The foreigners must delay their departure. There may be complications.” “Why? And why has His Excellency not left the palace yet?” “Something extraordinary and quite unexpected has happened.” Xu spoke still more quietly. “The Grand Council was not summoned to an audience today. We were told that the Empress Dowager had caught a chill and felt unwell. Everyone supposed it was a cold. We went to the Inner Memorial Office to look at the prescription, but the eunuch in charge said that none had been sent down. Later, someone from the Imperial Household Department told us that she fell ill suddenly yesterday afternoon, foaming at the mouth as though in an epileptic seizure. Her pulse has been examined three times today: in the morning, at noon, and in the early afternoon. I fear she may not survive.” “Wait, Mr Xiaoyun. Do you mean the Eastern Empress Dowager or the Western?” “The Eastern.” “The Eastern?” Hu was still more astonished. “Of course. The Western Empress Dowager has not attended court for a long time. It was because the Eastern Empress Dowager was unwell that the Grand Council was not called.” “I understand.” Hu nodded. “I shall keep the foreigners here.”

于是胡雪岩向古应春密言经过，关照他先带洋人回去，随便找个理由，请他们暂留几天。“如果东太后真的驾崩了，宫里要办丧事，洋款的事就会搁下来。”胡雪岩问道：“应春，你看左大人会怎么办？”“这一搁下来，”古应春答非所问地：“人家款子早已筹好了，吃利息犹在其次，倘或一搁搁得不办了，对人家怎么交代？”“这不会的。”胡雪岩说：“吃利息还是小焉者也，刘毅斋、杨石泉筹饷急如星火，这上头耽误了才是大事。”“那么，大先生，你看左大人会怎么办呢？”“自然是独断独行，办了再说。”以左宗棠的性情，这是可能的，但古应春终有疑惑，因为四百万银子到底不是个小数目，左宗棠即令有魄力，也不敢如此擅专。

Hu privately explained the situation to Gu and told him to take the foreigners back, find some pretext, and ask them to remain for a few days. “If the Eastern Empress Dowager really dies, the court will have a funeral to arrange, and the loan will be put aside. What do you think His Excellency will do, Yingchun?” Gu answered a different question. “The money has already been assembled on their side. Interest is the least of it. What if the matter is put off and then abandoned altogether? How could we explain that to them?” “That will not happen. Interest is a minor matter. Liu Yizhai and Yang Shiquan need military funds with desperate urgency. Delay there would be the serious problem.” “Then what do you think His Excellency will do, Mr Hu?” “Act on his own authority and settle the rest afterward.” Given Zuo’s character, this was possible. Yet Gu remained doubtful. Four million taels was no small amount. Bold though Zuo was, surely he would not dare act so arbitrarily.

左宗棠是过了四点才回贤良寺的，一到就传胡雪岩，“国将大变！”他一开口就发感慨，接着又说：“应变要早。你告诉福克他们，事情就算定局了，请他们一回上海就预备款子。印票现成，我带得有盖了陕甘总督关防的空白文书，一填就是，让他们带了去。”果如胡雪岩所料，但他不能不为左宗棠的前程着想，“大人，”他很直爽地说，“数目太大，将来宝大人会不会说闲话？”“说闲话也是没法子的事。”左宗棠又说：“将在外，君命有所不受。现在连‘君命’都没有，我辈身为勋臣，与国同休戚，不能不从权处置。““大人，我倒有个想法。这件事，大人何妨跟醇王说一说，醇王是带兵的，总知道‘闹饷’不是闹着玩的。”“通极！”左宗棠拍着膝盖说：“有他知道这回事，谅宝佩蘅也不敢再说闲话。”宝佩蘅就是宝鋆.胡雪岩心想，要他不说闲话，只有找海岳山房朱铁口，否则即使不敢说闲话，也尽有刁难的手段。

Zuo returned to Xianliang Temple after four and immediately summoned Hu. “The state faces a great upheaval!” he exclaimed. “We must prepare early. Tell Focke and the others to consider the matter settled and have the money ready as soon as they return to Shanghai. The sealed documents are at hand. I brought blank forms bearing the official seal of the Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu. We need only fill them in and let the foreigners take them.” It was exactly as Hu had predicted. But he could not neglect the danger to Zuo’s future. “Your Excellency,” he said frankly, “the sum is so large. Might Grand Secretary Bao criticize it afterward?” “If he does, it cannot be helped. A commander in the field may decline to obey the sovereign’s orders. Here we have not even received an order. As ministers who have served the state and share its fortunes, we must act as circumstances require.” “I have a suggestion. Why not mention it to Prince Chun? He commands troops and must understand that disturbances over unpaid wages are no laughing matter.” “Excellent!” Zuo slapped his knee. “Once he knows, I doubt Bao Peiheng will dare say anything against it.” Bao Peiheng was Bao Yun. Hu thought that the only way to silence him was through Zhu Tiekou at Haiyue Shanfang. Otherwise, even if Bao dared not speak against the plan, he had plenty of ways to obstruct it.

“我得躺一会。”左宗棠说：“今天晚上，说不定宫里会出大事。”“是。”胡雪岩乘机打听，“刚才徐筱云来传大人的话，说起东太后政躬违和，仿佛来势不轻呢？”“岂止来势不轻，牙齿都撬不开了。”“那么，到底是什么病呢？”“谁知道？”左宗棠将两手一拍，“牝鸡司晨，终非佳事。”胡雪岩听不懂他说的什么，站起身来告辞，“明天再来伺侯。”他请了个安。“明天，明天还不知道怎么样呢？”

“I must lie down for a while,” Zuo said. “Something momentous may happen in the palace tonight.” “Yes.” Hu took the opportunity to inquire further. “When Xu Xiaoyun brought your message just now, he mentioned that the Eastern Empress Dowager was unwell. It sounded serious.” “More than serious. They cannot even pry her jaws apart.” “What illness is it, then?” “Who knows?” Zuo brought his hands together. “A hen crowing to announce the dawn never bodes well.” Hu did not understand him. He rose to take his leave and made his salutation. “I shall come again tomorrow.” “Tomorrow? Who knows what tomorrow will bring?”
