第四章 美人计｜CHAPTER FOUR: THE BEAUTY STRATAGEM

胡雪岩在上海，一直等得到左宗棠的确实信息，已于十月十八日出京，但不是由天津乘海轮南下，经上海转江宁去接两江总督的任，而是先回湖南扫墓，预计要到年底快封印时，才会到任。胡雪岩本打算在上海迎接左宗棠，等他动身赴江宁后，再回杭州，见此光景，决定先回去了再来。回到杭州的第二天，他就将公济典的管总唐子韶约了来，将打算全盘调动二十三家典当的管总，趁彼此移交的机会，自然而然作了一次大清查的计划，告诉了他。“子韶，”他说，“我这二十三家典当，你算是他们的头儿。这件事，我要请你来做，你去拟个章程来，顶好在年里办妥当，明年开头，家家都是一本新帐，界限分明，清清楚楚。你说呢？”唐子韶一愣，心里七上八下，念头很多，定一定神说：“大先生，年底下，景况好的要来赎当头，年过不去的，要求当当，生意正忙的时候，来个大调动，不弄得天下大乱？”“这话倒也不错。不过章程可以先拟，叫大家预备起来，一过了年，逢到淡月，再来调动。”“是的。这样子才是正办。”

Hu Xueyan remained in Shanghai until definite news of Zuo Zongtang arrived. Zuo had left Beijing on the eighteenth day of the tenth lunar month, but instead of taking a seagoing steamer south from Tianjin and passing through Shanghai on his way to assume the Liangjiang governor-generalship at Jiangning, he was first returning to Hunan to visit his ancestral graves. He was not expected to take up his post until near the year's end, when official seals were put away for the New Year holiday. Hu had intended to welcome him in Shanghai and return to Hangzhou only after Zuo left for Jiangning. Now he decided to go home first and come back later. On his second day in Hangzhou, he summoned Tang Zishao, manager of Gongji Pawnshop, and explained his plan to transfer all twenty-three pawnshop managers. Their handovers would naturally provide an opportunity for a thorough inspection of every shop. “Zishao, you are the senior man among the managers of my twenty-three shops. I want you to handle this. Draw up a scheme, preferably to be completed before the year's end. Then every shop can begin next year with a fresh set of accounts, the dividing line clear and everything in order. What do you say?” Tang was taken aback. A multitude of anxious thoughts crowded his mind. Collecting himself, he replied, “At the end of the year, Mr Hu, those who are doing well come to redeem their pledges, while those who cannot make ends meet must pawn things. Wouldn't a general reshuffle at our busiest time throw everything into confusion?” “There is something in that. But you can prepare the scheme first and let everyone get ready. Once the New Year is over, make the transfers in a quiet month.” “Yes. That is the proper way.”

奉命回来，唐子韶立即找到管包潘茂承，关起门来密谈。原来唐潘勾结舞弊，已历多年，毛病最多的是满当的衣服，公济典为了满当的衣服太多，特为设了一家估衣铺，招牌叫做“公济衣庄”，各典满当的衣服，都发衣庄去叫卖，有的原封不动，有的是掉了包的，明明一件八成新“萝卜丝，，的羊裘，送到衣庄，变了一件”光板“，当铺”写票“，向来将值钱的东西写得一文不值，明明是个金打簧表，当票上却写的是”黄铜烂表一个“。那笔龙飞凤舞的狂草，除了朝奉自己，无人能识，所以从无顾客提过抗议，而因为如此”写票“记帐，满当之物要掉包，亦就无从查考了。公济典掉包掉的最凶，紫貂换成紫羔，纺绸换成竹衣，拿来跟公济衣庄的进货帐一对，清弊毕现，那时就会弄得难看了。谈来谈去，唯一的挽救之道，便是根本打消这个计划。但除了以年底生意忙碌，不宜大事更张的说法，将此事缓得一缓以外，别无可以驳倒此一计划的理由。潘茂承一筹莫展，唐子韶却想到了一个万不得已的主意，不过这个主意只能悄悄去做，决不能声张，而且能不能做，还要看他的姨太太肯不肯。

On returning from these instructions, Tang immediately sought out Pan Maocheng, the custodian of pledged goods, and shut the door for a confidential discussion. The two had been defrauding the business together for years. Their worst irregularities involved clothing left unredeemed after the loan period expired. Gongji had so much of this that it had opened a second-hand clothing shop of its own, under the sign of Gongji Clothing House. Forfeited garments from the various pawnshops were sent there for sale. Some arrived unchanged; others had been switched. A nearly new sheepskin coat of the fine “radish-thread” variety, for instance, would turn into a plain-skin coat by the time it reached the clothing shop. Pawn tickets habitually described valuable objects as worthless. A gold repeater watch might be entered as “one broken brass watch.” The wildly cursive handwriting could be read by nobody but the appraiser himself, so customers never protested. With tickets and accounts written in this fashion, substitutions among forfeited pledges were impossible to trace. At Gongji, the switching was particularly flagrant: dark sable became dark lambskin, and silk garments became bamboo ones. A comparison with the clothing shop's stock-entry accounts would expose the irregularities, and then matters would look very ugly. After much discussion, they could see only one way out: have the whole transfer scheme abandoned. The year-end rush provided a reason to postpone it, but beyond that they could find no argument against it. Pan was at his wits' end. Tang, however, thought of one desperate expedient. It must be carried out secretly, without a word getting about, and whether it could be attempted at all depended on his concubine's willingness.

原来唐子韶是徽州人，徽州朝奉到外地谋生，都不带家眷，胡雪岩看他客中寂寞，三年前送了他一个名叫月如的“丫头做姨太太。月如自从嫁了唐子韶，不到半年功夫，竟似脱胎换骨变了另一个人，头发本来发黄，变黑变多了，皮肤本来粗糙，变白变细了，她的身材本来不坏，此时越发显得蜂腰丰臀，逗人避思，尤其是那双眼睛，本来呆滞失神，老象没有睡足似的，忽然变得水汪汪地，顾盼之间，仿佛一道闪光，慑人心魄。为此，胡雪岩颇为动心，言谈神气之间，每每流露出跃跃欲试之情。唐子韶早已发觉，只是装做不知而已。如今事急无奈，才想到了这条美人计，若能说服月如，事成一半了。

Tang came from Huizhou. Appraisers from that region did not bring their families when they went elsewhere to earn a living. Seeing how lonely he was away from home, Hu had given him a maid named Yueru as a concubine three years earlier. Within six months of her marriage, she had seemed transformed into another woman. Her yellowish hair had grown thick and black; her coarse skin had become fair and smooth. Her figure had always been good, but now her slender waist and full hips stood out more strikingly, inviting thoughts of pleasure. Most remarkable were her eyes. Once dull and vacant, as though she never had enough sleep, they had become bright and liquid; a glance from them seemed to flash with a power that seized the heart. Hu was greatly attracted. His manner and conversation often betrayed an eagerness to try his chances. Tang had noticed long ago and merely pretended not to know. Now, in desperate circumstances, he thought of using her beauty as his stratagem. If he could persuade Yueru, half the task would be done.

事先经过一番盘算，决定胁以利害，“月如，”他说：“祸事临头了。”“祸事？”月如自不免吃惊，急急问说：“你闯了什么祸？”“也可以说是我自己闯的祸。”他指着月如头上插的一只翠玉钗，手上戴的一个祖母绿的戒指问道：“你知道不知道，这些东西哪里来的？”“不是满当货吗？”不错，应该是满当货，我当做原主来赎了回去了。“唐子韶说：”这就算做手脚舞弊，查出来不得了。““不会的，大先生为人顶厚道，你跟他老实说一声，认个错，他不会为难你的。”“没有用，不是我一个人的事，一定会查出来。到那时候，不用大先生开口请我走路，我自己也没有这张脸再在杭州混了，只好回家吃老米饭。”

After considering his approach, Tang decided to frighten her with the consequences. “Disaster is upon us, Yueru.” “Disaster?” Alarmed, she asked at once, “What trouble have you got yourself into?” “You could say I brought it on myself.” He pointed to the jade hairpin in her hair and the emerald ring on her hand. “Do you know where those came from?” “Weren't they forfeited pledges?” “They should have been. I entered them as having been redeemed by their original owners and took them away. That counts as tampering with the records and fraud. If it comes out, there will be terrible trouble.” “There won't. Mr Hu is a very generous man. Tell him honestly and admit your fault. He won't make things difficult for you.” “That is no use. I am not the only person involved. It is bound to come out. Then he won't have to dismiss me: I shall have no face to remain in Hangzhou. I shall have to go home and live on what we have put by.”

唐子韶紧接着又哭丧着脸说：“在我自己是自作孽，心里难过的是害了你。”“害了我？”月如大惊，“怎么会害了我？”“你想，第一，作弊抓到，自然要赔，你的首饰只怕一样都不会剩，第二，你跟我回徽州要吃苦，那种苦，你怎么吃得来？”月如平时听唐子韶谈过家乡的情形，徽州在万山丛中，地少人多，出产不丰，所以男人都出外经商，女人就要做男人做的事，挑水劈柴，样样都来，比江浙那个地方的女人都来得辛苦。而况，她又想到自己的身分，见了唐子韶的原配，要她做低服小，早晚伺候，更是件宁死也不愿的事。转念到此，不由得大为着急，“你也真是！”她埋怨着说：“正薪俸以外，每个月分”存箱“、”使用“、”公抽“、”当厘“、”赎厘“，外快已经不少了，年底还有分红，舒舒服服的日子不过，何苦又另外去搞花样？”

Looking utterly miserable, Tang continued, “I have brought this on myself. What grieves me is that I have ruined you too.” “Ruined me?” Yueru was shocked. “How have you ruined me?” “Think. First, once the fraud is discovered, restitution must be made. I fear not one piece of your jewellery will remain. Second, you will have to come back to Huizhou with me and suffer hardships. How could you bear that kind of life?” She had often heard him describe his home country. Huizhou lay among countless mountains, with little land, many people and scant produce. The men therefore went away to trade, leaving the women to do men's work: carrying water, chopping firewood, everything. Their lives were harder than those of women anywhere in Jiangsu or Zhejiang. Then she thought of her own position. Before Tang's principal wife she would have to be humble and submissive, serving her morning and evening. She would rather die than endure that. The thought made her frantic. “Really, what were you thinking?” she reproached him. “Besides your salary, every month you get shares of storage fees, sales commissions, pooled extra interest, loan fees and redemption fees. Those extras are substantial, and there is a dividend at the year's end. Why couldn't you enjoy a comfortable life instead of devising further tricks?”

月如嫁过来虽只三年，当铺的规矩，已经很熟悉了。典当从，“内缺”的管总、管包、管钱、管帐，到“外缺”站柜台的朝奉，以下“中缺”的写票、清票、卷包、挂牌，还有学徒，每月正薪以外，还有“外快”可分，贵重衣服，须加意保管，例收当本百分之一的酬劳，称为“存箱”，满当货卖出，抽取六厘，归伙友所得，称为“使用”，典当宽限，例不过五，赎当时不超过五天，不另计息，但如超过六天，要付两个月利息。遇到这种情形，多出来的一个月利息亦归伙友，称为“公抽”。至于“当厘”是照当本抽一厘，“赎厘”是照赎本抽三厘，譬如这个月当本支出十万两银子，赎本收回五万银子，就有一百两银子的“当厘”，一百五十两银子的“赎厘”。这些外快，汇总了每月公分，所得多寡的比例不同，唐子韶是管总，当然得大份，每个月少则五六十两，多则上百，日子过得着实宽裕。

Though she had been married to Tang for only three years, Yueru already knew the customs of the pawnshop well. Every grade of staff shared in extras beyond the regular monthly wage: the inside posts of manager, pledge custodian, cashier and bookkeeper; the outside posts of appraisers at the counter; the intermediate ranks who wrote and checked tickets, wrapped bundles and attached labels; and even the apprentices. Valuable clothes required special care, for which a fee of one per cent of the loan principal was customarily charged, called cunxiang, the storage fee. When forfeited goods were sold, six-tenths of one per cent was deducted for the employees, under the name shiyong, the sales commission. The customary grace period did not exceed five days: redemption within five extra days incurred no additional interest, but a delay of more than six days required payment of two months' interest. The extra month's interest then went to the staff as gongchou, the pooled levy. The dangli, or loan fee, was one-tenth of one per cent of the principal advanced; the shuli, or redemption fee, was three-tenths of one per cent of the principal repaid. Thus, if a shop advanced a hundred thousand taels in a month and recovered fifty thousand, the loan fees would amount to a hundred taels and the redemption fees to a hundred and fifty. These extras were pooled and divided each month in differing proportions. As manager, Tang naturally received a large share: fifty or sixty taels in a lean month, over a hundred in a good one. His circumstances were decidedly comfortable.

唐子韶自然亦有悔意，不过，“事情做也已经做了，你埋怨也没胡。”他说，“如今只有想法子来补救，你如果愿意，我再来动脑筋。”“我愿意有什么用？”“当然有用。只要你说一句，愿意不愿？”“哪里会不愿意？你倒说，为啥只要我说一句愿意，就有用处？”“这因为，你身上就有一样有用处的东西，只问你肯不肯借出来用一用？你要肯，拿出来就是。“月如将他的话，细细体味了一会，恍然大悟，板起脸问：“你要我借给哪个用？”“还有哪个？自然是胡大先生。”“哼！”月如冷笑，“我就晓得你会出这种不要脸的主意！”“人人要脸，树树要皮，我哪里会不要脸？不过事急无奈，与其让同行骂我不要脸，不如在胡大先生面前不要脸。你说，我的打算莫非错了？”“你的打算没有错。不过，你不要脸，我要脸。”“这件事，他知、你知、我知，没有第四个人晓得，你的脸面一定保得住。”月如不作声，显然是同意了。

Tang did feel regret, but he said, “What is done is done. Reproaches are no use. We must find a remedy now. If you are willing, I can think of something.” “What use is my being willing?” “Of course it is useful. Just tell me: are you willing or not?” “How could I be unwilling? But explain why a single word from me could help.” “Because you possess something useful. The question is whether you will lend it for a little while. If you agree, you need only offer it.” Yueru considered his words, then suddenly understood. Her face hardened. “To whom do you want me to lend myself?” “Who else? Mr Hu, naturally.” She gave a cold laugh. “I knew you would come up with some shameless scheme like this!” “Every man needs his face, as every tree needs its bark. Do you think I want to lose mine? But there is no other way in an emergency. Better to lose face before Mr Hu than be called shameless by everyone in the trade. Is my calculation wrong?” “Your calculation is sound. But though you can do without your dignity, I cannot.” “Only he, you and I will know. There will be no fourth person. Your reputation will be safe.” Yueru said nothing. Her silence plainly signified agreement.

“大先生。”唐子韶说：“这件事我想要跟蓉斋商量，他的脑筋好，一定有妥当办法想出来。”蓉斋姓施，此人是湖州德清城内公顺典的管总。为人极其能干，公顺典在他一手经营，每年盈余总是居首，论规模大小，本来在二十三家典当中排列第五、六，如今是最大的一家，架本积列三十万千文之多，胡雪岩心想，唐子韶要跟施蓉斋去商量，是办事的正道，所以毫不迟疑地同意了。“大先生，有没有话要我带给蓉斋？”“有的。”胡雪岩问道：“你哪一天走？”“我随时可以走。”“好的，等我想一想再告诉你。”

“Mr Hu,” Tang said, “I should like to discuss this matter with Rongzhai. He has a good head and is sure to devise a suitable arrangement.” Rongzhai's surname was Shi. He managed Gongshun Pawnshop in Deqing, in Huzhou Prefecture, and was exceptionally capable. Under his sole management, its annual profit consistently ranked first. Originally fifth or sixth in size among the twenty-three shops, it was now the largest, with accumulated shelf capital of three hundred thousand strings of a thousand cash. Hu thought that consulting Shi Rongzhai was precisely the right way to proceed and agreed without hesitation. “Have you any message for Rongzhai, sir?” “Yes. When are you leaving?” “Whenever you wish.” “Good. Let me think, and I shall tell you.”

“这样好了，”唐子韶问：“大先生哪天中午有空？”这些问胡雪岩十二个姨太太中，排行第五的宋娘子，胡雪岩有应酬部归她管，当下叫丫头去问，回话是一连十天都不定，而且抄了一张单子来。哪天人家请，哪天请人家，写得清清楚楚。“你问我哪天中午有空，为啥？”“是月如，总想弄几个菜孝敬大先生。我想不如请大先生来便饭，有什么交代蓉斋的话，顺便就可以告诉我了。”听这一说，胡雪岩心里高兴，因为不但可以看看月如，而且也很想吃月如所做的菜。于是拿起单子来，仔细看了一会说：“后天中午的两个饭局，我都可以不去。就是后天中午好了。”“是，是。”唐子韶又说：“请大先生点几个菜。”

“Here is a suggestion,” Tang said. “On what day are you free for lunch?” That had to be asked of Madam Song, fifth among Hu's twelve concubines, who managed all his social engagements. A maid was sent to inquire. The reply was that he had engagements throughout the next ten days, and a list was copied out, clearly showing when he was to be a guest and when a host. “Why do you ask when I am free for lunch?” “Yueru has long wanted to prepare a few dishes to show her respect. I thought you might come for a simple meal, and give me your instructions for Rongzhai at the same time.” This pleased Hu. Besides seeing Yueru, he was eager to taste her cooking. He took the list and studied it. “I can miss both lunch engagements the day after tomorrow. Let us make it then.” “Yes, sir. Would you name a few dishes you would like?”

原来月如本在厨房中帮忙，虽非灶下婢，也只是往来奔走，传递食盒，只是她生性聪明，耳儒目染，也做得一手好菜，当初胡雪岩挑这个貌不出众的丫头送唐子韶，就因为他讲究饮馔，而她善于烹调之故。这三年来，唐子韶拿“三荒十月愆余”、“随园食单”中开列的食谱，讲给月如听了，如法炮制，复加改良，颇有几味连胡家的厨干都佩服的拿手菜，只是月如颇自矜其手艺，不肯轻易出手，因而不大为人所知而已。“月如的菜，样样都好，不过有几样做起来很费事。”“不要紧，大先生尽管吩咐。”胡雪岩点点说：“做一样核桃腰子。”这就是颇费功夫的一样菜。先拿羊腰或猪腰用盐水加生姜煮熟，去膜切片，再挑好核桃肉剥衣捣烂，与腰片拌匀，下锅用极小的火，不停手地炒，直到核桃出油，渗入腰片，再用好酱油、陈酒、香料烹透，是下酒的妙物。

Yueru had originally helped in the kitchen. She was not a scullery maid, but her duties had consisted merely of running about and carrying food boxes. Naturally quick-witted, she had learned through watching and listening and become a good cook. It was because Tang cared about fine food and she could prepare it that Hu had chosen this otherwise unremarkable-looking maid for him. During the past three years, Tang had explained recipes from the Sanhuang Shiyue Qianyu and the Suiyuan Food Manual to her. She reproduced them and made improvements, developing several specialities that even Hu's cooks admired. But she took pride in her skill and would not readily display it, so it remained little known. “All Yueru's dishes are good,” Hu said, “though some take a great deal of trouble.” “Never mind that. Please ask for whatever you wish.” Hu nodded. “Kidneys with walnuts.” This was indeed laborious. Lamb or pig kidneys were first boiled in salted water with fresh ginger, then stripped of their membrane and sliced. Selected walnut kernels were peeled, crushed and mixed with the slices. The mixture was stirred constantly over a very low fire until oil came out of the walnuts and penetrated the kidneys. Good soy sauce, aged wine and spices were then added, and everything cooked through. It made an excellent dish to accompany wine.

“还有呢？”“有一回月如做来孝敬老太太的蒸蛋，也不错。”“喔，那是三鲜蛋，不费事，还有呢？”“我就想到这两样。”胡雪岩又说：“菜千万不要多，多了糟蹋。再说，一个人的工夫到底有限，菜多了，照顾不到，味道总不免要差。”“是，是。后天中午，请大先生早早赏光。”

“What else?” “The steamed eggs Yueru once made for my mother were good too.” “Ah, the three-delicacy eggs. Those are easy. Anything else?” “Those are the only two that occur to me. Be sure not to make too many dishes. It would be wasteful. Besides, one person's time and effort are limited. With too many dishes to attend to, the flavour is bound to suffer.” “Certainly. Please come early the day after tomorrow.”

唐子韶就住在公济典后面，分租了人家一进房子，三楼三底，前后厢房，后厢房朝东的一问，月如用来做厨房。楼上外面两间打通，作坐起之用，最里面一间，才是卧室。胡雪岩一到，接到楼上去坐，雪白铜的火盆，生得极旺，窗子是新糊的，虽关紧了，屋子里仍旧雪亮，胡雪岩卸了玄狐袍子，只穿一身丝绵袄裤，仍旧在出汗。坐定不久，楼梯声响，上来的月如，她上身穿一件紫色湖绉袄裤，下面是散脚的贡呢夹裤。胡雪岩最讨厌年轻妇女着裙子，胡家除了胡老太太，全都是袄裤，月如也是如此。见了胡雪岩，裣衽为礼，称呼一直未改，仍旧叫“老爷”，她说：“发福了，气色更加好，红光满面。”“ 红光是太热的缘故。”胡雪岩摸着脸说。“老爷穿的是丝绵，怪不得了。”月如转脸向唐子韶说，“你快去看看，老爷的衣包里面，带了夹袄裤没有？”“对，对，”唐子韶猛然拍一下自己的额角，“我早该想得到的。”说着，起身就走。

Tang lived behind Gongji Pawnshop, having rented one section of another household's residence: three rooms upstairs and three below, with wings at front and back. An east-facing room in the rear wing served as Yueru's kitchen. Upstairs, the two outer rooms had been joined to make a sitting room; the innermost was the bedroom. On Hu's arrival, he was taken upstairs. A white-copper brazier burned fiercely. The windows had been newly papered, and although tightly shut, they admitted a bright light. Hu removed his black-fox-fur robe. Even in only his silk-floss-padded jacket and trousers, he was sweating. Soon footsteps sounded on the stairs and Yueru appeared, wearing a purple Huzhou-crepe padded jacket and loose-legged, lined trousers of fine woollen cloth. Hu particularly disliked skirts on young women. Apart from his mother, every woman in his household wore a jacket and trousers; Yueru did the same. She made her formal greeting, still addressing him as she always had: “Master.” “You have put on weight, and your complexion is better than ever. Your whole face is glowing.” “That is because it is so hot,” Hu said, touching his face. “You are wearing silk floss. No wonder.” Yueru turned to Tang. “Go and see whether Master's clothes bundle contains an unpadded jacket and trousers.” Tang suddenly slapped his forehead. “Of course! I should have thought of that earlier.” He rose and went out.

于是，月如坐下来问老太太、太太和当家的大姨太太——姓罗行四，家住螺狮门外，因而称之为“螺蛳太太”，再就是“少爷”、“小姐”，一一问到，唐子韶已经从胡雪岩的跟班手里，将衣包取来了。“老爷，”月如接过衣包说道：“我伺候你来换。”当着唐子韶，自然不便让她来执此役，连连说道：“不敢当，不敢当。我自己来。““那就到里面来换。”月如将胡雪岩引入她的卧室，随手将房门掩上。胡雪岩便坐在床沿上，脱棉换夹，易衣既毕，少不得打量打量周围，家具之中只有一张床最讲究，是张红木大床，极厚的褥子，簇新的丝绵被，雪白的枕头套，旁边摆着一枚蜡黄的佛手，拿起来闻一闻，有些桂花香，想来是沾了月如的梳头油的缘故。“换好了没有？”房门外面在问。“换好了。”“换好？我来收拾。”接着，房门“呀”地一声推开，月如进来将换下的丝绵袄裤，折齐包好。

Yueru sat down and asked after the old lady, Mrs Hu, and the senior concubine who managed the household. The last was surnamed Luo, fourth in her family; as her home had been outside Luosi Gate, she was known as Madam Luosi. Yueru went on to inquire about the young master and young lady. By the time she had asked after them all, Tang had brought the clothes bundle from Hu's attendant. “Let me help you change, Master,” she said, taking it. With Tang present, Hu could hardly allow her to perform such a service. “No, no. I can manage myself.” “Then come inside to change.” Yueru led him into her bedroom and drew the door shut. Hu sat on the edge of the bed and exchanged his padded clothes for lined ones. Once dressed, he naturally looked about. Of all the furniture, only the bed was particularly fine: a large rosewood frame, a very thick mattress, a brand-new silk-floss quilt and snow-white pillowcases. Beside it lay a wax-yellow Buddha's-hand citron. He picked it up and sniffed. It carried a trace of osmanthus, presumably from Yueru's hair oil. “Have you finished?” came a voice outside the door. “Yes.” “Then I'll put things away.” The door creaked open, and Yueru came in to fold and wrap the padded jacket and trousers he had taken off.

胡雪岩这时已走到外面，正在吸水烟的唐子韶站起来问道：“大先生，是不是马上开饭？”“好了就吃。”胡雪岩问道：“你啥辰光到湖州。”“喔，那我要把交代蓉斋的话告诉你，第一，今年丝的市面不大好，养”今天下半天就走。“蚕人家，今年这个年，恐怕很难过，你叫他关照柜台上，看货稍微放宽些。““是的。”“第二，满当的丝不要卖……”“满当的丝，大半会发黄，”唐子韶抢着说：“不卖掉，越摆越黄，更加不值钱了。”“要卖，”胡雪岩说：“也要先把路脚打听打听清楚，如果是上海缫丝厂的人来收，决不可卖给他们。”“是的。”唐子韶答应着，却又下了一句转语：“其实，他们如果蓄心来收，防亦无从防起。”“何以见得？”“他们可以收了当票来赎啊！”“我就是要这样子。”胡雪岩说：“人家赎不起当头，当票能卖几个钱，也是好的。”“大先生真是菩萨心肠。”唐子韶感叹着说。

Hu had already returned to the outer room. Tang, who was smoking his water pipe, rose. “Shall we eat now, sir?” “If it is ready. When are you leaving for Huzhou?” “This afternoon.” “Then I must give you my instructions for Rongzhai. First, the silk market has been poor this year, and the silkworm-raising households may have difficulty getting through the New Year. Tell him to have the counter staff be a little more generous in assessing their pledges.” “Yes.” “Second, don't sell the forfeited silk…” “Most forfeited silk turns yellow,” Tang interrupted. “Unless we sell it, the longer it sits, the yellower and less valuable it becomes.” “If you sell,” Hu said, “first make careful inquiries about the buyers. If they are buying for the Shanghai silk-reeling factories, you must not sell to them.” “Yes,” Tang agreed, then added a qualification. “But if they are determined to obtain it, there is really no way to stop them.” “Why not?” “They can buy the pawn tickets and redeem the silk.” “That is precisely what I want. If the owners cannot afford to redeem their pledges, it is something, at least, if they can sell the tickets for a little money.” “You have the heart of a bodhisattva, sir,” Tang said admiringly.

“也不是啥菩萨心肠，自己没有啥损失，能帮人的忙，何乐不为！说老实话，一个人有了身价，惠而不费的事、不知道有多少好做，只在有心没有心而已。”“大先生是好心，可惜有些人不知道。”何必要人家晓得？惠而不费而要人家说一声好，是做官的诀窍，做生意老老实实，那样做法，晓得的人在背后批评一句沽名钓誉，你的金字招牌就挂不牢了。““是，是。大先生真见得到，不过……”“你不要‘白果’、红枣的，谈得忘记辰光！”月如大声打断他的话，“开饭了。”抬头看时，已摆满了一桌的菜，除了胡雪岩所点的核桃炙腰与三鲜蛋以外，另外蒸的是松子鸡，炒的是冬笋鱼，烩的是火腿黄芽菜，再就是一大碗鱼圆莼菜汤与杭州到冬天家家要制的腌菜。“老爷吃啥酒？”月如说道：“花雕已经烫在那里了。”“好，就吃花雕。”斟上酒来，月如又来布菜，“我怕方裕和的火腿，老爷吃厌了。”她说：“今天用的是宣威腿。”

“It is hardly a bodhisattva's heart. If I lose nothing and can help someone, why shouldn't I? To tell the truth, once a man has standing, there are countless ways for him to benefit others at no cost to himself. It depends only on whether he has the will.” “Your intentions are good, sir. It is a pity some people do not realize it.” “Why must they? To confer benefits at no cost and still insist on praise is an official's trick. In business one must be straightforward. Behave like that, and those who understand will criticize you behind your back for fishing for a reputation. Your gilded shop sign will no longer hang securely.” “Quite so, sir. You see deeply into things. But…” “Enough of your ‘buts’ and ginkgo nuts and red dates!” Yueru interrupted loudly. “You will talk until you forget the time. Dinner is ready.” Looking up, they saw a table covered with dishes. Besides the walnut kidneys and three-delicacy eggs Hu had requested, there were chicken steamed with pine nuts, fish stir-fried with winter bamboo shoots, ham braised with yellow-heart cabbage, a large bowl of fish-ball and water-shield soup, and the pickled greens every Hangzhou household made in winter. “What wine will you have, Master? The Huadiao is already warming.” “Good. Huadiao will do.” After pouring the wine, Yueru began serving the food. “I was afraid you might be tired of Fang Yuhe hams, so I have used Xuanwei ham today.”

“你的话也说得过分了，好火腿是吃不厌的。”胡雪岩挟了一块宣威腿，放在口中，一面咀嚼，一面说道：“谈起宣威腿，我倒说个笑话你们听听。盛杏荪最喜欢吃宣威腿，有人拍他马屁，特为托人从云南带了两条宣威腿，送到他电报局，礼帖上写的是‘宣腿一双’，这一来犯了他的忌讳……““盛杏荪名字叫盛宣怀。”唐子韶乘间为月如解释。“犯他的忌讳，他自然不高兴罗？”月如问说。“是啊！”胡雪岩答道：“当时他就发脾气：”什么宣腿不宣腿的？拿走，拿走！‘过了几天，他想起来了，把电报局的饭司务叫了来问：“我的腿呢？’饭司务听董了，当时回报他：”大人的两条腿，自己不要，局里的各位老爷把大人的两条腿吃掉了。“

“You exaggerate. One never tires of good ham.” Hu picked up a piece of Xuanwei ham and chewed it. “Speaking of Xuanwei ham, let me tell you a funny story. Sheng Xingsun is particularly fond of it. Someone hoping to flatter him had a pair brought all the way from Yunnan and sent to his Telegraph Bureau. The gift card said, ‘A pair of Xuan legs.’ That offended his taboo…” “Sheng Xingsun's name is Sheng Xuanhuai,” Tang explained to Yueru. “So he was displeased?” she asked. “Indeed. He flew into a temper. ‘What do you mean, Xuan legs? Take them away!’ A few days later he remembered them and summoned the bureau's cook. ‘Where are my legs?’ The cook understood and replied, ‘Your Excellency did not want your two legs, so the gentlemen in the bureau have eaten them.’”

胡雪岩说得极快，象绕口令似的，逗得月如咯咯地笑个不停。“笑话还没有完。”胡雪岩又说：“盛杏荪这个人很刻薄，专门做得便宜卖乖的事。有人恨在心里，存心寻他的开心，叫人送了一份礼去，礼帖上还是‘宣腿一双’。看那两条火腿，墨黑，大小比不上金华腿，更不要说宣威腿了。心想，这是啥火腿？就叫了饭司务来看。““饭司务懂不懂呢？”月如又问。“饭司务当然识货，当时就说：”大人，你的这两条腿是狗腿！‘这一来，月如自然又大笑，笑停了说：“原来是’戌腿‘！我也只听说，没有见过。”“本来就难得见的。”唐子韶说：“一缸火腿当中，只摆一条‘戌腿’，为的是取他的香味。“

Hu spoke rapidly, almost in a tongue-twister, and Yueru could not stop giggling. “The story is not finished,” he continued. “Sheng Xingsun is a spiteful fellow, always taking advantage and then putting on a virtuous air. Someone who resented him decided to make fun of him and sent another gift. Again the card said, ‘A pair of Xuan legs.’ But the two hams were black and smaller than Jinhua hams, let alone Xuanwei ones. Wondering what sort they could be, he called in the cook.” “Did the cook know?” Yueru asked. “Of course he knew his goods. ‘Your Excellency,’ he said, ‘these two legs of yours are dog's legs!’” Yueru laughed again. When she could speak, she said, “So they were dog hams! I have heard of them, but never seen one.” “They are seldom seen,” Tang said. “Only one dog ham is put in a vat of hams, to impart its fragrance.”

“狗肉是真香。可惜老太太不准进门。”胡雪岩转脸看着月如说：“老太太常常提起你炖的蛋，你明天再弄一碗去孝敬孝敬她。”“唷！老太太真是抬举我。她老人家喜欢，我天天做了送去。”“蒸蛋要现蒸现吃。”唐子韶有个更好的办法，“倒不如你把诀窍传授了小刘妈，老太太想吃就有，多么好？”原来胡家也仿佛宫中那样，有好几个小厨房，胡老太太专用的小厨房，归小刘妈管，诀窍传了给她，就省事得多了。“子韶这话，通极。”胡雪岩深以为然，“月如，我倒要问你，凡是蒸蛋，不管你加多少好作料，端上桌来，总归上清下浑，作料沉在碗底，结成绷硬一块。只有你蒸的这碗三鲜蛋，作料都匀开在蛋里面，嫩而不老，诀窍在哪里？”“诀窍是分两次蒸……”月如的方法是，第一次用鸡蛋三枚，加去油的火腿汤一茶杯、盐少许，打透蒸熟，就象极嫩的水豆腐，这时才加作料、火腿屑、冬菇屑、虾仁之类，另外再打一个生鸡蛋，连同蒸好的嫩蛋，一起打匀，看浓淡酌量加冬菇汤。这样上宠蒸出来的蛋，就是此刻胡雪岩所吃的三鲜蛋。

“Dog meat certainly is fragrant. Unfortunately, my mother will not allow it into the house.” Hu turned to Yueru. “She often mentions your steamed eggs. Make her another bowl tomorrow.” “How kind of the old lady! If she likes them, I will make them and send them every day.” “Steamed eggs should be eaten straight from the steamer,” Tang said, suggesting a better plan. “Why not teach Young Madam Liu the secret? Then the old lady can have them whenever she wants.” Like an imperial household, Hu's establishment had several small kitchens. The one devoted to his mother was managed by Young Madam Liu. Teaching her the method would make everything much easier. “An excellent suggestion, Zishao.” Hu thoroughly approved. “Tell me, Yueru, why is it that however many fine ingredients one puts into steamed eggs, they always come to table clear on top and thick below, with everything sunk to the bottom in a hard mass? Only in your three-delicacy eggs is everything evenly distributed, tender rather than overdone. What is the secret?” “They must be steamed twice…” First she beat three eggs thoroughly with a teacup of ham stock from which the fat had been removed and a little salt, then steamed them until set, as tender as the softest tofu. Only then did she add the chopped ham, chopped dried mushrooms, shrimp and other ingredients. She beat another raw egg into the steamed custard, mixed everything thoroughly, and added mushroom stock according to the consistency. Steamed again, it became the three-delicacy egg dish Hu was now eating.

“凡事说破不得。”唐子韶笑道：“说破了就不值钱了。”“不然。”胡雪岩说：“光晓得诀窍，不用心、不下工夫，弄出来也是个‘三不象’，更不必说胜过人家。月如，你说我这话是不是？”月如听了他的话，必里当然很舒服，绽开的笑容很甜，“老爷这么说，就趁热再吃点。”说着，用汤匙舀了一匙，伸到胡雪岩口边。“我自己来。”胡雪岩捏住她的手，不让她将汤匙送入他口中。见此光景，唐子韶便回头关照侍席的丫头：“你替我盛碗饭来，吃完了，我要赶上船，辰光已经很局促了。”“啥辰光开船？”胡雪岩问。“两点钟。”“呃，这倒是要快了。已经一点过头了。现在小火轮拖航船，一拖七八条，到时候不等的。”

“One should never reveal a secret,” Tang said with a laugh. “Once explained, it is worth nothing.” “Not so,” Hu replied. “Merely knowing the method is no use without care and effort. The result will be neither one thing nor another, let alone better than anyone else's. Am I right, Yueru?” His words naturally pleased her, and her smile was sweet. “If you say so, Master, have some more while it is hot.” She scooped up a spoonful and held it towards his lips. “I can manage myself.” Hu caught her hand and prevented her from putting the spoon into his mouth. At this, Tang turned to the maid waiting at table. “Bring me a bowl of rice. Once I have eaten, I must catch my boat. Time is getting very short.” “When does it leave?” Hu asked. “At two.” “Then you must hurry. It is past one already. These days a small steamer tows seven or eight passenger boats together. They won't wait once it is time to leave.”

于是唐子韶匆匆吃完了饭，向胡雪岩告辞。月如要送他下楼，到得楼梯口，却让唐子韶拦住了。“你陪陪大先生。辰光够的，航船一定赶得上。去了总有三天耽搁，你火烛小心。”“我晓得，你放心去好了。”月如又叫那丫头：“你送老爷下楼，就到厨房里去帮陈妈的忙，这里有我。”月如说完了，却仍站在原处，直待脚步声消失，方始回身，顺手把楼梯间的门关上，活络门闩一拨，顿时内外隔绝。胡雪岩心中一动，这倒有点象《金瓶梅》开头那种情形了。“胡大先生”变了“西门大官人”，不过唐子韶虽说看起来象王婆，倘或航船赶不上，回家来撞见了，一下变成了武大郎，那不是开玩笑的事。“会不会唐子韶起黑心，做好仙人跳的圈套要我来钻？”胡雪岩在心中自问，同时抬眼去看月如的脸色。她的脸色很平静，使得胡雪岩心里也平静了，想想唐子韶即令“起黑心”，也还没有这样的胆子。月如更没有理由陪唐子韶扮演仙人跳，看起来是有所求，出此下策，没有什么大不了的。

Tang hurriedly finished his rice and took his leave. Yueru went to see him downstairs, but he stopped her at the stairhead. “Stay and keep Mr Hu company. There is plenty of time; I shall catch the boat. I expect to be away three days. Be careful with the fires and candles.” “I know. Go without worrying.” She told the maid, “See the master downstairs, then help Mother Chen in the kitchen. I will attend here.” Yet she remained where she was until the footsteps had died away. Then she turned, shut the door to the staircase and slipped the movable bolt into place, cutting them off from the outside. Hu felt a stir of excitement. This was rather like the opening of The Plum in the Golden Vase: Mr Hu had become Master Ximen. Tang might look like the procuring Granny Wang, but if he missed his boat and came home to find them, he would suddenly turn into the cuckolded Wu Dalang. That would be no joke. Could Tang have conceived a malicious plan and laid a honey trap for him? Hu asked himself the question while studying Yueru's face. Her calm expression reassured him. Even if Tang had such wicked intentions, he would lack the courage to carry them through. Yueru had still less reason to join him in a blackmail trap. Apparently they wanted something and had resorted to this desperate means of obtaining it. There was nothing very serious to fear.

这样想着，心思便野了，“月如，”他说：“我好懊悔，不该把你许给老唐的。”“为啥？”“还要问我？”胡雪岩捏着她的手说：“你是不是装糊涂？”“我不是装糊涂，我是怨我自己命苦。一样是做小，为啥不配住‘十二楼’？”胡雪岩造了一座走马楼，共分十二区，安置十二个姨太太，所以这座走马楼又称十二楼。听她话中有怨怼之意，胡雪岩便即说道：“你也不要怪我。哪晓得你今天会是这样子的！”“我怎样？月如还不是月如。”“苏秦不是旧苏秦。女大十八变，不过人家没有你变得厉害。你除了……”胡雪岩将话咽住了。月如却要追问：“除了什么？除了会弄几样菜，没有一样中老爷的意的。”“样样中意。除了……”“喏，说说又不说了。我顶不欢喜话说半句。”“你不动气，我就说。你美中不足的是，一双大脚。”“脚大有什么不好？李中堂的老太太就是一双大脚。”

Reassured, Hu let his thoughts roam. “Yueru, how I regret having given you to Old Tang.” “Why?” “Must you ask?” He took her hand. “Are you pretending not to understand?” “I am not pretending. I am only lamenting my hard fate. If I must be a concubine anyway, why am I unworthy to live in the Twelve Chambers?” Hu had built a gallery-lined residence divided into twelve sections for his twelve concubines, hence its name, the Twelve Chambers. Hearing her resentment, he replied, “Don't blame me. How could I know you would become what you are today?” “What have I become? Yueru is still Yueru.” “Su Qin was no longer the Su Qin of old. A woman goes through eighteen transformations as she grows, but nobody has changed as much as you. Except for…” He swallowed the rest. She pressed him. “Except for what? Except that I can cook a few dishes, there is nothing about me that pleases you.” “Everything pleases me. Except…” “There you go again, beginning and then stopping. I hate half-finished remarks.” “I will tell you if you promise not to be angry. The one flaw in your beauty is that pair of large feet.” “What is wrong with large feet? Grand Secretary Li's mother has them too.”

李中堂是指李鸿章。据说李瀚章当湖广总督时，迎养老母，李鸿章亦先期由天津赶到武昌去迎候，官船靠岸，码头上挤满了一城文武。上岸到总督衙门，顶马、跟马几十匹，职事衔牌加上“导子”，长到前面鸣锣喝道，后面听不见。李太夫人的绿呢大轿，左右扶轿杠的是两个当总督的儿子。倾巷来观的武昌百姓，无不羡慕，说“李老太太真好福气”。那李老太太自然也很得意，得意忘形，不知不觉间将脚尖伸出轿帘以外，原来李老太太是天足，看热闹的百姓，不免窃窃私议，李鸿章发觉了，自不免有些窘，当下向轿中说道：“娘，请你把脚缩进去，露出来不雅观。”谁知一句话恼了李老太太，实在也是因为她最恨人家说她大脚，不免恼羞成怒，当时大声说道：“你老子不嫌我大脚，你倒来嫌我！”这是很有名的一个笑话，所以月如也知道，胡雪岩便即笑笑说道：“好，好，我不嫌你。”

She meant Li Hongzhang. The story went that when his brother Li Hanzhang was Governor-General of Huguang and brought their mother to live with him, Li Hongzhang came ahead from Tianjin to Wuchang to welcome her. When her official boat reached the landing, the city's civil and military officers crowded the quay. From there to the governor-general's residence, the procession included dozens of horses ridden by outriders and attendants, placards displaying offices and ranks, and the full ceremonial escort. It stretched so far that the gong and cries clearing the way at the front could not be heard at the rear. Beside the shafts of the old lady's great green-wool-covered sedan walked her two sons, both governors-general, one on either side. The people of Wuchang poured out to watch, all envying her good fortune. The old lady herself was naturally delighted. In her exhilaration, she unconsciously extended the tips of her feet beyond the curtain. Her feet were unbound, and the spectators began whispering. Li Hongzhang noticed and felt embarrassed. “Mother, please draw your feet in. It does not look seemly to show them.” This angered her, for she particularly hated remarks about her large feet. Mortification turned to fury. “Your father never objected to my large feet. Now you object!” It was a famous joke, and Yueru knew it. Hu smiled. “Very well. I do not object to yours.”

“实在也没啥好嫌的。你不晓得大脚的好处。”“喔，你倒说说看。”月如眨着眼思索着，突然脸一红，而且白了他一眼说：“偏不告诉你。”胡雪岩心里有点发痒，笑嘻嘻他说道：“你倒把脚伸出来让我看看。”“不要！”月如答得很简截，同时将一双脚往椅子后面缩了去。于是胡雪岩又想到了《金瓶梅》，很想照西门庆的办法，故意拂落筷子，俯身去捡时，便好捏一捏她的脚。不道念头还未转定，月如却开口说话了。“我的一双脚，你总看得见的。”“喔，”胡雪岩问：“啥辰光？”月如不答话。“月如，”胡雪岩伸过手去，握着她的手说：“你坐过来，我有话跟你说。”“你坐在那里，不也好说？”“不！这话要‘咬耳朵”才有味道。“杭州话“咬耳朵”是耳语之意，“又没有人，要咬啥耳朵？”月如话虽如此，还是将一张红木圆凳移了过来，坐在胡雪岩身边。

“There is really nothing to object to. You don't know the advantages of large feet.” “Then tell me.” She blinked thoughtfully, suddenly blushed and gave him a sidelong look. “I shan't.” Hu felt a pleasurable stir of desire. Smiling, he said, “Put your feet out and let me look.” “No!” she answered sharply, drawing them back behind her chair. Again he thought of The Plum in the Golden Vase. He longed to follow Ximen Qing's example: deliberately knock down his chopsticks and, while bending to retrieve them, take the opportunity to squeeze her foot. Before the thought had fully formed, however, she spoke. “You will see my feet in time.” “When?” She did not answer. He reached over and held her hand. “Come and sit nearer. I have something to tell you.” “Can't you say it from where you are?” “No. This is something that tastes better when one ‘bites an ear.’” In Hangzhou speech, biting an ear meant whispering. “There is nobody here. Why must you bite my ear?” Despite her words, she brought over a round rosewood stool and sat beside him.

胡雪岩将左手伸了过去，揽着她那又细又软的腰，凑过头去，先好好闻一闻她的头发，然后低声说道：“你现在就去洗脚，好不好？”“不好！”月如很快地回答。“咦！不是你自己说的。”“不错，我说过的。不过不是今天。”“那么，哪一天呢？”月如不答，但任由胡雪岩越搂越紧，却并无挣拒之意，好久，才说了声：“好热。”接着略略坐直了身子，伸左手去摘衣钮，从领子到腋下那一颗，都解开了，衣襟半锨，芗泽微闻。胡雪岩坐在她的右面，要探摸她的胸前，只是一举手之劳，但他宁愿先把话问清楚。“你为什么不说话？”“叫我说哈？螺蛳太太晓得了，我怎么还有脸到元宝街？”“她从哪里去晓得？跟我出来的人，个个都是嘴紧的人。”月如又不作声了，看样子是肯了，胡雪岩便耐心地等着。“我炖了鸭粥在那里，要不要吃一碗？”“等歇再吃。”胡雪岩站起身来，顺手拉了她一把。

Hu put his left arm around her soft, slender waist, leaned close and first breathed in the scent of her hair. Then he whispered, “Go and wash your feet now, will you?” “No,” she answered promptly. “But wasn't it you who said…” “Yes, I said it. But not today.” “Then when?” Yueru did not reply. She let him hold her tighter and tighter without trying to pull away. After a long while she murmured, “It is so hot.” Sitting a little straighter, she raised her left hand and undid the fastenings from her collar to the button beneath her arm. Her jacket fell half open, releasing a faint fragrance. Seated on her right, Hu needed only to lift his hand to touch her bosom. But he preferred first to settle the question. “Why won't you speak?” “What can I say? If Madam Luosi found out, how could I ever show my face in Yuanbao Street again?” “How would she find out? Every man who comes out with me knows how to keep his mouth shut.” Yueru fell silent again. To Hu, she now seemed willing, and he waited patiently. “I have some duck congee simmering. Would you like a bowl?” “Later.” Hu rose and gave her a tug.
