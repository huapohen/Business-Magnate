萧瑟洋场｜The Desolate Foreign Settlement

第一章（本章第4部分，共4部分）

阅读说明：每段中文后接对应英文；两版英文正文一致。下载站水印已清除，原始疑字在文末说明。

不久，胡雪岩到了，拉着古应春到一边说道∶『我看原来请到我那里吃西餐的办法得不通了。』『怎么呢？』『赫德到杭州来的消息，不知道怎么传出去了。德晓峰派人通知我，说要来作陪，他是好意，我怎么好挡驾？』胡雪岩又说∶『这一来，邀赫德到家，似乎不太方便。』古应春想了一下说∶『不要紧，中午在这里开席，晚上请他到府上好了。』『只好这样。』

Hu Xueyan arrived shortly afterward and drew Gu Yingchun aside. “I am afraid our plan to invite him to my house for a Western lunch will not work.” “Why not?” “Somehow the news that Hart is in Hangzhou has got out. De Xiaofeng has sent to say that he wishes to join us. He means well; how can I turn him away? But with him there, inviting Hart to the house would be rather awkward.” Gu thought for a moment. “Never mind. We can entertain him here at noon and invite him to your house in the evening.” “That will have to do.”

刚说完，已隐隐传来呜锣喝道之声，料想是德馨到了。胡雪岩迎出去一看，方知来的是赫德，原来此人极其醉心中国官场的气派，特为借了巡抚的绿呢大轿，全副『导子』，前呼后拥，趁机会大过了一番官瘾。他穿的自然是二品补服。红顶花翎的大帽子后面还装了根乌油油的大辫子；胡雪岩是见过的，不足为奇，其他游客闲人，何曾见过洋人有这样的打扮？顿时都围了上来，好在胡家的下人多，两面推排，留出一条路来，由胡雪岩陪着，直趋寿堂。于是『清音堂名』，咪哩吗啦地吹打了起来；赫德甩一甩马蹄袖，有模有样地在红毡条上跪了下去，磕完头起身，与陪礼的胡雪岩相互一揖，方始交谈。

They had scarcely finished speaking when the faint sound of gongs and cries clearing the way reached them. They supposed Dexin had arrived. Hu went out to receive him and discovered that the visitor was Hart. Enamored of the grandeur of Chinese official life, Hart had specially borrowed the governor’s large green-cloth sedan and a complete ceremonial escort, with attendants calling before and behind, to indulge his taste for official splendor. He naturally wore the insignia robe of the second rank. His hat had a red button and a peacock feather, and a long, glossy black queue had even been attached at the back. Hu had seen him like this before and found nothing unusual in it. But what sightseer or idle spectator had ever seen a foreigner dressed in such a manner? The crowd immediately closed around him. Fortunately, the Hu household had plenty of servants. They pushed people back on either side to open a path, and Hu escorted Hart straight into the birthday hall. The ceremonial musicians struck up a lively clamor. Hart flicked back his horsehoof cuffs and knelt on the strip of red felt with a convincing air of knowing what he was about. After kowtowing, he rose and exchanged a bow with Hu, who had knelt beside him to return the courtesy. Only then did they begin to talk.

『恭喜，恭喜。』赫德说得极好的一口京片子，『老太太在哪里，应该当面拜寿。』胡雪岩略有些踌躇，有这第一个戴红顶子的洋大人去见老母，实在是件很有起的事；但一进去他，女着就得回避，不免会有屏风后面，窃窃私议，失礼闹笑话就不妙了，因而答说∶『不敢当，我说到就是了。』赫德点点头，回身看见古应春说∶『昨天拜托转达雪翁的话，想必已经说过。』『是的。』古应春门见山地答说∶『雪翁的意思，今天晚上想请阁下到他府上便饭，饭后细谈。』『那就叨扰了。』赫德向胡雪岩说∶『谢谢。』

“Congratulations, congratulations!” Hart spoke excellent Beijing Chinese. “Where is the old lady? I ought to offer my birthday wishes in person.” Hu hesitated. It would be a matter of considerable interest for this first foreign dignitary to wear a red official button to meet his mother. Yet if he went inside, the women would have to withdraw. There might then be whispering behind screens, and an embarrassing breach of manners would be most unfortunate. “You are too kind,” he replied. “Allow me to convey your wishes.” Hart nodded, then turned and saw Gu. “I presume you have passed on the message I asked you to give Mr Xue yesterday.” “Yes.” Gu came straight to the point. “Mr Xue would like to invite you to a simple meal at his house this evening, with a fuller discussion afterward.” “Then I shall impose on your hospitality.” Hart turned to Hu. “Thank you.”

于是让到一边待茶。正在谈着，德馨到了；他是有意结纳赫德，陪着很敷衍了一阵。中午一起坐了面席，方始回城。这天原是比较清闲的一天，因为来拜寿洋人，毕竟有限。到得下午三点钟，古应春便已进城；略息一息亲自去接赫德，顺便邀梅藤更作陪，这是胡雪岩决定的。到时天还未黑，但萃锦堂上的煤油打汽灯，已点得一片烨烨白光。那萃锦堂是五开间的西式洋楼，楼前一个大天进，东面有座喷水池；西面用朱漆杉木，围成一个圆形栅栏，里面养着雌雄一对孔雀，一见赫德进来，冉冉开屏，不由得把他吸引住了。『这只孔雀戴的是「三眼花翎」。』赫德指着雀屏笑道∶『李中堂都没有它阔。』

They moved aside for tea. While they were talking, Dexin arrived. He was eager to cultivate Hart’s acquaintance and entertained him attentively for some time. They shared a birthday noodle banquet at noon before returning to the city. It was, in fact, a relatively quiet day: the number of foreigners coming to offer birthday greetings was limited. By three in the afternoon, Gu had already returned to the city. After a short rest, he went personally to fetch Hart, also inviting Dr Meitenggeng to join them, as Hu had decided. It was not yet dark when they arrived, but the pressurized kerosene lamps in Cuijin Hall were already blazing with brilliant white light. The hall was a Western-style building five bays wide, with a large courtyard in front. A fountain stood to the east. To the west, a circular enclosure of red-lacquered firwood housed a pair of peacocks. As soon as Hart appeared, the male slowly spread his tail and held the visitor spellbound. “This peacock wears a ‘three-eyed feather,’” Hart said, pointing at the fan and laughing. “Even Grand Secretary Li is not as grand as he is.”

于是入座以后，便谈李鸿章了。赫德带来最新的消息，直隶总署是调两广总督张树声署理，湖广总督果然是由湖南巡抚涂宗瀛升任。『那末，两广呢？』『现在还不知道。』赫德答说∶『听说曾九帅很有意思谋这个缺。』『湖南，』胡雪岩又问∶『湖南巡抚不晓得放的哪个？』『这倒没有听说。』

Once they were seated, the conversation naturally turned to Li Hongzhang. Hart had brought the latest news: Zhang Shusheng, Governor-General of Liangguang, was being transferred to act as Governor-General of Zhili, while the governorship-general of Huguang would indeed go to Tu Zongying, Governor of Hunan. “And Liangguang?” “I do not know yet,” Hart replied. “I hear Ninth Marshal Zeng is very interested in obtaining that post.” “Hunan?” Hu asked. “Do you know who is to be appointed Governor of Hunan?” “I have heard nothing about that.”

就这时候，瑞香翩然出现，进门先福一福，拢总请了一个安，然后向胡雪岩说道∶『太太要我来说，小小姐有点发烧，怕是出痧子，想请梅先生去看一看。』『喔，』胡雪岩皱着眉说∶『梅先生是来作客的，皮包听筒也不晓得带了没有？』『带了，带了。』梅藤更是一口杭州话，『听筒是我的吃饭家伙，随身法宝，哪里会不带。』说着，从口袋中掏出一副听筒，向瑞香扬一扬说∶『我们走。』『小小姐』是螺蛳太太的小女儿，今年七岁，胡雪岩爱如掌珠；听说病了，不免有神思不属的模样，幸而有古应春陪着赫德闲谈，未曾慢客。『怎么样？』一见梅藤更回来，胡雪岩迎上去问∶『不要紧吧？』『不要紧，不要紧。』

At that moment Ruixiang entered gracefully, curtsied to the company as a whole, and addressed Hu. “Madam has sent me to say that the little miss has a slight fever. She is afraid it may be measles and would like Dr Mei to look at her.” “Oh.” Hu frowned. “Dr Mei is here as a guest. I do not even know whether he has brought his medical bag and stethoscope.” “I have it, I have it,” the doctor replied in his Hangzhou accent. “My stethoscope is how I earn my living, my indispensable companion. How could I leave it behind?” He took it from his pocket, held it up to Ruixiang, and said, “Let us go.” The little miss was Madam Luosi’s younger daughter, seven years old that year, whom Hu cherished like the pearl of his palm. Hearing that she was ill, he became somewhat distracted. Fortunately, Gu kept Hart engaged in conversation, so their guest was not neglected. As soon as the doctor returned, Hu went to meet him. “How is she? Nothing serious, I hope?” “Nothing serious at all.”

当梅藤便在开药方，交代胡家的管家到广济医院去取药时，赫德已开始与古应春谈到正事，刚开了一个头，因人入席而将话题打断了。进餐当然是照西洋规矩。桃花心木的长餐桌，通称『大餐桌』，胡雪岩与古应春分坐两端主位，胡雪岩的右手方是赫德，左手方是梅藤更。菜当然很讲究，而酒更讲究；古应春春有意为主人炫耀，命待者一瓶一瓶地将香槟酒与红葡萄酒取了来，为客人介绍哪一瓶为法国哪一位君王所御用；哪一瓶已有多少年陈，当然还有英国人所喜爱的威士忌，亦都是英国也很珍贵的名牌。

While Dr Meitenggeng wrote a prescription and instructed the Hu steward to fetch the medicine from Guangji Hospital, Hart began discussing business with Gu. They had barely started when the call to dinner interrupted them. The meal was naturally served in the Western manner. Hu and Gu took the host’s places at opposite ends of the long mahogany table, the kind commonly called a “Western dining table.” Hart sat on Hu’s right and Dr Meitenggeng on his left. The food was elaborate, but the wines were more remarkable still. Deliberately displaying his host’s wealth, Gu had the servants bring bottles of champagne and red wine one after another. He explained which had been served to which French monarch and how many years others had aged. There was also, of course, the whisky the English liked, all distinguished brands highly prized even in Britain.

这顿饭吃了有一个钟头，先是海阔天空地随意闲谈，以后便分成两对，梅藤更跟胡雪岩谈他的医院，说诊务愈来愈盛，医院想要扩充，苦于地基不足，胡雪岩答应替他想想办法；又说门前的路太狭，而且高低不平，轿马纷纷，加以摊贩众多，交通不便，向胡雪岩诉了许多苦，胡雪岩许了替他修路，但梅藤更请他向杭州府及钱塘县请一张告示驱逐摊贩，胡雪岩却婉言谢绝了。另一对是赫德与古应春，断续入席以前的话题，而是用英语交谈，谈的是广东丝业的巨头陈启沅。这陈启沅是广州南海县人，一直在南洋一带经商，同治末年回到家乡开了一家缫丝厂，招牌叫继昌隆，用了六、七百女工，规模很大，丝的品质亦很好，行销欧美，很受欢迎。

The meal lasted an hour. At first they talked freely of everything under the sun; later they split into two pairs. Dr Meitenggeng spoke to Hu about his hospital. The number of patients kept increasing, and he wished to expand, but there was not enough land. Hu promised to consider what could be done. The doctor went on to complain that the road in front was too narrow and uneven. With sedans and horses constantly coming and going, and so many street vendors besides, access was difficult. After hearing his many complaints, Hu promised to improve the road. But when the doctor asked him to obtain an official notice from the Hangzhou prefecture and Qiantang county authorities ordering the vendors away, Hu politely refused. The other pair, Hart and Gu, resumed their earlier conversation in English. They were discussing Chen Qiyuan, the leading figure in Guangdong’s silk industry. Chen came from Nanhai county in Guangzhou and had long traded in Southeast Asia. Near the end of the Tongzhi reign, he returned home and opened the Jichanglong Silk-Reeling Mill. It was a large establishment employing six or seven hundred women. Its silk was of excellent quality and sold well in Europe and America.

『他的丝好，是因为用机器，比用手工好。』赫德说∶『机器代替人工，是世界潮流。我在中国二十年，对中国的感情，跟对英国一样，甚至更为关切，因为中国更需要帮助；所以，我这一回来，想跟胡先生谈怡和丝厂开工一事，实在也为中国富强着眼。』『是的。我们都知道你对中国的爱护，不过，英国讲民主，中国亦讲顺应民情，就象细昌隆的情形，不能不引以为鉴。』原来陈启沅前两年改用机器，曾经引起很大的风潮；陈启沅不能不设法改良，制造一种一型的缫丝机，推广到农村，将机器之利，与人共享。赫德在宣扬机器的好处；古应春承认这一点，但隐然指出，想用机器替代人手，独占厚利是行不通的。

“His silk is good because he uses machinery, which is better than working by hand,” Hart said. “The replacement of manual labor by machinery is the way the world is going. I have been in China for twenty years. I feel as much for China as I do for Britain—indeed, I am even more concerned for her, because she is in greater need of help. That is why, in coming here to speak to Mr Hu about opening Jardine Matheson’s silk mill, I truly have China’s wealth and strength at heart.” “Yes. We all know your concern for China. But just as Britain speaks of democracy, China too speaks of respecting the feelings of the people. We cannot ignore the lesson of what happened at Jichanglong.” Chen’s adoption of machinery two years earlier had provoked considerable unrest. He had been obliged to find a better arrangement and developed a small silk-reeling machine for use in the villages, thus sharing the benefits of machinery with others. Hart was extolling its advantages. Gu acknowledged them but quietly made the point that replacing people with machines in order to monopolize the enormous profits would not work.

及至席散，梅藤更告辞先行，赫德留下来；与胡雪岩正式商谈时，赫德的话又同了。『雪翁！』他用中国官场的称呼，『你能不能跟怡和合伙？』胡雪岩颇为诧异，怡和洋行是英国资本，亦等于是英国官方的事业，何以会邀中国人来合伙？事情没有弄清楚以前，他不愿表示态度，只是含蓄地微笑着。

After dinner, Dr Meitenggeng took his leave, while Hart stayed. When he began formal discussions with Hu, his approach was different again. “Mr Xue,” he said, adopting the Chinese official style of address, “could you go into partnership with Jardine Matheson?” Hu was rather surprised. Jardine Matheson was financed by British capital and, as he understood it, virtually an official British enterprise. Why should it invite a Chinese merchant into partnership? Until he understood the matter, he would give no indication of his position. He merely smiled reservedly.

『我是说怡和洋行所人的丝厂。』赫德接下来说∶『他们愿意跟你订一张合同，丝都由你供应；市价以外，另送佣金。』还是为了原料！原来怡和丝厂，早在光绪元年便已开设，自以为财大势雄，派人到乡下收购茧子，价钱虽出得不坏，但挑剔得也很厉害，派人到乡下收购茧子，价线虽出得不坏，但执，甚至大起纠纷，恼了自浙江嘉与苏州一带，丝产旺地的几闲置的机器，又因保养不善，损坏，生的生锈，只好闭歇。

“I mean the silk mill owned by Jardine Matheson,” Hart continued. “They would be willing to sign a contract with you for all their silk supplies. On top of the market price, they would pay a commission.” So it was still a question of raw materials! Jardine Matheson’s silk mill had opened as early as the first year of the Guangxu reign. Confident in its wealth and influence, the firm had sent buyers into the countryside to purchase cocoons. Though the prices it offered were not bad, its buyers were extremely exacting. Serious disputes had even arisen [the ensuing passage about opposition in the silk-producing districts around Jia— in Zhejiang and Suzhou is damaged in the source]. Machinery left idle had then suffered from poor maintenance: some was damaged, some rusted. The mill had no choice but to close.

但就这两三年，日本的机器缫丝业，大为发达，怡和丝厂在去年重整鼓，新修厂房，买了意利造新机器，准备复业。此外，有个澄州人叫黄佐卿，开一家公和永丝厂，向法国买的机器，亦已运到；另有公平洋行，亦打算在这方面投资。这三家丝厂一开工，需要大量原料，丝价必定上涨，胡雪岩早就看准了。可是，他是站在反对丝厂这方面的，因为有陈启沅的例子在，机器马达一向，不知道有多少养蚕做丝人心惊肉跳。

During the past two or three years, however, machine silk-reeling had made great advances in Japan. Last year Jardine Matheson had regrouped, renovated its mill buildings, and bought new Italian machinery in preparation for reopening. In addition, Huang Zuoqing, a man from Chengzhou, had opened the Gongheyong Silk Mill, and the machinery he had bought from France had arrived. Gongping Trading House also intended to invest in this field. Once the three mills began work, they would require huge quantities of raw material, and silk prices would inevitably rise. Hu had foreseen all this long ago. Yet he stood with the opponents of the mills. Chen Qiyuan’s example was there for all to see: the moment the motors began to run, who could say how many people raising silkworms and making silk would tremble for their livelihoods?

翻译与校勘
1. 覆盖 source_desolate.txt 第 309—373 行全部 33 个非空行，组织为 12 个阅读段落；第一章结束，下一章由下批承接。
2. 第 315 行“呜锣”按鸣锣语义读；第 323 行“有起”“女着”按上下文为有趣、女眷，第 327 行“门见山”依固定表达直译开门见山。保留源文“第一个戴红顶子的洋大人”的说法，不把它变成独立历史考证结论。
3. 第 335 行“直隶总署”依同时谈总督调署的语境译张树声署理直隶总督，保留 acting，不译成永久实授；涂宗瀛升任湖广为赫德带来的消息，曾九帅谋两广仍是听说的意愿。
4. 第 345 行小小姐明确七岁，只有发热、疑出痧及请医生诊病的叙述，无不当性描写，完整照译。未将“怕是出痧子”确诊化，采用 may be measles。
5. 第 355 行胡雪岩答应设法扩充医院用地、答应修路，却婉拒请官府驱赶摊贩；三种回应严格区分，均未写成工程已完成。
6. 第 359 行陈启沅雇六七百女工，保留 six or seven hundred。第 361 行“细昌隆”据第 359 行继昌隆统一 Jichanglong，“一型”据推广农村机器语境读小型。机器利弊为人物谈判立场，不添现代分析。
7. 第 369 行重复“派人到乡下收购茧子，价钱虽出得不坏”并有数处严重断裂。英文仅保留购茧、挑剔、纠纷、浙江嘉某地及苏州的丝产区、机器闲置失修坏锈、闭歇这些可读信息，重复语句不重译；不可恢复的地方以方括号明确标出。未补造乡民抵制的具体方式与人物，也未将“嘉”擅补成嘉兴。
8. 第 369 行合同台词写“丝都由你供应”，后文采购写“茧子”；分别保留 silk supplies 与 cocoons，不把不同原词一律改成同一个原料名。价格为市场价另加佣金，属拟议合同，未写已签或已付款。
9. 第 371 行“澄州人”原样音译 Chengzhou，未根据人物履历擅改成湖州；“意利”依造机器国家名称读意大利。公和永与公平洋行沿用已定术语 Gongheyong / Gongping Trading House，二者不混同。
10. 第 373 行“马达一向”依语义读一响。对手工蚕丝户生计的忧虑在英译完整保留。本批无安全改写。
