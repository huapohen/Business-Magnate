胡雪岩｜中英对照版
原著：高阳

萧瑟洋场｜The Desolate Foreign Settlement

第二章（本章第3部分，共6部分）

阅读说明：每段中文后接对应英文；两版英文正文一致。下载站水印已清除，原始疑字在文末说明。

『那太好了。』古应春很高兴地说∶『既然替湖州府帮忙办洋务，教会里的情形一定熟悉，赵宝禄不能不买他的帐。悟心，你这个忙帮得大了。』到了湖州城里，问清楚赵宝禄的教堂在何处，就在附近挑个清静之处泊舟。古应春与雷桂卿带着一个跟班上岸；悟心在船上等，她带来一个食盒，现成的素菜，在船上热一下便可食用，正整治好了尚未动箸，不道古应春一行已经回船了。『怎么这么快？』『事情很顺利。不过太顺利了。』『这是怎么说？』悟心又说∶『我总当你们办完事下馆子，我管我自己吃饭了，现在看样子，你们也还没有吃，要不要先将就将就？』『我们也还有点船菜，不必再上岸了。我要把经过情形告诉你，看有什么法子，不让赵宝禄耍花样。』原来古应春到得教堂，见到赵宝禄，道明来意，原以为他必有一番支吾，哪知他绝口否认有任何耍赖的企图。『做人要讲信用，对洋人尤其重要，我吃了多年的教，当然很明白这层道理。两位请放心，我收了怡和洋行的定洋，丝也定好了，到时候大家照约行家，决无差错。』『可是，』古应春探询似地说∶『听说赵先生跟教友之间，有些瓜葛？』

“Excellent!” Gu Yingchun said with pleasure. “If he helps the prefecture with foreign affairs, he must know the church’s circumstances well. Zhao Baolu will have to take him seriously. Wuxin, you have done us a great service.” On reaching Huzhou, they asked where Zhao’s church was and chose a quiet mooring nearby. Gu and Lei Guiqing went ashore with an attendant, while Wuxin waited aboard. She had brought a food box containing vegetarian dishes that needed only heating. Everything was ready, but she had not yet lifted her chopsticks when the men returned. “Back so soon?” “Things went smoothly. Too smoothly.” “What do you mean?” Wuxin added, “I assumed you would eat at a restaurant after finishing your business, so I was going to eat by myself. It seems you have not eaten either. Will you make do with this?” “We have some food aboard too. There is no need to go ashore again. I must tell you what happened and see whether you can think of a way to prevent Zhao Baolu from playing tricks.” At the church, Gu had found Zhao and explained his purpose. He had expected evasions, but Zhao flatly denied any intention of defaulting. “A man must keep his word, especially when dealing with foreigners. I have lived within the church for many years and naturally understand that. Rest assured, gentlemen. I received Jardine Matheson’s deposits, and the silk has been contracted for. When the time comes, everyone will fulfill his agreement. Nothing will go wrong.” “But,” Gu asked tentatively, “I hear there is some difficulty between you and your fellow Christians?”

『什么瓜葛？』赵宝禄不待古应春回答，自己又说∶『无非说我逼教友捐献。那要自愿，他不肯我不好抢他的；总而言之，到时候如果出了差错，两位再来问我，现在时候还早。』明知道他是敷衍，也明知他将来会耍赖，但却什么劲都用不上，真叫无可奈何。古应春从未遇到过这样的对手，所以神色之间，颇为沮丧。『你不要烦恼！』悟心劝慰着说∶『一定有办法，你先吃完了饭再说。』古应春胃口不开，但经不住悟心殷殷相劝，便拿茶泡了饭，就着悟心带来的麻辣油焖笋，匆匆吞了一碗；雷桂卿吃得也不多，两个都搁下筷子，看悟心捏着三镶乌木筷，慢慢在饭中拣稗子，拣好半天才吃一口。

“What difficulty?” Without waiting for an answer, Zhao went on, “Merely the claim that I force believers to make donations. Donations must be voluntary. If someone refuses, I cannot rob him. In short, if anything goes wrong when the time comes, come and question me then. It is too early now.” Gu knew he was being put off and was equally certain that Zhao would evade his obligations later. Yet there was nothing he could bring to bear on him. It was maddeningly helpless. Never having encountered such an opponent, Gu looked thoroughly discouraged. “Do not worry,” Wuxin comforted him. “There is bound to be a way. Finish your meal first.” Gu had no appetite, but could not resist her repeated urging. He poured tea over his rice and hurriedly swallowed a bowl with the spicy oil-braised bamboo shoots she had brought. Lei ate little too. Both laid down their chopsticks and watched Wuxin slowly pick barnyard-grass grains from her rice with ebony chopsticks bearing three inlays. Only after a long search would she take a mouthful.

『这米不好，是船老大在这里买的。』古应春歉意地说∶『早知道，自己带米来了』。悟心也省悟了，『对不起，对不起。』她说∶『我吃得慢，两位不必陪我，请宽坐用茶。』雷桂卿却舍不得走，尤其是悟心垂着眼皮注视碗中时，是个恣意贪看的好机会，所以接口说道∶『不要紧，不要紧，你尽管慢用。』悟心嫣然一笑，对她的饭不再多挑剔，吃得就快了。

“The rice is poor,” Gu said apologetically. “The boatman bought it here. Had I known, I should have brought some.” Wuxin realized that she was keeping them waiting. “Forgive me. I eat slowly. Please do not feel obliged to sit with me. Make yourselves comfortable and have some tea.” Lei was reluctant to leave. With her eyes lowered over her bowl, he could gaze at her to his heart’s content. “Not at all, not at all,” he said. “Take your time.” Wuxin gave a lovely smile and ceased to be so particular about the rice. Her meal went more quickly.

等小玉来收拾了桌子，水也开了。沏上一壶茶来，扑鼻一股杏子香，雷桂卿少不得又要动问了。『那没有什么诀窍。』悟心答说∶『挑没有熟的杏子，摘下来拿皮纸包好，放在茶叶罐里，隔两天便有香味了。不但杏子，别的果子，也可以如法炮制。』『悟心师太，』雷桂卿笑道∶『你真会享清福。』悟心笑笑不作声，转脸问古应春∶『你的心事想得怎么样了？』古应春确是在想心事，他带着藩司衙门的公文，可以去看湖州知府，请求协助；但如传了赵宝禄到案，他仍旧是这套说法，那就不但于事无补，而且还落一个仗势欺人的名声，太划不来了。

By the time Xiaoyu had cleared the table, the water was boiling. The tea she brewed gave off a fragrance of apricots, prompting another question from Lei. “There is no great secret,” Wuxin answered. “Pick unripe apricots, wrap them in bark paper, and put them in the tea canister. After two days the tea will be fragrant. Other fruit can be used in the same way.” “Mistress Wuxin,” Lei said, laughing, “you certainly know how to enjoy a peaceful life.” She smiled without answering, then turned to Gu. “Have you made any progress with what is troubling you?” He had indeed been thinking. With the provincial treasurer’s dispatch, he could call on the Huzhou prefect and ask for assistance. But if Zhao were summoned and repeated the same arguments, nothing would be gained. Worse, Gu would acquire a reputation for using official influence to bully others. It would scarcely be worth it.

等他说了心事，悟心把脸又转了过去∶『雷先生，要托你办件事。』『是、是。』雷桂卿一叠连声地答应，『你说，你说。』『我写个地址，请你去找一位杨师爷；见了面，说我请他来一趟，有事求他。』悟心又加一句∶『他是乌程县的刑名师爷。』做州县官，至少要请两个幕友，一个管刑名、一个管钱谷，权柄极大。请乌程县的刑名师爷来料理此案，不怕赵宝禄不就范。雷桂卿很高兴地说∶『悟心师太，你真有办法！把这位杨师爷请了来对付赵宝禄，比什么都管用。』『也不见得，等请来了再商量。』于是悟心口述地址，请古应春写了下来，船老大上岸雇来一顶轿子，将欣欣然的雷桂卿抬走了。

When he had explained, Wuxin turned back to Lei. “Mr Lei, I must ask you to do something.” “Yes, yes,” he answered eagerly. “Tell me.” “I shall give you an address. Go and find a certain Secretary Yang. Tell him I should like him to come here; there is something I must ask of him.” She added, “He advises the Wucheng magistrate on criminal and legal affairs.” A prefect or magistrate needed at least two private secretaries: one for criminal and legal matters, the other for revenue and grain. Their influence was considerable. With Wucheng’s legal secretary brought in to manage the affair, Zhao could surely be made to comply. Lei was delighted. “Mistress Wuxin, you truly know how to get things done! Bringing Secretary Yang to deal with Zhao will be more effective than anything.” “Not necessarily. Let us discuss it once he is here.” She dictated the address and had Gu write it down. The boatman hired a sedan ashore, and Lei was carried off in high spirits.

『你要不要去睡个午觉？』悟心说道∶『雷先生要好半天才会回来。』『怎么？那杨师爷住得很远，是不是？』『不但住得远，而且要去两个地方。』『为什么？』悟心诡秘地一笑说道∶『这位雷先生，心思有点歪，我要他吃点小苦头。』『什么苦头？』古应春有点不安，『是我的朋友，弄得他惨兮兮，他会骂我。』『他根本不会晓得，是我故意罚他。』原来这杨师爷住在县衙门，但另外租了一处房子，作为私下接头讼事之用，为了避人耳目，房子租在很荒僻的地方，又因为荒僻之故，养了一条很凶的狗。雷桂卿找上门去，一定会扑空，而且会受惊。

“Would you like an afternoon nap?” Wuxin asked. “Mr Lei will not be back for some time.” “Why? Does Secretary Yang live far away?” “Not only far away. He will have to go to two places.” “Why?” Wuxin gave a mysterious smile. “This Mr Lei’s thoughts stray a little. I mean to make him suffer a small penalty.” “What penalty?” Gu was uneasy. “He is my friend. If you make him miserable, he will blame me.” “He will never know I am punishing him deliberately.” Secretary Yang lived at the county yamen, but had rented another house for private consultations about lawsuits. To avoid attention, he had chosen a very secluded place. Its isolation in turn made it necessary to keep a fierce dog. Lei would certainly fail to find him there and would get a fright into the bargain.

『怎么会扑空呢？悟心解释∶』除非杨师爷自己关照，约在哪里见面，不然他就是在那里，下人也会说不在，有事到衙门去接头。『』怎么会倒在其次，让狗咬了怎么办？『『不会！那条狗是教好了的，来势汹汹把人吓走了就好了，从不咬人。』听这一说，古应春才放下心来；他知道悟心有午睡的习惯，便即说道∶『我倒不困，你去打个中觉。』『好！』悟心问说∶『哪张是你的铺？』『跟我来。』

“Why will he fail to find him?” “Unless Secretary Yang himself has arranged where to meet,” Wuxin explained, “even if he is there, the servants will say he is out and direct callers to the yamen.” “Missing him is a lesser matter. What if the dog bites Lei?” “It will not. The dog is trained. It rushes at people fiercely and frightens them away, but never bites.” Only then was Gu reassured. Knowing that Wuxin habitually slept after lunch, he said, “I am not sleepy. You should take your nap.” “Very well. Which berth is yours?” “Come with me.”

后舱一张大铺，中间用红木隔成两个铺位，上铺洋式床垫，软硬适度，悟心用手揿一揿床垫，又看一看周围的陈设，不由得赞叹∶『财神家的东西，到底不同。』『这面是我的铺。』古应春指着左面说∶『你睡吧，我在外面。有事拉这根绳子。』悟羽将一根红弦绳一拉，前舱的银铃琅琅作响；小玉恰好进前舱，闻声寻来，一看亦有惊异之色。『真讲究！』小玉抚摸着红木～＊子说∶『是可以移动的。』『索性把它推了过去。』古应春说∶『一人个睡也宽敞些。』小玉便依言将红木～＊子推到一边。古应春也退了出去，在中舱喝茶闲眺，心里在盘算，杨师爷来了，如果谈得顺利，还来得及回庵；倘或需要从长计议，是回庵去谈呢？还是一直谈下去，夜深了上岸觅客栈投宿，让悟心师徒住在船上。转念未定，听得帘挂钩响动，是小玉出来了，『古老爷，』她说，『你请进去吧，我师父有事情商量。』到得后舱，只见悟心在他的铺位上和衣侧卧，身上半盖着一条绣花丝被，长发纷披，遮盖了大半个枕头；一手支颐，袖子褪落到肘弯，奇南香手串的香味，俞发馥郁了。『你有事？』古应春在这一面铺前的一张红木骨牌凳上坐了下来。

A broad sleeping platform in the aft cabin was divided into two berths by a rosewood partition. Western mattresses, neither too soft nor too hard, covered it. Wuxin pressed one with her hand and surveyed the furnishings. “Things belonging to the God of Wealth’s household really are different!” she exclaimed. “Mine is this side.” Gu pointed to the left. “Lie down. I shall be outside. Pull this cord if you need anything.” Wuxin tugged a red cord, and a silver bell rang in the forward cabin. Xiaoyu, who had just entered it, followed the sound and looked equally impressed. “How beautifully arranged!” She ran a hand over the rosewood partition. “It can be moved.” “Push it aside altogether,” Gu said. “There will be more room for one person to sleep.” Xiaoyu did so, and Gu withdrew to the middle cabin to drink tea and look out at the scenery. He considered their plans. If the discussion with Secretary Yang went well, there would still be time to return to the retreat. If more thought was needed, should they go back there to continue it, or talk on aboard, however late it grew? They could then find an inn ashore and leave the boat to Wuxin and Xiaoyu. Before he decided, he heard the curtain rings move. Xiaoyu emerged. “Mr Gu, please come in. Teacher wishes to discuss something.” In the aft cabin, Wuxin lay on her side in his berth, fully clothed, with an embroidered silk quilt half covering her. Her long hair spread over most of the pillow. One hand supported her chin; the sleeve had slipped down to her elbow, and the fragrance of her agarwood prayer beads seemed richer than ever. “What is it?” Gu sat on a rosewood stool beside the other berth.

『杨师爷很晚才回来。』悟心说道∶『恐怕要留他吃饭，似乎要预备预备。』『菜倒是有。』古应春说，『船家一早就上岸去买了菜，只以为中午是在城里吃了，你又带了素菜来，所以没有弄出来。你闻！』悟心闻到了，是火腿炖鸡的香味，『你引我动凡心了。』她笑着又说∶『酒呢？』『那更是现成，一坛花雕是上船以后才开的。我还有白葡萄酒，你也可以喝。』古应春又说∶『倒是有件事得早早预备，今晚上你跟小玉睡在船上，我跟雷桂卿住客栈，得早一点去定妥当了它。』『不！』悟心说道∶『睡在船上不妥当了，我还是回庵；不过船家多吃一趟辛苦。』『那没有什么。好了，说妥当了，你睡吧！』

“Secretary Yang will not be back until quite late,” Wuxin said. “We may have to ask him to dinner. Perhaps something should be prepared.” “There is food. The boatman went ashore to buy it early this morning. We thought we should lunch in the city, and you brought vegetarian dishes as well, so it was not served. Smell that!” She caught the aroma of chicken stewing with ham. “You are tempting me back to worldly desires,” she said with a laugh. “And wine?” “Ready too. We opened a jar of huadiao after coming aboard. I have white wine as well, which you can drink. There is one thing we ought to arrange early: you and Xiaoyu can sleep aboard tonight while Lei and I stay at an inn. I had better reserve the rooms.” “No. It would not be proper for me to sleep here. I shall return to the retreat, even if it means another journey for the boatmen.” “That is no trouble. Then it is settled. Sleep now.”

『我还不困，陪我谈谈。』说着，悟心拍拍空铺位，示意他睡下来。古应春有些踌躇，但终于决定考验自己的定力，在雷桂卿的铺位横倒，脸对脸不到一尺的距离。『古太太的病怎么样？好点了没有？』『还是那样子。总归是带病延年了。』『那末，你呢？』悟心幽幽地说∶『没有一个人在身边，也不方便。』古应春想把瑞香的事告诉她，转念一想，这一来悟心一定寻根究底，追问不休，不如不提为妙。

“I am not sleepy yet. Stay and talk.” Wuxin patted the empty berth, inviting him to lie down. Gu hesitated, then decided to test his self-command. He stretched out in Lei’s berth, their faces less than a chi apart. “How is Mrs Gu? Is she any better?” “Much the same. She can only nurse her illness and hope to live on.” “And you?” Wuxin asked softly. “It must be inconvenient, with no one beside you.” Gu thought of telling her about Ruixiang. But she would be sure to pursue every detail and question him endlessly. Better not mention it.

『也没有什么不便。多一事不如少一事。』『什么事都好省，这件事省不得，除非——』悟心忽然笑了起来。这一笑实在诡秘，古应春忍不住问∶『话说半句，无缘无故发笑，是什么花样？除非什么？』『除非你也看破红尘，出家当了和尚，那件事才可以省，不然是省不了的。』『这话也没有啥好笑啊！』『我笑是笑我自己。』

“It is no great inconvenience. One less complication is better than one more.” “You may dispense with anything else, but not this—unless—” Wuxin suddenly laughed. There was something so mysterious in the laugh that Gu could not help asking, “You leave your sentence unfinished and laugh for no reason. What game is this? Unless what?” “Unless you too renounce the world and become a monk. Only then can you dispense with that need. Otherwise you cannot.” “There is nothing so funny about that.” “I am laughing at myself.”

『在谈我，何以忽然笑你自己。』古应春口滑，想不说的话。还是说了∶『总与我有关吧？』『不错，与你有关。我在想，你如果出家做了和尚，不晓得是怎么个样子？想想就好笑了。』『我要出家，也做头陀，同你一样。』『啥叫头陀？』『亏你还算出家，连头陀都不懂。』古应春答说∶『出家而没有剃发，带发修行的叫做头陀；岂不是跟你一样。』『喔，我懂了，就是满头乱七八糟的头发，弄个铜环，把它箍住，象武松的那种打扮？』『就是。』『那叫「行者」！不叫头陀，我那里有本《释氏要贤》说得清清楚楚。』原来她是懂的，有意相谑，这正是悟心的本性；古应春苦笑着叹了口气，无话可说。

“We are discussing me. Why suddenly laugh at yourself?” Gu’s tongue slipped into the very question he had meant not to ask. “It must have something to do with me.” “It does. I was imagining what you would look like as a monk. The thought made me laugh.” “If I renounced the world, I would become a toutuo—like you.” “What is a toutuo?” “And you call yourself a religious person! You do not even know what a toutuo is. Someone who renounces the world without shaving his head and practices with his hair intact is called a toutuo. Is that not just like you?” “Oh, I see. A head of unruly hair held in place with a copper band, dressed like Wu Song?” “Exactly.” “That is called a xingzhe, not a toutuo! My copy of Shishi Yaoxian makes it perfectly clear.” So she had known all along and was deliberately teasing him. It was typical of Wuxin. Gu gave a rueful smile and sighed, with nothing to say.

『应春，我们真希望你是出家的行者。』『为什么？』『那一来，你不是一个人了吗？』古应春心一跳，故意问说∶『一个人又怎么样了呢？』『你不懂？』『我真不懂。』『不跟你说了。』悟心突然一翻身，背对古应春。古应春心想，这就是考验自己定力的时候了，心猿意马地几次想伸手去扳她的身子，却始终迟疑不定。终于忍不住要伸手了，而且手已快碰到悟心的身子了，突然听得扑通一声，是重物落水的声音，古应春一惊缩手，随即听见有人大喊∶『有人掉到河里去了！』

“Yingchun, I really wish you were a xingzhe who had renounced the world.” “Why?” “Then you would be alone, would you not?” Gu’s heart gave a leap, but he deliberately asked, “And what if I were?” “Do you not understand?” “I truly do not.” “Then I shall say no more.” Wuxin suddenly turned her back on him. This, Gu thought, was the moment to test his resolve. His thoughts raced. Several times he wanted to reach out and turn her toward him, but hesitation held him back. At last he could resist no longer. His hand was almost touching her when a sudden splash—the sound of something heavy falling into water—made him start and draw back. Someone shouted, “A person has fallen into the river!”

悟心也吓得坐了起来，推着古应春说∶『你去看看。』等他出去一看，失足落水的一个半大孩子，已经被救了起来。是一场虚惊。回到后舱，略说经过，只见悟心眼神湛然，脸色恬静，从容说道∶『刚才「扑通」那一声，好比当头棒喝。』绮念全消的古应春，亦有这样的感觉，不过当悟心『面壁』而卧时，居然亦跟他一样意马心猿，却使他感到意外。『我在想一个人能不做坏事，也要看看运气。』悟心一翻身拉开丝绒窗帘，指着透过纱窗，影绰绰看得到的一座贞节牌坊说∶『我不相信守寡守了几十年的人，真正是自始至终，冰清玉洁，没有动过不正经的念头，不过没有机会，或者临时有什么意外，打断了「好事」而已。如果因为这样子，自己就以为怎样了不起，依我说，是问心有愧的。』这番话说得古应春自惭不如，笑笑说道∶『你睡吧！我不陪你「参禅」了。』雷桂卿直到黄错日落，方始回船，样子显得有些狼狈，一双靴子溅了许多烂泥。古应春心知其故，也有些好笑，但不敢现于形色，只是慰劳地说∶『辛苦，辛苦。』『还好，还好！』雷桂卿举起脚说∶『路好难走，下了轿，过一顶独木桥，又是一段烂泥路，好不容易找到那里，说杨师爷在县衙门。』『那么，你又到县衙门？』

Wuxin sat up in alarm and pushed him. “Go and see.” By the time he went out, the half-grown boy who had lost his footing was already rescued. It had been no more than a fright. Gu returned to the aft cabin and briefly explained. Wuxin’s eyes were clear, her expression tranquil. “That splash just now was like a blow and a shout awakening a deluded mind,” she said calmly. Gu, whose amorous thoughts had vanished, felt the same. What surprised him was that even while lying with her back to him, she too had been as agitated by desire as he. “I was thinking that whether a person avoids doing wrong depends partly on luck.” Wuxin turned and pulled back the velvet curtain, pointing to a chastity arch dimly visible through the gauze. “I do not believe a woman who has remained a widow for decades has truly been pure as ice and jade from beginning to end, never once entertaining an improper thought. She may simply have had no opportunity, or some accident at the last moment may have interrupted her ‘happy encounter.’ If she then thinks herself wonderfully virtuous on that account, I say she has something to answer for in her own conscience.” Gu felt that he fell short of her candor. Smiling, he said, “Sleep now. I shall not stay to meditate with you.” Lei returned only at sunset, looking somewhat disheveled, his boots splashed with mud. Gu knew why and was amused, but dared not show it. “You have had a tiring errand.” “Not so bad.” Lei lifted a foot. “What a dreadful road! After leaving the sedan I crossed a single-plank bridge, then trudged through mud. When I finally found the place, they said Secretary Yang was at the county yamen.” “So you went there as well?”

『当然。』雷桂卿说，『还好，这一回没有扑空。人倒很客气，问我悟心是不是有什么事找他？我说∶请你来了就知道了。他说还有件公事，料理完了就来。大概也快到了。』正在谈着，悟心翩然出现，脸上刚睡醒的红晕犹在，星眼微饧，别具一种媚态。雷桂卿一看，神情又不同了。『交差，交差。他很起劲地，但却有些埋怨地∶』悟心师太，你应该早告诉我，杨家有条大狗——『』怎么？『悟心装得吃惊是，』你让狗咬了？『』咬倒没有咬，不过性命吓掉半条。『雷桂卿面有余悸，指手划脚地说∶『我正在叫门，忽然发现后面好象有两只手搭在我肩膀上，回头一看，乖乖，好大一条狗，拖长了舌头，朝我喘气。这一吓，真正魂灵要出窍了。』『唷，唷，对不起，对不起！』悟心满脸歉意，『我是晓得他家有条狗，不晓得这么厉害。后来呢？』『后来赶出来一个人，不住口跟我道歉，问我吓到了没有？我只好装「大好佬」，我说∶没有什么，我从前养过一条狗，比你们的狗还大。』『好！』古应春大笑，『这牛吹得好。』悟心也笑得伏在桌上，抬不起头来；雷桂卿颇为得意，觉得受一场虚惊，能替他们带来一场欢乐，也还值得。『你看！』他指着远远而来的一顶轿子，『大概杨师爷来了。』

“Of course. Fortunately, I did not miss him this time. He was very courteous and asked whether Mistress Wuxin wanted him for something. I told him he would find out when he came. He said he had an official matter to finish first. He should be here soon.” As they talked, Wuxin appeared, her face still flushed from sleep, her eyes slightly drowsy, with a charm all their own. At the sight, Lei’s expression changed again. “I have carried out my commission!” he said eagerly, though with a trace of complaint. “Mistress Wuxin, you ought to have told me there was a great dog at the Yang house.” “What?” She feigned alarm. “Did it bite you?” “No, but it frightened half the life out of me.” Still shaken by the memory, Lei gestured as he told them. “I was calling at the door when I suddenly felt what seemed like two hands on my shoulders from behind. I turned—and heavens, what a dog! Its tongue was hanging out, and it was panting in my face. My soul nearly left my body.” “Oh, I am so sorry!” Wuxin looked thoroughly apologetic. “I knew they had a dog but not that it was so fierce. What happened then?” “A man came running out, apologizing over and over and asking whether I had been frightened. I had to put on a brave face. I said, ‘Not at all. I once kept a dog even bigger than yours.’” Gu burst out laughing. “Good! That was a fine boast!” Wuxin laughed too, until she bent over the table and could not lift her head. Lei was rather pleased. A fright was worth enduring if it brought them such merriment. “Look,” he said, pointing to a sedan approaching in the distance. “That must be Secretary Yang.”

果然，轿子停了下来，一个跟班正在打听时，雷桂卿出舱走到船头上去答话。『是不是杨师爷？』于是杨师爷下轿，古应春亦到船头上去迎接，进入舱内，由悟心正式引见。那师爷是绍兴人，年纪不大，只有三十四、五岁，不过绍兴师爷一向古貌古心，显得很老成的样子，所以骤看竟似半百老翁了。彼此请教名字，那杨师爷号叫莲坡，古应春便以『莲翁』相称，寒暄了一会，悟心说道∶『你们喝酒吧！一面喝，一面谈。』于是摆设杯盘，请杨莲坡上坐；悟心不上桌，坐在一旁相陪。

Sure enough, the sedan stopped. While an attendant made inquiries, Lei came out of the cabin and went forward to answer. “Is that Secretary Yang?” Yang alighted, and Gu also went to the bow to welcome him. Once they were inside, Wuxin made the formal introductions. The secretary came from Shaoxing and was only thirty-four or thirty-five. But Shaoxing legal advisers cultivated an old-fashioned appearance and manner that made them seem exceptionally mature; at first glance he looked almost fifty. They exchanged names. Yang’s literary name was Lianpo, so Gu addressed him as “Mr Lian.” After a little polite conversation, Wuxin said, “Have some wine. You can talk as you drink.” Cups and dishes were set out, and Yang Lianpo was invited to take the place of honor. Wuxin did not join the table but sat to one side to keep them company.

话题当然也要她开头，『老杨！』她说，『雷老爷我是初识；应春是多年的熟人，他有事请你帮忙。他的事就是我的事。』『我晓得。』杨莲坡答说∶『四海之内皆兄弟，你就不说，我也要尽心尽力，交个朋友。』『多谢、多谢！』古应春敬了一杯酒，细谈此行的来意，以及跟赵宝禄见面的经过。杨莲坡喝着酒，静静听完全，开口问道∶『应翁现在打算怎么办？』『这要问你啊！』悟心在一旁插嘴，『人家无非要有个着落。』『所谓着落有两种，一是将来要他依约行事，一是现在就有个了断。不知道应翁要哪一样？』

Naturally, she had to open the subject. “Yang, I have only just met Mr Lei, but I have known Yingchun for years. He needs your help. His business is my business.” “I understand,” Yang replied. “All within the four seas are brothers. Even without your asking, I would do my best and make a friend.” “Thank you, thank you!” Gu offered him a cup of wine, then described the purpose of his journey and his meeting with Zhao Baolu. Yang drank and listened quietly to the whole account before asking, “Mr Yingchun, what do you propose to do now?” “That is what we are asking you,” Wuxin put in. “He simply wants a dependable outcome.” “There are two kinds of outcome: having Zhao fulfill the agreement later, or settling the matter now. Which does Mr Yingchun want?”

『这个人很难弄，将来一定会有麻烦，不如现在就来个了断。』古应春说，『此刻要他退钱，不知道办得到，办不到？』『不怕讨债的凶，只怕欠债的穷。如果他钱已经用掉了，想退也没法子。』这是实话，不过古应春亦并不是要赵宝禄即时退钱不可，怡和洋行那方面，只要将与赵宝禄所订的契约转过来，胡雪岩已承诺先如数退款，但将来要有保障，赵宝禄有丝交丝，无丝退还定洋。只是要如何才有保障，他就不知道了。『最麻烦的是，他手里有好些做丝人家写给他的收据，一个说付过钱了，一个说没有收到，打起官司来，似乎对赵宝禄有利。』『不然。』杨师爷说∶『打官司一个对一个，当然重在证据，就是上了当，也只好怪自己不好。如果赵宝禄成了众矢之的，众口一词说他骗人，那时候情形就不同了。不过上当的人，官司要早打，现在就要递状子进来。』『你也是。』悟心插嘴说道∶『这是啥辰光，家家户户都在服侍蚕宝宝！哪里来的工夫打官司？』

“He is a difficult man, and trouble is certain to arise later. Better to settle things now,” Gu said. “Could he be made to return the money at once?” “A fierce creditor is less to be feared than an impoverished debtor. If he has spent the money, he cannot return it even if he wishes.” This was true. Gu, however, did not insist that Zhao repay immediately. As far as Jardine Matheson was concerned, Hu had already promised to refund the full sum in advance once its contract with Zhao was transferred to him. But there had to be security for the future: Zhao must deliver silk if he had it, or return the deposit if he did not. How to secure that guarantee, Gu did not know. “The greatest difficulty is that he holds receipts from a number of silk-producing families. He says he has paid; they say they received nothing. If it comes to a lawsuit, the position seems to favor him.” “Not necessarily,” Yang replied. “In a case between two individuals, the evidence naturally carries great weight. Even if one party has been cheated, he may have only himself to blame. But if Zhao becomes the target of everyone’s complaints, and they all say with one voice that he deceived them, the situation changes. The victims must bring their case early, however. Their petitions should be filed now.” “You too!” Wuxin broke in. “What season do you think this is? Every household is tending its silkworm babies. Where are they to find time for a lawsuit?”

翻译与校勘
1. 覆盖 source_desolate.txt 第 573—729 行的全部 79 个非空行。赵宝禄保证按约履行是他自己的说法，古应春怀疑他将来赖账是判断，均未当作已经验证的事实。
2. 第 581 行“照约行家”按照约行事语义处理。第 621 行“怎么会倒在其次”依前句寻找杨师爷及后句担忧狗咬读“扑空倒在其次”。第 633 行悟羽统一为悟心，第 635 行两处“红木～＊子”由可移动、隔开铺位的上下文译 partition，不猜具体被损毁的汉字。
3. 第 635 行悟心和衣侧卧及后续暧昧为成年人（前批明确三十五六岁与古应春），完整保留。第 699 行落水的半大孩子只有失足获救叙述，与成人暧昧无性互动，无需安全改写。第 695 行下载站水印不译。
4. 第 675—679 行关于头陀、行者的释义是人物互相调笑的对白，保留 toutuo / xingzhe 区别，不作为宗教术语考据结论；原书名“释氏要贤”暂保留 Shishi Yaoxian，未擅校为其他佛典书名。
5. 第 683 行“我们真希望”在此单人谈话语境译 I really wish；第 701 行黄错按黄昏语义译日落。悟心自述侥幸与贞节的观点完整保留，未替她加入评价或伦理结论。
6. 雷桂卿被故意支去偏僻住处，实际受惊但未被狗咬；悟心已知狗凶却当面佯称不知，叙事反差保留。杨莲坡三十四五岁，看似近五十，两个年龄意义不混同。
7. 第 729 行胡拟先向怡和如数退款，前提为合同转让给他；赵未来须有丝交丝、无丝退还定洋。尚未实际退钱或落实担保；杨建议众人立即告状，尚未递状。
阅读提示
- too smoothly 表明顺利反而触发怀疑，后文以赵既不拒绝也不现偿的应对解释其含义。
- He says... they say... 区分当事人各执一词与已确立事实；would / could 标明法律方案仍在商议。
- though... but... 之外，完整理解真正的冲突在收据证据与集体证言的分量，而非简单“赵有收据所以必胜”。
