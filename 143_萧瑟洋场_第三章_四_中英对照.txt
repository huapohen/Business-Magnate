萧瑟洋场｜The Desolate Foreign Settlement

第三章（本章第4部分，共4部分）

阅读说明：每段中文后接对应英文；两版英文正文一致。下载站水印已清除，原始疑字在文末说明。

袁世凯答说∶『身为将门之子，投笔从戎。』又说他带来的十几个少年，都是难得的将才，『请老伯全数录用。』吴长庆大为诧异，不好骂他荒唐、斥之为冒昧。当下派了一名军官携带银票，到旅舍里，将他的同伴好言资遣。当然，袁世凯是被留下来了。『你进了学没有？』『没有。』袁世恺连秀才都不是，不过捐了监生，照例可应北闱顺天乡试；吴长庆便叫他在营读书，拜张謇为师。此人号季直，是南通的名士，他在吴长庆幕府中参赞军务，同时也是吴开庆次子吴保初的业师。

“As the son of a military family, I have laid down the writing brush to take up arms,” Yuan replied. The dozen or so young men he had brought, he added, were all rare military talents. “I ask you, Uncle, to employ every one of them.” Wu was astonished, but could hardly call him absurd or rebuke his presumption. Instead, he sent an officer to the inn with banknotes to dismiss his companions kindly and provide their travelling expenses. Yuan himself was, of course, kept on. “Have you gained admission to the county school?” “No.” Yuan was not even a licentiate. He had merely purchased the status of an Imperial Academy student, which entitled him to sit the Shuntian provincial examination in the northern capital. Wu told him to study in camp under Zhang Jian. Known by the courtesy name Jizhi, Zhang was a distinguished scholar of Nantong. Besides advising Wu on military affairs, he tutored Wu’s second son, Wu Baochu.

既然要应考，张謇当然教他做八股文。袁世恺兴趣缺缺；但陪着张謇谈谈时事，以及用人驭士的手段，却头头是道，很得张謇的赏识。吴长庆幕府中，还有个朱铭盘，也是南通人，与张謇及另一个诗做得极好的范肯堂，号称为『通州三生』；这朱铭盘对袁世恺亦颇有好感，因此，当张謇保荐袁世恺时，而朱铭盘在一旁帮腔以后，吴长庆便委袁为营务处帮办，而且派了两名勤务兵给他。这是前年光绪六年四月间的事。及至朝鲜发生内乱，张树声派丁汝昌特召吴长庆议事。吴长庆带同张謇，在天津密商三日，定策平乱。这年壬午，『子午犯酉，大比之年』，袁世恺奉命入京乡试，恰好也在天津；听说要出兵朝鲜，便去见张謇，想弃文就武，不赴乡试而赴朝鲜。张謇答应了，为他向吴长庆要求，如愿以偿。

Since Yuan was to take the examination, Zhang naturally taught him eight-legged essays. Yuan had little interest in them. But in conversations about current affairs and the management of men, he spoke so cogently that Zhang came to admire him. Another member of Wu’s staff, Zhu Mingpan, was also from Nantong. He, Zhang, and the gifted poet Fan Kentang were known as the Three Scholars of Tongzhou. Zhu too liked Yuan. When Zhang recommended him, with Zhu supporting the recommendation, Wu appointed him assistant in the military administration office and assigned him two orderlies. This occurred in the fourth month of the sixth year of Guangxu, two years before. When the Korean disturbances broke out, Zhang Shusheng sent Ding Ruchang specially to summon Wu for consultation. Wu took Zhang Jian to Tianjin, where three days of secret discussions produced a plan to suppress the disorder. It was the renwu year—one of the years of the Rat, Horse, Rabbit, or Rooster in which the triennial examinations were held. Ordered to go to the capital for the provincial examination, Yuan happened also to be in Tianjin. Hearing of the projected Korean expedition, he sought out Zhang Jian and asked to abandon letters for arms, going to Korea instead of the examination. Zhang agreed to intercede with Wu, and Yuan obtained his wish.

到了烟台以后，吴长庆回登州去调兵遣将，在烟台派船征粮，须备辎重，由张謇负责，事多且杂，张謇顺理成章的找了袁世凯作帮手，由吴长庆下札子委为『前敌营务处』，居然独当一面了。七月十二日黄昏，吴长庆带领大队人马，由烟台抵达朝鲜仁川；可是日本海陆军已经早一小时到达。只是天色已晚，中日两军都住在船上，预备天亮登陆。哪知就夜色苍茫中，闵妃所遣的密使到了。原来朝鲜国王李熙，也象光绪皇帝一样，是旁支入继；李熙的生父『大院君』李是应，便等于醇亲王，所不同的是，『大院君』摄政。

After they reached Yantai, Wu returned to Dengzhou to mobilize his troops. Zhang Jian was responsible for assigning ships, requisitioning provisions, and assembling supplies at Yantai. With so many varied tasks, he naturally turned to Yuan for assistance. Wu issued him a written appointment to the field military administration, and he found himself entrusted with independent responsibility. At dusk on the twelfth day of the seventh month, Wu’s main force arrived at Incheon from Yantai. The Japanese land and naval forces, however, had arrived an hour earlier. As it was already late, both sides remained aboard, intending to land at dawn. Then, through the gathering darkness, secret envoys arrived from Queen Min. Korea’s king, Yi Hui, had succeeded from a collateral branch of the royal house, like the Guangxu emperor. His biological father, Yi Haeung, the Daewongun, occupied a position comparable to Prince Chun’s, with the difference that the Daewongun served as regent.

李熙成年以后，『大院君「自然看不过去，便跟闵妃争权。那闵妃象慈禧太后一样，非常能干，心想朝鲜是中国的藩属，只要倾心结交中国官吏，自然就占上风，此时日本的野心日炽，看朝鲜两派对立，各不相下，便蓄心要找机会，作为入侵的借口。机会终于来了。朝鲜内政不修，人民困苦，士兵的饷欠了好几个月，一再『闹饷』，发又发得不足数，于是便常有造反作乱之事，日本人便买通乱党，故意让他们抢劫日本领事馆，日本便以保护领事馆为名，酝酿出兵朝鲜。闵妃得到消息，向中国官吏告密；驻日公使亦有急电到北洋，中日双方军队都想抢个先着，但同时到达，不分先后，而闵妃的密使一来，情势就不同了。

After Yi Hui came of age, the Daewongun resented the situation and struggled with Queen Min for power. Like the Empress Dowager Cixi, she was highly capable. Since Korea was China’s tributary state, she reasoned, wholeheartedly cultivating Chinese officials would give her the advantage. Japan’s ambitions were growing daily. Seeing two irreconcilable factions in Korea, it watched for an opportunity that could furnish a pretext for invasion. At last one arose. Domestic government was neglected, the people were impoverished, and soldiers had gone unpaid for months. Repeated protests over their pay produced only incomplete payments, so mutinies and disturbances were frequent. The Japanese bought over some of the rebels and deliberately had them plunder the Japanese consulate. They then prepared to send troops under the pretext of protecting it. Queen Min learned of this and secretly informed Chinese officials; China’s envoy in Japan also telegraphed Beiyang urgently. Both countries sought to arrive first, but their forces reached Korea at virtually the same time. The queen’s envoys changed the situation.

这些密使谒见吴长庆、丁汝昌，说日本与李癵应已有勾结。哪一个军队先到朝鲜京城汉城，哪一国便控制了整个局势。这就象楚汉相争，先入咸阳为胜是一样的道理。『为今之计，我们劝天朝大军，乘黑夜登陆，由间道入汉城，一昼夜可以抵达。这条间道捷径是日本人所不知道的。』『主意是很好，可是这一昼夜的供应呢？士兵不能不吃饭啊！』『请放。』闵妃的密使说∶『沿途都设备好了。』吴长庆大喜，立即召集张謇及马建忠密议，决定接受闵妃的计划，先派五百人连夜登陆；另派一千人在黎明下船，其余守在船上待命。密议既定，吴长庆在招商局轮船的大餐间点兵发令。

Meeting Wu and Ding, the envoys said that Japan and the Daewongun were already in collusion. Whichever army reached the capital, Hanseong, first would control the whole situation. It was like the struggle between Chu and Han, when entry into Xianyang determined the advantage. “We advise the Celestial Empire’s forces to land under cover of darkness and take a side road to Hanseong. You can reach it in a day and a night. The Japanese do not know this shortcut.” “An excellent idea. But how are the troops to be supplied for that day and night? They must eat.” “Do not be concerned. Everything has been arranged along the route.” Delighted, Wu immediately called Zhang Jian and Ma Jianzhong into a secret conference. They decided to accept the queen’s plan: five hundred men would land that night, another thousand at dawn, while the rest remained aboard awaiting orders. Once the decision was made, Wu assembled his officers in the China Merchants’ steamer’s dining saloon and issued his commands.

这本来应该是士气昂扬、踊跃争先的一个场面，不过吴长庆下达了命令，肃静无声，约有五分钟之久；这一下气氛便显得很僵硬了。终于有个姓刘的帮带，凑到吴长庆面前低声说道∶『本营都是陆军，从来没有出过海，现在轮船刚停下来，弟兄晕船的很多，能不能请大帅体谅，让大家休息一夜，到天亮再上岸。』此言一出，吴长庆即时变色，偏偏另外还有同样的请求，吴长庆勃然大怒，拍桌骂道∶『这是什么时候，什么地方，你敢不遵我的命令，莫非不知道军法？』说着，要拔令箭斩那个刘帮带。张謇在旁，不等他再开口抢着说道∶『大帅，刘帮带不宜再带兵了；另外派人吧！』『派谁呢？』『我看袁世凯可以接替。』『好！』吴长庆向左右说道∶『把姓刘的先看管起来，等我办完了大事再来处置。』

This should have been an occasion of high spirits and eager volunteers. Instead, after Wu gave his orders, silence lasted for about five minutes. The atmosphere grew painfully strained. At last a deputy battalion commander named Liu approached and spoke quietly. “Our men are land soldiers who have never been to sea. The steamer has only just stopped, and many are seasick. Might you allow them a night’s rest before they go ashore at dawn, Marshal?” Wu’s expression changed instantly. Worse still, others voiced the same request. He flew into a rage and struck the table. “At a time and in a place like this, you dare disobey? Do you not know military law?” He reached for a command token to order Liu’s execution. Zhang Jian hastily intervened before he could speak again. “Marshal, Liu is no longer fit to lead troops. Appoint someone else!” “Whom?” “I suggest Yuan Shikai.” “Very well!” Wu turned to his attendants. “Place Liu under guard. I shall deal with him once the important business is done.”

这时袁世凯已得到通知，进来行了礼；张謇说道∶『大帅有差使派给你，你仔细听着。』吴长庆接口下令∶『刘帮带不遵命令，我已把他革职看管，现在派你为帮带，接管他的队伍；即刻预备，半点钟以后，先领一营人，坐朝鲜派来的船登陆，由朝鲜向导带领，连夜行军。袁世凯，这个差使，你担当得下来，担当不下来？』『能担当。』『好！你部下如有人不遵命，违反军法，准你先斩后报。』说着，吴长庆将手中的令箭，往前一递。袁世凯接令在手，高声答道∶『遵大帅将令。』半点钟不到，袁世凯已扎束停当，草鞋短裤，干净利落，进来向吴长庆禀报∶『已经跟朝鲜的译官商量决定，登陆后连夜急行军，天明到果山早饭，在那里恭候大帅驾临。』辞行既华，立即下船，到得天亮，吴长庆亲统两营，接续前进，中午抵达果山，袁世凯下马迎谒，说已派先锋五百人，由营官率领先走，他特为在此候驾。

Yuan had meanwhile been summoned. He entered and saluted. “The Marshal has a duty for you,” Zhang told him. “Listen carefully.” Wu took up the command. “Deputy Commander Liu has disobeyed orders. I have dismissed him and placed him under arrest. You are now deputy commander and will take over his men. Prepare immediately. In half an hour, lead one battalion ashore in the boats supplied by the Koreans. Their guides will take you forward on a night march. Yuan Shikai, can you carry out this assignment?” “I can.” “Good. If any of your men disobey or violate military law, you may execute them first and report afterwards.” He held out the command token. Yuan accepted it and answered loudly, “I obey your orders, Marshal.” In less than half an hour, he was ready, neatly equipped in short trousers and straw sandals. He returned to report: “I have settled everything with the Korean interpreter. After landing, we shall march rapidly through the night, take breakfast at Guoshan at dawn, and await Your Excellency there.” Having taken his leave, he immediately went ashore. At daybreak, Wu followed with two battalions and reached Guoshan at noon. Yuan dismounted to greet him. He had sent five hundred men ahead under the battalion commander and had remained expressly to await the Marshal.

『路上怎么样？』『一路平安，朝鲜的供应很完备，一切请大帅放心。』『好！』吴长庆又问∶『还有什么事要报告的？』『士兵的纪律不大好，抢民间的东西，还有对妇女无礼，王师戡乱，这样子会让人家看不起，世凯已遵大帅将令，就地正法了七个人。』一听这话，吴长庆放心了。原以为他不会带兵，现在看来，倒真不愧将门之后，当下慰了一番，关照袁世凯继续前进。

“How was the journey?” “Entirely peaceful. The Koreans have supplied everything we need. You may be at ease.” “Good. Anything else to report?” “The men’s discipline was poor. They plundered the inhabitants and insulted women. For an imperial army sent to restore order, such behaviour would earn contempt. Acting under your orders, I have executed seven men on the spot.” Wu felt reassured. He had thought Yuan incapable of commanding troops; now he seemed indeed a worthy descendant of a military family. Wu commended him and instructed him to continue the advance.

当天深夜，先锋五百人到了汉城，在南门扎营。第二天上午十点多钟，吴长庆亲统的一千人亦复疾驰而至，在距汉城七里的屯子山扎下大营，其时『大院君』李癵应已经得到消息，派了他的儿子大将军李载冕来见吴长庆，表示慰劳。吴长庆亦很客气地敷衍了一番，等李载冕一走，立刻进城去拜访李癵应，作礼貌上的周旋。出城回大营以后，吴长庆立即召集高级将领及幕僚密商马建忠建议，擒贼擒王，等李是应来回拜时，设法扣留，送往天津，以寒乱党之胆。倘或乱党不受安抚，再行进剿。吴长庆认为此计大妙，其余的人众都同意，于是密密部署，设下了陷阱，只等李癵应来自投。

Late that night, the five hundred advance troops reached Hanseong and camped at its south gate. After ten the following morning, Wu arrived at speed with his thousand men and established his main camp at Tunzishan, seven li from the city. The Daewongun had learned of their arrival and sent his son, General Yi Jae-myeon, with greetings and gifts for the troops. Wu responded courteously. Once Yi had left, he immediately entered the city to pay the Daewongun a formal visit. Returning to camp, he called his senior officers and advisers into a secret council. Ma Jianzhong proposed seizing the leader to subdue his followers: when the Daewongun returned the visit, they should detain him and send him to Tianjin, intimidating the rebels. If the rebels still refused pacification, military suppression could follow. Wu thought the plan excellent, and everyone agreed. They made their preparations in secret and laid the trap, waiting for the Daewongun to enter it.

李癵应来回拜时，是在下午四点钟，带的卫队有数十名之多；接入帐内，由张謇与马建忠二人，与李癵应笔谈，这样交换意见，即令是泛泛的寒暄，一来一往，亦很费事。等营外李癵应的卫队被隔离开来，看看时候差不多了∶吴长庆便即说道∶『本人奉朝廷之命传旨，着贵藩亲自到北京，面陈乱党的一切。』说完，也不管李癵应听得懂听不懂，由马建忠扶起李癵应出营；外面有一顶轿子，将他塞入轿内，抬起便走，健卒百余人前后夹护，连夜冒雨急驰一百二十里，第二天一早到南阳港口，登上威远兵轮，李是应才知道是怎么回事。

He returned the visit at four in the afternoon, accompanied by several dozen guards. Received inside the tent, he conversed with Zhang Jian and Ma Jianzhong by exchanging written notes. Even commonplace civilities took considerable time in this manner. Once his guards outside had been separated from him, Wu judged the moment ripe. “By command of the court, I convey an imperial order: Your Highness is to go personally to Beijing and report fully on the rebels.” Without waiting to discover whether the Daewongun understood, Ma helped him to his feet and took him out of camp. A sedan chair stood ready. They thrust him inside, lifted it, and hurried away. More than a hundred picked soldiers escorted him before and behind. Through the night and the rain they covered a hundred and twenty li, reaching Namyang harbour early the next morning. Only after he was aboard the warship Weiyuan did the Daewongun understand what had happened.

下一个目标是李癵应的长子，亦即韩王李熙的胞兄李载冕，据说，乱党是由他指挥的。吴长庆派袁世凯领兵入城，逮捕了李载冕，而乱党却已逃散了。当天晚上，吴长庆接到李熙的密报，乱党是屯驻在两个地方，一个叫利泰院，一个叫枉寻里。枉寻里就在吴长庆大营附近，便由他亲自出马；利泰院的任务派了袁世凯，乘黑夜奇袭，抓了一百多人，其余的乌合之众纷纷走避；枉寻里的情形亦差不多。等日军三千人沿大路开到汉城，局势已经平定了。这一来，日军便没有进城的理由，为了避免与清军冲突，驻扎在城外。日本驻韩公使花房义质亦回汉城，向韩国提出赔偿的交涉，这不是吴长庆的事；他将大营移驻东门外关帝庙以后，随即行文北洋，奏请论功行赏。

The next target was his eldest son, Yi Jae-myeon, full brother of King Yi Hui, who was said to direct the rebels. Wu sent Yuan with troops into the city to arrest him, but the rebels themselves had dispersed. That evening, a secret message from the king told Wu that they were concentrated in two places: Itaewon and Wangxunli. Wangxunli lay close to Wu’s main camp, so he dealt with it himself. Yuan was assigned Itaewon. A surprise attack in the darkness captured more than a hundred men, while the remaining rabble fled in every direction. Much the same happened at Wangxunli. By the time three thousand Japanese troops reached Hanseong along the main road, order had already been restored. They therefore had no grounds for entering the city and camped outside to avoid a clash with the Qing forces. Japan’s envoy to Korea, Hanabusa Yoshitada, also returned to Hanseong and opened negotiations for compensation. That was not Wu’s concern. After moving his headquarters to the Temple of Lord Guan outside the east gate, he sent an official report to Beiyang requesting rewards according to merit.

译者说明与学习提示（不属于小说正文）

译者说明
覆盖1389–1453，《萧瑟洋场》第三章余文。袁世恺／袁世凯依同一人物统一Yuan Shikai；吴开庆依上下文吴长庆；“子午犯酉”中的“犯”依十二地支及科考年语境作卯，英译Rat, Horse, Rabbit, Rooster四个纪年生肖，并说明三年一次。
大院君的本名在本批又作李是应，与上批李癵应互证坏字；经韩国国立中央博物馆直接确认“Yi Haeung”为高宗生父、大院君，中文藏品页记“李昰應”。142的英文亦同步改用Yi Haeung，中文源文件未改。资料只用于确认人名，小说的政治叙述与行为归因没有按外部史料改写。来源：https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0402000000.do?relicId=1086&schM=view&searchId=search ；https://www.museum.go.kr/MUSEUM/contents/M0502000000.do?relicId=115641&schM=view 。
果山Guoshan、屯子山Tunzishan、枉寻里Wangxunli照底本音译，位置未能仅从本文确定，不擅修为相近地名。利泰院音近通行地名Itaewon，按该音译书写；仁川Incheon、汉城Hanseong、南阳Namyang。1403明确日军早一小时，1411概括双方同时到达，英文后处用virtually the same time保存这个概括，不删前处一小时。
部队数量和次序：先500人夜登陆、另1000人天明登陆、其余留船；袁半小时内出发；吴带两营跟进；袁报告已斩7人；汉城500先锋、后到1000人；日军3000人。大院君下午四点回访，百余卫兵押送120里，次晨登舰，全部照原文。源仅称袁处置士兵对妇女无礼，未擅自扩展具体侵害细节。
1407李熙成年后大院君“自然看不过去”前后承接略缺，保留其不满及争权，未补写原文没有的退政经过。日方买通乱党的说法是小说叙述，不能当作本译者另行考据的史实证明。本批无需安全改写。
阅读与表达积累
“Instead…”将本应踊跃出战与实际沉默连接，是下一步换将的因果转折。
“who was said to direct the rebels”保留“据说”的证据限制，区别于随后已实施的逮捕。
“By the time… order had already been restored.”可积累过去时背景下，主句过去完成时标出更早完成的动作。
