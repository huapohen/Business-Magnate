萧瑟洋场｜The Desolate Foreign Settlement

第七章（本章第4部分，共6部分）

条件都谈好了，厂房、生财、存货八万银子『一脚踢』。古应春便通知宓本常，照数开出银票；哪知所得的回答是∶『不便照拨。』『怎么？』古应春诧异，『不是有「的款」存在那里的吗？』当初汇丰借出来的五十万银子，除了左宗棠所借的二十万以外，余数由胡雪岩指明，借给尤五出面所办的茧行，作为收买新式缫丝厂之用，这一点宓本常并不否认，但他有他的说法。

All the terms had been settled: the buildings, equipment, and stock were to go for a lump sum of eighty thousand taels. Gu Yingchun therefore notified Mi Benchang to issue a bank draft for the full amount. The answer, to his astonishment, was that the funds could not be released. “What?” he asked. “Was there not a sum specifically set aside for this?” Of the original five hundred thousand taels borrowed from HSBC, two hundred thousand had been lent to Zuo Zongtang. Hu Xueyan had expressly designated the remainder as a loan to the cocoon firm headed by Brother You, to finance the purchase of modern silk-reeling mills. Mi did not deny this, but he had his own explanation.

『应春兄，「死店活人开」，大先生是有那样子一句话，不过我做档手的，如果只会听他的话，象算盘珠一样，他拨一拨、我动一动，我就不是活人，只不过比死人多口气。你说是不是呢？』古应春倒抽一口冷气，结结巴巴说∶『你的话不错，大先生的话也要算数。』『我不是说不算数，是现在没有钱，有，钱又不是我的，我为啥不给你。』『这钱怎么会没有？指明了做这个用途的。』『不错，指明了作这个用途的。不过，应春兄，你要替我想一想，更要替大先生想一想。几次谈到缫丝厂的事，你总说「难，难，不晓得啥辰光才会成功？」如果你说∶快谈成功了，十天半个月就要付款，我自然会把你这笔款子留下来。你自己都没有握，怎么能怪我？』『你不必管我有没有把握，指明了给我的，你就要留下来。』

“Brother Yingchun, a shop may be dead, but the man who runs it must be alive. The Master did say that, certainly. But if, as his manager, I can do nothing except obey him—if I am like a bead on an abacus, moving only when he pushes me—then I am hardly a living man. I have only one breath more than a corpse. Would you not agree?” Gu drew in a sharp breath. “You—you are right,” he stammered, “but the Master’s word must count for something too.” “I am not saying it counts for nothing. I am saying there is no money at present. If there were, why should I withhold it? It is not mine.” “How can the money be gone? It was expressly assigned to this purpose.” “Quite true. But you must consider my position, Brother Yingchun—and the Master’s even more. Every time we discussed the silk mills, you said, ‘Difficult, difficult! Who knows when anything will come of it?’ If you had told me, ‘The agreement is almost settled; payment will be due in ten days or a fortnight,’ naturally I should have kept the money for you. When you yourself had no certainty, how can you blame me?” “My certainty is no concern of yours. Money expressly assigned to me ought to have been kept for me.”

这话很不客气；宓本常冷笑一声说道∶『如果那时候你请大先生马上交代，照数拨给你，另外立个折子，算是你的存款，我就没有资格用你这笑钱。没有归到你名下以前，钱是阜康的。阜康的钱是大先生所有；不过阜康的钱归我宓某所管。受人之禄，忠人之事，银根这么紧，我不把这笔钱拿来活用；只为远在杭州的大先生的一句话，把这笔钱死死守住，等你不知道哪天来用，你说有没有这个道理？』这几句话真是将古应春驳得体无完肤，他不能跟他辩，也不想跟他辩了。

This was distinctly brusque. Mi gave a cold laugh. “Had you asked the Master then and there to order the sum transferred, with a separate passbook opened so that it became your deposit, I should have had no right to use it. Until it was entered in your name, however, the money belonged to Fukang. Fukang’s money belongs to the Master, but its management belongs to me. A man who takes another’s pay must serve his interests faithfully. With credit as tight as it is, am I to leave that money idle merely because of a word from the Master, far away in Hangzhou, and guard it until some unknown day when you may want it? Would that make sense?” These arguments left Gu without a leg to stand on. He could not argue further and no longer wished to.

可是宓本常却还有话∶『你晓得的，大先生的生意愈做愈大，就是因为一个钱要做八个钱、十个钱的生意。大先生常常说∶』八个坛子七个盖，盖来盖去不穿帮，就是会做生意。「以现在市面上的现款来说，岂止八个坛子七个盖？顶多只有一半，我要把他搞得不穿帮，哪里是件容易的事。老兄，我请问你，今天有人来提款，库房里只有那二十几万银子，我不拿来应付，莫非跟客户说∶那笔银子不能动，是为古先生留在那里收买缫丝厂用的？古先生啊古先生，我老宓跟你，到那时候，不要说本来就是阜康的钱，哪怕是两江总督衙门的官款，明天要提了去给兄弟们关饷，我都要动用。客户这一关过不去，马上就有挤兑的风潮，大先生就完完大吉了。『』四姐，老宓的说法，只要是真的，就算不肯帮我忙，我亦没话说。因为虽然都是为小爷叔办事，各有各的权限，各有各的难处，我不能怪他。『』那末，『螺蛳太太立即钉一句∶』你现在是怪他罗？『古应春老实答道∶』是的。有一点。『』这样说起来，是老宓没有说真话！不然你就不会怪他。『螺蛳太太问道∶』他那几句话不真？『

Mi, however, had more to say. “You know how the Master’s business keeps expanding: he makes one coin do the business of eight or ten. He is always saying, ‘Eight jars and seven lids—keep moving the lids without letting anyone see an uncovered jar, and you know how to do business.’ With the cash available in the market today, we have nothing like seven lids for eight jars. At most we have half as many lids as jars. Do you think it is easy to keep everything covered? Let me ask you this, my dear fellow. If a depositor came to withdraw his money today, and the only cash in the vault was that two hundred thousand taels and more, should I refuse to use it? Should I tell him, ‘That money cannot be touched; it is being kept for Mr Gu to buy silk mills’? My dear Mr Gu, at such a moment I tell you I would use not merely Fukang’s own money, but even official funds belonging to the Governor-General of Liangjiang, due to be withdrawn tomorrow to pay the troops. Fail to satisfy our customers and we shall have a run on the bank at once. That would finish the Master.” “Sister Luo,” Gu went on with his account, “if what Old Mi said was true, then even if he would not help me, I had nothing to complain of. We both work for Little Uncle, but each of us has his own authority and his own difficulties. I could not blame him.” “Then,” she immediately pressed him, “you do blame him now?” “Yes,” Gu admitted frankly. “A little.” “In that case Old Mi cannot have been telling the truth, or you would not blame him. Which part of what he said was untrue?”

『还不是头寸。』话到此处，古应春如箭在弦，不发不可，『他头寸是调得过来的，而且指定了收买缫丝厂的那笔款子，根本没有动，仍旧在汇丰银行。』一听这话，螺蛳太太动容了，『姐夫，』她问，『你怎么知道他没有动过？』『我听人说的。』『是哪个？』『这——』古应春答说∶『四姐，你不必问了。我的消息很靠得住。』螺蛳太太有些明白了，阜康管总帐的周小棠，跟宓本常不甚和睦，也许是他透露的消息。

“The part about the shortage of funds, of course.” Gu had now drawn the bow and could no longer hold back the arrow. “He could have arranged the funds. Moreover, the money earmarked for purchasing the mills has never been touched. It is still at HSBC.” Madam Luosi’s expression changed. “Brother-in-law, how do you know he has not touched it?” “Someone told me.” “Who?” “Well—Sister Luo, please do not ask. My information is thoroughly reliable.” She thought she understood. Zhou Xiaotang, who kept Fukang’s general accounts, was not on particularly good terms with Mi Benchang. Perhaps he had let the information out.

『姐夫要我不问，我就不问。不过我倒要问姐夫，这件事现在怎么办？』『收买缫丝厂的事，已经不必再谈了。现在就有八万银子，也买不成功；人家黄佐卿看我拿不出现银，另外寻了个户头，卖了九万五千银子。』古应春说到这里，摇一摇头，脸色非常难看，『四姐，我顶难过的是，在上海滩上混了几十年，听了一句教人要吐血的话。』『噢！』螺蛳太太大为同情，『你说了出来，我来替你出气。』『出气？』古应春连连摇头，『那一来变成「窝里反」了，不好「不好。』『就算我不响，你也要说出来；心里有委屈，说出来就舒服。』古应春沉吟了说∶『好，我说。那天——』

“If you would rather I did not ask, I shall not. But there is something else I must ask: what are you going to do now?” “There is no longer any point in discussing the purchase. Even with eighty thousand taels in hand, I could not buy the mill now. When Huang Zuoqing saw that I could not produce the cash, he found another buyer and sold it for ninety-five thousand.” Gu shook his head, his face dark with distress. “What hurts me most, Sister Luo, is that after all my decades in Shanghai, I had to hear something that could make a man spit blood.” “Oh!” Her sympathy was immediate. “Tell me what he said. I shall get satisfaction for you.” “Satisfaction?” Gu shook his head repeatedly. “That would mean fighting among ourselves. No, no, that would never do.” “Even if I say nothing about it, you must tell me. Speaking about an injustice makes it easier to bear.” Gu hesitated. “Very well. I shall tell you. That day—”

那天——螺蛳太太到上海的前两天，黄佐卿发了个帖子请古应春吃花酒。买卖不成，朋友还是朋友，古应春准时赴约；场面很热闹，黄佐卿请了有近二十位的客，两桌麻将，一桌牌九，打了上千大洋的头。接下来吃花酒，摆的是『双双台』；客人连叫来的局，不下五十人之多，须将整楼三个大房间打通，才摆得下四桌酒。

That day—two days before Madam Luosi arrived in Shanghai—Huang Zuoqing had sent Gu an invitation to a banquet with courtesans. Though their business had fallen through, they were still friends, and Gu arrived punctually. It was a lively gathering, with nearly twenty guests. Two tables of mahjong and one of pai gow yielded over a thousand silver dollars in table fees. The banquet that followed was arranged with courtesans seated alongside the guests. Counting the women summoned to join them, there were at least fifty people. The partitions between three large upstairs rooms had to be opened before there was room for four dining tables.

主客便是收买公和永的潮州帮『鸦片大王』陈和森；古应春也被邀在这一桌坐。笙歌嗷嘈之余，黄佐卿举杯向古应春说道∶『应春兄，我特为要敬你一杯酒；如果十天之前不是你头寸不便，我就不会跟「陈大王」谈公和永，也就少卖一万五千银子了。说起来这一万五千两，是你老哥挑我赚的，我是不是应该敬杯酒。』说完哈哈大笑，管自己干了酒。讲完了这一段，古应春又说∶『四姐，你想，这不是他存心给我难堪？当时，我真正是眼泪往肚子里流。』螺蛳太太亦为他难过，更为他不平，『这件事，大先生晓不晓得？』她问。

The guest of honour was the purchaser of Gongheyong, Chen Hesen, the Chaozhou merchants’ “Opium King.” Gu was seated at the same table. Amid the clamour of music and singing, Huang raised his cup to him. “Brother Yingchun, I particularly want to drink to you. Had you not been short of funds ten days ago, I should never have discussed Gongheyong with King Chen, and I should have received fifteen thousand taels less. In a manner of speaking, it was you who helped me make that extra fifteen thousand. Ought I not to drink to you?” He laughed heartily and drained his cup. Having related this, Gu added, “Sister Luo, was he not deliberately trying to humiliate me? I truly had to swallow my tears.” She grieved for him and felt the injustice even more keenly. “Does the Master know about this?” she asked.

『这件事，我怎么好告诉大先生？不过收买公和永不成这一节，我已经写信给大先生了。』『我在杭州没有听说。』古应春想了一下说∶『算起来你从杭州动身的时候，我的信还没有到。』

“How could I bring myself to tell him such a thing? But I have written to him about the failure to buy Gongheyong.” “I heard nothing of it in Hangzhou.” Gu considered a moment. “My letter probably had not arrived when you left.”

『好！这一节就不去谈它了。至于老宓勒住银不放，有意跟你作对，这件事我一定要问问他。』『不！』古应春说∶『请四姐一定要顾大局，现在局势不大好，全靠大家同心协力，你一问他，必生是非，无论如何请你摆在心里。』『你晓得的，我也同七姐一样，有不平的事，摆在心里，饭都吃不下的。』螺蛳太太说∶『我只要不「卖原告」，他哪里知道我的消息是哪里来的。

“Very well, we shall say no more about that. But as for Old Mi holding back the money and deliberately obstructing you, I must certainly question him.” “No!” Gu exclaimed. “Please think of the larger situation, Sister Luo. Things are difficult at present; everything depends on our working together. If you question him, it is bound to stir up trouble. Whatever you do, please keep this to yourself.” “You know I am like Seventh Sister. If I keep an injustice bottled up, I cannot even eat. So long as I do not give away the complainant, how can he know where my information came from?”

看她态度非常坚决，古应春知道无法打消她的意向；考虑了一会说∶『四姐，你以为不提我的名字，他就不会疑心到我，那是自己骗自己。你总要有个合情理的说法，才可以瞒得过他。』『你讲，应该怎么个说法？』『在汇丰银行，你有没有认识的人？』螺蛳太太想了一下说道∶『有个张纪通，好象是汇丰银行的。』『不错，张纪通是汇丰银行「二写」。』古应春问∶『四姐跟他熟？』『他太太，我们从前是小姊妹。去年还特为到杭州来看过我。』『好！那就有说法了。四姐，你如果一定问这件事，见了老宓就这样子说∶你说，古应春告诉我，阜康的头寸紧得不得了；可是，我听张政通的太太说∶阜康有廿几万银子，一直存在汇丰没有动过。看他怎么说？』

Seeing her determination, Gu knew he could not dissuade her. After some thought he said, “Sister Luo, if you think that merely leaving out my name will keep him from suspecting me, you are deceiving yourself. You need a plausible explanation if you are to put him off the scent.” “Tell me, then. What explanation should I give?” “Do you know anyone at HSBC?” She thought for a moment. “There is a Zhang Jitong. I believe he works there.” “Yes, he is HSBC’s second clerk. Do you know him well?” “His wife and I were friends as girls. She made a special trip to Hangzhou to see me last year.” “Good. Then you have your explanation. If you insist on questioning Old Mi, say this when you see him: ‘Gu Yingchun tells me that Fukang is desperately short of funds. Yet I hear from Mrs Zhang Jitong that Fukang has more than two hundred thousand taels sitting untouched at HSBC.’ See what he says to that.”

『我懂了，我会说得一点不露马脚；明天早晨我先去看张太太，做得象真的一样。我看他一定没话可说；那时候我再埋怨他几句，替你出气。』『出气这两个字，不必谈它。』『好，不谈出气，谈你圆房。』螺蛳太太急转直下地说∶『这件事就算不为你，也不为瑞香，为了七姐，你也要趁我在这里，请我吃这杯喜酒。』古应春终于答应了。于是螺蛳太太便将与七姑奶奶商量好的计划，一一说知；事到如今，古应春除了唯唯称是以外，别无话说。

“I understand. I shall give nothing away. Tomorrow morning I shall first call on Mrs Zhang, to make the story look genuine. I am sure he will have no answer. Then I can reproach him a little and get satisfaction for you.” “Please leave satisfaction out of it.” “Very well. Let us leave that and discuss your wedding night.” She changed the subject with startling abruptness. “Even if you will do nothing for your own sake or Ruixiang’s, you must do this for Seventh Sister: hold the wedding while I am here, so that I may drink a cup at your celebration.” Gu finally agreed. Madam Luosi then explained, point by point, the arrangements she had settled with the Seventh Lady. Matters had gone so far that Gu could only murmur his assent.

第二天早饭既毕，螺蛳太太便催瑞香瑞得出门。这是前一天晚上就说好了；但瑞香因为一出门便是一整天，有好些琐屑家务要安排好，因而耽误了工夫，七姑奶奶帮着一催再催，快到不耐烦时，方始相偕登车，看表上已经十一点了。『刚刚当着七姑奶奶，我不好说，我催你是有道理的，先要到张太太家去一趟，稍为坐一坐到阜康去开银票。现在，辰光不对了，吃中饭的时候去了，一定留住；下半天等去了阜康，就办不成事了。看首饰不能心急；不然十之八九要后悔。现在，没法子，张家只好不去了。』『都是我不好。』瑞香陪笑说道∶『太太何不早跟我说一句。』

The next morning, as soon as breakfast was over, Madam Luosi urged Ruixiang to get ready to go out. This had been agreed the previous evening. But since they would be away all day, Ruixiang had a number of small household matters to arrange, and these delayed her. The Seventh Lady joined in urging her to hurry. They were almost out of patience before the two women finally entered the carriage together; a glance at the watch showed that it was already eleven. “I could not explain in front of the Seventh Lady just now, but I had a reason for hurrying you. We were to call briefly on Mrs Zhang, then go to Fukang to get the bank drafts. The timing is all wrong now. If we arrive at lunchtime, she is sure to keep us for the meal. By the time we have been to Fukang in the afternoon, we shall get nothing done. One must take one’s time choosing jewellery, or nine times out of ten one regrets it. We shall have to miss the Zhangs.” “It is all my fault,” Ruixiang said with an apologetic smile. “Why did you not tell me earlier, Madam?”

『我也不晓得你这么会磨！摸东摸西，忘记掉辰光。喔！』螺蛳太太特为关照∶『回头我同宓先生说，我们是从张家来，你不要多说什么，免得拆穿西洋镜。』瑞香答应着，随同螺蛳太太坐轿子到了阜康；宓本常自然奉如上宾，他的礼貌很周到，从胡老太太起，胡家全家，——问到。接下来又敷衍瑞香，笑嘻嘻地问道∶『瑞姑娘，哪天请我们吃喜酒？』

“I did not know you could dawdle so! Fiddling with this and that until you forget the time. Oh—and remember: when I tell Mr Mi that we have come from the Zhangs, do not say anything that will give the game away.” Ruixiang agreed and accompanied her by sedan chair to Fukang. Mi Benchang naturally treated Madam Luosi as an honoured guest. His courtesies were meticulous: beginning with Old Madam Hu, he asked after every member of the family in turn. He then turned his attentions to Ruixiang and asked with a broad smile, “Miss Rui, when are you going to invite us to your wedding feast?”

瑞香红着脸不答；螺蛳太太接口∶『快了，快了！』她说∶『今天就是为此到钱庄来的，我想支两千银子，七姑奶奶也有个折子在这。』取出七姑奶奶的折子来一看，存银四千五百余两，螺蛳太太作主，也提二千，一共是四千银子，关照宓本常开出数目大小不等的十来张银票，点收清楚，要谈古应春的事了。『宓先生，』她闲闲问说∶『这一晌，上海市面怎么样？』『不好，不好！银根愈来愈紧了。』『我们阜康呢？』『当然也紧。』

Ruixiang blushed and said nothing. “Soon, soon!” Madam Luosi answered for her. “That is why we have come to the bank today. I should like to withdraw two thousand taels. I also have the Seventh Lady’s passbook here.” She took it out and found a balance of more than four thousand five hundred taels. On her own authority, she withdrew another two thousand from that account, making four thousand in all, and asked Mi to issue a dozen or so bank drafts in assorted amounts. Once she had counted and received them, she was ready to raise Gu’s affairs. “Mr Mi,” she asked casually, “how has business been in Shanghai lately?” “Bad, bad! Money grows tighter every day.” “And at our Fukang?” “Tight here too, of course.”

『既然紧，』螺蛳太太摆出一脸困惑的神情，『为啥我们有廿几万银子摆在汇丰银行，动都不动？』一听这话，宓本常心里一跳；正在难于作答时，不道螺蛳太太又添了一句话，让松了口气。『这笔款子是不是汇丰借出来的？』『是的。』

“If money is tight,” she said with a look of bewilderment, “why do we leave more than two hundred thousand taels at HSBC without touching it?” Mi’s heart gave a jolt. He was struggling for an answer when she unexpectedly added a question that allowed him to breathe again. “Is this the money borrowed from HSBC?” “Yes.”

『汇丰借出来的款子，当然要出利息；存在汇丰虽也有利息，不过一定放款利息高，存款利息低，是不是？』『是的。』『借他的钱又存在他那里，白贴利息的差额；宓先生，这把算盘是怎么打的，我倒不太懂了。』这时宓本常已经想好了一个很巧的理由，可以搪塞；因而好整以暇地答说∶『罗四太太，这里头学问很大，不是我吹，其中的诀窍是我跟了大先生十几年才摸出来的。我们先吃饭，等我慢慢讲给罗四太太你听。』已是午饭辰光，而且宓本常已有预备，螺蛳太太也就不客气了。不过既无堂客相陪，而瑞香的身分不同，不肯与螺蛳太太同桌，却颇费安排；最后是分了两样菜让瑞香在另一处吃，密本常陪螺蛳太太一面吃、一面谈。

“Naturally, interest must be paid on money borrowed from HSBC. A deposit there earns interest too, but surely the rate they charge on a loan is higher than the rate they pay on a deposit?” “Yes.” “Then we borrow their money and leave it with them, paying the difference in interest for nothing. Mr Mi, I cannot quite follow the reckoning.” By now Mi had thought of an ingenious excuse with which to fend her off. At his leisure, he replied, “Madam Luo, there is a great deal to be learned here. I am not boasting: I discovered the secret only after working for the Master for more than ten years. Let us eat first, and I shall explain it to you slowly.” It was lunchtime, and Mi had already made preparations, so she accepted readily. Seating them posed some difficulty, however. There was no lady of the household to accompany her, while Ruixiang, conscious of their difference in status, would not sit at the same table. In the end two dishes were set aside for Ruixiang to eat elsewhere, and Mi kept Madam Luosi company over lunch and conversation.

『罗四太太，阜康有款子存在汇丰，想来是应春告诉你的？』『不是。』螺蛳太太从从容容地答说∶『今天去看一个张太太，他们老爷也在汇丰，是她告诉我的。』『呃，是弓长张，还是立早章？』『弓长张。』『那末是张纪通？』『对的，他们老爷叫张纪通。』

“Madam Luo, I imagine it was Yingchun who told you that Fukang had money deposited at HSBC?” “No,” she answered composedly. “I called on a Mrs Zhang today. Her husband works at HSBC too. She told me.” “Ah, is that Zhang written with ‘bow’ and ‘long,’ or Zhang written with ‘stand’ and ‘early’?” “The one with ‘bow’ and ‘long.’” “Then it must be Zhang Jitong?” “Yes. Her husband’s name is Zhang Jitong.”

宓本常心想，螺蛳太太明明是撒谎。张纪通跟他也是朋友，前一天还在一起打牌；打到深夜一点钟，张纪通大输家，『扳轿杠』一定要再打四圈。当时就有人说∶『老张，你向来一到十二点，一定要回去的。今天夜不归营，不怕张大嫂罚你跪算珠珠、顶马桶盖。』原来张纪通惧内，所以这样打趣他；哪知他拍一拍胸脯说∶『放心，放心，雌老虎前天回常熟娘家，去吃她侄儿的喜酒去了。』这是所谓『欲盖弥彰』，愈发可以证实，汇丰存款的消息是古应春所泄露。不过他绝不说破，相反地，在脸上表现了对古应春抱歉的神态。

Mi knew perfectly well that she was lying. Zhang Jitong was a friend of his; they had played cards together only the previous evening. At one in the morning, Zhang, the heaviest loser, had insisted on another four rounds to try to recover his money. Someone had teased him: “Old Zhang, you always go home on the stroke of twelve. If you stay out all night today, are you not afraid your wife will make you kneel on abacus beads and balance a chamber-pot lid on your head?” Zhang was afraid of his wife, hence the joke. But he had slapped his chest and declared, “No fear, no fear! The tigress went back to her family in Changshu the day before yesterday, for her nephew’s wedding.” Here was an attempt at concealment that only made the truth more conspicuous. It gave Mi further confirmation that Gu had disclosed the HSBC deposit. He said nothing to expose the lie, however. Instead, he assumed an expression of regret towards Gu.

『螺蛳太太，阜康的存款、放款都有帐可查的，存在汇丰的这笔款子当然也有帐；不过每个月倒贴的利息，在帐上看不出是亏损。啥道理呢？这笔利息的差额是一厘半，算起来每个月大概要贴四百两银子，我是打开销里面，算正当支出。』说到这里他停了下来，看螺蛳太太的表情。她当然是面现惊异之色，『是正当开支？』她问，仿佛自己听错了似的。『如果她声色不动，宓本常便不能确定，她是不是把他的话听了进去；而惊讶却是正常的，他就更有把握能将她的疑团消除了。

“Madam Luosi, all Fukang’s deposits and loans are recorded in the books, including this deposit at HSBC. But the extra interest we pay each month does not appear as a loss. Why? The difference in the rates is 0.15 percent a month, which costs us about four hundred taels monthly. I enter it among our expenses, as a legitimate operating cost.” He paused to observe her expression. Naturally, she looked astonished. “A legitimate operating cost?” she asked, as if she must have misheard. Had her expression remained unchanged, Mi could not have been sure she had taken in his words. Surprise was the natural response and increased his confidence that he could dispel her doubts.

『不错，是正当开支，好比逢年过节要应酬官场一样，是必不可少的正当开支。』他说∶『螺蛳太太，你晓得的，阜康全靠公家同大户的存款，阜康的利息比人家低，为啥愿意存阜康，就因为可靠如果有人存点疑惑怕靠不住，来提存款，一个两个不要紧，人一多，消息一传，那个风潮一闹开来，螺蛳太太我就只有一条路好走。』『喔！哪一条路？』

“Exactly. A legitimate expense—as necessary as the money spent maintaining relations with officials at New Year and the other festivals. You know, Madam Luosi, that Fukang depends entirely on deposits from public offices and wealthy households. We pay less interest than other banks. Why do people still put their money with us? Because we are dependable. If someone begins to doubt that and comes to withdraw his deposit, one or two withdrawals do no harm. But if the numbers grow, word spreads, and a run begins, there will be only one road left for me.” “Oh? What road is that?”

『死路。不是一条绳子，就是三钱鸦片烟。』宓本常说∶『我只有来生报答大先生了。』螺蛳太太再精明，也不能不为宓本常蓄意表示尽忠负责的神态所感动，『宓先生，你不要这么说！只要你实心实力，一定不会没有好结果。』她说∶『你的忠心，大先生晓得的。』『就为了大先生得罪了人也值得。』宓本常马上又将话拉回来，『螺蛳太太，有阜康这块金字招牌，存款不必我去兜揽，自会送上门来。我的做法，就是要把我们的这块金字招牌擦得晶光丈亮，不好有一点点不干净的地方。款子存在汇丰，倒贴利息，就是我保护金字招牌的办法。』『嗯！嗯！』螺蛳太太想了一会说∶『你的意思是阜康有廿几万银子在汇丰，不去动它，显得阜康的头寸很宽裕，人家就放心不来提存了。』『一点不错。螺蛳太太，你真是内行。』宓本常举一举杯，自己喝了一大口，得意之情，溢于言表。

“The road to death. A rope, or three mace of opium. I could repay the Master only in my next life.” Shrewd as she was, Madam Luosi could not help being moved by this carefully contrived display of loyalty and responsibility. “Mr Mi, you must not talk like that! So long as you do your utmost in good faith, things cannot fail to turn out well. The Master knows how loyal you are.” “For his sake, even offending people is worthwhile.” Mi immediately brought the conversation back to his point. “With Fukang’s golden signboard, I need not go out soliciting deposits. They come to our door of their own accord. My method is to polish that signboard until it positively shines, without the smallest blemish. Keeping money at HSBC and paying the difference in interest is how I protect our reputation.” “I see, I see.” She thought for a moment. “You mean that leaving those two hundred thousand taels and more untouched at HSBC makes Fukang appear amply supplied with ready money. People feel reassured and do not come to withdraw their deposits.” “Exactly! Madam Luosi, you are a true expert.” Mi raised his cup and took a long drink, unable to conceal his satisfaction.

『原来有这样一招在里面。说起来也是迫不得已。』『先是迫不得已，后来我才悟出诀窍，实在是正当的做法，就银根不紧，也应该这么办。有一回法大马路周道台的五姨太来提款，我说∶你是不是要转存汇丰？如果要存汇丰，我打汇丰的票子给你，转帐不但方便，而且进出不必「贴水」，比较划算。螺蛳太太，你道她听了我的话怎么说？』『我猜不着。她怎么说？』

“So that is the trick. Necessity drove you to it, then.” “At first it was necessity. Later I understood the principle and saw that it was really the right thing to do. We ought to do it even when money is not tight. Once the fifth concubine of Intendant Zhou, who lives on French Main Road, came to withdraw some money. I asked her, ‘Are you transferring it to HSBC? If so, I can give you a draft on HSBC. The transfer will be convenient, and you will lose nothing in exchange deductions when the money goes out and comes in. It will be more economical.’ Madam Luosi, what do you think she said?” “I cannot guess. What did she say?”

译者说明与学习提示（不属于小说正文）

第154批：萧瑟洋场，源行2611–2724（非空行2611–2723），57行，23个语义段。

忠实性与数字：
- 原拟以八万两一次买断公和永的厂房、设备与存货，宓本常拒绝拨款，古应春并未买成。黄佐卿随后以九万五千两售予陈和森，差额一万五千两；不得把原谈妥条件误作已付款成交。
- 汇丰原借五十万两，其中左宗棠借走二十万，余款指定借给尤五出面的茧行买厂。宓先说无钱，古后称款项仍在汇丰，螺蛳太太再向宓求证。周小棠泄露消息仅是螺蛳太太的猜测，并非叙述确认。
- 2653“打了上千大洋的头”按赌局抽头译作 table fees，保留银元计价，不改为银两，也不把抽头算作下注总额。“双双台”按客人与应召来侑酒的妓女同席说明，保留近二十位客人、总人数不少于五十、三间房和四桌。
- 2685螺蛳太太本人提二千两，七姑奶奶存折余额四千五百余两也提二千，共四千，已领数额不一的十来张银票；没有把存折全部余额取走。
- 2713“一厘半”按同句“每月约四百两”与前文“二十几万”本金，推为每月0.15%的利差，与月贴约四百两的量级吻合。此为文内金额支持的单位解释，不是经史料确认的历史利率；不得与149批“一厘”含混处不加区别混用。
- 2723向周道台五姨太提出开汇丰票、免去进出贴水，属宓回忆的建议；其答复在155批承接，本批没有提前写她同意转存或实际转账。

源文缺字、异体与合理辨读：
- 2625“没有握”按“没有把握”；2629“你这笑钱”按“你这笔钱”；2631杂乱引号按发言和叙事切换复原，前半宓对古，后半古向螺蛳太太转述并回答她追问。整条源行保留在同一译段，不把后半移至下一行。
- 2655“挑我赚的”据上下文读为帮助我赚到，不擅加中介佣金；只保留黄的讥讽归功说法。
- 2673张政通与2667、2669及2705–2711张纪通为同一汇丰职员，统一 Zhang Jitong；“二写”保守译 second clerk，不擅定精确西式职位。
- 2681“催瑞香瑞得出门”明显排印损坏，按催她准备出门处理；2683“一一问到”的字形噪点恢复通常语义。
- 2681先称登车，2683又称坐轿子，译文分别保留 carriage / sedan chair，不擅自补换乘情节。
- 2697“密本常”统一 Mi Benchang；2717段首多余引号按叙述处理。
- 2701–2707以字形区分张、章，译为 two spellings of Zhang 的实际部件询问（bow / long 与 stand / early）；此辨认解释是对白组成，不省略。
- 2721三钱鸦片为人物夸大效忠的自杀话语，按原文简短保留，不补充任何实施信息。
- 确切站点水印“奇#書*網收集整理”“奇--書∧網”未译入正文；源行号完整覆盖。无安全改写，无中文override。

英语一综合阅读提示（例句来自本批）：
- “Had you asked ... I should have had no right ...”和“Had you not been short of funds ...”均是省略if的过去反事实条件；分别说明未发生的转款手续与黄佐卿事后讥讽，不能读作已发生事实。
- 宓的论证从“资金周转紧”转为“留存汇丰维护信用”，读段落排序时可追踪反问、回答与“Why?”后的解释链；人物推理不等于作者确认其动机正当。
- “So long as I do not give away the complainant ...”的条件和2711“不揭穿”的行动并置，构成读者知道而人物自以为隐藏的反讽。
- “On her own authority”对应螺蛳太太作主提款，“assumed an expression”对应宓刻意装出神态；精确动词有助于把握人物行为与动机。
