萧瑟洋场｜The Desolate Foreign Settlement

第七章（本章第6部分，共6部分）

阅读说明：每段中文后接对应英文；两版英文正文一致。下载站水印已清除，原始疑字在文末说明。

原是一桩喜事，落了些不中听的闲话，就犯不着了。『这话倒提醒古应春了。七姑奶奶是最讨厌闲言闲语的，场面过于俭朴，就可能会有人说∶』古应春不敢铺张；因为讨小老婆的场面太热闹了，大老婆会吃醋。『倘或有这样的一种说法，传到七姑奶奶耳朵里，她会气得发病。这是非同小可的一件事，古应春很感谢宓本常能适时提醒，让他有此警惕。因而拱着手说∶『老宓，你完全是爱护我的意思，我不敢不听，不过到底只有两天的工夫预备，也只好适可而止。』『当然、当然，一定要来得及。现在第一件要紧的是，把请客的单子拟出来。你的交游一向很广，起码也要请个十桌八桌，我看要另外借地方。』『不，不！那一来就没有止境了。请客多少只能看舍间地方大小而定。』于是细细估量，将内外客厅、书房、起坐间都算上，大概只能摆七桌，初步决定五桌男客，两桌女客。『本来天井里搭篷，还可以摆四桌，那一来「堂会」就没地方了。』宓本常说∶『好，准定七桌，名单你开，帖子我叫我那里的人来写，至晚明天下午一定要发出。菜呢，你看用哪里的菜？』

“It is a happy occasion. There is no point in giving people something unpleasant to say about it.” Mi’s words reminded Gu of a danger. The Seventh Lady detested gossip. If the celebration was too modest, someone might say that Gu dared not make a fuss over taking a concubine because his wife would be jealous. Should that reach her ears, she would make herself ill with anger. This was serious. Grateful for the timely warning, Gu clasped his hands. “You have my interests entirely at heart, Mi, and I must listen. Still, with only two days to prepare, we must observe some limits.” “Certainly. Everything must be ready in time. First we need a guest list. Your acquaintance is wide: at least eight or ten tables. I think we shall need another venue.” “No! Then there would be no end to it. The number must depend on the space in my house.” They calculated carefully. Including the inner and outer reception rooms, study, and sitting room, there was room for about seven tables. Provisionally they allotted five to men and two to women. “A canopy over the courtyard would give us four more,” Mi said, “but then there would be nowhere for the entertainment. Very well, seven tables. You make the list; my staff will write the invitations. They must go out by tomorrow afternoon at the latest. Where shall we order the food?”

请你斟酌，只要好就好。『』不但要好，还要便宜。『宓本常又问∶』客人是下半天四五点钟前后就来了，堂会准定四点钟开场，到晚上九点钟歇锣，总要三档节目；应春兄，你看，用哪三档？『』此道我亦是外行，请你费心提调。『』我看？『宓本常一面想，一面说∶』先来档苏州光裕社的小书；接下来弄一档魔术，日本的女魔术师天胜娘又来了，我今天就去定好了；压轴戏是「东乡调大戏」，蛮热闹的。『古应春称是，都由宓本常作主。等他告辞而去，古应春将所作的决定告诉七姑奶奶，她却颇有意见。『我看堂客不要请。』她说，『请了，人家也未见得肯来。』本来纳宠请女客，除非是儿孙满堂的老封翁，晚辈内眷为了一尽孝心，不能不来贺喜见礼；否则便很少有请女客的。上海虽比较开通，但吃醋毕竟是妇人天性，而嫡庶之分，又看得极重；如果是与七姑奶奶交好的，一定会作抵制。古应春觉得自己同意请女客，确是有欠思量。

“Choose for me. So long as it is good.” “Good and reasonably priced. The guests will arrive around four or five. The entertainment must begin at four and finish at nine, so we shall need three acts. Which would you like?” “I know little about that. Please arrange it.” Mi considered. “First, a programme of ballad-storytelling from Suzhou’s Guangyu Society. Then magic: the Japanese conjuror Tianshengniang is back, and I shall book her today. The finale can be Dongxiang opera. That is very lively.” Gu agreed and left everything to him. After Mi departed, Gu told the Seventh Lady what they had decided. She had objections. “I do not think we should invite women. Even if invited, they may not come.” Ordinarily, women were not invited to a celebration of taking a concubine unless the man was an elderly patriarch surrounded by children and grandchildren. The younger women of his family would then have to offer congratulations and pay their respects from filial duty. Otherwise such invitations were rare. Shanghai might be more liberal, but jealousy was still regarded as natural to women, and the distinction between wife and concubine remained strict. Women close to the Seventh Lady would certainly stay away in protest. Gu realized that he had agreed too thoughtlessly.

『再说，我行动不便，没法子作主人；更不便劳动四姐代我应酬。』七姑奶奶又说∶『如果有几位堂客觉得无所谓的，尽管请过来；我们亦就象平常来往一样不拘礼数，主客双方都心安，这跟特为下帖子是不同的。你说是不是呢？』『完全不错。』古应春从善如流地答说∶『不请堂客。』『至于堂会热闹热闹；顺便也算请四姐玩一天，我赞成。不过，东乡调可以免了。』原来东乡调是『花鼓戏』的一种，发源于浦东，所以称为『东乡调』，又名『本滩』是『本地滩簧』的简称。曲词卑俚，但连唱带做，淫治异常，所以颇具号召力，浦东乡下，点起火油灯唱东乡调的夜台戏，真有倾村来观之盛。但却难登大雅之堂。『「两只奶奶抖勒抖」，』七姑奶奶学唱了一句东乡调说，『这种戏，怎么好请四姐来看？』看她学唱东乡调的样子，不但古应春忍俊不禁，连下人都掩着嘴笑了。

“Besides, I cannot get about to act as hostess, and it would be awkward to trouble Sister Luo to entertain on my behalf. If a few women feel comfortable coming, by all means let them. We can treat it as an ordinary informal visit, without ceremony, so that everyone feels at ease. That is different from issuing special invitations. Do you agree?” “Entirely,” Gu readily conceded. “We shall not invite women formally.” “As for some lively entertainment, I approve. It will give Sister Luo a pleasant day too. But omit the Dongxiang opera.” Dongxiang was a form of flower-drum opera originating in Pudong, hence its name, “eastern district tunes.” It was also called bentan, short for local tanhuang. Its lyrics were coarse, and the combination of singing and acting was extremely bawdy, which made it popular. In rural Pudong, whole villages would turn out for night performances by kerosene light. It was hardly suitable for a refined gathering. “‘Those two breasts go jiggle-jiggle,’” the Seventh Lady sang in imitation. “How could we invite Sister Luo to watch that?” Her performance made Gu laugh despite himself; even the servants covered their mouths and giggled.

『不唱东乡调，唱啥呢？』『杭州滩簧，文文气气，又弹又唱，说是宋朝传下来，当时连宫里都准去唱的。为了请四姐，杭州滩簧最好；明天倒去打听打听，如果上海有，叫一班来听听。』『好！』古应春想了下说∶『堂客虽不请，不过你行动不便，四姐可是作客总要请一两个来帮忙吧！『请王师母好了。』王师母的丈夫王仲文是古应春的学生，在教堂里当司事，也收学生教英文，所以称的他的妻子为『师母』，七姑奶奶也是这样叫她。但七姑奶奶却不折不扣地是王师母的『师母，』因此，初次听她们彼此的称呼，往往大惑不解。

“What shall we have instead?” “Hangzhou tanhuang. It is elegant, with strings and singing. They say it dates from the Song dynasty and was even admitted to the palace. That would be best for Sister Luo. Inquire tomorrow: if there is a troupe in Shanghai, ask it to perform.” “Very well.” Gu considered. “Even without women guests, you cannot get about, and Sister Luo is a visitor. We ought to ask one or two women to help.” “Ask Mrs Wang.” Her husband, Wang Zhongwen, was Gu’s pupil. He served as a church clerk and also took pupils in English, so his wife was called shimu, “teacher’s wife.” The Seventh Lady used that title too. Yet as the wife of Wang’s own teacher, the Seventh Lady was in turn Mrs Wang’s shimu. Anyone first hearing their forms of address was apt to be thoroughly confused.

螺师太太即是如此，那天王师母来了，七姑奶奶为她引见，又听王师母恭恭敬敬地说∶『师母这两天的气色，比前一晌又好得多了。』便忍不住要问。『你们两位到底哪个是哪个的师母？』『自然是师母是我的师母；我请师母不要叫我小王师母，师母不听，有一回我特为不理师母，师母生气了，只好仍旧听师母叫我小王师母。』一片叽叽喳喳的师母声，倒象在说绕口令；螺蛳太太看她二十五六岁年纪，生就一张圆圆脸，觉得亲切可喜，自然而然地便熟悉得不象初见了。

Madam Luosi was no exception. When Mrs Wang arrived that day and was introduced, she heard the young woman respectfully say, “Shimu, you look better again than you did a little while ago.” Madam Luosi could not resist asking, “Which of you is whose shimu?” “Naturally, Shimu is my shimu. I have asked Shimu not to call me Young Mrs Wang, but Shimu will not listen. Once I deliberately failed to answer Shimu, and Shimu was angry, so I had to let Shimu go on calling me Young Mrs Wang.” The torrent of shimu sounded like a tongue-twister. Madam Luosi found the round-faced woman, about twenty-five or twenty-six, warm and agreeable. They quickly felt as though they had known each other for years.

尤其是看到小王师母与瑞香相处融洽的情形，更觉欣慰。原来瑞香虽喜终身有托，但在好日子的这一天，跟一般新嫁娘一样，总不免有凄惶恐惧之感，更因是螺蛳太太与七姑奶奶虽都待她不坏，但一个是从前的主母，一个是现在的大妇，平时本就拘谨，这一天更不敢吐露内心的感觉，怕她们在心里会骂她『轻狂不识抬举』。幸而有热心而相熟的小王师母殷勤照料，不时嘘寒问暖，竟如同亲姊妹一般；瑞香一直悬着的一颗心才能踏实，脸上也开始有笑容了。在螺蛳太太，心情非常复杂，对瑞香，多少有着嫁妇儿的那种心情；但更重要的是古家的交情。因此，她虽了解瑞香心里的感觉；却苦于没有适当的话来宽慰她；如今有了小王师母能鼓舞起瑞香的一团喜气，等于自己分身有术，可以不必顾虑瑞香，而全力去周旋行动不便的七姑奶奶，将这场喜事办得十分圆满。当然，这场喜事能办得圆满，另一个『功臣』是宓本常。对于他的尽心尽力，殷勤周到，不但螺蛳太太大为嘉许，连古应春夫妇都另眼相看了。

She was especially relieved by the easy affection between Young Mrs Wang and Ruixiang. Ruixiang was glad to have her future settled, yet on this auspicious day she felt the anxiety and fear common to a bride. Madam Luosi and the Seventh Lady treated her well, but one was her former mistress and the other now the principal wife. She had always been reserved with them, and today dared reveal still less of her feelings, lest they think her frivolous or ungrateful. Fortunately, the familiar and warm-hearted Young Mrs Wang attended to her needs with the care of a sister. Only then did Ruixiang’s anxious heart settle and smiles begin to appear. Madam Luosi’s feelings were complicated. Towards Ruixiang she felt something of a mother marrying off her daughter, but her friendship with the Gu household mattered still more. She understood Ruixiang’s emotions without finding the right words to comfort her. Now that Young Mrs Wang could bring out her happiness, Madam Luosi felt almost as though she had gained a second self. Free of that concern, she could devote herself to the Seventh Lady and help make the celebration a complete success. Another contributor, of course, was Mi. His tireless and thoughtful attention won not only Madam Luosi’s praise but a changed opinion from Gu and his wife.

果如七姑奶奶的估计，堂客到得极少，连一桌都凑不满，但男客却非常踊跃。当堂会开始时，估计已经可以坐满五桌了。由于是纳妾，铺陈比较简单，虽也张灯结彩，但客堂正中却只挂了一帽大红缎子彩绣的南极寿星图，不明就里的，只当古家做寿。这是七姑奶奶与螺蛳太太商量定规的，因为纳妾向来没有什么仪节，只是一乘小轿到门，向主人主母磕了头，便算成礼。如今对瑞香是格外优遇，张灯结彩，已非寻常，如果再挂一幅和合二仙图，便象正式结裏，礼数稍嫌过分，所以改用一幅寿星图。瑞香的服饰，也是七姑奶有与螺蛳太太商量过的。妇人最看重的是一条红裙，以瑞香的身份，是没有资格着的；为了弥补起见，许她着紫红夹袄，时日迫促，找裁缝连夜做亦来不及；仍旧是宓本常有办法，到跟阜康钱庄有往来的当铺中去借了一件全新的来，略微显得小了些，但却更衬托出她的身材苗条。

As the Seventh Lady had predicted, few women came—not enough even for one full table. The men, however, arrived eagerly. By the start of the entertainment, there seemed enough to fill five tables. Since the occasion was the taking of a concubine, the decorations were relatively simple. There were lanterns and festive hangings, but the centre of the reception hall displayed only a brightly embroidered picture on red satin of the Old Man of the South Pole, the God of Longevity. An uninformed visitor might have thought it a birthday celebration. The Seventh Lady and Madam Luosi had agreed upon this. Taking a concubine ordinarily involved little ceremony: a small sedan arrived, the woman kowtowed to master and mistress, and the matter was complete. Ruixiang was receiving exceptional consideration already. Lanterns and coloured hangings were unusual; to display the Two Immortals of Harmony as well would make it resemble a formal marriage and exceed the proper rites. They had therefore chosen the longevity picture instead. The two women had also discussed Ruixiang’s clothes. A red skirt was what women valued most, but her status did not entitle her to wear one. As compensation she was allowed a plum-red lined jacket. Time was too short even for a tailor working through the night. Again Mi found a solution: he borrowed a completely new one from a pawnshop that dealt with Fukang. It was slightly small, but that only emphasized her slender figure.

到得五点钟吉时，一档『白蛇传』的小书结束，宾客纷纷从席棚下进入堂屋观礼。七姑奶奶由仆妇背下楼来，纳入一张太师椅中，抬到堂前；她的左首，另有一张同样的椅子，是古应春的座位。于是便有人起哄地喊道∶『新郎倌呢？新郎倌！』『新郎倌』古应春为人从人丛中推了出来，宝蓝贡缎夹袍，玄色西洋华丝葛马褂，脚踏粉底皂靴，头上一顶硬胎缎帽，帽檐正中镶一块碧玉，新剃的头；他是洋派不留胡子，翕显得年轻了。等他一坐下来，视线集中，自然而然地看到了七姑奶奶，下身百褶红裙，上身墨绿夹袄，头上戴着珠花，面如满月，脸有喜气，真正福相。再看到旁边，扶着七姑奶奶的椅背的一个中年妇人，一张瓜子脸，脂粉不施，天然丰韵，一双眼睛，既黑且亮，恍如阳光直射寒潭，只觉得深不可测，令人不敢逼视。她穿的是玄色缎袄，下面也是红裙；头上没有什么首饰，但扶着椅背的那只手上戴着一枚钻戒，不时闪出耀眼的光芒，可以想见戒指上镶的钻，至少也有蚕豆瓣那么大。

At the auspicious hour of five, the storytelling performance of The White Snake ended. Guests streamed from beneath the courtyard awning into the reception hall to watch the ceremony. A maidservant carried the Seventh Lady downstairs on her back. She was settled in a ceremonial armchair, which was then carried to the front of the hall. An identical chair on her left awaited Gu. “Where is the bridegroom? Bridegroom!” someone shouted playfully. Gu was pushed forward from the crowd. He wore a lined robe of sapphire-blue tribute satin, a black riding jacket of imported silk faille, black boots with white soles, and a stiff satin cap with a piece of green jade set centrally above its brim. His head had been freshly shaved. Following foreign fashion, he wore no beard, which made him look younger. Once he sat down, everyone’s attention naturally took in the Seventh Lady: a pleated red skirt, a dark-green lined jacket, pearl flowers in her hair, a face round as the full moon and bright with happiness—a truly fortunate appearance. Beside her, a middle-aged woman rested a hand on the chair back. Her oval face needed no powder or rouge; its grace was natural. Her dark, bright eyes resembled sunlight falling directly into a cold, deep pool: unfathomable, almost impossible to meet. She wore a black satin jacket and, like the Seventh Lady, a red skirt. There was little jewellery in her hair, but the diamond ring on the hand resting against the chair flashed dazzlingly. Its stone must have been at least the size of half a broad bean.

『那是谁？』有人悄悄在问。『听说是胡大先生的妾。』『是妾，怎么着红裙？』『又不是在她自己家里，哪个来管她？』『不！』另有一个人说∶『她就是胡家的螺蛳太太，着红裙是胡老太太特许的。』

“Who is she?” someone whispered. “I hear she is Mr Hu’s concubine.” “Then why is she wearing a red skirt?” “She is not in her own house. Who can stop her?” “No,” another man said. “That is Madam Luosi of the Hu household. The Old Madam has specially permitted her to wear red.”

那两个人还想谈下去，但视线为瑞香所吸引了。只见她低着头，但见满头珠翠，却看不清脸，不过长身玉立，皮肤雪白，已可想见是个美人。她是由小王师母扶着出来的，嬝嬝婷婷地走到红毡条前立定；古家的老王妈赞礼∶『新姑娘见老爷、太太磕头∶一叩首、二叩首、三叩首、兴！』小王师母便将瑞香扶了起来；七姑奶奶抬抬手喊一声∶『你过来！』老王妈便又高唱∶『太太赏新姑娘见面礼。』这时螺蛳太太便将一个小丝绒匣子悄悄递了给七姑奶奶，她打开匣了——也是一枚钻戒，拉起瑞香的手，将戒指套在她右手九名指上。『谢谢奶奶！』瑞香低声道谢；还要跪下去，却让螺蛳太太拉住了。

The first two men would have continued, but Ruixiang drew their eyes. Her head was lowered beneath a wealth of pearls and jade, hiding her face. Yet her tall, graceful figure and fair skin already suggested beauty. Supported by Young Mrs Wang, she approached the strip of red felt and stood poised before it. Old Nanny Wang of the Gu household called out the ritual directions: “The new lady pays her respects to Master and Mistress. First kowtow! Second kowtow! Third kowtow! Rise!” Young Mrs Wang helped Ruixiang to her feet. The Seventh Lady raised her hand. “Come here.” Old Nanny Wang announced, “Mistress presents the new lady with a welcoming gift.” Madam Luosi quietly handed the Seventh Lady a small velvet box. She opened it to reveal another diamond ring, took Ruixiang’s hand, and slipped the ring onto her right ring finger. “Thank you, Madam,” Ruixiang said softly. She was about to kneel again, but Madam Luosi stopped her.

这就算礼成了，不道奇峰突起，古应春站起身来，看着螺蛳太太说道∶『四姐，你请过来，应该让瑞香给你磕头。』『没有这个规矩，这算啥一出？』说着，便待避开，哪知七姑奶奶早就拉住了她的衣服；适时瑞香竟也走上前来，扶着她说∶『太太请坐。』小王师母与老王妈亦都上前来劝驾，螺蛳太太身不由主，只好受了瑞香的大礼。乱轰轰一阵过去，正要散开，奇峰又起，这回是宓本常，站到一张凳子上，举双手喊道∶『还要照照相、照照相。

The ceremony was complete—until Gu unexpectedly stood and turned to Madam Luosi. “Sister Luo, come here. Ruixiang ought to kowtow to you.” “There is no such custom. What are you doing?” She tried to withdraw, but the Seventh Lady had already caught her clothing. Ruixiang also stepped forward and supported her. “Please sit, Madam.” Young Mrs Wang and Old Nanny Wang joined in the urging. Unable to escape, Madam Luosi had to accept Ruixiang’s full obeisance. When the bustle subsided and everyone was about to disperse, another surprise followed. This time Mi climbed onto a stool, raised both hands, and called, “Photographs! We must have photographs!”

这一下大家都了下来，听从他的指挥，照了两张相，一张是古应春、七姑奶奶并坐，瑞香侍立在七姑奶奶身后；一张是全体合照，螺蛳太太觉得自己无可位置，悄悄地溜掉了。照相很费事，第二张镁光不亮，重新来过；到开席时，已经天黑了。女客只有一桌，开在楼上，螺蛳太太首座；七姑奶奶因为不耐久坐，行动也不便，特意命瑞香代作主人，这自然是抬举她的意思。螺蛳太太也觉得很有面子，不由得又想到了宓本常，都亏他安排，才能风风光光嫁了瑞香，了却一桩心事，成全了主婢之情。

Everyone stopped and followed his instructions. Two photographs were taken: one of Gu and the Seventh Lady seated together, with Ruixiang standing behind her; another of the entire company. Feeling there was no appropriate place for her, Madam Luosi quietly slipped away. The photography took time. The magnesium flash failed for the second picture, and the attempt had to be repeated. By the time the meal began, it was dark. The women had a single table upstairs, with Madam Luosi in the seat of honour. Unable to sit for long or move easily, the Seventh Lady specially instructed Ruixiang to act as hostess. This was plainly intended to honour her. Madam Luosi too felt that she had been shown great respect. Once more she thought of Mi. His arrangements had enabled her to send Ruixiang into her new life with dignity, settle a longstanding concern, and fulfil the affection between mistress and maid.

译者说明与学习提示（不属于小说正文）

译者说明
覆盖2799–2877，是提供底本的最后一批。2799承接前批宓关于场面不能太俭的对白。原拟七桌（男五女二）、天井还可加四桌但会占演出地方；最后女客不足一桌、男客已够五桌，是后续实际到场情况，不擅把计划桌数改成实际人数。
原拟堂会四点开、九点歇锣，三档为苏州小书、日本女魔术、东乡调；七姑奶奶改提杭州滩簧，书中并未在本批逐项详述最后所有表演。五点吉时白蛇传小书结束后行礼、拍照重来至天黑开席，全部保留。
关于女子天性嫉妒及嫡庶礼数，按小说历史语境呈现；红裙限制与螺蛳太太获胡老太太特许形成刻意对照，不能抹去。东乡调“两只奶奶抖勒抖”为成人曲辞粗俗玩笑，译为Those two breasts go jiggle-jiggle；无指定未成年人，未作安全替换。瑞香本段是非露骨的家庭仪式与心理描写。
“师母”称谓绕口令保留shimu并先在叙述中解释teacher’s wife；七姑奶奶是王仲文老师的妻子，王妻也因丈夫教英语而被叫师母。英语部分以Young Mrs Wang对应小王师母，并重复Shimu，避免全译Mrs而丢失笑点。王仲文Wang Zhongwen、老王妈Old Nanny Wang与小王师母不是同一人。
夹袄/夹袍均为lined，不误作棉袄padded；华丝葛作silk faille。源“九名指”依常用指名及148前文同样部位作右手无名指。钻石至少蚕豆瓣大译half a broad bean，未改成整颗蚕豆。拍照两张、第二张镁光失灵重拍，罗悄然离开合照，均保留。
本批结尾止于罗感念宓帮助安顿瑞香，未增写原文没有的卷终解释或后来人物结局。
阅读与表达积累
“An uninformed visitor might have thought it a birthday celebration.”用寿星图与正式婚礼图的区别表现礼制，读者需结合后文解释推断。
“Whatever… / Since… / Yet…”贯穿宾客安排、瑞香心理与罗的顾虑，可练习让步、原因与转折分别发挥的作用。
最后对宓的感谢与155揭示的私心构成戏剧性反讽：人物眼中的热心，与读者掌握的动机并不相同。
