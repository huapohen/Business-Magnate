月如近年来难得进府。原因很多，最主要的是怕见旧日伙伴，原是烧火丫头，不道“飞上枝头作凤凰”，难免遭人妒嫉，有的叫她“唐姨太”，有的叫她“唐师母”，总不如听人叫月如来得顺耳。尤其是从她出了新闻以后，她最怕听的一句话就是：“老爷这两天有没有到你那里吃饭？”这天情势所逼，只好硬着头皮去走一趟，由大厨房后门进府，旁边一间敞厅，是各房仆妇丫头到大厨房来提开水、聚会之地，这天长条桌上摆着两个大箩筐，十几个丫头用裁好的红纸在包“桂花糖”——杭州大小人家嫁娶都要讨“桂花糖”吃，白糖加上桂花，另用玫瑰、薄荷的浆汁染色，用小模子制成各种花样，每粒拇指大小，玲珑精致，又好吃、又好玩，是孩子们的恩物。胡三小姐出阁，在方裕和定制了四百斤加料的桂花糖，这夭早晨刚刚送到，找了各房丫头来帮忙。进门之处恰好有个在胡老太太那里管烛火香蜡的丫头阿菊，与月如一向交好，便往里缩了一下，拍拍长条桌说：“正好来帮忙。”月如便挨着她坐了下来，先抬眼看一看，熟识的几个都用眼色默然地打了招呼，平时顶爱讲话的两个，这天亦不开口，各人脸上，当然亦不会有什么笑容。

Yueru had seldom entered the Hu residence in recent years. There were several reasons, but chiefly she dreaded meeting her old companions. Once a kitchen drudge tending the fires, she had unexpectedly “flown up to a branch and become a phoenix,” and envy was inevitable. Some called her Mistress Tang, others Madam Tang; neither sounded as pleasant as her own name. Since her affair had become common knowledge, the question she dreaded most was, “Has the master been to your place for a meal these last two days?” Today circumstances forced her to brave a visit. She entered through the back door of the main kitchen. Beside it stood an open hall where the maids and serving women from the different households collected boiling water and gathered to talk. Today two large baskets stood on a long table, and more than a dozen maids were wrapping osmanthus sweets in pieces of red paper. In Hangzhou, people expected these sweets at weddings in households great and small. White sugar was mixed with osmanthus blossoms, tinted with rose or mint juice, and pressed into small molds of various shapes. Each piece was about the size of a thumb: delicate, delicious, and fun to play with, a delight to children. For the Third Miss’s wedding, four hundred jin of specially enriched sweets had been ordered from Fang Yuhe. They had arrived that morning, and maids from the different households had been called in to help. A’ju, who tended the lamps, candles, and incense for Old Madam Hu, happened to be sitting by the door. A good friend of Yueru’s, she shifted along and patted the table. “Just in time to help.” Yueru sat beside her and glanced around. Those she knew greeted her silently with their eyes. Even the two most talkative girls said nothing today. Naturally, there were no smiles.

见此光景，月如亦就不敢高声说话了，“三小姐的喜事，会不会改日子？”她先问她最关心的一件事。“你不看仍旧在包桂花糖？”阿菊低声答说：“今朝天朦朦亮，大太太、螺蛳太太在‘公所’交代，一切照常。”“怎么会出这种事？”月如问说：“三小姐怎么样？有没有哭？”“哭？为啥？跟三小姐啥相干？”“大喜日子，遇到这种事，心里总难过的。”“难过归难过，要做新娘子，哪里有哭的道理？不过，”阿菊说道：“笑是笑不出来的！”“你看，阿菊，”月如将声音压得极低，“要紧不要紧？”“什么要紧不要紧？”“我是说会不会……”“会不会倒下来是不是？”阿菊摇摇头，“恐怕难说。”“会倒？”月如吃惊地问：“真的？”“你不要这样子！”阿菊白了她一眼，“螺蛳太太最恨人家大惊小怪。”月如也自知失态，改用平静的声音说：“你从哪里看出来的，说不定会倒？”“人心太坏！”话中大有文章，值得打听，但是来不及开口，月如家的一个老妈子赶了来通知，唐子韶要她赶紧回家。

Yueru dared not speak loudly in such an atmosphere. “Will the Third Miss’s wedding have to be postponed?” she asked, putting her chief concern first. “Can’t you see we’re still wrapping the sweets?” A’ju answered quietly. “At first light, the First Madam and Madam Luosi told everyone in the Common Hall that everything was to go on as usual.” “How could this happen? How is the Third Miss? Has she been crying?” “Crying? What for? What has it to do with her?” “Such a thing happening just before her wedding—she must be upset.” “Being upset is one thing, but whoever heard of a bride crying? Still,” A’ju added, “she certainly can’t manage a smile.” Yueru lowered her voice almost to a whisper. “A’ju, do you think it’s serious?” “What do you mean, serious?” “I mean, could it…” “Could the whole thing collapse? Is that what you mean?” A’ju shook her head. “I’m afraid it’s hard to say.” “Collapse?” Yueru asked in alarm. “Really?” “Don’t carry on like that!” A’ju gave her a reproachful look. “Madam Luosi hates people making a fuss.” Realizing she had lost her composure, Yueru resumed a calmer tone. “What makes you think it might collapse?” “People are too wicked!” There was much behind those words, well worth probing, but before Yueru could ask, a serving woman from her own house hurried in. Tang Zishao wanted her home at once.

“那几张当票呢？”唐子韶问。月如开了首饰箱，取出一叠当票，唐子韶一张一张细看。月如虽也认得几个字，但当票上那笔“鬼画符”的草书，只字不识，看他捡出三张摆在一边，便即问说：“是些啥东西？”原来唐子韶在公济典舞弊的手法，无所不用其极，除了在满当货上动手脚以外，另外一种是看满当的日期已到，原主未赎，而当头珍贵，开单子送进府里，“十二楼”中的姨太太，或许看中了要留下来，便以“挂失”为名，另开一张当票。此外还有原主出卖，或者来路不明，譬如“扒几手”扒来，甚至小偷偷来的当票，以极低的价钱收了下来，都交给月如保管，看情形取赎。这捡出来的三张，便是预备赎取的，一张是一枚帽花，极大极纯的一块波斯祖母绿，时价值两千银子，只当了五百两；一张是一副银台面，重六百两，却当不得六百银子，因为回炉要去掉“火耗”，又说它成色不足，再扣去利息，七折八扣下来，六百两银子减掉一半，只当三百两，可是照样打这么一副，起码要一千银子。

“Where are those pawn tickets?” Tang Zishao asked. Yueru opened her jewelry box and took out a stack of them, which he examined one by one. She could recognize a few characters, but the wild cursive scrawls on pawn tickets were utterly beyond her. When he selected three and laid them aside, she asked, “What are those for?” Tang had resorted to every conceivable form of fraud at Gongji Pawnshop. Besides tampering with goods whose redemption periods had expired, he would make lists of particularly valuable unredeemed articles and send them to the Hu residence. If one of the concubines in the Twelve Chambers took a fancy to something and wanted to keep it, a new ticket would be issued on the pretext that the original had been lost. There were also tickets sold by their owners, or acquired from dubious sources—picked from pockets or stolen by thieves—which Tang bought for next to nothing and entrusted to Yueru, to be redeemed when convenient. The three he had selected were to be redeemed now. One covered a hat ornament: an exceptionally large, flawless Persian emerald worth two thousand taels at current prices, on which only five hundred had been lent. Another covered a set of silver tableware weighing six hundred taels. That did not mean a loan of six hundred taels, for a deduction had to be made for the loss in remelting; its purity was also pronounced deficient, and interest was deducted. After one reduction and another, the six hundred taels of silver had yielded a loan of only three hundred. Yet making another set to match it would cost at least a thousand taels.

第三张就更贵重了，是一副钻镯，大钻十二、小钻六十四，不算镶工，光是金刚钻就值八千两银子，只当得二千两，是从一个小毛贼那里花八两银子买来的，第二天，原主的听差气急败坏来挂失，唐子韶亲自接待，说一声：“实在很对不起，已经有人来赎走了。”拿出当票来看，原主都说“不错”，但问到是什么人来赎的？又是一声：“实在对不起，不晓得。”天下十八省的当铺，规矩是一样的，认票不认人，来人只好垂头丧气去回复主人。“这三张票子赶紧料理。”唐子韶说，“阜康存了许多公款，从钱塘、仁和两县到抚台衙门，都有权来封典当，不赎出来，白白葬送在里面。”“阜康倒了，跟公济典有啥关系？”“亏你问得出这种话！只要是胡大先生的产业都可以封。”说完，唐子韶匆匆忙忙地去了。

The third article was even more valuable: a pair of diamond bracelets set with twelve large diamonds and sixty-four small ones. Without counting the workmanship, the diamonds alone were worth eight thousand taels, but the loan had been only two thousand. Tang had bought the ticket from a petty thief for eight taels. The next day the owner’s servant had come rushing in, distraught, to report its loss. Tang had attended to him personally. “I’m terribly sorry. Someone has already redeemed the goods.” He had produced the ticket, which the owner’s side acknowledged was indeed the right one. But who had redeemed them? Again: “I’m terribly sorry. I have no idea.” Throughout the empire’s eighteen provinces, pawnshops followed the same rule: they recognized the ticket, not the person presenting it. The servant could only return dejectedly to report to his master. “Get these three tickets taken care of at once,” Tang now said. “Fukang holds a great deal of public money. Every authority from the Qiantang and Renhe county offices up to the governor’s yamen has grounds to seal the pawnshops. Unless we redeem the goods, we’ll simply lose them.” “If Fukang fails, what has that to do with Gongji Pawnshop?” “How can you ask such a question? Anything belonging to Mr Hu can be seized.” With that, Tang hurried away.

月如送他到门口，顺便看看热闹。她家住在后街，来往的人不多，但前面大街上人声嘈杂，却听得很清楚，其中隐隐有鸣锣喝道之声，凝神静听，果然不错，月如想起刚才唐子韶说过的话，不由得一惊，莫非宫府真的来封阜康钱庄与公济典了？她的猜测恰好相反，由杭州府知府吴云陪着来的藩司德馨，不是来封阜康的门，而是劝阜康开门营业。原来这天上午，螺蛳太太照谢云青的建议，特地坐轿到藩司衙门去看德藩台的宠妾。相传这座衙门是南宋权相秦桧的住宅，又说门前两座石栏围绕的大池，隐藏着藩库的水门，池中所养的大鼋，杭州人称之为“癫头鼋”，便是用来看守藩库水门的，这些传说，虽难查证，但“藩司前看癞头鼋”，是杭州城里市井中的一景，却是亘亘数十年不改。螺蛳太太每次轿子经过，看池边石栏上，或坐或倚的人群，从未有何感觉，这天却似乎觉得那些闲人指指点点，都在说她：“喏，那轿子里坐的就是胡大先生的螺蛳太太。财神跌倒，变成赤脚，螺蛳太太也要抛头露面来求人家了。”这样胡思乱想着，她心里酸酸的，突然觉得眼眶发热，赶紧拭去眼泪，强自把心定下来，自己对自己说：不要紧的！无论如何自己不可先摆出着急的样子。于是她将平日来了以后的情形回忆了一下，警惕着一切如常，不能有甚异样的态度。由于她那乘轿子格外华丽，更由于她平时出手大方，所以未进侧门以前，不待执帖家人上前通报，便有德藩台的听差迎了出来，敞开双扉，容她的轿子沿着正厅西面的雨道，在花园入口处下轿。

Yueru saw him to the door and took the opportunity to look at the commotion outside. Her house stood in a back street with little traffic, but the clamor from the main street was clearly audible. Among the voices she thought she could hear gongs and calls to clear the way. Listening carefully, she found she was right. Tang’s warning came back to her, and she started in alarm. Could officials really be coming to seal Fukang Bank and Gongji Pawnshop? The truth was precisely the reverse of her guess. Provincial Treasurer Dexin, accompanied by Wu Yun, the prefect of Hangzhou, was coming to urge Fukang to reopen, rather than to seal its doors. Earlier that morning, following Xie Yunqing’s suggestion, Madam Luosi had gone by sedan chair to the provincial treasurer’s yamen to visit his favorite concubine. The building was said to have been the residence of Qin Hui, the powerful Southern Song chief minister. According to another story, the two great pools enclosed by stone balustrades outside concealed water gates leading to the provincial treasury, guarded by the enormous soft-shelled turtles that Hangzhou people called “scabby-headed turtles.” Such legends were difficult to verify, but watching the turtles outside the treasurer’s yamen had been a familiar city pastime for decades. Normally Madam Luosi paid no attention to the people sitting or leaning against the balustrades as her sedan passed. Today she imagined them pointing and saying, “Look, that’s Mr Hu’s Madam Luosi in the chair. The God of Wealth has fallen and lost even his shoes. Now she has to show herself in public and beg for help.” These imaginings made her heart ache. Suddenly her eyes grew hot; she hastily wiped away the tears and steadied herself. It would be all right, she told herself. Whatever happened, she must not be the first to show alarm. Recalling how she usually behaved on these visits, she resolved to do everything as usual. Her sedan was unusually splendid, and she had always tipped generously. Before her card bearer could announce her at the side entrance, one of the treasurer’s attendants came out and opened both doors. The sedan passed along the covered passage west of the main hall, and she alighted at the garden entrance.

德藩台的宠妾，名叫莲珠，在家行二，她们是换帖姐妹，莲珠比螺狮太太大一岁，所以称之为二姐，莲珠唤她四妹，出来迎接时，象平时一样，彼此叫应了略作寒暄，但一进屋尚未坐定，莲珠的神情就不一样了。“四妹，”她执着螺蛳太太的手，满腹疑惑地问：“是怎么回事？一早听人说，阜康不开门，我说没有的事。刚刚我们老爷进来，我问起来才知道上海的阜康倒了，这里挤满了人，怕要出事。我们老爷只是叹气，我也着急，到底要紧不要紧？”这一番话说得螺蛳太太心里七上八下，自己觉得脸上有点发烧，但力持镇静，不过要想象平时那样有说有笑，却怎么样也办不到了。“怎么不要紧？一块金字招牌，擦亮来不容易，要弄脏它很方便。”螺蛳太太慢条斯里地说：“怪只怪我们老爷在路上，上海、杭州两不接头。我一个女人家，就抛头露面，哪个来理我？说不得只好来求藩台了。”“以我们两家的交情，说不上一个求字。”莲珠唤来一个丫头说：“你到中门上传话给阿福，看老爷会客完了，马上请他进来。”

The treasurer’s favorite concubine was named Lianzhu. Second-born in her family and a year older than Madam Luosi, she had exchanged sworn-sisterhood pledges with her. Madam Luosi called her Second Sister; Lianzhu called Madam Luosi Fourth Sister. They greeted one another with their usual affectionate names and exchanged a few pleasantries. But once they were inside, even before they had settled into their seats, Lianzhu’s expression changed. “Fourth Sister,” she said, taking Madam Luosi’s hand, “what is happening? Early this morning I heard Fukang hadn’t opened, and I said it couldn’t be true. Only when the master came in just now and I asked him did I learn that the Shanghai Fukang has failed, and that there are crowds outside the bank here and trouble may break out. He kept sighing, and I’ve been worried too. How serious is it?” Her words threw Madam Luosi into fresh confusion. Her face felt hot. She struggled to remain calm, but it was quite impossible to laugh and talk as she normally did. “How could it not be serious? A gilded signboard is hard to polish bright and very easy to soil,” she said slowly. “The trouble is that our master is still on the road, with neither Shanghai nor Hangzhou able to reach him. Even if a woman like me comes out in public, who will listen? I can only ask the treasurer for help.” “Between our two families, there’s no question of asking a favor.” Lianzhu called a maid. “Go to the inner gate and send word to A’fu: as soon as the master has finished receiving visitors, ask him to come in.”

阿福是德馨的贴身跟班，接到中门上传来的消息，便借装水烟袋之便，悄悄在德馨耳际说了一句：“姨太太请。”德馨有好几个妾，但不加区别仅称“姨太太”便是指莲珠。心想她有什么要紧事，等不及他回上房吃午饭时谈？一定是胡家的事。这样想着，便对正在会见的一个候补道说：“你老哥谈的这件案子，兄弟还不十分清楚，等我查过了再商量吧！”接着不由分说，端一端茶碗，花厅廊上的听差，便高唱一声：“送客！”将那候补道硬生生地撵走了。看“手本”，还有四客要接见，三个是候补知县。一个是现任海宁州知州，他踌躇了一回，先剔出两个手本，自语似他说：“这两位，今天没工夫了。”阿福取手本来一看，其中一个姓刘，送过很大的一个门包，便即说道：“这位刘大老爷是姨太太交代过的。”“交代什么？”“刘大老爷想讨个押运明年渣米的差使。姨太太交代。老爷一定要派。”“既然一定要派，就不必见了。”“那么，怎么样回他？”“叫他在家听信好了。”“是。”“这一位，”德馨拿起另一份手本，沉吟了一下，用快刀斩乱麻的手法，连海宁州知州的手本，一起往外一推：“说我人不舒服，都请他们明天再来。”

A’fu was Dexin’s personal valet. On receiving the message from the inner gate, he took the opportunity of filling his master’s water pipe to whisper, “Madam wishes to see you.” Dexin had several concubines, but “Madam,” without qualification, meant Lianzhu. What could be so urgent that she could not wait until he returned to the inner apartments for lunch? It must be something to do with the Hus. He turned to the expectant circuit intendant he was receiving. “My dear fellow, I’m not yet entirely familiar with this case. Let me look into it, and we can discuss it later.” Without allowing an answer, he lifted his teacup. An attendant in the reception-hall corridor immediately called, “See the gentleman out!” The expectant intendant was thus summarily dismissed. Four visiting cards remained: three expectant county magistrates and the serving magistrate of Haining Department. Dexin hesitated, then singled out two. “No time for these gentlemen today,” he said, almost to himself. A’fu took the cards and saw that one belonged to a man named Liu, who had given him a substantial gatekeeper’s tip. “Madam has given instructions about this Mr Liu.” “What instructions?” “He wants the assignment of escorting next year’s grain shipment. Madam said you must appoint him.” “If I’m bound to appoint him, there’s no need to see him.” “What shall I tell him, then?” “To wait at home for word.” “Yes, sir.” “As for this one…” Dexin picked up another card, paused, then cut through the difficulty at a stroke: he pushed that card away together with the Haining magistrate’s. “Say I’m feeling unwell. Ask them to come back tomorrow.”

说完，起身由花厅角门回到上房，径自到了莲珠那里。螺蛳太太一见急忙起身，裣袄为礼。德馨跟胡雪岩的交情很厚，私底下管他叫“胡大哥”，对螺蛳太太便叫“罗四姐”，他一开口便问：“罗四姐，雪岩什么时候回来？”“今天下半天。”“唉！”他顿一顿足说：“就差这么一天工夫。”意思是胡雪岩只要昨天到，今天的局面就不会发生。螺蛳太太不知道他能用什么办法来解消危机？但愿倾全力相助的心意是很明显的。患难之际，格外容易感受他人的好意，于是螺蛳太太再一次裣衽行礼，噙着泪光说道：“藩台这样照应我们胡家，上上下下都感激的。”“罗四姐，你别这么说，如今事情出来了，我还不知道使得上力，使不上力呢？”“有什么使得上，使不上？”莲珠接口说道：“只要你拿出力量来，总归有用的，”“我当然要拿力量出来。胡大哥的事，能尽一分力，尽一分力，罗四姐，你先请回去，我过了瘾，马上请吴知府来商量。”德馨又说：“饭后我亲自去看看，我想不开门总不是一回事。不过，事也难说，总而言之，一定要想个妥当办法出来。”有最后一句话，螺蛳太太放心了。莲珠便说：“四妹，今天你事情多，我不留你了。”说着，送客出来，到了廊上悄悄说道：“我会钉住老头子，只要他肯到阜康，到底是藩台，总能压得下去的。”

He rose, returned to the inner apartments through a side door of the reception hall, and went straight to Lianzhu’s rooms. Madam Luosi hastily stood and made a formal curtsy. Dexin was a close friend of Hu Xueyan, whom he privately called Brother Hu; to Madam Luosi he used the familiar Sister Luo. His first question was, “Sister Luo, when will Xueyan be back?” “This afternoon.” “Ah!” He stamped his foot. “Just one day too late.” He meant that if Hu had arrived yesterday, today’s crisis would never have arisen. Madam Luosi had no idea how he might resolve it, but his willingness to do everything he could was evident. In adversity, people feel kindness all the more keenly. With tears glistening in her eyes, she curtsied again. “Everyone in our household is grateful for your concern, Treasurer.” “Please don’t say that, Sister Luo. Now that this has happened, I don’t even know whether I can be of any use.” “What do you mean, whether you can be of use?” Lianzhu put in. “If you put your authority behind it, it’s bound to help.” “Of course I shall. Whatever I can do for Brother Hu, I’ll do. You go home first, Sister Luo. Once I’ve had my smoke, I’ll send for Prefect Wu and discuss it with him.” He added, “After lunch I’ll go and see for myself. Keeping the doors shut can’t be the answer. Still, it’s difficult to say. In any case, we must find a sound way to handle it.” That last sentence reassured Madam Luosi. “You have so much to do today, Fourth Sister,” Lianzhu said. “I won’t keep you.” Walking her out, she whispered in the corridor, “I’ll keep after the old man. If only he’ll go to Fukang—he is the provincial treasurer, after all—he should be able to keep things under control.”

“是的。二姐，我现在象‘没脚蟹’一样，全靠你替我作主。”螺蛳太太又放低了声音说，“上次你说我戴的珠花样子好，我叫人另外穿了一副，明后天送过来。”“不必，不必，你现在何必还为这种事操心？喔，”莲珠突然想起，“喜事呢？”“只好照常，不然外头的谣言更多了。”螺蛳太太又说：“人，势利的多，只怕有的客人不会来了。”“我当然要来的。”“当然，当然。”螺蛳太太怕她误会，急忙说道：“我们是自己人。且不说还没有倒下来，就穷得没饭吃了，二姐还是一样会来的。”“正是这话。”莲珠叮嘱，“胡大先生一回来，你们就送个信来。”“他一回来，一定首先来看藩台。”“对！哪怕晚上也不要紧。”“我晓得。”螺蛳太太又说：“我看珠花穿好了没有，穿好了叫他带来，二姐好戴。”回到家，螺蛳太太第一件要办的，就是这件事。说“叫人另外穿一副”是故意这样说的，螺蛳太太的珠花有好几副，挑一副最莹白的，另外配一只金镶玉的翠镯，立即叫人送了给莲珠。

“Yes, Second Sister. I’m like a crab without legs just now. I’m depending entirely on you to act for me.” Madam Luosi lowered her voice still further. “Last time, you admired the design of the pearl ornaments I was wearing. I’ve had another set strung. I’ll send them over tomorrow or the day after.” “No, no. Why worry about such things at a time like this? Oh!” Lianzhu suddenly remembered. “What about the wedding?” “It will have to go ahead as usual, or there’ll be even more rumors outside. People are so worldly—I’m afraid some of the guests may not come.” “I certainly shall.” “Of course, of course!” Fearful of being misunderstood, Madam Luosi hurried on. “We’re our own people. Never mind that we haven’t collapsed yet—even if we were too poor to eat, you’d still come, Second Sister.” “Exactly. Send us word as soon as Mr Hu gets back.” “The treasurer will certainly be the first person he visits.” “Yes. Even if it’s late at night, that doesn’t matter.” “I understand. I’ll see whether the pearl ornaments are ready. If they are, I’ll have him bring them for you to wear.” The first thing Madam Luosi attended to on reaching home was this gift. Her claim that she had commissioned another set was deliberate. She owned several sets of pearl ornaments already. Choosing the one with the whitest, most lustrous pearls, she added a gold-mounted jadeite bracelet and had both sent to Lianzhu at once.

这份礼真是送在刀口上，原来德馨在旗员中虽有能吏之称，但出身纨垮，最好声色，听说胡家办喜事，来了两个“水路班子”——通都大邑的戏班，都是男角，坤角另成一班，称为“髦儿戏”，惟有“水路班子”男女合演，其中有一班叫“福和”，当家的小旦叫灵芝草，色艺双全，德馨听幕友谈过这个坤伶，久思一见，如今到了杭州，岂肯错过机会，已派亲信家人去找班主，看哪一天能把灵芝草接了来，听她清唱。也就是螺蛳太太辞去不久，德馨正在抽鸦片过痛时，亲信家人来回复，福和班主，听说藩台“传差”，不敢怠慢，这天下午就会把灵芝草送来，德馨非常高兴，变更计划，对于处理阜康挤兑这件事，另外作了安排。就这时莲珠到了签押房，她是收到了螺蛳太太的一份重礼对阜康的事格外关切，特意来探问究竟。德馨答说：“我已经派人去请吴知府了，等他来了，我会切切实实关照他。”

The gift arrived at precisely the critical moment. Although Dexin had a reputation as an able official among the bannermen, he had been born to privilege and was passionately fond of women and entertainment. He had heard that two traveling waterway troupes had come for the Hu wedding. In the great cities, opera companies consisted entirely of male performers; women formed separate companies, known as mao’er troupes. Only the waterway troupes mixed men and women. One of these companies, Fuhe, had a leading actress named Lingzhicao, who specialized in young female roles and was celebrated for both her beauty and her art. Dexin had heard his advisers talk of her and had long wanted to see her. Now that she was in Hangzhou, how could he miss the chance? He had sent a trusted retainer to ask the manager when she could come and sing for him privately. Shortly after Madam Luosi left, while he was satisfying his craving for opium, the retainer returned. On hearing that the treasurer had summoned her for official duty, the Fuhe manager had not dared delay: Lingzhicao would be brought that very afternoon. Delighted, Dexin changed his plans and made other arrangements for dealing with the run on Fukang. Just then Lianzhu came into his private office. Madam Luosi’s substantial gift had made her particularly concerned about the bank, and she had come specifically to find out what was happening. “I’ve already sent for Prefect Wu,” Dexin told her. “When he comes, I’ll give him very thorough instructions.”

“关照他什么？”“关照他亲自去弹压。”“那么，”莲珠问道：“你呢？你不去了？”“有吴知府一个人就行。”“你有把握，一定能料理得下来？”“这种事谁有把握。”德馨答说：“就是我也没有。”“你是因为没有把握才不去的？”“不是。”“是为什么？”“我懒得动。”“老头子，你叫人寒心！胡雪岩是你的朋友，人家有了急难，弄得不好会倾家荡产，你竟说懒得动，连去看一看都不肯。这叫什么朋友？莫非你忘记了，放藩台之前，皇太后召见，如果不是胡雪岩借你一万银子，你两手空空，到了京里，人家会敷衍你，买你的帐？”莲珠停了一下，直截了当地说：“你如果觉得阜康的事不要紧，有吴知府去了就能料理得下来，你可以躲懒，不然，你就得亲自去一趟，那样，就阜康倒了，你做朋友的力量尽到了，胡雪岩也不会怪你。你想呢？”

“What instructions?” “To go in person and keep order.” “And you? Aren’t you going?” “Prefect Wu will be enough on his own.” “Are you certain he can handle it?” “Who can be certain about something like this? Even I can’t.” “Is that why you aren’t going?” “No.” “Why, then?” “I can’t be bothered to move.” “Old man, you make my heart turn cold! Hu Xueyan is your friend. He’s in desperate trouble and may lose everything he owns, yet you can’t be bothered even to go and see him. What sort of friendship is that? Have you forgotten your audience with the empress dowager before you were appointed provincial treasurer? If Hu hadn’t lent you ten thousand taels, would anyone in the capital have paid you any attention or shown you consideration when you arrived empty-handed?” Lianzhu paused, then spoke bluntly. “If you think Fukang’s difficulties are minor and Prefect Wu can settle them alone, you may spare yourself the effort. Otherwise, you must go yourself. Then, even if Fukang fails, you’ll have done your duty as a friend, and Hu Xueyan won’t blame you. What do you say?”

德馨正待答话，只听门帘作响，回头看时，阿福兴冲冲奔了进来，脸上挂着兴奋的笑容，一见莲珠在立即缩住脚，脸上的笑容也消失了。“什么事？”莲珠骂道：“冒冒失失，鬼头鬼脑，一点规矩都不懂！”阿福不作声，只不住偷看着德馨，德馨却又不住向他使眼色。这种鬼鬼祟祟的模样，落在莲珠眼中，不由得疑云大起，“阿福！”她大声喝道：“什么事？快说！”“是，”阿福赔笑说道：“没有什么事。”“你还不说实话！”莲珠向打烟的丫头说道：“找张总管来！看我叫人打断他的两条狗腿。”藩台衙门的下人，背后都管莲珠叫“泼辣货”，阿福识得厉害，不觉双膝一软，跪倒在地，“姨太太饶了我吧。”他说：“下回不敢了。”“什么下回不敢，这回还没有了呢！说！说了实话我饶你。”阿福踌躇了一会，心想连老爷都怕姨太太，就说了实话，也不算出卖老爷，便即答说：“我来回老爷一件事。”“什么事！”此时德馨连连假咳示意，莲珠冷笑着坐了下来，向阿福说道：“ 说了实话没你的事，有一个字的假话，看我不打你，你以后就别叫我姨太太。”说到这样重的话，阿福把脸都吓黄了，哭丧着脸说：“我是来回老爷，福和班掌班来通知，马上把灵芝草送来。”

Dexin was about to answer when the door curtain rustled. Turning, he saw A’fu hurry in, beaming with excitement. The moment the valet saw Lianzhu, he stopped short and his smile vanished. “What is it?” she snapped. “Barging in, looking so shifty—you haven’t the least idea how to behave!” A’fu said nothing, merely stealing glances at Dexin, who kept signaling with his eyes. Their furtiveness aroused Lianzhu’s suspicions. “A’fu!” she shouted. “What is it? Speak!” “Yes, madam.” He forced a smile. “Nothing at all.” “Still refusing to tell the truth?” She turned to the maid preparing the opium. “Fetch Steward Zhang! I’ll have both his wretched legs broken.” Behind her back, the yamen servants called Lianzhu “the firebrand.” A’fu knew what she could do. His knees buckled and he dropped to the floor. “Please spare me, madam. I won’t do it again.” “Again? We haven’t finished with this time! Speak! Tell the truth and I’ll let you off.” A’fu hesitated. Even the master was afraid of her; surely telling the truth could hardly count as betraying him. “I came to report something to the master.” “What?” Dexin was now giving a succession of warning coughs. Lianzhu sat down with a cold smile. “Tell the truth and nothing will happen to you. But one false word—if I don’t have you beaten, you need never call me Madam again.” At this solemn threat, A’fu turned pale. Looking close to tears, he answered, “I came to tell the master that the Fuhe troupe’s manager has sent word. They’re bringing Lingzhicao over at once.”

“喔，灵芝草，男的还是女的？”“女的。”“好。我知道了。你走吧！”阿福磕一个头站起身来，德馨把他叫住了，“别走！”他说：“你通知福和班，说我公事忙，没有工夫听灵芝草清唱，过几天再说。”“是！”阿福吐一吐舌头，悄悄退了出去。“老头子……”“你别罗嗦了！”德馨打断她的话说：“我过足了瘾就走，还不行吗？”“我另外还有话。”莲珠命打烟的丫头退出去：“我替老爷打烟。”这是德馨的享受，因为莲珠打的烟，“黄、高、松”三字俱全，抽一筒长一回精神。但自她将这一手绝技传授了丫头，便不再伺候这个差使，而他人打的烟总不如莲珠来得妙，因此，她现在自告奋勇，多少已弥补了不能一聆灵芝草清唱之憾。莲珠暂时不作声，全神贯注打好了一筒烟，装上烟枪，抽腋下手绢，抹一抹烟枪上的象牙嘴，送到德馨口中，对准了火，拿烟签子替他拨火。德馨吞云吐雾，一口气抽完，拿起小茶壶便喝，茶烫得常人不能上口，但他已经烫惯了，舌头乱卷了一阵，喝了几口，然后拈一粒松子糖放入口中，悠闲地说道：“你有话说吧！”

“Oh? Lingzhicao? A man or a woman?” “A woman.” “Very well. I understand. You may go.” A’fu kowtowed and rose, but Dexin stopped him. “Wait! Tell the Fuhe troupe I’m busy with official business and have no time to hear Lingzhicao sing. We’ll discuss it in a few days.” “Yes, sir!” A’fu stuck out his tongue and slipped away. “Old man…” “Stop nagging!” Dexin interrupted. “I’ll go as soon as I’ve had enough to smoke. Will that do?” “There’s something else I want to say.” Lianzhu dismissed the maid. “I’ll prepare the master’s pipe myself.” This was a treat for Dexin. Lianzhu’s opium fulfilled all three desirable qualities—golden, tall, and loose—and each pipe seemed to give him fresh energy. Since teaching the maids her special skill, she had ceased performing this service herself, but nobody else did it so well. Her offer now went some way toward compensating him for missing Lingzhicao’s singing. Without speaking, she concentrated on preparing a pipeful, fitted it to the pipe, drew the handkerchief from beneath her arm, and wiped the ivory mouthpiece. She placed it between Dexin’s lips, held it to the flame, and tended the heat with her opium needle. Dexin drew in and exhaled clouds of smoke, finishing the pipe in one continuous effort. Then he picked up a small teapot and drank. The tea was too hot for an ordinary person to touch, but he was accustomed to it; after rolling his tongue about, he took several mouthfuls, popped a pine-nut sweet into his mouth, and said at leisure, “Now, what have you to say?”

“我是在想，”莲珠一面打烟一面说：“胡雪岩倒下来，你也不得了！你倒想，公款有多少存在那里？““这我不怕，可以封他的典。”“私人的款子呢？”莲珠问说：“莫非你也封他的典？就算能封，人家问起来。你怎么说？”“是啊！”德馨吸着气说：“这话倒很难说。”“就算不难说，你还要想想托你的人，愿意不愿意你说破。象崇侍郎大少爷的那五万银子，当初托你转存阜康的时候，千叮万嘱，不能让人知道。你这一说，崇侍郎不要恨你？““这……这……”德馨皱着眉说：“当初我原不想管的，崇侍郎是假道学，做事不近人情，替他办事吃力不讨好，只为彼此同旗世交，他家老大，对我一向很孝敬，我才管了这桩事。我要一说破，坏了崇侍郎那块清廉的招牌，他恨我一辈子。”“也不光是崇侍郎，还有孙都老爷的太太，她那两万银子是私房钱，孙都老爷也是额角头上刻了‘清廉’两个字的，如果大家晓得孙太太有这笔存款，不明白是她娘家带来，压箱底的私房钱，只说是孙都老爷‘卖参’的肮脏钱。那一来孙都老爷拿他太太休回娘家，那说在哪里的。老头子啊老头子，你常说‘宁拆八座庙，不破一门婚’，那一来，你的孽可作得大了！”

“I was thinking,” Lianzhu said as she prepared another pipe, “if Hu Xueyan collapses, you’ll be in terrible trouble too. Just think how much public money is deposited there.” “That doesn’t worry me. I can seal his pawnshops.” “And private money? Will you seal his pawnshops for that too? Even if you can, what will you say when people ask?” “Yes…” Dexin drew in his breath. “That would be difficult to explain.” “Even if it weren’t, you must consider whether the people who entrusted the money to you want you to reveal it. Take the fifty thousand taels belonging to Vice-Minister Chong’s eldest son. When he asked you to deposit it with Fukang for him, he insisted again and again that nobody must know. Wouldn’t Vice-Minister Chong hate you if you told?” “Well…well…” Dexin frowned. “I didn’t want to have anything to do with it at first. Chong is a hypocritical moralist, unreasonable in everything he does. Working for him is thankless labor. But we belong to the same Banner, our families are old friends, and his eldest son has always treated me very respectfully, so I agreed. If I expose the business and ruin Chong’s reputation for incorruptibility, he’ll hate me for the rest of his life.” “And it isn’t only Chong. There’s Censor Sun’s wife. Her twenty thousand taels are her own private savings. Sun might as well have ‘incorruptible’ engraved on his forehead. If people find out his wife has that deposit, they won’t know it came from her own family, the reserve she brought with her in marriage. They’ll say it’s dirty money he made by selling his power of impeachment. If he then repudiates her and sends her back to her family, how could such a wrong ever be put right? Old man, you’re always saying you’d rather demolish eight temples than break up one marriage. What a terrible sin you’d have committed!”

叽哩呱啦一大篇话，说得德馨汗流浃背，连烟都顾不得抽了，坐起身来，要脱丝绵袄。“脱不得，要伤风。”莲珠说道：“你也别急，等我慢慢儿说给你听。”“好、好！我真的要请教你这位女诸葛了！”“你先抽了这筒烟再谈。”等德馨将这筒烟抽完，莲珠已经盘算好了，但开出口来，却是谈不相干的事。“老头了，你听了一辈子的戏，我倒请问，戏班子的规矩，你懂不懂？”“你问这个干什么？”“你甭管，你只告诉我懂不懂？”“当然懂。”“好，那么我再请问：一个戏班子是邀来的，不管它是出堂会也好，上园子也好，本主儿那里还没有唱过，角儿就不能在别处漏一漏他的玩艺。有这个规矩没有？”“有。”德馨答说：“不过这个规矩用不上。如今我是不想再听灵芝草，如果想听，叫她来是‘当差’，戏班子的规矩，难道还能拘束官府吗？”“不错，拘束不着。可是，老头子，你得想想，俗语说的‘打狗看主人面’，人家三小姐出阁，找福和班来唱戏，贺客还没有尝鲜，你倒先叫人家来唱过了，你不是动用官府力量，扫了胡家的面子？”

This torrent of words left Dexin drenched in sweat. Forgetting even his opium, he sat up and began to take off his silk-wadded jacket. “Don’t—you’ll catch cold,” Lianzhu said. “And don’t panic. Let me explain things properly.” “Yes, yes! I really must seek the advice of my female Zhuge Liang.” “Smoke this pipe first.” By the time he had finished it, Lianzhu had worked out her plan. Yet she began with something apparently unrelated. “Old man, you’ve listened to opera all your life. Tell me, do you understand the rules of a troupe?” “Why ask me that?” “Never mind why. Just tell me whether you do.” “Of course I do.” “Then let me ask you this. When a troupe is invited somewhere, whether to perform at a private celebration or in a theater, its actors mustn’t display their art elsewhere before performing for the original host. Is that the rule?” “Yes. But it doesn’t apply here. I no longer want to hear Lingzhicao; if I did, summoning her would be calling her to official duty. Can theatrical rules bind the authorities?” “No, they can’t. But remember the saying: before beating a dog, consider its master. The Hus brought Fuhe here for their Third Miss’s wedding. Their guests haven’t had the first taste, yet you want the actors to perform for you beforehand. Isn’t that using official power to make the Hu family lose face?”

莲珠虽是天津侯家浚的青楼出身，但剖析事理，着实精到，德馨不能不服，当下说道：“好在事情已经过去了，不必再提。”“不必再提的事，我何必提。我这段话不是废话，你还听不明白，足见得我说对了。”“咦！怪了，什么地方我没有听明白？”“其中有个道理，你还不明白。我说这段话的意思是，你不但要顾胡雪岩的交情，眼前你还不能让胡雪岩不痛快。你得知道，他真的要倒了，就得酌量酌量为人的情分，他要害人，害那不顾交情，得罪了他的人，如是平常交情厚的人，他反正是个不了之局，何苦‘放着河水不洗船’？你要懂这个道理，就不在了我那篇废话了。”话中有话，意味很深，德馨沉吟了好一会说：“我真的没有想到。想想你的话是不错，我犯不上得罪他，否则‘临死拉上一个垫背的’，我吃不了，兜着走，太划不来了。来，来，你躺下来，我烧一筒烟请你抽。”“得了！我是抽着玩儿的，根本没有痛，你别害我了。”莲珠躺下来，隔着烟盘说道：“阜康你得尽力维持住了，等胡雪岩回来，你跟他好好谈一谈，我想他也不会太瞒你。等摸清了他的底，再看情形，能救则救，不能救，你把你经手的款子抽出来，胡雪岩一定照办。那一来，你不是干干净净，什么关系都没有了。”“妙啊妙！这一着太高了。”于是两人并头密语，只见莲珠拿着烟签子不断比划着，德馨不断点头，偶尔也开一两句口，想来是有不明白之处，要请教“女诸葛”。

Although Lianzhu had come from a brothel in Houjiajun, Tianjin, her analysis was remarkably shrewd, and Dexin could not help conceding her point. “Fortunately, the matter is over. There’s no need to bring it up again.” “Why would I bring it up if there were no need? I’m not talking nonsense. The fact that you still don’t understand proves my point.” “This is strange! What haven’t I understood?” “The principle behind it. I mean that you must not only honor your friendship with Hu Xueyan: at this moment, you must also avoid upsetting him. If he really is going under, he’ll have to weigh what he owes to people. If anyone is to suffer, it will be those who disregarded their friendship and offended him. For his close friends, on the other hand—since he can’t settle the whole affair anyway, why leave the river water unused when he could wash a boat with it? If you understood that, you wouldn’t think all I’ve said was idle talk.” There was a great deal beneath her words. Dexin pondered for some time. “I truly hadn’t thought of that. You’re right. I’ve nothing to gain by offending him. If a dying man drags someone down into the grave with him, I’d be in more trouble than I could bear. It wouldn’t be worth it. Come, lie down. Let me prepare a pipe for you.” “Enough! I only smoke for amusement. I have no craving, so don’t ruin me.” Lianzhu lay down across the smoking tray from him. “Do everything you can to keep Fukang going. When Hu returns, have a proper talk with him. I don’t think he’ll conceal too much from you. Once you know how matters really stand, judge what to do. Save him if you can; if you can’t, withdraw all the funds you handled. He’ll certainly oblige. Then you’ll be entirely clear, with nothing tying you to the affair.” “Splendid! A brilliant move!” They put their heads together and whispered. Lianzhu gestured continually with the opium needle, while Dexin nodded and occasionally spoke a few words, presumably asking his female Zhuge Liang to clarify some point.

阿福又来了，这回是按规矩先咳嗽一声，方始揭帘入内，远远地说道：“回老爷的话，杭州府吴大人来了。”“喔，请在花厅坐，我马上出来。”“不！”莲珠立即纠正，“你说老爷在换衣服，请吴大人稍等一等。”“是。”阿福心想换衣服当然是要出门，但不知是便衣还是官服，便衣只需“传轿”，官服就还要预备“导子”，当即问道：“老爷出门，要不要传导子？”“要。”阿福答应着，自去安排。莲珠便在签押房内亲手伺候德馨换官服，灰鼠出风的袍子，外罩补褂，一串奇南香的朝珠是胡雪岩送的，价值三千银子，德馨颇为爱惜，当即说道：“这串朝珠就不必挂出去了。”他不知道这是莲珠特意安排的，为了让他记得胡雪岩的好处：这层用意当然不宜说破，她只说：“香喷喷，到处受欢迎倒不好？而且人堆里，哪怕交冬了，也有汗气，正用得着奇南香。”“言之有理。”“来，升冠！”莲珠捧着一顶貂檐暖帽，等德馨将头低了下来，她替他将暖帽戴了上去，在帽檐上弹了一下，说道：“弹冠之庆。”接着，莲珠从丫头手里接过一柄腰圆形的手镜，退后两步，将镜子举了起来，德馨照着将帽子扶正，口中说道：“不知道什么时候才能换顶戴？”藩司三品蓝顶子，换顶戴当然是换红顶子，德馨的意思是想升巡抚，莲珠便即答说：“只要左大人赏识你，换顶戴也快得很。”

A’fu returned. This time, observing proper etiquette, he coughed before lifting the curtain and announced from a distance, “Prefect Wu of Hangzhou is here, sir.” “Ah. Ask him to sit in the reception hall. I’ll be out at once.” “No!” Lianzhu corrected him immediately. “Say the master is changing and ask Prefect Wu to wait a moment.” “Yes, madam.” Changing clothes meant going out, A’fu thought, but would it be in informal dress or official robes? The former required only a sedan; the latter required a ceremonial escort as well. “Will you need the escort called out, sir?” “Yes.” A’fu went to make the arrangements. In the private office, Lianzhu helped Dexin into his official clothes: a robe with gray squirrel fur showing at its edges, and an outer coat bearing his rank badge. The court beads of fragrant qinan wood, worth three thousand taels, had been a gift from Hu Xueyan. Dexin treasured them. “There’s no need to wear these beads outside,” he said. He did not realize Lianzhu had laid them out deliberately to remind him of Hu’s kindness. Naturally she could not explain that. “Isn’t it pleasant to smell so sweet and be welcome everywhere? Besides, crowds smell of sweat even in winter. That’s just where qinan fragrance comes in useful.” “Very reasonable.” “Come—let me raise your cap!” Holding a sable-brimmed winter hat, she waited for him to bow his head, then put it on him. She flicked the brim. “Dusting off the cap to celebrate promotion!” Taking an oval hand mirror from a maid, she stepped back and held it up. Dexin straightened his hat in the glass. “I wonder when I’ll be able to change the finial.” A provincial treasurer wore the blue finial of the third rank; changing it meant changing to red. He was thinking of promotion to governor. Lianzhu answered, “Once Lord Zuo appreciates you, you’ll soon have a new finial.”
