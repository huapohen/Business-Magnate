第四章 夜访藩司｜CHAPTER FOUR: A NIGHT VISIT TO THE PROVINCIAL TREASURER

胡雪岩船到望仙桥，恰正是周少棠舌战黄八麻子，在大开玩笑的时候，螺蛳太太午前便派了亲信，沿运河往北迎了上去，在一处关卡上静候胡雪岩船到，遇船报告消息。这个亲信便是乌先生。他在胡家的身分很特殊，即非“师爷”，更非“管事”，但受胡雪岸或螺蛳太太的委托，常有临时的差使。这个人当螺蛳太太与胡雪岩之间的“密使”，自然是最适当的人选。“大先生，”，他说：“起暴风了。”不说起风波，却说“起暴风”，胡雪岩的心一沉，但表面不露声色，只说：“你特为赶了来，当然出事了。什么事？慢慢说。”“你在路上，莫非没有听到上海的消息？”等乌先生将由谢云青转到螺蛳太太手里的电报，拿了出来，胡雪岩一看色变，不过他矫情镇物的功夫过人，立即恢复常态，只问：“杭州城里都晓得了？”“当然。”“这样说，杭州，亦会挤兑？”“罗四姐特为要我来，就是谈这件事……”乌先生遂将谢云青深夜报信，决定卑康暂停营业，以及螺蛳太太亲访德馨求援，德馨已答应设法维持的经过，细说了一遍。

When Hu Xueyan’s boat reached Wangxian Bridge, Zhou Shaotang was in the midst of his verbal battle with Huang Bamazi, making jokes at his opponent’s expense. Before noon, Madam Luosi had sent a trusted man north along the canal to wait at a checkpoint and report the news as soon as Hu’s boat arrived. That man was Mr Wu. His position in the Hu household was unusual: he was neither a secretary nor a steward, but Hu or Madam Luosi often entrusted him with particular errands. No one could have been better suited to serve as their confidential messenger. “Sir,” he said, “a violent storm has broken.” He had said a violent storm, not merely a disturbance. Hu’s heart sank, but his face betrayed nothing. “Since you have come all this way, something must have happened. Tell me what it is. Take your time.” “Have you heard nothing of the news from Shanghai on your journey?” Mr Wu produced the telegram that Xie Yunqing had passed to Madam Luosi. Hu changed colour as he read it, but his ability to command his expression and steady others was exceptional. He recovered at once and asked only, “Does everyone in Hangzhou know?” “Of course.” “Then there will be a run here too?” “That is exactly why Sister Luo sent me…” Mr Wu gave a full account of Xie’s visit late at night, the decision to suspend business at Fukang, Madam Luosi’s personal appeal to Dexin, and the treasurer’s promise to do what he could to keep matters under control.

胡雪岩静静听完，第一句话便问：“老太太晓得不晓得？”“当然是瞒牢的。”“好！”胡雪岩放心了，“事情已经出来了，着急也没有用。顶要紧的是，自己不要乱。乌先生，喜事照常办，不过，我恐怕没有工夫来多管，请你多帮一帮罗四姐。”“我晓得。”乌先生突然想起：“罗四姐说，大先生最好不要在望仙桥上岸。”胡雪岩上船下船，一向在介乎元宝街与清河坊之间的望仙桥，螺蛳太太怕惹人注目，所以有此劝告。但胡雪岩的想法不同。“既然一切照常，我当然还是在望仙桥上岸。”胡雪岩又问：“罗四姐原来要我在啥地方上岸？”“万安桥。轿子等在那里。”乌先生答说：“这样子，我在万安桥上岸，关照轿子仍旧到望仙桥去接。”

Hu listened quietly to the end. His first question was, “Does the old lady know?” “Naturally, it has been kept from her.” “Good!” Hu felt relieved. “What has happened has happened. Worrying is no use. Above all, we must not lose our heads. Mr Wu, the wedding is to go ahead as usual, but I am afraid I shall have little time to attend to it. Please give Sister Luo all the help you can.” “I understand.” Mr Wu suddenly remembered something. “Sister Luo says it would be better if you did not go ashore at Wangxian Bridge.” Hu invariably boarded or left his boat there, between Yuanbao Street and Qinghefang. Madam Luosi had made the suggestion because she feared attracting attention, but he thought differently. “If everything is to proceed as usual, I must go ashore at Wangxian Bridge as usual. Where did she intend me to land?” “At Wan’an Bridge. Your sedan chair is waiting there,” Mr Wu replied. “In that case, I shall get off there and tell the bearers to go on to Wangxian Bridge to meet you.”

胡雪岩的一乘绿呢大轿，华丽是出了名的，抬到望仙桥，虽然已经暮色四合，但一停下来，自有人注目。加以乌先生了解胡雪岩的用意，关照来接轿的家人，照旧摆出排场，身穿簇新棉“号褂子”的护勇，码头上一站，点起官衔灯笼，顿时吸引了一大批看热闹的行人。见此光景，胡雪岩改了主意。往时一回杭州，都是先回家看娘，这一次怕老娘万一得知沪杭两处钱庄挤兑，急出病来，更加不放心。但看到这么多人在注视他的行踪，心里不免设身处地想一想，如果自己是阜康的客户，又会作何想法？只要一抛开自己，胡雪岩第一个念头便是：不能先回家！多少人的血汗钱托付给卓康，如今有不保之势，而阜康的老板居然好整以暇地光顾自己家里，不顾别人死活，这口气是咽不下的。因此船一靠岸，他先就询问：“云青来了没有？”谢云青何能不来？不过他是故意躲在暗处，此时闪出来疾趋上前，口中叫一声：“大先生！”“好，好！云青，你来了！不要紧，不要紧，阜康仍旧是金字招牌。”他特意提高了声音说，“我先到店里。”

Hu’s large green-cloth sedan chair was famous for its splendour. Though dusk had fallen by the time it reached Wangxian Bridge, its arrival inevitably drew attention. Understanding Hu’s purpose, Mr Wu told the household men to make their usual display. Guards in brand-new padded livery took their places on the landing and lit the lanterns bearing his official titles. A large crowd of passers-by soon gathered to watch. At this sight, Hu changed his mind. Whenever he returned to Hangzhou, he normally went home first to see his mother. Now he was all the more anxious to do so, fearing that news of the runs on the Shanghai and Hangzhou banks might have reached her and made her ill. But with so many people watching his movements, he could not help putting himself in their place. What would he think if he were a Fukang customer? The moment he set himself aside, his first thought was that he must not go home first. So many people had entrusted their hard-earned money to Fukang. Now it was in danger, yet the owner was calmly attending to his own household, regardless of what happened to anyone else. Who could swallow that? As soon as the boat touched the bank, he asked, “Has Yunqing come?” How could Xie have stayed away? He had deliberately kept in the shadows, but now stepped out and hurried forward. “Sir!” “Good, good! You are here, Yunqing. Nothing to worry about! Fukang still has its golden reputation.” Raising his voice deliberately, Hu added, “I shall go to the bank first.”

店里便是阜康。轿子一到，正好店里开饭，胡雪岩特为去看一看饭桌，这种情形平时亦曾有过，但在这种时候，他竟有这种闲情逸致，就不能不令人惊异了。“天气冷了！”胡雪岩问谢云青说：“该用火锅了。”“年常旧规，要冬至才用火锅。”谢云青说：“今年冬至迟。”“以后规矩改一改。照外国人的办法，冬天到寒暑表多少度，吃火锅，夏天，则多少度吃西瓜。云青，你记牢。”这是稳定“军心”的办法，表示阜康倒不下来，还会一年一年开下去。谢云青当然懂得这个奥妙，一叠连声地答应着，交代“饭司务”从第二天起多领一份预备火锅的菜钱。“阜康的饭碗敲不破的！”有人这样在说。

When his sedan chair reached Fukang, the staff were just sitting down to their meal. Hu made a point of inspecting the dinner table. He had done so before, but that he should have leisure for such things at a moment like this was astonishing. “The weather has turned cold,” he said to Xie. “You ought to be having hotpot.” “Our standing rule is to start at the winter solstice. It falls late this year.” “Change the rule from now on. Follow the foreigners’ practice: in winter, serve hotpot when the thermometer falls to a certain temperature; in summer, serve watermelon when it rises to a certain temperature. Remember that, Yunqing.” This was his way of steadying his troops. It told them that Fukang would not fall, but would carry on year after year. Xie understood perfectly. Agreeing at once, he told the cook to draw an extra allowance for hotpot ingredients from the following day. “No one is going to smash our rice bowls at Fukang!” someone said.

在听谢云青的细说经过时，胡雪岩一阵阵胃冷中，越觉得侥幸，越感到惭愧。事业不是他一人能创得起来的，所以出现今天这种局面，当然也不是他一个人的过失，但胡雪岩虽一想起宓本常，就恨不得一口唾沫当面吐在他脸上，但是，这种念头一起即消，他告诉自己，不必怨任何人，连自己都不必怨，最好忘记掉自己是阜康的东家，当自己是胡雪岩的“总管”，颇雪岩已经“不能问事”，委托他全权来处理这一场灾难。他只有尽力将得失之心丢开，心思才能比较集中，当时紧皱双眉，闭上眼睛，通前彻后细想了以后说：“面子就是招牌，面子保得住，招牌就可以不倒，这是一句总诀。云青，你记牢！”“是，我懂。”

As Xie described what had happened, chills ran through Hu. He felt increasingly fortunate, and increasingly ashamed. He had not built the business single-handed, and its present predicament could not be his fault alone. Whenever he thought of Mi Benchang, he wanted to spit in the man’s face. Yet the thought vanished almost as soon as it arose. He told himself not to blame anyone, not even himself. Best of all would be to forget that he owned Fukang and imagine himself as Hu Xueyan’s chief steward, entrusted with full authority to handle this disaster because Hu himself could no longer attend to his affairs. Only by setting his personal hopes and fears aside could he concentrate. He closed his eyes, his brows tightly knitted, and thought the matter through from beginning to end. Then he said, “Our standing is our reputation. Preserve that standing, and the name need not fall. That is the key to it all. Remember it, Yunqing.” “Yes. I understand.”

“你跟螺蛳太太商量定规，今天早晨不开门，这一点对不对，我们不必再谈。不过，你要晓得，拆烂污的事情做不得。”“我不是想拆烂污……”“我晓得。”胡雪岩摇摇手阻止他说：“你不必分辩，因为我不是说你。不过，你同螺蛳太太有个想法大错特错，你刚才同我说，万一撑不住，手里还有几十万款子，做将来翻身的本钱。不对，抱了这种想法，就输定了，永远翻不得身。云青，你要晓得，我好象推牌九，一直推得是‘长庄’，注码不管多少都要，你输得起，我赢得进，现在手风不顺，忽然说是改推‘铲庄’，尽多少铜钱赌，自己留起多少，当下次的赌本，云青，没有下次了，赌场里从此进不去了！“谢云青吸了口冷气，然后紧闭着嘴，无从赞一词。

“You and Madam Luosi decided not to open this morning. There is no need to discuss now whether that was right or wrong. But you must understand: we cannot simply shirk our responsibilities and leave a mess behind.” “I did not mean to—” “I know.” Hu raised a hand to stop him. “You need not defend yourself. I am not accusing you. But you and Madam Luosi have one idea that is utterly mistaken. You told me just now that, if we could not hold out, we would at least have several hundred thousand taels left as capital for a fresh start. Wrong! Think that way, and we are certain to lose. We shall never recover. Yunqing, imagine me keeping the bank at pai gow. I have always played with an unlimited bank: whatever the stake, I take it. If you can afford to lose it, I can accept the winnings. Now my luck turns against me, and I suddenly announce that I am limiting the bank: I shall risk only so much and keep the rest for another game. Yunqing, there will be no other game! I shall never be allowed into the gambling house again.” Xie drew a sharp breath, then pressed his lips together. He could not add a word.

“我是一双空手起来的，到头来仍旧一双空手，不输啥！不但不输，吃过、用过、阔过，都是赚头。只要我不死，你看我照样一双空手再翻起来。”“大先生这样气概，从古到今也没有几个人有。不过，”谢云青迟疑了一下，终于说了出来：“做生意到底不是推牌九。”“做生意虽不是推牌九，道理是一样的，‘赌奸赌诈不赌赖’。不卸排门做生意，不讲信用就是赖！”“大先生这么说，明天照常。”“当然照常！”胡雪岩说：“你今天要做一件事，拿存户的帐，好好看一看，有几个户头要连夜去打招呼。”“好。我马上动手。”“对。不过招呼有个打法，第一，一向初五结息，现在提早先把利息结出来，送银票上门。”“是。”“第二，你要告诉人家年关到了，或者要提款，要多少，请人家交代下来好预备。”“嗯、嗯、嗯。”谢云青心领神会地答应着。

“I began with empty hands. If I end with empty hands, what have I lost? Nothing! Everything I have eaten, enjoyed and spent in grand style has been a gain. So long as I live, you will see me rise again with nothing.” “Few men in any age have had your spirit, sir. But…” Xie hesitated, then finally said it. “Business is not quite the same as keeping the bank at pai gow.” “It may not be, but the principle is the same: you may gamble with cunning and deception, but never welsh on a bet. Keeping the shutters up and refusing to honour your word is welshing!” “Then, as you say, we open as usual tomorrow.” “Of course! There is one thing you must do tonight. Go carefully through the depositors’ accounts. There are several customers we must approach before the night is out.” “Very well. I shall begin at once.” “Good. But there is a way of approaching them. First, interest is normally settled on the fifth. Settle it early this time and send notes for the amount to their homes.” “Yes.” “Second, tell them the year’s end is approaching. Perhaps they will need to withdraw money. Ask them to let us know how much, so that we can have it ready.” Xie nodded repeatedly, understanding exactly what Hu meant.

能将大户稳定下来，零星散户，力能应付，无足为忧。胡雪岩交代清楚了，方始转回元宝街，虽已入夜，一条街上依旧停满了轿马，门灯高悬，家人排班，雁行而立，仿佛一切如常，但平时那种喧哗热闹的气氛，却突然消失了。轿子直接抬到花园门口，下轿一看，胡太太与螺蛳太太在那里迎接，相见黯然，但只转瞬之间，螺蛳太太便浮起了笑容，“想来还没有吃饭？”她问：“饭开在哪里？”这是没话找话，胡雪岩根本没有听进去，只说：“到你楼上谈。”他又问：“老太太晓得不晓得，我回来了。”“还没有禀告她老人家。”“好！关照中门上，先不要说。”“我晓得。不会的。”胡家的中门，仿佛大内的乾清门一般，禁制特严，真个外言不入，螺蛳太太早已关照过了，大可放心。到得螺蛳太太那里，阿云捧来一碗燕窝汤，一笼现蒸的鸡蛋糕，另外是现沏的龙井茶，预备齐全，随即下楼，这是螺蛳太太早就关照好了的。阿云武守在楼梯口，不准任何人上楼。

If the large depositors could be kept steady, the scattered small withdrawals were within their means and gave no cause for fear. Only after giving these instructions did Hu return to Yuanbao Street. Night had fallen, but the street was still packed with sedan chairs and horses. Lanterns hung high at the doors, and household attendants stood in orderly ranks. Everything seemed as usual, except that the customary bustle and cheerful noise had suddenly disappeared. His chair was carried straight to the garden entrance. When he alighted, the First Madam and Madam Luosi were waiting for him. Their meeting was sombre, but within a moment Madam Luosi produced a smile. “I suppose you have not eaten? Where shall dinner be served?” She was merely finding something to say. Hu scarcely heard her. “Let us talk upstairs in your rooms. Does the old lady know I am back?” “She has not been told yet.” “Good. Tell the people at the inner gate not to say anything for the moment.” “I know. They will not.” The household’s inner gate was guarded by restrictions as strict as those at the Gate of Heavenly Purity in the imperial palace: no word from outside was allowed through. Madam Luosi had already given her orders, and he could be easy on that score. Upstairs, A’yun brought a bowl of bird’s-nest soup, a steamer of freshly made egg cakes and newly brewed Longjing tea. Once everything was ready, she went downstairs, as Madam Luosi had instructed beforehand, and stood guard at the foot of the stairs to keep everyone else away.

“事情要紧不要紧？”胡太太首先开口。“说要紧就要紧，说不要紧就不要紧。”胡雪岩说： “如今是顶石臼做戏，能把戏做完，大不了落个吃力不讨好，没有啥要紧，这出做不下去，石臼砸下来，非死即伤。”“那么这出戏要怎样做呢？”螺蛳太太问说。“要做得台底下看不出我们头上顶了一个石臼，那就不要紧了。”“我也是这样关照大家，一切照常，喜事该怎么办，还是该怎么办。不过，场面可以拿铜钱摆出来的，只怕笑脸摆不出来。”“难就难在这里。不过，”胡雪岩加重了语气说：“再难也要做到，场面无论如何要好好儿把它绷起来，不管你们用啥法子。胡太太与螺蛳太太相互看了一眼，都将这两句话好好地想了一下，各有会心，不断点头。“外头的事情有我。”胡雪岩问说：“德晓峰怎么样？”“总算不错。”螺蛳太太说：“莲珠一下午都在我这里，她说：你最好今天晚上就去看看德藩台。”“晚上，恐怕不方便。”“晚上才好细谈。”“好，我等一下就去。”

“Is it serious?” the First Madam asked first. “You could call it serious, or you could call it nothing,” Hu replied. “We are performing a play with a stone mortar balanced on our heads. If we can finish it, the worst that happens is that we work hard and get no thanks. That is nothing. But if we cannot carry the performance through, the mortar falls, and we are killed or injured.” “Then how are we to perform it?” Madam Luosi asked. “So that the audience never sees the mortar on our heads. Then we shall be all right.” “That is what I told everyone too. Everything as usual: the wedding is to be conducted exactly as it should be. But money can provide the grand display. It may not provide the smiling faces.” “That is precisely the difficulty. Still”—Hu spoke with added force—“however difficult it is, it must be done. Whatever means you use, you must keep up the display.” The two women looked at each other. Both considered his words carefully, understood them in their own ways and nodded. “Leave the outside affairs to me. How has De Xiaofeng behaved?” “Well enough,” Madam Luosi said. “Lianzhu spent the whole afternoon here. She says you would do best to see Treasurer De tonight.” “Would it not be inconvenient at night?” “Night is the best time for a proper conversation.” “Very well. I shall go shortly.”

胡雪岩有些踌躇，因为这时候最要紧的事，并不是去看德馨，第一件是要发电报到各处，第二件是要召集几个重要的助手，商量应变之计。这两件事非但耽误不得，而且颇费功夫，实在抽不出空去看德馨。“有应春在这里就好了。”胡雪岩叹口气，颓然倒在一张安乐椅，头软软地垂了下来。螺蛳太太大吃一惊，“老爷！老爷！”她走上前去，半跪着摇撼着他双肩说：“你要撑起来！不管怎么样要撑牢！”“胡雪岩没有作声，一把抱住她，将头埋在她肩项之间，”罗四姐，“他说，“怕要害你受苦了，你肯不肯同我共患难？“怎么不肯？我同你共过富贵，当然要同你共患难。”说着，螺蛳太太眼泪掉了下来，落在胡雪岩手背上。“你不要哭！你刚才劝我，现在我也要劝你。外面我撑，里面你撑。”“好！”螺蛳太太抹抹眼泪，很快地答应。“你比我难。”胡雪岩说：“第一，老太太那里要瞒住，第二，亲亲眷眷，还有底下人，都要照应到，第三，这桩喜事仍旧要办得风风光光。”螺蛳太太心想第一桩还好办，到底只有一个人，第二桩就很吃力了，第三桩更难，不管怎么风光，贺客要谈煞风景的事，莫非去掩住他们的嘴？正这样转着念头，胡雪岩又开口了，“罗四姐，”他说：“你答应得落答应不落？如果答应不落，我……”

Hu hesitated. Calling on Dexin was not the most urgent business before him. First he had to send telegrams to various places; then he must bring his principal assistants together to discuss how to meet the emergency. Neither task could wait, and both would take time. He really could not spare any to visit Dexin. “If only Yingchun were here!” With a sigh, he sank dejectedly into an armchair, his head drooping. Madam Luosi was alarmed. “My dear! My dear!” She hurried over and half knelt before him, shaking his shoulders. “You must hold yourself together. Whatever happens, you must!” Without a word, he clasped her and buried his face between her neck and shoulder. “Sister Luo,” he said, “I am afraid I may bring suffering upon you. Will you share my troubles?” “How could I refuse? I have shared your prosperity. Of course I shall share your troubles.” Her tears fell onto the back of his hand. “Do not cry. You were encouraging me just now; now I must encourage you. I shall hold things together outside. You must do the same within.” “I will!” She wiped her eyes and answered at once. “Your task is harder than mine. First, you must keep the old lady from knowing. Second, you must look after the relatives and all the servants. Third, the wedding must be as splendid as ever.” The first task was manageable, she thought: after all, it concerned only one person. The second would be taxing, and the third harder still. However splendid the wedding, how could she stop the guests discussing things that would spoil it? Cover their mouths? While she was thinking, Hu spoke again. “Sister Luo, can you undertake it or not? If you cannot, I…”

等了一会不听他说下去，螺蛳太太不由得要问：“你怎么样？”“你撑不落，我就撑牢了，也没有意思。”“那么，怎么样呢？”“索性倒下来算了。”“瞎说八道！”螺蛳太太跳了起来，大声说道，“胡大先生，你不要让我看不起你！”胡雪岩原是激励她的意思，想不到同时也受了她的激励，顿时精神百倍地站起身来说：“好！我马上去看德晓峰。”“这才是。”螺蛳太太关照：“千万不要忘记谢谢莲珠。”“我晓得。”“还有，你每一趟外路回来去看德藩台，从来没有空手的，这回最好也不要破例。”

She waited, but he said no more. “You will do what?” she asked. “If you cannot hold things together, there is no point in my holding on.” “Then what?” “We may as well let everything collapse.” “Nonsense!” She sprang to her feet and cried, “Mr Hu, do not make me despise you!” He had meant to stir her courage. Unexpectedly, she had stirred his as well. Suddenly full of energy, he rose. “Very well! I shall go and see Mr Xiaofeng at once.” “That is more like it. Whatever you do, do not forget to thank Lianzhu.” “I know.” “And whenever you come home from a journey and call on Treasurer De, you never go empty-handed. It would be best not to make an exception this time.”

这下提醒胡雪岩，“我的行李在哪里？”他说：“其中有一只外国货的皮箱，里头新鲜花样很多。”“等我来问阿云。”原来胡雪岩每次远行，都是螺蛳太太为他收拾行李，同样地，胡雪岩一回来，行李箱亦照例卸在她这里，所以要问阿云。“有的。等我去提了来。”那只皮箱甚重，是两个丫头抬上来的，箱子上装了暗锁，要对准号码，才能打开。急切间，胡雪岩想不起什么号码，怎么转也转不开，又烦又急，弄得满头大汗。“等我来！”螺蛳太太顺手捡起一把大剪刀，朝锁具的缝隙中插了下去，然后交代阿云：“你用力往后扳。”阿云是大脚，近尺莲船抵住了皮箱，双手用足了劲往后一扳，锁是被撬开了，却以用力过度，仰开摔了一交。“对！”胡雪岩若有所悟地自语：“快刀斩乱麻！”一面说，一面将皮纸包着的大包小包取了出来，堆在桌上，皮箱下面铺平了的，是舶来品的衣料。

That reminded Hu. “Where is my luggage? There is a leather trunk of foreign goods, with all sorts of novelties in it.” “Let me ask A’yun.” Madam Luosi always packed for his journeys, and when he returned the luggage was invariably brought to her rooms, so A’yun was the person to ask. “Yes, it is here. I shall fetch it.” The trunk was heavy enough to require two maids to carry it upstairs. It had a combination lock that would open only when the numbers were correctly aligned. In his haste, Hu could not remember the combination. However he turned it, the lock would not open. Frustration and anxiety brought the sweat pouring from his forehead. “Let me!” Madam Luosi picked up a large pair of scissors and thrust a blade into a gap in the lock. “Pull it back hard, A’yun.” A’yun had unbound feet. Planting one, almost a chi long, against the trunk, she pulled back with both hands. The lock gave way, but she had used so much force that she fell backwards onto the floor. “Exactly!” Hu murmured, as though something had dawned on him. “Cut through the tangle with one stroke!” As he spoke, he took out parcels large and small, wrapped in tough paper, and piled them on the table. Imported cloth lay flat at the bottom of the trunk.

“这个是预备送德晓峰的。”胡雪岩将一个小纸包递给螺蛳太太，又加了一句：“小心打碎。”打开来一看，是个乾隆年间烧料的鼻烟壶，配上祖母绿的盖子。螺蛳太太这几年见识得多，知道名贵，“不过，”她说：“一样好象太少了。”“那就再配一只表。”这只表用极讲究的皮盒子盛着，打开来一看，上面是一张写着洋文的羊皮纸，揭开来，是个毫不起眼的银表。“这只表……”“这只表，你不要看不起它，来头很大，是法国皇帝拿破仑用过的，我是当古董买回来的。这张羊皮纸是‘保单’，只要还得出‘报门’不是拿破仑用过，包退还洋，另加罚金。”“好！送莲珠的呢？“只有一个金黄寇盒子。如果嫌轻，再加两件衣料。”

“This was intended for Mr Xiaofeng.” Hu handed Madam Luosi a small parcel and added, “Careful. It may break.” Inside was a glass snuff bottle made in the Qianlong reign, with an emerald stopper. She had seen enough fine things in recent years to recognize its value. “But one item seems rather little.” “Then add a watch.” The watch came in an exquisite leather case. When she opened it, she found a sheet of parchment covered in foreign writing. Beneath it lay a thoroughly unremarkable silver watch. “This watch…” “Do not look down on it. It has a very distinguished history. Napoleon, the Emperor of France, used it. I bought it as an antique. This parchment is the guarantee: anyone who can produce proof that Napoleon did not use it gets a refund, with a penalty payment besides.” “Very well. And for Lianzhu?” “There is only a gold box. If that seems too slight a gift, add two lengths of cloth.”

从箱子下面取出几块平铺着的衣料出来，螺蛳太太忽生感慨，从嫁到胡家，什么绫罗绸缎，在她跟毛蓝布等量齐观，但一摸到西洋的衣料，感觉大不相同。这种感觉形容不出。她见过的最好的衣料是“贡缎”，这种缎子又分“御用”与“上用”两种，“御用”的贡缎，后妃所用，亦用来赏赐王公大臣。皇帝所用，才专称为“上用”。但民间讲究的人，当然亦是世家巨族，用的亦是“上用”的缎子，只是颜色避免用“明黄”以及较“明黄”为暗的“香色”，“明黄”只皇帝、太上皇帝能用，“香色”则是皇子专用颜色，除此以外，百无禁忌，但争奇斗妍，可以比“上用”的缎子更讲究，譬如上午所着与晚间所着，看似同样花样的缎袍，而暗花已有区分，上午的花含苞待放，下午的花已盛开。这些讲究，已是“不是三世做官，不知道穿衣吃饭”的人家所矜重，但是，比起舶来品的好衣料来，不免令人兴起绚烂不如平淡之感。

As she lifted out several lengths laid flat beneath the other things, Madam Luosi was suddenly moved. Since marrying into the Hu household, she had come to regard every kind of silk and satin as no more remarkable than coarse blue cotton. Yet the touch of Western cloth gave her quite a different feeling, one she could not describe. The finest material she had known was tribute satin, divided into two categories: “imperial use” and “the sovereign’s use.” The former was used by the empress and imperial consorts, and was also bestowed on princes and high officials. Satin for the emperor himself was specifically designated “the sovereign’s use.” Discerning private households—naturally, the great families of generations’ standing—also used satin of this quality. They merely avoided bright yellow and the darker shade known as incense colour. Bright yellow was reserved for the emperor and a retired emperor; incense colour was for imperial princes. Apart from those colours, there were no restrictions. In their rivalry for novelty and beauty, private households could be more exacting even than the sovereign’s wardrobe. A satin robe worn in the morning might appear to have the same pattern as one worn in the evening, yet the subtly woven designs would differ: the morning flowers were buds about to open, while those for the afternoon were in full bloom. Such refinements were cherished in families of whom it could be said, “It takes three generations in office to learn how to dress and dine.” Yet compared with fine imported cloth, they prompted the thought that splendour could not equal simplicity.

螺蛳太太所拣出来的两件衣料，都是单色，一件藏青、一件玄色，这种衣料名叫“哔叽”，刚刚行销到中国，名贵异常，但她就有四套哔叽袄裤，穿过了才知道它的好处。这种在洋行发售，内地官宦人家少见，就是上海商场中，也只有讲时髦的阔客才用来作袍料的“哔叽”，在胡家无足为奇。胡雪岩爱纤足，姬妾在平时不着裙子，春秋佳日用“哔叽”裁制夹袄夹裤，稳重挺括，颜色素雅，自然高贵。她常说：“做人就要象哔叽一样，经得起折磨，到哪里都显得有分量。”此时此地此人，想到自己常说的话，不由得凄然泪下。幸好胡雪岩没有注意，她背着灯取手绢醒鼻子，顺便擦一擦眼睛，将拣齐了的礼物，关照阿雪用锦袱包了起来，然后亲自送胡雪岩到花园的西侧门。“

The two lengths she selected were plain colours, one navy blue and the other black. The fabric was called serge. It had only just begun to be sold in China and was exceptionally precious, yet she already had four suits of serge jackets and trousers. Only by wearing it had she discovered its virtues. Sold through the foreign trading houses, it was seldom seen among official families inland. Even in Shanghai’s commercial circles, only rich men with a taste for fashion had it made into robes. In the Hu household, however, it was nothing unusual. Hu loved slender feet, so his concubines did not ordinarily wear skirts. On fine spring and autumn days, they wore lined jackets and trousers made of serge: dignified and crisp, sober in colour, with a natural distinction. She often said, “One should be like serge: able to withstand wear and carry weight wherever one goes.” Remembering her own words at this moment, in these circumstances, she could not help weeping. Fortunately, Hu did not notice. Turning away from the light, she took out her handkerchief to blow her nose and wiped her eyes at the same time. She told A’xue to wrap the chosen gifts in a brocade cloth, then personally escorted Hu to the western side gate of the garden.

这道门平时关闭，只有胡雪岩入夜“微行”时才开，坐的当然也不是绿呢大轿，更没有前呼后拥的“亲兵”，只有两个贴身小跟班，前后各擎一盏灯笼，照着小轿直到藩司衙门。由于预先已有通知，德馨派了人在那里等候。胡雪岩下了轿，一直就到签押房。“深夜过来打搅晓翁，实在不安。胡雪岩话是这么说，态度还是跟平时一样，潇洒自如，毫不显得窘迫。“来！来！躺下来。”刚起身来迎的德馨，自己先躺了下去！接过丫头递过来的烟枪，一口气抽完，但却用手势指挥，如何招待客人。他指挥丫头，先替胡雪岩卸去马褂，等他侧身躺下来，丫头便将他的双腿抬到搁脚凳上，脱去双梁鞋，然后取一床俄国毯子盖在腿上，掖得严严的，温暖无比。“雪岩，”德馨说道：“我到今天才真佩服你！”没头没脑的这一句话，说得胡雪岩唯有苦笑，“晓翁，”他说：“你不要挖苦我了。”

This gate was normally kept shut and opened only when Hu went out privately at night. Naturally, he did not use the large green-cloth sedan chair, nor did a crowd of guards precede and follow him. Only two personal attendants accompanied the little chair, one before and one behind, each holding a lantern to light the way to the provincial treasurer’s office. Notice had already been sent, and Dexin had someone waiting. Hu alighted and went straight to the private office. “I am very sorry to disturb you so late, Mr Xiaofeng.” Despite his words, his manner was as easy and composed as ever, without a trace of embarrassment. “Come, come! Lie down.” Dexin had risen to greet him, but lay down again first. He took the opium pipe a maid handed him and smoked it in one breath, directing his guest’s reception by gestures. The maid first removed Hu’s riding jacket. When he lay on his side, she lifted his legs onto a footstool, took off his double-ridged shoes and covered his legs with a Russian blanket, tucking it closely around him. It was wonderfully warm. “Xueyan,” Dexin said, “only today have I truly come to admire you.” With no explanation for this sudden remark, Hu could only smile wryly. “Mr Xiaofeng, please do not mock me.”

“不是我挖苦你。”德馨说道：“从前听人说，孟尝君门下食客三千，鸡鸣狗盗，到了紧要关头，都会大显神通。你手下有个周少棠，你就踉孟尝君一样了。”周少棠大出风头这件事，他只听谢云青略为提到，不知其详，如今听德馨如此夸奖，不由得大感兴趣，便问一句：“何以见得？”好让德馨讲下去。“我当时在场，亲眼目睹，实在佩服。”德馨说道：“京里有个丑儿叫刘赶三，随机应变，临时抓限是有名的，可是以我看来，不及周少棠。”接着德馨眉飞色舞地将周少棠玩弄黄八麻子于股掌之上的情形，细细形容了一遍，胡雪岩默默地听着，心里在想，这周少棠以后有什么地方用得着他。“雪岩，”德馨又说：“周少棠给你帮的忙，实在不小。把挤兑的那班人哄得各自回家，犹在其次，要紧的是，把你帮了乡下养蚕人家的大忙，大大吹嘘了一番。这一点很有用，而且功效已显出来了，今儿下午刘仲帅约我去谈你的事，他就提到你为了跟英国人斗法，以至于被挤，说应该想法子维持。”刘仲帅是指浙江巡抚刘秉璋，他跟李鸿章虽非如何融洽，但总是淮军一系，能有此表示，自然值得珍视，所以胡雪岩不免有兴奋的语气。“刘仲帅亦能体谅，盛情实在可感。”

“I am not mocking you. I used to hear that Lord Mengchang kept three thousand retainers, and even those whose talents lay in crowing like cocks or thieving like dogs could work wonders in a crisis. With a Zhou Shaotang among your men, you are another Lord Mengchang.” Xie had briefly mentioned Zhou’s great performance, but Hu did not know the details. Such praise from Dexin naturally interested him. “How so?” he asked, inviting him to continue. “I was there and saw it myself. Truly admirable! There is a comic actor in Beijing called Liu Gansan, famous for his quick responses and improvised jokes, but in my judgement he is no match for Zhou.” With animated delight, Dexin described in detail how Zhou had made a plaything of Huang Bamazi. Hu listened silently, wondering where he might make use of Zhou in future. “Xueyan, the service he has done you is considerable. Talking the people demanding their money into going home was the lesser part of it. More important was his great account of how you helped the country people who raise silkworms. That is very useful, and it has already had an effect. Commander Liu asked me to discuss your affairs this afternoon. He mentioned that your struggle with the British had brought this pressure upon you, and said we ought to find a way to support you.” Commander Liu was Liu Bingzhang, the governor of Zhejiang. Although his relations with Li Hongzhang were not particularly close, he belonged to the Huai Army faction. Such words from him were therefore to be valued, and Hu’s voice betrayed some excitement. “Commander Liu understands my position too! His kindness is deeply appreciated.”

“你先别高兴，他还有话；能维持才维持，不能维持趁早处置，总以确保官款为第一要义。雪岩，”德馨在枕上转脸看着胡雪岩说：“你得给我一句话。”这句话自然是要胡雪岩提供保证，决不至于让他无法交代。胡雪岩想了一下说：“晓翁，我们相交不是一天，你看我是对不起人的人吗？”“这一层，你用不着表白。不过，雪岩，你的事业太大了，或许有些地方你自己都不甚了了。譬如，你如果对你自己的虚实，一清二楚的话，上海的阜康何至于等你一走，马上就撑不住了？”这番话说得胡雪岩哑口无言，以他的口才，可以辩解，但他不想那样做，因为他觉得那样就是不诚。“雪岩，你亦不必难过。事已如此，只有挺直腰杆来对付。”德馨紧接着说：“我此刻只要你一句话。”“请吩咐。”“你心里的想法，先要告诉我。不必多，只要一句话好了。”这话别具意味，胡雪岩揣摩了半天，方始敢于确定，“晓翁，”他说。“如果我真的撑不下去了，我一定先同晓翁讨主意。”这话的意思是一定会维护德馨的利益，不管是公、是私？“好！咱们一言为定。现在，雪岩，你说吧，我能替你帮什么忙？”

“Do not rejoice yet. He had more to say: support you if you can be supported; otherwise, make arrangements without delay. The first consideration must always be the safety of public funds.” Turning his face on the pillow towards Hu, Dexin added, “Xueyan, you must give me your word.” Clearly, he wanted a guarantee that Hu would not leave him unable to account for the money. Hu thought for a moment. “Mr Xiaofeng, we have known each other for years. Do you think I am a man who would let others down?” “You need not assure me of that. But your enterprises are so extensive that you may not fully understand every part of them yourself. If you knew your own position through and through, for instance, why did the Shanghai bank lose its footing the moment you left?” Hu had no answer. With his eloquence, he could have defended himself, but he did not wish to, because that would not have been honest. “There is no need to take it to heart. Things being as they are, you can only stand straight and face them. For the moment, I want just one word from you.” “Please tell me.” “You must let me know what you are thinking. Nothing more than one word is needed.” There was a particular meaning beneath this. Hu considered it for some time before he felt certain enough to reply. “If I really cannot hold on, I shall consult you first, Mr Xiaofeng.” What he meant was that he would protect Dexin’s interests, public and private alike. “Good! That is agreed. Now, Xueyan, tell me what I can do to help.”

“不止于帮忙，”胡雪岩说：“我现在要请晓翁拿我的事，当自己的事办。”这话分明一也很重，德馨想了一下说：“这不在话下。不过，自己的事，不能不知道吧？”“是，我跟晓翁说一句，只要不出意外，一定可以过关。”“雪岩，你的所谓意外是什么？”“凡是我抓不住的，都会出意外。”胡雪岩说：“第一个是李合肥。”说到这里，他不由得叹了一口报导，“唉！原以为左大人到了两江，是件好事，哪晓得反而坏了。”“喔。这一层，你倒不妨谈谈。”谈起来很复杂，也很简单，左宗棠一到两江，便与李鸿章在上海的势力发生冲突。如果左宗棠仍有当年一往无前笼罩各方的魄力，加上胡雪岩的精打细算，则两江总督管两江，名正言顺，李鸿章一定会落下风。无奈左宗棠老境颓唐，加以在两江素无基础，更糟糕的是对法交涉，态度软硬，大相径庭，而李鸿章为了贯彻他的政策，视左宗棠为遇事掣时、非拔除不可的眼中钉，而又以剪除左宗棠的党羽为主要手段，这一来便将胡雪岩看作保护左宗棠的盾牌，集矢其上了。

“I ask more than help,” Hu said. “I want you to treat my affairs as your own.” This too carried considerable weight. Dexin reflected. “That goes without saying. But one must know about one’s own affairs, must one not?” “Yes. Let me tell you this: provided nothing unforeseen happens, I can certainly get through.” “What do you mean by unforeseen?” “Anything beyond my control can produce a surprise. First of all, Li of Hefei.” He could not suppress a sigh. “I thought Lord Zuo’s coming to Liangjiang was a good thing. Who could have known it would turn out to be the opposite?” “Indeed? Tell me about that.” The explanation was at once complex and simple. As soon as Zuo came to Liangjiang, he clashed with Li Hongzhang’s power in Shanghai. Had Zuo retained the irresistible energy with which he once dominated every side, reinforced by Hu’s careful calculations, Li would certainly have had the worst of it. After all, the Governor-General of Liangjiang had every right to govern Liangjiang. But Zuo was declining with age and had no established base there. Worse still, their positions on negotiations with France—one hard, the other soft—were worlds apart. To carry out his own policy, Li regarded Zuo as an obstacle at every turn, a thorn that had to be removed. His principal method was to strip away Zuo’s supporters. Hu thus became the shield protecting Zuo, and all the arrows were directed at him.

“我明白了。”德馨说道：“冤家宜解不宜结，李合肥那方面要设法去打个照呼。这一层，我可以托刘仲帅。”“这就重就拜托了。”胡雪岩问：“刘仲帅那里，我是不是应该去见一见？”“等明天‘上院’见了他再说。”德馨又说：“你倒想一想，李合肥如果要跟你过不去，会用什么手段？”“别的我都不在乎，”胡雪岩说：“最怕他来提北洋属下各衙门的官款，提不到可以封我的典当，那一来就要逼倒我了。”“封典当，影响平民生计，果然如此，我可以说话。”“正要晓翁仗义执言。不过后说不如先后，尤其要早说。”“好！我明天就跟刘仲帅去谈。”“能不能请刘仲帅出面，打几个电报出去，就说阜康根基稳固，请各处勿为谣言所惑，官款暂且不提，免得逼倒了阜康。”“说当然可以说。不过，刘仲帅一定会问：是不是能保证将来各处的官款，分文不少？”德馨又加一句：“如果没有这一层保证，刘仲帅不肯发这样子的电报。”胡雪岩默然半晌，方始答说：“如果我有这样的把握，也就根本不必请刘仲帅发电报了。”

“I understand,” Dexin said. “Better to resolve enmity than create it. We must find some way to approach Li of Hefei. I can ask Commander Liu to do that.” “Then I entrust it to you most earnestly. Should I go and see Commander Liu myself?” “Let me see him at the official audience tomorrow first. Think, though: if Li of Hefei means to make trouble for you, what means will he use?” “Nothing else concerns me. What I fear most is that he will demand the public funds deposited by the offices under Beiyang. If I cannot repay them, he can seal up my pawnshops. That would force me under.” “Closing pawnshops affects ordinary people’s livelihoods. If it comes to that, I can speak out.” “That is precisely what I ask you to do. But it is better to speak beforehand than afterwards, and the sooner the better.” “Very well. I shall speak to Commander Liu tomorrow.” “Could he send out a few telegrams, saying that Fukang stands on firm foundations and asking the various offices not to be misled by rumours? They should refrain from withdrawing public funds for the moment, to avoid forcing Fukang into collapse.” “He can certainly be asked. But he will want to know whether you can guarantee that every office’s public money will ultimately be repaid in full. Without that guarantee, he will not send such telegrams.” Hu was silent for a long time before he answered. “If I could be that certain, there would be no need to ask him to send them in the first place.”

这下是德馨默然。一直等将烟瘾过足，方又开口：“雪岩，至少本省大小衙门存在阜康的官款，我有把握，在一个月之内不会提。”“只要一个月之内，官款不动，就不要紧了。”胡雪岩说：“我在天津的丝，可以找到户头，一脱手，头寸马上就松了。”“上海呢？”德馨问道：“你在上海不也有许多丝囤在那里吗？”“上海的不能动！洋人本来就在杀我的价钱，现在看我急需周转，更看得我的丝不值钱。晓翁，钱财身外之物，我不肯输这口气，尤其是输给洋人，更加不服。”“唉！”德馨叹口气，“大家都要象你这样子争气，中国就好了。”

Now it was Dexin’s turn to fall silent. He did not speak again until he had satisfied his craving for opium. “Xueyan, at least I can assure you that none of the public funds deposited with Fukang by the offices of this province, great or small, will be withdrawn for a month.” “If the public funds remain untouched for a month, we shall be all right,” Hu said. “I can find a buyer for my silk in Tianjin. Once it is sold, funds will immediately become easier.” “What about Shanghai? Have you not a great deal of silk stored there too?” “The Shanghai stock must not be touched! The foreigners were already beating down my price. Now that they see how urgently I need funds, they will value my silk even less. Mr Xiaofeng, money is only a worldly possession. I will not swallow this defeat—least of all at the foreigners’ hands.” Dexin sighed. “If everyone had your determination to stand their ground, China would be better off.”
