正在谈着，闪出一个梳长辫子的丫头，带着老妈子来摆桌子，预备吃消夜。胡雪岩本想告辞，转念又想，应该不改常度：有几次夜间来访，到了时候总是吃消夜，这天也应该照常才是。“姨太太呢？”德馨问说，“说我请她。”“马上出来。”原来莲珠是不避胡雪岩的，这天原要出来周旋，一则慰问，再则道谢。

While they were talking, a maid with a long braid slipped in, followed by older women who set the table for a late supper. Hu Xueyan had been about to take his leave, but on reflection he decided that he ought to keep to his usual ways. On several previous evening visits he had stayed for supper when the time came; he ought to do the same tonight. “Where is your mistress?” Dexin asked. “Tell her I should like her to join us.” “She will be out directly.” Lianzhu did not observe the convention of avoiding Hu Xueyan. She had already intended to come out and entertain him that evening, both to express her concern and to thank him for his gifts.

及至胡雪岩刚刚落座，听得帘钩微响，扭头看时，莲珠出现在房门口，她穿的是件旗袍，不过自己改良过了，袖子并不太宽，腰身亦比较小，由于她身材颀长，而且生长北方，穿惯了旗装，所以在她手握一方绣花手帕，一摇三摆地走了来，一点都看不出她是汉人。“二太太！”胡雪岩赶紧站起来招呼。“请坐，请坐！”莲珠摆一摆手说：“胡大先生，多谢你送的东西，太破费了。”“小意思，小意思。”胡雪岩说：“初五那天，二太太你要早点来。”“胡大先生，你不用关照，我扰府上的喜酒，不止一顿，四姐请我去陪客，一前一后，起码扰你三顿。”原来杭州是南宋故都，婚丧喜庆，有许多繁文褥节，富家大族办喜事，请亲友执事，前期宴请，名为“请将”，事后款待，称为“谢将”。莲珠是螺蛳太太特为邀来陪官眷的“支宾”。

Hu Xueyan had scarcely taken his seat when he heard the faint clink of a curtain hook. Turning, he saw Lianzhu in the doorway. She was wearing a Manchu gown, though she had altered the design herself: the sleeves were less wide and the waist more closely fitted. She was tall and slender, had grown up in the north, and was accustomed to Manchu dress. As she approached with an embroidered handkerchief in her hand and a graceful swaying walk, there was nothing to suggest that she was Han Chinese. “Second Madam!” Hu rose at once to greet her. “Please sit down, please!” She waved him back to his seat. “Mr Hu, thank you for all those things you sent me. You have been far too extravagant.” “A mere trifle, a mere trifle,” Hu replied. “You must come early on the fifth, Second Madam.” “There is no need to remind me, Mr Hu. I shall be enjoying more than one wedding feast at your house. Fourth Sister has asked me to help entertain the guests. With the gatherings before and after the wedding, I shall be eating at your expense at least three times.” Hangzhou, the former capital of the Southern Song, retained many elaborate observances for weddings, funerals, and other family occasions. When a wealthy family held a wedding and enlisted relatives and friends to help, the preliminary banquet was called “inviting the generals,” and the feast afterwards, “thanking the generals.” Madam Luosi had specially invited Lianzhu to act as a hostess to the wives of officials.

“雪岩！”德馨问道：“喜事一切照常？”胡雪岩尚未答话，莲珠先开口了，“自然照常。”她说：“这还用得着问？”“你看！”德馨为姨太太所抢白，脸上有点挂不住，指着莲珠，自嘲地向胡雪岩说：“管得越严了，连多说句话都不得。”“只怕没有人管。”胡雪岩答说：“有人管是好事。”“我就是爱管闲事，也不光是管你。”莲珠紧接着又说：“胡大先生的事，我们怎么好不管，有件事我要提醒你，到了好日子那天，要约了刘抚台去道喜！”这正是胡雪岩想说不便说，关切在心里的一句话，所以格外注意德馨的反应，只听他答了一句：“当然非拉他去不可。”顿觉胸怀一宽。

“Xueyan,” Dexin asked, “is everything for the wedding going ahead as usual?” Before Hu could answer, Lianzhu spoke. “Of course it is. Do you even need to ask?” “There, you see!” A little embarrassed at being put down by his concubine, Dexin pointed at her and said to Hu with a self-mocking smile, “She keeps me under tighter control every day. I am not even allowed an extra word.” “The thing to fear is having no one to look after you,” Hu replied. “Having someone who does is a blessing.” “I do like to meddle, and you are not the only person I meddle with,” Lianzhu went on. “How could we stand aside when Mr Hu needs us? There is something I must remind you of: on the wedding day, you must arrange to go with Governor Liu and offer your congratulations.” This was precisely what Hu had been longing to ask but had felt unable to put into words. He watched Dexin's reaction with particular attention. When Dexin answered, “Of course I must drag him along,” a weight lifted from his heart.

“胡大先生，我特为穿旗袍给你看，你送我的哔叽衣料，我照这样子做了来穿，你说好不好看？”通家之好，到了这样的程度，似乎稍嫌过分，胡雪岩只好这样答说：“你说好就好。”“好是好，太素了一点儿。胡大先生，我还要托你，有没有西洋花边，下次得便请你从上海给我带一点来。”“有！有！”胡雪岩一叠连声地答说：“不必下一次。明天我就叫人送了来。”他接着又说：“西洋花边宽细都有，花式很多，我多送点来，请二太太自己挑。”“那就更好了。”“别老站着。”德馨亲自移开一张凳子，“你也陪我们吃一点儿。”于是莲珠坐了下来，为主客二人酌酒布菜，静静地听他们谈话。

“Mr Hu, I put on this Manchu gown especially to show you. I want to have the serge you sent me made up in this style. Do you think it would look well?” Even between families on such intimate terms, this seemed a little too familiar. Hu could only answer, “If you think it looks well, then it does.” “It would, only it would be a little too plain. There is something else I must ask you, Mr Hu. Can one get Western lace? Next time you have an opportunity, would you bring me some from Shanghai?” “Certainly, certainly!” Hu answered eagerly. “There is no need to wait until next time. I will have some sent over tomorrow. Western lace comes in broad and narrow widths, and in a great many patterns. I shall send a selection for you to choose from.” “That would be even better.” “Do not keep standing there,” Dexin said, moving a stool himself. “Sit down and have something with us.” Lianzhu sat down, poured wine and served food for host and guest, and quietly listened to their conversation.

“雪岩，我听说你用的人，也不完全靠得住。你自己总知道吧？”“过了这个风潮，我要好好整顿了。”胡雪岩答说：“晓翁说周少棠值得重用，我一定要重用。”“你看了人再用。”莲珠忍不住插嘴，“不要光看人家的面子，人用得不好，受害的是自己。”“是，是！二太太是金玉良言。”胡雪岩深为感慨，“这回的风潮，也是我不听一两个好友的话之故。”“其实你不必听外头人的话，多听听罗四姐的话就好了。”“她对外面的情形不大明白。这一点，比二太太你差多了。”听得这话，莲珠颇有知己之感，“胡大先生，你是明白的。不比我们老爷，提到外面的事，总说：“你别管‘。一个人再聪明，也有当局者迷的时候。刚才你同我们老爷在交谈的情形，我也听到了这一点儿。”说到这里，她突然问道：“胡大先生，上海跟杭州两处的风潮，左大人知道不知道？”

“Xueyan, I hear that not all the people you employ can be relied upon. You must know that yourself?” “Once this crisis is over, I shall put things thoroughly in order,” Hu replied. “You said Zhou Shaotang deserved to be given greater responsibilities, Mr Xiaofeng. I shall certainly see that he gets them.” Lianzhu could not resist intervening. “Judge the man for yourself before you employ him. Do not do it merely out of regard for someone else. If you choose badly, you are the one who suffers.” “Yes, yes! Those are words of gold, Second Madam.” Hu spoke with deep feeling. “This crisis, too, has come about because I failed to listen to one or two good friends.” “In fact, you need not listen to outsiders. You would do well enough if you listened more to Luo Sijie.” “She does not understand very much about affairs outside the household. In that respect she is a long way behind you, Second Madam.” Lianzhu felt that here, at least, was someone who appreciated her. “Mr Hu, you understand. Our master is quite different. Whenever I mention outside affairs, he says, ‘Keep out of it.’ However clever a person may be, there are times when being in the middle of things prevents him from seeing clearly. I caught something of what you and our master were discussing just now.” At this point she suddenly asked, “Does Lord Zuo know about the trouble in Shanghai and Hangzhou, Mr Hu?”

“恐怕还不晓得。”“你怎么不告诉他？”“告诉他？”胡雪岩有些茫然，多少年来，凡是失面子的事，他从不告诉左宗棠，所以阜康的风潮一起，他根本就没有想到过左宗棠。“为什么不告诉他？”莲珠说道：“你瞒也瞒不住的。”“说得不错。”德馨也说：“如果左大人肯出面，到底是两江总督部堂！”这个衔头在东南半壁，至高无上，但到底能发生什么作用，却很难说。哪知道莲珠别有深心，“胡大先生这会心很乱，恐怕不知道该跟左大人说什么好？”她随即提出一个建议：“是不是请杨师爷来拟个稿子看看？”那杨师爷是苏州人，年纪很轻，但笔下很来得，而且能言善道，善体人意，莲珠对他很欣赏。德馨只要是莲珠说好就好，所以对杨师爷亦颇另眼相看，此时便问胡雪岩：“你的意思怎么样？”“好是好！不过只怕太缓了。”“怎么缓得了？发电报出去，明天一早就到了。”“我密码本不在这里。”“用我们的好了。”莲珠接口。“对啊！”德馨说道，“请杨师爷拟好了稿子，就请他翻密码好了。小妾也可以帮忙。”“这，怎么好麻烦二太太？”“怕什么，我们两家什么交情！”真是盛情难地，胡雪岩只有感激的分儿。在请杨师爷的这段时间中，离座踱着方步，将要说的话都想好了。

“I am afraid he does not yet.” “Why have you not told him?” “Tell him?” Hu sounded rather bewildered. For many years he had never reported anything to Zuo Zongtang that would cost him face. When the trouble at Fukang broke out, Zuo had therefore never even entered his thoughts. “Why should you not tell him?” Lianzhu asked. “You cannot keep it from him in any case.” “Quite right,” Dexin agreed. “If Lord Zuo were willing to intervene—he is, after all, the Governor-General of Liangjiang!” In the southeastern half of the empire, no office stood higher. What practical effect its intervention could have, however, was another question. Lianzhu, as it happened, had a further purpose in mind. “Mr Hu's thoughts must be in turmoil at present. Perhaps he does not know what he ought to say to Lord Zuo?” She promptly made a suggestion. “Why not ask Secretary Yang to draw up a draft for us to look at?” Secretary Yang was a young man from Suzhou, an excellent writer, an accomplished speaker, and particularly good at understanding other people's wishes. Lianzhu thought highly of him. Since Dexin was content to approve anyone she approved of, he too treated the young secretary with special favour. “What do you think?” he now asked Hu. “It would be a good idea, only I am afraid it would take too long.” “How could it? Send a telegram and it will be there first thing tomorrow morning.” “I have not brought my codebook.” “Use ours,” Lianzhu put in. “Of course!” Dexin said. “Once Secretary Yang has drafted it, he can put it into code. My concubine can help him.” “But how could I trouble Second Madam with that?” “What trouble? Think how close our two families are!” Faced with such warmth, Hu could only feel grateful. While they sent for Secretary Yang, he left his seat and paced slowly about, arranging in his mind what he wished to say.

“杨师爷，拜托你起个稿子，要说这样子几点：第一，请左大人为了维持人心，打电报给上海道，尽力维持阜康。第二，请两江各衙门，暂时不要提存款。第三，浙江刘抚台、德馨台很帮忙，请左大人来个电报，客气一番。”“客气倒不必。”德馨说道：“要重重托一托刘抚台。”“是！是！”杨师爷鞠躬如也地问：“还有什么话？”“想到了，再告诉你。”莲珠接口说道：“杨师爷，你请到外面来写，清静一点儿。”莲珠很热心地引领着杨师爷到了外屋，悄悄嘱咐了一番。他下笔很快，不到半个钟头，便将稿子送了上来，除了照胡雪岩所要求的三点陈述以外，前面特为加一段，盛称德馨如何帮忙，得以暂度难关，实在令人感激，同时也说了些德馨在浙江的政绩。着墨不多，但措词很有力量，这当然是莲珠悄悄嘱咐的结果。

“Secretary Yang, may I trouble you to prepare a draft covering these points? First, to preserve public confidence, ask Lord Zuo to telegraph the Shanghai circuit intendant and instruct him to do everything possible to support Fukang. Second, ask the various government offices in Liangjiang to leave their deposits untouched for the time being. Third, Governor Liu and Treasurer Dexin here in Zhejiang have been most helpful; ask Lord Zuo to send a telegram expressing his appreciation.” “There is no need for compliments,” Dexin said. “But he ought to make a strong personal appeal to Governor Liu.” “Certainly, certainly!” Secretary Yang bowed deferentially. “Is there anything else?” “If we think of anything, we shall tell you,” Lianzhu interposed. “Come and write in the outer room, Secretary Yang. It will be quieter there.” She led him out with great solicitude and gave him some instructions in a whisper. He wrote quickly, and in less than half an hour brought back the draft. Besides setting out the three points Hu had requested, it opened with a special passage praising Dexin's assistance, which had enabled Hu to weather the immediate difficulty and deserved his profound gratitude. It also mentioned some of Dexin's achievements in the administration of Zhejiang. The passage was short but forcefully worded. This was plainly the result of Lianzhu's whispered instructions.

胡雪岩心里雪亮，德馨曾透露过口风，希望更上层楼，由藩司升为巡抚，作一个真正的方面大员，而目标是江西。这就需要两江总督的支持了。原来所谓两江是明朝的说法，安徽是上江，江苏是下江，两江总督只管江苏、安徽两省，但江西与苏皖密迩，两江总督亦管得着，犹之乎直未总督，必要时能管山东。将来江西巡抚出缺，如果左宗棠肯保德馨，便有一言九鼎之力。所以电报中由胡雪岩出面，力赞德馨如何帮忙，实际上即是示好于左宗棠，为他自己的前程“烧冷灶”。当然胡雪岩是乐于帮这个惠而不费的忙，而且电报稿既出于杨师爷之手，便等于德馨作了愿全力维持的承诺，更是何乐不为！因此，他看完稿子，口中连声说道：“好极，好极！杨师爷的一支笔实在佩服。”“哪里，哪里？”杨师爷递过一支毛笔来，“有不妥的地方，请胡大先生改正。”“只字不改！都是我心里的话，为啥要改？”说着，接过毛笔来，写了个“雪”字，表示同意。

Hu understood perfectly. Dexin had previously let fall a hint that he hoped to rise another step, from provincial treasurer to governor, and become a true provincial chief. The province he had in mind was Jiangxi. For that he needed the support of the Governor-General of Liangjiang. The expression “Liangjiang,” or “the Two Jiangs,” dated from the Ming: Anhui was the Upper Jiang and Jiangsu the Lower Jiang. The Governor-General of Liangjiang governed only Jiangsu and Anhui, but because Jiangxi adjoined them, his authority could extend there too, just as the Governor-General of Zhili could, when necessary, intervene in Shandong. If the governorship of Jiangxi should fall vacant and Zuo Zongtang were willing to recommend Dexin, his word would carry decisive weight. By having Hu lavish praise on Dexin's assistance in the telegram, they were in effect making an overture to Zuo and tending a currently cold hearth for the sake of Dexin's future career. Naturally Hu was happy to do a favour that cost him nothing. Moreover, since the draft came from Dexin's own secretary, it virtually amounted to a promise by Dexin to give him his fullest support. Why should he not welcome it? Having read it, he said repeatedly, “Excellent, excellent! I truly admire your pen, Secretary Yang.” “You are too kind.” Yang offered him a writing brush. “If anything is unsuitable, please correct it, Mr Hu.” “I would not change a single word. It says exactly what is in my heart. Why should I alter it?” Taking the brush, he wrote the character Xue from his name to signify his approval.

正谈到里，只见阿福掀帘人内，悄悄地走到德馨身边，送上一个卷宗，口中轻声说道，“刚到的。”“喔！”德馨将卷宗掀开，内中只有一张纸，胡雪岩遥遥望去，看出是一通电报，字迹却看不清楚。“我的眼镜呢？”德馨一面说，一面起身找眼镜，借此走到间壁，杨师爷随即跟了过去。胡雪岩有点心神不定，深夜来了电报，是不是有关阜康的消息？如果是阜康的消息，德馨应该告诉他才是。这样想着，双眼不由得一直注视里间。“胡大先生，”莲珠说道：“你不要着急，有什么为难的事，你不便出面，让罗四姐来跟我说，我来告诉我们老爷。”“是，是，多谢二太太！”莲珠还有话要说，但德馨已经出来了，她跟胡雪岩都盯着他看，希望他宣布深夜来电报，是何事故。但德馨却不作声，坐了下来，举杯徐饮。“哪里来的电报？”莲珠问说。“不相干的事。”只说了这句又没话了。

At that moment A’fu lifted the curtain and came in. Going quietly to Dexin's side, he handed him a folder and murmured, “Just arrived.” “Oh?” Dexin opened it. There was only one sheet inside. From where he sat, Hu could see that it was a telegram, but he could not make out the writing. “Where are my spectacles?” Dexin rose to look for them, using this as an excuse to go into the adjoining room. Secretary Yang immediately followed. Hu began to feel uneasy. Could a telegram arriving so late concern Fukang? If it did, Dexin ought to tell him. As he thought this, his eyes remained fixed on the inner room. “Mr Hu,” Lianzhu said, “do not distress yourself. If there is any awkward matter you cannot conveniently raise in person, have Luo Sijie come and tell me. I will speak to our master.” “Thank you, thank you, Second Madam.” Lianzhu had more to say, but Dexin had already returned. Both she and Hu looked intently at him, waiting to hear what the late-night telegram concerned. Dexin said nothing. He sat down, raised his cup, and sipped his wine. “Where was the telegram from?” Lianzhu asked. “Nothing to do with this.” That was all he said.

原来这个电报是宁波海关监督候补道瑞庆打来的，说他得到密报，上海阜康钱庄的档手宓本常潜回宁波来筹现银。阜康在宁波的联号，共有两家，一家叫通泉钱庄，一家叫通裕银号。但因宁波市面亦以越南战事的影响，颇为萧条，通泉、通裕都无从接济阜康。而且通泉的档手不知避匿何处，通裕银号的档手则自行请求封闭，因此，瑞庆即命鄞悬知县查封通裕，请德馨转知通泉、通裕的东主，即速清理。德馨对通泉、通裕的情况还不清楚，一时不知如何处置，因而就不便公开这通电报。直到胡雪岩告辞以后，才跟莲珠商量。首先问她，这个消息暂且瞒着胡雪岩，是不是做错了？““当然错了！”莲珠问道：“你为什么当时不说？”“我一说，雪岩当时就会要我复电请老瑞维持，通泉启封，那两家庄号的情形，我一点都不知道，现在一启封，一定挤兑，撑不住出了事，还是要封，那又何苦？“

The telegram was in fact from Ruiqing, the expectant circuit intendant serving as superintendent of the Ningbo Customs. He had received confidential information that Mi Benchang, manager of the Shanghai Fukang Bank, had slipped back to Ningbo to raise cash. Fukang had two affiliated establishments there, Tongquan Bank and Tongyu Banking House. But business in Ningbo had also been badly depressed by the war in Vietnam, and neither establishment was in a position to assist Fukang. Worse still, Tongquan's manager had gone into hiding and could not be found, while Tongyu's manager had himself requested that his establishment be sealed. Ruiqing had accordingly instructed the magistrate of Yin County to seal Tongyu, and was asking Dexin to notify the proprietors of both banks to settle their affairs without delay. Dexin knew too little about their circumstances to decide what to do, and had therefore felt unable to disclose the telegram. Only after Hu had left did he discuss it with Lianzhu, first asking whether he had been wrong to withhold the news for the time being. “Of course you were!” she replied. “Why did you not tell him then?” “If I had, Xueyan would immediately have asked me to telegraph Ruiqing for help and have Tongquan unsealed. I know nothing whatever about those two banks. The moment they reopen there is bound to be a run. If they cannot withstand it and trouble follows, they will have to be sealed again. What would be the point?”

“你把他看错了，他决不会这么冒昧，让你做为难的事。”莲珠又说：“你说那两家庄号的情形一点都不知道，可是人家原主，知道啊！听他说了，看要不要紧，再想办法。你现在瞒着他不说，又不知道该怎么办，请问怎么回复人家？公事哪有这样子办的？”一顿排揎，将德馨说得哑口无言。“看起来我是没有做对。”他问：“如今该怎么弥补？”“只有我去一趟，去看罗四姐，就说你当时怕胡大先生心境不好，没有敢说，特为要我通知罗四姐，看是要怎么办才妥当。”“好！”德馨答说：“不过也不必今天晚上，明儿一大早好了。”“不！这跟救人一样，耽误不得。”“好吧！那就辛苦你了。”“辛苦小事，你得给我一个底，我才好跟人家去谈。”莲珠又说：“我的意思是你能给他担多少风险？”“这要看他们的情形，譬如说一二十万银子可以维持住的，我就打电报请宁波关代垫，归藩库归还。窟窿太大，可就为难了。”“那么，到底是十万呢？还是二十万？”“二十万吧！”

“You have misjudged him. He would never be so inconsiderate as to put you in an impossible position. You say you know nothing about those two banks, but their owner does! You could have listened to him, decided how serious the situation was, and then looked for a solution. Instead you conceal the news from him and have no idea what to do. How, may I ask, are you going to answer the telegram? Is that any way to conduct official business?” Her scolding left Dexin speechless. “It appears I did the wrong thing,” he admitted. “How can I make it right now?” “I shall have to go and see Luo Sijie. I will say that you were afraid Mr Hu was too distressed to bear the news and did not dare tell him, so you have specially sent me to inform her and discuss what ought to be done.” “Very well,” Dexin answered. “But there is no need to go tonight. First thing tomorrow morning will do.” “No! This is like saving someone's life. We cannot afford to delay.” “All right, then. I am sorry to put you to the trouble.” “The trouble is nothing. But you must give me something definite to work with before I can talk to them. What I mean is, how much risk are you prepared to take for him?” “That depends on their position. If, for example, one or two hundred thousand taels would keep them afloat, I could telegraph the Ningbo Customs and ask them to advance it, with repayment from the provincial treasury. If the hole is too large, it would be difficult.” “Then which is it: a hundred thousand or two hundred thousand?” “Two hundred thousand.”

于是先遣阿福去通知，随后一乘小轿，悄悄将莲珠抬到无主街。其时三更已过，胡雪岩在百狮楼上与螺蛳太太围炉低语，谈的却不是阜康，也不是丝茧，而是年轻时候的往事。这是由扶乩谈起来的，“乌先生接了你回来，你到阜康，他回家，顺路经过一处乩坛，进去看了看，也替我们求了一求，看前途如何？哪晓得降坛的是一位大忠臣，叫什么史可法。乌先生知道这个人，说是当初清兵到扬州时殉难的。”螺蛳太太问道：“老爷，你晓得不晓得这个人？”“听说过。”胡雪岩问：“史可法降坛以后怎么说？”“做了一首诗。喏，”螺蛳太太从梳妆台抽斗中取出一张黄纸，递给胡雪岩说：“你看。”黄纸上写的是一首七绝：“江黑云寒闭水城，饥兵守堞夜频惊，此时自在茅檐下，风雨萧萧听柝声。”胡雪岩将这首诗吟哦数过，方始开口。

A’fu was sent ahead to announce her visit. Then a small sedan chair quietly carried Lianzhu to Yuanbao Street. The third watch had already passed. Upstairs in the Hundred Lions Tower, Hu Xueyan and Madam Luosi sat by the brazier talking softly. Their subject was neither Fukang nor silk and cocoons, but memories of their youth. The conversation had begun with spirit-writing. “After Mr Wu went to meet you, you went on to Fukang and he came home. On the way he passed a spirit-writing altar and went in to have a look. He also asked on our behalf what the future held. Who should descend to the altar but some great loyal minister called Shi Kefa! Mr Wu knew who he was. He said the man had died a martyr when the Qing troops reached Yangzhou. Have you heard of him, Master?” “I have heard the name,” Hu replied. “What did Shi Kefa say after he descended?” “He composed a poem. Here.” Madam Luosi took a sheet of yellow paper from a drawer in her dressing table and handed it to him. “Read it.” On the paper was a seven-character quatrain: “Dark river, cold clouds: the moated city closed; / Hungry soldiers guard the walls, startled again at night. / Now, free from care beneath a thatched roof, / Through sighing wind and rain, I hear the watchman's clapper.” Hu recited it several times before speaking.

“乌先生看了这首诗，有没有给你破解？”“有的。乌先生说，这首诗一定是史可法守扬州的时候做的，情形是很危险，不过为人要学史可法，稳得住！管他后荒马乱，自自在在睡在茅檐下，听风听雨，听城头上打更。”“他人是很稳，不过大明的江山没有稳住。我看这首诗不是这个意思。”“那么，老爷你说，是啥意思。”“那时候史可法手里有几十万人马，可惜史可法不是曾文正、左大人，兵多没有用，真正叫一筹莫展。早知如此，不如不要当元帅、带兵马，做个一品老者姓，肩上没有千斤重担，就困在茅檐下面，自自在在一颗心是安逸的。”胡雪岩声音凄凉地说：“罗四姐，如果当年你嫁了我，我没有同王抚台的那番遭遇，凭我们两个人同心协力，安安稳稳吃一口饱饭，哪里会有今天的苦恼。”由此开始，细数往事，又兴奋、又悲伤，但不管兴奋悲伤都是一种安慰。

“Did Mr Wu explain the poem to you?” “Yes. He said Shi Kefa must have written it while defending Yangzhou. The situation was very dangerous, but we should learn from him to remain steady. No matter how wild the fighting grows, lie at ease beneath the thatch, listening to the wind and rain and to the watch being called on the walls.” “He may have remained steady, but he did not keep the Ming empire standing. I do not think that is what the poem means.” “Then what do you think it means, Master?” “At that time Shi Kefa had several hundred thousand men under his command. Unfortunately he was no Zeng Guofan or Lord Zuo. Numbers were no use; he truly did not know which way to turn. Had he known what was coming, he would have done better never to become a commander or lead an army, but to remain a commoner of the highest order. Without that enormous burden on his shoulders, he could have slept beneath a thatched roof with an easy, untroubled heart.” Hu's voice was desolate. “Luo Sijie, if you had married me in those days, and I had never had those dealings with Governor Wang, the two of us could have worked together and made a quiet, sufficient living. How could we have ended up with the misery we have today?” From there they began recalling the past in detail, sometimes with excitement and sometimes with sadness. Yet whether their memories excited or saddened them, they brought comfort.

正在谈得入神时忽然得报，说莲珠马上要来，不由得都愣住了。莲珠此来，目的何在，虽不可知，但可断定的是，一定出于好意，而且一定有极紧要的事谈。因此，要考虑的是在什么地方接见，胡雪岩应该不应该在场。在这时候，当然不容他们从容商议，螺蛳太太本想在那间专为接待贵客，装饰得金碧辉煌的“藏翠轩”接见，但时已隆冬，即令现搬几个在火盆过去，屋子也一时暖和不起来，所以稍想一想，当机立断地对胡雪岩说：“你先从后楼下去，等一下从前楼上来。”胡雪岩点一点头，匆匆而去。螺蛳太太便下楼亲自接了莲珠上来，一大群丫头围绕着，捧凤凰似地接莲珠安置在靠近火盆的一张安乐椅上，手炉、脚炉、清茶、水果一一送到面前。螺蛳太太顾不得跟她说话，只是指挥着丫头招待客人，直待告一段落，丫头都退了出去，她才开口。“有啥事情，打发人来通知我一声，我去看你就是。这么冷的天，万一冻出病来，叫我们心里怎么过意得去？”“你我不分彼此，与其请你来，多费一层周折，我也仍旧是耽误工夫，倒不如我亲自来一趟。”莲珠四面看了一下问：“胡大先生不在这里？”“去通知他了，马上就会来的，”

They were absorbed in their conversation when word suddenly came that Lianzhu would shortly arrive. Both were taken aback. They could not know her purpose, but they could be certain that it sprang from goodwill and concerned something extremely urgent. What they had to decide was where to receive her and whether Hu should be present. There was no time for leisurely discussion. Madam Luosi first thought of the resplendent Cangcui Pavilion, reserved for distinguished guests. But it was the depths of winter, and even if several large braziers were carried in at once, the room would not warm up quickly enough. After a moment's thought she decided. “Go down by the back stairs first,” she told Hu, “and come up by the front stairs in a little while.” Hu nodded and hurried away. Madam Luosi went downstairs to meet Lianzhu herself and bring her up. A whole cluster of maids surrounded their visitor, attending her as reverently as if she were a phoenix. They settled her in an easy chair close to the brazier and brought hand warmers, foot warmers, tea, and fruit. Madam Luosi was too busy directing the maids to speak to her. Only when everything was ready and the maids had withdrawn did she begin. “Whatever it is, you need only have sent someone to tell me, and I would have gone to see you. In weather as cold as this, if you caught a chill and fell ill, how could we forgive ourselves?” “There is no distinction between you and me. Sending for you would only have added another step and wasted more time. It was better to come myself.” Lianzhu looked about the room. “Is Mr Hu not here?” “They have gone to tell him. He will be here directly.”

“趁胡大先生不在这里，我先跟你说了吧！胡大先生在我们那里，不是来了个电报吗？是宁波打来的，通泉、通裕都出毛病了！我们者爷怕他刚回杭州，心境不好，没有敢告诉他，特为让我来一趟，跟你来谈。”螺蛳太太心里一跳，但不能不强自镇静，“多谢，多谢！”她还要再说下去时，只听楼梯上有脚步声，便停了下来。“老爷来了！”有个丫头掀开门帘说。“罗四姐！”莲珠问说：“要不要当着他的面谈？”“瞒也瞒不住的。”“好！”其时胡雪岩已经衣冠整齐地一路拱手、一路走进来说道：“失迎，失迎！二太太这么晚还来，当然是为我的事，这份情分，真正不知道怎么说了！““自己人不必说这些话。”莲珠说道：“刚刚宁波来的电报，没有拿给你看的缘故，我跟罗四姐说过了，她说不必瞒你，那就请你先看电报。”

“While he is away, let me tell you first. A telegram came while Mr Hu was at our house, did it not? It was from Ningbo. Both Tongquan and Tongyu are in trouble. Our master was afraid that, having only just returned to Hangzhou, he was already too distressed, and did not dare tell him. He has specially sent me to discuss it with you.” Madam Luosi's heart gave a leap, but she forced herself to remain composed. “Thank you, thank you!” She was about to continue when footsteps sounded on the stairs, and she stopped. “The master is here!” a maid announced, lifting the curtain. “Luo Sijie,” Lianzhu asked, “shall we discuss it in front of him?” “We cannot keep it from him in any case.” “Very well.” By now Hu was approaching neatly dressed, saluting with clasped hands as he entered. “Forgive me for not coming out to meet you! If you have come so late, Second Madam, it can only be on my account. I truly do not know how to express my gratitude for such kindness.” “We are all one family; there is no need for that,” Lianzhu said. “I have told Luo Sijie why we did not show you the telegram from Ningbo just now. She says there is no need to conceal it, so please read it first.”

宁波的情形，在胡雪岩真所谓变起不测，因为宓本常在那里，他维持不住上海的阜康，莫非连宁波的“两通”都会撑不起来？但因此使他想到，这或许是宓本常的运用，亦未可知，虽不知他葫芦里卖的什么药？不过有一点是很明显的，宓本常本来就已有“拆烂污”的迹象，如果自己再出头去管宁彼的事，越发会助长他“天塌下来有长人顶”的想法，因此，他觉得如今首要之着，是借重宁波官场的势力，逼一逼宓本常，让他的把所有的力量拿出来。

To Hu, the situation in Ningbo was indeed an unforeseen turn for the worse. Mi Benchang was there. If he could not save the Shanghai Fukang, was he incapable even of keeping Ningbo's two Tong banks afloat? Then another possibility occurred to him: perhaps this was some manoeuvre of Mi's. He did not know what Mi was up to, but one thing was clear. Mi had already shown signs of being prepared to let the whole affair fall to pieces. If Hu now stepped forward to take charge in Ningbo as well, he would only encourage Mi to think that when the sky fell, someone taller would hold it up. The first necessity, Hu decided, was to use the influence of Ningbo's officials to put pressure on Mi and make him employ every resource at his disposal.

于是他说：“不瞒二太太说，这回的事情，总怪我有眼无珠，用错了人。上海阜康的档手叫宓本常，他是宁波人，瞒着我私下同他的亲戚做南北货生意，听说有两条沙船在海里，叫法国兵船打沉了，亏空的是阜康的款子，数目虽然不大，而在目前银根极紧的当口，就显得有关系了。此刻他人在宁波，通泉、通裕的情形，是不是他弄出来的，我不敢说。不过，以他的手面，要维持通泉、通裕是办得到的。藩台肯替我垫二十万银子，实在感激不尽，不过二太太头痛医头，脚痛医脚，说实话，徒然连累好朋友，并不是好办法，做事要做得干净、彻底，我胡某人最好面子，如今面子撕了一条缝，补起来容易，就怕这里弥补了，那面又裂开，所以我现在的想法是，先要保住没有裂开的地方。二太太，请你先替我谢谢藩台，同时请你把我的意思，同藩台说一说。“

“To speak frankly, Second Madam,” he said, “the blame for this affair lies with me for being blind enough to choose the wrong man. The manager of the Shanghai Fukang is called Mi Benchang. He comes from Ningbo. Without telling me, he went into the trade in northern and southern produce with his relatives. I hear that two of their sand junks were sunk at sea by French warships. The money lost belonged to Fukang. The sum was not especially large, but at a time when cash is so desperately scarce, it matters. He is in Ningbo now. I cannot say whether he has deliberately brought about the trouble at Tongquan and Tongyu. But with his connections and resources, he is capable of keeping them going. I am profoundly grateful that the provincial treasurer is willing to advance two hundred thousand taels for me. Yet, Second Madam, treating the head when the head aches and the foot when the foot aches is no real solution. To be frank, it only drags good friends into trouble. A matter must be settled cleanly and thoroughly. No one values his reputation more than I do. Mine has now developed a tear. It would be easy enough to patch, but what I fear is that as soon as one tear is mended, another will appear elsewhere. My present concern, therefore, is to protect the places that have not yet split. Please thank the treasurer for me, Second Madam, and explain my thinking to him.”

听他长篇大套地在谈，莲珠不断点头，表示完全能领会他的意思，等他说完，随即答道：“胡大先生的做法是对的，我一定把你的话，同我们老爷说到，帮你的忙，要从大处去落墨。不过，宁波的事，你还没有说出一个办法来！”“是。”胡雪岩答说：“宓本常在宁波，找到宓本常，就可以责成他来维持。请藩台就照意思拟复电好了。”“如果宓本常不听呢？”莲珠问说：“是不是什么手段都可以用？”这便是说，是否可以拘禁到讯？螺蛳太太对宓本常犹有好感，深恐他吃亏便即说道：“打狗看主人面，他虽做错了事，到底是我们的人。这一点……”她顿住了，不知道该怎么说。“这一点，我们都很明白。不过，人家不知道，电报当中也很难说得清楚。”莲珠想了一下说：“是不是胡大先生请你的师爷拟个稿子，我带回去，请我们老爷照发？”

Throughout his long explanation Lianzhu nodded, showing that she understood him perfectly. When he finished she answered at once, “You are right, Mr Hu. I shall certainly tell our master what you have said. If we are to help you, we must address the larger picture. But you have still not proposed a way of dealing with Ningbo.” “Quite so,” Hu replied. “Mi Benchang is in Ningbo. Once he is found, he can be held responsible for keeping the banks going. Please ask the treasurer to draft his reply along those lines.” “And if Mi Benchang refuses?” Lianzhu asked. “May any means be used?” What she meant was whether he could be detained and brought in for questioning. Madam Luosi still had a good opinion of Mi and feared that he might suffer. “Even when beating a dog, one must consider its master,” she said at once. “He may have done wrong, but he is still one of our people. That point…” She stopped, unsure how to continue. “We understand that very well. But the officials there do not, and it is difficult to make the position clear in a telegram.” Lianzhu thought for a moment. “Why not have your own secretary prepare a draft, Mr Hu? I could take it back and ask our master to send it exactly as written.”

胡雪岩答应着，下楼而去。莲珠目送他走远了，执着螺蛳太太的手，欲言又止，脸上是万般无奈的神情，让螺蛳太太反过来不能不安慰她了。“我晓得你替我们难过，不过，你请放心，不要紧的，船到桥门自会直。”“罗四姐，”莲珠叹口气说：“我同我们老爷，真是恨不得能平空发一笔大财！”“你不要这样子说。”螺蛳太太极其感动，也紧握着她的双手，“我同胡大先生最难过的，也就是连累藩台同你替我们担心。这份人情债，只怕要欠到来生了。”

Hu agreed and went downstairs. Lianzhu watched him go, then took Madam Luosi's hand. She seemed about to speak but checked herself, her face so full of helpless distress that Madam Luosi had to comfort her instead. “I know you are grieving for us. But do not worry. It will be all right. A boat will find its way straight when it reaches the bridge.” “Luo Sijie,” Lianzhu sighed, “our master and I wish we could suddenly come into a great fortune from nowhere!” “Please do not say that.” Deeply moved, Madam Luosi clasped both her hands. “What pains Mr Hu and me most is that we have made you and the treasurer worry on our account. I fear we shall still owe you this debt of kindness in our next life.”

听得这话，莲珠怀然动容，紧盯着她看了好一会，方始问道：“罗四姐，你到底有什么打算？”螺蛳太太愕然，好一会才明白她的意思，“你倒说说看，”她反问一句：“应该怎么个打算？”“我不知道。我总觉得到了这个时候，总应该仔细想一想。罗四姐，”莲珠是极冷静的语气，“我们是自己人，旁观者清，我见到了不能不提醒你。”这话大有文章了，螺蛳太太急急问说：“是不是藩台有什么消息？”“不是他有什么消息，如果他有了什么消息，事情只怕就来不及了。”螺蛳太太心一沉，怔怔地思索了好一会问说：“藩台是不是有什么话？”“话是没有。不过他着急是看得出来的。”迂回吞吐，说了好一会，螺蛳太太方始明白莲珠的意思，是暗示她如果觉得有将财物寄顿他处的必要，她可以效劳。

At these words Lianzhu's expression changed. She looked steadily at Madam Luosi for a long time before asking, “Luo Sijie, what plans have you actually made?” Madam Luosi was startled. It took her some time to grasp what Lianzhu meant. “Tell me, then,” she asked in return, “what plans ought I to make?” “I do not know. I only feel that at a time like this, you ought to think things through very carefully. Luo Sijie”—Lianzhu's tone was perfectly calm—“we are close enough to speak plainly. An onlooker sometimes sees more clearly. When I see something, I cannot help reminding you.” There was much beneath these words. Madam Luosi asked anxiously, “Has the treasurer heard something?” “It is not that he has heard anything. If he had, I am afraid it might already be too late.” Madam Luosi's heart sank. She sat thinking in a daze for some time, then asked, “Has he said anything?” “Nothing. But anyone can see that he is worried.” Only after a good deal of hesitation and indirect talk did Madam Luosi understand. Lianzhu was hinting that if she felt it necessary to place money and valuables elsewhere for safekeeping, Lianzhu would be willing to help.

莲珠一向言辞爽脆深刻，隐微难达之情，在她往往三、五句话，便能直透深处。唯独这件事如此难于出口，其中的道理，在同样善体人情的螺蛳太太；不难明白，正因为交情厚了，才不易措词。因为，要谈这件事，便有一个不忍出口的前提，就是阜康的风潮，会牵连到许多衙门来提公款，倘或无以应付，即可查封财产备抵，而犹不足，不可避免地就会抄家。莲珠一面说，一面心里就有一种顾忌，是设想螺蛳太太听了她的话以后的想法：什么！已经看得我们胡家要抄家了。照此看来是黄鼠狼给鸡拜年，没有存着好心。如果再谈到寄顿财物，似乎坐实了她没有存着好心，胡家抄家于她有什么好处？不就可以吞没了寄存的财物了吗？不但抄家，最好充军、杀头，才能永绝后患。在这佯的顾虑之下，微稍聪明些的人都知道，这不是谈这件事的时候。但象这种寄顿家财，以防籍没的事，时机最要紧，愈早部署愈好。莲珠必是想到了这一点，正见得是为好朋友深谋远虑的打算。

Lianzhu usually spoke with briskness and penetration. Feelings too subtle to express could often be laid bare by her in three or five sentences. That this subject alone should prove so difficult was not hard for Madam Luosi, equally sensitive to human feelings, to understand. Their very closeness made the words harder to find. For to raise the matter was to imply something too cruel to say aloud: the crisis at Fukang might cause numerous government offices to demand their public funds. If Hu could not pay, his property could be sealed as security; if that still proved insufficient, confiscation of the household would inevitably follow. As Lianzhu spoke, she was troubled by the thought of how Madam Luosi might interpret her words: What! Had she already decided that the Hu household was going to be confiscated? In that case, she was like a weasel paying a New Year's call on a hen—her intentions were far from kind. If she then offered to keep their valuables, would that not seem to confirm the suspicion? What could she gain from the confiscation of the Hu estate? Surely she could simply swallow up whatever they had entrusted to her. And confiscation alone would not be enough: exile or execution would be better still, leaving no one to trouble her afterwards. With such considerations in mind, anyone of even modest intelligence would know that this was no time to broach the subject. Yet in arranging safekeeping for family wealth against confiscation, timing was crucial: the earlier the preparations, the better. Lianzhu must have thought of that. Her willingness to speak showed how carefully she was considering her friend's future.

转念到此，螺蛳太太异常感动，“莲姐，不枉我们同烧过一炉香。真正是急难可以倚靠，比同胞还亲的姐妹。”她声音急促他说：“不过，莲姐，我现在只能作我自己的主，我有点首饰，初五那天还要戴，过了这场喜事，我理好了送到你那里去。”这一说莲珠反倒推辞了，她主要的是要提醒螺蛳太太，应该有最坏的打算。如今看她显然已领会到了，那就不必亟亟，“罗四姐，你懂我的意思就好。”她说，“现在也还不到那步田地，不过人无远虑，必有近忧，但愿你们逢凶化吉，遇难成祥，我今天的这番心里的话，完全是多余的。”“莲姐，算命的都说我命中有‘贵人’，你今天就是。但愿如你金口，等这场凤潮过了，莲姐，我们到普陀去烧香，保佑藩台高升抚台，你老来结子，生个白胖儿子。”

When this occurred to Madam Luosi, she was deeply moved. “Sister Lian, we did not burn incense together in vain. You truly are a sister I can rely upon in adversity, closer to me than my own flesh and blood.” She spoke quickly. “But for now I can only dispose of what belongs to me. I have some jewellery. I shall still need to wear it on the fifth. Once the wedding is over, I will put it in order and send it to you.” This time Lianzhu held back. Her main purpose had been to remind Madam Luosi to prepare for the worst. Now that her meaning was clearly understood, there was no need to hurry. “It is enough that you understand me, Luo Sijie,” she said. “Things have not yet come to that. Still, without thought for the future, trouble will soon be at one's door. May every danger turn to good fortune for you, and may all these words from my heart prove entirely unnecessary.” “Sister Lian, the fortune-tellers always said there would be a benefactor in my life. Today, that benefactor is you. May your kind words come true. When this crisis is over, let us go to Putuo to burn incense together. We will pray for the treasurer to be promoted to governor, and for you to bear fruit in your later years and have a fine, plump baby boy.”

“不要说笑话了。”莲珠的脸一红，嗫嚅了好一会说，“不知道你们胡庆余堂，有没有好的调经种子丸？”“有，有！我明天叫人送来。”“不要，不要！”莲珠连连摇手，“传出去笑死人了。”“那么，改天我亲自带来。”于是促膝低语谈了许多房帏间的心得，一直到胡雪岩重新上楼，方始结束。此时此地居然有这样的闲情逸致，且不说螺蛳太太，连莲珠亦觉得是件不可思议的事。

“Do not tease me.” Lianzhu blushed and hesitated for some time before saying, “I wonder whether your Hu Qingyu Hall has any good pills for regulating the monthly courses and promoting conception?” “Yes, indeed! I shall have some sent over tomorrow.” “No, no!” Lianzhu waved both hands. “If word got out, people would laugh themselves to death.” “Then I shall bring them myself another day.” Sitting close together, they went on to exchange a great many private observations about married intimacy, and stopped only when Hu returned upstairs. That they could find leisure for such a conversation at such a moment seemed extraordinary even to Lianzhu, let alone Madam Luosi.

“稿子是拟好了，请二太太看看，有不妥当的地方，再改。”“唷！胡大先生，我哪里看得懂。你说给我听听好了。”“大意是……”大意是告诉宁波关监督瑞庆，说胡雪岩的态度光明磊落，通泉、通裕的倒闭，虽非始料所及，但一定会负责到底，而且以胡雪岩的实力，亦必能转然为安。但阜康受时间的影响，事出无奈，为了维持市面，只可尽力协助，不宜逼迫过急，反生事端。接着提到窗本常在宁彼，希望瑞庆即刻传他到案，责成他料理‘两通’，但所用手段，宜以劝导为主。语气婉转周至，而且暗示瑞庆，若能费心尽力，料理妥当，德馨会面陈巡抚，今年的年终考绩，必有优异的“考语”。“好！好！”莲珠满口答应，“我请我们老爷，马上发出去。”“是！多谢二太太。”“我要走了。”莲珠起身说道：“你们也早点休息，初五办喜事，一定要把精神打起来。

“The draft is ready,” Hu said. “Please look it over, Second Madam, and alter anything that is unsuitable.” “Oh, Mr Hu, how could I make sense of it? Tell me what it says instead.” “The substance of it is…” In substance, it informed Ruiqing, superintendent of the Ningbo Customs, that Hu Xueyan's intentions were open and honourable. Although the failure of Tongquan and Tongyu had taken him by surprise, he would accept responsibility to the end, and his resources were certainly sufficient to turn danger into safety. Fukang's position, however, had been affected by the times, and the present difficulty was unavoidable. To preserve stability in the market, every assistance should be given; excessive pressure might merely provoke further trouble. The telegram then mentioned that Mi Benchang was in Ningbo and requested that Ruiqing summon him at once and hold him responsible for putting the two Tong banks in order, relying chiefly on persuasion. Its tone was tactful and considerate throughout. It also hinted that if Ruiqing would take the necessary trouble and settle the affair satisfactorily, Dexin would report his efforts personally to the governor, ensuring an excellent assessment in that year's official review. “Good, very good!” Lianzhu agreed readily. “I shall ask our master to send it at once.” “Thank you, Second Madam.” “I must be going.” Lianzhu rose. “You should go to bed early too. With the wedding on the fifth, you must both keep your spirits up.”
