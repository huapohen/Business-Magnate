“少棠，你我相交一场，我有力量帮你的时候，没有帮你什么……”“不，不！”周少棠插嘴拦住，“你不要说这话，你帮我的忙，够多了。”“好！我现在还要帮你一个忙，替你好好儿物色一个人。”“大先生！”周少棠笑道：“你现在倒还有闲工夫来管这种闲事？”“正事轮不到我管，有刘抚台、德藩台替我操心，我就只好管闲事了。”满腹牢骚，出以自我调侃的语气，正见得他的万般无奈。周少棠不免兴起一种英雄末路的苍凉之感。再谈下去，说不定会掉眼泪，因而起身告辞。胡雪岩握着他的手臂，仿佛有话要说，却两次欲言又止，终于松开了手说：“再谈吧！”

“Shaotang, we have been friends for so long. When I had the means to help you, I did very little…” “No, no!” Zhou interrupted. “Do not say that. You have helped me more than enough.” “Very well. Now I want to do you another service and find a suitable woman for you.” Zhou laughed. “Mr Hu! At a time like this, you still have leisure to concern yourself with such trifles?” “My serious affairs are no longer mine to manage. Governor Liu and Treasurer De worry about them for me. I can only occupy myself with trifles.” His self-mocking tone, beneath which lay so much resentment, revealed his utter helplessness. Zhou felt the desolation of a hero brought to the end of his road. If they talked much longer, he might weep. He therefore rose to take his leave. Hu grasped his arm as though he had something to say. Twice he almost spoke, then stopped. At last he released his hold. “We shall talk again.”

半夜里叩中门，送进来一封信，说是藩台衙门的专差送来的。螺蛳太太将胡雪岩唤醒了，拿一盏水晶玻璃罩的“洋灯”，让他看信。看不到几行，胡雪岩将信搁下，开口说道：“我要起来。”于是螺蛳太太叫起丫头，点起灯火，拨旺炭盆，服侍胡雪岩起身，他将德馨的信，置在桌上细看。一张八行笺以外，另有一个抄件，字迹较小，需要戴老花眼镜，才看得清楚。

At midnight there was a knock at the inner gate, and a letter was brought in, delivered by a special messenger from the provincial treasurer's yamen. Madam Luosi woke Hu and held a Western lamp with a crystal-glass shade for him to read it. Before he had gone through more than a few lines, he put it down. “I must get up.” She roused the maids, had the lights lit and the brazier stirred, and helped him dress. He laid Dexin's letter on the table for closer examination. Besides the sheet of eight-column stationery, there was a copy of another document, written so small that he needed his reading spectacles.

抄件是一道上谕：“谕内阁：给事中郎承修奏请，责令贪吏罚捐巨款，以济要需一折，据称该给事中所开赃私最著者，如已故总督瑞麟、学政何廷谦、前任粤海关监督崇礼及俊启、学政吴宝恕、水师提督翟国彦、盐运使何兆瀛、肇难道方浚师、广州府知府冯端本、潮州府知府刘湘年、廉州府知府张丙炎、南海县知县杜凤治、顺德县知县林灼之、现任南海县知县卢乐戌，皆自宫广东后，得有巨资，若非民膏，即是国帑等语，着派彭玉麟将各该员在广东居官声名苦何，确切查明，据实具奏。”这跟胡雪岩无关。

It was an imperial edict: “To the Grand Secretariat. Supervising Censor Deng Chengxiu has memorialized, requesting that corrupt officials be ordered to make substantial compulsory contributions to meet urgent needs. He lists as particularly notorious for illicit gains the late Governor-General Ruilin; Education Commissioner He Tingqian; former superintendents of the Guangdong Maritime Customs, Chongli and Junqi; Education Commissioner Wu Baoshu; Naval Commander Zhai Guoyan; Salt Transport Commissioner He Zhaoying; Circuit Intendant Fang Junshi of Zhaonan; Prefect Feng Duanben of Guangzhou; Prefect Liu Xiangnian of Chaozhou; Prefect Zhang Bingyan of Lianzhou; Magistrate Du Fengzhi of Nanhai; Magistrate Lin Zhuozhi of Shunde; and the present Magistrate Lu Lexu of Nanhai. All, he states, acquired great wealth after serving in Guangdong. Such wealth must have come either from the people's substance or from the public treasury. Peng Yulin is hereby directed to ascertain precisely the reputations of these officials during their service in Guangdong and report the facts.” This did not concern Hu.

另有一个附片，就大有关系了：“另片奏：闻阜康银号关闭，协办大学士刑部尚书文煜，所存该号银数至七十余万之多，请即查明确数，究所从来，据实参处等语，着顺天府确查具奏。”接下来再看德馨的亲笔信，只有短短的两行：“事已通天，恐尚有严旨，请速为之计。容面谈。”

The supplementary memorial, however, concerned him very closely: “The supplementary memorial states: ‘I hear that Fukang Bank has closed, and that Wenyu, Assistant Grand Secretary and Minister of Punishments, has more than seven hundred thousand taels deposited there. I request an immediate investigation to ascertain the exact sum and its source, followed by a report seeking appropriate disciplinary action.’ The Shuntian prefectural authorities are hereby ordered to investigate and report.” Dexin's own letter contained only two short lines: “The affair has reached the throne. Further severe orders may follow. Please make preparations without delay. We must discuss this in person.”

“你看！”胡雪岩将信递了给螺蛳太太，“话没有说清楚，‘容面谈’是他来，还是要我去？”“等我来问问看。”螺蛳太太将递信进来的丫头、由镜槛阁调过来的巧珠唤了来，关照她到中门上传话，赶到门房去问，藩司衙门来的专差，是否还在？如果已经走了，留下什么话没有？这得好一陈工夫才会有回话，胡雪岩有点沉不住气了，起身蹀躞，喃喃自语：“严旨，严旨！是革职还是抄家？”螺蛳太太一听吓坏了，但不敢现诸形色，只将一件大毛皮袍，一件贡缎马褂堆在椅子上，因为不管是德馨来，还是胡雪岩去，都要换衣服，所以早早预备在那里。“ ‘速为之计’，怎么‘计’法？”胡雪岩突然住足，“我看我应该到上海去一趟。“为啥？”“至少我要把转运局的公事，弄清楚了，作个交代，不要牵涉到左大人，我就太对不起人了。”“光是为这件事，托七姐夫就可以了。”“不！还有宓本常，我要当面同他碰个头，看看他把上海的帐目，清理得怎么样了。”商议未定之际，只见巧珠急急来报，德馨已经微服来访。胡雪岩急忙换了衣服，未及下楼，已有四名丫头，持着宫灯，前引后拥地将德馨迎上楼来。

“Look!” Hu handed the letter to Madam Luosi. “He has not made himself clear. Does ‘discuss this in person’ mean he is coming here, or that I am to go to him?” “Let me find out.” She called Qiaozhu, the maid who had brought in the letter and who had been transferred from Jingjian Pavilion. Qiaozhu was to send word through the inner gate to the gatehouse and ask whether the treasurer's messenger was still there. If he had gone, had he left any message? It would take some time for an answer to return. Hu could no longer sit still. He paced about, muttering, “Severe orders, severe orders! Dismissal from office, or confiscation of the household?” Madam Luosi was terrified, but dared not show it. She merely laid a heavy fur robe and a tribute-satin riding jacket on a chair. Whether Dexin came to Hu or Hu went to Dexin, he would need to change, and she wanted the clothes ready. “‘Make preparations without delay’—what preparations?” Hu suddenly stopped. “I think I must go to Shanghai.” “Why?” “At the very least I must put the Transport Bureau's official business in order and give a proper account. I cannot let Lord Zuo become involved. I would never forgive myself.” “If that is all, you can ask Brother-in-Law Gu to do it.” “No. There is Mi Benchang as well. I must meet him face to face and see how far he has got with settling the Shanghai accounts.” Before they had reached a decision, Qiaozhu hurried back: Dexin had arrived in plain clothes. Hu changed quickly. Before he could go downstairs, four maids with palace lanterns were already escorting Dexin up, some leading and others following.

胡雪岩在楼梯口迎着，作了一个揖，口中不安地说：“这样深夜，亲自劳步，真正叫我不知道怎么说了！”“自己弟兄，不必谈这些。”德馨进了门，还未坐定，便即说道：“文中堂怕顶不住了。”“文中堂”便是文煜，现任协办大学士刑部尚书，所以称之为“中堂”。他是八旗中有名的殷实大户，发财是在福州将军任上。海内冲要重镇，都有驻防的将军，位尊而权不重，亦谈不到什么入息，只有福州将军例外，因为兼管闽海关，五口通商以后，福州亦是洋商贸易的要地，税收激增，所以成了肥缺，文煜因为是恭王的亲戚，靠山甚硬，在这个肥缺上盘踞了九年之久，及至内调进京，又几次派充崇文门监督，这也是一个日进斗金的阔差，数十年宦囊所积，不下千万之多。在阜康，他是第一个大存户，一方面是利害相共，休戚相关；一方面他跟胡雪岩的交情很厚，所以从阜康出事以后，他一直在暗中支持，现在为邓承修一纸“片奏”所参，纸包不住火，自顾不暇，当然不能再替胡雪岩去“顶”了。“雪岩，”德馨又问：“文中堂真的有那么多款子，存在你那里？”“没有那么多。”胡雪岩答说：“细数我不清楚，大概四五十万是有的。”“这也不少了。”

Hu met him at the head of the stairs and bowed. “To trouble you to come in person at such an hour—I truly do not know what to say!” “We are brothers. There is no need to speak of that.” Dexin had scarcely entered and had not yet settled into his seat when he said, “I am afraid Lord Wen can no longer hold the line.” Lord Wen was Wenyu, the current Assistant Grand Secretary and Minister of Punishments, and was therefore addressed as a grand secretary. He was a famously wealthy man among the Eight Banners. His fortune had been made as Fuzhou Garrison General. Garrison generals were stationed in important strategic cities throughout the empire. Their rank was high, but their power modest, and their income generally unremarkable. Fuzhou was the exception because its general also supervised the Fujian Maritime Customs. After the five treaty ports opened, Fuzhou became a major centre of foreign commerce, customs receipts rose sharply, and the appointment grew lucrative. Related to Prince Gong and backed by powerful protection, Wenyu had occupied this profitable post for nine years. After his transfer to the capital, he had several times been appointed superintendent at Chongwen Gate, another office where wealth poured in by the day. Over decades in office he had accumulated no less than ten million taels. At Fukang he was the largest depositor. His interests were bound up with Hu's, and their personal friendship was close. Since the crisis began, he had supported Hu behind the scenes. Now Deng Chengxiu's supplementary memorial had impeached him. The fire could no longer be wrapped in paper. Preoccupied with protecting himself, he could naturally no longer shield Hu. “Xueyan,” Dexin asked, “does Lord Wen really have so much deposited with you?” “Not that much. I do not know the exact figure, but there must be four or five hundred thousand.” “That is no small sum either.”

“晓翁，”心乱如麻的胡雪岩，终于找到一句要紧话：“你看，顺天府据实奏报以后，朝廷会怎么办？”“照定制来说，朝廷就不会听片面之词，一定是要文中堂明白回奏。”“文中堂怎么回奏呢？”“那就不知道了。”德馨答说：“总不会承认自己的钱，来路不明吧！”“他历充优差，省吃俭用，利上滚利，积成这么一个数目；似乎也不算多。”“好家伙，你真是‘财神’的口吻，光是钱庄存款就有四五十万，还不算多吗？”胡雪岩无词以对，只是在想：文煜究竟会得到怎么一种处分？“文中堂这回怕要倒楣。”德馨说道：“现在清流的气焰正盛，朝廷为了尊重言路，只怕要拿文中堂来开刀。”胡雪岩一惊，“怎么？”他急急问道：“会治他的罪？”“治罪是不会的。只怕要罚他。”“怎么罚？罚款？”“当然。现在正在用兵，军需孔急，作兴会罚他报效饷银。数目多寡就不知道了。”德馨语重心长地警告：“雪岩，我所说的早为之计，第一步就是要把这笔款子预备好。”“哪笔款子？”胡雪岩茫然地问。“文中堂的罚款啊！只要上谕一下来，罚银多少，自然是在他的存款中提的。到那时你就变成欠官款了，而且是奉特旨所提的官款，急如星火，想拖一拖不都不成。”

At last Hu's confused thoughts yielded an essential question. “Mr Xiaofeng, once the Shuntian authorities have reported the facts, what do you think the court will do?” “Under the established procedure, the court will not listen to one side alone. It will certainly order Lord Wen to submit a clear explanation.” “What explanation can he give?” “That I cannot say. He will hardly admit that his money comes from a dubious source.” “He has held profitable posts for years, lived frugally, and let the interest accumulate. A sum like that hardly seems excessive.” “Good heavens, you really do speak like the God of Wealth! Four or five hundred thousand in bank deposits alone, and you call it nothing excessive?” Hu had no answer. He could think only of what punishment Wenyu might receive. “I fear Lord Wen is in for trouble,” Dexin continued. “The Pure Stream group is very much in the ascendant. To show respect for the right of remonstrance, the court may make an example of him.” Hu started. “What? Will he be convicted of an offence?” “Not that. But he may be penalized.” “How? A fine?” “Of course. We are at war, and military funds are urgently needed. He may be ordered to contribute to the war chest as a penalty. I cannot say how much.” Dexin's warning was earnest. “When I urged you to prepare early, Xueyan, the first thing I meant was that you must have this money ready.” “Which money?” Hu asked blankly. “Lord Wen's fine! Once the edict arrives, whatever sum he is required to pay will naturally be drawn from his deposit. Then it becomes public money that you owe—and public money demanded by a special imperial order. It will be as urgent as a spark in dry tinder. You will not be able to put it off for a moment.”

“喔！”胡雪岩心想，要还的公私款项，不下数千万，又何在乎这一笔？但德馨的好意总是可感的，因而答说：“晓翁关爱，我很感激，这笔款子我这回一到上海，首先把它预备好，上谕一到，当即呈缴。”“这才是。”德馨问道：“你预备什么时候动身？”“明天来不及，后天走。”“哪天回来？”“看事情顺手不顺手。我还想到江宁去一趟，看左大人能不能帮我什么忙？”“你早就该去了。”德馨紧接着说：“你早点动身吧！这里反正封典当这件事正在进行，公款也好，私款也好，大家都要看封典当清算的结果，一时不会来催。你正好趁这空档，赶紧拿丝茧脱手，‘讲倒帐’就比较容易。”“讲倒帐”，便是打折扣来清偿。任何生意失败，都是如此料理。但讲倒帐以前，先要准备好现款，胡雪岩一直在等待情势比较缓和，存货就比较能卖得比较好的价钱，“讲倒帐”的折扣亦可提高。但照目前的情势看，越逼越紧，封典当以后，继以文煜这一案，接下来可能会有革职的处分，那时候的身分，一落千丈，处事更加困难，真如德馨所说的，亟应“速为之计”。

“Oh!” Hu thought of the tens of millions he already owed in public and private funds. What difference could this one sum make? Still, Dexin's concern deserved gratitude. “I am very grateful for your care, Mr Xiaofeng. As soon as I reach Shanghai this time, I shall make preparing this sum my first task. When the edict comes, it can be submitted at once.” “That is the right approach. When do you intend to leave?” “I cannot manage tomorrow. The day after.” “When will you return?” “That depends on how smoothly things go. I also want to visit Jiangning and see whether Lord Zuo can help.” “You should have gone long ago,” Dexin said at once. “Leave as soon as you can. The sequestration of the pawnshops is proceeding here. Public and private creditors alike are waiting to see what it yields, and will not press you for the moment. Use the interval to dispose of the silk and cocoons quickly. Then it will be easier to negotiate a reduced settlement.” Such a settlement meant paying debts at a discount, the usual way of dealing with a failed business. But cash had to be ready before negotiations could begin. Hu had been waiting for conditions to ease so that his stocks might fetch a better price and creditors could be offered a larger proportion of what they were owed. Now the pressure was tightening. After the seizure of the pawnshops came Wenyu's case; dismissal from office might follow. If Hu's standing collapsed, everything would become harder. As Dexin said, preparations really must be made without delay.

因此，等德馨一走，胡雪岩跟螺蛳太太重作计议，“箭在弦上，不得不发了。”他说：“有句话叫做‘壮士断腕’，我只有自己斩掉一条膀子，人虽残废，性命可保。你看呢？”“都随你！”螺蛳太太噙着眼泪说：“只要你斩膀子，不叫我来动手。”“虽不叫你来动手，只怕要你在我的刀上加一把劲，不然斩不下来。这一点，你一定要答应我。”螺蛳太太一面流泪，一面点头，然后问道：“ 这回你以上海，预备怎么办？”“我托应春把丝茧全部出清，款子存在汇丰银行，作为讲倒帐的准备金。再要到江宁去一趟。请左大人替我说说话，官款即令不能打折扣，也不要追得那么紧，到底我也还有赚钱的事业，慢慢儿赚了来还，一下子都逼倒了，对公家也没有什么好处。“

After Dexin left, Hu discussed their plans afresh with Madam Luosi. “The arrow is on the bowstring. It must be released,” he said. “There is a saying about a brave man cutting off his wrist. I must cut off one of my own arms. I shall be crippled, but I shall live. What do you think?” “Whatever you decide.” Tears stood in her eyes. “Only cut off the arm yourself. Do not ask me to do it.” “I shall not ask you to wield the blade, but I may need you to add your strength to mine. Otherwise I shall never bring myself to cut. You must promise me that.” Weeping, she nodded, then asked, “What do you intend to do in Shanghai this time?” “Ask Yingchun to sell off all the silk and cocoons and put the proceeds in HSBC as a reserve for negotiating settlements. Then go to Jiangning and ask Lord Zuo to speak for me. Even if the public debts cannot be reduced, they need not be pressed so hard. I still have profitable businesses. Let them earn the money to repay gradually. Driving them all to ruin at once would do the authorities no good either.”

“怎么？”螺蛳太太忽有意会，定神想了一下说：“你是说，譬如典当，照常开门，到年底下结帐，赚了钱，拿来拉还公款，等还清了，二十几家典当还是我们的？“胡雪岩失笑了，“你真是一手只如意、一只手算盘，天下世界哪里有这么好的事？”他说，“所谓‘慢慢儿赚了来还’，意思是赚钱的事业，先照常维持，然后再来估价抵还公款。”“这有啥区别呢？迟早一场空。”螺蛳太太大失所望，声音非常凄凉。“虽然迟早一场空，还是有区别的。譬如说：这家典当的架本是二十万两，典当照常营业，当头有人来赎，可以照二十万两算；倘或关门不做生意了，当头只好照流当价来估价，三文不值两文，决不能算二十万两，不足之数，仍旧要我们来赔，这当中出入很大。这样子一说，你明白了吧？”“明白是明白。不过，”螺蛳太太问道：“能不能留下一点来？”“那要看将来。至少也要等我上海回来才晓得，现在言之过早。”

“Do you mean…” A thought struck her, and she considered it carefully. “For example, the pawnshops would stay open, and at the year-end reckoning their profits would go towards the public debts? Once those were paid, the twenty-odd pawnshops would still be ours?” Hu could not help laughing. “You have a wish-granting sceptre in one hand and an abacus in the other! Where could one find such a wonderful arrangement? By earning the money gradually, I mean keeping the profitable businesses running for the time being, then valuing them and handing them over against the public debts.” “What difference does that make? Sooner or later, everything is gone.” Her disappointment sounded desolate. “There is a difference, even if everything must go in the end. Suppose a pawnshop has capital of two hundred thousand taels. If it continues trading and customers come to redeem their pledges, it can be valued at two hundred thousand. If it closes, the pledges have to be valued at the prices they would fetch as unredeemed goods. What should be worth three cash may not fetch two. The shop certainly cannot be counted at two hundred thousand, and we must still make up the shortfall. That is a considerable difference. Do you understand now?” “I understand. But can we keep a little for ourselves?” “That depends on what happens. We shall know no sooner than my return from Shanghai. It is too early to say.”

螺蛳太太前前后后想了一遍，问出一番极紧要的话来：“从十月底到今天，二十天的工夫，虽然天翻地覆，总当做一时的风波，除了拿老太太搬城外去住以外，别的排场、应酬，不过规模小了点，根本上是没有变。照你现在的打算，这家人家是非拆散不可了？”听得这话，胡雪岩心如刀割，但他向来都是先想到人家，将心比心，知道螺蛳太太比他还要难过，眼泪只是强忍着不让它流下来而已。这样转着念头，便觉得该先安慰螺蛳太太，“我同你总归是拆不散的。”他说，“不但今生今世，来世还是夫妻。”螺蛳太太的强忍着的眼泪，哪禁得起他这样一句话的激荡！顿时热泪滚滚，倚着胡雪岩的肩头，把他的湖绉皮袍湿了一大片。“罗四姐，罗四姐，”胡雪岩握着她的手说：“你也不要难过。荣华富贵我们总算也都经过了，人生在世，喜怒哀乐，都要尝到，才算真正做过人。闲话少说，我同你商量一件事。“这件事，便是遣散姬妾。两个人秘密计议已定，相约决不让第三者知道，包括胡太太在内，都不能知道，只等胡雪岩上海回来，付诸实行。

Having considered everything, Madam Luosi asked a crucial question. “From the end of the tenth month until today, it has been twenty days. Everything has turned upside down, but we have still treated it as a passing storm. Apart from moving the old lady outside the city, our display and entertaining have merely been reduced a little. Nothing fundamental has changed. With the course you are proposing now, must this household really be broken up?” Her words cut Hu to the heart. Yet he always thought of others first. Putting himself in her place, he knew that she suffered still more and was holding back her tears by sheer effort. He felt that he must comfort her. “You and I will never be parted,” he said. “Not only in this life—we shall be husband and wife in the next.” How could the tears she had restrained withstand that assurance? They poured down. Leaning on his shoulder, she soaked a large patch of his Huzhou-crepe fur robe. “Luo Sijie, Luo Sijie.” He held her hand. “Do not grieve. We have had our share of wealth and splendour. To have truly lived, a person must taste joy, anger, sorrow, and pleasure alike. But enough of that. There is something I must discuss with you.” It was the dispersal of his concubines. They settled the matter privately and agreed that no third person—not even Mrs Hu—was to know. They would put the plan into effect only after Hu returned from Shanghai.

“你看，”胡雪岩突然问道：“花影楼的那个，怎么样？”花影楼住的是朱姨太，小名青莲，原是绍兴下方桥朱郎中的女儿。朱郎中是小儿科，只为用药错误，看死了周百万家三房合一的七岁男孩，以致官司缠身，家道中落。朱郎中连气带急，一病而亡，周百万家却还不放过，以至于青莲竟要落入火坑。幸而为胡雪岩看中，量珠聘来，列为第七房姬妾。螺蛳太太不明白他的话，愣了一下问道：“你说她什么怎么样？没头没脑，我从哪里说起？”“我是说她的为人。”“为人总算是忠厚的。”螺蛳太太答说：“到底是郎中的女儿，说话行事，都有分寸。”“你看她还会不会生？”问到这话，螺蛳太太越发奇怪，“怎么？”她问：“你是不是想把她留下来？”“你弄错了。”胡雪岩说：“你光是说她会生不会生好了。”“只要你会生，她就会生。圆脸、屁股大，不是宜男之相？”“好！”胡雪岩说：“周少棠的独养儿子，本来在洋行里做事，蛮有出息的，哪晓得还没有娶亲，一场春瘟死掉了。周少棠今年五十四，身子好得出奇，我想青莲如果跟了他，倒是一桩好事。“

“What do you think of the one in Huaying Tower?” Hu suddenly asked. Huaying Tower housed Mistress Zhu, whose childhood name was Qinglian. She was the daughter of Doctor Zhu of Xiafangqiao in Shaoxing. Her father treated children, but a mistake in prescribing had killed a seven-year-old boy who was the common heir of three branches of the Zhou Millionaire's family. Lawsuits followed, and the doctor's fortunes collapsed. Overwhelmed by anger and anxiety, he fell ill and died. Even then the Zhou family would not relent, and Qinglian was on the point of being forced into prostitution. Fortunately Hu had taken a liking to her, paid a generous marriage settlement, and brought her into his household as his seventh concubine. Madam Luosi was puzzled. “What do you mean, what do I think of her? With no beginning or end to your question, where am I to start?” “I mean her character.” “She is a decent, honest sort. A doctor's daughter, after all. She knows how to conduct herself and what to say.” “Do you think she can still bear a child?” This puzzled Madam Luosi still more. “Why? Are you thinking of keeping her?” “You have misunderstood. Just tell me whether you think she can bear children.” “If you can father one, she can bear one. A round face and broad hips—is that not the look of a woman who will bear sons?” “Good. Zhou Shaotang's only son worked in a foreign firm and had excellent prospects. But before he had even married, a spring epidemic carried him off. Shaotang is fifty-four and remarkably strong. If Qinglian went to him, I think it would be a good thing.”

“你怎么想出来的？”螺蛳太太沉吟了一会说：“好事倒是好事，不过周太太愿意不愿意呢？”“愿意。”胡雪岩答得非常爽脆。“你问过他？”“是啊。不然怎么会晓得？”“这也许是嘴里的话。“不！我同少棠年纪轻的时候，就在一起，我晓得他的为人，有时候看起来油腔滑调，其实倒是实实惠惠的人，对我更不说假话。”“那好。”螺蛳太太说：“不过青莲愿不愿意，就不晓得了。等我来问问她看。”“我看不必问，一问她一定说不愿。”胡雪岩用感慨的声音说：“ ‘夫妻本是同林鸟，大限来时各自飞。’夫妻尚且如此，别的不必说了，到时候，她自会愿意。”

“How did that occur to you?” Madam Luosi pondered. “It might be a good thing, but would Mrs Zhou consent?” “She would,” Hu answered promptly. “Have you asked him?” “Of course. Otherwise how should I know?” “Perhaps that is only talk.” “No. Shaotang and I have known each other since we were young. I know his character. At times he seems glib and flippant, but in fact he is a thoroughly straightforward man, and he would never lie to me.” “Very well. But we do not know whether Qinglian would agree. Let me ask her.” “I do not think you need to. If you ask now, she will certainly refuse.” Hu's voice was full of feeling. “‘Husband and wife are birds of the same forest; when the final calamity comes, each flies away.’ If even spouses are like that, there is no need to speak of anyone else. When the time comes, she will agree of her own accord.”

胡雪岩是早就打算好了的，到了上海，哪里都不住，到城里找了一家小客栈住了下来，为的是隐藏行迹，租界上熟人太多，“仕宦行台”的茶房头脑，更是见多识广，岂能没有见过鼎鼎大名的“胡财神”？所以要遮掩真相，只有隐身在远离租界的小客栈中。安顿既定，派跟班去通知古应春来相会。古应春大出意外，但亦不难体会到胡雪岩的心境，所以尽管内心为他兴起一种英雄末路的凄凉，但见了面神色平静，连“小爷叔为啥住在这里”这么一句话都不问。“七姐怎么样？身子好一点没有？”“还好。”“我的事情呢？”胡雪岩问：“她怎么说？”“她不晓得。”“不晓得？”胡雪岩诧异：“怎么瞒得住？”“多亏瑞香，想尽办法不让她晓得。顶麻烦的是报纸。每天送来的《申报》，我总先要看过，哪一张上面有小爷叔的消息，就把这张报纸收起来，不给她看。”“喔！”胡雪岩透了一口气，心头顿感轻松，他本来一直在担心的是，见了七姑奶奶的面，不知道说什么话来安慰她，现在不必担心了。

Hu had already decided where he would stay in Shanghai. He went nowhere familiar, but found a small inn in the Chinese city and settled there to conceal his movements. He knew too many people in the foreign settlement. The head attendants at the inns catering to officials were particularly well informed; how could they fail to have seen the celebrated God of Wealth? His only way of hiding his circumstances was to disappear into a small inn far from the settlement. Once settled, he sent an attendant to fetch Gu. Gu was astonished, but could readily understand Hu's state of mind. Though inwardly moved by the desolation of a great man at the end of his road, he met him calmly and did not even ask why he was staying there. “How is Seventh Sister? Is her health any better?” “Fairly well.” “And what does she say about my affairs?” “She does not know.” “Does not know?” Hu was surprised. “How have you kept it from her?” “Thanks to Ruixiang. She has tried every possible means of keeping her from hearing. The greatest difficulty is the newspaper. Every day I read Shen Bao first, and if a page carries news of you, I put that sheet away so that she cannot see it.” “Oh!” Hu breathed out and felt immediately easier. He had been worrying about what he could say to comfort the Seventh Lady when they met. Now that concern was lifted.

接下来便谈正事。胡雪岩首先将他所作的“壮士断腕”的决定，告诉了古应春，当然也要问问他的看法。“小爷叔己下了决心，我没有资格来说对不对，我日日夜夜在想的是，怎么样替小爷叔留起一笔东山再起的本钱……”“应春，”胡雪岩打断他的话说：“你不要痴心妄想了。我胡某人之有今天，是天时、地利、人和，再加上两个可遇不可求、可一不可再的机会凑成功的。试问，天时、地利、人各，我还占得到哪一样？就算占全了，也不会再有那样两个机会了。”“小爷叔说的是两个机会是啥？一个大概是西征，还有一个呢？”“还有一个海禁大开。当时懂得跟外国人打交道的，没有几个，现在呢？懂洋务的不晓得有多少，同洋人打交道，做生意，不但晓得他们的行情，而且连洋人那套吃中国人的决窍都学得很精了，哪里还轮得到我来做市面？再说，中国人做生意要靠山。“胡雪岩摇摇头换了个话题，”你说要替我留一笔钱，我只好说，盛情可感，其实是做不到的。因为我的全部帐目都交出去了，象丝茧两样，都有细数，哪里好私下留一部分？“

They turned to business. Hu first told Gu of his decision to sacrifice an arm to save himself, and naturally asked his opinion. “Since you have made up your mind, Little Uncle, it is not for me to say whether you are right. What I think about day and night is how to preserve some capital for you to begin again…” “Yingchun,” Hu interrupted, “do not indulge in impossible hopes. What I achieved depended on favourable times, favourable circumstances, and the support of the right people, together with two opportunities that cannot be sought at will and never come twice. Which of those advantages do I still possess? Even if I had them all, those two opportunities would never return.” “Which two opportunities do you mean? The western campaign must be one. What was the other?” “The opening of the maritime trade. In those days very few people knew how to deal with foreigners. Now countless men understand foreign affairs. In trading with foreigners, they know not only their prices but also all their tricks for exploiting Chinese people. What place is left for me? Besides, a Chinese businessman needs a powerful patron.” Shaking his head, Hu changed the subject. “You say you want to save some money for me. I can only be grateful for the thought; it cannot be done. All my accounts have been handed over. The silk and cocoons are recorded in detail. How could I secretly hold back a portion?”

“办法还是有。”古应春说：“顶要紧的一点是，丝茧两项，小爷叔一定要坚持，自己来处理。”“我懂你的意思。不过现在一步都错不得，东西虽然在我手里，主权已经不是我的了。我们有户头，卖不卖要看刘抚台愿意不愿意，他说价钱不好，不卖，我们没有话说。”“价钱好呢？”“好到怎样的程度？”胡雪岩脱口相问，看古应春不作声，方又说道：“除非价钱好到足抵我的亏空有余，我马上可以收回，自己处理。无奈办不到，只有请刘抚台出面来讲折扣，那就只好由他作主了。”“不过，刘抚台一时也未见得找得到主顾。”“不错，我也晓得他找不到。我原来的打算是，他找不到，就拖在那里，拖它几个月，或者局面好转了，或者洋商要货等不及了，行情翻醒，或放我们可以翻身。不过照目前的情形看，再拖下去，会搞得很难看。”于是胡雪岩将言官参劾，可能由文煜的案子，牵连到他受革职处分的情形细说了一遍，接着又细谈此行的目的。

“There are still ways,” Gu replied. “The essential thing is that you must insist on handling the silk and cocoons yourself.” “I understand what you mean. But I cannot afford one wrong step now. The goods may still be in my hands, but the right to dispose of them is no longer mine. Even with a buyer of our own, the sale depends on Governor Liu's consent. If he says the price is too low and refuses, we have nothing to say.” “And if the price is good?” “How good?” Hu asked at once. Seeing that Gu remained silent, he continued, “Only if the price were high enough to cover my deficit with something to spare could I immediately recover control and handle matters myself. Unfortunately that cannot be done. I can only ask Governor Liu to negotiate reduced settlements, so the decision must be his.” “But Governor Liu may not be able to find a buyer immediately.” “True. I know he cannot. My original idea was to let the matter wait if he found none. After a few months, conditions might improve, or the foreign merchants might become too impatient for supplies. Prices could recover, and perhaps we could regain our footing. But in the present circumstances, further delay may end very badly.” He then explained in detail how the censors' impeachment of Wenyu might implicate him and lead to his own dismissal from office, before describing the purpose of his journey.

“我这趟来，第一件事，就是找丝茧的买主，你有没有？”“有。就是价码上下，还要慢慢儿磨。”“不要磨了。我们以掮客的身分，介绍这生意。刘抚台答应了，佣钱照样也要同他说明。”“那么刘抚台呢？”古应春问：“佣金是不是也要分他一份？”“当然，而且应该是大份。不过，这话不便同他说明，一定要转个弯。”“怎么转法？是不先跟德藩台去谈？”“不错，要先同德晓峰谈。我同他的关系，你是晓得的，既然你有了户头，我们马上打外电报给他。”“这要用密电。”“是的。”胡雪岩说：“临走以前，我同他要了一个密码本，而且约好，大家用化名。”“那就很妥当了。”

“My first business here is to find a buyer for the silk and cocoons. Have you anyone?” “Yes. Only the price still needs some patient bargaining.” “No more bargaining. We shall introduce the transaction as brokers. If Governor Liu agrees, we shall also explain the commission openly.” “And Governor Liu? Is he to have a share of the commission?” “Naturally, and the largest share. But we cannot put that to him directly. We must take a roundabout approach.” “How? Speak to Treasurer De first?” “Exactly. You know my relationship with De Xiaofeng. Since you have a buyer, let us telegraph him at once.” “It must be a confidential cable.” “Yes. Before leaving, I obtained a codebook from him. We also agreed to use assumed names.” “Then everything is in order.”

接下来，古应春便细细地谈了他所接洽的户头，有个法国的巨商梅雅，开的条件比较好。胡雪岩听完以后，又问了付款的办法、担保的银行，认为可以交易，但仍旧追问一句：“比梅雅好的户头还有没有？”“没有。”“好！就是他。”胡雪岩又说：“至于佣金，你的一份要扣下来，我的一份，归入公帐。”“我的也归公帐。”“不必，不必！我是为了显我的诚心诚意，你又何必白填在里头？如果说，折扣打下来，不足之数仍旧要在我身上追，你这样做，让我少一分负担，犹有可说，如今总归是打折了事，你这样做，于我没啥好处，连我都未必见你的情。至于旁人，根本不晓得你不要佣金，就更不用谈了。”“我是觉得我应该同小爷叔共患难……”“好了，好了！你不必再说了。”胡雪岩拿他的话打断，“铜钱掼到水里还听个响声，你这样牺牲了都没有人晓得，算啥？”“好吧！”古应春另外打了主意，不必说破，只问：“电报什么时候打？”

Gu went through the buyers he had approached in detail. A wealthy French merchant named Meiya offered comparatively favourable terms. Having heard them, Hu asked about the method of payment and the bank providing the guarantee. He considered the transaction acceptable, but pressed one further question. “Is there any buyer better than Meiya?” “No.” “Good. Then he is the man. As for the commission, deduct your share. Mine is to go into the general settlement account.” “Mine can go into it too.” “No, no! I am doing it to show my sincerity. Why should you put your money in for nothing? If, after a reduced settlement, I still had to make up the balance, you could reasonably say that your sacrifice lightened my burden. But the whole matter is to be settled at a discount. Your contribution would do me no good. Even I might scarcely appreciate the favour. Others would not know that you had forgone your commission, so there is no question of their gratitude.” “I feel that I ought to share your hardship…” “Enough, enough!” Hu cut him short. “Even a copper coin thrown into the water makes a sound. If you sacrifice this money and no one knows it, what is the point?” “Very well.” Gu formed a different intention but kept it to himself. “When shall we send the telegram?”

“现在就打，你先起个稿子看。”古应春点点头，凝神细想了一会说：“佣金的话，怎么说法？”“这先不必提，你只报个价，叙明付款办法，格外要强调的是，没有比这个价钱更好的了。如果刘抚台有意思，由你到杭州同他当面接头，那时候再谈佣金。”“小爷叔，你自己回去谈，不是更妥当吗？”“不！第一，我要到江宁去一趟；第二，这件事我最好不要插手，看起来置身事外，德晓峰才比较好说话。”“好！我懂了。”于是唤茶房取来笔砚，古应春拟好一个电报稿，与胡雪岩斟酌妥当，然后取出密码本来，两人一起动手，翻好了重新誊正校对，直到傍晚，方始完事。“我马上去发，否则，电报局要关门了。”古应春问：“小爷叔是不是到我那里去吃饭，还是苦中作乐，去吃一台花酒？”“哪里有心思去吃花酒？”胡雪岩说：“我们一起出去逛逛，随便找个馆子吃饭，明天再去看七姐。”“也好。”于是胡雪岩连跟班都不带，与古应春一起出了客栈，先到电报局发了密电，安步当车，闲逛夜市。

“Now. Draft it first and let us look at it.” Gu nodded and thought carefully for a while. “How shall I put the matter of the commission?” “Do not mention it yet. Give only the price and explain the terms of payment. Stress that no better price can be obtained. If Governor Liu is interested, you can go to Hangzhou and discuss it with him in person. That will be the time to speak of the commission.” “Would it not be more suitable for you to go back and negotiate yourself, Little Uncle?” “No. First, I must go to Jiangning. Second, it would be better for me to keep out of this. If I appear uninvolved, De Xiaofeng will find it easier to speak.” “I understand.” They called for the inn attendant to bring writing materials. Gu prepared a draft, which they considered and revised together. They then took out the codebook, encoded the message, copied it afresh, and checked it. By the time they finished, evening had come. “I shall send it at once, or the telegraph office will close,” Gu said. “Will you dine at my house, Little Uncle, or shall we find pleasure in adversity and have a courtesan dinner?” “How could I be in the mood for that? Let us go out for a walk and find somewhere to eat. I shall see Seventh Sister tomorrow.” “Very well.” Leaving even Hu's attendant behind, they went out together, first sent the confidential telegram at the telegraph office, and then walked leisurely through the night market.

英语一综合学习提示
阅读：细辨谁已知、谁未知，谁同意、谁尚未被征询。公文要求调查、人物预测处罚、作者叙述已采取行动，是三种不同证据强度。对青莲意愿及顾客成交的推断尤其不能超出文本。
完形：at the very least 引出最低必要动作，even if / unless 设置交还产权与折扣清偿的条件，naturally 在德推演中并不代表事情已发生。any / all / a portion 与原文总体、全盘和部分留存之别有关。
段落排序：午夜信→德微服到访→谈文煜款风险→夫妻重新处置家业→遣妾具体人选→上海隐居会古→说明不能自行处分→买主与佣金→密电发送。地点、信息递进与说话对象变化共同作衔接。
翻译：the right to dispose of... 是处分权，不要把 dispose of 一概译成丢弃；It is too early to say 强调未定；No sooner than my return... 表最早时间边界，和 no sooner...than... 的刚一就结构不同。
写作：可积累 make preparations without delay、take a realistic view、meet an urgent need、retain the right to decide、give a clear account。可用于决策、信息透明和责任议题，但不把小说中的排斥当事人意愿或分佣安排当作现代写作建议。
