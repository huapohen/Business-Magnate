第九章 少年绮梦｜CHAPTER NINE: YOUTHFUL DREAMS OF LOVE

走过一家小饭馆，胡雪岩止住了脚，古应春亦跟着停了下来。那有饭馆的金字招牌，烟熏尘封，已看不清是何字号，进门炉灶，里面是一间大厅，摆着二三十张八仙桌，此时已将歇市，冷冷清清的，只有两桌客人，灯火黯淡，益显萧瑟。古应春忍不住说：“小爷叔，换一家吧，或者到租界上去，好好找家馆子。这家要打烊了。”“问问看。”说着，举步踏了进去。跑堂的倒很巴结，古应春亦就不好意思打断人家的生意了。“两位客人请坐，吃饭还是吃酒。”“饭也要，酒也要。”胡雪岩问道：“你们这家招牌，是不是叫老同和？”“是的。老同和。”“老板呢？”胡雪岩问：“我记得他左手有六个指头。”“那是我们老老板，去世多年了。”“现在呢？小开变老板了？”“老老板没有儿子，只有一个女儿，现在是我们的老板娘。”“啊！”胡雪岩突然双眼发亮，“你们老板娘的小名是不是叫阿彩？”“原来你这位客人，真正是老客人了。”跑堂的说道：“现在叫得出我们老板娘名字的，没有几个人。”接着，便回过去，高声喊道：“老板娘，老板娘！”看看没有回音，古应春便拦住他说：“不必喊了。有啥好东西，随意配几样来，烫一斤酒。”

Passing a small restaurant, Hu Xueyan stopped, and Gu Yingchun stopped with him. Smoke and dust had so obscured the gold lettering on its sign that the name was no longer legible. The cooking range stood just inside the entrance; beyond it was a large room containing twenty or thirty square tables. The restaurant was about to close. Only two tables were occupied, and the dim lights made the deserted room seem still more forlorn. “Little Uncle, let us try somewhere else,” Gu said. “Or go to the settlement and find a proper restaurant. This one is closing.” “Let us ask.” Hu stepped inside. The waiter was so eager to oblige that Gu could hardly deprive him of their custom. “Please sit down, gentlemen. Will you be eating or drinking?” “Both. Is this place called Laotonghe?” Hu asked. “Yes, sir. Laotonghe.” “Where is the proprietor? I remember he had six fingers on his left hand.” “That was our old proprietor. He died years ago.” “And now? Has the young master taken over?” “He had no son, only a daughter. She is our mistress now.” “Ah!” Hu's eyes suddenly brightened. “Was her childhood name A’cai?” “You really are an old customer! Very few people still know our mistress's name.” The waiter turned and shouted for her. When there was no answer, Gu stopped him. “Never mind calling. Bring us a few of whatever dishes are good, and warm a jin of wine.”

等跑堂离去，胡雪岩不胜感慨地说：“二十多年了！我头一回到上海，头一顿饭就是在这里吃的。”“小爷叔好象很熟嘛！连老板女儿的小名都叫得出来。”“不但叫得出来……”胡雪岩摇摇头，没有再说下去。这种欲言双止的神态，又关涉到一个“女小开”，很容易今人想到，其中必有一段故事。如此寒夜，如此冷店，听这段故事，或者可以忘忧消愁。就这样一转念间，古应春便觉得兴致好得多了。等跑堂端来“本帮菜”的白肉、乌参，一个“糟钵头”的火锅，看到熊熊的青焰，心头更觉温暖，将烫好的酒为胡雪岩斟上一杯，开口说道：“小爷叔，你是什么都看得开的，吃杯酒，谈谈当年在这里的情形。”正落入沉恩中的胡雪岩，啜了一口酒，夹了一块白肉送入口中，咀嚼了一会说：“不晓得是当年老板的手艺好，还是我的胃口变过了，白肉的味道，大不如前。”“说不定两个原因都有。”古应春笑道：“还说不定有第三个原因。”“第三个？”“是啊！当年还有阿彩招呼客人。”“她不管招呼，坐帐台。那时我在杭州钱庄里的饭碗敲破了，到上海来寻生意，城里有家钱庄，字号叫做源利，有个得力的伙计是我一起学生意的师兄弟，我到上海来投奔他，哪晓得为他兄弟的亲事，他回绍兴去了，源利的人说就要回上海的，我就住在一家小客栈里等。一等等了十天，人没有等到。盘缠用光了，只好在小客栈里‘孵豆芽’。”

When the waiter had gone, Hu said with deep feeling, “More than twenty years! The first meal I ever ate in Shanghai was here.” “You seem to know the place well, Little Uncle. You even remember the proprietor's daughter's childhood name.” “More than remember it...” Hu shook his head and said no more. His hesitation, involving a young heiress to a business, readily suggested a story. On such a cold night, in such a chilly, empty restaurant, hearing it might help them forget their cares. The thought lifted Gu's spirits. The waiter brought boiled pork and dark sea cucumber, both local specialties, and a hotpot flavored with wine lees. Its vigorous blue flame warmed Gu's heart as well. He poured Hu a cup of the heated wine. “You have always been able to take things in your stride, Little Uncle. Have a drink and tell me what happened here in those days.” Lost in thought, Hu sipped his wine, put a slice of pork into his mouth, and chewed it for a while. “I cannot tell whether the old proprietor was a better cook or my appetite has changed. This pork tastes nothing like as good.” “Perhaps both,” Gu said, smiling. “There may even be a third reason.” “A third?” “A’cai was here to wait on you then.” “She did not wait on customers. She sat at the accounts desk. I had lost my job at a bank in Hangzhou and came to Shanghai to look for work. A capable assistant at Yuanli Bank, here in the city, had served his apprenticeship with me. I came to ask his help, only to find that he had gone home to Shaoxing for his brother's marriage. The people at Yuanli said he would soon be back, so I took a room in a small inn and waited. Ten days passed without a sign of him. My traveling money ran out, and I could do nothing but ‘sprout beans’ in the inn.”

囊底无钱，一筹莫展，只好杜门不出，上海的俗语叫做“孵豆芽”。但客栈钱好欠，饭不能不吃，他每天到老同和来吃饭，先是一盘白肉、一碗大血汤，再要一样素菜，后来减掉白肉，一汤一素菜，再后来大血汤变为黄豆汤，最后连黄豆汤都吃不起了，买两个烧饼，弄碗白开水便算一顿。“这种日子过了有七、八天，过不下去了。头昏眼花，还在其次，心里发慌，好象马上要大祸临头，那种味道不是人受的。这天发个狠，拿一件线春夹袍子当掉后，头一件事就是到老同和来‘杀馋虫’，仍旧是白肉、大血汤，吃饱惠帐，回到小客栈，一摸袋袋，才晓得当票弄掉了……”“掉在老同和了？”古应春插嘴问说。

To have an empty purse and no way out, and therefore stay shut indoors, was what Shanghai people called “sprouting beans.” His lodging bill could wait, but he had to eat. He had been coming to Laotonghe every day, ordering a plate of boiled pork, a bowl of blood soup, and a vegetable dish. First he gave up the pork, keeping only the soup and vegetables. Then soybean soup replaced the blood soup. Finally, unable to afford even soybean soup, he made a meal of two baked flatbreads and a bowl of hot water. “After seven or eight days of that, I could stand it no longer. The dizziness and blurred eyesight were the least of it. What was worse was the panic, as though some terrible disaster were about to fall on me. It was more than a man could bear. One day I steeled myself and pawned a lined spring robe. The first thing I did was come here to satisfy my cravings. I had the pork and blood soup as before, ate my fill, paid, and went back to the inn. Only when I felt in my pocket did I discover that I had lost the pawn ticket.” “Here at Laotonghe?” Gu interrupted.

“当时还不晓得。不过，也无所谓，掉了就掉了，有钱做新的。”胡雪岩停下来喝口酒，又喝了两勺汤，方又说道：“到第二天，出了怪事，有个十二三岁的伢儿，手里捧个包裹，找到我住的那间房，开口说道：”客人，客人，你的夹袍子在这里。‘一看，这个伢儿是老同和小徒弟。我问他：“哪个叫你送来的？’他说：”客人，你不要问。到我们店里去吃饭，也不要讲我送衣服来给你。‘我说：“为啥？’他说：”你不要问，你到店里也不要说。你一定要听我的话，不然有人会打死我。‘““有这样怪事！”古应春兴味盎然地问：“小爷叔，你总要逼他说实话罗！”“当然。”胡雪岩的声音也很起劲了，“我当时哄他，同他说好话，他就是不肯说，逼得我没法子，只好耍无赖，我说：我不说，我也要打死你，还要拿你当小偷，送你到县衙门去打屁股，你说了实话，我到你店里吃饭，一定听你的话，什么话都不说。两条路，随你自己挑。”

“I did not know that then. Nor did I particularly care. Let it be lost; when I had money, I could have a new robe made.” Hu paused for a drink of wine and a couple of spoonfuls of soup. “The next day, something extraordinary happened. A boy of twelve or thirteen appeared at my room with a bundle. ‘Sir, here is your lined robe!’ I recognized him as the apprentice here. ‘Who sent you?’ I asked. ‘Do not ask, sir. And when you come to eat at our shop, do not say that I brought it.’ ‘Why not?’ ‘Please do not ask or say anything. You must do as I say, or someone will beat me to death.’” “Extraordinary!” Gu said, enthralled. “You must have made him tell you the truth.” “Of course.” Hu's voice grew animated. “I coaxed him and spoke nicely, but he would not tell me. Finally I had to turn bully. ‘If you do not tell me, I shall beat you to death myself. I shall call you a thief and take you to the county yamen to have your backside beaten. Tell me the truth, and when I come to your shop I promise to do as you ask and say nothing. Those are your two choices.’”

“这一来，便把实话逼出来了？”“当然。那个小徒弟叫阿利，是阿彩的表弟，我的夹袍子，就是阿彩叫他送来的。原来……”原来胡雪岩掏钱惠帐时，将当票掉落在地上，至晚打烊，阿利扫地发现，送交帐台。阿彩本就在注意胡雪岩，见他由大血汤吃到黄豆汤，而忽然又恢复原状，但身上却变了“短打”，便知长袍已送入当铺。于是，就悄悄赎了出来，关照阿利送回。特为交代，要守秘密，亦望胡雪岩不必说破，倒不是怕她父亲知道，是怕有人当笑话去讲。“照此说来，阿彩倒真是小爷叔的红粉知己了。”古应春问道：“小爷叔见了她，有没有说破？”“从那天起，我就没有看见她。”胡雪岩说：“当时我脸皮也很薄，见了她又不能还她钱，尴尬不尴尬？我同阿利说：请你代我谢谢你表姐。她替我垫的钱，我以后会加利奉还。”不道此一承诺竟成虚愿。大约一年以后，胡雪岩与杨昌浚重逢，开始创业，偶然想到其事，写信托上海的同业，送了一百两银子到老同和，不道竟碰了一个钉子。“那次是怪我的信没有写对。”胡雪岩解释其中的缘故：“信上我当然不便说明缘故，又说要送给阿利或者女小开阿彩，人家不知道是啥花佯，自然不肯收了。”

“And that brought out the truth?” “Naturally. The apprentice was called A’li. He was A’cai's younger cousin, and she had sent him with the robe. It turned out...” When Hu had taken out his money to pay, the pawn ticket had fallen to the floor. A’li found it while sweeping up at closing time and brought it to the accounts desk. A’cai had already been watching Hu. She had seen his descent from blood soup to soybean soup and his sudden return to his former order, but now he wore a short jacket instead of a long robe. She understood that the robe had gone to the pawnshop. Quietly she redeemed it and told A’li to take it back. Her particular instructions to keep the matter secret, and her hope that Hu would not mention it, came not from fear of her father finding out, but from fear of being made a subject of joking gossip. “Then A’cai was a sympathetic friend indeed,” Gu said. “When you saw her, did you say anything?” “I never saw her again after that day. I was thin-skinned then. How could I face her when I could not repay her? I told A’li, ‘Please thank your cousin for me. I shall return what she advanced, with interest.’” Yet that promise had remained unfulfilled. About a year later, Hu met Yang Changjun again and began his career. Remembering the incident one day, he wrote to a colleague in the Shanghai banking trade, asking him to deliver a hundred taels to Laotonghe, only to be met with a flat refusal. “The fault was in my letter,” Hu explained. “Naturally I could not explain the reason. I merely said to give it to A’li or to the proprietor's daughter, A’cai. They had no idea what was behind it and naturally refused the money.”

“那么，以后呢？小爷叔一直在上海，莫非自己就不可以来一趟？”“是啊！有一回我想起来了，用个红封袋包好一张五百两银子的银票，正要出门，接到一个消息，马上把什么要紧的事，都掼在脑后了。”“什么消息？”古应春猜测着：“不是大坏，就是大好。”“大好！”胡雪岩脱口答说：“杭州光复了。”“那就怪不得了。以后呢？以后没有再想到过？”“当然想到过。可惜，不是辰光不对，就是地方不对。”“这话怎么说。”“譬如半夜里醒过来，在枕头上想到了，总不能马上起床来办这件事，这是辰光不对；再譬如在船上想到了，也不能马上回去办，这是地方不对。凡是这种时候，这种地方想到了，总觉得日子还长，一定可以了心愿。想是这样想，想过忘记，等于不想。到后来日子一长，这件事就想了起来，也是所谓无动于衰了。“古应春深深点着头，“人就是这样子，什么事都要讲机会。明明一定办得到的事，阴错阳差，叫你不能如愿。”他心里在想胡雪岩今日的遭遇，也是一连串阴错阳差的累积，如果不是法国构衅，如果不是左宗棠出军机，如果不是邵友濂当上海道，如果不是宓本常亏空了阜康的款子……这样一直想下去，竟忘了身在何地了。

“And afterward? You were in Shanghai all that time. Could you not have come yourself?” “Yes. Once I remembered, put a draft for five hundred taels into a red envelope, and was just going out when a piece of news arrived. Everything else, however urgent, went clean out of my head.” “What news? It must have been either very bad or very good.” “Very good!” Hu answered at once. “Hangzhou had been recaptured.” “Then one can hardly blame you. But afterward? Did you never think of it again?” “Of course I did. Unfortunately, it was always either the wrong time or the wrong place.” “What do you mean?” “Suppose I woke in the middle of the night and remembered it while lying on my pillow. I could hardly get up and attend to it immediately: the wrong time. Or suppose I remembered while aboard a boat. I could not turn straight back: the wrong place. Whenever it came to mind at such a time or in such a place, I felt there was plenty of life ahead and that I would surely fulfill my wish. That was what I thought, but once I had thought it, I forgot it, which was as good as never having thought it at all. Eventually, so much time had passed that even remembering it no longer stirred me.” Gu nodded deeply. “That is human nature. Everything depends on opportunity. Something that ought to be perfectly possible may still be frustrated by one accident after another.” His thoughts turned to Hu's present misfortunes, themselves the accumulation of a series of unhappy accidents. If the French had not provoked a conflict; if Zuo Zongtang had not left the Grand Council; if Shao Youlian had not become Shanghai Circuit Intendant; if Mi Benchang had not left that deficit in Fukang's funds... Following these thoughts, he quite forgot where he was.

“应春！”古应春一惊，定定神问道：“小爷叔，你说啥？”“我想，今天的辰光、地方都对了，这个机会决不可以错过。”“啊，啊！”古应春也兴奋了，“小爷叔你预备怎么样来补这个情？”“等我来问问看。”当下招一招手，将那伙计唤了来，先问：“你叫啥名字？”“我叫孙小毛。”“喔，”胡雪岩向古应春问道：“你身上有多少洋钱？”“要多少？”“十块。”“有。”古应春掏出十块鹰洋，摆在桌上。“孙小毛，”胡雪岩指着洋钱说：“除了惠帐，另外的是你的。”“客人！”孙小毛睁大了眼，一脸困惑，“你说啥？”“这十块洋钱，”古应春代为回答，“除了正帐，都算小帐。”“喔唷唷！太多，太多，太多了！”孙小毛仍旧不敢伸手。“你不要客气！”胡雪岩说：“你先把洋钱拿了，我还有话同你说。”“这样说，我就谢谢了。客人贵姓？”“我姓胡。”“胡老爷，”孙小毛改了称呼：“有啥事体，尽管吩咐。”

“Yingchun!” Gu started and collected himself. “What did you say, Little Uncle?” “I think both the time and the place are right today. I must not let the opportunity pass.” “Ah, yes!” Gu too grew excited. “How do you intend to repay the kindness?” “Let me make some inquiries.” Hu beckoned the waiter over. “What is your name?” “Sun Xiaomao.” “I see.” Hu turned to Gu. “How many silver dollars have you with you?” “How many do you need?” “Ten.” “I have them.” Gu took out ten Mexican eagle dollars and laid them on the table. Pointing to them, Hu said, “Sun Xiaomao, whatever is left after the bill is yours.” “Sir?” Sun's eyes widened in bewilderment. “What did you say?” Gu answered for Hu. “Take the price of our meal out of these ten dollars. The rest is your tip.” “Oh, my goodness! It is far too much, far too much!” Sun still dared not reach for the money. “Do not stand on ceremony,” Hu said. “Take it first. There is something I want to ask you.” “Then thank you very much, sir. May I ask your name?” “Hu.” “Master Hu,” Sun said, changing his form of address, “whatever you wish, you have only to say.”

“你们老板娘住在哪里？”“就在后面。”“我托你去说一声，就说有个还是二十多年前的老老板的朋友，想同她见个面。”“胡老爷，我们老板在这里。”“也好！先同你们老板谈一谈。”孙小毛手捧十块鹰洋，转身而去，来了这么一个阔客，老板当然忙不迭地来招呼，等走近一看，两个人都有些发愣，因为彼此都觉得面善，却记不起在哪里见过。“你不是阿利？”“你这位胡老爷是……”“我就是当年你表姐叫你送夹袍子的……”“啊，啊！”阿利想起来了，“二十多年的事了。胡老爷一向好？”“还好，还好！你表姐呢？”胡雪岩问道：“你是老板，你表姐是老板娘，这么说，你娶了你表姐？”“不是。”阿利不好意思地说：“是入赘。”“入赘也好，娶回去也好，总是夫妻，恭喜，恭喜！”胡雪岩又问：“有几个伢儿？”“一男一女。”“一男一女一盆花，好极、好极！”胡雪岩转脸向古应春说道：“我这个把月，居然还遇到这样巧的一件事，想想倒也有趣。”

“Where does your mistress live?” “Just behind the shop.” “Would you take her a message? Tell her that a friend of her father's from more than twenty years ago would like to see her.” “Our master is here, Master Hu.” “Very well. Let me speak to him first.” Sun went off carrying the ten eagle dollars. With so lavish a customer in the shop, the proprietor naturally hurried over. As he drew close, both men stared: each found the other's face familiar, yet neither could remember where he had seen it. “Are you not A’li?” “Master Hu, you are...?” “The man your cousin sent you to with the lined robe.” “Ah, of course!” A’li remembered. “More than twenty years ago! Have you been well, Master Hu?” “Quite well, quite well. And your cousin? If you are the proprietor and she is the mistress, you must have married her.” “Not exactly,” A’li said bashfully. “I married into her family.” “Whether you joined her family or brought her into yours, you are husband and wife. Congratulations! How many children?” “A boy and a girl.” “A boy and a girl make a perfect flowering pair. Excellent!” Hu turned to Gu. “After the month or so I have had, to encounter such a coincidence! There is something rather delightful about it.”

看他满脸笑容，古应春也为之一破愁颜，忽然想到两句诗，也不暇去细想情况是否相似，便念了出来：“山穷水尽疑无路，柳暗花明又一村。”这时孙小毛远远喊道：“老板，老板你请过来。”“啥事体，我在陪客人说话。”“要紧事体，你请过来，我同你说一句话。”阿利只好说一声，“对不起，我去去就来。”等他去到帐台边，孙小毛又好奇又兴奋地说：“老板你晓得这位胡老爷是啥人？他就是胡财神。”“胡雪岩？”“是啊！”“哪个说的？”阿利不信，“胡财神多少威风，出来前前后后跟一大班人，会到我老同和来吃白肉？”“是一个刚刚走的客人说的。我在想就是因为老同和，他才进来的。”孙小毛又说：“你倒想想看，正帐不过两把银子，小帐反倒一出手八、九两。不是财神，哪里会有这样子的阔客？““啊！啊！这句话我要听。”阿利转身就走，回到原处，赔笑说道：“胡老爷，我有眼不识泰山，原来你老人家就是胡财神。”“那是从前，现在是‘赤脚财神’了。”“财神总归是财神。”阿利非常高兴地说：“今天是冬至，财神临门。看来明年房了翻造，老同和老店新开，我要翻身了。“他又加了一句：”我们老丈人的话要应验了。““呃！”胡雪岩随口问说：“你老丈人怎么说？”“我老丈人会看相，他说我会遇贵人，四十岁以后会得发，明年我就四十岁了。”

Hu's beaming face dispelled Gu's gloom as well. Two lines of poetry came to him, and without stopping to consider whether they quite suited the occasion, he recited them: “Mountains end, streams run dry; surely there is no road ahead. Then, beyond dark willows and bright blossoms, another village.” At that moment Sun called from a distance, “Master! Please come here!” “What is it? I am talking to our guests.” “Something important. Come here and let me tell you.” A’li could only excuse himself. “I shall be right back.” At the accounts desk, Sun said, full of excitement and curiosity, “Do you know who this Master Hu is? He is Hu, the God of Wealth.” “Hu Xueyan?” “Yes!” “Who told you?” A’li was incredulous. “The God of Wealth goes everywhere in great style, with a whole crowd before and behind him. Would he come to Laotonghe for boiled pork?” “A customer who just left told me. I think he came precisely because this is Laotonghe. Think of it: the meal costs only a small sum, yet he gives eight or nine taels as a tip. Who but the God of Wealth would be so lavish?” “Ah! There is something in that.” A’li went straight back, smiling apologetically. “Master Hu, I failed to recognize greatness when I saw it. So you are the God of Wealth himself!” “I used to be. Now I am the Barefoot God of Wealth.” “The God of Wealth is still the God of Wealth!” A’li said joyfully. “Today is the winter solstice, and he has come to my door. Next year, when we rebuild and reopen Laotonghe, my fortunes are going to change. What my father-in-law said is coming true.” “Oh? What did he say?” Hu asked casually. “He could read faces. He said I would meet a benefactor and prosper after forty. I shall be forty next year.”

胡雪岩算了一下，他初见阿利是在二十七年前，照此算来，那里的阿利只有十三岁，而阿彩至少有十六七岁，记得她长得并不丑，何以会嫁一个十三岁的小表弟？一时好奇心起，便即问：“你表姐比你大几岁？”“大四岁。”阿利似乎猜到了胡雪岩的心思，“阿彩眼界高，高不成，低不就，一直到二十七岁，老姑娘的脾气怪，人人见了都怪她，只有……”他不好意思地笑一笑不肯再说下去了。“只有你不怕？”“不是我不怕。我是从小让她呼来喝去惯了的，脾气好是这样，脾气坏也是这样，无所谓。”阿利停了一下又说：“后来我老丈人同我说：我把阿彩嫁给你，你算我女婿，也算我儿子。你嫌不嫌阿彩年纪大？”“你老丈人倒很开通，很体恤。”胡雪岩问道：“你怎么回答他呢？”“我说，只要阿彩不嫌我年纪小就好了。”胡雪岩与古应春都哈哈大笑，“妙，妙！”胡雪岩说，“再烫壶酒来。”

Hu made a quick calculation. He had first met A’li twenty-seven years earlier, which meant that A’li had been only thirteen then and A’cai at least sixteen or seventeen. He remembered her as by no means plain. Why had she married a little cousin of thirteen? Curious, he asked, “How much older than you is she?” “Four years.” A’li seemed to guess what was in Hu's mind. “A’cai set her sights high. Those above her were out of reach; those below she would not accept. By the time she was twenty-seven, she had developed an old maid's temper. Everyone found her difficult. Only...” He smiled bashfully and stopped. “Only you were not afraid of her?” “It was not that I was unafraid. She had ordered me about since I was a child. Whether she was in a good temper or a bad one, it was all the same to me.” He paused. “Later my father-in-law said, ‘I shall give you A’cai. You will be my son-in-law and my son as well. Would you mind her being older?’” “An enlightened and considerate man, your father-in-law. What did you say?” “I said it would be all right so long as A’cai did not mind my being younger.” Hu and Gu both burst out laughing. “Excellent!” Hu said. “Warm another pot of wine.”

“胡老爷，我看，你如果不嫌委屈，请你同这位古老爷，到我那里坐坐。今天做冬至，阿彩自己做了几样菜，你倒尝尝看。“胡雪岩还未有所表示，古应春已拦在前面，“多谢，多谢！”他说：“辰我晚了，我们还有事，就在这里多谈一息好了。”这话矛盾，既然有事，何以又能多谈？阿利听不出话中的漏洞。胡雪岩却明白，因为他们以前同洋人谈生意、办交涉是合作惯了的，经常使用这种暗带着机关的话，当面传递信息。胡雪岩虽不知道他的本意何在，但暗示必须谢绝，却是很明白的，因而顺着他的语气说：“不错，我们还有要紧事情，明天再说吧！”“那么，明天一定请过来。”阿利又说：“我回去告诉了阿彩，她一定也想见一见胡老爷。”“好，好！”胡雪岩将话题宕开，“你们的房子要翻造了？”“是的。要造马路了。房子前面要削掉一半。不过，地价有补贴的，左邻右舍大家合起来，平房翻造楼房，算起来不大吃亏。”“翻造楼房还要下本钱？”“ 是啊！就是这一点还要想法子。”

“Master Hu, if you would not find it too humble, why not come to our house with Master Gu? It is the winter-solstice festival. A’cai has cooked several dishes herself. You could try them.” Before Hu could answer, Gu spoke. “Thank you very much, but it is getting late and we still have business to attend to. Let us stay and talk here a little longer.” This was inconsistent: if they had business elsewhere, how could they stay and talk? A’li missed the flaw, but Hu understood. In their long partnership negotiating and doing business with foreigners, they had often conveyed messages to one another in such apparently innocent phrases. Hu did not know Gu's exact purpose, but the hint that he must decline was unmistakable. He therefore followed Gu's lead. “Quite right. We still have something important to do. Let us leave it until tomorrow.” “Then you must come tomorrow. Once I tell A’cai, she will certainly want to see you.” “Yes, yes!” Hu turned the conversation aside. “You are going to rebuild?” “Yes. They are putting a road through. Half the front of the building has to go, though there will be compensation for the land. All the neighbors are joining together to replace the single-story houses with upper stories. On balance, we shall not lose much.” “But you will still need money for the rebuilding?” “Yes. That is the difficulty we have to solve.”

“翻造要花多少钱？”“那要看情形。如果拿后面的一块地皮买下来，方方正正成个格局，总要用到一千五百银子。”“你翻造了以后，做啥用场？老店新开，扩大营业？”“想是这样想，要看有没有人合股。”阿利又说：“老店新开，重起炉灶，一切生财都要新置，这笔本钱不小。”“要多少？”“总也要一千五百银子。”“那么，你股东寻着了没有？”“倒有两三个在谈，不过谈不拢。”“为啥？”“合伙做生意，总要合得来才好。”阿利停了一下说：“阿彩不愿意。她说，店小不要紧，自己做老板，自己捏主意，高兴多做，不高兴少做，苦是苦一点，人是自由的。一合了伙，大家意见不合，到后来连朋友都没得做了。““不错！”胡雪岩深深点头，“阿彩的话你要听。”“是啊，没办法，只好听她的话。”

“How much will it cost?” “That depends. If we buy the plot behind us and make the premises a proper, regular shape, it will take fifteen hundred taels.” “And what will you do with the new building? Reopen the old shop on a larger scale?” “That is the idea, if we can find someone to invest. Reopening the old shop means starting afresh. We shall need all new equipment, and that takes quite a sum.” “How much?” “Another fifteen hundred taels.” “Have you found any partners?” “We have been talking to two or three, but we cannot come to terms.” “Why?” “Partners in business have to get along.” A’li paused. “A’cai does not want them. She says a small shop does not matter. When you are your own master, you make your own decisions. You can work more when you please and less when you do not. It is hard work, but you are free. Once you take partners, disagreements come, and in the end you may not even remain friends.” “Quite right!” Hu nodded emphatically. “You should listen to A’cai.” “Yes. There is nothing else for it. I have to listen to her.”

“听她的话才有办法。”古应春接口说了一句，举杯复又放下，从大襟中探手进去，从夹袄表袋中掏出金表，打开表盖来看了看说：“小爷叔，辰光到了。”在看表的这个动作中，胡雪岩便已得到暗示：此时便顺着他的语气对阿利说：“今天晚上我们还有事，辰光到了，明天再来。”“明天来吃中饭。”古应春订了后约：“请你留张桌子。”“有，有！”阿利一叠连声地答应，“胡老爷、古老爷，想吃点啥，我好预备。”“我要吃碗‘带面’。”胡雪岩兴高采烈地说：“拣瘦、去皮、轻面、重洗、盖底、宽汤、免青。”“所谓”带面“便是大肉面，吃客有许多讲究，便是”拣瘦“云云的一套”切口“。胡雪岩并不是真想吃这样一碗面，不过回忆当年贱时的乐事，自然而然地说了出来，而且颇以还记得这一套”切口“而兴起一种无可言喻的愉快。

“Listening to her is precisely what will give you a way forward,” Gu put in. He lifted his cup, set it down again, and reached inside the overlapping front of his coat for the gold watch in his padded jacket's watch pocket. Opening the cover, he glanced at it. “Little Uncle, it is time.” Hu had understood the signal even while Gu was taking out the watch. Following his lead, he told A’li, “We still have business this evening. It is time we went. We shall come again tomorrow.” “For lunch,” Gu added, making the arrangement definite. “Please keep us a table.” “Certainly, certainly!” A’li agreed eagerly. “What would you gentlemen like? I can have it ready.” “I want a bowl of ‘dai noodles,’” Hu said with delight. “Lean meat, no skin, fewer noodles, extra topping, meat at the bottom, plenty of broth, no greens.” These “dai noodles” were noodles with a large piece of pork. Their devotees had a whole vocabulary of special instructions, such as “lean meat” and the rest. Hu did not particularly want such a bowl. Memories of the pleasures of his humble days had brought the words naturally to his lips, and remembering the entire string of terms gave him an inexpressible pleasure.

顺路买了四两好茶叶，古应春陪胡雪岩在小客栈住夜长谈。他们都同意，这是此时此地，为胡雪岩排遣失意无聊的最好法子。“应春，你为啥不愿意到阿彩那里去吃饭？”古应春原以为他能默喻他的深意，不想他还是问了出来，那就不能不提醒他了。“小爷叔，阿彩为啥‘高不成，低不就’？你想想他替你赎那件夹袍子，还不明白？”胡雪岩一愣，回想当时情景，恍然大悟，低徊久久，才说了句：“看起来是‘落花有意，流水无情’。”古应春很少听到胡雪岩这种“文绉绉”的语意说话，不由得笑了，“小爷叔，”他故意开玩笑：“如果你当时娶了阿彩，现在就是老同和的老板，不晓得是不是还有后来的一番事业。”“那就不晓得了。不过，”胡雪岩加重了语气说，“ 如果我是老同和的老板，我一定也会把它弄成上海滩上第一家大馆子。”“这话我相信。”胡雪岩多日无聊，此时突然心中一动，想小施手段，帮阿得来“老店新开”，要轰动一时，稍抒胸中的块垒。但念头一转到阜康，顿时如滚汤沃雪，自觉是可笑的想法。

They bought four liang of good tea on the way, and Gu accompanied Hu back to the little inn for a long conversation that night. Both agreed that, in his present circumstances, this was the best way to relieve Hu's dejection and listlessness. “Yingchun, why did you not want to eat at A’cai's house?” Gu had expected Hu to grasp his deeper meaning without explanation. Since he had asked, however, Gu must point it out. “Why do you suppose A’cai could find no husband good enough for her? Think of her redeeming your robe. Do you still not understand?” Hu was taken aback. Recalling the circumstances, he suddenly saw it. After dwelling on the thought for a long time, he said, “It seems the falling blossom had feelings, but the flowing water had none.” Gu rarely heard such literary language from him and could not help smiling. “If you had married A’cai then,” he teased, “you would be the proprietor of Laotonghe now. Who knows whether you would ever have built your later enterprises?” “Who knows? But,” Hu added emphatically, “if I had become Laotonghe's proprietor, I would certainly have made it the finest restaurant in Shanghai.” “That I believe.” After so many dispiriting days, Hu suddenly felt an impulse to show a little of his old skill. He would help A’li reopen the old shop with a sensation that would relieve some of the frustration in his heart. But the moment he thought of Fukang, the idea melted like snow under boiling water. He saw how ridiculous it was.

看他眼神闪烁、脸上忽热忽冷，古应春大致也能猜到他心里，此时此地，心思决不可旁骛，因而决定提醒他一番。“小爷叔，我刚才的话没有说完，其实到阿彩那里去吃一顿饭，看起来也是无所谓的事，不过，我怕阿彩冷了多少年的一段旧情，死灰复燃，而小爷叔你呢，一个人不得意的时候，最容易念旧，就算不会有笑话闹出来，总难免分你的心。是不是呢？”“是的。”胡雪岩深深点头。“还有，看样子当初阿彩也是不得意才嫁阿利，她总有看得阿利不如意的地方，事隔多年，老夫老妻，也忘记掉了。不过，‘人比人，气煞人’，有小爷叔你一出现，阿利的短处，在阿彩面上又看得很清楚了。”“啊，啊！”胡雪岩很不安地说：“亏得你想到，万一害他们夫妇不和，我这个孽就作得大了。”他停了一下又问：“应春，你说我现在应该怎么办？”古应春想了一下说：“我明白你的意思，要送阿利三千银子。我来替你料理妥当。不过，小爷叔，你明天要搬地方，省得纠缠。”“搬到哪里？”“还是搬到我那里去住，一切方便。”“好！”胡雪岩很爽快地答应下来。

Watching the flicker in Hu's eyes and the changing expression on his face, Gu could roughly guess his thoughts. At such a time, he must not let his mind stray. Gu decided to remind him. “I had not quite finished explaining, Little Uncle. A meal at A’cai's house might seem harmless enough. But I feared that feelings which had lain cold for years might flare up again. As for you, a man in adversity is especially prone to dwell on old affections. Even if nothing happened to set tongues wagging, it could hardly help distracting you. Is that not so?” “Yes.” Hu nodded deeply. “And it seems that disappointment was what led her to marry A’li in the first place. There must have been things about him that dissatisfied her. Years have passed; as an old married couple, they have forgotten them. But comparisons can be maddening. Once you appear, all A’li's shortcomings may become plain to her again.” “Ah!” Hu looked uneasy. “Thank goodness you thought of that. If I caused trouble between them, I should have a great deal to answer for.” He paused. “What do you think I ought to do now?” Gu considered. “I know you want to give A’li three thousand taels. Leave it to me to arrange properly. But you must move tomorrow, to avoid complications.” “Where?” “Come and stay at my house. It will be more convenient in every way.” “Good!” Hu readily agreed.

于是古应春回去安排，约定第二天上午来接。胡雪岩静下来想一想，三千两银子了却当年的一笔人情债，是件很痛快的事，所以这一夜很难得地能够恬然人梦。一觉醒来，漱洗甫毕，古应春倒已经到了。“你倒早。”“想陪小爷叔去吃碗茶。”古应春问道：“昨天晚上睡得好不好。”“交关好，一觉到天亮。”“大概是路上辛苦了的缘故。”“也不光是这一点。”胡雪岩说：“实在说，是你提醒了我，这笔人情债能够了掉，而且干干净净，没有啥拖泥带水的麻烦，我心里很痛快，自然就睡得好了。”“银票我带来了。”古应春又说，“我这么早来，一半也是为了办这件事。请吧，我们吃茶去。”城里吃茶，照常理说，自然是到城隍庙，但胡雪岩怕遇见熟人，古应春亦有这样的想法，所以走到街上，找到一家比较干净的茶馆，也不看招牌，便进去挑张桌子，坐了下来。哪知“冤家路窄”，刚刚坐走便看到阿利进门。吃他们这行饭的，眼睛最尖不过，满面堆笑地前来招呼：“胡老爷！古老爷！”“倒真巧！”古应春说：“请坐，请坐，我本来就要来看你。”“不敢当，不敢当！古老爷有啥吩咐？”古应春看着胡雪岩问：“小爷叔，是不是现在就谈？”“稍微等一等。”阿利自然不知道他们在谈些什么，只很兴奋地告诉胡雪岩：阿彩得知昨夜情形以后，说是“做梦都没有想到”。二十多年前，当掉夹袍子来吃白肉的客人，竟然就是天下无人不知的“胡财神”。真是太不可恩议了。

Gu went home to make the arrangements, promising to collect him the next morning. Left alone, Hu reflected that settling an old debt of kindness with three thousand taels would be a deeply satisfying thing to do. For once he fell asleep in peace. When he awoke and had barely finished washing, Gu arrived. “You are early.” “I thought I would take you out for tea. Did you sleep well?” “Extremely well. Straight through until daylight.” “You must have been tired from the journey.” “It was more than that. The truth is, you showed me how to settle this debt of kindness cleanly, with no troublesome entanglements. That made me happy, and naturally I slept well.” “I have brought the payment. That is part of the reason I came so early. Come, let us have tea.” Normally, a man seeking tea in the Chinese city would go to the City God Temple. But Hu wished to avoid acquaintances, and Gu felt the same way. They found a reasonably clean teahouse on the street and went in without even looking at its sign. Yet chance would not leave them alone. They had barely sat down when A’li entered. People in his line of business had exceptionally sharp eyes, and he came over beaming. “Master Hu! Master Gu!” “What a coincidence!” Gu said. “Sit down. I was just going to see you.” “You are too kind. What can I do for you?” Gu looked at Hu. “Shall we discuss it now, Little Uncle?” “Let us wait a moment.” A’li had no idea what they meant. Excitedly he told Hu that, on hearing what had happened the night before, A’cai had said she could never have dreamed of it: the customer who had pawned his lined robe to eat boiled pork more than twenty years ago had become the universally famous God of Wealth. It was simply beyond belief.

“胡老爷，”阿利又说：“阿彩今天在店里，她是专门来等你老人家，她说她要看看胡老爷比起二十多年前，有啥不同的地方？”“有啥不同？”胡雪岩笑道：“头发白了，皮肤皱了，肚皮鼓起来了。”阿利忽然笑了，笑得很稚气，“胡老爷，”他说：“你不是说你自己，是在说阿彩，头发白了，不多；皮肤皱了，有一点；肚皮鼓起来了，那比胡老爷要大得多。”“怎么？”胡雪岩说：“她有喜了？”“七个月了。”阿利不好意思地笑一笑，得意之情，现于辞色。“恭喜，恭喜！阿利，你明年又添丁、又发财，好好儿做。”胡雪岩站起身来说：“我到街上逛一逛，等下再来。”古应春知道他的用意，将为了礼貌起身送胡雪岩的阿利拉了一把，“你坐下来！”他说，“我有话同你说。”“是！”“啊利，遇见‘财神’是你的运气来了！可惜，稍为晚了一点，如果是去年这时候你遇见胡老爷，运气还要好。”说着，他从身上掏出皮夹子，取出一张花花绿绿的纸头，伸了过来，“阿利，你捏好，胡老爷送你的三千两银子。”

“A’cai is at the shop today especially to wait for you,” A’li continued. “She says she wants to see how you have changed in more than twenty years.” “How have I changed?” Hu laughed. “My hair has turned gray, my skin has wrinkled, and my belly has grown.” A’li suddenly laughed too, with an almost childish air. “You are describing A’cai, not yourself. Gray hair? A little. Wrinkles? A few. But as for her belly, it is much bigger than yours!” “What? Is she expecting?” “Seven months.” A’li smiled bashfully, his pride plain in both voice and face. “Congratulations! Next year you will have another child and prosper in business. Work hard, A’li.” Hu rose. “I shall take a walk and come back presently.” Gu understood his purpose. As A’li stood out of politeness to see Hu off, Gu caught his arm. “Sit down. I have something to tell you.” “Yes, sir.” “Meeting the God of Wealth means your luck has turned, A’li. Only it is a little late. Had you met Master Hu this time last year, your luck would have been even better.” Taking out his wallet, Gu drew forth a brightly printed slip of paper and held it out. “Keep this safely. Master Hu is giving you three thousand taels.”

啊利愣住了！首先是不相信有人会慷慨到萍水相逢，便以巨款相赠的事，不过，“胡财神”的名声，加上昨夜小帐一赏八九两银子，可以改变他原来的想法。但疑问又来了，这位“财神”是真是假？到底是不是胡雪岩？会不会有什么言人的阴谋诡计在内？这最后的一种想法，便只有上海人才有，因为西风东渐以来，上海出现了许多从未见过的花样，譬如保险、纵火烧屋之外，人寿保险亦有意想不到的情节，而且往往是在穷人身上打主意，有人认丐作父，迎归奉养，保了巨额的寿险，然后设计慢性谋杀的法子，致之于死，骗取赔偿。这种“新闻”已数见不鲜，所以阿利自然而然会有此疑虑。不过，再多想一想，亦不至于，因为自问没有什么可以令人凯觎的。但最后的一种怀疑，却始终难释，这张花花绿绿的纸头，是啥名堂？何以能值三千两银子？原来古应春带来的是一张汇丰银行的支票，上面除了行名是中国字以外，其余都是蟹行文。阿利知道钱庄的庄票，却从未见过外国银行的支票，自然困惑万分。古应春当然能够了解他呆若木鸡的原因。事实是最好的说明，“阿利！”他说，“我们现在就到外滩去一趟，你在汇丰照了票，叫他们开南市的庄票给你。”南市是上海县城，有别于北面的租界的一种称呼。

A’li was stunned. At first he could not believe that anyone would give such an immense sum merely upon a chance meeting. Yet the fame of the God of Wealth, together with the eight or nine taels he had casually given as a tip the night before, could overturn A’li's previous assumptions. Then another doubt arose. Was this God of Wealth genuine? Was he really Hu Xueyan? Could there be some trap behind the gift? That last suspicion was particularly a Shanghai man's thought. Since Western influences had reached the city, it had seen all sorts of previously unknown schemes. Insurance had brought not only arson but unforeseen complications in life insurance as well, often involving the poor. Someone might adopt a beggar as his father, bring him home and support him, insure his life for a vast sum, and then arrange his gradual murder to collect the money. Such stories were no longer unusual, and A’li naturally felt apprehensive. On reflection, however, he could see no reason for it: he possessed nothing anyone would covet. One final doubt remained. What was this gaudy piece of paper, and how could it be worth three thousand taels? Gu had brought a cheque drawn on HSBC. Apart from the bank's name, everything on it was written in the foreign script that ran sideways like a crab. A’li knew Chinese bank drafts but had never seen a foreign bank cheque. No wonder he was bewildered. Gu understood his stupefaction perfectly. Facts would explain best. “Let us go to the Bund now. Present it at HSBC and ask them to give you a draft on a bank in the Southern City.” The Southern City meant the Chinese county town of Shanghai, distinguishing it from the settlements to the north.

原来是外国银行的支票，阿利又惭愧，又兴奋，但人情世故他也懂，总要说几句客气话，才是做人的道理，想一想答道：“古老爷，这样大的一笔数目，实在不敢收。请古老爷陪了老爷一起来吃中饭，等阿彩见过了胡老爷再说。”“谢谢你们。胡老爷今天有事，恐怕不能到你们那里吃饭。你先把支票收了，自己不去提，托钱庄代收也可以。”古应春问道：“你们是同哪一家钱庄往来的？”“申福。”“喔，申福，老板姓朱，我也认识的。你把这张票子轧到申福去好了。”这一下越见到其事真实，毫无可疑。但老同和与申福往来，最多也不过两三百两银子，突然轧进一张三千两的支票，事出突兀，倘或申福问到，这张票子怎么来的？应该如何回答？“怎么？”古应春看到他阴阳怪气的神情，有些不大高兴，“阿利，莫非你当我同你开玩笑？”“不是，不是！古老爷，你误会了，说实话，我是怕人家会问。”这一下倒提醒了古应春。原来他替胡雪岩与洋人打交道，购买军火，以及他自己与洋商有生意往来，支付货款，都开外国银行的支票，在钱庄里的名气很大，他的英文名字叫William ，昵称Billy ，那些喜欢“寻开心”的“洋行小鬼”，连他的姓在内，替他起了个谐音的外号叫“屁股”。申福钱庄如果问到这张支票的来历，阿利据实回答，传出去说胡雪岩的钱庄倒了人家的存款，自己依旧大肆挥霍，三千两银子还一个人情债，简直毫无心肝。这对胡雪岩非常不利，不能不慎重考虑。

So it was a cheque on a foreign bank. A’li felt both embarrassed and excited. But he knew enough of social propriety to realize that some polite demurral was necessary. After a moment he said, “It is such an enormous sum, Master Gu. I really dare not accept it. Please come to lunch with Master Hu. Let A’cai see him first, and then we can discuss it.” “Thank you, but he has business today and may not be able to come. Take the cheque first. If you do not wish to collect it yourself, a Chinese bank can do so for you. Which bank do you use?” “Shenfu.” “Shenfu? Its proprietor is Mr Zhu. I know him too. Deposit the cheque there.” This made the gift's reality clearer than ever. Yet Laotonghe's dealings with Shenfu had never exceeded two or three hundred taels. A sudden cheque for three thousand would be extraordinary. What could he say if they asked where it had come from? Seeing his peculiar expression, Gu began to feel annoyed. “Surely you do not think I am joking?” “No, no! You misunderstand. The truth is, I am afraid people will ask questions.” That brought something home to Gu. When buying arms from foreigners for Hu, or paying foreign firms in his own business, he used foreign bank cheques. He was well known among the Chinese banks. His English name was William, shortened to Billy; the mischievous young Chinese clerks in foreign firms had joined this to his surname, Gu, and given him the teasing nickname pigu, “buttocks.” If Shenfu asked about this cheque and A’li answered truthfully, word would spread that Hu's bank had defaulted on people's deposits while he himself continued to squander money, spending three thousand taels to repay a personal kindness. People would call him utterly heartless. This would be extremely damaging to Hu and required careful consideration.

情势有点尴尬，古应春心里在想：人不能倒霉，倒起霉来，有钱都会没法子用。为今之计，只有先把阿利敷衍走了，再作道理。于是他说：“阿利，你先把这张支票拿了。回头我看胡老爷能不能来？能来，一起来，不能来，我一个人一定来。支票是轧到申福，还是到汇丰去提现，等我来了再说。““古老爷，”阿利答说：“支票我决不敢收，胡老爷一定请了来，不然我回去要‘吃排头’。”因为人家已经知道他怕老婆，所以他对可能会挨阿彩的骂，亦无需隐讳了。“好！好！我尽量办到。你有事先请吧！”等阿利殷殷作别而去，胡雪岩接着也回来了，古应春半刚才的那番情形，约为提了一下，表示先将胡雪岩送回家，他另外换用庄票，再单独去赴阿利之约。“不必多跑一趟了。我带了十几张票子在那里，先凑了给他。我们先回客栈。”到得客栈，胡雪岩打开皮包，取出一叠银票，两张一千、两张五百，凑成三千，交到古应春手里时，心头一酸，几乎掉泪——自己开钱庄，“阜康”这块响当当的金字招牌，如今分文不值，要用山西票号的银票给人家，真正是穷途末路了。

The situation was awkward. Gu reflected that a man must not fall on bad times: once he did, he could not spend even the money he still had. For the moment, Gu could only put A’li off and think of another solution. “Take the cheque first,” he said. “I shall see whether Master Hu can come. If he can, we shall both come; if not, I shall certainly come myself. We can decide then whether to deposit it at Shenfu or collect cash at HSBC.” “I really dare not accept it, Master Gu. You must bring Master Hu, or I shall get a scolding when I go home.” They already knew he was afraid of his wife, so he saw no need to conceal the likelihood of being berated by A’cai. “Very well. I shall do my best. Do not let me keep you from your business.” A’li took an earnest leave, and Hu returned soon afterward. Gu briefly explained what had happened. He would first take Hu home, exchange the cheque for Chinese bank drafts, and then visit A’li alone. “There is no need for the extra journey. I have a dozen or more drafts at the inn. We can make up the sum from those. Let us go back there first.” At the inn, Hu opened his leather bag and took out a sheaf of drafts: two for a thousand taels and two for five hundred, making three thousand in all. As he handed them to Gu, grief struck him so sharply that he almost wept. He owned a bank himself; once the name Fukang had carried enormous weight, and now it was not worth a penny. To give someone money, he had to use drafts issued by Shanxi remittance houses. He had truly reached the end of his road.

古应春不曾注意到他的脸色，拿起四张庄票，匆匆而去，在客栈门口，跨上一辆刚从日本传来的“东洋车”，说一声“老同和”，人力车硬橡皮轮子，隆隆然地滚过石板呼，拉到半路，听见有人在叫：“古老爷，古老爷！”一听声音，古应春心想，幸而是来替人还人情，倘或是欠了人家的债，冤家路窄，一上午遇见两次，真是巧了。“停停，停停！”等东洋车在路边停了下来，阿利也就迎上来了。“车钱到老同和来拿。”车夫是阿利认识的，关照了这一句，他转脸对古应春说：“古老爷，我家就在前面弄堂里，请过去坐一坐。胡老爷呢？”“他有事情不来了。”古应春问：“你太太呢？”“现在还在家，等一下就要到店里去了。”古应春心想，在他店里谈件事，难免惹人注目，倒不如去他家的好，于是连连点头：“好！好！我到你家里去谈。”于是阿利领路走不多远，便已到达。他家是半新不旧的弄堂房子，进石库门是个天井，阿利仰脸喊道：“客人来了！”语声甫毕，楼窗中一个中年妇人，探头来望，想必就是阿彩了。古应春不暇细看，随着阿利踏进堂屋，楼梯上已有响声了。“阿彩，赶紧泡茶！”“是你太太？”“叫她阿彩好了。”阿彩下楼，从堂屋后面的一扇门，挺着个大肚子闪了出来，她穿得整整齐齐，脸上薄施脂粉，含笑问道：“这位想来是古老爷？”

Gu did not notice Hu's expression. He took the four drafts and hurried out. At the inn door he climbed into one of the “Japanese carriages” newly introduced from Japan and told the puller, “Laotonghe.” The rickshaw's hard rubber wheels rumbled over the stone paving. Halfway there, he heard someone calling, “Master Gu!” Recognizing the voice, Gu thought how fortunate it was that he was repaying another man's kindness rather than a debt of his own. To meet the same man twice in one morning: what a coincidence! “Stop!” When the rickshaw drew up at the roadside, A’li came over. “Collect your fare at Laotonghe.” He knew the puller. Turning to Gu, he said, “My house is just ahead, in the lane. Please come and sit a while. Where is Master Hu?” “He has business and cannot come. Where is your wife?” “Still at home. She will go to the shop presently.” Discussing the matter at the restaurant might attract attention, Gu thought. Their house would be better. He nodded readily. “Very well. Let us talk at your house.” A’li led him a short distance to a lane house, neither new nor particularly old. Inside its stone-framed gateway was a small courtyard. A’li looked up and called, “We have a guest!” A middle-aged woman leaned out of an upstairs window. She must be A’cai. Gu had no time to look closely before following A’li into the main room, where they could already hear footsteps on the stairs. “A’cai, make some tea!” “That is your wife?” “Just call her A’cai.” She came through a doorway at the back of the room, her pregnant belly prominent. Neatly dressed and lightly powdered, she smiled. “You must be Master Gu.”

“不敢当。”“胡老爷呢？”“有事情不来了。”是阿利代为回答。阿彩脸上浮现出的失望神色，便如许了孩子去逛城隍庙，看变把戏，吃南翔馒头、酒酿圆子，新衣服都换好了，却突然宣布，有事不能去了那样，直可谓之惨不忍睹，以至于古应春不能不将视线避了开去。不过阿彩仍旧能若无其事地尽她做主妇的道理，亲自捧来细瓷的盖碗茶，还开了一罐虽已传到上海，但平常人家很少见的英国“茄力克”纸烟。显然，她是细心安排了来接待胡雪岩的。但如说她是“接财神”，古应春便觉得毫无歉意，探手入杯，将一把银票捏在手里，开口问道：“阿利老板，你贵姓？”“小姓是朱。”“喔，”古应春叫一声：“朱太太，听说你们房子要翻造，扩充门面，胡老爷很高兴，他有三千两银子托我带来送你们。”

“You are too kind.” “Where is Master Hu?” “He has business and cannot come,” A’li answered for him. The disappointment on A’cai's face was like that of a child promised an outing to the City God Temple to watch tricks and eat Nanxiang steamed buns and sweet fermented-rice dumplings, already dressed in new clothes, only to be told that something had come up and the outing was canceled. It was so painful to witness that Gu had to look away. Yet she continued to perform her duties as hostess as though nothing had happened. She personally brought tea in fine porcelain covered cups and opened a tin of English “Jialike” cigarettes, already available in Shanghai but seldom seen in an ordinary household. Clearly she had prepared with great care to receive Hu. If all she wanted, however, was to welcome the God of Wealth, Gu need feel no regret. Reaching inside his coat, he took the drafts in his hand. “May I ask your surname, Mr A’li?” “Zhu.” “Ah. Mrs Zhu, I hear you intend to rebuild and enlarge your premises. Master Hu was delighted. He has asked me to bring you three thousand taels as a gift.”

其实阿彩亦非薄漂母而不为，而是“千金”与“韩信”之间，更看重的是后者。从前一天晚上，得知有此意外机缘之后，她就有种无可言喻的亢奋，絮絮不断地跟阿利说，当时她是如何看得胡雪岩必有出息，但也承认，做梦也没有想到他会创这么一番大事业，而这番大事业又会垮于旦夕之间，因而又生了一种眼看英雄末路的怜惜。这些悲喜交集的复杂情绪夹杂在一起，害得她魂梦不安了一夜。及至这天上午，听阿利谈了他在茶馆中与胡雪岩、古应春不期而遇的经过，以及他对那张汇丰银行支票的困惑，阿彩便嗔怪他处理不当，照她的意思是，这笔巨款尽可不受，但不妨照古应春的意思，先到汇丰银行照一照票，等证实无误，却不必提取，将古应春请到老同和或家里来，只要缠住了古应春，自然而然地也就拉住了胡雪岩。她的判断不错，古应春一定会来，但胡雪岩是否见得到，却很难说，因而患得患失地坐立不安，到此刻她还不肯死心，心里有句话不便说出来：“你三千两银子除非胡老爷亲手送给我我不会收。”就因为有这样一种想法，所以她并未表示坚辞不受，彼此推来让去，古应春渐渐发觉她的本意，但当着阿利，他亦不便说得太露骨，只好作个暗示。“朱太太，”他说：“胡老爷是我的好朋友，他的心境我很清楚，如果早些日子，他会很高兴来同你谈谈当年落魄的情形，现在实在没有这种心情，也没有工夫。你收了这笔银子，让他了掉一桩心事，就是体谅他，帮他的忙；等他的麻烦过去，你们老同和老店新开的时候，我一定拉了他来道喜，好好儿吃一顿酒。”

It was not that A’cai scorned the washerwoman who had once fed the impoverished Han Xin and later accepted his reward. But between the thousand pieces of gold and Han Xin himself, she valued the man more. Ever since hearing of the unexpected encounter the night before, she had felt an indescribable excitement. Over and over she told A’li how she had recognized Hu's promise all those years ago. Even so, she admitted that she had never dreamed he would build so great an enterprise. And to see that enterprise collapse overnight awakened her pity for a hero brought to the end of his fortunes. These mingled feelings of joy and sorrow had disturbed her dreams all night. That morning, when A’li described his chance meeting with the two men and his bewilderment over the HSBC cheque, she reproached him for handling it badly. They need not accept the enormous gift. But he could have followed Gu to HSBC and had the cheque verified, without collecting the money, and then invited Gu to Laotonghe or home. Once they held on to Gu, Hu would naturally be drawn in as well. She was right that Gu would certainly come. Whether she could see Hu was another matter. Torn between hope and fear, she could not sit still. Even now she refused to give up. There was something she could not say aloud: “Unless Master Hu puts the three thousand taels into my own hands, I shall not accept them.” For that reason, her refusal was not absolute. As they exchanged offers and demurrals, Gu gradually understood her real purpose. In A’li's presence, however, he could only hint. “Mrs Zhu, Master Hu is a close friend, and I know his state of mind. A little earlier, he would gladly have come to talk about those poor days. Now he has neither the heart nor the time. Accept this money and let him settle something that weighs on him. That would show consideration and help him. Once his troubles are over and Laotonghe reopens, I shall certainly bring him to congratulate you, and we shall have a good meal together.”

“是的，是的。”阿彩口中答应着，双眼却不断眨动，显然只是随口附和，心中别有念头，等古应春说完，她看着她丈夫说：“你到店里去一趟，叫大司务把菜送了来，请古老爷在家里吃饭。”“不必，不必！”古应春连连摇手，“我有事。多谢！多谢！”“去啊！”阿彩没有理他的话，管自己催促阿利。阿利自然奉命唯谨，说一声：“古老爷不必客气。”掉头就走。这是阿彩特意遣开丈夫，有些心里的话要吐露，“古老爷，”她面色深沉地说：“我实在没有想到，今生今世，还会遇见二十几年前的老客人；更没有想到，当年当了夹袍来吃饭的客人，就是名气这样子大的胡财神。古老爷，不瞒你说，我昨天晚上一夜没有睡着，因为这桩事情，想起来想不完。”说着，将一双眼睛低了下去，眼角微显晶莹，似乎泪水要流出来。古应春当然能体会她的心情，故意不答，他觉得既不能问，更不能劝慰，只要有这样一句话，她的眼泪就会忍不住，惟有保持沉默，才能让她静静地自我克制。果然，停了一会，阿彩复又抬眼，平静地说道：“古老爷，请你告诉胡老爷，我决不能收他这笔钱，第一，他现在正是为难的时候，我收了他的这笔钱，于心不安；第二，我收了他的这笔钱，变成我亏欠他了，也没有啥好想的了。”

“Yes, yes,” A’cai murmured, blinking repeatedly. She was plainly agreeing only out of politeness, with other thoughts in her mind. When Gu finished, she turned to her husband. “Go to the shop and tell the head cook to send the dishes here. Master Gu will eat with us at home.” “No, please!” Gu waved his hands. “I have business. Thank you all the same.” A’cai ignored him. “Go!” she urged A’li. Obedient as ever, he said, “You must not stand on ceremony, Master Gu,” and went out. She had deliberately sent her husband away so that she could speak her heart. “Master Gu,” she said gravely, “I never imagined I would meet that old customer again in this life, after more than twenty years. Still less could I have imagined that the man who pawned his robe for a meal was the famous God of Wealth. To tell you the truth, I did not sleep all night. There seemed no end to the thoughts it brought me.” She lowered her eyes. Moisture glimmered at their corners, as though tears were about to fall. Gu understood her feelings, but deliberately said nothing. He must neither question nor console her: a single word might release her tears. Only silence would allow her to regain control in peace. After a while, she raised her eyes and spoke calmly. “Please tell Master Hu I cannot accept his money. First, he is in difficulties himself. My conscience would trouble me if I took it. Second, if I accepted it, I should become the one indebted to him. There would be nothing left to cherish in my thoughts.”

古应春觉得事态严重了，比他所想象的还要严重，这三千两银子，可能会引起他们夫妇之间的裂痕。转念到此，颇为不安，也深悔自己多事。细细想去，要割断她这一缕从云外飘来的情丝，还是得用“泉刀”这样的利器，于是他说：“朱太太，我说一句不怕你见气的话，如果说，胡老爷现在三千两银子部花不起，你未免太小看他了。朱太太，”古应春将声音压得低低地，同时两眼逼视着她，“我有两句肺腑之言，不晓得你要不要听？”“当然要听。”“只怕我说得太直。”“不要紧，没有旁人在这里。”这表示连阿利不能听的话都能说，古应春便不作任何顾忌了，“朱太太，”他说：“三千两银子，不是一个小数日，而况是号称财神的胡老爷送你的，更何况人家是为了完当年的一笔人情债，送的人光明正大，受的人正大光明。朱老板如果问一句：你为啥不收？请问你怎么同他说？“阿彩根本没有想到阿利，如今古应春提出来一问，才发现自己确有难以交代之处。见她语塞，古应春知道“攻心”已经生效，便穷追猛打地又钉一句：“莫非你说，我心里的那段情，万金不换，三千两算得了什么？”“我当然有我的说法。”这是遁词，古应春觉得不必再追，可以从正面来劝她了。

Gu felt that matters had become serious, more serious than he had imagined. The three thousand taels might open a breach between husband and wife. This troubled him, and he deeply regretted involving himself. On reflection, the sharp blade of money remained the best means of severing this thread of affection that had drifted down from distant clouds. “Mrs Zhu, I hope you will not take offense. If you think Master Hu can no longer afford three thousand taels, you underestimate him.” Lowering his voice and looking straight into her eyes, he continued, “There are a couple of things I should like to say from the heart. Are you willing to hear them?” “Of course.” “I am afraid I may speak too plainly.” “It does not matter. There is nobody else here.” That meant he might say even what A’li must not hear. Gu put aside his remaining scruples. “Three thousand taels is no small sum, Mrs Zhu, especially when it is a gift from the man known as the God of Wealth, given to repay an old kindness. The giver has nothing to conceal, and neither has the recipient. If your husband asks why you will not accept it, what are you going to tell him?” A’cai had not considered A’li at all. Only now did she realize that she would have difficulty explaining herself. Seeing her at a loss for words, Gu knew he had struck home and pressed the point. “Will you say, ‘All the gold in the world could not buy the feeling I cherish. What are three thousand taels to me?’” “Naturally I shall have my own explanation.” It was an evasion. Gu felt there was no need to press further; he could now appeal to her directly.

“不管你怎么说，朱老板嘴里不敢同你争，心里不会相信的。这样子，夫妇之间，就有一道裂痕了。二十几年的夫妇，你肚皮里还有个老来子，有这三千两银子，将老同和老店新开，扩充门面，兴兴旺旺做人家，连你们死掉的老老板，在阴世里都会高兴。这种好日子不过，要自寻烦恼，害得一家人家可能会拆散，何苦？再说，胡老爷现在的环境，几千银子还不在乎，精神上经不起打击，他因为能先还笔人情债，心里很高兴，昨天晚上睡了个把月以来从没有睡过的好觉。倘或晓得你有这种想法，他心里一定不安，他现在经不起再加什么烦恼了。总而言之，你收了这笔银子，让他了掉一桩心事，就是帮他的忙。不然，说得不客气一点，等于存心害他！朱太太，你不是十七八岁的姑娘了，而且有儿有女，闹出笑话来，不好听。”这长篇大套一番话，将想得到的道理都说尽了，阿彩听得惊心动魄，终于如梦方醒似地说了一句：“我收！请古老爷替我谢谢胡老爷。”“对啊！”古应春大为欣慰，少不得乘机恭维她几句：“我就晓得你是有见识、讲道理、顾大局的人。朱太太，照你的面相，真所谓‘地角方圆’，是难得的福相，走到一步帮夫运，着实有一番后福好享。”

“Whatever you say, your husband may not dare argue, but in his heart he will not believe you. That will open a breach between you. You have been married more than twenty years; you are carrying another child in your later years. With these three thousand taels, you can rebuild Laotonghe, expand the business, and make a prosperous home. Even your late father would be pleased in the world below. Why throw away such good days, seek out trouble, and risk breaking up your family? Besides, in Master Hu's present circumstances, a few thousand taels do not matter so much, but emotionally he cannot bear another blow. He was delighted that he could settle this old debt of kindness first. Last night he slept better than at any time in the past month or more. If he knew what was in your mind, it would trouble him, and he cannot stand any further worries now. In a word, accepting the money and relieving him of this concern will help him. Otherwise, to speak bluntly, it is as though you were deliberately trying to hurt him. You are no longer a girl of seventeen or eighteen, Mrs Zhu. You have a son and a daughter. It would not sound well if people began talking.” This long speech exhausted every argument he could think of. A’cai listened, deeply shaken. At last, as though waking from a dream, she said, “I shall take it. Please thank Master Hu for me.” “That is right!” Greatly relieved, Gu could not resist adding some compliments. “I knew you were a woman of judgment and reason, who could see the larger picture. Your face, with its broad, well-rounded lower contours, is the very image of good fortune. When you enter a period that brings prosperity to your husband, there will be a great deal of happiness waiting for you in later life.”

说着，他将捏在手里的一把银票摊开来，三张“蔚丰厚”，一张“百川通”，这两家票号在山西帮中居领袖地位，联号遍布南北，商场中无人不知的。“朱太太，你收好。”“古老爷，其实你给我阜康的票了好了。”阿彩也知道阜康已经在清理，票款能收到几成，尚不可知，所以如此说法，亦依旧是由于一种不愿接受赠款的心理。古应春明白这一点，却正好借此道出胡雪岩的心境。“朱太太，这四张银票，是胡老爷身上摸出来的。不过一个多月以前，阜康的名气比蔚丰厚、百川通响亮得多，而现在，只好用人家的票子了。你倒想，换了你是他，还人啥心思回想当初当了夹袍子来吃白肉的情形？”阿彩爽然若失，慢条斯理地一面理银票，一面说道：“胡老爷自然不在乎这三千银子，不过在我来说，总是无功受禄。”“不是，不是！我想你们在城隍庙听说书，总听过韩信的故事，一饭之恩，千金以报，没有哪个说漂母不应该收。”

He spread out the drafts in his hand: three issued by Weifenghou and one by Baichuantong. Both were leading Shanxi remittance houses, with branches throughout the north and south, known to everyone in commerce. “Keep these safely, Mrs Zhu.” “You could really have given me Fukang drafts instead.” A’cai knew that Fukang was already settling its affairs and that no one yet knew what proportion of a draft's value would be paid. Her remark still sprang from a reluctance to accept the gift. Gu understood, and took the opportunity to explain Hu's feelings. “These four drafts came out of Master Hu's own pocket. Only a little more than a month ago, Fukang's name stood higher than either Weifenghou's or Baichuantong's. Now he has to use other people's paper. Imagine yourself in his place. Would you have any heart to recall the days when you pawned your lined robe for a plate of boiled pork?” A’cai felt a blank sense of loss. Slowly arranging the drafts, she said, “Of course he does not mind the three thousand taels. But for me, it is still a reward I have done nothing to deserve.” “Not at all! Surely you have heard storytellers at the City God Temple tell the story of Han Xin. He repaid a single meal with a thousand pieces of gold. No one ever said the washerwoman should have refused.”

“那，我就算漂母好了，人家问起来……”“喔，喔，”古应春被提醒了，急急打断她的话说：“朱太太，有件事，请你同朱老板一定要当心，千万不好说：胡财神送了你们三千两银子。那一来，人家会说闲话。这一点关系重大，切切不可说出去。千万，千万。”见他如此郑重叮嘱，阿彩自然连连点头，表示充分领会。“古老爷，”阿彩说道：“我晓得你事情忙，不留你吃饭了。不过，古老爷，你要把府上的地址告诉我，改天我要给古太太去请安。”“请安不敢当。内人病在床上，几时你来陪她谈谈，我们很欢迎。”古应春留下了地址，告辞出门，回想经过，自觉做了一件很潇洒的事，胸怀为之一宽。

“Then I shall be the washerwoman. If people ask...” “Ah!” Reminded of something, Gu hastily interrupted. “There is one thing you and your husband must be very careful about. Never say that the God of Wealth gave you three thousand taels. People would gossip. This is extremely important: you must not tell anyone. Please, whatever you do, remember that.” His insistence made A’cai nod repeatedly, showing that she understood completely. “I know you are busy, Master Gu, so I shall not keep you to lunch. But you must give me your address. Another day I should like to call and pay my respects to your wife.” “There is no need for ceremony. My wife is ill in bed. Whenever you can come and talk with her, you will be very welcome.” Gu left his address and took his leave. Looking back over what had happened, he felt he had handled the matter with some elegance, and his heart grew lighter.
