第十三章 烟消云散｜CHAPTER THIRTEEN: VANISHING INTO THIN AIR

胡雪岩谈朱宝如夫妇的故事，话到此处，忽然看着乌先生问道：“你晓不晓得，是哪个抓的朱宝如？”“不是团练局的巡防队吗？”“不是。是他自己。这是一条苦肉计，巡防队的人是串出来的。”胡雪岩说，“朱宝如一抓进去，问起来在我善后局做事，巡防队是假模假样不相信。”“朱宝如就写了张条子给我，我当然派人去保他。等他一保出来，戏就有得他唱了。”据胡雪岩说，他释放之前，向朱家驹、王培利，拍胸担保，全力营救。其时这两个人，已由防巡队私设的“公堂”问过两回，还用了刑，虽不是上“夹棍”或者“老虎凳”，但一顿“皮巴掌”打下来，满嘴喷血，牙齿打掉了好几颗，当然出言恫吓，不在话下——朝廷自平洪杨后，虽有“胁从不问”的恩诏，但太平军的零散败兵，除非投诚有案，倘为私下潜行各处，地方团练，抓到了仍送官处治。因此，朱家驹、王培利惊恐万状，一线生机，都寄托在朱宝如身上，朝夕盼望，盼到第三夭盼到了。

At this point in his story of Zhu Baoru and his wife, Hu Xueyan suddenly looked at Mr Wu. “Do you know who had Zhu Baoru arrested?” “Was it not the militia bureau's patrol?” “No. He did it himself. It was a scheme to win sympathy by suffering along with them; the patrolmen were his accomplices. When he was taken in and said that he worked at my Reconstruction Bureau, they pretended not to believe him. He wrote me a note, and naturally I sent someone to stand surety for him. Once he was out, he could stage the rest of his performance.” According to Hu, before his release Zhu had solemnly promised Jiaju and Wang Peili that he would do everything possible to rescue them. By then they had been questioned twice in the patrol's unauthorized courtroom and tortured. They had not suffered the leg press or the tiger bench, but blows from a leather slapping paddle had left their mouths streaming blood and knocked out several teeth, to say nothing of the threats. After suppressing the Taiping rebellion, the court had issued a merciful decree exempting those coerced into joining it. But unless scattered former soldiers had formally surrendered and been registered, local militias could still arrest them as they slipped secretly from place to place and hand them over for punishment. Terrified, the two men placed their only hope of survival in Zhu Baoru. They waited day and night. On the third day he finally came.

朱宝如告诉他们，全力奔走的结果，可以办个递解回籍的处分，不过要花钱。朱家驹、王培利原有款子在阜康钱庄，存折还在。朱宝如说，这笔存款不必动，他们回到上海仍可支取。至于刘家的房子，出了这件事以后，眼前已经没有用处，不如牺牲定洋，设法退掉，存在阜康的三千银子提出来，在团练局及钱塘、仁和两县，上下打点，大概也差不多了。好在宝藏埋在刘家，地图在他们身边，等这场风波过去，再回杭州，仍旧可以发财。到此境界，朱家驹、王培利只求脱却螺绁，唯言是从。但朱宝如做事，显得十分稳重，带着老婆天天来探监送牢饭，谈到释放一节，总说对方狮子大开口，要慢慢儿磨，劝他们耐心等待。这样，过了有十天工夫，才来问他们两人，说谈妥当了，一切使费在内，两千八百两银子，剩下二百两还可以让他们做路费，问他们愿意不愿意。

Zhu told them that, after all his efforts, he could arrange for them to be sent under escort to their native place, but it would cost money. They still had their own funds at Fukang and their passbooks. Those deposits need not be touched; they could withdraw them when they reached Shanghai. The Liu house, however, was of no immediate use after what had happened. Better to forfeit the deposit, cancel the purchase, and withdraw the three thousand taels held at Fukang for it. That should be about enough to pay everyone who mattered in the militia bureau and the Qiantang and Renhe county offices. Fortunately, the treasure remained buried at the Liu house and they still had the map. Once the trouble had passed, they could return to Hangzhou and make their fortune. Desperate only to escape their bonds, they agreed to everything. Yet Zhu proceeded with great apparent caution. He and his wife visited daily with food, saying that those who could release them were making outrageous demands which must be bargained down. They must wait patiently. About ten days passed before he announced an agreement: all expenses included, twenty-eight hundred taels, leaving two hundred for their journey. Would they agree?

“你们想，”胡雪岩说：“岂有不愿之理。存折的图章在王培利身边，交给朱宝如以后，第二天就‘开笼子’放人了。不过，两个人还要具一张甘结，回籍以后，安分守已，做个良民，如果再潜行各地，经人告发，甘愿凭官法办。”“好厉害！”乌先生说，“这是绝了他们两个人的后路，永远不敢再到杭州。”“手段是很厉害，不过良心还不算太黑。”乌先生又说：“那两个人，叫天天不应，叫地地不灵，如果要他们把存折拿出来，五千银子全数吞没，亦未尝不可。”“不然！朱宝如非要把那张合约收回不可，否则会吃官司。为啥呢？因为从头到底都是骗局，那家的房主，根本不姓刘，孙四也不是‘瓦摇头’，完全是朱宝如串出来的。如果这张合约捏在他们两个人手里，可以转给人家，到了期限，依约付款营业，西洋镜拆穿，朱宝如不但要吃官司，也不能做人了。”“啊，啊！”乌先生深深点头，“这个人很高明。不吞他们的五千银子，放一条路让人家走，才不会出事。”“不但不会出事，那两个人还一直蒙在鼓里，梦想发财。”

“How could they refuse?” Hu said. “Wang Peili handed over the seal for the bank account, and the next day the cage was opened. But both had to sign an undertaking: after returning home, they would live quietly as law-abiding subjects. If they again traveled about in secret and were reported, they agreed to submit to official punishment.” “Ruthless!” Mr Wu said. “That cut off their retreat. They would never dare return to Hangzhou. Still, his conscience was not entirely black. Those two had nobody to appeal to. He could have demanded their passbooks and swallowed the entire five thousand taels as well.” “No. He had to recover the purchase contract or risk a lawsuit. The whole thing was fraudulent. The owner was not named Liu, and Sun Four was no property broker. Zhu had arranged it all. With that contract, the two men could transfer their rights to someone else. When the time came to pay and take possession, the fraud would be exposed. Zhu would face a lawsuit and lose all standing.” “Ah!” Mr Wu nodded deeply. “Very clever. Leaving them their five thousand and a way to live kept him out of trouble.” “More than that. They remained completely in the dark, still dreaming of a fortune.”

“对了！”乌先生问：“严进士家的房子呢？”“我先讲他骗了多少？”胡雪岩扳着手指计算：“房价一共三千四百两，付定洋四百两是孙四的好处，整数三千两听说巡防队分了一千，朱宝如实得二千两，典严家的房子够了。”“典了房子开粥厂？”“是啊！朱宝如来同我说，他看中严家房子的风水，想买下来，不过现在力量不足，只好先典下来，租给善后局办粥厂。他说：”做事情要讲公道，粥厂从第一年十一月办到第二年二月，一共四个月，租金亦只收四个月，每个月一百两。‘我去看了房子，告诉他说，’这样子的房子，租金没有这种行情，五十两一个月都勉强。善后局的公款，我不能乱做人情。不过，我私人可以帮你的忙。“承他的情，一定不肯用我的钱。不过办粥厂当然也有好处。”“那么，掘藏呢？掘到了没有？”“这就不晓得了。这种事，只有他们夫妇亲自动手，不能让外人插手的。不过，朱宝如后来发了财，是真的。“

“And the jinshi's house?” Mr Wu asked. “Let me first tell you how much Zhu got.” Hu counted on his fingers. “The price was thirty-four hundred taels. Sun Four took the four-hundred-tael deposit as his reward. Of the remaining three thousand, I heard the patrol took one thousand and Zhu kept two thousand. That was enough to take Mr Yan's house in pledge.” “And open a soup kitchen there?” “Yes. He came to tell me he liked the house's feng shui and wanted to buy it, but could not yet afford to. He would take it in pledge first, then rent it to the Reconstruction Bureau as a soup kitchen. ‘We must be fair,’ he said. ‘The kitchen will operate from the eleventh month through the second month of the following year, four months altogether. I shall charge rent only for those four months, a hundred taels a month.’ I inspected the house and told him, ‘That is above the going rate. Even fifty a month would be generous. I cannot use public funds to do you favors. But I can help you personally.’ To his credit, he refused my money. Of course, running the soup kitchen had advantages of its own.” “And the treasure? Did he find it?” “That I do not know. Only he and his wife could have dug for it; they could not let outsiders take part. But he certainly became rich afterward.”

“大先生！”乌先生提出一大疑问：“这些情形，你是怎么知道的呢？”“有些情形是孙四告诉我的。他只晓得后半段，严家房子的事，他根本不清楚。”谈到这里，胡雪岩忽然提高了声音说：“若要人不知，除非己莫为。过了有四、五年，有一回我在上海，到堂子里去吃花酒，遇见一个江西人，姓王，他说：胡大先生，我老早就晓得你的大名了，我还是你杭州阜康钱庄的客户。”“不用说，这个人就是王培利了？”“不错。当时他跟我谈起朱宝如，又问起万安桥刘家的房子。我同他说：朱宝如，我同他沾点亲，万安桥刘家，我就不清楚了。”胡雪岩接着又说：“堂子里要谈正经事，都是约到小房间里，躺在烟铺上，清清静静私下谈，席面上豁拳闹酒，还要唱戏，哪里好谈正事？所以我说了一句：有空再变。原是敷衍的话。哪晓得……““他真的来寻你了？”乌先生接口问说。“不是来寻我，是请我在花旗总会吃大菜。帖子上写得很恳切，说有要紧事情请教，又说并无别客。你想想，我应酬再忙，也不能不去……”胡雪岩说，他准时赴约，果然只有王培利一个人。开门见山他说他做过太平军，曾经与朱宝如一起被捕。这下胡雪岩才想起他保释过朱宝如的往事，顿时起了戒心。王培利似乎知道胡雪岩在浙江官场的势力，要求胡雪岩设法，能让他回杭州。“你答应他没有呢？”乌先生插嘴发问。“没有。事情没有弄清楚，我不好做这种冒失的事。”胡雪岩说，“我同他说，你自己具了结的，我帮不上忙，不过，你杭州有啥事情，我可以替你办。他叹口气说，这件事非要我自己去办不可。接下来就把掘藏的事告诉我。我一面听，一面在想，朱宝如一向花样很多，他老婆更是个厉害角色……”

“Mr Hu, how did you learn all this?” Mr Wu asked. “Some of it came from Sun Four. He knew only the latter part, nothing at all about the Yan house.” Hu suddenly raised his voice. “If you do not want a thing known, do not do it! Four or five years later I was at a courtesan banquet in Shanghai when I met a man from Jiangxi named Wang. ‘I have known your great name for years,’ he said. ‘I was even a customer of your Fukang Bank in Hangzhou.’” “Wang Peili, of course?” “Exactly. He mentioned Zhu Baoru and asked about the Liu house at Wan’an Bridge. I told him Zhu was a distant relative, but I knew nothing of that house. In such places, serious matters are discussed privately in a small room, lying on the opium couch. With drinking games, noisy toasts, and singing at the banquet, how can anyone discuss business? So I said, ‘We can talk another time.’ It was merely a polite way of putting him off. Who would have thought...” “He really came to see you?” “He invited me to a Western meal at the American Club. His note was very earnest: an important matter on which he sought my advice, and no other guests. Busy as I was with engagements, I could hardly refuse.” Hu arrived punctually. Wang was indeed alone and began directly by saying that he had served in the Taiping army and been arrested with Zhu Baoru. Only then did Hu remember having secured Zhu's release, and immediately grew wary. Wang seemed aware of his influence among Zhejiang officials and asked him to find a way for him to return to Hangzhou. “Did you agree?” Mr Wu interrupted. “No. I could not act so rashly before understanding the matter. I told him, ‘You signed an undertaking yourself. I cannot help with that. But if you have business in Hangzhou, I can attend to it for you.’ He sighed and said he must do it himself. Then he told me about the treasure. As I listened, I thought of Zhu's many tricks and his still more formidable wife...”

说到这里，乌先生突然发觉螺蛳太太神色似乎不大对劲，便打断了胡雪岩的话问，“罗四姐，你怎么样，人不舒服？”“不是，不是！”螺蛳太太摇着手说：“你们谈你们的。”她看着胡雪岩问：“后来呢？”“后来，他同我说，如果我能想法子让他回杭州掘了藏，愿意同我平分。这时候我已经想到，朱宝如怎么样发的财，恐怕其中大有文章。王培利一到杭州，说不定是要去寻朱宝如算帐，可是，这笔帐一定算不出名堂，到后来说不定会出人命。““出人命？”乌先生想了一下说：“你是说，王培利吃了哑巴亏，会跟朱宝如动刀子？”“这是可以想得到的事。或者朱宝如先下手为强，先告王培利也说不定。总而言之，如果把他弄到杭州，是害了他，所以我一口拒绝。我说我不想发财，同时也要劝你老兄，事隔多年，犯不上为这种渺茫的事牵肠挂肚，如果你生活有困难，我可以帮你忙，替你寻个事情做。他说，他现在做洋广杂货生意，境况过得去，谢谢我，不必了。总算彼此客客气气，不伤感情。““这王培利死不死心呢？”“大概死心了。据说他的洋广杂货生意，做得不错。一个人只要踏上正途，勤勤恳恳去巴结，自然不会有啥发横财的心思。”胡雪岩说：“你们几时见过生意做得象个样子的人，会去买白鸽票？”“这倒是很实惠的话。”乌先生想了一下，好奇地问：“你倒没有把遇见王培利的事，同朱宝如谈一谈？”“没有。”胡雪岩摇摇头，“我从不挖人的痛疮疤的。”

Mr Wu suddenly noticed that Madam Luosi looked unwell and interrupted. “Sister Luo, what is wrong? Are you ill?” “No, no!” She waved her hands. “Go on talking.” Turning to Hu, she asked, “What happened next?” “He offered me half if I could get him back to Hangzhou to dig up the treasure. By then I suspected there was a good deal behind Zhu's fortune. If Wang returned, he might seek a reckoning with Zhu. But he could never obtain satisfaction that way; someone might end up dead.” “Dead?” Mr Wu considered. “You mean that, unable to obtain redress, Wang might take a knife to him?” “It was easy to imagine. Or Zhu might strike first and report him to the authorities. In any event, bringing him to Hangzhou would harm him, so I refused outright. I said, ‘I have no wish to make a fortune this way. And I advise you to stop worrying over such an uncertain prospect after all these years. If you are in difficulties, I can find you work.’ He thanked me but declined. He was trading in foreign and Cantonese sundries and doing reasonably well. At least we remained courteous, with no hard feelings.” “Did Wang give up?” “Probably. I heard his trade was doing well. Once a man finds an honest livelihood and works diligently at it, he naturally stops dreaming of windfalls. Have you ever seen a man with a respectable business buying White Pigeon lottery tickets?” “A very practical observation.” After a moment, Mr Wu asked curiously, “Did you never mention meeting Wang to Zhu?” “No.” Hu shook his head. “I never reopen people's old sores.”

“你不挖人家，人家要挖你。”一直默默静听的螺蛳太太开口了，“ 如果你同朱宝如谈过就好了。”这一说，即使是乌先生都不懂她的意思。连胡雪岩也用困惑的眼光催促她解释。螺蛳太太却无视于此，只是怨责地说：“我们这么多年，这些情形，你从来都没有跟我谈过。”“你这话埋怨得没有道理，朱宝如的事跟我毫不相干，我同你谈它作啥？”胡雪岩又说：“就是我自己的事，大大小小也不知经历过多少，有些事已经过去了，连我自己都记不得，怎么跟你谈？而况，也没有工夫。一个人如果光是谈过去，我看，这个人在世上的光阴，也就有限了。”“着！”乌先生击案称赏：“这句话，我要听。我现在要劝胡大先生的，就是雄心壮志，不可消沉。你的精力还蛮旺的，东山再起，为时未晚。”胡雪岩笑笑不作声。就这时听得寺院中晨钟已动，看自鸣钟上，短针指着四时，已是寅正时分了。“再不睡要天亮了！”胡雪岩说，“明天再谈吧。”于是等丫头们收拾干净，胡雪岩与螺蛳太太向乌先生道声“明朝会”，相偕上楼。

“You do not reopen theirs, but they will reopen yours.” Madam Luosi, who had listened silently all this time, spoke at last. “It would have been better if you had mentioned it to Zhu.” Neither Mr Wu nor Hu understood. Both looked at her for an explanation, but she ignored their questioning eyes and continued reproachfully, “After all these years, you have never told me any of this.” “That is an unfair complaint. Zhu's affairs had nothing to do with me. Why should I have told you? Even of my own experiences, great and small, there have been more than I can count. Some are so far in the past that I have forgotten them myself. How could I tell you? Besides, there was never time. A man who does nothing but talk about the past, I think, has little time left in this world.” “Exactly!” Mr Wu struck the table approvingly. “That is what I like to hear. What I want to tell you now is not to let your ambition die. You still have plenty of energy. It is not too late to make a fresh start.” Hu smiled without answering. Just then the temple's morning bell sounded. The clock's short hand pointed to four: the midpoint of the yin watch. “It will be daylight if we do not go to bed,” Hu said. “We can talk tomorrow.” When the maids had tidied the room, he and Madam Luosi said, “See you tomorrow,” to Mr Wu and went upstairs together.

到了楼上，螺蛳太太还有好些话要跟胡雪岩谈，顶要紧的一件是，十二楼中各房姨太太的私房，经过一整天的检查，收获极丰，现款、金条、珠宝等等，估计不下二三十万银子之多。她问胡雪岩，这笔款子，作何处置？“我没有意见。”胡雪岩说：“现在已经轮不到我作主了。”这句话听起来象牢骚，不过螺蛳太太明了他的本意，“你也不要这样说，现在你还可以作主。”她说：“过两三天，就难说了。”“你说我现在还可以作主，那么，请你替我作个主看。”“要我作主，我现在就要动手。”“怎么动法？”“趁天不亮，请乌先生把这些东西带出去。”螺蛳太太指着一口大箱子说：“喏，东西都装在里面。”“喔！”胡雪岩有些茫然，定定神说：“你刚才怎么不提起？”“现在也还不迟。”胡雪岩重新考虑下来，认为不妥，此举有欠光明磊落，于心不安，因而很歉疚地表示不能同意。“罗四姐，”他说，“我手里经过一百个二三十万都不止，如果要想留下一点来，早就应该筹划了，而且也决不止二三十万。算了，算了，不要做这种事。螺蛳太太大失所望，同时听出胡雪岩根本反对将财物寄顿他处，这就使得她担心的一件事，亦无法跟他谈了。

Upstairs, Madam Luosi still had much to discuss. Most important was the result of a full day's search through the concubines' private possessions in the Twelve Chambers. The yield was considerable: cash, gold bars, jewels, and other valuables estimated at no less than two or three hundred thousand taels. What did Hu want done with it? “I have no opinion. It is no longer for me to decide.” It sounded like a complaint, but she understood his real meaning. “Do not say that. You can still decide now. In two or three days, it may be another matter.” “If I still can, make the decision for me.” “Then I would act immediately.” “How?” “Have Mr Wu take everything away before daylight.” She pointed to a large chest. “It is all in there.” “Oh!” Hu looked bewildered, then collected himself. “Why did you not mention it just now?” “It is not too late.” Considering the matter again, Hu felt it would be wrong. Such concealment lacked the openness and integrity he valued; his conscience would not allow it. Apologetically, he refused. “Sister Luo, more than a hundred times two or three hundred thousand taels has passed through my hands. If I wanted to keep something back, I should have planned it long ago, and it would have been far more than this. Let it go. We must not do such a thing.” She was deeply disappointed. His fundamental objection to leaving valuables elsewhere for safekeeping also made it impossible for her to tell him of another matter that troubled her.

“我真的困了。”胡雪岩说：“明天起码睡到中午。”“你尽管睡。没有人吵醒你。”螺蛳太太等他吃了炖在“五更鸡”上的燕窝粥，服侍他上床，放下帐子，移灯他处，胡雪岩奇怪地问：“你怎么不睡？”“我还有两笔帐要记。你先睡。”“我眼睛都睁不开了！随你，不管你了。”果然，片刻之后，帐子里鼾声渐起，螺蛳太太虽也疲乏不堪，可是心里有事，就是不想上床。当然也不是记什么帐，靠在火盆旁边红丝绒安乐椅上，迷迷糊糊中突然惊醒，只觉一身冷汗。到得清晨，只听房门微响，她睁开酸涩的眼睛，看是阿云蹑着脚走进来。“怎么？”阿云诧异地问，“不上床去睡？”“啥辰光了？”螺蛳太太问。“七点还不到。”“乌先生起来了没有？”“还没有。”“你留心，等乌先生起来，伺候他吃了早饭，你请他等一等，上来叫我。”“晓得了。”阿云取床毛毯为她盖上，随即下楼而去。

“I really am sleepy,” Hu said. “Tomorrow I must sleep until noon at least.” “Sleep as long as you like. Nobody will disturb you.” Madam Luosi waited while he ate the bird's-nest porridge kept warm on the little “fifth-watch cock” stove, helped him into bed, lowered the curtains, and moved the lamp elsewhere. “Why are you not coming to bed?” he asked in surprise. “I still have a couple of accounts to enter. You sleep first.” “I can barely keep my eyes open. Do as you please; I shall leave you to it.” Soon snores came from behind the curtains. Exhausted though she was, Madam Luosi had too much on her mind to go to bed. Nor was she entering accounts. She leaned back in a red-velvet armchair beside the brazier, drifted into a doze, and suddenly woke with a start, drenched in cold sweat. At dawn the door sounded softly. Opening her tired, stinging eyes, she saw A’yun tiptoeing in. “Why have you not gone to bed?” the maid asked in surprise. “What time is it?” “Not quite seven.” “Is Mr Wu up?” “Not yet.” “Watch for him. When he gets up, see that he has breakfast, ask him to wait, and come and call me.” “Yes, Madam.” A’yun covered her with a blanket and went downstairs.

一半是累了，一半是想到乌先生，浮起了解消心事的希望，螺蛳太太居然蜷缩在安乐椅上，好好睡了一觉，直到十点钟方由阿云来将她唤醒。“乌先生起来一个钟头了。”阿云告诉她说：“他说尽管请你多睡一会，他可以等。我想想，让他多等也不好意思。”“不错。”螺蛳太太转过身来让阿云看她的发髻“我的头毛不毛？”“还好。”“那就不必重新梳头了，你打盆脸水来，我洗了脸就下去。”话虽如此，略事修饰，也还花了半个钟头，到得楼下，先问乌先生睡得如何，又问阿云，早饭吃的什么？寒暄了一会，使个眼色，让阿云退了出去，方始移一移椅子，向乌先生倾诉心事。“朱宝如同我们大先生是”一表三千里，的表叔，他太太，我记得你见过的？““见过，也听说过，生得慈眉善目，大家都说她精明能干，做事情同场面上的男人一样，很上路。”乌先生紧接着说：“昨天晚上听大先生谈起，才晓得她是好厉害的一个角色。”“我昨天听他一谈，心里七上八下。”螺蛳太太迟疑了好一会，放低了声间说：“乌先生，我有件事，只同你商量。我不晓得朱太太会不会起黑心，吞没我的东西？”乌先生问，“你寄放在她那里的是啥东西？”“是一个枕头。”

Partly from exhaustion, partly because thinking of Mr Wu had given her hope of relief, Madam Luosi finally slept well, curled in the armchair. It was ten before A’yun woke her. “Mr Wu has been up for an hour. He said to let you sleep longer; he could wait. But I thought it would be rude to keep him much longer.” “Quite right.” She turned so that A’yun could inspect her hair. “Is it untidy?” “Not very.” “Then there is no need to dress it again. Bring water for my face, and I shall go down.” Even these few preparations took another half-hour. Downstairs, she asked Mr Wu how he had slept and A’yun what he had eaten for breakfast. After some civilities, she dismissed the maid with a glance, moved her chair closer, and confided her trouble. “Zhu Baoru is a very distant cousin of one of Mr Hu's parents. I think you have met his wife?” “I have met her and heard of her. Such a kindly face. Everyone says she is shrewd and capable, as experienced in handling matters as a man of affairs. Only from last night's story did I learn how formidable she really is.” “That story left me terribly uneasy.” Madam Luosi hesitated for some time, then lowered her voice. “There is something I can discuss only with you. I wonder whether Mrs Zhu might have the wickedness to swallow up something of mine.” “What have you left with her?” “A pillow.”

当然，枕头里面有花样，第一样是各色宝石，不下四五十枚，原来胡雪岩是有一回在京里听人谈起，乾隆年间的权相和珅，一早起来，取一盘五色宝石要看好些辰光，名为“养眼”。回家以后，如法炮制，这一盘宝石，起码要值十万银子。第二样是螺蛳太太顶名贵的两样首饰，一双钻镯、一个胸饰，中间一枚三十多克拉重的火油钻镯，周围所镶十二粒小钻，每粒最少亦有两克拉，是法国宫廷中流出来的珍品，胡雪岩买它时，就花了二十五万银子。第三样的价值便无法估计了，是十枚“东珠”，此珠产于黑龙江与松花江合流的混同江中，大如桂圆，匀圆莹白，向来只供御用，采珠的珠户，亦由吉林将军严密管制，民间从无买卖，所以并无行情。这十枚“东珠”据说是火烧圆明园时，为英国兵所盗取，辗转落入一个德国银行家手中。由于胡雪岩为“西征”借外债，这个银行家想作成这笔生意，特意以此为酬，以后胡雪岩就没有再收他的佣金。乌先生体会到此事如果发生纠纷，对螺蛳太太的打击是如何沉重。因此，他认为首先要做的一件事，便是慰抚。

Naturally, there was more to the pillow than appeared. First, it contained at least forty or fifty precious stones of various colors. Hu had once heard in Beijing that Heshen, the powerful minister of the Qianlong era, would spend a long time every morning looking at a tray of multicolored gemstones, calling it “nourishing the eyes.” On returning home, Hu had copied him. That tray alone was worth at least a hundred thousand taels. Next came Madam Luosi's two most valuable pieces of jewelry: a pair of diamond bracelets and a breast ornament with a central “oil diamond” weighing more than thirty carats, surrounded by twelve smaller diamonds of at least two carats each. A treasure that had come out of the French court, it had cost Hu two hundred and fifty thousand taels. The third item's value could not be reckoned: ten Eastern Pearls, produced in the Huntong River where the Heilong and Songhua rivers met. Large as longans, evenly round and lustrous white, they had always been reserved for imperial use. The pearl-gathering households were strictly controlled by the General of Jilin. Since the pearls were never traded privately, there was no market price. These ten were said to have been stolen by British soldiers during the burning of the Old Summer Palace and passed eventually into the hands of a German banker. When Hu sought foreign loans for the western campaign, the banker offered them as a reward for arranging the business. Hu subsequently took no further commission from him. Mr Wu understood how devastating a dispute over these things would be for Madam Luosi. His first task, he thought, was to comfort her.

“罗四姐，世事变化莫测，万一不如意，你要看得开。”他紧接着：“这不是说，这件事已经出毛病了，不过做要往最好的地方去做，想要往最坏的地方去想。你懂不懂我的意思？”螺蛳太太心里很乱，“乌先生，”她答非所问地说：“我现在只有你一个人可以商量。”“那么，我现在有几句话要问你，第一，这件事是你自己托朱太太的，还是她劝你这么做的？”“是我自己托她的。不过，她同我说过，不怕一万，只怕万一，意思是我自己要有个打算。”“嗯嗯！”乌先生又问：“你把东西交给她的时候，有没有人看见？”“这种事怎么好让人看见？”“坏就坏在这里！”乌先生在心里想。“你交给她的时候，”他问：“有什么话交代？”“我说：枕头里面有点东西，寄放在你这里，我随时会来拿。”“她怎么说呢？”“她说：我也不管枕头里是什么东西，你交给我，我不能不替你存好，随便你什么时候来拿。不过，我收条是不打的。”“当然，这种事，哪有打收条之理？”乌先生说：“现在瞎猜也没有用，你不放心，把它去拿回来就是。”“我……”螺蛳太太很吃力地说：“我怕她不肯给我。”“你说她会不认帐？”“万一这样子，我怎么办？”说着，螺蛳太太叹了口气，“我真怕会见她。”不是怕见朱太太，是怕朱太太不认帐，她当时就会承受不住。既然如此，乌先生自觉义不容辞了。

“The world is unpredictable, Sister Luo. If things go badly, you must try to bear it. I do not mean that anything has gone wrong already. But while working for the best, we must prepare our minds for the worst. Do you understand?” In her confusion, she answered beside the point. “You are the only person I can discuss this with.” “Then let me ask a few questions. First, did you ask Mrs Zhu to keep it, or did she persuade you?” “I asked her. But she had told me that even a remote possibility must be guarded against. She meant I ought to make some provision for myself.” “I see. Did anyone see you hand it over?” “How could I let anyone see such a thing?” That was precisely the trouble, Mr Wu thought. “What instructions did you give?” “I said there were a few things inside the pillow, that I would leave it with her, and that I might come for it at any time.” “And she?” “She said, ‘I shall not concern myself with what is inside. Since you have entrusted it to me, I must keep it safely. Come for it whenever you please. But I shall give no receipt.’” “Naturally. How could there be a receipt for such a thing? Guessing is useless now. If you are worried, simply fetch it back.” “I...” She struggled to speak. “I am afraid she may refuse.” “You think she might deny having it?” “What could I do if she did?” She sighed. “I really dread seeing her.” It was not Mrs Zhu herself she feared, but a denial she might be unable to bear. Mr Wu felt he could not refuse to help.

“我陪你去，或者，我代你去，看她怎么说？”“对，你代我去，看她怎么说。”螺蛳太太说：“你带两样东西给她，她就晓得你是我请去的，会跟你说实话。”螺蛳太太随即唤了阿云来，命她去开药箱，取来两个锦盒，一个内贮一支吉林老山人参，是当年山西遇到百年未有的大旱，胡老太太特捐巨款助赈，山西巡抚曾国荃专折请奖，蒙慈禧太后颁赐一方“乐善好施”的御笔匾额，及四两人参，由于出自天家，格外珍贵，这是螺蛳太太为了结好，自动送朱太太的。另外一个锦盒中，只残存了两粒蜡丸，这是朱太太特为跟她索取的。“我们家大少奶奶、二小姐，各用了一个，还剩下两个舍不得送人。朱太太跟我要了几回，我说不知道放在哪里了，等找出来送她。如今也说不得了，舍不得也要舍得。”螺蛳太太又说：“但愿她想到，要为子孙修修福，阴功积德，才不会绝后。”原来还有这样深意在内，螺蛳太太真可说是用心良苦。乌先生点点头说：“我拿这两样东西去给她，等于是信物，她会相信，我可以做你的‘全权代表’。好，我今天就会。”

“I could go with you, or go on your behalf and see what she says.” “Yes. Go for me. Take two things, and she will know I sent you and speak frankly.” Madam Luosi called A’yun and told her to open the medicine chest and bring two brocade boxes. One contained an old mountain-grown Jilin ginseng root. During the drought in Shanxi, the worst in a hundred years, Old Madam Hu had given a vast sum for relief. Governor Zeng Guoquan had submitted a special memorial asking that she be rewarded, and Empress Dowager Cixi had bestowed a plaque inscribed “Delighting in Good Works and Generous in Giving,” together with four liang of ginseng. Coming from the imperial household, it was particularly precious. Madam Luosi had offered this root to cultivate Mrs Zhu's friendship. The other box held only two wax-sealed pills. Mrs Zhu had repeatedly asked for them. “Our eldest daughter-in-law and our second daughter each used one. Only two were left, and I could not bear to give them away. Mrs Zhu asked several times. I said I could not remember where I had put them and would send them when I found them. Now I have no choice. Reluctant or not, I must part with them. I only hope she remembers to earn blessings for her descendants, accumulating hidden merit so that her line does not die out.” There was a deeper purpose here as well; Madam Luosi had indeed taken great pains. Mr Wu nodded. “These gifts will serve as credentials. She will believe that I have full authority to represent you. Very well. I shall see her today.”

“乌先生，我还有件事跟你商量。”螺蛳太太要商量的，便是从各房姨太太住处查寻到的私房，本来装一只大箱子，想托乌先生寄顿，胡雪岩虽不赞成，螺蛳太太心却未死，想检出最值钱的一部分，打成一个不惹人注目的小包裹，交付给乌先生，问他意下如何？“既然大先生不赞成，我不能做。”乌先生又说：“不但我自己不做，罗四姐，我劝你也不要做。我说句不客气的话，今天朱太太那面的事，就是你没有先跟大先生商量，自己惹出来的烦恼。如果你再这样私下自作主张，将来不但我同大先生没有朋友做，连你，他都会起误会。”螺蛳太太接受了他的劝告，但这一来便只有将全部希望寄托在乌先生身上了，谆谆叮嘱，务必好好花点心思，将寄放在朱太太处的那个“宝枕”能收了回来。

“There is something else.” She described the concubines' private valuables. Though Hu had rejected her plan to send the large chest away, she still hoped to select the most valuable objects, make up an inconspicuous little bundle, and entrust it to Mr Wu. What did he think? “If Mr Hu objects, I cannot do it. Nor should you. To speak plainly, this trouble with Mrs Zhu arose because you acted without consulting him. If you continue making secret arrangements, you may ruin my friendship with him and make him misunderstand you as well.” She accepted his advice. Now all her hopes rested on him. Repeatedly she begged him to give careful thought to recovering the precious pillow.

乌先生不敢怠慢，回家好好休息了一夜，第二天起身破例不上茶馆，在家吃了早餐，泡上一壶上好龙井，一面品茗，一面细想螺蛳太太所托之事，假设了好几种情况，也想好了不同的对策。到得九点多钟，带一个跟班，坐轿直到朱家。跟班上前投帖，朱家的门房挡驾，“老爷出去了。”他说：“等我们老爷回来，我请我们老爷去回拜。”其时，乌先生已经下了轿，他已估计到朱宝如可能不在家，所以不慌不忙地说：“我是胡家托我来的。你家老爷不在，不要紧，我看你家太太。有两样胡家螺蛳太太托我送来的东西，连我的名帖一起送进去，你家太太就知道了。”门房原知主母不是寻常不善应付男客的妇道人家，听得此一说，料知定会延见，当时想了一下，哈着腰说：“本来要请乌老爷到花厅里坐，只为天气太冷，花厅没有生炉子，乌老爷不嫌委屈，请到门房里来坐一坐，比外面暖和。”“好，好，多谢，多谢。”坐得不久，门房回出来说：“我家太太说，乌老爷不是外人，又是螺蛳太太请来的，请上房里坐。“

Mr Wu did not take the commission lightly. He went home and rested well for a night. The following morning, unusually, he did not visit a teahouse. After breakfast at home, he brewed a pot of excellent Longjing and considered Madam Luosi's business as he drank, imagining several possible situations and preparing a response to each. A little after nine he took a servant and went by sedan chair to the Zhu house. The servant presented his card, but the gatekeeper refused admission. “The master is out. When he returns, I shall ask him to call on you.” Mr Wu had already stepped from his chair. He had anticipated Zhu's absence and answered calmly, “The Hu household sent me. If your master is out, it does not matter. I wish to see your mistress. Madam Luosi has asked me to bring two things. Take them in with my card, and she will understand.” Knowing that his mistress was no ordinary woman at a loss when receiving male callers, the gatekeeper felt certain she would see him. After a moment he bowed. “I should normally invite you into the reception hall, but it is cold and there is no fire there. If you do not mind, please wait in the gatehouse. It is warmer than outside.” “Thank you.” Soon the man returned. “My mistress says you are no stranger, and Madam Luosi has sent you. Please come to the inner rooms.”

上房在三厅上，进了角门，堂屋的屏门已经开了在等，进门便是极大的一个雪白铜炭盆，火焰熊熊，一室生春。门房将乌先生交给一个十七八岁的丫头，关上屏门，管自己走了。“阿春！”朱太太在东面那间屋子里，大声说道：“你问一问乌老爷，吃了点心没有，如果没有，马上关照厨房预备。”“吃过，吃过。”乌先生对阿春说：“谢谢你们太太，不必费心。”他的话刚完，门帘掀处，朱太太出现了，穿一件灰鼠皮袄，花白头发，梳得一丝不乱，小小一个发髻上，一面插一支碧玉挖耳，一面佩一朵红花，脸上还薄薄地搽一层粉，双眼明亮，身材苗条，是个“老来俏”。

These were in the third hall. Beyond the side entrance, the screen doors stood open to receive him. A huge, gleaming white-copper charcoal brazier filled the room with warmth. The gatekeeper handed him over to a maid of seventeen or eighteen and left, closing the doors. “A’chun!” Mrs Zhu called from the east room. “Ask Master Wu whether he has eaten. If not, tell the kitchen to prepare something at once.” “I have eaten. Thank your mistress; she need not trouble.” As he finished, the curtain lifted and Mrs Zhu appeared. She wore a gray-squirrel-fur coat. Her graying hair was impeccably arranged in a small knot, with a green-jade ear scoop on one side and a red flower on the other. Lightly powdered, bright-eyed, and slender, she was still a beauty in her later years.

“乌老爷，老久不见了，乌太太好？”她一面说，一面挽手为礼。“托福，托福！”乌先生作揖还礼，“宝如兄不在家？”“天不亮，去料理施粥去了。”朱宝如多少年来都是善堂的董事，公家有何赈济贫民的惠政，都有他一份。“可佩，可佩！”乌先生说：“积善之家，必有余庆。”“这也难说。”朱太太停了一下，未毕其词，先尽礼节，“请坐，请坐！”接着又在茶几上望了一下，已有一碗盖碗茶在，便不作声了。“朱太太，我今天是螺蛳太太托我来的。昨天我去，她正好把你要的药找到了，顺便托我送来。另外有一支人参，就算送年礼了。”“正是！”朱太太不胜歉然的，“胡大先生出了这种事，她还要为我的这点小事情操心，又送这么一支贵重的人参，我受是受了，心里实在说不出的，怎么说呢，只好说，实在是说不出的难过。”“彼此至交，总有补情的时候。喔，还有件事，螺蛳太太说有一个枕头寄放在你这里。”说到这里，乌先生很用心地注视她的反应，直到她点了头，他一颗心才放了下去。“有的。”她问：“怎么样？”“螺蛳太太说：这个枕头，她想拿回去。”“好极！”朱太太很快地答了这两个字，然后又说：“乌老爷，说实话，当初她带了一个枕头来，说要寄放在我这里。她没有多说，我也没有多问，明晓得是犯法的，我也只好替她挺。挺是挺了，心里是一直七上八下，担心会出事。现在要拿回去，在我实在是求之不得。乌老爷，你请稍为坐一坐，我马上拿出来，请你带回去。”说着，起身便走。

“Master Wu, it has been a long time. Is your wife well?” She greeted him with clasped hands. “Very well, thank you.” He returned her greeting with a bow. “Brother Baoru is out?” “He went before daylight to attend to the soup kitchens.” For years Zhu had been a director of charitable institutions; whenever the authorities organized relief for the poor, he had a part in it. “Admirable! A family that accumulates good deeds will surely leave blessings to its descendants.” “That is hard to say.” Before continuing, she remembered her duties as hostess. “Please sit.” A glance at the side table showed her that a covered cup of tea was already there. “Madam Luosi has sent me,” Mr Wu began. “When I visited yesterday, she had just found the medicine you wanted and asked me to bring it. The ginseng is a New Year gift.” “Indeed!” Mrs Zhu looked deeply apologetic. “With such a calamity upon Mr Hu, she still worries over this little matter of mine, and sends so valuable a root as well. I have accepted it, but I cannot tell you how I feel. How shall I put it? I can only say I am deeply distressed.” “Close friends always have another opportunity to repay a kindness. There is one other thing. Madam Luosi says she left a pillow with you.” He watched her closely. Only when she nodded did his heart settle. “Yes. What about it?” “She would like it back.” “Excellent!” Mrs Zhu answered at once. “To tell you the truth, when she brought it, she said little, and I asked no questions. I knew it was against the law, but I had to stand by her. All the same, I have been anxious ever since. Nothing could suit me better than returning it. Wait a moment. I shall fetch it for you to take.” She rose and went inside.

这一番话，大出乌先生的意料，在他设想的情况中，最好的一种是：朱太太承认有此物，说要收回，毫无异议，但不是她亲自送去，便是请螺蛳太太来，当面交还。不过她竟是托他带了回去。要不要带呢？他很快地作了一个决定：不带。因为中间转了一手，倘或有何差错，无端卷入是非，太不划算了。因此，他急忙向刚掀帘入内的朱太太说道：“朱太太，你不必拿出来，我请螺蛳太太自己来领回。”于是朱太太走了回来，等乌先生将刚才的话，复又说了一遍，她平静地答说：“也好！那就请乌老爷告诉螺蛳太太，请她来拿。不晓得啥时候来？”“那要问她。”朱太太想了一下说：“这样，她如果有空，今天下午就来，在我这里便饭。胡大先生的事，大家都关心，想打听打听，又怕这种时候去打搅，变成不识相，既然她要来，我同她谈谈心，说不定心里的苦楚吐了出来，也舒服些。“情意如此深厚，言语如此恳挚，乌先生实在无法想象她会是如胡雪岩所形容的，那种阴险的妇人。然而，胡雪岩的知人之明是有名的，莫非竟会看走了眼？

This went far beyond Mr Wu's expectations. The best outcome he had imagined was that Mrs Zhu would acknowledge the pillow and agree to return it, either delivering it personally or asking Madam Luosi to collect it in person. Instead, she meant to entrust it to him. Should he take it? He decided at once that he should not. If anything proved wrong after it had passed through another pair of hands, he would be drawn needlessly into a dispute. “Please do not fetch it,” he called as she lifted the curtain. “I shall ask Madam Luosi to collect it herself.” Mrs Zhu came back. After he repeated his words, she answered calmly, “Very well. Tell her to come for it. When will that be?” “I must ask her.” She considered. “If she is free, let her come this afternoon and have a simple meal here. We are all concerned about Mr Hu. I have wanted to inquire, but feared that disturbing them now would be tactless. Since she must come, we can have a heart-to-heart talk. Perhaps speaking of her troubles will ease them a little.” Such affection and sincerity made it difficult for Mr Wu to imagine her as the sinister woman Hu had described. Yet Hu was famous for his understanding of people. Could he really have misjudged her?

这个内心的困扰，一时没工夫去细想，他所想到的，只是赶紧要将这个好消息去告诉螺蛳太太，因而起身说道：“朱太太，我不打搅了。”“何不吃了便饭去？宝如也快回来了，你们可以多谈谈。”“改天！改天。”“那么，”朱太太沉吟了一会说：“螺蛳太太送我这么贵重的东西，照规矩是一定要‘回盘’的。不过，一则不敢麻烦乌老爷，再则，我同螺蛳太太下半天就要见面的，当面同她道谢。请乌老爷先把我的意思说到。”馈赠仪物，即时还礼，交送礼的人带回，称为“回盘”。朱太太礼数周到，越使乌先生觉得胡雪岩的话，与他的印象不符。坐在轿子里一直在想这件事，最后获得一个折衷的结论，胡雪岩看人不会错，自己的印象也信得过，“仓廪实而知礼节”，这朱太太从前是那种人，现在发了财要修修来世，已经回心向善了。他不但心里这样在想，而且也把他的想法告诉了螺蛳太太。她当然很高兴，使得胡雪岩很奇怪，因为她那种喜形于色的样子，在他已感觉到很陌生了。“有啥开心的事情？”螺蛳太太觉得事到如今，不必再瞒他了，“我同你老实说了吧！我有一个枕头寄放在朱太太那里。现在可以拿回来了……”她将整个经过情形，细说了一遍。胡雪岩不作声，只说了一句：“好嘛，你去拿了回来再说。”“对，拿了回来，我们再商量。”她想了一下说：“或者拿到手不拿回家，就寄放在乌先生那里，你赞成不赞成。”“赞成。”胡雪岩一口答应。他对这个枕头是否能顺利收回，将信将疑，倘或如愿以偿，当然以寄存在乌先生处为宜。

For the moment, Mr Wu had no time to pursue the puzzle. His only thought was to take the good news to Madam Luosi at once. He rose. “I shall not trouble you any longer.” “Why not stay for a simple meal? Baoru will soon be back, and you can talk.” “Another day.” “Then...” She hesitated. “Madam Luosi has sent such valuable gifts that custom requires me to send something in return. But I would rather not trouble you to carry it, and I shall see her this afternoon and thank her myself. Please explain that to her.” An immediate return gift carried back by the person who delivered the original was called “returning the tray.” Her punctilious courtesy further convinced Mr Wu that Hu's account did not match his own impression. He pondered it throughout the ride and finally reached a compromise. Hu's judgment could not be wrong, nor could his own experience be dismissed. With full granaries came knowledge of propriety: Mrs Zhu had once been that sort of woman, but now that she was wealthy and wished to earn blessings for the next life, she had turned toward goodness. He not only thought this but told Madam Luosi. Naturally she was delighted. Hu was puzzled: her openly joyful expression had become almost unfamiliar to him. “What has made you so happy?” She felt there was no need for further concealment. “I shall tell you frankly. I left a pillow with Mrs Zhu, and now I can have it back.” She explained everything. Hu offered no comment beyond, “Very well. Go and fetch it, and we shall see.” “Yes. Once I have it, we can discuss what to do. Or perhaps I should leave it with Mr Wu instead of bringing it home. Would you agree?” “Yes,” Hu answered readily. He was uncertain whether she would recover it without difficulty. If she did, leaving it with Mr Wu would naturally be best.

带着阿云到了朱家，螺蛳太太在大厅檐前下轿。朱太太已迎在轿前，执手问讯，她凝视了好一会：“你瘦了点！”接着自语似地说：“怎么不要瘦？好比天塌下来一样，大先生顶一半，你顶一半。“就这句话，螺蛳太太觉得心头一暖，对朱太太也更有信心了。到得上房里，盖碗茶，高脚果盘，摆满一桌，朱太太又叫人陪阿云，招呼得非常周到。乱过一阵，才能静静谈话。“天天想去看你，总是想到你事情多，心乱。”朱太太又说：“你又能干好客，礼数上一点不肯错的，我去了，只有替你添麻烦，所以一直没有去，你不要怪我。”“哪里的话！这是你体恤我，我感激都来不及。”“我是怕旁人会说闲话，平时那样子厚的交情，现在倒象素不往来似的。”“你何必去管旁人，我们交情厚，自己晓得。”螺蛳太太又加一句：“交情不厚，我也不会把那个枕头寄放在这里了。”“是啊！”朱太太紧接着她的话说：“你当初把那个枕头寄放在我这里，我心里就在想，总有点东西在里头。不过你不说，我也不便问。今天早晨，乌老爷来说，你要拿了回去，再好没有，我也少背多少风险。喔，”她似乎突然想起，“你送我这么贵重的一支参，实在不敢当。螺蛳太太，我说实话，大先生没有出事的时候，不要说一支，送我十支，我也老脸皮收得下，如今大不同了，我……”“你不要说了。”螺蛳太太打断她的话，“我明白你的意思。不过，我也要老实说：俗话说的是，‘穷虽穷，家里还有三担铜’，送你一支参当年礼，你不必客气。”“既然你这样说，我就安心了。不过我‘回盘’没有啥好东西。”“你不要客气！”螺蛳太太心里在想，拿那个枕头“回盘”，就再好都没有了。

Accompanied by A’yun, Madam Luosi reached the Zhu house and alighted beneath the eaves of the main hall. Mrs Zhu was waiting beside the chair. Taking her hands, she looked at her for some time. “You have grown thinner!” Then, almost to herself, “How could you not? It is as though the sky had fallen. Mr Hu bears half the weight, and you the other half.” That one remark warmed Madam Luosi's heart and increased her confidence. In the inner rooms a table was covered with tea cups and tall fruit stands. Mrs Zhu even arranged company for A’yun, attending to every detail. Only after the bustle subsided could they talk quietly. “Every day I have wanted to visit you, but I kept thinking how busy and upset you must be. You are such a capable hostess and never neglect a courtesy. My visit would only add to your troubles. Do not blame me for staying away.” “How could I? You were being considerate. I am grateful.” “I fear what others may say. We were such close friends, and now it looks as though we never knew one another.” “Why mind other people? We know our friendship.” Madam Luosi added, “If we were not close, I should never have entrusted the pillow to you.” “Indeed. When you brought it, I thought there must be something inside. But you did not say, and I could hardly ask. When Master Wu told me this morning that you wanted it back, I could not have been more pleased. It will relieve me of so much risk. Oh, and that precious ginseng root! I scarcely feel I can accept it. Before Mr Hu's troubles, I would have had the effrontery to accept ten, let alone one. But now...” “Say no more. I understand. But let me be equally frank: as the saying goes, however poor, we still have three loads of copper at home. You need not hesitate over a root of ginseng for New Year.” “Then my mind is easier. Only I have nothing fine to give in return.” “Do not stand on ceremony.” Privately, Madam Luosi thought that no return gift could be better than the pillow.

就这时丫头来请示：“是不是等老爷回来再开饭？”“老爷回来了，也是单独开饭。”朱太太说：“菜如果好了，就开吧！”这倒提醒了螺蛳太太，不提一声朱宝如，似乎失礼，便即问说：“朱老爷出去了？”接下来便是闲话家常，光是胡家遣散各房姨太太这件事，便谈不完，只是螺蛳太太有事在心，只约略说了些。然后吃饭，饭罢略坐一坐，便该告辞了。“现在只有你一个人了，大先生一定在等，我就不留你了。等我把东西去拿出来。”朱太太说完，回到后房。没有多久，由丫头捧出来一个包裹，一个托盘，盘中是一顶貂帽，一只女用金表，包裹中便是螺蛳太太寄存的枕头，连蓝布包袱，都是原来的。“ ‘回盘’没有啥好东西，你不要见笑。”“自己人。”螺蛳太太说：“何必说客气话。”“这是你的枕头。”朱太太说：“说实话，为了你这个枕头，我常常半夜里睡不着，稍为有点响动，我马上会惊醒，万一贼骨头来偷了去，我对你怎么交代，”“真是！”螺狮太太不胜歉疚地，“害你受累，真正过意不去。”“我也不过这么说说。以我们的交情，我同宝如当然要同你们共患难的。”这句话使得螺蛳太太自然而然地想到了朱家驹与王培利，他们不也是跟他们夫妇共患难的吗？这样转着念头，接枕头时便迫不及待地要想知道其中的内容，但也只有掂一掂分量——很大的一个长方枕头，亮纱枕套，内实茶叶，但中间埋藏着一个长方锡盒，珍藏都在里面。她接枕头时，感觉到中间重、两头轻，足证锡盒仍在，不由得宽心大放。

A maid came to ask whether dinner should wait until the master returned. “When he comes, he will eat separately,” Mrs Zhu said. “If the dishes are ready, serve them.” This reminded Madam Luosi that it was hardly polite not to have mentioned Zhu Baoru. “Has Master Zhu gone out?” she asked. They moved on to household matters. The dispersal of Hu's concubines alone could have supplied endless conversation, but with the pillow on her mind, she gave only a brief account. They ate, sat a little afterward, and it was time to leave. “You are the only one left now. Mr Hu must be waiting, so I shall not detain you. Let me fetch your things.” Mrs Zhu went into the back room. Soon a maid brought out a bundle and a tray. On the tray lay a sable hat and a lady's gold watch. In the bundle was the pillow, still in its original blue-cloth wrapping. “Nothing very fine as a return gift. Please do not laugh at it.” “We are old friends. There is no need for such politeness.” “Here is your pillow. To tell the truth, it has often kept me awake at night. The slightest sound would wake me. If a thief stole it, how could I answer to you?” “I am so sorry to have put you to such trouble.” “It is merely a remark. With our friendship, Baoru and I must naturally share your adversity.” That made Madam Luosi think of Zhu Jiaju and Wang Peili. Had they not shared adversity with this couple too? Eager to know what remained inside, she could only weigh the pillow in her hands. It was a large rectangular one with a sheer gauze cover, stuffed with tea leaves. At its center was a rectangular tin box containing the treasures. The middle still felt heavy and the ends light: the box was plainly still there. Her relief was immense.

“多谢，多谢！”螺蛳太太将枕头交了给阿云，看朱太太的丫头在包貂帽与金表时，微笑着说：“这顶貂帽，我来戴戴看。”是一顶西洋妇子戴的紫貂帽，一旁还饰着一支红蓝相间、十分鲜艳的羽毛。她是心情愉快，一时好玩，亲自动手拔去首饰。将貂帽戴在头上。朱太太的丫头，已捧过来一面镜子，她左顾右盼了一番，自己都觉得好笑。“象出塞的昭君。”朱太太笑着说：“这种帽子，也只有你这种漂亮人物来戴，如果戴在我头上，变成老妖怪了。”就这样说说笑笑，满怀舒畅地上了轿，照预先的约走，直到乌家。胡雪岩已经先到了，乌太太已由丈夫关照，有要紧事要办，所以只跟螺蛳太太略略寒暄了几句，便退了出去，同时将下人亦都遣在，堂屋里只剩下主客三人。“拿回来了。”螺蛳太太将貂帽取了下来，“还送了我这么一顶帽子，一个金表。”胡雪岩与乌先生都很沉着地点点头，默不作声，螺蛳太太便解开了蓝布包袱，拿起桌上的剪刀准备动手时，乌先生开口了。“先仔细看一看。”看是看外表，有没有动过手脚，如果拆过重缝，线脚上是看得出来的，前后左右上下都仔细检查了，看不出拆过的痕迹。“剪吧！”

“Thank you!” She handed the pillow to A’yun. Seeing the maid wrap the hat and watch, she smiled. “Let me try the hat.” It was a sable hat of the sort Western women wore, with a bright red-and-blue feather at one side. In her happy mood, she felt playful. Removing her hair ornaments, she put it on. The maid brought a mirror. She turned her head this way and that, amused by her own appearance. “Like Zhaojun leaving for the frontier,” Mrs Zhu laughed. “Only a beauty like you could wear that hat. On my head it would make an old monster of me.” Still talking and laughing, Madam Luosi entered her chair in excellent spirits and went straight to Mr Wu's house, as arranged. Hu had arrived before her. Mrs Wu, told by her husband that there was important business to settle, exchanged only a few greetings before withdrawing and sending the servants away. The three were left alone. “I have it back.” Madam Luosi removed the hat. “She gave me this too, and a gold watch.” Both men nodded calmly without speaking. She unwrapped the blue cloth and took up a pair of scissors from the table. “Examine it carefully first,” Mr Wu said. They inspected the outside for signs of interference. If it had been opened and sewn up again, the stitches should reveal it. They looked over every side and could find no sign of opening. “Cut it.”

剪开枕头，作为填充枕头的茶叶，落了一桌，螺蛳太太捧起锡盒，入手脸色大变，“分量轻浮多了！”她的声音已经发抖。“你不要慌！”胡雪岩依旧沉着，“把心定下来。”螺蛳太太不敢开盒盖，将锡盒放在桌上，自己坐了下来，扶着桌沿说：“你来开！”“你有点啥东西在里头？”胡雪岩问说。“你那盘‘养眼’的宝石，我的两样金刚钻的首饰、镯子同胸花。还有，那十二颗东珠。”胡雪岩点点头，拿起锡盒，有意无意地估一估重量，沉吟了一下说：“罗四姐，你不要看了好不好？”“为啥？”螺蛳太太刚有些泛红的脸色，一下子又变得又青又白了。“不看，东西好好儿在里面，你的心放得下来……”“看了，”螺蛳太太抢着说：“我就放不下心？”“不是这话。”胡雪岩说：“钱财是身外之物，生不带来，死不带去。这一次栽了这么大的一个跟斗，我总以为你也应该看开了。““怎么？”螺蛳太太哪里还能平心静气听他规劝，双手往前一伸，鼓起勇气说道：“就算她黑良心，我总也要看明白了才甘心。”

She cut the pillow open, spilling tea leaves over the table, and lifted out the tin box. Her face changed at once. “It is much lighter!” Her voice was trembling. “Do not panic,” Hu said, still calm. “Steady yourself.” Afraid to lift the lid, she set the box on the table and sat down, gripping its edge. “You open it.” “What did you have in it?” “Your tray of gemstones for ‘nourishing the eyes,’ my diamond bracelets and breast ornament, and those twelve Eastern Pearls.” Hu nodded and picked up the box, seeming almost casually to weigh it in his hands. After a moment he said, “Would it not be better not to look, Sister Luo?” “Why?” The faint color that had returned to her face vanished again. “If you do not look, you can think of everything as safely inside and be at peace.” “And if I look,” she broke in, “I shall have no peace?” “That is not what I mean. Wealth is something outside ourselves. We bring none when we are born and take none when we die. After so great a fall, I thought you too might have learned to let it go.” “What?” She could no longer calmly listen to persuasion. Stretching out her hands and gathering her courage, she said, “Even if she has betrayed me, I must see it clearly before I can accept it.”

说着，捏住盒盖，使劲往上一提。这个锡盒高有两寸，盒盖、盒底其实是两个盒子套在一起，急切间哪里提得起来，螺蛳太太心急如焚，双手一提，提得盒子悬空，接着使劲抖了两下，想将盒底抖了下来。“慢慢，慢慢！”乌先生急忙拦阻，“盒底掉下来，珠子会震碎。等我来。”于是乌先生坐了下来，双手扶着盒盖，一左一右地交替着往上提拔，慢慢地打开了。盒子里塞着很多皮纸，填塞空隙，螺蛳太太不取皮纸，先用手一按，立即有数，“我的钻镯没有了！”她说：“珠子也好象少了。”乌先生帮她将皮纸都取了出来，预期的“火油钻”闪烁出来的炫目的光芒，丝毫不见，不但钻镯已失，连胸饰也不在了。螺蛳太太直瞪着盒子，手足冰冷，好一会才说了句：“承她的情，还留了六颗东珠在这里。”“宝石也还在。”胡雪岩揭开另一个小木盒，拿掉覆盖的皮纸说。“什么还在？”螺蛳太太气紧败坏地说：“好东西都没有了。”“你不要气急……”“我怎么能不气急。”螺蛳太太“哇”地一声哭了出来，旋即警觉，用手硬掩住自己的嘴，不让自己出声，但眼泪已流得衣襟上湿了一大片。任凭胡雪岩与乌先生怎么劝，都不能让她把眼泪止住。最后胡雪岩说了句：“罗四姐，你不是光是会哭的女人，是不是？”这句话有意想不到的效果，顿时住了眼泪，伸手进入袖中去掏手绢拭泪。窗外的阿云早就在留意，而且已找乌家的丫头，预备了热手巾在那里，见此光景，推门闪了进来，将热毛巾送到她手里，螺蛳太太醒鼻子，抹涕泪，然后将手巾交回阿云，轻轻说了句：“你出去。”

She grasped the lid and pulled hard. The box was two cun deep, its top and bottom really two boxes fitted one inside the other. It would not come apart so readily. In desperation she lifted it clear of the table and shook it twice, trying to dislodge the bottom. “Slowly!” Mr Wu stopped her. “If the bottom falls, the pearls may break. Let me.” Sitting down, he held the lid with both hands and eased it upward, first on one side and then the other, until it came free. Much bark paper filled the empty spaces inside. Without removing it, Madam Luosi pressed down with her hand and knew at once. “My diamond bracelets are gone! There seem to be fewer pearls too.” Mr Wu helped take out the paper. None of the expected dazzling fire of the “oil diamond” appeared. Not only the bracelets but the breast ornament was missing. She stared into the box, her hands and feet cold. After a long time she said, “How kind of her to leave six Eastern Pearls.” “The gemstones are here too,” Hu said, opening a little wooden box and lifting off the covering paper. “What is here?” she cried in fury. “Everything good is gone!” “Do not upset yourself...” “How can I help it?” She burst into tears, then suddenly remembered herself and clapped a hand over her mouth to stifle the sound. Tears had already soaked a broad patch of her clothes. Neither man could console her. At last Hu said, “You are not a woman who can do nothing but cry, are you?” The effect was unexpected. Her tears stopped at once, and she reached into her sleeve for a handkerchief. A’yun had been watching outside the window and had already asked a maid of the Wu household to prepare a hot towel. Seeing her mistress's movement, she slipped in and handed it over. Madam Luosi blew her nose, wiped her face, and returned it. “Go outside,” she said softly.

等阿云退出堂屋，乌先生说道：“罗四姐，你的损失不轻，不过，你这笔帐，如果并在大先生那里一起算，也就无所谓了。”“事情不一样的。做生意有赚就有赔，没有话说。我这算啥？我一口气咽不落。”螺蛳太太又说：“从前，大家都说我能干，现在，大家都会说我的眼睛是瞎的；从前，大家都说我有帮夫运，现在大家都会说，我们老爷最倒霉的时候，还要帮个倒忙，是扫帚星。乌先生，你说，我怎样咽得落这口气？”乌先生无话可答，好半天才说了句：“罗四姐你不要输到底！”“乌先生，你是要我认输？”“是的。”“我不认！”罗四姐的声音又快又急，带着些负气的意味。“你不认！”胡雪岩问：“预备怎么样呢？”“我一直不认输的。前天晚上，你劝我同七姐夫合伙买地皮、造弄堂房子，又说开一家专卖外国首饰、衣料、家具的洋行，我的心动了，自己觉得蛮有把握，你倒下去了，有我来顶，这是我罗四姐出人头地的一个机会。”螺蛳太太加重了语气说：“千载难逢的机会。有你在场面上，我天大的本事，也不能抛头露面，现在有了机会，这个机会是怎么来的？是你上千万银子的家当，一夜工夫化为灰尘换来的。好难得噢！”原来她是持着这种想法，胡雪岩悄然大悟，心中立刻想到，从各房姨太太那里搜集到的“私房”，本要寄顿在乌先生处而为他所反对的，此刻看起来是要重新考虑。“有机会也要有预备，我是早预备好的。螺蛳太太指着那个锡盒说：”这一盒东西至少值五十万。现在呢，东珠一时未见得能脱手，剩下来的这些宝石，都是蹩脚货，不过值个一两万银子。机会在眼前，抓不住，你们说，我咽得落咽不落这个气。““机会还是有的。”胡雪岩说：“只要你不认输，总还有办法。”

When A’yun had left, Mr Wu spoke. “Your loss is heavy, Sister Luo. But set beside Mr Hu's losses, it need not weigh so much.” “It is different. In business there are profits and losses; one cannot complain. But what is this? I cannot swallow it. People used to call me capable. Now they will say I was blind. They used to say I brought my husband good fortune. Now they will say that, at his worst moment, I made matters worse—that I am an unlucky star. Tell me how I can bear that.” Mr Wu had no answer. At last he said, “Do not let this defeat you completely.” “You want me to admit defeat?” “Yes.” “I will not!” Her answer was quick, sharp, and defiant. “Then what do you intend to do?” Hu asked. “I have never admitted defeat. The night before last, you suggested that I buy land and build lane houses with Brother-in-Law Gu, or open a firm selling foreign jewelry, cloth, and furniture. I was tempted. I felt sure I could do it. If you have fallen, I can take your place. This is my chance to distinguish myself—a chance in a thousand years!” She emphasized every word. “While you stood at the center of affairs, I could never have come forward, however able I was. Now I have a chance. What bought it? Your fortune of over ten million taels, turned to ashes overnight. Think how precious it is!” Only now did Hu understand her thinking. Immediately he remembered the private valuables collected from the concubines, which he had refused to leave with Mr Wu. That decision must be reconsidered. “An opportunity is useless without preparation. I had prepared long ago.” She pointed to the tin box. “What was in here was worth at least five hundred thousand taels. Now? The Eastern Pearls may not sell readily. The remaining stones are inferior ones, worth only ten or twenty thousand. The opportunity is before me, and I cannot seize it. How can I bear that?” “There is still opportunity,” Hu said. “So long as you refuse to give up, there will be a way.”

“什么办法？”螺蛳太太摇摇头，“无凭无据，你好去告她？”“不是同她打官司，我另有办法。”胡雪岩说：“我们回去吧！不要打搅乌先生了。”“打搅是谈不到。”乌先生接口说道：“不过，你们两位回去，好好儿商量商量看，是不是有啥办法，可以挽回？只要用得着我的地方，我唯命是听。”“多谢，多谢！”胡雪岩加重了语气说：“一定会有麻烦乌先生的地方，明天我再请你来谈。”“是，是！明天下午我会到府上去。”于是，螺蛳太太将阿云唤了进来，收拾那个锡盒，告辞回家。一上了百狮楼，抽抽噎噎地哭个不停，胡雪岩无从解劝，阿云虽约略知道是怎么回事，但关系太大，不敢胡乱开口，只是一遍一遍地绞了热手巾让她擦眼泪。终于哭声渐住，胡雪岩亦终于打定了主意，“我明白你的心里的意思，你不肯认输，还想翻身，弄出一个新的局面来，就算规模不大，总是证明了我们不是一蹶不振。既然如此，我倒还有一个办法，不过，”他停了一下说：“你要有个‘以前种种，譬如昨日死；以后种种，譬如今日生’的想法。”“ ‘以后种种，譬如今日生’？”螺蛳太太问说：“生路在哪里？”“喏！”胡雪岩指着那口存贮各房姨太太私房的箱子说：“如今说不得了，只好照你的主意，寄放在乌先生那里。你同应春炒地皮也好，开洋行也好，一笔合伙的本钱有了。”螺蛳太太不作声，心里却在激动，“以前种种，譬如昨日死”的觉悟，虽还谈不到，而“以后种种，譬如今日生”的念头，油然而生，配合她那不认输的性格，心头逐渐浮起了“柳暗花明又一村”的憧憬。

“What way?” She shook her head. “Without proof, can we sue her?” “I do not mean a lawsuit. I have another idea. Let us go home; we must not trouble Mr Wu any longer.” “It is no trouble,” Mr Wu put in. “But go home and discuss whether anything can be done to recover the situation. If there is any way I can help, you have only to ask.” “Thank you.” Hu spoke emphatically. “There will certainly be something I must ask of you. I shall invite you over tomorrow.” “I shall come in the afternoon.” Madam Luosi called A’yun to pack the tin box, and they took their leave. Back at Hundred Lions Tower, she began sobbing again and could not stop. Hu could find no way to comfort her. A’yun knew roughly what had happened, but the matter was too grave for her to speak carelessly. She could only keep wringing out hot towels for her mistress's tears. At last the sobbing subsided, and Hu reached a decision. “I understand. You will not admit defeat. You want to recover and build something new. Even on a modest scale, it would prove that we have not fallen beyond recovery. There is one way. But you must think of everything before today as having died yesterday, and everything to come as being born today.” “‘Everything to come as being born today’? Where is this new life to be found?” “There.” Hu pointed to the chest of the concubines' private valuables. “We have no choice now. We must follow your plan and leave it with Mr Wu. Whether you speculate in land with Yingchun or open a foreign-goods firm, you will have capital for the partnership.” Madam Luosi said nothing, but her heart stirred. She could not yet think of the past as dead. The idea of a new life beginning today, however, arose naturally within her. With her refusal to surrender, it gradually brought a vision of another village beyond the dark willows and bright blossoms.

“现在也只好这样子了！”螺蛳太太咬咬牙说：“等我们立直了，再来同朱家老婆算帐。”“好了！睡觉了。身子要紧，”胡雪岩说：“留得青山在，不怕没柴烧。”“阿云！”螺蛳太太的声音，又显得很有力、很有权威了，“等老爷吃了药酒，服侍老爷上床，老爷睡楼下。”“为什么叫我睡楼下？”胡雪岩问。“我要理箱子，声音响动，会吵得你睡不着。”螺蛳太太又说：“既然托了乌先生了，不必一番手续两番做，值得拿出去的东西还多，我要好好儿理一理。”“理一只箱子就可以了！”胡雪岩说：“多了太显眼，传出风声去，会有麻烦。”“我懂，你不必操心。”

“There is nothing else for it now!” Madam Luosi clenched her teeth. “Once we are back on our feet, we shall settle accounts with that Zhu woman.” “Enough. Time for bed. Your health matters,” Hu said. “While the green hills remain, there will always be firewood.” “A’yun!” Her voice had regained its strength and authority. “When the master has had his medicinal wine, help him to bed downstairs.” “Why must I sleep downstairs?” Hu asked. “I have to sort the chest. The noise would keep you awake. Since Mr Wu has agreed to help, there is no point in doing the same work twice. There are other things worth taking away. I must sort them carefully.” “One chest is enough. More would attract attention. If word gets out, there will be trouble.” “I understand. You need not worry.”

第二天下午，乌先生应约而至，刚刚坐定，还未谈到正题，门上送进来一封德馨的信，核桃大的九个字：“有要事奉告，乞即命驾。”下面只署了“两浑”二字，没有上款也没有下款，授受之间，心照不宣。“大概京里有信息。”胡雪岩神色凝重地说：“你不要走，等我回来再谈。”“是，是。”乌先生答说：“我不走，我不走。”这时螺蛳太太得报赶了来，忧心忡忡地问：“说德藩台请你马上去，为啥？”“还不晓得。”胡雪岩尽力放松脸上的肌肉，“不会有啥要紧事的，等我回来再说。”说完，匆匆下楼，坐轿到了藩司衙门，在侧门下轿，听差领人签押房，德馨正在抽大烟，摆一摆手，示意他在烟榻上躺了下来。抽完一筒烟，德馨拿起小茶壶，嘴对嘴喝了两口热茶，又闭了一会眼睛，方始张目说道：“雪岩，有人跟你过不去。”“喔。”胡雪岩只答了这么一个字，等他说下去。“今儿中午，刘中丞派人来请我去吃饭，告诉我说，你有东西寄放在别处，问我知道不知道？”这件事来得太突然了！是不是朱宝如夫妇在捣鬼？胡雪岩心里很乱，一时竟不知如何回答。

Mr Wu arrived the next afternoon as promised. He had barely sat down, and they had not yet come to their business, when a letter from Dexin was brought in. Nine characters, each as large as a walnut, said: “Important news to impart; please come at once.” Below stood only the two characters “Lianghun,” without formal address or closing. Sender and recipient understood one another. “Probably news from Beijing,” Hu said gravely. “Do not go. We shall talk when I return.” “Of course. I shall wait.” Madam Luosi hurried in, anxious. “They say Treasurer De wants you at once. Why?” “I do not know yet.” Hu made an effort to relax his face. “It cannot be anything very serious. We shall talk when I get back.” He went quickly downstairs and took a chair to the provincial treasury yamen. Alighting at a side entrance, he was led to the private office. Dexin was smoking opium. With a wave, he indicated that Hu should lie on the couch beside him. Finishing a pipe, he took a small teapot, drank two mouthfuls of hot tea directly from its spout, and closed his eyes for a while before speaking. “Xueyan, someone is making trouble for you.” “Oh.” Hu waited. “Governor Liu invited me to lunch today. He said you had left valuables elsewhere and asked whether I knew about it.” The news was utterly unexpected. Were Zhu Baoru and his wife behind it? Hu's thoughts were in such confusion that for a moment he could not answer.

“雪岩，”德馨又说：“以咱们的交情，没有什么话不好说的。”胡雪岩定一定神，想到刘秉璋手中不知握有什么证据？话要说得活络，“晓翁，你晓得的，我决不会做这种事。”他说：“是不是小妾起了什么糊涂心思，要等我回去问了才明白。”“也许是罗四姐私下的安排。”德馨踌躇了一下说：“刘中丞为此似乎很不高兴，交代下来的办法，很不妥当。为了敷衍他的面子，我不能不交代杭州府派两个人去，只当替你看门好了。”很显然的，刘秉璋交代的办法，一定是派人监守，甚至进出家门都要搜查。果然如此，这个台坍不起。到此地步，什么硬话都说不起，只有拱拱手说：“请晓翁成全，维持我的颜面。”“当然，当然。你请放心好了。不过，雪岩，请你也要约束家人，特别要请罗四姐看破些。”“是，是。谨遵台命。”“你请回吧！吴知府大概就会派人去，接不上头，引起纷扰，面子上就不好看了。”胡雪岩诺诺连声，告辞上轿，只催脚夫快走。赶回元宝街，问清门上，杭州府或者仁和县尚未派人来过，方始放下心来。“如果有人来，请在花厅里坐，马上进来通报。”交代完了，仍回百狮楼，螺蛳太太正陪着乌先生在楼下闲谈，一见了他，都站起身来，以殷切询问的眼光相迎。

“Between friends like us,” Dexin continued, “there is nothing you cannot say.” Hu steadied himself. He did not know what evidence Liu held and must leave room to maneuver. “Mr Xiaofeng, you know I would never do such a thing. Whether one of my concubines has had some foolish idea, I shall have to ask when I get home.” “Perhaps Sister Luo has made a private arrangement.” Dexin hesitated. “The governor seems very displeased. His instructions are most unsuitable. To satisfy him, I have had to ask the Hangzhou prefecture to send two men. Think of them simply as gatekeepers.” Clearly, Liu had ordered a watch on the house, perhaps even searches of everyone entering or leaving. Such a humiliation would be unbearable. Yet Hu could no longer speak defiantly. He merely clasped his hands. “Please help me preserve my dignity.” “Naturally. Do not worry. But you too must restrain your household, especially Sister Luo. Ask her to learn to let go.” “Certainly. I shall follow your instructions.” “Go home now. Prefect Wu will probably send the men shortly. If nobody receives them properly and a disturbance follows, it will be embarrassing.” Hu agreed repeatedly, took his leave, and urged his bearers to hurry. Back at Yuanbao Street, he questioned the gatekeeper and was relieved to learn that neither the prefecture nor Renhe county had sent anyone yet. “If anyone comes, ask them to wait in the reception hall and inform me immediately.” He returned to Hundred Lions Tower. Madam Luosi was talking with Mr Wu downstairs. Both rose at his appearance, their eyes full of urgent questions.

想想是决瞒不过的事，胡雪岩决定将经过情形和盘托出，但就在要开口之际，想到还有机会，因而毫不迟疑地对螺蛳太太说：“你赶快寻个皮包，或者帽笼，检出一批东西来，请乌先生带走。”“为啥？”“没有工夫细说，越快越好。”螺蛳太太以为抄家的要来了，吓得手软心跳，倒是阿云还镇静，一把拉住她说：“我扶你上楼。”“对！阿云去帮忙，能拿多少是多少，要快。”螺蛳太太咬一咬牙，挺一挺胸，对阿云说道：“拿个西洋皮包来。”说完，首先上楼。“怎么？”乌先生问：“是不是京里有消息？”“不是。十之八九，是朱宝如去告的密，说罗四姐有东西寄放在外面。刘中丞交代德晓峰，要派人来……“一句话未完，门上来报，仁和县的典史林子祥来了。“有没有带人来？”“四个。”

Knowing he could not conceal what had happened, Hu decided to tell them everything. Just as he was about to speak, he realized that there was still an opportunity. Without hesitation he told Madam Luosi, “Find a leather bag or a hat case at once. Choose some things for Mr Wu to take away.” “Why?” “There is no time to explain. As quickly as possible.” Thinking that officials were coming to confiscate the house, she was so frightened that her hands went weak and her heart pounded. A’yun remained calm and took her arm. “I shall help you upstairs.” “Yes. Help her, A’yun. Take as much as you can, but hurry.” Madam Luosi clenched her teeth, straightened her back, and told the maid, “Bring a Western leather bag.” Then she led the way upstairs. “What is it?” Mr Wu asked. “News from Beijing?” “No. Most likely Zhu Baoru has informed on us, saying that Sister Luo has left valuables elsewhere. Governor Liu has told De Xiaofeng to send men...” Before he could finish, the gatekeeper announced Lin Zixiang, the chief clerk of Renhe county. “Has he brought anyone?” “Four men.”

胡雪岩提示了一个警戒的眼色，随即由门房引领着，来到接待一般客人的大花厅。林子祥跟胡雪岩极熟，远远地迎了上来，捞起衣襟打了个千，口中仍旧是以往见面的称谓：“胡大人！”“不敢当，不敢当！四者爷。”县衙门的官位，典史排列第四，所以通称“四老爷”。胡雪岩一面拱手还礼，一面说道：“现在我是一品老百姓了，你千万不要用这个称呼。”“胡大人说哪里话，指日官复原职，仍旧戴红顶子。我现在改了称呼，将来还要改回来，改来改去麻烦，倒不如一仍旧惯。““四老爷口才，越来越好了。请坐。”揖客升炕，林子祥不肯上坐，甚至不肯坐炕床，谦让了好一会，才在下首坐下，胡雪岩坐在炕旁一张红木太师椅上相陪。“今天德藩台已经跟我谈过了，说会派人来，四老爷有啥吩咐，我好交代他们照办。”“不敢，不敢！上命差遣，身不由己，县大老爷交代，我们仁和县托胡大人的福，公益事情办得比钱塘县来得风光，叫我不可无礼。”林子祥紧接着说：“其实县大老爷是多交代的，我带人到府上来，同做客人一样，怎么好无礼！”这话使得胡雪岩深感安慰。每年他捐出去“做好事”的款子不少，仁和县因为是“本乡本土”，捐款独多。如今听县官的话，可见好歹还是有人知道的。

Hu gave Mr Wu a warning glance and followed the gatekeeper to the large reception hall used for ordinary visitors. Lin knew him well and came forward eagerly, lifting the front of his robe to salute on one knee. He used the same address as always. “Your Excellency!” “Please, Fourth Master, I do not deserve it.” The chief clerk ranked fourth among the county yamen's officials, hence the customary title “Fourth Master.” Returning the greeting with clasped hands, Hu said, “I am now a first-rank commoner. You must not address me that way.” “What are you saying? You will soon be restored to office and wear the red button again. If I change how I address you now, I shall only have to change it back. Better to keep the old custom and save the trouble.” “Your eloquence improves all the time. Please sit.” Hu invited him to the kang, but Lin would take neither the seat of honor nor even a place on the couch. After much polite insistence, he finally took the lower seat, while Hu occupied a grand rosewood chair beside it. “Treasurer De told me today that men would be sent. Whatever instructions you have, let me know so that I can have them carried out.” “I would not presume. I am here on orders, with no choice in the matter. The magistrate said that, thanks to you, Renhe's charitable undertakings have outshone Qiantang's. He told me not to be discourteous. But he need hardly have said it. Coming here with my men is like coming as your guest. How could I be rude?” Hu found this deeply comforting. Every year he had donated large sums to good causes, and Renhe, his own home county, had received an especially generous share. The magistrate's words showed that someone still remembered.

“多谢县大老爷的美意。”胡雪岩说：“今年我出了事，现在所有的一切，等于都是公款，我也不敢随便再捐，心里也蛮难过的。”“其实也无所谓，做好事嘛！”林子样说：“哪怕抚台晓得了，也不会说话的。”“是，是！”胡雪岩不知如何回答。“现在辰光还来得及。”林子祥说：“今年时世不好，又快过年了，县大老爷想多办几个粥厂，经费还没有着落。”“好！我捐。”胡雪岩问：“你看要捐多少？”“随便胡大人，捐一箱银子好了。”胡雪岩只觉得“一箱银子”这句话说得很怪，同时一心以为县官索贿，却没有想到人家是暗示，可以公然抬一个箱子出去，箱子之中有夹带，如何转移，那是出了胡家大门的事。“现银怕不多，我来凑几千两外国银行的票子。等一息，请四老爷回去。”林子祥苦于不便明言，正在思索着如何点醒胡雪岩，只见胡家的听差进来说道：“仁和县的差人请四老爷说话。”差人就在花厅外面，从玻璃窗中望得见。林子祥怕胡雪岩疑心他暗中弄鬼，为示坦诚，随即说道：“烦管家叫他进来说。”这一进来反而坏事，原来乌先生拎着着一个皮包，想从侧门出去，不道林子祥带来的差人，已经守在那里，乌先生有些心虚，往后一缩，差人拦住盘问，虽知是胡家的客人，但那个皮包却大有可疑，所以特来请示，是否放行？

“Thank the magistrate for his kindness. Since this year's disaster, everything I possess is effectively public money. I dare not make donations as I please, and that troubles me.” “Surely it does not matter if it is for charity,” Lin said. “Even the governor would raise no objection.” “Yes, yes.” Hu did not know what else to say. “There is still time,” Lin continued. “Times are hard, and New Year is coming. The magistrate wishes to open more soup kitchens, but the funds have not been found.” “Very well. I shall contribute. How much do you suggest?” “Whatever you please. A chest of silver would do.” Hu found “a chest of silver” an odd expression. Assuming that the magistrate wanted a bribe, he missed the hint: a chest could openly be carried out as a donation. If something was hidden inside, how it was transferred afterward was a matter to be arranged beyond the Hu family gates. “I may not have much silver in cash. I shall make up a few thousand taels in foreign bank paper. Wait a little, and you can take it back.” Unable to speak plainly, Lin was wondering how to make him understand when a servant entered. “A Renhe runner wishes to speak to Fourth Master.” The man stood outside the hall, visible through the glass window. Afraid that Hu might suspect secret maneuvering, Lin wanted to show his openness. “Please ask him to come in and speak here.” That made matters worse. Mr Wu had tried to leave by a side gate carrying a leather bag, only to find one of Lin's men already stationed there. Uneasy, Wu had drawn back. The runner stopped him to question him. Though he knew Wu was a guest, the bag looked highly suspicious. He had therefore come to ask whether he should let him pass.

“当然放。”林子祥没有听清楚，大声说道：“胡大人的客人，为啥盘问？”这官腔打得那差人大起反感，“请四老爷的示，”他问：“是不是带东西出去，也不必盘查。”“带什么东西？”“那位乌先生带了个大皮包，拎都拎不动。”这一说，胡雪岩面子上挂不住，林子祥也发觉自己在无意中弄成一个僵局，只好继续打官腔：“你不会问一问是啥东西。”“我问过了，那位乌先生结结巴巴说不出来。”见此光景，胡雪岩暗暗叹气。他知道林子祥的本意是要表明他在他心目中，尊敬丝毫不减，但形禁势恪，今非昔比，要帮他的忙，只有在暗中调护，林子祥将差人唤进来问话，便是一误，而开口便打官腔，更是大错特错，事到如今，再任令他们争辩下去，不仅于事无补，而且越来越僵，面子上会弄得很难看。转念到此，他以调人的口吻说道：“四老爷，你不要怪他，他也是忠于职守，并没有错。那皮包里是我送我朋友的几方端砚，不过也不必去说他了，让我的朋友空手回去好了。”“不要紧，不要紧！”林子祥说：“几方端砚算啥，让令友带回去。”胡雪岩心想，如果公然让乌先生将那未经查看的皮包带出去，那差人心里一定不服，风声传出去，不仅林子祥会有麻烦，连德馨亦有不便，而刘秉璋说不定采取更严厉的措施，面子难看且不说，影响到清理的全局，所失更大。因此，他断然地答一声：“不必！公事公办，大家不错。”随即吩咐听差：“你去把乌先生的皮包拎进去。”

“Of course you should!” Lin had not listened carefully. “Why question a guest of His Excellency?” His official bluster thoroughly antagonized the runner. “May I have your instructions, Fourth Master? Are we to make no inquiries even if he is taking things out?” “What things?” “Mr Wu has a leather bag so heavy he can barely carry it.” Hu could not hide his embarrassment. Lin realized that he had unwittingly created a deadlock, but could only continue in his official manner. “Could you not ask what is in it?” “I did. Mr Wu stammered and could not answer.” Hu sighed inwardly. He knew Lin meant to show that his respect remained undiminished. But circumstances had changed, and he was constrained by them. Any help must be given discreetly. Calling the runner in had been one mistake; meeting him with official bluster had been worse. Further argument would accomplish nothing and only deepen the impasse, making the humiliation greater. Hu intervened in a conciliatory tone. “Do not blame him, Fourth Master. He is doing his duty and has done nothing wrong. The bag contains a few Duan inkstones I gave my friend. But let us say no more about it. My friend can go home empty-handed.” “There is no need! What do a few inkstones matter? Let him take them.” Hu thought that allowing Wu openly to leave with the unexamined bag would certainly leave the runner resentful. If word spread, Lin would be in trouble, Dexin would be embarrassed, and Liu might impose even stricter measures. Apart from the loss of face, the whole settlement of Hu's affairs might suffer. “No,” he answered firmly. “Let official business be handled properly, and nobody will be at fault.” He told the servant, “Bring Mr Wu's bag back inside.”

林子祥老大过意不会，“令友乌先生在哪里？”他说：“我来替他赔个不是。”对这一点，胡雪岩倒是不反对，“应该我来赔。”说着，也出了花厅。林子祥跟在后面，走近侧门，不见乌先生的踪影，问起来才知道已回到百狮楼楼下了。结果还是将乌先生请了出来，林子祥再三致歉以后，方始辞去。面子是有了，里子却丢掉了。乌先生一再引咎自责，自嘲是“贼胆心虚”。螺蛳太太连番遭受挫折，神情沮丧。胡雪岩看在眼中，痛在心里，而且还有件事，不能不说，踌躇再四，方始出口。“还要凑点钱给仁和县。快过年了，仁和县还想添设几座粥厂，林子祥同我说，县里要我帮忙，我已经答应他了。”螺蛳太太先不作声，过了一会才问：“要多少？”“他要我捐一箱银子。我想……”“慢点！”螺蛳太太打断他的话问：“他说啥？‘一箱银子’？”“不错，他是说一箱银子。”“箱子有大有小，一箱是多少呢？”“是啊！”胡雪岩说：“当时我也觉得他的话很怪。”

Lin felt deeply apologetic. “Where is your friend? Let me apologize.” Hu had no objection to that. “It is I who should apologize.” He left the hall, followed by Lin. At the side gate Wu was nowhere to be seen. They learned that he had returned to the ground floor of Hundred Lions Tower. In the end he was invited out, and Lin apologized repeatedly before leaving. Appearances had been preserved, but their attempt had failed. Wu blamed himself again and again, ruefully calling it a thief's guilty conscience. After repeated setbacks, Madam Luosi looked utterly dejected. The sight hurt Hu, and there was still something he had to tell her. He hesitated for a long time before speaking. “We must raise some money for Renhe county as well. New Year is near, and they want to open more soup kitchens. Lin said the county wanted my help. I have already agreed.” She was silent at first, then asked, “How much?” “He asked for a chest of silver. I thought...” “Wait!” she interrupted. “What did he say? ‘A chest of silver’?” “Exactly.” “Chests come in different sizes. How much is a chest?” “Yes,” Hu said. “I thought it sounded odd at the time.”

“大先生。”一直未曾开口的乌先生说：“请你把当时的情形，说一遍看。”“我来想想看。”胡雪岩思索当时交谈的经过，将记得起来的情形，都说了出来。一面回想，一面已渐有领悟。“莫非他在‘豁翎子’？”乌先生说。“豁翎子”是杭州俗语，暗示之意。暗示什么呢？螺蛳太太明白了，“现在也还来得及。”她说：“趁早把林四老爷请了回来，请乌先生同他谈，打开天窗说亮话好了。”乌先生不作声，只看着胡雪岩，等候他的决定，而胡雪岩却只是摇头。“事情未见得有那么容易。箱子抬出去，中间要有一个地方能够耽搁，把东西掉包掉出来，做得不妥当，会闯大祸。”他停了一下，顿一顿足说：“算了！一切都是命。”

Mr Wu, who had said nothing, now spoke. “Tell us exactly what happened.” “Let me think.” Hu recalled as much of the conversation as he could. In retelling it, he gradually began to understand. “Could he have been dropping a hint?” Mr Wu asked, using a Hangzhou expression, huo lingzi, for hinting. A hint of what? Madam Luosi understood. “There is still time. Send for Fourth Master Lin at once. Let Mr Wu speak to him frankly.” Mr Wu said nothing, merely looking to Hu for his decision. Hu only shook his head. “It may not be so easy. After the chest leaves, there must be somewhere along the way to stop and switch the contents. If it is mishandled, there could be terrible trouble.” He paused, then stamped his foot. “Let it go. Everything is fate.”

这句话等于在濒临绝望深渊的螺蛳太太身后，重重地推了一把，也仿佛将她微若游丝的一线生机，操刀一割。从那一刻开始，她的神思开始有些恍惚了，但只有一件事，也是对一个人的记忆是清楚的，那就是朱宝如的老婆。“阿云，”她说：“佛争一柱香，人争一口气，一口气咽不下，艮在喉咙口，我会发疯。我只有想到一件事，心里比较好过些，我要叫起黑心吞没我活命的东西，还狠得下去，到巡抚衙门去告密的人，一辈子会怕我。”阿云愕然，“怕点啥？”她怯怯地问。“怕我到阎罗大王那里告状告准了，无常鬼会来捉她。”“太太，你，”阿云急得流眼泪，“你莫非要寻死？”螺蛳太太不作声，慢慢地闭上眼，嘴角挂着微笑，安详地睡着了。这一睡再没有醒了。事后检查，从广济医院梅藤更医生那里取来的一小瓶安神药，只剩了空瓶子了。

Those words were like a heavy push from behind as Madam Luosi stood at the brink of despair, or a blade severing her last thread of hope, already thin as gossamer. From that moment, her thoughts began to grow confused. Only one thing, one person, remained clear in her memory: Zhu Baoru's wife. “A’yun,” she said, “a Buddha needs his incense, and a person needs her dignity. This indignation sticks in my throat. It will drive me mad. Only one thought gives me any relief. The woman who stole what I needed to live, and was cruel enough to inform on us at the governor's yamen—I shall make her fear me for the rest of her life.” A’yun stared. “Fear what?” she asked timidly. “That my complaint before the King of Hell will be upheld, and the underworld’s messengers will come to seize her.” “Madam, you...” A’yun was weeping in alarm. “Surely you are not thinking of ending your life?” Madam Luosi did not answer. Slowly she closed her eyes. A smile lingered at the corners of her mouth as she fell peacefully asleep. She never woke again. When they looked afterward, the little bottle of sedative obtained from Dr Mei Tenggeng at Guangji Hospital was empty.
