内容简介｜SYNOPSIS


胡光墉（1823—1885 ），字雪岩，安徽绩溪人。
Hu Guangyong (1823–1885), known by his courtesy name, Xueyan, was a native of Jixi, Anhui.


作者在书中极力铺陈了主人公的传奇经历，
In this novel, the author recounts his protagonist's extraordinary career in rich detail.

胡雪岩初在钱庄学徒，
Hu Xueyan begins as an apprentice in a private bank.

因囊助潦倒的冗吏王有龄旋升，以致自身失业，
After providing financial assistance that helps Wang Youling, an impoverished minor official, advance in his career, Hu loses his own job.

王有龄感其思，遂二人结生死之交。
Deeply grateful, Wang forms a bond with him that neither life nor death can break.

后胡雪岩利用王有龄在宫场上的发达，开设钱庄，
Later, drawing on Wang's growing influence in official circles, Hu establishes his own bank.

在官府势力、漕帮首领和外商买办之间，层层投靠，左右逢源，
He cultivates patrons among government officials, leaders of the grain-transport associations, and Chinese intermediaries working for foreign merchants, building connections on every side.

以精细的连环计算，收买人民、网罗赌棍、拉拢富商，
Through a succession of carefully calculated moves, he buys people's loyalty, recruits gamblers, and courts wealthy merchants.

混迹于勾栏赌场，竭力经营丝茶生意，大做军火勾当。
Frequenting brothels and gambling houses, he energetically develops his silk and tea businesses and engages extensively in the arms trade.


其问，收姬纳妾，邀友狎妓，不惜重金为妓女赎身，又转手奉送于高官……
Meanwhile, he acquires concubines, visits courtesans with his friends, and spends lavishly to buy women out of prostitution, only to present them in turn to high officials...


由于胡雪岩精于谋划，手段灵活，因此，很快得以暴发，
An astute planner with flexible methods, Hu rapidly amasses a fortune.

终至在上海、杭州立足，由市井布衣，跻于江浙大贾之列。
He establishes himself in Shanghai and Hangzhou, rising from the ranks of ordinary townspeople to become one of the great merchants of Jiangsu and Zhejiang.


列于《胡雪岩》（上、中、下）之后的《红顶商人》和《灯火楼台》等书（亦为台湾著名作家高阳先生所著，本公司出版），将继续向广大读者展现胡雪岩，这个半封建、半殖民地让会中的特定历史产物如何由商而宫、暴起暴落的曲折激烈的场面。
The Red-Topped Merchant and Towers of Light, which follow the three volumes of Hu Xueyan, are also by the celebrated Taiwan-based author Gao Yang and are published by our company. They continue the turbulent story of this figure shaped by a particular historical age: a semi-feudal, semi-colonial society. Readers will see how he moves from commerce into official life, and how his spectacular rise gives way to a dramatic fall.


楔子｜PROLOGUE


在清朝咸丰七年，英商麦加利银行设分行于上海以前，全国金融事业，为两个集团所掌握，商业上的术语称为“帮”，
Before the British Chartered Bank opened a branch in Shanghai in the seventh year of the Xianfeng reign, China's financial business was controlled by two groups, known in commercial usage as bang, or merchant networks.

北方是山西帮，南方地宁绍帮，
In the north stood the Shanxi network; in the south, the Ningbo–Shaoxing network.

所业虽同，其名则异，
Although they pursued the same line of business, their establishments bore different names.

大致前者称为“票号”，后者称是“钱庄”。
Broadly speaking, those in the north were called piaohao, or remittance banks, while those in the south were known as qianzhuang, or private banks.


山西帮又分为祁、太，平三帮，祁县、太谷、平遥，
The Shanxi network was itself divided into three groups, based in Qixian, Taigu, and Pingyao.

而始创票号者，为平遥人雷履泰。
The founder of the remittance-bank business was Lei Lütai, a native of Pingyao.

他最初受雇于同县李姓，在天津主持一定颜料铺，招牌叫做“日升昌”，
He had originally been employed by a man surnamed Li from the same county to manage a pigment shop in Tianjin called Rishengchang.

其时大约在乾隆末年。
This was around the end of the Qianlong reign.

日升昌在雷履泰的悉心照料之下，营业日盛，声誉日起，
Under Lei's careful management, the business prospered and its reputation grew.

连四川都知道这块“金字招牌”，因为雷履泰经常入川采购铜绿等等颜料，信用极好。
Its name became a guarantee of quality known even in Sichuan, where Lei frequently purchased verdigris and other pigments and enjoyed an excellent reputation for reliability.


四川与他省的交通最不便，
Travel between Sichuan and the other provinces was particularly difficult.

出川入川携带大批现金，不但麻烦，而且有风险。
Carrying large amounts of cash into or out of the province was not only inconvenient but also dangerous.

于是雷履泰创行汇兑法，由日升昌收银出票，凭票到指走地点的联号兑取现银。
Lei therefore introduced a system of remittance: Rishengchang accepted silver and issued a draft that could be exchanged for silver at an affiliated establishment in a designated location.

当然，汇兑要收汇费，名为“汇水”。
A remittance fee, known as huishui, was naturally charged.

汇水并无定额，是根据三个因素计算出来的：
The fee was not fixed but depended on three factors.

第一，路途的远近，远则贵，近则廉。
The first was distance: the farther the destination, the higher the charge.

第二，银根的松紧，
The second was the availability of ready money.

大致由小地方汇到大地方来得便宜，由大地方汇到小地方来得贵，因为地方大则银根松，地方小则银根紧，
As a rule, it cost less to remit funds from a small town to a large city than in the opposite direction, since ready money was more plentiful in larger places and scarcer in smaller ones.

如某处缺乏现金，而有待兑的汇票，则此时有客户交汇，正好济急，
If a branch was short of cash and had outstanding drafts to redeem, a customer bringing in silver for remittance would provide much-needed relief.

反有倒过来贴补客户汇费的。
In such cases, the bank might even pay the customer a premium instead of charging a fee.


最后是计算银锭的成色，
The final consideration was the purity of the silver ingots.

银锭的大小，通常分为三种，
Ingots generally came in three sizes.

最大的五十两，为了便于双手携捧，做成两头翘起的马蹄式，
The largest weighed fifty taels and were shaped like a horseshoe with raised ends, making them easier to lift with both hands.

即所谓“元宝”，而出于各省藩库的，称为“官宝”，
These were the ingots known as yuanbao; those issued by provincial treasuries were called official ingots.

其次是中锭，重十两，
Medium-sized ingots weighed ten taels.

有元宝形的，称为“小元宝”，但通常都做成秤锤式，
Some resembled the larger yuanbao and were called small yuanbao, though most were shaped like scale weights.

最小的或三两，或五两，通称“银锞”。
The smallest weighed three or five taels and were commonly called silver lumps.

再就是碎银，轻重不等。
There was also broken silver, which varied in weight.

此外各省有其特殊的形制，
Each province had its own distinctive forms.

如江浙称为“元丝”，底凹上凸，以便叠置。
In Jiangsu and Zhejiang, for example, the ingots known as yuansi had concave bases and convex tops so that they could be stacked.

但不管任何形状、大小，银子的成色，各地不同，
Whatever their shape or size, however, the purity of silver differed from place to place.

需要在交汇时核算扣足。
These differences had to be calculated and fully allowed for when a remittance was made.


由于汇兑凭票兑银，所以叫做“票号”。
Since silver was paid out against a draft, these establishments came to be called piaohao, literally “draft houses.”

早先运送现银的方法，如果不是随身携带，就得交镖局保送，
Previously, anyone wishing to move silver had either to carry it personally or entrust it to an armed escort agency.

费用大，麻烦多，走得慢，而且还有风险，
The latter option was expensive, troublesome, and slow, and it still involved risk.

万一被动或者出了其他意外，镖局虽然照赔，但总是件不愉快的事，
If the silver was stolen or some other mishap occurred, the escort agency would reimburse the loss, but the experience was unpleasant nonetheless.

所以票号一出，请教走镖英雄好汉的人就少了。
Once remittance banks appeared, fewer people had occasion to seek the services of those valiant escorts.


早期的票号，多为大商号兼营的副业，
The earliest remittance banks were mostly side businesses run by large trading firms.

到咸丰初年，始有大量专营的票号出现。
Only in the early years of the Xianfeng reign did establishments devoted exclusively to remittance begin to appear in considerable numbers.

但票号的势力不得越长江而南，
Their influence, however, did not extend south of the Yangtze.

因为江南的钱庄，为保护本身的利益，一方面仿照票号的成例，开办汇兑业务，一方面力拒票号的侵入。
To protect their own interests, the private banks of the lower Yangtze region copied the remittance system while firmly resisting the northern banks' expansion.

至于票号除汇兑以外，以后亦经营存款及放款，
The remittance banks, in turn, later began accepting deposits and making loans.

所以票号与钱庄的业务，由于彼此仿效的结果，几乎完全相同，只是在规模上，钱庄逊于票号而已。
Through this process of mutual imitation, the two types of bank came to conduct almost identical business, although private banks generally remained smaller.


钱庄业多为宁绍帮所经营，而镇江帮有后来居上之势。
Most private banks were operated by the Ningbo–Shaoxing network, though the Zhenjiang network was beginning to catch up and overtake it.

但在同治到光绪初年，全国最大的一家钱庄，规模凌驾票号而上之，
Yet from the Tongzhi reign into the early years of Guangxu, the largest private bank in China surpassed even the remittance banks in scale.

同时他的主人亦不属于宁绍帮，
Its proprietor did not belong to the Ningbo–Shaoxing network either.

是为当时金融业中的一个特例。
It was an exception in the financial world of its time.


这家钱庄的字号叫“阜康”，
The bank was called Fukang.

它的主人是杭州人。
Its proprietor was a man from Hangzhou.

第一章｜CHAPTER ONE


有个福州人，名叫王有龄，
There was a man from Fuzhou named Wang Youling.

他的父亲是候补道，分发浙江，在杭州一住数年，没有奉委过什么好差使。
His father, an expectant circuit intendant assigned to Zhejiang, had lived in Hangzhou for several years without receiving any worthwhile appointment.

老病侵寻，心情抑郁，死在异乡。
Worn down by age and illness, and burdened by disappointment, he died far from home.

身后没有留下多少钱，
He left little money.

运灵柩回福州，要好一笔盘缠，而且家乡也没有什么可以倚靠的亲友，
Transporting his coffin back to Fuzhou would have cost a substantial sum, and there were no relatives or friends in their home town on whom the family could rely.

王有龄就只好奉母寄居在异地了。
Wang Youling therefore had little choice but to remain in Hangzhou with his mother.


境况不好，而且举目无亲，王有龄混得很不成样子，
Short of money and with no one to turn to, Wang lived in miserable circumstances.

每天在“梅花碑”一家茶店里穷泡，一壶“龙井”泡成白开水还舍不得走，
He spent his days lingering in a teahouse at Meihuabei, reluctant to leave even after his pot of Longjing tea had been brewed until it tasted like plain water.

中午四个制钱买两个烧饼，算是一顿。
At noon he spent four copper coins on two baked flatbreads and called that a meal.


三十岁的人，潦倒落拓，无精打采，叫人看了起反感。
At thirty, he was shabby, dispirited, and down on his luck—a sight that put people off.

他的架子还大，经常两眼朝天，那就越发没有人爱理他了。
Yet he still gave himself airs, often looking down his nose at everyone, which made them even less inclined to have anything to do with him.


唯一的例外是个二十岁左右的少年，王有龄只知道他叫“小胡”。
The sole exception was a young man of about twenty, whom Wang knew only as Young Hu.

小胡生得一双四面八方都照顾得到的眼睛，加上一张常开的笑口，而且为人“四海”，所以人缘极好。
He had eyes that seemed to take in everything around him, a ready smile, and an easy generosity that made him welcome wherever he went.

不过，王有龄跟他只是点头之交，也识不透他的身分，
Wang, however, knew him only well enough to exchange a nod and could not make out his circumstances.

有时很阔气，有时似乎很窘，
At times he seemed well supplied with money; at others, decidedly short of it.

但不管如何，总是衣衫光鲜，
Whatever his fortunes, he was always neatly dressed.

象这初夏的天气，一件细白夏布长衫，浆洗得极其挺括，里面是纺绸小褂裤，
In this early-summer weather, for instance, he wore a fine white ramie gown, washed and starched until it was beautifully crisp, over a short silk jacket and silk trousers.

脚上白竹布的袜子，玄色贡缎的双梁鞋，
His socks were of white cotton, and his black satin shoes had twin raised seams.

跟王有龄身上那件打过补钉的青布长衫一比，小胡真可以说是“公子哥儿”了。
Beside Wang's patched blue cotton gown, Young Hu's appearance was positively that of a wealthy young gentleman.


他倒是有意结交王有龄，王有龄却以自惭形秽，淡淡地不肯跟他接近。
Young Hu clearly wished to make friends with Wang, but Wang felt ashamed of his own poverty and kept him at a distance.


这一天下午的茶客特别多，小胡跟王有龄“拼桌”，
One afternoon the teahouse was unusually crowded, and the two shared a table.

他去下了两盘象棋，
Young Hu went off to play a couple of games of Chinese chess.

笑嘻嘻走回来说：“王有龄，走，走，
When he returned, he was beaming. “Wang Youling, come along!

我请你去‘摆一碗’。”
Let me buy you a drink.”

摆一碗是杭州的乡谈，意思是到小酒店去对酌一番。
His expression, “set out a bowl,” was a Hangzhou phrase for going to a little tavern to share some wine.


“谢谢。不必破费。”
“Thank you, but you needn't go to the expense.”


“自有人请客。
“Someone else is paying.

你看！”
Look!”

他打开手巾包，里面包有二两碎银子，
He opened a bundle wrapped in a handkerchief to reveal two taels of broken silver.

得意地笑道：“第一盘‘双车错’，第二盘‘马后炮’，
Grinning proudly, he said, “In the first game I caught him with a pair of chariots; in the second, with a cannon behind a horse.

第三盘，小卒‘逼宫’，
In the third, a little pawn cornered his general.

杀得路断人稀。
I routed them so thoroughly that there was no one left to play.

不然，我还要赢。”
Otherwise, I'd have won still more.”


为了盛情难却，王有龄跟着去了。
Unable to resist such a warm invitation, Wang went along.

一路走到“城隍山”——“立马吴山第一峰”的吴山，挑了个可以眺望万家灯火的空旷地方，
They walked to City God Hill—the Wushan celebrated in the line, “Rein in your horse upon Wushan's foremost peak”—and chose an open spot overlooking the lights of countless homes.

一面喝酒一面闲谈。
There they drank and talked.


酒到半酣，闲话也说得差不多了，小胡忽然提高了声音说：“王有龄，我有句话，老早想问你了。
When the wine had begun to take effect and their small talk was nearly exhausted, Young Hu suddenly raised his voice. “Wang Youling, there's something I've been wanting to ask you for a long time.

我看你不是没本事的人，
You don't strike me as a man without ability.

而且我也懂点‘麻衣相法’，看你是大贵之相，
I also know a little of the Mayi art of face-reading, and yours looks like the face of a man destined for high office.

何以一天到晚‘孵’茶店？”
Why do you spend all day nesting in a teahouse?”


王有龄摇摇头，
Wang shook his head.

拈了块城隍山上有名的油饼，慢慢咬着，双眼望着远处，是那种说不出来的茫然落寞。
He picked up one of the fried cakes for which City God Hill was famous and ate it slowly, gazing into the distance with an expression of bewildered loneliness that was hard to put into words.


“叫我说什么？”
“What am I supposed to say?”

王有龄转过脸来盯着小胡，仿佛要跟他吵架似的，
He turned and stared at Young Hu as though about to quarrel with him.

“做生意要本钱，做官也要本钱，
“It takes capital to go into business, and it takes capital to become an official.

没本钱说什么？”
Without capital, what's there to talk about?”


“做官？”
“Become an official?”

小胡大为诧异，
Young Hu was astonished.

“怎么做法？
“How would you manage that?

你同我一样，连‘学’都没有‘进’过，
You're like me: you've never even earned the first examination qualification.

是个白丁。
We're ordinary men without degrees.

哪里来的官做？”
Where would an official post come from?”


“不可以‘捐班’吗？”
“Couldn't I enter by purchasing a qualification?”


小胡默然。心里有些看不起王有龄。
Young Hu said nothing, but inwardly he thought rather less of Wang.

捐官的情形不外乎两种，
There were generally two kinds of people who bought official status.

一种是做生意发了财，富而不贵，美中不足，
One was the merchant who had made his fortune but felt that wealth without rank left something to be desired.

捐个功名好提高身价，
He bought a title to raise his standing.

象扬州的盐商，个个都是花几千两银子捐来的道台，
The salt merchants of Yangzhou, for example, had each spent several thousand taels to acquire the rank of circuit intendant.

那一来便可以与地方官称兄道弟，平起平坐，
That enabled them to address local officials as brothers and meet them as equals.

否则就不算“缙绅先生”，育事上得公堂，要跪着回话。
Without such standing, they did not count as members of the gentry and had to kneel when answering questions in court.

再有一种，本是官员家的子弟，书也读得不错，就是运气不好，三年大比，次次名落孙山，
The other kind was the son of an official: well educated, perhaps, but unlucky enough to fail the examinations every three years.

年纪大了，家计也艰窘了，总得想个谋生之道，
As he grew older and his family resources dwindled, he had to find a way to earn a living.

走的就是“做官”的这条路，改行也无从改起，
Official service was the only career for which he had been prepared; he could scarcely begin again in another trade.

只好卖田卖地，拜托亲友，凑一笔去捐个官做。
So he sold fields and property, sought help from relatives and friends, and scraped together enough money to buy a qualification.

象王有龄这样，年纪还轻，
Wang Youling, however, was still young.

应该刻苦用功，从正途上去巴结，
Surely he ought to study hard and work his way up through the regular examinations.

不此之图，而况又穷得衣食不周，却痴心妄想去捐班，
Instead, though he could barely feed and clothe himself, he was dreaming of buying his way into office.

岂不是没出息？
Did that not show a lack of ambition?


王有龄看出他心里的意思，
Wang guessed what he was thinking.

有几杯酒在肚里，便不似平时那么沉着了，
With several cups of wine inside him, he was less restrained than usual.

“小胡！”他说，“我告诉你一句话，信不信由你，
“Young Hu,” he said, “I'll tell you something, whether you believe it or not.

先父在日，替我捐过一个‘盐大使’。”
While my father was alive, he bought me a qualification as a salt-station superintendent.”


小胡最机警，
Young Hu was exceptionally quick-witted.

一看他的神情，就知道决非假话，
One look at Wang's expression told him this was no invention.

随即笑道：“唷！失敬，失敬，
He immediately smiled. “Oh! My apologies!

原来是王老爷，
So you are His Honour Wang.

一直连名带姓叫你，
And all this time I've been calling you by your full name.

不知者不罪。”
You must forgive an ignorant man.”


“不要挖苦我了！”王有龄苦笑道，
“Don't make fun of me.” Wang smiled bitterly.

“说句实话，除非是你，别人面前我再也不说，
“To tell the truth, you're the only person I'd say this to.

说了反惹人耻笑。”
Anyone else would only laugh.”


“我不是笑你。”
“I'm not laughing at you.”

小胡放出庄重的神态问道，“不过，有一层我不明白，
Young Hu grew serious. “But there's one thing I don't understand.

既然你是盐大使，我们浙江沿海有好几十个盐场，为什么不给你补缺？”
If you're qualified to be a salt-station superintendent, why haven't you been appointed? There must be dozens of saltworks along the Zhejiang coast.”


“你只知其一，不知其二……”
“You know only half the story...”


捐官只是捐一个虚衔，
Purchasing office meant, in the first instance, purchasing an empty title.

凭一张吏部所发的“执照”，取得某一类官员的资格，
A certificate issued by the Board of Civil Appointments made its holder eligible for a particular category of post.

如果要想补缺，必得到吏部报到，称为“投供”，然后抽签分发到某一省候补。
To obtain an actual appointment, he first had to report to the Board for registration and then draw lots for assignment to a province, where he would wait for a vacancy.

王有龄尚未“投供”，
Wang had not even completed the registration.

哪里谈得到补缺？
How could he expect a substantive post?


讲完这些捐官补缺的程序，王有龄又说：“我所说的要‘本钱’，就是进京投供的盘缠。
After explaining the procedure, Wang added, “The ‘capital’ I mean is the money to travel to the capital and register.

如果境况再宽裕些，我还想‘改捐’。”
If I had a little more to spare, I'd also like to purchase a different qualification.”


“改捐个什么‘班子’？”
“For what sort of post?”


“改捐个知县。
“A county magistracy.

盐大使正八品，知县正七品，
A salt-station superintendent holds the principal eighth rank; a county magistrate holds the principal seventh.

改捐花不了多少钱。出路可就大不相同了。”
Changing qualifications wouldn't cost very much, but the prospects would be entirely different.”


“怎么呢？”
“How so?”


“盐大使只管盐场，
“A salt-station superintendent manages a saltworks, nothing more.

出息倒也不错，不过没有意思。
The income can be quite good, but the work has little meaning.

知县虽小，一县的父母官，
A county magistrate may be a minor official, yet he is responsible for the welfare of an entire county.

能杀人也能活人，
He has the power to take lives or save them.

可以好好做一番事业。”
He can accomplish something worthwhile.”


这两句话使得小胡肃然起敬，把刚才看不起他的那点感想，一扫而空了。
These words filled Young Hu with respect, sweeping away every trace of his earlier contempt.


“再说，知县到底是正印官，
“Besides, a county magistrate is the chief official of his jurisdiction.

不比盐大使，说起来总是佐杂，又是捐班的佐杂，
A salt-station superintendent is only a subordinate, and one who has purchased his qualification at that.

到处做‘磕头虫’，
He has to bow and scrape wherever he goes.

与我的性情也不相宜。”
That doesn't suit my temperament.”


“对，对！”小胡不断点头，
“Quite right!” Young Hu nodded repeatedly.

“那么，这一来，你要多少‘本钱’才够呢？”
“Then how much ‘capital’ would you need?”


“总得五百两银子。”
“At least five hundred taels of silver.”


“噢！”
“Oh.”

小胡没有再接口，王有龄也不再提，
Young Hu said no more, and Wang let the subject drop.

五百两银子不是小数目，
Five hundred taels was no small sum.

小胡不见得会有，就有也不见得肯借。
Young Hu might not possess it; even if he did, he might not be willing to lend it.


两人各有心事，
Each was now absorbed in his own thoughts.

吃闷酒无味，天也黑上来了，
Drinking in silence had lost its charm, and darkness was gathering.

王有龄推杯告辞，
Wang pushed his cup away and rose to leave.

小胡也不留他，
Young Hu did not try to detain him.

只说：“明天下午，我仍旧在这里等你，
He merely said, “I'll be here again tomorrow afternoon.

你来！”
Come and meet me.”


“有事吗？”王有龄微感诧异，
“Is there something you want?” Wang asked, slightly surprised.

“何不此刻就说？”
“Why not tell me now?”


“我有点小事托你，此刻还没有想停当。
“There's a small favour I'd like to ask, but I haven't quite thought it through.

还是明天下午再谈。
Let's talk tomorrow afternoon.

你一定要来，
You must come.

我在这里坐等，不见不散。”
I'll wait here until you do.”


看他如此叮嘱，王有龄也就答应了。
Young Hu was so insistent that Wang agreed.

到了第二天下午，依约而至，不见小胡的踪影。
The next afternoon he arrived as promised, but there was no sign of his young acquaintance.

泡一碗茶得好几文钱，对王有龄来说，是一种浪费，于是沿着山路一直走了过去。
A bowl of tea cost several copper coins, which Wang considered an unnecessary extravagance, so he continued along the hillside path.

城隍山上有好几座庙，
There were several temples on City God Hill.

庙前有耍把戏的，打拳卖膏药的，摆象棋摊的，
Before their gates stood acrobats, performers demonstrating martial arts to sell their medicinal plasters, and men with chessboards set up for challengers.

不花钱而可以消磨时光的地方多得很。
There were plenty of ways to pass the time without spending money.

他这里立一会，那面看一看，到红日衔山，方始走回原处，
Wang stopped here and watched there, returning to the meeting place only when the red sun touched the hills.

依旧不见小胡。
Young Hu was still nowhere to be seen.


是“不见不散”的死约会。
They had expressly agreed to wait until they met.

王有龄顿感进退两难，
Wang found himself in a quandary.

不等是自己失约，要等，天色已暮，晚饭尚无着落。
If he left, he would be breaking his word; if he stayed, night would soon fall, and he had made no provision for supper.

呆了半天，越想越急，
He stood there for some time, growing more anxious with every thought.

顿一顿足，往山下便走，
Finally, he stamped his foot and started downhill.

心中自语：明天见着小胡，非说他几句不可！
Tomorrow, he told himself, he would certainly give Young Hu a piece of his mind.

他又不是不知道自己的境况，在外面吃碗茶都得先算一算，
The young fellow knew perfectly well how things stood with him: even a bowl of tea away from home had to be carefully considered.

何苦捉弄人？
Why torment him like this?


走了不多几步，听见后面有人在叫：“王有龄，王有龄！”
He had gone only a few steps when someone called from behind, “Wang Youling! Wang Youling!”


转身一看，正是小胡，手里拿着手巾包，跑得气喘吁吁，满脸是汗。
He turned to see Young Hu running towards him, a handkerchief bundle in his hand, breathless and drenched in sweat.

见着了他的面，王有龄的气消了一半，
Half of Wang's anger vanished at the sight.

问道：“你怎么这时候才来？”
“Why are you only arriving now?” he asked.


“我知道你等得久了，
“I know you've been waiting a long time.

对不起，对不起！”
I'm sorry, truly sorry.”

小胡欣慰地笑着，
Young Hu smiled with relief.

“总算还好，
“Still, all's well.

耽迟不耽错。
I'm late, but I haven't missed you.

来，来，坐下来再说。”
Come, let's sit down and talk.”


王有龄也不知道他这话是什么意思？
Wang had no idea what he meant.

默默地跟着他走向一副设在橱下的座头，泡了两碗茶。
He followed silently to a table beneath the eaves, where they ordered two bowls of tea.

小胡有些魂不守舍似的，
Young Hu seemed distracted.

目送着经过的行人，手里紧捏住那个手巾包。
He watched the passers-by while gripping the handkerchief bundle tightly.


“小胡！”王有龄忍不住问了：“你说有事托我，快说吧！”
“Young Hu!” Wang could contain himself no longer. “You said you wanted a favour. What is it?”


“你打开来看，
“Open this and look.

不要给人看见。”他低声地说，把手巾包递了给王有龄。
Don't let anyone see,” he whispered, handing over the bundle.


他避开行人，悄悄启视，
Shielding it from passing eyes, Wang opened it discreetly.

里面是一叠银票，还有些碎银子，约莫有十几两。
Inside was a stack of bank drafts and some broken silver, perhaps a little over ten taels.


“怎么回事？”
“What's all this?”


“这就是你做官的本钱。”
“The capital you need to become an official.”


王有龄愣住了，
Wang was stunned.

一下子心里发酸，眼眶发热，
His heart suddenly ached and his eyes grew hot.

尽力忍住眼泪，把手巾包放在桌上，却不知怎么说才好。
Fighting back tears, he put the bundle on the table, unable to find a word to say.


“你最好点一点数。
“You'd better count it.

其中有一张三百两的，是京城里‘大德恒’的票子，
One draft is for three hundred taels, issued by Dadeheng in the capital.

认票不认人，你要当心失落。
It's payable to whoever presents it, so be careful not to lose it.

另外我又替你换了些零碎票子，都是有名的‘字号’，
I've also changed some of the money into smaller drafts, all from well-known firms.

一路上通行无阻。”
You'll be able to use them all along the way.”

小胡又说：“如果不为换票子，我早就来了。”
Young Hu added, “If it hadn't taken so long to change the drafts, I'd have been here much earlier.”


这里王有龄才想出来一句话：“小胡，你为什么待我这么好？”
Only then did Wang find something to say. “Young Hu, why are you so good to me?”


“朋友嘛！”小胡答道，
“Because we're friends!” Young Hu replied.

“我看你好比虎落平阳，英雄末路，心里说不出的难过，
“Seeing you like this—a tiger stranded on the plain, a hero with nowhere to turn—makes me more miserable than I can say.

一定要拉你一把，才睡得着觉。”
I couldn't sleep unless I gave you a hand.”


“唉！”王有龄毕竟忍不住了，
“Ah!” Wang could hold back no longer.

两行热泪，牵连不断。
Tears streamed down his face.


“何必，何必？
“Come now, come now!

这不是大丈夫气概！”
Where's your courage?”


这句话是很好的安慰，也是很好的激励、
The words comforted him and restored his spirits.

王有龄收拾涕泪，定一定神，
Wang wiped his tears away and composed himself.

才想起一件事，相交至今，受人绝大的恩惠，却是对他的名氏、身世，一无所知，
Only then did something occur to him: he had received an immense kindness from a man whose full name and background he did not even know.

岂不荒唐？
How absurd!


于是他微有窘色地问道：“小胡，还没有请教台甫？”
Slightly embarrassed, he asked, “Young Hu, I haven't yet had the honour of learning your courtesy name.”


“我叫胡光墉，字雪岩，
“My name is Hu Guangyong, and my courtesy name is Xueyan.

你呢，你的大号叫什么？”
What about yours?”


“我叫雪轩。”
“Xuexuan.”


“雪轩，雪岩！”胡雪岩自己念了两遍，抚掌笑道：“好极了，
“Xuexuan, Xueyan!” Hu repeated the names twice, then clapped his hands and laughed. “Splendid!

声音很近，好象一个人。
They sound so alike we might almost be the same person.

你叫我雪岩，我叫你雪轩。”
You call me Xueyan, and I'll call you Xuexuan.”


“是，是！
“Yes, yes!

雪岩，我还要请教你，
Xueyan, there's something else I'd like to ask.

府上……”
Your family...”


这是问他的家世，胡雪岩笑笑不肯多说：
He was asking about Hu's background, but Hu merely smiled and declined to say much.

“守一点薄产过日了，
“I live on a little property.

没有什么谈头。
There's nothing worth mentioning.

雪轩，我问你，你几时动身？”
Tell me, Xuexuan, when will you leave?”


“我不敢耽搁。
“I dare not delay.

把舍间咯略安排一番，总在三、五日内就动身。
Once I've put things in order at home, I'll set off within three to five days.

如果一切顺利，年底就可以回来。
If all goes well, I should be back by the end of the year.

雪岩，我一定要走路子，分发到浙江来，你我弟兄好在一起。”
Xueyan, I'll do everything I can to secure an assignment to Zhejiang, so that we two brothers can be together.”


“好极了。”
“Splendid!”

胡雪岩的“好极了”，已成口头禅，
The word was already becoming Hu's habitual response.

“后天我们仍旧在这里会面，
“Let's meet here again the day after tomorrow.

我给你饯行。”
I'll give you a farewell dinner.”


“我一定来。”
“I'll certainly come.”


到了第三天，王有龄午饭刚过，就来赴约。
On the appointed day, Wang came just after lunch.

他穿了估衣铺买的直罗长衫，亮纱马褂，手里拿一柄“舒莲记”有名的“杭扇”，
He wore a gauze gown and a glossy gauze riding jacket bought at a second-hand clothes shop, and carried one of the famous Hangzhou folding fans made by Shulianji.

泡着茶等，
He ordered tea and waited.

等到夭黑不见胡雪岩的踪影，
Darkness fell without any sign of Hu.

寻亦没处寻，只好再等。
There was nowhere he could go to look for him, so he could only keep waiting.


天气热了，城隍山上来品茗纳凉的，络绎不绝。
The weather had grown hot, and a steady stream of people came up City God Hill to drink tea and enjoy the cool air.

王有龄目迎目送着每一个行人，把脖子都摆得酸了，就是盼不着胡雪岩。
Wang watched every arrival and departure until his neck ached, but the man he hoped to see never appeared.


夜深客散，茶店收摊子，这下才把王有龄撵走。
Late at night, when the customers had gone and the teahouse was closing, Wang finally had to leave.

他已经雇好了船，无法不定，
He had already hired a boat and could not cancel his departure.

第二天五更时分上船，竟不能与胡雪岩见一面话别。
He boarded in the hours before dawn the next morning, without having had a chance to see Hu and say goodbye.


在王有龄北上不久，浙江的政局有了变化：
Shortly after Wang left for the north, Zhejiang's provincial administration changed.

巡抚常大淳调湖北，云南巡抚黄宗汉改调浙江，
Governor Chang Dachun was transferred to Hubei, and Huang Zonghan, governor of Yunnan, was reassigned to Zhejiang.

未到任以前由布政使——通称“藩司”、老百姓尊称为“藩台”的旗人椿寿署理。
Until Huang arrived, the acting governorship was held by Chunshou, a bannerman serving as provincial treasurer—a post commonly called fansi and respectfully referred to by ordinary people as fantai.


黄宗汉字寿臣，福建晋江人。
Huang Zonghan, whose courtesy name was Shouchen, came from Jinjiang in Fujian.

他是道光十五年乙未正科的翰林，
He had entered the Hanlin Academy after passing the regular metropolitan examination in the fifteenth year of the Daoguang reign, the yiwei year.

这一榜人才济济，科运甚隆，
That examination produced a remarkable crop of talented men whose careers prospered.

那年，咸丰二年，当到巡抚的就有三个，广东叶名琛、江西张芾，
By the year in question, the second year of Xianfeng, three had become provincial governors: Huang himself, Ye Mingchen in Guangdong, and Zhang Fu in Jiangxi.

当到二品大员的有何桂清、吕贤基、彭蕴章、罗惇衍，
Others who had reached the second official rank included He Guiqing, Lü Xianji, Peng Yunzhang, and Luo Dunyan.

还有杭州的许乃钊，与他老兄许乃普，都当内阁学士。
Xu Naizhao of Hangzhou and his elder brother Xu Naipu had both become grand secretariat academicians.


这黄宗汉据说是个很能干的人，但是关于他的操守与治家，批评极坏。
Huang was said to be highly capable, but his personal integrity and the management of his household were subjects of severe criticism.


到任以后，传说他向椿寿索贿四万两银子，
After taking office, he was said to have demanded a bribe of forty thousand taels from Chunshou.

椿寿没有买他的帐，于是多事了。
Chunshou ignored the demand, and trouble followed.


其时漕运正在改变办法。
At the time, the system for transporting the grain tax was undergoing change.

因为海禁已开，而且河道湮淤，加以洪杨的起事，
Maritime restrictions had been lifted, river channels were silting up, and the rebellion led by Hong Xiuquan and Yang Xiuqing had broken out.

所以江苏的苏、松、太各属改用海运，浙江则是试办，
The jurisdictions of Suzhou, Songjiang, and Taicang in Jiangsu therefore switched to sea transport, while Zhejiang was preparing to try it.

椿寿既为藩司，又署理巡抚，责无旁贷，
As both provincial treasurer and acting governor, Chunshou could not evade responsibility.

当然要亲自料理这件公事。
He had to oversee the matter personally.


漕运的漕，原来就是以舟运谷的意思。
The word cao in caoyun, the grain-tax transport system, originally meant carrying grain by boat.

多少年来都是河运，先是黄河，后来是运河，
For centuries the grain had travelled by river, first on the Yellow River and later on the Grand Canal.

而运河又有多少次的变迁兴作，
The canal itself had undergone repeated changes and construction.

直到康熙年间，治河名臣靳辅，于成龙先后开“中河”，历时千余年的运河，才算大功告成。
Only during the Kangxi reign, when the celebrated river administrators Jin Fu and Yu Chenglong successively opened sections of the Middle Canal, was this waterway, more than a thousand years in the making, finally considered complete.


这条南起杭州，北抵京师，流经浙江、江苏、山东、河北四省，全长两千多里的水道，为大清朝带来了一百五十年的盛运。
Running from Hangzhou in the south to the capital in the north, through Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Shandong, and Hebei, the waterway extended for more than two thousand li and had brought the Qing dynasty a century and a half of prosperity.

不幸的是，黄河的情况，越来越坏，
Unfortunately, conditions on the Yellow River grew steadily worse.

有些地方，河底积淤，高过人家屋脊，全靠两面堤防约束，
In places, accumulated silt raised its bed above the rooftops of nearby houses, leaving the river confined only by the embankments on either side.

“春水船如天上行”，真到了束手无策的地步。
“On spring waters, boats seem to sail through the sky”: matters had truly reached the point where no remedy seemed possible.

而运河受黄河的累，在嘉庆末年，几乎也成了“绝症”。
Burdened by the Yellow River's troubles, the Grand Canal itself was almost beyond cure by the end of the Jiaqing reign.

于是道光初年有海运之议。
Hence the proposal, early in the Daoguang reign, to ship the grain by sea.


在嘉应末年时有齐彦槐其人，著有一篇《海运南僧议》，
Towards the end of the Jiaqing reign, a man named Qi Yanhuai wrote an essay setting out the case for shipping the southern grain tax by sea.

条分缕析断言“一举而众善备”，
He examined the matter point by point and declared that a single measure would secure a whole range of benefits.

但地方大吏不愿轻易更张。
Senior provincial officials, however, were reluctant to change established arrangements.

直到湖南安化的陶文毅公陶澍，由安徽巡抚调江苏，锐意革新，消除盐、漕两事的积弊，齐彦槐的建议，才有一个实验的机会。
The proposal received a trial only after Tao Shu of Anhua, Hunan—later honoured with the posthumous title Wenyi—was transferred from the governorship of Anhui to that of Jiangsu. Determined to reform the administration, Tao set about eliminating longstanding abuses in both the salt system and grain transport.


这次实验由陶澍亲自主持，
Tao personally supervised the trial.

在上海设立“海运总局”，他亲自雇好专门运载关东豆麦的“沙船”一千艘，名为“三不象”的海船几十艘，
He established a Sea Transport Bureau in Shanghai and hired a thousand flat-bottomed sand junks normally used to carry beans and wheat from the northeast, together with several dozen seagoing vessels of the type known as sanbuxiang, or “unlike any of the three.”

分两次运米一百五十多万石到天津，
In two shipments, they transported more than one and a half million shi of rice to Tianjin.

结果获得极大的成功，省时省费，米质受损极微。
The experiment was an outstanding success: it saved both time and money, and very little of the rice deteriorated.


承运的船商，运漕而北，回程运豆，
The shipowners carried grain-tax rice north and beans on the return journey.

一向漕船南下“回空”，海船北上“回空”，
Previously, canal grain boats had returned south empty, while the coastal vessels had sailed north without cargo.

现在平白多一笔收入，
Now an additional source of income had appeared.

而且出力的船商，还“赏给顶戴”做了官，
Shipowners who distinguished themselves were even rewarded with official rank and the right to wear the corresponding hat insignia.

真正是皆大欢喜。
Everyone was delighted.


但是到了第二年，这样的好事竟不再做下去！依然恢复河运。
Yet the very next year this excellent arrangement was abandoned, and river transport resumed.

因为，不知道有多少人靠这条运河的漕船来剥削老百姓，
Countless people depended on the canal grain boats as a means of exploiting the population.

他们不愿意革新！
They had no desire for reform.


漕运的弊端与征粮的弊端是不可分的，
Abuses in grain transport were inseparable from abuses in grain collection.

征粮的权责属于州县，
Responsibility for collecting the tax lay with the departments and counties.

这七品的正印官，特称为“大老爷”，在任两件大事：刑名、钱谷。
A county magistrate, a seventh-rank chief official respectfully called “His Honour,” had two major concerns: criminal justice and revenue.

延请“绍兴师爷”至少亦得两名：“刑名师爷”和“钱谷师爷”。
He needed at least two of the professional advisers known as Shaoxing secretaries, one specialising in legal cases and the other in money and grain.

县大老爷的成名发财，都靠这两个人。
His reputation and his fortune depended on these two men.


钱谷师爷的本事不在算盘上，在于能了解情况，善于应付几种人，
A revenue adviser's chief skill lay not in his use of the abacus but in his grasp of local conditions and his ability to deal with several kinds of people.

第一种是书办，世代相传，
The first were the clerks, whose positions passed down through their families.

每人手里有一本底册，哪家有多少田？该纳粮多少？都记载在这本册子上，
Each kept a master register recording how much land each household possessed and how much grain tax it owed.

为不传之秘。
These records were jealously guarded secrets.


第二种是“特殊人物”，
The second group consisted of people with special standing.

他们所纳的粮，都有专门名称，
Even the grain they paid had distinctive names.

做过官的绅士人家的“衿米”，举人、秀才、监生是“料米”，
Grain from gentry households whose members had held office was called jinmi; that paid by holders of provincial examination degrees, lower examination qualifications, or Imperial Academy status was called liaomi.

这两种米不能多收，该多少就多少，否则便有麻烦。
Neither group could be made to pay more than its proper assessment without causing trouble.

再有一种名为“讼米”，专好无事生非打官司的讼棍所纳的粮，
Another category, songmi, was the grain paid by habitual litigants who delighted in stirring up lawsuits.

也要当心。
They too had to be handled carefully.

总而言这一名话，刁恶霸道，不易对付的那班“特殊人物”，必须敷衍，
In short, the troublesome, overbearing people who were difficult to manage had to be kept satisfied.

分量不足，米色粗劣，亦得照收不误。
Their grain had to be accepted even if it was short in weight or poor in quality.

甚至虚给“粮串”——纳粮的凭证，买得个安静二字。
Sometimes tax receipts were issued without payment at all, simply to buy peace.


有人占便宜，当然有人吃亏，
If some people gained, others inevitably lost.

各种剥削耗费，加上县大老爷自己的好处，统统都出在良善小民头上，
Every exaction and expense, together with the magistrate's own share of the proceeds, fell on the honest, ordinary taxpayer.

这叫做“浮收”，
This was called “excess collection.”

最“黑”的地方，“浮收”到正额的一半以上，该纳一石米的，起码要纳一石五斗。
In the worst places, the excess amounted to more than half the official assessment: a household owing one shi of rice had to deliver at least one and a half.

于是有所谓“包户”，
Hence the rise of tax contractors.

他们或者与官吏有勾结，或者能挟制官吏，
Some colluded with the officials; others could bring pressure to bear on them.

小户如托他们“包缴”，比自己到粮柜上去缴纳，便宜得多。
For small households, paying through these contractors was much cheaper than delivering their grain directly to the tax office.


第三种就是漕船上的人。
The third group consisted of the men who worked on the grain boats.

漕船都是官船，额定数字过万，实际仅六千余艘，
These were government vessels, officially numbering more than ten thousand but in reality only a little over six thousand.

分驻运河各地，一地称为一帮。
They were stationed in groups along the canal, each local group being called a bang.

这就是游侠组织“青帮”之帮的出典。
This was the origin of the word bang in the name of the Green Gang, the fraternity of men who lived by their own code.


帮中的管事及水手，都称为帮丁，
Both the managers and the sailors in these associations were known collectively as transport servicemen.

其中又有屯丁、旗丁、尖丁之分。
Among them were men attached to military farming settlements, ordinary registered boatmen, and headmen.

尖丁是实际上的头目，连护漕的千总、把总都得听他的指挥。
The headmen were the real leaders: even the military captains and lieutenants assigned to escort the grain had to follow their instructions.

州县衙门开仓怔粮，粮户缴纳，漕船开到，验收装船，
When local offices opened their granaries for tax collection, households delivered their grain, and the transport boats arrived to inspect and load it.

名为“受兑”。
This process was called acceptance and transfer.

一面征粮，一面受兑，川流不息，那自然是再顺利不过的事，
If collection and loading proceeded continuously together, everything could hardly have gone more smoothly.

但是这一来漕船上就玩不出花样来了。
But such efficiency would have left the boatmen no opportunity to play their tricks.


他们的第一个花样是“看米色”。
Their first trick concerned the inspection of the rice.

由于漕船过淮安时，漕运总督要“盘粮”点数，到通州起岸入仓时，仓场侍郎要验看米质，
When the boats passed Huai'an, the director-general of grain transport checked the cargo quantities; when the grain was landed and stored at Tongzhou, the granary vice-minister inspected its quality.

如有不符，都由漕船负责，
The boats were responsible for any discrepancy.

因此，他们在受兑时，验看米色，原是分所当为。
It was therefore perfectly proper for their crews to examine the rice before accepting it.

但米色好坏，仅凭目视，并无标准，
Quality, however, was judged by sight alone, without an objective standard.

这样就可以挑剔了，
That left ample room for fault-finding.

一廒一廒看过去，不是说米色太杂，就是不够干燥，不肯受兑。
The inspectors went from one granary compartment to another, claiming that the rice was too mixed or insufficiently dry, and refused to accept it.


以一般的情况而言，开仓十日，所有的仓厥就都装满了，
Under normal circumstances, all the granary compartments would be full within ten days of the start of collection.

此时如不疏运上船，则后来的粮户，无仓可以贮米，势必停征。
Unless the grain was moved out and loaded onto boats, later arrivals would have nowhere to deposit their rice, and collection would have to stop.

粮户也就要等待，
The taxpayers would be left waiting.

一天两天还不要紧，老百姓无非发发牢骚而已，日子一久，废时失业，还要贴上盘缠，
A day or two might bring no more than grumbling, but a longer delay kept them from their work and forced them to spend money on lodging and food.

自然非吵不可，
Trouble was inevitable.

这叫做“闹潜”，是件极严重的事，地方官往往会得到极严厉的处分。
Such disturbances over the grain tax were a serious matter and often brought severe punishment upon local officials.

倘或是个刮地皮的贪官，这一闹漕就不定就会激起民变，更是件可以送命的大祸。
If the magistrate was especially rapacious, a disturbance might even develop into a popular revolt—a disaster that could cost him his life.


因此，钱谷师爷，便要指挥书办出来与“看米色”的旗丁讲斤头，
The revenue adviser would therefore send the clerks out to bargain with the boatmen responsible for inspecting the rice.

倘或讲不下来，而督运的委员，怕误了限期，催令启程，那些帮丁就不问兑足不兑足，只管自己开船。
If no agreement could be reached and the transport commissioners, anxious about the deadline, ordered the fleet to leave, the crews would sail regardless of whether their full quota had been loaded.

这时的州县可就苦了，
That left the local authorities in a miserable position.

必须设法自运漕米，一路赶上去补足，称为“随帮交兑”。
They had to arrange their own transport, pursue the fleet, and deliver the outstanding grain along the way, a practice known as “following the fleet to complete delivery.”


幸而取得妥协，漕米兑竣，应该出给名为“通关”的收据，
If a compromise was reached and the grain finally accepted, the crews were supposed to issue a clearance receipt.

这时尖丁出面了，
At this point the headman appeared.

先议“私费”，就是他个人的“好处”，
First came negotiations over his “private expenses”—his personal reward.

私费议妥，再议“通帮公费”，是全帮的好处。
Once that had been settled, there were the “public expenses of the whole association,” a payment for everyone's benefit.

这些看米色所受的勒索，以及尖丁私费、通帮公费，自然羊毛出在羊身上，由浮收来支付。
The money extorted during inspection, the headman's private reward, and the association's collective payment all came, of course, from the same source: the excess grain collected from the taxpayers.


这以后，就该帮丁受勒索了，
After that, it was the boatmen's turn to be extorted.

首先是“过淮”投文过堂，照例有各种陋规，
First came the formal submission of documents and appearance before the authorities at Huai'an, accompanied by the customary unofficial fees.

一帮船总要花到五六百两到一千两银子。
A fleet generally had to spend between five or six hundred and a thousand taels to pass this stage.

这一关一过，沿路过闸过坝，处处要送红包，大概每一艘船要十几两银子。
Beyond it, every lock and dam required another payment, adding up to a dozen or more taels per boat.

最后到了通州，花样更好，
At Tongzhou, the arrangements were still more elaborate.

要投四个衙门的文，
Documents had to be submitted to four government offices.

有人专门代办，每船十三两银子，十两铺排四个衙门，三两是代办者的酬劳。
Professional intermediaries handled the process for thirteen taels a boat: ten to distribute among the offices and three for themselves.

等漕米上岸入仓，伸手要钱的人数不清，总要花到三五十两。
When the rice was finally landed and stored, so many hands reached out for money that another thirty to fifty taels would be spent.

所以帮丁勒索州县，无非悖入悖出。
What the boatmen extorted from the counties was, in effect, taken from them in turn.


帮丁的苦楚犹不止此，
Their troubles did not end there.

一路还要受人的欺侮。
They also suffered intimidation along the route.

在运河里，遇到运铜运铅的船，以及木排，千万要当心，那是在运河是蛮不讲理出了名的，
On the canal, they had to be especially wary of vessels carrying copper or lead and of timber rafts, whose crews were notorious for their lawlessness.

撞沉了漕船，他们可以逃散，帮丁则非倾家荡产来赔不可，
If they rammed and sank a grain boat, they could scatter and disappear, while the grain boat's crew might have to sacrifice everything they owned to compensate for the loss.

因为如此，帮丁便格外团结，以求自保，
The boatmen consequently formed unusually close bonds for their own protection.

“青帮”之起因如此，
This was how the Green Gang began.

所以，他们的“海底”名为“通漕”，并不是世俗所称的“通草”。
Its secret code was therefore called Tongcao, meaning “the grain-transport connection,” rather than the similarly pronounced name of the rice-paper plant, as people commonly supposed.


一度行之有效，但以积习已深，惯于更张的南漕海运，终于咸丰元年旧事重提。
Sea transport for the southern grain tax had proved effective once, only to be abandoned because entrenched habits resisted change. In the first year of Xianfeng, it was finally proposed again.

这出于两个原因，第一个是人，第二个是地。
Two factors brought this about: one concerned a person, the other the physical circumstances.


这个人是两江总督陆建瀛，
The person was Lu Jianying, the governor-general of Liangjiang.

湖北人，极能干，而且善于结交，
A native of Hubei, he was highly capable and skilled at cultivating connections.

所以公卿延誉，负一时物望。
Senior officials praised him, and he enjoyed considerable public esteem.

他颇有意步武陶澍，留一番政绩。
He wished to follow Tao Shu's example and leave a record of achievement.

陶澍改盐法，淮北行之大效，而淮南依旧，
Tao's salt reforms had worked well north of the Huai River, while the old system remained in place to the south.

陆建瀛在淮南继陶未竟之功。
Lu took up Tao's unfinished work there.

漕运也是如此，他得到户部尚书孙瑞珍的支持，准备恢复海运。
He intended to do the same with grain transport and, with the support of Sun Ruizhen, president of the Board of Revenue, prepared to restore sea shipments.


适逢其会的是，运河出了问题，
At just this moment, disaster struck the canal.

在徐州附近的丰县以北决口，
A breach opened north of Fengxian, near Xuzhou.

“全河北趋，由沛县之华山、戚山分注微山、昭阳等湖，挟清水外泛，运河闸、坝、纤堤，均已漫淹”，
“The entire river turned north; from Huashan and Qishan in Peixian it poured into Weishan, Zhaoyang, and the other lakes, driving their waters outward until the canal's locks, dams, and towpath embankments were all submerged.”

朝廷一方面拨巨款抢救，一方面也加强了改用海运的决心。
The court allocated substantial funds for emergency repairs, while its resolve to adopt sea transport grew stronger.


海运之议，奉旨由两江总督陆建瀛、江苏巡抚杨文定、浙江巡抚常大淳，会同筹划。
The emperor ordered Lu Jianying, Governor Yang Wending of Jiangsu, and Governor Chang Dachun of Zhejiang to draw up a joint plan.

结果决定咸丰二年江苏的苏州，松江、常州、镇江、太仓等四府一州的溜米，改用海运。
They decided that in the second year of Xianfeng the grain from Suzhou, Songjiang, Changzhou, and Zhenjiang prefectures, together with Taicang department, would travel by sea.

浙江则是试办，但其间又有反复，未成定议。
Zhejiang was to conduct a trial, but repeated changes of direction there prevented a final decision.


就在这段期间中，椿寿由湖南布政使调浙江。
It was during this period that Chunshou was transferred from the provincial treasury of Hunan to that of Zhejiang.

当朝命初下时，黄宗汉是掌理一省司法的浙江按察使，通称“臬司”，
When the appointment was first announced, Huang Zonghan was serving as Zhejiang's provincial judicial commissioner, commonly known as the niesi, responsible for the administration of justice throughout the province.

等椿寿到任时，他已经调差了。
By the time Chunshou arrived, Huang had already been transferred elsewhere.


第二天，洪军由广西而湖南，湖北吃紧，清文宗把善于“捕盗”的常大淳，调为湖北巡抚。
The next day, with Hong's forces moving from Guangxi into Hunan and the situation in Hubei becoming urgent, the Xianfeng emperor transferred Chang Dachun, known for his skill at suppressing bandits, to the governorship of Hubei.

浙江巡抚由藩司椿寿署理。
Chunshou became acting governor of Zhejiang.


椿寿的运气太坏。
Chunshou's luck could hardly have been worse.

这年的浙江，省城杭州及附近各州县，自五月以后，雨量稀少，旱荒已成，
From the fifth month onward, little rain fell in Hangzhou or the surrounding districts, and drought had taken hold.

于是对他发生两大不利，
This created two serious difficulties for him.

第一是钱粮征收不起，第二是河浅不利于舟行，影响漕运。
Tax revenues could not be collected, and shallow waterways obstructed navigation, delaying the grain shipments.


江苏的海运，非常顺利，
Jiangsu's sea transport proceeded smoothly.

四府一州的漕粮，糙米三十二万多石，白米二万七千余石，于三月间出海北上，安然运到。
More than 320,000 shi of unpolished rice and over 27,000 shi of polished rice from the four prefectures and one department sailed north in the third month and arrived safely.

而浙江的漕米，到九月间还未启运，这是前所未有现象。
Zhejiang's grain, however, had still not been dispatched by the ninth month—an unprecedented situation.


在此以前，也就是渐江正闹旱灾的五月间，为了军事上的需要，各省巡抚有个小小的调整，
Earlier, in the fifth month, while Zhejiang was suffering from drought, military needs had prompted a small reshuffle of provincial governors.

云南巡抚张亮基调湖南，遗缺由甘肃布政使黄宗汉接充。
Zhang Liangji, governor of Yunnan, was transferred to Hunan, and Huang Zonghan, then provincial treasurer of Gansu, was appointed to succeed him.


他不愿意去云南，
Huang did not want to go to Yunnan.

经过一番活动，很快地改调浙江。
After some manoeuvring, he soon secured a reassignment to Zhejiang.

不过一年的功夫，重回杭州时，已非昔比。
Barely a year had passed, yet when he returned to Hangzhou, his position was greatly changed.


署理巡抚椿寿交卸以后，仍旧干他的藩司。
After handing over the acting governorship, Chunshou resumed his duties as provincial treasurer.

据说黄宗汉在第一天接见椿寿时，就作了个暗示，椿寿的“纱帽”在他手里，
It was said that at their first meeting Huang hinted that Chunshou's official cap—his position—was in Huang's hands.

如果想保全，赶快送四万两银子的“红包”过去。
If he wished to keep it, he should promptly send a gift of forty thousand taels.

黄宗汉敢于作此勒索，就因为椿寿在漕运上，已经迟延，
Huang dared make this demand because Chunshou was already behind schedule with the grain shipments.

如果上司肯替他说话。可以在天灾上找理由，有处分，亦属轻微。
A sympathetic superior could plead the natural disaster on his behalf and keep any penalty light.

否则，耽延了“天瘦正供”，将获严谴。
Otherwise, delaying the grain due to the imperial storehouses could bring severe punishment.


椿寿没有理会他，于是黄宗汉想了个极狠毒的手法来“整”人。
Chunshou ignored him, so Huang devised a particularly cruel way to deal with him.

他认为本年漕粮，启运太迟，到达通州交仓，粮船不能依照限期“回空”，
This year's grain, he argued, had been dispatched so late that, after delivering it at Tongzhou, the boats could not return empty within the prescribed time.

这样便要影响下一年的漕运。
That would delay the following year's shipments.

就在这个言之成理的说法上来整椿寿。
It was upon this plausible argument that he built his attack.


心里已有成算，表面丝毫不露，
With his plan already formed, Huang revealed nothing.

把椿寿请到抚院来谈公事，问起清运的情形。
He invited Chunshou to the governor's office to discuss business and asked how the grain transport was progressing.


一提到这上面，椿寿自己先就紧张，
The subject immediately made Chunshou uneasy.

“回大人的话，”他说，“今年浙江的漕运，无比如何要担处分了！”
“Your Excellency,” he replied, “there is no avoiding a penalty over Zhejiang's grain transport this year.”


“谁担处分啊？”黄宗汉故意这样问。
“A penalty for whom?” Huang asked deliberately.


“自然是司里。”
“For this office, naturally.”

藩、臬两司向巡抚回话，照例自称“司里”。
When addressing the governor, both the provincial treasurer and the judicial commissioner customarily referred to themselves as “this office.”


“这也不担处分的事。”
“It is not simply a question of a penalty.”

黄宗汉用这句话先做一个伏笔，却又立即撇开不谈，
Having planted this warning, Huang immediately moved on.

“贵司倒先说说看，究竟因何迟误？”
“First tell me exactly what caused the delay.”


“自然是因为天旱
“The drought, naturally.

水浅，河道干淤。
The water is low and the channels have dried up and silted over.

已经奏报过的。”
This has already been reported to the throne.”


“天旱是五月以后的事。
“The drought began after the fifth month.

请问，照定例，本省漕船，每年什么时候开，什么时候‘过淮’，什么时候‘回空’？”
Tell me, under the regulations, when should this province's grain boats depart, when should they pass Huai'an, and when should they return empty?”


一连三问，把椿寿堵得哑口无言。
The three questions in succession left Chunshou speechless.

照定例，江西和浙江的漕船，限在二月底以前尽数开行。
Under the regulations, all grain boats from Jiangxi and Zhejiang were supposed to sail before the end of the second month.

年深日久，定例有变，但至迟亦不会过四月。
Time had modified the practice, but even the latest departures should not extend beyond the fourth month.

现在秋风已起，漕船开行的还不过一半，
Now the autumn winds were blowing, and scarcely half the boats had left.

这该怎么说呢？
How could he explain that?


他迟迟不答，黄宗汉也不开口，是逼着他非说不可。
Chunshou hesitated, and Huang remained silent, forcing him to answer.

椿寿无奈，只好这样答道：“大人也在浙江待过，
At last he said, “Your Excellency has served in Zhejiang before.

漕帮的积弊，还有什么不明白的？
Surely you know the longstanding abuses in the transport associations.

漕丁有种种花样，譬如说陈漕带私货罗……”
The boatmen have all sorts of tricks—for instance, carrying private cargo with the previous season's grain...”


椿寿的话未完，抚台便一个钉子碰了过来：
Before he could finish, the governor cut him short.

“天下乌鸦一般黑，
“Crows are black everywhere.

各省漕丁都是一样的。”
The boatmen are the same in every province.”


“今年略微不同，
“This year is somewhat different.

因为奉旨筹议南漕海运，漕帮不免观望，
Since the imperial order to consider shipping the southern grain by sea, the associations have been waiting to see what will happen.

这也是延误的原因之一。”
That is also one reason for the delay.”


“观望什么？”黄宗汉大声问道，
“Waiting to see what?” Huang demanded loudly.

“议办海运是来年新漕之事，
“The proposed sea shipments concern next year's new grain.

跟今年何干？”
What have they to do with this year's?”


振振有词一问，椿寿语塞，
The forceful question left Chunshou without an answer.

既然来年有此改变之议，漕丁自不免有所瞻顾，以致鼓不起劲来，
Naturally, the prospect of change the following year made the boatmen uncertain and dampened their enthusiasm.

但身为藩司，署理抚院，这些地方正该督催，
But as provincial treasurer and acting governor, he ought precisely to have pressed them to proceed.

否则便是失职，
Otherwise, he had neglected his duty.

所以椿寿无同可解。
There was no defence he could offer.


“现在怎么办呢？”黄宗汉又忧形于色地说，
“What are we to do now?” Huang went on, assuming a worried expression.

“事情总要办通才行啊！”
“The business must be carried through somehow.”


“是，是！”椿寿赶紧答道，
“Yes, indeed!” Chunshou answered quickly.

“司里尽力去催，
“This office will press them as hard as possible.

总在这个把月里，一定可以全数启运。”
Within a month or so, all the boats will certainly be on their way.”


“个把月？”黄宗汉皱着眉说，
“A month or so?” Huang frowned.

“说老实话，这上面我还不大弄得清楚。
“To be frank, I am not yet very familiar with this business.

反正本年漕运，自前任常中丞调任以后，都由老兄一手经理。
In any event, since Governor Chang's transfer, this year's grain transport has been entirely under your management.

以后该如何办理，等我商量了再说。“
As for what should be done next, let me consult others before deciding.”


他这段话有两层用意，
His words served two purposes.

第一是说目前还不甚了解漕运的情况，等了解了又当别论，留下翻覆的余地，
By saying that he did not yet fully understand the situation, he left himself free to take a different position once he had learned more.

第二是“一手经理”四个字，指明了全部责任。
And by stressing that Chunshou had managed it entirely himself, he placed the whole responsibility on him.


椿寿原是“上三旗”的公子哥儿，
Chunshou had grown up as a privileged young gentleman of the Upper Three Banners.

这几年在外面历练了一番。纨袴的积习，固已大减，而人心的险，却无深知，
Several years of provincial service had done much to reduce his idle, aristocratic habits, but he still knew little of the treachery people were capable of.

哪里去理会得黄宗汉的深意？
How could he grasp Huang's real meaning?

还只当抚台语气缓和，事无大碍，
He took the governor's milder tone as a sign that nothing was seriously wrong.

所以连声应诺，辞出抚院，赶紧召集手下，
Assenting repeatedly, he left the governor's office and hurried to summon his subordinates.

商议如何设法把未走的船，能够早日开行，
They must find a way to get the remaining vessels moving as soon as possible.

只要一出浙江省境，责任就轻得多了。
Once the boats crossed Zhejiang's border, his responsibility would be considerably reduced.


于是椿寿即刻召集督粮道和其他经办漕运的官员，
He immediately assembled the grain intendant and the other officials involved in transport.

一面宣达了抚台的意思，一面力竭声嘶地要大家“各秉天良”，务必在最短期间内，设法让漕船全数开出。
After conveying the governor's views, he urged them, almost shouting himself hoarse, to act according to their consciences and find a way to dispatch every boat with the least possible delay.


别处都还好办，
Most places presented manageable difficulties.

麻烦的是湖属八帮，
The real problem lay with the eight associations under Huzhou.

浙江湖川府是东南膏腴之区，额定漕粮三十八万八千余石，关系重大，
Huzhou prefecture occupied some of the richest land in the southeast and had an assessed grain quota of more than 388,000 shi, making it especially important.

偏偏这八帮的漕船，一艘都动弹不得。
Yet not one boat belonging to its eight associations could move.


椿寿看看情势严重，不得不亲自到湖州去督催。
Recognising the seriousness of the situation, Chunshou had no choice but to go to Huzhou and supervise matters himself.


湖州运漕，有朵运河的支流，往东沿太湖南岸，入江苏省境平望的大运河。
Huzhou's grain boats used a branch of the canal running east along the southern shore of Lake Tai to join the Grand Canal at Pingwang in Jiangsu.

这种交流不到一百里长，但所经的双林，南浔两镇，为膏腴中的膏腴。
The branch was less than a hundred li long, but it passed through Shuanglin and Nanxun, two towns that were rich even by the standards of this fertile region.


南浔的殷富，号称“四狮八象”，海内闻名。
The wealth of Nanxun's great families, known collectively as the “Four Lions and Eight Elephants,” was famous throughout the country.

听得藩台驾到，照例以捐班道台的身分，尽地主之谊，
On learning that the provincial treasurer was coming, they extended the hospitality expected of local men who had purchased the rank of circuit intendant.

他们饮食起居的讲究，虽不比盐商、河工的穷奢极侈，但已远非一般宫贵之家可比。
Their refinements in food and daily living might not match the extravagant excesses of the salt merchants or river-administration circles, but they far surpassed those of ordinary official and aristocratic households.


身处名匠经营的园林，坐对水陆并陈的盛馔，
Chunshou found himself in gardens designed by master craftsmen, seated before sumptuous dishes drawn from land and water.

开宴照例开戏，
As was customary, the banquet began with an opera performance.

南浔富家都有自己的戏班，砌末、行头，无不精美，
Nanxun's wealthy families maintained their own troupes, with exquisite props and costumes.

这时集合精英，奏演名曲，
Now their finest performers were brought together to present celebrated scenes.

而椿寿索然寡欢，却又不得不勉强敷衍，
Yet Chunshou took no pleasure in any of it and had to force himself to be polite.

因而这样豪华享受的场台，在他反觉得受罪，
What should have been a luxurious entertainment felt instead like an ordeal.

耳中听着《长生殴》的《夜雨闻铃》，心里想的却是怎得下他三天三夜的大雨，
As he listened to “Hearing Bells in the Night Rain” from The Palace of Eternal Life, he could think only of how welcome three days and nights of heavy rain would be.

运河水满，让搁浅的漕船，得以趁一帆西风，往东而去？
If the canal filled, the stranded grain boats could catch a west wind and sail eastward.


想着漕船，椿寿无论如何坐不住了。
The thought of the boats made it impossible for him to remain seated.

托词“身子不爽”，向主人再三道歉告辞，回到行辕。
Pleading indisposition, he repeatedly apologised to his hosts and returned to his temporary headquarters.


行辕里已经有许多人在等着。
Many people were already waiting there.

这些人分为三类，
They fell into three groups.

一类是漕帮中的“领运千总”，名义上算是押运的武官，照原来的传统，多由武举人中选拔，
First were the captains charged with leading the grain convoys, military officers traditionally selected from men who had passed the provincial military examination.

一类是临时委派的押运官，大多为候补州县，走路子钻上这个差使，多少弄几文“调剂调剂”，
Second were the temporarily appointed escort officials, mostly expectant local magistrates who had used their connections to secure an assignment that might bring them a little money to ease their circumstances.

再一类就是各帮中真正的头脑：“尖丁”。
Third were the real leaders of the associations: the headmen.


“尖丁”的身分是小兵，这还是明朝“卫所”演变下来的制度。
In official status, a headman was merely a common soldier, a survival of the military settlement system of the Ming dynasty.

小兵与二品大员的藩台，身分相差不知几许？
The gulf between such a man and a provincial treasurer of the second rank was immense.

照平日来说，连见椿寿的面都难，
Ordinarily, he would scarcely have been allowed into Chunshou's presence.

但此刻也顾不得这些官派了！
Now, however, official dignity had to yield.

要设法能让漕船开动，非找尖丁来谈，才商议得出切实的办法，所以椿寿吩咐，一体传见。
Any practical plan for moving the boats required the headmen's participation, so Chunshou ordered that everyone be admitted.


行辕借在一家富户的两进屋子，
His temporary headquarters occupied a wealthy family's house with two successive courtyards.

时已入夜，
Night had fallen.

轩敞的大厅上，点起明晃晃的火油灯，照出椿寿的满面愁容！
Bright oil lamps lit the spacious hall and illuminated the worry on Chunshou's face.

他居中坐在红木炕床上，
He sat in the centre on a mahogany couch.

两旁梨花木的“太师椅”上，坐的是候补州县身分的押运官，千总和尖丁便只有站的份儿了。
The escort officials awaiting local appointments occupied the pearwood armchairs on either side; the military captains and headmen had to stand.


在鸦雀无声的沉重的气氛中，椿寿扯开嘶哑的嗓子说道：“今年的漕粮，到底还运得出去，运不出去？”
In the heavy silence, Chunshou raised his hoarse voice. “Are we going to get this year's grain shipped or not?”


这一同大家面面相觑，都要看一看对方的脸色。
They looked at one another, each trying to read the others' expressions.

最有资格答话的是尖丁，但以身分关系，还轮不到他们开口。
The headmen were best qualified to answer, but their low status gave them no right to speak first.


“我在抚台面前，拍了胸脯的，一个月当中，一定全数开船。
“I gave the governor my word that every boat would leave within a month.

现在看了实在情形，我觉得我的话说得过分了。
Now that I've seen the actual conditions, I fear I promised too much.

今天一定先要定个宗旨出来，
Today we must decide on a course of action.

船能动是动的办法，不能动是不能动的办法。
If the boats can move, we need one plan; if they cannot, we need another.

这样子一天一天等下去，非把脑袋等掉了不可。”
If we go on waiting day after day, we'll wait until we lose our heads.”


这是提出了要砍脑袋的警告，在座的人，无不悚然！
The warning that heads might fall sent a shiver through everyone present.

坐在左首太师椅上的一名候补州县，便欠身说道：“总得仰仗大人主持全局，
One of the expectant magistrates in the armchairs on the left leaned forward. “We must rely on Your Excellency to direct the whole affair.

属下便赔上性命，也得把漕船开出去。
Even at the cost of our lives, we must get the grain boats moving.

漕粮关乎国家正用，
This grain is essential to the state's regular needs.

今年天旱水浅，纵然耽迟，还有可说，
With this year's drought and shallow water, delay may still be excused.

倘或不走，那就是耽错了。”
But if the boats do not leave at all, that will be failure.”


“耽迟不耽错”这一说，凡是坐在大师椅上的，无不齐声附和。
Everyone seated in an armchair promptly endorsed this distinction between delay and failure.

这些候补州县，没有一个不闹穷，
These expectant magistrates were all short of money.

有些在省城住了十几年，始终没有补上一个缺，穷得只剩下一叠当票，
Some had lived in the provincial capital for more than ten years without obtaining a substantive appointment, until their possessions had dwindled to a stack of pawn tickets.

好不容易才派上这一个押运的差使，
At last they had secured escort duties.

指望着漕船一动，便好先支一笔公费安家。
Once the boats moved, they hoped to draw an advance on official expenses to provide for their families.

至于这一去什么时候才能到达通州，他们不必担心，迟延的处分，落不到他们头上。
They had no need to worry about when the fleet would reach Tongzhou: any punishment for delay would fall on someone else.


倘说漕船不走，他们便回不得省城，
If the boats did not sail, however, they could hardly return to the provincial capital.

因为船不走，便无所谓押运，
No sailing meant no escort duty.

不仅万事全休，而且比不得这个差使还要坏——
They would not merely lose everything they had hoped for; they would be worse off than if they had never received the assignment.

不得这个差使，不必借了盘缠来到差。
Without it, they would not have borrowed money to travel to their posts.

现在两手空空回杭州，债主那里如何交代？
How could they return to Hangzhou empty-handed and face their creditors?


椿寿当然明白他们的用心，
Chunshou understood their motives.

而且也知道这些人无足轻重，既出不了什么力，也担不了什么责任，
He also knew that they carried little weight: they could neither contribute much nor assume any real responsibility.

所以不理他们的话，望着站在他们身后的“领运千总”说：“他们有什么主意，说出来商量。”
Ignoring their remarks, he looked towards the convoy captains standing behind them. “Tell us what ideas you have, so that we can discuss them.”


“领运千总”的想法，与那些候补州县差不多，只是他们不能胡乱作主，
The captains thought much as the expectant magistrates did, but they could not make decisions on their own.

凡事要听尖丁的招呼，
In everything they had to follow the headmen's lead.

因而有个年纪大些的便这样回答：“请大人作主！”
One of the older captains therefore answered, “We ask Your Excellency to decide.”


“如果我说不走呢？”
“And if I say the boats are not to go?”


大家都不响，
No one spoke.

没有一个人赞成他的主意，只是不敢驳回。
None agreed with the suggestion, but none dared oppose it.

但这样不作声，也就很明显地表示出反对的意思了。
Their silence, however, made their opposition plain enough.


在座的一个实缺同知，此时忍不住开口：
A substantive deputy prefect present could no longer hold back.

“跟大人回话，还是让他们推出一两个人来，看看有何话说？”
“Your Excellency, perhaps they should choose one or two representatives and let us hear what they have to say.”


“他们”是指尖丁，
By “they,” he meant the headmen.

椿寿点点头，对那些尖丁说：“我看也非你们有句话不可。”
Chunshou nodded and turned to them. “I think we must hear from you after all.”


“是！”有个“有头有脸”的尖丁答应一声，请个安说：
“Yes, Your Excellency!” replied one of the more prominent headmen, making a respectful salute.

“请大人先休息。我们商量出一个宗旨，再跟大人回禀。”
“Please rest a little while we confer. Once we have agreed on a proposal, we will report to you.”


“好，好，
“Very well.

你们商量。”
Go and discuss it.”


椿寿坐在炕床上咕噜噜吸水烟，八帮的尖丁便退到廊下去悄悄商议，
Chunshou sat drawing on his water pipe, which gurgled softly, while the headmen of the eight associations withdrew to the corridor to confer in low voices.

好久尚无结论，
A long time passed without agreement.

因为各帮的情况不同，看法各异，牵涉的因素很多。
Conditions differed from one association to another, opinions varied, and many interests were involved.

今年的漕运，吃力不讨好是公认的看法，但走与不走，却有相反的主张，
Everyone agreed that this year's transport would mean hard work with little reward, but they disagreed over whether to sail.

一派认为赔累已不可免，不加不走，还省些事，
One faction argued that losses were inevitable, so they might as well stay and spare themselves the trouble.

一派则以在漕船上带着许多私货，
The other pointed out that the boats carried substantial private cargoes.

不走则还要赔一笔，
Failing to sail would bring losses on those goods too.

“公私交困”，简直要倾家荡产了。
Caught between public obligations and private business, they could be ruined.


谈来谈会，莫衷一是，
They argued back and forth without reaching a common view.

椿寿已经派人来催了，只好听凭上面云决定走与不走。
When Chunshou sent someone to hurry them, they could only leave the decision to their superiors.

不过总算也有了一点协议“那就是走也好，不走也好，各帮的赔累，只能一次，不能两次。
They had, however, agreed on one point: whether the boats sailed or not, the associations could bear one round of losses, but not two.


“如果不走，本年的漕粮便要变价缴纳，户部定章是每石二两银子，现在市价多少？”椿寿问。
“If the boats do not sail, this year's grain must be sold and the proceeds remitted,” Chunshou said. “The Board of Revenue fixes the rate at two taels per shi. What is the current market price?”


“这要看米的成色。”被推定去回话的那个尖丁答道：
“That depends on the quality,” answered the headman chosen to speak for them.

“总在七钱到八钱这个数目之间。”
“Somewhere between seven and eight tenths of a tael.”


“船上的漕粮有多少？”
“How much grain is on the boats?”


“一共二十七万六千石。”
“A total of 276,000 shi.”


“那么，”椿寿问道，“就算每石赔一两二钱银子，共该多少？”
“Then,” Chunshou asked, “if we lose one tael and two tenths on every shi, what does that come to?”


那尖丁的心算极快，
The headman was quick at mental arithmetic.

略略迟疑了一下，便报出确数：
After scarcely a pause, he supplied the exact figure.

“共该三十三万一千二百两银子。”
“331,200 taels.”


“如果漕船不走，奏请变价缴银，上头一定会准的。
“If the boats cannot sail, the throne will certainly approve a request to sell the grain and remit silver instead.

不过，”椿寿面色凝重地问，“这三十三万两银子，该谁来赔？”
But”—Chunshou's face grew grave—“who is to make up those three hundred and thirty-odd thousand taels?”


“大人晓得的，湖属八帮是‘疲帮’，
“Your Excellency knows that the eight Huzhou associations are financially exhausted.

力量实在够不上。
We simply haven't the means.

总要请大人格外体恤，留漕丁一条命。”
We must beg you to show special consideration and leave the boatmen a chance to survive.”


“哼！”椿寿冷笑，
“Hmph!” Chunshou gave a cold laugh.

“你们要命，
“You want to save your lives.

难道我的命就可以不要？”
Is mine the only one that does not matter?”


这是双方讨价还价，有意做作。
Both sides were bargaining, putting on a show.

漕帮有“屯田”，有“公费”，
The transport associations possessed agricultural estates and collective funds.

遇到这种情形，便得从公众的产业和收入中，提出款子来赔，
In circumstances like these, they had to draw on their common property and income to cover losses.

赔累的成数，并无定章，
There was no fixed rule governing their share.

但以上压下，首先要看帮的好坏，
Pressure came from above, and the first consideration was an association's financial condition.

公产多的“旺帮”便赔得多，负债累累的“疲帮”便赔得少。
Prosperous associations with substantial communal assets paid more; exhausted associations burdened with debt paid less.

说也奇怪，越是富庶的地区，漕帮越疲，
Curiously enough, the richer the district, the poorer its transport associations tended to be.

第一疲帮是江苏松江府属各帮，溯州府属八帮的境况也不见得好，
Those under Songjiang in Jiangsu were the most impoverished, and the eight Huzhou associations were not much better off.

这因为是越富庶的地区，剥削越多的缘故。
The explanation was that richer districts suffered heavier exactions.


这赔累的差额，除了漕帮以外，主要的使得由藩司从征收漕粮的各种陋规和“浮收”中，提成分赔。
Beyond the associations' contribution, most of the shortfall had to be met by the provincial treasurer out of a share of the unofficial fees and excess collections associated with the grain tax.

所以处理这件棘手的案子，实际上只是藩台衙门和湖属八帮间的事。
In practice, therefore, this difficult affair concerned only the provincial treasury and Huzhou's eight associations.

椿寿软哄硬逼，总算把分赔的成数谈好了。
By alternately coaxing and pressuring them, Chunshou finally secured agreement on how the loss would be divided.


然而这也不过是万不得已的退路。
That, however, was merely a last resort.

眼光总是朝前看的，
It was still better to look for a way forward.

能够把漕船开出去，交了差，也免了赔累，
If the boats could sail, the obligation would be fulfilled and the loss avoided.

何乐不为？
Why not try?

所以椿寿又回过头来问：“照你们看，漕船到底能不能动呢？
Chunshou therefore returned to the original question. “In your judgement, can the boats move at all?

能动还是照开的好。”
If they can, it would still be better to send them.”


这一句话自然大受欢迎，
The suggestion was warmly welcomed.

在座的候补州县，一看事有转机，无不精神夏振，纷纷颂赞椿寿的明智。
Seeing a turn for the better, the expectant magistrates brightened at once and competed in praising Chunshou's wisdom.


惟有那名代表漕帮说话的尖丁，大摇其头。
Only the headman who had spoken for the associations shook his head firmly.

不过他首先声明，他自己有点意见，并有代表漕帮，
Before explaining, however, he made it clear that he had a personal opinion and was not speaking on behalf of the associations.

不知该说不该说？
He was unsure whether he ought to express it.


“说，说！
“Go on!

集思广益，
We need everyone's ideas.

说出来商量。”
Tell us, and we can discuss it.”


照那尖丁个人的看法，漕船要能开行，首先得要疏浚河床，同时在各支流加闸，提高运河中的水位，
In the headman's personal view, getting the boats moving would first require dredging the channel and installing gates on the tributaries to raise the water level in the canal.

然后另雇民船分载漕米，减轻漕船的载重，
Civilian boats would then have to be hired to take part of the grain and lighten the official vessels.

这样双管齐下，才有“动”的可能。
Only by combining these two measures would movement be possible.


“那就这样办啊！
“Then let us do precisely that!

有何不可呢？”有个押运官兴奋地说。
What is there to prevent us?” one escort official exclaimed excitedly.


那尖丁苦笑了一下，没有作声。
The headman gave a bitter smile and said nothing.

椿寿却明白他的意思，
Chunshou, however, understood.

以讥嘲的口吻答道：“老兄说得容易！
“You make it sound easy,” he replied sarcastically.

可知道这一来要多少钱？”
“Have you any idea what it would cost?”


“于其赔累，何不把赔累的钱，花在疏浚河床和雇用民船上？
“Rather than pay compensation, why not spend the money on dredging and hiring boats?

不但交的差，而且治理了运河，
That would not only fulfil the transport obligation but improve the canal as well.

也是大人的劳绩。”
It would be an achievement to Your Excellency's credit.”


这两句话说动了椿寿的心，
The argument appealed to Chunshou.

点着头沉吟，
He nodded thoughtfully.

“这倒也是一说。”他自语似的问：“就不知道要多少日子？”
“There is something in that,” he murmured. “The question is how long it would take.”


疏浚的计划，施工的日程，要多少工、多少料，都要仔细计算，才能知道确数，
The dredging plan, the construction schedule, and the labour and materials required all had to be carefully calculated before a definite answer could be given.

在这样人多口杂的场台中，是不可能得到结果的，
Nothing of the sort could be settled in a crowded meeting where everyone was talking.

所以椿寿叫大家散一散，别外找了些实际能负责，能办事的人来重作商量。
Chunshou therefore dismissed the gathering and summoned a smaller group of people who could actually take responsibility and get things done.


这个少数人的集议，首先要谈的就是工料的来源。
Their first concern was where the labour and materials would come from.

这实在也只有一个字：钱。
In the end, that came down to a single word: money.

漕帮中被推派出来说话的那名尖丁，以久历江湖的经验，预感到此举不妥，
Drawing on his long experience of the world, the headman who had represented the associations sensed that the undertaking would end badly.

但人微言轻，无法扭转椿寿的“如意算盘”，
But a man of his low standing had little influence and could not overturn Chunshou's hopeful calculations.

便很干脆地答应了所派的经费，而且保证漕帮一定全力支持这件事。
He therefore readily accepted the sum assigned to the associations and promised their full support.

不过他也很郑重地声明，漕帮出了这笔钱，漕船不管如何非走不可。
At the same time, he made one solemn condition: once they paid this money, the boats had to sail, come what might.

如果再出了什么花样。漕帮不能负责。
If anything else went wrong, the associations would accept no further liability.


于是疏浚河道的计划，很快地便见诸实际行动。
The dredging scheme was soon put into effect.

这件事地方官原来也有责任，只是湖州府和运河所经的乌程、归安、德清三县，要办这件事惟有派工派料。
The local authorities had responsibilities in the matter too, but Huzhou prefecture and the three counties through which the canal passed—Wucheng, Gui'an, and Deqing—could act only by requisitioning labour and materials.

公文往返，以及召集绅士磋商，需要好久才能动工，未免缓不济急。
The exchange of official documents and consultations with local gentry would take so long that work could not begin soon enough to meet the emergency.


为了与天争时，自己拿钱出来征雇民工是最切实的办法。
With time running out, the most practical solution was to supply the money themselves and hire local labourers.

等这一切安排好了，预计八月底以前，漕船一定可以开行。
Once these arrangements were made, it was estimated that the boats would certainly be able to sail before the end of the eighth month.

这样，椿寿才算松了一口气，动身回省。
Only then did Chunshou breathe more easily and set off for the provincial capital.


走的那天，秋风秋雨，一般行旅闷损不乐的天气，
On the day he left, autumn wind and rain made the weather miserable for most travellers.

在椿寿却大为高兴，
Chunshou, however, was delighted.

心里在想，这雨最好落大些，连下几天，
Let the rain grow heavier, he thought, and continue for several days.

前溪水涨，起漕的时间，还好提前。
If the streams ahead rose, the grain boats might depart even sooner.


* * *回到省城，他第一件事便是去见抚台黄宗汉。
On returning to the provincial capital, his first act was to call on Governor Huang Zonghan.


听完报告，黄宗汉还夸奖了一番，说他实心办事。
After hearing his report, Huang actually praised his conscientious work.

还告诉他一些京里来的消息，
He also passed on news from the capital.

说朝廷已有旨意，严饬直隶总督和驻北通州的仓场侍郎，自天津杨村地方，调派一千五百艘驳船到山东临清，准备驳运漕粮。
The court had ordered the governor-general of Zhili and the granary vice-minister stationed at North Tongzhou to send fifteen hundred barges from Yangcun near Tianjin to Linqing in Shandong, ready to take over the grain.

不过直隶总督已经复奏，怕杨村的驳船，到达临清，河水已经结冰，
The governor-general of Zhili had replied, however, that the river might be frozen by the time the Yangcun barges reached Linqing.

所以这样请求：江浙的漕粮在临清、德州一带卸下来，暂时存贮，到明年开春解冻，再转漕北上，
He proposed that the grain from Jiangsu and Zhejiang be unloaded around Linqing and Dezhou and stored there until the spring thaw, then sent north.

这个请求，能不能奉准，尚不可知。
Whether the request would be approved was not yet known.


椿寿认为这是个好消息，
Chunshou regarded this as good news.

他原有顾虑，怕北地天寒，到了十月以后，河里结冰，漕船依旧受阻。
He had been worried that the northern cold would freeze the rivers after the tenth month and leave the grain boats obstructed once again.

现在既有直隶总督据实奏陈，等于为他把心里想说的话说了出来，
Now the governor-general of Zhili had reported the facts, effectively saying what Chunshou himself had wanted to say.

格于事实，朝廷不能不准，
Faced with reality, the court could scarcely refuse.

这样就只要到了临清，便算达成任务。
Reaching Linqing would therefore count as completion of the task.

倘说迟延，则各地情形相同，
As for any delay, conditions were the same for everyone.

处分的案子混在一起，变成“通案”就不要紧了。
The disciplinary cases would be combined into a general case, and the matter would lose its danger.


椿寿吃了这颗定心丸，对于疏浚河道的工程，进度不甚理想，就不太着急。
With this reassurance, Chunshou became less anxious about the unsatisfactory progress of the dredging.

他最关心的是直隶总督那个复奏的下文，
What interested him most was the response to the governor-general's memorial.

等漕船开出，才看到明发上谕：“浙江嘉杭等帮米石，如能拨船赶运，当仍遵前旨，
Only after the boats had sailed did he see the publicly circulated imperial edict: “If the rice carried by the Jiaxing, Hangzhou, and other Zhejiang fleets can be expedited by allocating additional vessels, the previous order shall remain in force.

酌拨杨村船只，趁此天气晴和，迅往拨运。
A suitable number of boats from Yangcun shall be sent promptly to transfer it while the weather remains fine and mild.

设或沿途必须截卸，临情、德州等仓，是否足资容纳？
Should unloading en route prove unavoidable, do the granaries at Linqing, Dezhou, and elsewhere have sufficient capacity?

着仓场侍郎、直隶总督、漕运总督、山东巡抚各将现在应办急务，迅速妥为办理，
The granary vice-minister, the governor-general of Zhili, the director-general of grain transport, and the governor of Shandong shall each promptly and properly attend to the urgent duties before them.

毋得听任属员推诿恶习，各分畛域，再赴贻误。
They must not permit their subordinates to persist in shirking responsibility, confining themselves to their own jurisdictions, and causing further delay.

懔之！“
Let them take heed!”


“亏得赶运出去。”椿寿心里在想，
“Thank goodness we got the boats away,” Chunshou thought.

“照上谕来看，在临清、德州截卸，暂时存贮，已经准了。
“The edict appears to allow them to unload at Linqing and Dezhou for temporary storage.

不过粮仓恐怕不够，
But there may not be enough granary space.

湖帮的漕米到了那里，倘或无仓可储，倒是棘手之事。”
If the Huzhou grain arrives and there is nowhere to put it, we shall have a difficult problem.”


于是，他“上院”去见抚台。
He therefore went to see the governor.

黄宗汉一见他就说：“啊，来得正好。
As soon as Huang saw him, he said, “Ah, just in time!

我正要叫‘戈什哈’去请你，
I was about to send an attendant for you.

有件要紧事商量。”
There is an important matter we must discuss.”


“请大人吩咐。”
“Please give me your instructions, Your Excellency.”


“不，不！
“No, no.

你有事你先说。”
If you have business, speak first.”


椿寿便说明来意，
Chunshou explained why he had come.

意思是想请抚台出奏，浙江湖属八帮的漕米，已出省境北上。
He wished the governor to submit a memorial stating that the grain boats of Huzhou's eight associations had left Zhejiang and were heading north.

如果到了临清，无法驳运，需要截卸时，请饬下漕运总督及山东巡抚，预留空仓。
If they reached Linqing but could not transfer their cargo and had to unload it, the director-general of grain transport and the governor of Shandong should be instructed to reserve empty granaries.

他是怕湖属八帮的漕船最后到达，仓位为他帮捷足先登，所以有此要求。
Chunshou feared that the Huzhou boats would arrive last, after other fleets had occupied all the available space.


黄宗汉一面听，一面不断摇头，
Huang shook his head repeatedly as he listened.

等他说完，俯身向前问道：“漕运一事，贵司内行，而且今年由贵司一手料理，
When Chunshou finished, he leaned forward. “You are the expert on grain transport, and this year you have managed it entirely yourself.

我要请问，可曾计算过‘回空’的日子？”
May I ask whether you have calculated when the empty boats will return?”


原来是这一层顾虑，
So that was his concern.

椿寿略略放了心，
Chunshou felt a little relieved.

“回大人的话，”他说，“回空自然要衍期……”
“Your Excellency, their return will naturally be delayed...”


“衍期多少时候？”黄宗汉不待辞毕，枪着问道，
“By how long?” Huang interrupted.

“请贵司算与我听一听。”
“Please work it out for me.”


“这要看临清的情形。
“That depends on conditions at Linqing.

如果在那里截卸，等明年开冻驳运，又要看前面漕船的多寡，
If the grain is unloaded there and transferred after the thaw next year, it will also depend on the number of boats ahead of ours.

多则慢，少则快。
The more there are, the longer it will take.”


“最快什么时候？”
“What is the earliest possible date?”


“总要到明年四月。”
“Not before the fourth month of next year.”


“回空呢？”
“And the return journey?”


“也要两个月。”
“That would take another two months.”


“这就是说，漕船明年夏天才能回家，
“So the boats will not return home until next summer.

还要经过一番修补，又得费个把月，
They will then need repairs, which will take another month or so.

最快也得在七月里才能到各县受兑漕米。
At the earliest, they will not be ready to collect grain from the counties until the seventh month.

请问贵司，明年新漕，不是又跟今年一样，迟到八九月才能启运吗？”
Tell me, then: will next year's new grain not be delayed until the eighth or ninth month, just like this year's?”


“是！”椿寿答道，
“Yes,” Chunshou answered.

“不过明年改用海运，亦无关系。”
“But next year we shall use sea transport, so it will not matter.”


“什么叫没有关系？”
“What do you mean, it will not matter?”

黄宗汉勃然变色，
Huang's expression changed sharply.

“你说得好轻巧。
“You speak very lightly of it.

年年把漕期延后，何时始得恢复正常？
If the schedule slips later every year, when will it ever return to normal?

须知今年是贵司责无旁贷，明年就完全是我的责任。
This year's responsibility rests squarely on you; next year's will be entirely mine.

贵司这样子做法，简直是有意跟我过不去呀！”
The way you are handling this, one would think you were deliberately trying to make trouble for me!”


椿寿一看抚台变脸，大出意外，
The governor's sudden anger took Chunshou completely by surprise.

他亦是旗下公子哥儿出身，一个忍不住，当即顶撞了过去：“大人言重了！
He too had been raised as a privileged bannerman, and for a moment he lost his restraint. “Your Excellency goes too far!

既然我责无旁贷，该杀该剐，自然由我负责，
If the responsibility is mine, then I shall answer for it, even if I must be executed.

大人何必如此气急败坏？”
There is no need for Your Excellency to lose his temper in this fashion.”


“好，好！”
“Very well! Very well!”

黄宗汉一半真的生气，一半有意做作，
Huang was partly angry and partly acting.

脸上一阵青，一阵红地说：“你负责，你负责！
His face alternated between pallor and a flush. “You will answer for it, will you?

请教，这责任如何负法？”
May I ask how?”


“本年漕运虽由我主管，但自从大人到任，凡事亦曾禀命而行。
“Although I have supervised this year's grain transport, since Your Excellency arrived I have acted only after reporting to you.

今年江苏试办海运，成效甚佳，
Jiangsu's trial of sea transport has been highly successful this year.

请大人出奏，明年浙省仿照江苏成例，不就行了吗？“
If Your Excellency requests permission for Zhejiang to follow its example next year, would that not settle the matter?”


“哼，哼！”
“Hmph! Hmph!”

黄宗汉不断冷笑，
Huang kept giving cold little laughs.

“看贵司的话，好象军机大臣的口吻，
“You speak as though you were a grand councillor.

我倒再要请教，如果上头不准呢？”
Then let me ask another question: what if the throne refuses?”


“没有不准之理。”
“There is no reason it should refuse.”


“又是这样的口吻！”
“That tone again!”

黄宗汉一拍炕几，大声呵斥，“你到底是来议事，还是来抬杠？”
Huang struck the small table on the couch and shouted, “Have you come to discuss business or to argue with me?”


椿寿做了二十几年的官，从未见过这样的上司，
In more than twenty years of official service, Chunshou had never encountered such a superior.

心里在想：你是科甲出身，我亦不是捐班佐杂爬上来的，受惯了气的，
You entered through the examinations, he thought, but I am not some subordinate who bought his way in and has grown accustomed to being insulted.

论宦途经历，我放浙江藩司，你还不过是浙江臬司，
As for our careers, when I was appointed provincial treasurer of Zhejiang, you were still only its judicial commissioner.

只不过朝中有人，道光十五年乙未那一榜……
You merely have people at court—the men from that yiwei examination of the fifteenth year of Daoguang...


转念到此，椿寿打了个寒噤，暗叫一声：大事不好！
At that thought, Chunshou shuddered. This was serious!

黄宗汉的同年，已有当了军机大臣的，那是苏州的彭蕴章。
One of Huang's examination classmates, Peng Yunzhang of Suzhou, had already become a grand councillor.

还有户部两侍郎，
There were also the two vice-presidents of the Board of Revenue.

一个是福建的王庆云，最爱照应同乡，
One, Wang Qingyun of Fujian, was particularly eager to help men from his home province.

另一个又是他的同年，而且是好友的伺桂清。
The other was He Guiqing, another examination classmate of Huang's and a close friend.


俗语说得好，“朝里无人莫做官。”
The saying was true: “Without someone at court, do not seek office.”

黄宗汉敢于如此目中无人，无非仗着内有奥援，
Huang's arrogance rested on powerful backing in the capital.

而且听说他今年进京，皇上召见六次之多，
Moreover, it was said that during his visit there that year the emperor had received him no fewer than six times.

圣眷正隆，
He was enjoying exceptional imperial favour.

自己无论如何碰不过他。
Chunshou could not possibly prevail against him.

这些念头雷轰电掣般闪过心头，顿感气馁，
These thoughts flashed through his mind like thunder and lightning, draining his courage.

只得忍气吞声地陪个罪。
Swallowing his anger, he could only apologise.


“大人息怒。
“Please do not be angry, Your Excellency.

我岂敢跟大人抬杠？
How could I dare argue with you?

一切还求大人维持。”
I can only ask for your protection in this matter.”


这一说，黄宗汉的脸色才和缓了一些，
Only then did Huang's expression soften a little.

“既为同僚，
“We are colleagues.

能维持总要维持。
Naturally, I shall support you wherever I can.

不过，“他使劲摇着头，一字一句地说：”难，难！“
But...” He shook his head emphatically and pronounced each word with care. “Difficult. Very difficult.”


椿寿的心越发往下沉，
Chunshou's heart sank further.

强自镇静着问道：“大人有何高见？
Forcing himself to remain calm, he asked, “What course would Your Excellency advise?

要请教诲。”
I beg you to instruct me.”


“岂敢，岂敢。
“Oh, I could hardly presume.

等我想一想再说吧！”
Let me think it over first.”


说完，端一端茶碗，
With that, Huang lifted his teacup.

堂下侍候的戈什哈便拉开嗓子：“送客！”
An attendant waiting below called out loudly, “Show the guest out!”


这送客等于逐客。
This dismissal amounted to an expulsion.

椿寿出了抚台衙门，坐在轿子里，只催轿扶加快，
Chunshou left the governor's office and, once in his sedan chair, kept urging the bearers to go faster.

急急赶回本衙门，让听差把文案请到“签押房”，关上房门，
Back at his own headquarters, he summoned his secretaries to his private office and shut the door.

细说了上院的经过，惊疑不定地问道：“各位看看，黄抚台这是什么意思？”
After describing the interview in detail, he asked in alarm and uncertainty, “What do you think Governor Huang means by all this?”


“黄抚台外号‘黄阎罗’，
“His nickname is Huang the King of Hell.

翻脸不认人是出名的，
He is notorious for turning on people without warning.

这件事要好好铺排一下。”
This business will need careful handling.”


“唉！”
“Ah!”

椿寿摇摇头，欲言又止，
Chunshou shook his head, beginning to speak and then stopping.

失悔在黄抚台刚到任，不理他索贿的暗示。
He regretted having ignored Huang's hint about a bribe when the new governor first arrived.


“ ‘天大的公事，地大的银子’，”有个文案说很很率直，
“‘A mountain of official business can be moved by a mountain of silver,’” one secretary said bluntly.

“先去探探口气看，院上到底打的什么主意？”
“We should first sound him out and learn what he really has in mind.”


于是连夜走路子去打听，总算有了确实的消息，
They used their connections that very night to make inquiries and eventually obtained definite news.

据说黄宗汉为了明年的新漕得以早日受兑装载，照限期抵达通州，决定上奏，把湖属八帮的瘤船追了回来，
It was said that, to ensure next year's new grain could be accepted and loaded early and reach Tongzhou on time, Huang had decided to petition the throne to recall the boats of Huzhou's eight associations.

漕米卸岸入仓，连同明年的新漕，一起装运。
Their grain would be unloaded and stored, then shipped together with next year's collection.


这样做法，只苦了漕帮，白白赔上一笔疏浚河道的费用。
Such a measure would chiefly hurt the associations, which would have paid for the dredging to no purpose.

其次，那些奉委押运的候补州县，没有“公费”可派，一笔过年的盘缠便落空了。
The expectant magistrates appointed as escorts would suffer too: without official expense allowances to draw upon, the money they had hoped to take home for the New Year would vanish.

椿寿心中虽有不忍，但到底是别人的事，
Chunshou felt sorry for them, but their troubles were, after all, their own.

藩司能够不赔，已是上上大吉，
If the provincial treasury could escape a loss, that was already the best he could hope for.

只好狠一狠心不理他们了。
He would have to harden his heart and leave them to it.


果然，第二天抚台衙门来了正式公事，
Sure enough, an official order arrived from the governor's office the next day.

惟恐影响来年新漕的期限，“所有本年湖属八帮漕船，仰该司即便遵照，全数追回，候命办理。”
To avoid disrupting the schedule for the following year's grain, “all boats belonging to this year's eight Huzhou associations are to be recalled without delay, in full compliance with this order, and held pending further instructions.”

椿寿不敢怠慢，
Chunshou dared not hesitate.

立即派出人去，把湖属八帮的漕船截了回来，同时上院去见抚台，请示所谓“候命办理”是如何办法？
He immediately sent men to intercept the boats and bring them back, then went to the governor's office to ask what the “further instructions” would be.


黄宗汉一直托病不见。
Huang repeatedly declined to see him, pleading illness.

过了有五、六天，一角公文送到，
Five or six days later, another official dispatch arrived.

拆开一看，椿寿几乎昏厥，
Chunshou opened it and almost fainted.

顿足骂道，“黄寿臣，黄寿臣，你好狠的心！
Stamping his foot, he cried, “Huang Shouchen! Huang Shouchen! How can you be so cruel?

我与你问冤何仇，你要置我于死地！“
What wrong have I done you that you must drive me to my death?”


黄宗汉的手段，的确太毒辣了，
Huang's methods were indeed ruthless.

他以一省最高行政长官的地位，统筹漕运全局的理由，为了使来年新漕的输运，如期完成，以期此后各年均得恢复正常，作了一个决定，本年湖属八帮的漕米，留浙变价，
Invoking his authority as the province's highest administrative official and his responsibility for the entire grain-transport system, he had issued a decision. To ensure that the following year's grain arrived on schedule and that regular arrangements could thereafter be restored, this year's grain from Huzhou's eight associations would remain in Zhejiang and be sold.

全部漕米二十七万六千石，
The total quantity was 276,000 shi.

照户部所定价格，每石二两银子，共该五十五万二千两，限期一个月报缴。
At the Board of Revenue's prescribed rate of two taels per shi, the proceeds due were 552,000 taels, to be remitted within one month.


这是椿寿与尖丁早已算过了的，市价与部价的差额，一共要三十三万两银子。
Chunshou and the headman had already worked out what this meant: the gap between the market price and the official rate was some 330,000 taels.

如果在他第一次到湖州开会之前，抚台就作了这个决定，那么漕帮赔大部分，藩司赔小部分，
Had the governor made this decision before Chunshou's first meeting in Huzhou, the associations would have borne the larger share and the provincial treasury the smaller.

这笔小部分的赔款，也还可以在浮收的款项中拨付，
The treasury's portion could still have been drawn from the excess collections.

说起来只是今年白吃一场辛苦，没有“好处”而已。
In effect, everyone would merely have worked for nothing that year and forfeited their customary gains.

但现在的情况完全不同了，
Now the situation was entirely different.

漕帮负担了疏浚河道的全部经费，事先已经声明，出了这笔钱，漕船非走不可，
The associations had paid the full cost of dredging and had explicitly stipulated that, once the money was spent, the boats must sail.

于今截回不定，已觉愧对漕帮，
Recalling them already left Chunshou ashamed.

再要他们分赔差额，就是漕帮肯赔，自己也难启齿，何况看情形是决无此可能的。
He could hardly ask them to contribute towards the price shortfall as well, even if they were willing—which, from all appearances, they certainly were not.


至于浮收的“好处”，早已按股照派，“分润”有夫人员，
As for the profits from excess collection, those had already been divided into shares and distributed among the people concerned.

哪里再去追索？
How could they now be recovered?

即使追索得到，也不过五、六万银子，还差着一大截呢！
Even if recovery were possible, they amounted to only fifty or sixty thousand taels, far short of what was needed.


事情的演变，竟会弄得全部责任，落在自己一个人头上。
Events had somehow left the entire burden resting on Chunshou alone.

椿寿悔恨交并，而仍不能不拼命作最后的挣扎，
Tormented by regret and anger, he nevertheless had to make one last desperate effort.

愁眉苦脸地召集了亲信来商议，
With a miserable expression, he called his trusted advisers together.

大家一致的看法是：“解铃还须系铃人”，
They all reached the same conclusion: only the man who had tied the knot could untie it.

惟有去求抚台，收回“变价”的成命，应解的二十多万石漕米，随明年新漕一起启运。
He must beg the governor to withdraw the order to sell the grain and allow the more than two hundred thousand shi to be shipped with the next year's collection.

就这样起卸入仓，从船上搬到岸上，明年再从岸上搬到船上，来回周折的运费、仓费，以及两次搬动的损耗，算起来也要赔好几万两银子，
Unloading it now, storing it on shore, and loading it again the following year would still cost tens of thousands of taels in handling charges, storage fees, and losses from repeated movement.

而且一定还会受到处分，
A disciplinary penalty would also be unavoidable.

但无论如何总比赔三十三万两银子来得好。
Even so, it would be better than finding 330,000 taels.


两害相权取其轻，椿寿只得硬着头皮上院，
Choosing the lesser of two evils, Chunshou steeled himself and went to the governor's office.

把“手本”送了进去，门上出来答道：
He sent in his visiting card, but the gatekeeper returned with a message.

“上头人不舒服，请大人回去吧！
“His Excellency is unwell and asks you to go home.

上头交代，等病好了，再请大人过来相叙。”
He says he will invite you to call when he has recovered.”


棒寿愤不可遏，
Chunshou's anger became uncontrollable.

吩咐跟班说：“回去取铺盖！
“Go back and fetch my bedding,” he told his attendant.

抚台不见我不走，
“If the governor will not see me, I shall not leave.

就借官厅的炕床睡。”
I can sleep on the couch in the reception hall.”


门上一看，这不象话，赶紧陪笑道：
Seeing how unseemly the situation was becoming, the gatekeeper hastened to placate him.

“大人不必，不必！
“There is no need, Your Excellency, no need at all!

想来是有急要公事要回，
You must have some urgent official matter to report.

我再到上房去跑一趟。”
I shall go to the inner quarters once more.”


于是椿寿就在官厅中坐等，
Chunshou waited in the reception hall.

等了半个时辰，黄宗汉出来了，仰着头，板着脸，
After about an hour, Huang appeared, head raised and face stern.

一见面不等椿寿开口，就先大声问道：“你非见我不可？”
Before Chunshou could speak, he demanded loudly, “You insist on seeing me?”


“是！”椿寿低声下气地回答：
“Yes,” Chunshou replied humbly.

“大人贵恙在身，本不该打搅，只是实在有万分困难的下情上禀。”
“I ought not to disturb Your Excellency during your illness, but I am in the greatest difficulty and must explain my circumstances.”


“如果是湖属漕米的事，你不必谈。
“If it concerns the Huzhou grain, there is no need to say anything.

已经出奏了。”
I have already reported the decision to the throne.”


这句话就如焦雷轰顶，
The words struck him like a thunderbolt.

一时天旋地转，不得不颓然坐倒，
The room seemed to spin, and he collapsed into a seat.

等定定神看时，黄宗汉已无踪影，
When he recovered enough to look around, Huang had vanished.

抚院的戈什哈低声向他说道：“大人请回吧！
An attendant said softly, “Please go home, Your Excellency.

轿子已经伺侯半天了。”
Your chair has been waiting for some time.”


椿寿闭上眼，
Chunshou closed his eyes.

眼角流出两滴眼泪，
Two tears escaped from their corners.

拿马蹄袖拭一拭干净，由听差扶掖着，一步懒似一步地走官厅。
He wiped them away with the curved cuffs of his robe and, supported by an attendant, left the hall, each step more reluctant than the last.


就在这天晚上，椿寿在藩司衙门后院的签押房里，上吊自杀。
That night, Chunshou hanged himself in the private office behind the provincial treasury.

第二天一早为家人发觉，哭声震动内外，
His household discovered him early the next morning, and their cries of grief filled the compound.

少不得有人献殷勤，把这个不幸的消息，飞报抚台。
Someone inevitably seized the opportunity to curry favour by rushing the unhappy news to the governor.


黄宗汉一听，知道闯了祸，
Huang knew at once that he was in trouble.

逼死二品大贝，罪名不轻。
Driving an official of the second rank to his death was no minor offence.

但转念想起一重公案，
Then he remembered an earlier case.

觉得可以如法炮制，
If he could follow the same procedure, perhaps he would be safe.

心便放了一半。
The thought eased half his fear.


他想起的是陕西蒲城王鼎尸谏的往事，这重公案发生在十年以前，
The case he recalled was that of Wang Ding of Pucheng, Shaanxi, who had taken his own life as a final remonstrance ten years earlier.

王鼎与奸臣穆彰阿，同为大学士值军机。
Wang and the corrupt minister Mujangga had both been grand secretaries serving on the Grand Council.

这位“蒲城相国”性情刚烈，嫉恶如仇，
Wang, known as the grand secretary from Pucheng, was fierce in temperament and hated wrongdoing.

而遇到穆彰阿是阴柔奸险的性格，
Mujangga was subtle, yielding in manner, and treacherous.

每在御前争执，一个声色俱厉，一个从容自如，
When they argued before the Daoguang emperor, one spoke with visible anger while the other remained composed.

宣宗偏听不明，总觉得王鼎不免过分。
The emperor, swayed by one side and failing to see clearly, invariably felt that Wang had gone too far.


道光二十二年，为了保荐林则徐夏用，王鼎不惜自杀尸谏，遗疏痛劾穆彰阿。
In the twenty-second year of Daoguang, Wang Ding pressed for Lin Zexu's restoration to office and went so far as to hang himself in remonstrance, leaving a memorial that bitterly denounced Mujangga.

那时有个军机章京叫陈孚恩，是穆彰阿的走狗，一看王鼎不曾入值，亦未请假，
A Grand Council secretary named Chen Fu'en, one of Mujangga's henchmen, noticed that Wang had neither attended duty nor asked for leave.

心里一支，借故出宫，赶到王鼎家一看，
Growing suspicious, he found an excuse to leave the palace and hurried to Wang's residence.

听得哭声震天，越发有数。
The sound of overwhelming grief confirmed his fears.

趁王鼎的儿子，翰林院编修王抗骤遭大故，五中昏瞀的当儿，劝他把王鼎的尸首解下来，同时把遗疏抓到手里，
Wang's son, Wang Kang, a compiler in the Hanlin Academy, was dazed by the sudden calamity. Taking advantage of his confusion, Chen urged him to have the body lowered and at the same time secured the final memorial.

一看内容，不出所料，
Its contents were exactly what he had expected.

便又劝王抗以个人前程为重，不必得罪穆彰阿，
He then advised Wang Kang to think of his own future and avoid offending Mujangga.

又说“上头”对王鼎印象不佳，而大臣自杀，有伤国体，
He added that the emperor already held a poor opinion of Wang Ding and that a senior minister's suicide damaged the dignity of the state.

说不定天颜震怒，不但王鼎身后的恤典落空，而且别有不测之祸。
If the emperor became angry, the family might lose not only the customary posthumous honours but also face unforeseen disaster.


这一番威胁利诱，教王抗上了当，
Threats and promises together deceived Wang Kang.

听从穆彰阿更改遗疏，并以暴疾身故奏报。
He accepted Mujangga's alteration of the final memorial, and his father's death was reported as the result of sudden illness.

宣宗也有些疑心，但穆彰阿布置周密，“上头”无法获知真相，
The Daoguang emperor had some suspicions, but Mujangga had arranged matters so thoroughly that the truth could not reach the throne.

也就算了。
The affair was allowed to rest.


陈孚恩帮了穆彰阿这个大忙，收获也下小，
Chen Fu'en profited handsomely from this service.

不久，穆彰阿就保他当山东巡抚。
Before long, Mujangga recommended him for the governorship of Shandong.

而王抗则以不能成父之志，为他父亲的门生，他自己的同年，以及陕甘同乡所不齿，
Wang Kang, on the other hand, was despised for failing to fulfil his father's purpose—by his father's protégés, by his own examination classmates, and by fellow natives of Shaanxi and Gansu.

辞官回里，郁郁以终。
He resigned, returned home, and died in sorrow.


穆彰阿是道光十五年乙未科会试的大主考，
Mujangga had been chief examiner at the metropolitan examination in the yiwei year, the fifteenth year of Daoguang.

黄宗汉是他的门生，颇为巴结这位老师。
Huang Zonghan was therefore one of his examination protégés and had diligently cultivated his patron.

秦桧门下有“十客”，穆彰阿门下有“十子”，
Just as Qin Hui had once had his “Ten Guests,” Mujangga had his “Ten Sons.”

黄宗汉与陈孚恩都在“穆门十子”之数，自然熟知其事。
Huang and Chen Fu'en both belonged to that circle, so Huang knew the Wang Ding affair very well.

所以，一遇椿寿的变故，他立即遣派亲信，以釜底抽薪的宗旨，先设法把椿寿的遗嘱弄到手，
As soon as Chunshou's death occurred, he sent trusted men to secure Chunshou's final statement, aiming to remove the danger at its source.

然后亲自拜访驻防的将军和浙江学政，
He then personally visited the commander of the local banner garrison and Zhejiang's education commissioner.

因为这两个人是可以专折奏事的，
Both were entitled to submit independent memorials to the throne.

先要把他们稳住，才可以不使真相上闻。
He first had to secure their cooperation if the truth was to be kept from the emperor.


当然，另一方面他还要间接拜托旗籍的官员，安抚椿寿的家属，然后奏报藩司出缺。
Meanwhile, he used intermediaries to ask banner officials to placate Chunshou's family, then reported that the provincial treasurer's post had fallen vacant.

上吊自杀是瞒不住的，
The suicide itself could not be concealed.

所以另外附了个“夹片”，说是“浙江钱漕诸务支出，
Huang therefore attached a supplementary note: “The administration of Zhejiang's revenues and grain entails many expenditures.

本年久旱岁歉，征解尤难，
This year, prolonged drought and a poor harvest have made collection and remittance especially difficult.

该司恐误公事，日夜焦急，以至迫切轻生。”
Fearing that official business would suffer, the treasurer grew anxious day and night until, overcome by desperation, he took his own life.”

把湖属八帮应运漕米，留浙变价的事，只字不提，
He made no mention of the order to retain and sell the Huzhou grain.

同时录呈了经过修改的椿寿的遗嘱。
He also submitted an altered copy of Chunshou's final statement.

咸丰帝此时初登大宝，相当精明，看遗嘱内有“因情节所逼，势不能生”两句话，大为疑惑，
The Xianfeng emperor, newly enthroned and still quite discerning, was struck by two phrases in it: “Driven by the circumstances, I can no longer live.”

认为即令公事难办，何至遽尔自尽？
Even if official business was difficult, why should it lead so abruptly to suicide?

是否另有别情，命令黄宗汉“再行详细访察，据实奏闻，毋稍隐饰。”
Suspecting another cause, he ordered Huang to “make further detailed inquiries and report the facts, concealing nothing.”


接着，浙江学政万青藜也有专折奏报，
Zhejiang's education commissioner, Wan Qingli, then submitted his own memorial.

说椿寿身后，留有遗嘱，“实因公事棘手，遽行自尽。”
He stated that Chunshou had left a final declaration and “had indeed taken his own life because official business had become intractable.”

与黄宗汉的奏折，桴鼓相应。
This neatly echoed Huang's account.

皇帝批示：“已有旨，令黄宗汉详查具报。
The emperor replied: “An order has already been issued directing Huang Zonghan to investigate thoroughly and report.

汝近在省垣，若有所闻，亦可据实具奏。”
Since you are in the provincial capital, you too may submit a factual report if anything comes to your attention.”


看来事情要闹得很大，但事态真正严重的关键所在，只有黄宗汉自己知道。
The affair seemed likely to grow serious, but only Huang knew where the real danger lay.

因为椿寿的自尽，如果真的是由于他的措施严峻、则虽良心有愧，亦不过课以道义上的责任，在公事上可以交代得过，
If Chunshou had truly killed himself merely because Huang's administrative measures were severe, Huang might feel guilty and bear moral responsibility, yet he could still defend his official conduct.

那就不必有所畏惧。
There would have been little to fear.

而事实上并非如此，
But that was not what had happened.

椿寿之死，是死在他虚言恫吓的一句话上。
Chunshou had died because of a false statement Huang had used to intimidate him.


所谓“留浙变价”，原是黄宗汉有意跟椿寿为难的一种说法，
The proposal to retain and sell the grain in Zhejiang had been devised to make Chunshou's life difficult.

暗地里他并不坚持这样做，
In private, Huang did not insist upon it.

不但不坚持，他还留着后手，以防椿寿无法做到时，自己有转圜的余地。
Indeed, he had kept another course in reserve so that, if Chunshou could not comply, he himself would still have room to manoeuvre.


由于在军机处和户部都有极好的关系，所以黄宗汉对来年新漕改用海运，以及本年湖属各帮漕米，不能如限北运的处置办法，都有十足的把握，
With excellent connections in both the Grand Council and the Board of Revenue, Huang was entirely confident about securing sea transport for the following year's grain and about the arrangements for this year's Huzhou grain, which could not be sent north within the prescribed time.

私底下书函往还，几乎已有成议。
Private correspondence had already brought these matters close to agreement.

但这些情形，椿寿无从知道，他亦瞒着不说。
Chunshou had no way of knowing this, and Huang deliberately kept him in the dark.

以改用海运并无把握，河运粮船难以依限回空的理由，下令截回漕船，留浙变价，
Claiming that sea transport remained uncertain and that the canal boats could not return on schedule, Huang ordered the fleet recalled and the grain sold in Zhejiang.

这一套措施与他所奏报的改革办法，完全不符。
These measures were wholly at odds with the reforms he had actually proposed to the throne.

他向椿寿所说的，留浙变价一事，“已经出奏”，事情到了推车撞壁的地步，再也无可挽回，
When he told Chunshou that the decision to sell the grain had “already been reported,” he made it appear that matters had reached an immovable barrier and that no remedy remained.

这才使椿寿感到已入绝路，不能不一死了之。
That was what persuaded Chunshou that he had reached a dead end and could only die.

其实，“已经出奏”这句话，根本是瞎说。
In fact, the claim that the decision had already been reported was a complete fabrication.


就凭这句谎言，黄宗汉便得对椿寿之死，负起全部责任。
That lie alone made Huang fully responsible for Chunshou's death.

因而他必须多方设法掩饰遮盖，不使真相上闻，
He therefore had to do everything possible to conceal the truth from the throne.

一面活动万青藜等人，帮着他瞒谎，一面遣派亲信，携带巨贤，到京师活动。
While persuading Wan Qingli and others to help sustain the deception, he sent trusted men to the capital with large sums of money to work on his behalf.

当然，象军机大臣彭蕴章那里，是不必也不能行贿的，
With someone like Grand Councillor Peng Yunzhang, of course, bribery was neither necessary nor possible.

只有以同年的身份，拜托关顾照应。
Huang could only appeal to their bond as examination classmates and ask for his protection.


不过这样一件案子，也不是轻易压得下去的。
Even so, a case like this could not easily be suppressed.

椿寿是“上三旗”的旗人，亲戚之中，颇有贵官，
Chunshou belonged to the Upper Three Banners and had highly placed relatives.

认为他的死因可疑，自然要出头为他讲话，
They suspected that his death had a hidden cause and naturally spoke up for him.

这样军机处要帮黄宗汉的忙，就不能不费一番手脚，来遮人耳目。
If the Grand Council was to help Huang, it would have to take some pains to conceal what it was doing.


照一向的惯例，类似这种情况，一定简派大员密查。
In such circumstances, established practice called for a senior official to conduct a secret investigation.

既称密查，自然不能让被查的人知道，
Naturally, the person under investigation was not supposed to know.

可是一二品的大员出京，无论如何是件瞒不住的事，
Yet the departure from the capital of a first- or second-rank official could hardly be hidden.

于是便有许多掩护其行踪及任务的方法，
Various methods were therefore used to disguise his route and assignment.

一种是声东击西，
One was to announce one destination while aiming at another.

譬如明发上谕：“着派某某人驰往江苏查案”，
A public edict might, for instance, order a named official to proceed urgently to Jiangsu to investigate a case.

这人便是“钦差”的身分，所经之处，接待的礼节极其隆重。
Travelling as an imperial commissioner, he would receive elaborate honours along the way.

这样一路南下，到了济南，忽然不定了，
On reaching Jinan, however, he would suddenly stop.

用钦差大臣的关防，咨会山东巡抚，开出一张名单，请即传提到案，迅雷不及掩耳地展开了查案的工作。
Using his commissioner's seal, he would send the governor of Shandong a list of people to be summoned immediately, launching the investigation before anyone had time to prepare.


再有一种是暗渡陈仓，乘某某大员外放到任的机会，密谕赴某处查案。
Another method was to use an outwardly ordinary appointment as cover: a senior official leaving the capital to take up a provincial post would receive secret instructions to investigate a case elsewhere on the way.


这道密谕照例不发“邸抄”，被查的省分，毫无所知，
Such instructions were not published in the official court gazette, so the province concerned knew nothing of them.

行到目的地，拜访总督或巡抚，出示密谕，
On reaching his destination, the investigator called on the governor-general or governor and produced the secret edict.

于是一夕之间，可以掀起大狱。
A major judicial inquiry could thus erupt overnight.

查黄宗汉逼死椿寿一案，就是用的这一种办法，
This was the method chosen to investigate Huang's role in driving Chunshou to his death.

所以在表面上看不出黄宗汉出了毛病的痕迹。这当然又是军机处帮他的忙。
Outwardly, there was therefore no sign that Huang was in trouble—another favour done him by the Grand Council.


这位钦差名叫何桂清，是黄宗汉的同年。
The commissioner chosen was He Guiqing, Huang's examination classmate.

在他们乙未一榜中，何桂清的年纪比较轻，
Among the successful candidates of their yiwei year, He was relatively young.

仪表清俊，吐嘱渊雅，人缘极好。
Handsome, refined in speech, and graceful in manner, he was exceptionally well liked.

这年秋天，由户部侍郎外放江苏学政，
That autumn he had been transferred from a vice-presidency at the Board of Revenue to the post of education commissioner in Jiangsu.

在京里饯行送别的应酬甚多，所以一直迟迟不能启程。
So many farewell engagements awaited him in the capital that his departure was repeatedly delayed.

就在这殷摒挡行囊，准备到任的期间内，出了椿寿这件案子，
Chunshou's case arose while he was making ready to leave.

彭蕴章和他一些在京同年商量的结果，奏请密派问桂清于赴江苏学政途中，顺道查办。
After consulting other examination classmates in the capital, Peng Yunzhang proposed that He be secretly instructed to investigate it on his way to Jiangsu.

“上头”只对椿寿的死因怀疑，不曾想到是他所信任的黄宗汉干的好事，
The emperor suspected something about Chunshou's death, but had not imagined that Huang, whom he trusted, was responsible.

自然不会以何桂清与黄是同年为嫌，便准了军机处的建议。
He therefore saw no reason to object to He's relationship with Huang and approved the Grand Council's recommendation.


这个消息，很快、很秘密地传到了杭州，
The news reached Hangzhou swiftly and in strict secrecy.

黄宗汉等于服下一位定心丸。
To Huang, it was profoundly reassuring.


何桂清以钦命在身，不敢耽搁，
With an imperial commission now entrusted to him, He Guiqing dared delay no longer.

也就在岁暮之际，出京南下。
As the year drew to its close, he left the capital and headed south.
