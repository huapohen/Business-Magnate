CHAPTER TWO｜第二章


On that very day, Wang Youling arrived at North Tongzhou.
就在同一天，王有龄到了北通州。

Leaving Hangzhou, he had taken a black-canopied boat to Suzhou and then boarded a grain boat heading north.
他从杭州动身，坐乌篷船到苏州，然后换搭漕船北上，

Unfortunately, his journey coincided with the breach north of Fengxian.
偏偏又逢丰北决口，

He had to leave the water and travel by cart, yet he was unwilling to spend more than necessary.
舍舟换车，却又舍不得多花盘缠，

Along the way, he asked innkeepers to find carts or boats on which he could share a ride.
一路托客店代找便车、便船，

This saved considerable expense but wasted so much time that nearly half a year passed before he reached North Tongzhou.
花费固然省得多，时间却虚掷了，以至于走了几乎半年，才到北通州。


The town was a major junction for travel by land and water.
这里是个水陆大码头，

The granary vice-minister was stationed there, and countless local people earned their living from the grain boats and warehouses.
仓场侍郎驻扎在此，当地靠漕船、廒仓为生的，不知其数。

It was now the busy season, when southern fleets converged and their rice was brought into storage.
这时正是南漕云集、漕米入仓的旺季。

The transport associations had all sorts of official and private dealings with the registered grain-handling operators, while the boatmen's private cargoes also had to be unloaded for sale.
漕帮与“花户”，有各种公务私事接头，漕丁所带的私货，也要运上岸来销售，

Teahouses, taverns, inns, and bathhouses were crowded everywhere.
因此茶坊酒肆、客店浴池，到处都是客满。

Wang hired a porter to carry his two bundles of luggage on a shoulder pole and tried several inns, but could find nowhere to stay.
王有龄雇了个脚伕，挑着一担行李，运投数处客店，找不到下榻之处。


At last he came to the Xingfa Inn outside the west gate.
最后到了西关一家“兴发店”，

There were relatively few people, carts, or horses loitering at its entrance.
看门口的闲人车马还不多，

This one ought to do, he thought.
王有龄心想：这一处差不多了。

Repeated refusals had taught him something: a polite request at the counter was all too easily turned down.
几次碰壁的经验，让他学了个乖，跟柜上好言商量，反而易于见拒。

Better to put on an official air and intimidate the tradesmen.
不如拿出官派来，反倒可以把买卖人唬倒。


He straightened his riding jacket, took a large pair of dark smoky-quartz spectacles from his breast, put them on, and strode inside.
于是，他把身上那件马褂扯一扯平，从怀中取出来一副茶晶大墨镜戴上，昂然直入，

An attendant hurried to meet him.
伙计赶紧迎出来，

Before the man could speak, Wang issued an imposing command: “Find me a quiet room.”
他不等他开口，先就大模大样地吩咐：“给找一间清静的屋子。”


Smiling respectfully, the attendant first asked, “May I have your honourable surname, sir?”
伙计陪着笑先请教：“你老贵姓？”


“Wang.”
“王。”


“Ah.
“喔，

You have come from the south, I presume?”
想是从南边来？”


“Mm,” Wang replied.
“嗯。”王有龄答道：

“I am going to the capital on business at the Board of Civil Appointments.”
“我上京到吏部公干。”


The attendant had seen plenty of officials awaiting appointment and had recognised the type at a glance.
那伙计对这些候补官儿见得多了，一望便知，

Now that Wang had confirmed it, he changed his form of address.
现在由他自己口中证实，便改了称呼：

“Your Honour Wang!”
“王老爷！”

Then, hesitating, he added, “We do still have two rooms, but I am afraid I cannot let you have either.”
然后踌躇着说：“屋子倒是还有两间，不敢让王老爷住！”


“Why not?”
“为什么？”


“The department magistrate's office has sent someone to reserve them.
“知州衙门派人来定下了。

An imperial commissioner is expected in a day or two, with a large entourage.
有位钦差大人一半天就到，带的人很多，

Every vacant room in the inns outside the west gate has been booked.
西关这几家客店的空房，全给包了。

I am very sorry, Your Honour, but perhaps you could try elsewhere.”
实在对不起，王老爷再找一家看看。”

He made another respectful salute, repeating, “Please forgive us, Your Honour.”
说着又请了个安，连声：“王老爷包涵。”


The man's manner left Wang no grounds for an unreasonable demand.
看他这副神情，王有龄不便再说不讲理的话，

He could only return to persuasion.
依然只好软商量：

“I've already tried several places.
“我已经走了好几家，

You really must help me find a room.
务必托你想办法，给腾一间屋子。

I'll stay just one night and leave first thing tomorrow.”
我住一宿，明天一早就走。”


A stay of one night was easier to accommodate.
只住一宿，便好说话，

The attendant agreed to consult the proprietor.
伙计答应跟柜上去商量。


The guests innkeepers dreaded most were the military officers from the grain boats.
柜上最头痛的客人，是漕船上的武官，

Their rank was low, but their airs were grand.
官儿不大，官架子大，

They shouted abuse at the slightest provocation, struck attendants who answered back, and made trouble whenever the service fell short.
动辄“混帐王八蛋”地骂，伙计回句嘴就得挨打，伺侯得稍欠周到便要闹事。

As mere captains and lieutenants, they were not even senior enough to serve as personal attendants to a governor-general or governor.
他们以“千总”、“把总”的职称，给总督、巡抚当“戈什哈”还不够格的官儿，

They dared behave so outrageously because their associations stood behind them.
敢于如此蛮横无理，就因为有他们的“帮”在撑腰。

The grain-transport men also had a secret organisation, so closely guarded that outsiders could learn nothing of its inner workings.
漕帮暗中还有组织，异常隐秘，局外的“空子”无从窥其堂奥，

All anyone knew was that they were fiercely united.
所知道的就是极其团结，

At a shout of “Fight!” every fist would rise, and they would smash the inn to pieces before anything else.
一声喊“打”，个个伸拳，先砸烂客店再说。

If a lawsuit followed, so be it: men and money could be produced at a word.
至于闹出事来，打官司就打官司，要人要钱，呼叱立办，

No inn could possibly stand against them.
客店里是无论如何斗不过他们的。

In such circumstances, invoking a government reservation was the simplest way to avoid endless trouble.
所以遇到这样的情形，干脆往官府一推，倒省了多少麻烦。


Wang was different.
但王有龄不同，

He too gave himself official airs, but he was courteous and cultivated, and did not look unreasonable.
虽然也有些官架子，文质彬彬，不象个不讲理的人，

Nor did he appear to be one of those hardened travellers who knew every trick and were difficult to manage.
再说，看他也不象习干行旅，相当难缠的“老油子”，

The proprietor therefore agreed to take him in, subject to one condition.
因而答应容留，但有一句话要声明在先。


“Your Honour Wang,” the attendant said, “there is something we must make clear beforehand.
“王老爷！”那伙计说：“有句话说在头里，

We hear that the commissioner has already left the capital.
听说钦差已经出京了，

We cannot be sure whether he will arrive tonight or tomorrow morning.
是今天晚上到，还是明天早晨列，可保不定，

If he comes tonight, we shall have to ask you to put up with some inconvenience.
倘或今天晚上到呢，那就只好委屈您老了。

Of course, we won't leave you with nowhere to sleep.
话说回来，也不能让您老没有有地方住，

But... well, in that case, you would have to squeeze onto the big heated sleeping platform with the rest of us.”
不过……嘿、嘿，那时候，只好跟我们一起在大炕上挤一挤了。“


“Certainly, certainly!”
“行，行！”

Exhausted by his journey, Wang was entirely satisfied and readily agreed.
疲累不堪的王有龄，心满意足，满口应承：

“As long as there is somewhere to sleep, that will do.”
“只需有地方睡就行了。”


The attendant found him a single room in the western side courtyard.
于是伙计在西跨院给他找了个单间。

Wang paid the porter and had his luggage brought in.
开发了脚夫，把行李拿到屋内。

The attendant, whose name was Liu Si, supplied tea and water, then chatted with him about his journey as he unwrapped his bedding.
那伙计叫刘四，伺候了茶水，一面替他解铺盖，一面就跟他搭话，问问来踪去迹。

Once Wang had washed and settled down with his tea, Liu brought an oil lamp and asked what he would like for supper.
等他洗完脸喝茶休息的时候，拿来一盏油灯，顺便问他晚饭怎么吃？


Reaching Tongzhou felt almost like reaching the capital itself, and Wang was in a relaxed mood.
到了通州就等于到了京城了，王有龄心情颇为悠闲，

He ordered two dishes and a pot of clear grain liquor, which he drank slowly.
要了两个碟子，一壶白干，慢慢喝着。

Pleasantly tipsy, he was recalling his days with Hu Xueyan when the door curtain lifted and someone asked, “Would Your Honour care to hear a song?”
正醺醺然在回忆与胡雪岩相处的那一段日子，只见门帘一掀，随即有人问道：“老爷！听个曲儿吧？”


The voice was clear and lively.
说话的声音倒还脆，

Looking up, Wang saw a woman of about thirty, her face heavily powdered and her hair arranged in a high “magpie's tail,” jingling with silver pins and little gold bells.
王有龄抬眼一看，是个三十岁左右的妇人，擦了一脸的粉，梳得高高的一个“喜鹊尾巴”，叮铃当啷插着些银钗小金铃的。

She wore a green jacket, black trousers, and embroidered red shoes scarcely larger than rice dumplings.
绿袄黑裤，下面穿一双粽子大的绣花红鞋。

On closer inspection, her complexion was rather dark, but every movement of her eyes seemed to overflow with charm.
重新再看到她脸上，皮肤黑一些，那眼睛却顾盼之间，娇韵欲流。

Half drunk and looking at her by lamplight, Wang thought her a perfect beauty.
王有龄有了五分酒意，醉眼又是灯下，看过去便是十足的美人了。


He had encountered this kind of business several times on his journey north.
这北道上的勾当他也领教过几次，

Beckoning, he said, “Come here.”
便招一招手说：“过来！”


The woman smiled, waved to the older woman behind her, and entered alone.
那妇人嫣然一笑，向她身后的老妇摆一摆手，然后一个人走了进来，

After a respectful greeting, she asked, “May I know your surname, sir?”
请个安问道：“老爷贵姓啊？”


“Wang.
“我姓王。”

And yours?”
王有龄问她：“你呢？”


“They call me Jincui.”
“小名儿叫金翠。”


“Jincui.
“金翠！

Mm, yes.”
嗯，嗯！”

He examined her once more from head to foot and nodded his approval.
他把她从头到脚，又细细端详了一番，点点头表示满意。


“Are you here alone, Your Honour?”
“王老爷，就是一个人？”


“Yes, alone.” Wang added, “Go out for now.
“对了，一个人。”王有龄又说，“你先出去，

I'll ask Liu Si to call you later.”
回头我找刘四来招呼你。”


Jincui gave him another inviting glance.
于是金翠又飞了个媚眼，

In a slightly cloying voice, she said, “Thank you, Your Honour.
用她那有些发腻的声音说道：“多谢王老爷，

Do remember me.
您老可别忘了，

Be sure to have Liu Si call me.”
千万叫刘四招呼我啊！”


“I won't forget.”
“不会，不会！”


She lifted the curtain and left.
金翠掀着帘子走了。

Wang continued drinking, now pouring less and sipping more slowly than ever.
王有龄依然喝他的酒，于是浅斟低酌，越发慢了。


He drank and waited, but Liu Si failed to appear.
就这样一面喝，一面等，刘四却老是不露面。

Instead, other wandering prostitutes came to solicit him.
反倒又来了些游娼兜搭。

Having already made his choice, Wang scarcely looked at them and soon grew irritated.
因为心有所属，他对那些野草闲花，懒得一顾，且有厌烦之感，

Finally, he stepped outside himself and called loudly, “Liu Si! Liu Si!”
便亲自走出屋去，大声喊道：“刘四，刘四！”


Liu was still in the front courtyard.
刘四还在前院，

At the sound of Wang's voice, he hurried over, assuming that his guest was impatient for food.
听得呼唤，赶紧奔了来伺候，他只当王有龄催促饭食，

He began by apologising: there were so many travellers that day that the kitchen could not keep up.
所以一进来先道歉，说今天旅客特别多，厨下忙不过来，

He suggested another four liang of liquor.
建议王有龄再来四两白干：

“Take your time and enjoy a drink, Your Honour,” he said with a conspiratorial smile.
“您老慢慢喝着。”他诡秘地笑道，

“I'll find you some entertainment later.”
“回头我替您老找个乐子。”


“What sort of entertainment?” Wang asked, though he knew perfectly well.
“什么乐子？”王有龄明知故问地。


“It's still early.
“这会儿还早，

There's no hurry.
您老别忙。

If nobody comes after the second watch, this room will be yours for the night.
等二更过后，没有人来，这间屋就归您老住了。

I'll bring someone you're certain to like.” Liu added, “The woman I have in mind is the very best in her trade.
我找个人来，包管您老称心如意。”刘四又说：“我找的这个人，是她们这一行的顶儿、尖儿，

Her name is Jincui.”
名叫金翠。”


Wang smiled.
王有龄笑了，

“Bring more liquor!” he ordered loudly.
“再拿酒来！”他大声吩咐。

喝酒喝到二更天，吃了两张饼，

Liu cleared away the remains and brewed another pot of tea.
刘四收拾残肴，又沏上一壶茶来，

接着便听见帘钩一响，金翠不速而至了。

“好好伺候！”刘四向她叮嘱了这一句，退身出去，顺手把房门带上。

金翠便斟了一碗茶，还解下衣襟上的一块粉红手绢，擦一擦碗口的茶渍，才双手捧到王有龄面前。


For a northern woman of her calling, her manner was gentle and quiet.
虽是北地胭脂，举止倒还温柔文静，

Wang liked her all the more.
王有龄越有好感，

拉着她的手问道：“你今年多大？”

“If I tell you the truth, I'm afraid you won't want me.

不说实话，我又不肯骗你。”

“我嫌你什么？”王有龄很认真地说：

“I don't mind.”
“我不嫌！”

金翠那双灵活的眼珠，在他脸上绕了一下，低下头去，把眼帘垂了下来，只见长长的睫毛不住跳运。

这未免有情的神态，足慰一路星霜，

王有龄决定明天再在这里住一天。


After a night of intimacy, added to the fatigue of travelling, he slept deeply and comfortably.
一夜缱绻，加以旅途辛劳，他第二天睡得十分酣适，

中间醒了一次，从枕头下掏出一个银壳表来看了看，将近午时，

It was late, but since he intended to spend another evening with Jincui, there was no reason to hurry.
虽已不早，但有心与金翠再续前缘，便无须亟亟，

He turned over, pulled the covers over his head, and went back to sleep.
翻个身依旧蒙头大睡。

这一睡睡不多时，为窗外的争吵声所谅醒，

听出一个是刘四，正低声下声地在赔罪，

说原知屋子早已定下，不能更赁与别的旅客，

“But His Honour Wang had already tried several inns without success, and he looked unwell.
“不过，这位王老爷连找了几家鄙不行，看样子还带青病，

出门哪里不行方便？

Please don't be angry, sir.
总爷，你别生气，

Sit down for a moment and have some tea.
清稍坐一坐，喝碗茶，

I'll clear the room for you at once.”
我马上给你腾。”

王有龄一听，原来是为了自己占了别人的屋子，

Wang could not let Liu Si get into trouble over that.
这不好让刘四为难，

He hurriedly sat up, put on his clothes, and got out of bed.
急忙一翻身坐了起来，披衣下床。

他一面拔闩开门，一面向外大声招呼：“刘四，你不必跟客人争执，

我让就是了。”

请进来坐吧！”

“不要紧，不要紧。”姓杨的也很客气了，

“Take your time, Your Honour.”
“王老爷你慢慢儿来！”

开出口来是云南乡音。

喉音特重的云南话，本就能予人以纯挚的感觉，

Wang had lived in Yunnan as a boy, so the accent sounded especially familiar and warm to him.
王有龄又从小在云南住过，所以入耳更觉亲切，

随即含笑问道：“你家哪里，昆明？”

他这一句也是云南话，

字虽咬得不太准，韵味却足。

两人连连拱手，互道“回见”。
