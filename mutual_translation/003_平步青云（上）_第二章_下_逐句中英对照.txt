胡雪岩
《平步青云（上）》第二章（下，至本章结束）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume I — Chapter Two, concluding part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


“话虽如此，我未免受之有愧。”
“Even so, I cannot help feeling that I do not deserve it.”


“这不须如此想。
“You needn't think that way.

倒是那位在你穷途之际，慷慨援手的胡君，
But you must remember Mr Hu, who generously helped you when you had nowhere to turn.

别人非亲非故帮你的忙，
He was neither a relative nor an old family friend.

无非看你是个人才，会有一番事业，
He helped because he saw ability in you and believed you could accomplish something.

你该记着这一点！”
You should keep that in mind.”


王有龄自然深深受教。
Wang took the advice deeply to heart.

他本来就不是没有大志，连番奇遇的鼓舞，越发激起一片雄心，
He had never lacked ambition, and these successive strokes of fortune stirred it still further.

只一闭上眼，便看得前程锦绣，目迷神眩，
Whenever he closed his eyes, he saw a future of dazzling promise.

虽还未补缺，却已在享受做官的乐趣了。
Though he had not yet obtained an appointment, he was already savouring the pleasures of office.


第二天早晨起身，何桂清已写好了一封致黄宗汉的信在等他。
When he rose the next morning, He Guiqing was waiting with a letter to Huang Zonghan.

这封信不是泛泛的八行，甚至也不象一封荐信，
It was no ordinary note of introduction; indeed, it scarcely resembled a recommendation at all.

里面谈了许多知交的私话，
Much of it concerned private matters between close friends.

然后才提到王有龄，说是“总角之交，谊如昆季”，特为嘱他指捐分发浙江，以便请黄宗汉培植造就，
Only then did it mention Wang, describing him as “a friend since boyhood, as dear as a brother,” whom He had specifically advised to purchase assignment to Zhejiang so that Huang might nurture his abilities and advance his career.

照这封信的恳切结实来说，就差何桂清当面拱手拜托了。
The letter was so earnest and substantial that little was lacking except He's presence in person, hands clasped in appeal.


等看过封好，王有龄便跟何桂清要人。
After Wang had read it and it had been sealed, he asked He to provide someone to accompany him.

以他的意思，很想请杨承福做个帮手，这一点何桂清无法满足他的希望，
He very much wanted Yang Chengfu, but this was a wish his friend could not grant.

因为杨承福是他最得力的人，
Yang was his most capable assistant.

许多公事、关系只有他清楚首尾，非他人所能替代。
Many official matters and personal connections were fully understood by Yang alone; no one could replace him.


“这样吧，”杨承福建议，“叫高升跟了王老爷去，
“Why not send Gao Sheng with His Honour?” Yang suggested.

也很妥当。”
“That would work very well.”


高升也很诚实能干，他自己也愿意跟王有龄，事情就算定局。
Gao Sheng was honest and capable too, and willing to accompany Wang, so the arrangement was settled.

拜别何桂清，谢了杨承福，由高升照料着，当天就到了京里。
Wang took leave of He Guiqing, thanked Yang, and, under Gao's care, reached the capital that same day.

本来想住会馆，
He had intended to stay at the provincial guildhall.

因为本年王子恩科，明年癸丑正科，
But the current renzi year had brought a special examination, and the following guichou year would bring the regular one.

接连两年会试，落第的、新到的举人，挤得满坑满谷，
With metropolitan examinations in two successive years, unsuccessful candidates and newly arrived provincial graduates packed the place.

要找一间空房实在很难。
An empty room was almost impossible to find.

而且三有龄以监生的底子来加捐，
Moreover, Wang was seeking to upgrade a purchased qualification on the basis of his Imperial Academy status.

跟那些明年四月便可一举成名的举人在一起，相形之下，仙凡异途，也自觉难堪。
Beside provincial graduates who might win distinction in the fourth month, he felt that he belonged to a lesser world, and the comparison embarrassed him.


便索性破费些，在两河沿找了家客店住。
He therefore decided to spend a little more and take a room at an inn in Liangheyan.


天气极冷，
The weather was bitterly cold.

生了炉子还象坐在冰害里，
Even with a stove burning, the room felt like an ice cellar.

高开上街买了皮纸和面，在炉子上打了一盆浆糊。
Gao went out to buy tough paper and flour, then cooked a basin of paste on the stove.

把皮纸裁成两指宽的纸条，把窗户板壁上所有的缝隙都糊没。
Cutting the paper into strips two fingers wide, he sealed every crack in the windows and wooden partitions.

西北风进不来，炉人才能发生作用，
Once the northwest wind was shut out, the stove could do its work.

立刻满室生春，十分舒服。
The room soon felt as warm as spring.

王有龄吃过晚饭，便跟高开商量正事。
After supper, Wang discussed their business with him.


“老爷，我有个主意，
“Your Honour, I have an idea.

你看使得使不得？”离升说道，
See what you think of it,” Gao said.

“明天就是腊八，
“Tomorrow is the eighth day of the last month.

还有十几天工夫就‘封印’了。”
In little more than ten days, the government offices will seal up their official seals for the New Year.”


“啊！”一下提醒了王有龄，
“Ah!” Wang suddenly remembered.

“一‘封印’就是一个月，
“Once the seals are put away, the offices will be closed for a month.

这十几天办不成，在京里过年空等，
If we cannot finish in these ten-odd days, we'll have to spend the New Year here waiting.

那耽误的工夫就大了。”
That would mean a long delay.”


“最啊！
“Exactly.

打哪儿来说，都是件划不来的事。
Whichever way you look at it, that would be a poor bargain.

所以我在想，不如多花几个钱，尽这十几天把事情办妥，赶年里就动身回南。”
I was thinking that it might be better to spend a little extra and finish the business before the offices close, then set off south before the year ends.”


“年里就动身？
“Before the year ends?

不太急了吗？”
Wouldn't that be rather hurried?”


“我是替老爷打算。
“I'm thinking of your interests, sir.

京里如果没有什么熟人，在店里过年，也不是味儿。
If you have few friends in the capital, spending the New Year at an inn won't be much pleasure.

再说从大年初一到元宵，到哪儿也得大把花钱，
Besides, from New Year's Day to the Lantern Festival, you have to spend freely wherever you go.

真正划不来。
It really isn't worth it.

与其这个样，莫如就在路上过年。
Better to spend the holiday on the road.

再有一层，“高升凑近了他说，”
There's another point.” Gao leaned closer.

老爷最好赶在何大人之前，或者差不多的日子到浙江见黄抚合，
“It would be best to reach Zhejiang and see Governor Huang before His Excellency He arrives, or at about the same time.

何大人的信才管用。“
That's when his letter will carry weight.”


王有龄恍然大悟，觉得高升的话，实在有见识。
Wang understood at once and recognised the wisdom of Gao's advice.

黄宗汉此人既有刻薄的名声，
Huang had a reputation for harshness.

保不定在椿寿那件案子结束以后，过河拆桥，不买何桂清的帐。
Once Chunshou's case was settled, he might well discard the man who had helped him and cease to oblige He Guiqing.

如果正是何桂清到浙江查案时，有求于人，情形自然不同。
While He was in Zhejiang investigating the case, Huang would still need his goodwill, and the situation would be different.

总之，宁早勿迟，无论如何不错。
In any event, arriving sooner rather than later could only be the right course.


“我听你的话，
“I'll take your advice.

就这么办。
Let's do that.

不过，你可有路子呢？”
But do you know how to go about it?”


“路子总有的。
“There will be a way.

明天我就去找。”高升极有把握地说：
I'll start looking tomorrow,” Gao answered confidently.

“包管又便宜又好。”
“I'll see that it is done well and at a reasonable cost.”


于是王有龄欣然开了箱子，把旧捐的盐大使“部照”取了出来，
Pleased, Wang opened his trunk and took out the Board's certificate for his original purchased qualification as a salt-station superintendent.

接着磨墨伸纸开具“三代”，细陈经历，
He then ground ink, spread out paper, and prepared a statement of his ancestry for three generations and a detailed account of his background.

把文件都预备妥当，一一交代明白。又取二十两银子交给高升，作为应酬花费。
Once the documents were ready, he explained everything to Gao and gave him twenty taels for the expenses of making the necessary contacts.


从第二天起，高升开始奔走。
Gao began his rounds the next day.

起初的消息不大好，不是说时间上没有把握，就是额外需索的费用太高。
The first reports were discouraging: either completion in time could not be guaranteed, or the extra fees demanded were too high.

这样过了三四天，
Three or four days passed.

不但王有龄心里焦灼，连高升自己也有些气馁了。
Wang grew anxious, and even Gao began to lose heart.


就在放弃希望，打算着在京过年时，事情突然有了转机，
Just as they were abandoning hope and preparing to spend the New Year in the capital, matters unexpectedly changed.

吏部有个书办，家里遭了回禄之灾，还烧死了一母一子，
A clerk at the Board of Civil Appointments had suffered a fire at home in which a mother and child had died.

年近岁逼，逢此家破人亡的惨事，
With the year drawing to a close, his household was ruined.

偏偏这书办又因案下狱，
To make matters worse, he himself was in prison over a case.

雪上加霜，濒临绝境，必须求援于他的同事们。
Desperate and with nowhere else to turn, he had to appeal to his colleagues.


帮忙无非“有饯出钱，有力出力”，
Helping meant contributing money if one had it, or effort if one could.

但出钱的不过十两、八两银子，倒是出力的帮忙得大。
Financial contributions amounted to no more than eight or ten taels apiece; practical assistance proved more substantial.

年下公事特忙，
Official business was especially heavy before the New Year.

部里从司官到书办，知道各省差官，以及本人来候选捐纳，谋干前程的，都希望提前办理，
From departmental officials down to clerks, everyone knew that provincial messengers and men seeking appointments or purchasing qualifications wanted their affairs settled early.

在京里过年，赔贴盘缠，空耗辰光还不说，有些限期的公事，耽误了还有处分。
Spending the holiday in the capital meant wasted time and additional living costs; missing a deadline on official business might also bring punishment.

所以这时是留难需索，择肥而噬的好机会，
It was therefore an ideal opportunity to obstruct proceedings and extract money from those who could pay.

现在为了帮同事的忙，他们私下定了章程，出了“公价”，
To assist their unfortunate colleague, the clerks privately agreed on a standard schedule of charges.

凡是想限期办妥的公事，除了照平时的行市纳规费以外，另外看情况加送若干，
Anyone wanting business completed within a fixed time would pay the usual unofficial fees, plus an extra sum according to the circumstances.

多下的钱就归那遭祸的书办所得。
The addition would go to the stricken clerk.

对外人来说，这比自己去撞木钟，辗转托人，重重剥削要便宜得多。
For outsiders, this was much cheaper than trying their luck, seeking one intermediary after another, and being fleeced at every stage.


高升从琉璃厂的笔墨庄里得到了这个消息，
Gao heard of the arrangement at a brush-and-ink shop in Liulichang.

又去找熟人打听，果有其事，匆忙回来说与王有龄。
He checked with an acquaintance, confirmed that it was real, and hurried back to tell Wang.

就托那个熟人，代为接洽，说定了价钱，一共四百八十两银子，加捐为候补州县，分发浙江。
Through this acquaintance they negotiated a total of 480 taels to upgrade Wang's qualification to an expectant local magistracy with assignment to Zhejiang.

其中三分之二“正项”，三分之一是“杂费”，
Two-thirds represented the official payment; one-third covered miscellaneous expenses.

打成两张银票，
They obtained two bank drafts.

正项自己去缴，杂费托经手人转交，
Wang paid the official amount himself, while the intermediary delivered the other.

不过五天工夫，就把簇新的一张“部照”和称为“实收”的捐纳交银收据都拿到手了。
In only five days, Wang had both a new certificate from the Board and the official receipt for his contribution.


这件大事倒办好了，长行回南，却颇费周章。
The main business was settled, but arranging the long journey south proved troublesome.

急景调年，车船都不大愿意做此一笔买卖。
With the year almost over, carters and boatmen were reluctant to undertake it.

王有龄便又跟高升商议，
Wang consulted Gao again.

大事已妥，随时可走，也不争在这几天，
Now that everything important was done, they could leave whenever they wished; a few days made little difference.

不如过了“破五”再说。
Why not wait until after the fifth day of the New Year?

高升原是为主人打算，唯命是从，
Gao's only concern was his master's interests, and he accepted the decision.

当时使先订好了两辆大车，付了一半车价，约定开年初七、宜于长行的黄道吉日动身。
They booked two large carts, paid half the fare, and agreed to depart on the seventh day, an auspicious date for a long journey.


这时京里除了军机处，大小衙门，都已封印。
By then, every government office except the Grand Council had closed for the holiday.

满街都是匆匆忙忙的行人，有的忧容满面，四处告帮过年，有的提着灯笼，星夜讨债。
The streets were full of hurried figures: some wore anxious faces as they sought help to get through the New Year; others carried lanterns and pursued debts late into the night.

王有龄却是心定神闲，每天由高升领着，到各处去闲逛。
Wang, however, was at ease and went sightseeing with Gao every day.

他在京里也有些熟人，
He knew some people in the capital, but there were several reasons not to visit.

但一则年节下大家都忙，不便去打搅，
Everyone was busy with the holiday, and he did not wish to disturb them.

二则带的土仪不多，空手登门拜访，于礼不台，
He had few local gifts left, and arriving empty-handed would be discourteous.

三则是他自己觉得现在境况不佳，不如下见，
He also felt that his present circumstances were not impressive enough.

等将来得意了，欢然道故，才有人情酬醉之乐。
Better to wait until he had prospered; then recalling old times would bring the real pleasure of friendly hospitality.

因此，除了极少的一两家至亲，登门一揖以外，其余同乡亲友那里，一概下去。
Apart from brief calls on one or two close relatives, he visited none of his fellow provincials or other friends.


到了大年三十，会馆里的执事邀去过年，
On New Year's Eve, the guildhall stewards invited him to join their celebration.

吃完年夜饭，厅上拉开桌子，摇摊的摇摊，推牌九的推牌九，
After dinner, tables were set up in the hall for various forms of gambling, including the shaking of counters and games of pai gow.

王有龄不好此道，早早回到了西河沿客店。
Wang had no taste for these pursuits and returned early to his inn at Xiheyan.


高升是他事先放了他假的，不在客店。
He had already given Gao the evening off, so the servant was away.

伙计替他拨旺了炉人，沏了热茶，
An inn attendant stirred up the stove and made fresh tea.

枯坐无聊，又弄了酒来喝，无奈“独醉不成欢”，
Bored with sitting alone, Wang ordered some wine, but drinking by himself brought no cheer.

有心摘一朵野花，点缀佳节，想想自己已是“父母官”的身分，
He considered seeking a woman's company to enliven the festival, then remembered that he was now qualified to become a magistrate, a parent to the people.

怕让高升发觉了瞧不起。
He feared Gao would find out and think less of him.

“八大胡同”倒是近在咫尺，但“清吟小班”是有名的销金窝，
The Eight Great Lanes were close by, but their fashionable singing houses were notorious places to squander money.

这一年异遇甚多，保不定又逢一段奇缘，
After so many strange encounters that year, who knew whether another romance might await him?

那一下，五百年前的风流债还不情，岂不辜负了胡、何二人的盛情厚望？
If he became entangled in some endless debt of passion, would he not betray the generosity and hopes of both Hu and He?


在满街爆竹声中，王有龄一个人悄俏地睡下了，
Amid firecrackers sounding throughout the streets, Wang quietly went to bed alone.

却是怎么样也没有睡意。
Yet sleep would not come.


通前彻后，细思平生，有凄凉，也有欢欣，有感慨，却更多希望。
Looking back over his life, he found sadness and joy, much to reflect on, and still more to hope for.

他在想，不走何桂清那样的“正途”，已是输人一着，
By failing to take the regular examination route as He Guiqing had done, he had already fallen behind.

但也不能就此认输，
But that was no reason to admit defeat.

一个人总要能展其所长，
A man must develop his own strengths.

虽说书读得没有何桂清好，但从小跟在父亲身边，了解民生，熟悉吏治，以及吃苦耐劳，习于交接，
He might be less learned than He, yet he had grown up beside his father, observing people's livelihoods, becoming familiar with administration, learning to endure hardship, and dealing with all sorts of people.

却不是那班埋首窗下，不通世务的书生可比。
In these things, scholars buried in their books and ignorant of the world could not compare with him.

“世事洞明皆学问”，
“A thorough understanding of human affairs is itself learning.”

妄自匪薄，志气消沉，聪明才智也就灰塞萎缩了。
If he belittled himself and lost his ambition, his abilities would only wither.

于今逢到大好机会，又正当国家多事，明主求治之际，
Now a splendid opportunity had come, at a time when the country faced grave difficulties and an enlightened ruler sought effective government.

风尘俗吏的作为，亦未见得会比金马玉堂的学士逊色！
A practical official seasoned by the world might achieve as much as a scholar in the splendid halls of court.


转念到此，顿时浮起一片要做一番事业的雄心壮志。
These reflections stirred a powerful determination to accomplish something.

但以大器自期，觉得肚子里的货色还不够，不是同赋文章，而是于国计民生有关的学问。
Yet if he expected great things of himself, he needed more knowledge—not poetry and elegant essays, but learning relevant to the country's administration and the people's welfare.


因此年初一那天逛琉璃厂，别人买吃的、玩的，王有时象那些好书成癖的名士一样，只在书铺里坐。
Thus, when he visited Liulichang on New Year's Day, while others bought food and amusements, Wang remained in the bookshops like a scholar with a passion for collecting books.

王有龄此时的气度服饰，已非昔比，
His bearing and clothes were now very different from before.

掌柜的十分巴结，先拜了年，摆上果盘，然后请教姓氏、乡里、科名。
The proprietor was eager to please him: first New Year greetings, then a plate of fruit, and finally polite inquiries about his surname, home province, and examination achievements.


“敝姓王，
“My surname is Wang.

福建，
I'm from Fujian.

秋闹刚刚侥幸。”
I was fortunate enough to pass last autumn.”

王有龄的口气是自表新科举人，
He spoke as though he were a newly qualified provincial graduate.

好在“王”是大姓，便冒充了也不怕拆穿。
Wang was such a common surname that he felt safe from exposure.


“喔，喔”王老爷春风满面，本科一定‘联捷’。
“Ah, indeed! With good fortune shining in your face, Your Honour is certain to succeed again in the coming metropolitan examination.

预贺，预贺！“
My congratulations in advance!”


“ 谢谢。
“Thank you.

‘场中莫论文’，
In the examination hall, literary merit is no guarantee.

看运气罢了。”
We must leave it to luck.”


“王老爷说得好一口官话，
“Your Honour speaks excellent Mandarin.

想来随老太爷在外多年？”
You must have spent many years accompanying your father away from home.”


“是的。”
“Yes.”

王有龄心想，再盘问下去要露马脚了，
Further questions might expose him, Wang thought.

便即问道：“可有什么实用之学的好书？”
He promptly changed the subject. “Have you any good books on practical affairs?”


“怎么没有？”
“Certainly.”

那掌柜想了想，自己从书架子取了部新书来，
The proprietor considered, then took a new set of books from a shelf himself.

“这部书，不知王老爷有没有？”
“Does Your Honour already own this?”


一看是贺长龄的《皇朝经世文编》王有龄久闻其名，欣然答道：“我要一部。”
It was He Changling's Collected Essays on Statecraft under the Present Dynasty. Wang had long heard of it and replied with pleasure, “I'll take a set.”


“这部书实在好。
“An excellent work.

当今讲究实学，
Practical learning is much valued nowadays.

读熟了这部书，殿试策论一定出色。”
Master this book, and your policy essay in the palace examination is bound to be distinguished.”


“有没有‘洋务’上的书？”
“Have you any books on foreign affairs?”


“讲洋务，有部贵省林大人编的书，非看不可。”
“On that subject, there is a work compiled by His Excellency Lin of your own province that you must read.”


那是林则徐编的《四洲志》，王有龄也买了。
This was Lin Zexu's Account of the Four Continents, which Wang also bought.

书店掌柜看出王有龄所要的是些什么书，牵连不断，搬出一大堆来，
Understanding now what his customer wanted, the proprietor brought out one related work after another until a pile had accumulated.

一时也无暇细看内容，好在价钱多还公道，便来者不拒，捆载而旧。
There was no time to inspect them all closely, but the prices were generally fair, so Wang accepted them and had them bundled up to take back.


从这天起，王有龄就在客店里“闭户读书”，
From that day onward, he shut himself in his room to study.

把一部《皇朝经世文编》中，谈盐法、河务、漕运的文章，反复研读，一个字都不肯轻易放过。
He repeatedly read the essays on salt administration, river management, and grain transport in the Collected Essays on Statecraft, unwilling to pass lightly over a single word.

他对湖南安化陶文毅公陶澍的政绩，原就敬仰已久，
He had long admired the achievements of Tao Shu of Anhua, Hunan, posthumously honoured as Wenyi.

此时看了那些奏议、条陈，了解了改革盐法槽运的经过，越发向往。
Reading these memorials and proposals, and learning how the reforms of salt administration and grain transport had been carried out, deepened his admiration.

同时也有了一个心得，兴利不难，难于除弊！
He also reached a conclusion of his own: creating benefits was not the hardest task; removing abuses was.

“革路蓝缕，以启山林”，只要功夫用到了，自能生利，
To open up new ground through hardship, effort was enough to produce results.

但已生之种，为人侵渔把持，弊端丛生，
Once those benefits existed, however, people appropriated and controlled them until abuses multiplied.

要去消除，使成了侵害人的“权利”，自会遭遇到极大的反抗阻挠。
Removing the abuses then meant infringing what those people regarded as their rights, and fierce resistance was inevitable.

他看陶澍的整顿盐务，改革漕运，论办法也不过实事求是，期于允当，并没有什么了不得的地方，
Tao's methods themselves were not extraordinary. He had sought truth in the facts and aimed at workable, fair arrangements.

所可贵的是，他的除弊的决心与魄力。
What deserved admiration was his determination and courage in abolishing abuses.


这又归结到一个要点：权力。
This led to a further essential point: power.

王有龄在想：俗语说的“大丈夫不可一日无权”，话实在不错。不过这个道理要从反面来看。
The saying that a capable man cannot go a day without authority was true, Wang thought, but its meaning had to be considered from the other side.

有权在手，不能有所作为，庸庸碌碌，随波逐流，则虽未作恶，
A man who held power yet accomplished nothing, drifting along in mediocrity, might commit no direct wrongdoing.

其恶与小人相等，因为官场弊端，就是在此辈手中变得根深蒂固，积重难返的。
Still, his harm was no less than that of a villain, for it was through men like him that official abuses became deeply rooted and almost impossible to remove.


由于有用世之志，不得不留意时局，
His wish to serve the country compelled him to follow current events.

正好客店里到了一个湖北来的差官，就住在他间壁，
By chance, an official messenger from Hubei arrived at the inn and occupied the next room.

客中寂寞，携酒消夜，谈起两湖的情形，
Lonely away from home, they shared wine one evening and discussed Hunan and Hubei.

王有龄才知道洪杨军攻长沙不下，克宁乡、益阳，拥有了几千艘发船，出临资口，渡侗庭湖，占领岳州，
Wang learned that, after failing to take Changsha, the forces of Hong and Yang had captured Ningxiang and Yiyang, acquired several thousand boats, emerged at Linzikou, crossed Lake Dongting, and occupied Yuezhou.

乘胜东下，十一月陷汉阳，十二月里省城武昌也失守了！
Pressing eastward, they had taken Hanyang in the eleventh month and the provincial capital, Wuchang, in the twelfth.

巡抚常大淳、学政、藩司、臬司、提督、总兵，还有道员、知府、知县、同知，几乎全城文武，无不身亡。
Governor Chang Dachun, the education commissioner, the provincial treasurer, the judicial commissioner, the provincial military commander, the brigade commanders, and the circuit intendants, prefects, magistrates, and deputy prefects—almost all the city's civil and military officials had died.

说到悲惨之外，那差官把眼泪掉落在酒杯里。
At the most tragic passages of his account, the messenger's tears fell into his wine cup.


王有龄也为之惨然停杯。
Wang too set down his cup in sorrow.

常大淳由浙江巡抚调湖北，还不到一年，
Less than a year had passed since Chang Dachun's transfer from Zhejiang to Hubei.

他在杭州曾经见过，纯粹是个秉性仁柔的书生，
Wang had seen him in Hangzhou: a scholar of gentle and kindly temperament.

只因为在浙江巡抚往内平治过海盗，朝廷当他会用兵，调到湖北去阻遏洪杨军，
Because he had suppressed pirates while governing Zhejiang, the court had assumed that he understood warfare and sent him to Hubei to halt Hong and Yang's advance.

结果与城同亡，
He had died with the city.

说起来死得有点冤枉。
In a sense, his death was a grievous injustice.


但是，地方官守上有责，
Yet local officials were responsible for defending their territories.

而且朝廷已有旨意，派在籍大臣办理“团练”，以求自保，
The court had already ordered senior officials living in retirement to organise local militias for self-defence.

生逢乱世，哪里管得到文是文，武是武？
In a troubled age, who could insist that civil and military duties remain separate?

必须得有“上马杀贼，下马草露布”的本理，做官才能出人头地。
To distinguish himself in office, a man needed the ability to mount a horse and fight, then dismount and write the announcement of victory.

有了这层省悟，玉有龄又到琉璃厂去买了些《圣武记》之类谈征点方略、练兵筹饷的书，
Recognising this, Wang returned to Liulichang and bought works such as Records of Imperial Military Achievements, dealing with campaign strategy, troop training, and military finance.

预备利用旅途，好好看他一遍。
He intended to read them carefully on his journey.


依照约定的日子，正月初七一早，由陆路自京师动身，经长辛店一直南下，
On the appointed day, the seventh of the first month, he left the capital early by road, heading south through Changxindian.

出京除了由天津走海道以外，水陆两途在山东边境的德州文汇，
Apart from the sea route through Tianjin, the land and water routes out of the capital met at Dezhou on the Shandong border.

运河自京东来，过此偏向西南，经临清、东昌南下，
The canal approached from the east of the capital, then turned southwest through Linqing and Dongchang.

陆路自京西来，过此偏向东南，由平原、禹城、泰安、临沂，进入江苏省境，
The road came from the west and turned southeast through Pingyuan, Yucheng, Tai'an, and Linyi into Jiangsu.

到清江浦，水陆两途又文汇了。
At Qingjiangpu, the two routes met again.


王有龄陆路走了二十天，在整天颠簸的大车中，依旧于不释卷，
For twenty days on the road, Wang kept a book in his hands despite the cart's constant jolting.

到晚宿店，豆大油灯下还做笔记。
At night, in the inns, he continued taking notes beneath the tiny flame of an oil lamp.

就这样把《经世文编》、《圣武记》、《四洲志》都已看完。
In this way he finished the Collected Essays on Statecraft, Records of Imperial Military Achievements, and Account of the Four Continents.

有时车中默想，自觉内而漕、盐、兵事，外面夷情洋务，大致都已了然于胸。
Sometimes, reflecting in the cart, he felt that he now had a general grasp of grain transport, salt administration, and military affairs at home, as well as conditions abroad and relations with foreign countries.


他在路上早就打算好了。
He had already planned the next stage of his journey.

车子讲定到王家营子，
The carts were hired as far as Wangjiayingzi.

渡过黄河就是清江浦，由此再雇船沿运河直放杭州。
Across the Yellow River lay Qingjiangpu, where he would hire a boat to take him along the canal to Hangzhou.

为了印证所学，不妨趁此弃车换船的机会，在情江浦好好住几天。
To test his reading against reality, he might spend a few days there while changing from cart to boat.

这个以韩信而名闻天下的古淮阴，是南来水陆要冲的第一大码头，
This was ancient Huaiyin, famous as the home of Han Xin, and the foremost junction of land and water routes for travellers coming from the south.

江南河道总督专驻此地，
The governor-general of the southern river administration was stationed there.

河务、漕运、以及淮盐的运销，都以此此为枢纽，
River management, grain transport, and the distribution of Huai salt all centred upon the town.

能够实地考察一番，真个“胜读十年书”。
A firsthand investigation would truly be worth ten years of reading.


哪知来到王家营子，就听说太平军起兵，越发厉害。
But at Wangjiayingzi he heard that the Taiping advance was growing ever more alarming.

一到清江浦，立刻就能闻到一种风声鹤嗅的味道，
Upon reaching Qingjiangpu, he immediately sensed panic in the air.

车马络绎，负载着乱糟糟的家具杂物，
An endless stream of carts and horses carried disorderly loads of furniture and household goods.

衣冠不整，口音杂出的异乡人，不计其数，个个脸上有惊惶忧郁的神色，
Countless strangers in dishevelled clothes spoke in unfamiliar accents, every face anxious and distressed.

显而易见的，都是些从南而逃来的难民。
Plainly, they were refugees fleeing from the south.


“老爷！”高升悄悄说道，“大事不妙！
“Your Honour,” Gao said quietly, “things look bad.

我看客店怕都客满了。
I fear the inns will all be full.

带着行李去瞎闯，累赘得很。
Dragging the luggage from one to another would be a burden.

他老先在茶馆坐一坐，看好了行李，我找店，
Why don't you sit in a teahouse and watch it while I find us a room?

找妥当了再来请者爷过去。”
Once I've secured one, I'll come back for you.”


“好，好！”
“Very well.”

王有龄抬头一望，路南就是一家大茶馆，
Looking up, Wang saw a large teahouse on the south side of the street.

便说，“我就在这里等。”
“I'll wait here.”


到了茶馆，先把行李堆在一边，开发了挑侍，要找座头休息。
Inside, they stacked their luggage to one side and paid the porter, then looked for somewhere to sit.

举目四顾，乱哄哄一片，
The place was noisy and crowded.

只有当门之处一张直摆的长桌子空着。
Only a long table set lengthwise by the entrance was empty.

高升便走过去拂拂凳子上的尘土说道：“老爷请这里坐！”
Gao went over, dusted a bench, and said, “Please sit here, sir.”


他是北方人，没有在南方水路上走过，不懂其中的规矩。
He was a northerner who had never travelled the southern waterways and knew nothing of their customs.

王有龄却略微有些知道，
Wang knew a little.

那张桌子叫“马头桌子”，要漕帮里的“尤头”才有资格坐，
That was the “head table,” reserved for leaders of the grain-transport fraternity.

所以慌忙拉住高升：“这里坐不得！”
He hastily caught Gao's arm. “You can't sit there!”


“噢！”高升一愣。
“Oh!” Gao stopped in surprise.


王有龄此时无法跟他细说，同时茶博士也已赶了来招呼他与人拼桌。
There was no time for an explanation, and a tea attendant had already come to find Wang a place at another table.

高升见安顿好了，也就匆匆自去。
Once his master was settled, Gao hurried off.

王有龄喝着茶，便向同桌的人打听消息。
Over his tea, Wang asked his companions for news.


消息坏得很？
The news was dreadful.

自武昌失守，洪杨军有了大小船只一万多艘。
Since the fall of Wuchang, Hong and Yang's army had acquired more than ten thousand vessels of all sizes.

把金银财货，军械粮食，都装了上去，又用了几十万老百姓，沿着长江两岸，长驱而东。
They loaded them with gold, silver, other valuables, weapons, and provisions, and drove eastward along both banks of the Yangtze with hundreds of thousands of civilians.


就这样一直到了广济县的武穴镇，跟两江总督陆建瀛碰上了。
At Wuxue, a town in Guangji county, they encountered Lu Jianying, governor-general of Liangjiang.


湖北不归两江总督所管，
Hubei lay outside his jurisdiction.

陆建瀛是以钦差大臣的身分，出省迎敌。
He had entered the province as an imperial commissioner to meet the enemy.

绿营暮气沉沉，早已不能打仗，新招募的兵又没有多少，
The Green Standard troops were exhausted in spirit and had long since lost their ability to fight, while the newly recruited forces were few.

哪经得住洪杨军如山洪暴发般顺流直冲，以致节节败退。
They could not withstand Hong and Yang's men pouring downstream like a mountain torrent and fell back at every engagement.


这时洪杨军的水师，也由九江，过湖口、彭泽，到了安微省境。
Meanwhile, the rebels' fleet passed Jiujiang, Hukou, and Pengze and entered Anhui.

守小孤山的江苏按察使，弃防而逃，这一下省城安庆的门户洞开。
The judicial commissioner of Jiangsu, defending Xiaogushan, abandoned his position and fled, leaving the approach to Anqing, the provincial capital, wide open.

安徽巡抚蒋文庆只有两干多兵守城，
Governor Jiang Wenqing had only a little over two thousand men to defend the city.

陆建瀛兵败过境，不肯留守，直回江宁。
Lu Jianying passed through after his defeat but refused to remain and went straight back to Jiangning.

蒋文庆看看保不住，把库款、粮食、军人的一部分，侈运庐州，自己坚守危城。
Seeing little hope of holding out, Jiang transferred part of the treasury funds, provisions, and weapons to Luzhou, then stayed to defend the endangered city.

其时城里守卒已经溃散，
By then the garrison had already dispersed.

洪杨军轻而易举地破了城，蒋文庆被杀于抚署西辕门。
Hong and Yang's men took the city easily, and Jiang was killed at the western outer gate of the governor's compound.

这是十天前的事。
This had happened ten days earlier.


“十天前？”王有龄大惊问道：
“Ten days ago?” Wang exclaimed in alarm.

“那么现在‘长毛’到了什么地方了呢？”
“Where are the Long-Haired rebels now, then?”


“这可就不知道了。”那茶客摇摇头，愁容满面的，
“That I don't know.” His fellow customer shook his head, looking deeply troubled.

“芜湖大概总到了。
“They must have reached Wuhu.

说不定已到了江宁。“
Perhaps they're at Jiangning already.”


王有龄大惊失色！
Wang turned pale.

洪杨军用兵能如此神速？
Could the army have moved so fast?

他有点将信将疑。
He found it difficult to believe.

但稍为定一定心来想，亦无足奇，
But when he steadied himself and thought, it was not surprising.

这就是他在旅途中读了许多书的好处，
Here his reading on the journey served him well.

自古以来，长江以上游荆州为重镇，
Since ancient times, Jingzhou had been the strategic stronghold of the upper Yangtze.

上游一失，顺流东下，下游一定不保，
Once the upper reaches were lost, an enemy could sweep downstream, and the lower reaches could scarcely be held.

所以历史上南朝如定都金陵，必遣大将镇荆襄，保上游，
Thus, southern dynasties that made Jinling their capital always stationed able commanders in Jingzhou and Xiangyang to protect the upper river.

而荆襄有变，金陵就如俎上之肉，
If that region changed hands, Jinling was like meat on a chopping board.

此所以桓温在荆州，东晋君臣，寝食难安，而南唐李氏以上游早失，终于为宋太祖所平。
Hence the sleepless anxiety of the Eastern Jin court while Huan Wen controlled Jingzhou, and the eventual conquest of the Southern Tang by the founding Song emperor after its upper-river defences had been lost.


这一下，他对当前的形势得失，立刻便有了一个看法，
He immediately formed a judgement of the present situation.

朝中根本无知将略的人，
No one at court seemed to understand strategy.

置重兵于湖广、河南、防洪杨北上，却忽略了江南的空虚，
They had concentrated troops in Hunan, Hubei, and Henan to prevent the rebels from moving north, while overlooking the weakness of the lower Yangtze region.

这是把他们逼向东南财赋之区，
This had driven the enemy towards the southeast, the country's great source of revenue.

实在是极大的失策。
It was a grave mistake.


照这情形看，金陵迟早不保。
Jinling, it seemed, must fall sooner or later.

他想到何桂清，一颗心猛然往下一沉，
Thinking of He Guiqing, Wang's heart suddenly sank.

随即记起，问桂清不在金陵，
Then he remembered that He was not in Jinling.

抹一抹额上的汗，松口气失声自语：“还好，
Wiping the sweat from his forehead, he breathed out and murmured, “Thank goodness!

还好！”
Thank goodness!”


同桌的茶客抬起忧郁的双眼望着他，
His companions looked up at him with troubled eyes.

他才发觉自己的失态，便陪着笑说，
Realising that he had behaved oddly, he offered an apologetic smile.

“我想起一个好朋友，
“I was thinking of a good friend.

他……”王有龄忽然问道：“请问，学台衙门，可是在江阴？”
He...” Suddenly he asked, “Isn't the education commissioner's office at Jiangyin?”


“我倒不大清楚。”那人答道：
“I'm not sure,” the man replied.

“江苏的大官儿最多，真搞不清什么衙门在什么地方？”
“Jiangsu has so many high officials that I can never remember which office is where.”


“怎么搞不清。”邻桌上有人答话，
“How can you not know?” someone at the next table interjected.

“不错，江苏的大官最多，不过衙门都在好地方。”
“Jiangsu certainly has the most high officials, but all their offices are in fine places.”

他屈着手指数道：“从情江浦开始数好了，
He counted them on his fingers. “Start with Qingjiangpu.

南河总督驻清江浦，漕运总督驻淮安，
The southern river governor-general is here; the director-general of grain transport is at Huai'an.

两江总督、驻防将军、江宁藩司驻江宁，
The governor-general of Liangjiang, the garrison general, and the Jiangning provincial treasurer are at Jiangning.

江苏巡抚、江苏藩司驻苏州，学政驻江阴，两淮盐政驻扬州。”
The governor and provincial treasurer of Jiangsu are at Suzhou; the education commissioner is at Jiangyin; and the Lianghuai salt commissioner is at Yangzhou.”


果然是在江阴。
So Jiangyin it was.

王有龄心里在盘算，
Wang began planning.

由运河到了扬州，不妨沿江东去，到江阴看一看何桂清，然后再经无锡、苏州、嘉兴回杭州，
Once he reached Yangzhou by canal, he could travel east along the river to visit He Guiqing, then return to Hangzhou by way of Wuxi, Suzhou, and Jiaxing.

也还不迟。
There would still be time.


刚刚盘算停当，高升气喘吁吁地寻了来了，
He had just settled the plan when Gao returned, out of breath.

他好不容易才觅着一间房，
After great difficulty, he had found a room.

虽丢了定钱在那里，去迟了保不定又为他人所得，
Though he had left a deposit, someone else might take it if they delayed.

兵荒马乱，无处讲理，所以催着主人快走。
In the confusion of war there would be no one to whom they could appeal, so he urged his master to hurry.


于是王有龄起身付了茶钱，主仆两人走出店来，
Wang paid for the tea, and they went outside.

拦着一名挑伕，把笨重箱笼挑了一担。
They stopped a porter to carry the heavy trunks and cases on a shoulder pole.

高升背了铺盖卷，其余帽笼之类的轻便什物，便由王有龄亲手拿着，
Gao took the bedding roll on his back, while Wang himself carried the lighter things, including the hat case.

急匆匆赶到客店。
They hurried to the inn.

是一间极狭窄的小屋，而且靠近厨房，抽烟弥漫，
The room was tiny and stood beside the kitchen, where cooking smoke filled the air.

根本不宜作为客房。
It was hardly fit for a guest.

Yet when Wang thought of the refugees outside, supporting the old and leading children, not knowing where they could shelter, this little room seemed positively heavenly.


“你呢？”他关切地问高升，
“What about you?” he asked Gao with concern.

“也得找个铺才好。”
“You need somewhere to sleep too.”

“I'll spread my bedding on the floor beside your bed.

也不过天把的事。”
It'll only be a day or so.”


“高升！我想绕到江阴去看一看何大人。”王有龄把他的打算说了出来。
“Gao Sheng, I'd like to make a detour to Jiangyin to see His Excellency He.” Wang explained his plan.


“这个……”高升迟疑地答道：
“Well...” Gao hesitated.

一则要早早禀到，

二则多换两次船，在平常不费事，这几天可是很大的麻烦。
For another, changing boats twice more would be easy enough in ordinary times, but it could be very troublesome now.

老爷，消息很不好，
The news is bad, sir.

万一路断了，怎么办？“
What if the route is cut off?”


高升的见识着实不低，
Gao's judgement was sound.

分发浙江的候补州县，如果归路中断，逗留在江苏，那是一辈子都补不到缺的，
An expectant magistrate assigned to Zhejiang could wait for ever without obtaining a post if he became stranded in Jiangsu.

所以王有龄一听他的放，翻然变计，
Wang changed his mind at once.

当夜商量定规，尽快雇船赶回浙江。
That evening they agreed to hire a boat and reach Zhejiang as quickly as possible.


第二天早晨一看，难民已到了许多，同时也有了确实消息，
By the next morning, many more refugees had arrived, along with definite news.

芜湖已经失守，官军水师大败，福山镇总兵阵亡，

王有龄极细心地听着，
Wang listened closely.

等听到最后一句，随即完全明白，
At the last sentence, he understood everything.

浙江的漕米实在也没有运足，
Zhejiang had not actually delivered its full quota.

万一倪良耀革职查办，那时无所顾忌，将实情和盘托出，
If Ni were dismissed and investigated, he would have nothing left to lose and might reveal all the facts.

黄抚台奏报不实，这一下出的纰漏可就大了。
Huang's inaccurate report would then expose him to serious trouble.


为今之计，除却尽快运米到上海，由海船承兑足额以外，别无善策。
There was only one sound course: get the rice to Shanghai as quickly as possible and have the seagoing vessels accept the full quantity.

他把这番意思说了出来，黄宗汉的脸上没有什么表示。
Wang said so, but Huang showed no reaction.


没有表示就是表示，表示不满！
No reaction was itself a reaction: displeasure.

王有龄心想，除非告诉他，五天或者十天，一定运齐，他是不会满意的。
Wang thought that nothing short of a guarantee to deliver everything within five or ten days would satisfy him.

但自己实在没有这个把握，
Yet he could not honestly offer one.

只能这样答道：“我连夜派员去催，
He could only reply, “I'll send agents out tonight to press the shipments forward.

总之一丝一毫不敢疏忽。”
I shall neglect nothing, however small.”


“也只好这样了。”黄宗汉淡淡地说了这一句，一端茶碗，自己先站起身来，哈一哈腰，往里走去。
“That will have to do.” Huang spoke coolly, lifted his teacup, rose before his guest, gave a slight bow, and went inside.


王有龄大为沮丧。
Wang was deeply discouraged.

接事数天，第一次见抚台，落得这样一个局面，
Only a few days into the job, his first working audience had ended like this.

不但伤心，而且寒心，
He felt not merely hurt but chilled.

黄抚台是这样对部属，实在难伺候。
A superior who treated his subordinates in such a manner would indeed be difficult to serve.


坐在轿子里，闷闷不乐，
Sitting in his chair, he brooded.

前两天初坐大轿，左顾右盼的那份得意心情，已消失无余。
The proud pleasure with which he had looked about on his first rides a few days earlier had vanished.

想着心事自然也不会注意到经过了哪些地方？
Lost in thought, he scarcely noticed where they were passing.

就在这迷惘恍惚之中，蓦地里兜起一个影子，
Then, through his distracted state, a figure suddenly caught his eye.

急忙顿足喊道：“停轿，
Stamping his foot, he shouted, “Stop the chair!

停轿！”
Stop!”


健步如飞的轿班不知怎么回事，
The bearers, moving at a brisk pace, had no idea what was wrong.

拼命煞住脚，还是冲了好几步才能停住。
They tried desperately to halt but carried on for several steps before they could stop.


挟着“护书”跟在轿旁的高升，立即也赶到轿前，
Gao, walking beside the chair with the document folder under his arm, hurried to the front.

只见主人已掀开轿帘，探出头来，睁大了眼回头向来路上望。
His master had already lifted the curtain and thrust out his head, staring wide-eyed back along the street.


这个突如其来的动作，引起了路人的好奇，纷纷住足，
The sudden commotion drew curious passers-by to a halt.

遥遥注视，高升看看有失体统，便轻喊一声：“老爷！”
Seeing them watching from a distance, Gao felt that his master's dignity was at risk and called softly, “Your Honour!”


一见高升，王有龄便说：“快，快，
At the sight of him, Wang said, “Quick!

有个穿墨布夹袍的，快拉住他。”
The man in the black lined robe—stop him!”


穿黑布夹袍的也多得很，
There were plenty of men in black lined robes.

是怎等样一个人呢？
What sort of man did he mean?

高或矮，胖还是瘦，年纪多大，
Tall or short, stout or thin, and how old?

总要略略说明了，才好去找。
Gao needed at least some description before he could search.


他还在踌躇，王有龄已忍不得了，
While he hesitated, Wang lost patience.

拼命拍轿杠，要轿班把它成倒，意思是要跨出轿来自己去追。
He beat on the carrying pole, demanding that the bearers lower the chair so he could leap out and pursue the man himself.

这越发不象样了，
This was still less seemly.

高升连声喊道：“老爷，老爷，体统要紧，
Gao cried repeatedly, “Your Honour, your dignity!

到底是谁？
Who is it?

说了我去找。”
Tell me, and I'll find him.”


还有谁？
“Who else?

胡少爷！“
Young Master Hu!”


“啊！”高升拔脚便奔，
“Ah!” Gao set off at a run.

“胡少爷”是怎么个人，他听主人说过不止一遍，脑中早有了极深的印象。
He had heard his master describe Young Master Hu many times and already had a vivid picture of him in his mind.


一路追，一路细察行人，
He ran along, examining the passers-by.

倒有个穿黑布袍的，却是花白胡须的老者，
One man wore a black robe but had a grey beard.

再有一个已近中年，形容猥琐，
Another was approaching middle age and had an unprepossessing appearance.

看去不象，姑且请问“尊姓”，
He did not seem right either, but Gao asked his surname just in case.

却非姓胡。
It was not Hu.

这时高升有些着急，也不免困惑，
Gao grew anxious and puzzled.

他相信他主人与胡雪岩虽失之交臂，却决不会看错，
He trusted that, though his master had only caught a passing glimpse, he could not have mistaken Hu Xueyan.

然则就此片刻的工夫，会走到哪里去了呢？
Where could the man have gone in these few moments?
