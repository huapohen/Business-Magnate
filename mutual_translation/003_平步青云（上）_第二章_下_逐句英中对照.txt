胡雪岩
《平步青云（上）》第二章（下，至本章结束）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume I — Chapter Two, concluding part
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


“Even so, I cannot help feeling that I do not deserve it.”
“话虽如此，我未免受之有愧。”


“You needn't think that way.
“这不须如此想。

But you must remember Mr Hu, who generously helped you when you had nowhere to turn.
倒是那位在你穷途之际，慷慨援手的胡君，

He was neither a relative nor an old family friend.
别人非亲非故帮你的忙，

He helped because he saw ability in you and believed you could accomplish something.
无非看你是个人才，会有一番事业，

You should keep that in mind.”
你该记着这一点！”


Wang took the advice deeply to heart.
王有龄自然深深受教。

He had never lacked ambition, and these successive strokes of fortune stirred it still further.
他本来就不是没有大志，连番奇遇的鼓舞，越发激起一片雄心，

Whenever he closed his eyes, he saw a future of dazzling promise.
只一闭上眼，便看得前程锦绣，目迷神眩，

Though he had not yet obtained an appointment, he was already savouring the pleasures of office.
虽还未补缺，却已在享受做官的乐趣了。


When he rose the next morning, He Guiqing was waiting with a letter to Huang Zonghan.
第二天早晨起身，何桂清已写好了一封致黄宗汉的信在等他。

It was no ordinary note of introduction; indeed, it scarcely resembled a recommendation at all.
这封信不是泛泛的八行，甚至也不象一封荐信，

Much of it concerned private matters between close friends.
里面谈了许多知交的私话，

Only then did it mention Wang, describing him as “a friend since boyhood, as dear as a brother,” whom He had specifically advised to purchase assignment to Zhejiang so that Huang might nurture his abilities and advance his career.
然后才提到王有龄，说是“总角之交，谊如昆季”，特为嘱他指捐分发浙江，以便请黄宗汉培植造就，

The letter was so earnest and substantial that little was lacking except He's presence in person, hands clasped in appeal.
照这封信的恳切结实来说，就差何桂清当面拱手拜托了。


After Wang had read it and it had been sealed, he asked He to provide someone to accompany him.
等看过封好，王有龄便跟何桂清要人。

He very much wanted Yang Chengfu, but this was a wish his friend could not grant.
以他的意思，很想请杨承福做个帮手，这一点何桂清无法满足他的希望，

Yang was his most capable assistant.
因为杨承福是他最得力的人，

Many official matters and personal connections were fully understood by Yang alone; no one could replace him.
许多公事、关系只有他清楚首尾，非他人所能替代。


“Why not send Gao Sheng with His Honour?” Yang suggested.
“这样吧，”杨承福建议，“叫高升跟了王老爷去，

“That would work very well.”
也很妥当。”


Gao Sheng was honest and capable too, and willing to accompany Wang, so the arrangement was settled.
高升也很诚实能干，他自己也愿意跟王有龄，事情就算定局。

Wang took leave of He Guiqing, thanked Yang, and, under Gao's care, reached the capital that same day.
拜别何桂清，谢了杨承福，由高升照料着，当天就到了京里。

He had intended to stay at the provincial guildhall.
本来想住会馆，

But the current renzi year had brought a special examination, and the following guichou year would bring the regular one.
因为本年王子恩科，明年癸丑正科，

With metropolitan examinations in two successive years, unsuccessful candidates and newly arrived provincial graduates packed the place.
接连两年会试，落第的、新到的举人，挤得满坑满谷，

An empty room was almost impossible to find.
要找一间空房实在很难。

Moreover, Wang was seeking to upgrade a purchased qualification on the basis of his Imperial Academy status.
而且三有龄以监生的底子来加捐，

Beside provincial graduates who might win distinction in the fourth month, he felt that he belonged to a lesser world, and the comparison embarrassed him.
跟那些明年四月便可一举成名的举人在一起，相形之下，仙凡异途，也自觉难堪。


He therefore decided to spend a little more and take a room at an inn in Liangheyan.
便索性破费些，在两河沿找了家客店住。


The weather was bitterly cold.
天气极冷，

Even with a stove burning, the room felt like an ice cellar.
生了炉子还象坐在冰害里，

Gao went out to buy tough paper and flour, then cooked a basin of paste on the stove.
高开上街买了皮纸和面，在炉子上打了一盆浆糊。

Cutting the paper into strips two fingers wide, he sealed every crack in the windows and wooden partitions.
把皮纸裁成两指宽的纸条，把窗户板壁上所有的缝隙都糊没。

Once the northwest wind was shut out, the stove could do its work.
西北风进不来，炉人才能发生作用，

The room soon felt as warm as spring.
立刻满室生春，十分舒服。

After supper, Wang discussed their business with him.
王有龄吃过晚饭，便跟高开商量正事。


“Your Honour, I have an idea.
“老爷，我有个主意，

See what you think of it,” Gao said.
你看使得使不得？”离升说道，

“Tomorrow is the eighth day of the last month.
“明天就是腊八，

In little more than ten days, the government offices will seal up their official seals for the New Year.”
还有十几天工夫就‘封印’了。”


“Ah!” Wang suddenly remembered.
“啊！”一下提醒了王有龄，

“Once the seals are put away, the offices will be closed for a month.
“一‘封印’就是一个月，

If we cannot finish in these ten-odd days, we'll have to spend the New Year here waiting.
这十几天办不成，在京里过年空等，

That would mean a long delay.”
那耽误的工夫就大了。”


“Exactly.
“最啊！

Whichever way you look at it, that would be a poor bargain.
打哪儿来说，都是件划不来的事。

I was thinking that it might be better to spend a little extra and finish the business before the offices close, then set off south before the year ends.”
所以我在想，不如多花几个钱，尽这十几天把事情办妥，赶年里就动身回南。”


“Before the year ends?
“年里就动身？

Wouldn't that be rather hurried?”
不太急了吗？”


“I'm thinking of your interests, sir.
“我是替老爷打算。

If you have few friends in the capital, spending the New Year at an inn won't be much pleasure.
京里如果没有什么熟人，在店里过年，也不是味儿。

Besides, from New Year's Day to the Lantern Festival, you have to spend freely wherever you go.
再说从大年初一到元宵，到哪儿也得大把花钱，

It really isn't worth it.
真正划不来。

Better to spend the holiday on the road.
与其这个样，莫如就在路上过年。

There's another point.” Gao leaned closer.
再有一层，“高升凑近了他说，”

“It would be best to reach Zhejiang and see Governor Huang before His Excellency He arrives, or at about the same time.
老爷最好赶在何大人之前，或者差不多的日子到浙江见黄抚合，

That's when his letter will carry weight.”
何大人的信才管用。“


Wang understood at once and recognised the wisdom of Gao's advice.
王有龄恍然大悟，觉得高升的话，实在有见识。

Huang had a reputation for harshness.
黄宗汉此人既有刻薄的名声，

Once Chunshou's case was settled, he might well discard the man who had helped him and cease to oblige He Guiqing.
保不定在椿寿那件案子结束以后，过河拆桥，不买何桂清的帐。

While He was in Zhejiang investigating the case, Huang would still need his goodwill, and the situation would be different.
如果正是何桂清到浙江查案时，有求于人，情形自然不同。

In any event, arriving sooner rather than later could only be the right course.
总之，宁早勿迟，无论如何不错。


“I'll take your advice.
“我听你的话，

Let's do that.
就这么办。

But do you know how to go about it?”
不过，你可有路子呢？”


“There will be a way.
“路子总有的。

I'll start looking tomorrow,” Gao answered confidently.
明天我就去找。”高升极有把握地说：

“I'll see that it is done well and at a reasonable cost.”
“包管又便宜又好。”


Pleased, Wang opened his trunk and took out the Board's certificate for his original purchased qualification as a salt-station superintendent.
于是王有龄欣然开了箱子，把旧捐的盐大使“部照”取了出来，

He then ground ink, spread out paper, and prepared a statement of his ancestry for three generations and a detailed account of his background.
接着磨墨伸纸开具“三代”，细陈经历，

Once the documents were ready, he explained everything to Gao and gave him twenty taels for the expenses of making the necessary contacts.
把文件都预备妥当，一一交代明白。又取二十两银子交给高升，作为应酬花费。


Gao began his rounds the next day.
从第二天起，高升开始奔走。

The first reports were discouraging: either completion in time could not be guaranteed, or the extra fees demanded were too high.
起初的消息不大好，不是说时间上没有把握，就是额外需索的费用太高。

Three or four days passed.
这样过了三四天，

Wang grew anxious, and even Gao began to lose heart.
不但王有龄心里焦灼，连高升自己也有些气馁了。


Just as they were abandoning hope and preparing to spend the New Year in the capital, matters unexpectedly changed.
就在放弃希望，打算着在京过年时，事情突然有了转机，

A clerk at the Board of Civil Appointments had suffered a fire at home in which a mother and child had died.
吏部有个书办，家里遭了回禄之灾，还烧死了一母一子，

With the year drawing to a close, his household was ruined.
年近岁逼，逢此家破人亡的惨事，

To make matters worse, he himself was in prison over a case.
偏偏这书办又因案下狱，

Desperate and with nowhere else to turn, he had to appeal to his colleagues.
雪上加霜，濒临绝境，必须求援于他的同事们。


Helping meant contributing money if one had it, or effort if one could.
帮忙无非“有饯出钱，有力出力”，

Financial contributions amounted to no more than eight or ten taels apiece; practical assistance proved more substantial.
但出钱的不过十两、八两银子，倒是出力的帮忙得大。

Official business was especially heavy before the New Year.
年下公事特忙，

From departmental officials down to clerks, everyone knew that provincial messengers and men seeking appointments or purchasing qualifications wanted their affairs settled early.
部里从司官到书办，知道各省差官，以及本人来候选捐纳，谋干前程的，都希望提前办理，

Spending the holiday in the capital meant wasted time and additional living costs; missing a deadline on official business might also bring punishment.
在京里过年，赔贴盘缠，空耗辰光还不说，有些限期的公事，耽误了还有处分。

It was therefore an ideal opportunity to obstruct proceedings and extract money from those who could pay.
所以这时是留难需索，择肥而噬的好机会，

To assist their unfortunate colleague, the clerks privately agreed on a standard schedule of charges.
现在为了帮同事的忙，他们私下定了章程，出了“公价”，

Anyone wanting business completed within a fixed time would pay the usual unofficial fees, plus an extra sum according to the circumstances.
凡是想限期办妥的公事，除了照平时的行市纳规费以外，另外看情况加送若干，

The addition would go to the stricken clerk.
多下的钱就归那遭祸的书办所得。

For outsiders, this was much cheaper than trying their luck, seeking one intermediary after another, and being fleeced at every stage.
对外人来说，这比自己去撞木钟，辗转托人，重重剥削要便宜得多。


Gao heard of the arrangement at a brush-and-ink shop in Liulichang.
高升从琉璃厂的笔墨庄里得到了这个消息，

He checked with an acquaintance, confirmed that it was real, and hurried back to tell Wang.
又去找熟人打听，果有其事，匆忙回来说与王有龄。

Through this acquaintance they negotiated a total of 480 taels to upgrade Wang's qualification to an expectant local magistracy with assignment to Zhejiang.
就托那个熟人，代为接洽，说定了价钱，一共四百八十两银子，加捐为候补州县，分发浙江。

Two-thirds represented the official payment; one-third covered miscellaneous expenses.
其中三分之二“正项”，三分之一是“杂费”，

They obtained two bank drafts.
打成两张银票，

Wang paid the official amount himself, while the intermediary delivered the other.
正项自己去缴，杂费托经手人转交，

In only five days, Wang had both a new certificate from the Board and the official receipt for his contribution.
不过五天工夫，就把簇新的一张“部照”和称为“实收”的捐纳交银收据都拿到手了。


The main business was settled, but arranging the long journey south proved troublesome.
这件大事倒办好了，长行回南，却颇费周章。

With the year almost over, carters and boatmen were reluctant to undertake it.
急景调年，车船都不大愿意做此一笔买卖。

Wang consulted Gao again.
王有龄便又跟高升商议，

Now that everything important was done, they could leave whenever they wished; a few days made little difference.
大事已妥，随时可走，也不争在这几天，

Why not wait until after the fifth day of the New Year?
不如过了“破五”再说。

Gao's only concern was his master's interests, and he accepted the decision.
高升原是为主人打算，唯命是从，

They booked two large carts, paid half the fare, and agreed to depart on the seventh day, an auspicious date for a long journey.
当时使先订好了两辆大车，付了一半车价，约定开年初七、宜于长行的黄道吉日动身。


By then, every government office except the Grand Council had closed for the holiday.
这时京里除了军机处，大小衙门，都已封印。

The streets were full of hurried figures: some wore anxious faces as they sought help to get through the New Year; others carried lanterns and pursued debts late into the night.
满街都是匆匆忙忙的行人，有的忧容满面，四处告帮过年，有的提着灯笼，星夜讨债。

Wang, however, was at ease and went sightseeing with Gao every day.
王有龄却是心定神闲，每天由高升领着，到各处去闲逛。

He knew some people in the capital, but there were several reasons not to visit.
他在京里也有些熟人，

Everyone was busy with the holiday, and he did not wish to disturb them.
但一则年节下大家都忙，不便去打搅，

He had few local gifts left, and arriving empty-handed would be discourteous.
二则带的土仪不多，空手登门拜访，于礼不台，

He also felt that his present circumstances were not impressive enough.
三则是他自己觉得现在境况不佳，不如下见，

Better to wait until he had prospered; then recalling old times would bring the real pleasure of friendly hospitality.
等将来得意了，欢然道故，才有人情酬醉之乐。

Apart from brief calls on one or two close relatives, he visited none of his fellow provincials or other friends.
因此，除了极少的一两家至亲，登门一揖以外，其余同乡亲友那里，一概下去。


On New Year's Eve, the guildhall stewards invited him to join their celebration.
到了大年三十，会馆里的执事邀去过年，

After dinner, tables were set up in the hall for various forms of gambling, including the shaking of counters and games of pai gow.
吃完年夜饭，厅上拉开桌子，摇摊的摇摊，推牌九的推牌九，

Wang had no taste for these pursuits and returned early to his inn at Xiheyan.
王有龄不好此道，早早回到了西河沿客店。


He had already given Gao the evening off, so the servant was away.
高升是他事先放了他假的，不在客店。

An inn attendant stirred up the stove and made fresh tea.
伙计替他拨旺了炉人，沏了热茶，

Bored with sitting alone, Wang ordered some wine, but drinking by himself brought no cheer.
枯坐无聊，又弄了酒来喝，无奈“独醉不成欢”，

He considered seeking a woman's company to enliven the festival, then remembered that he was now qualified to become a magistrate, a parent to the people.
有心摘一朵野花，点缀佳节，想想自己已是“父母官”的身分，

He feared Gao would find out and think less of him.
怕让高升发觉了瞧不起。

The Eight Great Lanes were close by, but their fashionable singing houses were notorious places to squander money.
“八大胡同”倒是近在咫尺，但“清吟小班”是有名的销金窝，

After so many strange encounters that year, who knew whether another romance might await him?
这一年异遇甚多，保不定又逢一段奇缘，

If he became entangled in some endless debt of passion, would he not betray the generosity and hopes of both Hu and He?
那一下，五百年前的风流债还不情，岂不辜负了胡、何二人的盛情厚望？


Amid firecrackers sounding throughout the streets, Wang quietly went to bed alone.
在满街爆竹声中，王有龄一个人悄俏地睡下了，

Yet sleep would not come.
却是怎么样也没有睡意。


Looking back over his life, he found sadness and joy, much to reflect on, and still more to hope for.
通前彻后，细思平生，有凄凉，也有欢欣，有感慨，却更多希望。

By failing to take the regular examination route as He Guiqing had done, he had already fallen behind.
他在想，不走何桂清那样的“正途”，已是输人一着，

But that was no reason to admit defeat.
但也不能就此认输，

A man must develop his own strengths.
一个人总要能展其所长，

He might be less learned than He, yet he had grown up beside his father, observing people's livelihoods, becoming familiar with administration, learning to endure hardship, and dealing with all sorts of people.
虽说书读得没有何桂清好，但从小跟在父亲身边，了解民生，熟悉吏治，以及吃苦耐劳，习于交接，

In these things, scholars buried in their books and ignorant of the world could not compare with him.
却不是那班埋首窗下，不通世务的书生可比。

“A thorough understanding of human affairs is itself learning.”
“世事洞明皆学问”，

If he belittled himself and lost his ambition, his abilities would only wither.
妄自匪薄，志气消沉，聪明才智也就灰塞萎缩了。

Now a splendid opportunity had come, at a time when the country faced grave difficulties and an enlightened ruler sought effective government.
于今逢到大好机会，又正当国家多事，明主求治之际，

A practical official seasoned by the world might achieve as much as a scholar in the splendid halls of court.
风尘俗吏的作为，亦未见得会比金马玉堂的学士逊色！


These reflections stirred a powerful determination to accomplish something.
转念到此，顿时浮起一片要做一番事业的雄心壮志。

Yet if he expected great things of himself, he needed more knowledge—not poetry and elegant essays, but learning relevant to the country's administration and the people's welfare.
但以大器自期，觉得肚子里的货色还不够，不是同赋文章，而是于国计民生有关的学问。


Thus, when he visited Liulichang on New Year's Day, while others bought food and amusements, Wang remained in the bookshops like a scholar with a passion for collecting books.
因此年初一那天逛琉璃厂，别人买吃的、玩的，王有时象那些好书成癖的名士一样，只在书铺里坐。

His bearing and clothes were now very different from before.
王有龄此时的气度服饰，已非昔比，

The proprietor was eager to please him: first New Year greetings, then a plate of fruit, and finally polite inquiries about his surname, home province, and examination achievements.
掌柜的十分巴结，先拜了年，摆上果盘，然后请教姓氏、乡里、科名。


“My surname is Wang.
“敝姓王，

I'm from Fujian.
福建，

I was fortunate enough to pass last autumn.”
秋闹刚刚侥幸。”

He spoke as though he were a newly qualified provincial graduate.
王有龄的口气是自表新科举人，

Wang was such a common surname that he felt safe from exposure.
好在“王”是大姓，便冒充了也不怕拆穿。


“Ah, indeed! With good fortune shining in your face, Your Honour is certain to succeed again in the coming metropolitan examination.
“喔，喔”王老爷春风满面，本科一定‘联捷’。

My congratulations in advance!”
预贺，预贺！“


“Thank you.
“ 谢谢。

In the examination hall, literary merit is no guarantee.
‘场中莫论文’，

We must leave it to luck.”
看运气罢了。”


“Your Honour speaks excellent Mandarin.
“王老爷说得好一口官话，

You must have spent many years accompanying your father away from home.”
想来随老太爷在外多年？”


“Yes.”
“是的。”

Further questions might expose him, Wang thought.
王有龄心想，再盘问下去要露马脚了，

He promptly changed the subject. “Have you any good books on practical affairs?”
便即问道：“可有什么实用之学的好书？”


“Certainly.”
“怎么没有？”

The proprietor considered, then took a new set of books from a shelf himself.
那掌柜想了想，自己从书架子取了部新书来，

“Does Your Honour already own this?”
“这部书，不知王老爷有没有？”


It was He Changling's Collected Essays on Statecraft under the Present Dynasty. Wang had long heard of it and replied with pleasure, “I'll take a set.”
一看是贺长龄的《皇朝经世文编》王有龄久闻其名，欣然答道：“我要一部。”


“An excellent work.
“这部书实在好。

Practical learning is much valued nowadays.
当今讲究实学，

Master this book, and your policy essay in the palace examination is bound to be distinguished.”
读熟了这部书，殿试策论一定出色。”


“Have you any books on foreign affairs?”
“有没有‘洋务’上的书？”


“On that subject, there is a work compiled by His Excellency Lin of your own province that you must read.”
“讲洋务，有部贵省林大人编的书，非看不可。”


This was Lin Zexu's Account of the Four Continents, which Wang also bought.
那是林则徐编的《四洲志》，王有龄也买了。

Understanding now what his customer wanted, the proprietor brought out one related work after another until a pile had accumulated.
书店掌柜看出王有龄所要的是些什么书，牵连不断，搬出一大堆来，

There was no time to inspect them all closely, but the prices were generally fair, so Wang accepted them and had them bundled up to take back.
一时也无暇细看内容，好在价钱多还公道，便来者不拒，捆载而旧。


From that day onward, he shut himself in his room to study.
从这天起，王有龄就在客店里“闭户读书”，

He repeatedly read the essays on salt administration, river management, and grain transport in the Collected Essays on Statecraft, unwilling to pass lightly over a single word.
把一部《皇朝经世文编》中，谈盐法、河务、漕运的文章，反复研读，一个字都不肯轻易放过。

Wang listened closely.
王有龄极细心地听着，

等听到最后一句，随即完全明白，

Zhejiang had not actually delivered its full quota.
浙江的漕米实在也没有运足，

If Ni were dismissed and investigated, he would have nothing left to lose and might reveal all the facts.
万一倪良耀革职查办，那时无所顾忌，将实情和盘托出，

Huang's inaccurate report would then expose him to serious trouble.
黄抚台奏报不实，这一下出的纰漏可就大了。

为今之计，除却尽快运米到上海，由海船承兑足额以外，别无善策。

他把这番意思说了出来，黄宗汉的脸上没有什么表示。


No reaction was itself a reaction: displeasure.
没有表示就是表示，表示不满！

Wang thought that nothing short of a guarantee to deliver everything within five or ten days would satisfy him.
王有龄心想，除非告诉他，五天或者十天，一定运齐，他是不会满意的。

但自己实在没有这个把握，

只能这样答道：“我连夜派员去催，

总之一丝一毫不敢疏忽。”


“That will have to do.” Huang spoke coolly, lifted his teacup, rose before his guest, gave a slight bow, and went inside.
“也只好这样了。”黄宗汉淡淡地说了这一句，一端茶碗，自己先站起身来，哈一哈腰，往里走去。


Wang was deeply discouraged.
王有龄大为沮丧。

Only a few days into the job, his first working audience had ended like this.
接事数天，第一次见抚台，落得这样一个局面，

不但伤心，而且寒心，

黄抚台是这样对部属，实在难伺候。

坐在轿子里，闷闷不乐，

前两天初坐大轿，左顾右盼的那份得意心情，已消失无余。

想着心事自然也不会注意到经过了哪些地方？

Then, through his distracted state, a figure suddenly caught his eye.
就在这迷惘恍惚之中，蓦地里兜起一个影子，

急忙顿足喊道：“停轿，

停轿！”

健步如飞的轿班不知怎么回事，

拼命煞住脚，还是冲了好几步才能停住。

挟着“护书”跟在轿旁的高升，立即也赶到轿前，

只见主人已掀开轿帘，探出头来，睁大了眼回头向来路上望。

这个突如其来的动作，引起了路人的好奇，纷纷住足，

遥遥注视，高升看看有失体统，便轻喊一声：“老爷！”

穿黑布夹袍的也多得很，

What sort of man did he mean?
是怎等样一个人呢？

高或矮，胖还是瘦，年纪多大，

Gao needed at least some description before he could search.
总要略略说明了，才好去找。

他还在踌躇，王有龄已忍不得了，

He beat on the carrying pole, demanding that the bearers lower the chair so he could leap out and pursue the man himself.
拼命拍轿杠，要轿班把它成倒，意思是要跨出轿来自己去追。

This was still less seemly.
这越发不象样了，

Gao cried repeatedly, “Your Honour, your dignity!
高升连声喊道：“老爷，老爷，体统要紧，

到底是谁？

说了我去找。”


“Who else?
还有谁？

Young Master Hu!”
胡少爷！“

He had heard his master describe Young Master Hu many times and already had a vivid picture of him in his mind.
“胡少爷”是怎么个人，他听主人说过不止一遍，脑中早有了极深的印象。

一路追，一路细察行人，

倒有个穿黑布袍的，却是花白胡须的老者，

再有一个已近中年，形容猥琐，

看去不象，姑且请问“尊姓”，

It was not Hu.
却非姓胡。

Gao grew anxious and puzzled.
这时高升有些着急，也不免困惑，

He trusted that, though his master had only caught a passing glimpse, he could not have mistaken Hu Xueyan.
他相信他主人与胡雪岩虽失之交臂，却决不会看错，

Where could the man have gone in these few moments?
然则就此片刻的工夫，会走到哪里去了呢？
