胡雪岩
《平步青云（上）》第三章（上）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume I — Chapter Three, first part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第三章｜CHAPTER THREE


正徘徊瞻顾，不知何以为计时，突然眼前一亮，
As Gao hesitated, looking this way and that and wondering what to do, he suddenly spotted the man.

那个在吃“门板饭”的，一定是了。
The fellow eating at the improvised counter must be him.

杭州的饭店，犹有两宋的遗风，
Hangzhou's eating houses still preserved customs from the Song dynasties.

楼上雅坐，楼下卖各样熟食，卸下排门当案板，摆满了朱漆大盘，盛着现成菜肴，
Upstairs were comfortable private seats; downstairs, cooked dishes were sold from large red-lacquered platters laid out on doors taken from their frames and used as counters.

另有长条凳，横置案前，贩夫走卒，杂然并坐，称为吃“门板饭”。
Long benches stood in front, where pedlars and labourers sat together for what was called a “door-plank meal.”

一碗饭盛来，象座塔似地堆得老高，
A bowl of rice arrived heaped as high as a little pagoda.

不是吃惯了的，无法下箸，不知从顶上吃起，还是从中腰吃起？
Anyone unused to it hardly knew where to put his chopsticks: should he begin at the summit or halfway down?

所以那些“穿短打”的一见这位“寄大衫儿的”落座，都不免注目，
The men in short working clothes therefore stared when this customer in a long gown sat down.

一则是觉得衣冠中人来吃“门板饭”，事所罕见，二则是要看他如何吃法？
A gentleman dining in such a place was unusual enough; more than that, they wanted to see how he would manage the rice.

不会吃，“塔尖”会倒下来，
If he did not know the trick, the top would collapse.

大家在等着看他的笑话。
They were waiting for the joke.


就在这时，高升已经赶到，
Gao had reached him by then.

侧面端详，十有八九不错，便冒叫一声：“胡少爷！”
Studying his profile, he felt almost certain and ventured a call: “Young Master Hu!”


这一声叫，那班“穿短打的”都笑了，
The men in working clothes laughed.

哪有少爷来吃门板饭的？
What young master would come here for a door-plank meal?


高升到杭州虽不久，对这些情形已大致明白，
Though new to Hangzhou, Gao had already learned something of local ways.

自己也觉得“胡少爷”叫得不妥，
He realised that his form of address was ill chosen.

真的是他，他也不便答应，
Even if this was Hu, he might be embarrassed to answer.

于是走到他身边问道，“请问，贵姓可是胡？”
Gao therefore approached and asked, “Excuse me, is your surname Hu?”


“不错。
“Yes.

怎地？”
What of it?”


“台甫可是上雪下岩？”
“And is your courtesy name Xueyan?”


正是胡雪岩，
It was indeed Hu Xueyan.

他把刚拈起的竹箸放下，问道：“我是胡雪岩。从未见过尊驾……”
Setting down the bamboo chopsticks he had just picked up, he said, “I am Hu Xueyan, but I don't believe we've met...”


高升看他衣服黯旧，于思满面，知道这位“胡少爷”落魄了，才去吃门板饭。
His faded clothes and unshaven face told Gao that Young Master Hu had fallen on hard times, which explained his choice of eating place.

如果当街相认传出去是件新闻，对自己老爷的官声，不大好听，
A public reunion in the street would make a story, and might not sound well for his master's official reputation.

所以此时不肯说破王有龄的姓名，只说：
He therefore avoided naming Wang Youling.

“敝上姓王，
“My master's surname is Wang.

一见就知道。
You'll know him as soon as you see him.

胡少爷不必在这里吃饭了，
There's no need to eat here, Young Master Hu.

我陪了你去看敝上。”
Let me take you to him.”


说罢不问青红皂白，一手摸一把铜钱放在案板上，一手便去搀扶胡雪岩，跨出条凳，
Without waiting for an answer, he put a handful of copper coins on the counter with one hand and helped Hu step over the bench with the other.

接着便招一招手，唤来一顶待雇的小轿。
Then he beckoned to a small hired sedan chair waiting nearby.


胡雪岩有些摸不着头脑，不肯上轿，
Hu was bewildered and refused to get in.

拉住高升问道：“贵上是哪一位？”
Catching Gao's arm, he asked, “Which Mr Wang is your master?”


“是……”
“He is...”

高升放低了声音说：
Gao lowered his voice.

“我家老爷的官印，上有下龄。”
“His official name is Youling.”


“啊！”
“Ah!”

胡雪岩顿时眼睛发亮：
Hu's eyes lit up.

“是他。
“It's him!

现在在哪里？”
Where is he now?”


“公馆在清和坊。
“His residence is in Qinghefang.

胡少爷请上轿。”
Please get into the chair.”


等他上了轿，高升说明地址，
Once Hu was seated, Gao gave the bearers the address.

等小轿一抬走，他又赶了去见王有龄，略略说明经过。
As soon as the little chair moved away, he hurried back to Wang and briefly explained what had happened.

王有龄欢喜无量，
Wang was overjoyed.

也上了蓝呢大轿，催轿班快走。
He too climbed into his blue-cloth chair and urged the bearers to hurry.


一前一后，几乎同时抬到王家，
The two chairs reached the Wang residence almost together.

高升先一步赶到，叫人开了中门，两顶轿子，一起抬到厅前。
Gao had run ahead and ordered the central gates opened, so both were carried straight to the hall.

彼此下轿相见，都有疑在梦中的感觉，
When the friends stepped out and faced each other, each felt as though he were dreaming.

尤其是王有龄，看到胡雪岩穷途末路的神情，
Wang in particular was overwhelmed by the sight of Hu's poverty and desperation.

鼻子发酸，双眼发热。
His nose stung and his eyes grew hot.


“雪岩！”
“Xueyan!”


“雪轩！”
“Xuexuan!”


两个人这样招呼过，却又没有话了，
After those greetings, neither could speak.

彼此都有无数话梗塞在喉头，还有无数话积压在心头，但嘴只有一张，不知先说哪一句好？
Countless things crowded their throats and weighed upon their hearts, but with only one mouth apiece, neither knew what to say first.


一旁的高升不能不开口了：
Gao, standing beside them, had to intervene.

“请老爷陪着胡少爷到客厅坐！”
“Perhaps Your Honour would take Young Master Hu into the reception room.”


“啊！”
“Ah!”

王有龄这才省悟，
Wang came to himself.

“来，来！
“Come, Xueyan.

雪岩且先坐下歇一歇再说。
Sit down and rest before we talk.

也不必在外面了，
We needn't stay out here.

请到后面去，舒服些。”
Let's go to the back, where it's more comfortable.”


一引引到后堂，
He led Hu into the rear hall.

躲在屏风后面张望的王太太，慌忙回避。
Mrs Wang, who had been watching from behind a screen, hurried away.

胡雪岩瞥见裙幅飘动，也有些踌躇。
Catching sight of her moving skirt, Hu hesitated.

这下又提醒了王有龄。
This reminded Wang of something else.


“太太！”他高声喊道，
“My dear!” he called loudly.

“见见我这位兄弟！”
“Come and meet this brother of mine.”


这样的交情，比通家之好更进一层，真个如手足一样，
Their bond went beyond ordinary family friendship; they were truly like brothers.

王太太便很大方地走了出来，
Mrs Wang therefore came out without embarrassment.

含着笑，指着胡雪岩，却望着她丈夫问：
Smiling, she pointed to Hu but spoke to her husband.

“这位就是你日思夜梦的胡少爷了！”
“So this is the Young Master Hu you've been thinking of by day and dreaming of by night!”


“不敢当这个称呼！”
“I hardly deserve that title.”

胡雪岩一躬到地。
Hu bowed deeply.


王太太还了礼，很感动地说：
She returned the bow and spoke with feeling.

“胡少爷！真正不知怎么感激你？
“Young Master Hu, we really don't know how to thank you.

雪轩一回杭州，就去看你，
As soon as Xuexuan came back to Hangzhou, he went to look for you.

扑个空回来，长吁短叹，不知如何是好？
He returned without finding you and sighed and fretted, not knowing what to do.

我埋怨雪轩，这么好的朋友，哪有不请教人家府上在哪里的道理？
I blamed him: how could anyone have so good a friend without asking where he lived?

如今好了，
Now all is well.

是在哪里遇见的？”
Where did you meet?”


“在，在路上。”
“In... in the street.”

胡雪岩有些窘。
Hu looked embarrassed.


王存龄的由意外凉喜所引起的激动，这时已稍稍平伏，
Wang's first rush of emotion had subsided a little.

催着他妻子说：
He hurried his wife along.

“太太！我们的话，三天三夜说不完，
“My dear, we couldn't finish all we have to say in three days and nights.

你此刻先别问，
Leave the questions for now.

我们都还没有吃饭，
Neither of us has eaten.

看看，有现成的，先端几个碟子来喝酒。”
See what is ready and bring a few dishes so we can have a drink.”


“有，有。”
“Certainly.”

王太太笑着答道，
She smiled.

“请胡少爷上书房去吧，
“Take Young Master Hu to the study.

那里清静。”
It's quiet there.”


“对了！”
“Just so!”


王有龄又把胡雪岩引到书房，
Wang led Hu to the study.

接着王太太便带着丫头、老妈子，亲来照料。
His wife soon followed with the maid and older servant to look after them herself.

胡雪岩享受着这一份人情温暖，顿觉这大半年来的飘泊无依之苦，受得也还值得。
Surrounded by such warmth, Hu felt that even the hardship and insecurity of the past half-year had been worth enduring.


“雪轩！”他问，“你几时回来的？”
“Xuexuan,” he asked, “when did you return?”


“回来还不到一个月。”
“Less than a month ago.”

王有龄对自己心满意足，但看到胡雪岩却有些伤心，
Wang was thoroughly content with his own circumstances, but the sight of Hu saddened him.

“雪岩，你怎么弄成这样子？”
“Xueyan, how did you come to this?”


“说来话长。”
“It's a long story.”

胡雪岩欲言又止地，
Hu hesitated.

“你呢？
“What about you?

我看很得意？”
You seem to have done very well.”


“那还不是靠你。
“And whom have I to thank but you?

连番奇遇，
I've had one extraordinary encounter after another.

什么《今古奇观》上的‘倒运汉巧遇洞庭红’，比起我来，都算不了什么！”
That tale in Strange Stories Old and New about the unlucky man whose fortune turns through Dongting oranges is nothing beside my experience.”

王有龄略停一停，大声又说，“好了！反正只要找到了你就好办了。
He paused, then exclaimed, “Well, now that I've found you, everything can be put right.

来，来，
Come!

今天不醉不休。“
Today we drink until we're drunk.”


另一面方桌上已摆下四个碟子，两副杯筷，
Four dishes and two sets of cups and chopsticks had been laid on a square table nearby.

等他们坐下，王太太亲自用块手中，裹着一把酒壶来替他们斟酒。
When they sat down, Mrs Wang wrapped a cloth around the wine pot and poured for them herself.

胡雪岩便慌忙逊谢。
Hu hurriedly protested at the honour.


“太太！”王有龄说，“你敬了兄弟的酒，就请到厨房里去吧，
“My dear,” Wang said, “drink a toast to my brother, then go and attend to the kitchen.

免得兄弟多礼，反而拘束。”
Otherwise he'll feel obliged to keep standing on ceremony and won't be at ease.”


于是王太太向胡雪岩敬过酒，退了出去，留下一个丫头侍候。
She toasted Hu and withdrew, leaving a maid to serve them.


于是一面吃，一面说，王有龄自通州遇见何桂清开始，一直谈到奉委海运局坐办，其间也补叙了他自己的家世。
As they ate, Wang told his story, from meeting He Guiqing in Tongzhou to his appointment at the Sea Transport Bureau, filling in his own family background along the way.

所以这一席话谈得酒都凉了。
By the time he finished, the wine had gone cold.


“恭喜，恭喜！”
“Congratulations!”

胡雪岩此时已喝得满面红光，那副倒霉相消失得无形无踪，
Hu's face was flushed with drink, and every trace of his defeated appearance had vanished.

很得意地笑道：
He smiled proudly.

“还是我的眼光不错，
“My judgement was right after all.

看出你到了脱运交运的当儿，
I could see that your bad luck was ending and your good fortune about to begin.

果不其然。”
And so it did.”


“交运也者，是遇见了你。
“My good fortune began when I met you.”

雪岩，”王有龄愧歉不安地说，“无怪乎内人说我湖涂，
Wang looked ashamed. “Xueyan, no wonder my wife calls me muddle-headed.

受你的大恩，竟连府上在哪里都不知道。
You did me such an immense kindness, yet I never even learned where you lived.

今天，你可得好好儿跟我说一说了。”
Today you must tell me all about yourself.”


“自然要跟你说。”
“Of course I shall.”

胡雪岩喝口酒，大马金刀地把双手撑在桌角，微偏着头问他：
Hu drank, planted both hands squarely on the corners of the table, and tilted his head slightly.

“雪轩，你看我是何等样人？”
“Xuexuan, what sort of man do you take me for?”


王有龄看他的气度，再想一想以前茶店里所得的印象，认为他必是个官宦人家的了弟，但不免有些甘于下流，所以不好好读书，成天在茶店里厮混。
Considering his manner and recalling his earlier impressions in the teahouse, Wang supposed that Hu must be the son of an official family, but one who had lowered his ambitions and preferred idling in teahouses to serious study.


当然，这“甘于下流”四字，他是不能出口的，
Naturally, he could not voice that judgement so bluntly.

便这样答道：“兄弟，我说句话，你别生气。
“Brother, don't be offended,” he said.

我看你象个纨袴.”
“I took you for a well-born young idler.”


“纨袴？”胡雪岩笑了，
“An idler?” Hu laughed.

“你倒不说我是‘撩鬼儿’！”
“You might have called me a liaogui'er instead!”

这是杭州话，地痞无赖叫“撩鬼儿”。
That was the Hangzhou expression for a street loafer or local ruffian.


“那我就猜不到了。
“Then I can't guess.

请你实说了吧，
Tell me the truth.

我心里急得很！”
I'm terribly anxious to know.”


“那就告诉你，
“Very well.

我在钱庄里‘学生意’……”
I learned my trade in a private bank...”


胡雪岩父死家贫，从小就在钱庄里当学徒，
Hu's father had died, leaving the family poor, and he had entered a private bank as an apprentice while still a boy.

杭州人称为“学生子”，从扫地倒溺壶开始，
In Hangzhou, such apprentices were called “trade students.” He began with sweeping floors and emptying chamber pots.

由于他绝顶聪明，善于识人，而且能言善道，手面大方，
But he was extraordinarily quick, a shrewd judge of people, persuasive in speech, and generous in his dealings.

所以三年满师，立刻便成了那家钱庄一名得力的伙计，
When his three-year apprenticeship ended, he immediately became one of the bank's most useful employees.

起先是“立柜台”，
At first he worked at the counter.

以后获得东家和“大伙”的信任，派出去收帐，
Later, having won the confidence of both proprietor and manager, he was sent out to collect debts.

从来不曾出过纰漏。
He had never made a mistake.


前一年夏天跟王有龄攀谈，知道他是一名候补盐大使，打算着想北上“投供”、加捐时，胡雪岩刚有笔款子可收。
The previous summer, when he learned from Wang that he held a salt-station superintendent's qualification and wanted to go north to register and purchase a higher one, Hu happened to have a debt to collect.

这笔款子正好五百两，
It was exactly five hundred taels.

原是吃了“倒帐”的，在钱庄来说，已经认赔出帐，
The account had gone bad, and the bank had already accepted the loss and written it off.

如果能够收到，完全是意外收入。
Any recovery would be an unexpected gain.


但是，这笔钱在别人收不到，
No one else could collect it.

欠债的人有个绿营的营官撑腰，他要不还，钱庄怕麻烦，也不敢惹他。
The debtor had the backing of a Green Standard officer, and if he refused to pay, the bank preferred to avoid trouble rather than challenge him.

不过此人跟胡雪岩很谈得来，
But he and Hu got on well.

不知怎么发了笔财，让胡雪岩打听到了去找他，
Hu heard that the man had somehow come into money and went to see him.

他表示别人来不行，胡雪岩来另当别论，
The debtor said that anyone else would have been refused, but Hu was a different matter.

很慷慨地约期归清。
He readily fixed a date to settle the debt in full.


胡雪岩一念怜才，决定拉王有龄一把。
Moved by sympathy for Wang's wasted abilities, Hu decided to help him.

他想，反正这笔款子在钱庄已经无法收回，如今转借了给王有龄，
Since the bank had already treated the money as irrecoverable, he reasoned, he could lend it on to Wang.

将来能还最好，
If Wang repaid it later, all the better.

不能还，钱庄也没有损失。
If not, the bank would be no worse off than before.


这个想法也不能说没有道理，
There was a certain logic in the argument.

悄悄儿做了，人不知，鬼不觉，一时也不会有人去查问这件事。
Had he acted quietly, nobody would have known, and nobody would have thought to inquire for some time.

坏就坏在他和盘托出，
The trouble was that he disclosed everything.

而且自己写了一张王有龄出面的借据送到总管店务的“大伙”那里。
He even drew up a promissory note in Wang's name and delivered it to the manager responsible for the bank's business.


“大伙”受东家的委托，如何能容胡雪岩这种“一厢情愿”的想法，
Entrusted with the proprietor's affairs, how could the manager accept such unilateral reasoning?

念在他平日有功，也不追保，请他卷了铺盖。
In view of Hu's past service, he did not pursue Hu's guarantor, but he told him to pack his bedding and leave.

这一下在同行中传了出去，
Word spread through the trade.

都说他胆大妄为，
Everyone said Hu was recklessly presumptuous.

现在幸亏是五百两，
Luckily, this had been only five hundred taels.

如果是五千两、五万两，
What if it had been five thousand or fifty thousand?

他也这样擅作主张，岂不把一爿店都弄“倒灶”了？
If he took such liberties, might he not ruin an entire firm?


为了这个名声在外，同业间虽知他是一把好手，却谁也不敢用他。
With that reputation, no bank would employ him, although everyone knew his abilities.

同时又有人怀疑他平日好赌，或许是在赌博上失利，无以为计，饰词挪用了这笔款子。
Some also suspected that, given his fondness for gambling, he had lost money and invented the story to cover its misappropriation.

这个恶名一传，生路就越加困难了。
Once this further accusation spread, earning a living became harder still.


“谢天谢地，”胡雪岩讲到这里，如释重负似地说，“你总算回来了！
“Thank heaven you've finally returned,” Hu said, as though relieved of a heavy burden.

不管那笔款子怎么样，以你现在的身分，先可以把我的不白之冤，洗刷干净。“
“Whatever happens about the money, your present position will at least enable me to clear my name.”


润湿的双眼的王有龄，长长叹了口气：
With moist eyes, Wang sighed deeply.

“唉，如果你我没有今天的相遇，谁会想得到我冥冥中已经害得你好惨。
“If we hadn't met again today, who would have imagined that I had unknowingly caused you so much suffering?

如今，大恩不言谢，
Such kindness is beyond thanks.

你看我该怎么办？”
Tell me what I should do.”


“这要看你。
“That's for you to decide.

我如何能说？”
How can I say?”


“不，不！”
“No, no!”

王有龄发觉自己措词不妥，赶紧抢着说道，
Realising that he had expressed himself badly, Wang hurried to explain.

“我不是这意思，
“That's not what I mean.

我是说，你的事就是我的事。
Your affairs are mine.

怎么样把面子十足挣回来，这我有办法，
I know how to restore your reputation fully.

现在要问你的是，你今后作何打算？
What I want to ask is what you intend to do next.

是不是想回原来的那家钱庄？”
Would you like to return to the bank?”


胡雪岩摇摇头，说了句杭州的俗语：“ ‘回汤豆腐干’，没有味道了。”
Hu shook his head and quoted a Hangzhou saying: “Dried bean curd put back in the broth has lost its flavour.”


“那么，是想自立门户？”
“Then do you want to set up on your own?”


这句话说到了他心里，
That was exactly what Hu wanted.

但就在要开口承认时，忽然转念，
But as he was about to admit it, another thought stopped him.

开一家钱庄不是轻而易举的事，
Opening a bank was no simple matter.

要本钱也要有人照应。
It required capital and powerful support.

王有龄现在刚刚得了个差使，力量还有限，
Wang had only just obtained an appointment; his resources were still limited.

如果自己承认有此念头，看他做人极讲义气，为了感恩图报，一定想尽办法来帮自己，
If Hu declared his ambition, Wang's loyalty and gratitude would compel him to do everything possible to help.

They were embarrassed to have their superior refer to the gambling.

周委员红着脸答道：
Zhou blushed.

“Fortune favoured us, thanks to Your Excellency.”

“A pleasure to contribute, a pleasure.”


说笑了一会，阿珠来摆桌子开饭。
After some joking, A'zhu laid the table for dinner.

“无锡快”上的“船菜”是有名的，这天又特别巴结，自然更精致了。
The cooking aboard the fast Wuxi boats was famous, and special efforts had been made that day, so the food was finer still.


除此以外，各人都还带得有“路菜”，
Everyone had also brought provisions for the journey.

桌子上摆不下，另外端两张茶几来摆。
There was not enough room on the main table, and two side tables had to be added.

胡雪岩早关照庶务多带陈年“竹叶青”，
Hu had instructed the general affairs officer to bring plenty of aged Zhuyeqing wine.

此时开了一坛，烫得恰到好处，
A jar was opened and warmed to precisely the right temperature.

斟在杯子里，糟香四溢，
Its rich aroma filled the air as it was poured.

连一向不善饮的周委员，都忍不住想来一杯。
Even Zhou, ordinarily a poor drinker, could not resist a cup.


这样的场合，再有活色生香的阿珠侍席，应该是淳于髡所说的“饮可八斗”的境界，
With such a meal and the lively, attractive A'zhu serving them, it should have been an occasion on which, as the ancient wit Chunyu Kun said, a man could drink an astonishing amount.

无奈有王有龄在座，大家便都拘束了，
Unfortunately, Wang's presence made everyone restrained.

他谈话的对象也只是一个吴委员，
He conversed only with Wu.

这天下午倚舷平眺，做了四首七绝，题名《春望》，十分得意，
That afternoon, gazing from his boat, he had composed four seven-character quatrains entitled “Spring Views” and was very proud of them.

此时兴高采烈地跟吴委员谈论，什么“这个字不响”，“那个字该用去声”，
Now he enthusiastically discussed which word lacked resonance and which should carry a departing tone.

大家听不大懂，也没有兴致去听，但礼貌上又非装得很喜欢听不可的样子，
The others understood little and cared less, yet courtesy required them to look fascinated.

以致于变成喝闷酒，
They drank in gloomy silence.

嘉肴醇醒，淡而无味，可餐的秀色，亦平白地糟蹋了，
Fine food and mellow wine lost their flavour; even the girl's charms were wasted.

真是耳朵受罪，还连带了眼睛受屈！
Their ears suffered, and their eyes were deprived as well.

Hu saw that things were going wrong.

一番细心安排，都叫王有龄的酸气给冲掉了。
His careful arrangements were being spoiled by Wang's pedantry.

好在有约在先，此行凡事得听他作主，
Fortunately, they had agreed that Hu should direct this journey.

所以他找了个空隙，丢过去一个眼色，意思请他早些回自己的船，好让大家自由些。
Seizing a pause, he gave Wang a glance, asking him to return early to his own boat and leave the others free to enjoy themselves.


王有龄倒是酒酣耳热，谈得正痛快，所以对胡雪岩的暗示，起初还不能领会，
Warm with wine and delighted by his conversation, Wang at first missed the hint.

看一看大家的神态，再细一想，方始明白，心头随即浮起歉意。
Then he examined the others' expressions, thought a little, understood, and felt apologetic.


“我的酒差不多了！”他也很机警，

He asked A'zhu for a small half-bowl of rice, ate it, and left the table.

胡雪岩知道他的酒不曾够，特地关照船家，另外备四个碟子，烫一斤酒送到前面船上。
Hu knew that he had not really finished drinking, so he specially asked the boatman to prepare four dishes and warm a jin of wine to send to the leading boat.
