胡雪岩
《平步青云（上）》第三章（下，至本章结束）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume I — Chapter Three, concluding part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


“好了！”周委员挺一挺腰说，
“There!” Zhou said, straightening his back.

“这下可以好好喝两杯了。”
“Now we can enjoy a proper drink.”

略略清理了席面，洗盏更酌，
The table was tidied, the cups rinsed, and more wine poured.

人依旧是五个，去了一个王有龄，补上一个庶务，他姓赵，人很能干，不过，这几天的工夫，已经让胡雪岩收服了。
There were still five at dinner: Wang had been replaced by Zhao, the general affairs officer, a capable man whom Hu had already won over in these few days.

“行个酒令，如何？”吴委贝提议。
“How about a drinking game?” Wu suggested.

“我只会豁拳。”张胖子说。
“I only know finger-guessing,” Zhang said.

“豁拳我倒会。”周委员接口，
“I can play that too,” Zhou put in.

“就不会喝酒。”
“It's the drinking I can't manage.”

“不要紧，
“Never mind.

我找个人来代。”胡雪岩便喊：“阿珠，你替周老爷代酒。”
I'll find someone to drink for you.” Hu called out, “A'zhu, drink Mr Zhou's forfeits for him.”

“嗯。”
“Oh!”

阿珠马上把个嘴撅得老高，上身摇两摇，就象小女孩似地撒娇。
A'zhu immediately pushed out her lower lip and swayed her shoulders in a playful pout, like a little girl.

“好，好！”
“All right, all right!”

胡雪岩也是哄小孩似地哄她，
Hu coaxed her as though soothing a child.

“不代，不代！”
“You needn't, you needn't!”

阿珠嫣然一笔，自己觉得不好意思了：
She smiled prettily, then felt a little embarrassed.

“这样，
“How about this?

周老爷吃一杯，我代一杯！”
For every cup Mr Zhou drinks, I'll drink one for him.”

“如果周老爷吃十杯呢？”赵庶务问。
“And if he drinks ten?” Zhao asked.

阿珠想了想，毅然答道：“我也吃十杯。”
She considered, then declared firmly, “I'll drink ten too.”


大家都鼓掌称善，
Everyone applauded.

周委员便笑着摇手：
Zhou laughed and waved his hands.

“不行，不行！
“No, no!

你们这是存心灌我酒。”
You're deliberately trying to get me drunk.”

说着便要逃席。赵庶务和阿珠，一面一个拉住了他，
He made to leave the table, but Zhao and A'zhu caught him, one on either side.

吴委员很威严地说：“我是令官，
Wu declared sternly, “I'm master of the drinking game.

酒令大似军令，
Its rules are stricter than military orders.

周公乱了我的令，先罚酒一杯！”！
Mr Zhou has disobeyed me: one penalty cup to begin with!”

“我替他计个饶。”胡雪岩说。
“Let me plead for him,” Hu said.

“不行！
“No!

除非阿珠来求情。”
Unless A'zhu does the pleading.”

“呀！吴老爷真正在说笑话了！”阿珠笑道：
“Oh, Mr Wu, you really are joking!” she said with a laugh.

“这关我什么事啊？”
“What has this to do with me?”

“你不是替他代酒吗？
“Aren't you drinking for him?

既会你跟周老爷好，为什么不可以替他求情呢？”
Since you're so fond of Mr Zhou, why shouldn't you plead for him too?”

这算是哪一方的道理？
What sort of reasoning was that?

阿珠让他缠糊除了，
He had talked her into a muddle.

虽知他的话不对，却无法驳他。
She knew his argument was wrong, but could not refute it.

不过，说她跟周老爷“好”，她却不肯承认。
Still, she would not admit to being particularly fond of Zhou.


“我伺候各位老爷都是一样的，
“I serve all you gentlemen alike.

要好大家都好……”
If I'm fond of one, I'm fond of all...”

下面那半句话不能再出口，偏偏张胖子促狭，故意要拆穿：
She could not very well finish the thought, but the mischievous Zhang deliberately exposed it.

“要不好大家都不好，是不是？”
“And if you dislike one, you dislike us all—is that it?”

“啊呀呀！
“Oh, no, no!

不作兴这样子说的。”
You mustn't say that.”

阿珠有些窘，面泛红晕，越发妩媚，
A'zhu looked flustered, her blush making her more charming still.

“各位老爷都好，只有一位不好。”
“All the gentlemen are good, except one.”

“哪一个？”
“Which one?”

“就是你张老板！”阿珠说了这一句，自己倒又笑了，接着把腰肢一扭，到船梢上去取热酒。
“You, Mr Zhang!” She laughed at her own retort, then turned with a swing of her waist and went to the stern for hot wine.

取来热洒，吴委员开始打通关。
When she returned, Wu began challenging each man in turn.

个个逸兴遗飞，加以有阿珠如蛱蝶穿花般，周旋在席间，周、吴二人乐不可支，
They were all in high spirits, and with A'zhu moving among them like a butterfly among flowers, Zhou and Wu were beside themselves with pleasure.

欢饮大醉。
They drank until thoroughly drunk.

就这样天天打牌饮酒，跟阿珠调笑，
So the days passed in cards, wine, and flirtation with A'zhu.

船走得极慢，但船中的客人还嫌快！
The boats moved very slowly, but their passengers thought them too fast.


第四天才到嘉兴，
They did not reach Jiaxing until the fourth day.

吴委员向胡雪岩暗示，连日在船上，气闷之至，想到岸上走走。
Wu hinted to Hu that several days aboard had become unbearably confining: he would like a walk ashore.

这是托词，
That was an excuse.

实在是想多停留一天。
He really wanted another day's delay.

胡雪岩自然明白，便跟王有龄说了，在嘉兴停一天。
Hu understood perfectly, spoke to Wang, and arranged to stay a day in Jiaxing.

既到嘉兴，不能不逛南湖，
A visit to Jiaxing necessarily meant an excursion to South Lake.

连王有龄一起，在烟雨楼头品茗。
Wang joined them for tea upstairs in the Tower of Mist and Rain.

那天恰好是个阴天，春阴漠漠，柳色迷离，
The day happened to be overcast; spring haze hung over the water, and the willows blurred in the dim light.

王有龄的诗兴又发了。
Wang felt another poem coming on.

张胖子却坐不住，
Zhang, however, could not sit still.

“找只船去划划？”他提议。
“Shall we hire a boat and row about?” he suggested.

“何必？”
“What for?”

吴委员反对，
Wu objected.

“一路来都是坐船，也坐腻了。
“We've been sitting in boats all the way and have had enough of it.

坐这里的船，倒不如坐自家的船。”
If we're going boating, we might as well use our own.”

自家的船上有阿珠，
Their own boat had A'zhu.

南湖的船上也有不少船娘，但未见得胜过阿珠，就算胜得过，片时邂逅，也没有什么主意好打。
South Lake had plenty of boatwomen too, but none necessarily surpassed her; and even if one did, so brief an encounter offered little prospect of anything further.


“我倒有个主意了。”
“I have an idea!”

张胖子失声说了这一句，
Zhang blurted out.

发觉王有龄在注意，不便再说，悄悄把胡雪岩一拉，到一旁去密语。
Then, noticing Wang's attention, he stopped, drew Hu aside, and spoke privately.

张胖子是想去访“空门艳迹”，
He wanted to seek out the amorous diversions concealed behind monastic doors.

嘉兴有些玷辱佛门的花样，胡雪岩也知道，但王有龄的身分不便去，
Hu too knew of certain practices in Jiaxing that disgraced the Buddhist establishment, but Wang's official position made such a visit unsuitable.

当时商定，张胖子带周、吴去结“欢喜缘”，胡雪岩陪着王有龄去闲逛。
They agreed that Zhang should take Zhou and Wu to form these “blissful connections,” while Hu accompanied Wang on a stroll.

于是分道扬镳，
They therefore separated.

胡雪岩掉了个花枪，陪着王有龄先走，
With a little manoeuvring, Hu took Wang away first.

两顶小轿到了闹市，下轿浏览，信步走进一家书坊。
Two small sedan chairs carried them to the busy streets, where they got out to browse and wandered into a bookshop.

王有龄想买部诗集子，
Wang wanted a collection of poems.

胡雪岩随手翻着新到的京报，看见一道上谕，上有黄宗汉的名字，便定睛看了下去。
Hu idly leafed through a newly arrived Peking Gazette and, noticing Huang Zonghan's name in an imperial edict, read more closely.

上面除了黄宗汉奏复椿寿自尽原因的原折，说“该司因库款不敷，漕务棘手，致肝疾举发，因而自尽，
The entry included Huang's memorial in reply to the inquiry into Chunshou's suicide: “The treasurer, distressed by the shortage of treasury funds and the difficulties of grain transport, suffered a recurrence of liver illness and consequently took his own life.

并无别情。”
There were no other circumstances.”

皇帝批的是，“知道了。”
The emperor's response was, “Noted.”

胡雪岩知道，黄宗汉的那个麻烦已经没有了。
Hu knew that this particular trouble was now behind Huang.

这是否何桂清的功劳呢。
Was He Guiqing responsible for that?

王有龄买了诗集子，胡雪岩也买了京报，
Wang bought his poems and Hu the gazette.

无处可去，正好乘周、吴两人不在，回到船上去密谈。
With nowhere else to go, and Zhou and Wu conveniently absent, they returned to the boats for a private talk.


看完京报上那道上谕，王有龄的心情，可说是一则以喜，一则以惧，
After reading the edict, Wang felt both pleased and afraid.

喜的是黄字汉脱然无累，圣眷正隆，今后浙江的公事，好办得多，
He was pleased that Huang had emerged unencumbered and stood high in imperial favour: Zhejiang's affairs should now be much easier to manage.

惧的是久闻他刻薄奸狡，
Yet he had long heard of the governor's harshness and cunning.

说不定过河拆桥，不再买何桂清的帐，
Huang might discard a bridge once he had crossed it and cease to heed He Guiqing.

那就失去了一座靠山。
That would cost Wang an important source of support.

“雪公！”胡雪岩对他，新近改了这样一个公私两宜的称呼，“我说你是过虑。
“Mr Xuexuan,” Hu said, using a form of address he had recently adopted as suitable for both public and private occasions, “I think you're worrying too much.

黄抚台想做事，要表功，
Governor Huang wants things done and wants credit for doing them.

我们照他的意思来做，做得比他自己所想的还要好，那还有什么话说？
If we follow his wishes and do better than he himself expects, what can he possibly complain of?

俗语说得好，‘师父领进门，修行在各人’，
As the saying goes, the master shows you through the gate, but the practice is your own.

何学台把你领进门就够了，
Education Commissioner He has done enough by bringing you in.

自己修行不到家，靠山再硬也不中用。
If you don't develop your own abilities, even the strongest patron is no use.

你看！”
Look!”


他指着京报中的一道上谕让王有龄看，写的是：“谕内阁大学士、军机大臣会同刑部定拟徐广缙罪名一折，己革署湖广总督徐广缙，经朕简派钦差大臣，接办军务，沿途行走，已属迟延；
He pointed out another edict in the gazette: “Concerning the memorial jointly submitted by the Grand Secretaries, the Grand Council, and the Board of Punishments, as instructed, proposing a sentence for Xu Guangjin: Xu Guangjin, formerly acting Governor-General of Huguang and since dismissed, was specially appointed by Us as imperial commissioner to take charge of military affairs, yet was already dilatory in his progress along the route.

迫贼由湖南下窜，汉阳、武昌相继失守，犹复株守岳州，一筹莫展，
When the rebels descended from Hunan and Hanyang and Wuchang fell in succession, he still clung to Yuezhou without devising a single plan.

实属调度失机，
He gravely mishandled the deployment of forces and lost his opportunity.

徐广缙着即照裕诚等所拟，按定律为斩监候；秋后处决。”
Let Xu Guangjin be sentenced, in accordance with the recommendation of Yucheng and his colleagues and the established law, to beheading after detention, the sentence to be carried out after autumn.”

“这位徐大帅，皇帝特派的钦差大臣，
“This Commander Xu was an imperial commissioner personally appointed by the emperor.

靠山算得硬了！
You could hardly have a stronger patron!

自己不好还是靠不住，
But when a man fails, even that support is unreliable.

还是要杀头。”
He still faces execution.”

胡雪岩似乎很感慨他说，
Hu sounded deeply moved.

“一切都是假的，
“Everything else is uncertain.

靠自己是真的，
You can truly depend only on yourself.

人缘也是靠自己，
Even good connections depend on what you are.

自己是个半吊子，哪里来的朋友？”
What friends can a good-for-nothing expect?”

这番话听得王有龄连连点头，
Wang nodded repeatedly.

“雪岩，”他说：“不是我恭维你，你可惜少读两句书，
“Xueyan, this is no flattery: it's a pity you haven't had more schooling.

不然一定比何根云、黄抚台还要得意。”
Otherwise you'd certainly do better than He Genyun or Governor Huang.”

“我不是这么想，
“I don't see it that way.

做生意的见了官，好象委屈些，其实做生意有做生意的乐趣。
A merchant may seem at a disadvantage before an official, but business has pleasures of its own.

做官许多拘束，
An official faces all sorts of restrictions.

做生意发达了才快活！”
A prosperous merchant can really enjoy himself!”


“喔！”
“Oh?”

王有龄很感兴趣地说：
Wang was interested.

“ ‘盍言尔志’！”
“Then let us hear your ambitions!”

这句话胡雪岩是懂的，
This was one classical phrase Hu understood.

“说到我的志向，与众不同，
“My ambition is rather different from other men's.

我喜欢钱多，越多越好！”
I like plenty of money—the more the better!”

他围拢两手，做了个搂钱的姿势，
He brought both hands round as though gathering a heap towards him.

“不过我有了钱，不是拿银票糊墙壁，看看过瘾就算数，
“But once I have it, I don't mean to paper the walls with bank notes and be satisfied just looking at them.

我有了钱要用出去！
I mean to spend it!

世界上顶顶痛快的一件事，就是看到人家穷途末路，几几乎一钱逼死英雄汉，刚好遇到我身上有钱，”
The finest pleasure in the world is to find someone at the end of his tether, some fine fellow whom the lack of a single coin is almost driving to his death, and happen to have money on me.”

他做了个挥手斥金的姿态，仿佛真有其事似他说：“拿去用！
He flung out a hand as though scattering silver and, almost acting out the scene, exclaimed, “Take it!

够不够？”
Is that enough?”

王有龄大笑：
Wang burst out laughing.

“听你说说都痛快！”
“It's a pleasure even to hear you say it!”

“还有一样，
“There's something else.

做生意发了财，尽管享用，
A merchant who makes a fortune can enjoy it freely.

盖一座大花园，计十七八个姨太太住在里面，没有人好说闲话。
He can build a great garden and put seventeen or eighteen concubines in it, and nobody has any right to complain.

做官的发了财，对不起，不好这样子称心如意！
But an official who grows rich—I'm sorry, he can't please himself so freely!

不说别的，叫人背后指指点点，骂一声‘赃官’，这味道就不好过了。”
Leave everything else aside: having people point behind your back and call you a corrupt official would be unpleasant enough.”

“唉！”
“Ah!”

王有龄被他说动了心，
Wang was tempted.

“照此看来，我都想弃官从商了。”
“The way you put it, I feel like giving up office and going into business myself.”


“这也不是这么说。
“That's not quite what I mean.

做官也有做官的乐趣，
Office has its pleasures too.

起码荣宗耀祖，父母心里就会高兴。
At the very least, it brings honour to your ancestors and makes your parents happy.

象我，有朝一日发了大财，我老娘的日子自然会过得极舒服。
If I make a great fortune one day, my mother will naturally live in tremendous comfort.

不过一定美中不足，
Yet there'll always be something missing.

在她老人家心里，十来个丫头伺候，不如朝廷一道‘诰封’来得值钱！”
In her eyes, ten or a dozen maidservants wouldn't be worth as much as an imperial patent granting her a title.”

“这也不是办下到的事。”王有龄安慰他说，
“That isn't impossible either,” Wang assured him.

“不过一品夫人的诰封请不到而已。”
“You simply couldn't obtain a patent making her a lady of the first rank.”

捐班可以捐到三品道员，自然也就有诰封。
Purchased advancement could reach a third-rank circuit intendancy, which naturally carried eligibility for such a patent.

胡雪岩此时还不敢存此奢望，
Hu did not yet dare entertain that ambition.

“请个诰封，自然不是太难的事，
“Obtaining a patent wouldn't be too difficult, of course.

只是做官要做得名符其实，官派十足，那就不容易了。”
But actually living up to an official title, with all the standing that ought to go with it—that isn't easy.”

他笑笑又说：“不是我菲薄做官的，有些候补老爷，好多年派下上一个差使，穷得来吃尽当光。
He smiled and added, “I'm not disparaging officials, but some expectant gentlemen go years without a single assignment, until poverty has made them eat through their resources and pawn everything they own.

这样子的官，不做也罢。”
That sort of office is hardly worth having.”


这话，王有龄颇有感触，便越觉眼前的机会可贵。
Wang felt the force of that remark from experience and valued his present opportunity all the more.

“雪岩，”他问，“周、吴二人，怎么说法？”
“Xueyan,” he asked, “what about Zhou and Wu?”

什么事怎么说？
What about them in what respect?

胡雪岩无法回答，但他的意思是能够懂的：
Hu could not answer so vague a question, but understood its intention.

“雪公，你放心！
“Rest easy, Mr Xuexuan.

这两位全在我手里，
Those two are completely in my hands.

要他长就长，要他短就短，
They'll do exactly as I wish.

不必放在心上。
There's no need to worry about them.

我现在担心的是怕寻不着这么一位肯垫货的大粮商。”
What concerns me now is whether we can find a large grain merchant willing to supply the rice in advance.”

“是呀！”
“Yes.”

王有龄也上了心事，
Wang too grew anxious.

“我还怕找到了，他不肯相信。”
“I'm also afraid that, even if we find one, he won't trust us.”

“这……”
“That...”

胡雪岩摇摇头：
Hu shook his head.

“不要紧！
“That doesn't matter.

只要他有实力，不怕他不听我们的话。”
Provided he has the resources, I can make him listen.”

看到他这样有信心，再想到他笼络人的手段，王有龄果然放心了。
His confidence, together with what Wang had seen of his skill in winning people over, reassured Wang at once.


等闲谈到晚，张胖子带着周、吴两人兴尽归来。
They talked until evening, when Zhang returned with Zhou and Wu, all well satisfied with their outing.

仔细看去，脸上都浮着诡秘的笑容。
A closer look revealed secretive smiles on their faces.

胡雪岩当着王有龄不便动问，心里明白，他们此行，必为平生所未历。
Hu could not ask questions in Wang's presence, but knew they must have experienced something quite new to them.

“喔，喔，我想起件事。”张胖子忽然一本正经他说，
“Oh, I've just remembered something,” Zhang suddenly said with an air of complete seriousness.

“我今天遇到一个朋友，偶然谈起，松江有一家大粮行，跟漕帮的关系密切，
“I met a friend today, and it came up in conversation that a large grain firm in Songjiang has close ties to the grain-transport association.

他们有十几万石米想卖。
They have a hundred thousand shi or more of rice to sell.

倒不妨打听一下。”
It might be worth making inquiries.”

胡雪岩还未开口，王有龄大为兴奋：“这下对了路了！”
Before Hu could speak, Wang exclaimed excitedly, “Now we're on the right track!”

“咦，雪公！”胡雪岩奇怪地，“事情不过刚刚一提，
“Why, Mr Xuexuan,” Hu asked in surprise, “we've only just heard of it.

也不知内情如何？
We know nothing of the circumstances.

你何以晓得对了路了！
How can you tell it's the right track?”

““你也有不懂的事！”
“So there are things even you don't understand!”

王有龄得意地笑了，为他讲解其中的道理。
Wang smiled triumphantly and explained his reasoning.


他对于漕运已经下过一番功夫，
He had made a serious study of grain transport.

知道松江出米，又当江浙交界，水路极便，
Songjiang produced rice, lay at the boundary between Jiangsu and Zhejiang, and enjoyed excellent water communications.

所以松江的漕帮是个大帮，也应该是个富帮。
Its transport association was therefore large and ought also to have been rich.

但唯其既大且富，便成了一个俎上之肉。
But its very size and wealth made it meat on the chopping board.

松江府知府所以与四川成都府、湖南长沙府，成为府缺中有名的三个肥缺，各有特殊的说法，
The prefecture of Songjiang ranked with Chengdu in Sichuan and Changsha in Hunan as one of the three famously lucrative prefectural appointments, each for its own particular reasons.

松江府兼管水路夫隘，
One important reason in Songjiang was the prefect's control of waterway labour and checkpoints.

漕帮过闸讨夫，不能不买他的帐是一大原因。
When the association needed passage through sluices or men to haul its boats, it had to accommodate him.

年深月久，饱受剥削，松江槽帮的公款亏空甚巨，成了“疲帮”。
