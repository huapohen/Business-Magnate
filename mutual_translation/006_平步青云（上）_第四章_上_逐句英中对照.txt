胡雪岩
《平步青云（上）》第四章（上）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume I — Chapter Four, first part
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


CHAPTER FOUR｜第四章


Shanghai's county town had been fortified in the thirty-second year of the Ming Jiajing reign, originally as a defence against Japanese raiders.
上海县城筑于明朝嘉靖三十二年，原是用以“备倭”的，

The walls extended nine li around the town and stood two zhang and four chi high.
城周九里，城墙高二丈四尽，

There were six gates, large and small.
大小六个城门，

The principal eastern, southern, western, and northern gates were named Chaozong, Kuahai, Yifeng, and Yanhai.
东南西北四门，名为朝宗、跨海、仪风、晏海，

Two others, Baodai and Chaoyang, were commonly called the Little East Gate and Little South Gate.
另外有宝带、朝阳两门，俗称小东门、小南门。

Their boats moored outside the Little East Gate.
他们的船就泊在小东门外。


As the boats arrived, someone on the landing waved.
船刚到就有人在码头上招手，

You Laowu, standing in the bow, answered with a gesture.
立在船头上的尤老五，也报以手势，

Before the gangplank was properly in place, the man came aboard in a few quick steps, as agile as though walking on level ground.
跳板还不曾搭妥，那人己三脚两步，走上船来，身手矫捷，如履平地，

One glance showed that he was accustomed to life on the water.
一望便知是过惯了水上生涯的。

“A'xiang,” You asked, “is everything ready?”
“阿祥！”尤老五问他，“都预备好了？”

“Everything.
“都好了。”阿祥答道，

I've had rooms kept at the Gaosheng Inn by the North Gate, and notified Sanduo Hall.
“叫北门高升栈留了屋子，三多堂也关照过了，

The chairs are waiting on the landing.”
轿子在码头上。”

“Good.
“好，

Go back and attend to things.
你到码头上去招呼，

Make sure nothing is overlooked.”
凡事要周到。”

Once A'xiang had left, You returned to the cabin.
等阿祥一走，尤老五随即回到舱中。

Hu and Zhang were discussing where to stay and in what order to conduct their business.
胡雪岩正在跟张胖子商量，住哪家客栈，先干什么，后干什么，

Neither knew Shanghai particularly well, and after considerable discussion they had settled nothing.
两个人对上海都不大熟，所以商量了半天，尚未停当。

You's arrival made further deliberation unnecessary.
等尤老五一出现，就不必再商量了。

He told Hu that he had sent a man ahead and prepared everything.
他告诉胡雪岩，已预先派了人来招呼，一切都有预备，

They need not trouble themselves.
不劳大家费心，

Shanghai County belonged to Songjiang Prefecture, he added, so he was their host.
同时声明，上海县属于松江府，他是地主，

All their needs in Shanghai would be supplied by him as his “official duty.”
所以在上海的一切供应，都由他“办差”。


“How could we accept that?” Hu protested.
“这怎么敢当？”胡雪岩说，

“And those words ‘official duty’—Brother You, you're positively insulting us!”
“尤其是‘办差’两个字，五哥，你是在骂人了！”

You only smiled, then asked, “Uncle, do you know Shanghai well?”
尤老五笑笑不响，然后问道：“爷叔，你上海熟不熟？”

“No.”
“不熟。”

“Then let's go ashore at once.
“那就快上岸吧，

There are plenty of places to enjoy ourselves.
好白相的地方多得很，

We mustn't waste time.”
不必耽误工夫了。”

They all went ashore, Wang included.
于是，连王有龄在一起，都上了岸，

Several blue-cloth sedan chairs were already waiting at the landing.
码头上已经有几顶蓝呢轿子停在那里。

Only ten years had passed since the opening of the five treaty ports, but Shanghai had become luxurious.
五口通商不过十年的工夫，上海已变得很奢华了，

Violations of the restrictions governing dress and display counted for little.
服饰僭越，更不当回事，

Apart from Wang, every man in the party rode in a large blue-cloth chair for the first time in his life.
所以除却王有龄，大家都生平第一遭坐了蓝呢大轿。


The chairs entered the city and turned north.
轿子进城，折而往北。

When they stopped, the travellers found themselves among inns of various sizes.
停下一看，附近都是客栈，大小不同，

The larger establishments proclaimed in gilded lettering that they offered “temporary residences for officials”; the smaller promised “comfortable lodgings for merchants.”
大的金字招牌上写的是“仕宦行台”，小的便写“安寓客商”。

The Gaosheng Inn naturally belonged to the former category.
高升栈自然是仕宦行台，

You had reserved a courtyard with five spacious, clean rooms.
尤老五派人包下一座院落，共有五间房，十分宽敞干净。

Their luggage had arrived too.
这时行李也送到了，

Once they were settled, he drew Hu aside and quietly asked, “Is His Honour Wang a very proper gentleman?”
等安顿妥帖，尤老五把胡雪岩拉到一边，悄悄问道：“王老爷为人是不是很方正？”

That was difficult to answer.
这话很难回答，

Hu replied, “Brother You, you must have a reason for asking.
胡雪岩便这样答道：“五哥，你问这句话，总有道理在内，

Tell me that first.”
先说来我听听。”

“Here's the arrangement.
“是这样，

I'll give everyone a welcoming meal, then we'll visit the City God Temple.
我先替大家接风，饭后逛逛邑庙。

The bankers' guildhall is in the garden behind it, so Mr Zhang can conveniently call on his fellow bankers.
钱业公所在邑庙后花园，张老板要看同行朋友，也很方便。

Tonight I'd like to entertain you all with courtesans.
到了晚上，我请大家吃花酒，

If His Honour Wang doesn't care to come, we'll make other arrangements.”
如果王老爷不肯去，另作商量。”

So that was it!
原来如此！

Wang seemed a man with a taste for romance, Hu thought, but visiting courtesans compromised official propriety.
胡雪岩心想，看样子王有龄也是个风流人物，不过涉足花丛，有玷官常，

He would have to ask him personally.
这非要问他本人不可。

“There's still plenty of time,” You continued.
“时候也还早。”尤老五又说，

“Perhaps we should eat first and discuss it over tea at the temple later.”
“或者我们先去吃了饭，等下在邑庙吃茶的时候再说。”

“Yes, exactly.
“对，对！

Let's do that.”
就这样。”


You's welcoming meal was at the leading restaurant for local Shanghai cooking, inside the Little East Gate, before the City God Temple, in Huacaobang's Guiyuan Lane—properly called Guanyi Lane.
尤老五替他们接风的地方，是上海城风第一家本帮馆子，在小东门内邑庙前花草滨桂圆弄，实在是馆驿弄。

Wang had already said that he wanted only a modest meal and would decline a formal banquet.
王有龄先就说过，只要小吃，若是整桌的席，他便辞谢，

You therefore ordered local dishes such as zaobotou, tufei, and juancai.
因此尤老五点了本帮菜，糟钵头、秃肺、卷菜之类，

Their flavours were strong and rich.
味极浓腴，

But this was a moment when hungry men were easily satisfied, and nobody found them too heavy.
而正当“饥者易为食”之时，所以也不嫌腻了。


Afterwards, they walked to the temple, which was only a few steps away.
饭后去逛邑庙，近在咫尺，便都走着去了。

The county temple was the City God Temple.
邑庙就是城隍庙。

Worship of this deity began under the Northern Qi, developing from the earth gods of the Qin and Han.
城隍这位尊神起于北齐，原是由秦汉的社神转化来的。

At first the cult existed only south of the Yangtze.
起初只有江南一带才有，

Somewhere in that cultured southeastern region, some clever person had devised the excellent notion of assigning the City God a definite identity: he was the local official of the underworld.
不知是东南人文荟萃之区，哪个聪明人。想出来的好法子，赋予城隍以一种明确的身分：它是阴间的地方官，

A metropolitan City God corresponded to a governor, a county City God to a county magistrate.
都城隍等于巡抚，县城隍便是县令，

Like an earthly yamen, his establishment had its runners and administrative offices.
一般也有三班六房。

In the unseen world, he could arrest people and hear cases.
在冥冥中可以抓人办案。

Thus ordinary people who suffered injustice had a final court of appeal.
因此，老百姓受了冤屈的，就有了一个最后申诉的地方。

The county magistrate acknowledged this local colleague of equal standing; moreover, the magistrate of the dead appeared to exercise authority over the magistrate of the living, so the latter could not help feeling some apprehension.
县官也承认本地有这么一位地位完全相等的同僚，而这位阴世的县官似乎也管着阳世的县官，是以不能不心存忌惮。

The Complete Book of Happiness and Benevolence, a manual on local administration, expressly instructed a magistrate arriving in his jurisdiction to spend a night of ritual fasting at the City God Temple one or three days before taking office.
有部教人如问做地方官的《福惠全书》，就曾写明，县官莅境，“于上任前一日，或前三日至城隍庙斋宿”，

This served, first, as a courtesy visit, an advance request for forbearance; and second, as an opportunity to seek advice in dreams about such matters as the local bullies and corrupt gentry who preyed on the people, or the hidden circumstances of unresolved injustices.
一则是礼貌上的拜访，先打个招呼：“请多多包涵”，再则是在梦中请教，本地有哪些鱼肉乡里的土豪劣绅，或含悬而未结的冤案，内幕如何之类。


The City God was not appointed by the court but chosen by the people, much as the worthy and capable were selected for office in the living world.
城隍不归朝廷指派，而是老百姓选出来的，就如阳世的选贤与能一般，

The guiding principle was that intelligence and uprightness made a spirit worthy of divine honour.
选城隍是“聪明正直之谓神”，

Without uprightness, he would not wish to redress the people's wrongs; without intelligence, he would be unable to do so.
不正直不愿为老百姓伸冤，不聪明则不能为老百姓伸冤。

Shanghai's City God was such a popular choice, one of the three most famous in the southeast.
上海县的城隍就是老百姓所选的，他是东南最有名的三位城隍之一。

Suzhou had Huang Xie, Lord Chunshen; Hangzhou had Wen Tianxiang.
苏州城隍春申君黄歇，杭州城隍文天祥，

Shanghai had originally belonged to Lord Chunshen's fief, but the people of Suzhou had already claimed him, so Shanghai had to choose another.
上海原是春申君的采邑，他被苏州人请了去，上海人只好另选一位城隍，

Their choice was Qin Yubo, a native of Daming Prefecture.
此公叫秦裕伯，大名府人氏，

Late in the Yuan dynasty he had risen to a departmental directorship in the Fujian provincial administration.
元朝末年当到“福建行省郎中”，

When the realm fell into disorder and rival leaders rose, he abandoned office and sought refuge in Shanghai.
因为天下大乱，群雄并起，弃官避难到了上海。

After Zhu Yuanzhang, founder of the Ming, won the empire, he summoned Qin to court, appointed him an academician serving as an imperial reader, then sent him out as prefect of Longzhou.
明太祖朱元璋得了天下，征辟至朝，授官侍读学士，外放陇州知州，

On retiring, Qin returned not to Daming but to Shanghai, his adopted home.
告老以后，不回大名府回到寄籍的上海，

After his death, he repeatedly manifested his powers to protect the people, who therefore chose him as their City God.
死后屡显灵迹，保障生民，所以上海人选他来做城隍。


Like the Great Xiangguo Monastery in Kaifeng, Shanghai's City God Temple was a lively market full of food and diversions.
上海的城隍庙跟开封的大相国寺一样，是个有吃有玩的闹市、

Inside the first entrance gate, miscellaneous shops lined both sides.
一进头山门，两旁郡是杂货铺，

A theatre stage stood in the centre of the second gate, with the passage running beneath it.
二山门正中是个戏台，台下就是通路，

Stalls on either side sold snacks such as sweet osmanthus rice porridge and rice balls in fermented rice wine.
过道两旁是卖桂花糖粥、酒酿圆子等等的小吃摊。

Beyond the stage lay an enormous open court.
戏台前面是个极大的广场，

Engravers occupied the western gallery; in the eastern stood a teahouse used as a meeting place by the county yamen's clerks and runners.
西廊是刻字铺，东廊有家茶店，是上海县衙门书办、皂隶的“茶会”，

People arranging lawsuits or seeking favours made contact there.
老而姓打官司、托人情都在这里接头。

Farther north stood the temple's main hall, flanked by four stone bailiffs.
再往北就是城隍庙的大殿了，两旁石壁拱立四个石皂隶，

Legend said they had drifted in from the sea, probably former subordinates of Qin Yubo in Fujian who had crossed the East Sea to rejoin their old master.
相传是海上飘来的，大概是秦裕伯在福建的旧属，特地浮东海而来，投奔故主。

Inside the hall, a great abacus hung over the doorway facing the god, with eight large characters beside it: “Man has a thousand calculations; Heaven has one.”
一进殿门，面对城隍的门楣上悬一把大算盘，两旁八个大字：“人有千算，天有一算”。

This was the parting admonition offered to worshippers as they left.
这是给烧香出殿的人的“临别赠言”。

Opposite the abacus, above a statue about a zhang high, hung a plaque bearing the City God's formal title, “Divine Lord of Jinshan.”
正对大算盘，丈许高的神像上面有块匾，题作“金山神主”，是为上海县城隍的正式尊号。

Beyond lay the rear hall, where both the god and his wife were worshipped.
再进去就是后殿，供奉城隍及城隍夫人，

Her sleeping chamber was on the western side, a secluded inner room that ordinary mortals were permitted to view on her birthday.
她的寝宫就在西面，寂寂深闺，在她生日那天亦许凡夫俗子一瞻仰。


The temple's chief attraction was Yuyuan, the finest garden within Shanghai's walls.
城隍庙的好玩，是在庙后有座豫园，为上海城内第一名园，

It had originally belonged to Pan Yunduan, who had served as provincial treasurer of Sichuan during the Ming Jiajing reign.
原是明朝嘉靖年间，当过四川布政使的潘允端的产业，

Naturally, the upheavals at the end of the Ming had left it neglected.
明末大乱自然废记，

By the middle of the Qianlong reign, prosperity was at its height and the country extraordinarily wealthy.
乾隆中叶，正值全盛，海内富丽无比，

To provide the City God, protector of this coastal district, with somewhere to relax after his official duties, local people raised money to buy the ruined garden from Pan's descendants and restore it.
本地人为了使“保障海隅”的城隍有个公余游憩之地，特地集资向潘氏后裔买了这个废园，重新修建，

The work took more than twenty years and enormous sums of silver.
历时二十余年，花了巨万的银子，方始完工。

Because it lay northwest of the temple, it was called the West Garden.
因为地处庙的西北，所以名力西园，

An East Garden already stood on the other side, commonly known as the City God Temple's Rear Garden.
而庙东原有个东园，俗称“城隍庙后花园”。

The bankers maintained and repaired the East Garden each year.
东园每年由钱庄同业保养修理，

It opened only for the birthdays of the City God and his wife, and for the elegant orchid gathering in early summer.
只有逢到城隍及城隍夫人生日，以及初夏的“蕙兰雅集”才开放。

Yuyuan, however, remained open all year, with several teahouses and a very large storytelling hall.
豫园却是终年洞开，里面有好几家茶店，还有极大的一座书厅。

You entertained the party with tea in Qingfen Hall, popularly called the Osmanthus Hall.
尤老五招待大家在俗称“桂花厅”的清芬堂喝茶。

A bird-fighting contest was taking place that day.
这天有人在斗鸟，

Several of the participants were his brethren, who greeted him warmly and urged him to join them.
其中颇多尤老五的“弟兄”，走来殷殷致意，请他“下场去玩”。

Like cricket fighting, the sport allowed heavy betting.
这就象斗蟋蟀一样，可以博采，输赢甚大。

You invited Zhou, Wu, and Zhang to take part, leaving Hu free to speak privately with Wang.
尤老五便把周、吴两委员和张胖子请了去一起玩，留下胡雪岩好跟王有龄说私话。


“Mr Xuexuan,” Hu asked casually, “would you care for a little diversion tonight?”
“雪公！”他意态闲豫地问道：“今天晚上，逢场作戏，可有兴致？”

Assuming he meant cards, Wang shook his head.
王有龄只当要他打牌，摇摇头说：

“You go ahead as usual.
“你们照常玩吧！

I'm no hand at gambling.”
我对赌钱不内行。”

“We'd be admiring flowers, not bamboo.”
“不是看竹是看花！”

Wang understood.
王有龄懂了，

Bamboo meant bamboo-backed playing tiles; flowers, needless to say, meant lingering among the alluring blossoms of the pleasure quarters like drifting catkins.
竹是竹牌，花则不用说，当然是“倡条冶时恣留连，飘荡轻子花上絮”，

He immediately smiled.
例即笑道：

“Flowers and bamboo—what a delightful turn of phrase!”
“看竹看花的话，隽妙得很！”

Close though they were, this was the first time they had discussed visiting courtesans together.
两人交情虽深，结伴作狎邪游的话，却还是第一次谈到。

Wang was older and could never quite put aside the thought that he was an official.
王有龄年纪长些，又去不了一个“官”字的念头，

Embarrassed inwardly, he could manage only this evasive answer.
所以内心不免有忸怩之感，只好作这样不着边际的答复。

Hu understood human nature thoroughly.
胡雪岩熟透人情，自然了解，

He knew Wang was tempted, but might not wish to dine with courtesans in Zhou and Wu's company.
知道他心里有些活动，但跟周、吴二人一起去吃花酒，怕他未见得愿意，

Even if he agreed, he might find little pleasure in it.
就是愿意也未见得有乐趣。

Hu therefore formed another plan.
这样一想，胡雪岩另有了计较，

For the moment he said nothing and turned to official business.
暂时不响，只谈公事，

They agreed to rest that day.
决定这天休息，

On the following day Wang would pay his calls, while Hu and Zhang joined You to arrange the loan.
第二天起，王有龄去拜客，胡雪岩、张胖子会同尤老五去借款。


“One important matter remains,” Wang said.
“还有件要紧事，”王有龄说，

“We must promptly arrange the twenty thousand taels Governor Huang wants remitted to Fujian.”
“黄抚台要汇到福建的那两万银子，得赶紧替他办妥。”

“I know.
“我知道。

Secrecy matters more than speed in that affair.
这件事不在快，要秘密，

I'll see to it.
我自会弄妥当，

You needn't worry.”
你不必操心。”

Hu rose as he spoke.
说着，便站起身来。

You was a man who heard and saw everything around him.
尤老五是耳听六路、眼观八方的角色，

Seeing Hu get up, he excused himself from the game.
见胡雪岩一站起身来，便借故离座，

They met, spoke quietly, and made their arrangements.
两人会合在一起，低声密语，作了安排。

That evening the visitors from Hangzhou separated into three unconnected parties.
这天夜里，杭州来的人，便分作各不相关的三起去玩，

One went to Sanduo Hall.
一起是到三多堂，

Another consisted of Gao alone, accompanied around town by a junior member of the association whom You had assigned to him.
一起是高升一个人，由尤老五派了个小弟兄陪他各处去逛。

When everyone else had gone, only Wang remained aboard, dressed in ordinary clothes, with a pair of dark crystal spectacles beside him, reading as he waited.
等人都走光了，只剩下一个王有龄，换了便服，把一副墨晶眼镜放在手边，在船上看书坐等。

Just after nightfall, Hu slipped away from Sanduo Hall.
天刚刚黑，胡雪岩从三多堂溜了出来，

One of You's men was already waiting.
尤老五已有人在等候，

They took a sedan chair to the landing outside the Little East Gate and collected Wang.
坐轿到了小东门外码头上，把王有龄接了出来。

Their guide told the bearers, “Meijia Lane.”
陪伴的人呛咐轿夫：“梅家弄。”


Meijia Lane was fairly secluded, but its winding approach led to a place of unexpected charm.
梅家弄地方相当偏僻，但曲径通幽，别有佳趣。

When the chairs arrived, the guide softly tapped the brass ring on a small stone-framed gate, which was promptly opened.
等轿子抬到，领路的人，在一座小小的石库门上，轻叩铜环，随即便有人来开门。

A woman of about forty received them, speaking in a particularly pleasing Suzhou accent.
应接的是一个四十左右的妇人，说得一口极好听的苏州话。

Only when they reached the lighted reception room did Wang, looking through his dark glasses, see that she retained considerable grace despite her faded youth.
到了客厅里灯光亮处，王有龄从黑晶眼镜里望出去，才发觉这个妇人，秋娘老去，风范犹存。

The room was arranged with neatness and taste, quite free of vulgarity, and he felt at ease.
再看客厅里的陈设，布置得楚楚有致，着实不俗，心里便很舒服。

“Third Aunt,” the guide told their hostess, “these are Mr Wang and Mr Hu, both distinguished guests.
“三阿姨！”领路的人为“本家”介绍：“王老爷，胡老爷，都是贵客，

Take special care of them.” She answered with repeated assent.
格外招呼！”三阿姨喏喏连声，

Her manner showed not merely compliance but a measure of fear.
