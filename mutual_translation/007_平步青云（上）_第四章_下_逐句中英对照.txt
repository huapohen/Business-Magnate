胡雪岩
《平步青云（上）》第四章（下，至本章结束）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume I — Chapter Four, concluding part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第二天一早，胡雪岩悄悄到梅家弄把王有龄接回船。
Early the next morning, Hu quietly went to Meijia Lane and brought Wang back to the boat.

这位王大老爷春风满面，步履轻快，
His Honour was radiant and light of step.

大家都道他异乡遇故，快谈竟夕，才有这份轻松的情绪，
Everyone supposed that a reunion with an old acquaintance far from home, followed by a whole night of pleasant conversation, accounted for his cheerful ease.

谁也不知道他微服私行，比起三多堂的喧闹轰饮，另有一番屋小如舟，春深似海的旖旎风光。
Nobody knew that his discreet excursion in ordinary clothes had offered pleasures quite unlike the noisy drinking at Sanduo Hall: a room as small as a boat, with spring's enchantment as deep as the sea.

这天开始要办正事了，
Serious work was to begin that day.

王有龄把周、吴两委员请了来，连胡雪岩一起，先作个商量。
Wang summoned Zhou and Wu for a preliminary discussion with Hu.

他原定这一天上午去拜客，胡雪岩主张不必亟亟。
He had intended to pay his calls that morning, but Hu thought there was no need to hurry.

“今天中午，尤老五和张胖子出面，请‘三大’的人吃饭，
“At noon, You and Zhang are entertaining the men from the Big Three.

放款的事一谈好，通裕的米，随即可以拨借。”他说：
Once the loan is settled, Tongyu can immediately advance the rice.

“雪公，索性再等一等，
Mr Xuexuan, wait a little longer.

也不会太久，一两天工夫，
It won't take much time—a day or two.

等我们自己这里办妥了再说。”
Let us put our own arrangements in order first.”

“这样好！”
“Excellent!”

周委员首先表示赞成，
Zhou was the first to agree.

“到明后天，王大人去拜这里的按察使，那就直接谈交兑漕米了，
“When you call on the judicial commissioner tomorrow or the day after, you can discuss delivery of the grain directly.

差使显得更漂亮。”
The assignment will look all the better.”

“好！
“Very well.

我听你们的主意。”王有龄欣然同意。
I'll take your advice,” Wang readily agreed.


“中午的饭局，不请周、吴两公了。”胡雪岩说第二件事，
“We won't ask you two gentlemen to the luncheon,” Hu continued.

“商人总是怕官的，
“Merchants are always nervous of officials.

有周、吴两公在座，怕‘三大’的人拘束……”
With you both present, the Big Three might feel constrained...”

“不错，不错！”周委员抢着说道，
“Quite right!” Zhou interrupted.

“你无须解释。”
“No explanation is needed.”

“不过有件大事要请周、吴两公费心，
“But there is an important matter on which we need your help.

‘民折官办’的这道手续，马上就要办一办。
The paperwork for cash payment by the taxpayer and official procurement must be dealt with at once.

公事上我不懂，
I know little of official procedure.

雪公看怎么处置？”
What do you suggest, Mr Xuexuan?”

“那要奉托两位了。”王有龄看着他们说：
“We must leave that to you two,” Wang said, looking at them.

“两位是熟手，一定错不了。
“You're experienced men and won't go wrong.

该我山面的，尽管请吩咐！
If anything requires my intervention, just tell me.”

“于是周、吴二人相视沉吟，似乎都有些茫然不知如何着手的样子。
Zhou and Wu exchanged thoughtful glances, both apparently uncertain where to begin.

胡雪岩等了一会，看他们很为难，忍不住又说了，
Hu waited a moment, then could not refrain from helping.

“我看这件事，公文上说不清楚，
“I don't think this can be explained clearly in a document.

得有一位回杭州去当面禀陈。”
One of you ought to return to Hangzhou and report in person.”

“对了！”
“Exactly!”

吴委员抚掌接口，
Wu clapped his hands.

“我也是这么想。
“That was my thought too.

当然，公文还是要的，只不过简单说一说，‘民折官办’一案，十分顺手，特饬某某人回省面禀请示云云。
Of course a document is still needed, but it can simply state that the official-procurement arrangement is proceeding favourably and that so-and-so has been specially ordered back to the provincial capital to report and seek instructions.

这样就可以了。”
That will do.”


“那好！
“Good.

两位之中，哪一位辛苦一趟？”
Which of you will undertake the journey?”

这一向，周、吴二人又迟疑了。
At this question, both hesitated again.

甫到繁华之地，不能尽兴畅游，心里十分不愿。
They had only just reached this prosperous city and were most unwilling to leave before enjoying it fully.

而且这一案的内容十分复杂，
Besides, the affair was complicated.

上面有所垂询，不能圆满解释，差使就算砸了。
If their superiors asked questions they could not answer satisfactorily, the mission would be a failure.

畏难之念一起，更不敢自告奋勇。
Once this apprehension arose, neither dared volunteer.

“怎么？”
“Well?”

王有龄有些不悦，
Wang was displeased.

“看样子只好我自己回去一趟了。”
“It seems I shall have to go back myself.”

“那没有这个道理。”周委员很惶恐他说，
“That would never do,” Zhou said in alarm.

“我去，我去！”
“I'll go.”

看周委员有了表示，吴委员倒也不好意思了，
Now that Zhou had spoken, Wu felt embarrassed too.

“自然是我去。”他说。
“Naturally, I should go,” he said.

两个人争是在争，其实谁也不愿意去，
They competed for the duty, though neither wanted it.

王有龄不愿硬派，便说，“这样吧，我们掣签！”
Unwilling to impose a choice, Wang said, “Then let us draw lots.”

“不必了！”周委员很坚决他说，
“No need!” Zhou declared firmly.

“决定我去。
“It's settled: I'll go.

吴兄文章好，留在这里帮大人料理公事。
Brother Wu writes well and should stay to help Your Honour with the documents.

我今天下午就走，尽快回来复命。”
I'll leave this afternoon and return to report as soon as possible.”


“也不必这么急。”
“There's no need for such haste.”

胡雪岩作了个诡秘的微笑，
Hu gave a knowing smile.

“今天晚上我替周老爷饯行。
“I'll give you a farewell dinner tonight.

明天动身好了。”
You can leave tomorrow.”

“雪岩兄的话不错。公事虽然紧要，也不争在这半天工夫。”吴委员也说，
“Brother Xueyan is right,” Wu agreed. “Important though the business is, half a day won't matter.

“晚上替周兄饯行，我跟雪岩兄一起作主人。”
He and I will jointly entertain Brother Zhou tonight.”

王有龄也表示从容些的好，
Wang too favoured a more leisurely departure.

并且颇有嘉勉之词，暗示将来叙功的“保案”中，一定替周委员格外说好话，作为酬庸。
He praised Zhou warmly and hinted that, when recommendations for rewards were submitted, he would speak particularly well of him.

自告奋勇的收获，可说相当丰富。
Volunteering had yielded a considerable return.


为了周委员回杭州，那个庶务却是大忙而特忙，
Zhou's return to Hangzhou kept the general affairs officer exceptionally busy.

第一要雇船，
First, a boat had to be hired.

照周委员的意思，最好坐原来的那只“无锡快”，由阿珠一路伺奉着来回。
Zhou would have preferred one of their original fast Wuxi boats, with A'zhu serving him on the outward and return journeys.

但那只船名“快”而实不快，
But these boats were fast only in name.

只宜于晚开早到，多泊少走，玩赏风景之用，
They were suited to late departures, early arrivals, frequent stops, and leisurely sightseeing.

赶路要另雇双桨奇快的“水上飞”。
For speed, he needed a different vessel, a double-oared “water flyer.”

第二件更麻烦，也是胡雪岩的建议，
The second task was more troublesome, and again Hu's idea.

杭州抚、藩、臬三大宪，加上粮道，还有各衙门有关系的文案、幕友，都应该有一份礼。
Gifts were due to the governor, treasurer, and judicial commissioner in Hangzhou, to the grain intendant, and to the relevant secretaries and advisers in their offices.

“十里夷场”，奇珍异物无数，
The foreign settlement, stretching for miles, offered countless rare and curious objects.

会选的花费不多而受者惬意，不会的，花了大价钱却不起眼，变成“俏眉眼做给瞎子看”，
A skilful buyer could please the recipient at little cost; an unskilful one might spend a fortune on something that attracted no notice, wasting his charms on a blind man.

因此，备办这十几份礼物，不是一件轻松的差使。
Choosing these dozen or more gifts was no easy assignment.

胡雪岩主意，请尤老五派个人，带着那庶务和高升，到“夷场”上外国人所开最大的一家洋行“亨达利”去采办。
Hu decided to ask You for a guide to take the general affairs officer and Gao to Hengdali, the largest foreign trading house in the settlement, to make their purchases.


这天人人有事，
Everyone had work to do that day.

王有龄和周、吴二人在船上办文稿，开节略，把此行的经过，如何繁难吃力，而又如何圆满妥帖，字斟句酌地叙了进去。
Wang, Zhou, and Wu remained aboard drafting reports and summaries, carefully weighing every word to show how difficult the assignment had been and how thoroughly and satisfactorily they had carried it out.

胡雪岩和张胖子的任务，自然更重要，
Hu and Zhang naturally had still more important duties.

中午与尤老五请“三大”的档手，在英租界的“番菜馆”赴宴谈生意。
At noon they joined You in entertaining the managers of the Big Three at a Western restaurant in the British concession, where they intended to discuss business.

结果生意不曾在番菜馆谈，
In the event, no business was discussed there.

因为照例要“叫局”，莺莺燕燕一大堆，不是谈生意的时候。
Courtesans were summoned as usual, and with so many women around, the occasion was unsuitable.

饭罢一起到城隍庙后花园钱业公所品茗，
After lunch, the party went for tea to the bankers' guildhall in the garden behind the City God Temple.

这时张胖子才提到正事。
Only then did Zhang raise the matter.

“三大”之中，大亨钱庄姓孙的档手资格最老，
Of the three banks, Daheng's manager, Sun, had the greatest seniority.

他代代表发言，首先就表示最近银根很紧，
Speaking for them all, he began by saying that money was very tight.

“局势不好，
“The outlook is poor.

有钱的人都要把现银子捏在手里，怕放了倒帐。
People with money want to keep the silver in their own hands, fearing bad debts if they lend it.

这句实在话，钱庄本来是空的。”
Frankly, a bank has little money of its own.”

这是照例有的托词，
This was the customary excuse.

银根紧的理由甚多，不妨随意编造，目的就在抬高利息。
There were endless reasons for tight money, any of which could be invented to push up the interest.

张胖子和胡雪岩都懂这个道理，
Zhang and Hu understood perfectly.

尤老五却以受过上海钱庄的气，怀有成见，
You, however, had suffered at the hands of Shanghai bankers before and was already prejudiced against them.

大为不快。
He was deeply displeased.


“我看不是银根紧，
“I doubt it's the money that's tight.

只怕是借的人招牌不硬，”
Perhaps the borrower's name isn't good enough.”

他的话有棱角，态度却极好，是半带着开玩笑的语气说的，
His words had an edge, but his manner remained amiable, almost joking.

“漕帮现在倒霉，
“Our association is down on its luck.

要是‘沙船帮’的郁老大开口，银根马上就松了。”
If Boss Yu of the sand-junk association asked, the money would loosen at once.”

尤老五说的这个人是沙船帮的巨擘，名叫郁馥山，拥有上百艘的沙船，
He meant Yu Fushan, a leading shipowner who possessed more than a hundred sand junks.

北走关东，南走闽粤，照海洋的方位，称为“北洋”、“南洋”，
They sailed north to Manchuria and south to Fujian and Guangdong, on routes known by their maritime positions as the Northern and Southern Seas.

郁馥山就以走南北洋起家，是上海县的首富。
Yu had made his fortune on those routes and become the richest man in Shanghai County.

近年因为漕米海运，更是大发利市，
The recent shift of grain shipments to the sea had brought him still greater profits.

新近在小南门造了一所巨宅，崇楼杰阁，参以西法，
Near the Little South Gate he had lately built a vast residence, with imposing towers and halls incorporating Western methods.

算是“海天旭日”、“黄浦秋涛”等等“沪城八景”以外的另一景。
It counted as another sight to be set beside Shanghai's celebrated Eight Views, such as “Sunrise over Sea and Sky” and “Autumn Waves on the Huangpu.”

沙船帮与漕帮，本来海永不犯河水，但漕运改了新章，使有了极厉害的利害冲突，
The sand-junk men and the canal association had once kept to their separate waters, but the new grain-transport arrangements had brought their interests into sharp conflict.

所以尤老五那句话斤两很重，
You's remark therefore carried considerable weight.

姓孙的有些吃不消。
Sun found it difficult to answer.

“啊，尤五哥，”姓孙的惶恐地说，“你这话，我们一个字也不敢承认。
“Brother You,” he said anxiously, “we cannot accept a single word of that.

吝户都是一样的，
All clients are treated alike.

论到交情，尤五哥的面子更加不同。
As for friendship, your standing with us is exceptional.

好了，今天就请尤五哥吩咐！“
Very well: tell us what you require today.”


象尤老五这样在江湖上有地位的，轻易说不得一句重话，
A man of You's standing could not lightly speak harshly.

刚才话中有牢骚，已不够漂亮，
The resentment in his earlier remark had already fallen short of proper grace.

此刻听姓孙的这样回答，更显得自己那句话带着要挟威胁的意味，越觉不安，
Sun's answer now made it sound positively coercive, and You was still more uneasy.

所以急忙抱拳笑道：
He hastily clasped his hands and smiled.

“言重，言重！
“You are too kind.

全靠各位帮忙。”
I rely entirely on your help.”

张胖子总归是站在同行这方面的，而且自己也有担保的责任，
Zhang ultimately stood with his fellow bankers, and he too bore responsibility for the guarantee.

心里在想，姓孙的吃不消尤老五，说到“请吩咐”的话，未免冒失！
Sun's inability to withstand You had led him rashly to offer to do whatever was asked.

如果凭一句话草草成局，以后一出麻烦，吃亏的心是钱庄，自己也会连带受累。
If a transaction were concluded on the strength of one remark and trouble arose later, the banks would suffer, and Zhang would be involved as well.

由于这样的了解，他不希望他们讲江湖义气，愿意一板一眼谈生意，不过他的话也很圆到，
He therefore preferred straightforward business negotiation to appeals to brotherhood, though he expressed himself tactfully.

“大家都是自己人，尤五哥更是好朋友，
“We're all friends here, and Brother You is a particularly good one.

没有谈不通的事，”他说，
Nothing is beyond agreement.

“ ‘三大，愿意帮忙，尤老哥一定也不会叫’三大‘吃亏。
The Big Three are willing to help, and Brother You certainly won't let them lose by it.

是不是？”
Isn't that so?”

尤老五当然听得出他话中的意思，立即接口：
You immediately understood.

“一点不错！
“Exactly.

江湖归江湖，生意归生意。
Friendship is one thing, business another.

我看这样，”
Here's my suggestion.”

他望着胡雪岩说，
He looked at Hu.

“小爷叔，这件事让张老板跟孙老板他们去谈，
“Young Uncle, let Mr Zhang discuss it with Mr Sun and the others.

应该怎么样就怎么样，我无不照办，
Whatever terms are proper, I'll accept.

我们就不必在场了。”
There's no need for us to stay.”

胡雪岩听他这一说，暗暗佩服，
Hu privately admired him.

到底是一帮的老大，做事实在漂亮。
He was indeed the leader of an association and knew how to handle things handsomely.

于是欣然答道：“对，对！
“Yes,” he readily answered.

我也正有事要跟五哥谈。”
“I have something to discuss with you as well.”

说着，两人相偕起身，向那几个钱庄朋友点一点头，到另外一张桌子去吃茶，让张胖子全权跟“三大”谈判。
They rose together, nodded to the bankers, and moved to another table, leaving Zhang full authority to negotiate.


“小爷叔！”尤老五首先表明，“借款是另外一回事，通裕垫米又是一回事，
“Young Uncle,” You began, “the loan is one matter; Tongyu's advance of rice is another.

桥归桥，路归路。
Let each stand on its own.

米，我已经叫通裕启运了，
I've already ordered Tongyu to begin shipping.

在哪里交兑，

“好极了！
“Excellent!

五哥跟老太爷这样放交情，我现在也不必说什么！
With you and the old master showing us such friendship, there's no need for more words now.

‘路遥知马力，日久见人心‘，
A long road tests a horse's strength; time reveals a man's heart.

将来就晓得了。“胡雪岩接着又说，”在哪里交兑，等我问明白了来回报五哥。

要不要另外派人，公事上我不大懂，也要回去问一问。
Whether we must send someone separately is an official detail I don't understand; I must ask about that too.

如果我好作主，当然拜托五哥，辛苦弟兄们替我办一办。
If it were mine to decide, I would naturally entrust it to you and trouble the brethren to handle it for me.”

““好的，
“Good.

就这样说定了，
Then it's settled.

我关照通裕老顾去伺候，
I'll tell Gu at Tongyu to attend upon you.

王大老爷有什么话，尽管交代他。”
His Honour Wang can give him whatever instructions are needed.”

一件有关浙江地方大吏前程的大事，就这样三言两语作了了结。
An affair upon which the careers of Zhejiang's senior officials depended was thus settled in a few words.

胡雪岩还有件要紧事要请尤老五帮忙。
Hu still had another important request.


“五哥，我还有个麻烦要靠你想办法。”
“Brother You, there's another difficulty I need your help with.”

他放低了声音说：
He lowered his voice.

“我有两万银子要汇到福建，不能叫人知道，
“I have twenty thousand taels to remit to Fujian without anyone knowing.

你有什么办法？”
Can you arrange it?”

尤老五沉吟了一会问道：
You considered.

“是现银，还是庄票？”
“Is it silver, or a bank draft?”

“自然是庄票。”
“A draft, naturally.”

“那容易得很。”尤老五很随便地说：
“Then it's very easy,” You said casually.

“你自己写封信，把庄票封在里面，
“Write a letter yourself and enclose the draft.

我找个人替你送到，拿回信回来。
I'll find someone to deliver it and bring back a reply.

你看怎么样？”
Would that suit?”

“那这样太好了。”
“That would be ideal.

胡雪岩又问：“不晓得要几天工夫？”
How long would it take?”

“不过五六天工夫。”
“Only five or six days.”

胡雪岩大为惊异：
Hu was astonished.

“这么快？”
“So quickly?”

“我托火轮船上的人去办。”
“I'll ask someone aboard a steamship to do it.”


从道光十五年起，英国第一艘“渣甸号”开到，东南沿海便有了轮船。
Steamships had appeared off the southeastern coast since the arrival of the first British vessel, the Jardine, in the fifteenth year of the Daoguang reign.

不久为了禁鸦片开仗，
Soon afterwards, the suppression of opium led to war.

道光二十一年辛丑七月，英国军队攻陷镇江，直逼江宁，
In the seventh month of the twenty-first Daoguang year, the xinchou year, British troops captured Zhenjiang and pressed towards Jiangning.

运了大炮安置在钟山，预备轰城。
They transported cannon to Zhongshan, preparing to bombard the city.

朝廷大震，决计议和，
The court was deeply alarmed and decided to negotiate peace.

派出耆英、伊里布和两江总督牛鉴为“全权大臣”，与英国公使谈和，
It appointed Qiying, Yilibu, and the Governor-General of Liangjiang, Niu Jian, as plenipotentiaries to negotiate with the British representative.

订立和约十三条，赔军费，割香港，开广州、厦门、福州、宁波、上海力通商口岸，称为“五口通商”，
A treaty of thirteen articles provided for a war indemnity, the cession of Hong Kong, and the opening of Guangzhou, Xiamen, Fuzhou, Ningbo, and Shanghai to trade: the opening of the five treaty ports.

大英公司的轮船，源源而至，
Steamships of the British company came in increasing numbers.

从上海到福州经常有班轮，但一路停靠宁波、温州，来回要半个月的工夫，
Regular vessels sailed between Shanghai and Fuzhou, but with stops at Ningbo and Wenzhou, the round trip took half a month.

何以说是只要五六天？
How could You promise five or six days?

胡雪岩越发不解。
Hu was more puzzled than ever.

“我到英国使馆去想办法，
“I'll arrange it through the British mission,” You explained.

他们有直放的轮船。”
“They have ships that sail directly.”


“噢！”
