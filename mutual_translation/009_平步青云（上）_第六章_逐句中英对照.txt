第六章｜CHAPTER SIX


他请教的不是别人，是王有龄。
The person he consulted was none other than Wang.

“题招牌我还是破题儿第一遭。”王有龄笑道，
“This is my first attempt at naming a business,” Wang laughed.

“还不知怎么题法，
“I don't even know how to begin.

有些什么讲究？”
What are the requirements?”

“第一要响亮，容易上口，
“First, it must sound impressive and be easy to say.

第二字眼要与众不同，省得跟别家搅不清楚。
Second, the characters must be distinctive, so nobody confuses it with another firm.

至于要跟钱庄有关，要吉利，那当然用不着说了。
Naturally, it must suit a bank and be auspicious.”

““好，
“Very well.

我来想想看。”
Let me think.”

他实在有些茫然，
He was rather at a loss.

随便抽了本书，想先选几个字写下来，然后再来截搭选配。
Taking a book at random, he intended to select a few characters, write them down, and then try different combinations.

书架上抽出来的那本书是《华阳国志》，
The volume he pulled from the shelf was the Chronicles of Huayang.

随手一翻，看了几行，
He opened it and read a few lines.

巧极了，现成有两个字。

“这两个字怎么样？”
“What about these?”

王有龄提笔写了《华阳国志》上的两句话：“世平道治，民物阜康。”
He wrote out a passage: “The age is peaceful, the way well governed; the people and their possessions flourish in abundance and well-being.”

在“阜康”上面打了两个圈。
He circled the characters fu and kang.

“阜康，阜康！”胡雪岩念了两遍，欣然答道，“好极！
“Fukang, Fukang!” Hu repeated the name twice, then answered gladly, “Excellent!

既阜且康，
Both abundance and well-being.

就是它。”
That's the one.”


说着，他就要起身辞去，王有龄唤住他说，
As Hu rose to leave, Wang called him back.

“雪岩，我有个消息告诉你，
“Xueyan, I have news.

我要补实缺了。”
I'm to receive a regular appointment.”

“喔！
“Oh!

哪个州县？”
Which department or county?”

“现在还不晓得。
“I don't know yet.

他顺便透露的消息。

照我想，也该补我的缺了。”
I suppose it is time I received a post.”

胡雪岩眼尖，告诉王有龄说：

“是吴委员。”
“It's Commissioner Wu.”

真正大喜！”
Wonderful news!”

“喔，喔，”王有龄愣了一下，旋即会意，
Wang paused, then understood.

吴委员跟藩署接近，必是有了放缺的消息，
Wu had connections at the provincial treasury and must have heard of the appointment.

便站起身来，连连拱手：

“多谢，多谢！”
“Thank you!”

“我刚从藩署来，”他走近两步说，
“I've just come from the treasurer's office,” Wu said, stepping closer.

“确确实实的消息，委大人署理湖州府，”
“The news is certain: you are to act as prefect of Huzhou.”

这一说，连不十分熟悉官场情形的胡雪岩都觉得诧异，
Even Hu, who knew comparatively little of official practice, was astonished.

候补州县，“本班”的实缺不曾当过一天，忽然一跃而被委署知府，
An expectant department or county magistrate, who had not served a single day in a regular post of his own grade, was suddenly to leap to an acting prefecture?

这不是太离谱了吗？
Surely that was extraordinary.

王有龄自然更难置信，
Wang found it still harder to believe.

“这，这似乎不大对吧？”他迟疑地问。
“That... that doesn't sound quite right.”

“决不错！
“There can be no mistake!

明天就‘挂牌’。”
The appointment will be posted tomorrow.”


王有龄沉吟了一会，总觉得事有蹊跷，

便央求吴委员再去打听究竟，一面又叫高升到刘二那里去问一问，或者倒有确实消息。

消息来得太突兀，却也太令人动心，
The news was both abrupt and intensely exciting.

王有龄患得患失之心大起，在海运局签押房，坐立不宁，
Torn between hope and fear, Wang could neither sit nor stand comfortably in his office.

胡雪岩便劝他说：“雪公，你沉住了气！

“Even if it isn't a prefecture, it must be a large county.

到晚上见了抚台就知道了。”

“我在想，”
“I was thinking...”

王有龄答非所问，

“那天藩台说的话，当时我没有在意，

“Then that explains it!”

胡雪岩拍着自己的大腿说，

““说得有理。”
“That makes sense.”

王有龄大为欣慰。
Wang was greatly reassured.


“不过，雪公！”胡雪岩说，“湖州大户极多，

公事难办得很。”
Its affairs are very difficult.”

“就是这话罗！
“Exactly!

所以，雪岩，你还是要帮我，跟我一起到湖州去。
That's why you must still help me and come with me.”

这句话胡雪岩答应不下，

便先宕开一句：

“慢慢再商量。
“We can discuss it later.

雪公，倒是有件事，不可不防！

这里的差使怎么样？”
What happens to your assignment here?”

“这里”自是指海运局，
“Here” meant the Sea Transport Bureau.

一句话提醒了王有龄，“坐办”的差使要交卸了，亏空要弥补，经手的公事要交代清楚。
The question reminded Wang of all that handing over its executive directorship would involve: the deficit must be made good and every affair under his management explained.

后任有后任的办法，
A successor would have his own methods.

倘或海运局的公款不再存信和，关系一断，替松江漕帮借款担保这一层，就会有很大的麻烦，
If the bureau's funds ceased to be deposited with Xinhe and the relationship ended, their guarantee for the Songjiang association's loan could create serious trouble.

真个不可不防。
It was indeed a danger to consider.

“是啊！”
“Yes!”

王有龄吸着气说，
Wang drew a breath.

“这方面关系甚重，
“A great deal depends on that.

得要早早想办法，
We must act early.

我想，跟抚台老实说明白，最好仍旧让我兼这个差使。
I think I should tell the governor frankly and ask to retain the bureau as an additional duty.

就怕他说，人在湖州，省城的公事鞭长莫及，那就煞费周章了。”
But if he says I couldn't manage provincial-capital business from Huzhou, we'll be in considerable difficulty.”

“雪公，我倒要问一句，
“Then let me ask you this.

到了鱼与熊掌不可兼得的那一步，你怎样打算？”
If you reach the point where you can't have both, what will you choose?”

“我情愿不补实缺，把这里先顾住。”王有龄说，
“I'd rather decline the regular appointment and preserve our position here.

“我靠朋友帮忙，才有今天，
I owe my present standing to my friends' help.

不能留下一个累来害你和张胖子、尤老五！”
I can't leave a burden that harms you, Zhang, and You.”

“雪公！”胡雪岩深深点头，一个字，一个字他说道：
Hu nodded deeply and spoke word by word.

“有了这个念头，就不怕没有朋友。”
“With that principle, you'll never lack friends.”

经此一番交谈，王有龄彻底了解了自己的最后立场，心倒反而定了来了。
Having clarified his ultimate position, Wang felt calmer.


两个人接着便根据不同的情况，商量在见黄宗汉时，如何措同。
They discussed how he should address Huang under various circumstances.

这样谈了有半个时辰，高升首先回来复命，
After about an hour, Gao returned.

如胡雪岩所意料的，这天一早，黄宗汉待为把麟桂找了去，有所密谈，
As Hu had expected, Huang had specially summoned Lingui that morning for a private consultation.

可见得吴委员的消息，不是无因而至。
Wu's news plainly had a basis.

不久，吴委员带回来更详细的喜信，王有龄是被委署为乌程县知县，兼署湖州府知府。
Soon Wu himself brought fuller confirmation: Wang was to act as magistrate of Wucheng County and concurrently as prefect of Huzhou.

事到如今，再无可疑。
There could be no further doubt.

海运局上上下下，也都得到了消息，约齐了来向坐办贺喜，
Everyone at the bureau had heard and came together to congratulate their director.

又商量凑公份办戏酒，为王有龄开贺。
They also discussed subscribing for a feast and theatrical performance in his honour.

这大招摇了！王有龄一定不肯，
Wang thought this far too ostentatious and firmly refused.

托吴委员向大家道谢疏通，千万不可有此举动。
He asked Wu to thank them and make absolutely clear that no such celebration should be held.

扰攘半日，莫衷一是，
Half a day of commotion produced no agreement.

他也只得暂且丢下不问，准时奉召去看黄宗汉。
At last he put the matter aside and went punctually to the governor's summons.


“今年的钱粮，一定要想办法征足，
“We must find a way to collect this year's full revenue.

军费浩繁，
Military expenses are enormous.

催京饷的部文，接二连三飞到，
Ministry dispatches demanding remittances to the capital arrive one after another.

你看，还有一道上谕。”
Look—another edict.”

王有龄起身从黄宗汉手中上谕来看，
Wang rose and took the document from Huang.

只见洋洋千言，尽是有关筹饷和劝谕捐输的指示，
Its lengthy text concerned raising military funds and encouraging contributions.

最后一段说：“户部现因外省拨款，未能如期解到，奏请将俸银分别暂停一年。

朕思王公大臣，俸人素优，即暂停给发，事尚可行，
We consider that princes and senior ministers have long enjoyed generous emoluments, so a temporary suspension is practicable.

其文职四品以下，武职三品以下各员，仍着户部将本看春季暂停俸银，照数补行给领。
For civil officials below the fourth rank and military officials below the third, however, let the Board pay in full the salaries withheld this spring.

并着发内库帑银五十万两，交部库收存，以备支放俸饷要需。”
Let five hundred thousand taels also be released from the privy treasury into the Board's treasury to meet essential salary and military expenditure.”

王公大臣的俸银，岂肯长此停发？
Would princes and senior ministers tolerate a prolonged interruption of their salaries?

当然要严催各省解款。
Naturally, the provinces would face relentless demands for funds.

王有龄心有警惕，今年的州县官，对于征粮一事，要看得比什么都重。
Wang took warning: this year, local officials must give revenue collection precedence over everything.

“本省的钱粮，全靠杭、嘉、湖三府，湖州尤其是命脉所在。
“Our province depends on Hangzhou, Jiaxing, and Huzhou, with Huzhou its particular lifeblood,” Huang continued.

我跟麟藩台商量，非你去不可。
“Treasurer Lin and I have agreed that you must go.

时逢二百年来未有之变局，朝廷一再申谕，但求实效，不借破格用人。
In this crisis, unparalleled in two hundred years, the court has repeatedly urged practical results and exceptional appointments where necessary.

所以保你老兄署湖州府，我想不至于被驳。”
I therefore doubt it will reject my recommendation that you act as prefect.”

王有龄是早就预备好了的，
Wang was prepared.

听黄宗汉一口气说下来，语声暂停之际，赶快起身请安：

“大人这样子栽培，真是叫人感激涕零，惶恐万分，
“Such favour overwhelms me with gratitude and apprehension.

““要谈报答，只要把公事办妥了就是报答。
“Handle the public business properly, and that will be repayment enough.

湖州地方，与众不同，雪轩兄，

“是！”王有龄紧接着说，
“Yes.

“不过我有下情，还要大人格外体恤。”

“你说。
“Speak.

“这是海运局的公事。”王有龄说，
“It concerns the Sea Transport Bureau.

“我接手还不久，
I've only recently taken charge.

I venture to ask you to remove this concern.”

“What would you have me do?”

“Allow me to hand over only after this affair has been concluded.”

“He...”

“Certainly.

“理当效劳，

“Ah!”

王有龄恍然大悟，

“No wonder!”

黄宗汉一听他不识窍，立刻就端茶送客，

真个翻脸无情，想想也不免寒心。

“No more talk.

这件事办得要快，
Act quickly.

‘药到病除’，不宜耽误！”

“当然，当然。”
“Certainly.”

这个富家子弟是三世单传，
He was the family's sole male heir for three generations.

所以他家上下打点，只想救出一条命来。
They spent money on every possible approach in the hope of saving him.

无奈情真罪实，遇着的又都是清官，
But the facts were clear and the officials honest.

以致钱虽花得不少，毫无作用，只都便宜了中间经手的人。
Their considerable expenditure benefited only the intermediaries.

那富家翁眼睁睁看着要绝后，百万家财，身后将为五服以外的族人所瓜分，无论如何于心不甘。
His father could not bear to see the line end and his enormous fortune eventually divided among distant clansmen beyond the prescribed degrees of mourning.

于是经人指点，备了一份重礼去请教一个以善于出奇计，外号“鬼见愁”的刑名师爷，
On someone's advice, he took a substantial gift to a legal secretary renowned for ingenious schemes, nicknamed “the Man Even Ghosts Fear.”

不得已而求于次，只想他的在狱中的儿子，能够留下一点骨血，哪怕是个女孩子也好，问那刑名师爷，可有办法？
Unable to save his son, he asked for the next best thing: could the prisoner leave a child, even a girl?

办法是有，但不能包养儿子，因为这是任何人所无能为力的。但就照“鬼见愁”的办法，已能令人满意。
There was a method, though naturally nobody could guarantee a son.

他答应可以让那富家子，多活三个月，
The secretary promised three more months of life.

在这三个月中，以重金觅得数名且男的健妇，送到狱中为富家子荐寝。
During that time, several healthy women considered likely to bear sons would be paid generously to visit the prisoner.

当然，狱中是早已打点好的出入无阻，
The jailers had already been bribed, so access was unrestricted.

每天黎明有人在监狱后门迎接，接着健妇送到家供养。
Each dawn someone would meet the women at the back gate and take them to the wealthy household, where they were maintained.

事先已讲明白，要在他家住几个月，
It was agreed beforehand that they would stay several months.

若无喜信，送一笔钱放回，有了喜信就一直住下去，直到分娩为止，那时或去或留，另有协议。
Those who did not conceive would receive money and leave; those who did would remain until the birth, with further arrangements made afterwards.


这样过了十几天，刑部的复文到了，是“钉封文书”，
Yet after only a dozen days, the ministry's reply arrived in a cord-fastened sealed dispatch.

一望便知是核准了“斩立决”。
One glance showed that it authorised immediate execution.

“慢来，慢来！”胡雪岩打断秦寿门的话问道：
“Wait!” Hu interrupted.

“不是说可以活三个月？
“Didn't you say he had three months?

何以前后一个月不到？
It hasn't even been one.”

““少安毋躁，”
“Be patient.”

秦寿门笑道，
Qin smiled.

“当然另有道理，
“There is naturally another explanation.

不然何以鬼见了都愁？”他接着又讲……
Otherwise, why would even ghosts fear the man?” He continued the story.


既称“斩立决”，等“钉封文书”一到，就得“出红差”，
An immediate-execution order required the execution party to be assembled as soon as the sealed dispatch arrived.

知县升堂，传齐三班六房和刽子手，把犯人从监狱里提了出来，
The magistrate took his seat, summoned the yamen personnel and executioner, and had the prisoner brought from jail.

当堂开拆文书。打开来一看，知县愣住了，
He opened the dispatch in court—and froze.

封套上的姓名不错，里面的文书，完全不对，姓名不对，案情不对，地方也不对，
The name on the cover was correct, but everything inside was wrong: the name, the case, the place.

应该发到贵州的，发到浙江来了。
A document meant for Guizhou had reached Zhejiang.

没有核准斩立决的文书，如何可以杀人？
Without proper authorisation, how could he execute the man?

犯人依旧送回监狱，文书退了回去。
The prisoner returned to jail and the document was sent back.

杭州到京师，再慢也不过二十天，但是要等贵州把那弄错了的文书送回刑部，
Even a slow journey from Hangzhou to the capital took no more than twenty days, but the ministry had to wait for Guizhou to return the other misdirected document.

“云贵半爿天”，
Yunnan and Guizhou seemed half a world away.

一来一往就三个月都不止，
The round trip would take more than three months.

便宜了贵州的那犯人，平白多活了几个月。
The prisoner there benefited too, unexpectedly gaining several months of life.

“这不用说，当然是在部里做了手脚？”王有龄问。
“Naturally, someone at the ministry had arranged it?” Wang asked.

“是的。”秦寿门答道，
“Yes.

“运动了一个刑部主事。
A secretary at the Board of Punishments was persuaded.

这算是疏忽，罚俸三个月，不过几十两银子，
It counted as negligence, punishable by three months' salary—only a few dozen taels.

但就这样一举手之劳的‘疏忽’，非一千银子不办。”
But this effortless act of ‘negligence’ cost a thousand taels to arrange.”

“这是好事！为人延嗣，绝大阴功，还有一千两银子进帐。”胡雪岩笑道：
“A good deed!” Hu laughed. “To preserve a family line is great hidden merit, and brings a thousand taels besides.

“何乐不为？”
Why not do it?”


“其奈坏法何？”秦寿门说，
“But what of the damage to the law?” Qin asked.

“倘或查封、抄家的文书，也是这么横生枝节，国库的损失，谁来认赔？”
“If orders for sealing property or confiscating an estate suffered similar complications, who would compensate the treasury?”

“若有其事，也算疏忽？”
“Would that count as negligence too?”

“此是何等大事，不容疏忽也不会疏忽。
“Such important matters admit no negligence, nor would it occur by accident.

国法不外乎人情，
Law cannot be separated from human motives.

所以听讼执法，只从人情上去揣摩，疑窦立见。
When hearing cases and enforcing it, examine those motives and the doubts become apparent.

譬如说某人向来精细，而某事忽然疏忽，此一疏忽又有大出入，
Suppose a habitually meticulous man suddenly makes a mistake, and the mistake makes an enormous difference.

其事便可疑了。
That is suspicious.

又譬如‘例案’，向来如此办理，
Or take a class of cases always decided one way.

而主管其事的忽然说，这么办是冤枉的，驳了下来，甚至已定谳的案子，把它翻案。
Suddenly the responsible official declares that practice unjust, rejects it, or even overturns a final judgement.

试问，这一案冤枉，以前同样的案子就不冤枉？
If this case was unjust, were the earlier identical cases not unjust too?

何以不翻？
Why not reverse them?

只从这上面去细想一想，其中出了什么鬼？不言可知。”
Consider that carefully, and the hidden interference becomes obvious.”

听这番话，足见得秦寿门是个极明白事理的人。
These remarks showed Qin's clear understanding of affairs.

王有龄当然觉得欣慰。
Wang was naturally pleased.


但刑名一道对县官的前程，关系太大，
But legal affairs mattered enormously to a magistrate's career.

老百姓对父母官的信服与否，首先也就是从刑名上看。
They were also the first basis upon which the people judged their parent-official.

只要年成好，地方富庶，钱粮的浮收及各种摊派，稍微过分些，都还能容忍，
With good harvests and a prosperous district, they might tolerate somewhat excessive additions to taxes and miscellaneous levies.

若是审理官司，有理的一方受屈，无理的一方赢了，即或是无心之失，也会招致老百生极大的不满，说起来必是“贪赃枉法”。
If the rightful party lost a lawsuit and the wrongdoer won, however, even an innocent mistake would provoke deep resentment and accusations of corruption and injustice.

所以王有龄对秦寿门看得比杨用之重，
Wang therefore valued Qin more highly than Yang.

事先跟胡雪岩说好了的，自己不便频频质疑，要他借闲谈多发问，借以考一考秦寿门的本事，
He had agreed with Hu beforehand that, since he could not repeatedly question his new adviser himself, Hu should test him through conversation.

此时便又递了个眼色过去。
He now gave another signal.

于是胡雪岩装得似懂非懂的样子，用好奇而仰慕的语气问道，“都说刑名老夫子一支笔厉害，一个字的出入，就是一家人的祸福，
Hu adopted an expression of partial understanding and asked with curious admiration, “Everyone says a legal master's pen is formidable: one altered character can determine a family's fortune.

又说‘天下文章在幕府’，
They also say the finest writing comes from secretaries' offices.

我问过人，也就不出个所以然。
I've asked about it without receiving a clear explanation.

今天遇见秦老夫子，一定可以教一教我了！”
Today you can surely enlighten me.”

又捧刑名师爷又捧他本人，这顶双料的高帽子，秦寿门戴得很舒服，
This double compliment, to his profession and himself, pleased Qin.

致且酒到半酣，谈兴正好，便矜持地笑道：
Half warmed by wine and enjoying the discussion, he smiled with restrained pride.

“读书万卷不读律，致君尧舜知何术？
“‘Read ten thousand volumes but neglect the law—how will you guide your sovereign to the virtue of Yao and Shun?’

‘所谓’天下文章，出于幕府‘，言其实用而已，
The claim about writing refers to its practical use.

至于一个字的出入，关乎一家人祸福，这话倒也不假。
As for a single character determining a family's fortune, that is true.

不过，舞文弄墨，我辈大忌。
But manipulating words is our profession's great taboo.

总之，无事不可生事，有事不可怕事。”
In short: where no trouble exists, don't create it; where trouble arises, don't fear it.”

在座的人连连点头，
Everyone nodded.

吴委员肚千里有些墨水，尤其觉得“舞文弄墨，我辈大忌”八个字，近乎见道之言，因而说道。“我也要请教！”
Wu, who had some literary education, particularly admired the warning against manipulating words and asked for an explanation.

“先说无事不可生事……”
“First, not creating trouble where none exists...” Qin began.


秦寿门讲了个故事作例证：
He gave an example.

曾有一省的巡抚与藩司不和，巡抚必欲去之而后快，
A governor disliked his provincial treasurer and wanted him removed.

苦于那藩司既清廉又能干，找不着他的错处。
Unfortunately, the treasurer was both honest and capable, leaving no fault to seize upon.

后来找到一个机会，
At last an opportunity came.

文庙丁祭，那藩司正好重伤风，行札的时候，咳个不停，
During a sacrifice at the Confucian temple, the treasurer had a severe cold and coughed continually through the ceremony.

巡抚抓住他这个错，跟幕友商量，
The governor discussed this lapse with his secretary.

那幕友顺从东家的意思，舞文弄墨，大张旗鼓，奏劾那藩司失仪不敬。
Obeying his employer's wishes, the secretary used elaborate wording to make a great affair of it, impeaching the treasurer for ceremonial misconduct and disrespect.

凡有弹劾，朝廷通常总要查了再说，
The court usually investigated before acting upon an accusation.

情节重大则由京里特派钦差，驰驿查办。
Grave cases brought a special commissioner from the capital by express relay.

类此事件，往往交“将军”或者“学政”查报。
Matters of this kind were often referred to the garrison general or provincial education commissioner.

那一省没有驻防的将军，但学政是每一省都有的，
That province had no resident garrison general, but every province had an education commissioner.

这位学政文庙丁祭也在场，知道藩司的失仪，情非得已。
He too had attended the sacrifice and knew that the treasurer's conduct was involuntary.

就算真的失仪，至多事后教训一顿，
Even if it amounted to a breach of decorum, a reprimand afterwards should have sufficed.

又何至于毛举细故，专折参劾？
Why magnify a trifle into a special memorial of impeachment?

由于这一份不满的心情，那学政不但要帮藩司的忙，还要给巡抚吃点苦头。
Resenting this, he wanted not only to help the treasurer but to make the governor suffer.

但是他不便公然指摘巡抚，让朝延疑心他有意袒护藩司，所以措词甚难。
Yet an open criticism might lead the court to suspect partiality, making the wording difficult.

这位学政未曾中举成进士以前，原学过刑名，
Before passing the provincial and metropolitan examinations, the education commissioner had trained in legal work.

想了半天，从巡抚原奏的“亲见”二字中，欣然有悟，
After much thought, he found inspiration in the governor's claim to have “personally witnessed” the offence.

随即提笔复奏，他说他丁祭那天，虽也在场，但无法复查这一案，因为他“位列前班，理无后顾”，不知道藩司失仪了没有？
His reply said that, although present, he could not verify it: “My place was in the forward rank; propriety forbade looking behind.”

就这轻描淡写八个字，军机大臣一看便知道，是巡抚有意找藩司的麻烦，
Those unobtrusive words made the matter plain to the Grand Councillors.

因为行礼时巡抚也是跪在藩司前面，
The governor too had knelt ahead of the treasurer.

如何知道后面的藩司失仪？
How could he have seen misconduct behind him?

照此说来，是巡抚抚失仪往后面看了，才发现藩司失仪。
He must himself have broken decorum by turning round.

结果两个人都有处分。
Both officials were punished.

原被告各打五十板，自然是原告失面子，被告虽受罚，心里是痛快的。
With plaintiff and defendant treated alike, the plaintiff lost face, while the defendant, though punished, felt satisfied.


“这真是‘世不本无事，庸人自扰之’。”吴委员说，
“Truly, there was no trouble until a foolish man made it,” Wu said.

“坏在那巡抚的幕友不能痛切规劝。”
“The fault lay in the governor's secretary failing to remonstrate firmly.”

“这话说中的症结所在。”
“You've identified the essential point.”

秦寿门向王有龄看了一眼，
Qin glanced at Wang.

“我辈既蒙东家不弃，处事自有必不可摇的宗旨，
“Once an employer has honoured us with his trust, we must hold to unshakable principles.

一时依从，留下后患，自误误人，
Compliance for a moment that leaves trouble for the future harms both him and us.

千万不可。
It must never happen.

只是忠言往往逆耳，难碍有几位东家没有脾气。”
Unfortunately, loyal advice often jars upon the ear, and few employers have no temper.”

“老大子请放心！”王有龄急忙表明态度，
“Rest assured,” Wang promptly declared.

“我奉托了老替子，将来刑名方面，自然都请老夫子作主。”
“Having entrusted the post to you, I shall naturally leave legal affairs to your judgement.”

“有东翁这句话，我可以放心放手了。
“With that assurance, I can act confidently.

今天我借花献佛，先告个罪，将来要请东翁恕我专擅之罪。”
Taking advantage of this hospitality, let me apologise in advance and ask you to forgive any future appearance of overstepping my authority.”

说着他举杯相敬，
He raised his cup.

王有龄欣然接受，
Wang gladly accepted the toast.

宾主如鱼得水，在座的人亦都觉得很愉快。
Host and guest were perfectly at ease, and everyone shared their pleasure.

轰然祝饮，闹过一阵，重拾中断的话题。
After a noisy round of drinking, they returned to the interrupted subject.


“现在要谈有事不可怕事。”吴委员提高了声音说道，
“Now for not fearing trouble when it comes,” Wu said loudly.

“索性也请老夫子举例以明之。”
“Please give us an example of that too.”

秦寿门略略沉吟了一下说“有事不可怕事者，是要沉得住气，
Qin considered briefly. “It means keeping one's composure.

气稳则心定，心定则神闲，
A steady temper settles the mind; a settled mind allows clear thought.

死棋肚里才会出仙着。
Then an apparently lost position may yield an inspired move.

大致古今律法，不论如何细密，总有漏洞，
However detailed the law, ancient or modern, there are always gaps.

事理也是一样，
Reasoning is similar.

有时道理不通，大家习焉不察，也就过去了，
Sometimes an argument is unsound, but familiarity keeps everyone from noticing.

而看来不可思议之事，细想一想竟是道理极通，无可驳诘。
Something apparently inconceivable may, on examination, prove entirely reasonable and beyond refutation.

所以只要心定神闲，想得广、想得透，蹈瑕乘隙，避重就轻，大事化小，小事化无，亦并不难。
With composure, breadth, and depth of thought, one can find the opening, avoid the heavier consequence, and turn a great problem into a small one, then into none.

刚才提到‘ 钉封文书’，我就说个钉封文书的妙事。
Since we mentioned cord-fastened dispatches, let me tell you an ingenious story about one.

在座各位，“他看着王有龄问道，”想来东翁一定见过这玩意？
I suppose my employer has seen them?”

““见过。”王有龄答道，
“Yes,” Wang replied.

“原来钉封文书，用意在示机密，
“Originally their fastening signified confidentiality.

亦不光是州县处决犯人非受领钉封文书不可，访拿要犯也用钉封文书。
They were used not only for execution orders but for arresting important criminals.

久而久之，成为具文，封套上钉个‘瓣’，用细麻绳一拴，
In time the practice became a formality: a flap fixed to the cover, tied with thin hemp cord.

人人可以拆开来看，
Anyone could open it.

最机密变成最不机密，真正是始料所不及！”
The most confidential document became the least secure—an outcome surely never intended.”

“一点都不错。
“Exactly.

这件妙事，毛病就出在‘人人可以拆开来看’上面。
This story turns upon that freedom to open them.

钉封文书按驿站走，
Such dispatches travelled by post relay.

每经一县，都要加盖大印。
Each county through which they passed added its official seal.

公事过手，遇着好事的县大爷，就拆开来看一看依旧封好。
An inquisitive magistrate handling one might open it, read it, and tie it up again.

有这么一位县太爷，鸦片大瘾，
One such magistrate was a heavy opium smoker.

每天晚上在签押房里，躺在烟铺上看公事。
Every evening he read official papers while reclining beside his pipe.

这天也是拆了一封钉封文书看，迷迷糊糊，把那通文书在烟灯上饶掉了，”
One night, half dazed, he opened a dispatch and accidentally burned its contents over the opium lamp.”


这一下，那县太爷才惊醒过来，
That brought him sharply to his senses.

烧掉了钉封文书，是件不得了的事！
Destroying a confidential dispatch was an appalling offence.

急忙移樽就教，到刑名师爷那里求援。
He hurried to seek his legal secretary's help.

“封套在不在？”那刑名师爷问。
“Do you still have the cover?”

“封套还在。”
“Yes.”

“那不要紧！
“Then don't worry.

请东翁交了给我。顺便带大印来。”
Give it to me, and bring the official seal.”

县太爷照办不误，
The magistrate obeyed.

等封套取到，那刑名师爷取张白纸折好，往里一塞，拴好麻绳，盖上大印，交了回去。
The secretary folded a blank sheet, put it inside, tied the cord, stamped the seal, and handed it back.

“交驿递发下一站！”
“Send it by relay to the next station.”

“老夫子，”县太爷迟疑地问道：“这行吗？
“Will that do?” the magistrate asked hesitantly.

下一站发觉了怎么办？”
“What if the next station discovers it?”

“东家，请你自己去想。”那刑名师爷说，
“Think for yourself.

“换了你是下一县，
Suppose you're the next magistrate.

打开来一看，里头是张白纸，
You open it and find a blank sheet.

请问你怎么办？”
What would you do?”

秦寿门把那个故事讲到此处，不需再往下说，
Qin needed to go no further.

在座的人应都明白，
Everyone understood.

显然的，有人发现了是张白纸，也不敢声张，更不敢多事退回去。因为倘或如此，便先犯了窃视机密文书的过失，
Anyone finding the blank page would dare neither announce the discovery nor return it and cause trouble: that would first expose his own offence of opening a confidential document.

这与那学政的“位列前班，理无后顾”八字，有异曲同功之妙。
The reasoning resembled the education commissioner's statement that his forward position forbade looking behind.


“刑名虽是‘法家’，也要多读老庄之书，才能有些妙悟。”王有龄感叹着说，
“Though legal advisers belong to the school of law, they should also read Laozi and Zhuangzi to gain such insights,” Wang reflected.

“人不能有所蔽，有所蔽则能见秋毫，不见舆薪。
“A man must not allow his vision to be obstructed, seeing an autumn hair but missing a cartload of firewood.

世上明明有许多极浅显的道理，偏偏有人看不破，
There are so many obvious truths that people nevertheless fail to see.

这是哪里说起？”
How can one explain it?”

这番议论一发，便把话题引了开去。
The discussion moved to other matters.

闲谈到夕阳衔山，方始散席，依旧荡桨回城。
They talked until sunset, then left the table and rowed back to the city.

第二请钱谷师爷杨用之，在西湖里的一条画舫上设席，陪客依旧是胡雪岩和周、吴两委员。
The following day, Wang entertained the revenue secretary Yang Yongzhi aboard a pleasure boat on West Lake, again accompanied by Hu, Zhou, and Wu.

由于阜康钱庄创设以后，预计是要用湖州府和乌程县解省的公款，作为资本，
Fukang was expected to use Huzhou Prefecture's and Wucheng County's public remittances to the provincial treasury as capital.

这与钱谷师爷有密切的关系，因此胡雪岩对杨用之，特别笼络。
Since the revenue secretary was closely involved, Hu made a special effort to cultivate him.

杨用之赋性忠厚老实，是最容易对付的人，
Yang was straightforward and honest, and therefore easy to manage.

以胡雪岩的手腕，把他摆布得服服帖帖，颇有相见恨晚之感。
Hu soon made him wholly accommodating, with the feeling that they should have met long before.

其实胡雪岩的手腕也很简单，
In fact, the method was simple.

凡是忠厚老实的人，都喜欢别人向他请教，而他自己亦往往知无不言，言无不尽。
Straightforward men liked to be consulted and generally shared all they knew.

胡雪岩会说话，更会听话，
Hu spoke well, but listened still better.

不管那人是如何地语言无味，他能一本正经，两眼注视，仿佛听得极感兴味似地，
However dull another man's words, he could attend gravely, eyes fixed upon him, as though fascinated.

同时，他也真的是在听，紧要关头补充一两语。引申一两义，
He really did listen, adding a remark or developing a point at the crucial moment.

使得滔滔不绝者，有莫逆于心之快，自然觉得投机而成至交。
The speaker then felt deeply understood, and congenial conversation became close friendship.


杨用之的本事不怎么好，但以他的性格随和，所以交游甚广，
Yang was not exceptionally able, but his agreeable temperament had given him many acquaintances.

加以遇着胡雪岩，不知不觉地提起了谈兴，讲了许多时人的轶闻，
Hu's company drew him into an animated account of contemporary figures.

最后谈到湖州府的人物，他提起一个人叫钱江，问王有龄认不认识？

“我听说过他，
“I've heard of him.

是湖州府长兴县人，

曾跟我们福建的林文忠公，一起遣戍伊犁，由此出名。

听说他是个奇士。
They say he's an extraordinary man.

想来林文忠公所赏识的人物，总不会错的。”
Someone appreciated by Lord Lin can't be ordinary.

王有龄问道：“怎么老夫子忽然提到这个人，
Why do you mention him?

莫非有他的新闻？”
Is there news?”

“也好说是新闻。
“You could call it news.

“喔，要请教。”
“Please explain.”

“这要从一位达官谈起，
“We must begin with a senior official.

雷以諴其人，东翁总知道？”
You know Lei Yixian?”

“知道。”王有龄说，
“Yes.

“此公湖北人，
A Hubei man.

以左副御史会同河道总督巡视黄河口岸。
As senior vice censor-in-chief, he joined the Director-General of River Conservancy to inspect the Yellow River's outlet.

前些日子看邸抄，说他自请讨贼，

现在募了一万人，驻军江北高邮，扼守扬州东南，
He has recruited ten thousand men, stationed them at Gaoyou north of the Yangtze, and is guarding the southeastern approach to Yangzhou.

很打了几场胜仗。”
He's won several battles.”

“是的，
“Exactly.

钱江就在他幕府里。”杨用之说，
Qian Jiang serves on his staff.

“有兵无饷，仗是打不下去的，
Troops without pay cannot fight.

朝廷的宗旨，反正只要你能募兵筹饷，自己去想办法，无不赞成的。
The court's position is that anyone able to recruit men and raise funds should devise his own means, and it will approve.

听说钱江现在为雷军划一策，在水陆要冲，设局设卡，
I hear Qian has proposed collection offices and checkpoints at important land and water routes.

凡行商经过，看他所带货物，估价抽税，大致千取其一，
Travelling merchants are assessed on the value of their goods, generally one part in a thousand.

称为‘厘捐’，
The levy is called lijin.

Local shops pay on the same basis.

收入颇为可观，听说各省都有仿照的意思。

只是此法病商，朝廷或者不许。”

This news, and Yang's view that the proposed lijin would harm merchants, gave Wang his opportunity.

聘用刑、钱两幕友，他跟胡雪岩曾仔细谈过，
He and Hu had discussed the two secretaries carefully.

刑名是外行，非倚托秦寿门不可，所以先要考一考他的本事。
Law was outside Wang's expertise, so he must rely upon Qin and first test his ability.

钱谷则王有龄自己就很精通，
Revenue administration was different: Wang understood it well.

但幕友的传统，向来独立办事，不喜东家干涉，
Yet secretaries traditionally worked independently and disliked interference.

平和的还表面上有所敷衍，专断的根本就置之不理，

所以胡雪岩设计，由他自己用感情来笼络杨用之，而王有龄则要拿点本事给他看看，
Hu's plan was therefore to win Yang's affection while Wang demonstrated enough knowledge to command respect.

这样双管齐下，让杨用之怀德畏威，把他收服，才能指挥如意。
With gratitude and apprehension working together, Yang could be brought under effective direction.

所以王有龄听了他的话，觉得不妨趁些机会，展示所学。
Wang seized the moment to display his learning.

“ ‘病商’恐未必！”他一开口就是辩驳语气，
“I'm not sure it necessarily harms trade,” he began in an argumentative tone.

“本朝的赋税制度，异于前代，
“Our dynasty's tax system differs from those of earlier times.

一遇用兵之时，必须另筹军费，
Whenever war comes, military funds must be raised separately.

以我看，开办‘厘捐’，比较起来，还不失为利多害少的好办法。”
Comparatively speaking, lijin may still offer more benefit than harm.”

这笼统一句话，是做文章的一个“帽子”，
This broad proposition served as the introduction to an essay.

王有龄既有炫耀之意，便得从头讲起。
Intent upon demonstrating his knowledge, Wang began at the beginning.

自古以来，国家岁收的主要项目，就是地了与钱粮，
From ancient times, the chief annual revenues had been land and poll taxes and grain levies.

明朗未年不断“加派”，搞得民不聊生，
Repeated additional assessments late in the Ming had made life intolerable.

庄稼人苦得要死，到最后只好弃地而逃，
Farmers suffered so greatly that they abandoned their fields and fled.

此为“流寇”猖獗，终以亡明的一大关键。
This was a major cause of the spread of roaming rebels and, ultimately, the dynasty's fall.


清兵入关，到圣祖平定三藩之乱，始得奠定国基。
After the Qing entered China, the Kangxi emperor's suppression of the Three Feudatories finally secured the dynasty's foundations.

鉴于前朝之失，颁发“永不加赋”的诏令，
Taking warning from the Ming, he issued an edict promising never to increase taxation.

此为清朝的一大仁政，亦为满族得以长主中原的一大凭借。
This was one of the dynasty's great benevolent measures and a principal reason the Manchus could long rule the central plains.

后世诸帝，对圣祖的这个诏谕，信守不坠。
Later emperors faithfully maintained it.

此外国家岁收，还有关税、盐课两项，但地丁占岁收总额的三分之二，
Customs and salt duties also supplied annual revenue, but land and poll taxes represented two-thirds of the total.

既有永不加赋的限制，则岁收就有了定额。
With an undertaking never to increase them, annual receipts were effectively fixed.

风调雨顺、刀兵不起的太平岁月，固然可以支应，
They could cover expenditure in peaceful years of favourable weather.

但一遇用兵，额外的军费负担，即无着落，
But war brought extra military costs with no provision.

倘或水旱年荒，一面要减免丁漕，一面要办赈济，收入减少，支出增加，
Drought or flood required both tax remissions and famine relief: income fell while expenditure rose.

又如何应付？
How could that be managed?

再如刀兵水旱一齐来，火上加油，两面发烧，更是件不得了的事。
If war and natural disaster coincided, both sides of the crisis intensified.


“这有两个办法弥补，一靠平时蓄积。”王有龄从容议论：
“There are two ways to meet the shortfall,” Wang continued calmly. “The first is savings accumulated in ordinary times.

“虽然天子富有四海，国家收入与宫廷收入，还是有区分的。
Though the emperor possesses all within the four seas, state revenue and palace revenue are separate.

这个制度从汉朝就很完备了，‘大司农’掌国家度支，‘少府’管天子的私财。
The distinction was already well developed under the Han: the Grand Minister of Agriculture managed public expenditure, while the Privy Treasurer managed the sovereign's private wealth.

私财有余，国币不足，国家必乱。
If the ruler has a surplus while the state lacks funds, disorder follows.

宋太祖平服十国，所得金银珍宝虽输于内府，但另行封存，称为‘封桩银’。
When the founding Song emperor subdued the Ten Kingdoms, their gold, silver, and jewels went into the palace treasury, but were separately sealed as a special reserve.

他的打算是积到相当数目，要把‘燕云十六州’买回来。
He intended to accumulate enough to buy back the Sixteen Prefectures of Yan and Yun.

可惜徽宗不肖，以内府所积，用来起‘民岳’，才有金兵入寇之事。
Unfortunately, the unworthy Huizong spent the palace reserves on the Genyue pleasure garden, after which the Jin invaded.

前明更不必说，户部穷得要命，宫内蓄积如山，到最后，白白便宜了‘流寇’。
The Ming needs no elaboration: its Board of Revenue was desperately poor while palace wealth piled high, only to fall into the rebels' hands.

本朝就不同了，
Our dynasty is different.

蓄只于国库而非内务府。”
Reserves are held in the state treasury, not the Imperial Household Department.”


接着王有龄便举了几个户部存银的数目，康熙四十八年到过五千万两，最后剩下八百万两，但雍正十三年的极力整顿，到乾隆即位时，库存到了前所未有的六千万两的巨数，以后乾隆四十六年，到过七千万两。
Wang then cited treasury balances: fifty million taels in Kangxi's forty-eighth year, falling to eight million by the end; after thirteen years of vigorous reform under Yongzheng, an unprecedented sixty million at Qianlong's accession; and seventy million in Qianlong's forty-sixth year.

但嘉庆以后就不行了，到道光朝更是每况愈下。
After Jiaqing, however, the position deteriorated, worsening further under Daoguang.

“先帝崩逝当时，户部存银八百万两，
“When the late emperor died, the Board held eight million taels.

这三年来的数目不详。
I don't know the figures for the last three years.

洪杨军兴以来，用财如流水，
Since Hong and Yang rose, money has flowed like water.

想来现在正是开国以来最穷的时候。”
I imagine the dynasty has never been poorer.”


这一番夹叙夹议的谈论，不但周、吴等人有茅塞顿开之感，就是杨用之也觉得长了一番见闻。
The mixture of narrative and analysis enlightened not only Zhou and Wu but Yang himself.

钱谷一道虽是他的专业，却只了解一隅之地的财政，朝廷大藏，十分隔膜，
Revenue was his profession, yet he knew only local finance and little of the central treasury.

现在听王有龄讲得头头是道，心里便有这样一个想法：这位东翁，莫道他是捐班出身，肚子里着实有些货色。
Hearing Wang explain it so coherently, he thought that, purchased qualification or not, his employer had considerable learning.

他想到了王有龄的出身，王有龄恰好也要谈到捐班，
At that very moment Wang turned to purchased office.

“弥补国用不足，再有一个办法是靠捐纳的收入。”他说，
“The second way to meet a public shortfall is through contributions paid for official qualifications.

“捐官的制度，起于汉朝，即所谓‘纳赀为郎’。
The system began under the Han, when money could obtain a position as a palace gentleman.

此后历代都有，但不如本朝的盛行。”
Later dynasties retained it, but none used it as extensively as ours.”

接着，王有龄便细谈清朝捐纳制度演变的经过，以及对中枢岁收的关系。
He described its development and connection with central revenue.

捐纳实缺虽由康熙为三藩之乱，筹措军费而起，
Contributions for actual appointments had begun under Kangxi to fund the war against the Three Feudatories.

捐例之滥，始于嘉庆朝，
Excessive use began under Jiaqing.

它的收入常为岁收的一半，嘉庆七年那一年，更高达岁收总额百分之八十以上。
The proceeds often equalled half the year's revenue, and in his seventh year exceeded eighty per cent.

“捐例一滥，其弊不可胜言。”王有龄泰然说道，
“Once the system is abused, its evils are beyond description,” Wang said evenly.

“Though I myself entered by purchase, I can hardly respect that class of official.

只要有钱，不管什么胸无点墨的人，都可以做官。
Anyone with money, however ignorant, can obtain office.

做官既要先花本钱，那就跟做生意一样，
Having first invested capital, he treats administration like a business.

一补上实缺，先要捞回本息。
Once appointed, he must recover principal and interest.

How can clean government result?”


“这也不可一概而论。”吴委员说，
“That cannot be said of everyone,” Wu objected.

“赴试登进，自是正途，
“Advancement by examination is naturally the proper path.

但‘场中莫论文’，
But examination success doesn't depend on writing alone.

要靠‘一命、二运、三风水’，所以怀才不遇的也多的是。
Fate, luck, and favourable circumstances play their part, leaving many capable men unrecognised.

捐例开了方便之门，让他们有个发挥机会，不致埋没人才，
Purchase opens a door through which they can exercise their talents instead of being buried.

That too is a great service.”

这是在暗中恭维王有龄，他当然听得懂，
The compliment to Wang was implicit and understood.

He saw no need to disclaim it.

“象兄弟这种情形到底不多。”他说，
“Cases like mine are uncommon.

“纵有一利，奈有百害何？

如今为了军费，越发广开已滥的捐例，搞得满街是官，

“Your view is entirely sound,” Yang answered, sincerely convinced.

“Commercial taxes in our dynasty are not heavy.

杂赋中的牙帖税、当税、牲畜税以外，买卖的商税，只有买别地货物到店发卖的‘落地税’，也就是‘坐税’。

A moderate additional lijin levy would not be excessive.”

“就是这话罗！”王有龄口中这样在说，心中却己想到厘捐是否亦可在浙江开办？

一场议论，算是有了结果。
The discussion had achieved its purpose.

胡雪岩换了个话题，
Hu changed the subject.

“那怎么称做‘奇士’呢？”吴委员笑道，

“He proposed another measure now being adopted across the provinces.”

杨用之忽然看着胡雪岩说，

“You could make use of it yourself.”

“本省亦已照样进行。

雪岩兄大可捐个前程。”
You could purchase a qualification.”


这话倒把胡雪岩说动了，
That appealed to Hu.

这几个月他在官场打了几个滚，深知“身分”二字的重要，倒不是为了炫耀，而是为了方便，

是个“白丁”，便处处有格格不入之感，
As an untitled commoner, he felt out of place everywhere.

Friends could address one another as brothers, but with unfamiliar officials he risked either diminishing himself or offending them.

实在是一大苦事。
It was a serious difficulty.

因此，这天晚上他特地跟王有龄去商量。王有龄自然赞成：
That evening he specially consulted Wang, who naturally approved.

“我早就劝你快办了！
“I've urged you to do it for ages!

我真不知道你什么意思？一直拖着。”
I don't know why you've kept delaying.”

“都是为了没工夫，”胡雪岩说，
“No time.

“这件事麻烦得很，
The business is troublesome.

费辰光不说，还有层层挑剔需索，把人的兴致都消磨光了。
Apart from the time it takes, there are objections and demands for fees at every stage, enough to destroy anyone's enthusiasm.

象现在这样。随捐随发，一手交钱，一手取照，自然又当别论。”
Immediate issue, with money handed over and the certificate received in return, is different.”

“需索还是会有的。
“There will still be demands.

讲是讲‘随捐随发’，到底也没有那么快。
‘Immediate’ won't be quite so immediate.

不过，部照不必到部里去领，当然快得多。”
But avoiding collection from the ministry itself will certainly make it much faster.”


“于此可见，凡事总要动脑筋。
“That shows how necessary it is to think.

说到理财，到处都是财源。”
In finance, opportunities are everywhere.”

胡雪岩又得到启示：
Hu drew another lesson.

“一句话，不管是做官的对老百姓，做生意的对主顾。 你要人荷包里的钱，就要把人伺候得舒服，才肯心甘情愿掏荷包。”
“Whether an official dealing with the people or a merchant dealing with customers, if you want the money in their purses, make them comfortable enough to open those purses willingly.”

“这话有道理。”
“There's truth in that.”

王有龄深深点点，
Wang nodded thoughtfully.

“我这趟到湖州，也要想办法把老百姓‘伺候’得舒舒服服，好叫他们高高兴兴来完钱粮。”
“At Huzhou, I too must find a way to ‘serve’ the people comfortably, so they'll gladly pay their taxes.”

“其实老百姓也很好问候，
“They're easy enough to serve.

不打官腔，实事求是，老百姓自会说你是好官。”
Avoid official bluster and deal with realities; they'll call you a good official.”

胡雪岩又谈到他自己的事，
Hu returned to his own business.

“雪公，你看我捐个什么班子？”
“What qualification should I purchase?”

“州县。”王有龄毫不考虑地答说，
“Department or county magistrate,” Wang answered without hesitation.

“这件事你托杨用之好了。”
“Entrust it to Yang Yongzhi.”

胡雪岩受了他的教，第二天特地具个柬帖，把杨用之请了在馆子里小酌。
Taking his advice, Hu sent Yang a formal invitation the next day and entertained him at a restaurant.


酒过三巡，谈起正事，
After three rounds of wine, they discussed the matter.

杨用之一诺无辞，而且声叫：
Yang readily agreed and made an announcement.
