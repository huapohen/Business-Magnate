第七章｜CHAPTER SEVEN


这是胡雪岩第一次听见老张谈到他女儿，
This was the first time Hu had heard Old Zhang speak of his daughter.

“叫”这个如何，“叫”那个如何，口气倒象是佣人听小姐的吩咐，
She had “told” one person to do this and another to do that; his tone suggested a servant carrying out his young mistress's instructions.

不免有些诧异，但也明了阿珠在他家，真正是颗掌上明珠，
Hu was a little surprised, but he also understood that A’zhu truly was the cherished jewel of the household.

她父母是无话不听的。
Her parents did whatever she asked.

“胡老爷，”老张又说，“我备了只小划子，划了你去。
“Mr Hu,” Old Zhang continued, “I've brought a little skiff to row you over.

这里也实在太闹了，
It's much too noisy here.

连我都厌烦，
Even I find it tiresome.

城河里清静得多。”
The moat is far quieter.”

于是下桥上船，
They went down from the bridge and boarded the skiff.

向南穿过万安桥，折而往东，出了水关，就是极宽的护城河，
Heading south beneath Wan'an Bridge, they turned east and passed through the water gate into the broad moat.

一面城墙，一面菜畦，
On one side stood the city wall; on the other lay vegetable plots.

空阔无人。
The space was open and deserted.

端午将近的黄梅天，蒸闷不堪，
The Dragon Boat Festival was approaching, and the plum rains brought oppressive, steaming heat.

所以一到这地方，胡雪岩顿觉精神一爽，
As soon as they reached this place, Hu felt refreshed.

脱口赞了句：“阿珠倒真会挑地方！”
“A’zhu certainly knows how to choose a spot!” he exclaimed.

“喏！”老张指着胡雪岩身后说：
“There!” Old Zhang pointed behind him.

“我们的船停在那里。”
“That's where our boat is.”

船泊在一株柳树下面。那株杨柳极大，而且斜出临水，茂密的柳绿，覆盖了大半条船，
It lay beneath an enormous willow leaning over the water, its dense green foliage sheltering more than half the vessel.

不仔细看，还真不大容易发现。
Without looking carefully, one might easily have missed it.

胡雪岩未到那条船上，已觉心旷神怡，
Even before reaching the boat, Hu felt his heart lift.

把一脑子的海运局、钱庄之类的念头，忘了个干净。
Every thought of the Sea Transport Bureau and the bank vanished.

倒转身来，一直望着柳下的船。
He turned round and kept his eyes on the boat beneath the willow.


那条船上有也有在望，自然是阿珠。
Someone aboard was looking out too—A’zhu, naturally.

越行越近，看得越清楚，
As they approached, he could see her more clearly.

她穿一件浆洗得极挺拓的月白竹布衫，外面套一件玄色软缎的背心，
She wore a pale blue cotton blouse, washed and starched until it was crisp, with a waistcoat of soft black satin.

一根漆黑的长辫子，仍然是她改不掉的习惯，把辫梢捞地手里捻弄着。
As always, she held the end of her long black braid and twisted it between her fingers, a habit she could never break.

小船划近，船上的伙计帮忙把他扶上大船，
When the skiff drew alongside, the boat hand helped him aboard the larger vessel.

只见阿珠回和身向后梢喊道：“娘，好难请的贵客请到了！”
A’zhu turned towards the stern and called, “Mother, our distinguished guest has arrived at last! Such trouble to persuade him!”

阿珠的娘在后悄上做菜，分不开身来招呼，
Her mother was cooking at the stern and could not leave the stove to greet him.

只高声带笑地说：“阿珠，你说话要摸摸良心，
She merely called back with a laugh, “A’zhu, have a conscience!

胡老爷一请就到，
Mr Hu came the moment we asked him.

还说‘好难请’！”
How can you say he was hard to persuade?”

“也不知道哪个没有良心？”阿珠斜脱着胡雪岩，
“I wonder who has no conscience?” A’zhu glanced sideways at Hu.

“人家的船是长途，
“Other people's boats get the long journeys.

我们的船就该是短程。”
Ours is only good for short ones.”

阿珠的娘深怕她女儿得罪了“贵客”，随即用呵斥的声音说道：“说话没轻没重，
Afraid that her daughter would offend their distinguished guest, her mother immediately scolded her. “You don't know when to stop!

越说越不好了。”
Every word makes it worse.”

接着，放下锅铲，探身出来，一面在围裙上擦着双手，一面向胡雪岩含笑招呼：“胡老爷，你怎么这时候才来？
Then she put down her spatula and leaned out, wiping her hands on her apron as she smiled at Hu. “Mr Hu, what kept you so long?

阿珠一遍一遍在船头上望……”
A’zhu has been going to the bow to look for you over and over...”


这句话羞着了阿珠，
This embarrassed A’zhu.

原是白里泛红的一张脸，越发烧得如满天晚霞，
Her fair cheeks, already tinged with pink, flamed like a sunset sky.

抢着打断她的话说：“哪个一遍一遍在船头上望？
She hastily interrupted. “Who's been going to the bow over and over?

瞎说八道！”
What nonsense!”

说一完，只见长辫子一甩，扭身沿着船舷，往后舱就走。
With a flick of her long braid, she turned and walked along the gunwale towards the rear cabin.

水上女儿走惯了的，
A girl brought up on the water was accustomed to this.

看似风摆杨柳般摇摇欲坠，其实安然无事，
She seemed to sway as precariously as a willow in the wind, but was perfectly safe.

但胡雪岩大为担心，慌忙喊道“阿珠，
Hu, however, was alarmed. “A’zhu!

阿珠，
A’zhu!

你当心！
Be careful!

不要掉到河里！”
Don't fall into the river!”

阿珠没有理他，不过听他那发急乱叫的声音，心里觉得很舒服，
She ignored him, though his anxious cries pleased her.

不由得就把脚步放慢了，一步一步很规矩地走着。
Almost without realising it, she slowed down and began taking careful, orderly steps.

“胡老爷，你看！”阿珠的娘仿佛万般无奈地，
“Just look at her, Mr Hu!” her mother said with an air of complete helplessness.

“疯疯癫癫，
“Wild as a mad girl.

拿她真没法子。”
There's nothing I can do with her.”

“你也少罗嗦了！”老张这样埋怨他老婆，
“That's enough of your nagging too!” Old Zhang told his wife.

转脸又说，“胡老爷，你请舱里坐。”
Then he turned to Hu. “Please come and sit in the cabin.”


进舱就发现，这条船油漆一新，收拾得比以前更加整齐，
Inside, Hu immediately noticed fresh paint and an even tidier appearance than before.

便点点头说：“船修理过了？”
He nodded. “You've had the boat repaired?”

“老早就要修了，
“It needed doing long ago.

一直凑不出一笔整数，
We could never put together enough money.

多亏胡老爷上次照顾。”
We have your custom last time to thank.”

“以后机会还有。”胡雪岩说，
“There will be more opportunities,” Hu said.

“王大老爷放了湖州府，在杭州还有差使，
“Mr Wang has been appointed to Huzhou, but he still has duties in Hangzhou.

常来常往，
He'll often travel between the two.

总有用得着你船的时候。”
There are bound to be occasions when your boat is needed.”

“那要请胡老爷替我们留意。”
“Then we must ask you to keep us in mind.”

“本来，这种事不该我管。
“Ordinarily, arranging that sort of thing isn't my business.

不过，你的船另当别论，
But your boat is a special case.

我来想个办法。”
I'll think of something.”

胡雪岩沉吟着，想把老张的这条无锡快，当作海运局或者湖州府长期租用的“官船”，让他按月有一笔固定的收入。
He considered having the Sea Transport Bureau or the Huzhou prefecture hire Old Zhang's fast Wuxi boat on a long-term basis for official use, giving him a fixed monthly income.

沉吟未定，阿珠又出现了，打来一盆脸水。
Before he had decided, A’zhu reappeared with a basin of water.

这下提醒了老张，
This reminded Old Zhang of his duties as host.

站起身说：“胡老爷先宽宽衣，洗洗脸，吃碗菜。
He stood. “First make yourself comfortable, Mr Hu, wash your face, and have some tea.

哪天到临平，要吃些什么菜？
What dishes would you like for the journey to Linping?

等下叫阿珠的娘来跟胡老爷商量。”
I'll have A’zhu's mother come and discuss them with you presently.”


等老张一走，胡雪岩就轻松了，
Once Old Zhang had gone, Hu relaxed.

起身笑道，“阿珠，你的脾气必厉害！”
Rising with a smile, he said, “A’zhu, what a temper you have!”

“还要说人家！
“And you still criticise me!

你自己不想想，
Think of yourself.

一上了岸，把人家抛到九霄云外。
The moment you go ashore, you forget all about us.

平常不来还不要去说它，
Never mind that you don't ordinarily visit.

王大老爷到湖州上任，明明现成有船，他故意不用。
When Mr Wang goes to take up his appointment in Huzhou, there's a boat ready for him here, yet he deliberately won't use it.

你说说看，有没有这个道理？”
Tell me, is that reasonable?”

她一面说一面替胡雪岩解钮扣卸去马褂、长衫，依偎在身边，
As she spoke, she stood close beside Hu and helped unbutton and remove his outer jacket and long robe.

又是那种无限幽怨的声音，胡雪岩自然是“别有一般滋味在心头。”
Her voice was full of grievance, stirring feelings in him that he could scarcely name.

等她低头去解他腋下的那颗钮扣上，他不由得就伸头去摸她的如退光黑漆般的头发，
When she lowered her head to undo a fastening beneath his arm, he reached out to touch her hair, black and glossy as polished lacquer.

阿珠把头再往下低，避开了他的手，同时抗议：“不要动手动脚，
She ducked away from his hand and protested, “Keep your hands to yourself!

把我头发都弄毛了！”
You'll mess up my hair!”


“你的头发是自己梳的？”
“Do you comb it yourself?”

“自然罗！
“Naturally!

我自己梳，我娘替我打辫子。
I comb it, and Mother plaits it.

我们这种人，难道还有丫头、老妈子来伺候的福气？”
Do you suppose people like us are fortunate enough to have maids and serving women to wait on us?”

“也不见得没有。”胡雪岩说，
“You might be,” Hu said.

“丫头、老妈子又何足为奇？”
“There's nothing so extraordinary about having servants.”

这话一说完，阿珠立刻抬起眼来，双目流转，在他的脸上绕了一下，马上又低下头去，
At once she raised her eyes and searched his face, then looked down again.

捞起他的长衫下摆，解掉最后一个扣子，卸去外衣，然后绞一把手巾送到他手里。
Gathering the skirt of his robe, she undid the last fastening, removed the outer garment, and wrung out a towel for him.

他发现她眼中有期待的神色，
He saw expectation in her eyes.

不用说，那是希望他对她刚才所说的那句话，有个进一步的解释。
Clearly she hoped he would explain what he had just said.

但是他已悔出言轻率，便装做不解，很快地扯到别的事。
But he already regretted his rash words, so he pretended not to understand and quickly turned to something else.


这件事，足以让阿珠立刻忘掉他刚才的那句话，
The new subject was enough to make A’zhu forget his remark immediately.

他解开他带来的那个包袱，
He unwrapped the bundle he had brought.

里面是一个小小的箱子，仿照保险箱的做法。用铁皮所装，漆成墨绿色，也装有暗锁。
Inside was a small chest made in imitation of a safe, clad in sheet iron, painted dark green, and fitted with a concealed lock.

“这是什么箱子？”
“What sort of chest is that?”

“ ‘杜十娘怒沉百宝箱’的百宝箱。”
“A treasure chest, like the one in the story of Du Shiniang, who sank her treasure chest in anger.”

他把暗锁打开，
He opened the lock.

相内却只有“四宝”，一瓶香水，一个八音盒，一把日本女人插在头上当装饰的象牙细篦，一只景泰蓝嵌珠的女表。
This chest, however, contained only four treasures: a bottle of perfume, a musical box, a fine ivory comb of the kind Japanese women wore as an ornament, and a lady's watch in pearl-set cloisonné enamel.


阿珠惊多于喜，
A’zhu was more astonished than delighted.

看看这样，摸摸那样，好半天说不出话。
She looked at one thing and touched another, unable to speak for a long while.

胡雪岩先把牙篦插在她头发上，
Hu first placed the ivory comb in her hair.

接着把那只表用钥匙上足了弦，以自己的金表校准了时刻，替阿珠挂在钮扣上，
Then he wound the watch with its key, set it by his own gold watch, and hung it from one of her buttons.

再把八音盒子开足了发条，让它叮叮当当响着，
Next he wound the musical box and let its tinkling notes ring out.

最后拿起那瓶香水，
Finally he picked up the perfume.

阿珠忽然失声喊道：“不要，不要！”
A’zhu suddenly cried, “No, no!”

胡雪岩愕然：“不要什么？”
He looked surprised. “No what?”

“傻瓜！”阿珠嫣然一笑，
“Silly!” She smiled.

“不要打开来！”
“Don't open it!”

这时老张和那船伙计，为从未听过的叮叮当当的声音所招引，都在船舱外探望，要弄明白是什么东西在响？
Drawn by the unfamiliar chiming, Old Zhang and the boat hand peered into the cabin, trying to discover what was making the sound.

阿珠却不容他们看个究竟，
A’zhu gave them no chance to inspect it.

一手八音盒，一手香水，头插牙蓖，衣襟上晃荡着那只表，急忙忙走向后梢，到她娘那里“献宝”去了。
With the musical box in one hand and the perfume in the other, the ivory comb in her hair and the watch swinging from her clothes, she hurried to the stern to show her mother her treasures.


于是只听得她们母女俩赞叹说笑的声音，
Hu heard mother and daughter exclaiming, laughing, and talking.

最后是做娘的在告诫：“好好去放好。
At last the mother cautioned her: “Put them away safely.

有人的地方少拿出来，
Don't keep bringing them out when other people are about.

胡家的阿毛手脚不干净，
A'mao, the Hu girl, has light fingers.

当心她顺手牵羊。
Mind she doesn't help herself.”

““怕什么！
“What is there to fear?

我锁在‘百宝箱’里！”
I'll lock them in my treasure chest!”

“什么‘百宝箱’？”
“What treasure chest?”

“喏，”大概是阿珠在比划，
“Look,” A’zhu said, presumably indicating its size with her hands.

“这么长，这么宽，是铁的，还有暗锁，
“It's this long and this wide, made of iron, with a hidden lock.

怎么开法只有我一个人晓得，
I'm the only one who knows how to open it.

偷不走的。”
No one can steal them.”

原来是首饰箱！“阿珠的娘说：”
“Oh, a jewellery box!” her mother said.

傻丫头，人家不会连箱子一起偷？
“Silly girl, couldn't someone steal the box as well?”

““啊！”阿珠醒悟了。
“Ah!” That had not occurred to A’zhu.

接着便又重新出现在中舱，高兴之外，似乎还有些忧虑的神色。
She soon reappeared in the middle cabin, still delighted, but now looking a little anxious too.


为了知道她的忧虑想安慰她，胡雪岩招把手说：“阿珠，你过来，
Understanding her anxiety and wanting to reassure her, Hu beckoned. “A’zhu, come here.

我有话说。”
I have something to tell you.”

“你说好了！”她这样回答，
“Go ahead!” she answered.

一面打开那只百宝箱，除了头上的那把蓖以外，其余“三宝”都收入箱内。
Opening the chest, she put away the three treasures, leaving only the comb in her hair.

却把个开了盖的箱子捧在手里，凝视不休。
Yet she continued to hold the open box and gaze into it.

“你到底想不想听我的话。”
“Do you want to listen to me or not?”

“好，好！
“Yes, yes!

我听。”阿珠急忙答应，
I'm listening.”

锁好箱子，走到胡雪岩对面坐下，右手支颐，偏着头等他开口。
She hurriedly locked it and sat opposite him, her chin supported by her right hand and her head tilted as she waited for him to speak.

这又是一个极动人的姿态，胡雪岩也偏着头紧盯着她看。
He found the pose so appealing that he too tilted his head and gazed at her.

阿珠大概心里还在百宝箱里，以致视而不见。
A’zhu's thoughts were apparently still in the treasure chest: she looked at him without seeing him.


她不作声，他也不开口，
She said nothing, and neither did he.

好久，她方省悟，张皇而抱歉地问道：“你，你刚才说什么？”
Eventually she came to herself and asked in apologetic confusion, “What—what were you saying?”

“咦！”胡雪岩故意装作十分诧异地，
“Why!” Hu pretended to be astonished.

“我说了半天，你一句都没有听进去？”
“I've been talking all this time, and you haven't heard a word?”

阿珠为他一诈，歉意越发浓了，
Taken in, she became even more apologetic.

陪着笑说：“对不起！
“I'm sorry,” she said with a conciliatory smile.

我想起一桩要紧事情。”
“I suddenly remembered something important.”

“什么要紧事？”
“What was it?”

原是托词，
It had merely been an excuse.

让他钉紧了一问。得要想几句话来圆自己的谎，
Now that he pressed her, she had to find something to make her story convincing.

偏偏脑筋越紧越笨，越笨越急，
The more she strained to think, the less she could manage; the less she could manage, the more anxious she grew.

涨红了脸，好半天说不出一句话来。
Her face reddened, and for a long time she could not produce a word.

“好了，好了！”胡雪岩大为不忍，
“Never mind, never mind!” Hu could not bear to torment her.

“不便说就不说。”
“If it's private, don't tell me.”

“是啊，这桩事情不便说。”阿珠如释重负似地笑道：
“Yes, it's something private.” Relieved, she smiled.

“现在，你有什么话，请你尽管话，
“Now say whatever you wanted to say.

我一定留心听。”
I'll listen carefully.”


“我劝你，不要把你娘的话太当真！”他放低了声音说，
“My advice is not to take your mother's warning too seriously,” he said, lowering his voice.

“身外之物要看得开些……”他讲了一套“身外之物”的道理，
“Don't let possessions weigh on your mind...” He explained his view of material things.

人以役物，不可为物所役，
People should make use of possessions, not become their servants.

心受之物固然要当心被窃，
Naturally one should guard a treasured object against theft.

但为了怕被窃，不敢拿出来用，甚至时进忧虑，处处分心，这就是为物所役，
But if fear of losing it prevented one from using it, or caused constant anxiety and distraction, one had become its servant.

倒不如无此一物。
Better not to own it at all.

“所以，”他说，“你的脑筋一定要转过来。
“So,” he concluded, “you must change the way you think.

丢掉就丢掉，
If something is lost, it's lost.

没有什么了不得！
It isn't the end of the world!

不然，我送你这几样东西，倒变成害了你了。”
Otherwise my presents will have done you harm instead of good.”

他把这番道理说得很透彻，无奈阿珠大不以为然，
He had made his reasoning perfectly clear, but A’zhu strongly disagreed.

“你倒说得大方，
“How generous you sound!

‘丢掉就丢掉’！
‘If it's lost, it's lost!’

你不心疼我心疼。”
You may not mind, but I do.”

她忽有怨怼，
Suddenly her tone grew resentful.

“你这个人就是这样，
“That's just like you.

说丢掉就丢掉，

一点情分都没有。对人对东西都一样！”

“你说‘对人对东西部一样’，
“You say ‘people or possessions.’

这个‘人’是哪个？”
Which person do you mean?”

“你还问得出口？”阿珠冷笑，
“You can actually ask?” She gave a bitter little laugh.

“可见得你心里早没有那个‘人’了！”
“That proves you've long since forgotten that person!”


“亏你怎么想出来了？”胡雪岩有些懊恼，
“How did you manage to think of that?” Hu was a little exasperated.

“我们在讲那几样东西，
“We were talking about those presents.

你无缘无故会扯到人上面？
Why suddenly drag people into it?

我劝你不必太看重身外之物，正是为了看重你，
I tell you not to care too much about possessions precisely because I care about you.

你连这点道理都想不明白？
Can't you understand even that?

再说，我那么忙，你娘来一叫我就来，
Besides, busy as I am, I came the moment your mother invited me.

还要怎么样呢？
What more do you want?

至于王大老爷上任要雇船，你也得替我想想，
As for hiring a boat for Mr Wang's departure, put yourself in my position.

照我在王大老爷面前的身分，好不好去管这种小事情？
Given my standing with him, would it be proper for me to interfere in such a small matter?”

““我晓得，
