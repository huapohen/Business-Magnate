胡雪岩
《平步青云（上）》第七章（下，至本章结束）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume I — Chapter Seven, concluding part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


一宵无话，
The night passed without incident.

第二天一早胡雪岩起身，阿珠服侍他漱口洗脸，
Early next morning Hu rose, and A’zhu helped him wash and rinse his mouth.

由于急着要上岸办事，连点心都顾不得吃，就起身去了。
In his haste to go ashore on business, he could not even stay for breakfast.

临走留下话，中午约在盐桥一家叫“纯号”的酒店见面，又说，如果阿珠和她娘有兴致，也一道来逛逛。
Before leaving, he arranged to meet them at midday at a tavern called Chunhao, near Yan Bridge, and said that A’zhu and her mother might come along for an outing if they liked.

母女俩的兴致自然极好。
Naturally, both were delighted.

盐桥大街多的是布店和估衣店，
Yan Bridge Street had plenty of cloth shops and shops selling second-hand clothes.

阿珠跟她娘商量：“爹要做老板了，
A’zhu consulted her mother. “Father's going to be a proprietor.

总不能再穿‘短打’，
He can't go on wearing a boatman's short jacket.

先到估衣店去买件长衫，再自己剪布来做。”
Let's buy him a long gown at a second-hand shop first, then get some cloth and make him more clothes ourselves.”

“好啊！”她娘欣然同意，
“Good!” her mother agreed happily.

“我们早点去！”
“Let's go early!”

她们母女俩高高兴兴在收拾头面，预备出门。老张一个人坐在船头上闷闷不乐，
While they cheerfully dressed their hair and prepared to go out, Old Zhang sat alone in the bow, sunk in gloom.

心里在想，中午一见了面，胡雪岩当然会把银子交过来，
When they met at noon, Hu would surely hand him the money.

只要一接上手，以后再有什么话说，就显得不够味道了。
Once he accepted it, anything he might say afterwards would come too late and look bad.

要说，说在前面，或者今天先不接银子，等商量停当了再说。
He must speak first—or decline to take the money today and wait until everything had been settled.

他要跟他妻子商量，无奈有阿珠在，不便开口，
He wanted to discuss it with his wife, but with A’zhu there he could not open his mouth.

心里踌躇无计，而一妻一女倒已经头光面滑，穿上“出客”的衣服，预备动身了。
While he hesitated helplessly, mother and daughter finished making themselves immaculate, put on their visiting clothes, and stood ready to leave.


“该走了吧！”阿珠的娘催促老张。
“Time to go!” his wife urged.

“爹！”阿珠又嫌她爹土气，“你把蓝布小衫换一换，好不好，
A’zhu, too, found fault with her father's rustic appearance. “Father, will you change that little blue cotton jacket?

寿头寿脑的，
You look such a bumpkin!

真把人的台都坍光的！”
You'll disgrace us completely.”

由于宠女儿的缘故，老张一向把她这些没规没矩的话，当作耳边风。
Old Zhang spoiled his daughter and generally let her impudent remarks pass as if he had not heard them.

但话虽不理，该有行动，
Still, even if he ignored her words, he ought to have done something.

而他望着她们母女，怔怔地好象灵魂出窍了似的，好半天不开口。
Instead he stared at the two of them as though his soul had left his body, remaining silent for a long time.

“呀！”他妻子不胜讶异地：
“Why!” his wife exclaimed in astonishment.

“怎的？”
“What's the matter?”

老张摇摇头，接着说了句：“你们娘儿俩去好了。
He shook his head, then said, “You two go.

我不去了。”
I'm staying here.”

“咦！为啥？”
“Why ever not?”

老张想了想说：“我要帮阿四把船摇回万安桥去。”
After a moment's thought, he answered, “I have to help A’si take the boat back to Wan'an Bridge.”

这是不成理由的理由，
It was no excuse at all.

阿珠和她娘的脸上，顿时象眼前的天气一样，阴睛不定了。
The expressions on mother and daughter changed as quickly as the uncertain weather outside.

“你在想什么古里古怪的心思？”阿珠娘脸板得一丝笑容都没有，眼圈都有些红了，
“What strange notions have you got into your head now?” His wife's face lost every trace of a smile, and her eyes reddened.

“生来是吃苦的命！
“You were born to toil!

好日子还没有过一天，就要‘作’了！”
You haven't enjoyed a single comfortable day yet, and already you're making trouble for yourself!”

“作”是杭州话，通常只用来骂横也不是，竖也不是，不讨人喜欢的孩子，
The Hangzhou expression she used normally described a tiresome child who was dissatisfied whichever way things went.

用来责备老张，便有“自作孽、不可活”的意思，
Applied to Old Zhang, it carried the sharper implication that he was bringing his own ruin upon himself.

话重而怨深，
Her words were harsh and her resentment deep.

他不能不做个比较明白的表示了。
He had to make his position somewhat clearer.


“你不要一门心里只想自己！”他说，
“Don't think of nobody but yourself,” he said.

“人家白花花一千两银子，不是小数目，
“A thousand taels of good silver is no trifling sum.

把它蚀光了怎么办？”
What happens if we lose it all?”

“你啊，
“You!

‘树叶儿掉下来怕打开头’，
You'd fear a falling leaf might split your head!

生意还没有做，开口闭口蚀本！
You haven't begun trading, and all you can talk about is losses.

照我这样子说，一辈子摇船好了，
If that's the way you look at it, you might as well spend your whole life rowing.

摇到七老八十，一口气不来，棺材都用不着买，
Row till you're seventy or eighty, and when you draw your last breath we needn't even buy a coffin.

往河里一推，喂鱼拉倒！
Just push you into the river and let the fish have you!”

“爹娘吵架，遇到紧要关头，阿珠总是站在她爹这面，
Whenever her parents quarrelled, A’zhu sided with her father at the critical moment.

这时便埋怨着说：“娘！
Now she protested, “Mother!

何苦说这些话？
Why say such things?

爹不肯去，让他不去好了。”
If Father doesn't want to go, let him stay.”

“对！”阿珠的娘真的生气了，
“Very well!” Her mother was truly angry.

“枉为他是一家之主。
“Some head of the family he is!

我们敬他，他不受敬，
We give him his due and he won't accept it.

随他去，
Let him please himself.

我们走！”
We're going!”

听得这负气的话，阿珠又觉得不安，
This angry declaration troubled A’zhu in turn.

想了想只好这样说：“怎么走？
After thinking for a moment, she could only ask, “How are we to get there?

路好远到那里。”
It's such a long walk.”

路不但好远，而且郊野小径，泥泞不堪，
Besides the distance, the country paths were deep in mud.

就能走进城，一又脚上的鞋袜亦已不成样子，
Even if they managed to walk into town, their shoes and stockings would be a sorry sight.

不过，这也难不倒她娘，
This did not defeat her mother, however.

高声喊道：“阿四，阿四！”
“A’si! A’si!” she shouted.

“阿四到万安桥去了。”老张说。
“A’si's gone to Wan'an Bridge,” Old Zhang answered.

亏得他接了这句口，局面才不致僵持，
Fortunately, this reply prevented a complete deadlock.

他妻子气消了些，声音却依旧很大，
His wife's anger subsided a little, though her voice remained loud.

“我们今天把话说说清楚，
“We'll have this out today.

你到底是怎么个意思？”
Just what do you mean?”


“等下再说。”老张这样回答，一面看了阿珠一眼。
“We'll talk about it later,” Old Zhang replied, with a glance at A’zhu.

这一下她们母女俩都懂了他的意思，
Both women now understood.

阿珠有些羞，有些恼，更有些焦忧，
A’zhu felt embarrassed, annoyed, and, above all, anxious.

看爹这神气，事情怕要变卦。
Judging by her father's expression, their plans might fall through.

“阿珠！
“A’zhu!

你到后面去看看，炖在炉子上的蹄筋，
Go and look at the tendons stewing on the stove.

怕要加水了。”
They may need more water.”

借这个因由把她支使了开去，夫妻俩凑在一起谈私话。
Having sent her daughter away on this pretext, his wife drew close to him for a private conversation.

老张第一句话就问：“人家姓胡的，对阿珠到底是怎么个主意？你倒说说看！”
His very first question was, “Tell me this: what exactly does that fellow Hu intend to do about A’zhu?”

“何用我说？
“Why ask me?

你还看不出来？”
Can't you see for yourself?”

“我怎么看不出？
“Of course I can.

不过昨天看得出，
Or I could yesterday.

今天看不出了。”
Today I'm not so sure.”

“这叫什么话？”
“What do you mean by that?”

“我问你，”老张想了想说，“他到底是要做丝生意，是要我们阿珠，还是两样都要？”
He considered, then asked, “Does he want to trade in silk, does he want our A’zhu, or does he want both?”

“自然两样都要。”
“Both, naturally.”

“他要两样，我只好做一样，
“He may want both, but I'll agree to only one.

他要我们阿珠，开丝行请他去请教别人，
If he wants our A’zhu, let him find someone else to run his silk firm.

要我替他做伙计来出面，娶阿珠的事就免谈。”
If he wants me to work for him and act as its proprietor, there can be no question of his marrying her.”

“这为啥？”他妻子睁大了眼问，
“Why?” she demanded, her eyes widening.

“你倒说个道理我听听看。”
“Give me one good reason.”

他的道理就是不愿意让人笑他，靠裙带上拖出一个老板来做，
His reason was that he would not let people laugh at him for becoming a proprietor through his daughter's skirts.

“一句话，”他很认真地说，“我贫虽贫，还不肯担个卖女儿的名声！”
“In a word,” he said earnestly, “poor I may be, but I won't have a name for selling my daughter!”

人人要脸，树树要皮！
Every person had a reputation to protect, just as every tree had its bark.

他妻子在想，也不能说他的话没有道理。
His wife could not say he had no grounds for his objection.

但事难两全，只好劝他委屈些。
But since they could not have everything, she could only urge him to swallow his pride a little.


“你脾气也不要这么倔，
“Don't be so stubborn.

各人自扫门前雪，
Everyone has enough to do sweeping the snow from his own doorstep.

没有哪家来管我们的闲事。”
Nobody will meddle in our affairs.”

“没有？”老张使劲摇着头，
“Nobody?” Old Zhang shook his head vigorously.

“你女人家，
“You're a woman.

难得到茶坊酒肆，听不到。
You seldom visit teahouses or taverns, so you don't hear what people say.

我外头要跑跑的，
I have to go about outside.

叫人家背后指指点点，我还好过日子？
How am I to live with people pointing at me behind my back?

好了，好了，
Enough, enough!”

“他越想越不妥，
The more he thought about it, the more intolerable it seemed.

大声说道：”我主意打定了。
He raised his voice. “I've made up my mind.

你如果一定不肯依我，我也有我的办法。
If you absolutely refuse to listen, I have a way of dealing with it.”

““什么办法？”她不安地问。
“What way?” she asked uneasily.

“丝行你去开，
“You run the silk firm.

算老板也好，算老板娘也好，
Call yourself the proprietor or the proprietor's wife, as you please.

我不管。
I shan't interfere.

我还是去做我的老本行，做一天吃一天，
I'll go back to my old trade, working each day for my daily bread.

有生意到了湖州，我来看你们娘儿两个。”
Whenever business takes me to Huzhou, I'll come and see you and our daughter.”


听他这番异想天开的话，居然说得象煞有介事，她失笑了，
He spoke of this fantastic scheme so seriously that she could not help laughing.

便故意这样问：“那么，你算是来做客人？”
“So you'll be coming as a visitor?” she asked deliberately.

“是啊！
“Yes.

做客人。”
As a visitor.”

“照这样说，你是没良心把我休掉了？”
“In that case, you've no conscience: you're casting me off?”

虽是半带玩笑，这“没良心”三个字，在老张听来就是劈脸一个耳光，
She was half joking, but the accusation struck him like a slap across the face.

顿时觉得脸上火辣辣地，极力分辩着：“怎么说我没良心？
His cheeks burned as he protested, “How can you say I've no conscience?

你不好冤枉我！”
You mustn't accuse me unjustly!”

“我没有冤枉你！
“I'm not accusing you unjustly.

如果你有良心，就算为我受委屈，好不好呢？”
If you have a conscience, can't you put up with a little humiliation for my sake?”

他不作声了，
He fell silent.

她看得出，自己真的要这么做，也可以做得到，
She could see that, if she insisted, she could have her way.

但是他嘴上不说，心里不愿，到底是夫归的情分，
But he would submit without agreeing in his heart, and they were husband and wife, after all.

何苦如此？
Why make him suffer?

想想还是要把他说得心甘情愿，
Better to persuade him to accept willingly.

这件事才算“落胃”。
Only then would the matter sit comfortably with them both.


于是她想着想着，跟她女儿想到一条路上去了，
As she thought it over, she reached the same conclusion as her daughter.

“这样行不行呢？”她说，
“Would this do?” she asked.

“你无非怕人家背后说闲话，
“All you're afraid of is gossip behind your back.

如果人家在湖州照样请过客，见过礼，算是他在湖州的一房家小，
Suppose he gives a proper wedding feast in Huzhou, observes the ceremonies, and establishes her as his wife there.

这总没有话说了吧？”
Surely there could be no objection then?”

见他妻子让步，他自然也要让步，
Since his wife had yielded a little, he must do the same.

点点头：“照这样子还差不多。”
He nodded. “That would be more like it.”

“那好了，
“Good.

我来想法子。
I'll find a way.

萝卜吃一截剥一截，眼前的要紧事先做。
Peel a radish a piece at a time as you eat it; let's deal with what's urgent first.

你换换衣裳，我们也好走了。
Change your clothes so that we can go.”

“老张换好一套出客穿的短衣，黑鞋白袜扎脚裤，上身一件直贡呢的夹袄。
Old Zhang changed into a short outfit he kept for visits: black shoes, white stockings, trousers tied at the ankles, and a lined jacket of fine woollen cloth.

正好阿四划了一只小船，买菜回来，
Just then A’si returned in a small boat with the provisions he had bought.

留他看船，老张自己把他妻儿划到盐桥上岸，
Leaving him to watch the larger vessel, Old Zhang rowed his wife and daughter to Yan Bridge himself.

从河下走上熙熙攘攘的盐桥大街。
They landed and climbed from the waterside to the crowded street.


水上生涯的人家，难得到这条肩摩毂击的大街上来，
Families who lived on the water seldom visited this busy thoroughfare, where shoulders brushed and carriage wheels almost touched.

阿珠颇有目迷五色之感，
A’zhu was dazzled by everything around her.

顾上不顾下，高一脚，低一脚地不小心踩着了一块活动的青石板，
Looking up without watching her feet, she stumbled on an uneven stretch and stepped on a loose stone slab.

泥浆迸溅，弄脏了新上身的一条雪青百褶裙，
Mud splashed up and stained the new mauve pleated skirt she was wearing.

于是失声而喊，顿时引得路人侧目而视。
Her involuntary cry made the passers-by turn to look.

“唷，唷，
“Careful, careful!

走路要当心！”
Watch where you're going!”

有个二十来岁的油头光棍，仿佛好意来扶她，趁势在她膀子捏了一把。
A sleek-haired loafer in his twenties pretended to help her, taking the opportunity to pinch her arm.

阿珠涨红了脸，
Her face flamed.

使劲把膀子一甩，用力过猛，一甩上去，正好打了他一个反手耳光，其声清脆无比。
She flung her arm away so violently that the back of her hand caught his face with a resounding slap.

“唷，
“Oh!

好凶！”
What a fierce one!”

有人吃惊，也有人发笑。
Some bystanders exclaimed in surprise; others laughed.

这一下使得被误打了的人，面子上越发下不来，
This made it still harder for the man to save face.

一手捂着脸，跳脚大骂。
Clutching his cheek, he stamped and cursed.

阿珠和她娘吓得面色发白。
A’zhu and her mother turned pale.

老张一看闯了祸，赶紧上前陪笑道歉：“对不起，对不起，
Seeing trouble, Old Zhang hurried forward, smiling apologetically. “I'm sorry, I'm sorry!

无心的！”
It was an accident!”


杭州人以掴脸为奇耻大辱，特别是让妇女打了，认为是“晦气”，
In Hangzhou a slap on the face was considered a terrible disgrace, and to be struck by a woman was thought particularly unlucky.

而那个油头光混又是杭州人所谓“撩鬼儿”的小流氓，事态使越发严重了，
The sleek-haired fellow was also one of the petty street ruffians known locally as liaoguier, which made matters worse.

立刻便有五六个同党围了上来。
Five or six companions immediately surrounded them.

其中一个一面口沫横飞地辱骂，一面劈胸一把将老张的衣服抓住，伸出拳来就要打。
One seized Old Zhang's clothing at the chest, showering him with abuse, and raised his fist.

“打不得，打不得！
“Don't hit him! Don't hit him!

有话好讲。”阿珠的娘大喊。
We can talk this over!” A’zhu's mother cried.

“讲你娘的……”一拳伸了过来，
“Talk to your bloody mother—” A fist shot out.

老张接住，
Old Zhang caught it.

下面一腿又到，老张又避开，
A kick followed, and he dodged.

他打过几个月的拳，也练过“仙人担”，抛过“石锁”，
He had trained in boxing for several months, practised with a weighted carrying pole, and tossed stone exercise weights.

两条膀子上有一两百斤力气，这五六个人还应付得了，
His arms had the strength to lift a hundred or two hundred jin, and he could have dealt with these five or six men.

不过一则是自己的理屈，再则为人忠厚，不愿打架，
But he felt that his side was in the wrong, and his honest, peaceable nature made him unwilling to fight.

所以只是躲避告饶。
He merely ducked and pleaded.

拉拉扯扯，身上已经着了两下，还是趁火打劫的，挨挨蹭蹭来轻薄阿珠，
In the scuffle he took two blows, while others used the confusion to crowd against A’zhu and molest her.

就在这她眼泪都快要掉来来的当儿，来了个救星。
She was on the verge of tears when a rescuer appeared.


“三和尚！
“Third Monk!

啥事体？”
What's going on?”

叫得出名字就好办了，那人手上的劲，立刻就松。
The fact that the newcomer knew a name made all the difference: the man immediately relaxed his grip.

阿珠的娘如逢大赦，
A’zhu's mother felt as though she had been reprieved.

赶紧抢上来说：“张老板，
She hurried forward. “Mr Zhang!

张老板，
Mr Zhang!

请你来说一句！
Please say a word for us!

本来没事……”“没事？”
There was nothing to begin with—” “Nothing?”

被打的那人也要抢着来做原告，指着阿珠说：“张老板，请你老人家评评理看，
The man who had been slapped also rushed to present his complaint, pointing at A’zhu. “Mr Zhang, you judge for yourself!

我看她要惯倒，好意扶她一把，
I thought she was about to fall and kindly tried to help her.

哪晓得她撩起一个嘴巴！
Who'd have expected her to slap me across the mouth?

端午脚边，晦气不晦气？
With the Dragon Boat Festival so close, isn't that a fine piece of bad luck?”

“张胖子肚里雪亮，自然是调戏人家，有取打之道，
Fat Zhang understood perfectly: the fellow had been pestering the girl and had brought it on himself.

而心里却有些好笑，故意问道：“阿珠，你怎么出手就打人？”
He was rather amused, however, and deliberately asked, “A’zhu, why did you hit him?”

一听他叫得出阿珠的名字，原是熟人，抓住老张的那个人，不自觉地就把手松开了。
Hearing him address her by name, the man holding Old Zhang realised that they knew one another and instinctively let go.

又羞又窘，脸色象块红布样的阿珠，这才算放了心，
A’zhu, blushing scarlet with embarrassment, felt safe at last.

得理不让人，挺起了胸说，“我也不是存心打他，
Sure of her ground, she straightened up. “I didn't mean to hit him.

是他自己不好。”
It was his own fault.”

“好了，好了！”她娘赶紧拦她，
“All right, all right!” her mother hastily intervened.

“你也少说一句。”
“You needn't say any more.”

“看我面子！
“Let it pass for my sake.

是我侄女儿。”张老板对被打的那人说，
She's my niece,” Zhang told the injured party.

“等下我请你们吃老酒。”
“I'll buy you all a drink later.”

一场看来不可开交的纠纷，就此片言而决。
A quarrel that had seemed impossible to resolve was settled in a few words.

老张夫妇向张胖子谢了又谢，
Old Zhang and his wife thanked him again and again.

阿珠心里却是连自己都辨不出的滋味，仿佛觉得扫兴，又仿佛觉得安慰，站在旁边不开口。

就这样三言两语，张胖子对女家的重托，算是圆满地交了差，
In those few exchanges, Fat Zhang had satisfactorily discharged the bride's family's commission.

走回中舱，避开阿珠的视线，向胡雪岩笑一笑，表示事情办得很顺利。
He returned to the middle cabin and, out of A’zhu's sight, smiled at Hu to show that everything had gone smoothly.


于是到了北新关前，等候王有龄的官船一到，
At the Beixin barrier they waited for Wang's official boat.

讨关过闸，把王有龄和秦寿门、杨用之一起请到张家的船上，
When it arrived, they obtained clearance and passed through the lock, then invited Wang, Qin Shoumen, and Yang Yongzhi aboard the Zhang family's vessel.

一面在水波不兴的运河中，缓缓行去，一面由阿珠伺候着，开怀畅饮。
As it moved slowly over the still waters of the canal, A’zhu served them and they drank with pleasure.

因为有秦、杨两师爷在座，既不能一无顾忌，畅抒肺腑，也不便放浪形骸，大谈风月，
With the two secretaries, Qin and Yang, present, however, they could neither speak their hearts without reserve nor abandon propriety to discuss romantic adventures.

所以终席只是娓娓清谈。
The entire meal therefore passed in gentle, agreeable conversation.

这席酒从拱宸桥吃到临平，也就是从中午吃到晚上。
It lasted from Gongchen Bridge to Linping—from noon until evening.

宴罢又移到王有龄船上去品茗闲话，
Afterwards they moved to Wang's boat for tea and more talk.

到了起更时分，秦、杨二人告辞回自己的船，
At the first night watch, Qin and Yang took leave and returned to their own vessel.

张胖子跟着也走了，
Fat Zhang followed them away.

只有胡雪岩为王有龄留了下来话别。
Wang detained only Hu for a private farewell.


虽只有几个月的相聚，而且也只是一水可航，两无可达的腰隔，但王有龄的离愁无限，
They had been together for merely a few months, and only two days' journey along a navigable waterway would separate them, yet Wang felt an immense sorrow at parting.

除了感情以外，他还有着近乎孤立无倚的恐惧，
Besides affection, he felt something close to the fear of being left alone and helpless.

因为这些日子来，倚胡雪岩如左右手，已养成“一日不可无此君”的习惯了。
In recent months he had relied on Hu as on his own hands and had grown so accustomed to him that he could scarcely do without him for a day.

不过他也知道，要胡雪岩舍却自己的事业，到他衙门中去当遇事可以随时商议的客卿，不但办不到，
Yet he knew that he could not ask Hu to abandon his business and become a resident adviser at the yamen, available for consultation whenever anything arose.

就算办到了，又置秦、杨三人于何地？
Even if it were possible, where would that leave Qin and Yang?

因此，这条心他是死了，
He had therefore given up that hope.

退而求其次，唯有希望常见见面。
His next best wish was that they should meet often.

于是他问：“雪岩，你什么时候到湖州来？”
“Xueyan, when are you coming to Huzhou?” he asked.

“不会太远。”
“Before long.”

他算了算日子，
Hu calculated the days.

等阜康开了张，立即就要到湖州支看老张这方面的情形，
As soon as Fukang opened, he would go to Huzhou to see how Old Zhang was getting on.

“快则半个月，迟则月底。”他说。
“In a fortnight at the earliest; by the end of the month at the latest.”


“我倒起起来了。”王有龄说，
“That reminds me,” Wang said.

“ 前两天忙得不可开交，没有工夫问们。
“I've been so busy these last two days that I haven't had time to ask you.

你要在湖州开丝行，是怎么回事？
What's this about your opening a silk firm in Huzhou?”

““这件事，我本来想到了湖州再跟你谈。
“I meant to discuss it with you when I got there.

此刻不妨就说给你听。”他把前后经过，细细讲了一遍，包括阿珠的亲事在内。
But I may as well tell you now.” Hu explained the whole story in detail, including the proposed marriage to A’zhu.

事情相当复杂，王有龄一时还抓不着头绪，
It was rather complicated, and Wang could not immediately grasp all its threads.

只是深感兴味地说：“你搞的花样真热闹。”
Deeply interested, he merely remarked, “You certainly keep things lively.”

“雪公，热闹都从你身上来的。”
“Mr Xuexuan, all this activity comes from you.”

胡雪岩放低了声音说：“丝行当然有你一份。”
Hu lowered his voice. “Naturally, there's a share in the silk firm for you.”

“这不必，
“No need for that.

怕外面知道了，名声不好听。
If word got out, it wouldn't do my reputation any good.

反正你我之间，无事不可商量，这些话现在都不必去谈它。
There's nothing you and I can't discuss in any case; we needn't talk about these things now.

倒是杨用之那里，你得想办法下些功夫。
But you must find some way to work on Yang Yongzhi.

不然，他有他的主张，在公款的调度上，不无麻烦。”
Otherwise, with his own views, he may make it difficult to arrange the movement of public funds.”

“我早已想到了。
“I've already thought of that.

不过，我仍旧要用雪公你的名义来办。”
But again, I intend to act in your name.”

“怎么办？”王有龄问。
“How?” Wang asked.

“秦、杨两家的眷属，住在哪里，我都打听清楚了。
“I've found out where both Qin's and Yang's families live.

我会派人照应，到时候该送东西送东西，该送钱送钱，
I'll send people to look after them, providing gifts when gifts are called for and money when money is called for.

他们家里自会写信到湖州，
Their families will naturally write to Huzhou.

秦、杨两位知道了，当然会见你的情。
When Qin and Yang hear of it, they'll feel grateful to you.

那时候一切都好办了。”
Then everything will be easy.”


“对，对！”王有龄欣然嘉许，
“Yes, yes!” Wang approved delightedly.

“这样最好！
“That's the best way.

我也不必先说破，
I needn't explain beforehand.

等他们来跟我道谢时，我自会把交情卖到你身上。”
When they come to thank me, I'll make sure you get the credit.”

胡雪岩笑着说了句杭州的俗语：“花花轿儿人抬人！”
Hu smiled and quoted a Hangzhou saying: “We take turns carrying one another's flowered sedan chairs!”

“那么，”王有龄突然露出顽皮的笑容，“你什么时候让阿珠坐花轿？”
“Speaking of which,” Wang said, suddenly smiling mischievously, “when will you put A’zhu in a bridal sedan chair?”

“现在还谈不到。
“It's too soon to talk of that.

走到哪里算哪里。”
We'll take things as they come.”

“你太太知道这件事不？”
“Does your wife know?”

胡雪岩摇摇头：“最好不要让她知道。”
Hu shook his head. “I'd rather she didn't.”

“这一点我不赞成。”王有龄说，
“There I disagree,” Wang said.

“你是绝顶聪明的人，总该晓得这两句话：”糟糠之妻不下堂，贫贱之交不可忘。
“A man as clever as you should surely know those two sayings: ‘Do not turn out the wife who shared your coarse meals and hardship; do not forget the friends you made in poverty.’

‘如今虽非停妻再娶，也得跟你太太商量一下才好。
You're not dismissing your wife to marry another, but you ought to discuss the matter with her all the same.”

“胡雪岩默然，
Hu said nothing.

觉得王有龄的话，有点打官腔的味道。
He felt that Wang's words had something of an official sermon about them.
