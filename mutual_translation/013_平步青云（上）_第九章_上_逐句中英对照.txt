胡雪岩
《平步青云（上）》第九章（上）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume I — Chapter Nine, first part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第九章｜CHAPTER NINE


一到湖州，胡雪岩就为王有龄接到知府衙门去住，
As soon as Hu Xueyan arrived in Huzhou, Wang Youling brought him to stay at the prefectural yamen.

虽只是小别重逢，但以交情太深，彼此都有无法言喻的喜悦，
Their separation had been brief, but their friendship was so deep that both felt a joy beyond words at meeting again.

心里各有好些话，却还没有工夫深谈，
Each had much to tell the other, yet there was no time for a private conversation.

为了礼貌，也为了切身利害关系，胡雪岩先要去拜两位“师大老爷”。
Courtesy, as well as his own immediate interests, required Hu first to pay his respects to the two senior secretaries.

幕友照例有自己的小天地，秦寿门和杨用之各占一座院落，办公住家都在一起，
Private advisers customarily had a little domain of their own: Qin Shoumen and Yang Yongzhi each occupied a courtyard where they both lived and worked.

王有龄陪着他，先去拜访秦寿门，
Wang accompanied Hu to Qin’s quarters first.

欢然道故之余，向胡雪岩深深致谢。
After an affectionate exchange of news, Qin thanked Hu most earnestly.

端午节前，他有一份极丰富的节礼，包括两石白米，一担时新蔬果，还有十吊钱，送到秦家，
Before the Dragon Boat Festival, Hu had sent Qin’s household a remarkably generous present: two shi of white rice, a carrying load of fresh seasonal fruit and vegetables, and ten strings of copper cash.

秦太太已经从杭州写信告诉了秦寿门，所以这时对胡雪岩的态度，比以前更不同了。
Mrs Qin had written from Hangzhou to tell her husband, and his manner towards Hu was warmer than ever.

“我发湿气戒酒。”秦寿门说，
“I have given up wine on account of my rheumatism,” he said.

“今天要开戒了，陪雪岩兄痛饮一番。”
“Today I shall make an exception and have a proper drink with you, Brother Xueyan.”


“好极了！”王有龄接口问道，“老夫子，你看我们在哪里替雪岩接风？”
“Excellent!” Wang put in. “Master Qin, where do you think we should give Xueyan his welcoming dinner?”

以常理来说，第一天自然是他自己做东道主，
Ordinarily, Wang himself would naturally entertain his guest on the first evening.

问到这话，秦寿门便知有深意在内，
The question therefore suggested that he had something else in mind.

想了想笑道：“东翁莫说出口，
Qin considered it, then smiled. “Do not tell me, sir.

我们各自一猜，看看是不是一条路。”
Let us each write down a guess and see whether we are thinking along the same lines.”

于是秦寿门取管笔，撤张纸，背转身去，悄悄写好，
He took a brush and a sheet of paper, turned away, and wrote something down in secret.

王有龄如法炮制，
Wang did likewise.

把纸条伸开来一看，一个写着“则行”，一个写着“木易”，
When they unfolded their slips, one read “Then put into practice,” and the other “Wood and easy.”

两人哈哈大笑。
Both men burst out laughing.

“木易”是杨，“用之则行”这句成语，胡雪岩也知道，
Hu knew that the written characters for “wood” and “easy” combined to make the surname Yang; he also knew the saying in which “put to use”—Yongzhi—was followed by “then put into practice.” Both slips pointed to Yang Yongzhi.

就不明白到杨用之那里去喝酒，有何可笑。
What he could not understand was why drinking at Yang’s quarters should be so amusing.

“我来告诉你。”王有龄说，
“Let me explain,” Wang said.

“杨老夫子有极得意之事，
“Master Yang has something to be very pleased about.

到湖州不多几天，已经纳了宠了。
He had scarcely been in Huzhou a few days before he took a concubine.

这位如夫人生得宜男之相，而且贤惠能干，
The lady looks as though she will bear him sons, and she is capable and good-natured besides.

我们今天就扰他去。”
We shall descend upon him this evening.”


口说“扰他”，其实不是王有龄作东，他叫个伺候签押房的听差李成，备一桌翅席，抬一坛好酒，送到杨用之那里。
For all this talk of descending upon Yang, Wang instructed Li Cheng, the attendant who served his private office, to arrange a shark’s-fin banquet and have it delivered to Yang’s quarters with a jar of good wine.

胡雪岩却是别有用心，
Hu had a further purpose of his own.

此刻正用得着杨用之的时候，有些结纳示惠的机会，不肯放过，
Just now he needed Yang’s help and would not let slip any opportunity to cultivate the man and do him a kindness.

找个空隙，把王有龄拉到一边有话说。
Finding a moment, he drew Wang aside.

“杨老夫子纳宠，该送礼吧？”
“Surely Master Yang ought to have a present to mark his taking a concubine?”

“我送过了。”王有龄说，
“I have already given him one,” Wang replied.

“你可以免啦！”
“You need not bother.”

“礼不可废。”胡雪岩说，“而且礼不可轻。”
“The courtesy cannot be omitted,” Hu said, “and the gift must be substantial.”

王有龄略想了想，懂了他的用意，点点头说：“也好。
Wang thought for a moment, understood his intention, and nodded. “Very well.

你打算送什么？”
What do you propose to give?”

“总以实惠为主，
“Something of real value.

我想送一副金镯子，
A pair of gold bracelets, I thought.

趁早去办了来。”
I had better go and buy them at once.”

“不必这么费事，
“There is no need for all that trouble.

我那里现成有一副，你拿去用。
I have a pair ready to hand; you may use those.

不过，”王有龄放低了声音，指指里面：“可不能让他知道！”
Only”—Wang lowered his voice and pointed towards the inner room—“he must not hear of it!”

这是指秦寿门，
He meant Qin Shoumen.

胡雪岩报以领会的眼色。
Hu answered with a look of understanding.

于是王、胡二人托词换衣服，暂且告别，与秦寿门约好，准六点钟在杨用之那里会面。
They then excused themselves on the pretext of changing their clothes and arranged to meet Qin at Yang’s quarters at six precisely.


而胡雪岩五点钟就由李成引领着，到了杨用之那里。
At five, however, Li Cheng was already showing Hu into Yang’s rooms.

人逢喜事精神爽，杨用之那番红光满面，春风得意的神情，看来着实令人羡慕。
Good fortune lifts a man’s spirits, and Yang’s ruddy face and air of complete satisfaction were enough to make anyone envy him.

“啊，老兄！”
“Ah, my dear fellow!”

杨用之拉着他的手，亲热非凡，
He seized Hu’s hand with exceptional warmth.

“不敢说是‘一日思君十二时’，一静下来就会想到你，倒是一点不假。
“I cannot claim to think of you through every hour of the day and night, but whenever I have a quiet moment you do come to mind—that is perfectly true.

如何，宝号开张，营业鼎盛？”
Well, has your bank opened to a roaring trade?”

“托福，托福！”
“Very well, thanks to your good wishes.”

胡雪岩特意很仔细地看了他一眼，
Hu made a point of studying him closely.

“老夫子的气色好极了！
“You are looking splendid, Master Yang.

想来宾主都很对劲？”
I take it that you and your employer suit each other admirably?”

“那还用说。
“Need you ask?

我与雪公，真正是如鱼得水。”
With Mr Xuexuan, I am truly a fish in water.”

“对，对！”如鱼得水。“胡雪岩笑道：”
“Quite so—a fish in water!” Hu laughed.

听说老夫子另外还有鱼水之欢？“杨用之哈哈大笑，向里喊道：“锦云，锦云，你出来！”
“And I hear you have another sort of fish-and-water happiness as well?” Yang roared with laughter and called into the inner room, “Jinyun! Jinyun, come out here!”


不用说，锦云就是他的新宠。
Jinyun was, of course, his new concubine.

门帘启处，走出来一个面团团如无锡大阿福，年可二十的姑娘，
The curtain lifted, and a young woman of about twenty appeared, her face as round as one of the plump Da A’fu clay figures of Wuxi.

很腼腆的向客人笑了笑。
She gave the visitor a shy smile.

“锦云，这位就是我常跟你提你的胡老爷，
“Jinyun, this is Mr Hu, whom I am always telling you about.

见一见！”
Come and greet him.”

“啊，胡老爷！”
“Oh—Mr Hu!”

锦云把双眼睁得滚圆，将胡雪岩从上青到下，然后捡衽为礼。
Her eyes widened as she looked him up and down; then she gathered her skirts and curtsied.

“不敢当！”
“You do me too much honour.”

胡雪岩朝上作了个揖，顺势从袖子里取出一个红纸包递了给杨用之，
Hu returned her greeting with a bow and, in the same movement, took a red-paper packet from his sleeve and handed it to Yang.

“一点点薄礼，为如夫人添妆！”
“A trifling present, to add to the lady’s bridal ornaments.”

“不，不！
“No, no!

没有这个规矩。”杨用之极力推辞。
There is no such obligation.” Yang protested vigorously.

“若是嫌菲薄，老夫子就不收。
“Unless you consider it too paltry, you must accept it.

再说，这是送如嫂夫人的，与老夫子无关。”
Besides, it is for the lady, not for you.”

这一说，杨用之不能不收，
After that Yang could hardly refuse.

捏在手里，才发觉是一副镯子，却不知是金是银，
Feeling the packet, he realized that it contained a pair of bracelets, though he could not tell whether they were gold or silver.

只好再叫锦云道谢。
He asked Jinyun to thank their guest again.

“礼太菲薄，老夫子暂且不必打开，也不必说起，
“The present is so insignificant,” Hu added, “that you had better not open it just now, or mention it.

免得叫人笑话。”
I should hate to be laughed at.”

这一说杨用之也有数了，
Yang understood what that meant.

把那个红纸包拿在手里，显得为难而感激，
Holding the packet, he looked both embarrassed and grateful.

“惠我甚厚，
“You are far too generous.

真正是受之有愧！
I scarcely deserve such kindness.

我就恭敬不如从命了。”
But it would be discourteous to resist you further.”

说罢，深深一揖，把红纸包塞入衣袋。
He bowed deeply and slipped the packet into his pocket.


这番揖让折冲刚刚完毕，王有龄和秦寿门相偕到了。
They had barely concluded this exchange of bows, protests, and persuasion when Wang and Qin arrived together.

少不得又有一番以锦云作话题的调侃戏谑。然后开席，
Naturally there was more teasing, with Jinyun as its subject, before they sat down to dinner.

胡雪岩首先声明，他不算是客，仍奉王有龄首座，
Hu announced at once that he did not count as a guest and insisted that Wang take the place of honour.

而王有龄又要逊两位幕友居上席，
Wang, in turn, urged the two secretaries to sit above him.

谦让了半天，还是王有龄居首，胡雪岩其次，杨用之坐了主位，
After a lengthy exchange of courtesies, Wang finally took the first seat, Hu the next, and Yang the host’s place.

同时也叫锦云入席。
Jinyun was invited to join them.

宾主的交情都够了，不妨脱略形迹，
They were all sufficiently close to dispense with ceremony.

锦云的脾气极好，说话总是带着一团甜笑，而且温柔殷勤，
Jinyun had a delightful disposition, speaking always with a sweet smile and attending to everyone gently and thoughtfully.

所以这一席酒，吃得秦寿门醺醺大醉。
Qin drank himself thoroughly tipsy.

王有龄心想，这是个机会，
Wang saw his opportunity.

由阜康代理府库的事，他已经跟杨用之提过，此时正好让他们去深谈，
He had already mentioned to Yang the proposal that Fukang should act as agent for the prefectural treasury; now was the moment to leave the two men to discuss it in detail.

因此他起身告辞。
He rose to take his leave.

“你们谈谈吧！”他说，
“Stay and have a talk,” he said.

“我有些困了，先走一步。”
“I am rather sleepy and shall go on ahead.”


“只伯雪岩兄也困了。”
“I fear Brother Xueyan must be tired too.”

杨用之的话，出人意外，竟无留客之意，
Unexpectedly, Yang seemed to have no intention of keeping Hu.

好得下面还有表示：
Fortunately he followed this with an invitation.

“明天早晨，奉展雪岩兄来吃点心，
“May I ask you to breakfast tomorrow morning?

湖州的点心，着实讲究，
Huzhou is justly proud of its delicacies.

来试试小妾的手段。”
Come and try my concubine’s cooking.”

“好好！
“Gladly!

一定来叨扰。”
I shall certainly come.”

“东翁有兴也请过来。”杨用之又说。
“And you too, sir, if you feel inclined,” Yang added to Wang.

“谢谢！”
“Thank you.”

王有龄当然不肯来，而且也正好有事：
Wang naturally had no wish to join them, and in any case had business to attend to.

“东乡出了命案，
“There has been a killing in the eastern countryside.

我明天一早就要下乡验尸，不来了。”
I must go out early tomorrow to examine the body, so I cannot come.”

第二天一早，胡雪岩应邀赴约，
Hu kept his appointment the next morning.

锦云的手段真个不坏，
Jinyun’s cooking was indeed excellent.

有样“千张包子”煮线粉，加上平望的辣油，
One dish consisted of parcels wrapped in thin bean-curd skins, cooked with vermicelli and served with chilli oil from Pingwang.

胡雪岩在张家的船上亦未曾吃过，
Even on the Zhang family’s boat Hu had never tasted it.

连尽两器，赞不绝口。
He ate two bowls and praised it without ceasing.

吃完了泡上茶来，开始谈判。
When breakfast was over and tea had been brought, they began their discussion.


“东翁关照过了，
“My employer has given his instructions.

湖州府库跟乌程县库，都托阜康代理，一句话！”杨用之问道：“者兄在湖州可有联号，或者是将来要设分号？”
Fukang is to handle the funds of both the Huzhou prefectural treasury and the Wucheng county treasury. That is settled.” Yang continued, “Have you an affiliated bank in Huzhou, or do you intend to open a branch?”

“分号是一定要设的。
“We shall certainly open a branch.

目前托恒利代收。”
For the present, Hengli will collect the funds on our behalf.”

“恒利信用还不错。”
“Hengli’s credit is quite good.”

杨用之站起身来说，“请到我书房里来！”名为书房，闻不出一丝书卷气，
Yang rose. “Come into my study.” Though called a study, the room had nothing bookish about it.

当窗一张五斗桌，铺着蓝布，
A five-drawer desk stood at the window, covered in blue cloth.

除去笔砚，便是算盘、帐簿，
Apart from brushes and an inkstone, it held only an abacus and account books.

旁边一具极厚实的木柜，
Beside it stood a very solid wooden cupboard.

他打开来取出一只拜盒，从拜盒取出一张纸递给胡雪岩。
Yang opened this, took out a document box, and from the box produced a sheet of paper, which he handed to Hu.

“我都替老兄预备好了，
“I have everything ready for you.

填上恒利的名字，敲一个保，做个样子，就叫恒利来收款。”
Fill in Hengli’s name and obtain a guarantor’s endorsement—merely as a formality—and Hengli can come to collect the money.”


胡雪岩接过那张纸看，
Hu examined the paper.

是一张承揽代理公库的“禀帖”，
It was a formal petition to undertake the handling of public treasury funds.

此事他还是初次经手，不由得问了句：“这样子递了进来，就算数了？”
He had never dealt with such an arrangement before and could not help asking, “Once this has been submitted, is that all that is required?”

“是啊！
“Yes.

衙门里给你个批，就算数了。”
The yamen gives you its written approval, and the matter is settled.”

“那么，”胡雪岩知道，凡有公事，必有花费，所以很恳切他说：“老夫子，该当多少费用，交到哪里，请吩咐了，我好照办。”
Hu knew that official business invariably entailed expenses. “In that case, Master Yang,” he said earnestly, “please tell me how much I should pay, and to whom, so that I may make the necessary arrangements.”

“说句老实活，别人来，花上千银子，未见得能如此顺利。
“To speak frankly, another man might spend a thousand taels without finding things so easy.

老兄的事，没有话好说。
In your case, there is nothing to discuss.

不过，我为老兄设想，以后要诸事方便，书办那里不可不点缀点缀。
Still, for your own sake, if you want things to run smoothly afterwards, you must give the clerks a little something.

我为你引见一个人，
I shall introduce you to one of them.

你邀他出去吃个茶，说两句客气话，封一个数给他好了。”
Invite him out for tea, say a few civil words, and give him a packet containing this amount.”

说着，伸了一个指头。
He held up one finger.

这一个指头当然不是代表一千两，那么是十两呢，还是一百两呢？
It could hardly mean a thousand taels; did it mean ten, then, or a hundred?

想一想是宁可问清楚为妙。
Hu thought it wiser to make certain.

“好的。
“Very well.

我封一百二十两银子好了。”
I shall make it a hundred and twenty taels.”

他这样旁敲侧击他说，如果是十两，杨用之当然会纠正他。
This was an indirect way of asking: if Yang meant ten, he would naturally correct him.

“不必，不必！
“No, no!

一百两够了，
A hundred is enough.

通通在里头，
That covers everything.

你另外不必再花冤枉钱。”
There is no need to throw away any more money elsewhere.”


于是杨用之派人去找了户房一个书办来，五十多岁，衣着相当够气派。
Yang sent for a clerk from the revenue department, a man in his fifties whose clothes were decidedly impressive.

书办的官称为“书吏”，
Such men were formally known as clerical functionaries.

大小衙门基层的公务，只有书办才熟悉，这一点就是他们的“本钱”，
In yamens large and small, they alone understood the everyday workings of administration, and that knowledge was their capital.

其中的真实情况，以及关键、决窍，为不传之秘，
The true state of affairs, the crucial points, and the tricks of the business were secrets never divulged to outsiders.

所以书办虽无“世袭”的明文，但无形中父子相传，有世袭的惯例。
Although no written rule made a clerkship hereditary, in practice it passed from father to son.


府、县衙门“三班六房”，
Prefectural and county yamens had three divisions of runners and six administrative departments.

六房皆有书办，而以“刑房”的书办最神气，“户房”的书办最阔气。
All six departments employed clerks, of whom those in the criminal department put on the most imposing airs and those in the revenue department were the wealthiest.

户房书办简你“户书”，他之所以阔气，是因为额征钱粮地丁，
The latter owed their prosperity to the system for collecting land and poll taxes.

户部只问总数，不问细节，
The Board of Revenue concerned itself with the total assessed, not with the particulars.

当地谁有多少田、多少地，座落何方，等则如何？只有“户书”才一清二楚。
Who owned how much farmland, where each plot lay, and how it was graded—only the local revenue clerk knew all this exactly.

他们所凭借的就是祖传的一本秘册，称为“鱼鳞册”，
His authority rested on a secret register handed down through his family, known as a fish-scale register.

没有这本册子，天大的本事，也征不起钱粮。
Without it, a man of the greatest ability could not collect the taxes.

有了这本册子，不但公事可以顺利，户书本人也可以大发其财，
Possession of that book not only made official business possible but also enabled the clerk to grow rich.

多少年来钱粮地丁的征收，是一盘混帐，
For years the collection of taxes had been a hopeless muddle.

纳了钱粮的，未见得能收到“粮串”，不纳粮的却握有纳粮的凭证，
Those who paid did not necessarily receive receipts, while those who paid nothing might possess proof that they had paid.

反正“上头”只要征额够成数，如何张冠李戴，是不必管也无法管的。
As long as the required proportion of the assessment reached the authorities, no one above concerned himself with whose payment had been credited to whom; indeed, there was no practical way of untangling it.


因此，钱谷老夫子必得跟户书打交道。
A revenue secretary therefore had to deal with the revenue clerks.

厉害的户书可以控制钱谷老夫子，同样地，厉害的钱谷老夫子，也可以把户书治得服服帖帖。
A formidable clerk might keep the secretary under his thumb; an equally formidable secretary might bring his clerks completely to heel.

一般而论，总是和睦相处，情如家人，
Generally, however, they lived on good terms, as intimate as members of a family.

杨用之跟这个名叫郁四的户书就是这样。
That was the relationship between Yang and this clerk, whose name was Yu Si.

“老四！”杨用之用这个昵称关照：“这位是王大老爷的，也是我的好朋友，胡老爷！”
“Old Four,” Yang said familiarly, “this is Mr Hu, a close friend both of Prefect Wang and of mine.”

书办的身分本低，
A clerk’s standing was low.

郁四见这位胡老爷的来头不小，要行大礼，
Seeing what influential connections this Mr Hu possessed, Yu prepared to kneel in formal homage.

但胡雪岩的动作快，刚看他弯膝，便抢上去扶住他说：“郁四哥！幸会，幸会！”
Hu was quicker: the moment Yu bent his knees, he stepped forward and supported him. “Brother Yu! A pleasure to meet you.”

“胡老爷，这个称呼万万不敢当，
“Mr Hu, I could never presume to accept that form of address.

你叫我郁四好了。”
Please call me Yu Si.”

杨用之也觉得他不必如此谦虚，
Yang too felt that Hu need not be so modest.

便说：“你也叫他老四好了。”
“You may call him Old Four as well.”
