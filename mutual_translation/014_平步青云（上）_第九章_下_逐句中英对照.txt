A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume I — Chapter Nine, concluding part

“你到轿行里去喊顶轿子，
“Go to the chair agency and have them send a chair.

Tell them it's for me.”

很快地，簇新的一顶轿子抬到，三个年轻力壮的轿伕，态度非常谦恭，这自然是郁四吩咐过了的缘故，
A brand-new sedan chair soon arrived, carried by three strong young men whose manner was exceptionally deferential—naturally, because Yu had sent for them.

胡雪岩说了地址，上轿就走。
Hu gave the address, got in, and set off.

张家住在城外，就在码头旁边一条小巷子里，

轿子一抬进去就塞住了，

这条巷子，实在也难得有轿子经过，所以路人不但侧身而让，并且侧目而视，
Sedan chairs seldom came this way, so passers-by not only turned sideways to let it pass but stared at it.

其中一个就是阿珠。
Among them was A’zhu.

他没有看见，她却发现了，
Hu did not see her, but she saw him.

“喂，
“Hey!

喂！”她望着抬过门的轿子喊：

“怎么到这时候才来？”一见面就是埋怨的口气，
“Why have you come so late?” she complained the instant they met.

显见得是“一家人”，让左邻右舍发觉了，会引起诧异。
Her manner was plainly that of someone addressing a member of the family, which would surprise any neighbours who noticed.

阿珠自觉失言，立刻红晕上脸，强笑道：

“我们这条巷子里，难得有坐轿来的贵客！
“We rarely have distinguished guests arrive by chair in this lane!

请进来，请进来。”
Please come in.”

“你先进去。”
“You go in first.”

胡雪岩心细，看轿子停在门口，妨碍行人会挨骂，
Hu had noticed that the chair would obstruct pedestrians and invite abuse if left at the door.

He told the bearers to wait at the mouth of the lane, then entered.


进门就是客堂。
The front door opened straight into the reception room.

里面说话，大门外的人都听得见，自然不便，
Anyone outside could hear what was said there, making conversation awkward.

阿珠把他领到后面，
A’zhu led him to the rear.

隔着一个小小的天井，东面两问，看样子是卧室，
Across a tiny courtyard stood two rooms on the east, apparently bedrooms.

“We'll have to sit in my room,” A’zhu said hesitantly.

“It really isn't very convenient.”

“ 还不是伺候你胡老爷！”阿珠微带怨怼地答道，

“爹到衙门看你去了，
“Father's gone to the yamen to look for you.

“And you?

“Who'd wait for you?

I was waiting for Mother.”

“闲话少说。”胡雪岩说，

“要去通知你爹一声，
“Someone must tell your father.

We can't leave him waiting for nothing.”

“不用，
“There's no need.

说好了的，等不到就回来，

He should be home soon.”

As she spoke, A’zhu pushed open her door.

屋中刚刚裱糊过，四白落地，十分明亮。

一张床，一张梳头桌，收拾得很洁净，桌上还有只花瓶，插着几朵荷花。

“You can't speak without saying something improper.”

“Come, sit down and talk.”

“Bolt the door.

“Now you can stop worrying,” he answered with a smile.

“I can't be bothered.”

“A gold chain.

这怎么不好？

阿珠直望着他：“这样子讲究？”

“这算得了什么？
“That's nothing.

You'll have plenty of fine things in future.”

“自然是鸳鸯戏水。”
“Mandarin ducks playing in the water, of course.”

阿珠脸红了，低着头不作声。

“怎么样？”他催问，

“Would that be a good pattern?”

“我娘回来了！”
“Mother's back!”

阿珠慌忙起身，诸事不做，先照镜子，

镜子里一张面泛桃花的脸，鬓边也有些乱，她着急他说：“都是你害人！

这样子怎么走得出去？”
How can I go out looking like this?”

“白天不做虚心事，夜半敲门心不惊！
“If you've done nothing wrong by day, a knock at midnight won't frighten you.

怕什么？
What's there to fear?

我去开门，
I'll open the door.

你把心定下来。”
Take a moment to calm down.”

胡雪岩倒真沉得住气，
Hu was indeed perfectly composed.

把长衫抹一抹，泰然自若地走了出去，开开门来，笑嘻嘻地叫了一声，“干娘！”

“When did you arrive?”

“Only a little while ago.”

“阿珠呢？”
“Where's A’zhu?”

“At the back.”

胡雪岩知道阿珠红晕未退，有心救她一救，便问这样，问那样，绊住了阿珠的娘，容不得她抽身。

This one was still alive, making it especially valuable.

急于想去“活杀”，偏偏胡雪岩絮絮不休，
She wanted to prepare it while it was fresh, but Hu would not stop talking.

只好找个空隙，向里大喊：“阿珠阿！”
Finding a pause at last, she called into the house, “A’zhu!”

阿珠已经心定神闲，把发鬓梳得整整齐齐的走了出来。
A’zhu emerged, calm now and with her hair neatly combed.

她娘便吩咐她去剖鱼，剖她了等她来动手，又问胡雪岩喜欢清蒸，还是红烧呢？

“活鳜鱼不容易买到，
“A live mandarin fish is hard to come by.

自然是清蒸。”阿珠替他作了主。
Steamed, of course,” A’zhu decided for him.

胡雪岩还有许多事要办，
Hu still had much to do.

只待见老张一面，交代几句话就要走，
He had intended only to see Old Zhang, give him a few instructions, and leave.

现在看样子，这顿饭是非吃不可了！
Now it seemed he could not escape this meal.

这就索性在这里，跟老张把事情都商量好了再说。
He might as well stay and settle everything with Old Zhang.

“干娘！”他说，“吃饭是小事，
“Auntie,” he said, “the meal isn't important.

越简单越好、
The simpler the better.

I've a great deal to say when Old Zhang comes back.

市面要弄得很热闹，

大家都有得忙，工夫不能白糟蹋！”
Everyone will be busy, and we can't waste time!”

阿珠的娘知道他是实话，
She knew he meant it.

好在她手下快，
Fortunately, she worked quickly.

好老张从县衙门回家，饭菜都已齐备，

四个人团团坐下，边吃边谈。
The four sat round the table, eating and talking.


“一家人，我先要说句老实话。”
“We're family, so let me speak frankly.”

高踞上座的胡雪岩说：“明天一早，第一件事就是搬家！

不管什么地方，搬了再说，
Anywhere will do for now.

这里实在太小了。”
This place is much too small.”

老张夫妇，面面相觑，
Old Zhang and his wife exchanged glances.

他们的感想一样，
Both were thinking the same thing.

搬家是件大事，
Moving was a major undertaking.

要看房子，拣黄道吉日，
They must find a house and choose an auspicious day.

Though they had little furniture and few possessions, packing would still take two or three days.

胡雪岩一看他们的脸色就知通他们的心思，数着手指说。
Hu read their thoughts in their faces and counted off the points on his fingers.

“第一，房子明天一大早去看，
“First, look at houses early tomorrow.

象个样子就可以，
Anything reasonably presentable will do.

先租下来住了再说，
Rent it and move in.

好在自己要买房子，不过一个短局，她歹都无所谓。

明天一天把它都弄舒齐。”
Get everything settled tomorrow.”

“I'm afraid we couldn't manage it all tomorrow,” A’zhu's mother said hesitantly.

“Then take two days.”

“You enjoy giving orders without thinking what they mean for other people.”

“We can manage, we can!”

阿珠的娘不愿违拗胡雪岩的意思，
Her mother did not want to oppose him.

“Don't be afraid to spend money!”

“Honestly, I never imagined that this year's change of fortune would bring me so far!

“是啊！
“Indeed.

“就是这话。
“Exactly.

大家帮我来做！

“老张老实，越是他这样说，越觉得不安，
