CHAPTER ELEVEN｜第十一章


Wang Youling’s boats reached Hangzhou and moored once more at Wan’an Bridge.
王有龄的船到杭州，仍旧泊在万安桥。

His return was rather more splendid than his departure had been.
来时风光，与去时又不大相同。

When he left to take up his appointment, his official establishment was not yet complete: he had only two government boats and a few flags and insignia.
去时上任，仪制未备，不过两号官船，数面旗牌，

Now he returned to the provincial capital with five large official boats, attendants and servants in attendance, and bright banners flying.
这一次回省，共有五只大号官船，隶役侍应，旗帜鲜明。

Before he reached the landing, runners sent by the counties of Renhe and Qiantang were already on shore, clearing away onlookers and waiting to receive him.
未到码头，仁和、钱塘两县已派了差役在岸上照应，驱散闲人，静等泊岸，

He stepped into a large sedan chair and went directly to his residence.
坐上大轿，径回公馆。

Hu Xueyan, however, was in no hurry to go home.
胡雪岩却不忙回家，

A small sedan carried him straight to Fukang.
一乘小轿直接来到阜康，

He had sent no advance notice, so Liu Qingsheng was rather surprised to see him.
他事先并无消息，所以这一到，刘庆生颇感意外。

That was deliberate: by arriving unexpectedly, Hu could see how the bank was being run under Liu’s sole management.
胡雪岩原是故意如此，叫他猝不及防，才好看出刘庆生一手经理之下的阜康，是怎么个样子。

As he talked about the journey and affairs in Huzhou, he let his gaze move naturally about the shop.
因此，他一面谈路上和湖州的情形，一面很自然地把视线扫来扫去，

He soon had a fair impression of everything.
店堂里的情形，大致都看清楚了，

The assistants were courteous to customers, and the exchange business in silver and copper cash was reasonably brisk.
伙计接待顾客，也还客气，兑换银钱的生意，也还不少，

He was pleased with Liu.
所以对刘庆生觉得满意。


“Of the twenty thousand taels lent to Treasurer Lin, five thousand have already been repaid...”
“麟藩台的两万银子，已经还了五千……”

Liu gave a brief report of business during Hu’s absence, then invited him to examine the accounts.
刘庆生把这些日子以来的业务情形，作了个简略的报告。然后请胡雪岩看帐。

“There is no need,” Hu replied.
“不必看了。”胡雪岩问道：

“What balance of ready money should the books show?”
“帐上应该结存的现银有多少？”

“Here is the general ledger.”
“总帐在这里，”

Liu consulted it.
刘庆生翻看帐簿，

Including notes that could be cashed immediately, the available balance was a little over seventy-five thousand taels.
说结存的现银，包括立刻可以兑现的票子，一共七万五千多银子。

“How much must be paid out within the next three days?”
“三天以内要付出去的有多少？”

“Less than thirty thousand.”
“三万不到。”

“And tomorrow?”
“明天呢？”胡雪岩又问。

“Nothing is due tomorrow.”
“明天没有要付的。”

“Good.
“那好！”胡雪岩说，

I shall take seventy thousand taels.
“我提七万银子，

I need it for only one day.”
只要用一天好了。”

Hu took up a brush, wrote an order for the withdrawal, and handed it over.
说着拿笔写了一张提银七万两的条子，递了过去。

This was a test of whether Liu’s actual holdings matched his accounts.
他这是一个试探，要看看刘庆生的帐目与结存是不是相符？

To demand to see the reserves would suggest distrust, so Hu had devised this little stratagem.
如果叫他拿库存出来看，显得对人不相信，所以玩了这么一记小小的花样。

Without the slightest hesitation, Liu opened the strongbox, counted out seventy thousand taels in customers’ bank drafts, and placed them in his hands.
等刘庆生毫不迟疑地开了保险箱，点齐七万两的客票送到他手里，

“I shall use these today and get the money back tomorrow,” Hu said.
他又说了：“今天用出去，明天就可以收回来。

“Do not worry; it will not interfere with the payments due the day after.
你放心，不会耽误后天的用途。

I may not need all seventy thousand.
说不定用不到七万，

I am merely allowing a margin.”
我是多备些。“


In those few moments, without anyone suspecting it, Hu had tested both Liu’s integrity and his ability.
就这么片刻的工夫，他已经神不知、鬼不觉地把刘庆生的操守和才干，考察了一番。

He went home to pay his respects to his mother and was telling his wife of the results of his journey when a messenger arrived from Wang.
回家拜见了老母，正在跟妻子谈此行的成就，王有龄派人来请，

There was an urgent matter to discuss; would he come at once?
说有要紧事商量，请他即刻到王家见面。

It was already nine in the evening when Hu reached Wang’s house.
到得王家，已经晚上九点钟了。

Wang was pacing his study.
王有龄正在书房里踱方步，

At the sight of him, he frowned.
一见胡雪岩就皱着眉说：

“I have been given a quite unexpected assignment.
“搞了件意想不到的差使，

I must go to Xincheng.”
要到新城去一趟。”

Xincheng, also known as Xindeng, was a county under Hangzhou Prefecture, between Fuyang and Tonglu.
新城又称新登，是杭州府属的一县，在富阳与桐庐之间，

That stretch of the Fuchun River, famous for Yan Ziling’s fishing terrace, was a place of beautiful scenery where poets and writers loved to linger.
那一条富春江以严子陵的钓台得名，风光明媚，是骚人墨客歌咏留连的胜区，

Xincheng itself, however, was only a small mountain town.
但新城却是个小小的山城。

What could the acting prefect of Huzhou have to do there?
湖州府署理知府，跑到那儿去干什么？

“Have you been commissioned to try a case?” Hu asked.
“莫非奉委审案子？”胡雪岩问。

“There is a case, but I am not being sent to hear it.
“案子倒是有件案子，不是去审问。”王有龄答道：

A monk in Xincheng has gathered people to resist the grain tax.
“新城有个和尚，聚众抗粮，

Governor Huang wants me to take troops and suppress them.”
黄抚台要我带兵去剿办。”


Hu was greatly alarmed.
听得这话，胡雪岩大吃一惊，

“This is no matter to trifle with.
“这不是当耍的事。”

Mr Xuexuan, have you ever commanded troops?”
他问，“雪公，你带过兵没有？”

“That is not the main difficulty.
“这倒不关紧要，

When I was with my father during his appointment in Yunnan, I led his bodyguard to arrest Miao tribesmen who had rebelled.
我从前随老太爷在云南任上，带亲兵抓过作乱的苗子。

But this is a different situation.
不过这情形是不同的，

I hear that the people of Xincheng are exceptionally fierce.”
听说新城的民风强悍得很。

The inhabitants of mountain towns generally tended to be tough and combative, and Xincheng was particularly so.
“凡是山城的百姓，总以强悍的居多。新城这地方，尤其与众不同，

During the Five Dynasties, in the time of King Qian Wusu, it had produced a celebrated man named Luo Yin.
那里在五代钱武肃王的时候，出过一个名人，叫做罗隐，

Among ordinary people in the two Zhe regions, Jiangxi, and Fujian, “Scholar Luo Yin” enjoyed great fame.
在两浙和江西，福建的民间，“罗隐秀才”的名气甚大，

His words were said to foretell events without fail, and many strange deeds and stories were associated with him.
据说出语成谶，言必有中，而他本人亦多奇行异事。

The people of Xincheng had inherited something of his proud, stubborn spirit and were therefore far from easy to handle.
新城的民风，继承了他的那股傲岸倔强之气，所以很不容易对付。


“Yes,” Hu said.
“是啊！”胡雪岩答道：

“This is troublesome.
“这很麻烦。

A monk who gathers a crowd to resist taxes is plainly a restless fellow.
和尚聚众抗粮，可知是个不安分的人。

If you take troops there, you may provoke a popular uprising.
如果带了兵去，说不定激成民变。

You must be careful.”
雪公，你要慎重。”

“That is precisely what I fear.
“我所怕的正就是这一点。

Besides, once soldiers are sent in...”
再说，一带兵去，那情形……”

Wang shook his head vigorously.
王有龄大摇其头，

“It will be worse still.”
“越发糟糕！”

Hu understood.
这话胡雪岩懂。

The Green Standard troops had deteriorated beyond remedy; the soldiers were truly worse than bandits.
绿营兵丁，已到了不可救药的地步，真正是“兵不如匪”，

Wherever a detachment was sent, the local people suffered.
一带队下去，地方老百姓行就遭殃。

Thinking of this, Hu saw a possible way forward.
想到这一天，胡雪岩觉得事有可为。

“Mr Xuexuan, wherever you go, there are always reasonable people.
“雪公！随便什么地方，总有明事理的人。

In my opinion, it would be best to keep the troops where they are.
照我看。兵以不动为妙，

You might go to Xincheng yourself, find respected local gentlemen, and explain the consequences clearly.
你不妨单枪匹马，到新城找着地方上有声望的绅士，把利害关系说明白。

The trouble should then be capable of settlement.”
此事自然能够化解。”

“That is sound advice.”
“话是不错。”

Wang lowered his voice.
王有龄放低了声音说，

“The difficulty is that reducing a large disturbance to a small one, and then making it disappear altogether, will not be enough.
“为难的是，大事化小，小事化无还不够。

The authorities fear that, with unrest everywhere, other troublemakers will imitate this example.
上头的意思是，现在各地风声都很紧，怕刁民学样捣乱，

They insist on severe punishment for the ringleaders.”
非要严办祸首不可。”

“Whether to be severe or lenient is a matter for the second stage.”
“不管是严是宽，那是第二步的事！”

“Exactly!”
“对！”

Wang understood at once.
王有龄一下领悟了，

Whatever else was to be done, the immediate situation must first be brought under control.
不管怎么样，要眼前先把局势平服了下来，

Only then could they consider the next step.
才能谈得到第二步。

He thought for a moment and rose.
他想了想，站起身来说，

“I must pay someone a visit and make a preliminary arrangement.”
“我要去拜个客，先作一番部署。”


“Whom are you going to see?”
“拜哪个？”

“Commander Kui.
“魁参将。

He was formerly stationed at Jiaxing and has now been transferred to the provincial capital.
他原来驻防嘉兴，现在调到省城。

Governor Huang has ordered him to lead the troops accompanying me to Xincheng.
黄抚台派他带兵跟我到新城，

I must discuss matters with him.”
我得跟他商量一下。”

“What do you intend to say?”
“雪公，你预备怎么跟他说？”

“I shall explain that conciliation comes first and ask him to wait for my instructions before marching.
“我把以安抚为先的宗旨告诉他，请他听我的招呼出队，

He must not act recklessly.”
不能胡来。”

“It may be difficult to keep him from marching,” Hu said.
“叫他不出队，怕办不到。”胡雪岩说，

“Whenever Green Standard soldiers hear of such an assignment, they think their chance to make money has come.
“绿营兵一听见这种差使，都当发财的机会到了。

Why should they listen to you?”
哪里肯听你的话？”

“Then what do you suggest?”
“那么照你说，该怎么办呢？”

“You must offer him some advantage.
“总要许他点好处。”

What you need from him now is not that he march, but that he refrain from marching.”
胡雪岩说，“现在不是求他出队，是求他不要出队。”

“If conciliation fails, I shall still have to rely on him.”
“万一安抚不下来，还是要靠他。”

Wang nodded, then reconsidered.
王有龄点点头，下了个转语：

“Still, you have put your finger on the matter.
“不过，你的话确是‘一针见血’，

If I promise him a reward first, I can then move him forward or hold him back as I choose.
我先许了他的好处，那就收发由心，

He will obey my direction.”
都听我的指挥了。”


That night Wang visited Commander Kui.
当夜王有龄去拜访了魁参将，

He promised to apply to Governor Huang for funds for the troops and, once the affair was settled, to place Kui first on the list recommended for rewards.
答应为他在黄抚台那里请饷，将来事情平定以后，“保案”中一定把他列为首功。

In return, he hoped Kui would listen to him—which really meant submit to his command.
但希望他听自己的话，实在是要他听自己的指挥。

Seeing that Wang understood how things should be managed, Kui agreed readily.
魁参将见王有龄很知趣，很爽快地答应照办。

This unexpected assignment overturned all Wang’s original plans.
由于王有龄遭遇了这么一件意外的差使，把他原来的计划都打乱了，

He could not divide himself among his other duties and had to rely on Hu’s help.
该办的事无法分身，只有胡雪岩帮他的忙。

Business with the provincial treasurer’s yamen came first.
首先是藩司衙门的公事要紧，

Hu used fifty thousand taels of the customers’ drafts withdrawn from Fukang to make the payment into the provincial treasury.
胡雪岩用他从阜康取来的客票，解入藩库，

He then returned to Liu Qingsheng the fifty thousand taels in banknotes brought from Huzhou and supplied by Yu Si, together with the twenty thousand left from his withdrawal.
把湖州带来，由郁四调来的五万银票，连同多下的两万，一起还了给刘庄生。

There were also many personal obligations of Wang’s to attend to: gifts to be delivered here, money to be sent there.
此外还有许多王有龄个人的应酬，何处该送礼，何处该送钱，

Hu enlisted Liu’s help, and the two spent an entire day rushing about before everything was finally arranged.
胡雪岩找着刘庆生帮忙，两个人整整奔走了一天，算是都办妥了。


“Now I must attend to my own affairs,” Hu said after reporting each item to Wang.
“这就该忙我自己的事了。”胡雪岩把经手的事项，一一向王有龄交代过后，这样对他说，

“I have built up this business from nothing, and it must now begin to produce results.
“我赤手空拳做出来的市面，现在都该要有个着落。

Everything depends on those boatloads of silk.
命脉都在这几船丝上面，

I cannot afford the slightest carelessness.”
一点大意不得。”

Wang fell silent.
王有龄哑然。

He, too, would be going to Xincheng with little to rely on.
他此刻到新城，也等于赤手空拳，

At the very least, he wanted a trusted man beside him, someone with whom he could discuss a difficulty or an emergency.
至少要有个心腹在身边，遇到疑难危急的时候，也有个人可以商量。

But Hu had made his position clear, and Wang knew how much this silk venture meant to him.
但胡雪岩既已做了这样的表示，而且也知道这一次的丝生意，对他的关系极大，

He could no longer bring himself to ask Hu to stay and help.
所以原想留他帮忙的话，这时候就无论如何说不出口了。

Hu naturally saw his disappointment and helplessness.
他的失望无奈的神色，胡雪岩自然看得出来。

He thought, with genuine regret, that there was nothing he could do.
心里在想：这真叫爱莫能助！

First, he truly had no time to spare.
第一，实在抽不出空，

Second, he knew nothing of Xincheng.
第二，新城地方不熟，

Third, leading troops and dealing with swords and guns was a distinctly alarming business.
第三，带兵出队，动刀动枪的事，也真有点“吓势势”，

Better not to involve himself unnecessarily.
还是不必多事为妙。

He therefore asked no more about the matter.
因为如此，他就不去打听这件事了。

Instead, he met Fat Zhang and Liu and made careful arrangements for everything that would need attention or communication during his month or so in Shanghai.
管自己跟张胖子和刘庆生去碰头，把他到上海这个把月中，需要料理或者联络的事，都作了妥帖的安排。

Three days passed.
三天工夫过去，

The silk boats reached Hangzhou, and Chen Shilong accompanied Old Zhang to Fukang to report their arrival.
丝船到了杭州，陈世龙陪着老张到阜康来报到。


Asked about the journey, Chen said that everything had gone smoothly.
问起路上的情形，陈世龙说一路都很顺利，

They had, however, heard a great deal of news.
不过听到许多消息，

Disturbances involving organized resistance to grain taxes were breaking out everywhere, and rumours were rife.
各地聚众抗粮的纠纷，层出不穷，谣言极盛，

None of it was a good sign.
都非好兆。

He urged Hu to board that very night and set off early next morning, reaching the Songjiang district as soon as possible.
因此，他劝胡雪岩当夜就下船，第二天一早动身，早早赶到松江地界，

With You Laowu there to protect them, they could feel secure.
有尤五“保镖”就可以放心了。

“Brother Shilong’s advice is sound,” Liu said.
“世龙兄这话很实在。

“The sooner Mr Hu arrives, the better.
胡先生早到早好。

Let me give a modest farewell dinner tonight.”
今天晚上我做个小东，给胡先生送行。”

He turned to Zhang and Chen.
刘庆生又面邀老张和陈世龙说：

“And for you two as well.”
“也是替你们两位送行。”

“In that case, invite one more guest—a lady.”
“既如此，你就再多请一位‘堂客’。”

“Certainly, certainly.”
“是，是。”

Liu knew that Hu meant A’zhu.
刘庆生知道胡雪岩指的是阿珠，

“The moon will still be lovely tonight.
“今天夜里的月亮还很好，

I shall take everyone for an outing on West Lake.”
我请大家到西湖上去逛逛。”

“One grows tired of boats after sitting in them all day,” Hu said with a smile.
“一天到晚坐船也坐厌了。”胡雪岩笑道，

“Let us go to City God Hill instead.”
“还是去逛城隍山的好。”

“City God Hill it shall be.
“就是城隍山！

The host follows his guests’ wishes.” Liu asked Zhang, “Is your daughter on the boat?”
主从客便。”刘庆生问老张：“令媛在船上？”

“Yes.
“是的，

I shall fetch her.”
我去接她。”

“Why go yourself?” Hu said.
“何必你自己去？”胡雪岩说，

“Let Shilong make the trip and bring her here first.”
“叫世龙走一趟，先接她到这里来再说。”


Chen agreed at once and hurried off.
听得这话，陈世龙连声答应着，站起来就走。

About two hours later, two small sedan chairs arrived outside.
等了有个把时辰，两乘小轿，抬到门前，

A’zhu stepped down, wearing, unusually, a silk skirt.
阿珠走下轿来，只见她破例着条绸裙子，

Her large, unbound feet showed beneath its hem, and the skirt swayed awkwardly as she walked.
但盈尺莲船，露在裙幅外面。走起路来，裙幅摆动得很厉害，

Before anyone else could shake his head, she laughed in embarrassment.
别人还不曾摇头，她自己先不好意思地笑了：

“This wretched skirt!
“这条断命的裙子，

I simply cannot get used to it.”
我真正着不惯！”

“Then why make life difficult for yourself?” Hu asked with a smile. “Why suffer needlessly?”
“那你何必自己跟自己过不去，找罪来受？”胡雪岩这样笑着问。

“There!
“喏！

It is all his doing.”
都是他。”

She meant Chen Shilong, pointing at him and naturally glancing in his direction.
他是指陈世龙。阿珠一面说，一面拿手指着，眼风自然而然地瞟了过去。

Though her words sounded like a complaint, neither her voice nor her expression conveyed blame.
话中虽带着埋怨，脸色和声音却并无责怪之意，

It was almost as though whatever Chen said was something she ought to obey.
倒象是陈世龙怎么说，她就该怎么听似地。

Old Zhang did not notice this subtle change; Liu was entirely in the dark; even A’zhu herself was unaware of anything unusual.
这微妙的神情，老张看不出来，刘庆生更是如蒙在鼓里，甚至连阿珠自己都没有觉察有什么异样，

Hu understood, however.
但胡雪岩心里明白，

He smiled at Chen and said no more.
向陈世龙笑了一下，没有再说下去。

“Let us decide where to eat,” Liu said.
“我们商量商量，到哪里去吃饭？”

Still regarding A’zhu as Hu’s sweetheart, he made a point of consulting her.
刘庆生还把阿珠当做胡雪岩的心上人，特地征询她的意见：

“Would Huangfan’er do?”
“ ‘皇饭儿’好不好？”

This was the finest restaurant serving local dishes, conveniently situated at the foot of City God Hill.
最好的一家本地馆子，就在城隍山脚下，

They could eat and then go up the hill, so everyone agreed.
吃完逛山，正好顺路，自然一致同意。

Liu gave them a generous dinner, after which they climbed the hill to drink tea and enjoy the cool air.
于是刘庆生作东，吃了一顿丰盛的晚饭，上城隍山去品茗纳凉。


The moon made the night almost as bright as day, and many people were out enjoying themselves.
这夜月明如昼，游客甚多，

Under the trees, Hu discussed the recent state of business with Old Zhang and Liu, while A’zhu and Chen talked quietly together.
树下纳凉，胡雪岩跟老张和刘庆生在谈近来的市面，阿珠和陈世龙便小声闲话。

She knew Hangzhou far better than he did, so she did most of the talking, pointing towards the myriad lights below and describing the city’s sights and customs.
杭州的一切，他不如她熟，所以尽是她的话，指点着山下的万家灯火，为他介绍杭州的风物。

As the second watch approached, Zhang yawned.
到得二更将近，老张打个哈欠说：

“Time to go back.
“回去吧！
