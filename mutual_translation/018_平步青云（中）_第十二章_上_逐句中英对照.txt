第十二章｜CHAPTER TWELVE


到新城先到富阳，走钱塘江这条水路。
To reach Xincheng, one first travelled to Fuyang by water along the Qiantang River.

等送行的王有龄一走，嵇鹤龄把胡雪岩留了下来，
Once Wang, who had come to see him off, had left, Ji asked Hu to stay a little longer.

说还有几句话要谈。
There were still a few things he wished to say.

到船舱中坐定，他从拜匣里取出一张梅红单帖，放在胡雪岩面前，
Seated in the cabin, he took a plum-red card from his visiting-card box and placed it before Hu.

上面写的是“嵇鹤龄，以字行。
It read: “Ji Heling, known by his courtesy name.

湖北罗田人，
Native of Luotian, Hubei.

嘉庆二十一年十月初四午时生。”
Born at the noon hour on the fourth day of the tenth month, in the twenty-first year of Jiaqing.”

“喔！”胡雪岩笑道：
“Oh!” Hu laughed.

“你倒真巴结，
“You certainly don't waste time.

应该我先去讨瑞云的八字来给你。
I ought to have obtained Ruiyun's birth particulars for you first.

其实，这也可以不必。
Though really, we could dispense with this.”

““不是，不是！”
“No, no!”

嵇鹤龄摇着手说，
Ji waved his hand.

“这张帖子是交给你的。
“The card is for you.

雪岩兄，我想高攀，我们拜个把子。”
Brother Xueyan, I hope you will grant me the honour of becoming my sworn brother.”

“这……”
“This...”

胡雪岩愣了一下，接着喜逐颜开地说：
Hu paused in surprise, then beamed.

“那是我高攀了！
“The honour would be mine!

不过，此刻来不及备帖子，但是也要磕个头。”
I haven't time to prepare a card now, but we must at least bow.”

“这都好办，
“That can all be arranged.

等我新城回来再行礼。”嵇鹤龄说：
We can perform the ceremony when I return from Xincheng.

“相知贵相知心。
What matters in friendship is understanding each other's hearts.

如果你不嫌弃，此刻我们就改称呼。
If you accept me, let's change our forms of address now.

你今年贵庚？”
How old are you?”


“我小得多。”
“Much younger than you.”

胡雪岩改了称呼，叫一声：

“大哥！”
“Elder Brother!”

接着便给“大哥”磕头。
Then he knelt to bow to him.

嵇鹤龄急忙也跪下还礼，自然称他“二弟”。
Ji hastily knelt in return, naturally calling him “Second Brother.”

两人对拜了一拜，连“撮土为香”都用不着，就结成了异姓手足。
They exchanged a bow and became brothers of different surnames without even taking a pinch of earth to serve as incense.

拜罢起身，彼此肩上的感觉便都不同了，
When they rose, each felt a change in the weight on his shoulders.

嵇鹤龄是减轻而胡雪岩是加重，
Ji's had lightened; Hu's had grown heavier.

“大哥！”他说，“你尽管放心到新城去，专心一致办事，
“Elder Brother,” Hu said, “go to Xincheng with your mind at ease and devote yourself entirely to the work.

家里一点都不用记挂，
Don't spend a moment worrying about things at home.

一切都有我！”
I'll attend to everything.”

“那自然要托你。”
“Naturally I must rely on you.

嵇鹤龄又说，“不过眼前有瑞云在，也没有什么不放心的，
But with Ruiyun there now, I have little cause for anxiety.

我走了，你也赶紧动身到上海去吧！
Once I've gone, hurry to Shanghai yourself.

早去早回，我们换帖子请客。
Go early and return early, and we'll exchange cards and give a feast.”

““好的，
“Very well.

我晓得，
I understand.

一路顺风。”
A safe journey!”


胡雪岩离船登岸，坐轿进城，
Hu left the boat, went ashore, and took a chair into the city.

等王有龄到家，他接着也到了他那里，脸上是掩抑不住的笑容，
He reached Wang's house just after Wang himself, unable to suppress his smile.

王有龄夫妇都觉得奇怪，问他什么事这么高兴。
Both husband and wife wondered what had made him so happy.

“你们两位再也想下到的，
“You'd never guess!

就雪公上了岸那一刻工夫，我跟鹤龄拜成把弟兄了。”
In the few moments after Mr Xuexuan went ashore, Heling and I became sworn brothers.”

“太好了！
“Splendid!

恭喜，恭喜！”
Congratulations!”

王有龄对他妻子说：
Wang turned to his wife.

“太太，这一来我们跟鹤龄的情分也不同了。”
“My dear, this changes our relationship with Heling too.”

“真成了一家人，
“Now we really are one family.

至亲好友，原是越多越好。”
The more close relations and good friends, the better.”

“说到这一层，我倒想起来了。”
“That reminds me.”

胡雪岩从马褂口袋里摸出个红封套递向王太太。
Hu took a red envelope from his jacket pocket and offered it to Mrs Wang.

她不肯接，
She would not accept it.

“这是什么？”
“What is this?”

“瑞云的聘金……”
“Ruiyun's betrothal money...”

话没有完，王有龄先就乱喊：
Before he could finish, Wang protested loudly.

“不行，不行！
“No, no!

这怎么好收他的？
How could we take his money?

你还给他。”
Give it back.”


“慢慢，
“Wait.

你不要吵！”
Don't make such a noise!”

玉太太挥挥手说：
Mrs Wang waved a hand.

“我先要问问清楚，瑞云怎么样？
“First I want to be clear about Ruiyun.

她自己答应了没有？”
Has she agreed herself?”

“看样子是千肯万肯的了。”
“She seems willing a thousand times over.”

“哪有这么快？”
“How could it happen so quickly?”

王太太不信，
Mrs Wang was unconvinced.

“她到底怎么说的？”
“What exactly did she say?”

“这也用不着明说。”
“It hardly needs to be said outright.”

胡雪岩把昨晚上的情形讲了一遍。
Hu recounted the previous evening.

这些眉目传情，灵犀暗通的事，本来就是最好的话题，胡雪岩又有意刻画入微，
Affection conveyed in looks and unspoken understanding always made an engaging story, and he deliberately described every nuance.

所以把王有龄夫妇听得津津有味，都是微张着嘴，耸起两面唇角，随时准备放声大笑的神态。
The couple listened with relish, mouths slightly open and the corners lifted, ready to burst out laughing at any moment.

“差也差不多了。”等他讲完，王有龄点点头说。
“It seems nearly settled,” Wang said with a nod when Hu finished.

“到底不是什么‘千肯万肯’，
“Nearly isn't ‘willing a thousand times over,’” Mrs Wang replied.

总还要我来说两句，她才会松口。”
“I shall still have to say a few words before she gives her consent.”


“拜托，拜托！”
“Then I entrust it to you!”

胡雪岩拱一拱手，趁势又把红封套递了过去。
Hu clasped his hands and used the opportunity to offer the red envelope again.

王太太已经接到手里，王有龄一把夺了回来，塞回胡雪岩：
Mrs Wang had just taken it when her husband snatched it back and thrust it at Hu.

“这不能收的。”
“We can't accept this.”

“没有什么不能收。”王太太接口，
“Of course we can,” she answered.

“我们瑞云是人家聘了去的，不是不值钱白送的。
“Ruiyun is being honourably betrothed, not given away as though she were worth nothing.

兄弟，你把聘金交给我，
Brother, give me the money.

我另有用处。”
I have another use for it.”

“你有什么用处？”
“What use?”

王有龄大为不悦，几乎要跟太太吵架了。
Wang was thoroughly displeased and on the verge of quarrelling with her.

“我说给你听！”
“I'll tell you!”

王太太的声音也很大，
Her voice rose too.

“瑞云一份嫁妆归我们预备。
“We shall provide Ruiyun's dowry ourselves.

这一千两银了，我另外交给她，是她的私房钱。
These thousand taels will go to her separately, as money of her own.

请问王大老爷，可以不可以？
May I ask whether Your Honour Wang permits that?”

“王有龄的表情立刻改变了，
His expression changed at once.

歉意地笑着，却用埋怨的语气回答：
He smiled apologetically, though his reply sounded reproachful.

“太太，你何不早说？”
“My dear, why didn't you say so earlier?”

“现在说也不晚。”
“It isn't too late to say it now.”

王太太拿着红封套，得意地走了。
Mrs Wang carried off the envelope in triumph.


“雪岩！”
“Xueyan.”

王有龄略有忧色，
Wang looked a little worried.

“我们先商量一下，万一嵇鹤龄此去无功，下一步该如何？”
“We ought to discuss what comes next if Ji fails.”

“先抚后剿”的宗旨是早已定好了的，
Their principle had long been settled: conciliation first, force afterwards.

抚既不成，自然是派兵进剿，
If conciliation failed, naturally troops would be sent.

何需问得？
Why ask?

但胡雪岩了解他的内心，便不肯这么回答，只说：
But Hu understood what was troubling him and would not answer so literally.

“你不必过虑！
“Don't worry too much.

鹤龄跟我说过，无论如何，自保之策，总是有的，
Heling told me that whatever happened, he had ways to protect himself.

可见得他极有把握。
He must be very confident.

而且，人逢喜事精神爽，
Besides, happy events lift a man's spirits.

他此去没有后顾之虑，专心一致对付公事，
He has no domestic worries now and can concentrate entirely on the public business.

当然无往不利。
Of course he'll succeed.”

“听他侃侃而谈，声音中极具自信，王有龄不知不觉受了鼓舞，
Hu spoke fluently and with such assurance that Wang was unconsciously encouraged.

愁怀一放，连连点头。
His worries eased, and he nodded repeatedly.

“还有，雪公，”胡雪岩又说，
“And another thing, Mr Xuexuan.

“你正鸿运当头，
Your fortune is at its height.

瑞云也要托你的福，她又是一副福相，
Ruiyun will share your good luck, and she has a fortunate face herself.

看起来必有帮夫运，
She looks certain to bring prosperity to her husband.

所以鹤龄一定马到成功。
Heling is bound to succeed at once.

瑞云迟早是个‘掌印夫人’！”
Sooner or later Ruiyun will be an official's lady!”

这一说，王有龄越发高兴，
Wang was still more pleased.

“不错，不错！
“Quite right!

我也觉得，这无论如何不是倒霉的时候。”
I too feel this cannot possibly be an unlucky time.

他又说：“等鹤龄功成回省，我一定力保他接归安县。
When Heling returns successfully to the provincial capital, I'll strongly recommend him for Gui'an County.

这个缺，一年起码有五万银子进帐。”
That post brings in at least fifty thousand taels a year.”


胡雪岩心想，归安县现在由王有龄兼署，
Hu reflected that Wang was currently acting magistrate of Gui'an himself.

保了嵇鹤龄，就等于从他自己荷包里挖五万银子出来。
Recommending Ji would be like taking fifty thousand taels out of his own pocket.

一时慷慨，终必失悔，却又是说不出的苦。
A moment's generosity would eventually bring regret, and regret he could not express.

朋友相交，到了这地步一定不能善始善终，
A friendship brought to that point could hardly end as well as it had begun.

倒要劝一劝他。
Hu ought to dissuade him.

“归安是一等大县，
“Gui'an is a large county of the first class.

只怕上头不肯。
The authorities might not agree.

如果碰个钉子，彼此不好，
A refusal would be awkward for both of you.

我倒有个想法。”
I have another idea.”

“噢！你说，
“Tell me!

一定是好主意。”
It's sure to be a good one.”

“你看是不是好主意？”
“See what you think.

胡雪岩说，“海运局的差使，你又兼顾不到，
You can't give proper attention to the Sea Transport Bureau as well.

何不保鹤龄接替？”
Why not recommend Heling to succeed you there?”

“啊！”
“Ah!”

王有龄恍然大悟，
Wang understood.

“对了！
“Exactly!

这才是一举数得。”
That would achieve several things at once.”

胡雪岩懂他这句话的意思，
Hu knew what he meant.

这一举数得就包括了他的便利在内，嵇鹤龄接替海运局的差使，他经手的几笔垫款、借款，料理起来就顺利了。
The benefits would include Hu's own convenience: with Ji in charge of the bureau, the advances and loans Hu had arranged would be easier to settle.


“准定这么办，”王有龄又问，
“That's what we'll do,” Wang said.

“你哪天走？”
“When are you leaving?”

“至迟后天一走要走了。”
“The day after tomorrow at the latest. I really must go.”

“那好，
“Good.

你办完了事就回来。”
Come back as soon as your business is finished.”

王有龄放低了声音说，
Wang lowered his voice.

“我托你带笔钱去。”
“I'd like you to take some money for me.”

带给谁？
For whom?

心照不宣，
Neither needed to say.

胡雪岩只问：“带多少？”
Hu asked only, “How much?”

“给她二三百两银子吧！”
“Give her two or three hundred taels.”

“知道了，
“Understood.

我替你垫付二百两，回来再算。”
I'll advance two hundred and settle with you when I return.”

于是胡雪岩回家重整行装。
Hu went home to repack.

第二天抽出工夫来，亲自上街买了好些茶食，去探望嵇鹤龄的子女，
The next day he found time to buy a variety of tea snacks himself and visit Ji's children.

只见瑞云把那六个孩子料理得干干净净，心里大为宽慰。
Seeing all six clean and well cared for by Ruiyun greatly reassured him.

他跟嵇鹤龄拜把子的事，没有跟他的儿女说，却跟瑞云说了。
He did not tell the children about his sworn brotherhood with their father, but he told Ruiyun.

正在谈着，来了位意想不到的“堂客”，是王太太。
As they talked, an unexpected lady visitor arrived: Mrs Wang.

她的来意，胡雪岩明白，
Hu understood why she had come.

他没有理由妨碍她们谈正事，便笑笑走了。
He had no reason to obstruct their serious conversation, so he smiled and left.


一到松江，仍旧在出四鳃鲈的秀野桥上岸，
On reaching Songjiang, Hu again went ashore at Xiuye Bridge, famed for its four-gilled perch.

胡雪岩没有带跟班，却有许多零零碎碎的行李，多是些杭州的土产，
He had brought no attendant, but carried many small pieces of luggage, mostly local products from Hangzhou.

但他不怕照应不了。
He had no fear of being unable to manage them.

叫船家找了轿子和挑伕来，关照到通裕米行，
He asked the boatman to fetch a chair and porters and told them to go to Tongyu Rice Firm.

那就连价钱都不用讲。
There was no need even to bargain over the price.

因为“车、船、店、脚、牙”虽然难惹，却也十分开窍，
Carters, boatmen, innkeepers, porters, and brokers might be difficult people, but they understood how the world worked.

通裕米行的后台是谁？码头上没有一个人不晓得，也没有一个人不买帐。
Everyone at the wharf knew who stood behind Tongyu, and everyone respected his influence.

到了通裕，却好遇见陈世龙在门口，一见面就说，

“胡先生，我天天在盼望，
“Mr Hu, I've been waiting for you every day!

为啥到今天才到？”

“说来话长。”
“It's a long story.

胡雪岩问道，“尤五哥在不在松江？”
Is Brother You in Songjiang?”

“昨天晚上刚从上海回来。”
“He came back from Shanghai only last night.”

“好，
“Good.

进去再说。”
Let's go inside and talk.”


通裕的人听见声音也迎了出来，

代为开发轿子挑伕，把他奉为上宾，同时赶紧派人去通知尤五。
They settled with the chair-bearers and porters, treated him as an honoured guest, and hurried to send word to You.

“不必，不必！”
“No need!”

明雪岩拦着他们说，
Hu stopped them.

“我去看尤五哥，跟他一起到老太爷那里请安。”
“I'll call on Brother You and go with him to pay my respects to the old master.”

He checked the gifts and told Chen to carry them and follow.

尤五家住得不远，不必再用轿马。
You's house was near enough to require neither chair nor horse.

陈世龙一面走，一面把到了松江以后的情形。扼要地报告，
As they walked, Chen briefly reported what had happened since their arrival in Songjiang.

“货色进上海丝栈了。”陈世龙说道，
“The silk is in a warehouse in Shanghai,” Chen said.

“是尤五叔作的主。
“Uncle You arranged it.

堆在上海二洋径桥北大街的裕记丝栈，

栈单在尤五叔那里，

他要交给我，我不肯收。

I do have a tally of the quantities.”

陈世龙算是机警的，栈单在人家那里，他自己留着一张计数的单子，多少算个字佯。
Chen showed some caution: while another man held the receipt, he kept a written tally as a form of evidence.

其实无用！

粑栈单收了下来，原是正办，
The proper course was to accept the warehouse receipt.

否则就索性大方到底。

捏一张记数单子算是啥名堂？

这是陈世龙做事不够老到，也正是自己要教导他的地方，
This showed Chen's inexperience, precisely the sort of thing Hu must teach him.

但此时此地，不便多说，点点头就算了。
But the time and place were unsuitable for a lecture, so he merely nodded.

胡雪岩方在踌躇，尤五已迎了出来，神情显得异常亲热。

“我以为你还有几天才来。
“I thought you wouldn't arrive for several more days.

王大老爷的公事有了头绪没有？”

胡雪岩飞快地在心里转念头，同时口中答道：“有头绪了！

“Good.

回头我们细谈。”

“厅里那班‘神道’，我不替你引见了。
“I won't introduce you to those ‘divinities’ in the hall.

你懂？”

胡雪岩一想就明白，很爽脆地签了一个字：“懂！”

“那好。你先请到通裕去，
“Then go back to Tongyu first.

“不要紧，不要紧！
“No hurry.

我们在老太爷那里碰头好了。”

“老太爷倒常提到你。
“He often speaks of you.

I'll send someone to take you.”

“这位小老弟也见过老太爷，
“This young fellow has met him too.

蛮喜欢他的。”

听得这句话，陈世龙脸上象飞了金一样：

“那还不是看胡先生的面子。”
“Only out of regard for Mr Hu.”

他一半谦虚，一半说的也是实话。
It was partly modesty and partly the truth.

尤太太拉着她的手站了起来，
Mrs You rose, still holding her hand.

“我们到亭子里乘凉去。”
“Let's cool off in the pavilion.”

尤家后园，小有花木之胜，还有一座假山，山上一座亭子，
The You family's back garden had its share of pleasant flowers and trees, as well as an artificial hill with a pavilion on top.

题名甚怪，叫做“不买亭”，大概是取“清凤明月不费一文钱买”的意思，
Its name was odd: the Bumai, or “No Purchase,” Pavilion, presumably because a clear breeze and a bright moon cost nothing to enjoy.

但题名虽怪，亭子倒构筑得相当古朴，而且地势极好，
Oddly named though it was, the pavilion had a pleasingly simple, old-fashioned design and an excellent position.

登高远眺，绿野遥山，颇能赏心悦目。
From its height, the green fields and distant mountains made a delightful prospect.

园子的围墙不高，
The garden wall was low.

假山上望得见行人，行人只望得见亭子里的鬓丝丽影。
Those on the hill could see passers-by, while those passing below could glimpse only the women's hair and graceful silhouettes inside the pavilion.

在谨饬的人家，这座亭子是不宜女眷登临的，
In a strictly proper household, the women would not have been allowed up there.

但尤五家与众不同，
You's family was different.

女眷向不避人，而外人也不敢订尤家女眷什么主意，
Its women never hid themselves from view, and no outsider dared entertain designs on them.

所以从阿珠来了以后，几乎每天晚上都随着尤太太在“不买亭”纳凉。
Since her arrival, A’zhu had therefore gone almost every evening with Mrs You to enjoy the cool air in the Bumai Pavilion.


经常在一起的，还有尤五的一个妹妹，行七，尤家都叫她“七姑奶奶”。
They were often joined by You's younger sister, seventh among the children, whom the household called the Seventh Lady.

七站奶奶早年居孀，与翁姑不和，住在娘家，
Widowed young, she had quarrelled with her parents-in-law and returned to her own family.

三十岁左右，长得极艳，
She was about thirty and strikingly beautiful.

但坐在那里不讲话，是个绝色美人，一开口出来，会把胆小的男人吓走，
Sitting in silence, she was a woman of exceptional loveliness; once she opened her mouth, she could frighten timid men away.

因为她伉爽有须眉气概，而且江湖气极重，
She had a man's bold, forthright manner and a strong flavour of the rougher world beyond respectable society.

不独言词犀利，表情丰富，横眉瞪眼，杀气腾腾，
Her speech was sharp and her face expressive: she could lower her brows, glare, and look positively murderous.

最让男人吃不消的是，口没遮拦，骂人也是如此，
What men found hardest to bear was her utterly unguarded tongue, especially when she was abusing someone.

什么“蠢话”都说得出口，
There was no coarse expression she would not use.

所以她嫂子叫她“女张飞”。
Her sister-in-law therefore called her “the female Zhang Fei,” after the famously fierce and outspoken warrior.
