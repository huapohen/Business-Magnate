“天气真热！”

Mrs You rose, still holding her hand.
尤太太拉着她的手站了起来，

“Let's cool off in the pavilion.”
“我们到亭子里乘凉去。”

The You family's back garden had its share of pleasant flowers and trees, as well as an artificial hill with a pavilion on top.
尤家后园，小有花木之胜，还有一座假山，山上一座亭子，

Its name was odd: the Bumai, or “No Purchase,” Pavilion, presumably because a clear breeze and a bright moon cost nothing to enjoy.
题名甚怪，叫做“不买亭”，大概是取“清凤明月不费一文钱买”的意思，

Oddly named though it was, the pavilion had a pleasingly simple, old-fashioned design and an excellent position.
但题名虽怪，亭子倒构筑得相当古朴，而且地势极好，

From its height, the green fields and distant mountains made a delightful prospect.
登高远眺，绿野遥山，颇能赏心悦目。

The garden wall was low.
园子的围墙不高，

Those on the hill could see passers-by, while those passing below could glimpse only the women's hair and graceful silhouettes inside the pavilion.
假山上望得见行人，行人只望得见亭子里的鬓丝丽影。

In a strictly proper household, the women would not have been allowed up there.
在谨饬的人家，这座亭子是不宜女眷登临的，

You's family was different.
但尤五家与众不同，

Its women never hid themselves from view, and no outsider dared entertain designs on them.
女眷向不避人，而外人也不敢订尤家女眷什么主意，

Since her arrival, A’zhu had therefore gone almost every evening with Mrs You to enjoy the cool air in the Bumai Pavilion.
所以从阿珠来了以后，几乎每天晚上都随着尤太太在“不买亭”纳凉。


They were often joined by You's younger sister, seventh among the children, whom the household called the Seventh Lady.
经常在一起的，还有尤五的一个妹妹，行七，尤家都叫她“七姑奶奶”。

Widowed young, she had quarrelled with her parents-in-law and returned to her own family.
七站奶奶早年居孀，与翁姑不和，住在娘家，

She was about thirty and strikingly beautiful.
三十岁左右，长得极艳，

Sitting in silence, she was a woman of exceptional loveliness; once she opened her mouth, she could frighten timid men away.
但坐在那里不讲话，是个绝色美人，一开口出来，会把胆小的男人吓走，

She had a man's bold, forthright manner and a strong flavour of the rougher world beyond respectable society.
因为她伉爽有须眉气概，而且江湖气极重，

Her speech was sharp and her face expressive: she could lower her brows, glare, and look positively murderous.
不独言词犀利，表情丰富，横眉瞪眼，杀气腾腾，

What men found hardest to bear was her utterly unguarded tongue, especially when she was abusing someone.
最让男人吃不消的是，口没遮拦，骂人也是如此，

There was no coarse expression she would not use.
什么“蠢话”都说得出口，

Her sister-in-law therefore called her “the female Zhang Fei,” after the famously fierce and outspoken warrior.
所以她嫂子叫她“女张飞”。
