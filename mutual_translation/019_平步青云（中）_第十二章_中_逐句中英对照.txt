《平步青云（中）》第十二章（中）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume II — Chapter Twelve, middle part
逐句中英对照

“女张飞”心肠热，跟阿珠尤其投缘，
The female Zhang Fei had a warm heart and was particularly fond of A’zhu.

一看她眉宇之间，隐现幽怨，忍不住要问：“怎么了，
Seeing the unhappiness that flickered across the girl's face, she could not help asking, “What's the matter?

有啥心事，跟我说！”
Tell me what's troubling you.”

这心事如何肯与人说？尤其是在她面前，阿珠更有顾虑。
How could A’zhu confide in anyone about this, especially her?

“没有，没有！”
“Nothing, nothing!”

她竭力装得很轻松的，
She did her best to sound carefree.

“住在你们这里，再‘笃定’不过，
“I couldn't be more comfortable here.

有啥心事？”
What could trouble me?”

“我倒不懂了。”
“There's something I don't understand.”

七姑奶奶心直口快，说话不大考虑后果，
The Seventh Lady spoke her mind without much thought for the consequences.

“你们那位胡老爷，既然来了，怎不来看你呢？”
“Since your Mr Hu has arrived, why hasn't he come to see you?”

这一问阿珠大窘，
A’zhu was deeply embarrassed.

而尤太太大为着急，赶紧拦着她说：
Alarmed, Mrs You hurried to stop her.

“你又来了！
“There you go again!

真正是莽张飞。”
A true headstrong Zhang Fei.”

“咦！
“Why?

这话有啥问不得？”
What's wrong with asking?”

尤太太也是很厉害的角色，
Mrs You was no fool either.

一看这样子，灵机一动，索性要利用“女张飞”，
Seeing how matters stood, she suddenly decided to use the female Zhang Fei to her advantage.

“唉！”她故意叹口气，
She sighed deliberately.

“家家有本难念的经，
“Every household has its own difficult scripture to recite.

我们总要相劝张家妹子体谅胡老板。”
We must try to persuade our Sister Zhang to understand Mr Hu's difficulties.”

一说“体谅”，再说“相劝”，这就见得错在胡雪岩。
To speak of understanding and persuasion implied that Hu was at fault.

阿珠还在玩味她这两句后，七姑奶奶忍不住了，
While A’zhu was still considering those words, the Seventh Lady could contain herself no longer.

拉住她的手，逼视着说道：“你明明有心事，
Taking her hand and looking straight at her, she said, “Of course something's troubling you!

有委屈嘛！
You've been badly treated.

不管再忙，说来见个面都抽不出工夫，
However busy he is, he can't spare a moment to see you?

这话除非骗鬼！
Tell that to the ghosts!

男人都是犯贱的，
Men are contemptible creatures.

想你的时候，你就是皇后娘娘，
When they want you, you're an empress.

一变了心，你给他磕头，他给你拳头。
Once they change their minds, you can kowtow to them and get a fist in return.

这种人我见得多了。”
I've seen plenty of that sort.”


“姑奶奶，姑奶奶！”
“My dear lady, my dear lady!”

尤太太仿佛告饶似地说，
Mrs You sounded almost as though she were pleading for mercy.

“你饶了我好不好？
“Will you spare me?

你这么大声小叫，算怎么回事？”
What is all this shouting about?”

“好！”
“Very well!”

七姑奶奶把声音低了下来，但说得更快更急，
The Seventh Lady lowered her voice but spoke still faster and more urgently.

一只手把着阿珠，一只手指着她嫂了：
Holding A’zhu with one hand, she pointed at her sister-in-law with the other.

“张家妹子说得再清楚都没有了，
“Sister Zhang couldn't have made it clearer.

既然答应好两处立门户，
He agreed to establish a household in each place.

早就应该办好了，为啥到现在不办？
Why hasn't it been done long ago?

索性到了松江都不肯见一面，

这算是啥？”
What is that supposed to mean?”

说到这里，她转过脸来，对阿珠说：“我老早就觉得这件事不大对，替你不平，

先还怕是我想错了，
At first I wondered whether I'd misjudged him.

照现在看，果不其然是‘痴心女子负心汉’！”
Now it's plain enough: a devoted woman and a faithless man!”

“莽张飞啊莽张飞！
“Headstrong Zhang Fei, headstrong Zhang Fei!

你真正是……”
You really are...”

尤太太不说下去了。
Mrs You left the sentence unfinished.

阿珠在旁边听得心里好不舒服！但是这不舒服是由七姑奶奶，还是由胡雪岩而来。一时之间，她却弄不明白。
Listening beside them, A’zhu felt acutely uncomfortable, though she could not immediately tell whether the cause was the Seventh Lady or Hu.

反正义羞又气，觉得忸怩得很，只有悄悄将身子挪一挪，把自己的脸避到暗处，不为她们姑嫂所见。
Ashamed and angry, she shifted quietly until her face was in shadow, hidden from the two women.


她们姑嫂却偏不容她如此，
They would not allow even that.

双双转过脸来看着她，
Both turned towards her.

“张家妹子”，尤太太握住她另一只手，安慰她说：“你不要听她的话！
Taking her other hand, Mrs You comforted her. “Sister Zhang, don't listen to her.

脾气生就，开出口来就得罪人。”
That's her nature: the moment she opens her mouth, she offends someone.”

这一来，阿珠倒不能不说客气话了，
A’zhu now had to be gracious.

“七姐也是为我。”她点点头，
“Seventh Sister means well by me.

“我不会怪她的。”
I don't blame her.”

“你说话有良心！”
“That's a fair-minded thing to say!”

七姑奶奶越发义形于色，
The Seventh Lady looked still more indignant on her behalf.

“这是你终身大事，
“This concerns your whole life.

既然说破了，我们索性替你好好想一想。”她问她嫂子。“胡老板这样子，到底存着什么心思？”
Now that we've brought it into the open, let's think it through properly.” She asked Mrs You, “What exactly is Mr Hu thinking, behaving like this?”

尤太太笑道，“你问的话，十句有九句叫人没法回答。
“Nine out of ten of your questions are impossible to answer,” Mrs You replied with a smile.

不过她故意不说下去，很谨慎地看着阿珠的脸色，想知道她心里的感觉。
“Still...” She deliberately stopped and watched A’zhu carefully, trying to read her feelings.

这当然不容易看出来，
That was not easy.

因为阿珠觉得她们的关切，事属多余，所以极力保持平静，作为一种拒绝“好意”的表示。
A’zhu considered their concern unnecessary and maintained her composure as a way of refusing their kindness.


七姑奶奶不甚明白她的意思，就明白也拦不住她自己的嘴，
The Seventh Lady hardly understood this signal; even if she had, it would not have restrained her tongue.

“张家妹子”，她换了比较文静的态度，“不是我说，你一表人才，
Assuming a gentler manner, she said, “Sister Zhang, you're such a fine young woman.

何苦委屈自己？”
Why accept an inferior place?”

尤太太一听她的话，与她哥哥的意思一佯，正好借她的口来为自己表达，
Hearing her express the same view as her brother, Mrs You saw a chance to speak through her.

所以看阿珠不答，便似唱戏对口一般，有意接一句：
When A’zhu remained silent, she supplied the next line like a performer in a duet.

“怎么叫委屈自己？”
“What do you mean, an inferior place?”

“做低服小，难道不是委屈自己！”
“Bowing and scraping as a lesser wife—isn't that accepting an inferior place?”

言者无意，听者有心，
The speaker meant no particular wound, but her listener felt one.

这句话正触着阿珠的“隐痛”，要想保持平静也不可能了。
The words touched A’zhu's hidden sore, and composure became impossible.

“再说，如果太太脾气好，也还罢了，
“Besides, it might be tolerable if the wife were good-tempered.

不然做低服小，就是热面孔贴人的冷屁股。”
Otherwise, lowering yourself like that is simply pressing your warm face against someone's cold backside.”

“蠢话”又来了！
Another of her coarse expressions!

尤太太已经一再告诫过这位姑奶奶，人家是“大小姐”身份，不登大雅的话要少说，
Mrs You had repeatedly warned her that their guest was a young lady and such language must be restrained.

谁知到底还是本性难移。
Yet nature would have its way.

不过这时候要用她来做“配角”，也顾不得指责，
Just now, however, she needed the Seventh Lady as her supporting player and could not stop to scold.

只叹口气说：“唉！
She merely sighed.

正就是为此，人家胡老板为难。”
“Yes, that's precisely Mr Hu's difficulty.”

话里有话，阿珠必得问个究竟，不过用不着她费心，自有人代劳，
There was a meaning behind this that A’zhu must discover, but someone else asked for her.

“怎么？”
“What?

七姑奶奶问：“胡家那个是雌老虎？”
Is the woman at home a tigress?”

“听胡老板的意思，厉害得很！”
“From what Mr Hu says, she's very fierce.”

“那就是他不对了！
“Then he's at fault!

既然家里有个醋坛，为啥来骗我们张家妹子？”
With a jar of vinegar at home, why deceive our Sister Zhang?”

“这我倒要为胡老板说句公平话，”尤太太很认真地说，
“I must say something fair on his behalf,” Mrs You answered earnestly.

“原来是想跟他太太商量好了，再办喜事，
“He intended to settle matters with his wife before holding the wedding.

商量不通，只好打退堂鼓。
When she wouldn't agree, he had to retreat.

这也不算骗人。”
That's hardly deception.”


“什么？”阿珠失声问道，
“What?” A’zhu exclaimed.

“五嫂，你怎么知道？”
“Fifth Sister-in-Law, how do you know?”

“她五哥，”尤太太指着七姑奶奶说，“都告诉我了。
“Her brother—Brother Five—told me everything.” Mrs You indicated the Seventh Lady.

胡老板实在有难处，
“Mr Hu really does have difficulties.

话又跟你说不出口，闷在心里不是回事，只好跟好朋友谈谈。
He couldn't bring himself to tell you, but keeping them to himself was no solution either, so he spoke to a close friend.

张家妹子，你不要着急，
Don't worry, Sister Zhang.

我们慢慢想办法。”
We'll think of a way.”

想什么办法？
What way?

语意不明，而阿珠心乱如麻，也无法细想，
The meaning was unclear, and A’zhu's mind was too tangled to examine it.

此时她唯一的意愿是要跟胡雪岩当面谈一谈。
Her sole wish was to speak to Hu face to face.

“办法总有的。
“There's always a way.

对付没良心的男人，不必客气。
We needn't stand on ceremony with a heartless man.

不过，”
But...”

七姑奶奶低声向阿珠问道：
The Seventh Lady lowered her voice.

“你要说句实话，
“Tell me honestly.

你们船上来来去去，在湖州又住在一起，
You've travelled back and forth together on the boat and stayed in the same place in Huzhou.

你到底跟他……”
Have you and he ever...”

不等她说完，阿珠便又羞又急地叫了起来，
Before she could finish, A’zhu cried out in embarrassment and alarm.

“没有！”
“No!”

她的语气异常决绝，唯恐他人不信：
Afraid they might not believe her, she spoke with absolute firmness.

“绝对没有！
“Absolutely not!

我不是那种人。”
I'm not that sort of girl.”

“我晓得，我晓得。”
“I know, I know.”

七姑奶奶很欣慰地说，
The Seventh Lady sounded greatly relieved.

“没有吃他的亏，就更加好办了。”
“If he hasn't taken advantage of you, things are easier still.”

“对！”尤太太附和，
“Exactly,” Mrs You agreed.

“这件事还不算麻烦。
“This isn't such a difficult affair yet.

全在你自己身上。”
It all depends on you.”

这话又有深意了，阿珠得好好想一想，可是七姑奶奶的话实在多，不容她有细想的工夫。
Again there was a deeper meaning for A’zhu to consider, but the Seventh Lady talked too much to give her time.


“幸亏发觉得早！”她说，
“It's fortunate you found out early,” she said.

“你想想，男人十个有十一个好新鲜，
“Out of ten men, eleven want something new.

还没有上手，对你已经这个样子，等一上了手，尝过甜头，
If he's treating you like this before he's even won you over, imagine what he'll do once he's had his way.

还不是一丢了事。那时候，你就朝他哭都没有用。”
He'll cast you aside, and then even your tears will do no good.”

她已经算是措词是含蓄了，但已把男女间事似解非解的阿珠听得红晕忸怩得不知如何是好。
She considered this restrained language, but A’zhu, who understood relations between men and women only imperfectly, flushed and lowered her head, not knowing where to look.

低着头想想，“女张飞”的话虽粗鲁，却说中了她从未了解过的一面，
Coarse though the female Zhang Fei's words were, they revealed a side of the matter she had never grasped.

男人喜新厌旧，这话听人说过，只不如她来得透彻。
She had heard that men tired of the familiar and sought the new, but never had it been explained so plainly.

转念到此，想起胡雪岩几次“不规矩”，得寸进尺地到了紧要关头，总算自己还守得住，
She remembered how Hu had sometimes overstepped the bounds, pressing further and further, and how she had stood firm when it mattered.

真正是做对了！
She had been right to do so.


庆幸之念一生，就不觉得那么羞窘了，
This sense of relief eased her embarrassment.

同时也不是那么一颗心系在胡雪岩身上，丝毫不能动弹了，
Nor did her whole heart now seem bound so immovably to Hu.

她抬起脸来，掠一掠鬓发，喝了口败毒消火的“金银花茶”，
She raised her face, smoothed her hair, and sipped the honeysuckle tea intended to clear heat and toxins.

平静地问道：“五嫂，七姐，你们说替我想办法，
Calmly she asked, “Fifth Sister-in-Law, Seventh Sister, you say you'll think of a way for me.

想什么办法？”
What way?”

尤太太是等着她来问这句话的，
Mrs You had been waiting for the question.

这到了关系出入的地方，言语必须谨慎，
They had reached a crucial point, and she must choose her words carefully.

所以一面按着七姑奶奶的手，示意她不要插嘴，一面反问了一句，“这要看你自己的意思。
Pressing the Seventh Lady's hand to keep her from interrupting, she asked in return, “That depends on your own wishes.

大主意要你自己拿！
The main decision must be yours.

你说往东，替你想东的路子，
If you want to go east, we'll find a road east.

你说往西，我们来看看，往西走不走得通？”
If you want to go west, we'll see whether that road is open.”

这话阿珠明白，
A’zhu understood.

两条路，一条是仍旧跟胡雪岩，一条是过去的甜言蜜语，海誓山盟，一笔勾销。
There were two paths: continue with Hu, or wipe away all the sweet words and solemn promises of the past.

但明白归明白，一时间要她作个抉择，却是办不到的事。
Understanding them was one thing; making the choice on the spot was beyond her.


“照我来想，这种事，总要两厢情愿。
“In my view, such things require willingness on both sides.

人家既然有了这样的话，一定要勉强人家也不大好。
Since he has said this, it wouldn't be right to force him.

不说别的，起码自己的身分要顾到。”
Leaving everything else aside, you must at least preserve your own dignity.”

“真的！”
“Exactly!”

七姑奶奶终于忍不住了，
The Seventh Lady could hold back no longer.

“五嫂这话说得真正有道理。
“Fifth Sister-in-Law is absolutely right.

我们娇滴滴一朵鲜花，又不是落市的鱼鲜，怕摆不起，要硬挜给他！”
You're a delicate flower, not stale fish left on the market stall that we must force on him before it spoils!”

听这句话就象吃了芥末，阿珠一股怨气直冲到鼻子里，差点掉眼泪了。
The words struck A’zhu like mustard, sending resentment up into her nose until she nearly wept.

自己是娇滴滴的一朵鲜花，胡雪岩却当做落市的鱼鲜，阴阳怪气，爱理不理，
She was a delicate flower, yet Hu treated her like unwanted fish, behaving strangely and scarcely deigning to notice her.

想想真有点伤心，
It hurt to think of it.

不由得咬着牙说：“哪个有那么贱，一定要硬挜给他！”
Through clenched teeth she said, “Who would be so cheap as to force herself on him?”

“好了，
“Good.

你想明白了。”七姑奶奶说，
You've seen it clearly,” the Seventh Lady answered.

“老实说一句，‘两头大’已经委屈得不得了，
“To be honest, even having equal standing in two households was a terrible concession for you.

他还说有什么难处。
And still he talks of difficulties!

这种男人，真是‘谢谢一家门’了。”
A man like that can keep his whole family to himself, thank you!”


事情已一半成功，
Half the task was accomplished.

何必再骂胡雪岩，徒结冤家？
Why continue abusing Hu and merely create an enemy?

尤太太便替他解释：
Mrs You defended him.

“七妹，你的话也太过分了。
“Seventh Sister, you're going too far.

胡老板人是再好没有，
Mr Hu is as good a man as one could find.

他也是力不从心，不肯耽误张家妹子的青春，
There are things beyond his power, and he doesn't wish to waste Sister Zhang's youth.

你不要冤枉他。”
Don't misjudge him.”

七姑奶奶有样好处，勇于认错。
One virtue of the Seventh Lady was her readiness to admit an error.

听了她嫂子的话，心里在想，胡雪岩有多少机会把阿珠弄上手，而到现在她还是“原封未动”，
Hu had had many opportunities to win A’zhu, yet nothing had happened between them.

同时他给张家的好处，也真不少。
He had also done a great deal for the Zhang family.

这样的人，说起来也很难得了。
A man like that was indeed uncommon.

于是她笑着说道：
She smiled.

“想想也是，
“On reflection, you're right.

费心费力，忙了半天一场空不说，还要挨骂，
After all his trouble and effort, he gets nothing—and is scolded into the bargain.

实在也太冤枉了！”
It really is unfair.”

阿珠的一颗心，一直动荡不定，只随着她们姑嫂俩的话，浮沉摆动。
A’zhu's unsettled heart had been rising and falling with the two women's words.

这时候听了七姑奶奶的话，使又想起胡雪岩的许多好处，心里实在割舍不下，
Now she remembered all Hu's kindness and could hardly bear to give him up.

但硬话已经说出去了，
Yet her defiant words had already been spoken.

落下来的逢，再要撑起来，十分不易，
A sail once lowered was difficult to raise again.

心中萌生悔意，却又是说不出的苦，
Regret stirred within her, but she could not express the pain.

因而滚落两滴泪珠。
Two tears rolled down her cheeks.


“咦！”七姑奶奶惊诧地说，
“Why!” the Seventh Lady asked in astonishment.

“你哭点啥？”
“What are you crying for?”

“不要伤心，不要伤心！”尤太太也劝她，
“Don't be sad,” Mrs You urged.

“路差点走错，及早回头，
“You nearly took the wrong road and have turned back in time.

你应该高兴。”
You ought to be happy.”

阿珠心想，怎么高兴得起来？
How could she be happy?

七姑奶奶说胡雪岩费心费力一场空，
The Seventh Lady had said Hu's efforts had come to nothing.

自己何尝不是？
Hadn't hers too?

他的落空是他自己愿意的，自己的落空是无奈其何！
His disappointment was of his own choosing; she had no choice in hers.

夜静更深，想起从前的光景，将来的打算，一起都变了镜花水月，

这日子怎么过法？
How was she to live with that?

她一个人怔怔地在想心事，尤太太使趁此机会给她小姑抛了个眼色过去，意思是不必再多说了。
As A’zhu sat lost in thought, Mrs You took the opportunity to signal to her sister-in-law that enough had been said.

但七姑奶奶却不明用意：
But the Seventh Lady did not understand.

“话是有两句。
“A word or two.

就怕你嘴快！”尤太太说，

“事情成功了一半，不过还有一半不成功，就算统统不成功。”

“怎么呢？”
“What do you mean?”

“胡老板的意思是，”
“Mr Hu intends...”

尤太太朝阿珠看了一眼，把她拉到亭子外面，低声说道：
Mrs You glanced at A’zhu and drew the Seventh Lady outside the pavilion before lowering her voice.

“还要替我们这位张家妹子做媒。”
“He also wants to arrange a marriage for our Sister Zhang.”


“做给哪个？”
