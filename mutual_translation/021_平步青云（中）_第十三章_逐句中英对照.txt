第十三章｜CHAPTER THIRTEEN


这个不速之客是嘉定的周立春派来的。
The unexpected visitor had been sent by Zhou Lichun of Jiading.

周立春与刘丽川有联系，所以上海一起事，周立春预备在嘉定响应，
Zhou was in touch with Liu Lichuan and intended to rise in Jiading as soon as the uprising began in Shanghai.

事先曾经跟尤五接头，希望“有福同享，有难同当”，
He had approached You Laowu beforehand, proposing that they “share good fortune and face hardship together.”

尤五不愿这浑水，但也不便得罪他们，所以一直采取敷衍的态度。
You had no desire to enter these troubled waters, yet could not afford to offend them, so he had always put them off with agreeable words.

但以前可以敷衍，
That had been possible before.

此刻到了真刀真枪要上场的时候，那就敷衍不过去了。
Now that the time had come for real swords and spears, agreeable words were no longer enough.

“我来跟他说。”七姑奶奶小声诅咒着，话又难听了：
“I shall speak to him,” the Seventh Lady said, swearing under her breath in her usual coarse fashion.

“他娘的！
“Damn it!

只有强奸，没有逼赌！
There is such a thing as forced sex, but not forced gambling.

造反又不是去吃花酒，
Rebellion is not an evening with singing girls.

还有啥硬拉牢了一起走的？”
How can they drag someone along against his will?”

“你又来了！”尤五嫂又气又急，
“There you go again!” Mrs You was both angry and anxious.

“求求你，姑奶奶！
“Please, my dear lady!

你要跟他去吵架，还是不要去的好。”
If you mean to quarrel with him, you had better not go.”

“唉！五嫂，你又看得我那样子草包了！
“Oh, Fifth Sister-in-Law, you still take me for such a fool!

我不过在这里发发牢骚，
I am only grumbling here.

见了面，人家总是客人，
Once we meet, he is a guest.

我无缘无故得罪他做什么？”七姑奶奶推着她说：
Why should I offend him without cause?” She urged Mrs You towards the door.

“你先去应酬应酬，
“Go and attend to him first.

要特别客气，不要冷落人家。”
Be especially courteous; do not leave him feeling neglected.”

“不要紧。
“There is no need to worry.

我开了早饭，请他在吃酒。”尤五嫂说：
I have had breakfast served, and he is drinking some wine.

“人家是连夜赶来的。”
He travelled all night to get here.”

“那么，你看他吃好了，请他在五哥的那间房子里见面。”
“Then, when he has finished, ask him to meet me in Brother Five’s room.”


尤五有间密室，看是孤零零一座院落，四外隔绝，其实有地道与外间相通。
You had a private meeting room in what appeared to be an isolated courtyard, cut off on every side, though in fact a tunnel connected it with the outside.

七姑奶奶为怕走漏风声，特意约在那里相会。
Fearing that word might leak out, the Seventh Lady deliberately chose this place for their conversation.

那个人是周立春的本家兄弟，排行第六，七姑奶奶也认识，
The visitor was a clansman of Zhou Lichun’s, sixth in his generation, and she knew him too.

但谈这些事，非另有凭信不可，
But for business of this kind, personal acquaintance was not enough; credentials were required.

因而一见面，她先这样问说：“周六哥，你要寻我五哥有啥话说呢？”
As soon as they met, she asked, “Brother Zhou Liu, what is it you wish to discuss with Brother Five?”

周六略略踌躇了一下答道：“七姑奶奶，立春有几句机密话……”
Zhou Liu hesitated. “Seventh Lady, Lichun has a few confidential words...”

“慢点！周六哥，”她拦着他说，
“A moment, Brother Zhou Liu.” She stopped him.

“既然是周大哥的机密话，你总晓得规矩？”
“If they are Brother Zhou Lichun’s confidential words, you know the rule?”

“喔，我倒忘记掉了。”
“Ah, I had forgotten.”

周六歉意的笑着，伸手到腰上去掏摸。他掏摸出来一块汉玉，送到七姑奶奶手里，
With an apologetic smile, he reached into his waistband and produced a piece of Han-dynasty jade.

这是信物。
This was his credential.

周立春因为造反是要杀头的机密大事，往来接洽，不便形诸笔墨，
Planning rebellion was a secret matter that could cost a man his head, so Zhou Lichun could not commit their dealings to writing.

而派人传话，却又口说无凭，
Yet a messenger’s unsupported word was no proof.

便与尤五作了个约定，用这块汉玉作为凭证。
He and You had therefore agreed that this jade would authenticate any message.

无此信物，守口如瓶，
Without it, lips must remain sealed.

七姑奶奶知道有这样一个约定，所以首先就要查问。
The Seventh Lady knew the arrangement and had asked for it at once.

验明无误，她把汉玉交了回去，
Having verified the piece, she returned it.

接着便说：“周六哥，你晓得我们这里情形的，
“Brother Zhou Liu, you know how things are managed here.

你有话跟我说也一样。”
You may speak to me just as you would to my brother.”


“是，是！
“Yes, yes.

我们也晓得七姑奶奶女中丈夫，令兄凡遇大事，都要跟你商量。”
We know that the Seventh Lady has the mettle of a man and that your brother consults you in every important affair.”

周六说到这里，不放心似的望外面看了一下，然后把声音放得极低：
Zhou Liu glanced uneasily outside, then lowered his voice almost to a whisper.

“上海方面的情形，七姑奶奶想必已有消息？”
“You have heard the news from Shanghai, I suppose?”

“我也是刚刚听说，
“Only just.

详细情形还不晓得。”
I do not yet know the details.”

“上海已经成功了。
“Shanghai has succeeded.

刘大哥事情很顺手，以后还要顺手。
Everything has gone smoothly for Brother Liu, and it will go more smoothly still.

苏州的绿营兵，湖州人居多，跟刘大哥是同乡，
Most of the Green Standard soldiers at Suzhou are from Huzhou, his own home district.

已经约定，就要起事。”
They have already agreed to rise.

周六顿了一下，很吃力地说：“立春也就要在这两三夭动手，
Lichun intends to act within the next two or three days.” Zhou paused, then continued with some difficulty.

以前跟尤五哥谈过，尤五答应到时候一定帮忙。
“He discussed it with Brother Five You before, and You promised to help when the time came.

我今天来，就是来谈这件事。”
That is what I have come about.”

“喔，”七姑奶奶从从容容地答道：“你们谈过这件事，我是晓得的，
“I know you discussed the matter,” she answered without haste.

不过我没有听我五哥说过一定帮忙的话。”
“But I never heard Brother Five promise that he would certainly help.”

这一下就谈不下去了，
There was no obvious way to continue after that.

周六愣住在那里，一脸懊丧之色。
Zhou sat dumbfounded, his face full of disappointment.


“周六哥，我五哥最讲义气，
“Brother Zhou Liu, no one values loyalty more than my brother.

为朋友上刀山、下油锅，他都肯的。
For a friend, he would climb a mountain of blades or plunge into boiling oil.

是不是？”
Would he not?”

“是啊！”周六连连点头，
“Indeed!” Zhou nodded repeatedly.

“就为此，立春才来请尤五哥帮忙的，
“That is why Lichun asked for his help.

大家‘有福同享，有难同当’。”
We should share good fortune and face hardship together.”

“实不相瞒，我五哥眼前就是难关。”七姑奶奶正好接住他的话，
“To tell you the truth, my brother is facing hardship himself at this very moment.” She seized the opening.

“如果是前一两年，我五哥有啥推辞是孙子王八蛋，
“A year or two ago, if he had made excuses, he would have been a contemptible bastard.

眼前真正叫有心无力。
Now he truly has the will but not the means.

为啥呢？
Why?

为来为去为的是，不晓得哪个赃官想出来的，断命的‘海运’呀！”
It all comes back to that damned sea transport, dreamed up by some corrupt official!”

“海运？”周六问道：
“Sea transport?

“是说漕米改海运？”
You mean sending the grain tax by sea?”

“是啊，
“Exactly.

漕米改了海运，挑沙船帮发财！
It fattens the purse of the Sand-Boat Guild.

走关东的沙船，本来一向是装了压舱石头到北边的，
Their vessels used to sail north towards Manchuria carrying nothing but stone ballast.

现在改装漕米，平白里赚一笔水脚银子，
Now they carry tax rice instead and earn freight money for what was once an empty voyage.

运到天津不出事，还有啥‘保举’，
If they reach Tianjin without mishap, there are recommendations for official rewards as well.

沙船帮老大也做官了，
Even the guild leaders are becoming officials.

气数不气数！”
Is that not enough to make one furious?”

七姑奶奶咽了口唾沫，接下去又说：“沙船帮交贼运，我们漕帮要没饭吃了。
She swallowed and continued. “The sand-boat men are enjoying their cursed good fortune, while our Grain Transport Brotherhood is losing its livelihood.

松江是疲帮，你也晓得的，
You know Songjiang is one of the impoverished branches.

我五哥当这个家，真正是黄连当饭，苦头吃足。
Keeping this household of men going has been like eating bitter coptis root instead of rice for my brother—nothing but hardship.

转眼重阳节边，西北风起，漕帮弟兄的夹衣裳都还在当铺里，
The Double Ninth Festival will soon be here, and the northwest winds will rise, yet the brothers’ lined coats are still in the pawnshops.

我五哥不能不想办法。
He has to find some means of helping them.

现在陪了个‘空子’到上海去做丝生意了，多少想掏摸几个，贴补贴补。
At present he has gone to Shanghai with an outsider to trade in silk, hoping to scrape together something to fill the gaps.

周六哥你倒想想，我五哥在江湖上的身份，
Think of his standing in our world, Brother Zhou Liu!

倘不是穷极无奈，怎么肯去服侍一个空子？
Would he attend on an outsider unless poverty left him no choice?

这样子泥菩萨过江，自身难保的时候，
He is like a clay Buddha crossing a river, unable even to save himself.

怎么帮得上周大哥的忙？“
How can he help Brother Zhou Lichun?”


一番话说得周六哑口无言，
Zhou was left speechless.

好半天才说了句：“既然如此，尤五哥为啥又说，到时候一定帮忙。”
After a long pause, he managed to ask, “Then why did he promise to help when the time came?”

“这就是我五哥的为人。
“That is the sort of man he is.

你现在跟他去说，他还是会答应帮忙。
Ask him now, and he will still promise.

不过这个忙，照我看，是越帮越忙。”
But in my opinion, the more he helped, the more trouble he would cause.”

“噢！”周六深为诧异，
“Oh?” Zhou was astonished.

“这是啥道理？”
“Why should that be?”

“啥道理？吃饭的道理。”七姑奶奶答得极其爽脆，
“Because men have to eat.” Her answer was crisp.

“漕米为啥改为海运，
“Why was the grain tax diverted to the sea?

说运河水浅，有时候漕船不通，
They say the canal is too shallow and the grain boats sometimes cannot pass.

这好想办法，
One can find a remedy for that.

时世一乱，漕船走不过去，那才是死路一条。
But if disorder makes the route impassable, that is a complete dead end.

帮里的弟兄，对‘长毛’都摇头，
The brothers already shake their heads at the Longhairs.

现在再要他们跟周大哥一起走，表面不说，心里另有打算。
Ask them to follow Brother Zhou Lichun now, and they may say nothing openly while thinking something quite different.

万一做出啥对不起人的事来，我五哥一定压不住。
If they did something disloyal, my brother could certainly not hold them down.

这不是越帮越忙吗？”
Would his help not make matters worse?”

周六听她这一说，打了个寒噤。
Zhou shuddered.

果然要松江漕帮协同起事，说不定洋枪到手，枪口朝里，那岂是儿戏之事？
If the Songjiang brotherhood joined the rising and, once it had foreign guns in its hands, turned them on its supposed allies, that would be no laughing matter.


不过，仔细想一想也不对。
On reflection, however, the argument did not seem right to him.

俗称“通草”的“通漕”，周六也见过，
Zhou had seen the brotherhood’s canonical text, the Tongcao, whose title was colloquially written with the similar-sounding character for “grass” in place of “grain transport.”

上面记着，陆祖命翁、钱、潘三祖下山行道，行的就是“反清复明”的道，
It recorded how Patriarch Lu sent the three patriarchs Weng, Qian, and Pan down from the mountain to spread the Way—the Way of overthrowing the Qing and restoring the Ming.

陆祖说的两首偈子，第一首中的“前人世界后人收”，就指的是光复大明江山，
Of the two verses spoken by Lu, the first contained the line “The later generation shall recover the world of those before,” referring to the recovery of the Ming realm.

第二首中“日月巍巍照玉壶”，日月合成“明”字，“壶”字谐音“胡”，指的是清，
In the second, “Sun and moon shine high upon the jade vessel,” the written characters for sun and moon combined to make Ming, while the word for vessel, hu, sounded like the word for the northern peoples and referred to the Qing.

也有反清复明的意思在内。
That too implied overthrowing the Qing and restoring the Ming.

那么，现在起事反清，漕帮弟兄何能倒戈？
How, then, could the brotherhood turn against a rising directed against the Qing?

他是想到就是，而七姑奶奶报以轻蔑说：
He voiced his thoughts, only to receive a scornful reply.

“周六哥，这些道理不晓得是啥辰光留下来的？
“Brother Zhou Liu, who knows how long ago those doctrines were handed down?

‘皇帝不差饿兵’，
Even an emperor cannot send hungry soldiers to fight.

饭都没得吃了，现在想大明江山，不好笑？”
With no food to eat, is it not ridiculous to dream of the Ming realm now?”

再说下去，依然无用。
There was no point in pursuing the matter.

这一趟完全白来。
His journey had been wasted.

周六想了想，只好这样说：“那么，七姑奶奶，我今天这番话，算是没有说，
After thinking for a moment, Zhou said, “Then let us regard everything I have said today as unsaid, Seventh Lady.

你也当作不曾听见过好了。”
You may treat it as something you never heard.”

这话她懂，
She understood.

“尽管请放心！
“Set your mind at rest.

我哪里会做这种半吊子的事？
I would never be such a bungler.

如果周六哥，你今天跟我说的话，漏一个字到外面，你尽管来寻我们兄妹说话。”她接下来又极诚恳地说：
If a single word of what you have told me escapes, come and call my brother and me to account.” She continued with great sincerity.

“周六哥，害你白来一趟，我心里真正过意不去。
“I truly regret that you have made this journey for nothing.

不过事情明摆在那里，实在力不从心。
But the situation is plain: we cannot do what we would wish.

请你回去跟周大哥说，这一次真对不起他，
Please tell Brother Zhou Lichun that we are sorry to fail him this time.

别处有用得着我们的地方，尽管吩咐。
If there is anything else in which we can be useful, he has only to ask.

话再说回来，我们也有请周大哥照应的时候，
We too may need his protection one day.

‘行得春风有夏雨’，
Send a spring breeze, and summer rain will follow.

只要力量够得到，帮朋友就是帮自己。”
As long as it is within our power, helping a friend is helping ourselves.”


周六暗暗点头，
Zhou nodded inwardly.

都说这位七姑奶奶办事跟男子汉一样，果然名不虚传。
People said that the Seventh Lady conducted affairs as capably as any man, and her reputation was justified.

这几句话还有打招呼的意思在内，
Her last words also carried an appeal.

事情不成，朋友要交，索性买买她的帐。
Even if the business had failed, the friendship should be maintained; he might as well show her the consideration she sought.

“这就是七姑奶奶的话了！尽管请放心！
“Since that is what you say, Seventh Lady, you may be entirely reassured.

嘉定过来青浦，青浦过来松江，
From Jiading one comes to Qingpu, and from Qingpu to Songjiang.

过几天到了贵宝地，有‘老太爷’在，决不敢惊动的！”
If we reach your district in a few days, with the Old Master here, we would never presume to cause a disturbance.”

“周六哥，你这句话值钱了。
“Brother Zhou Liu, those words are worth something.

我替松江老百姓，谢谢你！”
I thank you on behalf of the people of Songjiang.”

说着，她学男人的样子，抱拳作了个揖。
She clasped her hands and bowed in the manner of a man.

总算不伤和气，
Harmony had at least been preserved.

把周六送出后门，七姑奶奶心里不免得意，笑嘻嘻地回到后面，
After seeing Zhou out through the back door, she could not help feeling pleased with herself and returned to the rear quarters smiling.

尤五嫂迎着她问道：“怎么说法？”
Mrs You came to meet her. “What was said?”

“没事了！”她守着给周六的诺言，
“It is all settled.” She kept her promise to Zhou.

“详细情形也不必说，
“There is no need to go into the details.

总而言之一句话，五哥的麻烦，我统统把它扫干净了！”
In a word, I have swept away every one of Brother Five’s difficulties.”


“真正亏得你！”尤五嫂极欣慰他，
“We are fortunate to have you!” Mrs You was deeply relieved.

“实在也要谢谢胡老板，
“We should thank Mr Hu too.

不是他来，你五哥不会到上海去。
Without him, your brother would never have gone to Shanghai.

叫他自己来应付，还不如你出面来得好。”
You have handled this better than he could have done himself.”

“这话倒是真的。”
“That is true.”

七姑奶奶想了想说，“五嫂，我今天要到上海去一趟。”
After considering, the Seventh Lady said, “Fifth Sister-in-Law, I must go to Shanghai today.”

“应该去一趟。”尤五嫂说，
“You ought to.

“就怕路上不好走。”
I only fear the journey may be difficult.”

“怕什么？”七姑奶奶毫不在乎的，
“What is there to fear?” She was unconcerned.

“他们闹事是在陆路上，
“They are making trouble on land.

我们坐船去，根本就碰不见，
We shall go by boat and never encounter them.

碰见也不要紧，凭我还会怕他们？”
Even if we did, would I be afraid?”

“那好，你就赶快去一趟，
“Then go as soon as you can.

叫你五哥在那里躲一躲，省得那班‘神道’又来找麻烦。”
Tell Brother Five to stay out of the way there for a while, so that those troublemakers do not come looking for him again.”

“我晓得。
“I understand.

我去收拾东西，
I shall pack.

五嫂，你关照他们，马上替我备船。”
Please have a boat made ready at once.”


于是七姑奶奶回到自己卧室，匆匆收拾随身衣物，
She returned to her bedroom and hurriedly gathered the clothes she would need.

正在手忙脚乱的当儿，阿珠悄悄的走了进来，有所央告。
While she was busy, A’zhu slipped in with a request.

“七姐！”她用耍赖的神态说道：“我不管，你一定要带我一起走。”
“Seventh Sister, I do not care what you say—you must take me with you.”

“咦！”七姑奶奶有些诧异：
“Eh?” The Seventh Lady was surprised.

“我又不是去玩儿。”
“I am not going for an outing.”

“我也不是去玩儿。
“Neither am I.

我要去看我爹，
I want to see Father.

不然不放心。”
Otherwise I cannot feel easy.”

“话是不错，走起来有难处，
“That is understandable, but the journey is difficult.

路上不平靖。”七姑奶奶郑重其事地说，
The roads are unsettled.” She spoke gravely.

“你想想看，造反的人，哪个不是无法无天？
“Think of it: what rebel has any respect for law or restraint?

遇见了，不是好玩儿的。”
Meeting them would be no joke.”

“我不怕！”阿珠豁出去了，
“I am not afraid!” A’zhu declared recklessly.

“大不了一条命。”
“At worst, I have only one life to lose.”

“他不要你的命，要你的身子。”
“They may spare your life and assault you instead.”

听这句话，阿珠不能不怕，
That frightened her.

愣了一会说：“那么你呢？”
After a pause, she asked, “What about you?”

“我不要紧，
“I shall be all right.

跟他们‘滚钉板’，滚过明白。”七姑奶奶又说，
I can face them and settle things directly.

“我再告诉你，我学过拳头，
Besides, I have trained in martial arts.

象阿龙这样的，三、五个人，我一样把他们‘摆平’！”
Three or five fellows like A’long would be no match for me.”

说完，她拿起墙角的一枝青皮甘蔗，右掌平平的削过去，
Taking a green sugar cane from the corner, she struck it with the edge of her right palm.

也不见她如何用力，甘蔗却已断成两截。
She seemed to use scarcely any force, yet the cane broke in two.

这一说一试，效用恰好相反，
Her explanation and demonstration had exactly the opposite effect from the one intended.

阿珠对她本就信赖，
A’zhu already trusted her.

现在看她“露了一手”，益发放心，
Now, seeing this display of skill, she felt safer still.

轻松地笑道：“我有个女镖客保镖，还伯什么？
“With a woman bodyguard to protect me, what have I to fear?” she said lightly.

我跟你走定了！
“I am definitely coming.

时地相异，感觉不同，胡雪岩固然神态自若，阿珠也还显得从容。
A different time and place produced different feelings: Hu was composed as ever, and A’zhu too seemed at ease.

七姑奶奶略略道了决定到上海来的缘由，随即向尤五使个眼色，示意避人密谈，
The Seventh Lady briefly explained why she had decided to come to Shanghai, then signalled to her brother that she wanted a private conversation.

尤五因为跟胡雪岩已到了共机密的程序，所以顺手把他一拉，一起来听七姑奶奶的报告。
You now shared confidential matters with Hu and drew him along to hear her report.

“嘉定的人，昨天早晨来过了……”
“The man from Jiading came yesterday morning...”

她把经过情形，细说了一遍。
She gave a detailed account.

“这样应付也好！”尤五欣慰的。
“That was a good way to handle it,” You said, relieved.

默默在一旁听着的胡雪岩，不曾想到七姑奶奶，如此能干，不免刮目相看。
Listening quietly, Hu had not expected the Seventh Lady to be so capable and regarded her with new respect.

她发觉了他的眼色，心里觉得很舒服，
She noticed his look and was pleased.

便笑着问了句：“小爷叔，你看我说错了话没有？”
“Young Uncle,” she asked with a smile, “do you think I said anything wrong?”

“当然不错！”
“Certainly not!”

胡雪岩转脸对尤五说：“这下了掉一件心事，
Hu turned to You. “That removes one worry.

我们在上海可以好好动一动脑筋。”
Now we can give proper thought to our affairs in Shanghai.”

尤五先不答他的话，向他妹子低声叮嘱：“阿七，我一时不能回去，家里实在放不下心，
Before replying to him, You spoke quietly to his sister. “A’qi, I cannot return just yet, and I am anxious about things at home.

趁这一两天，路上还不要紧，
The route should still be passable for a day or two.

你赶紧回去吧！”
You had better go back at once.”


七姑奶奶点点头，问起他们在上海的情形：“ 生意怎么样？”
She nodded, then asked about their affairs in Shanghai. “How is business?”

这活在尤五就无从置答了，
You had no ready answer.

只是微微叹口气，见得不甚顺手。
He merely sighed faintly, showing that matters were not going smoothly.

“生意蛮好！”胡雪岩却持乐观的态度，
Hu, however, remained optimistic. “Very good.

“正在谈，就要谈出结果来了。”
We are negotiating and should have a result soon.”

事实上不容易谈得出结果，
In fact, agreement would not be easy.

胡雪岩扳持不卖，洋行方面因为小刀会起事的关系，是在观望之中，
Hu was holding firm and refusing to sell, while the foreign firms were waiting to see what would happen after the Small Swords uprising.

所以最大的两项“洋庄”货色，茶和丝都变成有行无市，混沌一团。
Tea and silk, the two largest items in the export trade, still had quoted prices but scarcely any transactions; the whole market was in confusion.

尤五因为生意方面不大在行，而局势甚乱，自不免悲观，
You knew little about commerce and, with the political situation so unsettled, naturally felt pessimistic.

因而才叹气不答。
That was why he had sighed instead of answering.

“阿七，”尤五又说，“你明天就回去吧！”
“A’qi,” he repeated, “go back tomorrow.”


“晓得了！”七姑奶奶不悦，
“I know!” the Seventh Lady replied with displeasure.

“我会走的。
“I shall go.

不过张家妹子是我带到上海来的，总要把她作个交代。”
But I brought our sister Zhang to Shanghai, and I must make some arrangement for her.”

“交代她爹就是了。”
“Leave her in her father’s care.”

话是不错，但七姑奶奶一心要牵那条红线，巴不得当时就有个着落，
That was reasonable, but the Seventh Lady was intent on tying the red thread of marriage and wanted a settlement at once.

这话又似乎不宜出口，因而沉默着。
She could hardly say so openly and fell silent.

“七姐！”胡雪岩看出她的热心，安慰她说，“事情是一定会有个好好交代的，
Hu understood her enthusiasm. “Seventh Sister, there will certainly be a proper arrangement.

急也急不得。
It is no use hurrying.

我想把她先送回湖州，叫世龙送了去，
I mean to send her back to Huzhou with Shilong.

那也就算是有交代了。”
That, too, would count as making provision for her.”

“嗯，嗯。”七姑奶奶不置可否地，
“Mm, mm.” She committed herself to neither agreement nor disagreement.

然后又说：“裕记老板娘，今天请我们一起去吃夜饭，
Then she said, “The proprietor’s wife has invited us all to dinner tonight.

也该走了。”
It is time to go.”

“不行！”尤五摇头，
“We cannot,” You replied, shaking his head.

“我们今天夜里约好一个要紧人在那里。
“We have arranged to meet an important man this evening.

你们去吧！”
Go without us.”

于是乍一相见，匆匆又别。
They had scarcely met before they parted again.

尤五和胡雪岩不暇暖地，赶到一家“堂子”里去赴约会。
Without even time to warm their seats, You and Hu hurried away to keep an appointment at a courtesan house.
