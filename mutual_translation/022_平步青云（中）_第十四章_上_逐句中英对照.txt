胡雪岩
《平步青云（中）》第十四章（上）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume II — Chapter Fourteen, first part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第十四章｜CHAPTER FOURTEEN


要会的那个要紧人姓古，广东人，是个“通事”，
The important man they were to meet was a Cantonese interpreter named Gu.

结交的洋朋友极多，对英国人尤其熟悉，
He had many foreign friends and was particularly familiar with the British.

而在上海的英国人，自从洪秀全在江宁开国，便有许多花样。
Since Hong Xiuquan had established his kingdom at Jiangning, the British in Shanghai had been trying all manner of schemes.

他们去会那姓古的，就是要打听这些花样。
It was these schemes that Hu and You wanted to learn about.

尤五在上海的路子也很广，辗转打听到，英国洋行已经跟洪军展开贸易。
You Laowu had extensive connections in Shanghai, and through a succession of inquiries he had discovered that a British firm was already trading with Hong’s army.

曾经有两只英国兵船，从上海开到下关。
Two British warships had sailed from Shanghai to Xiaguan.

洪军起初以为是清军邀来助阵的，大起戒备。
At first the rebels had assumed that the Qing forces had invited them to help fight, and had taken elaborate precautions.

谁知英国人带了一名通事上岸，一开口就表明，此来特为通商。
But the British had brought an interpreter ashore, and his opening words made it clear that they had come expressly to trade.

商品是枪械火药，以货易货。
Their merchandise consisted of firearms and gunpowder, to be exchanged for other goods.

那家洋行，大获其利，
The firm had made a handsome profit.

而所带的通事，就是这个姓古的，名叫古应春。
The interpreter who had accompanied them was this very man, Gu Yingchun.


于是胡雪岩又有了新的主意，
This gave Hu Xueyan another idea.

他跟尤五商量，最好能够跟古应春结交，在珍宝和枪械方面都有生意好做。
He told You that they ought to make Gu’s acquaintance: there would be business to do in both precious objects and arms.

尤五对胡雪岩已到了言听计从的地步，
By now You followed Hu’s advice in everything.

便设法托人，从中介绍，前一天已在吃花酒的场面上见过面，
He arranged an introduction through a mutual acquaintance, and the previous day they had met over a banquet with courtesans.

当时约定，这天是尤五回请，全班人马，一个不缺，
It had been agreed then that You would return the hospitality today, with the entire party invited again.

其实主客只有一个古应春。
In reality, there was only one guest who mattered: Gu Yingchun.

设席的地点在宝善街怡情院。尤五是这家“长三堂子”的主政，怡情老二的恩客，所以连带胡雪宕亦有宾至如归之乐。
The dinner was to be held at Yiqing House on Baoshan Street, an establishment of the prestigious changsan class. You was a favored patron of Yiqing’s Second Miss, the woman who ran the house, so Hu too enjoyed the pleasure of being received as one of the household.

到了那里，在“大房间”落座，刚刚卸去长衫，听“相帮”在喊客到，
They had scarcely taken their seats in the main room and removed their long gowns when an attendant called out that a guest had arrived.

怡情老二亲自打开帘子，只见古应春步履轻快地踏上台阶了。
Second Miss herself lifted the curtain, revealing Gu Yingchun coming briskly up the steps.

“古大少，真真够交情。”怡情老二盈盈笑着，
“Young Master Gu, you really do honor your friends!” she said with a graceful smile.

“第一个到。”
“The very first to arrive.”

“尤五哥请客不能不早点来。”古应春又说，
“When Brother You gives a dinner, I can hardly be late,” Gu replied.

“而且是在你这里请客，更不能不早到。”
“And when he gives it at your house, I must certainly come early.”

“这是我沾尤五少的光，
“Then I owe this honor to Fifth Master You.

谢谢，谢谢！”
Thank you, thank you!”


“承情之至。”尤五也拱手致谢，
“Much obliged,” You said, clasping his hands in thanks.

接着向里一指，“要不要里头躺一会？”
Then he gestured toward the inner room. “Would you like to lie down for a while?”

“我是过足了瘾来的。不过躺一会也可以。”
“I had my fill of opium before coming, but I wouldn’t mind lying down.”

一听这话，怡情老二便喊：“点灯！”
At this, Second Miss called, “Light the lamp!”

接着把古应春的哔叽袍子接过来，引入里间。
She took Gu’s serge gown and led him inside.

里间就是怡情老二的香闺，
The inner room was her own bedchamber.

一色红木家具，却配了一张外国来的大铜床，雪白珠罗纱的帐子吊得高高地，
Its furniture was all of rosewood, except for a large imported brass bed, whose snowy gauze curtains were drawn high.

床上已设着一副极精致的鸦片烟具。
An exquisite set of opium implements had already been laid out on it.

古应春略略客气了一下，先在上首躺下，
After a token show of reluctance, Gu reclined in the place of honor.

对面的空位，尤五让胡雪岩，胡雪岩又让尤五，
You offered the opposite place to Hu, and Hu offered it back to You.

这是一番做作，
These were formalities.

胡雪岩是客，而且有话要问古应春，自然该他相陪。
Hu was the guest, and he had questions for Gu; naturally, he was the one to keep him company.


“香”过两筒烟，说过一番闲话，怡情老二要去招呼“台面”，尤五也另有客要陪，
After two pipefuls and some idle conversation, Second Miss went out to attend to the dinner, while You had other guests to entertain.

小屋间里便只剩下胡、古二人。
Hu and Gu were left alone.

胡雪岩已经看出，古应春也是个很“外场”的人物，不难对付，
Hu had already seen that Gu was a man well accustomed to society and would be easy to approach.

因而一上来便用请教的口气说：“应春兄，我总算运气不错，夷场上得有识途老马指点，
He began in the tone of one seeking guidance. “Brother Yingchun, I count myself fortunate to have found an old hand to show me the way in the foreign settlement.

以后要请你多多指教。”
I shall often have to ask your advice.”

“不敢当。”古应春笑道，
“You flatter me,” Gu said with a smile.

“尤五哥是我久已慕名的，
“I have admired Brother You’s reputation for a long time.

他对你老兄特别推重，由此可见，足下必是个好朋友，
He speaks very highly of you, which tells me that you must be a friend worth having.

我们以后要多亲近。”
We ought to see more of each other.”

“是，是！
“Indeed!

四海之内皆弟兄，
All men within the four seas are brothers.

况且海禁已开，我们自己不亲近，更难对付洋人了。”
And now that the maritime prohibitions have been lifted, it will be harder still to deal with the foreigners unless we Chinese stick together.”

“着！”古应春拿手指拍着烟盘，
“Exactly!” Gu tapped the opium tray with a finger.

“雪岩兄，你这话真通达。
“Brother Xueyan, that is a thoroughly sensible remark.

说实在的、我们中国人，就是自己弄死自己，白白便宜洋人。”
The truth is that we Chinese ruin one another and let the foreigners reap the benefit.”


这话就有意思了，
There was something in that remark.

胡雪岩心想，出言要谨慎，可以把他的话套出来。
Hu decided that, with a little care, he could draw Gu out.

“现在新兴出来‘洋务’这两个字，
“These days everyone talks about ‘foreign affairs.’

官场上凡是漂亮人物，都会‘谈洋务’，最吃香的也是‘办洋务’，
All the fashionable figures in official circles discuss foreign affairs, and managing them is the most sought-after occupation of all.

这些漂亮人物我见过不少，象应春兄你刚才这两句话，我却还是第一次听见。”
I have met plenty of those gentlemen, but this is the first time I have heard anyone say what you have just said.”

“哼！”古应春冷笑着，对胡雪岩口中的“漂亮人物”，做了个鄙夷不屑的表情。
“Hmph!” Gu gave a contemptuous smile at Hu’s mention of these fashionable figures.

“那些人是闭门造车谈洋务，
“They discuss foreign affairs shut up in their own rooms, knowing nothing of the world outside.

一种是开口就是‘夷人’，把人家看做茹毛饮血的野人，
One sort calls foreigners barbarians and treats them as savages who eat raw flesh and drink blood.

再一种是听见‘洋人’二字，就恨不得先跪下来叫一声：”洋大人‘。
The other can scarcely hear the word ‘foreigner’ without wanting to fall on his knees and cry, ‘Your Excellency!’

这样子谈洋务、办洋务，无非自取其辱。
Discussing and managing foreign affairs in that spirit brings nothing but humiliation upon ourselves.”

““这话透彻得很。”胡雪岩把话绕回原来的话头上，
“You put it very clearly,” Hu said, steering the talk back to its original point.

“过与不及，就‘自己人弄死自己人’了。”
“Both extremes end in our own people ruining one another.”

“对了！”古应春拿烟签子在烟盘上比划着说：
“Precisely!” Gu gestured over the tray with his opium pick.

“恨洋人的，事事掣肘，怕洋人的，一味讨好，
“Those who hate foreigners obstruct everything; those who fear them do nothing but curry favor.

自己互相倾轧排挤，
Meanwhile, we undermine and push out our own people.

洋人脑筋快得很，有机可乘，决不会放过。
Foreigners are quick to see an opening, and they never let one slip.

这类人尤其可恶。”
People of that sort are especially detestable.”


胡雪岩看他那愤慨的神情，知道他必是受过排挤，有感而发。
His indignation convinced Hu that he had himself been pushed aside and was speaking from experience.

“不遭人妒是庸才”，受倾轧排挤的人，大致能干的居多，
Only a mediocrity escaped envy; those who suffered from rivalry and exclusion were generally capable men.

看他说话，有条有理，见解亦颇深远，
Gu spoke coherently, and his views showed considerable foresight.

可以想见其人。
Hu felt that he could judge the man from his conversation.

于是胡雪岩心想，自己正缺少帮手，尤其是这方面的人才，
He was short of helpers, particularly of men with this kind of ability.

倘或古应春能为己所用，岂不大妙？
How useful it would be to bring Gu into his service!

这个念头，几乎在他心里一出现，就已决定，但却不宜操之过急，
The thought had scarcely occurred to him before he resolved to act on it, though he must not be too hasty.

想了想，他提出一个自信一定可以引起古应春兴趣的话题。
After a moment’s reflection, he raised a subject he felt certain would interest Gu.

“应春兄！”他矍然而起，从果碟子，抓了几粒杏仁放在嘴里大嚼，嘴唇动得起劲，说话便似乎格外显得有力，
“Brother Yingchun!” He suddenly sat up, took a few almonds from a dish and chewed vigorously, which seemed to give his words added force.

“我有点不大服气！
“There is something I cannot accept.

我们自己人弄死自己人，叫洋人占了便宜，难道就不能自己人齐心一致，从洋人手里再把便宜占回来？”
If we ruin one another and let the foreigners gain at our expense, why can’t we unite and win the advantage back?”

古应春听了他的话，只是翻眼，一要烟签子不断在烟盘戳着，
Gu looked up, poking repeatedly at the tray with his opium pick.

好久，他说，“雪岩兄，从来没有人跟我说过这话。
At length he said, “Brother Xueyan, no one has ever said that to me before.

上次开了两条兵轮到下关去卖军火，价钱已经谈好，要成交了，有个王八蛋跑来见洋人，他会说洋文，
The last time we took those two warships to Xiaguan to sell arms, the price had been settled and the bargain was almost concluded when a bastard who spoke their language came to see the foreigners.

直接告诉洋人，说洪军急需洋枪火药，多的是金银珠宝。
He told them directly that Hong’s army desperately needed guns and powder and had gold, silver and jewels in abundance.

说这句话，
That was enough.

洋人翻悔了，重新议价，涨了一倍还不止。
The foreigners went back on their word, reopened negotiations and more than doubled the price.

这就是洋人占的大便宜！
What a windfall they made!

我也一直不服气。
I have resented it ever since.

能够把洋人的便宜占回来，哪怕我没有好处也干。
If I could win that advantage back from them, I would do it even without a profit for myself.

于今照你所说，自己人要齐心一致，
But you say our own people must unite.

这句话要怎么样才能做到，
How are we to accomplish that?

我要请教。“
I should like your advice.”


“这话倒是把我问倒了。”胡雪岩说，
“Now you have put me on the spot,” Hu said.

“事情是要谈出来的，
“Things have to be worked out through discussion.

现在我还不大知道洋人的情形，说不出个所以然来。
I know too little about the foreigners yet to offer a definite answer.

不过既说齐心一致，总要有个起头。
Still, if we are to unite, we must make a beginning.

譬如说，你、我，还有尤五哥，三个人在一起，至诚相见，遇事商量，
Take you, me and Brother You: the three of us could deal with one another in complete sincerity and consult over everything.

哪个的主意好，照哪个的做，就象自己出的主意一样，
Whoever has the best idea, we follow it as readily as if it were our own.

这样子一步一步把人拉拢来，
Then we bring more people together, step by step.

洋人不跟我们打交道则已，要打，就非听我们的话不可！”
The foreigners need not deal with us, but if they do, they will have to listen to us.”

“好！”古应春也一仰身坐了起来：
“Excellent!” Gu sat up too.

“三人同心、其利断金。
“When three men are of one mind, their strength can cut through metal.

就从你、我、尤五哥起头。
Let it begin with you, me and Brother You.

我洋行里那个‘康白度’也不要做了。”
I’ll give up my job as comprador at the foreign firm.”

洋行里管事的人叫“康白度”，是洋文的译音，
Comprador was a foreign term for the man who managed a foreign firm’s business dealings.

地位又非仅仅负传译之责的通事可比。
His position stood well above that of an interpreter responsible merely for translating.

胡雪岩觉得他不须如此做法。
Hu saw no need for Gu to resign.

“应春兄，”胡雪岩首先声明：“自己人说话，不妨老实。
“Brother Yingchun, between ourselves we should speak frankly.

你洋行里的职位，仍旧要维持，
You ought to keep your position at the firm.

不然跟洋人打交道不方便、而且这一来，洋人那里的消息也隔膜了。”
Otherwise our dealings with foreigners would become inconvenient, and we would lose touch with what was happening among them.”

古应春原是不假思索，想到就说的一句话，
Gu had spoken on impulse.

即使胡雪岩不点明，他回想一下，也会改变主意的。
Even without Hu’s reminder, a moment’s reflection would have made him change his mind.

因而当然一迭连声的表示同意。
Naturally, he agreed at once and repeatedly.


“我在想，”胡雪岩踌躇满志的说，“你刚才所说的‘三人同心，其利断金’。这句话真正不假。
“I was thinking,” Hu said with satisfaction, “how true your saying is about three men of one mind cutting through metal.

我们三个人，各占一门，你是洋行方面，尤五哥是江湖上，我在官场中也还有点路子。
Each of us has his own field: you have the foreign firms, Brother You the brotherhood world, and I have a few connections in official circles.

这三方面一凑，有得混了！”
Put those three together and we ought to get somewhere!”

古应春想一想，果然！
Gu considered this and saw that it was so.

受了胡雪岩的鼓舞，他也很起劲的说，“真的，
Encouraged by Hu, he replied enthusiastically, “Indeed!

巧得很！
What a fortunate combination.

这三方面要凑在一起，说实在的，真还不大容易。
Bringing those three fields together is no easy matter.

我们明天好好谈一谈，想些也众不同的花样出来，大大做它一番市面。
Let’s have a proper talk tomorrow, think of something different from everyone else and do some business on a grand scale.”

“因为有此契合，这顿花酒，吃得十分痛快，
Their understanding made the evening’s entertainment especially enjoyable.

尤五的手面很大，请的客又都是场面上人，
You was a generous host, and his guests were all men of the world.

每人都叫了两三个局，莺莺燕燕，此去彼来，弦管嗷嘈，热闹非凡。
Each summoned two or three courtesans; the women came and went amid a clamor of strings and pipes.

吃到九点多钟，又有人“翻台”，一直闹到子夜过后，
After nine, someone ordered another round of entertainment, and the festivities continued past midnight.

才回裕记丝栈。七姑奶奶和阿珠都已累了一天，早早入梦，
When Hu and You returned to Yuji Silk Warehouse, the Seventh Lady and A’zhu, tired by the day’s events, were already asleep.

老张是一向早睡早起，
Old Zhang always went to bed and rose early.

只有陈世龙一个人，泡了一壶好茶在等他们。
Only Chen Shilong was waiting, with a pot of good tea ready for them.


“五哥，你困不困？”胡雪岩兴致勃勃的问。
“Brother You, are you sleepy?” Hu asked eagerly.

“不困。”
“Not at all.

尤五问道：“你有啥事情要谈？”
What do you want to talk about?”

“事情很多。”
“A great many things.”

胡雪岩转脸说道：“世龙，你也一起听听，
Hu turned to Chen. “Shilong, you should listen too.

我今天替你找了个读洋文的先生。”
I found you a teacher of the foreigners’ language today.”

这一说，尤五立即明白：
You understood at once.

“你是说古应春！
“You mean Gu Yingchun!

你们谈得怎么样？”
How did your talk go?”

“谈得再好都没有了……”
“It couldn’t have gone better.”

胡雪岩把他跟古应春在烟榻上的那一席对话，源源本本地说了给尤五听。
Hu recounted their conversation on the opium couch from beginning to end.

尤五比较深沉，喜怒不大形于颜色，但就算如此，也可以发现他眉目轩豁，
You was a reserved man who seldom showed his feelings, but even so, his features visibly brightened.

这几天来阴沉沉的脸色，似乎悄然消失了。
The gloom of the past few days seemed quietly to have lifted.

“你的脑筋快，”他用徐缓而郑重的声音说，
“You have a quick mind,” he said slowly and earnestly.

“倒想想看，跟他有什么事可以做联手的。”
“Think what sort of business we could do together with him.”


“眼前就有一样，不过……”胡雪岩的尾音拖得很长。
“There is one opportunity before us already, only…” Hu let the last word linger.

“咦！”尤五诧异了，“有啥为难的话，说不出口？”
“Why, what is so difficult that you can’t say it?” You asked in surprise.

“我不晓得你跟卯金刀，到底有没有交情？”
“I don’t know whether you have any friendship with ‘Mao, Metal and Knife.’”

“卯金刀”是指刘丽川，
This was a coded way of writing the surname Liu and referred to Liu Lichuan.

尤五当然明白，很快地答了句：“谈不上。”
You understood immediately. “Nothing worth mentioning.”

“我这么在想，
“Here is what I was thinking.

英国人反正做生意，
The British are in business, after all.

枪炮可以卖给太平军，当然也可以卖给官军。
If they can sell guns and cannon to the Taiping forces, they can surely sell them to the government army.

今天我在席面上听说，两江总督和江苏巡抚，郡为了卯金刀在伤脑筋，
I heard at dinner that both the governor-general of Liangjiang and the governor of Jiangsu are worrying over Liu.

奏报出去，轻描淡写，好象是地方上闹事，其实是想多派兵，一仗把他打倒。
Their reports play the affair down as a local disturbance, but in reality they mean to send in a large force and crush him in one battle.

既然如此，枪炮、火药是要紧的，
In that case, guns and powder will be essential.

我们好不好先替他们办个‘粮台’，等他们的兵一到，就好出队打仗。
Could we establish a supply office for them in advance, so that when their troops arrive they can go straight into action?

如果你认为这个办法可以，我马上到苏州去跑一趟，
If you approve, I’ll go to Suzhou at once.

江苏巡抚许乃钊是我们杭州人，
Governor Xu Naizhao of Jiangsu is a fellow Hangzhou man.

一定可以找得到路子见一见他。”
I can certainly find a way to see him.”


“主意倒是不错。
“The idea is good enough.

不过我不能做。”
But I can’t take part.”

“是因为‘圈吉’的关系？”胡雪岩问。
“Because of ‘Ji in a Box’?” Hu asked.

“圈吉”周，是指周立春，
The character ji enclosed in a frame made the surname Zhou: he meant Zhou Lichun.

尤五点点头说，“一点不错，
You nodded. “Exactly.

不过你跟他没有交情，
But you have no ties to him.

你可以做。”
You could do it.”

“那就算了。
“Then let it drop.

第一，要做，就是大家一起来，
First, whatever we do, we do together.

第二，人家也晓得我跟你的交情，
Second, people know how close we are.

如果你觉得有妨碍，我做了一样也有妨碍。”
If it would put you in a difficult position, my doing it would cause the same difficulty.”

尤五听得这话，大感快慰，
You was deeply relieved to hear this.

他心里是巴不得胡雪岩不要做，但“光棍不断财路”，
In his heart he had wanted Hu to abandon the scheme, but a man of the brotherhood did not cut off another man’s livelihood.

明明是笔好生意，自己不能叫他罢手，
It was plainly a profitable venture; he could not ask Hu to give it up.

所以那样言不由衷地说“你可以做”。
That was why he had said, against his own wishes, “You could do it.”


“我还有第二条路子，
“There is another course,” Hu continued.

浙江现在正在办团练，
“Zhejiang is organizing militia forces.
