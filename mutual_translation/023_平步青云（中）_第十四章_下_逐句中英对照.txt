胡雪岩
《平步青云（中）》第十四章（下，至本章结束）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume II — Chapter Fourteen, concluding part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


就这样谈着夷场风月，不知不觉到了恰情院。
Talking of romance in the foreign settlement, they reached Yiqing House almost without noticing.

一进门就见相帮、娘姨、大姐聚在一起，指指点点在小声说笑，似乎遇见了什么神秘而有趣的事，
Inside the entrance, the male and female attendants and maids were gathered together, pointing and laughing quietly as though something mysterious and amusing had occurred.

胡雪岩便好奇地问道：“你们在讲啥？”
Curious, Hu asked, “What are you all talking about?”

“胡老爷，有位堂客在里面，跟二小姐谈得好亲热。”
“Mr Hu, there's a lady inside, having a very friendly conversation with the Second Miss.”

“堂客！”
“A lady!”

胡雪岩诧异：
Hu was astonished.

“堂子里只住官客，
“A courtesan house receives gentlemen.

哪来的堂客？”
Where has a lady come from?”

说着便站往了脚，
He stopped.

因为有堂客在里面，虽未“放门帘”，也不便乱闯。
With a woman visiting, it would be improper to walk straight in, even though the curtain had not been lowered.

“不要紧！胡老爷你请进去看了，就晓得了。”
“It's all right, Mr Hu. Go in and see, and you'll understand.”

古应春比胡雪岩更好奇，
Gu Yingchun was even more curious.

听得“不要紧”三字，首先就拔脚进门，
At the words “It's all right,” he stepped through the door ahead of Hu.

只觉眼前一亮，那位堂客如雪山皑皑，令人不可逼视。
His eyes were dazzled: the visitor was as radiantly fair as a snow-covered mountain, almost too brilliant to look upon.

这位丰腴丰皙、艳光照人的少妇，正是七姑奶奶。
The full-figured, fair-skinned young woman, radiant with beauty, was the Seventh Lady.

看见闯来的那个陌生男子，长身如鹤，英气勃勃，不觉心中一动，
Seeing the stranger—tall as a crane and full of spirit—she felt an involuntary stirring.

五百年风流冤家，就此在不该相遇的地方遇到了。
A pair of lovers destined for each other five hundred years before had met where they ought never to have met.


一半是不知如何招呼，一半是目炫心迷，
Neither knew quite how to greet the other, and both were dazzled and bewildered.

正当他们错愕无语，而怡情老二也觉得为难之际，
They stood in startled silence, while Yiqing's Second Miss wondered what to do.

胡雪岩跟了进来，一看亦大感意外：
Then Hu came in and was equally surprised.

“咦，七姐！
“Why, Seventh Sister!

是你。”
It's you.”

有人搭腔，事情便好办了，
With someone to begin the conversation, things became easier.

七姑奶奶向来说话粗声大气，不堪领教，
The Seventh Lady ordinarily spoke so loudly and roughly that she was difficult to endure.

这时不知是受了恰情老二一口吴侬软语的感染，还是因为有古应春这个一见便生好感的陌生男客在，心存顾忌，居然斯斯文文地喊一声：
Now, whether influenced by the Second Miss's soft Suzhou speech or restrained by the presence of a stranger who had pleased her at first sight, she spoke with remarkable gentleness.

“小爷叔，你想不到我在这里吧？”
“Young Uncle, you didn't expect to find me here, did you?”

自然想不到，
Certainly not.

胡雪岩心想，兄弟一起逛堂子的事，听说过，兄妹一起逛堂子，却是天大的新闻。
Hu had heard of brothers visiting courtesans together, but a brother and sister doing so was extraordinary news.

便点点头说：
He nodded.

“我道是哪位堂客？
“I was wondering who the lady could be.

怎么样也想不到是你。”
I'd never have thought of you.”

“请坐，请坐！”
“Please sit down, both of you!”

怡情老二看古应春和七姑奶奶偷眼相望，随即说道：“胡老爷，你来引见吧！”
Noticing Gu and the Seventh Lady stealing glances at one another, the Second Miss added, “Mr Hu, do introduce them.”

于是胡雪岩为古应春及七姑奶奶作了介绍，
Hu did so.

一个盈盈含笑，把双手放在左腰上，福了一福，
Smiling, the Seventh Lady placed both hands at her left waist and curtsied.

一个抱拳作揖说道：
Gu returned a clasped-hands bow.

“原来是七姐！
“So you are Seventh Sister!

真正伉爽不让须眉。”
Your spirit is the equal of any man's.”

七姑奶奶懂了他那句语，虽是恭维，却也有惊诧的意味在内，
She understood the compliment, but heard surprise in it too.

想想一个良家妇女，独闯娼门，说起来是有些不守妇道，
A respectable woman coming alone into a courtesan house could hardly be called proper conduct.

所以很难得地害了羞，红着脸报以微笑。
Unusually for her, she felt shy and answered with a blush and a smile.

她的笑容最甜，虽是窘笑，依然妩媚。
Her smile was exceptionally sweet; even an embarrassed one remained charming.

古应春心里在想：倒不曾料到，尤五有这样漂亮的一个妹妹！
Gu thought that he had never expected You Laowu to have such a beautiful sister.


等怡情老二招呼着坐定，胡雪岩自然要问来意，
Once the Second Miss had settled them in their seats, Hu naturally asked what had brought the Seventh Lady there.

七姑奶奶坦率相告，因为尤五一夜不曾回家，而她回松江之前还有许多话要问他，
She answered frankly: her brother had stayed out all night, and she had much to ask him before returning to Songjiang.

心里焦急，所以找上门来。
Growing anxious, she had come to find him.

“你一个人来的？”
“Did you come alone?”

“是啊！”
“Yes!”

七姑奶奶顽皮而得意地笑道，
She smiled with mischievous satisfaction.

“我那位妹子不许我来，阿龙也不肯带路，
“Our young sister wouldn't let me come, and A’long wouldn't show me the way.

我只好借故溜了出来，自己雇一顶小轿到这里。
I had to slip out on an excuse and hire a small sedan chair myself.

不曾遇着五哥，倒跟二小姐谈得好投机，”
I haven't found Brother Five, but I've had a splendid talk with the Second Miss.”

“啊呀！七姑奶奶，”
“Oh, Seventh Lady!”

怡情老二不安地笑着：
Their hostess smiled uneasily.

“真正不敢当你这么的称呼，
“Such a formal title is far too much for me.

叫我老二好了。”
Just call me Second.”

“或者叫小五嫂。”胡雪岩打着趣问：
“Or Little Fifth Sister-in-Law,” Hu teased.

“那么，人呢？”
“Well, where is he?”

这是指尤五，
He meant You Laowu.

怡情老二答道：“有朋友约了出去了。
“A friend invited him out,” the Second Miss replied.

说八点钟一定回来，请胡老爷、古老爷务必等他。”
“He said he'd certainly be back at eight and asked both of you to wait.”

“自然要等。”
“Of course we shall.”

胡雪岩问七姑奶奶，
Hu turned to the Seventh Lady.

“想来你也还没有吃饭，
“I suppose you haven't eaten either.

我们是上馆子，还是就在这里吃。”
Shall we go to a restaurant or eat here?”

“自然是在这里吃。”怡情老二急忙接口，
“Here, naturally!” the Second Miss interrupted eagerly.

“我请七姑奶奶吃便饭，请你们两位作陪客。”
“I'm inviting the Seventh Lady to a simple meal, with you two gentlemen to keep her company.”

“理当奉陪。”
“We shall be honoured to join you,” Gu replied.


古应春都答应了，胡雪岩还有什么话说？
Since Gu had accepted, what could Hu say?

七姑奶奶却是外场人物，
The Seventh Lady, however, was accustomed to dealing with people.

招招手把他叫到一边，悄悄问道：“小爷叔，这里的规矩，我不大懂。
She beckoned Hu aside and asked quietly, “Young Uncle, I don't know the customs here.

你看，这顿饭该不该吃？”
Do you think I ought to stay for this meal?”

“来都来了，还讲什么规矩？”
“Now that you've come, why worry about customs?”

七姑奶奶脸一红，
She blushed.

“本来是没有这种规矩的，
“There certainly isn't any custom of this kind.

我大着胆子乱闯。
I simply took my courage in both hands and came.

只怕叫人笑死了！”
I'm afraid people will laugh themselves to death at me.”

说着，悄伶伶一双眼睛瞟了过去。
As she spoke, her lively eyes glanced across the room.

胡雪岩顺着她的眼光看过去，恍炊大悟，
Hu followed her gaze and understood.

怪不得“女张飞”这般斯文！
No wonder the female Zhang Fei had become so refined!

当时只有一个念头，要成人之美。
His immediate impulse was to help a happy match along.

于是他轻轻一道：“七姐，你请过来，
“Seventh Sister, come over here,” he said softly.

我有句话说。
“There's something I want to tell you.”

“怡情院的那个“大房间”甚大，
The large room at Yiqing House was spacious indeed.

西面用个“多宝格”隔开，
Open display shelves divided off the western side.

他领着她到里面，在窗下红木太师椅上坐下，两人的脸都朝外，
He led her behind them, and they sat in rosewood armchairs beneath the window, both facing outwards.

透过多宝格，只见古应春和怡情老二也正谈到起劲，不会注意到他们的谈后，
Through the shelves, they could see Gu and the Second Miss deep in conversation, unlikely to notice their own talk.

于是胡雪岩才出言规劝。
Only then did Hu offer his advice.

“七姐”他用兄妹般，极恳切的声音说，“你不开口，是尊观音，
“Seventh Sister,” he said earnestly, as a brother might, “when you say nothing, you look like the Goddess of Mercy.

开出口来，说句实话，别人吃你不消！
But once you begin to speak, people frankly find you too much.

今天总算难得，替五哥做了面子。
Today, for once, you've done Brother Five credit.

回头你自己再做忌些，那样子，人家就不会笑你了。”
Keep a little more restraint as the evening goes on, and nobody will laugh at you.”

在平日，七姑奶奶对他这话，一定不服帖，
Ordinarily, she would have resented this.

这时却是窘笑着点一点头说：
Now she merely smiled awkwardly and nodded.

“我晓得了。
“I understand.

就是这句话吗？”
Is that all?”

“就是这句话。”
“That is all.

胡雪岩说：“你是玲珑七窍心，
You're clever enough to grasp the slightest hint.

自己有数就是，何必还要我多说呢？”
Once you understand, why should I say more?”

这话有言外之意，
There was meaning beneath the words.

七姑奶奶想再问些什么，到底还不好意思出口，
She wanted to ask further, but could not quite bring herself to do so.

只很妩媚地笑着道谢：“谢谢你，小爷叔！”
With a charming smile, she simply said, “Thank you, Young Uncle.”


两人走到外面，怡情老二迎上来说：
When they emerged, the Second Miss came to meet them.

“古老爷的话不错，
“Mr Gu is right.

这里大嘈杂，
It's too noisy here.

请到我‘小房了’去吃吧！”
Let's eat at my little house.”

姑娘与恩客另营不虑人干扰的双宿双飞之处，叫做“小房子”。
A courtesan's “little house” was a separate retreat where she and a favoured patron could be together undisturbed.

抬情老二的小房子就在这条弄堂的末尾，
Hers stood at the far end of the lane.

也是尤五每个月贴开销，但尤五的朋友多，在怡情院会客比较方便，所以难得到小房子去。
You Laowu paid its monthly expenses too, but with so many friends to entertain, he found Yiqing House more convenient and seldom used the retreat.

想不到这时候倒派上了用场。
Unexpectedly, it now had a purpose.

胡雪岩自然赞成，
Hu naturally approved.

回头对七姑奶奶说道：“那是老二住家的地方，
Turning to the Seventh Lady, he said, “It's where she lives.

比较清静，
Much quieter.

走吧！”
Let's go.”

于是怡情老二关照相帮，凡有“局票”来，只说病了，不能出“堂差”，
The Second Miss instructed the male attendant that, if any invitations arrived, he must say she was ill and could not attend outside engagements.

又关照，等尤五一来，请到小房子去。
When You arrived, he was to be directed to the little house.

这一下倒提醒七姑奶奶了，
This reminded the Seventh Lady of something.

依然是把胡雪岩喊到一边，悄悄说道：“我是溜出来的。
Calling Hu aside again, she said softly, “I slipped out.

不见我的人，他们会发急。”
If they can't find me, they'll worry.”

这是指阿珠和陈世龙而言，
She meant A’zhu and Chen.

“那好办！”他说，
“That's easily managed.

“叫人去通知一声就是了。”
We'll send someone to tell them.”

当时写了个便条，说七姑奶奶与尤五在一起，到时自回，不必着急。
He wrote a note saying that the Seventh Lady was with You Laowu and would return in due course; there was no need for anxiety.

胡雪岩掏了个银角子做力钱，叫怡情院的相帮，立刻送交陈世龙。
Taking out a small silver coin as a tip, he told the attendant to deliver it to Chen immediately.


办妥了这一切，一起走到怡情老二的小房子，
With everything settled, they went to the Second Miss's little house.

是一楼一底的石库门房子，
It was a two-storey building with a stone-framed gateway.

楼下是另一家，她住楼上，布置得楚楚有致，
Another household occupied the ground floor; she lived upstairs, where everything was arranged with taste and care.

看上去是很舒服的地方。
It looked very comfortable.

刚刚坐定，怡情院里自己做的酒菜。已经送到。
They had barely sat down when the food and wine prepared at Yiqing House arrived.

怡情老二和古应春都要推七姑奶奶上座，她则一定不肯，
Both their hostess and Gu urged the Seventh Lady to take the seat of honour, but she firmly refused.

结果是古应春首座，她和胡雪岩两对面，主人未座，正好各据一方。
In the end, Gu sat at the head, she and Hu faced one another, and their hostess took the lowest place, one person on each side of the table.

款客的是红葡萄酒，古应春送的洋酒。
The drink was a foreign red grape wine, a gift from Gu.

据说那是补血的，连宫里都经常饮用。
It was said to nourish the blood and to be drunk regularly even at the imperial court.

怡情老二把它看得很珍贵，殷殷相劝。
The Second Miss prized it highly and pressed it on her guests.

七姑奶奶的酒量，也还不坏，但一心只记着胡雪岩的忠告，强持着不肯多喝，也不多说话。
The Seventh Lady could hold her liquor, but with Hu's advice firmly in mind, she made herself drink little and speak little.

席面上只听古应春在谈胡雪岩上外国酒馆的经过，七姑奶奶和怡情老二都听得只是笑。
Gu did most of the talking, recounting Hu's visit to the foreign tavern and keeping both women laughing.

古应春这天的兴致很好，谈笑风生，滔滔不绝，一直到尤五出现，话锋才被打断。
He was in excellent spirits that day, witty and fluent, and his stream of conversation continued until You Laowu appeared.


兄妹相见，都有些不自然的表情，
Brother and sister greeted each other awkwardly.

尤五的不悦，还可以想象得到，但对七姑奶奶的微现俱惮，胡雪岩却有些意外，
You's displeasure was understandable, but the slight fear in the Seventh Lady's manner surprised Hu.

在他的印象中，七姑奶奶行事任性，从不知什么叫害怕？
In his experience, she did exactly as she pleased and knew nothing of fear.

平日只见尤五有些怕她，
Usually her brother seemed somewhat afraid of her.

此刻为何她怕尤五？
Why was she afraid of him now?

这就是为了有古应春在座的缘故。胡雪岩很快的想通了，
Hu soon understood: Gu was present.

她怕她哥哥责备她几句，当着古应春下不得台。
She feared a reprimand from her brother that would embarrass her before him.

既然如此，倒要小心防护她，
Hu must protect her.

因此，他首先就替她解释不能不来的缘故。接着便谈与哈德逊会面的经过，算是让尤五忘掉了对七姑奶奶的不快。
He began by explaining why she had had to come, then recounted their meeting with Hudson, successfully diverting You from his displeasure.

自此开始，就没有工夫说笑了，
After that there was no time for jokes.

许多正事要商量，头绪纷繁，一件事没有办妥，又扯到第二件。
Many practical matters needed discussion, so entangled that they passed to a second before resolving the first.

直到午夜，还未安排停当。
By midnight, everything was still not arranged.

“怎么办呢？
“What are we to do?

我非早早赶回杭州不可。”
I must get back to Hangzhou soon.”

胡雪岩有些着急，
Hu was anxious.

“一直都觉得人不够用，此刻越觉得摆布不开。”
“I've always felt short of people, but now I can hardly manage at all.”

半天未曾开口的七七姑奶奶开口了：
The Seventh Lady, silent for a long time, spoke up.

“也没有什么摆布不开！
“There's nothing so unmanageable about it.

小爷叔你明天尽管动身，
Go tomorrow, Young Uncle.

路上没有人送，我送，
If there's nobody to escort you, I'll do it.

保你到了嘉兴，我再回松江。”
I'll see you safely to Jiaxing and then return to Songjiang.”

“这倒也是个办法！”尤五点点头，
“That would be a solution,” You said, nodding.

“好在一路上，阿七都熟。
“A’qi knows the whole route well.

就这样吧！
Let's settle it like that.

你到了杭州，赶快派世龙拿了公事到松江来接洋枪。”
Once you reach Hangzhou, send Shilong to Songjiang as quickly as possible with the official papers to collect the foreign guns.”

他们兄妹这一番对答，使得古应春大为惊奇，
Gu was astonished by the exchange.

“原来七姐是这么能干！”
“So Seventh Sister is capable of all this!”

他自愧不如以外，也为她担忧，
He felt outdone, but worried for her too.

“这条路上，这几天很不好走，
“That route has been difficult these last few days.

要当心！”
You must be careful.”

“谢谢你！”
“Thank you.”

她报以矜持的微笑，
She gave him a restrained smile.

“不要紧的。”
“It will be quite all right.”


“真的不要紧！”
“Quite all right indeed!”

到这时候，尤五总算找到机会，可以说她一句了，
At last You had found an opportunity to reprimand her.

“我们家这位姑奶奶，一个人乱闯闯惯了的。”
“Our lady here is used to rushing off anywhere by herself.”

“也不是什么乱闯。”
“I don't simply rush off anywhere.”

七姑奶奶觉得必须分辩，
She felt obliged to defend herself.

“有把握的地方我才敢去，
“I go where I'm confident of things.

摸不清路道的地方，我也不敢乱闯。
I wouldn't venture where I didn't know my way.

象这里，我就晓得是不要紧的。”
Here, for instance, I knew there was nothing to worry about.”

“对啊！”怡情老二接口说道，
“Exactly!” the Second Miss said.

“要是不嫌弃，常常请过来，
“If you don't think it beneath you, please come often.

这里就跟自己家一样。”
Treat this place as your own home.”

“听见没有，五哥！”七姑奶奶碍意地，
“Did you hear, Brother Five?” the Seventh Lady asked triumphantly.

“就跟自己家一样！”
“My own home!”

“只有一件，”古应春也凑趣说笑，
“Only one thing,” Gu added playfully.

“回去在五嫂面前瞒着点。”
“Keep it quiet before Fifth Sister-in-Law when you return.”

“这倒不碍事。
“There's no need.

我五嫂最贤慧，
She's exceedingly good-natured.

不管他这笔帐。”
She doesn't hold this sort of thing against him.”

“好了，好了！”
“Enough, enough!”

尤五看看钟说，
You looked at the clock.

“该走了。”
“It's time to go.”


于是古应春首先告辞，却悄悄拉了胡雪岩一把。
Gu was the first to take leave, quietly giving Hu a tug.

知道是有话说。胡雪岩便跟着古应春下楼出门，
Understanding that he wished to speak, Hu followed him downstairs and outside.

站定了脚笑道：“你可是要跟我打听一个人？”
Stopping, he asked with a smile, “Do you want to inquire about someone?”

“咦！”
“Why!”

古应春诧异：
Gu was surprised.

“你怎么知道？”
“How did you know?”

“你别管！
“Never mind that.

说吧，可是要问七姑奶奶？”
Tell me—is it the Seventh Lady?”

“是的。”
“Yes.

古应春说，“我听老二告诉我，她似乎居孀多年。
The Second Miss told me she has apparently been widowed for many years.

可有这话？”
Is that true?”

“有的。不过也不算多年。”
“Yes, though it hasn't been so many years.”

“倒守得住？”
“And she's managed to remain a chaste widow?”

这是指七姑奶奶守节为何守不住，
He meant her fidelity to her late husband. Why should she not have managed it?

胡雪岩觉得他的话问得好笑，而且难以回答，
Hu found the question amusing and difficult to answer.

只好半开玩笑地答道：“你何不自己去问她？”
Half joking, he replied, “Why don't you ask her yourself?”
