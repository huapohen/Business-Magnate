CHAPTER FIFTEEN｜第十五章


With his anxiety relieved, Hu Xueyan once more devoted all his attention to business.
放下一颗悬着的心，胡雪岩又把全副精神放在正事上。

There was little to do aboard the boat, so it was a good opportunity to go over his accounts.
船上无事正好算帐，

When he worked out the total, he could scarcely believe it himself.
结出总帐一看自己都有些不相信了。

In barely half a year, as much as five hundred thousand taels had passed through his hands.
不过短短半年工夫，自己经手的款项，已有五十万两银子之多，

With business in Hangzhou, Huzhou, and Shanghai, he had certainly built up a lively operation, and everything was going smoothly.
杭州、湖州、上海三处做生意，局面搞得确是很热闹，事情也十分顺手。

Yet if anything unexpected happened, a tug at one thread could disturb the whole fabric.
但万一出了意外，牵一发动全身，

He would not fall alone: many others would be dragged down with him—first Wang Youling, then Fat Zhang, then Yu Si, and then You Laowu.
自己倒下来不说，还要牵连许多人，第一个是王有龄，第二个是张胖子，第三个是郁四，第四个是尤五。

At this thought, the autumn wind on the river felt especially cold.
这样转上念头，便觉得河上秋风，吹到身上格外冷了。

Pushing aside his abacus, he sat alone before the lamp and considered everything, growing deeply cautious.
推开算盘，独对孤灯，思前想后，生出无限警惕。

He must not rely on his quick mind and flexible methods to expand without restraint.
他告诉自己，不要自恃脑筋快、手腕活，毫无顾忌地把场面拉开来，

One man's energy was limited.
一个人的精力到底有限，

Anything he overlooked could open a gap that would rapidly widen; by the time he discovered it, matters might already be beyond repair.
有个顾不到，就会出漏洞，而漏洞会很快地越扯越大，等到发觉，往往已不可收拾。


Two conclusions followed naturally.
想到这里，自然而然生出两点觉悟，

First, he must conserve his energy and stop meddling in unnecessary disputes.
一是节省精力，不必去多管那些无谓的闹事，

Second, he must find more help.
二是还要多寻帮手，

Liu Qingsheng had been a good choice and could now take charge independently.
刘庆生算是找对了。已可独当一面，

Chen Shilong had promise, but Hu had not yet made proper use of him.
陈世龙是块好材料，却未曾善加利用。

During the remaining journey to Hangzhou, he would teach Chen the tricks and practices of business, then put him in charge of the Shanghai affairs.
于是他决走，趁这到杭州的一段旅程，将生意场中的各种“门槛”，好好教他一教，教会了就把上海这方面的事务都交给他。

Before teaching him the trade, however, he must arrange his marriage.
但是没有让他“学生意”以前，先要为他安排亲事，

That would also settle Hu's own relationship with A’zhu and remove another worry: a further way of conserving his energy.
那也就是连带了清了他自己跟阿珠之间的关系，从此心无牵挂，也是节省精力之道。

After considering the matter for some time, he decided how to begin.
于是盘算了好一会，想定了入手的办法。


The boat set off early the next morning.
第二天一早开船，

Apart from Old Zhang, who was helping at the helm in the stern, everyone was at leisure.
除了老张在船梢上帮同把舵以外，其余的人都没有什么事。

Hu deliberately called Chen into the cabin for a talk.
他特意叫陈世龙进舱谈话，

Since boarding, A’zhu had spent most of her time in the after cabin, saying little even when they ate together.
从一上船，阿珠便常在后舱。就是一起吃饭的时候，也不大交谈。

Chen, of course, often went back to see her.
当然，陈世龙是常到后舱去找她的。

Hu felt certain that she would listen carefully from there to whatever the two men said in the middle cabin.
胡雪岩料定他跟陈世龙在中舱谈什么，她一定会在后舱，留心静听，

He therefore meant to appear to speak casually while addressing his words to her.
所以他预备装作“言者无意”，其实是有心要说给她听。

“Shilong, my business has taken shape now.
“世龙！”他说，“我现在的场面是撑起来了。

But one man can't eat all the rice himself: we must all lend a hand.
不过饭是一个人吃不完的，要大家一起来动手。

Tell me, would you rather be in Huzhou or Shanghai?”
我现在问问你的意思，你是想在湖州，还是想在上海？”

Unaware that Hu had a settled plan and was asking deliberately, Chen thought he really was being offered a choice.
陈世龙不知道他胸有成竹，有意如此发问，只当真的要他自己挑一处，

Shanghai might be prosperous, but he was unsure of himself there.
上海虽然繁华，做事却无把握，

Huzhou was home, and he could stay close to A’zhu.
在湖州是本乡本土，而且又厮守着阿珠，

Naturally he preferred Huzhou.
自然是湖州好。

“I'd like to get the silk firm properly established in Huzhou first.”
“我想先在湖州，把丝行弄好了再说。”

“I knew you'd choose Huzhou.”
“我晓得你要挑湖州，”

With his back to the after cabin, Hu could make a face without A’zhu seeing.
胡雪岩背对后舱，不怕阿珠看见他的脸，

He winked emphatically at Chen, warning him that the next words had a purpose.
所以向陈世龙使劲挤一挤眼睛，表示下面那句话别有用心，叫他留神：

“You can't bear to leave A’zhu!”
“你是舍不得阿珠！”

Chen was quick to understand and gave an embarrassed smile of acknowledgment.
陈世龙也很聪明，做一个不好意思的笑容，表示默认。

With one man making the assertion and the other accepting it, the relationship among all three was settled in that single exchange—unless A’zhu herself came out and plainly declared that she would not marry Chen Shilong.
一个如此说，一个如此承认，除非阿珠自己走出来明明白白说一句，不愿嫁陈世龙！那么，他们三个人之间的关系，就在这一句话中交代清楚了。


Listening from the after cabin, A’zhu was furious.
在后舱听壁脚的阿珠，十分气恼，

They were treating a person like a bale of silk: a few words between them, and the transaction was complete!
心想：简直把一个人看成一包丝一样，凭你们一句话，就算交易过手了！

How could they take everything upon themselves like that?
世上哪有这样自说自话的事？

Yet, for all her angry thoughts, she remained where she was, scarcely breathing as she listened.
想归想，气归气，人还是坐在那里不动，屏声息气，细听外面，

Hu was speaking again.
胡雪岩又在说了。

“My idea is that the silk firm has your father-in-law and mother-in-law there...”
“我的意思，丝行有你丈人、丈母娘在那里。”

At this, A’zhu was astonished.
听到这里，阿珠惊异不止，

Whom did he mean by “father-in-law and mother-in-law”?
“丈人、丈母娘”是指谁？她自己这样在问。

Listening on, she could not mistake it.
细听下去，明明白白，

Who could Chen's parents-in-law be but her own parents?
陈世龙的丈人、丈母娘，不是自己父母是哪个？

Bewilderment, shame, and resentment mingled with an excitement she could not describe.
阿珠惊疑羞愤，外带一种说不出的兴奋，

Her thoughts raced like ten thousand horses, and only by repeatedly mastering herself could she make out what was being said.
心里乱得如万马奔腾，自己克制了又克制，才能勉强听得清外面的话。

“To tell the truth, A’zhu's mother hasn't quite the right idea.
“说起来，阿珠的娘的想法也不大对！

Because I helped the family, she thinks she ought to give me her daughter in marriage as repayment.
她以为我帮了她家的忙，她就得把女儿许配给我，作为报答。

But those are separate matters.
其实桥归桥，路归路，

I didn't help them because I wanted their daughter.”
我帮他们的忙，又不是在想他们的女儿。”

Hypocrite!
哼！假正经！

A’zhu cursed inwardly.
阿珠不由得在心里骂，

Remembering the many ways Hu had once tried to win her affection, she felt her face grow warm and silently added, Shameless!
同时想起胡雪岩当初许多勾引的行径，脸上有些发烧，暗暗的又骂了句：不要脸！


What followed made her feel rather better.
再听下去，她比较舒服了。

“In all conscience,” Hu said, “do I like A’zhu?
“讲句良心话，”胡雪岩说，“我喜欢不喜欢阿珠呢？

Of course I do.
当然喜欢的。

But I won't put her at a disadvantage.
不过，我不肯委屈阿珠。

Her character is spotless, and even a young lady of a great family might not equal her in looks and qualities.
冰清玉洁，大家小姐不见得有她那样子的品貌！

Shilong, even marrying you would be beneath her.”
世龙，她嫁了你也是委屈的。”

“I know.”
“我晓得。”

Chen nodded with a sense of his own unworthiness.
陈世龙自惭的点一点头。

“It's good that you know.
“你晓得就好。”

You must be especially considerate towards her.” Chen repeated the same answer.
胡雪岩又说，“总要格外对她体贴。”陈世龙依然是那句话：

“I know.”
“我晓得。”

He kept agreeing as though he had already married her!
口口声声顺从着，倒象真的已把人家娶到手了似的。

A’zhu deeply resented it.
阿珠心里非常不服气，

She was puzzled too.
同时也有些奇怪，

From their tone, it sounded as though they had long since made a private bargain behind her back.
听口风好象他们早就瞒着自己，暗中做了“交易”，

She must first discover exactly what had happened, then consider how to get her own back.
倒要仔仔细细先把事情弄清楚，然后再想报复的主意。

This time Chen spoke.
这回是陈世龙在说话：

“Mr Hu, what do you think I ought to do about the marriage?
“胡先生，那么，你看我这件事该怎么办？

I have nothing—no means at all.”
赤手空拳，一点底子都没有。”

“You have me!” Hu answered readily.
“有我！”胡雪岩答得极其爽脆，

“I have three matches to arrange at present.
“我今天一共有三头媒要做，

One has already succeeded; another needs more consideration; and then there's yours.
一头已经成功了，还有一头要看看再说，再有就是你这头媒。

A word from me will settle Old Zhang.
老张那里我一说就成功，

Your mother-in-law is easier still: she always listens to me.
你丈母娘更不用说；最听我的话。

A’zhu is devoted to her parents.
阿珠最孝顺，

Once they agree, we needn't fear she won't.”
只要跟两老说好了，不怕她不答应。”

So that was it!
原来如此，

They meant to use her parents to pressure her.
阿珠心想：拿我父母来压我，

No wonder they were so confident.
所以有这样子的把握，

They thought nothing of her at all.
那也太目中无人了。

Her first step must be to speak to her father and prevent him from agreeing too readily.
于今之计，第一步先要在爹面前说好，不可轻易答应。

Then they could stand there looking helpless!
到时候叫你干瞪眼！


She had scarcely settled this plan when she was startled again: Hu seemed to read her thoughts aloud.
刚想得好好地，立刻又是一愣，因为胡雪岩说破了她的心思，

“But A’zhu is extremely proud.
“不过”，他说，“阿珠的性子最做，

Kindness moves her; force doesn't.
服软不服硬，

We must allow for that too.
也要防她一脚！

Even if she obeyed her parents unwillingly, with a thousand resentments in her heart, she wouldn't treat you particularly well afterwards.
就算父母之命，勉强依从，心里一千一万个不甘心，将来也不会对你怎么样好的。

In the end, what matters most is that both people should be willing.
所以说到头来，两厢情愿最要紧。

Always remember what I've told you: A’zhu responds to kindness, not force.
你总要记住我这句话，阿珠服软不服硬。

Give way to her in everything, and you'll have happiness all your life.”
处处依她，包你一辈子有福享。”

These words left a mixture of sweetness and pain in her heart.
听到这几句话，阿珠心里又酸又甜，

They also took the wind out of her sails: she could no longer summon any resistance.
同时也觉得泄了气，什么劲道都拿不出来了。

Yet she still resented being managed in this way and felt that Chen ought not to have things so easily.
不过总还有些不甘，不甘于如此受人摆布，同时也觉得不能就这么便宜了陈世龙。

“My plan,” Hu continued, “is to see whether we can hold the wedding before the year's end.
“我的打算是这样，看看年底办喜事来不来得及。

If there isn't time, we can complete the formal betrothal this year and have you marry into her household next year...”
如果来不及，就今年‘传红’，明年‘入赘’……”

“Marry into her household!” Chen interrupted loudly.
“入赘！”陈世龙大声插嘴，

His voice alone showed his reluctance, and A’zhu's heart tightened as she listened.
光听声音，就知道他不愿，在后舱的阿珠不由得就把心悬了起来。

“You aren't being asked to change your surname to Zhang.
“又不是要你改姓张，

The two households will simply become one.
不过两家并作一家，

There's nothing disgraceful about it.”
也不是什么失面子的事！”

“As long as I don't have to change my surname.”
“不改姓就可以。”


“Don't fail to appreciate your good fortune!”
“你不要得福不知！”

Hu deliberately pitched this at A’zhu.
胡雪岩故意这样说给阿珠听：

“Even if you wanted to change your surname, she might not think you good enough.”
“就算你想改性，阿珠也许看你不上眼。”

Chen showed his white teeth in an embarrassed smile.
陈世龙露着一嘴雪白的牙齿，不好意思地笑了。

Through a crack in the partition, A’zhu saw it and felt a pleasant sense of triumph.
这笑容正落在壁缝中向外张望的阿珠眼中，她的感觉是得意的舒服。

“A good wife is all very well,” Hu continued, “but a man who never moves a step from her apron strings won't amount to much.
“老婆虽好，吊在裙带上一步不离，也太没有出息了。”胡雪岩说，

With your parents-in-law at the Huzhou silk firm, they can manage it perfectly well.
“湖州丝行有你丈人、丈母娘在，尽可以照料得了。

I want you to help me in Shanghai.
我希望你在上海帮我的忙，

Learn the foreign language properly from Old Gu, and it will serve you all your life.”
跟老古把洋文学学好，将来受用无穷。”

“Gladly!”
“好啊！”

Chen was excited.
陈世龙很兴奋地，

“Mr Gu speaks it wonderfully well.
“古先生的洋文，说得真是呱呱叫，

I'll make sure I learn it from him.”
我一定跟他学会了它！”

“That's the spirit!”
“这才是！”

Hu sounded pleased.
胡雪岩用欣慰的声音说，

“Fortunately, the silk business will bring you back to Huzhou often.
“好在丝生意上有关联，常常要回湖州，

You'll have plenty of time to show A’zhu your affection.”
有得你跟阿珠亲热的时候！”

The wretch!
要死！

Her face flushed crimson.
阿珠一下子绯红了脸，

She did not know whether to sit or stand, yet dared make no sound for fear that the men would discover her eavesdropping.
顿时觉得坐也不是，站也不是，却又不敢弄出声响来，怕前面发觉她在偷听，

She tiptoed back to her berth and, with a hand over her racing heart, carefully considered all they had said.
于是蹑手蹑脚，掩到自己铺位上，手抚着一颗突夹在跳的心，细细去想他们所说的那些话。


She became so absorbed that she lost track of time.
这一想恩得忘掉辰光，

Only when Old Zhang called did she come to herself.
直到老张在喊，她才警觉，

Looking out, she saw the sun overhead: it was noon already.
朝窗外望了一下，太阳当头，已经中午了。

“Come and eat!
“来吃饭！”

A’zhu, what have you been doing?
老张问道，“阿珠，你在作啥？

I haven't seen you all morning.”
一直不见你的人？”

“I fell asleep!”
“我睡着了！”

She was pleased with that answer.
她自己觉得这句话答得很好，

Being asleep meant that she could not have heard Hu and Chen talking, which would make it easier to pretend ignorance when she faced them.
睡着了便表示根本没有听见胡雪岩和陈世龙的话，见了面就容易装糊涂了。

They were pretending too.
她装人家也装，

At the table, both men behaved as usual.
在饭桌上胡雪岩和陈世龙一如平时，

It was Old Zhang who had much to say.
倒是老张有许多话，

The boat would stop at Deqing that afternoon, where they would part: Hu and Chen would continue south to Hangzhou, while Old Zhang took his daughter north towards home in the same boat.
因为这天下午船泊德清，就要分手，胡雪岩和陈世龙往南到杭州，老张带着女儿，原船往北回家，

Naturally, various things had to be settled.
自然有些事要交代交代。

They reached Deqing early that afternoon.
当天下午，很早就到了德清，

As soon as the boat was moored, Hu invited Old Zhang ashore for a stroll.
船一泊定，胡雪岩邀老张上岸走走。

A’zhu immediately guessed that they had something concerning her to discuss.
阿珠立刻想到，他们是有关自己的话要谈，

While lying in her berth that morning, she had decided that, whatever happened, she must first return to Huzhou and talk to her mother about this lifelong matter.
她上午躺在床上想心事，就已经盘算过，这件终身大事，不管怎么样，要自己回到湖州先告诉了娘，再作道理。

If her father agreed now, there would be no room left for discussion.
如果她爹一答应，便毫无商量的余地。

Unwilling to let others arrange everything for her, she resolved to remain beside him all day and give Hu no opportunity to speak.
她不甘于随人摆布，因而打定主意，这一天要一直跟爹在一起，不容胡雪岩有开口的机会。

What could she do now?
那么此刻怎么办呢？

There was only one way: follow them.
唯一的办法，仍是跟着不放，

Surely Hu would not openly discuss matchmaking with her standing there!
胡雪岩总不见得当面锣，对面鼓，有自己在场，便好意思提做媒的话！


“Father, I'm coming too!” she called.
于是她接口喊道：“爹，我也去！”

Naturally Hu did not want her along.
胡雪岩自然不要她去。

Preventing it was easy; he did not even need to think.
这容易得紧，想都不用想，便有了话，

“A’zhu, would you do me a favour and pack my odds and ends?”
“阿珠，拜托你，替我把零碎东西收拾收拾，好不好？”

“Yes!” Honest Old Zhang agreed.
“是啊！”老张老实，

“We're changing boats.
“要掉船了，

Everyone's things need sorting out.
各人的东西该归一归。

You stay here.”
你不要去！”

This gave Hu another opening.
这一说，胡雪岩又有了话，

“Exactly!
“对的！”

Shilong, go through the presents that are to be taken to Huzhou, and explain everything to A’zhu.
他喊道，“世龙，你也看一看，哪些东西该带到湖州送人的，跟阿珠交代清楚，

We mustn't have any mistakes.”
不要弄错了！”

With that, he strode ashore with Old Zhang, deliberately leaving Chen behind to confide his feelings to her.
说完，他跟看张扬长上岸，有意把陈世龙留在船上，好跟阿珠细诉衷曲。

A’zhu could scarcely contain her indignation.
阿珠心里实在有些气不过，

She felt like Monkey in Journey to the West: however she tried, she could never escape Hu Xueyan's palm.
想想自己真象《西游记》的孙悟空，怎么样也翻不出胡雪岩的手掌。

For the moment, Chen would naturally have to bear the brunt of her frustration.
这份闲气，此刻自然要发在陈世龙头上了。


“What have they gone ashore for?” she demanded angrily.
“他们上岸去做啥？”她气鼓鼓地问。

Chen was naturally clever.
陈世龙本来就聪明，

During his time with Hu, he had also picked up many ways of handling people and situations simply by watching and listening.
加以这阵子跟着胡雪岩，耳濡目染，学会了许多待人处事的诀窍。

In something affecting his entire future, he could hardly afford carelessness.
这样一件有关自己一辈子的大事。当然更不敢疏忽，

Rather than answer at once, he looked up and considered her carefully, determined to understand her attitude first.
所以这时不忙着答阿珠的话，先抬眼看，用心想，要把她的态度弄明白了再说。

That question proved she had heard their whole conversation that morning.
他在想：阿珠问到这句话，就可以证明，他们上午的那一番谈话，她已经听得清清楚楚，

Now she suspected that Hu had gone ashore to discuss her marriage with her father.
此刻是疑心胡雪岩跟她父亲去谈她的终身。

If so, why had she not come forward to speak that morning?
既然如此，上午为何不站出来说话，

Why fly into a temper now?
此刻却大光其火？

Clearly, this was a fit of temper.
可见得光火是闹脾气。

He understood that too: the more he opposed her, the fiercer she would become. The best course was to leave her nothing on which to vent her anger.
她的脾气他也摸透了，越顶越凶最好的应付办法是让她发不出火。

Smiling apologetically, he said, “I really don't know.
