胡雪岩
《平步青云（中）》第十五章（中）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume II — Chapter Fifteen, middle part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


* * *一到杭州，胡雪岩回家坐得一坐，立刻便到阜康，
On reaching Hangzhou, Hu Xueyan stopped briefly at home, then went straight to Fukang.

陈世龙已押了行李先在那里等候。
Chen Shilong had already brought the luggage there and was waiting.

行李虽多，尽是些送人的礼物，
There was a great deal of it, but everything was a present for someone.

由刘庆生帮着料理，一份份分配停当，派了一个“出店”陪着陈世龙一家家去分送。
With Liu Qingsheng's help, the gifts were sorted, and one of the bank's errand clerks was sent with Chen to deliver them from house to house.

胡雪岩则趁此刻工夫，听取刘庆生的报告。
Hu used the interval to hear Liu's report.

“胡先生，请你先看帐。”刘庆生捧着一叠帐簿，很郑重地说。
“Mr Hu, please examine the accounts first,” Liu said gravely, bringing a pile of ledgers.

“不忙，不忙！
“No hurry, no hurry.

你先跟我说说大概情形。”
Give me a general account first.”

“请你看了帐再说。”
“Please look at the books before we talk.”

听他如此坚持，料知帐傅中就可以看出生意好坏，
His insistence suggested that the books themselves would show how business had gone.

于是他点点头先看存款。
Hu nodded and began with the deposits.

一看不由得诧异了，
What he saw astonished him.

存户中颇多“张得标”、“李德胜”、“王占魁”、“赵虎臣”之类的名字，存银自几百到上万不等，
There were many names such as Zhang Debiao, Li Desheng, Wang Zhankui, and Zhao Huchen, with sums ranging from a few hundred to more than ten thousand taels.

而名下什九注着这么四个小字，“长期无息。”
Nine out of ten entries bore the brief annotation: “Long-term; no interest.”

“唷，唷！”胡雪岩大为惊异，
“Well, well!” Hu exclaimed.

“阜康真的要发财了！
“Fukang really is going to prosper!

怎么会有这么多的户头？”
How did we acquire so many accounts?”

“胡先生！”刘庆生矜持着说：“你再看这一笔帐。”
With restrained pride, Liu said, “Please look at this entry too.”

他翻到的一笔帐是支出，上面写着：“八月二十五日付罗尚德名下本银一万一千两。
He turned to a payment: “Twenty-fifth day of the eighth month: paid out eleven thousand taels, principal standing in Luo Shangde's name.

息免。”
Interest waived.”

“喔，
“Oh!

原来罗尚德的那笔款子，提回去了？”
So Luo has withdrawn his deposit?”

“不是！”
“No.

刘庆生说，“罗尚德阵亡了，
Luo Shangde was killed in action.

银子等于是我送还的。
In effect, I returned the money.

我不知道这件事做得对不对……”
I don't know whether I did the right thing...”


刘庆生细谈这件事的经过，
Liu explained what had happened.

是八月二十五那天，有两个军官到阜康来问，说是听闻罗尚德曾有一笔款子存在阜康，可有其事？
On the twenty-fifth of the eighth month, two officers had come to ask whether it was true that Luo had deposited money at Fukang.

又说罗尚德已经阵亡，但他在四川还有亲属，
They said he had been killed in action but still had relatives in Sichuan.

如果有这笔款子，要提出来寄回去。
If the deposit existed, they wished to withdraw it and send it home.

罗尚德的存折在刘庆生手里，
Luo's passbook was in Liu's keeping.

倘或否认其事，别无人证。
Had he denied the account's existence, there would have been no other witness.

但他不肯这样做，
But he refused to act that way.

一口承认，同时立即取出存折，验明银数，
He acknowledged it at once, produced the passbook, and verified the amount.

但他表示，不能凭他们两个人的片面之词就付这笔存款。
He made clear, however, that he could not pay out the deposit on the two men's unsupported statement.

“那么该怎么办呢？”
“What should we do, then?”

“我知道罗老爷跟抚台衙门的刘二爷是朋友，
“I know that Mr Luo was a friend of Mr Liu Er at the governor's office.

要刘二爷跟你们营官一起出面，出条子给阜廉。”刘庆生说：
Mr Liu Er and your commanding officer must jointly give Fukang written authorisation,” Liu replied.

“只要罗老爷是真的阵亡，你们各位肯担责任，阜康立刻照付。
“Provided Luo really has been killed in action and you gentlemen accept responsibility, Fukang will pay immediately.”

“于是那两个军官，当天便我了刘二爷来，公同具了领条，
The two officers brought Liu Er that same day, and a joint receipt was signed.

刘庆生立即捧出一万一千两银子，还要算利息，人家自然不肯再要。
Liu immediately produced eleven thousand taels and offered to calculate the interest as well, but they naturally refused anything more.

这样到了第二天，张得标、李德胜等等，便都上门来了。
The following day, Zhang Debiao, Li Desheng, and the others began coming to the bank.


胡雪岩听他讲完，异常满意，
Hu was exceedingly pleased by the story.

“庆生，”他说，“阜康的牌子打响了！
“Qingsheng, Fukang's reputation is made!

你做得高明之极。
You handled it brilliantly.”

““老实说，”刘庆生自己也觉得很安慰，“我是从胡先生你这里学来的窍门。
“To be honest,” Liu said, feeling much comforted himself, “I learned the method from you.

做生意诚实不欺，只要自己一颗心把得定就可以了，
To deal honestly, one need only hold firm to one's principles.

诚实不欺要叫主顾晓得，到处去讲，那得要花点心思，
But to make customers know of that honesty and speak of it everywhere requires some thought.

我总算灵机一动，把机会抓住了。”
I had a sudden idea and managed to seize the opportunity.”

“对！
“Exactly.

做生意把握机会，是第一等的学问。
Seizing opportunities is the highest art in business.

你能够做到这一点，我非常高兴。
I'm delighted that you can do it.

庆生，我现在帮手不够，
Qingsheng, I haven't enough assistants.

你还是替我享管点事，
You must take more off my hands.

以后钱庄的生意都归你。”胡雪岩说：
From now on, the bank's business is all yours to manage.

“我一切不管，都归你调度。”
I shall leave everything to your direction.”

“这……”刘庆生兴奋之余，反有恐惧不胜之感，
Alongside his excitement, Liu felt overwhelming apprehension.

“这副扭子我怕挑不下。”
“I'm afraid I may not be able to carry such a burden.”

“不要紧！
“Don't worry.

你只要多用心思，凡事想停当了去做，
Put more thought into things and work them out before acting.

就冒点风险也不要紧。
A little risk doesn't matter.

不冒风险的生意，人人会做，
Everyone can do business that involves no risk.

如问能够比头？
How could that put you ahead?

只要值得，你尽管放手去做。“
As long as it's worthwhile, go ahead boldly.”


“这话就很难说了，
“That's difficult to judge.

怎么叫值得，怎么叫不值得？
What counts as worthwhile and what doesn't?

各人看法不同。”
People see things differently.”

“人生在世，不为利，就为名。
“In life, people seek either profit or a good name.

做生意也是一样，
Business is the same.

冒险值得不值得，就看你两佯当中能不能占一样？”
Whether a risk is worth taking depends on whether you can gain one of the two.”

胡雪岩停了一下指着帐簿说，
Hu paused and pointed to the ledger.

“譬如这笔放款，
“Take this loan.

我知道此人是个米商，借了钱去做生意，
I know the borrower is a rice merchant, using the money for trade.

你就要弄弄清楚，他的米是运到什么地方？
You must establish where he's sending his rice.

运到不曾失守的地方，不要紧，
If it's going somewhere that hasn't fallen, that's all right.

运到长毛那里，这笔放款就不能做！
If it's going to the Longhairs, you mustn't lend.

为啥呢，
Why?

万一这笔帐放倒了，外面说起来是：哪个要你去帮长毛？
If the loan goes bad, people will say, ‘Who asked you to help the Longhairs?

倒帐活该！
You deserved to lose your money.’

这一来名利两失，
You lose both profit and reputation.

自然犯不着冒险。”
Naturally, that risk isn't worth taking.”

“我懂了！”刘庆生深深点头，
Liu nodded firmly. “I understand.

“凡事总要有个退步。
There must always be a way to fall back.

即使出了事，也能够在台面上说得过去。”
Even if something goes wrong, we must be able to justify ourselves openly.”

“对啊！庆生，”
“Exactly, Qingsheng!”

胡雪岩拍着他的肩说，
Hu patted his shoulder.

“你完全懂了！
“You understand completely.

我们的生意，不管是啥，都是这个宗旨，万一失手，有话好说。
Whatever business we do, that is our principle: if we fail, we must have an explanation.

这样子，别人能够原谅你，就还有从头来起的机会，
If others can forgive us, we still have a chance to begin again.

虽败不倒！”“虽败不倒！”刘庆生把这句话在心里念了好几遍，颇有领悟。
Defeated, but not destroyed!” Liu repeated the phrase inwardly several times and grasped much from it.

接着便谈了些业务扩充的计划，
They then discussed plans for expanding the business.

胡雪岩因为自己在杭州只有几天耽搁，一拖便无结果，
Since Hu could remain in Hangzhou only a few days, delay might leave matters unsettled.

所以或可或否，当时便要作出决定。
He therefore gave an immediate decision, yes or no, on each proposal.


正在从长计议时，只听有人一路喊了进来：“二弟，二弟！”
In the midst of their deliberations, someone came in calling, “Second Brother! Second Brother!”

听这称呼便知是嵇鹤龄，
The form of address could belong only to Ji Heling.

胡雪岩急忙迎了出去，
Hu hurried out to greet him.

只见他红光满面，梳一条又黑又亮的辫子，
His face glowed, and his queue was black and glossy.

身上穿一件极挺括的紫酱色线春夹袍，外面套一件黑缎“巴图鲁”坎肩，平肩一排珊瑚套扣，
He wore a crisp, lined robe of purplish-brown xianchun cloth beneath a black satin baturu waistcoat, with a row of coral fastening buttons level with the shoulders.

卷着袖子，露出雪白纺绸的袖头，
His rolled sleeves revealed snowy-white silk cuffs.

左手盘一对核桃，右手拿昔支湘妃竹镶翠的短烟袋，
In his left hand he turned a pair of walnuts; in his right he held a short pipe of spotted bamboo mounted with jade.

十足一副纨袴公子的打扮，
He looked every inch the fashionable young gentleman.

以前的那副不修边幅的名士派头，连影子都找不到了。
Not a trace remained of his former careless scholar's appearance.

“大哥！”胡雪岩笑道：
“Elder Brother!” Hu laughed.

“你年轻了十几岁，
“You look more than ten years younger.

差点都认不得了。”
I hardly recognised you.”

“都是瑞云啊！”
“It's all Ruiyun!”

嵇鹤龄有着掩抑不往的喜色，
Ji could not suppress his delight.

“打扮了几个孩子，还要打扮我。
“After dressing up the children, she insists on dressing me up too.

不作无益之事，何以遣有生之涯？
If we never did anything useless, how would we pass our allotted years?

这且不去说它。
But never mind that.

我是奉命来邀客，
I've been ordered to invite you.

瑞云叫我来说，晚上为你接风，
Ruiyun says we're to give you a welcome-home dinner tonight.

没有什么菜吃，但一定要到。”
There won't be much to eat, but you must come.”

“一定到。
“Certainly.

只是时候不会太早。”
Only I may not be very early.”

“你是要先去记雪公？”嵇鹤龄说，
“You mean to call on Mr Xuexuan first?

“那就不必了。
There's no need.

我已约了雪公，
I've invited him too.

他到舍间来会你，吃完饭，你们一起走好了。”
He'll meet you at my house, and you can leave together after dinner.”

“那好，
“Excellent.

省了我多少事。”
That saves me a great deal of trouble.”

胡雪岩笑着问道，
Hu smiled.

“瑞姑娘怎么样？”
“How is Miss Rui?”

“那是尽在不言中了。
“That goes without saying.

总而言之一句话，承情不尽。”
In a word, I'm endlessly grateful.”


“新城的案子，雪公已经写信告诉我了，说得语焉不详，
“Mr Xuexuan wrote to tell me about the Xincheng affair, but without much detail.

我在上海记挂得很。”
I've been concerned about it in Shanghai.

胡雪岩问道，“对你总有个安排？”
Has an arrangement been made for you?” Hu asked.

“是的，
“Yes.

我正要跟你详细谈。”
I was just going to tell you all about it.”

嵇鹤龄略一踌躇，
Ji hesitated.

接着又说，“话太长，
“But it's a long story.

一说开头，就无法收场了。
Once I begin, there'll be no end.

这样吧，我还要去办点事，
Let's do this: I still have an errand to run.

瑞云要我去买儿盆菊花，
Ruiyun wants me to buy a few pots of chrysanthemums.

我把轿子留在这里，回头你坐了来。
I'll leave my chair here for you to come in later.

最好早些到，雪公未来之前，我们先可以好好谈一谈。”
Try to arrive early, so that we can talk properly before Mr Xuexuan comes.”

看他春风满面，服饰华丽，此时又知道养了“轿班”，可知情况很不坏，
Seeing his cheerful expression and fine clothes, and now discovering that he maintained his own chair-bearers, Hu could tell that matters were going reasonably well.

胡雪岩先忧放心了，点点头答应，尽快赴约。
Reassured, he nodded and promised to come as soon as possible.

在阜康把几件紧要的事处置完毕，胡雪岩坐了轿子径到嵇家。
After dealing with several urgent matters at Fukang, he took the chair straight to Ji's house.

嵇鹤龄也刚回来不久，正穿着短衣在指挥花匠陈设菊花，
Ji had only recently returned and was wearing short working clothes while directing a gardener in arranging the chrysanthemums.

一见他来，便说一声：“你到里面坐，
“Go inside and sit down,” he said on seeing Hu.

我洗了手就来。”
“I'll wash my hands and join you.”

这时张贵已来肃客，看见胡雪岩异常恭敬，也格外亲热，
Zhang Gui came to receive the guest, exceptionally respectful and warmly attentive.

一面伛偻着身子引路，一面殷殷问讯，直接领到后厅，迎面遇着瑞云。
Bowing as he led the way and inquiring after Hu's well-being, he took him directly to the rear hall, where they met Ruiyun.


“二老爷！”因为胡雪岩与嵇鹤龄拜了把子，所以她这样含笑称呼，
“Second Master!” she said with a smile, using the title because Hu and Ji were sworn brothers.

略一凝视，接着又说，“清瘦了些，
After studying him briefly, she added, “You've grown a little thinner.

想来路上辛苦了！
The journey must have been tiring.

不过精神气色都还是老样子。”
But your spirits and complexion are just the same.”

“你象是发福了。”
“You seem to have filled out.”

胡雪岩笑着问，
Hu smiled.

“日子过得还称心吧！”
“Are you happy with life here?”

“托二老爷的福。”瑞云向里喊道：“荷官，领了弟弟、妹妹来见二叔！”
“Thanks to you, Second Master.” She called inside, “Heguan, bring your younger brothers and sisters to greet Second Uncle!”

“噢！”里面娇滴滴地答应一声，
“Yes!” came a sweet voice.

只见丹荷领头，带着一群小家伙，
Danhe led the little troop out.

摇摇摆摆走了来，一个个都穿得很干净，等丹荷一站定，便也都站住了。
They toddled forward in clean clothes and stopped as soon as she did.

“叫啊！二叔。”瑞云看着丹荷说。
“Greet him—Second Uncle,” Ruiyun told her.

于是丹荷先叫，她叫过了再叫弟、妹们叫。
Danhe spoke first, then instructed the others to follow.

胡雪岩一看这情形，对瑞云佩服得不得了。
Seeing this, Hu admired Ruiyun enormously.

她是用的“擒贼擒王”的手段，
She had followed the principle of catching the bandits' chief first.

不知怎么一来，把最调皮的丹荷笼络得服服帖帖！那一群小家伙便也都安分了。
Somehow, she had won over the most mischievous child, Danhe, so completely that the rest had settled down too.

“老大呢？”他问。
“Where's the eldest?” he asked.

“我送他‘附馆’去了。”嵇鹤龄进门接口，
“I've sent him to join another household's school,” Ji answered as he entered.

两个小的立刻便都扑了过去。
The two youngest immediately rushed to him.

胡雪岩心里着实羡慕嵇鹤龄，自然也深感安慰，
Hu truly envied Ji, and felt deeply comforted as well.

拉着丹荷的手问长问短，好半天不放。
He took Danhe's hand, asked her all manner of questions, and did not let go for a long time.

“好了好了！”瑞云大声说道，
“Enough, enough!” Ruiyun said loudly.

“都跟着二姐到里头去，
“Follow your second sister inside.

不要来烦你们二叔！”
Don't bother Second Uncle.”


遣走了孩子们，瑞云也告个便回到厨下。
After sending the children away, Ruiyun excused herself and returned to the kitchen.

于是嵇鹤龄跟胡雪岩谈起别后的光景。
Ji then described what had happened since they parted.

新城之行，先抚后剿的宗旨定得不错，
The policy for Xincheng—conciliation first, force afterwards if needed—had been correct.

当地士绅对嵇鹤龄革枪匹马，深入危城，都佩服他的胆气，也了解他的诚意，
The local gentry admired his courage in going alone into the endangered county town and recognised his sincerity.

因此都愿意跟他合作，设法把为首的“ 强盗和尚”意心，引诱到县自首。
They willingly cooperated, finding a way to persuade the leader, the “bandit monk” Huixin, to come to the county office and surrender.

蛇无头而不行，乌合之众，一下子散得光光。
A snake cannot move without its head: the disorganised followers scattered completely.

前后不过费了半个月的工夫。
The whole affair had taken no more than half a month.

功成回来，王有龄自然敬礼有加，万分亲热，私人先送了五百两银子，作为谢礼。
When Ji returned successful, Wang naturally treated him with special respect and warmth, offering him five hundred taels from his own purse in gratitude.

嵇鹤龄不肯收，王有龄则非送不可，
Ji refused, and Wang insisted.

“到后来简直要吵架了。”他说，
“In the end, we almost quarrelled,” Ji said.

“我想你跟他的交情不同，我跟你又是弟兄，
“Then I thought of your close friendship with him and our own brotherhood.

就看在这一层间接的渊源上，收了下来。”
On the strength of that indirect connection, I accepted.”

“你真是取与舍之间，一丝不苟。”胡雪岩点点头说，
Hu nodded. “You are scrupulous indeed about what to accept and what to decline.

“用他几个也不要紧。
There's no harm in using a little of his money.

这且不去说他，
But leave that aside.

你补缺的事呢？
What about a substantive appointment?

雪公说过，补实缺的事，包在他身上。
Mr Xuexuan said he would take responsibility for securing you a permanent post.

现在怎么样了？“
Where does that stand now?”


“这件事说起来，有点气人，”
“It's rather exasperating.”

嵇鹤龄急忙又加了一句：“不过，雪公对我是没有什么好说的，
Ji hastened to add, “Though I have nothing to complain of in Mr Xuexuan.

他保我署理归安县，黄抚台不肯，
He recommended me as acting magistrate of Gui'an, but Governor Huang refused.

又保我接海运局，他也不肯，说等‘保案’下来再说。”
Then he proposed that I take over the Sea Transport Bureau, and Huang refused that too, saying we must wait for the decision on the recommendations for reward.”

地方上一件大案子，或则兵剿，或则河工，或则如漕运由何运改为海运等等大事曲张的案子，办妥出奏，照例可以为出力人员请奖，
When a major local undertaking—suppressing disorder, repairing waterways, or changing grain transport from the canal to the sea—had been completed and reported to the throne, it was customary to recommend rewards for those who had contributed.

称为“保案”，保有“明保”、“密保”之分，
Such recommendations might be public or confidential.

自然是密保值钱。
Naturally, the confidential kind was more valuable.

“黄抚台给了我一个明保，反是雪公倒是密保……”
“Governor Huang has put me forward publicly, whereas Mr Xuexuan has received a confidential recommendation...”

“这太不公平了。”胡雪岩打断他的话说：
“That's most unfair,” Hu interrupted.

“莫非其中有鬼？”
“Could there be something underhand behind it?”

“嗨！”
“Ah!”

嵇鹤龄一拍大腿，
Ji slapped his thigh.

“真正机灵不过你！
“Nobody is sharper than you.

黄抚台手下一个文案委员，要我两千银子，
One of the governor's clerical commissioners asked me for two thousand taels.

我也不知道这两千银子是他自己，还是他替黄抚台要？
