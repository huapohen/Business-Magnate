胡雪岩
《平步青云（中）》第十五章（下，至本章结束）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume II — Chapter Fifteen, concluding part
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


One of the two matters had been settled.
要谈的两件事谈妥了一桩，

The other had to begin with Ji Heling.
另一桩得要从嵇鹤龄身上谈起，

“Mr Xuexuan,” Hu asked directly, “what has happened about Heling?”
“雪公！”他开门见山地问：“鹤龄的事怎么了？”

Wang lowered the glass he had almost raised to his lips and sighed dispiritedly.
一提到这话，王有龄把已送到唇边的酒杯又放下，意兴阑珊地先叹了口气。

“It has been keeping me awake at night.
“为这件事，我睡觉都不安枕。”王有龄说，

I was waiting to discuss it with you.
“我也正要等你商量。

I don’t know what the governor intends.
抚台不知打的什么主意？

It looks very much as though he means to destroy the bridge after crossing the river.
迹近过河拆桥，

How can I face Brother Heling?”
叫我怎么对得起鹤龄兄？”

He described his successive approaches to Huang Zonghan.
于是他把几次为嵇鹤龄的事，跟黄宗汉去谈的经过，说了一遍，

He had first made a request; when no definite answer came, he had submitted a recommendation.
先是请求，没有确实答复，便改做保荐，

That too had achieved nothing, so he had argued the case forcefully.
保荐依旧不得要领，就只好力争，

Still there had been no result.
无奈至今争不出名堂来。

“Xueyan, you judge these things more accurately than anyone.
“雪岩！”王有龄说到最后，又要请教他了，“你料事比别人来得准，

What do you make of it?”
倒看看，是何道理？”

“There is no death without a ghost at work,” Hu said bluntly.
“ ‘无鬼不死人’！”胡雪岩很坦率地说，

“There must be something underhand behind this.”
“其中必定有鬼。”

“I thought so too.
“我也想到了这一层。”

I asked the men handling the correspondence whether some little inducement was needed.
王有龄答道，“问过文案上的人，说要不要有所点缀？

They answered quite sincerely that everything depended on the governor.
文案上的人，回话很诚恳，说这件事全看抚台的意思，

They did not dare accept anything yet, for fear of taking a favor and then failing to accomplish the business.
他们此刻还不敢受好处，怕受了好处，事情办不成，对不起人。

Once Mr Ji’s appointment order was issued, of course, they would expect him to treat them to a celebratory drink.
等将来嵇某人的委札下来，自然少不得要讨他一杯喜酒吃。

Xueyan, surely that says all there is to say?”
雪岩，你听，这话不是说到头了吗？”


Comparing Wang’s account with Ji’s, Hu understood at once.
王、嵇两个人两样的活，摆到胡雪岩心里一辨味道，立刻就懂了。

The two thousand taels were wanted by Huang Zonghan, who was unwilling to have Wang supply them.
两千银子是黄宗汉要，却又不肯叫王有龄出，

That explained these answers.
所以才有这样的话，

If the money were merely for the correspondence staff, why would they care whether it belonged to Wang or Ji, so long as the silver was good?
如果是文案上要钱，管你这银子姓王姓嵇，只要成色足就行了！

Hu understood, but would not reveal it.
懂是懂了，却不肯说破。

If he did, Wang would still feel guilty that he had failed to do enough for Ji, even if he paid.
说破了，王有龄即或花了钱，仍旧会觉得替嵇鹤龄不曾尽到心而感疚歉，

And Ji, having declared that he would not buy an office, might bring the whole affair to an impasse.
在嵇鹤龄则既有那样不愿花钱买官做的表示，说破了更会成僵局。

Hu therefore smiled.
于是他笑笑说道：

“Let them play their ghostly tricks.
“他们闹鬼，

I am a Maoshan Taoist who specializes in catching such ghosts.
我就是专捉这路鬼的‘茅山道士’。

Just watch me!”
且看我的手段！

“And how do you intend to catch them?” Wang asked.
““那么，你预备如何‘捉鬼’？”王有龄问。

“Heaven’s secrets must not be disclosed.”
“天机不可泄漏。”

Hu pointed to Ji.
胡雪岩拿手一指嵇鹤龄，

“Mr Xuexuan, in his letter Heling mentioned that the tribute rice would go out to sea through Liuhe.
“雪公，鹤龄给我的信上，谈到漕米海运，由浏河出口，

Because the Small Swords uprising might cause trouble, he suggested a plan. See what you think of it.”
因为小刀会起事，怕出乱子，出了个主意你看行不行？”

Ji was astonished.
听得这话，嵇鹤龄大为诧异，

When had he suggested any plan?
自己何尝出过什么主意？

He was about to speak when someone gave him a light kick.
正要开口，发觉有人轻轻踢了他一脚，

Recognizing Hu’s signal, he remained silent.
这自然是胡雪岩递过来的暗号，嵇鹤龄便不作声了。


“What plan?” Wang asked attentively.
“什么主意？”王有龄极注意地问，

“Our superiors are worrying about it, and it is giving me a headache too.
“上头正为这件事在担心，我也很头痛，

Escorting tribute grain is a public duty, yet the troops make demands at every turn.
派兵护漕，原是公事，谁知百端需索，

The whole Green Standard Army ought to be abolished!”
绿营兵真正都该裁撤！”

“Then this suggestion will be useful,” Hu said calmly.
“那好！这个主意用得着了。”胡雪岩不慌不忙他说道，

“Heling knows my friendship with You Laowu and the extent of You’s influence.
“鹤龄晓得我跟尤五的交情，也晓得尤五的手面，

His idea is to contract the transshipment to You.
出的主意就是包给尤王驳运。

What do you think?”
你看如何？”

Wang considered, then struck the table in approval.
王有龄思索了一下，拍案称赏：

“A splendid idea!
“这个主意想绝了！

You belongs to the Songjiang grain-transport brotherhood, so the benefit would not go to outsiders.
尤五是松江漕帮，说起来便宜不落外方，

It can be justified on every side.
哪方面都交代得过。

Brother Heling, you truly have remarkable talent.
鹤龄兄，你真正才气纵横。

Please draw up a memorandum today, and I’ll present it to the governor in person tomorrow.
这样吧，请你今天就做个说帖，我明天上院面递。

If he still raises objections, there really must be a ghost at work!”
如果抚台再有罗嗦，那就真正是出了鬼了！”

“Certainly, certainly,” Ji replied.
“是，是！”嵇鹤龄答应是在答应，

His face and ears grew hot, though the wine concealed his embarrassment.
不免有些面红耳热，只是借酒盖脸，一时看不出来。

“Excellent!”
“甚好，甚好！”

Wang raised his glass.
王有龄举杯说道，

“I have drunk brandy several times, but none seemed as fragrant or mellow as this.”
“白兰地我也喝过几回，似乎都不如今天的来得香，来得醇。”

“‘Friendship with Zhou Gongjin is like drinking mellow wine,’” Ji quoted from the Records of the Three Kingdoms.
“ ‘与周公谨交，如饮醇醪’！”嵇鹤龄引了句《三国志》上的话，

He raised his glass toward Wang, but his eyes rested on Hu.
端杯向王有龄一举，眼却看着胡雪岩。

After they drank, Wang said, “That is enough wine.
干了这一杯，王有龄说：“酒差不多了。

Brother Heling still has his memorandum to write tonight.
鹤龄兄今晚上还要写说帖，

Come to my house tomorrow evening, and we’ll enjoy a proper drink.”
明天晚上到我那里再喝个痛快！”


As he finished, Ruiyun lifted the curtain and came out smiling.
话刚完，只听瑞云一面掀帘子走了出来，一面笑道：

“But I haven’t yet offered a toast to Master Hu or to you, sir.”
“我还没有敬胡老爷、敬老爷呢？”

“You certainly ought to toast Master Hu—to thank your matchmaker!”
“敬胡老爷应该，谢媒！”

That had indeed been her intention, but Wang’s saying it aloud made things difficult.
瑞云原有这意思，让王有龄一说破，便不好办了，

With the wine pot in one hand and a cup in the other, she stood awkwardly at a loss.
一手执壶、一手持杯，僵在那里有些手足无措，

It lasted only a moment before Ji came cleverly to her rescue.
幸好，这不过眨眨眼的工夫，因为嵇鹤龄很机警地替她解了围。

“Mr Xuexuan should be toasted first,” he said, taking the pot.
“还是应该先敬雪公！”他接过壶来说，

“Xueyan and I are brothers.
“雪岩跟我弟兄，

He is one of the family.”
那是自己人。”

“How unfortunate!” Wang laughed.
“糟了！”王有龄笑道，

“You are all family, and I’m the only outsider.”
“你们都是自己人，只剩下一个我是外人。”

“You mustn’t say that, sir.”
“老爷也不要这么说，”

Ruiyun recovered her composure and her skill in social conversation.
瑞云窘意消失。依然很会应酬了，

“Neither Master Hu nor Master Ji regards you as an outsider.”
“胡老爷跟格老爷都没有拿老爷当外人看。”

“Exactly!”
“对了！”

A little wine had sharpened Wang’s wit.
有了几分酒意的王有龄，词锋特别锐利，

“A woman’s heart turns toward her husband.
“女心外向，

It is you who regard me as an outsider.”
倒是你拿我当外人看了。”

“I would never dare.”
“我不敢！”

Though he was joking, she answered seriously.
虽是戏言，瑞云却当作正经话回答，

“I spent more than ten years in your household.
“我在老爷家十几年，

I could never forget your kindness and your wife’s.”
不敢忘记者爷、太太待我的好处。”

Even with wine as an excuse, Wang could hardly continue teasing after that.
说到这样的话，王有龄就是借酒盖脸，也不好意思跟她再说笑话，

He accepted her toast with proper courtesy.
规规矩矩受了她一杯酒。

She then toasted Hu and put down the cup to leave, but Hu stopped her and insisted she toast Ji as well.
接着，瑞云又敬了胡雪岩，放下杯子要走，他喊住了她，要她也敬嵇鹤龄。

Now she could not remain at ease.
这时候的瑞云可大方不起来了！

The more she demurred, the more Hu teased, until several children gathered behind the curtain to peep at the fun.
但越是不肯，胡雪岩越闹得厉害，把几个小把戏都招引了来，在门帘后面遮遮掩掩地看热闹，

Danhe, the most mischievous, giggled incessantly.
特别是最调皮的丹荷，格格地笑个不住。

By turning to scold his children, Ji gave Ruiyun another opportunity to escape.
嵇鹤龄借着去叱斥儿女的机会，算是替瑞云又解了围。


After dinner they returned to the study for tea and resumed their business.
饭罢回到书房里去喝茶，又谈正经。

“Have you discussed the transshipment with You?” Wang asked Hu.
王有龄问起胡雪岩说：“驳运一节，你跟尤五谈过没有？”

“We have discussed it, but nothing is settled.
“谈是谈过，没有定局。

I did not know your wishes.”
因为不知道你的意思究竟如何？”

“You might just as well have decided yourself.
“其实你就作了主也一样。”王有龄问：

What does You say?”
“尤五怎么说？”

“One word is enough with him.
“尤五还不是一句话！

The cost can be negotiated; he only needs an official communication from Zhejiang to the Jiangsu grain transport circuit intendant.”
费用好商量，不过要浙江给他们江苏督粮道一件公事。”

“The document is easily arranged.
“公事现成！

Even a letter to Governor Xu of Jiangsu would be no trouble.
哪怕就是给汪苏许抚台，也不费什么事。

But we need an approximate figure for the expense before we can plan.”
倒是费用一层，还要有个大概数目，才好筹划。”

“I imagine it will be much cheaper than having Green Standard troops provide an escort.”
“我想，”胡雪岩说，“总比请派绿营兵保护，要便宜得多。”

“Then that will do.”
“ 那行！”

Wang considered carefully.
王有龄很仔细的想了想道说：

“If You can truly guarantee its safety, this is an excellent arrangement.”
“只要尤五真的能够保险，这件事就太妙了！”

Hu heard the uncertainty about You.
胡雪岩听出他的意思，是有些不放心尤五，

There were many things he could not conveniently explain, such as You’s friendship with Zhou Lichun.
但许多话亦不便跟他说，譬如尤五跟周立春的交情之类。

Yet Wang’s concern, and the fact that the proposal was now being attributed to Ji, made reconsideration necessary. If something went wrong, it would jeopardize both men’s careers.
不过既然王有龄有这话，而且又扯上嵇鹤龄，算是他的“条陈”，那么一出纸漏，于他们两个人的前程，都有妨碍，不能不重新考虑。

“I am reasonably confident, but we must guard against the one chance in ten thousand,” Hu said, looking at Ji.
“事情是有七分把握，不过‘不怕一万，只怕万一’，我想，”胡雪岩看着嵇鹤龄说，

“Perhaps the memorial should leave some room for maneuver and let Governor Huang decide.”
“条陈里写活动些，让黄抚台去作主。”

“No, no!”
“不行，不行！”

Wang waved his hand.
王有龄摇着手说，

“He will not take the responsibility.”
“他不肯担责任的。”


The whole matter seemed to require fresh discussion.
这一下，事情变得就要重新再谈，

Hu felt the responsibility was too heavy for any plan to be entirely secure.
胡雪岩因为责任太重、总觉得很难有万全之计，

While he was pondering, Ji spoke.
方在沉思之际，嵇鹤龄开了口。

“We must first circle the horse and bend the bow—prepare the ground before acting.
“此事要盘马弯弓，有一番做作。”嵇鹤龄说：

Defense is pressing everywhere, and every locality needs more troops.
“现在防务吃紧，各地方都要增添兵力，

The existing forces are already insufficient.
原有的兵勇尚不敷用，

How could men be spared to escort the tribute rice?”
何能再抽人护送漕米？”

“Ah!” Wang exclaimed.
“啊，啊！”王有龄恍然大悟，

“I understand.”
“我懂了。”

“So do I,” Hu said.
“我也懂了。”胡雪岩说，

“But it would be better if those words did not come from you, Mr Xuexuan.”
“不过这话，最好不由雪公来说。”

“You mean the Green Standard officers should say it themselves?”
“你是说由绿营自己来说？”

Wang shook his head.
王有龄摇摇头，

“They won’t.
“他们不肯说的，

It is a comfortable and profitable assignment.
这是趟好差使，又舒服，又有出息，

Why would they refuse?”
何乐不为？”

“Not necessarily comfortable.
“舒服却未见得，

If they really encounter the Small Swords forces and have to fight, they will be no match for them.”
真的遇见小刀会，开起仗来，绿营不是他们的敌手。”

“Unfortunately, they do not think so, and I cannot say it.
“无奈他们不这么想。我也不能这么说。”

I’ll see the governor myself and act as circumstances require.”
王有龄下了个决定：“准定由我面见抚台，相机行事。”

“What about the memorandum?”
“那么，”胡雪岩问道，“条陈呢？”

“Have it drafted tonight all the same.
“条陈还是今夜把它拟好，

I’ll take it with me.
我带了去，

Better to have it ready and not need it than need it and not have it.”
宁可备而不用，不可要用而未备。”

“Then I’ll finish it tonight,” Ji said.
“既如此，我连夜赶起来。”

After a pause, he added, “I should like Xueyan to stay so that we can discuss it and make it as sound as possible.
嵇鹤龄慢了一下说，“我想把雪岩留下来，一起商量，斟酌尽善。

What do you think?”
雪公看如何？”

“Very well.”
“也好！”

Wang looked at Hu.
王有龄看着胡雪岩说：

“We shall meet tomorrow morning, then.”
“我们就明天上午碰头好了。”


With that agreed, Wang took his leave.
这样说停当了，王有龄告辞回家。

Hu and Ji began immediately.
胡雪岩和嵇鹤龄也就毫无耽搁，立即动手，

One arranged the arguments clearly; the other wrote fluently.
一个条理清楚，一个笔下来得，

They soon produced a draft, reconsidered it, settled the final wording and made a fair copy for Hu to take away.
不费什么事就已把草稿拟好，重新斟酌一遍，作成定稿，随手誊清，由胡雪岩带走。

The next morning Wang stayed home especially to await Hu.
第二天上午王有龄不出门，专诚在家等候胡雪岩。

As soon as he arrived, they shut themselves in the study.
一到便在书房里闭门密谈，

Since the Xincheng disturbances had been put down, Wang had gained still more of Huang’s confidence.
自从新城之乱平服，王有龄愈得黄宗汉的信任，

That also meant more jealous rivals and more obstruction.
因而妒忌他的人也不少，办事不免多掣时的人，

He had many troubles to pour out to Hu.
为此他有许多苦恼，要向胡雪岩倾吐。

“Xueyan, there is an important matter I want to discuss.
“雪岩，”他说，“我现在有件大事，要跟你商量。

I hear that Governor Huang may be transferred.
听说黄抚台有调动的消息，

If he leaves, who knows what his successor will be like?
如果他一走，来接他的人不知怎么样。

I am strongly tempted to withdraw while things are going well.”
所以我颇有急流勇退之想。”

Hu was greatly alarmed.
一听这话，胡雪岩大吃一惊，急急说道：

“Mr Xuexuan, why should you think of that?
“雪公你怎么起了这么个念头？

We have only just built things up, and everything is running smoothly.
局面刚刚摆开，正搞得顺手，

Why retreat now?”
为啥要打退堂鼓。

“First, if the new man and I do not get on, winding up the affairs I have handled could become troublesome.
““一则我怕后任一来，如果彼此不甚对劲，我许多经手的事，收拾起来就会有罗嗦，

It would be easier to hand them over while Governor Huang is here.
趁黄抚台在这里，办交卸比较容易，

Second, Jiang Zhongyuan has been promoted from judicial commissioner of Hubei to governor of Anhui.
二则江忠源由湖北臬司调升安徽巡抚，

We are old acquaintances, and he has written to ask whether I would join him there.
他跟我有旧，来信问我，愿意不愿意到安徽去？

He and Zeng Guofan are both enjoying great imperial favor.
他跟曾国藩两个，现在圣眷甚隆，

Going to him might be a good move.”
我想到他那里去也不错。”

“I disagree!”
“不然！”

Hu shook his head vigorously.
胡雪岩大为摇头，

“You do not know Anhui, and neither do I.
“安徽地方你不熟悉，我也不熟悉。

To speak plainly, if you go there, I shall not go with you, because I could do nothing to help you there.”
而且说句老实话，你到安徽，我不会去的，因为我去了也帮不了你的忙！”

“Very well.”
“好！”

Wang nodded.
王有龄点点头，

“Since you put it that way, there is no more to say.
“你说到这话，我不必再多说，

I will write today to decline Jiang’s kind invitation.”
今天就写信，回谢江忠源的好意。”


Hu was naturally reassured, but felt his responsibility grow heavier.
听他这样表示，胡雪岩自然感到安慰了，然而也不免觉得责任愈重，

After reflecting, he asked, “Is the report of Governor Huang’s transfer reliable?”
想了想说：“黄抚台调动的消息，确不确？”

“There is such a rumor, and we must be prepared.
“有此一说，不可不防。”

Besides, disturbances by bandits are occurring throughout Zhejiang.
王有龄又说，“现在浙江各地，都有土匪滋事的情形，

A spark can set the prairie ablaze.
星星之火，可以燎原，

The governor takes the matter very seriously.
黄抚台对这方面非常认真。

Because the Xincheng affair was handled well, I fear all these assignments will fall to me in future.
因为新城的案子办得不错，所以这些差使，以后怕都会落在我头上。

Meanwhile I cannot get free of the Sea Transport Bureau.
海运局的事又不能不拖在那里，

I really am beginning to lack the strength for everything.”
实在有点心余力绌。”

This made Ji’s appointment all the more important.
这就见得嵇鹤龄的事，格外重要。

In truth, Wang was more anxious about it than Ji himself, but he could not exert his full influence with Huang.
说实话，王有龄比嵇鹤龄本人还急，但他在黄宗汉面前，却是有力使不上，

In allocating the rewards for Xincheng, Wang had made a wrong move—or rather, failed to make a move at all.
因为论功行赏，王有龄走错了一着棋，或者说，这一着棋，他没有去走，

Huang had decided from the outset that Wang and Ji would receive one confidential recommendation and one open recommendation between them.
在黄宗汉，对新城一案的酬佣，是早就分配好了的，王有龄和嵇鹤龄两人，给一个密保，一个明保，

He had no preference as to which man received which.
谁密谁明，他没有意见。

Had Wang said when the report went forward, “Ji Heling did more of the work; please favor him with the confidential recommendation,” Huang would have agreed.
