第十六章｜CHAPTER SIXTEEN


当天回家，胡雪岩叫阿福把住在附近客栈里的陈世龙去找了来，
When Hu returned home that day, he sent A’fu to fetch Chen Shilong from the nearby inn.

他是要告诉他一个好消息，到松江接枪，已经用不着他了。
He had good news for him: Chen was no longer needed to collect the guns at Songjiang.

眼前在杭州也没有什么事，可以先回湖州一趟，去见一见“丈母娘”。
There was nothing for him to do in Hangzhou just now either, so he could go back to Huzhou first and visit his “mother-in-law.”

“不必！”陈世龙说，
“There’s no need,” Chen said.

“接枪的事情，也很麻烦，
“Collecting the guns will be troublesome too.

我跟了裘老爷去好了。”
I had better go with Mr Qiu.”

“为什么呢？”
“Why?”

胡雪岩倒有些诧异，
Hu was surprised.

心想这是求之不得的“美差”，
Surely this was a delightful errand anyone would welcome.

陈世龙不该不领情。
Why should Chen refuse the kindness?

他何尝不领情，
Chen was far from ungrateful.

心里也巴不得去看一看小别数日，便如数年的阿珠，
He longed to see A’zhu; a few days apart had seemed like years.

只是为了感恩图报，自愿出力。
But he wanted to repay Hu by doing whatever work he could.

而这话他又不愿说，觉得说了便没意思了，因而沉默不答。
To explain that would take the meaning out of it, so he remained silent.

胡雪岩是察言观色，只需稍力用点心，便可以看透他的腑肺，
Hu needed only a little attention to read his expression and see into his heart.

心里暗暗欣慰，也不说破，只这样告诉他：
Secretly pleased, he did not expose the reason.

“叫你去看丈母娘是‘顺带公文一角’，
“Your visit to your mother-in-law is merely a dispatch carried on the way.

湖州我一时去不了，有好些事，要你替我去办。
I cannot go to Huzhou just yet, and there are several things you must do for me.

你不必到松江去了！”
You are not going to Songjiang.”

最后一句话，完全是长辈的口气，
The final sentence had all the authority of an elder’s command.

没有讨价还价的余地，
There was no room for bargaining.

陈世龙只好点点头。
Chen could only nod.


“第一件，你跟你郁四叔去说，如果有多余的头寸，我要用，
“First, tell your Uncle Four that, if he has spare funds, I need them.

请他汇到阜康来，期限最好长一点，
Ask him to remit them to Fukang, preferably for a fairly long term.

利息我特别加厚。
I will pay him an especially generous rate of interest.

第二件……”
Second...”

说到第二件，他沉吟了，
Hu paused.

意思是想把黄仪调开，但丝行才开始做，
He wanted to move Huang Yi elsewhere, but the silk firm had only just begun operations.

总得把这一“季”做出个起落来，净赚多少，该分多少花红，
They ought at least to finish this season and settle how much net profit had been made and what bonus Huang should receive.

有个实实惠惠的交代，则宾主尽欢而散，才是正办。
A tangible settlement that let employer and employee part happily was the proper course.

照目前这样子，仿佛有些过河拆桥，传出去于自己的名声有损。
To move him now would look like destroying the bridge after crossing it, and word of that would damage Hu’s reputation.

“世龙，”他问：“你看黄仪这个人怎么样？”
“Shilong, what do you think of Huang Yi?”

“本事是有的，不大合得来群。”陈世龙直抒观感。
“He has ability, but doesn’t get on well with others,” Chen replied frankly.

“对！
“Exactly.

你说到了他的短处。”
You’ve put your finger on his weakness.

胡雪岩说，“你丈人自己说过，‘吃不住他’，
Your father-in-law himself says he can’t manage him.

我要想个办法，把他调开，不过目前还不到时候，
I must find a way to move him elsewhere, but the time isn’t right yet.

你跟你丈人说，好歹先敷衍敷衍他，
Tell your father-in-law to get along with him as best he can for now.

到明年我自有妥当办法。”
I’ll have a suitable arrangement next year.”

“我晓得了。”
“I understand.

陈世龙又说，“郁四叔那里，最好请胡先生写封信。”
It would be best if you wrote Uncle Four a letter too.”

“信我是要写的，还有东西带去。
“I shall write, and there are things for you to take.

啊！”胡雪岩突然喊了起来，
Ah!” Hu suddenly exclaimed.

“我倒想起来了，
“That reminds me.

老黄文墨很不错，
Old Huang writes very well.

我想请他来帮忙，专门替我写写信，
I’d like him to help me by attending solely to my correspondence.

你倒探探他的口气看！
Sound him out.

送他的酬劳，一定够他用，
I’ll pay enough to meet his needs.

你看他的意思如何？写信来告诉我。”
Find out what he thinks and write to tell me.”


“这倒也不错。
“That’s a good idea too.

老黄这个人也只有胡先生能收服，
You’re the only person who can bring Old Huang round.

他做事最好自己做自己的，不跟人联手，
He works best on his own, without having to cooperate with anyone.

一定做得好。”
He would certainly do it well.”

这样商量定了，陈世龙便整整忙了两天，把胡雪岩要带到湖州送人的土仪什物，以及他自己“孝敬”丈人丈母娘的衣料与食物，向阿珠献殷勤的胭脂花粉，一起采办齐全，
With this agreed, Chen spent two full days buying the local delicacies and other gifts Hu wanted taken to Huzhou, together with cloth and food for his own offerings to his future parents-in-law, and rouge and scented face powder to please A’zhu.

再下一天就下了航船，直放湖州。
The following day he boarded a passenger boat and went straight to Huzhou.

一上岸先到大经丝行，
His first call ashore was at Dajing Silk Firm.

迎面就遇见阿珠的娘，心里没有预备，顿时搞得手足无措。
He met A’zhu’s mother coming towards him and, wholly unprepared, became awkward and flustered.

首先称呼就为难，
Even a greeting was difficult.

自然不能再叫“张太太”，但又老不出面皮喊声，“娘！”
He could hardly call her Mrs Zhang any longer, yet could not bring himself to say “Mother.”

阿珠的娘，却是又惊又喜，
She, however, was surprised and delighted.

“你怎么回来了？”
“What brings you back?

她说，“来，先坐了再说，
Come and sit down first.

你丈人也在里头。”
Your father-in-law is inside too.”

说着，她自己先转身走了进去。
She turned and went ahead.

陈世龙定定神，
Chen collected himself.

心里在想，看这样子，丈母娘对自己是中意的，
Evidently his future mother-in-law approved of him.

他唯一的顾虑，是怕阿珠的娘，觉得受胡雪岩的好处太多，不一定以这头亲事为然，或者口中不说，心里起了个疙瘩。
His one fear had been that, having received so much from Hu, she might dislike this marriage or silently harbour a grievance about it.

现在，这个疑虑似乎是多余的了。
Now that worry seemed unnecessary.


由店堂绕过屏风，走人第二进就是客帚，
Beyond the shopfront and its screen, the second courtyard led into the reception hall.

这时不是收丝的季节，空荡荡地一个客人都没有，
It was not the season for buying silk, and there was not a customer in the empty hall.

但旁边厢房却有人，是黄仪，
Huang Yi was in a side room, however.

在窗子里望见了便喊：“啊呀，新贵人上门了！”
Catching sight of Chen through the window, he called, “Why, the honoured new son-in-law has arrived!”

一路喊，一路抢了出来，笑脸迎人。
Still calling, he hurried out with a welcoming smile.

陈世龙有些发窘，站定了脚招呼一声：
Chen stopped, somewhat embarrassed.

“黄先生，你好！”
“How are you, Mr Huang?”

“你发福了！”
“You’ve filled out!”

黄仪歪着头，从上到下把陈世龙端详了一遍，
Tilting his head, Huang looked him over from head to foot.

“上海住了几个月，样子变过了！”
“A few months in Shanghai have changed your appearance.”

这一说引起了阿珠的娘的注意，
This drew the mother’s attention.

也是退后两步，直盯着陈世龙看。
She too stepped back and stared.

夷场上的衣饰总要漂亮些，又是“丈母娘看女婿”，所以她脸上的笑意越堆越浓，
Clothes from the foreign settlement were always finer; and with a mother-in-law looking at her prospective son-in-law, her smile naturally grew broader and broader.

这样就更要惹得黄仪开玩笑。
Huang could not resist more teasing.

“张太太，”他笑着说，“回去慢慢看！
“Mrs Zhang, you can study him at leisure when you get home.

新贵人脸嫩，
Our new son-in-law is shy.

看得他不好意思了。”
You’re embarrassing him.”

“晓得他脸嫩，你就少说一两句！”阿珠的娘已经在卫护女婿，这样笑着说，
Already taking his side, she laughed. “If you know he’s shy, say a little less!

“都到里头来坐！”
Come inside, both of you.”

“对！”黄仪兴味盎然地，
“Yes!” Huang said enthusiastically.

“我到里头来看你们‘见礼’。”
“I’ll come and watch you exchange the formal greetings.”

阿珠的娘心里一动，立刻有了个主意，
This gave her an idea.

她是体恤女婿，看陈世龙有点发窘，心里便想，“毛脚女婿”第一次上门，总要有个媒人，或者男女两家都熟悉的亲友陪着，彼此才不致尴尬。
A young man’s first visit to his future wife’s family ought to be made with a matchmaker or someone who knew both families, to spare everyone embarrassment.

现在陈世龙象个“没脚蟹”似地，
Chen had come like a crab without legs.

要请黄仪来帮忙，
Why not enlist Huang?

媒人照规矩是两位，
Custom required two matchmakers.

有了一个胡雪岩，另一个不是现成在眼前？
Hu was one; wasn’t the other standing ready before her?


于是她说：“黄先生，我们女家的大媒是胡先生，
“Mr Huang, Mr Hu is the matchmaker on our daughter’s side.

男家的大媒老爷，拜托了你好不好？”
Would you be so kind as to act for the bridegroom?”

“怎么不好？
“Gladly!

现成的媒人，求之不得。”
A ready-made match to arrange—what could be better?”

陈世龙也听出丈母娘意存体恤，
Chen understood her consideration.

这样安排，再好不过，
Nothing could suit him better.

便向黄仪拱手作揖：
He clasped his hands and bowed to Huang.

“黄先生，我重重拜托！”
“I entrust it to you, Mr Huang.”

“好说，好说！”
“Of course, of course!”

黄仪很高兴地，
Huang was delighted.

“那么，张太太，我要叫你亲家太太了！”
“Then, Mrs Zhang, I must greet you as our new relation by marriage.”

就这样说笑着，一起进了胡雪岩以前所住的那个院子，
Talking and laughing, they entered the courtyard Hu had formerly occupied.

老张闻声迎了出来，也有意外的惊喜，
Old Zhang came out at the sound, equally surprised and pleased.

陈世龙喊一声：“爹！”
“Father!” Chen called.

有了爹自然有娘，
With a father, there was naturally a mother too.

黄仪以媒人的身份，从中牵引，陈世龙便又替老张夫妇磕了头，正式见过礼，改了口，
Guided by Huang in his new capacity as matchmaker, Chen kowtowed to the couple, formally paid his respects and adopted his new forms of address.

把阿珠的娘笑得合不扰口。
A’zhu’s mother could scarcely stop smiling.

这时大经丝行里用的伙计，出店、烧饭司务，还有两三个缫丝的女工，都跑了来看热闹，
The firm’s clerks, outside errand man, cook and two or three women who reeled silk all came to watch.

因为陈世龙平常人缘极好，所以都替他高兴，但也多要开几句玩笑。
Chen had always been well liked, so everyone was happy for him, though most had a joke to make.

陈世龙觉得最艰难的是见丈母娘这一关，
He had considered meeting his mother-in-law the hardest ordeal.

这一关一过就不在乎，
Once through it, he no longer minded.

脸皮也厚了，随他们去说，只报以矜持的微笑。
His skin had grown thicker; he let them say what they pleased and answered only with a restrained smile.


然而另一个难关又来了，这一关不是他自己难过，是替阿珠担心，
Another ordeal now arrived, though it was A’zhu, rather than himself, for whom Chen was anxious.

说巧不巧，阿珠从家里到丝行，
By an awkward coincidence she had just come from home to the firm.

一路走进来，就看见大家想笑不笑，已在怀疑，
The half-suppressed smiles she encountered on her way in had already made her suspicious.

等踏入院子，第一眼就看见陈世龙，
As soon as she entered the courtyard, she saw Chen.

心里一慌，赶紧想溜，已来不及。
In sudden confusion she tried to slip away, but it was too late.

“阿珠！”老张在里头喊。
“A’zhu!” her father called from inside.

阿珠不理，依旧往外走，
Ignoring him, she kept going out.

有个缫丝的女工叫阿翠，生性最好事，偏偏就在她身后，堵着门不让她出去。
A silk-reeling woman named A’cui, who loved to meddle, happened to be behind her and blocked the doorway.

“走开！”她低声怒喝。
“Get out of the way!” A’zhu whispered angrily.

“你不要逃嘛！”阿翠笑道，
“Don’t run away!” A’cui laughed.

“又不是不认识。”
“It isn’t as though you don’t know him.”

于是里面也笑，外面也笑，
Laughter broke out inside and outside.

终于让阿珠夺门逃走，陈世龙才算松了一口气。
Only when A’zhu finally escaped through the door did Chen breathe freely.

阿珠的娘记挂着女儿，同时为女婿设想，料知他一颗心也早就飞了出去，
Her mother was concerned for her daughter and considerate of Chen, whose heart, she guessed, had already flown after her.

因而看一看天色，提议回家，顺便邀黄仪一起去吃晚饭。
Looking at the hour, she proposed returning home and invited Huang to supper.

黄仪大喜。
He was delighted.

他不喜欢赌钱，也不会花花草草在外头搞女人，甚至连旱烟都不抽，
He disliked gambling, did not chase women, and did not even smoke a dry pipe.

唯一的嗜好，是口腹之欲，这位“老板娘”的烹调手段，他是领教过的，
Food was his one indulgence, and he knew his employer’s wife could cook.

只是在老张父女到上海去的那些日子，只有阿珠的娘带着个使女爱珍在家，他不便上门去叨扰。
While Zhang and A’zhu were in Shanghai, she had been alone at home with her maid Aizhen, and he could hardly impose on them.

从老张回来以后，才又去吃过两次饭，家常肴馔、精洁有余，丰腆不足，未能大嚼，
Since Zhang’s return he had dined there twice, but the everyday dishes, though excellent and beautifully prepared, had not been plentiful enough for him to eat his fill.

今天又是款待“毛脚女婿”，又是请媒人，
Today they were entertaining both a prospective son-in-law on his first visit and a matchmaker.

自然有一顿称心满意的晚饭好吃了。
A satisfying dinner was assured.


“你先去！”老张对他妻子说，
“Go ahead,” Old Zhang told his wife.

“胡先生带来送人的东西，我跟世龙先料理料理，
“Shilong and I will sort out the presents Mr Hu has sent.

弄好了就回来。”
We’ll come when we’ve finished.”

“今天也晚了，
“It’s late already.

留到明天再说。”
Leave them until tomorrow.

阿珠的娘这样嘱咐：“世龙就住在店里好了，要茶要水也方便。
Shilong had better stay at the shop, where tea and water will be handy.

要住哪一问自己挑，挑好了叫他们打扫，铺盖到家里去拿。”
Let him choose whichever room he likes, have them clean it and fetch bedding from home.”

这番体贴，完全是父母之心，
Her care was entirely a parent’s.

陈世龙极其感动，但也很不安，
Chen was deeply touched, but uneasy too.

就此刻他已觉得岳家的恩情太重，不知何以报答？
Already he felt his debt to his future wife’s family was too great to repay.

加上胡雪岩的一手提拔，越有恐惧不胜之情，
Added to Hu’s efforts to advance him, it almost frightened him.

于是不由得又想到阿珠的那番激励：“ ‘好女不穿嫁时衣’，
Inevitably he remembered A’zhu’s encouragement: “A good wife doesn’t depend on the clothes she brought at marriage.

这些首饰，可惜不是你买给我的！”
It’s a pity you didn’t buy these jewels for me yourself!”

同时也记了胡雪岩对阿珠说过的那句话：“等世龙将来发达了，给你买金刚钻。”
He also remembered Hu telling her, “When Shilong prospers, he’ll buy you diamonds.”

两下凑在一起，陈世龙死心塌地了！
The two remarks together fixed his determination.

“爹！”等阿珠的娘一走，陈世龙这样对老张说：“你先陪了黄先生回去。
Once her mother had gone, he told Old Zhang, “Father, take Mr Huang home first.

我把胡先生交代的事，办完了就来。
I’ll come when I’ve finished Mr Hu’s business.

今天我仍旧回家去住，省得麻烦。”
I’ll sleep at my own place tonight to save trouble.”

“何必？”黄仪劝他：
“Why?” Huang urged.

“明天一早来料理也一样。”
“You can see to it early tomorrow.”

“不！”陈世龙固执地：
“No,” Chen insisted.

“今日事，今日毕，
“Today’s work must be finished today.

明天有明天的事，
Tomorrow has its own business.

积在一起，拖到后天，那就永远料理不清楚了！”
Let them pile up and carry over to the day after, and they’ll never be cleared.”

听这一说，已入中年的黄仪不断点头，
Huang, already middle-aged, nodded repeatedly.

“老张！”他说，“你这个女婿，人又变过了，
“Old Zhang, your son-in-law has changed again.

不但聪明勤快，而且老成扎实！
Clever and diligent, and now mature and dependable too!

真正是乘尤快婿，
An excellent match for your daughter.

恭喜，恭喜！”
Congratulations!”

老张是忠厚老实到了家的，自然更欣赏陈世龙的作风。
Thoroughly honest and conscientious himself, Zhang valued this attitude still more.

要这样，后半世才有依靠！
Here was someone he could rely on in later life.

照他的想法，当时就想下手帮忙，但既邀了黄仪回家吃饭，也不便让他空等。
He wanted to help at once, but having invited Huang to dinner, could hardly keep him waiting.

就这踌躇之间，有了个主意，正不妨趁此机会跟黄仪先谈一谈如何办喜事。
While hesitating, he saw an opportunity: they could discuss the wedding first.


陪他到家，刚一进门，里面阿珠便躲了开去，爱珍来开了门，
As they entered the house, A’zhu withdrew from sight and Aizhen came to open the door.

第一个先寻陈世龙，
Her eyes searched first for Chen.

看看不见，便失望地问了出来：“咦！姑少爷呢？”
Not seeing him, she asked in disappointment, “Where is the young son-in-law?”

骤然改口，老张倒是一愣，想一想才明白，随即答道：
The sudden change of title startled Zhang before he understood.

“在收拾东西，
“Sorting things out.

要等下才来。”
He’ll come later.”

听这一说，爱珍便急忙到厨房里去报告消息。
Aizhen hurried to the kitchen with the news.

阿珠跟她一样失望，但似乎又觉得轻松。
A’zhu was just as disappointed, though also somewhat relieved.

不过，还有个黄仪，这时一走出去，必定受窘，
Huang was still there, however; if she went out now, he would certainly embarrass her.

因而又有些上心事。
This gave her another worry.

她娘看不出她的心事，正忙得不可开交，
Her mother was too busy to notice.

要在个把钟头以内，弄出一桌象样的菜来，着实要费一番手脚。
Preparing a respectable dinner within an hour or so required considerable effort.

而且不但手脚忙，口中也不闭，一面调理咸酸，一面不厌其详地讲解，让阿珠都听得有些烦了。
Her tongue was as busy as her hands: while adjusting the seasoning, she explained every detail until A’zhu grew impatient.

“娘！”她说，“这时候哪里有工夫讲空话？”
“Mother, how have you time for idle talk now?”

“你当是空话？”
“You call it idle talk?”

做母亲的大为不悦。
Her mother was displeased.

“马上要自己做人家了，
“You’ll soon have a household of your own.

我教得你一样是一样，你还不肯学！”
Every little thing I can teach you will help, yet you won’t learn!

阿珠的娘埋怨女儿，“虽然上头没有婆婆，旁人要说闲话。
You may have no mother-in-law above you, but other people will gossip.

Little Monk, tell me—could any woman be more vicious?”


话说到这里，全盘情况，皆已了解，
The whole picture was now clear.

郁四听了女儿的话，决定跟阿七散伙。
Yu Si had listened to his daughter and decided to part with A’qi.

既说“好来好散”自然有一笔钱可拿，照郁四的手面，这笔钱还不会少，没有五千，也有三千。
Since they had parted on good terms, she must have received a settlement, and, given his generous habits, it could hardly have been small: if not five thousand taels, then at least three thousand.

只不知道阿七自郁家下堂以后，是不是重张艳帜？
Chen wondered whether, after leaving Yu, she intended to return to her former profession.

Curious as he was, he refrained from asking.

因为他要表示出事不干已，不闻不问的态度，好让阿七自己识趣，知难而退。
He wanted to appear wholly unconcerned, neither asking nor listening, so that she would take the hint and withdraw.

阿七却决不会如他的愿，
But A’qi had no intention of obliging him.

“现在谈到正事上头来了。”她说：
“Now we come to the real business,” she said.

“小和尚，我随郁老头唱了半出‘乌龙院’，
“Little Monk, Old Yu and I have acted out half of The Black Dragon Courtyard.

他走他的清秋大路，我也没有什么麻烦好找他的。

We parted quite civilly.

He bought the house, but he had already transferred it to my name, so he was the one who had to move out.

He also gave me a passbook for five thousand taels at his bank.

I can draw the interest, but not the principal.

这以后再说了，
We’ll see about that later.

It’s money in my name, and naturally I mean to withdraw it.

他识相的，拉倒，
If he sees sense, well and good.

不识相我要打官司，
If not, I’ll sue.

好在王大老爷跟胡老板是好朋友……
Luckily, Prefect Wang and Mr Hu are close friends...”

““慢慢！”
“Wait!”

陈世龙当头泼她的冷水：
Chen threw cold water over this at once.

“你不要做梦！
“Don’t dream of it!

人家胡老板跟郁四叔等于弟兄一样，
Mr Hu and Uncle Four are practically brothers.

打到官司，一定帮他不帮你！”
If you sue, he’ll help him, not you.”

“那就不要他帮！”阿七答得极爽利，
“Then I don’t need his help!” she answered readily.

“我自己到堂上去告，说他那爿钱庄要‘倒灶’了，我不相信他，
“I’ll go into court myself and say his bank is about to fail and I don’t trust him.

可以不可以？”
Can I withdraw it then or can’t I?”

陈世龙为她那种自说自话的神态逗得笑了，
Her confidence that everything would happen just as she wished made Chen laugh.

“都随你！”
“Have it your own way!

他说，“你跟阿兰姐一样，都算是厉害角色！”
You and Sister A’lan are both formidable women.”


“我啥厉害？
“What’s formidable about me?

做人全靠心好！
All that matters is having a good heart.

象阿兰姐，哼，
As for A’lan—hmph!

也是到现在没有儿子，
She has no son herself yet.

将来有苦头吃。
She’ll have trouble enough in years to come.

这都不去说它了。”
But never mind all that.”

话到此处，阿七的神情变得郑重而兴奋，
A’qi grew earnest and excited.

都叫我回绝掉了，
I turned them all away.

不识相的，我就爽爽快快的把他骂了出去。

I hardly ever go out.

出门就是去打听你的消息。

Move in with me first.

We can talk everything over at leisure.”

长篇大套，自说自话完了，一只手就搭了过来，按在陈世龙肩膀上，

陈世龙并不觉得好笑，
Chen found nothing amusing in this.

是着急，
He was alarmed.

没有想到她一厢情愿到痴的程度！
He had never imagined her wishful thinking could reach such a pitch of obsession.

Perhaps throughout her two or three years with Yu Si she had been thinking of Chen all the time.

牵丝攀藤这么多日子下来，要想好好摆脱是无论如何办不到的事。
If she had been winding these tendrils around him for so long, there could be no gentle way to break free.

那么怎么办呢？
What was he to do?

“说嘛！”她又催促，
“Tell me!” she urged.

“啥辰光搬？
“When will you move?

我那里通通现成，
Everything’s ready at my place.

不象你这里，一早起来，要茶要水，什么都没。
Not like here, where you get up in the morning and there’s neither tea nor water.

洗个脸都要到茶店里去。
You even have to go to a teashop to wash your face.

这种光棍打流的日子，你自己想想看，苦不苦？”
Think what a miserable life it is, drifting about as a bachelor!”

不对了！
This would never do.

就这片刻工夫，又是结结实实的一根藤缠了上来，
In those few moments another stout vine had wrapped itself around him.

这样下去，非让她捆得动弹不得不可。
At this rate she would bind him so tightly he could not move.

陈世龙心想，只有快刀一挥，才能斩断纠葛，
Only one swift cut could sever the tangle.

这在她自己受不了，但为了自保，不能不下辣手。
It would hurt her badly, but to save himself he must be ruthless.

“阿七！我骗你我天诛地灭！”他先罚个咒，让她知道决非设词推托：
“A’qi, may Heaven destroy me if I’m lying!” He began with an oath, to show that this was no excuse.

“小和尚老早有小厄姑了！”
“Little Monk already has his Little Nun!”


阿七的脸色大变，
A’qi’s face changed completely.

眼猜倒还是水汪汪的，不过象含了两泡泪水，
Her eyes were still liquid, but now they seemed full of tears.

脸上一阵青、一阵白，
Her face turned pale and ashen by turns.

摇摇头说：
She shook her head.

“我不相信！
“I don’t believe it.

是哪个？”
Who?”

“张家的阿珠。”
“A’zhu, from the Zhang family.”

“哪个张家的阿珠？”
“Which Zhang family?”

“The one who used to work a boat and now runs the Dajing Silk Firm...”

“What are you saying?”

“我还是不相信，
“I still don’t believe it.

“You’ve got it all wrong.

人家是把阿珠当女儿看，

He never meant anything else.”

聘礼都送过去，四样首饰，也是胡老板买的。

We’ll be inviting everyone to the wedding feast before the year is out.”

言之凿凿，不象撒谎，
He spoke with such assurance that it did not sound like a lie.

把阿七听得目瞪口呆，
A’qi was dumbfounded.

背脊上一阵阵发凉，颓然坐倒，只是喃喃地说，“有这种事情？

想都想不到的！”
I could never have imagined it.”

“Exactly.”

陈世龙此时如释重负，
Chen felt an enormous weight lift from him.

“就象你跟郁四叔散伙一样，
“Just like you and Uncle Four parting.

Nobody could have imagined that either.”

“不过……”
“But...”

A’qi suddenly rose, as though she still refused to admit defeat and meant to make one last attempt to wrest him from A’zhu.

Yet she had no strength for it, and her courage failed.

“A’qi,” Chen said soothingly, “everything depends on fate.

我们缘分不到，没有话说。
We weren’t meant for each other, and there’s nothing to be done.

你也不要难过，
Don’t be unhappy.

象你这样的人，不怕没人要。
A woman like you need never fear that nobody will want her.

“他又说：”你的心好，好心自有好报。
You have a good heart, and a good heart gets its reward.

Go home now.

我送你回去。
I’ll see you off.”

“阿七象斗败了的公鸡似地，垂头不语，慢慢站起身来，
She hung her head like a defeated fighting cock and slowly rose without a word.

脸上浑不似初来时那种芍药带露、艳光逼人的神采，

气色灰暗，倒象一下子老了十年。

陈世龙瞻念旧情，不能无动于衷，
Remembering their old affection, Chen could not remain unmoved.

Ignoring A’qi’s dejection, he went out of the alley and hailed a small sedan chair.

同时早就拈了块只多不少的碎银子在手里，

“你回去吧！”
“Go home.”

陈世龙觉得要有句话，哪怕是敷衍的话，也得说一句，才能叫她上轿，因而顺口又说：“有空我来看你！”

回身倒退着进轿时，又是深深地一瞥，为陈世龙留下来无数幽怨。

这时太阳已经很高了，
The sun was high by now.

十月小阳春，阳光明亮，照得人有些目，陈世龙觉得有些晕淘淘，
In this brief return of spring in the tenth month, its bright light was almost dazzling, and Chen felt rather dazed.

信步踏进一爿小茶店，洗脸喝茶点心，
He wandered into a little teashop, washed his face and had tea and something to eat.

静静坐了一会，脑子才算完全清醒。
Only after sitting quietly for a while did his mind become fully clear.

想想这天该做的事，
He considered the day’s business.

第一件就是到阿虎灵前一拜，同时把胡雪岩的话交代了郁四。
First he must pay his respects before A’hu’s spirit tablet and deliver Hu’s message to Yu Si.

进门就淌眼泪，一路淌到灵前，
Tears began to flow as he entered, and continued all the way to the mourning altar.

焚烛上香，
He lit the candles and incense and bowed.

拜罢起身，只见阿兰头上簪一朵白花，手扶在一个小丫头的肩上，袅袅婷婷地走了出来。

一见了面少不得又是“流泪眼观流眼泪”，
At their meeting, each looked through tears at the other’s tearful face.

阿兰姐一面抹眼泪，一面为陈世龙说阿虎得病的经过。
Wiping her eyes, A’lan told him how A’hu had fallen ill.

接着又说她父亲晚年丧子，家门如何不幸，
She spoke of their family’s misfortune, and of her father losing his son in old age.

然后再谈阿七，指她不安于室，
Then she turned to A’qi, accusing her of being restless and unfaithful.

又说阿七日夜吵着要进郁家的门，
A’qi, she said, had clamoured day and night to enter the Yu family household.

不但进门，还要做阿虎嫂的婆婆，要给她磕头。
Nor was that all: she wanted to become A’hu’s widow’s mother-in-law and have the young woman kowtow to her.

“小和尚，你想想看！
“Little Monk, think!

这是做不做得到的事情？”
Was such a thing possible?

阿兰姐说，“明晓得做不列，天天又哭又闹，
She knew perfectly well it wasn’t, yet every day she cried and made scenes.

她打的是什么主意？
What was she after?

还不是一想就明白！
One moment’s thought makes it plain.

所以大家都劝爹，放她走路算了，
We all urged Father to let her go.

这件事提来鸭屎臭，
The whole affair stinks of duck dung.

你见了我爹，不必说起。
Don’t mention it when you see him.

免得他老人家心里不舒服。”
It will only upset him.”

照她说来，是阿七不对。
In her account, A’qi was to blame.

不过陈世龙也不尽相信她的话，只觉得事不关己。不必多问，
Chen did not entirely believe her, but it was no affair of his and there was no need to ask further.

所以点点头说：
He nodded.

“我晓得了。
“I understand.

四叔是不是在茶店里？”
Is Uncle Four at the teahouse?”

“是啊！”
“Yes.

阿兰说，“你昨天叫人送了胡老板的礼来，他才晓得你回来了。

Go and see him there.”

人瘦了不少。

在“家门”里，他的“前人”跟郁四是“同参”，
Within their brotherhood, his initiating master had been Yu Si’s fellow initiate.

师父一死，郁四就算嫡亲的长辈，
Since the master’s death, Yu counted as his closest elder.

Forbidding him to visit because of A’qi did not mean that Yu had ceased to look after him.

起码胡雪岩这条路子就是从这位长辈身上来的，

“家门”里讲究饮水思源，“引见”之恩不可忘。
In the brotherhood, a man must remember the spring from which he drank: the favour of an introduction must never be forgotten.

自己是走错了一步，
That had been a mistake.

“Oh!”

郁四显然自这喜讯中，受到了鼓舞，
The good news visibly heartened Yu.

失神的双眼，有了闪闪的亮光，

“People are always coming and going here.

静不下来。
