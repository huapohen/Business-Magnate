胡雪岩
《平步青云（中）》第十六章（下，至本章结束）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume II — Chapter Sixteen, concluding part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


聚成钱庄中，特为给郁四预备了一个房间，他有许多衙门里的公事，都在这里处理。
Jucheng Bank kept a room especially for Yu Si, and he dealt with much of his official business there.

这天却是清闲无事，
Today, however, he was free.

陈世龙从容细谈，
Chen Shilong could talk at leisure.

先把胡雪岩在上海、杭州的情形，大致说了一遍，最后谈到他头寸的话。
He first gave a general account of Hu Xueyan's affairs in Shanghai and Hangzhou, then came to his need for funds.

郁四跟胡雪岩是有约定的，阜康代为放款，比同行拆息还便宜，照一般放款利息折半计算，当然也不需要什么担保。
Yu and Hu already had an arrangement: Fukang lent out Yu's money and paid him half the interest charged to its borrowers—a cost lower even than the interbank rate—and naturally no security was required.

郁四把聚成的档手喊了进来，
Yu called in Jucheng's manager.

一问可以调拨三万银子，便即关照，马上汇到杭州阜康。
On learning that thirty thousand taels could be made available, he instructed him to remit the money to Fukang in Hangzhou immediately.


谈完“公事”，陈世龙谈私事，把胡雪岩对阿珠的用心及处置，从头细叙。
Their business concluded, Chen turned to his private affairs and recounted, from the beginning, Hu's intentions and arrangements concerning A’zhu.

郁四觉得比听书还要有味，
Yu found it more entertaining than a storyteller's performance.

从烟榻听到饭桌上，再由饭桌听到烟榻上。
He listened on the opium couch, continued listening at the dining table, and returned to the couch for more.

听完说道：“老胡这个人，真要佩服他！
When Chen finished, he said, “You have to admire Old Hu!

做出来的事，别出心裁，真正漂亮！”
The things he does are so original, and so beautifully managed.”

“四叔，”陈世龙说，“喜事总在年底，
“Uncle Four, the wedding should be towards the end of the year.

那时候发帖子，要你老人家替我出面。”
When we send out invitations, I'll need you to represent me.”

“那当然！”
“Naturally!”

说到这里，长叹一声：
He gave a long sigh.

“你倒好了……”
“Things have turned out well for you...”

这自是触景生情，想起阿虎，
The occasion had evidently brought A’hu to mind.

陈世龙赶紧说道：“四叔，你老人家不要难过！
Chen hastened to say, “Don't grieve, Uncle Four.

阿虎不在了，还有我侍奉你老人家。”
A’hu is gone, but I'm still here to look after you.”

一听这话，郁四的眼圈红了，
Yu's eyes reddened.

也不知是伤子还是为陈世龙而感动？
Whether from grief for his son or gratitude to Chen, it was hard to tell.

但终于强自振作起来，
At last he forced himself to rally.

“小和尚！”他说，“你晓得的，我这个做四叔的，也有对不起你的地方，
“Little Monk, you know I've wronged you in some ways.

现在事情过去了，也不必多说了。
That's past, and we needn't say more.

你现在成家立业，朝正路上走去，
Now you're establishing a home and a livelihood and taking the proper road.

我高兴得很，
I'm delighted.

亲事自然我来出面，一切都是我的。
Of course I'll represent you in the marriage, and I'll take responsibility for everything.

那四样首饰，你打听打听看，老胡是花多少银子办的，
Find out how much Old Hu paid for those four kinds of jewellery.

我来还他。
I'll repay him.

有我在，这笔聘礼不好叫他出。”
With me here, he ought not to pay for your betrothal gifts.”


陈世龙自然感激。
Naturally Chen was grateful.

但他虽只跟了胡雪岩短短一段日子，因为人既聪明灵活，又是衷心受教，人情世故的阅历上，大非昔比，
Yet, brief as his time with Hu had been, his intelligence, adaptability, and wholehearted willingness to learn had greatly advanced his understanding of the world.

此时心里在想，自己是出于一番至诚，安慰长辈，而郁四居然拿自己当亲人看待，
He had sincerely meant to comfort an elder; now Yu was treating him as family.

原是好事，
That was good in itself.

但郁家迟早要闹家务，阿兰姐正在动娘家的脑筋，
But trouble would come sooner or later within Yu's household, and Sister A’lan was already scheming about her parents' property.

自己再受郁四的好处，叫别人看来，仿佛他也是乘虚而入，在打郁四的主意，
If he accepted benefits now, people might think he too was taking advantage of Yu's vulnerability.

这个嫌疑不可不避。
He must avoid that suspicion.

避嫌疑犹是小事，
Even that was a lesser concern.

眼前看样子是阿兰姐在替郁四当家，
A’lan appeared to be managing Yu's household now.

买那四样首饰也要千两银子，由郁四捧出来还给胡雪岩，阿兰姐知道了，心里先将不舒服，闲话可就多了！
The four kinds of jewellery would cost a thousand taels; if Yu paid that sum back to Hu, A’lan would be displeased as soon as she heard, and there would be no end of talk.

“怎么？”
“Well?”

郁四见他不作声，倒真有困惑了，
Yu was puzzled by his silence.

“那还有什么话说？”
“What is there to say about it?”

陈世龙已决定辞谢郁四的好意，不过这话不知如何措词？
Chen had decided to decline but could not find the words.

经他一逼，只好这样答道：“四叔！不是我不识拾举，
Pressed, he answered, “Uncle Four, it isn't that I fail to appreciate your kindness.

我是想争口气，这件事我要自己来办。
I want to prove myself and manage this myself.

为来为去也是为四叔争气，
Ultimately, that will do you credit too.

说起来，四叔可以告诉人家，小和尚是自己讨的亲，
You'll be able to tell people that Little Monk paid for his own marriage.

我要替他出聘礼，他用不着。
You offered to provide the betrothal gifts, but he didn't need it.

这不是四叔也有面子。”
Won't that give you something to be proud of?”


江湖上讲究面子，也看重“人贵自立”这句话，
In their world, standing mattered, and so did the saying that a man should support himself.

尤其是做长辈的，听他这样说，自然要嘉许，
An elder could hardly withhold approval.

“你这两句话，我听了倒高兴。
“I'm pleased to hear you say that.

不过，”
But...”

郁四又以告诫的语气说，
Yu's tone became admonitory.

“你刚刚出道，
“You're only just starting out.

不要别的本事没有学会，先学会说大话。
Don't learn to boast before you learn anything else.

那就不对了！”
That wouldn't do.”

“我是实实在在的话。
“I'm speaking honestly.

尤其是在四叔面前，说大话算哪一出？”
What would be the point of boasting, especially before you?”

“那么，我倒问你。”郁四很认真地，“你哪里来的钱讨亲？
“Then let me ask you seriously: where will you get the money to marry?

你不是说四样首饰是老胡替你买的吗？”
Didn't you say Old Hu bought the jewellery for you?”

“是啊！
“Yes.

胡先生替我垫银子买的，
Mr Hu advanced the money.

将来我分了花红可以还他。
I can repay him from my share of the profits later.

如果是四叔替我出了这笔钱，将来我说拿了来还四叔，不是要挨骂了吗？”
If you provided the money, wouldn't you scold me when I tried to pay you back?”

“那也一样。
“It's all the same.

你有了钱也可以孝敬孝敬我的！”
When you have money, you can show your respect for me too.”

“那还用说？
“Of course!

我有了钱不孝敬四叔，把哪个用？
If I don't honour you with it, whom should I spend it on?

不过眼前要请四叔，帮我做过面子争口气，
For now, though, please help me keep my pride and prove myself.

一切让我自己来。”
Let me manage everything.”

听了他的话，郁四又高兴、又困扰，
Yu was both pleased and perplexed.

高兴的是他前面那两句话，就算是米汤，心里也舒服。
The first part delighted him: even if it was flattery, it felt good.

困扰的是后面那两句话，
The second troubled him.

不管他，让他自己去料理，是帮他争气做面子，出钱替他办喜事，反倒不是！
Leaving Chen to his own devices would help him prove himself and preserve his standing; paying for his wedding would apparently do the opposite!

这成何话说。
What sort of reasoning was that?


虽不成话，却驳不倒！
Unreasonable though it sounded, Yu could not refute it.

郁四把头往后仰一仰，打量了陈世龙一番，拿签子指指点点地说：
Leaning back, he studied Chen and pointed at him with the opium needle.

“两三个月不见，我看你是变过了！
“I haven't seen you for two or three months, and you've changed!

长衫上身，倒也蛮象个‘大二先生’的样子，
In that long gown, you look quite like a senior business clerk.

说两句话，异出异样，比上头的‘官腔’还要难应付。
And the things you say are so extraordinary that they're harder to answer than an official pronouncement from above.

这都是你从老胡那里学来的？”
Did you learn all this from Old Hu?”

其词若憾，其实深喜，
He sounded regretful but was secretly delighted.

陈世龙笑笑不答，站起身来说：
Chen merely smiled, then stood.

“四叔，我还有几桩事情，等着要去接头。
“Uncle Four, I have several matters to attend to.

明天再来看你老人家。”
I'll come and see you again tomorrow.”

“明天到我家来，北门！”
“Come to my house tomorrow—the North Gate house.”

郁四特地交代明白，接着又叹口气，
Yu made the address quite clear, then sighed again.

“唉，这一阵的日子，不是人过的，
“These days have been unbearable.

今天见了你，心里好过得多。
Seeing you today has made me feel much better.

你晚上有空，最好再来一趟，
If you're free tonight, do come again.

我还有些话要告诉你，
I have more to tell you.

如果今天晚上没空，明天上午一定来，
If you can't come tonight, you must come tomorrow morning.

茶店里我这一向也少去，
I seldom go to the tea shop now.

今天是为了等你，
I went today only to wait for you.

不然我也就在家里孵孵算了，
Otherwise I'd have stayed brooding at home.

衙门里的差使，我都想辞掉。
I even think of giving up my post at the yamen.

没有意思！”
There's no point!”

说着，摇头不止。
He shook his head repeatedly.

郁四居然连世袭的差使，都不想要了，可知心境灰恶。
That Yu could contemplate abandoning even his hereditary position showed how despondent he had become.

陈世龙于心不忍，颇想再陪他坐一会，说些夷场上有趣的见闻，为他遣愁破闷，
Chen felt sorry for him and wanted to stay, entertaining him with interesting stories of the foreign settlement.

无奈这一夭，从水晶阿七来访开始，已经耽误了太多的工夫，
But the day had already been delayed too long, beginning with Crystal A’qi's visit.

不得不走，去办正事。
He had to leave and attend to business.


等一个圈子兜下来，把胡雪岩交代的事情办妥，已是近夕照黄昏，
By the time he completed his rounds and finished the tasks Hu had entrusted to him, the evening light was fading.

匆匆赶到大经丝行，只见黄仪迎着他说道：
He hurried to Dajing Silk Firm, where Huang Yi came forward to greet him.

“你丈母娘刚走，
“Your mother-in-law has just left.

把你的房间铺陈好，还等了好一歇辰光，看看你不来，只好回去。
She made up your room and waited quite a while, but when you didn't come, she had to go home.

临走千叮万嘱，一定要你到家吃饭。
Before leaving, she told me over and over to send you there for supper.

丈母娘待女婿，真正是没话说。”
Nobody could fault the way she treats her son-in-law!”

“我心里也急。”
“I've been anxious too.”

陈世龙有些不安。
Chen felt uneasy.

“实在是分不开身，
“I simply couldn't get away.

现在也还不能去，
Even now I can't go immediately.

我想先给胡先生写封信，好趁早叫航船带出。”
I want to write to Mr Hu first and send the letter on the regular boat as soon as possible.”

“晚上回来写也不迟。
“You can write when you come back tonight.

好在你今天总要住在这里。”
You're staying here anyway.”

“不！”
“No!”

陈世龙觉得住在大经，便好似“入赘”一般，
Living at Dajing seemed to Chen much like marrying into his wife's household.

有骨气的男子汉是不肯做赘婿住在岳家的，
A man with backbone would not live as a dependent son-in-law in his wife's family home.

因而很坚决地表示：“我还是住在我自己那里。”
He therefore said firmly, “I'm still staying at my own place.”

黄仪了解他的用心，点点头说：
Huang understood.

“这也随你。
“That's up to you.

不过我劝你早点到张家，
But I'd advise you to go to the Zhangs soon.

信到那里去写也一样。”
You can write the letter there just as well.”


这个建议，陈世龙接受了。
Chen accepted that suggestion.

赶到张家，正好是阿珠来开的门。
When he reached the Zhang house, A’zhu herself opened the door.

这一次不象昨天那样不好意思了，
This time she was not as embarrassed as she had been the day before.

她用微带埋怨的口吻说，“怎么到这时候才来？”
“Why have you come so late?” she asked with a trace of reproach.

“遇以好些意想不到的事。
“Several unexpected things happened.

唉！”
Ah!”

陈世龙摇摇头。
He shook his head.

“一进门就叹气，”阿珠十分关切地，“为啥？”
“Sighing as soon as you come in! Why?” She was deeply concerned.

“不是我的事。”陈世龙怕她误会，先这样说一句，好叫她放心，
“It isn't anything that's happened to me,” he said first, lest she misunderstand and worry.

“一个要好弟兄，想不到死掉了。
“A close friend has died unexpectedly.

真正天有不测风云，人有旦夕祸福。”
Truly, storms come without warning, and a person's fortune can change overnight.”

看他神情不怡，阿珠也郁郁地不开心。
His unhappy expression saddened her too.

关上大门，把他带到客堂说道：
Closing the front gate, she led him into the sitting room.

“爹吃喜酒去了。没有人陪你。
“Father has gone to a wedding feast, so there's nobody to keep you company.

要不要到厨房里来？”
Would you like to come into the kitchen?”

“要来的！”
“Certainly.

陈世龙说，“等我到厨房里去打个招呼，抽空给胡先生写信。”
I'll greet your mother there, then find a moment to write to Mr Hu.”

这个招呼一打就是好半天工夫，
The greeting took a long time.

阿珠的娘一面炒菜，一面问长问短问陈世龙这天做了些什么？
As she cooked, A’zhu's mother asked question after question about what Chen had done that day.

于是谈阿虎就谈不完，
Once they began discussing A’hu, there was no end to it.

自然水晶阿七那一段，他只字不会提的。
Naturally, he said nothing whatever about Crystal A’qi.

“好了！”阿珠等要开饭时笑道，“信也写不成了。”
“Well,” A’zhu said with a smile when the meal was ready, “you haven't managed to write your letter after all.”

“吃了饭写，
“I'll write after supper.

今天非写不可。”
It must be done today.”


这是正事，阿珠的娘把它看得很重要，
This was proper business, and A’zhu's mother regarded it as important.

吃完饭，忙着收桌子，泡上茶来，摆出笔砚，
After the meal, she hurried to clear the table, make tea, and set out brush and inkstone.

阿珠又替他铺纸磨墨，
A’zhu spread the paper and ground the ink for him.

连陈世龙自己都觉得这样子未免太郑重，
Even Chen felt that they were making too much of it.

便自嘲似地说，“不象写信，倒象给皇帝写奏折。”
“This looks less like a letter than a memorial to the emperor,” he said with a self-mocking smile.

“闲话少说，
“Less chatter!

快点写好了，送到航船上。
Finish it quickly and take it to the boat.

晚上，人家都睡了，那就得明天起个大早才赶得上。”
If they've gone to sleep, you'll have to get up very early tomorrow to catch them.”

明天有明天的事，
Tomorrow would have its own business.

陈世龙感恩图报，决心要好好巴结，守定今日事今日毕的宗旨，
Determined to repay kindness through diligent work, Chen held to the rule of finishing today's tasks today.

当时定一定心，把胡雪岩交代的事，办得如何，逐项写明。
He collected his thoughts and reported, item by item, how he had carried out Hu's instructions.

最后提到郁四，说他独子病故，而且要闹家务，精神颓唐，
At the end, he mentioned Yu: his only son's death, the threatened family troubles, and his low spirits.

当然，也提到了他的喜事。
Naturally, Chen included news of his own marriage arrangements.

写完看一看钟，已经九点敲过，
When he finished and looked at the clock, it had already struck nine.

匆匆告辞，自己送到去杭州的航船上。然后径自回家。
He hurriedly took his leave and delivered the letter himself to the boat bound for Hangzhou, then went straight home.


未曾进门就已发现了怪事，
Before he reached his door, he noticed something strange.

他屋里亮着灯，而且不止一盏灯亮。
There was light inside—and more than one lamp was burning.

陈世龙出门向来不上锁，
Chen never locked his door when he went out.

因为没有什么东西好偷，而钥匙忘记带出来，或者虽带出来而遗失反倒麻烦，
There was nothing worth stealing, and forgetting or losing a key would be a nuisance.

好在同一个大门里的邻居会替他照看，不锁更不要紧。
Besides, neighbours who shared the entrance could keep an eye on the place.

有时朋友来访，见他不在家径自推门入内坐等，事或有之，
Sometimes a visiting friend, finding him absent, would push open the door and sit down to wait.

但都在白天，
But that happened by day.

象这样的情形，还是头一回，不免令人诧异，同时也逗人的好奇心，
This was the first such occasion at night, and it surprised and intrigued him.

陈世龙心想，倒要看看是哪一个？
Who could it be?

这样转着念头，就不肯直接推门去看，蹑手蹑脚走到窗下，找个窗纸破了的洞洞，凑眼过去张望。
Instead of entering directly, he crept beneath the window and peered through a hole in the paper.

一望就知道麻烦大了。
One look told him he was in serious trouble.

里面是水晶阿七，对着一盏擦得雪亮的油灯在喝茶，两眼怔怔地望着另一张桌上的油灯，仿佛有无数心事在盘算。
Crystal A’qi sat drinking tea by a lamp polished bright, her eyes fixed on another lamp across the room, as though turning over countless worries.

看她身上穿一件紫红宁绸的小夹袄，
She wore a purplish-red, lined jacket of Ning silk.

领子上的纽扣未扣，敞得极大，一股系肚兜的金链子，隐约可见，
Its collar was unfastened and spread wide, revealing glimpses of the gold chain that held her underbodice.

这副样子让人看见了，不说“水晶阿七跟小和尚有一腿”，那才真叫有鬼！
Anyone seeing her like this would certainly say that Crystal A’qi and Little Monk were having an affair.

陈世龙十分火冒，走到房门口，提脚就踢，
Furious, Chen went to the door and raised his foot to kick it.

但就在拉起脚的刹那，心中自语，
At that instant, he checked himself.

慢来！
Wait!

看样子阿七不知安着什么心？
Who knew what she intended?

他知道她的为人，心是不坏，但吃了那碗饭，脸皮就撕破了，什么好刁泼辣的事，都做得出来。
Her heart was not bad, but her former occupation had stripped away her inhibitions, and she could be as wilful and defiant as anyone.

也许她是故意的，好说不行，存心来撩拨得自己跟她吵架，
Perhaps, having failed by persuasion, she deliberately meant to provoke a quarrel.

传到阿珠耳朵里，这饥荒有得打。
If word reached A’zhu, there would be trouble enough.

万一吵散，阿七就得其所哉了！
Should the engagement break off, A’qi would have got just what she wanted.

念头转到这里，自觉是“小人之心”，
Chen felt that this suspicion was mean-minded.

但记起黄仪常说的两句话：“害人之心不可有，防人之心不可无”，
Yet he remembered Huang's saying: never intend harm, but never fail to guard against it.

象阿七这种人不可不防，
He must be cautious with someone like A’qi.

只看眼前的情形，就是自己防不到的。
Her presence here already showed what he had failed to foresee.


想停当了，气也平了，
Once he had thought it through, his anger subsided.

伸手把门一推，
He pushed open the door.

阿七似乎猝不及防，霍地站起身来，两眼睁得极大，
Apparently taken by surprise, A’qi sprang up with wide eyes.

看见陈世龙才拍拍胸说：
Seeing Chen, she patted her chest.

“咄！
“Oh!

吓得我来！”
You frightened me!”

“你倒不说我吓一跳！”陈世龙平静地答道，
“What about the fright you gave me?” he answered calmly.

“你这样子，象不象半夜里跑出一只狐狸精来？”
“Don't you look like a fox spirit appearing in the middle of the night?”

“你骂好了！”
“Scold away!”
