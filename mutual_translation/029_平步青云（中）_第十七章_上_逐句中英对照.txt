胡雪岩
《平步青云（中）》第十七章（上）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume II — Chapter Seventeen, first part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第十七章｜CHAPTER SEVENTEEN


刚睡下不久，小徒弟来敲门，送来一封夜班航船刚刚带到的信，
Chen had not been in bed long when a young apprentice knocked and brought him a letter just delivered by the night passenger boat.

信是胡雪岩寄来的，
It was from Hu Xueyan.

拆开一看，寥寥数语，只说得知郁四有伤子之痛，深为惦念，
In a few brief lines, Hu said that he had heard of Yu Si's bereavement and was deeply concerned.

特地抽空，专程到湖州来一趟，慰唁郁四，发信以后，即日下船。
He had made time for a special journey to Huzhou to offer his condolences and would take a boat the very day he sent the letter.

这一下，陈世龙的愁怀尽夫，
All Chen's worries vanished.

有胡雪岩到，凡事都不碍了。
With Hu coming, nothing need trouble him any longer.

一觉好睡，第二天一早，悄悄到码头上去等，
He slept soundly, then slipped down to the wharf early the next morning to wait.

等到十点多钟，将胡雪岩等到了。
It was after ten when Hu arrived.

泊舟下碇，搭好跳板，陈世龙先到船上，
Once the boat had anchored and the gangplank was in place, Chen went aboard.

笑嘻嘻叫过一声：“胡先生：”
“Mr Hu!” he called with a smile.

接着又说，“没有想到胡先生会来，
“I never expected you to come.

真是太好了。”
This is wonderful.”

听他这样说法，便知自己这一趟适逢其分，有什么事要自己来料理，
His manner told Hu that he had arrived at just the right moment: something needed his attention.

胡雪岩便点点头说，
He nodded.

“我是包了一只船夹的，只有三天的工夫。
“I've hired a boat for the journey, and I have only three days.

来，你坐下来，
Come, sit down.

我们先细谈一谈。”
Let's go through everything first.”


这一谈便长了，
The conversation proved a long one.

由郁四丧子谈到他的家务，由阿七谈到自己的麻烦，由自己又谈到黄仪。
From Yu's loss of his son, Chen passed to his household troubles; from A’qi to his own difficulties; and from himself to Huang Yi.

自然，也谈到郁四尽释前嫌，替自己出面办喜事，如何会亲送聘金，以及阿珠的娘要替女儿办嫁妆，婚期得延到明年。
Naturally, he also told Hu how Yu had put aside all past grievances and undertaken to arrange Chen's wedding, how Yu had formally met the bride's family and delivered the betrothal money, and how A’zhu's mother wished to prepare her daughter's dowry, which meant postponing the wedding until the following year.

结语是：“我一切都要请胡先生来作主。”
He concluded, “I must ask you to decide everything for me.”

“想不到我一走，出了这么多花样！”
“Who would have thought so many complications could arise the moment I left?”

胡雪岩紧皱着眉，想了好半天才开口：
Hu frowned deeply and considered for some time before speaking.

“你的喜事，怎么样都可以，
“Your wedding can be arranged one way or another.

慢慢再说。
We can discuss that later.

你郁四叔搞成这样子，倒有些伤脑筋了。
Your Fourth Uncle Yu's situation is more troublesome.

他的大小姐我没有见过，
I've never met his daughter.

你看她为人如何？
What do you think of her?

天性厚不厚？”
Is she naturally kind?”

“阿兰姐的精明强干，早就有名的。
“Sister A’lan has long been known for her intelligence and ability.

天性呢，”
As for her nature...”

陈世龙出语很谨慎，
Chen chose his words carefully.

“自然不会太薄，
“Surely she can't be too unkind.

郁四叔只有这么一个女儿。”
She's his only daughter.”

“现在是唯一的亲骨肉了！
“And now his only surviving child.

我想，她不会不孝顺，也不敢不孝顺。”
I don't think she would fail in her duty to him—or dare to.”

最后一句话，骤然难以索解，
The last words were not immediately clear.

细想一想，才察出这句话中的分量，
On reflection, Chen understood their weight.

如果阿兰姐敢于不孝顺老父，胡雪岩以父执的资格，一定会出来说话。
If A’lan dared to mistreat her elderly father, Hu would certainly intervene as her father's friend.

至少限度，他会劝郁四，一个沙壳子都不要给阿兰姐，
At the very least, he would advise Yu not to give her a single copper.

“嫁出的女儿泼出的水”，阿兰姐在娘家硬争是争不到财产的。
“A married daughter is water poured away”: however fiercely she fought at her parents' home, that would not secure her the property.


“胡先生，”陈世龙忽有灵感，“你何不帮郁四叔把家务料理一下子？”
“Mr Hu,” Chen said, struck by an idea, “why not help Fourth Uncle put his household affairs in order?”

胡雪岩沉吟不语，
Hu considered in silence.

显然是觉得陈世龙的提议，不无考虑的余地。
Clearly, the proposal deserved thought.

照他的性情，以及与郁四的交情来说，不能不管这桩闲事，
Given his own temperament and his friendship with Yu, he could hardly stand aside.

只是不管则已，一管就要弄得漂漂亮亮，
Yet once he intervened, he must see the whole affair properly settled.

三天的工夫来不及，就算再加一两天，未见得能料理清楚，
Three days would not be enough; even another day or two might not suffice.

而上海、杭州的事却要耽误，
Meanwhile, business in Shanghai and Hangzhou would suffer.

变成“驼子跌跟斗，两头落空”，
Like a hunchback taking a tumble, with both ends left in the air, he would fail on both fronts.

不智之至。
That would be foolish indeed.

“还有，”陈世龙又说，带些愁眉苦脸地，“阿七是个麻烦！
“And there's A’qi,” Chen added with a troubled face. “She's a nuisance.

从前我不伯她，随她怎么好了！
I wasn't afraid of her before; she could do as she pleased.

现在我不能跟她一起在烂泥塘里滚。
But now I can't go rolling in the mud with her.

胡先生，你看我该怎么办？”
What am I to do?”

这就是“混市面”的人的苦衷！
Such was the predicament of men who had made their way on the streets.

人之好善，谁不如我？
Who did not wish to better himself?

略有身价，总想力争上游，成为衣冠中人，
Once a man had acquired a little standing, he wanted to rise and become respectable.

但虽出淤泥，要想不染却甚难，
Yet emerging from the mud without a stain was difficult.

因为过去的关系，拉拉扯扯，
Old connections tugged at him.

自己爱惜羽毛不肯在烂泥塘里一起打滚，无奈别人死拉住不放，结果依旧同流合污。
He might value his good name and refuse to roll in the mire, but others clung to him and would not let go; in the end he remained involved in the same disreputable ways.

胡雪岩对这一点十分清楚，当然要替陈世龙想办法。
Hu understood this thoroughly and would naturally find a way to help Chen.

郁四的家务是个难题，陈世龙的麻烦又是一个难题，
Yu's household was one difficult problem, Chen's predicament another.

两个难题加在一起，反激出胡雪岩的灵感，
Together, the two suddenly gave Hu an idea.

站起身来说：
He stood up.

“走！
“Come.

我们上岸。”
Let's go ashore.”


看他欣然有得的神情，陈世龙知道他又要出“奇计”了，
Seeing the pleased look of discovery on Hu's face, Chen knew that another ingenious plan was forming.

便笑嘻嘻地问道：“胡先生，你一定又有啥人家想不到的主意，
“Mr Hu,” he asked with a smile, “you must have thought of something nobody else would imagine.

好不好先讲给我听听？”
Could you tell me first?”

“没有啥不好讲的。”
“There's no reason not to.

胡雪岩说，“我想叫阿七‘船并老码头’。”
I mean to have A’qi moor her boat at the old landing.”

陈世龙一愣，
Chen was startled.

再细想一想，不由得衷心钦服，
Then, thinking it through, he felt profound admiration.

郁四少不得阿七，是他早就深知的。
He had long known that Yu could not do without A’qi.

现在硬生生的拆散，完全是阿兰姐夫妇在捣鬼。
Their enforced separation was entirely the work of A’lan and her husband.

倘能破镜重圆，且不说阿七这方面，起码郁四的心情，就不会这么颓丧。
If the broken mirror could be made whole again, then, quite apart from A’qi's feelings, Yu would at least cease to be so dejected.

当然，自己的麻烦，就此烟消云散，更不在话下。
Naturally, Chen's own troubles would vanish too.

“胡先生！真正是，有时候我们看事情总不够透彻，自己不晓得什么道理？
“Mr Hu, sometimes we fail to see things clearly and can't understand why.

现在我懂了，
Now I know.

差的就是那一层纸，一个指头可以戳破的，我们就是看不到！”
There's only a sheet of paper between us and the answer, thin enough to pierce with a finger, yet we simply can't see it!”

“你不要恭维我。
“Don't flatter me.

事情成不成，还不晓得。
We don't know yet whether it will work.

等我先去探探口气。”
Let me first find out how matters stand.

胡雪岩说，“先去看你郁四叔。”
We'll go and see your Fourth Uncle.”


于是陈世龙上岸，在码头上雇了两乘轿子，一直抬到郁四家。
Chen went ashore and hired two sedan chairs at the wharf to carry them straight to Yu's house.

陈世龙先下轿，一直奔了进去，只见郁四一个人在喝闷酒，
Getting out first, he hurried inside and found Yu drinking alone in low spirits.

叫应一声，接下来说：“胡先生来了！”
He greeted him, then announced, “Mr Hu is here!”

郁四顿有惊喜之色，
Delight and surprise instantly appeared on Yu's face.

“在哪里？”他站起身问。
“Where?” he asked, rising.

“从船上下来，就到这里，
“He's come straight here from the boat.

他是专程来看四叔的。”
He made the journey especially to see you, Fourth Uncle.”

正说到这里，胡雪岩已经走进二门，
As Chen spoke, Hu had already entered the inner gate.

郁四急忙迎了上去，
Yu hurried to meet him.

执手相看，似乎都有千言万语，不知从何说起？
They clasped hands and looked at one another, each seemingly with a thousand things to say and no idea where to begin.

好半天，胡雪岩才说了句：“四哥，你不要难过！”
At last Hu said, “Brother Yu, don't grieve so.”

不说还好，
Until then Yu had held himself together.

一说正说到郁四伤心之处，眼泪簌簌地流个不住，
Those words touched the heart of his sorrow, and tears streamed down his face.

顿足哭道：“做人真没有意思！”
Stamping his foot, he cried, “There's no point in living!”

接着又哽哽咽咽，断断续续地说，不逢知己，连痛哭一场都不能够。
Between broken sobs, he went on: without a true friend, he could not even have a proper cry.

自己有多少心事，无人可诉，这份苦楚，一时也说不尽。
He had so much on his mind and nobody to tell; all his suffering could not be expressed at once.

如今交代了胡雪岩，便要辞掉衙门里的差使，找个清静地方去吃素念佛，了此余生。
Now that Hu had come, he would explain everything to him, resign his post at the yamen, and find somewhere quiet to eat vegetarian food and recite the Buddha's name for the rest of his days.

“四哥，四哥！”胡雪岩连声叫唤，“不必如此，不必如此！”
“Brother Yu, Brother Yu! There's no need for that!” Hu kept repeating.

就这样解劝着，他半搀半携地把郁四扶到里面，
With these consoling words, he half supported and half led Yu indoors.

接着阿兰姐出来拜见，
A’lan came out to greet him.

虽是初见，久已闻名，她知道这是自己父亲的一个很够分量的朋友，
Although they had never met, she had long heard his name and knew that he was an influential friend of her father's.

所以礼数甚恭，好好敷衍了一阵，接着重治酒肴，留客便饭。
She was therefore scrupulously courteous, attending to him for a while before having fresh dishes and wine prepared and asking him to stay for a simple meal.


胡雪岩在席问只听郁四诉苦，很少说话，
At the table, Hu listened to Yu's complaints and said little.

一则是要多听，二则此时也不便深谈。
He wanted to hear more, and this was not the place for a confidential discussion.

等郁四倾吐了心里的愁郁，精神显得振作了些，他才说道：“四哥，我有几句心腹话想说。”
Only after Yu had poured out his distress and begun to look somewhat revived did Hu say, “Brother Yu, there are a few things I'd like to say in confidence.”

“噢！”
“Ah!”

郁四懂了他的意思，
Yu understood.

“到我钱庄里去坐。”
“Let's go and sit at my bank.”

到了聚成钱庄，郁四那间密室里没有第三者，两人靠在烟榻上，聚首密谈，
At Jucheng Bank, with nobody else in Yu's private room, they reclined together on the opium couch and spoke quietly.

胡雪岩的第一句话是，“四哥，阿七到底是怎么回事？”
Hu's first question was, “Brother Yu, what exactly happened with A’qi?”

“唉！”郁四长叹一声，又息了好一会才说：
Yu gave a long sigh, then paused for some time.

“我不晓得从何说起？
“I don't know where to begin.

这件事……”
This affair...”

他摇摇头，又叹口气。
He shook his head and sighed again.

察言观色、这没有说完的一句话是：这件事我做错了。
From his expression and manner, Hu could complete the unfinished sentence: I was wrong.

有此表示，见得胡雪岩的那句话一针见血！
Clearly, his question had touched the sore spot.

这就用不着再迂回试探了，
There was no need for further indirect probing.

“四哥，”他开门见山地说：“我替你把阿七弄回来！”
“Brother Yu,” he said plainly, “I'll get A’qi back for you.”

一听这话，郁四仰直了头看着胡雪岩，仿佛弄不懂他的意思，当他在说笑话，
Yu raised his head and stared, as though he could not understand and thought Hu must be joking.

当然不会是笑话！
But of course he was not.

胡雪岩从不说这些笑话的，
Hu never joked about such things.

就算是笑话，他也相信胡雪岩有把笑话变成真事的手段。
Even if it were a joke, Yu believed he had the means to make it real.

要考虑的只是自己这方面。
He needed only to consider his own position.


“难处也很多……”
“There would be a great many difficulties...”

“不！”胡雪岩打断他的话说，
“No!” Hu interrupted.

“四哥，你不要管这些个。
“You needn't concern yourself with those.

你说的难处，我都知道，
I know the difficulties you mean.

第一，怕阿兰姐跟阿七不和，
First, you're afraid A’lan and A’qi won't get on.

第二，怕阿七心里有气，故意拿跷。
Second, you're afraid A’qi is still angry and will deliberately make herself difficult to please.

这些都不是难处，
Neither is a problem.

包在我身上，安排得妥妥帖帖，
Leave them to me, and I'll arrange everything properly.

只看四哥你自己。
It depends only on you.

如果你一定要唱一出《马前泼水》，那就不必再谈。
If you're determined to act out Water Spilled Before the Horse and refuse to take her back, there's nothing more to discuss.

否则，一切归我来办。
Otherwise, leave it all to me.

你倒说一句看！
Tell me what you think.”

““有你这样的好朋友，我还说什么？”
“With a friend like you, what more can I say?”

“那就行了，
“That's settled, then.

我就要你这一句话，
Your word is all I need.

你请躺一躺，
Lie here and rest.

我跟世龙说句话，马上就回来。”
I'll have a word with Shilong and come straight back.”

于是胡雪岩离榻而起，把陈世龙找到，拉至僻处，密密嘱咐了一番，
Hu rose from the couch, found Chen, and drew him aside to give him detailed instructions in private.

等陈世龙领计而去，他才回到原处。
Only after Chen had left to carry them out did Hu return.


“四哥，”他说，“我话先说在前面，
“Brother Yu, let me say this first.

谈到你的家务，只怕我言语太直，你会不会动气？”
If I speak too bluntly about your family affairs, will you be offended?”

“这叫什么话？
“What a thing to ask!

你我的交情，哪怕你就责备我不是，我也要听你的。”
With our friendship, even if you reproached me outright, I should listen.”

“既然如此，我就老实说了，
“Then I'll speak frankly.

你那位令嫒，大家都说她厉害得很，
Everyone says that daughter of yours is extremely formidable.

可有这话？”
Is it true?”

“有的。”
“Yes.”

郁四点点头，
Yu nodded.

“我也在防她。”
“I'm on my guard against her myself.”

“至亲骨肉，时时刻刻要防备，那就苦了。
“It's a miserable thing to have to guard against your own flesh and blood all the time.

打开天窗说亮话，
Let's speak openly.

人为财死，鸟为食亡，
Men die for wealth as birds die for food.

为来为去，为两个钱。
Whatever the twists and turns, it's all about money.

我劝你不如趁此机会分家。
I advise you to take this opportunity to divide the estate.

女儿也得一份，叫她不必再想东想西，
Give your daughter a share too, so that she needn't keep scheming.

岂不爽快吗？”
Wouldn't that settle things neatly?”

“嗯，嗯！”
“Yes, yes.”

郁四慢慢点头道：
Yu nodded slowly.

“这倒也是个办法。
“That is one way.

你再说，
Go on.

你总还有话。”
You must have more to say.”

“分家也有个分法。”
“There are different ways to divide an estate.

胡雪岩说，“我先要问你，你自己总也有过打算？”
First I must ask whether you've made any plans yourself.”


“我哪里有什么打算？
“What plans could I have?

阿虎一死，我的心冰凉，
When A’hu died, my heart turned to ice.

恨不得出家做和尚！
I wished I could become a monk.

他们怎么说，怎么好，
Whatever they said was good enough for me.

反正我都丢开了，随他们去搞。
I'd abandoned everything; they could do as they pleased.

不过，”
But...”

郁四顿了一顿，显得有些激动，
Yu paused, visibly moved.

“小和尚一来，听说了他的情形。我心里才高兴了些。
“When Little Monk came and I heard how he was getting on, I began to feel a little happier.

今天，你路远迢迢抽出工夫来看我，
And today you've made time to travel all this way to see me.

想想这个世界上也还有些好东西。
It makes me think there are still some good things in this world.

说句实话，到现在我才稍微有点做人的乐趣。”
To tell you the truth, only now am I beginning to find a little pleasure in being alive.”

这才真的是肺腑之言，
These were words from the heart.

胡雪岩觉得很安慰，也越觉得要替他尽心，
Hu felt comforted and all the more determined to do his utmost.

“四哥，”他说，“承蒙你看得起我，我倒不能不多事了，
“Brother Yu, since you think so well of me, I can't avoid taking a hand.

索性变得深些。
I might as well go further.

府上的事，要通盘筹划，
We must plan for the whole household.

麻烦虽多，不能怕事，
However many difficulties there are, we mustn't shrink from them.

挺一挺胸，咬一咬牙，把它一起理清楚了，
Straighten your back, grit your teeth, and let's untangle everything at once.

好不好？”
Shall we?”

“好啊！”郁四很兴奋的回答，
“Yes!” Yu answered eagerly.

他自己也盘算过家务，但越想越头痛，
He too had tried to plan for his household, but the more he thought, the worse his head ached.

始终鼓不起勇气来清理这一团乱丝，
He had never found the courage to sort out that tangled skein.

现在听胡雪岩这样说法，先就如释重负，心里好过得多。
Hu's words alone lifted a burden from him, and he already felt much better.

“那么，一样样地谈。
“Then let's take things one at a time.

我先请问，你衙门里的差使，将来怎么样处置？”
First, what do you intend to do about your post at the yamen?”


户书是世袭的差使，
The revenue clerk's post was hereditary.

因为手中有一本将全县田地业主、座落、亩数、赋额记载得明明白白的“鱼鳞册”，
In his hands was a fish-scale register, recording every landowner in the county, the location and acreage of each holding, and the tax assessed on it.

这就是世世代代吃着不穷的衣食饭碗。
This provided an inexhaustible livelihood from generation to generation.

如果阿虎不死，自然归他承袭父职，
Had A’hu lived, he would naturally have succeeded his father.

现在膝下无子，
Now Yu had no son.

即令将来有后，要把儿子教养成人，是二三十年后的事。
Even if he later had another, it would take twenty or thirty years to bring the child to manhood.

渺焉无凭，作不得那佯不切实际的打算，
Such a distant and uncertain prospect could not form the basis of a practical plan.

所以郁四曾经一度想辞差，这是绝少有的事，
That was why Yu had once considered resigning, an exceedingly rare step.

通常总是有亲子则亲子承袭，否则就收个螟蛉子，
Ordinarily, a natural son inherited the post; failing that, the clerk adopted a son.

甚至高价顶让，改姓承袭。

Her gratitude was towards Hu.
