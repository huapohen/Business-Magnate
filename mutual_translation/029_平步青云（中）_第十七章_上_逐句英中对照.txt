CHAPTER SEVENTEEN｜第十七章


Chen had not been in bed long when a young apprentice knocked and brought him a letter just delivered by the night passenger boat.

有胡雪岩到，凡事都不碍了。

“I never expected you to come.

“He's come straight here from the boat.
