胡雪岩
《平步青云（中）》第十七章（下，至本章结束）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume II — Chapter Seventeen, concluding part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


湖州之行，三天之内，胡雪岩替自己办了两件要紧事。
During three days in Huzhou, Hu Xueyan accomplished two important things for himself.

第一件是约妥了黄仪，随他到杭州去办笔墨。
First, he secured Huang Yi’s agreement to accompany him to Hangzhou as his secretary.

黄仪改变了心意，一则想到外面去闯闯，二则是觉得跟了胡雪岩这样的东家，十分够味，
Huang had changed his mind partly because he wanted to try his fortune elsewhere, and partly because serving an employer like Hu promised to be thoroughly satisfying.

当然也知道这位东家不会薄待，所以薪水酬劳等等，根本不谈。
He also knew that Hu would not treat him poorly, so salary and remuneration were never even discussed.

第二件是进一步赢得了郁四的友谊。
Second, Hu strengthened his friendship with Yu Si.

郁四自从跟阿七言归于好，他的颓唐老态，一扫而空，
Since making peace with A’qi, Yu had lost all his dejection and signs of age.

不再谈衙门里辞差的话，家务也不劳胡雪岩再费心，表示自己可以打点精神来料理。
He no longer spoke of resigning his post at the yamen and told Hu not to trouble himself further about his family affairs: he could now summon the energy to manage them himself.

胡雪岩要头寸周转，除了已经拨付的那一笔以外，另外又调动了五万两银子，让他带走。
As Hu needed working funds, Yu arranged another fifty thousand taels for him to take away, in addition to the sum already transferred.

“你的事就是我的事。
“Your business is mine.

为你这样的朋友，倾家荡产也值得。
For a friend like you, even losing my entire fortune would be worthwhile.

况且，我相信你一定有办法。”他这样对胡雪岩说：
Besides, I am sure you will find a way.

“你要头寸，只要早点告诉我，我一定替你调齐。”
Whenever you need funds, give me a little notice, and I will have them ready.”

有了郁四的十万银子和他的那句话，胡雪岩又是雄心万丈了。
With Yu’s hundred thousand taels and that assurance, Hu’s ambitions soared again.

他目前最困难的，就是头寸，
His greatest difficulty was liquidity.

在上海堆栈里的丝，搁煞了他的大部分本钱，
Most of his capital was tied up in the silk stored in Shanghai.

阜康钱庄的生意，做得极其热闹，已成“大同行”中的“金字招牌”之一，但唯其如此，决不能露丝毫捉襟见时的窘态，
Fukang was flourishing and had become one of the most respected names among the leading banks; for that very reason, it could not afford the slightest appearance of financial strain.

而海运局方面，正当新旧交替之际，亏空只能补，不能拉。
Meanwhile, the Sea Transport Bureau was changing hands: its deficit must be reduced, not increased.

在这青黄不接的当口，萌雪岩一度想把那批丝，杀价卖掉，
In this interval before fresh funds became available, Hu had considered selling the silk at a heavy discount.

虽仍有盈余，但已有限，费心费力的结果，变成几乎白忙一场，
There would still be a profit, but a small one, making all his effort almost unrewarded.

自是于心不甘，同时也不肯错过这个机会。
He could not reconcile himself to that, nor did he wish to abandon the opportunity.

左右为难之下，有郁四的这一臂之力，帮忙帮得大了。
Caught between these difficulties, he found Yu’s help invaluable.


“四哥！”他兴奋地说，“只要你相信我，我包你这笔款子的利息，比放给哪个都来得划算。
“Brother Yu,” he said excitedly, “if you trust me, I guarantee that the return on this money will be better than you could obtain from lending it to anyone else.

我已经看准了，这十万银子，我还要‘扑’到洋庄上去。
I have made up my mind to commit these hundred thousand taels to the foreign trade too.

前两天我在杭州得到消息，两江总督怡大人，要对洋人不客气了，
A few days ago in Hangzhou, I learned that Governor-General Yiliang intends to take a hard line with the foreigners.

这是个难得的机会，一抓住必发大财。
It is a rare opportunity, and whoever seizes it will make a fortune.

不过，机会来了，别人不晓得，我晓得，别人看不准，我看得准。
When an opportunity comes, others do not know of it, but I do; others cannot judge it clearly, but I can.

这就是人家做生意，做不过我的地方。
That is why they cannot match me in business.”

“说了半天是什么机会呢？
What opportunity did he mean?

两江总督怡良，郁四倒是晓得的，他是当权的恭亲王的老丈人，也算是皇亲国戚，
Yu knew who Yiliang was: the father-in-law of the influential Prince Gong, and therefore a relative of the imperial house.

如果有什么大举措，朝廷一定会支持他，
If he undertook any major action, the court would certainly support him.

然而对洋人是如何不客气？
But what did taking a hard line with the foreigners mean?

“莫非，”他迟疑地问，“又要跟洋人开仗？”
“Surely,” Yu asked hesitantly, “we are not going to war with them again?”

“那是不会的……”胡雪岩说，他听到的消息是，因为两件事，两江总督怡良对洋人深为不满，
“No, not that.” Hu explained that, according to his information, Yiliang was deeply displeased with the foreigners for two reasons.

第一，小刀会的刘丽川，有洋人自租界接济军火粮食，
First, foreigners were sending arms and provisions from the settlement to Liu Lichuan of the Small Swords Society.

这是“助逆”而不是“助顺”，
This was supporting rebellion instead of lawful authority.

就算实际上对刘丽川没有什么帮助，朝廷亦难容忍，而况对刘丽川确为一大助力。
The court would scarcely tolerate it even if the supplies did Liu little practical good; in fact, they were a considerable help.

第二是从上海失守以后，“夷税”也就是按值百抽五计算的关税，洋人借口虞乱影响，商务停顿，至今不肯缴纳。
Second, since Shanghai had fallen, the foreigners had refused to pay the so-called foreign tax, the customs duty levied at five percent of value. They claimed that the disturbances had brought commerce to a halt.

商务受影响自是难免，如说完全停顿，则是欺人之谈。
Trade was inevitably affected, but to say it had stopped entirely was deception.

洋商缴纳关税，全靠各国领事代为约束，
Payment of customs duties by foreign merchants depended on their consuls enforcing the obligation.

现在有意不缴，无奈其何！
If they deliberately refused, what could be done?

那就只有一个办法：不跟洋人做生意。
There was only one answer: cease trading with them.


“租界上的事，官府管不到，
“The authorities cannot control affairs inside the settlement.

再说不跟洋商做生意，难道把销洋庄的货色，抛到黄浦江里？
And even if we say we will stop trading with foreign merchants, are we to throw all our export goods into the Huangpu River?

这自然是办不到的，
Obviously not.

所以，再退一步说，只有一个办法，这个办法也很厉害，内地的丝茶两项，不准运入租界。
So there is only one practicable step left, and it is a powerful one: prohibit silk and tea from the interior from entering the settlement.

这是官府办得到的事。”
That is something the authorities can enforce.”

“我懂了！还是你原来的办法，”郁四点点头说，
“I see!” Yu nodded. “Your original plan again.

“那样子一来，丝茶两项存货的行情，一定大涨。
The price of silk and tea already held there would certainly soar.

这倒是好主意！”
A fine idea!”

“自然是好生意。”胡雪岩说，
“A fine business, certainly.

“丝我有了，而且现在也不是时候，收不到货，
I already have silk, and this is not the season to buy more.

茶叶上面，大有脑筋可动，
But there is plenty to be done with tea.

官府做事慢，
Officials move slowly.

趁告示没有出来以前，我还来得及办货。
Before the proclamation appears, I still have time to acquire stock.

此外，我还想开一爿当铺，开一家药店，阜康也想在上海设分号……”
Besides, I want to open a pawnshop and a pharmacy, and establish a Shanghai branch of Fukang.”

“老胡，”郁四打断他的话，“我说一句，怕不中听，
“Old Hu,” Yu interrupted, “I want to say something you may not like.

不过我声明在先，决不是我有啥别的心思，
But let me make it clear first: I have no ulterior motive.

无非提醒你，
It is only a reminder.

事情还是你去做，你说怎么办就怎么办。”
You will still conduct the business, and whatever you decide will be done.”

“四哥，我们的交情，你这番表白是多余的。”
“With our friendship, Brother Yu, such an explanation is unnecessary.”

话虽多余，不能不先交代，
Unnecessary though it was, it had to be made.

这就是江湖上的“过节”，
This was one of the formal observances of the brotherhood world.

其实就是郁四以下要说的话，也近乎多余，
Indeed, what Yu went on to say was almost unnecessary too.

他劝胡雪岩说，一个人本事再大，精力有限，
However capable a man might be, his energy was limited.

头绪太多，必有照顾不到的地方。
With too many undertakings, something would escape his attention.

而且他的生意，互相关联，牵一发而动全身，
Moreover, Hu’s businesses were interconnected: pull one thread and the whole fabric moved.

一垮下来，不可收拾。
A collapse could become irretrievable.

不如暂时收敛，稳扎稳打。
Better to restrain himself for the present and advance steadily.

这番话语重心长，见得郁四的关切，
The earnest advice showed Yu’s concern.

但胡雪岩自己何尝不知道？
But how could Hu be unaware of these dangers?

其间的利害关系，他远比郁四了解得更透彻，
He understood the interests involved far better than Yu did.

不过他自己足以应付得了，哪一处出了毛病，该如何急救？也曾细细策划过，
He believed he could manage them and had carefully planned emergency measures for trouble in each quarter.

有恃无恐，所以我行我素。
Confident of his resources, he intended to follow his own course.

只是郁四说到这样的话，休戚相关，虽不能听，亦不宜辩，
Yet when a friend whose fortunes were bound to his offered such advice, he ought not argue even if he could not follow it.

因而不断点头，表示接受。
He therefore nodded repeatedly, as though accepting it.


接受不是一句空话可以敷衍的，
Acceptance could not be reduced to an empty assurance.

而郁四有大批本钱投在自己名下，也得替他顾虑。
Yu had invested a large amount with him, and Hu had to consider his interests.

胡雪岩的思考向来宽阔而周密，
Hu’s thoughts were always wide-ranging and thorough.

心里在想郁四的话，可有言外之意？
Was there something behind Yu’s advice?

却是不能不问清楚的。
He must find out.

“四哥，你的话十分实在。
“Brother Yu, what you say is entirely sound.

当铺、药店，我决定死了心，暂且丢下。
I have decided to put the pawnshop and pharmacy aside for now.

不过，我要请问一句，四哥一定要跟我说实话。”
But there is something I must ask, and you must answer frankly.”

“你这话也是多余的。”郁四答道，
“That remark is unnecessary too.

“我几时跟你说过假话？”
When have I ever lied to you?”

“是的，是的，
“Of course, of course.

我晓得。”胡雪岩连连点头，
I know.” Hu nodded repeatedly.

“不过，我怕我或者有啥看不列的地方，要请四哥指点。
“But I may have overlooked something and need your guidance.

你看，我们在上海的那批丝，是不是现在脱手比较好？
Do you think we should sell our Shanghai silk now?”

““嗐！”郁四的神色和声音，大似遗憾，
“Ah!” Yu’s expression and voice showed deep regret.

“你完全弄错我的意思了！
“You have completely misunderstood me.

你当我不放心我投在你那里的本钱，
You think I am worried about the capital I have entrusted to you.

决不是！
Not at all!

我早就说过了，我相信你，
I have told you before: I trust you.

生意你去做，我不过问。”
You conduct the business; I do not interfere.”


“四哥是相信我，结果弄得‘鸭屎臭’，叫我怎么对四哥交代？”
“You trust me, Brother Yu, but if the result is a foul mess, how could I answer to you?”

“不要交代！要啥交代？
“You needn’t answer for it!

做生意有亏有蚀，
Business has losses as well as gains.

没话可说！
There is nothing to complain of.

只有‘开口自己人，独吃自己人’的才是‘鸭屎臭’，
The only foul men are those who call you one of their own and then swallow all the profit themselves.

你不是那种人。
You are not such a man.

再说一句，就算你要存心吃我，我也情愿，
I’ll say more: even if you deliberately took advantage of me, I would accept it.

这话不是我现在说，
I am not saying this only now.

你问阿七。”说着便连声喊着：“阿七，
Ask A’qi.” He called repeatedly, “A’qi!

阿七！
A’qi!

胡老板有话问你。”
Boss Hu has something to ask you.”

阿七在打点送胡雪岩的土仪，正忙得不可开支，但听说是胡雪岩有话问，还是抽出身子来了。
A’qi was extremely busy preparing local gifts for Hu to take away, but at the mention of his request she came out.

“我昨天晚上跟你谈到上海的那批丝，我是怎么跟你说的？”郁四问。
“What did I tell you last night about the silk in Shanghai?” Yu asked.

“你说，那批丝上的本钱，你只当赌铜钿输掉了。
“You said you treated the capital in it as money lost at gambling.

赚了，你不结帐，蚀了，你也睡得着觉。”
If it made a profit, you would not call for a settlement; if it made a loss, you would still sleep soundly.”

听这样一说，胡雪岩既感激，又不安，
Hu felt both grateful and uneasy.

听郁四的口气，大有把那笔本钱奉送之意，这无论如何是受之有愧的。
Yu almost seemed to intend the capital as a gift, which Hu could not accept with an easy conscience.

但此时无需急着表白，
But there was no need to protest at once.

朋友相交不在一日，
Friendship was not measured in a single day.

郁四果有此心，自己倒要争个面子，将来叫他大大地出个意外。
If that was indeed Yu’s intention, Hu would make a special effort and give him a great surprise in the future.

于是他说：“四哥你这样说，我的胆就大了。
“Brother Yu, that gives me courage,” he said.

人生难得遇着知己，
“It is rare to find a friend who understands one.

趁这时候我不好好去闯一闯，也太对不起自己了。”
If I don’t venture out while I have the chance, I shall scarcely be doing myself justice.”


在这一刻，胡雪岩又有了新的主意，
At that moment Hu had a new idea.

但决定等那批丝脱手以后，把郁四名下应得的一份，替他在上海买租界上的地皮。
Once the silk was sold, he would use Yu’s rightful share to buy land for him in the Shanghai settlement.

这是一个突如其来的念头，细细想去，
The thought came suddenly, but it bore examination.

第一，不受炮火的影响，
First, the settlement was protected from bombardment.

各地逃难到上海租界的人，一定会越来越多，市面当然要兴旺，
More and more people would flee there from troubled districts, and its commerce would flourish.

第二，朝廷对洋人不欢迎，但既然订了商约，洋人要来，不欢迎也办不到。
Second, the court might dislike foreigners, but having signed commercial treaties, it could not prevent them from coming.

“五口通商”只有上海这个码头最热闹，
Of the five treaty ports, Shanghai was the busiest.

一旦洪杨战败，逃难的人会相携还乡，但做生意的人，是不会走的。
Once Hong and Yang were defeated, refugees would return home together, but businessmen would remain.

所以，趁现在把上海租界里那些无甚入息，地价便宜的苇塘空地买下来，将来一定会大发其财。
Buying cheap, unproductive reed beds and vacant land in the settlement now would therefore bring a great fortune later.

不过，这是五年、十年以后，
That, however, lay five or ten years ahead.

如果有闲钱无甚用处，不妨买了摆在那里，
A man with idle money could buy land and leave it.

象自己现在这样，急需头寸周转，就不必去打这个主意。
A man like Hu, urgently needing working funds, ought not to consider it for himself.

“老胡！”郁四见他沉吟不语，便即问道：“你在想啥？”
“Old Hu,” Yu asked, noticing his silence, “what are you thinking about?”

“还不是动生意上的脑筋。”
“Business again.”

说了这一句，胡雪岩才想起郁四劝他的话，
Then Hu remembered Yu’s warning.

自然不宜再出花样，
He should not propose another venture now.

因而自己摇着手说：“不谈，不谈。
Waving his hand, he added, “Never mind.

是空想！”
It is only a fancy.”

“不要去多想了！
“Stop thinking so much.

我们吃酒，谈点有趣的事。”
Let’s have a drink and talk of something amusing.”


趣事甚多，
There was plenty to amuse them.

胡雪岩讲了七姑奶奶逛堂子的笑话，把阿七听得出了神。
Hu told the story of the Seventh Lady’s visit to a courtesan house, and A’qi listened spellbound.

郁四也觉得新奇，表示很想会一会这样一个“奇女子”。
Yu too found it remarkable and said he would like to meet such an extraordinary woman.

“那容易得很！”胡雪岩说，
“Easily arranged,” Hu replied.

“只要你抽得出空，我陪你走一遭，
“If you can find the time, I’ll go with you.

尤家兄妹一定也会觉得你很对劲。”
Both Brother You and his sister would certainly take to you.”

“真的，”阿七接口向郁四说，“你也该到外头走走，见见世面。
“Indeed,” A’qi urged Yu, “you ought to go out and see something of the world.

年纪一大把，乐得看开些，吃吃喝喝，四处八方去逛逛，
At your age, why not take things easily, enjoy good food and drink, and travel about?

让我也开开眼界。”
Let me broaden my horizons too.”

这番怂恿把郁四说动了心，
This tempted Yu.

平生足迹不出里门，
He had never traveled beyond his home district.

外面是怎么样的一个花花世界，只听人说，未曾目睹，到底是桩憾事，
All he knew of the colorful world outside came from other people’s stories, which was a real regret.

如果能带着阿七去走一走，会一会江湖上的朋友，也是暮年一大乐事。
Traveling with A’qi and meeting friends from the brotherhood would be a great pleasure in his later years.

只是怎么能抽得出身。
But how could he free himself?

因此，他又想到衙门里的差使，要找个替手这件大事，
He thought again of finding a replacement for his work at the yamen.

“老胡，”他毫不考虑地问了出来，“上次我跟你谈过的，想叫小和尚来当差，你可曾问过他？”
“Old Hu,” he asked without hesitation, “did you ask Little Monk about taking the post I mentioned last time?”

“还不曾问。”
“Not yet.”

胡雪岩心想，陈世龙大概不会愿意，
Hu thought Chen would probably be unwilling.

而且有阿七在，陈世龙也实在不宜过分接近郁家，
Moreover, with A’qi there, it would be unwise for Chen to become too closely involved with the Yu household.

再为自己打算，也难放手，
Hu’s own affairs would also make it hard to spare him.

所以索性再加一句：“我想不问也罢。
He therefore added, “I think there is no need to ask.

我看他十之八九不肯！”
I am almost certain he would refuse.”

“那就算了。”郁四偶惘地说，
“Then let it drop,” Yu said disconsolately.

“我另外物色。”
“I’ll look for someone else.”


这两句对答，使得阿七深为注意，
A’qi listened closely to this exchange.

在过去，如果谈到陈世龙，她立刻会插嘴来问，
In the past, she would immediately have asked questions whenever Chen was mentioned.

但自从有了那两番私晤，倾诉心曲的经历，变得“做贼心虚”，在郁四面前，处处要避嫌疑，
But since their two private meetings, when she had poured out her feelings, she had developed a guilty conscience and took care to avoid suspicion in Yu’s presence.

所以当时不敢搭腔，
She dared not join in then.

过后才找个机会，悄悄问胡雪岩是怎么回事？
Only later did she find an opportunity to ask Hu privately what it was about.

胡雪岩也正要这样一个单独相处的机会，好问她一个明白，
Hu had wanted just such a chance to question her.

因而说明其事以后，紧接着便是这样一句：“郁四嫂，我有句话，不晓得能不能问？
After explaining, he went straight on: “Sister-in-Law Yu, there is something I do not know whether I may ask.

问了伯你不高兴，不问，我心里总不安稳。
If I ask, I fear you will be displeased; if I don’t, I cannot feel at ease.

真正不知道该怎么办？”
I really do not know what to do.”

阿七是很聪明、也很爽荡的人，
A’qi was intelligent and candid.

微微红着脸说：“我晓得你要问的是啥？
Blushing slightly, she said, “I know what you want to ask.

那件事我做错了。
I was wrong about that.

不过当时并不晓得做错。
But at the time I did not know it was wrong.”

““这话怎么说？”
“What do you mean?”

胡雪岩觉得她的话，很有意味，
Hu found this interesting.

“是你跟郁四哥讲和以后，才晓得自己错了？”
“Did you realize you were wrong only after making peace with Brother Yu?”

“是的！”阿七羞涩地一笑，别具妩媚之姿，
“Yes.” She gave a shy, charming smile.

“想想还是老头子好，
“When I think it over, the old man is best after all.

样样依我，
He gives way to me in everything.
