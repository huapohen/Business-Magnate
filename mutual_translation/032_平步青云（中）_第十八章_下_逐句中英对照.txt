陈世龙打听到了刘不才的住处，一径就寻上门去，
Chen Shilong found out where Liu Bucai lived and went straight there.

他跟嵇鹤龄一样，也是祖了一家式微世家的余屋住，不过另外开了个门，
Like Ji Heling, Liu had rented spare rooms in the house of an old family fallen on hard times, though his quarters had a separate entrance.

敲了两下，有个眉清目秀，但十分瘦的孩子来开门，
After two knocks, a fine-featured but very thin boy opened the door.

转着乌黑的一双眼珠问道：
His dark eyes turned towards Chen.

“你找谁？”
“Who are you looking for?”

陈世龙听胡雪岩谈过，猜想他必是芙蓉的弟弟，
Remembering Hu Xueyan's account, Chen guessed that this must be Furong's brother.

随即说道：“小兔儿，你三叔呢？”
“Little Rabbit, where is your Third Uncle?”

“在里头。”
“Inside.”

等陈世龙要踏进去，他却堵着门不放，
But when Chen tried to enter, the boy blocked the doorway.

“你不要进来，
“Don't come in.

先告诉我，你姓啥？”
Tell me your name first.”

“怎么？”陈世龙答道，
“Why?

“你怕是我跟你三叔来讨债的？
Are you afraid I've come to collect a debt from your uncle?

不是，不是！
No, no!

我姓陈，
My name is Chen.

送钱来给你三叔的。
I've come to bring him money.”

“小兔儿有些将信将疑，但毕竟还是让步了。
Little Rabbit remained doubtful, but at last let him through.

陈世龙一进门就觉得香味扑鼻，不由得咽了口唾沫，
At once a delicious aroma made Chen swallow.

仔细辨一辨味道，是炖火腿的香味。
He recognised the scent of stewing ham.

“这家伙，真会享福！”
“The fellow certainly knows how to enjoy himself!”

一句话未完，看见刘不才的影子，哼着戏踱了出来，
Before he had finished, Liu appeared, strolling out and humming an operatic tune.

身上穿一件旧湖绪棉袄。下面是黑洋绉扎脚裤，两只裤脚扎得极其挺括，显得极有精神。
He wore an old padded jacket of Hu silk and black imported-crepe trousers, their ankles tied with such neat precision that he looked remarkably brisk.


“小和尚！
“Little Monk!

想不到是你。”
I didn't expect you.”

“刘三爷！待为来跟你老人家请安。”
“Third Master Liu, I've come especially to pay my respects.”

过于谦恭，反成戏谑，刘不才便骂：“去你的，
Excessive humility turned the greeting into mockery, and Liu retorted, “Away with you!

寻什么穷开心！”
Why make fun of a poor man?”

“不是这话。”陈世龙答道，
“That isn't what I mean.

“从前叫你刘不才，
I used to call you Liu Bucai.

如今不同了，
Now things are different.

你变成我的长辈，规矩不能不讲。”
You've become my elder, and I must observe the proper forms.”

“咦！”
“Well!”

刘不才眨着眼说，
Liu blinked.

“我倒没有想到，忽然爆出来的这么个晚辈！
“I never expected a younger relation to spring up so suddenly.

是怎么来的，你说来听听！
Tell me, where have you come from?”

““你跟我先生结成亲戚，
“You've become related to my teacher.

不就是我的长辈？”
Doesn't that make you my elder?”

刘不才愣了一下，换了副傲慢的神色：
Liu paused, then assumed an arrogant expression.

“我不晓得你的先生是哪个？
“I don't know who your teacher is.

反正我最近没有跟什么人结亲，
In any case, I haven't recently formed a marriage connection with anyone.

谦称奉壁，
I return the honour you offer.

蜗居也不足以容大驾，
My humble dwelling cannot accommodate your distinguished presence.

请！”
This way, please!”

说着将手向外一指，竟下了逐客令。
Pointing outside, he ordered his guest to leave.

陈世龙有些发窘，但当然不能翻脸，
Chen felt embarrassed, but could hardly quarrel.

在平时，翻脸就翻脸，也无所谓，
Ordinarily he might have done so without concern.

此刻是奉命差遣，
Today he had been sent on a mission.

不能不忍一忍，同时还得想办法让刘三才取消逐客令。
He must endure it and find some way to make Liu Sancai withdraw the dismissal.


于是他尽量装出自然的笑容，
He forced the most natural smile he could.

“刘三爷，你真不够朋友，
“Third Master Liu, you're no friend!

炖着那么好吃的东西，一个人享用，好意思？
Stewing something so delicious and keeping it all to yourself—aren't you ashamed?

莫非，”他说，“你不想在赌场里见面了？”
Or don't you expect to meet me at the gambling table again?”

提到赌场，刘三才的气焰一挫。
Mention of gambling weakened Liu's defiance.

彼此的交情虽不深，但输了就顾不到体面、曾有两三次向陈世龙伸手借过赌本，
They were not close friends, but after losing, he had forgotten his dignity and borrowed gambling money from Chen two or three times.

想起这点情分，也是话柄，他的脸板不成了。
Remembering the obligation—and the hold it gave Chen—he could no longer maintain his stern face.

“要怪你自己不知趣！
“Blame your own tactlessness!

‘哪壶水不开，偏提哪一壶’，
You always pick up the kettle that won't boil.

你晓得我讨厌我那个侄女儿，你偏要拿她来触我的心境，
You know I can't stand that niece of mine, yet you bring her up to provoke me.

叫人光火不光火？”
How could I help being angry?”

“好了，好了，
“Very well.

说过算数。
Let that be the end of it.

如果你留我吃饭，你出菜，我出酒。
If you'll keep me to dinner, you provide the food and I'll provide the drink.

小兔儿，你来！”
Little Rabbit, come here!”

陈世龙摸出块五六钱的碎银子问道：

“你会不会上街买东西？”
“Can you go shopping?”

“你要买什么？”刘不才问。
“What do you want to buy?” Liu asked.

“巷口那家酒店的‘绍烧，我吃过，
“I've tried the Shaoxing spirit at the shop by the lane entrance.

不坏，
It's good.

叫他们送两斤来，把酒钱带去给他。”
Ask them to send two jin and take the money.”

说着，他把银子塞到小兔儿子里，
He put the silver into the boy's hand.

“多下的送你买梨膏糖吃！”
“Keep the change for pear-syrup sweets!”

“没有要你破费的道理！”
“There's no reason for you to spend money!”

刘不才赶上来插在他跟小兔儿中间，一只手到他侄儿手里去夺银子，一只手又推陈世龙，仿佛不让他给钱似地。
Liu hurried between them, reaching with one hand for the silver and pushing Chen with the other, as though preventing payment.

这就象下馆子抢着惠帐，只拉住了别人的不管用的左手一样，完全是“障眼法”。
It was pure sleight of hand, like pretending to fight over a restaurant bill while holding only the other man's unoccupied left hand.

结果是那块碎银子到了刘不才手里，却叫小兔儿到酒店里去赊帐。
The silver ended up in Liu's possession, while Little Rabbit was sent to obtain the liquor on credit.

从这个行为上，陈世龙看透了他；
Chen saw through him.

骨头硬不到哪里去！
His backbone was not so stiff after all!

他跟芙蓉也决不会决裂。
He would never truly break with Furong either.


“来，来！”
“Come along!”

刘不才的兴致又很好了，
Liu's spirits had recovered.

把沙锅盖一揭，鼻子闻了两下，得意的笑道：
Lifting the earthenware pot's lid, he sniffed twice and smiled proudly.

“ ‘走得着，谢双脚’，
“‘Thank your feet for bringing you here.’

你的口福不坏！
You're fortunate in your food!

陈火腿全靠收拾得干净，
With aged ham, everything depends on cleaning it properly.

整整搞了一上午，才把上面的毛钳干净。”
I've spent the whole morning plucking every hair from it.”

“刘三爷！”陈世龙趁机说道，“你的陈火腿吃不光！
“Third Master Liu,” Chen seized the opening, “you'll have more aged ham than you can eat.

我今天来拉拢一桩生意。”
I've come to bring you a business opportunity.”

“生意？”
“Business?”

刘不才不信他，
Liu was unconvinced.

“怎么找到我头上？
“Why come to me?

跟我有啥生意好谈？”
What business could we discuss?”

自然有！
“There is some.

等下我再告诉你。
I'll tell you shortly.”

“等酒杯一端上手，陈世龙才道明来意，
Only when they had their cups in hand did Chen explain.

他说他有个朋友，预备在杭州开一家极大的药店，
A friend intended to open a very large medicine shop in Hangzhou.

知道“刘敬德堂”的名气，也知道刘不才是行家，特地托他来探问一下，想邀刘不才合伙。
Knowing Liu Jingde Hall's reputation and Liu's expertise, he had asked Chen to inquire whether Liu would become his partner.

“合伙？
“A partner?

怎么合法？”
On what terms?”

刘不才摇着头说，
Liu shook his head.

“别的事都好谈，这件事谈不拢，
“We can discuss anything else, but this won't work.

我哪里有股本？”
Where would I find capital?”

“你不是有几张祖传的药方子？”
“Haven't you several medicinal formulas handed down in your family?”

这话一说出口，刘不才的脸色顿时就很难看了，
Liu's expression darkened immediately.

笑容尽敛，
His smile vanished.

冷冷笑道：“原来是打我这个主意！
“So that's what you're after!

怪道，
No wonder!

我说世界上还有这样子的好人，不嫌我穷，来邀我台伙！”
I thought it strange that someone should be kind enough to overlook my poverty and invite me into partnership.”


话和神色，都让陈世龙忍不住心头火发，
His words and manner angered Chen.

“咦！”他也很不客气地回敬：
“Well!” he replied just as sharply.

“怪道叫你刘不才！
“No wonder people call you Liu Bucai!

‘狗咬吕侗宾，不识好人心’，
Like the dog that bit Lü Dongbin, you don't recognise kindness.

怎见得人家打你那几张药方的主意？
What makes you think he wants to steal your formulas?

你晓得人家是怎么说？”
Do you know what he actually proposes?”

“且慢！”
“Wait.”

刘不才的态度变得受商量了，
Liu became more amenable.

“我先问一声，想跟我合伙的是哪一个？
“First, who is this would-be partner?

是不是姓胡的？”
Is his surname Hu?”

陈世龙很机警，趁机反问一句：
Chen promptly turned the question back on him.

“你见过我那位胡先生没有？”
“Have you ever met my Mr Hu?”

“从来不曾见过。”
“Never.”

“那我告诉你，”
“Then let me tell you.”

陈世龙既不说破，也不否认，
Chen neither confirmed nor denied the identity.

“此人是个候补知县，在官场中很红，
“This man is an expectant county magistrate, very well regarded in official circles.

本人虽不出面，却有好些差使跟他有关系。
Though he doesn't appear in person, he is connected with many public appointments.

他要开药店也不光是为了做生意，
He wants a medicine shop for more than trade.

是存心济世……”
His intention is to help the world...”

“好了，好了！”刘不才不屑地，
“Enough!” Liu said scornfully.

“ ‘修合虽无人见，存心自有天知’，
“‘Though none see the compounding, Heaven knows the heart.’

药店里挂的这副对子，是啥花样，难道我还不知道？
Do you think I don't understand that couplet hanging in medicine shops?

何必到我面前来卖这种膏药？”
Why try to sell me that remedy?”

“不是我在你面前卖膏药，
“I'm not selling you anything.

人家这么告诉我，我照本宣科，
I'm merely repeating what I was told.

信不信在你！”
Believe it or not, as you please.”

“闲话少说，
“Spare me the talk.

他做生意也好，存心济世也好，与我无关。
Whether he wants profit or to help the world is nothing to me.

如说要邀我合伙，看中我那几张祖传秘方，请他趁早少打主意。”
If the partnership means getting hold of my family's secret formulas, tell him to abandon the idea.”

“你为来为去是怕方子落在人家手里，
“It all comes down to your fear of the formulas falling into someone else's hands.

你要晓得，人家并不要你的什么宝贝方子！”
You should know that he doesn't want possession of your precious recipes!”

“那……”
“Then...”

刘不才愕然，不知这话从何说起了。
Liu was astonished and could not understand.


于是陈世龙转述了合伙的办法，
Chen explained the arrangement.

刘不才的祖传秘方，当然要用，可是不要求他把方子公开，
Naturally the inherited formulas would be used, but Liu would not have to disclose them.

将来开了药店，清他以股东的身分在店里坐镇，这几张方子上的药，请他自己修合。
Once the shop opened, he would work there as a partner and compound those medicines himself.

“君臣佐使”是哪几味药？分量多少？如何炮制？只有他自己知道，
Which ingredients served as sovereign, minister, assistant, and envoy, their quantities, and the methods of preparation would be known to him alone.

何虑秘方泄漏？
Why fear the secrets leaking out?

原来人家不是来图谋自己的秘方，刘不才倒觉得刚才的态度，未免鲁莽，
Realising that nobody meant to take his formulas, Liu felt he had been rash.

因而歉意地点点头：

“这倒还可以谈谈！”
“That is something we could discuss.”

“我再告诉你，人家提出来的条件，合情合理，
“And the terms are reasonable.

药归你去台，价钱由人家来定，你抽成头。

你的药灵，销得好，你的成头就多，

If they don't work and nobody wants them, then, I'm sorry, you take your precious formulas and pack your bags.”

“药怎么会不灵？
“How could they fail to work?

尤其是一种‘狗皮膏药，明朝的一个皇帝，靠了它才生的太子，
There's one dogskin plaster that enabled a Ming emperor to father a crown prince.

真正是无价之宝！”
It's priceless!”

“吹什么牛！”陈世龙笑道：
“What boasting!” Chen laughed.

“刘敬德堂的狗皮膏药，哪个不晓得，
“Everyone knows Liu Jingde Hall's dogskin plaster.

完全是骗人的东西！”
It's a complete fraud!”

“这你就不懂了！
“You don't understand.

老实告诉你，方于是真的，药太贵重，而合起来交关麻烦，
Honestly, the formula is genuine, but the ingredients are terribly costly and the preparation very troublesome.

只好马马虎虎，效验当然就差了。
We had to cut corners, so naturally its effects were poorer.

这且不去说它！”
Never mind that now.”

刘不才把腰挺一挺，双手靠在桌上，凑近陈世龙，显得相当认真地说：
Liu straightened, braced his hands on the table, and leaned earnestly towards Chen.

“这位老朋友说的话很上路，
“Your friend speaks like someone who knows his way about.

看起来决不是半吊子。

他的办法在我有益无损，

可进可退，

“我先要问一句，价钱为啥要归他定？

“这没有商量的余地，

“这话也对。
“That's true.

“不过，这都好商量，

“I've decided to make this man's acquaintance.

The sooner the better!”

“刘三爷！你还有什么话，自己不便说，
“Third Master Liu, is there anything you find awkward to say?

我可以替你转达。

刘不才原就想开口，听陈世龙这一说，恰中下怀，

我的境况，你是晓得的，

“I understand.

“你身上有多少债务？”
How much debt do you have?”

“Only a few hundred taels.”

“不必！
“No need.

I'll take him to Hangzhou.”

“I see.”

陈世龙站起身来说，

“那么，我先去告诉人家，
“Then I'll report to him first.

什么时候碰头，我明天一早来给你回音。”

一夜过去，刘不才起来得特别早。

他家里不象样，“出客”的衣服，依旧很漂亮，
His home might be shabby, but his visiting clothes remained splendid.

不但料子，连花样都有讲究，

一件铁灰摹本缎的袍子，松竹梅的暗花，

For an afternoon outing, he had another robe on which they were in full bloom.

打扮好了，在家坐等陈世龙的回音。
Dressed, he sat waiting for Chen's message.

At nine, someone knocked.

刘不才亲自去开门一看，不由得愣住了，门外两顶轿子四个人，一个老妈子，一个丫头，一个是极艳丽的少妇，还有一个是自己的侄女儿！

“三叔！”
“Third Uncle!”

穿着红裙的芙容，叫了一声，
Furong, wearing a red skirt, called to him.

不等他应声，便回身为那少妇引见：
Without waiting for his reply, she turned to introduce her companion.

“这位是郁太太，
“This is Mrs Yu.

这是我三叔！”
This is my Third Uncle.”

郁太太自然是阿七，
Mrs Yu was naturally A’qi.

当时盈盈含笑地喊道：
With a graceful smile, she greeted him.

“刘三爷！”
