CHAPTER NINETEEN｜第十九章


Spending every day at leisure could not go on.
这样老是玩不是事。

What tormented Liu Bucai most was having nothing to do.
刘不才最感苦恼的是，无事可做，

His fingers itched, and he kept wanting to gamble.
手会发痒，老想赌钱，

But whenever the thought arose, he remembered what he had told Chen Shilong and fought it down.
但每一转到这个念头，随即想起自己对陈世龙说过的话，拼命压制着。

After ten days of this, he could bear it no longer.
如是十天下来，他实在忍不住了。

What he could no longer bear was waiting for Hu Xueyan to tell him something definite.
忍不住的是要胡雪岩说句话，

He waited another two days and finally caught him on the third.
等了两天，到第三天终于把胡雪岩等到了。

“Xueyan!” he said with some agitation.
“雪岩！”他有些激动，

“I’ve been here more than half a month and haven’t done a thing.
“来了半个多月，什么事也没有做，

I know you’re busy, but if this goes on I shall fall ill from boredom.”
我也晓得你事情忙，不过，这样子下去，我要闷出病来了！”

“I know, I know!
“我晓得，我晓得！

I’m terribly sorry.
实在对不起，

There were matters in several places that I had to attend to myself.
几处的事情，都非我亲自料理不可。

Most are now in hand.
现在大致有了头绪，

Above all, the transfer of cargo for sea transport has at last been settled.
尤其海运转驳，总算办妥当了。

I can spare some time.
我可以抽得出工夫来，

Tomorrow we shall begin by looking at sites.” Then Hu asked, “Third Uncle, how much can you drink?”
明天开始，我们第一步就是去看地皮。“胡雪岩问道，”三叔，你酒量怎么样？

“I can hold my own.”
““还可以对付。”

“Then let me introduce you to a friend.”
“那么，我先给你介绍一个朋友。”


The friend was Qiu Fengyan.
他介绍的是裘丰言。

He had carried out the escort of the foreign guns very efficiently.
押运洋枪的差使，裘丰言办得很妥当，

Wang Youling had given him a sum of money and praised him warmly, so he had lately been in excellent spirits.
王有龄送了他一笔钱，看实夸奖了一番，所以他最近的心境极好，

He and Liu took to one another at once.
跟刘不才一见如故，

Moreover, Hu had asked Qiu to look after him, and Qiu made a special effort to please.
加以受了胡雪岩的委托，刻意敷衍，

Liu felt that acquiring such a friend was something to be happy about.
因而刘不才也觉得交了裘丰言这个朋友，是件很可以叫人高兴的事。

Qiu therefore undertook to accompany him in looking at land.
陪着看地皮的事，便由裘丰言来承当，

Every morning they began with tea at a teahouse by Fengle Bridge.
每天一早到丰乐桥茶馆里喝茶。

Qiu had lived in Yangzhou and was particular about his morning meal.
裘丰言在扬州住过，早晨这一顿很讲究，

He ordered two stir-fried dishes to go with his early wine, followed by noodles.
炒两个菜吃早酒，酒罢吃面，

Then a broker led them out to inspect sites.
然后由赔客领着去看地皮，

Some were too small; elsewhere they could not agree on the price.
有的嫌小，有的价钱不合，

By the time they had weighed one consideration against another, it was three in the afternoon, and Qiu wanted tea and wine again.
这样一番折中下来，到了下午三点钟，裘丰言又要喝茶吃酒了。

With his company, Liu was no longer so bored.
刘不才因为有他作陪，不如以前那样无聊，

The days passed peaceably, and his desire to gamble subsided.
倒也相安无事，把想赌的念头歇了下来。


One morning Hu unexpectedly came to see Liu and began, “Third Uncle, I need you to entertain a guest.
突然间有一天，胡雪岩一大早来找刘不才，第一句话就是：“三叔，我要请你陪一位客，

He knows everything about women, gambling, fine food and clothes.
这位客嫖赌吃着，无所不精，

You are the only man who can keep him company.”
只有你可以陪他。”

For a moment Liu could not answer.
刘不才一时开不得口，

First, the request was unexpected.
第一，觉得突兀，

Second, it contradicted Hu’s original intention: he had wanted Liu to give up gambling, and now was asking him to gamble.
第二，觉得胡雪岩违反了他自己的来意，本来要求人家戒赌的，此刻倒转头来，请人去赌，

Third, Liu had promised to stop and had actually done so.
第三，觉得自己说了戒赌，而且真的已经戒掉，

To break that resolution now, throwing away his hard-won determination and self-control, seemed a pity.
却又开戒，这番来之不易的决心和毅力，轻易付之东流，未免可惜。

“Third Uncle,” Hu said seriously, “have no misgivings.
“三叔！”胡雪岩正色说道，“你心里不要嘀咕，

This is precisely where I need your help.
这些地方就是我要请你帮忙的。

To put it plainly, I am making use of your strengths.”
说得再痛快一点，这也就是我用你的长处。”

There was nothing more to say.
那就没话好说了，

“If it will help you, of course I shall do it.
“既然是帮你的忙，我自然照办。”

But first you must explain what it is all about.”
刘不才问，“不过是怎么一回事，你先得跟我说清楚。”

Hu hesitated.
胡雪岩略微踌躇了一下，

“It is a long story, with complications I cannot explain in a moment.”
“说来话长，其中有点曲折，一时也说不清楚。”

He paused.
他停了停又说：

“In a word, if we keep this young gentleman amused, it will be a great help to my business.”
“总而言之一句话，除这位公子哥儿玩得高兴了，对我的生意大有帮助。”

“Mm, I understand.
“嗯，嗯！我懂了，

You want me to be his companion and entertainer?”
你要请我做清客？”

“Not a dependent companion—a wealthy guest.
“不是做清客，是做阔客。

Of course, a wealthy guest who also entertains him will be all the more effective!”
当然，以阔客做这位公子哥儿的清客，不就更加够味道了！”

Only now did Liu truly understand.
这一下，刘不才方始真的懂了，

Nodding gravely, he said, “Provided you don’t mind the expense, I know how to play the rich man, and how to make friends with rich men.”
点点头很沉重地道：“只要你不心疼，摆阔我会，结交阔客我也会。”


“Of course!
“自然！

Why should I mind the expense?
怎么谈得到心疼的话？

Third Uncle, what is the most you have ever lost at one sitting?”
三叔，”胡雪岩问，“你一场赌，最多输过多少？”

“I once lost...” Liu said.
“输过……”刘不才说，

“I lost a pawnshop.
“输过一爿当店，

Not a large one.
规模不大，

It was worth thirty thousand taels.”
折算三万银子。”

“Good.
“好的，

You have seen high play.
你经过大场面。

That will do.
那就行了！”

Have no worries: I can put thirty or fifty thousand taels in ready silver into your hands.
胡雪岩说，“你不必顾虑，三五万银子，我捧现银给你，

More would be no trouble either.
再多也不要紧，

I can raise it whenever it is needed.
我随时都调得动。

Losing does not matter.
总之，输不要紧，

What matters is never to look small-minded or mean.”
千万不能露出小家子气的样子来！”

“You can trust me there.
“这你放心好了，

At the gaming table my nerve is second to none.” They agreed that Hu would return that afternoon and accompany him to meet the young gentleman, bringing banknotes then.
赌上头，我的胆子最大。”当时约定，胡雪岩下午来陪他去结交那位公子哥儿，银票在那时带来。

Liu went off in high spirits to have his head shaved and dressed himself as an elegant man of the world, then sat waiting.
刘不才便也精神抖擞地去剃了头，打扮成个翩翩浊世公子的样子，在那里坐等。

Shortly after noon Hu returned.
午后不久，胡雪岩又来了，

Liu wore a grey-squirrel fur robe faced with iron-grey satin, a maroon Baturu waistcoat, and a little black satin cap with a coral knot on top and a piece of green jade carved with the longevity character at the front.
看刘不才穿的是铁灰色缎面的灰鼠皮袍，枣红色巴图鲁坎肩，头戴一顶珊瑚结子的玄色缎子的小帽，正中镶着一块寿字纹的碧玉。

There was a snow-white spun-silk jacket, crisply fitted trousers bound at the ankles, and a pair of double-ridged Zhangzhou velvet shoes.
雪白的纺绸褂子，下面是笔挺的扎脚裤和一双漳绒的双梁鞋。

“Splendid!
“漂亮得很！

I have brought two things that will go perfectly with your clothes.”
我有两样东西带了来，正好配你这一身打扮。”

One was a gold repeating watch on a very thick gold chain; the other, a thumb ring of mutton-fat white jade.
那两佯东西是一个金打簧表，带着恨极粗的金链子，一个羊脂白玉的班指。

There were also twenty thousand taels in banknotes, none smaller than five hundred taels.
另外有两万银票，起码是五百两一张。


“It is still early.
“时候还早，

Let me tell you who this rich young gentleman is.”
我先把这个阔少的来历告诉你。”

His surname was Pang.
这位阔少姓庞，

Hu had met him during his two days in Nanxun, and everyone called him Second Master Pang.
是胡雪岩到南得去的那两夭认识的，大家都叫他庞二爷。

His family had been in the silk trade for generations, accumulating wealth; then, when foreign trade opened late in the Daoguang reign, they had made large profits in the export business.
这位庞二爷是丝业世家，几代蓄积，再加上道光末年中外通商，在洋庄上很赚了些，

Even if their fortune did not rival a state’s, its depth was far beyond what outsiders imagined.
所以虽不是富堪敌国，而殷厚之处，远非外人所能想象。

Second Master Pang was an idler of the first rank, but his family upbringing had given him a thorough knowledge of business.
庞二爷虽然是一等一的纨袴，但家学渊源，做生意极其在行，

That was why Hu wanted to deal with him.
此所以胡雪岩要跟他打交道。

He had purchased the rank of circuit intendant, though naturally he would never wait at an official headquarters for an appointment or an assignment.
庞二爷是个捐班的道台，自然不会“辕门听鼓”去候补等差使，

He did not ordinarily wear official dress.
平常也不穿官服，

But if any prefectural or county official put on airs before him, Pang could outdo them all in official grandeur.
但如果有什么州县官在他面前，以官派骄人，那一下他摆出来的官派，比什么人都足，

That alone showed his character: he could be won by courtesy, but never bullied.
就从这一点上，把庞二爷吃软不吃硬的性情，完全显出来了。

So that was the man!
原来是他！

Liu listened and considered.
刘不才一面听，一面心里在想。

As a fellow Huzhou man, he naturally knew of Second Master Pang, but among wealthy young gentlemen he himself had stood at least two grades lower.
同是湖州人，他自然知道庞二爷，不过论“少爷班子”的等级，刘不才起码要比他差两等。

Now he had fallen on hard times as well.
而且现在已经“落薄”了，

To be introduced as “the third son of the old Liu Jingde Hall family” was hardly glorious.
提起来，说是“当年刘敬德堂的老三”，这句话并不见得光彩，

He could not help wondering beforehand what Pang would think.
庞二爷心里作何感想，却不能不预先顾虑。


“Third Uncle,” Hu continued, “to draw Second Master Pang closer, I specially asked Prefect Wang to give this party.
“三叔，”胡雪岩接下来说，“为了拉拢庞二爷，我特地托王大老爷出面请客，

He is your district’s governing official.
他是你们湖州的父母官，

However busy Pang may be, he cannot refuse.
庞二爷再忙也不能不到。

Today is only a dinner, so the gaming will not be on a very large scale—just some mahjong.
不过今天只是为了请客吃饭，‘场头，拉不大，只不过打打麻将。

You must show your skill and make him want a second game with you after the first.
你要拿本事出来，让他跟你赌过一场，还愿意跟你赌第二场，

That is how the friendship will grow.”
这样子交情才可以越拉越拢。”

“I understand.
“我晓得了。

You can rely on me for that.
这一点你放心！

But...”
不过，”

Liu found it difficult to say.
刘不才很吃力地说，

“We have never met, and he spends most of his time in Shanghai, but he probably knows who I am.”
“我们虽没有会过，他是在上海的时候多，大概总也晓得我这个人。”

“That does not matter.
“ 晓得也不要紧，

A prodigal who reforms is worth more than gold.
‘败子回头金不换，，

Nobody will laugh at you.
没有哪个笑话你！

Besides, I have spoken to Prefect Wang.
再说，我跟王大老爷关照过了，

He will treat you with particular courtesy.
对你会特别客气，

With the host showing you such respect, nobody will be able to fathom your circumstances.”
有主人抬举着，人家也识不透你的底细。”

Hearing this, Liu looked down at his finery and then at the twenty thousand taels in notes.
刘不才听了他的话，看一看自己那身装柬，再看一看那两万银票，

His outlook changed.
想法变过了，

Everything else might be a pretence, but money was real.
什么都可以假，银子不假，

Money gave a man courage.
钱就是胆，

What was there to fear?
怕什么！

“You are right, Xueyan.”
“雪岩，你的话不错。”

His spirits rose.
他精神抖擞地问，

“When do we leave?”
“我们什么时候走？”

He opened the repeating watch.
说着，便打开那只打簧表，

It was only two in the afternoon.
一看才午后两点钟。

“We are expected at four.
“约的是四点，

Naturally I must arrive early.
我自然要早到。

Rest a little longer and come to Mr Wang’s residence at the appointed time.”
你再养养神，准时到王公馆好了。”

Hu left a slip of paper bearing the address.
胡雪岩留下一张纸条，上面写着王家的地址。

With this agreed, they parted.
约定了各自分手。

Liu duly reclined on a couch and closed his eyes, carefully considering Pang’s temperament and how best to approach him.
刘不才果然靠在一张软榻上，闭目养神，把庞二爷的脾气作了一番很周详的考虑，然后又细想应付的态度。

Feeling fairly confident, he opened his eyes cheerfully, made a few further adjustments to his appearance and hired a small chair to take him to the party.
自己觉得颇有把握，欣然睁眼，重新又修饰了一番，方始雇一顶小轿，专程赴约。


At the Wang residence the host was indeed most courteous, addressing him at every turn as “Brother Sancai.”
到了王家，主人果然很客气，口口声声称他“三才兄”，

They sat exchanging civilities while the other guests arrived.
坐下寒暄了一阵，请的客人陆续都到了，

Apart from Ji Heling and Qiu Fengyan, there were two wealthy young men.
除了嵇鹤龄和裘丰言，另外两个都是阔少，

One was Zhou Wu, fifth in his family and a younger brother of the former Tianjin customs intendant.
一个是做过天津海关道的周道台的弟弟，行五，

The other was Gao Si, the fourth son of a family whose members combined official service with commerce.
一个是亦官亦商的高家老四。

Their conversation had no serious purpose.
坐下来言不及义，

Either they spoke of how much they had lost at pai gow, or of some extraordinary beauty newly appeared aboard the Jiangshan pleasure boats.
不是说一顿牌九输了多少，就是谈“江山船”上出了怎么样的一个尤物。

Second Master Pang arrived last.
最后，庞二爷到了，

He was thirty-four or thirty-five, with a broad, pale face like a silver basin, resembling Cao Cao on the stage.
三十四五岁年纪，一张银盆大脸，赛似戏台上的曹操。

His grandfather had recently died, so mourning restrictions obliged him to wear a plain grey-cloth robe lined with sheepskin.
因为祖父死了不久，有限制在身，只穿一件灰布羊皮袍，

Yet on his hand was an “oil diamond” ring full of fire.
但手上戴一只翻头十足的“火油钻”戒指，

He wore the stone turned inwards; whenever he raised his hand, flashes from his palm caught the eye all the more.
戒面朝里，偶尔扬手之间，掌中光芒乱闪，格外引人注目。

The host made the introductions.
主人一一引见，

Only Ji, Qiu and Liu were new acquaintances for Pang.
庞二爷初见面的只是嵇鹤龄、裘丰言和刘不才。

Hearing Liu’s Huzhou accent, he warmed to him.
听到他是胡州口音，便觉亲热，

“Brother Liu,” he asked, “where do you live?
“刘三哥，”他问，“你府上哪里？

How is it that we have never met?”
我怎么没育见过？”

Liu gave his address.
刘不才声明住处，

“I have long heard of you, Second Master Pang.
接着又说：“久仰庞二爷的大名，

I am delighted to make your acquaintance.”
幸会之至。”

“The pleasure is mutual.”
“彼此，彼此！”

Pang was courteous, without the airs of a grand young idler. “Come, come!” cried the impatient Zhou.
庞二也很客气，不象有架子的纨袴.“喂，喂！”周老五性子最急，

“Time to play!”
“该上场了！”

Their host led them into a side room, where a mahjong table had been prepared.
于是主子引寻，进入厢房，里面已摆好一桌麻将牌在那里，

They stood discussing who should join.
站着商议入局，

Pang, Zhou and Gao were obvious choices.
庞、周、高三人是用不着说的，

For the fourth place, the host deferred to Ji, Ji to Liu, and Liu to Hu.
剩下一个搭子，主人让嵇鹤龄，嵇鹤龄让刘不才，刘不才让胡雪岩，

Hu declined, settling the matter: Liu would play.
胡雪岩一推辞，便即定局，仍由刘不才上场。


They drew seats and agreed on “one-two” mahjong, a base stake of ten thousand taels, a forty-point base score and sixteen rounds.
扳好位于坐定，讲好一万银子一底的“幺二”，四十和底十六圈，

Soon the tiles were clattering.
随即噼噼啪啪打了起来。

Liu was in no hurry to go out; first he studied the others’ play.
刘不才先不忙着和牌，细看各人的牌路，

Pang and Gao were both highly skilful, but Gao had one weakness: he liked building elaborate hands.
庞二和高四都打得很精，但高四有个毛病，喜欢做牌，

Zhou played as impatiently as he did everything else, taking any winning hand, large or small.
周五打牌跟他的脾气一样，性子急，不问大小，见牌就和，

Once he needed only one tile, he turned his tiles face down and stared into the pool of discards as though longing to snatch out the one he wanted.
一等张便把脾扣了下来，两眼瞪着“湖”里，恨不得拣一张来和牌似地。

Yet bad player though he was, luck favoured him.
然而牌虽打得蹩脚，手气却是他好。

In four rounds he made two all-one-suit hands and a Three Dragons hand.
四圈牌下来，和了两副清一色，一副三元，

He had won nearly a full base stake and was delighted.
已经赢了将近一底，把他高兴得不得了。

“All because Gao insists on building big hands and discards so freely,” Pang said coolly as he threw the dice to change seats.
“这都是老四做牌做得太厉害，张子太松！”庞二一面掷骰子扳位，一面冷冷地说，

“If you sit below me this time, watch out!”
“这回圈如果你坐我下家，可要当心一点儿！”

As it turned out, Liu sat immediately before Zhou, with Gao before him and Pang opposite.
结果刘不才坐了周五的上家，他的上家是高四，跟庞二对面。

Gao persisted in his old habit: if seven of his thirteen tiles belonged to the same suit, he aimed for an all-one-suit hand.
高四老脾气不改，十三张牌只要七张花色一样，就想做清一色，

His discards were therefore still generous.
所以张子仍旧很松。

Liu decided he must not claim too many of them.
刘不才心想，不能多吃，

Otherwise his own discards would become loose, allowing Zhou to profit; once Zhou’s luck gained momentum, it would be hard to check.
不然自己的张子也会松，让周五捡了便宜，手风一上去就很难制了。

He refused even tiles that would complete an edge or a gap in a sequence and concentrated on the player after him, keeping Zhou firmly pinned down.
打定这个主意，连边嵌都不吃，全神贯注在下家，把周五钉得死死地，

Within two rounds, Zhou had lost half his winnings.
两圈牌下来，周五“氽”出去一半，

But Pang, the heaviest loser, had made no recovery.
但大输家的庞二却并无起色。

Liu now decided that besides restraining Zhou, he must help Pang win.
于是刘不才又想，现在不但要扣住周五，还得想办法让庞二和牌才好。

He played so well that a little attention to the tiles Pang drew and discarded showed him what Pang needed.
他的牌打得极精，稍微注意一下进出张子，就能料到庞二要的牌，

Just as Pang reached a waiting hand, Liu would discard the winning tile.
总是在他刚听张的时候“放铳”。

Pang went out twice in succession; his luck turned and he recovered his losses.
庞二连着和了两副，手风一顺扳了回去。

When they stopped for dinner after eight rounds and counted up, three were winning from one.
等八圈下来吃饭，计算一下，成了三吃一的局面，
