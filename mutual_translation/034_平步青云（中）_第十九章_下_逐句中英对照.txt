胡雪岩
《平步青云（中）》第十九章（下，至本章结束）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume II — Chapter Nineteen, concluding part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第二天胡雪岩借了王有龄家请客，依旧是“小玩玩”。
The next day, Hu Xueyan borrowed Wang Youling's house to entertain the guests, again for “a little play.”

两天下来，刘不才赢了一万多银子，大为兴奋。
Over the two days, Liu Bucai won more than ten thousand taels and was greatly excited.

胡雪岩却提醒他，不可因此改变初衷，赌上绝不能成功立业，
Hu reminded him, however, not to let this alter his original intentions: gambling could never establish a man's career.

同时也喜一次拜托，务必把庞二笼络得服服帖帖，然后好相机进言。
Once more, he urged Liu to win Pang over completely, then find an opportunity to put their proposal to him.

“看样子我们很投缘。”刘不才说，
“We seem to get on very well,” Liu said.

“长线放远鹞，‘火到猪头烂’……”
“A long line flies the kite far; enough fire will soften the pig's head...”

“不！”
“No!”

胡雪岩不容如此闲豫，
Hu would not allow such leisurely patience.

“我要托他的事，很急！
“What I need from him is urgent.

三叔，你无论如何，趁明天这个机会，就要把他收服。
Third Uncle, you must use tomorrow's opportunity to win him over.

象昨天那样子就很好，
Yesterday you did wonderfully.

连我都佩服。
Even I admired you.

不过你今天就不大对了，
But today you went wrong.

全副心思放在赌上，误了正事。
You put your whole mind into gambling and neglected the real business.”

““今天的机会很好，
“The opportunity was good today.

我先弄它几个，好做赌本。”
I wanted to win something first, to use as gambling capital.”

刘不才不好意思地笑一笑，
Liu smiled sheepishly.

“以后没有机会了，
“There may be no chance later.

你就先放我一马！”
Let me off this once.”

“赌本你不必愁。
“You needn't worry about capital.

有机会能赢几个，我自然也没有反对你，非要你输的道理，
Naturally, if there's a chance to win something, I've no reason to object and insist on your losing.

只是你要顾到你去赌的原意。”
But remember why you went there to gamble.”

胡雪岩又重重地说：
Hu emphasised his point.

“做生意就是这样！
“That's how business works.

处处地方不要忘记自己是为的什么！”
Wherever you are, never forget what you're there for.”

刘不才想了一会，点头答道：
Liu considered, then nodded.

“好！
“Very well.

我明天全副精神对付庞二。”
Tomorrow I'll give all my attention to Pang Er.”


庞二请客的场面很阔，
Pang's entertainment was on a grand scale.

他家在西湖葛岭山脚下有一所别墅，请客就请在那里。
His family had a villa at the foot of Geling Hill beside West Lake, and he received his guests there.

十一月的天气，外面西北风刮得人重裘不暖，
Outside, the north-west wind of the eleventh month penetrated even heavy furs.

但在庞二的别墅中，却是温暖如春，
Inside the villa, it was warm as spring.

在那间背山面湖的温室中开筵，
The luncheon was served in a heated room with the hill behind it and the lake in front.

一共三桌客，身分极杂，但都穿的便衣，也就不容易分得出来了。
There were three tables of guests, drawn from widely differing ranks, though their informal clothes made those distinctions difficult to see.

宴是午宴，吃完已经下午两点，
By the time they finished eating, it was already two in the afternoon.

除了少数几个人以外其余都是知名的赌客，
Apart from a few exceptions, they were all well-known gamblers.

一散席便商量如何赌法？
As soon as they left the tables, they discussed what to play.

“做主人的摇场摊吧！”这个提议，立刻有人附和。
“Let our host bank a dice-shaking game!” Others immediately agreed.

庞二喜欢摇摊是出名的，而在这个场合中，最有资格做庄的，自然也是庞二。
Pang was famous for his fondness for the game, and of everyone present he was naturally the best qualified to act as banker.

在他虽有当仁不让之心，却不免踌躇，因为缺少一个帮手。
Willing though he was, he hesitated: he lacked an assistant.

但转眼看到刘不才，立即欣然答应：
Then he caught sight of Liu and readily agreed.

“好的！
“Very well!

各位有兴致，我就先遥儿十摊。”
Since you're in the mood, I'll shake a few dozen rounds first.”

于是除了一桌麻将以外，近二十个人都预备打摊。
Apart from one table of mahjong, nearly twenty people prepared to play.

听差的准备桌子、座位、赌具，庞二却把刘不才找到一边有话说。
While servants arranged the table, seats, and equipment, Pang drew Liu aside.


“老刘！我们合伙。
“Old Liu, let's be partners.

我六成，你四成，
Sixty per cent for me, forty for you.

你看如何？”
How does that sound?”

“当然好罗！
“Excellent, of course.

不过，我先要‘灵一灵’市面，
But let me first feel out the scale of the game.

我只带了三万银子在身上，
I've only thirty thousand taels on me.

场面太大，我要派人回去拿钱。”
If the stakes are too large, I'll have to send home for more.”

“不必，不必，
“No need.

钱我有。
I have the money.

你也不要先拿本钱，等场头散了再算。
You needn't put up any capital beforehand; we'll settle when the game breaks up.

只有一件事，请你替我做‘开配’。”庞二又说，“我摇摊有个臭脾气，
There's just one thing: I'd like you to handle the collection and payment of bets.” Pang continued, “I've a bad habit at this game.

开配不灵光，我摇起来就没劲。
If the man settling the bets isn't up to the mark, I lose all enthusiasm.

那天在周五家摇摊，临时请了位朋友帮忙，
The other day, at Zhou's, I asked a friend to help at short notice.

我不过出了五个‘老宝’，输不到两万银子，那位开配朋友的手就有些发抖了。
I'd only thrown five Old Treasures and lost less than twenty thousand taels when his hands began to tremble.

不是人家帮我的忙，我不见情，还要说人家，象那位朋友开配，真把我的脸面都丢完了！”
It's not that I fail to appreciate someone helping me and still complain about him, but the way that fellow handled the settlements made me lose all face!”

“我没有替你做过开配，
“I've never done it for you before.

不过，你的事，自然没话说。
Naturally, if you ask, there's nothing more to say.

就怕我应付不下来，”
I'm only afraid I won't be able to manage.”

“你别客气了。”
“Don't be modest.”

庞二拱拱手，
Pang clasped his hands.

“捧我小弟的场！
“Help me put on a good show.

承情，承情。”
I'll be grateful indeed.”


于是刘不才到场执行开配的任务。
Liu took his place to settle the bets.

只见台面已经布置好了，
The table was already prepared.

那张台子，是专为摇摊用的，紫檀桌子，黄杨木的桌面，比平常方桌大一号，
Made specially for the game, it had a zitan-wood frame and a boxwood top and was one size larger than an ordinary square table.

四角用象牙嵌出界线，
Ivory inlay marked the boundaries at its four corners.

每一方又用象牙嵌出茶杯大的圆点，庄家一点，对门三点，右方是二，左方是四，
Each side also had ivory dots the size of teacups: one on the banker's side, three opposite, two to his right, and four to his left.

左青龙，右白虎，
The left was the Green Dragon, the right the White Tiger.

开配照例站立在左上角的三与四之间，那是吉利的“青龙角”。
The settling clerk customarily stood at the upper-left corner between three and four, the auspicious Green Dragon Corner.

等他在青龙角上站定，随即便有听差送过一盒筹码来，
Once Liu stood there, a servant brought him a box of counters.

筹码是四寸长的牙筹！上面刻着金字“世载堂庞”四字，作为标识，
They were ivory tallies four cun long, engraved in gold with the identifying words “Pang of Shizai Hall.”

筹码共分五种，
There were five kinds.

分别刻着骨牌中“天、地、人、和”的点子，另外还有一种只刻堂名的白筹，自然是最小的码子。
Four bore the Heaven, Earth, Man, and Harmony markings from domino tiles; the fifth bore only the hall's name and was naturally the lowest denomination.

刘不才把筹码定为五等，一千、五百、一百、五十、十两，
Liu assigned them five values: one thousand, five hundred, one hundred, fifty, and ten taels.

等赌客买好筹码，才是“皇帝”庞二落座，
Only when the players had bought their counters did Pang, the “emperor,” sit down.

拿起一个明朝成化窑的青花摇缸，“察浪浪，察浪浪”地摇了三下，
He picked up a blue-and-white shaking bowl from the Chenghua kilns of the Ming dynasty and gave it three rattling shakes.

打开摇缸来看，十二点是四。
Opening it, he found twelve points, which counted as four.

“不错！
“Good!

‘开青龙’！”庞二说着又摇。
An opening Green Dragon!” he said, and shook again.


前三下，名为“亮摊”，好供赌客“画路”，
The first three throws were called “showing the game,” allowing the players to chart its course.

摊路的名堂甚多，大路、小路、荤路、素路，
There were many kinds of patterns: main patterns, minor patterns, “meat” patterns, and “vegetable” patterns.

各人相信各人的。
Each player believed in his own.

到第四下摇过，那才正式开始下注，
Only after the fourth shake did betting formally begin.

场面极其热闹，刘不才的本事也就要拿出来了。
The scene grew lively, and Liu had to display his ability.

摇摊在赌里面最公平，做下手的一点亏都不吃，而下手押注的花样也最多，
Of all gambling games, this was the fairest: the players suffered no disadvantage, and they had the widest variety of wagers.

跟牌九一洋，打“角”、打“横堂”以外，还可以打“大头”。
As in pai gow, besides betting a “corner” or “across the hall,” they could bet a “big head.”

角与横堂，下手与庄家各占两门，所以是一赌一，
With corner and across-the-hall bets, player and banker each held two of the four numbers, so the wager was one against one.

“大头”就不同了，虽也是各占两门，但赢法有差别，
A big-head bet also gave each side two numbers, but the method of winning differed.

二带么的大头，开出“白虎”赢两倍，开出“进门”算和气。
With a two-led big head carrying one, a White Tiger paid double, while an Entrance was a draw.

此外还有“放鹞子”，下手打三门，赢了吃二配三，
Then there was “flying a kite”: the player backed three numbers, and on a win the settlement was “take two, pay three.”

在钱上是以三赌一，大本钱卜小利，好象吃亏，
In money terms, this meant staking three to win one: large capital for a small return, seemingly a disadvantage.

但在骰子上，下手占了便宜，赢三门输一门，
With the dice, however, the player held the advantage, winning on three numbers and losing on only one.

当然，偏开不下注的一门，也是有的，
Of course, the unbacked number could still come up.

那一下三注都吃，全军皆墨，就变成“放鹞子断线”了。
Then all three stakes were taken, the whole force was wiped out, and the flying kite's string had snapped.


“放鹞子”还是“孤丁”，照吃照配，不伤脑筋，
Whether the wager was a kite or a single-number bet, collecting and paying it was straightforward enough.

伤脑筋的是改注码，
The difficult part was a changed wager.

有的大头改为孤丁，有的把这门注码移到另一门，注码不动，只凭口说，
One player might turn a big head into a single-number bet; another might transfer his stake from one number to another without moving the counters, merely announcing the change.

都要开配记住。
The settling clerk had to remember it all.

不该配的配了，自然没有人说话，不该吃的吃了，便有人提出抗议。
Nobody complained if he paid a bet that did not deserve payment, but if he collected a stake that should not have been taken, someone would protest.

赔钱是小事，
Losing money was a small matter.

出了错便是不够格，会替庞二丢面子，
Making a mistake showed incompetence and would embarrass Pang.

所以刘不才不敢轻忽，每一注都得注意。
Liu dared neglect nothing; he watched every wager.

暗中用心，表面却很悠闲，等摇缸亮出，该吃的吃进，该配的配多少倍，一一计算清楚，
Inwardly attentive but outwardly at ease, he waited for the bowl to be opened, then collected the losing bets and calculated each payment and its proper multiple.

没有下手说闲话，更不曾起争执。
There were no complaints and no disputes.

刘不才不但计算得清楚，而且计算得特别快，庄家不会等得无聊，所以摇起来格外起劲。
He worked not only accurately but exceptionally fast, so the banker never grew bored waiting and shook with increasing enthusiasm.

不多时候，二十摊已经摇完，
Before long, twenty rounds were over.

做庄做了一半，庞二才看一看面前的银票。
Halfway through his turn as banker, Pang finally looked at the banknotes in front of him.

开配手边，只存筹码和不足一万的银票，
The settling clerk kept only counters and less than ten thousand taels in notes beside him.

满了一万，就得摆到庄家面前，
Once the notes reached ten thousand, he had to put them before the banker.

名为讨口彩的“进庄”，其实是防范开配落入自己荷包。
This was auspiciously called “bringing money into the bank,” but its real purpose was to prevent the clerk from slipping money into his own pocket.

刘不才与庞二初交，兼以负有争取信任的责任，对这些细节，自然特别当心。
Newly acquainted with Pang and charged with winning his trust, Liu was especially careful over such details.

庞二这时略略点了下，共有十四五叠之多，
Pang made a quick count: there were fourteen or fifteen stacks.

自己是十万银子的本钱，
His own capital had been a hundred thousand taels.

算来赢得也不能说少。
The winnings were by no means small.


但后半场的手风就不如前半场了，
The second half went less well.

只见刘不才不断伸手到他面前取钱，转眼间，只剩下七叠。
Liu kept reaching towards Pang for money, and before long only seven stacks remained.

而摊路更坏，一缸青龙，一缸白虎，来回地甩，
The sequence was worse still: a Green Dragon followed by a White Tiger, swinging back and forth.

这名为“摇路”，又称“摇橹”，
This was called a “swaying course,” or “rowing.”

周五看准了，一下就在白虎上打了两万孤丁，
Zhou judged it ripe and placed twenty thousand taels on White Tiger as a single-number bet.

另外在这一门上还有万把银子，
Another ten thousand or so was already on that number.

假如庄家开个二，便得配九万银子，
If the banker threw a two, he would have to pay ninety thousand taels.

虽有三门可吃，为数极微，
Although he could collect the stakes on the other three numbers, they were negligible.

庄家面前的钱是不够输的。
The money before him would not cover the loss.

这是开配的责任，得要提醒庄家，但也有些庄家不爱听这罄其所有还不够配的话，
It was the settling clerk's responsibility to warn him, but some bankers disliked being told that even everything they had would not cover the payouts.

所以刘不才有些踌躇。
Liu hesitated.

一抬眼恰好看到胡雪岩，不自觉略一皱眉，
Looking up, he happened to catch Hu's eye and frowned involuntarily.

胡雪岩立刻便抛过一个阻止的眼色来。
Hu immediately gave him a warning glance.

刘不才警觉了，嘴向庄家面前一努，随即恢复常态。
Liu took the hint, indicated the money before Pang with his lips, and resumed his usual expression.

“老刘！”庞二自己当然有个计算，问道：“怎么样？”
“Old Liu, how is it?” Pang naturally had his own reckoning.

这一问当然是问本钱够不够？
He meant: was the capital enough?

刘不才不能给他泄气，但也不便大包大揽，说得太肯定，
Liu must not discourage him, yet could not confidently undertake to cover everything.

只这样含含糊糊地说：“开吧！”
He merely answered, “Open it.”

开开来是三，
The result was three.

刘不才松了口气，
Liu breathed freely.

等吃配完毕，只见庞家的听差，取了两张银票，悄悄往庞二面前一放。
When collection and payment were complete, a servant of the Pang household quietly laid two banknotes before Pang.

他看了看，略有诧异之色，欲言又止地点一点头，
He glanced at them, looked slightly surprised, and nodded without speaking.

不知是表示会意，还是嘉许。
Whether in understanding or approval, it was hard to tell.

“老五！”庞二看着周五说，“你打吧！
“Old Five,” he told Zhou, “go on betting.

我添本钱了，再添十万。”
I've added more capital—another hundred thousand.”

说也奇怪，一添本钱，手风便又不同，
Strangely enough, the extra capital seemed to change his luck.

摊路变幻莫恻，专开注码少的那门。
The sequence became unpredictable, repeatedly favouring whichever number carried the smallest stake.

等四十摊摇完，结帐赢了七万银子。
When the forty rounds were over, the accounts showed a profit of seventy thousand taels.


接下来是周五做庄，也要求刘不才替他做开配，
Zhou took the bank next and also asked Liu to settle the bets.

二十摊终了，看钟已是晚上八点，暂停吃饭。
After twenty rounds, they saw that it was eight in the evening and stopped for dinner.

趁这空隙，庞二把刘不才找到书房里，
Pang used the interval to take Liu into his study.

打开抽屉，取出两个信纣，递了给他。
Opening a drawer, he brought out two envelopes and offered them to him.

刘不才不肯接，
Liu would not take them.

“庞二哥！”他问，“这是啥？”
“Brother Pang, what are these?”

“你打开来看。”
“Open them and see.”

打开第一只信封，里面是三张银票，
The first held three banknotes.

两张由阜康钱庄所出，每张五万，另外还有一张别家钱庄的，数目是五千。
Two, issued by Fukang Bank, were for fifty thousand taels each; the third, from another bank, was for five thousand.

“老胡很够朋友，
“Old Hu is a true friend.

叫我听差送了十万银子约我添本钱，
He had my servant bring me a hundred thousand taels to add to my capital.

我用不着，不过盛情可感。
I didn't need it, but I appreciate the kindness.

五千银子算是彩，
The five thousand is a share of the luck.

请你转交给他。”
Please take it to him.”

“雪岩不肯收的……”
“Xueyan won't accept...”

“你别管。”庞二打断他的话说，
“Never mind that,” Pang interrupted.

“只托你转交就是了。”
“I'm only asking you to deliver it.”

刘不才也是大少爷出身，
Liu too had been brought up as a wealthy young master.

知道替胡雪岩辞谢，反拂他的意，便收了下来。
He knew that refusing on Hu's behalf would offend Pang, so he accepted the envelope.


看第二只信封，里面是三万二千多两银子。
The second envelope held more than thirty-two thousand taels in notes.

“这是你的一份。”庞二解释，
“This is your share,” Pang explained.

“原说四六成，我想还是‘南北开’的好。”
“We agreed on forty and sixty per cent, but I think an even division would be better.”

刘不才当年豪赌的时候，也很少有一场赌三万银子进出的手面，
Even in his former days of extravagant gambling, Liu had seldom played on a scale that involved thirty thousand taels in a single session.

而此时糊里糊涂的赢了这么一笔钱，
Now, almost without understanding how, he had won such a sum.

有些不大能信其为真实，因而愣在那里，说不出话来。
He could scarcely believe it and stood speechless.

庞二不免觉得奇怪。
Pang found this puzzling.

他在想，莫非他意有不足？
Could Liu be dissatisfied?

这个疑惑的念头，一起即灭，
The suspicion vanished as soon as it arose.

那是绝不会有的事！
That was impossible.

然则必是在想一句什么交代的话。
He must be considering what to say.

这交代，并非道一声谢，就可以了事的，三万二千银子，不是小数目，
A mere thank-you would hardly suffice: thirty-two thousand taels was no small sum.

庞二对自己能给人带来这么大的好处，已觉得很得意。
Pang already felt pleased with himself for being able to confer so great a benefit.

当然还想再听两句“过瘾”的话，
Naturally, he wanted a few words that would give him further satisfaction.

大少爷的脾气，就是这样。
Such was the temperament of a wealthy young master.


刘不才的感动，不言可知，不过他倒也没有让这笔倘来之财，冲昏了头脑，
Liu was deeply moved, but the unexpected windfall had not turned his head.

心想，胡雪岩的意思，是要自己争取庞二的信任，最好还能叫他见自己的情。
Hu wanted him to win Pang's confidence and, if possible, place Pang under some obligation to him.

现在分到了这笔巨数，就得见人家的情了。
Accepting this enormous sum would put the obligation the other way round.

再说，赌场里讲究的就是“现钱”两个字，
Besides, ready money was what counted at a gambling table.

当时讲好四六成比例合伙，就该先出本钱，把身上的三万银票交了过去，
Having agreed to a partnership on a forty-sixty basis, Liu should have supplied his capital first and handed over the thirty thousand taels in notes he carried.

到此刻来分红，就毫无愧作了。
Then he could take his profit with a clear conscience.

虽然庞二是有名的阔少，不在乎此，但人家漂亮，自己也要漂亮，
Pang was famously wealthy and might not care, but if the other man behaved generously, Liu must conduct himself handsomely too.

“信我也是照写，其实不写也不要紧，
“I'll write him a letter as well, though it scarcely matters.

郁四听见是庞二哥的事，不敢不尽心。”
Once Yu hears that it's your business, Brother Pang, he won't dare spare any effort.”

这是胡雪岩拿高帽子往庞二头上戴，意思是以庞家的名望，郁四自然要巴结。
Hu was flattering him, implying that the Pang family's prestige would naturally make Yu eager to oblige.

只是恭维得不肉麻，庞二听了非常舒服，
The compliment was subtle enough to give Pang great pleasure.

心里在想，他们杭州人的俗语，“花花轿儿人抬人”，
He thought of the Hangzhou saying: people take turns carrying one another's decorated chairs.

胡雪岩越是如此说，就越要买他的面子。
The more Hu spoke like this, the more Pang ought to honour him.

“老胡，听你这一说，郁四跟你的交情一定不错。
“Old Hu, from what you say, you and Yu must be good friends.

你的朋友就是我的朋友，
Your friends are mine.

我这趟回湖州，倒要交他一交，
When I return to Huzhou, I should like to get to know him.

请你替我写介绍信。”
Please write me an introduction.”

“一句话！”
“Consider it done.”

胡雪岩起身告辞，
Hu rose to leave.

“你就要走了，总还有些事要料理，
“You're going tomorrow and must still have things to arrange.

我不耽搁你的工夫，
I won't take up your time.

明天一早，我把信送来。”
I'll bring the letters early in the morning.”

这天晚上胡雪岩备下三封极其切实的信，第二天一早带到庞二那里。
That night, Hu prepared three letters, all giving firm and practical support, and took them to Pang the next morning.

投桃报李，他交给胡雪岩的两封信也很实在，一封是委托书，一封是写给他在上海的管事的，
Returning favour for favour, Pang gave him two equally substantial letters: one was an authorisation; the other was addressed to his manager in Shanghai.

特意不封口，请胡雪岩代发，意思是让他过了目，好放心。
He had deliberately left the latter unsealed and asked Hu to send it, meaning that Hu should read it first and be reassured.

这使得胡雪岩对庞二又有深一层的了解，
This gave Hu a deeper understanding of Pang.

做事不但豪爽，而且过节上的交代，一丝不苟，十分漂亮。
He was not only generous and forthright; in attending to every detail of an arrangement, he was scrupulous and admirable.
