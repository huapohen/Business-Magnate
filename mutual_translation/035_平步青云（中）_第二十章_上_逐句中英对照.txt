第二十章｜CHAPTER TWENTY


有了这封委托书，胡雪岩要好好的动脑筋了。
Now that he had the letter of authorisation, Hu Xueyan needed to think carefully.

他不断跟古应春有书信往来，上海方面的生意，是托古应春代为接头，尤五的一切情形，也是由古应春代达。
He had kept up a steady correspondence with Gu Yingchun, who handled his business contacts in Shanghai and passed on all news of You Laowu.

所以庞二这面谈成功，他第一件事，就是写信告诉古应春，
Once the agreement with Pang Er was settled, his first act was to write to Gu.

然后开了年，另外再推出新的计划，大干一番。
After the New Year, he would launch fresh plans and make a great effort.

不多几天，古应春的回信来了，让胡雪岩大出意外的是，洋人那方面变了卦，表示年关以前，无意买丝。

表面是说，他们国内来信，存货已多，可以暂停。

实际上照古应春的了解，外国人也学得门槛精了，
In Gu's view, however, they had grown shrewd.

知道中国商场的规矩，三节结帐，年下归总，需要大笔头寸。
They knew the Chinese business custom of settling accounts at the three major festivals and making a final reckoning at year-end, when large sums of ready money were needed.

有意想“杀年猪”。
They meant to “slaughter the New Year pig.”

如果胡雪岩价钱不是扳得太高，则洋人为了以后的生意，也下会赶尽杀绝。

“我自己要头寸在其次，
“My own need for funds is secondary.

还有许多小户，不能过关，

一定会倒过来恳求洋商，

“那就只有一个办法，”刘不才已经把胡雪岩佩服得五体投地，认为世上没有难得倒他的麻烦，所以语气非常轻松，

Keep them out of the foreigners' trap, and the problem is solved.”

胡雪岩最初的计议就是如此，

He had reached the point of having to submit to the foreign merchants.

胡雪岩想想实在于心不甘，
The thought was intolerable.

多少时间心血花在上面，就为的是要弄成“一把抓”的优势，

如今有庞二的支持，优势已经出现，

一方面不甘屈服，一方面急景调年，时不我待，胡雪岩彻夜彷徨，想不出善策。
Unwilling to yield, with the year's end fast approaching and time running out, he worried through the night without finding an answer.

急得鬓边见了白发。

觉得庞二是个可共患难的人，

“Very well.

“胡雪岩点点头，未置可否，
Hu nodded without committing himself.

心里在盘算杭州跟上海两方面的交代，

细想一想，就是三、五天的工夫也不容易抽出来，
On reflection, even three or five days were hard to spare.

年底下的商场，虽不是瞬息万变，却往往会出意外，
Business at year-end might not change every instant, but unexpected developments were frequent.

万一有何变化，自己措手不及，岂不误了大事，
If something happened while he was unprepared, it could ruin everything.

刘不才看他踌躇不决，知道他必须坐镇在杭州，因而试探着说：“雪岩，你看是不是我代你去走一趟？”

这倒是个办法。
That offered a solution.

刘不才的才干，办这样一件事，可以胜任。但他还有一件事不放心，
Liu had the ability to handle such a mission, but one concern remained.

“三爷！”他说，“你去了不能露出急吼吼的样子……”
“Third Master, when you arrive, you mustn't look desperate...”

“这何消说得？”刘不才抢着说，
“Surely you don't need to tell me that!” Liu interrupted.

“我不能连这一点都不懂。”
“I understand that much.”

“不是！我还有话。”胡雪岩说，
“No, there's more.

“既然不是急如星火的事，那就可以从从容容来。
If it doesn't seem desperately urgent, there will seem to be time to spare.

大少爷的脾气，你是最明白不过的，”
You know that rich young master's temper better than anyone.”

他模拟着庞二的态度说：“ ‘好了，好了，
Imitating Pang, Hu said, “‘All right, all right.

凡事有我。
Leave everything to me.

先赌一场再说。’
Let's have a game first.’

那时候你怎么样？”
What will you do then?”

刘不才想想不错，
Liu saw the point.

这一赌下来，说不定就耽误了胡雪岩的工夫，
Once they started gambling, he might delay Hu's business.

千万赌不得！
He must not play.

“我这样跟他说：我自己在杭州还有许多事，要赶回去料理，
“I'll tell him I have a great deal to attend to in Hangzhou and must hurry back.

到年三十，我赶到南浔来，陪你好好赌几场。”
On New Year's Eve I'll come to Nanxun and play as many games as he likes.”

“对！
“Exactly.

就是这么说。”胡雪岩又郑重的加了一句：“三爷，你可不能拆我的烂污！”
Say just that.” Hu added solemnly, “Third Master, you mustn't let me down!”

“你不相信我，就不要叫我去。”
“If you don't trust me, don't send me.”

说到这话，胡雪岩不能再多提一句，
After that, Hu could say no more.

当时写了信，雇了一只船，加班添人，星夜赶到南浔去会庞二，
He wrote the letter and hired a boat, adding extra hands to speed it on its way through the night to Nanxun.

约定无论事成与否，三天以后，必定回来。
They agreed that, successful or not, Liu must return in three days.


这三天自是度日如年的光景，
Those three days naturally seemed like years.

但胡雪岩决不会独坐愁城，听天由命，
But Hu would never sit helplessly with his troubles and leave everything to fate.

他要作万一的打算，
He needed a fallback.

所以依然每天一早，坐镇阜康，不断派出人去联络试探，希望能找出一条得以筹集这笔巨款的路子来。
Each morning he took his place at Fukang and kept sending people out to make contacts and test possibilities, hoping to find some means of raising the large sum.

第一天第二天都毫无结果，
The first two days produced nothing.

到了第三天，他就有些沉不住气了，
On the third he was beginning to lose his composure.

正在攒眉苦思时，嵇鹤龄到阜康钱庄来相访，

一见面便讶然说道：“雪岩，
“Xueyan!” Ji exclaimed.

几天不见，
“It's only been a few days since I saw you.

你何以清瘦如此？”
How have you grown so thin?”

异姓手足，无需掩饰，
There was no need to conceal anything from a sworn brother.

胡雪岩老实答道：“还差三十万银子，
“I'm still three hundred thousand taels short,” Hu said frankly.

怎么不急得人瘦？”
“How could I help worrying myself thin?”

听这话，嵇鹤龄大吃一惊，
Ji was shocked.

“你怎不跟我说？
“Why didn't you tell me?

那天我问你，你不是说可以‘摆平’吗？”
When I asked the other day, didn't you say you could put everything in order?”

“跟你说了，害你着急，何苦？”
“What good would it do to make you anxious too?”

胡雪岩改用宽慰的语气说，
Hu adopted a reassuring tone.

“只要海运局的那笔宕帐，你能给我维持住，别的也还不要紧。”
“As long as you can keep that outstanding Sea Transport Bureau account from being pressed, the rest isn't too serious.”

怎么又说不要紧？
Not serious?

显见得他是故意叫人宽心。
Plainly he was trying to put Ji at ease.

嵇鹤龄想了想问道：“你总得想办法罗！”
After thinking a moment, Ji asked, “But you must be doing something?”

“是的。”他说了遣刘不才到南浔乞援的事，
“Yes.” Hu explained Liu's mission to Nanxun.

“我给庞二的信上说，我愿意照市价卖多少包丝给他，便宜不落外方。
“In my letter, I offered to sell Pang a quantity of silk at the current market price, so the benefit would stay among ourselves.

我这样吃亏还卸面子，他应该可以帮我这个忙。”
Since I'm accepting a loss and swallowing my pride, he ought to help.”

“年底下一下子要调动三十万的头寸，不是件容易的事。”
“Raising three hundred thousand at once at the year's end is no easy matter.”

“其实，有一半也可以过关了。”
“Actually, half would see me through.”

“十五万也不是少数。”
“A hundred and fifty thousand is no small sum either.”

嵇鹤龄招招手说，

“你来，
“Come here.

我跟你句话。”
I have something to tell you.”

就在这两天要立约付款了。
The contract was to be signed and the money paid within the next day or two.

买洋枪是他的创议，
Buying foreign guns had been his own initiative.

如果试用满意，大量购置，当然是他原经手来办，

何以中途易手，变成龚家父子居间？

当然，这是不用说的，其中必有花佯，

“No.

I hear he's Prussian.”

“Then it isn't Hudson.

这样子办公事，良心未免太黑了一点。”

“Leave that aside.

我告诉你这个消息，是提醒你想一想，这笔款子，能不能在你手里过一过，

能够办得到，岂不是眼前的难关，可以过去？”
If that could be arranged, wouldn't it solve your immediate difficulty?”

胡雪岩对任何他不曾想到的主意，都有兴趣，
