陈世龙非常巴结，
Chen Shilong was eager to oblige.

接信立刻到南浔。

刘不才已经在牌九桌上了，抽不出空写信，

陈世龙随即坐了刘不才包雇的快船，连夜赶到杭州。
Chen then took the fast boat Liu had hired and travelled through the night to Hangzhou.

胡雪岩一块石头落地。
A weight lifted from Hu Xueyan's heart.

尤其是意外发现的买洋枪这件事，

更加放不下心。

看他左右为难，陈世龙便自告奋勇：

“胡先生！”他说，“如果我能办得了，就让我去一趟好了。”
“Mr Hu, if it's something I can manage, let me make the journey.”

胡雪岩想了想，
Hu considered this.

这倒也是个办法，

“你一个是办不了的，
“You couldn't do it alone.

要托尤五！”
You'll have to ask You Laowu.”

其余的事，我托老古。”

Chen left early the next morning.

一大早便有一顶蓝呢大轿，抬到裘家门口，

跟班在拜匣里取了张名帖，投到裘家“门上”。

看门的是早就受了嘱咐，
The doorkeeper had already been instructed.

一看帖子便回说主人出门了，其实裘丰言刚刚起身。

裘丰言大为兴奋，一直赶到阜康钱庄，

见了胡雪岩就说：“鹤龄好准的阴阳八卦！
“Heling reads the signs like a master diviner!” he told Hu.

“Look—the elder Gong has come to seek my counsel, just as he predicted.”

“自然不见。
“Of course not.

One meeting would have ruined everything.

他要一问，我什么也不知道，

“没有那样子不得了，
“It isn't as terrible as all that.

你别害怕。
Don't be frightened.

走，我们到鹤龄那里去。”

海运局年底清闲无事，嵇鹤龄在家纳福，
There was little to do at the Sea Transport Bureau at the end of the year, so Ji was enjoying a quiet time at home.

冬日晴窗之下，正在教小儿子认字号。
Seated by a sunny window on this winter day, he was teaching his little son to recognize characters.

“必是有消息了。”
“There must be news.”

“是啊！”
“Indeed there is!

裘丰言答道：“一路上我在嘀咕，

从来不曾干过这种‘戳空枪’的把戏，

不知道应付得下来不能？”
I don't know whether I can carry it off.”

他担心的是本无其事，亦无其人，

问到洋人在何处，先就难得回答。
A question about the foreigner's whereabouts would be enough to put him in difficulty.

Under Hu and Ji's guidance, however, this was easy enough to manage.

细细教了他一套话。裘丰言才真的有了笑容。
They carefully coached him in what to say, and at last a real smile appeared on his face.


“我要去回拜，
“I must return his call.

得借你的轿子和贵管家一用。”
I'll need to borrow your chair and your steward.”

“不好！”嵇鹤龄未置可否，胡雪岩先就表示异议，
Ji had yet to answer when Hu objected. “No!

“那一下就露马脚了。”
That would give the game away.”

“不错，不错！
“Quite right, quite right.

不要紧，
Never mind.

我可以将就。”
I'll manage.”

裘丰言朋友也很多，
Qiu had plenty of friends himself.

另借一顶轿子，拿他的门上充跟班，将就着到炮局去回拜，
He borrowed a chair elsewhere, took his own doorkeeper along as an attendant, and thus equipped went to return the call at the Cannon Bureau.

“Brother Fengyan, I've long admired your fame as an immortal of the wine cup.

“I really ought not to impose on you like this!”

“老兄说这话就见外了。”龚振麟嘱咐儿子：“你去看看裘老伯的管家在哪里？
“You're treating me as a stranger.” Gong told his son, “Go and see where Uncle Qiu's steward is.

把衣包取了来。”
Have his bundle of clothes brought in.”

“不必，不必！”裘丰言说，
“No need, no need.

“原来是打算着稍微坐一坐就告辞，不曾带便衣来。”
I'd intended to stay only a moment, so I brought no informal clothes.”

“既如此，”
“In that case...”

龚振麟看看客人，又看看儿子：
Gong glanced from his guest to his son.

“之棠，你的身材跟裘老伯相仿，
“Zhitang, you're much the same size as Uncle Qiu.

取一件你的皮袍子来。伺候裘老伯替换。”
Fetch one of your fur robes and help him change.”

裘丰言心想，穿着官服喝酒，也嫌拘束，就不作假客气，
Drinking in official dress would be rather stiff, Qiu reflected, so he dispensed with polite refusals.

等龚之棠叫个丫头把皮袍子取了来，随即换上，
When Zhitang had a maid bring the robe, he put it on.

是件俗称“萝卜丝”的新羊皮袍，极轻极暖，刚刚合身。
It was a new lambskin robe of the kind commonly called ‘shredded radish’, exceptionally light and warm, and it fitted perfectly.


未摆酒，先设茶，福建的武夷茶，器具精洁，烹制得恰到好处。
Before the wine came tea: Wuyi tea from Fujian, prepared to perfection and served in exquisite, spotless utensils.

裘丰言是随遇而安的性格，
Qiu was at ease wherever he found himself.

跟点头之交的龚振麟虽是初次交往，却象熟客一样，一面品茗，一面鉴赏茶具，显得极其舒适随便。
Though he and Gong had previously been no more than nodding acquaintances and this was his first visit, he behaved like an old guest, tasting the tea and admiring the utensils in complete comfort.

而龚振父子也是故意不谈正事，只全力周旋着想在片刻之间，结成“深交”。
Father and son deliberately avoided business, concentrating instead on their attentions to him, as though they could become intimate friends in a few moments.

品茗未毕，只见龚家两个听差，抬进一坛酒来，
Before they had finished their tea, two servants carried in a jar of wine.

龚振麟便说：“老兄对此道是大行家，
“You're a great connoisseur,” Gong said.

请过来看看。”
“Come and have a look.”

裘丰言见此光景，意料必是一坛名贵的佳酿，
Judging from the ceremony, Qiu expected a rare vintage.

便欣然离座，跟龚振磷一起走到廊下，
He rose gladly and accompanied Gong into the corridor.

只见是一坛二十五斤的花雕，
It was a twenty-five-jin jar of huadiao wine.

坛子上的彩画，已经非常黯淡，泥头尘封，变成灰色，
The painted designs had faded badly, and dust had turned the clay seal grey.

隐约现得有字。
A faint inscription appeared beneath the dust.

拂尘一看，上面写着：道光十三年嘉平月造。

“哟！”裘丰言说：
“Oh!” Qiu exclaimed.

“整整二十年了！”
“A full twenty years!”

“是的。
“Yes.

在我手里也有五六年了。
I've had it myself for five or six.

一共是两坛，
There were two jars altogether.

前年家母七十整寿，开了一坛，
We opened one for my mother's seventieth birthday the year before last.

这一坛是‘ 尊因吾辈到时开’！”
This one has waited for a guest worthy of its opening.”

裘丰言自然感动，长揖致谢，
Naturally touched, Qiu bowed deeply in thanks.

心里却有些不安，
Yet he felt uneasy.

这番隆情厚意，不在胡、嵇估计之中、
Hu and Ji had not anticipated such lavish kindness.

以后投桃报李，倒下不了辣手了。
Once he owed a return for all this generosity, it would be difficult to deal harshly with his host.


就在这沉吟之际，龚家听差已经将泥头揭开，取下封口的竹著说：
While he hesitated, the servants removed the clay seal and the bamboo covering beneath it.

“裘老爷，你倒看一看！”
“Have a look, Mr Qiu.”

探头一看，
He leaned forward.

坛口正好有光直射，只见一坛酒剩了一半，而且满长着白毛，
Light fell directly into the jar, revealing that only half the wine remained and that its surface was covered with white growth.

这就证明了确是极陈的陈酒，
This confirmed how old it was.

裘丰言果然是内行，点点头说：
Qiu knew his wine: he nodded.

“是这样子的。”
“That's how it should be.”

于是，龚家听差拿个铜久，极小心地撇净了白花，然后又极小心地把酒倒在一个绿瓷大坛中，留下沉淀的不要，

又开了十斤一坛的新酒，注入瓷坛，
He opened a ten-jin jar of new wine and added it.

顿时糟香扑鼻，裘丰言不自觉地在喉间咽下一口口水。
Immediately, the fragrance of fermented grain filled the air, and Qiu swallowed involuntarily.

回屋入座，
They went back inside and took their places.

但见龚家的福建菜，比王有龄家的更讲究，
The Gong household's Fujian dishes were even more elaborate than those served at Wang Youling's.

裘丰言得其所载，
Qiu was in his element.

在他们父子双双相劝之下，一连就干了三杯，

“这酒上口淡，后劲足，

“喝醉了不好！”

“老伯太谦虚了！

Zhitang gulped down his wine in one draught and tilted the empty cup towards his guest.

裘丰言也只好照干不误。
Qiu had no choice but to follow suit.

自然，他的意思，龚家父子明白，是要趁未醉之前，先谈正事。

“抚台是肯做事的人，

“Yes.”

龚振麟长篇大套，从容细叙，

裘丰言则酒在口中，事在心里，只字不遗地听着，

The supposed subject was bargaining with a foreign merchant, yet they had ended up talking about Hu Xueyan.

这篇文章做得离题了！

黄抚台是否说过那些话，莫可究诘，但意在安抚胡雪岩，则意思极明。

自己不便有所表示，
Qiu could make no commitment himself.

依然只能守住“不置可否”的宗旨，唯唯称是而已！
