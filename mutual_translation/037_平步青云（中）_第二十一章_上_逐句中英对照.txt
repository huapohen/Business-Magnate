第二十一章｜CHAPTER TWENTY-ONE


年初四夜里“接财神”。
On the night of the fourth day of the New Year, the household welcomed the God of Wealth.

胡雪岩因为这一年顺利非凡，真象遇见了财神菩萨似地，所以这天夜里“烧财神纸”，他的心情异常虔诚，
Hu Xueyan’s extraordinary success during the past year had made him feel as though he had indeed encountered the god, and he burned the ritual paper offerings with unusual devotion.

照规矩，凡是敬神的仪节，妇女都得回避，
According to custom, women withdrew from ceremonies honouring the gods.

胡雪岩一个人孤零零地上香磕头，既鲜兄弟，又无儿子，忽然感从中来，
As he stood alone offering incense and kowtowing, with neither brothers nor a son beside him, he was suddenly overcome by sadness.

觉得身后茫茫，
What lay beyond him seemed utterly empty.

就算财神菩萨垂青，发上几千万两银子的大财，有何用处。
Even if the God of Wealth favoured him with a fortune of tens of millions of taels, what use would it be?

等把财神“接”回来，全家在后厅“散福饮胙”，
When the god had been welcomed, the family gathered in the rear hall to share the blessed offerings.

胡老太太倒很高兴，胡雪岩却神情忧郁，
Old Mrs Hu was delighted, but her son looked melancholy.

勉强吃了两杯酒、半碗鸡汤面，放下筷子就回卧房去了。
Forcing down two cups of wine and half a bowl of noodles in chicken broth, he put down his chopsticks and returned to his bedroom.

“怎么了？”胡老太太很不安地低声问儿媳妇：
“What is wrong?” his mother asked her daughter-in-law anxiously in a low voice.

“接财神的日子，
“On the very day we welcome the God of Wealth!

而且吃夜饭辰光，还是有说有笑的，
He was still laughing and talking at dinner.

忽然变成这副样子，
Why the sudden change?

是不是你又跟他说了啥？”
Have you said something to him again?”

“没有！
“No.

我什么话也没有说。”胡太太说，
I haven’t said a word,” Mrs Hu replied.

“新年新岁，一家要图个吉利，
“At the beginning of a new year, we all want good fortune for the household.

我不会跟他淘闲气的。”
I wouldn’t pick a quarrel with him.”

他婆婆的连连点头，显得十分欣慰，
Her mother-in-law nodded repeatedly, much comforted.

“我晓得你贤惠，
“I know how good you are.

雪岩有今天，也全亏你。”她抚慰着说，
He owes his success to you too.

“不过，他外面事情多，应酬也是免不了的。
But he has so much business outside, and entertaining is unavoidable.

你的气量要放宽来！”
You must be more broad-minded.”

前面的话都好，最后一句说坏了，
All had been well until that last remark.

胡太太对婆婆大起反感，
It provoked a sharp resentment in Mrs Hu.

想答一句：“我的气量已经够大了！”但话到口边，到底又咽了下去。
She wanted to say that she was broad-minded enough already, but swallowed the words.


回到卧房，只见胡雪岩一个人在灯下想心事，
Back in the bedroom she found Hu sitting alone under the lamp, lost in thought.

胡太太想起婆婆的话，忘掉了那令人不怡的一句，只记着“他外面事多”这句话，
Remembering his mother’s words, she forgot the offending sentence and thought only of his many affairs outside.

心便软了，
Her heart softened.

也亏他一个赤手空拳，打出这片天下，
He had built all this from nothing, with his own hands.

在家里，凡事总要让他。
At home she ought to give way to him.

于是她问：“你好象没有吃饭，
“You hardly ate,” she said.

有红枣莲子粥在那里，
“There is rice porridge with red dates and lotus seeds.

要不要吃点甜的？”
Would you like something sweet?”

胡雪岩摇摇头，两眼依旧望着那盏水晶玻璃的“洋灯”。
Hu shook his head, his eyes still fixed on the clear glass of the foreign lamp.

“那么，睡吧！”
“Then come to bed.”

“你不要管我！”胡雪岩不耐烦他说，
“Leave me alone,” he answered impatiently.

“你睡你的。”
“Go to bed yourself.”

一片热心换他的冷气，胡太太心里很不舒服，
His cold response to her kindness hurt her.

“他在想啥？”她暗中自问自答：
What was he thinking of?

“自然是想湖州的那个狐狸精！”
Naturally, that fox-spirit in Huzhou!

这一下，只觉得酸味直冲脑门，
Jealousy rushed straight to her head.

忍了又忍，噙着眼泪管自己铺床，
Controlling herself with difficulty, she made the bed with tears in her eyes.

而胡雪岩却发了话。
Then Hu spoke.

“喂！”他说：
“Listen.

“我看你要找个妇产医生去看看！”
I think you ought to consult a physician about childbearing.”

听这一说，朝太太大为诧异，
She was astonished.

“为啥？”她问，
“Why?”

不敢转过脸去，怕丈夫发现她的泪痕。
She dared not turn round, lest he see the traces of tears.

“为啥？”胡雪岩说，
“Why?

“ ‘屁股后头光塌塌’，
There is nothing behind us but emptiness.

你倒不着急？”
Doesn’t that worry you?”

这是指她未生儿子。
He meant that she had borne him no son.

胡太太又气又恼，倏地转过身来瞪着她丈夫。
Angry and distressed, she turned abruptly and stared at him.


“没有儿子是犯‘七出之条’的。”
“Bearing no son is one of the seven grounds for putting away a wife.”

胡太太瞪了一会，爆出这么句话来。
After staring a moment, she burst out with this.

这句话很重，胡雪岩也愣了，
It was a heavy accusation, and Hu too was taken aback.

“怎么说得上这话？”
“How can you say such a thing?”

他实在有些困惑，
He was genuinely bewildered.

原也知道妻子胸有丘壑，不是等闲的女流，却想不到说出话来比刀口还锋利。
He knew his wife had a mind of her own and was no ordinary woman, but had not expected words sharper than a knife.

“我怎么不要说？”胡太太微微冷笑着：
“Why shouldn’t I?” she asked with a faint, cold smile.

“生儿育女是两个人的事，
“Having children concerns two people.

莫非天底下有那等人，只会生女儿，不会生儿子？
Is there anyone in the world who can bear only daughters and never sons?

你既然要这样说，自然是我退让，你好去另请高明。”
Since that is what you say, naturally I must give way, and you can seek someone more capable.”

为来为去为的是芙蓉，
It all came back to Furong.

胡雪岩听出因头，不由得笑了，
Recognising the cause, Hu could not help smiling.

“你也蛮高明的。”他说：
“You are capable enough yourself.

“ ‘先开花，后结果’，
First the blossom, then the fruit.

我的意思是不妨请教请教妇科医生，配一服‘种子调经丸’试试看。”
I only meant that we might consult a physician who treats women and try a course of Fertility and Menstrual-Regulating Pills.”

胡太太实在厉害，
His wife was formidable indeed.

不肯无理取闹，态度也变得平静了，但话很扎实，
She would not quarrel without reason, and now grew calm, but her words remained firm.

掌握机会，谈到要紧关头上：
Seizing the opportunity, she came to the crucial point.

“试得不灵呢？”她问。
“And if they don’t work?”

胡雪岩已具戒心，不敢逞强，
Hu was on his guard and dared not press his advantage.

“不灵只好不灵，”他带点委屈的声音，
“Then they don’t work,” he said, sounding a little aggrieved.

“命中注定无子，还说点啥？”
“If fate has decreed that I shall have no son, what is there to say?”

有道是“柔能克刚”，他这两句仿佛自怨自艾的话，倒把胡太太的嘴堵住了。
Softness overcomes strength: those two apparently self-pitying remarks left her with no answer.

这一夜夫妇同床异梦，
That night they shared a bed but followed different thoughts.

胡太太通前彻后想了一遍，打定了一个主意。
Mrs Hu considered the whole matter from beginning to end and made up her mind.


于是第二天胡老太太问儿子：“你打算哪一天到上海去？”
The next morning his mother asked, “When do you intend to leave for Shanghai?”

“到上灯就走。”
“When the lanterns go up.”

“今天初五，
“Today is the fifth.

上灯还有八天。”胡老太太说，
There are eight days before then.

“也还来得及。”
There is still time.”

“娘！”胡雪岩诧异的问道：
“Mother!” Hu asked in surprise.

“什么来得及来不及？”胡老太太告诉他，胡太太要回娘家，
“Time for what?” She explained that his wife wanted to visit her own family.

得要算一算日子，趁胡雪岩未走之前，赶回家来。
They must count the days so that she could return before he left.

胡太太娘家在杭州附近的一个水乡塘栖，
Her family lived in Tangqi, a water town near Hangzhou.

往返跋涉，也辛苦得很，
The journey there and back was tiring.

如果日子局促，一去就要回来，便犯不着吃这一趟辛苦了。
If time was so short that she had to turn round almost as soon as she arrived, it would hardly be worth the trouble.

“那倒奇怪了，
“How strange!

她怎么不先跟我谈？”
Why didn’t she discuss it with me first?”

“我也问她，说你晓得不晓得？
“I asked whether you knew.

她说先要我答应了，再告诉你。”
She said she must have my consent before telling you.”

话是说得礼与理都占到了，而其实不是那么一回事，
That explanation satisfied both courtesy and reason, but it was not how things usually happened.

每一次归宁都是夫妇俩先商量好了，方始禀告堂上的，
On every previous visit, husband and wife had agreed first and then informed their elders.

何以这一次例外？
Why was this time different?

同时一接了财神，商场上便得请吃春酒，
Besides, once the God of Wealth had been welcomed, merchants began giving their New Year dinners.

胡雪岩要趁这几天大请其客，不能没有人照料，
Hu intended to entertain extensively over the next few days and needed someone to manage matters.

此刻怎抽得出工夫回娘家？
How could she leave just now?

他把这一层意思一说，胡老太太答道：“ 我也提到了。
When he explained this, his mother answered, “I mentioned that too.

她说你请客是在店里，用不着她，她也帮不上忙。
She said you would entertain at the shop and wouldn’t need her; she could be of no help there.

请几家亲眷吃春酒，日子也定了，
As for the family dinner, the date has already been fixed.

就是明天。”
It is tomorrow.”

“岂有此理！”
“This is absurd!”

胡雪岩不悦，
Hu was annoyed.

“怎么不先告诉我？”
“Why wasn’t I told first?”


胡老太太因为已经知道芙蓉的事，觉得儿媳妇受了委屈，不免袒护，
His mother already knew about Furong and felt her daughter-in-law had been wronged, so she was inclined to protect her.

所以这时候便“揽是非”，说是她的主意，与胡太太无关。
Taking the blame, she said the arrangement was her own idea and had nothing to do with Mrs Hu.

看这样子，胡雪岩认为以少开口为妙，
Seeing how matters stood, Hu thought it best to say little.

冷笑一声答道：
He gave a cold laugh.

“随便她！
“Let her please herself.

反正在家里是她大！”
At home she is the one in command, anyway.”

这句话的言外之意，做娘的自然听得出来，
His mother naturally understood the implication.

“这个家也亏得她撑恃，”她警告儿子：
“This household depends on her,” she warned him.

“你不要以为你在外头，就没有人管你，高兴怎么样就怎么样！
“Don’t imagine that because you are outside, nobody can control you and you can do whatever you please.

如果你真的存了这个念头，将来苦头有得你吃！
If that is what you think, there is trouble in store for you.”

“知子莫若母，
Nobody knows a son like his mother.

一句话说到胡雪岩心里，他也颇生警惕，
Her words went straight to his thoughts and made him wary.

不过事情多想一想也不能无怨，
Yet on reflection he could not entirely suppress his grievance.

“娘！”他说，“ ‘不孝有三，无后为大’，
“Mother, of the three failures in filial duty, leaving no heir is the greatest.

难道你老人家就不想抱孙子？”
Don’t you want a grandson?”

“我怎么不想？”胡老太太平静他说，
“Of course I do,” she answered calmly.

“这件事我们婆媳已经商量过了。
“Your wife and I have discussed it.

媳妇不是不明事理的人，
She is a reasonable woman.

我做婆婆的，自然要依从她的打算。
As her mother-in-law, naturally I should follow her plan.”

““她是怎么样打算？”
“What is her plan?”

“你先不要问。”
“Don’t ask yet.”

胡老太太笑道，
His mother smiled.

“总于你有好处就是了。”
“It will be to your benefit.”

胡雪岩猜不透她们婆媳葫芦里卖的是什么药，也就只好暂且丢开。
Unable to guess what the two women were plotting, Hu could only leave the matter for the time being.


第二天在家请过了春酒。
The next day they gave the family New Year dinner.

胡太太便带着八岁的小女儿，雇了一只专船回塘栖，
Then Mrs Hu took her younger daughter, aged eight, and hired a boat for the journey to Tangqi.

这一去只去了五天，正月十一回杭州。
She was away only five days, returning to Hangzhou on the eleventh of the first month.

他们夫妇感情本来不坏，
The couple’s relationship had always been fairly good.

虽然略有龃龋，经此小别，似乎各已忘怀，仍旧高高兴兴地有说有笑。
After this short separation, their slight disagreement seemed forgotten, and they laughed and talked happily as before.

胡雪岩打算正月十四动身，所以胡太太一到家，使得替丈夫打点行李，
Hu intended to leave on the fourteenth, so as soon as his wife returned she had to pack for him.

他个人的行李不多，
He needed little personal luggage.

多的是带到松江、上海去送人的土产，
The bulk consisted of local products to be given away in Songjiang and Shanghai.

“四杭”以外，吃的、用的，样数很不少，
Besides Hangzhou’s “four specialities,” there were many kinds of food and useful articles.

一份一份料理，着实累人。
Sorting each gift separately was tiring work.

土产都是凭折子大批取了来的，
These local goods had been obtained in bulk on account.

送礼以外，当然也留坐自用，
Naturally, some were kept for the household.

胡雪岩打开一包桂花猪油麻酥糖，
Hu opened a packet of crumbly sesame sweets made with lard and scented with osmanthus.

吃了一块不想再吃，
After one piece he wanted no more.

便喊者他的小女儿说：“荷珠，你来吃了它。”
“Hezhu,” he called to his younger daughter, “come and finish these.”

拿起酥糖咬了一口，荷珠直摇头：
She picked one up, took a bite and shook her head.

“我不要吃！”
“I don’t want it.”

“咦！
“Why?

你不是顶喜欢吃酥糖？”
Aren’t these your favourite sweets?”

“不好吃！”荷珠说，
“They don’t taste nice.

“没有湖州的好吃。”
The ones from Huzhou are better.”

“你在哪里吃的湖州酥糖？”
“Where did you have sweets from Huzhou?”

这句话其实问得多余，自然是在外婆家吃的，
The question was really unnecessary: obviously, at her grandmother’s house.

但“一滴水恰好溶入油瓶里”，
Yet it was like a drop of water falling into an oil bottle.

略懂人事的荷珠，
Hezhu was old enough to understand a little of grown-up affairs.

忽然有所顾忌，竟答不上来，涨红了脸望着他父亲，仿佛做错了什么事伯受责似地。
Suddenly she grew cautious, could not answer, and looked at her father with a flushed face, as though expecting punishment for some wrongdoing.


这一来胡雪岩疑云大起，
Hu’s suspicions rose at once.

看妻子不在旁边，便拉着荷珠的手，走到窗前，悄悄问道：“你告诉爸爸，哪里来的湖州酥糖？
Seeing that his wife was not nearby, he took Hezhu to the window and asked quietly, “Tell Papa where those Huzhou sweets came from.

我上海回来，买个洋囡囡给你。”
When I return from Shanghai, I’ll bring you a foreign doll.”

荷珠不知怎么回答？
She did not know what to say.

想了半天说：“我不晓得！”
After thinking for a long while, she answered, “I don’t know.”

做父亲的听这回答，不免生气，但也不愿吓得她哭，只说：
This annoyed him, though he did not want to frighten her into tears.

“好！
“Very well.

你不肯告诉我，随便你！
If you won’t tell me, please yourself.

等我上海回来，姐姐有新衣裳，洋囡囡，
When I return from Shanghai, your sister will have new clothes and a foreign doll.

你呢，什么没有！”
You will have nothing.”

威胁利诱之下，荷珠到底说了实话：
Between the threat and the reward, Hezhu finally told the truth.

“娘带回来的。”
“Mother brought them back.”

“娘到湖州去过了？”
“Mother went to Huzhou?”

“嗯。”
“Mm.”

荷珠委屈他说，
She sounded aggrieved.

“我也要去，娘不许！”
“I wanted to go too, but she wouldn’t let me.”

“噢！
“Oh!

去了几天？”
How many days was she away?”

“一天去，一天回来。”
“One day to go, one day to come back.”

“那么是两天。”
“Two days, then.”

胡雪岩想了想又问，
Hu thought a moment.

“你娘回来以后，跟外婆说了些什么？”
“When she came back, what did she say to Grandmother?”

“我不晓得。
“I don’t know.

我走过去要听。娘叫我走开。
I went over to listen, and she sent me away.

娘又说，不准我说，娘到湖州去过。”
She also said I mustn’t tell anyone she had been to Huzhou.”

荷珠说到这里，才感觉事态严重，
Only now did Hezhu realise how serious this was.

“爸爸，爸爸，你千万不要跟娘去说，说我告诉你，娘到湖州去过。”
“Papa, Papa, you mustn’t tell Mother that I told you she went to Huzhou.”

“不会，不会！”
“I won’t, I won’t.”

胡雪岩把她搂在怀里，
He gathered her into his arms.

“我买洋囡囡给你。”
“I’ll buy you the foreign doll.”


安抚了荷珠，胡雪岩大上心事。
Having reassured Hezhu, Hu was deeply troubled.

他妻子的湖州之行，不用说，自然是为了芙蓉，但她干了些什么，却难以揣恻，
Naturally his wife’s journey to Huzhou concerned Furong, but what she had done was hard to guess.

是去打听了一番，还是另有什么作为？
Had she merely made inquiries, or taken some other action?

照他的了解，她做事极有分寸，决不是蛮横无理的悍泼之妇可比。
He knew she had an excellent sense of measure and was nothing like a violent, unreasonable shrew.

意识到这一点，他越觉得自己不可鲁莽，
That made it all the more necessary for him to avoid rashness.

必须谋定后动，或者说，兵来将挡，水来土掩，看她是用的什么办法，再来设计破她。
He must plan before acting—or, as the saying went, meet soldiers with generals and floodwater with earth: discover her move and devise the proper answer.

只要知道了她的用意和行动，一定有办法应付，这一点胡雪岩是有信心的。
Once he knew her purpose and actions, he was confident he could deal with them.

不过他也有警惕，自己所遭遇的“对手”太强，不可造次，
But this was a powerful opponent, and he must be careful.

同时估量形势，在家里他非常不利，
At home the odds were against him.

上有老母，下有一双女儿，都站在他妻子这面，
His mother above and his two daughters below all stood on his wife’s side.

自己以一敌四，孤掌难鸣。
One against four, he could hardly prevail.

所以眼前的当务之急，是要争取优势，而这个工作只能在暗地里做，
His immediate task was therefore to gain an advantage, and he could do it only in secret.

让妻子知道了，只要稍加安抚，“地盘”就会非常稳固。
If his wife learned of it, a little reassurance from her would make her position unshakable.

于是他首先还是找到荷珠，告诫她不可将他所问的话，告诉她母亲。
First he sought Hezhu out again and warned her not to tell her mother what he had asked.

然后又找他的大女儿，十五岁的梅王。
Then he approached his elder daughter, fifteen-year-old Meiyu.

梅玉很懂人事了，
She understood a good deal of the world.

虽是她母亲的“死党”，却很崇拜父亲，因而胡雪岩跟她说话，另有一套计算，一开口就说：
Though firmly her mother’s ally, she admired her father, so he adopted a different approach.

“梅王，你跟爸爸一起到上海去，好不好？”
“Meiyu, would you like to come to Shanghai with Papa?”

这话让梅玉又惊又喜。
The proposal surprised and delighted her.
