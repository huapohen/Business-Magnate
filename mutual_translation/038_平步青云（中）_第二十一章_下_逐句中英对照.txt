胡雪岩
《平步青云（中）》第二十一章（下，至本章结束）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume II — Chapter Twenty-One, concluding part

原著：高阳


到了动身那天，胡雪岩带着一女一婢上路，
When the day of departure came, Hu Xueyan set off with his daughter and a maid.

当夜在北新关前泊舟，

父女俩灯下吃闲食说闲活，
Father and daughter sat by the lamp, eating snacks and chatting.

做父亲的刻意笼络女儿，把个梅玉宠得依依不舍，一直不肯上床。
Hu deliberately set out to win his daughter over, indulging Meiyu so thoroughly that she could not bear to leave him and kept refusing to go to bed.

梅玉点点头：
She nodded.

“爸爸一年到头在外头，
“You're away all year round, Father.

自然辛苦的。”
Of course you have a hard time.”

“辛苦在其次，
“The hard work is the least of it.

每到一处地方，没有人照应，是最苦的事。
The worst thing is having no one to look after me wherever I go.

不过，这一趟不会苦了，有你陪我在一起，情形不同。”
This journey will be different, though. With you beside me, I shan't suffer.”

“那……”梅玉答道，
“Then...

“以后爸爸出门，我陪你好了。”
I'll go with you whenever you travel.”

“好倒是好，只怕办不到。”胡雪岩说，
“That would be lovely, but I'm afraid it isn't possible.

“梅玉，我说句话，你会不会动气？”
Meiyu, will you be angry if I say something?”

“不会的，爸爸，
“No, Father.

你尽管说。”
Say whatever you like.”

“我是说老实话，在家是女儿好；出门是儿子好。
“To tell the truth, a daughter is best at home, but a son is best on the road.

So it can only be an occasional treat.”

梅玉不作声，只拿忧愁的眼光，看着她父亲。
Meiyu said nothing, merely looking at him with troubled eyes.


“我倒问你看，
“Let me ask you this.

假使到一处地方，有人能代替你来服侍我，
Suppose there were someone in one of the places I visit who could look after me in your place.

你觉得怎么样？”
What would you think?”

梅玉不明他的意思，
Meiyu did not grasp his meaning.

只直觉的答道：“那自然好罗！”
She simply answered instinctively, “Of course that would be good!”

“乖！”
“Good girl!”

胡雪岩愉悦的拍拍她的肩，
Delighted, Hu patted her shoulder.

“真正是我的好女儿。”
“You're truly a good daughter to me.”

于是第二天胡雪岩吩咐船家，先到湖州去弯一弯，再直放松江。
Accordingly, the next day he told the boatman to make a detour to Huzhou before proceeding directly to Songjiang.

“咦，爸爸，”梅玉不解而问，“怎么忽然想到湖州去，为啥？”
“But Father,” Meiyu asked in bewilderment, “why have you suddenly decided to go to Huzhou?”

“为了你，我要到湖州去一趟。”
“It's for your sake that I must go there.”

这话越发令人困惑，
This only puzzled her more.

“为我？”
“For my sake?”

十五岁的梅玉，情窦初开，

因为梅王心神不定，不能去细想他的话，就得不到他想到的效果。
Meiyu was too unsettled to consider his words carefully, and he would not achieve the effect he wanted.

“It's about something of my own,” he said.

“I want you to help me decide.”

饭罢睡了一个午觉，起来天倒又快黑了，

彤云密布，大有雪意，

胡雪岩叫早早泊了船，命船家到岸上去买了一尾鲜鱼，一大块羊肉，

这顿晚饭就非常丰盛了。

梅玉心里好生疼怜，

“你吃嘛！”
“Go on, eat!”

胡雪岩夹了一块红烧羊肉放在她碗里，

“在家千日好，出外一时难。
“A thousand days at home are pleasant; a single hour away can be hard.

你娘不晓得我在外头的苦楚，

你该晓得了？”
Now you understand, don't you?”

梅玉点点头，

She had felt no hardship herself, but since her father spoke of suffering, she too began vaguely to sense how difficult travelling could be.


“梅玉！”
“Meiyu!”

胡雪岩急转直下他说，

问她母亲，母亲反叱斥她“少管闲事”，

“真的？”
“Really?”

胡雪岩懂她的意思，
Hu understood.

“Of course,” he replied solemnly.

我跟你娘说不清楚。
“I can't make your mother understand.

“我……”
“I...”

梅玉不知道怎么说了，
Meiyu did not know what to say.

心里只想帮父亲的忙，却苦于无从表达，

“她叫芙蓉。”
“Her name is Furong.”

接着，胡雪岩便大谈芙蓉人如何好，命如何苦！使得梅玉除却芙蓉，就不会想别的念头了。
He then spoke at length of Furong's goodness and the hardships of her life, until Meiyu could think of nothing else.

When he had finished, Hu asked, “Well, Meiyu, what do you think of her?”

“这个人，”梅玉答说，
“This woman...

“爸爸，你怎么跟她认识的？”
Father, how did you come to know her?”

这其中的曲折，做父亲的就不肯细说了，
Her father was unwilling to explain all the circumstances.

“也是人家做的媒。
“Someone made the match.

不足为奇。”胡雪岩又说，

“我看她人还不错，而且人家讲的话，也是实在情形，就接了她来住。

“怎么说要我答应？”
“Why should you need my agreement?”

她摇摇头，
She shook her head.

“我哪里敢来管爸爸的事？”
“How would I dare interfere in your affairs?”

“你不敢管，我还非要你管不可。
“You may not dare, but I insist that you do.

为啥呢？”
Why?”

胡雪岩喝口酒，一层层往下说，

“First, of course, I must tell Grandmother.

你娘答应了，我还要问你，

Do you understand?”

“我懂。”梅王答道，
“I do.

“面和心不和，大家都难过。”
If people seem friendly but don't feel it, everyone is unhappy.”

“就是这话罗！
“Exactly!

我为啥非要你管不可呢？

梅玉从来没有为人这么重视过，
No one had ever attached such importance to Meiyu before.

自觉责无旁贷，当时答道：“爸爸这么说，我回去就先跟奶奶讲。”
She felt that the responsibility was hers alone and answered at once, “Since you say so, Father, I'll speak to Grandmother as soon as we get home.”

“你预备怎么讲法？”
“What will you tell her?”

梅玉想了想答道：

“我说她是好人，蛮可怜的。”
“I'll say she's a good person and very unfortunate.”

“怎么好法呢？
“Good in what way?

奶奶问你，你见过没有，你怎么说？
What will you say if Grandmother asks whether you've met her?

所以我一定要带你去看了她再谈。”

到此光景，胡雪岩已有把握，女儿是自己的不叛之臣，
By now Hu was confident that his daughter was his unwavering ally.

只是父女之情是一回事，梅玉看芙蓉怎么样，又是一回事。

所以此时他的心思，抛开了梅玉，在思索着应该怎么安排，才能让芙蓉跟梅玉一见投缘？

一夜过去，第二天午前就可抵达湖州，

事先他把在湖州的朋友和关系，如何称呼，都细细告诉了梅玉。
