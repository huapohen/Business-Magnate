胡雪岩
《平步青云（下）》第二十二章（中）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume III — Chapter Twenty-Two, middle part
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


到了怡情院，已经灯火阑珊，
By the time they reached Yiqing House, most of the lights had dimmed.

只有楼上前厢房还有一台酒在闹。
Only a drinking party in the front room upstairs was still making a noise.

到了怡情老二的大房间略坐一坐，
They sat briefly in Second Miss's large room.

古应春首先告辞，接着是尤五道声“明朝会”，
Gu Yingchun left first; then You Laowu said, “See you tomorrow.”

怡情老二诡秘地一笑，相偕离去。
Second Miss gave a mysterious smile and went away with him.

阿巧姐却始终不曾露面，
A’qiao had not appeared at all.

一个小大姐名叫阿翠的，替胡雪岩铺衾安枕，接着端了热水来，服侍他洗脚。
A young maid called A’cui arranged Hu's bedding, brought hot water, and helped him wash his feet.

杂事已毕，掩上房门，管自己走了。
Her work finished, she shut the door and went off.

胡雪岩有些心神不安，不知怡情老二是怎么一个安排？
Hu felt uneasy, wondering what Second Miss had arranged.

只凝神静听房门外面，
He listened intently outside.

脚步声倒有，都是由远而近再由近而远，不曾见有人推门进来，
There were footsteps, approaching and receding, but nobody opened the door.

而自鸣钟已经打了数下，
The striking clock had sounded several times.

自笑是“痴汉等老婆”，懒洋洋地上了床。
Laughing at himself as a fool waiting for a wife, he climbed languidly into bed.

这一天相当累，
The day had been tiring.

心里有事，眼皮却酸涩得很，蒙蒙胧胧地睡了去。
Though his mind was occupied, his eyelids ached, and he drifted into sleep.

也不知过了多少时候，突然发觉被中伸进一口冰冷的手来，
He did not know how much later it was when an icy hand suddenly entered the bedding.

“啊！地一声，不等他开口，又有一只冰冷的手，掩在他嘴上。
Before his startled exclamation could become a word, another cold hand covered his mouth.

胡雪岩会意，身子往里面一缩，腾出地方来容纳阿巧姐。
Understanding, he moved farther into the bed to make room for A’qiao.

她钻进被窝，牙齿冻得“格格”发抖，同时一把抱往了他，前胸紧贴着他的后背，意在取暖。
She slipped under the quilt, her teeth chattering, and clasped him, pressing her chest against his back for warmth.

“怎么冻得这样子？”胡雪岩转过脸悄悄问说。
“How did you get so cold?” he whispered, turning his head.

“前厢房断命客人，到三点钟才走。”
“Those wretched guests in the front room didn't leave until three.

阿巧姐说，“今天轮着我值夜，风又在，
It was my turn on night duty, and the wind was fierce.

冻得我来！”
I'm frozen!”

说着吸了口气，把他抱得更紧了。
She drew a breath and held him tighter.

胡雪岩好生怜惜，翻个身伸手把被掖一掖，
Filled with tenderness, Hu turned over and tucked the quilt around her.

阿巧索性把头钻在他胸前，他的一双手自然也就不老实了。
She buried her head against his chest, and his hands naturally began to wander.


一面膜索着，他一面问：“阿巧，你今年几岁？”
As he caressed her, he asked, “A’qiao, how old are you?”

“猜猜看呢？”
“Guess.”

“二十三。”
“Twenty-three.

胡雪岩说，“至多二十四。”
Twenty-four at most.”

“二十四是要来生了。”
“I'll have to wait for another life to be twenty-four.”

“那么多少呢？”
“How old, then?”

“我属羊的。”
“I was born in the Year of the Goat.”

“属羊？”
“The Goat?”

胡雪岩在多底拿起阿巧姐的纤纤五指，扳数着说，
Taking her slender fingers beneath the quilt, he counted on them.

“今年咸丰四年甲寅，
“This is the fourth year of Xianfeng, jiayin.

道光二十七年丁未，十五年乙未，
The twenty-seventh of Daoguang was dingwei, and the fifteenth was yiwei.

正好二十岁。”
That makes you exactly twenty.”

“越算越好了！”
“Your reckoning gets better and better!”

阿巧姐当然知道他是有意这样算法，但心里总是高兴的。
She knew he was doing it deliberately, but it pleased her all the same.

“阿巧，”胡雪岩做了反面文章，又做正面，
Having taken the playful approach, Hu now spoke seriously.

“你真正看不出三十二岁。”
“You really don't look thirty-two.”

“大家都说胡老爷一双眼睛厉害，
“Everyone says Master Hu has such sharp eyes.

会看不出？”
Could you really fail to see?”

“真的看不出！”
“I truly couldn't.

胡雪岩问道：“象你这样的人才，为啥不自己铺房间，要帮人家？”
A woman of your qualities—why don't you set up a room of your own instead of working for someone else?”

“吃这碗饭，三十二岁就是老太婆了！
“In this trade, thirty-two makes you an old woman.

人老珠黄不值钱，
Old age and faded looks are worth nothing.

啥人要？”
Who would want me?”

“我要，”胡雪岩不假思索的回答。
“I would,” Hu answered without thinking.

阿巧姐见多识广，当然不会拿他的话当真，接口答道：
A’qiao had seen too much of the world to take that seriously.

“既然有人要，我还要铺啥房间？”
“If someone wants me, why would I need to set up my own room?”

“这话倒也不错。”
“True enough.

胡雪岩又问：“你家里有些什么人？”
Who is there in your family?”

问到这话，近乎多余，而偏偏客人常喜欢问这句话，
It was an unnecessary question, yet one customers often asked.

阿巧姐都腻烦回答了，
She was tired of answering.

“问它作啥！”
“What do you want to know that for?

她说，“总不见得是千金小姐出身。”
I can hardly have been brought up as a young heiress.”

言语简峭，胡雪岩又多一层好感，
Her crisp reply increased his liking for her.

不由得想起了尤五的话，认真地开始考虑。
Remembering You's suggestion, he began to consider it seriously.


此时此地，忽然既不动口，又不动手，那是大为反常的事，
At such a time and in such a place, suddenly to cease both talking and touching was decidedly unusual.

阿巧狙不由得有些奇怪，伸一只手去摸在他的胸前，左一按，右一按，这使得胡雪岩也奇怪了。
Puzzled, A’qiao put a hand on his chest, pressing here and there until he too was puzzled.

“做什么？”
“What are you doing?”

“看看可能摸得出你的心事？”
“Seeing whether I can feel what's on your mind.”

“心事怎么摸得出？
“You can't feel a thought.

只能猜。
You can only guess it.

你倒猜猜我的心事看。”
Go on, guess mine.”

“我不用猜，
“I needn't guess.

我摸得出。”
I can feel it.

阿巧姐说，“你不喜欢我。”
You don't like me.”

“奇了！
“How extraordinary!

哪有这话？
What makes you say that?

你倒讲个道理给我听听。”
Give me a reason.”

“你喜欢我就会心跳。
“If you liked me, your heart would beat faster.

现在心一点不跳，
It isn't stirring at all.

是‘当伊煞介事’。”
You're merely going through the motions.”

“妙！”胡雪岩笑道，
“Wonderful!” Hu laughed.

“还有这么一套说法？
“What a theory!

不晓得你这样子摸过几个男人？”
I wonder how many men's chests you've tested like that.”

这句话说得失于检点，
It was a thoughtless remark.

阿巧姐恼怒伤心，兼而有之，慢慢抽开手，背脸向外。
Hurt and angry, she slowly withdrew her hand and turned her face away.

胡雪岩这才发觉，说了句极无趣的话，
Only then did Hu realise how unpleasantly he had spoken.

深为失悔，
He regretted it deeply.

扳她身子不动，
When he tried to turn her towards him, she would not move.

仰头去看，
He raised his head.

梳妆台上一只洋灯的残焰映照，阿巧姐两粒泪珠，晶莹可见。
By the dying flame of the foreign lamp on the dressing table, he could see two shining tears.

“生气了是不是？”
“You're angry, aren't you?”

胡雪岩尴尬地说，
he said awkwardly.

“说说笑话，
“It was a joke.

何苦当真！”
Why take it seriously?”

说着，拿手指替她拭去眼泪，顺势就亲着她的脸。
He wiped her tears with his fingers and kissed her face.

阿巧姐不作声，但也没有再作何不快的表示，
She said nothing, but showed no further displeasure.

她只是尽力为自己譬解，敷衍怡情老二和尤五的面子，好歹应付了这一夜。
She merely did her best to reason herself out of her distress and, for Second Miss's and You's sake, get through the night somehow.

胡雪岩却是由于这个言语上的波折，失去了兴趣，
The awkward exchange had dampened Hu's interest too.

同时也累得懒于说话，
He was tired and disinclined to talk.

一合上眼，便觉双目酸涩，
As soon as he closed his eyes, they ached with fatigue.

真的借了一夜“干铺”。
In the end, he really did spend the night in a “dry bed.”


到第二天一觉醒来，时已近午，
When he awoke, it was nearly noon.

侧身一望，阿巧姐自然不在，枕边却遗下一根长长的头发，
Turning over, he found A’qiao gone, as he had expected, but a long hair remained beside the pillow.

拈到手里，
He picked it up.

想起宵来的光景，倒有无端的怅惆，同时也觉得有些歉疚，
The memory of the night brought an unaccountable wistfulness and a sense of guilt.

心想阿巧姐一定很不高兴，并且也辜负了尤五和怡情老二玉成的美意。
She must be very unhappy; he had also disappointed You and Second Miss in their kindly attempt to bring them together.

这样转着念头，便打算要跟阿巧姐先谈一谈，
He decided to speak to her first.

披衣起床，咳嗽一声，
Putting on his clothes, he rose and coughed.

房门随即“呀”地推开，进来的正是阿巧姐，
The door immediately creaked open, and A’qiao herself entered.

梳一个极光极亮的头，脸却是不施脂粉的清水脸，
Her hair was dressed smooth and glossy, but her face was bare of powder or rouge.

新象牙似的皮肤，淡红的嘴唇，颊上有几点茶叶未似的雀斑，徐娘丰韵，别有动人之处。
With skin like new ivory, pale red lips, and a few freckles like specks of tea on her cheeks, she had the distinctive charm of a mature woman.

“起来了！”
“You're up.”

她说，眼睛一瞟，撮两个手指放在嘴唇，示意禁声。
She glanced at him, then put two fingers to her lips to enjoin silence.

看她这个姿态，明雪岩自然什么话都不敢说，而实在有些困惑，不知道要顾忌的是哪些话？
He dared say nothing, though he was puzzled about which words required caution.

“夜里的事，不要漏出来！”
“Don't let last night's business slip out.”

原来如此！
So that was it!

胡雪岩不知是不是因为她来相伴，不合于“长三”。的规矩，所以有所忌讳。
He wondered whether her coming to him had broken some rule of the changsan establishment.

只觉得这样子倒有偷情的趣味，越发觉得昨夜的机会可惜。
In any case, the secrecy gave the affair the flavour of a clandestine romance and made him regret the lost opportunity all the more.


要再找这样一个机会也不难。
Another opportunity would not be hard to arrange.

等小大姐打了脸水进来，阿巧姐理好了床，来替他打辫子时，胡雪岩便说：“今天晚上我仍旧要借干铺。”
Once the maid had brought washing water and A’qiao had straightened the bed and begun to braid his queue, Hu said, “Tonight I shall borrow a dry bed again.”

“随便你。”阿巧姐淡淡地应声。
“As you please,” she replied indifferently.

“还跟昨天一样。”
“Just like last night.”

“啥个一样？”
“What is to be the same?”

他不知她是真不明白，还是有意装傻？
Was she genuinely puzzled or pretending not to understand?

想了想笑道，
After a moment, he smiled.

“来摸摸我的心跳不跳？”
“Come and feel whether my heart is beating.”

阿巧姐不响，把眼垂了下去，似乎专心一致在他那条辫子上。
She lowered her eyes without answering, apparently intent on his braid.

“还在生我的气？”
“Still angry with me?”

“哪有这话？
“How could you say that?

我们什么人，敢生贵客的气？”
What are we, to dare be angry with distinguished guests?”

阿巧姐正色说道：
She spoke seriously.

“胡老爷，你千万不能说这话，
“Mr Hu, you must never say that.

传到二小姐耳朵里，一定会说我。”
If Second Miss heard, she would blame me.”

“不会，不会！”
“She won't!”

胡雪岩灵机一动，
An idea came to him.

“你能不能请一天假？”
“Could you take a day off?”

“为啥？”
“Why?”

“我带你到一个地方去玩。”
“I'd like to take you out.”

停了一会，见她不作声，便知不是不能请假的，因而又加了一句：“我来跟老二说，放你一天假。”
When she remained silent, he concluded that leave was possible and added, “I'll ask Second Miss to give you the day.”

“不！”
“No.

阿巧姐说，“我自己跟二小姐讲。
I'll ask her myself.

不过，胡老爷，你要带我到啥地方去玩？”
But where would you take me?”

“玩就是玩。看戏，吃大菜，再到外国洋行看看，有什么新样子的首饰？”
“Just out for pleasure: a play, a Western meal, then a look at a foreign shop to see what new jewellery they have.”

这一说，阿巧姐不由得露了笑容，
At that, she could not help smiling.

昨夜那一言之夫所引起的不愉快，至此才算消除。
The unhappiness caused by his thoughtless words was finally dispelled.

“胡老爷！”小大姐走了来说：“尤五少说，请胡老爷到小房子去吃中饭。”
The maid came in. “Mr Hu, Fifth Master You asks you to lunch at his little house.”

“好。
“Very well.

我就去。”
I'll go now.”

胡雪岩暗示阿巧姐说，
Hu gave A’qiao a hint.

“我吃完饭就要走了。”
“I'll be leaving as soon as lunch is over.”


等胡雪岩一到，只见古应春也在那里，
When Hu arrived, Gu was there too.

踉尤五和怡情老二的脸上一样，都挂着愉悦的笑容，仿佛正在谈一件很有趣的事，
He, You, and Second Miss all wore delighted smiles, as though discussing something amusing.

看到胡雪岩出现，笑容更浓了，显然的，所谈的这件趣事，与他有关。
At Hu's appearance their smiles broadened: clearly he was the subject.

“昨晚我竟蒙在鼓里。”古应春迎着他说，
“I was completely in the dark last night,” Gu greeted him.

“这也算‘小登科’，
“A little wedding celebration, eh?

恭喜，恭喜！”
Congratulations!”

“怎么样？”尤五问了这一句，
“How was it?” You asked.

又说：“老二说，她在床上……”
“Second Miss says that in bed she...”

“瞎三话四！”
“Nonsense!”

怡情老二赶紧拦住，同时又给了尤五一个白眼，
Second Miss hurriedly stopped him and gave him a sidelong glare.

“胡老爷自己不知道，
“Doesn't Mr Hu know for himself?

要你来说？”
Must you tell him?”

“是啊！
“Exactly,” Gu said.

阿巧姐好在哪里，小爷叔身历其境，
“Little Uncle has experienced A’qiao's merits first-hand.

最清楚不过，
Who could know better?

何用旁人告诉他？”
He hardly needs anyone else to explain.”

古应春这一说，胡雪岩才完全懂得，急于求得补偿的心也更热了，
Only now did Hu fully understand, and his desire to make up for the lost opportunity grew stronger.

然而口中却不知道该怎么说才好？
Yet he did not know what to say.

唯有笑而不答。
He could only smile.

“先吃饭，还是先谈事？”
“Shall we eat first or discuss business?”

古应春一面问，一面从怀里掏出两张纸来。
Gu drew two sheets of paper from his clothes.

“先谈事吧！”
“Business first.”

胡雪岩望着一窗的好太阳，兴致勃勃地问：“老古，你的马车坐了来没有？”
Looking at the sunshine filling the window, Hu asked eagerly, “Did you bring your carriage, Old Gu?”

“在弄堂口。
“It's at the lane entrance.

你要到哪里去？”
Where do you want to go?”

“难得有空，又是好天气，
“Such good weather, and a rare free afternoon!

我想好好去逛半天。”
I'd like a proper outing.”

那三个人互相望了望，
The three exchanged glances.

仍旧是古应春开口动问：
Gu spoke again.

“你预备怎么逛法？
“What have you in mind?

我来替你安排。”
I'll arrange it.”

“回头再说。”
“We can discuss that later.”

胡雪岩指着他手中的纸问：

“What are those?”

“两通信稿子。
“Two draft letters.

你看吧！”一通是致王有龄的，请他出信给何桂清，介绍古应春去谒见，一通是致刘不才的，要他到上海来。

胡雪岩看完，仍旧交了回去，请古应春誉正发出。
Hu read them and handed them back, asking Gu to make fair copies and send them.


要谈的事，就是这些。
That completed their business.

开出饭来，
Lunch was served.

正在喝酒，阿巧姐到了，大大方方的一招手，最后向怡情老二抛了个眼色，
While they were drinking, A’qiao arrived, greeted them with an easy gesture, and finally gave Second Miss a glance.

两人走到后房会谈心。
The two went into a back room to talk privately.

“真不错！”古应春望着阿巧姐的苗条背影说，
“Very fine indeed!” Gu said, watching A’qiao's slender retreating figure.

“是扬州‘瘦马’的样子。
“She has the look of one of Yangzhou's ‘slender horses.’”

““什么‘瘦马’？
“Slender horse?

活马！”尤五笑道：
A lively horse!” You laughed.

“小爷叔，你怎么谢媒？”

Second Miss, naturally.”

“那容易。
“Easy enough.

回头我要到洋行里去，挑点首饰，

不过，”
Still...”

“你买首饰给哪个？
“Whom are you buying jewellery for?

阿巧是厉害角色，

“如果她是厉害角色，就不会当我洋盘。”
“If she's truly shrewd, she won't take me for one.”

“对！”
“Exactly!”

古应春击节称赏，

“小爷叔这句话，真是一针见血，

深极了。”

“也好！”尤五笑着对胡雪岩说，

“It's rare enough for you to play the gullible customer.

老二就不必了。”
Second Miss needn't go.”

“Let her come too!

胡雪岩说。“打搅老二的地方很多，

于是喝着酒谈些夷场趣事。

不久，看见怡情老二和阿巧姐一前一后走了出来，一个是春风满面，一个是故作矜持，反正神色之间，都显得不平常。

“都坐下来吃吧！”
“Sit down and eat.”

怡情老二坐下来当女主人，阿巧则无论如何不肯，
Second Miss took her place as hostess, but A’qiao refused.

说“没有这个规矩”，
“It isn't the rule.”

侍立在旁，递菜热酒，
She stood beside them, passing dishes and warming wine.
