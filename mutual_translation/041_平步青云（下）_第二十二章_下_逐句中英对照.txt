Everywhere you go, the women favour you.”

“I don't know what to do myself now.

“Don't worry.

我托了个人在那里，
I've entrusted someone with that.

尤五哥也认识的，
Brother Five knows him too.

If there's any word from the foreigners, he'll come to find Brother Five.

Nothing will be delayed.”

“番菜真没有吃头，
“There's nothing satisfying about Western food.

Outside the restaurant they parted, some to visit friends, others to attend to business, agreeing to meet that evening for a courtesan banquet at Yiqing House.


胡雪岩这两天的心有点野了，
Hu's thoughts had strayed from work these past two days.

正经事虽有许多，却懒得去管，
Though there was plenty of serious business to attend to, he could not be bothered with it.

仍旧回到客栈，打算静下心来，将公私杂务，好好想它一想。

“Not long ago.

“坐，坐！
“Sit down, sit down.

湖州怎么样？”

胡雪岩问道，“到上海来作啥？”

“王大老爷叫我来的。
“Prefect Wang sent me.

有封信在这里。”
I have a letter here.”


拆开信一看，又是求援。

为了漕米改为海运，原来粮船上的旗丁水手，既无口粮，又少人约束，
Since the tribute grain had been transferred to the sea route, the transport servicemen and sailors formerly employed on the grain boats had lost their rations and were largely without supervision.

所以往往聚众闹事，甚至发生抢案，
They frequently gathered to make trouble, and there had even been robberies.

黄宗汉颇为头涌。
Huang Zonghan found the situation a serious headache.

由于王有龄在筹办海运时，对这方面曾有建议，要为旗丁水手，妥筹生计，所以黄宗汉仍旧责成他设法安抚。
During the preparations for sea transport, Wang Youling had proposed making proper arrangements for these men's livelihood, so Huang now made him responsible for finding a way to pacify them.

王有龄在信中说，如果当初照他的条陈，拨出一笔费用来办理这事，比较容易收功，
Wang wrote that success would have been relatively easy if funds had been allocated as he originally recommended.

因循未办，
Instead, the matter had been allowed to slide.

如今看形势不妙，再来安抚，显得是受了此辈的威胁挟制，事倍功半，
To offer relief now that the situation looked dangerous would seem to be yielding to their threats and pressure, doubling the effort for half the result.

十分棘手。
It was a most awkward business.

同时湖州的团练，正在密锣紧鼓地编练，
Meanwhile, the Huzhou militia was being organised and trained at a furious pace.

而江浙交界的平望、泗安两处防务，又相当重要，经常耍去察看，
The defences at Pingwang and Si’an, on the Jiangsu-Zhejiang border, were important too and required frequent inspections.

他实在无力来顾及此事。
He simply could not attend to this as well.

本来想推给嵇鹤龄，再又想到，推给了嵇鹤龄，他仍旧要求助于胡雪岩，与其如此，不如直接写信乞援。
At first he had thought of passing it to Ji Heling; then he realised that Ji would still have to seek Hu's help, so he might as well write directly.

希望胡雪岩能请尤五一起到浙江去一趟，以同为漕帮的情谊，设法排解。
He hoped Hu could invite You to accompany him to Zhejiang and use the bonds of their common brotherhood to settle the trouble.

“王大老爷叫了我去，当面跟我说，他也晓得胡先生很忙，
“Prefect Wang summoned me and told me himself that he knew how busy you were,” Chen added.

如果真的分不开身，叫我陪了尤五爷去。”
“If you really can't get away, I'm to accompany Fifth Master You.”

“这件事有点麻烦。
“This could be troublesome.

他们槽帮里面的事，外人不清楚。
Outsiders don't understand the internal affairs of their brotherhood.

尤五跟浙江漕帮的头脑，是不是有交情，还不晓得。
We don't even know whether You has any connection with its leaders in Zhejiang.

说不定不肯插手。”胡雪岩又说，“你郁四叔怎么说？”
He may refuse to get involved.” Hu added, “What did your Uncle Four say?”

“请尤五爷去排解，就是郁四叔出的主意。”
“It was Uncle Four's idea to ask Fifth Master You to mediate.”

“喔！”
“Oh!”

胡雪岩欣慰的说，
Hu was reassured.

“那就不错了。
“Then that sounds promising.

走！
Come along.

我们到恰情院去。”
Let's go to Yiqing House.”


于是一起到了怡情老二的小房子里，
They went together to Second Miss's little house.

尤五还没有回来，胡雪岩便趁此机会，向陈世龙细问湖州的情形，
You had not yet returned, and Hu used the opportunity to question Chen closely about Huzhou.

不过陈世龙又说了他的看法，认为这是一时的现象，
Chen thought this was only temporary.

如果有钱，可以放给蚕农，明年以新丝作抵，倒是一笔好生意。

“有钱，好做的生意多得很，
“With money, there are plenty of good businesses to be done.

眼前还谈不到明年的事。”胡雪岩说，
It's too soon to discuss next year,” Hu replied.

How many households in Huzhou Prefecture are raising silkworms?

Don't try to save a few small coins.”


“是的。”
“Yes.”

“That's what I said.

That calculation doesn't work.”


他说一句，阿世龙应一句，

A’qiao put in quickly.

都是实惠的东西，

Even if I hadn't brought them, she'd have to spend money buying them.”

接着是尤五和胡雪岩一路走，一面谈着，相偕出现，

陈世龙听出来，他们去拜访了一位人物，这位人物对于调处浙江溜帮的纠纷，大有用处，

所以相当拘谨，跟胡雪岩隔得远远地。
