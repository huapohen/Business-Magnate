第二十三章｜CHAPTER TWENTY-THREE


回到大兴客栈，阿巧姐一面收拾随身动用什物，一面问起胡雪岩此行的目的，
Back at Daxing Inn, A’qiao packed the things they would need and asked Hu Xueyan about the purpose of the journey.

这没有什么隐瞒的必要，
There was no reason to conceal it.

而且也深知她不是那种无知无识，不懂轻重的妇女，所以他把实话都告诉了她。
He knew, moreover, that she was no ignorant woman without judgment or a sense of what mattered, so he told her the truth.

“学台是个啥个官？”
“What sort of official is an education commissioner?”

“专管考秀才的。”
“He oversees the examinations for licentiates.”

“有没有外快？”
“Does he have any extra income?”

“这我倒不大懂了。”胡雪岩说，
“That I don't really understand.

“听说四川学台、广东学台是肥缺。
I hear that Sichuan and Guangdong are lucrative appointments.

江苏就不晓得了。
I don't know about Jiangsu.

照我想，现在兵荒马乱，好些地方连去都不能去。
But with war and disorder everywhere, there are many places he can't even visit.

地盘一小，就有外快也有限。
With his territory reduced, any extra income must be limited.”

““如果是这样子，要请何学台去谋干一个好地方的官，只怕不成功。”
“If that's so, asking Commissioner He to seek office in a good province may not work.”

“怎么呢？”
“Why?”

“要钱呀！”
“It takes money!”

阿巧姐笑一笑又说，
She smiled.

“我是不懂啥！
“Not that I understand these things.

有一次一个候补道台汪老爷在怡情院请客，大讲官场的生意经，
But once, at Yiqing House, a Mr Wang awaiting a circuit appointment gave a party and talked at length about the business side of official life.

说是京里的大老倌那里，都要送钱的。
You have to send money to the great gentlemen in the capital, he said.

钱越多，越容易升官。”
The more money, the easier promotion becomes.”

“嗯，嗯！”
“Mm, mm.”

胡雪岩被提醒了，暗地里打了主意，却不愿说破，
Reminded of this, Hu privately formed a plan, but did not explain it.

因为其中出入关系甚大，即令是对阿巧姐这样的人，也是不说的好。
Too much depended on it; even to a woman like A’qiao, it was better left unsaid.

“总还要送点礼啊！”阿巧姐又说。
“You must at least take some gifts,” she added.

“那有了，备了四色洋货。”
“I have those—four kinds of foreign goods.”

“何学台哪里人？”
“Where is Commissioner He from?”

“云南。”
“Yunnan.”

“那不如送云南东西……”
“Then wouldn't things from Yunnan be better?”

“啊，对！”
“Ah, exactly!”

胡雪岩大力赞赏：
Hu praised her warmly.

“阿巧，你的脑筋真不错。”
“A’qiao, you have a very good head.”


于是第二天一早，胡雪岩便去寻古应春，要觅云南土产，
Early the next morning, Hu went to Gu Yingchun for help in finding Yunnan specialities.

结果找着一个解铜到江苏藩司衙门的云南候补州判，
They eventually found a Yunnan official awaiting appointment as an assistant departmental magistrate, who had escorted copper to the Jiangsu provincial treasurer's yamen.

在他那里转让了四佯云南土产。这四样土产是宣威火腿、紫大头菜、鸡踪菌和咸牛肉干，
From him they obtained four local products: Xuanwei ham, purple datoucai, jizong mushrooms and dried salted beef.

可惜数量不多，但也正因为数量不多，便显得物以稀为贵了。
Unfortunately, there was not much of any of them, but their scarcity made them all the more precious.

中午在怡情老二那里吃了饭，彼此约定，互不相送。
After lunch at Second Miss's, the party agreed not to see one another off.

等古应春替他安排护送的那个人一到，胡雪岩很客气地请教了“尊姓台甫”，然后一起上船，
When the man Gu had arranged to escort Hu arrived, Hu courteously asked his name, and they boarded together.

船是小火轮拖带的一条“无锡快”，
Their vessel was a ‘Wuxi fast boat’ towed by a small steam launch.

胡雪岩带着阿巧姐住后舱，前舱止给护送的那个人住。
Hu and A’qiao occupied the aft cabin, while the forward cabin was left to their escort.


此人名叫周一鸣，湖南人，
His name was Zhou Yiming, and he came from Hunan.

原在江南水师中当哨官，因为喜欢喝酒闹事，
He had once served as a detachment officer in the Jiangnan naval force, but was fond of drinking and making trouble.

一次打伤了长官的小舅子，被责了二十军棍，开革除名。
After injuring his superior's younger brother-in-law, he had received twenty strokes of the military cane and been dismissed.

但同一鸣的酒德虽不好，为人倒极豪爽重义气，
Though badly behaved in drink, Zhou was generous and loyal to his friends.

由于在水师当差，认识的船户颇不少，所以起先是跑码头、打秋风，大家也乐予周济，
His naval service had acquainted him with many boatmen, so at first he went round the wharves seeking handouts, which people willingly gave.

有时托他带个把口信，
Sometimes they asked him to carry a message.

他倒也“食人之禄，忠人之事”，一定确确实实做到，
Having accepted their support, he faithfully discharged their business, making certain that every errand was properly done.

慢慢地有了信用，便在上海船户的“茶会”上帮忙。
Gradually he earned a reputation and began helping at the Shanghai boatmen's tea association.

各行各业的茶会，犹如同业公所，
Such associations existed in every trade and functioned much like guildhalls.

或者按头生意，或者与官场打交道，或者同业中有纠纷“吃讲茶”，都在茶会上商谈，
Whether to arrange business, deal with officials or settle disputes over tea, members discussed their affairs there.

周一鸣就成了船户茶会上的一名要角，
Zhou became an important figure in the boatmen's association.

特别是“抓船”、“派差”等等官面上硬压下来的公事，都由周一鸣出面去接头。
In particular, when officials imposed requisitions for boats or other compulsory service, he handled the negotiations.

这次也是有公事到苏州，
He had official business in Suzhou on this occasion.

古应春跟他相熟，正好把胡雪岩托了他，
Gu, who knew him, therefore entrusted Hu to his care.

连雇船带护送，都归他包办，讲好送二十两银子。
He would hire the boat and provide the escort, for an agreed twenty taels.

胡雪岩的出手大方是出名的，
Hu was famous for his generosity.

一上船就找了个红封套，装了一张三十两银子的银票，当面双手奉上。
As soon as they boarded, he put a thirty-tael note into a red envelope and presented it with both hands.

周一鸣还要客气，禁不住胡雪岩言词恳切，他千恩万谢地收了下来。
Zhou tried to decline, but Hu was so earnest that he accepted with repeated thanks.

这一路招呼得自是格外周到。
His attentions throughout the journey were naturally all the more thorough.


胡雪岩出门一向不喜欢带听差，于是周一鸣自告奋勇，到了苏州雇轿子，提行李，下客栈，都由他一手经理。
Hu never liked taking a personal attendant on his travels, so Zhou volunteered to manage everything on arrival in Suzhou: hiring chairs, carrying luggage and finding an inn.

客栈在阎门外，字号就叫“金阎”，
The inn stood outside Chang Gate and was called Jinchang.

等安置停当，周一鸣要告辞了。
Once they were settled, Zhou prepared to leave.

“胡大老爷！”因为胡雪岩是捐班候补知县，所以他这要称呼他，“我在苏州有个‘门口’，现在回去看一看。
“Your Honour”—he addressed Hu this way because Hu had purchased eligibility for appointment as a county magistrate—“I have a place here in Suzhou and shall go and look in.

明天上半天到水师衙门去投文办事，
Tomorrow morning I must deliver documents and attend to business at the naval yamen.

中午过来伺候。
I'll come to wait on you at midday.

你老看，行不行？”
Would that suit you?”

“我有个不情之请。”胡雪岩说，
“I have a rather troublesome favour to ask.

“有四件东西，一封信，想拜托你此刻就送一送。”
Four gifts and a letter—could you deliver them now?”

“是了。”周一鸣问，
“Certainly.

“送到哪里？”
Where to?”

“送给何学台。
“To Education Commissioner He.

还得先打听一下，何学台公馆在哪里？”
First we must find out where he's staying.”

“这容易，
“That's easy.

都交给我好了。”
Leave it to me.”

于是胡雪岩托金间栈的帐房，写了个手本，下注：“寓阊门外金阎栈第三进西头”，
Hu had Jinchang Inn's bookkeeper prepare a formal visiting card, with the address below: “Lodging outside Chang Gate, Jinchang Inn, west side of the third courtyard.”

连同四样云南土仪和一封王有龄的信，都交了给周一鸣。
He gave this to Zhou, together with the four Yunnan gifts and Wang Youling's letter.

信是胡雪岩密封了的，内中附着一张五千两的银票，作为王有龄送何桂清的，
Hu had sealed the letter himself, enclosing a five-thousand-tael note as a gift from Wang to He Guiqing.

这封信当然重要，所以胡雪岩特别叮嘱：
The letter was important, so he gave particular instructions.

“老周，还要麻烦你，务必跟何公馆的门上说明白，讨一张有何学台亲笔的回片。”
“Old Zhou, one more trouble: you must explain to the doorkeeper that I need an acknowledgement in Commissioner He's own hand.”

“是！”周一鸣问，
“Yes.

“今天要不要把回片送来？”
Shall I bring it back today?”

胡雪岩心想，疑人莫用，用人莫疑，
Hu reflected that one should not employ a man one distrusted, or distrust a man one employed.

而且周一鸣人既重义气，又是有来历的，
Zhou valued loyalty and had an established background.

因而很快地答道：“如果回片上只写收到，那就不必来了，
He answered at once, “If the card merely acknowledges receipt, you needn't come again.

明天再说。”
Tomorrow will do.”


等周一鸣一走，胡雪岩的迫不及待的想跟阿巧姐去观光。
Once Zhou had gone, Hu was eager to go sightseeing with A’qiao.

苏州不比上海，
Suzhou was unlike Shanghai.

虽然妇女喜欢小庙烧香，凡有出会报赛等等人声鼎沸的场面，都要去轧个热闹，但一男一女不论是出现在玄妙观，还是虎丘山塘，总是招摇过市、惹人物议的一件事，
Women there liked visiting temples to burn incense and joining the crowds at festivals and processions, but for a man and woman to appear together, whether at Xuanmiao Temple or along Shantang by Tiger Hill, was to parade themselves before the public and invite gossip.

而且阿巧姐是本乡本土，难免遇见熟人，尤须顾忌，
A’qiao was a native of the district and might meet acquaintances, which required still greater caution.

因此，她更觉为难。
She was consequently reluctant.

就在这软语相磨，未定行止之际，只见周一鸣把顶红缨帽捏在手里当扇子扇，
While they were gently arguing the point, with no decision reached, Zhou returned, holding his red-tasselled cap as a fan.

跑得满头大汗，却是笑容满面，
He had run himself into a sweat, yet his face was all smiles.

胡雪岩当是何桂清有什么话交代，赶紧迎了出去。
Thinking He had sent some instructions, Hu hurried out.

“送到了！”周一鸣说，
“Delivered!

“回贴在这里。”
Here is the reply.”

接过回贴来一看，
Hu took the card.

只见上面写着一行字：“王太守函一件，收讫。
It read, “One letter from Prefect Wang received.

外隆仪四色，敬领谢谢。”
The four generous gifts are also accepted with thanks.”

贴尾又有一行字，“敬使面致。”
A further line at the foot stated, “Your messenger has been duly thanked in person.”

“胡大老爷，真要谢谢你挑我。”周一鸣垂着手打个千说：
Zhou made a formal greeting, hands lowered. “Your Honour, I must thank you for giving me this opportunity.

“何学台出手很阔，
Commissioner He is most generous.

赏了我二十两银子。”
He gave me twenty taels.”

听这一说，胡雪岩觉得很有面子，便说：
This enhanced Hu's own standing, and he was pleased.

“很好，
“Excellent.

你收下好了。”
Keep it.”

“我特为跟你老来说一声，何学台住在苏州府学。”
“I came specially to tell you that the commissioner is staying at the Suzhou Prefectural Academy.”

“喔，
“Oh.

你见着何学台没有？”
Did you see him?”

“见是没有见着。
“No.

不过听他们二爷出来说，学台很高兴。”
But his steward came out and said he was delighted.”

高兴的是收到五千两银子，还是四色云南土产，或则两者兼而有之？胡雪岩就不知道了。
Whether He was delighted by the five thousand taels, the four Yunnan gifts, or both, Hu could not tell.

不过不管怎么样，都算是得阿巧姐的力。
In any event, he owed it to A’qiao.


因为如此，他便依从了她的意思，不勉强她一起出游。
For that reason, Hu accepted her wishes and did not press her to go out with him.

但打算一个人出去逛逛，这得先跟阿巧姐请教，
He still intended to take a walk alone, but first sought her advice.

正在谈着苏州城里的名园古刹，突然发现金阎栈的掌拒，行色匆匆，直奔了进来。
They were discussing Suzhou's famous gardens and old temples when the innkeeper hurried straight in.

“胡大老爷，胡大老爷！”掌柜说道：
“Your Honour!

“何学台来拜，
Education Commissioner He has come to call.

已经下轿了。”
He's already out of his chair.”

听这一说，胡雪岩倒有些着慌，
Hu was alarmed.

第一，没有听差“接贴”，
First, he had no attendant to receive the visiting card.

第二，自己该穿公服肃迎，时间上来不及了。
Second, he ought to put on official dress to welcome his visitor, and there was no time.

所以一时有手足无措之感。
For a moment he did not know what to do.

还是阿巧姐比较沉着，
A’qiao was more composed.

“何学台穿啥衣服来的？”她问。
“What is the commissioner wearing?”

“穿的便服。”
“Informal clothes.”

“这还好！”胡雪岩接口说道：
“That's a relief,” Hu said.

“来不及了，
“There's no time.

我也只好便服相迎。”
I shall have to receive him informally too.”

说着，他便走了出去，
He went out.

阿巧姐也赶紧将屋里刚刚倒散未曾归理的行李，略略收拾了一下，在窗口张望，只等何桂清一到，便要回避。
A’qiao hastily tidied the luggage they had unpacked but not yet put away, then watched at the window, ready to withdraw as soon as He arrived.


何桂清是走到第二进中门遇着胡雪岩的。
He Guiqing met Hu at the middle gateway of the second courtyard.

虽然穿的便衣，但跟着两名青衣小帽的听差，便能认出他的身分，
Though informally dressed, he was followed by two attendants in dark gowns and small caps, which revealed his standing.

胡雪岩却还下敢造次，
Hu nevertheless dared not take anything for granted.

站住脚一青，
He stopped for a look.

这位来客年纪与自己用仿，生得极白净的一张脸，这模样与王有龄所形容的何桂清的仪表，完全相符，
The visitor was about his own age, with an exceptionally fair, clean-cut face, exactly as Wang had described.

便知再不得错了。
There could be no mistake.

“何大人！”他迎面请个安说：
He stepped forward and made a formal bow of greeting. “Your Excellency!

“真不敢当。”
I don't deserve such an honour.”

“请起，请起！”
“Please rise, please rise.”

何桂清拱拱手说：
He returned a clasped-hands salute.

“想来足下就是雪岩兄了？”
“You must be Brother Xueyan?”

“不敢当此称呼！
“I don't deserve that form of address.

我是胡雪岩。”
I am Hu Xueyan.”

“幸会之至。”说着，何桂清又移动了脚步。于是胡雪岩引路，将何桂清引到自己屋里。
“A great pleasure to meet you.” He began walking again, and Hu led him towards his rooms.

就这几步路，做主人的转了好些念头，
In those few steps, many thoughts passed through the host's mind.

他发觉情况很尴尬，
The situation was awkward.

二品大员拜访一个初交，地点又是在客栈里，既没有象佯的堂奥可以容纳贵客，又没有听差可以供奔走之役。
An official of the second rank was calling on a new acquaintance at an inn, with neither a proper reception hall for such a guest nor servants to attend them.

这样子就很难讲官场的仪节了。
The ceremonies of official life would be difficult to observe.

索性当他自己人！
He would simply treat He as an intimate.

胡雪岩断然作了这样一个决定，首先就改了称呼，
Having decided, he first changed his form of address.

何桂清字根云，便仿照“雪公”的例，称他“云公”。
He's courtesy name was Genyun; following the familiar respectful form he used for Wang as Mr Xuexuan, Hu addressed He as Mr Genyun.

接入客座，他这样说道：“公云，礼不可废，
“Mr Genyun,” he said on bringing him into the reception area, “we mustn't neglect propriety.

请上坐，让我这个候补知县参见！”
Please take the place of honour and let this county magistrate awaiting appointment pay his respects.”

这是打的一个“过门”，
This was merely an opening flourish.

既是便服，又是这样的称呼，根本就没有以官场礼节参见的打算，
Informal dress and so familiar an address showed that he had no intention of observing full official ceremony.

何桂清是绝顶聪明的人，一听就懂，
He, a man of exceptional intelligence, understood at once.

再替他设身处地想一想，倒又佩服他这别出一格的处置，
Putting himself in Hu's place, he rather admired this unconventional solution.

因而笑道：
He smiled.

“雪岩兄，不要说杀风景的话。
“Brother Xueyan, don't spoil the occasion.

我听雪轩谈过老兄，神交已久，
I've heard Xuexuan speak of you and have long felt I knew you.

要脱略形迹才好！”
Let's dispense with formalities.”

“是！
“Certainly.

恭敬不如从命！”
Obedience is better than ceremony.”

胡雪岩一捐到地，站起身来说：
Hu made a deep bow, then straightened.

“ 请里面坐吧！”
“Please come inside.”


这才真的是脱略形迹，一见面就延入内室，
This was truly dispensing with formality: at their first meeting, Hu invited him straight into the inner room.

何桂清略一踌躇，也就走了进去。
He hesitated briefly, then entered.

一进门却又赶紧退了出来，因为看到一具闺阁中用的镜箱，还有两件女衣。
But he immediately stepped out again on seeing a woman's mirror case and two articles of female clothing.

“宝眷大此，
“Your lady is here.

不好唐突！”
I mustn't intrude.”

“不妨，不妨。”胡雪岩一面说，一面便喊：“阿巧，你出来见见何老爷。”
“Not at all, not at all.” Hu called, “A’qiao, come and meet Mr He.”

何桂清还在迟疑之际，突然眼前一亮，就不肯再退出去了，
While He was still hesitating, his eyes suddenly brightened, and he no longer wished to withdraw.

望着走几步路如风摆杨柳似的阿巧姐，向胡雪岩问道：“怎么称呼？
Watching A’qiao approach, swaying gracefully like a willow in the breeze, he asked Hu, “How should I address her?

是如嫂夫人？”
Is she your concubine?”

“不是！”胡雪岩说：
“No.

“云公叫她小名阿巧好了。”
Just call her by her own name, A’qiao.”

就这对答间，阿巧姐已经含笑叫一声：
As they spoke, she smiled and greeted him.

“何老爷！”
“Mr He.”

同时盈盈下拜。
Then she gracefully sank to her knees.

“不敢当，不敢当！
“I don't deserve this.

请起来。”
Please rise!”

男女授受不亲，不便动手去扶，到底让阿巧姐跪了一跪，
Propriety forbade physical contact between men and women, so he could not help her up and had to accept the kneeling salutation.

她站起来说一声：“何老爷请坐！”然后翩然走了出去，
She rose, invited him to sit and went lightly out.

听她在喊客栈里的伙计泡盖碗茶。
They heard her calling to the inn's waiter for tea in lidded cups.


真是当做自己人看待，何桂清也就不再拘束，
Treated genuinely as one of the household, He dropped his reserve.

坐在窗前上首一张椅子上，首先向胡雪岩道谢：
Taking the chair in the place of honour by the window, he first thanked Hu.

“多蒙专程下顾，隆仪尤其心感。
“You've honoured me with a special journey, and I'm especially touched by the gifts.

天南万里，何况烽火，
My home is so far away in the south, with war between us as well.

居然得尝家乡风味，太难得了。”
To taste its flavours here is a rare pleasure.”

“说实话，是阿巧姐的主意。”
“To tell the truth, it was A’qiao's idea.”
