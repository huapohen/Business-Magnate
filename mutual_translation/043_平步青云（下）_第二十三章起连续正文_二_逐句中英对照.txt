But Hu had come to savour the atmosphere of a Suzhou tavern, not its food, and did not much mind.

等坐定了，吃过一巡酒，他放眼四顾，开始观察，

苏州本地人雍容揖让，文文气气，一望而知，
Suzhou natives were easily recognized by their composed manners, courteous deference and scholarly air.

The two old men were recalling the Jiaqing years, discussing inkstones, Yixing Gongchun teapots and bamboo carvings—the elegant playthings of scholars in a peaceful, prosperous age.

“Simply to eat and drink his way through life?”

Zhou stared blankly.

He could not answer; he could scarcely even understand the question.

“我是说，象隔壁那两位老太爷，”胡雪岩放低了声音说：

“大概是靠收租过日子的乡绅。
“They're probably country gentry living on rents.

这样的人家，我们杭州也很多，
We have plenty of such families in Hangzhou.

祖上做过官，挣下一批田地，
An ancestor held office and acquired some land.

Unless a spendthrift appears, quiet living leaves enough to feed a generation or two.

本身也总有个把功名，
The men themselves generally have some examination standing.

好一点是进过学的秀才，不然就是二三十两银子捐来的监生，

They count as people of standing too.

一年到头无事忙，白天孵茶馆，晚上‘摆一碗’，

逍遥自在到六七十岁，一口气不来，回老家见阎王，
They drift pleasantly on until sixty or seventy, then stop breathing and go home to meet the King of Hell.

说是我阳世里走过一遭了。
‘I've had my turn in the world of the living,’ they say.

问他阳世里做点啥？啥也不做！
Ask what they did there, and the answer is nothing!

胡雪岩深为满意。
Hu was thoroughly pleased.

The results were good.

He did not mind and readily agreed.

“我伺候胡大老爷去。”
“I'll attend you.”

于是雇好一顶轿子，
They hired a sedan chair.

周一鸣持着拜匣，跟随胡雪岩到了潘家。
Carrying the card case, Zhou accompanied Hu to the Pan house.

帖子一投进去，潘家的男主人莫名其妙，
When the card was brought in, the master was bewildered.

但他的姨太太心里明白，说了经过，
His concubine understood, however, and explained.

方始恍然，立刻吩咐接见。
Once he grasped the situation, he immediately ordered that the visitor be received.
