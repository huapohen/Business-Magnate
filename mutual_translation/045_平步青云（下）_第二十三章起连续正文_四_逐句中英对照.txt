* * *小狗子果然很巴结，
* * * Xiaogouzi proved most eager to oblige.

“午炮”刚刚放过，人就来了，一共来了五个人，
The noon gun had scarcely sounded when he arrived with his party, five men in all.

三个留在院子里，带着麻袋和扁担。一个带进屋来，不用说，是阿巧姐的丈夫。
Three remained in the courtyard with sacks and carrying poles; the one he brought indoors was, plainly, A’qiao's husband.

据说他姓陈。四十岁左右，畏畏缩缩是个极老实的人，
Said to be surnamed Chen, he was about forty, a timid, thoroughly honest-looking fellow.

臃臃肿肿一件棉袄，外面罩着件簇新的毛蓝布衫，赤脚草鞋。
Over a bulky padded jacket he wore a brand-new blue cotton gown, with straw sandals on his bare feet.

进得门来，只缩在门边，脸上说不出是忸怩还是害怕。
Once inside, he shrank against the doorway, looking either embarrassed or frightened.

“请坐，请坐！”
“Sit down, please.”

胡雪岩转脸问小狗子，
Hu turned to Xiaogouzi.

“都谈好了？”
“Everything agreed?”

“谈好了。”
“Everything.”

说着，他从身上掏出来两张桑皮纸的笔据，
He took out two written agreements on mulberry-bark paper.

连“休书”都预备好了。
Even the letter of divorce was ready.

胡雪岩接过来看了一遍，
Hu read them through.

写得十分扎实，表示满意，
They were drawn up very securely, and he expressed his satisfaction.

“就这样！”
“This will do.”

他指着周一鸣说，“我们这面的中人在这里，
Pointing to Zhou Yiming, he said, “Here's the intermediary for our side.

你算是那方面的中人。
You represent the other side.

还要个‘代笔’，
We still need a scribe.

就挑金阊栈的帐房赚几个。”
Let the bookkeeper at Jinchang Inn earn a little.”

“胡大老爷，”小狗子赶紧抢着说，“代笔我们带来了。”接着便往外喊了一声：“刘先生！”

五个人当中，只有这个“刘先生”是穿了长衫的，
Of the five men, this Mr Liu alone wore a long gown.

獐头鼠目，不似善类。
His furtive, ratlike appearance suggested no good character.


胡雪岩忽然动了疑心，
Hu suddenly grew suspicious.

然后发觉自己有一步棋，非走不可的，却忘了去走。
Then he realized that he had forgotten an indispensable move.

因此，一面敷衍着，一面把周一鸣拉到一边，悄悄说道：“有件事，我疏忽了。

你看，这姓陈的，象不象阿巧姐的男人？”
Does this Chen fellow look like A’qiao's husband to you?”

“这怎么看得出来？”
“How could anyone tell by looking?”

“万一是冒充的，怎么办？
“Suppose he's an impostor?

钱还是小事，要闹大笑话！”
The money would be a small matter; we'd make fools of ourselves.

胡雪岩说，“我昨天忘了关照一句话，应该请他们族长到场。”
Yesterday I forgot to tell them to bring their clan head.”

“那也可以。
“That can still be done.

我跟小狗子去说。”
I'll speak to Xiaogouzi.”

“一来一往，耽误工夫也麻烦。”
“Going back and forth would waste time and cause trouble.

胡雪岩说：“只要‘验明正身’，不是冒充，他们陈家族长来不来，倒也不生关系。”
So long as we establish his identity and know he isn't an impostor, it doesn't matter whether the Chen clan head comes.”

“哪个晓得他是不是冒充？”周一鸣说，
“Who can tell whether he's an impostor?” Zhou said.

“除非请阿巧姐自己来认。”
“Unless we ask A’qiao herself to identify him.”

这倒是一语破的！
That went straight to the heart of it.

除此以外，别无善策，
There was no better way.

胡雪岩考虑了一下，断然定下了缓兵之计。
Hu considered, then resolved on a delaying manoeuvre.

于是周一鸣受命招待小狗子吃午饭，胡雪岩则以要到钱庄去兑银子作托词，出了金阊栈，坐轿直奔潘家。
Zhou was told to take Xiaogouzi to lunch, while Hu left Jinchang Inn on the pretext of going to a bank to obtain silver, and took a sedan chair straight to the Pans' house.


一张名帖，附上一个丰腴的门包，胡雪岩向潘家的门房，坦率道明来意，
With a visiting card and a generous gratuity for the doorkeeper, Hu frankly explained his purpose.

他家主人见不见都无所谓，目的是要跟阿巧姐见面。
Whether the master received him did not matter; he had come to see A’qiao.

潘叔雅是惮于世俗应酬的“大少爷”，
Pan Shuya was a young master who dreaded worldly social obligations.

听得门房的通报，乐得偷懒，便请阿巧姐径自出见。
On hearing the doorkeeper's report, he was glad of the excuse to spare himself the trouble and asked A’qiao to go out directly.

她一见胡雪岩空手上门，颇为失望，不免埋怨，
Seeing Hu arrive empty-handed, she was disappointed and could not help complaining.

“你也要替我做做人！
“You ought to help me make a proper return.

我在这里，人家客气得不得了，真正叫人不安。”
They're so kind to me here that I really feel uneasy.”

“你放心！
“Don't worry.

我已经打算好了，
I've already made plans.

一定叫你有面子。
You'll certainly have every reason to hold your head high.

现在闲话少说，
But there's no time for that now.

你马上跟我回客栈，去认一个人。”
Come back to the inn at once and identify someone.”

“认一个人！
“Identify someone?

认哪个？”
Who?”

阿巧姐眨闪着极长的睫毛，异常困惑的问。
She blinked her exceptionally long lashes, thoroughly puzzled.

“你想想看，
“Think.

还有哪个是非要你去认不可的？”
Who else could there be that only you could identify?”

这句反问，就点得很清楚了，然而阿巧姐却越感困惑，
The question made his meaning plain, yet she was more bewildered than ever.

“到底怎么回事？”
“What exactly has happened?”

她有些不悦，觉得胡雪岩办这样的大事，不该不先商量一下，
She was displeased that he should handle so important a matter without consulting her.

所以很认真的表示：“你不说清楚，我决不去。”
“Unless you explain, I won't go,” she said firmly.

胡雪岩十分见机，赔着笑说：
Hu quickly took the hint and smiled apologetically.

“你不要怪我独断独行，
“Don't blame me for acting on my own.

一则是没有机会跟你说，二则是免得你操心，
First, I had no chance to tell you; second, I wanted to spare you worry.

我是好意。”
I meant well.”

“谢谢你的好意。”
“Thank you for your good intentions.”

阿巧姐接受了他的解释，但多少还有些余憾，
She accepted his explanation, though some resentment remained.

而且发觉处境颇为尴尬，
She also found her position awkward.

“当面锣，对面鼓，你叫我怎么认法。”
“Face to face, like a gong answering a drum—how am I supposed to identify him?”

“不是，不是！”
“No, no.

用不着你照面，
You needn't face him.

你只要在壁缝里张一张，
Just look through a crack in the partition.

认清楚了人，就没你的事了。“接着，胡雪岩把如何收服了小狗子的话，扼要说了一遍。
Once you've identified him, there's nothing more for you to do.” He briefly explained how he had brought Xiaogouzi round.


“你的花样真多！”阿巧姐笑着说了这一句，
“You have so many tricks!” A’qiao said with a smile.

脸色突然转为严肃，
Then her face suddenly grew serious.

眼望着砖地，好久不作声。
She looked down at the brick floor and remained silent for a long time.

这神态使得胡雪岩有些着急，同时也有些失悔，事情真的做得欠检点了！
Hu became anxious and began to regret his lack of care.

阿巧姐与她丈夫的感情不太好，只是听了怡情老二的片面之词，
That she and her husband were on bad terms was something he had heard only from Second Miss at Yiqing House.

她本人虽也在行为上表现出来，与夫家几乎已断绝往来，但这种门户人家的话，靠不住的居多，
Her own conduct did suggest that she had almost severed relations with her husband's family, but much of what people in such establishments said was unreliable.

俗语说得好：“骗死人不偿命”。
As the saying went, they could deceive a man to death without paying with their lives.

自己竟信以为真，一本正经去办，到了紧要关头，就会变成自讨苦吃，
He had taken it all for truth and set about the matter in earnest; at the critical moment, he might merely bring trouble upon himself.

阿巧姐固在不见得有意欺骗，
A’qiao might not have meant to deceive him.

然而“家家有本难念的经”，
Still, every household had its own difficult scripture to recite.

看样子是别有衷曲，须当谅解？
She evidently had some private concern that deserved understanding.

说来说去是自己鲁莽，怪不得她。
In the end, he had been rash and could not blame her.

怪不怪她在其次，
Whether to blame her was secondary, however.

眼前的难题是，阿巧姐如果不肯点头，小狗子那面就不好交代。
If she refused her consent now, how was he to explain matters to Xiaogouzi?

跑到苏州来做这么一件荒唐事，传出去成为笑话，自己的这个面子却丢不起。
To come to Suzhou on such an absurd errand and become a laughingstock was more loss of face than he could bear.

因而急于要讨她一句实话。
He urgently needed a truthful answer.

“阿巧姐！”他神色严重地说，“到这时候，你再不能敷衍我了，
“Sister A’qiao,” he said gravely, “you can't put me off any longer.

你心里的意思，到底怎么佯，要跟我实说！”
Tell me honestly what you really want.”

“咦！”
“Why!”

阿巧姐深感诧异：
She was astonished.

“我几时说假话敷衍过你？”
“When have I ever lied to put you off?”

“那么，事情到了这地步，你象煞要打退堂鼓，是为啥？”
“Then why do you seem ready to beat a retreat, now that things have come this far?”

阿巧姐觉得好笑，
She found this amusing.

“我又不曾象县大老爷那样坐堂，
“I've never sat in court like a county magistrate.

啥叫打退堂鼓？”她这样反诘。
How can I beat the drum that closes the hearing?”


话越发不对了，
Her words sounded more troublesome still.

细辨一辨，其中有刺，
On closer examination, there was a barb in them.

意思是说，胡雪岩做这件事之先，既未告诉过她，更未征求同意，这就是“不曾坐堂”，然则又何来“退堂鼓”可言？
Before undertaking the matter, Hu had neither told her nor obtained her consent: she had never ‘opened court’, so how could she now beat the drum to close it?

胡雪岩心想，阿巧姐是厉害角色，
She was a formidable woman.

此时不宜跟她讲理，因为自己道理欠缺，讲不过她。
This was no time to reason with her, since the right was not on his side and he could not win the argument.

唯有动之以情，甚至骗一骗她再说。
He could only appeal to her feelings, even deceive her a little if necessary.

于是他先认错：
First, he admitted his fault.

“这件事怪我不好。
“I've handled this badly.

不过我一定顺你的心意，决不勉强。
But I'll certainly follow your wishes and won't force you.

现在人在那里，
The man is there now.

你先去认一认，再作道理。
Come and identify him first, and we'll decide afterwards.

人不对，不必再谈，
If he's the wrong man, there's nothing more to discuss.

人对了，看你的意思，
If he's the right one, it's entirely up to you.

你说东就东，你说西就西，我决无二话。
Whichever way you wish to go, I won't object.”

“人心到底是肉做的，
After all, a human heart was made of flesh.

听得他这样说，阿巧姐不能再迟疑了，
Hearing this, A’qiao could hesitate no longer.

其实她的迟疑，倒不是对她丈夫还有什么余情不忍割舍，
In fact, she was not reluctant because of lingering affection for her husband.

只是想到她娘家，应该让胡雪岩拿笔钱出来，替她娘养老。
She was thinking of her own family: Hu ought to provide some money to support her mother in old age.

这个条件，似乎应该在此时一并来谈，却又不知如何谈法？
This condition seemed best discussed along with the other arrangements, but she did not know how to raise it.

迟疑者在此，而胡雪岩是误会了。
That was the cause of her hesitation; Hu had misunderstood.

“那么你请坐一坐，
“Then sit down a moment.

我总要跟主人家去说一声。”她又问：“你可曾雇了轿子？”
I must tell my hosts.” She added, “Did you hire a chair?”

“这方便，
“Easily arranged.

我轿子留给你，我另雇一乘。”
I'll leave mine for you and hire another.

胡雪岩说，“到了金间栈，你从边门进来，
When you reach Jinchang Inn, come through the side entrance.

我叫人在那里等你。”
I'll have someone waiting.”


这样约定了，胡雪岩先离了潘家，
With this settled, Hu left first.

轿子是阊门附近的，坐过两回，已经熟识，
He had twice used these chair-bearers from near Chang Gate and knew them already.

等吩咐妥当，另雇一乘，赶回金阊栈，
After giving his instructions, he hired another chair and hurried back to Jinchang Inn.

再赁一间屋子，关照伙计，专门守在边门上，
There he rented an additional room and told an attendant to wait expressly at the side entrance.

等阿巧姐一到，悄悄引人，然后进来照一照面，无需开口。
When A’qiao arrived, he was to bring her in quietly, then show himself to Hu without speaking.

一切布置妥帖，胡雪岩方回到自己屋里，
Only after everything was arranged did Hu return to his own room.

坐候不久，周一鸣领着小狗子等人，吃了饭回来，
Before long, Zhou brought Xiaogouzi and the others back from lunch.

一个个脸上发红，似乎喝了不少酒。
All their faces were red; they seemed to have drunk a good deal.

彼此又作了一番寒暄，胡雪岩便海阔天空地谈苏州的风光，
After further courtesies, Hu launched into a wide-ranging account of Suzhou's scenery.

同一鸣会意，是要拖延辰光，就在一旁帮腔，
Zhou understood that he meant to gain time and joined in.

谈得极其热闹，却始终不提正事。
The conversation was lively, but never touched their business.

小狗子有些忍不住了，
Xiaogouzi could hardly bear it.

好不容易找到一个空隙，插进一句话去：“胡大老爷，我们今天还想赶回木读，
At last, finding an opening, he interjected, “Mr Hu, we still want to get back to Mudu today.

时间太迟了不方便。
It'll be inconvenient if we're too late.

现在就动手吧！”
Shall we begin?”

“喔，喔，”
“Oh, yes.

胡雪岩歉意地说：“对不起，对不起，
Forgive me,” Hu replied apologetically.

再略等一等，
“Just a little longer.

等钱庄的伙计一到，凑够了银钱，我们马上动手。
As soon as the bank's man comes and we have the full amount of silver, we'll begin.

好在只是画一个花押，
It's only a matter of putting down a mark.

快得很。”
It won't take long.”

这样一说，小狗子就又只好耐心等候，但局促不安的情状，越来越明显。
Xiaogouzi had to wait patiently again, though his uneasiness became increasingly apparent.

胡雪岩冷服旁观，心头疑云愈密，暗暗又打了第二个主意。
Watching coolly, Hu grew more suspicious and secretly devised a second plan.

正想托词把周一鸣找到一边商量，那守候的伙计出现了，
He was about to find an excuse to consult Zhou privately when the attendant appeared.

他也很机警，提着茶壶来冲茶，暗中使了一个眼色，
Showing presence of mind, the man came with a pot to replenish the tea and gave Hu a covert glance.

竟连周一鸣都不曾发觉。
Even Zhou failed to notice.


于是胡雪岩告个便，在另一层中见着阿巧姐，悄悄说道：
Hu excused himself and went to see A’qiao in another part of the inn.

“回头我引一个人出来，
“In a moment I'll bring someone out.

你细细看，不要作声。
Look carefully, but don't speak.

我马上又会回来。”
I'll come back shortly.”

Returning to the others, he beckoned to the supposed husband.

那个畏畏缩缩的中年人，只是望着小狗子，用眼色在讨主意。
The shrinking middle-aged man merely looked at Xiaogouzi, seeking guidance with his eyes.

“胡大老爷，你有啥话，跟我说！”
“Mr Hu, whatever you want to say, say it to me.”

“没有啥要紧话，
“Nothing important.

不过，这句话也不便让外人听见。”
But it isn't something for outsiders to hear.”

胡雪岩又连连招手，
Hu beckoned repeatedly.

“请过来，请过来。”
“Come here, please.”

乡下人纵或不上“台盘”，但私底下说句话，何至于如此畏缩不前？
A countryman might be unused to polite society, but why be so reluctant to exchange a few private words?

所以小狗子不便再加阻挠，
Xiaogouzi could hardly obstruct him further.

那个姓陈的，也只好硬着头皮，跟了主人出去。
Chen had no choice but to steel himself and follow his host outside.

胡雪岩是何等角色？
Hu was no ordinary man.

一看这姓陈的，木头人似地只由小狗子牵线，便不待阿巧姐来“验明正身”，即已料到了七八分，
Seeing Chen as wooden as a puppet, with Xiaogouzi pulling the strings, he had already guessed most of the truth without waiting for A’qiao's identification.

因而引到外面，面对着阿巧姐所隐藏的窗户，
He led him out to face the window behind which she was concealed.

他开口第一句话问的是：“你到底姓啥？”
His first question was, “What is your actual surname?”

“我姓陈。”
“Chen.”

这句话答得极爽利，显见不假，
The answer came readily enough to be true.

于是胡雪岩又问第二句：
Hu asked his second question.

“你是阿巧姐的什么人？”
“What relation are you to A’qiao?”

这句话问得他显了原形，
This exposed him.

支支吾吾地嗫嚅着不知所云。
He stammered and muttered incoherently.

果然，
Just as I thought!

胡雪岩暗叫一声：惭愧！
Hu silently reproached himself.

若非临时灵机一动，叫小狗子骗了一千多两银子去，
Without that last-minute inspiration, Xiaogouzi would have cheated him of more than a thousand taels.

那才真是明沟里翻船，吃了亏还不能声张，声张出去，是个绝大的话柄。
He would have capsized in an open ditch—a humiliation he could not even complain of, since publicizing it would give people an irresistible story against him.


心里是又好气又好笑，脸上却是声色不动，
Inwardly both angry and amused, Hu kept his expression and voice unchanged.

反倒好言安慰。
Instead, he offered reassurance.

“老陈，小狗子玩的把戏，我都晓得，
“Old Chen, I know all about Xiaogouzi's trick.

你跟我说实话，我不难为你。
Tell me the truth and I won't make trouble for you.

回头在小狗子面前，我也不识破，免得害你为难。”
I won't expose you to him afterwards either, so you needn't be put in a difficult position.”

最后这句话，说到了这个老实人心里，
This last assurance touched the honest fellow where it mattered.

“胡大老爷，我跟你说了实话，”他很认真地问：“你真的不会告诉小狗子？”
“Mr Hu, if I tell you the truth,” he asked earnestly, “you really won't tell Xiaogouzi?”

“真的。
“Really.

你要不要我罚咒？”
Do you want me to swear an oath?”

说到这话，姓陈的放心了，当时将内幕实情，和盘托出，
Reassured, Chen revealed everything.

他是阿巧姐的堂房“大伯子”，
He was an elder cousin of A’qiao's husband.

欠了小狗子的钱，所以不得不受小狗子的挟制，让他来冒充阿巧姐的丈夫。
He owed Xiaogouzi money and had been forced to submit to him and impersonate her husband.

讲明了旧欠一笔勾销，另外送他一个大元宝。
The agreement was that his old debt would be cancelled and he would receive a large silver ingot besides.

有这样荒唐事！
What an absurd affair!

胡雪岩问道：“你不怕吃官司？”
“Aren't you afraid of being taken to court?” Hu asked.

“我也怕！”那姓陈的哭丧着脸说，
“I am,” Chen answered miserably.

“小狗子说不要紧，
“But Xiaogouzi says there's nothing to fear.

中人、代笔都是自己人，
The intermediary and the scribe are all our own people.

告到县衙门里，只说那张笔据是假的，根本没得这回事。”
If a complaint reaches the county yamen, we'll just say the document is false and nothing of the sort happened.”

“这家伙！”
The scoundrel!

胡雪岩心想，
Hu thought.
