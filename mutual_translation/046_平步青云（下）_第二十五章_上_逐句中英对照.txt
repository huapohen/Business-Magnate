第二十五章｜CHAPTER TWENTY-FIVE


等这些人走了，阿巧姐也可以露面了。
Once the men had gone, A’qiao could come out of hiding.

萌雪岩觉得已到了一切跟她说明白的时候，
Hu Xueyan felt that the time had come to explain everything.

于是凝神想了想，开口问道，“阿巧，我替你做个媒如何？”
After a moment's concentrated thought, he asked, “A’qiao, how would you like me to find you a husband?”

他是故意用此突兀的说法，为的一开头就可以把阿巧姐的心思扭了过来。
He had deliberately chosen this abrupt opening to turn her thoughts in a new direction at once.

这不是一下子可以办得到的，
That could not be achieved so easily.

被问的人，眨着一双灵活的眼睛，在不曾想好话回答以前，先要弄清楚他这句话是什么意思。
Her lively eyes blinked as she tried to understand his meaning before deciding how to answer.

“你这句话是什么意思？”
“What do you mean?”

她摇着头，一双翠玉耳环晃荡不停，
She shook her head, setting her jade earrings swinging.

“我真不懂。”
“I really don't understand.”

“你是不是当我说笑话？”
“Do you think I'm joking?”

“我不晓得。”
“I don't know.

阿巧姐答道，“反正我领教过你了，
I've had some experience of you now.

你的花佯百出，诸葛亮都猜不透。”胡雪岩笑了：
You have so many tricks that even Zhuge Liang couldn't see through them.” Hu laughed.

“你这句话是捧我，还是骂我？”
“Is that praise or abuse?”

“也不是捧，也不是骂，
“Neither.

我说的是实话。”
It's the truth.”

“我跟你说的也是实话。”
“I'm telling you the truth too.”

胡雪岩收敛笑容，一本正经地说，
His smile vanished and he spoke with complete seriousness.

“我替你做的这个媒，包你称心如意，
“I guarantee you'll be happy with the match I arrange.

将来你也想看我一点好处，能替我说话的时候要替我说话。”
In future, remember that I've done you some good, and speak for me when you have the chance.”

这几句话说得相当率直，也相当清楚，
This was frank enough, and clear enough.

阿巧姐很快地懂了，
A’qiao understood quickly.

特别是“包你称心如意”这六个字，撞在心坎上非常舒服。
The promise that she would be happy struck a particularly pleasing chord.

然而，到底是怎样一个人呢？
But who was the man?

不用她问，胡雪岩也要说：
Hu answered before she could ask.

“这个人，你见过，
“You've met him.

就是学台何大人。”
Education Commissioner He.”

听得是这一个人，阿巧姐不由得脸就发热，一颗心跳得很厉害。
Her face grew hot and her heart beat rapidly.

她还想掩饰，要做出无动于衷的神情，无奈那双眼睛瞒不过目光如炬的胡雪岩。
She tried to conceal it and appear unmoved, but her eyes could not deceive Hu's penetrating gaze.


“怎么样？”他故意问一句：
“Well?” he asked deliberately.

“何大人真正是白面书生，官场中出名的美男子。
“Commissioner He is a true fair-faced scholar, famous in official circles for his good looks.

马上进了京，就要外放，
He'll soon go to the capital and then receive a provincial appointment.

听说大太太身子不好，
I hear his principal wife is unwell.

万一有三长两短，说不定拿你扶了正，
If anything should happen to her, he might even make you his lawful wife.

不就是坐八抬大轿的掌印夫人？”
Then wouldn't you be a great official's lady, riding in a sedan chair with eight bearers?”

这说得多有趣！
What a delightful prospect!

阿巧姐心花怒放，嘴角上不由得就绽开了笑意。
A’qiao's heart blossomed with joy, and a smile appeared at the corners of her mouth.

只是这笑容一现即逝。
It vanished almost at once.

因为阿巧姐突然警觉，事太突兀，
Suddenly on her guard, she thought the proposal too unexpected.

多半是胡雪岩有意试探，
Most likely Hu was testing her.

如果信以为真，等拆穿了，便是一个绝大的话柄。
If she believed him and the trick was exposed, she would have given him endless grounds for ridicule.

别样事可以开玩笑，这件事绝不是一个玩笑，
Other matters could be joked about; this was no joke.

太天真老实，将来就会难做人！
Being too innocent and trusting could leave her in an impossible position.

这样一转念间，不由得有愠色，
Her expression darkened.

冷笑一声，管自己退到床帐后面的夹弄中去换衣服。
With a cold little laugh, she withdrew to the narrow space behind the bed curtains to change.

胡雪岩见她态度突变，自然诧异，不过细想一想，也就懂了。
Hu was naturally surprised by the sudden alteration, but soon understood.

这也难怪她，
He could hardly blame her.

“你不相信我的话，是不是？”他平静地问，
“You don't believe me, do you?” he asked calmly.

“你说，要怎么样，你才相信？”
“Tell me what would convince you.”

这正也就是阿巧姐在自问的话。
That was precisely what A’qiao was asking herself.

只是不知有何办法，能够证明此事真假，
She could think of no way to establish whether it was true.

在此刻的态度，要表现得对此根本漠不关心，才是站稳了脚步。
For now, her safest position was to show complete indifference.

因此，她故意用不耐烦的声音答道：“不晓得。
Deliberately sounding impatient, she replied, “I don't know.

你少来跟我罗嗦。”
Stop bothering me.”

这样水都泼不进去的话锋，
Her defence admitted not even a drop of water.

倒有点叫人伤脑筋。胡雪岩踱着方步在盘算，
Hu paced slowly, considering how to reach her.

回头有句话，可以让她相信自己不是跟她开玩笑。
Later, something he said could convince her that he was serious.

反正真是真，假是假，事情总会水落石出，
Truth was truth and falsehood falsehood; the facts would emerge in time.

该说的话，此时尽不妨先说，
He might as well say what needed saying now.

她自会记在心里，到她信其为真的那一刻，这些话就会发生作用了。
She would remember, and once she believed him, his words would take effect.


于是他“自说自话”地大谈何桂清的一切，以及他预备采取的步骤，
He therefore went on “talking to himself,” describing He Guiqing's circumstances and the steps he intended to take.

最后便必然又要问到：“现在要看你的意思怎么样？”
Inevitably, he ended by asking, “Now it depends on what you think.”

阿巧姐的衣服早已换好了，故意躲在床后不出现，
A’qiao had long since finished changing, but deliberately remained seated behind the bed.

坐在那里听他说得有头有尾，活龙活现，心思倒又活动了。
His orderly, vivid account began to stir her hopes again.

只是自己的态度，依然不肯表示，
Still, she would not disclose her attitude.

而万变不离其宗的还是“装佯”二字。
Whatever course she took, the essential thing was to keep up appearances.

“什么我的意思？”
“What do I think?”

她袅袅婷婷地走了出来，一面折衣服，一面答道，
She emerged gracefully and began folding her clothes.

“我不晓得。”
“I don't know.”

胡雪岩知道再逼也无用，
Hu knew that further pressure would achieve nothing.

只有反跌一笔，倒有些效用，
Reversing his approach might be more effective.

于是装出失望的神情说道：
He assumed a disappointed expression.

“你既然不肯，那也无法。
“If you're unwilling, nothing can be done.

什么事可以勉强，这件事必得两厢情愿才行。
Other things may be forced, but this requires consent on both sides.

幸亏我在那面还没有说破，
Fortunately, I haven't spoken plainly over there yet.

不然就搞得两面不是人了。”
Otherwise I'd have offended everyone.”

一听这话，阿巧姐怕煮熟了的鸭子，就此飞掉，岂不是弄巧成拙？
A’qiao feared that the cooked duck might fly away—that her cleverness would cost her the opportunity.

但如果老实说一句“愿意”，则装了半天的腔，又是前功尽弃。
Yet honestly admitting that she was willing would undo all her elaborate pretence.

左右为难之下，急出一计，
Caught between the two, she desperately found another tactic.

尽力搜索记忆，去想七岁当童养媳开始，受婆婆虐待，冬天生冻瘃，还得用冷水洗粗布衣服，夏天在柴房里，为蚊子叮得一夜到天亮不能睡觉的苦楚，
She searched her memory for the sufferings that had begun when she became a child bride at seven: her mother-in-law's cruelty, washing coarse cotton clothes in icy water despite winter chilblains, and summer nights in the woodshed when mosquitoes kept her awake until dawn.

渐渐地心头发酸，眼眶发热，
Gradually her heart ached and her eyes grew hot.

抽抽噎噎地哭出声来。
She began to sob.


漂亮女人的眼泪威力绝大，
A pretty woman's tears possessed immense power.

胡雪岩什么都有办法，就怕这样的眼泪，
Hu had a way of dealing with almost anything, but he feared tears like these.

当时惊问：“咦，咦，怎么回事？
Alarmed, he asked, “Why, what's happened?

有啥委屈好说，
Tell me if you've been wronged.

哭点啥？”
What are you crying for?”

“我的委屈哪里去说？”
“Where can I speak of my wrongs?”

阿巧姐趁机答话，带着无穷的幽怨，
A’qiao seized the opening, filling her voice with resentment.

“象我们这样的人，还不是有钱大爷的玩儿的东西，象只猫、象笼鸟一样，
“People like us are only playthings for rich men, like a cat or a caged bird.

高兴了花钱买了来，玩厌了送人！
When they fancy us, they spend money to buy us; when they're tired of us, they give us away.

叫她到东，不敢到西，
Told to go east, we daren't go west.

还有啥好说？”
What is there to say?”

“你这话说得没良心。”
“That's an ungrateful thing to say!”

胡雪岩气急了，
Hu was exasperated.

“我是为你好。”
“I'm doing this for your good.”

“哪个晓得是坏是好？
“Who knows whether it's good or bad?

你倒想想看，
Think about yourself.

你做事自说自话，从来不跟人商量，还说为我好！”
You decide everything without consulting anyone, and then say it's for my good!”

这是有所指的，指的就是周一鸣去办的那件事。
She meant something specific: the business Zhou had gone to settle.

胡雪岩自觉有些理亏，只好不作声。
Hu felt himself somewhat in the wrong and could only remain silent.

沉默带来冷静，冷静才能体味，
Silence brought calm, and calm made it possible to understand.

细想一想阿巧姐的话，似逆而实顺，也可以说是似怨而实喜，
Thinking over her words, he perceived compliance beneath apparent opposition, pleasure beneath apparent resentment.

她心里已是千肯万肯了，只是不能不以退为进地做作一番。
Inwardly, she was perfectly willing; she merely had to retreat before advancing.

这是人之常情，甚至不妨看作她还有“良心”，
That was human nature, and could even be taken as proof that she still had a conscience.

如果一定要逼她说一句：愿意做何家的姨太太，不但不可能，就可能又有什么意味？
To force her to say outright that she wanted to become He's concubine was impossible—and even if it were possible, what would be the point?

想透了这一层，便不觉她的眼泪有什么了不起。
Once he understood this, her tears no longer seemed formidable.

胡雪岩心里在想，此刻必得争取她的好感，让她对自己留下一个感恩图报的想法，将来她才会在何桂清那里，处处为自己的利益着想。
What he must do now was win her affection and leave her feeling grateful, so that in future she would always consider his interests with He Guiqing.

他想起听嵇鹤龄谈过的秦始皇身世的故事，
He remembered Ji Heling telling him the story of the First Emperor's parentage.

自己倒有些象吕不韦，
He himself was rather like Lü Buwei.

不知不觉地笑了出来。
The thought brought an involuntary smile.

“别人哭，你笑！”阿巧姐还在装腔作势，白着眼，嘟着嘴说：
“I cry, and you laugh!” A’qiao continued her performance, rolling her eyes and pouting.

“男人最没有良心，
“Men have no conscience.

真正叫人看透了。”
One can see right through them.”

“对！”胡雪岩顺着她的语气说，
“Quite right,” Hu answered in the same spirit.

“我也承认这句话。
“I admit it.

不过男人也很聪明，
But men are clever too.

不大会做赶尽杀绝的事，
They seldom drive things to the point of leaving no way out.

该讲良心的时候，还是讲良心的。”
When conscience is called for, they still have one.”


阿巧姐不答，
A’qiao did not answer.

拭一拭眼泪，自己倒了杯热茶喝，
Wiping her tears, she poured herself a cup of hot tea.

茶刚送到唇边，忽又觉得这样不是道理，
Just as she raised it to her lips, it struck her that this was not proper.

于是把那杯茶放在胡雪岩面前，自己又另倒一杯。
She placed it before Hu and poured another for herself.

“阿巧！”胡雪岩喝着茶，很悠闲地问：“你家里到底还有些什么人？”
Drinking at his ease, he asked, “A’qiao, who exactly is there in your family?”

“不跟你说过，
“Haven't I told you?

一个老娘，一个兄弟。”
An old mother and a younger brother.”

“兄弟几岁，干啥营生？”
“How old is he, and what does he do?”

“兄弟十人岁，
“Eighteen.

在布店里学生意。”
He's an apprentice in a cloth shop.”

“可曾讨亲？”
“Is he married?”

“还没有‘满师’，
“He hasn't even finished his apprenticeship.

哪里谈得到此？”阿巧姐说，
How could he think of that?

“再说，讨亲也不是桩容易的事。”
Besides, marrying isn't easy.”

“也没有什玄难。
“Not so difficult, either.

阿巧，”胡雪岩说：“我另外送你一千银子，
A’qiao, I'll give you another thousand taels.

你找个妥当的钱庄去存，动息不动本，贴补家用，
Deposit it in a sound bank, use the interest to help with household expenses, and leave the principal untouched.

将来等你兄弟满师，讨亲也好，弄爿小布店也好，都在这一千银子上。
When your brother finishes his apprenticeship, it can pay for a wife or a little cloth shop of his own.”

“阿巧姐看一看他，眨着眼不响。
She looked at him, blinking without a word.

胡雪岩以为她不相信自己的话，便很大方地，取出一千两银票，塞到了她的手里。
Thinking she doubted him, Hu generously took out a thousand-tael note and pressed it into her hand.


“你真的要帮我的忙？”
“Do you really want to help me?”

“这还有啥假的。”胡雪岩笑道，
“How could there be any doubt?” Hu laughed.

“你真当我没有良心？”
“Do you truly think I have no conscience?”

“我也是说说而已！
“I was only saying that.

人心都是肉做的，
Everyone has a human heart.

你待我好，我难道心里没有数？”阿巧姐又说，“你真的要帮我的忙，不要这样帮。”
If you're good to me, surely I know it.” She added, “But if you really want to help, don't help in this way.”

“那怎么帮法？”
“How, then?”

“我兄弟人很聪明，长得也不难看，
“My brother is clever, and good-looking too.

在我们镇上，是有名的漂亮小官人……”
He's famous in our town as a handsome young fellow...”

“你不用说了。”
“You needn't tell me.”

胡雪岩笑道，
Hu smiled.

“看姐姐，就晓得做兄弟的一定长得很秀气。”
“One look at the elder sister tells me the younger brother must have fine features.”

“不是娘娘腔的那种秀气，
“Not in an effeminate way.

长得又高又大，站出来蛮登样的。
He's tall and well built, and cuts quite a figure.

这也不去说他，
But never mind that.

我在想，你如果肯照应我兄弟，我叫他出来，跟了你去，
I was thinking that if you'd look after him, I could bring him out to work for you.

不比在我们那个小地方学生意来得强？
Wouldn't that be better than learning business in our little place?”

“说着，把银票退了回来。
She returned the note.

“原来如此！
“So that's it!

可以，可以。
Certainly.

我一定提拔你兄弟，只要他肯上进。
I'll help your brother advance, provided he wants to get on.

银子你还是收着，
Keep the money too.

算我送你老娘的‘棺材本’。”
Consider it my gift towards your mother's ‘coffin fund.’”

明知跟胡雪岩不用客气，但阿巧姐总觉得不便收受，于是这样说道：
Though she knew she need not stand on ceremony with Hu, A’qiao still felt uncomfortable accepting it.

“我替我娘磕个头谢谢你。
“I'll kowtow in thanks on my mother's behalf.

钱，暂时先存在你这里。”
I'll leave the money with you for the present.”

“不必！
“No.

你还是自己保管好了。”
Keep it yourself.”

阿巧姐不肯，他也不肯，
She would not take it, and he would not yield.

取过银票来，塞到她口袋里。她穿的是件缎子夹袄，
Taking the note, he slipped it into the inner pocket of her lined satin jacket.

探手入怀，温软无比，心头不免荡漾起绩思，
The soft warmth he felt as his hand reached inside stirred his desire.

倒有些失悔，
Regret returned.

这样一个人，遣之远离，实在不大舍得。
He was reluctant indeed to send such a woman far away.


因此，他一时无语，心里七上八下地，思绪极乱。
For a while Hu said nothing, his mind in confused agitation.

阿巧姐当然猜他不透，又提到他兄弟的事。
A’qiao could not guess what he was thinking and returned to her brother.

“我兄弟小名阿顺。
“His childhood name is A’shun.

你看，什么时候叫他出来？”
When should I bring him out?”

胡雪岩定定神说：“学生意是写好了‘关书’的，
Collecting himself, Hu said, “An apprenticeship is governed by a written contract.

也不能说走就走，
He can't simply leave whenever he likes.

我这里无所谓，随便什么时候来好了。”
As far as I'm concerned, any time will do.”

学生意未曾满师，中途停止，要赔饭食的银子，这一点阿巧姐也知道，
A’qiao knew that leaving before the term was complete meant repaying the cost of board.

不过有一千两银子在身上，有恃无恐，便即答道：
But with a thousand-tael note on her, she felt secure.

“这不要紧，
“That doesn't matter.

我自会安排妥当。”
I'll arrange it properly.”

“那好。
“Good.

你写信叫他出来好了。”
Write and tell him to come.”

阿巧姐心想，除了这件事以外，还有许多话要跟家里人说，
Besides this, she had much to discuss with her family.

那就不如再回去一趟，
Better to go home again herself.

这样转念，便即问道：“你哪天走？”
“When are you leaving?” she asked.

“工夫已经耽误了。
“I've already lost time.

等老周一回城，如果你的事情已经办妥当，我明天一早就走。”
Once Zhou gets back, if your business is settled, I'll leave first thing tomorrow.”

“那，”阿巧姐怏怏然说：“那来不及了。”
“Then it's too late,” she said disappointedly.

“怎么样？”
“For what?”

“如果你还有一两天耽搁，我想回去一趟。
“If you were staying another day or two, I'd have gone home once more.

现在，当然不必说它了。”
Now there's no point discussing it.”


经此片刻工夫，胡雪岩的浮思已定，
By this time Hu's wandering desires had settled.

话已经说了出去，决无翻悔的道理。
He had given his word and could not go back on it.

既然如此，原来打算让阿巧姐仍旧住在潘家的计划，不妨更改一下。
That being so, he might alter his original plan for A’qiao to remain with the Pans.

“我是这样在想，
“This is what I think.

在外面做事，决不可受人批评。
In conducting affairs, one must give people no grounds for criticism.

从此刻起，你算是何学台的人了，
From this moment, you belong to Commissioner He's household.

我们就不便再住在一起，不然不象话。
We ought not to stay together any longer; it wouldn't look right.

我原来的意思，想让你住在潘家，现在你自己看，
I originally meant you to stay at the Pans', but now you can decide.

你住到娘家去也可以。
You may go to your mother's instead.”

“这番话在阿巧姐颇有意外之感，
This surprised her.

细想一想，又觉得胡雪岩做事，真个与众不同，心思细密，手法漂亮。
After considering it, she felt that Hu really did act unlike other men: his thinking was careful and his methods elegant.

既然他如此说，自己将来在何桂清面前也占身分，
Since he put it that way, she would have greater standing before He Guiqing later.

就无需多说什么了。
There was no need to say more.

转念又想，作此表示，显得毫无留恋，象煞没有良心，
Then it occurred to her that accepting without comment would suggest she felt no attachment, as though she truly had no conscience.

所以还是得有一句话交代，这句话很难，
She must say something, but what?
