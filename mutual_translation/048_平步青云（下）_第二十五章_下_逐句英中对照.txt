胡雪岩
《平步青云（下）》第二十五章（下）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume III — Chapter Twenty-Five, Part Three
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


By the time they finished discussing business, the sumptuous meal was nearly over.
谈完正事，一席盛宴，亦近尾声，

Four substantial dishes were brought out to conclude the feast, but they could do no more than look at them.
端上来四样“压桌菜”，只好看看，

The eight little plates of pickles, however, were swept clean.
倒是小碟子装的八样酱菜，一扫而空，

Hu Xueyan drank a bowl of fragrant rice porridge, patted his stomach, and rose.
胡雪岩喝了一碗香梗米粥，抚抚肚子站起来说：

“I must take my leave.
“我要告辞了，

I shall probably set off tomorrow, so I will not come round to say goodbye again.
大概明天动身，不再来向各位辞行，

After the Dragon Boat Festival, I shall certainly try to find time to come and escort Brother Zhixiang myself.
等过了端午，我一定设法抽空，亲自来接芝香兄，

We can talk further then.”
那时候再叙吧！”

Pan Shuya wanted him to stay a little longer, while Wu Jizhong and Lu Zhixiang both invited him to dinner.
潘叔雅还要留他多坐，吴季重和陆芝香又要请他吃晚饭。

Hu felt there was no need to indulge these wealthy young gentlemen too much.
胡雪岩觉得对这班“大少爷”，不必过于迁就，

He found excuses to decline all their invitations, tipped the Pan household’s maids and servants generously, and rode out through Chang Gate in the same splendid four-bearer sedan chair.
所以一律托词拒绝，厚犒了潘家的婢仆，仍旧坐着那乘装饰华美的四人大轿出阊门。

It was only two in the afternoon.
这时不过午后两点钟，

Resting with his eyes closed, he considered his plans.
胡雪岩一面在轿中闭目养神，一面在心里打算，

There was just one thing left to do that afternoon: have the document drawn up restoring A’qiao’s freedom.
这一下午只剩下一件事，就是立阿巧姐恢复自己之身的那张笔据，

That would take no longer than a cup of tea.
一杯茶的工夫就可了事。

He could spend the rest of his time with Ji Heling.
余下来的工夫，都可用来陪嵇鹤龄，

When he went into the city, he might also visit Sun Chunyang, the southern-goods shop he had long heard of, which was said to date from the Ming dynasty.
等下进城，不妨到慕名已久，据说还是从明朝传下来的一家“孙春阳”南货店去看看。


It was a sound enough plan, but the four-bearer chair proved his undoing.
打算得倒是不错，不想那顶四人大轿害了他，

The district outside Chang Gate was a major junction of land and water routes, and Jinchang Inn had become a veritable residence for traveling officials.
阊门外是水陆要道，金间栈成了名符其实的“仕宦行台”，

Since the fall of Jiangning, more major government offices had been established in Suzhou, bringing a corresponding increase in officials awaiting appointments, seeking commissions, or traveling on official business.
而苏州因为江宁失守，大衙门增多，所以候补的、求差的、公干的官员，平空也添了许多，

For convenience, many preferred to stay at Jinchang Inn.
近水楼台，都喜欢住在金阊栈，

The sight of the imposing sedan chair naturally set them asking what eminent official it carried.
看见这顶四乘大轿，自然要打听轿中是哪位达官？

Hu’s easy manner and generous spending had won over every attendant at the inn.
胡雪岩性情随和，出手豪阔，金阊栈的伙计，无不巴结，

Eager to please him, they embellished his accomplishments without restraint.
于是加油添酱，为他大大吹嘘了一番，

He was an influential figure in Zhejiang official circles, they said, and equally well connected in Liangjiang.
说他是浙江官场上的红人，在两江也很吃得开，

Governor Xu came from the same locality as he did; Education Commissioner He was an intimate friend and had visited him twice in person.
许巡抚是小同乡，何学使是至交，亲自来看过他两次。

Governor-General Yi had sent a guardsman with a whole banquet of bird’s-nest dishes.
总督佑大人派了戈什哈送过一桌燕菜席，

The chair belonged to Young Master Pan, Suzhou’s richest young gentleman, who had held the rank of circuit intendant from the day he was born.
这顶四人大轿是苏州城里第一阔少，一生下来就做了道台的潘大少爷派来的。

They made Hu sound like the all-powerful Patriarch Tongtian, with three heads and six arms, able to command the winds and rain.
把胡雪岩形容成了一个三头六臂、呼风唤雨的“通天教主”。

As it happened, the Pan, Wu, and Lu families were all punctilious about social courtesies.
恰好潘、吴、陆三家又讲究应酬，

They sent dishes for his journey and gifts of local produce, carried by handsome, smartly dressed servants.
送路菜的送路菜，送土仪的送土仪，派来的又都是衣冠整齐的俊仆，

This seemed to confirm both the breadth of his connections and every word the attendants had said.
这一下越显得胡雪岩交游广阔，伙计所言不虚。

Visitors streamed in to make his acquaintance, cultivate a connection, or even beg his assistance.
于是纷纷登门拜访，套交情，拉关系，甚至还有来告帮的，

Hu was overwhelmed and scarcely knew which way to turn.
把个胡雪岩搞碍昏头搭脑，应接不暇。

Only when the lamps were lit did he get a little peace.
直到上灯时分，方始略得清静。


“Mr Hu,” Zhou Yiming warned, “you cannot go on staying here.”
“胡先生！”周一鸣提出警告：“你老在这里住不得了！”

“Indeed I cannot,” Hu replied with a rueful smile.
“是啊！”胡雪岩苦笑着说，

“Isn’t this a calamity I have done nothing to deserve?”
“这不是无妄之灾？”

“I would not quite put it that way.
“潘倒不是这样说。

Some people could not attract this sort of attention however hard they tried.
有人求还求不来这洋的场面，

It is simply that you do not care for such a display.
不过你老不喜欢这样子招摇。

I think you should move to an inn inside the city.”
我看，搬进城去住吧！”

“I am leaving tomorrow.
“明天就要走了。

Better to stay put than make another change.
一动不如一静，

I need only keep out of the way myself.”
只我自己避开就是了。”

Fortunately, the most important piece of business had already been settled.
好在最要紧的一件大事，已经办妥，

Taking the document concerning A’qiao with him, Hu arranged to meet Zhou again the next day, then went into the city and straight to Ji Heling’s lodgings.
于是胡雪岩带着阿巧姐的那张笔据，与周一鸣约了第二天再见，然后进城，一直去访嵇鹤龄。

His account of that day’s banquet at Pan Shuya’s astonished Ji, who was naturally delighted for him.
谈起这天潘叔雅的晚宴，嵇鹤龄大为惊奇，自然也替他高兴。

“Wealth and distinction really are forcing themselves upon you!
“真正是‘富贵逼人来’！

Xueyan, I never imagined you would meet with so many opportunities.”
雪岩，我真想不到你会有这么多际遇！”


Ji was a scholar, however, and never forgot the need for self-examination.
不过嵇鹤龄是读书人，总忘不了省察的工夫，

Hu was enjoying such smooth sailing, with unforeseen opportunities presenting themselves one after another, that Ji could not help worrying about him.
看胡雪岩一帆风顺，种种意想不到的机缘，纷至沓来，不免为他忧虑，

He went on at length about the need to preserve prosperity once it had been attained, urging Hu to proceed as warily as though approaching a deep abyss or treading on thin ice.
所以接下来便大谈持盈保泰的道理，劝他要有临深履簿的警惕，

He must be careful at every turn: one false step could not be afforded.
处处小心，一步走错不得。

The advice was somewhat pedantic, but Hu admired this elder brother above all others.
话是有点迂，但胡雪岩最佩服这位“大哥”，

He heard the affection and gravity behind the words, considered every one of them sound, and took them all to heart.
觉得语重心长，都是好话，一字一句，都记在心里。

At last they turned to their travel plans.
最后便谈到了彼此的行期。

“I keep putting back my departure, and there has been no letter from Shanghai.
“动身的日子一改再改，上海也没有信来，

I am terribly anxious,” Hu said.
我心里真是急得很！”胡雪岩问，

“How many more days will you be in Suzhou, Elder Brother?
“不知道大哥在苏州还有几天耽搁？

If it is only a day or two, I might as well wait and travel with you.”
如果只有一两天，我就索性等你一起走。”

“There is no need.
“不必。

My date is uncertain.
我的日子说不定。

You go first, and we shall meet in Hangzhou.”
你先走吧！我们在杭州碰头。”

“Very well.
“那也好！”胡雪岩说，

Tomorrow morning I shall look round Sun Chunyang and buy a few things.
“明天上午我要到孙春阳看一看，顺便买买东西。

I shall definitely sail in the afternoon, so I will not come to say goodbye tomorrow.”
铁定下午开船。明天我就不来辞行。”

“And I shall not see you off.
“我也不送你的行。

We can dispense with both formalities,” Ji replied.
彼此两免。”桩鹤龄说，

“Speaking of Sun Chunyang reminds me of a story I heard just before leaving Hangzhou.
“提起孙春阳，我倒想起在杭州临走以前，听人谈起的一个故事，

Let me tell you.
不妨讲给你听听。

It concerns Fang Yuhe.”
这个故事出在方裕和。”


Like Sun Chunyang, Fang Yuhe was a very large dealer in goods from both northern and southern China.
方裕和跟孙春阳一样，是一家极大的南北货行，

Its proprietor, Fang, came from Huizhou, a district famous for producing pawnshop appraisers, whose people were known as the “Huizhou camels” for their capacity for hard work and endurance.
方老板是有“徽骆驼”之称、专出典当朝奉的徽州人，刻苦耐劳，

He attended to everything himself, and his business flourished until it ranked first in its trade in Hangzhou.
事必躬亲，所以生意做得蒸蒸日上，提起这一行业，在杭州城内首屈一指。

Yet two years earlier goods had begun to disappear, and the missing stock consisted of the most expensive delicacies: shark’s fins, edible bird’s nests, dried scallops, and the like.
哪知道从两年以前，开始发生货色走漏的毛病，而且走漏的是最贵重的海货、鱼翅、燕窝、于贝之类，

Fang investigated both openly and in secret.
方老板明查暗访，

First he examined his own staff to discover who might be light-fingered.
先在店里查，伙计中有谁手脚不干净？

Then he made inquiries among other dealers and restaurants, looking for anyone who had bought stolen goods of uncertain origin.
再到同行以及馆子里去查，看哪家吃进了来路不明的黑货？

He found no trace.
然而竟无线索可寻。

Only recently had he discovered the method, quite by chance.
到了最近，终于查到了，是偶然的发现，

The trouble lay in the torches: bundles of bamboo bound together, about a cun across and three chi long, normally sold by the bundle.
发现有毛病的是“火把”——用于竹子编扎的火炬，寸许直径三尺长，照例论捆卖，

The valuable delicacies were hidden inside these and carried out of the shop.
贵重的海货，就是藏在火把里，走漏出去的。

Fang kept his head.
方老板头脑很清楚，

He could not simply accuse a customer buying torches of conspiring with his staff to steal goods.
不能找买火把的顾客，说他勾结店中的伙计走私，

The customer could deny it and counter by accusing him of falsely branding an honest man a thief, leaving Fang facing a lawsuit.
因为顾客可以下承认，反咬一口，“诬良为盗”，还得吃官司。

After considering the matter, he betrayed nothing and left the bundle containing the stolen goods where it was.
考虑结果，声色不动，那捆有挟带的火把，亦依旧摆在原处。


Before long someone came to buy torches.
不久，有入来买人把，

The clerk who attended to this “customer” was one of Fang’s most trusted employees, a member of his own clan, both capable and honest.
去接待“顾客”的，是他最信任的一名伙计，也是方老板的同宗，不但能干，而且诚实。

Fang was puzzled.
这一下方老板困惑了，

Surely so loyal and reliable a man could not be the thief?
这个人忠诚可靠，决不会是他走私。

Perhaps he had merely happened to serve the customer by chance.
也许误打误撞，一时巧合，

Fang decided to wait and see.
决定看一看再说。

A few days later he found stolen goods in the torches again.
过了几天，又发现火把中有私货，

This time a different man came to buy them, but the same clerk named Fang attended to him.
这次来买火把的是另一个人，但接待的却仍是那方姓伙计。

That could no longer be coincidence.
这就不会是巧合了，

Fang sent a young apprentice to follow the “customer” in secret, and the boy trailed him all the way to a grain-transport boat.
他派了个小徒弟，暗中跟踪那名“顾客”，一跟跟到漕船上。

The explanation was now plain.
这就很容易明白了，

No wonder nothing had been found locally: the stolen goods were being carried to other towns aboard the grain boats.
怪不得本地查不出，私货都由漕船带到外埠去了。

One day Fang called his kinsman aside and quietly asked, “Do you have any friends on the grain boats?”
于是有一天，方老板把他那同宗的伙计找来，悄悄地问道：“你在漕船上，有朋友没有？”

“No.” So he said, but a slight look of alarm crossed his face.
“没有。”说是这样说，神色之间，微微一惊，

Fang now had no doubt.
方老板心里明白，事无可疑了，

The question was what to do about it.
如今要想的是处置的办法。

At this point Ji asked, “Xueyan, if you had been the proprietor, how would you have handled the matter?”
谈到这里，嵇鹤龄问道：“雪岩，换了你做方老板，如何处置？”


“I do not know much about the northern- and southern-goods trade.
“南北货这一行，我不大熟悉。

But from the sound of it, there must have been accomplices in the shop.”
不过看这样子，店里总还有同伙勾结。”

“Yes, there were.”
“是的，有同伙勾结。”

Hu considered for a moment.
胡雪岩略想一想说：

“I may not know the rules of the trade, but the principles of dealing with people are the same.
“南北货行的规矩，我虽不懂，待人接物的道理是一样的。

I have my own way of handling it.
我有我的处置办法，

First tell me what Fang did.” Fang reasoned that a man who could carry on stealing for two years without being discovered must be exceptionally capable.
你先说，那方老板当时怎么样？”方老板认为他这个同宗走私，能够两年之久，不被发觉，是个相当有本事的人，

Besides, since others were involved, exposing the affair would damage the firm’s reputation.
同时这件事既有同伙勾结，闹出来则于信誉有损，

He would also have to dismiss a number of experienced employees, which would hurt the business.
而且势必要开除一班熟手，生意亦有影响，

He therefore decided to give the man greater responsibility, promote him, and raise his pay.
所以决定重用此人，升他的职位，加他的薪水。

Grateful for this kindness and eager to repay it, the clerk naturally put an end to the thefts.
这一来，那方伙计感恩图报，自然就不会再有什么偷漏的弊病发生。

When Ji finished, Hu nodded.
听嵇鹤龄讲完，胡雪岩点点头说：

“The proprietor had the right idea, but his way of carrying it out could have been better.”
“那个老板的想法不错，做法还差一点。”

Ji was astonished.
嵇鹤龄大为诧异，

He had thought Fang’s handling of the matter could scarcely be improved upon.
在他觉得方老板的处置，已经尽善尽美，

To hear Hu still find fault made him feel rather indignant on Fang’s behalf.
不想在胡雪岩看，还有可批评之处，倒有些替方老板不服气。

“Oh?
“噢！

Then I should like to hear what better method you have.”
我倒要看看，还有什么更好的办法？”


“You must never unmask a thief.
“做贼是不能拆穿的！

Once you do, it leaves a mark that cannot be rubbed away, and however you manage things, you cannot continue working together for long.
一拆穿，无论如何会落个痕迹，怎么样也相处不长的。

I will make a prediction now, and time will test it: that kinsman of Fang’s will leave within a year at most.”
我放句话在这里，留待后验，方老板的那个同宗，至多一年工夫，一定不会再做下去。”

“Hmm.” Ji began to see some sense in this.
“嗯，嗯！”嵇鹤龄觉得有些道理了，

“Then would you simply turn a blind eye?”
“那么，莫非不闻不问？”

“How could I?” Hu replied.
“这怎么可以？”胡雪岩说，

“My method would be to establish the facts in secret, then promote him and raise his pay without ever saying I knew.
“照我的做法，只要暗中查明白了，根本不说破，就升他的职位，加他的薪水，

I would put him in sole charge of detecting theft.
叫他专管查察偷漏。

Would he still steal the very goods he was appointed to guard?”
莫非他再监守自盗？”

“Exactly!” Ji exclaimed.
“对！”嵇鹤龄很兴奋他说，

“You really are cleverer than any other merchant.
“果然，你比哪个生意人都高明。

‘The antelope hangs by its horns and leaves no trace.’
‘羚羊挂角，无迹可寻’，

That is mastery so complete that the art disappears.”
这才是入于化境了。”

“Still, there is another side to it.
“不过话要说回来，

Unless the man really has ability, the consequences could be very bad: it would amount to rewarding theft.
除非那个人真正有本事，不然，这样；做法，流弊极大、变成奖励做贼。

So what I have said is only an exercise in strategy on paper.
所以我的话也不过是纸上谈兵。

Elder Brother, I often think of something you once told me: ‘The art of command lies in the commander’s mind.’
大哥，”他说，“我常常在想到你跟我说过的那句话：”用兵之妙，存乎一心‘！

Business and warfare are much alike.
做生意跟带兵打仗的道理是差不多的，

You must act according to the person and speak according to the situation.
只有看人行事，看事说话，

Beyond adapting to changes, you must find opportunities within them.
随机应变之外，还要从变化中找出机会来！

That is the highest skill of all.”
那才是一等一的本事。

“I think you have very nearly reached it,” Ji said, then added with deep regret, “If only you had read a few more books.”
““我看你也就差不多这个本事了。”嵇鹤龄又不胜惋惜地说，“你就是少读两句书。”

Hu could only shake his head.
说到此事，胡雪岩只有摇头，

Ji wanted to urge him to devote himself seriously to study, but, considering how busy he was, where could he find the time?
嵇鹤龄倒是想劝他折节读书，但想想他那样子忙法，何来读书的工夫？

There was nothing more to say.
也就只好不作声了。


The next morning Hu had scarcely risen when a minor official from Zhejiang, visiting Jiangsu on government business, sent in his card.
到了第二天，刚刚起身，又有个浙江到江苏来公差的佐杂官儿，投帖来拜。

Zhou Yiming had been right: he could no longer stay here.
胡雪岩一看这情形，果真应了周一鸣的话。此地不能再住了，

He asked the inn to notify his boatman that he would leave that afternoon, then hurried out to escape.
因此托客栈去通知他的船老大，当天下午启程，自己匆匆忙忙避了出去，

Before going, he left instructions that if Zhou came, he should be sent to meet him at Wuyuan Teahouse inside the city.
临走时留下话，如果周一鸣来了，叫他到城内吴苑茶馆相会，

Hu would wait until he arrived.
不见不散。

Once in his sedan chair, he found the situation almost laughable.
坐上轿子，自觉好笑，

Life could produce the most unexpected troubles.
世间的麻烦，有时是意想不到的，

He was the last man who wanted an official career, yet people insisted on treating him as though he possessed all an official’s importance.
自己最不愿做官，偏偏有人拿官派套上头来，

What was one to make of that?
这是哪里说起？

He was also irritated, of course.
自然，他也有些懊恼，

To be driven out of his own lodgings first thing in the morning was difficult to accept.
一清早在自己住处存不住身，想想真有些不甘心。

He entered the city in this disgruntled mood and lost interest in doing anything.
这样怏怏然进了城，便觉意兴阑珊，

He sat drinking tea at Wuyuan, listening to a man nearby hold forth on current affairs.
只在吴苑喝茶，听隔座茶客大谈时事。

The speaker’s thick Hunan accent was hard to follow.
那人是浓重的湖南口音，相当难懂，

He spoke very loudly, gesticulating and spraying saliva in a thoroughly ungraceful manner, and Hu found him exasperating.
而且声音甚大，说话的神态，亦颇不雅，指手画脚，口沫横飞，胡雪岩深为不耐。

Yet everyone around the man was listening with rapt attention.
但看他周围的那些听众，无不聚精会神，十分注意，

Curiosity got the better of Hu, and he made himself listen carefully too.
不由得有些好奇，也耐着心细听。


Gradually he understood.
慢侵听懂了，

The man was describing Zeng Guofan’s defeat at Jinggang, sixty li outside Changsha, the Hunan provincial capital.
是谈曾国藩在湖南省城长沙城外六十里的靖港，吃了败仗，

In his anguish Zeng had thrown himself into the water, but had been rescued.
愤而投水，为人所救的情形。

Hunan’s provincial treasurer, Xu Youwang, and judicial commissioner, Tao Enpei, had already resented him.
湖南的藩司徐有王、

Here was a vice-minister living at home in mourning for a parent: why must he meddle by organizing militia forces and encroaching on their authority?
