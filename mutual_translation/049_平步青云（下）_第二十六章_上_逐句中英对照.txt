第二十六章｜CHAPTER TWENTY-SIX


胡雪岩到了上海，仍旧在投大兴客栈，
On reaching Shanghai, Hu Xueyan again put up at the Daxing Inn.

行李还不曾安顿好，就写条子叫客栈专人送到七姑奶奶的寓所，请古应春来相会。
Before he had even settled his luggage, he wrote a note and had a man from the inn take it to the Seventh Lady’s lodgings, asking Gu Yingchun to call.

不到一个钟头，古应春亲自驾着他的那辆“亨斯美”赶到大兴客栈，
In less than an hour Gu arrived, driving his Hengsimei carriage himself.

一见面叫应了，什么话不说，先仔细打量胡雪岩的行李。
After their first greetings, he said nothing but looked Hu’s luggage carefully over.

“怎么回事，老古！”
“What is it, Gu?”

“阿巧姐呢？”
“Where is A’qiao?”

“没有来！”胡雪岩说，
“She hasn’t come,” Hu replied.

“事情大起变化，你想都想不到的。”
“Things have taken a turn you could never have imagined.”

“怎么样呢？”
“What has happened?”

“说来话长。
“It’s a long story.

回头有空再谈。
We’ll talk when we have time.

喂，”他问，“五哥回来了没有？”
Tell me, has Brother Five come back?”

“还没有。”
“Not yet.”

古应春又问：
Gu returned to his question.

“阿巧姐呢？
“What about A’qiao?

怎么事情起了变化？
What has changed?

你要言不烦说两句。”
Give me the gist of it.”

胡雪岩不知道他何以对阿巧姐特别关心，便反问一句：
Unable to understand this particular concern for her, Hu asked a question of his own.

“你是不是派人到木渎去谈过？”
“Did you send someone to Mudu to discuss her affairs?”

“你先不用管这个，
“Never mind that for the moment.

只说阿巧姐怎么样了？”
Just tell me what has happened to her.”

“名花有主，是我一手经理。
“That lovely flower has found her owner, and I arranged the whole thing.

不久，就是何学台的姨太太了。”
Before long she will be Education Commissioner He’s concubine.”

接着，便讲移植这株名花的经过，
He went on to explain how he had transplanted this celebrated flower.

胡雪岩虽长于口才，但经过太曲折，三言两语说不完，
Eloquent as he was, the affair had too many twists to be disposed of in a few words.

站着讲了一刻钟，才算说清楚。
He stood talking for a quarter of an hour before he had made it clear.

“这样也好！”
“That’s a good outcome too!”

古应春拉着他的袖子说，
Gu took him by the sleeve.

“走！
“Come along.

去晚了，七姐的急性子，我是晓得的，又要埋怨我。”
I know Seventh Sister’s impatience. If we’re late, she’ll be blaming me again.”

“慢来，慢来！”
“Not so fast!”

胡雪岩按住他的手说，
Hu pressed down his hand.

“我的话告诉你了，
“I’ve told you my story.

你一定也有话，
You must have something to tell me.

怎么不告诉我？”
Why keep it to yourself?”

“当然要告诉你的。
“Of course I’ll tell you.

到家再说。”
When we get home.”

等坐上马车，古应春承认曾派人到木渎去谈过阿巧姐的事，
Once they were in the carriage, Gu admitted that he had sent someone to Mudu about A’qiao.

但一场无结果，派去的人下会办事，竟连未能成功的原因何在，都弄不清楚。
Nothing had come of it, and his messenger had proved so incompetent that he could not even explain why he had failed.

“我倒比你清楚。
“I know more about it than you do.

阿巧姐吃了一场惊吓，由此让我还交了三个朋友，都是苏州的阔少，有一大笔款子要我替他们用出去。”胡雪岩笑道：
A’qiao had quite a fright, and through that affair I made three friends, all wealthy young men from Suzhou, with a large sum they want me to put to work for them.” Hu laughed.

“老古，我这一趟苏州，辛苦真没有白吃，
“Gu, the trouble I took on this trip to Suzhou was certainly not wasted.

谈起个中的曲折，三天三夜都谈不完。”
Three days and three nights would hardly be enough to tell you all the twists and turns.”

事情大多，东一句，西一句，扯来扯去，
There was so much to relate that he darted from one subject to another.

古应春一时也听不清楚，只知道他这趟大有收获。
Gu could not immediately make sense of it all, but he understood that the journey had yielded a great deal.

彼此在生意上休戚相关，胡雪岩有办法，他自然也感到兴奋。
Their business fortunes were bound together, and Hu’s resourcefulness naturally excited him too.


转眼间到了七姑奶奶寓所，
They soon reached the Seventh Lady’s lodgings.

马蹄声音是她听熟的，亲自下楼来开门，老远就在喊：“小爷叔，你回来了。”
Recognising the sound of the horse’s hooves, she came downstairs to open the door herself and called out from a distance, “Little Uncle, you’re back!”

“回来了，回来了！”胡雪岩说：
“Yes, I’m back!

“先告诉你一桩开心的事，
First, something to make you happy.

你总说苏州的糖食好吃，我替你带了一大篓来，
You’re always saying how good Suzhou sweets are, so I’ve brought you a whole big hamper.

放在‘石灰缸，里，包你半年都吃不完。”
Keep them in a lime jar, and I guarantee you won’t finish them in half a year.”

“谢谢，谢谢！”
“Thank you, thank you!”

七姑奶奶口中是对胡雪岩说话，眼睛却看着古应春。
Though she was speaking to Hu, her eyes were on Gu.

“阿巧姐不来了！”古应春轻声对她说，
“A’qiao isn’t coming,” Gu told her quietly.

“她也不会姓胡了。”
“And she won’t be taking the name Hu.”

“怎么闹翻了？”
“Have they quarrelled?”

“不是，不是。
“No, no.

你不要乱猜，
Don’t start guessing.

回头再跟你说。
I’ll tell you later.

总而言之，可以放心了！”
At any rate, we can stop worrying.”

“嗯，嗯！”
“Good, good!”

七姑奶奶很高兴地拍拍胸。
She patted her chest with evident relief.

胡雪岩听他们这番对答，越觉困惑，
This exchange left Hu more puzzled than ever.

“老古，”他用低沉的声音问：“到底是怎么回事？
“Gu,” he asked in a low voice, “what is all this about?

什么事可以放心？”
What can we stop worrying about?”

“现在不会‘白板对煞，了，”七姑奶奶搭腔，
“There won’t be a head-on collision now,” the Seventh Lady put in.

“大家都可以放心。
“We can all relax.

小爷叔，快上楼来，看看哪个来了？”
Come upstairs, Little Uncle, and see who’s here.”

上楼掀帘一看，
He went up and lifted the curtain.

合笑凝睇的竟是芙蓉，
It was Furong who gazed at him with a smile.

胡雪岩惊喜之余，恍然大悟所谓“白板对煞”作何解。
Amid his delighted surprise, the meaning of that “head-on collision” suddenly became clear.

“你是怎么来的？”
“How did you get here?”

“我跟三叔一起来的。”芙蓉说，
“I came with Third Uncle,” Furong replied.

“一到就住在七姐这里。
“I’ve been staying with Seventh Sister ever since we arrived.

本来要写信告诉你，七姐说不必，你就要回来的。”
I meant to write and tell you, but she said there was no need, as you would soon be back.”


“那么三叔呢？”
“And where is Third Uncle?”

“他就住在不远一家客栈。”古应春笑道：
“At an inn quite close by.” Gu laughed.

“这位先生真是妙人！
“He really is a character.

从他一来，你晓得哪个最开心？”
Do you know who has been happiest since he arrived?”

“哪个最开心？”
“Who?”

胡雪岩想了想说：
Hu considered.

“照我看，只有他自己。”大家都笑了，
“As far as I can see, only the man himself.” Everyone laughed.

“还有一个，”
“There’s someone else.”

古应春指着七姑奶奶：
Gu pointed to the Seventh Lady.

“她！”
“Her!”

这一说，胡雪岩又大惑不解了，
Once again Hu was mystified.

“何以七姐最开心？”
“Why should Seventh Sister be the happiest?”

“你想呢？
“What do you think?

我们这位姑奶奶一刻都静不下来的，
Our lady here cannot sit still for a moment.

现在听了你小爷叔的话，要学做千金小姐，大门不出，二门不迈，叫她怎么坐得住？
Now that she has taken your advice and means to behave like a sheltered young lady, never stepping beyond the outer gate or the inner door, how can she bear to stay put?

刘三爷一来算救了她了，
Third Master Liu has been her salvation.

他每天到各处去逛，看了希奇古怪的花样，回来讲给她听，
Every day he goes about seeing all sorts of strange and wonderful things, then comes back and tells her about them.

真好比听大书。”
It is as good as listening to a storyteller.”

“听大书都没有听刘三叔说笑话来得发噱。”七姑奶奶也爽郎地笑着，
“No storyteller could make me laugh as much as Third Uncle Liu does,” she said, laughing freely herself.

“这个人真有趣。”
“He is wonderfully entertaining.”

“来了，来了！”古应春说，
“Here he comes,” Gu said.

“他的脚步声特别。”
“There’s no mistaking his footsteps.”


因为有此一句话，胡雪岩便先注意门帘下的脚，
At that remark Hu looked first at the feet below the door curtain.

原来刘不才着的是一双只有洋人用的黑色革履，上了油，擦得闪闪发亮。
Liu Bucai was wearing the sort of black leather shoes otherwise worn only by foreigners, dressed and polished to a brilliant shine.

身上只穿长袍，未着马褂，
He wore a long robe without a riding jacket.

那件袍子纯黑，非绸非缎，
The robe was jet black, neither silk nor satin.

细细看去，才知是洋人用来做礼服的呢子，
Only on closer inspection did Hu realise that it was made from the woollen cloth foreigners used for formal dress.

刘不才别出心裁，做成长袍，配上水钻的套扣，
With characteristic ingenuity Liu had had it made into a Chinese robe, complete with rhinestone clasps.

显碍相当别致，也相当轻佻。
The effect was strikingly unusual and decidedly frivolous.

“喔！”
“Ah!”

刘不才先开口，
Liu spoke first.

“你总算回来了！
“You’re back at last!

人象胖上点。”胡雪岩先答他的话，忍着笑将他从头看到底，
You seem to have put on a little weight.” Hu answered him, then surveyed him from head to foot, suppressing a laugh.

“刘三爷，”他又似嘲弄，又似佩服他说：“你真正时髦透顶了！”
“Third Master Liu,” he said, half in mockery and half in admiration, “you are the very height of fashion!”

“刘三爷真开通。”古应春也说：
“Third Master Liu is wonderfully open-minded,” Gu added.

“叫我就不敢穿了这一身奇装异服，招摇过市。”
“I would never dare parade through the streets in an extraordinary outfit like that.”

“这有啥要紧？
“What does it matter?

人穿衣服，不是衣服穿人。”七姑奶奶帮刘不才说话，
People wear clothes; clothes don’t wear people,” the Seventh Lady said in Liu’s defence.

“ ‘女要俏，一身孝，男要俏，一身皂’，
“‘For a woman to look fair, let her wear mourning white; for a man to look fine, let him dress in black.’

刘三爷这身打抢真叫俏！
Third Master Liu looks handsome indeed!

看上去年纪轻了十几岁。”
It takes more than ten years off him.”


这一说大家都笑了，
Everyone laughed at this.

“闲话少说，”古应春问道：
“Enough talk,” Gu said.

“我们是下馆子，还是在家吃饭？”
“Shall we eat out or at home?”

“在家吃吧！”胡雪岩说，
“At home,” Hu replied.

“我不想动了。”
“I don’t feel like going out again.”

于是七姑奶奶和芙蓉都下厨房去指挥娘姨料理晚餐，胡雪岩开始畅谈此行的经过，
The Seventh Lady and Furong went down to the kitchen to direct the maidservant’s preparations for dinner, while Hu began an expansive account of his journey.

因为有刘不才在座，关于阿巧姐的曲折，自然是有所隐讳的。
With Liu present, he naturally concealed some of the complications concerning A’qiao.

“照此看来，刘不才来得正好，”等听完了，古应春异常兴奋他说，
“In that case, Liu Bucai has come at just the right moment,” Gu said excitedly when he had finished.

“五月初七去接陆芝香，就请刘三爷去。”
“Let Third Master Liu go to meet Lu Zhixiang on the seventh day of the fifth month.”

“是的。”
“Yes.”

胡雪岩点点头，
Hu nodded.

“我也这么想，
“That was my thought too.

将来陪他们吃喝玩乐，都是刘三爷的事。
In future, entertaining them—food, drink and amusements—will all be Third Master Liu’s responsibility.

何学使经过上海，也归刘三爷接待。”
He can also receive Education Commissioner He when he passes through Shanghai.”

“好的！”刘不才欣然答应，
“Gladly!” Liu agreed.

“都交给我。
“Leave them all to me.

包管伺候得他们服服帖帖。”
I guarantee they’ll be perfectly satisfied with my attentions.”

“你这身衣服，”古应春说，“陆芝香或许不在乎，在何学使一定看不顺眼。”
“As for those clothes of yours,” Gu said, “Lu Zhixiang may not mind, but Commissioner He is certain to disapprove.”

“我懂，我懂！”刘不才说，
“I know, I know!

“陪啥人穿啥衣裳，我自己有数。”
I know how to dress for the company I’m keeping.”


“我在想，”胡雪岩说，“将来刘三爷跟官场中人打交道，甚至到家里去的机会都有，
“I was thinking,” Hu said, “that Third Master Liu will be dealing with officials in future, and may even be invited into their homes.

有个功名在身上，比较方便得多。
An official qualification would make things much easier.

我看，捐个官吧？”
Why not purchase a rank?”

“最好不捐。
“Better not.

一品老百姓最大。”
A first-rank commoner outranks them all.”

胡雪岩很机警，听出刘不才的意思，不捐官则已，要捐就要捐得象样，
Hu was quick to detect his meaning: if Liu were to buy a rank at all, he wanted a respectable one.

不过自己也不过“州县班子”，不能替刘不才捐个“知府”，
Yet Hu himself belonged only to the class of subprefectural and county magistrates, and could hardly buy Liu the rank of prefect.

所以这样说道：“我们是做生意，不是做官，
He therefore said, “We are in business, not in government service.

大小不在乎，只为了做生意方便。
High rank or low doesn’t matter; it is simply to make business easier.

譬如说逢关过卡，要讨个情，
Suppose you need a favour at a customs post or checkpoint.

一张有官衔的名帖投进去，平坐乎起，道弟称兄，
Send in a visiting card with an official title, and you can meet on equal terms and call one another brother.

比一品老百姓，就好说话很多了。”
That makes it far easier to speak than if you were a first-rank commoner.”

“小爷叔的话不错，
“Little Uncle is right.

我也想捐一个，捐他个正八品的县丞，”
I’ve been thinking of buying a rank too—a county assistant magistrate, full eighth rank.”

“那也不必，
“There’s no need for that.

都是州县班子好了，弄个‘大老爷’做做。”
Let us all join the magistrates’ class and enjoy being addressed as ‘Your Honour.’”

接着胡雪岩的话，那边笑了；
Laughter came from across the room as Hu finished.

七姑奶奶手里捧着一瓶洋酒，高声说道：“各位‘大老爷，请上桌吧！”
Holding a bottle of foreign wine, the Seventh Lady called out, “Your Honours, dinner is served!”


“啊呀！”古应春突然说道，
“Oh dear!” Gu exclaimed suddenly.

“我倒忘记了，有位仁兄应该请了他来。”
“I’ve forgotten someone we ought to have invited.”

“谁啊？”胡雪岩问。
“Who?” Hu asked.

“裘丰言。”
“Qiu Fengyan.”

“喔，他也来了。
“Oh, he’s here too.

这可真有得热闹了。”
We really shall have a lively time.”

胡雪岩笑着说了这一句，却又摇摇头：
Hu smiled, then shook his head.

“不过今天不必找他。
“But there’s no need to fetch him tonight.

我们还有许多事要谈。”
We still have a great deal to discuss.”

生意上的许多机密，只有他们俩可以知道，连刘不才都不宜与闻，
Much of their business was confidential to the two of them; even Liu Bucai ought not to hear it.

因此饭桌上言不及义，只听刘不才在大谈这天下午所看的西洋马戏，马背上的金发碧眼的洋美女，如何婀娜多姿，大露色相。
At dinner, therefore, they avoided serious matters and listened to Liu describe the Western circus he had seen that afternoon: a blonde, blue-eyed beauty performing on horseback, displaying her charms and her figure with astonishing freedom.

别人倒都还好，英蓉初涉洋场，听了目瞪口呆，
The others took it in their stride, but Furong, on her first visit to this foreign world, listened in open-mouthed amazement.

只是不断他说：“哪有这样子不在乎、不顾脸面的？
“How could anyone be so shameless, so careless of appearances?” she kept saying.

我不信！”
“I don’t believe it!”


“百闻不如一见。”胡雪岩说，
“Seeing once is better than hearing a hundred times,” Hu told her.

“你明天自己去看一次就晓得”对的！
“Go and see for yourself tomorrow, and you’ll know it’s true.”

“七姑奶奶的兴致也来了，
The Seventh Lady was interested too.

“明天我们也去看一场，”
“Let’s go to a performance tomorrow.”

“女人也许看吗？”
“Are women allowed to watch?”

“女人难道不是人？
“Aren’t women people?

为啥不许！”
Why shouldn’t they be?”

“有没有女人去看？”英蓉问她三叔。
“Do women actually go?” Furong asked her uncle.

“有，有。
“Yes, yes.

不但有，而且还跟不认识的男人坐在一起……”“三叔又要瞎说了。”芙蓉老实不客气的指责，
Not only do they go, they sit beside men they don’t even know—” “There goes Third Uncle talking nonsense again,” Furong said bluntly.

“这话我绝对不信。”
“I simply don’t believe that.”

“我话没有说完，你就怪我！”刘不才说，
“You blame me before I’ve finished!

“我说的是西洋女人。”
I mean Western women.”

古应春衔杯在口，忍俊不住一口酒喷了出来，
Gu, with his cup at his lips, could not contain himself and spat out a mouthful of wine.

亏得脸转得快，才没有喷到饭桌上，
Only a quick turn of his head saved the dinner table.

但已呛了嗓子，又咳又笑好半天才能静下来。
As it was, he choked, coughed and laughed for quite some time before recovering.

“小爷叔！”七姑奶奶也笑着对胡雪岩说：“我们这位刘三爷跟‘酒糊涂’裘大老爷，真正是‘宝一对’，
“Little Uncle,” the Seventh Lady said to Hu, laughing too, “our Third Master Liu and His Honour Qiu, that Wine Muddlehead, are a perfect pair of treasures.

两个人唱双簧似他说起死后来，简直把人肚肠都要笑断。
When they put on their double act and start joking, they nearly make you split your sides.

我情愿每天备了好酒好菜请他们吃，听他们说说笑话，
I would gladly provide them with good wine and good food every day just to hear their jokes.

消痰化气、延年益寿。”
It would clear the phlegm, settle the spirits and lengthen one’s life!”

“你倒真阔！”古应春笑道，
“You do live grandly,” Gu laughed.

“请两位州县班子的大老爷做清客。”
“Two gentlemen of magistrate rank as your entertaining companions!”


“我倒想起来了。”七姑奶奶问道：
“That reminds me,” she said.

“刚才你们在谈，是不是刘三爷也要捐个官做？”
“Were you saying just now that Third Master Liu means to purchase an official rank?”

“老古也是！”胡雪岩接口，
“Gu too,” Hu put in.

“老古槽通洋务，现在刚正吃香的时候，
“Gu understands foreign affairs, and that is just the knowledge people need nowadays.

说不定将来有人会借重，真的挂牌出来，委个实缺。
Someone may come to value his services and actually give him a substantive appointment, with an official signboard of his own.

七姐，那时候你就是掌印夫人了。”
Seventh Sister, then you will be the wife of an official who holds a seal.”

“谢谢！”
“Thank you very much!”

七姑奶奶撇着嘴说，
She curled her lip.

“我才不要做啥官太太。”
“I have no wish to be an official’s wife.”

“老古！”
“Gu!”

胡雪岩先是当笑话说，转一转念头，觉得倒不是笑话，
Hu had begun in jest, but on reflection the idea seemed worth taking seriously.

“说真的！考古，我看你做官，倒是蛮好一条路子。
“Really, Gu, an official career might be a very good path for you.

于你自己有益，对我们大家也有好处。
It would benefit you and the rest of us as well.”

“七姑奶奶口快，紧接着问：“对老古自己有没有益处，且不去说它，
Quick as ever, the Seventh Lady asked, “Never mind for the moment whether it would benefit him.

怎么说对大家都有好处？”
How would it benefit everyone?”

“自然罗！”胡雪岩答道，
“Naturally it would,” Hu replied.

“你只看王雪公，
“Look at Mr Xuexuan.

他做了官，不是我们都有好处？”

“In that leather bag of yours.”

Plainly A’qiao had deliberately left this token of their private attachment while packing his things.

Hu saw no need either to conceal the matter or to defend himself.

神色从容地点点头说：

“I see.

“Yes.
