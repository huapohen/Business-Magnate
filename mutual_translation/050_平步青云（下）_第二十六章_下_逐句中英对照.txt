“It would be a pity if delay allowed someone else to get there first.”

“That reminds me.”

“ ‘酒糊涂，住在哪里？

“It's all arranged.

还是到我们那里去。”
Come straight to our place.”

“怎么？”
“What?

芙蓉惊喜地问道，“你也要到湖州去？”
Are you going to Huzhou too?” Furong asked in delighted surprise.

“是啊！”七姑奶奶洋洋得意他说，
“Yes, of course.

“我哥哥在做知府，
My brother is the prefect.

我为啥不去。”
Why shouldn't I?”

不谈阿巧姐是为了怕替胡雪岩惹麻烦，
She had withheld A’qiao's story for fear of causing Hu trouble.

事情不成，徒落话柄，所以她不愿告诉芙蓉。
If it came to nothing, she would only expose herself to ridicule.

Now he had actually agreed and seemed delighted at the prospect.

当然喜出望外，加以芙蓉一见投缘，不算外人，所以有那得意忘形的神态。
She was overjoyed; and since she and Furong had taken to one another at once, she felt no need to restrain her delight.

听她自己约略说明缘由，芙蓉也替她高兴，
After hearing a brief explanation, Furong was happy for her too.

“恭喜，恭喜！”她笑着说，
“Congratulations!

“从今以后，不叫你七姑奶奶，要叫你王大小姐了。”
From now on, we must stop calling you the Seventh Lady and call you Miss Wang instead.”

“好了，好了！
“Enough!

自己人，不作兴笑我的。
We're friends; you mustn't make fun of me.

我是沾了小爷叔的光。
It's all thanks to Little Uncle.

来！”
Come!”

七姑奶奶一把拉着她走，
She caught Furong by the hand.

“到厨房里帮帮我的忙。”
“Help me in the kitchen.”

七姑奶奶闲着无事，也就在烹调上消磨辰光，所以家里没有客来，饭菜也很丰腆，

厨房里早已预备得差不多了，
Most of the preparations were already made.

The four of them continued discussing Huzhou as they ate.

Their earlier conversation had gone only halfway: Hu had decided to escort the Seventh Lady himself, and now they must settle when to leave.

这是个难题，
This was difficult.

胡雪岩的事情太多，不容易抽出工夫来，

“After the seventh of the fifth lunar month is impossible—the people from Suzhou are coming.

又不相宜。”
That won't do either.

“七姑奶奶不愿强人所难，这样很爽快地表示了态度。

“那不行。
“No.

Let me think.”

谈到一半又被打断了，

刘不才和裘丰言连翩而至，两个人脸上红着，是喝了酒来的，但也不妨再来几杯。

“事情都弄好了。”裘丰言说，

“As soon as Brother You comes, I'll leave.”
