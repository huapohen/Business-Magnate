第二十七章｜CHAPTER TWENTY-SEVEN


到了松江，船泊秀野桥下，都上了岸，先到尤家休息。
On reaching Songjiang, they moored below Xiuye Bridge and went ashore, stopping first at the You household to rest.

尤五奶奶大出意外，少不得有一番寒暄张罗。
Mrs You was greatly surprised and busied herself with greetings and arrangements.

尤家常年备着好些客户，
The family always kept plenty of guest rooms ready.

除了芙蓉是七姑奶奶早就约好，跟她一起往以外，尤五奶奶又坚邀胡、裘二人在她家下榻。
Furong had already agreed to stay with the Seventh Lady; Mrs You now insisted that Hu and Qiu stay in the house as well.

略略安顿，随即去见老太爷。
Once they had settled in, they went to see the Old Master.

因为裘丰言是生客，又是一位官儿，老太爷十分客气，
Since Qiu Fengyan was a stranger and an official, the old man observed every courtesy.

叫人取来长袍马褂，衣冠整齐，肃然陪坐。
He had his long robe and riding jacket brought out, dressed formally, and sat with them in solemn state.

这一下不但裘丰言大为不安，连胡雪岩亦颇为局促，
Qiu was deeply uncomfortable, and even Hu felt constrained.

幸好，七姑奶奶接踵而至，
Fortunately, the Seventh Lady soon followed.

有她在座，能说会道，亲切随和，才把僵硬的气氛改变过来。
Her easy, affectionate conversation dispelled the stiffness.

说过一阵闲话，七姑奶奶谈到正事，
After some small talk she came to the point.

“老太爷，”她说，“今天我有桩大事来禀告你老人家。
“Old Master, I have something very important to tell you today.

不过，有点说不出口。”
Only I find it rather hard to say.”

老太爷已经看出来，裘丰言跟她也相熟，这样，自己说话，就无需有所避忌：
He had already seen that Qiu knew her well, so there was no need to restrain his own remarks.

“真正新鲜话把戏！”他似笑非笑地说，
“Now that is something new!” he said, half smiling.

“你还有啥说不出口的话！”
“Is there anything you find hard to say?”

“老太爷也是，就看得我那样子的老脸厚皮。”
“You make me sound quite shameless.”

七姑奶奶笑着站了起来，
She rose with a laugh.

“我先进去跟老姑太太谈谈，
“I shall go in and talk to Old Aunt first.

请小爷叔代我说吧！”
Little Uncle can tell you for me.”

老姑太太是老太爷的妹妹，也七十多了，耳聋口拙，
Old Aunt was the Old Master’s sister, also over seventy, hard of hearing and slow of speech.

没有什么可谈的，
There was little to discuss with her.

七姑奶奶无非是托词避开，好让胡雪岩谈她的亲事。
The Seventh Lady merely wanted to withdraw so that Hu could speak of her marriage.

七姑奶奶的没有一个归宿，原是者太爷的一桩心事，
Her unsettled future had long weighed on the Old Master.

所以听得胡雪岩细谈了经过，十分高兴。尤其是听说王有龄以知府的身分，降尊纡贵，认出身江湖的七姑奶奶作义妹，
Hu’s detailed account delighted him, especially the news that Wang Youling, a prefect, had condescended to take a woman of her background in the brotherhood as his sworn sister.

更觉得是件有光彩的事。
He considered that a great honour.

这一切都由胡雪岩而来，饮水思源，说了许多感谢的话，
It was all Hu’s doing, and, remembering the source of these blessings, he thanked him repeatedly.

同时因为裘丰言作胡雪岩的代表，在尤家与王家之间，要由他来从中联合安排，所以老太爷又向裘丰言拜托道谢。
Since Qiu was to represent Hu and coordinate arrangements between the You and Wang families, he also entrusted the matter to Qiu and thanked him.

言出至诚，着实令人感动。
His heartfelt sincerity was deeply moving.


“老太爷，”胡雪岩最后谈到他自己的请求，“有件事，
“Old Master,” Hu said, finally turning to his own request, “there is something else.

尤五哥不在这里，要劳动你老人家替我调兵遣将了！”
Since Brother Five is away, I must trouble you to muster a few men for me.”

“噢！”老太爷一叠连声地说：
“Certainly!” the old man said eagerly.

“你吩咐，你吩咐！”等胡雪岩说明，要派两个人护送，
“Tell me what you need!” Hu explained that he wanted two men to accompany a shipment.

料想是件轻而易举的事，却不道老太爷竟沉吟不语。
He had expected this to be easily arranged, but the Old Master fell into thoughtful silence.

这就奇怪了，他忍不住要问：“老太爷，莫非有什么难处？”
Puzzled, Hu could not help asking, “Is there some difficulty?”

“是的。”
“Yes.

老太爷答道，“你老弟是自己人，裘爷也是一见如故的好友，
You are one of us, my young friend, and Mr Qiu already feels like an old acquaintance.

这件事说不巧真不巧，说巧真巧。
This is a most unfortunate coincidence—and a fortunate one too.

不巧的不去说它了，
Never mind the unfortunate part.

只说巧的是，亏得你跟我说，
The fortunate part is that you have told me.

不然，真要做出对不起朋友的事来了。”
Otherwise, I should have done a friend a very serious wrong.”

听得这话，以胡雪岩的精明老到，裘丰言的饱经世故，都察出话中大有蹊跷，
Hu’s shrewdness and Qiu’s long experience both told them that something lay behind these words.

两人面面相觑，交换了一个眼色，
They exchanged a glance.

自然还是胡雪岩开口。
Naturally it was Hu who spoke.

“老太爷既当我们是自己人，那么，是怎么的‘不巧’？何妨也说一说！”
“Since you regard us as your own people, why not tell us the unfortunate part as well?”

“不必说了！
“There is no need.

不巧的是老五不在这里，
The misfortune is that Old Five is away.

在这里就不会有这件事。”老太爷平静地问道：“裘老爷预备什么时候走？”
Had he been here, this would never have happened.” The Old Master calmly asked, “When do you intend to leave, Mr Qiu?”


“我的货色还在上海，
“My cargo is still in Shanghai.

雇船装货，总得有三、五天的工夫。
Hiring boats and loading them will take three to five days.

我听老太爷的吩咐！”
I shall follow your instructions.”

“吩咐不敢当。”老太爷说，
“I would not presume to give instructions.

“你明天就请回上海去预备。
Please go back to Shanghai tomorrow and make your preparations.

今天四月十四，
Today is the fourteenth day of the fourth month.

准备四月二十开船，
Be ready to sail on the twentieth.

我们四月十九，在上海会齐。”
We shall meet in Shanghai on the nineteenth.”

“怎么？”
“What?”

胡雪岩不解“我们”两字，
Hu did not understand his use of “we.”

“莫非……”
“Surely you do not mean…”

“是的。”
“Yes.

老太爷说，“我送了裘老爷去！”
I shall escort Mr Qiu myself.”

“那怎么敢当？”
“How could we allow that?”

裘丰言跟胡雪岩异口同声地说。
Hu and Qiu exclaimed together.

“不！”
“No!”

老太爷做了个很有力的手势，
The old man made an emphatic gesture.

“非我亲自送不可。”
“I must go in person.”

说着，嘴唇动了两下，
His lips moved as though he had more to say.

看看裘丰言，到底不曾说出口来。
He looked at Qiu, but finally left the words unspoken.

“对不起，老裘！”胡雪岩看事态严重，也就顾不得了，径自直言：
Recognising the seriousness of the situation, Hu abandoned ceremony. “Forgive me, Qiu.

“你请外面坐一坐，
Would you sit outside for a moment?

我跟老太爷说句话。”
I must have a word with the Old Master.”

“是，是！”
“Of course.”

裘丰言也会意了，赶紧起身回避。
Qiu understood and hastily rose to withdraw.

“不必！
“There is no need.

裘老爷请这里坐！”
Please stay here, Mr Qiu.”

老太爷起身又道歉：
The Old Master rose and apologised.

“实在对不起！
“I am very sorry.

我跟我们胡老弟说句‘门槛里’的话。
I must speak to our friend Hu about something within the brotherhood.

不是拿你当外人，因为有些话，说实在的，裘老爷还是不晓得的好。”
I do not mean to treat you as an outsider, but, to be frank, there are things you would be better off not knowing.”

交代了这番话，老太爷陪着胡雪岩到佛堂里去坐，这是他家最庄严、也最清静的一处地方，
With this explanation he took Hu to the family’s Buddhist shrine room, the most solemn and secluded place in the house.

胡雪岩很懂这些过节，
Hu understood such observances.

一进去立刻摆出极严肃的脸色，双手合十，先垂头低眼，默默地礼了佛，
He immediately assumed a grave expression, joined his palms, lowered his head and eyes, and silently paid reverence to the Buddha.

才悄悄在经桌的下方落座。
Only then did he quietly take a seat below the scripture table.


老太爷在他侧面坐了下来，慢慢吞吞地说道：
The Old Master sat beside him and spoke slowly.

“老弟台，我不晓得这件享有你‘轧脚’在内，
“My young friend, I did not know you had a hand in this affair.

早晓得了，事情就比较好做。
Had I known earlier, things would have been easier.

现在，好比生了疮，快要破头了，只好把脓硬挤出来！”
Now it is like a sore about to burst: there is nothing for it but to squeeze out the pus.”

胡雪岩很用心地听着，始终猜不透，裘丰言押运的这一批军火，跟他有何关系？
Hu listened closely, still unable to guess what connection the old man could have with the arms Qiu was escorting.

但有一层是很清楚的，老太爷的处境相当为难，
One thing was clear: the Old Master was in a very difficult position.

只是难在何处，却怎么佯也想不出。
Yet Hu could not imagine where the difficulty lay.

江湖上做事，讲究彼此为人着想，
Among men of their world, consideration for one another mattered above everything.

所以胡雪岩在这时候，觉得别样心思可以暂时不想，自己的态度一定得先表明。
Hu felt that all other questions could wait; first he must make his own position clear.

“老太爷，”他说，“我晓得你拿我这面的事，当自己的事一样，
“Old Master, I know you treat my concerns as your own.

既然这样子，我们就当这件事你我都有分，好好商量着办。
Let us regard this as something we are both involved in, and work out a solution together.

如果难处光是由你老一肩挑了过去，即使能够办通，我也不愿意。”
If you shoulder all the difficulty alone, I shall not accept it, even if you succeed.”

“老弟台！”老太爷伸出一只全是骨节老茧的手，捏着胡雪岩的手腕说：“我真没有白交你这个朋友。
Stretching out a hand all knuckles and calluses, the old man gripped Hu’s wrist. “My young friend, I have not been mistaken in choosing you as a friend.

我把事情说给你听。”
Let me explain.”


真如他自己所说的：“事情说巧真巧，说不巧真不巧”，
It was indeed just as he had said: an unfortunate coincidence, yet a fortunate one.

这一批军人跟他的一个“同参弟兄”有关，这个人名叫俞武成，地盘是在扬州、镇江一带。
The shipment of arms concerned one of his fellow initiates, a man named Yu Wucheng, whose territory lay around Yangzhou and Zhenjiang.

这时太平军虽已退出扬州，但仍留赖汉英扼守辰州，与清军刑部左侍郎雷正诚的水师，相持不下。
Though the Taiping forces had withdrawn from Yangzhou, they had left Lai Hanying holding Chenzhou, where he was locked in a stalemate with the Qing naval forces under Lei Yixian, left vice-minister of punishments.

太平军全力谋求打开局面，所以跟上海的洋商有交易，希望买到一批军火。
Determined to break the deadlock, the Taipings had been dealing with foreign merchants in Shanghai in the hope of buying arms.

“这件事要派洋商的不是！”老太爷说：
“The foreign merchants are to blame for this,” the Old Master said.

“浙江买的那批洋枪，原来洋商是答应卖给‘长毛’的，
“They had originally promised the rifles Zhejiang has bought to the Longhairs.

已经收了人家的定洋，约期起运，由英国兵舰运了去。
They had already taken their deposit and agreed on a shipping date, with a British warship to carry the cargo.

哪知道事情变了卦，
Then the arrangement changed.

听说替浙江方面出头交涉的人，手腕很灵活……”“老太爷，”胡雪岩很高兴地抢着说，
I hear that the man negotiating for Zhejiang was extremely resourceful…” Hu interrupted with pleasure.

“这个人不是别人，就是未来的‘七姑爷’古应春。”
“That was none other than the Seventh Lady’s future husband, Gu Yingchun.”

“噢！
“Oh!

我不晓得。
I had no idea.

老五这两个月一直在上海，消息隔绝了。
Old Five has been in Shanghai these past two months, and I have been cut off from the news.

这且不去说他，
Never mind that for now.

先说我那个同参弟兄俞武成。”
Let us return to my brother Yu Wucheng.”

俞武成跟赖汉英相熟，
Yu knew Lai Hanying well.

因而一半交情，一半重礼，赖汉英托出俞武成来，预备等这批军火从上海起运，一入内河，就要动手截留。
Appealing partly to their friendship and partly through a substantial gift, Lai had enlisted him to intercept the arms as soon as they left Shanghai and entered the inland waterways.

由于是松江漕帮的地盘，所以俞武成专程到松江来拜访他这位老师兄，很客气地打了招呼。
Since this was the territory of the Songjiang Grain Transport Brotherhood, Yu had made a special journey to call on his senior fellow initiate and courteously notify him.

“这怪我一时疏忽。”老大爷失悔地说，
“It was my own carelessness,” the Old Master said regretfully.

“我是久已不管闲事，一切都交给老五，
“I have long stopped attending to these matters and left everything to Old Five.

偏偏者五又到杭州去了。
Unfortunately, he had gone to Hangzhou.

俞武成又是当年一炷香一起磕头的弟兄！
And Wucheng is a brother who knelt with me before the same stick of incense!

五十年下来，同参的只剩了三个人，
After fifty years, only three of our fellow initiates remain.

这个交情，我不能不买。
I could not disregard that friendship.

哪晓得大水冲了龙王庙！
Who could have imagined the flood would wash into the Dragon King’s own temple?

如今说不得了，
Now there is nothing for it.

只好我说了话不算！“
I shall have to break my word.”


“那怎么可以？”胡雪岩口答道，
“How can that be right?” Hu replied.

“俞老虽是你老的同参，但是答应过他的，
“Elder Yu may be your fellow initiate, but you have given him your word.

也不能脸一抹，说是自己人的东西，不准动！
You cannot simply change your face and tell him that the goods belong to friends and must not be touched.

光棍不断财路，
A man of our world does not cut off another man’s livelihood.

我来想办法。
Let me find a way.”

““老弟台！没有叫你伤脑筋的道理。
“There is no reason for you to rack your brains over it.

我是因为当你自己人，所以拿门槛里的话告诉了你，
I have told you what lies within our doors because I regard you as one of us.

照规矩是不能说的。”老太爷又说：
Under our rules, I ought not to have spoken at all.

“我只请你做个参赞，
I ask only for your advice.

事情是我的，无论如何要掮它下去，
The responsibility is mine, and I must bear it whatever happens.

你请裘老爷放心好了。”
Tell Mr Qiu he need not worry.”

“怎么放得下心！”胡雪岩说，
“How can we stop worrying?” Hu said.

“如今只有‘按兵不动’，
“For now we must hold our position.

那批洋枪先放在那里，等跟俞老谈好了再说。”
Leave the rifles where they are until we have reached an understanding with Elder Yu.”

老太爷不答，
The Old Master did not answer.

身往后一靠，双眼望空，紧闭着嘴唇，是那全心全意在思索如何解开这难题的神气。
Leaning back, he stared into space with his lips tightly closed, utterly absorbed in finding a way out.

胡雪岩见此光景，颇为不安，心里也在打算：
Hu watched uneasily, working through the problem himself.

如果俞武成不是他的“同参弟兄”，事情就好办，
Had Yu not been the old man’s fellow initiate, matters would have been simpler.

若是这批军火，不是落到太平军手里，事情也好办。
Had the weapons not been destined for the Taipings, that too would have made things easier.

此刻既是投鼠忌器，又不能轻易松手，
But now they dared not strike at the rat for fear of breaking the precious vessel, nor could they readily let go.

槁成了软硬都难着力的局面，
Neither firmness nor persuasion offered a clear path.

连他都觉得一时真难善策。
Even Hu could think of no immediate solution.


“难！”老太爷说，
“It is difficult,” the Old Master said.

“想来想去，只有我来硬挺。”
“After turning it over and over, I can see no way but to stand firm myself.”

“硬挺不是办法。”
“That will not solve it.

胡雪岩问道，“照你老看，俞老跟那面的交情如何？”
How close do you think Elder Yu is to the people on the other side?”

“这就不清楚了。
“I do not know.

不过江湖上走走，一句话就是一句话，
But among men of our world, a promise is a promise.

他答应了人家，我又答应了他，
He has promised them, and I have promised him.

反正不管怎么样，这票东西，我不让他动手，我们弟兄的交情就算断了。”
Whatever the circumstances, if I prevent him from laying hands on this cargo, our brotherhood is at an end.”

“话不能这么说！”
“Not necessarily!”

胡雪岩脑际灵光一闪，欣然说道：
An idea flashed into Hu’s mind.

“我倒有个无办法中的办法，
“I have a way out where there seems to be none.

我想请你老派个专人，将俞老请来，有话摆在台面上说：
Send a special messenger to invite Elder Yu here, and put everything openly before him.

两面都是自己人，
Both sides are friends.

不能帮一面损一面。
You cannot help one at the other’s expense.

事情该怎么办？请俞老自己说一句。”
Ask him himself what ought to be done.”

“这叫什么办法？”
“What sort of solution is that?”

老太爷笑道：
The old man smiled.

“那不就表示：这闲事我管不下来，只好不管吗？”
“It would simply mean that I cannot manage the affair and must withdraw from it.”

“正就是这话！”
“Exactly!”

胡雪岩点点头，
Hu nodded.

“你老不肯管这闲事，俞老怨不着你。
“If you decline to intervene, he cannot blame you.

而在我们这面，就承情不尽了。
And we, on our side, shall be endlessly grateful.”

“老太爷略想一下问道：
The old man considered.

“莫非你另有法子，
“Have you some other plan?

譬如请官兵保护，跟武成硬碰硬较量个明白？”
Perhaps you mean to seek protection from government troops and settle things by force with Wucheng?”

“我哪能这么做？”胡雪岩笑道，
“How could I do that?” Hu laughed.

“我这样一做，将来还想不想在江湖上跑跑？”
“If I did, could I ever move among men of the brotherhood again?”

“那么，你是怎么办呢？”
“Then what would you do?”

“我想跟俞老谈了再说。”
“I must speak to Elder Yu first.

胡雪岩答道，“我要跟他老实说明白，这票货色，如果不是太平军那面要，我可以放手，由他那面的户头承买，我另找洋商打交道，
I shall tell him plainly that if the Taipings were not the intended buyers, I could give way, let his clients buy the cargo, and negotiate another purchase with the foreigners myself.

现在可不行，
But as things stand, I cannot.

这是请俞老不要管闲事。
I shall ask him to stay out of it.

至于那面送了怎样一笔重礼，我照送就是。”
As for the handsome gift they have given him, I shall match it.”

“听说是一万银子。”
“I hear it was ten thousand taels.”

“一万银子小事，
“Ten thousand is no great matter.

我贴也贴得起。
I can afford to bear the cost.

我看俞老也不见得看得如何之重！
I doubt Elder Yu attaches that much importance to it either.

我要劝他的是，一定不可以帮长毛。
What I must persuade him of is that he must never help the Longhairs.

为人忠逆之辨，总不可以不分明。”
A man cannot fail to distinguish loyalty from rebellion.”


听到最后一句，老太爷很注意地望着他，
At these last words the Old Master looked at him intently.

好久，才点点头说：
After a long pause he nodded.

“老弟台，你虽是空子，漕帮的来龙去脉，清清楚楚，
“Though you are an outsider, you know the origins and history of the Grain Transport Brotherhood very well.

说句实话，二百年下来，
To tell you the truth, two hundred years have passed.

现在的时世，不是翁、钱、潘三祖当年立家门的时世了。
These are no longer the times in which our three patriarchs, Weng, Qian, and Pan, founded the brotherhood.

长毛初起，我们漕帮看得两‘秀’很重。
When the Longhairs first rose, we thought highly of the two Xius, Hong Xiuquan and Yang Xiuqing.

哪晓得越来越不象话，
Who could have foreseen how disgraceful they would become?

The Seventh Lady saw this and encouraged her.

Hu understood too.

七姑奶奶是玲珑七窍心，当然心领神会，

一口应承，包管主客双方，不但不至于会在礼节上出现僵窘，而且皆大欢喜。
She readily promised that neither hosts nor guests would suffer an awkward moment over etiquette, and that everyone would be delighted.

这种种情谊相孚的迹象，都显示着明天见了俞三婆婆，一切难题都可迎刃而解。

They needed only a prompt reply from A’tu confirming that Yu Wucheng was not under Lai Hanying’s coercion, and success would be almost assured.
