胡雪岩
《平步青云（下）》第二十七章（中）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume III — Chapter Twenty-Seven, Part Two
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


“真正不敢当！
“Really, this is more than I deserve!

胡老爷、裘老爷这么隆重的礼数，又赏了那么贵重的东西，
Such elaborate courtesies from you, Mr Hu and Mr Qiu, and such valuable gifts as well!

叫我老婆子真不知道怎么说才好。”
You leave an old woman quite at a loss for words.”

俞三婆婆说到这里，又转脸对七姑奶奶说，
Third Granny Yu turned to the Seventh Lady.

“我的耳朵不好，
“My hearing is poor.

回头两位有什么吩咐，你替我仔细听着！”
If these gentlemen have any instructions for us, listen carefully on my behalf.”

这就显得俞三婆婆是个角色了！
This revealed what a formidable woman she was.

她明朗耳聪目明，却偏这样子交代，为的是留下一个退步，等胡雪岩有所干求而无法办到时，便好装聋作哑，得有闪转腾挪的余地。
Her hearing and sight were perfectly clear, yet she deliberately said this to leave herself a way out: if Hu Xueyan asked for something she could not provide, she could pretend not to have heard or understood, and still have room to manoeuvre.

因为如此，胡雪岩越发不敢大意，
Hu therefore took even greater care.

要盲不烦地叙明来意，
He explained his purpose briefly and plainly.

一方面表示不愿使松江漕帮为难，开脱了老太爷的窘境，一方面又表示不愿请兵护运，怕跟俞武成发生冲突，伤了江湖的义气。
On the one hand, he wished to spare the Songjiang grain-transport brotherhood any embarrassment, thus relieving the Old Master of his predicament; on the other, he was reluctant to request a military escort, for fear of a clash with Yu Wucheng that would damage their bonds of loyalty.

这番话真如俗语所说“ 绵里针”，表面极软，骨子里大有讲究。
His words were a needle wrapped in cotton: soft on the surface, but with a sharp point within.

俞三婆婆到底老于江湖，熟悉世面，
Third Granny Yu knew the world and its ways.

听胡雪岩说到“不愿请兵护运”这句话，暗地里着实吃惊。
When Hu spoke of his reluctance to request a military escort, she was secretly alarmed.

话中等于指责俞武成抢劫军械，这是比强盗还重的罪名，
His words amounted to an accusation that Yu Wucheng intended to seize military arms, a crime more serious than ordinary robbery.

认起真来，灭门有余。
If pursued in earnest, it could destroy his entire household.


“胡老爷，
“Mr Hu!

裘老爷！”
Mr Qiu!”

俞三婆婆装出气得不得了的样子，
Third Granny Yu put on a show of furious indignation.

“我这个儿子，真正无法无天！
“That son of mine has no respect for law or heaven!

活到六十多，实在还不及我这个孙子懂事。
Over sixty years old, and still he has less sense than his own son.

两位看我老婆子的面上，千万不必生气，
For my sake, gentlemen, please do not be angry.

等我找了他来问。”
Let me have him brought here and question him.”

她回头拄一拄拐杖，厉声吩咐俞少武：“赶快多派人，
She turned, struck the floor with her stick, and ordered Yu Shaowu sternly, “Send more men at once.

把你那个糊涂老子找回来！”
Find that muddleheaded father of yours and bring him home!”

不管她是真的动气，还是有意做作，来客都大感不安，
Whether her anger was real or assumed, her guests were deeply uncomfortable.

“三婆婆！”
“Granny!”

胡雪岩急忙相劝，
Hu hastened to soothe her.

“这件事怪不得俞大哥！
“We must not blame Brother Yu.

我们也是道听途说，事情还不知道真假，
We have only heard rumours, and do not yet know whether there is any truth in them.

我想俞大哥亦不至于敌友不分。
I cannot believe Brother Yu would confuse friend and foe.

我们的来意，是想请三婆婆做主，
We came to ask you to decide what should be done.

就算没有这回事，少不得也要仰仗俞大哥的威名，保一保我们。”
Even if there is nothing in the story, we shall still need the protection of Brother Yu’s great name.”

听得这一说，俞三婆婆的脸色和缓了，
Her expression softened.

转眼对七姑奶奶说：“这倒还罢了！
Turning to the Seventh Lady, she said, “That is better!

我想你师叔也不至于这么糊涂！”
I did not think your Uncle Yu could be quite so foolish.”

略停一下，她又对客人说道：
After a pause she addressed her guests again.

“既承两位看得起我，武成理当效劳。
“Since you have honoured me with your confidence, Wucheng ought to serve you.

他心直口快，外面得罪的人多，每每有人造他的谣言，
He is a straightforward man who speaks his mind and has offended many people; they are forever spreading stories about him.

亏得两位贤明，决不会误听人言。
Fortunately, you gentlemen are too discerning to be misled by gossip.

事情好办，
This will be easily settled.

请两位在苏州玩个两三日，我一定叫两位高高兴兴回杭州。”
Spend two or three days enjoying Suzhou, and I promise to send you back to Hangzhou happy.”

胡雪岩将她的话，一字一句，听得明明白白，心里着实佩服俞三婆婆，
Hu took in every word, and could not help admiring her.

就这么轻描淡写地，将俞武成意图劫械的一行罪嫌，洗刷掉了。
With a few casual remarks she had washed away the entire suspicion that Yu Wucheng intended to seize the arms.

话是从自己口里说出去的：“道听途说”、“不知真假”，
“Rumours”; “we do not yet know whether there is any truth in them”: those were Hu’s own words.

即使将来翻脸，要想改口，已是不能。
Even if they later became enemies, he could hardly retract them.

真正姜是老的辣！
Old ginger was indeed the hottest.

自己竟糊里糊涂被她骗了一句话去、
Without realizing it, he had been tricked into making a concession.

可以说是这一年多一帆风顺的境遇中，唯一的一次栽跟斗。然而，这个跟斗栽得不能不服输。
In more than a year of uninterrupted success, this was the one occasion on which he had stumbled—and he had to acknowledge himself fairly beaten.


“多谢三婆婆，
“Thank you, Granny.

我们不敢打搅了。静听好音！”
We must not trouble you further, and shall await your good news.”

胡雪岩站起身说：
Hu rose.

“不过，我们还有句话，
“There is just one more thing.

实在想交一交俞大哥。
We would very much like to make Brother Yu’s acquaintance.

等他来了，务必请三婆婆派人给我们个信，我们好当面跟俞大哥解释。”
When he comes, please be sure to send word, so that we may explain things to him in person.”

“都是好朋友，一切心照，
“We are all good friends and understand one another.

何用解释？”俞三婆婆说，
What need is there for explanations?” she replied.

“两位抬举武成，我们母子祖孙三代都是感激的。
“Your kindness to Wucheng makes all three generations of our family grateful.

等武成一回来，我马上叫他给两位去请安。”
As soon as he returns, I shall send him to pay his respects.”

这几句交代，漂亮之至。
Nothing could have been more gracefully said.

胡雪岩和裘丰言，心满意足，但要告辞，却被留住了。
Hu and Qiu were entirely satisfied, but when they tried to leave, she detained them.

“无论如何，要让我们租孙，尽一点意思，
“You must at least allow my grandson and me to show a little hospitality.

吃了便饭再请回去！”
Have a simple meal before you go.

俞三婆婆又说：“看见两位，我倒想起有件心事，
Besides, seeing you has reminded me of something that has been weighing on my mind.

还要重托。”
I have an important favour to ask.”

俞三婆婆的话，其实是留客的托词。
This was really an excuse to keep them.

筵席是早就预备好的，俞家还请了陪客，有些是俞少武的同僚，有些是俞武成的师兄弟。
A banquet had been prepared beforehand, and the family had invited other guests to help entertain them: some were Yu Shaowu’s colleagues, others fellow disciples of Yu Wucheng.

不管是何身分，对胡、裘二人的礼数，都极恭敬。
Whatever their standing, all treated Hu and Qiu with the utmost respect.

好在胡雪岩长于词令，裘丰言为人风趣，所以很快地都消除了拘束的感觉，
Fortunately, Hu’s easy eloquence and Qiu’s wit soon dispelled the stiffness.

快谈豪饮，颇为酣畅。
They talked freely and drank heartily, and the occasion became thoroughly enjoyable.


酒到一半，俞少武告个罪，回到二厅，
Halfway through the drinking, Yu Shaowu excused himself and returned to the second hall.

那里也有一桌丰盛筵席，是俞三婆婆亲自做主人，款待芙蓉和七姑奶奶。
Another sumptuous table had been laid there, where Third Granny Yu was entertaining Furong and the Seventh Lady herself.

这一桌就不如外面那样轻松自如了，主要的原因是，芙蓉被奉为首席，深感不安，过于矜持。
This gathering was less relaxed than the one outside, chiefly because Furong, installed in the seat of honour, felt uncomfortable and was excessively reserved.

俞少武一进来，先敬堂客的酒。
Shaowu first offered the ladies a toast.

照官称叫芙蓉是“胡姨太太”，他也学了京里的规矩，将“姨”字念成“亦”子，表示“亦是一位太太”。
In official usage he addressed Furong as “Madam Hu,” with the title given to a concubine, but followed the polite Beijing custom of altering the tone of the word yi so that it suggested “also”: she was “also a wife.”

敬了“胡亦太太”，再敬七姑奶奶，
After toasting her, he drank to the Seventh Lady.

她跟俞少武是青梅竹马之交，一个叫“七姐”，一个叫“大弟弟”。
They had known one another since childhood: he called her Seventh Sister, and she affectionately called him her eldest little brother.

这一番周旋过后，俞少武才搀着祖母到大厅向官客来敬酒。
When these courtesies were over, he helped his grandmother into the main hall to toast the gentlemen.

在座的陪客都是她的晚辈，胡、裘二人亦以晚辈自居，
All the guests there belonged to a younger generation, and Hu and Qiu likewise treated her as their elder.

所以一齐起身离座，再三谦辞。
They rose from their seats together and repeatedly protested that she was too kind.

结果由俞三婆婆总敬一杯，然后向他孙子说道：“少武，你要向胡老爷、裘老爷磕头道谢。
In the end she drank one toast to the whole company, then told her grandson, “Shaowu, you must kowtow to Mr Hu and Mr Qiu and thank them.

这两位真正够义气！”
These are men who truly understand loyalty.”

俞少武也已知道他父亲的所作所为，倘或认真，是件不得了的事，
Shaowu now knew what his father had been doing, and understood how grave the consequences would be if the matter were pursued.

所以连声答应着，要来行礼。
He readily agreed and stepped forward to perform the ceremony.

胡雪岩和裘丰言，自然不肯受这个头。逊席相避，
Hu and Qiu naturally refused to accept it, moving away from their seats to avoid receiving his bow.

于是俞三婆婆又说话了。
Third Granny Yu spoke again.


“两位请听我说。
“Gentlemen, hear me out.

我就是这个孙子，
This is my only grandson.

如今大小也是朝廷的命官，在我们这种人家，也算荣宗耀祖了。
He now holds an imperial appointment, however modest, and in a family such as ours that brings honour to our ancestors.

不过，江湖上的家世，跟官场难免合不拢，
But a background in the brotherhoods does not sit easily with official life.

这是我一直不放心的一件事，总想托个人照应，
That has always worried me, and I have long wanted someone to look after him.

说实话，官场中也认识几位，不是人家看不起我们，就是自己觉得高攀不上。
To tell you the truth, we do know a few officials, but either they look down on us or we feel we are reaching above ourselves.

难得两位赏面子，
You gentlemen have been exceptionally kind.

再说句放肆的话，我也看得两位跟官场中人不同，真正是重情分，讲义气。
If I may speak freely, I also find you different from other officials: you value affection and honour your obligations.

所以，今天当着大家的面，我把我这个孙子，托付给两位，
So today, before everyone here, I entrust my grandson to you.

要让少武磕了头，我才放心。”
I shall not feel at ease until Shaowu has kowtowed to you.”

这一套长篇大论，旁人只觉得俞三婆婆是特别看重两位贵客，
To the others, this lengthy speech showed only how highly she valued her distinguished guests.

在胡雪岩却听出弦外之音，
Hu heard the meaning beneath it.

拜托照应俞少武，实在是拜托回护俞武成。
In asking them to protect Yu Shaowu, she was really asking them to shield Yu Wucheng.

照此看来，俞三婆婆用的心思极深，处处在防备自己这方面会动用官面上的力量来对付她的儿子。
Her calculations went deep: at every turn she was guarding against their using official power against her son.

有此疑忌存在，总不是件妙事。
Such suspicions could do no good.

为了消释可能会有的误会，胡雪岩不肯说谦辞的话，
To dispel any possible misunderstanding, Hu abandoned polite refusals.

“既然三婆婆如此吩咐。我们倒不能不老着脸受少武一个头。”他说，
“Since those are your instructions, Granny, we must have the effrontery to accept Shaowu’s bow.

“三婆姿，从今天起，少武的事，就等于我自己兄弟的事一样。”
From today, I shall treat his affairs as I would my own brother’s.”

“胡老爷，你的话错了！”俞三婆婆平静地说：
“You have that wrong, Mr Hu,” she said calmly.

“是你侄儿的事。”
“Your nephew’s.”

“侄儿也罢，兄弟也罢，只当我自己的事！”
“Nephew or brother, I shall treat them as my own affairs.”

“少武！”俞三婆婆极欣慰地说：
“Shaowu!” she said, deeply pleased.

“你听见没有？
“Did you hear that?

还不快磕头！
Hurry and bow!

你说想调回来，跟在我身边，
You say you want a transfer home so that you can stay with me.

胡老爷一定会替你想法子。”
Mr Hu is sure to find a way for you.”

这一说，俞少武更是心甘情愿地跪了下来，胡雪岩也就坦然受了他的大礼。
Shaowu now knelt with even greater willingness, and Hu accepted his solemn obeisance without further demur.

江湖上重然诺，
In the brotherhoods, a pledge was binding.

经此当筵一拜，俞少武的穷通富贵，便与胡雪岩息息相关了。
After this bow before the assembled guests, Shaowu’s future fortunes were intimately linked with Hu’s.

而父子的安危祸福是不可分的，所以俞武成如果遇到了什么难题，胡雪岩由于对俞少武有责任，自然也不能袖手。
A father’s and a son’s safety and prosperity could not be separated; if Wucheng encountered trouble, Hu’s obligation to Shaowu would prevent him from standing aside.

俞三婆婆这着棋，实在高明，然而也只有胡雪岩喻得其中的深意。
Third Granny Yu had played a masterly move, though Hu alone understood its full significance.


因此，他对松江的消息，特感关心。
This made Hu particularly anxious for news from Songjiang.

为了不愿让裘丰言担心，他只好独任其忧，在肚子里默默做功夫，将俞武成的情况，重新作一番深入的估计。
Unwilling to alarm Qiu, he bore the worry alone, silently working through a fresh and more searching assessment of Yu Wucheng’s position.

想得越多，疑虑越深，
The more he thought, the deeper his misgivings grew.

到了第二天早晨，尚无消息，他觉得不能再因循株守，坐失时机了。
When the following morning came without news, he felt that he must stop waiting passively and letting his opportunities slip away.

于是约了俞少武在吴苑茶馆见面，找个僻静之处，悄悄问道：“你晓不晓得令尊此刻在哪里？”
He arranged to meet Yu Shaowu at the Wuyuan teahouse, found a secluded place, and asked quietly, “Do you know where your father is at present?”

“大概是在青浦叉袋角。”
“Probably at Chadaijiao, near Qingpu.

俞少武说，“不瞒老世叔说，家父在那里有一房家眷，
To be frank with you, Uncle, my father keeps another household there.

叉袋角又有几家大赌场，是家父喜欢去的地方。
There are also several large gambling houses at Chadaijiao, and he likes to visit them.

我昨天就请人分头去找了，
Yesterday I sent people off in different directions to look for him.

到今天晚上一定会有消息的。”
There should certainly be news by tonight.”

“我倒要问问你，令尊跟赖某人到底是啥交情？
“Tell me, then: what exactly is your father’s relationship with this Lai?

他想动那票‘货色’，你知道不知道？
Did you know he intended to lay hands on that consignment of ‘goods’?”

“这一问，俞少武的脸色显得异常认真，
Shaowu’s face became intensely serious.

用一种近乎要赌咒的语气答道：
He answered in a tone that came close to swearing an oath.

“在老世叔面前，我不敢说一个字的假话，
“I would not dare tell you a single falsehood, Uncle.

我一点都不晓得。
I knew absolutely nothing.

家父不会跟我说，我也不便去问。
My father would not tell me, and it would hardly be proper for me to question him.

而且我一直在京城里，回来还下到半个月，
Besides, I have been in the capital all this time and returned less than a fortnight ago.

一共见过家父两面，谈不了几句话。
I have seen him only twice, and we exchanged very few words.

如果我晓得有这件事，无论如何要想法子，劝家父打消了它！”
If I had known of any such plan, I would have done everything I could to persuade him to abandon it.”


话说得很诚恳，也相当坦率，胡雪岩觉得跟他谈论，不必象对他祖母那样，要加几分小心，
His sincerity and frankness convinced Hu that he need not be as guarded with him as with his grandmother.

便直抒所感，
He spoke his mind.

“这件事，照我看有麻烦。
“I foresee trouble here.

令尊客居异地，手下的弟兄都不在这里，
Your father is living away from his own territory, and his men are not here.

虽然出头来主持，无非因人成事。
Even if he takes the lead, he must depend on others to carry it through.

上山容易下山难，
It is easier to climb the mountain than to come down.

不是凭一句话就可以罢手的。
He cannot necessarily call the whole thing off with a word.

如果脱不得身，怎么办？”
What if he cannot extricate himself?”

俞少武是现任的武官，当然能够领会胡雪岩所说的话，
As a serving military officer, Shaowu readily grasped the point.

想一想果然，截掠军械，是件非同小可的事，
Seizing a shipment of arms was no small undertaking.

调兵遣将，如何下手，得手以后，如何将这批枪械运交赖汉英？
Men had to be deployed, the attack planned, the captured weapons delivered to Lai Hanying.

官军派出大队拦截剿办，又如何应付？
And what would they do if government troops came in force to intercept and suppress them?

自然得有一番布置，
Arrangements must already have been made.

而入不是自己的人，中途变卦，想凭一句话就撤消原有的布置，哪有这么容易的事？
If the men were not his father’s own, how could he change his mind halfway through and cancel everything with a word?

这样一层一层想下来，脸上顿现愁云：
As Shaowu followed the implications, anxiety clouded his face.

“事不宜迟！”
“There is no time to lose.

他说，“及早劝阻，还容易着手。
The sooner we dissuade him, the easier it will be.

我马上就到青浦去一趟。”
I shall go to Qingpu at once.”

见他如此果断，胡雪岩深感安慰，不过他的计算到底比俞少武深得多，
Hu was reassured by his decisiveness, but his own calculations went further.

按着他的手说：
He laid a hand on Shaowu’s.

“你不宜去！
“You should not go.

因为虽是父子，到底是朝廷的五品武官，
He may be your father, but you are an imperial military officer of the fifth rank.

去了容易让人起疑。
Your presence could arouse suspicion.

而且，只要令尊是在青浦，这时候就一定到了松江，
Besides, if your father was at Qingpu, he will certainly have reached Songjiang by now.

你去了也是扑空。”
You would miss him.”

“那么，老世叔说怎么办，
“Then tell me what to do, Uncle.

我听命。”
I shall obey.”

“我想我马上赶回松江去看看。
“I think I had better hurry back to Songjiang and see for myself.

你派个得力的人跟了我去。”
Send a capable man with me.

胡雪岩紧接着说，“令祖母有什么话交代，最好也由这个人带了去，
And if your grandmother has any instructions, it would be best for him to carry them too.

那就更省事了。”
That will save trouble.”

“是！”
“Certainly.

俞少武说，“我马上回去告诉我奶奶。
I shall go straight home and tell her.

老世叔是不是一起到舍下坐坐？”
Would you like to come with me?”

“不必！”
“No.

胡雪岩答道：“我先回金阊栈料理，在那里等你的信息。
I shall return to the Jinchang Inn to make arrangements and wait there for your message.

再托你转告七姑奶奶，小妾烦她照应。”
Please also tell the Seventh Lady that I must trouble her to look after Furong.”

“是，是！
“Of course.

我奶奶跟姨太太极谈得来，
My grandmother and Madam Hu get on wonderfully.

就请她在舍下玩两天，一切我们都会伺候，
Let her spend a couple of days at our house; we shall attend to everything.

老世叔请放心！”
Please set your mind at rest.”

“打搅不安。
“I am sorry to impose on you.

而后叔雅对他的尊敬，则从“常有往来，常有馈遗”这些话中，表现得明明白白。
Pan’s respect for him was equally plain in those frequent visits and gifts.

胡雪岩的愿意，就是要替他们拉拢，所以听得何桂清的话，当然感到欣慰。
Bringing the two together had been Hu’s intention, and he was naturally pleased.

照规矩，他亦还需有所表示，
Courtesy required an acknowledgment.

“云公爱屋及乌，真是感同身受。”他拱拱手说。
“Your kindness extends from me to my friends, Mr Genyun, and I feel it as though it were shown to myself,” he said, clasping his hands.

“哪里，哪里！”
“Not at all, not at all!”

何桂清心里在想，真叫“三日不见，刮目相看”，
He thought of the saying that after only three days apart one must look at a man with fresh eyes.

相隔没有多少日子，不想他也会掉文了！
They had been separated for so short a time, yet Hu had learnt to turn a literary phrase!

虽是尺牍上的套话，总算难能可贵，
Even if it was merely a conventional expression from letter-writing, it was a creditable accomplishment.

这样想着，便又笑道：“雪岩兄，曾几何时，你的谈吐大不相同，
Smiling, he said, “Brother Xueyan, in no time at all your conversation has become quite different.

可喜之至。”
How pleasing!”

胡雪岩略有窘色，
Hu looked a little embarrassed.

“叫云公见笑！”
“You flatter me, Mr Genyun.”

他急转直下地说：
He abruptly changed the subject.

“有件事，想跟云公请教。”
“There is something on which I should like your advice.”

说着，他看了看站在门口的听差。
He glanced at the attendant by the door.

这是有要紧话说，何桂清便吩咐听差回避，然后由对面换到胡雪岩下首，侧过头来，等他发话。
Understanding that an important confidence was coming, He dismissed the man, moved from the seat opposite Hu to the place beside him on the less honoured side, and inclined his head to listen.


“我想请教云公一件事，”胡雪岩低声说道，
“I should like to ask you about a certain matter, Mr Genyun,” Hu began in a low voice.

“现在有一批人，一时糊涂，误犯官军，
“There is a group of men who lost their heads for a time and wrongly took up arms against the government.

很想改过，
They are anxious to mend their ways.

不知道朝廷能不能给他们一条自新之路？”
Could the court give them a chance to make a fresh start?”

“怎么不能？
“Why ever not?

这是件绝好之事！”
That would be an excellent thing!”

何桂清大为兴奋，
He grew highly excited.

“这批人是哪里的？”
“Where are these men?”

问到这话，胡雪岩当然不肯泄底，
Hu would certainly not disclose that.

“我亦是辗转受人之托，
“The request reached me through intermediaries.

来手做事很慎重，详情还不肯说。
The man handling it is very cautious and will not yet give the details.

不过，托我的那人，是我相信得过的。
But the person who approached me is someone I trust.

我也觉得这是件好事，心想云公是有魄力、肯做事的人，所以特地来请教。”
I too thought it a good thing, and knowing that you have both courage and a willingness to act, I came expressly for your advice.”

他略停一下又说：
He paused.

“如今我要讨云公一句话，此事可行与否？
“What I need now is your word: can it be done?

朝廷可有什么安抚奖励的章程？”
Does the court have any regulations for granting assurances or rewards?”

“一般都是朝廷的子民，
“They are all subjects of the court.

如能悔过自新，朝廷自然优容，
If they repent and reform, the court will naturally treat them generously.

所以安抚奖励，都责成疆吏，相机处理。”何桂清又说，
Such assurances and rewards are therefore left to the provincial authorities to arrange as circumstances require.

“我为什么要问这批人在哪里，就是要看看归谁管，
I asked where they were because we must see whose jurisdiction they come under.

如果是苏州以西，常州、镇、扬一带，归江南、江北两大营，
West of Suzhou, around Changzhou, Zhenjiang, and Yangzhou, they would fall under the Jiangnan and Jiangbei Grand Camps.

怡制台都难过问。
Even Governor-General Yi would find it difficult to intervene.

倘或是苏州以东，许中丞是我同年，
But east of Suzhou, Governor Xu is a fellow graduate of my year.

我可以跟他说，诸事都好办。”
I can speak to him, and everything will be easier.”

听得这话，胡雪岩暗暗心喜，
Hu was secretly delighted.

“那么，等我问清了再回报云公。
“Then I shall ascertain the facts and report back to you.

不过，”胡雪岩试探着问：“我想，招抚总不外有官做、有饷领，
But I imagine,” he ventured, “that winning men over generally comes down to giving them appointments and paying them.

云公，你说是不是呢？
Would you agree?”

““给官做是一走的，
“Appointments are certain.

看那方面人数多少，枪械如何，改编为官军，要下委札派相当的官职。
According to their numbers and their weapons, they would be reorganized as government troops and commissions issued for suitable posts.

饷呢，至多只能过来的时候，关一次恩饷，
As for pay, at most they could receive a special gratuity when they first came over.

以后看是归谁节制，自有‘粮台’统筹发放。”
Afterwards, it would depend on whose command they entered; the military finance office would arrange their regular pay.”


胡雪岩所想象的，亦是如此。
This was what Hu had expected.

只是授官给饷，都还在第二步争取，
Appointments and pay, however, belonged to the second stage of negotiation.

首先有句话，关系极重，不能不问清楚。
First there was something of far greater importance that he had to establish.

“云公，”他特意摆出担忧的沉重脸色，“我听说有些地方弃械就抚的，结果上了大当，悔之莫及。
“Mr Genyun,” he said, deliberately assuming a grave, anxious expression, “I have heard that in some places men who laid down their arms and accepted pacification were badly deceived, and regretted it when it was too late.

不知可有这话？”
Is there any truth in that?”

“你是说‘杀降’？”
“You mean the killing of men who surrender?”

何桂清大摇其头，
He shook his head emphatically.

“杀降不祥，古有明训。
“Since ancient times we have been warned that killing those who surrender brings ill fortune.

这件事你托到我，就是你不说，我也一定要当心。
If you entrust this affair to me, I shall take care over that even without your asking.

你想想，我无缘无故来造这个孽干什么？
Why should I bring such evil upon myself for no reason?

再说，我对你又怎么交代？”
And how could I answer to you?”

“是！是！”
“Of course, of course!”

胡雪岩急忙站起来作了个揖：
Hu rose hastily and bowed.

“云公厚爱，我自然知道，
“I know your kindness to me.

只不过提醒云公而已。”
I merely wished to remind you.”

“是你的事，我无有不好说的。
“Since it is your affair, I am ready to accommodate you in every way.

不过，这件事要快，
But it must be done quickly.

迟了我就管不到了。”
If it is delayed, it will be beyond my reach.”

“我明白，
“I understand.

就在这两三天内，此事必有个起落。
Within the next two or three days it will certainly be settled one way or the other.

不过还有句话，我要先求云公体谅。”
There is just one more thing for which I must ask your understanding.

胡雪岩说：“人家来托我，只是说有这件事，
The people who approached me told me only that such a matter existed.

详情如何，一概不知。
I know none of the details.

也许别有变化，作为罢论，
Circumstances may change and the whole thing be dropped.

到那时候，我求云公不要追究。”
If that happens, I beg you not to pursue it.”

“当然。
“Naturally.

我不会多事的。”
I shall not make trouble.”

“还要求云公不必跟人谈起。”
“And please do not mention it to anyone.”

“我知道，我知道。
“I understand.

如果此事作为罢论，我就当根本没有听你说过。
If it is dropped, I shall behave as though you had never told me.

总而言之，我决不会给你惹麻烦。”
In any event, I shall certainly cause you no difficulties.”


“云公如此体恤，以后我效劳的地方就多了！”
“With such consideration from you, Mr Genyun, there will be many more ways in which I can be of service.”

这句话中有深意，意思是说，
The words carried a deeper meaning.

只要何桂清肯言听计从，不是自作主张，他就会有许多办法拿出来，帮何桂清升官发财。
If He would follow Hu’s advice instead of acting on his own initiative, Hu could devise any number of ways to help him gain promotion and wealth.

“正要倚重。”何桂清说：
“That is exactly the assistance I hope to rely on,” He replied.

“老兄阛阓奇才，佩服之至。
“I have the greatest admiration for your extraordinary commercial gifts.

前几天又接得雪轩的长函，说老兄帮了他许多忙。
A few days ago I received another long letter from Xuexuan, telling me how much you had done for him.

我跟雪轩的交情，不同泛泛，
My friendship with him is no ordinary one.

以后要请老兄以待雪轩者待我！”
In future, I hope you will treat me as you treat him.”

于是由此又开始叙旧，一谈就谈得无休无止。
This led them into reminiscence, and the talk went on and on.

许多客来拜访，何桂清都吩咐听差，请在花厅里坐，却迟迟不肯出见，尽自应酬胡雪岩。
Many other visitors arrived; He instructed his attendant to seat them in the reception pavilion, yet repeatedly put off seeing them while he continued to entertain Hu.

这让客人很不安，同时也因为还有许多事要料理，
This made Hu uncomfortable, and he also had many matters still to attend to.

所以一再告辞，而主人一再挽留，最后还要留着吃晚饭，
He repeatedly tried to leave, but his host repeatedly detained him, finally insisting that he stay for dinner.

胡雪岩无论如何不肯。
Hu would not agree.

等到脱身辞了出来，太阳已快下山了。
By the time he managed to take his leave, the sun was almost down.
