胡雪岩
《平步青云（下）》第二十七章（下）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume III — Chapter Twenty-Seven, Part Three
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


轿伕请示去处，胡雪岩有些踌躇，
When the chair bearers asked where to go, Hu hesitated.

照道理要去看一看三婆婆，却又怕天黑了不方便。
He ought to call on Third Granny Yu, but it would be inconvenient after dark.

如果回到金阊栈，则出了城就无需再进城，这一夜白耗费在客栈里未免可惜。
If he returned to Jinchang Inn, there would be no reason to come into the city again, and wasting the whole evening at the inn seemed a pity.

左右为难之下，想到了第三个去处，去拜访潘叔雅。
Caught between the two, he thought of a third destination: Pan Shuya's house.

不过天黑拜客，似乎礼貌有亏，
Yet a call after dark might seem discourteous.

而且一见要谈到他所托的事，如何应付，预先得好好想一想，
Besides, Pan would ask about the business he had entrusted to him, and Hu needed to consider his answer carefully.

仓促之间，还是以不见面为宜。
Better not meet him in haste.

于是又想到了第四个去处，
Then he thought of a fourth possibility.

“喂！”他问轿伕：“有个有名的姑娘，叫黄银宝，住在哪里，你晓不晓得？”
“Tell me, do you know where a famous courtesan called Huang Yinbao lives?”

轿伕歉然赔笑：
The bearer smiled apologetically.

“这倒不晓得了。”
“I'm afraid not.”

“苏州的堂子，多在哪一带？”
“Where are most of the houses in Suzhou?”

“多在山塘。
“Along Shantang.

上塘丁家巷最多。”轿伕建议：
Dingjia Lane in Shangtang has the most.

“我们抬了胡老爷到那里问一问就知道了。”
We could carry you there and ask.”

一家一家去访艳，胡雪岩觉得无此闲工夫，大可不必。
Hu had no time to search from house to house for a beauty.

而且就寻到了，无非陪着裘丰言吃一顿花酒，也干不了什么正经。
Even if he found her, it would mean no more than drinking with Qiu Fengyan in her company; nothing useful would be done.

这样一想，便断然决定了主意，回客栈再说。
He made up his mind: back to the inn first.


一到金阊栈，迎面就看到周一鸣，一见胡雪岩如获至宝，
As soon as he reached Jinchang Inn, he saw Zhou Yiming, who greeted him as though he had found a treasure.

“胡先生，胡先生！”
“Mr Hu!

他说，“等了你老一下午。”
I've been waiting all afternoon.”

胡雪岩未及答言，只见又闪出来一个后生，
Before Hu could answer, a young man stepped forward.

长得高大白皙，极其体面，那张脸生得很清秀，而且带点脂粉气，
Tall, fair and extremely presentable, he had a delicate, almost feminine face.

胡雪岩觉得仿佛在哪里见过似地，一时愣在那里，忘了说话。
Hu felt he had seen him somewhere before and stood speechless for a moment.

“他叫福山。”周一鸣说，
“This is Fushan,” Zhou explained.

“是阿巧姐的兄弟。”
“A’qiao's brother.”

“怪不得！”
“No wonder!

胡雪岩恍然大悟，“我说好面熟，象是以前见过！
I thought you looked familiar, as though we'd met before.

这就不错了，
That explains it.

你跟你姐姐长得很相象。”
You and your sister look very much alike.”

福山有些腼腆，“胡老爷！”
Somewhat shyly, Fushan said, “Master Hu.”

那一口苏州话中的脂粉气更浓，
His Suzhou accent sounded even more feminine.

然后，跪了下去磕头。
Then he knelt and kowtowed.

“请起来，请起来！”
“Get up, please!”

福山是他姐姐特地关照过的，非磕头不可，
His sister had specifically insisted on this courtesy.

胡雪岩连拖带拉把他弄了起来，
Hu had to pull and lift him to his feet.

心里十分高兴，但他自己也不知道是因为福山长得体面，还是爱屋及乌的缘故。
He was very pleased, though he could not tell whether it was the young man's fine appearance or affection extending from the sister to the brother that pleased him.

“我一大早到木渎去了。特地把他带了出来见胡先生。”周一鸣说。
“I went to Mudu early this morning,” Zhou said, “especially to bring him to meet you.”


“怪道，早晨等你不来。”
“No wonder you didn't come this morning.”

胡雪岩接着又转脸来问福山：
Hu turned to Fushan.

“你今年几岁？”
“How old are you?”

“十九岁。”
“Nineteen.”

“学的布店生意？”
“You've been learning the cloth trade?”

“是的。”
“Yes.”

“有几年了？”
“For how many years?

胡雪岩问，“满师了没有？”
Have you finished your apprenticeship?”

“满师满了一年了。”
“I finished a year ago.”

只问了两句话，倒有三处不符的地方。
In only a few questions, three discrepancies had emerged.

胡雪岩的记性极好，记得阿巧姐告诉过他的话，因而问道：
Hu's memory was excellent, and he remembered what A’qiao had told him.

“你的小名不是叫阿顺吗？”
“Isn't your childhood name A’shun?”

“是的。”
“Yes.

福山答道，“进布店学生意，老板叫我福山，就这样叫开了。”
When I entered the cloth shop, the proprietor called me Fushan, and the name stuck.”

“我记得你姐姐说你今年十八岁，还没有满师。”
“I remember your sister saying you were eighteen and hadn't finished your apprenticeship.”

“我是十九岁。
“I'm nineteen.

我姐姐记错了。”
My sister remembered wrong.”

“那么，你满师不满师，你姐姐总不会记错的罗？”“也可以说满师，也可以说不满师。”周一鸣代为解释：
“But surely she couldn't be mistaken about whether you'd finished?” Zhou explained for him. “You could say he has, or that he hasn't.

“他学生意是学满了，照例要‘帮师三年’，
His training is complete, but by custom he must serve his master for another three years.

还没有帮满。”
That term isn't finished yet.”

“现在都弄妥当了？”胡雪岩看着周一鸣问。
“Is everything settled now?” Hu asked Zhou.

“早已弄妥当。”
“All settled.

周一鸣答道，“ ‘关书’已经拿了回来。”
We've got his apprenticeship contract back.”

“那好。”
“Good.”

胡雪岩又问福山，
He turned to Fushan again.

“你姐姐拿你托付给我，
“Your sister has entrusted you to me.

我倒要问你，你想做点啥？”
Tell me, what would you like to do?”

“要请胡老爷……”
“I should like Master Hu to...”

“不要叫老爷！”胡雪岩打断他的话说，
“Don't call me Master,” Hu interrupted.

“叫先生好了。”
“Call me Mr Hu.”

“噢！”
“Yes, sir!”

福山也觉得叫“老爷”碍口，所以欣然应声：“先生！”
Fushan too had found “Master” awkward on his tongue and gladly adopted the new form of address.


“你是学布生意的，对绸缎总识货罗？”
“Since you've learned the cloth business, I suppose you can judge silks and satins?”

“识是识。不过那爿布店不大，货色不多，
“I can, but the shop was small and didn't stock much.

有些贵重绸缎没有见过。”
There are some costly silks I've never seen.”

“那倒不要紧，
“That doesn't matter.

我带你到上海，自然见识得到。”
I'll take you to Shanghai, where you'll have the chance.

胡雪岩又说，“做生意最要紧一把算盘。”
The most important tool in business is an abacus.”

“他的算盘打得好。”周一鸣插嘴说道：
“He's good with one,” Zhou put in.

“飞快！”
“Very quick.”

“噢，我倒考考你。
“Then let me test you.

你拿把算盘坐下来。”
Get an abacus and sit down.”

等福山准备好了，胡雪岩随口出了一个题目，
Once Fushan was ready, Hu casually gave him a problem.

四匹布一共十两银子，
Four bolts of cloth cost ten taels in all.

每匹布的尺寸不同，四丈七、五丈六、三丈二、四丈九，
Their lengths differed: forty-seven, fifty-six, thirty-two and forty-nine chi.

问每尺布合到多少银子？
What was the price per chi?

他说得很快，
He spoke quickly.

用意是考福山的算盘之外，还要考他的智慧。
Besides testing the boy's abacus, he wanted to test his intelligence.

如果这些罗里罗嗦的数目，听一遍就能记得清楚，便是可造之材。
If he could remember all those troublesome figures after hearing them once, he had promise.

福山不负所望，
Fushan did not disappoint him.

五指翻飞，将算盘珠拨得清脆流利，
His fingers flew, and the beads clicked briskly and smoothly.

只听那“大珠小珠落玉盘”似的声音，就知道是好手。
The sound alone, like “large and small pearls falling onto a jade dish”, showed an expert hand.

等声音一停，报告结果：“四匹布一共一百八十四尺，
When it stopped, he reported, “The four bolts total a hundred and eighty-four chi.

总价十两，每尺合到五厘四毫三丝四忽挂零。”
At ten taels altogether, each chi costs five li, four hao, three si and four hu of a tael, with a fraction left over.”

胡雪岩亲自拿算盘复了一遍，果然不错，
Hu checked it on the abacus himself and found it correct.

深为满意。便点点头说：
Thoroughly satisfied, he nodded.

“你做生意是学得出来的。
“You can learn this business.

不过，光是记性好、算盘打得快，别样本事不行，只能做小生意。
But a good memory and quick reckoning will only take you as far as small business if your other abilities fall short.

做大生意是另外一套本事，一时也说不尽。
Large business needs another set of skills, more than I can explain at once.

你跟着我，慢慢自会明白，
Stay with me, and you'll understand in time.

今天我先告诉你一句话：要想吃得开，一定要说话算话。
Today I'll tell you one thing: to get on in the world, you must keep your word.

所以答应人家之前，先要自己想一想，做得到，做不到？
Before promising anything, consider whether you can do it.

做不到的事，不可答应人家，答应了人家一定要做到。”
Don't promise what you can't do; once you've promised, you must do it.”

他一路说，福山一路深深点头，等胡雪岩说完，他恭恭敬敬地答一声：“我记牢了！”
Fushan nodded earnestly throughout, then answered respectfully, “I'll remember.”


“你苏州城里熟不熟？”
“Do you know Suzhou well?”

“城里不熟。”
“Not the city itself.”

“那么，山塘呢？”
“What about Shantang?”

“山塘熟的。”福山问道，
“I know Shantang.

“先生要问山塘啥地方？”
Which part do you want, sir?”

“我自己不去，
“I'm not going myself.

想请你去跑一趟。
I want you to run an errand.

有个姑娘叫黄银宝，
There's a courtesan called Huang Yinbao.

我有两个朋友在那里，一个姓裘，一个姓刘，
Two friends of mine are there, one named Qiu and one Liu.

你看看他们在那里做什么？回来告诉我。”胡雪岩紧紧接着又说，“你不要让他们知道，有人在打听他们。”
Find out what they're doing and come back to tell me.” Hu added at once, “Don't let them know anyone is making inquiries.”

“噢！”福山很沉着地答应着，站起身来，
“Yes, sir.” Fushan answered calmly and rose.

似乎略有踌躇，但终于很快地走了。
He seemed to hesitate briefly, but soon left.

等他背影消失，周一鸣微带不以为然的语气说：
When he was out of sight, Zhou spoke with mild disapproval.

“胡先生，我知道你是考考他‘外场’的本事，
“I know you're testing his ability to handle himself, Mr Hu.

不过，他这种小后生，到那种地方去，总不大相宜！”
But such places hardly suit a young fellow like him.”

“你怕他落入‘迷魂阵’是不是？”胡雪岩笑道：
“Afraid he'll fall into a web of enchantment?” Hu laughed.

“不要紧的！
“Never fear.

我看他那个样子，早就在迷魂阵里闯过一阵子了。
From the look of him, he's already spent some time in one.

我倒不是考他，就是要看看他那路门径熟不熟？”
I'm not so much testing him as seeing how well he knows his way around.

停了一下他又说：“少年入花丛，总比临老入花丛好。
Better enter the garden of pleasure young than in old age.

我用人跟别人不同，
I employ people differently from others.

别人要少年老成，
They want young men with old heads.

我要年纪轻的有才干、有经验，什么事看过经过，
I want young men with ability and experience, who've seen and been through everything.

到了要紧关头，才不会着迷上当。”
Then, when it matters, they won't be dazzled and deceived.”

这番见解，在周一鸣不曾听说过，
This was a view Zhou had never heard before.

一时无话可答，仔细想想，似乎也有些道理。
He had no answer at first, though reflection suggested there was some sense in it.

不过，他在想，年轻后生，一个个都见过世面，经过阵仗，学得调皮捣蛋，驾驭可就不容易了。
Still, young men who had all seen the world and learned its tricks might be hard to control.

“也只有胡先生，有本事吃得住他们。”周一鸣毕竟想通了，
At last he understood. “Only you could keep them in hand, Mr Hu.

“旁人不敢象胡先生这样子做法。”
Other people wouldn't dare do things your way.”

“对！”
“Exactly!”

胡雪岩表示欣慰，
Hu was pleased.

“你算是懂得我了。”
“Now you understand me.”

“不过，”周一鸣又替福山担心，“他身上没有什么钱，
Zhou still worried about Fushan. “But he hasn't much money.

就找到了黄家，那种‘门口’怎么踏得进去？”
Even if he finds the Huang house, how will he get through the door?”

“这就要看他的本事了。
“That depends on his resourcefulness.

不去管他。
Never mind him for now.

我倒问你，阿巧姐怎么样？”
How is A’qiao?”


“她仍旧住在潘家，
“Still at the Pans'.

人胖了，自然是日子过得舒服。”
She's grown plumper, so she must be living comfortably.

周一鸣又说，“福山的事，也就是胡先生你来之前两三天才办好。
Fushan's business was settled only two or three days before you arrived.

如果你老不来，我已经带着福山回上海。
If you hadn't come, I'd already have taken him back to Shanghai.

现在是怎么样一个情形，请胡先生吩咐。”“唉！”胡雪岩摇摇头，
What are your instructions now?” Hu sighed and shook his head.

“事情一桩接一桩，好象捏了一把乱头发。
“One thing after another—it's like holding a handful of tangled hair.

你问的话，我现在无法告诉你，
I can't answer you yet.

你跟福山先住下来再说。
You and Fushan had better stay here for now.”

于是周一鸣到楼房去作安排，
Zhou went upstairs to arrange rooms.

胡雪岩一个人倚枕假寝，心里一桩一桩的事在想。
Left alone, Hu leaned against his pillow, resting without sleeping and considering his affairs one by one.

发觉自己犯了个极大的错误，因而想到一句话：“君子务本”。
He realized that he had made a grave mistake and thought of the saying, “A gentleman attends to the essentials.”

自己的根本，第一是钱庄，第二是丝。
His own essentials were banking first and silk second.

钱庄现成有潘叔雅的一笔钱在那里，
For the bank, Pan Shuya's money was ready to hand.

丝则湖州方面的新丝又将上市，
New silk would soon reach the Huzhou market.

今年是不是还做这生意？要做是怎么个做法？
Was he going to trade in it this year, and if so, how?

得要赶快拿定主意，通知陈世龙去办。
He must decide promptly and tell Chen Shilong what to do.

这样子专管闲事，耽误了正经，将来是个不了之局。
If he kept attending to incidental matters and neglected his proper business, he would end in an impossible position.

于是，他当机立断，作了个决定，
He therefore made a firm decision.

只等明天杨凤毛回来，看怎么说，
Tomorrow he would hear what Yang Fengmao had to say.

事情如果麻烦，只好照裘丰言的办法，把那批洋枪丢在上海再说，自己赶紧陪着七姑奶奶回浙江去干正经，
If the business proved troublesome, he would follow Qiu's suggestion and leave the guns in Shanghai for the time being, then hurry back to Zhejiang with the Seventh Lady and attend to real work.

闲事能管则管，不能管的只好丢下再说。
He would deal with other matters if he could; if not, they must wait.

想停当了，便又另有一番筹划，将能管的闲事，派定了人去管，
Having settled that, he made further arrangements, assigning manageable tasks to others.

第一个是刘不才，可以管潘家的事，第二个是周一鸣，可以管何桂清跟阿巧姐的事。
Liu could look after the Pan family's business; Zhou could handle He Guiqing and A’qiao.

多少天来积压在心头的沉重之感，就由于这样一转念间，大见轻松，
The mere change of outlook lifted much of the weight that had burdened him for days.

当然，刘不才和周一鸣去代他管那两件闲事，决不会做得比自己好，似乎有些不能放心。
Neither man would do his task as well as Hu himself, of course, and it was hard not to worry.

但是他实在疲倦了，管不得那许多了。
But he was exhausted and could no longer attend to everything.

心一横，想起不知哪里看来的两句诗，脱口念了出来：“闭门推出窗前月，吩咐梅花自主张！”
Resolving to let go, he recalled two lines of verse seen somewhere and recited them aloud: “Shut the door and push the moon beyond the window; leave the plum blossoms to decide things for themselves.”


然而三件闲事毕竟有一件不能不管，
Of the three incidental matters, however, one could not be neglected.

心思集中，顾虑便能周详，心里在想：
With his thoughts concentrated, he could consider it more thoroughly.

何必路远迢迢先回杭州，再转湖州？
Why travel all the way back to Hangzhou and then turn towards Huzhou?

由苏州到湖州，现成的一条运河，
There was a canal from Suzhou to Huzhou.

算起位置来，苏州在太湖之东，湖州在太湖之南，应该是条捷径。
Suzhou lay east of Lake Tai and Huzhou south of it; that ought to be the shorter route.

“老周，”胡雪岩向他请教，“苏州到湖州的水路怎么走法？”
“Old Zhou, how does the water route from Suzhou to Huzhou run?”

“胡先生是问运河？”
“Do you mean the canal?

周一鸣答说，“这条路我走过，
I've travelled it.

由苏州到吴江叫北塘河，吴江到平望这一段叫官塘河，
From Suzhou to Wujiang it's called the Beitang River; from Wujiang to Pingwang, the Guantang River.

到了平望分两支，
At Pingwang it divides.

一支往南到嘉兴叫南塘河，
The southern branch to Jiaxing is the Nantang River.

往西经南浔到湖州，就是西塘河。
The western branch, through Nanxun to Huzhou, is the Xitang River.

一共一百二十里路。”
A hundred and twenty li altogether.”

于是胡雪岩打定了主意，
Hu made up his mind.

剪烛磨墨，亲笔写好一封信，
He trimmed the candle, ground ink and wrote a letter himself.

封缄完毕，福山也就回来了。
Just as he finished sealing it, Fushan returned.


“黄银宝住在下塘水潭头。”福山回报：
“Huang Yinbao lives at Shuitantou in Xiatang,” Fushan reported.

“刘老爷、裘老爷都在那里，
“Mr Liu and Mr Qiu are both there.

刘先生在推牌九。”
Mr Liu is banking at pai gow.”

“推牌九？”
“Pai gow?”

胡雪岩诧异，
Hu was surprised.

“跟哪些人在赌？”
“With whom?”

“ 都是那里的人，娘姨、小大姐，拥了一屋子。”
“The people of the house—older maids and young maids, a whole roomful.

福山又说，“只有裘老爷一个人在吃酒。”胡雪岩笑了：
Only Mr Qiu is drinking.” Hu laughed.

“一个酒鬼，一个赌鬼，
“One a drunkard, the other a gambler.

到哪里都一样。”
They're the same wherever they go.”

“福山，”周一鸣问，“你是不是亲眼看见的？
“Did you see them yourself?” Zhou asked.

怎么晓得是他们两位？”
“How did you know who they were?”

福山脸一红，
Fushan blushed.

“那里有个‘相帮’，我认识，”他说，
“I know an attendant there.

“是我们木渎人，
He's from Mudu too.

我托他领我进去看的。”
I asked him to take me in and show me.”

这就见得胡雪岩说他“在迷魂阵里闯过一阵”的话，有点道理了。
This lent some support to Hu's suggestion that the youth had already ventured into the web of enchantment.

周一鸣笑笑不响。胡雪岩却对福山夸奖了两句。
Zhou smiled and said nothing, while Hu praised him.

“你倒蛮能干，
“Very capable.

在外面自己会想办法，
You can find your own way when you're out on an errand.
