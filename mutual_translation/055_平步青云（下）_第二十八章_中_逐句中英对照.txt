胡雪岩
《平步青云（下）》第二十八章（中）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume III — Chapter Twenty-Eight, Part Two
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


消息传播得真快，
News travelled remarkably fast.

第二天一早，俞武成从青浦回同里，中途在一处村镇歇脚吃茶，便有人向他打听胡雪岩和刘不才。
Early the next morning, as Yu Wucheng returned from Qingpu to Tongli, he stopped for tea in a village along the way and was already being asked about Hu Xueyan and Liu Bucai.

因此，在朱老大家的水阁初见面，他向胡雪岩说的第一句话就是：“老兄一到，名气就响。
Thus, when he first met Hu in Eldest Brother Zhu’s waterside pavilion, his opening words were, “You have only just arrived, brother, and already your name is on everyone’s lips.

我们在江湖上混了几十年的，真要甘拜下风了！”
Those of us who have spent decades in the brotherhoods must acknowledge ourselves outdone!”

这话不是句好话，胡雪岩自然听得出来，
There was an unpleasant edge to this, which Hu naturally detected.

只好这样答道：“我们是仰仗大哥的声光。
He could only reply, “We owe it all to your reputation, Brother Yu.

这种毫无道理的风头，不出为妙，
Such pointless attention is better avoided.

所以今天步门不敢出，专诚等候大哥，一切听大哥的吩咐。”
That is why I have not ventured outside today, but have waited here expressly for you, ready to follow your instructions in everything.”

宾主之间，一见面便有些格格不入的模样，
Their first encounter had scarcely begun before host and guest seemed at odds.

杨凤毛大为不安，赶紧将俞武成的袖子一拉：
Alarmed, Yang Fengmao tugged at Yu’s sleeve.

“师父！”他轻声说道：“你老请到这面来！”
“Master,” he said softly, “please come this way.”

将俞武成拉到一边，杨凤毛将三婆婆如何看重这门干亲，一一细陈，
Drawing him aside, Yang explained in detail how highly Third Granny valued this new bond of adopted kinship.

最后极郑重地说：“临走之前，三婆婆特为拿我喊到一边，叫我告诉师父：这位胡大叔是极能干、极讲义气的人。
Finally, with particular gravity, he said, “Before I left, Third Granny called me aside especially to give you this message: Uncle Hu is an exceptionally capable and loyal man.

她老人家说：几十年工夫当中，看过的也不少，狠的有，忠厚的也有，象胡大叔这样又狠又忠厚的人，还是第一趟见……”
She says that in all her decades of experience she has seen plenty of ruthless men and plenty of kindly, honest ones, but this is the first time she has met a man who is both ruthless and kindly.”


“什么？”俞武成说，
“What?” Yu asked.

“我倒不懂她老人家的话，
“I do not understand her.

怎么叫又狠又忠厚？”
How can a man be both ruthless and kindly?”

“忠厚是说他的本性，狠是说他办事的手段。”杨凤毛又说：
“Kindly in his nature, ruthless in his methods.

“我倒觉得三婆婆的眼光到底厉害，
I must say Third Granny’s judgment is extraordinary.

这‘又狠又忠厚’五个字，别人说不出。”
No one else could have found those words.”

“那么，你说对不对呢？”
“And do you think she is right?”

“自然说得对！”杨凤毛接下来又转述“慈训”：
“Of course she is!” Yang continued to relay the maternal instructions.

“三婆婆说，我们在这里，寄人篱下，受人的气，也不是办法。
“Third Granny says it is no way to go on, living here dependent on others and having to swallow their slights.

想要打开局面，都在胡大叔身上。
Our chance to improve matters rests entirely with Uncle Hu.

师父要格外尊敬他！
You must treat him with particular respect.”

““昨天章老板赌场里又是怎么回事？”
“And what happened at Boss Zhang’s gambling house yesterday?”

“ ”这件事，“杨凤毛的神色显得很兴奋，”师父也有面子！
“That,” Yang replied with an excited expression, “brought you credit too, Master!”

“接着，他将当时的情形，细说了一遍。
He recounted the whole affair.

“这倒难得！
“That is unusual.

说他忠厚不错。”俞武成又说，
She was right to call him kindly,” Yu said.

“那姓刘的，看起来也是‘老白相’，居然对他服服帖帖，
“That fellow Liu looks like an old hand at the pleasures of life, yet he obeys Hu meekly enough.

这就看得出来，有点本事的。”
You can see from that that Hu has some ability.”

“本事不止一点点。
“More than a little.

师父，你老跟他一谈就知道了。”
Once you talk to him, Master, you will see for yourself.”


于是俞武成再跟胡雪岩交谈时，态度就大不相同了，
When Yu resumed his conversation with Hu, his manner was quite different.

他很客气，一定要让胡雪岩和刘不才“升炕”，
He was exceedingly courteous and insisted that Hu and Liu take the places of honour on the raised couch.

而叙起礼节来，刘不才是芙蓉的叔叔，长了一辈，所以称谓亦自各别，
In reckoning their relationships, Liu, as Furong’s uncle, belonged to the senior generation, so their forms of address differed accordingly.

俞武成叫胡雪岩“老胡”，叫刘不才则是官称“刘三爷”，刘三爷却又尊称他“俞老”，跟胡雪岩所叫的“大哥”一比，仿佛又矮了一辈。
Yu called Hu “Old Hu,” but addressed Liu formally as “Third Master Liu.” Liu in turn respectfully called him “Elder Yu,” which, compared with Hu’s “Brother Yu,” seemed to put Liu a generation below him again.

反正江湖上各叙各的，
In the brotherhoods everyone reckoned such relationships separately.

称呼虽乱，其实都是一律平等的朋友。
The titles might be confused, but in practice they were all friends on an equal footing.

俞武成的门规甚严，
Yu enforced strict rules among his disciples.

杨凤毛、朱老大都是站着服劳，他自己则坐在水阁临窗的一张太师椅上相陪，跟胡雪岩大谈松江漕帮。
Yang and Zhu remained standing to attend to their needs, while he sat in a grand armchair beside the pavilion window, talking at length with Hu about the Songjiang grain-transport brotherhood.

他称“老太爷”为“松江老大”，说起许多他们年轻时一起闯荡江湖的故事，感叹着日子不如从前好过。
He referred to the Old Master as the “Songjiang Chief” and told many stories of their adventures together in their youth, lamenting that life was harder now than it had been.

刘不才在这场合，只有静听的份儿。
Liu could only sit and listen.

一面听，一面打量俞武成，
As he did so, he studied Yu.

年纪六十开外，打扮得却如纨袴子弟，缎鞋、缎袍、雪白的袖头，不时卷上翻下，
Although over sixty, the man dressed like a wealthy young idler, in satin shoes and a satin robe, with snow-white cuffs that he kept turning up and down.

等袖子翻下来时，已经盖过手面，所以必得翘起一只大拇指来，将袖口挡住，才便于行动，
When lowered, the cuffs covered his hands, so he had to raise a thumb to hold the cuff back before he could move freely.

这原是江湖上人特有的一种姿态，只是俞武成身材魁梧，服装华丽，
This was a characteristic gesture among men of the brotherhoods, but Yu’s imposing build and splendid clothes gave it a particular flourish.

大拇指一翘起来，那只通体碧绿的“玻璃翠”扳指，异常耀眼，所以格外显得有派头。
Whenever his thumb rose, the ring of glass-clear green jade upon it flashed brilliantly, adding still further to his air of distinction.


然而刘不才感觉兴趣，也感到困惑的是，俞武成那件在斜阳里闪闪发光的缎袍，无风自动，
What both fascinated and puzzled Liu, however, was the way Yu’s satin robe, glittering in the slanting sunlight, moved without any breeze.

不时东面凸起一块，西面蠕动片刻，
A bulge would appear here, something would wriggle there.

不知是何缘故！
What could cause it?

目不转睛地看了半天，总想不透，心便痒得厉害，
He watched intently for a long time without solving the mystery, until his curiosity became almost unbearable.

正忍不住要动问时，谜底揭晓了。
Just as he was about to ask, the answer appeared.

朱老大捧了一大冰盘出于太湖中洞庭东山的樱桃来款客，
Zhu brought in a large platter of cherries from Dongting East Mountain in Lake Tai.

但见俞武成抓了一串在手里，平伸手掌，
Yu took a cluster and held out his open palm.

很快地，袖子里钻出一只毛茸茸的小松鼠来，
A furry little squirrel promptly emerged from his sleeve.

一对极大、极明亮的眼睛，灵活地转了转，然后拱起两只前爪，就俞武成掌中捧着樱桃咬。
Its large, bright eyes darted about; then it raised its forepaws, grasped a cherry in Yu’s palm, and began nibbling.

刘不才嘻开了嘴笑，
Liu broke into a broad smile.

“俞老，你真会玩！”他问：
“Elder Yu, you certainly know how to amuse yourself!

“怎么养只松鼠在身上？
Why keep a squirrel on your person?

不觉得累赘？”
Is it not a nuisance?”

“养熟了就好了。”
“Once it is tame, there is no trouble.”

“整天在身上？”
“You keep it there all day?”

“嗯！”
“Yes.”

俞武成点点头，
Yu nodded.

“几乎片刻不离。”
“We hardly ever part.”

“一天到晚，在你身上爬来爬去，不嫌烦吗？”
“Does it not bother you, crawling all over you from morning to night?”

“自然也有睡觉的时候；
“It sleeps sometimes, naturally.

只要拿它一放到口袋里，它就不闹了。”俞武成又说：
Put it in my pocket and it settles down.

“刘三爷喜欢，拿了去玩！”
If you like it, Third Master Liu, take it and amuse yourself.”

“不，不！”
“No, no!”

刘不才播着手说：
Liu waved his hands.

“君子不夺人所好。
“A gentleman does not deprive another man of something he loves.

而且，说实话，在我身上爬来爬去，也嫌肉麻！”
Besides, to tell the truth, I should find it rather creepy having it crawl all over me.”


俞武成笑笑不响，回头问朱老大：
Yu smiled without answering, then turned to Zhu.

“快开饭了吧？”
“Will the meal be ready soon?”

“听胡大叔跟师父的意思。”朱老大答道，
“Whenever you and Uncle Hu wish, Master.

“如果不怎么饿，不妨稍等一等，
If you are not particularly hungry, it would be better to wait a little.

火腿煨鱼翅，火功还不大够。”
The shark’s fin stewed with ham needs more time over the fire.”

“那就等一下。
“Then let us wait.

先弄些点心来给胡大叔点饥，
Bring Uncle Hu some refreshments to take the edge off his hunger.

等我们谈好了正事，痛痛快快吃酒。”
Once we have discussed our business, we can enjoy our wine properly.”

这段话中要紧的是“谈正事”这一句，
The significant words were “discussed our business.”

胡雪岩怕他不愿刘不才与闻机密，便不经意地使个眼色，
Hu feared that Yu might not wish Liu to hear their secrets, and gave him a casual-looking glance.

刘不才会意，站起身来说：
Liu understood and rose.

“你们谈吧！
“You talk.

我趁这会儿工夫，上街去看个朋友。
I shall use the time to go into town and see a friend.”

““那么，”朱老大自告奋勇，“我陪着刘三爷一起去。”
“Then I shall accompany you,” Zhu volunteered.

刘不才是想去看周一鸣，这是暗中埋伏的援兵，
Liu intended to see Zhou Yiming, their reserve force concealed from view.

不便让俞武成这方面的人知道，
He could hardly let Yu’s people know about him.

所以拱拱手说：“不敢，不敢！
Clasping his hands, he said, “Please do not trouble yourself.

你做主人，要留在府上，
You are our host and must stay here.

而且，同里我也熟，绝不致迷路。”
Besides, I know Tongli well; there is no danger of my getting lost.”

这是假话，他也是第一次到同里，
This was untrue: it was his first visit to Tongli too.

只是不如此说，朱老大还会派上引路。
Unless he said so, however, Zhu would send someone to guide him.

果然，做主人的不再客气，放他一个人走了。
Sure enough, his host pressed the matter no further and let him go alone.


于是，俞武成跟胡雪岩，还有杨凤毛在一起密认。
Yu, Hu, and Yang then conferred in private.

俞武成表示愿意听从胡雪岩的安排，老实相告，
Yu said that he was willing to follow Hu’s arrangements and explained the position frankly.

原来准备动那船洋枪的人马，都由周立春手下一个得力的头目“跷脚长根”安排。
The men who had been preparing to seize the boatload of foreign guns were all under the direction of Lame Changgen, one of Zhou Lichun’s capable subordinate leaders.

所要借重俞武成的，是因为这条水路，是松江漕帮的势力范围，必须请他出面，来打通“松江老大”的路子。
They needed Yu because this water route lay within the Songjiang brotherhood’s sphere of influence: he had to approach the Songjiang Chief and clear the way.

现在松江方面，由于守着“两方面都是朋友，只好袖手中立”的立场，
Now Songjiang had adopted the position that, since both sides were friends, it could only stand aside and remain neutral.

所以“跷脚长根”也踌躇着不敢下手。
Changgen therefore hesitated to act.

如今得有这样一条出路，深符所愿，但条件如何？必得跟胡雪岩谈一谈。
The proposed way out suited him very well, but he would have to discuss the terms with Hu.

“那当然。”胡雪岩问道，
“Naturally,” Hu said.

“怎么样跟这位朋友碰头？”
“How can I meet this friend?”

“那还得再联络。
“That still has to be arranged.

老胡，我是直心直肚肠，”
Old Hu, I am a plain-spoken man.”

俞武成很郑重地说：
Yu’s tone became solemn.

“有句话我想先请教你，
“There is something I want to ask you first.

你是一家人了，而且我老娘的眼光是不会错的，我当然相信。
You are family now, and my mother’s judgment is never wrong, so of course I trust you.

不过，那批做官的，我吃过他们的苦头，实在不大相信。
But I have suffered at the hands of those officials, and cannot put much faith in them.

当初我儿子要去考武举，我就跟他说：”做官也没啥意思，
When my son first wanted to take the provincial military examination, I told him, ‘There is nothing so wonderful about being an official.

不要去考。
Do not bother with it.’

‘也是我老娘’望孙成龙‘亲自料理，亲自送考。
But my mother wanted her grandson to make a name for himself; she arranged everything and escorted him to the examination herself.

至于招抚这一节，我是无所谓的，
As for this amnesty, it makes no difference to me personally.

办成功了，帮里弟兄，可以去吃一份粮，也算是糊口，
If it succeeds, the men can draw military pay and at least feed themselves.

再说，拿他们拉过来，也总算是替朝廷出了力。
Bringing them over would also be a service to the court.

就怕那批做官的老爷，口是心非，
What I fear is that those grand officials will say one thing and mean another.

等出了毛病，我怪你也无用，
If something goes wrong, blaming you will be no use.

那时候，我就不是在江湖上好混不好混的事了！“
And for me it will then be more than a question of whether I can still get along in the brotherhoods!”


听他这夹枪带棒一大顿，胡雪岩相当困惑，
This long speech, with its barbed insinuations, left Hu rather puzzled.

不知他说的什么？
What exactly did Yu mean?

只是抓住“出了毛病”这四个字极力思考，慢慢悟出道理来了。
He seized on the phrase “something goes wrong” and thought hard until the meaning gradually became clear.

“你是说，人过去以后，当官儿的，翻脸不认人，是不是？”
“You mean that once the men have gone over, the officials might turn on them and disown their promises?”

“对了！”俞武成说，
“Exactly!

“光是翻脸不认人，还好办，
If they merely turned their backs on them, we could still manage.

就怕……”他摇摇头，
What I fear is—” Yu shook his head.

“真的有那么一下子，那就惨了。”
“If they really did that, it would be terrible.”

“你是说……”胡雪岩很吃力地问：“会‘杀降’？”
“Do you mean”—Hu found the question difficult to utter—“that they might kill the men who surrender?”

“保不定的。”
“There is no telling.”

“不会！”
“They will not!”

这时候胡雪岩才用斩钉截铁的声音：
Only now did Hu speak with absolute firmness.

“我包你不会，
“I guarantee they will not.

大哥，我跟你实说吧，我接头的是何学使的路子，
Brother, let me tell you plainly: my contact is Education Commissioner He.

他马上要放好缺了。
He is about to receive an excellent appointment.

京里大军机是他们同年，各省巡抚也有许多是他同年。
The leading grand councillor in the capital graduated in the same examination year as he did, and so did many provincial governors.

这一榜红得很，说出话来有分量的。”
His year is very much in favour, and their words carry weight.”

“那么，何学使跟你的交情呢？”
“And how close are you to Commissioner He?”

“何学使托我替他置妾。
“He has asked me to find him a concubine.

交情如此而已！”
That is all there is to our friendship!”

“那就没话说了。”
“Then there is nothing more to say.”

俞武成欣然问道，
Yu looked pleased.

“何学使可曾谈起，给点啥好处？”他赶紧又补了一句，“不是说我。
“Did he mention what advantages he could offer?” He hurriedly added, “I do not mean for me.

是说对跷脚长根他们。”
I mean for Changgen and his men.”


“提到这一层，就我不说，大哥也想象得到：
“On that point, brother, you can imagine the answer without my telling you.

弃暗投明，朝廷自然有一番奖励，
If they abandon darkness for the light, the court will naturally reward them.

官是一定有得做的。”
Official appointments will certainly be available.”

接下来，胡雪岩便根据何桂清的指示说道：
Hu went on, following He Guiqing’s instructions.

“弟兄们总可以关一个月恩饷，作为犒赏。
“The men should at least receive a special gratuity equal to a month’s pay.

以后看拔到哪里，归哪里的粮台发饷。
After that, their pay will come from the military finance office wherever they are assigned.

本来，一个月的恩饷好象少了点，不过也实在叫没法子，
A single month’s gratuity may seem a little meagre, but there is really no help for it.

地方失得太多，钱粮少收不少，
So much territory has been lost that tax receipts have fallen greatly.

这些情形，大哥你当然清楚。”
You know the circumstances as well as anyone.”

俞武成当然清楚，他自己和这一帮无事可做，便是朝廷岁入减少的明证，
Yu certainly did: the fact that he and his followers had nothing to do was itself evidence of the decline in imperial revenue.

所以点点头表示领会，
He nodded.

“恩晌不恩饷，倒不在话下，
“The gratuity is not the main thing.

照跷脚长根的意思，将来投过去，变成官兵，驻扎的地方要随他挑，
Changgen wants to choose where his men are stationed after they become government troops.

说老实话，也就是仍；日想驻扎在这一带。
To be frank, he wants to remain in this district.

这一点，”俞武成很难出口似地，“总要把它做到！”
That is something”—he seemed to find it difficult to say—“we really must arrange.”

胡雪岩对这方面虽不在行，但照情理而论，觉得不容易做到，
Hu was no expert in such matters, but common sense told him this would be difficult.

他略想一想问道：“那么我倒请问大哥，如果叫他去打小刀会，他肯不肯？”
After a moment’s thought he asked, “Then let me ask you, brother: if he were ordered to fight the Small Swords Society, would he agree?”

“还不肯的。
“No.

原来是一条跳板上的人，
They have all stood on the same gangplank.

怎么好意思？”
How could he bring himself to do it?”

“这样子就难了！”胡雪岩说，
“Then we have a difficulty.

“这一带驻了兵，都是要打小刀会的。
The troops stationed here are all intended to fight the Small Swords Society.

军情紧急，一道命令下来，就要开拔，
If the military situation became urgent, a single order would require them to march.

如果不肯出队，就是不服调度。
Refusing to go would be disobedience.

大哥，你想想看，你做了长官，
Imagine you were their commanding officer.

会怎么样处置？
How would you deal with it?”

““我倒没有想到这一层……”
“I had not thought of that.”

俞武成搔搔头皮，显得很为难似的。
Yu scratched his head, looking deeply troubled.


胡雪岩看得出来，俞武成大概已拍了胸脯，满口应承，必可做到，所以才有此着急的神情。
Hu could see that Yu had probably thumped his chest and promised without reservation that this could be arranged; hence his present anxiety.

正在替他伤脑筋时，杨凤毛已先开了口。
While Hu was still puzzling over a way to help him, Yang spoke.

“师父只有这样回复他，还是调得远些的好，
“Master, you must tell him it would be better to accept a posting farther away.

本乡本土，如果小刀会不体谅他的处境，
If he remains in his home district, the Small Swords men may fail to understand his position.

或者事急相投，
Or they may come to him for refuge in an emergency.

拒而不纳，就伤了感情，要帮忙呢，窝藏叛逆的罪名，非同小可。
If he turns them away, he will hurt their feelings; if he helps them, harbouring rebels is no trifling charge.

何不远离了左右为难的窘境？”
Why not move away and escape that dilemma?”

“这话说得透彻。”胡雪岩趁机劝道：
