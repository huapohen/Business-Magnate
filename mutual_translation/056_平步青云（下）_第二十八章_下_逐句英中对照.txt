胡雪岩
《平步青云（下）》第二十八章（下）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume III — Chapter Twenty-Eight, Part Three
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


Liu had guessed exactly right: this was precisely what Hu Xueyan wanted.
他的测度，丝毫不差，胡雪岩正是这样希望。

Hu now understood how much real influence Yu Wucheng possessed, what abilities he had, and how he thought and behaved.
他对俞武成有多少实力，肚子里有些什么货，以及他的想法和脾气。尽皆了然，

He felt that a discussion with Yang Fengmao would be more useful than one with Yu.
觉得跟他谈，不如跟杨凤毛谈，来得有用。

There was, of course, another indispensable participant: Zhou Yiming.
当然，还有个少不得的人：周一鸣。

The three sat close together in the waterside pavilion to work out a plan.
三个人是在水阁中促膝画策。

Hu began by explaining his position.
胡雪岩首先表明了态度，

His purpose had changed.
他的目的，已经有所更改，

Getting that boatload of foreign rifles to Hangzhou was now a secondary concern; his chief aim was to help Yu Wucheng regain his standing and thus prove worthy of Third Granny’s regard.
那一船洋枪如何运到杭州，犹在其次，主要的是想帮俞武成翻身，也不枉三婆婆一番器重的情意。

In the brotherhood, such a gesture counted for everything.
江湖上就讲这一点“意思”。

Yang’s feelings toward Hu had changed again and again, from indifference to considerable admiration; now they had become profound gratitude.
杨凤毛对胡雪岩的态度，一变再变，由不甚在意，到相当佩服，而此刻是十分感激了，

“Uncle Hu,” he said frankly, “my master may not yet understand what is in your heart, but I understand it completely.
“胡大叔，”他说了句很坦率的话：“你老的心，我师父或许还不明白，我是完全晓得的。

Give us your instructions, and whatever we can do, we shall do with all our strength.
只要胡大叔吩咐，我们做得到的，一定出全力去做。

Since this is what you have in mind, may I ask how you think my master can recover his position?”
现在胡大叔是这样的用心，我倒想请问一句，照胡大叔看，我师父要怎么样才能翻身？”

“On two fronts: with the authorities and within the brotherhood,” Hu answered at once.
“官私两面。”胡雪岩很快地回答：

“As far as the authorities are concerned, successfully arranging this surrender would naturally be best.
“官的，譬如说能够办好这一次招抚，自然最好，

Failing that, there must be action to put the men down by force.
不然，就要有杀搏的做法，

That, too, would be a great service.”
也是大功一件。”

Yang understood.
杨凤毛领会得他的意思，

His heart began to pound, but he was not yet ready to commit himself.
一颗心怦怦然，相当紧张，但还不便表示态度，

He watched Hu intently, waiting for him to continue.
只眼神专注着，等他再说下去。

“Within the brotherhood, we must make your master’s name ring out again!”
“私的，在江猢上要把你师父的名气，重新打它响来！”

“Exactly.”
“是的。”

On this point Yang agreed wholeheartedly.
对这一点，杨凤毛深有同感，

“That is what I have always wanted.
“我也一直这样子在想。

But we need an opportunity.
不过，也要有机会，

If only he could accomplish one or two things that would really impress people!”
能够有机会干一两件漂亮的事就好了。”


“The opportunity is right before us.
“眼前就是个机会。

But let us put that aside for a moment.
这且摆下来再说。

An idea has just occurred to me; see what you think of it.
我现在想到一个主意，说出来你看看，行不行？“胡雪岩说：”

There is a saying: ‘Repair the plank road in plain sight, but secretly cross at Chencang.’
有句话叫做‘明修栈道，暗渡陈仓’，

Lame Changgen’s whole attention is fixed on your master and me.
现在跷脚长根全副精神，都在你师父跟我身上，

With both eyes on Tongli, he is neglecting everything else.
一双眼睛，只顾看着同里，别的方面就疏忽了。

I want to take advantage of that opening to get the arms shipment out of Shanghai at once.
我想趁这个空档，将上海的那船军火，赶紧起运。

Fortunately, we have people in Songjiang to look after it.
好在松江那方面有照应，

Nothing should go wrong.”
一定不会出毛病。

“Yes, yes!”
““嗯，嗯！”

Yang nodded repeatedly.
杨凤毛连连点头，

“That is a risk worth taking.”
“这个险值得冒。”

“But there is another part to it.
“不过也有个做法，

I should like Shaowu to escort the shipment.
我想请少武押运。

Of course,” Hu added quickly, “if anything does go wrong, he will certainly not be held responsible.
当然，”胡雪岩紧接着说：“万一出了毛病，决不要他负责任。

My idea is that once the arms reach Hangzhou, this service will allow us to put his name near the top when recommendations for rewards are submitted to the throne.
我的意思是，有这样一趟‘劳绩’，等军火到了杭州，奏保议叙，就可以拿他的名字摆在前面，

It should do him some good, and it will give us something to show Third Granny.”
多少有点好处，对三婆婆也是个交代。”

“Excellent.
“好的。

If you have chosen him, Uncle Hu, what more is there to say?
胡大叔挑他，那还有什么话说？

I shall go back to Suzhou and tell him myself.”
等我回苏州去一趟，当面告诉他。”

“You need not go.
“不必你去，

I shall arrange it.”
我会安排。”


They next discussed how to deal with Changgen.
接下来便是商量如何对付跷脚长根。

Hu and Yang agreed that everything depended on evidence.
胡雪岩与杨凤毛的看法相同，整个关键，就在证据！

Once they had proof, any course would be open to them.
有了证据，怎么样都好办，

They could call in government troops to surround and suppress his men with full justification; or, on a smaller scale, they could convene an incense-hall tribunal under their own brotherhood’s rules.
大则动用官兵围剿，是师出有名，小则照他们帮里“家门”的规矩，“开香堂”问罪，

Confronted with the evidence, he could have no defense and would have to submit to their punishment, even the sentence of “three knife thrusts and six holes.”
亦可问得他俯首无辞，三刀六洞，任凭处置。

“At present all we have is this report.
“现在只有这样的消息，

There are no letters or written documents, and no one will come forward to testify.
既无书信字迹，也没有人肯挺身指证，

What can we do to him?
这就莫亲其何？

Naturally, I could find a way to have him brought before a magistrate and interrogated under torture.
当然，我也可以想法子拿他抓到公堂上，严刑拷问，

The resentment that would earn me is a minor matter.
不过这一来，我结了怨还在其次，

What matters is the damage to your leader’s reputation: people would say he had used official power to harm another man.
损了你们老头子的威名，说他仗势损人，

I am sure he would never accept that name.”
这个名声，我想他也决不肯背的。”

“Certainly not, certainly not,” Yang said repeatedly.
“ 当然，当然。”杨凤毛一叠连声地说，

“Once a man acquires that reputation, he can scarcely hold his ground in the brotherhood.”
“一落这个名声，在江湖上就难混了。”

“So unless we abandon the matter and keep clear of his trap, there is only one course left: ‘How can you catch the tiger’s cub without entering the tiger’s den?’”
“所以，除非罢手，不上他的圈套，不然就只有一条路子，叫做‘不入虎穴，焉得虎子’！”

“I have thought of that too, but it seemed too dangerous.”
“我也想到过，觉得太危险！”

“Provided the rescue is properly coordinated, there is no danger.
“只要接应得好，决不要紧。

Here is what I propose...”
我想这样子做法……”


Hu’s plan was to accompany Yu Wucheng to this Banquet at Hongmen, prepared for the negotiations to break down and for both of them to be kidnapped.
胡雪岩的做法是跟俞武成去赴这一场“鸿门宴”，准备谈判决裂，准备被绑架，

When their captors’ boat reached a checkpoint, men would board unexpectedly on the pretext of an inspection and rescue them.
等船到关卡，借稽查为名，出其不意，上船相救，

Changgen’s treachery would then be established.
那时候就证实了跷脚长根的不逞之心，

Whether to settle the affair through the authorities or within the brotherhood could be decided afterward.
是官了还是私了，到时候再说。

Yang listened closely, working through every stage, and found Hu’s plan more thorough than his own.
杨凤毛极注意地听着，从头到底，细作盘算，认为他的计划，比自己的打算来得周密，

Their ideas for the first part were the same; the difference lay in the escape.
前面的一段经过相同，不同的是脱险的方法，

Yang had intended to station men in ambush and stage a rescue like the opera Seizing Adou by the River.
杨凤毛预备邀人埋伏，唱一出“临江夺斗”，

Hu would use official authority as cover, “besieging Zhao to save Yan.”
胡雪岩是动用官方的力量作掩护，围赵救燕。

One meant a rescue by force, the other by strategy; the latter was plainly superior.
一个力夺，一个智取，自然后者比前者高明。

“Uncle Hu, I have complete confidence in your ability to meet whatever happens.
“胡大叔，你老随机应变的功夫，我是信得过的，

My only fear is that my master’s violent temper may provoke Changgen into a rage.
就怕我师父脾气暴躁，搞得跷脚长根恼羞成怒。

Apart from that, with the rescue properly arranged, we cannot fail.”
除此以外，只要接应得好，不会不成功。”

“Success depends on who knows what,” Hu replied.
“成败的关键在明暗之间。”胡雪岩说：

“Changgen thinks he is hidden while we stand in the open.
“跷脚长根以为他在暗处，我们在明处，

In fact, it is he who is exposed and we who are hidden.
其实他明我暗。

If word gets out, we shall be the ones exposed again.”
如果消息泄漏出去，就又变成我们在明处了。”

“Yes,” Yang said solemnly.
“是的。”杨凤毛郑重地答道：

“I suggest that only you, Mr Zhou, and I know of this.
“我想，这件事就胡大叔、周先生跟我三个人知道。

Once the arrangements are complete, we can tell my master.”
等筹划好了，再告诉我师父。”

“Exactly.”
“一点不错。”

They gave nothing away.
于是彼此不动声色，

After the meal, Liu Bucai again kept Yu company at the gaming table while the three resumed their discussion.
吃罢了饭，仍旧由刘不才陪着俞武成赌钱，他们三个人接续未完的话题，

When every detail had been settled, they went about their separate tasks.
将一切细节，都筹划到了，然后分头行事。


The first task, naturally, was to inform Yu Wucheng.
首先当然是要告诉俞武成。

He objected to parts of the plan.
对于整个计划，他有不以为然的地方，

Having his own son escort the rifles, for instance, might allow people to say afterward that he had been the first to betray Changgen.
譬如由他儿子去押运那一船洋枪，俞武成就觉得将来说出去，是他先背弃了跷脚长根，

That would sound very bad.
名声不好听。

But he had always relied on others to accomplish things.
但他一向倚人成事，

Yang was his most capable disciple, while Hu had shown himself superior at every turn to anyone on Yu’s side.
杨凤毛是他最得力的学生，胡雪岩又处处显得比自己这面高明，

Their newly established family connection made it still harder to object.
加以有那一层干亲在，越发不便多说什么。

He therefore agreed with an expansive air.
所以慨然答应：

“I leave it all to you.
“都随你们，

Whatever you say, I shall do!”
你们怎么说，我怎么做！”

“There is one point on which I need your decision, Brother Yu.
“有一层要请示大哥，

Once everything has been exposed, shall we settle it officially or privately?
等事情抖明了，是官了，还是私了？”胡雪岩说，

If it is to go through the authorities, I shall make the arrangements.
“官了，我来奔去，

If it is to be settled privately, that is a matter for your brotherhood, and I cannot interfere.”
私了，是你们家门里的事，我就不能过问了。”

Yu considered.
俞武成想了想说：

“Privately, I think.
“我想还是私了。

Bringing in the authorities would not be a good thing.”
惊官动府也不大好。”

“Then we shall do as you wish.
“那都随大哥的意思，

I shall be with you throughout, in any case, ready to follow your instructions whenever anything arises.”
好在我跟大哥始终在一起，有事随时听招呼就是了。”


The words “with you throughout” sank deep into Yu’s mind.
“始终在一起”这五个字，俞武成深深印入脑中，

They gave him the sense that here was a man who would share his troubles and good fortune alike, and his feelings toward Hu changed accordingly.
不由得便有患难祸福相共的感觉，因而对胡雪岩的情分也就不同了。

He was generous by nature, with something of a spoiled young gentleman’s reckless habit of acting on impulse.
他是豪爽，加上些纨袴子弟想到就做的鲁莽性格，

“Fengmao,” he said at once, “tell your fellow disciples and all the younger men that from now on Uncle Hu’s word carries exactly the same weight as yours or mine.”
当时便说：“凤毛，你告诉你那些兄弟和‘小角色’，以后胡大叔说的话，就跟我同你说的一样。”

“Yes,” Yang answered with willing sincerity.
“是！”杨凤毛心悦诚服地答道：

“We would not dare treat Uncle Hu with anything less than respect.”
“我们不敢不敬胡大叔。”

“You are too kind, far too kind!”
“不敢当，不敢当！”

Hu felt both pleased and abashed.
胡雪岩既得意，又惭愧，

“With such affection from you and your disciple, how can I ever repay you?”
“贤师弟如此厚爱，叫我不知何以为报？”

“My dear Hu, you have it the wrong way round...”
“老胡，你说反了……”

“Master!” Yang interrupted.
“师父！”杨凤毛打断他的话说：

“This is not the time for that.
“这不是谈这些话的时候。

Uncle Hu still has urgent business to attend to.
胡大叔还有正事要赶着办，

We can talk over supper tonight.”
晚上宵夜再谈吧！”


Hu knew that in the world of the brotherhood, the more seasoned a man was, the more readily he took offense or grew suspicious.
胡雪岩深知江湖上行事，越是光棍，越易多心，

Every point of etiquette had to be made clear.
过节上的话，要交代得清楚，

Zhou Yiming had appeared without warning, which already suggested that Hu was setting up a separate camp and acting on his own.
无端冒出个周一鸣来，已有些自张一帜，独行其是的味道，

To keep Qiu Fengyan hidden as well, another man working in the shadows, would be altogether improper.
再藏着个“黑人”裘丰言，再不成话，

He therefore took the opportunity to explain.
因而把握机关，作了说明。

“There is something I must make clear to you, Brother Yu.
“有件事，我要跟大哥回明白。

Zhou and I have another friend, Qiu Fengyan, the officer appointed to escort that shipment of rifles.
老周跟我还有个朋友，也就是那一船洋枪的押运委员裘丰言，

Both of them were worried about me and have now hurried to Tongli to help.
他们两位不放心我，现在都赶到同里，预备帮忙。

More hands make the work easier.
人多好做事，

Since we are deploying our people, we ought by rights to be together; but too many of us in one place might attract attention.
我们调兵遣将，原该在一起，不过，人一多，怕风声太大，

I should like your opinion: should we all lodge together or remain apart?”
我跟大哥请示，是大家住一起，还是分开来的好？”

Yu had no means of deciding which arrangement was better, but he could understand what he was being told.
是合是分，俞武成无从作判断，不过听话是听得懂的，

Since Hu was worried about attracting attention, his own preference was obvious.
胡雪岩既“怕风声太大”，则意向如何？不言可知。

“Apart would be better, apart would be better!” Yu replied without hesitation.
于是俞武成毫不迟疑地答道：“分开来的好，分开来的好！”

“Mr Qiu belongs to the class of officials eligible for subprefectural and county magistracies.
“那位裘大老爷是‘州县班子’，

Like Third Master Liu, he is excellent company.
跟刘三爷一样，极有趣的人，

It was through him that Third Granny came to take Mrs Hu as a sworn daughter.”
三婆婆认胡大婶，算是他引进。”

“Oh!” said Yu.
“喔！”俞武成说，

“Then I must show him some courtesy.
“那么，我该尽点道理，

I shall send an invitation tomorrow and ask him to dinner.”
明天下个帖子，请裘大老爷吃饭。”

“That is unnecessary.
“那就不必了。

Once the business is successfully concluded, we can celebrate properly.
等事情成功了，我们再好好热闹一下子。

If you would like to meet him, I can bring him here tonight.”
如果大哥想跟他见一面，我今晚上就把他带了来。”

“Excellent!
“那好极了！

I only hope he will forgive the informality.”
只怕简慢不恭。”


With that settled, Hu went with Zhou to see Qiu.
这样说定了，胡雪岩便由周一鸣陪着去看裘丰言。

At the inn they found him holding a volume of Huang Zhongze’s Complete Works of Liangdang Studio, reciting poetry with a tipsy, misty gaze.
他正在客栈里，捏着一卷黄仲则的《两当轩全集》，醉眼迷离地在吟哦。

At the sight of Hu he laughed.
一见胡雪岩便即笑道：

“My dear Hu, I truly admire you!
“老胡，我真服了你！

Come, come, let me offer you a cup first.”
来，来，先奉敬一杯。”

“Wait, wait.
“等等，等等，

I shall drink with you over supper later.
回头消夜，我再陪你吃。

For the moment, ‘the military situation is urgent.’
如今‘军情紧急’，

Put down your cup.”
你先把酒杯放下来。”

To deprive Qiu of his wine was certainly to spoil his pleasure.
夺去他的酒杯，自是件极扫兴的事，

But his admiration for Hu was genuine, and he accepted whatever Hu said.
但他是真的服胡雪岩，说什么是什么，

He joined him at another table to discuss the business in detail.
当时便陪着胡雪岩到另一张桌子坐下，细谈正事。

Hu explained the plan for the secret shipment and had Qiu write three letters on the spot.
胡雪岩将“暗渡陈仓”的计划说了一遍，当时便请他写了三封信，

One was to the Songjiang Chief, explaining the circumstances and requesting protection along the waterways.
一封是给松江老大，说明经过，请求在水路上照应，

Another, written in Qiu’s own name to Wang Youling, set out Yu Shaowu’s appointment to escort the rifles, providing a record on which a future recommendation for reward could be based.
一封是由裘丰言自己出面，写给王有龄，说明委任俞少武押运洋枪，作为将来叙功的根据，

The third introduced Zhou Yiming to He Guiqing, saying that Zhou had confidential matters of importance to report in person.
再一封是写给何桂清，介绍周一鸣晋谒，说有“机密要事”密陈。


When the letters were finished, Hu invited Qiu to the Zhu house for supper and a meeting with Yu Wucheng.
写完了信，胡雪岩邀他到朱家消夜，跟俞武成见面。

Qiu, the Wine Muddlehead, suddenly became exceedingly cautious.
“酒糊涂”的裘丰言，却忽然谨小慎微了，

Secrecy should come first, he argued, and there was no need for him to meet Yu.
认为做事以隐秘为上，而且他也没有跟俞武成见面的必要。

Hu insisted, however, that since the meeting had already been promised, a last-minute change might arouse Yu’s suspicions.
但胡雪岩认为说好了见面，临时变卦，怕俞武成多心，所以坚持原议。

Qiu therefore had to go.
这样便不得不有此一行。

The two exchanged expressions of admiration; wine made friendship easy, and Yu found him very congenial.
见了面互道仰慕，而且酒杯中容易交朋友，俞武成觉得此人颇为投机。

When they spoke of Shaowu’s escort assignment, Yu, though he disapproved of it, now felt obliged to commend his son earnestly to Qiu’s care.
谈到俞少武押运的差使，做父亲的虽不以为然，而此时竟不能不郑重拜托。

Supper lasted far into the night.
这顿消夜，直吃到深夜才罢，

Qiu and Zhou then took their leave together, returned to the inn for a short nap, and boarded boats hired in advance.
裘丰言和周一鸣双双告辞，回到客栈打个盹，上了预先雇定的船，

One went north to Suzhou, to see He Guiqing and notify Yu Shaowu that he should join the others in Shanghai.
一个往北到苏州去见何桂清，并通知俞少武到上海会齐，

The other went east, first to Songjiang to see the Old Master, then back to Shanghai to ship the rifles.
一个往东，先到松江见“老太爷”，然后回上海去运洋枪。


Arranging the rescue at the checkpoints would take some days, so they first employed the delaying tactic they had agreed upon.
由于关卡上的安排援救脱险，得有些日子来部署，所以依照预先的商议，先用一条缓兵之计，

Yu told Changgen that Hu insisted on entertaining him first as a mark of respect.
俞武成向跷脚长根说，胡雪岩为表敬意，坚持要先请他吃饭，

A visitor should always call on the local man, but the local man should first extend hospitality.
从来“行客拜坐客”，但坐客却须先尽地主之谊，

Yu therefore offered a compromise: he would give a small dinner at which the two sides could meet and talk.
因此俞武成提出折中办法，由他作东，先请双方小叙会面，

Once the terms had been settled, they could accept Changgen’s invitation.
等条件谈妥当了，再领跷脚长根的情。

This was perfectly reasonable.
这个说话，合情合理，

Changgen suspected no hidden purpose and felt that postponing his own plan for a few days would do no harm.
跷脚长很当然想不到其中别有作用，只觉得自己的计划，晚几天实行，也无所谓，

He readily agreed.
因而欣然应诺。
