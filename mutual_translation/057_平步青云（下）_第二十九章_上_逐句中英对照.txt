第二十九章｜CHAPTER TWENTY-NINE


这轻飘飘的一封八行，在胡雪岩感觉中，仿佛肩上压下一副沉重的担子。
That flimsy letter seemed to lay a heavy burden on Hu's shoulders.

地方的安危，跷脚长根的祸福，以及何桂清的前程，都系于他的一句话中。
The safety of the district, Lame Changgen's fate and He Guiqing's future all depended on a word from him.

说一声：是预备点验，不是别有用心，则清军自然撤围，
If he said that the men were assembling for inspection and had no hidden purpose, the Qing troops would withdraw their encirclement.

但万一跷脚长根乘机作乱，则追究责任，岂仅何桂清不得了，自己亦有脑袋搬家的可能。
But if Changgen seized the chance to rebel, He would face ruin, and Hu himself might lose his head.

倘或答说：情况不明，难作判断，则清军便可能围剿，
If, on the other hand, he said the situation was unclear and impossible to judge, the troops might attack.

有如杀降，
It would be like killing men who had surrendered.

自己在场面上如何交代，还在其次，身上等于背了一笔血债，
How he could explain himself was a secondary matter; he would carry a debt of blood.

以后的日子怎么过得下去？
How could he live afterwards?

跟俞武成商量的结果，只有这样答复：已经遵谕开始调查，真相未明之前，请何桂清转告营务处，按兵不动，加意防范。
After consulting Yu Wucheng, he replied that inquiries had begun as instructed, and asked He to tell the military affairs office to hold its position and exercise particular vigilance until the truth was clear.

这是搪塞眼前，究竟真相如何，亟待澄清，
This put off the immediate difficulty, but the facts urgently needed establishing.

周一鸣却又不知到哪里去了？
Where had Zhou Yiming gone?

胡雪岩心想，形势象炉子上烘着一罐火药，随时可以爆发，
Hu felt as though a jar of gunpowder were heating on a stove, ready to explode at any moment.

这罐火药不早早设法拿开，令人片刻难安。
Until it was removed, he could have no peace.

因而当机立断，决定了一个开门见山的办法。
He therefore decided to tackle the matter directly.


这天晚上打听到，跷脚长根歇在妙珍那里，
That evening he learned that Changgen was staying with Miaozhen.

胡雪岩请朱老大派了个人引导，径造妙珍香阁。
He asked Eldest Brother Zhu for a guide and went straight to her rooms.

这是不速之客，跷脚长根深感意外。
His unexpected arrival greatly surprised Changgen.

内心紧张，表面却甚闲豫，
Though tense within, Hu appeared perfectly at ease.

胡雪岩先打量妙珍，
He first took stock of Miaozhen.

貌不甚美，但长身玉立，身段极好，而且花信年华，正是风尘女子中最妙的那段年岁。
Her face was not especially beautiful, but she was tall and graceful, with an excellent figure, and at the flowering age considered the finest in a courtesan's life.

至于谈吐应酬，更见得气度不凡，
Her conversation and social manner showed unusual distinction.

配了跷脚长根那样一个草莽英雄，他倒替她觉得可惜。
Hu felt it a pity that she should be paired with a rough adventurer like Changgen.

等摆出碟子来小酌，胡雪岩才看一看妙珍问跷脚长根：“有封信，想给你看。”
Only after small dishes had been set out for a drink did he glance at her and tell Changgen, “There's a letter I'd like you to see.”

“喔，”
“Oh.”

跷脚长根会意了，“请到这边来，”
Understanding, Changgen said, “Come this way.”

一引引入妙珍的卧室，请胡雪岩坐在妆台边，
He led Hu into Miaozhen's bedroom and seated him by the dressing table.

跷脚长根自己坐在床沿上，俯身相就，静候问话。
Changgen sat on the edge of the bed, leaning towards him, and waited.

“我听你一句话，
“I'll take your word.

你说怎么样，我就怎么样答复前途。”
Whatever you tell me, that is what I'll report to the authorities.”

胡雪岩一面说，一面把信递了过去。
Hu handed him the letter.

看完了信，跷脚长根的脸色显得很不安，
After reading it, Changgen looked troubled.

静静想了一会答道：
He considered silently for a moment.

“老兄，你看我是什么意思？”
“Brother, what do you think my intentions are?”

这话问得很有分量，
It was a weighty question.

胡雪岩很慎重地答道：
Hu answered carefully.

“如果我不相信，我就不拿这封信给你看了。”
“If I didn't trust you, I wouldn't show you this letter.”

跷脚长根点点头，表示满意：
Changgen nodded, satisfied.

“好的！
“Good.

我晓得你为难。
I know your difficulty.

该怎么办，请你吩咐。”
Tell me what I should do.”

“言重，言重！”
“You honour me.”

胡雪岩想了想答道：
Hu thought for a moment.

“也难怪官军！
“We can hardly blame the troops.

实在时世太乱，不能不防，
These times are so unsettled that they must take precautions.

弄出误会来，说句实话，总是我们吃亏。
If a misunderstanding arises, frankly, we shall be the ones to suffer.

所以，我想不如等一等，
Why not wait?

到有了点验的日子，大家再来，官军就不会疑心了。”
Once an inspection date is fixed, everyone can assemble, and the troops will have no reason for suspicion.”

“是！”
“Very well.

跷脚长根说：“吃酒去！”
Let's have a drink.”


走到外间，他立刻找了贵生来，嘱咐他连夜派人，分头通知部下，各回原处。
Back in the outer room, Changgen immediately summoned Guisheng and ordered him to send men out that night in every direction, telling his followers to return to their original quarters.

这样明快的处置，胡雪岩也深感满意。
Hu was deeply satisfied by this prompt decision.

喝酒闲谈之际，由于撤除了内心的戒备，两个人越谈越投机，
With his inner guard lowered, their conversation over wine grew increasingly congenial.

胡雪岩不待周一鸣来回报，就已知道了跷脚长根改变态度，愿意就抚的原因，当然，这是出于他的自叙。
Before Zhou returned to report, Hu learned why Changgen had changed his attitude and decided to accept the amnesty—at least, according to Changgen's own account.

一言以蔽之，是为了胡雪岩的态度。
In a word, it was Hu's conduct.

那副牌九上的“高抬贵手”，当然是促成跷脚长根改变态度的主要原因，但不是唯一原因。
His generosity over that pai gow hand had been the chief cause, but not the only one.

他认为胡雪岩讲江湖义气讲得“上路”，固然心服，
Changgen admired the way Hu understood and practised loyalty among friends.

而真正使他能够信任的，还在胡雪岩的才干。
What truly won his trust, however, was Hu's ability.

讲义气也要有个讲法，
Even loyalty needed the right means of expression.

同生共死算得是最义气的，但同年同月同日的同死，究竟不如一起吃酒吃肉的同生来得有味道。
Living and dying together might be its highest form, but dying together on the same day of the same month and year was less enjoyable than living together over wine and meat.

跷脚长根很坦白地表示，他就是相信胡雪岩有让他吃酒吃肉的本事。
Changgen frankly admitted that he trusted Hu to provide the wine and meat.

这番推心置腹的话，自然令胡雪岩有着意外的感动，
Such confidence moved Hu unexpectedly.

不过他向来的处世之道是，大家越尊敬他，他越替人着想，
Yet his usual rule was that the more respect people showed him, the more consideration he owed them.

所以一再谦虚，认为跷脚长根“够朋友”，给他这么一个面子。
He repeatedly disclaimed the credit, saying that Changgen was a true friend to grant him such favour.

同时又极力推崇俞武成，让跷脚长根清楚地感觉到，能尊敬俞武成，则比尊敬他更能使他高兴。
He also praised Yu Wucheng warmly, making Changgen understand that respect shown to Yu would please him even more than respect shown to himself.

这一番小酌，吃到深更半夜，
They drank far into the night.

俞武成却有些不放心，特为派朱老大来探问，托词苏州有连夜送到的信，要请他回去看。
Yu grew anxious and sent Zhu to inquire, using the excuse that a letter had arrived from Suzhou and Hu must return to read it.

到家相见，彼此说明经过，俞武成便越发对他刮目相看了。
Once home, they explained what had happened, and Yu's esteem for him rose still higher.

第二天一早，周一鸣带来的消息，与跷脚长根自己所说的，大致相仿，
Early next morning, Zhou brought information broadly matching Changgen's account.

而他，此刻又有了新的任务。
Zhou now had another task.

在苏州那方面，胡雪岩的布置是七分防备，三分招抚，
Hu's arrangements in Suzhou had been seven parts precaution and three parts amnesty.

现在防备不需要了，关卡上所设的暗桩，应该撤回，
The precautions were no longer needed; the concealed men at the checkpoints should be withdrawn.

而招抚的准备工作，只做了三分是不够的，必得立刻替跷脚长根去安排，
Three parts preparation for the amnesty was insufficient, however, and arrangements for Changgen must be made at once.

特意先派周一鸣去见何桂清，报个信息，他自己打算在这晚上赴宴以后，连夜回苏州去料理。
Hu sent Zhou ahead to inform He, intending to return to Suzhou himself overnight after that evening's banquet.


一场“鸿门宴”，变成了庆功宴，
The banquet meant as a trap became a celebration of success.

在妙珍姐妹殷勤侍奉，以及跷脚长根的不断相劝之下，胡雪岩跟俞武成一样喝得酪酊大醉。
Attended devotedly by Miaozhen and her sister, and urged on continually by Changgen, Hu became as thoroughly drunk as Yu.

等酒醒过来，忽切间不辨身在何处？
When he woke, he could not for a moment tell where he was.

一只手无意间一伸，触摸到极软、极滑的肌肤，
An unthinking movement of his hand brought it against skin exquisitely soft and smooth.

于是接着闻到了脂香，看到了粉光，
Then came the scent of cosmetics and the pale gleam of a powdered face.

昏昏罗帐中有个妙年女子陪他睡着，只是脸朝外面，一时看不出是谁？
A young woman lay sleeping beside him in the dim bed curtains, but her face was turned away, and he could not see who she was.

定定神细想，除了猜拳闹酒的情形，再也想不起酒阑人散的光景。
Collecting himself, he remembered the drinking games, but nothing of the party's end.

于是摇摇他身边那段藕也似的手臂，摇醒了一看，
He shook the arm beside him, pale as a lotus root, and woke her.

是妙珍的妹妹，颜色远胜于她姐姐的妙珠。
It was Miaozhen's younger sister, Miaozhu, far more beautiful than her elder sister.

“喔，胡老爷，你醒了！”
“Oh, Master Hu, you're awake!”

和衣而睡的妙珠，急忙坐了起来，
Still fully dressed, she sat up quickly.

“要不要喝茶？”
“Would you like tea?”

“要的。”
“Yes.

胡雪岩觉得嗓子干涩，说话都很吃力，“要冷茶，大大来一杯！”
Cold tea—a great cupful!” His throat was so dry that speech was difficult.

“酒吃得忒多了。
“You drank far too much.

俞大爷也醉得人事不知。”
Master Yu was insensible too.”

说着，她掀帐下床，剔亮了灯，倒了一大杯半温的茶，
She lifted the curtains, got out of bed, brightened the lamp and poured a large cup of lukewarm tea.

挂起帐子，拿茶杯送到胡雪岩唇边。
Hooking up the curtains, she brought it to his lips.

他一饮而尽，喘口气问道：“什么时候了？”
He drank it all, caught his breath and asked, “What time is it?”

“快四点钟了。”
“Almost four.”

“只怕害你半夜不曾好睡，
“I'm afraid I've kept you from sleeping half the night.

真正过意不去。”
I'm very sorry.”


“胡老爷为啥这样子说？
“Why say that?

你是李七爷的朋友。”
You're Seventh Master Li's friend.”

李七爷是指跷脚长根，
By Seventh Master Li she meant Changgen.

胡雪岩便问：“他醉了没有？”
“Was he drunk?” Hu asked.

“李七爷从不醉的。”
“Seventh Master Li never gets drunk.”

“喔！”
“Oh!”

胡雪岩很诧异，
Hu was surprised.

“他的酒量这么大？”
“Can he hold so much?”

“李七爷的酒量并不大，不过，他会得吃酒。”“你这话倒有趣！”胡雪岩讪笑地说，
“He can't hold much, but he knows how to drink.” Hu laughed teasingly. “That's an amusing way to put it.

“又说他会吃酒，又说他酒量并不大。”
You say he knows how to drink, and then that he can't drink much.”

“喔唷！胡老爷，你不作兴‘扳差头’的！”
“Oh, Master Hu, don't pick holes in what I say!”

妙珠的神态，声音都嗲得令人发腻，
Her voice and manner were cloyingly coy.

“我是说李七爷吃酒上会变把戏。”
“I mean he can play tricks when he drinks.”

“我不是扳你的差头，
“I'm not picking holes.

你说话真的有趣。”
You really do say amusing things.”

胡雪岩捧着她的脸说：
Hu cupped her face in his hands.

“吃酒还会变把戏，你自己想想，话可有趣！”
“Think of it yourself: playing tricks with drinking!”

“真的！
“It's true.

不作兴瞎说。”
You mustn't talk nonsense.

妙珠问道：“胡老爷，你跟李七爷熟不熟？”
Do you know Seventh Master Li well?”

“也算熟，也算不熟。”
“In one way, yes; in another, no.”

“你自己呢？”妙珠反唇相讥，
“And what about you?” she retorted.

“说话也是一脚进、一脚出。”
“One foot in and one foot out whenever you speak!”

“这有个说法，
“There's an explanation.

相交的日子不久，不能算熟，
We haven't known each other long, so I can't say I know him well.

不过交情已很深了，所以也可以说是很熟。”
But our friendship is already deep, so in that sense I do.”

“熟了你就知道了，
“Once you know him, you'll understand.

豁拳敬酒，你要当心李七爷，
Watch him during drinking games and toasts.

You may see him pouring wine into his mouth when it's really going onto the floor or into his sleeve.

He knows his own capacity and always stops at seven-tenths of it.

But he'll never say he can't drink any more.

那时候……”
At that point...”

妙珠笑笑不再说下去，

意思是到那时候，就有“把戏”看了。
That was when the tricks began.


这句毫不相干的闲谈，在胡雪岩觉得极其有用，
Hu found this apparently irrelevant chatter extremely useful.

喝酒赌钱，最可以看出性情，
Drinking and gambling were the best ways to reveal a man's character.

照跷脚长根这种喝酒的情形来看，显然是个极能自制的人，但也是极难惹的人，
Changgen's way of drinking showed exceptional self-control, but also that he was difficult to push.

到他不说做这件事，而逼着他非做不可时，他就出花样了。
When he would not agree to do something and others insisted, he resorted to tricks.

因此，胡雪岩对他仍不免引起了一两分戒心。
A little caution therefore returned to Hu's mind.

妙珠极其机敏，
Miaozhu was perceptive.

“That won't be easy.

李七爷花样多得很，你防不住的。”
He has too many tricks for you to guard against.”

“Oh!”

Do you feel the same as your sister?”

“Of course.”

“Of course.”

胡雪岩静静享受着那种温馨的滋味，

芙蓉沉静，阿巧姐老练，而妙珠娇柔灵动，自有一番魅力。
Furong was reserved, A’qiao experienced, while Miaozhu had a tender, lively charm of her own.

他沉浸在眼前的亲密之中。
He gave himself over to their present intimacy.

一番缱绻，万种风情，胡雪岩心满意足地沉沉睡去。
After loving embraces and countless endearments, he fell into a deep, contented sleep.

His first thought was that he must go to Suzhou.

算了！
