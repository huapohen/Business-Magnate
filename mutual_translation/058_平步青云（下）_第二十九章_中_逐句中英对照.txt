胡雪岩
《平步青云（下）》第二十九章（中）
A Swift Rise to Prominence, Volume III — Chapter Twenty-Nine, Part Two
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


于是胡雪岩将古应春留了下来，就拿妙珠的梳妆台当书桌，
Hu therefore kept Gu Yingchun with him and used Miaozhu’s dressing table as a desk.

她倒是心口如一，
She was as good as her word.

备好了纸笔茶水，关照娘姨、大姐都去睡觉，然后自己也避了到套房里。
After providing paper, brushes and tea, she sent the maids to bed and withdrew into the adjoining room herself.

“老古，”胡雪岩坐在床沿上低声说道：“直到今天晚上，长根回来，这件招抚的大事，才算定局。
“Gu,” Hu said quietly, sitting on the edge of the bed, “this whole amnesty business was only settled tonight, when Changgen came back.

我把前后经过，详详细细说给你听，
I’ll tell you everything that happened.

请你替我写封信给何学台，明天一早交给老周专送。”
Please write a letter to Education Commissioner He for me, and tomorrow morning Old Zhou can take it there specially.”

“你不是马上就要到苏州去了，
“Aren’t you about to return to Suzhou yourself?

当面谈倒不好？”
Wouldn’t it be better to speak to him in person?”

“情形不稳，事未定局，不好留什么笔迹。
“When the situation was uncertain and nothing was settled, it was better not to leave anything in writing.

照现在的样子，一个要有个正式的书面，才显得郑重。
As matters stand now, a formal written account is needed to show that we take it seriously.

而况，何学使还要跟营务处去谈，
Besides, Commissioner He still has to discuss it with the military affairs office.

口头传话，或许误会意思，
An oral message might be misunderstood.

不如写在纸上，明明白白，不会弄错。”
Put it clearly on paper, and there can be no mistake.”

这一封长信写完，自鸣钟正打三下。
By the time the long letter was finished, the clock was striking three.

夏至前后，正是昼最长、夜最短的时候，
It was near the summer solstice, when the days were longest and the nights shortest.

看窗外曙色隐隐，夜深如水，
A faint dawn was showing outside, though the deep night was still cool as water.

想来妙珠的好梦正酣，胡雪岩不忍唤醒她，便跟古应春商量，两个人睡一张大床。
Imagining Miaozhu fast asleep, Hu did not want to wake her and suggested that he and Gu share the large bed.

“这又何必？”古应春笑道：
“Why should we?” Gu laughed.

“放着‘软玉温香’，不去‘拥满怀’，未免暴殄天物。
“With such warm, fragrant beauty waiting to fill your arms, it would be a shame to waste Heaven’s gifts.

自然是我用小床，你们用大床。”
Naturally, I shall take the small bed and you two the large one.”

一句话说得胡雪岩动了心，便改了主意，
The remark stirred Hu, and he changed his mind.

“你一个人睡大床吧！”他说，
“You take the large bed yourself.

“我跟她去挤一挤。”
I’ll squeeze in beside her.”

“挤有挤的味道。
“There are pleasures in being squeezed together too.

随便你。”
As you please.”

说着，古应春便解衣上床了。
Gu undressed and got into bed.


胡雪岩悄悄推开套房的门，
Hu quietly pushed open the adjoining door.

只见残焰犹在，罗帐半垂，
A flame still flickered, and the gauze curtains hung half drawn.

妙珠裹着一幅夹被，面朝里睡，微有鼾声。
Wrapped in a lined coverlet, Miaozhu lay facing the wall, snoring faintly.

他蹑手蹑脚地走了进去，轻轻关好了门，卸衣灭灯，摸到床上，跟妙珠并头睡下。
He tiptoed in, closed the door softly, undressed, put out the light and felt his way into bed beside her.

他不想惊动她，但心却静不下来，
He had no wish to disturb her, but could not settle his own thoughts.

只为了她头上的一串珠兰，此物最宜枕上，沾染妇人的发脂而香味愈透，浓郁媚冶，令人心荡。
In her hair she wore a string of pearl orchids, flowers particularly suited to a pillow: mingling with a woman’s hair oil, their scent grew fuller, rich and alluring enough to set the heart astir.

胡雪岩挤在这张小床上，又想起从前在老张那条“无锡快”上与阿珠相处的往事，
Crowded into the little bed, Hu also recalled his time with A’zhu aboard Old Zhang’s Wuxi fast boat.

心中不免惆怅，越发难以平静。
Those memories brought a sense of melancholy and left him still more restless.

不知不觉的转身反侧，吵醒了妙珠，
Without realising it, he turned from side to side and woke Miaozhu.

睡梦里头忽然发觉有个男人在自己身边，自然一惊，
Discovering a man beside her in her sleep naturally frightened her.

她仿佛着魔似的，倏然抬起半身，双手环抱，眼睛睁得好大地斜视着。
As though possessed, she suddenly raised herself halfway, clasped her arms across her body and stared sideways with wide eyes.

“是你！”
“It’s you!”

她透口气，
She let out a breath.

“吓我一大跳。”
“You gave me such a fright.”

“你倒不说吓我一跳。”胡雪岩失笑了。
“You might say you gave me one!” Hu laughed.

“真正是，鬼头鬼脑！”妙珠嗔道：
“Sneaking about like a ghost!

“为啥要这样子偷偷摸摸？”
Why creep in like that?”

“偷偷摸摸才有趣。”
“Creeping about makes it more amusing.”

胡雪岩伸手一拉，把她拉得又重新睡下，
He took her arm and drew her down again.

“我本来不想吵醒你，
“I didn’t mean to wake you.

实在是睡不着。”
I simply couldn’t sleep.”


“古老爷呢？”
“Where is Mr Gu?”

“他在大床上，
“In the large bed.

也是刚睡下。”
He has only just lain down too.”

“恐怕还不曾睡着，
“Then he may still be awake.

声音轻一点。”妙珠又问：“信写好了？”
Keep your voice down.” She asked, “Is the letter finished?”

“自然写好了才睡。”
“Of course. We wouldn’t go to bed before finishing it.”

“写给谁的？”
“Who was it to?”

“写到苏州去的。”
“Someone in Suzhou.”

“你不是要回苏州了吗？
“Aren’t you going back to Suzhou?

为啥还要写信？
Why write, then?

照这样说，你还住两天？”
Does that mean you’ll stay another couple of days?”

这一连串的问句中，留他的意思，表露无遗。
The succession of questions plainly revealed her wish to keep him.

胡雪岩心想，如果说了实话，又惹她不快，因而使含含糊糊地答道：“嗯，嗯，也没有定规。”
Hu thought that the truth would only upset her again, so he answered vaguely, “Well, well, nothing is fixed.”


于是妙珠便问胡雪岩家里的情形。
Miaozhu began asking about his family.

由于她是闲谈解闷的语气，胡雪岩便不作戒备，
Since her tone suggested nothing more than idle conversation to pass the time, Hu was unguarded.

老母在堂，一妻一妾，还没有儿子等等，都老实告诉了她。
He told her frankly that his elderly mother was still living, that he had a wife and a concubine, and that he had no son yet.

“刘三爷是极精明、极能干的人，
“Third Master Liu is so shrewd and capable.

想来你那位‘湖州太太’也厉害得很！”
I imagine your ‘Huzhou wife’ must be formidable too.”

“一点不厉害。
“Not in the least.

真正阿弥陀佛的好人。”
She is goodness itself, a truly gentle soul.”

“这是你的福气！”
“That is your good fortune.”

“谢谢你！”
“Thank you.”

胡雪岩带些得意的笑着，
Hu smiled with some satisfaction.

“我的福气还不错。”
“My luck is not bad.”

“也是你那位湖州太太的福气。”
“It is your Huzhou wife’s good fortune too.”

“这倒不见得。”
“Not necessarily.”

“嫁着你胡老爷这样又能干、又体贴的人，过的是不愁吃、不愁穿的你心日子。
“To marry someone as capable and considerate as you, Master Hu, and live comfortably without a care about food or clothing!

你胡老爷人缘又好，
You are so well liked, too.

走到哪里都是热热闹闹，风风光光。
Wherever you go, there is lively company and every distinction.

这还不叫福气？”
Isn’t that good fortune?”

“我这个人好说话时很好说话，
“When I’m easy to deal with, I’m very easy.

难弄的时候也很难弄。”
When I’m difficult, I can be very difficult indeed.”

“我倒看不出来。”
“I can’t see that.

妙珠紧接着说，“照我看，你最随和不过。”
You seem the most accommodating of men.”

“随和也有随和的坏处，
“Being accommodating has its drawbacks.

外头容易七搭八搭，
It is easy to get involved with women elsewhere.

气量小的会气煞。”
Someone jealous could be driven wild.”

“男人家有出息的，三妻四妾也是常事。”妙珠忽然问道，“你有了湖州太太，总还有上海太太、苏州太太？”
“It is perfectly ordinary for a successful man to have several wives and concubines.” Miaozhu suddenly asked, “If you have a Huzhou wife, surely you have a Shanghai wife and a Suzhou wife too?”

“那倒还没有。”
“Not yet.

胡雪岩说，“一时也遇不着中意的人。”
I haven’t happened to meet anyone who suits me.”


妙珠恨不得凑过脸去说一声：你看我怎么样？
Miaozhu longed to bring her face close and ask, “What do you think of me?”

但这样毛遂自荐，一则老不起这张面皮，二则也怕他看轻了自己，
But she could not bring herself to offer herself so openly, and feared that he might think less of her.

只好忍着。
She held back.

但转念一想，放着自己这样的人才，哪一样比别人差？
Then she reflected: with all her qualities, in what way was she inferior to anyone else?

他竟说“遇不着中意的人”，倒着实有点不服气。
For him to say in her presence that he had not met anyone who suited him was rather hard to bear.

“那么，”她问，“要怎样的人，你才算中意呢？”
“Then what sort of person would suit you?”

胡雪岩听出因头来了，答话便很谨慎，
Hu now heard where this was leading and chose his answer carefully.

“这很难说，”他有意闪避，
“That is hard to say,” he replied, deliberately evading her.

“情人眼里出西施，
“Beauty lies in a lover’s eyes.

没有定规的。”
There is no fixed rule.”

这一来，妙珠就说不下去了，
That left her nothing to say.

总不能这样质问：难道我不是你的情人？
She could hardly demand, “Am I not your lover?”

这话就问得出来，也乏味。
Even if she could bring herself to ask, what pleasure would there be in it?

自己这佯一片痴心待他，而他真当自己路柳墙花，随折随弃，
Her heart was wholly devoted to him, yet he treated her as a roadside willow or a flower on a wall, to be plucked and thrown away at will.

真是叫人寒心。
It chilled her.

念头转到这里，顿觉有无限难诉的委屈，
With that thought came an inexpressible sense of injustice.

心头凄楚，眼眶随即发热，眼泪滚滚而下。
Sorrow filled her heart, her eyes grew hot, and tears began to roll down.


两个人是贴着脸的，
Their cheeks were touching.

虽然眼睛都朝着帐顶，他看不见她哭，但热泪下流，沾着胡雪岩的右颊，
Both looked up at the bed curtains, so Hu could not see her crying, but her warm tears ran onto his right cheek.

不能没有感觉，
He could hardly fail to notice.

转脸一看，大惊问道：“咦！你又哭了！
Turning towards her, he exclaimed in alarm, “Why, you’re crying again!

为什么？
What is it?”

““我有心事。
“I have something on my mind.

你不晓得！”
You don’t understand.”

“又是触动什么心境了？”
“What has touched your feelings this time?”

“我在想，珍姐倒快有归宿了，
“I was thinking that Sister Zhen will soon have a settled home.

李七爷跟她说，这次招安做了官，要好好做人，干一番事业，
Seventh Master Li told her that once he has accepted the amnesty and become an official, he means to live properly and accomplish something.

预备把珍姐接了回去。
He plans to take her into his household.

我们姐妹相差一岁，自小到现在没有分开过。
We are only a year apart and have never been separated since childhood.

从今以后，她归她，我归我，
Now she will go her way and I mine.

想想可要伤心？”
How can I help being sad?”

“原来为的姐妹情深。”
“So it is sisterly affection.”

胡雪岩笑道：
Hu smiled.

“我倒有个主意，
“I have an idea.

何不你跟你姐姐一起嫁了李七爷？”
Why don’t you marry Seventh Master Li along with your sister?”

这句话说坏了，
It was a disastrous remark.

妙珠的眼泪，倾江倒海一般，
Miaozhu’s tears poured out like a river bursting its banks.

身子一蹦，面朝里边，拉起夹被蒙着头，“嗬嗬”地哭出声来。
She jerked round to face the wall, pulled the coverlet over her head and began sobbing aloud.

胡雪岩悔恨莫及，同时也有些昏头搭脑地弄不明白，一句笑话，何至于惹得她如此？
Hu regretted it bitterly, yet was too bewildered to understand why a single joke should distress her so much.

当然，这时不暇细思，
There was no time to examine the question.

只有好言解释，继以赔罪，只求她住了哭声。
He explained as gently as he could, then apologised, asking only that she stop crying.

哭声不但不止，且有变本加厉之势，
Instead, her sobbing grew louder.

结果，门上有了响声，
At last there was a sound at the door.

古应春被惊醒了，来探问究竟。
Gu had been woken and had come to ask what was wrong.

“你听！”
“Listen!”

胡雪岩推着她说，
Hu nudged her.

“拿人家吵醒了。”
“You’ve woken him.”

妙珠不理，
She paid no attention.

心里倒巴不得有个第三者从中排解，好事方始有望，
In fact she welcomed a third person who might mediate, giving her hopes a chance of fulfilment.

所以反哭得更起劲了。
She cried harder still.

“你真是，‘越扶越醉’！”
“The more you’re supported, the drunker you get!”

胡雪岩无奈，只好起床去开了门。
Helpless, Hu rose to open the door.


“怎么回事？”古应春踏进来问说，同时仔细看着胡雪岩的脸色，
“What has happened?” Gu asked as he entered, studying Hu’s face.

是啼笑皆非的神情。
It wore an expression between laughter and tears.

“哪晓得怎么回事？
“How should I know?

讲话讲得好好地，忽然说舍不得她姐姐从良，伤起心来。”
We were talking quite pleasantly, and suddenly she said she couldn’t bear her sister leaving the profession to marry, and became upset.”

最后一句话不曾说完，妙珠将被一掀，
Before he had finished, Miaozhu threw back the coverlet.

恨恨他说：“你死没良心！”然后又将头转了过去，掩面而啼。
“You heartless wretch!” she said bitterly, then turned away again and covered her face to weep.

这是有意抛出一个疑团，好让古应春去追问，果然，他中了她的计。
She was deliberately creating a mystery for Gu to investigate, and he duly fell into the trap.

“小爷叔，你有啥地方得罪妙珠了？
“Little Uncle, how have you offended her?

拿你恨得这样子，
She seems to hate you so much.

真叫人不懂！”
I can’t understand it.”

“你不懂，我也不懂。”
“Neither can I.”

胡雪岩唯有装傻，
Hu could only pretend ignorance.

而且不希望古应春介入，所以接着便做了个送客出门的姿态，将身子往旁边一挪，手一扬，
He did not want Gu to intervene, so he shifted aside and gestured towards the door.

“天快亮了，
“It is almost daylight.

请上床去吧，
Go back to bed.

睡不了多少时候了。”
You haven’t much time left to sleep.”

听这一说，妙珠的哭声突然提高，仿佛第三者一走，她就孤立无援，有冤难诉似地，
At this, Miaozhu raised her voice, as though losing the third person would leave her helpless and unable to obtain redress.

于是古应春踌躇了。
Gu hesitated.

“到底为什么？”
“What is it really about?”

“她要跟我，又不肯好好谈。
“She wants to belong to me, but won’t discuss it properly.

弄这‘一哭二闹三上吊’的一套，
Instead, she goes through the whole performance—first tears, then a scene, then threats to hang herself.

你说好笑不好笑？”
Isn’t it absurd?”

古应春大感意外，不假思索他说了句：“这是好事啊！”
Taken by surprise, Gu said without thinking, “But that is a good thing!”

“好事多磨！
“Good things take time and trouble.

总也要慢慢儿谈，慢慢儿磨，才可以谈得拢。”
We must talk it over slowly and work things out before we can agree.”

胡雪岩打个呵欠，又催他走：
Hu yawned and again urged him to leave.

“你请吧，
“Please go.

我也要睡了。”
I want to sleep too.”


等古应春一走，妙珠的哭声也停住了，
Once Gu left, Miaozhu stopped crying.

因为胡雪岩已有表示，她便等着他来谈。
Hu had at least given some indication of his position, and she waited for him to talk.

谁知他一口将灯吹熄，上了床却不开口。
Instead, he blew out the lamp, got into bed and said nothing.

事情成了僵局，
They had reached a deadlock.

妙珠又羞又恼，而且初次领略到胡雪岩的手段，真个因爱成仇，
Ashamed and angry, and encountering his methods for the first time, she found her love turning to resentment.

心思拨不转，拼命往牛角尖里去钻。
Unable to turn her thoughts aside, she pursued them deeper into a blind alley.

越想越气，越想越觉得做人乏味，
The more she thought, the angrier she grew and the less life seemed worth living.

再看胡雪岩时，鼾声大起，这一下更把她的心思迟到了绝路上，
When she looked at Hu and heard him snoring loudly, her thoughts were driven to the brink.

悄悄起床，流着眼泪，找了根带子出来，端张椅子到床脚，
Quietly she rose, found a sash through her tears and brought a chair to the foot of the bed.

在床顶栏杆上，将圈套结好，头一伸上了吊。
She tied the sash to the rail above the bed and put her head into the loop.

胡雪岩的鼾声是假的，
Hu’s snoring was feigned.

有意冷落妙珠；好逃避纠缠，
He was deliberately ignoring her to escape her entreaties.

她起来从他身上跨过下了地，他都知道，只不知道她下了地做些什么，
He knew that she had climbed over him and got up, though not what she was doing.

只觉得床突然一震，
Suddenly the bed shook.

不由得眼开了眼，一望之下，吓得心胆俱裂，
He opened his eyes and was horrified by what he saw.

跳起身来，赤脚下了地，将妙珠的下半身一抱，往上一耸，那个圈套总算卸掉了。
Leaping out barefoot, he caught her lower body and lifted her until the loop came free.

妙珠的气刚要闭过去，
Her breath had only just begun to fail.

上了圈套，后悔嫌迟，那一刹那，只觉得世间样样可爱，人人可亲，
Once she had put her head into the loop, she had realised too late that she regretted it; in that instant everything in the world had seemed precious and everyone dear.

所以此时遇救，把胡雪岩的薄情都抛在九霄云外，
Now, rescued, she forgot his coldness.

一片心中，除了感激，还是感激，
Her whole heart was filled with gratitude.

趁势抱往他的头，“哇”地一声大哭而特哭。
She threw her arms about his head and burst into uncontrollable sobbing.

这一下，不但惊醒了古应春，也惊动了妙珍和前后院的闲人，
This woke not only Gu but Miaozhen and the people in both courtyards.

纷纷赶来探望，但心存顾忌，只在窗前门外，探头探脑，窃窃私议，
They hurried over, though, uncertain of their welcome, most remained at the doors and windows, peering in and whispering.

只有妙珍排闼直入，
Miaozhen alone pushed straight inside.

但见妙珠伏在床上抽噎不止，胡雪岩穿一身白洋布小褂裤，赤着脚坐在那里，样子相当窘迫。
She found her sister sobbing on the bed and Hu sitting barefoot in a white foreign-cotton undershirt and trousers, looking thoroughly embarrassed.

她只有向站在一边，仿佛遭遇了绝大难题，不知如何应付的古应春探问：“古老爷，到底为了啥？
Gu stood to one side as though faced with an impossible problem. Miaozhen could only ask him, “Mr Gu, what is all this about?

是不是妙珠得罪了胡老爷？”
Has Miaozhu offended Master Hu?”

古应春不答，
Gu did not answer.

只将嘴一努，视线上扬，
He pursed his lips and glanced upwards.

她顺着他的眼风看过去，才发觉朱漆床栏杆上，束着一条白绸带子，
Following his gaze, she saw the white silk sash tied to the vermilion bed rail.

莫非妙珠曾寻死觅活来着？
Had Miaozhu tried to take her life?

心里疑惑，却怎么样也问不出口来，
She wondered, but could not bring herself to ask.

因为这太不可思议了。
It seemed too incredible.
