Where can we find somewhere quiet?” Hu asked Miaozhen, who answered at once, “Miaozhu's room is empty.”

“不错！”
“Of course!

胡雪岩倒想起来了，
That reminds me.

“妙珠是怎么回事？”
What's happened to her?”

用又象埋怨，又象调侃的声音说，

“I should be asking what's happened to you, Master Hu.”

Concerned though he was, Hu had no time to inquire.

“回头再谈！”转身而去。

“唉！”胡雪岩不知不觉地轻轻叹了口气。

尤五一天都在忙着商谈“大事”，布解所谓，便愕然相问：

“小爷叔，你叹啥气？”

胡雪岩是深感于这短短一天之中，妙珠由一念轻生到毅然脱出风尘。已经历了好一番沧桑，情动乎中，不能自已，

“Little Uncle, from now on the Grain Transport Brotherhood north and south of the Yangtze will depend on you.”

“啊！”胡雪岩吸着气，已感到双肩沉重不胜了，

当然，尤五与他的同道，亦决不会仅仅定下这么一个宗旨，便将千斤重担，不问青红皂白，压在胡雪岩肩上，

Wouldn't that be good business?”

“Certainly.

I'll try to find a way.”

“Exactly!”

尤五十分欣慰，
You was delighted.

“我们要请小爷叔来出头，

“I can't promise they can be cleared.

What other businesses did you discuss?”

We thought of pooling capital and asking you to trade for us.”

“这当然可以。

“是啊！”
“True.

他搔搔头皮说，“我倒没有想到这一点，
I hadn't considered that.

现在是请小爷叔来替漕帮弟兄想办法，

“我也是这个意思。
“That's my view too.”

五哥，”胡雪岩迟延了一下，终于问了出来，“我倒要请教，你的意思，是为公，还是……”

“我的情形，你晓得的，
“You know my situation.

“这不是办法，
“That won't do.

你总要定个章程出来。
You must establish rules.

不要说你是一帮之主，就是我自己的生意，对伙计们也要一碗水往平处端，大家才会心服，”

“是！小爷叔说得是。”尤五深深点头。

“这件事你不妨请老古替你参赞。

现在不必会谈它。
We needn't discuss it now.

When I have some leisure, I'll think them through for you.”


“是的。
“Good.

我先跟你说明白了，
I wanted to explain first.

回头席面上，他们还有话说。”
The others will have more to say at dinner.”

这一夜的盛宴，算是漕帮公众特请，

虽非鸿门宴，但这顿饭也着实难吃，
It was no trap, yet the meal was hard to endure.

大家越是恭维，胡雪岩越觉责任沉重。
The more they praised Hu, the heavier his responsibility felt.

有时要“冲”，不管做得到做不到，硬做了去。
Sometimes he had to charge ahead, whether he could accomplish a thing or not.

象此时的情形，
This was such an occasion.

充对了、冲过了，未见得见好，

充不好、冲不过，则误人大事，吃力而不讨好，

“Brother You has explained your wishes.

First, his own business and the many other affairs he had undertaken had reached a point where further delay was impossible.

所以一时还无法为漕帮效劳，

对于第一点，自是同声应承，

胡雪岩对“麻布筋多，光棍心多”这句江湖上人人皆知的谚语，深具戒心，

With the Tongli business finished and much still awaiting him in Songjiang and Shanghai, You decided to leave early next morning.

特地到胡雪岩那里话别。 不想一谈起来就没有完，

胡雪岩一再催促，他总舍不得走，

“Who is it?”

“Who else?”

“I don't know.”

“Just back.”

Inside were jade bangles, gems, rings, pearl ornaments, gold sovereigns and fragrant aloeswood beads, each wrapped in fresh cotton.

此时一样一样揭开来放在桌上，五光十色，令人目眩。
As she uncovered them one by one and placed them on the table, their colours dazzled the eye.

胡雪岩不解所谓，忍不住问道：
Hu did not understand.

“你这样献宝干什么？”
“Why are you showing off your treasures?”

“我的私房都在这里。
“These are all my private savings.

喏，你看！”
Look.”

她捡起一扣存折，递给胡雪岩。
She picked up a passbook and handed it to him.

“你自己的东西，用不着给我看！”
“Your own belongings needn't be shown to me.”

他不着存折，顺手抛在首饰箱里。
Without looking, he tossed it back into the jewel box.

“这些首饰，我自己估一估，值两万银子。
“I estimate the jewellery at twenty thousand taels.

你看呢？”
What do you think?”

“我不大懂。”
“I'm no expert.

胡雪岩说：“快收起来！
Put it away quickly.

财不露白。
Wealth shouldn't be displayed.

如果这时候外面有个贼在偷看，以后就危险了。”
If a thief were watching outside, there could be trouble later.”

“ 不要紧的！
“Don't worry.

这房子严密得很，围墙极高，不怕贼来。”
The house is secure and the walls very high.”

妙珠略停一下，回入正题：
After a pause she came to the point.

“我留着这些东西无用，
“I have no use for these things.

说不定如你所说，叫贼偷了去，反害得我心疼，
Perhaps, as you say, a thief will steal them and leave me heartbroken.

不如交了给你。”
Better give them to you.”

“交给我做什么？”
“What for?”

“咦！那还不是随便你，
“Whatever you like.

做生意派点本钱也是好的。”
They could provide some capital for your business.”

听得这两句话，胡雪岩的感想极多，但最后却是笑了出来，
Hu had many feelings at this, but finally laughed.

想到“唱本”上的故事：公子落难，花园赠金，大魁天下，奉旨归娶。
He thought of the stories in songbooks: a young gentleman fallen on hard times, a gift of gold in a garden, triumph in the examinations, then an imperial command to return and marry his benefactress.

看起来，妙珠多少也有这样子的想法。
Miaozhu seemed to harbour some such notion.


这一笑，显得有些轻侮，
The laugh seemed slightly contemptuous.

妙珠微感不悦，正色说道：“我是诚心诚意的正经话。”
A little hurt, she said seriously, “I mean it sincerely.”

“我晓得你是诚心诚意。
“I know.

可惜，”
Unfortunately...”

胡雪岩想了想，还是将那句话说了出来：
He considered, then said what he had been thinking.

“你这番诚心，用错了地方。”
“Your sincerity has found the wrong object.”

“怎么呢？诚心待人还会错？”
“How can sincerity towards someone be wrong?”

“本心不错，用得不得当。
“The intention is good; its use is mistaken.

你要遇见一个肯上进的穷书生就好了，
You should have met an ambitious, poor scholar.

将来不说中状元，进京赶考中个进士好了，
Even if he didn't finish first in the empire, he might go to the capital and gain the jinshi degree.

明媒正娶，还挣副诰封给你。
Then he could marry you properly as his wife and earn you an imperial title.

那有多好？”
How fine that would be.”

“我不稀罕。
“I don't care for that.

只要……”
All I want...”

“只要怎么样？”
“What?”

“只要……”妙珠很吃力地说：“只要你不变心就好了。”
With difficulty, she answered, “For you not to change your heart.”

胡雪岩默然。
Hu was silent.

觉得所遇到过的几个女子，以妙珠用心最苦，胁之以死，动之以利，先怕嫁不成，嫁成了又怕人变心，
Of all the women he had known, Miaozhu had taken the greatest pains: first threatening death, then appealing with wealth; first fearing she could not marry him, then fearing that he would change once she had.

心眼儿这么多，将来怕难得相处。
With so many calculations, she might be hard to live with.

他的心里很矛盾，有畏惧也有怜惜，
He felt both apprehension and pity.

因而既想设法将刚结上的红丝剪断，却又觉得割舍不下，
Part of him wanted to cut the red thread they had just tied, yet he could not bear to part with her.

就这踌躇莫决之际，听得妙珠幽幽地叹了口气。
While he hesitated, she sighed softly.

“唉！嫁鸡随鸡，嫁狗随狗，
“Marry a cock, follow the cock; marry a dog, follow the dog.

我也跟你一样，做事不会懊悔的。
Like you, I won't regret what I've done.

将来都看你！
The future rests with you.

反正不管怎么样，我姓胡是姓定了。”
Whatever happens, my name is Hu now.”

听得出来，这是从心底掏出来的真话。
The words came from her heart.

她有这样的表示，自己便再无别的主意好打。
After that declaration, he could entertain no other plan.

但是胡雪岩也警觉到，此时不宜轻许诺言，
Yet he realized that easy promises would be unwise.

宜乎硬起心肠来，言明在先。
He must harden himself and speak plainly first.


“你这样一片诚心待我，我怎么肯变心。
“With such sincerity towards me, how could I change?

不过，我有为难之处，你也该体谅。
But I have difficulties too, and you must understand them.

将来有不得不让你委屈的地方，你肯不肯咬起牙关来承受？”
If circumstances force me to make you bear some hardship, will you grit your teeth and accept it?”

妙珠咬一咬牙，答了一个字：“肯！”
Miaozhu clenched her teeth and gave one word: “Yes.”

“那就好了。
“Good.

什么委屈，这时候也不必去说它，
We needn't discuss what hardships now.

总之将心比心，到时候你肯为我设想，就晓得我要你受那种委屈，也是无奈。”
Put yourself in my place when the time comes, and you'll understand that I have no choice.”

这番话暧昧难明，
The words were too vague.

妙珠认为必须问个清楚：
She had to ask.

“你倒说说看，是啥委屈？让我心里也好有个预备。
“Tell me what you mean, so I can prepare myself.”

““譬如说，我明天一早就要走了，丢下你一个人在这里，
“For instance, I must leave early tomorrow and leave you alone here.

岂不是委屈了你？”
Won't that be hard on you?”

“That doesn't count.

“还有呢？”
What else?”

“还有？”胡雪岩摇摇头，
He shook his head.

“一时无比说起。
“I can't list everything now.

反正都是这种事出无奈的情形。
They will be things beyond my control.

我们先谈明天，
Let's talk about tomorrow.

我起了以后，你怎么样？”
Once I've left, what will you do?”

“自然是关起门来过日子。”
“Close my doors and live quietly, of course.”

这样的答复，是可以意料得到的。
He could have predicted that answer.

但说出口来，有声音灌入耳中，少不得要想一想，这一想，便有疑问了。
Hearing it aloud, however, made him consider it, and a question followed.

“你是过惯了热闹日子的，
“You're used to a lively life.

一个人清清冷冷，熬得下来吗？”
Can you endure the loneliness?”

话问得很坦率，也很实在，可是妙珠却觉得不中听，
It was a frank, practical question, but she disliked it.

因而语声中便有不服气的意味：
Defiance entered her voice.

“你看着好了，看我熬得下来，熬不下来？”
“Wait and see whether I can.”

熬不下来又如何？
And if she could not?

胡雪岩心里在想，
Hu thought.

将来红杏出墙丢了自己的面子。
An affair would disgrace him.

这件事非同小可，必得好好想个办法。
This was serious; he must find a sound arrangement.

生米已经煮成熟饭，
The rice was already cooked.

说不算也不行，
He could not pretend otherwise.

那就只有一条路好走。
There was only one course.


对这一重姻缘，一直优柔寡断、访煌游移、自己都不知道如何是好的胡雪岩，恢复了他的明快果断的性格，
After all his indecision over this marriage, Hu recovered his usual clarity and firmness.

“妙珠！”他用毫不含糊的语气说：“这些东西你自己先收起来，
“Miaozhu, put these things away for now.

有机会我替你做点‘小货’，
When there's an opportunity, I'll do some small trading for you.

是你的私房，我决不来动你，
They are your private assets; I won't touch them for myself.

至于丢你一个人在这里，我也不放心，
As for leaving you here alone, I can't feel easy about that either.

你等我明天一走，就收拾收拾行李，
Once I go tomorrow, begin packing.

我再来接你，
I'll come back for you.

我想把你摆在上海。”
I mean to settle you in Shanghai.”

到底有了个明确的了断！
At last there was a definite decision.

转弯抹角，终于逼出了他心里的话，妙珠大为欣慰。
After so many twists, she had drawn out his real intention, and was deeply relieved.

但是，他还有个芙蓉在那里，
But there was still Furong.

又将作何处置？
What would become of her?

“此刻在苏州的‘那一个’呢？”
“And the one in Suzhou?”

“你是说芙蓉？”胡雪岩毫不迟疑地答道：
“You mean Furong?” He answered without hesitation.

“我拿她摆在湖州。”
“I'll settle her in Huzhou.”

这就很容易明白了，
It was plain enough to her.

他预备立三个“门口”，除了杭州在老家，上海、湖州各一处。
He meant to establish three households: the old family home in Hangzhou, and others in Shanghai and Huzhou.

上海是繁华之地，而且要做生意，就碍常住上海，
Shanghai was prosperous, and business would keep him there often.

比较上以自己的处境最优越。
Her position was the best of the three.

妙珠苦心设计，做作得太久，这时候再也不愿掩饰她的真情，
She had schemed and acted for too long to conceal her true feelings any more.

收好她的首饰箱往床里枕头边一放，随即便贴住他的身子坐下，两手环抱，抱住他的上半身，将脸偎依在他肩头，深深地吸着气，显得极其满足恬适似地。
Putting away her jewel box beside the pillows, she sat close against him, wrapped both arms round his upper body, laid her face against his shoulder and drew a deep breath, looking utterly content and at peace.
