显然的，这是为了让他好受恒记的同人着重，有意这么说，

He was a loyal friend indeed.
庞二真的很够交情。


Shao hosted a sumptuous Western meal.
* * *由邵仲甫作东，吃了一顿丰盛的“番菜”，

Pang, who was to accompany Yiyun to the foreign shops to buy jewellery and dress fabrics, left in haste.
庞二要陪怡云老七到洋行里去买首饰衣料，匆匆走了，

Their host detained Hu over “English black tea”; he had something to discuss.
主人留胡雪岩在原处喝“英国红茶”，有话要谈。

Pang had told Shao too that Hu held a substantial stake in Hengji.
在邵仲甫面前，庞二也说胡雪岩在恒记有大股份，

Shao’s manner had consequently changed since their first meeting, as had his form of address.
因而他的神态也显得跟第一次见面不同，连称呼也改过了，

Instead of calling him brother, he now called him “Mr Hu.”
不是称兄道弟，而是叫“胡先生”。

“Mr Hu, there is something I must ask.
“胡先生！”他说，“我有句话请教，

Second Master Pang has just instructed us that you will henceforth handle Hengji’s dealings with Tongxing.
刚刚庞二少爷关照，以后恒记跟同兴往来，归胡先生你经手，

Does that mean we should still act on Zhu Funian’s instructions?”
那么，朱福年来说的话，算不算数？

The question went straight to the heart of the matter.
“一下子问到要害上，

Hu would not answer hastily.
胡雪岩不敢轻率回答，

“What sort of instructions?”
先反问一句：“是什么话？”

“Until now, Zhu alone has dealt with us for Hengji.
“恒记跟同兴的往来，本来都归朱福年一个人接头，

Payments and receipts of over a hundred thousand taels, or transfers between accounts—we have acted on his word.
上十万银子的出入，或者调拨户头，都听他一句话。

Should we continue to do so?”
以后，我们听不听呢？”

“Transfers between accounts” was exactly what Hu wished to understand.
这“调拨户头”四个字，正就是胡雪岩要弄明白的，

He followed it up.
当然往下追问：

“How many accounts does Hengji have with your bank?”
“恒记在宝号有几个户头？”

“Three: Hengji, Jijia Hall, and Fuji.”
“三个。”邵仲甫答道：“恒记、继嘉堂、福记。”

Jijia Hall was the Pang family’s hall name, while Fuji was, of course, Zhu Funian’s account.
“继嘉堂”是庞家的堂名，“福记”当然是朱福年，

The latter two were private accounts.
这个都算是私人户头，

Hengji and Jijia Hall could not be separated, but how could Zhu’s own account properly be mixed with Hengji’s?
但恒记与继嘉堂不可分，福记的私人户头如何可以跟恒记混在一起？

The existence of irregularities was plain.
这其间，不言可知有了弊病。

Instead of answering Shao, Hu made a request.
于是胡雪岩不但不答邵仲甫的询问，而且提出要求：

“First have Tongxing copy out a statement of all Fuji’s receipts and payments over the past years for me.”
“请同兴先将福记历年进出的数目，抄个单子给我。”


Shao was startled.
邵仲甫一听吓一跳。

This was a grave taboo in banking.
这是钱庄的大忌，

Wealthy people followed the old rule of never displaying their riches and would not tell others what they had deposited.
有钱的人，守着“财不露白”的古训，在钱庄里存款是决不肯告诉人的，

They used a family hall name or an account name ending in ji precisely to conceal the truth.
用堂名或用个什么“记”的户名，就是为了隐藏真相，

Banks, in turn, were obliged to keep their customers’ affairs secret.
而钱庄里也有义务为客户守机密，

To disclose Fuji’s deposits and withdrawals to a third person—if word of it spread, confidence would collapse.
如今将福记存款进出的数目，泄漏给第三者，这话一传出去，信用一失，

Every customer would fear for himself and withdraw his money.
人人白危，都来提存，

Tongxing would be ruined by a run.
岂不把同兴挤垮。

“Mr Hu, you know our business,” Shao said miserably.
“胡先生，你是内行。”他哭丧着脸说：

“I really cannot comply.”
“这件事实在不敢从命。”

Hu understood his difficulty perfectly and had already considered it.
他的难处，胡雪岩完全了解，所以早就想好了的，

“Brother Zhongfu, is there any enmity between us?”
这时便即问道，“仲甫兄，我跟你有没有仇？”

“Why should there be?”
“哪里来的仇？”

“Exactly.
“那不就是了！

Since we have no grievance against one another, why should I wish to harm you?
我跟你无冤无仇，何必来害你？

If Fuji were a purely private account, I should have no right to inspect it.
福记是纯粹的私人户头，我没有资格查他的帐，

But since it has been mixed with Hengji’s, naturally I must find out what has happened.
既然跟恒记混在一起，当然我要弄弄清楚。

Tongxing too has an obligation to show me its transactions.
就是在同兴来说，也有义务拿福记的进出开给我看。”

Set your mind at rest.
胡雪岩又说：“你放心好了！

I shall not break the rules of our profession.
我不会坏同业的规矩的。

Heaven knows, Earth knows, you know, and I know.
这件事，无知地知，你知我知，

I shall not tell even Pang.
连庞老二我都不告诉他，

What is there to fear?”
你还怕什么？

Shao considered this.
“邵仲甫想了想问道：

“Mr Hu, what will you use the statement for?
“胡先生，你要这张单子做啥用场，

To settle accounts with Zhu?”
是不是跟朱福年去算帐？”

“No.
“不是！”

He will not even know it exists.
胡雪岩说：“朱福年也不会晓得有这件事，

I shall use the figures you give me to examine Hengji’s books.”
我是根据你开的单子，盘恒记的帐。”

Shao was in a genuine quandary.
邵仲甫真的为难了，

He drank cup after cup of “English black tea,” unable to give an answer.
“英国红茶”喝了一杯又一杯，只是答不出来。


Hu knew that this was a matter of the utmost seriousness.
胡雪岩也知道这是件极严重的事，

Without pressure, Shao would never agree.
不加点压力，邵仲甫决不肯就范，

He therefore said in a rather cold voice, “Pang wanted me to establish a Fukang branch in Shanghai.
所以用相当冷峻的声音说道：“庞老二本有意叫我在上海立阜康的分号，

Because of what you had already said to me, I did not agree.
我因为你老兄有言在先，没有答应他。

Now it seems that only by having a bank of my own can I get a clear view of the accounts.”
现在在看来，只有自己有钱庄，帐目才能弄得清楚。”

He made as though to rise and leave.
说着，便有起身告辞的模样。

If Fukang opened a branch, Tongxing would naturally lose Hengji’s business.
阜康一设分号，同兴当然再也做不成恒记的生意，

The move put Shao in check, and he could not help growing anxious.
这一着棋是“将”邵仲甫的“军”，他不能不着急。

“Mr Hu, let us discuss this.
“胡先生，胡先生，有话好商量。

Could you allow me until tomorrow to answer?”
你能不能让我明天答你的话。”

“Certainly.
“那自然可以。

But remember one thing, brother.
不过有一层，仲甫兄你千万记住，

Whether you agree or refuse, this must remain known only to the two of us.”
无论你答应也好，不答应也好，这件事只有你我两个人晓得。”

He meant that Zhu must hear nothing of it.
意思是不可泄露其事给朱福年。

Shao understood perfectly and repeatedly assured him, “I know.”
邵仲甫当然意会得到，连连答说：“我知道，我知道。”


Early the next morning a messenger came from Tongxing with a letter.
到了第二天一早，同兴钱庄派人送了信来，

Shao asked Hu to meet him at noon at the same Western restaurant.
邵仲甫约胡雪岩，中午仍旧在那家番菜馆见面。

Hu arrived on time.
准时赴约，

Once they had ordered and the waiter had withdrawn, Shao took out an envelope, laid it before him, and said that there were some points he must first make clear.
点好了菜，等“仆欧”迟了出去，做主人的取出一个信封，摆在面前，跟他先有番话要交代。

邵仲甫提出了“约法三章”：

First, the statement must not be disclosed to anyone.
第一，这份清单不得泄漏给任何人，

Second, it must not be used as evidence against Zhu.
第二，不得以此作为对付朱福年的根据，

第三，不管胡雪岩是不是在上海设阜康的分号，恒记不能与同兴断绝往来。

The third point was really a request, but Shao’s unfortunate wording made it sound almost like coercion.
第三点其实是请求，只是邵仲甫的措词不甚恰当，有些近乎要挟的意味。

Hu was displeased.
胡雪岩颇为不悦，

“Brother Zhongfu, I shall observe the first two conditions.
“仲甫兄，”他这样答道：“第一、第二两点，我谨遵台命，

第三点，我只能这么说，我一定讲同业的义气。

恒记如果是我一个人的事业，老兄吩咐，闲话一句，

Unfortunately, the principal owner is Pang, and he has a young gentleman’s temper.

如果恼了他，翻脸不认人，我说的话，他也未见得听。

所以这一点，完全要看你自己的做法，

I shall always speak well of Tongxing at his side.”
我在旁边总替同兴说好话就是。”

The implication was that if Tongxing adopted this almost coercive manner, Pang would be the first to take offence.
这是暗示邵仲甫，如果同兴是这种近乎要侠的做法，庞二首先就会着恼，

Shao was experienced enough to recognize his mistake at once and tactfully apologized.
邵仲甫也是极老到的人，一听他这话，自知失态，很见机地道歉。

“Mr Hu, I have expressed myself badly.
“胡先生，我不会说话，

Please do not hold it against me.
请你不要见怪。

I shall have much to depend on you for in future, and we understand one another.
将来仰仗的地方还多，一切心照。

我也不多说了，

总而言之，听你的吩咐就是。”


Hu was generous in temper, and those words dispelled his displeasure.
胡雪岩的度量宽，有他这两句话，不满之意，随即消失。

When Shao pushed the envelope across the table, he drew out the statement.
等邵仲甫将他面前的信封移了过来，便即抽出里面的单子来看，

只见开头写的是“福记名下收付清单”，后面盖着“同兴协记钱庄”的书柬图章。

他不暇细看内容，将前后折起，用桌上现成的餐，裁下“福记”字样及同兴图章，

各约一指宽的两张纸条，交回邵仲甫。

这个小小的动作，使得邵仲甫大为服帖，

一则见得胡雪岩的诚意，不会拿这张清单作为对付朱福年的把柄，

二则也见得他心细，

邵仲甫发觉自己做错了，

本来就不必写明“福记”字样，更不必盖上书柬图章，

纵然胡雪岩无他，万一遗失了这张清单，落入旁人手中，依然是件极不妥的事。

幸好，他的这个错误，为胡雪岩及时纠正了。

“Mr Hu,” Shao said with heartfelt admiration, “bold men tend to overlook details, while careful men lack breadth of action.

实在是没话可说了。”

“You praise me too highly!”
“谬奖，谬奖！”

Hu too was pleased.
胡雪岩亦颇欣慰，

Shao spoke with complete sincerity, and it seemed that Hu had gained a very useful friend in business.
因为邵仲甫言出至诚，看起来自己是在事业上结交了一个很有用的朋友。
