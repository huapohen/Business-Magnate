Their business was settled, but they were still in the mood for drinking.
正事谈毕，酒兴未已，

Hu had always been interested in pawnshops, and this was a good opportunity to learn.
胡雪岩一直对典当有兴趣，此时正好讨教，

“Brother Funian,” he began, “did you start out in the pawnbroking trade?”
“福年兄，”他先问：“你是不是典当出身？”

“No, but I know something about it.
“不是。不过我懂，

My late third uncle was a pawnshop appraiser, and I lived with him for a year.”
我故世的三叔是朝奉，我在他那里住过一年。”

Zhu went on to describe many of the trade’s rules and abuses, speaking so engagingly that Hu heard one thing after another entirely new to him.
接下来，朱福年便谈了典当中的许多行规和弊端，娓娓道来，闻所未闻。

At last, with something between a sigh and an expression of regret, he said, “I never entered the trade.
最后似感叹，又似遗憾地说，“当初未曾入典当，

自己都不知道是得计，还是失策？

Earning your living in a pawnshop is unlike any other occupation.
因为‘吃典当饭’与众不同，

Of all the three hundred and sixty trades, it is the most comfortable: good accommodation, good food, generous pay, and light work.
是三百六十行生意中，最舒服的一行，住得好、吃得好，入息优厚，工作轻松，

Once you have eaten from that bowl, it is hard to stomach any other.”
因此吃过这碗饭，别的饭就难吃了！”

“From what you say, finding good staff to open a pawnshop cannot be easy.
“照你这样说，如果开爿典当，要寻好手还不容易。”

胡雪岩问，“典业中的好手，宾主相得，一动不如一静，

He will not readily take another position.
轻易不肯他就。

Is that right?”
是这样吗？”

“Broadly speaking, yes.
“大致是这样子。

But new talent keeps emerging.
不过人材是不断在冒出来的，

Sometimes there is no room for advancement in a man’s own shop, and he seeks an opportunity elsewhere.”
本典无可位置，另求发展，也是有的。”

“Then keep an eye out for me.
“那么，我倒要请你留意，

I should like to meet someone of that sort.”
有这样的人，我想见见。”

Clearly Hu was considering opening a pawnshop himself.
这表示胡雪岩也有创办典当的打算，

Zhu readily agreed and seemed eager to try his hand, treating his partial knowledge as full expertise and making up for the opportunity he had missed in his youth.
朱福年欣然应诺，而且跃跃欲试地，颇有以半内行作内行，下手一试，以补少年未曾入此业之憾的意思。


Zhu met Gilbert the next day, just as Chen Shunsheng was enquiring about the shipping space and a demand had arrived from the London head office, forwarded by the East India Company, asking why this year’s silk had still not been shipped.
* * *朱福年是在第二天跟吉伯特见面的，那是陈顺生来探问运货舱位消息的时候，也正是由东印度公司转来伦敦总公司发出的何以今年的丝，至今未曾起运的质问之时，

所以，吉伯特一见他的面，便先追问恒记和裕记两处的货色，可曾运离上海？

“明天就要开船了。”朱福年用英语答说，

“Mr Gilbert, I feel a moral obligation towards you.

不知道你听说了没有？”

“我不知道你指的是什么？”

“Hengji’s proprietor, my employer Mr Pang, has entered into a business partnership with Hu Xueyan.
“恒记的东家，也就是我的雇主庞先生，跟胡雪岩在事业上达成了合作的协议，

胡雪岩的实力并不充足，但他是商场上一个非常特殊的人物，

这两项就是他最大的资本，

没有任何人能够在商场上击败他，包括你吉伯特先生在内。”

“I have no need to defeat him.
“我不需要击败他，

I am concerned only with my company’s interests.
我只为我的公司的利益打算。

最初是我采纳了你的建议，

否则，也不至于有今天的僵局。”

Zhu’s face hardened. “Mr Gilbert, do you wish to discuss who is responsible?”
“吉伯特先生！” 朱福年放下脸来问：“你是不是要讨论这件事的责任？”

“No.”
“不！”

吉伯特摇摇头，

“That would serve no purpose.
“那是没有用的。

你觉得对我有种道义上的责任，足见得你对我还存着友谊，

I hope we remain friends.”
我希望我们仍旧是朋友。”


Zhu responded with a look of sincerity.
听他这一番话，朱福年报之以诚恳的神色，

“That is precisely why I want to do what friendship requires.”
“就因为如此，我要尽我的友谊。”

He paused, then continued in a calm but firm voice.

“Mr Gilbert, you have not been defeated.
“吉伯特先生，你并没有失败，

一切都可以照你原来的计划实现。

但你如果错过此这个最后的机会，那么，你的失败不止于这一次，是明年及以后的日子。

用最简单的话说：你将不能在上海买到你所需要的丝。”

“Do you think the price could be reduced at all?” Gilbert asked.
“照你看，丝价是不是能够减少若干？”吉伯特说，

“If you can arrange that, we shall of course pay you the commission you deserve.”
“如果你办得到，我们当然会付你应得的佣金。

“No!”
““不！”

Zhu was emphatic.
朱福年斩钉截铁地说，

“It is absolutely impossible.
“决无可能！

You should know that Hu Xueyan’s shrewdness in business is unequalled.
你应该知道，胡雪岩做生意的精明，是无人可及的，

现在他不向你提出延期损失的赔偿，已经是很宽大了。”

“Very well.”
“好！”

Gilbert finally yielded.
吉伯特终于低头了，

“I agree to everything.
“我一切照办，

I ask only that we sign the contract quickly.”
只希望赶快订约。”


Once the contract was signed, payment collected, and the silk delivered, Hu felt a great weight lift from him.
订了约，收银交货，胡雪岩如释重负。

Yet after a whole night’s calculations he was left strangely empty, scarcely knowing for whom he had worked and worried so hard.
但经过一整夜的计算，却又爽然若失，自己都不知道为谁辛苦为谁忙。

The profit was indeed a hundred and eighty thousand taels, but it only sounded impressive: not even a handsome figure on paper survived.
赚是赚了十八万银子，然而，不过说来好听，甚至于连帐面上的“虚好看”都没有。

There were too many partners and too many expenses.
因为合伙的关系太多，开支也太大。

The profits had to be shared with You Laowu and Gu Yingchun, and then with Yu Si.
跟尤五、古应春分了红利以外，还要跟郁四再分，

Interest had to be paid in various quarters; Wang Youling’s deficit at the Sea Transport Bureau had to be made good; Qiu Fengyan and Ji Heling also had to receive their tokens of appreciation.
付了各处的利息，还要为王有龄弥补海运局的亏空，加上裘丰言和嵇鹤龄那里都要点缀。

That left very little.
这一下已经所余无几，

There were still the outstanding bills from his three households in Hangzhou, Huzhou, and Tongli.
却还有开销杭州、湖州、同里三个“门口”所拉下来的“宕帐”，

A close reckoning showed that, apart from the capital of Fukang Bank, which remained entirely a debt, he was another ten thousand taels or so short.
细看一算，除了阜康钱庄的本钱，依旧是一整笔债务以外，还有万把银子的亏空。

Ten thousand taels in itself did not worry him.
万把银子在他当然不必发愁，

What troubled him was that so much thought and effort should end in a deficit.
要愁的是这样子费心费力，到头来还闹了一笔亏空，

If that was how he meant to build a business, was he not climbing a tree to catch fish?
则所谓“创业”也者，岂非缘木求鱼？

It ought not to have worked out this way.
照道理不应该如此！

There must be a reason, some fault in his own methods.
落到这样的地步，总有个道理在内，当然是自己的做法有了毛病。

Until he found it, he could neither eat nor sleep in peace.
这个毛病不找出来，令人寝食难安。

Although he had been awake all night and his head was heavy, a peculiar nervous excitement kept him from wanting to go to bed.
为此，他虽然一整夜未睡，脑子里昏昏沉沉地，但精神有种异样的亢奋，怎么样也不想上床。


Towards noon, Gu Yingchun and Liu Bucai came together.
到了快中午时，古应春和刘不才相偕来访，

The moment Gu saw him, he exclaimed, “Little Uncle, you look dreadful!
一见了面，古应春失声说道：“小爷叔，你的气色好难看！

Are you ill?”
是不是病了？”

Liu had once run a pharmacy and could diagnose such ordinary ailments as colds and fevers.
刘不才开过药店，对于伤风发烧之类的毛病，也能诊察，

He felt Hu’s forehead, asked him to put out his tongue, examined its coating, and gave a very accurate diagnosis.
当时伸手一探他的额头，又叫他伸舌头出来看了舌苔，很准确地作了判断：

“Too little sleep and too much mental exertion.
“睡得太少，用心过度，

Deficiency heat is rising.
是虚火上升。

Eat a good meal and have a comfortable sleep, and you will feel yourself again.”
好好吃一顿，舒舒服服睡一觉，精神马上就好了。”

“Exactly.”
“一点不错。”

Hu deliberately found a reason to send him away.
胡雪岩有意将他遣开：

“Please arrange for me to meet Pang somewhere this evening.
“请你替我去约一约庞二，晚上在哪里叙一叙。

Come and find me at Yudechi Bathhouse at four or five.”
回头四、五点钟，你到浴德池来找我。”

Once Liu had left, Hu brought out the individual statements he had calculated beforehand and handed Gu his.
等刘不才一走，胡雪岩将预先一张张计算好的单子，取了出来，捡出古应春的一张交了给他，

According to Hu’s reckoning, Gu was entitled to a profit of more than fifteen thousand taels.
照胡雪岩的算法，古应春应该分一万五千多银子的盈余。

Gu glanced through it and pushed it back. “Little Uncle, first, you have allotted me too much.
“小爷叔！”古应春略看了一看，将单子推了回去，“第一，你分得我多了，

Second, let us make no distribution now.
第二，现在不要分，

We shall go on working together.
我们仍旧在一起做，

What matters is to discuss how we should proceed.”
商量商量以后怎么个做法，才是正经。”

“That is precisely what I do not know!” Hu blurted out, frowning and shaking his head repeatedly.
胡雪岩脱口答道：“我正就是不晓得以后怎么个做法？”接着便皱起了眉不断摇头。

His strange manner startled Gu.
这态度很奇怪，古应春大为惊疑，

With some difficulty, he said, “Little Uncle, you seem to have some trouble you find hard to speak about.
“小爷叔！”他很吃力地说，“你好象有啥难言之隐似地。

We are all friends.
大家自己人，

Tell me what you need.
你尽吩咐，

If there is anything you cannot settle, leave my share out of the reckoning.”
有啥‘摆不平’，我的一份不必计算在内。”


Deeply moved, Hu answered frankly, “Brother Yingchun, I have gained nothing—except the loyalty and affection of friends, which a fortune could never buy.”
“应春兄！”胡雪岩相当感动，率直答道：“我一无所得，就是朋友的情分义气，千金不换。”

“Worth more than any fortune!
“岂止于千金不换？

But do not say you have gained nothing, Little Uncle.
小爷叔，你不要说一无所得，

To my mind you have gained a great deal.
在我看，所得正多。

Take Zhu Funian alone.
不说别的，只说朱福年好了，

Pang has something of a pampered young gentleman’s temper and sometimes speaks without sparing anyone’s feelings, but he is generous to his staff in a way few employers are.
庞二虽有些大少爷的脾气，有时讲话不给人留情面，到底御下宽厚，非别的东家好比，

可是朱福年还是有二心，

Only when he encountered you did an enemy become a friend and submit completely.
只有遇到小爷叔你，化敌为友，服服帖帖，

That is your great ability and your great capital.”
这就是你的大本事，也就是你的大本钱。

These observations went to the heart of the matter; they were far more than empty compliments.
“由于说得中肯，不是一般泛泛的恭维可比，

Hu was greatly encouraged.
所以胡雪岩听了这几句话，深受鼓舞，

“老古，”他便索性问道：“你直言谈相，

看我做生意有啥毛病要改？”


“I would not call them faults.
“毛病是谈不到。

这个‘勤’字照我讲，应该当做敬业的敬，

“古应春又说，”人的精力到底有限，你经手的事情到底太多了，

Hu nodded throughout.
“他一路说，胡雪岩一路点头，

“In summer the fashionable thing is a se-bai Western dinner: cold, lightly flavoured food, raw or only half cooked.

“那就算了。

We had better…”
还是……”

“We had better eat at my place.
“还是到我这里去吃饭吧！

Seventh Sister has returned to simpler ways.

今天在做粉蒸鸡，还有你们西湖上的莼菜……”

“Say no more!”
“你不要再说了。”

Hu swallowed.
胡雪岩咽了口唾沫答道，

“If you go on, I shall start drooling.”

汗水蒸润，她那张银盆似的脸，和两条藕也似的手臂，格外显得红白分明，

“我饿了！”胡雪岩老实答说，

回头慢慢吃酒。”

等一碗鸡汤火腿笋干米粉下肚，接着便摆桌子喝酒，

恰好尤五也到了，胡雪岩越有兴致。

“这话就是很难说了。”尤五答说，

Hu turned to Gu.

Hu nodded, recalling the advice Gu had given him that morning.

“Thanks to him and his entire family!”
“谢谢一家门！”

The Seventh Lady curled her lip.
七姑奶奶撇着嘴说，

“Little Uncle, he is a bungling strategist.
“小爷叔，他是狗头军师，

Do not listen to him.”
你不要听他的话。”

Gu disagreed but dared not argue with her.
古应春不服气，但也不敢跟她争辩，

He merely said, “Little Uncle, ‘Take care never to heed a woman’s words.’”
只说：“小爷叔，‘妇人之言，慎不可听’。”

“What do you mean, ‘a woman’s words’?” she retorted instantly.
“啥叫‘妇人之言’？”七姑奶奶的反应快得很，

“Surely the larger your sphere of activity, the better.
“场面总是越大越好。

By your reasoning, a man should refuse to be emperor because he would have too many different affairs to manage!”
照你的说法，有皇帝做也不要做了，因为管的事太多太杂？

That left Gu without an answer.
“一句话驳得古应春哑口无言，

He shook his head and murmured, “Eighteen varieties of crooked reasoning.”
摇摇头轻轻说了句：“歪理十八条。”

Hu was amused by Gu’s helpless embarrassment.
胡雪岩看他那无奈七姑奶奶之何的尴尬神态，未免好笑，

You, however, who usually took a dim view of his precious younger sister, now supported her.
但一向不以他那个“宝贝妹子”为然的尤五，却帮着她说话：

“What Seventh Sister says is not altogether unreasonable.
“阿七说的倒也不是歪理。

There is no harm in having many affairs, provided there are people to manage them.
事情不怕多，要有人管，

Being emperor is easy; the difficulty is finding a good chief minister.
皇帝好做，难的是用不着一个好宰相。

Little Uncle, I think Old Gu is right, but Seventh Sister’s comparison also makes sense.
小爷叔，我想，考古的话也不错，阿七的比喻也有道理，

You are a clever man.
你是聪明人，

Think over what they have both said and work out a plan.”
不妨拿他们两个人的话好好想一想，作一番打算。

“Yes.”
““是的！”

Hu nodded gravely.
胡雪岩深深点头。

As he ate, drank, and chatted, he kept turning the matter over.
于是他一面吃喝闲谈，一面在心中盘算，

By the time he had enjoyed enough wine and food, his plan was largely settled.
等酒醉饭饱，他的盘算也大致停当了。


“Brother Five, Old Gu,” he began, “first let us divide up the accounts…”
“五哥，老古！”他说，“我们先把帐分了……”

“There is no need!” You interrupted.
“不必分！”尤五抢着说，

His view was the same as Gu’s: the original capital and the profits should remain entirely at Hu’s disposal, to earn profit upon profit.
他的意思跟古应春一样，主张就原来的资本和盈余，听候胡雪岩全权运用，能够“利上滚利”。

“I understand,” Hu said.
“我懂你们的意思。”胡雪岩说，

“But I want to make a fresh start, with several businesses managed separately by different people, while I take overall charge.
“我要重起炉灶，做几样事业，大家分开来管，我只抓个总。

Like an emperor, I shall have ministers handling their own affairs without needing to do everything myself.”
就好比做皇帝一样，要宰相大臣分开来办事，用不着我亲自下手。”

All three listeners nodded their agreement.
“嗯，嗯！”在座的三个人，不约而同地颔首表示同意。

“First comes banking.
“第一样是钱庄，

That is my foundation, and I know the trade, so I shall probably still have to attend to it myself.
这方面是我的根本，我也内行，恐怕还是要亲自下手。

Second comes silk.
第二样是丝，

In Huzhou I shall put Chen Shilong in charge; in Shanghai, Old Gu.”
在湖州，我交给陈世龙，在上海，我交给老古。

“Good,” Gu replied.
““好的！”古应春说，

“I shall accept that responsibility without standing on ceremony.
“我当仁不让，无需客气。

Later we can also export tea and tung oil.
将来茶叶、桐油也好做洋庄，

We can discuss those in due course.”
慢慢儿再说。”

“All our exports will be in your hands.
“将来销洋庄都归你一手担当。

I have thought about tea and tung oil too.
茶叶、桐油我也想过，

If you think they are worth doing, I shall support you.
只要你认为可以做，我无不赞成。

But the new silk will soon be on the market, so you must begin planning at once.
不过眼前新丝就要上市了，所以要请你赶紧筹划，

Concentrate entirely on that and leave everything else alone.
专心一致，百事不管。

Except…”
不过……”

He glanced at the Seventh Lady and stopped with a smile.
胡雪岩看一看七姑奶奶，笑笑不再说下去。


There was plainly something behind the remark, and the Seventh Lady could not help asking, “Except what, Little Uncle?”
这大有皮里阳秋的意味，七姑奶奶免不了要问：“小爷叔，不过什么？”

“Except,” Hu answered, laughing, “that however much you leave everything else alone, your marriage must be attended to.
“不过，”胡雪岩笑道，“百事不管，你们的终身大事是非管不可的。

The same goes for me.
我也是这样子，

I can no longer concern myself with every other matter, but I must see this one through for you.
别样闲事不能再管，你的这桩大事，非效劳到底不可。

With Brother Five here, let me say this as your chief matchmaker: choose a day and arrange the wedding.
当着五哥在这里，我做大媒的说一句，你们挑日子、办喜事，

The bride’s and bridegroom’s families can discuss it directly without having me carry messages between them.
乾坤两宅，自己商量，不必我来传话。

I shall bring the Gu family’s old clan head round.
古家老族长那里的归我疏通，

I am certain it can be done, so set your minds at rest.”
一定不会办不通，你们放心好了。

“Yes,” You said, nodding.
““是的。”尤五点点头说，

“We must have a proper talk about it in the next few days.
“这件事，我就这几天要好好谈一谈。

But let us leave it for now.
现在且不去说它，

Go on with your plans, Little Uncle.”
小爷叔你再讲你的打算。”


“I also intend to start two other businesses: a pawnshop and a pharmacy.
“我还打算办两样事业，一样是典当，一样是药店。

I shall ask Third Master Liu to run the pharmacy.
药店请刘三爷来做，

As for the pawnshop, I want to speak to Pang and ask for Zhu Funian’s help.”
典当，我想跟庞二谈一谈，请朱福年帮我的忙。”

You and Gu offered neither approval nor objection.
对他的这番打算，尤五和古应春默然不置可否，

Their silence meant that they did not agree.
这意思就是不以为然，

Gu thought Hu should not enter trades he did not understand.
在古应春觉得他不宜做此自己不懂的事业，

Liu Bucai’s temperament was also ill suited to the hard work of establishing a new business.
而刘不才的本性，也不宜于苦干创业，

As for Zhu, they had known him only a short time.
朱福年则相交未几，

Though they might now say of him, “The southern barbarians will never rebel again,” his actual abilities were still uncertain.
虽说“南蛮不复反矣”，但他究竟有几许本事，尚未明了，

How could such important responsibilities be entrusted to him so readily?
何以轻付以重任？

You had similar thoughts and another doubt besides, which he expressed frankly.
尤五也略有这样的想法，此外他还有疑虑，率直问道：

“Little Uncle, banking needs a great deal of capital, and so does silk.
“小爷叔，一样钱庄，一样丝，都是大本钱，

Where will you find the means to open a pawnshop and a pharmacy as well?”
你哪里还有余力开当铺、开药店？”

“Brother Five has put his finger on the point,” Hu said eagerly.
“五哥说到要害上来了。”胡雪岩很起劲地，

“Naturally I have made plans.”
“自然我都有打算。”

His plan was to use his reputation and ability to enlist other people’s help.
胡雪岩的打算，是凭他的信誉、本领，因人成事。

Pang had promised support for a Fukang branch, while the silk business would continue to be financed by pooled shares.
阜康设分号，是庞二有过承诺，愿意支持的，做丝生意，仍旧是大家集股。

For the pawnshop’s capital, he had his eye on Pan Shuya and the other wealthy young gentlemen of Suzhou.
开典当的本钱，他看中了苏州潘叔雅那班富家公子，

For the pharmacy, he intended to find a way through official circles in Jiangsu and Zhejiang.
开药店则预备在江浙官场上动脑筋。


“Let me explain why I want a pawnshop and a pharmacy.
“我再说，为啥要开典当、开药店？

Neither will make a profit immediately.
这两样事业，一时都无利可图，

I mean them entirely for the public good.
完全是为了公益，

A pawnshop will help poor people.
我开典当是为方便穷人。

Quite a few people already know the name Hu Xueyan, but they are all officials and businessmen.
胡雪岩三个字，晓得的人，也不算少了，但只有做官的和做生意的晓得，

In future I want ordinary people to know it too.
我以后要让老百姓都晓得，

When they hear my name, I want them to say, ‘That is a decent man!’
提起胡雪岩，说一声：这个人不错！

Then my businesses will keep growing.
事业就会越做越大。

That is why I want a pharmacy: it is the best way to make a name.
为此，我要开药店，这是扬名的最好办法。

Besides, troubled times bring illness, and great upheavals are always followed by epidemics.
再说，乱世多病痛，大乱之后，必有瘟疫，

A pharmacy will benefit others and ourselves.
将来药店的生意，利人利己，

It is one of the best businesses there is.”
是一等一的好事业。

The Seventh Lady was the first to express her admiration.
“听得这一说，七姑奶奶首先就钦佩不止，

“Listen to him,” she told Gu, with a touch of admonition.
“你听听，”她带点教训意味地对古应春说：

“That is real foresight!
“小爷叔的眼光，才真叫眼光！

Little Uncle is already looking beyond the war.
看到大乱以后了。

You ought to learn from him.”
你要学学小爷叔。”

“I am learning from him.”
“本来就跟小爷叔在学。”

Gu turned to Hu.
古应春转脸问道，

“You said the pharmacy’s capital would come from public funds.
“小爷叔，你说开药店的本钱，出在公家，

How would that work?”
是怎么个办法？”

“Through our connections.
“ 这要靠关系了。

The army must have medicines.
军营里自然要用药，

I shall discuss it with Third Master Liu and get a couple of good prescriptions, then prepare them properly with genuine ingredients—wound medicine, for instance, Zhuge Marching Powder, or Biwen Pills.
我要跟刘三爷商量，弄两张好方子，真材实料修合起来，譬如刀伤药、诸葛行军散、辟瘟丹之类，

They must work with a single dose and stand apart from ordinary remedies.
要一服见效，与众不同。

Then we can ask the various army supply offices to place advance orders and use their payments as capital.”
这样子就好禀请各路粮台，先定我们多少，领下价款来做本钱。”

The Seventh Lady was delighted. “Really!
“真是！”七姑奶奶听得眉飞色舞，

I do not believe there is anything in the world Little Uncle cannot find a way to do!”
“我看世界上，没有小爷叔没有办法的事！”

Hu was a little alarmed. “Seventh Sister, that is far too much praise.
“七姐，”胡雪岩有些惶恐，“这话捧得我太过分了。

A single man’s strength is limited.
一个人的力量到底有限，

Even with three heads and six arms, how much could he do?
就算三头六臂，也办得了多少事？

Great undertakings depend entirely on people working together.
要成大事，全靠和衷共济，

You might say I have nothing except friends.
说起来我一无所有，有的只是朋友。

Only if you treat your friends’ affairs as your own will they treat yours as theirs.
要拿朋友的事当自己的事，朋友才会拿你的事当自己的事。

Without friends, the greatest ability in the world will still get you nowhere.”
没有朋友，就是有天大的本事，也还是没有办法。”


“You have struck the nail on the head, Little Uncle,” You said at once.
“小爷叔这话一针见血，”尤五紧接着他的话说，

“All our brothers regard you as a good friend.
“我们那一伙弟兄，都当小爷叔好朋友，

They are waiting for your orders.”
现在等着你老发号施令呢！”

“Not so fast!
“你别忙！

I promised to find a livelihood for you, and I shall keep that promise.
我答应替你们筹出一条生路来，一定要做到，

To tell you the truth, opening that path is my most urgent business.
说句老实话，我眼前第一件大事，就是替你们去开路，

I already have the outline of a plan…”
大致的办法，我已经有了……”

This was another major undertaking affecting both the people’s livelihood and the country’s economy.
这是胡雪岩另一项与民生国计有关的大事业，

Hu intended to use the grain-transport brotherhoods’ manpower, influence along the waterways, and existing boats to carry government and private cargo.
他准备利用漕帮的人力、水路上的势力跟现成的船只，承揽公私货运，

At the same time, he would use the Songjiang brotherhood’s Tongyu Rice Shop as the basis for a large grain business.
同时以松江漕帮的通裕米行为基础，大规模贩卖粮食。

“There are several reasons why rice costs as much as pearls and firewood as much as cassia in wartime.
“乱世米珠薪桂，原因有好多，

We must examine them one by one.
要一样样去考究。

Fighting leaves fields untended and reduces production: that is one reason.
兵荒马乱，田地荒了，出产少了，当然是一个原因，

Another is the difficulty of transport.
再有一个原因是交通不便，

How wasteful it is when places have rice but cannot move it out or sell it!
眼看有米的地方因运不出，卖不掉，多么可惜！

But there is something more wasteful still: grain destroyed outright.
这还不算，最可惜的是糟蹋掉了！

Some households have large stores, only to see them burned in the fighting.
有些人家积存了好多粮食，但打起仗来，烧得光光，

Or autumn comes and the war draws near: rice stands ready in the fields, but there is no one to cut it, and it is wasted.
或者秋收到了，战事迫近，有稻无人割，白白作践。

If we could prevent that waste, think what a benefit it would be to the public and to ourselves!”
能够想办法不糟蹋，你们想，于公于私多么好！”

“That makes sense!”
“有道理！”

You sprang up.
尤五矍然而起，

“I understand the first two reasons, but I have never heard this last point made before.
“前面两个原因，我懂，后面说的这一层道理，我还是第一次听到。

Tell me, Little Uncle, how can we prevent the waste?”
倒要请教小爷叔，怎么样才能不糟蹋？”

“By watching the situation.
“这就要看局势了。

We must see clearly and act quickly.
眼要明，手要快，

When a place is becoming unsafe, we send plenty of boats and get its stored grain out in time.
看啥地方快靠不住了，我们多调船过去，拿存粮抢运出去。

Any rice ready for cutting must be harvested quickly too.
能割的稻子，也要抢着割下来。”

Of course we shall need official help: troops to protect us, perhaps, or special accommodation at the checkpoints.
胡雪岩又说：“这当然要官府帮忙，或者派兵保护，或者关卡上格外通融，

Once it is all arranged, Brother Five, you will have both the cooperation of your people and the advantages of the waterways.
只要说好了，五哥，你们将来人和、地利都具备，

No one else could match the business.”
是独门生意。”


You and Gu were silent.
尤五和古应春都不作声，

They considered Hu’s words carefully before grasping the broad outline of his method.
两个人将胡雪岩的话，细细体味了一会，才大致懂得了他的做法。

It was indeed an excellent business that no one else could wrest from them, but carrying it out would not be easy.
这确是一项别人所抢不去的好生意，但是做起来不容易。

“You know how officials are, Little Uncle.
“官场的情形，小爷叔你晓得的，

They may not be willing to help.”
未见得肯帮我们的忙。”

“They certainly will!
“一定肯！

It all depends on how the case is put.
只看怎样说法？

There is another reason: war requires two things, troops and grain—enough food and enough soldiers.
其中还有个道理：打仗两件事，一是兵，二是粮，叫做足食足兵。

There is only so much grain, and both sides are in the same area.
粮食就这么多，双方又是在一块地方，

Every measure we gain is one the Taipings lose.
我们多出一分粮食，长毛就少一分粮食，

The effect on both sides is considerable.
一进一出，关系不轻。

Once I explain that, the authorities are bound to approve.”
所以，我去一说这层道理，上头一定会赞成。”

“Exactly!” You asked, “Whom do you intend to speak to?
“ 对！”尤五问道：“小爷叔你预备跟哪个去说？

Prefect Wang?”
王大老爷？”

“Yes, I shall speak to him first.
“是的。我先跟他去说。

There is no time to lose.
事不宜迟，

I leave tomorrow!
明天我就走！

I have plenty of other ways to deal with the Taipings, such as tightening the suppression of smuggling and cutting off their supplies of everyday necessities.”
我还有好多法子可以治长毛，譬如加紧缉私，断绝他们的日用百物的供应之类。”

Hu stood up and waved his hand with enthusiasm.
胡雪岩站起身来，很起劲地挥着手：

“Small traders adapt themselves to circumstances.
“做小生意迁就局势，

A businessman on a large scale first helps the government change the circumstances.
做大生意先帮公家拿局势扭过来。

Once the overall situation improves, our business will find its way.
大局好转，我们的生意就自然有办法。

Just wait.
你们等着，

When I reach Hangzhou, I shall make a fresh start and launch undertakings that will really stir the world.”
看我到了杭州，重起炉灶，另有一番轰轰烈烈的事业。”


End of this part.

By this point, Hu Xueyan has relied on official support, made use of the grain-transport brotherhoods, established connections with foreign merchants and compradors, recruited gamblers, and cultivated wealthy businessmen.
至此，胡雪岩依靠官府、利用漕帮、结识外商买办、网罗赌徒、拉拢富商，

He has at last risen to prosperity in an age of war, establishing himself in Shanghai and Hangzhou.
终于发迹于战乱的年代，在上海、杭州立足。

此后，高阳先生在《红顶商人》和《灯火楼台》等书中，依然以其丰富的历史知识和深厚的写作功力，铺叙了胡雪岩自上海、杭州发迹后，资财逐渐过于鼎盛之势，而最终归于破产的过程。

书中说道，身为江浙大贾的胡雪岩，运饷粮助清军解杭州之围，

After the city falls, he turns to Zuo Zongtang as his patron, raising military funds, buying and transporting modern Western guns and artillery, borrowing immense sums from foreign merchants to support Zuo’s western campaigns, and assisting in his Western-style enterprises.
城破，即转倚左宗棠为靠山，为其筹措军饷，购运西洋新式枪炮，借巨资于洋商，助左宗棠西征并协大办洋务；

Relying on official patronage, Hu then expands his power and comes to dominate commerce in Jiangsu and Zhejiang.
继而倚仗官府，扩充实力，操纵了江浙商业，

His reputation spreads throughout the southeast.
声名盛于东南，

He is eventually granted the insignia of the second official rank and the privileges of wearing a yellow riding jacket and riding a horse inside the Forbidden City, becoming the only merchant of the late Qing to wear the red-topped official hat.
直至身受二品顶戴，获赏穿黄马褂、紫禁城骑马的殊荣，赫赫然成为晚清唯一顶红顶子的商人；

Extravagant in the extreme and passionately fond of women, he builds splendid residences and gardens in Shanghai, Hangzhou, and elsewhere, surrounds himself with concubines, and delights in unrestrained debauchery.
胡雪岩穷奢极侈、酷好女色，在上海、杭州等地，营造多处富丽宅园，姬妾成群，荒淫无度，以为乐事，

For more than a decade, his wealth and influence reach extraordinary heights.
十数年间，财势鼎盛至极；

Yet Hu does not possess truly remarkable strategy or far-reaching ability.
不想胡雪岩非真有奇计雄略，

Unable to withstand the pressure of foreign merchants, his business gradually declines.
经不住洋商的排挤，商事日渐中落，

Prosperity turns to ruin, until he is bankrupt and his reputation destroyed.
由盛转衰，终至破产，身败名裂。

He thus becomes one of the singular figures of China’s modern history.
胡雪岩因而成为我国近代史中一奇特人物。


With a distinctive eye and an inspired pen, Gao Yang creates intricate plots, lifelike characters, fluent prose, and scenes of great scope, bringing a vivid picture of the turbulent society of the late Qing to the page.
高阳先生独具慧眼，落笔如神，情节曲折，人物如生，文字畅达，场景开阔，使一幅描绘动荡的晚清社会的画面，跃然纸上。
