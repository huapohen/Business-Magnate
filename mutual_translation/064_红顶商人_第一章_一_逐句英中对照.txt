胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant

CHAPTER ONE
第一章


“Your Excellency,” said the bodyguard, saluting Zeng Guofan, who was enjoying his customary game of go after dinner, “a dispatch officer from Zhejiang requests an audience.
『禀大帅，』戈什哈向正在『饭后一局棋』的曾国藩请个安说，『浙江的差官求见。

Will Your Excellency receive him?”
请大帅的示∶见是不见？』

Zeng was in the middle of a ko fight.
曾国藩正在打一个劫；

The survival of his entire southeastern quarter of the board depended on it; he could not afford to abandon the struggle.
这个劫关乎『东南半壁』的存亡，非打不可，

Yet in the end he put down his stones and rose.
然而他终于投子而起。

“Of course I must see him.
『没有不见之理。

Tell him to come in.”
叫他进来好了。』


The dispatch officer wore travelling clothes that were all but in rags.
那名差官穿着一身破破烂烂的行装；

It was late in the ninth month, long past the time for changing to a winter hat, yet he still wore a summer one.
九月底的天气，早该换戴暖帽了，而他仍是一顶凉帽，

Its bright blue finial showed that he was a military officer of the third rank.
顶戴是亮蓝顶子，可知是个三品武官。

“You Tianyong, assistant brigade commander of the Zhejiang governor’s own troops, pays his respects to Your Excellency.”
『浙江抚标参将游天勇，给大帅请安。』

He hurried forward a couple of paces, knelt and kowtowed.
那游天勇抢上两步，跪下去磕头，

A great hole in the back of his coat exposed a patch of yellow-brown skin.
背上衣服破了个大洞，露出又黄又黑的一块皮肉。

“Get up, get up!”
『起来，起来！』

His face looked as though it had never been washed, and Zeng could scarcely bear the sight.
曾国藩看他那张脸，仿佛从未洗过似的；内心老大不忍，

“Take Commander You somewhere to rest,” he told the bodyguard.
便吩咐戈什哈说，『先带游参将去息一息，

“Give him a meal, then ask him back to speak with me.”
吃了饭再请过来说话。』

“If I may report to Your Excellency,” You interrupted, “Governor Wang ordered me to reach Anqing within a fixed time and deliver these dispatches.
『回大帅的话，』游天勇抢着说道∶『卑职奉敝省王抚台之命，限期赶到安庆，投递公文，

I beg Your Excellency to read them first.”
请大帅先过目。』

“Very well, give them to me.
『好，好！你给我。

Get up and speak.”
你起来说话！』

“Thank you, Your Excellency.”
『谢大帅！』


You stood and retreated a couple of paces.
游天勇站起身来，略略退后两步；

Turning a little sideways, he opened his coat and drew out an oilpaper packet he had carried against his skin.
微侧着身子，解开衣襟，取出一个贴肉而藏的油纸包，

It was thick and heavy, evidently containing more than a letter of a few sheets.
厚甸甸地，似乎里面装的不止是几张纸的一封信。

The oilpaper had split, but the large envelope within, a foot or so in length, remained as clean and sound as new.
那油纸已经破裂，但解开来看，里面的一个尺把长的大信封却完好如新；

The moment Zeng took it, he knew it held cloth or silk, not paper.
曾国藩接到手里，便发觉里面装的不是纸，是一幅布或绸。

He turned it over to read the address: “For the personal and exclusive attention of Governor-General Zeng at Headquarters, Anqing.”
翻过来先看信面，写的是∶『专呈安庆大营曾制台亲钧启。』

Below stood: “Respectfully sealed in his own hand by Wang Youling.”
下面署明∶『王有龄亲笔谨缄。』

He broke it open.
再拆开来，

As he had expected, it contained a folded square of snow-white Hangzhou silk.
果不其然，是一方折叠着的雪白杭纺；

With an idle flick of his hand he shook it out, then started.
信手一抖，便是一惊，

The writing was black tinged with red, and several purplish-red spots were plainly bloodstains.
字迹黑中带红；还有数处紫红斑点，一望而知是血迹——

Wang Youling had written in blood just four characters, each as large as a great bowl: “We await your mighty reinforcements.”
王有龄和血所书的，只有四个海碗大的字∶『鹄候大援』；

Beneath them ran a line in smaller characters: “Wang Youling, Governor of Zhejiang, respectfully leads the millions of officials and people of this province in a hundred prostrations, and begs you with tears.”
另有一行小字∶『浙江巡抚王有龄谨率全省数百万官民百拜泣求。』

Zeng had cultivated himself all his life towards the ideal of an unshaken mind.
曾国藩平主修养，以『不动心』三字为归趋；

Now, however, his expression changed despite himself.
而此时不能不色变了。


The civil advisers and military officers at headquarters were all visibly moved by this startling, extraordinary appeal.
大营中的幕友材官，见了这幅惊心动魄∶别具一格的求援书，亦无不动容，

They watched Zeng, waiting to see what he would do.
注视着曾国藩，要看他如何处置？

Slowly he rolled up the silk.
曾国藩徐徐卷起那幅杭纺，

“You have had a hard and arduous journey,” he told You.
向游天勇说道∶『你一路奔波，风尘劳苦，

“Go and rest first.”
且先休息。』

“Thank you, Your Excellency,” You replied solemnly.
『是，多谢大帅。』游天勇肃然答说∶『卑职得见大帅，

“Nothing could comfort me more than to have reached you.
比什么都安慰；

All my hardships are forgotten now.
种种苦楚，这会都记不起来了。

I beg only that Your Excellency send troops at once.”
只求大帅早早发兵。』

“I have my own plans.”
『我自有道理。』

Since You would not leave to rest, Zeng began asking about Zhejiang.
看他不愿休息，曾国藩便问他浙江的情形，

“When did you set out?”
『你是哪天动身的？』

“I left Hangzhou on the twentieth of the ninth month.
『卑职是九月二十从杭州动身的，

Yuhang had already fallen.
那时余杭已经沦陷。』游天勇答道，

I imagine Hangzhou is under siege by now.”
『看样子，现在杭州已经被围。』

“Hangzhou has very strong walls and moats.
『杭州的城池很坚固。

If I remember the Comprehensive Gazetteer of the Empire correctly, it has ten gates.”
我记得「一统志」一说，是十个城门。』

Zeng recited, “‘Awaiting the Tide’, one hears the ‘Clear Waves’ sound; ‘Surging Gold’ and ‘Qiantang’ make ‘Great Peace’ secure.
曾国藩念道∶『「候潮」听得「清波」响，「涌金」「钱塘」定「太平」。

In the reign of Emperor Renzong of the Song, the recluse Xu Zhonghui wished that his descendants might never leave Qiantang.
宋仁宗的时候，处士徐仲晦，愿子孙世世不离钱塘，

He said it would always be spared the ravages of war.
说是永无兵燹之灾。

I should think Hangzhou could hold out.”
想来杭州可以守得住。』

You had heard the verse: it strung together the names of Hangzhou’s ten gates, such as Houchao, Awaiting the Tide, and Qingbo, Clear Waves.
他念的那句诗，游天勇倒是听过，是拿杭州的十个城门，候潮门、清波门等等缀成诗成；

But this talk of some man from the Song dynasty meant nothing to him.
至于什么宋朝人的话，他就莫名其妙了。

He understood only that, by saying Hangzhou could hold out, Zeng seemed to have no intention of sending troops.
只是听语气，说杭州守得住便无发兵之意，

Desperately anxious, he had to speak.
游天勇大为着急，不能不说话。


“Hangzhou’s walls are strong, certainly.
『杭州的城坚固，倒是不错。

But the city cannot hold out for long.”
不过守不长久的。』

“Oh?”
『喔，』

Zeng spread his fingers and combed them through his beard. “Why is that?
曾国藩揸开五指，抓梳着胡须问∶『这是什么道理？

Tell me.”
你倒说来我听听。』『杭州存粮不足——。』

“Hangzhou has insufficient stores of grain—” Prosperous though Hangzhou was said to be, its households did not keep large stocks of rice.
杭州虽称富足，但从无积米之家。

The rice market of western Zhejiang was at Chang’an, a hundred li northeast of the city.
浙西米市在杭州东北方一百里处的长安镇；

Each year the landlords retained only enough of the grain collected as rent to feed their own families, sending the rest to Chang’an to await a favourable price.
杭州的地主，每年所收租谷，除了留下一家食米之外，都运到长安镇待价而沽，

Thus the city had not even ten days’ provisions.
所以城里无十日之粮。

That spring and summer, in the lean months before the harvest, rice prices had soared.
这年春夏，青黄不接之际，米价大涨；

Hangzhou had been captured and stripped bare in the second month of the previous year, and the inhabitants who remained were struggling to survive.
而杭州经过上年二月间的一番沦陷，劫掠一空，留下来的百姓，艰苦度日，

Where could they find money to buy and store grain?
哪里来的钱购粮存贮？

They had meant to lay in proper reserves when the new crop came in, but no one had foreseen the army’s headlong collapse.
本来是想等新谷登场，好好作一番储粮的打算，谁知兵败如山，

The grain lying in abundance across the fields had all fallen to the Taiping forces.
累累满野，都便宜了太平军。

Zeng sighed deeply.
『唉！』曾国藩深深叹息，

“If only Zhang Yuliang and Li Dingtai in eastern Zhejiang had been willing to fight with all their strength and hold them off for a while!” Then he asked, “Ample provisions are the first requirement in defending a city.
『在浙东的张玉良、李定太，如果肯拼命抵挡一阵就好了。』他接着又问，『守城最要紧的是粮食丰足。

Has Governor Wang done nothing?”
王抚台难道就不想办法？』


“Governor Wang has been doing everything he can.
『王抚台也在极力想办法，

Last year he issued a proclamation inviting merchants to buy supplies, promising exemption from likin duties wherever they passed.
去年就出告示，招商采买，答应所过地方，免抽厘税。

But the roads were unsafe, and the rice merchants dared not come.
不过路上不平靖，米商都不敢来。』游天勇说，

When I left, I heard that he intended to ask Intendant Hu to go to Shanghai to purchase food and arms.
『卑职动身的时候，听说王抚台预备请胡道台到上海去采办粮食军火，

I do not know whether they have arrived.”
也不知运到了没有？』

“Which Intendant Hu?” Zeng asked.
『哪个胡道台？』曾国藩问，

“Hu Yuanbo?”
『是胡元博吗？』

“No, Hu Xueyan.”
『不是。是胡雪岩。』

“Ah, yes, him!
『喔，喔，是他！

I hear he is a very capable man.”
听说他非常能干？』

“He is indeed.
『是！胡道台很能干的；

All the great local gentlemen in Hangzhou have either fled or gone into hiding.
杭州城里，大绅士逃的逃，躲的躲，

It is entirely through Intendant Hu’s efforts to borrow grain and raise contributions that the imperial troops have been kept going.”
全靠胡道台出面，借粮借捐维持官军。』

Zeng nodded and considered Hangzhou’s situation for a moment.
曾国藩点点头，默想了一下杭州的形势，

“What about the south bank of the Qiantang?
随又问道∶『钱塘江南岸呢？

Zhejiang’s military funds now come from Ningbo and Shaoxing.
现在浙江的饷源在宁绍；

That route is still open, I take it?”
这条路线是畅通的吧？』

“Yes.
『是。

We depend entirely on it.
全靠这条路。

But—” “Go on.
不过——。』『你说！

Why should you hesitate?”
有什么碍口的？』


“If I may report to Your Excellency, once one crosses the Qiantang, Xiaoshan, Shaoxing and Ningbo are all under Commissioner Wang.
『回大帅的话，过钱塘江，萧山、绍兴、宁波一带，都归王大臣管；

He and Governor Wang do not get on.
他跟王抚台不和。

Things—” You shook his head slightly, unable to continue.
事情——。『游天勇略微摇一摇头，说不下去了。

Commissioner Wang was Wang Lüqian, the imperially appointed commissioner for militia organisation.
王大臣是指钦命团练大臣王履谦。

Zeng knew him well and had received a letter from him complaining that Shaoxing and Ningbo prefectures raised a hundred thousand taels a month for the provincial capital, yet Wang Youling had not sent a single soldier across the river.
曾国藩亦深知其人，并且曾接到他来信诉苦，说绍兴、宁波两府，每月筹饷十万两银子解送省城；而王有龄未发一卒渡江。

From what You was now saying, the truth seemed otherwise.
现在听游天勇的话，似乎事实并非如此。

But whoever was right or wrong, discord among commanders and hostility between soldiers and civilians could bode no good.
但不论谁是谁非，将帅不和，兵民相仇，总不是好兆。

The situation in Zhejiang was profoundly discouraging.
浙江的局势，真是令人灰心。


“Go and rest.”
『你下去休息。』

A man in Zeng’s position had no need to tell You what measures he proposed, much less explain his reasons.
以曾国藩的地位，若有所处置，自不须跟游天勇明说，更不必向他作解释，

He merely gave his orders. “Get a good night’s sleep.
只这样吩咐∶『你今晚上好好睡一觉，

Collect my reply tomorrow, then return to Hangzhou at once and report.
明来取了回信，即刻赶回杭州去复命。

I shall have someone prepare the dispatches, a horse and your travelling expenses.”
公文、马匹、盘缠，我会派人给你预备。』

“Yes, Your Excellency.”
『是！』

You rose and saluted.
游天勇站起身来请个安，

“Thank you, Your Excellency.”
『多谢大帅。』


The steamer plying between Shanghai and Anqing was the Siming, owned by the British firm of Swire.
跑上海、安庆的轮船，是英商太古公司的四明号，

Her comprador, Xiao Jiaji, came from a wealthy Shanghai family and had been born with a taste for seeking out the strange and unexplored.
船上的买办叫萧家骥，原是上海的富家子，生就一副喜欢搜奇探秘的性格，

Curiosity had first led him to study English under Gu Yingchun.
最初是因为好奇，拜了古应春做老师学英文。

Through his teacher’s wife, the Seventh Lady, he had come to know “Uncle” You Wu—the name he used in imitation of her child—and so acquired connections with the Grain-Boat Guild.
再由他的『师娘』七姑奶奶而认识了『舅舅』尤五——他跟着七姑奶奶的孩子这样叫，因而对漕帮也有了渊源。

Yet, like Hu Xueyan, he understood everything that went on inside its “threshold” while remaining an uninitiated outsider.
但是，他跟胡雪岸一样，是一个深懂『门槛』里的内幕，却是个在『门槛』外面的『空子』。

Zeng Guofan had sent Li Hongzhang with troops to relieve Shanghai, and the Siming had consequently made several trips to Anqing in succession.
为了曾国藩派李鸿章 领兵援沪，四明号接连跑了几趟安庆；

By the time the business was over, it was late autumn before Xiao could find time to visit Gu.
到得事毕，已在深秋，萧家骥方得抽空去看古应春。


Gu Yingchun was doing very well.
古应春很得意了，

His partnership with Hu in silk and tea had brought him a tidy fortune.
先跟胡雪岩合作丝茶生意，很发了点财；

When the worsening situation in Jiangsu and Zhejiang cut off supplies, he had turned to land.
及至江浙局势大变，丝茶来路中断，改行经营地皮，

Wealthy families seeking refuge poured into the foreign settlements at Shanghai, driving land prices higher and higher and swelling his profits.
由于逃难的富室大族，纷纷涌向上海租界，地价大涨特涨，越发财源茂盛。

Being on the spot also made it easy to choose a site, engage workmen and buy materials, so he had built an exquisite residence on the newly opened Second Road.
而且近水楼台，选地鸠工购料都方便，所以在新辟的二马路上，造了一所极精致的住宅；

His family of three—the Seventh Lady had borne him a son—employed more than ten servants.
一家三口——七姑奶奶生了个儿子；倒用了上十口的下人。

Teacher and pupil had always been close.
他们师弟的感情一向深厚，

Naturally, they began with easy conversation about Xiao’s travels.
自然先谈些旅途情况之类的闲话。

They had exchanged only a few remarks when they heard the Seventh Lady’s voice; then she appeared before them.
说不到几句，听得七姑奶奶的声音；接着便出现在他们面前，

Her broad, round face was heavily made up, the white exceptionally white and the red exceptionally red.
浓妆艳抹，一张银盆大脸，白的格外白，红的格外红，

Her dazzling jewellery made her almost too brilliant to look at.
加以首饰炫耀，更令人不可逼视。

“Are you going out, Madam?”
『师娘要出门？』

Xiao rose to greet her.
萧家骥站起身来招呼。

“Yes.
『是啊，

Two relatives have come from far away, and I am taking them to see something of Shanghai.
有两个远道来的亲戚，去见见上海的市面。

We shall browse in the foreign shops and take a little air—”
逛逛洋行兜兜风——。』


“In this cold weather?” Gu interrupted, laughing. “You must be mad!”
『这么冷的天去兜风？』古应春打断她的话笑道∶『你在发疯！』

He delighted in picking holes in his wife’s expressions.
古应春就爱捉他妻子话中的漏洞，

Long accustomed to it, she ignored him and continued. “At noon I shall treat them to a foreign meal, and in the afternoon we shall see the Western circus.
七姑奶奶听惯了不理他，管自己往下说∶『中午请客人吃番菜；下午去看西洋马戏。

Nothing is settled for the evening.
晚上还没有定，

Shall we dine together?”
要不要在一起吃饭？』

“No need to arrange anything.
『不必了！

Come home for dinner.
晚上回家吃饭。

The crabs are good just now; I shall get a basket.”
这两天蟹好，我去弄一篓蟹来。』

“Yes!” she exclaimed with delight.
『对！』七姑奶奶大为高兴，

“We have not had a proper feast of crabs this year.” Then she sighed. “What a calamity!
『今年还没有好好吃过一顿蟹。』接着又叹口气∶『遭劫！

With all this war and turmoil, even the supply of crabs has been cut off.
兵荒马乱，蟹的来路都断了。

There is no pleasure in being alive these days.”
这个年头，做人真没味道。』

“There, there!
『好了，好了，

You should be content,” Gu said.
不要不知足了！』古应春说，

“You live in the foreign settlement, with no worries about food or clothing, yet you say life has no pleasure.
『你住在夷场上，不忧穿、不忧吃，还说做人没有味道；

What of the people trapped among the Longhairs?”
那末陷在长毛那里的人呢？』

“It is precisely because people are trapped among them, with no news coming through and no knowing whether they are alive or dead, that one cannot stop worrying.
『就为的有人陷在长毛那里，消息不通，生死不明；教人牵肠挂肚，

That is why I say there is no pleasure in life.”
所以说做人没有味道。』

Her face clouded.
说着，便是满脸不欢。

“We cannot attend to everything,” Gu said consolingly. “Go out and look around; take your minds off it.
『顾不得那么多了。』古应春用劝慰的语气说∶『你们去逛逛散散心；

Come back this evening for crabs.”
晚上回来吃蟹。』

She said no more, but went out with her head lowered.
七姑奶奶没有再说什么，低着头走了。


Gu, too, grew despondent.
古应春亦不免黯然，

“Things are very bad.”
『局势很坏。』

He shook his head.
他摇摇头，

“I fear Hangzhou may be finished within the next few days.”
『杭州只怕就在这几天完蛋。』

“And Mr Hu?” Xiao asked. “I wonder how he is managing there.”
『胡先生呢？』萧家骥问道∶『不晓得在杭州怎么样？』

“No letter has come.”
『没有信来。』

Two tears suddenly rolled down Gu’s face.
古应春忽然流下两滴眼泪，

“Such a good friend, and we can only watch him being trapped inside!
『这么一个好朋友，眼看他失陷在里面，

Who knows whether we shall ever see him again?
也不晓得将来还有没有见面的日子？

These last two nights, whenever your teacher’s wife and I have spoken of him, neither of us has slept till morning.”
这两天晚上跟你师娘谈起来，都是一整夜睡不着觉。』

“Heaven protects good men,” Xiao said to comfort him.
『吉人天相！』萧家骥劝慰他说，

“Nothing about Mr Hu—his features, his temperament or his conduct—suggests a man destined for disaster.
『我看胡先生，不管他的相貌、性情、行为，都不象是遭劫的人。

Besides, with his insight and ingenuity, how could he sit helpless in a beleaguered city, unable to think of anything?”
再说，以胡先生的眼光、心思，又哪里会坐困愁城，束手无策？』

These words helped a great deal.
这几句话很有用，

Gu thought for some time, then nodded. “I cannot see the signs of a short life in him either, however I look.”
古应春想了好一会，点点头说∶『我也怎么样都看不出他是短命相。』


After a meal at Gu’s house, teacher and pupil went out in the same carriage.
在古家吃了饭，师弟二人，同车而出；

Gu dropped Xiao at the shipping company, then went to his property firm and sent its senior outside clerk to buy crabs.
古应春将他送到了船公司，自己便到他的做地产的号子里，派『出店老司务』去买蟹；

His instructions were explicit: they must be good, whatever the price.
特为关照∶只要好，价钱不论。

With that authority, the task was easy.
有这一句话，事情就好办了。

The clerk was an able man.
那老事务也很能干，

He waited at the inland-waterway wharf until a boat from Jiaxing arrived with a dozen or more baskets of crabs.
到内河码头上等着，等到一只嘉兴来的船，载来十几篓蟹；

Quick of eye and hand, he laid hold of a good basket and refused to release it before discussing the price.
眼明手快，先把住一篓好的不放手，然后再谈价钱。

“Half a tael each, large or small.
『五钱银子一个，大小不论；

There are thirty-two in this basket.
这一篓三十二个，

I shall be especially generous and ask only fifteen taels.”
格外克己，算十五两银子。』

“Fifteen taels—and you call that generous?”
『十五两银子，还说克己？』

“Take them or leave them.
『要就要，不要拉倒。

You ought to understand: crabs are not dear in Jiaxing, but we have risked our lives bringing them all the way to Shanghai.
你要晓得，蟹在嘉兴不贵，这一路到上海，是拿性命换来的；

Surely that is worth half a tael each?”
难道不值五钱银子一个？』

He reached to reclaim his goods.
说着，就要来夺回他的货色。

The clerk would not let go, but neither would he pay the asking price.
老司务哪里肯放，但是也不能照数付价；

He took out twelve taels in silver and pressed them into the owner’s hand.
摸出十二两现银，塞到货主手里；

The man refused.
此人不肯接，

After much coaxing and noisy bargaining, they settled at fourteen taels.
软磨硬吵，十四两银子成交。


The Seventh Lady had just returned when the crabs reached the house.
将蟹送到古家，七姑奶奶刚好回家；

She took them out to look at them: golden-haired, purple-backed, extraordinarily large and strong.
拿蟹来看，只见金毛紫背，壮硕非凡，

Set upon a round Fujian lacquer table as smooth as a mirror, they braced themselves on their eight legs and scuttled imperiously in every direction.
取来放在光滑如镜的福建漆圆桌上，八足挺立，到处横行。

The sight made the clerk swallow.
那老司务看着，不由得就咽唾沫。

Kind-hearted by nature and skilled at putting people at their ease, she said at once, “You have bought too many.
七姑奶奶本性厚道，也会做人，当时便对老司务说，『买得多了，

Take a few back to the firm and share them with your colleagues.”
你拿几个带到号子里，跟同事分着尝尝。』

She lifted a string from the basket—five with pointed abdominal flaps and five with round ones, ten in all—and handed it to him.
说着便从篓子里拎了一串出来，恰好五尖五团，整整十个，就手递了过去。

He refused, but she insisted that everyone should have a taste, and he finally took them away.
老司务却不肯要，无奈七姑奶奶执意要大家分尝，只好带了回去。

She then went into the kitchen herself to direct the cook in steaming the crabs with perilla leaves.
然后亲自下厨，指挥厨子用紫苏蒸蟹。

Afterwards she opened a chest and brought out a set of silver implements: tiny pincers and hammers, exquisitely made.
接着又开箱子找出一套银餐具，小钳子、小钉锤，做得极其玲珑可爱。


They were eating with great animation when shifting shadows appeared outside, accompanied by voices and footsteps.
正在吃得热闹的当儿，只见人影幢幢，有人声、也有脚步声——

The Seventh Lady feared neither heaven nor earth, but such a commotion terrified her.
七姑奶奶天不怕、地不怕，就怕见这种情形，

Her limbs suddenly weakened and her face went white.
一下子吓得手足发软、脸色苍白；

When she was six, her family’s house had caught fire; this was exactly how it had begun.
因为她家在她六岁的时候，遭过一阵火灾，当时的情形就是如此，

Nearly thirty years had passed, yet the impression had never faded, and the old terror remained.
快三十年了，印象不消，余悸犹在。

“Do not do that!” she cried, at once angry and alarmed.
『不要这样子，』她又气又急地喊，

“What are you all rushing about for?”
『你们在乱什么？』

Before she had finished, she saw the male servants helping someone inside.
一句话没有完，只见男仆扶进一个人来；

Her alarm grew, though she was somewhat reassured to see at a glance that Gu was unhurt.
七姑奶奶越发惊心，但总算还好，一眼瞥见古应春是好好的。

He hurried ahead to lift the door curtain himself, calling repeatedly, “Hold him steady!
他抢上几步，亲手揭开门帘，不断地喊∶『扶好，

Hold him steady!”
扶好！』

Then he found a moment to call into the room, plainly addressing her, “Get someone to bring a reclining cane chair, quickly!”
又抽空向里说了句，自是对七姑奶奶而发∶『快叫人搬一张藤靠椅来！』

Recovering a little, she asked, “Who is it?”
惊魂初定的七姑奶奶问道∶『谁啊？』


Xiao Jiaji appeared from somewhere.
不知从哪里闪出来一个萧家骥，

“Mr Hu!” he answered.
接口说道∶『胡先生！』

“Which Mr Hu?”
『哪个胡先生？』

“Who else?
『还有哪个？

Little Uncle!”
小爷叔！』

At that her heart contracted.
七姑奶奶一听心就酸了；

She hurried to the door just as the servants brought Hu there.
急急往门口迎了出去，正好男仆扶着胡雪岩到门口，

In the lamplight she could scarcely recognise him.
灯光映照，哪里还认得出来？

“Little Uncle?”
『是小爷叔？』

“Seventh Sister!”
『七姐！』

His face was covered in beard, and he looked dreadfully worn. He managed a faint smile that exposed a startling row of white teeth.
满脸于思，憔悴异常的胡雪岩勉强笑了笑，露出一嘴森森的白牙，

“It is I.”
『是我。』

“It really is Little Uncle!”
『真是小爷叔？』

Tears streamed from her eyes.
七姑奶奶双泪交流，

“How have you come to this?”
『怎么弄成这个样子？』

“This is no time for questions!” Gu urged impatiently. “Where is that cane chair?”
『这时候哪里有功夫说话？』古应春不耐烦地催促∶『还不快搬藤椅来？』


She turned at once and told the maids to bring it and lay a padded quilt upon it.
七姑奶奶赶紧回身指挥丫头，搬来一张藤椅，铺上褥子；

The male servants crowded around to help Hu recline, and only then did she notice the injury to his leg.
男仆们七手八脚地将胡雪岩扶着躺下，她这时才发觉，胡雪岩一条腿受伤了。

“Fetch a doctor!
『快请医生来！

Bring ginger broth!”
拿姜汤！』

Gu gave his orders in rapid succession. “Put some congee on!”
古应春一叠连声地吩咐∶『熬粥！』

Taken by surprise, the Seventh Lady had lost all presence of mind.
事出突兀，七姑奶奶乱了枪法，

Xiao was calmer. “Sir, let Mr Hu settle himself first.”
倒是萧家骤比较镇静∶『师父，你让胡先生先坐定了再说。』

By the time Hu was resting in the chair, a maid had brought a bowl of ginger broth with red dates.
胡雪岩那边坐定下来，已有丫头端来一碗红枣姜汤，

He drank it between gasps, his hands shaking and his legs twitching.
他一面喝，一面喘气，手在发抖、腿在抽筋，

At the sight, the Seventh Lady’s vision blurred with tears.
那副样子看在七姑奶奶眼里，视线立刻就模糊了。


“He is utterly exhausted!” Gu told his wife.
『这是虚极了！』古应春对他妻子说，

“He must not eat much at a time yet.
『这时候还不能多吃东西；

Bring out that old wild ginseng.”
你把那枝老山人参拿出来。』

Hu had not eaten a full meal for two months.
这是因为胡雪岩已经两个月没有吃过一顿饱饭；

He had escaped all the way in a little boat, but because he carried official dispatches he dared not show himself.
坐只小船一路逃出来，由于身上带着公事，不敢露面，

Hiding by day and travelling by night, he had passed through one Longhair stronghold after another, with scarcely any chance to find food.
昼伏夜行穿过一个接一个的『长毛窝』，沿途也不容易弄到食料；

Even when he found some, he could not eat his fill: his stomach and intestines were so weakened that a sudden full meal would have been impossible to digest.
就算有，也不能尽情饱餐，因为肠胃太弱，骤饱之下，无法消化。

It was said that whenever the soup kitchens opened in winter, a few starving men died on the first day because they ate too greedily.
相传每年冬天开施粥厂，头一天总有几个穷汉因为过于贪心而胀死；

The Seventh Lady understood this too.
七姑奶奶也懂这个道理，

She hurried to fetch the old wild Jilin ginseng she had treasured for so long, originally obtained from the imperial palace, and gave it to Hu to chew and swallow, to sustain his failing strength.
急急去取了那枝出自大内、珍藏已久的吉林老山人参来，让胡雪岩嚼咽而食，扶保元气。

“Little Uncle,” she said, looking at his injured leg, “let me see the wound.”
『小爷叔，』七姑奶奶望着他那条受伤的腿说∶『我看看你的伤口。』


She reached to lift his foot, but he hastily drew it away.
说着，就要伸手去捧他的脚，胡雪岩急忙往里一缩。

Near Jiaxing, while the Longhairs were questioning him, a single unfortunate answer had earned him a sword cut.
伤是在嘉兴附近为长毛盘问时，一句话不对劲被砍了一刀；

Without doctor or medicine, he had found some incense ash in a deserted country temple and roughly covered the wound, then bound it with strips torn from his underjacket.
无医无药，在荒郊野庙胡乱找了些香火掩敷，从小褂子上撕了些布条扎紧，

Now it was festering, filthy with blood and discharge, and he would not let her soil her hands.
如今正在溃烂，血污淋漓，肮脏不堪，所以胡雪岩不愿让她沾手，

“Seventh Sister, do not touch it.”
『七姐，你不要动它。』

The few words left him breathless.
胡雪岩说一句便喘气，

After a pause he managed two more: “I’m hungry!”
停了一下又说了两个字∶『我饿！』

“I know, I know!
『我晓得、我晓得！

The congee is cooking.”
粥在熬了。』

An idea occurred to her.
七姑奶奶想到一个办法，

“I shall get Little Uncle something to eat first.”
『我先弄些东西来给小爷叔吃。』

She went into the kitchen herself, ladled out a bowl of ready-made chicken broth and skimmed off the fat.
我亲自入厨，舀了一碗现成的鸡汤，撇去浮油，

She tore off a piece of breast meat, chopped it to a paste and stirred it into the broth.
撕一块脯子肉剁成肉泥，倒在汤里；

Then she took a rice-flour milk cake, crushed it and soaked it in the soup until the whole became a bowl of gruel, which she carried to Hu herself.
然后取一块米粉做的奶糕，在鸡汤中捣碎泡化，成了一碗『浆糊』，亲手捧给胡雪岩。

The smell alone made him swallow repeatedly.
一闻见香味，胡雪岩先就忍不住连连咽着唾沫；

Once the bowl was in his hands, he longed to gulp it down at once.
接到手里恨不得一下子吞进肚里，

But he thought that an open display of his ravenous hunger would wound the couple’s feelings.
但他想到，过于露出『馋相』，会伤他们夫妻的心，

Forcing himself to exercise restraint, he pretended to be composed and ate delicately, spoonful by spoonful.
所以不得不强自抑制着，装得斯文从容地，一匙一匙舀着吃。
