He finished the great bowl of gruel without leaving a trace, yet it was nowhere near enough.
一大碗浆糊吃得光光，实在意有未尽；

“Seventh Sister,” he said helplessly, “the temple of my five organs is still in revolt.”
便用无可奈何的声音说道∶『七姐，五脏庙还在造反。』

“Little Uncle,” Gu urged, “wait a little before you eat again!”
『小爷叔，』古应春劝他，『等下再吃！

“Oh!”
『喔！』

Hu nodded, but his disappointment was plain.
胡雪岩点点头，但脸上是异常失望的神色。

The Seventh Lady could scarcely bear it, though she could not disregard the condition of his stomach.
七姑奶奶大为不忍，但也不能不顾他的肠胃，

“I know.
随即说道∶『这样吧，

Let us find something that will do you no harm.”
弄点吃不坏的东西来吃。』

She brought out several plates of small delicacies—pine nuts, almonds, honeyed dates and candied kumquats—to appease his craving.
于是装了几盘零食，松子、杏仁、蜜枣、金橘饼之类，为他『煞馋』；

Just then the doctor who treated injuries arrived.
而就在这个时候，伤科医生到了，

After examining the wound, he pronounced it serious: Hu would need at least a fortnight before he could move about.
检视伤口，认为相当严重，总要半个月才能行动。


“That—that is impossible,” Hu said anxiously.
『这，这办不到，』胡雪岩很着急地说，

“I must go back in three to five days at the very most.”
『至多三、五天，我一定要回去。』

“What?” the Seventh Lady asked urgently.
『什么？』七姑奶奶急急问道，

“Little Uncle, you are going back?
『小爷叔，你还要回去？

Back to Hangzhou?”
回杭州？』

“Yes.
『是啊！

Think how many mouths are open there, waiting for me.”
杭州城里，多少张嘴都朝天张大了在等我。』

“Governor Wang has entrusted Little Uncle with a special mission to buy rice in Shanghai,” Gu explained to her. “Lives depend on it.
『小爷叔是受王抚台的重托，特为到上海来买米的。』古应春向七姑奶奶解释∶『这是救命的事，

He really cannot afford to linger.
小爷叔确是不便耽搁；

I have already sent someone to ask Brother Five to come and discuss matters.
我已经派人去请五哥来商量了。

不过，』他转脸向伤科医生问道∶『先生，无论如何要请你费心；

No medicine is too costly.
不管用什么贵重药，

Please find some way of getting Little Uncle on his feet within three to five days.”
总要请你想个法子，让我们这位小爷叔，三五天以内，就能走动。』

“Indeed!”
『真的。』

The Seventh Lady joined her pleas to his.
这时的七姑奶奶也帮着恳求，

“Doctor, you must perform this act of kindness.
『郎中先生，你要做做好事；

Every day sooner that Little Uncle gets back means many more lives saved in Hangzhou.
我们这位小爷叔早到一天，杭州城里就要多活好些人。

You will earn untold merit for such a deed.
这是阴功积德的大好事；

In all your life, you have never treated a patient more important than this one.”
郎中先生，你一生看过的病人，没有比这位再要紧的。』

That last sentence had great force.
最后这句话很有力量，

Deeply moved, the doctor examined the wound from every angle, frowning and clicking his tongue for a long time.
伤科医生大为动容，将他的伤口左看右看，攒眉咂嘴了好半天，

At last he spoke.
说出一句话来。

“There is a way.
『办法是有，

I fear only that the patient could not bear the pain.”
只怕病人吃不起痛苦。』

“Never mind that!”
『不要紧！』

Hu clenched his teeth.
胡雪岩咬一咬牙说，

“I care nothing for pain, so long as I recover quickly.”
『什么痛我都不在乎，只要早好！』


“Easy enough to say.”
『说说容易。』

The doctor shook his head.
伤科医生大摇其头，

“You are exhausted to the very limit.
『看你的样子，人是虚弱到了极点；

Severe pain could make you faint.
痛得厉害，人会昏过去。

Let me think.”
等我想想。』

He turned to Gu. “Mr Gu, do you not know a foreign doctor?”
他转脸问道∶『古先生，你不是认识外国医生？』

The question reminded Gu of something he bitterly regretted forgetting.
这一说，提醒了古应春；悔恨不迭——

Hu’s appearance had so shocked him that he had been too bewildered to think of summoning a Western physician.
只为胡雪岩的模样，令人震惊；一时昏瞀，竟想不起请西医，

Now it would be awkward to seek another doctor’s services.
如今倒不便『另请高明了』了。

“Yes,” he answered, having little choice.
『是！』他只好先回答了再说。

“Foreign doctors take their time over a case, but they have a couple of very useful medicines.
『外国医生的看法来得慢∶不过他们有两样药很管用；

Could you obtain some painkiller from them?”
你能不能去要点止痛药来。』

“Well—” Gu looked troubled.
『这，』古应春面有难色，

He knew that Western physicians differed from Chinese practitioners: they would not readily prescribe for a patient they had not examined.
他知道西医跟中医不同，不曾诊视过病人，不肯随便给药；

Moreover, there was more than one kind of painkiller, some applied externally and others taken internally.
而且止痛的药也不止一种，有外敷、有内服，

“Which kind would you want?
『要哪一种止痛药，

I should at least need its name.”
总得有个药名才好。』


“I cannot tell you its name.
『药名就说不出来了；

I cannot make sense of that foreign gibberish.”
叽哩咕噜的洋文，弄不清楚。』

The doctor paused, then made up his mind.
伤科医生略停一下，下了决心，

“Never mind!
『算了！

We cannot keep wasting time.
耽误时候，也不是一回事，

I shall begin.”
我先动手。』

From his medicine chest he took a cloth bundle.
于是他从药箱里取出一个布包，

Unfolded, it revealed several knives and forceps of different sizes, gleaming brightly.
一打开来，雪亮耀眼，是几把大小不同的刀钳；

He cleaned the wound with fresh cotton, then cut away the dead flesh.
然后用新棉花擦拭伤口，运刀剜去腐肉，

The pain brought sweat pouring from Hu’s head.
疼得胡雪岩满头大汗。

Gu and the Seventh Lady were trembling with alarm and sweating with him, yet they had to pretend to be calm and find something to say to comfort him.
古应春和七姑奶奶心惊肉跳，也陪着他淌汗；同时还得胡作镇静，想出话来安慰病人，

Like someone coaxing a child, she kept repeating, “It does not hurt, it does not hurt.
七姑奶奶象哄小孩似地，不断地说∶『不疼、不疼，

It will soon be over.”
马上就好了。』

At last it was over.
毕竟好了，

The doctor applied Jiangxiang Powder to stop the bleeding and ease the pain, then bandaged the wound carefully.
敷上止血定痛的『降香散』包扎妥当；

He too breathed a great sigh of relief.
伤科医生自己也大大地舒了口气，

“Fortunately it has not developed into tetanus,” he said.
『总算还好，没有变成破伤风。』他说，

“‘When a blade wound has bled heavily, a weak and fine pulse foretells survival.’
『「金疮出血太多，其脉虚细者生。」

He must now be looked after with the utmost care.
如今千万要好好照料，

There must be no neglect.”
疏忽不得。』

He went on to enumerate many prohibitions: no exertion, no anger, no loud talk or laughter.
接着他又说了许多禁忌，不能劳动，不能生气，不能大说大笑；

Hu must also restrict his diet—nothing salty, sour or spicy, no hot wine or soup, not even hot congee.
还要『忌口』，咸、酸、辣和热酒、热汤都不能喝，连热粥也在禁忌之列。

“Oh dear!” the Seventh Lady exclaimed.
『糟了！』七姑奶奶说，

“He has just had a great bowl of hot chicken soup.”
『刚喝了一大碗热鸡汤。』

“He has already drunk it.
『喝也喝过了，

What is the use of mentioning it now?” Gu said.
提它干什么？』古应春说，

“We must be careful from now on.”
『以后小心就是了。』


Once the doctor had left, Gu proposed bringing in a Western physician instead.
等伤科医生一走，古应春要改请西医来看；

The Seventh Lady objected, and Hu too said it was unnecessary.
七姑奶奶不赞成，胡雪岩也表示不必，

He felt the pain had already diminished, which proved that the doctor had some skill; they ought not to replace him.
因为他自觉痛楚已经减轻，证明这位伤科医生有些手段，自不宜更换医生。

“I feel much stronger,” Hu said.
『我精神好多了。』胡雪岩说，

“We must attend to the important business.
『办大事要紧。

Why is Brother Five still not here?”
五哥怎么还不来？』

“One of his pupils is marrying again today, and he is at the wedding feast.
『今天是他一徒弟续弦，在吃喜酒，

I have sent someone after him.
我已经派人去追了。

Little Uncle, give me your instructions first.”
小爷叔，』古应春说∶『有事你先分派我。』

“Very well.”
『好！』

Hu put a hand inside his coat and felt about for a long time before bringing out an oilpaper packet.
他探手入怀，掏摸了好半天，才掏出一个油纸包；

He handed it to Gu.
递了给古应春。


Inside were two appalling letters written in Wang Youling’s blood.
打开油纸包，里面是惊心动魄的王有龄的两通血书，

One was addressed to Qingduan, Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang.
一通致闽浙总督庆端，

Besides begging for reinforcements, Wang hoped he would press Li Yuandu, who was still lingering at Hengzhou, to lead the Hunan recruits he had raised towards Hangzhou.
乞援以外，更望设法督催一直逗留在衡州的李元度，带领所募的湘勇，往杭州这方面打，

This would hold the Longhairs in check and ease the pressure on the city.
好牵制长毛，减轻杭州的压力。

The other was for Xue Huan, Governor of Jiangsu, asking him to raise funds and provisions.
还有一通是给江苏巡抚薛焕的，要求筹饷筹粮，

Enclosed was the draft of a memorial, which Wang asked Xue to have copied out and forwarded to the throne on his behalf.
同时附着一件奏稿，托薛焕代缮拜发。


The memorial described in detail how Hangzhou had been besieged and deprived of food.
其中详叙杭州被围绝粮，

It laid the blame on Wang Lüqian, the militia commissioner stationed in Shaoxing, who had colluded with corrupt local gentry to monopolise power and regarded the provincial capital’s peril as indifferently as the people of distant Qin might regard the troubles of Yue.
归咎于驻在绍兴的团练大臣王履谦，勾结劣绅，把持地方，视省城的危急，如秦人之视越；

Still more shocking, he had actually incited ruffians to assault an imperial official.
更骇人听闻的是，居然唆使莠民戕害命官——

On the twenty-fourth of the ninth month, the Longhairs had overrun Xiaoshan, across the Qiantang from Hangzhou.
九月廿四，长毛窜陷钱塘江南岸，与杭州隔水相望的萧山，

Liao Zongyuan, prefect of Shaoxing, sent gunboats to intercept them.
如兴知府廖宗元派炮船，迎头拦击；

Hopelessly outnumbered, the imperial troops were forced to retreat.
寡不敌众，官军败退。

Wang Lüqian and the people of Xiaoshan and Shaoxing had long been on bad terms with the government forces and distrusted them deeply.
王履谦和萧绍一带的百姓，平时就与官军不和，猜忌甚深；

Now they supposed the gunboats had gone over to the enemy and were returning to guide the Longhairs.
这时以为炮船通敌，回来是替长毛带路，

Wang ordered the boats surrounded and their crews captured, with no penalty for killing them.
王履谦便下令包围活捉，格杀不论。

When Liao heard the report, he knew that, even if the accusation was not deliberately false, it was a grave misunderstanding.
廖宗元得报，知道这纵非诬陷，也是极严重的误会，

He hurried outside the city in person to restore order.
赶紧亲自出城弹压。

With a roar the mob dragged him from his horse and beat him savagely, while Wang stood by, silently approving.
暴民一声呼啸，将廖宗元从马上拉下来痛殴，王履谦袖手旁观，默赞其事。

This internal strife gave the enemy their opportunity.
由这一番内讧，替敌人制造了机会；

The Longhairs swept forward and took Shaoxing without shedding a drop of blood.
长毛长驱猛扑，兵不血刃而陷绍兴。

The day before they entered, Wang had fled with his family and baggage from Shaoxing to Ningbo, and thence by sea to Fujian.
长毛进城的前一天，王履谦携带家眷辎重，由绍兴逃到宁波，经海道逃到福建；

Hangzhou’s supply route had thus been severed.
而杭州的粮道，也就此断了。

Naturally, Wang Youling wished to impeach him.
王有龄自然要参劾王履谦，

His language was exceptionally severe, even declaring, “Your servant could not close his eyes in death.”
措词极其严厉；甚至有『臣死不瞑目』的话，

One could imagine the depth of his hatred.
可以想见他对王履谦怨恨入骨。


“What shall I do with these two letters?” Gu asked.
『这两封血书，』古应春问道，『怎么样处置？』

“Send them both to Governor Xue—” “Very well.”
『都送薛抚台——。』『好。』

Before Hu had finished, Gu was already making to rise.
古应春不等他话完，就要起身，

“I shall take them tonight.”
『我连夜送去。』

“There is no need for that.
『这倒不必。

First thing tomorrow will do.
明天一早送去好了；

I have more to say.”
我还有话。』

“Of course.
『是！

Tell me.”
你说。』

“I want you to see Governor Xue in person.”
『我要托你面见薛抚台。』

Weak though Hu was, his low voice still carried great resolution. “I shall find the rice myself.
胡雪岩虽然气弱，但低微的语声中，仍然显得很有决断∶『米，我自己想办法；

As for boats to carry it, I must ask Brother Five when he comes.
运米的船，回头要问五哥，

It would be best if we could manage without troubling the authorities.
能够不麻烦官府最好。

不过，他要替我派兵护运。』

“Is there a route open?”
『这条路通吗？』


“There is one way through.
『有一条路好走，

You would not know it, but Brother Five does.
你不明白；五哥知道，

We shall discuss it when he comes.” Hu continued, “There are some poems for Governor Xue as well.
等他来了再说。』胡雪岩又说∶『还有几首诗，也请你送给薛抚台；

要问杭州惨状到什么样子？请他看这几首诗就知道了。』

一面说，一面又在衣襟中摸索半天，才掏出几张极皱的纸。

Gu spread them on the table, smoothed them out and read carefully.
古应春摆在桌上抹平了细看，

标题叫《辛酉杭城纪事诗》，作者名叫张荫榘。一共是十二首七绝；每首都有注解，

At the fifth, Gu read aloud: “Dignified officials gather in the commander’s hall; / The ninth month’s clear autumn breathes an air of ease.
看到第五首，古应春念道∶雍容铃阁集簪裾，九月秋清气象舒；

诗下的注解是∶『九月二十六日，贼以数十万众围城，

居民如笼中鸟，釜中鱼。』

古应春念到这里，屈指数了一下∶『今天十一月初五，

围了四十天了。』

“Forty days is not so long, but the shortage of food began much earlier.
『四十天不算多，无奈缺粮已久；

围到第十天就人心大乱了。』胡雪岩叹口气说∶『你再看下去。』

饶军门从旁阻之云∶』明日总来得及。

「不料伪逆李秀成连夜筑成木城，于是饷道与张营隔绝。

当张军门令况副将入城见中丞，以灭贼自任，百姓延颈觇伺，均言贼必扑灭。

『看完这首诗和原注，古应春问道∶』饶军门是谁？

“Rao Tingxuan.
『』饶廷选。

He became famous through Madam Shen when he relieved Guangxin Prefecture.
这个人因为救过广信府，靠沈夫人出了大名，

In truth, he is useless.” Hu sighed. “How many times have I told Governor Wang that Rao’s achievements depended on others, and that the man himself is a coward!
其实没用。『胡雪岩叹口气说∶』我劝过王雪公多少次，说他因人成事，自己胆子小得很。

王雪公不听我的话。

救杭州就靠这个机会；

错过这个机会，神仙来都没救了。

“And Zhang Yuliang?” Gu asked.
『』张玉良呢？『古应春又问，』

“Everyone says he is no good.
这个人大家都说他不行，

到底怎么样？

“Read on.
『『你再往下看。

下面有交代。』

诗中是这样交代∶桓侯勇健世无双，飞炮当前岂肯降？

万马不嘶军尽泣，将星如斗落长江。

“What?
『怎么？

Was he killed?”
阵亡了？』

“Killed in battle.”
『阵亡了。』

Hu shook his head.
胡雪岩摇摇头，

“He too did enormous harm through his failures.
『这个人也耽误了大事，

After his defeat at Jiaxing, he could not hold Jinhua or Lanxi either, and Hangzhou was placed in danger.
嘉兴一败，金华兰溪又守不住，杭州就危险了。

不过，总算亏他。』

“The poem compares him to Zhang Fei.
『诗里拿他比做张飞，

It praises him highly.”
说得他很好。』

“He died fighting for his country.
『他是阵亡殉国的，

Naturally they must speak well of him.”
自然要说得他好。』

Hu’s face darkened. “I urged Governor Wang to withdraw for a while.
胡雪岩黯然说道∶『我劝王雪公暂且避一避。

It is like banking at pai gow or playing the dice-shaking game: if luck runs against you, stop for a spell and begin again.
好比推牌九摇摊一样，这一庄手气不顺；歇一歇手，重新来过。

王雪公不肯，

他说他当初劝何根云，守土有责，决不可轻离常州；

现在自己倒言行不符；怎么交代得过去？

“It seems Governor Wang is a loyal servant of the throne after all.”
『『看起来王雪公倒是忠臣。』


“A loyal servant?”
『忠臣？』

Hu gave a bitter laugh. “How many coins a catty are loyal servants worth?
胡雪岩冷笑∶『忠臣几个钱一斤？

我看他——。』语声哽咽欲绝。

Gu had never heard him speak with such angry bitterness.
古应春从未听胡雪岩说过什么愤激的话，

而居然将『忠臣』说得一文不值，可以想见他内心的沉痛悲愤。

Yet Gu could think of nothing to offer him in comfort.
只是苦于没有话可以安慰他。

『先吃饭吧！』七姑奶奶说，

“However great the trouble, we must fill our stomachs before we can think what to do.
『天大的事，总也得吃饱了才好打主意。

而且小爷叔真的也饿了。』

『提到杭州，我哪里还吃得下饭？』

Hu looked up, his eyes full of tears.
胡雪岩泪汪汪地抬眼，

“Read the last two poems.”
『你看最后那两首诗。』

Gu read them closely and his face changed.
古应春细细看了下，颜色大变；

七姑奶奶不免奇怪，

“What is it?” she asked.
『怎么了？』她问，

『说什么？』

『你听我念！』

古应春一个字一个字地念。


“Men come to carve flesh, not to heal a wound; / The starving vie to eat it—grief beyond words.
剜肉人来非补疮，饥民争啖事堪伤；

一腔热血三升血，强作龙肝凤脯尝。

『什么？』七姑奶奶大惊问道『人吃人？』

古应春不即回答，一个字一个字地念着注解∶『兵勇肆掠，

居民鸣锣捕获，解送凤山门王中丞常驻之处。

中丞询实，请王命尽斩之；

Their bodies lay heaped by the roadside.
尸积道旁，

Soldiers fought for their hearts and livers to eat with wine, and starving civilians likewise competed for morsels of their flesh.
兵士争取心肝下酒，饥民亦争脔食之。

‘Eating human flesh’, something formerly encountered only in histories, I now witnessed with my own eyes.”
「食人肉」，平日见诸史乘者，至此身亲见之。』

Everyone within hearing, before the hall and behind it, turned pale.
就这一段话，将厅前厅后的人，听得一个个面无人色，

The Seventh Lady shook her head again and again. “The world has changed!
七姑奶奶连摇摇头∶『世界变了！

That such things should happen!”
有这样的事！』

“I can scarcely believe it myself,” Gu said.
『我也不大相信。

小爷叔真有其事？』

“Not only true; there is scarcely anything surprising in it.”
『不但真有其事，简直叫无足为奇。』

Hu’s face was bleak as he struggled for breath. “People will eat anything when they are starving.”
胡雪岩容颜惨淡地喘着气说∶『人饿极了，什么东西都会吃。』

He went on to describe the famine in Hangzhou.
他接下来，便讲杭州绝粮的情形——

Western Zhejiang had enjoyed a bumper harvest that year, but just as the crop was being gathered, the Longhairs had swept in like a tide.
这年浙西大熟，但正当收割之际，长毛如潮水般涌到；

The imperial troops retreated at every turn, and the grain ready for harvesting supplied the enemy instead, enabling them to maintain a long blockade.
官军节节败退，现成的稻谷，反而资敌，得以作长围久困之计。

Otherwise their hundreds of thousands of men could not have sustained themselves, and Hangzhou’s siege would have collapsed of its own accord.
否则，数十万长毛无以支持；杭州之围也就不解而自解了。


The comfortably off households in Hangzhou naturally held some grain in reserve, but people who lived from one small measure of rice to the next felt the danger immediately.
杭州城里的小康之家，自然有些存粮；升斗小民，却立刻就感到了威胁，

The rice shops had closed even before the gates were shut.
米店在闭城之前，就已歇业。

Hu therefore took the lead in establishing soup kitchens: forty-seven throughout the upper, middle and lower divisions of the city.
于是胡雪岩发起开办施粥厂，上中下三城共设四十七处，

They served twice daily, in the morning and late afternoon, cooking one shi of rice at each serving.
每日辰、申两次，每次煮米一石，

There was too little congee and too great a crowd.
粥少人多，

The old and infirm, women and children could not force their way to the front; some went three or four times and came away empty-handed.
老羽妇孺挤不到前面，有去了三、四次空手而回的。

Before long, the kitchens had to close.
没有多久，粥厂就不能不关闭。

Government grain was still sold at controlled prices in quantities allotted by household size.
但官米还在计口平卖，

When the rice was gone, they sold beans; when the beans were gone, wheat.
米卖完了卖豆子，豆卖完了卖麦子。

The rich had another source of rice: they secretly exchanged gold and opium with the Eight Banner troops for their military rations.
有钱的人家，另有买米的地方，是拿黄金跟鸦片向旗营的八旗兵私下交换军粮。

Soon the rice, wheat and other grains were all exhausted, and people turned to medicinal roots and dried provisions.
又不久，米麦杂粮都吃得光光，便吃药材南货，

Prepared rehmannia, Job’s tears and Solomon’s seal could all serve in place of meals.
熟地、米仁、黄精，都可以代饭；

Dates and chestnuts became treasures, while sea cucumbers, shark’s fins and other delicacies of the banquet table became the food of the poor.
枣栗之类，视如珍品，而海参，鱼翅等等席上之珍，反倒是穷人的食料。


After that they ate bran, leather trunks and hobnailed shoes—the shoes were made of oxhide—then duckweed, grass roots and tree bark.
再后来就是吃糠、吃皮箱、吃钉鞋——钉鞋是牛皮做的；吃浮萍，吃草根树皮。

The people of Hangzhou were devoted to Buddhism, and old ladies of wealthy families particularly loved to purchase animals and spare their lives.
杭州人好佛，有钱人家的老太太，最喜欢『放生』；

At a place called Little Cloud Tower, cattle, sheep, pigs and ducks were kept for this purpose.
有处地方叫小云楼，专养放生的牛羊猪鸭，

Naturally, they were all swept away.
自然一扫而空了。

“The people in Hangzhou are no longer people.
『杭州城里的人，不是人，

They are ghosts, every one of them a bundle of bones.
是鬼；一个个骨头瘦得成了一把，

Look at a face and you see three holes: two for the eyes and one for the mouth.
望过去脸上三个洞，两个洞是眼睛，一个洞是嘴巴。

In the streets they are like empty eggshells blown by the wind, swaying and drifting, unable to keep their feet.”
走在路上，好比「风吹鸭蛋壳」，飘飘荡荡，站不住脚。』

Hu paused for breath, then continued with great effort. “Two people might meet in the road and talk, both too weak to raise their voices.
胡雪岩喘口气，很吃力地说∶『好比两个人在路上遇着，有气无力在谈话；

As they speak, one suddenly falls down for no apparent reason.
说着，说着，有一个就会无缘无故倒了下去。

The other tries to help him.
另一个要去扶他；

It would be better if he did not: the moment he bends to lift him, his own head swims, his eyes blur, and down he goes too, unable to rise.
不扶还好，一扶头昏眼花，自己也一跟头栽了下去，爬不起来了。

Who can count the bodies left in the roads like that?
象这样子的，「倒路尸」，不晓得有多少？

It is fortunate that this is winter.
幸亏是冬天，

In summer, plague would have broken out long ago.”
如果是夏天，老早就生瘟疫了。』

“Then what of your own family?” the Seventh Lady asked urgently.
『那末，』七姑奶奶急急问道∶『府上呢？』


“I do not know whether they are alive or dead.”
『生死不明。』

Tears fell as Hu spoke. “Back in the eighth month, my mother thought we would be safer taking refuge in the country.
胡雪岩垂泪说道∶『早在八月里，我老娘说是避到乡下好；

I sent the whole household, young and old, to Upper Tianzhu below the Northern Peak.
全家大小送到北高峰下的上天竺，

Once the city gates shut, all news of them ceased.”
城一关，就此消息不知。』

“They will be all right,” the Seventh Lady said.
『一定不要紧的。』七姑奶奶说，

“Yours is a family that has accumulated merit.
『府上是积善之家，

Your mother is always doing good and helping people.
老太太又喜欢行善做好事，

Heaven protects the virtuous.
吉人天相，

They will certainly be safe.”
一定平安无事。』

“Ah!” Gu sighed.
『唉！』古应春叹口气，

“What a catastrophe!”
『浩劫！』

By now the clock was striking eight.
这时已经钟打八点，

The great crabs had been steamed and had grown cold, but after learning that Hangzhou, so often called a Buddhist sanctuary, had been reduced to the horror of people eating one another, no one in the household could swallow a meal.
一串大蟹，蒸而又冷，但得知素称佛地的杭州，竟有人吃人的惨状，上上下下，谁都吃不下饭。

As hostess, the Seventh Lady was obliged to urge them to eat; they merely went through the motions and soon left the table.
七姑奶奶做主人的，自不能不劝；但草草终席，塞责而已。

The only man who had eaten his fill was You Wu, who arrived after hearing the news.
吃饱了的，只有一个闻信赶来的尤五，

At his pupil’s wedding feast he had naturally been treated as an honoured guest.
吃他徒弟的喜酒，自然奉为上宾；

When he learnt that Hu had come, he was eager to leave, but still had to drink a good deal more before he could get away.
席间听得胡雪岩已到的消息，急于脱身，但仍旧被灌了好些酒，方得离席。

Now the sight of Hu drove most of the wine from his head.
此时一见之下，酒意去了七八分，

只望着胡雪岩发愣。


“Little Uncle, how have you come to this?”
『小爷叔，怎么弄成这个样子？』

“Brother Five, do not ask him about it.
『五哥，你不要问他了。

真正人间地狱，九死一生，

现在商量正事吧！』

“Please come inside,” the Seventh Lady said.
『请到里头来。』七姑奶奶说，

“I have everything ready for Little Uncle.”

She had prepared a small room beside Gu’s study as Hu’s bedroom.

“Brother Five, the people of Hangzhou are starving to death.
