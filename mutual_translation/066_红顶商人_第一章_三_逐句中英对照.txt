胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第一章（本章第3部分，共6部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


『运河不通了，
“The Grand Canal is blocked.

嘉兴这一关就过不去。』胡雪岩说，
We cannot get past Jiaxing,” Hu Xueyan said.

『只有一条路，走海道经鳖子门。』
“There is only one route left: by sea, through Biezimen.”

鳖子门在海宁，是钱塘江入海之处、在明朝是杭州防备倭患的第一门户。
Biezimen lay in Haining, at the mouth of the Qiantang River; in the Ming dynasty it had been Hangzhou’s first line of defense against Japanese pirates.

尤五对运河相当熟悉，海道却陌生得很，
Brother You knew the canal well, but the sea routes were another matter.

便老实说道∶『这我就搞不清楚了。
“That is beyond me,” he admitted frankly.

要寻沙船帮想办法。』
“We shall have to find the Sea-Junk Guild and ask them to work something out.”


沙船帮走海道，
The Sea-Junk Guild operated along the coast.

从漕米海运之议一起，漕帮跟沙船帮就有势不两立的模样。
Ever since the proposal to ship the tribute grain by sea, relations between it and the Grain-Boat Guild had verged on open hostility.

现在要请他跟沙船帮去打交道，未免强人所难；
Asking Brother You to approach its members now was asking a great deal.

胡雪岩喝着参汤，还在肚子里盘算，应该如何进行，古应春却先开口了。
Hu drank his ginseng broth, still turning over in his mind how best to proceed, but Gu Yingchun spoke first.

『沙船帮的郁老大，我也有一面之识；
“I have met Chief Yu of the Sea-Junk Guild once.

事到如今，也说不得冒昧了。
Things have come to such a pass that I cannot stand on ceremony.

我去！』
I shall go!”

说着，就站起身来；尤五将他一拉，
He rose as he spoke, but Brother You caught his arm.

慢条斯理地说∶『不要忙，
“No hurry,” he said deliberately.

等我想一想。』
“Let me think.”


胡雪岩依然非常机敏，
Hu’s wits were as quick as ever.

看出尤五的意思，便挣扎着起身；
Seeing what Brother You had in mind, he struggled to rise.

七姑奶奶紧赶一面扶，
The Seventh Lady hurried to support him.

一面问∶『小爷叔，你要啥？』
“What do you want, Little Uncle?”

胡雪岩不答她的话，
He did not answer.

站起身，叫一声∶『五哥！』便跪了下去。
Getting to his feet, he cried, “Brother Five!” and dropped to his knees.

尤五大惊，一跳老远，
Brother You recoiled in alarm and sprang well out of his way.

大声说道∶『小爷叔、
“Little Uncle!

小爷叔，
Little Uncle!

你这是为啥？
What are you doing?

折熬我了。』
You do me far too much honor!”


古应春夫妇，双双将他扶了起来，
Gu and his wife helped him up together.

七姑奶奶要开口，他摇摇手说∶『我是为杭州的百姓求五哥！』
The Seventh Lady was about to speak, but Hu waved her into silence. “I am begging Brother Five on behalf of the people of Hangzhou!”

『小爷叔，你何必如此？』
“Little Uncle, why must you do this?”

尤五只好说痛快话了∶『只要你说一句，
Brother You had no choice but to speak plainly. “You have only to say the word.

哪怕郁老大跟我是解不开的对头，我也只好去跟他说好话。』
Even if Chief Yu were my bitterest enemy, with no hope of a reconciliation, I should still have to go and ask him nicely.”

他跟郁老大确是解不开的对头——
In fact, Chief Yu was just such an enemy.

郁老大叫郁馥华，家住小南门内的乔家滨，
His name was Yu Fuhua, and he lived at Qiaojiabin, inside the Little South Gate.

以航行南北洋起家，发了好大一笔财。
Coastal shipping had made him a considerable fortune.

本来一个走海道，一个走运河，真所谓『河水不犯井水』；并无恩怨可言，
Originally, one man worked the sea routes and the other the canal: river water and well water never crossed, as the saying went, and there had been no cause for either friendship or enmity.

但从南漕海运以后，情形就很不同了。
The transfer of the southern tribute grain to sea transport had changed everything.

尤五倒还明事理，大势所趋，不得不然，
Brother You himself was reasonable enough to recognize an inevitable development.

并非郁馥华有意想承揽这笔生意，打碎漕帮的饭碗；
Yu Fuhua had not deliberately sought the contract in order to take the Grain-Boat Guild’s livelihood away.

但他手下的小弟兄，却不是这么想。
His younger followers, however, saw it differently.

加以沙船帮的水手，趾高气扬；茶坊酒肆，出手阔绰，
To make matters worse, the sea-junk sailors strutted about, spending lavishly in teahouses and taverns.

漕帮弟兄相形出绌，越发妒恨交加，常起摩擦。
The grain-boat men looked poor beside them; jealousy bred resentment, and clashes were frequent.


有一次两帮群殴，说起来，道理是漕帮这面欠缺。
On one occasion the two guilds fought a pitched brawl, and in fairness the grain-boat men were more at fault.

但江湖事，江湖了；
Even so, disputes among men of their world ought to have been settled within it.

郁馥华听信了江苏海运局中几个候补佐杂的话，将尤五手下的几个弟兄，扭到了上海县衙门。
Instead, Yu Fuhua listened to several minor expectant officials at the Jiangsu Maritime Grain Transport Bureau and had some of Brother You’s men hauled before the Shanghai county magistrate.

知县刘郇膏是江苏的能员，
Liu Xungao was one of Jiangsu’s capable officials.

也知道松江漕帮是『百足之虫，死而不僵』，不愿多事；
He also knew that the Songjiang Grain-Boat Guild was like a hundred-legged insect that went on wriggling even after death, and he had no wish to stir up trouble.

同时古应春在上海县衙门也算是吃得开的，受尤五之托，去说人情。
Gu Yingchun, meanwhile, had some influence at the county yamen and, at Brother You’s request, went to intercede.

两下一凑，刘郇膏大事化小，小事化无；
These considerations together led Liu to turn a major affair into a minor one and the minor one into nothing at all.

传了尤五到堂，当面告诫一番，叫他具了『不再滋事』的切结，
He summoned Brother You, admonished him in open court, and required a written pledge that there would be no further disturbances.

将人领了回去。
Then he allowed him to take his men away.


这一下结怨就深了。
This only deepened the grudge.

在尤五想，连县大老爷都知道松江漕帮不好惹，网开一面；
As Brother You saw it, even the county magistrate knew better than to provoke the Songjiang guild and had left an opening for mercy.

郁馥华反倒不讲江湖义气，
Yu Fuhua, by contrast, had shown none of the loyalty their way of life demanded.

不想想大家都是『靠水吃水』，一条线上的人。
Had he forgotten that they all lived by the water and belonged to the same fraternity?

既然如此，两不往返；
Very well, then: there would be no further dealings between them.

尤五特地召集所属码头的头脑，郑重宣布∶凡是沙船帮的一切，松江漕帮，不准参预。
Brother You summoned the heads of the wharves under his authority and formally announced that the Songjiang Grain-Boat Guild was to take no part in anything connected with the Sea-Junk Guild.

有跳槽改行到沙船帮去做水手的，就算『破门』，从今见面不认。
Any man who changed allegiance and became a sailor on a sea junk would be considered expelled; thereafter, even if they met, he would no longer be acknowledged.

郁馥华自己也知道做错了一件事，深感不安；
Yu Fuhua knew that he had made a mistake and was deeply uneasy about it.

几次托人向尤五致意，希望修好。
Several times he sent intermediaries to express his goodwill and seek a reconciliation.

尤五置之不理，
Brother You ignored them.

如今却不得不违反自己的告诫，要向对方去低头了。
Now he would have to break his own rule and bow to the other man.


『为小爷叔的事，三刀六洞，我也咬一咬牙「顶」了；
“For you, Little Uncle, I would grit my teeth and take three knife thrusts clean through me.

不过这两年，我的旗号扯得忒足，一时无法落篷。
But these past two years I have flown my colors so high that I cannot bring myself to lower the sail all at once.

难就难在这里。』
That is the difficulty.”

『五哥，你是为杭州的百姓。』胡雪岩说，
“Brother Five, you would be doing it for the people of Hangzhou,” Hu said.

『我腿伤了，没办法跟郁老大去办交涉——
“With my leg injured, I cannot go and negotiate with Chief Yu myself.

话说回来了，出海进鳖子门这一段，不要紧；一进鳖子门，反有风险，
Besides, the sea passage to Biezimen is safe enough; the danger begins once we are through it.

郁老大作兴不肯点头只有你去托他，他要卖你一个交情，不肯也得肯。
Chief Yu may well refuse. Only if you ask him will he feel bound to do you a favor and agree, however unwillingly.

至于你说旗号扯得太足，落不下篷，这也是实话；
As for your having flown your colors so high that you cannot lower the sail, that is true too.

我倒有个办法，能够让你落篷，不但落篷，还让你有面子，
But I have thought of a way to let you lower it—and save face into the bargain.

你看怎么样？』
What do you say?”

『小爷叔，你不要问我，
“There is no need to ask me, Little Uncle.

你说怎么样，就怎么样。
Whatever you say goes.

其实我也是说说而已；
I was only talking, after all.

真的没有办法也只好硬着头发去见郁老大。』
If there were no other way, I should have to swallow my pride and see Chief Yu just the same.”

『不会让你太受委屈。』
“We shall not make you swallow too much.”

胡雪岩转脸说道∶『老古，我请你写封信；写给何制台——。』『写给何制台？』古应春说，
Hu turned to Gu. “Old Gu, I should like you to write a letter for me—to Governor-General He—” “To Governor-General He?” Gu repeated.

『他现在不知道躲在哪里？』
“Who knows where he is hiding now?”

『这难道打听不到？』
“Surely we can find out?”


『打听是一定打听得到的。』尤五接口说道，
“We certainly can,” Brother You put in.

『他虽然革了职，要查办，到底是做过制台的人，
“He may have been dismissed and be facing an investigation, but he was a governor-general, after all.

不会没人晓得。
Someone must know where he is.

不过，小爷叔，江苏的公事，他已经管不到了，
But, Little Uncle, he no longer has any say in Jiangsu’s affairs.

你写信给他为啥？』
Why write to him?”

『江苏的公事他虽管不到，老长官的帐，人家还是要卖的。』胡雪岩说，
“He has no authority now, but people will still show their former superior some respect,” Hu replied.

『我想请他交代薛抚台或者上海道，让他们出来替五哥跟郁老大拉拉场。』
“I want him to speak to Governor Xue or the Shanghai circuit intendant and have one of them mediate between you and Chief Yu.”

『不必，不必！』
“No, no!”

尤五乱摇双手，
Brother You waved both hands.

『现任的官儿，我跟他们身分不配；
“I am no match in status for an official in office.

这种应酬，场面上尴尬得很，
Such occasions are terribly awkward.

多一事不如少一事。』
Better to avoid an unnecessary complication.”

古应春倒觉得胡雪岩的话，大有道理，
Gu, however, thought Hu’s suggestion very sensible.

便道∶『冤家宜解不宜结，
“Better to settle a feud than to prolong it.

如有地方大员出面调停，双方都有面子，
If a senior local official mediates, both sides will save face.

应该顺势收篷了。』
You ought to take the opportunity to lower your sail.”

『这还在其次，』他接下来讲第二个理由∶『为了小爷叔的公事，郁老大的沙船是无论如何少不了的；
There was another reason too. “And that is only part of it. For Little Uncle’s mission, Chief Yu’s junks are indispensable.

不过风险太大，就算卖五哥你的面子，欠他的这个情，将来很难补报。
But the risk is so great that, even if he agrees out of regard for you, it will be hard to repay the favor.

有官府出面，一半就等于抓差；
If the authorities intervene, it will be half an official requisition.

五哥，你的人情债不就可以轻得好多？』
That would make your debt of gratitude much lighter, would it not, Brother Five?”

『老古的话，一点不错。』胡雪岩连连点头，
Hu nodded repeatedly. “Exactly, Old Gu.

『我正是这个意思。』
That was precisely what I meant.”


既然他们都这样说，尤五自然同意。
With both of them taking this view, Brother You naturally agreed.

于是胡雪岩口述大意，古应春代为执笔，写好了给何桂清的信；
Hu dictated the substance of the letter, and Gu wrote it out for He Guiqing.

约定第二天一早分头奔走，中午都得办妥。
They arranged to go their separate ways early the next morning and have everything settled by noon.

至于运米的细节，要等尤五跟郁馥华言归于好以后才谈得到。
The details of transporting the rice would have to wait until Brother You and Yu Fuhua had made their peace.


安顿好了两拨客人，七姑奶奶上床已交半夜子时了；
By the time the Seventh Lady had settled both sets of guests and gone to bed, it was already midnight.

向丈夫问好胡雪岩的公事，
She asked her husband about Hu’s mission.

听说其中有写信给何桂清的这一段周折，当时就『跳』了起来。
When she heard of the elaborate plan to write to He Guiqing, she fairly leaped up.

『这是什么时候？还容得你们「城头上出棺材，大兜大转」！
“At a time like this, can you still afford to carry the coffin round the city wall—taking every possible detour?

且不说杭州城里的老百姓，都快饿死光了；
Never mind that the people inside Hangzhou are nearly all starving to death.

光是看小爷叔这副样子来讨救兵，就该连夜办事。』
One look at Little Uncle, coming in that condition to beg for help, ought to have set you working through the night!”

她气鼓鼓地说，
She was positively bristling.

『真正是，
“Honestly!

看你们男子汉，大丈夫，做事怎么这样子娘娘腔？』古应春笑了，
You call yourselves grown men, and yet you go about things like a pack of dithering women!” Gu laughed.

『你不要跟我跳脚，
“Do not stamp your feet at me.

你去问你哥哥！』
Ask your brother!

他说∶『不是我劝，五哥跟郁老大的过节还不肯解呢！』
If I had not persuaded him, Brother Five would not even have agreed to settle his quarrel with Chief Yu.”

『等我去！』七姑奶奶毫不迟疑地，
“I shall go!” she declared without a moment’s hesitation.

『等我去跟五哥说。』
“I shall go and speak to Brother Five.”

不用她去，
She had no need to.

尤五恰好还有私话要跟妹夫来说；一开门就遇见，
Brother You happened to have something private to discuss with his brother-in-law, and she met him as she opened the door.

见她满脸不悦的样子，不由得诧异。
Her angry expression astonished him.

『怎么？
“What is it?

跟哪个生气？』
Who has upset you?”


古应春一听这话，赶紧拦阻∶『七姐，你跟五哥好说。
At this, Gu hastened to intervene. “Seventh Sister, speak gently to Brother Five.

五哥有五哥的难处，
He has difficulties of his own.

只要你讲得有道理，五哥会听的。』
If you put your case reasonably, he will listen.”

『好，我就讲道理。
“Very well, I shall be reasonable.

五哥，你进来坐，
Come in and sit down, Brother Five.

我请问你一句话，是小爷叔的交情要紧？还是什么制台、抚台的面子要紧？』
Let me ask you one thing: which matters more, your friendship with Little Uncle or the prestige of some governor or governor-general?”

『你问这话啥意思？』
“What do you mean by asking that?”

『自然有讲究。
“I have my reasons.

你先回了我的话，我再讲缘故给你听。』
Answer me first, and I shall explain.”

『当然小爷叔的交情要紧。』
“Of course my friendship with Little Uncle matters more.”

『好！』
“Good!”

七姑奶奶脸色缓和下来了，
Her expression softened.

『我再问一问，
“Now let me ask you something else.

杭州一城百姓的命，跟我们漕帮与郁老大的过节，五哥，你倒放在天平上称一称，
Put the lives of the whole population of Hangzhou on one side of a scale and our guild’s quarrel with Chief Yu on the other.

哪一方来得重？』
Which weighs more, Brother Five?”


尤五哑然，被驳得无话可说。
Brother You was silenced; he had no answer to that.

古应春又高兴，又有些不安；
Gu was delighted, but also a little uneasy.

因为虽是娘舅至亲，到底要保持一分客气，有些话不便率直而言，
Close as he was to his wife’s brother, courtesy still imposed a certain reserve, and there were things he could not say outright.

现在有了『女张飞』这番快人快语，足以折服尤五，
Now this female Zhang Fei, with her fearless plain speaking, had said enough to convince Brother You.

但又怕她妻子得理不让人，再说下去会使得尤五起反感，
Yet Gu feared that, having proved her point, she might refuse to leave it there and provoke resentment by pressing too hard.

希望她适可而止。
He hoped she would know when to stop.

七姑奶奶长了几岁，又有了孩子，自然亦非昔比；
The Seventh Lady had grown a few years older and had a child now; she was naturally no longer quite the woman she had been.

此时声音放得平静了∶『依我说，小爷叔是想替你挣面子，其实主意不大高明。』
Her voice grew calm. “As I see it, Little Uncle wants to help you save face, but his plan is not a very good one.”

『这样说，你必有高明主意？』
“Then you must have a better plan?”

古应春点她一句∶倒不妨慢慢说给五哥听一听，看看行不行得通？『
Gu prompted her. “Why not explain it calmly to Brother Five and see whether it will work?”


『要做官的出来拉场，就有点吃罚酒的味道，不吃不行——。』『对！』
“If you bring officials in to mediate, there is something of a punishment cup about it: you have to swallow it, whether you like it or not—” “Exactly!”

尤五一拍大腿，大为称赏，
Brother You slapped his thigh in hearty approval.

『阿七这话说到我心里了，
“A’qi has put her finger on it.

小爷叔那里我不好驳，实实在在是有点这样的味道。』
I could hardly contradict Little Uncle, but that is precisely how it feels.”

『江湖事，江湖了。』七姑奶奶又有些慷慨激昂了，
“Let men of our own world settle their own affairs,” the Seventh Lady continued, warming to her subject again.

『五哥，你明天去看郁老大，
“Go and see Chief Yu tomorrow, Brother Five.

只说为了杭州一城百姓的性命，小爷叔的交情，向他低头，请他帮忙。
Tell him plainly that, for the sake of every life in Hangzhou and your friendship with Little Uncle, you have come to bow your head and ask for help.

这话传出去，哪个不说你大仁大义？』
When word of that gets about, who will fail to praise your humanity and generosity?”


尤五凝神想了一下，倏然起身，一句话不说就走了——
Brother You considered this intently, then rose abruptly and left without a word.

他要跟妹夫说的私话，就是觉得不必惊动官府，看看另外有更好的办法没有？
The private matter he had meant to discuss with his brother-in-law was his feeling that they need not involve the authorities: could they not find a better way?

这话，现在也就不必再说了。
There was no longer any need to ask.


一到小南门内乔家滨，老远就看到郁家的房子，
As soon as he reached Qiaojiabin, inside the Little South Gate, he could see the Yu residence from a considerable distance.

既新且大。
It was both new and large.

郁馥华的这所新居，落成不久，就有小刀会起事，为刘丽川头尾盘踞了三年；
Soon after Yu Fuhua had completed it, the Small Swords Society had risen in revolt, and Liu Lichuan had occupied the house for a period extending over three calendar years.

咸丰五年大年初一，江苏巡抚吉尔杭阿由法国海军提督辣尼尔帮忙，克复了上海县城，
On New Year’s Day in the fifth year of Xianfeng’s reign, Jiangsu’s governor, Jirhangga, had recaptured the walled city of Shanghai with the help of the French admiral Lani’er.

郁馥华收复故居，大事修葺，比以前更加华丽了。
Yu recovered his home and had it extensively refurbished, making it even more splendid than before.


尤五还是第一次到郁家来，
This was Brother You’s first visit to the Yu house.

轻车简从，无人识得；
He had come without pomp and with few attendants, and no one recognized him.

他向来不备名帖，只指一指鼻子说∶『我姓尤，
He never carried a visiting card; he merely pointed to himself and said, “My name is You.

松江来的。』
I am from Songjiang.”

尤五生得劲气内敛，外貌不扬，衣饰亦朴素得很；
His strength lay hidden beneath an unimposing exterior, and his clothes were very plain.

郁家的下人不免轻视，当他是来告帮求职的，
The servants looked down on him, assuming that he had come to beg assistance or seek employment.

便淡淡地说了句∶『我们老爷不在家，
“Our master is out,” one said indifferently.

你明天再来。』
“Come back tomorrow.”

『不，我有极要紧的事，
“No, I have something extremely urgent to discuss.

非见你家老爷不可。
I must see him.

请派人去找一找，
Please send someone to find him.

我就在这里立等。』
I shall wait here.”

『到哪里去找？』
“And where are we supposed to look?”

郁家的下人声音不好听了。
The servant’s tone had become unpleasant.


尤五是极有涵养的人，
Brother You had great self-control.

而且此来既然已下了降志以求的决心，亦就容易接受委屈，
Having already resolved to humble himself in pursuit of his purpose, he found it easier to put up with a slight.

便用商量的语气说道∶『既然如此，你们这里现成的条凳，
“In that case,” he said reasonably, “you have benches here already.

让我坐等，可以不可以？』
Might I sit down and wait?”

郁家门洞里置两条一丈多长的条凳，原是供来客随带的跟班和轿夫歇脚用的，
In the entrance passage stood two benches, each more than a zhang long, provided for visiting servants and sedan-chair bearers to rest their feet.

尤五要坐，有何不可？
If he wished to sit there, why should he not?

尽管请便就是。
He was welcome to do so.

这一坐坐了个把时辰，只见来了一辆极漂亮的马车，
He had sat there for the better part of a two-hour watch when a handsome carriage arrived.

跨辕的俊仆，跳下车来，将一张踏脚凳放在车门口，
A smartly dressed servant riding on the shaft jumped down and placed a step beneath the carriage door.

车厢里随即出来一名华服少年，昂然入门。
A young man in rich clothes emerged and strode proudly inside.


这个华服少年是郁馥华的大儿子郁松年，人称『郁家秀才』——郁馥华虽发了大财，总觉得子侄不得功名，虽富不贵，心有未足，
This was Yu Fuhua’s eldest son, Yu Songnian, known as “the Yu Family Licentiate.” For all his wealth, Yu Fuhua had felt something lacking so long as none of the younger men in the family held a scholarly degree: they were rich, but not distinguished.

所以延请名师，督促郁松年下帷苦读。
He therefore engaged eminent tutors and drove Songnian to study in seclusion.

但『场中莫论文』，一直连个秀才都中不上，
But examination success did not necessarily follow literary merit, and the young man repeatedly failed to obtain even the lowest degree.

因而捐银五万，修葺文庙，
His father consequently donated fifty thousand taels toward repairing the Confucian temple.

朝廷遇有这种义举，不外两种奖励，一种是饬令地方官为此人立牌坊褒奖，一种是增加『进学』，也就是秀才的名额。
The court usually rewarded such public benefactions in one of two ways: it might order the local authorities to erect a commemorative arch for the donor, or it might increase the quota of successful candidates admitted as licentiates.

郁馥华希望得到后一种奖励，经过打点，如愿以偿。
Yu Fuhua wanted the latter reward and, after making the necessary arrangements, obtained it.

这是为地方造福，但实在也是为自己打算。
The donation benefited the locality, but he had certainly considered his own interests too.

学额既已增加，『入学』就比较容易；郁松年毕竟得青一衿。
With the quota enlarged, admission became easier, and Songnian at last earned his scholar’s blue collar.

秀才的官称叫做『生员』；其间又有各种分别，占额外名额的叫做『增生』，
The official term for a licentiate was shengyuan, and there were various distinctions within that grade; those admitted to supplementary places were called zengsheng.

但不论如何，总是秀才，
Whatever the category, however, a licentiate was a licentiate.

称郁松年为『郁家秀才』，表示这个秀才的名额，是郁家斥巨资捐出来的，当然有点菲蒲的意味在内。
Calling Songnian “the Yu Family Licentiate” implied that his place had been purchased by the family’s huge donation, and was naturally a little disparaging.


但是郁松年倒非草包，
Yet Songnian was no fool.

虽不免纨绔习气，却是有志于学，彬彬有礼；
Though he had some of the habits of a rich young idler, he sincerely aspired to learning and was courteous and well mannered.

当时已经在下人一片『大少爷』的招呼声中，进入屏门，忽然发觉有异，
Amid the servants’ cries of “Young Master!” he had already passed through the screen doorway when he sensed that something was wrong.

站定了，回身注视，
He stopped and looked back.

果然看到了尤五。
Sure enough, there was Brother You.

『尤五叔！』
“Uncle You!”

他疾趋而前，请了个安，惊喜交集地问，『你老人家怎么在这里？』
He hurried over, bowed respectfully, and asked in mingled surprise and delight, “What brings you here, sir?”

『我来看你老人家，』
“I have come to see your father.”

尤五气量甚宽，不肯说郁家下人的坏话，
Brother You was too generous to complain of the servants.

『听说不在家，我等一等好了。』
“I hear he is out, so I am waiting for him.”

『怎么在这里坐？』
“But why are you sitting here?”

郁松年回过脸去，怒声斥责下人∶『你们太没有规矩了，
Songnian turned on the servants angrily. “Where are your manners?

尤五爷来了，怎么不请进去，让贵客坐在这里？』
Master You has come to see us, and you leave an honored guest sitting here instead of asking him inside?”


原先答话的下人，这才知道自己『有眼不识泰山』。
Only then did the servant who had spoken earlier realize that he had failed to recognize a great man under his very nose.

自家主人跟尤五结怨，以及希望修好而不得的经过，平时早就听过不止一遍；
He had heard more than once of his master’s quarrel with Brother You and his unsuccessful attempts to mend it.

如今人家登门就教，反倒慢客，
Now that the visitor had come of his own accord, the servant had treated him discourteously.

因此而得罪了尤五，过在不宥，说不定就此敲碎了绝好的一只饭碗，
If Brother You took offense, it would be an unforgivable fault; he might have smashed his own excellent rice bowl.

所以吓得面无人色。
The thought drained his face of color.

尤五见此光景，索性好人做到底了，
Seeing this, Brother You decided to carry his kindness through to the end.

『你不要骂他，你不要骂他。』他赶紧拦在前面，
“Do not scold him, do not scold him,” he said, stepping quickly between them.

『管家倒是一再邀我进去，
“Your man invited me inside more than once.

是我自己愿意在这里等，比较方便。』
I chose to wait here myself; I thought it more convenient.”

听得这一说，郁松年才不言语，
At this Songnian said no more.

『尤五叔，请里面坐！』他说，
“Please come inside, Uncle You.

『家父在勘察城墙，
Father is inspecting the city wall.

我马上派人去请他回来。』
I shall send for him at once.”

『好的，好的！
“Thank you, thank you.

实在是有点要紧事，不然也不敢惊动你老人家。』
It really is rather urgent; otherwise I would not presume to trouble your father.”

『尤五叔说哪里话？
“Please do not say that, Uncle You.

请都请不到。』
We could hardly have hoped to bring you here even with an invitation.”


肃客入厅，
Songnian ceremoniously led him into the reception hall.

只见华堂正中，悬一块蓝底金字的匾额，御笔四个大字∶『功襄保赤』。
In the center hung a blue plaque with four large gold characters in the emperor’s own hand: “Meritorious Service in Protecting the People.”

这就是郁馥华此刻去勘察城墙的由来——
The plaque explained why Yu Fuhua was inspecting the wall.

当上海收复时，外国军舰在浦江南码头开炮助攻，从大南门到大东门的城墙，轰坏了一大片；
When Shanghai was retaken, foreign warships had fired from the southern wharf on the Huangpu to support the assault, wrecking a long stretch of wall between the Great South Gate and the Great East Gate.

朝廷以郁家巨宅曾为刘丽川盘踞，郁馥华难免资匪之嫌，罚银十万两修复城墙，
Since Liu Lichuan had occupied the Yu mansion, the court considered its owner open to suspicion of having supported the rebels and fined him one hundred thousand taels to repair the damage.

而经地方官陈情，又御赐了这一方匾额。
After the local officials had pleaded his case, however, the emperor also bestowed this plaque.

如今又有长毛围攻上海的风声；
Rumors were now circulating that the Longhairs would attack Shanghai.

郁馥华怕自己所修的这段城墙，不够坚固；
Yu feared that the stretch he had rebuilt might not be strong enough.

万一将来由此攻破，责任不轻，所以连日勘察，未雨绸缪。
If an enemy broke through there, the responsibility would be serious, so he had spent several days inspecting it and taking precautions.

听郁松年说罢究竟，尤五趁机安了个伏笔，
When Songnian had finished explaining, Brother You took the opportunity to prepare the way for his request.

『令尊一向热心公益，
“Your father has always been devoted to the public good.

好极、好极！』
Excellent, excellent!

他说，『救人就是救己，
In saving others, one saves oneself.

我今天就是为了这件事来的。』
That is what has brought me here today.”

『是！』郁松年很恭敬地问道∶『尤五叔是先吩咐下来，还是等家父到了再谈？』
“Indeed,” Songnian said respectfully. “Would you care to give me your instructions first, Uncle You, or would you prefer to wait for Father?”


『先跟你谈也一样。』于是尤五将胡雪岩间关乞粮的情形，从头细叙；
“I can tell you first; it makes no difference.” Brother You began a detailed account of Hu’s perilous journey in search of grain.

谈到一半郁馥华到家，打断了话头。
Halfway through it, Yu Fuhua came home and interrupted them.

『尤五哥；』
“Brother You!”

郁馥华是个中号胖子，走得上气不接下气，
He was a plump man of medium height, and his haste had left him breathless.

又喘又笑地说，
He spoke between panting and laughing.

『哪阵风把你吹来的。
“What wind has blown you here?

难得，难得！』
What a rare pleasure!”

『无事不登三宝殿，
“One does not visit a temple without a purpose.

有件事来求你；
I have come to ask a favor.

正跟你们老大谈。』
I was just telling your eldest boy.”

郁松年接口提了一句∶『是要运粮到杭州——。』
“It is about shipping grain to Hangzhou—” Songnian began.

郁馥华脑筋极快，手腕极其圆滑，
Yu Fuhua was quick-witted and exceptionally smooth in his dealings.

听他儿子说了一句，立刻就猜想到一大半；
His son’s few words were enough for him to guess most of the story.

急忙打岔说∶『好说，好说！
He hastened to interrupt. “Of course, of course!

尤五哥的事，总好商量。
Anything concerning Brother You can be discussed.

先坐定下来；
First, settle yourself comfortably.

多时不见，谈谈近况。
We have not met for so long; let us hear how you have been.

尤五哥，你的气色好啊，
You are looking well, Brother You.

要交鸿运了！』
Good fortune must be on its way!”

『托福、托福。
“Thanks to your good wishes.

郁老大，今天我来——。』
Chief Yu, I have come today—”


『我晓得，我晓得。』
“I know, I know.”

郁馥华不容他谈正事；
Yu Fuhua would not let him begin the business.

转脸向他儿子说道∶『你进去告诉你娘，尤五叔来了；
Turning to his son, he said, “Go in and tell your mother that Uncle You is here.

做几样菜来请请尤五叔，要你娘亲手做。
Ask her to prepare a few dishes for him—with her own hands.

现成的「糟钵头」拿来吃酒，
Bring out the pot of wine-lees stew that is ready, and we shall have a drink.

我跟你尤五叔今天要好好叙一叙。』
Your Uncle You and I must have a proper talk today.”

尤五早就听说，郁馥华已是百万身价，起居豪奢；
Brother You had long heard that Yu Fuhua was worth a million and lived in great luxury.

如今要他结发妻子下厨，亲手治馔款客，
Now he was asking the wife who had shared his early years to go into the kitchen and cook for his guest herself.

足见不以富贵骄人，
Clearly he did not intend to put on airs because of his wealth.

这点象熬不忘贫贱之交的意思，倒着实可感，也就欣然接受了盛情。
This gesture of remembering friendships formed in humbler days was genuinely touching, and Brother You gladly accepted the hospitality.
