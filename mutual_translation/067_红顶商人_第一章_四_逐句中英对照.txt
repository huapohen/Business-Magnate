胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第一章（本章第4部分，共6部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


摆上酒来，宾主相向相坐；
The wine was brought out, and host and guest sat facing each other.

郁馥华学做官人家的派头，子弟侍立执役，
Yu Fuhua had adopted the style of an official household, with the younger members standing by to wait on their elders.

任凭尤五怎么说，郁松年不敢陪席。
However much Brother You urged him, Songnian dared not join them at the table.

等他执壶替客人斟满了，郁复华郑重其事地双手举杯，高与鼻齐，专敬尤五；
Once he had filled the guest’s cup, Yu Fuhua solemnly raised his own in both hands to the level of his nose and offered a toast to Brother You alone.

自然有两句要紧话要交代。
Naturally, there were a few important words to be said.

『五哥，』他说，『这几年多有不到的地方，
“Brother Five, I have been remiss in many ways these past few years.

一切都请包涵。
I beg you to overlook it all.

江海一家，无分南北西东；
River and sea belong to one family; north, south, east, or west makes no difference.

以后要请五哥随处指点照应。』
From now on, I shall rely on your guidance and help.”

说着，仰脸干了酒，翻杯一照。
He tipped back his head, drained the wine, and turned the empty cup toward his guest.

尤五既为修好而来，自然也干了杯，
Since Brother You had come to make peace, he drank his own cup dry as well.

『郁老大，』他也照一照杯，『过去的事，今天一笔勾销。
“Chief Yu,” he said, showing that it was empty, “let us wipe out the past today.

江海一家这句话不假，
River and sea do belong to one family.

不过有些地方，也要请老大你手下的弟兄，高抬贵手！』
Still, in certain matters, I must ask you to have your men show us a little consideration.”

『言重、言重！』郁馥华惶恐地说了这一句，
“You are too kind, too kind!” Yu said with an air of trepidation.

转脸问道∶『看福全在不在？』
Then he turned aside. “See whether Fuquan is here.”


尤五也知道这个人，是帮郁复华创业的得力助手；如今也是面团团的富家翁。
Brother You knew the man: he had been an able assistant to Yu Fuhua when the latter was building his fortune and was now a prosperous, round-faced gentleman himself.

当时将他唤了来，不待郁复华有所言语，便兜头作了个大揖，
Summoned into the room, Fuquan did not wait for his employer to speak, but immediately made a deep bow.

满脸暗笑地寒暄∶『尤五叔，你老人家还认得我吧？』
With a broad smile he offered his greeting. “Uncle You, you still remember me, I hope?”

『喔，』
“Oh!”

尤五有意眨一眨眼，作出惊喜的神气，
Brother You deliberately blinked and assumed an expression of delighted surprise.

『是福全哥，
“Brother Fuquan!

你发福了。』
You have filled out.”

『不敢当，不敢当。尤五叔，
“I would not presume to be called your brother, Uncle You.

你叫我小名好了。』
Just call me Fuquan.”

『真的，他们是小辈；
“Indeed, they are all your juniors.

尤五哥你客气倒是见外了。』
Such politeness only puts a distance between us, Brother You.”

郁馥华接着转脸告诫福全∶『你关照下去，江海一家，
Yu then turned to instruct Fuquan. “Pass the word: river and sea are one family.

松江漕帮的弟兄，要当自己人一样，处处尊敬、处处礼让。
The men of the Songjiang Grain-Boat Guild are to be treated as our own, with respect and consideration in everything.

尤五叔有啥吩咐，就跟我的话一式一样。』他说一句，福全答应一句；
Whatever Uncle You orders is exactly as if I had said it myself.” Fuquan acknowledged each instruction.

神态不但严肃，而且诚恳。
His manner was not merely solemn, but sincere.

江湖上讲究的是『受人一尺，还人一丈』；
In their world, a man repaid a foot of kindness with ten feet in return.

尤五见此光景，少不得也有一番推诚相与、谦虚退让的话交代。
Seeing this, Brother You could hardly fail to respond with assurances of trust, friendship, modesty, and mutual consideration.


多时宿怨，一旦解消，郁馥华相当高兴。
Yu Fuhua was delighted to see a long-standing feud dissolved in a single meeting.

从利害关系来说，沙船帮虽然兴旺一时，而漕帮到底根深蒂固，势力不同，
In terms of their respective interests, although the Sea-Junk Guild was prospering for the moment, the Grain-Boat Guild had deep roots and a different order of influence.

所以两帮言归于好，在沙船帮更尤其来得重要。
Reconciliation therefore mattered even more to the sea-junk men.

郁馥华是个极有算计的人，觉得这件事值得大大铺张一番；
Yu was an exceptionally calculating man, and he felt that the occasion deserved a splendid public celebration.

传出去是尤五自己愿意修好，岂不是足可以增加光彩与声势的一件好事？
If word spread that Brother You had come voluntarily to restore friendly relations, would that not add greatly to Yu’s prestige and standing?

打定了主意，当即表示，就在这几天，要挑个黄道吉日，大摆筵宴，略申敬意。
Having made up his mind, he immediately announced that he would choose an auspicious day within the next few days and give a grand banquet as a modest token of respect.

言语恳切，尤五不能也不宜推辞；
He spoke so earnestly that Brother You could neither refuse nor properly wish to.

当下未吃先谢，算是定了局。
He thanked his host for the meal before it had even been prepared, and the matter was settled.


这一下情分就更觉不同，
Their friendship now seemed closer still.

郁馥华豪饮快谈，兴致极好。
Yu Fuhua drank freely and talked merrily, in the highest of spirits.

尤五却颇为焦急，
Brother You, however, was growing anxious.

他是有要紧事要谈，哪有心思叙旧？
He had urgent business to discuss; how could he give his mind to reminiscence?

但又不便扫他的高兴；
Yet he could hardly spoil his host’s pleasure.

这样下去，等主人喝得酪酊大醉，岂不白来一趟？
If this went on until Yu was hopelessly drunk, would the whole visit not have been wasted?

等了又等，也是忍了又忍，
He waited and waited, restraining himself again and again.

快将忍不住时，郁松年看出苗头，提醒他父亲说∶『爹！尤五叔有事要跟爹商量呢！』
Just as his patience was about to give out, Songnian noticed and reminded his father, “Father, Uncle You has something to discuss with you.”

『喔，喔，是的。』
“Oh, yes, of course.”

郁馥华不能再装马虎了，
Yu could no longer pretend to have forgotten.

随即转脸说道∶『尤五哥，你倒请再说一遍看。』
He turned to his guest. “Please tell me again, Brother You.”


『是这样的，
“It is this.

有一批米，要借重老大你的船；
There is a consignment of rice for which we need your boats.

走海道，由海宁进鳖子门，入钱塘江，运到杭州。』
It must go by sea, enter Biezimen at Haining, and travel up the Qiantang River to Hangzhou.

尤五又说，『杭州城里的百姓，不但吃草根树皮，
The people inside the city are no longer merely eating roots and bark.

在吃人肉了；
They are eating human flesh.

所以这件事务必要请老大你帮忙，越快越好。』
We absolutely must have your help, Chief Yu, and the sooner the better.”

『尤五哥，你的事，一句话。
“For anything of yours, Brother You, you have only to say the word.

不过，沙船帮的情形，瞒不过你，
But you know our circumstances.

鳖子门这条路从来没有去过，
The Sea-Junk Guild has never taken the Biezimen route.

水性不熟，会得搁浅，
We do not know the waters and might run aground.

岂不耽误大事？』
That could ruin the whole undertaking.”

他紧接着说，
He hurried on.

『当然，漕帮弟兄可以领路，不过沙船走到江里，路道不对。
“Of course, your grain-boat men could pilot us, but taking sea junks into the river is not the proper way to use them.

这样子，我马上找人来商量，
Let me send for some people at once and discuss it.

总要想条万全之计。
We must find a plan that is safe in every respect.

好不好明天给你回话？』
May I give you an answer tomorrow?”


听得这一说，尤五颇为不悦；
Brother You was distinctly displeased.

心里在想，这种兵荒马乱的时候，到哪里都是冒险；
In such a time of war and disorder, he thought, where could one go without taking risks?

就算承平时候，风涛险恶，也没有什么保险不出事的把握。
Even in peace, with treacherous winds and waves, no one could guarantee freedom from mishap.

说要想一条万全之计，不就是有心推托？
Was this talk of finding a wholly safe plan not simply a deliberate excuse?

想是这样想，当然决没有发作的道理，
He thought so, but there was naturally no question of losing his temper.

不过话要点他一句，
Still, he had to give the man a hint.

『郁老大，』他说，『亲兄弟，明算帐，
“Chief Yu, even brothers keep clear accounts.

人情归人情，生意归生意；
Friendship is friendship, and business is business.

请你仔细盘算一下，
Work out the figures carefully.

运费出公帐，
The freight will be paid from public funds.

何必放着河水不洗船？』
Why leave the river water unused when you could wash your boat in it?”

『言重，言重！
“Please, please!

尤五哥，你误会了，
You misunderstand me, Brother You.

我决不是在这上头打算盘。
I am certainly not calculating my advantage in that respect.

为的是┅┅。』郁馥华觉得怎么样说都不合适，
My concern is—” Whatever he said seemed wrong.

而且也要问问路上的情形，便改口问道∶『尤五哥，
He also needed to learn more about conditions along the route, so he changed tack.

那位胡道台，我久仰大名，
“I have long heard of that Circuit Intendant Hu.

好不好领我会一会他？』
Might you introduce me to him?”

胡道台就是胡雪岩；
Circuit Intendant Hu was Hu Xueyan.

这几年连捐带保，官运亨通，成了浙江省城里亦官亦商的一位特殊人物；
Over the past few years, purchased rank and recommendations for promotion had carried him smoothly upward, making him a peculiar figure in Zhejiang’s provincial capital: part official, part merchant.

尤五原就有意替他们拉拢见一面，
Brother You had intended to bring the two men together in any case.

现在郁馥华自己开口，当然毫无推辞，
Now that Yu had asked, he agreed without hesitation.

而且表示∶『说走就走，
“We can go this moment, if you wish.

悉听尊便。』
I am entirely at your disposal.”


『今天太匆促了！
“Today would be too hurried.

一则喝了酒，二则，草草未免不恭。
For one thing, I have been drinking; for another, a hastily arranged call might seem disrespectful.

准定明天一早，我去拜访；
I shall certainly visit first thing tomorrow.

不知道胡道台耽搁在哪里？』
Where is Circuit Intendant Hu staying?”

『他住在舍亲古应春家。
“At the house of my relative, Gu Yingchun.

明天一早我来接。』
I shall come and fetch you in the morning.”

『原来是老古那里。
“So he is at Old Gu’s!

我们也是熟人，他府上我去过；
We know each other too, and I have been to his house.

不必劳驾，
Do not trouble yourself.

我自己去就是了。』
I can go on my own.”

谈到这里，告一段落；而且酒也够了，
That brought their discussion to a close, and they had drunk enough as well.

尤五起身告辞。
Brother You rose to take his leave.

一回到古家，七姑奶奶迎上前来，
When he returned to Gu’s house, the Seventh Lady came forward to meet him.

虽未开口，那双眼睛却比开口还显得关切。
Even before she spoke, her eyes expressed a concern stronger than words.


『怎么样？』
“Well?”

尤五不答，只问胡雪岩的伤势如何？
Brother You did not answer, but asked after Hu’s injury.

这倒是使得七姑奶奶可以高兴的，
Here the Seventh Lady had good news.

夸赞伤科医生有本事；胡雪岩的痛楚大减，伤口好得很快，预计三天以后，就可以下床走动了。
She praised the doctor’s skill in treating wounds: Hu was in far less pain, the wound was healing quickly, and in three days he should be able to get out of bed and walk.

『这也是人到了这里，心就安了。』七姑奶奶又说，
“Part of it is that his mind has been at rest since he arrived here,” she added.

『人逢喜事精神爽，
“Good news lifts a man’s spirits.

郁老大如果肯帮忙；真比吃什么药都有用。』
If Chief Yu will help, it will do him more good than any medicine.”

『帮忙是肯帮的，事情没有那么快。
“He is willing to help, but these things cannot be settled so quickly.

先跟小爷叔谈了再说。』
Let me speak to Little Uncle first.”


于是从头谈起。
Brother You began his account.

一旁静听的七姑奶奶，先是一直含着笑；
The Seventh Lady stood listening beside them, smiling throughout the first part.

听到郁馥华说要明天才有回话，一下子跳了起来。
When he reached Yu Fuhua’s promise to give an answer the next day, she sprang up.

『这明明是推托嘛！』
“That is plainly an excuse!”

『七姐，』胡雪岩赶紧拦住她说∶『人家有人家为难的地方。
Hu hurried to restrain her. “Seventh Sister, he has difficulties of his own.

你先不要着急；
Do not be anxious yet.

慢慢儿商量。』
Let us talk it through.”

『我是替你着急，小爷叔！』
“It is for you that I am anxious, Little Uncle!”


『我晓得，我晓得。』胡雪岩依旧从容不迫地，
“I know, I know,” Hu replied, as composed as ever.

『换了我是郁老大，也不能不仔细；
“If I were Chief Yu, I too should have to consider the matter carefully.

海面上没有啥，一进了鳖子门，走在钱塘江里，两岸都是长毛，
There is little to fear at sea, but once we pass through Biezimen and enter the Qiantang, the Longhairs are on both banks.

他自然要担足心事。
Naturally, he has every reason to be worried.

这件事只有这样办，
There is only one way to proceed.

一方面，我们要跟他说实话，哪里有危险，哪里没有危险，出了危险，怎么样应付？
On the one hand, we must tell him the truth: where the danger lies, where it does not, and what we shall do if trouble comes.

一方面得要请他放点交情；冒一冒险。
On the other, we must ask him to put friendship before caution and take a risk.

俗语说∶』前半夜想想人家，后半夜想想自己。
As the saying goes, spend the first half of the night thinking about others and the second half thinking about yourself.

「我们现在先想自己，有什么好处到人家那里；人家肯看交情上头，一冒一冒险。
For the moment, let us consider our own part: what can we do for him that will make him willing, out of friendship, to take that risk?”

『』对！
“Quite right!”

『尤五不胜倾倒，』
Brother You was full of admiration.

小爷叔这两句话入情入理；
“Little Uncle, that is both reasonable and fair.

照这样去想，事情就可以办通了。
If we approach it that way, we can get the thing done.”

『』好吧！
“Very well!”

『七姑奶奶无可奈何；
The Seventh Lady could do nothing with them.

转个念头，自己女流之辈，可以不必来管这桩大事，
Then she reflected that, as a woman, she need not concern herself with such weighty affairs.

便即说∶』天塌下来有长人顶，
“If the sky falls, there are taller people to hold it up.

与我不相干，
It is nothing to do with me.

你们去商量。
You can discuss it yourselves.”

『说完转身就走。
She turned to go.


『七姐！』胡雪岩急忙喊道∶『有件事非跟你商量不可。
“Seventh Sister!” Hu called after her. “There is something I simply must consult you about.

你请回来！』
Please come back!”

她自然又立脚站定。
Naturally, she stopped.

胡雪岩原是听她的话近乎赌气，其实并没有什么事要她商量，
He had only called because her words sounded rather petulant; in fact, he had nothing to ask her.

不过既已说出口，倒又不得不找件事跟她商量了。
Now that he had spoken, however, he had to find something.

灵机一动，
An idea flashed into his mind.

开口只道∶『七姐，上海我半年不曾来过了，
“Seventh Sister, I have not been in Shanghai for six months.

最近有没有好的棺子？』
Have any good restaurants opened lately?”

『有啊！』七姑奶奶答道∶『新开一家泰和馆，
“Yes!” she replied. “There is a new one called Taihe.

一统山河的南北口味，
It serves northern and southern dishes from all over the country.

我吃过几次，
I have eaten there several times.

菜刮刮叫。』
The food is first-rate.”

『地方呢，宽敞不宽敞？』
“And the place itself—is it spacious?”

『岂止宽敞？
“Spacious? It is more than that!

庆兴楼、复新园、鸿运楼，数得出的几家大馆子，哪一家都没有它讲究。』
Qingxinglou, Fuxinyuan, Hongyunlou—none of the big restaurants you can name is so splendidly fitted out.”

七姑奶奶问道∶『小爷叔，你是不是要请客？』
She looked at him. “Are you thinking of giving a banquet, Little Uncle?”


『我的心思瞒不过七姐。』
“Nothing escapes you, Seventh Sister.”

胡雪岩笑着回答，是有意恭维她一句；
Hu smiled; the compliment was deliberate.

然后转脸看着尤五说∶『五哥，你既然委屈了，索性看我们杭州一城百姓的面上，委屈到底，
Then he turned to Brother You. “Brother Five, since you have already humbled yourself, do it to the end for the sake of all the people of Hangzhou.

请你出面请个客拿郁老大手下的大小脚色都请到；
Give a banquet in your name and invite every man under Chief Yu, great or small.

我们漕帮弟兄，最好也都到场，
It would be best if all our grain-boat men were there too.

给足了他面子，看他怎么说？』
Let us pay him every possible honor and see what he says.”

『好的。
“Certainly.

一句话。』
You have only to ask.”

『那就要托七姐，定泰和馆的席。
“Then we must trouble Seventh Sister to book the tables at Taihe.

名归五哥出，钱归我出┅┅。』『这用不着你交代。』七姑奶奶抢着说，
The invitation will be in Brother Five’s name, and I shall pay—” “You need not tell me that,” she broke in.

『就不知道有多少人；要定多少桌席。』
“What I need to know is how many guests there will be, and how many tables to order.”

这当然要问尤五，
That, of course, was for Brother You to answer.

他慢吞吞地答道∶『要么不请；请了就不管他多少人了。
“If we are going to invite them at all,” he said slowly, “we need not worry about how many.

我只一张帖子，统请沙船帮全体弟兄；
I shall send a single invitation to every brother in the Sea-Junk Guild.

拿泰和馆包下来，开流水席，有一桌算一桌。』
We shall take the whole restaurant and serve a continuous banquet: as many tables as are needed.”

『这倒也痛快。
“That is a generous way to do it.

就这么说了。』
Agreed.”

胡雪岩向七姑奶奶拱拱手∶『拜托、拜托！』
Hu clasped his hands toward the Seventh Lady. “I leave it in your capable hands.”


七姑奶奶最喜欢排场热闹，一诺无辞；
The Seventh Lady loved a grand, lively occasion and agreed at once.

但粗中有细，
Beneath her expansive manner, however, she had an eye for detail.

想了想问道∶『哪一天请？』
After a moment’s thought she asked, “What day?”

『不是要快嘛！』尤五答说，
“Are we not in a hurry?” Brother You replied.

『要快就在明天。』
“If we want to be quick, make it tomorrow.”

七姑奶奶不作声，将排在门背后的皇历取了下来，翻了翻说∶『明天怕不成功，
Without a word, she took down the almanac hanging behind the door and turned its pages. “Tomorrow may not be possible.

是好日子；总有人做亲，在它那里请客。
It is an auspicious day; someone is sure to be holding a wedding feast there.

后天是个平日，「宜祭祀、订盟、余事不宜。」
The day after is an ordinary day: ‘Suitable for sacrifices and the making of covenants; unsuitable for other undertakings.’

不晓得可以不可以？』
Would that do?”

『可以！』胡雪岩接口便说∶『我们这就算「订盟」。』
“Certainly,” Hu answered at once. “We can call this the making of a covenant.”

事不宜迟，
There was no time to lose.

七姑奶奶当时便取了一封银洋，亲自坐马车到泰和馆去定席。
The Seventh Lady took a wrapped packet of silver dollars and drove to Taihe herself to arrange the banquet.

尤五便找古家的帐房赵先生来，写好一封大红全帖，送到乔家滨郁家，
Brother You asked Mr Zhao, the Gu household’s accountant, to write out a full formal invitation on red paper and sent it to the Yu residence at Qiaojiabin.

同时又派人去找他一个心爱的徒弟李得隆来办事。
At the same time he summoned a favorite disciple, Li Delong, to help with the arrangements.


他们兄妹在忙，胡雪岩一个人躺在床上盘算；
While brother and sister were busy, Hu lay alone in bed and thought things through.

等尤五再回进来时，他已经盘算停当了。
By the time Brother You returned, his plans were ready.

『五哥，我们现在一桩桩来谈。
“Brother Five, let us take things one at a time.

米怎么样？』
What about the rice?”

『我已经关照下去，
“I have passed the word.

今天下午就可成局。』
We can have it settled this afternoon.

尤五答道∶『虽说多多益善，也要看郁老大有多少船？
The more the better, of course, but we must also see how many boats Chief Yu can provide.

总而言之一句话，只要他有船，我就有米。』
