胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第一章（本章第5部分，共6部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


『既然如此，我们要把宗旨先定下来；
“In that case, we must first decide what we mean to do.

请兵护送的事，能够说动英、法提督，派兵护送，不但力量够强，足可保险，而且还不用花钱，
As for an escort, if we can persuade the British and French admirals to send troops, we shall have a force strong enough to make the voyage safe, and it will cost us nothing.

不过有两层顾虑，
But there are two difficulties.

第一、恐怕仍旧要江苏巡抚出公事；
First, they may still require an official request from the governor of Jiangsu.

第二、不是三、五天之内可以办得成的。』
Second, it cannot be arranged in three or five days.”

『慢就不行！』胡雪岩立即答说，
“A delay is out of the question!” Hu Xueyan answered at once.

『我现在度日如年，
“Every day seems like a year to me.

巴不得明天就走。』
I wish I could leave tomorrow.”

『要快只有雇华尔的部下。
“Then the only quick solution is to hire some of Ward’s men.

这笔钱，恐怕不在少数。』
That will probably cost a considerable sum.”

『要多少？』
“How much?”

『要看雇多少人？
“It depends on how many we hire.

每个人起码三十两银子；死一下抚恤一千。
At least thirty taels a man, with a thousand in compensation for each man killed.

照五十个人算，最少一千五；
For fifty men, that means fifteen hundred at the very least.

如果——。』如果全数阵亡，就得另外抚恤五万；
And if—” If they were all killed, another fifty thousand would be payable in compensation.

话到口边，古应春才发觉这话太丧气，
Only as the words reached his lips did Gu Yingchun realize how ominous they sounded.

果然如此，胡雪岩的性命自然也就不保，
If that happened, Hu himself would surely not survive.

所以把话硬咽了下去了。
He swallowed the rest of the sentence.


胡雪岩却不以为意，
Hu took no offence.

『一千五就一千五；
“Fifteen hundred it is, then.

带队官总要多送些，
The officer in command will need an extra payment, of course.

我不在乎。
I do not mind.

倒是，』他指着萧家骥说，『他的顾虑不错，
But”—he pointed to Xiao Jiaji—“his concern is sound.

只怕在岸上打惯了仗的，一上了船，有劲使不出，有力用不上。』
Men accustomed to fighting ashore may find, once they are aboard a boat, that all their strength and courage are of no use.”

『这要问他们自己才知道。
“We shall have to ask them.

虽说重赏之下，必有勇夫，性命到底是拿钱换不来的；
A generous reward may bring brave men forward, but money cannot buy back a life.

如果他们没有把握，当然不敢贸然答应。
Unless they feel reasonably certain, they will not agree rashly.

我们局外人，不必自作聪明。』
We are outsiders; there is no need to imagine that we know better.”

古应春最后这句话，颇有告诫学生的意味；因而原有一番意见想陈述的萧家骥，就不便开口了。
Gu’s last remark had something of a master’s admonition to his pupil about it, and Xiao, who had been preparing to offer an opinion, could hardly speak now.


『说到杨坊，我也认识；
“I know Yang Fang, as it happens.

交情虽不深，倒承他不弃，还看得起我。
We are not intimate, but he has been kind enough to think well of me.

今天晚上我就去看他。』
I shall call on him tonight.”

『对了！
“Good!

我们分头行事。
We shall divide the work.

此刻大家规定一下，米跟沙船，归我；请洋将归你。』
Let us settle it now: I shall see to the rice and the sea-going junks; you arrange for the foreign officers.”

尤五对古应春说，
Brother You turned to Gu.

『还有件事，你要调一批现头寸来。』
“One other thing: you must get some ready funds together.”

『这不要紧！』
“That presents no difficulty.”

胡雪岩从手上取下一个戒指，交给古应春∶『我往来的几家号子你是晓得的；
Hu took a ring from his hand and gave it to Gu. “You know the firms with which I do business.

看存着有多少头寸，你随意调度就是。』
Find out how much money I have with them and draw on it as you need.”

戒指是赤金的，
The ring was of pure gold.

没有一两也有八钱，
If it did not weigh a whole tael, it weighed at least eight mace.

其大无比，其俗也无比；
It was enormous, and equally ostentatious.

但实际上是一枚图章 ，
In fact, however, it was a seal.

凭戒面上『胡雪岩印』四个朱文篆字，调集十万八万银子，叱嗟立办。
The four characters on its face, “Seal of Hu Xueyan,” cut in relief in seal script, could summon eighty or a hundred thousand taels almost at a word.

不过以古应春实力，也还用不到此。
Gu’s own resources were sufficient without it.

『不必！』
“There is no need.

你这个戒指片刻不离身，
You never take that ring off.

还是你自己带着。
Keep it yourself.”

『』不然！『胡雪岩说，』
“No,” Hu replied.

我另外还有用意。
“I have another reason.

这一次回杭州，好便好；
If all goes well on this return to Hangzhou, well and good.

如果将来再不能见面，一切托你料理。
But if we never meet again, I entrust everything to you.

人欠欠人，等我明天开出一张单子来交给你。
Tomorrow I shall give you a list of what others owe me and what I owe them.”

『托到后事，无不惨然；
This provision for his own death distressed everyone.

古应春也越发不肯收下他那枚戒指图章 ，
Gu was even more unwilling to accept the seal ring.

拉过他的手来，硬要替他戴上，
Taking Hu’s hand, he tried to force it back onto his finger.

正在拉拉扯扯的时候，七姑奶奶回来了；少不得询问究竟。
They were still struggling over it when the Seventh Lady returned and naturally asked what was going on.

大家都知道她重感情，说破了一定会惹她伤感，所以彼此使了个眼色，随意扯句话掩饰了过去。
Knowing how deeply she felt things, and that the truth would grieve her, they exchanged a glance and put her off with some casual explanation.


『菜定好了，八两银子一桌的海菜席；
“The food is ordered: seafood banquets at eight taels a table.

包他们四十桌。』七姑奶奶说，
We have booked forty tables,” the Seventh Lady announced.

『那里老板说是亏本生意，不过要借这桩生意创招牌。
“The proprietor says he will lose money, but he wants to make his name through this engagement.

人家既然看得这么重，人少了，场面不够热闹，面子上不好看，
Since he attaches so much importance to it, we shall look foolish if too few people come and the occasion falls flat.

五哥，我倒有点担心。』
Brother Five, I am a little worried.”

『担什么心？
“What is there to worry about?

叫人来场面、吃酒席，
We are inviting people to a gathering and a feast.

还怕没有人？
Do you suppose we cannot find enough guests?

回头我会关照李得隆。』
I shall speak to Li Delong presently.”

『那末郁老大那里呢？』
“And Chief Yu?”

『这你更可以放心。
“You need worry even less about him.

小爷叔想的这个办法，在郁老大求之不得，
This plan of Little Uncle’s is just what he could wish for.

来的人一定多。』尤五又说，『你再要不放心，我叫李得隆放个风出去，说我们包了泰和馆，大请沙船帮，
Plenty of his people are sure to come.” Brother You added, “If you are still uneasy, I shall have Li Delong spread the word that we have taken over the Taihe Restaurant to entertain the whole Sea-Junk Guild.

不来就是看不起我们。』
Anyone who stays away will be showing contempt for us.”

『那好。
“Good.

我叫人去通知，再预备十桌在那里。』
I shall send word for another ten tables to be held in readiness.”

七姑奶奶一面说，一面就走了出去。
With that, she went out again.


『七姐真有趣。』胡雪岩笑道∶『好热闹，一定是福气人。』
“Sister Seven is a delightful woman,” Hu said with a smile. “So fond of a lively gathering—she is surely born to good fortune.”

『闲话少说。
“Never mind that.

我还有一桩事，
There is another matter.

应春，你看如何？』尤五说道∶『小爷叔要人帮忙；
Yingchun, what do you think of this?” Brother You continued, “Little Uncle needs men to help him.

我说实话，你我去都没啥用处。
To be frank, neither you nor I would be much use on the journey.

我派李得隆，你派萧家骥，跟了小爷叔一路到杭州。』
I shall send Li Delong, and you can send Xiao Jiaji to accompany him all the way to Hangzhou.”

『嗯１』古应春略有迟疑的神情。
“Well…” Gu looked hesitant.

『不必，不必。』胡雪岩最知趣，赶紧辞谢。
“No, no, there is no need,” Hu hastened to say, sensitive as ever to other people’s difficulties.

古应春实在很为难。
Gu was genuinely in a quandary.

因为萧家骥跟他的关系，与漕帮的情形不同；漕帮开香堂收徒弟，师父之命，其重如山，而且出生入死，不当回事。
His relationship with Xiao differed from those in the Grain-Boat Guild, where disciples were received in a formal initiation, a master’s command carried absolute weight, and men thought nothing of risking death.

萧家骥到底只是学洋文，学做生意的徒弟，
Xiao was merely his apprentice in foreign languages and business.

到这种性命出入的事，不便勉强，要问问他本人。
In a matter of life and death, he could not compel him; he must ask the young man himself.

但是胡雪岩这方面的交情，实在太厚；能有一分力，一定要尽一分力，
Yet his friendship with Hu was so deep that, wherever he could help, he was bound to do all he could.

决说不出推辞的话来。
He could not bring himself to refuse.

同时看出胡雪岩口称『不必』；脸上却有失望的表情，越觉得过意不过去了。
Moreover, although Hu said there was no need, his face betrayed disappointment, which made Gu feel worse.

想一想只有老实说∶『小爷叔，如果我有个亲兄弟，我都一定叫他跟了你去。
After considering the matter, he could only speak frankly. “Little Uncle, if I had a brother of my own, I would certainly send him with you.

家骥名为徒弟，到底姓萧；
Jiaji may be my apprentice, but he belongs to the Xiao family.

我来问问他看。』
Let me ask him.”

说到这里，发觉话又不妥，如果萧家骥胆怯不肯去；岂不又显得自己的徒弟『不够料』，
Then it struck him that this too was ill judged: if Xiao proved afraid and refused, would it not make his apprentice seem unworthy?

因而只好再加一句掩饰的话∶『他老太太病在床上，
He added an excuse to cover a possible refusal. “His mother is ill in bed.

如果病势不碍；我想他一定会去的。』
If her condition permits, I am sure he will go.”

话刚完，门外有人接口，是萧家骥的声音；
He had scarcely finished when Xiao’s voice came from outside the door.

他正好走了来听见，自告奋勇∶『我去！
Arriving just in time to hear, he volunteered at once. “I shall go!

我一定去！』
Of course I shall go!”

这一下解消了古应春的难题；也觉得脸上很有光彩，
This solved Gu’s difficulty and did him credit as well.

但胡雪岩却不能不辞谢——
Hu, however, still had to make a polite refusal.

他也知道萧家骥母亲病在床上的话，是古应春为了体恤徒弟，有意留下的一个退步。
He knew that the sick mother was an excuse Gu had provided out of consideration for his apprentice, leaving him a way to retreat.

只是『光棍好做，过门难逃』；而且这个『过门』，古应春不便来打，
But even men who understood one another still had to observe the proper forms; and this particular formality could not be left to Gu.

要自己开口。
Hu must say the words himself.


『家骥，我晓得你义气，
“Jiaji, I know you are loyal to your friends.

不过为人忠孝当先，
But loyalty and filial duty come first.

令堂老太太身体不舒服，你该留下来侍奉。』
Since your mother is unwell, you ought to stay and look after her.”

『不碍，不碍！』
“It is nothing serious!”

萧家骥也很机警，很快地答说∶『我娘胃气痛是老毛病；
Xiao, quick to take the hint, answered promptly. “She has an old stomach complaint.

两三天就好了。』
She will be better in two or three days.”

『那就这样吧！』
“Then it is settled.”

古应春站起身来∶『既然你要跟了去，一切事情要接得上头才好；
Gu rose. “Since you are going, you had better be involved in the arrangements from the beginning.

你跟我一起去看「大记」杨老板。』
Come with me to see Mr Yang at Daji.”

杨坊开的一家专销洋庄的号子，就叫『大记』；
Daji was the name of Yang Fang’s firm, which dealt exclusively in goods for the foreign trade.

师徒二人到了那里，杨坊正在大宴客商，
When master and apprentice arrived, Yang was giving a large dinner for visiting merchants.

相邀入座应酬一番，亦无不可；但古应春为了表示事态紧急，
They could quite properly have accepted his invitation to join the company and exchanged the usual courtesies, but Gu wished to make clear the urgency of his business.

坚辞婉拒；同时表示有个不情之请∶需要当然就单独交谈。
He declined politely but firmly, adding that he had an awkward favour to ask. Naturally, it would have to be discussed in private.

『好！』杨坊慨然许诺，
“Certainly!” Yang readily agreed.

『请到这面来。』
“Come this way.”

就在客厅一角，促膝并坐；
They sat close together in a corner of the reception room.

古应春开门见山地道明来意，
Gu came straight to the point.

杨坊吸了口气，样子显得颇为棘手似地。
Yang drew in his breath, looking as though the request presented considerable difficulty.

『杨兄，恕我再说句不该说的话，
“Brother Yang, forgive me for saying something more that I ought perhaps to leave unsaid.

浙东浙西，休戚相关；
Eastern and western Zhejiang share their fortunes and their troubles.

看在贵省同乡的面上，无论如何要请你想办法。』
For the sake of the people of your own province, you must somehow find a way.”

『我自然要想办法，
“Of course I shall try.

自然要想办法。』杨坊一叠连声地说∶『为难的是，最近华尔跟吴道台闹意气。
Of course I shall.” Yang repeated the assurance several times. “The difficulty is that Ward has recently quarrelled with Intendant Wu.

洋人的脾气很倔，
Foreigners can be very stubborn.

说好什么都好；犯了他的性子，不容易说得进话去。
Get on the right side of him, and anything is possible; cross him, and it is hard to make him listen.

现在只有这样∶我先派人去约他，今天晚上见个面。
The only thing to do now is send someone to arrange a meeting with him tonight.

等我敷衍完了客人，我们一起去；
Once I have finished attending to my guests, we can go together.

便菜便酒，你何妨就在这里坐了。
Meanwhile, stay and have a little food and wine.”


说到这话，古应春自然不便再推辞；
At this, Gu could hardly decline again.

入席酬酢，同时在肚子里盘算，如何说动华尔？
He joined the dinner and exchanged courtesies while turning over in his mind the problem of persuading Ward.

『师父，我想我先回去一趟，等下再来。』萧家骥忽然说道∶『我要好好去问一问胡先生。』
Suddenly Xiao said, “Master, I think I ought to go back for a while and return later. I must question Mr Hu carefully.”

『问什么？』
“About what?”

洋人做事情仔细，又是打仗；
“Foreigners are very thorough in their work, and this involves fighting.

路上的情形，一定要问得清清楚楚。
They are sure to want a precise account of the conditions along the route.

不然决不肯答应。
Otherwise they will never agree.”

『』一点不错。『杨坊大为赞许，』
“Exactly!” Yang said approvingly.

这位小阿弟实在有见识。
“This young fellow has excellent judgement.

那你就快去吧！
Go quickly, then.

两个钟头谈得完谈不完？
Will two hours be enough for your questions?”

『『够了。』
“Quite enough.”

『好。
“Good.

我就约华尔九点钟碰头；
I shall arrange to meet Ward at nine.

八点半钟请你无论如何赶了来。』
Whatever happens, be back here by half past eight.”


萧家骥不到顶定的时间，就已去而复回；
Xiao returned before the appointed time.

除了将他想到该问的情形都问明白以外，还带来胡雪岩一句话。
Besides obtaining answers to all the questions he had thought necessary, he brought a message from Hu.

『师父！胡先生叫我跟师父说∶请将不如激将！』
“Master, Mr Hu asked me to tell you: ‘A challenge will move a commander more readily than a request.’”

这真有点『军师』的味道了；运筹帷幄，决胜千里，付下来这样一个『锦囊』。
This had the true flavour of a strategist’s advice: directing events from the command tent, he had sent out a secret prescription for victory at a distance.

古应春在颠簸的马车上，反复体味着『请将不如激将』这六个字。
As the carriage jolted along, Gu pondered Hu’s brief maxim over and over.

华尔扎营在沪西静安寺附近；
Ward was encamped near Jing’an Temple in western Shanghai.

杨坊是来惯的，
Yang was a familiar visitor.

营门口的卫兵拿马灯一照，挥挥手放行，
The sentry at the gate held up a lantern, took a look, and waved them through.

马车一直驶到华尔的『签押房』。
The carriage drove straight to Ward’s office.


介绍过后，四个人围坐在一张小圆台上；
After the introductions, the four men sat around a small round table.

杨坊开个头，说古应春是浙江官场的代表之一，有事相恳。
Yang began by saying that Gu was one of the representatives of the Zhejiang authorities and had a favour to ask.

接着便由古应春发言，首先补充杨坊的话，表明自己的身分，
Gu then took over, first clarifying his position.

说浙江官场的正式代表是胡雪岩；一个受有清朝官职的很成功的商人，而他是胡雪岩所委派的代表。
The authorities’ official representative, he explained, was Hu Xueyan, a highly successful merchant who held office under the Qing government; he himself had been appointed to act on Hu’s behalf.

说到这里，华尔提出第一个疑问∶『胡先生为什么要委派代表？』
At this, Ward raised his first question. “Why has Mr Hu sent a representative?”

『他受伤了，伤势很重；
“He has been wounded, quite seriously.

为了希望在三到五天以内赶回去，他需要遵守医生的嘱咐，绝不能行动。』
He hopes to return within three to five days, and to do so he must obey the doctor’s orders and remain absolutely still.

古应春说∶『他就住在我家养伤。』
He is recovering at my house.”

『喔！』
“I see.”

华尔是谅解的神态∶『请你说下去。』于是古应春道及本意，
Ward appeared to understand. “Please go on.” Gu explained his purpose.

提出希望以外，还有一番恭维；说华尔一定会站在人道的立场，助成这场义举，而他的勇敢的部下，亦一定会圆满达成任务。
Along with his request, he offered a generous compliment: Ward, he was sure, would support this charitable undertaking on humanitarian grounds, and his brave men would certainly carry out the mission successfully.


说到一半，华尔已在不断摇头；
Before Gu was halfway through, Ward was already shaking his head.

等他说完，随即用冷峻的声音答道∶『抱歉！
As soon as he finished, Ward answered coldly, “I am sorry.

我很同情，但是没有办法给你们什么帮助。』
You have my sympathy, but I can do nothing to help you.”

『这太教我失望了。』
“That is a great disappointment.

古应春问道∶『你能不能告诉我，不能予以帮助的原因？』
May I ask why you cannot help?”

『当然！
“Certainly.

第一，浙江不是我应该派兵的范围；
First, Zhejiang lies outside the area in which I am supposed to deploy troops.

第一，任务很危险，
And the mission is dangerous.

我没有把握。』
I cannot be sure of success.”

『第一个理由，似乎不成立。
“Your first reason hardly seems valid.

我已经说过，这是慈善任务——。』『不！』华尔抢着说∶『我有我的立场。』
As I have already explained, this is a charitable mission—” “No!” Ward interrupted. “I have my responsibilities.”

『你的立场不是助顺——帮助中国政府吗？』
“Are they not to support the established authorities—to assist the Chinese government?”

『是的。』华尔很勉强地说，
“Yes,” Ward said reluctantly.

『我必须先顾到上海。』
“But Shanghai must come first.”

『但是，抽调五十个人，不致于影响你的实力。』
“Detaching fifty men would scarcely impair your strength.”

『是不是会影响，要我来判断。』
“Whether it would or not is for me to judge.”


『上校，』杨坊帮着说好话，『大家都对你抱着莫大的希望，
“Colonel,” Yang put in soothingly, “everyone has placed great hopes in you.

你不应该这样坚拒。』
You should not refuse so categorically.”

『不！』华尔仅自摇头，
“No!” Ward continued to shake his head.

『任务太危险。
“The mission is too dangerous.

这是毫无价值的冒险。』
It is a worthless risk.”

『并不危险！』
“It is not dangerous.”

古应春指萧家骥说∶『他可以为你解释一切情况。』
Gu pointed to Xiao. “He can explain all the circumstances.”

『不！
“No.

我不需要听他的解释。』
I do not need his explanation.”


这样子拒人于千里之外，且大有藐视之意，古应春忍不住火发，
This flat refusal to hear them, with its unmistakable suggestion of contempt, angered Gu.

想到胡雪岩的话，立即有了计较，
Remembering Hu’s advice, he immediately saw what to do.

冷笑一声，面凝寒霜地对杨坊说∶『人言不可信。

都说客将讲公理正义，急人之急，忠勇奋发；

谁知道完全不是这回事。
Who would have thought it was nothing of the kind?

一群胆怯贪利的佣兵而已！』
They are merely a pack of cowardly, money-grubbing mercenaries!”

说到最后这一句，华尔勃然变色；
At the last words, Ward’s expression changed violently.

霍地站起来，居高临下地俯视着古应春喝道∶『你说谁是胆怯贪利的佣兵？』
He sprang up and glared down at Gu. “Whom are you calling cowardly, money-grubbing mercenaries?”

“You ought to know.”

We are not mercenaries.”

“Of course.”

是薛还是吴；
Is it Xue or Wu?

Just give me the name, and I shall find a way.”

这一下击中了华尔的要害，
This touched Ward’s most vulnerable point.

如果承认有人可以指挥他；那末找了可以指挥他的人来下命令，
If he admitted that someone could command him, they could simply have that person issue an order.

岂不是自贬身分。
Would that not diminish his own standing?

『说老实话，贪利这一点，也许我过分了；
Gu continued, “To be fair, perhaps I went too far in accusing you of greed.

But I do not admit that I was wrong to call you a coward.”

『你最大的错误，就是这一点。
“That is your greatest mistake.

Do you realize what an insult it is to call a soldier a coward?”


古应春丝毫不让，针锋相对地顶了过去∶『如果是侮辱；也因为你自己的表现就是如此！』
Gu gave no ground, meeting him word for word. “If it is an insult, your own conduct has invited it.”

『什么！』
“What!”

华尔一把抓住了古应春的肩，使劲地摇撼着∶『你说！
Ward seized him by the shoulder and shook him hard. “Tell me!

我何处有胆怯的表现？』
How have I shown cowardice?”

一看他要动武，萧家骥护师心切，首先就横身阻挡；
Seeing violence threaten his master, Xiao immediately stepped between them.

接着杨坊也来相劝，无奈华尔的气力大，又是盛怒之际，死不放手。
Yang also tried to intervene, but Ward was strong and furious, and would not let go.

古应春却是神色泰然，
Gu remained perfectly composed.

冷冷说道∶『凡是胆怯的人，都是勇于私斗的。』
“Cowards are always brave enough in private quarrels,” he said coldly.

一句话说得华尔放了手，
At that, Ward released him.

转身对杨坊说道∶『我必须维持我的威信；
Turning to Yang, he declared, “I must maintain my authority.

此人的行为，所侮辱的不是个人，是整个团体。
This man has insulted not merely an individual but the entire force.

这件事相当严重。
It is a serious matter.

如果他没有合理的解释，他将要担负一切不良的后果。』
Unless he has a satisfactory explanation, he will be responsible for all the consequences.”

杨坊不知道古应春葫芦里卖的什么药？不免怨责∶『这样子不大好！
Yang had no idea what Gu was trying to do and could not help reproaching him. “This is hardly the way to proceed.

本是来求人的事，
We came to ask a favour.

怎么大破其脸？
Why provoke an open quarrel?

如今，有点不大好收场了。』
It will be difficult to put matters right now.”

他是用中国话说的，古应春便也用中国话回答他∶『你放心！
He had spoken in Chinese, and Gu answered in the same language. “Do not worry.

我就要逼得他这个样子！
This is precisely the position I wanted to force him into.

我当然有合理的解释。
Of course I have a satisfactory explanation.”

『杨坊哪知道他是依照胡雪岩』请将不如激将『这条』锦囊妙计『，另有妙用；
Yang knew nothing of the further purpose behind Hu’s clever prescription of challenging a commander rather than petitioning him.

只郑重其事地一再嘱咐∶』千万平和，千万平和，不要弄出纠纷来。『
He could only urge Gu repeatedly, and with great earnestness, to keep calm and avoid a dispute.


『你请放心，
“Set your mind at rest.

除非他蛮不讲理，不然一定会服我。』古应春用中国话说了这几句；转脸用英语向华尔说∶『上校！杭州有几十万人，濒临饿死的命运；

他们需要粮食，跟你我现在需要呼吸一样。
They need food as you and I need air at this moment.

如果由于你的帮助，冒险通过这条航路，将粮食运到杭州，有几十万人得以活命。
With your help, we could risk this passage and carry grain into the city, saving hundreds of thousands of lives.

这是「毫无价值的冒险」吗？』
Is that a ‘worthless risk’?”

一句话就将华尔问住了。
The question silenced Ward.

他卷了根烟就着洋灯点燃，在浓密的烟氛中喷出答语∶『冒这个险，没有成功的可能。』
He rolled a cigarette, lit it at the lamp, and sent his answer out through a thick cloud of smoke. “There is no possibility of success.”

『是不是有可能，我们先不谈；
“Let us leave the possibility aside for a moment.

Answer my question first. If the risk brought success, would it be worth taking?”

华尔被逼得没有办法，只能承认∶『如果能成功，当然有价值。』
Cornered, Ward could only admit, “If it succeeded, of course it would.”


『很好！』
“Excellent!”

古应春紧接着他的话说∶『我认为你是一个有价值的人，当然也愿意做有价值的事。
Gu seized on the admission. “I regard you as a man of worth, and naturally you would wish to do something worthwhile.

你应该记得，我向你说过，这个任务并不危险；
You will recall that I told you the mission was not dangerous.

萧可以向你说明一切情况。
Xiao could explain all the circumstances.

而你，根本不作考虑；
Yet you refused even to consider it.

听到洪杨的部队，先就有了怯意——。』『谁说的！』华尔不大服气，
At the mere mention of the Taiping forces, you lost your nerve—” “Who says so!” Ward protested.

『你在侮蔑我。』
“You are slandering me.”

『我希望你用行为表现你的勇敢；表现你的价值。』
“I should like you to demonstrate your courage by your actions—and show your worth.”

『好！』
“Very well!”

华尔受激，脱口说道∶『让我先了解情况。』
Stung into agreement, Ward blurted out, “Let me hear the circumstances first.”

说着，便站起身来，走到一张地图面前立定。
He rose and went over to a map.

事情有了转机，杨坊既佩服，又兴奋，赶紧取一桌上的洋灯，同时示意萧家骥去讲解情况。
Delighted by the change and filled with admiration, Yang hurriedly picked up the lamp from the table and motioned Xiao to explain.

连古应春一起跟着过去，
Gu joined them.

在洋灯照映下都望着墙壁上所贴的那张厚洋纸画的地图；
In the lamplight, they all studied the map pinned to the wall, drawn on a sheet of thick foreign paper.

It was far more complicated than a Chinese map, and the many little red and blue triangular flags made it harder still to understand.

但萧家骥在轮船上也常看航海图；
Xiao, however, often looked at navigation charts aboard steamships.

所以略略注视了一会，便已了然。
After studying it briefly, he understood it.


『在海上不会遭遇任何敌人；
“We shall encounter no enemy at sea.

可能的危险从这里开始。』
The possible danger begins here.”

萧家骥指着鳖子门说∶『事实上上也只有一处比较危险的地方，

『那么这个隘口的江面，有多宽？』
“Then how wide is the river at that passage?”
