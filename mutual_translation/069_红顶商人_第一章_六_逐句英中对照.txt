胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第一章（本章第6部分，共6部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


“It has never been measured.
『没有测量过。

But even if a shot fired from the shore reached a vessel, it would have lost its force.”
但是在岸上用长枪射击，就能打到船上也没有力量了。』

Ward shook his head. “Rifles do not worry me.” Then he asked, “Are there any batteries?”
华尔摇摇头∶『我不怕步枪。』他接着又问∶『有没有炮台？』

“Certainly not,” Gu answered from beside him.
『决没有。』古应春在旁边接口。

“Even without permanent batteries, there must be temporary gun positions.
『即使没有炮台，也一定有临时安置的炮位。

If I were there, I should certainly deploy artillery at this point.”
如果是我，一定在这里部署炮兵阵地。』

“You must not rate the Taiping forces too highly,” Gu said.
『你不要将洪杨部队，估计得太高。』古应春又说，

“They cannot understand your methods of warfare.”
『他们不可能了解你们的兵法。』

Ward thought there was truth in this.
这一点，华尔认为说得不错；

He had fought the rebels many times and knew something of their abilities.
他跟长毛接过许多次仗，对此颇有了解，

They were not even fully proficient with foreign firearms; naturally, they would not understand the tactics of holding a strategic passage by concentrated fire.
他们连用洋枪都不十分熟练，当然不会懂得用火力扼守要隘的战法。

Moreover, even if they did understand, there would be no reason to defend this particular passage.
要进一步看，即使懂得，亦用不着防守这个隘口，

The Qing forces in the area were weak, and there was no naval force likely to pass through it to relieve Hangzhou.
因为在这一带的清军，兵力薄弱，更无水师会通过这个隘口，增援杭州；

To station guns there would be to put valuable weapons where they served no purpose.
那末，布炮防守，岂不是置利器于无用之地。


Still, the principle that victory favoured the side which planned more carefully was common to Chinese and foreign military thinking.
但是，『多算胜』的道理，中外兵法都是一样的；

Ward preferred to take the safer course.
华尔觉得还是要采用比较安全的办法，

“Is this narrow passage very long?” he asked.
所以又问∶『这个隘口，是不是很长？』

“No,” Gu estimated. “Eight or ten li at most.”
『不会。』古应春估计着说∶『至多十里八里路。』

“And what sort of vessels will you use?”
『那末，用什么船呢？』

“Sea-going junks.”
『用海船。』

These were the flat-bottomed trading vessels known as shachuan.
所谓海船就是沙船。

Ward was trained for land warfare and knew little about ships, but he understood the effect of a favourable or contrary wind.
华尔学的是陆军，对船舶是外行；不过风向顺逆之理总知道的，

Pointing to the map, he said, “This is the season of northwesterly winds.
指着地图说道∶『现在是西北风的季节，

Sailing from east to west, you will have the wind against you.”
由东向西行驶；风向很不利。』

“On that point,” Gu answered cautiously, “I do not think you need be too anxious.
『这一点，』古应春很谨慎地答道∶『我想你不必过虑，

Besides sails, there must be other ways of helping the vessels along.
除了用帆以外，总还有其它辅助航行的办法。

These sea-going junks are large and strongly built.
海船坚固高大，

Their hulls alone provide considerable protection.
船身就具备相当的防御力；

I should think them fairly safe.”
照我想，是相当安全的。』

“I must look into that further.
『这方面，我还要研究；

I shall have to discuss it with the commander of the flotilla.
我要跟船队的指挥者研究。

Ideally, we should slip through the passage at night and avoid a direct engagement with the Taiping forces.”
最好，我们能在黑夜之间，偷渡这个隘口，避免跟洪杨部队发生正面的冲突。』

His tone already suggested agreement to provide an escort.
这样的口气，已经是答应派兵护航了，

Yang spoke with delight. “Thank you, Colonel!
杨坊便很高兴地说∶『谢谢上校！

Shall we settle the matter today—the number of men and the contribution you would like towards their pay?”
我们今天就作个决定，将人数以及你所希望补助的饷银，定规下来，你看如何？』


“You asked for fifty men.
『你们要五十个人，

I shall give you fifty.
我照数派给你们。

Discuss the other details with my quartermaster tomorrow.”
其他的细节，请你们明天跟我的军需官商量。』

“Certainly!” Yang replied gladly. “We shall do exactly as you wish.”
『好的！』杨坊欣然答道∶『完全遵照你的意思。』

Hostility had given way to friendship.
于是『化干戈为玉帛』，

Gu too smiled, offered his thanks, and took his leave.
古应春亦含笑道谢，告辞上车。

“Gu,” Yang said admiringly in the carriage, “you really know how to handle a situation.
『老古，』在车中，杨坊表示钦佩∶『你倒是真有一套。

We must work together more often.”
以后我们多多合作。』

“It was good luck, with some guidance from a wiser man.
『侥幸！亏得高人指点。』

Intendant Hu gave me one piece of advice: a challenge will move a commander more readily than a request.
古应春说∶『也是胡道台一句话∶请将不如激将。

And it did move Ward.”
果然把华尔激成功了。』

“So Intendant Hu is skilled in dealing with foreigners as well.”
『原来胡道台也是办洋务的好手。』

“He knows little of foreign affairs.
『他倒不十分懂洋务，

What he understands through and through is human nature.”
只是人情熟透熟透！』

“I must meet him sometime,” Yang said. “Ward’s quartermaster is Chinese, and I know him extremely well.
『几时我倒要见见他。』杨坊又说∶『华尔的「军需官」，也是我们中国人；我极熟的。

Tomorrow evening I shall invite him to dine with courtesans.
明天晚上我约他出来吃花酒，

Everything will be easy to discuss.”
一切都好谈。』

“Excellent.
『那好极了。

I ought to be the host.
应该我做东。

Tomorrow morning I shall send the invitations to your house and ask you to arrange it.”
明天早晨，我就行帖子送到你那里，请你代劳。』


“It makes no difference whether you give the dinner or I do.
『你做东，还是我做东，都一样。

Let us leave that aside.
这就不去说它了，

There is something I should like to ask you. Is Hangzhou not under siege?
倒是有句话，我要请教∶杭州不是被围了吗？

Once the grain boats get there, how will the rice enter the city?”
粮船到了那里，怎么运进城。』

Gu was taken aback.
这句话让古应春一楞，

“Ah!” he said, as though waking from a dream.
『啊，』他如梦初醒似地，

“Of course.
『这倒是！

I had not thought of that.
我还没有想到。

I shall ask when I get back and let you know.”
等我回去问了，再答复你。』

“Could you give me a definite answer tonight?”
『可以不可以今天就给我一个确实回音？』

It seemed premature to worry about what would happen once the boats reached Hangzhou.
到了杭州的事，此刻言之过早；

Besides, whether the rice could enter the city was no concern of Yang’s.
而且米能不能运进杭州城，与杨坊无干，

Why did he want an answer so urgently?
何以他这么急着要答复？

There seemed to be another purpose behind it, and Gu felt he must find out.
看起来，别有作用，倒不能不弄个明白。

“Why the hurry?”
这样想着，便即问道∶『为什么这么急？』

“I have another idea.
『我另外有个想法。

If the rice can enter Hangzhou, there is nothing more to say.
如果能运进杭州城，那就不必谈了；

Otherwise—” Yang broke off to ask, “Could you introduce me to him now?
否则——。』杨坊忽然问道∶『能不能此刻就替我引见，

I should like to discuss it with Intendant Hu himself.”
我想跟胡道台当面谈一谈。』

“Why not?”
『这有什么不可以？』


The carriage changed direction and drove straight to Gu’s house.
于是马车转向，直驶古家；

As soon as it stopped, Xiao ran inside to announce the visitor.
车一停，萧家骥首先奔了进去通知。

A great stickler for courtesy, Hu wanted to get out of bed and receive him in the reception room.
胡雪岩很讲究礼节，要起床在客厅里迎接会面；

The Seventh Lady firmly opposed this.
七姑奶奶坚决反对，

They finally compromised: he might get up, but must remain in his bedroom and receive the guest there.
结果折衷办法，起床而不出房门，就在卧室里接见客人。

Naturally, the women withdrew.
女眷自然回避。

By the time Gu ushered Yang inside, Hu had put on a long gown and riding jacket and was waiting at the doorway, leaning on Xiao’s shoulder.
等古应春将杨坊迎了进来，胡雪岩已经穿上长袍马褂，扶着萧家骥的肩，等在门口了。

Each had long known the other by reputation.
彼此都闻名已久，

Once the formal greetings were over, they spoke with great warmth and mutual admiration, their conversation continuing for some time.
所以见礼以后，非常亲热，互相仰慕，话题久久不断。

Gu found an opening to give a brief account of the negotiations with Ward.
古应春找个机会，插进话去，将与华尔交涉的经过，略略说了一遍；

Hu had already heard the outline from Xiao, but now he naturally thanked Yang most earnestly.
胡雪岩原已从萧家骥口中，得知梗概，此刻少不得要向杨坊殷殷致谢。

“It concerns our home province.
『都是为家乡的事，

I ought to do what I can.
应当出力。

But”—Yang turned abruptly to the business that concerned him—“once the grain boats reach Hangzhou, how is the rice to enter the city?”
不过，』杨坊急转直下的转入本题∶『粮船到了杭州，不晓得怎么运进杭州？』


At this, Hu’s face immediately clouded.
提到这一层，胡雪岩的脸色，马上转为忧郁了；

He sighed.
叹口气说∶『唉！

“That was another mistake.
这件事也是失策。

Before the city was closed, the high officials disagreed.
关城之先，省城里的大员，意见就不一，

Some wanted all ten gates shut; others thought one or two should be left open.
有的说十个城门统通要关；有的说应该留一两个不关。

In the end, they shut them all.
结果是统统关了。

As soon as that happened, the rebels dug trenches and put up wooden barriers outside.
这里一关，长毛马上在城外掘壕沟，做木墙。

They sealed us in completely.” Having said this in one breath, he paused for air, then continued. “Someone had proposed building a sloping causeway from the city wall right down to the river, to serve as a supply route.
围困得实腾腾。』他一口气说到这里，喘息了一下又说∶『当初还有人提议，从城上筑一道斜坡，直到江边，作为粮道。

It sounded so extraordinary that everyone laughed.
这个主意听起来出奇∶大家都笑。

The work would also have been enormous, so nothing was done.
而且工程也浩大，所以就没有办。

Looking back now, it was an excellent idea.
其实，此刻想来，实在是一条好计；

It would have taken some trouble, but with the supply route open, Hangzhou could have held out.”
如果能够这么做，虽费点事，可是粮道不断，杭州就能守得住！』

He gave another long sigh.
接着，又是一声长叹。

Gu was the first to show alarm. “Little Uncle, if that is so, will all our efforts be wasted?”
听得这样说法，古应春先就大为着急∶『小爷叔，』他问∶『照你这么说，我们不是劳而无功？』

“Not necessarily.
『这也不见得。』

Once the grain boats arrive, the people inside will fight their way out at all costs and escort the grain into the city.”
胡雪岩说∶『只要粮船一到，城里自然拼死命杀开一条血路，护粮进城。』

Yang nodded, glanced at Gu, and seemed about to speak, then stopped.
杨坊点点头，看一看古应春，欲语不语地；

Watching his expression, Hu knew he had something to say.
胡雪岩察言观色，便知其中有话。


“Brother Yang, we have met as though we were old friends.
『杨兄，』他说，『你我一见如故，

Please speak freely.”
有话尽请直说。』

“The situation is this.
『是这样的，

Naturally, I too want our fellow provincials in Hangzhou to have enough food to stay alive.
我当然也希望杭州的同乡，有一口活命的饭吃。

But one must allow for the worst. Suppose you get the grain boats to Hangzhou after endless hardships, only to find all communication between the city and the outside world cut off.
不过，凡事要从最坏的地方去打算∶万一千辛万苦将粮船开到杭州，城里城外交通断绝，

What will you do then, Mr Hu?”
到时候，胡先生，你怎么办？』

“What would you advise?”
『我请问杨兄，依你看，应该怎么办？』

“Speaking as a businessman, you can hardly hand all that rice to the rebels or throw it into the river.
『在商言商，这许多米，总不能送给长毛，更不能丢在江里。』

If it cannot enter Hangzhou, could I ask you to divert it to Ningbo?”
杨坊说道∶『如果运不进杭州城，可以不可以请胡先生改运宁波？』

So this was why he had been so eager to meet Hu.
原来他急于要见胡雪岩，是为了这句话。

Gu thought to himself that Yang was a formidable fellow, shrewd in every calculation, and not to be underestimated.
古应春心想∶此人倒也是厉害脚色，『门槛』精得很，不可小觑了他。

He listened closely for Hu’s reply.
因此，很注意地要听胡雪岩如何回答。

“That is a practical suggestion.
『杨兄的话很实在。

If the rice cannot enter Hangzhou, of course I shall send it elsewhere.
如果米运不进杭州城，我当然改运别处，

So long as it does not fall into rebel hands, any destination will do.”
只要不落在长毛手里，运到什么地方都可以。』

Then Hu qualified his answer.
说到这里，胡雪岩下了一个转语∶『不过，

“But I cannot promise what you ask just yet.
杨兄的话，我倒一时答应不下。

Why?
为什么呢？

Because I do not know the situation in Ningbo.
因为宁波的情形，我还不晓得；

If I gave you my word and then could not fulfil it, I should be breaking faith.”
许了杨兄，倘或办不到，岂不是我变成失信用。』

“The situation in Ningbo is much like Shanghai’s—”
『宁波的情形，跟上海差不多——。』


Ningbo too had a foreign settlement.
因为宁波也有租界。

Just as wealthy Jiangsu families had fled to Shanghai, the rich households of eastern Zhejiang had taken refuge in the Ningbo settlement.
江苏的富室逃到上海，浙东的大户，则以宁波租界为避难之地；

As early as the summer, Ningbo’s gentry had submitted proposals to the local authorities, offering to raise five hundred thousand taels to hire British and French warships to defend the city.
早在夏天，宁波的士绅就条陈地方官，愿集资五十万两银子，雇英法兵船代守宁波，

After Xiaoshan and Shaoxing fell, the Taiping army swept eastward, taking Yuyao, Cixi, and Fenghua in rapid succession.
及至萧绍失守，太平军一路向东，势如破竹，攻余姚、下慈溪、陷奉化，

Ningbo might fall at any moment.
宁波旦夕不保；

The British, French, and American consuls therefore conferred and decided to send representatives to Fenghua to meet the Taiping commander, Fan Ruzeng, and urge him to postpone an attack on Ningbo.
于是英、法、美三国领事，会商以后，决定派人到奉化会晤太平军守将范汝增，劝他暂缓进攻宁波。

Fan gave no direct answer to the request, but promised to protect foreigners.
范汝增对这个请求，不作正面答复，但应允保护洋人，

The three consuls had consequently issued a joint proclamation guaranteeing the safety of the settlement.
因此三国领事已经会衔了布告，保护租界；

Yet land communications were virtually severed, travelling merchants dared not move, and the city was suffering a severe grain shortage.
但陆路交通，近乎断绝，商旅裹足，也在大闹粮荒。

Yang’s purpose was partly to help his native place.
杨坊的打算，一方面固然是为桑梓尽力；

It was also to obtain a good price and make a profitable transaction out of the opportunity.
另一方面亦有善价而沽，趁此机会做一笔生意的想法。


His private interest, of course, was something Yang had no intention of revealing.
不过杨坊的私心，自然不肯透露，

“Mr Hu, I understand that many Hangzhou people have taken refuge in Ningbo as well.
『胡先生，』他说，『据我晓得，逃在宁波的杭州人也不少。

If you cannot achieve your first object, sending the grain there is really the only alternative.”
所以你拿粮食改运宁波，实在是不得已而求其次的唯一出路。』

“And when it reaches Ningbo?
『那末，到了宁波呢？

What if it cannot be landed?”
如果不能上岸，又怎么办？』

“That will not happen.
『不会的。

Any one of the three consuls—British, French, or American—can arrange protection for you.
英、法、美三国领事，哪一位都可以出面保护你，

Naturally, I shall make the necessary contacts.”
到那时候，我当然会从中联络。』

“In that case—” Hu sprang to his feet.
『既然如此——。』胡雪岩矍然而起——

A plan had occurred to him, and in his excitement he forgot his injured leg.
想好了主意，一时兴奋，忘却腿伤，

He fell to the floor, pain bringing beads of sweat as large as soybeans to his forehead.
一下子摔倒在地，疼得额上沁出黄豆大的汗珠。

Xiao quickly stepped forward to help him up.
萧家骥动作敏捷，赶紧上前扶起；

Gu, badly startled, examined the injury.
古应春也吃了一惊，为他检视伤势。

Only after the commotion subsided could Hu finish what he had been saying.
乱过一阵，胡雪岩方能接着他自己的话说下去。

“Brother Yang, in that case, let us make a bargain.
『杨兄，既然如此，我们做一笔交易。

Hangzhou is short of grain; so is Ningbo.
杭州缺粮，宁波也缺粮，

We can work together.
我们来合作；

I shall undertake to send a consignment of rice to Ningbo and find both the rice and the vessels.
宁波，我负责运一批米过去，米、船，都归我想办法。

In return, could you ask the foreigners to intervene on charitable grounds and escort this consignment of mine into Hangzhou?”
杭州这方面，可以不可以请你托洋人出面，借个做善事的名义，将我这一批米护送进城？』


“That proposal…”
『这个办法——。』

Yang looked at Gu, visibly uneasy.
杨坊看着古应春，颇有为难的神情。

“Little Uncle, when it comes to business and ingenuity, we must acknowledge you as a Zhuge Liang,” Gu said tactfully.
『小爷叔，做生意，动脑筋，不能不当你诸葛亮。』古应春很委婉地说，

“Unfortunately, you are something of a novice in foreign affairs.
『可惜，洋务上，小爷叔你略为有点外行，

This cannot be done.”
这件事行不通。』

“Why not?”
『怎么呢？』

“Foreign consuls need grounds for intervening.
『因为外国领事，出面干预，要有个名目；

Grain can be sent to Ningbo in the name of protecting their nationals, since foreigners must have food.
运粮到宁波，可以「护侨」为名，为的洋人不能没有食物接济。

Hangzhou is different.
但杭州的情形就不同了，

There are no British, French, or American residents there in need of relief.
并无英法美三国侨民，需要救济；

As for feeding Chinese civilians, it depends on the place.
而救济中国百姓，要看地方，

In a war zone, there is no way to distinguish food for civilians from provisions for troops.”
在交战区域，民食军粮是无从区分的。』

When Gu had finished, Yang added, “In the eighth month, the British government sent its minister orders to maintain strict neutrality.
等古应春解释完了，杨坊接着补充∶『八月里，英国京城有一道命令给他们的公使，叫做「严守中立」；

That means helping neither side.
这就是说，哪一面也不帮。

Your proposal is excellent, Mr Hu, but unfortunately it is impracticable.”
所以胡先生的这个打算，好倒是好，可惜办不通。』

Hu was disappointed, of course, but did not want to show it.
胡雪岩当然失望，但不愿形诸颜色；

Returning to Yang’s request, he declared generously, “Then it is settled.
将话题回到杨坊的要求上，慨然说道∶『那就一言为定了。

If this rice cannot enter Hangzhou, we shall divert it to Ningbo.
这批米如果运不进杭州城，就转运宁波。

But Chief Yu must be told beforehand.
不过，这话要跟郁老大先说明白；

If the junk owners refuse to discharge at a different destination, we shall have an argument on our hands.”
到时候，沙船不肯改地方卸货，就要费口舌了。』

“Of course I shall ask Brother Yingchun to make that clear.
『这一层，我当然会请应春兄替我打招呼；

What I should like your instructions on is the price of the rice—”
我要请胡先生吩咐的是粮价——。』


“That is no difficulty!”
『这不要紧！』

Hu cut in firmly.
胡雪岩有力地打断他的话，

“We can settle it however you wish.
『怎么样说都可以。

If this were business, every fraction of a tael would have to be reckoned.
如果是做生意，当然一分一厘都要算清楚；

But this is not business.”
现在不是做生意。』

“Yes, yes.”
『是，是！』

Yang felt an inward pang of shame.
杨坊不免内惭；

Almost to himself, he said, “Of course, we are doing a good deed.”
自语似地说∶『原是做好事。』

With that, the discussion reached a conclusion.
谈话到此告一段落，

Afraid that Hu was tiring himself and delaying his recovery, Gu invited Yang into the reception room.
