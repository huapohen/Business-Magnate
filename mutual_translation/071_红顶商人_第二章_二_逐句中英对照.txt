胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第二章（本章第2部分，共4部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


『好在你会说英文，上海洋行的情形也熟；
“You speak English, and you know the foreign firms in Shanghai.

人又聪明，
You are clever too.

一定装得象。』胡雪岩说∶『你要记住，长毛也是土里土气的，
You will certainly make a convincing show,” Hu said. “Remember, the Longhairs are provincial fellows as well.

要拿外国人唬他。』
Use the foreigners to overawe them.”

——交代停当，却不曾写信；
Once his instructions were given, however, he wrote no letter.

这也是胡雪岩细心之处，怕搜到了这封信，大事不成，反惹来杀身之祸。
This too showed his care: if a letter were found in a search, the whole undertaking would fail and Xiao would lose his life.

但见了王有龄，必须有一样信物为凭；
Yet Xiao needed some token to prove his credentials when he met Wang Youling.

手上那个金戒指本来是最真确的，又怕长毛起眼劫掠，
The gold ring on Hu’s hand would have been unmistakable, but he feared it would catch a Longhair’s eye and be stolen.

胡雪岩想了半天，只有用话来交代了。
After long consideration, he could think of nothing but an oral message.

『我临走的时候，王抚台跟我谈了好些时候，
“Before I left, Governor Wang talked with me for a long time.

他的后事都托了我。
He entrusted all his affairs after his death to me.

他最钟爱的小儿子，名叫苕云，今年才五岁，
His youngest son, whom he loves most dearly, is called Tiaoyun and is only five this year.

要寄在我名下；
He wanted to place the boy under my name.

我说等我上海回来再说。
I said we should discuss it after I returned from Shanghai.

这些话，没有第三个人晓得，
No third person knows of this conversation.

你跟他说了，他自然会相信是我请你去的。』
Tell him, and he will know that I sent you.”

这是最好的征信办法，
This was the best possible proof.

萧家骥问清楚了『苕云』二字的写法，紧记在心。
Xiao asked exactly how the two characters of Tiaoyun’s name were written and committed them to memory.

但是，一时还不能走；先要想办法找只小船。
But he could not yet leave: first they had to find a small boat.


小船是有，
There were boats about.

过往载运逃难的人的渡船，时有所见，但洋兵荷枪实弹，在沙船上往来侦伺，没有谁敢驶近。
Ferries carrying refugees passed from time to time, but with the foreign soldiers patrolling the junks, loaded guns in hand, none dared approach.

这就要靠李得隆了，
Here they needed Li Delong.

借了孔联络官的望远镜，看准远远一只空船；泅水迎了上去，
Borrowing Kong’s telescope, he picked out an empty boat in the distance and swam towards it.

把着船舷，探头见了船老大，
He caught hold of its side and raised his head to face the boatman.

先不说话，身上摸出水淋淋的一块马蹄银，递了过去；
Before saying a word, he drew a dripping horseshoe-shaped silver ingot from his clothing and handed it up.

真是『重赏之下，必有勇夫』，很顺利地雇到了船。
Truly, a handsome reward will always find a brave man: he hired the boat without difficulty.

这是天色将暮，视界不明，却更易混上岸去；
Dusk was falling and visibility was poor, making it easier to slip ashore.

胡雪岩亲自指点了方向，
Hu pointed out the direction himself.

就在将要开船时，他忽然想到了一件事。
Just as they were about to cast off, another thought struck him.

『喂，喂，船老大，
“Wait, boatman!

你贵姓？』
What is your surname?”

船老大指指水面∶『我就姓江。』
The man pointed at the water. “Jiang—River.”

『老江，亲苦你了。』胡雪岩说∶『你拿我这位朋友送到岸，回来通个信给我，
“Old Jiang, thank you for your trouble,” Hu said. “Take my friend ashore, then come back and bring me word.

我再送你十两银子。
I shall give you another ten taels.

决不骗你；
I will not deceive you.

如果骗你，教我马上掉在钱塘江里，不得好死。』
If I do, may I fall straight into the Qiantang and die a dreadful death.”

听他罚得这么重的咒，江老大似乎颇为动容，
Such a terrible oath seemed to move the boatman.

『你老爷贵姓？』他问。
“What is your honourable surname, sir?” he asked.


『我姓王。』
“Wang.”

『王老爷，你老人家请放心；
“Rest assured, Mr Wang.

我拿这位少爷送到了，一定来报信。』
Once I have landed this young gentleman, I shall certainly come back and report.”

『拜托、拜托！』
“I am relying on you!”

胡雪岩在沙船上作揖，
Hu bowed from the junk.

『我备好银子在这里等你，
“I shall have the silver ready and wait for you here.

哪怕半夜里都不要紧，你一定要来！
Even if it is the middle of the night, come back without fail.

你船上有没有灯笼？』
Have you a lantern on your boat?”

『灯笼是有的。』
“A lantern, yes.”

江老大也很灵活，知道他的用意，
Jiang was quick-witted too and understood his purpose.

『晚上如果挂出来，江风一吹，马上就灭了。』
“But if I hang it outside at night, the wind off the river will blow it out at once.”

『说得有理。
“Quite right.

来，来，
Come, then!

索性「六指头搔痒」，格外奉承你了。』
I shall scratch your back with six fingers—an extra favour for you.”

胡雪岩另外送他一盏燃用『美孚油』的马灯，
Hu gave him a storm lantern burning Mobil kerosene.

作为报信时挂在船头的信号，免得到时洋兵不明就里，误伤了他。
He was to hang it at the bow as a signal when returning with news, lest the foreign soldiers, unaware of his purpose, fire on him by mistake.


等萧家骥一走，李得隆忍不住要问，何以要这样对待江老大，甚至赌神罚咒，唯恐他不信似的。
Once Xiao had gone, Li Delong could not help asking why Hu had treated the boatman in that manner, even swearing by the gods as though afraid of being disbelieved.

是不是不放心萧家骥？
Was he worried about Xiao?

『已经放他出去了，没有什么不放心。』胡雪岩说，
“Once I have let him go, there is no point worrying,” Hu replied.

『我是防这个船老大；
“It is the boatman I am guarding against.

要防他将人送到了，又到长毛那里去密告讨赏。
He might land our man and then go to the Longhairs to inform on him for a reward.

所以用十两银子拴住他的脚，好教他早早回来。
I tied his feet with ten taels so that he would come straight back.

这当然要罚咒，不然他不相信。』
Naturally I had to swear an oath, or he would not believe me.”

『胡先生，实在服了你了，
“Mr Hu, I truly admire you.

真正算无遗策。
You leave nothing to chance.

不过，胡先生，你为啥又说姓王呢？』
But why did you tell him your surname was Wang?”

『这另外有个缘故，
“That has another cause.

钱塘江摆渡的都恨我；
The Qiantang ferrymen all hate me.

说了真姓要坏事。
My real name would have caused trouble.

你听我说那个缘故给你听；
Let me tell you why.

二十年前——。』二十年前的胡雪岩，还在钱庄里学生意，
Twenty years ago—” Twenty years before, Hu had still been an apprentice in a bank.

有一次奉命到钱塘江南岸的萧山县去收一笔帐款；
On one occasion he was sent to Xiaoshan County on the south bank of the Qiantang to collect a debt.

帐款没有收到，有限的几个盘缠，却在小菜馆里掷骰子输得只剩十个摆渡所需的小钱。
He failed to collect it, and in a little eating house he lost almost all his scant travelling money at dice, keeping only the ten copper coins needed for the ferry.

『船到江心，收钱了。』胡雪岩说，
“When the boat reached midstream, they collected the fares,” he said.

『到我面前，我手一伸进衣袋里，拿不出来了。』
“The man came to me, and I put my hand in my pocket—but could bring nothing out.”

『怎么呢？』李得隆问。
“Why?” Li asked.

『也叫祸不单行，
“Misfortunes never come singly.

衣袋破了个沿；十个小钱不知道什么时候漏得光光。
A hole had worn in the pocket, and all ten coins had slipped out without my noticing.

钱塘江的渡船，出了名的凶，
The Qiantang ferrymen were notoriously fierce.

听说真有付不出摆渡钱，被推到江里的事。
I had heard of people actually being pushed into the river because they could not pay.

当时我自然大窘，
Naturally I was desperately embarrassed.

只好实话实说，答应上岸到钱庄拿了钱来照补。
I could only tell the truth and promise to fetch the money from my bank as soon as we landed.

叫啥说破了嘴都无用，
I might as well have talked my mouth off.

硬要剥我的衣服。』
They insisted on stripping off my clothes.”

『这么可恶！』李得隆大为不平，
“The villains!” Li exclaimed indignantly.

『不过，难道一船的人，都袖手旁观？』
“But surely the whole boat did not just stand by and watch?”


当然不致于，
Of course not.

有人借了十文钱给他，方得免褫衣之辱。
Someone lent him ten cash, sparing him the humiliation of being stripped.

但胡雪岩经此刺激，上岸就发誓∶只要有一天得意，力所能及，一定买两只船；雇几个船夫，设置来往两岸不费分文的义渡。
Yet the experience stung him so deeply that, once ashore, he swore that if he ever prospered and had the means, he would buy two boats, engage some boatmen and establish a charitable ferry to carry passengers between the banks without charging a single coin.

『我这个愿望，说实话，老早就可以达到。
“To tell the truth, I could have fulfilled that wish long before I did.

哪知道做好事都不行！
Who would have thought one could be prevented from doing good?

得隆，你倒想想看，是啥道理？』
Delong, what do you suppose the reason was?”

『这道理好懂。
“Easy enough.

有人做好事，就有人没饭吃了。』
When one man does good, another loses his livelihood.”

『对！
“Exactly.

为此钱塘江摆渡的，联起来来反对我，不准我设义渡。
The Qiantang ferrymen joined forces against me and would not let me establish the free crossing.

后来幸亏王抚台帮忙。』
Fortunately Governor Wang helped me in the end.”

那时王有龄已调杭州知府，
At the time Wang had been transferred to the post of prefect of Hangzhou.

不但私人交情，帮胡雪岩的忙义不容辞；就是以地方官的身分，为民造福，奖励善举，亦是责无旁贷的事。
His personal friendship made helping Hu a duty; equally, as the local official, he was bound to benefit the people and encourage charitable acts.

所以一方面出告示不准告摆渡为生的人，阻挠这件好事；一面还为胡雪岩请奖。
He issued a proclamation forbidding those who earned their living by ferrying passengers to obstruct the scheme, and also applied for an official reward for Hu.

自设义渡，受惠的人，不知凡几；
Countless people benefited from the free ferry.

胡雪岩纵非沽钓誉，而声名洋溢，就此博得了一个『胡善人』的美名。
Even though Hu had not set out to fish for praise, his reputation spread everywhere, and he became known as “Hu the Benefactor”.

只是钱塘江里的船家，提起『胡善人』，大多咬牙切齿，
But most Qiantang boatmen gnashed their teeth at the mention of that name.

此所以他不肯对江老大透露真姓。
That was why he had concealed his true surname from Jiang.

小小的一个故事，由于胡雪岩心情已比较开朗，恢复了他原有的口才，
Now that his spirits had lifted, Hu had recovered his usual gift for telling a story.

讲得颇为风趣，所以李得隆听得津津有味，同时也更佩服了。
He made this little incident so entertaining that Li listened with delight—and with growing admiration.


『胡先生，因果报应到底是有的。
“Mr Hu, there must be such a thing as moral recompense.

就凭胡先生你在这条江上，做下这么一桩好事；应该决不会在这条江上出什么风险。
After the good you have done on this river, you surely cannot come to harm upon it.

我们大家都要托你的福。』
We shall all share in your good fortune.”

这两句话说得很中听，
This was exactly what Hu wished to hear.

胡雪岩喜逐颜开地说∶『谢谢！谢谢！
His face lit up. “Thank you, thank you!

一定如你金口。』
May your gracious words come true.”

不但胡雪岩自己，
Nor was he the only one encouraged.

船上别的人，也都受了李得隆那几句话的鼓舞，认为有善人在船，必可逢凶化吉。
Everyone aboard took heart from Li’s remarks, believing that with a good man among them, calamity would turn to blessing.

因而也就一下子改变了前两天那种坐困愁城，忧郁不安，令人仿佛透不过气来的味道；
At once the oppressive atmosphere of the past two days—their sense of being trapped, their anxiety and despondency—lifted.

晚饭桌上，兴致很好，
Dinner was a cheerful affair.

连不会喝酒的李得隆也愿意来一杯。
Even Li, who was no drinker, agreed to take a cup.


『说起来鬼神真不可不信。』孔联络官举杯在手，悠闲地说，
“One really cannot disbelieve in spirits and gods,” Kong observed comfortably, cup in hand.

『不过行善要不教人晓得，才是真正做好事；
“But true goodness is doing good without letting anyone know.

为了善人的名声做好事，不足为奇。』
There is nothing remarkable in charity performed merely to gain a reputation for virtue.”

『不然。
“I disagree.

人人肯为了善人的名声，去做好事，这个世界就好了。
If everyone would do good for the sake of a good name, this world would be a fine place.

有的人简直是「善棍」。』胡雪岩说，
Some people are simply charity swindlers,” Hu said.

『这就叫「三代以下，唯恐不好名」。』
“As the saying goes, ‘Since the Three Dynasties, the fear should be that men care too little for a good name.’”

『什么叫「善棍」？』李得隆笑道，
“Charity swindlers?” Li laughed.

『这个名目则是第一次听见。』
“That is a new name to me.”

『善棍就是骗子。借行善为名行骗，
“It means cheats who use charity as a cover.

这类骗子顶顶难防。不过日子一久，总归瞒不过人。』
They are the hardest sort of swindler to guard against, though in time people always see through them.

胡雪岩说，『什么事，一颗心假不了；
In anything a man does, he cannot counterfeit his heart for ever.

有些人自以为聪明绝顶，人人都会上他的当；
Some imagine themselves so clever that everyone will fall into their trap.

其实到头来原形毕露，自己毁了自己。
In the end their true nature shows, and they ruin themselves.

一个人值不值钱，就看他自己说的话算数不算数；
A man’s worth depends on whether he keeps his word.

象王抚台，
Take Governor Wang.

在我们浙江的官声，说实话，并不是怎么样顶好；
To speak frankly, his reputation as an official in Zhejiang was not among the very best.

可是现在他说不走，就不走，
But now he says he will not leave, and he stays.

要跟杭州人同祸福，共存亡，
He means to share the fortunes of Hangzhou’s people, to live or die with them.

就这一点上他比何制台值钱得多。』
In that alone he is worth far more than Governor-General He.”

话到这里，大家不期而然地想到了萧家骥，
At this, everyone’s thoughts turned naturally to Xiao.

推测他何时能够进城？
When would he enter the city?

王有龄得到消息，会有什么举动？
What would Wang do on receiving the news?

船上该如何接应？
How should the boats respond?

『举动是一定会有举动的。
“He is certain to take action.

不过——，』胡雪岩忽然停杯不饮，
But—” Hu suddenly held his cup without drinking.

容颜惨淡，
His face grew bleak.

好久，才叹口气说∶『我实在想不出，怎样才能将这批米运上岸；
After a long silence he sighed. “I really cannot see how we are to get this rice ashore.

就算杀开一条血路，又哪里能够保得住这条粮道畅通？』
Even if they fight a passage through, how can they keep the supply route open?”


『胡先生，有个办法不晓得行不行？』李得隆说∶『杭州不是有水城门吗？
“Mr Hu, I wonder whether this might work,” Li said. “Does Hangzhou not have water gates?

好不好弄几条小船，拿米分开来偷运进城？』
Could we get a few little boats, divide the rice among them and smuggle it into the city?”

『只怕不行——。』话刚说得半句，只听一声枪响；
“I fear not—” Hu had scarcely begun when a shot rang out.

随即有人喊道∶『不能开枪，
Immediately someone shouted, “Do not fire!

不能开枪；
Do not fire!

是报信的来了。』
The messenger is coming back!”

于是胡雪岩、李得隆纷纷出舱探望，
Hu and Li hurried out of the cabin.

果然，一点星火，冉冉而来；
Sure enough, a point of light was slowly approaching.

渐行渐近，看出船头上挂的是盏马灯。
As it drew nearer they could see a storm lantern hanging at the bow.

等小船靠近，李得隆喊一声∶『江老大！』
When the boat came alongside, Li called, “Boatman Jiang!”

『是我。』江老大答应着，将一根缆索抛了过来。
“It is I!” Jiang answered, throwing up a rope.

李得隆伸手接着，系住小船，将江老大接了上来，延入船舱；
Li caught it, made the boat fast, helped him aboard and invited him into the cabin.

胡雪岩已将白花花一锭银子摆在桌上了。
Hu had already placed a gleaming silver ingot on the table.

『那位少爷上岸了。』江老大说，
“The young gentleman has gone ashore,” Jiang said.

『我来交差。』
“I have come to report.”

『费你的心。』
“Thank you for your trouble.”

胡雪岩将银子往前一推，
Hu pushed the silver towards him.

『送你做个过年东道。』
“A little something for your New Year’s entertainment.”


『多谢，多谢。』
“Thank you, thank you.”

江老大将银子接到手里，略略迟疑了一下才说∶『王老爷，有句话想想还是要告诉你∶那位少爷一上岸，就教长毛捉了去了。』
Jiang took the silver, then hesitated before speaking. “Mr Wang, there is something I think I had better tell you. As soon as the young gentleman landed, the Longhairs took him.”

捉去不怕，要看如何捉法？
Capture itself was nothing to fear; what mattered was how he had been taken.

胡雪岩很沉着地问∶『长毛是不是很凶？』
Hu asked calmly, “Were they very rough with him?”

『那倒还好。』
“No, not particularly.

江老大说，『这位少爷胆子大，
He is a brave young gentleman.

见了长毛不逃；长毛对他就客气点了。』
He did not try to run when he saw them, so they treated him rather politely.”

胡雪岩先就放了一半心，顺口问道∶『城里有啥消息？』
Half relieved already, Hu asked casually, “Is there any news from inside the city?”

『不晓得，』
“I do not know.”

江老大摇摇头，面容顿见愁苦，
Jiang shook his head, his face suddenly full of distress.

『城里城外象两个世界。』
“Inside and outside are two different worlds.”

『那末城外呢？』
“Then what about outside?”

『城外？
“Outside?

王老爷，你是说长毛？』
You mean the Longhairs, Mr Wang?”

『是啊！
“Yes.

长毛这方面有啥消息？』
What news is there on their side?”

『也不大清楚。
“I do not know much.

前几天说要回苏州了；
A few days ago they were saying they would return to Suzhou.

有些长毛摆地摊卖抢来的东西，三文不值两文，好象急于脱货求现；
Some of them were selling their plunder from roadside stalls for next to nothing, as though anxious to turn it into cash.

这两天又不听见说起了。』
But these last two days I have heard no more about leaving.”

胡雪岩心里明白，
Hu understood.

长毛的军粮亦有难乎为继之势∶现在是跟守军僵持着，
The Longhairs too were finding their provisions hard to maintain. For the moment they were locked in a stalemate with the defenders.

如果城里有粮食接济，能再守一两个月，长毛可以不战自退。
If the city could be supplied and hold out for another month or two, the enemy might retreat without a fight.

但从另一方面看，长毛既然缺粮，那末这十几船粮食摆在江面上，必启其觊觎之心，
Yet there was another side to it: since the Longhairs were short of food, the dozen or more grain-laden boats lying on the river must tempt them greatly.

如果调集小船，不顾死命来扑，实在是件很危险的事。
If they massed small craft and attacked regardless of their lives, the danger would be severe.

因此，这晚上他又急得睡不着，
That night Hu was once more too anxious to sleep.

心心念念只望萧家骥能够混进城去，王有龄能够调集人马杀开一条血路，保住粮道；
His every thought was a hope that Xiao would get into the city and Wang would assemble enough men to fight a passage through and hold the supply route.

只要争到一天的工夫，就可以将沙船撑到岸边，卸粮进城。
A single day would suffice: they could bring the junks alongside the bank, unload the grain and carry it into the city.


萧家骥果然混进城了。
Xiao did indeed find his way inside.

被捕之时，长毛就对他『另眼相看』；
From the moment of his capture, the Longhairs had regarded him differently.

因为凡是被掳的百姓，没有不吓得瑟瑟发抖的。
Every civilian they seized ordinarily trembled with terror.

只有这个『新家伙』——长毛对刚被掳的百姓的通称——与众不同。
Only this “new fellow”—their usual term for a newly captured civilian—was different.

因此别的『新家伙』照例双手被缚，这个的辫子跟那个的辫子结在一起，防他们『逃长毛』；
The others had their hands bound, and one man’s queue was tied to the next man’s to prevent them escaping.

对萧家骥却如江老大所说的，相当『客气』，
Xiao, however, was treated with considerable courtesy, just as Jiang had reported.

押着到了『公馆』，问话的语气亦颇有礼貌。
They escorted him to the officer’s “residence”, where even the questioning was polite.

『看你样子，是外路来的。
“You look as though you come from elsewhere.

你叫什么名字，干什么行当？』一个黄衣黄帽，说湖北话的小头目问。
What is your name, and what is your business?” asked a minor commander in a yellow robe and cap, speaking with a Hubei accent.

『我姓萧，
“My name is Xiao.

从上海来。』萧家骥从容答道∶『说实话，我想来做笔大生意。
I have come from Shanghai,” Xiao answered calmly. “To tell you the truth, I hope to conclude a large piece of business.

这笔生意做成功，杭州城就再也守不住了。』
If it succeeds, Hangzhou will be unable to hold out any longer.”

那小头目听他口气不凡，顿时肃然起敬，改口称他∶『萧先生，请问是什么大生意？
The breadth of his claim instantly impressed the commander, who changed his form of address. “Mr Xiao, what great business is this?

怎么说这笔生意成功，他们杭州就会守不住？』
Why should Hangzhou become unable to hold out once it succeeds?”


『这话我实在不能跟你说。』萧家骥道∶『请你送我去见忠王。』
“I really cannot discuss it with you,” Xiao said. “Please send me to see the Loyal King.”

『忠王不知道驻驾在哪里？我也见不着他，
“I do not know where His Highness is stationed, and I cannot gain an audience with him myself.

只好拿你往上送。
I can only refer you to my superiors.

不过，萧先生，』那小头目踌躇着说∶『你不会害我吧？』
But, Mr Xiao—” The man hesitated. “You will not get me into trouble, will you?”

『怎么害我？』
“How could I?”

『如果你说的话不实在，岂不都是我的罪过？』萧家骥笑了。
“If what you say is untrue, will not all the blame fall on me?” Xiao laughed.

见此人老实可欺，有意装出轻视的神色，
Seeing that the man was simple and easily deceived, he deliberately assumed a look of contempt.

『你的话真教人好笑？
“What an amusing thing to say!

你怎么知道我的话不实在；
Why should you suppose I am not telling the truth?

我在上海住得好好的，
I was living very comfortably in Shanghai.

路远迢迢跑到这里来干什么？
Why else would I travel all this way?

跟你实说吧，我是英国人委托我来的，
Let me be frank: the British have commissioned me to come here.

要见忠王，有大事奉陈。』他突然问道∶『请问尊姓大名？』
I must see the Loyal King and lay an important matter before him.” Suddenly he asked, “May I know your name?”

『我叫陆德义。』
“Lu Deyi.”

『见了忠王，我替你说好话，
“When I see the Loyal King, I shall put in a good word for you.

包有重赏。』
You are certain to receive a handsome reward.”

李秀成治军与其他洪杨将领，本自不同，
Li Xiucheng managed his army differently from the other Taiping commanders.

一向注重招贤纳士；
He had always set great store by attracting able men.

所以陆德义听了他这话，越发不敢怠慢，
At Xiao’s words, Lu was more anxious than ever to show him proper attention.

『萧先生，』他很诚恳地答道∶『多蒙你好意，我先谢谢。
“Mr Xiao,” he answered sincerely, “thank you for your kindness.

不过，今天已经晚了，
But it is late today.

你先住一夜；我一面派人禀报上头，
Stay the night while I send a report to my superiors.

上头派人来接。
They will send someone to fetch you.

你看好不好？』
Would that suit you?”


这也不便操之过急，
It would not do to press too hard.

萧家骥心想，先住一夜，趁这陆德义好相与，打听打听情形，行事岂不是更有把握？
Xiao thought that a night’s stay would allow him to question this obliging man and learn more about the situation, giving him a firmer basis for action.

便即欣慰答道∶『那也好。
He therefore replied with apparent satisfaction, “Very well.

我就住一夜。』
I shall stay the night.”

于是陆德义奉之为上宾，设酒款待。
Lu treated him as an honoured guest and entertained him with wine.

萧家骥跑惯长江码头，而陆德义是汉阳人；
Xiao knew the Yangtze ports well, and Lu came from Hanyang.

因而以湖北近况为话题，谈得相当投机。
Recent events in Hubei supplied a topic, and they soon fell into congenial conversation.

最后谈到杭州城内的情状，
Eventually they came to conditions inside Hangzhou.

那陆德义倒真不失为忠厚人，
Lu showed himself a man of genuine kindness.

愀然不乐，
His face fell.

『真正是劫数！』
“Truly a calamity!”


他叹口气说∶『一想起来，教人连饭都吃不下。
He sighed. “The thought of it spoils one’s appetite.

但愿早早破城，杭州的百姓，还有生路；
I only hope the city falls soon, so that its people may have a chance to live.

再这样围困着，只怕杭州的百姓都要死光了。』
If this siege goes on, I fear they will all perish.”

『是啊！』萧家骥趁机说道，
“Yes,” Xiao said, seizing his opportunity.

『我来做这笔大生意，当然是帮你们，实在也是为杭州百姓好。
“Naturally, the great business I have come to arrange will help your side, but it will really benefit Hangzhou’s people too.

不过，我也不懂，
Yet there is something I do not understand.

忠王破苏州，大仁大义，百姓无不感戴。
When the Loyal King took Suzhou, his humanity and generosity won everyone’s gratitude.

既然如此，何不放杭州百姓一条生路。』
Why, then, does he not give the people here a way to survive?”

『现在是骑虎难下了。』陆德义答道∶『听说忠王射箭进城，箭上有封招降的书信，
“It is hard to dismount once one has mounted a tiger,” Lu replied. “I hear the Loyal King had an arrow shot into the city with a letter urging surrender.

说得极其恳切；无奈城里没有回音。』
It was most earnestly expressed, but no answer came.”

『喔！』
“Oh?

萧家骥问道∶『招降的书信怎么说？』
What did the letter say?”

『说是不分军民满汉，愿投降的投降，不愿投降的遣散。
“That soldiers and civilians, Manchus and Han alike, might surrender if they wished, and those who did not wish to surrender would be allowed to leave.

忠王已经具本奏报「天京」，请天王准赦满军回北，
The Loyal King has already sent a memorial to the Heavenly Capital, asking the Heavenly King to pardon the Manchu troops and let them return north.

从这里到「天京」往返要二十几日，「御批」还没有因来。
The journey there and back takes more than twenty days, and the imperial rescript has not yet arrived.

一等「御批」发回，就要派人跟瑞昌议和。
Once it comes, someone will be sent to negotiate peace with Ruichang.

那时说不定又是一番场面了。』
Things may look very different then.

陆德义说∶『我到过好多地方，看起来，杭州的满兵顶厉害。』
I have been to many places,” Lu added, “but the Manchu soldiers in Hangzhou seem to be the fiercest of all.”

这使得萧家骥又想起胡雪岩的话，杭州只要有存粮，一年半载都守得住，
This recalled Hu’s words to Xiao: with food in store, Hangzhou could hold out for six months or a year.

因而也越发感到自己的责任重大，
He felt the weight of his responsibility more keenly than ever.

所以这一夜睡在陆德义的『公馆』里，一遍一遍设想各种情况，
Lying that night in Lu’s “residence”, he imagined every possible turn of events again and again.

盘算着如何能够取信于李秀成，脱出监视；
How could he gain Li Xiucheng’s trust and free himself from surveillance?

如何遇到官军以后，能够使得他们相信他不是奸细，带他进城去见王有龄？
Once he encountered the imperial troops, how could he convince them that he was no spy and have them take him into the city to see Wang?

这样辗转反侧，直到听打四更，方始朦胧睡去；
He tossed and turned until he heard the fourth watch being called, and only then drifted into an uneasy sleep.

也不知睡了多少时候，突然惊醒，
He had no idea how long he had slept before he woke with a start.

只听得人声嘈杂，脚步匆遽，仿佛出现了极大的变故。
Voices clamoured and footsteps hurried outside, as though something momentous had happened.

萧家骥一惊之下，睡意全消，
All sleep vanished.

倏然坐起，凝神静听；听出一句话∶『妖风发了，
He sat bolt upright and listened intently, catching a cry: “The demon wind is up!

妖风发了！』
The demon wind is up!”

这句话似乎在哪里听过，
He seemed to have heard the expression somewhere.

萧家骥咬紧了牙，苦苦思索，终于想到了，是沙船上无事，听胡雪岩谈过，长毛称清军为『妖』，
Clenching his teeth, he searched his memory until at last he remembered: in an idle moment aboard the junk, Hu had told them that the Longhairs called the Qing troops “demons”.

『妖风发了』就是清军打过来了。
“The demon wind is up” meant that imperial troops were attacking.


一想到此，又惊又喜，急忙起床，扎束停当；
Alarmed and delighted at once, Xiao hurried out of bed and dressed himself securely.

却还不敢造次，
Still he dared not act rashly.

推开一条门缝，往外张望，
Opening the door a crack, he peered out.

只见长毛蜂拥而出，手中的武器，种类不一，有红缨枪、有白蜡杆、有大砍刀、也有洋枪——枪声已经起了；杂着呼啸之声，忽远忽近，忽东忽西，随着风势大小在变化，
Longhairs were pouring outside with weapons of every kind—red-tasselled spears, ash-wood staffs, great chopping swords and foreign firearms. Gunfire had begun already, mingled with shouts that seemed now near, now far, now east, now west as the wind rose and fell.

似乎清军颇不少。
The attacking imperial force appeared considerable.
