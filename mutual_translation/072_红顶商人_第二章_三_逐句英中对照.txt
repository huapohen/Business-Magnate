胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第二章（本章第3部分，共4部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


What should he do?
怎么样？

Xiao Jiaji asked himself.
萧家骥在心中自问；

If he meant to escape, this was an excellent opportunity.
要脱身，此时是大好机会，

But he knew nothing of conditions outside.
但外面的情况不清楚，

To blunder into a hail of bullets and die without anyone even knowing what had happened—what a senseless end that would be!
糊里糊涂投入枪林弹雨中，死了都只怕没人知道，岂不冤枉？

Yet if he stayed, there was at least one obvious difficulty: he could hardly expect to meet Li Xiucheng at once.
然而不走呢？别的不说，起码要见李秀成，就不是一下子办得到的；

Apart from the time he would lose, Lu Deyi might be killed in this very battle, leaving him without such a reasonable man to deal with.
耽误了工夫不说，也许陆德义就死在这一仗中，再没有这样一个讲理的人可以打交道，

The consequences did not bear thinking about.
后果更不堪设想。

While he wavered, another group hurried past outside the courtyard.
就在这样左右为难之际，只见院子外面又闪过一群人，

Their footsteps and voices were light, but urgent.
脚步轻，语声也轻，但很急促，

“Quick, quick!” someone urged.
『快，快！』有人催促，

“Get away from the Longhairs!
『快「逃长毛」，

It does not matter where we end up!”
逃到哪里算哪里？』

“Getting away from the Longhairs” was a common expression; Xiao had often heard Hu use it.
『逃长毛』是句很流行的话，萧家骥听胡雪岩也常将这三个字挂在口头，

It meant escaping from their hands.
意思是从长毛那里逃走；

But “it does not matter where we end up” was a revelation.
而『逃到哪里算哪里』，更是一大启示。

“Escape!” he told himself.
『逃！』他对自己说，

“If I do not, am I really going to sell arms to Li Xiucheng?”
『不逃，难道真的要跟李秀成做军火生意？』


His mind made up, he wasted no time, though he moved quickly rather than rashly.
打字主意，更不怠慢；不过虽快不急，

Making sure no one was about, he slipped down the narrow passage and emerged into an open space.
看清楚无人，一溜烟出了夹弄，豁然开朗，

The smell of cooking reached him.
同时闻到饭香，

He looked up: a kitchen.
抬头一看，是个厨房。

It was large and apparently deserted.
厨房很大，但似乎没有人。

He examined it carefully, crossing the courtyard a step at a time.
萧家骥仔细察看着，一步一步走过院落，

Only when he reached the stove did he discover a man sitting beside its base, warming himself.
直到灶前，才发现有个人生在灶下烤火；

The man was terribly thin, with enormous eyes; coming upon him so suddenly, Xiao found his appearance frightening and recoiled two paces.
人极瘦，眼睛大，骤见之下，形容格外可怖，吓得他倒退了两步。

The man merely stared, as if half-witted, his large, lifeless eyes showing no expression.
那人却似一个傻子，一双虽大而失神的眼，瞅着萧家骥，什么表情都没有。

“Who are you?” Xiao asked.
『你是什么人？』他问。

“Do not bother asking me,” the man replied feebly. “I am not running away.
『你不要来问我！』那人用微弱的声音答道∶『我不逃！

Run where you will, you never get out of their hands.
逃来逃去逃不出他们的手；

I shall leave it to fate.”
听天由命了。』

Half of Xiao’s courage ebbed away.
听得这话，萧家骥的心凉了一半，

He stared at the man, unable to speak for some time.
怔怔地望着他，半晌无语。


“By the look of you, you are not from here.
『看你这样子，不是本地人；

Where have you escaped from?”
哪里逃来的？』

The man had a kindly face.
看他相貌和善、

His voice was weak, as though life held no interest for him, and Xiao’s fear and suspicion subsided.
而且说话有气无力，生趣索然似的，萧家骥便消除一恐怕戒备之心，

“I have come from Shanghai,” he answered truthfully.
老实答道∶『我从上海来。』

“Is there not a foreign settlement in Shanghai?
『上海不是有夷场吗？

Everyone fleeing the troubles wants to get there.
大家逃难都要逃到那里去，

Why have you come into this place instead?”
你怎么反投到这里来？』

His voice sounded hollow with despair. “Heaven’s road lay open, and you would not take it; hell had no gate, yet you forced your way in.
那人用听起来空落落的绝望的声音说∶『天堂有路你不走，地狱无门闯进来！

Why bring such trouble on yourself?”
何苦？』

“I had no choice.”
『我也是无法，』

Xiao used the opportunity to sound him out, though he dared not yet tell the truth.
萧家骥借机试探，却又不便说真话，

“I have a friend in Hangzhou, a man bound to me for life and death.
『我有个生死至交，陷在杭州，

I wanted to go into the city to see him.”
我想进城去看他。』

“You are mad!
『你发疯了！』

People are eating each other inside Hangzhou.
那人说道，『杭州城里人吃人，

Your friend is probably dead of starvation already.
你那朋友，只怕早饿死了；

Where would you go to see him?
你到哪里去看他？

And even if you found him, you could not save him.
就算看到了，你又不能救他；

You would be trapped yourself and starve to death.
自己陷在里头，活活饿死。

What sort of reckoning is that?
这打的是什么算盘？

Truly, it must be fate.”
真正气数。』

His reproaches revealed a good heart.
话中责备，正显得本心是好的，

Xiao decided to tell him the truth, first asking, “May I know your name, sir?”
萧家骥决定跟他说实话，先问一句∶『你老人家贵姓？』


“Everyone calls me Old He.”
『人家都叫我老何。』

“Old He, my name is Xiao.
『老何，我姓萧，

I shall be honest with you.
跟你老人家老实说吧，

I have come to save the people of Hangzhou—or rather, it is not I, but a philanthropist of great renown in your city who is doing this good deed.
我是来救杭州百姓的——也不是我，是你们杭州城里鼎鼎大名的一位善人做好事；

He has brought a large quantity of grain from Shanghai and sent me to see Governor Wang with a message.”
带了大批粮食，由上海赶来。教我到城里见王抚台送信。』

Xiao paused briefly, then assumed the manner of a man staking everything on a single throw. “I have told you what is in my heart.
萧家骥略停一下，摆出一切都豁出去的神态说∶『老何，我把我心里的话都告诉你，

If you belong to the Longhairs, then it is my fate to die here, in this year, this month, this day, and this hour.
你如果是长毛一伙，算我命该如此，今年今月今日今时，要死在这里。

If you do not, please show me a way through.”
如果不是，请你指点我条路子。』

Old He listened and sat in thought without speaking.
老何听他说完，沉思不语，

After a long time he raised his head.
好久，才抬起头来；

Xiao saw that his eyes had changed.
萧家骥发觉他的眼神不同了，

They no longer had the dull, almost dying look of before.
不再是那黯然无光，近乎垂死的人的神色，

They shone with determination, as though all the strength in his body had gathered within them.
是闪耀着坚毅的光芒，仿佛一身的力量都集中在那方寸眸子中似的。


He held out his hand. “Where is the letter?”
他将手一伸∶『信呢？』

Xiao was taken aback. “What letter?”
萧家骥愕然∶『什么信？』

“Did you not say that this great benefactor had sent you with a message for Governor Wang?”
『你不是说，那位大善人托你送信给王抚台吗？』

“Yes, but it is a spoken message.
『是的。是口信。』

If there were black words on white paper and they fell into the Longhairs’ hands, would that not be disastrous?”
萧家骥说，『白纸写黑字，万一落在长毛手里，岂不糟糕？』

“A spoken message?”
『口信？』

Old He hesitated.
老何踌躇着，

“That would be rather difficult to carry.”
『口信倒不大好带。』

“What do you mean, Old He?”
『怎么？老何，』

Xiao understood him. “Were you intending to take it for me?”
萧家骥了解了他的意思∶『你是预备代我去送信？』

“Yes.
『是啊？

I should have a better chance than you.
我去比你去总多几分把握。

But looking as I do, no one would believe me if I said I had an oral message for the governor.”
不过，凭我这副样子，说要带口信给王抚台，没有人肯相信的。』

“Then let me ask you”—Xiao bowed to the ground—“to take me into the city.”
『那这样，』萧家骥一揖到地，『请老何你带我进城。』

“It will not be easy.
『不容易。

I might slip through alone, but someone who looks like you could never get in.”
我一个人还好混；象你这样子，混不进去。』

“Then what must I do?”
『那末，要怎样才混得进去？』


“First of all, your face is pink and white, as if you ate fish and meat every day.
『第一、你这副脸色，又红又白，就象天天吃大鱼大肉的样子，

If you got inside looking like that, there would be trouble.
混进城里，就是麻烦。

If you really mean to enter the city, you must put up with some unpleasantness.”
如果，你真想进城，要好好受点委屈。

“That does not matter.
『』不要紧！

I shall put up with anything.”
什么委屈，我都受。

“Very well.”
『『那好！』

Old He nodded.
老何点点头，

“I have one foot in the grave already.
『反正我也半截入土的了，

If I can do something like this first, it will have been worthwhile.
能做这么一件事，也值！

Let us see what is happening outside.” They observed quietly.
先看看外头。』于是静心细看，

The voices were still noisy, but the gunfire had slackened.
人声依旧相当嘈杂，但枪声却稀了。

“The government troops have been beaten,” Old He said confidently.
『官军打败了。』老何很有把握地说，

“This is the right time to leave.”
『这时走，正好。』


Xiao found it extraordinary that the man could judge the outcome of a battle merely by listening.
萧家骥觉得这是件不可思议的事，听一听声音，就能判断胜负，未免过于神奇。

This was a crucial moment; a single wrong step might be fatal.
眼前是重要关头，一步走错不得，

He could not help asking, “How do you know, Old He?”
所以忍不住问了一句∶『老何，你怎么知道？』

“I knew it from the beginning.
『我早就知道了。』

The government troops are so hungry that their sight is going black.
老何答道∶『官军饿得两眼发黑，

How can they fight?
哪里还打得动仗？

All they can do is make one charge.”
无非冲一阵而已。』

That explained the dwindling gunfire.
这就是枪声所以稀下来的缘故了。

On reflection, Xiao found it reasonable, and followed Old He out through a side gate of the Longhairs’ headquarters with renewed confidence.
萧家骥想想也有道理，便放心大胆地跟着老何从边门出了长毛的公馆。

Sure enough, the rebels were returning to their quarters.
果然，长毛已经收队，

They filled the main streets like ants, walking, talking, and laughing with all the air of victors.
满街如蚁，且行且谈且笑，一副打了胜仗的样子。

Fortunately, Old He knew the neighborhood intimately. Keeping clear of the main streets, he led Xiao through one narrow lane after another until they reached a dilapidated temple to the God of Wealth.
幸好长毛走的是大街，而老何路径甚熟，尽从小巷子里穿来穿去，最后到了一处破败的财神庙，

Inside, seven or eight beggars were huddled together, gambling with dice.
里面是七八个乞儿，正围在一起掷骰子赌钱。


“So you are not dead yet, Old He!” one of them said.
『老何，』其中有一个说，『你到没有死！』

Old He ignored him and addressed a man whose clothes were slightly less ragged than the others’. “A’mao, take off that tattered padded jacket.”
老何不理他，向一个衣衫略为整齐些的人说∶『阿毛，把你的破棉袄脱下来。』

“What for?”
『干什么？』

“Lend it to this friend.”
『借给这位朋友穿一穿。』

“And what am I to wear?”
『借了给他，我穿啥？』

“He will give you his clothes in exchange.”
『他把他的衣服换给你。』

At that, several men clamored to make the exchange themselves.
这一说便有好些人争着要换，

“Me!
『我来，

Me!” they cried in confusion.
我来！』乱糟糟地喊着。


Old He had made up his mind to deal only with A’mao.
老何打定主意，只要跟阿毛换；

Though the padded jacket was a little more presentable than the rest, it was coated in such a thick layer of grease and filth that it looked like a butcher’s apron.
他的一件破棉袄虽说略为整齐些，但厚厚一层垢腻，如屠夫的作裙，

The sight alone made Xiao nauseous.
已经让萧家骥要作呕了。

“There is no other way,” Old He said. “Without this, we cannot succeed.
『没有办法。』老何说道∶「不如此就叫不成功。

Worse than that, we shall be in danger as soon as we go out.
不但不成功，走出去还有危险。

And it is not just you: I must change too.”
不要说你，我也要换。

Xiao had no choice.
『听这一说，萧家骥无奈，

Gritting his teeth, he put on the jacket, along with torn shoes and socks.
只好咬紧牙关，换上那件棉袄，还有破鞋破袜。

His whole body crawled as if insects and ants were moving over his skin.
萧家骥只觉满身虫行蚁走般肉麻，

Never in his life had he endured anything like it.
自出娘胎，不曾吃过这样的苦头，

Yet now that the clothes were on, there could be no question of taking them off.
只是已穿上身，就决没有脱下来的道理。

Looking at Old He, he saw that the man too had exchanged clothes with someone and was wearing garments even dirtier and more ragged than his own.
再看老何也找人换了一身衣服，比自己的更破更脏，

Why was another man, with no obligation to him, taking on this misery as well?
别人没来由也受这样一分罪，所为何来？

Thinking of that made it easier to bear.
这样想着，便觉得容易忍受了。


“A’mao,” Old He asked, “what is today’s password?”
『阿毛！』老何又说∶『今天是啥口令？』

“I do not know.”
『我不晓得。』

“I know,” someone answered loudly.
『我晓得。』有人响亮地回答，

“What do you want it for, Old He?”
『老何，你问它做啥？』

“I have a use for it, naturally.”
『自然有用处。』

Old He turned to Xiao. “Have you any silver dollars?
老何回头问萧家骥∶『你有没有大洋钱，

Take one out.”
摸一块出来。』

Xiao did as he was told, and Old He bought a password with the coin.
萧家骥如言照办；老何用那块银洋买得了一个口令。

“But what is this password for?” Xiao asked.
但是，『这是什么口令呢？』萧家骥问。


“For entering the city,” Old He replied.
『进城的口令。』老何答道，

“The gates may be closed, but they still send a few beggars out to gather intelligence.
『城虽闭了，城里还是弄些要饭的出来打探军情，

Not that it does any good.”
一点用处都没有。』

To Xiao it was immensely useful.
在萧家骥却太有用了；

Now he also understood why he had had to submit to this ordeal.
同时也恍然大悟，为何非受这样的罪不可？

They had not gone far when a timber fort came into view in the distance.
走不多远，遥遥发现一道木城；

Xiao knew they were still only halfway to the city gate.
萧家骥知道离城门还有一半路程。

Hu had told him how, after Hangzhou’s ten gates were surrounded, Wang Youling had strained every effort to open a route to the river.
他听胡雪岩谈过杭州十城被围以后，王有龄全力企图打开一条江路，

Vastly outnumbered, he had lacked the strength to do it.
但兵力众寡悬殊，有心无力。

Just then Zhang Yuliang had withdrawn from Fuyang.
正好张玉良自富阳撤退；

Wang immediately sent men to contact him and proposed an advance by fortified stages: Zhang would build camps inward from the riverbank, while the defenders built outward from the city.
王有龄立即派人跟他联络，采取步步为营的办法，张玉良从江干往城里扎营；城里往江干扎营，

Each position, once established, would be held firmly.
扎住一座，坚守一座，

They would seek no quick result, but proceed methodically until the two lines eventually joined and a route to survival opened.
不求速效而稳扎稳打，总有水到渠成，联成一气打开一线生路的时候。


Wang’s handwritten appeal had been exceptionally urgent: “The survival of Hangzhou depends upon this one undertaking.
由于王有龄的亲笔信，写得极其恳切，说『杭城存亡，视此一举，

We must not lose the opportunity and ruin the attempt.”
不可失机误事，』

Zhang Yuliang had therefore dared not delay.
所以张玉良不敢怠慢，

Fighting as he advanced, he built more than a dozen camps from the outer embankment by the river, until another waterway barred his way.
从江干外堤塘一面打、一面扎营，扎了十几座，遭到一条河，成了障碍，

He sent men through the siege lines into the city, asking Wang to launch a coordinated attack and enclosing a clear map of his positions.
张玉良派人夺围进城，要求王有龄派兵夹击；同时将他扎营的位置，画成明明白白的图，一并送上。

Wang immediately instructed Rao Tingxuan to dispatch a large body of troops into the city.
王有龄即时通知饶廷选调派大队进城；

But Rao delayed for a night, and the secret leaked out.
谁知饶廷选一夜耽误，泄潜心机密，

Li Xiucheng set his men to work overnight and erected a timber fort halfway along the route, with cannon mounted on it.
李秀成连夜兴工，在半路上筑成一座木城，城上架炮。

Outside stood an earthen wall pierced with firing holes for muskets.
城外又筑土墙，墙上凿眼架枪，

This cut off Zhang’s forces from Rao’s, and Zhang himself was killed by cannon fire.
隔绝了张玉良与饶廷选的两支人马；而且张玉良因此中炮阵亡。

That was the position when Hu left Hangzhou.
这是胡雪岩离开杭州的情形，

The timber fort still stood and was plainly impassable.
如今木城依旧，自然无法通过；

Old He led Xiao well around it, keeping clear of the Longhairs, until at last they saw a city gate.
老何带着萧着骥，避开长毛，远远绕过木城，终于见了城门。


“This is Houchao Gate.”
『这是候潮门。』

“I know.”
『我晓得。』

Xiao recited, “‘Awaiting the Tide’, one hears the ‘Clear Waves’ sound; ‘Surging Gold’ and ‘Qiantang’ make ‘Great Peace’ secure.”
萧家骥念道∶『「候潮」听得「清波」响，「涌金」「钱塘」定「太平」。』

The couplet wove together the names of five of Hangzhou’s gates, a piece of lore known only to local people.
这两句诗中，嵌着杭州五个城门的名称，只有本地人才知道；

Hearing it, Old He felt a sudden warmth toward him.
所以老何听他一念，浮起异常亲切之感，

For the first time, a smile appeared on the withered, shrunken, wrinkled face that scarcely seemed human.
枯干瘦皱，望之不似人形的脸上，第一次出现了笑容，

“So you know that!
『你倒懂！』

Where did you hear it?”
他说，『哪里听来的？』

Xiao smiled in return. “This may be my first visit to Hangzhou, but I know a good many of its stories.”
萧家骥笑笑答道∶『杭州我虽第一次来，杭州的典故我倒晓得很多。』

“You have a bond with Hangzhou,” Old He said, much comforted.
『你跟杭州有缘。』老何很欣慰地说，

“Everything is sure to go well.”
『一定顺利。』


As they spoke, they approached a ditch.
说着话，已走近壕沟；

Men were patrolling on its inner side; on the outer side, others lay with their ears pressed to the ground.
沟内有些巡逻，沟外却有人伏地贴耳，

Xiao did not know what they were doing and stopped in surprise.
不知在干什事？萧家骥不免诧异却步。

“Who are those men?”
『这些是什么人？』

“They are blind,” Old He replied.
『是瞎子。』老何答道，

“Blind men have particularly keen hearing.
『瞎子的耳朵特别灵；

Wine jars are buried underground too, so they can hear any noise all the more clearly.”
地下再埋着酒坛子，如有啥声音听得格外清楚。』

“Ah, I understand!”
『噢！我懂了。』

It dawned on Xiao.
萧家骥恍然大悟，

“These are the listening jars I have heard of.
『这就是所谓「瓮器」，

They are afraid the Longhairs will dig tunnels and plant explosives.”
是怕长毛挖地道，埋炸药。』

“Exactly.
『对了！

Come along—the soldier opposite is raising his gun.”
快走吧，那面的兵在端枪了。』

Old He lifted both hands and hurried forward.
说着，老何双手高举急步而行；

Xiao copied him, stopping only when they reached the edge of the ditch.
萧家骥如法而施，走到壕沟边才住脚。


“Password!” the soldier challenged them from the other side.
『口令！』对面的兵喝问。

“The sun and moon shine bright.”
『日月光明。』

Without another word, the soldier went over to a windlass and turned its handle.
那个兵不作声了，走向一座轴驴，摇动把手，

A plank that had been standing upright came down across the ditch to form a drawbridge.
将一条矗立着的跳板放了下来，横搁在壕沟上，算是一道吊桥。


Though the soldier looked haggard and exhausted, as if even speaking demanded too much effort, Xiao thought his continued devotion to duty worthy of respect.
萧家骥觉得这个士兵，虽然形容憔悴，有气无力，仿佛连话也懒得说似的，但依然忠于职守，也就很可敬了；

Perhaps the government troops’ discipline was not quite as dreadful as people said.
由此便想∶官军的纪律，并不如传说中那样糟不可言。

If so, why make trouble for himself by trying to slip into the city?
既然如此，何必自找麻烦，要混进城去。

He spoke as soon as the thought occurred. “Old He, would it not be easier if I explained my purpose and asked the officer commanding here to send some men to escort me inside?”
想到就说∶『老何！我看我说明来意，请这里驻守的军官，派弟兄送我进去，岂不省事？』

Old He considered. “Everyone says Deputy Commander Zeng, who guards Houchao Gate, is a good man.
老何沉吟了一下答道∶『守候潮门的曾副将，大家都说他不错的；

You might try.
不妨试一试。

But remember”—he added a warning—“a scholar confronted with soldiers may find that reason gets him nowhere.
不过，』老何提出警告∶『秀才遇着兵，有理说不清，也是实话。

You know the facts yourself.
到底怎么回事，你自己晓得；

Do not contradict yourself and invite trouble.”
不要前言不搭后语，自讨苦吃。』

“I shall not.
『不会，不会！

Every word I say is true.
我的话，货真价实；

All that white rice is waiting in the middle of the river.
那许多白米停在江心里，

Could anyone invent that?”
这是假得来的吗？』


Hearing this, Old He changed his plan.
听这一说，老何翻然改计，

He gave the sentry a brief account and asked to see an officer.
跟守卫的兵士略说经过，求见官长；

They were passed up the chain from a bazong to a qianzong, then to a garrison officer, until at last, at Houchao Gate, they met Zeng Desheng, a deputy commander under Rao Tingxuan.
于是由把总到千总，到守备，一层层带上去，终于候潮门见到了饶廷选的副将曾得胜。

“We know Circuit Intendant Hu went to Shanghai to buy rice,” Zeng said after hearing the story. “But you have no letter, and you speak with an outsider’s accent.
『胡道台到上海买米，我们是晓得的。』曾得胜得知缘由以后，这样问道∶『不过你既没有书信，又是外路口音，

I cannot make out exactly who you are.
到底怎么回事，倒弄不明白；

How can I take you to Governor Wang?”
怎么领你去见王抚台？』

Xiao understood his concern.
萧家骥懂他的意思，

“Sir, search me.
叫声∶『曾老爷！请你搜我身子，

I am no assassin, and a man openly asking for an audience is surely no spy.
我不是刺客；公然求见，当然也不是奸细。

I could not risk carrying a letter through the enemy’s lines.
只为穿越敌阵，实在不能带什么书信，

Once I see Governor Wang, I can tell him something that will prove Hu sent me.
见了王抚台，我有话说，自然会让他相信我是胡道台派来的。

If he does not believe me, you may have my head.
如果王抚台不相信，请曾老爷杀我的头。

I shall leave a written military pledge with you.”
我立一张军令状在你这里。』

“A military pledge?
『立什么军令状？

That is something out of a novel.
这是小说书上的话。

I shall take you.”
我带你去就是。』

Xiao had made Zeng laugh, though the smile looked more painful than weeping.
曾得胜被萧家骥逗得笑了；不过他的笑容比哭还难看。

“Thank you, sir!” Xiao answered briskly, then immediately made a request.
『是！』萧家骥响亮地答应一声，立即提出一个要求，

“Please give me a padded uniform like your soldiers’ to wear.”
『请曾老爷给我一身弟兄的棉军服穿！』

He was desperate to shed those filthy rags.
他急于脱卸那身又破又脏的衣服；

But his relief lasted only a moment.
但轻快不过片刻，

Once inside the city, the reek of corpses rose about him so powerfully that he nearly fainted.
一进了城，尸臭蒸熏，几乎让他昏倒。


Wang Youling had lost hope.
王有龄已经绝望了！

Jiechun had launched an attack early that morning—the very moment when Xiao had heard the gunfire.
一清早，杰纯冲过一阵——就是萧家骥听到枪声的那时刻；

More than a dozen boats of life-saving white rice lay waiting to be unloaded.
十几船活命的白米等着去运，

If even that incentive could not summon the soldiers’ strength, who could possibly open the supply route and win them a chance of survival?
这样的彭励，还不能激出士兵的力量来，又还有什么人能开粮通道，求得一线生路？

Wang therefore decided to write his final memorial to the throne: “Your servant Youling previously entrusted Governor Xue Huan of Jiangsu with the task of reporting on my behalf that Hangzhou was surrounded on all sides, the route to the river blocked, and the troops and people trapped within the city.
因此，他决定要写遗折了∶窃臣有龄前将杭城四面被围，江路阻绝，城中兵民受困各情形，托江苏抚臣薛焕，据情代奏，

I do not know whether that report has reached Your Majesty.
不识能否达到？

All ten gates are now closely besieged, and the rebel host grows ever larger.
现在十门围紧，贼众愈聚愈多，

Repeatedly we have led our starving troops into battle and secretly arranged coordinated attacks with the camps along the riverbank.
迭次督同饥军，并密约江干各营会合夹击，

In several dozen engagements, great and small, by day and night, we have failed to open even a single supply route.
计大小昼夜数十战，竟不能开通一线饷道。

The city’s food is entirely exhausted.
城内粮食净尽，

Horses have been slaughtered to feed the soldiers, followed by cats and rats; roots and tree bark are eaten.
杀马饷军，继以猫鼠，食草根树皮，

The roads are strewn with the starved dead, whose numbers increase daily.
饿殍载道，日多一日，

Officers and men endure hunger and hold their posts, but they no longer have the strength to wield their weapons.”
兵弁忍饥固守，无力操戈。


“At first we feared that the exhaustion of supplies would bring mutiny within.
初虞粮尽内变，

But after we pleaded with the men in tears and sought to comfort them, all pledged to follow us to the death, without a disloyal thought.
经臣等涕泣拊循，均效死相从，绝无二志，

We have discharged our duties so poorly that troops and people must sit awaiting death.
臣等奉职无状，致军民坐以待毙，

Long have we suffered such anguish that life is unbearable.”
久已痛不欲生。


At this point the pain in Wang’s eyes became so sharp that he had to lay down his brush.
写到这里，王有龄眼痛如割，不能不停下笔来。

He had suffered from the ailment for a whole year.
他这眼疾已经整一年了，

At first it had been described as “an excessive depletion of the heart’s blood, upward pressure from the liver and bowels, affliction of the spleen channel, and weakness of the lung’s qi.”
先是『心血过亏，肝肠上逼，脾经受克，肺气不好』，

It had then developed into “wind and fire flaming upward.” Without a moment’s peace of mind, his eyes had swollen into wartlike lumps; when pressed, they shed blood with the tears.
转为『风火上炎』而又没有一刻能安心的时候，以致眼肿如疣，用手一按，血随泪下；

Everyone who saw them was horrified.
见到的人，无不大骇。

Later, a renowned eye physician had treated him with medicines and other remedies, and there had been some improvement.
后来遇到一位眼科名医，刀圭与药石兼施，才有起色；

Since the siege began, however, the old trouble had returned, worsening day by day.
但自围城以来，旧疾复发，日重一日，

Wang bitterly resented it.
王有龄深以为恨，

He had long ceased to care about his life, but the failure of his eyes was a cruel affliction.
性命他倒是早已置之度外，就这双眼睛不得力，大是苦事。


Other official papers could have been dictated to a secretary, a son, or a nephew.
如果是其他文报，可以口授给幕友子侄代笔，

But Wang did not wish anyone to see this last memorial.
但这通遗折，王有龄不愿为人所见，

Forcing open eyes that felt as though needles pierced them, he continued: “While a breath remains in us, our sole thought is to kill one more rebel and hold out one more day.
所以强睁如针刺般疼痛的双眼，继续往下写∶第残喘尚存，总以多杀一贼，多持一日为念，

We reflect with tears that, after last year’s devastation by war, few households in Hangzhou retained any reserves.
泣思杭城经去年兵燹之后，户鲜盖藏，

Rice and other provisions were all brought from Shaoxing, and much of our military funding came from there as well.
米粮一切，均由绍贩运；军饷以资该处接济为多。

After the fall of Jin and Lan, we planned at once to defend the Ningbo–Shaoxing supply line in strength.
金、兰这法后，臣等早经筹计，须重防以固宁绍一线饷源，

First we ordered Zhang Jingqu, intendant of the Ningbo–Shaoxing–Taizhou circuit, and later repeatedly directed Salt Commissioner Zhuang Huanwen and the nominated circuit intendant Peng Siju to lead troops and militia to establish defenses.
乃始则饬宁绍台道张景渠，继又迭饬运司庄焕文，记名道彭斯举，各带兵勇设防，

Wang Lüqian opposed and obstructed every measure.
均经王履廉议格不行；

He also shielded the wealthy gentry, exhausting all possibilities of obtaining contributions or loans.
又复袒庇绅富，因之捐借俱穷，

Clinging obstinately to his own views, he thwarted us at every turn.
固执已见，诸事掣肘。

Still intent upon making defensive preparations, we noted that Liao Zongyuan, together with Zhao Jingxian of the Huzhou gentry, had held a city in peril for more than a year.
臣等犹思设防堵御，查有廖守元与湖绅赵景贤，历守危城，一载有余，

We transferred Liao to act as prefect of Shaoxing and had him devote every effort to organizing its defense.
调署绍兴府，竭筹布置。

But he went against the wishes of the great local gentry, who did not want defenses established.
乃违大绅不愿设防之意，

They falsely accused him of colluding with the rebels and beat him savagely, while Lüqian stood by without intervening.
诬以通贼痛殴，履谦从旁袖手；

When the city fell, Lüqian fled; Liao Zongyuan ultimately died with the city.
比及城陷而走，卒致廖宗元城亡与亡，

Thereafter, the districts of Ningbo and Shaoxing fell one after another, leaving Hangzhou an isolated last stake, beyond all hope of rescue.”
从此宁绍各属，相继失陷，而杭城已为孤注，无可解救矣！


Wang could not release the resentment that filled him.
写到这里，王有龄一口怨气不出，

He thought of Wang Lüqian taking his family and baggage out to sea from Ningbo and sailing away to Fujian, free and at ease, while the entire population of Hangzhou endured a catastrophe without precedent.
想到王履谦携带家眷辎重，由宁波出海到福建，远走高飞，逍遥自在，而杭州全城百姓，受此亘古所无的浩劫；

Even if he and the garrison general, Ruichang, died to repay their sovereign, their deaths could do nothing to remedy the situation.
自己与驻防将军瑞昌，纵能拼得一死报君主，却无补于大局，

He pressed on furiously with his brush: “Wang Lüqian has ruined the whole undertaking.
因而又奋笔写道∶王履谦贻误全局，

I shall die unable to close my eyes.
臣死不瞑目。

Our funds are exhausted and all hope of relief is gone; destruction may come at any moment.
眼下饷绝援穷，危在旦夕，

We have betrayed Your Majesty’s gracious trust, and our guilt admits of no pardon.
辜负圣恩，罪无可逭。

We beg only that Your Majesty select and dispatch a mighty army to sweep away the rebels without delay.
惟求皇上简发重兵，迅图扫荡，

Then the day of our death will be as though we lived again.
则臣等虽死之日，犹生之年。

At present our memorials cannot pass through, and it will be still harder to smuggle them out hereafter.
现在折报不通，以后更难偷达，

We therefore set forth jointly in this draft memorial the circumstances of Hangzhou’s impending collapse, for secret delivery to Shanghai, where Governor Xue Huan of Jiangsu may have it transcribed and submitted to the throne on our behalf.
谨将杭城决裂情形，合词备兵折稿，密递上海江苏抚臣薛焕代缮具奏。

Looking up toward Your Sacred Majesty as toward Heaven, we are overwhelmed by anguish and fearful awe.”
仰圣瞻天，无任痛切悚惶之至。
