胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第二章（本章第4部分，共4部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


遗折尚未写完，家人已经闻声环集∶王有龄看着奶妈抱着的五岁小儿子，肤色黄黑，骨瘦如柴，
Before Wang could finish the memorial, his family had heard his grief and gathered about him. He looked at his five-year-old son in the nurse’s arms, his skin sallow and dark, his body thin as a bundle of sticks.

越发心如刀割，
The sight cut him to the heart.

一恸而绝。
He gave a cry of anguish and lost consciousness.

等救醒过来，只见他的大儿子橘云含着泪强展笑容，
When they revived him, his eldest son, Juyun, stood before him, forcing a smile through his tears.

『爹！』他说，『胡大叔派人来了。』
“Father, Uncle Hu has sent someone.”

『喔，』
“Oh!”

这无论如何是个喜信，
Whatever else might happen, this was good news.

王有龄顿觉有了精神。
Wang felt his strength return.

『在哪里？』
“Where is he?”

『在花厅上等着。』
“Waiting in the reception room.

橘云说道∶『爹也不必出去了，
There is no need for you to go out, Father.

就请他上房来见吧！』
Let him come to your room.”

『也好。』
“Very well.

王有龄说，『这时候还谈什么体制？
What use is ceremony at a time like this?

再说，胡大叔派的人，就是自己人。
Besides, anyone Uncle Hu sends is one of our own.

请他进来好了。』
Ask him in.

他又问∶『来人姓什么？』
What is his name?”

『姓萧！
“Xiao.

年纪很轻，他说他是古应春的学生。』
He is very young, and he says he is Gu Yingchun’s pupil.”


进上房，萧家骥以大礼拜见。
Entering the inner room, Xiao Jiaji offered the full ceremonial obeisance.

王有龄力弱不能还礼，只叫∶『萧义士，
Wang was too weak to return it and could only protest, “Gallant Mr Xiao!

萧义士，
Gallant Mr Xiao!

万不敢当。』
I cannot possibly accept such an honor.”

萧家骥敬重他的孤苦忠节，依旧恭恭敬敬地一跪三叩首；
Respecting his lonely suffering and steadfast loyalty, Xiao nevertheless knelt reverently and touched his forehead to the floor three times.

只有由橘云在一旁还了礼，然后端张椅子，请他在王有龄床前坐下。
Juyun returned the courtesy on his father’s behalf, then brought a chair and asked the visitor to sit beside the bed.

『王大人！』
“Governor Wang!”

萧家骥只叫得这一声，下面的话就说不出来了。
Xiao got no further.

这倒不是怯官，
It was not awe of an official that silenced him.

只为一路而来，所见所闻，是梦想不到的惊心惨目；
Everything he had seen and heard along the way had been more appalling than he could ever have imagined.

特别是此一刻，王家上下，一个个半死不活，
Now, above all, every member of the Wang household seemed half dead.

看他们有气无力地飘来飘去，真如鬼影幢幢，以致于连他自己都不知道此身究竟是在人间，还是在地狱？
They drifted weakly about like wavering ghosts, until he could no longer tell whether he was still among the living or already in hell.

因而有些神志恍惚，一时竟想不起话从哪里开头？
His mind grew dazed, and for a moment he did not know where to begin.

于是反主为客，王有龄先问起古应春∶『令师我也见过，
Wang therefore took the initiative and asked about Gu. “I have met your teacher.

我们还算是干亲。
We are, in fact, connected by an adopted family relationship.

想来他近况很好？』
I trust he has been doing well?”

『是，是。
“Yes, yes.

托福，托福！』
Thanks to your good fortune, sir!”


等话出口，萧家骥才发觉一开口就错；
Only after the words escaped did Xiao realize that he had begun badly.

王有龄眼前是这般光景，还有何福可托？
With Wang in such a state, what good fortune could anyone borrow from him?

说这话，岂不近乎讥讽？
It almost sounded like mockery.

这样想着，急图掩饰失言，
Anxious to cover his mistake, he hurried on.

便紧接着说∶『王大人大忠大义，
“Your loyalty and righteousness are known to all, Governor Wang.

知道杭州情形的人，没有一个不感动的。
No one who knows the situation in Hangzhou can fail to be moved.

都拿王大人跟何制台相比——。』这又失言了！
Everyone compares you with Governor-General He—” Another blunder!

何桂清弃地而逃，拿他相比，自是对照；然仿佛责以与杭州共亡似的。
He Guiqing had abandoned his territory and fled; the comparison was naturally intended as a contrast, yet it seemed to demand that Wang perish with Hangzhou.

萧家骥既悔且愧又自恨，所以语声突住；
Ashamed, remorseful, and angry with himself, Xiao broke off.

平日伶牙利齿的人，这时变得笨嘴拙舌，不敢开口了。
Normally so fluent, he had become tongue-tied and hardly dared speak again.

谁知道这话倒是发生了意想不到的效用，
Yet his words produced an unexpected result.

王有龄不但不以为忤；脸上反而有了笑容，
Far from being offended, Wang smiled.

『上海五方杂处，议论最多。』
“Shanghai gathers people from every quarter, and everyone has an opinion.

他问∶『他们是怎么拿我跟何制军相比？』
How do they compare me with Governor-General He?”


既然追问，不能不说，
Since Wang pressed him, Xiao had to answer.

萧家骥定定神答道∶『都说王大人才是大大的忠臣。
He composed himself. “They all say that you are a truly loyal servant of the throne.

跟何制台一比，贤愚不肖，更加分明了。
Set beside Governor-General He, the difference between worth and unworthiness becomes all the clearer.

大家都在保佑王大人，逢凶化吉，遇难成祥了。』
Everyone prays that danger will turn to safety for you and misfortune to good.”

『唉！』
“Ah!”

王有龄长长地舒了口气，
Wang let out a long breath.

『有这番舆论，可见得公道自在人心。』
“If that is what people say, then justice does indeed live in human hearts.”

他略停一下又问∶『雪岩总有信给我？』
After a pause he asked, “Xueyan must have sent a letter?”

『怕路上遇到长毛，胡先生没有写信，
“Mr Hu feared that I might encounter the Longhairs on the road, so he wrote nothing.

只有口信。』
He entrusted me with a spoken message.”

萧家骥心想，胡雪岩所说，王有龄向他托孤的话，
Xiao remembered the private conversation Hu had described, in which Wang had asked him to care for his child after his death.

原是为了征信之用；
Its purpose had been to establish Xiao’s credentials.

现在王有龄既已相信自己的身分，这话就不必再提，免得惹他伤心，
Now that Wang already accepted his identity, he need not mention it and cause further grief.

所以接下来便谈正题∶『采办的米，四天前就到了，停在江心；
He went straight to the business. “The rice we bought arrived four days ago and is lying in the middle of the river.

胡先生因为王大人曾交代，米船一到，自会派人跟他联络，所以不敢离开。
Because you told Mr Hu that you would send someone to contact him as soon as the grain boats arrived, he has not dared leave them.

一直等到昨天，并无消息；
He waited until yesterday without news.

胡先生焦躁得食不甘味，夜不安枕，
Anxiety has robbed him of his appetite and his sleep.

特为派我冒险上岸来送信，请王大人赶快派兵，打通粮道，搬运上岸。』
He therefore sent me ashore at great risk to bring this message: please dispatch troops quickly to open the supply route so that the rice can be landed.”

话还未完，王有龄双泪直流，
Before he had finished, tears were streaming down Wang’s face.

不断摇头，哽咽着说∶『昨天就得到消息，
He shook his head repeatedly and sobbed, “We learned of it yesterday.

今天也派兵出城了。
We sent troops out this morning.

没有用！
It was no use!

叫长毛困死了；困得一点气力都没有了。
The Longhairs have penned us in until we have not a scrap of strength left.

可望而不可即；
We can see it, but cannot reach it.

有饭吃不到口，
Food is there, yet we cannot put it into our mouths.

真教我死不瞑目。』
How can I close my eyes in death?”

说到这里，放声一恸；
He broke into a great cry of anguish.

王家大小，亦无不抢天呼地，跟着痛哭。
Everyone in the household, old and young, wept with him, calling upon Heaven and earth.

萧家骥心头一酸，眼泪汨汨而下，也夹在一起号啕。
Xiao’s heart ached; tears poured down his cheeks, and he too sobbed aloud.

『流泪眼看流泪眼』，相互劝慰着收住了眼泪；
Tearful eyes met tearful eyes as they comforted one another and at last brought their grief under control.

萧家骥重拾中断话头，
Xiao then returned to the interrupted discussion.

要讨个确实主意。
He needed a definite decision.


问到这话，又惹王有龄伤心；
The question brought Wang fresh anguish.

这是唯一的一条生路，关乎全城数十万生灵，
This was the only road to survival for hundreds of thousands of people.

明知可望而不可即，却又怎么能具此大决断，说一声∶『算了！
Even knowing that it lay beyond their reach, how could he take the terrible decision to say, “There is no hope.

你们走吧！』
Go”?

不走等机会又如何？
And what if the boats stayed and waited for a chance?

能办得到这一点，自然最好；
If that were possible, it would naturally be best.

虽然画饼不能充饥，但是望梅或可止渴，
A painted cake could not satisfy hunger, but the thought of plums might ease thirst.

有这许多米停泊在钱塘江心，或者能激励军心，发现奇迹——
With so much rice lying in the Qiantang, the troops might find fresh courage and work a miracle.

王有龄见过这样的奇迹，幼时见邻家失火，有个病足在床的人，居然能健步冲出火窟。
Wang had seen such a miracle in childhood: when a neighbor’s house caught fire, a man who had been bedridden with crippled legs somehow leaped up and ran out of the flames.

人到绝处想求生时，那份潜力的发生，常常是不可思议的。
The hidden strength summoned by a desperate will to live could be astonishing.

然而这到底是可遇而不可求的事。
Yet such things might occur; they could not be commanded.

这许多米摆在那里，长毛必起觊觎之心∶就算他们自己不绝粮，但为了陷敌于绝境，亦必千方百计动脑筋不可，
All that rice was sure to tempt the Longhairs. Even if they had enough food themselves, they would devise every possible means of depriving the besieged of their last hope.

或明攻、或暗袭，只要有一于此，胡雪岩十之八九会葬身在钱塘江中，
Whether by open assault or a secret raid, one attack might very well send Hu Xueyan to his death in the Qiantang.

追随伍子胥于地下，呜咽朝夕，含恨千古。
He would join Wu Zixu beneath its waters, his bitter grief sounding in the tides morning and evening for a thousand years.

转念到此，王有龄凄然下泪，摇头长叹∶『何苦「临死还拉个垫背的」？
At the thought, Wang wept sorrowfully, shook his head, and sighed. “Why drag another man down with me when I die?

萧义士，你跟雪岩说∶心余力绌，
Mr Xiao, tell Xueyan that our will outstrips our strength.

坐以待毙。
We can only sit and await death.

请他快走吧！』
Ask him to leave at once.”

其实这倒是萧家骥想讨到的一句话；
This was, in fact, the answer Xiao had come hoping to obtain.

但听王有龄说出口来，他反答应不下了。
But now that Wang had spoken it, he could not bring himself to assent.


『王大人！再筹划筹划看！』
“Governor Wang, think it over once more. There may be a way.”

『不用筹划了。
“There is nothing left to think over.

日日盼望，夜夜盘算；
I hope all day and plan all night.

连想派个人跟雪岩联络，都不容易办得到。
Even sending someone to contact Xueyan is almost beyond us.

唉，
Ah!”

『王有龄痛心欲绝地说∶』我什么都不错，只错了两件事，
Wang was in mortal anguish. “I did nothing wrong except in two matters.

一件是当初有人劝我从城上筑一条斜坡，直到江边，派重兵把守，以保粮路，
First, someone advised me at the beginning to build a ramp from the city wall down to the riverbank and guard it with a strong force to secure our supply route.

我怕深累民力，
I feared the burden on the people.

而且工程浩大，担心半途而废，枉抛民力，
The undertaking was so vast that I worried we might have to abandon it halfway, wasting all their labor.

不曾采纳。
I did not accept the proposal.

如今想来，大错特错。
Now I see how terribly wrong I was.”

『这实在是个好办法，
It really had been an excellent plan.

有了这条路，当然也难免遭长毛的袭击；但九次失败，一次成功，城内亦可暂延残喘，
Such a route would naturally have been attacked by the Longhairs, but even if nine attempts failed and one succeeded, the city could have prolonged its existence a little.

决不会象现在这样被困得一点点生路都找不到。
It could never have been trapped as completely as it was now, without the smallest opening toward life.


当然，这话要说出来，会更使王有龄伤心，
To say so, however, would only deepen Wang’s grief.

所以只好反过来说，
Xiao therefore argued the opposite.

『那也不见得。』
“Not necessarily.

他说，『照我一路看到的情形，长毛太多，就有这条斜坡，也怕守不住。』
From what I saw on the way, there are so many Longhairs that even with such a ramp, I doubt it could have been held.”

『这不去说他了。
“Let us leave that.

第二件事最错！』
The second mistake was the worst.”

王有龄黠然说道∶『被围之初，有人说该闭城，有人说要开城放百姓，
Wang spoke dejectedly. “When the siege began, some said we must close the gates; others said we should open them and let the people out.

聚讼纷纭，莫衷一是。
There was endless argument and no agreement.

我不该听了主张闭城的人的话，
I should not have listened to those who urged closure.

当初该十门大开，放百姓去逃生才是正办。』
The proper course was to throw all ten gates wide and let the people escape.”

『王大人，你老也不必懊悔了。
“You must not torment yourself, Governor.

说不定当初城门一开，长毛趁机会一冲，杭州早就不保。』
If the gates had been opened, the Longhairs might have charged in and Hangzhou might have fallen long ago.”

『原来顾虑的也就是这一点。
“That was precisely what we feared.

总当解围是十天半个月的事，大家不妨守一守；
We thought relief would come in ten days or a fortnight, and that we might as well hold out.

开城放百姓，会动摇军心。
Opening the gates to let the civilians go would shake the army’s resolve.

哪知道，结果还是守不住。
Who could have known that, in the end, we should fail to hold the city anyway?

既有今日，何必当初？
If it was to come to this, why did we do it?

我对不起杭州的百姓啊！』
I have failed the people of Hangzhou!”

说到这道，又是一场号啕大哭；
Again he broke into loud sobs.

萧家骥再次陪泪，而心里却已有了打算，
Xiao wept with him, but a plan had already formed in his mind.

哽咽着喊道∶『王大人，王大人，
Choking back his tears, he called, “Governor Wang!

请你听我说一句。』
Please listen to what I have to say.”


等王有龄悲伤略减，萧家骥提出一个办法，
When Wang’s grief had eased a little, Xiao proposed a plan.

也可以说是许诺；而实在是希望——希望粮船能再安然等待三天；更希望城内官军能在这三天以内，杀出一条血路，运粮上岸。
It might have been called a promise, though in truth it was a hope: that the grain boats could remain safely where they were for another three days, and, more urgently still, that within those three days the troops inside the city could fight their way to the river and bring the rice ashore.

『但愿如此！』
“May it be so!”

王有龄强自振作着说，
Wang forced himself to rally.

『我们内外和继，尽这三天以内拼一拼命。』
“Let us work together, inside and outside, and stake our lives on these next three days.”

『是！』
“Yes!”

为了鼓舞城内官兵，萧家骥又大胆作了个许诺∶『只要城内官兵能够打到江边，船上的洋兵一定会得接应；
To encourage the defenders, Xiao ventured another promise. “If the troops from the city can reach the riverbank, the foreign soldiers aboard the boats will certainly support them.

他们的人数虽不多，火器相当厉害，很得力的。』
There are not many of them, but their firearms are powerful and highly effective.”

『能这样最好。
“That would be best.

果然天从人愿，杭州能够解围，将来洋兵的犒赏，都着落在我身上。
If Heaven grants our wish and Hangzhou is relieved, I shall be responsible for rewarding the foreign troops.

多怕不行；两万银子！』
I may not be able to manage more, but twenty thousand taels!”

王有龄拍着胸脯说，
Wang struck his chest.

『哪怕我变卖薄产来赔，都不要紧。』
“Even if I must sell my modest property to make up the sum, I shall not mind.”

『是了。』
“Very well.”

萧家骥站起身来说∶『我跟王大人告辞；早点赶回去办正事。』
Xiao rose. “I must take my leave and return as quickly as possible to arrange matters.”


『多谢你！萧义士。』
“Thank you, gallant Mr Xiao.”

王有龄衷心感激地说∶『杭州已不是危城，简直是绝地；
Wang spoke with heartfelt gratitude. “Hangzhou is no longer merely a city in danger; it is a place of certain death.

足下冒出生入死的大险来送信，
You have risked your life to bring this message.

这份云天高义，不独我王某人一个人，杭州全城的文武军民，无不感激。
For such boundless generosity, it is not only I who thank you, but every civil and military official, every soldier, and every citizen of Hangzhou.

萧义士——』他一面说，一面颤巍巍地起身，
Mr Xiao—” He struggled shakily to his feet.

『请受我一拜！』
“Allow me to bow to you.”

『不敢当，不敢当！』
“I could not accept it!”

萧家骥慌忙扶住∶『王大人，这是我义不容辞的事。』一个坚辞，一个非要拜谢，
Xiao hurried to support him. “Governor Wang, this is a duty I could not in honor refuse.” One insisted on offering thanks, the other steadfastly declined the honor.

僵持了好一会，终于还是由王有龄的长子代父行礼；
After a long impasse, Wang’s eldest son finally made the obeisance on his father’s behalf.

萧家骥自然也很感动，
Xiao was deeply moved.

转念想到生离几乎等于死别，不由得热泪盈眶，喉头梗塞，
Reflecting that this parting in life was almost certainly a parting in death, he felt hot tears fill his eyes and a lump rise in his throat.

只说得一声∶『王大人，请保重！』扭头就走。
He managed only, “Take care of yourself, Governor Wang,” then turned away.


踉踉跄跄地出了中门，只听里面在喊∶『请回来，请回来！』
He had stumbled through the inner gate when he heard voices calling from within. “Please come back!”

请了萧家骥回去，王有龄另有一件大事相托；将他的『遗疏』交了给萧家骥∶『萧义士！』这一次王有龄的声音相当平静∶『请你交付雪岩保管。
Wang had another important trust to place in him. Recalled to the room, Xiao received the governor’s final memorial. This time Wang’s voice was quite calm. “Mr Xiao, please give this to Xueyan for safekeeping.

城在人在，城亡人亡，
While the city stands, I live; when it falls, I die.

只听说杭州失守，就是我毕命之日；
News that Hangzhou has fallen will mean that my life is over.

请雪岩拿我这道遗疏，面呈江苏薛抚台，请他代缮出奏。
Ask Xueyan to take this final memorial in person to Governor Xue of Jiangsu and request that he have it transcribed and submitted to the throne.

这件事关乎我一生的结果，
It concerns the final accounting of my entire life.

萧义士我重重拜托了。』
Mr Xiao, I entrust it to you with all my heart.”

见他是如此肃穆郑重的神情，萧家骥不敢怠慢，
Faced with such grave solemnity, Xiao dared show no hesitation.

重重地应一声∶『是！』
“I shall,” he answered firmly.

然后将那道遗疏的稿子折成四叠，放入贴肉小褂子的口袋中；
He folded the draft into four and placed it in the pocket of the short undergarment next to his skin.

深怕没有放得妥当会遗失，还用手在衣服外面按了两下。
Anxious lest it slip out and be lost, he pressed it twice through his outer clothes to make sure it was secure.


『喔，还有句话要交代，
“Oh, one more instruction.

这道遗疏请用我跟瑞将军两个人的衔名出奏。』
Please have this final memorial submitted under the names and titles of both General Rui and myself.

王有龄又说∶『我跟瑞将军已经约好了，一起殉节，
The general and I have agreed to die for our duty together.

决不独生。』
Neither will survive the other.”

听他侃侃而谈，真有视死如归的气概；萧家骥内心的敬意，掩没了悲伤，
He spoke so calmly and firmly, with such readiness to meet death, that the respect Xiao felt overwhelmed his sorrow.

从容拜辞，
He took his leave with composure.

『王大人，』他说，『我决不负王大人的付托。
“Governor Wang, I shall not fail your trust.

但愿这个稿子永远存在胡先生手里！』
May this draft remain forever in Mr Hu’s keeping!”

『但愿如此！』王有龄用低微但很清晰的声音说∶『再请你转告雪岩，千万不必为我伤心。』
“May it be so,” Wang said in a low but perfectly clear voice. “And tell Xueyan again that he must not grieve for me.”
