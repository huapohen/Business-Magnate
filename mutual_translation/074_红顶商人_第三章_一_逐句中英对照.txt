The Red-Topped Merchant

第三章
CHAPTER THREE


胡雪岩岂有不伤心之理？
How could Hu Xueyan not grieve?

接到王有龄的遗疏，他的眼圈就红了；
His eyes reddened as soon as he received Wang Youling’s final memorial.

而最伤心的，则是王有龄已绝了希望。
What hurt most was the knowledge that Wang had abandoned hope.

他可以想象得到，王有龄原来一心所盼的是粮船，只怕胡雪岩不能顺利到达上海；到了上海办来粮食，又怕不能冲破沿途的难关到达杭州。
Hu could imagine it all: Wang had pinned his hopes on the grain boats, first fearing that Hu might not reach Shanghai safely, then that, even if he obtained the grain there, he might fail to overcome the obstacles on the journey back to Hangzhou.

哪知千辛万苦，将粮运到了，却是可望而不可即，
Who could have foreseen that, after all those hardships, the food would arrive only to remain within sight but beyond reach?

从此再无指望，
There was nothing left to hope for.

一线希望消失，就是一线生机断绝∶『哀莫大于心死』，
With that last thread of hope severed, the last thread of life had snapped. No sorrow is greater than a deadened heart.

王有龄的心化为成冰，有生之日，待死之时，
Wang’s heart had turned to ice; the days left to him were merely a wait for death.

做人到此绝境，千古所无，千古所悲。
Such an extremity of human suffering seemed without equal, a grief to be mourned for all time.

然而胡雪岩却不能不从无希望中去找希望，
Yet Hu had to search for hope in hopelessness.

希望在这三天中发生奇迹。
Perhaps a miracle would occur within these three days.

这是个飘渺的希望；但就悬此飘渺的希望亦似乎不易——
It was a tenuous hope, and even to cling to it seemed difficult.

形势在一夜之间险恶了；
Overnight, the situation had grown more threatening.

长毛一船一船在周围盘旋，位置正在枪弹所够不到的地方；
Boat after boat of rebels circled them, keeping just beyond gunshot.

其意何居，不言可知。
Their intention needed no explanation.


因此，护送的洋兵，已在不断催促，早作了结。
The foreign escort kept urging them to settle the matter and leave.

『要请他们等三天，只怕很难。』李得隆说，
“It will be difficult to persuade them to wait three days,” Li Delong said.

『派去的人没有回来，总要有了确实信息再说；
“While the man we sent had not returned, we could argue that we must wait for definite news.

这句话在道理上，他们就不愿也没奈何。
Whether they liked it or not, they could hardly dispute that.

现在家骥回来了，
Now Jiaji is back.

刚才一谈杭州的情形，大家也都知道了。
After what was just said about Hangzhou, everyone knows the situation.

没有指望的事，白白等在这里冒极大的危险，他们不肯的。』
They will not stay here in great danger, waiting uselessly for something hopeless.”

『无论如何要他们答应。
“They must agree, whatever it takes.

来了一趟，就此回去，于心不甘。
Having come all this way, I cannot bear to turn back just like that.

再说，有危险也不过三天；
Besides, it is only three more days of danger.

多大的危险也冒过了，
We have already faced far greater risks.

何在乎这三天？』
What are three days more?”

『那就早跟他们说明白。』
“Then we should tell them promptly.

李得隆说，『沙船帮看样子也不大肯。』
The Sea-Junk Guild does not look very willing either.”

『只要洋兵肯了，他们有人保护，自然没有话说。
“Once the foreign soldiers agree, the boatmen will have protection and nothing more to say.

这件事要分两方面做，
We must approach this from two directions.

重赏之下，必有勇夫。』
A generous reward will always find brave men.

胡雪岩说∶『请你们两位跟联络的人去说∶我有两个办法，随他们挑——。』
Ask the liaison officer to pass this on: I have two proposals, and they may choose between them.”

胡雪岩盘算着，两个办法够不够；是不是还有第三条兼筹并顾的路；
Hu considered whether two proposals were enough—whether there might be a third course that took everyone’s interests into account.

想了半天，只有两个办法。
After much thought, he could find only the two.


『第一个办法，
“The first is this.

如果城里能够杀出一条血路，请他们帮忙打，
If the troops in the city can fight their way out, I ask the escort to help them.

王抚台犒赏的两万银子，我一到上海就付；另外我再送一万。
The twenty thousand taels Governor Wang has offered as a reward will be paid as soon as I reach Shanghai, and I shall add ten thousand of my own.

如果有阵亡受伤的，抚恤照他们的营规加一倍。
For anyone killed or wounded, I shall pay twice the compensation laid down in their regulations.

这样等过实足三昼夜，
They are to wait three full days and nights on these terms.

如果没有动静，开船到宁波，我送三千银子。』
If nothing happens, we sail for Ningbo, and I give them three thousand taels.”

『这算得重赏了。
“That is a generous reward.

他们卖命也卖得过。』李得隆又问∶『不过人心不同，
It would be worth risking their lives for,” Li said. “But men differ.

万一他们不肯，非要开船不可呢？』
Suppose they refuse and insist on sailing?”

『那就是我的第二个办法，他们先拿我推在钱塘江里再开船。』
“Then there is my second proposal: let them push me into the Qiantang River before they sail.”

胡雪岩说这话时，脸色白得一丝血色都没有；
As he spoke, Hu’s face was utterly bloodless.

李得隆、萧家骥悚然动容，相互看看，久久无语。
Li and Xiao stared at one another in dismay and remained silent for a long time.

『不是我吓他们！
“I am not trying to frighten them.

我从不说瞎话，
I never make empty statements.

如果仁义义尽他们还不肯答应，你们想想，我除死路以外，还有什么路好走？』
If, after I have done everything that loyalty and friendship require, they still refuse, tell me what course remains to me except death.”

由于胡雪岩不惜以身相殉的坚决态度，一方面感动了洋兵；一方面也吓倒了洋兵，
His determination to sacrifice himself both moved the foreign soldiers and alarmed them.

但通过联络官提出一个条件，要求胡雪岩说话算话，
Through the liaison officer, however, they imposed one condition: he must keep his word.

到了三天一过，不要再出花样，拖延不走。
Once the three days were over, he must devise no further excuse for delay.

『「尽人事而听天命。』胡雪岩说，
“We do what we can, then submit to Heaven’s decree,” Hu said.

『留这三天是尽尽人事而已；
“These three days are only to make sure that we have done all that lies in our power.

我亦晓得没用的。』
I know they will be of no use.”


话虽如此，胡雪岩却是废寝忘食，一心以为鸿鹄之将至，
For all his words, Hu forgot both food and sleep, fixing his entire mind on the deliverance he expected.

日日夜夜在船头上凝望。
Day and night he stood at the bow and gazed into the distance.

江湖呜咽，虽淹没了他的吞声的饮泣；但江风如剪，冬宵寒重，引发了他的剧烈的咳嗽，却是连船舱中都听得见的。
The moaning river drowned his stifled sobs, but its cutting wind and the bitter winter nights brought on such violent coughing that it could be heard even inside the cabins.

『胡先生，』萧家骥劝他，『王抚台的生死大事，都在你身上，
“Mr Hu,” Xiao urged him, “Governor Wang’s affairs, in life and death, depend on you.

还有府上一家，都在盼望。
Your own family is waiting for you too.

千金之躯，岂可以这样不知道爱惜？』
Your life is too precious for you to neglect yourself like this.”

晚辈而有责备之词，情意格外殷切；
The reproach, coming from a younger man, made his concern all the more touching.

胡雪岩不能不听劝。
Hu had to yield.

但睡在铺上，却只是竖起了耳朵，
Yet even lying in his berth, he strained his ears.

偶尔听得巡逻的洋兵一声枪响，都要出去看了明白。
Whenever a patrolling foreign soldier fired a shot, he would insist on going out to see what had happened.

纵然度日如年，三天到底还是过去了；
Though every day seemed a year, the three days finally passed.

洋人做事，丝毫没有通融，
The foreigners allowed no latitude.

到了实足三昼夜届满，正是晚上八点钟，却非开船不可。
When the full three days and nights expired, at eight in the evening, they insisted on sailing.

胡雪岩无奈，
Hu could do nothing.

望北拜了几拜，权当生奠。
Turning north, he bowed several times, offering what was, in effect, a farewell tribute to a man still alive.

然后痛哭失声而去。
Then they departed, and he broke into uncontrollable weeping.


到了甬江口的镇海附近，才知道太平军黄呈忠和范汝增，从慈溪和奉化分道进攻，宁波已经在两天前的十一月初八失守。
Only when they reached the vicinity of Zhenhai, at the mouth of the Yong River, did they learn that the Taiping commanders Huang Chengzhong and Fan Ruzeng had advanced from Cixi and Fenghua respectively, and that Ningbo had fallen two days earlier, on the eighth day of the eleventh month.

不过宁波有租界，有英美领事和英法军舰；
Ningbo, however, had a foreign settlement, British and American consuls, and British and French warships.

而且英美领事，已经划定『外人居住通商区域』，正跟黄呈忠和范汝增在谈判，不准太平军侵犯。
The two consuls had marked out an area for foreign residence and commerce and were negotiating with Huang and Fan to keep the Taiping troops from entering it.

『那怎么办？』胡雪岩有气无力地说，
“What do we do now?” Hu asked weakly.

『我们回上海？』
“Return to Shanghai?”

『哪有这个道理？
“There is no reason to do that.

胡先生，你精神不好，
You are exhausted, Mr Hu.

这件事变给我来办。』
Leave this to me.”

于是萧家骥雇一只小船，驶近一艘英国军舰，
Xiao hired a small boat and approached a British warship.

隔船相语，军舰上准他登船，同时见到了舰长考白脱。
After an exchange between the vessels, he was allowed aboard and met its captain, Kaobaituo.

他的来意要跟杨坊开在宁波的商号联络；要求军舰派人护送。
He explained that he wished to contact Yang Fang’s Ningbo firm and asked for an escort from the warship.

同时说明，有大批粮食可以接济宁波。
He also told the captain that they had a large quantity of grain with which to supply Ningbo.

这是非常受欢迎的一件事，
The news was warmly welcomed.

『在「中立区」避难的华人，有七万之多，
“There are seventy thousand Chinese refugees in the neutral zone.

粮食供应，成为绝大的问题；
Food supplies have become an enormous problem.

你和你的粮食来得正是时候。
You and your grain have arrived at just the right moment.

不过，我非常抱歉，』考白脱耸耸肩说∶『眼前我还没有办法达成你的意愿。
But I am very sorry,” the captain added with a shrug. “At present I cannot do what you ask.

你是不是可以在我船是上住两三天？』
Could you stay aboard my ship for two or three days?”


『为什么？』
“Why?”

『领事团正在跟占领军谈判。
“The consular body is negotiating with the occupying forces.

希望占领军不侵犯中立区，同时应该维持市百。
We want them to respect the neutral zone and keep the markets functioning.

等谈判完成，你的粮食可以公开进口；
Once an agreement is reached, your grain can be brought in openly.

但在目前，我们需要遵守约定，
For the moment, however, we must abide by the existing understanding.

不能保护任何中国人上岸。』
We cannot escort any Chinese person ashore.”

『那末，是不是可以为我送一封信呢？』
“Could you send a letter for me, then?”

考白脱想了想答道∶『可以你写一封信，我请领事馆代送。
The captain considered. “Yes. Write a letter, and I shall ask the consulate to have it delivered.

同时我要把这个好消息告诉我们的领事。』
I must also tell our consul this good news.”

萧家骥如言照办。
Xiao did as he suggested.

考白脱的处置也异常明快，派一名低级军官，立即坐小艇登岸送信；
The captain acted promptly and efficiently, sending a junior officer ashore in a small boat with the letter.

同时命令他去谒见英国驻宁波的领事夏福礼，报告有大批粮食运到的好消息。
At the same time, he ordered him to call on the British consul at Ningbo, Xiafuli, and report the welcome arrival of a large consignment of grain.


为了等待复信，萧家骥很想接受考白脱的邀请，在他的军舰上住了下来但又不放心自己的船，
While waiting for a reply, Xiao was tempted to accept the captain’s invitation to stay aboard. Yet he was uneasy about his own vessels.

虽说船上有数十名洋兵保护，倘或与太平军发生冲突，麻烦甚大。
Though several dozen foreign soldiers guarded them, a clash with the Taiping forces would cause serious trouble.

如果跟考白脱要一面英国国旗一挂，倒是绝好的安全保障，
If he could ask for a British flag to fly, that would provide excellent protection.

却又怕属于美国籍华尔的部下，认为侮辱而拒绝。
But he feared that the men serving under the American Ward might regard this as an insult and refuse.

左思右想，只有先回船守着再说。
After turning the problem over, he could see no alternative but to return and keep watch himself.

乃至起身告辞时，考白脱正好接到报告，知道有华尔的兵在，
Just as he rose to leave, the captain received a report informing him that Ward’s men were present.

愿意取得联络，请萧家骥居间介绍。
He wished to establish contact and asked Xiao to introduce them.

这一来无形中解消了他的难题，
This unexpectedly solved Xiao’s difficulty.

喜出望外，连声许诺。
Delighted, he readily agreed.

于是由军舰上放下一条救生艇，陪着一名英国军官回到自己船上；
A lifeboat was lowered from the warship, and he accompanied a British officer back to his own vessel.

洋兵跟洋兵打交道的结果，华尔的部下接受了英国的建议，
After the soldiers had conferred, Ward’s men accepted the British suggestion.

粮船悬挂英国国旗，置于考白脱的保护之下。
The grain boats hoisted British flags and came under the captain’s protection.

到这地步，算是真正安全了。
Now they were truly safe.

萧家骥自觉这场交涉办得异常得意，兴冲冲要告诉胡雪岩。
Proud of how well he had managed the negotiations, Xiao hurried off to tell Hu.

到了舱里一看，只见胡雪岩神色委顿异常，面色难看得很。
But when he entered the cabin, he found him looking utterly drained, with a dreadful colour in his face.


『胡先生，』他大惊问说，
“Mr Hu!” he exclaimed in alarm.

『你怎么了？』
“What is wrong?”

『我要病了。』
“I am going to be ill.”

萧家骥探手去摸他的额头，
Xiao laid a hand on his forehead.

其烫无比，
It was burning hot.

『已经病了！』他说，
“You are ill already.

『赶快躺下来。』
Lie down at once.”

这一躺下就起不来了。
Once Hu lay down, he could not rise again.

烧得不断谵语，不是喊『雪公』就是喊『娘』；
He burned with fever and rambled continually, calling now for Xuexuan, now for his mother.

病中神志不清，只记得已到了岸上，却不知卧疾何处？
In his delirium, he knew only that he had been taken ashore; where he was lying ill, he had no idea.

有一天半夜里醒过来，只见灯下坐着一个人，且是女人；
One night he woke at midnight and saw someone sitting by a lamp—a woman.

背影苗条，似乎很熟，却一时再也想不起来是谁？
The slender figure, seen from behind, seemed familiar, yet for the moment he could not remember who she was.

『我在做梦？』
“Am I dreaming?”


虽是低声自语，自也惊动了灯下的人，
Though he had only murmured to himself, the woman heard.

她旋转身来，扭亮了洋灯；让胡雪岩看清了她的脸——
She turned and raised the lamp’s flame so that he could see her face.

这下真的象做梦了；
Now it truly seemed a dream.

连喊都喊不出来！
He could scarcely find his voice.

『你，你跟阿巧好象！』
“You—you look so like A’qiao!”

『我就是阿巧！』
“I am A’qiao.”

她抹一抹眼泪强笑着，
She wiped away a tear and forced a smile.

『没有想到是我吧？』
“You never thought it would be me, did you?”

胡雪岩不答，强自抬起身子；
Without answering, Hu struggled to sit up.

力弱不胜，摇摇欲倒，
He was too weak and swayed as though about to fall.

阿巧赶紧上来扶住了他。
A’qiao hurried forward to support him.

『你要做啥？
“What do you want?

是不是要茶水？』
Some tea?”

『不是！』胡雪岩吃力地说，
“No,” he said with difficulty.

『我要看看，我是不是在做梦？
“I want to see whether I am dreaming.

这是哪里；
Where is this?

你是不是真的阿巧？』
Are you really A’qiao?”

『是啊！我是真的阿巧。
“Yes, I really am.

我是特为来看你的；
I came especially to see you.

你躺下来，
Lie down.

有话慢慢说。』
We can talk slowly.”

话太多了，无从说起；
There was so much to ask that he did not know where to begin.

其实是头上昏昏沉沉地，连想都无从想起。
In truth, his head was too clouded even to think.

胡雪岩只好躺了下来，仰脸望望帐顶，又侧脸望望阿巧，
He could only lie back, look up at the canopy, then turn his eyes towards A’qiao.

先要弄清楚从得病到此刻的情形。
First he must discover what had happened between the onset of his illness and this moment.


『人泥？』他没头没脑地问。
“Where is he?” he asked without explanation.

『你是说那位萧少爷？』
“Do you mean young Mr Xiao?

阿巧答道，『他睡在外房。』
He is sleeping in the outer room.”

在外房的萧家骥，已经听见声音，
Xiao had already heard their voices.

急急披衣起床来探视，
Hastily throwing on his clothes, he came to the bedside.

只见胡雪岩虽然形容憔悴，但眼中已有清明的神色，
Hu was haggard, but his eyes had regained a clear look.

便又惊又喜地问道∶『胡先生，你认不认得我？』
Surprised and delighted, Xiao asked, “Mr Hu, do you recognize me?”

『你？』胡雪岩不解地问∶『你不是家骥吗？』
“You?” Hu replied in bewilderment. “Are you not Jiaji?”

『这位太太呢？』
“And this lady?”

『她是何姨太太。』
“She is Madam He.

胡雪岩反问一句∶『你问这些做啥？
Why are you asking me these things?

倒象我连人都认不得似的。』
You make it sound as though I could not recognize anyone.”

『是啊！』萧家骥欣慰地笑道∶『前几天胡先生你真的不认得人。
“Exactly!” Xiao said, smiling with relief. “For the past few days, you really did not recognize people.

这场湿温的来势真凶，
This damp-fever illness came on terribly fiercely.

现在总算「扳」回来了。』
At last you have turned the corner.”

『这么厉害！』
“As bad as that?”

胡雪岩自己都有些不信，
Hu could scarcely believe it.

咽着气说∶『我自己都想不到。
“I had no idea,” he said, catching his breath.

几天了？』
“How many days?”

『八天了。』
“Eight.”

『这是哪里？』
“Where are we?”

『在英国租界上；杨老板号子里。』
“In the British settlement, at Mr Yang’s firm.

萧家骥说，『胡先生你虚极了，
You are extremely weak.

不要多说话；
Do not talk too much.

先吃点粥，再吃药。睡过一觉，
Have some rice gruel, then your medicine, and sleep.

明天有了精神，听我们细细告诉你。』
When you feel stronger tomorrow, we shall tell you everything.”

这『我们』很明显地包括了阿巧姐，所以她接口说道∶『萧少爷的话不错，
His “we” plainly included A’qiao, who added, “Young Mr Xiao is right.

你先养病要紧。』
The first thing is to get well.”

『不要紧。』
“It does not matter.

胡雪岩说，『我什么情形都不知道，心里闷得很。
I know nothing of what has happened, and it weighs on me.

杭州怎么样？』
What news of Hangzhou?”


『没有消息。』
“None.”

胡雪岩转脸想问阿巧姐时；她正站起身来，
Hu turned towards A’qiao to question her, but she was already rising.

一面向外走，一面说道∶『我去热粥。』
“I shall warm the gruel,” she said, going out.

望着那依然袅袅婷婷的背影，再看到萧家骥似笑非笑，有意要装得不在意的诡秘神情，胡雪岩仍有相逢在梦中的感觉，
Watching her still graceful figure recede, then noticing Xiao’s curious half-smile and studied air of indifference, Hu again felt as though this meeting had taken place in a dream.

低声向萧家骥问道∶『她是怎么来的？』
“How did she come here?” he asked quietly.

『昨天到的。』萧家骥答道∶『一到就来找我——
“She arrived yesterday and came straight to me.

我在师娘那里见过她一次，所以认得。
I had met her once at my master’s house, with his wife, so I knew her.

她说，她是听说胡先生病重，特为赶来服侍的；
She said she had heard you were seriously ill and had hurried here especially to nurse you.

要住在这里。
She wanted to stay.

这件事师娘是知道的，我不能不留她。』
My master’s wife knew about it, so I could hardly refuse.”

胡雪岩听得这话，木然半晌，方始皱眉说道∶『你的话我不懂；
Hu stared blankly for a while, then frowned. “I do not understand.

想起来头痛。
Trying to think makes my head ache.

怎么会有这种事？』
How could such a thing happen?”

『难怪胡先生。
“No wonder you are puzzled.

说来话长，我亦不太清楚；
It is a long story, and I do not fully understand it myself.

据她说，她看师娘，正好师娘接到我的来信，
She says she was visiting my master’s wife just as my letter arrived.

听说胡先生病很重，她要赶来服侍。
When she heard how ill you were, she wanted to come and nurse you.

师娘当然赞成；请师父安排，
Naturally, my master’s wife approved and asked my master to make the arrangements.

派了一个人护送，坐英国轮船来的。』
He sent someone to escort her on a British steamer.”

『奇怪啊！』胡雪岩说∶『她姓人可何，我姓古月胡；
“Strange!” Hu said. “She belongs to the He family, and I am a Hu.

何家的姨太太怎么来服侍我这个病人。』
Why should a concubine of the He household come to nurse me?”

『那还用说？
“Surely that is obvious.

当然是在何家下堂了。』萧家骥说，
She must have left the He household.

『这是看都看得出来的，
Anyone can see that.

不过她不好意思说，我也不好意思打听。
But she was embarrassed to explain, and I was embarrassed to ask.

回头胡先生你自己问她就明白了。』
You can question her yourself presently.”

这一下，大致算是了解了来龙去脉。
Hu now understood the broad outline.

他心里在想，阿巧姐总不会是私奔；否则古应春夫妇不致派人护送她到宁波。
A’qiao could hardly have run away in secret, he thought, or Gu and his wife would not have sent someone to escort her to Ningbo.

但是——。
Yet—


『但是，她的话靠得住靠不住？
“But can we trust what she says?

何以知道她是你师娘赞成她来的？』
How do you know your master’s wife approved of her coming?”

『不错！
“There is no doubt about it.

护送的人，就是我师父号子里的出店老司务老黄。』
Her escort was Old Huang, the senior outside clerk at my master’s firm.”

胡雪岩放心了。
Hu was reassured.

老黄又叫『宁波老黄』，他也知道这个人。
He too knew the man, also called Old Huang of Ningbo.

胡雪岩还想再细问一番，听得脚步声，
He was about to ask more when he heard footsteps.

便住口不语，望着房门口；
Falling silent, he looked towards the doorway.

门帘掀动，先望见的是阿巧姐的背影，
The curtain stirred, and A’qiao’s back appeared first.

她端着托盘，腾不出手来打门帘，所以是侧着进来。
Carrying a tray, she had no free hand to lift the curtain and had slipped in sideways.


于是萧家骥帮着将一张炕儿横搁在床中间，端来托盘，
Xiao helped place a small table across the bed and took the tray from her.

里面是一罐香粳米粥，四碟清淡而精致的小菜，
It held a pot of fragrant rice gruel and four small dishes, light but delicately prepared.

特别是一样糟蛋，为胡雪岩所酷嗜，
One was an egg preserved in wine lees, a particular favourite of Hu’s.

所以一见便觉得口中有了津液，腹中也辘辘作响了。
At the sight, his mouth watered and his stomach began to rumble.

『胡先生，』萧家骥特地说明这些食物的来源，『连煮粥的米都是何姨太从上海带来的。』
“Mr Hu,” Xiao said, making a point of explaining where the food came from, “even the rice for the gruel was brought from Shanghai by Madam He.”

『萧少爷，』阿巧姐接口说道∶『请你叫我阿巧好了。』
“Young Mr Xiao,” A’qiao interrupted, “please call me A’qiao.”

这更是已从何家下堂的明显表示。
This was a still clearer sign that she had left the He family.

本来叫『何姨太』就觉得刺耳，因而萧家骥欣然乐从；
“Madam He” had already begun to sound jarring, so Xiao was happy to comply.

不过为了尊敬胡雪岩，似乎不便直呼其名，只拿眼色向他征询意见。
Out of respect for Hu, however, he felt he should not simply call her by her personal name, and glanced at him for guidance.

『叫她阿巧姐吧。』
“Call her Sister A’qiao.”

『是。』
“Yes.”

萧家骥用亲切中显得庄重的声音叫一声∶『阿巧姐！』
With a warmth tempered by respect, Xiao addressed her: “Sister A’qiao!”

『嗯！』她居之不疑地应声，真象是个大姐姐似的，
“There!” she replied readily, quite like an elder sister.

『这才象一家人。』
“Now we sound like a family.”


这话在他、在胡雪岩都觉得不便作何表示。
Neither he nor Hu felt able to respond to that.

阿巧姐也不再往下多说，
A’qiao said no more.

只垂着眼替胡雪岩盛好了粥，
Lowering her eyes, she ladled out the gruel.

粥在冒热气，她便又嘬起滋润的嘴唇吃得不太烫了，方始放下；
It was steaming, so she pursed her moist lips and blew on it until it was cool enough, then set it down.

然后从腋下抽出白手绢，擦一擦那双牙筷，连粥碗一起送到胡雪岩面前，
Taking a white handkerchief from beneath her arm, she wiped a pair of ivory chopsticks and placed them before Hu with the bowl.

却又问道∶『要不要我来喂你？』
“Would you like me to feed you?” she asked.

这话提醒了萧家骥，有这样体贴的人在服伺，何必自己还站在这里碍眼，
This reminded Xiao that, with such an attentive person to care for Hu, there was no need for him to stand there in the way.

便微笑着悄悄走出去。
He smiled and quietly left.

四只眼睛都望着他的背影，直待消失，
Both Hu and A’qiao watched his retreating figure until it disappeared.

方始回眸，相视不语，征征地好一会，
Then they looked back at one another and remained silent for a long moment.

阿巧姐忽然眼圈一红，
Suddenly her eyes reddened.

急忙低下头去，顺手拿起手绢，装着擤鼻子去擦眼睛。
She quickly lowered her head, took up the handkerchief, and pretended to blow her nose while wiping away her tears.

胡雪岩也是万感交集，但不愿轻易有所询问；
Hu too was filled with conflicting emotions, but was reluctant to question her.

她的泪眼既畏见人，他也就装作不知，扶起筷子吃粥。
Since she did not want him to see her tears, he pretended not to notice and picked up his chopsticks to eat.


这一吃粥顾不得别的了。
Once he began, he could think of nothing else.

好几天粒米不曾进口，真是饿极了，唏哩呼噜地吃得好不有劲；
He had not eaten a grain of rice for days and was ravenous, slurping the gruel with extraordinary energy.

等他一碗吃完，阿巧已舀着一勺子在等了，
When the first bowl was empty, A’qiao was already waiting with a filled ladle.

一面替他添粥，一面高兴地笑道∶『赛过七月十五鬼门关里放出来的！』
As she served him more, she laughed happily. “Worse than a hungry ghost let out on the fifteenth of the seventh month!”

话虽如此，等他吃完第二碗，便不准他再吃；
For all her teasing, once he had finished the second bowl she would allow no more.

怕病势刚刚好转，饱食伤胃。
His illness had only just begun to recede, and overeating might upset his stomach.

而胡雪岩意有未厌，说好说歹才替他添了半碗。
Hu was not satisfied, however, and after much persuasion she gave him another half-bowl.

『唉！』放下筷子他感慨着说∶『我算是饱了！』
“Ah!” he sighed, laying down his chopsticks. “At least I have had my fill.”

阿巧姐知道他因何感慨。
A’qiao knew what lay behind the words.

杭州的情形，她亦深知，只是怕提起来惹他伤心，
She too understood Hangzhou’s plight, but feared that mentioning it would distress him.

所以不理他的话，管自己收拾碗筷走了出去。
Without replying, she gathered up the dishes and went out.

『阿巧，你不要走，
“A’qiao, do not go.

我们谈谈。』
Let us talk.”

『我马上就来。』
“I shall be right back.

她说，『你的药煎在那里，
Your medicine is simmering.

也该好了。』
It should be ready now.”

过不多久，将煎好了的药送来。服侍他吃完，劝他睡下；
Soon she brought in the decoction, helped him drink it, and urged him to sleep.

胡雪岩不肯，说精神很好，又说腿上的伤疤痒得难受。
Hu refused, saying that he felt quite alert and that the scar on his leg itched unbearably.

『这是好兆头。
“That is a good sign.

伤处在长新肉，人也在复原了。』她说，
New flesh is growing over the wound, and you are recovering.

『我替你洗洗脚，
Let me wash your feet.

人还会更舒服。』不说还好，一说胡雪岩觉得混身发痒，恨不得能在『大汤』中痛痛快快泡一泡才好——
You will feel more comfortable.” Before she said this he had scarcely noticed, but now he felt itchy all over and longed for a good soak in a public bath.

他也象扬州人那样，早就有『上午皮包水，下午水包皮』的习惯。
Like the people of Yangzhou, he had long enjoyed “water inside the skin in the morning, skin inside the water in the afternoon”—tea first, a bath later.

自从杭州吃紧以来，就没有泡过『澡塘』；
Since Hangzhou’s situation had grown critical, he had not visited a bathhouse.

这次到了上海，又因为腿上有伤，不能入浴。
On reaching Shanghai, his injured leg had again prevented him from bathing.

虽然借助于古家的男佣抹过一次身，从里到外换上七姑奶奶特喊裁缝为他现制的新衣服，
A male servant at Gu’s house had washed him once, and he had changed from head to foot into new clothes that the Seventh Lady had a tailor make specially.

但经过这一次海上出生入死的跋涉，担忧受惊的冷汗，出了干、干了出，不知几多次？
But on the hazardous voyage since then, cold sweat from worry and fear had broken out and dried, then broken out again, countless times.

满身垢腻，
His whole body was coated in grime.

很不舒服，实在想洗个澡，无奈万无劳动阿巧姐的道理。
He was deeply uncomfortable and longed to bathe, yet could hardly ask A’qiao to undertake such a task.
