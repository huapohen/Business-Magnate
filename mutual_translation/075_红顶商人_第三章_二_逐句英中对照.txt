胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第三章（本章第2部分，共4部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


While he was thinking this, she had already gone to put her words into action.
他心里这样在想，她却说到就做，已转身走了出去，

Somehow she found a brand-new wooden footbath on high legs.
不知哪里找到了一只簇新的高脚木盆，

She brought a kettle of hot water, poured it into the basin, and lifted the quilt to take hold of his feet.
提来一铫子的热水，冲到盆里；然后掀被来捉他的那双脚。

“No, no!”
『不要，不要！』

Hu drew them back.
胡雪岩往里一缩，

“I have not washed them since I boarded the boat in Shanghai.
『我这双脚从上海上船就没有洗过，

They are filthy.”
太脏了。』

“What does that matter?” she said without hesitation.
『怕什么？』阿巧姐毫不迟疑地，

“I have come all this way to nurse a sick man.
『我路远迢迢赶了来，就是来服侍病人的；

Nothing would make me happier than to see you recover properly.”
只要你好好复原，我比什么都高兴。』

Her words filled Hu with both gratitude and reflection.
这两句话在胡雪岩听来，感激与感慨交并。

Through war and chaos, he had narrowly escaped death.
兵荒马乱，九死一生；

Thinking of his missing family, the people of Hangzhou on the verge of starvation, and Wang Youling trapped in a hopeless position, moving step by step towards hell, he had often asked himself what people lived for.
想到下落不明的亲人，快要饿死的杭州一城百姓，以及困在绝境，眼看着往地狱里一步一步在走的王有龄，常常会自问∶人生在世，到底为的什么；

Was it merely to endure suffering worse than death?
就为了受这种生不如死的苦楚？

Now he felt differently.
现在却不同了，

Life held joy as well as sorrow.
人活在世界上，有苦也有乐；

Which one a person experienced depended on his own actions.
是苦是乐，全看自己的作为。

It was just as the Treatise of the Exalted One on Response and Retribution said: misfortune and happiness have no fixed gate; people bring them upon themselves.
真是『太上感应篇』上所说的∶『祸福无门，惟人自召』。

With this change of thought, his heart seemed to come back to life, like a withered tree touched by spring.
这样转关念头，自己觉得一颗心如枯木逢春般，又管用了。

His mind grew active too.
脑筋亦已灵活；

Where he had been too weary to think about anything, thoughts now came readily and quickly.
本来凡事都懒得去想，此刻却想得很多，想复很快。

Once A’qiao had washed his feet, he smiled. “A’qiao, since you have started a good deed, see it through.
等阿巧姐替他将脚洗好，便又笑道∶『阿巧，送佛送到西天，

Give my body a wipe as well.”
索性替我再抹一抹身子。』

“That would not be wise.
『这不大妥当。

You are weak and must not catch cold…”
你身子虚，受不得凉┅┅』


“It does not matter!”
『不要紧！』

Hu thrust out his withered arm and punched the air twice, trying to look vigorous. “I feel well enough to get up.”
胡雪岩将枯瘦的手臂伸出来，临空捣了两下，显得很有劲似地说∶『我自己觉得已经可以起床了。』

“Nonsense!
『瞎说！

Lie down properly.”
你替我好好睡下去。』

She tucked his feet and hands beneath the quilt, pressed him back onto the bed, and pulled the covering snugly around him.
她将他的脚和手都塞入被中，硬扶他睡倒，而且还掖紧了棉被。

“Truly, A’qiao, I am well again.”
『真的。阿巧，我已经好了。』

“How can you be?
『哪有这种事？

After an illness like this, you do not recover just because you say so.
这样一场病，哪里会说好就好？

Even an elixir could not work that fast.”
吃仙丹也没有这样灵法。』

“Good fortune lifts the spirits.
『人逢喜事精神爽，

You are my elixir.
你就是仙丹。

Once it arrives, every illness vanishes.”
仙丹一到，百病全消。』

“Hmph!”
『哼！』

Her lips curled slightly.
阿巧微微撇着嘴，

“You are good at sweet talk.
『你就会灌米汤。

Go to sleep.”
睡吧！』

With a slender finger she brushed his eyelids shut.
她用纤行一指，将他的眼皮抹上。

As soon as she turned away, his eyes opened again.
等她转身，他的眼又睁开了。

He stared at the canopy and thought.
望着帐顶想心事；

There were many things he wanted to know, but for the moment, A’qiao was the one person he wished to talk to.
要想知道的事很多，而眼前却只有阿巧好谈。


She did not return for a long time.
阿巧却好久不来；

Unable to restrain himself, he called out.
他忍不住喊出声来，

It was Xiao who answered.
而答应的却是萧家骥，

“Mr Hu, you must not tire yourself.
『胡先生，』他说，『你不宜过于劳神。

It is two in the morning.
此刻半夜两点钟了，

Please rest.”
请安置吧！』

“Where is A’qiao?
『阿巧呢？』

Where is she sleeping?”
胡雪岩问道∶『她睡在哪里？』

Large wholesale firms commonly provided guest rooms and beds, but they did not ordinarily accommodate women.
做批发生意的大商号，备有客房客铺，无足为奇，但从不招待堂客；

Some merchants and their customers even objected to a woman’s presence, believing that a menstruating woman might offend the God of Wealth worshipped on the premises.
有些商家的客户，甚至忌讳堂客，因为据说月事中的妇女会冲犯所供的财神。

Yang’s Ningbo firm, also called Daji, specialized in wholesaling seafood and sundries.
杨坊的这家招牌也叫『大记』，专营海鲜杂货批发的商号，

It was comparatively broad-minded and did not forbid women to come and go, but it had few private guest rooms.
虽然比较开通，不忌妇女出入，但单间的客房不多；

Xiao had therefore arranged for A’qiao to stay at a clerk’s house, where she had shared a bed with the man’s newly married wife for one night.
所以阿巧姐是由萧家骥代为安排，借住在大记的一个伙计家中，与此人的新婚妻子同榻睡了一夜。

“That will not do tonight.
『今天不行了，

It is the clerk’s turn to sleep at home, and he gets only one night in ten.
是轮到那伙计回家睡的日子；十天才有这么一天，

Sister A’qiao said, ‘Such a fresh, sweet young bride!
阿巧姐说∶』人家喷喷香、簇簇新的新娘子；

How could I keep the two of them apart?’
怎好耽误他们夫妻的恩爱？

The clerk was very considerate and insisted it did not matter, but she would not agree.”
「那伙计倒很会做人，一再说不要紧；是阿巧姐自己不肯。

“Then where will she sleep tonight?”
『』那末今天睡在哪里呢？

“There.”
『『喏，』

Xiao pointed to a door panel and two benches standing to one side. “I have everything ready to make her a temporary bed.
萧家骥指着置在一旁的一扇门板，两张条凳说∶『我已经预备好了，替她搭「起倒铺」。

Still—” He smiled and said no more.
不过——。』他笑笑没有再说下去。

Something knowing in his expression invited suspicion, and Hu naturally pressed him. “Still what?”
神情诡秘，令人起疑，胡雪岩当然要追问∶『不过什么？』

“This bed looks quite large.
『我看这张床蛮大，

Why not let her sleep at the foot of it?
不如让阿巧姐就睡在胡先生脚后头。』

She wants to stay in this room so that she can look after you.
萧家骥又说，『她要这里搭铺就为了服侍方便；

Would sharing the bed not be more convenient still?”
睡在一床上，不更加方便了吗？』

Hu could not tell whether he was serious or joking, nor did he know what A’qiao herself intended.
不知他是正经话，还是戏谑？也不知阿巧姐本人的意思究竟如何？

He could only smile without answering.
胡雪岩只有微笑不答。


In the end, Xiao did set up a temporary bed for her.
到最后，萧家骥还是替阿巧姐搭了『起倒铺』；

Naturally, she arranged her own mattress, quilt, and pillow.
被褥衾枕自然是她自己铺设。

Once she had given the patient his medicine and closed the door, Hu spoke.
等侍候病人服了药，关好房门，胡雪岩开口了。

“Your mattress is too thin, and you have no bed curtains.
『你的褥子太薄，又没有帐子，

You would do better to sleep on the inner side here.”
不知睡到我里床来！』

He patted the place beside him.
他拍拍身边。

A’qiao, who was removing her hair ornaments and make-up, said nothing.
正在卸妆的阿巧姐没有说话，

She brought her quilt over, but took the outer side so that she could get up easily.
抱衾相就；不过为了行动方便，睡的是外床——

Ningbo people took pride in their beds.
宁波人讲究床铺；

This one, carved from boxwood, was enormous, with more than enough room for two.
那张黄杨木雕花的床极大，两个人睡还绰绰有余。

An oil lamp stood on a shelf at the inner side, its flame turned low. Its faint light fell on her close-fitting, light lined jacket of pale green satin.
里床搁板上置一盏洋灯，——捻得小小的一点光照着她那个葱绿缎子的紧身小夹袄；

Looking at her, Hu once again felt that their reunion was a dream.
看在胡雪岩眼里，又起了相逢在梦中的感觉。

“A’qiao, surely it is time you told me what has happened to you?”
『阿巧！你该讲讲你的事了吧？』

“It is a long story,” she replied gently. “You have tired yourself tonight and have just taken your medicine. Sleep well.
『说来话长。』阿巧很温柔地说∶『你这半夜也累了；刚吃过药好好睡一觉。

We can talk tomorrow.”
明天再谈。』

“I feel very alert now.”
『我现在精神很好。』

“It is good that you do.
『精神好自然好。

But listen—the cocks are already crowing.
你听，』阿巧姐说，『鸡都在叫了。

These last hours of sleep before dawn matter most.
后半夜这一觉最要紧，

Go to sleep.
睡吧！

I am here now.
好在我人都来了，

What is there to hurry about?”
你还有什么好急的？』

There was a depth of meaning in this that gave Hu plenty to think about.
这句话的意思很深，足够胡雪岩想好半天。

But his illness had only just begun to recede, and his strength was limited.
到底病势初转，精神不够，

Soon he grew drowsy and slept until daylight.
很快地便觉得困倦，一觉睡到天亮。


When he woke, she woke too and hurried to get up and attend to him.
他醒她也醒了，急急要起床料理，

Hu wanted her to rest a little longer.
胡雪岩却愿她多睡一会；

Holding her back, he said, “It is too cold.
拖住她说∶『天太冷，

Do not get up yet.
不要起来。

Let us have a proper talk.”
我们好好谈谈。』

“About what?
『谈什么？』

I only want you to get well soon.
阿巧姐说，『但愿你早早复原；

Everything else can wait until we are back in Shanghai.”
回到上海再说。』

“I thought it over last night.
『我昨天晚上想过了，

If I come safely through this, I shall never be an official again.
只要这一次能平平安过去，我再也不做官了；

I shall mind my own business and be content if I can meet a few good friends regularly for a chat.”
安安分分做生意，能够跟几个好朋友常在一起叙叙，我就心满意足了。』

“You think only of your friends,” she said with a trace of reproach.
『你只晓得朋友！』阿巧姐是微带怨态的神情，

“You never make plans for yourself.”
『就不替自己打算打算。』

Plans for himself would naturally have to include her.
替他自己打算，当然也就要包括她在内。

The implication was delicate.
言外之意，相当微妙；

Hu showed no reaction, but calmly asked, “How did you leave the He household?
胡雪岩很沉着地不作表示，只是问说∶『你是怎么从何家出来的？

Can you tell me now?”
现在可以告诉我了吧！』

“Of course I must tell you.
『当然要告诉你的。

But you are always thinking of your friends.
不过你处处为朋友，

I am afraid it will distress you.”
听了只怕心里会难过。』

She was counting He Guiqing among Hu’s friends—and his situation was now pitiable indeed.
她的意思是将何桂清当作胡雪岩的朋友——这个朋友现在惨不可言。

One mistaken decision at Changzhou had brought dismissal and an order for his arrest and investigation.
只为在常州一念之差，落得个『革职拿问』的处分；

For two years the matter had dragged on, largely because Xue Huan had contrived excuses and delays on his behalf.
迁延两年，多靠薛焕替他支吾敷衍，

Yet he had suffered more than enough of a fugitive’s existence.
然而『逃犯』的况味也受够了。


“It was no life for a human being,” A’qiao sighed. “With his status so compromised, he could go nowhere.
『这种日子不是人过的。』阿巧姐喟叹着说∶『人嘛是个黑人，哪里都不能去；

Whenever a visitor was announced, we first had to find out exactly why he had come.
听说有客人来拜，先要打听清楚，来做什么？

Most frightening of all was a call from the Shanghai magistrate: we feared he might have come to make an arrest.
最怕上海县的县大老爷来拜；防是来捉人的。

I have learned the truth of that saying, ‘Do no wrong by day, and a midnight knock will not frighten you.’
「白天不做亏心事，半夜敲门心不惊」这句俗语，我算是领教过了，

It could not be truer.
真正一点不错。

If I felt like that, imagine how he felt himself.”
我都这样子，你想想本人心里的味道？』

“In his place, I should have steeled myself and surrendered.”
『叫我，就狠一狠心，自己去投案。』

“He often said the same.
『他也常这样说；

But saying it was all he could do; he could never bring himself to act.
不过说说而已，就是狠不下心来。

Now…”
现在——。』

Now even those days of constant apprehension were nearly over.
现在，连这种提心吊胆的日子也快不多了。

Since the late emperor’s death, the accession of a child sovereign, and the two empress dowagers’ assumption of government behind the curtain with Prince Gong in high favour, a new spirit prevailed at court.
从先帝驾崩，幼主嗣位，两宫太后垂帘听政，垂用恭王，朝中又是一番气象；

To restore morale, civil and military officials who had lost armies and dishonoured the state were to be dealt with severely.
为了激励士气，凡是丧师辱国的文武官员，都要严办。

Worst of all, Zeng Guofan had been appointed governor-general of Liangjiang and placed in command of military affairs in Jiangsu, Anhui, Jiangxi, and Zhejiang.
最不利的是，曾国藩调任两江都督，朝命统辖江苏、安徽、江西、浙江四省军务；

All officials in those four provinces, up to governors in the civil administration and commanders-in-chief in the military, were subject to his authority.
四省官员，文到巡抚，武到提督，悉归节制。

He Guiqing had sent intermediaries to ask for an opportunity to redeem himself through service.
何桂清曾经托人关说，希望能给他一个效力赎罪的机会，

The reply had been brief: willing to help, but unable to do so.
而得到的答复只有四个字∶『爱莫能助。』

“Half a month ago someone brought word that Mr Zeng had recommended an intendant named Li to lead troops to defend Shanghai.
『半个月以前，有人来说，曾大人保了个姓李的道台，领兵来守上海。

They say that as soon as he arrives he will take over from Governor Xue.
这位李道台，据说一到上海就要接薛抚台的手；

He is Mr Zeng’s pupil and will naturally obey his teacher.
他是曾大人的门生，自然听老师的话。

Governor Xue will no longer be able to help, however much he wishes.
薛抚台再想帮忙也帮不上了。

Because of that—” Because of that, He had to make his final arrangements. He settled his household affairs and dismissed his concubines.
为此之故——。』为此，何桂清不能不作一个最后的打算∶家事已作了处分，姬妆亦都遣散，

That was how A’qiao had left his household.
阿巧姐就是这样下堂的。


Remembering how well he had treated her, she had felt uneasy about leaving him at such a moment.
想想他待她不错，在这个时候，分袂而去，未免问心不安。

But he would not change his mind, and she had no choice but to obey.
无奈何桂清执意不回；她也就只好听从了。

“He must have tried to provide for the rest of your life before you left,” Hu said. “Though how much could he have had left?
『那天，他也总要为你的后半辈子打算打算。』胡雪岩说∶『不过，他剩下几个钱，

Two years of living on his capital must have brought him close to the end.”
这两年坐吃山空，恐怕所余已经无几。』

“Ordinary expenses did not amount to much.
『过日子倒用不了多少，

Most of it was swindled out of him.
都给人骗走了，

One man said he could work through influential contacts in the capital; another said a gift must be sent to someone or other.
这个说，可以替他到京走门路；那个说某某人那里送笔礼。

Who knows how much money went down those holes?
这种塞狗洞的钱，也不知道花了多少。』

Before I left, he said he would scrape together two thousand taels for me.
阿巧姐说，『临走以前，他跟我说，要凑两千银子给我。

I would not take it.”
我一定不要。』

“That was loyal of you.
『你倒也够义气。

But in times like these, to be frank, you are better off relying on yourself than on others.”
不过，这种乱世，说老实话∶求人不如求己。』

“I have not left myself entirely without provision.
『我也不是毫无打算的，

I entrusted a little chest to the Seventh Lady.
我有一只小箱子托七姑奶奶替我收着；

The things in it must be worth thirty to fifty thousand taels.
那里面一点东西，总值三、五万。

When we return to Shanghai, I shall give it to you.”
到了上海我交给你。』

“What for?
『交给我做什么？』

I have no heart for managing investments for you just now.”
胡雪岩问道∶『我现在还没心思来替你经营。』

A’qiao said nothing.
阿巧姐先不作声，

She blinked and bit a fingernail, as though considering something of great importance.
一面眨眼，一面咬指甲，仿佛有极要紧的事在思索似的。

Ever since Hu had bade his distant farewell to Wang across the Qiantang River, he had felt that every desire was extinguished.
胡雪岩是从钱塘江遥别王有龄的那一刻，便有万念俱灰之感，

He had neither wished nor been able to think about anything, and had sunk into illness.
什么事都不愿、也不能想，因此恹恹成病，

Though he was now out of danger and recovering quickly, the old listlessness remained.
如今病势虽已脱险，而且好得很快，但懒散如旧，

He did not care to guess her thoughts.
所以不愿去猜她的心事，

Turning his face towards her, he merely admired her at leisure, as he might contemplate a piece of ancient jade he loved.
只侧着脸象面对着他所喜爱的古玉似的，恣意鉴赏。


It had been six years since he had looked at her in this way.
算一算有六年没有这样看过她了。

Six years of war and separation was a long time.
离乱六年，是一段漫长的岁月，

How many people had vanished, their survival uncertain, their whereabouts unknown?
多少人生死茫茫，音信杳然，

How many had risen or fallen, their fortunes utterly changed?
多少人升沉浮降，荣枯异昔，

Yet A’qiao as she had been six years ago seemed only a night and a dream away.
而想到六年前的阿巧姐，只如隔了一夜做了个梦；

Her appearance then came vividly before his mind.
当时形容清晰地浮现在脑际，

Comparing the two, he saw what had changed and what had not.
两相比较，有变了的，也有不变的。

The most obvious change was in her figure: she was fuller now.
变得最明显的是全体态，此刻丰腴了些；

Before, she had been rather too slender, so the change had made her more beautiful.
当时本嫌纤瘦，所以这一变是变得更美了；

She was also more composed and worldly-wise.
也更深沉老练了。

What had not changed was the feeling in her eyes, as deep and pure as ever.
不变的是她这双眼中的情竟，依然那么深，那么纯；

It seemed that, in her mind, there was no one but Hu Xueyan—not even herself.
似乎她心目中除了一个胡雪岩以外，连她自己都不关心。

At that thought, a spark appeared among the cold ashes of his heart.
转念到此，他那颗心就象冷灰发现一粒火星；

It was the beginning of a rekindled fire, and he himself felt how precious it was.
这是火种复炽的开始，他自己都觉得珍贵得很。


Without quite realizing it, he reached for her hand.
于是他不自觉地伸手去握住她的手；

“I came close to death on this journey,” he said with feeling.
感慨地说∶『这趟我真是九死一生——

“I do not mean that I was timid or feared the dangers on the road.
不是怕路上有什么危险，胆子小；

I mean what happened to my heart.
是我的心境。

From Hangzhou to Ningbo, it grew utterly cold.
从杭州到宁波，一路上我的心冷透了；

I lay in bed all day asking why one person should care for another.
整天躺在床上在想，一个人为啥要跟另外一个人有感情？

Without affection, he could be himself and I could be myself, and I need never fret over him.
如果没有感情，他是他，我是我，用不着替他牵肠挂肚，

So I told myself that, once my mind was at peace again, I would be more distant towards everyone.”
所以我自己对自己说，将来等我心境平静了，对什么人都要冷淡些。』

He had said this in a single rush and had to pause for breath.
一口气说到这里，有些气喘，停了下来；

A’qiao did not realize that he had more to say.
阿巧姐不曾听出他的语气未完，

只当他借题发挥，顿时脸色大变。

『你这些话，』她问，『是不是特为说给我听的？』

『是的——。』说了这两个字，胡雪岩才发觉她的神情有异；

立刻明白她是误会了，赶紧又接了一句∶『这话我什么人面前都没说过；

意思仍然令人莫名其妙，但他急于解释误会的态度，她是看出来的，

心先放了一半，另一半要听他下一句话如何？

“Do not make me guess.
『你不要让我猜了！

你晓得的，赌心思，跟别人我还可以较量较量；在你面前差了一大截。』

Hu smiled.
胡雪岩笑了，

It was not a handsome smile.
笑容并不好年；

His wasted face made his mouth seem large, and the two pointed canine teeth looked almost like fangs.
人瘦显得口大，两颗虎牙看上去象獠牙。

但毕竟是高兴的笑容，阿巧姐还是乐意看到的。


“You still know just what to say.”
『你还是那样会说话。』

他正一正脸色说∶『我特为谈我的心境，是想告诉你的一句话；此刻我的想法变过了。』

“How?”
『怎么变法？』

“People must have feelings after all.
『人还是要有感情的。

Even if they suffer for them, even if they die for them—” Before he finished, a warm, soft hand covered his mouth. “With so many things you could say, why talk so recklessly?”
就为它受罪，为它死——。』一句话未完，一只又软又暖的手掩在他口上∶『什么话不好说；说这些没轻重的话！』

『好，不说，不说。

So long as you understand.” He asked, “You seemed to be thinking hard about something just now.
你懂我的意思就可以了。』胡雪岩问道∶『你刚才好象在想心事？

何妨跟我谈谈。』

“There is much to say.
『要谈的话很多。

But as things are, you have no heart to listen, and I have no heart to speak.
现在这样子，你没心思听，我也没心思说，

Nothing need be hurried.
一切都不必急，

等你病养好了再说。』

“I shall not be fully well for some time.
『我的病一时养不好的。

Fortunately—” He had meant to say that at least he would not die, but remembering her objection to the word, he stopped himself.
好在是——。』他想说『好在是死不了的』；只为她忌讳说『死』，所以猛然咽住；

After a pause he continued, “In a day or two, I mean to return to Shanghai.”
停了一下又说∶『一两天我就想回上海。』

“How can you?”
『那怎么行？』

“Why not?
『没有什么不行。

Here in Ningbo, news is slow and there is nothing to do.
在宁波，消息不灵，又没有事好做；

A healthy man would fall ill from sheer boredom.
好人都要闷出病来，

How can a sick one recover?”
怎么会养得好病？』


“It cannot be helped.
『那是没有办法的事。

You have only just begun to improve.
你刚刚才有点好，

If you travel in the bitterest winter cold, exposed to the sea wind, and your illness returns, there will be no one to help in the middle of the ocean, however loudly we call.
数九寒天冒海风上路，万一病势反复；在汪洋大海里，叫天天不应，叫地地不灵，

It would mean two lives lost.”
那就是两条人命。』

“Why two?”
『怎么呢？』

“Have you not thought?
『你不想想，

If anything happened to you, what could I do but throw myself into the sea?”
万一你有个三长两短，我除了跳海，还有什么路好走？』

Faced with such devotion, extending to life and death, Hu could no longer bear to oppose her.
是这样生死相共的情分，胡雪岩再也不忍拂她的意了。

Privately, however, he thought that with care over his diet and A’qiao’s attentive nursing, there would be no great risk.
但是，他自己想想，只要饮食当心，加上阿巧姐细心照料，实在无大关碍。

不过，若非医生同意，不但不能塞阿巧姐的嘴，只怕萧家骥也未见得答应。

He decided to ask Xiao to sound out the doctor privately.
因此，他决定嘱咐萧家骥私下向医生探问。

But he could never find an opportunity.
但始终找不到机会；

From the moment she got up, A’qiao scarcely left his side.
因为阿巧姐自起床以后，几乎就不曾离开过他——

Snow had begun to fall again, and Xiao suggested that she set up all her operations in the room.
天又下雪了，萧家骥劝她就在屋子里『做市』；

With a brazier burning brightly, she prepared medicine, cooked gruel, and made dishes there.
就着一只熊熊然的炭盆，煎药煮粥做菜，都在那间屋里。

Hu found the bustle lively and amusing.
胡雪岩倒觉得热闹有趣，

Using a Hangzhou saying, he teased her about conducting a grand religious ceremony inside a snail shell.
用杭州的谚语笑她是『螺蛳壳里做道场』；

But it meant that, although Xiao was right before him, there was no chance for a private word.
但也因此，虽萧家骥就在眼前，却无从说两句私话。


The time was not wholly wasted.
不过，也不算白耗功夫。

Helping A’qiao prepare the food, Xiao told Hu everything that had happened over the past few days.
萧家骥一面帮阿巧姐做『下手』，帮她料理饭食，一面将这几天的情形都告诉了胡雪岩。

Apparently the negotiations between Huang Chengzhong and Fan Ruzeng and the British consul, Xiafuli, had gone well.
据说黄呈忠、范汝增跟英国领事夏福礼的谈判很顺利，

The commanders had promised to do all they could to protect foreign residents.
答应尽力保护外侨；

Two rebel soldiers who had attacked a British missionary had been arrested and executed.
有两名长毛侵袭英国教士，已经抓来『正法』。

Proclamations had also been issued to reassure the population, allowing trade outside the four gates.
而且还布告安民，准老百姓在四门以外做生意；

Business in Ningbo had largely returned to normal.
宁波的市面，大致已经恢复了。

“Our rice made the difference.
『得力的是我们的那批米。

Food is the people’s first necessity.
民以食为天，

Once there is no panic over grain, it is easier for them to settle down,” Xiao said consolingly. “At least that should ease some of your regret, Mr Hu.”
粮食不起恐慌，人心就容易安定。』萧家骥劝慰似地说∶『胡先生，你也可以稍稍弥补遗憾了。』

“It is a good deed that earns unseen merit,” A’qiao added. “For that deed alone, the Bodhisattva will surely protect your mother, turn danger into safety, and bring her through her troubles.”
『这是阴功积德的好事。』阿巧姐接口说道∶『就看这件好事，老太太就一定会有菩萨保佑，逢凶化吉，遇难成祥。』

Hu remained silent.
胡雪岩不作声。

Joy and sorrow mingled in him, and he could find no words adequate to express what he felt.
一则以喜，一则以悲；没有什么适当的话好表达他的复杂的心情。


“There is something important I must ask you,” Xiao continued.
『有句要紧话要告诉胡先生，

“The people at Daji wanted to know how the rice was to be accounted for.
那笔米价，大记的人问我怎么算法？

Should they sell it and divide the proceeds with us, or take it over at an agreed price?
是卖了拆帐、还是作价给他们？

I told them to receive the rice first.
我说米先领了去，

The terms could only be settled after I had asked you.
怎样算法，要问了你才能定规；

If they would not agree to that, I had no authority to do otherwise, and the rice would have to return on the same boats.
如果他们不肯答应，我作不了主，米只好原船运回。

Daji accepted.
大记答应照我的办法；

Now I must ask you.
现在要问胡先生了。

In my view, sharing the proceeds would be the more advantageous arrangement.”
照我看，拆算比较合算！』

“No,” Hu answered firmly. “I do not want money.”
『不！』胡雪岩断然答道∶『我不要钱。』

What did he want, then?
那末要什么呢？

Rice—and vessels to carry it.
胡雪岩要的是米；要的是运粮的船，

Once Hangzhou was recaptured, he wanted them available within three days.
只等杭州一旦克复，三天以内就要。

His purpose was easily understood.
他的用意是很容易明白；

When the city was recovered from the rebels, the roads would surely be strewn with those who had starved, and the city full of distressed survivors.
等杭州从长毛手里夺了回来，必定饿殍载途，灾民满城，

What they would need was rice.
那时所需要的就是米。

“Why arrange it like that?” Xiao urged.
『何必这么做？』萧家骥劝他∶『胡先生，

“Speaking strictly as a businessman—and everyone admires your calculations—would this not leave your capital tied up and idle?
在商言商，你的算盘是大家佩服的，这样做法，不等于将本钱「搁煞」在那里。

Besides, the recapture of Hangzhou may be a long way off.”
而况杭州克复，遥遥无期。』

“Not necessarily.
『不见得。

Fortunes will turn,” Hu said with some agitation.
气运要转的。』胡雪岩显得有些激动，

“The rebels’ conduct is erratic and unreliable.
『长毛搞的这一套，翻覆无常，

I do not think they can last.
我看他们不会久了。

In three or five years they will be finished.”
三、五年的功夫，就要完蛋。』

“Three or five years is a long time.
『三、五年是多少辰光，

With interest compounding, one dan of rice can become two or three.
利上盘利，一担米变成两三担米；

Even for the sake of Hangzhou’s people, you ought to consider the calculation.”
你就为杭州百姓，也该盘算盘算。』

“That is true.”
『话不错！』

Hu grew calmer again.
胡雪岩又比较平静了，

“But I have my own reasons.
『我有我的想法，

First, I have not entirely given up hope.
第一、我始终没有绝望，

Reinforcements may arrive, and Hangzhou may not fall.
也许援兵会到，杭州城可以不破，

如果粮道可以打通，我立刻就要运米去接济，

那时候万一不凑手，岂不误了大事；

第二、倘或杭州真的失守，留着米在那里，等克复以后，随时可以启运——

这是一种自己安慰自己的希望；

说穿了，是自己骗自己，总算我对杭州也尽到心了。』

“That makes sense too.
『这也有道理，

I shall negotiate with Daji on those terms.”
我就跟大记去交涉。』
