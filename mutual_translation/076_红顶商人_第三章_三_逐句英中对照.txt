胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第三章（本章第3部分，共4部分）

“There is no hurry about that,” Hu said. “When does the doctor come?”
『这不忙。』胡雪岩问道∶『医生啥时光来？』

“Every day after lunch.”
『每天都是中饭以后。』

“Then let us eat early, so she has time to clear everything away.”
『那就早点吃饭；吃完了她好收拾。』

He turned to A’qiao.
胡雪岩又问阿巧姐，

“When the doctor comes, will you need to withdraw?”
『等会医生来了，你要不要回避？』

Women of the household did not receive male visitors, but doctors were an exception; she need not necessarily hide herself.
虽然女眷不见男客，但对医生却是例外，不一定要回避；

His question, however, implied that he wanted her to withdraw.
只是他问这句话，就有让她回避的意思，

Understanding perfectly, she gave the answer he desired. “I shall listen from behind the screen.”
阿巧姐当然明白，顺着他的心意答道∶『我在屏风后面听好了。』

Hu had deliberately asked because he knew she would offer to leave.
胡雪岩是知道她会回避，有意这样问她；

But listening behind a screen hardly amounted to leaving at all: he had lured the tiger aside without getting it off the mountain.
不过她藏在屏风后面听，调虎不能离山，在自己等于不回避，

He needed another plan.
还要另动脑筋。

That was simple enough.
这也简单得很，

First he asked Xiao to write a letter for him, keeping his hands occupied.
他先请萧家骥替他写信，占住了他的手；

Then he said he fancied some sweet soup and sent A’qiao to the kitchen for foreign sugar.
然后说想吃点甜汤，要阿巧姐到厨房里去要洋糖，

Once she was out of the way, he could speak privately.
这样将她调遣了开去，就可以跟萧家骥说私了。

“You need not write the letter, Jiaji.
『家骥，你信不必写了，

There is something I want to tell you.
我跟你说句话，

Come here.”
你过来。』

Xiao came to the bedside.
萧家骥走到床前，

“I have decided to return to Shanghai at once,” Hu said.
他说∶『我决定马上回上海，

“Speak to the doctor.
你跟医生说一说；

Whatever happens, I must go.”
我无论如何要走。』

“Why?” Xiao asked in surprise.
『为什么？』萧家骥诧异，

“What is the hurry?”
『何必这么急？』


“No particular reason.
『不为什么？

I simply must go.
我就是要走。

Only in Shanghai can I find out what is happening.” Hu continued, “My heart had gone completely cold.
到了上海，我才好打听消息。』胡雪岩又说，『本来我的心冷透了。

But after talking with A’qiao this morning, I feel very differently.
今天一早跟阿巧谈了半天，说实话，我的心境大不相同。

I have two things to do now.
我现在有两件事，

The first is to save Hangzhou.
第一件是救杭州，

However hopeless its condition, I shall treat even a dead horse as though it could still be saved.
不管它病入膏盲，我死马要当活马医。

The second is to attend to my business.
第二件，我要做我的生意；

In business one cannot afford to fall a single step behind.
做生意一步落不得后，

The sooner I reach a place where news travels freely, the better.
越早到消息灵通的地方越好。

Do you understand?”
你懂了吧？』

“I understand the second point, but not the first.
『第二点我懂，头一点我不懂。』

How are you going to save Hangzhou?”
萧家骥问道∶『你怎么救杭州？』

“There is no time to explain.”
『现在没法子细谈。』

Hu glanced anxiously towards the window.
胡雪岩有些张皇地望着窗外。

A slender figure had just passed outside; A’qiao was about to come in.
这是因为苗条一影，已从窗外闪过，阿巧姐快进来了。

Seizing the few moments left, he instructed Xiao to negotiate privately with the doctor and let her know nothing of it.
胡雪岩就把握这短短的片刻，告诫萧家骥跟医生私底下『情商』，不可让阿巧姐知道。

Xiao could not readily understand his purpose, but there was no time to ask.
是何用意，不易明了；但时机迫促，无从追问，

He could only do as he was told.
萧家骥只有依言行事。

By the time Hu had finished a bowl of longan-and-egg soup sweetened with foreign sugar and A’qiao had put everything in order, the doctor arrived.
等胡雪岩喝完一碗桂圆洋糖蛋汤，阿巧姐收拾好了一切，医生也就到了。


The physician enjoyed a considerable reputation, distinguished as much for his professional integrity as for his skill.
那医生颇负盛名，医道医德都高人一等。

Though Hu was still thin and weak, his eyes shone brightly.
见胡雪岩人虽瘦弱，双目炯炯有光，

The doctor was astonished: in a single night, more than half the illness seemed to have gone.
大为惊异，一夜之隔，病似乎去了一大半，

He acknowledged that he had seldom seen such a thing in forty years of practice.
他自承是行医四十年来罕见之事。

“That is naturally due to your skill, sir.”
『这自然是先生高明。』

Somewhat apologetically, Hu asked, “May I know your surname?”
胡雪岩歉意地问∶『先生贵姓？』

“Dr Zhang,” Xiao answered for him, adding a flattering introduction.
『张先生。』萧家骥一旁代答，顺便送上一顶高帽子，

“The foremost physician in Ningbo.
『宁波城里第一块牌子；

His is a family of scholarly doctors, seven generations in the profession.
七世祖传的儒医。

Dr Zhang himself holds a scholarly degree.”
张先生本人也是有功名的人。』

Presumably that meant he had qualified as a licentiate.
所谓『功名』，想起来是讲过学的秀才，

“I have been wanting in respect,” Hu said. “I have no degree at all.”
『失敬了！』胡雪岩说∶『我是白丁。』

“You are too modest, Your Excellency.
『胡大人太客气了。

Wherever one goes in this great world, a man entitled to the hat button of the third rank is above ten thousand others.”
四海之大，三品顶戴无论如何是万人之上。』

“A pity there is still more than one man above me!” Hu laughed loudly at his own expense.
『可惜不是一人之下。』胡雪岩自嘲着纵声大笑。


He laughed too suddenly, caught his breath the wrong way and began coughing violently.
笑得太急，呛了嗓子，咳得十分厉害；

Xiao hurried to pat his back, but the cough only grew worse.
萧家骥赶紧上去替他捶背，却是越咳越凶，

Dr Zhang too was helpless and could only wait for it to stop.
张医生亦是束手无策，坐等他咳停。

A’qiao became desperately anxious.
这一下急坏了阿巧姐；

She knew Hu’s complaint: his throat needed stroking, and honey water would quiet the cough.
她知道胡雪岩的毛病，要抹咽喉，喝蜜水才能将咳嗽止住；

Xiao was using the wrong method, so naturally it did no good.
萧家骥不得其法，自然无效。

Honey water could not be procured at once, but the other remedy was within reach.
蜜水一时无法张罗，另一点却是办得到，

“Mr Xiao,” she could not help calling from behind the screen, “tilt his head back and stroke his throat.”
『萧少爷，』她忍不住在屏风后面喊∶『拿他的头仰起来，抹抹喉咙。』

The voice was soft and sweet, with the gentle inflections of the Wu dialect.
是娇滴滴的吴侬软语，

Curious, Dr Zhang turned to look and asked directly, “Is there a lady here?”
张医生不免好奇，转脸张望；而且率直问道∶『有女眷在？』

A physician might ask anything without offence.
医生是什么话都可以问，不算失礼；

Xiao, however, found the question awkward.
但萧家骥却很难回答，

As he stroked Hu’s throat, he replied indistinctly, “Mm, yes.”
一面替胡雪岩抹着喉头，一面含含糊糊地答道∶『嗯，嗯，是！』

The doctor seemed about to say more, then stopped.
张医生欲语又止；

Once the coughing ceased, he took Hu’s pulse, inspected his tongue and asked carefully about his food, sleep and daily habits.
等胡雪岩咳停了才切脉看舌苔，仔细问了饮食起居的情形，

He then announced with satisfaction, “The illness is no longer dangerous.
欣慰地表示∶『病势已经不碍，

He needs only nursing and rest.
只须调养，

He should recover in about a fortnight.”
大概半个月以后可以复原。』


“Thank you, thank you!”
『多谢，多谢！』

Hu clasped his hands. “Jiaji, take Dr Zhang to your room to write the prescription.”
胡雪岩拱拱手说∶『家骥你陪张先生到你那里开方子去吧！』

Xiao understood.
萧家骥会意，

When the prescription was finished, he raised the question of Hu’s wish to return to Shanghai.
等开好方子，便谈到胡雪岩想回上海的话。

The doctor was deeply perplexed.
张医生深为困惑，

“It would be inadvisable even to move him to another bed.
『病人连移动床铺都是不相宜的。』

His grave illness has only just begun to improve.
他问，『大病刚有转机，

How can he act so rashly?”
何可这样子轻率冒失？』

“There really is important business in Shanghai that cannot be managed without him,” Xiao said. “The journey takes only a day or two.
『实在是在上海有非他到场不可的大事要办。』家骥说∶『路上也只有一两天的功夫，

If you would prescribe several doses to support his recovery, and we were especially careful in looking after him, surely he would come to no harm?”
请张先生多开几服调理药带去；格外当心照料，想来不碍。』

“Look after him?
『照料！

Who would do that?
那个照料？

If the illness returned, I should not be aboard.
万一病势翻覆，我又不在船上；

What would you do then?”
你们怎么办。』

“Quite so,” Xiao said.
『是！』萧家骥说，

“We shall have to abandon the idea.”
『那就只好算了。』

But Hu had sharp ears.
而间壁的胡雪岩耳朵尖，

Listening from the next room, he had already found an answer: invite Dr Zhang to make a professional visit to Shanghai and care for him during the voyage.
听了张医生的话，已经有了主意，请他到上海出诊，随船照料。


Once the doctor had taken his leave and entered his sedan chair, A’qiao no longer needed to remain hidden.
等张医生开好方子，告辞上轿，阿巧姐自然也不必回避了，

With Xiao present, Hu revealed his idea.
胡雪岩便当着萧家骥透露了他的意思。

It was not inherently impracticable.
这个想法亦未始不可行；

Wealthy households often paid handsomely to engage a doctor in this manner and sent special carriages or boats to fetch him.
富室巨户，多有这样重金礼聘，专用车船奉迎的，

But at this particular time and place, both A’qiao and Xiao thought it would be difficult.
但是眼前时地不同，阿巧姐和家骥都觉得不易办到。

“It would be ideal if he agreed,” Xiao said. “I only fear he will not. First, Ningbo is still unsettled.
『他肯去当然最好；就怕他不肯。』萧家骥说∶『第一、宁波的市面还不甚平靖，

He may be uneasy about leaving his home.
离家远行，恐怕不放心；

Second, the New Year is approaching.
第二、快过年了，

Ningbo people attach great importance to the family reunion.
宁波人的风俗，最重过年团圆，

Even those doing business elsewhere hurry home.
在外头做生意的，都要赶回家来，

Who sets off on a long journey then?”
哪里反倒有出远门的？』

“There is still time before New Year.
『过年还早，

I shall certainly send him back beforehand,” Hu replied. “It is for me to ask and for him to agree or refuse.
我一定赶年前送他回来。』胡雪岩又说∶『说不说在我，肯不肯在他；

Why not go and speak to him?”
你何妨去谈一谈。』

“Of course I can.
『那当然可以。

I must go to his Qingyi Hall to have the prescription made up anyway, so I can see him there.”
我本来要到他清仪堂去撮药；顺便就看他。』

“He owns a pharmacy too?” Hu said.
『原来他也开着药店？』胡雪岩说，

“Excellent!
『那太好了！

Even if he will not come to Shanghai, I should like to talk with him.”
就是他不肯到上海，我也想跟他谈谈。』

Everyone knew Hu wanted to open a pharmacy.
胡雪岩想开药店是大家知道的；

An idea occurred to Xiao, and he nodded. “You might well find much to discuss.”
萧家骥心中一动，点点头说∶『这倒或许会谈得投机。』

“That is another matter.
『那是另外一回事，

Jiaji, so long as he agrees to come, we shall accept whatever terms he names.
家骥，只要他肯去，他怎么说，我们怎么依他。

And appeal to his particular interests.
还有，要投其所好。

You understand?”
你懂我的意思吧？』

“I understand.”
『我懂，』

Xiao smiled.
萧家骥笑道，

“But I fear you must invite him here and speak to him yourself.”
『不过，恐怕要请了他来，你自己跟他谈。』

More than two hours later he returned.
去了一个多时辰，萧家骥回来了，

Dr Zhang had accepted an invitation to dinner, he reported, and was fond of calligraphy and painting.
说张医生答应来吃晚饭，又说他喜欢字画。

Asked about inviting him to accompany them as physician, Xiao said he had not yet found it convenient to raise the matter.
问到邀他同行照料的话，萧家骥表示还不便开口；

He added that A’qiao ought to make the request.
又说最好由阿巧姐来说，

It was an unreasonable favour to ask; a lady’s appeal would be more likely to succeed.
因为这是不情之请，只有女眷相求，容易成功。


“That is true.
『这话也是。

When a man speaks, his word must mean what it says.
男人说话，一句就是一句，

If he is rebuffed or has to climb down, his words will carry less weight afterwards.
碰了钉子或者打了折扣，以后说话就不值钱了。

A’qiao, will you ask him?”
阿巧，』胡雪岩问道∶『你肯不肯说？』

“I ought to refuse.
『本来是不肯说的，

So a woman’s word is worth nothing, and it does not matter if she is rebuffed or has to climb down?
女人的话就不值钱；碰钉子、打折扣都不要紧？

What treatment!
真正气数！

Still—” She deliberately assumed a helpless expression. “Ah, there is nothing for it.
不过——』她故意做个无可奈何的表情∶『唉！不说又不行；

I shall have to do the asking.”
只好我来出面了。』

That settled, they had to prepare dinner for their guest.
说停当了，要准备肴馔款客。

Hu thought ordering dishes from a restaurant would be more respectful.
胡雪岩认为不如到馆子里叫菜，比较郑重；

A’qiao welcomed the saving of trouble and readily agreed.
阿巧姐也想省事，自然赞成；

Xiao was less enthusiastic.
但萧家骥不甚同意，

He had something else to say to her, out of Hu’s hearing.
他肚子里另有一番话，要避着胡雪岩跟阿巧姐说。

“You need not trouble about these small matters, Mr Hu.
『胡先生，这些小事，你不必操心了，

I shall discuss them with A’qiao.
我要跟阿巧姐去商量。

Let me go with you to look at the kitchen first.”
阿巧姐，我陪你到他们厨房里看了再说。』

When they reached a secluded stretch of the corridor where Hu could not hear, Xiao stopped.
走到廊下僻处，估量着胡雪岩听不见了，他站住脚，

“A’qiao, do you really mean to help Mr Hu carry this through?”
要问她一句话。『阿巧姐，你是不是真的想帮胡先生办成功这件事？』

“Yes.
『是啊！

I did not approve at first, but if he insists, I can only do as he wishes.”
本来我不赞成的，不过他一定要这样做，我无论如何只有依他。』

“If you mean to do as he wishes whatever happens, there is something I must say.
『既然无论如何要依他，那末，我有句话说出来，

But you must not strike me.”
你可不能动手。』


“I shall not.
『不会的。

Tell me.”
你说好了。』

“Dr Zhang is very interested in you.
『姓张的很关心你。

I do not know how he discovered it, but he knows you as Madam He.
也不知道他怎么打听到的，晓得你姓何；

It was ‘Madam He’ this and ‘Madam He’ that; he never stopped asking.”
何姨太长，何姨太短，不停地问。』

Xiao paused to watch her face.
说到这里，萧家骥停下来看她的脸色。

Naturally, she did not look pleased.
她的脸色自然不会好看，

She flushed scarlet with anger. “A doctor like that!
气得满脸通红∶『这种郎中，

A heartless beast!
狼心狗肺；

He deserves to be cut into a thousand pieces!”
杀千刀！』

“There, you see?” Xiao said calmly. “I knew you would be angry.”
『是不是？』萧家骥很冷静地说∶『我知道你要动气。』

一句话提醒了阿巧姐，知道他还有未说出来的话；如果自己还是这样子，那些话就听不到了。

转念又想，总怪自己的身分尴尬，

Madam He appearing in a man named Hu’s quarters—people would hardly suppose her a respectable woman.
何姨太出现在姓胡的这里，在人家看，当然也不是什么好女人；

Since that was so, she might as well use a little crooked ingenuity herself.
既然如此，就不妨动动歪脑筋了。

As these thoughts passed through her mind, her expression softened.
这样转着念头，脸色自然就缓和了，

“Let him talk whatever nonsense he pleases.
『随他去胡说八道，

So long as I behave properly, that is enough.
只要我自己行得正，坐得正好了。』

Go on.”
她催促着，『你再说下去。』

“Mr Hu must leave, and he cannot leave unless Dr Zhang comes too.
『只为胡先生不走不可；要走，就非姓张的一起走不可。

So I had to try a little trick.
所以，我只好耍记花枪。

You are an understanding woman, A’qiao, and for Mr Hu’s sake I am sure you will not blame me.”
阿巧姐，你是明白人，又看在胡先生分上，一定不会怪我。』

This sounded ominous and rather alarmed her, though she kept her wariness to herself. Outwardly she took a different tone. “Of course not.
话风不妙，阿巧姐有些吃惊，不过戒心起在暗中∶表面上又是一种态度∶『不会，不会。

I know you are doing it for him.
我晓得你是为他。

Tell me, and we shall discuss it.”
你说出来商量。』

“I thought that if I simply asked him to accompany us to Shanghai, he would refuse.
『我在想，如果直言相谈，说请他一起陪到上海；他一定不会答应。

Once he had said no, we should be at an impasse.
这话等他一出口，事情就僵了；

So an idea came to me.
所以我灵机一动，

I said, ‘Madam He specially asked me to invite you.
说是∶』何姨太特为要我来奉请，

She will cook a couple of dishes herself this evening and hopes you will join her for wine.
晚上她亲手做两样菜，请张先生喝酒。

She begs the honour of your company.’
一定要请你赏光。

He was delighted and said, ‘Certainly, certainly!’”
「他很高兴地答应了，说是」一定来，一定来！

It was a beauty’s lure.
「『这用的是一条美人计，

A’qiao naturally disliked it, but the thought that it was for Hu’s sake kept her from resenting Xiao.
阿巧姐心里当然不是味道；不过一想到是为胡雪岩，她自然就不会对萧家骥介意，

“What else did he say?” she asked calmly.
她很平静地问道∶』他还有什么话？『


“He said more, of course.
『自然还有话，

‘Why should Madam He invite me?’ I told him, ‘To thank you for curing Intendant Hu.
他问我∶』何姨太为什么要请我？「我说∶『是因为你看好了胡道台，略表谢意。

She also has another favour to ask.’
另外还有件事求你。」

He kept asking what it was, but I would not tell him.
他一再问我什么事，我不肯说。

Now everything depends on you.”
回头全要看你了。』

A’qiao nodded and considered all he had said.
阿巧姐点点头，将他前后的话细想了一遍，

A plan formed in her mind, but one point had to be settled first.
心里有了主意；只是有一点必须先弄清楚。

“When he asked why I was here, what did you tell him?”
『问到我怎么会在这里？你是怎么告诉他的？』

“I said, ‘Madam He has now been dismissed from her household.
『我说∶』何姨太现在下堂了。

She is Intendant Hu’s sister-in-law, the elder sister of his wife.
她是胡道台的大姨子；

Suzhou is occupied, so she cannot return to her own family and has had to seek refuge with a close relation.’ He said, ‘That explains it!
苏州现在沦陷在那里，娘家回不去，只好来投奔至亲。「他说∶『怪不得！

In troubled times one cannot be overparticular about appearances.
人在难中，谈不到避嫌疑；

It is only right that a wife’s elder sister should nurse her brother-in-law.’”
大姨子照料妹夫的病，也是应该的。

A’qiao understood.
「『阿巧姐明白，

This supposed sister-in-law relationship implied that she had a younger sister who was one of Hu’s concubines.
所谓』大姨子『是意指她有个妹妹嫁做胡雪岩的偏旁；

The arrangement made them close enough, yet not too close: it explained her freedom to come and go in his rooms and care for him, while leaving the amorous doctor enough hope to take the bait.
关系如此安排，是疏而亲，亲而疏，不但她穿房入户，照料病人，可以说得过去，而且让色迷迷的张郎中希望不绝，才会上钩。


She greatly admired Xiao’s ingenuity, but could not help laughing.
阿巧姐十分欣赏萧家骥的机智，但也不免好笑，

“You dreadful boy!
『要死快哉！
