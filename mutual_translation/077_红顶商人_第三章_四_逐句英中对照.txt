胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第三章（本章第4部分，共4部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


“Excellent!” he said.
『好极了！』他说，

“The signs of refinement and distinction.
『清贵之相。

Your blessings will increase as you grow older.”
越到晚年，福气越好。』

A’qiao glanced at Hu, smiled faintly and ignored his predictions.
阿巧姐看了胡雪岩一眼，淡淡一笑，不理他那套话，

“There are only a few dishes.
说一句∶『没有什么菜。

I fear we shall not entertain you properly, Dr Zhang.”
只怕怠慢了张先生！』

With that she rose and left.
随即站起身来走了。

He felt an inevitable sense of loss.
张医生自不免有怅然若失之感。

Men and women did not dine together, especially when the man was a stranger.
男女不同席，而况又是生客；

By appearing, she had fulfilled the courtesies of a hostess.
这一见面，就算表达了做主人的礼貌。

Indeed, by ordinary standards he had already enjoyed more than he could expect; there would be no further opportunity to see her.
而且按常理来说，已赚过分，此后就再不可能相见了。

“But does she not have another favour to ask?”
『但是，她不是另外还有事要求我吗？』

The thought reassured him.
想到这一点，张医生宽心了；

Whatever the request might be, he resolved that there could be no discussion unless she made it in person.
打定主意，不论什么事，非要她当面来说，才有商量的余地。


As expected, only Xiao kept him company at dinner.
果然，一顿饭只是萧家骥一个人相陪；

The dishes were delicately prepared.
肴馔相当精致，

When the hotpot was brought in at the end, A’qiao spoke a few polite words from behind the curtain, then vanished again.
最后送上火锅，阿巧姐才隔帘相语，说了几句客气话，从此芳踪杳然。

They talked for some time after the meal, until Dr Zhang could bear it no longer.
饭罢闲谈，又过了好些时候，张医生实在忍不住了；

“Did you not say that A’qiao wanted me to do something for her?”
开口问道∶『不是说阿巧姐有事要我办吗？』

“Yes.
『是的。

Let me go and ask.”
等我去问一问看。』

The doctor watched the screen, straining his ears.
于是张医生只注意屏风，侧着耳朵静听；

After a long while someone emerged, but it was only Xiao.
好久，有人出来了，却仍旧是萧家骥，

Behind the screen, however, a slender shadow could be seen.
但是屏风后面却有纤纤一影。

“A’qiao says you will certainly refuse, so she had better not mention it.”
『阿巧姐说了，张先生一定不会答应的，不如不说。』

“Why should she not?” he blurted out. “What makes her think I shall refuse?”
『为什么不说？』张医生脱口答道∶『何以见得我不会答应。』

“Then I shall tell you!” came the answer from behind the screen.
『那我就说吧！』是屏风后面在应声。


A’qiao appeared with the words.
人随话到，阿巧姐翩然出现。

She had changed her clothes.
衣服也换过了，

Earlier she had worn a black satin fur jacket edged with grey squirrel and a pleated skirt of palest blue silk.
刚才是黑缎灰鼠出锋的皮袄，下系月白绸子百褶裙；

Now she was in informal dress: a silk-padded jacket of bamboo-green ning silk.
此刻换了家常打扮，竹叶青宁绸的丝绵袄，

Too fond of looking pretty to wear bulky padded trousers, she would not bind the cuffs at the ankles as northern women did either.
爱俏不肯穿臃肿的棉裤，也不肯象北地胭脂那样扎脚；

Instead, her loose-cuffed trousers were of soft black satin, trimmed with very broad ornamental bands.
是一条玄色软缎，镶着极宽的『栏杆』的撒脚裤。

For warmth, the jacket had been cut to fit closely at the waist, making her figure all the more graceful and alluring.
为了保暖，衣服腰身裁剪得极紧；越显得体态婀娜，更富风情。

Dr Zhang was half-tipsy, yet at heart he remained a cautious, proper man.
有了五六分酒意的张医生，到底本心还是谨饬一路的人物；

Her beauty was so dazzling that he dared not look closely.
因为艳光逼人，意不敢细看，

Turning his face slightly aside, he asked, “Whatever you wish, A’qiao, please tell me.
略略偏着脸问道∶『阿巧姐有话就请吩咐。

Is it a particularly careful prescription for a tonic paste?”
是不是要我格外细心替你拟张膏滋药的方子？』

“That too, naturally,” she replied. “But there is no hurry.
『这当然也要。』阿巧姐答说∶『不过不忙。

My younger sister entrusted me with this duty.
我是受了我妹妹的重托，

She was uneasy about leaving our dear relation alone in Ningbo.
不放心我这位至亲一个人在宁波；

I cannot look after him constantly, and even when I do, I cannot be as attentive and considerate as my sister.
我又不能常川照应；就是照应总不及我妹妹细心体贴。

I have been thinking how fortunate it was that, in such a grave illness, he found you.
我在想，舍亲这场大病，幸亏遇着张先生，

Your skill has brought him back to health as surely as spring revives the earth.
真正着手成春，医道高明；

He must be out of danger now.
如今一定不碍了。

But if he travels to Shanghai by boat without you to care for him, I really cannot feel easy.
不过坐船到上海，没有张先生你照应，实在不放心。

So there is nothing for it but—” She drew an embroidered handkerchief from beneath her arm and smiled with her lips pressed together, as though too embarrassed to finish.
那就只好——。』说到这里，她抽出腋下的乡花手绢，抿着嘴笑了一下，仿佛下面的话，不好意思出口似的。

Dr Zhang was intoxicated by the clear, oriole-like notes of her voice.
在张医生；那沥沥莺啭似的声音，听得他心醉不已；

Absorbed in the sound, he had paid too little attention to its meaning.
只顾欣赏声音，不免忽略了话中的意思，

Her sudden silence puzzled him.
见她突然停住；不由得诧异。

“Why have you stopped?
『怎么不说下去。

Please go on.
请说，请说，

I am listening carefully.”
我在细听。』


Her meaning was already perfectly clear.
其实意思已经很明显，

If he had listened carefully and still failed to understand, his mind must have been elsewhere.
细听而竟听不出来，可见得心不在焉。

Seeing how thoroughly he had lost his senses, Xiao gave A’qiao a glance: she need not wheel her horse and draw her bow any longer, but should come straight to the point.
萧家骥见他有些丧魂落魄的样子，便向阿巧姐使个眼色，示意她实话直说，不必盘马弯弓，宛转透露了。

“Very well, I shall.
『好的，我就说。

But, Dr Zhang—” Her large eyes flashed with a lively, almost girlish innocence. “Once I have said it, you must not turn me down!”
不过，张先生，』阿巧姐一双大眼珠灵活地一闪，做出象娇憨的女孩子那样的神情∶『等我把话说出口，你可不能打我的回票！』

This was a serious demand.
这话相当严重，

The doctor collected himself and went over her words in his mind.
张医生定定神，将她的话回想了一遍，

Only then did he understand, and he found agreement rather difficult.
才弄清楚是怎么回事，倒有些答应不下了。

“There, you see?”
『是不是？』

A’qiao deliberately spoke softly to Xiao.
阿巧姐意轻声对萧家骥说，

“I said I had better not ask.
『我说不开口的好；

Now I have only invited a refusal—” “Not at all,” the doctor broke in uneasily.
开了口白白碰钉子——。』『没有这话。』张医生不安地抢着说，

“I understand what you mean.
『你的意思我懂了。

The question I am considering is not whether I ought to accompany him.”
我在想的，不是我该不该陪着去。』

“Then what is it?”
『那末是什么呢？』

“Whether the patient can travel.
『是病人能不能走？

A sea voyage in weather like this—if his illness returned, it would be no laughing matter.”
这样的天气，跋涉波涛，万一病势反复，可不是件开玩笑的事。』


His objection was reasonable, but it was hard to know whether it was sincere or merely an excuse.
话说得有理，但究竟是真话，还是托词，却不易估量；

A’qiao was formidable herself and deliberately pressed him, though indirectly.
阿巧姐也很厉害，便有意逼一逼；却又不直接说出来，

Looking at Xiao, she asked, “Did Dr Zhang not say that, with him to look after the patient on the journey, there would be nothing to worry about?”
望着萧家骥问∶『张先生不是说，一路有他照应，就不要紧吗？』

“Certainly.
『是！

With Dr Zhang there, what need we fear?”
有张先生在，还怕什么？』

Their exchange made him seem unwilling to help, which distressed him greatly.
两人一唱一和，倒象张医生不肯帮忙似的，使得他大为不安，

Still he dared not act rashly.
但到底还不敢冒失；

Rising, he said, “Let me examine him again.”
站起身来说∶『我再看看病。』

In the next room, Hu had heard every word.
在隔室的胡雪岩，将他们的对答，只字不遗地听了进去；

Partly because the prospect of having his wish fulfilled delighted him, and partly because he meant to conceal his remaining symptoms, the examination naturally showed a marked improvement.
一半是心愿可望达成，心中喜乐，一半是要隐瞒病情，所以诊察结果，自然又显得大有进境。

Only now could Dr Zhang consider his own circumstances.
这时候张医生才能考虑自己这方面的情形。

Amid war and disorder, with the year drawing to its close, it was hardly the time for a long journey.
兵荒马乱，年近岁逼，实在不是出远门的时候；

But he had spoken too generously to retreat or qualify his offer.
但话说得太慷慨，无法收科或者打折扣；

At the same time, romantic fancies filled his mind.
同时也存着满怀绮想，

He could not bear to relinquish the chance of sharing a sea voyage with A’qiao.
实在舍不得放弃这个与阿巧姐海上同舟的机会，

At last he made up his mind and agreed.
终于毅然答应了下来。


Hu was naturally grateful beyond measure.
这一下，胡雪岩自然感激不尽；

But it was A’qiao’s gratitude that Dr Zhang wanted.
不过张医生所要的是阿巧姐的感激。

Hu understood the delicate distinction perfectly.
此中微妙，胡雪岩也看得很清楚；

He wrapped a hundred taels in red paper and asked her to present them with her own hands.
所以用红纸包了一百两银子，让她亲手致赠。

“A physician should have a heart ready to sacrifice his own flesh,” the doctor said, waving both hands in refusal. “To speak of money would obscure the sincerity of my intentions.”
『医家有割股之心。』张医生摇着双手说∶『谈钱，反倒埋没我的苦心了。』

Fine words—but A’qiao understood those “intentions” only too well, and was the more determined to give him the money because she could not and would not repay them in the manner he desired.
话说得很漂亮，不过阿巧姐也深知他的这片『苦心』，越发要送；因为无法也不愿酬答他的『苦心』。

Naturally, she kept that thought deep within herself.
当然，这只是深藏在她心里的意思。

“I understand your kind intentions, Dr Zhang.
『张先生，你的苦心我知道。

This is a small token from my ‘brother-in-law’.
这是我那位「妹夫」的一点小意思；

He says that if you refuse it, his mind will be uneasy and his recovery will be slower.
他说了，若是张先生不受，于心不安，病好得不快；

In that case, he would not dare ask you to undertake the journey.”
他就不敢劳动大驾了。』

The doctor savoured every word.
张医生将她的话，细细咀嚼了一遍，

“I understand your kind intentions” was branded on his heart as if with a hot iron, never to be forgotten.
『你的苦心我知道』这几个字，简直就象用烙铁印了在心版上，再也忘不掉的了。

“In that case, I can only be shameless enough to accept.
『既然如此，我也只好老脸皮收下。

But—” He went no further.
不过——。』他没有再说下去。

To show A’qiao that he had agreed entirely for her sake, he decided to repay Hu’s gift with one of his own: a couple of valuable restorative medicines.
为了要在阿巧姐面前表示她这番交情，完全是卖给她的，他决定要补还胡雪岩的人情；投桃报李，想送两样贵重补药。

But there was no need to announce it beforehand.
但话不必先说，

To do so would spoil the gesture, so he checked himself.
说了味道就不够了；因而缩住了口。


“Then may I ask, Dr Zhang,” Xiao interposed, “when should we prepare to leave?”
『那末，要请问张先生。』萧家骥插进来说，『预备哪天动身？』

“The sooner the better.
『越早越好。

I must return before the New Year.”
我要趁年里赶回来。』

“You will certainly be back in time.”
『那是一定赶得回来的。』

Xiao considered the arrangements and made a proposal. “Let me have all tomorrow to prepare.
萧家骥盘算了一下，作了主张∶『我尽明天一天预备；

Shall we leave the day after?”
后天就动身怎么样？』

“The day after tomorrow must be a good day.”
『后天一定是好日子，』

A’qiao knew few characters, but could manage an almanac.
阿巧姐识得的字不多，但看皇历还能应付，

Confidently pointing to the entry for the first day of the twelfth month, she read, “‘Auspicious for setting out.’”
很有把握地指着十二月初一那一行说∶『「宜出门。」』
