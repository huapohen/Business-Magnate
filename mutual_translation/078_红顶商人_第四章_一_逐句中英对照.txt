胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第四章（本章第1部分，共2部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第四章
CHAPTER FOUR


尽一天的功夫安排妥帖；
They spent a full day making the arrangements.

第三天一早都上了船，略略安顿，鸣锣启碇。
Early on the third morning, everyone went aboard; after they had settled themselves, the gong sounded and the anchor was raised.

张医生捧着个蓝布包到了胡雪岩舱里。
Dr Zhang brought a bundle wrapped in blue cloth to Hu’s cabin.

『胡大人，』他说，『红包太丰厚了，
“Your gift was far too generous, Mr Hu.

受之有愧。
I feel ashamed to accept so much.

有两样药，请胡大人留着用。』
I have brought two medicines for you to keep.”

『多谢！多谢！
“Thank you, thank you!

真正不敢当。』
You are too kind.”

胡雪岩只当是普通药材，
Hu assumed that they were ordinary medicinal ingredients.

等他打开来一看，是两个锦盒，才知道是珍贵补药；
Only when the bundle was opened and revealed two brocade-covered cases did he realize that they were precious tonics.

长盒子里是全须全尾的一支参，红绿丝线扎住，
In the long case lay a complete ginseng root, every fine rootlet intact, secured with red and green silk threads.

上贴金纸红签，上写八字∶『极品吉林老山人参』。
A red label on gold paper bore eight characters: “Finest Wild Mountain Ginseng from Jilin.”

『这支参是贡品；
“This root was an item of tribute.

张尚书府上流出来的，真正大内的货色。』
It came out of Minister Zhang’s household—genuine imperial-palace stock.”

张医生一面说，一面打开方盒子。
As he spoke, the doctor opened the square case.

方盒子里是鹿茸。一寸多长一段，共是两段；上面长着细细的白毛，
Inside were two sections of deer antler, each a little over a cun long and covered with fine white hairs.

看不出是好是坏。
Nothing about their appearance told Hu whether they were good or bad.


『鹿茸就是鹿角，是大家都晓得的；不过鹿角并不就是鹿茸。
“Everyone knows that velvet antler comes from a deer’s horns, but not every deer’s horn is velvet antler.

老角无用，里面都是筋络；
Old antler is useless; inside it is all fibers and channels.

要刚长出来的新角，长满了精血，象这样子的才合适。』
What one wants is newly grown antler, filled with vital essence and blood, like this.”

张医生又说，『取鹿茸也有诀窍；
Dr Zhang went on, “There is an art to harvesting it too.

手段不高，一刀会拿鹿头砍掉——。』张医生是亲眼见过的——
If the man lacks skill, one stroke may take off the deer’s head—” He had seen the process himself.

春夏之交，万物茂盛；驱鹿于空围场中，不断追赶；
At the turn of spring into summer, when everything flourished, deer were driven into an empty enclosure and chased without respite.

鹿胆最小，自是尽力奔避，因而血气上腾，贯注于新生的鹿角中。
Timid creatures by nature, they fled with all their strength, driving their blood and vital energy upward into the newly growing antlers.

然后开放栅门，正好窗口一头鹿逃避；
A gate was then opened, just wide enough for one deer to escape at a time.

栅门外是曲栏，
Beyond it was a curved passage formed by rails.

一端有人手持利斧，聚精会神地在等待，
At one end a man waited with a sharp axe, concentrating intently.

等这头鹿将出曲栏时，看准了一斧下去，正好砍断了新生的那一段鹿角。
As the deer was about to emerge, he judged his moment and struck, severing precisely the newly grown section of antler.

要这样采取的鹿茸，才是上品。
Only velvet antler gathered in this way was of the finest quality.

胡雪岩对这段叙述深感兴趣，
Hu listened with great interest.

『虽说「修合无人见，存心有天知」，
“They say, ‘No one may see how medicines are prepared, but Heaven knows the maker’s heart.’

货色好坏，日子一久，总会有人知道的；
Yet good or bad quality will become known in time.

一传十，十传百，口碑就出去了。
One person tells ten, and ten tell a hundred; that is how a reputation spreads.

张先生，』他说，『听说你也有家药店，
Dr Zhang, I hear that you have a pharmacy too.

想来规模很大。』
It must be a large concern.”

『谈不到规模。
“I could hardly call it that.

祖传的产业，守守而已。』
It is a family business that I merely keep going.

张医生又说，『我诊断很忙，也顾不到。』
My medical practice keeps me so busy that I have little time for it.”


听得这样说，胡雪岩就不便深谈了——
At this, Hu could hardly pursue the subject.

刘不才陷溺于赌，对胡雪岩开药店的打算，不甚关切；
Liu Bucai had become so absorbed in gambling that he took little interest in Hu’s plan to open a pharmacy.

胡雪岩本想问问张医生的意见；现在听他的话，对自己的事业都照顾不周，自然没有舍己而耘人之田的可能，
Hu had thought of asking Dr Zhang’s advice, but a man who could scarcely tend his own affairs would hardly leave his own field to cultivate another’s.

那又何必谈它。
There was no point in discussing it.

不过既是特地延请来的上客，总得尽心招待，
Still, the doctor was an honored guest specially invited to accompany them, and they must do their best to entertain him.

找些什么消遣？
What diversion could they find?

清谈不如手谈，最合适也差不多是唯一的消遣，就是凑一桌麻将。
Conversation with the hands would be better than mere talk: the most suitable amusement, and almost the only one available, was a game of mahjong.

宁波麻将跟广东麻将齐名，
Ningbo mahjong was as famous as the Cantonese variety.

据说，由马吊变为麻将，就是宁波人由明朝以来，不断研究改进的结果。
It was said that the transformation of madiao cards into mahjong had resulted from Ningbo players’ continual experiments and improvements since the Ming dynasty.

张医生亦好此道，所以听得胡雪岩这个提议，欣然乐从。
Dr Zhang was a devotee himself and readily agreed to Hu’s proposal.


胡雪岩自己当然不能打；
Hu, of course, could not play.

眼前的搭子三缺一，拉上船老大一个才能成局。
They needed one more person to complete the table, so the boat’s master would have to join them.

萧家骥亦是此中好手；但不知阿巧姐如何？
Xiao Jiaji was an expert, but what of A’qiao?

少不得要问一声。
He had to ask.

『阿巧姐，你跟宁波人打过牌没有？』
“Sister A’qiao, have you played with Ningbo people before?”

『当然打过。』
“Of course.”

『有没有在这种船上打过？』
“Have you ever played aboard a boat like this?”

『这种船我还是第二次坐。』
“This is only the second time I have traveled in one.

阿巧姐说∶『麻将总是麻将；
But mahjong is mahjong.

船上岸上有啥分别？』
What difference does it make whether one plays afloat or ashore?”

『这种麻将要记性好——。』『那自然。』
“For this kind of mahjong, you need a good memory—” “Naturally.”

阿巧姐认为萧家骥无须关照，
She thought the warning unnecessary.

『打麻将记性不好，上下家出张进张都弄不清楚，这还打什么？』
“If you cannot remember what the players before and after you are discarding or taking, how can you play at all?”

听这一说，他不便再说下去了。
Hearing this, Xiao could hardly say more.

等拉开一张活腿小方桌，分好筹码，
They unfolded a small square table and divided the counters.

只见船老大将一系在舱顶上的绳子放了下来；拿只竹篮挂在绳端的钩子上，
The boat’s master then lowered a cord fastened to the cabin roof and hung a bamboo basket on the hook at its end.

位置恰好悬在方桌正中，高与头齐，伸手可及，
It hung exactly over the center of the table, level with their heads and within easy reach.

却不知有何用处。
A’qiao had no idea what it was for.


阿巧姐也是争强好胜的性格，一物不知，引以为耻，所以不肯开口相问；
She was competitive by nature and hated to admit ignorance of anything, so she would not ask.

反正总有用处，看着好了。
It must have some use; she would watch and see.

扳庄就位，
They determined the dealer and took their seats.

阿巧姐坐在张医生下家；对家船老大起庄，
A’qiao sat next in turn after Dr Zhang, while the boat’s master, opposite her, dealt first.

只见他抓齐了十四张牌，从左到右看了一遍，立即将牌扑倒，取出一张亮一亮，是张北风。
Having drawn his fourteen tiles, he inspected them from left to right, immediately laid them face down, and displayed a North Wind as his discard.

他的上家萧家骥叫碰；张医生便向阿巧姐说∶『这就是宁波麻将算得精的地方；
Xiao, who preceded him in the order of play, called “Pung!” Dr Zhang explained to A’qiao, “This is where the Ningbo players calculate so finely.

庄家头一张不打南风打北风，
On his first discard the dealer puts out North instead of South.

上家一碰，马上又摸一张，
If the player before him claims it for a pung, the dealer gets another draw at once.

也许是张南风，本来该第二家摸成后对的，现在是自己摸成双；
It may be a South Wind, giving him the pair that would otherwise have gone to the next player.

这一摸味道就好了。』摸呀摸的，阿巧姐听来有些刺耳，
A draw like that makes all the difference.” His repeated use of the word for drawing tiles, with its other sense of touching or feeling, grated on A’qiao.

便不理他；
She ignored him.

只见萧家骥拿张东风亮一亮，没有人要，便抬起手来将那张东风，往挂着的竹篮中一丢。
Xiao displayed an East Wind; when no one wanted it, he raised his hand and tossed it into the hanging basket.


原来竹篮是这样的用处，
So that was the basket’s purpose!

阿巧姐心里有些着慌，
A’qiao began to feel alarmed.

脱口说道∶『宁波麻将的打法特别。』
“The Ningbo way of playing is different,” she blurted out.

『是的——。』张医生马上又接口解释，
“Yes—” Dr Zhang immediately explained.

由于海上风浪甚大，船会颠簸，所以宁波麻将讲究过目不忘，合扑着打；
Heavy seas made the vessel pitch and roll, so Ningbo players prided themselves on remembering their tiles at a glance and playing with them face down.

又因为船上地方小，摆不下大方桌，
Space aboard was limited too; there was no room for a large square table.

甚至有时候团团围坐四个人，膝盖上支块木板，就当牌桌，
Sometimes four players merely sat in a circle with a board across their knees.

这样自然没有富裕的地方来容纳废牌，因而打在竹篮里。
Naturally, there was no spare space for discarded tiles, so these went into a basket.

『不过，』张医生看着船老大和萧家骥说，『这张桌子也不算太小，
“But this table is not so very small,” he added, looking at the boat’s master and Xiao.

我们照岸上的打法好了。』
“Why not play as we do ashore?”

船老大当然不会反对；
The master had no objection.

萧家骥却笑了笑——这一笑使得阿巧姐不大舒服；
Xiao, however, smiled—and that smile offended A’qiao.

觉得他有轻视之意，大不服气。
She thought he was looking down on her and resented it bitterly.


『不要紧，不要紧。』她说，
“Never mind, never mind,” she said.

『照规矩打好了。』
“Let us play by the rules.”

这等于不受张医生的好意，然而他丝毫不以为忤。
In effect, she had refused the doctor’s kindness, but he took no offense.

阿巧姐却是有点如俗语说的『死要面子活受罪』，
She, however, was discovering the truth of the saying about suffering any misery to save face.

硬记三家出张，颇以为苦。
Trying to remember the other three players’ discards was a torment.

打到一半，三家都似『听叫』，而她的牌还乱得很；
Halfway through the hand, all three seemed ready to win, while her own tiles were still a muddle.

而且越打越为难，生熟张子都有些记不住了。
The longer they played, the harder it became to remember which tiles had already appeared and which had not.

『这样子不是路道，只怕一副都和不成功。输钱在其次，面子输不起。』她这样在心中自语着，
“This is no way to go on,” she told herself. “I may never win a single hand. Losing money is one thing, but I cannot afford to lose face.”

决定改变打法。新的打法是只顾自己，不顾外面；
She decided to change her tactics and concentrate entirely on her own hand.

只要不是三副落地，包人家的辣子，她什么生张都敢打。
Provided she did not become liable for another player’s limit hand after three exposed sets, she would risk discarding any tile, however unfamiliar.

张医生打替她担心，不断提示，那张牌出了几张，那张牌已经绝；
The doctor worried on her behalf, continually reminding her how many of a tile had appeared and which were exhausted.

阿巧得其所哉，专心一致管自己做牌，
With this help, she settled comfortably into the game and devoted herself to building her own hands.

两圈不到，就和了一副清一色；一副三元；一副凑一色，
In less than two rounds she won with a pure one-suit hand, a Three Dragons hand, and a mixed one-suit hand.

手气大旺。
Her luck was running high.


『张先生，你下家的风头不得了。』船老大说，
“Dr Zhang, the player after you is on a tremendous streak,” the boat’s master said.

『要看紧点！』
“You must keep a closer watch on her.”

越是这样说，张医生的手越松，
The more he urged caution, the more freely the doctor played.

不但不扣她的牌，还会拆搭子给她吃，而且还要关照∶『阿巧姐，这张三万是第四张，
Not only did he refuse to withhold tiles she needed; he even broke up promising combinations to feed her, adding reminders such as, “Sister A’qiao, this is the fourth Three of Characters.

你再不吃就没有得吃了。』
If you do not take it now, there will be no other.”

加上萧家骥打得很厉害，扣住了船老大的牌，很难得吃到一张；
Xiao was playing sharply too, withholding the tiles the boat’s master wanted and scarcely allowing him a single claim.

这样就几乎变成三个对付一个，
It was almost three against one.

船老大一个人大输，却不敢得罪主顾，
The master lost heavily, but dared not offend his customers.

打完四圈装肚子痛，拆散了场头。
After four rounds he pleaded a stomachache and broke up the game.

阿巧姐一个人大赢；但牌打得并不有趣，
A’qiao alone had won a great deal, yet she had not found the game particularly enjoyable.

自己觉得赢船家的钱不好意思，将筹码一推，
Embarrassed to take the boatman’s money, she pushed away the counters.

『算了，
“Let it go!

算了！』
Let it go!”

接着起身离去。
Then she stood up and left.


这个慷慨大方的举动，自然赢得了船老大的感激与尊敬，因此照料得很周到；
Her generosity naturally earned the boat’s master’s gratitude and respect, and he attended to them with exceptional care.

一路顺顺利利到上海，
The voyage to Shanghai went smoothly.

胡雪岩也不劳张医生费心，按时服药，毫无异状。
Hu took his medicines regularly and developed no new symptoms, giving Dr Zhang little trouble.

话虽如此，对张医生还是很重视的，
Nevertheless, he continued to show the doctor great consideration.

所以一到上海码头先遣萧家骥去通知，说有这样一位贵客，请他预备招待。
As soon as they reached the Shanghai wharf, he sent Xiao ahead to announce the distinguished visitor and ask Gu to prepare a welcome.

古应春不在家，好在七姑奶奶一切都能作主。
Gu was out, but fortunately the Seventh Lady could decide everything herself.

宁波的情形，前半段她已听李得隆谈过；
She had already heard the earlier part of the Ningbo story from Li Delong.

虽替胡雪岩的病担忧，但有阿巧姐在照料，也略略可以放心，
Though anxious about Hu’s illness, she had been somewhat reassured to know that A’qiao was caring for him.

估量着总要到年后，病势才会养到能够长途跋涉，
She had expected him to remain too ill for a long journey until after the New Year.

不想这么快就已回上海，自觉惊喜交集。
His unexpectedly early return therefore filled her with both surprise and delight.


于是匆匆打点，雇了三乘暖轿，带着男女佣人，直奔码头；
She hurriedly made the arrangements, hired three enclosed winter sedan chairs, and rushed to the wharf with male and female servants.

上船先见阿巧姐，后见胡雪岩，
Going aboard, she saw A’qiao first and then Hu.

看他瘦得可怕，不免有点伤心，掉了两滴眼泪。
His frightening thinness saddened her, and she shed a couple of tears.

『张先生不要笑我！』七姑奶奶自己都觉得有些不好意思，
“You must not laugh at me, Dr Zhang,” she said, rather embarrassed herself.

『我们这位小爷叔，这一阵子真是多灾多难，
“Little Uncle has suffered one misfortune after another lately.

说到他的苦楚，眼泪好落一脸盆。
The story of his troubles would fill a basin with tears.

不过总算还好，命中有贵人相扶，逢凶化吉，遇难成祥，
But at least Heaven has sent him good people to help him, turning danger to safety and misfortune to good.

才会遇着张先生这种医道高明心又热的人。』
That is how he came to meet someone as skillful and kind-hearted as you.”

张医生也听说过有这样一位姑奶奶，心直口快，大家不但服她，也有些怕她；
The doctor had heard of this lady, whose frank, outspoken manner commanded both respect and a measure of fear.

自己要在阿巧姐身上打主意，还非得此人的助力不可，因而格外客气，
If he hoped to win A’qiao, her help would be indispensable, so he was especially courteous.

连声答道∶『好说，好说。
“You are too kind, too kind!

七姑奶奶才是天字第一号的热心人。』
You yourself are the most warm-hearted person under Heaven.”

七姑奶奶最喜欢听人说她热心，
The Seventh Lady loved nothing better than to be praised for her kindness.

觉得这个张医生没有名医的架子，人既和气，言语也不讨厌，
She found that he had none of a famous physician’s airs: he was pleasant and agreeable in conversation.

顿生好感；
She took to him at once.

原来打算请他住客栈的，此时改了主意，
Originally she had meant to put him up at an inn; now she changed her mind.

『张先生，』她说，『难得来一越，多玩些日子！
“Dr Zhang, since you have come all this way, stay and enjoy yourself for a few days.

就住在舍下好了；只怕房子太小，委屈了张先生。』
You must be our guest at home—though I fear our little house will not do you justice.”

话刚说完，阿巧姐拉了她一把，显然是不赞成她的办法；
A’qiao tugged at her, plainly disapproving.

但话已说出口，不能收回，
But the invitation had been given and could not be withdrawn.

只好看张医生如何答复，再作道理。
She could only see how the doctor answered and then decide what to do.


『不敢当，不敢当。
“You are too kind!

我年内要赶回去。
I must get home before the year is out.

打搅府上，只怕诸多不便。』
I fear staying with you would put you to a great deal of inconvenience.”

他是客气话，七姑奶奶却将计就计，不作决定∶『先到了舍下再说。』
He meant this as a polite demurral, but the Seventh Lady took advantage of it to leave the question open. “Let us get you home first and discuss it there.

她这样答道∶『现在就上岸吧！』
We should go ashore now.”

第一个当然安排胡雪岩，
Hu naturally had to be settled first.

轿子抬到船上，然后将胡雪岸用棉被包裹，象个『蜡烛包』似的，抱入轿内，遮紧轿帘。
A chair was carried aboard, and he was wrapped in a quilt like a swaddled infant, lifted inside, and screened securely behind its curtains.

上岸时，当然要特别小心，
Getting him ashore required particular care.

船老大亲自指挥，全船上下一起动手，搭了四条跳板，才将轿子抬到岸上。
The boat’s master directed the operation himself; the whole crew helped, laying four gangplanks before carrying the chair to the wharf.

再一顶轿子是张医生；
The second chair was for Dr Zhang.

余下一顶应该是阿巧姐，她却偏要跟七姑奶奶挤在一起，为的是有一番心事，迫不及待地要透露。
The remaining one should have been A’qiao’s, but she insisted on squeezing into it with the Seventh Lady: she had something on her mind that could not wait.

七姑奶奶听阿巧姐刚说了个开头，就忍不住笑了；
The Seventh Lady had barely heard the opening when she began to laugh.

阿巧姐便有些气，
A’qiao was annoyed.

『跟你规规矩矩说，你倒笑话我！』她说。
“I am speaking seriously, and you laugh at me!”

『我不是笑你，
“I am not laughing at you.

是笑张郎中癞虾蟆想吃天鹅肉。
I am laughing at that doctor—a toad dreaming of swan’s flesh.

不要紧！
Never mind.

你跟我说，我替你想办法。』
Tell me, and I shall find a way out for you.”

『这才象句话！』
“That is more like it!”

阿巧姐回嗔作喜，
Her annoyance vanished.

细细说明经过；话完，轿子也到家了。
She explained everything in detail, finishing just as the chair reached the house.


到家第一件事是安置胡雪岩；第二件事是招待客人，
The first task at home was to settle Hu; the second was to entertain their guest.

这得男主人回家才行，
For that the master of the house must return.

而且七姑奶奶已有了为阿巧姐解围的策略，也得古应春来照计而行。
Moreover, the Seventh Lady had devised a way of rescuing A’qiao from her predicament, and Gu would have to carry it out.

因此，她赴萧家骥要赶着回家省视老母之便，关照他先去寻到师父，说知其事。
Xiao was eager to go home and see his mother, so she asked him to find his teacher first and explain the situation.

找了两处都不见，
He tried two places without success.

最后才在号子里听说古应春去了一处地方，是浙江海运局。
Finally, at the firm, he learned that Gu had gone to the Zhejiang Maritime Grain Transport Bureau.

浙江的漕运久停，海运局已成了一个浙江派在上海的驿站，传递各处的文报而已。
Zhejiang’s tribute-grain shipments had long been suspended, and the bureau now served merely as the province’s dispatch station in Shanghai, forwarding official communications.

古应春到那里，想来是去打听杭州的消息。
Gu had presumably gone there for news of Hangzhou.

正留了话想离去时，他师父回来了，
Xiao was leaving a message and preparing to go when his teacher returned.

脸色阴郁，如果说是去打听消息，可想而知，消息一定不好。
His face was so gloomy that, if he had been seeking news, it was plainly bad.


然而见了徒弟，却有喜色。
The sight of his pupil, however, brought him pleasure.

他也跟他妻子一样，猜想着萧家骥必得过了年才会回来；
Like his wife, he had expected Xiao to remain away until after the New Year.

因而首先就问∶『病人呢？』
His first question was therefore, “Where is our patient?”

『一起回来了。』萧家骥紧接着说∶『是郎中陪着来的。
“He has come back too—with the doctor.

年底下不肯走这一趟，很承他的情；
It was very kind of the man to make the journey so near the year’s end.

师娘请师父马上回家，
Madam asks you to come home at once.

打算要好好陪他玩两天。』
She wants you to show him a good time for a couple of days.”

『这是小事。』
“That is easily done.

古应春问，『我们这位小爷叔的病呢？』
But how is Little Uncle?”

『不碍了。
“Out of danger.

调养几天就可以起床。』『唉！』古应春长叹一声，
A few days’ rest and care should get him on his feet.” Gu sighed deeply.

『起了床只怕又要病倒。』
“I fear that once he gets up, he will fall ill again.”

萧家骥一听就明白，
Xiao understood at once.

『是不是杭州失守了？』他问。
“Has Hangzhou fallen?”

『上个月廿八的事。』
“On the twenty-eighth of last month.”

回答的声音似乎有气无力，
Gu’s voice seemed drained of strength.

『刚才从海运局得来的消息。』
“I have just heard at the bureau.”

『王抚台呢？』
“And Governor Wang?”

『听说殉节了。』胡应春又说。
“They say he died for his duty.

『详细情形还不晓得。
We do not yet know the details.

也许逃了出来，亦未可知。』
Perhaps he escaped; one cannot be sure.”

『不会的。』
“No, he would not have.”

萧家骥想到跟王有龄一经识面，便成永诀的凄凉近事，
Xiao remembered their recent meeting: the first time he had seen Wang had become their final parting.

不由得两行热泪汩汩而下。
Hot tears streamed down his cheeks.


『唉！』
“Ah!”

古应春顿着足叹气，
Gu stamped his foot and sighed.

『你都如此，何况是他？
“If even you feel like this, what will it do to him?

这个坏消息，还真不知道怎么跟他开口？』
I truly do not know how to tell him.”

『现在说不得，
“You must not tell him now.

一说，病势马上反复。
His illness would return at once.

不但师父不能说，还得想法子瞒住他。』
You must not merely keep silent, but find some way of preventing him from hearing it.”

『我晓得。
“I know.

你回家去看一看；
Go home and see your family.

今晚上不必来了。
There is no need to come back tonight.

明天上午，再碰头。』
We shall meet tomorrow morning.”

于是师弟二人同车，
Teacher and pupil took the same carriage.

先送了萧家骥，古应春才回家。
Gu dropped Xiao at home before returning to his own house.

跟胡雪岩相见自有一番关切的问讯；然后才跟张医生亲切相叙，
Naturally, he first greeted Hu with affectionate inquiries, then sat down for a cordial conversation with Dr Zhang.

这样就快到了晚饭时分了。
By then it was almost dinnertime.


七姑奶奶找个机会将她丈夫唤到一边，商量款客；
The Seventh Lady found an opportunity to draw her husband aside and discuss the evening’s entertainment.

她的意思是，如果在家吃饭，加上一个李得隆，只有三个人，未免清冷，
At home, even with Li Delong, there would be only three men at dinner, making a rather dull party.

不如请张医生上馆子，
Better to take the doctor to a restaurant.

『最好是请他吃花酒。』她说。
“Best of all, give him a dinner with courtesans,” she said.

『花酒总要请他吃的。
“Of course we shall do that.

不过，你怎么知道他喜欢吃花酒？』
But how do you know he likes such dinners?”

『不但吃花酒，
“It should not stop at dinner.

最好还替他寻个好的；能够讨回去的。
Better still, find him a good woman whom he can take home.

其中自有道理，回头我再跟你细谈。』
There is a reason for it; I shall explain later.”

『我也不管你搞什么鬼！照办就是。』古应春又说，『有句要紧话关照你，
“I do not know what you are plotting, but I shall do as you say.” Gu continued, “There is something important I must tell you.

千万要当心，不能在小爷叔面前透露；不然不得了——。』『急煞人了！』七姑奶奶不耐烦了，
Be extremely careful not to let Little Uncle hear it, or the consequences will be dreadful—” “You are driving me frantic!

『到底是啥事，
What is it?

你倒是快说呀！』
Tell me!”

纵然如此知妻莫若夫，
Yet no one knew her better than her husband.

贸然说出杭州的变化，以七姑奶奶的性情，先就会大惊小怪，满不住人，
If he blurted out the news of Hangzhou, she would make such an outcry that everyone would hear.

因而又先要关照一句∶『你可不要叫！
He warned her once more. “Do not cry out.

杭州失守了；
Hangzhou has fallen.

王雪松不知存亡，十之八九殉了节。』
We do not know whether Governor Wang is alive, but almost certainly he died for his duty.”

七姑奶奶倒没有叫，
She did not cry out.

是半晌作不得声；
For a long time she could say nothing.

接着也跟萧家骥那样，热泪滚滚，
Then, like Xiao, she began to weep.

闭着眼睛说∶『我好悔！』
Closing her eyes, she said, “How bitterly I regret it!”


『悔！』
“Regret it?”

古应春大为不解，
Gu was bewildered.

『悔什么？』
“Regret what?”

『我们也算干亲。
“We are his adopted kin, after all.

虽说高攀，不敢认真；到底有那样一个名分在。
It may have been presumption on our part, and we dared not take it too seriously, but the relationship existed.

看了困在杭州等死，我们做亲戚的一点不曾尽心，
We watched him trapped in Hangzhou, waiting for death, and did nothing for him.

只怕他在地下也在怨我们。』
Perhaps even beneath the earth he is reproaching us.”

『这是劫数！
“This was fate.

小爷叔那样的本事，都用不上力；
Even a man as capable as Little Uncle could do nothing.

你我有什么办法？
What could you and I have done?

只有拿他的下落打听清楚，
We can only find out exactly what happened.

果然殉了节，替他打一场水陆，超度超度。』
If he really died for his duty, we shall arrange a Water-and-Land service to deliver his spirit.”

七姑奶奶不作声，皱紧双眉苦苦思索——
She said nothing, but knitted her brows in deep thought.

遇到这种情形，古应春总是格外留神；因为这是七姑奶奶遇到疑难，要拿出决断来的时候。
Gu always watched her particularly closely at such moments: this was how she looked when faced with a difficult matter on which she meant to reach a decision.

『你先陪客人出去。
“Take our guest out first.

能早回来最好早回来。
Come back early if you can.

再打听打听王抚台的下落。』
And make further inquiries about Governor Wang.”

她说一句，他应一句，最后问说∶『张先生住在哪里？』
He assented to each instruction, then asked, “Where will Dr Zhang stay?”

『住在我们的家。』七姑奶奶毫不迟疑地回答，
“Here in our house,” she answered without hesitation.

『这几天着实还有偏劳他的地方。』
“We shall have considerable need of his help over the next few days.”


古应春不知道她葫芦里卖的什么药？反正对这位郎中要格外巴结，他已能会意的；
Gu did not know what she had in mind, but he understood that he must court the physician’s favor with particular care.

因此，安排在最好的番菜馆『吃大菜』，在那里就叫了两个局。
He therefore arranged a full dinner at the best Western restaurant and summoned two courtesans to join them.

张医生对一个『红信人』艳春老四，颇为中意；
The doctor was much taken with a popular woman, the Fourth Miss of Yanchun House.

古应春便在艳春院摆了个『双台』，飞笺召客，奉张医生为首座。
Gu accordingly arranged a two-table banquet at her establishment, sent invitations flying out to his friends, and installed Dr Zhang in the seat of honor.

客人无不久历花丛，每人起码叫两个局，
The guests were all veterans of the pleasure quarters; each summoned at least two courtesans.

珠围翠绕，热闹非凡；
Surrounded by jewels and beauty, the party became extraordinarily lively.

将个初涉洋场的张医生弄得晕头转向，然而乐在其中了。
The doctor, new to the foreign settlement, was quite bewildered—and thoroughly enjoying himself.

席间闲话，当然也有谈时局的；
Naturally, their table talk included current events.

古应春正要打听杭州的情形，少不得要细细追问。
Gu was anxious for news of Hangzhou and questioned the speakers closely.


据说杭州城内从十一月二十以后，军心就已瓦解了；
According to their accounts, the army’s resolve inside Hangzhou had begun to collapse after the twentieth of the eleventh month.

最主要的原因，还在『绝粮』二字。
The chief reason was the exhaustion of food.

廿四那天，在一家海货行，搜到一批木耳，每人分得一两；
On the twenty-fourth, a quantity of wood-ear fungus had been found in a dried-seafood merchant’s shop, allowing each man one liang by weight.

廿五那天又搜到一批杭州人名『盐青果』的盐橄榄，每人分得五钱。
On the twenty-fifth, some salted olives, known locally as “salted green fruit,” had been discovered, yielding half a liang apiece.

于是外省军队，开始大家小户搜食物；
Troops from other provinces then began searching households, rich and poor, for food.

抚标中军都是本省人，在杭日久，熟人甚多，
The governor’s own central troops were all Zhejiang men who had spent many years in Hangzhou and knew many people there.

倒还略有羞耻之心，压低帽檐，索粮用福建或者河南口音；
Retaining a little shame, they pulled their hat brims low and demanded provisions in assumed Fujian or Henan accents.

当然，除去搜粮，还有别样违犯军纪的行为，
Nor was looting food their only breach of military discipline.

这一下秩序大乱，
Order collapsed.

王有龄带领亲兵小队，亲自抓了十几个人，当街正法。
Wang Youling personally led a small detachment of his own guards, seized more than a dozen offenders, and had them executed in the street.

然而无救于军纪，更无补于军心。
It did nothing, however, to restore either discipline or morale.
