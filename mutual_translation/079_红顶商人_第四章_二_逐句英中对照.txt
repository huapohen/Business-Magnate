胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第四章（本章第2部分，共2部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


Another strange phenomenon had appeared: people were “selling money.”
这时还有个怪现象，就是『卖钱』；

Copper cash was heavy and awkward to carry, so they exchanged it for silver bullion or silver dollars.
钱重不便携带，要换银子或者银洋，

Strings of coins were openly offered for sale with wisps of straw stuck into them as signs.
一串一串的铜钱，公然插上草标出卖，

Naturally, silver commanded a high price and copper fell cheap.
当然银贵钱贱。

These were preparations for flight.
这是预作逃亡之计，

With the soldiers in such a state of mind, the civilians were more frightened still.
军心如此，民心更加恐慌，

Whenever they met, there was only one subject of conversation: on what day would the Longhairs take the city?
这时相顾谈论的，只有一个话题∶长毛会在哪天破城？

On the twenty-seventh of the eleventh month, the defenders decided to seek life in the midst of death.
到了十一月廿七，守城的官军，决定死中求活，

At dawn the next day they would charge out through Genshan Gate, force a passage, and meet whatever relief forces might conceivably be outside.
第二天黎明冲出艮山门，杀开一条血路，接引可能会有的外援。

It was a desperate fantasy, yet at least it represented a determined attempt to save themselves, capable of heartening troops and people alike.
这虽是妄想，但无论如何是奋发自救的作为，可以激励民心士气，

It could do good and no harm.
有益无害。

During the night, however, matters changed.
不想到了夜里，情况起了变化，

Soldiers began lowering themselves from the wall in twos and threes.
士兵三三两两，缒城而下；

The planned assault had become desertion, each man seeking his own escape.
这就变做军心涣散，各奔前程的『开小差』了。

It was said that someone had incited this change, and that the instigator was no less a person than Zhejiang’s provincial treasurer, Lin Fuxiang.
据说，这个变化是有人从中煽动的结果。煽动的人还是浙江的大员∶藩司林福祥。

Lin commanded a force called the Dingwu Army, whose discipline was the worst and whose fighting was the least effective.
林福祥带领的一支军队，名为『定武军』，军纪最坏，而作战最不力。

He was skilled at empty display, forever devising useless flourishes.
而林福祥则颇善于做作，专干些毫无用处的花样；

He also liked ingenious stratagems, which usually cost him both the prize he sought and the men he risked.
又喜欢出奇计，但到头来往往『赔了夫人又折兵』，

Many consequently suspected that he was secretly in league with the Longhairs.
因此颇有人怀疑他已与长毛暗通了款曲。

It was said that he and an expectant prefect surnamed Gan had visited the enemy camp to discuss matters.
说他曾与一个姓甘的候补知府，到长毛营盘里议过事。


Those rumors could not be verified, but one incident did give substantial grounds for suspicion.
这些传闻虽莫可究诘，但有件事却实在可疑；

Wang Youling had captured a spy named Xu Zong’ao, a battalion commander whose appointment to the Dingwu Army Lin Fuxiang had recommended.
王有龄抓到过一个奸细名为徐宗鳌，就是林福洋保举在定武军当差的营官。

The messages exchanged between Wang inside the city and Zhang Yuliang outside, fixing dates for coordinated operations, had all passed through the Dingwu Army.
王有龄与张玉良在城内城外互通消息，约期会合的『战书』，都由定武军转送，

Xu had forwarded more than ten of these military dispatches to the Longhairs.
先后不下十余通之多，都为徐宗鳌转送到了长毛那里；

After an informer exposed him, he was arrested, interrogated, and proved guilty.
后来经人密告，逮捕审问属实，

His entire family was beheaded, apart from a three-year-old son who was spared.
徐宗鳌全家，除了留下三岁的一个小儿子以外，尽数斩决。

Yet only this principal offender was dealt with.
可是只办了这样一个罪魁祸首；

Though Wang had become deeply suspicious of Lin behind the scenes, he feared smashing the vessel while striking at the rat.
王有龄虽然对幕后的林福祥已大具戒心，却因投鼠忌器，

With a powerful enemy surrounding them, he would not risk an internal conflict that might throw their own ranks into disorder.
不愿在强敌包围之下，还有自乱阵脚的内讧出现，

He could only suppress his anger and remain silent.
只好隐忍不言。


Lin, however, had indeed reached a secret understanding with the Longhairs.
而林福祥却确确实实跟长毛已取得了默契，

Unwilling to surrender openly, he had promised to help undermine the defenses from within.
虽不肯公然投降，却答应在暗底下帮着『拆墙脚』，

His incitement of the Genshan Gate troops to desert that night was meant to bring down Hangzhou’s tottering wall.
这天晚上煽动艮山门守军潜逃，就是要拆杭州这座将倒的危墙。

The Longhairs did not notice the deserters during the darkness.
夜里的逃兵，长毛不曾发觉；

At dawn they discovered the traces and took them as evidence that the garrison was disintegrating.
到了天明，发现踪迹，长毛认为这是杭州城内守军溃散的迹象，

They launched an attack, and Fengshan, Houchao, and Qingbo gates were the first to fall.
于是发功攻势，凤山、候潮、清波三门，首先被破。

When the news reached Wang, he knew that all was lost.
报到王有龄那里，知道大势去矣！

“I have not failed the court,” he said, “only the hundreds of thousands of loyal and righteous people of Hangzhou.”
自道∶『不负朝廷，只负了杭州城内数十万忠义士民。』

He had long resolved to die for his duty.
殉节之志早决，

The moment had come.
这是时候了！

Returning to the governor’s yamen, he put on his formal robes and cap, bowed toward the imperial palace in gratitude for the emperor’s favors, and left a final letter.
回到巡抚衙门，穿戴衣冠，望阙谢恩，留下遗书，

He swallowed gold; fearing that it would not kill him, he also took opium.
然后吞金，唯恐不死，又服鸦片烟；

By then the cries of the household mingled with the uproar outside.
而这时衙门内的哭声和衙门外人声相应和，

The Longhairs were approaching, and, rather than suffer humiliation, Wang hanged himself.
长毛已经迫近，为怕受辱，王有龄上吊而死。

Among those who died for their duty at the same time were Education Commissioner Yun Xigeng, Wenrui, brigade commander of Chuzhou, and Wu Baofeng, magistrate of Renhe.
同时殉难的有学政员锡庚、处州镇总兵文瑞、仁和知县吴保丰。

Salt Commissioner Zhuang Huanwen led the Quanzhou troops, fierce and experienced fighters recruited in that district of Fujian.
盐运使庄焕文所带的是晓勇善战的福建泉州籍的『泉勇』，

They fought desperately to break out but were defeated, and Zhuang drowned himself.
奋战突围，不幸兵败，庄焕文投水自尽。


Lin Fuxiang, by contrast, received exceptional favor from the Longhairs.
林福祥却果然得到长毛的破格优遇，

He was lodged in the western reception hall of the provincial treasurer’s yamen, entertained with fine food and wine, and promised an escort to any destination he chose.
被安置在藩司衙门的西花厅；好酒好肉款待，而且答应听凭林福祥自己决定，要到哪里便护送到哪里。

He chose Shanghai.
林福祥选择的是上海，

It was said that he would also escort Wang Youling’s coffin and surviving family there, for onward travel to their native Fujian.
据说此来还有一项任务，是护送王有龄的灵柩及家眷，由上海转回福建原籍。

At this, Gu had to interrupt with questions.
听到这里，古应不能不打断话问了。

If Wang’s coffin came to Shanghai, Hu would inevitably insist on mourning before it.
因为王有龄的灵柩到上海，且不说胡雪岩凭棺一恸，决不可免；

Quite apart from that, Gu’s own relationship with the dead man obliged him to pay his respects.
就是他在情分上亦不能不吊祭一番。

He remembered his wife’s bitter regret that they had done nothing for their adopted kinsman while he lived.
尤其是想到刚听妻子听说，颇以对这位『干亲』生前，未能稍尽心意而引为莫大憾事；

Bowing before the coffin would not be enough; they must also offer comfort and assistance to the surviving family if they were to ease their sense of guilt.
那就不但灵前叩拜，还须对遗属有所慰恤，才能稍舟弥补歉疚的心情。

No one could tell him when the coffin would reach Shanghai.
问到王有龄灵柩到上海的日期，谁也不知道。

That did not matter greatly.
然而也不碍；

There would be a formal reception and roadside offerings when it arrived; any yamen would know, and the date would be easy to discover.
到时候必有迎灵、路祭等等仪式，不管哪个衙门都会知道，不难打听。


The courtesan banquet continued until midnight.
一顿花酒吃到半夜。

Seeing how fascinated the doctor was with the Fourth Miss of Yanchun House, Gu thought of playing matchmaker.
古应春看张医生对艳春老四有些着迷的模样，有心作个『红娘』；

He drew aside the proprietor, a man nicknamed Big Jin, and asked whether the doctor might “borrow a bed for the night.”
将外号『金大块头』的『本家』唤到一边，探问是否可以让张医生『借干铺』？

“Young Master Gu!” Big Jin laughed.
『古大少！』金大块头笑道，

“You are an old hand at pleasure.
『你是「老白相」，

Think for yourself: is that the way things are done?”
想想看可有这规矩？』

“Rules are made by people.
『规矩是人兴出来的。』古应春说，

Let me be frank.
『我跟你说老实话，

I owe this doctor a favor.
这位医生朋友我欠他的情，

Help me out, and leave the rules aside, will you?
你自帮我的忙，不要讲规矩好不好？

Besides, he is from out of town and will not be staying long.
再说，他是外路来的，又住不到多少日子，

He cannot spend weeks observing all your formalities.”
也不能跟你慢慢讲规矩。』

Gu was a lavish patron, and Big Jin wanted to cultivate him.
古应春是花丛阔客，金大块头要拉拢他，

He had inwardly consented as soon as the request was made.
听他一开口，心里便已允许，

But agreeing too readily would cheapen his concession and prevent Gu from appreciating the favor.
但答应得太爽快，未免自贬身价，也不是让古应春见情，

He therefore spoke of the young lady’s reputation, of how people would laugh if a man who had summoned her for the first time that day and observed none of the customary preliminaries were allowed to stay overnight.
所以说了些什么『小姐名声要紧』∶『头一天叫的局，什么「花头」都没有做过，就借干铺，会教人笑话』之类的言语；

In the end, naturally, “For Young Master Gu’s sake, even if I were unwilling, I must agree.”
而到头来是『古大少的面子，不肯也要肯。』

Once that side had consented, however, the other refused.
这面肯了，那面反倒不肯；

In Shanghai’s foreign settlement, Dr Zhang might be a countryman, but in Ningbo he was a person of standing.
张医生到了洋场，算『乡下人』，在宁波也是场面上的人物，

He would not give people grounds to say that he had spent his very first night in Shanghai in a courtesan house.
不肯留个『头一天到上海就住在堂子里』的话柄，

He insisted on going home.
所以坚持要回家。


On returning, he examined Hu again, carrying out all four stages of a physician’s inquiry: inspection, listening and smelling, questioning, and taking the pulse.
一到家，又替胡雪岩看了一回病，『望闻问切』四个字都做到，

He then told Gu and his wife, with great satisfaction, that the patient would certainly be able to get out of bed in ten days.
很高兴地告诉古应春夫妇，说病人十天一定可以起床。

“Then, Dr Zhang,” the Seventh Lady said, “will you do me the honor of staying with us for those ten days?”
『那末，张先生，』七姑奶奶说，『我留张先生住十天，肯不肯赏我一个面子？』

“You are too kind, too kind!”
『言重，言重！』

He looked troubled. “Another ten days would take us to the festival for sending off the Kitchen God.”
张医生面有难色∶『再住十天，就到了送灶的日子了。』

Gu too felt that, with the year drawing so rapidly to its close, forcing a man to remain far from home would be unreasonable and unkind.
古应春也觉得急景凋年，硬留人羁栖异乡，不但强人所难，也不近人情，

He proposed a compromise. “Stay five more days, then.” “Very well, five days.”
所以折衷提议∶『再住五天吧』『好，就住五天。』

The doctor looked a little bashful.
张医生略有些忸怩地说，

“There is another matter in which I may have to ask a considerable favor of you both.”
『我还有件事，恐怕要重托贤伉俪。』

A’qiao, just about to lift the curtain and enter, heard this and turned away at once.
这话正好为要掀门帘进屋的阿巧姐听见，扭头就走；

Gu did not understand and was about to ask, but his wife quickly tugged at his clothes to silence him.
古应春不明白是怎么回事，想开口相问；七姑奶奶机警，抢着悄悄拉了他一把衣服，才将他的话挡了回去。

“Please do not put it that way, Dr Zhang.
『张先生，不要这么说。』

If there is anything within our power, you have only to tell us.
七姑奶奶答道∶『只要我们办得到的事，你尽管吩咐。

You must be tired today.
今天怕累了，

Have some congee, then go to bed.”
吃了粥，请安置吧！』

“I shall not have any congee.
『粥是不吃了；

But yes, I am rather tired.”
累倒真有些累了。』

He sounded somewhat disappointed.
张医生略有些怏怏然。


The Seventh Lady was always sincerely attentive to guests.
七姑奶奶向来待客殷勤诚恳，

She had cooked an excellent pot of congee with fresh sliced fish and insisted that he taste her handiwork.
煮了一锅极道地的鱼生粥，定要请客人试试她的手段；

Besides, she said, there were still things to discuss.
又说还有话要谈。

He could hardly persist in refusing, so they talked over the late supper.
张医生自然没有坚拒之理；于是一面吃宵夜，一面谈正事。

The first important subject was Hangzhou.
第一件大事，就是古应春谈杭州的情形。

The doctor had already heard the details at Yanchun House, so Gu could not properly repeat them at length.
这些话张医生已经在艳春院听过一遍，所以古应春不便再详细复述，

The essential points to tell his wife were that Wang’s death had been confirmed and that Lin Fuxiang was escorting the coffin to Shanghai.
顶要紧的是证实王有龄殉节，以及由林福祥护送灵柩到上海的话，要告诉七姑奶奶。

“Then I was right.
『那就对了！

My idea was sound.”
我的想法不错。』

She turned to the doctor. “Perhaps you do not quite understand the situation yet.
她转脸对张医生说∶『张先生大概还不十分清楚。

Little Uncle and Governor Wang were friends bound to one another in life and death.
我们这位小爷，跟王抚台是生死之交；

To hear that the governor died so tragically must naturally upset him all the more while he is ill.
现在听说王抚台死得这么惨，病中当然更受刺激。

But Little Uncle is exceptionally reasonable and knows how to accept things.
不过我在想，我这位小爷叔，为人最明道理，最看得开；

He had already foreseen that the governor could hardly escape death, so the news will not be entirely unexpected.
而且王抚台非死不可，他也早已看到了的，所以这个消息也不算意外。

Now the coffin is coming to Shanghai and will soon leave for Fujian.
现在王抚台的灵柩到上海，马上要回福建，

If he cannot go and weep before it, he will blame us afterward.
如果他不能到灵前去哭一场，将来反倒会怪我们。

I therefore think we should tell him within the next day or two.
所以我想，不如就在这一两天告诉他。

What do you say, Dr Zhang?”
张先生，你看可以不可以？』


“It is very difficult to judge,” the doctor replied. “Nothing is worse for a sick man than grief.
『这就很难说了。』张医生答道∶『病人最怕遇到伤心的事；

Yet from what you say, perhaps it would do no harm.”
不过照你所说，似乎又不要紧。』

“Yingchun, what do you think?” She turned to her husband.
『应春，』七姑奶奶转脸问道∶『你看呢？』

Gu knew that she had already made up her mind.
古应春最了解妻子，知道她已经拿定了主意，

She was asking only to show respect for his position as her husband in front of their guest.
问这一句，是当着客人的面，表示尊重他做丈夫的身份。

He ought to take the hint—and taking it meant playing his part. “Dr Zhang is naturally cautious.
自己应该知趣。知趣就要凑趣∶『张先生自然要慎重。

But knowing Little Uncle’s temperament, one way might be to tell him plainly and put an end to his hopes.”
以小爷叔的性情来说，索性告诉了他，让他死了心，也是一个办法。』

“Exactly!”
『对！』

The doctor thought this perceptive.
张医生觉得这话有见地，

“His constant preoccupation with Hangzhou is not good for his recovery either.
『胡道台心心念念记挂杭州，于他养病也是不宜的。

But you must prepare him gradually.
不过告诉他这话，要一步一步来，

Do not give him the news too abruptly.”
不要说得太急。』

“Certainly.”
『是的。』

The Seventh Lady now came to her request.
七姑奶奶这时便要提出请求了，

“I imagine that once he knows, there must be an outpouring of grief.
『我在想，告诉了他，难免有一场伤心；

He may suffer a shock, and we shall need you to take particular care of him.
只怕他一时会受震动，要请张先生格外费心。

I may be a woman, Dr Zhang, but I do not like to beat about the bush.
张先生，我虽是女流之辈，做事不喜欢扭扭捏捏，

Let me say it beforehand: if his illness returns, I shall insist on keeping you in Shanghai for the New Year.”
话先说在前面，万一病势反复，我可要硬留张先生在上海过年了。』

What could the doctor say in such circumstances?
此时此地，张医生还能说什么？

He could only offer a wry smile and give a vague assent for the moment.
只好报以苦笑，含含糊糊地先答应下来。


When they had finished the congee, Gu personally escorted the doctor to his room.
等吃完粥，古应春亲送张医生到客房；

The Seventh Lady had arranged it herself: a large brass bed, entirely new bedding, and, as a special mark of honor, a canopy of grey squirrel fur.
是七姑奶奶亲自料理的，大铜床，全新被褥，还特为张了一顶灰鼠皮帐子，以示待客的隆重，

The lavish attention made the doctor quite uneasy.
害得张医生倒大为不安。

They chatted a little longer about where to go sightseeing the next day.
又说了些闲话，谈谈第二天逛些什么地方？

Then, with “Until tomorrow,” Gu returned to the bedroom, where his wife had removed her makeup and was waiting for him.
然后道声『明天见』，古应春回到卧室，七姑奶奶已经卸了妆在等他了。

“Did Dr Zhang enjoy himself tonight?”
『今天张医生高兴不高兴？』

“There was a Fourth Miss at Yanchun House whom he liked very much.
『有个艳春老四，他看了很中意，

I meant to arrange things so that he could stay there.
我本来想替他拉拢，就住在那里。

Everything was settled, but he absolutely refused, so I had to let him have his way.
都已经说好了，张医生一定不肯，只好由他。』

What is behind all this eagerness of yours?
古应春又问，『你这样子热心，总有道理在内吧？

I have been turning it over in my mind and cannot work it out.”
我一直在想，想不通。』

“It is an amusing story.
『说起来有趣。

Do you know what brought him on this journey?”
你晓得张医生这趟，怎么来的？』


Clearly the question had a meaning behind it.
这一问自然有文章 ，

Gu covered his wife’s mouth with his right hand. “Do not tell me.
古应春用右手掩着他妻子的嘴说∶『你不要开口，

Let me think.”
让我想一想。』

An intelligent man needs only a hint.
聪明人一点就透。

Once he began to think in terms of a woman, he immediately remembered A’qiao’s fleeting appearance at the curtain a moment earlier.
古应春只要从女人身上去思索，立刻就想到方才阿巧姐帘前惊鸿一瞥的情；

The doctor’s attentive behavior toward her on his arrival also came back to him.
于是张医生刚到时对阿巧姐处处殷勤的景象，亦都浮现脑际，

Now he understood.
恍然大悟，原来如此！

“Was that the reason?”
『是为了这个？』

He withdrew his hand and bent two fingers to make the sign for seven, hinting at the word qiao, “cleverness.”
他缩回右手，屈起两指。做了个『七』的手势；暗扣着一个『巧』字。

The Seventh Lady looked rather disappointed.
七姑奶奶似乎有些扫兴，

“How tiresome!
『真无趣！』她说，

How did you manage to guess?”
『怎么会让你猜到？』


“Guessing that much tells me nothing.
『猜到这一点没有用处。

Come.”
来，来，』

He drew her down to sit beside him.
他拉着妻子并肩坐下，

“Tell me the whole story.” She told it from beginning to end, omitting no detail, more clearly than anyone who had been present could have done.
『你讲这段新闻来听听。』这段新闻讲得有头有尾，纤细无遗，比身历其境的人还清楚；

Each participant knew only what he or she had witnessed or been told.
因为他们都只知道自己在场或者听说过的一部分，

There were things Xiao was reluctant to say, while A’qiao and Hu had kept much of their thinking to themselves.
萧家骥有些话不便出口；阿巧姐跟胡雪岩的想法，亦颇多保留，

Only before the Seventh Lady had they emptied their hearts, allowing her to grasp the whole truth.
唯有在七姑奶奶面前倾囊而出，反能了解全盘真相。

“That young rogue Jiaji!”
『家骥这个小鬼头！』

七姑奶奶性急的毛病发作了，

『就算我一窍不通，难道小爷叔的话也不对？』

“Of course he is.
『自然不对，

刚刚一场大病，脑筋自然不够用。

象这种「红倌人」，一句话，叫做不甘寂寞！

『照你说，那里头就没有一个能从良的？』

“Even among ten houses, fragrant herbs may be found.

但是不这么做，
