胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant

原著：高阳


CHAPTER FIVE
第五章


People in the foreign settlement, particularly those who spent their time in pleasure and revelry, were accustomed to late nights and late mornings.
住在洋场的人，特别是经常在花天酒地中的，都有迟睡迟起的习惯；

With business to attend to, Gu Yingchun rose comparatively early, but even he did not get out of bed until nine.
古应春因为有生意要照料，起得还算早的，但也要九点钟才下床。

This morning, at eight, a maid knocked at the bedroom door.
这天八点钟就有娘姨来敲房门；

Someone had come from the firm with a message.
说号子里派了人来，有话要说。

“What message?” Gu asked through the window.
『什么话？』古应春隔着窗子问。

“A Third Master Liu has arrived from Hangzhou.
『杭州有位刘三爷来。

He is at the firm.”
人在号子里。』

“Which Third Master Liu?”
『哪个刘三爷？』

Still sleepy, Gu could not think who it was.
睡眼惺松的古应春，一时想不起是谁。

The Seventh Lady, in the inner room, remembered at once.
七姑奶奶在后房却想到了，

Lifting the bed curtains, she said, “Is it not Liu Bucai—Third Master Liu?”
掀开帐子说道∶『不是刘不才刘三爷吗？』

“Him?
『是他？

Surely not.
不会是他！』古应春说，

He is one of our own people.
『刘三爷也是自己人；

He would come straight here.
一来，当然会到这里来，

Why go to the firm?”
跑到号子里去干什么？』

“Madam is right,” the clerk answered from outside the window.
『老板娘的话不错。』号子里的伙计在窗外接口，

“We asked him to come to the house, but he said his clothes were too ragged. He was embarrassed to call.”
『本来是要请刘三爷到家里来的。他说，他身上破破烂烂不好意思来。』

So it really was Liu Bucai.
果然是刘不才！

The unexpected news left Gu bewildered and momentarily speechless.
这个意外的消息，反替古应春带来了迷茫，竟忘了说话。

The Seventh Lady thought faster.
还是七姑奶奶的心思快，

They still knew nothing of the Hu family’s circumstances; some misfortune might have occurred.
胡家的情形还不知道，也许有了什么不幸之事；

If Hu learned that Liu was here, he would insist on seeing him immediately.
如果让胡雪岩知道了，一定立刻要见他，

Face to face, nothing could be concealed from him, and that might be disastrous.
当面锣，对面鼓，什么话都瞒不住他，大是不妥。

She therefore answered for her husband, telling the clerk to return to the firm and say that Gu would be there at once.
因此，她便替丈夫作主，吩咐伙计先回号子，说古应春马上去看他；

She also warned the servants not to let Hu know that Liu had arrived in Shanghai.
同时叮嘱下人，不准在胡雪岩面前透露刘不才已到上海的消息。


“To think that he has come,” Gu said.
『想不到是他来了。』古应春说，

“Will you go with me?”
『你要不要跟我一道去看他。』

“Of course!”
『自然要罗！』

Husband and wife hurried to the firm together in a carriage.
夫妇俩一辆马车赶到号子里；

When they met Liu, all three were silent for a moment.
相见之下，彼此都有片刻的沉默。

Gu and his wife studied him from head to foot.
沉默中，古应春夫妇将刘不才从头看到底，

His clothes were ragged, but his spirits and complexion were quite good.
衣衫虽然褴褛，精神气色都还不错，

He did not look like a man near starvation.
不象是快饿死了的样子。

“Third Uncle Liu!”
『刘三叔！』

The Seventh Lady was the first to speak.
终于是七姑奶奶先开口，

“Are you well?”
『你好吧？』

“Well enough, well enough!”
『还好，还好！』

Liu seemed suddenly to awaken.
刘不才仿佛一下子惊醒过来，

Blinking, he said, “Alive for a second time, and together with you again—of course I am well.”
眨一眨眼说∶『再世做人，又在一起了，自然还好！』

Husband and wife both breathed more easily.
听得这话，古应春夫妇不约而同地松了口气，

“And the Hu family?” she asked.
『胡家呢？』七姑奶奶问道，

“Are they all well?”
『都好吧？』

“Refugee life has been hard.
『逃难苦一点，

The old and young took turns falling ill, but now they have all recovered.”
大大小小轮流生病，现在总算都好了。』

“Ah!”
『啊——！』

She let out a long breath and pressed her palms together in thanksgiving. “Thank Heaven!” Then she added, “But now I do not understand.
七姑奶奶长长舒口气，双手合掌，当胸顶礼∶『谢天谢地。』然后又说∶『不过我倒又不懂了，

Countless people have starved to death in Hangzhou—” She swallowed the rest.
杭州城里饿死的人无其数——。』说到这里，她咽口唾沫，将最后那句话缩了回去。

Her unspoken question was plain: if so many had starved, how had every member of the Hu household survived?
那句话是个疑问∶饿死的人既然无其数，何以胡家上下一个人都没有饿死？

Liu understood, but no brief answer could explain it.
刘不才懂她的意思，但不是一句话所能解释答得了的，

“It was truly the Bodhisattva’s protection.
『真正菩萨保佑！

I could not tell it all in three days and nights.”
要谈起来三天三夜说不尽。』


He changed the subject abruptly. “I hear Xueyan brought grain to Hangzhou but could not get it into the city and returned to Shanghai.
他急转直下地问道∶『听说雪岩运粮到过杭州，不能进城又回上海。

Where is he?”
人呢？』

“He has been very ill and is not fully recovered, though he is out of danger now.”
『他一场大病，还没有好。不过，不要紧了。』

The Seventh Lady spoke apologetically. “Forgive us, Third Uncle Liu, but you must not see him yet.
七姑奶奶歉意地说∶『对不起，刘三叔，你现在还不能跟他见面；

Let us find out exactly what happened first.
等我们把事情问清楚了再说。

Did Governor Wang really die for his duty?”
王抚台是不是真的殉节了？』

“A good death!
『死得好，

A good death!”
死得好！』

Liu, ordinarily so easy-going that nothing could make him take matters seriously, spoke with loud conviction.
凡事吊儿郎当，从没有什么事可以教他认真的刘不才，大声赞叹，

“A death that counted.
『死得有价值。

To be honest, his reputation as an official was not particularly good.
王抚台的官声，说实在的，没有啥好；

Now there can be nothing but good said of him.
这一来就只好不坏了，

Even the rebels admire him.”
连长毛都佩服。』

According to Liu, on the day Hangzhou fell, Li Xiucheng, the Loyal King, rode alone straight to the governor’s yamen.
据刘不才说，杭州城陷那天，『忠王』李秀成单骑直奔巡抚衙门，

Expecting Wang to take his own life, he had hoped to prevent it.
原意是料到王有龄会殉节，想拦阻他不死；

But he was too late.
可是晚了一步，

Dressed in his court robes, Wang had already hanged himself from an osmanthus tree to the right of the main hall.
王有龄已朝服自缢于大堂右面的桂花树下。

Respecting his loyalty, Li had the body taken down and laid to the left of the pavilion by the eastern outer gate.
李秀成敬他忠义，解下尸首，停放在东辕门彭亭左侧，

He found a fine coffin for the burial preparations.
觅来上好棺木盛殓；

Naturally, Wang’s household, old and young alike, was placed under protection.
王家上下老幼，自然置于保护之下。

“The rebels have some humanity after all,” the Seventh Lady said. “Was there not talk of sending the coffin to Shanghai?”
『长毛总算也有点人心。』七姑奶奶问道∶『不是说要拿王抚台的灵柩送到上海来吗？』

“I heard nothing of that.”
『那倒没有听见说起。』


“What of the Manchu quarter?” Gu asked. “General Ruichang must have died for his duty too?”
『满城呢？古应春问∶』将军瑞昌，大概也殉节了？

“The Manchu quarter held out for another three days—” During those days, Li suspended his attacks and sent men to offer terms of surrender.
『』满城在三天以后才破——。『在这三天中，李秀成暂停进攻，派人招降，

The terms were generous.
条件相当宽大，

The bannermen would be allowed to leave freely, taking their portable valuables, and would receive travelling money as well.
准许旗人自由离去，准带随身细软以外，另发川资；

Ruichang was also shown an edict from the Heavenly King granting the Hangzhou bannermen a special pardon.
同时将』天王『特赦杭州旗人的』诏旨『送给瑞昌看，

The intention was to allay their fears, but it had the opposite effect.
目的是想消除他们的疑虑，而效用适得其反。

Perhaps the terms were so generous that they were difficult to believe.
也许是条件太宽大，反令人难以置信。

Besides, defeated officers returning to the banner establishment would certainly be punished.
而且，败军之将归旗，亦必定治罪，

Death would be hard to escape; they would lose the compensation their families might otherwise receive and might even be struck from the banner rolls, leaving their descendants disgraced and without a livelihood.
难逃一死；反倒失去了抚恤，甚至还褫籍，害得子孙不能抬头，无法生活，

Ruichang and his officers therefore agreed never to surrender.
所以瑞昌与部将约定，决不投降。

Once the three days were over, Li ordered an assault.
于是三天一过，李秀成下令攻击，

Every member of the garrison took up arms and resisted fiercely.
驻防旗人，个个上阵，极力抵抗；

The Manchu quarter had a circuit of nine li, five gates, and large hongyi cannon mounted on its walls.
满城周围九里，有五道城门，城上有红衣大炮，

More than three thousand rebels were killed by their fire.
轰死了长毛三千多人，

It was not until the afternoon of the first day of the twelfth month that the quarter fell.
到十二月初一午后城破。

Ruichang drowned himself in a lotus pond.
将军瑞昌投荷花池而死；

The deputy banner commanders Jiechun and Guanfu also took their own lives.
副都统杰纯、关福亦都自戕。

Countless men, women, and children died in fires they themselves had set or by throwing themselves into West Lake.
男女老小纵火自焚以及投西湖而死的，不计其数。


At this point in his account, Liu himself grew frightened and pale.
讲到这里，刘不才自我惊悸，面无人色；

Gu quickly ordered hot tea so that he could recover his breath, then asked about his own experiences.
古应春赶紧叫人倒了热茶来，让他缓一缓气，再问他个人的遭遇。

“I happened to be in Hangzhou when the situation became critical.
『杭州吃紧的时候，我正在那里。

Xueyan discussed it with me.
雪岩跟我商量，

Huzhou was under siege too, so I could not return there for the time being.
湖州亦已被围，总归一时回不去了；

He asked me to escort his family to the three Tianzhu temples for refuge.
托我护送他的家眷到三天竺逃难。

We parted then, and I never saw him again.
从此一别，就没有再见过他；

Later I decided that the temples were unsafe as well.
因为后来看三天竺亦不是好地方，

We kept retreating farther into the countryside.
一步一步往里逃，

Truly, the Bodhisattva protected us.
真正菩萨保佑，

We reached Liuxia.”
逃到留下。』

Liuxia was a place west of Hangzhou.
『留下』是个地名，在杭州西面；

Its name meant “keep it.” According to tradition, when Emperor Gaozong of the Song moved his capital to Hangzhou and surveyed possible sites for a palace, this place pleased him, and he ordered it kept for consideration. Hence the name.
据说当初宋高宗迁都杭州，相度地势，起造宫殿，此处亦曾中意，嘱咐『留下』备选，所以叫做留下。

With its hills, winding paths, streams, and many secluded places, it was well suited to sheltering refugees.
其地多山，峰回泉绕，颇多隐秘之处，是逃难的好去处。

“But there were so many refugees that a market sprang up.
『逃难的人很多，人多成市，

With all those people, there was no seclusion left.
就谈不到隐秘了。

Seeing the danger, I told Xueyan’s wife that we must go farther and deeper into the hills: the more remote and impoverished the place, the better.
我一看情形不妙，跟雪岩夫人说∶』要逃得远，逃得深，越是荒凉穷苦的地方越好。

She had good judgement and agreed.
雪岩夫人很有眼光，说我的话对。

I found a spot deep in the mountains where people scarcely ever went.
我就找到一处深山，真正人迹不到之处；

Best of all, there was a ravine with running water.
最好的是有一道涧，

Once we had water, there was nothing to fear.
有涧就有水，什么都不怕了。

I hired men to build a thatched shelter, only three chi high, with boards laid underneath.
我雇人搭了一座茅棚，只有三尺高，下面铺上水板；

Then I had seven or eight dan of rice, a jar of salted vegetables, and a dozen or so hams carried up.
又运上去七八担米，一缸盐菜，十来条火腿。

You may find it hard to believe: while untold numbers were starving in Hangzhou, we never went a day without solid rice.”
说起来不相信，那时候杭州城里饿死的人，不知道多少。就我们那里没有一天不吃干饭。

“No wonder you do not look as though you have gone hungry,” said the Seventh Lady.
『』怪不得。刘三叔不象没饭吃的样子。『七姑奶奶说，』

“Did the rebels never find you?”
长毛倒没有寻到你们那里？『


“They nearly did.
『差一点点。』刘不才说，

One day I went gambling—” “Wait a moment,” she interrupted. “Were there places to gamble even among the refugees?”
『有一天我去赌钱——』『慢点。』七姑奶奶插嘴问道∶『逃难还有地方赌钱？』

“Not only gambling.
『不但赌钱，

There were singing entertainers too.
还有卖唱的呢！

The market was thriving.”
市面热闹得很。』

It was the refugees who had created that market.
市面是由逃难的人带来的。

At first someone had put up a thatched hut to sell everyday necessities.
起先是有人搭个茅棚，卖些常用的杂物，

It had no business name and was simply called “the little shop.”
没有字号，通称『小店』；

Then it became a tea shop, where people gathered to hear the news.
然后小店成为茶店，作为聚会打听消息的所在；

Refugee life was full of anxiety and boredom; people needed distraction, and the tea shop became a gambling house.
难中岁月，既愁且闷，少不得想个排遣之道，于是茶店又变成赌场。

At first Liu had walked seven or eight li to this new market each day merely to avoid losing contact with the world and to learn what was happening.
刘不才先是不愿与世隔绝，每天走七八里路到那个应运而生的市集中去听听新闻，

Later he went chiefly to satisfy his appetite for gambling.
到后来就专为去过赌瘾，

Pai gow, the treasure game, dice—he played them all.
牌九、做宝、掷骰子，什么都来；

If he could hold the bank, he did; otherwise he played against it.
有庄做，就做庄家，没有庄做就赌下风，

He became one of the gambling house’s mainstays.
成了那家赌场的台柱。


One afternoon he was banking at the small version of pai gow and enjoying extraordinary luck.
这天午后，刘不才摊庄赌小牌九，手气极旺，

Often he turned up a zero-point hand, only to find that the heavily backed opposing hand was also zero, so that he still came out ahead.
往往他翻蹩十，重门也翻蹩十，算起来还有钱赢。

At the height of the game, someone suddenly shouted, “The rebels are coming!”
正赌得兴头时，突然有人喊道∶『长毛来了！』

Liu was reluctant to believe it, having once been deceived by the same cry.
刘不才不大肯相信，因为他上过一回当；

On that occasion, the players had fled in panic and nothing had happened.
有一次也是听说『长毛来了』，赌客仓皇走避，结果无事，

When they returned, however, the gaming table had been stripped bare.
但等回到赌场，台面上已空空如也。

They learned afterwards that someone had raised the false alarm to seize the money.
事后方知，是有人故意捣乱，好抢台面；

Suspecting another attempt to profit from panic, Liu stayed while the others ran, calmly collecting his stakes.
他疑心这一次也是有人想趁火打劫，所以大家逃，他不逃，不慌不忙地收拾起自己的赌注再说。

“Third Master Liu!” the proprietor warned him. “The rebels really are here.”
『刘三爷！』开赌场的过来警告∶『真的是长毛来了。』

Only then did Liu take fright.
这一说刘不才方始着慌，

He hurriedly thrust several dozen taels into his waistband, slung five or six strings of copper cash over his shoulder, and bolted through the door.
匆匆将几十两银子塞入腰际，背起五六串铜钱，拔脚夺门而走。

But it was already too late: two rebels pursued him relentlessly.
然而已经晚了，有两个长毛穷追不舍。

Urgent though the danger was, he kept his head.
刘不才虽急不乱，

His clothes were better than other people’s, and he had copper cash over his shoulder; they would never let him go.
心里在想，自己衣服比别人穿得整齐；肩上又背着铜钱，长毛决不肯放过自己。

If he simply kept running, would he not lead the enemy to the family’s hiding place?
这样一逃一追，到头来岂不是『引鬼进门』？

Once he saw that, he knew what to do.
念头转到此处，对付的办法也就有了；

He pulled a string of cash towards him, undid its slipknot, and sent a thousand coins clattering across the ground.
拉过一串铜钱来，将『串头绳』上的活结，一下扯开，『哗哗』地将一千铜元落得满地；

After a few more strides he did the same again.
然后跑几步，如法炮制。

When all five or six strings had been scattered, the weight was gone and he could run more lightly.
五六串铜钱撒完，肩上的重负全释，脚步就轻快了；

Even so, he dared not take the direct route for fear of revealing their shelter.
然而还是不敢走正路，怕引长毛发现住处，

He made a long circuit and did not get home until evening.
兜了好大一个圈子，到晚上才绕道到家。


“After that, Xueyan’s mother and wife forbade me to go gambling again.
『从那一次以后，胡老太太跟雪岩夫人就不准我再去赌了。

In any case, the market broke up.
其实，市面也就此打散了——

That first party was only a small detachment of rebels which had stumbled upon the place.
那一次是一小队长毛，误打误撞闯到了那里；

There were too few of them to do much.
人数太少，不敢动手。

The next day, or perhaps the day after, a large force came.
第二天，还是第三天，来了大队人马，

They raped, looted, seized people, and finally set the place on fire.
奸淫掳掠外加一把火；

Who knows how many refugees suffered?”
难民遭劫的不知多少？』

Liu’s expression was complicated.
刘不才说到这里，表情相当复杂，

Fear and sorrow still lingered, yet he also looked relieved, even pleased with himself.
余悸余哀都犹在，却又似乎欣慰得意，

“Luckily I saw what was coming.
『亏得我见机！

That was one wager I judged correctly.”
这一宝总算让我看准了。』

Even discussing matters of life and death, he could not shed a gambler’s language.
谈这样的生死大事，仍旧不脱赌徒的口吻，

The Seventh Lady felt both admiration and amusement, but concern predominated. “You never met the rebels again after that?”
七姑奶奶对他又佩服，又好笑，但更多的是关切∶『以后始终没有遇见长毛？』

“No.
『没有！

Several times we heard them, though.
不过好几次听见声音；

Only someone who has lived through it knows how dreadful that constant fear can be.”
提心吊胆的味道，只有尝过的人才晓得真不好受！』


Even now, the days of fear were not entirely over.
然而，此刻提心吊胆的日子，也并不算完全过去。

Once the rebels entered Hangzhou, Li’s discipline kept the usual burning, killing, rape, and robbery from becoming too severe.
长毛进城，由于李秀成的约束，照例会有的烧、杀、奸、抢倒不甚厉害；

Yet the people of Hangzhou were unwilling to submit.
但杭州人不肯从贼，

Men hanged themselves, women threw themselves into wells, and whole families took their own lives in countless numbers.
男的上吊、女的投井、阖家自尽的，不计其数。

This was not entirely an expression of loyalty to the dynasty.
这也不尽是忠义之气使然，

Many had lost all desire to live, for different reasons. Some feared humiliation and suffering.
而是生趣索然；其中又分成几类∶怕受辱吃苦头的是一类；

Some had seen horrors beyond anything they could emotionally endure and sought release.
满目极人间未有之惨，感情上承受不住，愿求解脱的，也是类；

Others had neither clothes nor food and could find no means of prolonging their lives; believing death inevitable, they chose it sooner rather than later.
无衣无食，求苟延残喘而不可得，以为迟早是死，不如早死的，又是一类；

Still others, having survived terrible calamities only to find everything lost in the end, killed themselves in bitter despair.
历尽浩劫，到头来仍不免一场空，于心不甘，愤而自裁的，更是一类。

Among a hundred thousand households, perhaps not one had escaped the catastrophe as the Hu family had.
象胡家这样『跳出劫数外，不在五行中』的；只怕十万人家找不出一家；

Yet now calamity threatened them again.
然而现在却又在劫数中了。

They could no longer remain in their mountain hut, chiefly because their food was exhausted and the whole family had to come down.
荒山茅棚，自然不能再住；最主要的原因是，存粮已罄，不能不全家『出山』；

城里尸臭不可向迩，如果不是严冬，瘟疫早已流行，当然不能再住。

自从胡雪岩平地一声雷，发达起来，更认定是菩萨保佑，

大小庙宇庵堂，只要和尚尼姑上门化缘，必不会空手而回；

三天竺是香火盛地，几座庙宇，无不相熟，

Finding a place to stay was therefore easy enough.
找一处安顿下来，倒也容易。

苦恼的仍旧是粮食。

整个杭州城，全靠李秀成从嘉兴运来两万石米；

如果不包括军食在内，倒也能维持一段时期，

无奈先发军粮，再办平粜，

The benefit to ordinary people was limited.
老百姓的实惠就有限了。

刘不才口中的『王八蛋』叫袁忠清，是钱塘县署理知县。

此人原来是袁甲三部下的一个『勇目』，

After making a windfall from the war, he worked through one of Yuan’s advisers to have his name added to a list of men recommended for honours.
打仗发了笔横财，活动袁甲三的一个幕友，在一次『保案』中将他添上了一个名字，

得了『六品蓝翎』的功名。

后来犯了军令，袁甲三要杀他；

吓得连夜开了小差，逃回江西原籍。
