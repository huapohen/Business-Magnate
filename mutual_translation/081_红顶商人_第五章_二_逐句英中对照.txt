胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第五章（本章第2部分，共3部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


But Yuan Zhongqing’s reputation for shabby conduct had spread.
但是，袁忠清『不够意思』的名声，却已转了出去；

He could no longer get on in Jiangxi.
江西不能再混，

In any case, he had to leave: having been recommended for promotion to county magistrate, he could not be appointed to a vacancy in the same province.
事实上也非走不可，因为保升了知县，不能在本省补缺，

Through contacts at the ministry, he was assigned to Zhejiang to await a post.
托人到部里打点，分发浙江候补。

He had originally hoped for Guangdong, but the intermediary proved less reliable than expected and had the assignment changed to Zhejiang.
袁忠清原来是指望分发广东，却以所托的人，不甚实在，改了分发浙江，

With no alternative, Yuan reported there.
万般无奈，只有『颤到』候补，

This was shortly after the provincial capital had fallen for the first time and been recovered.
那时浙江省城正当初陷收复以后，

Wang Youling was putting all his efforts into preparing its defences: long trenches were dug, batteries added, and rows of barracks built along the walls.
王有龄全力缮修战备，构筑长壕，增设炮台，城上鳞次栉比的营房；

Strong hoisting frames with winding drums worked all day, bringing up firearms and ammunition.
架起极坚固的吊车，安上轴辘，整天不停地储备枪械子药。

Everywhere were bright banners and gleaming weapons.
放眼一望，旗帜鲜明，刀枪雪亮，

The city certainly looked capable of holding out.
看样子是一定守得住了。

Yuan’s spirits revived.
于是袁忠清精神复振，

Working through Provincial Treasurer Linzhi, he actually secured appointment as acting magistrate of Qiantang.
走了藩司麟趾的门路，竟得『挂牌』署理钱塘县。

Hangzhou comprised two counties, Qiantang and Renhe; Qiantang was the senior one, and its magistracy carried particular weight.
杭州城内，钱塘仁和两县，而钱塘是首县。县官分更自不同。

Yuan was calculating and possessed the talents needed behind the scenes.
袁忠清工于心计，只具『内才』；

The magistrate of the senior county, however, needed public accomplishments: a fine appearance, engaging conversation, splendid clothes, smooth manners, and, above all, a generous hand and a gift for entertaining.
首县却是要『外才』的，讲究仪表出众、谈吐有趣、服饰华丽、手段圆滑，最要紧的是出手大方、善于应酬，

Yuan was not the natural choice.
袁忠清本非其选。

But with military affairs so urgent, few high officials passed through, and there was little ceremonial receiving and seeing off to do.
但此时军情紧急，大员过境的绝少，送往迎来的差使不繁，

He could put his own abilities to use.
正可发挥他的所长。

His strength lay in extracting money.
袁忠清的长处就在搞钱；

For that, one needed a pretext; and when defence took precedence over everything, how could pretexts be lacking?
搞钱要有名目，而在这个万事莫如守在急的时候，又何愁找不到名目？

Levies, compulsory donations, requisitioned labour and materials—all could be charged to military needs in accounts no one could untangle.
为了军需，摊派捐献，抓差征料，完全是一笔烂帐；

So long as he satisfied his superiors and did not drive the people into revolt, no one would ask how much he pocketed in between.
只要上面能够交差，下面不激出民变，从中捞多少都没有人会问的。


When Hangzhou was besieged in the ninth month, households ran out of food and everyone grew thin.
到了九月里杭州被围，家家绝粮，人人瘦瘠，

Yuan alone seemed full of vigour.
只有袁忠清似乎精神还很饱满；

Many suspected that he had hidden grain and was secretly feeding himself, but without evidence nothing could be proved.
多疑心他私下藏着米粮，背人『吃独食』，然而事无佐证，莫可究诘。

Naturally, such a man would not sacrifice his life when the city fell.
这样的人，一旦破城，自然不会殉节——

Some even said he had opened the gates to the rebels.
有人说他还是开城门放长毛进城的人；

That too was unsubstantiated.
这一点也无实据，

What was beyond doubt was that, on the second day after Li Xiucheng entered the city, Yuan accepted office under the rebels.
不过李秀成进城的第二天他就受了伪职，却是丝毫不假。

His new title was Military Supervisor of Qiantang, but the work was his old occupation: procuring military supplies.
他受的伪职，名为『钱塘监军』，而干的差使却是『老本行』，替长毛备办军需。

What the rebels most urgently needed was boats.
长毛此时最迫切需要的是船，

On the one hand, their plundered jewels, valuables, antiques, paintings, and calligraphy had to be carried to the Heavenly Capital as offerings to the Heavenly King.
因为一方面掳掠而得的大批珠宝细软、古董字画，要运到『天京』，进献天王；

On the other, grain had to be brought quickly into Hangzhou from the outlying ports.
一方面要从包埠赶粮食到杭州，

Yuan discarded his official hat and feather, took off his rank-badged coat, and dressed like a rebel in a red silk padded jacket with yellow silk wrapped around his head.
所以袁忠清摔掉翎领，脱去补挂，换上红绸棉袄，用一块黄绸子裹领，打扮得跟长毛一样，

Every day he went down to the river, brandishing Li’s command token, to requisition boats.
每天高举李秀成的令箭在江干封船。

Countless refugees were outside the city.
城外难民无数，

Any attractive woman who encountered the insatiably lustful Yuan was in danger of assault.
有姿色的妇女，遇到好色如命的袁忠清，就难保清白了。


“That bastard!” Liu said angrily.
『这个王八蛋！』刘不才愤愤地说，

“He actually went to the Hu residence himself and told the people left to look after it: ‘Hu took several tens of thousands of taels in public money to Shanghai to buy rice.
『居然亲自到胡家，跟留守在那里的人说∶胡某人领了几万银子的公款，到上海去买米，

Why has he not returned?
怎么不回来？

Send him word to bring the amount of rice he owes to Hangzhou at once.
你们带信给他，应该有多少米，赶快运到杭州来。

Otherwise he will suffer for it!’
不然，有他的罪受！

Tell me, is that not a deliberate attempt to make trouble?”
你们想想看，这不是有意找麻烦？』

It certainly was trouble.
这确是个麻烦。

With Yuan’s degraded character and vicious methods, unless precautions were taken promptly he might discover where the family was hiding and ill-treat the old people, women, and children.
照袁忠清这样卑污的人品，毒辣的手段，如果不早作铺排；说不定他就会打听到胡家眷属存身之处，凌辱老少妇孺，

The danger was alarming.
岂不可忧？

“What worries me most is that the bastard can ruin things more easily than he can achieve them.
『顶教人担心的是，这是王八蛋成事不足，败事有余；

If he arrested the family and could be persuaded through connections to release them all, that would be one thing.
如果说他拿胡家大小弄了进去，托到人情，照数释放，倒也还不要紧。

But I fear he may tell the rebels about them and have them arrested, then have no authority to get them freed.
就怕他跟长毛一说，人是抓进去了；要放，他可作不了主。

Once that happened, finding a way out might be harder than climbing to Heaven.”
这一来，要想走条路子，只怕比登天还难。』

Liu’s words, reinforced by an unusually grave expression, filled the Seventh Lady with anxiety.
刘不才这番话，加上难得出现的沉重的脸色，使得七姑奶奶忧心忡忡，

Her usual cheerful decisiveness deserted her.
也失去了平时惯有爽朗明快的词色。

Gu was worried too, naturally.
古应春当然也相当担心；

But he had always been thoughtful and calm, partly through Hu’s influence.
但他一向深沉冷静，一半也是受了胡雪岩的濡染，

He believed that difficulties became less formidable once one ceased to fear them.
总觉得凡事只要不怕难，自然就不难。

This was not the only problem before them.
眼前的难题，不止这一端；

In deciding what was urgent, they must remember that Hangzhou was far away.
要说分出缓急，远在杭州的事，

If disaster had already occurred, anxiety would achieve nothing.
如果已生不测，急也无用。

If there was in fact no danger, frantic attempts to find a remedy might only hasten calamity.
倘或根本不会有何危险，则病不急而乱投医，反倒是自速其祸。

Liu might have accepted this reasoning.
然而这番道理说给刘不才听，或许他能接受；

The Seventh Lady certainly would not.
在七姑奶奶却是怎么样也听不进去的。

To reassure his wife, Gu therefore took the whole burden on himself.
因而他只有大包大揽地先一肩担承了下来，作为安慰妻子的手段。


“It will be all right,” he said, tapping his chest.
『不要紧！不要紧！』他拍一拍胸说，

“I have ways and contacts.
『我有办法；我有路子，

I shall attend to it today.
我今天就去办。

First we must decide something else.”
眼前有件事，先要定个主意。』

That was how to tell Hu the news from Hangzhou.
这件事就是要将杭州的消息，告诉胡雪岩。

His family at the rebels’ mercy and his closest friend dead—these were two griefs he could scarcely bear.
家小陷贼，至交殒命，是他不堪承受的两大伤心之事；

Yet the news that his mother was alive and the whole family safe might counterbalance them.
可是老母健在，合家无恙，这个喜讯，也足以抵消得过，

Gu therefore favoured letting Liu speak to him in person.
所以古应春赞成由刘不才去跟他面谈。

The Seventh Lady agreed, and Liu naturally complied.
七姑奶奶表示同意，刘不才当然依从，

First, however, he wanted a bath, something he had longed for day after day.
不过；他要求先去洗个澡——这是他多少天来，梦寐以思的一种欲望。

“That is easily arranged.”
『那容易。』

She turned to Gu. “Take Third Uncle Liu to the bathhouse.
七姑奶奶对古应春说∶『你先陪刘三叔到澡塘子去；

I shall go home and have a room prepared.”
我回家去收拾间屋子出来。』

“No need, Sister Seven.
『不必，不必！七姐，』刘不才说，

I would rather stay at an inn.
『我还是住客栈，

It gives me more freedom.”
比较自由些。』

“He likes to come and go as he pleases.
『刘三叔喜欢自由自在，

Let him do so,” Gu agreed.
你就让他去。』古应春附和着；

He had another reason: if there were matters that could not be discussed in front of Hu, an inn would be a convenient place to meet Liu privately.
他是另有用意，想到或许有什么不便当着胡雪岩说的话，跟刘不才在客栈里接头，比较方便些。


On the newly laid-out Stone Road they bought a complete set of clothes, shoes, and headgear, from the innermost layer to the outermost.
在新辟的『石路』上，买好从里到外，从头到脚的全套衣衫鞋帽；

The ragged, filthy clothes Liu took off should by rights have gone straight into the rubbish.
照道理说，刘不才脱下来的那身既破且脏的旧衣服，可以丢进垃圾箱里去了。

But he insisted on keeping them.
但是，他却要留着。

“Before this, I never truly understood how hard it is to earn the necessities of life.
『从前，我真正是不知稼穑之艰难，

I have been down on my luck.
虽然也有落魄，

There were days when, after eating lunch, I had no idea where supper would come from.
混到吃了中饭不知夜饭在哪里的日子也有过，

But I never worried, and it never occurred to me to save when I had money.
可是我从来不愁，从没有想过有了钱要省俭些用。

This calamity has changed me.
经过这一场灾难，我变过了。』刘不才说，

I shall keep these clothes as a family treasure.
『这身衣服我要留起来，当作「传家之宝」。

I am not joking.
这不是说笑话，

I want my descendants to know that their ancestor suffered like this.”
我要子孙晓得，他们的祖宗吃过这样子的苦头！』

Gu was surprised.
古应春相当惊异，

“Third Uncle Liu, I never expected you to think in that way.”
『刘三叔，』他说，你有这样子的想法，我倒没有想到。

“Something else shook me too.
『』我也是受了点刺激；

A man must have the will to prove himself.
想想一个人真要争气。『刘不才说，』

The sights on the road from Tianzhu into the city were heartbreaking; there is no need to describe them.
从天竺进城，伤心惨目，自不必说，

But nothing is as frightening as the human heart.
不过什么东西可怕，都不如人心可怕。

Xueyan did a great deal for the place.
雪岩在地方上，总算也很出过一番力的，

Yet now not one person in ten has a good word for him.
哪知道现在说他好的，十个之中没有一个。

I cannot accept it.
我实在不大服气。

If he really falls, or can never return to Hangzhou, he will be wronged for the rest of his life and never clear his name.
如果雪岩真的垮了下来，或者杭州也真的回不去了，那就冤屈一辈子，坏名誉也不能洗刷。

One day, when the city is recovered and he returns as influential as before, I should like to hear what those people have to say.”
到有一天光复，雪岩依旧象从前那样神气，回到杭州，我倒要看看那班人又是怎么个说法？

This complaint struck Gu as unusual.
『这是一番牢骚，古应春颇有异样的感觉。

In all the time he had known Liu, he had seldom heard him grumble.
从他认识刘不才以来，就难得听他发牢骚；

On the rare occasions when he did, it was with detached, sardonic humour.
偶尔那么一两次，也总是出以冷隽嘲弄的口吻，

This was the first time Gu had heard such earnest indignation.
象这样很认真的愤激之词，还是第一次听到。

Thinking over his words, he began to sense something behind them. “Third Uncle Liu, you seem to be keeping something back.”
再将他话中的意思，好好咀嚼了一会，终于辨出一点味道来了∶『刘三叔，』他试探着问，『你好象还有什么话，藏在肚子里似的。』

Liu looked up sharply and stared at him.
『刘不才倏然抬眼，怔怔地望着古应春，

After a long pause, he nodded deeply.
好半晌才深深点头，』

“You have guessed correctly, Brother Yingchun.
应春兄，你猜对了。

There are things I ought to discuss with you.
我是还有几句话，倒真应该跟你谈才是。

Xueyan’s position is very unfavourable—” As Liu continued, Gu learned that Hu had become the target of attacks from every direction.
雪岩的处境很不利——。『听他谈了下去，才知道胡雪岩竟成众矢之的。

Some said he had used the purchase of rice as a pretext to abscond with a substantial sum from the provincial treasury.
有人说他借购米为名，骗走了藩库的一笔公款，为数可观；

Others alleged that Wang’s savings from office had all been invested through Hu, who, now that Wang was dead and could not testify, had appropriated them.
有人说王有龄的宦囊所识，都由胡雪岩替他营运，如今死无对证，已遭吞没。

There were further accusations that he had used public business for private profit and that his reputation for benevolence was false.
此外还有人说他如何假公济私；如何虚有善名；

They portrayed him as a thoroughly unscrupulous villain.
将他形容成一个百分之百的奸恶小人。


“These are people who were always jealous of him, or who suffered at Governor Wang’s hands and are taking their resentment out on him.
『这都是平时妒嫉雪岩的人，或者在王雪公手里吃过亏的迁怒到他头上。

When mad dogs snap, keep out of their way.
疯狗乱咬，避开就是；

He could have ignored them.
本来可以不必理他们，

But now the rebels have taken an interest in him too, and that makes trouble.”
哪知长毛也看中了雪岩，这就麻烦了。』

Why should the rebels want Hu?
越说越奇，如何长毛又看中胡雪岩？

Gu was greatly puzzled, though the explanation made it understandable enough.
古应春大感不解；不过一说破也就无足为奇了；

“Xueyan always liked taking the lead in good works.
『雪岩向来喜欢出头做好事，

Let us be honest: part of it was enthusiasm and a liking for lively affairs; part was a desire for reputation.
我们凭良心说，一半他热心好热闹；一半也是咕名钓誉。

Li Xiucheng heard about him and wants him to come forward and help restore order after the fighting.
李秀成打听到了，想找雪岩出来替他办善后。

That makes the jealousy worse.
这一来就越发遭忌；

There is already a group exploiting the situation.
原来有批人在搞，

If Xueyan appears, their opportunities will be gone.
如果雪岩一出面，就没得那批人好搞的，

First they sent a bastard like Yuan to threaten him.
所以第一步由袁忠清那样的王八蛋来恐吓；

That was bad enough.
这也还罢了，

Their next move is vicious.
第二步手段真毒辣了。

They are said to be planning to stir up officials in the capital to accuse him of taking Zhejiang’s public rice-purchasing funds and thereby obstructing supplies for the army and the people.
据说，那批人在筹划鼓动京官要告雪岩，说他骗走浙江购米的公款，贻误军需民食，

They want an imperial order for his investigation.”
请朝廷降旨查办。』

Gu changed colour.
听到这里，古应春大惊失色，

“How could they say such a thing?
『这，从何说起？

They mean to destroy his family and his life!”
不是要害他家破亡吗？』

He shook his head.
他大摇其头，

“But I do not understand.
『不过我又不懂，

If an imperial investigation makes it impossible for Little Uncle to remain in Shanghai, he will have no choice but to turn to the rebels.
果然降旨查办，逼得小爷叔在上海存身不住，只好投到长毛那里，

How would that benefit them?”
于他们又有何好处？』

“There is more.
『不要忙，还有话。』刘不才说，

They have also spread word that, if he returns to Hangzhou, they will beat gongs, summon a crowd, and settle accounts with him.”
『他们又放出风声来了，说是胡雪岩不回杭州便罢，一回杭州，要鸣锣聚众，跟他好好算帐。』

“What accounts?”
『算什么帐？』

“Who knows?
『哪晓得他们算什么帐？

The poison lies in summoning a crowd.
这句话毒在「鸣锣聚众」四个字上头；

Once they create a scene like that, he will be a rat crossing the street, with everyone shouting to strike him.”
真的搞成那样的局面，雪岩就变成过街老鼠了，人人喊打！』

Gu tapped his temple and frowned. “You have confused me.
古应春敲敲额角，『刘三叔，』他紧皱着眉着∶『你的话拿我搞糊涂了，

They forbid him to return, yet they want to make Shanghai uninhabitable for him and force him to join the rebels.
一方面不准他回去；一方面又逼得他在上海不能住，非投长毛不可，

What exactly do they want?
那末他们到底要怎么办呢？

Surely they cannot expect to drive him to hang himself.
莫非真要逼人上吊，

That would not be so easy.”
只怕没有那样容易吧？』


“Of course not.
『当然。

If he let them drive him into a corner, would he still be Hu Xueyan?
雪岩要让他们逼得走投无路，还能成为胡雪岩？

They know it cannot be done.
他们也知道这是办不到的；

They want to force one promise out of him: ‘Leave me alone, and I shall never interfere with your affairs.’
目的是想逼出雪岩一句话；你们饶了我，我决不会来坏你们的事。

Do you think he will say it?”
应春兄，你想雪岩肯不肯说这句话？』

“Whether he wishes to or not, he must.
『不肯也得肯，

His whole family is at stake.”
一家老少，关系太重了。』

“True.
『话是不错。

But there is another difficulty.”
但是另外又有一层难处。』

That difficulty was a knot that could not be untied.
这层难处是个不解的结，

Chen Bingwen, an able subordinate of Li’s who in practice controlled civil affairs throughout Zhejiang, found the work of restoring order so troublesome that he was determined to enlist Hu.
李秀成的一个得力部下，实际上掌握浙江全省政务的陈炳文，因为善后工作棘手，一定要胡雪岩出头来办事。

He was said to have found a senior clerk at Fukang Bank and told him to carry the message.
据说已经找到阜康钱庄的档手，嘱咐他转言。

Liu reckoned that the man would arrive in Shanghai within two or three days.
照刘不才判断，也就在这两三天之内，会到上海。

“Then we cannot keep it from Little Uncle,” Gu said, feeling how difficult the situation was.
『照这样说，是瞒不住我这位小爷叔的了。』古应春觉得情势棘手，

“You have seen it for yourself and must have thought about it these past few days.
问刘不才说∶『你是身历其境的人，这几天总也想过，

Is there any way out?”
有什么解救之方？』

“Of course I have thought about it.
『我当然想过。

There is only one way to protect the whole family. But—” Liu shook his head.
要保全家老小，只有一条路∶不过——。』刘不才摇摇头说，

“You will not approve.”
『说出来你不会赞成。』

“Tell me anyway.”
『说说何妨。』

“It is plain enough.
『事情明摆在那里，

There is only one word for it: go.
只有一个字∶去！

Frankly, if Xueyan really returned to Hangzhou, what could those people do to him?”
说老实话，雪岩真的回杭州去了，那班人拿他又有什么办法？』


Gu strongly disagreed.
古应春大不以为然。

But Liu had already anticipated his disapproval, which made it difficult to reproach him.
但因刘不才言之在先，料他不会赞成；他倒不便说什么责备的话了。

“Third Uncle Liu,” he said slowly, “we must meet the immediate danger, but we must also think about the years ahead.
『刘三叔，』他慢吞吞地说∶『眼前的急难要应付，将来的日子也不能不想一想。

I believe Little Uncle must decide this himself.”
我看，这件事，只有让小爷叔自己去定主意了。』

Bringing news that the whole family was safe also meant bringing confirmation of Wang’s death.
带来了全家无恙的喜讯，也就等于带来了王有龄殉难的噩耗；

Liu did not mention Wang; the silence said everything.
刘不才不提王有龄，真所谓『尽在不言中』，

Tears ran down Hu’s face, but the grief was bearable.
胡雪岩双泪交流，但哀痛还能承受得住，

This end had long been expected.
因为王有龄这样的下场，原在意中，

More than a month earlier, his bow from the boat on the Qiantang River had been a final farewell.
一个多月前，钱塘江中一拜，遥别也就是永诀；

The sharpest moment of sorrow was already past.
最伤心的时刻已经过去了。

What of Wang’s surviving family?
王有龄的遗属呢？

He wanted to ask, but feared hearing something so tragic that he hesitated to speak.
他想问，却又怕问出来一片悲惨的情形，有些不敢开口。

The Seventh Lady, meanwhile, deliberately steered the conversation towards reassurance.
而七姑奶奶则是有意要谈能教人宽心的事，

Beginning with Hu’s mother, she asked about every member of the family in turn, giving him still less opportunity to ask.
特意将胡家从老太太起，一个个挨次问到；这就越发没有机会让胡雪岩开口了。

They talked until supper.
谈到吃晚饭，

Dr Zhang returned just then, and after introductions they all sat down to eat and drink.
正好张医生回来，引见过后，同桌共饮；

He and Liu were in the same line of business, both having connections with medicine shops, which gave them an immediate sense of fellowship.
他们两人算是开药店的开行，彼此都别有亲切之感，

They talked readily.
所以谈得很投机。

After the meal, Gu specially asked the doctor to examine Hu again.
饭后，古应春特为又请张医生替胡雪岩去诊察；

Perhaps because of the good news, his spirits and vitality were stronger; his condition had improved even since the morning’s examination.
也许是因为有了喜讯的缘故，神旺气健，比上午诊脉时又有了进境。


“There is another very troubling matter we must discuss with him,” Gu asked the doctor quietly.
『还有件很伤脑筋的事要跟病人谈。』古应春悄悄问张医生，

“Would that be bad for his condition?”
『不知道对他的病势相宜不相宜？』

“Nothing troubling is good for a patient.
『伤脑筋的事，没有对病人相宜的。

But he is an unusual man.
不过，他的为人与众不同，

If he can bear the shock, it need not harm him.”
经得起刺激，也就不要紧了。』

Gu therefore decided to conceal it no longer—from Hu, at least.
既然如此，古应春便不再瞒——

It was his wife who must not hear.
要瞒住的倒是他妻子；

Once she had gone to the bedroom to see the child, he and Liu explained the threats from Hangzhou, gently but in full detail.
所以等七姑奶奶回卧房去看孩子时，他才跟刘不才将杭州对胡雪岩种种不利的情形，很委婉地，但也很详细地说了出来。

Hu remained composed, though his face was grave.
胡雪岩很沉着，脸色当然也相当沉重。

When they had finished, he sighed. “Disorder corrupts men’s hearts.
听完，叹口气∶『乱世会坏心术。

It is hardly surprising.
也难怪，

In times like these, a man who insists on reason and loyalty is liable to suffer for it.
这个时候哪个要讲道理，讲义气，只有自己吃亏。

But interests and consequences can still be discussed.”
不过，还可以讲利害。』

His tone suggested that he already saw a solution.
听这口气，胡雪岩似乎已有办法，

Gu asked at once, “Little Uncle, there is no time to lose.
古应春随即问道∶『小爷叔，事不宜迟，

Whatever you decide, it must be done quickly.”
不管定的什么主意，要做得快！』

“No hurry.
『不要紧，

Take it slowly and keep your temper!”
「尽慢不动气」！』

That Hu could still make so light a remark at such a moment irritated Gu a little.
到这时候，胡雪岩居然还有心思说这样轻松的俏皮话，古应春倒有点不大服气了，

“You really do not seem concerned,” he said with mild reproach.
『看样子，小爷叔倒真是不在乎！』他微带不满地说，

“Have you some miraculous strategy?”
『莫非真的有什么神机妙算？』

“Nothing miraculous.
『不是啥神机妙算！

It is quite plain.
事情摆明在那里，

Since they have sent a man from my bank with a message, naturally they must wait for a reply before deciding what to do next.
他们既然叫我钱庄里的人来传话；当然要等有了回信，是好是歹，再作道理。

The man has not even arrived.
现在人还没有到，

Why the panic?”
急什么？』

Gu could not help admiring him.
听得这一说，古应春实在不能不佩服；

The reasoning was simple; his own agitation had prevented him from seeing it.
原是极浅的道理，只为方寸一乱，看不真切。

Such composure was easy to praise but difficult to maintain when matters came to a crisis.
这一点功夫，说来容易，临事却不易做到；

It was precisely where Hu excelled.
正就是胡雪岩过人的长处。


“Very well!”
『那好！』

Gu smiled.
古应春笑道，

“Now that you have explained it, I feel easier.
『听小爷叔一说破，我也放心了。

We can discuss it calmly.”
就慢慢商量吧。』

His sudden tension and release revealed how deeply he shared his friend’s distress.
急人之急的义气，都在他这一张一弛的神态中表露无遗。

This greatly comforted Hu and gave him fresh confidence.
这在胡雪岩是个极大的安慰；也激起了更多的信心，

He spoke more calmly still.
因而语气就越发从容了。

“As for Yuan Zhongqing, I can see right through him.
『那个袁忠清，他的五脏六腑，我都看得见；

He is a clay Bodhisattva crossing a river—hard pressed to save himself.
他是「泥菩萨过江，自身难保」，

He will not dare stir up too much trouble.
绝不敢多事。

The others must think carefully too.
别的人呢，都要仔细想一想，

If they really make difficulties for my family, they know I am not a man to be trifled with.
如果真的跟我家眷为难，也知道我不是好惹的人。』

They will not push me too far.
胡雪岩说∶『他们不会逼我的！

It would do them no good.
逼急了我，于他们没有好处∶第一，

First, I could return to Hangzhou.
我可以回杭州，

The rebels want me and would listen to me.
长毛要我，就会听我的话，

Those people must consider whether they could prevail against me.
他们自己要想想，斗得过我，斗不过我。

Second, if I stay here, they have relatives and close connections in Shanghai.
第二，如果我不回杭州；他们总也有亲人至戚在上海，

They must fear retaliation.
防我要报复。

Third—but there is no need to speak of that.
第三——那就不必去说它了；

It concerns the future.”
是将来的话。』

Gu nevertheless pressed him. “What about the future?”
古应春却偏要打听∶『将来怎么样？』

“We are bound to meet again someday.
『将来，总有见面的日子，

They should leave themselves room to do so.
要留个余地。

One must not carry things to extremes.
为人不可太绝；

Even now, everyone is saying how bad I am.
就拿眼前来说，现在大家都说我如何如何不好，

But if they harm my family, they will put themselves in the wrong.
如果他们为难我的家眷，就变成他们不对了。

They will turn a seemingly justified position into an unjustifiable one.
有理变成无理，

Anyone with a little sense will avoid that.”
稍为聪明的人，不肯做这样的事。』

Gu could not agree with the last argument.
这一点古应春不能同意，

Leaving room for future encounters seemed excessively scrupulous.
留个相见余地的话，也未免太迂，

The first two reasons, however, were quite enough.
不过仅是前两点的理由也尽够了。

“Tell us your plan, Little Uncle.”
古应春便催着他说∶『小爷叔，你说你的办法！』


“My plan is to make a bargain.
『我的办法是做一笔交易。

If they do not want me back in Hangzhou, very well.
他们不愿意我回杭州，可以；

I shall not compete with them.
我不但不跟他们去争，

I shall even do them a favour: when the government troops recover the city, I shall find a way to protect them.
而且要放点交情给他们，有朝一日，官军光复杭州，我自有保护他们的办法。

In return, they must help me now and get my family out of Hangzhou.”
不过，眼前他们要替我想办法；拿我的家眷送出杭州。』

Could such a bargain really be made?
这样的一笔交易是不是做得成？

Gu was doubtful.
古应春颇为怀疑；

He said nothing, merely looking at Liu to hear what he thought.
因而默然不语，只望着刘不才，想听他的意见。
