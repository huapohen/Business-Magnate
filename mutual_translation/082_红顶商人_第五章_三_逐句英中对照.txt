胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第五章（本章第3部分，共3部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


Liu, however, was greatly interested.
刘不才却对他的话大感兴趣，

“That might work,” he said.
『这倒是个办法。』他说，

“As I see it, they want to eat the mutton without enduring its smell.
『照我看，那批人又想吃羊肉，又怕羊骚臭；

They are afraid that, once the government troops recover the city, they will be called to account.
怕将来官军光复了，跟他们算帐。

If you really can protect them, they will be willing to do as we ask.
如果真的有保护他们的把握，那批人肯照我们的办法做的。

But mere words will not suffice.”
不过，空口说白话可不行。』

“For the moment, words are all we have.
『现在当然只有空口说白话；

If the offer appeals to them and is something we can fulfil, they will believe it.”
话要动听，能够做得到，他们自然会相信。』

Hu paused. “Third Uncle, I must trouble you to make another journey. They would find it harder to believe anyone else.”
胡雪岩停了一下说∶『三叔，这件事只有你辛苦，再去一趟∶因为别人去说，他们不大容易相信。』

“Of course I shall go.
『这还用说？自然是我去。

What shall I say to them?”
你说，跟他们怎么个讲法。』

“A little boasting will be necessary.”
『当然要吹点牛。』

Hu broke off. “Let me think it over properly.”
胡雪岩停了下来∶『等我好好想一想。』

He thought for a long time—or perhaps put the matter aside for the moment.
这一想想了好多时候，或者是暂且丢开此事；

At any rate, he said no more about it.
总而言之，不见他再谈起，

Instead he asked about Hangzhou, showing concern for every trifling detail.
尽自问着杭州的情形，琐琐屑屑，无不关怀。

He had a wide acquaintance, but of the people he asked after, most had died in the calamity or vanished without trace.
雪岩的交游甚广，但问起熟人，不是殉难，就是下落不明，

Fewer than one in ten were known to have survived.
存者十不得一。

Even Gu, who had no personal connection with them, listened in deep distress.
连不相干的古应春，都听得凄怆不止。


After ten o’clock, the strain of the journey by land and water, followed by so much uninterrupted talking, had exhausted even Liu’s vigorous constitution.
到得十点多种，刘不才一路车船劳顿，又是说话没有停过，再好的精神也支持不住了。

Gu urged him to forget about returning to the inn and get a good night’s rest first.
古应春例劝他不必再住客栈，先好好睡一觉再说；

Liu agreed.
刘不才依从，

A maid of the household attended to him, and he went to bed.
由古家的丫头侍候着，上床休息。


Hu was still full of energy.
胡雪岩的精神却还很好，

“Gu,” he said, beckoning his friend to sit by the bed, “I do not use underhand methods against people.
『老古，』他招招手让古应春坐在床前，低声说道∶『我对人不用不光明的手段，

This time I must make an exception.
这一次要做它一次一百零一回的买卖，

My whole family is in the hands of those bastards; I cannot afford to leave myself unprotected.
全家大小在那班王八蛋手里，不能不防他们一着。

I intend to lay a fuse.
我现在要埋一条药线在那里；

If all goes well, well and good.
好便好，

If it does not, I shall light it and blast the devils out of their comfort.
搞得不好，我点上药线轰他娘的，教他们也不得安逸。

Now that I have explained, you know what is in my mind.
话说明了，你心里也有数了；

I need you to take some trouble over an official dispatch for me.”
要劳你的神，替我做一件公事。』

He might consider himself to have explained, but Gu was utterly bewildered.
他是『话说明了』，古应春却如丈二金刚摸不着头脑，

“Little Uncle,” he said, frowning, “I still have no idea what you mean.
『小爷叔，』他皱着眉说，『我还莫名其妙；

What fuse?
什么药线，

What official dispatch?”
什么公事？』


“The dispatch is the fuse, and the fuse is the dispatch,” Hu replied. “It is to be addressed to Governor-General Qing at the office of the governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang. I shall write in my capacity as a circuit intendant awaiting appointment in Zhejiang and a member of the militia bureau, commissioned by Governor Wang to arrange military supplies, food for the people, and local relief.
『公事就是药线，药线就是公事。』胡雪岩说∶『这件公事，是以我浙江候补道兼团练局委员，奉王抚台委派，筹划浙江军需民食，以及地方赈济事宜的身分，报给闽浙总督衙门庆制军。

Begin by explaining that, before his death, Mr Xuexuan feared that reinforcements might not arrive and Hangzhou might prove impossible to hold.
公事上要说明，王雪公生前就顾虑援兵不到，杭州恐怕保不住，

He expressly charged me with this duty: he himself was determined to die with the city and repay the court with his life, but the people of Hangzhou must not be abandoned.
特意嘱咐我，他是决定城亡人亡，一死报答朝廷；但是杭州的百姓，不可不顾，

I held no local administrative post and bore no responsibility for defending the territory.
因为我不是地方官，并无守土之责，

Therefore, should Hangzhou fall, I must remember my native place and find ways of protecting its people.
所以，万一杭州沦陷，必得顾念家乡，想办法保护地方百姓。

That is the first section.”
这是第一段。』

Gu listened closely and began to understand how Hu meant to proceed.
古应春很仔细地听着，已理会得胡雪岩入手的意思，

“The second section will presumably describe your attempt to bring rice to Hangzhou and your inability to enter the city?”
并即说道∶『第二段当然是叙你运粮到杭州，不能进城的情形？』

“Exactly.
『对！

But there is no need to mention the diversion to Ningbo.”
不过转道宁波这一层不必提。』

Hu paused briefly.
胡雪岩略停一下又说，

“Now comes the third and most important section.
『现在要叙顶要紧的第三段，

It must say this: since I am in Shanghai and cannot return to Hangzhou, I have sent someone to contact such-and-such persons, asking them to protect the inhabitants and secretly prepare to assist the government troops when a favourable opportunity arises upon their arrival.
要这样说法∶我因为人在上海，不能回杭州，已经派人跟某某人、某某人联络，请他们保护地方百姓，并且暗中布置，以便官军一到，可以相机策应。

These men are all upright local gentlemen, loyal at heart.
这批人都是地方公正士绅，秉心忠义，

At present they are trapped in the city and cannot act freely.
目前身陷城中，不由自主；

When Hangzhou is recovered, whatever offices they may have held under the rebels must not be counted against them.
将来收复杭州，不但不能论他们在长毛那里干过什么职司，

On the contrary, they should be generously rewarded.”
而且要大大地奖励他们。』

“Ah!”
『啊，啊！』

Gu nodded deeply.
古应春深深点头，

“I understand.
『我懂了，我懂了，

You are leaving them a way out for the future.”
这就是替他们的将来留个退步。』


“Precisely.
『对了。

We ought to wait for Governor-General Qing’s endorsement, but he is in Fuzhou and that cannot be obtained quickly.
这道公事要等庆制军的批示，他人在福州，一时办不到；

So we must improvise.
所以要来个变通办法，

While submitting the report to him, I shall also ask the governor of Jiangsu to forward an official communication to his office and give me a reply reciting my original report in full.
一方面呈报庆制军，一方面请江苏巡抚衙门代咨闽浙总督衙门，同时给我个复文，拿我的原文都叙在里头，

I can then show it to those men.”
我好给他们看。』

Gu reflected.
『嗯、嗯！』古应春想了一下，

“But what did you mean when you said the dispatch was the fuse?”
记起一句话∶『那么什么叫「公事就是药线」呢？』

“Do you still not see?
『这你还不懂？』

Think about the passage concerning assistance to the government troops.”
胡雪岩提醒他说∶『你先从相机策应官军这句话上去想，就懂了。』

A hint was enough for a man as shrewd as Gu.
真所谓『光棍一点就透』，古应春恍然大悟，

If those people refused to cooperate, or actually harmed Hu’s family, Hu could retaliate with this very dispatch.
如果那批人不肯就范，甚至真个不利于胡家眷属；胡雪岩就可用这件公事作为报复，

He could inform the rebels that the men were in league with the Qing forces, producing the reply from the governor of Jiangsu as conclusive evidence.
向长毛告密，说这班人勾结清军，江苏巡抚衙门的回文，便是铁证。

The consequences were easy to imagine.
那一来，后果就可想而知了。

It was a ruthless move.
这一着实在狠。

But it was intended for retaliation, and might serve as protection in itself.
但原是为了报复，甚至可以作为防卫；

If the men understood that the dispatch was a fuse which, once lit, could blow them to pieces, they would not dare act rashly.
如果那批人了解到这道公事是是一根一点便可轰发火药，炸得粉身碎骨的药线，自然不敢轻举妄动。

“Little Uncle,” Gu exclaimed in admiration, “you have found a brilliant move in a position that looked completely lost!
『小爷叔！』古应春赞叹着说『真正「死棋肚子里出仙着」；

How did you think of it?”
这一着，亏你怎么想出来的？』


“I did not invent it.
『也不是我发明的。

I have merely adapted a method used before.
我不过拿人家用过的办法，变通一下子。

In fact, I owe Mr Xuexuan my thanks.
说起来，还要谢谢王雪公，

He once told me a story from his own province.
他讲过一个故事给我听；这个故事出在他们家乡，

In the Kangxi reign there was a Minister Li, a very famous man in Fujian, apparently.
康熙年间有位李中堂，据说在福建名气大得很，

A fellow graduate of his, Hanlin Academician Chen, became his sworn enemy.”
他的同年陈翰林跟他有段生死不解的仇——。』

This was the story Wang had told him. Minister Li came from Anxi in Fujian; Academician Chen came from Fuzhou.
王有龄告诉胡雪岩的故事如此∶这位李中堂是福建安溪人，他的同年陈翰林是福州人。

When their period of training at the Hanlin Academy ended, the two took leave and travelled home together.
这年翰林散馆，两个人请假结伴回乡。

Not long afterwards came the Revolt of the Three Feudatories.
不久就有三藩之乱，

Geng Jingzhong responded to Wu Sangui’s call and rebelled in Fuzhou, establishing an administration and appointing officials.
耿精忠响应吴三桂，在福州也叛变了，开府设官，

Chen was compelled to accept an office under him.
陈翰林被迫受了伪职。

Li saw the opportunity with keen interest and also wanted to seek a post in Fuzhou.
李中堂见猎心喜，也想到福州讨个一官半职。

Chen, however, doubted that Geng’s venture would succeed and advised him against it.
而陈翰林却看出耿精忠恐怕不成气候，便劝李中堂不必如此。

The two then closeted themselves together and devised a plan.
而且两个人闭门密谈，定下一计，

Li would write a secret memorial setting out a strategy and urging the court to send a large army into Fujian at once.
由李中堂写下一道密疏，指陈方略，请朝廷速派大兵入闽。

The memorial was sealed in a pellet of wax.
这道密疏封在蜡丸之中，

A messenger from Li’s household carried it through Jiangxi to the capital, where a fellow provincial was asked to submit it to the emperor.
由李家派人取道江西入京，请同乡代为奏达御前。


“They meant to come out well whichever side won,” Hu continued. “Li and Chen agreed that, if the imperial troops entered Fujian and Geng fell, Li would of course have performed a great service.
『这是「刀切豆腐两面光」的打算。』胡雪岩说∶『李中堂与陈翰林约定，如果朝廷大兵到福建，耿精忠垮台，李中堂当然就是大大的功臣，

He could then clear Chen’s name, explaining that Chen had joined the rebels solely to discover their secrets and assist the government forces—” “Ah, excellent!
那时候他就可以替陈翰林洗刷，说他投贼完全是为了要打探机密，策应官军——。』『啊、啊，妙！

And if Geng succeeded, no one would know of Li’s secret memorial.
如果耿精忠成了功，李中堂这首密疏，根本没有人知道；

Chen could still recommend him for a fine position under the new regime.
陈翰林依旧可以保荐他成为新贵。

Was that the plan?”
是不是这样的打算？』

“Exactly.”
『一点不错。』

“What happened afterwards, then?” Gu asked with interest. “How did they become mortal enemies?”
『那末后来呢？』古应春很感兴趣地问∶『怎么说是成了生死不解的冤家？』

“Because Li was a scoundrel who betrayed his friend.
『就为李中堂不是东西，出卖朋友。

Geng fell, the court recovered Fujian, and the rebels were to be punished.
耿精忠垮台，朝廷收复福建，要办叛逆的罪；

Li, secure in his own success, did nothing to clear Chen, who was sent into military exile beyond the pass.”
李中堂自己得意了，竟不替他洗刷。害得陈翰林充军到关外。』

Hu went on, “I am now borrowing their method.
胡雪岩说，『我现在仿照他们的办法，

I only hope those people have the sense to cooperate.
但愿那批人很识相，

The means of clearing their names that I am providing will certainly be useful to them one day.”
我替他们留下的这条洗刷的路子，将来一定有用。』

“I understand your intentions completely, Little Uncle.
『对！小爷叔的意思，我完全懂了；

I shall prepare the dispatch for you tonight.”
这道公事我连夜替你预备起来。』


“There is no hurry.
『不忙。

Tomorrow will be soon enough,” Hu said.
明天动笔也不迟。』胡雪岩说，

“There is something else I must discuss with you first.”
『我还有件事要先跟你商量。』

This concerned provision for Wang after his death: his reputation and his property.
这件事是为王有龄身后打算，自不外名利两字。

His savings from office were not exceptionally large, but neither could it be said that he had taken nothing. As the saying went, even an honest prefect might save a hundred thousand taels in three years.
王有龄的宦囊虽不太丰，却决不能说是一清如水∶『三年清知府，十万雪花银』，

Officials had many sources of income, including surpluses from grain taxation and the customary allowance for wastage when small pieces of silver, collected in commutation of tribute grain, were melted down into official ingots weighing fifty taels each.
许多收入象征粮的『羡余』；漕粮折实，碎角子熔铸为五十两银子一个的『官宝』，照例要加收的『火耗』，

In the Yongzheng reign, such receipts had been brought into the open and formally designated as allowances for the maintenance of integrity.
在雍正年间就已『化暗为明』，明定为地方官的『养廉银』。

There were also gifts at the New Year, the Dragon Boat Festival, and the Mid-Autumn Festival, and on the birthdays of the official and his wife.
此外『三节两寿』——过年、端午、中秋三节；本人及太太的两个生日，

Subordinates invariably sent them, in amounts largely fixed by custom.
属员必有馈敬，而且数目亦大致有定规，

These were all forms of income permitted by the court.
这都是朝廷所许的收入。


Wang’s savings had naturally been entrusted to Hu for investment.
王有龄的积蓄，当然是交给胡雪岩营运；

What Hu now needed to discuss was how to account for the money that had passed through his hands.
他现在要跟古应春商议的，就因为经手的款子，要有个交代。

“They say I have Mr Xuexuan’s money.
『他们说王雪公有钱在我手里，

Of course I have.
这是当然的。

With the friendship there was between us, I would never dream of keeping it dishonestly.
我跟死者的交情，当然也不会「起黑心」。

But”—he grew agitated—“in a situation like this, how can I call in loans and recover invested capital at a moment’s notice?
不过，』说到这里，他有点烦躁，『这样的局面，放出去的款子；摆下去的本钱，一时哪里去回笼？

I truly do not know how to settle matters.”
真教我不好交代。』

It was indeed a serious difficulty.
这确是极为难的事。

Gu’s thoughts went further.
古应春的想法比胡雪岩还要深，

Wang had died for his duty and left a sizeable family.
王有龄已经殉节，遗属不少，

They needed money for their immediate living expenses and, later, to marry off the children.
眼前居家度日，将来男婚女嫁，不但在在要钱，

Even if they received the money, they could not simply live on their capital until it was exhausted.
而且有了钱也不能坐吃山空。

“You must think beyond settling the account now,” he said.
所以，他说∶『你还不能只顾眼前的交代，

“You ought to make lasting provision for the Wang family.”
要替王家筹个久长之计才好。』

“There is not much to plan there.
『这倒没有什么好筹划的，

So long as the Hu family has rice to eat, I shall never let the Wangs live on gruel.
反正只要胡雪岩一家有饭吃；决不会让王家吃粥，

It is the immediate problem that worries me.
我愁的是眼前！』

Mr Xuexuan and I were so close that he was as much myself as I was.
胡雪岩说∶『王雪公跟我的交情，可以说他就是我，我就是他。

His spirit in Heaven will understand my predicament.
他在天之灵，一定会谅解我的处境。

But Mrs Wang may not know what is in my heart; her relatives and friends will understand even less.
不过王太太或者不晓得我的心，他家的亲友更加隔膜，

All they know is that I hold this money, not that I cannot recover it at once.
只知道有钱在我这里，不知道这笔钱一时收不回来。

Since such rumours are already abroad, unless I can produce the shining silver itself, people will say I am taking advantage of a widow and orphans.
现在外头既有这样的闲话，我如果不能拿白花花的现银子捧出来，人家只当我欺侮孤儿寡妇。

Think what that would do to my reputation!”
这个名声，你想想，我怎么吃得消？』


Gu thought this sound reasoning.
古应春觉得这个看法不错，

He too understood human nature thoroughly, and carried the argument a step further. If Hu handed over the entire sum belonging to Wang, the family would naturally trust him and ask him to continue investing it, leaving it at his disposal.
他也是熟透人情世故的人，心里又有进一步的想法∶如果胡雪岩将王有龄名下的款子，如数交付，王家自然信任他，继续托他营运，手里仍可活动。

Otherwise they might become uneasy and insist on withdrawing it.
否则，王家反倒有些不大放心，会要求收回。

Given that, he might as well make a handsome job of it.
既然如此，就乐得做得漂亮些。

The trouble was that Hangzhou had fallen, while the capital invested in Shanghai could not be withdrawn immediately.
麻烦的是，杭州一陷，上海的生意又一时不能抽本，

A handsome settlement was therefore impossible without help from their friends.
无法做得『漂亮』。那就要靠大家帮忙了。

“Little Uncle, how much of Mr Xuexuan’s money do you have?”
『小爷叔，』他问∶『王雪公有多少款子在你手里？』

“Mrs Wang has the accounts.
『王太太手里有帐的，

About a hundred thousand taels.
大概有十万；

There are another twenty thousand in Yunnan, though I do not know whether she knows about those.”
另外还有两万在云南，不知道王太太知道不知道。』

“How strange!
『那就奇怪了。

How did twenty thousand taels come to be in Yunnan?”
怎么在云南会有两万银子？』


“This is how it happened,” Hu said.
『是这样子的，』胡雪岩说，

“In the winter of the sixth year of Xianfeng, He Genyun left his post as governor of Zhejiang, and Mr Xuexuan had little prospect of making a career there any longer.
『咸丰六年冬天，何根云交卸浙江巡抚，王雪公在浙江的官，也没有什么做头了；

Arrangements had already been made for him to become grain intendant in Yunnan.
事先安排，调补云南粮道。

I remitted twenty thousand taels there in advance.
我替他先汇了两万银子到云南。

Afterwards He was promoted to governor-general of Liangjiang, and Mr Xuexuan naturally followed him to Jiangsu.
后来何根云调升两江，王雪公自然跟到江苏；

The twenty thousand in Yunnan were never touched; they remained on deposit with a bank in Kunming, earning interest.
云南的两万银子始终未动，存在昆明钱庄是生息。

Mr Xuexuan never forgot Yunnan.
王雪公始终不忘云南，

He used to tell me that, given the opportunity, he would very much like to serve a term as governor there.
生前跟我说过，有机会很想做一任云南巡抚；

Better still if he could become governor-general of Yunnan and Guizhou.
能做到云贵总督，当然更好。

Sooner or later, he said, the money would be useful in Yunnan, so we need not withdraw it.
这两万银子在云南迟早有用处，不必去动它。

Now, of course, he will never need it again.”
现在，当然再也用不着了！』

At this, emotion overcame Hu once more, and tears rose to his eyes.
说到这里，胡雪岩又生感触，泫然欲涕。


Gu waited while he wiped his eyes, blew his nose, and recovered some composure, then resumed the discussion. “Money deposited with a bank must have a passbook.
等他拭一拭眼睛，擤一擤鼻子，情绪略略平伏，古应春便接着话题顺∶『款子放在钱庄里，总有折子；

Who has it?”
折子在谁手里？』

“That is the difficulty.
『麻烦就在这里。

There was a passbook, and I gave it to Mr Xuexuan.
折子是有一个，我交了给王雪公；

I suppose he lost it and forgot about it, for he later asked me where it was.
大概是他弄掉了，也记不起这回事，反来问我。

It did not seem to matter at the time.
这原是无所谓的事；
