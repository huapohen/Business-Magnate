胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第六章（本章第1部分，共5部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第六章
CHAPTER SIX


一个多月以后，刘不才重回上海，
More than a month later, Liu Bucai returned to Shanghai.

他的本事很大，为胡雪岩接眷，居然成功。
He had proved remarkably resourceful: his mission to bring out Hu Xueyan’s family had actually succeeded.

可是，全家将到上海，胡雪岩反倒上了心事，
Yet with the whole household about to arrive, Hu found himself burdened with a new worry.

就为借了『小房子』住在一起的阿巧，身分不明，
A’qiao was living with him in a rented “little house”, with no settled status.

难以处置，只好求救七姑奶奶。
He did not know what to do about her and could only appeal to the Seventh Lady.

『七姐，你要替我出个主意；
“Seventh Sister, you must find a way for me.

除你以外，我没有人好商量。』
There is no one else I can consult.”

『那当然！
“Naturally!

小爷叔的事，我不能不管。
I cannot refuse to concern myself with your affairs, Little Uncle.

不过，先要你自己定个宗旨。』
But first you must decide what you want.”

问到胡雪岩对阿巧姐的态度，正是他的难题所在，
His own attitude towards A’qiao was precisely the difficulty.

惟有报以苦笑∶『七姐，全本西厢记，不都在你肚子里？』
He could only smile bitterly. “Seventh Sister, is not the whole Romance of the Western Chamber already in your head?”

七姑奶奶对他们的情形，确是知之甚深，
She did indeed know their situation thoroughly.

总括一句话∶表面看来，恩爱异常；暗地里隔着一道极深的鸿沟。
In a word, they appeared devoted lovers, yet a deep gulf lay between them.

一个虽倾心于胡雪岩，但宁可居于外室，不愿位列小星，
A’qiao’s heart belonged to Hu, but she would rather remain his mistress outside the household than become a concubine within it.

因为她畏惮胡家人多，
She dreaded the number of people in his family.

伺候老太太以外，还要执礼于大妇，甚至看芙蓉的辞色；
Besides attending upon his mother, she would have to show deference to his principal wife and even watch Furong’s expressions for signs of favour or displeasure.

再有一种想法是∶出自两江总督行辕，
There was another consideration. She had come from the governor-general of Liangjiang’s official residence.

虽非嫡室，等于『署理』过掌印夫人；
Though not his principal wife, she had virtually acted as mistress of his household.

不管再做什么人的侧室，都觉得是一种委屈。
To become any other man’s concubine now seemed a descent.

在胡雪岩，最大的顾虑亦正是为此。
This was also the chief source of Hu’s misgivings.

阿巧姐跟何桂清的姻缘，完全是自己一手促成；
He himself had arranged her union with He Guiqing from beginning to end.

如今再接收过来，不管自己身受的感觉，还是想到旁人的批评，总有些不大对劲。
To take her over now felt somehow improper, whether he considered his own feelings or other people’s criticism.

在外面借『小房子』做露水夫妻，那是因为她千里相就于患难之中，因感生情，不能自己，
Renting a “little house” and living as temporary lovers could at least be explained: she had travelled a thousand li to join him in adversity, and gratitude had awakened a love he could not resist.

无论对本身，对旁人，总还有句譬解的话好说；
He could offer that excuse to himself and to others.

一旦接回家中，就无词自解了。
Bringing her into his family would leave him without an answer.


除此以外，还有个极大的障碍；
There was another formidable obstacle.

胡太太曾经斩钉截铁地表示过∶有出息的男人，三妻四妾，不足为奇；
Mrs Hu had stated her position with absolute firmness. A successful man might have several wives and concubines; that was nothing unusual.

但大妇的名分，是他人夺不去的，
But no one could take away the standing of the principal wife.

所以只要胡雪岩看中了，娶回家则可，在外面另立门户则不可。
If Hu chose another woman, he might marry her and bring her home, but he must not establish a separate household outside.

同时她也表示过，凡是娶进门的，她必须姊妹看待。
At the same time, she had promised to treat every woman he brought home as a sister.

事实上对待芙蓉的态度，已经证明她言行如一；
Her conduct towards Furong had already proved that she kept her word.

所以更显得她的脚步站得极隐，就连胡老太太亦不能不尊重她的话。
This made her position all the stronger; even Hu’s mother had to respect it.

然而这是两回事。七姑奶奶了解胡雪岩的苦衷，却不能替他决定态度，
But understanding Hu’s predicament and deciding his course were two different things. The Seventh Lady could do the first, not the second.

『小爷叔，你要我帮你的忙，先要你自己拿定主意，
“If you want my help, Little Uncle, you must first make up your own mind.

或留或去，
Will you keep her or part from her?

定了宗旨，才好想办法。
Once your purpose is settled, we can find a way.

不过，』她很率直地说∶『我话要说在前头，不管怎么样，你要我帮着你瞒；那是办不到的。』
But I must tell you plainly beforehand: whatever happens, I cannot help you conceal it.”

有此表示，胡雪岩大失所望。
Hu was bitterly disappointed.

他的希望，正就是想请七姑奶奶设法替他在妻子面前隐瞒；
Concealing the affair from his wife was exactly what he had hoped she would help him do.

所以听得这句话，作声不得。
He could find nothing to say.


这一下，等于心思完全显露，
His silence exposed his thoughts completely.

七姑奶奶便劝他∶『小爷叔，家和万事兴！
The Seventh Lady advised him, “Little Uncle, harmony at home brings success in everything.

婶娘贤慧能干，是你大大的一个帮手。
Your wife is good and capable, a tremendous help to you.

不过我再说一句∶婶娘也很厉害，
But let me add this: she is formidable too.

你千万别惹她恨你。
You must never give her cause to hate you.

如果说，你想拿阿巧姐接回去，我哪怕跑断腿，说破嘴，也替你去劝她。
If you want to bring A’qiao home, I shall wear out my legs and talk till my mouth is dry trying to persuade her.

当然，成功不成功，不敢保险。
Of course, I cannot promise success.

倘或你下个决断，预备各奔东西，那包在我身上，
If you decide instead that you and A’qiao must go your separate ways, leave that to me.

你跟她好合好散，决不伤你们的和气。』
I can arrange a friendly parting without spoiling the affection between you.”

『那，你倒说给我听听，怎么样才能跟阿巧姐好合好散？』
“Then tell me how we could part on such terms.”

『现在还说不出，
“I cannot yet.

要等我去动脑筋，
I must give it some thought.

不过，这一层，我有把握。』
But of that I am confident.”

胡雪岩想了好一会，委决不下，
Hu thought for a long time without reaching a decision.

叹口气说∶『明天再说吧。』
“We shall talk tomorrow,” he sighed.

『小爷叔，你最好今天晚上细想一想，把主意拿定了它；
“Think it over carefully tonight, Little Uncle, and make up your mind.

如果预备接回家，我要早点替你安排。』
If you mean to bring her home, I must begin preparing the way.

七姑奶奶指一指外面说，『我要请刘三叔先在老太太跟婶娘面前，替你下一番功夫。
I shall ask Third Uncle Liu”—she gestured outside—“to do some work with your mother and your wife beforehand.”

胡雪岩一楞，
Hu started.

是要下一番什么功夫？
What sort of work?

转个念头，才能领会，
A moment’s reflection made it clear.

虽说自己妻子表示不禁良人纳妾；但却不能没有妒意。
Though his wife had said she would not forbid him to take concubines, that did not mean she felt no jealousy.

能与芙蓉相处得亲如姊妹，一方面是她本人有意要作个贤慧的榜样；一方面是芙蓉柔顺，甘于做小服低。
Her sisterly affection for Furong arose partly from her determination to set a virtuous example, partly from Furong’s gentle willingness to accept a subordinate place.

这样因缘时会，两下凑成了一双两好的局面，是个异数；
Their dispositions and circumstances had combined to produce an unusually happy arrangement.

不能期望三妻四妾，人人如此。
He could not expect every wife and concubine to behave so well.

七姑奶奶要请刘不才去下一番功夫，自然是先作疏通；
The Seventh Lady meant Liu to sound them out and smooth the way.

果然自己有心，而阿巧姐亦不反对正式『进门』，七姑奶奶的做法是必要的。
If Hu truly wanted A’qiao at home, and A’qiao herself would agree to enter the household formally, such preparation was necessary.

不过胡雪岩也因此被提醒了；阿巧姐亦是极厉害的脚色，远非芙蓉可比。
Yet this also reminded him how formidable A’qiao was—quite unlike Furong.

就算眼前一切顺利，阿巧姐改变初衷，妻子亦能克践诺言，然而好景决不会长，
Even if everything went smoothly at first, if she changed her mind and his wife kept her promise, the happy state would not last.

两『雌』相遇，互持不下，
Two strong women confronting one another would neither yield.

明争暗斗之下，掀起醋海的万丈波澜，
Open quarrels and secret struggles would raise mountainous waves of jealousy.

那时候可真是『两妇之间难为夫』了。
Then he would truly know how hard it was to be a husband caught between two wives.


这样一想，忧愁烦恼，同时并生；因而胃纳越发不佳。
These reflections brought worry and vexation together, further spoiling his appetite.

不过他一向不肯扫人的兴；
But he never liked to dampen another person’s pleasure.

见刘不才意兴甚好，也就打点精神相陪，谈到午夜方散。
Liu was in excellent spirits, so Hu roused himself to keep him company, and they talked until midnight.

回到『小房子』，阿巧姐照例茶水点心，早有预备。
When he returned to the “little house”, A’qiao had tea and refreshments ready as usual.

卧室中重帷深垂，隔绝了料峭春寒；
Heavy curtains in the bedroom kept out the sharp chill of early spring.

她只穿一件软缎夹袄，剪裁得非常贴身，越显得腰肢一捻，十分苗条。
She wore only a lined jacket of soft satin, cut so close that her waist seemed slender enough to clasp in one hand.

入手相握，才知她到底穿得太少了些∶『若要俏，冻得跳！』他说，
Taking her hand, Hu found that she really was too lightly dressed. “For the sake of looking pretty, you freeze till you jump!

『当心冻出病来。』
Mind you do not make yourself ill.”

阿巧姐笑笑不响，
She smiled without answering.

倒杯热茶摆在他面前，自己捧着一把灌满热茶的乾隆五彩的小茶壶，当做手炉取暖；
She poured him hot tea, then cradled a small teapot of Qianlong five-colour porcelain, filled with hot tea, to warm her hands like a hand stove.

双眼灼灼地望着，等他开口。
Her bright eyes watched him, waiting for him to speak.

每天回来，胡雪岩总要谈他在外面的情形，在哪里吃的饭；遇见了什么有趣的人；听到了哪些新闻，
Every evening he would tell her what had happened outside—where he had eaten, what amusing people he had met, what news he had heard.

可是这天却一反常态，坐下来不作一声。
Tonight, contrary to his habit, he sat without a word.

『你累了是不是？』阿巧姐说，
“You are tired, are you not?” she said.

『早点上床吧！』
“Go to bed early.”

『嗯，
“Mm.

累了。』
Tired.”

口中在答应她的话，眼睛却仍旧望着悬在天花板下，称为『保险灯』的煤油吊灯。
His mouth answered, but his eyes remained fixed on the hanging kerosene light beneath the ceiling, called a “safety lamp”.

这神思不属，无视眼前的态度，在阿巧姐的记忆中只有一次；就是得知王有龄殉节的那天晚上。
She remembered only one other occasion when he had been so absent-minded, so blind to what lay before him: the night he learnt that Wang Youling had died for his country.


『那哼啦！』她不知不觉地用极柔媚的苏白相依，
“What is it?” she asked unconsciously in the softest, most coaxing Suzhou dialect.

『有啥心事？』
“What is troubling you?”

『老太太要来了！』
“My mother is coming.”

关于接眷的事，胡雪岩很少跟她谈。
Hu had said little to her about bringing out his family.

阿巧姐也只知道，他全家都陷在嘉兴，一时无法团圆，也就不去多想；
She knew only that they were all trapped in Jiaxing and that reunion was impossible for the moment; she had thought no further.

这时突如其来地听得这一句，心里立刻就乱了。
His sudden announcement threw her into confusion.

『这是喜事！』她很勉强地笑着说。
“That is happy news!” she said, forcing a smile.

『喜事倒是喜事，
“Happy, certainly.

心事也是心事。
But it brings worries too.

阿巧，你到底怎么说？』
A’qiao, what do you say?”

『什么怎么说？』
“About what?”

她明知故问。
She knew perfectly well.

胡雪岩想了一会，语意嗳昧地说∶『我们这样子也不是个长局。』
After a little thought he said ambiguously, “We cannot go on like this for ever.”

阿巧姐颜色一变，将头低了下去，
Her colour changed and she lowered her head.

只见她睫毛闪动，却不知她眼中是何神色？
He could see her lashes moving, but not the expression in her eyes.

于是，胡雪岩的心也乱了，
Now his own thoughts grew confused.

站起来往床上一倒，望着帐顶发楞。
He rose, threw himself on the bed and stared vacantly at the canopy.

阿巧姐没有说话，但也不是灯下垂泪；
She said nothing, but neither did she weep beneath the lamp.

放下手中的茶壶，将坐在洋油炉子上的一只瓦罐取了下来，倒出熬得极浓的鸡汤，
Setting down her teapot, she lifted an earthenware pot from the kerosene stove and poured out some richly simmered chicken broth.

另外又从洋铁匣子里取出七八片『盐饼干』，盛在瓷碟子里，一起放在梳妆台上。
From a tin she took seven or eight salted biscuits, arranged them on a porcelain plate and placed both dishes on the dressing table.

接着便替胡雪岩脱下靴子，套上一双绣花套鞋。
Then she removed Hu’s boots and slipped embroidered slippers onto his feet.

按部就班服侍到底，她才开口∶『起来吃吧！』
Only after attending to him in proper order did she speak. “Get up and eat.”


坐在梳妆台畔吃临睡之前的一顿宵夜，本来是胡雪岩每天最惬意的一刻，
Sitting at the dressing table for his bedtime supper had always been the most agreeable moment of Hu’s day.

一面看着阿巧姐卸妆；一面听她用吴侬软语有一搭，没一搭地，说些有趣而不伤脑筋的闲话，
As he ate, he watched A’qiao remove her make-up and listened to her soft Wu speech, now a remark, now a pause, full of entertaining trifles that required no thought.

自以为是南面王不易之乐。
He would not have exchanged that pleasure for a throne.

然而这天的心情却有些不同。
Tonight his mood was different.

不过转念之间，还是不肯放弃这份乐趣，从床上一个虎跳似地跳下地来，倒吓了阿巧姐一下。
Yet on second thoughts he would not surrender the pleasure, and sprang from the bed like a tiger, giving her a fright.

『你这个人！』
“You!”

她白了他一眼，
She cast him a reproachful glance.

『今朝真有点邪气。』
“You really are behaving strangely tonight.”

『得乐且乐。』
“Take pleasure while it lasts.”

胡雪岩忽然觉得肚子饿得厉害，
Suddenly he felt ravenously hungry.

『还有什么好吃的？』
“What else is there to eat?”

『这个辰光，只有吃干点心。
“At this hour, only dry refreshments.

馄饨担、卖湖州粽子茶叶蛋的，都来过了。』阿巧姐问道∶『莫非你在古家没有吃饱？』
The wonton seller and the man with Huzhou rice dumplings and tea eggs have already passed.” She asked, “Did you not eat enough at the Gus’?”

『根本就没有吃！』
“I scarcely ate at all.”

『为啥？
“Why?

菜不配胃口？』
Did the dishes not suit you?”

『七姑奶奶烧的吕宋排翅，又是鱼生，
“The Seventh Lady made Luzon shark’s fin, and there was raw fish too.

偏偏没口福，
Unfortunately I had no luck with such delicacies.

吃不下。』
I could not eat.”

『这又是啥道理？』
“Why ever not?”

『唉！』
“Ah!”

胡雪岩摇摇头，
He shook his head.

『不去说它了。
“Let us not speak of it.

再拿些盐饼干来！』
Bring me some more salted biscuits.”

他不说，她也不问，
He would say no more, and she asked no more.

依言照办；然后自己坐下来卸妆，
She did as he requested, then sat to remove her make-up.

将一把头发握在手里，拿黄杨木梳不断地梳着。
Gathering her hair in one hand, she combed it repeatedly with a boxwood comb.

房间里静得很，
The room was very quiet.

只听见胡雪岩『嘎吱、嘎吱』咬饼干的声音。
Only the crunch of Hu’s biscuits could be heard.


『老太太哪天到？』阿巧姐突如其来地问。
“When is your mother arriving?” A’qiao suddenly asked.

『快了！』
“Soon.

胡雪岩说，『不过十天半个月的功夫。』
In ten days or a fortnight.”

『住在哪里呢？』
“Where will she stay?”

『还不晓得。』
“I do not know yet.”

『人都快来了，住的地方还不知道在哪里；
“They are nearly here, and you still do not know where they will live?

不是笑话？』
Is that not ridiculous?”

『这两天事情多，还没有功夫去办这件事。
“I have been busy these last few days and have had no time to arrange it.

等明天刘三爷走了再说。
I shall see to it after Third Master Liu leaves tomorrow.

有钱还怕找不到房子？
With money, need one fear being unable to find a house?

不过——？』怎么？
Only—” “What?”

『阿巧姐转脸看着他问∶』怎么不说下去？
She turned towards him. “Why have you stopped?”

『』房子该多大多小，可就不知道了。
“I do not know how large a house it ought to be.”

『』这又奇了！
“Stranger still!

多少人住多大的房子，难道你自己算不出来？
Surely you can work out how much space you need for the number of people?”

『『就是多少人算不出来。』
“It is the number of people I cannot work out.”

胡雪岩看了她一眼，有意转过脸去；其实是在镜子里看她的表情。
Hu glanced at her and deliberately turned away, though he was watching her expression in the mirror.

阿巧姐沉默而又沉着，一副莫测高深的样子。
She remained silent and composed, inscrutable.

然后，站起来铺床叠被，始终不作一声。
Then she rose and prepared the bed, still without a word.

『睡吧！』
“Let us sleep.”

胡雪岩拍拍腰际，
Hu patted his waist.

肚子里倒饱了，心里空落落地，有点儿上不巴天，下不巴地似的。
His stomach was full, but his heart felt empty, suspended between heaven and earth with nothing to hold it.


『你到底有啥心事？
“What is really troubling you?

爽爽快快地说。
Say it plainly.

牵丝扳藤，惹得人肚肠根痒。』
All this trailing thread and tangled vine makes one itch with impatience.”

有何心事，以她的聪明机警，熟透人情，哪有不知之理？
How could a woman as clever, alert and worldly as she fail to understand?

这样子故意装作不解，自然不是好兆头；
Her deliberate pretence of ignorance was no good sign.

胡雪岩在女人面前，不大喜欢用深心，但此时此人，却成了例外，
Hu did not usually care to play intricate games with women, but with this woman, at this moment, he made an exception.

因此以深沉对深沉，笑笑答道∶『心事要慢慢猜才有味道。
Meeting reserve with reserve, he smiled. “A hidden thought is more enjoyable if you guess it slowly.

何必一下子揭破？』
Why expose it all at once?”

阿巧姐无奈其何，赌气不作声；
Unable to get through to him, A’qiao fell into offended silence.

叠好了被，伺候他卸衣上床。
She finished arranging the quilts and helped him undress and get into bed.

然后将一盏洋灯移到红木大床里面的搁几上，捻小了灯芯；让一团朦胧的黄光，隐藏了她脸上的不豫之色。
Then she moved a foreign lamp to a small stand inside the great rosewood bed and turned down the wick, letting the dim yellow light hide her displeasure.

这一静下来，胡雪岩的心思集中了；
In the stillness, Hu’s thoughts became clear.

发觉自己跟阿巧姐之间，只有两条路好走，一条是照现在的样子；再一条就是各奔西东。
He saw only two courses open to himself and A’qiao: continue as they were, or part.

『你不必胡思乱想。』他不自觉地说∶『等我好好来想个办法。』
“Do not torment yourself with fancies,” he said without quite intending to speak aloud. “Let me think of a proper solution.”


『没头没脑你说的是啥？』
“What are you talking about, out of the blue like that?”

『还不是为了你！』
“What else but you?

胡雪岩说，『住在外面，我太太不答应；住在一起，你又不愿意。
My wife will not allow you to live outside, and you will not live with the family.

那就只好我来动脑筋了。
I must find a way.”

『阿巧姐不作声。
