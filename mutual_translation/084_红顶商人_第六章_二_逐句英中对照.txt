“That is hard to say.
『这很难说！

From what I know, Xueyan’s wife has her own way of running the household.
照我晓得，雪碉岩夫人治家另有一套；

Break her rule once, and her words will carry less weight thereafter.”
坏了她的规矩，破一个例，以后她说的话就要打折扣了。』

“Little Uncle has said it would be this once only, never again.
『小爷叔说过的∶』只此一遭，下不为例。

If anything of the sort happens in future, I shall stand up for Mrs Hu before she even needs to speak.” Liu suddenly chuckled, then sighed without answering.
「将来如果再有这样子的情形；不用胡家婶娘开口发话，我先替她打抱不平！『听到这里，刘不才』噗哧『一声笑了；叹口气不响。

His manner suggested that she did not understand what she was doing, which rather annoyed her.
这大有笑人不懂事的意味，七姑奶奶倒有些光火；

“Third Uncle Liu, have I said something wrong?”
立即追一句∶『刘三叔，我话说错了？』

“Your words are right, and your heart is warm.
『话不错，你的心也热。

But that is precisely how you will bring trouble upon yourself.
不过，惟其如此，你就是自寻烦恼。

As the saying goes, even an honest magistrate cannot settle a family quarrel.
俗语道得好∶』清官难断家务事「；

Seventh Sister, even if you were Judge Bao himself and judged the matter perfectly, you would still have trouble.”
七姐，就算你是包公，断得明明白白，依旧是个烦恼！

“How?
『』怎么呢！

I do not understand.”
这话我就听不懂了。

“Seventh Sister, you are clever in everything else and confused only here.
『『七姐，你聪明一世，懵懂一时，

In a lawsuit, either the plaintiff wins or the defendant does.
打到官司，不是原告赢，就是被告赢，

You mend one thing and damage another.
治一经，损一经，

Why put yourself through it?”
何苦来哉！』

She understood at once.
七姑奶奶恍然大悟，

If she sided with Mrs Hu in future, she must offend Hu himself.
将来如果帮胡太太，就一定得罪了胡雪岩；

Was that not mending one thing at the cost of another?
岂不是治一经，损一经？

“Enough, enough!
『好了，好了，

Really, Third Uncle Liu, if you have a sound reason, why not state it plainly instead of going round in such circles?
刘三叔，你也是，有道理不直截了当说出来，要兜这么大一个圈子！

Fortunately I have changed and have the patience to question you.
亏得我不比从前，有耐心盘问，

Otherwise you would have led me astray!”
不然不是害我走错了路？』

Her complaint was quite unreasonable, but amusingly so.
这番埋怨的话，真有点蛮不讲理，但不讲理得有趣；

Liu could only laugh.
刘不才只好笑了。


“I do not want to be a female Judge Bao.
『我也不要做啥「女包公」！

I would rather remain a female Zhang Fei.”
还是做我的「女张飞」来得好。』

There was something behind this, which Liu recognised at once.
话外有话，刘不才一下子就听了出来，

He had to answer. “What do you intend, Seventh Sister?
不能不回∶『七姐！你是怎么个打算？

A female Zhang Fei is all very well, but a rash Zhang Fei would be another matter.”
做女张飞还则罢了，做莽张飞就没意思了。』

“Zhang Fei could be subtle beneath his roughness too.
『张飞也有粗中有细的时候，

Naturally I know where to stop.
我自然有分寸。

Rest assured: there will be no trouble.”
你放心好了，不会有啥风波。』

Liu considered. “Then do you still want me to prepare Xueyan’s wife?”
刘不才想了一下问道∶『那末，是不是还要我在雪岩夫人面前去做功夫？』

“Yes.
『要！

But the argument will no longer be the same.”
不过话不是原来的说法了。』

That puzzled him.
这下搞得刘不才发楞。

Surely this was a question of yes or no: either forget the matter entirely or raise it.
是一非二的事，要么一笔勾销不谈此事；要谈，

How could there be another way of raising it?
还要另一个说法吗？

“The first half can still be used.
『前半段的话，还是可以用，

Mrs Hu should understand how A’qiao and Little Uncle became attached again. If she knows the whole story, she will not begin with a prejudice against A’qiao.”
阿巧姐怎么跟小爷叔又生了感情，总有个来龙去脉，要让胡家婶娘知道，才不会先对阿巧姐有成见。』

The Seventh Lady paused. “The latter half must be changed as follows.”
七姑奶奶停了一下说∶『后半段的话改成这个样子——。』

Her plan was first to reassure Mrs Hu, which would also reassure Hu himself.
她的做法是先安抚胡太太，也就是先安抚胡雪岩。

Once the family reached Shanghai, his separate mistress could not be concealed.
因为胡家眷属一到上海，胡雪岩有外室这件事，是瞒不住的；

He would also ask the Seventh Lady what she had achieved.
而且胡雪岩本人也会向七姑奶奶探问结果，

She therefore needed his wife’s cooperation to keep the situation calm for the time being.
所以她需要胡太太跟她配合，先把局面安定下来。


“I need time to work patiently upon A’qiao.
『我要一段辰光，好在阿巧姐面前下水磨功夫。

My fear is that, before I have made any progress, husband and wife will already be quarrelling.
就怕事情还没有眉目，他们夫妇已经吵了起来；

Once people openly fall out, a deadlock often follows.
凡事一破了脸，往往就会弄成僵局。

Mrs Hu had best pretend to know nothing.
所以胡家婶娘最好装作不知道这回事；

If Little Uncle stays away from his own camp at night, she need not investigate.”
如果小爷叔「夜不归营」，也不必去查问。』

“I understand, and Xueyan’s wife can certainly do that.
『我懂你的意思，雪岩夫人也一定做得到。

But he often springs surprises.
不过，雪岩做事，常常会出奇兵，

What if she pretends ignorance and he comes straight out with the truth, face to face?”
倘或一个装糊涂；一个倒当面锣、对面鼓，自己跟她老实去谈了呢？』

“I doubt that will happen.
『我想这种情形不大会有，

If it does, she should neither agree nor object, but put him off as best she can.”
如果是这样，胡家婶娘不承认，也不反对，一味敷衍他就是了。』

“I suppose that is the only course.”
『我想也只好这样子应付。』

Liu nodded.
刘不才点点头，

“In a word, overcome firmness with softness.”
『一句话∶以柔克刚。』

“Which means tact.
『以柔克刚就是圆滑。

Tell Mrs Hu that I undertake to settle the matter within three months.
请你跟胡家婶娘说，总在三个月当中，包在我身上，将这件事办妥当。

What do I mean by settle?
什么叫妥当呢？

Her rule will remain intact.
就是不坏她的规矩，

If A’qiao will not enter the Hu household, she will certainly have joined some other man’s.”
如果阿巧姐不肯进门姓胡；那就一定姓了别人的姓了。』

“So you mean to transplant the blossom to another branch.”
『原来你是想用条移花接木之计。』

Liu was intrigued. “Have you already found a man for her?”
刘不才兴致盎然地问∶『七姐，你是不是替阿巧姐物色好了什么人？』

“No, no.
『没有，没有！

I must look slowly.” Suddenly she laughed. “Actually, Third Uncle Liu, you would be quite a match.”
要慢慢去觅。』七姑奶奶突然笑道∶『其实，刘三叔，你倒蛮配！』

“You are joking!
『开玩笑了！

How could I graduate in the same class as Xueyan?”
我怎么好跟雪岩「同科」？』


It was already the chou watch, after midnight, when Hu returned home, but he was still in excellent spirits.
回家已经午夜过后的丑时了，但是胡雪岩的精神却还很好，

Sitting by the dressing table as A’qiao removed her make-up, he asked about the women’s outing that evening.
坐在统妆台畔看阿巧姐卸妆，同时问起她们这一夜出游的情形。

“We went for a foreign dinner first.
『先去吃大菜。

There was nothing very good to eat.
实在没有什么好吃；

Their fried quail was not as good as the eight-piece fried chicken in a Beijing restaurant.
炸鹌鹑还不如京馆里的炸八块。

And with only the two of us, we looked like—” She shook her head and smiled bitterly, unwilling to finish.
又是我们这么两个人；倒象——。』阿巧姐摇摇头，苦笑着不肯再说下去。

Like what?
象什么？

Hu closed his eyes and imagined himself as one of the waiters serving them.
胡雪岩闭起眼睛，作为自己是在场执役的『两崽』去体会；

Two ladies without male companions, boldly showing themselves in public and handling knives and forks over a foreign dinner—what sort of women would they seem?
这样两位堂客，没有『官客』陪伴，抛头露面敢到那里『动刀动枪』去吃大菜，是啥路道？

Judging by their ages and their dress, they would look like two remarkably fine proprietresses of expensive courtesan houses.
照她们的年纪和打扮来说，就象长三堂子里的两个极出色的『本家』。

That must have been A’qiao’s thought, and the reason she refused to finish.
阿巧姐的想法必是如此，所以才不愿说下去。

Understanding this, he understood her feelings as well.
了解到这一点，自然而然地意会到她的心境，

Even if she did not aspire to an aristocratic mansion, she had certainly come to despise the pleasure quarters.
即令不是向往朱邸，确已鄙弃青楼，

Her desire for a respectable life was sincere.
真有从良的诚意。

This made it harder still to give her up.
由于这样的看法，便越觉得阿巧姐难舍；

He blurted out, “What did Seventh Sister say to you?”
因而脱口问道∶『七姐怎么跟你说？』


“What do you mean, what did she say?”
『什么怎么跟我说？』

A’qiao’s hands stopped in the act of undoing her hair.
阿巧姐将正在解髻的手停了下来，

“What should she have to say to me?
『她会有什么话跟我说？

You knew beforehand, did you not?
你是先就晓得的是不是？

Then tell me: why did she suddenly leave Master You at home today and invite me out to the theatre and a foreign dinner?”
你倒说说看，她今天拿五爷丢在家里，忽然要请我看戏吃大菜，到底是为了什么？』

This volley of questions threw Hu into confusion.
这一连串的疑问，将胡雪岩搞得枪法大乱，

He could not parry it.
无法招架。

But he possessed one gift: a smile could conceal any embarrassment, and his embarrassment never lasted long.
不过他有一样本事，善于用笑容来遮盖任何窘态；而那种窘态亦决不会保持得太久，

He soon recovered his composure.
很快地便沉着下来。

“I do not understand what you mean.
『我不懂你说的啥？』

I was asking whether she told you why she suddenly felt like taking you out.
他说，『我是问你，七姐有没有告诉你，她何以心血来潮约你出去玩？

Apparently you do not know.
看样子你也不知道；

Then I know still less.”
那我就更加不知道了。』

“Even a man as clever as you does not know?”
『连你这样聪明的人都不知道？』

She gave a faint, cold smile.
阿巧姐微微冷笑，

“Then there is nothing more to say.”
『那也就没有什么好说的了。』

“We are husband and wife chatting together.
『夫妇闲谈，

What harm is there in talking?”
说说何妨？』

She suddenly raised her head, her clear, bright eyes fixed upon him. “Husband and wife?
阿巧姐倏然抬头，炯炯清眸，逼着胡雪岩∶『夫妇？

Am I so fortunate?”
我有那么好的福气？』

His careless remark seemed to have given her a weapon, but it did not defeat him.
无意间一句话，倒似乎成了把柄；不过也难不倒胡雪岩，

“Here we are husband and wife,” he answered calmly.
『在这里我们就是夫妇。』他从容自在地回答。

“Then,” she nodded as though speaking to herself, “there is all the more reason not to listen to the Seventh Lady.”
『所以，』她点点头，自语似的，『我就更不能听七姑奶奶的话了。』

“What did she say?”
『她说了什么话？』

“She advised me to go back.”
『她劝我回去。』

“Go back” could mean either returning to her own family or entering Hu’s household as a concubine.
这『回去』二字可有两个解释，一是回娘家，二是进胡家的大门做偏房。

Her family home was in Mudu, near Suzhou, which was now in the Longhairs’ hands.
她的娘家在苏州木渎，而苏州此刻在长毛手里，

There could be no reason to advise her to go there.
自然没有劝她回娘家的道理。


Once her meaning was clear, he ought to ask what she intended.
弄清楚了她的话，该问她的意志；

But he already knew the answer, so there was no point.
但不问可知，就无须多此一举。

After a long silence he burst out, “Tomorrow I really must look for a house.”
停了好一会，他口中爆出一句话来∶『明天真的要去找房子了。』

His manner was rather inscrutable.
他的态度有些莫测高深。

A’qiao remembered how, when they had discussed finding a house a few days earlier, he had hinted that she should live with his principal wife.
她记起前几天谈到找房子的事，曾经暗示要让她跟大妇住在一起；

Did he still mean that?
而此刻还是那样的心思？

She must find out.
必得问一问。

“If you cannot find one in time,” she ventured, “why not let them stay here first?”
于是她试探地说∶『如果真的一时找不到；不如先住到这里来。』

“There is not room.”
『住不下。』

Did he mean there was no room in any case, or no room if she remained?
这住不下是说本来就住不下叱；还是连她在一起住不下？

She still could not tell and had to try again.
阿巧姐依然不明白！就只好再试探了。

“They can squeeze in for the moment.
『暂时挤一挤。』

Refugees cannot expect every comfort.”
她说，『逃难辰光也讲究不来那么多。』

“And you?”
『那么，你呢？』

“I?”
『我？』

She spoke resolutely.
阿巧姐毅然决然地说，

“I shall move elsewhere.”
『另外搬。』

“Why trouble?
『那又何必？

Better to leave things as they are.”
一动不如一静。』

Hu thought for a moment and decided plain speaking would be best.
胡雪岩想了一会，觉得还是把话说明了好，

“I feel as you do.
『我跟你的心思一样，

Continuing just like this would be best.
就照这个样子最好。

I have already asked the Seventh Lady to plead with my wife when she arrives, to say a few kind words for us and persuade her to make a special exception.”
我已经托了七姑奶奶了，等我太太一来，请她去疏通，多说两句好话，特别通融一次。』

“That is strange!”
『那就奇怪了！』

A’qiao sounded angry.
阿巧姐有些气愤，

“She urged me to go back instead—the exact opposite of what you asked.
『七姑奶奶反而劝我回去；跟你托她的意思，完全相反，

Why?”
这是为啥？』


Hu deeply regretted his carelessness.
胡雪岩深为失悔，自己太疏忽了！

Knowing what the Seventh Lady had advised, he should not have told the truth and made her seem disloyal to the task entrusted to her.
明知道七姑奶奶劝她的话是什么；不该再说实话，显得七姑奶奶为人谋而不忠。

At the same time, A’qiao’s question alerted him to something.
同时也被提醒了，

Yes—what did the Seventh Lady mean by acting so?
真的，七三奶奶这样做是什么意思，

It was puzzling.
倒费人猜疑。

Whatever the reason, he must defend her now.
然而，不论如何，眼前却必须为七姑奶奶辩白，

“Perhaps she was only trying to discover your attitude.
『也许她是先探探你的口气。』

What did she say?”
他问∶『她怎么说？』

“She said, ‘A woman must have a proper settled place.
『她说∶』妇道人家总要有个归宿，

Better to join the Hu family formally and kowtow upon entering the household.
还是正式姓了胡，进门磕了头的好。

Otherwise you should make a firm decision.’”
不然，就不如拿个决断出来！

“What did she mean by a firm decision?”
「『』何谓」拿个决断出来「？

“Ask her.”
『『你去问她。』

The weariness of A’qiao’s tone showed that this “decision” meant a course she could not possibly accept.
阿巧姐这懒得说的语气，可知所谓『决断』，是一种她绝不能同意的办法。

Putting her remarks together, Hu understood the Seventh Lady’s intention, but not why she had formed it.
胡雪岩将前后语言，合起来作一个推敲，懂了七姑奶奶的心思；只不懂她为何有那样的心思？

“She often takes unexpected measures.
『七姑奶奶做事，常有教人猜想不到的手段。

Do not be angry yet.
你先不必气急，

Calm yourself and wait to see what happens.”
静下心来看一看再说。


“How long must I wait?” A’qiao suddenly cried, her voice high and shrill. “Do you know what she said about you?
『要看到什么时候？』阿巧姐突然咆哮，声音又尖又高∶『你晓不晓得七姑奶奶怎么说你？

That you are slippery!
说你滑头；

That you have no constancy and fall in love with every woman you see!
说你没有常性，见一个爱一个！

Dogs have eaten the conscience of a man like that.
这种人的良心让狗吃掉了，

She urged me to part from you soon, or I should suffer for it later.
劝我早早分手；不然将来有苦头吃。

Well, I think she was right in every word.
我看啊，她的话一点不错。

Hmph!
哼！

You could deceive a person to death and never pay for it.”
骗死人不偿命。』

The barrage, striking both directly and by insinuation, left Hu bewildered.
这样夹枪带棒一顿乱骂，拿胡雪岩搞得晕头转向，

He could scarcely believe his ears.
几乎不相信自己的耳朵。

Naturally he was angry too—not with A’qiao, but with the Seventh Lady.
心里当然也很生气；气的不是阿巧姐，而是七姑奶奶，

She had not merely failed to act loyally for him: she had betrayed a friend.
不但为人谋而不忠，简直是出卖朋友。

With such friendship between them, how could she resort to so underhand a trick?
彼此这样的交情，而竟出此阴险的鬼蜮伎俩！

It was more than he could swallow.
这口气实在教人咽不下。

He had never been so angry.
胡雪岩从来没有这样生气过；

His face turned livid and his lips pale.
气得脸青唇白，

Just as he was about to erupt, a thought warned him.
刚要发作，突然警觉，

The Seventh Lady was called a hero among women.
七姑奶奶号称『女中丈夫』，

She was neither without judgement nor ignorant of loyalty between friends.
胸中不是没有丘壑的人，更不是不懂朋友义气的人，

There must be a reason for what she had said—a very deep reason, too deep even for him to fathom.
她这样说法，当然有她的道理在内——这层道理一定极深；深得连自己都猜不透。

At that thought his expression softened at once.
这样一转念间，脸色立刻缓和了，

“What else did she say?” he asked.
先问一句∶『七姑奶奶还说点啥？』

“What else?”
『说点啥？』

A’qiao’s anger was not merely lingering; it grew as she thought.
阿巧姐岂仅余怒不息，竟是越想越恨，

“Unless you had given her some hint that you meant to abandon me, would she have said such things?
『不是你有口风给她，打算不要我了，她会说这样的话！

You heartless—” Suzhou women were fond of the curse “may you be cut into a thousand pieces”, but some tenderness remained in A’qiao, and she swallowed the words.
死没良心的——。』苏州女人受骂『杀千刀』；而阿巧姐毕竟余情犹在，把这三个字硬咽了回去。

Hu offered no defence. Until he understood the Seventh Lady’s purpose, he feared that defending himself might spoil it.
胡雪岩不作辩白∶因为不知道七姑奶奶是何道理，怕一辩就会破坏了她的用意。

Yet he could not say nothing at all.
然而不辩白又不行；

He answered vaguely, “Why must you listen to her?”
只好含含混混地说∶『你何必听她的？』


“Then whom should I listen to?
『那末，我听谁？

You?”
听你的？』

A’qiao pressed him harder. “Speak!
阿巧姐索性逼迫∶『你说，

Give me one plain, firm assurance.”
你倒扎扎实实说一句我听。』

What assurance did she mean?
何谓『扎扎实实说一句』？

He was genuinely puzzled.
胡雪岩倒有些困惑了，

“Tell me what you want me to say.”
『你说！』他问，『你要我怎么说一句？』

“There!
『你看你！

I knew your heart had changed.”
我就晓得你变心了。』

She stamped her foot and spoke bitterly. “Do you really not know what to say?
阿巧姐踩着脚恨声说道∶『你难道不晓得怎么说？

You simply refuse to say it.
不过不肯说而已！

Enough, enough!
好了，好了，

At last I know you.”
我总算认识你了。』

Her angry cries in the quiet night roused the maids and older servants.
静夜娇叱，惊起了丫头娘姨；

Shadows gathered outside the window; they seemed to want to enter and mediate but dared not.
窗外人影幢幢，是想进来解劝而不敢的模样，

Hu felt thoroughly uncomfortable.
胡雪岩自觉无趣，

He rose and urged, “It is late.
