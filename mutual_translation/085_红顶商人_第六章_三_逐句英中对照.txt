胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第六章（本章第3部分，共5部分）

原著：高阳


Her first step had really been a test.
第一步实在是试探。

If A’qiao had trusted Hu alone, laughed off the Seventh Lady’s remarks behind his back about his inconstancy and apparent heartlessness, and forgotten them, the play would have been difficult to continue.
如果阿巧姐不信她只信胡雪岩∶拿她批评胡雪岩用情不专，迹近薄幸的种种『背后之言』，付之一笑，听过丢开；这出戏就很难唱得下去了。

If Hu’s infatuation had been so deep that he strenuously denied every charge and won A’qiao’s understanding, it would have been harder still.
或者，胡雪岩对阿巧姐迷恋已深，极力辩白，决无其事，取得阿巧姐的谅解；这出戏就更难唱得下去了。

Instead, A’qiao suspected that Hu had authorised the remarks, while Hu seemed to accept the accusation in silence.
谁知阿巧姐疑心她的话，出于胡雪岩的授意；而胡雪岩居然是默认的模样，

To let such a chance slip would betray the Seventh Lady’s own purpose.
这个机会若是轻轻放过，岂不大负本心？

She therefore composed her face and spoke with great earnestness. “Little Uncle, you must give A’qiao up.
于是，她正一正脸色，显得极郑重地相劝∶『小爷叔！阿巧姐你不能要了。

An onlooker sees clearly.
旁观者清，

I have considered it for you: if you refuse to let go, there will be no end to the trouble.”
我替你想过，如果你一定不肯撒手，受累无穷——。』

She advanced four reasons. First, if A’qiao insisted on establishing a separate household, breaking Mrs Hu’s rule would set husband and wife against each other.
照七姑奶奶的说法，胡雪岩对阿巧姐有『四不可要』∶第一、阿巧姐如果一定要在外面『立门户』，坏了胡太太的家法，会搞得夫妇反目。

Second, even if she agreed to join the family, she would do so unwillingly; the grievance in her heart would destroy harmony between wife and concubine.
第二、即令阿巧姐肯『回去』，亦是很勉强的事，心中有了芥蒂，妻妾之间会失和。

Third, since she had left He Guiqing, and Hu himself had arranged that union, he ought now to avoid suspicion.
第三、阿巧姐既由何家下堂，而且当初是由胡雪岩撮合，如今就该避嫌疑；

Otherwise people might say he had originally offered a beauty in exchange for advancement—a profoundly ugly reputation.
不然，保不定会有人说他当初不过『献美求荣』，这是个极丑的名声。

Fourth, she had come from the pleasure quarters and had seen the great world in a governor-general’s residence.
第四、阿巧姐出身青楼，又在总督衙门见过大世面；

Whether such a woman could settle permanently into respectable life with Hu was very doubtful.
这样的人，是不是能够跟着胡雪岩从良到底，实在大成疑问。

“Little Uncle,” she urged finally, “when Hangzhou fell and Governor Wang died, your roots were cut and your support collapsed.
『小爷叔！』最后七姑奶奶又恳切地劝说，『杭州一失守，王雪公一殉难；你的老根断掉了，靠山倒掉了。

You must begin again.
以后等于要重起炉灶，

It will take real effort to recover your former position.
着实得下一番功夫，才能恢复从前那种场面。

If you were like Fatty Zhang, content to hold what you have and keep the family fed and clothed, I should have nothing to say.
如果说，你是象张胖子那样肯守的，只要一家吃饱穿暖就心满意足，那我没有话说；

But if you mean to build a career, this is the one time when you must not have domestic trouble.
想要创一番事业，小爷叔，你这个时候千万闹不得家务。

More than that, you need your wife’s solid support.
不但闹不得家务，还要婶娘切切实实助你一臂之力才行。

Think carefully what you stand to gain or lose.”
这当中的利害关系，你倒仔细想一想！』

Hu thought the first four reasons little more than alarming suppositions.
前面的『四不可要』，胡雪岩觉得也不过『想当然耳』的危言耸听；

But the last warning—that this was no time for domestic trouble—truly startled him.
最后一句『这个时候千万闹不得家务』，却真的让他悚然心惊了。

“Seventh Sister, you know I am not like Fatty Zhang.
『七姐，你晓得的，我不是张胖子那种人，

I mean not merely to begin again and build a career, but to build a great one.
我不但要重起炉灶创一番事业；而且要大大创它一番事业。

You have reminded me: my mind cannot be in two places at once.
你提醒了我，这个时候心无二用，

Where have I the time for family quarrels?”
哪里有功夫来闹家务——。』

“Exactly!” she broke in. “You may not want domestic trouble, but domestic trouble will come looking for you.
『是啊！』七姑奶奶抢着说∶『你不想闹家务；家务会闹到你头上来！

You cannot push it away or throw it off.
推不开，摔不掉，

That is when you will suffer.”
那才叫苦恼。

“That is just what I fear.
『』我就是怕这个！

It seems I must listen to you.”
看样子，非听你的不可了。

“At last!
『』这才是！

Thank Heaven, Little Uncle, you have come to see it!” she said joyfully.
谢天谢地，小爷叔，你总算想通了。『七姑奶奶高兴地说，』

“A’qiao is a fine woman, of course, but she is not the only one in the world.
阿巧姐自然是好的；不过也不是天下独一无二就是她！

There will be others in the future.”
将来有的是。

“The future!”
『』将来！

Hu stamped his foot. “Let it be for the sake of the future.
『胡雪岩顿一顿足∶』就看在将来上面。

Seventh Sister, let us discuss this properly.”
七姐，我们好好来谈一谈。『


They needed to decide what to do for A’qiao.
要谈的是如何处置阿巧姐。

Here the Seventh Lady hesitated. “To be honest, I must still think of a way.”
提到这一层，七姑奶奶不免踌躇∶『说实话，』她说，『我还要动脑筋！』

Hu seemed uneasy. “There is one thing you must promise me.
『七姐，』胡雪岩似乎很不放心，『我现在有句话，你一定要答应我。

Whatever plan you devise, explain it to me first.”
你动出啥脑筋来，要先跟我说明白。』

This made her a little uncomfortable and a little resentful. “Oh, listen to you! You make it sound as though I might secretly push her into some dreadful fate.”
这话使得七姑奶奶微觉不安，也微有反感∶『哟！哟！你这样子说法，倒象我会瞒着你，拿她推到火炕里去似的。』

She defended herself with considerable feeling.
她很费劲地分辩，

“A’qiao and I have always got on well.
『我跟阿巧姐一向处得很好，

Now, for your sake, I am acting against my conscience, and you seem to blame me for taking too much upon myself—” “Seventh Sister, Seventh Sister!”
现在为了你小爷叔，抹熬良心做事；你好象反倒埋怨我独断独行——。』『七姐，七姐！』

Hu stopped her with a deep bow.
胡雪岩不容她再往下说，兜头长揖，

“I must not be the dog that bites Lü Dongbin and fails to recognise a benefactor.
『我不能「狗咬吕洞宾，不识好人心」，

I merely feel I have wronged her and want to compensate her properly.”
无非我自己觉得对不起她，要想好好补报她一番而已。』

“Do I feel otherwise?
『我还不是这样？

Rest assured.
你放心好了，

I shall never devise anything to harm her.”
我决不会动她的坏脑筋。』

Suddenly her eyes brightened.
说到这里，七姑奶奶的眼睛突然发亮；

A smile broke over her face as she gazed into space.
同时绽开笑靥，望空出神。

A very good idea had occurred to her.
这是动到了极好的脑筋。

Hu dared not interrupt, though he was impatient to hear it.
胡雪岩不敢打搅她；但心里却急得很！渴望她揭开谜底。

She, however, seemed determined to take a little revenge. “I have most of it worked out.
七姑奶奶却似有意报复∶『我想得差不多了。

But forgive me, Little Uncle: I have not begun yet.
不过，小爷对不起，我现在不没有动手，

When I do, I shall explain it clearly, and you will certainly approve.”
到开始做的时候，一定跟你说明白；你也一定会赞成。』

“Why not tell me first?” he asked with an ingratiating smile.
『七姐！』胡雪岩陪笑说道∶『你何妨先跟我说说？』

“No.
『不行，

At least let me think it through before I tell you.
起码要等我想妥当，才能告诉你。』

I am not deliberately keeping you in suspense.
七姑奶奶又说，『不是我故意卖关子，

I am simply not yet sure of it, so I had better say nothing for the moment.”
实在是还没有把握，不如暂且不说的好。』

Her evasions left him entirely uncertain what she was planning.
听她言词闪烁，竟不知她葫芦里卖的什么药？

Knowing her temperament, he realised further questions were useless and gave up with a sigh.
以她的性情，再问亦无用，胡雪岩只好叹口气算了。


The next day Hu called again.
到了第二天，胡雪岩又去看七姑奶奶，

Gu was there too.
恰好古应春也在，

They discussed the approaching arrival of Hu’s family and the need to find a house, furnish it and obtain everyday necessities.
谈起家眷将到，另外要找房子，置家具，备办日用物品，

Normally A’qiao could have been asked to see to these things, but now it seemed awkward to trouble her.
本来可以关照阿巧姐动手的，此刻似乎不便麻烦她了。

“Never mind!”
『不要紧！』

The Seventh Lady was always most enthusiastic about such matters and enjoyed them greatly.
七姑奶奶在这些事上最热心，也最有兴趣，

“Leave it all to me.”
慨然应承∶『都交给我好了。』

Listening beside them, Gu was puzzled.
在一旁静听的古应春，不免困惑，

“Why can A’qiao not help?”
『为啥不能请阿巧姐帮忙？』他问。

“There is a reason,” his wife interrupted. “I shall tell you later.”
『其中自然有道理。』七姑奶奶抢着说∶『回头告诉你。』

“What new device is this?” He warned her, “Do not give Little Uncle ill-considered advice.
『又是什么花样？』古应春跟他妻子提忠告∶『你可不要替小爷叔乱出主意。

At this moment, peace is what matters most.”
现在这个辰光，顶要紧的就是安静二字。』

“Peace is precisely the purpose.”
『正是为了安静两个字。』

She did not want her husband interfering and hurried him along. “Were you not invited to a courtesan banquet?
七姑奶奶不愿丈夫打搅，催着他说∶『不是说，有人请你吃花酒；

You can go now.”
可以走了。』

“One waits for a reminder before going to such a banquet.
『吃花酒要等人来催请，

Who hurries off on his own at this hour?”
哪有这么早，自己赶了去的？』

Gu saw what she meant and thought it best to comply.
古应春看出妻子的意思，觉得还是顺从为妙；

Finding his own way out, he added, “Still, I can visit a friend first.”
所以又自己搭讪着说∶『也好！我先去看个朋友。』

“Wait!” she said.
『慢点！』七姑奶奶说，

“I remember Mr Qin once mentioning that a relative of his had a house on Third Road, available either for sale or under a redeemable property pledge.
『我想起来了，有次秦先生说起，他的亲戚有幢房子在三马路，或卖或典都可以，

Ask about it for Little Uncle.”
你不妨替小爷叔去问一问。』

Mr Qin was the accountant at their firm.
秦先生是她家号子里的帐房。

Obedient to his wife’s command, Gu agreed to question him at once and asked Hu to return for news the following day.
古应春恪遵阃令，答应立刻去看秦先生细问；请胡雪岩第二天来听消息。

“Better still,” she said, “ask Mr Qin to come here early tomorrow.”
『这样吧，』七姑奶奶说，『你索性请秦先生明天一早来一趟。』

“You probably want him to write another letter,” Gu observed.
『大概又是请他写信。』古应春说，

“If he is free tonight, I shall send him over then.”
『如果今天晚上有空，我就叫他来。』


Once her husband had gone, the Seventh Lady returned to A’qiao’s affairs.
于是七姑奶奶等丈夫一走，便又跟胡雪岩谈阿巧姐，

“Little Uncle, is your mind made up?
『小爷叔，』他问∶『你的主意打定了？

You will not regret it later and complain behind my back that I struck apart a pair of loving mandarin ducks?”
将来不会懊悔，背后埋怨我棒打鸳鸯两分离？』

“How could I?
『哪有这样的事？

Do you still not know my temper?”
七姐在现在还不明白我的脾气？』

“I know: you do what you say, and do not regret what you have done.
『我晓得，小爷叔是说到做到、做了不悔的脾气。

Still, I had better ask.
不过，我还是问一声的好，

Since you are determined, I shall begin tomorrow.
既然小爷叔主意打定，明天我就要动手了。

You must merely pretend to know nothing.
你只装不知道，

Whatever unusual thing you notice, keep it to yourself.”
看出什么异样，放在肚子里就是。』

“I understand.
『我懂！』

But what if she presses me with questions?”
胡雪岩问∶『她如果要逼着我问，我怎么样？』

“She will not.
『不会逼着你问的，

Everything will continue as before, without a change.
一切照旧，毫无变动，

What would she ask?”
她问什么？』

“Very well.
『好的！

As we say in Hangzhou, I shall watch the fire from City God Hill.
那就是我们杭州人说的那句话∶』城隍山上看火烧！

I shall wait and enjoy the spectacle.”
「我只等着看热闹了。

Between less intimate friends, this might have sounded offensive or sarcastic.
『如果不是极深的交情，这句话就有讽刺意味的语病了。

Even so, she warned him not to suppose that he had removed himself from the affair.
不过七姑奶奶还是提醒他，不可自以为已经置身事外；

Once the fire began, events might alarm him and draw him in despite himself.
一旦火烧了起来，也许会惊心动魄，身不由主，

He must remain firm, act as though he saw nothing and above all avoid losing his composure and intervening.
那时一定要有定方，视如不见，切忌临时沉不住气，横身插入，

“Otherwise you will bring the fire upon yourself, and I shall suffer too.
那一来，她说∶『就会引火烧身；我也要受连累，

In a word, whatever A’qiao says, pay no attention.”
总而言之一句话，不管阿巧姐说什么，你不要理她！』

Hu’s need to buy a house had given her an idea.
原来七姑奶奶由胡雪岩要买房子，想到一个主意，

She would use it to provoke A’qiao into leaving of her own accord and settle the matter once for all.
决定借这个机会刺激阿巧姐，能把她气走了，一了百了。

But there might also be a great storm, hence her special warning.
但也可能会发生极大的风波，所以特意提出警告。


The purchase went smoothly.
购屋之事，相当顺利；

The house Mr Qin recommended stood on Third Road near the famous Huajin Lane.
秦先生所介绍的那幢房子，在三马路靠近有名的画锦里，

Though the neighbourhood was busy, the house was spacious and deep; closing its great door shut out the clamour and created quiet amid the bustle.
虽是闹事，但屋宇宏深，关紧大门，就可以隔绝市嚣，等于闹中取静。

Hu liked it very much.
胡雪岩深为中意，

The price was modest too—only two thousand five hundred eagle silver dollars—so he completed the purchase that day.
问价钱也不贵，只有鹰洋两千五百元；所以当天就成交了。

The Seventh Lady was full of zeal.
七姑奶奶奶非常热心，

“Little Uncle, give me another thousand dollars and leave everything to me.
『小爷叔，』她说，『你再拿一千块钱给我；一切都归我包办。

Attend to your own affairs for three days.
这三天你去干你的事；

On the fourth, come and see what I have made of it.”
到第四天你来看，是啥样子？』

“What more could I ask?
『这还有啥好说的？

But it is so much trouble for you, Seventh Sister.”
不过，七姐，太费你的心了！』

He knew her ways.
胡雪岩知道她的脾气，

That polite acknowledgement was enough.
这样说句客气话就行了。

If he worried that she was overworking and sent someone to share the burden, he would only displease her.
如果觉得她过于劳累，于心不安，要派人去为她分劳，反使得她不高兴，

He gave her a thousand silver dollars and neither asked nor interfered.
所以交了一千银洋给她，不闻不问。

During the three days he examined the accounts at his own bank and enquired into its business, accomplishing some solid work.
趁这三天功夫，在自己钱庄里盘一盘帐，问一问业务，倒是切切实实做了些事。

On the third day he came home from Fukang Bank in Jixian Lane and found A’qiao with her hair dressed, her face carefully powdered and her lips coloured.
第三天从集贤里阜康钱庄回家，只见阿巧姐头光面滑，点唇涂脂，是打扮过了；

Yet she wore informal clothes.
但身上却穿的是家常衣衫，

Was she about to go out or had she just returned?
不知是正要出门，还是从外面回来？

“I have only just come back.
『我刚回来。

I went to see the Seventh Lady,” she said.
我去看七姑奶奶了。』阿巧姐说，

“The house on Third Road has been made very handsome.”
『三马路的房子，弄得很漂亮啊！』


Her tone was calm, but Hu thought he heard a reproach for keeping it from her.
语气很平静，但在胡雪岩听来，似有怨责他瞒着她的味道；

Embarrassed, he hardly knew how to answer.
因而讪讪地有些无从接口。

“She asked whether I liked the house.
『七姑奶奶问我∶房子好不好？

Naturally I said yes.
我自然说好。

Then she asked if I wanted to live there.
她又问我想不想去住；

Do you know what I told her?
你道我怎么回答她？

That I was not so fortunate.” Hu almost replied, “I fear I am the one without that good fortune,” but checked the words.
我说∶我没有这份福气。』胡雪岩本来想答一句∶只怕是我没有这份福气。话到口边，忽又缩住；

Casually he said, “What good fortune is there in one of these so-called lane houses in the foreign settlement?
用漫不经意的口吻答道∶『住这种夷场上的所谓「弄堂房子」，算啥福气？

When Hangzhou is recovered, I shall build a proper estate beside West Lake.
将来杭州光复，在西湖上好好造一座庄子；

To live in such a paradise would indeed require merit accumulated in a former life.”
住那种洞天福地，可真就要前世修一修了。』

A’qiao said nothing.
阿巧姐不作声，

She went to the dressing table to remove her ornaments, while a maid helped Hu take off his riding jacket and change into slippers.
坐到梳妆台前去卸头面首饰；胡雪岩便由丫头伺候着，脱掉马褂，换上便鞋，

He sat by the window with his tea.
坐在窗前喝茶。

“I think I had better accumulate merit for the next life,” she said suddenly.
『我看，』阿巧姐突然说道∶『我修修来世吧！』

“In the next life we shall be husband and wife,” he answered without thinking.
『来世我们做夫妻。』胡雪岩脱口相答。

Her face changed dramatically.
阿巧姐颜色大变——

He meant that, since she would not become his concubine in this life, he could only hope that in the next they might be one husband and one wife, growing old together.
在胡雪岩的意思，既然她今生不肯嫁胡家的偏房；那就只好期望来世一夫一妻，白头到老。

But she misunderstood.
而阿巧姐误会了！

“I wondered why the Seventh Lady said those things.
『我原在奇怪，七姑奶奶为啥说那些话？

So your heart really has changed!
果不其然，你是变心了！

If you have something to say, say it yourself.
有话你很可以自己说，

Why go round corners and get someone else to do it?”
何必转弯抹角去托人？』


Hu knew he had spoken wrongly.
胡雪岩知道自己失言了。

Yet he could hardly be blamed.
然而也实在不能怪自己；

He had asked the Seventh Lady what to say if A’qiao pressed him about her future.
那天原就问过七姑奶奶，如果阿巧姐逼着要问她的归宿？如何作答。

She had assured him that, with everything unchanged, no questions would arise.
七姑奶奶认为『一切照旧，毫无变动』，她不会问。

But things were different now.
照现在看，情形不同了！

A’qiao had seen the new house; the change had begun.
新居既已为她所见，『变动』便已开始，

She would keep asking, and he could not have her fly into a rage every time, as she had now.
以后她不断会问；总不能每次一问，便象此刻一样，惹得她怨气冲天。

It seemed he must rely on his own ingenuity after all.
看来还是要靠自己动脑筋应付！他这样对自己说；

He began studying her attitude closely.
而且马上很用心地去体察她的态度。

Why did she not consider her own refusal to live with his principal wife?
为什么她不自己想一想，她这样不肯与大妇同住，

She was defying accepted practice and asking the impossible, yet repeatedly accused him of a change of heart.
悖乎常情，强人所准；而偏偏一再要指责他变心？

Could it be that she herself wished to leave but could not say so, and was pressing him and shifting blame in order to make him responsible for the break?
莫非她自己有下堂求去之意，只是说不出口，有意这样诿过，这样逼迫；想把决裂的责任，加在他头上？

The thought seemed almost absurd.
这是个看来近乎荒诞的想法。

Was he judging her with a petty mind?
胡雪岩自问∶果真自己是小人之心？

Not necessarily.
不见得！

She had once given her heart to He Guiqing too.
阿巧姐当初对何桂清亦曾倾心过，

Whatever might be said, in the end she had failed him—and when he was in misfortune.
到后来不管怎么说，总是负心；而且是在何桂清倒霉的时候负心。

Was it not rather naive to regard her as a paragon?
这样看起来，将她看成一个『君子』，似乎也太天真了些。

In that moment Hu felt his own heart harden.
就这一念之间，他自己觉得心肠硬了；

In a quiet voice, almost cold, he said, “I have nothing to say.
用不大带感情的、平静得近乎冷漠的声音说∶『我没有什么话好说。

If you wish to cultivate good fortune for another life, I can only hope that we shall be husband and wife then.”
你愿意修修来世，我当然也只好希望来世再做夫妻。』

She turned and looked directly at him. “You mean you do not want me in this life?”
『你的意思是，今生今世不要我了？』阿巧姐转过脸过来，逼视着他问。

He avoided her eyes.
他将视线避了开去，

“I did not say that.
『我没有说这话，

Only—” He stopped.
不过——。』他没有再说下去。


“Say it!
『说啊！

A grown man should not mumble and hesitate.”
男子汉大丈夫，说话不要吞吞吐吐！』

If she had been willing to yield for the sake of peace, she might have wept at his tone, but would not have pursued the question.
遇到他这种口吻语气，如果她是愿意委屈息事的，至多流泪，不会追问，

By pressing him, she showed that she was prepared for an open break.
既然追问，便有不惜破脸的打算。

Hu felt it was enough to understand her position.
胡雪岩觉得了解她的态度就够了；

There was no need to quarrel now—better never to quarrel, but to part amicably.
此时犯不着跟她破脸——最好永不破脸，好来好散！

He smiled. “Neither of us is a two- or three-year-old child.
于是他笑笑说道∶『我们都不是三岁两岁的小孩，

Why make ourselves ridiculous before the servants?”
这个样子教底下人笑话，何必呢？』

“Hmph!”
『哼！』

She gave a cold laugh and turned back to the mirror to remove her make-up.
阿巧姐冷笑了一下，依然回过脸去，对镜卸妆。

Hu felt thoroughly weary of the place, a feeling he had never known before.
胡雪岩觉得无聊得很。这种感觉是以前所从不曾有过的；

He spent little time at home, but whenever he returned, the mere sight of A’qiao had made it seem the most comfortable place in the world.
他在家的时候不多，所以一回到家，只要看见阿巧姐的影子，便觉得世界上只有这个家最舒服，

Only absolute necessity could draw him out again.
非万不得已，不肯再出门。

Now he wanted to go somewhere—anywhere, even for a walk in the street.
而此刻，却想到哪里去走走；哪怕就在街上逛逛也好。

The impulse grew irresistible.
此念一动，不可抑制；

Rising, he said, “I must go out again.”
站起身来说∶『我还要出去一趟。』

Then, feeling apologetic, he asked, “Is there anything you would like to eat?
说了这话，又觉歉然，因而问道∶『你想吃点啥？

I shall bring it back.”
我替你带回来。』

She merely shook her head, as though even speech were too much trouble.
阿巧姐只摇摇头，似乎连话也懒得说。

A chill passed repeatedly down his back.
胡雪岩觉得背上一阵一阵发冷；

He left at once, going straight downstairs in his slippers, without putting on his riding jacket.
拔步就走，就穿着那双便鞋，也不着马褂，径自下楼而去。


Outside the door he felt at a loss. A’fu, one of his sedan-chair bearers, hurried after him. “Where are you going, sir?
走出大门，不免茫然∶『轿班』阿福赶来问道∶『老爷要到哪里去？

I shall fetch the men.”
我去叫人。』

There were four bearers.
轿班一共四个人；

Hu had said on returning that he would not go out again that evening, so the other three, who lodged at Fukang Bank, had already left.
因为胡雪岩回家时曾经说过，这夜不再出门，所以那三个住在阜康钱庄的都已走了，

Only A’fu remained.
只剩下阿福在家。

“No need!”
『不必！』

Hu waved a hand and went out of the lane alone.
胡雪岩摆一摆手，径自出弄堂而去。

He wandered without purpose, dispirited.
茫然闲步，意兴阑珊；

He tried to think of something pleasant, but could not put A’qiao out of his mind.
心里要想些有趣的事，偏偏抛不开的是阿巧姐。

Her lovely, expressive eyes and enchanting smile remained before him; her soft Wu voice, sweet as an oriole’s, sounded clearly in his ears.
美目盼兮，巧笑倩兮，那些影子都在眼前；其美如莺的吴枕软语亦清清楚楚地响在耳际。

Suddenly he was seized by overwhelming regret.
突然间，胡雪岩有着浓重的悔意；

He turned and walked home at great speed.
掉头就走，而且脚步极快。


The stone-framed outer door was already shut.
到家只见石库墙门已经关上了，

He knocked several times with the bronze ring.
叩了几下铜环，

A’fu opened it, and Hu went straight upstairs.
来开门的仍是阿福；胡雪岩踏进门便上楼，

One glance chilled his heart.
一眼望去，心先凉了！

“Where is your mistress?”
『奶奶呢？』

Pointing to the dark bedroom, he questioned Suxiang, the maid who had come out of another room to meet him.
他指着漆黑的卧室；向从另一间屋里迎出来的丫头素香问说。

“She has gone out.”
『奶奶出去了。』

“Where?”
『到哪里？』

“She did not say.”
『没有说。』

“When did she leave?”
『什么时候走的？』

“As soon as you had gone, she said she was going out.
『老爷一走，奶奶就说要出去。』

I asked her a question and she scolded me: ‘Mind your own business.’”
素香答说∶『我问了一声，奶奶骂我∶少管闲事。』

“How did she go?
『那，怎么走的呢？』

Why did she not take you?”
胡雪岩问∶『为什么没有要你跟去？』

“She would not have me with her.
『奶奶不要我跟去；

She said she would be back shortly.
说是等一息就回来。

I asked if I should hire a chair, and she said she could get one herself at the entrance to the lane.”
我说∶要不要雇顶轿子？她说，她自己到弄堂口会雇的。』

Hu was deeply disappointed and full of misgivings.
胡雪岩大为失望，而且疑虑重重，

He had returned to tell her that everything would remain as before, without a change.
原来想跟阿巧姐来说∶『一切照旧，毫无变动』；

Whatever his wife might say, he had resolved to maintain this separate household.
不管胡太太怎么说，他决意维持这个外室。

Unless A’qiao herself wished to choose another man, he would never change his heart.
除非阿巧姐愿意另外择人而事，他是决不会变心的。

Now all that warmth sank into an abyss.
这一番热念，此刻全都沉入深渊。

Her movements seemed deeply suspicious.
而且觉得阿巧姐的行踪，深为可疑；

Suxiang was her personal maid and always accompanied her outside.
素香是她贴身的丫头，出门总是伴随的，

If she had left the girl behind, she must have gone somewhere the maid could not go—or somewhere she wished to conceal even from Suxiang.
而竟撇下不带，可知所去的这个地方，是素香去不得的，或者说，是她连素香都要瞒住的。


With that thought, an inexpressible bitterness and despondency filled him.
意会到此，心中泛起难以言宣的酸苦抑郁；

He stood in the sitting room without speaking for a long time.
站在客堂中，久久无语。

Suxiang grew frightened.
这使得素香有些害怕，

“Sir,” she asked timidly, “will you dine at home?
怯怯地问道∶『老爷！是不是在家吃饭？

I shall tell the kitchen.”
我去关照厨房。』

“I am not hungry.
『我不饿！』

Where is A’xiang?”
胡雪岩问∶『阿祥呢？』

“A’xiang has gone out.”
『阿祥，出去了。』

“Out?
『出去了！

Where?”
到哪里？』

“You would have to—” Suxiang faltered. “You would have to ask A’fu.”
『要——，』素香吞吞吐吐地说∶『要问阿福。』
