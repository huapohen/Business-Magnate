胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第六章（本章第4部分，共5部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


这神态亦颇为可疑，胡雪岩忍不住要发怒；
Her manner too seemed suspicious, and Hu nearly lost his temper.

但一转念间冷静了，
Then he steadied himself.

『你叫阿福来！』他说。
“Fetch A’fu.”

等把阿福喊来一回，才知究竟，
Once the bearer had been called and questioned, the truth emerged.

阿祥是在附近的一家小杂货店『白相』。
A’xiang was “amusing himself” at a little grocery nearby.

那家杂货店老夫妇两个，只有一个十七岁的女儿；
Its elderly owners had only one child, a daughter of seventeen.

胡雪岩也见过，生得象『无锡大阿福』，圆圆胖胖的一张脸，笑口常开。
Hu had seen her: she resembled a cheerful Wuxi clay doll, with a plump, round face and a smile that never left it.

阿祥情有所钟，
A’xiang had set his heart on her.

只等胡雪岩一出门，便到那家杂货店去盘桓；是他家不支薪工饭食的伙计兼跑街。
As soon as Hu went out, he would linger in the shop, acting as an unpaid assistant and errand boy, without even receiving his meals.

『老爷要喊他，我去把他叫回来。』
“If you want him, sir, I shall fetch him.”

『不必！』
“No need.”

胡雪岩听得这段『新闻』；心里舒服了些，
The news made Hu feel rather better.

索性丢下阿巧姐来管阿祥的闲事，
He might as well leave A’qiao aside and concern himself with A’xiang’s affairs.

『照这样说，蛮有意思了！
“That sounds promising.

那家的女儿，叫啥名字？』
What is the girl’s name?”

『跟——，』阿福很吃力地说∶『跟奶奶的小名一样。』
“The same as—” A’fu struggled to get the words out. “The same familiar name as our mistress.”

原来也叫阿巧，
So she too was called A’qiao.

『那倒真是巧了！』胡雪岩兴味盎然地笑着。
“A coincidence indeed!” Hu laughed with interest.


『我跟阿祥说，你叫人家的时候，不要直呼直令地叫人家的名字；
“I told A’xiang not to call her directly by her name.

那样子犯了奶奶的讳。做下人的不好这样子没规矩。』
It is our mistress’s name too, and a servant ought not to be so disrespectful.”

这是知书识礼的人才会有的见解，
This was the sort of observation one expected from a man versed in books and propriety.

不想出现在两条烂泥腿的轿班身上，胡雪岩既惊异又高兴；
Hearing it from a muddy-legged chairbearer surprised and pleased Hu.

但口中问的还是阿祥。
But his questions remained about A’xiang.

『他不叫人家小名叫啥？』
“What does he call her, then?

胡雪岩问∶『莫非叫姐姐、
Sister?

妹妹？
Little Sister?

那不是太麻肉了。』
That would be rather sickly.”

『是啊！那也太肉麻。
“Yes, far too sickly.

阿祥告诉我说，他跟人家根本彼此都不叫名字，
He told me they never use each other’s names.

两个人都是「喂」呀「喂」的。
They simply say ‘Hey!’ to one another.

在她父母面前提起来，阿祥是说「你们家大小姐」。』
When he speaks of her to her parents, he calls her ‘your young lady’.”

『这倒妙！』
“Excellent!”

胡雪岩心想男女之间，彼此都用『喂』字称呼，辨声知人，就决不是泛泛的情分了；
Hu thought that when a young man and woman needed only “Hey!” and recognised each other by their voices, theirs was no ordinary acquaintance.

只不知道∶『她父母对阿祥怎么样？』
But how did her parents feel?

『她家父母对阿祥蛮中意的。』
“They are very pleased with him.”

『怎么叫蛮中意？』
“How pleased?

胡雪岩问∶『莫非当他「毛脚女婿」看待？』
Do they regard him as a prospective son-in-law?”

『也差不多有那么点意思。』
“Something like that.”

『既然如此，你们应该出来管管闲事，吃他一杯喜酒啊！』
“Then you ought to lend a hand and earn a cup of wedding wine.”

『阿祥是老爷买来的，
“A’xiang was bought into your household, sir.

凡事要听老爷作主；
Everything must be decided by you.

我们怎么敢管这桩闲事，
We would not dare interfere.

再说，这桩闲事也管不了。』
Besides, we could not arrange it.”

『怎么呢？』
“Why not?”

『办喜事要——。』胡雪岩会意，点点头说∶『我知道了。
“A wedding needs—” Hu understood and nodded. “I see.

你把阿祥替我去叫回来。』
Fetch him for me.”


用不到一盏茶的功夫，阿祥被找了回来。
Before a cup of tea could have been drunk, A’xiang was back.

脸上讪讪地，有些不大好意思；显然的，他在路上就已听阿福说过，知道是怎么一回事了。
His embarrassed expression showed that A’fu had explained the matter on the way.

『你今年十几？』
“How old are you?”

『十七。』
“Seventeen.”

『十七！』
“Seventeen!”

胡雪岩略有些踌躇似的，
Hu hesitated slightly.

『是早了些。』
“Rather early.”

他停了一下又问∶『「他们家大小姐」几岁？』
After a pause he asked, “And how old is ‘their young lady’?”

这句对阿巧的称呼，是学着阿祥说的；自是玩笑，听来却有讥嘲之意，阿祥大窘，
He was playfully repeating A’xiang’s own phrase, but it sounded like mockery to the boy, who grew painfully embarrassed.

嗫嚅着说∶『比我大两月，
“Two months older than I am.

我是九月里生的，她的生日是七月七。』
I was born in the ninth month; her birthday is the seventh of the seventh month.”

『连人家的时辰八字都晓得了！』
“You even know her birth particulars!”

胡雪岩有此忍俊不禁；但为了维持尊严，不得不忍笑问道∶『那家人家姓啥？』
Hu could scarcely keep from laughing, but restrained himself to preserve his dignity. “What is the family’s surname?”

『姓魏。』
“Wei.”

『魏老板对你怎么样？』
“How does Mr Wei treat you?

胡雪岩说，『不是预备拿女儿给你？
Is he thinking of giving you his daughter?

你不要难为情，
Do not be shy.

跟我说实话。』
Tell me the truth.”

『我跟老爷当然说实话。』
“Of course I would tell you the truth, sir.

阿祥答道∶『魏老板倒没有说什么；老板娘有口风透露了，
Mr Wei has said nothing, but his wife has dropped a hint.

她说∶他们老夫妇只有一个女儿，舍不得分开。
She says they have only one daughter and cannot bear to part with her.

要娶她女儿就要入赘。』
Whoever marries her must join their household.”

『你怎么说呢？』
“What did you say?”

『我装糊涂。』
“I pretended not to understand.”

『为啥？』
“Why?

胡雪岩说∶『是不肯入赘到魏家？』
Are you unwilling to marry into the Wei family?”

『我肯也没有用。
“It would be no use if I were willing.

我改姓了主人家的姓，
I have taken my master’s surname.

怎么再去姓魏？』
How could I change it again to Wei?”

『你倒也算是有良心的。』
“You have a conscience, at least.”

胡雪岩满意地点点头，
Hu nodded approvingly.

『我自有道理。』
“I shall see what can be done.”

这当然是好事可谐了！
That plainly meant the happy outcome was possible.

阿祥满心欢喜；
A’xiang was delighted.

但脸皮到底还薄，
Yet he was still shy.

明知是个极好的机会，却不敢开口相求，就此『敲打转脚』拿好事弄定了它。
He knew this was an excellent opportunity, but could not bring himself to ask and settle the matter while the chance was before him.


不说话却又感到僵手僵脚，一身不自在；于是搭讪着问道∶『老爷恐怕还没有吃饭？
Saying nothing left him awkward and uncomfortable, so he found an excuse to speak. “You have probably not eaten, sir.

我来关照他们！』接着便喊∶『素香，
I shall tell them.” He called, “Suxiang!

素香！』
Suxiang!”

素香从下房里闪了出来，正眼都不看阿祥；
She emerged from the servants’ room without looking him in the face.

走过他面前，低低咕哝了一句∶『叫魂一样叫！』然后到胡雪岩面前问道∶『老爷叫我？』
Passing him, she muttered, “Must you call as though summoning a soul?” Then she asked Hu, “Did you call me, sir?”

做主人的看在眼里，恍然大悟；
He saw it all and understood.

怪不得问她阿祥在哪里？她有点懒得答理的模样！
No wonder she had seemed reluctant to answer when he asked where A’xiang was.

原来阿祥跟魏阿巧好了，她在吃醋。
She was jealous of his attachment to the Wei girl.

照此说来，落花有意，流水无情；阿祥倒辜负她了。
The falling blossom was willing, but the flowing water felt nothing: A’xiang had disappointed her.

这样想着，便有些替素香委屈。
Hu felt sorry for her.

不过事到如今，没有胡乱干预，扰乱已成之局的道理，
Yet there was no reason to interfere blindly and upset what was already taking shape.

惟有装作不解；找件事差遣素香去做。
He could only pretend not to understand and find her something to do.


『我不在家吃饭了。』他嘱咐阿祥∶『你马上到张老板那里去，说我请他吃酒。
“I shall not dine at home.” He told A’xiang, “Go straight to Mr Zhang and say I invite him for wine.

弄堂口那家酒店叫啥字号？』
What is the name of the tavern at the entrance to the lane?”

『叫王宝和。』
“Wang Baohe.”

『我在王宝和等他。
“I shall wait for him there.

你去快点，请他马上来。』
Be quick, and ask him to come at once.”

『是！』
“Yes, sir!”

阿祥如奉了将军令一般，高声答应，急步下楼。
The boy answered as loudly as though he had received a general’s command, then hurried downstairs.

等他一走，胡雪岩喝完一杯素香倒来的茶，也就出门了。
After he had gone, Hu finished the tea Suxiang had poured and went out too.

走到王宝和，朝里一望；王老板眼尖，急忙迎了出来，哈腰曲背地连连招呼∶『胡大人怎么有空来？
As he looked into Wang Baohe, the sharp-eyed proprietor hurried forward, bowing repeatedly. “What brings Your Excellency here?

是不是寻啥人？』
Are you looking for someone?”

『不是！
“No.

到你这里来吃酒。』
I have come for a drink.”

王老板顿时有受宠若惊之感∶『请！请！
Mr Wang was overwhelmed by the honour. “Please come in!

正好雅座有空。
The private table is free.

胡大人来得巧了。』
You have chosen just the right moment.”

所谓雅座是凸出的一块方丈之地，一张条案配着一张八仙桌；
The private space was a small projecting recess, about a square zhang, furnished with a narrow side table and a square dining table.

条案上还供着一座神龛，内中一方『王氏昭穆宗亲之位』的神牌。
On the side table stood a shrine containing a tablet to the ancestors of the Wang family.

胡雪岩看这陈设，越发勾起乡思；
The setting deepened Hu’s homesickness.

仿佛置身在杭州盐桥附近的小酒店中，记起与张胖子闲来买醉的那些日子了。
He seemed to be in a little tavern near Salt Bridge in Hangzhou, remembering the days when he and Fatty Zhang would drink together in their leisure.

『胡大人，我开一坛如假包换的绍兴花雕；
“Your Excellency, I shall open a jar of genuine Shaoxing Huadiao—guaranteed.

您老人家尝尝看。』
Try it.”

『随你。』
“As you please.

胡雪岩问∶『有啥下酒菜？』
What have you to eat with it?”

『蛏子刚上市。
“Razor clams have just come into season.

还有鞭笋；嫩得很。再就是酱鸭，糟鸡。』
There are tender whip bamboo shoots too, as well as soy-braised duck and chicken in wine lees.”

『都拿来好了。
“Bring them all.

另外要两样东西，「独脚蟹」，油炸臭豆腐干。』
And two other things: one-legged crabs and fried stinking bean curd.”

『独脚蟹』就是发芽豆，大小酒店必备；
“One-legged crabs” meant sprouted beans, available in every tavern, large or small.

油炸臭豆腐干就难了，
The bean curd was harder.

『这时候，担子都过去了。』王老板说，
“The vendors have all passed by at this hour,” Mr Wang said.

『还不知有没有？』
“I do not know whether any can still be found.”

『一定要！』胡雪岩固执地说，
“I must have it,” Hu insisted.

『你叫个人，多走两步路去找，
“Send someone a little farther to look.

一定要买来！』
He must bring some back.”

『是，是！
“Certainly, certainly!

一定买来，一定买来！』王老板一叠连声地答应，
We shall find it.”

叫个小徒弟遍处去找，还特地关照一句∶『快去快回。』
Mr Wang sent a young apprentice searching, with particular orders to hurry back.


于是，胡雪岩先独酌。
Hu began drinking alone.

一桌子的酒菜，他单取一样发芽豆；
Of all the dishes on the table, he touched only the sprouted beans.

咀嚼的不是豆子，而是寒微辰光那份苦中作乐的滋味。
What he savoured was not the beans, but the little pleasures wrested from hardship in his humble days.

心里是说不出的那种既辛酸、又安慰的隽永向往的感觉。
An inexpressible mixture of pain, comfort and lingering longing filled him.

一抬眼突然发觉，张胖子笑嘻嘻地站在面前；
Suddenly he looked up and saw Fatty Zhang smiling before him.

才知道自己是想得出神了。
Only then did he realise how far his thoughts had wandered.

定定神问道∶『吃了饭没有？』
Collecting himself, he asked, “Have you eaten?”

『正在吃酒，阿祥来到。』
“I was just having a drink when A’xiang arrived.”

阿胖子坐下来问道∶『今天倒清闲；居然想到这里来吃酒？』
Zhang sat down. “You must be at leisure today, to think of drinking here.”

『不是清闲，
“Not at leisure.

是无聊。』
At a loose end.”

张胖子从未听他说过这种泄气的话，
Zhang had never heard such dispirited words from him.

不由得张大了眼想问∶但烫来的酒，糟香扑鼻，就顾不得说话先要喝酒了。
His eyes widened and he was about to ask, but the fragrance of the warmed wine reached him, and drinking took precedence.

『好酒！』他喝了一口说；啧啧地咂着嘴唇，
“Fine wine!” he declared after a sip, smacking his lips.

『嫡路绍兴花雕。』
“True Shaoxing Huadiao.”

『酒再好，也比不上我们在盐桥吃烧酒的味道好。』
“However fine it is, it cannot taste as good as the spirits we drank at Salt Bridge.”

『呕！』
“Oh!”

张胖子抬头四顾，
Zhang looked around.

『倒有点象我们常常去光顾的那家「纯号」酒店。』
“This does resemble the Chunhao tavern we used to frequent.”

『现在也不晓得怎么样了？』胡雪岩微微叹息着；一仰脸，干了一碗。
“Who knows what has become of it?” Hu sighed faintly and drained a bowl.

『你这个酒，不能这样子喝！
“You must not drink like that.

要吃醉的。』
You will be drunk.”

张胖子停杯不饮，愁眉苦脸地说∶『啥事情不开心？』
Zhang set down his own cup, looking troubled. “What has upset you?”

『没有啥！
“Nothing.

有点想杭州，有点想从前的日子。
I miss Hangzhou, and the old days.

老张，「贫贱之交不可忘，糟糠之妻不下堂」；
Old Zhang, ‘Never forget friends made in poverty; never cast out the wife who shared your bran and chaff.’

来，我敬你！』
Here, I drink to you.”

张胖了不知他是何感触？惴惴然看着他说∶『少吃点，少吃点！
Zhang did not know what had stirred him and watched uneasily. “Less, less!

慢慢来。』
Take it slowly.”

还好，胡雪岩是心胸开阔的人，酒德甚好；
Fortunately, Hu was broad-minded and well behaved in drink.

两碗酒下肚，只想高兴的事。
After two bowls he wanted to think only of pleasant things.

想到阿祥，便即问道∶『老张，前面有家杂货店，老板姓魏，你认不认识？』
Remembering A’xiang, he asked, “Old Zhang, do you know the owner of the grocery up ahead? His name is Wei.”


『我们是同行，
“We are in the same trade.

怎么不认识？
Of course I know him.

你问起他，总有缘故吧？』
You must have a reason for asking.”

『他有个女儿，也叫阿巧，长得圆圆的脸，倒是宜男之相。
“He has a daughter called A’qiao, with a round face—the sort of face that promises sons.

你总也很熟？』
You must know her well too?”

听这一说，张胖子的兴致来了，
Zhang’s interest awakened.

精神抖擞地坐直了身了，睁了眼睛看着胡雪岩，一面点头，一面慢吞吞地答道∶『我很熟，
He sat up briskly and looked at Hu, nodding as he answered slowly, “Very well.

十天、八天总要到我店里来一趟。』
She comes to my shop every week or so.”

『为啥？』
“Why?”

『她老子进货，到我这里来拆头寸；
“When her father needs money to restock, he borrows from me.

总是她来。』
She is always the one he sends.”

『这样说，他这个杂货店也可怜巴巴的。』
“Then his little grocery must be rather hard pressed.”

『是啊，
“Yes.

本来是小本经营。』张胖子说，
He has always had very little capital.

『就要他这样才好。
That is just what you want.

如果是殷实的话，铜钾银子上不在乎；做父母的就未必肯了。』
If the family were comfortably off and cared little for money, her parents might not agree.”

『肯什么？』
“Agree to what?”

胡雪岩不懂他的话。
Hu did not understand.

『问你啊！
“You tell me!

不是说她宜男之相？』
Did you not say she had a face that promised sons?”

胡雪岩楞了一下，突然意会；
Hu stared, then suddenly understood.

一口酒直喷了出来，赶紧转过脸去，一面呛，一面笑。将个张胖子搞得丈二金刚摸不着头。
He sprayed out a mouthful of wine and hastily turned aside, coughing and laughing, while Zhang was left completely bewildered.

『啊老张，你一辈子就是喜欢自作聪明；
“Old Zhang, you have spent your whole life thinking yourself clever.

你想到哪里去了？』
Where has your imagination taken you?”

『你，』张胖子嗫嚅着说，『你不是想讨个会养儿子的小？』
“Were you not thinking of taking a concubine who could bear you sons?” Zhang asked hesitantly.

『所以说，你是自作聪明。
“That is why I say you are too clever by half.

哪有这回事？
Nothing of the sort!

不过，谈的倒也是喜事；媒人也还是要请你去做。』接着，胡雪岩便将阿祥与阿巧的那一段情，都说给了张胖子听。
But there is a marriage to arrange, and I still want you to be the matchmaker.” He then told Zhang the whole story of A’xiang and the Wei girl’s affection.


『好啊！』张胖子秀高兴地，
“Excellent!” Zhang said delightedly.

『这个媒做来包定不会「春梅浆」！』
“This match certainly will not become a bowl of ‘spring-plum pulp’.”

『春梅浆』是杭州的俗语，
That was a Hangzhou expression.

做媒做成一对怨偶，男女两家都嗔怨媒人，
If a matchmaker brought together an unhappy couple, both families blamed him.

有了纠纷，责成媒人去办交涉，搞得受累无穷，就叫『春梅浆』。
Every dispute was referred back to him for negotiation, burdening him without end; such a predicament was called “spring-plum pulp”.

老张说这话，就表示他对这头姻缘，亦很满意；使得胡雪岩越发感到此事做得惬意称心。
Zhang’s words showed that he approved of the match, which made Hu feel still more satisfied with what he was doing.

一高兴之下，又将条件放宽了。
In his good humour, he made the terms more generous.


『你跟魏老板去说，入赘可以，改姓不可以；
“Tell Mr Wei that A’xiang may marry into his household, but may not change his surname.

既然他女儿是宜男之相，不怕儿子不多，
With her son-bearing looks, there should be no shortage of sons.

将来他自己挑一个顶他们魏家的香烟好了。
Mr Wei can choose one to continue his family line.

至于阿祥，我叫他也做杂货生意；我借一千银洋给他做本钱。』
I shall put A’xiang into the grocery trade too, lending him a thousand silver dollars as capital.”

『既然这样，也就不必谈聘金不聘金了。
“Then there is no need to discuss a separate betrothal payment.

嫁妆、酒席，一切都是男家包办；
The groom’s side can provide everything—the trousseau, the feast and the rest.

拜了堂，两家并作一家。
Once the marriage ceremony is held, the two households will become one.

魏老板不费分文，有个女婿养他们的老，
Mr Wei need spend nothing, and he will have a son-in-law to support them in old age.

有这样便宜的好事，他也该心满意足了。
Such favourable terms should satisfy him completely.

你看我，明天一说就成功；马上挑日子办喜事。』
You will see: I shall settle it tomorrow, and we can choose a day for the wedding at once.”

『那就重重拜托。
“Then I entrust it to you.

我封好谢媒的红包，等你来拿。』
I shall have a red packet ready to thank the matchmaker.”

『谢什么媒！
“Thank me?

你帮我的忙还帮得少了不成？』
Have you not helped me often enough already?”


谈到这里，小徒弟捧来一大盘油炸臭豆腐干；
Just then the apprentice arrived with a great plate of fried stinking bean curd.

胡雪岩不暇多说，
Hu had no time for further talk.

一连吃了三块，有些狼吞虎咽的模样，便又惹得爱说话的张胖子要开口了。
He ate three pieces in succession, almost wolfing them down, which gave the talkative Zhang another opening.

『看你别的菜不吃，发芽豆跟臭豆腐干倒吃得起劲！』
“You leave the other dishes alone, but how you enjoy those sprouted beans and that bean curd!”

胡雪岩点点头，停箸答道∶『我那位老把兄嵇鹤龄，讲过一个故事给我听∶从前有个穷书生，去庙里住；跟一个老和尚做了朋友。
Hu nodded and set down his chopsticks. “My old sworn brother Ji Heling once told me a story. A poor scholar went to live in a temple and became friends with an old monk.

老和尚常常掘些芋头，煨在热灰里；穷书生吃得津津有味。
The monk would dig up taro roots and roast them in hot ashes, and the scholar ate them with delight.

到后来穷书生十年寒窗无人问，一举成名天下知，飞黄腾达，做了大官。
Later, after ten years of study in obscurity, the scholar won fame at a single stroke, rose rapidly and became a great official.

衣锦还乡，想到煨芋头的滋味，特地去拜访老和尚，要尝一尝，
Returning home in glory, he remembered the flavour of those taros and went specially to visit the monk and taste them again.

一尝之下，说不好吃。
This time he said they were no good.

老和尚答他一句∶芋头没有变，
The monk answered, ‘The taros have not changed.

你人变了！
You have.’

我今天要吃发芽豆跟臭豆腐干，也就仿佛是这样一种意思。』
My wish for beans and stinking bean curd today means something rather like that.”

『原来如此！
“So that is it!

你倒还记得，当初我们在纯号「摆一碗」，总是这两样东西下酒。』张胖子接着又问∶『现在你尝过了，是不是从前的滋味？』
You remember how those were always the two things we ate with a bowl of wine at Chunhao.” Zhang asked, “Now you have tasted them, do they taste as they did before?”

『是的。』
“Yes.”

『那倒难得！』
“That is remarkable!”

张胖子有点笑他言不由衷的意味，
His tone suggested he doubted Hu’s sincerity.

『鱼翅海参没有拿你那张嘴吃刁？』
“Shark’s fin and sea cucumber have not spoiled your palate?”

『你弄错了，
“You misunderstand.

我不是说它们好吃！
I did not say they tasted good.

从前不好吃，现在还是不好吃。』
They did not then, and they do not now.”

『这话我就不懂了！
“I do not understand that!

不好吃何必去吃它？』张胖子说。
Why eat what you do not like?

『从前也不晓得吃过多少回，从来没有听你说过，发芽豆、臭豆腐干不好吃。』
We ate them countless times in the old days, and I never heard you say they were no good.”

『不好吃，不必说；
“There is no need to say it.

想法子去弄好吃的来吃。
Find a way to get better things to eat.

空口说白话，一点用都没有；反而害得人家都不肯吃苦了！』
Empty talk achieves nothing; it only makes others unwilling to endure hardship.”

这几句话说得张胖子楞住了，怔怔地看了他好半天，
These words left Zhang staring for a long time before he could speak.

方始开口∶『老胡，我们相交不是三年、五年；到今天我才晓得你的本性。
“Old Hu, we have been friends far longer than three or five years, but only today have I understood your nature.

这就难怪了！
No wonder!

你由学生意爬到今天大老板的地位；我从钱庄大伙计弄到开小杂货店，
You have climbed from apprentice to great proprietor, while I have gone from senior bank clerk to owner of a tiny grocery.

都是有道理的。』
There is a reason for both.”

一向笑嘻嘻的张胖子，忽然大生感触，面有抑郁之色。
The habitually cheerful Zhang was suddenly overcome by feeling, and his face grew gloomy.

胡雪岩从他的牢骚话中，了解他不得意的心情；
Hu understood the disappointment beneath his complaint.

多年的患难贫贱之交，心里自然也很难过。
They had shared poverty and adversity for so many years that he too felt distressed.


他真想安慰他。
He wanted sincerely to comfort his friend.

因而想到跟刘不才与古应春所商量的计划，
He thought of the plan he had discussed with Liu Bucai and Gu Yingchun.

不久联络好了杭州的小张和嘉兴的孙祥太，预备大举贩卖洋广杂货，
Once they had made contact with Little Zhang of Hangzhou and Sun Xiangtai of Jiaxing, they intended to trade on a large scale in imported and southern goods.

不正好让张胖子也凑一股？
Why not give Fatty Zhang a share?

股本当然是自己替他垫；
Naturally Hu would advance the capital.

只要他下手帮忙；无论如何比株守一爿小杂货店来得有出息。
Zhang need only lend a hand; it would certainly offer more promise than clinging to a little grocery.

话已经要说出口了，想想不妥；
He was about to speak when he thought better of it.

张胖子嘴不紧，而这个贩卖洋广杂货的计划，是有作用的，
Zhang could not keep his mouth shut, and the trading scheme served a particular purpose.

不宜让他与闻。
He ought not to know of it.

要帮他的忙，不如另打主意。
Better to find another way of helping him.

想了一下，倒是有个主意，
After a moment, an idea came.

『老张，』他说，『我也晓得你现在委屈。
“Old Zhang, I know that your present position is beneath you.

不过时世不对，
But the times are wrong.

暂时要守一守。
You must hold on for a while.

我的钱庄，你晓得的，杭州的老根一断，就没有源头活水了！
You know my bank: with its old roots in Hangzhou cut off, its living source of funds is gone.

现在也是苦撑在那里的局面。
We too are struggling to keep it going.

希望是一定有的；要摆功夫下去。
There is certainly hope, but work must be put into it.

你肯不肯来帮帮我的忙？』
Would you come and help me?”

『你我的交情，谈不到肯不肯。
“Between us, willingness does not enter into it.

不过，老胡，实在对不起，饭庄饭我吃得寒心了；
But forgive me, Old Hu: I have had my heart chilled by banking.

你想想，我从前那个东家，
Think of my former employer.

我那样子替他卖力，弄到临了，翻脸不认人。
I worked so hard for him, and in the end he turned against me.

如果不是你帮我一个大忙，吃官司都有份。
Without your great help, I might have faced a lawsuit.

从那时候起，我就罚过咒，再不吃钱庄饭！
From that day I swore never to earn my living in a bank again.

自己小本经营，不管怎么样，也是个老板。』
However small my own business, at least I am its proprietor.”

说到这里，张胖子自觉失言；赶紧又作补充∶『至于对你，情形当然不同。
Realising he had spoken awkwardly, he hurried to qualify it. “With you, naturally, it would be different.

不过我罚过咒，不帮人家做饭庄；这个咒是跪在关帝菩萨面前罚的，
But I swore not to work in another man’s bank, and I made that oath kneeling before Lord Guan.

不好当耍。
It is not a thing to treat lightly.

老胡，千言万语并一句∶对不对你！』
A thousand words come to one: I am sorry, Old Hu.”

说完，举杯表示道歉。
He raised his cup in apology.


『这杯酒，我不能吃。
“I cannot drink to that.

我有两句话请问你，
Let me ask you two questions.

你罚咒，是不帮人家做钱庄？』
Your oath was that you would not work in someone else’s bank?”

『是的。』
“Yes.”

『就是说，不给人家做伙计？』
“That is, you would not be another man’s clerk?”

『是的！』张胖子重重地回答。
“Exactly!” Zhang answered emphatically.

『那末，老张，你先要弄清楚，我不是请你做阜康的伙计。』
“Then understand this first, Old Zhang: I am not asking you to become a clerk at Fukang.”

『做啥？』张胖子愕然相问。
“What, then?” Zhang asked in surprise.

『做股东。
“A shareholder.

等于你自己做老板！
In effect, your own proprietor.

这样子，随便你罚多重的咒，都不会应了。』
However solemn your oath, that cannot violate it.”

『做股东！』
“A shareholder!”

张胖子心动了，
He was tempted.

『不过，我没有本钱。』
“But I have no capital.”

『本钱我借你。
“I shall lend it to you.

我划一万银子，算你的股份；
I shall assign ten thousand taels as your share.

你来管事，另外开一份薪水。』
You will manage the business and draw a separate salary.

胡雪岩说，『你那家小杂货店，我也替你想好了出路；盘给阿祥，他自然并到他丈人那里。
I have thought of a solution for your grocery too: transfer it to A’xiang, and he will naturally combine it with his father-in-law’s.

你看，这不是顺理成章 的事？』
Does that not all follow neatly?”
