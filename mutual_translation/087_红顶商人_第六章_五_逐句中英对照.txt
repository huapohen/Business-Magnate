胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第六章（本章第5部分，共5部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


这样的条件，这样的交情，照常理说，张胖子应该一诺无辞；
With such terms and such friendship behind them, Fatty Zhang ought, by all ordinary reasoning, to have accepted without hesitation.

但他仍在踌躇，
Yet he still wavered.

因为第一，钱庄这一行，他受过打击，确实有些寒心；
For one thing, the blow he had suffered in banking had truly chilled his heart.

第二，交朋友将心换心，惟其胡雪岩如此厚爱，自己就更得忖量一下，倘或接手以后，没有把握打开局面，整顿内部，
For another, friendship required feeling to be repaid with feeling: precisely because Hu Xueyan treated him so generously, he must consider whether he could open up new business and put the bank in order.

让好朋友失望，倒不如此刻辞谢，还可以保全交情。
If he could not be sure of doing so, it would be better to decline now and preserve their friendship than to disappoint his friend later.

当然，他说不出辞绝的话，而且也舍不得辞绝；
Of course, he could neither bring himself to refuse nor bear to let the opportunity go.

考虑了又考虑，说了句∶『让我先看一看再说。』
After thinking it over and over, he said, “Let me look into it first.”

『看？
“Look into it?

你用不着看了！』胡雪岩说∶『阜康的情形比起从前王雪公在世的时候那样热闹，自然显得差了。
There is no need!” Hu replied. “Fukang is naturally less lively than it was when Governor Wang was alive.

跟上海的同行比一比，老实说一句，比上不足，比下着实有余。
Compared with the other Shanghai banks, I can honestly say that we fall short of the best but stand well above the worst.

阜康决没有亏空，
There is absolutely no deficit.

放款出去的户头，都是靠得住的；几个大存户亦都殷实得很，
Our borrowers are reliable, and the few large depositors are all substantial men.

不至于一下子都来提款。
They will not all descend on us to withdraw their money at once.

毛病是我不能拿全副精神摆在上头；
The trouble is that I cannot give it my whole attention.

原来请的那个大伙，人既老实，身子又不好，所以弄得死气沉沉，没有起色。
The chief clerk I engaged is a straightforward fellow, but his health is poor; the place has become lifeless and never gets ahead.

你去了，当然会不同；
With you there, things would certainly be different.

等我来出两个主意，请你一手去做，
Let me provide an idea or two and you carry them through.

同心协力拿阜康这块招牌再刷得它金光闪亮。』
Working together, we shall polish Fukang’s signboard until it shines like gold again.”

照这样说，大可一干；
Put that way, the venture was worth undertaking.

不过，『我到底是啥身分到阜康呢？』他说，
Still, Zhang asked, “In exactly what capacity am I to join Fukang?

『钱庄的规矩，你是晓得的。』
You know the rules of banking.”

钱庄的规矩，大权都在大伙手里，股东不得过问；
By those rules, authority rested entirely with the chief clerk; shareholders were not to interfere.

胡雪岩原就有打算的，
Hu had already thought this through.

毫不迟疑地答道∶『对我来说，你是股东；
“In relation to me, you are a shareholder,” he answered without hesitation.

对阜康来说，你是大伙。
“In relation to Fukang, you are the chief clerk.

你不是替人家做伙计，是替自己做。』
You will be working for yourself, not serving another man.”

这个解释很圆满，张胖子表示满意，
The explanation was complete, and Zhang was satisfied.

毅然决然地答道∶『那就一言为定。
“Then it is settled,” he said firmly.

主意你来出，事情我来做；
“You supply the ideas; I carry them out.

对外是你出面，在内归我负责。』
You represent us outside; I take charge within.”

『好极！
“Excellent!

我正就是这个意思——。』
That is exactly what I mean—”


『慢来。』
“Wait!”

张胖子突然想到，迫不及待地问∶『原来的那位老兄呢？』
A sudden thought struck Zhang, and he asked urgently, “What about the man already there?”

『这你不必担心。
“You need not worry about him.

他身体不好，而且儿子已经出道；
His health is poor, and his son has made his way in the world.

在美国人的洋行里做「康白度」，老早就劝他回家享福。
He is a comprador with an American trading firm and has long urged his father to retire and enjoy life.

他因为我待他不错，
The old fellow is grateful for my treatment of him.

虽然辞过几次，我不放他，也就不好意思走。
He has offered his resignation several times, but when I refused to let him go he felt ashamed to insist.

现在有你去接手，在他真正求之不得。』
He could wish for nothing better than to have you take over.”

张胖子释然了，
Zhang was relieved.

『我就怕敲了人家饭碗！』
“I was afraid of breaking another man’s rice bowl.”

他又生感慨，
Once more he grew reflective.

『我的东家不好；
“My own employer treated me badly.

不能让他也在背后骂东家不好。』
We must not make him say behind our backs that his employer treated him badly too.”

『你想想我是不是那种人？』胡雪岩问道，
“Do you think I am that sort of man?” Hu asked.

『老张，君子一言，驷马难追；
“Old Zhang, once a gentleman gives his word, four horses cannot overtake it.

从此刻起，我们就算合伙了！
From this moment, we are partners.

倒谈谈生意经；
Now let us talk business.

你看，我们应该怎么个做法？』
How do you think we should proceed?”

这一下，将张胖子问住了。
That stumped Zhang.

他是钱庄学徒出身，按部就班做到大伙，
He had begun as an apprentice and risen step by step to chief clerk.

讲内部管理，要看实际情形而定；谈到外面的发展，也要先了解了解市面。
Before advising on internal management he would have to examine the actual position, and before discussing expansion he would have to study the market.

如要他凭空想个主意出来，可就抓瞎了。
Asked to produce an idea out of thin air, he was helpless.

想了好一会，他说∶『现在的银价上落很大；
At last he said, “The price of silver fluctuates sharply nowadays.

如果消息灵通，兑进兑出一转手之间，利息不小。』
With prompt information, we could make a handsome profit simply buying and selling.”

『这当然。
“Certainly.

归你自己去办，用不着商量。』
You can deal with that yourself; there is no need to consult me.

胡雪岩说∶『我们要商量的是，长线放远鹞，看到三年以后，大局一定，怎么样能够飞黄腾达，一下子窜了起来。』
What we must discuss is how to let out a long line and fly our kite far ahead: three years from now, once affairs have settled down, how can we make a sudden leap into prosperity?”

『这——』张胖子笑道，『我就没有这份本事了。』
“That,” Zhang said with a laugh, “is beyond my abilities.”


谈生意经，胡雪岩一向最起劲；
Business talk always roused Hu’s enthusiasm.

又正当微醺之时，兴致更佳，
Now, pleasantly warmed by wine, he was more eager than ever.

『今天难得有空，
“We seldom have time to ourselves.

我们索性好好儿筹划一番。』
Let us make some proper plans today.

他问∶『老张，山西票号的规矩，你总熟悉的吧？』
Old Zhang, you must know the practices of the Shanxi remittance houses?”

『隔行如隔山；
“Different trades are mountains apart.

钱庄、票号看来是同行，做法不同。』
Banks and remittance houses may look like the same business, but their methods differ.”

张胖子在胡雪岩面前不敢不说老实话，
Before Hu, Zhang dared give nothing but an honest answer.

『而且，票号的势力不过长江以南；
“Besides, their influence does not extend south of the Yangtze.

他们的内幕，实在没有机会见识。』
I have had no opportunity to see their inner workings.”

『我们做钱庄，唯一的劲敌就是山西票号。
“For us bankers, the Shanxi houses are the only formidable rivals.

知己知彼，百战百胜；
Know yourself and your enemy, and you can win a hundred battles.

所以这方面，我平时很肯留心。
That is why I always pay close attention to them.

现在，不妨先说点给你听。』
Let me tell you something about them first.”

照胡雪岩的了解，山西票号原以经营汇兑为主；而以京师为中心。
As Hu understood it, the Shanxi houses had originally dealt chiefly in remittances, with the capital as their centre.

这几年干戈扰攘，道路艰难，公款解京，诸多不便；
In recent years, war and difficult roads had made it inconvenient to transport government funds to Beijing.

因而票号无形中代理了一部分部库与省库的职司，
Almost without a formal arrangement, the houses had taken over some of the duties of the central and provincial treasuries.

公款并不计息，汇水尤为可观，自然大获其利。
They paid no interest on government balances, while their remittance charges were substantial: naturally, the profits were great.

还有各省的巨商显宦，认为天下最安稳的地方，莫如京师；
Moreover, wealthy merchants and high officials in the provinces considered the capital the safest place under heaven.

所以多将现款，汇到京里，实际上就是存款。
They therefore sent much of their ready cash there, which in practice meant depositing it with the houses.

这些存款的目的不是生利，而是保本，所以利息极轻。
Such deposits were intended to preserve capital, not earn income, so the interest paid was very low.


『有了存款要找出路。
“Once you have deposits, you must find an outlet for them.

头寸烂在那里，大元宝不会生小元宝的。』胡雪岩说，
Leave funds to rot, and large ingots will never give birth to little ones,” Hu said.

『山西票号近年来通行放款给做京官的，
“In recent years, the Shanxi houses have commonly lent to officials in the capital.

名为「放京债」；
They call it making ‘capital loans’.

听说一万两的借据，实付七千——』『什么？』张胖子大声打断，
I hear that against a note for ten thousand taels they actually advance seven thousand—” “What?” Zhang interrupted loudly.

『这是什么债，
“What sort of loan is that?

比印子钱还要凶！』
Worse than the usurers who collect their instalments every day!”

『你说比印子钱还要凶，借的人倒是心甘情愿；
“You think it worse, but the borrowers agree willingly enough.

反正羊毛出在羊身上，
The wool comes off the sheep’s back in the end.

老百姓倒霉！』
It is the common people who suffer.”

『怎么呢？』
“How so?”

『你想，
“Think about it.

做官借债，拿什么来还？
When an official borrows, what can he repay with?

自然是老百姓替他还。
Naturally, the people repay for him.

譬如某人放了你们浙江藩司，
Suppose someone is appointed provincial treasurer of your Zhejiang.

京里打点，上任盘费；
He must make payments in the capital and pay for his journey.

到任以后置公馆、买轿马、用底下人，哪一样不用钱？
Once he arrives, he needs a residence, chairs and horses, servants—what does not cost money?

于是乎先借一笔京债；
So he first takes a capital loan.

到了任想法子先挪一笔款子还掉，随后慢慢儿弥补；
After assuming office, he finds some public money to divert and pays it off, then gradually makes up the sum.

不在老百姓头上动脑筋，岂不是就要闹亏空了？』
Unless he devises ways to extract it from the people, he will be left with a deficit in his accounts.”

『这样子做法难道没有风险！
“But is that not risky?

譬如说，到了任不认帐？』
Suppose he refuses to acknowledge the debt once he takes office?”

『不会的。
“He will not.

第一、有保人；保人一定也是京官。
First, there is a guarantor, who must also be an official in the capital.

第二、有借据；
Second, there is a signed note.

如果赖债，到都察院递呈子，
If he repudiates the debt, a petition can be lodged with the Censorate.

御史一参，赖债的人要丢官。
One impeachment from a censor, and the debtor loses his post.

第三、自有人帮票号的忙，不准人赖债。
Third, others will help the houses and prevent defaults.

为啥呢，
Why?

一班穷翰林平时都靠借债度日；
The poor Hanlin scholars live on loans.

就盼望放出去当考官，当学政，收了门生的「贽敬」来还债；
They hope to be sent out as examiners or education commissioners, collect ceremonial gifts from their pupils and repay their debts.

还了再借，日子依旧可以过得下去。
Having repaid, they can borrow again and keep going.

倘若有人赖了债，票号联合起来，说做官的没有信用，从此不借；
If anyone defaults, the houses may act together, declare officials untrustworthy and stop lending.

穷翰林当然大起恐慌，
The poor scholars would panic.

会帮票号讨债。』
They will help recover the money.”

胡雪岩略停一下又说∶『要论风险，只有一样；新官上任，中途出了事，
Hu paused, then went on. “There is only one real risk: something may happen to a newly appointed official on his journey.

或者死掉，或者丢官。
He may die or lose his appointment.

不过也要看情形而定，
Even then, it depends.

保人硬气的，照样会一肩担承。』
A sound guarantor will shoulder the whole obligation.”

『怪不得！』张胖子说∶『这几年祁、太、平三帮票号，在各省大设分号。
“No wonder!” Zhang exclaimed. “These past few years the Qi, Tai and Ping groups of remittance houses have been opening branches all over the provinces.

原来有这样的好处！』
So this is the advantage!”

他跃跃欲试地，『我们何不学人家一学？』
Eager to try it himself, he asked, “Why should we not learn from them?”

『着啊！』
“Exactly!”

胡雪岩干了一杯酒，
Hu drained his cup.

『我正就是这个意思。』
“That is just what I have in mind.”


胡雪岩的意思是，仿照票号的办法，办两项放款。
Hu intended to follow their example with two kinds of loan.

第一是放给做官的。
The first would go to officials.

由于南北道路艰难，时世不同，这几年官员调补升迁，多不按常规；
With travel between north and south so difficult and circumstances so altered, transfers and promotions in recent years had often departed from normal procedure.

所谓『送部引见』的制度，虽未废除，却多变通办理；
The requirement that officials be sent to the appropriate ministry and presented for an imperial audience had not been abolished, but exceptions were commonly made.

尤其是军功上保升的文武官员，尽有当到藩司、皋司，主持一省钱谷、司法的大员，而未曾进过京的。
In particular, among civil and military officials recommended for promotion on the strength of wartime service, there were even provincial treasurers and judicial commissioners—men responsible for the finances or justice of an entire province—who had never been to Beijing.

由京里补缺放出来，自然可以借京债；
A man appointed from the capital could, of course, take a capital loan.

如果在江南升调，譬如江苏知县，调升湖北的知府，没有一笔盘缠与安家银子就『行不得也』！
But an official promoted within the lower Yangtze region—a Jiangsu magistrate made prefect in Hubei, for instance—could not move without money for the journey and for establishing his household.

胡雪岩打算仿照京债的办法，帮帮这些人的忙。
Hu proposed to help such men with loans on the same model.

『这当然是有风险的。
“There is risk, of course.

但要通扯扯算，以有余补不足。
But we must reckon the whole account, using the surplus here to cover the shortfall there.

自从开办厘金以来，不晓得多少人发了财；
Since the likin taxes began, goodness knows how many men have grown rich.

象这种得了税差的，早一天到差，多一天好处，
Someone given a tax post gains an extra day’s profit for every day earlier he arrives.

再高的利息，他也要借；而且不会吃倒帐。
However high the interest, he will borrow, and we shall not suffer a bad debt.

我们的做法是要在这些户头上多赚他些，来弥补倒帐。
Our method is to earn more from these accounts to make up for losses on others.

话不妨先说明白，我们是「劫富济贫」的做法。』
We can say it plainly from the start: we shall ‘take from the rich to help the poor’.”

『劫富济贫！』张胖子念一两遍，点点头说∶『这个道理我懂了。
“Take from the rich to help the poor!” Zhang repeated the phrase once or twice and nodded. “I understand the principle.

第二项呢？『
What is the second kind?”


『第二项放款是放给逃难到上海来的内地乡绅人家。
“The second is for families of the inland gentry who have fled to Shanghai.

这些人家在原籍，多是靠收租过日子的，
At home, most lived on rents.

一早拎只鸟笼泡茶店；下午到澡塘子睡一觉；晚上「摆一碗」，吃得醉醺醺回家。
In the morning they carried a birdcage to the teahouse; in the afternoon they slept at the bathhouse; in the evening they sat down to a drinking party and went home tipsy.

一年三百六十天，起码三百天是这样子。
Of the three hundred and sixty days of the year, at least three hundred passed like that.

这种人，恭维他，说他是做大少爷；
To flatter such men, you call them young gentlemen of leisure.

讲得难听点，就是无业游民。
Less politely, they are idle vagabonds.

如果不是祖宗积德，留下大把家私，一定做「伸手大将军」了。
Had their ancestors not earned merit and left them great estates, they would certainly have become generals of the outstretched hand.

当初逃难来的时候，总有些现款细软在手里，一时还不会「落难」；
When they first fled here, they had some cash and valuables, so they did not immediately fall on hard times.

日久天长，坐吃山空，又是在这个花天酒地的夷场上，
But spend without earning, and even a mountain is exhausted—especially in this foreign settlement, with its feasting and pleasure.

所以这几年下来，很有些赫赫有名的大少爷，快要讨饭了！』
After these few years, a good many once-famous young gentlemen are close to begging.”

这话不是过甚其词，
This was no exaggeration.

张胖子就遭遇到几个；境况最凄惨的，甚至倚妻女卖笑为生。
Zhang had encountered several himself; the most wretched even depended on their wives and daughters to earn a living entertaining men.

因此，胡雪岩的话，在他深具同感；只是放款给这些人，他不以为然，
He therefore sympathised deeply with Hu’s account, but disapproved of lending to such people.

『救急容易教穷难！』
“It is easy to relieve an emergency and hard to rescue poverty.

他说，『非吃倒帐不可！』
We should be certain to lose the money.”

『不会的。』胡雪岩说，
“No,” Hu said.

『这就要放开眼光来看；
“We must look further ahead.

长毛的气数快尽了！
The Longhairs’ allotted days are almost over.

江浙两省一光复，逃难的回家乡，
Once Jiangsu and Zhejiang are recovered, the refugees will go home.

大片田地长毛抢不走；
The rebels cannot carry off their great tracts of farmland.

他们苦一两年，仍旧是大少爷。
After a year or two of hardship, they will be gentlemen of leisure again.

怎么会吃倒帐？』
Why should the loans be lost?”

『啊！』
“Ah!”

张胖子深深吸了口气，
Zhang drew a deep breath.

『这一层我倒还没有想到。
“That had not occurred to me.

照你的说法，我倒有个做法。』
On that basis, I have a method to suggest.”

『你说！』
“Let us hear it.”

『叫他们拿地契来抵押。
“Require their title deeds as security.

没有地契的，写借据，言明如果欠款不还，甘愿以某处某处田地作价抵还。』
If they have no deeds with them, have them sign a note stating that, if they fail to repay, specified land shall be valued and surrendered in settlement.”

『对！
“Quite right!

这样做法，就更加牢靠了。』
That makes it safer still.”


『还有！』
“One thing more!”

张胖子跟胡雪岩一席长谈，启发良多，也变得聪明了；
Their long conversation had taught Zhang a great deal and sharpened his wits.

他说∶『既然是救穷，就要看远一点。
“Since we are relieving poverty, we must look ahead.

那班大少爷出身的，有一万用一万，不顾死活的；
These men brought up as young masters spend ten thousand if they have ten thousand, regardless of the consequences.

所以第一次来抵押，不可以押足，
We should not advance the full amount on a property at the first application.

预备他不得过门的时候来加押。』
Leave something in reserve for an additional advance when they cannot make ends meet.”

这就完全谈得对路了，
Now they were thinking along exactly the same lines.

越谈越多，也越谈越深；
Their discussion grew wider and deeper.

然而仅谈放款，
Yet they spoke only of lending.

又哪里来的款子可放？
Where were the funds to come from?

张胖子心里一直有着这样一个疑问，却不肯问出来；因为在他意料中，心思细密的胡雪岩，一定会自己先提到，无须动问。
The question had been in Zhang’s mind all along, but he had held it back, expecting so meticulous a man as Hu to raise it without prompting.

而胡雪岩却始终不提这一层，这就逼得他不能不问了∶『老胡，这两项放款，期限都是长的；
Hu never did, however, and Zhang was obliged to ask. “Old Hu, both these kinds of loan are long-term.

尤其是放给有田地的人家，要等光复了，才有收回的确期，
Those secured on land, especially, cannot have a definite prospect of repayment until the country is recovered.

只怕不是三两年的事。
That may take more than two or three years.

这笔头寸不在少数，
It will require a great deal of capital.

你打算过没有？』
Have you considered that?”

『当然打算过。
“Of course.

只有放款，没有存款的生意，怎么做法？
How can we do business with loans going out and no deposits coming in?

我倒有个吸收存款的办法；只怕你不赞成。』
I have a way of attracting deposits, but I fear you may not approve.”

『何见以得我不赞成？
“Why should you think so?

做生意嘛，
We are in business.

有存款进来，难道还推出去不要？』
If deposits come our way, surely we shall not turn them down?”

胡雪岩不即回答，
Hu did not answer at once.

“What conditions?”

三年或者五年，到期来提，

“Of course we repay the full amount.

Would you dare accept that risk?”

Only now did Zhang see.

『我不敢！』他大摇其头，

“If the authorities came looking for the money, we would have to disclose it.

『我晓得你不敢！』胡雪岩说∶『我敢！
“I knew you would not dare,” Hu said. “I would.

Why?

我料定将来不会追。』

“Oh?

大劫来时即令逃得了命，也逃不了财产。
When it came, they might escape alive but lose their property.

换句话说，保命容易保产难；
In other words, saving their lives was easier than saving their wealth.

所以要早作安排。
Hence the need to arrange matters early.


想通了，不由得连连称『妙！』
Once he saw the scheme, Zhang could not help exclaiming, “Excellent! Excellent!”

但张胖子不是点头，而是摇头，
Yet he shook his head instead of nodding.

『老胡，』他带着些杞人忧天的味道∶『你这种脑筋动出来，要遭天忌的！』
“Old Hu,” he said, with something of the man who feared the sky might fall, “such ingenuity may provoke Heaven’s jealousy!”

“There is nothing so extraordinary about it.

“But is it against the law?”

俗语说的是∶好事多磨！
As the saying goes, good things meet many obstacles.

深怕其中有办不通的地方；

I cannot feel quite at ease.”

犯法的事，我们不能做；

隐匿罪犯的财产，固然犯法；
Concealing a criminal’s property is certainly illegal.

If he deposits under an assumed name, who knows his identity?”

『『其实我们晓得的，
“In fact, we do know.

良心上总说不过去！』
Our consciences cannot accept that.”


『老张，老张！』
“Old Zhang, Old Zhang!”

胡雪岩喝口酒，又感叹，又欢喜地说∶『我没有看错人，
Hu took a sip, at once moved and pleased. “I was not mistaken in you.

你本性厚道，
You are good-hearted by nature.

实在不错。
That is excellent.

Officials must have a conscience towards the court.

这实在也跟做生意跟主顾讲良心是一样的道理，
It is really the same principle as a merchant’s duty to his customers.

「学成文武艺，卖与帝王家」，
‘Master the arts of letters and arms, then sell them to the imperial house.’

朝廷是文武官儿的主顾，是他们的衣食父母，
The court is the customer of civil and military officials; it feeds and clothes them.

They cannot disregard their consciences towards it.

在我们就可以不讲了。』
We need not consider conscience in that way.”

『不讲良心讲啥？』
“What should we consider, then?”

『讲法，
“The law.

对朝廷守法，就是对朝廷讲良心。』
To obey the court’s laws is to satisfy our conscience towards the court.”

张胖子点点头，喝着酒沉思；
Zhang nodded and drank in thought.

好一会才欣然开口∶『老胡，我算是想通了。
After a long while, he spoke with pleasure. “Old Hu, I think I understand at last.

多少年来我就弄不懂，士农工商，为啥没好奸士、奸农、奸工、只有奸商？

可见得做生意的人的良心，别有讲究；
Evidently, a merchant’s conscience needs a different explanation.

不过要怎么个讲究，我想不明白。
I could never work out what it was.

现在明白了！
Now I understand.

对朝廷守法、对主顾讲公平，就是讲良心；就不是奸商！』
Obey the court’s laws and treat customers fairly: that is conscience, and that keeps one from being a dishonest merchant.”

『一点不错！
“Exactly!

老实说一句∶做生意的守朝廷的法，做官的对朝廷有良心，一定天下太平。

如果做官的对朝廷没有良心，要我们来对朝廷讲良心，未免迂腐。』
When officials have no conscience towards the court, to demand that merchants supply it is mere pedantry.”

『嗯，嗯；
“Yes, yes.

你这句话，再让我来想一想。』
Let me think that through.”

张胖子一面想，一面说∶『譬如，有长毛头子抓住了，抄家；
Zhang spoke as he reflected. “Suppose a Longhair leader is captured and his household’s property confiscated.

做官的抹煞良心，侵吞这个人的财产，
An official stifles his conscience and pockets the property.

那就是不讲良心。
That is acting without conscience.

But if we act conscientiously?

长毛化名来存款，说是应该充分的款子，我们不能收。

What is the result?

白白便宜赃官；仍旧让他侵吞了。

对！』
Exactly!”

他一拍桌子，大声说道∶『光是做生意的对朝廷讲良心，没有用处。

我们只要守法就够了！』
Obeying the law is enough!”

『老张啊！』
“Old Zhang!”

胡雪岩也欣然引杯，
Hu too raised his cup with pleasure.

『这样才算是真正想通。』
“Now you have truly understood.”


这一顿酒吃得非常痛快；最后是张胖子抢着做的东。
They thoroughly enjoyed their drinking, and in the end Zhang insisted on paying.

Even the most pressing business would have to be put aside.

张胖子诺诺连声；
Zhang agreed repeatedly.

She had some judgement of her own.

听了丈夫的话，又高兴，又伤感；
His account made her both happy and wistful.

做丈夫的莫名其妙，
Her husband was puzzled.

这支镯子与所谈的事有何相干？
What could it have to do with their discussion?

“This bracelet is Xueyan’s.

『咦！』
“Really!”

张胖子大感兴趣，
Zhang was fascinated.

『还有这么一段故事，倒没听你说过。
“There was such a story, and you never told me!

钱，后来还你没有？』
Did he repay you?”


『你不要打岔，
“Do not interrupt.

听我说！』张太太说∶『当时雪岩对我说∶』现在我境况不好。

这五两银子不知道啥时候能还；不过我一定会还。

「说老实话，我肯借给他，自然也不打算他一时会还，

所以我说∶『不要紧！

Repay me when you have it.’

‘It cannot count as security.

『』这不象雪岩的为人，
“That does not sound like Xueyan.

『』你以为镯子摆在我这里，就是他没有还我那五两银子？

三四年的经营，就这片刻间决定割舍；夫妇俩都无留恋之意，

因为对『老本行』毕竟有根深蒂固的感情在，而且又是跟胡雪岩在一起。

Beside that prospect, the little grocery was no chicken rib, too poor to relish but too good to throw away. It was a worn-out shoe, to be discarded without a thought.
