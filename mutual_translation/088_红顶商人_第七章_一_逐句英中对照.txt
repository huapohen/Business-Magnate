胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第七章（本章第1部分，共4部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


CHAPTER SEVEN
第七章


On rising early, Fatty Zhang followed the habit of many years and took his birdcage to the teahouse.
一早起身，张胖子还保持着多年的习惯，提着鸟笼上茶店；

Sometimes, passing Mr Wei’s shop, he would stop to greet a fellow tradesman.
有时候经过魏老板那里，因为同行的缘故，也打个招呼。

Wei was industrious and frugal and never went to teahouses, but that morning Zhang was determined to take him out for tea: the proposed marriage could hardly be discussed in A’qiao’s presence.
魏老板克勤克俭，从来不上茶店；但张胖子这天非邀他去吃茶不可，因为做媒的事，当着阿巧不便谈。

Entering the shop, he stated his immediate purpose plainly.
踏进店堂，开门见山道明来意，

Wei was taken by surprise and inclined to decline.
魏老板颇有突然之感；因而便有辞谢之意。

Just then A’qiao brought her father breakfast, a bowl of soy milk and a ball of glutinous rice.
就在这时候，阿巧替她父亲来送早点，一碗豆腐浆，一团粢米饭，

Seeing their visitor, she greeted him sweetly. “Good morning, Uncle Zhang!
看到张老板甜甜地招呼∶『张伯伯早！

Have you had breakfast?”
点心吃过没有？』


Zhang did not answer at once.
张胖子不即回答，

He looked her over from head to foot, very much as though appraising a prospective bride, until she grew embarrassed.
将她从头看到脚，真有点相亲的味道；看得阿巧有些发窘。

Since he had not yet answered her greeting, however, she could hardly turn and leave.
但客人还未答话，不便掉身而去；

She merely averted her face from his fixed gaze.
只有将头扭了开去，避开张胖子那双盯住了看的眼睛。

“A’qiao,” he asked, “how old are you this year?”
『阿巧！』张胖子问道，『你今年几岁？』

“Seventeen.”
『十七。』

“Your birthday is naturally the seventh day of the seventh month.
『生日当然是七月初七。

At what hour were you born?”
时辰呢？』

That startled her.
这下惊了阿巧！

What could a man mean by calling first thing in the morning and asking for her birth details except that he had come to arrange a match?
一早上门，来问时辰八字，不是替自己做媒是做啥？

Her thoughts flew at once to A’xiang, and she panicked.
这样转着念头，立刻想到阿祥；也立刻就着慌了！

“Who asked you to come matchmaking and ruin my life?” she thought.
『那个要你来做啥断命的媒？』她在心中自语；

Hurrying to the back, she found her mother. “What has Fatty Zhang come for so early?”
急急地奔到后面，寻着她母亲问道∶『张胖子一早跑来为啥？』

“Which Fatty Zhang?”
『哪个张胖子？』


“Who else?
『还有哪个？

The one in the same trade as Father—his rival!”
不就是同行冤家的张胖子！』

“Has he come?
『他来了？

I did not know.”
我不晓得啊！』

“Mother!”
『娘！』

A’qiao clutched her clothes. “He is up to something.
阿巧扯着她的衣服说∶『张胖子不晓得啥心思，

First he asks my birthday, then my birth hour.
又问生日，又问时辰。

I—” She stamped her foot. “I am not getting married!
我——，』她顿一顿足说∶『我是不嫁的！

No one need come meddling.”
用不着啥人来罗嗦。』

Her mother, however, became interested at once.
这一说，做母亲的倒是精神一振；

What sort of young man had Zhang found for her daughter?
不晓得张胖子替女儿做的媒，是个何等样人？

“Do not make a fuss yet.
当时便说∶『你先不要乱！

Let me ask.”
等我来问问看。』


Her mother’s interest disappointed and alarmed A’qiao.
发觉母亲是颇感兴趣的神气，阿巧非常失望，也很着急。

Surely she knew that her daughter’s heart was already given.
她心里在想，此身已有所属，母亲是知道的，

Her ordinary manner toward A’xiang had long hinted that she regarded him as half a son.
平时对阿祥的言语态度，隐隐然视之为『半子』；

She therefore knew whom A’qiao wanted and approved of him herself.
那就不但知道自己属意于什么人，而且这个人也是她所中意的。

Why, then, should she still want to “ask”?
既然如此，何必又去『问问看』？

Was she growing foolish and unreasonable in her old age?
岂不是不明事理的老糊涂了？

The trouble was that A’qiao could say none of this aloud, nor could she think of any hint that would remind her.
苦的是心里这番话说不出口；也无法用任何暗示提醒她。

In desperation she could only behave like a spoiled child, clinging to her mother’s clothes.
情急之下，只有撒娇；拉住她母亲的衣服不放。

“Do not ask!
『不要去问！

No ivory comes from a dog’s mouth.
狗嘴里吐不出象牙，

There is nothing worth asking him.”
没有啥好问的。』

“What harm can it do?
『问问也不要紧。

Why are you behaving like this?”
你这样子做啥？』

Mother and daughter tugged at each other, delaying and reaching no conclusion.
母女俩拉拉扯扯，僵持着，也因循着；

Meanwhile, Wei had found it impossible to refuse Zhang’s invitation.
而魏老板却因为情面难却，接受了张胖子的邀请，

From the front he raised his voice. “A’qiao’s mother!
在外面提高了声音喊∶『阿巧娘！

Come and mind the shop.
你出来看店；

I am going out for tea with Mr Zhang.”
我跟张老板吃茶去了。』

Now A’qiao was more anxious than ever.
这一下阿巧更为着急。

She had meant her mother to call her father in and warn him not to listen if Zhang proposed a match.
原意是想母亲拿父亲叫进来，关照一句∶如果张胖子来做媒，不要理他。

Instead, they had wasted precious time without making the important point clear.
不想要紧话未曾说清楚，白白耽误了功夫。

Naturally, if the men went out for tea, they would discuss the marriage.
如今一起去吃茶，当然是说媒；

Marriage was said to be decided by both parents, but seven-tenths of the authority lay with her father.
婚事虽说父母之命，而父亲可以做七分主，

If he carelessly swallowed Zhang’s honeyed words at the teahouse, she would have cause for resentment all her life.
如果在茶店里糊里糊涂听信了张胖子的花言巧语，那就是一辈子不甘心的恨事。

Her thoughts raced like lightning.
念头风驰电掣般快，转到此处，

“Father, come inside!” she blurted out.
阿巧脱口喊道∶『爹，你请进来，

“Mother has something important to say.”
娘有要紧话说。』

Wei returned, and his wife asked, “Has Fatty Zhang come to arrange a match for A’qiao?”
魏老板听这一说，便回了进来；他妻子问他∶『张胖子是不是来替阿巧做媒？』


Before he could answer, A’qiao broke in. “Who asked him to arrange any match?
魏老板还未答话，阿巧接口∶『哪个要他来做啥媒？

I am not getting married.”
我是不嫁的。』

“Eh?”
『咦！』

Wei looked from his wife to his daughter in genuine bewilderment.
魏老板看看妻子，又看看女儿，真有些莫名其妙了，

“How did you come to think of that?”
『你们怎么想到这上头去了？』

Quick-eared and quick-witted, A’qiao brightened at once. “Then what has he come for?”
阿巧耳朵灵，心思快，立刻喜孜孜地问道∶『那末，他来做啥呢？』

“He says he wants to discuss a piece of business.”
『他说要跟我谈一笔生意。』

“Business?” his wife asked. “Can he not discuss it in the shop?”
『谈生意？』他妻子问道∶『店里不好谈？』

“That is what I said.
『我也是这么说。

But he says he must have his morning tea or he has no energy.
他说他一早起来一定要吃茶，不然没有精神。

There is no harm in my joining him for once.”
我就陪他去吃一回也不要紧。』

“Very well!”
『好，好！』

A’qiao gave her father a little push.
阿巧推一推她父亲，

“Off you go, then.
『你老人家请！

But you may discuss business only—nothing else.”
不过，只好谈主意，不好谈别的。』


Two hours passed, and he did not return.
这一去去了两个钟头还不回来；

Uneasy, A’qiao sent the little apprentice to make a discreet inquiry at the teahouse Zhang visited every day.
阿巧心里有嘀咕，叫小徒弟到张胖子每天必到的那家茶店里去悄悄探望。

The boy soon came back: neither man was there.
须臾回转，张胖子跟魏老板都不在那里。

This looked suspicious.
这就显得可疑了。

By noon there was still no sign of Wei.
等到日中，依然不见魏老板的影子，

Mother and daughter waited a long time, then decided to eat without him.
母女俩等了好半天等不回来，只有先吃午饭。

They had just picked up their chopsticks when he appeared, his face flushed and wreathed in smiles.
刚扶起筷子，魏老板回来了，满脸红光，也满脸的笑容。

A’qiao was relieved but reproachful. “Where have you been?
阿巧又是欣慰又是怨∶『到哪里去了？』

You did not even come home for lunch.”
她埋怨着∶『吃饭也不回来！』

“Fatty Zhang invited me for a drink.
『张胖子请我吃酒；

A thoroughly enjoyable drink it was too.”
这顿酒吃得开心。』

“What was so enjoyable?
『啥开心？

Did you settle the business?
生意谈成功了？』

What business was it?”
阿巧问∶『是啥生意？』

“We discussed more than business.
『不但谈生意，

There was something else—something important.”
还谈了别样。是件大事！』

Wei sat down, smiling. “You guessed correctly.
魏老板坐下来笑道∶『你们猜得不错，

Zhang did come to arrange our daughter’s marriage.”
张胖子是来替我们女儿做媒的。』

A’qiao’s hands and feet turned cold.
听到这里，阿巧手足发冷；

She threw herself against her mother’s shoulder and shook from head to foot.
一下扑到母亲肩上，浑身抖个不住。


Both parents were startled and alarmed.
魏老板夫妇俩无不既惊且毫！

They asked what was wrong, but she seemed unwilling to explain.
问她是怎么回事？却又似不肯明说；

With an effort she sat down again and stared at her father.
只勉强坐了下来，怔怔地望着她父亲。

No one knew a daughter better than her mother, who at last guessed what was troubling her.
到底知女莫若母，毕竟猜中了她的心事；

“You must not agree carelessly to a match Fatty Zhang proposes,” she told her husband urgently.
急急向丈夫说∶『张胖子做媒，你不要乱答应人家。』

“Why should I not agree?”
『为啥不答应？』

“You have agreed already?
『你答应人家了！

What sort of family is it?
是怎么样的人家；

What is the bridegroom like?”
新郎倌什么样子？』

“Why should I tell you what he is like?
『新郎倌什么样子，何用我说？

You see him every day.”
你们天天看见的。』


The first person A’qiao thought of was Runsheng, the young assistant at the fruit shop next door.
提到每天看到的人，第一个想起的是间壁水果店的小伙计润生，

He worked hard and was quite presentable.
做事巴结，生得也还体面；

He also had a remarkable skill: if a customer bought a pear and wanted it peeled, Runsheng would set two thin-backed, flat-tipped fruit knives whirling in his hands.
他有一手『绝技』，客人上门买只生梨要扦皮，润生手舞两把平头薄背的水果刀，旋转如飞，

In the blink of an eye the pear was bare, its skin removed in one long strip.
眼睛一霎的功夫，扦得干干净净，梨皮成一长条。

A’qiao loved to watch him do it, and he loved to see her gazing at him with a smile.
陈巧最爱看他这手功夫；他也最爱看阿巧含笑凝视的神情。

Once he had become so absorbed in looking at her that his hand slipped and he sliced off a small piece of his finger.
有一次看得出神失了手，自己削掉一小节指头；

The whole street had made a joke of it.
一条街上传为笑谈。

To avoid further gossip, she had kept out of his way ever since.
以此话柄为嫌，阿巧从此总是避着他；

But the shops were neighbors, so they could not help seeing each other daily, and his employer thought highly of him.
但彼此紧邻，无法不天天见面，润生颇得东家的器重，

It was quite possible that he had asked for her.
当然是可能来求婚的。

The second person she thought of was the son of the incense-and-candle shop owner opposite.
第二个想起的是对面香蜡店的小开，

He was handsome, and their families were well matched, but he could never remove his cap: his scalp was diseased and patchy.
生得倒是一表人才，而且门当户对，可惜终年揭不得帽子；因为是个癞痢。

The thought repelled her, and she hurriedly cast him from her mind.
阿巧想起来就腻味，赶紧抛开再想。


Then she thought of A’xiang.
这一想就想到阿祥了；

Her face flushed and her heart beat wildly.
顿时面红心跳。

She could not bring herself to ask, but fortunately her mother could.
要问问不出口，好在有她母亲，

“Who is it?” she asked her husband.
『是哪个？』她问她丈夫。

“Who else?
『还有哪个，

A’xiang, naturally.”
自然是阿祥！』

The name had scarcely left his lips when A’qiao sprang up and fled into the back, her feet light as air.
『祥』字刚刚出口，阿巧便霍地起身，躲了进去；脚步轻盈无比。

Wei stared for a moment, then burst into laughter.
魏老板楞了一会，哈哈大笑。

“What are you laughing at?
『笑啥？

Tell us!
快说！

How did A’xiang come to ask Fatty Zhang to act as matchmaker?
阿祥怎么会托张胖子来做媒？

What did he say, and what did you answer?
他怎么说？你怎么答复他？

Begin at the beginning.”
从头讲给我们听。』

As the story unfolded, everyone—including A’qiao, listening from the other side of the wall—was entirely satisfied, so happy that tears seemed near.
这一讲，连『听壁脚』的阿巧在内，无不心满意足；喜极欲涕，

All shared the same thought: A’xiang had found a benefactor in an employer like Circuit Intendant Hu.
心里都有句话∶『阿祥命中有贵人；遇见胡道台这样的东家！』

Hu himself, however, could not attend to A’xiang’s affairs just then.
然而胡道台此时却还管不到阿祥的事；

Another A’qiao was giving him a headache.
正为另一个阿巧在伤脑筋。


Sister A’qiao had been out all night and had not returned until nine that morning.
阿巧姐昨夜通宵不归；一直到这天早晨九点钟才回家。

Asked where she had been, she said she had felt depressed and spent the night talking at the home of an old friend.
问起她的行踪，她说心中气闷，昨天在一个小姊妹家谈了一夜。

These “young companions” of hers were all over thirty now.
她的『小姊妹』也都三十开外了，

They had either left the profession to marry or become proprietresses—in other words, madams.
不是从良；便是做了本家——老鸨。

If she had visited a woman who had married into a respectable household, the family would hardly have kept an unaccompanied visitor overnight; someone would have escorted her home the evening before.
如是从了良的『人家人』，不会容留她只身一个人过夜；一定在头天夜里就派人送了她回来。

Her movements therefore looked suspicious.
这样看来，行踪就很有疑问了。

Without betraying his thoughts, Hu sent A’xiang to investigate.
于是胡雪岩不动声色地派阿祥去打听。

She had not used the household sedan chair, but he did not imagine she had walked.
阿巧姐昨天出门虽不坐家里轿子，但料想她也不会步行；

A’xiang accordingly questioned the bearers waiting for hire at the mouth of the lane.
所以阿祥承命去向弄堂口待雇的轿夫去探问。

He found what he wanted.
果然问到了；

She had gone to Zhaorong Lane, north of Baoshan Street.
阿巧姐昨天是去了宝善街北的兆荣里，

The bearer remembered setting her down at a stone-framed doorway, the second house from the far end.
那轿夫还记得她是在倒数第二家，一座石库门前下的轿。

As the saying went, “Zhaorong and Zhaofu, with Gongxing close beside—all are roads to the pleasure quarters.”
所谓『有里兆荣并兆富，近接公兴，都是平康路』，

Those lanes were famous haunts of luxurious dissipation.
那一带的兆荣里、兆富里、公兴里是有名的纸醉金迷之地；

Why had A’qiao dismissed her attendants and visited them secretly?
阿巧姐摒绝从人，私访平康，其意何居？

It was deeply suspicious.
着实可疑。


Only the Seventh Lady could solve the mystery.
要破这个疑团，除却七姑奶奶更无别人。

Hu counted the days: this was the fourth day since she had undertaken to furnish his new home, the day agreed upon for him to inspect it.
胡雪岩算了一下，这天正是她代为布置新居，约定去看的第四天；

He therefore took his chair directly to Huajin Lane instead of Gu’s house.
因而坐轿不到古家，直往画锦里而去。

Sure enough, the rooms had been repainted and looked entirely new.
果然，屋子已粉刷得焕然一新；

The Seventh Lady herself was directing the servants as they arranged brand-new rosewood furniture.
七姑奶奶正亲自指挥下人，在安放簇新的红木家具。

It was late in the third month; brilliant sunshine filled the courtyard, and the weather was quite warm.
三月底的天气，艳阳满院，相当燠热，

Her face was flushed as if she had been drinking, her forehead beaded with sweat, and her hair coming loose.
七姑奶奶一张脸如中了酒似的，而且额上见汗，头发起毛，

Plainly she had worked hard.
足见劳累。

Hu felt deeply indebted and made a profound bow of thanks.
胡雪岩大不过意，兜头一揖，深深致谢；

Her answer was graceful. “There is no need to thank me, Little Uncle.
七姑奶奶答得漂亮∶『小爷叔用不着谢我，

Your honored mother and my aunt—your wife—are coming.
老太太，婶娘要来了；

We of the younger generation ought to show our filial respect.”
我们做小辈的，该当尽点孝心。』

She led him through the house, room by room.
说着，她便带领胡雪岩一间屋子、一间屋子去看；

Not only were the family apartments arranged in perfect order; even the servants’ quarters had been supplied with everything necessary.
不但上房布置得井井有条，连下房也不疏忽，应有尽有。

Such care left the owner nothing to do but praise it without reservation.
费心如此，作主人的除了没口夸赞以外，再不能置一词。

After making the circuit, they returned to the drawing room to rest over tea.
一个圈子兜下来，回到客厅喝茶休息，

Only then did Hu explain A’qiao’s movements the previous night and ask for advice.
这时候胡雪岩方始开口，细诉阿巧姐一夜的芳踪；向七姑奶奶讨主意。


The matter was so unexpected that the Seventh Lady had no ready answer.
事出突兀，她一时哪里有主意？

She considered his account from beginning to end and decided that a few points must first be clarified.
将胡雪岩所说的话，前前后后细想了一遍，觉得有几件事先要弄清楚。

“Little Uncle, how did she behave toward you when she came back?
『小爷叔，』她问∶『阿巧姐回来以后，对你是啥样子？

Did she complain?”
有没有发牢骚？』

“No.
『没有，

She was very cold.”
样子很冷淡。』

“Did she pack any clothes or valuables, as though she meant to move out?”
『有没有啥收拾细软衣服，仿佛要搬出去的样子？』

“No.
『也没有。』胡雪岩答说，

She just sat there picking at her nails and thinking, as if I were not in the room.”
『坐在那里剥指甲想心事，好象根本没有看到我在那里似的。』

Those two questions were enough.
就问这两句话便够了。

The Seventh Lady nodded slowly, almost talking to herself. “That fits.
七姑奶奶慢慢点着头，自言自语似地说∶『这就对了！

That must be her plan.”
她一定是那么个主意！』

The exchange had revived Hu’s own recollections, and he too had begun to understand, though he would rather have been wrong.
由于刚才一问一答印证了回忆，胡雪岩亦已有所意会；然而他宁愿自己猜得不对，

“Seventh Sister,” he asked painfully, “can she have arranged with her old companions to appear in public again—to open a courtesan establishment of her own?”
『七姐，』他很痛苦地问∶『莫非她跟她小姊妹商量好了，还要抛头露面，自己去「铺房间」？』

A curse escaped him. “The shameless woman!”
『贱货！』脱口骂了一句。


“Little Uncle, there I must take her side.”
『小爷叔！这，我要替阿巧姐不服。』

The Seventh Lady’s nature asserted itself; righteous indignation showed in her face. “A person must find some settled place in life.
七姑奶奶的本性露出来了，义形于色地说∶『一个人总要寻个归宿。

She is willing to accept a subordinate position, but she feels that, having come from outside respectable society and been accustomed to freedom, she could not bear the restrictions of a great household.
她宁愿做低服小，只为觉得自己出身不是良家，一向自由惯了的，受不得大宅门的拘束，

She wants to live separately.
要在外头住；

That is not an excessive request.
说起来也不算过分。

If it cannot be granted, she must find another way.
这一层既然办不到，只有另觅出路；
