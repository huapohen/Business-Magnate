胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第七章（本章第2部分，共4部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


怡情老二想了一下答说∶『有的。
The Second Miss thought for a moment. “Yes.

她从前没有到我这里来之前，在心想红老六那里帮忙；
Before she came to my establishment, she worked for the Sixth Miss of Xinxianghong House.

跟同房间的阿金很谈得来。
She was very friendly with A’jin, who worked there too.

阿金我也认识的，
I know A’jin as well.

现在就住在兆富里，养着个小白脸。』
She lives in Zhaofu Lane now and keeps a handsome young lover.”

『这个阿金，现在做啥？』
“What does she do nowadays?”

『现在也是铺房间。』
“She runs an establishment herself.”

『我猜得恐怕不错。』
“Then I may have guessed correctly.”

七姑奶奶将阿巧姐瞒着人私访兆富里的经过，细细说了一遍，推断她是跟阿金在商量，也要走这条路。
The Seventh Lady described A’qiao’s secret visit to Zhaofu Lane and explained her inference that she had gone to discuss following the same path.

『奇怪！
“How strange!

她为什么不来跟我商量？』
Why did she not consult me?”

『二阿姐，你问得对。
“That is a fair question, Second Sister.

不过，我倒要请问你，如果阿巧姐要走这条路，你赞成不赞成？』
But let me ask you: if A’qiao wanted to do such a thing, would you approve?”

『我怎么会赞成？
“How could I?

这碗饭能不吃最好不吃！』
Anyone who can avoid earning a living that way should do so.”

『那就对了。
“There you have it.

她晓得你不会热心，
She knew you would not be enthusiastic.

何必来跟你商量？』
Why should she consult you?”

『这话倒也是。』
“That is true.”

怡情老二仍然困惑∶『我就不懂。她为啥还要回头来「触祭」这碗断命饭？』
The Second Miss was still puzzled. “I simply cannot understand why she should return to that wretched livelihood.”


七姑奶奶认为要商量的正就是这一点。
That, the Seventh Lady felt, was precisely what they must discuss.

猜测阿巧姐预备重堕风尘的动机，不外三种∶第一是为生计所逼；第二是报复胡雪岩；第三是借此为阅人之地，要好好觅个可靠的人，为一世的归宿。
If A’qiao intended to return to the pleasure quarters, there could be only three motives: the need to support herself, revenge upon Hu, or a wish to use the place to meet men and find a dependable partner for life.

『我在想，』七姑奶奶分析过后，谈她自己的意见∶『第一，她不必愁日子不好过，
Having analyzed the possibilities, she gave her own view. “First, she need not worry about money.

她自己跟我说过，手里有两三万银子的私房；
She told me herself that she has twenty or thirty thousand taels put away.

而况分手的时节，胡老爷总还要送她一笔钱。
Besides, Mr Hu will certainly give her another sum when they part.

至于说到报复，到底没有深仇切恨，
As for revenge, there is no deep hatred between them.

要出人家的丑，自己先糟蹋名声出了丑；
To disgrace him, she would first have to disgrace herself.

她不是那种糊涂人。
She is not so foolish.

想来想去，只有这样子一个理由∶想挑个好客人嫁！』
The more I think of it, the more I can see only one explanation: she wants to choose a suitable patron to marry.”

『为了要嫁人，先去落水？
“Go back into that life in order to find a husband?

这种事从来没有听说过。』
I have never heard of such a thing.”

怡情老二大为摇头，
The Second Miss shook her head vigorously.

『除非象阿金那样，挑个小白脸养在小房子里；
“Unless she means to follow A’jin’s example and keep some handsome young man in a little house.

要挑好客人是挑不到的。』
She will never find a good patron to marry that way.”

这话可以分两方面来听，一方面听怡情老二始终是不信阿巧姐会出此下策的语气；另一方面亦可以听出她不以阿巧姐此举为然。
Her words could be understood in two senses: she still did not believe that A’qiao would adopt such a poor plan, and she certainly disapproved of it.

而无论从哪方面来听，都能使七姑奶奶感到欣慰的。
Either way, the Seventh Lady found them reassuring.


『二阿姐，我亦不相信七阿巧姐会走上这条路。
“I too can hardly believe that A’qiao would take that road.

不过，打开天窗说亮话，
But let us speak plainly, Second Sister.

我一面是帮我小爷叔的忙；一面也是为阿巧姐的好。
I am trying both to help Little Uncle and to do what is best for her.

二阿姐，这件事上头，你要看我五哥的分上，帮一帮我的忙！』
For Brother Five’s sake, you must help me with this.”

怡情老二有些不好意思地笑了，
The Second Miss smiled in embarrassment.

『七姑奶奶，说到这话，你该罚！
“You deserve a penalty for saying that!

你的吩咐，我还有个不听？』她质问着，
Would I ever refuse something you asked?

『为啥要搬到五少来？』
Why must you bring Fifth Master into it?”

『是我的话说得不对，
“I spoke wrongly.

你不要动气。
Do not be angry.

我们商量正经；
Let us discuss the real business.

我原有个主意——。』七姑奶奶是打算着一条移花接木之计，
I already had a plan—” The Seventh Lady intended to transplant the blossom onto another branch.

特地托号子里的秦先生，写信给宁波的张郎中，想撮合他与阿巧姐成就一头姻缘。
She had specially asked Mr Qin at the firm to write to Dr Zhang in Ningbo, hoping to arrange a marriage between him and A’qiao.

这话说来又很长；
There was a long story behind it.

怡情老二从头听起，得知张郎中如何与阿巧姐结识，以及后来落花有意，流水无情，怅然而返的经过，
The Second Miss listened from the beginning, learning how they had met and how the doctor, whose feelings had found no response, had returned home disappointed.

对此人倒深为同情。
She felt considerable sympathy for him.


『七姑奶奶，你这个主意，我赞成。
“I approve of your plan.

不过，是不是能够成功，倒难说得很。
But whether it can succeed is another matter.

男女之间，完全靠缘分；看样子，阿巧姐好象跟他无缘。』
Between a man and a woman, everything depends on affinity, and A’qiao does not seem to have any with him.”

『不是！
“That is not it.

当初是因为我小爷叔横在中间，
Little Uncle stood between them.

这面一片心都在他身上，张郎中再好也不会中意；
Her whole heart was fixed on him, so however good the doctor might be, she would never consider him.

那面，看阿巧姐是有主儿的，知难而退。
The doctor, for his part, saw that she already belonged to someone and withdrew before the obstacle.

其实，照我看，阿巧姐既然不愿意做人家的偏房，嫁张郎中就再好不过。
As I see it, if she is unwilling to become a concubine, she could do no better than marry him.

第一，张郎中的太太最近去世了，
First, his wife has recently died.

以他对阿巧姐那一片痴情来说，讨她回去做填房，也是肯的；
Given his devotion to A’qiao, he would be willing to take her as his new principal wife.

第二，张郎中年纪也不大。』
Second, he is not very old.

七姑奶奶问道∶『阿巧姐今年多少？』
How old is A’qiao now?”

『她属羊的。
“She was born in the year of the Sheep.

今年——，』怡情老二扳指头算了一下，失声惊呼∶『今年整四十了！』
This year—” The Second Miss counted on her fingers, then exclaimed in surprise. “Exactly forty!”

『她生得后生，四十倒看不出。
“She looks young; one would never guess forty.

不过总是四十了！』
But forty she is.”

七姑奶奶停了一下，歉然地说∶『二阿姐，我说一句你不要生气，四十岁的人，又是这样子的出身；只怕要做人家的正室，不大容易！』
The Seventh Lady paused, then added apologetically, “Do not be offended, Second Sister, but a woman of forty, with her background, cannot easily become a man’s principal wife.”

『岂止不大容易？
“Not easily?

打着灯笼去找都难。』
One could hardly find such a match with a lantern.

怡情老二很郑重地问道∶『七姑奶奶，张郎中那里，你有几分把握？』
Tell me seriously: how certain are you of the doctor?”

『总有个六七分。』
“Six or seven chances in ten, at least.”

『六七分是蛮有把握的了。
“That is fairly certain.

我今天就去看阿巧姐，问她到底是啥意思？
I shall see A’qiao today and ask what she means to do.

如果没有这样的打算，自然最好；
If she has no intention of returning to the trade, so much the better.

倘使有的，我一定要拦住她。
If she has, I must stop her.

总而言之，不管她怎么样打算，我一定要做个媒。』
Whatever she intends, I shall act as matchmaker.”

『你是女家的媒人，我是男家的。
“You will represent the bride, and I the groom.

我们一定拿它做成功也是件好事。』
We must make it succeed. It will be a good deed.”


『当然是好事。
“A good deed, certainly.

不过，好象委屈了张郎中。』
Though it seems rather unfair to the doctor.”

提到这一层。七姑奶奶想起自己嫁古应春以前，由胡雪岩居间安排，拜王有龄的老太太做义女的往事，
At that, the Seventh Lady remembered how, before she married Gu, Hu had arranged for Wang Youling’s mother to adopt her as a daughter.

顿时又有了灵感。
Inspiration came at once.

『二阿姐，既然你这样说，我们倒商量商量看，怎么样把阿巧姐的身分抬一抬？』
“Since you feel that way, Second Sister, let us consider how to raise A’qiao’s standing.”

七姑奶奶的安排是，请胡老太太收阿巧姐为义女；
Her plan was to ask Hu’s mother to adopt A’qiao.

于是胡雪岩便是以『舅爷』的身分唱一出『嫁妹』了。
Hu would then take the part of a brother marrying off his sister, receiving the honors due to the bride’s brother.

这原是古人常有之事，
Such arrangements had often been made in former times.

在此时此地来说，特别显得情理周至，
In their present circumstances, it seemed a particularly considerate and reasonable solution.

怡情老二自然赞成，也为阿巧姐高兴，认为这样子做，她倒是『修成正果』了。
The Second Miss naturally approved and rejoiced for A’qiao: through this, she thought, her long journey would reach a proper fulfillment.


七姑奶奶也很得意于自己的这个打算；
The Seventh Lady herself was delighted with the idea.

性子本来急，也正兴头的时候，当时就要邀怡情老二一起去看阿巧姐，当面锣、对面鼓，彻底说个明白。
Impatient by nature and now carried away by enthusiasm, she wanted to take the Second Miss straight to A’qiao and settle the whole matter face to face.

倒还是怡情老二比较持重，
The Second Miss was more cautious.

认为应该先跟阿金碰个头，打听清楚了邀她一起去谈，更容易使阿巧姐受劝。
She thought they should first see A’jin, establish the facts, and invite her to join them; A’qiao would then be more easily persuaded.

『那也好！』
“Very well.

七姑奶奶问道∶『我们就去看阿金。』
Let us go and see A’jin.”

『这——，』怡情老二知道阿金因为养着小白脸，忌讳生客上门；但这话不便明说，
“Well—” The Second Miss knew that A’jin disliked unfamiliar visitors because she kept a young lover, but she could hardly say so outright.

所以掉个枪花∶『七姑奶奶，你的身分不便到她那里。
She changed tack. “A woman of your standing should not go there.

我叫人去喊她来。』于是她唤带来的小大姐，赶到兆富里去请阿金；特别叮嘱喊一乘『野鸡马车』，坐催阿金一起坐了来。
I shall send for her.” She called the young maid who had accompanied her and sent her hurrying to Zhaofu Lane, with particular instructions to hire an ordinary street carriage and bring A’jin back in it without delay.

在这等候的当儿，少不得又聊家常。
While they waited, the two women naturally talked of their own affairs.

怡情老二的话中，颇有厌倦风尘之意；但也不曾表示要挑个什么样的人从良，
The Second Miss sounded weary of life in the pleasure quarters, yet said nothing of choosing a man to marry.

七姑奶奶思路快，口也快，听出她的言外之意，忍不住要提出诤劝。
Quick in both thought and speech, the Seventh Lady heard what lay beneath her words and could not resist offering candid advice.


『二阿姐，你不要一门心思不转弯，
“Second Sister, do not fix your mind on a single course and refuse to turn aside.

那样也太痴了！
That is too foolishly devoted.

你始终守着我五哥，守到头发白也不会成功。
If you go on waiting for Brother Five, you may wait until your hair is white and still achieve nothing.

这里头的原因，五哥想必跟你说过。
He must have told you why.

他领一帮，做事要叫人心服；
He leads a whole guild and must act in ways that earn its members’ respect.

弟兄穷得没饭吃，他还要多立一个门户，你想，这话怎么说得过去？
While they are too poor to feed themselves, how could he justify setting up another household?

二阿姐，你死了这条心吧！』
Give up that hope.”

怡情老二无词以对。
The Second Miss had no answer.

默然泫然，惟有背人拭泪。
Silent and tearful, she turned away to wipe her eyes.

七姑奶奶也觉得心里酸酸地好不自在；倒有些懊悔，不该拿话说得这么直。
The Seventh Lady too felt an ache of unhappiness and regretted having spoken so plainly.

『说真的，』她没话找话，用以掩饰彼此都感到的不自然，『那位张郎中倒是好人，家道也过得去；
“Truly,” she said, searching for something to ease their mutual embarrassment, “that doctor is a good man, and comfortably off.

我就怎么没有想到，
Why did I not think of it?

早应该替你做这个媒。』
I ought to have arranged a match for you long ago.”

『多谢你，七姑奶奶！
“Thank you.

命生得不好，吃了这碗断命饭；
But I was born to ill fortune and took up this wretched livelihood.

连想做小都不能够，
I cannot even become a concubine.

还说啥？』
What more is there to say?”

话中依然是怨怼之意。使得一向擅于词令的七姑奶奶也无法往下接口了。
Her words still held resentment, and the usually eloquent Seventh Lady could find nothing to add.


幸好，兆富里离此不远；
Fortunately, Zhaofu Lane was nearby.

一辆马车很快地去而复回，载来了阿金。
The carriage soon returned with A’jin.

她在路上便已听小大姐说过，所以一见七姑奶奶，不必怡情老二引见，很客气地问道∶『是尤家七姑奶奶？
The maid had explained matters on the way, so A’jin needed no introduction before greeting the Seventh Lady courteously. “You are the Seventh Lady of the You family?

生得好体面！』
What a fine-looking woman you are!”

『不敢当！
“You are too kind.”

这位，』七姑奶奶问怡情老二，
The Seventh Lady turned to her companion.

『想来就是阿金姐了？』
“Then this must be Sister A’jin?”

『是啊！』
“Yes.”

怡情老二做主人，先替阿金要了食物饮料；然后开门见山地说∶『七姑奶奶为了关心阿巧姐，特意请你来，想问问你；
Acting as hostess, the Second Miss ordered food and drink for the newcomer, then came straight to the point. “The Seventh Lady is concerned about A’qiao and has specially asked you here.

这两天阿巧姐是不是到你那里去了？』
Has A’qiao been to see you these past few days?”

『她常到我那里来的。』
“She comes to see me often.”

『阿金姐，』七姑奶奶说，『我们是初会，二阿姐知道我的，心直口快。
“Sister A’jin,” the Seventh Lady said, “we have not met before, but Second Sister knows me: I am frank and speak my mind.

我说话有不到的地方，请你不要见气。』
If I say anything clumsy, please do not take offense.”

这是因为阿金跟怡情老二，谈到阿巧姐时，一上来便有针锋相对之势；
A’jin and the Second Miss had seemed at odds as soon as A’qiao was mentioned.

七姑奶奶深怕言事碰僵，不但于事无补，反倒伤了和气，所以特为先打招呼。
The Seventh Lady feared that a collision would accomplish nothing and only damage their friendship, so she made this precautionary apology.

阿金也是久历风尘，熟透世故的人，
A’jin too had long experience of the world and understood its ways.

自知一句『她常到我这里来的』答语，语气生硬，隐含敌意，成为失言；
She knew that her stiff answer had carried a hint of hostility and had been ill judged.

所以歉然答道∶『七姑奶奶你言重了！
“Please do not say that.

我的嘴笨；
I am clumsy with words.

二阿姐又是好姊妹，说话不用客气。
Second Sister and I are old friends, so I do not stand on ceremony with her.

你可千万不能多我的心！』
You must not misunderstand me.”

既然彼此都谦抑为怀，就无须再多作解释，反倒象真的生了意见。
With both sides showing humility, further explanations would only suggest that there really was a quarrel.

不过，有些话，七姑奶奶因为彼此初交，到底不便深问；
Still, as a new acquaintance, the Seventh Lady could hardly press certain questions herself.

要由怡情老二来说，比较合适。
The Second Miss would be better placed to ask them.

因而报以一笑之外，向旁边抛了个眼色示意。
She smiled and gave her a meaningful glance.


怡情老二点点头，接下来便用平静的语气，向阿金说明原委∶『阿巧姐跟胡老爷生了意见。
The Second Miss nodded and explained calmly to A’jin. “A’qiao and Mr Hu have fallen out.

「清官难断家务事」，谁是谁非也不必去说它；
Even a wise magistrate cannot settle family troubles, so there is no need to decide who is right or wrong.

总而言之，恐怕是要分手了。
In short, they will probably separate.

七姑奶奶跟阿巧姐的感情一向是好的；当初作成他们的姻缘，又是七姑奶奶出过力的，
The Seventh Lady has always been close to A’qiao and helped bring them together in the first place.

不管怎么说，阿巧姐的事，她不能不关心。
Naturally, she cannot be indifferent to what happens now.

刚刚特地寻了我来问我；我实在不晓得。
She came specially to ask me, but I know nothing.

阿巧姐好久没有碰过头了，
I have not seen A’qiao for some time.

听说这两天到你那里去过，想必总跟你谈了，
Hearing that she had visited you in the last few days, I thought she must have told you something.

她到底有什么打算？』
What does she intend to do?”

『喔，』
“Oh.”

阿金听完，不即回答，
A’jin did not answer immediately.

却转脸问七姑奶奶，
Instead, she turned to the Seventh Lady.

『阿巧姐跟胡老爷的感情，到底怎么样？』
“How are things really between A’qiao and Mr Hu?”

『不坏啊！』
“Not bad.”


『那就奇怪了！』
“Then it is strange.”

阿金困惑地，
A’jin looked puzzled.

『她每次来，总怨自己命苦。
“Whenever she comes, she complains of her unhappy fate.

我问她∶胡老爷待你好不好？她总是摇头不肯说。
I ask whether Mr Hu treats her well, but she only shakes her head and refuses to say.

看样子——。』下面那句话，她虽不说，亦可以猜想得到。
From the look of things—” She left the next words unspoken, though their meaning was easy to guess.

这一下，却是轮到七姑奶奶有所困惑了；
Now the Seventh Lady was puzzled in turn.

『阿巧姐为啥有这样的表示？』她问，
“Why should she give that impression?

『他们要分手，也是最近的事；
This talk of parting is very recent.

只为胡老爷的家眷要到上海来了，大太太不容老爷在外面另立门户，
Mr Hu’s family is coming to Shanghai, and his principal wife will not allow him to keep a separate household.

阿巧且又不肯进她家的门，以致于弄成僵局。
A’qiao refuses to enter the family home, so they are at an impasse.

要说以前，看不出来他们有啥不和的地方！』
Before this, I could see no sign of disagreement between them.”

阿金点点头，
A’jin nodded.

『这也不去说它了。』
“Let us leave that aside.”

她的脸色阴沉了，
Her face grew gloomy.

『也许要怪我不好。
“Perhaps I am to blame.

我有个堂房姑婆，
I have a great-aunt on my father’s side, from a collateral branch of the family.

现在是法华镇白衣庵的当家师太；
She is now abbess of White-Robed Convent in Fahua Town.

一到上海，总要来看我，
Whenever she comes to Shanghai, she visits me.

有时候跟阿巧姐遇见，两个人谈得很起劲。
Sometimes she has met A’qiao here, and they have talked with great enthusiasm.

我们那位老师太，说来说去无非「前世不修今世苦」，劝她修修来世。
The old abbess is always saying that suffering in this life comes from a failure to cultivate virtue in the last, and urging her to prepare for a better life to come.

这也不过出家人的老生常谈；
It was only the usual talk of a religious person.

哪知道阿巧姐倒有些入迷的样子。』
Who could have thought A’qiao would become so absorbed in it?”

一口气说到这里，七姑奶奶才发觉自己的猜想完全错了！
Only then did the Seventh Lady realize that her guesses had been entirely wrong.

照这段话听来，阿巧姐去看阿金，或者与那位师太有关；不是为了想铺房间。
A’qiao’s visit might have concerned the abbess, not the opening of a courtesan establishment.

因而急急问道∶『怎样子的入迷？』
“What do you mean, absorbed?” she asked urgently.

『说起来真教人想不到。
“It is quite unexpected.

她那天来问我白衣庵的地址，
The other day she asked me for the address of White-Robed Convent.

我告诉了她；又问她打听地址何用？

At first she would not say.

『我猜不会的。』怡情老二却有泰然的神情，
“I do not think she will do it,” the Second Miss said calmly.

To go on would have been unkind.

照七姑奶奶想，阿巧姐亦未必会走到这条路上去。
The Seventh Lady too thought A’qiao might not take that road.

自宽自慰之余，却又另外上了心事；
Yet even as she comforted herself with this thought, another worry arose.

她不愿重堕风尘，固然可以令人松一口气，
It was a relief that A’qiao did not intend to return to the pleasure quarters.

但这种决绝的样子，实在也是抓住胡雪岩不放的表示。
But such a drastic gesture also showed that she would not release her hold upon Hu.

看起来麻烦还有的是。
There seemed to be plenty of trouble still ahead.

『现在怎么办呢？』七姑奶奶叹口气说，
“What are we to do now?” she sighed.

『我都没有招数了。』
“I have no more ideas.”

怡情老二跟她交往有年，从未见她有这样束手无策的神情。
In all their years of friendship, the Second Miss had never seen her so helpless.

一半是为她，一半为阿巧姐，自觉义不容辞地，在此时要出一番力。
Partly for her sake and partly for A’qiao’s, she felt bound to make an effort.

“A’qiao will not shave her head and become a nun.

How good of the Seventh Lady to take such trouble!

We are A’qiao’s old companions and ought to work even harder.

这头媒做成功，实在是你阴功积德的好事。
Bringing this match about would be a true deed of merit.

There is no use sitting here talking.

不如此刻就去看她，
Let us go and see her now.

I refuse to believe that the three of us cannot persuade one woman.”


由于怡情老二与阿金很起劲，七姑奶奶的信心也恢复了，
Their enthusiasm restored the Seventh Lady’s confidence.

略想一想问道∶『阿金姐，二阿姐，你们是不是决心要帮阿巧姐的忙？』
After a little thought she asked, “Sister A’jin, Second Sister, are you determined to help A’qiao?”

『自然。』怡情老二说，『只要帮得上。』
“Naturally,” the Second Miss replied, “if we can.”

凡事事缓则圆；
Matters settle more smoothly when one takes time.

又道是只要功夫深，铁杵磨成针，

从今天起。索性叫胡老爷不必再跟阿巧姐见面；

我们先把她的心思引开来，让她忘记有姓胡的这个人。
We shall first divert her thoughts and make her forget that such a person exists.

这当然不是三天两天的事，所以我要先问一问两位；
That cannot be done in two or three days, which is why I had to ask you first.

If we truly mean to help, we must devote time to it.

Beginning today, the three of us will keep her company constantly.

If she refuses, we shall say that we want to go.

人总是重情面的，
People care about their friends’ feelings.

她决计不好意思推辞；也不好意思哭丧了脸扫大家的兴。
She will be too embarrassed to refuse and too considerate to spoil everyone’s pleasure with a gloomy face.

At night we shall take turns staying with her, both to watch over her and to talk and ease her mind.

这样有半个月二十天下来，她的心境就不同了；
After fifteen or twenty days of that, her mood will change.

到那时候再跟她提到张郎中，事情就容易成功！
Then we can mention Dr Zhang, and success will be much easier.

至于这些日子在外头玩儿的花费，我说句狂话，我还用得起，

统通归我！』
I shall pay for everything.”

We can only follow her lead.

“Of course not.”

Do not argue.

This was a Western custom called “signing,” an acknowledgment of the debt.

Ordinarily one wrote one’s name; an illiterate person could make any distinctive mark, provided the proper form was observed.

A’fu!”

楼梯上匆匆奔下来一个人，晃荡着长辫子，满脸惊惶；
