胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第七章（本章第3部分，共4部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


三个人面面相觑，
The three women exchanged glances.

都猜到了是怎么回事？
Each guessed what had happened.

七姑奶奶遇到这种情形，却很沉着，反安慰她说∶『素香，你不要急！
In such a situation the Seventh Lady remained calm and even comforted the maid. “Suxiang, do not be frightened.

有话慢慢说。』
Tell us slowly.”

『奶奶不见了！』素香用带哭的声音说，
“Madam has disappeared!” Suxiang said, almost in tears.

『不晓得到哪里去了？』叫她慢慢说，她说得还是没头没脑，
“No one knows where she has gone.” Asked to speak slowly, she still began in the middle of the story.

七姑奶奶只好问道∶『你怎么知道你奶奶不见了？
The Seventh Lady had to question her. “How do you know she has disappeared?

她什么时候出的门？』
When did she leave?”

『老爷一走，没有多少时候，她叫我到香粉弄去买丝线；又差阿祥去叫米叫柴。
“Not long after Master went out, she sent me to buy silk thread in Xiangfen Lane and sent A’xiang to order rice and firewood.

等到我跟阿祥回来，她已经不知道什么时候出门了；连门上都不知道，
When we came back, she had already gone. No one knew when she had left, not even the man at the front door.

再看后门；是半开在那里。
Then we looked at the back door and found it half open.

一直到下半天三点钟都不见回来；
By three this afternoon she still had not returned.

我进房去一看，一只小首饰箱不见了，替换衣服也少了好些。
I went into her room: a small jewelry box was missing, and quite a few changes of clothes.

这——这——！』素香着急地，不知如何表达她的想法。
That—that—” Too anxious to explain, Suxiang faltered.

这不用说，自然是到老师太那里去了。
There was no need to say it: she must have gone to the elderly abbess.

七姑奶奶倒吸一口冷气，怔怔地望着同伴；
The Seventh Lady drew in a sharp breath and stared at her companions.

怡情老二便问∶『素香，你们老爷知道不知道？』
The Second Miss asked, “Suxiang, does your master know?”

『不知道。』
“No.

素香答说∶『阿祥跟轿班去寻老爷去了。』
A’xiang and the chairmen have gone to look for him.”

『你们老爷在钱庄里。』七姑奶奶说，
“Your master is at the bank,” the Seventh Lady said.

『你看，轿班还有哪个在？
“See whether any of the chairmen are still here.

赶快去通知；
Send someone to tell him at once.

请你们老爷到这里来，我有要紧话说。』
Ask him to come here; I have something important to say.”


就在这时候，雪岩已经赶到；同来的还有萧家骥。
At that moment Hu arrived, accompanied by Xiao Jiaji.

胡雪岩跟怡情老二熟识，与阿金却是初见，
He knew the Second Miss well but had never met A’jin.

不过此时亦无暇细问，同时因为有生客在，要格外镇静，免得『家丑』外扬，
There was no time for inquiries; indeed, the presence of a stranger made him all the more determined to remain composed and keep his domestic troubles from becoming public.

所以只点点头，平静地问∶『你们两位怎么也来了？』
He merely nodded and asked quietly, “How do you two come to be here as well?”

『我们是碰上的。』七姑奶奶答说，
“We happened to meet,” the Seventh Lady replied.

『有话到里面去说。』
“Let us talk inside.”

进入客厅，她方为胡雪岩引见阿金。
In the sitting room she introduced A’jin.

话要说到紧要地方了，却不宜让素香与阿祥听到；所以她要求跟胡雪岩单独谈话。
They were approaching matters that Suxiang and A’xiang ought not to hear, so she asked to speak with Hu privately.

『阿巧姐去的地方，我知道，在法华镇，一座尼姑庵里，
“I know where A’qiao has gone—to a convent in Fahua Town.

事不宜迟，现在就要去寻她。
We must lose no time; someone must go for her at once.

我看，『七姑奶奶踌躇着说，』
I think—” She hesitated.

只好我跟阿金姐两个人去；
“It had better be just Sister A’jin and me.

你不宜跟她见面。
You ought not to meet her.”

『胡雪岩大惑不解，』
Hu was utterly bewildered.

到底怎么回事？
“What has happened?

『他问∶』何以你又知道她的行踪？
How do you know where she is?

那位阿金姐，又是怎么回事？
And who is this Sister A’jin?”

『『这时候没有办法细说。
“There is no time to explain everything now.

小爷叔，你只安排我们到法华好了。』
Little Uncle, just arrange for us to get to Fahua.”


『法华一带都是安庆来的淮军。
“The whole Fahua district is occupied by Anhui Army troops from Anqing.

还不知道好走不好走呢！』
I do not even know whether we can get through.”

『不要紧！』萧家骥说，
“That does not matter,” Xiao said.

『我去一趟好了。』
“I shall go.”

『好极！
“Excellent!

你去最好。』
You are the best person.”

七姑奶奶很高兴地说；
The Seventh Lady was delighted.

因为萧家骥跟淮军首领很熟，此去必定有许多方便。
Xiao knew the Anhui Army commanders well, and his presence would make many things easier.

『七姐，我想我还是应该去。』胡雪岩说，
“Seventh Sister, I still think I ought to go,” Hu said.

『不见面不要紧，至少让她知道我不是不关心她。
“I need not see her, but at least let her know that I am not indifferent.

你看呢？』
What do you think?”

『我是怕你们见了面吵起来，弄得局面很不好收场。
“I was afraid you would quarrel if you met and make matters impossible to resolve.

既然小爷叔这么说，去了也不要紧。』
Since you put it that way, Little Uncle, there is no harm in your going.”

到得法华镇，已经黄昏。
It was dusk when they reached Fahua Town.

萧家骥去找淮军大将程家启部下的一个营官，姓朱；
Xiao sought out a battalion commander named Zhu, who served under the Anhui Army general Cheng Xueqi.

人很爽朗热心，问明来意，请他们吃了一顿饭，然后命手下一个把总将地保老胡找了来，说知究竟。
A frank and helpful man, Zhu heard their errand, gave them a meal, then ordered a subordinate officer, a bazong, to fetch the local constable, Old Hu, and explain what was needed.


『好的，好的！
“Certainly, certainly.

我来领路。』老胡说道∶『请三位跟我来。』
I shall show the way,” Old Hu said. “The three of you, please follow me.”

于是迎着月色，往东面去；走不多远，折进一条巷子，
They headed east in the moonlight and soon turned into a lane.

巷底有处人家，一带粉墙，墙内花木繁盛，
At its far end stood a residence behind a whitewashed wall, with luxuriant flowers and trees inside.

新月微光，影影绰绰；
In the faint light of the new moon, the shapes were indistinct.

薰风过处，传来一阵浓郁的『夜来香』的香味，
A warm breeze carried out the rich scent of night-blooming flowers.

每个人都觉得精神一振，而一颗心却无缘无故地飘荡不定，有着一种说不出的胀满的感觉。
Everyone felt suddenly refreshed, yet for no reason their hearts grew restless, filled with a swelling emotion they could not name.

这份感觉以萧家骥为尤甚，
Xiao felt it most strongly.

不由得便问∶『这是什么地方？』
“What is this place?” he could not help asking.

『这里？』
“Here?

地保答道∶『就是白衣庵。
White-Robed Convent.

晚上来，要走边门。』
At night we use the side entrance.”

边门是一道厚实的木板门，
The side entrance had a solid wooden door.

举手可及的上方，有个不为人所注意的扁圆形铁环；
Above it, within reach, was an inconspicuous flattened iron ring.

地保一伸手拉了两下，
Old Hu reached up and pulled it twice.

只听『克啷、克啷』的响声。
Clang, clang.

不久，听得脚步声、然后门开一线，有人问道∶『哪位？』
After a little while came footsteps; the door opened a crack, and someone asked, “Who is it?”

『小音，是我！』
“Xiaoyin, it is me.”

『噢！』
“Oh.

门内小音问道∶『老胡，这辰光来做啥？』
Old Hu, what brings you here at this hour?”

『你有没有看见客人？』
“Can you not see the visitors?”

地保指着后面的人说，
He pointed behind him.

『你跟了尘师父去说，是我带来的人。』
“Tell Sister Liaochen that I have brought them.”


门『呀』地一声开了。
The door swung open with a creak.

灯光照处，小音是个俗家打扮的垂发女郎；
By the light they saw Xiaoyin, a girl in ordinary secular dress with her hair hanging loose.

等客人都进了门，将门关上；然后一言不发地往前走，穿过一条花径，越过两条走廊，到了一处禅房，看样子是待客之处；
Once the visitors were inside, she shut the door and led them forward without a word, along a flower-lined path and through two covered passages to a monastic room apparently used for receiving guests.

她停了下去，看着地保老胡。
There she stopped and looked at Old Hu.

老胡略有些踌躇，『总爷！』他哈腰问∶『是不是我陪着你老在这里坐一坐？』
He hesitated a little, then bowed to the officer. “Sir, shall I keep you company here for a while?”

这何消说得？那把总自然照办。
There was scarcely any need to ask; the officer naturally agreed.

于是老胡跟小音悄悄说了几句；然后示意胡雪岩跟着小音走。
Old Hu whispered a few words to Xiaoyin, then motioned Hu to follow her.

穿过禅房，便是一个大院子∶绕向西边的回廊，但见人影、花影一齐映在雪白的粉墙上；
Beyond the room lay a large courtyard. As they turned into the western covered walk, their shadows mingled with those of the flowers upon the whitewashed wall.

还有一头猫的影子，弓起背，正在东面屋脊上『叫春』。
There was also the shadow of a cat, arching its back and yowling for a mate on the roof to the east.

萧家骥用手肘轻轻将胡雪岩撞了一下，同时口中在念∶『「曲径通幽处，禅房花木深」！』
Xiao nudged Hu gently with his elbow and murmured a couplet: “A winding path leads into seclusion; the monks’ quarters lie deep among flowers and trees.”

胡雪岩也看出这白衣庵大有蹊跷。
Hu too could see that something about this convent was unusual.

但萧家骥的行径，近乎佻亻达；不是礼佛之道，
But Xiao’s behavior verged on frivolity and was scarcely respectful in a Buddhist sanctuary.

便咳嗽一声，示意他检点。
He coughed to warn him to restrain himself.

于是默默地随着小音进入另一座院落，一庭树木，三楹精舍，
They followed Xiaoyin silently into another courtyard, planted with trees and facing an elegant three-bay retreat.

檀香花香，交杂飘送；
Sandalwood incense mingled with the scent of flowers.

萧家骥不由得失声赞道∶『好雅致的地方！』
Xiao exclaimed involuntarily, “What a refined place!”

『请里面坐。』小音揭开门帘肃客，
“Please sit inside,” Xiaoyin said, lifting the curtain to admit them.

『我去请了尘师父来。』
“I shall fetch Sister Liaochen.”

说完，她又管自己走了。
Then she went off on her own.


两个人进屋一看，屋中上首供着一座白瓷观音；
Entering, the two men saw a white porcelain Guanyin enshrined at the upper end of the room.

东面是一排本色的桧木几椅；西面一张极大的木榻，上铺蜀锦棉垫。
To the east stood a row of tables and chairs of unpainted cypress; to the west, an enormous wooden couch covered with padded Sichuan brocade.

瓶花吐艳、炉香袅袅，配着一张古琴，布置得精雅非凡；
Flowers glowed in vases, incense curled from a burner, and an ancient qin completed an exceptionally tasteful arrangement.

但这一切，都不及悬在木榻上方的一张横披，更使得萧家骥注目。
Yet none of these things caught Xiao’s attention as much as the horizontal scroll hanging above the couch.


『胡先生！』
“Mr Hu!”

萧家骥显得有些兴奋，
Xiao sounded excited.

『你看！』
“Look!”

横披上是三首诗；
The scroll contained three poems.

胡雪岩总算念得断句∶闲叩禅关访素娥，醮坛药院覆松萝，
Hu could at least make out where the lines began and ended: “At leisure I knock at the Buddhist gate to visit a moonlike beauty; / Pine and creepers cover the ritual altar and the garden of herbs.

一庭桂子迎人落，满壁图书献佛多；
/ A courtyard of osmanthus flowers falls to greet the visitor; / Books and paintings fill the walls, rich offerings to the Buddha.

作赋我应惭宋玉，拈花卿合伴维摩。
/ In writing rhapsodies I must blush before Song Yu; / With a flower in your hand, you should be Vimalakirti’s companion.

尘心到此都消尽，细味前缘总是魔！
/ Coming here, my worldly thoughts all melt away; / Yet savoring our former ties, I find them all temptation.”


旧传奔月数嫦娥，今叩云房锁丝萝，
“Old legend tells of Chang’e’s flight up to the moon; / Now I knock at a cloudlike cell enclosed in clinging vines.

才调玄机应不让，风怀孙绰扇区我；
/ Your gifts need yield nothing to Xuanji’s; / Your romantic ardor seems greater even than Sun Chuo’s.

谁参半分优婆塞？
/ Who has attained even half a lay disciple’s practice?

待悟三乘阿笈摩。
/ We wait to grasp the Three Vehicles and the Agamas.

何日伊蒲同设馔，清凉世界遣诗魔。
/ When shall we share a Buddhist vegetarian feast, / And in this cool, pure world dispel the demon of verse?”


群花榜上笑良多，梓里云房此日过。
“On the register of flowers are many traces of smiles; / Today I visit this cloudlike cell in our native place.

君自怜才留好然，我曾击节听高歌；
/ Cherishing talent, you have left fine lines behind; / I once beat time in praise as I heard you sing aloud.

清阴远托伽山竹，冶艳低牵茅屋萝。
/ Cool shade finds distant shelter in the holy mountain’s bamboo; / Lush beauty trails low among a thatched hut’s vines.

点缀秋光篱下菊，尽将游思付禅魔。
/ Chrysanthemums beneath the hedge adorn the autumn light; / All wandering thoughts I give to the demon of meditation.”


胡雪岩在文墨这方面，还不及萧家骥，
In literary matters Hu knew even less than Xiao.

不知道宋玉、孙绰是何许人？也不知道玄机是指的唐朝女道士鱼玄机。
He had no idea who Song Yu and Sun Chuo were, nor that Xuanji meant Yu Xuanji, the Tang dynasty Taoist nun.

佛经上的那些出典是莫名其妙。
The allusions to Buddhist scriptures were wholly beyond him.

但诗句中的语气不似对戒律森严的女僧，却是看得出来的。
Yet he could tell that the tone of the poems was hardly one in which to address a nun who kept strict vows.

因而愕然相问∶『这是啥名堂？』
Astonished, he asked, “What is all this?”

『你看着好了。』萧家骥轻声答道∶『这位了尘师父，不是嘉兴人就是昆山；
“Wait and see,” Xiao answered softly. “This Sister Liaochen must come from Jiaxing or Kunshan.

不然就是震泽、盛泽。』
If neither, then Zhenze or Shengze.”

昆山的尼姑有何异处，胡雪岩不知道；但嘉兴的尼庵是亲自领教过的。
Hu knew nothing of what distinguished the nuns of Kunshan, but he had personal experience of the convents of Jiaxing.

震泽和盛泽的风俗，他在吴江同里的时候，也听人说过，
When staying at Tongli in Wujiang, he had also heard about the customs of Zhenze and Shengze.

这两处地方，盛产丝绸，地方富庶，风俗奢靡。
Both were prosperous silk-producing towns, luxurious in their habits.

盛泽讲究在尼姑庵宴客，一桌素筵，比燕菜席还要贵；
In Shengze it was fashionable to entertain guests at convents, where a vegetarian banquet cost more than a feast featuring bird’s nest.

据说是用肥鸡与上好的火腿熬汁调味，所以鲜美绝伦。
The dishes were said to be flavored with stock made from plump chickens and the finest ham, which accounted for their extraordinary savor.

震泽尼姑庵的烹调，亦是有名的，荤素并行，不逊于无锡的船菜。
The convents of Zhenze were equally famous for their cooking: meat and vegetarian dishes alike rivaled the celebrated boat cuisine of Wuxi.

当然，佳肴以外，还有可餐的秀色。
Besides the delicacies on the table, of course, there were beauties to feast one’s eyes upon.

这样回忆着，再又从初见老胡，说夜访白庆庵『没有啥不便』想起，
Remembering all this, Hu thought back to Old Hu’s assurance when they first met that there was “no inconvenience” in visiting White-Robed Convent at night.

一直到眼前的情景，觉得无一处不是证实了萧家骥的看法，
Everything from that moment to the scene before him seemed to confirm Xiao’s view.

因而好奇大起，渴望着看一看了尘是什么样子？
His curiosity rose, and he longed to see what Liaochen looked like.

萧家骥反显得比他沉着，
Xiao, however, had grown more sober than he.

『胡先生，』他说，『只怕弄错了！
“Mr Hu, I am afraid there has been a mistake.

阿巧姐不会在这里。』
A’qiao cannot be here.”


『何以见得？』
“Why do you say that?”

『这里，哪是祝发修行的地方？』
“Is this a place for shaving one’s head and practicing religion?”

胡雪岩正待答话，一眼瞥见玻璃窗外，一盏白纱灯笼冉冉而来，
Hu was about to reply when he glimpsed a white gauze lantern floating toward them beyond the glass window.

便住口不言，同时起身等候；
He fell silent and rose to wait.

门帘启处，先见小音，次见了尘一若非预知，不会相信所见的是个出家人。
The curtain lifted; first came Xiaoyin, then Liaochen. Had they not known beforehand, they would never have believed her to be a nun.

她当然也不是纯俗家打扮，
Her appearance was not entirely secular, certainly.

不曾『三绺梳头，两截穿衣』发长齐肩，穿的是一件圆领长袍；
She wore neither an ordinary woman’s three-part coiffure nor separate upper and lower garments. Her hair reached her shoulders, and she had on a long, round-necked robe.

说它是僧袍固然可以，但僧袍不会用那种闪闪生光的玄色软缎来做，更不会窄腰小袖，裁剪得那么称体。
One might have called it a nun’s robe, but a nun’s robe would hardly be made of such glossy soft black satin, still less be cut so closely to the body, with a narrow waist and slender sleeves.

看到脸上，更不象出家人，
Her face looked less monastic still.

虽未敷粉，却曾施朱；她的皮肤本来就白，亦无须敷粉。
Though she wore no powder, she had applied rouge; her naturally fair skin needed no powder.

特别是那双眼睛，初看是剪水双瞳，再看才知别蕴春情。
Above all there were her eyes: at first glance clear as water, but at a second revealing an undertone of amorous feeling.


是这样的人物，便不宜过于持重拘谨，
With such a person, excessive solemnity and reserve seemed unnecessary.

胡雪岩笑嘻嘻地双掌合十，打个问讯∶『可是了尘师太？』
Smiling, Hu brought his palms together in greeting. “Are you Sister Liaochen?”

『我是了尘。
“I am Liaochen.

施主尊姓？』
May I ask your surname, benefactor?”

『我姑胡。
“Mine is Hu.

这位姓萧。』
This gentleman is Mr Xiao.”

于是了尘——行礼，请『施主』落座；
Liaochen greeted them in turn and invited her “benefactors” to sit.

她自己盘腿坐在水榻上相陪，动问来意。
She herself sat cross-legged on the wooden couch and asked what had brought them.

『原是来见当家老师太的；
“We originally came to see the elderly abbess,” Hu said.

听地保老胡说，宝庵其实是由了尘师太当家。
“But Old Hu, the constable, tells us that you are really in charge here.

有点小事打听，
There is a small matter we wish to ask about.

请我这位萧老弟说吧！』
Perhaps my young friend Xiao had better explain.”

萧家骥点点头，
Xiao nodded.

不谈来意却先问道∶『听了尘师太的口音是震泽？』
Instead of coming to the point, however, he first asked, “Judging by your accent, Sister Liaochen, are you from Zhenze?”

了尘脸上一红∶『是的。』
She blushed. “Yes.”


『这三首诗，』萧家骥向她上方一指，『好得很！』
“These three poems,” Xiao said, pointing above her, “are very fine.”

『也是三位施主，一时雅兴；
“They were written by three benefactors in moments of poetic inspiration.

疯言疯语的，
Such wild nonsense!

无奈他何！』
What can one do with them?”

说着，了尘微微笑了，
Liaochen smiled faintly.

『萧施主在震泽住过？』
“Have you lived in Zhenze, Mr Xiao?”

『是的。住过一年多；
“Yes, for more than a year.

那时还是小孩子，什么都不懂。』
But I was only a child then and understood nothing.”

『意思是现在都懂了？』
“Meaning that you understand everything now?”

这样率直反问，有些咄咄逼人的意味；
So direct a question had something challenging about it.

萧家骥自非弱者，不会艰于应付，从容自若地答道∶『也还不十分懂，
Xiao was no weak opponent and had no difficulty answering with composure. “Not everything, even now.

改日再来领教。
I shall return another day for instruction.
