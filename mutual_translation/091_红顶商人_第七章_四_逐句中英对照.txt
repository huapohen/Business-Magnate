胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第七章（本章第4部分，共4部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


『你是说，你的话不能告诉我师父，师娘？』
“You mean I must not tell my master or his wife what you say?”

『对了。』
“Exactly.”

『好！我起誓∶如果阿巧姐对我说的话，我告诉了我师父师娘，叫我天打雷劈。』
“Very well, I swear it: if I tell either of them what A’qiao tells me, may Heaven strike me dead with lightning.”

阿巧姐点头表示满意；
A’qiao nodded, satisfied.

然后说道∶『你师娘真叫「又做师娘又做鬼」——。』用这句苟刻的批评开头，阿巧且将七姑奶奶几次劝她的话『夹叙夹议』地从头细诉，
“Your master’s wife really does play both the spirit-medium and the ghost—” Beginning with this harsh judgment, she recounted in detail the several occasions on which the Seventh Lady had advised her, interweaving the conversations with comments of her own.

照她的看法，完全是七姑奶奶有意要拆散她跟胡雪岩的姻缘，
In her view, the Seventh Lady had deliberately set out to break her bond with Hu.

七姑奶奶劝她委屈，入门见礼正正式式做胡家的偏房，看似好意，
Her advice that A’qiao swallow her pride, enter the household, make the proper obeisances, and formally become Hu’s concubine only appeared well meant.

其实是虚情，
It was false kindness.

因为明知也决不愿这么做，就尽不妨这么说，好逼得不能不下堂求去。
Knowing perfectly well that A’qiao would never agree, she could safely urge it upon her and thereby force her to ask to leave.


对胡雪岩，七姑奶奶在她面前一再说他『滑头』，『没常性，见一个爱一个』；
As for Hu, the Seventh Lady had repeatedly called him “slippery,” a man with “no constancy, who falls in love with every woman he sees.”

听来是骂胡雪岩而其实是帮他。
It sounded like abuse of Hu, but in fact it helped him.

『萧少爷你想，
“Think, Young Master Xiao.

你这位师娘开口「小爷叔」，闭口』小爷叔『，
Your master’s wife is forever saying ‘Little Uncle’ this and ‘Little Uncle’ that.

敬得他来象菩萨。
She reveres him as though he were a bodhisattva.

就算他真的「滑头」、「没常性」，又怎好去说他？』
Even if he really were slippery and inconstant, how could she speak of him that way?”

阿巧姐说到这里很激动了，
A’qiao had grown agitated.

「我先倒也当她生来爽直，真的是为我抱不平，所以有啥说啥。
“At first I thought she was naturally outspoken and was truly indignant on my behalf, so she said whatever came into her head.

后来越想越不对，
Later, the more I thought, the less it seemed right.

前前后后，想了又想，才晓得她的意思，
I went over everything again and again until I understood.

无非说胡某人怎么样不是人，犯不着再跟他而已！
All she meant was that Hu was such a scoundrel that he was not worth staying with!”

『听她对七姑奶奶的指责，实在不无道理。
Her accusations against the Seventh Lady were not without reason.

但越觉得她有道理，越觉得心里难过；
Yet the more reasonable they seemed, the more painful Xiao found them.

因为萧家骥对他的这位师娘，有如幼弟之于长姐，既敬且爱。
He regarded his master’s wife as a young brother regards an elder sister, with both respect and affection.

多少年来存在心目中的一个伉爽、正直、热心、慷慨的完美印象，
For years he had cherished a perfect image of her as forthright, honest, warmhearted, and generous.

此时似乎发现了裂良，
Now he seemed to see a crack in it.

怎不教人痛心？
How could that not hurt?

因此，他竟没有一句话说。
He could not utter a word.

这一方面是感到对阿巧姐安慰，或为七姑奶奶辩护都不甚合适；另一方面也实在是沮丧得什么话都懒得说了。
Neither comforting A’qiao nor defending the Seventh Lady seemed quite appropriate; besides, he was too dejected to feel like saying anything at all.


一见萧家骥的脸色，胡雪岩吓一大跳；
The sight of Xiao’s face gave Hu a shock.

他倒象害了一场病似的。
He looked as though he had been ill.

何以跟阿巧姐见了一次，有这样的似乎受了极大刺激的神情？
What could one meeting with A’qiao have done to leave him so visibly shaken?

令人惊疑莫释，而又苦于不便深问；
Hu was troubled and bewildered, yet could hardly question him closely here.

只问得一句∶『见过面了？』
He merely asked, “Have you seen her?”

『见过了。
“Yes.

我们谢谢了尘师太，告辞吧！』
Let us thank Sister Liaochen and take our leave.”

了尘又变得很沉着了，
Liaochen had recovered her composure.

她也不提阿巧姐，只殷勤地请胡雪岩与萧家骥再来『随喜』。
She too said nothing of A’qiao, only warmly invited the two men to return and “share in the religious joys” of the place.

居姑庵中何以请男施主来随喜？
Why should men be invited to enjoy religious visits to a convent?

这话听来便令人有异样之感；只是无暇去分辨她的言外之意。
The words had an odd sound, but there was no time to consider what lay beneath them.


不过，胡雪岩对人情应酬上的过节，一向不会忽略，
Hu, however, never neglected the niceties of social dealings.

想到有件事该做，随即说了出来∶『请问，缘簿在哪里？』
Remembering something he ought to do, he asked at once, “Where is the book of donations?”

『不必客气了！』
“There is no need for such ceremony.”

胡雪岩已经发现，黄色封面的缘簿，就挂在墙壁上，
He had already spotted the yellow-covered book hanging on the wall.

便随手摘下，文给萧家骥说∶『请你写一写，写一百两银子。』
Taking it down, he handed it to Xiao. “Please enter a hundred taels for me.”

『太多了！』
“That is too much!”

了尘接口说道∶「如果说是为了宝眷住在我们这里，要写这么多，那也用不着！
Liaochen interposed. “If you mean to give so much because your lady is staying here, there is no need.

出家人受十方供养，也供养十方；
Those who have left the world receive support from all quarters and offer support to all quarters.

不必胡施主费心。
You need not concern yourself.”

『』那是两回事。『萧家骥越出他的范围，代为回答∶』各人尽各人的心意。
“Those are separate matters,” Xiao replied on Hu’s behalf, going beyond his own role. “Each of us does what is in his heart.”

『接着，萧家骥便用现成的笔砚，写了缘簿；
He used the brush and ink already laid out to make the entry.

胡雪岩取一张一百两的银两，夹在缘簿中一起放在桌上，随即告辞出庵。
Hu put a banknote for a hundred taels inside the book, placed it on the table, and took his leave.

回营谢过朱管带，仍旧由原来护送的人送回上海。
They returned to the camp to thank Commander Zhu, then rode back to Shanghai under the same escort.

一路奔驰，无暇交谈，
They traveled too fast for conversation.

到了闹区，萧家骥才勒住马说道∶『胡先生，到你府上去细谈。』
Only when they reached the busy streets did Xiao rein in his horse. “Mr Hu, let us go to your house and talk at length.”


于是遣走了那名马弁，一起到胡雪岩与阿巧姐双栖之处。
They dismissed the mounted orderly and went together to the house Hu had shared with A’qiao.

粉奁犹香，明镜如昨；
Her powder box still held its fragrance; the bright mirror stood as before.

但却别有一股凄凉的意味；
Yet the rooms had taken on a desolate air.

胡雪岩换了一个地方，在他书房中闭门深浅。
Hu chose another room, shutting himself in his study with Xiao for a private conversation.

听萧家骥转述了阿巧姐的愤慨之词，胡雪岩才知道他为何有那样的痛苦的神态。
When Xiao repeated A’qiao’s indignant words, Hu understood why he had looked so distressed.

当然，在胡雪岩也很难过；
He too was deeply troubled.

自他认识七姑奶奶以来，从未听见有人对她有这样严苛的批评，
In all the time he had known the Seventh Lady, he had never heard anyone criticize her so harshly.

如今为了自己，使她在阿巧姐口中落了个阴险小人的名声，
Now, because of him, A’qiao had branded her a treacherous schemer.

想想实在对不起七姑奶奶。
He felt he had done the Seventh Lady a great wrong.

『胡先生，』萧家骥将一路上不断在想的一句话，问了出来∶『我师娘是不是真的象阿巧姐所说的那样，是有意耍手段？』
“Mr Hu,” Xiao asked, voicing a question that had occupied him all the way back, “has my master’s wife really been deliberately maneuvering things as A’qiao says?”

『是的。』
“Yes.”

胡雪岩点点头，
Hu nodded.

『这是她过于热心之故。
“She is too eager to help.

阿巧姐的话，大致都对；
Most of what A’qiao says is true.

只有一点她弄错了。你师娘这样做，实实在在是为她打算。』
She has misunderstood only one point: your master’s wife is doing it entirely for her sake.”

接着胡雪岩便为七姑奶奶解释，
He went on to explain.

她是真正替阿巧姐的终身打算，
The Seventh Lady was sincerely considering A’qiao’s lifelong welfare.

既然不愿做偏房，不如分手，择人而事。
Since she would not become a concubine, she would do better to part from him and choose another man.

他虽不知道七姑奶奶有意为阿巧姐与张郎中撮合，但他相信，以七姑奶奶的热心待人，一定会替阿巧姐觅个妥当的归宿。
Hu did not know that the Seventh Lady intended to match her with Dr Zhang, but he believed that, with her warm concern for others, she would certainly find A’qiao a suitable home.


这番解释，萧家骥完全能够接受；
Xiao could accept this explanation completely.

甚至可以说，他所希望的，就是这样一番能为七姑奶奶洗刷恶名的解释。
Indeed, it was precisely what he had hoped for: an explanation that would clear the Seventh Lady of the charge against her.

因此神态顿时不同；
His manner changed at once.

轻快欣慰，仿佛卸下了肩上的重担似的。
He became cheerful and relieved, as though a heavy burden had slipped from his shoulders.

『原说呢，
“That is what I thought!

我师娘怎么会做这种事？
How could my master’s wife do such a thing?

她如果听说阿巧姐是这样深的误会，不知道要气成什么样子？』
If she heard how deeply A’qiao misunderstood her, there is no knowing how angry she would be.”

『对了！』
“Exactly!”

胡雪岩矍然惊觉∶『阿巧姐的话，绝对不能跟她说。』
Hu suddenly took alarm. “You must on no account tell her what A’qiao said.”

『不说又怎么交代？』
“But if we say nothing, how are we to explain?”

于是两个人商量如何搪塞七姑奶奶？
They discussed how to put the Seventh Lady off.

说没有找到，她会再托阿金去找；
If they said they had not found A’qiao, she would ask A’jin to look again.

说是已经祝发，决不肯再回家，她一定亦不会死心，自己找到白衣庵去碰钉子。
If they said A’qiao had shaved her head and absolutely refused to return, she would still persist and go to White-Robed Convent herself, only to be rebuffed.

想来想去没有妥当的办法。
However much they thought, they could find no satisfactory answer.


丢下这层不谈，萧家骥问道∶『胡先生，那末你对阿巧姐，究竟作何打算呢？』
Leaving that difficulty aside, Xiao asked, “Mr Hu, what do you mean to do about A’qiao?”

这话也使得胡雪岩很难回答；
That too was hard for Hu to answer.

心里转了好半天的念头，付之一叹∶『我只有挨骂了！』
He turned it over in his mind for a long time, then sighed. “I can only resign myself to being cursed.”

『这是说，决定割舍？』
“You mean you have decided to give her up?”

『不割舍又如何？』
“What else can I do?”

『那就这样，索性置之不理。』萧家骥说∶『心肠要硬就硬到底！』
“Then leave the matter entirely alone,” Xiao said. “If you are to harden your heart, harden it all the way.”

『是我自己良心上的事。』胡雪岩说，
“It is a matter of my own conscience,” Hu replied.

『置之不理，似乎也不是办法。』
“Ignoring her altogether hardly seems right.”

『怎么才是办法？』
“What would be right, then?

萧家骥说，『要阿巧姐心甘情愿地分手，是办不到的事。』
There is no way to make A’qiao agree willingly to the separation.”


『不求她心甘情愿，
“I do not ask for willing agreement.

只望她咽得下那口气。』
I only hope she can swallow the bitterness.”

胡雪岩作了决定∶『我想这样子办——。』他的办法是一方面用缓兵之计，隐住七姑奶奶，只说阿巧姐由白衣庵的当家师太介绍，已远赴他乡，目前正派人追下去劝驾了；
Hu made his decision. “This is what I propose—” He would first gain time and keep the Seventh Lady quiet by saying that the abbess of White-Robed Convent had sent A’qiao to another place far away, and that someone was already following to persuade her to return.

一方面要拜托怡情老二转托阿金∶第一、帮着瞒谎，不能在七姑奶奶面前道破真相；
At the same time, he would ask the Second Miss to enlist A’jin’s help. First, A’jin must support the story and never reveal the truth to the Seventh Lady.

第二、请她跟阿巧姐去见一面，转达一句话，不管阿巧姐要干什么，祝发也好，从良也好，乃至于步了尘的后尘也好，胡雪岩都不会干预，
Second, she must see A’qiao and deliver a message: whatever A’qiao wished to do—take the tonsure, marry respectably, or even follow in Liaochen’s footsteps—Hu would not interfere.

而且预备送她一大笔钱。
He was also ready to give her a large sum of money.

说完了他的打算，胡雪岩自己亦有如释重负之感；
Once he had explained the plan, Hu too felt relieved.

因为牵缠多日，终于有了快刀斩乱麻的处置。
After so many days of entanglement, he had at last found a way to cut through it.

而在萧家骥，虽并不以为这是一个好办法；只是除此以外，别无善策，
Xiao did not consider it a good solution, but could think of no better one.

而况毕竟事不干己，要想使劲出力也用不上，
Besides, it was not his own affair; however much he wished to help, there was little he could do.

只有点点头表示赞成。
He could only nod his agreement.

『事不宜迟，
“We must act at once,” Hu said.

你师娘还在等回音；
“Your master’s wife is still waiting for news.

该干什么干什么，
Let us do what needs doing.

今天晚上还要辛苦你。』
I must trouble you with more work tonight.”

『胡先生的事就等于我师父的事，』
“Your business is as much my concern as my master’s.”

萧家骥想了一下说，
Xiao thought for a moment.

『我们先去看怡情老二。』
“Let us see the Second Miss first.”


到了怡情老二那里，灯红酒绿，夜正未央。
At the Second Miss’s establishment, lamps glowed red, wine flowed, and the night was still young.

不过她是『本家』，另有自己的『小房子』；
But she was the proprietress and had a separate little house of her own.

好在相去不远，『相帮』领着，片刻就到。
Fortunately it was nearby; an attendant led them there in a few moments.

入门之时，正听得客厅里的自鸣钟打十二下；
As they entered, they heard the clock in the sitting room strike twelve.

怡情老二虽不曾睡，却已上楼回卧室了。
The Second Miss had not gone to sleep, though she had retired upstairs to her bedroom.

听得小大姐一报，她请客人上楼。
When her maid announced the visitors, she invited them up.

端午将近的天气，相当闷热；
With the Dragon Boat Festival approaching, the weather had grown close and hot.

她穿一件家常绸夹袄对客，袖管很大也很短，露出两弯雪白的膀子，
She received them in an informal lined silk jacket whose broad, short sleeves exposed her white forearms.

一只手膀上戴一支金镯，一只手腕上戴一支翠镯，
On one arm she wore a gold bracelet, on the other a jade bangle.

丰容盛髻、一副福相；
Her full face and abundant, carefully dressed hair gave her a look of prosperity and good fortune.

这使得萧家骥又生感触，相形之下，越觉得阿巧姐憔悴可怜。
The sight stirred Xiao again: by comparison, A’qiao seemed all the more worn and pitiable.

由于胡、萧十分是初次光临，怡情老二少不得有一番周旋，
Since this was the two men’s first visit to her house, the Second Miss had to observe the usual courtesies.

倒茶摆果碟子，还要『开灯』请客人『躺一息』。
She poured tea, set out dishes of fruit, and even offered to light the opium lamp and let them recline for a while.

主要殷勤，客人当然也要故作闲豫，
Before such hospitality, the guests too had to make a show of being at leisure.

先说些不相干的话，然后谈入正题。
They began with irrelevant conversation before turning to their purpose.


萧家骥刚说得一句『阿巧姐果然在白衣庵』，小大姐端着托盘进房；
Xiao had barely said, “A’qiao was at White-Robed Convent after all,” when the maid came in with a tray.

于是小酌宵夜，一面继谈此行经过。
Over a little wine and a late supper, he continued his account of the visit.

萧家骥话完；胡雪岩接着开口，拜托怡情老二从中斡旋。
When he finished, Hu asked the Second Miss to act as intermediary.

一直静听不语的怡情老二，不即置答；
She had listened without a word and did not answer at once.

事情太离奇了，她竟一时摸不清头绪。
The whole affair was so strange that she could scarcely make sense of it.

眨着眼想了好一会才摇摇头说∶『胡老爷，我看事情不是这么做法。
For a while she blinked in thought, then shook her head. “Mr Hu, I do not think that is the way to handle matters.

这件事少不得七姑奶奶！』接着，她谈到张郎中；认为七姑奶奶的做法是正办。
We cannot do without the Seventh Lady in this.” She told him about Dr Zhang and argued that the Seventh Lady’s course was the right one.

至于阿巧姐有所误会，无论如何是解释得清楚的。
A’qiao’s misunderstanding could surely be explained away.

为今之计，只有设法将阿巧姐劝了回来；化解误会，消除怨恨，归嫁张宅，
What they must do now was persuade her to return, clear up the misunderstanding, dispel her resentment, and arrange her marriage into the Zhang household.

这一切只要大家同心协心花功夫下去，一定可以有圆满的结局。
If everyone worked together patiently, there was every prospect of a happy conclusion.

『阿金不必让她插手了；决绝的话，更不可以说。
“There is no need to involve A’jin, and you certainly must not send a message that closes every door.

现在阿巧姐的心思想偏了，要耐心拿它慢慢扭过来。
A’qiao’s thoughts have taken a wrong turning; we must patiently guide them back.

七姑奶奶脾气虽毛糙，倒是最肯体恤人、最肯顾大局；
The Seventh Lady may be hasty, but no one is more considerate or more willing to think of everyone’s interests.

阿巧姐的误会，她肯原谅的，也肯委屈的。
She will forgive A’qiao’s misunderstanding and put up with some unfairness.

不过话可以跟她说明白；犯不着让她一白衣庵去碰钉了。
We can explain matters to her openly; there is no reason to let her go to the convent and be rebuffed.

我看，胡老爷——。』她有意不再说下去，是希望胡雪岩有所意会，自动作一个表示。
As I see it, Mr Hu—” She deliberately stopped, hoping that Hu would understand and volunteer the necessary offer.

而胡雪岩的心思很乱，不耐细想，
But his mind was in turmoil and he had no patience for subtlety.

率直问道∶『二阿姐，你要说啥？』
“What do you mean, Second Sister?”


『我说，胡老爷，你委屈一点，
“I mean that you should put your pride aside a little.

明天再亲自到白衣庵去一趟，陪个笑脸，说两句好话，拿阿巧姐先劝了回来再说。』这个要求，
Go to White-Robed Convent again yourself tomorrow, smile, say a few kind words, and first persuade A’qiao to return. We can consider the rest afterward.”

胡雪岩答应不下。
Hu could not agree.

三番两次，牵缠不清，以致于搁下好多正事不能办；他心里实在也厌倦了。
This repeated and endless entanglement had forced him to neglect a great deal of important business; he was thoroughly weary of it.

如今好不容易有了个快刀斩乱麻的措施，却又不能实行；
Now that he had finally found a way to cut himself free, he was to be prevented from using it.

反转要跟阿巧姐去陪笑脸，说好话，
Instead, he must smile at A’qiao and speak ingratiatingly.

不但有些于心不甘，也怕她以为自己回心转意，觉得少不得她，
He resented that; worse, he feared she would think he had changed his mind and could not do without her.

越发牵缠得紧，
She would cling to him still more tightly.

岂不是更招麻烦？
Would that not merely invite further trouble?

看他面难色，怡情老二颇为着急说∶『胡老爷，』她说∶『别样见识，我万万不及你们做官的老爷们；
Seeing his reluctance, the Second Miss grew anxious. “Mr Hu, in every other kind of judgment I am far below gentlemen in official life such as yourself.

只有这件事上，我有把握。
But on this matter I am confident.

为啥呢？
Why?

女人的心思，只有女人晓得；
Because only a woman understands a woman’s thoughts.
