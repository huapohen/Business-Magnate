胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第八章（本章第1部分，共9部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第八章
CHAPTER EIGHT


左宗棠从安徽进入浙江，也是稳扎稳打，先求不败；
Entering Zhejiang from Anhui, Zuo Zongtang advanced cautiously, securing each position and making safety from defeat his first concern.

所以第一步肃清衢州，作为他浙江巡抚在本省境内发号施令之地，
His initial move was to clear Quzhou and establish a base within the province from which he could exercise his authority as governor.

这是同治元年六月初的事。
This was early in the sixth month of the first year of Tongzhi.

在衢州定了脚跟，左宗棠进一步规取龙游、兰溪、寿昌、淳安等地，将新安江以南、信安江以西地区的长毛，都撵走了；
Once firmly established there, he moved against Longyou, Lanxi, Shouchang, Chun’an, and the surrounding districts, driving the rebels out of the country south of the Xin’an River and west of the Qu River, also called Xin’an.

然后在十一月下旬，攻克了新安、信安两江交会的严州。
Late in the eleventh month, he captured Yanzhou at the confluence of the two rivers.

由此虎过山高水长的严子陵钓台，沿七里泷湖江北上，
From there his forces passed Yan Ziling’s Fishing Terrace, with its lofty hills and long reaches of water, and proceeded north along the river through Qililong.

第二年二月间进围杭州南面的富阳；距省城不足百里了。
In the second month of the following year they laid siege to Fuyang, south of Hangzhou and less than a hundred li from the provincial capital.

钱塘江南面，洋将德克碑的常捷军；丢乐德克的常安军，在不欠以前，攻克绍兴，接着，太平军又退出萧山。
South of the Qiantang River, the Changjie Army under the foreign commander d’Aiguebelle and the Chang’an Army under Diuledeke had recently captured Shaoxing; the Taiping forces then withdrew from Xiaoshan.

整个浙江的东西南三面，都已肃清；
The eastern, western, and southern regions of Zhejiang had all been cleared.

然而膏腴之地的浙北，也就是杭州以北，太湖以南，包括海宁、嘉兴、湖州在内的这一片活土，仍旧在太平军手里。
But the rich northern tract, between Hangzhou and Lake Tai and including Haining, Jiaxing, and Huzhou, remained in Taiping hands.

这时，左宗棠升任闽浙总督；
At this point Zuo was promoted to governor-general of Fujian and Zhejiang.

浙江巡抚由曾国荃补授，他人在金陵城外，无法接事，仍由左宗棠兼署。
Zeng Guoquan was appointed governor of Zhejiang, but, being outside the walls of Jinling, he could not take up the post; Zuo continued to act in his place.

为了报答朝廷，左宗棠全力反攻，
To repay the court’s favour, Zuo threw all his strength into the counter-offensive.

谁都看得出来，杭州克复是迟早间事。
Everyone could see that the recovery of Hangzhou was only a matter of time.


那时攻富阳、窥杭州的主将是浙江藩司蒋益澧。
The commander attacking Fuyang with an eye to Hangzhou was Jiang Yili, Zhejiang’s provincial treasurer.

左宗棠本人仍旧驻节衢州，设厂督造战船；
Zuo himself remained at Quzhou, where he established workshops and supervised the building of war vessels.

富阳之战，颇得舟师之力。
The flotilla proved of considerable assistance in the fighting at Fuyang.

但太平军在富阳的守将，是有名骁勇的汪海洋，因而相持五月，蒋益澧仍无进展。
But the Taiping defender was Wang Haiyang, famous for his courage, and after five months Jiang had made no progress.

左宗棠迫不得已，只好借重洋将，
Zuo was obliged to call upon the foreign commander.

札调常捷军二千五百人，由德碑率领，自萧绍渡江，会攻富阳；
He ordered d’Aiguebelle to bring twenty-five hundred men of the Changjie Army across the river from Xiaoshan and Shaoxing for a combined attack.

八月初八终于克复。
Fuyang was finally recovered on the eighth day of the eighth month.

其时也正是李鸿章 、刘铭传、郭松林合力攻克江阴；李秀成与李世贤自天京经溧阳到苏州，想设法解围的时候。
At about the same time, Li Hongzhang, Liu Mingchuan, and Guo Songlin joined forces to capture Jiangyin, while Li Xiucheng and Li Shixian travelled from the Heavenly Capital through Liyang to Suzhou, hoping to find a way to relieve the siege.

浙江方面，蒋益澧与德克碑由富阳北上，进窥杭州；同时分兵攻杭州西面的余杭。
In Zhejiang, Jiang and d’Aiguebelle advanced north from Fuyang towards Hangzhou, sending a separate force against Yuhang to the west.

太平军由『朝将』汪海洋∶『归王』邓光明∶『听王』陈炳文，连番抵御，却是杀一阵败一阵。
The Taiping commanders Wang Haiyang, the Court General, Deng Guangming, the Gui King, and Chen Bingwen, the Ting King, resisted repeatedly, but lost battle after battle.

到十一月初，左宗棠亲临余杭督师，
Early in the eleventh month, Zuo went in person to Yuhang to direct operations.

但杭州却仍在太平军苦守之中。
Hangzhou, however, was still being stubbornly defended.


其时李鸿章 已下苏州、无锡。
By then Li Hongzhang had taken Suzhou and Wuxi.

按照他预定的步骤，不愿往东去占唾手可得的常州，免得『挤』了曾国荃；
In accordance with his earlier plan, he would not move east to seize the easily obtainable prize of Changzhou, lest he encroach upon Zeng Guoquan.

却往浙北去『挤』左宗棠；
Instead, he moved into northern Zhejiang to encroach upon Zuo.

一面派翰林院侍讲面奏调到营的刘秉璋，由金山卫沿海而下，
On one side he sent Liu Bingzhang, a Hanlin lecturer whom he had personally requested from the emperor for service in his camp, south along the coast from Jinshanwei.

收复了浙北的平湖、乍浦、海盐；
Liu recovered Pinghu, Zhapu, and Haiyan.

一面派程学启由吴江经平望，南攻嘉兴。
On the other, he sent Cheng Xueqi from Wujiang through Pingwang to attack Jiaxing from the north.

收复了浙北各地，当然可以接收太平军的辎重，征粮收税；
Recovery of these districts naturally brought possession of Taiping baggage and stores, as well as the right to collect grain levies and taxes.

而且仿照当年湖北巡抚胡林翼收复安徽边境的先例，
Li also invoked the precedent of Hu Linyi, governor of Hubei, who had recovered districts on the Anhui border.

以为左宗棠远在杭州以南，道理隔阻，鞭长莫及，应该权宜代行职权，派员署理浙西收复各县的州县官。
Since Zuo was far to the south of Hangzhou, with communications obstructed and these places beyond his effective reach, Li considered it proper to exercise authority temporarily on his behalf and appoint acting magistrates to the recovered districts of western Zhejiang.

这一下气得左宗棠暴跳如雷。
Zuo was furious.

李鸿章 不但占地盘，而且江苏巡抚这个官做到浙江来了，
Li had not merely seized territory; he was carrying his office as governor of Jiangsu into Zhejiang.

未免欺人太甚！
It was an intolerable affront.

但一时无奈其何，
For the moment, however, Zuo could do nothing about it.

只好先全力收复了杭州再说。
He could only concentrate on recovering Hangzhou first.


于是，胡雪岩开始计划，重回杭州；
Hu now began planning his return to Hangzhou.

由刘不才打先锋；北去是要收服一个张秀才，化敌为友，做个内应。
Liu was to go ahead, his task being to win over a certain Licentiate Zhang, turn an enemy into a friend, and secure help from within the city.

这个张秀才本是『破靴党』，
Zhang belonged to the shabby fraternity of petty scholars who exploited their status.

自以为衣冠中人，可以走动官府，平日包揽讼事，说合是非，欺软怕硬，
Considering himself a gentleman entitled to enter official offices, he habitually took charge of lawsuits and meddled in disputes, bullying the weak and cringing before the strong.

十分无赖。
He was an utter scoundrel.

王有龄当杭州知府时，深恶其人；久已想行文学官，革他的功名，
When Wang Youling had been prefect of Hangzhou, he had detested the man and long wanted to ask the educational authorities to strip him of his degree.

只是一时不得其便，隐忍在心。
Lacking a suitable opportunity, he had kept his anger to himself.

这张秀才与各衙门的差役都有勾结——
Zhang was in league with the runners at the various government offices.

杭州各衙门的差役，有一项陋规收入，凡是有人开设商铺，照例要向该管地方衙门的差役缴纳规费，
These men enjoyed a customary illicit source of income: anyone opening a shop had to pay a fee to the runners attached to the office with jurisdiction over the premises.

看店铺大小，定数目高下，缴清规费，方得开张，
The amount varied with the size of the shop, and the fee had to be settled before business could begin.

其名叫做『吃盐水』。
This was called “drinking salt water.”

王有龄锐于任事，贴出告示，永远禁止；
Eager to discharge his duties, Wang posted a proclamation forbidding it for good.

钱塘、仁和两县的差役，心存顾忌，一时敛迹；
The runners of Qiantang and Renhe, fearful of the consequences, temporarily restrained themselves.

巡抚、藩司两衙门，自觉靠山很硬，不买知府的帐，照收不误，
Those at the governor’s and provincial treasurer’s offices, confident in their powerful superiors, paid no heed to the prefect and continued collecting.

不过自己不便出面，指使张秀才去『吃盐水』，讲明三七分帐。
Since they could not conveniently appear in person, they sent Zhang, agreeing to divide the proceeds in a ratio of three to seven.


谁知运气不好，正在盐桥大街向一家刚要开张的估衣店讲斤头，讲不下来的时候，遇到王有龄坐轿路过，
Unfortunately for him, Wang happened to pass in his sedan chair while Zhang was haggling over the sum demanded from a second-hand clothing shop about to open on Yanqiao Street.

发现其事，停轿询问，
Wang noticed the disturbance, stopped, and made inquiries.

估衣店的老板，照实陈述；
The proprietor told him exactly what had happened.

王有龄大怒，决定拿张秀才『开刀』，立个榜样。
In a rage, Wang decided to make an example of Zhang.

当时传到轿前，先申斥了一顿；疾言厉色警告，一定要革他的功名。
He summoned him before the chair, reprimanded him, and sternly declared that his degree would be revoked.

这一下张秀才慌了手脚，
Zhang panicked.

一革秀才，便成白丁，
Once stripped of his licentiate’s status, he would be a commoner.

不但见了地方官要磕头，而且可以拖翻在地打屁股；锁在衙门照墙边『枷号示众』。
He would have to kneel before the local magistrate and could be thrown to the ground and beaten on the buttocks, or fastened in a cangue beside the screen wall of the yamen for public humiliation.

想来想去只有去托王有龄言中计从的胡雪岩。
After much thought, he could see only one course: appeal to Hu, whose advice Wang ordinarily followed.

带了老婆儿女到阜康钱庄，见了胡雪岩便跪倒在地，苦苦哀示。
He brought his wife and children to Fukang Bank, fell to his knees before Hu, and pleaded desperately.

胡雪岩一时大意，只当小事一件，王有龄必肯依从，
Hu carelessly assumed that this was a small matter and Wang would oblige him.

因而满口答应，包他无事。
He promised without reservation that Zhang would come to no harm.

哪知王有龄执意不从，
But Wang refused to yield.

说这件事与他的威信有关；
His authority was at stake, he said.

他新兼署了督粮道，又奉命办理团练，
He had recently become acting grain intendant and had also been ordered to organize militia.

筹兵筹饷，号令极其重要，
In raising troops and funds, obedience to his commands was essential.

倘或这件为民除害的陋习不革，号令不行，何以服众？
If he could not abolish an abuse that harmed the people, or enforce his own proclamation, how could he command respect?

说之再三，王有龄算是让了一步。
After repeated persuasion, he finally made one concession.

本来预备革掉张秀才的功名，打他两百小板子，枷号三月；
He had intended to revoke Zhang’s degree, give him two hundred strokes with the small paddle, and expose him in the cangue for three months.

现在看胡雪岩的份上，免掉他的皮肉受苦，出乖露丑，
For Hu’s sake, he would spare the physical suffering and public disgrace.

秀才却非革不可。
But the degree must go.

说实在的，胡雪岩已经帮了他的大忙；
In truth, Hu had done Zhang an enormous service.

而他只当胡雪岩不肯尽力，塘塞敷衍，
Zhang, however, thought he had merely made excuses instead of exerting himself.

从此怀恨在心，处处为难。到现在还不肯放过胡雪岩。
He bore a grudge thereafter, obstructing Hu wherever he could, and had not relented even now.


幸好一物降一物∶『恶人自有恶人磨』，
Fortunately, everything has its master, and one rogue can be made to curb another.

张秀才什么人不怕，除了官就只怕他儿子。
Apart from officials, the only person Zhang feared was his son.

小张是个纨绔嫖赌吃着，一应俱全。
Young Zhang was a wastrel, devoted to whoring, gambling, and every luxury.

张秀才弄来的几个造孽钱，都供养了宝贝儿子。
The ill-gotten money his father scraped together all went to maintain this treasured son.

刘不才也是纨绔出身，论资格比小张深得多；
Liu had himself been a rich young idler and possessed far more experience than the younger Zhang.

所以胡雪岩想了一套办法，用刘不才从小张身上下手。
Hu therefore devised a plan to reach the father through the son.

收服了小张，不怕张秀才不就范。
Once the son was won over, the father could be brought to terms.

到杭州的第二天，刘不才就进城去访小张——
On his second day back in Hangzhou, Liu went into the city to look for the young man.

杭州的市面还萧条得很，十室九空，
Business remained desperately depressed; nine houses in ten stood empty.

只有上城清河坊、中城荐桥、下城盐桥大街，比较象个样子；
Only Qinghefang in the upper city, Jianqiao in the middle, and Yanqiao Street in the lower city retained something of their former appearance.

但是店家未到黄昏，就都上了排门，
Even there, shops put up their shutters before dusk.

入夜一片沉寂，
After nightfall everything fell silent.

除掉巡逻的长毛，几乎看不见一个百姓。
Apart from patrolling rebels, scarcely a civilian was to be seen.

但是，有几条巷子里，却是别有天地；
A few lanes, however, belonged to another world.

其中有一条在荐桥，因为中城的善后局设在这里，
One was in Jianqiao, where the middle city’s reconstruction bureau had been established.

一班地痞流氓，在张秀才指使之下，假维持地方供应长毛为名，派捐征税，俨然官府；
Under Zhang’s direction, a band of local ruffians levied contributions and taxes in the name of preserving order and supplying the rebels, acting as though they were the government itself.

日常聚会之处，少不得有烟有赌有土娼。
Their usual meeting places naturally offered opium, gambling, and unlicensed prostitutes.

刘不才心里在想，小张既是那样一个脚色，当然倚仗他老子的势力，在这种场合中当『大少爷』；
Liu reasoned that a young man of Zhang’s character would surely exploit his father’s influence to play the grand young master in such places.

一定可以找到机会跟他接近。
There he would find an opportunity to approach him.


去的时候是天刚断黑，
It was just growing dark when he arrived.

只见门口两盏大灯笼，一群挺胸凸肚的闲汉在大声说笑；
Two large lanterns hung at the entrance, where a crowd of swaggering idlers were laughing and talking loudly.

刘不才踱了过去朝里一望，
Liu strolled over and looked inside.

大门洞开，直到二厅，
The doors stood open through to the second hall.

院子里是各种卖零食的担子，厅上灯火闪耀照出黑压压的一群人，
Vendors had set up their snack stalls in the courtyard, and bright lights in the hall illuminated a dense crowd.

一望而知是个赌局。
It was plainly a gambling establishment.

是公开的赌局，就谁都可以进去；
Since it was open to the public, anyone could enter.

刘不才提脚跨上门槛，
Liu stepped over the threshold.

有个人喝一声∶『喂！』
“Hey!” someone called.

刘不才站住脚，陪个不亢不卑的笑，
He stopped with a smile that was neither servile nor arrogant.

『老兄叫我？』他问。
“Were you calling me, friend?”

『你来做啥？』
“What have you come for?”

『我来看小张。』
“To see young Zhang.”

『小张！
“Young Zhang?

哪个小张？』
Which young Zhang?”

『张秀才的大少爷。』刘不才不慌不忙地答道∶『我跟他是老朋友。』
“Licentiate Zhang’s son,” Liu replied unhurriedly. “An old friend of mine.”

这下还真冒充得对了；
His pretence was well chosen.

因为张秀才得势的缘故，他儿子大为神气，除非老朋友，没有人敢叫他小张。
With his father’s rise to power, the son had grown so important that only an old friend would dare call him merely “young Zhang.”

那个人听他言语合拢，挥挥放他进门。
Finding the answer plausible, the man waved him in.


进门到二厅，两桌赌摆在那里，一桌牌九一桌宝；
In the second hall stood two gambling tables, one for pai gow and one for bao.

牌九大概是霉庄，所以场面比那桌宝热闹得多。
The pai gow banker appeared to be losing, and his table was much livelier.

刘不才知道赌场中最犯忌在人丛中乱钻，
Liu knew that pushing through a crowd was particularly unwelcome in a gambling house.

只悄悄站在人背后，踮起脚看。
He stood quietly behind the others and rose on tiptoe to watch.

推庄的是个中年汉子，满脸横肉，油光闪亮；
The banker was a middle-aged man with a fleshy, belligerent face shining with oil.

身上穿一件缎面大毛袍子，袖口又宽又大，显然的这件贵重衣服不是他本人所有。
He wore a satin-covered robe of heavy fur, with sleeves so broad and large that the expensive garment plainly did not belong to him.

人多大概又输得急了，但见他解开大襟衣纽，一大块毛茸茸的白狐皮翻了开来，斜挂在胸前，
Whether from the crowd or the strain of his losses, he had unfastened the buttons across his chest, exposing a broad fold of white fox fur that hung diagonally down the front.

还不住喊热，扭回头去向身后的人瞪眼，是怪他们不该围得这么密不通风，害他热得透不过气来的神情。
Still complaining of the heat, he turned to glare at those behind him, as though they were to blame for crowding so close that he could hardly breathe.

『吴大炮！』上门一个少年说，
“Wu Bigmouth!” said a young man at the upper hand.

『我看你可以歇歇了。
“I think you should rest.

宁与爷争，莫与牌争！』
Argue with a man if you must, but never with the tiles!”

输了钱的人，最听不得这种话；
A losing gambler could bear no advice less easily.

然而那吴大炮似乎敢怒而不敢言，
Yet Wu seemed afraid to answer.

紧闭着嘴，将两个肋帮子鼓得老高，那副生闷气的神情，教人好笑。
He kept his lips tightly shut and puffed out his cheeks in such silent resentment that he looked comical.


『好话不听，没有法子。』那少年问家∶『你说推长庄，总也有个歇手的时候；
“If you will not listen to good advice, there is nothing I can do,” the young man continued. “You said you would bank for a long run, but you must stop sometime.

莫非一个人推到天亮？』
Do you mean to sit there until dawn?”

『是不是你要推庄？』
“Do you want to be banker?”

吴大炮有些沉不住气了，
Wu was losing patience.

从身上摸出一叠银票，
He pulled out a bundle of banknotes.

『这里二百两只多不少，
“There are at least two hundred taels here.

输光了拉倒。』
I shall stop when I have lost them.”

『银票！』
“Banknotes!”

少年顾左右而言，
The young man looked from side to side.

『这个时候用银票？
“Banknotes at a time like this?

哪家钱庄开门，好去兑银子？』
Which bank is open to redeem them?”

『一大半是阜康的票子。』吴大炮说，
“Most are Fukang notes,” Wu replied.

『阜康上海有分号，
“Fukang has a branch in Shanghai.

为啥不好兑？』
Why should they not be redeemable?”

『你倒蛮相信阜康的！
“You certainly have faith in Fukang.

不过要问问大家相信不相信？』
But you must ask whether everyone else does.”

少年扬脸回顾，
The young man looked around.

『怎么说？』
“Well?”

『银票不用，原是说明了的。』有人这样说，
“We agreed at the start: no banknotes,” someone said.

『不管阜康啥康，统通一样。
“Fukang or any other bank, it makes no difference.

要赌就是现银子。』
Gambling means silver in hand.”

『听见没有？』少年对吴大炮说，
“Did you hear?” the young man said to Wu.

『你现银子只有二、三十两了，
“You have only twenty or thirty taels in silver left.

我在上门打一记，
I shall stake against it on the upper hand.

赢了你再推下去；
If you win, keep banking.

输了让位。
If you lose, give up your place.

好不好？』
Agreed?”


吴大炮想了一下，咬一咬牙说∶『好！』
Wu thought, gritted his teeth, and agreed.

开门掷骰，是个『五在首』，
The dice were thrown: a five at the head.

吴大炮抓起牌来就往桌上一番，
