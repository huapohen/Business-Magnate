胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第八章（本章第2部分，共9部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


How could Zhang fail to know her?
小张怎么不知道？

Madam A’gou was a celebrated procuress who ran a very large private brothel.
阿狗嫂是有名的一个老鸨；主持一家极大的『私门头』，

The boatwomen who offered their favours aboard the Jiangshan boats on the Fuchun River all lodged with her when they came ashore.
凡是富春江上『江山船』中投怀送抱的船娘，一上了岸都以阿狗嫂为居停。

Zhang knew her well.
小张跟她，亦很相熟；

But the siege of Hangzhou had ruined the pleasure trade, and he had not seen her since the upheaval.
只是杭州被围，花事阑珊，乱后却还不曾见过。

“So Madam A’gou did not starve to death!” he exclaimed, surprised and delighted.
因而小张又惊又喜地问∶『阿狗嫂倒不曾饿杀！』

“Her place is lively again.
『她那里又热闹了。

But I am staying in the rear, where it is very quiet.”
不过我住在她后面，很清静。』

“Good!
『好！

I shall certainly come tomorrow afternoon.”
明天下午我一定来。』


Madam A’gou had prepared the rooms specially for Liu.
刘不才的住处是阿狗嫂特地替他预备的，

They occupied a separate courtyard at the back, with a dividing gate that could be bolted to shut them off from the front and an entrance of their own.
就在后面，单成院落，有一道腰门，闩上门便与前面隔绝；另有出入的门户。

When Zhang arrived, Liu changed his form of address. “Brother Zhang, will you accept Fukang notes?”
『』张兄，『刘不才改了称呼，』阜康的票子你要不要？

“Oh, I nearly forgot.”
『』喔，我倒忘记了。

Zhang took a small tissue-paper parcel from his pocket and handed it over.
『小张从身上掏出一个棉纸小包，递了过去，』

“Here is your property.
东西在这里，

Have a look.”
你看一看！

“No need.”
『『不必看。』

Liu gave him a fifty-tael banknote.
刘不才交了五十两一张庄票；

With the transaction settled, he opened a cupboard. “Brother Zhang, I have a few little things for you.
银货两讫以后，拉开橱门说道∶『张兄，我有几样小意思送你。

Let us be friends.”
我们交个朋友。』

The little things varied in size and shape: a long walking stick, a small gold watch, a large box of Manila cigars, and an angular rectangular parcel less than five cun long that was harder to identify.
那些『小意思』长短大小不一，长的是一枝『司的克』；小的是一个金表；大的是一副吕宋烟；还有短不及五寸，方楞折角的一包东西，就看不出来了——

It looked like books, but Zhang could not believe that Liu would give him books.
样子象书；小张却不相信他会送自己一部书。

Besides, to give books to a gambler was to invite bad luck—the word sounded like “lose.”
而且给好赌的人送书，也嫌『触霉头』。


“Look at this stick.
『你看这枝「司的克」，

An excellent thing for protecting yourself.”
防身的好东西。』

Liu raised it. “Watch out!”
刘不才举起来喝一声∶『当心！』

Then he brought it down towards Zhang’s head.
接着便当头砸了下来。

Zhang naturally threw up a hand to block it and caught the far end.
小张当然拿手一格，捏住了尾端。

With some movement of Liu’s, the stick parted in two.
也不知刘不才怎么一下，那根『司的克』分成两截，

What remained in his hand was a gleaming short sword.
握在刘不才手里的，是一枝雪亮的短剑。

“How does it work?” Zhang asked eagerly.
『怎么搞的？』小张大感兴趣，

“Let me see!”
『我看看，我看看。』

The blade was quite unlike a Chinese sword: triangular, with a needle-sharp point and a groove along each of its three faces.
看那短剑，形制与中国的剑完全不同；三角形；尖端如针；剑身三面血槽，

It was unquestionably a deadly weapon.
确是可以致人于死的利器。

“There is a mechanism in the middle,” Liu explained.
『你看，这中间有机关。』

The join in the stick fitted with remarkable precision.
原来司的克中间有榫头，做得严丝合缝，极其精细；

If someone attacked, its owner could strike with it.
遇到有人袭击，拿司的克砸过去，

An opponent who did not catch it would merely suffer a blow; one who tried to seize it would fall into the trap.
对方不抓不过挨一下打；若是想夺它就上当了，

A twist, using the other man’s grip, released the short sword for an unexpected thrust.
正好借势一扭，抽出短剑刺过去，突出不意，必定得手。

Once Zhang understood its ingenuity, he liked it all the more.
了解了妙用，小张越发喜爱；

It would protect him, certainly; better still, he could display it and boast of it to others when nothing threatened him.
防身固然得力；无事拿来献献宝，夸耀于人，更是一乐。


He was still grinning with delight when Liu said, “Here are some foreign books.”
所以笑得嘴都合不拢了。『这里是几本洋书。』

So they were books after all!
果然是书！

That gift was wide of the mark.
这就送得不对路了，

Zhang clasped his hands. “Old Liu, between friends, let me be frank. I can barely understand Chinese books.
小张拱拱手说∶『老刘！好朋友说实话∶中国书我都不大看得懂；

Foreign ones would leave me completely at sea.”
洋书更加「赵大人看榜」，莫名其妙。』

“You will understand these.”
『你看得懂的。』

Liu put them in his hands.
刘不才将交到他手里，

“Take them home and look through them slowly, by yourself.”
『带回去一个人慢慢看。』

The suggestion was too intriguing: Zhang had to open the parcel immediately.
这句话中，奥妙无穷，小张就非当时拆开来看不可了。

A glance made his blood race.
打开来一翻，顿觉血脉贲张——

It was a foreign erotic picture book.
是一部『洋春宫』。

Nothing else could hold his attention now.
这一下就目不旁观了。

Liu quietly brought a chair and helped him sit, then took a seat some distance away.
刘不才悄悄端了张椅子扶他坐下；自己远远坐在一边，

He watched coolly as Zhang stared and swallowed, every expression betraying his absorption.
冷眼旁观，看他眼珠凸出，不断咽口水的穷形极相，

Liu’s confidence grew.
心里越发泰然。

At last Zhang finished.
好不容易，小张才看完，

“What a treat!” he said with a somewhat embarrassed smile. “Old Liu, where did you find this?”
『过瘾！』他略带些窘地笑道∶『老刘，你哪里觅来的？』


“In the foreign settlement at Shanghai, of course.”
『自然是上海夷场上。』

“Plenty of people have been to Shanghai, but I have never seen them bring back anything like these.”
『去过上海的也很多，从没有看着他们带过这些东西回来。』

Zhang was full of admiration.
小张不胜钦服地说，

“Old Liu, you certainly know how to manage!”
『老刘，你真有办法！』

“Not at all.
『我也没办法。

I would not know where to find them myself.
这些东西，我也不知道哪里去觅？

I picked them up from a relative.
是一个亲戚那里顺手牵来的。

But we can discuss that later.
这话回头再说；

Look at these other two things first.”
你先看看这两样东西。』

These were the large box and the small one.
这就是一大一小两个盒子；

Zhang examined both carefully.
小张倒都仔细看了。

As he did so, he felt ashamed to receive such presents for nothing.
一面看，一面想，凭空受人家这份礼，实在不好意思；

Yet he could not bear to give up the stick and the foreign books.
不受呢，那支司的克和那部『洋书』真有些舍不得放手。

After prolonged indecision, he could only speak honestly. “Old Liu, we have only just met.
想了半天，委决不下，只有说老实话∶『老刘，我们初交，

You are so generous that I do not know what to say.
你这样够朋友，我也不晓得怎么说才好？

But I really feel rather embarrassed.”
不过，我真的不大好意思。』

“Then you are treating me as a stranger, my dear fellow.
『这你就见外了。老弟台，

Friendship is not for a single day.
朋友不是交一天；

If you insist on drawing such distinctions between us, I shall scarcely dare presume upon your acquaintance again.”
要这样分彼此，以后我就不敢高攀了。』

“I make no distinctions!” Zhang protested earnestly.
『我不分，我不分。』小张极力辩白，

“Only you must let me do something in return.”
不过，『你总也要让我尽点心意才好。』


He appeared won over.
看样子是收服了，

There was no need to labour the point; Liu would strike while the iron was hot.
那就不必多费功夫，打铁趁热，

“I shall be frank too.
『我也说老实话，

These things do not belong to me.
这些东西，不是我的；

A relative asked me to bring them.” He added, “Your father has misunderstood this relative of mine.
是我一个亲威托我带来的。』他接着又说∶『你家老太爷，对我这个亲戚有点误会；

More than misunderstood him—he has really done him an injustice.”
不但误会，简直有点冤枉。』

“Who is your relative?”
『喔，』小张问道∶「令亲是哪一个？

“Hu Xueyan of Fukang Bank.”
『『阜康钱庄的胡雪岩。』

“Him!” Zhang exclaimed.
小张失声说道∶『是他啊！』

“Yes.
『是他。

Why do I say your father has wronged him through this misunderstanding?
怎么说你家老太爷对他的误会是冤枉的呢？

Such things must be explained.
话不说不明，

I happen to know something about it.”
我倒晓得一点。』

Zhang waited attentively, but Liu seemed reluctant to proceed.
小张很注意地在等他说下去，而刘不才却迟疑着不大愿意开口的样子；

This was puzzling.
这就令人奇怪了，

“Old Liu, did you not say that you knew the inside story?”
『老刘！』小张问道∶『你不是说晓得其中的内情吗？』


“Yes, I know it thoroughly.
『是的，我完全晓得。

When Governor Wang was transferred from Huzhou to Hangzhou, I came with him and managed miscellaneous affairs in his office.
王抚台由湖州府调杭州的时候，我是从湖州跟了他来的，在他衙门里办庶务，

I know exactly what happened.
所以十分清楚。

But if we discuss the rights and wrongs, your father is my elder too.
不过，这件事谈起来若论是非；你家老太爷也是我长辈。

I ought not to criticize him.”
我不便说他。』

“What does that matter?
『那有什么关系？

We are among friends.
自己人讲讲不要紧。

The old man at home is famous enough.
我们家「老的」，名气大得很，

Heaven knows how many people have criticized him.
不晓得多少人说过他，

I have heard plenty; why should I mind hearing you?”
我也听得多了，又何在乎你批评他？』

“It is not criticism, really.
『我倒不是批评他老人家，

I only think he was too careless and impatient.
是怪他太大意，太心急了。

A newly appointed official is eager to make his authority felt; he ought to have kept out of the way for a while.
「新官上任三把火」，该当避他一避；

Instead, he was caught collecting ‘salt-water’ fees.
偏偏「吃盐水」让他撞见。

The proclamation was still posted there—the paste was scarcely dry—yet someone was already treating the prefect’s words as nothing.
告示就贴在那里浆糊都还没有干，就有人拿他的话不当话，

It was like a slap in the face.
好比一巴掌打在他脸上——

After all, he was the head of Hangzhou prefecture, responsible for several counties and more than a million people.
人家到底是杭州一府之首，管着好几县上百万的老百生；

How could he hold his authority securely after that?
这一来他那个印把子怎么捏得牢？

My dear fellow, spend the first half of the night considering the other man, and the second half considering yourself.
老弟，「前半夜想想人家。后半夜想想自己。」

If you had been Governor Wang, would you not have been furious?”
换了你是王抚台，要不要光火？』

Zhang said nothing.
小张默然。

Liu’s reasoning was clear enough, but it was their new friendship that principally disposed him to listen.
倒不仅因为刘不才的话说得透彻；主要的还是因为有交情在那里，就什么话都容易听得进去了。

“True, Xueyan failed to save your father’s degree.
『不错，雪岩当时没有能保得住你家老太爷的秀才。

But outsiders know only half the story.
不过，外头只知其一，不知其二；

Governor Wang not only wrote to the educational authorities to have it revoked; he intended to punish your father afterwards to satisfy his anger.
王抚台动公事给学里老师，革掉了秀才还要办人出气。

Xueyan would not consent to that.
这个上头，雪岩一定不答应，

He tried entreaty first and then defiance, until the governor reluctantly yielded.”
先软后硬，王抚台才算勉强卖了个面子。』

“Oh?”
『喔，』

Zhang blinked repeatedly. “I did not know that.
小张乱眨着眼说∶『这我倒不晓。

What do you mean by entreaty and defiance?”
怎么叫「先软后硬？」』


“Entreaty meant kneeling; defiance meant a quarrel.
『软是下跪，硬是吵架。

Xueyan was ready to break with Governor Wang for your father’s sake.
雪岩为了你家老太爷，要跟王抚台绝交；

Afterwards they said he had failed as a friend and would not help.
以后倒反说他不够朋友不帮忙，

Was that not unjust?”
你说冤枉不冤枉？』

“As you explain it, he certainly seems to have been wronged.
『照你这么说，倒真的是冤枉了他？』

But why send me these things?
小张紧接着说∶『那末，他又为啥要送我这些东西。

It would be extraordinary to be so kind.”
好人好到这样子，也就出奇了。』

“Nothing extraordinary.
『一点不奇。

Naturally, he has a favour to ask.”
他自然有事拜托你。』

“Then ask!” Zhang replied generously. “I do not know Mr Hu well, but you have treated me as a friend.
『可以！』小张慨然答道∶『胡老板我不熟，不过你够朋友。

If it is something I can do, I shall certainly help.”
只要我做得到，你说了我一定帮助。』

“I am not praising my own relative without cause.
『说起来，不是我捧自己亲戚，

Hu Xueyan really does stand by his friends.
胡雪岩实在是够朋友的；

Although your father has misunderstood him, he has already made provision for your father’s future and left him room to retreat.”
你家老太爷对他虽有误会，他倒替你家老太爷伸好后脚，留好余地在那里了。』

This was too obscure for Zhang to follow, though he could tell it meant something favourable.
这两句话没头没脑，小张不明所以；但话是好话，却总听得出来，

“Then I must thank him.
『这倒是谢谢他了。』他问，

What sort of provision?”
『不知道伸好一只什么后脚？』

“Let me show you something.”
『我先给你看样东西。』


Liu dragged a leather case from beneath the bed, unlocked it, took out a document folder, and handed Zhang an official communication.
刘不才从床底下拖出皮箱来，开了锁，取出一本『护书』，抽了一通公文，送到小张手里。

Zhang’s learning was limited.
小张肚子里的墨水有限，

He could recognize the great purple seal of the governor of Jiangsu and his father’s name, but the elaborate official phrasing left him unable to make out the meaning.
不过江苏巡抚部堂的紫泥大印，是看得懂的；他父亲的名字也是认识的，此外由于公文套子转来转去，一时就弄不明白是说些什么了。

“You must never speak of this dispatch outside.
『这件公事，千万不能说出去。

If word reaches the rebels, the consequences will be terrible.”
一说出去，让长毛知道了不得了。』

Liu assumed an air of solemn warning, then lightened his manner. “Take it home and ask your father to keep it secretly.
刘不才故作郑重地嘱咐；然后换了副轻快的神情说∶「你带回去，请老太爷密密收藏；

When the government troops recover Hangzhou, he can produce it.
有一天官军克复杭州，拿出公文来看，

Far from being guilty of aiding rebellion, he will be credited with preserving order.
不但没有助逆反叛之罪，还有维持地方之功。

Tell me, has Hu Xueyan done your father a great service or not?” Zhang began to understand.
你说，胡雪岩帮你家老太爷这个忙，帮得大不大。『这一说，小张方始有点明白；

One point still puzzled him. “What about now?
不解的是∶』那末眼前呢？

What are we to do now?”
眼前做点啥？『


“For the present, do whatever needs doing.
『眼前，当然该做啥就做啥。

Are you not preserving order?
不是维持地方吗，

Continue as before.”
照常维持好了。』

“Ah!”
『喔，喔！』

Zhang finally saw.
小张终于恍然大悟，

“A foot in each boat.”
『这就是脚踏两头船。』

“Exactly.
『对！脚踏两头船。

But the boat you are standing in now will capsize sooner or later.
不过，现在所踏的这只船，早晚要翻身的；

The other boat matters more.”
还是那只船要紧。』

“I understand.”
『我懂。我懂。』

“And your father?”
『你们老太爷呢？』

“I shall tell him.
『我去跟他说，

He will be delighted.
他一定很高兴。』

You shall have an answer tomorrow.
小张答说∶『明天就有回话。

It is getting late; I must go.”
时候不早，我也要去了。』

Early the next morning, Zhang came to invite Liu to the family home.
第二天一早，小张上门，邀刘不才到家。

The licentiate was already waiting with wine warming.
张秀才早就煮酒在等了。


To cultivate their intimacy, Liu addressed him as “Uncle” and performed a full ceremonial bow.
为了套交情，刘不才不但口称『老伯』；而且行了大礼，

Zhang was almost overwhelmed by the honour.
将张秀才喜得有些受宠若惊的模样。

“You are too kind, much too kind!
『不敢当，不敢当！

Brother Liu,” he said, pointing to his son, “this wretch never makes respectable friends.
刘三哥，』他指着小张说，『我这个畜生从来不交正经朋友；

Who would have thought he would meet a man like you?
想不到交上了你刘三哥。

A blessing for our family indeed!”
真正我家门之幸。』

“Uncle, you will make my face red before I have even drunk any wine.”
『老伯说得我不曾吃酒，脸就要红了。』

“Quite right!
『对了，

Let us drink.
吃酒，吃酒！

Drinking makes friendship stronger; gambling makes it weaker.”
朋友交情，吃酒越吃越厚，赌钱越赌越薄。』

He scolded his son again.
他又骂儿子，

“This wretch loves gambling.
『这个畜生，就是喜欢赌；

Nine times out of ten, when I go to a gambling house, I find him there.”
我到赌场里去，十次倒有九次遇见他。』

“You need not complain about others,” the son retorted.
『你也不要说人家。』小张反唇相讥，

“If you go ten times and find me there nine, I still go once less than you!”
『你去十次，九次遇见我；总还比你少一次！』

“Listen to him!”
『你看看，你看看！』

Zhang’s two wisps of yellow moustache quivered with anger.
张秀才气得两撇黄胡子乱动，

“The wretch twists every argument!”
『这个畜生说的话，强词夺理。』


The sight of a father and son each so far from the proper dignity of his role amused Liu greatly. “I suppose my young friend is your only son,” he said.
刘不才看他们父不父，子不子，实在好笑∶『老伯膝下，大概就是我这位老弟一个。』他说，

“Spoiled from childhood!”
『从小宠惯了！』

“His mother spoiled him.
「『都是他娘宠的。

A misfortune for the family, Brother Liu. We make ourselves ridiculous before you.”
家门不幸，叫你刘三哥见笑。』

“Not at all.
『说哪里话！

I find him most capable and personable—admirably suited to dealing with the world.”
我倒看我这位老弟，着实能干、漂亮。绝好的外场人物。

This touched the father’s pride.
『一句话说到张秀才得意的地方，

Composing his face, he replied, “Brother Liu, jade must be polished before it becomes useful.
敛容答道∶』刘三哥，玉不琢，不成器；

The wretch has a sort of cleverness, but he needs to follow someone and gain experience.
我这个畜生，鬼聪明是有的，不过要好好跟人去靡炼。

We can discuss that later.
回头我们细谈，

First, let us drink.”
先吃酒。

The three sat around the brazier, eating and drinking, with the usual preliminary talk of inconsequential matters.
『于是宾主三人，围炉小炊；少不得先有些不着边际的闲话。


When enough had been said, the father signalled with his mouth.
谈到差不多，张秀才向他儿子呶一呶嘴；

The son rose, went out of the hall, looked around, and called loudly to a servant. “Guisheng, tell the gatekeeper that the master is unwell today and will receive no visitors.
小张便起身出堂屋，四面看了一下，大声吩咐他家的男仆∶『贵生，你去告诉门上；老爷今天身子不舒服，不见客。

If anyone asks for me, say I am out.
问到我，说不在家。

Official business can be brought to the bureau this afternoon.”
如果有公事，下午到局子里去说。

This exclusion of outsiders announced that a confidential discussion was about to begin.
『这便是摒绝闲杂，倾心谈秘密的先声，

Liu prepared himself and waited.
刘不才心里就有了预备，只待张秀才发话。

“Brother Liu, are you a close relative of Xueyan?”
『刘三哥，你跟雪岩至亲？』

The question itself was ordinary, but the form of address was significant.
话是泛泛之词，称呼却颇具意味；

Instead of calling him Intendant Hu, Zhang used his personal name.
不叫『胡道台』而直呼其号，

This suggested both familiarity and equality.
这就是表示∶一则很熟；二则平起平坐的朋友。

刘不才再往深入细想一想，是张秀才仿佛在暗示∶他不念前嫌，有紧要话，尽说不妨。

如果自己猜得不错，那就是好征兆；

不过知人知面不知心，

又想起胡雪岩的叮嘱∶『逢人只说三分话』，所以很谨慎地答道∶『是的，我们是亲戚？』

『怎么称呼？』

『雪岩算是比我晚一辈。』

“Ah!
『啊呀呀，

张秀才说，『你又跟小儿叙朋友，

这样算起来，辈分排不清楚了。

刘三哥。我们大家平叙最好！』

“You are too kind.
『不敢！不敢！

我叫张大爷吧。』

Liu had no wish to spend more time on ceremonial niceties.
刘不才不愿在礼节上头，多费功夫，

嘱咐我这趟回杭州，一定要来看看张大爷，替他说声好。』

『说患难之交，倒是一点不错。

当初雪岩不曾得发的时候，我们在茶店里是每天见面的。

后来他有跟王抚台这番遇合，平步青云，

眼孔就高了。

一班穷朋友不大在他眼里；我们也高攀不上。

患难之交，变成了「点头朋友」。』

这是一番牢骚，刘不才静静听他发完，自然要作解释∶『雪岩后来忙了，礼节疏漏的地方难免；

“Yes, he knows how to conduct himself in the world,” Zhang conceded. “Since he is good enough to remember me, and times are what they are, whatever was unpleasant in the past should be wiped away.
『是啊！他外场是漂亮的。』张秀才说∶『承蒙他不弃，时世又是这个样子，过去有啥难过，也该一笔勾销，

大家重新做个朋友。』

“That is exactly his wish,” Liu replied.
『是！』刘不才答说，『雪岩也是这个意思。

“After all, we belong to the same place.

雪岩现在就是处处在留相见的余地。』

这番话说得很动听，

是劝张秀才留个相见的余地，却一点不着痕迹；

使得内心原为帮长毛做事而惶惑不安的张秀才，越发觉得该跟胡雪岩『重新做个朋友』了。

听这一说，刘不才将自己的椅子拉一拉，凑近了张秀才；两眼紧紧望着，是极其郑重、也极其诚恳的倾听之态。

既然这样子，我倒要请教刘三哥，雪岩还凭啥来混？』

The question went to the heart of the matter.
这话问在要害上，

Liu dared not answer carelessly.
刘不才不敢随便，

Better slow than wrong, he thought.
心里第一个念头是∶宁慢勿错。

所以一面点头，一面细想；

如果随意编上一段关系，说胡雪岩跟京里某大老如何如何；跟某省督抚又如何如何？话也可以编得很圆，无奈张秀才决不会相信；

That would be a foolish course.
所以这是个很笨的法子。

A more independent answer would be more persuasive.
刘不才认为话说得超脱些，反而动听，

“Patrons are unreliable.
因而这样答道∶『靠山都是假的，

Ability and friends are what count.
本事跟朋友才是真。

有本事、有朋友，自然寻得着靠山。』他又补上一句∶『张大爷，我这两句话说得很狂。

Please do not take offence.”
你老不要见气。』

“Good!”
『好！』

Zhang was plainly impressed.
张秀才倒是颇为倾心，

“Those two sentences show that you are a man of substance yourself.”
『刘三哥，听你这两句话，也是好脚色！』

“You flatter me.
『不敢，

I am merely talking.”
我乱说。』

『刘三哥，我再请教你，』

张秀才将声音放得极低∶『你看大局怎么样？』

This could not be answered lightly.
这话就不好轻易回答了；

Liu glanced at the son, who understood and nodded emphatically: he could speak freely.
刘不才拿眼看一看小张——小张会意，重重点头；表示但说不妨。

『我从前也跟张大爷一样，人好象闷在坛子里，黑漆一团；

People from every quarter gather in the foreign settlement, and news travels quickly.

大家都在说∶』这个「不长的！

『一面说，一面做了个手势，指一指头发，意示『这个』是指长毛。

张秀才听罢不响，

拿起水烟袋，噗噜噜、噗噜噜，抽了好一会方始开口。『你倒说说看，为啥不长？』

『这不是三言两语说得尽的——。』

刘不才的口才很好，何况官军又实在打得很好；

With both advantages on his side, he analysed the situation so persuasively that Zhang became entirely convinced.
两好并一好，刘不才分析局势，将张秀才说得死心塌地。

他也知道他们父子的名声不好，

They needed some extraordinary deed, a conspicuous service to their native place, to cover their many misdeeds and make people see them anew.
必得做一件惊世骇俗，大有功于乡邦的奇行伟举，才能遮掩得许多劣迹，令人刮目相看。

果然，

张秀才趁机进言，杭州的善后，非把胡雪岩请回来主持不可。
