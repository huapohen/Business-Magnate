胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第八章（本章第3部分，共9部分）

Jiang Yili thoroughly agreed.
蒋益澧深以为然。

The special mission to bring Hu back therefore fell to young Zhang.
于是专程迎接胡雪岩的差使，便落到了小张身上。

On reaching Shanghai, he settled at the Changfa Inn, a lodging favoured by travelling officials.
到得上海，先在『仕宦行台』的长发客栈安顿下来；

He then found the address Liu had given him and sent a junior attendant from the inn to fetch him.
随即找出刘不才留给他的地址，请客栈里派个小伙计去把刘不才请来。

“I reckoned you must be due, and here you are.”
『我算到你也该来了，果不其然。』

Without wasting words, Liu said exactly what Zhang wanted to hear.
刘不才再无闲话，开口就碰到小张的心坎上，

“I shall take you to see my relative first.
『我先带你去看舍亲，

Once your business is settled, you can enjoy yourself to your heart’s content.”
有啥话交代清楚；接下来就尽你玩了。』

“Old Liu, I am now a member of the Zhejiang Reconstruction Bureau, with the seventh rank.
『老刘，』小张答说，『我现在是浙江善后局的委员，七品官儿。

Provincial Treasurer Jiang has specially sent me to invite His Honour Hu back to Hangzhou.
这趟奉蒋藩台委派，特地来请胡大人回杭州；

That is all I have to say.”
要说的就是这句话。』

“Good.
『好！

I understand.
我晓得了。

Let us go at once.”
我们马上就走。』

Zhang took out his seventh-rank official robes and changed in front of his visitor.
于是小张将七品官服取出来，当着客人的面更衣；

Once dressed, he looked embarrassed, feeling rather like a monkey in an official hat.
换好了不免面有窘色，自觉有些沐猴而冠的味道。

Liu did not laugh.
刘不才倒没有笑他；

“Have your servant bring a change of clothes,” he said.
只说∶『请贵管家把衣包带去，

“That will save us returning here when you want to dress informally.”
省得再回来换便衣了。』

Zhang’s attendant, Zhang Sheng, was accustomed to serving officials.
小张带的一个长随张升，倒是一向『跟官』的，

He already had the visiting cards and clothes bundle ready.
名帖、衣包，早就预备好了，

The three took a carriage straight to Fukang Bank.
三个人一辆马车，径自来到阜康钱庄。


Hu was at a crucial stage in negotiations with several rice merchants.
胡雪岩跟一班米商在谈生意，正到紧要关头；

But Zhang had come from a distance and was calling in official dress.
因为小张远道而来，又是穿官服来拜访，

Hu had to apologize to his earlier visitors and leave them to greet the newcomer.
只得告个罪，抛下前客，来迎后客。

Zhang had seen him before.
小张是见过胡雪岩的，

As soon as Hu entered the small reception room, without waiting for an introduction, he cried, “Uncle Hu!” and respectfully knelt to kowtow.
所以一等他踏进小客厅，不必刘不才引见，便即喊一声∶『胡老伯！』恭恭敬敬地磕下头去。

“You are too kind!
『不敢当，不敢当！

My young friend, you are much too ceremonious.”
世兄忒多礼了。』

Hu hastily knelt as well.
胡雪岩赶紧亦跪了下去。

After exchanging bows and helping one another up, they made the usual inquiries after the family, then turned to business.
对磕过头，相扶而起，少不得不家几句寒暄；然后转入正题。

When Zhang had explained his mission, Hu replied, “It is a duty I cannot refuse.
等小张道明来意，胡雪岩答说∶『这是我义不容辞的事，

I am already making preparations.
已经在预备了。

Enjoy a few days in Shanghai, and we shall travel together.”
世兄在上海玩几天，我们一起走。』

“Yes, sir.”
『是！』

“Good!”
『好了！』

Liu interposed.
刘不才插进来对小张说，

“The business is settled.
『话交代清楚了；

Change your clothes, and we can go.”
你换一换衣服，我们好走了。』


Liu took Zhang sightseeing in the dazzling foreign settlement.
于是刘不才带着小张观光五光十色的夷场；

In the evening they ate a Western dinner and watched Peking opera.
到晚来吃大菜、看京戏。

Zhang’s eyes were opened to an entirely new world.
小张大开眼界，

Though tired late at night, he remained excited.
夜深入倦，兴犹未央；

Liu stayed with him at the Changfa Inn, and they talked beside their beds until nearly dawn before falling asleep.
刘不才陪他住在长发客栈，临床夜语，直到曙色将明，方始睡去。

Hu was still awake.
这时的胡雪岩却还未睡，

He needed to ship ten thousand shi of rice to Hangzhou.
因为他要运一万石米到杭州，

Several merchants had agreed to supply it, only to change their minds at the last moment.
接头了几个米商，说得好好的，到头来却又变了封，

He had no choice but to seek out You Wu.
迫不得已只好去找尤五；

Finding him only in the middle of the night, he stated his need directly.
半夜里方始寻着，直截了当地提出要求。

You knew the rice trade, but the rice firm collectively run by the Grain-Boat Guild in Songjiang had long since closed.
尤五对米生意本是内行，但松江漕帮公设的米行，早已歇业，

He had been out of touch for some time, and such a vast quantity could not be produced at a word.
隔膜已久；而且数量甚巨，并非叱嗟可办。

Over the past few years he had kept out of sight, guarded his speech, and grown increasingly cautious.
他这几年韬光隐晦，谨言慎行，做事越发仔细；

He dared not promise what he could not guarantee.
没把握的事，一时不敢答应。

“Little Uncle, naturally I would not refuse an instruction from you.
『小爷叔，你的吩咐，我当然不敢说个「不」字；

But you know my circumstances.
不过，我的情形你也晓得的，

To buy rice now, I should first have to find the people.
现在要办米，我还要现去找人。

Without my old team ready to hand, I cannot be certain of the time.
「班底」不凑手，日子上就捏不住了。

When you could not get your rice into Hangzhou and diverted it to Ningbo, did they not promise to replace the full quantity whenever you needed it?”
从前你运米到杭州进不了城，改运宁波，不是他们答应过你的，一旦要用，照数补米？』


Yang Fang had indeed made that agreement with Hu to obtain relief for his native place.
这是当初杨坊为了接济他家乡，与胡雪岩有过这样的约定。

But Yang’s position had changed.
只是杨坊今非昔比，

Implicated in the affair in which Burgevine seized military funds and assaulted an official, he had fallen badly from Li Hongzhang’s favour.
因为白齐文劫饷殴官一案受累，在李鸿章 那里栽了大跟头，

His suspension and investigation had not yet been lifted.
现在撤职查办的处分未消，

He had neither the inclination nor the means to fulfil his promise.
哪里有实践诺言的心情和力量。

Hu would not take advantage of a man in distress and had decided to find his own solution.
胡雪岩不肯乘人于危，决定自己想办法。

After hearing the explanation, You said admiringly, “Little Uncle, you truly stand by your friends.
听完他所讲的这番缘由，尤五赞叹着说∶『小爷叔，你真够朋友；

But Yang is not like you.
不过人家姓杨的不象你。

He made a considerable fortune out of the Ever-Victorious Army, which is also why Governor Li will not let him off.
他靠常胜军，着实发了一笔财；李抚台饶不过他，亦是如此。

Even if you do not require replacement rice, he should repay the money.
如今米虽不要他补，米款应当还你；

It cost a little over two taels a shi then.
当初二两多银子一石；

Now it is nearly six, and hard to buy even at that price.
现在涨到快六两了，还不容易采办。

Surely you do not mean to settle with him at the old rate?”
莫非你仍旧照当初的价钱跟他结算？』

“Certainly not.
『那当然办不到的。

He must pay me at the current market price.
要衣他照市价结给我。

Otherwise I shall proceed against him through official channels and see whether he can bear it.”
不然我跟他动公事，看他吃得消，吃不消？』


“Then the money need not worry us,” You said, nodding.
『钱是不愁了，』尤五点点头，

“But, Little Uncle, finding ten thousand shi will be extremely difficult.
『不过，小爷叔，你想办一万石米，实在不容易。

Jiangsu has been short of grain these past two years and has depended on shipments from Huguang and Jiangxi.
这两年江苏本来缺粮，靠湖广、江西贩来；

Last year, when Governor Li bought rice to send to the capital, he had to purchase foreign rice as well.
去年李抚台办米运进京，还采办了洋米，

Thirty thousand men worked for two months before the quantity was complete.
三万人办了两个月才凑齐；

I fear you cannot obtain ten thousand shi within a month now.”
你此刻一个月当中要办一万石，只怕办不到。』

“It is not a month.
『不是一个月。

The month must include delivery to Hangzhou.
一个月包括运到杭州的日子在内，

The purchase must be complete in twenty days at most.”
最多二十天就要办齐。』

“That is harder still.
『那更难了。

I doubt even the authorities could manage it.”
只怕官府都办不到。』

“If we can do what the authorities cannot, that will show our ability.”
『官府办不到，我们办得到，才算本事。』

The remark put You’s capabilities to the test.
这句话等于在掂尤五的斤两。

Having spoken of difficulty twice, he could hardly do so a third time.
说了两次难，不能再说第三次了；

He remained silent, considering every possibility at length, before sighing. “We can only all put our heads to it.”
尤五不作声，思前想后打算了好久，还是叹口气说∶『只好大家来想办法。』


They went off in different directions to seek help.
分头奔马，

In the end it was the Seventh Lady who found the proprietress of Dafeng Rice Firm, known as the Fair-faced Tigress.
结果是七姑奶奶出马，找到大丰米行的老板娘『粉面虎』；

She combined rice due for delivery to the capital with two thousand shi of foreign rice held at Jardine Matheson, making eighty-five hundred shi for Hu.
将应交的京米，以及存在怡和洋行的两千石洋米，都凑了给胡雪岩，一共是八千五百石，

You found the remainder, quickly bringing the total to ten thousand.
余数由尤五设法，很快地凑足了万石之数。

Negotiations with Yang yielded fifty thousand taels towards the purchase price.
米款跟杨坊办交涉，收回五万两银子；

Hu temporarily made up the shortfall from the hundred and twenty thousand he was raising to return to Wang’s surviving family.
不足之数由胡雪岩在要凑还王有龄遗族的十二万银子中，暂时挪用。

Everything went smoothly.
一切顺利，

Within thirteen days, the sea junks had spread their sails.
只十三天的工夫，沙船已经扬帆出海，

Following the same route as the first expedition, they sailed by way of Haining and the Qiantang River to the waters outside Hangzhou’s Wangjiang Gate.
照第一次的行程，由海宁经钱塘江到杭州望江门外。


Zhang went ahead to Hangzhou.
小张打前站，先回杭州，

At Hu’s suggestion, he said only that a few hundred shi of rice were to be donated to the authorities.
照胡雪岩的主意，只说有几百石米要捐献官府；

With a handsome gift he then cultivated Battalion Officer Zhang, who commanded at Wangjiang Gate, and arranged how they were to be received.
再用一笔重礼，结交了守望江门的营官张千总，讲好接应的办法，

He came out in a small boat to meet them and report.
然后坐小船迎了上来复命，

His detailed account of conditions in Hangzhou was far from encouraging.
细谈杭州的情形，实在不大高明；

Hu listened, depressed, and remained silent for a long time.
胡雪岩听完，抑郁地久久不语。

As a close relative and an elder, Liu could speak to him with comparative freedom.
既是至亲，而且也算长辈，刘不才说话比较可以没有顾忌；

“Xueyan, are you afraid someone is plotting against you?”
他很坦率地问道∶『雪岩，你是不是在担心有人在暗算你？』

“Do you mean someone might accuse me to Governor-General Zuo?
『你是指有人在左制军那里告我？

That is nothing.
那没有什么，

They cannot harm me that way.”
他们暗算不到我的。』

“What troubles you, then?”
『那末，你是担啥心事呢？』

“How can I be easy?
『怎么不要担心事？

Great difficulties lie ahead, and danger is before our eyes.”
来日大难，眼前可忧！』

The polished phrasing was unlike Hu’s usual speech, making both men more curious.
这八个字说得很雅驯，不象胡雪岩平时的口吻，因而越使得刘不才和小张奇怪。

Liu understood him better than Zhang did.
当然，刘不才对胡雪岩，要比小张了解得多，

He knew what the future difficulties meant: Hu had often spoken of the countless tasks of reconstruction after recovery—caring for the dead, relieving the living, and restoring commerce.
『来日大难，这句话他懂，因为平时听胡雪岩谈过，光复以后，恤死救生，振兴市面善后之事，头绪万端。

But what danger threatened them now?
可是，眼前又有何可忧呢？』

“I never imagined the government troops’ discipline would be little better than the rebels’,” Hu said.
我没有想到，官军的纪律亦不比长毛好多少！『胡雪岩说，』

“What young Zhang has just told us about the city gives real cause for anxiety.
刚才听小张说起城里的情形，着实要担一番心事。

Daylight may be safe enough.
白天总还好，

But once night falls, I fear there will be robbery, arson, rape, and looting.”
只怕一到了夜里，放抢放火，奸淫掳掠都来了！

No wonder he was so troubled.
『怪不得他这样子忧心忡忡，

Whether or not he exaggerated, precautions were plainly necessary.
不管他是不是过甚其词；总不可不作预防。

Zhang, whose home was in the city, was especially alarmed. “Mr Hu!
小张家在城里，格外关切，失声问道∶』胡先生！

What can we do?”
那，怎么办呢『


“There is one way.
『办法是有一个。

But it will be of use only if I can see the man in charge.”
不过要见着「当家人」才有用处。』

Jiang Yili now controlled the whole city.
整个杭州城现在是蒋益澧当家；

Zhang thought for a moment. “May I ask your wishes, Mr Hu?
小张想了一下问道∶『胡先生，我请你老人家的示，

When we enter, shall we see my father first, or go directly to the man in charge?”
进了城是先跟家父见见面呢？还是直接去看杭州的「当家人」？』

“The man in charge, naturally.”
『当然先看「当家人」。』

“Very well.”
『好的！』

Zhang could be decisive too.
小张也很有决断，

“Old Liu, let us divide the work.
『老刘，我们分头办事；

Once we land, please help Battalion Officer Zhang with the unloading.
等到上了岸，卸米的事，请你帮帮张千总的忙。

Everything is disordered just now.
现在秩序很乱，

By helping, I mean directing the labourers.
所谓帮忙，无非指挥指挥工人；

There is no need for you to concern yourself with anything else.”
别的，请你不必插手。』

Liu understood the implication.
刘不才懂得他的言外之意，

He was not to assume responsibility for guarding the grain.
不须负保管粮食之责；

If stragglers or wandering soldiers tried to extort it by force or persuasion, he could leave the matter to the officer.
如果有散兵游勇，强索软要；听凭张千总去处理，大可袖手旁观。

“I understand.
『我知道了。

Let us agree where to meet afterwards.”
我们约定事后见面的地方好了。』

“At my house,” Zhang replied.
『在我舍间。』小张答说，

“I shall ask the officer to have you escorted there.”
『回头我会拜托张千总，派人护送你去。』

Hu opened a small chest containing a serviceable suit of official robes and the hat insignia of the third rank.
于是，胡雪岩打开小箱子，里面是一套半新旧的三品顶戴官服；

By the time he had changed, they had reached the shore.
等他换穿停当，船也就到岸了。


Reached it, that is, as nearly as they could.
虽说到岸，其实还有一段距离，

Laden with rice, the sea junks drew considerable water.
因为沙船装米，吃水很深；

The landing outside Wangjiang Gate had fallen into disrepair, and silt had made the shallows dangerous.
而望江门外的码头失修，近岸淤浅，

Coming too close would risk running aground.
如果沙船靠得太近，会有搁浅之虞。

Fortunately, a generous reward inspired willing service.
好在重赏之下，自有勇夫，

Battalion Officer Zhang had taken considerable pains.
张千总颇为尽心，

He had found a large abandoned house, posted soldiers to clean and guard it as a warehouse, and held small boats ready to transfer the cargo.
不但已找好一所荒废的大房子，派兵打扫看守，备作仓库之用；而且也扣着小船，预备接驳。

Now, on examining the conditions, he changed his plan and ordered the soldiers to wade through the shallows with the loads, which would be quicker and simpler.
此时相度情势，又改了主意，下令士兵在浅河滩涉水负载，更为简捷。

Only one small boat was used, carrying Hu, young Zhang, Liu, and Hu’s attendant Changgui ashore.
小船只用了一只，将胡雪岩、小张、刘不才和胡雪岩的跟班长贵送到岸下；

Once the arrangements had been explained, Hu and Zhang entered the city, accompanied by Changgui with the visiting-card case under his arm.
交代明白，胡、张二人就由挟着拜匣的长贵陪着，先进城了。

At the sight of banners fluttering above the walls, emotion overcame Hu and tears streamed down his face.
望见城头上飘拂的旗帜，胡雪岩感从中来，流涕不止，

He was thinking of Wang.
他是在想王有龄；

How wonderful it would have been if the victorious commander entering the city today were Wang, not Jiang!
如果今天凯旋入城的主帅，不是蒋益澧而是王有龄，那有多好？

People naturally judged heroes by success or failure.
今日之下，自然是以成败论英雄，

But would the victorious men remember how Wang had clung to the doomed city, exhausting not merely his strength and spirit but his very blood and tears?
但打了胜仗的人不知道可会想到，王有龄当年苦守危城，岂仅心力交瘁，直是血与泪俱；

His suffering and exertions had far exceeded theirs.
所吃的苦、所用的力，远比打胜仗的人要多得多？

Hu longed to go first to the place where Wang had died and weep aloud.
这样想着，恨不得一进城先到王有龄殉节之处，放声痛哭一场。

But the people were still in dire distress.
无奈百姓还在水深火热之中，

There was no time to release his own grief and indignation.
实在没有功夫让他去泄痛愤，

He wiped his eyes, straightened his shoulders, and walked on.
只好拭拭眼泪，挺起胸膛往里走！


The guard had changed, and a fourth-rank military officer now commanded the gate.
守城的已经换了班，是个四品都司；

Seeing Hu’s third-rank civil robes—the same rank as Jiang’s—he dared not be discourteous.
一见胡雪岩的服气，三品文官，与蒋益澧相同，不敢怠慢，

He came forward, saluted, and asked his official title.
亲自迎上来行了礼问道∶『大人的官衔是？』

“This is His Honour Hu,” young Zhang explained.
『是胡大人。』小张代为解说，

“He has come urgently from Shanghai to discuss important official business with Provincial Treasurer Jiang.”
『从上海赶来的，有紧要公事跟蒋藩台接头。』

Changgui had already taken a visiting card from the case and handed it over.
这时长贵已经从拜匣里取出一张名帖递了过去；

The officer could not read.
那都司不识字，

He accepted the card, looked at it upside down, and said with an air of comprehension, “So His Honour Hu wishes to see His Honour Jiang!
接过名帖，倒着看了一下，装模作样的说道∶『原来胡大人要见蒋大人！

May I offer an escort?”
请问，要不要护送？』

“Nothing could be better,” Zhang replied.
『能护送再好不过！』小张说道，

“Most importantly, could you find two horses?”
『顶要紧的是，能不能弄两匹马来？』

“There are no horses.
『马可没有。

But His Honour Hu could take a chair.”
不过，胡大人可以坐轿子。』

A sedan-chair firm stood beside the gate, with two empty chairs still available.
城门旁边，就是一家轿行；居然还有两乘空轿子在，

There were naturally no bearers.
轿夫自然不会有，

The officer obligingly offered to press some townspeople into service, but Hu firmly declined.
那都司倒很热心，表示可以抓些百姓来抬轿。可是胡雪岩坚决辞谢——

To ride in a chair at a time like this would be utterly heartless.
这时候还要坐轿子，简直是毫无心肝了。


Without horses, and unwilling to use a chair, they had to depend on their own legs.
没有马，又不肯坐轿，自然还借重自家的一双腿。

The officer’s soldiers escorted them, however, and they passed everywhere without obstruction, reaching the Sun residence at Sanyuanfang, where Jiang had established his headquarters.
不过都司派兵护送，一路通行无阻；很顺利又到了三元坊孙宅，蒋益澧的公馆，

When Hu’s card went in, the central gate was opened wide and Jiang’s staff officer came out to conduct the guest inside.
投帖进去，中门大开；蒋益澧的中军来肃客入内。

Approaching the hall, Hu saw a commander in a yellow riding jacket standing beneath the eaves.
走近大厅，但见滴水檐前站着一个穿了黄马褂的将官，

Taking him for Jiang, he bowed deeply. “Congratulations!
料知便是蒋益澧；胡雪岩兜头长揖∶『恭喜，

Congratulations!”
恭喜！』

He was saluting the victory.
这是贺他得胜，

Jiang returned the bow with clasped hands.
蒋益澧拱手还礼，

“A joy we share!” he replied repeatedly.
连声答道∶『彼此，彼此！』

Young Zhang stepped forwards to make a formal introduction. They entered the hall and sat opposite one another as equals, host and guest, beginning with the customary civilities.
于是小张抢上一步，为双方正式引见∶进入大厅，宾主东西平坐，少不得先有一番寒暄。

Speaking as a member of Zhejiang’s gentry, Hu thanked Jiang.
胡雪岩先以浙江干绅的身分，向蒋益澧道谢；

He then observed that, among the southeastern provinces devastated by war, Hangzhou had suffered most.
然后谈到东南兵燹，杭州受祸最深。

From there he turned to Jiang’s own interests, beginning with a compliment.
接下来便是为蒋益澧打算，而由恭维开始。


Jiang’s courtesy name was Xiangquan, so Hu addressed him respectfully as “Mr Xiang.”
蒋益澧字芗泉，所以胡雪岩之称为『芗翁』；

“With such a great achievement to your credit, your further promotion to governor of Zhejiang must be close at hand.”
他说，『芗翁立这样一场大功，将来更上层楼，巡抚两浙，是指日可待的事。』

“Not necessarily.
『不见得，

I would not dare entertain such a vain hope,” Jiang replied. “Ninth Marshal Zeng has a fine elder brother.
我亦不敢存这个妄想。』蒋益澧说∶『曾九帅有个好哥哥；

Once Jinling falls, he will come to take office, and I shall still have to present myself as his subordinate.”
等金陵一下，走马上任，我还是要拿「手本」见他。』

Zeng Guoquan held the appointment as governor but had never assumed his duties; Zuo was acting in his place.
浙江巡抚是曾国荃，一直未曾到任；现在是由左宗棠兼署。

Jiang understood his own position.
蒋益澧倒有自知之明，

In military distinction and in connections alike, replacing Zeng would be no easy matter.
不管从勋名、关系来说，要想取曾国荃而代之，是件不容易的事。

Hu saw things differently. “Ninth Marshal Zeng is a great general.
但是胡雪岩另有看法∶『曾九帅是大将，

Once Jinling is taken, the court will surely have important work for him elsewhere.
金陵攻了下来，朝廷自然另有重用之处。

I believe his governorship of Zhejiang is merely an appointment held from a distance for the present.
至于浙江巡抚一席，看亦止于目前遥领；

He will not come to take it up.
将来不会到任的。

Do not lose heart, Mr Xiang.”
芗翁，你不要泄气！』

“Oh?”
『噢？』

Jiang leaned forwards involuntarily.
蒋益澧不自觉地将身子往前俯了一下，

“May I ask what makes you think he will not come?”
『倒要请教，何以见得曾九帅将来不会到任？』


“The reasons are plain.
『这道理容易明白，

First, Ninth Marshal Zeng has no previous connection with Zhejiang.
第一，曾九帅跟浙江素无渊源，

He knows neither the place nor its people, which makes the appointment unsuitable.
人地生疏，不大相宜；

Second, his elder brother is modest and most considerate of others.
第二，曾大帅为人谦虚，也最肯替人设想，

Governor-General Zuo has secured Zhejiang; Commander Zeng will never allow his younger brother to share out Zuo’s territory.”
浙江的局面是左大人定下来的，他决不肯让他老弟来分左大人的地盘。』

“Ah!”
『啊，啊！』

Jiang’s spirits rose.
蒋益澧精神一振，

“You see the matter very clearly, Mr Xueyan.”
『雪翁见得很透彻。』

“In my view, the reconstruction of Zhejiang, and especially of the provincial capital, will depend entirely upon you.”
『照我看，将来浙江全省，特别是省城里的善后事宜，要靠芗翁一手主持。』

Hu paused.
胡雪岩停了一下，

Seeing Jiang listen intently, he knew the moment for his real advice had come.
看蒋益澧是聚精会神在倾听的神态，知道进言的时机已到；

Reinforcing his words with a gesture, he spoke earnestly. “The welfare or misery of Hangzhou’s people lies in your hands.
便用手势加强了语气，很恳切地说∶『杭州百姓的祸福，都在芗翁手里，

Every measure of strength preserved now will save a measure of effort later.”
目前多保存一分元气，将来就省一分气力！』

“Quite so!”
『说的是，说的是！』

Jiang rubbed his hands, showing a little anxiety.
蒋益澧搓着手，微显焦灼地，

“Please advise me.
『请雪翁指教；

I shall spare no effort in preserving that strength.”
只要能保存元气，我无有不尽力的！』

“With such words, you truly are the saviour of Hangzhou’s people.”
『芗翁有这样的话，真正是杭州百姓的救星。』

Hu rose and bowed. “On their behalf, I thank you.”
胡雪岩站起来就请了个安∶『我替杭州百姓给芗翁道谢！』

Jiang hastily returned the courtesy. “You honour me too much!”
『真不敢当！』蒋益澧急忙回礼；

Touching his chest, he added, “Tell me, Mr Xueyan: where should we begin in preserving what remains after this calamity?”
同时拍着胸说∶『雪翁，你请说；保存劫后元气，应该从哪里着手？』


“Forgive my frankness.
『请恕我直言，

You may not be fully aware that men from the various camps are still disturbing the inhabitants.”
芗翁只怕未必知道，各营弟兄，还难免有骚扰百姓的情形。』

“Well—” Hu knew that Jiang was in a difficult position.
『这——。』胡雪岩知道他有些为难。

To win battles, commanders inevitably recalled the old saying that generous rewards produced brave men, and promised rewards beforehand.
官军打仗，为求克敌制胜，少不得想到『重赏之下，必有勇夫』这句老古话，预先许下赏赐；

Yet when even military pay and provisions were hard to obtain, where could they find another great sum for bonuses?
但筹饷筹粮，尚且困难，哪里还筹得出一笔巨款可作犒赏之用。

They were consequently generous at other people’s expense.
这就不免慷他人之慨了；

Whether by hint or tacit permission, soldiers might be allowed, for three days after taking a city, to disregard the two military prohibitions against robbery and rape.
或者暗示、或者默许，只要攻下一座城池，三日之内，可以不守两条军法∶抢劫与奸淫。

Jiang might have made such a promise.
蒋益澧可能亦曾有过这样计诺；

To issue a proclamation forbidding these acts now would make him a commander who broke his word and would hamper his ability to lead the troops in future.
这时候要他出告示禁止，变成主将食言，将来就难带兵了。

Hu therefore interrupted quickly. “Mr Xiang, there is more I must explain.”
因此，胡雪岩抢着打断了他的话∶『芗翁，我还有下情上禀。』


“You speak too gravely,” Jiang said.
『言重、言重！』

Fearing further unwelcome words that might embarrass them both, he tried to forestall them.
蒋益澧怕他还有不中听的话说出来，搞得彼此尴尬，所以招呼打在先，

“Your reproach is entirely just.
『雪翁的责备，自是义正辞严。

I can only feel ashamed.”
我惟有惭愧而已。』

He spoke of shame, not of enforcing discipline.
不说整饬军纪，只道惭愧；

Courteous on the surface, the answer quietly rejected the criticism.
这话表面客气，暗中却已表示不受责备。

Hearing his tone, Hu felt still more certain that his own plan was the wiser course.
胡雪岩听他的语气，越觉得自己的打算是比较聪明的做法；

He could also speak more directly.
而且话也不妨得率直些。

“As you know, I am a merchant.
『芗翁知道的，经商人。

In business, we speak of fair exchange.
在商言商，讲究公平交易；

The customary courtesy of giving and receiving rests upon the same principle.
俗语说的礼尚往来，也无非讲究一个公平。

Your men risked their lives to save Hangzhou’s people.
弟兄们拼性命救杭州的百姓，

Their exertions and achievements were great.
劳苦功高，

The court may reward them, but if the locality offers no token of appreciation, it is unfair and does them an injustice.”
朝廷虽有奖赏，地方上没有点意思表示，也就太不公平，太对不起弟兄了。』

Jiang was puzzled.
蒋益澧听他这段话，颇为困惑，

The opening sounded very worldly, the conclusion exceedingly polite.
前面的话，说得很俗气；而后面又说得很客气，

What was the purpose?
到底主旨何在？

He needed to think before answering.
要细想一想，才好答话。

This was an impressive man, but a formidable one.
他心里在想，此人很漂亮，但也很厉害；

Handle him badly and a friend might become an enemy, with considerable consequences.
应付不得法，朋友变成冤家，其中的出入很大，不可不慎。

After reflection, Jiang understood.
于是他细想了一下，终于弄明白了胡雪岩的意思；

He replied modestly, “You praise us too much.
谦虚地答道∶『雪翁太夸奖了。

Clearing out bandits for the people is simply our duty.
为民除寇，份所当为，

What merit can we claim?”
哪里有什么功劳可言？』


“Now it is you who are too modest.
『芗翁这话才真是太客气了。

Since we meet as though we were old friends, I shall speak plainly.” Hu continued with composure. “Our people are famous for their stubborn Hangzhou heads, but they know how to distinguish kindness from injury.
彼此一见如故，我就直言了。』胡雪岩从从容容地说∶『敝处是出了名的所谓「杭铁头」，最知道好歹，

The government troops have served us well and deserve a reward.
宫军有功，理当犒劳。

At present, however, nine homes in ten stand empty, and the rebels have stripped the place bare over the past two years.
不过眼前十室九空，这两年也让长毛搜括净了；

There is really nothing with which to honour the army.
实在没有啥好劳军的。

Fortunately, a number of Hangzhou gentlemen took refuge elsewhere, and I can reach most of them.
好在杭州士绅逃难在外的，还有些人，我也大多可以联络得到。

I have ventured to take the responsibility of raising a hundred thousand taels and placing it in your hands, so that you may thank the men on our behalf.”
如今我斗胆做个主，决定凑十万两银子，送到芗翁这里来，请代为谢谢弟兄们。』

Jiang found it difficult to answer.
这话让蒋益澧很难回答，

Refusal seemed discourteous, acceptance impossible.
颇有却之不恭，受之不可之感。

Hu’s intention was unmistakable: a hundred thousand taels to purchase absolute freedom from molestation.
因为胡雪岩的意思是很显然的，十万两银子买个『秋毫无犯』，

That was his “fair exchange,” his “giving and receiving.”
这就是他所说∶『公平交易』∶『礼尚往来』。

But although a hundred thousand sounded enormous, divided among several tens of thousands of men it would give each only a modest amount.
只是十万两银子听上去是个巨数，几万人一分，所得有限，

Whether it could settle the matter remained very doubtful.
能不能『摆得平』，大成疑问。
