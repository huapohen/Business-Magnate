胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第八章（本章第4部分，共9部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


见他踌躇的神气，胡雪岩自能猜知他的心事，
Hu could guess the reason for Jiang’s hesitation.

若问一句∶『莫非嫌少？』未免太不客气；如果自动增加，又显得讨价还价地小气相。
To ask, “Is it too little?” would be discourteous; to increase the amount unprompted would make him seem petty, as though haggling over a price.

考虑下来，只有侧面再许他一点好处。
After consideration, he could see only one course: offer a further benefit indirectly.

『至于对芗翁的敬意，自然另有筹划——』『不，不！』蒋益澧打断他的话，
“As for a token of respect to yourself, Mr Xiang, separate arrangements will naturally be made—” “No, no!” Jiang interrupted.

『不要把我算在里头。
“Do not include me.

等局势稍为平定了，贵省士绅写京信的时候，能够说一句我蒋某人对得起浙江，就承情不尽了。』
When matters have settled a little and your province’s gentlemen write to their friends in the capital, if they can say that Jiang Yili has done his duty by Zhejiang, I shall be more than grateful.”

『那何消说得？
“That need hardly be said.

芗翁，你对得起浙江，浙江也一定对得起你！』
Mr Xiang, if you do your duty by Zhejiang, Zhejiang will certainly do its duty by you.”

『好，
“Good!

这话痛快！』
That is well spoken.”

蒋益澧毅然决然地说∶『雪翁的厚爱，我就代弟兄们一并致谢了。』接着便喊一声∶『来啊！
Jiang decided. “Then let me thank you for your generosity on behalf of all the men.” He called for an attendant.

请刘大老爷！』
“Ask Mr Liu to come.”

『刘大老爷』举人出身，捐出州县班子；
Mr Liu held the provincial examination degree and had purchased entry to the ranks eligible for prefectural and county appointments.

蒋益澧倚为智囊，也当他是文案委员。
Jiang relied upon him as an adviser and employed him as a drafting officer.

请了他来，是要商议出告示，整饬军纪，严禁骚扰。
He was summoned to discuss a proclamation enforcing military discipline and strictly prohibiting interference with the inhabitants.


这是蒋益澧的事，胡雪岩可以不管；
That was Jiang’s business; Hu need not concern himself with it.

他现在要动脑筋的是，如何实践自己的诺言，有十万两白花花的银子，解交藩库，供蒋益澧分赏弟兄？
His own problem was how to fulfil his promise: how to deliver a hundred thousand taels of shining silver to the provincial treasury for distribution among Jiang’s men.

一想到藩库，胡雪岩心中灵光一闪，仿佛暗夜迷路而发现了灯光一样，
At the thought of the treasury, an inspiration flashed through his mind, like a light seen by someone lost at night.

虽然一闪即灭，但他确信不是自己看花了眼而生的错觉，
Though it vanished at once, he was certain it had not been an illusion.

一定能够找出一条路来。
There must be a way forward.

果然，息心静虑想了一会，
He composed himself and thought quietly for a while.

大致有了成算；
A plan began to take shape.

便等蒋益澧与他的智囊谈得告一段落时，开口问道∶『芗翁的粮台在哪里？』
When Jiang’s discussion with his adviser reached a pause, Hu asked, “Where is your military supply office, Mr Xiang?”

『浙江的总粮台，跟着左大帅在余杭；
“Zhejiang’s main supply office is at Yuhang with Commander Zuo.

我有个小粮台在瓶窑。
I have a smaller one at Pingyao.

喏，』蒋益澧指着小张说，『他也是管粮台的委员。』
He”—Jiang pointed to young Zhang—“is also an officer concerned with supply.”

『那末，藩库呢？』
“And the provincial treasury?”

『藩库？』蒋益澧笑道，
“The treasury?” Jiang laughed.

『藩司衙门都还不知道在不在；
“I do not even know whether the provincial treasurer’s office still stands.

哪里谈得到藩库？』
How can we speak of a treasury?”

『藩库掌一省的收支，
“The treasury controls the revenue and expenditure of the entire province.

顶顶要紧；要尽快恢复起来。
It is of the greatest importance and should be restored as quickly as possible.

藩库的牌子一挂出去，自有解款的人上门。
Once its sign is up, people with money to remit will find their way there.

不然，就好象俗语说，「提着猪头，寻不着庙门。」
Otherwise it is like the saying: carrying a pig’s head for an offering, but unable to find the temple gate.

岂不耽误库收？』
Will that not delay receipts?”


蒋益澧也不知道这时候会有什么人来解款？
Jiang did not know who might have money to remit at such a time.

只觉得胡雪岩的忠告极有道理，藩库应该赶快恢复；
Still, Hu’s advice sounded entirely reasonable: the treasury ought to be restored promptly.

可是该如何恢复，应派什么人管库办事？却是茫无所知。
How to restore it, and whom to put in charge, he had no idea.

于是胡雪岩为他讲解钱庄代理公库的例规与好处。
Hu therefore explained the established practice and advantages of a private bank acting for a public treasury.

阜康从前代理浙江藩库，如今仍愿效力；
Fukang had formerly handled Zhejiang’s provincial treasury and was willing to serve again.

不过以前人欠欠人犹待清理，
But its old debts and credits still required settlement.

为了划清界限起见，他想另立一爿钱庄，叫做『阜丰』。
To keep the old and new business distinct, he proposed to establish another bank, called Fufeng.

『阜丰就是阜康，不过多挂一块招牌。
“Fufeng is Fukang, with an additional signboard.

外面有区分，内部是一样的，
They will be distinct to the outside world, but internally the same.

叫阜丰，叫阜康都可以。
Either name will do.

芗翁！』胡雪岩说，『我这样做法，完全是为了公家；
Mr Xiang, I am doing this entirely for the public service.

阜康收进旧欠，解交阜丰，也就是解交芗翁。
When Fukang collects outstanding debts and transfers the proceeds to Fufeng, that means transferring them to you.

至于以前藩库欠人家的，看情形该付的付，该缓的缓，急公缓私，
As for what the old treasury owed others, we can judge each case: pay what must be paid and postpone what may wait, giving urgent public needs priority over private claims.

岂非大有伸缩的余地？』
Will that not leave us considerable room to manoeuvre?”

『好，好！
“Excellent!”

准定委托雪翁。』蒋益澧大为欣喜，
Jiang was delighted. “I shall certainly entrust it to you.

『阜丰也好，阜康也好，
Fufeng or Fukang, it makes no difference.

我只认雪翁。』
You are the man I rely on.”

『既蒙委任，我一定尽心尽力。』
“Since you honour me with the commission, I shall do my utmost.”

胡雪岩略停一下又说∶『应该解缴的十万银子，我去筹划；看目前在杭州能凑多少现银？
Hu paused briefly. “I shall arrange the hundred thousand taels I have promised, seeing how much ready silver can be raised in Hangzhou.

不足之数归我垫；
I shall advance the shortfall myself.

为了省事，我想划一笔帐；
For convenience, I propose an adjustment between the accounts.

这一来粮台、藩库彼此方便。』
That will suit both the supply office and the treasury.”

『这，这笔帐怎么划法？』
“How would the accounts be adjusted?”


是这样，譬如说现在能凑出一半现银，
“Suppose we can raise half the amount in silver here.

我就先解了上来；
I shall deliver that first.

另外一半，我打一张票子交到粮台，随时可以在我上海的阜丰兑现。
For the other half, I shall give the supply office a note redeemable at any time at my Fufeng bank in Shanghai.

倘或交通不便，一时不能去提现，那也不要紧，
If communications are difficult and the money cannot immediately be collected, that need not matter.

阜丰代理藩库，一切代垫，就等于缴了现银；
As Fufeng will be acting for the provincial treasury, any expenditure it advances on the treasury’s behalf will count as cash paid against the note.

藩库跟粮台划一笔帐就可以了。
The treasury and the supply office need only make the corresponding adjustment.

垫多少扣多少；按月结帐。
Each advance will be deducted, with the accounts settled monthly.”

『听他说得头头是道，
Hu made it sound entirely orderly.

蒋益澧只觉得振振有词，到底这笔帐怎么算，还得要细想一想，才能明白。
Jiang found the argument persuasive, but needed to think carefully before he understood how the entries would work.

想是想明白了，却有疑问∶『藩库的收入呢？
When he did, a question remained. “What about the treasury’s receipts?

是不是先还你的垫款？』
Will those first repay your advances?”

这，怎么可以？
“How could I do that?”

『胡雪岩的身子蓦然往后一仰，靠在椅背上，不断摇头；似乎觉得他所问的这句话，太出乎常情似的。
Hu suddenly leaned back against his chair, shaking his head repeatedly, as though the very suggestion were beyond ordinary reason.

光是这一个动作，就使得蒋益澧死心塌地了。
That gesture alone won Jiang’s complete confidence.

他觉得胡雪岩不但诚实，而且心好，真能拿别人的利害当自己的祸福。
He felt that Hu was not merely honest but kind-hearted, genuinely able to treat another man’s interests as his own.

不过太好了反不易使人相信；
Yet the offer seemed almost too generous to believe.

他深信是自己有所误会，还是问清楚的好。
Jiang was sure he must have misunderstood and thought it best to clarify.

『雪翁，』他很谨慎地措词，『你的意思是，在你开给粮台的银票数目之内，你替藩库代垫；就算是你陆续兑现。
“Mr Xueyan,” he asked cautiously, “do you mean that, up to the amount of the note you give the supply office, your advances for the treasury will count as successive payments redeeming it?

至于藩库的收入，你还是照缴。是不是这话？』
Meanwhile, you will still remit the treasury’s receipts in full?”


『是！就是这话。』胡雪岩紧接着说，
“Yes, exactly.” Hu continued at once.

『哪怕划帐已经清楚了，阜丰既然代理浙江藩库，当然要顾浙江藩司的面子，
“Even after that account has been cleared, Fufeng, as agent for Zhejiang’s treasury, must uphold the credit of Zhejiang’s provincial treasurer.

还是照垫不误。』
We shall continue to make advances.”

这一下，蒋益澧不但倾倒，简直有些感激了，
Jiang was now not merely convinced but grateful.

拱拱手说∶『一切仰仗雪翁，
Clasping his hands, he said, “We shall depend upon you in everything.

就请宝号代理藩库；
Your bank shall act for the treasury.

要不要备公事给老兄？』
Would you like an official letter of appointment?”

『芗翁是朝廷的监司大员，
“You are a high provincial official appointed by the court.

说出一句话，自然算数；
Your word is sufficient.

有没有公事，在我都是无所谓的。
To me, the presence or absence of a document makes no difference.

不过为了取信于人，阜丰代理藩库，要请一张告示。』
But to establish confidence with others, I should like a proclamation announcing Fufeng’s appointment.”

『那方便得很！
“Easily done.

我马上叫他们办。』
I shall have it prepared at once.”

『我也马上叫他们连夜预备；明天就拿告示贴出去。
“I too shall have preparations made overnight, and tomorrow we can post the notices.

不过，』胡雪岩略略放低了声音，『什么款该付，什么款不该付，实在不该付，阜丰听命而行。
But”—Hu lowered his voice—“as to which payments should be made, which should wait, and which really must not be made, Fufeng will obey your instructions.

请芗翁给个暗号，以便遵循。』
Please give us a private sign to guide us.”

『给个暗号？』
“A private sign?”

蒋益澧搔搔头，显得很为难似的。
Jiang scratched his head, looking perplexed.

这倒是小张比他内行了，
Young Zhang understood this better than he did.

『大人！』
“Your Honour!” he said.


他是『做此官，行此礼』，将『大人』二字叫得非常自然；
Now that he held office, Zhang performed its courtesies, and “Your Honour” came very naturally.

等蒋益澧转脸相看时，他才又往下说∶『做当家人很难，
When Jiang turned towards him, he continued. “It is difficult to be the man in charge.

有时候要粮与饷，明知道不能给，却又不便驳，
Sometimes men ask for provisions or pay, and although you know it cannot be given, you cannot conveniently refuse.

只好批示照发；粮台上也当然遵办。
You have to endorse the request for payment, and the supply office must of course comply.

但实在无银无饷，就只好婉言情商。
Yet if there is truly neither money nor food, it must find tactful ways of discussing the matter.

胡观察的意思，就是怕大人为难，
Intendant Hu means to spare you embarrassment.

先约定暗号，知道了大人的意思，就好想办法敷衍了。』
With a signal agreed beforehand, he will know your wishes and can find a way to put the applicants off.”

『啊，啊！』
“Ah!”

蒋益澧恍然大悟，『我懂了。
Jiang understood.

我一直就为这件事伤脑筋。
“This has troubled me continually.

都是出生入死的老弟兄，何况是欠了他们的饷；
These are old comrades who have faced death with me, and their pay is already in arrears.

你说，拿了「印领」来叫我批，我好不批照发吗？
When they bring sealed claims for my endorsement, how can I refuse to approve them?

批归批，粮台上受得了、受不了，又是另外一回事。
But whether the supply office can bear the expenditure is another matter.

结果呢，往往该给的没有给；不该给的，倒领了去了。
Often those who should be paid receive nothing, while those who should not receive anything get their money.

粮台不知有多少回跟我诉苦，甚至跳脚。我亦无可奈何。
The supply office has complained to me countless times, sometimes stamping in exasperation, and I have been helpless.

现在有这样一个「好人」我做，「坏人」别人去做的办法，那是太好了。
A method that lets me play the good man while someone else plays the bad one—that is excellent.

该用什么暗号，请雪翁吩咐。』
Tell me what signal to use.”

『不敢当！』胡雪岩答道，
“You are too kind,” Hu replied.

『暗号要常常变换，才不会让人识透。
“The signal must change frequently to prevent people from discovering it.

现在我先定个简单的办法，
For the moment, let us begin simply.

芗翁具衔只批一个「澧」字，阜丰全数照付；
If you sign with the single character ‘Li,’ Fufeng will pay in full.

写台甫「益澧」二字，付一半；
If you write the two characters of your given name, ‘Yili,’ it will pay half.

若是尊姓大名一起写在上头，就是「不准」的意思，阜丰自会想办法塘塞。』
If you sign your full name, surname included, that will mean refusal, and Fufeng will find an excuse.”

『那太好了！』
“Excellent!”

蒋益澧拍着手说∶『「听君一席话，胜做十年官。」』
Jiang clapped his hands. “One conversation with you is worth ten years in office!”


宾主相视大笑，真有莫逆于心之感。
Host and guest looked at one another and laughed, feeling a complete understanding between them.

文情到此，胡雪岩觉得有些事，大可不必保留了；
With relations so warm, Hu thought there were matters he need no longer withhold.

因而向小张使个眼色，只轻轻说了一个字∶『米！』然后微一努嘴。
He glanced at young Zhang, whispered the single word “Rice,” and made a slight motion with his lips.

小张也是玲珑剔透的一颗心，察言辨色，完全领会，
Zhang was quick to read both words and expression.

斜欠着身子，当即开口向蒋益澧说道∶『有件事要跟大人回禀，
Leaning forwards, he addressed Jiang. “There is something I must report.

那几百石米，已经请张千总跟胡观察的令亲在起卸了。
Battalion Officer Zhang and Intendant Hu’s relative are unloading the few hundred shi of rice.

暂时存仓，听候支用。
It will be stored temporarily pending instructions.

这几百石米，我先前未说来源；
I did not previously explain where it came from.

如今应该说明了，就是胡观察运来的。数目远不止这些。』
I should now say that Intendant Hu brought it, and the quantity is far greater.”

『喔，有多少？』蒋益澧异常关切地说。
“How much?” Jiang asked with intense interest.

『总有上万石。』胡雪岩说道∶『这批米，我是专为接济官军与杭州百姓的。
“Ten thousand shi or more,” Hu replied. “I brought it expressly to relieve the government troops and the people of Hangzhou.

照道理说，应该解缴芗翁，才是正办。
Strictly speaking, the proper course would be to deliver it to you.

不过，我也有些苦衷；
But I have a difficulty of my own.

好不好请芗翁赏我一个面子，这批米算是暂时责成我保管；
Could you do me the favour of leaving it temporarily in my custody?

等我见了左制军，横竖还是要交给芗翁来作主公派的。
After I have seen Governor-General Zuo, it will in any case be placed at your disposal for public distribution.

只不过日子晚一两天而已。』
The delay will be only a day or two.”

蒋益澧大出意外。
Jiang was astonished.

军兴以外，特别是浙江，饿死人不足为奇；
Since the war began, especially in Zhejiang, death by starvation had become commonplace.

如今忽有一万石米出现，真如从天而降，
The sudden appearance of ten thousand shi of rice was like a gift from Heaven.

怎不令人惊喜交集。
How could he fail to be amazed and delighted?


『雪翁你这一万石米，岂止雪中送炭？
“Mr Xueyan, your rice is more than charcoal brought through the snow.

简直是大旱甘霖！
It is rain in a great drought!

这样，我一面派兵保护，就请张委员从中联络襄助；
I shall send soldiers to guard it and have Officer Zhang assist with the arrangements.

一面我派妥当的人，送老兄到余杭去见左大帅。
At the same time, I shall send a reliable man to escort you to Yuhang to see Commander Zuo.

不过，我希望老兄速去速回，
But I hope you will return quickly.

这里还有多少大事，要请老兄帮忙。』
There are so many important matters here on which we need your help.”

『是！我尽快赶回来。』
“I shall come back as quickly as I can.”

『那末，老兄预备什么时候动身？
“When do you intend to leave?

今天晚上总来不及了吧？』
It must be too late tonight.”

『是的！
“Yes.

明天一早动身。』
Early tomorrow morning.”

蒋益澧点点头，随即又找中军，又找文案；将该为胡雪岩做的事，——分派停当。
Jiang nodded, summoned his staff and drafting officers, and assigned every task required for Hu.

护送他到余杭的军官，派的是一名都司，姓何，是蒋益澧的表侄；也是他的心腹。
The escort to Yuhang was an officer named He, a nephew through Jiang’s cousin and a trusted subordinate.

于是胡雪岩殷殷向何都司道谢，很敷衍了一番，约定第二天一早在小张家相会，陪同出发。
Hu thanked Officer He warmly, cultivated his goodwill, and arranged to meet him early the next morning at Zhang’s house for their departure.


到了张家；张秀才对胡雪岩自然有一番尽释前嫌、推心置腹的话说。
At the Zhang house, the father naturally spoke to Hu with complete openness, declaring all former grievances forgotten.

只是奉如上宾，只有在礼貌上尽心，没有什么酒食款待。
He honoured him as a distinguished guest, though circumstances allowed courtesy rather than elaborate food and wine.

而胡雪岩亦根本无心饮食，
Hu had no appetite for either.

草草果腹以后，趁这一夜功夫，还有许多大事要交待；
After a hasty meal sufficient to fill his stomach, he had many important instructions to give during the one night available.

苦恨人手不足，只好拿小张也当作心腹了。
Bitterly short of help, he had to treat young Zhang as a trusted assistant too.

胡雪岩没有功夫跟他们从容研商；只是直截了当地提出要求。
There was no time for leisurely discussion; he stated his requirements directly.

『第一件大事，请小张费心跟你老太爷商量，能找到几位地方上提得起的人物，
“First, young Zhang, please consult your father and find a few respected local men.

大家谈一谈，想法子凑现银给蒋方伯送了去，作为我阜丰暂借。
Talk to them and see how much silver can be raised to deliver to Provincial Treasurer Jiang as a temporary loan to Fufeng.

要请大家明白，这是救地方，也是救自己；
Make them understand that this is to save the locality and themselves.

十万银子的责任都在我一个人身上，
Responsibility for the hundred thousand taels rests entirely upon me.

将来大家肯分担最好，
If they are willing to share it later, so much the better.

不然，也就是我一个人认了。
If not, I shall bear it alone.

不过，此刻没有办法从上海调款子过来，要请大家帮我的忙。』
But I cannot bring the funds from Shanghai at this moment, and must ask for their help.”

『好的。』小张连连点头，
Zhang nodded repeatedly.

『这件事交给我们父子好了。
“Leave that to my father and me.

胡先生仁至义尽，
You have done everything a man could do for us, Mr Hu.

大家感激得很；
Everyone is grateful.

只要有现银，一定肯借出来的。』
Anyone who has ready silver will certainly be willing to lend it.”

『其次，阜康马上要复业，阜丰的牌子要挂出去。
“Next, Fukang must reopen at once, and Fufeng’s sign must go up.

这件事我想请三爷主内，小张主外。』
I should like Third Master Liu to handle the internal arrangements and you to deal with matters outside.”

胡雪岩看着刘不才说，
Hu turned to Liu.

『先说内部，第一看看阜康原来的房子怎么样？
“First, inspect Fukang’s old premises.

如果能用，马上找人收拾，
If they can be used, have them cleaned immediately.

再写两张梅红笺，一张是「阜康不日复业」；一张是「阜丰代理藩库」，
Write two notices on plum-red paper: ‘Fukang will reopen shortly,’ and ‘Fufeng acts for the provincial treasury.’

立刻贴了出去。』
Post them at once.”

『藩司衙门的告示呢？』
“What about the treasurer’s proclamation?”


『到复业那天再贴。』
“Post that on the day we reopen.

胡雪岩又说，『第二，准备一两千现银；
Next, prepare one or two thousand taels in cash.

顶要紧的是，弄几十袋米摆在那里。
Most importantly, get several dozen sacks of rice into the premises.

然后贴出一张红纸∶』阜康旧友，即请回店。
Then put up a red notice: ‘All former Fukang staff are requested to return immediately.’

「来了以后，每人先发十两银子五斗米。
As each man arrives, give him ten taels and five dou of rice.

我们这台戏，就可以唱起来了。
Then our performance can begin.”

『』那末，『小张抢着说道，』
Young Zhang hurried to speak.

胡先生，我有句话声明在先，
“Mr Hu, I must make one thing clear.

您老看得起我，汤里来，火里去，惟命是从。
Since you think well of me, I would go through boiling water and fire at your command.

不过，我也要估计估计我自己的力量，
But I must judge my own abilities.

钱庄我是外行；功夫又怕抽不出来，
I know nothing of banking, and I fear I may not have enough time.

不要误了胡先生的大事。
I must not delay your important business.

那时候胡先生不肯责备我，我自己也交代不过去。
Even if you were too kind to reproach me, I could not forgive myself.”

『』不要紧。
“Never mind.

我晓得你很忙，
I know you are busy.

只请你量力而为。
Do what you can.”

『胡雪岩放低了声音说，』
Hu lowered his voice.

我为什么要代理藩库？
“Why do I want to act for the treasury?

为的是要做牌子。
To establish the name.

阜康是金字招牌，固然不错；可是只有老杭州才晓得。
Fukang has an excellent reputation, certainly, but only old residents of Hangzhou know it.

现在我要吸收一批新的存户，非要另外想个号召的办法不可。
I now want to attract new depositors, and I need another way of appealing to them.

代理藩库，就是最好的号召，
Acting for the treasury is the best possible recommendation.

浙江全省的公款，都信托得过我，还有啥靠不住的？
If all Zhejiang’s public funds can be entrusted to me, what doubt can remain about my reliability?

只要那批新存户有这样一个想法，阜丰的存款就会源源不绝而来；
Once the new depositors think that way, money will flow steadily into Fufeng.

应该解蒋方伯的犒赏银两和代理藩库要垫的款子，就都有了。
Then we shall have both the reward money promised to Jiang and the funds needed for advances on the treasury’s behalf.”

『看着事情都交代妥当了，刘不才有句话要跟胡雪岩私下谈；
With the instructions apparently settled, Liu had something private to say.

使个眼色，将他拉到一边，低声说道∶』你跟蒋芗泉搞得很好，没有用；
He signalled to Hu, drew him aside, and spoke quietly. “Getting on well with Jiang Xiangquan will not help.

我今听到一个消息，颇为可靠，
I heard something today from a fairly reliable source.

左制军要跟你算帐，
Governor-General Zuo intends to call you to account.

已经发话下来了，
He has already spoken of it.

弄得不好，会指名严参。
If matters go badly, he may name you in a severe memorial of impeachment.”

『』你不要担心！
“Do not worry.”

『胡雪岩夷然不以为意，』
Hu remained wholly untroubled.

我亦没有啥算不算清的帐。
“There is no account of mine that cannot be explained.

外面的话听不得。『
You cannot believe all the talk outside.”


刘不才见他是极有把握的样子，也就放心了。
Seeing his confidence, Liu felt reassured.

小张却还有话问。
Young Zhang still had a question.

『胡先生的算计真好。
“Your calculation is excellent, Mr Hu.

不过，说了半天，到底是怎样的新存户呢？』
But after all this, who exactly are these new depositors?”

『长毛！』胡雪岩说，
“The rebels,” Hu answered.

『长毛投降了；这两年搜括的银子带不走，
“Once they surrender, they cannot carry away all the silver they have squeezed out of people these two years.

非要找个地方去存不可！』
They must find somewhere to deposit it.”

胡雪岩所要吸收的新存户，竟是长毛！
The new customers Hu meant to attract were the rebels themselves!

小张和刘不才都觉得是做梦亦想不到的事；
Neither Zhang nor Liu could have dreamed of such an idea.

同时亦都觉得他的想法超人，但麻烦亦可能很多。
Both thought his conception remarkable, but both also foresaw considerable trouble.

那种目瞪口呆的带些困惑的表情，是说明了他们内心有些什么疑问，
Their astonished and puzzled expressions told Hu what questions lay behind them.

胡雪岩完全了解；
He understood perfectly.

但是，这时候不是从容辩理的时候，所以他只能用比较武断的态度∶『事情决不会错！
But there was no time to debate at leisure, and he could only adopt a somewhat peremptory tone. “The plan is sound.

你们两位尽管照我的活去动脑筋。
Both of you should think along the lines I have laid down.

动啥脑筋，
Think about what?

就是怎么样让他们死心塌地拿私蓄存到阜丰来？
How to make them fully confident in entrusting their private savings to Fufeng.

两位明白了吧？』
Do you understand?”

『我明白。
“I understand.

不过——。』刘不才没有再说下去。
But—” Liu stopped.

『我也明白。杭州的情形我比较熟；找几个人去拉这些存户，一定不会空手而回。不过，在拉这些客户以前，人家一定要问，钱存到阜丰会不会泡汤？这话我该怎么说？』小张这样问说。
“So do I,” Zhang said. “I know conditions in Hangzhou better. If I find a few people to solicit these deposits, they certainly will not return empty-handed. But before depositing, the men will ask whether their money will be lost at Fufeng. How should I answer?”


『你告诉他∶决不会泡汤。
“Tell them it will not be lost.

不过朝廷的王法，也是要紧的，
But the court’s law must also be respected.

如果他自己觉得这笔存款可能有一天会让官方查扣，那就请他自己考虑。』
If a man himself thinks his deposit may one day be seized by the authorities, he must consider that for himself.”

胡雪岩停一下又说∶『总而言之一句话∶通融方便可以；违犯法条不可以。
Hu paused, then continued, “In a word, we can offer convenience and accommodation; we cannot break the law.

户头我们不必强求，
There is no need to force anyone to open an account.

我们要做气派，做信用。
We must establish our standing and our credit.

信用有了；哪怕连存折不给人家；只凭一句话，照样会有人上门。』
Once we have credit, people will come even without a passbook, trusting our word alone.”

刘不才和小张都觉得他的话一时还想不透；
Neither Liu nor Zhang could immediately fathom this.

好象有点前后不符。
It seemed somewhat inconsistent.

不过此刻无法细问；而且也不是很急的事，无须在这时候追根究底去辨清楚。
But they could not question him closely now, and the matter was not so urgent that its every implication must be settled that night.

因此，两人对看了一眼，取得默契；决定稍后再谈。
They exchanged a glance, reached a silent understanding, and decided to discuss it later.


『做事容易做人难！』
“Doing the work is easy; conducting oneself is hard.”

胡雪岩在片刻沉默以后，突如其来地以这么一句牢骚之语发端，作了很重要的一个揭示；也是一个警告∶『从今天起，我们有许多很辛苦，不过也很划算的事要做；
After a brief silence, Hu unexpectedly began with this complaint, leading into an important explanation—and a warning. “From today, we have much hard work ahead, but it will be worthwhile.

做起来顺利不顺利，全看我们做人怎么样？
Whether it proceeds smoothly depends entirely on how we conduct ourselves.

小张，你倒说说看，现在做人要怎么样做？』
Young Zhang, tell me: how should we behave now?”

小张想了一会，微微笑道，
Zhang considered, then smiled slightly.

『做人无非讲个信义。
“Conduct comes down to good faith and loyalty.

现在既然是帮左制军，就要咬定牙关帮到底。』
Since we are helping Governor-General Zuo, we should clench our teeth and help him to the end.”

『我们现在帮左制军，既然打算帮忙到底，就要堂堂正正站出来。不过这一下得罪的人会很多。』刘不才说。
“If we intend to help him through to the end, we must stand openly on his side,” Liu added. “But that will offend a great many people.”

『面面讨好，面面不讨好！
“Try to please everyone and you please no one,” Hu replied.

惟有摸摸胸口，
“We need only examine our hearts.

如果觉得对得起朝廷，对得起百姓，问心无愧，哪就什么都不必怕。
If we can say we have done our duty by the court and the people, with a clear conscience, there is nothing to fear.

时候不早了，
It is late.

上床吧！』
Let us go to bed.”

这一夜大家都睡不着；
None of them slept well that night.

因为可想的事太多。除此以外，更多的是情绪上的激动。
There was too much to think about, and still more emotional excitement.

上海、杭州都已拿下来，金陵之围的收缘结果，也就不远了。
Shanghai and Hangzhou had both been secured; the conclusion of the siege of Jinling could not be far away.

那时是怎样的一种局面？
What would the situation be then?

散兵游勇该怎么料理，
How were the scattered and unattached soldiers to be dealt with?

遣散还是留用，
Should they be discharged or retained?

处处都是疑问，实在令人困惑之至！
Everywhere were questions, perplexing beyond measure.
