胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第八章（本章第5部分，共9部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


Suddenly Hu Xueyan heard someone outside the wall striking a gong and wooden clapper.
忽然，胡雪岩发觉墙外有人在敲锣打梆子，

A watchman was making his rounds.
这是在打更。

The long-besieged city had only just been recovered; everywhere still bore the confusion of war, yet a night watchman was abroad.
久困之城，刚刚光复，一切还都是兵荒马乱的景象，居然而有巡夜的更夫；

As the “tok, tok, clang; tok, tok, clang” drew nearer, Hu felt the joy and gratitude of a man hearing footsteps in an empty valley.
听着那自远而近『笃、笃、镗；笃、笃、镗』的梆锣之声，胡雪岩有着空谷足音的喜悦的感激。

His mood changed.
而心境也就变过了，

Everything before him vanished from his mind, and he remembered his boyhood: snug beneath his quilt on a cold night, hearing a distant cry borne on the northwest wind—“In this bitter winter weather, take care with fire and candles!”
眼前的一切都抛在九霄云外；回忆着少年时候，寒夜拥衾，遥听由西北风中传来的『寒冬腊月，火烛小心！』的吆喝，

How incomparably peaceful it had felt.
真有无比恬适之感。

That was the sound of settled times, and now he heard it again.
那是太平时世的声音。如今又听到了！

Suddenly invigorated, he could no longer remain in bed.
胡雪岩陡觉精神一振，再也无法留在床上。

All three men slept in the same room.
三个人是睡一房，

He rose quietly, unwilling to disturb Liu Bucai and young Zhang, but Zhang had already noticed.
他怕惊扰了刘不才和小张。悄悄下地；可是小张已经发觉了。

“What are you doing, Mr Hu?”
『胡先生，你要作啥？』

“Have you not fallen asleep?”
『你没有睡着？』

“No.
『没有。』

Have you?”
小张问道∶『胡先生呢？』

“Neither have I.”
『我也没有。』

“We are all alike,” Liu said from behind his bed curtains.
『彼此一样。』刘不才在帐子中接口，

“I have been listening all along.
『我一直在听，

There is quite a stir outside.
外面倒不安静；

Treasurer Jiang has kept his word; his efforts to restrain the troops are already taking effect.”
蒋藩司言而有信，约束部下，已经有效验了。』

“Mr Hu has earned merit in Heaven’s hidden account.”
『这是胡先生积的阴德。』

Young Zhang, suddenly inspired too, sprang from bed.
小张也突然受了鼓舞，一跃下床，

“There is more to do these next two days than we can finish.
『这两天的事情做不完，

Who has time to sleep?”
哪里有睡觉的功夫？』

As soon as they rose, a light appeared in the Zhang family’s kitchen.
等他们一起床，张家的厨房里也就有灯光了。

They washed and drank tea.
洗完脸，先喝茶，

Zhang expected Hu to return to their unfinished business, but he asked instead, quite at his ease, “Did you hear the watchman’s clapper just now?”
小张以为胡雪岩会谈未曾谈完的正事，而他却好整以暇地问道∶『刚才你们听到打更的梆子没有？』

“Yes.
『听到。』

Everything in Hangzhou has changed except Old Zhou the watchman.
小张答道∶『杭州城什么都变过了，只有这个更夫老周没有变；

He has made his rounds every night, without missing a single one.”
每夜打更，从没有断过一天。』

Hu’s expression grew solemn.
胡雪岩肃然动容，

“Remarkable!
『难得！

Truly remarkable!
真难得！』

How old is he?”
他问，『这老周多大年纪？』


“Over sixty.
『六十多岁了。

He was still strong, though I do not know how he is now.”
身子倒还健旺；不过，现在不晓得怎么样了。』

“He has escaped starvation and still makes his rounds every day.
『他没有饿死，而且每天能打更，

He must have an unusually blessed constitution,” Liu observed. “A pity he is only a watchman.”
看来这个人的禀赋，倒是得天独厚。可惜，』刘不才说，『只是打更！』

“Third Master, that is not the way to look at it.
『三爷，话不是这么说。

There are many tasks in the world that require no special talent.
世界上有许多事，本来是用不着才干的，

Anyone can do them.
人人能做；

The question is whether you are willing to do them, and whether you take them seriously.
只看你是不是肯做，是不是一本正经去做？

A man who does that is remarkable.”
能够这样，就是个了不起的人。』

Hu turned to Zhang.
胡雪岩说，

“Do something for me: ask Old Zhou whether he would like to change his occupation.”
『小张，我托你，问问那老周看，愿意不愿意改行？』

“Change his occupation?
『改行？』小张问道，

Do you mean to advance him, Mr Hu?”
『胡先生，你是不是要提拔他？』

“Yes.
『是啊！

Or you might say I mean to make use of his strengths.
我要提拔他；也可以说是借重他。

We are short of hands.
现在我们人手不够，

We cannot let a man so faithful to his duty pass us by.
象这种尽忠职守的人，不可以放过。

I should like him to help us.”
我打算邀他来帮忙。』

“I am sure he would be willing.
『我想他一定肯的。

I only fear there is little he can do.”
就怕他做不来啥。』

“I shall put him in charge of a warehouse.
『我派他管仓库。

If he cannot manage, I shall give him someone to help.
他做不来，再派人帮他的忙；

He need only inspect it at the proper hours, as he makes his rounds.”
只要他象打更那样，那时候去巡查就是。』

At this point, the Zhangs’ manservant laid the table for breakfast.
说到这里，张家的男佣来摆桌子开早饭。

It was only leftover rice boiled into a pot of thin porridge, with a single dish of salted greens.
只不过拿剩下的饭煮一锅饭泡粥；佐粥的只有一样咸菜，

But the hungry are easily fed, and after their half-night’s rest the men’s appetites were keen.
可是『饥者易为食』，尤其是在半夜休息以后，胃口大开，

Everything tasted unusually good.
吃得格外香甜。

“I have not eaten anything so delicious in years!” Hu declared contentedly.
『我多少年没有吃过这样好吃的东西了！』胡雪岩很满意地说，

“Third Master Liu was right: ‘If it serves the purpose, it is good.’
『刘三爷说得不错，「用得着就好」！

At a time like this, rice in hot water and salted greens are more precious than every delicacy of land and sea.”
泡饭咸菜，今日之下比山珍海味还要贵重。』

Young Zhang gained another deep insight.
这使得小张又深有领悟，

The art of employing people was not bound by a single pattern.
用人之道，不拘一格；

Its secret was to adapt to time and place.
能因时因地制宜，就是用人的诀窍。

He nodded thoughtfully, knowing now where to look for people Hu could use.
他深深点头，知道从什么地方去为胡雪岩物色人才了。


Officer He arrived at dawn with two mounted orderlies and an extra horse. The animal had a distinguished history: a gift from the Mongol prince Sengge Rinchen, it had been carefully tended on Jiang Yili’s orders as a mount reserved for Zuo Zongtang.
何都司是天亮来到张家的，带来两个马弁；另外带了一匹马来∶『提起此马来头大』，是蒙古亲王僧格林沁所送，蒋益澧派人细心喂养，专为左宗棠预备的坐骑，

Now it was being lent specially to Hu.
现在特借给胡雪岩乘用。

He also brought news.
何都司同时也带来了一个消息，

The Longhairs in Yuhang had abandoned that city the previous day and fled towards Huzhou.
余杭城内的长毛，亦在昨天弃城向湖州一带逃去。

Zuo was pursuing them in person and was now encamped before Anxi Pass, north of Pingyao.
左宗棠亲自领兵追剿；如今是在瓶窑以北的安溪关前驻扎。

To visit him meant risking arrows and blades.
要去看他，得冒锋镝之危，

What did Hu wish to do?
问胡雪岩的意思如何？

“Life and death are decreed.
『死生有命，

If the Commander-in-Chief can go there, so can I.
左大帅能去，我当然也能去。

There is no need for fear.”
用不着怕！』

“But it is a long journey.
『不过，路很远，

We cannot arrive in a day, and there is nowhere to lodge along the way.
一天赶不到，中途没有住宿的地方，

That is another difficulty.”
也很麻烦。』

“We shall go as fast as we can.
『尽力赶！

If we cannot arrive, there is nothing to be done.
赶不到也没有办法；

With you beside me, I feel quite at ease.”
好在有你老兄在，我放心得很。』

It was merely a casual reply, but He heard it as a deeply sincere expression of trust.
这本是随口一句对答之词，而在何都司听来，是极其恳切的信任。

He therefore took particular care over the arrangements.
因而很用心地为他筹划，

After some thought, he asked, “Your Excellency, can you ride at speed?”
好一会方始问道∶『胡大人，你能不能骑快马？』

“I can manage.”
『勉强可以。』

“And your steward?”
『贵管家呢？』

“I am afraid he cannot.”
『他恐怕不行。』

“Then there is no need to bring him.
『那就不必带贵管家一起走了。

We have four men here; let them attend to anything you require.” He added, “We can use the relay system, changing horses.
现成四个弟兄在这里，有什么差遣，尽管让他们去做。』何都司又说，『我们可以用骚递的办法，换马走；

That will be faster.”
反而来得快。』

An urgent dispatch travelled by changing horses at each post.
紧急骚递的办法是到一站换一匹马；

Since each animal covered only one stage, it could be driven at full speed; this was much faster than riding the same horse all the way.
由于一匹马只走一站路，不妨尽全力驰驱，因而比一匹马到底要快得多。

Fine as the prince’s mount was, it could serve for only one stage, and if it were lost in an exchange it would be troublesome to recover.
僧王的这匹名驹虽好，也只得走一站，换马时如果错失了找不回来，反是个麻烦，

Hu therefore suggested finding another.
因此胡雪岩表示另外找一匹马。

“Easy enough.
『这容易，

We shall exchange it at the stables before we leave.”
我们先到马号去换就是。』


Hu took leave of the Zhang household, saying that he would certainly return on the morning of the third day.
于是胡雪岩辞别张家，临走时交代，第三天早晨一定赶回来。

He and Officer He first exchanged the horse at the provincial treasurer’s headquarters, then rode out through Wulin Gate and galloped to Gongchen Bridge.
然后与何都司同行，先到藩司行台的马号里换了马，出武林门，疾驰到拱宸桥；

There He found a military camp he knew, obtained fresh, good horses, and set off northwest.
何都司找着相熟的军营，换了好马，再往西北方向行进。

There were naturally checkpoints, delays and moments of danger, but they came safely through them all.
一路当然有盘查、有阻碍、也有惊险，但都安然而返。

At three in the afternoon, they reached Pingyao and at last stopped for food and rest.
下午三点钟到了瓶窑，方始打尖休息，

Enquiries placed Zuo at Anxi Pass, eighteen li farther north.
同时探听左宗棠的行踪∶是在往北十八里外的安溪关。

“It is a mountain road, and a difficult one,” He urged earnestly.
『这是条山路，很不好走。』何都司恳切相劝。

“Your Excellency, to speak frankly, you are a southerner.
『胡大人，我说实话，你老是南边人，

As the saying goes, southerners sail boats; northerners ride horses.
「南人行船，北人骑马」。

You do not ride very well.
你的马骑得不怎么好。

For safety’s sake, we should rest a night before continuing.
为求稳当，还是歇一夜再走。

What do you think?”
你看怎么样？』

Hu reflected that he knew neither the place nor its people and could not insist on having his way.
胡雪岩心想，人地生疏，勉强不得；

Even if they reached Anxi that night, he could not see Zuo.
就算赶到安溪，当夜也无法谒见左宗棠，

He agreed, with one condition: “We must leave at first light tomorrow.”
因而点头同意，不过提出要求∶『明天天一亮就要走。』

“Of course.
『当然。

I shall not delay your business.”
不会耽误你老的功夫。』

With that settled, they could rest at leisure.
既然如此，不妨从容休息。

Imperial troops had long been stationed at Pingyao, and its market was fairly prosperous, with many food stalls.
瓶窑由于久为官军驻扎，市面相当兴盛，饭摊子更多；

Hu never put on official airs and personally invited the four orderlies to drink with him.
胡雪岩向来不摆官架子，亲邀四名马弁，一起喝酒。

They were so ill at ease, however, that he finally let them sit at another table.
而那四名弟兄却深感局促，最后还是让他们另桌而坐。

He drank with Officer He and listened to everything he had to say about Zuo.
他自己便跟何都司对酌，听他谈左宗棠的一切。


“Our Commander-in-Chief is excellent in every respect but his temper.
『我们这位大帅，什么都好，就是脾气不好。

When he flies into a rage, though, you must not be afraid.
不过，他发脾气的时候，你不能怕；

The more frightened you are, the worse it gets.”
越怕越糟糕。』

“He responds to firmness rather than submission,” Hu said. “That sort of man is easier to deal with.”
『这是吃硬不吃软的脾气。』胡雪岩说∶『这样的人，反而好相处。』

“Yes.
『是的。

But you must not try to outdo him in firmness.
可也不能硬过他头！

The best thing is to take no notice, let him finish his scolding and say all he has to say, then present your own reasons.
最好是不理他，听他骂完，说完，再讲自己的道理，

He will regard you differently.”
他就另眼相看了。』

Hu found this advice valuable and raised his cup to He.
胡雪岩觉得这两句话，受益不浅；便举杯相敬；

Then he asked, “How many years have you been with Treasurer Jiang?”
同时问说∶『老兄，你跟蒋方伯多少年了？』

“We are close relations.
『我们至亲，

I have always been with him.”
我一直跟他。』

“Forgive a presumptuous question: how does the Commander-in-Chief regard him?
『我有句冒昧的话要请教，左大帅对蒋方伯怎么样？

Does he consider him a possible replacement for himself?”
是不是当他是自己的替手？』

“Hardly!” He replied.
『不见得！』何都司答说，

“What sort of man is our commander?
「左大帅是何等样人？

He thinks himself another Zhuge Liang.
当自己诸葛亮；

Who could replace him?”
哪个能替代他？

To another listener, those casual words might have meant little.
『这两句闲谈，在旁人听来，不关紧要；

To Hu, they led to a most important decision.
而在胡雪岩却由此而作成了一个很重要的决定。


He had continually pondered his future course and the powerful support he would need to rebuild his fortunes and begin anew.
他对于自己今后的出处，以及重整旗鼓，再创事业的倚傍奥援，一直萦回脑际，

Jiang Yili had seemed an honest, kindly man.
本来觉得蒋益澧为人倒还憨厚，

If their friendship grew close, he might become a second Wang Youling, trusting Hu’s advice and treating him like a brother.
如果结交得深了，便是第二个王有龄，将来言听计从，亲如手足；

That would be far pleasanter than serving Zuo, whose temper was notorious.
那就比伺候脾气大出名的左宗棠，痛快得多了。

Now He’s words brought sudden understanding.
现在听何都司一说，憬然有悟，

Zuo would never spare no effort to advance Jiang as He Guiqing had advanced Wang.
左宗棠之对蒋益澧，不可能象何桂清之对王有龄那样，提携惟恐不力。

A provincial governorship was no small appointment.
一省的巡抚毕竟是个非同小可的职位，

Unless another suitable post could be found for Zeng Guoquan, Jiang himself proved fully qualified, and Zuo gave him exceptional support, the seal of the governor of Zhejiang was unlikely to fall into his hands.
除非曾国荃另有适当的安排；蒋益澧本身够格；而左宗棠又肯格外力保，看来浙江巡抚的大印，不会落在蒋益澧手里。

Hu therefore had only one course: devote himself wholeheartedly to Zuo.
既然如此，惟有死心塌地，专走左宗堂这条路子了。

They rose in the middle of the night and set off at dawn, taking three hours to cover the eighteen li of mountain road.
半夜起身，黎明上路。十八里山道，走了三个钟头才到。

Zuo’s headquarters occupied a temple to Lord Guan.
左宗棠的行辕，设在一座关帝庙里。

Even amid the haste of campaigning, he maintained considerable gubernatorial ceremony.
虽是戎马倥偬之际，他的总督派头，还是不小；

A great sedan chair covered in green wool stood before the temple.
庙前摆着一顶绿呢大轿；

Against the screen wall were several vermilion boards on tall poles, their inscriptions in imitation Song lettering picked out in gold.
照墙下有好几块朱红『高脚牌』，泥金仿宋体写着官衔荣典，

One read, “Imperially Appointed Superintendent of Military Affairs in Zhejiang”; another, “Concurrently Acting Governor of Zhejiang”; a third, “First-Rank Insignia, Minister of War, Concurrent Right Censor-in-Chief, Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang”; and a fourth, “Granted the Right to Wear the Peacock Feather”.
一块是『钦命督办浙江军务；』一块是『兼署浙江巡抚』；一块是『头品顶戴兵部尚书兼都察院右都御史闽浙总督部堂』；一块是『赏戴花翎』；

The last was less glorious, and a lifelong grievance to Zuo: his examination distinction was merely “Successful Candidate in the Hunan Provincial Examination of the Renchen Year, the Twelfth Year of Daoguang”.
再一块就不大光彩，也是左宗棠平生的恨事，科名只是『道光十二年壬夺辰科湖南乡试中式』，

He held only the provincial degree.
不过一名举人。


Looking inside, Hu saw two lines of armed guards stretching from the main entrance to the statues of Guan Ping and Zhou Cang before the great hall.
再往庙里看，两行带刀的亲兵，从大门口一直站到大殿关平、周仓的神像前；

Several military officers with blue hat-buttons were there too.
蓝顶子的武官亦有好几个。

Unwilling to affront Zuo’s imposing dignity, Hu led his horse aside, took out his formal visiting paper and asked He to present it.
胡雪岩见此光景，不肯冒犯左宗棠的威风；牵马在旁，取出『手本』，拜托何都司代为递了进去。

A long time passed before an officer of the headquarters guard emerged with it in his hand.
隔了好久，才看见出来一个『武巡捕』，手里拿着胡雪岩的手本；

Though he had plainly seen Hu, he called out in an official tone, “Which gentleman is Intendant Hu from Hangzhou?”
明明已经看到本人，依然拉起官腔问道∶『哪位是杭州来的胡道台？』

Hu nodded, assumed an official manner himself, and advanced with measured steps.
胡雪岩点点头，也摆出官派，踱着四方步子，

“I am.”
上前答道∶『我就是。』

“The Commander-in-Chief will receive you.”
『大帅传见。』

“Very well.
『是的。

Please lead the way.”
请引路。』

They entered the gate but passed to the west of the hall through a side entrance into a small courtyard, also crowded with guards.
进门不进殿，由西边角门口进去，有个小小的院落，也是站满了亲兵，

An attendant in a grey cloth gown was more courteous: he lifted the door curtain and motioned Hu inside.
另外有个穿灰布袍的听差，倒还客气，揭开门帘，示意胡雪岩入内。

A short, stout old man sat at a square table by the window, holding a long-stemmed pipe in his left hand while the brush in his right flew across the paper.
进门一看，一个矫胖老头，左手捏一管旱烟袋；右手提着笔，在窗前一张方桌上挥毫如飞。

He seemed unaware of Hu’s footsteps.
听得脚步声，浑似不觉；

Hu could only wait.
胡雪岩只好等着，

When the brush was laid down, he gathered up his gown, made his formal obeisance and announced himself.
等他放下笔，方捞起衣襟请安，同时报名。

“Hu Guangyong, expectant circuit intendant of Zhejiang, pays his respects to Your Excellency.”
『浙江候补道胡光墉，参见大人。』

“Oh, so you are Hu Guangyong!”
『喔，你就是胡光墉！』

Zuo’s authoritative eyes seemed to flash as they surveyed him from head to foot.
左宗棠那双眼睛，颇具威严，光芒四射似的，将他从头望到底，

“I have heard of you for a long time.”
『我闻名已久了。』

This was no compliment, and Hu saw no reason for modest disclaimers.
这不是一句好话，胡雪岩觉得无须谦虚；

“Your Excellency has achieved an extraordinary victory.
只说∶『大人建了不世之功，

I have come expressly to congratulate you.”
特为来给大人道喜！』

“Indeed?
『喔，

You are quick to anticipate the prevailing wind.
你倒是得风气之先！

No wonder you were known as a capable officer in Governor Wang’s day.”
怪不得王中丞在世之日，你有能员之名。』


Hu naturally caught the sarcasm, but there was no need to dispute it yet.
话中带着讥讽，胡雪岩自然听得出来，一时也不必细辨；

The first necessity was to secure a seat.
眼前第一件事是，要能坐了下来——

Zuo could not be ignorant of official etiquette: a civil officer visiting a governor or governor-general, however low his rank, was entitled to sit.
左宗棠不会不懂官场规矩，文官见督抚，品秩再低，也得有个座位；

His deliberate omission of the invitation was meant to humiliate Hu, who must first find a way to meet it.
此刻故意不说『请坐』，是有意给人难堪，先得想个办法应付。

The moment he considered it, a solution came.
念头转到，办法便即有了；

Gathering his gown again, he made another obeisance.
捞起衣襟，又请一个安；

“I have come not only to congratulate Your Excellency, but to thank you.
同时说道∶『不光是为大人道喜；还要跟大人道谢。

The people of Zhejiang have been hanging upside down in their distress, and you have rescued them.”
两浙主灵倒悬，多亏大人解救。』

Everyone spoke of Zuo’s “Hunan mule” temper, but this repeated courtesy at last turned the mule.
都说左宗棠是『湖南骡子』的脾气；而连番多礼，到底将他的骡脾气拧过来了，

“I do not deserve it.”
『不敢当！』

His voice remained cool, suggesting that flattery would not move him, but he finally answered courtesy with courtesy.
他的语声虽还是淡淡的，有那不受奉承的意味；但亦终于以礼相待了，

“Please be seated, Intendant.”
『贵道请坐！』

An attendant had long been waiting with a tray, unable to offer tea while the guest remained standing.
听差是早捧着茶盘等在那里的，只为客人不曾落座，不好奉茶；

Now he placed a covered cup on the small table at Hu’s side.
此时便将一碗盖碗茶摆在他身旁的茶几上。

Hu inclined himself and breathed more freely.
胡雪岩欠一欠身，舒一口气；

So long as appearances were not humiliating, he thought, they could talk.
心里在想∶只要面子上不难看，话就好说了。


“During my two years in Zhejiang, I have heard a great deal about you,” Zuo said without a smile.
『这两年我在浙江，很听人谈起贵道。』左宗棠面无笑容地说，

“I hear you are very grand.”
『听说你很阔啊！』

“I would not presume to say so.”
『不敢！』

Hu leaned forward slightly. “May I ask what Your Excellency means by ‘grand’?”
胡雪岩欠身问道∶『请大人明示所谓「阔」是指什么？』

“They say you live and enjoy yourself like a prince.
『说你起居享用，俨如王侯；

It may be exaggerated, but one can imagine something of the truth.”
这也许是过甚之词。然而也可以想象得知了。』

“Yes.
『是！

I shall not conceal it.
我不瞒大人，

Compared with expectant officials living in hardship, I am very comfortable.” He admitted it frankly without explaining the source of his comfort, which seemed to leave Zuo with nothing to say.
比起清苦的候补人员来，我算是很舒服的。』他坦然承认，而不说舒服的原因，反倒象塞住了左宗棠的口；

After a pause, Zuo spoke directly. “I have received a number of complaints describing your conduct.
停了一下，他直截了当地说∶『我也接到好些禀帖，说你如何如何！

Such reports may not all be invention.
人言未必尽属子虚，

I intend to investigate.
我要查办；

If they prove true, my duty to reform the administration will oblige me to impeach you by name in the strongest terms.”
果真属实，为了整饬吏治，我不能不指名严参！』

“Certainly.
『是！

If I have acted unlawfully, I shall willingly accept the punishment Your Excellency seeks.
如果光墉有什么不法之事，大人指名严参，光墉亦甘愿领罪。

But I believe I have not dared commit evil or practise corruption for private gain.
不过，自问还不敢为非作歹；亦不敢营私舞弊。

Governor Wang recognised and favoured me, and I swore to share life and death with him.
只为受王中丞知遇之恩，誓共生死，

In carrying out my duties, I shrank neither from toil nor from resentment.
当时处事不避劳怨，

That may indeed have earned me enemies.”
得罪了人亦是有的。』

“Whether you committed evil or practised corruption remains to be established.
『是不是为非作歹，营私舞弊，犹待考查。

As for your oath to share life and death with Governor Wang, that is difficult to believe.
至于你说与王中丞誓共生死，这话就令人难信了。

He has died at his post, while you remain perfectly well, do you not?”
王中丞已经殉难，你现在不还是好好的吗？』


“If Your Excellency reproaches me for failing to follow Governor Wang beneath the earth, I have nothing to say.
『如果大人责光墉不能追随王中丞于地下，我没有话说；

But if you believe that dying for loyalty or honour has a rightful purpose, whereas dying merely to accompany a friend is lighter than a feather and no course for a gentleman, then I have a few words in my defence.”
倘或以为殉忠、殉节，都有名目，而殉友死得轻如鸿毛，为君子所不取，那末，光墉倒有几句辩白。』

“Speak.”
『你说。』

“You mean that I shared Governor Wang’s hardship in the besieged city but left him at the crucial moment, making my oath to share his life and death a deception?”
『大人的意思是，光墉跟王中丞在危城之中共患难；紧要关头，我一个人走了，所谓「誓共生死」，成了骗人的话？』

“Yes!”
『是啊！』

Zuo stared hard at him. “How do you explain yourself?
左宗棠逼视着问∶『足下何词以解？

I should like to hear.”
倒要请教！』

“First, may I ask Your Excellency this?
『我先请教大人，

When Hangzhou was surrounded and Governor Wang struggled to hold it, shedding blood rather than tears from his eyes, what was he hoping for?”
当时杭州被围，王中丞苦苦撑持，眼睛里所流的不是泪水，而是血，盼的是什么？』

“Reinforcements, naturally.”
『自然是援军。』

“Yes,” Hu said in a low voice.
『是！』胡雪岩用低沉的声音说，

“Li Yuandu’s army was at Quzhou.
『当时有李元度一军在衙州，

We tried every means to hasten its arrival, but it never came.
千方百计想催他来，始终不到。

We therefore had no choice but to prepare for a prolonged defence.
这一来，就不能不作坚守的打算；

What does a beleaguered city need in order to hold out?”
请问大人，危城坚守靠什么？』

“Food, naturally.
『自然是靠粮食。

‘To the people, food is Heaven.’”
「民以食为天」。』

“True enough.
『「民以食为天」固然不错；

When every last resource is exhausted, civilians may exchange their children to eat.
如果罗掘俱穷，亦无非易子而食。

But what happens when soldiers have no food?
但是，士兵没有粮食，会出什么乱子？

I need not say; Your Excellency knows far better than I do.
不必我说；大人比我清楚得多。

Governor Wang discussed it with me and asked me to buy rice in Shanghai.”
当时王中丞跟我商量，要我到上海去办米。』

Hu suddenly raised his voice. “Though he had entered official life by purchase, Governor Wang had read books.
胡雪岩突然提高了声音说∶『王中丞虽是捐班出身，也读过书的；

He told me the story of the orphan of Zhao in the Records of the Historian.
他跟我讲史记上赵氏孤儿的故事，

If the city could not be held, he said, its defender could only die, and that was easy.
他说，守城守不住，不过一死而已，容易；

To buy rice in Shanghai was harder, like preserving the orphan’s life.
到上海办米就跟「立孤」一样比较难。

He wanted me to be Cheng Ying, who saved the orphan of Zhao.
他要我做保全赵氏孤儿的程婴。

That was, of course, his generous opinion of me.
这当然是他看得起我的话；

But consider, Your Excellency: as governor, he was responsible for defending his territory.
不过，大人请想，他是巡抚，守土有责，

Even if he could have procured rice, he could not leave Hangzhou.
即使他有办法办得到米，也不能离开杭州。

I was the only one who could go to Shanghai for it.
所以，到上海办米这件事，只有我能做；

I had no right to refuse.”
不容我不做。』

“Mm.
『嗯，嗯！』

And afterwards?” Zuo asked.
左宗棠问道∶『后来呢？

“Did you obtain the rice?”
你米办到了没有？』

“Of course.
『当然办到。

But—” Hu’s voice sank sorrowfully. “It was of no avail.”
可是——，』胡雪岩黯然低语∶『无济于事！』


He then explained how he had brought the rice to the Qiantang River outside Hangzhou, how every effort to open a supply route had failed, how he had bowed towards the city and withdrawn in tears, and how he had used the rice to relieve Ningbo.
接着，他将如何办米来到了杭州城外的钱塘江中，如何想尽办法，不能打通粮道，如何望城一拜，痛哭而回；如何将那批米接济了宁波。

He said nothing of the grave illness there that had nearly killed him; that would sound like a claim to merit and would only weaken his account with unnecessary detail.
只是不说在宁波生一场大病，几乎送命；因为那近乎表功的味道，说来反成蛇足了。

Zuo listened closely, then tilted his face upwards in thought for a long while.
左宗棠听得很仔细；仰脸想了半天，

At last he said something Hu had never expected: “You seem to have read quite a few books yourself!”
突然冒出一句话来，却是胡雪岩再也想不到的。『你也很读了些书啊！』

Hu was startled, then understood.
胡雪岩一楞，随即想到了；

Throughout their exchange his language had been rather polished, and he had referred to the story in the Records of the Historian.
这半天与左宗棠对答，话好象显得很文雅，又谈到史记上的故事，

Zuo must suspect that he had consulted some learned adviser and prepared a set speech.
必是他以为预先请教过高人，想好一套话来的。

There was a measure of truth in it: Hu had repeatedly rehearsed what he would say when they met.
这多少也是实情；见了左宗棠该如何说法，他曾一再打过腹稿。

But he could not admit to having deliberately concocted pleasing falsehoods.
但如说是有意说好听的假话，他却不能承认，

“How could I claim to be a learned man?
所以这样答道∶『哪里敢说读过书？

I merely know enough to respect those who are.”
光墉只不过还知道敬重读书人而已！』

“That too is commendable,” Zuo said.
『这也难得了。』左宗棠说，

“I shall investigate those accusations.
『人家告你的那些话，我要查一查。

If matters are indeed as you describe, that is naturally another question.”
果真象你所说的那样子，自然另当别论。』

“It is more than that.
『不然。

Having received public money, I must account for its use in public business.
领了公款，自然公事上要有交代。

The money did not come from Your Excellency’s hands, but you are now the head of this province.
公款虽不是从大人手上领的；可是大人现任本省长官，

It is to you alone that I must render my account.”
光墉的公事，就只有向大人交代。』

“Oh, you have come to account for official business?
『喔，你来交代公事。

For that sum of public money?” Zuo asked.
是那笔公款吗？』左宗棠问，

“How much did you receive?”
『当时领了多少？』
