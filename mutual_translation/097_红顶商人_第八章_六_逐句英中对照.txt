胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第八章（本章第6部分，共9部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


“Twenty thousand taels.
『领了两万两银子。

I now return them to Your Excellency in person.”
如今面缴大人。』

Hu took a red envelope from his clothing and offered it.
说着，从身上掏出一个红封袋来，当面奉上。

Zuo refused to accept it.
左宗棠不肯接红封袋，

“This is public money.
『这是公款，

It cannot be handed over privately.
不便私相授受。』

Please deal with the military supply office.”
他说，『请你跟粮台打交道。』

He summoned the officer responsible for receipts and payments.
当时便唤了粮台上管出纳的委员前来，

The officer took Hu’s note, issued a receipt and affixed the great seal.
收取了胡雪岩的粮票，开收据，盖上大印，

One official matter seemed settled, but Hu had more to say.
看来是了却了一件公事，却不知胡雪岩还有话说。

“Your Excellency, there is still my commission to account for.
『大人，我还要交代。

I was ordered to procure rice.
当初奉令采办的是米，

Until the rice has arrived, I cannot consider the task discharged.”
不能拿米办到，就不能算交差。』

“But—?”
『这——？』

Zuo was puzzled, uncertain what Hu meant or how to respond.
左宗棠相当困扰；对他的话，颇有不知所云之感，因而也就无法作何表示。

“To speak frankly, until I delivered that rice, I could not face Governor Wang’s spirit in Heaven.
『说实话，这一批米不能办到，我就是对不起王中丞的在天之灵。

Now, at last, I can truly render an account.”
现在，总算可以真正有交代了！』

Hu spoke calmly.
胡雪岩平静地说，

“I have ten thousand shi of rice on the river outside Hangzhou.
『我有一万石米，就在杭州城外江面上，

Please send an officer to inspect and receive it.”
请大人派员验收。』

Zuo grew still more bewildered.
此言一出，左宗棠越发困惑，

“What are you saying?
『你说的什么？』

There are ten thousand shi of rice?”
他问∶『有一万石米在？』

“Yes.”
『是！』

“On the river outside Hangzhou?”
『就在杭州城外江面上？』

“Yes.
『是！』

Several hundred shi have already been assigned to Treasurer Jiang as military provisions.”
胡雪岩答说，『已有几百石，先拨了给蒋方伯，充作军粮了。』


Zuo turned to those beside him. “Who escorted Mr Hu here?”
左宗棠听得这话便左右问道∶『护送胡大人来的是谁？』

“Officer He.”
『是何都司』。

He was summoned, and Zuo’s first question was, “Do you know whether several hundred shi of military grain have been brought into the city from the Qiantang River?”
于是找了何都司来，左宗棠第一句话便是∶『你知道不知道，有几百石军粮从钱塘江上运到城里？』

“They have, Commander-in-Chief.”
『回大帅的话，有的。』

He pointed towards Hu. “Mr Hu brought them from Shanghai.”
何都司手一指∶『是胡大人从上海运来的。』

“Good.
『好！

You may withdraw.”
你先下去吧。』

Zuo instructed the attendant, “Invite Mr Hu to the kang.”
左宗棠向听差吩咐∶『请胡大人升炕！』

The courtesies changed at once.
礼数顿时不同了！

From being denied a seat to sitting opposite Zuo on the raised platform—how completely fortune could turn in a moment!
由不令落座到升炕对坐，片刻之间，荣枯大不相同；

Hu felt both reflective and triumphant, and naturally more confident of handling him.
胡雪岩既感慨，又得意，当然对应付左宗棠也更有把握了。

The attendant moved the covered teacup to the small table on the kang.
等听差将盖碗茶移到炕几上，

Hu thanked his host and sat down.
胡雪岩道谢坐下；

Zuo spoke slowly. “With these ten thousand shi of rice, not only can the people of Hangzhou be saved; I can be confident of clearing all Zhejiang.
左宗棠徐徐说道∶『有这一万石米，不但杭州的百姓得救；肃清浙江全境，我也有把握了。

What you have done is beyond expectation, a service of immeasurable merit.
老兄此举，出人意表，功德无量。

I shall not be the only man grateful to you.”
感激的，不止我左某一个人。』

“Your Excellency is too generous.”
『大人言重了。』


“I speak the truth.
『这是实话。

But there is something else I must say frankly.
不过我也要说实话。』

At current prices, ten thousand shi of rice must be worth fifty or sixty thousand taels.
左宗棠说，『一万石米，时价要值五六万银子；

The supply office cannot pay so much at once.
粮台上一时还付不起那么多。

We have just won a great victory, and the troops must be rewarded in ready silver.
因为刚打了一个大胜仗，犒赏弟兄是现银子。

I suggest that you first take back the money you have just returned.
我想，你先把你缴来的那笔款子领了回去；

Then we can discuss how to pay the balance.”
余数我们倒商量一下，怎么样个付法？』

“Your Excellency need not trouble yourself.
『大人不必操心了。

I am donating all ten thousand shi.”
这一万石米，完全由光墉报。』

“Donating them?”
『报效？』

Zuo feared he had misheard.
左宗棠怕自己是听错了。

“Yes.
『是！

They are my contribution.”
光墉报效。』

“That is a great expense.
『这，未免太破费了。』

What do you hope to obtain, my friend?
左宗棠问道∶『老兄有什么企图，

You may speak plainly.”
不妨实说。』

“Nothing for myself.
『毫无企图。

First, I do it for Governor Wang; second, for the people of Hangzhou; third, for Your Excellency.”
第一，为了王中丞；第二，为了杭州百姓；第三，为了大人。』

“I am deeply obliged.”
『承情之至！』

Zuo clasped his hands.
左宗棠拱拱手说，

“I shall memorialise the throne at once and request a commendation.”
『我马上出奏，请朝廷褒奖。』

“I am naturally grateful for your favour.
『大人栽培，光墉自然感激，

But there is something I must say, though it may seem unappreciative.
不过，有句不识抬举的话，

It is like a bone in my throat.
好比骨鲠在喉；

Please do not be angry if I speak.”
吐出来请大人不要动气。』

“Not at all, not at all!” Zuo answered repeatedly.
『言重，言重！』左宗棠一叠连声地说，

“Speak freely.”
『尽管请说。』

“I am certainly not giving this rice to obtain an imperial commendation.
『我的报效这批米，决不是为朝廷褒奖。

I am a businessman.
光墉是生意人，

I know how to get things done, not how to make a career in office.”
只会做事，不会做官。』

“How well said! ‘Get things done, not make a career in office’!”
『好一个只会做事，不会做官！』

The words went straight to Zuo’s heart.
这一句话碰到左宗棠的心坎上，

He slapped the kang table and spoke loudly, his admiration unmistakable.
拍着炕几，大声地说；赞赏之意，真个溢于言表了。

“I believe Your Excellency too thinks only of getting things done, never of honours and wealth,” Hu continued.
『我在想，大人也是只晓得做事，从不把功名富贵放在心上的人。』胡雪岩说，

“In my view, your temperament is exactly the opposite of that of a certain great man today.”
『照我看，跟现在一位大人物，性情正好相反。』

The first part was perfectly judged flattery.
前半段话，恭维得恰到好处；

The last naturally aroused Zuo’s particular interest.
对于后面一句话，左宗棠自然特感关切，

He leaned forward. “Whom do you mean?”
探身说道∶『请教！』

“Governor Li of Jiangsu.
『大人跟江苏李中丞正好相反。

He knows how to make a career; Your Excellency knows how to do the work.” Hu added, “It is not that you do not know how to make a career.
李中丞会做官；大人会做事。』胡雪岩又说∶『大人也不是不会做官，

You merely disdain it.”
只不过不屑于做官而已。』

“Ah, how refreshing!
『啊，痛快，

How refreshing!”
痛快！』

Zuo tilted back his face and shook his head, looking like a man who had found someone who understood him.
左宗棠仰着脸，摇着头说；是一副遇见了知音的神情。


Hu stopped while his flattery was well received and offered no more extravagant compliments.
胡雪岩见好即收，不再奉上高帽子；

Instead he said modestly, “I have been speaking at random, taking liberties before Your Excellency.”
反而谦虚一句∶『我是信口胡说。在大人面前放肆。』

“My friend,” Zuo said seriously, “you must not undervalue yourself.
『老兄，』左宗棠正色说道，『你不要妄自菲薄，

Among all the grandees in purple and scarlet at court, few possess your insight.
在我看满朝朱紫贵，及得上老兄识见的。实在不多。

What are the characters of your courtesy name?”
你大号是哪两个字？』

“Xueyan: xue for snow, yan for rocky crags.”
『草字雪岩。风雪的雪，岩壑的岩。』

“Brother Xueyan, you have presumably spent these past few years in Shanghai.
『雪岩兄，』左宗棠说，『你这几年想必一直在上海，

You must know Li Shaoquan’s conduct well.
李少荃的作为，必然深知；

Compare him with me.”
你倒拿我跟他比一比看。』

“In what respect?” Hu asked.
『这，』胡雪岩问道，『比哪一方面？』

“Our achievements.”
『比比我们的成就。』

“Very well.”
『是！』

Hu thought for a moment. “Governor Li’s recovery of Suzhou was naturally a great achievement.
胡雪岩想了一下答道∶『李中丞克复苏州，当然是一大功；

But he succeeded through the efforts of others.
不过，因人成事；

It cannot compare with your struggle alone, which was far more difficult and therefore more admirable.”
比不上大人孤军奋战，来得难能可贵。』

“That, at least, is a fair statement,” Zuo said.
『这，总算是一句公道话。』左宗棠说，

“I have suffered two disadvantages: my territory is less favourable, and I have fewer able men.”
『我吃亏的有两种，第一是地方不如他好；第二、是人才不如他多。』

“Yes.”
『是的。』

Hu nodded gravely.
胡雪岩深深点头，

“Governor Li does know how to employ people.”
『李中丞也算会用人的。』

“Then may I ask a rather presumptuous question?
『那末，我有句很冒昧的话请教，

With your great abilities and your record under Governor Wang, did he never think of recruiting you?”
以你的大才，以你在王中丞那里的业绩，他倒没有起延揽之意？』


“He did.
『有过的。

I could not go.”
我不能去！』

“Why?”
『为什么？』

“First, Governor Li was prejudiced against Governor Wang.
『第一、李中丞对王公有成见，

To serve him despite that would show too little spirit.”
我还为他所用，也太没有志气了。』

“Good!
『好！』

And second?”
左宗棠接着问∶『第二呢？』

“Second, I am a Zhejiang man and wish to work for Zhejiang.
『第二、我是浙江人，我要为浙江出力；

Besides, Governor Wang left me unfinished public business: this money for buying rice.
何况我还有王中丞委托我未了的公事，就是这笔买米的款子，

I had to account for it.”
总要有个交代。』

“Admirable, admirable!
『难得，难得，

Brother Xueyan, you are truly trustworthy.” Zuo called out, “Attend me!
雪岩兄，你真有信用。』左宗棠说到这里，喊一声∶『来呀！

Mr Hu will stay for a simple meal.”
留胡大人吃便饭。』

According to official custom, when a superior made such an invitation, the attendants had two duties: to ask the guest to change into informal clothing, and to arrange his move to a garden reception room or even the private apartments.
照官场中的规矩，长官对属下有这样的表示，听差便得做两件事，第一件是请客人更换便衣；第二件是准备将客人移到花厅甚至『上房』中去。

Ordinarily Hu would bring a servant on a visit, carrying a bundle of clothes and ready to help him change.
在正常的情况之下，胡雪岩去拜客，自然带着跟班；跟班手中捧着衣包，视需要随时伺候主人更换。

Today he had come alone and naturally had nothing informal to wear.
但此时只有胡雪岩一个人，当然亦不会有便衣；

Zuo therefore ordered the attendant to bring one of his own lightly padded gowns for “Mr Hu”.
左宗棠便吩咐听差，取他自己的薄棉袍来为『胡大人』更换。

Zuo was short and stout, Hu tall and lean.
左宗棠矮胖；胡雪岩瘦长，

The sleeves hung loosely, while the hem stopped far too high, exposing a long stretch of boots caked with yellow mud.
这件棉袍穿上身，大袖郎当，下摆吊起一大截，露出一大截沾满了黄泥的靴帮子，

The effect was not merely inelegant but rather comical.
形容不但不雅，而且有些可笑。

Yet the kindness behind it was touching, and Hu felt thoroughly comfortable in it.
但这份情意是可感的。所以胡雪岩觉得穿在身上很舒服。

Moving to a garden reception room was equally impossible.
至于移向花厅，当然也办不到了。

In this little temple, where could spare rooms be found?
一座小关帝庙里，哪里来的空闲房屋，

The governor-general’s audience room, office and bedroom were all confined here.
闽浙总督的官厅，签押房与卧室，都在那里了。

Behind the temple, however, was a small earthen hill with a thatched pavilion, a modest place from which to enjoy a view.
不过，庙后倒有一座土山，山上有座茅亭，亦算可供登临眺望的一景；

To escape interruptions from officers seeking audiences, Zuo ordered the meal served there.
左宗棠为了避免将领请谒的纷扰，吩咐就在茅亭中置酒。


The wine was naturally excellent.
酒当然是好酒。

Shaoxing had long since been recovered, and the province’s highest official was supplied with aged wine that had survived the war undamaged in its cellars.
绍兴早经克复，供应一省长官的，自然是历经兵燹而无恙的窑藏陈酿；

The food was Hunan style.
菜是湖南口味，

Though only two men were dining, there were great dishes and long chopsticks.
虽只两个人对酌，依然大盘长筷，

Finally the cook, wearing a hat with a red tassel, personally brought a food box.
最后厨子戴着红缨帽，亲自来上菜，

He opened it to reveal nothing more than a small plate of Hunan cured pork.
打开食盒，只是一小盘湖南腊肉。

Why such ceremony?
不知何以郑重如此？

“My wife prepared this herself,” Zuo explained.
『这是内子亲手调制的，

“It came all the way from Hunan through countless difficulties.
间关万里，从湖南送到这里，

It is no longer good to eat.
已经不中吃了。

I keep it simply to remind myself of all the pains she once took to manage our affairs in trying circumstances.”
只不过我自己提醒我，不要忘记内子当年委曲绸缪的一番苦心而已。』

Hu had heard something of Madam Zhou.
胡雪岩也听说过，左宗棠的周夫人，

She came from a wealthy family, but when she married Zuo he was a poor scholar.
是富室之女；初嫔左家时，夫婿是个寒士。

She understood that this man, who “owned not half a mu yet worried for all under heaven”, possessed extraordinary talent.
但是周夫人却深知『身无半亩，心忧天下』的左宗棠，才气纵横，

Though he repeatedly failed the metropolitan examination, one day he would burst through the wall and soar.
虽然会试屡屡落弟，终有破壁飞去的一日；

She encouraged and consoled him with unceasing care.
所以鼓励慰藉，无怕不至。

Later he moved into her family’s home.
以后左宗棠移居岳家，

The large Zhou clan could hardly admire a poor son-in-law with such a temper.
而周家大族，不会看得起这个脾气的穷姑爷。

Afraid that her husband might storm away in anger, yet anxious to uphold his family’s dignity, she mediated on every side until mind and strength were exhausted.
周夫人一方面怕夫婿一怒而去，一方面又要为夫家做面，左右调停，心力交痤，

Now, at last, her day of vindication had come.
如今到底也有扬眉吐气的一天了。

Hu drew another lesson from this.
这对胡雪岩又是一种启示。

Gratitude, more than anything else, lay behind Zuo’s present respect for his wife.
左宗棠如今尊重周夫人，报恩的成分，多于一切，

Clearly he was a man of deep feeling, unwilling to fail those who had helped him.
足见得是不会负人，不肯负人而深具性情者，

Compared with Li Hongzhang, who controlled his subordinates through rewards, advancement and political manoeuvring, this was the man Hu would rather befriend wholeheartedly.
这比起李鸿章 以利禄权术驾驭部下来，宁愿倾心结交此人。

Accordingly, when Zuo asked questions, he held back still less.
因此，当左宗棠有所询问时，他越发不作保留，

From Hangzhou’s reconstruction to raising military funds, he had a method for everything and spoke without reserve.
从杭州的善后谈到筹饷，他都有一套办法拿出来，滔滔不绝，言无不尽。

Host and guest soon approached the point where all formality could be discarded and nothing was beyond discussion.
宾主之间，很快地已接近脱略形迹，无所不谈的境地了。


Their meal lasted two double-hours.
一顿酒喝了两个时辰方罢。

At last Zuo sighed. “Brother Xueyan, now I am troubled.
左宗棠忽然叹口气说∶『雪岩兄，我倒有些发愁了。

In what capacity should I ask your help?
不知应该借重你在哪方面给我帮忙？

Reconstruction is the immediate need, yet the monthly military payroll of two hundred and fifty or sixty thousand taels still has no assured source, and there too I must rely on you.
当务之急是地方善后，可是每个月二十五、六万的饷银，尚无的款，又必得仰仗大力。

If only you could be in two places at once!
只恨足下分身无术！

Tell me yourself: what would you like to do?”
雪岩兄，请你自己说一说，愿意做些什么？』

“Raising funds is important, but once a method is established, anyone whose integrity can be trusted can carry it out.”
『筹饷是件大事，不过只要有办法，凡是操守靠得住的人，都可以干得。』

Hu sounded apologetic.
胡雪岩歉然地说，

“I have a small selfish wish: to do something for my native place.”
『光墉稍微存一点私心，想为本乡本土尽几力。』

“How is that selfish?
『这哪里是私心！

It shows your generous and honourable heart.
正见得你一副侠心义肠。

Since the war began, Hangzhou has suffered most grievously.
军兴以来，杭州被祸最惨，

Its reconstruction has countless threads to draw together.
善后事宜，经纬万端，

As acting governor, I cannot evade responsibility.
我兼摄无篆，责无旁贷，

To have a man of your ability, willing to labour and bear complaints, helping me serve his homeland—that is excellent indeed.” Zuo asked, “You have met Jiang Xiangquan, I suppose?”
有你老兄这样大才，而且肯任劳任怨，又是为桑梓效力的人帮我的忙，实在太好了。』左宗棠说到这里，问道∶『跟蒋芗泉想来见面了？』

“Yes.”
『是！』

“What do you think of him?”
『你觉得他为人如何？』

“He is very straightforward.
『很直爽的人。

We got on extremely well.”
我们谈得很投机。』

“Excellent, excellent!” Zuo asked gladly, “Then you must have discussed the work of reconstruction?”
『好极，好极！』左宗棠欣然问道∶『地方上的一切善后，总也谈过了？』

“Not in depth.
『还不曾深谈。

But Treasurer Jiang has honoured my little bank by entrusting it with the business of the provincial treasury.
不过承蒋方伯看得起，委托我的一个小小钱庄，为他代理藩库；

I shall do my utmost to meet the immediate expenses.”
眼前急需的支出，我总尽力维持。』

“Better still.
『那更好了。

Nothing is more urgent than relief.
万事莫如赈济急；

With ten thousand shi of rice, the soldiers and people can be fed for a month or two, until further supplies arrive.
如今有一万石米，在军需民食，能维持一两个月，后援就接得上了。

And with your bank meeting the treasury’s expenses, there will be advances available for helping the wounded and caring for the dead.
再有宝号代为支应藩库的一切开销，扶伤恤死，亦不愁无款可垫。

Relief in Hangzhou should therefore begin at once.
然则杭州的赈济事宜，应当马上动手。

I propose establishing a reconstruction bureau.
我想，设一善后局，

Brother Xueyan, will you serve as its director?”
雪岩兄，请你当总办，如何？』

“Yes.” Hu answered solemnly. “Both public duty and private feeling forbid me to decline.”
『是！』胡雪岩肃然答说∶『于公于私，义不容辞。』

“Then I thank you on behalf of Hangzhou’s people.”
『我就代杭州百姓致谢了。』

Zuo clasped his hands.
左宗棠拱拱手说，

“I shall have the papers prepared at once and send them through Jiang Xiangquan.”
『公事我马上叫他们预备，交蒋芗泉转送。』


This arrangement was precisely what Hu wanted.
这样处置，正符合胡雪岩的希望。

He had always taken “arouse no jealousy” as his guiding rule.
因为他为人处世，一向奉『不招忌』三字为座右铭；

His status was much the same as Jiang Yili’s, but under Zuo he was, after all, only an invited adviser.
自己的身分与蒋益澧差不多，但在左宗棠手下，到底只算一个客卿，

If he appeared too intimate with Zuo, or even bypassed Jiang to take orders directly, Jiang could hardly feel comfortable. Hu had only to put himself in the other man’s place to see that.
如果形迹太密甚至越过蒋益澧这一关，直接听命于左宗棠，设身处地为人想一想，心里也会不舒服。

Though Hu was present, the appointment would be sent through Jiang.
现在当着本人在此，而委任的札子却要交由蒋益澧转发，

This respected the provincial treasurer’s authority and quietly helped Hu draw closer to him.
便是尊重藩司的职权；也是无形中为他拉拢蒋益澧，

Such a small official procedure, yet so much lay behind it!
仅不过公事上小小的一道手续，便有许多讲究；

Holding office and employing men were no simple matters.
只见得做官用人，不是件容易的事。

His admiration for Zuo increased, and he wanted to do a little more for him—or at least devise a few more useful ideas.
这样想着，他对左宗棠又加了几分钦佩之心；因而厚意替他多做一点事，至少也得为他多策划几个好主意。

Just then Zuo returned to military finance, and Hu decided to offer an ingenious scheme.
心念刚动，左宗棠正好又谈起筹饷，他决定献上一条妙计。

He had long thought it through.
这一计，他筹之已熟；

Originally he had meant to sell his wares to a buyer who knew their worth, waiting for the proper price: without sufficient reward, he would not readily have disclosed it.
本来的打算是『货卖识家』，不妨『待价而沽』。这也就是说，如果没有相当的酬庸，他是不肯轻易吐露的；

Now, feeling that Zuo had recognised his worth, he emptied his store of ideas before him.
此刻对左宗棠，多少有知遇之感，因而就倾囊而出了。


“There are many ways of raising military funds, but broadly they fall into two kinds.
『筹饷之道多端，大致不外两途，

One is the likin tax.
第一是办厘金，

That depends on a thriving market and cannot be forced.
这要靠市面兴旺，无法强求；

The other is soliciting contributions.
第二是劝捐，

In recent years, everyone able to give has already given, and persuasion is difficult.
这几年捐得起的都捐过了，「劝」起来也很吃力。

But I have thought of a class of people who can afford to contribute and will certainly be willing.
如今我想到有一路人，他们捐得起，而且一定肯捐；

We might turn our attention to them.”
不妨在这一路人头上，打个主意。』

“They can afford it and are willing?
『捐得起，又肯捐，

What could be better?” Zuo asked eagerly. “Who are they?”
那不太妙了吗？』左宗棠急急问道∶『是哪一路人？』

“The Longhairs.
『是长毛！』胡雪岩说，

They have held the southeast for more than ten years and amassed a great deal by plunder.
『长毛盘踞东南十几年，搜括得很不少；

Is it not only right that they should now contribute something?”
现在要他们捐几文，不是天经地义？』

Understanding dawned on Zuo.
这一说，左宗棠恍然大悟，

He nodded repeatedly. “Yes, yes.
连连点头∶『对，对，

Go on.” Hu explained that during those years, quite a number of rebels had recognised the right moment, made their fortunes and retired into hiding.
请你再说下去。』于是胡雪岩为他指出，这十几年中，颇有些见机而作的长毛，发了财退藏于密；

Once the Hong-Yang rebellion was suppressed, those who had joined it would naturally be punished by law.
洪杨一旦平定，从逆的当然要依国法治罪。

Yet though rebellion was an unpardonable crime, many had been forced into it—too many to prosecute.
可是叛逆虽罪在不赦，而被裹胁从逆的人很多，办不胜办。

To implicate large numbers and keep society in turmoil would not allow a country exhausted by great disorder to recover.
株连过众，扰攘不安，亦非大乱之后的休养生息之道；

The best course was to leave an opening in the net, a way for people to begin anew.
所以最好的处置办法是，网开一面，予人自新之路。

Still, to overlook everything would let them off too cheaply.
只是一概既往不咎，亦未免太便宜了此辈；

There should be a mild punishment, with each person free to choose between a beating and a fine.
应该略施薄惩。愿打愿罚，各听其便。

“Your Excellency knows human nature,” Hu said. “People prefer a fine to a beating, unless they cannot afford it.
『大人晓得的，人之常情，总是愿罚不愿打；除非罚不起。』胡雪岩说，

I know there are many who can.
『据我知道，罚得起的人很多。

Most hide in the foreign settlements, relying on foreign protection, and for the moment the authorities can do little about them.
他们大都躲在夷场上，倚仗洋人的势力，官府一时无奈其何，

Yet they remain men without a lawful identity, unable to emerge openly.
可是终究是个出不了头的「黑人」，

Explain the advantages and risks, appeal to their sense of right: their money was ill-gotten anyway.
如果动以利害，晓以大义；反正手头也是不义之财，

Why should they not part with some of it in exchange for a fresh start?”
舍了一笔，换个重新做人的机会，何乐不为？』

“Quite so,” Zuo said with a smile.
『说得是。』左宗棠笑道，

“Such men will not be content with obscurity.
『此辈不甘寂寞，

They may want not only to become respectable again, but to take office.”
不但要爬起来做人，只怕还要站出来做官。』

“Exactly.”
『正是这话。』

Hu raised two fingers.
胡雪岩撮起两指一伸，

“Men of that kind must contribute twice.”
『象这种人，要捐他两笔。』


“How so?”
『怎么呢？』

“Once to become respectable, and once to become officials.
『一笔是做人；另外一笔是做官。

Does entering office not require a contribution?” Zuo laughed.
做官不要捐吗？』左宗棠失笑了，

“I had confused myself.
『我倒弄糊涂了！』

In that case, I had better request several thousand blank contribution certificates from the ministry at once.”
他说，『照此看来，我得赶快向部里领几千张空白捐照来。』

“Yes.
『是！

Please send the official request.”
大人尽管动公事去领。』

“Suppose I obtain them, Brother Xueyan,” Zuo asked deliberately. “Whom shall I entrust with them?”
『领是领了。雪岩兄，』左宗棠故意问道∶『交给谁去用呢？』

Hu was silent for a while. “Allow me time to find a suitable man and recommend him.”
胡雪岩不作声，停了一会方说∶『容我慢慢物色好了，向大人保荐。』

“I do not think you need look.
『我看你也不用物色了，

You must undertake the difficulty yourself.”
就是你自己勉为其难吧！』

“I fear that—” “No, no!”
『这怕——。』『不，不！』

Zuo waved aside his objection.
左宗棠挥手打断了他的话，

“Do not decline.
『你不必推辞了！

Once you have met me, Brother Xueyan, you can no longer insist on your own wishes.
雪岩兄，你遇见我，就容不得你再作主张。

That may sound unreasonable, but it is not.
这话好象蛮不讲理；不是的！

I know now that your abilities are as vast as the sea.
足下才大如海，我已深知。

Never mind these two tasks: you could handle several more with ease.
不要说就这两件事，再多兼几个差使，你也能够应付裕如。

You must have capable men under you.
我想，你手下总有一班得力的人；

Give me their names, and I shall instruct Jiang Xiangquan to appoint them all.
你尽管开单子来，我关照蒋芗泉，一律照委。

You need only travel between Shanghai and Hangzhou and provide the ideas.”
你往来沪杭两地，出出主意就行了。』

Such confidence reminded Hu of two lines Wang Youling had often repeated during the siege: “Bend every effort to the task; cease only in death.” He answered with spirit. “If Your Excellency believes I can do it, I shall try.”
如此看重，不由得使胡雪岩想起王有龄在围城中常说的两句话说∶『鞠躬尽痤，死而后已。』便慨然答道∶『既然大人认为我干得了，我就试一试看。』

“No need to try.
『不用试，

I guarantee you will succeed,” Zuo said.
包你成功！』左宗棠说，

“I want you to attend to both matters together.
『我希望你两件事兼筹并顾。

Military affairs in Zhejiang are at a crucial stage.
浙江的军务，正在紧要关头上，

We must on no account have a disturbance over unpaid wages.”
千万不能有「闹饷」的活把戏弄出来。』

“I shall do my utmost.
『是。我尽力而为。』

What I need your instructions on now is the name of this contribution.
胡雪岩说，『如今要请示的是，这个捐的名目。

I suggest ‘penalty contribution’.”
我想叫「罚捐」。』

“That describes it accurately.
『罚捐倒也名副其实。

But—” He pondered for a long time without finishing.
不过——。』他沉吟着，好久未说下去。


He plainly had a misgiving, and Hu could imagine it.
这当然是有顾忌；胡雪岩也可以想象得到，

A scheme of “penalty contributions” might provoke criticism and the charge of “sheltering rebels”, a very serious offence.
开办『罚捐』可能会惹起浮议，指作『包庇逆党』。这是很重的一个罪名。

Yet whether it amounted to sheltering them depended on the circumstances.
然而是否『包庇』，要看情节而定；

That and allowing people to reform were superficially similar but different things.
与予人自新之路，是似是而非的两回事。

Hu kept these thoughts to himself, watching Zuo’s expression closely to see whether he would take responsibility.
他心里这样在想，口头却保持沉默；而且很注意左宗棠的表情，要看他是不是有担当？

Zuo naturally would; indeed, that was one of the qualities on which he prided himself.
左宗棠自然是有担当的；而且这正也是他平时自负之处。

What troubled him was finding a different name.
他所考虑的改换名目；

He thought for some time without discovering a suitable term and finally decided to use this one for the present.
想了好一会，竟找不出适当的字眼，便次定暂进先用了再说。
