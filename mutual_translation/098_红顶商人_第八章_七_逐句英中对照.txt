Another question followed. “Should this penalty contribution be reported to the throne?
接着，又有疑问∶『这个罚捐，要不要出奏？』他问，

What do you think?”
『你意下如何？』

“If it is reported first, opposition may prevent the scheme from starting.
『出奏呢，怕有人反对，办不成功；

If it is not, the ministry may later raise procedural objections, or a censor may impeach us.
不出奏呢？又怕将来部里打官腔，或者「都老爷」参上一本。』

There are advantages and disadvantages on both sides.
胡雪岩说，『利弊参见，

Your Excellency must decide.”
全在大人作主。』

“It must be done.
『办是一定要办；

But though I am not afraid of trouble, there is no reason to take the blame unnecessarily.
不过我虽不怕事，却犯不上无缘无故背个黑锅，

Think again: is there a way to prevent interference while leaving us a defensible position?”
你倒再想想，有什么既不怕他人掣肘，又能为自己留下退步的办法。』

“Act impartially in the public interest, and there will always be a way to defend ourselves.
『凡事只要秉公办理，就一定会有退步。

I suggest not reporting before we begin.
我想，开办之先，不必出奏；

Once the scheme has produced results, memorialise the throne with the amounts collected.
办得有了成效，再奏明收捐的数目，

Thereafter, notify the ministry directly for its records, providing the basis for the eventual accounting.”
以后直接咨部备案，作为将来报销的根据。』

“Good!
『好！

We shall do exactly that.”
准定这样办。』

Zuo was delighted. “‘Act impartially in the public interest, and there will always be a way to defend ourselves’—very well said.
左宗棠大为赞赏∶『「凡事只要秉公办理，就必有退步。」这话说得太好了。

But the results you mention are important too.
不过，你所说的「成效」也很要紧；

The state once kept more than ten million taels sealed in the inner treasury for major campaigns.
国家原有上千万的银子，经常封存内库，就为的是供大征伐之用。

That great sum was squandered by Saishang’a and his fellow Banner grandees.
这笔巨款，为赛尚阿之流的那班旗下大爷挥霍一空；

So the saying ‘The emperor does not send hungry soldiers on his errands’ no longer applies.
所以「皇帝不差饿兵」那句俗语，不适用了！

Now the court not only sends hungry soldiers and requires each province to find its own military funds; it also demands remittances for the capital’s military expenses.
如今朝廷不但差的是饿兵，要各省自己筹饷；而且要协解「京饷」。

If we establish a productive tax or contribution and are forbidden to continue it, very well: let the court assign us a reliable source for the same amount.”
如果说，我们办得有成效的税捐，不准再办；那好，请朝廷照数指拨一笔的款好了。』

That brought his argument to its conclusion.
这番话说到尽头了；

Hu now understood more deeply Zuo’s position, his thinking and his response to circumstances.
胡雪岩对左宗棠的处境、想法、因应之道亦由这番话中有了更深的了解。

Any method of raising military funds that was not utterly wicked could gain his approval.
只要不是伤天害理，任何筹饷的办法，都可以得到他的同意。


Hu remained at Zuo’s headquarters for two days before returning to Hangzhou.
胡雪岩在左宗棠行辕中盘桓了两天，才回杭州。

His homecoming was far more splendid than his departure.
归来的这番风光，与去时大不相同；

An escort of Zuo’s personal guards was only to be expected.
左宗棠派亲兵小队护送，自不在话下，

What surprised him most, outside Wulin Gate, was a welcoming party of distinguished-looking men.
最使他惊异的是，到了武林门外，发现有一班很体面的人在迎接，

More than half were active members of Hangzhou’s gentry, including Licentiate Zhang; the rest wore official robes, though Hu knew none of them.
一大半是杭州的绅干，包括张秀才在内；其余的都穿了官服，胡雪岩却一个都不认识。

A great green-covered sedan chair stood in the gateway.
此外，还有一顶绿呢大轿，放在城门洞里；

What was that for?
更不知作何用处？

Puzzled, he asked Officer He, “Have they come to meet me?”
胡雪岩颇为困惑，『是接我的吗？』他问何都司。

Before He could answer, the sight of Liu Bucai and young Zhang settled the question.
不用何都司回答，看到刘不才和小张；胡雪岩知道接自己是不错的了。

Zhang ran up beaming, caught the ring of his horse’s bridle and cried, “The news reached us the night before last!
果然，小张笑容满面地奔了上来。一把拉住马头上的嚼环，高声说道∶『这里前天晚上就得消息了！

We have awaited you like clouds and rain in a scorching drought.”
盼望大驾真如火旱之望云霓！』

What news?
是何消息？

Why such eagerness for his return?
盼望他回来又为何如此殷切？

Before Hu could ask, a guard officer bowed before his horse.
胡雪岩正待动问，却不待他开口；首先是一名武巡捕在马前打躬，

“Please dismount, Your Excellency, and take the sedan chair.”
同时说道∶『请胡大人下马，换大轿吧！』

Young Zhang hurried to explain.
『是这样的，』小张赶紧代为解释，

“Treasurer Jiang sent this officer.
『这是蒋方伯派来的差官；

The green-covered chair is his own, brought specially for you.”
绿呢大轿是蒋方伯自己用的，特为来伺候。』

“Yes.”
『是！』

The officer opened a visiting-card box and presented Jiang’s card and invitation.
那名武巡捕打开拜匣，将蒋益澧的一份名帖与一份请柬递了上来，

“My superior sent me to attend you.
『敝上派我来伺候胡大人；

He expressly instructed me to say that he intended to come himself, but several urgent matters require immediate decisions, and he cannot get away.
特为交代，本来要亲自来迎接，只为有几件紧要公事，立等结果，分不开身。

He asks to see you as soon as you arrive.
敝上又说∶』请胡大人一到就会个面，

There is much awaiting discussion.”
有好些事等着商量。

Hu understood.
『这一说胡雪岩明白了，

The “news” was his appointment as director of the reconstruction bureau.
小张所说的』消息『，是指他奉委为善后局总办一事；

Everyone’s eagerness, and Jiang’s insistence on seeing him at once, arose naturally from the urgency of relief.
大家如此殷切盼望，以及蒋益澧立等会面，当然是因为』万事莫如赈济急『，

The work of reconstruction awaited his decisions before it could begin.
一切善后事宜，都待他来作了决定，方能动手兴办。


Precisely for that reason, he thought it unwise to see Jiang first.
领会及此，他觉得不宜先跟蒋益澧见面。

Yet Jiang was now effectively the head of the province.
但此刻的蒋益澧。『等于一省长官，

To reject such attentive hospitality would be discourteous; only an excellent excuse would do.
这样殷勤相待，如果不领他的情，是件很失礼的事；必得找一个很好的借口才能敷衍得过去。

Hu thought quickly.
他的心思很快，

By the time he dismounted, his answer was ready.
下马之顷，已想好一套说词，』

“Please tell your superior that I too am anxious to pay my respects and seek his instructions on many matters.
拜烦回复贵上，『他说∶』我也急于要进见，有好些公事请示。

But after these days of travelling back and forth, I am disgracefully dirty.
不过，这几天来回奔波，身上脏得不成样子；

To visit a superior in this state would be disrespectful.
这样子去见长官，太不恭敬。

Let me wash and change into clean clothes, and I shall come at once.
等我稍为抹一抹身子，换一套干净衣服，马上就去。

His green-covered chair is not one I am entitled to ride in, but it would be rude to refuse his kindness.
贵上的绿呢大轿，不是我该坐的；不过却之不恭，

Please ask the bearers to follow me with it empty.”
请你关照轿班，空轿子跟着我去好了。

He went first to the Zhang house for a short rest and wrote an outline of the principal reconstruction measures and the support he needed from Jiang.
『于是先到张家暂息，将善后应办的大事，以及要求蒋益澧支持的事项，写了个大概，

Only then did he attend the banquet.
方始应约赴宴。

They met warmly.
相见欢然，

Jiang handed him the letter of appointment, and Hu took out a sheet of paper in return.
蒋益澧当面递了委札；胡雪岩便从身上掏出一张纸来，递了过去，

It was headed “Urgent Reconstruction Measures” and contained seven proposals.
上面写的是∶『善后急要事项』，

First: bury the dead within half a month.
一共七条∶第一、掩埋尸体，限半个月完竣。

Great epidemics followed great wars; this would both give the departed rest and prevent disease.
大兵之后大疫，此不仅为安亡魂，亦防疫疠。


Second: provide free porridge for six months.
第二、办理施粥，以半年为期。

The government should supply the grain, while impartial local gentlemen supervised its distribution.
公家拨给米粮，交地方公正绅士监督办理。

Third: invite merchants to bring in food, clothing, bricks, tiles, timber and other necessities, exempting these goods from likin to encourage trade.
第三、凡粮食、衣着、砖瓦、木料等民生必需品类，招商贩运，免除厘税，以广招徕。

Fourth: investigate those who had died loyally in the disaster, collect accounts of their deeds, and petition the throne for a shrine commemorating the loyal dead.
第四、访查殉难忠烈，采访事迹，奏请建立昭忠祠。

Fifth: trace the families of women rescued from the rebel camps and send them home with trustworthy escorts.
第五、贼营拔出妇女，访查其家，派妥人送回。

Sixth: make every effort to arrange spring cultivation, on which livelihoods in the coming autumn and winter depended.
第六、春耕关乎今年秋冬生计，应尽全力筹办。

Seventh: reopen the academies and give favourable treatment to scholars.
第七、恢复书院，优待士子。


“Quite right, quite right!” Jiang said.
『应该，应该！』蒋益澧说，

“I agree to everything.
『我无不同意。

If you need men, appointments or official papers, tell me, Mr Xueyan.
至于要人，或者要下委札，动公事，请雪翁告诉我，

Whatever lies within my power shall be done.”
只要力之所及，一定如命。』

“Thank you, Mr Xiang, for helping the people of Zhejiang.
『多谢芗翁成全浙江百姓。

But there is one matter on which I must ask your exceptional support.”
不过眼前有件事，无论如何要请芗翁格外支持。』

Hu spoke plainly. “The troops must be kept under discipline.”
胡雪岩率直说道∶『弟兄们的纪律一定要维持。』

Jiang reddened.
蒋益澧脸一红，

He knew his men’s discipline was poor, but offered a defence. “To be honest, the men have gone hungry for so long—” “Mr Xiang,” Hu interrupted, “I shall take responsibility for their pay.
他也知道他部下的纪律不好；不过，他亦有所辩解∶『说实话，弟兄们亦是饿得久了——。』『芗翁，』胡雪岩打断他的话说，『饷，我负责；

Please take responsibility for their discipline.”
军纪，请芗翁负责。』

Jiang reflected that Hu could now see Zuo directly and was said to have all his advice accepted.
蒋益澧心想，胡雪岩现在直接可以见左宗棠，而且据说言听计从；

If he raised the matter with their superior and an order came back down, Jiang would lose face.
倘或拿此事跟上面一说，再交下来，面子就不好看了。

Better to resolve it himself.
既然如此，不如自己下决心来办。

He therefore decided on two measures: a proclamation reaffirming military discipline, with offenders to be executed on the spot; and, from the next day, his own presence throughout the day at Guanxiangkou in the city centre to enforce military law personally.
于是他决定了两个办法∶一是出告示重申军纪，违者就地正法；二是他从第二天开始，整天坐镇杭州城中心的官巷口，亲自执行军法。

Discipline did indeed improve considerably, and reconstruction became easier to undertake.
这一来，纪律果然好得多了。善后事宜，亦就比较容易着手；

The burden fell on Hu, who slept fewer than three double-hours a day and lost several jin in weight.
只是苦了胡雪岩，一天睡不到三个时辰，身上掉了好几斤的肉，

Yet he remained vigorous and showed no fatigue.
不过始终精神奕奕，毫无倦容。


Zuo reached the provincial capital on the second day of the third month.
左宗棠是三月初二到省城的；

The first man he summoned after stepping from his chair was Hu.
一下了轿，约见的第一个人就是胡雪岩。

“Terrible!”
『惨得很！』

There was an unusually dejected look on his face.
左宗棠脸上很少有那样沮丧的颜色，

“Since the war began, I have visited many places, but never seen one devastated like Hangzhou.
『军兴以来，我也到过好些地方；从没有见过杭州这样子遭劫的！

What was its former population?”
以前杭州有多少人？』

“Eight hundred and ten thousand.”
『八十一万。』胡雪岩答说。

“And now?”
『现在呢？』

“A little over seventy thousand.”
『七万多。』

“A little over seventy thousand!” Zuo sighed.
『七万多？』左宗棠嗟叹着；

Then he looked up. “Mr Xueyan, you do not say eighty thousand or sixty thousand, but a little over seventy thousand.
忽然抬眼问道∶『雪翁，不说八万，不说六万，独说七万多；

On what evidence?”
请问何所据而云然？』

“It is an estimate, but not an idle guess.
『这是大概的估计。不过，亦不是空口瞎说。』

I have calculated it from the tokens issued at the porridge kitchens and the places selling grain at reduced prices.”
胡雪岩答道∶『是从各处施粥厂、平粜处发出的「筹子」算出来的。』

“Excellent!”
『好极！』

Zuo was full of praise.
左示棠大为嘉许，

“You truly combine great ability with care for detail.
『雪翁真正才大心细。

What do you consider the most urgent measures now?”
照你看，现在办善后，当务之急是哪几样？』


“Naturally, reviving trade.
『当务之急，自然是振兴市面；

A market can flourish only if people are willing to come and do business.
市面要兴旺，全靠有人肯来做生意；

Merchants are timid.
做生意的人胆子小，

If Your Excellency can make them confident enough to come to Hangzhou, trade will revive, the people will have a livelihood, and public revenue from likin will rise.
如果大人有办法让他们放心大胆地到杭州来，市面就会浴量，百姓有了生路，公家的厘金税收，亦会增加。

It will benefit both the government and the people enormously.”
于公于私，都有莫大的好处。』

“That simply means taking still greater pains to enforce discipline.
『这无非在整饬纪律四个字，格外下功夫，

Tell the merchants not to be afraid.
你叫商人不要怕，

Let them come and trade in Hangzhou.
尽管到杭州来做生意。

If they suffer any wrong, they may complain directly at my office.
如果吃了亏，准他们直接到我衙门来投诉；

I shall punish the offenders severely.”
我一定严办。』

“With that assurance, they will dare come.” Hu added, “Reconstruction covers a vast range of matters.
『有大人这句话，他们就敢来了。』胡雪岩又问，『善后事宜，千头万绪，包罗太广；

At present, relief and reassurance are the chief tasks.
目前以赈抚为主，

Might the reconstruction bureau be renamed the relief bureau?”
善后局是否可以改为赈抚局。』

“Yes.
『不错！

An excellent suggestion.”
这个意见很好。』

Zuo immediately issued a written instruction.
左宗棠随即下条子照办；

Everything else remained as before; only the name changed.
一切如旧，只是换了个名字。

The bureau’s troublesome, minute affairs consumed so much of Hu’s time that he could never find a suitable opportunity for another meeting with Zuo.
赈抚局的公事，麻烦而琐碎，占去了胡雪岩许多的功夫；以致想见一次左宗棠，一直找不到适当的时间。


Half a month passed in this way.
这样迁延了半个月，

Then the courier who had carried the special memorial announcing Hangzhou’s recovery returned from Beijing with good news for Zuo personally.
专折奏报克复杭州的折差，已由京里回到杭州，为左宗棠个人带来一个好消息，

“The Grand Secretariat has received an oral decree: Since taking charge of military affairs in Zhejiang, Zuo Zongtang, Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang, has successively recovered prefectural, subprefectural and county seats.
『内阁奉口谕∶闽浙总督左宗棠自督办浙江军务以来，连克各府州县城池。

He has now taken the provincial capital of Hangzhou and the county seat of Yuhang, demonstrating skilful direction.
兹复将杭州省城、余杭县城攻拔，实属调度有方。

As a special favour, he is granted the title of Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent and permission to wear the yellow riding jacket.”
着加恩赏太子少保衔；并赏穿黄马褂。』

Jiang Yili too received the yellow riding jacket. “Zuo Zongtang shall investigate the services of all officers and men involved and recommend the most deserving for reward.”
此外，蒋益澧亦赏穿黄马褂∶『所有在事出力将士，着左宗棠查明，择优保奉。』

When the news spread, every civil and military official in the city entitled to see the governor-general dressed in formal robes and went to headquarters to offer congratulations.
消息一传，全城文武官员，够得上资格见总督的无不肃具衣冠，到总督行辕去叩圆。

Wearing his new yellow jacket, Zuo received them in groups with generous words of encouragement.
左宗棠穿上簇新的黄马褂，分班接见，慰勉有加；

When he saw Hu kowtowing with the expectant circuit intendants, he specially instructed a guard to wait at the second hall and detain him.
看到胡雪岩随着候补道员同班磕头，特为嘱咐戈什哈等在二堂门口，将他留了下来。

After the guests and staff had dispersed, the two met in informal dress in the garden reception room.
等宾僚散尽，左宗棠在花厅与胡雪岩以便服相见。

Hu naturally congratulated him again.
一见少不得再次致贺；

Zuo spoke of the weight of imperial favour and how difficult it was to repay.
左宗棠自道受恩深重，对朝廷益难报称，

Then he apologised: the memorial recommending rewards for the recovery of Hangzhou was already drafted and about to be sent, but Hu’s name was not in it.
紧接着又向胡雪岩致歉，总克复杭州有功人员报奖，奏稿已经办好，即将拜发；其中并无胡雪岩的名字，

This first list was confined to those who had stormed the city.
因为第一次保案，只限于破城将士，

When the reconstruction staff were recommended later, Hu would certainly head the list.
以后奏保办理地方善后人员，一定将他列为首位。


Hu thanked him and briefly reported progress.
胡雪岩自然要道谢，同时简单扼要地报告办理善后的进展，

His guiding principles were “relief through work; revival through trade”, so that assistance to the needy also accomplished the work of restoration.
奉『以工代赈，振兴市面』八个字为宗旨，这样一方面办了赈济；一方面做了复旧的工作。

Zuo nodded repeatedly in approval and asked what difficulties he faced.
左宗棠不断点头，表示满意。然后问起胡雪岩有何困难？

“There are naturally too many to recount.
『困难当然很多，言不胜言，

I would not trouble Your Excellency with them.
也不敢麻烦大人；

Whatever I can manage, I shall attend to myself.
只要力所能及，我自会料理，请大人放心。

But without foresight, trouble soon comes close.
不过，人无远虑，必有近忧；

It is already late in the third month, and the hungry fifth and sixth months will be upon us before we know it.
如今已经三月下旬了，转眼「五荒六月』；

Every household must meet its immediate needs.
家家要应付眼前。

In the gap between the old crop and the new, people endure because they have something to hope for: the autumn harvest, which will let them repay their debts and pass the New Year.
青黄不接的当口，能够过得过去，都因为有个指望；指望秋天的收成，还了债好过年，

Your Excellency, that may be difficult this year.”
大人，今年只怕难了！

The words alerted Zuo.
『一句话提醒了左宗棠，

Startled, he rubbed his hands. “Yes!
悚然而惊，搓着手说∶』是啊！

The autumn harvest depends on spring cultivation.
秋收全靠春耕。

This is the time to transplant rice seedlings.
目前正是插秧的时候，

If we miss it, the consequences will be dreadful.”
如果耽误了，可是件不得了的事！

“With Your Excellency recognising that, the people of Zhejiang can be saved.”
『』大人说这话，两浙的百姓有救了。

“Do not think it so easy.
『『你不要看得太容易，

We must discuss it carefully.
这件事着实要好好商量。

Mr Xueyan, how should we encourage cultivation?”
雪翁，你看，劝农这件事，该怎么样做法？』


“Your Excellency has read the ancient books widely.
『大人古书读得多，

Every dynasty has known great disorder.
历朝历代，都有大乱；

You must remember clearly how they helped country people return to their fields afterwards.”
大乱之后，怎么帮乡下人下田生产，想来总记得明明白白？』

“Ah, yes.
『啊，啊，

A sound observation.
言之有理。』左宗棠说，

I do.
『我有，

The early Han handled this well.
这方面是汉初办得好，

Empress Dowager Bo followed the teachings of the Yellow Emperor and Laozi: quietness and non-interference.
薄太后的黄老之学，清静无为，

That truly allowed the people to recover.
才是真是与民休息。

I only wonder whether our present two empress dowagers can do as she did.”
