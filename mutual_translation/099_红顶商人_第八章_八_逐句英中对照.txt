胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第八章（本章第8部分，共9部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


“Perhaps I am mistaken.”
『只怕我想得不对。』

“You are not!” Zuo sighed.
『不会错！』左宗棠叹口气，

“I have thought the same all along, but refused to admit it to myself.
『我一直也是这么在想，不过不肯承认我自己的想法；

Li Shaoquan is, after all, a Hanlin scholar.
我总觉得李少荃总算也是个翰林，

His learning may amount only to a well-rehearsed Book of Songs, but he must understand loyalty to his sovereign and love of country.
肚子里的货色，虽只不过温熟了一部诗经，忠君爱国的道理总也懂的，

Having received such great favours, how could he disappoint his sovereign’s earnest wish to destroy this great bandit and bring peace to the empire?
而况受恩深重，又何忍辜负君父灭此大盗，以安四海的至意？

Now you have reached the same conclusion independently, which shows that we are both right.
如今你跟我的看法不约而同，就见得彼此的想法都不错。

Shaoquan is not the sort to flatter Zeng the Ninth merely for his own sake.
论少荃的为人，倒还不致巴结曾九；

But his teacher commands military affairs in five provinces and stands high in imperial favour.
只为他老师节制五省军务，圣眷正隆，

His own eagerness for advancement makes him yield and accommodate others.
不免功名心热，屈己从人。

As for Zeng the Ninth, though he cannot help openly, he will do all he can in secret, supplying money and arms.
至于他对曾九，虽不便明助，睹底下却要帮忙，助饷助械，尽力而为；

The recovery of Jinling cannot be far away.”
所以金陵克复的日子，仍旧不会远。』

“Yes.
『是的。

That is plain to see.
这是明摆在那里的事；

Jiangning is surrounded and outside help cut off.
江宁合围，外援断绝，

Once the food in the city is exhausted, the Longhairs are finished.
城里的存粮一完，长毛也就完了。

In my view, success must come around the turn of summer and autumn.”
照我看，总在夏秋之交，一定可以成功。』

“Then the trouble will begin.
『那时候就有麻烦了。

Look at this first.”
你先看着这个——。』


Zuo took a thick letter from inside his robe, perhaps ten or more sheets, examined it and selected two to hand to Hu.
说着左宗棠从怀中掏出一封信来，厚甸甸地，总有十来张信笺；他检视了一下，抽出其中的两张，递了给胡雪岩。

The two sheets concerned a single piece of news.
这两张信笺中，谈的是一件事；也就是报告一个消息。

The clerks in the Ministries of War and Revenue saw that the Hong-Yang rebellion would soon be suppressed.
说兵部与户部的书办，眼看洪杨肃清在即；

When military operations ended, the war accounts would have to be submitted for audit, and they were already congratulating themselves.
军务告峻，要办军费报销，无不额手相庆。

The Xiang and Huai armies had risen from the countryside; their commanders knew little of official regulations and could hardly know how military expenditure should be accounted for.
但以湘淮两军，起自田间，将领不谙规制，必不知军费应如何报销？

Someone had therefore organised clerks from both ministries to undertake the work as a package.
因而有人出头，邀约户兵两部的书办，商定了包揽的办法，

They would hire extra copyists, prepare brushes, ink and paper, and travel south specially to compile the armies’ accounts on the spot.
多雇书手，备办笔墨纸张；专程南下，就地为湘淮两军代办报销。

The commanders need trouble themselves with nothing, merely paying the customary “ministry fees”.
一切不用费心，只照例奉送『部费』即可。

The clerks considered this mutually beneficial and were certain both armies would welcome it.
在他们看，这是利人利己的两全之计，必为湘淮两军乐予接纳，

They had not only agreed on a plan but raised twenty thousand taels as capital.
所以不但已有成议，而且已经筹集了两万银子，作为『本钱』，

Their purchases of fine lianshi paper for the accounts alone had emptied the stocks of several paper shops in Liuli.
光是办购置造报销的连史纸，就将琉璃几家纸店的存货都搜空了。

“An ingenious arrangement,” Hu said, deliberately keeping his manner light.
『这个花样倒不错！』胡雪岩有意出以轻松的姿态，

“But the ministry fees will be substantial.
『不过这笔「部费」可观。

I dealt with them for the late Governor Wang.
我替殉节的王中丞经手过，

They amount to at least two per cent.”
至少要百分之二。』

“Exactly!” Zuo said.
『就是这话罗！』左宗棠说，

“That is what I wish to discuss.
『我要跟你商量的就是这件事。

I have spent seventy million taels in all.
我前后用过七千万的银子，

At the customary rate, I should have to pay two hundred thousand.
如果照例致送，就得二十万银子。

Leave aside where I could find such spare money: even if I had it, I would not stuff it down a dog’s hole.
哪里来这笔闲钱，且不去说它；就有这笔闲钱，我也不愿意塞狗洞。

Think of a way to put an end to this.”
你倒想个法子看，怎么样打消了它！』

“Putting an end to it is easy.
『打消是容易，

Send word declining their services.
放句话出去挡驾就是。

But afterwards?
可是以后呢？

The trouble may be endless.
恐怕不胜其烦了！

Auditing military expenditure is the most tedious business.
军费报销是最噜苏的事，

A single case can remain unsettled for years.
一案核销，有几年不结的。

Consider carefully, Your Excellency: is dealing with these men worth your precious time and energy?”
大人倒仔细想一想，宝贵的精神，犯得着犯不着花在跟这些人打交道上头？』


“No!”
『不！』

Zuo disagreed emphatically.
左宗棠大不以为然，

“I mean that there should be no audit at all.
『我的意思是，根本不要办报销。

Military accounts were scrutinised most strictly in the Qianlong reign, when the ministry clerks had the most tricks.
军费报销，在乾隆年间最认真；部里书办的花样也最多。

But those were different times.
不过此一时，彼一时，

Then, under another man’s eaves, one had to bow one’s head.
那时是「在人檐下过，不敢不低头」；

Why should I bow mine now?
如今我又何必低头？

The Ministry of Revenue has no right to demand my accounts.”
户部也没有资格跟我要帐！』

That was rather too high-handed.
这话说得太霸道了些。

The Xiang and Huai armies had indeed raised their own funds locally; the ministry had paid none of their expenses.
诚然，湘军和淮军的军费，都是在地方自筹，户部并没有支付过；

Yet whether the money came from likin or contributions, it was still public money.
但在地方自筹，不管是厘金、捐募，总是公款，

How could the ministry have no right even to ask for an account?
何致于户部连要个帐都没有资格？

Hu disagreed and remained silent.
胡雪岩不以左宗棠的话为然，因而沉默未答。

“Mr Xueyan,” Zuo pressed, “if you have an opinion, please enlighten me.”
『雪翁，』左宗棠催问着，『有何高见，请指教！』

Hu had to answer.
这就不能不回答了，

After a moment, he said, “This does not concern Your Excellency alone.”
胡雪岩想了一下答道∶『那不是大人一个人的事。』

“True.
『是啊！

But when the trouble comes, I cannot escape it.”
不过事情来了，我可是脱不了麻烦。』

“Even so, it will hardly be greater than the trouble facing the governor-general at Liangjiang.”
『就有麻烦，也不致于比两江来得大。』

Zuo understood.
这一说，左宗棠明白了，

“You mean we should prompt Grand Secretary Zeng to resist?”
『你的意思是，策动曾相去顶？』他问。

He meant Zeng Guofan, whose concurrent appointments as assistant grand secretary and governor-general of Liangjiang entitled him to be addressed as a chief minister.
这是指曾国藩，他以协办大学士兼领两江总督，也算入阁拜相，所以称之为『曾相』；

Hu nodded. “An argument presented by him seems more likely to succeed.”
胡雪岩正是此意，点点头答说∶『似乎以曾相出面去争，比较容易见效。』

“I have thought of that.
『我也想到过，

It is useless.
没有用。

Zeng fears slander and criticism; he is as timid as a mouse.
曾相忧谗畏讥，胆小如鼠；

He recently sent a confidential memorial asking the court to appoint another trusted minister to share his responsibilities.
最近还有密折，请朝廷另简亲信大臣，分任重责。

How could he disregard suspicion and request exemption from an audit?
你想，他怎么肯不避嫌疑，奏请免办报销？

Besides, the time is not yet ripe for such a memorial.”
何况时机亦还未到可以上折的时候？』

“That is the difficulty,” Hu said.
『难处就在这里。』胡雪岩说，

“The campaign is not over.
『军务究竟尚未告竣，

A premature request could only create needless complications.”
贸然奏请免办报销，反会节外生枝，惹起无谓的麻烦。』

“But we must remove the hidden danger now.
『可是消弭隐患，此刻就得着手。

Suppose the clerks win over the ministry’s departmental officers, then persuade its senior officials, and finally have the Grand Council present the matter to the two empress dowagers.
倘或部里书办勾结司员；然后说动堂官；再进而由军机奏闻两宫，

Once a decision is made, it will be hard to undo.”
一经定案，要打消就难了。』

Hu could not dismiss that concern.
胡雪岩觉得这番顾虑，决不能说是多余；

The words about clerks conspiring with departmental officers gave him an idea, and he answered without hesitation, “Then we might block the clerks at the first stage.”
而且由他的『书办勾结司员』这句话，触机而有灵感，不暇思索地答说∶『既然如此，不妨在第一关上就拿书办挡了回去。』

“Ah, ah!”
『喂，喂！』

Zuo pondered.
左宗棠一面想，

“There is something in that.
一面说，『你这话很有意味。

But how?”
然而，是如何个挡法呢？』


“The clerks cannot decide so great a matter themselves.
『这等大事，书办不能做主；

As you say, they must secure departmental support.
就如大人所说的，得要勾结司官。

An officer could throw cold water on the scheme from the outset, or seem to acquiesce and then speak out at the crucial moment.
司官给他们来盆冷水，迎头一浇；或者表面上敷衍，到紧要关头，挺身出来讲话，

If his argument is sound, the ministry’s senior officials cannot ignore it.”
只要有理，户部堂官亦不能不听。』

“True.
『话是有理。

But where shall we find a Revenue officer with the breadth of view and courage to do it?”
难在哪里去找这么一位明大体、有胆识的户部司官？』

“He need not possess either.
『不一定要明大体、有胆识。』

He need only see an advantage to himself.
胡雪岩答说，『只要这位司官，觉得这么做于他有利；

Then he will step forward.”
自然就会挺身而出。』

“Exactly!”
『着！』

Zuo again struck his thigh.
左宗棠又是猛拍自己的大腿，

“Your insight is a cut above others’, Mr Xueyan.
『雪翁，你的看法，确是高人一筹，

It clears away confusion.”
足以破惑。』

After a pause, he added, “You sound as though you have someone in mind already.”
略停一下，他又说道∶『听你的口气，似乎胸有成竹；已经想到有这么一个人了。』

“I do.
『是的。

A man from Hangzhou.”
就是杭州人。』

“A Hangzhou man?”
『杭州人，』

Zuo tilted his head.
左宗棠偏着头想，

“Who is serving as a departmental officer in Revenue?
『在户部当司官的是谁？

I cannot recall.”
我倒想不起来了。』

“He gained his metropolitan degree in the second year of Xianfeng and was assigned to Revenue.
『这个人是咸丰二年的进士，分发户部，

He began as a secretary and is now a director in charge of a departmental seal.
由主事做起，现在是掌印郎中了。

His name is Wang Wenshao.
他叫王文韶；

Have you heard of him?”
大人听说过此人没有？』

Zuo concentrated for a while, then remembered. “I seem to have heard him mentioned.
左宗棠凝神了一会，想起来了∶『似乎听人提起过。』他问，

Is his courtesy name Kuishi?”
『他的号，是叫夔石吗？』

“Yes.
『正是。

Wang Kuishi.”
王夔石。』

“What sort of man is he?
『此人怎么样？

Capable?”
很能干吧？』

“Very capable, and very smooth.
『很能干，也很圆滑；

He is well liked.
人缘不错。

Moreover, Shen Guifen, the ministry’s left vice-minister, was his examiner in the provincial examination and looks after this pupil.
加以户部左侍郎沈桂芬是他乡试的座师，很照应这个门生，

Wang is consequently well placed at Revenue.”
所以王夔石在户部很红。』

“If he is so smooth, he may not be willing to lead an argument.
『既然人很圆滑，只怕不肯出头去争！』左宗棠说，

Only a rather upright, outspoken man would do that.”
『这种事，只有性情比较耿直的人才肯做。』

“You are right.
『大人见得是。

But I do not propose urging him to argue openly.
不过，我的意思不是鼓动王夔石出头去力争，

I would ask him to work quietly behind the scenes.
是托他暗底下疏通。

For the sake of his own prospects, I think he would be willing.”
我想，为了他自己的前程，他是肯效劳的。』

“Why?
『何以见得？

Explain in detail, Mr Xueyan.”
雷翁，请道其详。』


In Hu’s view, a capital official who relied on himself rather than connections had two resources: learning or practical ability.
照胡雪岩的看法，做京官若说不靠关系靠自己，所可凭借者，不是学问，便是才干。

Hanlin scholars depended on learning, departmental officers on ability.
当翰林靠学问；当司官就要靠才干。

That meant the capacity to get things done, not some grand theory of government: when a practical difficulty arose, a man must produce a concrete solution, or at least find a way to get past it.
这才干是干济之才，不在乎腹有经纶，而是在政务上遇到难题，能有切切实实的办法拿出来。至少也要能搪塞得过去。

This was precisely Wang Wenshao’s strength.
王文韶之所长，正就是在此。

Yet in the capital, ability was a less useful resource than learning.
可是，做京官凭才干，实在不如凭学问。

A learned official advancing through the usual stages found constant opportunities to display his gifts.
因为凭学问做京官，循资推转，处处得以显其所长；

A Hanlin scholar could serve as a daily lecturer and recorder of the emperor’s activities, then enter the offices of the heir apparent and rise to senior metropolitan rank.
翰林做到兼日讲起注官，进而『开坊』升任京堂，

Such officials could submit memorials directly—another opportunity to show their learning.
都可以专折言事，更是卖弄学问的时候。

One memorial might catch the emperor’s attention and bring rapid advancement, even a red hat-button within three or four years.
也许一道奏疏，上结天知，就此飞黄腾达，三数年间便能戴上红顶子。

Practical ability offered no such convenient path.
而凭才干做官。就没有这样便宜了！

“Why?” Hu asked.
『为啥呢？

“Because a hero needs a field on which to use his weapons.
因为英雄要有用武之地。

Departmental officers handle official matters daily and wield considerable power to approve or reject.
做部里司官，每天公事经手，该准该驳，权柄很大；

There is a reason for approval and a reason for rejection.
准有准的道理，驳有驳的缘故，

Put the case correctly, and your ability shows.
只要说得对，自然显的的才干。

But you cannot remain a departmental officer all your life.
可是司官不能做一辈子；

Wang Kuishi has been a director for years.
象王夔石，郎中做了好多年了，

If he rises to a fourth-rank metropolitan post, he may go to the Court of State Ceremonial or the Office of Transmission.
如果升做四品京堂，那些鸿胪寺、通政司，

Those offices are like a deaf man’s ears: without them, the appearance is wrong; with them, they serve no purpose.
都是「聋子的耳朵」，没有它不象样子，有了它毫无用处。

However great Wang’s abilities, he cannot use them in an idle office with nothing to do.”
王夔石就有天大的本事，无奈冷衙门无事可做，也是枉然。』

Hu paused. “There is another route for departmental officers: examination for a censor’s post.
胡雪岩略停一下又说∶『司官推转，还有一条出路就是考御史；

But a censor’s work requires literary composition above all, and Wang cannot manage it.
当御史更是只要做文章 的差使，王夔石搞不来。

Nor is he a stern, impartial man.
而且他也不是什么铁面无情的人；

He always tries to avoid making enemies.
平时惟恐跟人结怨，

How could he be a censor?”
哪里好当什么都老爷？』

“I understand!” Zuo said.
『我懂了！』左宗棠说，

“He does not want to remain in the capital.
『王夔石是不愿做京官，

He wants a provincial appointment?”
只想外放？』

“Yes, as a prefect.
『是的。外放做知府；

If he does well, he can become a circuit intendant in two or three years.”
做得好，三两年就可以升道员。』

Hu smiled. “A provincial official must rely on governors and governors-general.”
胡雪岩笑笑说道∶『做外官，就要靠督抚了！』

Zuo now understood completely.
这一下，左宗棠一心领神会，彻底明了。

No one knew better than he how dependent provincial officials were on their superiors.
因为做外官靠督抚，没有比他更清楚的。

Under the Qing, governors and governors-general held great power.
清朝的督抚权重，

If they disliked a capital official appointed as a prefect or intendant, they had formerly been able to declare him unequal to his duties and request his return to the capital—effectively overturning the imperial appointment.
京官外转府道；督抚如果不喜此人，从前可以『才不胜任』的理由，奏请『请京任用』，等于推翻朝旨。

Early in the Qianlong reign, an edict had sharply rebuked this practice and forbidden its repetition.
乾隆初年，虽曾下诏切责，不准再有这样的事例；

Even so, provincial chiefs still had ways to prevent a man from taking up his post or to request his transfer.
可是督抚仍旧有办法可以不使此人到任，或者奏请调职。

For officials sent to a province merely to await vacancies, with no specific appointment, the timing and the richness or poverty of the eventual post lay still more completely in their hands.
至于未经指明缺分，只分省候补任用的，补缺的迟早；缺分的优瘠，其权更操之督抚。


If Wang Wenshao wished to serve outside the capital, he must therefore cultivate them.
因此可以想象得到，王文韶如果志在外官，就必得与督抚结缘；

Securing exemption from the audit of expenditure against the Hong-Yang rebellion was an opportunity that might never come again.
而能够设法搞成免办平洪杨的军费报销，正是可遇而不可求的良机。

Every Xiang and Huai commander would be grateful.
因为这一条，湘港将领，无不感戴；

Consider the provincial chiefs then in office: Zeng Guofan at Liangjiang, Zuo himself in Fujian and Zhejiang, Li Hongzhang in Jiangsu, Liu Changyou in Zhili, Luo Bingzhang in Sichuan, Guanwen in Huguang, Zhang Zhiwan in Henan, Shen Baozhen in Jiangxi, Yan Shusen in Hubei, and Guo Songtao in Guangdong.
而天下督抚，就眼前来说，两江曾国藩、闽浙是左宗棠自己、江苏李鸿章 、直隶刘长佑、四川路秉章 、湖广官文、河南张之万、江西沈荷桢、湖北严树森、广东郭嵩焘，

Every one had spent great sums on the war.
哪一个都花过大把银子的军费；

Exemption from an audit would leave each personally obliged to Wang.
能够免办报销，个人要见王文韶的情，

If he were assigned to their province, how could they fail to favour him?
等他分发到省，岂有不格外照应之理？

Half the knot in Zuo’s mind loosened.
想到这里，左宗棠心头的一个疙瘩，消减了一半，

“If Wang is truly capable, he should seize this chance to make friends with governors throughout the empire.”
『王夔石果然是能干的，就得好好抓住这个机会，普结天下督抚之缘。』

Then he recalled something surprising in Hu’s account.
他又回想了一下胡雪岩的话，发现有件事令人惊异，

“Have you ever been to Beijing, Mr Xueyan?”
便即问道∶『雪翁，你到京里去过没有？』


“Not yet.”
『还不曾过去。』

“That is strange.
『那就怪了！

You have never visited the capital and are half official, half merchant.
你没有上过京，又是半官半商，

How do you know so much about promotion through the metropolitan service?”
何以倒对京官的推迁升转，如此熟悉？』

“Originally I knew nothing.
『我本来也不懂。

I met Wang Kuishi in Shanghai the year before last, and we talked for several nights.
前年跟王夔石在上海见面，长谈了好几夜；

I heard it all from him.”
都是听他说的。』

“So that is it.
『原来如此！

Still, to explain the whole course of it so clearly is no small achievement.
不过能说得清源流，也很难得的了。』

Do you know him well?”
左宗棠又问∶『你跟王夔石很熟？』

“Yes, though we are not close friends.”
『是的。』胡雪岩又说，『不过并无深交。』

“Your conversations seem to have gone deeply into things.”
『看你们谈得倒很深。』

“Common interests can make a discussion profound.
『有利害关系，谈得就深了；

Friendship is another matter.
交情又另是一回事。

Wang has no great brilliance or ambition.
王夔石没有什么才气，也没有什么大志，

He is so smooth in dealing with people that he lacks sincerity.
做人太圆滑，未免欠诚恳。

I do not like him.”
我不喜欢这个人。』

These words suited Zuo’s taste and gave him a clearer understanding of Hu’s nature.
左宗棠觉得胡雪岩这几句话，颇对自己的胃口；同时对他的本性，也更为了解，

Here was a man with whom he could discuss great matters and share adversity.
确是个可以论大事、共患难的人。

He nodded repeatedly in silent approval.
因而不断点头，表示心许。


“Then Your Excellency would like me to write to Wang and ask him to do what he can?”
『大人的意思是，』胡雪岩问道∶『让我写封信给王夔石，请他从中尽力？』

“Yes.
『是的。

But I fear he cannot accomplish it alone.
我有这个意思。不过，我怕他一个人的力量不够；

If he goes knocking at every door and stirs up a public commotion, he will do harm rather than good.”
四处去瞎撞木钟，搞得满城风雨，无益有害。』

“His own strength is indeed insufficient, but he knows what is at stake.
『他一个的力量，诚然不够；不过事情的轻重，他是识得的。

He is cautious by nature, not a flighty man who will talk recklessly everywhere.
他的本性也是谨慎小心一路，决不致于飞扬浮躁，到处瞎说。

Since you mention it, I shall particularly stress secrecy.”
大人这样说，我信上格外关照，叫他秘密就是。』

“That would be best.”
『能这样最好。』

Zuo turned to his attendants. “Bring the Official Register.”
说到这里，左宗棠向左右吩咐∶『拿「缙绅」来！』

This was a directory printed by Beijing booksellers, its full title the Complete Register of Officials of the Great Qing.
缙绅是京师书坊刻的一部职官录，全名叫做『大清缙绅全书』。

Beginning with the Imperial Clan Court and continuing down to the subordinate and miscellaneous officials in every province, it gave brief records of every holder of an official title, from princes to men below the ranked service.
由『宗人府』开始，一直到各省的佐杂官儿，从亲王到未入流，凡是有职衔的，无不有简历记载。

Zuo wanted to examine the Ministry of Revenue.
左宗棠索取缙绅，是要查户部的职官。

At the head of that section he found: “Woren, Grand Secretary of the Wenyuan Pavilion, supervising the affairs of the Ministry of Revenue.”
翻到『户部衙门』这一栏，头一行是『文渊阁大学士管理户部事务倭仁』。

His face brightened immediately. “Good!
左宗棠顿时喜孜孜地说∶『行了！

There is hope of success.”
此事可望有成。』


“Oh?” Hu asked. “What have you discovered?”
『喔，』胡雪岩问道∶『大人参透了什么消息？』

“Grand Secretary Woren is a Mongol, but his family has long served in the Kaifeng garrison, so he is much like a Henan man.
『这倭相辊蒙古人。他家一直驻防开封；所以跟河南人没有什么两样。

Henan is a home of Neo-Confucian learning.
河南是讲理学的地方，

He follows every rule and convention; a little pedantic, perhaps, but an orthodox moralist through and through.
这倭相国规行矩步，虽然有点迂，倒是不折不扣的道学先生；

The late emperor valued him highly, and both empress dowagers respect him.
先帝对此人颇为看重，所以两宫太后亦很尊敬他，

If we can get the old gentleman to speak, the matter cannot fail.”
能得此老出头说话，事无不成之理。』

“May I tell Wang Kuishi this?”
『那末，』胡雪岩问道∶『这话可以不可以跟王夔石说？』

“He knows these circumstances far better than we do.
『这些情形，王夔石比我们清楚得多。

You may tell him or leave it unsaid.” Zuo added, “Woren and Zeng passed the metropolitan examination in the same year.
说亦可、不说亦可。』左宗棠又说，『这倭相国与曾相会试同榜；

Presumably he would help an old examination colleague.”
想来他亦肯帮帮老同年的忙的。』

“Then why not write to Grand Secretary Zeng and ask him to make a firm appeal to Woren?”
『既然如此，何不由大人写封信给曾相；结结实实托一托倭中堂？』

“That is one method.
『这也是一法。

But I fear Zeng may be too full of moral propriety to write such a letter.”
我怕曾相亦在道学气，未见得肯写这样的信。』

“Yes,” Hu said, while thinking differently.
『是！』胡雪岩口里答应着，心中另有盘算。

The affair was important, but did not concern him personally.
兹事体大，而不与自己相干。

Even Zuo need not be so anxious.
甚至左宗棠亦不必太关切；

If the sky fell, the tallest men would hold it up.
天塌下来有长人顶，

The Zeng brothers had spent several times as much on the war as Zuo.
曾氏弟兄所支销的军费比左宗棠所经手的，多过好几倍；

An audit would strike them first, and they would naturally seek a way to resolve the difficulty.
要办军费报销，曾氏弟兄，首当其冲，自然会设法疏通化解。

In advising Zuo, Hu need not be overzealous.
如今自己替左宗棠出主意，不须太起劲；

Better to avoid mistakes before trying to win credit.
不求有功，先求无过，最为上策。


This reflection made him more cautious.
这样转念，步子便踏得更稳了，

“For safety’s sake, we might first write to Wang, tell him the idea and ask his opinion.
『为求妥当，我看莫如这么办，先写信透露给王夔石，问问他的意思，

Can it be done?
看看能不能做得到？

If so, how should we begin?
要做，如何着手；

Let him send a brief outline.”
请他写个节略来！』

“Nothing could be better.
『这样做再好都没有。

But will he agree?”
可是，』左宗棠怀疑地问，『他肯吗？』

“Certainly.
『一定肯！

I have a favour on account with him.”
我有交情放给他。』

“Did you not say you were not close friends?”
『你不是说∶你们没有深交吗？』

Having a “favour on account” was an expression from the world outside official circles, and did not mean close friendship.
『放交情』是句江湖上的话，与深交有别，

Zuo did not understand it, so Hu explained. “I mean that Wang owes me a favour.
左宗棠不懂这句话，胡雪岩便只好解释∶『我是说，王夔石欠下我一个人情在那里；

If I ask him to do something, he will not begrudge the trouble.”
所以我托他点事，他一定不会怕麻烦。』

“Then that settles it.
『那就是了。

Success will benefit you as well.
此事能办成功，与你也有好处；

Zeng and Li Shaoquan will both be obliged to you.” Zuo laughed heartily.
曾相、李少荃都要见你的情。』说罢，左宗棠哈哈一笑。

There was something unfathomable in the laugh.
这一笑便有些莫测高深了。

People said this man liked to assert his own heroic superiority; naturally, he liked political manoeuvring too.
胡雪岩心想，大家都说此公好作英雄欺人之谈；当然也喜欢用权术。

Was he testing Hu with that remark and inexplicable laughter?
他说这话，又打这么一个莫名其妙的哈哈，莫非有什么试探之意在内？

On reflection, whether it was a test or not, Hu could use the opportunity to declare his intentions.
继而转念，不管他是不是试探？自己正不妨借此机会，表明心迹，

He answered seriously. “Your Excellency, I am different from Wang Kuishi.
因而正色说道∶『大人！我跟王夔石不同，

He seeks rapid advancement in office.
王夔石是想做官上头飞黄腾达；

I want to do business on a grand scale.
我是想做大生意。

When I look at myself in the mirror, I do not see the makings of an official.
因为自己照照镜子，不象做官的材料。

I therefore care nothing whether Grand Secretary Zeng or Governor Li feels obliged to me.
所以曾相跟李中函见不见我的情，我毫不在乎；

Even if they do, I shall not seek their favour.
他们见我的情，我亦不会去巴结他们的。

There is only one man whose favour I seek.”
如今，我倒是只巴结一个人！』

He paused deliberately to see Zuo’s response.
谈到这里，他有意停了下来，要看左宗棠是何反应？

Zuo naturally asked, with great interest, “Whose?”
左宗棠当然要问；而且是很关切地问∶『巴结谁？』

“Whose else but Your Excellency’s?
『还有谁？自然是大人。』

I seek your favour not to obtain office, but out of gratitude.
胡雪岩说，『我巴结大人，不是想做官，是报答。

First, you have saved Zhejiang, above all by recovering Hangzhou.
第一、大人是我们浙江的救星，尤其是克复了杭州；

When drinking water, one remembers its source.
饮水思源，

I can return home today, and Governor Wang’s spirit can rest in peace.
想到我今天能回家乡；王雪公地下有知，可以瞑目，

How could I not be grateful?
不能不感激大人。

Second, you have thought well of me and recognised my worth at our first meeting.
第二、承蒙大人看得起我，一见就赏识，

‘A man will die for one who understands him.’ Whose favour should I seek if not yours?”
所谓「士为知己者死」不巴结大人巴结谁？』

“Too much, too much!
『言重，言重！

My dear fellow, you flatter me excessively,” Zuo replied, his face wreathed in smiles.
你老哥太捧我了。』左宗棠笑容满面地回答。
