胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第八章（本章第9部分，共9部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


『这是我的真心话。
“Those are my true feelings.

大人想来看得出来。』胡雪岩又说，
I believe Your Excellency can see that,” Hu continued.

『除此以外，我当然也有我的打算，
“Beyond that, naturally, I have ambitions of my own.

很想做一番事业，
I want to build something substantial.

一个人如果要想有所成就，一半靠本事；一半靠机会。
If a man is to achieve anything, half depends on ability and half on opportunity.

遇见大人就是我的一个机会；
Meeting you is an opportunity for me.

当然不肯轻易放过。』
Naturally I am unwilling to let it pass lightly.”

『你的话很老实，
“You speak honestly.

我就是觉得象你这路性情最投缘。
It is precisely your kind of nature that I find congenial.

你倒说与我听听，你想做的是什么事业？』
Tell me what sort of enterprise you wish to build.”

这一问，很容易回答；容易得使人会觉得这一问根本多余。
That was an easy question—so easy that it might seem unnecessary.

但照实而言，质直无味；
Yet a plain answer would be flat and tasteless.

胡雪岩虽不善于词令，却以交了嵇鹤龄这个朋友，学到了一种迂回的说法，有时便觉俗中带雅。
Hu was no master of rhetoric, but his friendship with Ji Heling had taught him a roundabout manner of expression that sometimes lent elegance to ordinary thoughts.

好在他的心思快，敏捷可济腹笥的不足；
Fortunately, his quick mind could make up for his limited store of learning.

此时想到一个掌故，大可借来一用。
An anecdote occurred to him that he could put to good use.


『大人总晓得乾隆皇帝南巡，在镇江金山寺的一个故事？』
“Your Excellency must know the story of the Qianlong Emperor at Jinshan Temple in Zhenjiang during his southern tours?”

左宗棠笑了。
Zuo smiled.

笑的原因很复杂，笑的意味，自己亦不甚分明。
His reasons were complicated, and even he could not have defined everything in that smile.

不称『高宗』或者『纯庙』，而说『乾隆皇帝』是一可笑；
To call the ruler “the Qianlong Emperor” instead of “Gaozong” or “Emperor Chun” was one cause for amusement.

乾隆六次南巡，在左宗棠的记忆中，每次都驻驾金山寺，
Qianlong had made six southern tours and, as Zuo remembered it, stayed at Jinshan Temple each time.

故事不少，却不知指的是哪一个？
There were many stories; which did Hu mean?

是二可笑∶『铜钱眼里翻跟斗』的胡雪岩，居然要跟他谈南巡故事，那就是三可笑了。
That was a second cause. Third, here was a man who spent his life turning somersaults through the hole in a copper coin, proposing to discuss imperial tours with him.

可笑虽可笑，不过左宗棠仍持着宽容的心情；
Amusing as it was, Zuo remained indulgent.

好比听稚龄童子说出一句老气横秋的『大人话』那样，除笑以外，就只有『姑妄听之』了。
It was like hearing a small child utter a solemn grown-up remark: one could only smile and hear him out.

『你说！』
“Go on.”

他用一种鼓励的眼色，表示不妨『姑妄言之』。
His encouraging glance invited Hu to tell his tale.

胡雪岩当然不会假充内行，
Hu naturally did not pretend to expertise.

老老实实答道∶『我也不晓得是哪一年乾隆皇帝南巡的事？
“I do not know in which year of the southern tours it happened.

我是听我的一个老把兄谈过，觉得很有意思，所以记住了，
An older sworn brother told me, and I found it interesting enough to remember.

据说——。』据说∶有一次乾隆与金山寺的方丈，在寺前闲眺，遥望长江风帆点点；
They say—” They said that once Qianlong and the abbot were standing before the temple, gazing idly at the sails scattered over the Yangtze.

乾隆问方丈∶江中有船几许？
The emperor asked how many boats were on the river.

方丈答说∶只有两艘，
“Only two,” the abbot replied.

一艘为名；一艘为利。
“One for fame, one for gain.”

这是扬州的盐商，深知乾隆的性情，特意延聘善于斗机锋的和尚，承应皇差的佳话。
The story arose from the Yangzhou salt merchants’ knowledge of Qianlong’s temperament: to receive the imperial party, they had specially engaged monks skilled in witty exchanges.

只是传说既久，变成既俗且滥的一个故事；
Long repetition, however, had made the anecdote commonplace and stale.

胡雪岩引此以喻，左宗棠当然知道他的用意，
Zuo naturally supposed he knew Hu’s point.

是说他的事业，只是『做大生意』图利而已。
His enterprise was merely business on a grand scale, pursued for profit.


然而，他没有想到，胡雪岩居然另有新义，
But Hu unexpectedly gave it a new meaning.

『照我说，那位老和尚的话，也不见得对。』
“In my view, the old monk was not necessarily right.”

胡雪岩很起劲地举手遥指∶『长江上的船，实在只有一艘，既为名，亦为利！』
He lifted a hand and pointed into the distance with animation. “There is really only one boat on the Yangtze: it sails for both fame and gain.”

『噢！』
“Oh!”

左宗棠刮目相看了，
Zuo looked at him with fresh respect.

『何以见得？』
“How do you make that out?”

『名利原是一样东西。』
“Fame and gain are really the same thing.”

胡雪岩略有些不安地，
Hu seemed a little uneasy.

『大人，我是瞎说。』
“Your Excellency, I am only talking at random.”

这比『既然为名，亦为利』，企求兼得的说法，又深一层了。
This went deeper than saying a boat pursued both fame and gain, hoping to obtain the two together.

左宗棠越感兴味；正待往下追问时，但见听差悄悄掩到他身边，
Zuo was increasingly interested and about to press him when an attendant slipped quietly to his side.

低声问道∶『是不是留胡老爷便饭？』
“Will Mr Hu stay for a meal?”

『当然。』
“Of course.

左宗棠问道∶『什么时候了？』
What time is it?”

『未正！』
“The middle of the wei hour.”

未正就是午后两点，
That meant two in the afternoon.

左宗棠讶然，
Zuo was surprised.

『一谈谈得忘了时候了。』他歉然地问，『雪翁，早饿了吧？』
“We have talked until I forgot the time.” He asked apologetically, “You must have been hungry for a while, Mr Xueyan?”

『大人不提起，倒不觉得饿。』
“Until you mentioned it, I did not notice.”

『是啊！
“Nor did I.

我亦是谈得投机，竟尔忘食。
Our conversation was so congenial that I forgot to eat.

来吧，我们一面吃，一面谈。』
Come, we can talk over the meal.”


于是午饭就开在花厅里。
Lunch was served in the garden reception room.

左宗棠健于饮啖，但肴馔量多而质不精；
Zuo ate and drank heartily, but his dishes were abundant rather than refined.

一半是因为大劫以后，百物皆缺，亦无法讲求口腹之欲，
Part of the reason was the general shortage after the catastrophe; there was little scope for indulging the palate.

席中盛馔，不过是一大盘红辣椒炒子鸡。
The finest dish was a great platter of young chicken stir-fried with red chillies.

再有一小碟腊肉；
There was also a small plate of cured pork.

胡雪岩知道是左宗棠的周夫人，远自湖南寄来的，
Hu knew it had been sent all the way from Hunan by Madam Zhou.

客人非吃不可，而且非盛赞不可，所以下箸便先挟腊肉。
A guest must eat it, and praise it lavishly, so it was the first thing he took with his chopsticks.

腊肉进口，左宗棠顾不得听他夸赞周夫人的贤德，急于想重拾中断的话题，
Before he could properly commend her virtue, Zuo hurried back to their interrupted discussion.

『雪翁，』他说，『你说名利原是一样东西，这话倒似乎没有听人说过；
“Mr Xueyan, I do not think I have heard anyone say before that fame and gain are the same thing.

你总有一番言之成理的说法吧？』
You must have a reasoned explanation.”

『我原是瞎说。』胡雪岩从容答道∶『我常在想，人生在世应该先求名、还是先求利？
“I was only speaking casually,” Hu answered calmly. “I often wondered whether a man should seek fame first or profit.

有一天跟朋友谈到这个疑问，
One day I put the question to a friend.

他说∶别的我不知道，做生意是要先求名，
‘I know nothing of other matters,’ he said, ‘but in business reputation must come first.

不然怎么叫「金字招牌」呢？
Otherwise, why do we speak of a golden signboard?’

这话大有道理，
That made excellent sense.

创出金字招牌，自然生意兴隆通四海，
Once you establish such a reputation, business naturally flourishes far and wide.

名归实至。
Fame comes, and substance follows.

岂非名利就是一样东西？』
Does that not make fame and gain the same thing?”

『你把实至名归这句话，颠倒来说，倒也有趣。』左宗棠又问，
“An interesting reversal of the saying ‘Substance comes, and fame follows’,” Zuo remarked.

『除了做买卖呢？别处地方可也能用得上你这个说法不能？』
“But apart from trade, can your argument be applied elsewhere?”


『也有用得上的。
“In some cases.

譬如读书人，名气大了，京里的大老，都想收这个门生，
Take a scholar: once he becomes famous, the great men in the capital all want him as a pupil.

还不曾会试，好象就注定了一定会点翰林似的。』
Before he even sits the metropolitan examination, his selection for the Hanlin Academy seems assured.”

说到这里，胡雪岩记起左宗棠数上春官，铩羽而归，
At this point Hu remembered Zuo’s repeated failures in that examination.

至今还是一个举人，所以听见人谈中进士、点翰林，心里便酸溜溜地不好受；
He still held only the provincial degree, and talk of metropolitan success and Hanlin appointments left him with a sour feeling.

自己举这个例，实在不合时宜。
The example was ill chosen.

好在他的机变快，就地风光，恰有一个极好的例子可举。
Fortunately, Hu’s wits were quick, and an excellent alternative lay immediately before him.

『再譬如大人。』
“Take Your Excellency yourself.

他说，『当年我们远在浙江，就听说湖南有位「左师爷」，真正了不起！
Years ago, far away in Zhejiang, we heard of an extraordinary ‘Adviser Zuo’ in Hunan.

大人名满天下，连皇上都知道，
Your name was known throughout the empire, even to the emperor.

跟贵省的一位翰林说∶叫左某人出来给我办事。
He told a Hanlin scholar from your province, ‘Tell that fellow Zuo to come and work for me.’

果不其然，不做官则已，一做便是抚台。
And so it proved: either you held no office, or you became governor at once.

从来初入仕途，没有一下子就当巡抚的；
No one begins official life as a provincial governor.

大人的恩遇，空前绝后。
The favour shown you was without precedent and will never be repeated.

这也就是名归实至的道理。』
That too is fame bringing substance in its wake.”

这顶高帽子套在左宗棠头上，顿时使他起了与天相接之感，
The compliment lifted Zuo’s spirits to the heavens.

仿佛在云端里似的，飘飘然好不轻快！
He seemed to float among the clouds, light and exhilarated.

不自觉地拈着花白短髭，引杯笑道∶『虽蒙过奖，倒也是实情。
Unconsciously stroking his short, greying moustache, he raised his cup with a smile. “You praise me too highly, but it is true.

一介举人而入仕便是封疆大吏，这个异数，老夫独叨，
To begin with only a provincial degree and enter office as a provincial chief—mine alone has been that exceptional fortune.

足令天下寒儒吐气！
It should hearten every poor scholar in the empire.

雪翁，来，来，我敬你一杯！』
Come, Mr Xueyan, I drink to you!”

就这杯酒交欢之间，左宗棠与胡雪岩的情谊又加深了；深到几乎可以推心置腹的地步。
With that friendly cup, their intimacy deepened almost to the point of complete confidence.

因而说话亦越发无所隐讳顾忌。
Their conversation accordingly grew freer still.

谈到咸丰曾向湖南一位翰林表示，『叫左某人出来给我办事』时；胡雪岩问说，这位翰林可是现任广东巡抚郭嵩焘？
Returning to the Hunan scholar whom the Xianfeng Emperor had told to summon Zuo, Hu asked whether he was Guo Songtao, now governor of Guangdong.

『正是他！』
“The very man!”

左宗棠的声音不自觉地高了，似乎有些激动似的。
Zuo’s voice rose involuntarily, as though stirred by strong feeling.


这使得胡雪岩不免困惑。
Hu was puzzled.

因为他曾听说过，郭嵩焘救过左宗棠；
He had heard that Guo had once saved Zuo.

对于己有恩的故交，出之以这种的异样口吻，听来真有些刺耳。
Such an unusual tone towards an old friend and benefactor sounded distinctly jarring.

左宗棠也是善于察言观色的人；而且心里也有牢骚要吐，
Zuo too was adept at reading expressions, and had grievances he wished to air.

所以很快地接下来问∶『他跟我的渊源，想来你总知道？』
He quickly asked, “You presumably know the connection between us?”

『知道得不多。』
“Not much of it.”

『那么，我来说给你听。
“Then let me tell you.

是咸丰八年的事——。』咸丰八年春天，湖南永州镇总兵樊燮，贪纵不法，
It happened in the eighth year of Xianfeng—” In the spring of that year, Fan Xie, brigade-general of Yongzhou in Hunan, had been greedy, lawless and unrestrained.

又得罪了势焰熏天的『左师爷』，
He had also offended the immensely powerful “Adviser Zuo”.

因而为左宗棠主稿上奏，严劾樊燮，
Zuo accordingly drafted a severe impeachment.

拜折之时，照例发炮；
When the memorial was dispatched, a cannon was fired according to custom.

骆秉章坐在签押房里听见声音，觉得奇怪。看时候不是午炮，然则所为何来『听差的告诉他说∶』左师爷发军报折。
Luo Bingzhang heard it from his office and wondered: it was not the time for the noon gun, so what was happening? An attendant told him, “Adviser Zuo is sending a military memorial.”

『左宗棠在路秉章 幕府中，一向这样独断独行；因而又有个外号叫『左都御史』——
Zuo had always acted with such independence in Luo’s secretariat, earning another nickname, “Left Censor-in-Chief”.

巡抚照例挂两个衔∶一个是兵部右侍郎，便于管辖武官；一个是右副都御史，便于整饬吏治，参劾官吏。
Governors routinely carried two additional titles: Right Vice-Minister of War, to give them authority over military officers, and Right Deputy Censor-in-Chief, to allow them to discipline and impeach officials.

而『左师爷』的威权高过骆秉章 ，称他『左都御史』是表示右副都御史得要听他的。
Since Adviser Zuo’s power exceeded Luo’s, calling him “Left Censor-in-Chief”—a play on his surname—implied that the official on the right had to obey him.

这一次参劾樊燮，骆秉章 事前亦无所闻；
Luo had heard nothing beforehand of the impeachment of Fan.

此时才要了奏折来看，
Now he asked to see the memorial.

措词极其严厉，但也不是无的放矢，
Its language was severe, but the charges were not groundless.

譬如说樊燮『目不识丁』，便是实情。
The claim that Fan could not read a single character, for example, was true.

既已拜折，没有追回来的道理，
Since the memorial had already been sent, there was no recalling it.

也就算了。
Luo let the matter rest.


其时朝廷正倚任各省带兵的督抚，凡有参劾，几乎无一不准；
At the time, the court relied heavily on provincial chiefs commanding armies, and almost every impeachment they submitted was accepted.

樊燮就此革了职。
Fan therefore lost his post.

只以左宗棠挟有私怨，大为不服；便向湖广总督衙门告了一状，又派人进京向都察院呈控，
Convinced that Zuo had acted from a private grudge, he indignantly lodged a complaint with the governor-general of Huguang and sent someone to Beijing to petition the Censorate.

告的是左宗棠，也牵连到路秉章 ，说湖南巡抚衙门是『一官两印』。
His charge was against Zuo, but implicated Luo as well: the Hunan governor’s office, he said, had “one post and two seals”.

这是大案，当然要查办。
So grave a case naturally required investigation.

查办大员一个是湖广总督官文；另外一个是湖北乡试的主考官钱定青。
The commissioners appointed were Guanwen, governor-general of Huguang, and Qian Dingqing, chief examiner of the Hubei provincial examination.

官文左右已经受了樊燮的赌；形势对左宗棠相当不利。
Guanwen’s attendants had already accepted Fan’s bribes, and matters looked unfavourable for Zuo.

幸亏湖北巡抚胡林翼，与官文结上一层特殊的关系——官文的宠妾是胡老太太的义女；
Fortunately, Hu Linyi, governor of Hubei, had established a special family connection with Guanwen: the latter’s favourite concubine was the adopted daughter of Hu’s mother.

所以连官文都称胡林翼为『胡大哥』。
Even Guanwen therefore addressed Hu as “Brother Hu”.

这位胡老太太的义女，常对官文说∶『你什么都不懂！
The adopted daughter often told Guanwen, “You understand nothing.

只安安分分做你的官，享你的福；
Just hold your office quietly and enjoy yourself.

什么事都托付给胡大哥，包你不错。』
Leave everything to Brother Hu, and you cannot go wrong.”

官文亦真听她的话；
Guanwen genuinely listened to her.

所以胡林翼得以从中斡旋，极力排解，帮了左宗棠很大的一个忙。
Hu Linyi could therefore mediate and work hard to settle the affair, giving Zuo great assistance.


『总而言之，郭筠仙平地青云，
“In short, Guo Yunxian has soared into the clouds.

两年之间，因缘时会，得任封疆，
Within two years, a fortunate conjunction of circumstances has made him a provincial chief.

其兴也暴；
His rise has been abrupt.

应该虚心克己，以期名实相称。
He should be modest and self-disciplined, striving to make his qualities match his reputation.

不然，必成笑柄；甚至身败名烈！
Otherwise he will become a laughing-stock, perhaps even ruin himself utterly.

我甚为筠仙危。』
I am very anxious for him.”

说到这里，左宗棠忽然忍俊不禁了，
At this point, Zuo suddenly could not contain his amusement.

『曾相道貌俨然，出语亦有很冷隽的时候了。
“Grand Secretary Zeng looks so solemnly moral, but sometimes his remarks have a wonderfully dry edge.

前几天有人到营里来谈起，说郭筠仙责备「曾涤生平生保人甚多，
Someone came to camp a few days ago and reported Guo’s complaint: ‘Zeng Disheng has recommended many men in his life.

可惜错保了一个毛寄云」。
Unfortunately, one of them was the ill-chosen Mao Jiyun.’

这话传到曾相耳里，你道他如何？』
When Zeng heard it, what do you think he did?”

『以曾相的涵养，自然付之一笑？』
“With his self-command, surely he merely smiled?”

『不然。
“Not at all.

曾相对人说∶』毛寄云平生保人亦不少，
He said, ‘Mao Jiyun has also recommended many men in his life.

可惜错保一个郭筠仙！
Unfortunately, one of them was the ill-chosen Guo Yunxian.’

「针锋相对，妙不可言。
Point for point—exquisite!”

『左宗棠说完大笑。
Zuo burst out laughing.

胡雪岩亦不由得笑了；一面笑一面心里在想，郭嵩焘做这个巡抚，可说四面受敌，
Hu laughed too, reflecting that Guo was assailed on all sides in his governorship.

亏他还能撑得下去！
Remarkable that he could hold on!

看起来是一条硬汉；有机会倒要好好结识。
He seemed a tough man, worth getting to know if the opportunity arose.

左宗棠却不知怎么，笑容尽敛，忧形于色，』
But for some reason Zuo’s smile vanished, and anxiety appeared.

雪翁，『他说，』我有时想想很害怕！
“Mr Xueyan, sometimes the thought frightens me.

因为孤掌难鸣。
One hand cannot clap.

论天下之富，苏、广并称，都以海关擅华洋之利。
For wealth, Jiangsu and Guangdong stand together, each drawing riches from Chinese and foreign trade through its maritime customs.

如今江苏跟上海有曾、李；广东又为曾氏兄弟饷源。
Now Zeng and Li have Jiangsu and Shanghai, while Guangdong supplies funds to the Zeng brothers.

郭筠仙虽然官声不佳，但如金陵一下，曾老九自然要得意；
Guo’s official reputation may be poor, but once Jinling falls, Zeng the Ninth will naturally be pleased with himself.

饮水思源，以筹饷之功，极力维持郭筠仙，亦是意中之事。
Remembering where his resources came from, he can be expected to support Guo vigorously for his fundraising.

照此形势，我的处境就太局促了！
In that situation, I shall have very little room to move.

雪翁，你何以教我？『
What advice can you give me?”


这番话，左宗堂说得很郑重，很深；
Zuo spoke gravely and with profound intent.

胡雪岩亦听得很用心，很细。
Hu listened just as carefully.

话外有话、意中有意；
There was meaning beneath the words, purpose beneath the thought.

是有关左宗棠的前程，也可能有关自己利害的一件大事，
This was a great matter touching Zuo’s future and perhaps Hu’s own interests.

不宜也不必遽尔回答，
There was neither need nor reason to answer hastily.

便以同样严肃的神色答道∶『大人看得很远；
With equal gravity, he replied, “Your Excellency looks far ahead.

要让我好好想一想，才能奉答。』
I must think carefully before answering.”
