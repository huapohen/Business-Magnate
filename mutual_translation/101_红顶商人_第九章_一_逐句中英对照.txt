胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第九章（本章第1部分，共7部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第九章
CHAPTER NINE


局势的发展，实在出人意表。
Events developed in unexpected ways.

第一、常州在李鸿章 部下郭松林、刘铭传、周盛波、张树声、李鸿章 及常胜军戈登合力猛攻之下，于四月初六十复；
First, on the sixth day of the fourth lunar month, Changzhou was recovered after a concerted assault by Li Hongzhang, his subordinate commanders Guo Songlin, Liu Mingchuan, Zhou Shengbo, and Zhang Shusheng, and Gordon’s Ever-Victorious Army.

接着久守镇江的冯子材进克丹阳。
Feng Zicai, who had long held Zhenjiang, then advanced and took Danyang.

大家都以为这两支军队会师以后，一定乘胜西趋，直扑金陵，为曾国荃助攻。
Everyone assumed that once the two armies joined they would follow up their victories, turn west, and march straight on Jinling to assist Zeng Guoquan.

哪知李鸿章 尽管朝旨催促，却以伤亡过重，亟须整补为名，按兵不动。
Yet despite repeated orders from the court, Li Hongzhang remained where he was, pleading heavy casualties and an urgent need to replenish and reorganize his forces.

这是为左宗棠、胡雪岩所预料到的，
Zuo Zongtang and Hu had foreseen this.

李鸿章 不愿分曾国荃一心想独到的大功，有意作态。
Li did not wish to share the great achievement that Zeng Guoquan was determined to claim alone; his posture was deliberate.

第二、是『天王』洪秀全忽然下了一道有如梦呓的『诏令』，说『即上天堂，向天父天兄，领到天兵，保固天京』。
Second, the “Heavenly King,” Hong Xiuquan, suddenly issued a dreamlike decree: he would “ascend forthwith to Heaven, obtain heavenly troops from the Heavenly Father and Heavenly Elder Brother, and secure the Heavenly Capital.”

过了两天，『天王』服毒自尽，实现了他『上天堂』的诺言。
Two days later he poisoned himself, fulfilling his promise to “ascend to Heaven.”

接位的是洪秀全的十六岁儿，名叫『洪天贵福』；称号唤做『幼天王』。
His successor was his sixteen-year-old son, Hong Tianguifu, styled the “Young Heavenly King.”


消息外传，都知道曾国荃成大功在即，
As the news spread, everyone knew that Zeng Guoquan’s great triumph was near.

颇有人高吟杜少陵的『青春作伴好还乡』，作乱后重整家园之计。
Some recited Du Fu’s line, “With spring for company, how gladly I shall journey home,” and began planning to rebuild their homes after the war.

而京里重臣、京外督抚，有良心，肯做事的，亦都在默默打算，
Conscientious and industrious ministers in the capital and governors in the provinces were also quietly making calculations.

曾国荃一下金陵，太平天国十余年的积聚，尽萃于『天王府』，足可用来裁遣将士，恢复地方；
Once Jinling fell, the accumulated wealth of more than a decade of the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom, concentrated in the Heavenly King’s Palace, should suffice to discharge soldiers and restore the devastated country.

固然，金陵所得，必是用于江南及湘军，但应解的协饷，可以不解，就等于增加了本地的收入。
Admittedly, the spoils of Jinling would be used for the southern Yangtze region and the Hunan Army; nevertheless, if other provinces no longer needed to send their military contributions, the savings would be equivalent to increased local revenue.

象左宗棠就是打着一把如意算盘，认为曾国荃一克金陵，广东便将复成浙江的饷源。
Zuo, for example, had an agreeable calculation of his own: once Zeng Guoquan took Jinling, Guangdong would again become a source of military funds for Zhejiang.

他曾跟胡雪岩谈过，到那时候，要专折奏，派他到广东去会办厘捐。
He had told Hu that when the time came he would submit a special memorial requesting Hu’s appointment to assist in administering the likin levy there.

胡雪岩口头一诺无辞，其实不当它一回事；
Hu had agreed without demur, but privately attached little importance to the scheme.

在他看来，此事渺茫得很，
To him the prospect was extremely uncertain.

只是不便扫左宗棠的兴，所以只是唯唯敷衍而已。
Unwilling to spoil Zuo’s pleasure, he merely assented politely.


在李鸿章 所拨借的炮队协攻之下，曾国荃所部在五月底攻占了『龙膊子』，
With supporting fire from artillery lent by Li Hongzhang, Zeng Guoquan’s troops captured Longbozi at the end of the fifth month.

其地在江宁城外东北的钟山之巅，居高临下，俯瞰全城。
It stood on the summit of Zhongshan, northeast of Jiangning, commanding a view over the whole city.

此地一失，『忠王』李秀成束手无策了。
With this position lost, the Loyal King, Li Xiucheng, was at his wits’ end.

曾国荃用兵，独得一『韧』字；
Zeng Guoquan’s art of war rested upon a single quality: persistence.

苦苦围困到这般地步，要韧出头了，更不肯丝毫怠慢，
Having maintained the bitter siege until persistence was about to triumph, he was less willing than ever to relax.

下令各营，由四面收束，直往里逼，逼近城下，昼夜猛攻。
He ordered every battalion to tighten the ring on all sides, press up to the walls, and attack furiously day and night.

而真正的作用是，借无时或已的炮声，遮掩他掘地道的声响。
The true purpose, however, was to use the incessant cannon fire to conceal the sound of tunneling.

金陵围了两年，曾国荃从朝阳门到钟阜门，挖过三下多处地道，
During the two-year siege of Jinling, his men had dug more than thirty tunnels between Chaoyang Gate and Zhongfu Gate.

有时是『落磐』，挖地道的士兵随死随埋，丛葬其中；
Sometimes the roofs collapsed; the diggers died and were buried where they fell, together in the tunnels.

有时是为长毛所发觉，烟熏水浇，死者论百计。
Sometimes the rebels discovered them and sent in smoke or water, killing men by the hundred.

有一次快成功了，地道内的士兵，忽然发现一枝长矛刺了下来；
Once, when a tunnel was nearly complete, the men inside suddenly saw a spear thrust down from above.

其实是长毛行军休息，随意将矛一插，
In fact, a rebel had merely planted it in the ground while resting on the march.

而官军轻躁没脑筋，使劲将那枝矛往下拉，
But the imperial soldiers, rash and thoughtless, seized it and pulled hard.

长毛始而大骇，继而大喜，掘地痛击，
First alarmed, then delighted, the rebels dug down and attacked.

功败垂成，死了四百人之多；都是朱洪章的部下。
The enterprise failed at the very brink of success, and more than four hundred men died, all from Zhu Hongzhang’s command.


朱洪章 是贵州人，也是曾国荃部下高级将领中，唯一的非湖南人。
Zhu Hongzhang came from Guizhou, the only senior commander under Zeng Guoquan who was not a Hunan man.

因为孤立其间，不能不格外卖力，免得遭受排挤。
Isolated among them, he had to exert himself all the more to avoid being pushed aside.

曾国荃亦很看重他，一直保到提督衔记名总兵，
Zeng valued him highly too, repeatedly recommending his promotion until he held the honorary rank of provincial military commander and was registered for appointment as a brigade commander.

派他经理营务处。
He had also put him in charge of the military administration office.

此时再挖地道，由他与记名提督河南归德镇总兵李臣典共同负责。
The new tunneling operation was jointly entrusted to Zhu and Li Chendian, brigade commander of Guide in Henan and a registered candidate for provincial military command.

从六月初八开始，日夜不停，
Beginning on the eighth day of the sixth month, the work continued night and day.

挖了七天才挖成，填塞炸药，可以作最后的攻击了。
After seven days the tunnel was finished and packed with explosives; all was ready for the final assault.

曾国荃问部下诸将∶哪一营『头敌』；哪一营『二敌』？
Zeng asked which battalion would form the first attacking wave and which the second.

诸将默无一言。
The generals said nothing.

便按官职大小，个别征询。
He then questioned them individually in order of rank.

官阶最高的是萧孚泗，已经补上福建陆路提督，
The most senior was Xiao Fusi, who had already been appointed commander of Fujian’s land forces.

他依旧沉默；便只好问李臣典了。
He too remained silent, leaving Zeng to ask Li Chendian.

李臣典倒愿打头阵，但要朱洪章 拨一两千精兵给他。
Li was willing to lead, provided Zhu gave him one or two thousand picked men.

朱洪章 表示∶『既然如此，不如我来当头。』
“In that case,” Zhu said, “I might as well take the lead myself.”

事情便这样定局，
The matter was settled accordingly.

还立了军令状，畏缩不前者斩！
A written military pledge was drawn up: anyone who shrank back would be executed.


六月十六日正午，由朱洪章 下令施放炸药。
At noon on the sixteenth day of the sixth month, Zhu ordered the charge fired.

地道中的炸药有三万斤之多，进口之处用巨石封固；
More than thirty thousand jin of explosives filled the tunnel, whose entrance had been sealed with enormous stones.

另外以极粗的毛竹伸入地道，内用粗布包炸药填塞，作为引线；
Thick bamboo poles extended into it, packed with explosives wrapped in coarse cloth to serve as a fuse.

引线点燃以后，但闻地底隐隐如雷声，却不爆发，
Once the fuse was lit, a low rumbling like distant thunder came from underground, but no explosion followed.

天空中的骄阳，流水烁金一般，炸药决无不燃之理；
The sun blazed overhead with a heat that seemed to melt metal; surely the charge could not fail to burn.

万千将士挥汗屏息，等得焦灼不堪。
Thousands upon thousands of officers and men waited, sweating and holding their breath, until their anxiety became unbearable.

这样过了一个钟头之久，
An hour passed.

地底连那隐隐雷声都消失了。
Even the faint underground rumbling ceased.

过去亦常有不能引发炸药的事情；这一次看起来又是陡劳无功。
Charges had often failed before, and this attempt too seemed to have come to nothing.

各营将士，无不失望，
Throughout the camps the men were bitterly disappointed.

正准备先撤退一批部队，分班休息时；突然间，霹雳之声大作，仿佛天崩地裂似的。
They were about to withdraw some of the troops for a rest in shifts when a tremendous crash suddenly burst forth, as though heaven and earth were breaking apart.

太平门的一段城墙，约有二十多丈长，随烟直上，耸得老高，成为闻所未闻的奇观。
A section of wall at Taiping Gate, more than twenty zhang long, rose high into the air with the smoke: an unheard-of spectacle.


这有个说法。
There was an explanation for it.

明太祖建都南京，洪武二年始建都城，
The founder of the Ming dynasty had made Nanjing his capital and begun building its walls in the second year of Hongwu.

征发大量民夫，花了四年功夫，方始完工，
Vast numbers of laborers had been conscripted, and the work took four years.

周围六十一里，不但比北平城周四十余里、西安城周二十四里都大；而且亦是世界第一大城。
The circuit measured sixty-one li, greater not only than Beiping’s forty-odd li and Xi’an’s twenty-four, but than that of any other city in the world.

南京城不但大，而且高，平均都在四十尺以上。
Nanjing’s walls were tall as well as extensive, averaging more than forty chi in height.

大与高之外，最大的特色是坚，
Their most remarkable quality, however, was their strength.

城以花岗石为基，特为烧制的巨砖为墙；砖与砖之间，用石灰泡糯米浆水砌合。
Granite formed the foundations, and specially fired giant bricks made up the walls, bonded with mortar of lime steeped in glutinous-rice paste.

全城告成，再以石灰泡糯米浆水涂敷，
When the whole circuit was finished, the same mixture was spread over it.

所以在城外随便指一处敲击，都会显出白印。
Strike the outer wall at any point and a white mark would show.

五百年来刀枪不入，水火不侵的城墙，毕竟还敌不过西洋的炸药；
For five hundred years it had resisted weapons, water, and fire; yet in the end even this wall could not withstand Western explosives.

只是被炸以后，砖砖相砌，过于坚牢，所以才会造成二十余丈长的整段城墙，飞入空中的奇观。
Its bricks were bonded so firmly, however, that the blast hurled an entire section more than twenty zhang long into the air.

后来知道，这段城墙飞出一里多外，裂成数段落地，打死了数百人之多。
It was later learned that the section had traveled more than a li before breaking into several pieces and falling to earth, killing several hundred people.


在当时，朱洪章 奋身向前，左手执旗，右手操刀，大呼上城。
At the moment of the breach, Zhu rushed forward with a flag in his left hand and a sword in his right, shouting for the men to mount the wall.

于是九门皆破，有所谓『先登九将』，
All nine gates were taken, giving rise to the name “the Nine Generals Who First Mounted the Walls.”

除朱洪章 、李臣典、萧孚泗以外，还有记名总兵武明良、熊登、伍维寿、提督张诗日、记名按察使刘连捷、记名道员彭毓橘。
Besides Zhu, Li Chendian, and Xiao Fusi, they were the registered brigade commanders Wu Mingliang, Xiong Deng, and Wu Weishou; Provincial Military Commander Zhang Shiri; Liu Lianjie, registered for appointment as a provincial judicial commissioner; and Peng Yuju, a registered circuit intendant.

捷报到京，自然要大赏功臣。
When the news reached Beijing, great rewards naturally followed.

据说文宗在日，曾有诺言∶平洪杨者封王。
The late Xianfeng Emperor was said to have promised a princely title to whoever suppressed Hong Xiuquan and Yang Xiuqing.

但清朝自三藩之后，异姓不王；甚至封公爵的亦没有。
Yet since the rebellion of the Three Feudatories, the Qing had made no one outside the imperial clan a prince, or even a duke.

因此，亲贵中颇有人反对实现文宗的诺言；形成难题。
Many imperial nobles therefore opposed fulfilling the late emperor’s promise, creating a difficult problem.

最后是慈安太后出了个主意，将一个王爵，析而为四，
At last Empress Dowager Ci’an proposed dividing one princely honor into four lesser titles.

曾国藩功劳最大，封侯；其是曾国荃，封伯；接下来是一个子爵、一个男爵，封了李臣典和萧孚泗。
Zeng Guofan, whose merit was greatest, became a marquis; Zeng Guoquan came next and became an earl; the remaining titles, a viscountcy and a barony, went to Li Chendian and Xiao Fusi respectively.


朝旨一下，朱洪章 大为不服。
When the decree appeared, Zhu was deeply dissatisfied.

论破城当日之功。他实在应该第一，
Judged by their achievements on the day of the breach, he should surely have ranked first.

首先登城，生擒伪勇王洪仁达，占领『天王府』。
He had been the first over the wall, captured the rebel King Yong, Hong Renda, alive, and occupied the Heavenly King’s Palace.

而曾国荃奏报叙功时，却以李臣典居首；
Yet Zeng Guoquan’s report placed Li Chendian first.

据说，当朱洪章 占领『天王府』，看守到黄昏时分，李臣典领兵驰到，自道『奏九帅之命接防』。
It was said that Zhu had held the palace until dusk, when Li arrived with troops, announcing that he had come “on the Ninth Marshal’s orders to take over the defense.”

于是『天王府』归李臣典的控制，
The palace thus passed into Li’s control.

看守到第二天上午八点钟，光天化日之下，『天王府』无缘无故起火，烧得精光。
He guarded it until eight the following morning, when, in broad daylight and for no apparent reason, it caught fire and burned to the ground.

事后曾国荃奏报，搜索『天王府』，除了一颗伪玺以外，什么都没有了。
Zeng subsequently reported that a search had yielded nothing but a rebel imperial seal.

李臣典叙功居首的奥妙是如此！
Here, then, lay the secret of Li’s place at the head of the list!

朱洪章 在『先登九将』中甚至不如孚泗还落得一个五等爵末位的『一等男』；
Among the nine first over the walls, Zhu did not fare even as well as Xiao Fusi, who at least received a first-class barony, the lowest of the five grades of nobility.

他所得的恩典，是『无论提督总兵缺出，尽先提奏；并赏穿黄马褂，赏给骑都尉世职』，
Zhu’s rewards were an order that he be given priority for recommendation whenever a provincial military command or brigade command fell vacant, permission to wear the yellow riding jacket, and the hereditary dignity of cavalry commandant.

虽亦不薄，但名列第三，太受委屈。
These were by no means insignificant honors, but to rank third was a bitter injustice.


一口气咽不下，朱洪章 去找『九帅』理论。
Unable to swallow it, Zhu went to argue with the Ninth Marshal.

曾国荃大概早有防备，应付之道甚绝，
Zeng had evidently prepared his response, and it was masterly.

他说∶『我亦认为你应居首功。
“I too believe you should have received first credit.

但叙功的奏折，是由我老兄拜发；
But the memorial listing the achievements was submitted by my elder brother.

听说是他的幕友李某捣鬼。』
I hear that a secretary of his, a fellow named Li, has been manipulating things.”

说着，从靴页子里拔出一把雪亮的雪子，倒持着递向朱洪章 ，
As he spoke, he drew a gleaming knife from his boot and held it out hilt first.

『你去宰了那个姓李的。』
“Go and kill the man.”

朱洪章 为之啼笑皆非。
Zhu did not know whether to laugh or cry.

但李臣典亦如黄梁一梦，
Li Chendian’s glory, meanwhile, proved as fleeting as a dream.

锡爵之恩；黄马褂、双眼花翎之荣，竟不克亲承宠命；
He never lived to receive the decree granting his title, yellow jacket, and two-eyed peacock feather.

恩旨到时，已经一命呜呼。
By the time it arrived, he was dead.

据曾国荃奏报，说他攻城时，『伤及腰穴，气脉阻滞』，因而于七月初二日不治出缺。
According to Zeng’s memorial, Li had suffered an injury to an acupuncture point at the waist during the assault, obstructing the flow of his vital energy; he died on the second day of the seventh month.

却又有人说，李臣典死在『牡丹花下』——
Others, however, said that he had died “beneath the peony blossoms.”

破城之日，玉帛子女，任所取携；
When the city fell, treasure, women, and children had been there for the taking.

李臣典一夜之间，御十数女子，溽暑不谨，得了『夹阴伤寒』，一命呜呼！
In a single night Li was said to have slept with more than ten women; careless in the oppressive summer heat, he contracted a fever associated with sexual indulgence and died.

当然，这是私下的传说；
This, of course, was private rumor.

反正死因如出于床第之间，真相是再也不能水落石出的。
If the cause of death lay in the bedchamber, the truth would never be established.

萧孚泗的封男爵，亦有一段故事。
There was a story behind Xiao Fusi’s barony too.


当城破无可为计时，李秀成在乱军中带着一个亲信书僮，出通济门往东南方向逃走；
When the city fell and all hope was gone, Li Xiucheng took a trusted page through the disordered troops and escaped southeast through Tongji Gate.

目的是越过茅山，经溧阳、长兴到湖州，与由杭州遁走的长毛会合。
He intended to cross Maoshan and travel by way of Liyang and Changxing to Huzhou, joining the rebels who had fled from Hangzhou.

走到一处叫方山的地方，撞见八个樵夫，
At a place called Fangshan he encountered eight woodcutters.

其中有人认识他，却确不定，便冒叫一声∶『忠王！』
One thought he recognized him but was not certain, so ventured to call, “Loyal King!”

李秀成一看行藏被人识破，便长跪相求∶『哪位领路带我到湖州，我送三万银子酬谢。』
Seeing that his identity had been discovered, Li sank to his knees and begged, “If any of you will guide me to Huzhou, I shall give him thirty thousand taels.”

说着，他与他的书僮都将袖子抹了上去；
He and his page pulled back their sleeves.

但见四条手臂上，戴满了金镯子；
All four arms were covered with gold bracelets.

另外有一匹马，驮着一只箱子，看上去并不大，可是压得马的腰都弯了，
A horse also carried a chest that looked quite small but bent the animal’s back beneath its weight.

可以想见其中装的是金银珠宝。
One could readily imagine it filled with gold, silver, and jewels.

这八个樵夫见此光景，大起贪心，
The eight woodcutters grew greedy.

一方面想侵吞李秀成的钱财，一方面还想报功领赏。
They wanted both to seize Li’s wealth and to earn a reward for reporting him.

于是这八个人将李秀成主仆骗入山下的『涧西村』，公推一个姓陶的去向官军报信；
They therefore tricked master and servant into Jianxi Village at the foot of the hill and chose a man named Tao to inform the imperial troops.

目的地是驻扎太平门外的李臣典营中，因为姓陶的有个同族弟兄是李臣典的部下，托他转报，比较妥当。
He was to go to Li Chendian’s camp outside Taiping Gate, because a kinsman of his served there and could safely pass on the information.


姓陶的经过钟山，又饥又渴；
Passing Zhongshan, Tao grew hungry and thirsty.

想起这里是萧孚泗的防区，营中有个伙夫，因为供应柴草的关系而熟识，
Remembering that this was Xiao Fusi’s sector, he thought of a cook in the camp whom he knew through supplying firewood.

不妨到他那里歇脚求食。
He might as well stop there to rest and get a meal.

姓陶的得意忘形，休息闲谈之间，透露了生擒李秀成的经过。
Carried away by his good fortune, Tao let slip during their conversation how they had captured Li Xiucheng.

这个伙夫便转告亲兵；亲兵转报萧孚泗，姓陶的便注定要做枉死鬼了。
The cook told a bodyguard, who informed Xiao; from that moment Tao was doomed.

一番密密嘱咐，将姓陶的好酒好肉款待；萧孚泗自携亲兵二十多人，烈日下疾驰到涧西村，
After giving secret instructions that Tao be entertained with good meat and wine, Xiao took more than twenty bodyguards and rode hard through the blazing heat to Jianxi Village.

将李秀成手到擒来；价值十余万银子的金银珠宝，亦归掌握。
There he seized Li with ease and took possession of gold, silver, and jewels worth more than a hundred thousand taels.

姓陶的被一刀斩讫，借以灭口；
Tao was killed with a single sword stroke to silence him.

不过萧孚泗总算还有良心，没有杀那个伙夫，给了他五颗上好的珠子，一匹好马，暗示他连夜『开小差』，走得越远越好。
Xiao retained enough conscience to spare the cook: he gave him five fine pearls and a good horse, hinting that he should desert that very night and go as far away as possible.

萧孚泗的得封男爵，就以生擒李秀成之功。
It was for capturing Li Xiucheng that Xiao received his barony.

曾国荃到后来才知道真相，吩咐赏那八家樵夫，每家一百两银子。
Only later did Zeng Guoquan learn the truth and order a reward of a hundred taels for each of the eight woodcutters’ families.

结果为亲兵吞没大半，
His bodyguards pocketed most of it.

只拿出去一个『大元宝』——五十两银子，由八家均分。
All that reached the eight households was one large ingot—fifty taels—to divide among them.


如果李秀成真是为萧孚泗凭一己之力所生擒，这份功劳，就真值得一个男爵了。
Had Xiao truly captured Li Xiucheng by his own efforts, the achievement would indeed have deserved a barony.

因为『天京』虽破，『幼天王』未获，
For although the Heavenly Capital had fallen, the Young Heavenly King had not been captured.

只说已死在乱军之中，对朝廷似难交代。
Merely claiming that he had died in the fighting would scarcely satisfy the court.

幸好有个李秀成，论实际，其人之重要又过于『幼天王』，
Fortunately there was Li Xiucheng, whose practical importance exceeded that of the young ruler.

足可弥补元凶下落不明之失。
His capture could compensate for the failure to establish the principal rebel’s whereabouts.

其时曾国藩已由安庆专船到江宁，
Zeng Guofan had by then come from Anqing to Jiangning in a specially provided vessel.

抚循将士，赈济百姓以外，另一件大事，就是处置李秀成，
Besides reassuring the troops and relieving the people, he had another important task: deciding Li Xiucheng’s fate.

委派道员庞际云、知府李鸿裔会审，
He appointed Circuit Intendant Pang Jiyun and Prefect Li Hongyi to interrogate him jointly.

这李鸿裔，就是曾国荃向朱洪章 所说『捣鬼』的『李某』。
Li Hongyi was the very “fellow named Li” whom Zeng Guoquan had accused of manipulating the rewards when speaking to Zhu.


从六月廿七到七月初六，十天的功夫，审问的时间少，
During the ten days from the twenty-seventh of the sixth month to the sixth of the seventh, comparatively little time was spent questioning Li.

李秀成在囚笼写『亲供』的时候多；每天约写七千字，总计约七、八万言。
Most of it he spent in his prison cage writing a personal confession, producing some seven thousand characters a day and seventy or eighty thousand in all.

却为曾国藩大删大改、所存不过三分之一；方始奏报。
Zeng Guofan then cut and altered it extensively, retaining no more than a third before forwarding it to the throne.

其中谈到城破后，洪秀全两个儿子的下落，说是『独带幼主一人，
In discussing what had become of Hong Xiuquan’s two sons after the city fell, the confession stated: “I took only the young lord with me.

幼主无好马，将我战马交与骑坐。』
As he had no good horse, I gave him my own warhorse to ride.

『三更之后，舍死领头冲锋，带幼主冲由九帅攻倒城墙缺口而出。
After the third watch I led a desperate charge, bringing him out through the breach made in the wall by the Ninth Marshal.

君臣数百人，舍命冲出关外，
Several hundred of us, sovereign and followers together, fought our way beyond the city.

所过营塞，叠叠层层、壕满垒固。
The camps we passed stood layer upon layer, with deep ditches and strong defenses.

幼主出到城外，九帅营中，营营炮发，处处喊声不绝；
As the young lord came out, cannon fired from every camp of the Ninth Marshal, and shouts rose everywhere without cease.

我与幼主两个分离，九帅之兵，马步追赶，
He and I became separated, while the Ninth Marshal’s mounted and foot soldiers pursued us.

此时虽出，生死未知。
Though he had escaped the city, whether he lived or died was unknown.

十六岁幼童，自幼至长，并未骑过马，又未受过惊慌，
A child of sixteen, he had never ridden a horse in all his life and had never endured such terror.

九帅四方兵进，定然被杀矣，
With the Ninth Marshal’s troops advancing from every side, he must surely have been killed.

若九帅马步在路中杀死，亦未悉其是幼主，
If the cavalry or infantry killed him on the road, they would not have known he was the young lord.

一个小童，何人知也？』
Who would recognize a little boy?”


这段供词，与曾国藩奏报『幼逆已死于乱军之中』，有桴鼓相应之妙；
This passage chimed perfectly with Zeng Guofan’s report that “the young rebel has already died amid the fighting.”

不道弄巧成拙，反显删改之迹——『幼天王』未死，逃到湖州了。
But the attempt to be clever defeated itself and exposed the alterations: the Young Heavenly King was alive and had escaped to Huzhou.

在曾国藩封侯的同时，又有恩旨赏赉东南各路统兵大帅及封疆大臣；
At the same time that Zeng became a marquis, further decrees rewarded the principal commanders and provincial authorities of the southeast.

亲王僧格林沁，加赏一贝勒；
Prince Sengge Rinchen received an additional grant of a beile title.

湖广总督官文，赐封一等伯爵，世袭罔替；江苏巡抚李鸿章 一等伯爵；
Guanwen, Governor-General of Huguang, became a first-class earl, with his title perpetually hereditary without reduction; Li Hongzhang, Governor of Jiangsu, also became a first-class earl.

陕甘总督杨岳斌、兵部右侍郎彭玉麟赏给一等轻车都尉世职，并赏加太子少保衔；
Yang Yuebin, Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu, and Peng Yulin, Right Vice-Minister of War, received the hereditary dignity of first-class light-chariot commandant and the honorary title of Junior Guardian of the Heir Apparent.

四川总督骆秉章 、浙江提督鲍超，一等轻车都尉世职；
Luo Bingzhang, Governor-General of Sichuan, and Bao Chao, Zhejiang’s provincial military commander, likewise received first-class light-chariot commandancies.

西安将军都兴阿、江宁将军富明阿、广西提督冯子材、均赏给骑都尉世职。
Duxing’a, the Xi’an general; Fuming’a, the Jiangning general; and Feng Zicai, Guangxi’s provincial military commander, received hereditary cavalry commandancies.

东南大员，向隅的只有左宗棠和江西巡抚沈葆桢，
Among the senior officials of the southeast, only Zuo and Shen Baozhen, Governor of Jiangxi, were left out.

上谕中特为交代∶『俟浙赣肃清后再行加恩。』
The decree expressly stated that further favor would be granted once Zhejiang and Jiangxi had been cleared of rebels.

这虽是激励之意，但相形之下，未免难堪；
Intended as encouragement though this was, the contrast was humiliating.

尤其是李鸿章 封爵，使得左宗棠更不服气。
Li Hongzhang’s ennoblement especially angered Zuo.

往深一层去想，曾国藩节制五省军务，江西、浙江亦在其列；
More fundamentally, Zeng Guofan directed military affairs in five provinces, including Zhejiang and Jiangxi.

这两省既未肃清，就是曾国藩责任未了，
If those two were not pacified, his own task was unfinished.

何以独蒙上赏？
Why should he receive such outstanding rewards?

再有一件事，使左宗棠气恼的是，
There was another cause for anger.

江宁溃败的长毛，只有往东南一路可逃；
The defeated rebels from Jiangning had only one escape route, southeastward.

因而湖州一带，本来打得很顺利的，忽然增加了沉重的压力。
Operations around Huzhou had been progressing well; suddenly they faced a crushing new pressure.

如果事先密商，曾国荃定于何时破城，进兵围剿的策略如何？都能让左宗宗知道，先期派兵填塞缺口，伏路拦截，
Had Zeng secretly consulted Zuo beforehand about the date of the assault and the plans to encircle and destroy the enemy, Zuo could have stationed troops to block the gaps and ambush the fugitives.

又何致于让溃败的长毛，如山倒堤崩般涌过来？
How else could the defeated rebels have come pouring in like a collapsing mountain or a bursting dike?

然则曾军只顾自己争功，竟是『以邻为壑』了！
Zeng’s army, seeking glory only for itself, was simply diverting disaster onto its neighbor.


朝中当国的恭王，以及上获信任，下受尊重，确能公忠体国，为旗中贤者的军机大臣文祥，却不知东南将帅之间，存着如此深刻的矛盾；
Prince Gong, who directed affairs at court, and Grand Councilor Wenxiang—a worthy bannerman, trusted above and respected below, genuinely loyal in his service to the state—knew nothing of the depth of this antagonism among the southeastern commanders.

紧接着大赏功臣的恩诏之下，又有一道督责极严的上谕，
Close upon the decrees rewarding merit came another edict, sternly pressing Zuo to act.

让左宗棠看了，更不舒服。
Reading it made him more uncomfortable still.

上谕中说∶『江宁克复，群丑就歼，无逸出之贼』，
It began: “Jiangning has been recovered, the entire band of villains destroyed, and no rebel has escaped.”

这几句话，便使左宗棠疑心，曾氏弟兄奏报克复江宁的战功，不知如何铺张扬厉，夸大其词？
Those words made Zuo wonder how extravagantly the Zeng brothers had embellished their reports of victory.

因此对于后面∶『着李鸿章 将王永胜等军，调长兴，协防湖郡；左宗棠当督率各军，会合苏师，迅将湖州、安吉之贼，全行殄灭，克复坚城，勿令一贼上窜』的要求，越起反感。
He therefore resented all the more the ensuing instructions: “Li Hongzhang shall transfer the forces of Wang Yongsheng and others to Changxing to assist in defending Huzhou. Zuo Zongtang shall direct his armies, in concert with the Jiangsu forces, speedily to annihilate all rebels in Huzhou and Anji, recover their fortified cities, and allow not a single rebel to escape farther inland.”


『你看，』他对胡雪岩说∶『曾氏兄弟，不但自己邀功，还断了别人的建功之路。
“Look,” he said to Hu. “The Zeng brothers have not merely claimed glory for themselves; they have blocked everyone else’s path to earning it.

照字里看，大功已经告成，浙江可以指日肃清；
Judging by the language here, the great work is finished and Zhejiang can be pacified within days.

湖州长毛如毛，攻起来格外吃力，
Yet Huzhou is swarming with rebels and will be harder than ever to take.

即使拼命拿下来，也讨不了好。
Even if we fight to the death and capture it, we shall get no credit.

因为有曾氏兄弟先人之言，说江宁的「群丑就歼，无逸出之贼」；
The Zeng brothers have spoken first: at Jiangning ‘the entire band of villains was destroyed, and no rebel escaped.’

朝廷一定以为我们虚报军功。
The court will certainly think we are inventing victories.

你想，可恨不可恨？』
Is that not infuriating?”

胡雪岩当然只有劝慰，但泛泛其词，不能发生作用；
Hu could only try to soothe him, but vague consolations had no effect.

而谍报一个接一个，尽是长毛的某『王』、某『王』，由皖南广德，窜入浙江境界，越过天目山，直奔湖州的消息。
Intelligence reports arrived one after another, all describing this or that rebel “king” moving from Guangde in southern Anhui into Zhejiang, crossing the Tianmu Mountains, and making straight for Huzhou.

最后来了一个消息，是难民之中传出来的；
At last came a report originating among the refugees.

飞报到杭州，
It was rushed to Hangzhou.

左宗棠一看，兴奋非凡。
When Zuo read it, he became extraordinarily excited.
