胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第九章（本章第2部分，共7部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


The report stated that after the fall of Jiangning, the Young Heavenly King, Hong Tianguifu, had been escorted by King Gan, Hong Rengan; King Yang, Ji Qingyuan; King Yu, Li Ruisheng; and King Yang, Li Mingcheng.
这个报告中说∶『幼天王』洪福真，在江宁城破以后，由『干王』洪仁干、『养王』吉庆元、『誉王』李瑞生、『扬王』李明成『保驾』，

They reached Guangde on the twenty-first day of the sixth month.
六月廿一那天，到达广德；

Five days later Huang Wenjin, King Du, who held Huzhou, personally welcomed him into the city.
然后由守湖州的『堵王』黄文金，在五天以后亲迎入湖州城内，

Learning that the Loyal King, Li Xiucheng, had been captured by the imperial forces, the young ruler had appointed Hong Rengan chief military counselor.
并且已得知『忠王』李秀成为官军所获的消息，所以改封洪仁干为『正军师』。

Zuo thought he could now put the Zeng brothers to shame.
这一下，左宗棠认为可以要曾氏弟兄的好看了；

He immediately ordered a secretary to draft a memorial, intending to send it by express rider and expose Zeng Guofan’s false claim that the young rebel had died in the fighting.
当即嘱咐幕友草拟奏稿，打算飞骑入奏，拆穿曾国藩所报『幼逆已死于乱军』中的谎言。

Just as he was exulting and laughing openly, Hu arrived at his temporary headquarters.
而正当意气洋洋，解颜大笑之际；胡雪岩正好到达行辕，

On hearing the news, Hu felt obliged to dampen his pleasure with a word of caution.
听得这个消息，不能不扫左宗棠的兴，劝他一劝。


“My lord, might this memorial wait a little?”
『大人，这个奏折，是不是可以缓一缓？』

“What is there to wait for?
『何缓之有？

Now that the principal rebel’s whereabouts are known, how should I dare conceal them?” Zuo answered with righteous confidence.
元凶行藏已露，何敢匿而不报？』左宗棠振振有词地说。

Hu knew that an appeal against quarrels among commanders as harmful to the state would go unheard.
胡雪岩知道用将帅互讦，非国家之福的话相劝，是他听不入耳的，

He therefore appealed to practical consequences.
因而动以利害，

“We have a saying in Hangzhou: lift a stone yourself, and crush your own foot.
『我们杭州人有句俗语，叫做「自扳石头自压脚」，

My lord, this is a stone you must not lift.
大人，你这块石头扳不得！』

Handle it badly, and it may break your head.”
他说，『扳得不好，会打破头。』

“What do you mean?”
『这是怎么说？』

“Consider what will happen when the memorial arrives.
『大人请想，这样一奏，

The court will naturally be delighted.
朝廷当然高兴，

‘Excellent!
说是「很好！

You must capture the young rebel.
你务必拿幼逆抓来；

On no account may he escape.
无论如何，不准漏网。

Take him, and we shall make you a marquis.’
抓到了，封你的侯。」

But suppose you fail?”
大人抓不到呢？』

“Ah!
『啊，

Ah!”
啊！』

Zuo suddenly understood.
左宗棠恍然大悟，

“Then I shall be the man who let the principal rebel escape!”
『抓不到，变成元凶从我手中漏网了！』


Hu deliberately said no more.
胡雪岩是有意不再往下说。

A man as intelligent as Zuo needed only a hint, certainly; but Hu’s chief reason was another consideration.
象左宗棠这样的聪明人，固然一点就透，无烦词费；最主要的，还是他另有一种看法使然。

On his recent visit to Shanghai he had heard a great deal about the Zeng brothers and Li Hongzhang from their secretaries and trusted associates.
他这一次上海之行，听到许多有关曾氏兄弟和李鸿章 的近况，皆由曾、李的幕友或亲信所透露。

Even remarks in private correspondence, which by rights no third person should ever have heard, had leaked out.
有许多札中的话，照常理而论，是不容第三人入耳的，而居然亦外泄了！

Evidently Zeng and Li had spoken freely to those about them.
这当然是曾李本人毫无顾忌，说与左右，

The discreet passed the information only to people who could appreciate it; the shallow boasted of their intimacy with great officials and their access to inside news, adding flourishes until every story came vividly to life.
深沉的只为知者道∶浅薄的自诩接近大僚，消息灵通，加枝添叶，说得活龙活现，

Thus they created endless needless disputes and damaged relationships for no reason.
无端生出多少是非，也没来由地伤害了好些人的关系，

This had made Hu wary of Zuo.
因为如此，胡雪岩对左宗棠便有了戒心。

His own lord was notorious for speaking without restraint.
他在想，这位『大人』的大没遮拦，也是出了名的。

If Hu devised a scheme to sow discord between Zeng and Li, Zuo might one day tell someone exactly how he had done it.
如果自己为他设计，离间曾李之间的感情；说不定有一天，左宗棠会亲口告诉别人如何如何。

That would be curing one ailment by causing another.
这岂非『治一经、损一经』；

To offend both Zeng and Li for no good reason was hardly worthwhile.
无缘无故得罪了曾、李，就太犯不着了！

Zuo, however, had heard enough.
而左宗棠有他这句话，已经足够。

Delighted, he kept saying, “I understand!
当时很高兴地，一叠连声地说∶『吾知之矣！

I understand!”
吾知之矣！』

Hu found this less than satisfactory.
这样的回答，在胡雪岩却又不甚满意；

He wanted a definite plan, so that he could grasp Zuo’s intention and help carry it out.
他希望左宗棠有个具体的打算说出来，才好秉承宗旨，襄助办事。

He therefore asked, “Then does Your Excellency find something useful in my humble opinion?”
因而追问一句∶『大人是不是觉得愚见还有可采之处？』

“Humble opinion?
『什么愚见？

Your judgment is excellent!”
你的见解太高明了！』

Zuo paused thoughtfully. “Still, I am not a man to change my face at a turn of the hand.
左宗棠沉吟着说道∶『不过，在我到底不是翻手为云覆手为雨的人；

Besides, Li Shaoquan has always been, to me—” He too left the sentence unfinished.
而况李少荃一向为我——。』他也没有再说下去，

Anyone familiar with his usual remarks could supply the rest: he had always despised Li Hongzhang.
只是知道他平日言论的人，都能猜想得到，李鸿章 一向为他所藐视。

To seek friendship now seemed like asking a favor, and he resented it.
如今与他修好，仿佛有求于人似的，未免心有不甘。

Hu decided that open advice toward a proper end was different from privately criticizing others.
胡雪岩认为从正面设词规劝，与在私底下说人短处不同，

Even if such words leaked out, they could stand in public without injuring his reputation.
即令密语外泄，亦是『台面上』摆得出去的话，并无碍于自己的名声，

He resolved to persuade Zuo to cooperate with Li.
因而决定下一番说词，促成左、李的合作。


“My lord,” he asked deliberately, “what is the one urgent task before us?”
『大人，』他有意问道∶『如今唯一的急务是什么？』

“Do you mean public business, or my own affairs?”
『你是指公事，还是指我自己的事？』

“It is the same in both.
『公事也是如此，大人的私事也是如此。

Two matters in one, and one in two: what is the great task that concerns both?”
一而二，二而一，无大不大的一件大事是什么？』

“Naturally, to clear all Zhejiang of rebels.”
『自在是肃清全浙。』

“Exactly.
『是，

And there is only one obstacle left: Huzhou.
肃清全浙只剩一处障碍；就是湖州。

Once it falls, you can report the province pacified.
拿湖州攻了下来，就可奏报肃清。

Then you too will be made a marquis and a grand minister.”
那时候，大人也要封侯拜相了。』

“A grand minister is far in the future, and a marquisate is nothing remarkable.
『拜相还早，封侯亦不足为奇。

If such a grant of nobility is offered, I shall insist upon declining it.”
果然膺此分茅之赏，我是要力辞的。』

Hu could not tell whether Zuo spoke from real feeling or affected indifference.
胡雪岩不知道他这话是有感而发，还是故作矫情，

There was no point in arguing.
反正不必与他争辩，

Only by following the direction of his words could Hu move him.
惟有顺着他的语气想话来说，才能打动他的心。


“A masterly move, my lord!”
『大人这一首高！』

Hu raised his thumb. “To think nothing of a marquisate shows that wealth and rank are no more to you than passing clouds.
他着大拇指说∶『封侯不希罕，见得富贵于我如浮云，

That places you above both Grand Secretary Zeng and Governor Li.
比曾相、李中丞都高一等了。

But unless the court first offers the honor, how can you display such superior character?”
不过，朝廷如无恩命，大人又怎能显得出高人一等的人品？』

“There is truth in that.”
『这话倒也是。』

Zuo nodded deeply.
左宗棠深深点头。

At last he had yielded a little, and Hu could breathe freely.
左宗棠终于松了口，胡雪岩也就松了口气。

How cooperation with Li was to be arranged no longer required Hu’s attention.
至于如何与李鸿章 合作？就不用他费心了；

Zuo understood the whole situation, and military strategy was not a subject on which Hu could presume to speak.
一切形势，左宗棠看得很清楚，而且谈用兵，亦不是他所能置喙的。

He merely reminded Zuo of one point: Li had disobeyed the court’s orders to assist at Jiangning and now urgently wished to make amends.
他只提醒左宗棠一点，会攻江宁，李鸿章 忤了朝旨；目前急图补救，

Even if Zuo refused cooperation, Li would send troops toward Huzhou on his own, demonstrating obedience to the repeatedly stated principle that provincial authorities suppressing rebels must not let territorial boundaries divide their efforts.
所以即使左宗棠不愿与他合作，他自己亦会派兵进窥湖州，表示遵从朝廷所一再揭示的，『疆臣办贼，决不可有轸域之分』的要求。

Zuo, moreover, truly needed his support.
左宗棠亦实在需要李鸿章的支援。


The first reason was manpower.
第一是兵力。

Huzhou had become the refuge of the rebels of the southeast.
湖州已成为东南长毛的逋光薮，

Their scattered armies and defeated commanders had gathered there in numbers several times greater than Zuo’s forces.
残兵败将交集结在一起，人数超过左军好几倍。

Pressed too hard, they would fight like trapped beasts and must not be underestimated.
而且逼得急，会作困兽之斗，决不可轻视。

The second reason was terrain.
第二是地形。

Around Huzhou, waterways branched in every direction, offering ambush positions everywhere.
湖州四周，港汊纵横，处处可以设仗邀击，

The place was naturally easy to defend and difficult to attack.
本是易守难攻之地；

When Zhao Jingxian had held the isolated city, he had adapted his defense to the ground and used it to the utmost.
当年赵景贤孤城坚持，因势制宜，将地形的利用，发挥到了极致。

Its present rebel commander, Huang Wenjin, was no weakling either.
如今长毛守湖州的主将黄文金，亦非弱者；

Acting in the Young Heavenly King’s name, he could issue orders readily.
且假『幼主』洪福真的名号以行，指挥容易。

Reports also said that Huzhou held enough grain for a year, placing him in a much better position than Zhao had enjoyed.
而且湖州所贮存的粮食，据报可以支持一年，这又比赵景贤当时的处境好得多了。

Zuo could overcome neither obstacle alone; only Li could help him surmount both.
这进取湖州的两大障碍，都不是左宗棠独力所能克服的；而亦惟有李鸿章可以帮助他克服这两大障碍。

In manpower, the assistance of the Jiangsu forces was needed to complete the investment of Huzhou—not, of course, a four-sided encirclement like Zeng Guoquan’s siege of Jinling.
论兵力，有苏军的协力，才可以完成对湖州的包围——当然不是象曾国荃攻金陵那样的四面包围。

Even if enough troops could be deployed for that, it would be unwise.
如果采取这样的方略，即使兵力部署上能够做得到，亦是不智之举；

Since ancient times, besiegers had often left one side open.
从古以来，围城往往网开一面，

Give the enemy no way to live, and he must fight to the death.
因为不放敌人一条生路，必然作生死的搏斗，

Even if annihilated, he would inflict terrible losses.
就算能够尽歼敌人，自己这方面的伤亡，亦一定是惨重无比。

Leave an outlet, by contrast, and the hope of survival would weaken his will to resist.
反过来看，留下一个纵敌的缺口，正可以激起敌军的恋生之念，瓦解他的斗志。

Moreover, ambushes could be laid along a carefully prepared escape route.
而况在预先安排好的敌人逃生路上，可以处处设伏，

That was the better calculation.
反为得计。


As for terrain, the first vital position outside Huzhou was Daqiankou, opening northward onto Lake Tai.
论地形，湖州外围的第一要隘是北面出太湖的大钱口；

Zhao Jingxian’s loss of Daqian on a snowy night had led to the fall of the city.
当年赵景贤雪夜失大钱，导致湖州的不守。

The circumstances were much the same now: Huzhou could not be taken until Daqian was seized.
以今视昔，情势不殊，要破湖州须先夺大钱；

It was far easier for the Jiangsu troops to cross Lake Tai from the north than for Zuo’s forces to march around to it.
而夺大钱，苏军渡太湖南下，比左军迂道而北要方便得多。

Most importantly, an attack on Daqian required a naval force—the Jiangsu army’s strength and Zuo’s weakness.
同时最大的关键是，攻大钱必须要用水师，而这又是左军之所短，苏军之所长。

Li would naturally use that strength to the full, both to atone for remaining idle at Changzhou and to justify his newly bestowed title.
李鸿章 当然要用他之所长，尽力有所作为，既以弥补常州顿兵之咎；亦以无负锡封爵位之恩。

After his long talk with Hu, Zuo considered matters quietly.
左宗棠自与胡雪岩深谈以后，默默打算；

In local advantages and connections, his position was inferior to Li’s.
自己这方面地利、人和都不及李鸿章 ，

Unless he could undertake the whole task himself and promise to capture Huzhou unaided within a fixed time, he could not prevent Li from advancing against it from the north.
如果不能大包大揽，放下诺言，限期独力攻克湖州，就不能禁止李鸿章 驰驱前路，自北面攻湖州。

Without cooperation, the two armies would compete for glory.
两军不能合作，便成争功的局面；

If Li lost the contest, little harm was done to him.
李鸿章 争不过无所谓，

If Zuo lost and allowed his guest to displace him as host, the reputation of a lifetime would be ruined.
自己争不过，让李鸿章 喧宾夺主，那就一世英名付之流水了。

After much thought, Zuo decided that the best course was to achieve his purpose with Li’s assistance.
他想来想去，因人成事，利用李鸿章 相助，是为上策。

If he fulfilled his duties as host, the visiting army would have to show him courtesy at every turn.
自己只要尽到了地主的道理，客军不能不处处情让，

Even if the Jiangsu troops entered Huzhou first, Li could scarcely send his own independent memorial.
即使苏军先攻入湖州，李鸿章 亦总不好意思，径自出奏。

Provided the report of victory went through Zuo’s hands, he could control the account of the achievements.
只要光复湖州的捷报由自己手中发出，铺叙战功，便可以操纵了。


His mind made up, Zuo would for the moment be a great man capable of bending as well as standing firm.
打定了主意，暂且做一个能屈能伸的大丈夫；

He personally wrote Li a most earnest letter, discussing how to sweep away the remaining rebels of the southeast and hinting at his need for assistance.
左宗棠亲自提笔，写了一封极恳切的信给李鸿章 ，在商略扫荡东南余孽的策略中，透露出求援之意。

Li responded handsomely, promising to commit all four of his “Guo, Liu, Pan, and Yang armies” to Huzhou.
李鸿章 亦很漂亮，答应将他部下的『郭刘潘杨四军』，全数投入湖州战场。

These commands—Guo Songlin’s, Liu Mingchuan’s, Pan Dingxin’s, and Yang Dingxun’s—formed the backbone of the Anhui Army.
郭刘潘杨——郭松林、刘铭传、潘鼎新、杨鼎勋四军，是淮军的中坚；

In fact, Li sent more than those four.
其实李鸿章投入湖州战场，还不止这四军，

Liu Bingzhang, who had joined the army from the Hanlin Academy, was also ordered to assist, as was the naval force of Huang Yisheng, Jiangnan’s provincial military commander and a close fellow townsman of Zeng Guofan.
另有以翰林从军的刘秉璋，与曾国藩小同乡、江南提督黄翼升的水师，亦奉委派，

They would advance by different routes.
分道助攻。

Li’s intentions broadly resembled Zuo’s.
朱鸿章 的心思与左宗棠大致相同，

He meant to make a great display and treat the Huzhou campaign as scarcely less important than the recovery of Jinling.
有意大张声势，将进攻湖州一役，看得不下如金陵之复，

Like a gambler placing a wager, he hoped to capture the young rebel and uncover a hoard of treasure; at the same time, he wished to dampen the Hunan Army’s elation.
一方面象押宝似的，希望能俘获『幼逆』，掘得『金穴』；一方面亦是有心扫扫曾军的兴头。


The rebels at Huzhou claimed two hundred thousand men and probably had at least sixty percent of that number.
在湖州的长毛，号称二十万，至少亦有六折之数；

Zuo’s and Li’s regular forces together numbered no fewer than eighty thousand; with civilian officials, laborers, and other followers, the total exceeded a hundred thousand.
左李两方，正规军合起来不下八万，加上随军的文员、夫役，总数亦在十万以上。

The sides were roughly matched, and fierce fighting was expected.
彼此旗鼓相当，发生恶战是意中之事；

Yet the outcome was virtually predetermined, and even the general course of the rebel retreat could be foreseen.
但胜负已如前定，而且长毛败退的情况，大致亦在估计之中。

The ground constrained the directions of advance.
因为由于地形的限制，进取的方向，只能顺势而行。

Zuo’s troops would press from the southeast and southwest, while the Jiangsu forces attacked from northeast and northwest and moved directly south to block Daqiankou, preventing escape onto Lake Tai.
左宗棠所部由湖州东南、西南两方面进逼；苏军则由东北、西北分攻，并从正北进扼大钱口，以防长毛窜入太湖。

East of Huzhou lay the richest country in the southeast, heavily defended; beyond it was the coast, offering no way out.
湖州的东面，是东南最富庶的地区，有重兵防守，而且东到海滨，并无出路；

The rebels’ only escape lay westward.
在湖州的长毛，唯一的出路，只是向西，

If they broke through Guangde, they might join Li Shixian and Wang Haiyang in Jiangxi, both famous fighting commanders, and perhaps prolong their existence a little.
如能冲过广德，则江西有李世贤、汪海洋，都是长毛中有名的悍将，能会合在一起，或者还有苟延残喘的可能。

A battlefield was like a chessboard.
战场如棋局，

Deception flourished not only between enemies, but among allies competing against one another.
不但敌我之间，尔虞我诈；就是联手的一方，亦在钩心斗角——

Li did, after all, make a move for his own advantage.
李鸿章 毕竟还是下了一着专为自己打算的棋，

He sent Liu Mingchuan’s twenty battalions, one after another, toward the Zhejiang–Anhui border, ostensibly to attack Guangde and cut the rebels’ retreat.
将刘铭传的二十营，陆续拔队，指向浙皖之交；名为进攻广德，断贼归路，

His real aim was to intercept Huang Wenjin, capture the young ruler, and seize the baggage train.
其实是想拦截黄文金，俘『幼逆』，夺辎重。


Huzhou was finally recovered on the twenty-sixth day of the seventh month.
湖州终于在七月二十六克复了。

As predicted, Huang opened the western gate and fled.
如事先所估计的，黄文金果然开湖州西门遁走。

The main rebel forces escaped west in three columns.
大队长毛分三路西窜，

At Guangde they divided again into two routes: one led into southern Anhui; the other, taken by Huang with the young ruler, went through Ningguo, over the western Tianmu Mountains, and by Kaihua and Yushan into Jiangxi.
到了广德，又分两路，一路向皖南；一路是由黄文金带着『幼逆』，由宁国过西天目山，经开化、玉山窜入江西境内。

Liu Mingchuan pursued relentlessly.
刘铭传穷追不舍；

The other armies, eager for credit, also pressed forward with all their strength.
其他各军为了争功，亦无不奋勇当先，

After five days and nights of pursuit, the rebels were scattered beyond cohesion, and Huang died amid the fighting.
连追五日五夜，长毛溃不成军，黄文金死在乱军之中了。

Hong Tianguifu, however, was still missing.
但是洪福真却还是下落不明；

The more credible reports said that he was moving south through Jiangxi to join Li Shixian and Wang Haiyang on the Guangdong–Fujian border.
比较可靠的传说是由江西南下，打算与窜至广东、福建边境的李世贤、汪海洋会合。

From there they intended to turn west toward Hubei, link up with King Fu, Chen Decai, and pass from the Jingzhou–Xiangyang region into Shaanxi, establishing themselves anew in Guanzhong.
然后西趋湖北；与『扶王』陈德才联结，自荆襄西入陕西，在关中另起一个局面。

This was certainly a hopeful calculation.
这当然是一把如意算盘。

Yet even if it failed, such ceaseless movement was dangerous: a junction with the Nian rebels of Anhui and Henan would give grave cause for concern.
但即令打不成功，这样窜来窜去，如与安徽、河南的捻匪合流亦是大可忧之事。

The court was therefore deeply angered that repeated, relentless pursuit had still failed to capture the young rebel and bring him to justice.
因此，朝廷对两次三番，穷追猛打，而竟未能促住『幼逆』，置之于法，深为恼火。

Zuo was angrier still.
更恼火的是左宗棠。

His memorial announcing “all Zhejiang cleared of rebels” had already gone forward, but with Hong Tianguifu uncaptured, the achievement remained incomplete.
『全浙肃清』的折子已经拜发，而洪福真未获，就不能算克竟全功，

For the time being, he could hardly expect his grant of nobility.
一时还难望分茅之赏。


Having disposed of the claim of “a hundred thousand,” Zuo turned to responsibility for impeaching negligent subordinates, and his language grew still sharper: “As for the statement that the entire enemy force escaped from Hangzhou and no impeachments were heard of, this is particularly incomprehensible.
辨明了『十万』之说；再论纠参部下的责任，言语晚为犀利∶『至云杭城全数出窜，未闻纠参，尤不可解。

Jinling had long been completely surrounded; Hangzhou had not been surrounded at all.
金陵早已合围，而杭州则并未能合围也；

From Jinling came the report, ‘All the rebels have been killed.’
金陵报』杀贼净尽『，

From Hangzhou came the report, ‘The principal rebels have indeed escaped.’”
杭州报「首逆实已窜出」也！』

Those two sentences alone had the force of an experienced magistrate’s judgment: Zeng Guoquan had been responsible for preventing escape, and Zeng Guofan had falsely reported the military situation.
仅是这两句话，便如老吏断狱，判定曾国荃有不容贼众逸出的责任，而曾国藩有谎报军情的罪过。

At the close, however, the pen turned again with calculated subtlety. “In military affairs, truth and substance are of the first importance; I have therefore had no choice but to explain.
但在结尾上，却又笔锋一转，故弄狡猾∶『臣因军事最尚质实，故不得不辩。

In all future public business I shall continue to consult and cooperate harmoniously.
至此后公事，均仍和衷商办，

I would not dare harbor the slightest prejudice and thereby increase my own fault.”
臣断不敢稍存意见，自重衍尤。』

These were needles wrapped in silk.
这段话是所谓『绵里针』，

Cautious and modest on the surface, the words were sharp beneath; every sentence contained a concealed accusation against Zeng Guofan.
看来戒慎谦和；其实棱角森然，句句暗隐着指责曾国藩的意思在内。


Within half a month of the memorial’s dispatch, a reply came.
这通奏折发出，不过半个月便有了回音。

Issued through Prince Gong, the confidential court dispatch was extraordinarily tactful.
由恭王出面的『廷寄』，措词异常婉转，

Instead of saying that Zuo could not yet be ennobled, it declared: “Since entering Zhejiang, Zuo Zongtang has recovered dozens of cities and fortified positions and cleared the whole province.
不说一时还不能封左宗棠的爵，却说『左宗棠自入浙以来，克复城隘数十处，肃清全境，

His achievement is very great.
厥功甚伟。

We had intended at once to bestow a substantial reward, but feared that, with the young rebel Hong still undestroyed, the governor-general would insist upon declining it.
本欲即加懋赏，恐该督以洪幼逆未灭，必将固辞；

As soon as the remaining rebels are eliminated, a decree of favor will be issued.”
一俟余孽净尽，即降恩旨。』

This clearly implied that Zuo’s ennoblement was only a matter of time.
是很明显地暗示，左宗棠封爵，不过迟早间事。

There was gentle reassurance concerning his dispute with Zeng as well. “The court does not wish to press meritorious servants over minor details.
关于他与曾国藩的争辩，亦有温愉∶『朝廷有功诸臣，不欲苛求细故。

When the young rebel Hong entered Zhejiang, the governor-general reported the facts; when he left the province, troops were sent in pursuit.
该督于洪幼逆之入浙，则据实入告；于其出境则派兵跟追，

Both were proper actions.
均属正办。

His declaration that he will continue to consult harmoniously with Zeng Guofan in public affairs, harboring no prejudice, particularly accords with the bearing of a great minister and deserves high praise.
所称此后公事仍与曾国潘和衷商办，不敢稍存意见，尤得大臣之体。深堪嘉尚。

The court’s expectations of him are great and far-reaching.
朝廷所望于该督者，至大且远；

Let him exert himself still further and become an eminent minister of his age, fulfilling our hopes.”
该督其益加勉励，为一代名臣，以副厚望。』

The edict did not reproach Zeng, but its judgment of right and wrong was clear.
上谕中虽未责备曾国藩，但是非好恶，已表现得很清楚。

The promise that Zuo might be “an eminent minister of his age” was especially rare language in an imperial decree.
而许左宗棠以『一代名臣』，更是上谕中难得一见的字样。

In this dispute of memorials, submitted to the sovereign’s judgment, Zuo had gained the advantage on every point.
总之这一场御裁的笔墨官司，左宗棠占尽上风；

Naturally, his enmity with Zeng deepened as well.
而与曾国藩的怨，自然也结得更深了。


On the surface, the quarrel between Zeng and Zuo was a war of words; beneath it lay a fierce struggle for territory.
曾左结怨，形诸表面的，是口舌之争；暗中拼命抵拒的，是地盘之争。

Caught between them, and at the same time bearing the first impact, was Zeng Guoquan.
而又象在夹缝中受挤，又象首当其冲的是曾国荃。

His substantive appointment was Governor of Zhejiang.
曾国荃的本职是浙江巡抚。

In wartime, to encourage commanders, appointments had not followed ordinary administrative boundaries.
用失之时，为了鼓励将帅，不按建制任职；

A senior official of one province leading troops in another was nothing unusual.
此省大员在他省领兵，事所常有。

Once a phase of the war ended, however, the situation changed.
但战事告一段落，情形就不一样了。

By ordinary reasoning, whatever faults Zeng Guoquan might have committed after taking Jiangning, his distinction in a hundred battles was undeniable.
照常理而论，曾国荃即令破江宁以后有过失，到底百战功高；

The proper course was to let him assume his governorship.
应该让他赴浙江巡抚本任，才是正办。

But Zuo, Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang and acting Governor of Zhejiang, had no intention of giving way.
无奈左宗棠以闽浙总督兼署浙巡，绝无退让之意。

Zeng Guofan requested sick leave for his brother partly out of anxiety over slander and criticism, seeking prudent retirement at the height of success.
而曾国藩为曾国荃告病，虽由于忧谗畏讥，以急流勇退作明哲保身之计；

More fundamentally, he had perceived the hidden uncertainty of his brother’s position.
其实亦是看透了老弟有『妾身不分明』的隐衷，

Judging that Guoquan could never take up the post, he thought it better to recognize the situation and withdraw voluntarily.
估量他决不能到任，不如自己知趣。


The court was in a dilemma.
在朝廷却又能左右为难之苦。

On one hand, military operations in the southeast had culminated in the recovery of Huzhou and the pacification of Zhejiang, so Zuo’s dignity had to be considered.
一方面东南军务地穴于湖州克复、全浙肃清，不能不敷衍左宗棠的面子；

On the other, to let a newly created earl lay down his arms and retire to the countryside seemed an improper way to treat a meritorious servant.
一方面却又觉得真个让簇新的一位伯爵，解甲归田，不是待功臣之道。

The request for Zeng Guoquan’s retirement on grounds of illness was therefore left unresolved.
因此，对于曾国荃告病，一直采拖延着不作明确的处置；

The court hoped that Zeng and Zuo might put aside their resentment and be reconciled, with Zuo himself requesting permission to relinquish the acting governorship and an order for Guoquan to take office.
希望曾左之间，能够消释嫌怨，言归于好，由左宗棠出面奏请交卸篆，饬令曾国荃到任。

That hope was impossible to fulfill.
这是个不能实现的奢望。

At last the court saw that indefinite delay would not do and agreed to Guofan’s wish, allowing Guoquan to resign.
朝廷看看拖着不是回事，决定成全曾国藩的心愿，许曾国荃辞职。

Who should fill the vacancy, however, required careful thought.
可是空出来的浙江巡抚这个缺，由谁替补？却颇费斟酌。

The court knew what Zuo wanted: Jiang Yili promoted from provincial treasurer to governor, with the treasury vacancy filled on Zuo’s recommendation.
朝廷也知道左宗棠的意思，最好是让蒋益澧由藩司升任，而浙江藩司一缺，则由左宗棠保荐。

But Jiang lacked sufficient seniority and standing; such an arrangement would also humiliate Zeng Guofan.
无奈蒋益澧的资望还浅；并且这样处置，在曾国藩的面子上太难看。

In balancing its commanders, the court would not lightly give the impression of favoring one man.
朝廷调和将帅，决不肯轻易予人以偏袒某人的印象，

Zuo’s wishes were therefore ruled out.
所以左宗棠的意愿是不考虑的了。


Three considerations remained. First, the new Governor of Zhejiang must be honest, experienced, and capable.
要考虑的是∶第一、新任浙江巡抚确需清廉练达的干才，

Of all the provinces ravaged by Hong Xiuquan and Yang Xiuqing, Zhejiang had suffered most, and its reconstruction would be the hardest.
因为洪杨所蹂躏的各省，浙江被祸最惨；善后事宜亦最难办，

Nothing less than integrity and practical ability would suffice.
非清廉干练，不足以胜任。

Second, he should have neither obligations nor grievances toward Zuo, while enjoying the support of Zeng Guofan and even Li Hongzhang, so that Zhejiang could obtain its neighbors’ help.
第二、此人要与左宗棠没有什么恩怨；而又能为曾国藩，甚至李鸿章 所支持，然后浙江的善后事宜，才能取得邻省的援助。

Third, with the great rebellion suppressed, arms were to give way to civil government.
第三、大乱已平，偃武修文；

This would be the first provincial governorship filled since the defeat of the Taipings, marking the restoration of civil rule.
浙江巡抚是洪杨平后委派的第一员封疆大吏，也是恢复文治的开始，

The appointee should preferably have risen through the examinations.
所以此人最好科甲出身。

Military achievements as well would make him ideal.
如果有过战功，更为理想。

An exceptionally suitable man was eventually chosen: Ma Xinyi, courtesy name Gushan.
结果选中了一个很理想的人。此人名叫马新贻，字谷山；

His ancestors were Hui Muslims who had helped the founding Ming emperor win the empire.
先世是回回，从明太祖打天下有功，

Appointed military officers in a Shandong garrison, they settled in Heze County, Caozhou Prefecture.
派在山东卫所当武官，定居曹州府荷泽县，

More than four hundred years had passed.
已历四百余年之久，

Apart from his adherence to Islam, Ma was thoroughly a native son of Shandong.
因此，马新贻除了信回教以外，彻头彻尾是个山东土著。
