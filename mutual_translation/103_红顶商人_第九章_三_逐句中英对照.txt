胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第九章（本章第3部分，共7部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


在马新贻的新命传至浙江的同时；江西来了一个重要而有趣的消息，『幼逆』洪福真终于落网了。
As news of Ma Xinyi’s appointment reached Zhejiang, an important and intriguing report arrived from Jiangxi: the young rebel Hong Tianguifu had at last been captured.

收束平洪杨的军务，却还有相当艰巨的戡乱大任，需要部署。
The campaign against the Taipings was drawing to a close, but formidable work remained before disorder could be put down.

恭王、文祥的计议，犹有三处叛乱要平服，才能臻于太平盛世。这三处叛乱是∶第一、南窜的洪杨余孽；第二是扰乱中原的捻匪；第三是荼毒生灵、为患西陲的回乱。
In the judgment of Prince Gong and Wenxiang, three rebellions still stood between the empire and lasting peace: the Taiping remnants who had fled south, the Nian bands disturbing the central provinces, and the Muslim rebellions devastating the western frontier.

幸好人才旺盛，冠绝前朝；
Fortunately, men of talent abounded in numbers surpassing earlier reigns.

恭王与文祥决定托付四个人去平这三处的叛乱。
Prince Gong and Wenxiang decided to entrust the suppression of these three troubles to four men.

第一个仍然是曾国藩。
The first was still Zeng Guofan.

在十月初一曾国荃功成身退，率领裁撤的湘军回湖南的同时，朝中有一道廷寄递到江宁，说『江宁已臻底平，军务业经藏事，
On the first day of the tenth month, as Zeng Guoquan retired in triumph and led the disbanded Hunan troops home, a confidential dispatch reached Jiangning: “Jiangning is now entirely pacified, and military operations there are concluded.

即着曾国藩酌带所部，前赴皖鄂交界，督兵剿贼，
Zeng Guofan shall select a portion of his forces and proceed to the Anhui–Hubei border to direct the suppression of the rebels.

务期迅速前进，勿少延缓。』
He must advance speedily and permit no delay.”

这所谓『贼』，便是捻匪。
These “rebels” were the Nian.


捻匪原以皖北为老巢，
The Nian had originally made northern Anhui their base.

自经僧王全力攻剿，流窜到湖北、河南一带。
Under Prince Sengge Rinchen’s vigorous attacks, they had moved into Hubei and Henan.

张洛行虽死，他的侄子张总愚亦非弱者；
Though Zhang Luoxing was dead, his nephew Zhang Zongyu was no weak successor.

加以陈玉成的旧部赖文光由关中回窜，
Lai Wenguang, formerly one of Chen Yucheng’s commanders, had also returned from Guanzhong.

因为『天京』已破，成了丧家之犬，自然而然地与捻匪合流，大为猖獗。
With the Heavenly Capital fallen, he was a man without a home; naturally he joined the Nian, greatly increasing their strength and boldness.

朝廷深知僧王的马队，追奔逐北，将捻匪撵来撵去的打法，并非善策；
The court knew that the prince’s cavalry tactics—pursuing and driving the Nian from place to place—were unsound.

一旦疲于奔命，为捻匪反扑，非大败不可。
Once his troops exhausted themselves, a rebel counterattack must bring disaster.

同时，又因为僧王的身分尊贵，连西宫太后都不能不格外优容，是位极难伺候的王爷，
Yet his exalted position made him extremely difficult to handle. Even the Western Empress Dowager had to show him special indulgence.

指授方略，则『将在外君命有所不受』；
Offer strategic instructions, and he could invoke the maxim that a commander in the field need not obey every order of his sovereign.

稍加督责又怕惹恼了他，索性独断独行。
Press him a little, and he might take offense and act entirely on his own.

因此，倒不如设法让他交卸军权，回京享福，才是公私两便之计。
It would be better, for public and private reasons alike, to find a way for him to relinquish his command and return to enjoy life in Beijing.

能代僧王指挥数省的，只有一个曾国藩。
Only Zeng Guofan could replace him in directing operations across several provinces.

不仅威望足够；而且他那『先求稳当，次求变化』，以静制静，稳扎稳打的作风，亦正可救僧王之失。
His prestige was sufficient, and his practice of “seeking security first and flexibility afterward”—meeting movement with stillness, advancing steadily from firm positions—would correct the prince’s mistakes.

至于筹饷之责，朝廷也想到了一个必不可少的人。
For the financing of the campaign, the court had thought of another indispensable man.


这个人就是李鸿章.上谕派他接替曾国藩，暂署两江总督；江苏巡抚则调慈禧太后的恩人，漕运总督吴棠署理。
That man was Li Hongzhang. An edict appointed him to act in Zeng’s place as Governor-General of Liangjiang, while Wu Tang, Director-General of Grain Transport and a benefactor of Empress Dowager Cixi, was transferred to act as Governor of Jiangsu.

上谕中虽未明言，曾国藩带兵驻扎皖鄂交界，从路粮台由李鸿章 负其全责；可是这样部署的用意是很明白的，
The decree did not expressly state that Li would bear full responsibility for the rear supply bureau supporting Zeng on the Anhui–Hubei frontier, but the intention was plain.

第一，曾、李师生，『有事弟子服其劳』，天经地义；
First, Zeng and Li were teacher and pupil; it was only proper that “when there is work to be done, the disciple undertakes the labor.”

第二，李鸿章 带兵，曾国藩替他筹过饷，如今曾国藩带兵，自然该李鸿章 筹饷；
Second, Zeng had raised funds when Li commanded in the field; now their roles should be reversed.

第三，两江最富，是海内最主要的一处饷源，所以谁当两江总督，都有筹饷的责任。
Third, Liangjiang was the richest region and a principal source of military revenue, so whoever governed it was bound to provide funds.

这样的安排，就大局而言，不能算错；
Viewed in terms of the general situation, the arrangement was sound.

只是委屈了曾国藩，便宜了李鸿章与吴棠，可也就顾不得那么许多了。
It merely disadvantaged Zeng and benefited Li and Wu—but not every consideration could be accommodated.


再有一个是杨岳斌。他是与彭玉麟齐名的水师名将，
Another man selected was Yang Yuebin, a naval commander whose fame equaled Peng Yulin’s.

本名杨载福；
His original name was Yang Zaifu.

因为同治皇帝这一辈，玉牒谱系上第一字为『载』，不免有犯讳的不便，所以改名岳斌。
Since the first character of the Tongzhi Emperor’s generation in the imperial genealogy was Zai, however, the name risked offending the naming taboo, and he changed it to Yuebin.

当江宁未克复以前，他已升任陕甘总督；打算赋以敉平回乱的重任。
Before Jiangning fell he had already been appointed Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu, with the intention of entrusting him with the suppression of the Muslim rebellion.

回乱不仅生于陕甘；也生于云南与新疆。
Such rebellions had broken out not only there but in Yunnan and Xinjiang as well.

云南将次平服，而新疆方兴未艾；
Yunnan was approaching pacification, while Xinjiang’s troubles were gathering force.

朝廷寄望于新封子爵的鲍超，
For the latter, the court placed its hopes in the newly created viscount Bao Chao.

特降温旨，认为新疆平乱，『非得勇略出群如鲍超者，前往剿办，恐难壁垒一新』，
A warmly worded decree declared that without “a man of exceptional courage and strategy such as Bao Chao” it would be difficult to transform the military situation in Xinjiang.

所以命曾国藩传旨鲍超，在他回籍葬亲的两月假期一满，『即行由川起程，出关剿办回乱。』
Zeng was therefore ordered to transmit instructions that, upon the expiry of Bao’s two-month leave to bury his parent, he should “depart immediately from Sichuan, pass beyond the frontier, and suppress the Muslim rebellion.”

恭王和文祥知道鲍超好名，特地拿乾嘉名将杨遇春，与他相提并论，很灌了一番米汤。
Knowing Bao’s love of renown, Prince Gong and Wenxiang flattered him by comparing him with Yang Yuchun, the celebrated general of the Qianlong and Jiaqing reigns.

上谕中说∶『从前回疆用兵，杨遇春即系川省土著，立功边域，彪炳旅常。
“In former campaigns in the Muslim territories, Yang Yuchun, himself a native of Sichuan, won frontier victories whose glory shone upon the military banners.

鲍超务当督率诸国，肃清西陲，威扬万里，以与前贤后先辉映。
Bao Chao must lead his forces to pacify the western marches, carrying his fame ten thousand li and matching the splendor of his predecessor.

该提督忠勇性成，接奉此旨，必即遵行，以逼朝廷委任。』
Loyal and brave by nature, he will surely obey upon receiving this decree and fulfill the court’s trust.”

话说得很诚挚，
The language was most earnest.

而命曾国藩传旨，亦有暗示他帮着催劝之意。
By requiring Zeng to transmit it, the court also hinted that he should help urge Bao forward.

无奈曾国藩对湘军的急流勇退，明哲保身，早有定算；
Zeng, however, had long resolved that the Hunan Army should withdraw prudently at the height of its success.

鲍超是他的爱将，当然要加意保全，
Bao was a favorite commander whom he particularly wished to protect.

所以只是照例传旨，并不劝驾。
He simply conveyed the edict in the usual way and offered no encouragement to depart.


再有一个朝廷寄以重望的，便是左宗棠。
The other man in whom the court placed great hopes was Zuo.

他是现任的闽浙总督，由江西瑞金为鲍超所败，而窜入福建境内的李世贤、汪海洋两大股，顺理成章 地该由他负责清剿。
As Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang, he was naturally responsible for suppressing the two large forces of Li Shixian and Wang Haiyang, defeated by Bao at Ruijin in Jiangxi and driven into Fujian.

左宗棠不是怕事的人，
Zuo was not a man to shrink from trouble.

对此亦自觉当仁不让，义不容辞；
He regarded the duty as his by right and could not honorably refuse.

可是朝廷一连串的处置，却使他即气又急，愤愤不平。
Yet a succession of court decisions made him angry, anxious, and deeply resentful.

首先大失所望的是，浙江巡抚派了马新贻；
His first disappointment was Ma Xinyi’s appointment to Zhejiang.

蒋益澧落了空，也就等于是他失去了浙江这个地盘。
Jiang Yili had been passed over, which meant that Zuo had lost control of the province.

其次是李鸿章 调署两江，名位已在己之上，使他很不舒服。
Next came Li’s transfer to act as Governor-General of Liangjiang, placing him above Zuo in standing and precedence—a most unpleasant thought.

其次是在江西的陕甘总督杨岳斌，奉旨迅即到任；
Then Yang Yuebin, still in Jiangxi, was ordered to proceed promptly to his post in Shaanxi and Gansu.

朝廷责成浙江每月拨给陕甘协饷十万两，并先筹措八万银子，作为杨军的开拔费用。
Zhejiang was required to contribute a hundred thousand taels a month toward his military expenses and first to raise eighty thousand for his army’s departure.

为此，左宗棠的肝火很旺，
Zuo’s temper flared.

每日接见僚属，大骂曾国藩、李鸿章 和郭嵩焘。
Every day, when receiving his staff, he roundly abused Zeng Guofan, Li Hongzhang, and Guo Songtao.

这样骂了几天，怒火稍减；
After several days his anger abated a little.

想想既不肯辞官归田，就得有声有色地大干一番。
Since he would not resign and return to farming, he must undertake something substantial and impressive.

军务是有把握的，就是饷源越来越绌，
He was confident of his military ability; the difficulty was an ever-shrinking revenue base.

得要找个足智多谋的人，趁马新贻末曾到任以前，好好筹划妥当。
Before Ma arrived, he needed a resourceful man to help arrange matters properly.


这个人自然非胡雪岩莫属。
That man could only be Hu.

『雪翁，』他说，『你看，挤得我无路可走了！
“Mr Xueyan,” Zuo said, “they have squeezed me until I have nowhere to turn.

你算算看，我该到哪里筹饷？
Where do you think I can raise funds?

哪里都难！』
It is difficult everywhere.”

两个人将十五行省一个一个地算。
Together they went through the fifteen provinces one by one.

除开穷瘠的省份，有饷可筹的富庶之地，都已为他人早着先鞭；
Leaving aside the poor provinces, every wealthy source of revenue had already been claimed.

江苏、安徽是两江辖区，曾李师弟的势力，根深蒂固；
Jiangsu and Anhui lay within Liangjiang, where Zeng and his pupil Li were deeply entrenched.

江西沈葆桢，对待曾军的前例，足以令人望而却步；
Shen Baozhen’s treatment of the Hunan Army in Jiangxi was warning enough.

山东、山西供应京饷，而且两省巡抚阎敬铭、沈桂芬清刚精明，都不是好相与的人；
Shandong and Shanxi supplied the capital, and their governors, Yan Jingming and Shen Guifen, were upright, shrewd, and difficult men to deal with.

湖北食用川盐，在沙市设局征厘，收入相当可观，
Hubei consumed Sichuan salt, collecting substantial likin revenue through an office at Shashi.

可是官文是督抚中唯一的一个旗人，有理无理，皆受朝廷袒护，不容易打得进去；
But Guanwen was the only bannerman among the governors and governors-general; right or wrong, he enjoyed the court’s protection, and gaining access there would be hard.

至于天府之国的四川，有骆秉章 在那里，
As for prosperous Sichuan, Luo Bingzhang governed it.

顾念旧日宾主之谊，自然不好意思唱一出『取成都』。
Remembering their old relationship as patron and secretary, Zuo could hardly bring himself to play out a “seizure of Chengdu.”

『福建穷得很；
“Fujian is desperately poor.

我能筹饷的地方，只有贵省和广东了。
The only places left to me are your province and Guangdong.

东该给我的饷不给；
Guangdong withholds the contributions due to me.

可恨郭筠仙，心目中只认得曾涤生、李少荃。
That hateful Guo Yunxian recognizes no one but Zeng Disheng and Li Shaoquan!

此恨难消！』
I cannot forget it.”

左宗棠停了一下又说，
Zuo paused.

『至于马谷山，听说倒还讲理；
“As for Ma Gushan, I hear he is reasonable enough.

不过既是曾涤生所保，又是李少荃的同年，
But Zeng recommended him, and he passed the examinations in the same year as Li.

不见得肯助我一臂。
He may not be willing to help me.

雪翁，你看我该怎么办？』
Mr Xueyan, what am I to do?”


胡雪岩默然。
Hu remained silent.

因为他觉得自己的处境很难，
His position was difficult.

左宗棠的知遇要报答；而浙江是自己的家乡，
He must repay Zuo’s recognition and favor, yet Zhejiang was his home province.

为左宗棠设谋画策，可不能挨地方父老的骂。
He could not devise schemes for Zuo that would earn the curses of its people.

胡雪岩一向言词爽利，而且不管天大的难事，一诺无辞；象这样迟疑不答的情形，可说绝无仅有。
Hu usually spoke decisively and accepted even the greatest difficulties without hesitation; such a prolonged silence was almost unprecedented.

左宗棠微感诧异，不免追问缘故。
Mildly surprised, Zuo pressed him for an explanation.

『不瞒大人说，我很为难。
“To be frank, my lord, I am in a difficult position.

大人现在只有浙江一个地盘，粮饷当然出在浙江，
Zhejiang is now the only province you can draw upon, so naturally your supplies and pay must come from it.

筹得少了不够用；筹得多了，苦了地方。
Raise too little, and it will not suffice; raise too much, and the people suffer.

说起来是我胡某人出的主意；
Everyone will say it was my idea.

本乡本土，我不大好做人。』
Among my own countrymen, that would be an uncomfortable position.

雪岩又说，『如果大人兼署浙江巡抚，我还可以出出主意，截长补短，见机行事，总还兼顾得到。
If you continued to act as Governor of Zhejiang, I could still suggest ways to balance one source against another and adapt to circumstances, taking both interests into account.

现在换了马中丞，
But Governor Ma is coming.

我又是分发江西的试用道，是大人奏调我在浙江当差；
I am only a probationary circuit intendant assigned to Jiangxi, whom you had transferred here for service.

大人一离浙江，我当然不能再问浙江的公事，
Once you leave Zhejiang, I shall have no right to concern myself with its affairs.

善后局的差使亦要交卸，何况其他？』
I must even hand over my post at the Reconstruction Bureau, let alone anything else.”


他一路说，左宗棠一路点头，
Zuo nodded throughout.

等他说完，做个『稍安毋躁』的手势答道∶『你刚才所说的情形，我完全清楚，
When Hu finished, he gestured for patience. “I understand all the circumstances you have described.

我们要好好谈谈。
We must discuss them carefully.

万变不离的宗旨是∶雪翁，你仍旧要帮我的忙。
Through every change, one principle remains: you must continue to help me.

怎么个帮法，我们回头再商量，
How you are to help we shall consider presently.

现在先谈你的难处；
First, let us deal with your difficulty.

诚如所言，我现在只有浙江一个地盘，粮饷只有着落在浙江，
As you say, Zhejiang is my only base, and Zhejiang must provide my army’s funds.

而且要定一个确数，按月一定汇到，连日子都错不得一天。
Moreover, there must be a fixed sum, remitted each month on a fixed date, not one day late.

雪翁，凡事先讲理，后讲情；
Mr Xueyan, in everything we first establish what is reasonable, then appeal to personal feeling.

情理都站得住，还争不过人家，我当然也有我的手段。』
If both reason and feeling support us and we still cannot prevail, I naturally have methods of my own.”

胡雪岩不知他最后这几句话，意何所指？
Hu did not know what the last words implied.

只能就事论事，问一事∶『大人预备定一个啥数目？』
Keeping to the immediate issue, he asked, “What sum do you intend to fix, my lord?”

『你看呢？』
“What would you suggest?”

左宗棠放低了声音说∶『我们自己人，我告诉你实话∶我的兵，实数一万八千，不过筹饷要宽，
Zuo lowered his voice. “Between ourselves, I shall tell you the truth. I actually have eighteen thousand men, but funding must allow a margin.

照两万三千人算。』
We should calculate for twenty-three thousand.”

胡雪岩的心算极快。
Hu reckoned rapidly.

士兵每人每月饷银、军粮、器械、弹药、马草，加上营帐、锅碗等等杂支，平均要五两银子；
A soldier’s monthly pay, grain, weapons, ammunition, horse fodder, and miscellaneous needs—tents, cooking pots, bowls, and the rest—averaged five taels.

两万三千人就是十一万五千两。
Twenty-three thousand men meant a hundred and fifteen thousand taels.

另加统帅个人的用途；文案、委员的薪水伙食；送往迎来的应酬费用，每个月非十五万银子不可。
Add the commander’s own expenses, the salaries and board of secretaries and commissioned officials, and the costs of hospitality, and no less than a hundred and fifty thousand taels a month would do.


这笔巨数，由浙江独力负担，未免太重；
For Zhejiang to bear that enormous sum alone was too much.

胡雪岩便很婉转地说道∶『闽浙一家。
Hu therefore spoke tactfully. “Fujian and Zhejiang are one family.

福建拨给浙江的协饷，前后总计，不下三百万两之多；
Over the years, Fujian has contributed at least three million taels toward Zhejiang’s military expenses.

如今福建有事，当然要帮忙。
Now that Fujian has trouble, we should naturally help.

而况大人带的又是浙江的兵，理当浙江支饷。
Besides, the troops you command are Zhejiang troops; Zhejiang ought to pay them.

不过，浙江的情形，大人是再明白不过的；
But you understand the province’s condition better than anyone.

如果能够量出为入，事情就好办了。』
If we could determine our income by our expenditure, matters would be easier.”

成语是量入为出，胡雪岩却反过来说，倒也新鲜；
The usual maxim was to determine expenditure by income; Hu had reversed it, which was something new.

左宗棠便捻着八字胡子，含笑问道∶『何以谓之量出为入？
Stroking his drooping mustache, Zuo asked with a smile, “What do you mean by determining income by expenditure?

倒要请教。』
I should like to hear.”

『譬如一碗汤，
“Suppose there is a bowl of soup.

你也舀，他也舀，到嘴都有限┅┅。』『啊！』左宗棠抢着说道∶『我懂了！
One man takes a ladleful, another takes a ladleful; very little reaches anyone’s mouth—” “Ah!” Zuo interrupted. “I understand.

我亦本有此意，
I had the same thought.

第一，陕甘的协饷，决不能答应；
First, we absolutely cannot agree to the contribution for Shaanxi and Gansu.

第二，广东解浙江的协饷，有名无实，
Second, Guangdong’s contribution to Zhejiang exists only in name.

我要奏请停拨。』
I shall petition to have it discontinued.”

说到这里，他眼珠打转，慢慢地笑了，笑得极其诡秘。
At this his eyes moved from side to side, and a slow, exceedingly secretive smile spread over his face.


这一笑，大有文章.胡雪岩觉得非搞明白不可；便有意套问一句∶『广东的协饷是个画饼，
There was much behind that smile. Hu felt he must understand it and deliberately probed. “Guangdong’s contribution is a picture of a cake.

虽不能充饥，看看也是好的。』
Though it cannot feed us, it is something to look at.”

『不然！
“No.

奏请停拨，就是要让朝廷知道，这是个画饼。
By asking for its cancellation, I shall show the court that it is only a painted cake.

雪翁，』左宗棠突然兴奋了，
Mr Xueyan—” Zuo suddenly grew excited.

『你看老夫的手段！
“Watch this old man’s methods!

画饼要把它变成个又大又厚，足供一饱的大麦饼。
I shall turn the painted cake into a great thick barley cake, enough to fill our bellies.

你信不信？』
Do you believe me?”

『怎么不信？』
“Of course.

胡雪岩紧接着问，『大人变这套戏法，可要我做下手？』
Will you need me as an assistant in this conjuring trick?”

『当然！
“Naturally!

少了你，我这套平地抠饼，外带大锯活人的戏法就变不成了。』
Without you I could never perform this trick of producing a cake from bare ground—and sawing a living man in two besides.”

『大锯活人』四字，虽是戏言，却也刺耳，
Though meant as a joke, the last words were jarring.

胡雪岩便用半开玩笑的语气问道∶『大人，你要锯哪一个？』
Half in jest, Hu asked, “Whom do you mean to saw in two, my lord?”

『哪一个？』
“Whom?”

左宗棠有种狞笑的神色，
Zuo’s smile had grown fierce.

『锯我那位亲家。』
“My own in-law.”

胡雪岩骇然。
Hu was appalled.

他早知左宗棠跟郭嵩焘有心病，而此心病，不但未能由时光来冲淡，反有与日俱深之势；
He had long known that Zuo bore a grievance against Guo Songtao, one that time had deepened rather than eased.

但何致于说出『大锯活人』的这样的话来？
But how had it reached the point of such language?

因此一时楞在那里作声不得。
For a moment he stood speechless.

左宗棠的脸上，也收起嬉笑之态，
Zuo too ceased smiling.

变得相当认真，眼睁得好在，嘴闭得好紧；但眼神闪烁，嘴唇翕动，竟似心湖中起了极大的波澜似的。
His face grew serious, his eyes wide, his mouth firmly closed; yet his gaze flickered and his lips moved, as though powerful waves were passing through his mind.

这就使得胡雪岩越发贯注全神，要听他如何『大锯活人』了。
Hu listened with even closer attention to hear how this “sawing a living man in two” was to be done.


『雪岩！』
“Xueyan!”

左宗棠第一次改口，以别字相呼，表示对胡雪岩以密友看待，
For the first time Zuo dropped the formal address and used his courtesy name, treating him as an intimate friend.

『你的书读得不多，我是知道的；
“I know you have not read many books.

不过「世事洞明皆学问」，照这一层来说，我佩服你。』
But understanding the world is itself learning, and in that respect I admire you.”

『不敢当。』
“You are too kind.”

胡雪岩有些局促，但也很率直，
Hu was a little uneasy but remained frank.

『大人有什么话要说，尽管吩咐；
“If you have something to say, please give me your instructions.

拿顶「高帽子」套在我头上，就有点吃不消了。』
Such a tall hat of praise is rather too much for me.”

『你我之间，何用要什么送高帽子的手段？
“Why should I need flattery between us?

我的意思是，我的为人，我的处世，只有你能明白五分；
What I mean is that you understand half of my character and conduct.

还有五分，你不但不明白，或许还会大不以为然。
The other half you not only fail to understand, but may strongly disapprove of.

这就因为你少读书；
That is because you have read too little.

如果你也多读过一点书，就会明白我那另外五分，而且谅解我不得不然；势所必然！』
If you had studied more, you would understand that other half as well, and see that I have no choice: circumstances compel it.”

原来如此，
So that was what he meant.

胡雪岩倒有些受宠若惊了，
Hu felt almost overwhelmed by the confidence shown him.

『大人』他说∶『你老跟我谈「大家之道，在明明德」，我是不懂的。』
“My lord, if you speak to me of ‘the Way of Great Learning lies in manifesting luminous virtue,’ I shall understand nothing.”

『我不跟你谈经，我跟你谈史。
“I shall speak of history, not the Classics.

雪岩，我先请问你两句成语，「大义灭亲」、「公而忘私」怎么讲？』
First, tell me what these two sayings mean: ‘For a greater righteousness, sacrifice even one’s kin,’ and ‘For public duty, forget private interests.’”


胡雪岩无以为答；觉得也不必答，
Hu had no answer, nor felt one was needed.

老实回复∶『大人不要考我了。
He replied honestly, “Please do not examine me, my lord.

就从这两句成语上头，谈你老的打算。』
Begin with those sayings and tell me your plans.”

『我不是考你，
“I am not examining you.

我的意思是，我的行事，照世俗之见，或许会大大地骂我。
My point is that ordinary people may condemn my actions bitterly.

不过，我的行事，于亲有亏，于义无悖；于私有惭，于公无愧。
Yet though I fail my kin, I do not fail what is right; though I may be ashamed in private relationships, I have nothing to blush for in public duty.

这都非世俗之见所能谅解，
Common opinion cannot understand this.

而只有读过书的人，才会在心里说一声∶左某人命世之英，
Only a learned man will say in his heart: ‘Zuo is a hero born for his age.

不得不然。』
He could do no otherwise.’”

这段话很掉了几句文，不过胡雪岩也大致还能听得懂；而且听出意思，他对郭嵩焘要下辣手了！
The speech was rather bookish, but Hu understood it well enough and grasped its implication: Zuo meant to deal Guo Songtao a severe blow.

所想不通的是，他有何辣手可对郭嵩焘？
What he could not see was what blow lay within Zuo’s power.

他的疑问，立刻得到了解答；
His question was answered at once.

左宗棠起身坐在书桌前面，伸毫铺纸，
Zuo rose, sat at his desk, took a brush, and spread out paper.

很快地画成一幅地图，在那些曲线、圆点之中，写上地名；
He rapidly drew a map, adding place-names among the curved lines and dots.

胡雪岩看出是一幅闽粤交界的形势图。
Hu recognized the Fujian–Guangdong frontier.

『李世贤在漳州。漳州是九月十四沦陷的，
“Li Shixian is at Zhangzhou, which fell on the fourteenth day of the ninth month.

总兵禄魁阵亡；汀漳龙道徐晓峰殉难。
Brigade Commander Lukui was killed in action; Circuit Intendant Xu Xiaofeng of Tingzhou, Zhangzhou, and Longyan died at his post.

李世贤大概有八千多人，不可轻敌。』
Li has probably more than eight thousand men and must not be underestimated.”

左宗棠又指着长汀、连城、上杭这三角地带说∶『汪海洋在这一带；
Pointing to the triangle formed by Changting, Liancheng, and Shanghang, Zuo continued, “Wang Haiyang is here.

照我的看法，他比李世贤更凶悍。
In my judgment he is fiercer than Li.

然而，不足为虑，贼不足平！
Still, there is no cause for alarm about putting these rebels down.

雪岩，你这几年总也懂得一点兵法了！
Xueyan, you must have learned a little strategy in recent years.

你看李、汪二贼的出路在哪里？』
Where do you think Li and Wang can escape?”


这一下好象考倒了胡雪岩。
For a moment Hu seemed stumped.

他仔细看了半天，方始答说『他们是由西面江西逃过来的；
He studied the map closely before answering. “They came from Jiangxi in the west.

往东是出海，有好长一段路，再说没有船也出不了海。
To the east lies the sea, a long way off—and without ships they could not put to sea anyway.

北面呢，大人带兵压了下来，
In the north, your troops are pressing down.

啊，』
Ah!”

胡雪岩恍然大悟，很有把握地说∶『这两个长毛的出路，只有南面的广东，
Understanding came suddenly, and he spoke with confidence. “Their only way out is south into Guangdong.

嘉应州首当其冲！』
Jiaying Prefecture will bear the first attack.”

左宗棠深深点头，拈髭微笑，
Zuo nodded deeply, stroking his mustache with a smile.

『对，』
“Exactly.

他说，『嘉应州首当其冲！
Jiaying will bear the first attack.

到了那时候充饥的就不是画饼了！』
Then we shall no longer be feeding ourselves on painted cakes.”

语中有深意。
There was a hidden meaning in his words.

左宗棠没有说下去；胡雪岩不便回——怕自己猜错了，冒昧一关，是大大的失言。
He did not continue, and Hu dared not ask, fearing that a mistaken guess might lead him into a grave indiscretion.

谁知左宗棠毫不忌讳，
Zuo, however, had no such reservations.

真的拿胡雪岩当可共极端机密的心腹看待，
He truly regarded Hu as a confidant fit to hear the deepest secrets.

『郭筠仙一直担心曾涤生「驱寇入粤」，
“Guo Yunxian has always feared that Zeng Disheng would drive the rebels into Guangdong.

他没有想到「驱寇入粤」的是他的亲家。』
He never imagined it would be his own in-law who did so.

他说∶『雪岩，到那时候，又另是一番局面了。』
When that happens, Xueyan, the situation will be entirely different.”

胡雪岩不自觉地打了个寒噤，
Hu shuddered involuntarily.

觉得左宗棠的手段真是太辣了些！
Zuo’s methods seemed altogether too ruthless.

虽然，这正是他所猜想到的，但测度是测度，听别人亲口证实，感觉又自不同。
He had guessed this very thing, certainly; but a conjecture was one matter, and hearing it confirmed in the man’s own words was another.


『雪岩，』左宗棠问道∶『你倒说说看到那时候是怎么样的一番局面？』
“Xueyan, tell me what the situation will be then.”

『是。』
“Yes.”

胡雪岩想了想说，
Hu considered.

『到那时候，朝廷当然借重大人的威望，拜钦差大臣，节制福建、浙江、广东三省的军务。
“The court will naturally turn to your prestige and ability, appoint you imperial commissioner, and place military affairs in Fujian, Zhejiang, and Guangdong under your authority.

郭中丞——。』他没有再说下去；
Governor Guo—” He stopped.

意思是郭嵩焘在左宗棠『大锯活人』的摆布之下，非吃足苦头不可。
His meaning was clear: under Zuo’s handling, Guo would suffer severely.

『不错，
“Precisely.

此亦是势所必然之事。
Circumstances must lead to that.

到那时候，雪岩，我不会再累浙江了，
Then, Xueyan, I shall no longer burden Zhejiang.

不怕郭筠仙不乖乖替我筹饷。
Guo Yunxian will have to raise funds obediently for me.

不过，』左宗棠沉吟了好一会，
Unless—” Zuo pondered for a long while.

『也说不定！
“There is no certainty.

郭筠仙愚而好自用；
Guo is foolish and obstinate.

怕他仍旧执迷不悟。』
He may still refuse to see reason.”

『果然如此，大人又怎么办？』
“And if he does, my lord, what will you do?”
