胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第九章（本章第4部分，共7部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


『那就不能怪我了！
“Then he cannot blame me.

可惜！』
What a pity!”

前后两句话不接气，
The two remarks seemed disconnected.

胡雪岩再机敏也猜不透他的意思；
Quick-witted as he was, Hu could not guess their meaning.

只以此事于减轻浙江的负担关系甚大，不能不追问∶『大人，可惜些什么？』
Since the matter concerned a possible reduction of Zhejiang’s burden, he had to ask. “A pity in what respect, my lord?”

『可惜，我夹袋里没有可以当巡抚的人物。』
“A pity I have no suitable man in reserve to become a governor.”

这是说，如果将来郭嵩焘不能替左宗棠筹得足够的饷；他不惜攻倒他派人取而代之。
In other words, if Guo Songtao failed to raise enough money for him, Zuo would not hesitate to bring him down and put someone else in his place.

这样做法，却真是『公而忘私』、『大义灭亲』了。
That would indeed be “forgetting private interests for public duty” and “sacrificing kin for a greater righteousness.”

『到时候看吧！
“We shall see when the time comes.

言之过早。』
It is too early to speak of it.”

左宗棠对着他手绘的地图凝视了好一会，
Zuo stared for some time at his map.

突然拍案而起，
Suddenly he struck the desk and sprang up.

『对，
“Yes!

就是这么办！』
That is how it shall be done!”


接着，左宗棠谈了他的突如其来的灵感。他指着地图为胡雪岩解释，
He explained his sudden inspiration, pointing to the map.

自己的兵力还不够；
His forces were still insufficient.

倘或想用三面包抄的办法，将长毛向广东方面挤，相当吃力。
Enveloping the rebels on three sides and squeezing them toward Guangdong would be difficult.

万一有个漏洞填塞不住，长毛一出了海，不管在福建或浙江的海面，自己都脱不了干系，
If one gap remained unblocked and the rebels escaped to sea, whether off Fujian or Zhejiang, he would still bear responsibility.

岂不是弄巧成拙？
Would that not defeat his own purpose?

因此，左宗棠想请李鸿章 的淮军助以一臂。
He therefore wished to enlist Li Hongzhang’s Anhui Army.

克复湖州之役，彼此合作得还满意；如今再申前请，想来李鸿章 不致于拒绝。
Their cooperation at Huzhou had been satisfactory; surely Li would not refuse another request.

『不过，这话我不便开口。』左宗棠说，
“But I cannot make the approach myself,” Zuo said.

『如果是我出面相邀，就得替客军筹饷；
“If I invite them, I must fund the visiting troops.

譬如他派一万人，一个月起码就得五六五银子，
Suppose he sends ten thousand men: that means at least fifty or sixty thousand taels a month.

再加上开拔的盘缠，第一笔就非拨十万银子不可，
Add their departure expenses, and the first allocation must be a hundred thousand.

实在力有未逮。
That is beyond me.

倘或朝廷有旨意，让淮军自备粮饷，来闽助剿；我们至多备五万银子作犒赏，
If the court orders the Anhui Army to bring its own pay and provisions and assist in Fujian, we need provide no more than fifty thousand as a reward.

面子上也就把好看了。
That would satisfy appearances.

雪岩，你说，我这把如意算盘如何？』
Xueyan, what do you think of my calculation?”

『是好算盘。
“It is a good one.

不过淮军自备粮饷，恐怕李中丞不肯。
But Governor Li may refuse to provide both pay and food.

他出饷，我们出粮；李中丞就没话好说了，
If he supplies the pay and we the grain, he will have nothing to object to.

因为他的军队闲摆在那里，一样也是要发饷的。
His men must be paid even if they remain idle.

至于请朝廷降旨，只有请福建的京官在京里活动。』
As for obtaining an imperial order, we must ask Fujian officials in Beijing to work for it.”

『那怕不行。』
“That may not succeed.”

左宗棠摇摇头，
Zuo shook his head.

『福建京官，目前没有身居高位的，说话不大有力量。
“No Fujian man there currently holds a sufficiently high position; their words carry little weight.

闽浙唇齿相依。
Fujian and Zhejiang depend on one another like lips and teeth.

浙江在京的大老，雪岩你倒想想看，
Think of the senior Zhejiang men in Beijing.

有什么人可托？『
Whom could we approach?”


『浙江在京的大老，自然要数许大人；
“Among the senior Zhejiang men, Lord Xu naturally comes first.

不过，他的吏部尚书交卸了。
But he has retired from the Ministry of Personnel.

倒是他的大少爷，在南书房很红；
His eldest son, however, is much favored in the Southern Study.

还有他一位侄少爷，是小军机，专管军务——。』『对！
He also has a nephew in the Grand Council secretariat, dealing particularly with military affairs—” “Exactly!

对！』不等胡雪岩说完，左宗棠便抢着说，
Exactly!” Zuo interrupted.

『这条路子再好都没有，
“There could be no better approach.

请你替我进行。
Please arrange it for me.

许家杭州望族，
The Xu family is one of Hangzhou’s great clans.

你总有熟人吧？』
Surely you know someone?”

『他家的人很多，我倒认得几痊；
“There are many of them, and I know several.

不过象这样的大事，也不好随便托人。』
But a matter of this importance cannot be entrusted to just anyone.”

胡雪岩想了一会说，
Hu thought a moment.

『大人，我想到上海去一趟；去看许七大人。
“I should like to go to Shanghai and see Seventh Master Xu.

一面拿大人交办的事托他；一面想拿许七大人搬到杭州，出面来办善后。』
I can put your business before him and at the same time try to persuade him to return to Hangzhou and take charge of reconstruction.”

左宗棠想了一下。觉得胡雪岩这个办法极好——
After considering, Zuo found this an excellent plan.

所谓『许七大人』就是小刀会刘丽川起事之时的江苏巡抚许乃钊；如今逃难在上海。
“Seventh Master Xu” was Xu Naizhao, Governor of Jiangsu at the time of Liu Lichuan’s Small Swords uprising, now living as a refugee in Shanghai.

他的胞兄，也就是胡雪岩口中的『许六大人』许乃普，以吏部尚书致仕，
His elder brother, Hu’s “Sixth Master Xu,” was Xu Naipu, retired Minister of Personnel.

因为闹长毛不能南归；在京里是浙江同乡的『家乡』。
Unable to return south because of the rebels, he had become a kind of home away from home for Zhejiang men in Beijing.

而且科名前辈，久掌文衡，京中大老，颇加尊礼。
A senior examination graduate who had long presided over literary selection, he was treated with particular respect by the capital’s elders.

许乃普的长子许彭寿，是李鸿章 的同年，也是道光二十七年丁未这一榜的会元；
His eldest son, Xu Pengshou, had graduated in the same cohort as Li Hongzhang and headed the metropolitan examination list in the dingwei year, the twenty-seventh of Daoguang.

许乃普还有个胞侄许庚皋，在『辛酉政变』中出过大力，
Xu Naipu also had a nephew, Xu Gengshen, who had rendered important service during the Xinyou coup.

如今是极红的『小军机』——军机章京领班之一，熟谙兵事，精于方略，对军务部署有极大的发言权。
Now a highly favored senior secretary of the Grand Council, he understood military affairs and strategy and exercised considerable influence over deployments.

所以走这条路子，路路皆通；
This route opened doors in every direction.

必要时还可以请许彭寿以同年的交情，写封切切实实的信给李鸿章 ，
If necessary, Xu Pengshou could use his fellowship with Li to write him a particularly earnest letter.

更无有不能如愿之理。
There was every reason to expect success.


至于将许乃钊请回杭州来主持善后，这也是一着非下不可的好棋。
Bringing Xu Naizhao back to direct Hangzhou’s reconstruction was likewise an excellent and necessary move.

因为马新贻一到任，胡雪岩有不得不走之势；而要找替手，最适当的人选就是许乃钊。
Once Ma arrived, Hu would have to leave, and Xu was the best possible replacement.

第一，他做过封疆大吏，科名是翰林出身，名副其实的『缙绅先生』；
First, he had governed a province and risen through the Hanlin Academy, a gentleman of established rank in every sense.

第二，马新贻不仅是许乃钊的后辈，而且与他的胞侄许彭寿同榜，
Second, Ma was not only his junior but a fellow graduate of his nephew Xu Pengshou.

以『老世叔』的身分去看马新贻，照例应受『硬进硬出』——开中门迎送的礼遇，
Visiting Ma in the position of an honored uncle of the older generation, Xu would be entitled to the formal courtesy of the central gate being opened for his arrival and departure.

这样为地方讲话就有力量就得多了；
His voice on local affairs would carry correspondingly greater weight.

第三，许乃钊公正廉洁，德高望重，足以冠冤群伦。
Third, he was just, incorruptible, and universally respected, eminently qualified to lead the local gentlemen.

因此，左宗棠欣然接纳胡雪岩的建设；而且自己表示，要亲笔写封很恳切的信，向许乃钊致意。
Zuo therefore gladly accepted Hu’s proposal and offered to write Xu a most earnest personal letter.


谈完了公事谈『私事』；而私事也就是公事∶胡雪岩的出处。
With public business settled, they turned to “private” business, which was itself public: Hu’s future position.

左宗棠打算将他调到福建；
Zuo intended to have him transferred to Fujian.

但不必随他一起行动，专驻上海，为他经理一切。
He need not travel with the army, however; he would remain in Shanghai and manage all Zuo’s affairs there.

胡雪岩毫不迟疑地答应了下来。
Hu agreed without hesitation.

从第二天起，左宗棠便照商定的步骤，积极开始部署；
From the following day, Zuo energetically began carrying out their agreed steps.

除了战报以外，一连拜发了好几道奏折。
Besides military reports, he sent several memorials in succession.

第一道是∶浙江的兵饷军需，十分困难，自顾不暇；应该拨给陕甘的协饷，请饬户部另筹改拨。
The first pleaded that Zhejiang could scarcely meet its own army’s needs and asked the Ministry of Revenue to find another source for the contribution to Shaanxi and Gansu.

第二道是，请饬新任浙江巡抚马新贻，从速到任，至于马新贻未到任前，浙江巡抚请由藩司蒋益澧『护理』。
The second urged Ma Xinyi to take office quickly and requested that Provincial Treasurer Jiang Yili temporarily administer the governorship until his arrival.

第三道是，奉旨拨解杨岳斌的『行资』八万两，于无可设法之中，勉强设法筹拨半数。
The third stated that, of the eighty thousand taels ordered for Yang Yuebin’s departure expenses, only half had been raised, and that with the greatest difficulty.


第四道奏折与浙江无关——
The fourth concerned Fujian.

每天夏秋之交，户部照例催各省报解『京饷』；
Every year, as summer passed into autumn, the Ministry of Revenue pressed the provinces to remit their contributions to the capital.

京饷不止于发放在京八旗禁军的粮饷，举凡王公大臣，文武百官的廉俸；大小衙门办公的经费；宗庙陵寝的祭祀费用；以及专供两宫太后及皇帝私人花用，每年分三节呈上的『交进银』，无不出在京饷之内，
These paid not merely the Eight Banner guards but the salaries and allowances of princes, ministers, and officials; the expenses of government offices; sacrifices at ancestral temples and imperial tombs; and the silver presented at three annual festivals for the personal use of the emperor and the two empress dowagers.

所以协饷可欠，京饷不可欠。
Provincial military contributions might fall into arrears; the capital’s funds could not.

福建欠海关税银十万两；茶税二万两，
Fujian owed a hundred thousand taels in customs revenue and twenty thousand in tea tax.

上谕催解∶『务于十二月内，尽数解齐。倘仍饰辞宕延，致误要需，即由户部查照奏定章 程，指名严参。』
An edict demanded payment in full during the twelfth month and threatened named impeachments under the prescribed rules if further excuses delayed essential expenditure.

虽奉这样的严旨，左宗棠仍要欠上一次；
Despite this stern order, Zuo meant to default once more.

因为非如此，不足以表示福建之穷，必须浙江接济。
Only thus could he demonstrate Fujian’s poverty and its need for Zhejiang’s help.

当然，欠有欠的方法，不是硬顶可以了事的；
Yet default required method; blunt defiance would not suffice.

左宗棠的方法是，哭穷之外，将他闽浙总督应得的『养廉银』一万两，由票号汇到户部，作为京饷报解。
Besides pleading poverty, he remitted his own ten-thousand-tael governor-general’s integrity allowance through a draft bank to the Ministry of Revenue, to be credited toward the capital’s funds.


第五道是请停止广东解浙的协饷。
The fifth memorial requested an end to Guangdong’s military contributions to Zhejiang.

主要的作用是借此机会让朝廷知道，广东的协饷，对浙江来说是个『画饼』。
Its chief purpose was to show the court that these contributions had been no more than a painted cake.

所以，停止的理由，不过『现在浙省军务肃清，所有前项协饷，自应停止』这样一句；
The stated reason for discontinuance was brief: “Military operations in Zhejiang having now ended, the above contributions should naturally cease.”

而『停止』以前的帐目，却算得很清楚，
But the preceding accounts were set out precisely.

从同治元年正月到这年八月，连闰共计三十三个月；
From the first month of the first Tongzhi year to the eighth month of the current year, including the intercalary month, thirty-three months had elapsed.

广东应解浙江协饷三百三十万两，
Guangdong should have remitted three million three hundred thousand taels.

可是实收仅二十八万。其中由厘金所拨者是二十二万两；
Zhejiang had actually received only two hundred and eighty thousand, of which two hundred and twenty thousand came from likin.

曾国藩奏道，广东厘金开办起至这年八月底止，共收一百二十万，
Zeng Guofan had reported that Guangdong’s likin receipts, from the levy’s introduction to the end of that eighth month, totaled one million two hundred thousand taels.

是则浙军『所得不过十成之二』。
Thus Zhejiang’s army had received “not even two-tenths.”


第六道是部署到福建以后的人事。
The sixth memorial concerned personnel after the move to Fujian.

奏折的案由是『办理饷需各员，请旨奖励』；附带请求调用。
Its heading requested rewards for officials who had managed military supplies and funding; it also sought their transfer.

其中当然有胡雪岩，
Hu was naturally included.

他本来是『盐运使衔』的『江西试用道』；
He was then a probationary circuit intendant assigned to Jiangxi, holding the honorary rank of salt commissioner.

左宗棠奏请『改发福建以道员补用，并请赏加按察使衔』，
Zuo asked that he be “reassigned to Fujian for substantive appointment as a circuit intendant, and granted the honorary rank of provincial judicial commissioner.”

这报奖的文字，看来并不如武官的『请赏戴花翎』、『请赏加巴图鲁称号』来得热闹起眼；
Such wording looked less splendid than military requests for a peacock feather or the title of baturu.

其实帮了胡雪岩很大的一个忙，
In reality, it did Hu a tremendous service.

因为由『试用道』改为『以道员补用』，只要一准，立刻可以补任何实缺；
Once approved, the change from probationary status to eligibility for substantive appointment meant that he could immediately fill any vacant circuit intendancy.

而『赏加按察使衔』，便可以署理阜司，成为实缺道员更上层楼的『监司大员』。
The judicial commissioner’s honorary rank would also qualify him to act in that higher office, rising above an ordinary circuit post into the senior provincial administration.

在左宗棠来说，这一保，起码等于三年的劳绩。
Zuo’s recommendation was worth at least three years of service.


不过左宗棠拜发这道奏折时，胡雪岩并不知道；因为他人已到了上海。
Hu knew nothing of this memorial when Zuo sent it, for he had already reached Shanghai.

拿着左宗棠的亲笔函件去见『许七大人』；谈得十分融洽。
Carrying Zuo’s personal letter, he called on Seventh Master Xu, and their conversation went most agreeably.

将左宗棠所托之事，一一办妥；只不过耽搁了两夜，陪老母谈一谈劫后的西湖，与古应春盘桓了半天，便即原船回到杭州。
He completed all the business entrusted to him, stayed only two nights, spoke with his aged mother of West Lake after the devastation, spent half a day with Gu Yingchun, and returned to Hangzhou in the same boat.

回到杭州，第一个要想见他的不是左宗棠，而是藩司『护理抚篆』的蒋益澧；
The first man seeking him there was not Zuo but Jiang Yili, the provincial treasurer temporarily administering the governorship.

他早就派人在阜康钱庄留下话，等胡雪岩一到，立刻通知，以便会面。
Jiang had already left word at Fukang Bank that he was to be notified the moment Hu arrived, so that they could meet.

『雪翁，』与胡雪岩见着了面，蒋益澧哭丧着脸说∶『你非帮我的忙不可！
“Mr Xueyan,” he said with a miserable expression, “you must help me.

大帅交代下来了，浙江每个月解福建协饷二十万两；按月十二号汇出，迟一天都不准。
The commander has ordered Zhejiang to send Fujian two hundred thousand taels every month, remitted on the twelfth without a single day’s delay.

这不是强人所难吗？』
Is that not asking the impossible?”

听得这话，胡雪岩也吓一跳。
Hu too was startled.

洪杨之乱，浙江遭劫特深，满目疮痛，百废待举，
Zhejiang had suffered especially grievously in the Taiping war; devastation lay everywhere, and everything awaited rebuilding.

何来每月二十万两银子，供养入闽之师？
Where could it find two hundred thousand taels a month to support an army entering Fujian?

当时估计，每月能凑十万两银子，已经至矣尽矣；
Hu had reckoned that scraping together a hundred thousand would already strain the province to the utmost.

不想左宗棠狮子大开口，加了一倍，而且日子都不准托，
Now Zuo demanded twice that amount and would not even permit a delay in payment.

这就未免太过分了。
It was excessive.


『雪翁，』蒋益澧又说，『于公于私，你都不能不说话，
“On both public and private grounds, you must speak,” Jiang continued.

私，老兄在大帅面前言听计从；
“Privately, the commander listens to you and follows your advice.

公，俗语说的「羊毛出在羊身上」，
Publicly, as the saying goes, wool must come from the sheep.

真是逼得非解这个数目不可，只有让地方受累。
If this sum is forced from us, the people must suffer.

雪翁，你也于心不忍吧！
You could not bear that either.

再说，我到底不过是藩司。』
Besides, I am, after all, only the provincial treasurer.”

最后这句话，才是蒋益澧真正的苦衷。
That last sentence revealed his real distress.

目前巡抚的大印握在手里，令出即行，办事还容易；
For the moment he held the governor’s seal and could issue effective orders.

等马新贻一到任，认为协饷数目太大要减，他当藩司的，不能不听命。
Once Ma arrived, however, if the new governor considered the contribution excessive and ordered a reduction, Jiang would have to obey.

而另一方面左宗棠又是一手提拔他的恩主，且有承诺在先，不能不维持原数。
Yet Zuo was the patron who had raised him, and Jiang had already undertaken to maintain the original sum.

这一下岂非挤在夹缝里轧扁了头？
Would he not be crushed between the two?

想了一会，胡雪岩觉得这个麻烦非揽下来不可，
After thinking it over, Hu concluded that he must take this difficulty upon himself.

便点点头说∶『好的。
He nodded. “Very well.

我来想办法。』
I shall find a way.”

『这一来有救了！』
“Then there is hope!”

蒋益澧如释重负，拱拱手问说∶『雪翁，谅来胸来成竹了。
Relieved, Jiang clasped his hands and asked, “I imagine you already have a plan.

是何办法，可以不可以先闻为快？』
Might I hear it?”

『当然，当然！
“Of course.

原要请教。』
I wished to consult you.

胡雪岩答说，『第一，我想请左大人酌减数目。』
First, I shall ask Lord Zuo to reduce the amount.”

『酌减？』蒋益澧问，『减多少？』
“Reduce it by how much?”

『总得打个七折。』
“At least by thirty percent.”


『打个七折，每月亦还得要十四万两。』蒋益澧说∶『如今军务肃清，我这个藩司不必带兵打仗，
“That would still be a hundred and forty thousand a month,” Jiang said. “Now that the province is pacified, I need no longer command troops.

要在本分上做点事。
I ought to accomplish something in my proper office.

你看——。』蒋益澧细数他该做的事，
Consider—” He listed his duties.

最有关国计民生的要政，便是兴修水利。
The most important for the economy and the people’s livelihood was the repair of waterworks.

浙江全境皆是土田，近山者瘠，近水者腴。
Zhejiang was a land of cultivated fields: those near the hills were poor, those near water rich.

兼以蚕丝之利，首重栽桑；而桑树的栽培灌溉，与水田的要求，没有什么两样。
Silk production made mulberry cultivation particularly important, and mulberries required much the same irrigation as paddy fields.

所以自古以来，在浙江做官，而遗爱在民，久留去思的，无不是因为在水利方面大有成就之故。
Since ancient times, officials whose kindness was long remembered in Zhejiang had invariably distinguished themselves in water management.


浙江的水利重在浙北；浙北的水利父重在海塘。
The chief concern was northern Zhejiang, and there the sea dikes were paramount.

乾隆六次南巡，都以巡视浙江海塘为名，可以想见其关系的重大。
The Qianlong Emperor had made inspection of these dikes the stated purpose of all six southern tours—proof enough of their importance.

海塘欲求完固足以捍御海潮，须用石塘；
To withstand the tides, they needed to be built of stone.

洪杨作乱以来，海宁一带的石塘没有修过，日渐坍圮，
Since the rebellion, the stone dikes around Haining had received no repairs and were steadily crumbling.

现在要及时修复，估计费用须上百万银子；
Restoration would cost more than a million taels.

迫不得已，只有先办土塘，暂且将就。
With no alternative, temporary earthen works would have to suffice.

『就是办土塘，亦要三十万银子。土塘料不贵，人工贵；大乱之后，壮丁少了，就是人工费。』蒋益澧说，
“Even earth dikes will cost three hundred thousand,” Jiang said. “The materials are cheap; labor is dear. After the great disorder, there are fewer able-bodied men, which makes labor expensive.

『雪翁，这件事我亦要跟你好好商量；怎么筹得一笔款子，拿海塘修一修？
We must discuss how to raise money for repairs.

万一海塘溃决，可是件不得了的事，
If the sea dikes give way, the consequences will be terrible.

一想起来，我真连觉都睡不着。』
The thought keeps me awake.”

听蒋益澧这样表示，即令是娇饰之词，胡雪岩亦是十分可敬。
Even if this display was affected, Hu found it worthy of respect.

『三代以下惟恐不好名』，
“Since the Three Dynasties, the danger is only that men may not care for a good name.”

他的本心不必问。
Jiang’s deepest motives need not be examined.

听他的语气是想做好官；
He sounded as though he wanted to be a good official.

正不妨与人为善，趁此机会捧他一捧、扶他一扶，拿他逼到好官的路上，亦正是地方之福。
Hu might as well encourage and support him, pressing him into that path for the province’s benefit.

想到这里，他毫不迟疑地答道∶『请放心。
“Do not worry,” he answered at once.

我来策划一下，
“I shall make a plan.

大家量力捐办，不是难事。』
Let everyone contribute according to his means; it should not be difficult.”

『那就再好没有。』
“That would be excellent.”

蒋益澧很欣慰地，
Jiang was pleased.

『还有西湖的疏浚，也不能再拖了。
“The dredging of West Lake cannot wait either.

西湖水利，关乎杭州、海宁的水田灌溉；
Its waters irrigate fields in Hangzhou and Haining.

明年春天以前，一定要整理好，这也得好几万银子。
Everything must be put in order before next spring, and that too will cost tens of thousands of taels.

雪翁，你倒想，我这个藩司难做不难做？
Is a treasurer’s task easy?

有啥开源之道，真要好好向你请教。』
I must ask your advice on new revenue.”

『如今只有在盐上动脑筋。』胡雪岩答说，
“For the present, salt is our only resource.

『倘能照我的办法，可以救得一时之急，
My method could tide us over.

一年半载，福建军务，告个段落；浙江不必再负担协饷，
In six months or a year, the Fujian campaign should reach a conclusion, and Zhejiang will no longer have to contribute.

那时候就轻松了。』
Then things will be easier.”

『我也是这么想，
“I think so too.

不过，盐法我不大懂；
But I know little of salt administration.

大帅倒是内行。』
The commander is an expert.”

『左大人是内行？』胡雪岩很惊异地问。
“Lord Zuo is an expert?” Hu asked in surprise.


『这也无足为怪的。
“Nothing surprising about that.

雪翁，你莫非不知道？
Do you not know?

大帅是陶文毅公的儿女亲家。』
The commander’s family is joined by marriage to that of Lord Tao, posthumously honored as Wenyi.”

『啊！
“Ah!

啊！原来如此！』
So that is it!”

胡雪岩恍然大悟，
Hu suddenly understood.

左宗棠对盐法内行，渊源有自。
Zuo’s knowledge of salt administration had a history.

在他廿六岁时，两江总督陶澍在江西阅兵事毕，请假顺道回湖南安化原籍扫墓，
When Zuo was twenty-six, Tao Shu, Governor-General of Liangjiang, had completed an inspection of troops in Jiangxi and obtained leave to visit the ancestral graves at his native Anhua in Hunan.

经过醴陵，县官照例『办差』，
Passing through Liling, he was received with the customary official arrangements.

布置公馆时，请主讲醴陵渌江书院的左宗棠，做了一副对联，
While preparing his residence, the magistrate asked Zuo, then head of the Lujiang Academy at Liling, to compose a couplet.

陶澍一见，激赏不已；
Tao admired it immensely.

问知县官，出自左宗棠的手笔，当时便请来相见。
Learning who had written it, he immediately sent for Zuo.

果然，一谈到浙江的盐务，左宗棠立即表示，在他交卸浙江巡抚兼职以前，有几件必办的事，其中之一是就是整顿浙江盐务，
Sure enough, when Hu raised Zhejiang’s salt administration, Zuo declared at once that reforming it was one of the tasks he must complete before relinquishing the acting governorship.

改引行票，打算从同冶四年正月起，先试办一年。
He intended to replace the quota-certificate system with salt permits, beginning in the first month of the fourth Tongzhi year, on a one-year trial.


『我的办法，一共四款∶第一是缉私；第二是革浮费；第三是减价；第四是清查煎盐的灶户。
“My plan has four parts,” Zuo said. “First, suppress smuggling; second, abolish irregular fees; third, reduce prices; fourth, conduct a proper registration of salt-producing households.

至于盐课收入，全数提为军饷；
All salt-tax receipts will go to the army.

除去开销每个月至少有十万银子，够我一半的数目了。』
After expenses, there should be at least a hundred thousand taels a month—enough for half my requirement.”

这就是说，左宗棠援闽之师，每个月要浙江负担二十万两的饷银。与蒋益澧的话，完全相符。
So Zhejiang was indeed expected to provide two hundred thousand a month for his Fujian campaign, exactly as Jiang had said.

胡雪岩很沉着，暂且放在心；先谈盐务。
Hu remained composed and set that aside for the moment, speaking first of salt.

『大人这四款办法，后面三条是办得到的；
“The last three parts can be carried out.

就是缉私有些难处。
Suppressing smuggling will be more difficult.

浙盐行销松江；松江是江苏地面，鞭长莫及。
Zhejiang salt is sold in Songjiang, which is Jiangsu territory and beyond our reach.

这一层可曾想过？』
Have you considered that?”

『当然想过。』
“Certainly.

左宗棠答道，『我正要跟你商量，
I was going to discuss it with you.

你不是跟我提过，有个松江漕帮的首脑，人很诚朴能干吗？
Did you not once mention a leader of the Grain-Boat Guild in Songjiang, an honest and capable man?

他肯不肯帮帮浙江的忙？』
Would he help Zhejiang?”

『此人姓尤，
“His surname is You.

只要大人吩咐，他一定乐予效劳。』
If you give the word, he will gladly serve.

胡雪岩问道∶『就不知道这个忙怎么帮法？』
But what form would his help take?”


『自然是带队伍缉私。』
“Naturally, he would lead troops against smugglers.”

胡雪岩是明知故问；
Hu had asked a question whose answer he knew.

等左宗棠有了答复，因话答话，故意摇摇头说∶『这怕办不到。
Now he deliberately shook his head. “I fear that cannot be done.

他本人是个「运子」，不是官儿的身分；
He is a grain-transport boatman, not an official.

说到规矩，见了把总都要尊称一声「总爷」。
By the rules of rank he must address even a bazong respectfully as ‘sir.’

大人请想，他怎么带队伍？
How could he command troops?

就算他肯帮，分拨过示的官兵，也不服他的指挥。』
Even if he agreed, the officers and men assigned to him would not obey.”

『这话倒也是。』
“True.”

左宗棠踌躇了，
Zuo hesitated.

『不过，若非带队伍缉私，又有什么可以借重他之处？』
“But unless he leads troops against smugglers, how can we make use of him?”

『漕帮的底蕴，大人向来深知。
“You know the workings of the guild well.

尤某的手下，都听他一句话∶如果有个名义，对松江一带的缉私，成效是一定有的。』
His followers all obey his word. Give him an official capacity, and there will certainly be results against smuggling around Songjiang.”

『喔，我明白了。』
“Ah, I understand.”

左宗棠想了一会说∶『这样办也没有什么不可以；
After a moment Zuo said, “There is no objection to that.

让尤某自己去招人，当然也不能太多，招个两三百人，
Let him recruit his own men—not too many, of course; two or three hundred will do.

保尤某一个官职，让他管带。
We shall recommend him for an official rank and let him command them.

这件事，我交代盐运使去办；
I shall tell the salt commissioner to arrange it.

尤某那里，请你去接头。
Please make contact with You.

至于饷银公费，一概照我营里的规矩，由盐务经费里面开支。』
Pay and operating expenses will follow the rules of my own army and come from the salt administration’s funds.”

胡雪岩很高兴；
Hu was delighted.

这不但为尤五找到了一条生路，而且于公事亦有裨益，
He had found a livelihood for Brother You and a means of serving public business at the same time.

所以欣然应诺。
He gladly agreed.
