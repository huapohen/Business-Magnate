胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第九章（本章第5部分，共7部分）

原著：高阳


They next turned to the business Jiang Yili had entrusted to Hu: Zhejiang’s monthly contribution to the army in Fujian.
然后谈到蒋益澧所托之事；亦就是浙江按月协解福建饷银的数目。

“Zhejiang formerly depended on Fujian for more than three million taels,” Hu began.
『从前浙江靠福建协饷，前后用过三百万之多；

“Now that Zhejiang troops are going to its aid, Zhejiang should naturally support them.
如今浙师援闽，饷银自然应该由浙江接济。

What arrangement do you propose, my lord?
大人是怎么个主意，请交代下来，

We should know early enough to prepare.”
好趁早筹划。』

“I have settled it with Xiangquan: Zhejiang will send me two hundred thousand a month.”
『我已经跟芗泉谈妥当了，浙江每个月接济我二十万。』

“Two hundred thousand is not much.
『二十万不多，

The trouble is that Zhejiang’s strength has been so badly drained.”
只限浙江的元气丧得太厉害！』

Hu deliberately paused, then asked without warning, “Once you reach Fujian, do you intend to request the transfer of Jiang and Yang to assist you?”
胡雪岩故意沉吟了一会；然后突如其来地问说∶『大人是不是打算到了福建，要奏调蒋杨两位去帮忙？』

Zuo was baffled.
这话问得左宗棠莫名其妙，

“I have no such intention.
立即答说∶『我并没有这样的打算。

Both have worked their way up to senior provincial office.
而且蒋杨两位，也巴结到监司大员了，

For one thing, Fujian has no suitable positions; for another, the court might not approve.
一则福建无可位置；二则，朝廷也未见得会准。

Besides, why should I clear the road for Ma Gushan, vacating two important posts so that he can bring in his own men?”
再说，我又何苦为马谷山铺路，腾出这么两个紧要缺分，好方便他援引私人？』

Hu had expected this answer, particularly the last point.
这番回答，原在胡雪岩意料之中；尤其是最后一点，

Keeping Jiang as provincial treasurer and recommending Yang Changjun as judicial commissioner was a calculated move to restrain Ma and protect Zuo’s revenue in Zhejiang.
更有关系——蒋益澧留任浙江藩司；并保杨昌为浙江阜司，原是左宗棠所下的一着『行手棋』，用来箝制马新贻，保护他在浙江的饷源，

Why would Zuo surrender that advantage?
岂肯自我退让？

Hu had asked precisely to lead the conversation toward his real purpose.
而胡雪岩所以明知故问，亦正是因话答话，好引入正题的一种手法。

“Exactly.
『这就是了！

If Jiang and Yang remain secure in their posts, it will be almost as though you still held the acting governorship.
但愿蒋杨二分，安于其位；就等于大人仍旧兼摄浙江抚篆一样。

But I fear I must offer some unwelcome advice.”
不过，大人，我有句话，只怕忠言逆耳。』

“Go ahead.
『不要紧，

There is nothing you and I cannot discuss.
你我无话不可谈。

Besides, I know it will be for my good.”
而况你必是为我打算的好话。』


“Yes, I am thinking of your interests: a small but steady stream, a firm and lasting arrangement,” Hu replied calmly. “Zhejiang’s income is not merely limited; it cannot yet be estimated with certainty.
『是，我是替大人打算；细水长流，稳扎稳打。』胡雪岩很从容地答说∶『浙江的收入不但有限，而且没有确数可以预估。

The land-tax payments have been remitted by imperial order.
地丁钱粮，已经奉旨豁免；

Salt revenue will not improve before late spring or early summer next year.
盐课收入，决要明年春末夏初，才有起色；

The rice levy depends upon neighboring provinces’ willingness to help.
米捐要看邻省肯不肯帮忙？

Only likin can be relied upon.
靠得住的，只有厘金；

As trade revives, receipts should increase, though even that depends upon the honesty of the collectors.
市面越来越兴旺，收数自然越来越多，但也要看经手人的操守。

On the expenditure side, reconstruction comes first, and the sea dikes second; both require great sums.
至于支出，第一是善后；第二是海塘，都要大把花银子。

Government offices and civil and military salaries must also be provided for, and the province must maintain some troops of its own.
大小衙门，文武官员的经费俸禄，更不能不筹；地方上总还要养些兵。

Suppose two hundred thousand taels is scraped together each month and sent first to your supply bureau, leaving the provincial treasury bare and all other work at a standstill.
大人倒想一想看，倘或每个月先凑二十万银子解粮台；藩库一清如洗，什么事都动不了，

How could Jiang Xiangquan remain treasurer?”
蒋芗泉这个藩司，怎么还当得下去？』

“Surely—” Zuo was silent for a while.
『这，』左宗棠呆了半晌，

“Surely things cannot be as difficult as you suggest?”
方始说下去∶『这也不致于如你所说的那样子艰窘吧？』

“Of course.
『当然。

I have overstated them a little.
我是说得过分了一点。

But consider Governor Ma too.
不过，大人，请你也要替马中丞想一想；

Having just risen to provincial authority, he naturally wishes to accomplish something.
人家刚刚巴结到方面大员，自然也想做番事业。

If he is hampered on every side, what can he do but press Jiang?
如果处处捉襟见肘，动弹不得；那时候怎么办？只有逼蒋芗泉；

And to press Jiang is to press you.”
逼蒋芗泉就是逼大人。』

Hu paused. “Governor Shen of Jiangxi owed his advancement entirely to Grand Secretary Zeng.
胡雪岩停了一下又说∶『从前江西沈中丞是曾中堂一手提拔的；

Yet he withheld his province’s likin revenues when he chose, and the court did not blame him for delaying the Zeng brothers’ eastern campaign.
本省的厘金说截留就截留，朝廷也不曾责备他耽误了曾家弟兄的「东征」。

Governor Ma may not be as fierce as Shen, but neither is he content merely to drift.”
马中丞为人虽不如沈中丞那样子刚烈，然而也不是肯得过且过的人。』

The example of Shen Baozhen’s rupture with Zeng put Zuo on his guard.
提到沈葆桢与曾国藩交恶的往事，左宗棠不能不起警惕之心。

No one paid closer attention to practical interests.
他是最讲究利害关系；

Considered calmly, Ma’s position was secure and offered no weakness to attack.
冷静思量，马新贻的脚步站得很稳；亦无弱点可攻，

If a dispute arose, Zuo might not prevail.
果然为此有所争执，自己不见得能占上风。

Jiang would bear the first blow; once removed from Zhejiang, how could he provide two hundred thousand a month?
而且一闹开来，蒋益澧首当其冲；他一调离了浙江，每月又何有二十万银子可得？

With this thought, Zuo asked in a more even temper, “Then what do you advise, Xueyan?”
转念以此，便心平气和地问道∶『那末，雪岩，你说呢？我该怎么办？』


“The amount must be reduced,” Hu answered directly.
胡雪岩率直答道∶『只有减个数目。』

“By how much?”
『减多少呢？』

“I dare not fix a figure.
左宗棠问。『这我就不敢说了。』

You must ask the officers and men to bear some hardship for the present.
左宗棠答道，『惟有请大人交代下去，官兵弟兄先委屈些，

Once matters improve, they can be compensated.”
只要局面一好转，必然补报。』

“Very well.”
『好！』

Zuo nodded.
左宗棠点点头，

“I too cannot bear to overburden Zhejiang.
『我也不忍太累浙江；

As you suggest, I shall have the supply bureau recalculate and reduce the sum as far as possible.
就照你的意思，让粮台重新核算，减到减无可减为止。

But you know my position.
不过，雪岩，我的处境你是知道的，

I have always been isolated and unsupported.
一直孤立无援；

I must open a way forward.”
总要打开一条出路才好。』

“Yes,” Hu answered without expression.
『是！』胡雪岩毫无表情地应声。

“You must help me greatly.
『你要大大地帮我的忙！』

How do you think I should find that way?”
左宗棠问道，『你看，我的出路该怎么打？』

“Have you not already decided, my lord?”
『大人不是已有成算了吗？』

He meant the plan to gain control of Guangdong.
那是指谋取广东而言。

Zuo frowned slightly. “Removing Guo is not difficult.
左宗棠微微皱着眉说∶『驱郭不难；

The question is who can replace him.
难在执可取代？

Xiangquan still lacks the standing for a governorship.
芗泉的资望，当方面之任，总嫌不足。

If my recommendation is rejected, I shall find it hard to speak again.
万一碰个钉子，我以后就难说话了。

The consequences are serious.
这一层关系很大，

I cannot act rashly before I am sure.
没有把握以前，我不便贸然动手。

Yet I cannot tell Xiangquan this.”
然而，这话又不能向芗泉透露。』

Hu listened closely, searching beneath the words.
胡雪岩很用心地听着；细细体会，辩出味外之味，

If Jiang wanted Guangdong, he would need another source of support.
蒋益澧如果想当广东巡抚，不得另外去找一份助力。

In other words, if influential patrons at court could ensure that the recommendation would succeed, Zuo would gladly submit it.
这也就是说，只要朝中有奥援，保证左宗棠将来举荐时不会驳回；他是乐于出奏的。


Hu then asked himself whether he should help Jiang and whether he could.
想到这里，便又自问∶是不是该帮帮蒋益澧的忙？这个忙帮得上帮不上？

The first question required little thought: Jiang’s promotion to Guangdong would bring great public and private advantages.
前者无须多作考虑；能让蒋益澧调升广东巡抚，于公于私都大有好处。

As for whether help was possible, it was too early to say.
至于帮得上忙、帮不上忙？此时言之过早；

Much depended upon effort; if he did his utmost, there was hope.
反正事在人为，只要尽力，就有希望。

“You are a pillar of the court, my lord, and have always enjoyed great imperial favor,” he said.
想停当随即说道∶『大人是朝廷柱石，圣眷一直优隆。

“In Shanghai I heard from people newly arrived from Beijing that Prince Gong values you highly, and Prince Chun admires you still more.
我在上海听京里的人说起，恭王很看重大人；醇王尤其佩服。

Grand Secretary Zeng was allowed to recommend governors for provinces within his military command.
想当初，曾中堂可以保他督办军务有关省份的巡抚；

Why should you not have the same right?
如今大人又为什么不可以？

As for Xiangquan’s standing, promotion from Treasurer of Zhejiang to Governor of Guangdong would be no extraordinary leap.
至于说到芗泉的资望，由浙藩升粤抚，亦不算躐等；

Governor Ma is an example ready to hand.
马中丞不就是个现成的例子？

Guangdong is different because its maritime customs revenues are closely connected with the Imperial Household Department.
当然，广东因为粤海关的收入与内务府很有关系，情形与他省不同；

Still, if someone in Beijing looks after the matter, there is hope.”
但是，只要京里有人照应，亦不是没有希望的事。』

“Exactly—someone in Beijing must look after it.
『就是这话罗，要京里有人照应！

Xiangquan is at a disadvantage there.”
芗泉在这一层上头，比较吃亏。』

“It is not too late to begin tending those neglected fires.”
『就眼前烧起冷灶来，也还不晚。』

Zuo looked at him intently, pondered for some time, then finally said, “You might discuss it with Xiangquan.”
左宗棠深看了他一眼；沉吟又沉吟，终于说了一句∶『你不妨与芗泉谈谈！』

“Yes.”
『是！』


“He must rely on you,” Zuo continued.
『他的事要靠你。』左宗棠又说，

“And I can do without you even less.
『我更少你不得。

You command no troops for me and run no supply bureau, yet your work is more important than either.
你在我这里，既不带兵，又不管粮台；可是比带兵管粮台更要紧。

Once I leave, you must set out promptly and remain in Shanghai.
雪岩，等我一走，你也要赶紧动身，长驻上海；

If the supply bureau falls short—whether of pay, grain, or military equipment—you alone will be my resource.”
粮台接济不上，要饷要粮要军装，我就只靠你一个人了！』

The responsibility weighed heavily upon Hu, but he could show no hesitation.
这份责任太重，胡雪岩顿感双肩吃力；可是说什么也不能有所犹豫，

He braced himself. “Yes, my lord.
便硬着头皮答一声∶『是！

You may be at ease.”
大人请放心！』

“With that assurance, I truly can.” Zuo breathed out, then asked, “What would you like me to do for you?
『有你这句话，我真的可以放心了。』左宗棠舒了口气；然后问道∶『你有什么事，要我替你办的？

I intend to leave at the end of the month; there is still half a month.
我预备月底动身；还有半个月的功夫。

If you want anything, tell me early.”
有话你趁早说。』

Hu had already considered this.
胡雪岩早就想过了，

After Zuo left, Jiang would hold the acting governor’s seal and could still be consulted; yet a request to Zuo would be more direct and effective.
左宗棠一走，虽是蒋益澧护理巡抚的大印，有事仍旧可以商量得通；然而究竟不如托左宗棠来得简捷有力。

During their half-year together, Hu had asked no favor.
这半年的相处，自己从无一事求他；

He must not miss the opportunity now, especially when Zuo himself had invited him to speak.
如今却不能再错过机会了。更何况是他先开口相问；

Further silence would seem affected and false, and would hardly be wise.
倘再不言，反显得矫饰虚伪，未免太不聪明。


He decided to speak freely, first retreating a little in order to advance.
有此了解，便决定『畅所欲言』；先使个以退为进的手法，

“There are many things I wish to ask, but I fear you may find them tiresome.”
『想求大人的事情很多，』他说，『又怕大人厌烦，不敢多说。』

“Not at all!”
『不要紧，不要紧！』

Zuo waved both hands.
左宗棠连连摆手，

“I am always asking you for help and owe you many favors.
『一向都是我托你，欠你的情很多；

Speak.”
你尽管说。』

“First, the former Governor Wang died so tragically.
『是！』胡雪岩说∶『第一件，从前的王中丞，死得太惨。

You upheld justice, investigated what had happened, and reported the facts to the throne.
当时蒙大人主持公道，查明经过，查明参奏。

But the case is not finished.
不过这一案还没有了，

I should like you to see it through.”
想请大人始终成全。』

“Oh?”
『喔，』

More than two years had passed, and Zuo’s recollection was vague.
左宗棠有些茫然；因为事隔两年有余，记忆不清，

“In what way is it unfinished?”
只好问说∶『这一案怎么没有了？』


“I mean your memorial in the fourth month of the first Tongzhi year, reporting the inquiry into Wang Lüqian’s obstruction and failures—” “Ah!”
『就是同治元年四月里，大人所奏的「讯明王履谦贻误情形」那一案——』『啊，』

Reminded, Zuo said, “Wait a moment.”
左宗棠被提醒了，『你等一下。』

He lifted his riding jacket and took a key from his belt.
他欣开马褂，从腰带上去取钥匙——

Keys signified authority.
钥匙表示权威，

In institutions as great as the Shenji Camp or the Imperial Household Department, to be designated keeper of the keys meant being entrusted with leadership.
大而至于『神机营』、『内务府』，被指定为『蒙明』，即表示赋予首脑之任；

In a wealthy household, a steward—or Wang Xifeng in Dream of the Red Chamber—likewise displayed practical power by holding the keys.
小而至于一家大户人家的管家——或者象红楼梦中的王熙凤，都以掌管钥匙为实权在握的鲜明表示。

A key was small, however, insufficiently imposing to proclaim such authority.
只是钥匙甚小，不瞳以显示其权威的地位，

Hence the additions: usually quantity made the impression.
所以多加上些附丽之物；通常都是『以多取胜』，

Useless keys were tied together, sometimes threaded on a large iron ring that clattered in the hand.
弄些根本无用的钥匙拴在一起；甚至弄个大铁环串连，拎在手里『蒋朗蒋朗』地响，

The intention resembled a jailer’s: shake the bunch, and every prisoner would submit.
仿佛『牢头禁子』的用心，只要拎着那串钥匙一抖动，就足以慑服群囚。

Yet the truly weighty key was often a single one.
可是，真正能见钥匙之重的，却往往只有一枚，

Zuo had only one, threaded on a silk cord at his belt.
左宗棠亦是如此，他只有一枚钥匙，用根丝绳子穿起，挂在腰带上；

Pulling it out, he leaned toward a book chest, unlocked it, and removed a thick stack of red booklets.
此时往外一拉，以身相就，凑近一个书箱，打开来取出一大叠红簿册；

From a distance Hu could read the large characters on their covers: “Copies of Memorials.”
胡雪岩遥遥望去，只见上面写着四个大字∶『奏稿留底』。

Zuo found the booklet for the fourth month of the first Tongzhi year, put on his smoky-crystal reading spectacles, and studied it before asking, “What remains unsettled, Xueyan?”
检到同治元年四月的那一本，左宗棠戴上墨晶老花眼镜细看了一遍，方始发问∶『雪岩，你说此案未了；未了的是什么？』


“If you look at the reply received then, my lord, you will see.”
『请大人再检当时的批回；就知道了。』

The reply could not readily be found.
批回一时无从检取，

“You must know what it said.
左宗棠答说∶『想来你总清楚，

Tell me.”
说给我听吧！』

“Yes.”
『是！』

Hu now felt awkward.
胡雪岩倒有些为难了。


When Wang Youling had held Hangzhou through the siege, his principal source of funds had been Shaoxing.
因为当王有龄苦守杭州时，主要的饷源是在绍兴；

Wang Lüqian, the militia commissioner residing there, had been uncooperative.
而在籍团练大臣王履谦，却不甚合作。

Unscrupulous local gentry, skilled with their pens, surrounded him and portrayed the governor’s demands for military funds as callous extortion for private gain.
同时绍兴有些擅于刀笔的劣绅，包围王履谦，视王有龄以一省大吏征饷为不恤民困，勒索自肥，

Officials and people had almost imperceptibly become enemies.
无形中官民之间竟成了敌对的局面。

This made the position of Prefect Liao Zongyuan extremely difficult.
因此，绍兴府知府廖宗元的处境极其困难；

When the rebels advanced from Xiaoshan toward Shaoxing, imperial gunboats actually clashed with the local militia.
当长毛由萧山往绍兴进攻时，官军的炮船与团练竟发生了冲突。

Badly outnumbered, Liao’s bodyguards lost twelve men, and he himself suffered a head wound.
兵力悬殊，寡不敌众，廖宗元的亲兵被杀了十二个；廖宗元本人亦被打破了头。

Wang Lüqian should have intervened and restored order, but stood aside.
这本来是应该由王履谦去弹压排解的，而居然袖手旁观。

Soon Shaoxing fell and Liao died at his post.
不久，绍兴沦陷；廖宗元殉难；

Wang Lüqian had already fled to Ningbo and escaped by sea to Fujian.
而王履谦则先期逃到宁波，出海避难在福建。

The needless loss of Shaoxing, together with Wang’s constant obstruction and disregard of the larger cause, filled Governor Wang with bitter hatred.
绍兴不该失而失，以及王履谦的处处掣肘，不顾大局，使王有龄深恶痛绝，

In his blood-written letter from the doomed city, he said that he could not “close his eyes in death.”
在危城中寄出来的血书，表示『死不瞑目』。

Hu too had never forgotten and wished to avenge him.
胡雪岩亦就因为如此，耿耿于怀，一直想为王有龄报仇雪恨。


Even without Hu’s intervention, of course, the court would have investigated responsibility for Hangzhou’s fall and would not let Wang Lüqian escape punishment.
当然，就是胡雪岩不作此想，朝廷亦会追究杭州沦陷的责任，不容王履谦逍遥法外。

In the spring of the following year, the first Tongzhi year, Qingrui, Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang, was ordered to arrest Wang and send him to Quzhou for interrogation by the new Zhejiang governor, Zuo.
第二年——同治元年春天，闽浙总督庆瑞奉旨逮捕王履谦，解送衢州的新任浙江巡抚左宗棠审问，

Zuo recommended penal military exile to Xinjiang; the throne approved, giving some satisfaction to Governor Wang’s memory.
复奏定拟了充军新疆的罪名。朝旨准如所请，算是为王有龄出了一口气。

But the chief offender in the affair was Zhang Cunhao, a wealthy Shaoxing gentleman.
可是这一案中，首恶是绍兴的富绅张存浩，

He had led the false accusations that Liao’s gunboats were in league with the rebels, the killing of the guards, and the assault on the prefect.
诬赖廖宗元所带的炮船通贼，以及杀亲兵、打知府，都是他带的头。

Zuo’s memorial stated that Zhang and the others, enraged by Liao’s urgent demands for contributions, had acted from private resentment and committed unforgivable offenses.
左宗棠在复奏中说，『张存浩等因廖宗元催捐严紧，挟忿怀私，胆敢做出那些不法之事，罪不容赦。

Once Shaoxing was recovered, they should be arrested and punished to the full extent of the law.
应俟收复绍兴府后，严拿到案，尽法惩处。』

Now not merely Shaoxing but all Zhejiang had been cleared.
如今不但绍兴早已光复，而且全浙亦已肃清。

Yet no arrest had followed.
可是严拿张存浩到案一节，却无下文。

That was what Hu meant by the unfinished case.
胡雪岩所说的『这一案未了』，即是指此而言。


His present difficulty arose from a sudden reluctance to be harsh.
而此刻他的为难，却是一念不忍。

Amid the disorders of the age, who had failed whom, and who owed what to whom?
论到乱世中人与人的关系，谁负了谁，谁怎么亏欠谁？

Such questions were never simple.
本就是难说的一件事。

Years had passed, and everyone had survived a great catastrophe.
事隔数年，而彼此又都是大劫余生；

Perhaps they ought now to be calmer and more forgiving.
似乎应该心平气和，看开一步了。

Zuo could not guess this change of heart.
他这临时改变的心意，左宗棠当然不会猜得到；

“We have many matters to discuss,” he urged.
便催问着说∶『既然你我的事很多，

“Take them one by one, quickly.
就一件一件快说吧！

We must not waste time.”
不要耽误功夫。』

Hu had to tell the truth.
这一下他不能不说实话了。

As he spoke, another thought occurred to him.
口中谈着，心中又涌现了新的主意；

After explaining his original intention, he added, “You might show mercy and spare Zhang Cunhao, but he should not get off too easily.
所以在谈完原来的想法以后，接着又说∶『张存浩虽可以请大人宽恩饶他，可也不能太便宜他。

Let him make amends through some useful service.
我在想，他也应该将功赎罪；

Could he be required to contribute to a public undertaking?”
罚他为地方上做些公益。大人看，是不是可行？』

“Certainly.
『当然可行。』

What are his means?”
左宗棠问道∶『此人家道如何？』

“He was wealthy before, and I hear he is still comfortably off.”
『从前是富绅；现在的情况，听说也不坏。』

“Then I shall tell Xiangquan to instruct the Shaoxing prefect to summon him and require a contribution proportionate to his means for some local work of merit.”
『那好！我来告诉芗泉，转知绍兴府，传他到案；责令他量力捐款，为地方上做件功德之事。』

“That will satisfy both public justice and private feeling.
『能这样，于公于私都过得去了。

As for those loyal men who died during the two disasters, the Reconstruction Bureau is collecting their histories and reporting them in turn.
至于两次殉难的忠臣义士，善后局采访事迹，陆续禀报；

I hope you will memorialize promptly, to comfort the dead.”
亦要请大人早日出奏，安慰死者。』

“Naturally.
『当然。

I shall attend to that before leaving.
这件事我在动身以前，亦是要做好的。』

Now tell me the second matter.”
左宗棠又说∶『你再讲第二件。』


The second was important in both public and private affairs.
第二件是公私牵连，彼此有关的大事，

Since Ma’s appointment had begun to change Zhejiang’s political arrangements, Hu had wished to stop acting as banker to the provincial treasury.
胡雪岩从马新贻的新命下达，浙江政局开始变动之初，就希望不再代理藩库；

Jiang would not release him.
无奈蒋益澧不肯放他，

At the slightest mention, he clasped his hands repeatedly and begged Hu to “continue helping.”
略一提到，便连连拱手，要求『继续帮忙』。

Hu valued personal feeling too much to refuse and had reluctantly carried on.
胡雪岩最重情面，不能不勉为其难。

“Things are different now,” he said, after explaining.
『如今不同了。』胡雪岩谈过前半段的衷曲，

“You have ordered me to remain in Shanghai.
接着又说∶『大人命我长驻上海，

If grain, money, or arms are urgently needed, I alone must answer.
要粮要饷要军械，缓急之际，惟我是问；

The responsibility is too great for me to continue serving Zhejiang’s treasury as well.”
这个责任太重，没有余力再为浙江藩库效劳了。』

By “serving,” he meant finding advances whenever receipts failed to cover immediate needs.
所谓『效劳』，就是青黄不接之际，得要设法垫款。

Zuo understood, but saw it differently.
左宗棠当然明白他的意思；但却有不同的看法，

“Zhejiang’s treasury is to remit a hundred and forty thousand taels to me each month through your bank.
『雪岩，浙江藩库每个月要拨我十四万协饷，由你的钱庄转汇粮台。

If you remain its banker, it will be merely a transfer from your left hand to your right.
照这样子，你代理浙江藩库，等于左手交付右手，

It costs no trouble.
并不费事；

Why refuse so firmly?
何必坚拒呢？』

In my view, your continuing in that role can only benefit me.
他停了一下又说，『依我看，你代理浙江藩库，对我有利无害；

Whenever funds arrive, they can be remitted.
有款子收入，随时可以拨解。

If the front urgently needs money, you can arrange it more easily.”
如果前方有急用，你调度也方便。』

“No,” Hu replied.
『不！』胡雪岩说，

“First, once I have been transferred at your request for service in Fujian, it is improper for me to continue acting for Zhejiang’s treasury.
『第一，我既蒙大人奏调，归福建任用，就不便再代理浙江的藩库；

Second, precisely because I am managing your supplies, I must keep my dealings with Zhejiang clearly separate.
其次，惟其管了大人这方面的供应，我要跟浙江划分得清清楚楚。

If someone raises objections later, your reputation will not be involved.”
万一将来有人说闲话，也不致于牵涉到大人的名誉。』

“I am deeply obliged.
『承情之至！

You think of my interests at every turn.
你真是处处为我打算。

Since you insist, I shall tell Xiangquan.”
既然你一定坚持，我关照芗泉就是。』


The promise lifted a weight from Hu.
得此一诺，胡雪岩如释重负。

He alone saw the entire situation clearly.
因为整个情况，只有他看得最清楚；

Although the contribution for Fujian had been reduced by sixty thousand taels, the remaining sum was still an immense burden.
援闽之师的协饷虽已减去六万，对浙江来说，仍然极重的负担。

The new governor would naturally have plans of his own.
新任巡抚莅任后，自必有一番新猷展布，

Even if he could not restore everything at once, the sea dikes alone required a large expenditure.
纵汉有百废俱举，光是整修海塘，便须一笔极大的经费。

Frost’s Descent had already passed, the season when river officials reported that the waters were safely under control.
眼前霜降已过，河工是『报安澜』的时候；

At the beginning of the new year, however, work must start immediately.
一开了年，可就要立刻动手了！

From the spring “peach-blossom flood” onward, rising waters at the turn of spring and summer might bring catastrophe.
不然从『桃花汛』开始，春夏之交，洪水大涨，可能招致巨祸。

Would it be easy to act as banker to the treasury then?
那时藩库，岂是容易代理的？

Hu could plead inability to advance the cost of the dikes.
当然，海塘经费他可以表示无力代垫；

But if Ma said, “Then let Intendant Hu’s bank advance the contribution to Fujian,” could he refuse, on either public or private grounds?
但如马新贻说一句∶『那末福建的协饷请胡道台的钱庄垫一垫』；不论于公于仅，他总是义不容辞的吧？

In truth, even after relinquishing the treasury business he would still have to advance funds for Zuo.
事实确是如此，而且即使不代理浙江藩库，他亦仍得为左宗棠垫款。

The difference lay in how the transaction was understood.
只是同为一垫，说法不同。

As the treasury’s banker, he would be expected by Zhejiang to find the money.
在浙江来说，既是代理藩库，理当设法代垫；

Zuo, meanwhile, would regard it as an advance for Zhejiang and owe him no gratitude.
在左宗棠来说，胡雪岩是为浙江垫款，他不必见情。

Hu would please neither side.
这一来落得两头不讨好。

If instead Zhejiang, unable to remit, privately asked him for an advance, his action would be a public-spirited favor: Ma would be grateful, and Zuo would acknowledge his friendship.
倘或浙江解不出协饷，跟他情商代垫，那是私人急公好义；马新贻会感激，左宗棠亦会说他够朋友。

Most importantly, a formal loan from his bank to the provincial treasury would carry security and interest, leaving him no risk to bear.
而最要紧的是，浙江藩库向他的钱庄借款，有担保、有利息，不会担什么风险。

“What else is there?” Zuo asked.
『还有什么事？

“You might as well tell me everything now.”
你索性此刻都说了吧？』
