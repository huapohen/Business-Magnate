胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第九章（本章第6部分，共7部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


“I shall trouble you no further,” Hu said with a smile.
『不敢再麻烦大人了。』胡雪岩笑嘻嘻地说，

“The rest are small matters I can manage myself.”
『其余都是些小事，我自己料理得下来。』

Yet he had an enormous amount of public business in hand.
话虽如此，胡雪岩经管的公事太多；

Besides the bank, his own interests included silk and tea, and there were innumerable favors undertaken for others.
自己的生意，除钱庄以外，还有丝茶；加上受人之托，有许多闲事不能不管。

With the political arrangements changing and Zuo’s new responsibilities requiring him to remain in Shanghai, he had to settle his public and private affairs in Zhejiang during the interval between Zuo’s departure and Ma’s arrival.
如今政局变动，又受左宗棠的重托，要长驻上海；在浙江的公私事务，必得趁左宗棠离浙，马新贻未到任这段期间内，作个妥善的安排。

He became so busy that meals and sleep lost all regularity.
因而忙得饮食不时，起居失常，

He wished for another mouth and another pair of hands.
恨不得多生一张口，多长一双手，才能应付得下来。

Even amid this pressure, Zuo frequently summoned him.
在这百忙里，左宗棠还是时常约见，

One day a personal letter arrived, inviting him to visit West Lake the following morning.
有一天甚至来封亲笔信，约他第二天上午逛西湖；

Hu scarcely knew whether to laugh or cry, but could not refuse.
这下，胡雪岩可真有些啼笑皆非了！但亦不能不践约；

He stayed awake all night to finish several long-delayed and urgent matters originally scheduled for the next morning.
只好通宵不睡，将积压已久，不能不办理，原来预定在第二天上午必须了结的几件紧要事务，提前处理。

Just before dawn he dozed in his clothes.
到曙色将透之时，和衣打个盹；

Soon he woke with a start to find bright sunlight filling the windows.
睡不多久，一惊而醒，但见是个红日满窗的好天气，

He hurriedly washed, dressed, and took his chair toward the lake.
急急漱洗更衣，坐上轿子飞快地直奔西湖，来赴左宗棠的约会。

Passing the ruined Banner garrison, he noticed unusually large numbers of people walking north along the shore.
轿子抬过残破的『旗营』，西湖在望；胡雪岩忽然发现沿湖滨往北的行人特别多。

He sent an attendant to inquire and learned that they were going to see a “Western steamer.”
当时唤跟班去打听；才知道都是去看『西洋火轮船』的。

Then he understood.
胡雪岩恍然大悟，

Zuo had not invited him for a leisurely excursion but to witness the trial of a small steam vessel.
并非有逛西湖的闲情逸致；只是约他一齐去看小火轮试航——

Hu knew about the undertaking: as early as summer, Zuo had told him that suitable mechanics had been found to attempt its construction.
这件事胡雪岩当然也知道。早在夏天，就听左宗棠告诉过他，已觅妥机匠，试造火轮。

Too busy to ask further, Hu had not imagined that in three or four months they would actually produce a vessel of their own.
他因为太忙，不暇过问；不想三、四个月的功夫，居然有了一艘自己制造的小火轮。

This was momentous.
这是一件大事！


If they could build a small steamer, they could build a large one.
能造小轮船、就能造大轮船；

Hu’s mind ranged quickly over the many advantages China would gain from possessing large steamships.
胡雪岩的思路很宽也很快，立刻便想到了中国有大轮船的许多好处。

He became so absorbed that only the chair’s stopping roused him.
越想越深，想得出了神；直到停轿才警觉。

He stepped out before Zhaoqing Temple, one of West Lake’s four great monasteries.
下轿一看，是在西湖四大名刹之一的昭庆寺前。

A pavilion of canvas stood by the water.
湖滨一座篷帐；

Outside it, rank buttons and feathers glittered, and swords flashed like snow.
帐外翎顶辉煌，刀光如雪；

Most striking were several foreigners among the crowd.
最触目的是夹杂着几名洋人，

One wore Western clothes; another wore the uniform of a third-rank Chinese military officer, with an artificial queue attached behind his hat.
其中一个穿西装；一个穿着三品武官服色，大帽子后面，还缀着一条假辫子。

Hu knew them well.
胡雪岩跟他们很熟，

Both were French: Giquel had left military for civil employment and been appointed commissioner of the Ningbo New Customs, hence his civilian dress; d’Aiguebelle had been recommended for the rank of canjiang for military service and willingly adopted Chinese dress to signify allegiance.
这两个洋将都是法国人，一个叫日意格，已改武就文，被委充为宁波新关的税务局，所以换穿便服；另一个叫德克碑，因军功保到参将，愿易服色，以示归顺，

Zuo valued him highly.
颇为左宗棠所器重。

On the lake, a small steamer lay secured by a very thick cable, its fire already lit.
看到湖中，极粗的缆绳系着一条小火轮，已经升火待发。

Hu joined the inspection.
胡雪岩亦随众参观，

While explanations and pointing fingers occupied the group, Zuo emerged from the tent, passed slowly between the civil and military officials standing at attention, halted at the shore, and called loudly, “Ask Lord Hu to come!”
正在指点讲解时，左宗棠已经出帐；在文武官员肃立站班的行列中，缓缓穿过，直到湖边站定，喊一大声∶『请胡大人！』


Hu came forward, saluted, and first apologized for not arriving earlier.
胡雪岩被唤了过去，行完礼，首先道歉∶『没有早来伺候。』

Then he smiled. “Grand Secretary Zeng and Governor Li both concern themselves with foreign methods and powerful arms.
又笑着说∶『曾中堂李中丞都讲究洋务，讲究坚甲利兵，

Now they will have to fall behind you.”
现在都要落在大人后头了。』

The compliment delighted Zuo.
这句话恭维得左宗棠心花大开，

Stroking his short, graying beard, he nodded. “That is just what I want them to see!
『我就是要他们看看！』他摸着花白短髭点头，

I particularly asked you here because only you understand my intention.”
『所以我特意要请你来看，只有你懂得我的用意。』

Hu dared not pursue the subject.
胡雪岩不敢再接口，

A passing compliment was harmless, but he did not know what deeper purpose Zuo had in mind, and understood little of the matter himself.
因为随口恭维，无甚关系。一往深处去谈，不知道左宗棠到底有什么主意；而且他自己对此道亦还不甚了解，

Better to conceal his ignorance for the moment.
不如暂且藏拙为妙。

Fortunately, this was a time for watching rather than discussion.
好在此刻亦不是深谈的时候；主要的是要看。

At a command, the crudely built little vessel began to chug, churning a great patch of water astern.
一声令下，那条形式简陋的小火轮，发出『卜卜卜』的响声，激起船尾好大一片水花；

Its engine faltered and resumed like the coughing of a feeble old man, struggling for breath.
但机器声时断时续，就象衰迈的老年人咳嗽那样，有些上气不接下气的模样。

At least ten thousand officials, soldiers, and ordinary people watched from the shore in anxious silence.
这时在湖边屏息注视的官员、士兵、百姓，不下上万之多；

The vessel made noise but did not move; everyone feared it would fail.
都为那条只响不动的小火轮捏把汗，惟恐它动不了，

The four mechanics responsible, drenched in sweat, kept climbing in and out of the engine compartment.
四名负责制造的机器匠，更是满头大汗，不断地在舱中钻进钻出；

At last the sound grew stronger and steadier.
忙了好半天，终于听得机器声音响亮了起来，而节奏匀净。

The vessel suddenly surged forward.
然后蓦地往前一冲；

“Thank Heaven, it is moving!” Hu exclaimed involuntarily.
胡雪岩情不自禁地说了句∶『谢天谢地，动了！』

Moving it was, but scarcely at any speed: it staggered along, barely making headway.
动是动了，却走不快；蹒蹒跚跚，勉强推动而已。

In about half an hour it completed a circuit of the lake and returned.
费了有两刻钟的功夫，在湖面上兜了个圈子，驶回原处。

The expectant prefect responsible for the work brought the mechanics, now wearing red-tasseled hats, to report.
承办的一名候补知府，领着戴了红缨帽的机器匠来交差；

Zuo’s face was grave, but he nevertheless ordered twenty taels for each man.
脸色很深沉的左宗棠，仍旧吩咐，赏机器匠每人二十两银子。


Seeing his dissatisfaction, the onlookers lost their enthusiasm.
大家看左宗棠不甚满意，都觉得意兴阑珊；

Hu felt the same.
胡雪岩也是如此。

After standing in the ranks to see Zuo depart, he hurried back to his business in the city.
站班送走了左宗棠，急急赶回城去忙自己的公私事务。

Yet at dusk another bodyguard arrived with Zuo’s card: “The commander will not dine until Lord Hu arrives.”
那知到得傍晚，左宗棠又派了戈什哈持着名片来请，说的是『大帅要等胡大人到了才开饭。』

At headquarters Hu was surprised to find both foreign advisers present, with an interpreter named Cai.
到了行辕，很意外地发现两位客卿都在，此外就是一个姓蔡的通事。

He greeted Zuo, exchanged bows with d’Aiguebelle and Giquel, and went in to dinner.
胡雪岩先见左宗棠；然后与德克碑、日意格行礼，彼此一揖，相将入席。

Though host, Zuo took the highest seat, the two Frenchmen on either side; Hu sat below them.
左宗棠虽是主人，仍居首座，左右两洋将，胡雪岩下首相陪；

Interpreter Cai, like a bodyguard, could only stand behind Zuo.
蔡通事就跟戈什哈一样，只有站立在左宗棠身后的分儿了。

“For foreign methods, we must consult foreigners,” Zuo told Hu. “I asked Commander d’Aiguebelle and Commissioner Giquel to inspect the vessel.
『办洋务要请教洋人。』左宗棠对胡雪岩说∶『我请德参将与日税务司下船看过，

They say the copied design is broadly sound, and that making the engine work at all is a considerable achievement.
说仿制的式样，大致不差，机器能够管用，就很难为他们。

But speed requires Western machinery.
不过，要走得快，得用西洋的轮机。

The commander happens to have a book of shipbuilding drawings.
德参将正好有本制船的图册，

You should look at it.”
你不妨看看。

“Yes.” Hu ventured a question. “Your intention, my lord, is—?”
『』是！『胡雪岩试探着问∶』大人的意思是——？

“First hear what they say.”
『』你先听听他们的说法。

Without answering directly, Zuo turned slightly and instructed Cai, “Ask whether they can hire foreign mechanics for me. I wish to manufacture marine engines.”
『左宗棠答非所问；然后略略回头，嘱咐蔡通事∶』你问他们，我想造轮船机器，他们能不能代雇洋匠？

Cai translated into French.
『于是蔡通事用法语传译。

Both men answered eagerly, one after the other, speaking rapidly with rolling tongues.
德克碑与日意格立即作答，一个讲过一另一个讲；舌头打卷，既快且急，显得十分起劲。

“They say they can do more than hire mechanics,” Cai reported.
『回大帅的话，』蔡通事说道∶『德参将与日税务司说，不但可以代雇洋匠，

“They would also procure materials, establish a yard, and supervise construction.
而且愿意代办材料，设厂监造。

Now that fighting in Zhejiang is finished, Commander d’Aiguebelle intends to leave military service and return to France. If you wish, he will devote himself to arranging this.”
如果大人有意，现在全浙军务告竣；德参将打算退伍回国，专门为大人奔走这件事。』

“Ah.”
『喔！』

Zuo nodded and gave Hu a penetrating look.
左宗棠点点头，向胡雪岩深深看了一眼。


Hu understood and put a succession of questions to the two guests, concentrating upon expense.
胡雪岩会意，随即向两位洋客提出一连串的问询；最着重的是经费。

They knew only approximate figures and could not answer everything.
德克碑与日意格亦只知大概，并不能有问必答。

Yet they followed the old Chinese maxim: acknowledge what you know, and acknowledge what you do not.
不过洋人倒是守着中国『知之为知之，不知为不知』的古训，

They offered no evasive guesses.
决不模棱两可地敷衍。

Hu could therefore use the reliable figures they supplied, drawing comparisons and deductions, to arrive at a general estimate.
因此以胡雪岩的头脑，根据已知的确实数字，引伸推比，亦能获知全盘的概算。

Dinner lasted until the first watch.
这一顿饭吃到起更方散。

After seeing the foreigners off, Zuo kept Hu behind and invited him into his private office.
左宗棠送走洋客，留下胡雪岩，邀到签押房里坐定，

Once seated, he said at once, “Xueyan, I want to build steam warships ourselves.”
第一句话就说∶『雪岩，我想自己造兵轮。』

Hu was startled.
胡雪岩吓一跳，

“That is no easy matter.
『这谈何容易？』

A shipyard would cost at least five hundred thousand taels.
他说，『造一个船厂，没有五十万银子下不来；

Each warship would cost two or three hundred thousand—and one cannot build a yard for a single ship.
造一条兵轮总也得二三十万银子——也不能为造一条兵轮设个船厂；

Say only ten: that is two or three million.
不说多，算造十条，就是两三百万。

Fujian and Zhejiang, even with Liangjiang added, may not have the means.”
闽浙两省，加上两江，也未见得有这个力量。』

“True.
『不错！

But do not be hasty.
不过，你不要急；

Hear me out, and you will see that my plan is not only practicable but necessary.”
等我说完，你就知道我的打算不但办得通，而且非如此打算不可。

Zuo looked about him with satisfaction.
雪岩，』左宗棠顾盼自喜地说，

“Li Shaoquan’s knowledge comes from experience gained in these last few years.
『李少荃的学问，是从阅历中来的，不过这几年的事；

He entered the Hanlin Academy merely because he knew the Book of Songs well.
他点翰林，不过靠一部诗经熟。

When I began serious study, he probably could not yet finish an essay.
我做学问的时候，只怕他文章 还没有完篇。

As for great warships upon the open ocean, I began studying them in the nineteenth year of Daoguang—” In that year Lin Zexu destroyed opium in Guangdong, British warships entered Chinese waters, and the Opium War broke out.
说到汪洋大海中的艨艟巨舶，我从道光十九年起，就下过功夫——。』这年林则徐在广东查毁鸦片，英国军舰犯境，爆发了鸦片战争；

Tao Shu also died that year while Governor-General of Liangjiang.
也就是这一年，陶澍病殁在两江总督任上，

Zuo moved into the Tao household to look after its affairs and gained access to the books left at Yinxin Stone House.
左宗棠迁居陶家，代为照料一切，得能遍读印心石屋的遗书，

Histories, biographies, miscellaneous records, and tales from the Tang and Song onward, together with Qing chronicles and public and private documents—anything touching upon maritime lands—he read them all.
凡唐宋以来，史传、别录、小说；以及入清以后的志乘、载记、官私文书凡是有关海国故事的，无不涉猎。

Great ships on the ocean were therefore no wholly unfamiliar subject to him.
所以谈到『汪洋大海中的艨艟巨舶』，他不算全然外行。


“Modern foreign steam warships have no precedent in antiquity,” Zuo continued.
『如今洋人的火轮兵船，于古无征；

“But principles can be extended from one case to another.
不过举一反三，道理是一样的。

Ocean-going ships cannot be used in rivers without running aground.
海船不可行于江河，不然必致搁浅。

The absurd thing is that our distinguished officials fail to understand even so simple a point and let foreigners play them in the palms of their hands.
可笑的是，衮衮诸公，连这点浅近的道理都不懂，以致为洋人玩弄于股掌之上！

Li Shaoquan’s knowledge of foreign affairs is really limited.”
说起来，李少荃的洋务，懂得实在也有限。』

Hu could make nothing of this sudden declaration.
这番话在胡雪岩听来，没头没脑，无从捉摸；

They were now familiar enough to dispense with ceremony, so he asked plainly, “Which affair do you mean?”
他跟左宗棠的关系，已到熟不拘礼的程度，当即老实问道∶『大人指的是哪件事？』

“The purchase of warships from Britain at the end of the Xianfeng reign, of course.”
『不就是咸丰末年跟英国买兵轮那件事吗？』

“Oh, I remember.
『喔，我想起来了，是有那么一回事。

Hangzhou was under siege then.
当时杭州被围；

After it fell, I was seriously ill in Ningbo and cut off from everything.
后来杭州失守，我在宁波生一场大病，一切都隔膜了；

I knew there was such a purchase, but nothing of its history.”
只知有这样一件事，对来龙去脉，完全不清楚。』

“I know it well.
『我很清楚。

I have read all the memorials and can give you the outline.
这重公案的始末经过，我细看过全部奏折，可以约略跟你说个大概。

The Englishmen Lay and Hart were manipulating matters.
是英国人李泰国与赫德捣鬼，

Britain was to procure three medium-sized steamers and four small ones, at an agreed price of six hundred thousand taels.
英国代办中号火轮三只，小号火轮四只，船价讲定六十万银子，

Lay took it upon himself to increase the amount again and again, until it reached one million seventy thousand.
李泰国擅作主张，一加再加，加到一百零七万银子。

Once the ships arrived, their officers’ and men’s pay, coal, and miscellaneous expenses would cost a hundred thousand taels every month.
至于火轮到后，轮上官兵薪饷、煤炭杂用，每个月要用十万银子。

Worse still, the crews were all to be commanded by British officers—” “May I interrupt?” Hu asked. “Why was that?”
这还不算，火轮上的官兵，都要由英国人管带——。』『我打句岔，』胡雪岩截断了话问∶『这为了什么？』

“Here.
『喏，

Read this and you will understand.”
你看看这个就知道了。』

Zuo had carefully collected copies of the relevant edicts and memorials in a booklet.
左宗棠真是有心人，已将前几年购买英国兵轮的有关上谕与奏折，抄辑成册；

He opened it at one entry and handed it to Hu.
这时随手翻开一篇，递给胡雪岩，让他自己去细看。


It was a joint memorial submitted in the fifth month of the second Tongzhi year by Prince Gong, minister directing foreign affairs, Wenxiang, and their colleagues.
这一篇抄的是同治二年五月间，总理各国事务大臣恭亲王，及文祥等入会衔的奏折，

It began: “Your servants, faced with the continuing rebellion, sought every means of securing victory and proposed the purchase of foreign gunboats to assist in its suppression.
一开头就说∶窃臣等前以贼氛不清，力求制胜之方，因拟购买外洋炮船，以为剿贼之资，

A special memorial was submitted in the fifth month of the eleventh Xianfeng year, and the following decree was received: ‘The rebels’ power is spreading through the southeast.
于咸丰十一年五月间专折奏明，奏上谕∶『东南贼势蔓延，

If foreign gunboats can indeed be purchased, they will provide effective aid in suppressing them and benefit the general situation.’
果能购买外洋炮船，剿赋必可得力，实于大局有益。』

Reverently obeying, we communicated with the governors and governors-general concerned.
等因，钦此；遵即咨行各该督抚。

Thereafter Zeng Guofan, Governor-General of Liangjiang, reported that ‘the purchase of foreign ships and guns is the foremost urgent measure for rescuing the present situation.’”
旋据两江督巨曾国藩复奏，『购买外洋船炮为今日救时第一要务。』

Hu needed to read no further.
读到这里，就不必再往下看了。

“If the purpose was suppressing rebels, small gunboats able to navigate the Yangtze would suffice.
胡雪岩说道∶『如用于剿贼，只须能航行长江的小炮艇；

Why spend a million taels?”
何致于要花到一百万银子？』

“Exactly!
『就是这话罗！

It shows how blind our eminent gentlemen are.
衮衮诸公目贵目贵不明，于此可见。

Read this other memorial.”
你看年这一篇！』

Zuo pointed to one submitted by Zeng Guoquan late in the eighth month of the second Tongzhi year: “By imperial order, seven steamships have been purchased at great expense.
左宗棠指给胡雪岩看的是，同治二年八月下旬曾国荃的一道奏折，说的是∶查前后廷旨购办轮船七号，不惜巨资，

The undertaking has fortunately succeeded, and all are said to be approaching the coast.
幸而有成，闻皆将到海口矣！

Yet recent correspondence between the Zongli Yamen and the foreigner Lay shows that, besides the actual purchase price of a million taels, the foreign crews’ monthly maintenance will exceed seventy thousand, amounting in a year to no less than a million, all deducted from customs revenue.
惟近见总理衙门与洋人李泰国商定往复；除轮船实价百万之外，所用西人兵士每月口粮七万余两，每年大率不下百万两，俱于海关支扣。

The national treasury is empty.
窃计国家帑藏空虚，

To add so vast an annual expense must strain it further.”
倏而岁增巨款，度支将益不给。


“When the purchase was first proposed, it was assumed that Chinese personnel would be employed and could be freely commanded.
当始议购买之时，原以用中国人力，可以指挥自如，

At that time the Yangtze was obstructed; these powerful vessels were intended to defeat a powerful enemy.
且其时长江梗塞，正欲借此巨器，以平巨寇。

Since the capture of Jiufuzhou this summer, under the Emperor’s august protection, the river route has been opened.
自今夏攻克九氵伏州，仰仗皇上威福，江路已通，

Of the cities along it, only the provincial capital, Jinling, remains unrecovered.
江边之城，仅金陵省会，尚未恢复；

Our river navy, its masts thick as a forest, is cooperating fully with the land forces.
然长江水师，帆樯如林，与陆军通力合作，

Once the encirclement is complete, the enemy can certainly be swept away within a fixed time.
一经合围，定可克期扫荡。

I have observed steamships navigating the Yangtze.
巨窍见轮船经过长江，

Wherever sandbanks twist and intersect, a vessel may fail to turn aside in time and risk grounding.
每遇沙渚回互，或趋避不汲，时有胶浅之虞。

The river is narrow, offering none of the sea’s freedom of movement.
盖江路狭窄，非若大海之得以施展如意。

It is like a strong warrior holding a long spear in a short alley: before, behind, and on either side he must encounter obstacles.
譬犹健儿持长矛于短巷之中，左右前后，必多窒碍，

The conditions make this inevitable.
其势之使然也。

If even ordinary movement in a straight line is difficult, how can such ships maneuver freely in battle and display their full powers?
平时一线直行，犹且如此；临阵之际，何能盘旋往复，尽其所长？

On the river their effectiveness is not merely reduced; circumstances may leave them helpless.
是大江之用轮船，非特势力少逊，究亦有术穷之时，

If brought upriver now, their fighting strength is not truly needed.
今会其入江，实有不借彼战攻之力；

If kept at the river mouth, they will lie idle with nothing to do.”
若顿诸海口，则又安闲无所事事。


Again, Hu could close the booklet.
看到这里，亦可以掩卷了。

Purchasing and building great steamships was not for the purpose of suppressing rebels.
购造大轮船，非是为了剿匪；

When Zeng Guoquan submitted his memorial, Jinling was about to be encircled and Li Hongzhang was vigorously attacking Suzhou.
当曾国荃上此奏折时，金陵将次合围；苏州亦正由李鸿章 猛攻之中，

Success was already assured; naturally Guoquan wanted no one to share his glory.
大功之成，已有把握，曾国荃自然不想有人来分他的功。

His comparison with a long spear in a short alley, moreover, aptly described ocean-going ships operating on a river.
而况他所作的譬喻，如『健儿持长矛于短之中，左右前后，必多窒碍』，衡诸海轮和示范的实况亦甚贴切。

The court already regarded Lay as deceitful and difficult to deal with.
朝廷正以李泰国狡诈，难以与谋；

Such a memorial would naturally encourage it to abandon the proposal decisively.
得此一奏，当然会毅然决然地，打消此议。

“But circumstances have changed,” Zuo said. “Along Fujian’s coast, only steam warships can secure our frontiers and resist foreign enemies.
『然而，今昔异势，』左宗棠说∶『福建沿海，非兵轮不足固疆圉、御外敌。

What do you think?”
雪岩，你以为如何？』

“Yes.
『是！

You take the long view, my lord.
大人见得远。』

Governors and governors-general are responsible for their regions’ defense.
胡雪岩答说，『督抚担当方面军务；

Internal disorder is nearly ended; foreign threats must not be neglected.
如今内乱将平，外患不可不防。

If outsiders attack from the seas off Fujian and Zhejiang, responsibility for holding the territory will rest entirely upon you.
倘或外人由闽浙海面进犯，守土之责，全在大人。

Without long-term preparation, even if you do not repeat Lord Ye’s absurd failure in Guangdong, your reputation will suffer.”
如果不作远图；虽不致于闹出叶大人在广东的那种笑话来，可也伤了大人的英名。』

Lord Ye was Ye Mingchen, Governor-General of Guangdong and Guangxi, who “neither fought, negotiated, nor defended; neither died, surrendered, nor fled,” and finally died in exile in India.
所谓『叶大人』是指『不战不和不守，不死不降不走』，客死在印度的两广总督叶名琛。

The comparison was somewhat awkward, yet his fate offered a warning.
拿他作比，稍觉不伦；但就事论事，却是前车可鉴。

Zuo grew animated. “Exactly!
左宗棠很起劲地说∶『你说得一点不错！

It makes my duty all the clearer.
益见得我责无旁贷，

I am determined to establish a shipyard.”
雪岩，我决计要办船厂。』

“Provided the funds can be found, it should certainly be done.”
『只要经费有着，当然应该办。』

“There is no need to worry about funds.
『经费不必愁。

The former purchase was apportioned among the customs stations.
当然购船，是由各海关分摊；

We shall follow the same precedent.
如今当然仍照旧章.不过，

Fujian and Zhejiang’s customs will naturally have to exert themselves especially.”
闽浙两海关，格外要出力。』

“Certainly.
『那是一定的。

But—” Hu fell thoughtfully silent.
不过——。』胡雪岩沉吟着不再说下去了。


Zuo knew that such hesitation meant Hu saw a serious difficulty and could not readily voice it.
左宗棠知道，遇到这种情形，便是胡雪岩深感为难，不便明说的表示；

He also believed that in the end Hu would solve it.
可是他也知道，到头来，难题在胡雪岩也一定会解消。

The essential thing was to let him speak without reserve.
最要紧的是，让他无所顾忌，畅所欲言。

Adopting a leisurely air, Zuo said, “No hurry.
因此，他了以闲豫的神态，『不必急，

We can discuss it slowly.
我们慢慢谈。

The undertaking is necessary; its timing need not be fixed.”
事情是势在必行，时间却可不限。』

He smiled mysteriously.
他神秘地一笑到，

“Once I set out on this campaign, the whole situation will be in my hands.
『等我这趟出兵以后，局面就完全掌握在我手里了；

I can make it move quickly or slowly, tighten or relax it as I please.”
要紧要慢，收发由心。』

The last words were obscure.
这最后两句话，颇为费解；

Even Hu, alert as he was, had to study Zuo’s expression and weigh their meaning.
就连胡雪岩这样机警的人，也不能不观色察言，细细去咀嚼其中的意味。

At the sight of that confident yet enigmatic face, he suddenly understood.
看到左宗棠那种成竹在胸，而又诡谲莫测的神态，胡雪岩陡然意会；

Zuo meant the campaign in Fujian.
所谓『要紧要慢、收发由心』，是指入闽剿匪的军务而言。

He was confident not merely that the remaining rebels could be eliminated, but that he could determine when: soon if he wished, late if he preferred.
换句话说，残余的长毛，他不但自信，必可肃清；并且肃清的日子，是远是近，亦有充分的把握，要远就远，要近就近。

The choice would depend entirely upon his purpose.
这远近之间，完全要看他是怎么样一个打算？

Eager to serve the throne and win distinction, he could pursue vigorously and end the campaign quickly.
勤劳王事，急于立功，自是穷追猛打，克日可以肃清；

If the rebels were useful—for instance, as a reason to demand troops and money—he would preserve the enemy to increase his own importance.
倘或残余的长毛有可以利用之处，譬如借口匪势猖獗，要饷要兵，那就必然『养寇自重』了。


Hu must first understand his plan. “How many years do you intend to remain in Fujian, my lord?”
想到这里，就得先了解左宗棠的打算∶『大人，』他问，『预备在福建做几年？』

“An excellent question!”
『问得好！』

Zuo was delighted by this meeting of minds.
左宗棠有莫逆于心之乐；

“How many years do you think I should remain?”
然后反问一句∶『你看我应该在福建做几年？』

“If you are determined to establish a shipyard, several years will be necessary.”
『如果大人决心办船厂，当然要多做几年。』

“I think so too.”
『我也是这么想。』

“And how will you manage it?
『做法呢？』

Surely you cannot keep fighting the rebels indefinitely?”
胡雪岩问，『总不能一直打长毛吧？』

“Naturally not.
『当然，当然！

They are doomed souls at the bottom of the cauldron, unable to withstand a blow.
釜底游魂，不堪一击；

To prolong the campaign would injure my reputation.
迁延日久，损我的威名。

But neither need I succeed the moment I arrive.”
不过，也不必马到成功。』

He stroked his beard in thought, the smile fading.
说到这里，左宗棠拈髭沉思；脸上的笑容尽敛，

After a long silence he nodded. “As you know, I must gain Guangdong as a base.
好久才点点头说∶『你知道的，广东这个地盘非拿过来不可；

How long the fighting lasts depends upon whether my in-law understands the situation.
兵事久斩，只看我那位亲家是不是见机？

If he withdraws in good time, I shall gladly defeat the enemy promptly.
他肯急流勇退，我乐得早日克敌致果；

Otherwise, more money must be spent.
不然就得多费些饷了。

Do you understand?”
你懂我的意思吗？』
