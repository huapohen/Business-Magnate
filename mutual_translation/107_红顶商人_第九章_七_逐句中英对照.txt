胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第九章（本章第7部分，共7部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


『懂！』胡雪岩说，
“I understand,” Hu replied.

『我就是要明白了大人的意思，才可以为大人打算。』
“Only when I know your intentions can I make plans for you.”

『那末，如今你是明白了？』
“Then you understand now?”

这是提醒胡雪岩该作打算了。
The question reminded him that it was time to do so.

他精神抖擞地答说∶『只要广东能听大人的话，事情就好办了。
He answered with renewed energy. “If Guangdong follows your lead, matters will be manageable.

我在想，将来大人出奏，请办船厂，象这样的大事，朝廷一定寄谕沿海各省督抚，各抒所见。
When you petition to establish a shipyard, the court will surely ask the coastal governors and governors-general for their opinions on so important an undertaking.

福建、浙江不用说；
Fujian and Zhejiang are assured.

如果广东奏复，力赞其成。大人的声势就可观了。』
If Guangdong also replies in strong support, your position will be formidable.”

『正是！
“Exactly!

我必得拿广东拉到手，就是这个道理。
That is why I must bring Guangdong to my side.

南洋沿海有三省站在我这面；两江何敢跟我为难？』
With three southern coastal provinces behind me, how could Liangjiang oppose me?”

『两江亦不敢公开为难；
“It would not oppose you openly.

必是在分摊经费上头做文章.说到办船厂的经费，由海关洋税项下抽拨，是天经地义的事。
It would make difficulties over sharing the expense. Funding a shipyard from maritime customs revenue is perfectly reasonable.

北洋的津海关，暂且不提；
Set aside Tianjin’s northern customs for the moment.

南洋的海关，包括广东在内，一共五大关∶上海的江海关；广州的粤海关；福建的闽海关跟厦门关；我们浙江的宁波关。
In the south, including Guangdong, there are five major customs establishments: Jianghaiguan in Shanghai, Guangdong Customs at Guangzhou, Fujian Customs and Xiamen Customs, and our own Ningbo Customs.

将来分摊经费，闽、厦两关以外，粤海关肯支持，就是五关占其三；
When the cost is apportioned, support from Guangdong would give you three of the five, counting Fujian and Xiamen.

浙江归大人管辖，马中丞亦不能不卖这个面子。
Zhejiang lies under your jurisdiction, so Governor Ma would have to show you consideration too.

这一来，两江方面莫非好说江海关一毛不拔？』
Could Liangjiang then insist that Jianghaiguan contribute nothing?”


『对了！
“Precisely.

你的打算合情合理；其间举足重轻的关键，就在广东。
Your plan is fair and reasonable, and Guangdong is the decisive point.

雪岩，我想这样，你把我这个抄本带回去，参照当年购船成例，好好斟酌，写个详细节略来；
Take this collection of copies home, study the precedent of the former ship purchase, and prepare a detailed memorandum.

至于什么时候出奏，要等时机。
When to submit it will depend upon the opportunity.

照我想，总要广东有了着落，才能出奏。』
I think we must first settle Guangdong.”

『是的。我也是这么想。』
“I agree.

胡雪岩说，『好在时间从容得很，
Fortunately, we have ample time.

一主面我先跟德克碑他们商量；一方面大致算一算经费的来源。
I shall consult d’Aiguebelle and the others and make a rough calculation of the sources of revenue.

至于筹备这件大事，先要用些款子，归我想办法来垫。
Some money will be needed for preliminary work; I shall arrange to advance it.”

『』好极！
“Excellent.

就这么办。
We shall proceed that way.

不过，雪岩，江海关是精华所在；
But Jianghaiguan holds the richest resources.

总不能让李少荃一直把持在那里！
We cannot let Li Shaoquan keep them entirely in his grasp.

你好好想个法子，多挖他一点出来！
Think of a way to draw more out of him.”

『』法子有。不过，『胡雪岩摇摇头，』最好不用那个法子！
“There is a way,” Hu said, shaking his head, “but it would be better not to use it.”

『』为什么？
“Why?”

『『用那个法子要挨骂。』
“We should be roundly criticized.”

『这你先不必管。
“Leave that aside for the moment.

请说，是何法子？』
What is the method?”


『可以跟洋人借债。』
“We could borrow from foreigners.

胡雪岩说，『借债要担保。
A loan requires security.

江海关如说目前无款可拨，那末总有可拨的时候。
If Jianghaiguan says it cannot contribute now, there must be a time when it can.

我们就指着江海关某年某年收入的多少成数，作为还洋债的款，
We could earmark a certain proportion of its receipts in specified future years to repay the foreign loan.

这就是担保。
That would be the security.

不过，天朝大国，向洋人借债；一定有人不以为然。
But for our great Celestial Empire to borrow from foreigners would certainly provoke opposition.

那批都老爷群起而攻，可是件吃不消的事。
If the censors attacked together, it would be difficult to bear.”

『这番话说得左宗棠发楞；
Zuo was struck silent.

接着站起身来踱了好一回方步；最后拿起已交在胡雪岩手里的』抄本『，翻到一页，指着说道∶』你看看这一段！
He rose and paced solemnly for some time, then took back the booklet he had handed to Hu, turned to a page, and pointed. “Read this passage.”

『指的是恭亲王所上奏折中的一段，
It came from Prince Gong’s memorial.

据李泰国向恭王面称∶『中国如欲用银，伊能代向外国商人借银一千万两，分年带利归还。』
Lay had told the prince that, should China need money, he could arrange a loan of ten million taels from foreign merchants, repayable with interest over a term of years.

可是恭王又下结论∶『其请借银一千万两之说，中国亦断无此办法。』
The prince, however, had concluded: “As for the proposal to borrow ten million taels, China can under no circumstances adopt such a course.”

『大人请看，』
“There, my lord.”

胡雪岩指着那句话说∶『朝中决不准借洋债。』
Hu pointed to the sentence. “The court will never permit a foreign loan.”


『彼一时也，此一时也！』
“That was then; this is now!”

说到这里，左宗棠突然将话锋扯了开去，
Zuo abruptly changed the direction of the discussion.

『雪岩，你要记住一件事；办大事最要紧的是拿主意！
“Xueyan, remember this: in great affairs, the most important thing is to make up one’s mind.

主意一拿定，要说出个道理来并不难；
Once the decision is made, reasons are not hard to find.

拿恭王的这个奏折来说，
Take this memorial.

当时因为中国买船，而事事要听洋人的主张，朝中颇有人不以为然；
China was buying ships but had to submit to foreign wishes in everything; many at court objected.

恭王已有打退堂鼓的意思，所以才说中国断无借洋债的办法。
Prince Gong was already inclined to retreat, which is why he said China could never borrow abroad.

倘或当时军务并无把握，非借重洋人的坚甲利炮不可；
Suppose the military situation had been uncertain and foreign armor and guns indispensable.

那时就另有一套话说了；
There would have been a different argument.

第一、洋人愿意借债给中国，是仰慕天朝，自愿助顺；
First, foreigners lending to China would be showing admiration for the Celestial Dynasty and voluntarily supporting the rightful cause.

第二、洋人放债不怕放倒，正表示信赖中国，一定可以肃清洪杨，光复东南财赋之区，将来有力量还债。
Second, their willingness to lend without fearing loss would demonstrate confidence that China could suppress the Taipings, recover the rich southeastern provinces, and repay the debt.

你想想，那是多好听的话，
Consider how pleasing that would sound!

朝廷岂有不欣然许诺之理？』
Why would the court not gladly agree?”

这几句话，对胡雪岩来说，就是『学问』；
To Hu, these words were an education.

心诚悦服地表示受教。而左宗棠亦就越谈越起劲了。
He sincerely acknowledged the lesson, encouraging Zuo to speak still more eagerly.

『我再你讲讲办大事的秘诀。
“Let me tell you another secret of great undertakings.

有句成语，叫做「与其待时，不如乘势」；
There is a saying: ‘Better to use a favorable situation than wait for a favorable time.’

许多看起来难办的大事，居然顺顺利利地办成了，就因为懂得乘势的缘故。
Many apparently difficult enterprises succeed smoothly because someone understands how to use the force of circumstances.

何谓势？
What is that force?

雪岩，我倒考考你；
Let me test you.

你说与我听听，何谓势？』
Tell me what it means.”


『这可是考倒我了。』胡雪岩笑道∶『还是请大人教导吧！』
“You have defeated me with that question,” Hu said with a smile. “Please instruct me.”

『有些事，我要跟你请教；有些事我倒是当仁不让，可以教教你。
“In some matters I must seek your advice; in others I may properly claim the right to teach you.

谈到势，要看人、看事、还要看时。
The force of circumstances depends upon the person, the business, and the time.

人这势者，势力；也就是小人势利之势。
In a person, it is influence—the power that attracts the self-seeker.

当初我几乎遭不测之祸，就因为湖广总督的官文的势力，比湖南巡抚路秉章 来得大，
I once nearly met disaster because Guanwen, Governor-General of Huguang, had more influence than Luo Bingzhang, Governor of Hunan.

朝中自然听他的。
Naturally the court listened to him.

他要参我，容易得很。』
It was easy for him to impeach me.”

『是的。
“Yes.

同样一件事，原是要看什么人说。』
The same matter carries different weight according to who speaks.”

『也要看说的是什么事？』左宗棠接口，
“And according to what he speaks about,” Zuo added.

『以当今大事来说，军务重于一切；而军务所急，肃清长毛余孽，又是首要，
“Today, military affairs outweigh everything; among military affairs, eliminating the Taiping remnants comes first.

所以我为别的事说话，不一定有力量，
My words on other subjects may carry little weight.

要谈入闽剿匪，就一定会听我的。
Speak of suppressing the rebels in Fujian, however, and they will certainly listen.

你信不信？』
Do you believe that?”

『怎么不信？
“Of course!

信，信！』
Certainly I do.”

『我想你一定信得过。
“I thought you would.

以我现在的身分，说话是够力量了；
My present position gives my words sufficient force.

论事则还要看是什么事？在什么时候开口？
But I must still consider the business and the moment to speak.

时机把握得恰到好处，言听计从。
Choose the moment exactly, and advice is heard and plans followed.

说迟了自误；说早了无用。』
Speak too late, and you harm yourself; too early, and it is useless.”

左宗棠笑道∶『譬如撵我那位亲家，现在就还不到时候。』
Zuo smiled. “For example, the time has not yet come to drive out my in-law.”


『是的。』胡雪岩脱口答道，
“Exactly,” Hu answered at once.

『要打到福建、广东交界的地方，才是时候。』左宗棠大笑，笑完了正色说道∶『办船厂一事，要等军务告竣，筹议海防，那才是一件事。
“Not until the fighting reaches the Fujian–Guangdong border.” Zuo laughed heartily, then grew serious. “The shipyard must wait until military operations are concluded and coastal defense becomes the subject of discussion.

但也要看时机。
Even then, the moment must be right.

不过，我们必得自己有预备，才不会坐失时机。
But we must prepare in advance or the opportunity will pass.

你懂我的意思了吧？』
Do you understand?”

胡雪岩不但懂他的意思，而且心领神会，比左宗棠想得更深更远。
Hu understood not merely the words but their full implications, carrying the thought farther than Zuo himself.

结合大局，左宗棠的勋名前程，和他自己的事业与利益，了解了一件事∶左宗棠非漂漂亮亮地打胜仗不可！
Combining the general situation, Zuo’s future distinction, and his own business and interests, he saw one essential point: Zuo must win a brilliant military victory.

这是一个没有东西可以代替的关键。
Nothing else could take its place.


由于这个了解，他决定了为左宗棠办事的优先顺序；不过，这当然先要征得同意，
From this understanding Hu decided the order of his work for Zuo, though he must first obtain agreement.

因而这样说道∶『大人的雄心壮志，我都能体会得到；到什么时候该办什么事，我亦大致有数，
“I understand your ambitions and can broadly judge when each matter should be undertaken.

事先会得预备。
I shall prepare beforehand.

如今我要请问大人的是，这趟带兵剿匪，最着重的是什么？』
What I wish to ask now is this: in the coming campaign, what matters most?”

这句话将左宗棠问住了；
The question halted Zuo.

想了一会答道∶『自然是饷！』
After a moment he answered, “Funds, naturally.”

『饷我可以想法子垫。
“I can arrange advances.

不过，并不是非我不可；
But that is not a service only I can provide.

各处协饷，能够源源报解，何必我来垫借，多吃利息？』
If the provincial contributions arrive regularly, why borrow from me and pay extra interest?”

『啊，我懂你的话了。』
“Ah, I see what you mean.

左宗棠说，『工欲善其事，必先利其器；
A workman must sharpen his tools before he can work well.

兵坚而器不利，则能守而不能攻。
Good soldiers with poor weapons can defend but cannot attack.

我要西洋精良兵器，多多益善；
I need fine Western arms, the more the better.

雪岩，这非你不可！』
That is something I must rely on you for.”

『是！愚见正是如此。』
“Exactly.”

胡雪岩欣慰地答说∶『我替大人办事，第一是采办西洋兵器，
Hu was pleased. “Then procuring Western weapons will be my first concern.

不必大人嘱咐，我自会留意。
You need not remind me; I shall watch for them myself.

至于炮弹子药，更不在话下；决不让前方短缺。
Shells and ammunition go without saying—I shall never let the front run short.

第二是饷，
Second comes money.

份内该拨的数目，不管浙江藩库迟拨早拨，我总替大人预备好。
Whatever sum is due under the regular allocation, I shall have it ready, whether Zhejiang’s treasury remits early or late.

至于额外用款，数目不大，当然随时都有；
Small additional sums will naturally be available whenever needed.

如果数目太大，最好请大人预先嘱咐一声，免得措手不及。
For very large ones, please give advance notice so that I am not taken unprepared.

此外办造厂之类，凡是大人交代过的，我都会一样一样办到；
As for the shipyard and everything else you have entrusted to me, I shall attend to each in turn.

请大人不必费心，不必催，
You need neither worry nor press me.

我总不误时机就是。』
I shall not miss the proper moment.”

『好极了！』
“Excellent!”

左宗棠愉悦异常，
Zuo was delighted.

『汉高成功，功在萧何。
“The founding Han emperor owed his success to Xiao He.

我们就这样说了；
Then we are agreed.

你尽管放手去做，
Go ahead freely.

一切有我担待。』
I shall stand behind everything.”
