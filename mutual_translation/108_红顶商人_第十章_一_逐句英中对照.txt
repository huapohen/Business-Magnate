The Red-Topped Merchant

原著：高阳


CHAPTER TEN
第十章


At the end of the tenth month of the third year of Tongzhi, Zuo Zongtang relinquished his concurrent duties as acting governor of Zhejiang.
左宗棠在同治三年十月底，交卸了兼署浙江巡抚的职司；

Amid cannon salutes and the respectful farewell of Hangzhou’s civil and military officials, he set out for Fujian to direct the campaign.
在杭州全城文武官员，呜炮恭送之下，启程入闽督师。

The relief armies had already departed in three columns.
在此以前，援闽之师分三路出发。

The western column’s main force comprised eight thousand newly raised troops under Liu Dian, Zhejiang’s judicial commissioner and assistant director of military affairs in Fujian.
西路以帮办福建军务浙江按察使刘典所部新军八千人为主力；

With twenty-five hundred men under Wang Debang, who held the designation of judicial commissioner pending appointment, they entered Tingzhou from Jianchang in Jiangxi.
会同记名按察使王德榜的两千五百人，由江西建昌入汀州；

In the centre, Huang Shaochun, designated for appointment as provincial military commander, and Deputy Brigade Commander Liu Mingdeng led forty-six hundred men from Quzhou through Pucheng and Jianning towards Yanping.
中路记名提督黄少春，副将刘明灯两部共四千六百人，由浙江衢州，经福建浦城、建宁入延平；

In the east, Acting Zhejiang Military Commander Gao Liansheng and Wei Guangbing, a prefect awaiting appointment, led forty-five hundred men across the Qiantang River to Ningbo, where they boarded steamships for Fuzhou.
东路由署理浙江提督高连升会同候补知府魏光邴，领兵四千五百人，过钱塘江由宁波乘轮船，循海道至福州登陆。

All three columns were directed towards southern Fujian.
这三路军队的目标都是闽南——

Li Shixian occupied Zhangzhou, west of Xiamen; Ding Taiyang was at Wuping, where Fujian, Guangdong, and Jiangxi met; Wang Haiyang ranged between the eastern and western districts of southern Fujian.
李世贤踞厦门之西的漳州；丁太洋在福建、广东、江西三省交界的武平；而汪海洋则在闽南的东西之间流窜。

Zuo was determined to prevent them from putting to sea.
左宗棠的打算是，决不能让他们出海；

He would tighten the pressure from north, west, and east, forcing them south.
由北、西、东三面收紧，压迫敌人南窜。

South of Fujian lay Guangdong.
福建之南就是广东。

Mao Hongbin, governor-general of Liangguang, and Guo Songtao, governor of Guangdong, saw the danger.
两广总督毛鸿宾与广东巡抚郭嵩焘，见此光景，心知不妙。

If Zuo drove the rebels into their province, the relieving army would follow, leaving Guangdong squeezed between rebels and “friendly troops.”
左宗棠如果驱贼入粤，则援闽之师，随贼而至，会形成长毛与『友军』交困的窘境，

They were deeply alarmed.
所以非常着急。

Yet the Guangdong forces under the two deputy brigade commanders Fang Yao and Zhuo Xing amounted to only eight thousand.
可是由两员副将方耀、卓兴所率领的粤军，不过八千之众；

Kang Guoqi, intendant of the Yanping-Jianning-Shaowu circuit in Fujian, was a Guangdong man and commanded a newly formed force largely recruited there, but it could not be counted as a Guangdong army: he served under Zuo and took no orders from Guangdong’s senior officials.
福建延建邵道康国器，虽是广东人，新统一军，亦多粤籍，却不能算粤军，因为是左宗棠的部下，并不听命于广东大吏。

Mao and Guo had little choice.
毛鸿宾与郭嵩焘迫不得已，

They sent Fang and Zhuo into Fujian to join the campaign, openly blocking the rebels and covertly checking Zuo.
一而派方耀、卓兴入闽会剿，明阻长毛，暗挡左宗棠；

Meanwhile, hoping to strengthen their own position, they planned to ask the court to appoint Feng Zicai, the celebrated defender of Zhenjiang, to direct military affairs along the East River.
一面打算奏请起用守镇江的名将冯子材督办东江军务，自求振作。


Before the relieving armies arrived, Lin Wencha, Fujian’s land-force military commander, had already fought Li Shixian.
当援闽之师未到以前，福建陆路提督林文察已与李世贤接过仗。

A native of Changhua in Taiwan, Lin had entered military office as a youji commander in the eighth year of Xianfeng, after contributing funds and helping suppress bandits in Tamsui.
林文察是台湾彰化人，咸丰八年以助饷剿淡水的土匪，授职游击，做了武官；

His Taiwanese volunteers were fierce, adept with firearms, and effective in battle.
他所统率的台勇擅用火器，剽悍善战，

Their success helped him rise to brigade commander and gain the title Baturu.
助林文察当到总兵，获得『巴图鲁』的名号。

While Wang Youling was besieged in Hangzhou, Lin had been ordered to assist Zhejiang but was held up at Hengzhou.
王有龄被困杭州时，曾奉命援浙，而阻于衡州；

Later placed under Zuo’s command, he earned further distinction and received substantive appointment as commander of the Funing Brigade in Fujian.
以后归左宗棠节制，很立了些战功，补实为福建福宁镇总兵，

Soon promoted to the province’s land-force military command, he returned to Taiwan with troops to suppress disorder in his native district.
不久擢升为福建陆路提督，随即提兵回台，在他家乡平乱。

The rebel leader was Dai Chaochun, whose ancestral home was Longxi in Zhangzhou.
乱党的首领，是原籍漳州龙溪的戴潮春；

He belonged to the surviving adherents of the White Lotus sect, that old source of rebellion which never seemed to disappear from Chinese history.
他是中国历史上阴魂不散的老牌乱党白莲教的余孽。

Nominally he organized militia in Changhua; in reality he was in contact with the Taipings.
在彰化名义上办团练，实际上与长毛是勾通的。

At the transition from the Xianfeng to the Tongzhi reign, Zhejiang fell and many government troops in Fujian were transferred to northern Fujian and southern Zhejiang.
咸同之交，浙江沦陷，在福建的官军，多调闽北浙南；

Dai judged the moment favourable.
戴潮春认为是起事的好机会，

In the third month his follower Lin Gangsheng rose at Dadun.
三月间由其党羽林戆晟在大墩起事，

Five days later Changhua was occupied and Kong Zhaoci, Taiwan’s military intendant, was killed.
五天以后，占领彰化，台湾兵备道孔昭慈被杀。

Dai called himself the Eastern King and Lin the Southern King; there were also Western and Northern Kings, with a Grand Preceptor, chief ministers of the left and right, and ministers of the six boards beneath them.
戴潮春自称『东王』∶『南王』是林戆晟；此外还有『西王』与『北王』。下面的官职有『大国师』、『左右丞相』、『六部尚书』等等。

This rough imitation of the Taiping administration, a collection of pretenders playing ministers and generals as though on a stage, survived for a time because the southeastern war had reached a critical point. In response to Grain Intendant Ding Rijian’s urgent requests, the court had sent only six hundred men against it.
这个略仿太平天国建制，沐猴而冠，仿佛戏台出将入相的场面，由于东南战局正在紧要关头，朝廷只应粮道丁日健的力请，派了六百人去攻剿；因而得以维持一时。

Not until the autumn of the second year of Tongzhi, when Zuo was confident of recovering Zhejiang, did he send Lin back to Taiwan to rally his former troops.
及至同治二年秋天，左宗棠收复浙江，已有把握，才派林文察回台，号召旧部；

Fujian’s governor, Xu Zonggan, also sent Ding, who had served in Taiwan for many years, with reinforcements.
福建巡抚徐宗干，亦派久官台湾的丁日健领兵赴援，

Ding was appointed Taiwan’s military intendant and given charge of military affairs throughout the island.
并授为台湾兵备道，督办全台军务。


Early in the eleventh month, Changhua was recovered, followed by Douliu.
于是到了十一月初，彰化收复，继攻下斗六；

By the end of the year Dai had been captured at Zhangcuozhuang and Lin Gangsheng defeated and killed at Sikuai Cuo.
到了年底，戴潮春被擒于张厝庄、林戆晟败死于四块厝，

The situation could be considered stable.
局面可以算是稳定下来了。

Clearing out the remaining rebels was nevertheless arduous.
不过肃清残余乱党，亦很费力；

In particular, after Li Shixian occupied Zhangzhou, Dai’s surviving followers prepared to join forces with him and rise again.
尤其是当李世贤占据漳州以后，戴潮春的余党准备接应会合，图谋再举。

This was precisely why Zuo feared that Li and Wang Haiyang might put to sea.
左宗棠深恐李世贤、汪海洋等人出海，正就是为此。

Lin Wencha faced a dilemma.
林文察见此光景，深感为难，

On one hand, he could not relax his efforts to prevent the embers of rebellion from flaring up.
一方面要防止死灰复燃，放不得手；

On the other, as Fujian’s highest military officer on land, he had an inescapable responsibility for recovering Zhangzhou, Tingzhou, and the other occupied districts.
另一方面以福建陆路提督为一省最高武官的地位，对于收复漳州、汀州等地，责无旁贷。

After careful thought, he decided that he must return to Fujian.
仔细考虑下来，还是应该回福建；

If Li could be destroyed, the rebels in Changhua would lose both support and hope and collapse without a fight.
因为能够消灭李世贤，彰化的乱党便失去凭借与指望，不战而自溃。

He crossed hurriedly, taking only two hundred personal guards in his ship.
打定主意，仓卒内渡，同船只带了两百亲兵。

Having fought Li more than once, he did not underestimate him.
他与李世贤交过手不止一次，不敢轻敌；

His original intention was to make arrangements after reaching Fujian and only then advance.
原意到了福建，先作部署，然后出击，

But Li was already prepared, with an ambush at Wansong Pass awaiting his arrival.
那知李世贤早有准备，在万松关设下埋伏，专等他入网。


Lin then changed his plan.
而林文察则又改变了主意。

Feeling his force too small, he gathered many stragglers along the road into an improvised army.
因为他自感兵力孤单，一路收容了许多散兵游勇，杂凑成军；

Given ample pay and provisions, sufficient time, and capable assistants, such men could gradually be trained into a strong force.
如果粮饷充裕，时间从容，而又有得力的帮手，当然可以将此辈渐渐练成劲旅，

Without those advantages, the only course was to exploit their urgent desire for a livelihood or their thirst for revenge, sharpen their fighting spirit, and stake everything on one desperate effort.
否则就只有利用他们急于追求出路，或者怀仇报仇的心理，淬厉士气，作背城借一之计。

An experienced commander, Lin studied the circumstances and concluded that he must seek a rapid decision.
林文察老于兵事，默察情势，认为不得不速战速决；

Delay would merely consume supplies while discipline became impossible, and the army would collapse without fighting.
拖下去徒耗粮饷，且难部勒，将不战自溃。

Zuo’s original order had been to hold Quanzhou and Xiamen firmly.
本来左宗棠的檄令，是责成他『力保泉厦』，

That was a difficult task.
这是很难的任务，

The country from Zhangzhou eastwards to the two coastal cities was flat, easy to attack and hard to defend, while the opposing forces were grossly unequal in strength.
因为漳州以东，直到厦门、泉州，地势平衍，易攻难守，而况彼此兵力众寡悬殊。

Strategically, attack should serve as defence.
就方略讲，应该以攻为守；

In weighing the consequences, although attacking a larger force was arduous, defeat while attacking was preferable to defeat while merely holding a position.
就利害关系来看，以少攻多，虽然吃力，但与其守而败，不如攻而败。

Early in the tenth month, therefore, Lin advanced from Quanzhou and Xiamen and camped at Wansong Pass.
因此，在十月初便由泉厦而进，在万松关上扎营。

Also called Wansong Ridge, it stood on Phoenix Mountain, twenty-five li east of Zhangzhou, commanding the route into the city after crossing the river from the coastal towns.
万松关又名万松岭，在漳州以东二十五里的凤凰山上，为由泉厦渡江入漳的孔道。

His camp was scarcely established when Li sent a force to test its strength.
扎营刚定，李世贤派一队人马来攻，用意在试探虚实；

Deputy Brigade Commander Huishou proved incapable, and the rebels actually overran the camp.
哪知副将惠寿不中用，竟让长毛踩了营盘。

Lin had to withdraw to a place called Yuzhou.
林文察迫不得已，退驻叫做玉洲的地方，

Two days later, he attacked and returned with a minor victory.
隔了两天出队攻击，小胜而回。


At this point another order arrived from Zuo: dig deep trenches and raise high ramparts; avoid rash fighting in pursuit of victory; wait for the Zhejiang troops, then cooperate in recovering Zhangzhou.
就在这时候又接到左宗棠的札子，指示他『深沟高垒，勿浪战求胜；俟浙军到后，协力规复漳州。』

Lin now had to obey.
林文察这时不能不听命，

He encamped on Wansong Ridge and waited.
驻营在万松岭上，静候援军；

Naval Brigade Commander Zeng Yuming established a river camp at Haikou near Haicheng County on the Jiulong River, so that the two positions could support one another.
另由水师总兵曾玉明，在九龙江近海澄县地方的海口镇，结扎水营，以为犄角之势。

They held on until the end of the tenth month.
这样守到十月底，

Zuo had not yet entered Fujian, but the Zhejiang forces sent ahead were nearing positions from which the three columns could close in.
左宗棠还未进入福建境内，而先行出发的浙军，三路合围之势，将次形成。

Li had originally laid his ambush west of Wansong Pass, expecting Lin to enter it.
李世贤原来是在万松关以西设下埋伏，专候林文察入网；

Seeing him remain inactive while the Zhejiang troops approached, Li urgently needed to open a way out.
见他按兵不动，而浙军又已入闽，不能不急着打开一条出路，

On the third day of the eleventh month, he launched a surprise attack by land and water.
因而在十一月初三，发动突袭。突袭是分水陆两路进行。

The rebels striking the river camp blackened their faces with soot, both to appear terrifying and to recognize one another.
袭击水营的长毛，皆以烟煤擦脸，有意扮成狰狞可怖的鬼相；同时亦用作为『自己人』的识别。

Zeng’s naval force was caught unprepared.
曾玉明的水师，猝不及防，

Apart from resistance with the small guns aboard the gunboats, the camps collapsed.
除了用炮艇上的小炮轰击以处，其余各营，都垮了下来。

To the west, Lin’s force at Wansong Pass consisted of stragglers whose condition had steadily deteriorated with delay.
在西面万松关上的林文察所部，本是越拖越坏的散兵游勇；

Hearing that their rear had been attacked, they fell into disorder before fighting began.
听说后路被袭，未战先乱。

Huishou and Youji Commander Xu Zhongbiao could not hold the ranks and escaped as best they could.
副将惠寿，游击许忠标，压不住阵，只有溜之大吉；

Lin refused to flee and was shot dead.
林文察都不肯逃，结果中枪阵亡。

The routed troops were barely collected on the east bank of the Jiulong River, forming what protection they could for the approach to Quanzhou.
溃散下来的乱兵，勉强集结在九龙江东岸，算是保障泉州门户。


Three months later, Zuo reached Pucheng and formally entered Fujian, intending to establish his travelling headquarters there.
三月以后，左宗棠到了浦城，正式进入福建境界；预定就以此为行辕。

The first battle report received at those headquarters announced Lin’s defeat and death in action.
行辕所收到的第一件战报，便是林文察兵败殉职。

This was no omen of immediate success, and Zuo was greatly displeased.
这不是马到成功的征兆，左宗棠大为不悦。

In his view, Lin had blunted the Zhejiang army’s spirit and damaged his own prestige.
在他看林文察是挫了浙军的锐气，也伤了他的威名；

Although the man had not deserved death, his failure was unforgivable.
虽非死有余辜，却是决不可原谅的。

Zuo therefore would not devote a separate memorial to it.
因而出奏时，便不肯专叙此事，

Instead, under the heading “Arrival at Pucheng to Direct the Campaign, and Current Arrangements for Suppression,” he reproached Lin for disregarding orders and thereby suffering defeat.
只用一个『督师行抵浦城，现筹剿办情形』的案由，在折子中斥责林文察不听调度，致有此失；

Fortunately, Gao Liansheng’s troops had reached southern Fujian from Fuzhou, so Quanzhou and Xiamen could be secured.
幸亏高连升军一已由福州赶到闽南，泉厦可保无虞。

As for Lin’s posthumous honours, a separate request would be submitted; it was easy to infer that the provision would not be generous.
至于林文察的恤典，申明另案奏请；但可想而知的，恤典不会优厚。

The situation soon stabilized.
不过局势很快地稳住了。

Zuo’s greatest concern was that Li might break southeastwards to the sea.
左宗棠最担心的，就是李世贤向东南横窜入海，

If Gao’s force could move south from Fuzhou in time to block him and hold him briefly, the arrival by sea of the Jiangsu armies under Guo Songlin and Yang Dingxun would make the clearing of the interior virtually certain.
所以只要高连升一军，能自福州南下，及时拦堵，先挡得一阵；等苏军郭松林、杨鼎勋领兵航海而来，肃清腹地便有十足的把握了。


Zuo accordingly adopted a strategy of holding in the east, attacking from the north, and applying pressure from the west.
为此，左宗棠定下东守北攻西压的策略，

First he recovered Longyan, in the northern part of southern Fujian.
最先收复闽南偏北的龙岩；

Then, in cooperation with Fang Yao’s Guangdong troops, he recovered Yongding on the provincial border.
接着会同粤军方耀所部，收复闽粤交界的永定。

These two victories greatly raised morale and gave him more freedom of command.
这两场胜仗才下来，士气大振，指挥更加灵活；

He began to drive the rebels into Guangdong, first pushing Wang Haiyang’s men south from Tingzhou and Liancheng, near Jiangxi, towards Wuping and Shanghang on the Guangdong border.
左宗棠开始『驱贼入粤』，首先是由毗连江西的汀洲、连城一带、将汪海洋部下的长毛，往南撵向与广东交界的武平、上杭一带。

The Jiangsu reinforcements were arriving in succession.
其时援闽苏军已陆续到达，

They divided the defensive sectors with Gao’s and Huang Shaochun’s Zhejiang forces, taking Zhangzhou as their common objective.
与浙军高连升、黄少春所部，划分防区，而以进取漳州为目标，

The Jiangsu armies held the south of the city, the Zhejiang armies the north, completely cutting off Li’s route to the sea.
苏军守漳州之南浙军守漳州之北。这一来，李世贤出海之路是彻底被遮断了。

In the middle of the fourth month they attacked simultaneously from north and south.
到了四月中旬，浙苏各军由南北同时出击，会功漳州；

On the twenty-first, Zhangzhou was recovered.
到了四月廿一，漳州克复，

Li, however, opened the western gate and escaped to join Wang, leaving the two like cornered beasts.
可是李世贤却开西门而走，与汪海洋会合在一起，成为『困兽』了。

The strongest forces now lay along the southeastern coast around Quanzhou, Xiamen, and Zhangzhou.
当时的形势是东南方面泉、厦、漳沿海一带，兵力最厚；

More than seven thousand guarded Yongding to the northwest; a gap in the northeast had also been closed in time.
西北永定有七千余人防守；东北的漏洞，亦已及时防补，

Only the west remained weak, with almost no dispositions made by Zuo.
唯有西面最弱，左宗棠几乎毫无布置。

To the west lay Dabu and Raoping in Guangdong.
西面就是广东的大埔、饶平一带，

Fang Yao’s troops defended them, but anyone could see they were no match for Li and Wang.
虽有粤军方耀防守，可是决非李世贤、汪海洋的对手，是谁都看得出来的。

What, then, did Zuo intend?
然则，左宗棠之意何居？

A discerning observer could draw the conclusion.
明眼人自然看得出来。


That observer was Xu Gengshen, chief secretary of the Grand Council, far away in the capital.
这个明眼人是远在京城里的军机章 京领班许庚身，

On the twelfth day of the fifth month, a memorial passed down to him caused considerable surprise.
在五月十二那天，看到发下来的一个奏折，大为诧异；

Submitted by Li Hongzhang to record his troops’ achievements, it was headed, “Jiangsu Reinforcements in Fujian Join the Zhejiang Forces in Converging Attacks and Recover the Prefectural City of Zhangzhou on the Twenty-first Day of the Fourth Month.”
这个奏折是李鸿章 所上，作用是在表功，所以案由是『援闽苏军，会合浙军分路进逼，于四月二十一日克复漳州府城』；

In describing the attack, it stated that “the rebel Shi King, Li Shixian, secretly opened the western gate and fled.”
奏报进攻情形中，有一句话说∶『侍逆李世贤潜开西门而遁。』

This did not agree with Zuo’s report received at the same time.
这与同时收到的左宗棠的战报，情况不符。

Zuo’s heading was “Great Victory in the Advance West of Zhangzhou, and Current Arrangements.”
左宗棠的奏折，案由是『进逼漳西大捷，现筹办理情形』。

It mentioned neither the recovery of the city nor Li’s escape through its western gate.
并未提到漳州克复，再未变到李世贤由漳州西门而遁；

It said only that Li, repeatedly defeated and increasingly desperate, had tried to take a minor route north of Zhangzhou around to Anxi and strike the government troops’ rear.
只说『李逆世贤经官军叠次击败，势日穷蹙；图由漳北小路绕犯安溪，以抄官军后路。

Failing in that, he had attempted to stir up internal disorder in collusion with bandits in Tong’an.
其计未成，又图勾结同安土匪，内讧滋事；

Guo Songlin had then hurried to Tong’an with two battalions and, together with Intendant Zeng Xiande, cleared the bandit strongholds at Xitang, Shangzhai, and Hujing.
经离松林凰带所部两营驰赴同安，会同道员曾宪德将西塘、上宅、浒井各乡匪巢洗荡。』

The memorial was dated the twenty-sixth day of the fourth month and submitted from the provincial capital.
再看拜折的日期是四月廿六，拜折的地点是福建省城。

Fuzhou was only two or three days from Zhangzhou.
福州离漳州不过两三日路程；

If the city had been recovered on the twenty-first, Zuo could hardly have remained ignorant on the twenty-fifth.
廿一克复漳州，在福州的左宗棠不应该到廿五还不知道。

If he knew, why did he not announce the victory in his memorial on the twenty-sixth?
如果已经知道，廿六拜折何以不报捷？

This was a major puzzle, but a little consideration made it plain.
这是莫大的一个疑窦，但稍作参详，不难明白，

Zuo withheld the victory report because Li had escaped.
左宗棠只为李世贤『漏网』，不肯报捷；

By first describing an intended movement towards Anxi, then collusion with Tong’an’s bandits, and finally Guo’s actions, he meant quietly to shift responsibility for Li’s escape onto Guo Songlin.
先说他想『绕犯安溪』，又想『勾结同安土匪』，最后说由郭松林如何如何，是打算将李世贤『漏网』的责任，轻轻推到郭松林头上。


The reason for Zuo’s wish to deal with Guo could also be inferred.
至于左宗棠想『整』郭松林的缘故，亦可以推想得到。

After Lin Wencha’s death, Guo, commander of the Fushan Brigade, had been appointed acting land-force military commander of Fujian.
原来从林文察阵亡以后，福建陆路提督一缺便补了福山镇总兵的郭松林，

Acting or not, it was a promotion.
虽为署任，总是升官；

Had Zuo not requested the Jiangsu reinforcements, Guo might never have obtained this highest provincial military office.
而如没有左宗棠的奏请苏军援闽，这个武将中最高职衔的提督，请未见得轮得到郭松林。

Zuo reasoned that, since the promotion arose from service in Fujian and the post itself belonged to Fujian, both gratitude and the proper relationship between title and duty required Guo to enter his command.
照左宗棠的想法，郭松林的升官，既由援闽而来；而所升的官，又是福建的缺分，则不论感恩图报，还是循名责实，都该照建制归隶他的部下。

Yet despite repeated urgings from Governor Xu Zonggan, Guo refused to assume the appointment.
无如郭松林虽经福建巡抚徐宗干一再催促，始终不肯到任。

He fought in Fujian while holding a Fujian military office, but continued to regard himself as a visiting commander.
以福建的武官在福建打仗，却自居于客将的地位，

Zuo found that hard to tolerate.
在左宗棠是颇难容忍的；

Before Guo and Yang sailed south, however, Li had expressly stated that Guo would not take up the post and that he would not urge him to do so.
只是当郭杨两军航海南来之前，李鸿章 特为声明∶郭松林不履任，他亦『不劝驾』。

Zuo had agreed and could not now conveniently go back on his word.
左宗棠曾经同意，此时不便出尔反尔！

His resentment remained.
但又有所憾于郭松林，

He therefore planted a suggestion for future use: on one hand, his vague phrasing implied that pursuing Li Shixian was Guo’s responsibility; on the other, he could wait to see what happened.
因而此时先作一个伏笔，一方面隐约其词地表示，追击李世贤是郭松林的责任；另一方面可以看将来的情况，

If Tong’an’s bandits proved difficult to suppress, he could formally request that Guo remain in Fujian.
果真同安土匪一时不易收拾，便可正式奏请将郭松林留在福建——

A province’s own military commander suppressing its own bandits was an entirely proper arrangement.
以本省的提督剿本省的土匪，天经地义，名正言顺，

The court could scarcely refuse, Li would have to release him, and Guo would have to stay.
朝廷不能不准，李鸿章 不能不放，郭松林不能不留。


Once Zuo’s concealed manoeuvring was understood, Li’s memorial on the relief forces’ victory and the joint recovery of Zhangzhou became clear.
了然于左宗棠暗中的勾心斗角，再来看李鸿章 的『援闽获胜，会克漳州府』一折，才会恍然大悟，

Besides claiming distinction and rewards, it left the Jiangsu armies a means of disclaiming responsibility in advance.
除表功邀赏以外，还有预先为苏军留下卸责余地的作用。

Its account of the campaign was particularly precise about the sectors and dispositions of Guo’s and Yang’s forces.
因为折中铺叙战况，对于郭杨两军的防区及部署，说得特别详细，

First, it stated that Dongshan, ten li south of Zhangzhou on the main road to Zhangpu, was held by Guo with eight battalions.
一则谓∶『东山在漳州城南十里，系通漳浦大路，郭松林以八营扼之；

Another ten li farther lay Zhenmen, centrally placed between Dongshan, Haicheng, and Shima, where Yang held the ground with five battalions.
又十里为镇门，系东山、海澄、石码适中之地，杨鼎勋以五营扼之。

Haicheng County lay behind both armies, with mountain paths leading to Zhangpu; three more battalions had therefore been deployed inside and outside the county town to prevent a surprise attack on their rear.
海澄县为两军后路，有山径可通漳浦，复派三营分布县城内外，防贼抄袭。』

Second, it stated that Brigade Commander Liu Lianjie and Officer Wang Kaibang were in the northwest, while Military Commander Gao Liansheng, Huang Shaochun, and their forces were on the eastern route.
再则谓∶『总兵刘连捷、阜司王开榜在西北；提督高连升、黄少春等军在东路。

Once the Jiangsu troops occupied Dongshan, the southern route was cut.
自苏军扼扎东山，南路已断。』

Third, it recorded that the defeated rebels fled towards Nanjing County; the various battalions pursued for several li, then joined Gao, Huang, and the others in turning back southeast to clear all the rebel positions at Fangzi Bridge beyond the eastern gate, the Dongyue Temple, and the New Bridge near the southern gate.
三则谓∶『败逆向南靖一路纷逃，各营追剿数里，当会同高、黄等军，折回东南，将东关外放子桥、东岳庙及附近南门新桥各贼垒一律荡平。』

Every detail showed that, whether defending or attacking, Guo and Yang were chiefly responsible for Zhangzhou’s southern side, with its eastern side secondary.
处处可以看出，郭扬两军无论防守还是攻剿，都以担当漳州南面为主，东面其次；

Who, then, was responsible for Li’s escape through the western gate?
然则李世贤开西门而遁，责任谁属？

The answer was obvious.
不问可知。


After repeated examination, Xu concluded that Zuo was practising a dangerous form of political manipulation.
这样反复研判下来，许庚身认为左宗棠是在玩弄可怕的权术。

Since the war began, provincial commanders had habitually tried to drive rebels beyond their own borders.
从军兴以来，各省带兵大员，以驱贼出境为惯技；

Zuo, however, seemed deliberately to be making a neighbour bear the calamity, with some further malicious purpose concealed behind it.
而左宗棠则似乎有意以邻为壑，包藏着什么祸心。

The matter must be corrected early.
此非早作纠正不可。

Xu explained the consequences to Prince Gong, Wenxiang, and the others.
因此，他向恭王与文祥等人，指陈利害，

With the two empress dowagers informed, a court dispatch was drafted.
奏明两宫太后，拟发『廷寄』，

It first noted Li’s report that Zhangzhou had been recovered and the rebel Shi King had secretly escaped through its western gate.
首先指出李鸿章 已有奏报，漳州克复，『侍逆潜开西门而遁』；

It continued that, although the city was recovered, the chief rebel remained alive and would certainly join Wang Haiyang in further plans.
接下来便说，『漳州别经克复，而渠魁仍未授首，必将与汪逆合谋，计图复逞。

The southeastern routes were stable and Liu Mingdeng and others held the northeast in coordination, but Zhangpu, Yunxiao, Zhao’an, and Pinghe in the west remained in rebel hands.
现在东南两路局势既尚稳固；东北一路亦有刘明灯等联络扼守，而西面之漳浦、云霄、诏安、平和等城，均为贼踞，

There could be no doubt that the fugitives would try to escape that way.
该逆必思由此路窜走，已无疑义。

Fang Yao’s forces guarded Raoping and Dabu in Guangdong, but might be too few to cover the area.
粤省饶平、大埔一带，虽有方耀等军防守，尚恐兵力不敷分布，

Zuo and his colleagues were therefore to detach strong forces, make a circuit to the western route, join the Guangdong troops, and intercept the rebels, preventing them from crossing the border.
左宗棠等仍当分拨劲旅，绕赴西路，会同粤军，迎头拦截，杜其窜越之路。』

At this point, Zuo knew the moment had come to drive Guo Songtao from office.
到此地步，左宗棠知道撵走郭嵩焘的时机成熟了。

He had already laid groundwork for Jiang Yili, using Li Hongzhang as a favourable comparison.
在此以前，他曾为蒋益澧下过一次伏笔；并用李鸿章 作为陪衬，来提高蒋益澧的地位。

Early in the ninth month, when Ruilin and Guo fell out, Zuo had added a remark to his memorial requesting withdrawal of the order placing the armies of three provinces under his control: fighting in Liangguang might continue indefinitely, but if it had commanders such as Li Hongzhang and Jiang Yili, perhaps the disorder could be brought to an end.
这一伏笔，下在九月初，瑞麟与郭嵩焘交恶之时，而于『恳请收回节制三省各军成命』的奏折中，附带一提∶『恐两广兵事，尚无已时，若得治军之才如李鸿章 、蒋益澧其人，祸乱庶有豸乎！』

The implication was that Li should become governor-general there and Jiang governor of Guangdong.
意思是最好将李鸿章 调为粤督，而以蒋益澧升任粤抚；

This was a tentative hint, and the court had given no explicit response.
这是隐约其词的试探，朝廷即令没有明确的反应，

Yet the impression that Jiang could govern a province had been planted in the minds of the Western Empress Dowager and the grand councillors.
但蒋益澧可当方面之任的印象，却已在西宫太后与军机大臣的脑中留下了。


Zuo still could not openly recommend Jiang for promotion and transfer to Guangdong.
此时当然还不能明保蒋益澧升调广东；

Instead, he attacked Guo and supported Jiang in an attached memorandum on Guangdong’s military affairs and finances.
是用夹片的方式，在『陈明广东兵事饷事』中，攻郭保蒋。

It began by declaring that the province’s military condition would not bear examination, and its finances scarcely bore inquiry.
首先就说∶『广东一省兵事实足观，而饷事亦不可问。

The war had lasted a long time, it said; in some provinces military strength had grown from weakness, whereas Guangdong, once fierce, was now feeble.
军兴既久，各省兵事或由弱转强，粤则昔悍而今弩矣！

In some provinces funds had gradually recovered from scarcity, whereas Guangdong, once rich, was now exhausted.
各省饷事或由匮而渐裕，粤则昔饶而今竭矣！』

Those two sentences alone attacked the governors and governors-general of the preceding two years.
光是这两句话，便将近两年的督抚一起攻击在内；

Naturally, Guo bore more responsibility than Ruilin, since he had been in office longer.
当然，郭嵩焘的责任应更重于瑞麟，因为他在任之日比瑞麟久。
