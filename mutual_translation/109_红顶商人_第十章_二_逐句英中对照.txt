胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第十章（本章第2部分，共3部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


The memorandum then concentrated upon military finance, which was precisely the governor’s responsibility.
接着便专责饷事，而此正是巡抚的职责；

Although Guo Songtao was never named, most of the attack fell upon him, with particular emphasis on the likin collected in prosperous Chaozhou.
其中并无一语提及郭嵩焘的名字，而大部分的攻击却集中在郭嵩焘身上，特别提到广东富饶之区的潮州厘税。

Zuo declared that, on reaching Dabu, he had met officials, gentry, and ordinary people from Chaozhou and inquired into its taxes.
左宗棠是这样指责∶『臣抵大埔，接晤潮郡官绅士民，询及潮郡厘税，

The levies on miscellaneous merchandise and imported opium, Shantou’s trade levy, and the shipping contribution together yielded only a little over thirty thousand taels annually—not enough to pay six thousand men for a single month.
合计杂货之厘、洋药之厘、汕头行厘、船捐，每年所得，共止三万余两，是一年所入，不足六千人一月之饷也。

If so rich a prefecture in eastern Guangdong produced so little, the condition elsewhere could be inferred.
潮州为粤东腴郡，而厘税之少如此，外此已可类推。』

This deliberately distorted the facts.
这是有意歪曲事实。

Ever since Qian Jiang had introduced likin, a tax collected on goods, Guangdong had been the most difficult place in which to impose it.
从钱江创设就货征税的厘金以来，最难办的就是广东；

On first taking office, Guo had submitted a joint memorial with Governor-General Mao Hongbin explaining the circumstances.
当郭嵩焘莅任之初，就曾会同总督毛鸿宾奏明。


Guangdong differed from other provinces chiefly because of foreign interference.
广东办厘的情形，有异于他省，主要的原因是洋人的牵掣。

Macau lay to its west and Hong Kong to its east.
广东的形势，『澳门据其西，香港绕其东，

The strategic outlets of the provincial waterways were in foreign hands, and Hong Kong in particular had become a gathering place for merchant firms.
所有省河扼要海口，其地全属之洋人，而香港尤为行户屯聚之地。

One or two great establishments had moved there to place themselves under foreign protection and further their private interests.
一二大行店皆移设香港，以图倚附夷人，便其私计，

The provincial authorities dared not approach them about contributions or likin.
一切劝捐抽厘，从不敢一过问。

Others wishing to evade payment likewise lodged their goods in Hong Kong.
其有意规避捐输者，亦多寄顿香港，希图幸免。

The major trades all used it as a point of transfer.
统计出入各货，凡大宗经纪，皆由香港转输。

Other provinces merely had to prevent evasion; Guangdong also had a refuge to which evaders could withdraw.
是他省但防偷漏之途，而粤东兼有逋逃之薮。』

Local customs posed another difference.
其次是广东的风气与他省不同。

Guangdong’s gentry often undertook the collection of taxes and contributions.
广东的士绅，往往包揽税捐；

When likin began, unscrupulous gentry contracted to collect it and enriched themselves by extortion, harming both the public revenue and private interests.
厘金开办之初，亦由劣绅承包，任令侵渔中饱。而公私交受其病。

When collection was subsequently returned to officials, the former contractors resented the loss of their privilege and incited disorder.
其后收为官办，则原来包厘的劣绅，因为失去特权，心有不甘，从中煽动捣乱，

Mobs destroying likin offices were nothing unusual.
聚众捣毁厘局之事，不足为奇；

Timid officials dared not punish the instigators.
官府胆怯怕事，不敢惩办祸首，

Instead they dismissed the collecting officers or transferred prefects and magistrates in hopes of compromise.
反而撤去委员，或调动府县地方官，以裘发协。

The more they yielded, the harder the problem became.
而结果是越迁就，越棘手。


After taking office, Guo reorganized likin on the principles of eliminating private skimming and making the system reasonable.
从郭嵩焘到任后，以易除中饮，讲求合情合理的宗旨整顿厘捐，

He achieved considerable results.
颇有成效，

Offices were established successively in places where none had existed, including Qiongzhou, Lianzhou, and Heyuan in Huizhou.
从未设局的琼州府、廉州以及惠州的河源等地，次第开办。

Chaozhou, however, lay in a remote eastern corner of the province and had long remained beyond effective control.
至于潮州，就广东而言，偏处东隅，久成化外，

Only when Wang Haiyang approached the Guangdong border did Zhang Xian, intendant of the Chaozhou-Jiaying-Huizhou circuit, find a way to begin collection.
直到汪海洋逼近广东边境时，方由潮嘉惠道张铣，设法开办；

The amount was small, but it was a beginning.
数目虽少，但总是一个开端。

Chaozhou’s people would discuss anything if approached tactfully and in accordance with local circumstances; coercion merely made them refuse.
潮州的民风，因势利导，好话说在前面，无事不可商量；强制硬压，则偏不服从。

Zhang believed that, once they accepted likin in principle, the amount could gradually increase.
张铣的意思是，只要潮州肯承认厘捐，以后可以陆续增加；

Moreover, with the rebels pressing near, a quarrel between the authorities and local society over taxation would be extremely dangerous.
而况贼势方急，官府与绅民之间，为此先起争执，是件极危险的事。

Guo fully agreed.
这个看法，郭嵩焘深以为然；

Zuo, deliberately disregarding these facts, stressed the mere thirty thousand taels collected each year without mentioning how hard won that sum was, or how collecting thirty thousand now offered hope of three hundred thousand later.
但左宗棠有意抹煞事实，只强调每年只收得三万银子，却不说这三万银子来之不易，而只要能收此三万，以后三十万亦有希望。

Most maliciously, he also exaggerated Guangdong Customs revenue. He said he had heard that its various ports collected no less than two million taels annually, while the amount remitted to the capital could not be ascertained.
最恶毒的是，左宗棠又夸大广东海关的收入∶『闻海关各口所收，每岁不下二百万两，其解京之数，无从稽考。

If the governor-general and governor could arrange to use it, the regular imperial remittances need not suffer and provincial military finance would benefit greatly.
此项若能由督抚设法筹办，于正供固期无误；而于该省筹饷大局，实裨益非浅。

But this involved an arrangement two hundred years old, he added, which a provincial official dared not lightly question.
特此为二百年旧制，非外臣所敢轻议。』

He then recommended Jiang.
接下来便是保蒋益澧了。

Having entered Guangdong with a visiting army, he could not refrain from reporting what he had observed.
他说∶『臣率客军入粤，偶有闻见，自不敢不据实直陈。

Coordinating troops and funds was a great responsibility requiring an administrator of exceptional ability.
至兵饷兼筹，任大责重，非明于开济之才，不能胜任。

Jiang Yili, Zhejiang’s provincial treasurer, possessed unrivalled talent and judgment several degrees above his own.
浙江市政使蒋益澧，才气无双，识略高臣数等，

If the emperor ordered Jiang to Guangdong to direct military affairs and raise funds, the current situation would surely improve.
若蒙天恩，调令赴粤督办军务，兼筹军饷，于粤东目前时局，必有所济。』

This was a forceful recommendation indeed.
这就是所谓力保。

Its force lay in the claim of unrivalled talent and judgment superior to Zuo’s.
力保之『力』，端在一句话上∶『才气无双，识略高臣数等。』

Praise of that kind from a governor-general commanding military operations in three provinces carried extraordinary weight.
以节制三省军务的总督，如此推崇，分量实在太重了。


Zuo regarded himself as another Zhuge Liang and habitually looked down upon everyone.
左宗棠以诸葛武侯自命，目空一切，

Such humble admiration was quite unlike him.
竟这样降心推崇，也实在不类他的为人。

A rumour therefore circulated: the secretary responsible for memorials in the Fujian-Zhejiang governor-general’s office had received ten thousand taels from Jiang and insisted upon adding the ten Chinese characters asserting Jiang’s unrivalled talent and superior judgment.
因此有人传了来一个内幕，说是闽浙总督衙门主章 奏的幕友，受了蒋益澧一万银子的红包，力主加这『才气无双，识略高臣数等』十个字；

If true, the payment amounted to a thousand taels per character.
如果流言属实，算起来是一字千金。

Whether there had been a bribe was unknown.
不过，行贿之说，虽不可知；

On the merits of the situation, however, those ten characters were indispensable.
而就事论事，却非有此十字不可。

The grand councillors knew Jiang’s abilities and generally considered him unfit to govern a province.
蒋益澧的才具如何，军机大臣大都了解；无不以为他难当方面之任。

Although Zuo had hinted that he should replace Guo and had used Li as a favourable comparison to raise his standing, the court had consistently pretended not to hear.
是故虽经左宗棠在奏折中暗示，他可代郭而为粤抚，并利用李鸿章 作陪衬，来抬高他的身价；而朝廷始终装聋作哑。

Now the ten characters thundered too loudly to ignore.
现在左宗棠的这十个字，分量之重，如雷灌耳，

It could no longer pretend deafness, but it could pretend stupidity.
那就装不得聋，作不得哑了。不过，装聋不许，却可装傻，

Deliberately overlooking the implied request, the court granted only what had been asked on the surface.
朝廷有意不理左宗棠的暗示；只如他表面所请，

On the eighth day of the first month of the fifth year of Tongzhi, it ordered Jiang, as provincial treasurer of Zhejiang, to proceed swiftly to Guangdong to handle military affairs and arrange provisions and pay.
在同治五年正月初八降旨∶『着浙江布政使蒋益澧，驰赴广东办理军务，兼筹粮饷。』


By the time Zuo submitted the recommendation, he was confident of recovering Jiaying, where Wang Haiyang had entrenched himself.
当保荐蒋益澧的奏折拜发之时，左宗棠对克复汪海洋所盘踞的嘉应州，已有把握。

A general assault on the twelfth day of the twelfth month brought a decisive victory, and Wang was killed in the gunfire.
在十二月十二发动总攻，一仗大捷，汪海洋为乱枪所杀；

Ten days later the operation was complete.
十天以后，克竟全功。

At the year’s end, Zuo memorialized that Jiaying had been recovered, the rebel leaders exterminated, and the remaining forces swept away.
左宗棠在年底拜折∶『收复嘉应州城，贼首歼灭净尽，余孽荡平。』

This amounted to the final suppression of the Taiping remnants.
这一下等于肃清了长毛余孽，

Zuo would return to his own post, and the armies would be sent back to their respective provinces.
左宗棠本人班师回任，各军遣归本省；

What military affairs, then, was Jiang to handle in Guangdong, and for what troops was he to raise supplies?
然则蒋益澧『驰赴广东』，办何『军务』，筹何『粮饷』？

If someone influential asked that question, his new appointment might be revoked.
如果有力者作此一回，蒋益澧的新命，就可能撤消。

Zuo had of course anticipated it.
左宗棠当然早就计议及此，

He used another matter to press Guo still harder: Gao Liansheng was to take his troops with him when assuming office.
于是借题发挥，对郭嵩焘逼得更紧了。所借的题目是『高连升带所部赴任』。

Gao’s substantive post was land-force military commander of Guangdong.
高连升的本职是『广东陆路提督』；

With Zuo’s command over the three-province campaign at an end, Gao should naturally report to Guangdong.
如今左宗棠节制三省军务的任务告一段落，自回本省，则高连升亦应有广东履任。

A military commander taking up office needed no force beyond his own headquarters guard.
提督到职，除本标亲兵以外，无须另带人马；

Zuo nevertheless instructed him to bring his entire command.
而左宗棠却嘱咐高连升尽携所部赴新任。

The stated reason was to maintain order after the upheaval, while public feeling remained unsettled.
表面上的理由是大乱初平，民心不定，『以资镇压』；

The real object was to burden Guangdong.
实际上是有意给广东出难题，

Gao had five thousand men, requiring at least thirty thousand taels a month, which Guangdong would have to provide.
因为高连升所部有五千人，每月至少亦要三万金银子的饷银，当然归广东负担。


Guangdong welcomed Gao but not his army.
可是，广东欢迎高连升，却不欢迎高连长的部队。

Zuo consequently complained to the throne in forceful terms.
于是左宗棠上奏指责广东，大发牢骚，

He had done everything possible for Guangdong, he said, yet the province made difficulties and wanted him to order Gao’s men back to Fujian.
说是『臣扪心自问，所以为广东谋者，不为不至，而广东顾难之。欲臣一概檄饬高连升所部为旋闽，

He could not understand it.
兹则臣所不解也。

If Guangdong maintained that Fujian should still pay them, then let it consider its arrears: more than three hundred thousand taels of promised assistance to Fujian remained unpaid.
如谓高连升军饷仍应由闽支领，则试为广东筹之，应解协闽之饷，约尚有三十余万两，

Another fifty thousand or more was owed for sending refugees back to their provinces, relief and reduced-price grain sales in Jiaying and Zhenping, and the rice he had allocated to Bao Chao’s army.
此次资遣各省难民及嘉应州、镇平县赈恤平粜米粮及臣均拨鲍超一军军米价银，应由广东解还归款者亦约五万余两。

If all these sums were applied to Gao’s pay, Fujian’s own money would sustain the Fujian force for roughly a year.
即以此款悉数移充高连升军饷，以闽饷济闽军，约足一年之需；

By then disorder would have subsided, and gradual disbandment would still be timely.
一年之后，诸患渐平，陆续裁撤此军，亦未为晚。』

Interprovincial military subsidies formed accounts that could never be finally settled. Who owed what to whom was perpetually disputed.
各省协饷，哪一省亏欠哪一省，是笔永远算不清的帐，

In the end, being able to fight supplied an argument; winning supplied a stronger one.
反正能打仗就有理∶打胜仗更有理。

Zuo understood this perfectly and spoke with assurance, his confidence making his case seem all the more just.
左宗棠对这一层了解得最透彻，所以能够侃侃而言，气壮更显得理直。

His memorials often ended with general reflections.
左宗棠的折报，常在最后发议论，

This one did too.
此折亦不例外，

In attacking Guo, he extended his censure to Guangdong itself.
因为打击郭嵩焘的缘故，殃及广东，

The coastal troubles had originated there, he declared, and so had the rebellion that afflicted the central plains.
亦被恶声∶『伏思海疆之患，起于广东；中原盗贼之患，亦起于广东，

Now that the campaign was over, those responsible for soldiers and funds should learn from the past and strengthen their position.
当此军务甫竣之时，有筹兵筹饷之者，应如何惩前毖后，以图自强？

If they continued to mistake dullness for generosity and evasion for ability, clever in petty calculations but blind to major strategy, they could scarcely relieve the court’s concern for the south.
若仍以庸暗为宽厚；以诿卸为能事，明于小计，暗于大谋，恐未足纾朝廷南顾之忧也。

He requested an order directing Guangdong’s senior officials to deliberate carefully, and an imperial decision permitting Gao to bring his troops.
合无请旨敕下广东督抚熟思审处，仍檄高连升带所部赴任之处，出自圣裁。』

As with the earlier recommendation of Jiang, someone in Zuo’s secretariat who had received a gratuity copied the memorial privately and sent it to Zhejiang.
这个奏折，象以前所保蒋益澧的奏折一样。左宗棠幕府中得了红包的人，密抄折底，寄达浙江，

Jiang was a rough man, but he had perceptive advisers.
蒋益澧虽是粗材，但毕竟也还有高人，

They told him promotion was near.
告诉他说∶高升之期已不在远。

Overjoyed, he had sealing strips printed for the governor of Guangdong and began preparing to take office.
蒋益澧喜不可言，随即刻印了广东巡抚的封条，准备打点上任了。


The most effective feature of the memorial was its use of Ruilin to attack Guo.
这个奏折最厉害之处，是在借瑞麟以攻郭嵩焘。

The matter had begun with an official communication from Ruilin, and Zuo’s aggressive language initially named him.
事由瑞麟一咨而起，左宗棠的咄咄逼人的笔锋，在前面亦都指出瑞麟；

The implication was that, if the attack on Guo failed, it would turn upon Ruilin.
这是暗示，如果攻郭无效，便要转而攻端了。

Everyone at court knew the quality of Ruilin’s administration.
瑞鹿在广东的政绩如何？朝中大臣，尽人皆知；

Prince Gong and Wenxiang, however much more capable than certain Manchu dignitaries who had wielded power in the Daoguang and Xianfeng reigns, still believed that he must be preserved.
而恭王与文祥，较之道光、咸丰两朝若干用事的满州权贵，虽不知高明多少？但亦认为瑞麟必须保全，

First, since the war began, nine-tenths of the governors and governors-general had become Han Chinese, an unprecedented situation for the dynasty.
因为第一，军兴以来，督抚十分之九为汉人，此是清朝开国以来所未有之事。

Only Huguang and Liangguang were presently held by bannermen.
眼前亦仅只湖广、两广是旗人；

If Zuo’s relentless impeachment forced Ruilin’s removal, there was no suitable senior bannerman readily available to replace him.
倘或左宗棠对瑞麟参劾不已，逼得朝廷非调不可，一时却没有适当的旗下大员，可以承乏。

Second, Ruilin had Empress Dowager Cixi’s powerful support and could not be touched.
其次，瑞麟有慈禧太后的奥援，动他不得。

Third, though mediocre, he was obedient.
第三，瑞麟虽是庸材，但很听话；

The Imperial Household Department’s finances were closely connected with Guangdong Customs; an obedient governor-general in Guangzhou made many things convenient.
尤其内务府的经费，跟粤海关有很大的关连，能有个听话的粤督在广州，诸事方便。

The court therefore had to placate Zuo, both to preserve Ruilin and because the warning about continued danger in the south had aroused concern.
因此，朝廷就必须安抚左宗棠，不但为了保全瑞麟，亦因为由『恐未足纾朝廷南顾之忧』这句话而起了警惕。

The resulting edict rebuked Ruilin severely. After Zuo’s victorious withdrawal, it said, Guangdong would still have much to do in settling surrendered soldiers, hunting bandits, and restoring order.
所以上谕中责备瑞麟，措词相当严厉∶『左宗棠凯旋后，粤省安插降卒，搜诛土匪，善后之事方多；

Strong troops should remain to enforce peace.
正当留扎劲兵，以资镇压。

Since Ruilin had urged Gao to assume his substantive command, why now ask Zuo to send the force back to Fujian?
瑞麟既咨催高连升赴广东提督本任，何以反令左宗棠将其部典檄饬回闽？

If renewed disorder after its withdrawal required another campaign, could the acting governor-general bear such grave responsibility?
倘闽军凯撤，而降卒土匪又复滋生事端，重烦兵力，该署督其能当此重咎耶？』


The edict then granted Zuo’s request in full. Gao’s five thousand and more men required only a little over thirty thousand taels a month.
接下来便是悉如左宗棠所请∶『高连升所部五千余人，计每月饷需不过三万余两。

Zuo was to order him to proceed with his troops, and Ruilin and Guo were to provide their pay monthly, without the slightest shortage or arrears that might impede operations.
即着左宗棠檄饬该提督带所部赴任，月饷由瑞麟、郭嵩焘按月筹给，不准丝毫短少蒂欠，致有掣肘之患！』

Ruilin lost face under this rebuke, but his career was preserved.
瑞麟的受这顿申斥，当然很失面子，但前程是保住了；

The man whose career was not preserved was Guo, who had not been rebuked.
保不住前程的是末受申斥的郭嵩焘。

The court meant to sacrifice Guo in exchange for Zuo’s “loyalty” while keeping Ruilin.
朝廷的意思是决意保全瑞麟，牺牲郭嵩焘来换取左宗棠的『忠诚』。

Yet its edicts constantly proclaimed complete impartiality in appointments and administration.
不过上谕于『用人行政』，动辄申明，『一秉大公』，

Both governor and governor-general bore responsibility for delays in Guangdong’s military affairs; removing one and leaving the other untouched would be difficult to justify.
而广东军务的贻误，督抚同罪，不该一个被黜、一个无事。

An unwritten form of “fairness” therefore came into play: rebuke without punishment for one, punishment without rebuke for the other.
所以运用『打而不罚』，『罚而不打』这个不成文的『公平』之理，

Ruilin had received his rebuke; the absence of one for Guo foreshadowed his dismissal.
对瑞麟严加申饬是已打不罚；而对郭嵩焘之不『打』，正是将『罚』的先声。

Only seven or eight days later, two edicts concerning Guangdong appeared on the same day.
不过七八天的功夫，有关广东的政局，一日连发两谕，

A public decree issued through the Grand Secretariat summoned Guo to the capital and appointed Jiang governor of Guangdong.
一道是由内阁『明发』，『着郭嵩焘来京，以蒋益澧为广东巡抚』；

A court dispatch, sent to Zhejiang, Guangdong, and Fujian with urgency just below the highest six-hundred-li express rate, addressed Ma Xinyi’s reports on inspecting the coast, rebuilding war vessels, and whether Jiang should still proceed to Guangdong now that fighting there had ended.
另一道是仅次于『六百里加紧』的紧急军报的『廷寄』，分饬浙江、广东及福建，写的是∶马新贻奏∶巡视海口情形，酌议改造战船；粤省军事已定，藩司蒋益澧应否前往各一折。

Effective vessels and weapons, it said, were essential against maritime raiders.
官军搜捕洋盗，全赖船械得力，方能奏效。

Ma’s proposal to rebuild thirty red-banner Guangdong boats, combine them with the ten already under Zhang Qiguang, and distribute the forty among Wenzhou and other strategic ports was sound.
马新贻见拟改造红单广艇三十号，合之张其光原带广艇十只，共计四十号，分派温州等处各要口；

So was the purchase of one or two foreign steamships for mobile pursuit.
并购买外国轮船一两只，以为游击搜剿之用，所筹尚属周妥，

Both were approved.
均着照所请行。

Ma was to direct coastal officers to patrol vigorously with the vessels already available and suppress the remaining bandits without negligence.
仍着马新贻督饬沿海各将弁，就见有师船，认真巡缉，搜捕余匪，以靖地方，毋得稍涉疏懈。

The dispatch then announced Jiang’s appointment as governor, ordered him to hand over his duties and leave promptly, and charged him with energetic reform of military affairs, administration, and finance.
本日已明降谕旨；授蒋益澧为广东巡抚。即着蒋益澧赶紧交卸起程，前赴新任。蒋益澧经朝廷擢膺疆寄，责任非轻，到任后将军务吏治及筹饷各事宜，力加整顿，以期日有起色；

He must not disappoint the court through habitual delay.
毋得稍蹈因循积习，致负委任。

The dispatch was to travel by the five-hundred-li express service.
将此由五百里各谕令知之。


Zuo had driven Guo from office in order to gain Guangdong as part of his own sphere.
左宗棠驱逐郭嵩焘是为了想占得广东这个地盘。

On the surface, he had succeeded.
这个目的在表面看，算是达到了；

In reality, he had not.
其实不然。

The court had yielded to pressure in accepting his emphatic recommendation, but the combined judgment of its leading men still had resources for dealing with an imperious commander.
朝廷接纳左宗棠对蒋益澧的力保，虽说是要挟之下，不得不然；但到底集众之力对付独断独行的左宗棠，毕竟有其深谋远虑的过人之处。

Before long, discerning observers could see that the men in power at the centre had made the better move.
没有多久，明眼人都可以看得出来，到头来是朝中用事的人，棋高一着。

First, the court already had preliminary plans to employ Zuo in another important undertaking.
第一，朝廷已有初步的打算，还要重用左宗棠，

It could therefore make a special show of favour over Jiang’s appointment to bind him more closely.
因而借他力保蒋益澧这件事上，特加词色，以为笼络；

Second, Guangdong’s wealth was famous, and the province had long supplied the Imperial Household Department’s public and private demands.
第二，广东的富庶，早就有名，而且一向是内务府公私需索之地，

During more than a decade of Taiping devastation in the southeast, Guangdong had suffered comparatively little.
十多年来，洪杨荼毒遍东南，但广东受灾极轻。

Earlier, the need for military funds had forced the court to defer to Zeng Guofan’s recommendations concerning its senior appointments.
不过早年为了筹饷，广东督抚不得不迁就膺专阃之寄的曾国藩的保荐。

Now that the fighting was over, the time had come to reclaim authority.
事平以后，情况不同，收权之时已到；

But Zeng’s influence was an obstacle, and Guo’s background and administrative record made it impossible to remove him casually without a serious fault.
但一则碍着曾国藩，再则以郭嵩焘的出身与居官的绩效，如无重大过失，不能随便调动，

With Ruilin beside him as a comparison, any honest reform of Guangdong’s administration ought to begin with Ruilin.
尤其是有瑞麟在，相形对比，如说要整饬广东吏治，首先该调的应该是瑞麟而不是郭嵩焘。

At the least, both men should go.
即令退一步来看，至少亦该瑞郭同调；

Otherwise the repeated declarations of impartiality would become mere deception.
否则谕旨中一再申明的『用人行政，一秉大公』等等冠冕堂皇的话，就变成欺人之谈了。

Zuo’s determined attack on Guo offered a rare opportunity to recover control.
难得左宗棠力攻郭嵩焘，却好可用来作为收权的途径。

Guo was hard to dismiss; Jiang would be easy.
黜郭不易；要黜蒋益澧容易得很。

His background, standing, and abilities were all unsuitable for provincial government.
因为论他的出身资望与才具，都不适方面之任；

A future edict could move him with little difficulty and no complaint.
将来一纸上谕，轻易调动，决不会有人说闲话。

There was another advantage: compared with Jiang, Ruilin would look quite qualified to govern Liangguang.
再有层好处，便是有蒋益澧的比照；瑞麟当两广总督，便显得很够格了。

In the eighth month, therefore, Ruilin’s acting appointment was made substantive.
所以八月间降旨，瑞麟的两广总督真除；由署理变为实授。


On that same day, the seventeenth of the eighth month in the fifth year of Tongzhi, two further edicts appeared. One reported that Yang Yuebin, governor-general of Shaanxi and Gansu, pleaded inadequate ability and worsening illness and requested release from office.
同一天——同治五年八月十七，另有两道上谕∶一道是陕甘总督杨岳斌奏∶『才力不及，病势日增，恳请开缺』；

Zuo was transferred to replace him.
调左宗棠为陕甘总督。

The other explained that Yang had proved ill suited to the people and locality and had achieved no effective results.
另一道说∶『杨岳斌于人地不甚相宜，办理未能有效；

The court’s concern for the western frontier was profound.
眷顾西陲，实深廑系。

Zuo’s prestige, strategic knowledge, and ability to handle both military and civil affairs made him the proper choice to suppress the Hui rebellion swiftly and pacify the borderlands.
左宗棠威望素著，熟谙韧略，于军务地方，俱能措置裕如；因特授为陕甘总督，以期迅扫回氛，绥靖边陲。』

The edict thus expressly entrusted him with the great task of settling the northwest.
是特为表明，赋左宗棠以平服西北的重任。

Under customary procedure, an intended appointee to a major provincial post would first be summoned to the capital.
照历来的规制，封疆大臣的调动，首先将预定的人选召赴到京，

If his audience satisfied the emperor, a public edict followed, after which he received instructions and departed.
陛见称旨，方始明发上谕；然后『请训』出京。

If a direct appointment bypassed that procedure, the new official should himself request an audience and instructions.
如果不经这一番程序，直接降旨调补，那末新任就该自请陛见请训；

A transfer generally implied intended reform, and he needed to ascertain the court’s wishes.
意思是此一调动，必含有除旧布新的整顿之意在内。朝廷的希望如何，必先探询明白，所以应该请训。

There were exceptions: urgent military circumstances might make delay impossible, in which case the edict could explicitly direct him to proceed immediately without coming to the capital.
当然，亦有例外，例如军情紧急，不容耽误，便可在上谕中明示∶『即赴新任，毋庸来京请训。』

Zuo’s appointment contained just such a direction.
对左宗棠的新命，即是如此。


That was the outward explanation.
不过，这是表面的看法，

There was another reason beneath it. Travelling from the southeast to his new northwestern post by way of the capital, then through Shanxi into Shaanxi and Gansu, would not have been especially troublesome.
实际上另有文章.因为左宗棠由东南旧任赴西北新任，绕道京师，由山西入秦陇，并不算太费事；

Moreover, the Hui rebellion had persisted for some time and did not demand the immediate suppression required by a newly emerging danger.
而况回乱势缓，已经历相当时日，与防患将然，深恐一发不可收拾，愈早扑灭愈好的情况不同。

The real reason for preventing an audience was that the Grand Council, boards, and other offices had already experienced Zuo’s methods.
而所以阻止他赴京请训，只为左宗棠的手段，军机处及各部院都领教过了，

He demanded money and men without end.
要饷要人，需索不已；

Once in the capital, he might refuse to leave for his post until every demand was met.
一旦到京，非满足他的要求不到任，

Why invite that difficulty?
岂不麻烦？

Better simply to keep him away.
所以索性不要他上京。

The transfer edict reached Fuzhou more than twenty days later.
调任的上谕到达福州时，已在二十天之后。

Zuo was then preparing extensive lists of recommendations for rewards.
其时左宗棠正在大办『保案』，

Those who had helped suppress the remaining rebels in Fujian and Guangdong naturally expected a place; those who had not exerted themselves sought influence and intercession by every means to have their names added.
肃清福建广东残匪，出了力的人，固然个个有分；不曾出力的，亦千方百计，夤缘请托，希翼在保案上加个名字。

Fuzhou filled with official hats and carriages and became extraordinarily lively.
一时福州城内『冠盖云集』，热闹非凡；

When news of Zuo’s transfer spread, the men awaiting appointment in Fujian who had already secured the necessary connections for military promotions were deeply disappointed.
及至传出左宗棠调督陕甘的消息，在福建候补，已搭上了线，可以借军功升官补缺的人，无不大为失望，

Their patron had not fallen, but he had moved away, leaving them without support they could use.
因为靠山虽然未倒，却已移了地方，无可倚恃了。


Hu was also in Fuzhou.
胡雪岩这时也在福州。

To reward his service in supplying arms, provisions, and military funds from Shanghai, Zuo had prepared a separate memorandum and waited for his arrival before sending it with a memorial.
左宗棠为了酬谢他在上海接济军火粮饷的功劳，特地备好一个『附片』，等他到了，方始随折拜发。

It specially recommended Hu for advancement.
这个『附片』是专保胡雪岩加官；

A recommendation submitted individually, outside the general list, was called confidential and differed in effect from an open recommendation by list.
不列入名单而单独保荐，称为『密保』，效用与开单『明保』，不大相同，

Its language was naturally emphatic.
措词当然极有分量，

Hu Guangyong, it said, held the honorary rank of judicial commissioner and was eligible for appointment as a circuit intendant in Fujian.
说是∶『按察使衔福建补用道胡光墉，

Since Zuo’s entry into Zhejiang, he had handled every commission satisfactorily.
自臣入浙，委办诸务，悉臻妥协。

After Hangzhou’s recovery, his work in local reconstruction had been highly effective.
杭州克复后，在籍筹办善后，极为得力；

His devotion to public causes, loyalty, and sincere exertions far exceeded ordinary achievements in relief.
其急公好义，实心实力，迥非寻常办理赈抚劳绩可比。

As Zuo moved from Zhejiang to Fujian and Guangdong, Hu had repeatedly arranged arms and provisions, sending continuous shipments that invariably arrived when required and met the army’s needs.
迨臣自浙而闽而粤，叠次委办军火军糈，络绎转运，无不应期而至，克济军需。』

Zuo therefore requested an exceptional reward to encourage such service: might Hu be granted the honorary rank of provincial treasurer?
是故恳请『破格优奖，以昭激励，可否赏加布政使衔』。
