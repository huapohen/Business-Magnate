胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第十章（本章第3部分，共3部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


Advancement was naturally what Hu desired.
加官自是胡雪岩所希望的；

Yet what particularly excited him was not the title of provincial treasurer itself, but the change of hat insignia it permitted.
不过，使他特别兴奋的，还不在布政使这个衔头，而加了布政使衔，便可改换顶戴。

His existing honorary rank, judicial commissioner, belonged to the upper third grade and carried a bright blue finial.
原衔按察使——臬司是正三品，戴的是亮蓝顶子；

Provincial treasurer belonged to the lower second grade: he could wear a red one.
布政使——藩司是从二品，便可以戴红顶子了。

For a man who had entered officialdom by purchase, attaining a red finial was extremely difficult.
捐班出身的官儿，戴到红顶子，极不容易；

For a merchant, it was almost unheard of, apart from one or two salt merchants specially favoured in the Qianlong reign.
买卖人戴红顶子，更是绝无仅有的事；除非象乾隆年间的盐商那样出自特恩，但亦只有一两个人。

Remembering the source of his good fortune, Hu reflected that, although Zuo had recommended the honour he was about to receive, without Wang Youling there could have been no present success.
是故饮水思源，想起将有得戴的红顶子，虽出自左宗棠的保荐；但没有王有龄，何有今日？

He therefore made a special visit to Wang’s ancestral home.
因而又特地到王有龄的老家去了一趟——

Providing for Wang’s surviving family was his first concern at every festival and New Year; this visit was solely to remember the old friendship, weep, and offer sacrifice.
赡恤王氏遗属，是胡雪岩逢年过节的第一件大事；这次登门，完全是感念旧情，哭奠一番。

He had hoped to visit the grave as well, but an important undertaking kept him desperately busy.
本来还想亲谒墓门，无奈有件大事在办，忙得不可开交；

That would have to wait until the official business was finished.
只好等公事完了再说。


The undertaking was the plan to build steamships themselves.
这件大事就是打算自己造轮船。

Zuo possessed an iron determination: once he had set his mind on something, he could not rest until it was put into practice.
左宗棠的意志强毅，蓄志之事，非见诸实行，不能甘心。

His orders to direct the Fujian campaign had prevented personal attention to the project, but he had not abandoned it.
当时奉命入闽督师，不能躬亲料理，却并未搁下，

He entrusted it to the man he most trusted, Hu.
委托了一个他最信任的人，就是胡雪岩。

In dealings with foreigners, Hu invariably sought Gu’s help.
有关跟洋人打交道的事，胡雪岩必求救于古应春；

Gu had wide connections and saw no need to employ Giquel and d’Aiguebelle.
他的路子很广，认为造轮船不必找日意格、德克碑。

In steamships, railways, and firearms, he thought, the United States was catching up with and surpassing the older Western powers.
方今泰西各国，讲到轮船、铁路、火器的粮良，美国有后来居上之势。

Americans also seemed easier to deal with: in his view, they lacked the cunning of the British, the arrogance of the French, the stubbornness of the Germans, and the deviousness of the Japanese.
同时美国人不似英国人的狡猾、法国人的蛮横、德国人的顽固、日本人的阴险，比较易于相处。

Hu thought differently.
可是胡雪岩另有看法，

Britain possessed the greatest foreign influence in China, France the next.
外国在华势力，英国最大，法国其次。

Only France could be used to check Britain.
要制抑英国的势力，只有利用法国；

Americans and British were of the same stock, he reasoned, so cooperation with Americans would in effect help British expansion.
美国与英国同种，所以与美国合作，等于帮助英国扩张势力。

Moreover, Giquel and d’Aiguebelle had originated the proposal.
同时，日意格与德克碑是原始创议之人，

To abandon them without cause would be dishonourable.
无故背弃，道义有亏。


There was another reason Hu did not mention, though Gu, after their years together, could infer it.
其实胡雪岩还有一层没有说出来的意思；古应春与他多年相处，亦能揣摩得到——

Zuo’s rivalry with Li over power and advantage had almost become irreconcilable.
左宗棠与李鸿章 争权夺利，几已成不两立之势，

Li’s dealings with foreigners relied heavily upon the British Inspector General of Customs, Hart.
李鸿章 办洋务，倚总税务局英国人赫德为重；

If Zuo consulted the British too, the matter would probably pass through Hart’s hands.
然则左宗棠如果再请教英国人，将会逃不了仍由赫德经手。

Hart and Li supported one another; Hart might well advise Prince Gong and Wenxiang, who directed foreign affairs, that steamship construction should be entrusted to Liangjiang.
而赫德与李鸿章 互为表里，说不定会向总洋务的恭王与文建建议，制造轮船事务以由两江经办为宜。

Would that not open the way for Li?
那一来岂不是给李鸿章 开了路？

Gu therefore offered no further proposal and devoted himself to assisting Hu in negotiations with Giquel and d’Aiguebelle.
因此，古应春不再有何主张，只实心实力地作胡雪岩跟日意格、德克碑打交道的助手——

In practice, they dealt only with Giquel, since d’Aiguebelle had left military service and returned to France.
实际上只跟日意格一个人接头；因为德克碑已经退伍回国了。

He arranged there for the shipyard plans, drawings, and associated material to be prepared and sent to Giquel, who discussed them with Hu and Gu.
一切建船厂的计划、图样及瞀，都由德克碑在法国托人办理，寄给日意格，再找胡雪岩、古应春洽谈；

After more than a year, the scheme had been worked out in considerable detail.
一年多下来，已经策划得很周祥了。

When Zuo returned from Guangdong, Hu immediately accompanied Giquel to Fuzhou.
到得左宗棠由广东班师，胡雪岩立即陪着日意格到了福州；

The clarity of the drawings and explanations delighted Zuo.
左宗棠一看图说详明，非常高兴，

He personally inspected the proposed site near Luoxing Pagoda at Mawei, by the approach to Fujian’s coastal waters.
亲自去视察日意格所建议的设厂之地；地在福建海口、马尾罗星塔一带，

Clear water and firm ground made it suitable for slipways and docks, while proximity to the provincial capital made supervision convenient.
水清土实，宜于开槽建坞。兼以密迩省城，稽察方便，

He approved it as soon as he saw it.
所以一看便即中意。


The remaining problem was finance.
剩下来的事，就是筹划经费。

Building the yard, purchasing machinery, and hiring foreign specialists would require more than three hundred thousand taels in initial expenditure.
造厂买机器、雇募师匠，瞀开办费要三十多万银子，

Once production began, materials and wages would cost fifty or sixty thousand a month, or six to seven hundred thousand a year.
厂成开工，材料薪水，每月须银五六万两，一年就是六、七十万，

The first ship was expected after two years, by which time one and a half million taels would have been spent.
预计两年以后造出第一艘船，要花下去一百五十万银子。

Thereafter costs would fall: the total for five years was estimated at a little over three million.
不过以后就可以省了，五年通计，不过三百多万。

Raising that sum would naturally be troublesome.
这三百多万银子，从何筹集？当然煞费周章 ；

Zuo’s idea was to begin first.
左宗棠的意思是先办起来再说，

A million taels would carry the project through its first year.
只要有一百万银子，能应付得了头一年，

Once it had gone too far to abandon, the court would have to find a means of continuing it.
此后欲罢不能，不愁朝廷拿不出办法，

And with Hu at hand, some way to sustain it could surely be devised.
好在有胡雪岩，一定可以想出一条维持得下的路子来。

Zuo made a rough calculation.
因而粗粗计算，

He controlled the allocation of Fujian Customs revenue and provincial likin.
福建海关及本省厘税，提用之权在自己手里；

Zhejiang lay within his jurisdiction and would not stand aside.
浙江分属自己管辖，不会袖手；

Jiang in Guangdong owed his advancement entirely to Zuo and should contribute all the more readily.
广东蒋益澧是自己一手提拔，更当效劳。

With those three sources, the undertaking could proceed boldly.
有此三处财源，尽可放手办事了。


In the middle of the fifth month, Zuo submitted an initial account of the proposed purchase of machinery, recruitment of foreign artisans, and experimental construction of steamships.
因此，左宗棠在五月中旬，便先奏陈『拟购机器，雇洋匠，试造轮船大概情形』。

At the same time, responding to a request for opinions, he recommended wagon warfare against the Nian rebels and military agricultural colonies for the campaign against the Hui, where provisions otherwise had to be transported over immense distances.
同时应诏陈言，以为剿捻宜用车战；平回则千里馈粮，转运艰难，应该采用屯田之策。

The court’s reply showed little enthusiasm for the wagons or the agricultural proposal.
复旨对车战、屯田之议，不见得欣赏；

Steamship construction, however, was declared an urgent necessity of the day.
试造轮船则以为『实系当今应办急务』，

Funds could be drawn in suitable amounts from Fujian Customs, with provincial likin available if those proved insufficient.
所需经费，准予在闽海关关税中酌量提用；如果不够，准再提用福建厘金。

The proposed measures were approved, and Zuo was instructed to submit detailed further memorials on matters not yet settled.
同时指示∶『所陈各条，均着照议办理；一切未尽事宜，仍着详悉议奏。』

With that authority, he set to work vigorously.
有此一旨，左宗棠便密锣紧鼓地干了起来，

He instructed Hu to negotiate the details with d’Aiguebelle, now in Annam, through Giquel, who had been transferred to the Jianghan Customs at Hankou.
一面关照胡雪岩通过已调汉口江汉关税务司的日意格，与在安南的德克碑，商酌一切细节。

Giquel travelled through the summer heat and reached Fuzhou early in the seventh month.
日意格是七月初，冒暑到达福州的。

His first task was to survey the site.
第一件事是勘察船厂地址，

He selected ground below Mount Mawei, where the water reached twelve zhang in depth when the tide stood level.
择定马尾山下，潮平之时水深亦达十二丈的地方设厂；

They then discussed construction, workmen, and finance.
然后议土木、议工匠、议经费，

With broad agreement reached, they drew up a preliminary contract.
大致妥协，订立草约，

At Hu’s suggestion, the French consul-general in Shanghai, Brenier, was to act as guarantor.
担保人照胡雪岩的建议，由法国驻上海的总领事白来尼担保。

Arranging that naturally fell to Hu as well.
当然，这个差使必然又落在胡雪岩肩上。


Late in the eighth month, d’Aiguebelle came directly from Annam to Fuzhou.
到了八月下旬德克碑直接由安南到达福州，

On meeting Zuo, he raised no objection to the preliminary contract, but disputed the site.
与左宗棠晤见之下，对于所订草约，并无异词，但对所选定的建厂地点，却有意见，

The ground below Mount Mawei, he said, had formed from deposited silt and was not firm enough for foundations.
认为马尾山下是淤沙积成的一块陆地，基址不够坚固。

Zuo decided to invite Brenier and Giquel to Fuzhou for a final, comprehensive discussion, after which the plan could be settled and formally submitted.
因而左宗棠决定邀请白来尼、日意格到福州作客，作一个最后的，也是全面的商议，作成定案，正式出奏。

He first wrote asking Hu to come.
主意既定，先写信找胡雪岩到福州来谈。

Just as his enthusiasm was at its height, the edict transferring him to Shaanxi and Gansu arrived.
正在起劲的时候，忽然奉到调督陕甘的上谕；

He found it abrupt, but a little reflection showed it to be a natural outcome of events, not wholly unexpected.
在左宗棠虽觉突兀，但稍一细想，便知事所必然，势所必至，并非全出意外。

Recalling the many historical campaigns that had pacified the Western Regions, he felt his ambition rise and was eager for the challenge.
同时想起历史上许多平定西域的史实，雄心陡起，跃跃欲试，相当兴奋。

Hu, however, was deeply disappointed and anxious, almost at a loss.
在胡雪岩却是件非常扫兴的事，而且忧心忡忡，颇有手足无措之感。

When he called at the governor-general’s office to offer congratulations, his outward composure seemed unchanged, but those who knew him well could see beneath it.
因此，到总督衙门向左宗棠道贺时，虽然表面从容，一切如常；但逃不过相知较深的人的眼光。

One was his fellow townsman Wu Guanli, courtesy name Ziru and literary name Gui’an.
其中有一个是他的小同乡吴观礼。此人字子儒号圭庵，

A provincial examination graduate, Wu was brilliantly gifted and wrote excellent poetry.
本来是一名举人；才气纵横，做得极好的诗。

Hu had recommended him for Zuo’s staff, where he had won profound trust and become head of the military affairs office.
由于胡雪岩的推荐，入左宗棠幕府，深得信任，担任总理营务处的职司，

He was the one secretary in the governor-general’s establishment who participated in military strategy, a counsellor directing events from behind the scenes.
是闽浙总督衙门唯一参赞军务，可说是运筹帷幄的一位幕友。


Wu understood aspects of Zuo that Hu could not, a difference arising from the extent of their reading.
吴观礼对左宗棠所了解的，是胡雪岩所不能了解的，这就因为是读书多少的缘故。

Seeing dejection in Hu’s face, he readily guessed his thoughts.
看到胡雪岩的眉宇之间有落寞之色，当然也就猜想得到他内心的想法。

“Xueyan, are you afraid that, once Lord Zuo goes northwest, you will lose your patron?”
『雪岩，』吴观礼问道，『你是不是怕左公一去西北，你失掉靠山？』

The question was direct, and Hu answered honestly. “Yes.
话问得很率直，胡雪岩也就老实答道∶『是的！

Both public and private business will be difficult for me.”
以后无论公私，我都难了！』

Wu shook his head vigorously. “Not at all!”
『不然！不然！』吴观礼大为摇头。

In his view, a western expedition beyond the passes would take three to five years to show results.
照吴观礼的看法，出关西征，总得三年五载，才能见功；

It was a major campaign, certainly, but differed from the struggle against the Nian, who roamed the central plains and threatened the approaches to the capital.
这当然是一次大征伐，但情势与剿捻不同。捻匪窜扰中原，威胁京畿，

Even if not a disease at the heart, they were a danger close at hand.
在朝廷看，纵非心腹之患，但患在肘腋，

The court could not rest until they were removed, and commanders had to achieve success within a fixed time.
不除不能安心；所以督兵大臣，必得克日收功。

No delay could be tolerated.
事势急迫，不容延误。

The western war, by contrast, would be fought on a distant frontier, beyond the court’s anxious attention morning and evening.
西征则在边陲用兵，天高皇帝远，不致于朝夕关怀，

With less immediate pressure, official business would be easier.
其势较缓，公事自然比较好办。

As for private matters, these meant Hu’s own enterprises.
至于私事，无非胡雪岩个人的事业，

A patron nearby in the southeast could protect him and smooth his path; one far beyond the passes might be difficult to reach in an emergency.
有近在东南的左宗棠，可资荫庇，处处圆通。一旦靠山领兵出关，远在西陲，鞭长莫及；缓急之际呼应为难。

But Wu thought that fear excessive too.
吴观礼认为亦是过虑。

“You must understand that campaigns in the northwest have always depended upon support from the southeast.
『你要晓得，从来经营西北，全靠东南支持；

Your duties in Shanghai will grow more important, and your standing with them.
此后你在上海的差使，会更加吃重，地位也就更非昔比。

What happens depends on what a man makes of it.”
事在人为。』

He patted Hu’s shoulder.
吴观礼拍拍胡雪岩的肩说，

“You have not read the Record of Imperial Military Achievements and do not know Qianlong’s Ten Great Campaigns.
『你没有读过「圣武记」，不知道乾隆年间的「十大武功」。

In the past, frontier enterprises were entrusted to imperial relatives or great Manchu officials.
经营边疆，从前都是派亲贵或者满洲重臣挂帅；

Giving one to our Lord Zuo is no ordinary matter.
如今派了我们左公，是件非同小可的事。

Among the great commanders distinguished since the Taiping uprising, Lord Zeng has stood above the rest, with Zuo and Li next.
洪杨以来的元戎勋臣，曾相高高在上；左李两位两次；

From now on, Zeng and Zuo may well be spoken of together.”
从此以后，只怕曾左要并称了。』

That last sentence awakened Hu.
最后一句话，点醒了胡雪岩；

His anxiety vanished.
满腔忧烦，顿时一扫而空。

His patron would be farther away, but greater and more firmly established.
靠山虽远，却更高大稳固；

Once he understood that, there was no reason to fret.
了解到这一层，就不必发什么愁了。


“Thank you for enlightening me,” Hu said happily.
『多承指点。』胡雪岩很高兴地说，

“Let me trouble you further.
『索性还要费你的心，

Please explain the situation in the northwest in detail.”
西北是怎么个情形，请你细细谈一谈。』

“First let us discuss the steamships,” Zuo said firmly. “However urgently the court presses me to go beyond the passes, I shall not leave Fujian until this undertaking has been settled under my own hand.”
『我们先谈谈造轮船。』左宗棠极坚决地说∶『不管朝廷催得怎么紧，要我赶出关；这件事非在我手里先定了局，我不会离开福建。』

“Yes, sir,” Hu replied. “Once it is settled, to whom will you entrust it?”
『是的。』胡雪岩问道∶『定局以后，交给哪位？』

“Exactly!
『着！

You have reached the crucial point.
你问在要害上了。

I have cherished this ambition for three years and worked hard for months to bring it this far.
我蓄志三年，辛苦数月，才能有此结果，

If I entrust it to the wrong man and it is abandoned halfway, I shall never be reconciled to it.
倘或付托非人，半途而废，我是不甘心的。

I am still considering that.
这一层，我还在考虑；

For the present, I must ask you to take more trouble.”
眼前还要请你多偏劳。』

“That goes without saying.
『那何消说得。

But I can only attend to it until you leave Fujian.”
不过，我亦只能管到大人离福建为止。』

“No.
『不然。

You must continue after I leave,” Zuo said.
我离开福建，你还是要管。』左宗棠说，

“The shipyard will remain your concern.
『管的是船厂。

I will never abandon this halfway and give Li Shaoquan occasion to laugh.
这件事我决不能半途而废，为李少荃所笑。

I have considered it countless times: if it succeeds, it will surpass his foreign undertakings many times over.”
而且我不知道盘算过多少次，这件事办成，比李少荃所办的洋务，不知道要好过多少倍。』

His competitive motive was plain.
这就很明白的了，左宗棠是出于争胜之心。

No advice could diminish his desire to excel.
他的好胜心是决不因任何的规劝而稍减的；

Hu knew that he could not escape the burden; he must perform his part, however crushing the weight upon his head.
胡雪岩知道自己难卸仔肩，非『顶石臼做戏』不可了。

Yet the crucial question remained unanswered, and he had to ask again.
不过，刚才那句『问在要害』上的话，并无答复，还得追问。

“Then I can only obey.
『大人这么说，我当然只有遵命。』

But whom shall I have to serve in Fujian?”
胡雪岩说，『就不知道将来在福建还要伺候哪位？』

“Do not speak of serving.
『不要说什么伺候的话。

Xueyan, you are exceptionally clever.
雪岩，你最聪明不过；

There is no one you cannot get on with.
没有什么你不能相处的。

It is precisely because I shall entrust the work to this man that I need you still more—” He broke off. Hu understood perfectly: the chosen man would be difficult to serve.
惟其我付托了这个人，更得借重你——。』左宗棠没有再说下去∶胡雪岩却完全懂了他的意思，他所付托的，是个很难「伺候」的人。

He was all the more eager to know. “Who is it?”
这就更急着要问∶『是哪位？』

“Shen Youdan.”
『沈幼丹。』


It was Shen Baozhen, the former governor of Jiangxi, now at home observing mourning.
原来是丁忧回籍守制的前任江西巡抚沈葆桢。

Hu was genuinely surprised.
这在胡雪岩却真有意外之感。

On reflection, the choice was sound, but the regulations forbade a man to serve as an official—particularly a high official—in his native province.
细想一想，付托倒也得人；不过以本省人做本省官，而且必是大官，为法例所不许。

Mourning created a further obstacle.
兼以丁忧，更成窒碍。

What was Zuo’s intention?
不知左宗棠是怎么想来的？

Hu remained silent.
他只有付之默然了。

“I know what you are thinking.
『我知道你的想法，

Let me show you a draft memorial.”
我给你看个奏稿。』

The lengthy document argued the advantages of shipbuilding before reaching its principal point. The undertaking was imperative, it said; Zuo’s approaching departure from Fujian could not justify setting it aside.
奏向洋洋千言，畅论造船之利；最后谈到主题∶臣维轮船一事，势在必行，岂可以去闽在迩，忽为搁置？

He and the foreign officers had negotiated the many complicated arrangements for establishing the works.
且设局制造，一切繁难事宜，均臣与洋员议定，

Unless these were settled while he remained, his successor would face tangled affairs without anyone to consult; unclear agreements might also cause later controversy for which Zuo could not evade responsibility.
