胡雪岩 · 红顶商人
The Red-Topped Merchant
第十一章（本章第1部分，共2部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


CHAPTER ELEVEN
第十一章


The first great task was the western expedition.
他的第一件大事，便是西征。

Any major campaign began with two questions: troops and funds.
而凡有大征伐，首先要筹划的是兵、饷二事。

For several nights Zuo had remained awake, thinking beneath the lamp, and had written an outline of his plans.
左宗棠连日深宵不寐，灯下沉思，已写成了一个筹划的概略；

He now took it from a drawer and asked Hu to study it.
此时从书案抽斗中取了出来，要胡雪岩细看。

The outline dealt first with troops, then with finance.
这个节略先谈兵，次筹饷。

Military arrangements had to suit local conditions.
而谈兵又必因地制宜，

The terrain of the northwest differed completely from that of the southeast.
西北与东南的地势，完全不同；

Southern soldiers would find the food unfamiliar and the cold intolerable.
南方的军队，到了西北，第一不惯食科；第二不耐寒冷。

The effective commanders and units with whom Zuo had fought across the southeast, particularly those recruited in Fujian and Guangdong, could therefore not be taken northwest.
因此，左宗棠在东南转战得力的将领部队，特别是籍贯属于福建、广东两省的，都不能带到西北。

He intended to bring only three thousand and more men.
带到西北的，只有三千多人，

Liu Dian, formerly assistant director of military affairs in Fujian and now recommended for the corresponding duties in Shaanxi and Gansu, would return to Hunan to recruit another three thousand men and lead them west.
另外他预备派遣原来帮办福建军务，现已出奏保荐帮办陕甘军务的刘典回湖南，召募三千子弟兵，带到西北。

These six thousand and more would form Zuo’s personal force.
这六千多人，左宗棠用来当作亲兵；

The main fighting army he intended to raise locally, making common cause with the able men of Guanzhong.
至于用来作战的大批部队，他打算在本地招募，要与『关中豪杰』共事业。


At this point Hu exclaimed, “Your Excellency, if you proceed in this way, when can the Hui rebellion be suppressed?”
看到这里，胡雪岩不由得失声说道∶『大人，照你老人家的办法，要什么时候才能平得了回乱？』

“I do not quite understand you.”
『你这话，我不大懂。』

“Consider, sir.
『大人请想，

Recruiting an army is not the work of a day, and training it into an elite force is harder still.
招募成军，不是一朝一夕的事；练成精锐，更是谈何容易？

It will take a year or two.”
这一来，要花一两年的功夫。』

“A year or two?
『岂止一两年？』

Establishing the Western Regions will require no less than ten years.”
左宗棠说道∶『经营西域，非十年不足以收功。』

“Ten years!”
『十年？』

Hu was startled.
胡雪岩吓一跳，

“That would take—” He stopped, but Zuo knew he meant the cost.
『那得——。』他虽住口不语，左宗棠也知道，说的是要费多少饷？

Smiling, he said, “Do not be anxious.
笑笑说道∶『你不要争！

I intend to establish agricultural colonies in the northwest.
我要在西北办屯垦；

That is the way to lasting peace.
这是长治久安之计。

As with the shipyard, one must not seek an immediate return.
就象办船厂一样，不能急切图利；

Once the results appear, you will see that I have planned correctly.”
可是一旦见效，你就知道我的打算不错了。』

Hu set the outline down and bent his head in thought.
『是！』胡雪岩将那份节略搁下，低着头沉思。

“What are you considering?”
『你在想什么？』

“A long way ahead.
『我想得很远。』

I too am thinking eight or ten years into the future.”
胡雪岩答说∶『我也是想到十年八年以后。』

“Exactly!”
『着！』

Zuo clapped his hands in delight.
左宗棠拊掌欣然，

“Our thoughts coincide.
『你的意思与我不谋而合；

We must plan carefully and find provisions and pay for eight or ten years.”
我们要好好打算，筹出十年八年的饷米。』

Hu did not answer immediately.
胡雪岩暂且不答，

He picked up the outline and continued reading until he understood the broad scheme.
捡起节略再看，大致了解了左宗棠在西北用兵的计划。

Zuo intended to train cavalry, build two-wheeled gun carriages, and establish a central bureau for agricultural colonies.
他要练马队；又要造『两轮炮车』；开设『屯田总局』——

The colonies would need implements, seed, carts, horses, and advances for all food and incidental expenses until the first harvest.
办屯垦要农具、要种子、要车马、要垫发未收成以前的一切粮食杂用，

The amount was considerable.
算起来这笔款子，真正不在少数。

“Your Excellency, cavalry and gun carriages are necessary for victory, and the court will certainly approve them.
『大人，』胡雪岩问道∶『练马队、造炮车、是致胜所必需，朝廷一定会准。

But might it regard the agricultural colonies as a matter that can wait?”
办屯垦，朝廷恐怕会看作不急之务吧？』


“That is where you do not understand.
『这，你就不懂了。』

There are still many men of learning at court.
左宗棠说，『朝中到底不少读书人，

They will understand.”
他们会懂的。』

Hu reddened, but spoke sincerely. “Yes, sir.
胡雪岩脸一红，却很诚恳地说∶『是！

I do not fully understand.
我确是不大懂，

Please instruct me.”
请大人教导。』

Zuo outlined the restrictions upon campaigning in the Western Regions.
于是左宗棠为胡雪岩约略讲述用兵西域的限制，

Since the Qin and Han dynasties, armies had gone west in early spring and returned in autumn.
自秦汉以来，西征皆在春初，及秋而还。

When autumn came and the horses were fat, the enemy held the advantage; the severe cold that followed was also beyond the endurance of troops from within the passes.
因为第一，秋高马肥，敌人先占了优势；其次就是严寒的天气，非关内的士兵所能适应。

“Because of these difficulties, Emperor Wu of Han won victories almost every year against the Xiongnu in the early Yuanshuo period, yet had to send an army out again every year.
『就是为了这些不便，汉武帝元朔初年征匈奴，几乎年年打胜仗，而年年要出师，

The grass was cut, but the roots remained—a burden without end.”
斩草不能除根，成了个无穷之累。』

After citing precedents, Zuo returned to the present. “The Hui rebellion presents much the same problem. I could choose twenty or thirty thousand good troops, take them beyond the passes in spring, pursue the enemy through the summer, and return in autumn, as Wei Qing and Huo Qubing did.
左宗棠一番引经据典以后，转入正题∶『如今平回乱，亦仿佛是这个道理∶选拔两三万能打的队伍，春天出关，尽一夏天追奔逐北，交秋班师，如当年卫霍之所为，我亦办得到。

But would that settle the rebellion?”
可是，回乱就此算平了吗？』

“Naturally not.”
『自然没有平。』

Hu understood.
胡雪岩了然了，

“As the verse says, wildfire cannot burn it all away; the spring wind brings it back.
『有道是「野火烧不尽，春风吹又生。」

Only by thoroughly turning over the ground can the weeds be prevented from returning.”
只要花大功夫拿那块地彻底翻一翻，野草自然长不出来了。』

“Precisely!
『一点不错！

Your comparison is apt.”
你这个譬喻很恰当。』

Zuo was pleased.
左宗棠欣慰地说，

“If you understand my purpose, I can rest easy.
『只要你懂我的意思，我就放心了。

You will obtain what I need.”
你一定会把我所要的东西办妥当。』

Praise from Zuo was precious, but it was also a weighty burden.
这顶『高帽子』出于左宗棠之口，弥觉珍贵；然而也极沉重。

Hu knew that he was being asked to bear the principal responsibility for raising funds.
胡雪岩知道左宗棠的意思是要他负筹饷的主要责任。

After concentrated thought, he felt that so great and complicated a matter required some definite answers first.
凝神细想了一会，觉得兹事体大，而且情况复杂，非先问个明白不可。


“Your Excellency, how many battalions do you intend to train?”
『大人，将来要练多少营的队伍。』

“That is difficult to say.
『这很难说，

I must see the circumstances beyond the passes.”
要到了关外看情形再说。』

The very first question thus became a difficulty.
第一个疑问，便成了难题；

Without the number of men, monthly expenditure could not be calculated.
人数未定，月饷的数目就算不出来。

Hu could only estimate: at fifty thousand men, with pay, food, clothing, weapons, tents, cooking utensils, and other expenses kept within five taels per man, the total would be two hundred and fifty thousand a month.
胡雪岩只能约略估计，以五万人算，每人粮饷、被服、武器；以及营帐锅碗等等杂支，在五两银子以内开支，每月就要二十五万两。

He asked a second question. “You will take six thousand men beyond the passes?”
于是他再问第二问∶『是带六千人出关？』

“Yes.
『是的。

About sixty-five hundred.
大概六千五百人。』

Thirty-five hundred will set out from Fujian and Zhejiang.
左宗棠答说，『三千五百人由闽浙两省动手；

Another three thousand, once recruited in Hunan, will go directly west.”
另外三千人在湖南招募成军以后，直接出关。』

“What about travelling expenses?
『行资呢？

Would ten taels a man suffice?”
每人十两够不够？』

“I think it should.”
『我想，应该够了。』

“That is sixty-five thousand, needed immediately.” Hu asked his third question. “How much cavalry do you propose to train?”
『那就是六万五千两，而且眼前就要。』胡雪岩又问第三问∶『大人预备练多少马队？』


“I have never commanded cavalry and do not yet know its battalion organization very well.
『马队我还没有带过，营制也不甚了然。

My preliminary intention is three thousand men.”
只有自初步打算，要练三千马队。』

“Then at least three thousand horses will be needed,” Hu said.
『那就至少要有三千匹马。』胡雪岩说，

“They must be bought at Zhangjiakou.
『买马要到张家口，

That money is available; I can advance it.”
这笔钱倒是现成的，我可以垫出来。』

“You have money in Zhangjiakou?”
『怎么？你在张家口有钱？』

“Yes.
『是的。』

A hundred thousand taels, originally intended for furs and medicinal materials.
胡雪岩说，『我有十万银子在张家口，原来打算留着办皮货、办药材的，

I shall have to use it for horses first.”
现在只好先挪来买马。』

“Excellent.”
『这倒好。』

Zuo was delighted.
左宗棠很高兴地说，

“Then I can send a purchasing officer at once.”
『既然如此，我立刻就可以派委员去采办了。』

“Let me know whom you appoint, and I shall send someone with him.” Hu continued, “What about the two-wheeled gun carriages?
『是！大人派定了通知我；我再派人陪着一起去。』胡雪岩又问，『两轮炮车呢？

How many?”
要多少？』

“Like Han Xin with soldiers, the more the better.
『「韩信将兵，多多益善」。

Beyond the passes the country is vast.
塞外辽阔，

In addition to swift cavalry, guns on carriages can sweep the ground clear at a stroke.
险精骑驰骋以外，炮车轰击，一举而廓清之，

There is no better instrument for destroying the enemy’s strongholds.”
最是扫穴犁庭的利器！』

Hu’s heart grew heavy.
听这一说，胡雪岩觉得心头沉重。

He had often heard of government troops who cared nothing for civilian lives, turned their guns upon villages, and bombarded indiscriminately.
因为他也常听说，有那不恤民命的官军，常常使炮口对准村落，乱轰一气。

Bandits hiding there might be killed, wounded, or driven out, but many innocent inhabitants suffered with them.
窝藏在其中的盗匪，固然非死即伤或逃；而遭受池鱼之殃的百姓，亦复不少。


Most of the Western firearms and artillery in Zuo’s army had been purchased by Hu in Shanghai.
左宗棠所部的洋枪洋炮，多由胡雪岩在上海采办；

Follow the matter back to its origin, and he himself had unknowingly contributed to wrongdoing.
推原论始，便是自己在无形中造了孽，

His mother had remonstrated with him countless times over his procurement of weapons that killed people.
为了胡雪岩的购办杀人利器，胡老太太不知道劝过他多少次；

He was a devoted son and obeyed her in every detail of family affairs.
胡雪岩十分孝顺，家务巨细，母命是从，

Only in public business did he feel unable to follow her wishes.
惟独到公事上头，不能不违慈命。

Fortunately, she understood: it was not deliberate disobedience, but a situation from which he saw no escape.
好在胡老太太心地亦很明白；知道不是儿子不听话，实在是无可奈何。

She could only do her utmost to make amends for his “sins.”
因此，只有尽力为他弥补『罪过』，

Besides her customary worship, she provided medical treatment, medicines, and cooling tea in summer, padded clothing and rice vouchers in winter.
平时烧香拜佛，不在话下；夏天施医施药施凉茶，冬天舍棉衣、散米票，

Anyone seeking help to repair bridges or roads, care for the old, or relieve the poor received a generous promise.
其他修桥铺路，恤老怜贫的善举，只要求到她，无不慷慨应诺。

Yet however many good deeds she performed, buying birds to release could not compensate for human lives.
但是，尽管好事做了无其数；买鸟雀放生，总抵偿不了人命，

Every mention of arms purchases saddened her.
所以胡老太太一提起买军火，便会郁郁不乐。

Hearing Zuo speak so enthusiastically now, Hu saw his mother’s troubled face in his mind and remained silent.
胡雪岩此时听左宗棠说得那么起劲，不由得便想起了老母的愁颜；因而默不作声。


“What is it?”
『怎么？』

Zuo naturally did not understand.
左宗棠当然不解，

“Do you think building the gun carriages will be difficult?”
『你是不是觉得我要造两轮炮车，有困难？』

“No.
『不是。

I am wondering how many will be required and what each will cost.
我是在想，炮车要多少，每辆要多少银子？

I cannot draw up that budget.”
这笔预算打不出来。』

“That comes later.
『那是以后的事。

For now, we must allow a rough amount.
眼前只好算一个约数；

I should like to set aside two hundred thousand taels.”
我想最好能抽个二十万银子造炮车。』

“And the agricultural colonies?
『那末办屯田呢？

How much must be raised for those?”
请问大人，要筹多少银子？』

“Harder still to say.
『这更难言了。』

Fortunately, the colonies are not an undertaking of merely three or five years, and their burden will steadily diminish.
左宗棠说∶『好在办屯田不是三年五载的事；而且负担总是越来越轻。

I think five hundred thousand taels, used as a revolving fund, will certainly suffice.”
我想有个五十万银子，前后周转着用，一定够了。』

Hu calculated silently for a moment, then exclaimed, “This is tremendous!
『是的。』胡雪岩心里默算了一会，失声说道∶『这样就不得了！

Tremendous!”
不得了！』

“How so?”
『怎么？』

“Let me reckon it for you.”
『我算给大人听！』

He counted on his fingers. “Travelling expenses, sixty thousand.
胡雪岩屈指数着∶『行资六万。

Horses with saddles and bridles, say a hundred and twenty taels each: three thousand horses make thirty-six thousand.
买马连鞍辔之类，算他一百二十两银子一匹，三千匹就是三万六千。

Gun carriages, two hundred thousand.
造炮车二十万。

Half the agricultural fund to begin with, two hundred and fifty thousand.
办屯田先筹一半，二十五万。

Pay and provisions for fifty thousand men at five taels per month, two hundred and fifty thousand monthly, or three million a year.
粮饷以五万人计，每人每月五两，总共就是二十五万，一年三百万。

Altogether, three million five hundred and forty thousand.
合计三百五十四万，

That is what must be raised for the first year.”
这是头一年要筹的饷。』

Zuo too was stunned.
这一算，左宗棠也楞住了。

Raising three million five hundred and forty thousand taels was no easy matter.
要筹三百五十四万两的饷，谈何容易？

Even half—one million seven or eight hundred thousand—was a formidable sum.
就算先筹一半，也是一百七、八十万，实在不是一笔小数目了。


“And with transport so difficult in the northwest, we must make generous allowances everywhere.
『而且我想，西北运输不便，凡事都要往宽处去算。

The money has to be secured before you take it with you.
这笔饷非先筹好带去不可！

This is not like Fuzhou and Shanghai, only two days apart by sea, where I can somehow provide assistance in an emergency.
大人，这不比福州到上海，坐海轮两天功夫就可以到，遇有缓急之时，我无论如何接济得上。

Thousands of miles away, amid snow and ice, if you run short of provisions and cannot make yourself heard, what use will frantic anxiety be?”
西北万里之外，冰天雪地之中，那时大人乏粮缺食，呼应不灵，岂不是急死了也没用？』

“Exactly.
『说得是，说得是！

That is precisely my thought.
我正就是这个意思。

Xueyan, we must raise it beforehand.
雪岩，这笔饷，非先筹出来不可；

If not enough for a year, then at least enough to prevent shortage for six months.”
筹不足一年，至少也要半年之内不虞匮乏之好。』

“Provided there is a dependable source of military funds, I shall do everything I can to arrange advances and subsequent receipts.” Hu then explained why the western army could never be allowed even a temporary interruption.
『只要有了确实可靠的「军饷」，排前补后，我无论如何是要效劳的。』接着，胡雪岩又分析西征军饷，所以绝不能稍有不继的缘故。

Elsewhere, a gap could be filled from likin, land revenue, or customs receipts, or at worst by an accommodation with a neighbouring province.
在别的省份，一时青黄不接，有厘税可以指拨，有钱粮可以划提，或者有关税可以暂时周转，至不济还有邻省可以通融。

The northwest was barren and poor, leaving no room to move money around.
西北地瘠民贫，无可腾挪，

Only Shanxi offered a possible neighbouring resource, and that was limited.
邻省则只有山西可缓急之恃，但亦有限，

Communications were difficult; transporting silver might itself take a month.
而且交通不便，现银提解，往往亦须个把月的功夫。

If funds failed and hungry soldiers mutinied, the situation could become irretrievable.
所以万一青黄不接，饥卒哗变，必成不可收拾之势。

Zuo had foreseen this in his own careful deliberations.
这个看法，亦在左宗棠深思熟虑的预见之中。

He fully agreed that they must first secure funds guaranteed to arrive without a tael missing or a day’s delay: the contributions assigned to the various provinces for Gansu.
因而完全同意胡雪岩的主张，应该先筹好分文不短，一天不延的『的饷』；也就是各省应该协解的『甘饷』。

Here he felt pleased with his foresight.
谈到这一层上头，左宗棠便很得意于自己的先见了；

Without driving out his relative by marriage, Guo Songtao, he would have had only Fujian and Zhejiang as bases of support.
如果不是撵走了他的『亲家』郭嵩焘，便顶多只有福建、浙江两个地盘，

Now wealthy Guangdong was included.
而如今却有富庶的广东在内。

Those three provinces must be the starting point.
要筹的饷，自然先从这三省算起。


Fujian came first.
三省之中，又必先从福建开始。

It had been contributing forty thousand taels a month towards Zuo’s Zhejiang troops, with another ten thousand from Fujian Customs.
福建本来每月协济左宗棠带来的浙军军饷四万两；闽海关每月协济一万两。

After the Taiping remnants were cleared, the forty thousand had been reassigned to Gansu.
从长毛余孽肃清以来，协浙的四万两，改为协济甘肃；

It now naturally came under Zuo’s control.
现在自是顺理成章 归左宗棠了。

The customs contribution, however, had already been allocated to the shipyard.
至于海关的一万两，已籴接济船厂经费；

Since that was his own initiative, he could not reverse himself and had to relinquish those ten thousand.
此事是他所首创，不能出尔反尔，这一万两只得放弃。

Next came Zhejiang.
其次是浙江。

When Yang Yuebin became governor-general of Shaanxi and Gansu with responsibility for the western campaign, Zeng Guofan had helped arrange funds and assigned Zhejiang twenty thousand a month.
当杨岳斌接任陕甘总督，负西征全责时，曾国藩曾经代为出面筹饷，派定浙江每月协解两万。

In the tenth month of the previous year, Zuo had brought his army into Guangdong and achieved his plan of feeding it there.
上年十月间左宗棠带兵到广东，『就食于粤』的计划既已实现，

Under Hu’s indirect pressure, he had then had to honour his promise to relieve Zhejiang’s burden.
在胡雪岩的侧面催促之下，不得不守减除浙江负担的诺言。

That left Zhejiang with an additional hundred and forty thousand a month.
在浙江等于每月多了十四万银子；

Ma Xinyi, mindful of the wider situation, voluntarily increased the Gansu subsidy by thirty thousand, making fifty thousand monthly.
马新贻是很顾大局的人，自请增拨甘饷三万两，每月共讲五万银子。

“Zhejiang has treated me well,” Zuo said.

“Ma Gushan is a fine man.
马谷山为人亦很漂亮，

“One is the promised shipbuilding contribution, ten thousand a month.

This was an indirect way of increasing Fujian’s burden.

Hu found it privately amusing: Zuo’s calculations could be sharper than a petty merchant’s.
胡雪岩心里好笑，左宗棠的算盘，有时比市侩还精；

但只要不累浙江，他没有不赞成之理。

“Precisely.
『是啊！

算起来于曾无损，为什么不能划帐？』

On the merits, how could it be said to cost Zeng nothing?
就事论事，何得谓之『与曾无损』？

Hu wanted to advise against displeasing him for the sake of ten thousand taels.
胡雪岩本想劝他，犯不上为这一万两银子，惹得曾国藩心中不快。

转念又想，若是这样开口一劝，左宗棠又一定大骂曾国藩。正事便无法谈得下去。

因而将到口的话又缩了回去。

这下来就要算广东的接济了。

Its existing contribution to Gansu was only ten thousand; another ten thousand was intended for shipbuilding.
广东的甘饷，本来只定一万；造船经费也是一万，

Following the Zhejiang precedent, both could go to Gansu, making twenty thousand.
仿照浙江的例子协甘，共是两万。

Zuo hoped to double that to forty thousand, matching Fujian.
左宗棠意思，希望增加一倍，与福建一样，每月四万。

“That can certainly be done,” Hu said.
『这一定办得到的。』胡雪岩说，

“Governor Jiang owes his advancement to you and should exert himself for public and personal reasons alike.
『蒋中丞是大人一手提拔，于公于私，都应该尽心。

“That hardly matters.

差得很多。』

“There are others, of course.

The Ministry of Revenue has designated seven further provinces.

Jiangsu, Henan, Sichuan, and Shandong must wait until I reach Shaanxi.
江苏、河南、四川、山东四省的甘饷，只有到了陕西再说。

我想，通扯计算，一年两百四十万银子，无论如何是有的。』

“Then I shall raise half of that in advance for you,” Hu said as though it were a casual matter.
『那，我就替大人先筹一半。』胡雪岩若无其事地说。

“Half?” Zuo wondered if he had heard correctly.
『一半？』左宗棠怕是自己没有听清楚，

“Half is one million two hundred thousand taels.”
特意钉一句∶『一半就是一百二十万银子。』

“Yes.
『是，一百二十万。』

I shall have it ready for you to take.”
胡雪岩说∶『我替大人筹好了带走。』

“But—” Zuo scarcely knew what to say.
『这，』左宗棠竟不知怎么说才好了，

“Where will you find such an enormous sum?”
『你哪里去筹这么一笔巨数？』

“I have a method.
『我有办法。

Naturally, it will require your approval.
当然，这个办法，要大人批准。

Once I have worked out the arrangements, I shall explain them.”
等我筹划好了，再跟大人面禀。』

Zuo could not press further.
左宗棠不便再追着问。

He was doubtful, but more confident than doubtful.
他虽有些将信将疑，地是信多于疑；

Remembering that Hu had fulfilled every promise he had made, he felt reassured and pleased.
再想到胡雪岩所作的承诺，无一不曾实现，也就释然、欣然了。


“When will you leave, sir, and when do you expect to pass beyond the frontier gates?”
『大人什么时候动身，什么时候出关？』

“I intend to leave early in the eleventh month.
『我想十一月初动身，

I shall travel slowly, discussing affairs with governors and governors-general along the way.
沿途跟各省督抚谈公事，走得慢些，

I shall probably not reach the capital until the year’s end.”
总要年底才能到京。』

“The capital?
『到京？』

胡雪岩不解地问，『上谕不是关照，直接出关。』

“That was hardly the intention of Their Majesties.

想来两宫太后决不致于拦我。

『左宗棠停了一下又说∶』至于出关的日期，现在还不能预定。

“Then we have another three or four months.
『』那还有三四个月的功夫。

The million two hundred thousand will be ready before you go.

“Excellent!
『』那太好了！

Hu needed no such reminder.
『这又何消左宗棠说得？

He too wished to settle the matter quickly.
胡雪岩亦希望早早能够定局。

Although he gave his plan an eight-in-ten chance, the silver was not yet in his hands.
无奈自己心里所打的一个主意，虽有八成把握，到底银子不曾到手。

The proverb about a cooked duck flying away was an exaggeration, but where money was concerned, even an agreed arrangement could change at the last moment.
俗语说的『煮熟了鸭子飞掉了』，自是言过其实；但凡事一涉银钱，即有成议，到最后一刻变卦，亦是常有之事。

A million two hundred thousand was no trifling sum.
一百二十万两不是个小数目，

The western expedition’s success, and whether Zuo might rise from his noble title to a grand secretaryship, depended upon it.

If the plan failed just before completion, how could Hu answer for it?
倘或功败垂成，如何交代？

He deeply regretted forgetting the warning: a full bowl of rice is easy to eat, but a sweeping promise is hard to fulfil.
兴念及此，胡雪岩深深失悔，何以会忘却『满饭好吃，满话难说？之戒？

如今既不能打退堂鼓，就得全力以赴加紧进行。
