所苦的是眼前还脱不得身，
The difficulty was that Hu could not yet get away.

因为日意格、德克碑与中国官场打交道，大至船厂计划，小至个人生活，都要找他接头。
In their dealings with Chinese officials, Giquel and d’Aiguebelle came to him about everything, from the shipyard scheme to the smallest details of their daily lives.

在左宗棠，对洋人疑信参半；
Zuo, for his part, was divided between trust and suspicion of foreigners.

而有些话怕一说出来，洋人憨直，当场驳回，未免伤他的身分与威望，
He also feared that, if he said certain things himself, their bluntness might lead them to contradict him outright, injuring his dignity and authority.

因而亦少不得胡雪岩这样一个居间曲曲转达的人。
An intermediary such as Hu, who could convey his meaning tactfully, was indispensable.

这就难了！
It was a predicament.

左思右想，一时竟无以为答；坐在那里大大发楞。
Hu turned it over in his mind without finding an answer and sat lost in thought.

这是左宗棠从未见过的样子，
Zuo had never seen him like this.

不免诧异；却又不好问得。
He was surprised, but could hardly ask what was wrong.

主宾二人，默然相答；
Host and guest sat facing each other in silence.

使得侍立堂下的戈什哈亦惊愕不止，因为平日总见左宗棠与胡雪岩见了面，谈笑风生，滔滔不绝，
Even the guards below the hall were astonished: normally these two would be laughing and talking without pause.

何以此刻对坐发呆？
Why were they now staring blankly at each other?

于是，有个左宗棠亲信的戈什哈上前问道∶『可是留胡大人在这里便饭？』
At last a trusted guard approached. “Will His Excellency Hu be staying for a simple meal?”


这下使胡雪岩惊醒了，
Hu came to himself.

『不，不，多谢！』他首先辞谢，
“No, no, thank you,” he said at once.

“I must go to the wharf to see someone off.”

“Who is it?” Zuo asked.

“Yes.

“What business?

Could it concern the shipyard?”

胡雪岩灵机一动，

点点头答说∶『也许。』

Zuo told him not to worry and gave him his personal assurance that he would reason with them kindly and do his best to reassure them.

如果有什么意见不能相合之处，自会暂且搁下，等胡雪岩回到福州以后再说。
Any differences they could not resolve would be set aside until Hu returned.

Known in Chinese by a phonetic rendering of the foreign term, the comprador was the head of the bank’s Chinese staff.

It was one of Britain’s overseas banks, with its head office in London and branches in Bombay and Shanghai.

麦加利银行是英皇发布敕令，特许在印度、澳洲、上海设立分行的股份有限公司。
The Chartered Bank was a joint-stock company authorised by royal charter to establish branches in India, Australia, and Shanghai.

总行设在伦敦；咸丰七年在上海开设分行，
Its head office was in London; its Shanghai branch opened in the seventh year of Xianfeng.

麦加利银行完全是为了便利英商在印度、澳洲、上海的贸易而设，
It had been established specifically to facilitate British trade with India, Australia, and Shanghai.
