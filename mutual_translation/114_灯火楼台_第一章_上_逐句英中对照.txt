胡雪岩
《灯火楼台》第一章（上）
Towers of Light — Chapter One, Part One
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


Hu Xueyan: Towers of Light
胡雪岩全传——灯火楼台


CHAPTER ONE: FROM GENERAL TO MINISTER｜第一章 出将入相


On the seventh day of the third lunar month in the seventh year of Guangxu, Hu Xueyan at last fulfilled his promise and arrived in Beijing.
光绪七年三月初七，胡雪岩终于践约抵达北京。

Two foreigners accompanied him: Focke, a German who had traded in China for many years and managed the Tailai Trading House, and Cameron, a representative of the British-owned Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation.
同行的有两个洋人，一个是在华经商多年，泰来洋行的经理，德国人福克，一个是英商汇丰银行的代表凯密伦。

The weather had been exceptionally cold that year, and the harbour at Tianjin was still frozen, so Hu had travelled overland.
由于这年天气格外冷，天津海口尚未解冻，所以胡雪岩是从陆路来的，

His imposing procession of more than ten carriages entered You’an Gate and made straight for Fukangfu Bank in Caochang Shitiao Hutong outside Qianmen.
浩浩荡荡十几辆车，一进右安门，直投前门外草厂十条胡同阜康福钱庄。

Wang Weixian, the head manager, had begun preparing for his employer’s arrival ten days earlier.
为了接待东家，“大伙”汪惟贤十天以前就预备好了，

As soon as the carriages stopped, Hu and his guests, together with Gu Yingchun and Secretary Yang, who handled his correspondence, were ushered into the reception room.
车队一到，胡雪岩与他的客人，还有古应春与办笔墨的杨师爷，被接入客厅，

Four bright apprentices, specially chosen for the occasion, hurried about bringing water for washing and pouring tea.
特为挑出来的四名伶俐的学徒，倒脸水倒茶，忙个不停。

Wang attended to Hu himself, seeing to his needs while asking about the journey.
胡雪岩是汪惟贤亲自照料，一面伺候，一面问讯旅况。


When the bustle had subsided and they were seated, Baofu, one of Hu’s personal attendants, brought his gold water pipe and filled it for him.
乱过一阵，坐定下来，胡雪岩贴身小斯之一的保福，捧着金水烟袋来为胡雪岩装烟，

“Madam Zhang has already sent her maid to ask you to come in,” he murmured.
同时悄声说道：“张姨太已经打发丫头来催请了。”

“How can I possibly find time now?”
“现在哪里有工夫？”

Hu’s tone suggested that Madam Zhang ought to know better.
话中似嫌张姨娘不懂事。

Baofu said nothing.
保福不作声，

He merely waved a hand at a maid of sixteen or seventeen standing behind the screen, indicating that Hu could not come in yet.
只望着屏风后面一个十六七岁的丫头摇一摇手，表示胡雪岩还不能进去。

From south to north, in the great cities where Fukang had branches, Hu also maintained residences, most of them with a woman in charge.
由南到北，通都大邑中，有阜康钱庄，就有胡雪岩的一处“行馆”，大多有女主人，

Madam Zhang, the concubine who occupied the rear quarters of Fukangfu, was not a particular favourite; hence his tone.
住在阜康福后进的张姨娘，不甚得宠，所以胡雪岩有这种语气。


“Mr Hu,” Wang Weixian asked, “would you prefer Chinese food or Western food?” He promptly added a word about his own efforts.
“大先生，”汪惟贤来请示：“是用中菜，还是大菜。”紧接着又表功：

“I was afraid our two foreign guests might not take to Chinese dishes, so I specially borrowed a Western cook from His Excellency Wen.
“恐怕两位外国客人吃不来中菜，特为跟文大人借了个做大菜的厨子，

Everything is ready.”
都预备好了。”

His Excellency Wen was Wenyu, the Minister of Punishments, a Manchu of the Plain Blue Banner who had become Fuzhou Garrison General in the seventh year of Tongzhi.
所谓“文大人”指的是刑部尚书文煜，他是正蓝旗的满洲人，同治七年出任福州将军。

After the Qing forces entered the passes, garrison generals had been stationed at strategic points throughout the country.
清兵入关，在冲要之地没有驻防的将军坐镇，

The Fuzhou post was famously lucrative because its holder also supervised the Fujian Maritime Customs.
其中福州将军因为兼管闽海关之故，是有名的肥缺，

Wenyu had held it for ten years and accumulated a very substantial official purse; he had over a million deposited with Fukang.
文煜一干十年，官囊极丰，有上百万的款子，存在阜康。

Wang knew that he and Hu were old friends from Fuzhou and on excellent terms.
汪惟贤知道胡雪岩跟他是在福州的旧识，交情甚厚，

He had therefore felt free to borrow the cook Wenyu had brought from Fuzhou, who could prepare Western meals, to entertain Focke and Cameron.
所以不嫌冒昧，借了他从福州带来的会做大菜——西餐的厨子，来接待福克与凯密伦。

Since everything was ready, Western food it was.
既然预备好了，自然是吃大菜。

Hu had wanted to ask Wang several questions, but as they were both acting as hosts, Western custom placed them at opposite ends of the long dining table, making conversation difficult.
胡雪岩本有些话要问汪惟贤，但因他也是主人的身分，按西洋规矩，与汪惟贤分坐长餐桌的两端，不便交谈。

Only after dinner, when English-speaking clerks from Fukangfu took the two foreigners to see Dashilan, could Hu discuss business with Wang.
直到饭罢，两洋客由阜康福中会说英语的伙计陪着去观光大栅栏以后，胡雪岩才能跟汪椎贤谈正事。


The most important matter was the purpose of Hu’s journey: to discuss with Zuo Zongtang a loan of three or four million taels of silver.
正事中最要紧的一件，便是他此行的任务，跟左宗棠谈一笔三四百万两银子的借款。

Hu was eager to learn how Zuo had fared since entering the court.
胡雪岩急于想知道的是，左宗棠人朝以后的境遇，

Did he still enjoy the same great favour from the empress dowagers behind the curtain?
“帘眷”是否仍如以前之隆，

How did he stand with the two princes, Prince Gong, who headed the Grand Council, and Prince Chun, the Guangxu Emperor’s father?
与两王——掌枢的恭亲王及光绪皇帝的生父醇亲玉的关系，

What position did he hold within the Council itself?
以及在军机中的地位等等，

Hu needed to understand all this thoroughly before deciding what he could say and what he had better leave unmentioned.
必须了解得清清楚楚，他才能决定哪些话可以说，哪些事不必谈。

“I don’t reckon His Excellency Zuo will stop long in the capital,” Wang said.
“我看左大人在京里顿不长的。”

Wang too was from Hangzhou and spoke to his employer in their native dialect, using its local word for “stay long”.
汪惟贤也是杭州人，跟东家打乡谈，“待不长”称之为“顿不长”，

The remark gave Hu a sharp shock.
使得胡雪岩大吃一惊。

“Why won’t he stop long?”
“为啥顿不长？”

“His old temper has come out again: no amount of stewing will soften him, no amount of boiling will cook him.”
“还不是他的‘沃不烂，煮不熟’的老脾气又发作了。”

This was another Hangzhou expression, meaning obstinately determined to have one’s own way.
“沃不烂、煮不熟”也是杭州的俚语，有刚愎自用之意。

To support his opinion, Wang went on to describe Zuo’s conduct during Grand Council discussions of the Russian treaty and coastal defence.
接着，汪惟贤举左宗棠在军机处议俄约及“海防”一事，来支持他的看法。


When the Muslim uprising had begun in Xinjiang, Russia had sent troops to occupy Ili under the pretext of protecting its subjects.
原来新疆回民起义一起，俄国以保侨为名。出兵占领了伊犁，

It announced that the occupation was temporary and that Ili would be returned to China as soon as the uprising had been suppressed.
扬言暂时接管，回民起义一平，即当交还中国，

In his western campaign, Zuo Zongtang had recovered such important towns as Urumqi and Turfan, restoring order in succession on both sides of the Tianshan Mountains.
及至左宗棠西征，先后克复乌鲁木齐、吐鲁番等重镇，天山南北路次第平靖，

He then raised the question of recovering Ili and called on Russia to honour its promise.
开始议及规夏伊犁、要求俄国实践诺言，

Russia made one excuse after another, gradually revealing its true intention: to keep what it had supposedly borrowed.
而俄国推三阻四，久假不归的本意，逐渐暴露。

Zuo brought military pressure to bear, but neither side would yield.
于是左宗棠挟兵力以争，相持不下。

In the autumn of the fourth year of Guangxu, the court decided to seek a settlement through formal diplomacy.
这样到了光绪四年秋天，朝议决走循正式外交途径以求了结，

Chonghou, the Left Censor-in-Chief, was appointed imperial envoy to Russia and given the honorary title of Minister of the Inner Court.
特派左都御史崇厚为出使俄国饮差大臣，又赏内大臣衔，

As plenipotentiary for the treaty negotiations, he was authorized to act at his own discretion.
为与俄议约的全权大臣，许他便宜行事。

In the last lunar month of that year, Chonghou travelled through France and Germany to St Petersburg and immediately began negotiations with Giers, the Russian foreign minister.
这年腊月，崇厚取道法德两国，抵达俄京圣彼得堡，立即与俄国外务部尚书格尔思展开谈判。

After six months they reached an agreement.
谈了半年才定议，

Exercising his discretionary powers as plenipotentiary, Chonghou signed the Treaty of Livadia near the Black Sea.
而且崇厚以“便宜行事”的“全权大臣”资格，在黑海附近，签订了《里瓦几亚条约》，

It ceded the territory west and south of Ili to Russia, provided five million roubles in compensation for military expenses, opened several additional trading ports, and allowed Russian trade at Xi’an, Hanzhong and Hankou, as well as unrestricted trade along the Songhua River as far as Boduna.
内容是割伊犁以西以南之地予俄，偿付“兵费”五百万卢布，增开通商口岸多处，许俄人通商西安、汉中、汉口，以及松花江至伯都呐贸易自由。


When the news reached China, public opinion erupted, denouncing Chonghou for surrendering sovereign rights and humiliating the country.
消息传回国内，舆论大哗，痛责崇厚丧权辱国。

He had dared sign the treaty because two powerful men stood behind him.
而崇厚敢于订此条约，是因为背后有两个强有力的人在支持，

One was Grand Councillor Shen Guifen, leader of the Southern faction at court, who could hold his own against Li Hongzao, leader of the Northern faction, and enjoyed the deep confidence of both empress dowagers.
一个是军机大臣沈桂芬，他是朝中足以与“北派”领袖李鸿藻抗衡的“南派”领袖，深得两宫太后的信任。

The other was Li Hongzhang, Governor-General of Zhili and Beiyang Minister, who presented himself as the heir to Zeng Guofan’s mantle.
一个是直隶总督北洋大臣李鸿章，以继承曾国藩的衣钵标榜，

His influence in military and foreign affairs was firmly entrenched and difficult to shake.
在军务与洋务两方面的势力，已根深柢固，难以摇撼。

Throughout the six months of negotiations, Chonghou had regularly sought their advice by letter.
在议约的半年中，崇厚随时函商，

Only after receiving Shen’s and Li’s approval had he felt safe in signing.
获得沈、李二人的同意，才敢放心签约，

He had then set off home without first requesting an imperial command, leaving his counsellor, Shao Youlian, to serve as acting envoy.
而且未经请旨，即起程回国，留参赞邵友廉署理出使大臣。

Though Shen and Li both supported the Ili treaty, their motives differed.
沈桂芬、李鸿章虽都赞成伊犁条约而动机不同。

Shen believed that a continuing deadlock would oblige the court to maintain large forces on the frontier and Zuo to keep borrowing abroad.
沈桂芬是因为僵持的局面持续，朝廷即不能不派重兵防守，左宗棠的洋债就不能不借，

In time, the enormous military expenditure would impoverish the people and exhaust the treasury.
长此以往，浩繁的军费会搞得民穷财尽，

His intention could be described as accepting a compromise to preserve the greater good.
用心可说是委曲求全。

Li’s position was different and partly self-interested.
李鸿章就不同了，多少是有私心的，

First, if relations with Russia broke down and war followed, the Russian navy would surely attack Tianjin.
第一，如果中俄交恶而至于决裂，一旦开战，俄国出动海军，必攻天津，

As Beiyang Minister, he did not know what he could use to repel it.
身为北洋大臣的李鸿章，就不知道拿什么抵挡了，

Second, Zuo’s continual foreign borrowing to extend his power was hardly something Li welcomed.
其次，左宗棠不断借洋债扩充势力，自非李鸿章所乐见，

Once the Ili affair was settled and Zuo brought his army home, it would be virtually the same as laying down his arms and retiring.
伊犁事件一结束，左宗棠班师还朝，那就无异解甲归田了。


Unfortunately, Chonghou had conducted the negotiations so badly that both empress dowagers were furious and the scholarly world condemned him bitterly.
无奈崇厚的交涉办得实在不高明，两宫震怒，士林痛诋，

Even the ministers serving in the Zongli Yamen, the office for foreign affairs headed by Prince Gong and Shen Guifen, thought the concessions excessive and considered revision necessary.
连恭王与沈桂芬主持的总署——总理各国事务衙门行走的诸大臣，亦觉得过于委屈，有改议的必要。

The court accordingly removed Chonghou from his post as envoy to Russia for setting out home without awaiting an imperial order and referred him for severe disciplinary review.
于是朝命以出使俄国大臣崇厚不候谕旨，擅自启程回国的罪名，开缺交部严加议处。

The Russian treaty was submitted to the Six Boards, the Nine Ministers, the Hanlin and Heir Apparent’s establishments, and the supervising secretaries and censors, for careful deliberation and a joint report.
所议的俄约，交六部九卿、翰詹科道妥议具奏。

This was what the Ming dynasty had called a court deliberation.
这就是明朝的所谓“延议”。

Its conclusion was that not a single provision of Chonghou’s treaty could be accepted.
廷议的结果，崇厚所签的条约，无一可许，

The two empress dowagers therefore convened an imperial conference.
两宫因而召开“御前会议”，

Cixi already intended to punish him severely; now Baolian and Huang Tifang, two of the Four Remonstrants of the Hanlin, submitted fierce attacks on him that also touched on Li Hongzhang and Prince Gong.
慈禧太后原想严办崇厚，加以“翰林四谏”中的宝连与黄体芳，上奏力攻崇厚，而且语中侵及李鸿章与恭王，

Chonghou could no longer escape dismissal and arrest, followed by examination of his offences at the Ministry of Punishments.
这一来，崇厚便免不了革职拿问，交刑部议罪，

Though he was not thrown into prison in chains, he was confined in the heated room of the Ministry’s Prison Office.
虽非。 铛入狱， 而软禁在刑部提牢司的“火房”中，

It was easy to imagine how each day there must have seemed a year.
这度日如年的况味，也就可想而知了。


This treatment conflicted with international law, and the foreign envoys protested as one, but the court remained unmoved.
此举是抵触“万国公法”的，各国公使，群起抗议，但朝廷不为所动，

It appointed Zeng Jize, Zeng Guofan’s eldest son and currently imperial envoy to Britain and France, to serve concurrently as envoy to Russia and seek a revised treaty.
一面派使英兼使法的钦差大臣、曾国藩的长子曾纪泽兼使俄，谋求改约，

At the same time, Chonghou was sentenced to beheading, to await review in prison.
一面将崇厚走了“斩监”的罪名。

Yet the court had not abandoned hope of a peaceful settlement.
不过，朝廷亦并未放弃和平解决的意愿，

Alongside its preparations for war, Li Hongzhang induced the British, French and German envoys to mediate.
备战以外，由李鸿章策动英、法、德三国公使，出面调停，

Chonghou’s death sentence was remitted, though he remained confined.
免了崇厚的死刑，但仍监禁，

Only then, in the sixth lunar month of the sixth year of Guangxu, did Zeng leave London for Russia to revise Chonghou’s treaty.
然后曾纪泽才在光绪六年六月，由伦敦动身赴俄，修订崇约。

The negotiations were bound to be difficult at first.
交涉开始之时不会顺利，是可想而知的。

Fortunately, Zeng proved worthy of his distinguished father.
幸而曾纪泽不愧名父之子，

Drawing on his knowledge of international law, his diplomatic experience and his connections, he enlisted the help of the British and French envoys in Russia.
运用他对“万国公法”的知识、出使的经验及关系，促请英、法驻俄公使的协助，

Two days before Zuo Zongtang reached Beijing, he and Giers agreed on a revised draft: Ili would be recovered; trade through Jiayu Pass would be allowed without specifying how far inland it might extend; navigation on the Songhua River would be cancelled.
在左宗棠到京的前两天，与格尔思改走了约稿，伊犁收回，嘉峪通商，不明定可通至某处，松花江通航取消，

The compensation for military expenses, however, was increased by four million roubles, making nine million in all.
只是赔偿“军费”增加四百万卢布，共为九百万。


During the tension with Russia, Li Hongzhang had advocated coastal defence in direct opposition to Zuo Zongtang’s emphasis on land defence.
当中俄关系紧张时，李鸿章提出“海防论”的主张，与左宗棠的“陆防论”针峰相对。

When Zuo arrived in Beijing and entered the Grand Council, the Russian treaty was discussed first.
及至左宗棠到京入军机，先议俄约，

Zeng Jize had recovered more rights and interests than either the court or the public had expected, so Zuo too expressed satisfaction, and there was little dispute.
由于曾纪泽挽回利权之多，超过朝野的期望，左宗棠亦表示满意，无甚争执，

But when discussion turned to Li’s coastal-defence plans, he had much more to say.
后议李鸿章“海防”的计划，他的话就多了，

From coastal defence he moved to land defence, then to conditions on the western frontier and the story of his campaigns in Xinjiang.
由海防谈到陆防，一转而为西睡的形势，与他在新疆用兵的经过，

He talked endlessly, as though nobody else mattered.
滔滔不绝，目无余子，

In the Grand Council, only his voice and laughter could be heard.
军机处只听得他一个人又说又笑，

Prince Gong, whose rank placed him above all the officials, sat silently for two to four hours at a stretch without being able to get in a word.
“礼绝百僚”的恭王，默坐一两个时辰，连句话都插不上。

“Just think, Mr Hu,” Wang said.
“大先生你想，”汪惟贤说：

“Never mind Prince Gong; nobody could stand it.
“不要说恭王，哪个都吃不消他。

The prince endured it again and again, until at last he simply told the Council secretaries to put the original memorial away.
恭王忍了又忍，忍到后来，索性要军机章京把原折收了起来，

They would discuss it no further.”
不议了。”

“No further?”
“不议了？”

Hu was astonished.
胡雪岩诧异：

“Li of Hefei has enormous plans for coastal defence!
“李合肥的海防，规模大得很呢！

There’s the Beiyang Fleet and the Telegraph Bureau to be set up.
要开办北洋舰队、电报局，

So many people are waiting for a bite of that rich meat.
多少人等着吃这块大肥肉，

How can it all end with a few words and no more discussion?”
哪里就说说算数，不议了？”

“There,” Wang lowered his voice, “is the trouble.
“喏，”汪惟贤放低了声音说：“毛病就出在这里，

They have to discuss it, but if they do, they’re afraid our His Excellency Zuo will monopolize the conversation with empty talk.
不议不可以，要议又怕我们左大人独讲空话。

The only way is to lure the tiger away from the mountain before they resume.”
那就只有调虎离了山再议。”

Hu’s hopes sank halfway at these words.
一听这话，胡雪岩心冷了一半。

With a powerful patron like Zuo on the Grand Council, he had assumed that Zuo would inevitably take charge of future foreign loans, bringing an endless stream of profit.
原以为有左宗棠这样一座靠山当大军机，将来要借洋债，必然由他来主持，财源滚滚不绝。

Now Zuo seemed likely to be sent out to a provincial post again, and Hu’s expectations would come to nothing.
如今看样子怕又要外放，自己的想法也就落空了。

Worse still, Prince Gong appeared to dislike him.
而且恭王似乎有些讨厌左宗棠，

That did not bode well.
此事颇为不妙，

But how did Prince Chun treat him?
只不知醇王待他如何？


“Prince Chun treats him well.
“醇王待他是好的。

You know he has a taste for military affairs, Mr Hu.
大先生晓得的，醇王是好武的一伙，

With His Excellency Zuo’s military achievements, he regards him as a hero.
左大人有这样的战功，拿他当个英雄看，

As they say, men of spirit appreciate one another.
所谓惺惺相惜，

They visit often and are very close.
常常有往来，走得很近的，

The prince has even invited him to watch the drills at Shenji Camp.”
醇王还要请他到神机营去看操呢！”

“What did you say?” Hu asked.
“你说啥！”胡雪岩问道：

“Prince Chun has invited His Excellency Zuo to watch the drills at Shenji Camp?”
“醇王请左大人到神机营看操！”

“Yes.”
“是啊。”

“Who told you that?”
“你听哪个说的？”

The question carried a note of disbelief.
这话有不相信的意味，

It was also clear that Hu considered the matter important.
而且看得出来，胡雪岩很重视这件事，

Unable to fathom his reaction, Wang could only answer truthfully.
汪惟贤倒有些猜不透，只好据实作答。

“Mr Xu, the ‘junior grand councillor’, told me.
“我是听‘小军机’徐老爷说的。”汪惟贤又说：

His Excellency Zuo arrived at the end of the first month.
“左大人是正月底到京的，

Early in the second, Prince Chun invited him to dinner and to see the newly completed garden at Taiping Lake.
