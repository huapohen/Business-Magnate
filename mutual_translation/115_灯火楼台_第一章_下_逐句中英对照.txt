胡雪岩
《灯火楼台》第一章（下）
Towers of Light — Chapter One, Part Two
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


贤良寺本来是雍正朝抬贤亲王的故居，
Xianliang Temple had originally been the residence of Prince Yixian in the Yongzheng reign.

屋宇精洁、花木扶疏，而且离东华门很近，上朝方便，
Its buildings were clean and elegant, its grounds thick with flowers and trees, and its proximity to Donghua Gate made attendance at court convenient.

所以封疆大吏入觐述职，都爱住在这里。
Provincial governors and governors-general therefore liked to stay there when summoned to report to the emperor.

左宗棠下榻之处，是其中最大的一个院落，另外开门出入，
Zuo Zongtang occupied the largest courtyard, with a separate entrance.

门口站着七八名壮汉，服饰随便，举止粗率，形似斯养卒，
Seven or eight burly men stood at the gate, casually dressed and rough in manner, looking like ordinary menials or soldiers.

但古应春却丝毫不敢怠慢。
Gu Yingchun nevertheless dared not treat them lightly.

原来左宗棠镇压洪杨、捻回，二十年指挥过无数战役，
Over twenty years of suppressing the Taipings, the Nian, and the Muslim rebellions, Zuo had commanded countless battles.

麾下将校，百战余生，从军功上保到总兵，提督的，不知凡几？
Many officers who survived those campaigns had won promotion through military merit to brigade commander or provincial military commander.

但武人诚朴，
But soldiers were straightforward and loyal.

颇有不愿赴任，而宁愿跟着左杀棠当差官，出入相从，
A good number preferred not to take up their appointments, choosing instead to remain with Zuo as officers on special duty, accompanying him wherever he went.

不说破不知道他们都有红顶子，黄马褂，甚至双眼花翎。
Unless someone told you, you would never guess that they were entitled to red hat buttons, yellow riding jackets, and even double-eyed peacock feathers.


一次，有个何总兵奉左宗棠之命，去见陕西藩司谈公事。
Once, Zuo sent a brigade commander named He to discuss official business with the provincial treasurer of Shaanxi.

这个藩司是满洲的世家子，架子极大，
The treasurer came from an established Manchu family and put on tremendous airs.

平时视部属如仆从，呼来喝去，视作当然，因而都敬鬼神而远之。
He treated his subordinates as servants, ordering them about as a matter of course, so they kept a respectful distance.

此人本来对外事不大明白，加以部下疏远，对各方面的情形，更加隔膜，
He already knew little of affairs outside his office, and their remoteness made him still more ignorant.

不知道何总兵的来头，不过看在左宗棠的分上，接见时以平礼相待。
Unaware of Commander He’s standing, he received him as an equal only out of regard for Zuo.

只是心里有个想法：我是敬其上而重其下，你就该守着你的规矩，要谦虚客气才是。
Inwardly he thought: I honor the subordinate out of respect for his superior; you should know your place and show some humility in return.

不道何总兵全不理会，
He, however, paid no heed to any of this.

“升炕”就升炕！“上坐”就上坐，
Invited onto the raised couch, he took his seat; offered the place of honor, he accepted it.

而且翘起二郎腿，高谈阔论旁若无人。
Crossing his legs, he talked freely, paying no heed to anyone else.

藩司心里已很讨厌了，
The treasurer was already deeply annoyed.

及至“端茶”送客，何总兵昂然直出中门，将藩司抛任身后，竟似以长官自居了。
When he lifted his teacup to signal the end of the visit, He strode grandly out through the central doorway, leaving his host behind as though he were the senior official.

是可忍，孰不可忍？
This was intolerable.

藩司震怒之余，第二天谒见左宗棠时，谈及此事，惯愤不平之意，还现于词色。
The next day, when the treasurer called on Zuo, indignation still showed in his voice and face as he recounted the affair.

左宗棠笑一笑，将何总兵传了来训斥，他说：
Zuo smiled, summoned He, and rebuked him.

“你们自以为都出生入死，立过战功，在我面前随意坐卧淡笑，
“You men think that because you have risked your lives and earned distinction in battle, you may sit, lounge, talk, and laugh freely in my presence.

固无不可。
That is all very well with me.

藩台大人是朝廷大员，体制何等尊贵，
But His Excellency the Treasurer is a great officer of the court, whose position commands the highest respect.

你怎么可以放肆，当是在我面前一样，
How dare you behave as freely with him as you do with me?

何以这样不自量。
You have no sense of your place.

你现在赶快给藩台磕头陪罪，
Kowtow and apologize at once.

不然藩台发了脾气，我亦没有这张脸替你再求情。
Otherwise, if His Excellency loses his temper, I shall have no standing to plead for you.”

何总兵答应一声，跪倒在地，磕头请罪。
He obeyed, kneeling and kowtowing.

过了一会，左宗棠送客，藩司一出中门就看到十几个红顶花翎黄马褂的武官手扶腰刀在那里站班，
Presently, when Zuo saw the treasurer out, the man passed through the central doorway and found a dozen or so military officers standing in attendance, hands on their sword hilts, all wearing red buttons, peacock feathers, and yellow jackets.

其中有一个就是何总兵。
Commander He was among them.

这一下，头上蓝顶子，脑后只有一条辫子的藩司，大惊失色，手足无措。
The treasurer, whose own hat button was blue and who wore no peacock feather, turned pale in alarm and scarcely knew what to do.

还算见机，定定神伛偻着身子，一一请安招呼，
Recovering enough presence of mind, he bent deferentially and saluted each in turn.

步行到辕门外，方始上轿，但己汗透重棉了。
He walked all the way beyond the outer gate before entering his chair, by which time sweat had soaked his thick padded clothes.


古应春从听说这个笑话以后，就不敢小看这些“老粗”们，
Ever since hearing that amusing story, Gu had known better than to look down on these rough soldiers.

当时陪笑问道：“大人回来了？”
He therefore asked with a smile, “Has His Excellency returned?”

其时有个差官认识古应春，上前接话，
One of the officers recognized him and came forward.

“我们大人刚回来。”他说：
“He has just returned.

“胡大先生陪着洋人早就到了，派人出来问过你两次，
Mr Hu arrived with the foreigners some time ago and has sent out twice to ask for you.

赶快请进去吧！”
Please hurry in.”

到得花厅，见了胡雪岩，还来不及叙话，只见角门已开，闪出来两名差官，
Gu reached the reception hall and found Hu, but before they could speak, a side door opened and two officers appeared.

知道左宗棠要来了，当即招呼两名洋人站起来迎接。
Knowing that Zuo was coming, Gu at once asked the two foreigners to rise.

左宗棠自然是便衣，一件旧薄棉袍，头上是兰州织呢厂所出的一顶鼻烟色的毡帽。
Zuo was naturally in informal dress: an old, lightly padded robe and a snuff-colored felt hat made by the Lanzhou woolen mill.

胡雪岩跟古应春自然磕头请安，洋人则是一鞠躬，然后又跟左宗某拉手。
Hu and Gu kowtowed, while the foreigners bowed and then shook hands with him.

于是左宗棠独坐，
Zuo alone took a seat.

问了些“哪天到的”、“路上如何”、“江南有什么新闻”之类的话，
He asked when they had arrived, how the journey had gone, and what news there was from Jiangnan.

胡雪岩一一照答，一阵寒暄过后，谈入正题。
Hu answered each question, and after these preliminary courtesies they turned to business.


正题是借洋债。
The business was a foreign loan.

胡雪岩自同治五年至光绪四年，为左宗棠借过四次外债，以充“西饷”。
Between the fifth year of Tongzhi and the fourth year of Guangxu, Hu had arranged four foreign loans for Zuo to finance the western campaigns.

西睡用兵，需由各省补助军饷，称为“协响”。
Military operations on the western frontier depended on contributions from other provinces, known as supplementary military funds.

但协饷分年解送，
But those contributions arrived year by year.

而打仗不能说今年饷银用完，不打了，明年有了饷再打。
A war could not be stopped merely because the year’s money had run out, to be resumed when next year’s allocation arrived.

时而胡雪岩想出一个借洋债的办法，最大的“银主”是英商汇丰银行。
Hu had therefore devised a system of foreign borrowing, with HSBC as the principal lender.

还款的方式是由江海关开出期票，
Repayment was arranged through dated bills issued by Jianghaiguan Customs.

而由协饷省分，主要的是江苏、浙江、广东、福建四省的督抚、盖上大印，表示承诺在到期以前，将协饷解交江海关，偿还洋商。
The governors or governors-general of the contributing provinces—chiefly Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Guangdong, and Fujian—affixed their official seals, undertaking to remit their contributions to Jianghaiguan before the bills fell due so that the foreign lenders could be repaid.

年限总在六年上下，半年一期，付息拔本。
The term was generally about six years, with interest and principal paid in installments every six months.

方式是由胡雪岩秉承左宗棠的意思，找洋商谈妥细节，然后由左宗棠出奏。
Hu first negotiated the details with the foreigners in accordance with Zuo’s wishes; Zuo then submitted a memorial.

奏准后，以上谕饬协饷冬省出具印票，并江海关，
Once imperial approval had been obtained, an edict directed the contributing provinces to furnish sealed bills through Jianghaiguan.

同时由总理衙门照会英闰公使，转知贷款的汇丰银行照付。
At the same time, the Zongli Yamen notified the British minister, who instructed the lending bank to release the money.


这套手续很繁琐，其中还有两道关口，
This procedure was cumbersome, and two further barriers stood in the way.

一道是总税务司赫德。
One was Hart, the Inspector-General of Customs.

根据中英条约，关税是用来赔偿鸦片战争失败军费的保证，
Under the Sino-British treaties, customs revenue served as security for the indemnity arising from China’s defeat in the Opium War.

因此英国人要求控制中国新开各口岩，称为“洋关”的海关，
The British therefore demanded control of the customs offices at the newly opened ports, known as the foreign customs.

职称是税务司，都归总税务司赫德管辖，
Their commissioners were all under Hart.

赫德不下命令，江海关税务司不肯出票，钱就借不成了。
Without his orders, the commissioner at Jianghaiguan would not issue the bills, and the loan could not proceed.

再一道关口是英国驻华公使，
The other barrier was the British minister in China.

没有他的核准，汇丰银行不能拔款，
Without his approval, the bank could not release the funds.

有他批准了，即等于英国政府担保汇丰银行不会吃倒帐。
His approval effectively amounted to a British government guarantee that the bank would not suffer a default.

赫德还好，
Hart was comparatively easy to manage.

因为他毕竟是中国的客卿，不能不买总理衙门的帐，
After all, he was a foreigner in Chinese official service and could not disregard the Zongli Yamen.

而且有回佣好分，亦愿乐观其成。
There was also commission to be shared, so he was willing to see the business succeed.

但英国公使这一关很罗嗦，
The British minister was far more troublesome.

哪怕上谕批准了，各省的印票也备齐了，总理衙门跟赫德也说好了，没有英国公使点头，饯仍旧借不到。
Even with an imperial edict, all the provinces’ sealed documents, and an understanding between the Zongli Yamen and Hart, the money remained unavailable until he gave his assent.


以左宗棠天马行空的性格，这当然是件不能容忍的事，
To a man of Zuo’s unfettered temperament, this was intolerable.

中国人借洋债，要做中国官的英国人赫德同意，更起反感。
He especially resented the idea that Chinese borrowing required the consent of Hart, an Englishman serving as a Chinese official.

因此当德国泰来洋行的经理福克，向左宗棠表示，有钱可错，手续可以节减许多，左宗棠自然是欢迎的。
Naturally, therefore, he welcomed an offer from Focke, manager of the German firm Tailai Trading House, who said that money was available with much less formality.

福克之得以谒见左宗棠，出于胡雪岩的推荐，那是一年前的话，
Hu had introduced Focke to Zuo a year earlier.

西陲已经平定，左宗棠准备在陕甘大兴实业，
The western frontier had been pacified, and Zuo was preparing to develop industry in Shaanxi and Gansu.

关照胡雪岩招聘技师，胡雪岩找上了福克。
Asked to recruit technicians, Hu had approached Focke.

在哈密行营一席之谈，左宗棠认为福克“切实而有条理”，颇为欣赏，
During a conversation at the Hami field headquarters, Zuo found him practical and methodical and formed a favorable impression.

福克便抓住机会，为德国资本找出路，
Focke seized the opportunity to find an outlet for German capital.

当然，要谈这笔借款，仍旧需要胡雪岩。
Hu’s participation, of course, was still necessary to negotiate the loan.

当时正是崇厚擅自订约，被捕下狱，中俄关系搞得剑拔弩张之时，
At that time Chonghou had been arrested and imprisoned for signing a treaty without authorization, and Sino-Russian relations were dangerously tense.

左宗棠接到一个情报，说俄国举了一笔“国债”达五千二百万两之巨，用来扩充军备，
Zuo received intelligence that Russia had raised a national loan equivalent to fifty-two million taels to expand its armaments.

认为中俄难免一战，将来兵连祸结，其势难以停止，亦须未雨绸缪，
Believing war between China and Russia almost inevitable, and fearing that once begun it might continue indefinitely, he felt obliged to prepare.

如果能惜二三千万银子，分数十年偿还，则饷源一广，练兵必精，
A loan of twenty or thirty million taels, repayable over several decades, would provide abundant funds and allow the troops to be thoroughly trained.

写信给胡雪岩，要他跟泰来洋行谈判，而且约他在开年灯节以后，进京面谈。
He wrote to Hu, asking him to negotiate with Tailai and to come to Beijing after the Lantern Festival early in the new year.

不久，这件事打消了，
Before long the proposal was dropped.

因为由于曾纪泽斡旋，中俄形势已趋缓和，没有再大举外债的理由。
Through Zeng Jize’s diplomacy, relations with Russia had eased, and there was no longer any reason for such extensive foreign borrowing.


这是第一遍。
That was the first proposal.

第二遍旧事重提，又要借了。
Then the matter was revived, and borrowing was again required.

原来左宗棠内召入关进军机时，奉旨将他的一差一缺，分别交卸，
When Zuo was recalled within the passes to join the Grand Council, he was ordered to hand over both his special commission and his substantive office.

一差是“钦差大臣督办新疆军务”，交由刘锦棠接替，一缺是“陕甘总督”交由杨昌浚署理。
Liu Jintang would succeed him as Imperial Commissioner Supervising Military Affairs in Xinjiang; Yang Changjun would act as Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu.

刘、杨都是左宗棠麾下的大将，但资望不足，难当重任，
Both were senior commanders under Zuo, but neither had sufficient standing and prestige to bear these responsibilities easily.

陕甘贫瘠，全靠各省协饷，
Shaanxi and Gansu were poor and depended entirely on supplementary funds from the other provinces.

各省如果不买帐，刘、杨就一筹奠展，
If those provinces refused to cooperate, Liu and Yang would be helpless.

因此，左宗棠必须为刘锦棠、杨昌浚筹好了饷，西征的功绩，才算有了着落。
Only by securing their military funding could Zuo make the achievements of his western campaigns lasting.

照左宗棠的盘算，新疆与陕甘以玉门关为界，
He calculated the requirements on either side of Yumen Pass, which divided Xinjiang from Shaanxi and Gansu.

每年关外军饷要三百七十万，关内二百一十万，全年为五百八十万两。
The army beyond the pass needed 3.7 million taels a year; the forces within it needed 2.1 million, making 5.8 million in all.

光绪五年起，上谕各省协饷，必须解足五百万两，
From the fifth year of Guangxu, imperial edicts had required the provinces to remit a full five million annually.

相差八十万，前后套搭，总还可敷衍得过，
The gap of eight hundred thousand could still be bridged by shifting funds between payments.

哪知上谕归上谕，协饷归协饷，
But the edicts were one thing and the remittances another.

两年之间，各省协饷欠解竟达四百二十万两之巨。
In two years, provincial arrears had reached 4.2 million taels.

为此，刘锦棠忧心忡忡，
Liu was deeply troubled.

左宗棠为他出奏陈情说：“不虞兵机之迟钝，而忧饷事之难难，
Zuo memorialized on his behalf: “His concern is not that military operations will falter, but that funding will prove difficult.

深惧仔肩难卸，掣时堪虞，
He fears that the burden of responsibility cannot be discharged while his efforts are continually hampered.

将来恼不应手，必致上负圣恩，悔已无及。”
Should his plans prove impossible to carry out, he will fail the imperial trust, and regret will come too late.”

这也是实在情形，
These were real difficulties.

即令宝鋆表示：“西饷可缓，洋款不必着急。”朝廷仍旧许他再借一笔外债，弥补协饷之不足。
Although Grand Secretary Bao Yun argued that “the western army’s funding can wait; there is no need to hurry over a foreign loan,” the court nevertheless authorized further borrowing to make up the shortfall.

胡雪岩与福克，就是为这件事来的。
It was this business that had brought Hu and Focke to Beijing.


胡雪岩在左宗棠面前的信用，大不如前了。
Hu’s standing with Zuo was much lower than before.

一则是借洋债及商款的利息过重，人言藉藉，连左宗棠都没面子，
For one thing, the heavy interest on foreign loans and funds borrowed from merchants had provoked widespread criticism and embarrassed Zuo himself.

二则是采买军火有浮报情事。
For another, there had been inflated charges in the purchase of arms.

但左宗棠仍旧少不了胡雪岩，而胡雪岩亦想力盖前愆，
Yet Zuo still could not dispense with Hu, and Hu wished to make amends.

对这趟借洋债，格外尽心尽力，希望左宗棠能对他的成绩满意。
He was taking exceptional pains over this loan, hoping that the result would satisfy his patron.

“雪岩，你信上说票要出给汇丰，
“Xueyan, your letter says that the bills must be made out to HSBC.

怎么又是汇丰呢？”
Why that bank again?”

左宗棠指着福克说：
Zuo pointed to Focke.

“不是他们泰来洋行吗？”
“Isn’t the money coming from their Tailai firm?”

“是。
“Yes.

一大半是泰来的款子，不过要由汇丰出面。”
More than half comes from Tailai, but the bank must act as intermediary.”

“这是什么讲究？”
“Why is that?”

“汇丰是洋商的领袖。
“The bank is the leader among the foreign firms.

要它出面，款子调度起来才容易。
With it taking the lead, the funds can be assembled more easily.

这好有一比。
Let me give you a comparison.

好比刘饮差、杨制台筹饷筹不动，只要大人登高一呼，马上万山响应，
Commissioner Liu and Governor-General Yang may have difficulty raising military funds, but if Your Excellency calls from the heights, ten thousand mountains answer.

是一样的道理。
It is the same principle.”

“左宗棠平生一癖，是喜欢人恭维，听胡雪岩这一说，心里很舒服，
Zuo had a lifelong weakness for flattery, and this pleased him greatly.

“雪岩，”他说：“你这一阵子倚红偎翠之余，想来还读读书吧？”
“Xueyan, between your embraces of the fair sex, I take it you have been doing a little reading?”

这话，想来是指着“登高一呼”、“万山响应”这两句话而说的。
Hu supposed that the phrases about calling from the heights and the answering mountains had prompted the remark.

胡雪岩笑着答道：
He smiled.

“大人太夸奖我了，
“Your Excellency is too generous.

哪里谈得到读书？
I could hardly call it reading.

无非上次大人教导我，闲下来看看，《唐诗三百首》，
Following your advice last time, I look through Three Hundred Tang Poems when I am free.

现在总算平仄也有点懂了，王黄也分得清了。”
I now understand a little about level and oblique tones, and can even tell wang and huang apart.”


“居然平仄也懂了，
“You even understand the tones now?

难得，难得。”
Remarkable, remarkable!”

左宗棠转脸看着福克说：
Zuo turned to Focke.

“我本来打算借三百万，
“I originally intended to borrow three million.

你一定要我多借一百万，我也许了你了，
You insisted on my borrowing another million, and I agreed.

你利息上头，应该格外克己才是。”
You ought to be particularly accommodating over the interest.”

古应春司翻译之责，
Gu served as interpreter.

福克与凯密伦各有所言，及至他再翻给左宗棠听时，已非洋人原来的话了。
Focke and Cameron each had something to say, but what Gu translated back to Zuo was no longer what the foreigners had actually said.

福克的回答是：“不早就谈好了吗？”
Focke’s answer was, “Wasn’t that agreed long ago?”

经古应春翻给左宗棠听是：“一钱一分。”
What Gu rendered for Zuo was, “Eleven percent.”

“还是高了。”
“Still too high.”

左宗棠的话刚完，胡雪岩便即接口：“是不是？”
No sooner had Zuo spoken than Hu put in, “There!

他向古应春说：“我早说大人不会答应的。
