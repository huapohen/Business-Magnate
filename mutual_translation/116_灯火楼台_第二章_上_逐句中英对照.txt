胡雪岩
《灯火楼台》第二章（上）
Towers of Light — Chapter Two, Part One
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第二章 深宫疑云｜CHAPTER TWO: MISGIVINGS IN THE INNER PALACE


左宗棠只睡得两个时辰，刚交子时便让老仆左贵推醒了，
Zuo Zongtang had slept for only four hours when, just after eleven that night, his old servant Zuo Gui shook him awake.

告诉他说：“军机徐老爷有急信。”
“An urgent letter from Mr Xu at the Grand Council.”

说着，将左宗棠扶了起来，另有一仆擎着烛台，照着他看信，
He helped Zuo sit up while another servant held a candlestick for him to read by.

信封上浓墨淋漓地写着：“飞递左爵相亲钩启”，
In thick, dripping ink, the envelope was addressed: “Express. To Marquis Zuo, Grand Secretary, for his personal attention.”

抽出信笺，上面只有八个字：“东朝上宾，请速入宫。”
The letter contained only eight Chinese characters: “The Eastern Empress Dowager has died. Enter the palace at once.”

原来这天军机章京换班，徐用仪值夜，所以消息来得快。
The Grand Council secretaries had changed shifts that day, and Xu Yongyi was on night duty, which was why the news had come so quickly.

左宗棠遇到这种意外变故，最能沉得住气，
Unexpected emergencies brought out Zuo’s greatest composure.

下床看到红烛，便指着说道：
Getting out of bed, he pointed at the red candle.

“明天得换白的。”
“Tomorrow that must be changed for a white one.”

“老爷，”左贵服伺左宗棠多年，称呼一直未改，他怕自己听错了，侧耳问道：“换白蜡？”
Zuo Gui had served him for many years and had never altered his form of address. Afraid that he had misheard, he bent an ear towards him. “Master, a white candle?”

“对了。
“Yes.

这会别多问！
No questions now.

传轿，
Order the sedan chair.

我马上进宫。”
I am going to the palace immediately.”


进宫时为丑正，
It was two in the morning when he entered the palace.

乾清门未开，都在内务府朝房聚集，
The Gate of Heavenly Purity was still shut, and everyone had gathered in the court waiting rooms of the Imperial Household Department.

左宗棠一看，近支亲贵有惇亲王、醇亲王、惠亲王，御前大臣有伯彦讷漠诂、奕劻，军机大臣有宝鋆、李鸿藻、王文韶，此外便是六部尚书、“毓庆宫行走”的师傅、南书房翰林。
Zuo saw the close imperial kinsmen Princes Dun, Chun, and Hui; the ministers in imperial attendance Boyannemohu and Yikuang; Grand Councillors Bao Yun, Li Hongzao, and Wang Wenshao; and, besides these, the presidents of the Six Ministries, the imperial tutors serving in the Palace of Nurturing Happiness, and the Hanlin scholars of the Southern Study.

国家大事，权在军机，
Authority over great affairs of state rested with the Grand Council.

军机领班的恭王不在，便该左宗棠为首。
In the absence of its head, Prince Gong, Zuo should take the lead.

他此刻才发觉自己的地位特殊，
Only now did he recognize the peculiar difficulty of his position.

初次当京官，朝中典故，茫然莫晓，
This was his first appointment in the capital, and he knew nothing of court precedents.

且又遇着这样意想不到的情况，虽说他善能应变，亦有手足无措、尴尬万分之感。
Faced with such an unforeseen event, even his gift for handling emergencies could not prevent him from feeling helpless and deeply embarrassed.

正要开口动问，只见徐用仪疾趋而前，借搀扶的机会，贴身说道：“听宝中堂的。”
He was about to ask a question when Xu hurried forward and, under cover of supporting him, whispered close to his body, “Follow Grand Secretary Bao.”

争强好胜的左宗棠，到此亦不能不退让一步，
Even Zuo, competitive and eager to prevail as he was, had to yield on this occasion.

与三王略略招呼后，向宝鋆拱拱手说：
After briefly greeting the three princes, he clasped his hands towards Bao Yun.

“我初遇大丧，
“This is my first great state mourning.

军机职司何事，都请佩翁主持。”
Please take charge of all that falls to the Grand Council, Mr Peiheng.”

“这是责无旁贷的事。”
“It is a duty I cannot shirk.”


一语未毕，有人来报，乾清门开了。
Before Bao Yun had finished, someone announced that the Gate of Heavenly Purity was open.

于是惇王领头，入乾清门先到“内奏事处”。章奏出纳，皆经此处，
Prince Dun led the way through it to the Inner Memorial Office, through which all memorials passed in and out.

照规矩帝后违和，派案药方亦存内奏事处，王公大臣谁都可以看的。
By established practice, when an emperor or empress was ill, the medical records and prescriptions were also kept there, available for princes and ministers to inspect.

药方一共五张，
There were five prescriptions.

最后一张注明“西刻”，是左宗棠出宫以后请脉所开的，
The last, marked with the You hour, had been written after Zuo left the palace.

说是“六脉将脱，
It stated: “The pulses at all six positions are on the point of failing.

药不能下。”
Medicine cannot be swallowed.”

“宾天是什么时候？”惇王在问。
“At what time did she pass away?” Prince Dun asked.

“戌时。”
“At the Xu hour.”

戍时是晚上八点钟。
That meant eight in the evening.

左宗棠心里在想，接到徐用仪的信是十一点钟，计算他得知消息不会早于十点钟，
Zuo reflected that he had received Xu’s letter at eleven, and Xu could not have learned the news before ten.

相隔两个钟头，
Two hours had elapsed.

在这段辰光之中，不知道钟粹宫中是何境况？
What had happened during that interval in the Palace of Accumulated Purity?

“大人！”
“Your Excellency.”

徐用仪牵着他的袖子说：
Xu tugged at his sleeve.

“请到南书房。”
“Please come to the Southern Study.”

宫中定制，凡有大丧，都以乾清门内西边的南书房为“治丧办事处”。
Whenever there was a great imperial bereavement, palace rules made the Southern Study, west of the Gate of Heavenly Purity on its inner side, the funeral arrangements office.

一到了那里，第一件事便是将宫帽上的顶戴与红缨子都摘了下来，
On arrival, the first thing everyone did was remove the buttons and red tassels from their official hats.

然后各自按爵位官阶大小，找适当的座位坐下来。
They then took seats according to their titles and ranks.

“真是想不到！”醇王向宝鋆说道：
“Who could have expected this?” Prince Chun said to Bao Yun.

“得赶紧把六爷追回来。”
“We must get Sixth Brother back at once.”

“六爷”是指恭王，
He meant Prince Gong.

“已经派人去了。”宝鋆答说：
“Someone has already gone,” Bao Yun replied.

“大概明天下午才能回来。”
“He probably cannot return until tomorrow afternoon.”

“得找个人来问一问才好。”惇王说道：
“We ought to find someone to question,” Prince Dun said.

“譬如有没有遗言？”
“Whether she left any last words, for instance.”

“不会有的。”惠王接口：
“She could not have,” Prince Hui answered.

“中午的方子已经说‘神识不清’，
“The noon prescription already said she was unconscious.

以后牙关都撬不开，
Later they could not even prise her jaws apart.

怎么能开口说话？”
How could she speak?”

惇王默然，举座不语，
Prince Dun fell silent, and no one else said a word.

但每人心里都有一个疑问：到底是什么病？
Yet the same question was in everyone’s mind: what had her illness been?


“要问什么病，实在没有病。”
“If you ask what the illness was, there was really no illness at all.”

徐用仪左右看了一下，
Xu glanced about him.

下人都在廊上，客厅中除了胡雪岩的贴身跟班以外，别无闲人，
The servants were in the corridor; apart from Hu’s personal attendant, no outsiders remained in the reception room.

方始低声说道：
Only then did he lower his voice.

“是中了毒。”
“She was poisoned.”

此言一出，胡雪岩跟古应春互看了一眼。
Hu and Gu looked at each other.

原来胡雪岩因为创设胡庆余堂药号，自然而然地对药性医道，都不太外行，
Since founding Hu Qingyu Hall, Hu had naturally acquired some knowledge of medicines and medical practice.

看了从内奏事处抄出来的五张药方，又打听了慈安太后前一日御朝的情形，向古应春谈起，唯一可能的死因是中毒。
Having examined copies of the five prescriptions from the Inner Memorial Office and enquired how Empress Dowager Ci’an had appeared at court the previous day, he had told Gu that poisoning was the only possible cause of death.

此刻是证实了，
To him, Xu’s account now confirmed it.

只不知如何中的毒。
What he still did not know was how the poison had been given.

“毒是下在点心里头的。”徐用仪说：
“It was put in a cake,” Xu said.

“东太后有歇午党的习惯，睡醒以后，经常要吃甜点心。
“The Eastern Empress Dowager was accustomed to taking a nap at midday and often ate something sweet after waking.

初九那天，午觉醒来，西太后派梳头太监李莲英，进了一盘松仁百果蜜糕，
On the ninth, the Western Empress Dowager sent her hairdressing eunuch Li Lianying with a plate of honey cakes made with pine nuts and assorted dried fruits.

刚蒸出来又香又甜，
Fresh from the steamer, they were fragrant and sweet.

东太后一连吃了三块，不到半个钟头，病就发作了。”
The Eastern Empress Dowager ate three in succession, and within half an hour the illness began.”

胡雪岩骇然，
Hu was horrified.

“是西太后下的毒？”他问：
“The Western Empress Dowager poisoned her?

“为什么呢？”
Why?”

“这话说来就长了……”
“That is a long story…”


慈禧太后一直有桩耿耿于怀，说什么也无法自我譬解的事，就是为什么她该低于慈安太后一等，
One grievance had always rankled with Empress Dowager Cixi, and no reasoning could reconcile her to it: why should she stand one degree below Empress Dowager Ci’an?

而这一等非同小可，
That single degree mattered enormously.

皇后母仪天下，生日称为“千秋”，受群臣在宫门外朝贺。
An empress was the mother of the realm; her birthday was celebrated as a festival of a thousand autumns, and the ministers offered congratulations outside the palace gates.

下皇后一等的皇贵妃，不独无此荣耀，
An imperial noble consort, one rank lower, enjoyed none of this honour.

甚至连姓氏亦不为群臣所知。
The ministers did not even know her family name.

东西两宫——慈安、慈禧由“选秀女”进身，家世是一样的，
The occupants of the Eastern and Western Palaces, Ci’an and Cixi, had both entered through the selection of palace women and came from comparable families.

慈安之父为广西左江道，慈禧之父是安徽池太广道。
Ci’an’s father had been circuit intendant of Zuojiang in Guangxi; Cixi’s, circuit intendant of Chi-Tai-Guang in Anhui.

起初身分虽同，
Their standing had originally been equal.

但当文宗元后既崩，立第二后时，选中了慈安，
Yet after Emperor Wenzong’s first empress died, Ci’an was chosen as the second.

便使得那时封号为“懿贵妃”的慈禧，愤不能平，
Cixi, then bearing the title Noble Consort Yi, had been furious.

因为慈安无子而她有子，且是唯一的皇子，
Ci’an had no son, whereas she had borne the emperor’s only son.

不是她的肚子争气，大清朝的帝系，将从咸丰而绝。
Had her womb not proved its worth, the direct imperial line of the Great Qing would have ended with Xianfeng.

由此可知，她是大有功于宗社的人，
She had therefore rendered a great service to the dynasty.

有功之人反遭贬损，这口气如何咽得下？
How could she swallow the humiliation of being placed below another woman despite that service?

可是文宗却又是一种想法，
Wenzong, however, reasoned differently.

正因为她生了皇子，断送了被立为皇后的希望。
It was precisely because she had borne an imperial son that her hope of becoming empress was extinguished.

原来慈禧精明能干、争胜揽权的性格，文宗已看得很清楚，
He had already recognized her intelligence and ability, her determination to win and to seize power.

自知在世之日无多，一旦驾崩，幼主嗣位，皇后成为太后，倘或骄纵不法，无人可制。
Knowing that he had little time left, he feared that, once he died and a child inherited the throne, an empress who became empress dowager might behave arrogantly and unlawfully with no one able to restrain her.


纵然如此，仍有隐忧，
Even so, an anxiety remained.

因为母以子贵，
A mother’s status rose with her son’s.

将来仍旧会成为太后，两宫并尊，
Cixi would still become empress dowager, and the two women would share that dignity.

而慈安赋性忠厚，必受欺侮。
Kind and honest by nature, Ci’an would surely be bullied.

这重心事，偶尔与他的宠臣肃顺吐露，肃顺便劝文宗行“钩戈夫人”的故事。
Wenzong occasionally confided this concern to his favourite minister Sushun, who advised him to follow the precedent of Lady Gouyi.

“钩弋夫人”是汉武帝的宠姬。
She had been a favourite consort of Emperor Wu of Han.

当他六十三岁时，钩弋夫人为他生了一个儿子，取名弗陵，生得茁壮聪明，颇受钟爱。
When he was sixty-three, she bore him a son, Fuling, a sturdy, intelligent child whom he loved dearly.

汉武帝晚年多病，年长诸子，看来多不成材，几经考虑，决定传位幼子弗陵，
Ill in his old age, and finding his older sons generally unpromising, Emperor Wu considered the matter repeatedly before deciding to leave the throne to Fuling.

但顾虑得幼主在位，母后年轻，每每会骄淫乱政，
But a child emperor with a young mother often meant a licentious empress dowager disrupting government.

春秋战国，不乏其例，秦始皇初年的情形，更当引以为鉴。
There had been many examples in the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods; the early reign of the First Emperor of Qin provided a particularly clear warning.

因而狠心将钩弋夫人处死，以绝后患。
Emperor Wu therefore hardened his heart and put Lady Gouyi to death to forestall trouble.

文宗也觉得肃顺的建议不错，但却缺乏汉武帝的那一副铁石心肠。
Wenzong approved Sushun’s suggestion but lacked Emperor Wu’s iron resolution.

到得病入膏肓，势将不起时，特为用朱笔亲书密谕一道，交付慈安，
When his illness became incurable and death was near, he instead wrote a secret edict in vermilion and entrusted it to Ci’an.

大意是“西宫援母以子贵之义，不得不并尊为太后。
Its substance was: “The Western Palace must, by the principle that a mother’s dignity rises with her son’s, share the title of empress dowager.

然其人绝非可倚信者，
Yet she is by no means a person to be trusted.

即不有事，汝亦当专决，
Even when there is no trouble, decisions must remain yours alone.

彼果安分无过，当始终曲全恩礼，
If she keeps her place and commits no fault, preserve every kindness and courtesy towards her to the end.

若其失行彰著，汝可召集群臣，将联此言宣示，立即赐死，以杜后患。”
But if her misconduct becomes manifest, summon the ministers, show them these words of Mine, and order her death immediately, to prevent future calamity.”

不但有失谕，而且还口头叮嘱，倘或需要用这道密旨时，应该如何召集群臣，如何宣示，又如何可能有人为西宫求情，而决不可稍为之动，必须当机立断，斩草除根。
Besides the written edict, he gave detailed oral instructions: how she should summon the ministers and announce it; how someone might plead for the Western Palace; and how she must remain unmoved, decide at once, and eradicate the danger.

慈安含泪倾听，将朱谕珍重密藏，而心里却从未想过有用得到它的一天。
Ci’an listened in tears and carefully hid the vermilion edict, never imagining in her heart that she would have occasion to use it.


事隔二十年，慈禧已经四十六岁，
Twenty years had passed, and Cixi was forty-six.

这年，光绪六年二月初，忽然得了重病，
Early in the second month of Guangxu’s sixth year, she suddenly became seriously ill.

脉案对病因的叙述，含糊不清，而所开药方，则属于专治胎前产后诸症的“四物汤”，
The medical records were vague about the cause, but the prescription was Four-Substance Decoction, a treatment for disorders before and after childbirth.

群臣皆为之困惑不解。
The ministers were baffled.

据御医庄守和、李德立向人透露，说是“血崩”，但用血崩的药，却并不对症。
The imperial physicians Zhuang Shouhe and Li Deli told people that she was suffering from severe uterine bleeding, yet medicines for that condition did not suit her symptoms.

于是降旨征医。
An edict therefore called for doctors to be recommended.

直隶总督荐山东泰武临道无锡薛福辰，山西巡抚曾国荃荐太原府阳曲县知县杭州汪守正，
The governor-general of Zhili proposed Xue Fuchen of Wuxi, circuit intendant of Tai-Wu-Lin in Shandong; Zeng Guoquan, governor of Shanxi, recommended Wang Shouzheng of Hangzhou, magistrate of Yangqu County in Taiyuan Prefecture.

此两人都是世家子弟，饱读医书，精研方脉，
Both came from established families, had read widely in medical works, and had studied prescriptions and pulse diagnosis closely.

六月间先后到京，
They arrived in the capital, one after the other, in the sixth month.

一经“请脉”，都知病根所在，不约而同的表示慈禧太后患的是“骨蒸”，
As soon as they examined her, both understood the root of the illness and independently described it as “bone-steaming fever.”

其实是“蓐劳”，产后失血过多，成了俗语所说的“干血痨”，
In fact it was postpartum exhaustion: excessive loss of blood after childbirth had produced what was commonly called “dry-blood wasting disease.”

用温补甘平之法，病势日有起色。
With warming, nourishing medicines of a sweet and mild nature, her condition improved daily.

到了这年年底，已无危险，只待调养了。
By the end of that year she was out of danger and needed only convalescence.

宅心仁厚的慈安太后，自然亦为之庆幸。
Naturally, the kindly Ci’an rejoiced.

有一天，就在几天以前，在她所住的钟粹宫，邀慈禧共餐，
One day, only a few days earlier, she had invited Cixi to dine in her residence, the Palace of Accumulated Purity.

还喝了酒，
They drank wine as well.

到得席散，暗示宫女尽皆回避，促膝深谈，作了一番规劝。
After the meal, she signalled all the palace women to withdraw, drew close to Cixi, and began a long, intimate conversation in which she offered some counsel.


据私下窥视的宫女所传出来的消息，说是慈安真的动了感情，
According to reports passed on by palace women who secretly watched them, Ci’an was deeply moved.

首先追叙当年文宗逃难到热河的种种苦楚，文宗崩后，“孤儿寡妇”受肃顺欺侮，
She first recalled the hardships of their flight with Wenzong to Rehe and how, after his death, the “widows and orphan” had been bullied by Sushun.

幸而“姐妹”同心协力，诛除权臣，转危为安。
Fortunately the two “sisters” had acted together, destroyed the overmighty ministers, and turned danger into safety.

接着又谈同治十三年间的经历的大风大浪，种种苦乐，
She went on to recall the storms of Tongzhi’s thirteenth year and all their shared sorrows and joys.

说到伤心之处，“姐妹”俩相对流涕，互为拭泪，
At the painful passages they wept together and wiped each other’s tears.

看来慈禧也动了感情了。
Cixi too appeared moved.

于是慈安慨然说道：“我们姐妹也都老了，
Ci’an then said with feeling, “We are both growing old, Sister.

重新同侍先帝的日子，不会太远。
The day when we serve the late emperor together again cannot be far away.

二十多年相处，从来没有起过什么了不得的争执，
In more than twenty years together, we have never had any great quarrel.

以后当然亦是平平静静过日子。
Of course we shall live out the rest of our days in peace as well.

有样东西是先帝留下来的，我一直以为永远也用不着，
There is something the late emperor left me which I have always believed I should never need.

不过我怕我一死以后，有人捡到这样东西，会疑心我们姐妹表面和好，暗底下不是那回事，
But I fear that after my death someone may find it and suppose our harmony was only on the surface, concealing something quite different.

那就不但你我会觉得是一大恨事，先帝亦会自悔多事。
That would be a lasting grief to us both, and even the late emperor would regret his unnecessary concern.

这样东西，不如今天就结束了它吧！”
Let us put an end to it today.”

说完，从怀中掏出一个信封，递到慈禧手里，
She took an envelope from inside her clothes and handed it to Cixi.

打开来一看，慈禧脸色大变，原来就是文宗亲自以朱笔所写的那道密谕。
On opening it, Cixi’s expression changed sharply: it was the secret edict Wenzong had written in vermilion with his own hand.

“既然无用，就烧掉了吧！”
“Since it is of no use, let us burn it.”

慈安取回原件，就在烛火上点燃焚毁。
Ci’an took back the document and held it to the candle flame until it was destroyed.

慈禧作出感极而泣的神情，还需慈安多方安慰，方能收泪。
Cixi made a show of weeping with overwhelming gratitude, and Ci’an had to comfort her at length before she stopped.

但从此慈禧只要一见了慈安，便如芒刺在背，
Afterwards, however, the sight of Ci’an made Cixi feel as if thorns were pricking her back.

处处小心，象唯恐不能得慈安欢心似的。
She was careful in everything, seemingly afraid of failing to please her.

这一天，就是三天前的三月初九，慈安太后终于在一盘松仁百果蜜糕上送了命。
Then, on the ninth day of the third month, three days before this conversation, Ci’an met her death through a plate of honey cakes with pine nuts and dried fruits.


“这样说，以后是西太后一个人作主的局面了？”胡雪岩问说，
“So from now on the Western Empress Dowager will decide everything alone?” Hu asked.

“ ”筱翁，你看事情是比以前难办呢，还是比以前容易？

Wu thought this might retrieve in the west what he had lost in the east.

The visit reassured the family that the gift was indeed intended for them, and they could safely spend it.

果然“皇天不负苦心人”，
Heaven did indeed reward their earnest hopes.

The sisters married brothers.

Within ten years, the elder was supported by the wealth of the whole realm, while the younger had become Princess Chun.

After the Xinyou coup, when the two empress dowagers began governing from behind the curtain, one of Cixi’s first pleasures was to repay the kindness.

安德海在两宫太后口中，称之为“小安子”，
The two empress dowagers called An “Little An.”

He was a eunuch trusted by Cixi who had rendered service during the Xinyou coup and risen to chief eunuch of the Palace of Eternal Spring.

仗着慈禧太后的势力，招权纳贿，骄恣不法。
Relying on her power, he gathered influence, accepted bribes, and behaved with arrogant lawlessness.

有年夏天，打着太后的旗号，擅自出京，
One summer, claiming the empress dowager’s authority, he left the capital without permission.

连直隶总督曾国藩，部只能侧目而视，不敢动他。
Even Zeng Guofan, governor-general of Zhili, dared only watch him with displeasure.

不道丁宝帧却不买帐，
Ding refused to be intimidated.

As soon as An entered Shandong, he had him closely watched.

及至证实了他并未奉有赴江南采办的懿旨，便不客气地下令逮捕，飞章入奏，
Once it was established that he carried no imperial warrant to make purchases in Jiangnan, Ding ordered his arrest and sent an urgent memorial.

奉旨“毋庸讯问，
The reply directed: “No interrogation is necessary.

就地正法”，
Execute him on the spot.”

随即提出牢来，在济南处决。
An was accordingly taken from prison and executed at Jinan.

安德海既为慈禧所宠信，了宝祯杀了他，就很可能得罪了慈禧。
Killing Cixi’s trusted favourite might have been expected to offend her.

哪知事实适得其反，
In fact, the opposite happened.

慈禧不但不恨，而且很感激丁宝祯，因为安德海被斩以后，丁宝侦下令暴尸三日，济南的百姓看清了安德海是没有“那话儿”的真太监。
She was grateful, because Ding ordered the corpse exposed for three days, and the people of Jinan saw clearly that An was a genuine eunuch, without “the thing.”

This disposed of the persistent rumour that he was Cixi’s lover.

慈禧心感丁宝帧为她洗刷之德，所以吴棠出缺，将他自东抚擢为川督。
Grateful to Ding for clearing her name, Cixi promoted him to the Sichuan governor-generalship when Wu’s post became vacant.

Of course she also valued his integrity and firmness and hoped he would repair the provincial administration Wu had spoiled.

果然，丁宝帧一人川便大加整顿，
Ding began a thorough reform as soon as he arrived.

贪庸疲软的劣员，参的参，调的调。官场气象一新。
Corrupt, incompetent, and feeble officials were impeached or transferred, and the whole atmosphere changed.

象宝森这样的人，当然也在淘汰之列，
A man like Baosen naturally belonged among those to be removed.

但想到他是宝釜的胞弟，不免有投鼠忌器的顾虑，
Yet the fact that he was Bao Yun’s brother made Ding hesitate, afraid of damaging a precious vessel while throwing at the rat beside it.

A different method was required.


象这样的情形，原有个客客气气送出门的办法，
There was an established way of politely showing someone the door.

借款数目：库平足色宝银四百万两。期限：六年还清。利率：年息九分七厘五毫。
The principal was four million treasury-standard taels of full-fineness silver sycee, repayable within six years, at an annual interest rate of 9.75 per cent.

付息办法：每六个月一付，六年共十二期。
Interest was to be paid every six months, in twelve instalments over the six years.

还本办法：第一、第二两年不还本，
No principal would be repaid in the first two years.

第三年起，每年还本一百万两。利息照减。
From the third year, one million taels would be repaid annually, with interest reduced accordingly.

保证办法：请户部催饬各省关，将应解新旧协饷，径交上海采运局，据付息还本。
As security, the Ministry of Revenue was asked to urge the provinces and customs offices to remit their current and overdue military contributions directly to the Shanghai Purchasing and Transport Bureau for payment of interest and principal.

如协饷不至，上海采运局无款可拨，应准洋商凭陕甘总督所出印票，向户部如期兑取。
If the contributions failed to arrive and the bureau had no funds to disburse, the foreign merchants were to be allowed to present the sealed notes issued by the governor-general of Shaanxi and Gansu and collect payment from the Ministry of Revenue when due.

这些条件与过去比较，好处有三：
These terms offered three advantages over earlier loans.

一是不需海关及有关各省督抚出票，可免周折，
First, no notes were required from the customs or the governors and governors-general of the provinces concerned, avoiding much complication.

二是年息由一钱二分多减至不足一钱，合月息只八厘有零，
Second, annual interest had fallen from more than twelve per cent to less than ten, equivalent to just over eight-tenths of one per cent a month.

三是头两年不还本，俾各省得以清理旧欠，“其力尚纾，并无窘迫之患。”
Third, postponing repayment of principal for two years would allow the provinces to clear their earlier arrears: “Their resources will thus have some respite, without the danger of being pressed beyond their means.”

因为如此，“已饬胡光墉、福克、凯密伦即依照定议，
For these reasons, “Hu Guangyong, Focke, and Cameron have already been instructed to proceed under the agreed terms.

应仰恳天恩敕下总理衙门，札饬道员胡光塘及照会英国使臣转行汇丰银行，一体遵照，以便陕甘出票提银”。
We humbly implore Imperial grace to direct the Zongli Yamen to issue instructions to Circuit Intendant Hu Guangyong and to notify the British minister, for transmission to HSBC, that all parties shall comply, so that Shaanxi and Gansu may issue the notes and draw the silver.”


出奏那天是四月初一，当天就奉到批复：“该衙门知道。”
The memorial was submitted on the first day of the fourth month, and the reply came that same day: “Let the office concerned take cognizance.”

也就是准予备案的意思，
This meant that the arrangement was accepted for the record.

“该衙门”指总理各国事务衙门。
The office concerned was the Zongli Yamen, formally the Office for the General Management of Affairs with Foreign Countries.

这个衙门与军机处互为表里，
It worked in close conjunction with the Grand Council.

办事司官，亦称章京，待遇优厚，亦与军机章京相同，
Its officials were likewise called secretaries and enjoyed the same generous conditions.

规制不同的是，军机章京分为头班、二班、轮班入值，而所办之事并无两佯，总督章京则各有专司，
The difference in organization was that the Grand Council secretaries served in two alternating shifts but all handled the same kinds of business, whereas those of the Zongli Yamen each had their own field.

此案归“英国股”及“德国股”所管，自有徐用仪代为接头，
This case belonged to the British and German sections, and Xu naturally made the necessary contacts.

同时因为有汇丰银行的凯密伦同来，英国公使馆批准汇丰银行照借的手续，亦很顺利，
Cameron’s presence also made it easy to obtain the British legation’s authorization for HSBC to lend.

不过三天工夫，一切都齐备了。
In only three days, every formality was complete.
