胡雪岩
《灯火楼台》第三章（上）
Towers of Light — Chapter Three, Part One
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第三章 元宝街｜CHAPTER THREE: YUANBAO STREET


八月初，在西湖上正是“一年好景君须记，最是橙黄橘绿时”，在上海已略感厌倦于酒绿灯红，脂香粉腻的宝森：为胡雪岩接到了杭州。
Early in the eighth lunar month, when West Lake offered the prospect celebrated in the lines, “Remember the finest scenes of the year: the time when oranges turn yellow and tangerines are green,” Baosen was brought to Hangzhou by Hu Xueyan. He had begun to tire a little of Shanghai’s lights and wine, its perfumes and painted beauties.

他是由古应春陪着来的。
Gu Yingchun accompanied him.

船到望仙桥埠头上早有一乘绿呢、一乘蓝呢的大轿在等候，
When their boat reached the landing at Wangxian Bridge, two large sedan chairs, one covered in green woolen cloth and the other in blue, were already waiting.

另外一匹顶马、两匹跟马，
There were also a horse to lead the procession and two to follow it.

四名兵丁，都穿着布政司的号衣，四散排开，挡住了行人，留出一片空地，容宝森登岸。
Four soldiers in the livery of the Provincial Treasury stood apart, holding back passersby and clearing a space for Baosen to land.

船家将船泊稳，搭好跳板，
The boatmen moored the vessel and laid the gangplank.

船家与岸上胡家的听差合作，伸出一条粗竹杆，捏稳两端，高及腰际，宝森以竹杆作扶手，
Working with Hu’s servants on shore, they held a stout bamboo pole firmly at either end, waist-high, for Baosen to use as a handrail.

自跳板登上埠头，立即便有一个穿是极体面的中年人，含笑迎上前来。
As he stepped ashore, an impeccably dressed middle-aged man came smiling forward.

宝森在上海也见过此人，名叫陶敦甫，字厚斋，捐了个候补知县，作胡雪岩的清客，专责是接待宾客。
Baosen had met him in Shanghai: Tao Dunfu, courtesy name Houzhai, who had purchased the rank of expectant county magistrate and served as a gentleman retainer in Hu’s household, chiefly responsible for receiving guests.


“森二爷到底到了，
“You have come at last, Second Master Sen.

胡大先生盼望了好几天了。
Mr Hu has been looking forward to your arrival for days.

森二爷路上还舒服？”
Was the journey comfortable?”

“舒服得很。”
“Very comfortable.”

宝森舒了口气，游目四顾，看过往辐辏的行人，不由得赞叹：
Baosen drew a contented breath and looked about at the throng of people.

“都说杭州是洞夭福地，
“They call Hangzhou a blessed retreat of the immortals.

真是名不虚传。”
It certainly deserves the name.”

“森二爷只看到今天的热闹，
“You see how lively it is today, Second Master Sen.

哪知道十六七年前满目凄凉，
You cannot imagine the desolation sixteen or seventeen years ago.

惨不忍睹。”
It was unbearable to look at.”

杭州经两度战事，毁坏独重，善后复兴之功，推胡雪岩为首。
Hangzhou had suffered especially grievous destruction in two periods of warfare, and Hu deserved foremost credit for its relief and restoration.

做清客捧宾客以外，亦须不忌捧东主，但以不着痕迹为贵。
Besides flattering guests, a retainer must not shrink from praising his employer; the art lay in leaving no obvious trace.

听得这话，宝森连连点头，
Baosen nodded repeatedly.

“雪岩之有今日、实在是积德之报。”
“Xueyan’s good fortune is truly the reward of accumulated virtue.”

他跟胡雪岩的交情已很厚了，所以径以雪岩相称。
His friendship with Hu had become close enough for him to use Xueyan without further formality.


陶敦甫觑空跟古应春招呼过了，请宝森坐上胡雪岩自用的绿呢大轿，古应春坐蓝呢轿，
Finding a moment to greet Gu, Tao invited Baosen into Hu’s own green-covered chair and Gu into the blue one.

由顶马引导前行，陶敦甫乘一顶小轿自间道先赶往“元宝街”等候。
The lead horse guided them forward, while Tao took a small sedan by a side route to reach Yuanbao Street ahead of them.

“元宝街”满铺青石板，足容四马并行，
The street was paved throughout with blue stone slabs and wide enough for four horses abreast.

街中突起，两头低下，形似元宝，因而得名。
Raised in the middle and lower at either end, it resembled a silver ingot, hence its name, Yuanbao.

不过，胡雪岩当初铺这条街时，却并未想到这个能配合他的“财神”之号的俗气的街名，
When Hu had first laid it out, he had not intended this rather vulgar name, so appropriate to his own title of God of Wealth.

只是为了便于排水，
His purpose had simply been to provide good drainage.

当然，四周的阴沟经过细心修建，畅通无阻，
The surrounding drains had also been carefully constructed and flowed freely.

每遇夏日暴雨，它处积水两三尺，元宝街却只要雨停，便即水消。
After a summer downpour, water might stand two or three chi deep elsewhere, but in Yuanbao Street it disappeared as soon as the rain stopped.

由望仙桥到元宝街，只是一盏茶的工夫，
The journey from Wangxian Bridge took no longer than a cup of tea.

坐在绿呢轿中的宝森，由左右玻璃窗中望出去，只见五，六丈高的一大圈围墙墙脚基石，竞有一人多高。
Through the glass windows on either side of his chair, Baosen saw a vast enclosing wall five or six zhang high, with stone foundations rising above a man’s height.

大轿抬入可容两乘轿子进出的大门，穿过门楼，抬人二门歇轿，
His chair passed through a gateway wide enough for two sedans, beneath the gatehouse, and through the second gate before being set down.

胡雪岩已站在大厅滴水檐前等候了。
Hu was waiting beneath the eaves at the front of the main hall.

“森二爷，”胡雪岩拱拱手说：“一路好吧？”
“A good journey, Second Master Sen?”

“很好，很好。”
“Very good indeed.”

宝森扶着他的手臂，偏着脸细看了一下说：
Resting a hand on Hu’s arm, Baosen tilted his head to study him.

“雪岩，一个多月不见，你又发福了。”
“It has been over a month, Xueyan, and you have grown stouter again.”

“托福，托福。
“Thanks to your good wishes.

请里面坐。”
Please come inside.”

宝森点点头，已把脸仰了起来，
Baosen nodded, his face already turned upward.

倒不是他摆架子不理人，而是因为胡家的厅堂过于宏敞，必须仰着脸才能看清楚。
He was not putting on airs or ignoring his host; the hall was so lofty and spacious that he had to look up to take it in.


未看大厅，先回顾天井，
Before examining the hall, he looked back at the courtyard.

天井有七开间大，而且极深，为的是可以搭台唱戏。
It was seven bays wide and exceptionally deep, allowing room for a stage on theatrical occasions.

大厅当然也是七开间，估计可摆三十桌席。
The hall was likewise seven bays across and could, he estimated, hold thirty banquet tables.

由于高敞之故，堂奥虽深，却很明亮，
Its height made even the deep interior bright.

正中树一方蓝地金底，四周龙纹的大立匾，案巢大书“积菩衍庆”四个黑字，
In the center stood a great upright plaque, blue and gold with dragons around its edges, bearing four large black characters: “Accumulate Goodness, Extend Blessings.”

正中上端一颗大方印，一望即知是御玺，
A large square impression at the upper center was unmistakably an imperial seal.

上下款却因相距得远，看不清楚，不知是慈禧皇太后，还是先帝的御笔。
The inscriptions above and below were too far away to read, so Baosen could not tell whether the calligraphy was Empress Dowager Cixi’s or the late emperor’s.

转眼看去，东西两面板壁上，各悬一方五尺高、丈余宽的紫檀挂屏，
On the eastern and western paneled walls hung two red-sandalwood screens, each five chi high and more than a zhang wide.

西面是一幅青绿山水，东面是贝子奕模写的《滕王阁序》，
The western one showed a blue-and-green landscape; the eastern one bore the Preface to the Pavilion of Prince Teng, copied by Beizi Yimo.

旁有两扇屏门，料想其中当是家词。
Beside it were two screen doors, beyond which Baosen supposed the family shrine must lie.

旗人向来重礼节，当即表示，理当瞻拜。
Bannermen were always attentive to propriety, and he immediately said that he ought to pay his respects.

胡雪岩自然连称“不敢当”。
Hu protested that he could not presume to accept such an honor.

只是宝森意思诚敬，当下唤人开了屏门，点燃香烛，
Baosen was sincerely insistent, however, so the doors were opened and incense and candles lit.

宝森向神龛中“胡氏列祖神位”恭恭敬敬地磕了三个头，
Before the tablets of the Hu ancestors, Baosen respectfully kowtowed three times.

胡雪岩一旁陪礼，最后又向宝森磕头道谢。
Hu performed the rites at his side, then kowtowed to Baosen in thanks.

“还要见见老太太。”
“I must also call on your honored mother.”

“改天吧！”胡雪岩说：
“Another day, perhaps.

“家母今天到天竺烧香去了。”
My mother has gone to offer incense at Tianzhu today.”

“森二爷刚到，先请歇一歇。”陶敦甫插嘴说道：
Tao intervened. “Second Master Sen has only just arrived. Let him rest a little first.

“我来引路，”
I shall lead the way.”


于是出了大厅，由西面走廓绕出去，往北一折，一带粉墙上开着个月洞门，上榜“芝径”二字，
They left the main hall by the western corridor, turned north, and came to a moon gate in a whitewashed wall, inscribed “Zhijing,” the Path of the Auspicious Herb.

迎门一座玲珑剔透的假山，
An intricate, openwork rockery faced the entrance.

陶敦甫由东面绕了过去，豁然开朗，
Tao went around its eastern side, and suddenly the view opened out.

室森放眼一望，但见树木掩映，楼阁参差，窗子上的五色玻璃，为偏西的日光照耀得光怪陆离，真有目迷五色之感。
Baosen saw towers and pavilions at different heights among the trees, their windows of colored glass catching the declining sunlight in a bewildering play of hues.

“请过桥来！”
“This way across the bridge, please.”

宝森跟着陶敦甫经过一道三曲的石桥，踏上一座极大的白石露台，
Following Tao over a stone bridge with three turns, Baosen reached a broad terrace of white stone.

中间便是三开间大，正方的楠木“四面厅”，
In its center stood a square nanmu building, three bays wide, known as the Four-Sided Hall.

上悬一方黄杨木蓝字的匾额，榜书“迎紫”二字。
A boxwood plaque above it bore the blue characters “Welcoming Purple.”

进门可是一番光景，
Inside was a different scene.

用紫檀隔板，隔出两间大小的一个长方形房间，里面是西式布置，
Red-sandalwood partitions enclosed a rectangular room two bays in size, furnished in Western style.

四周红色丝绒的安乐椅，配着白色髹金漆的茶几，中间一张与茶几同一质料式样的大餐台，上面已摆好了八只纯银的高脚果盘。
Red-velvet armchairs stood around it with white-and-gilt lacquer tea tables; in the middle was a large dining table of the same material and design, already set with eight solid-silver footed fruit dishes.

等主客坐定，随即有两个面目姣好的丫头来奉茶敬烟，
Once host and guests were seated, two pretty maids brought tea and offered tobacco.

至此才是开始寒暄的时候。
Only now could leisurely conversation begin.

“森二爷这一晌的酒兴怎么样？”
“How has your appetite for wine been lately, Second Master Sen?”

“很好哇！”宝森笑道：
“Excellent!” Baosen laughed.

“从天津上船那天起，酒兴就没有坏过。”
“It has never failed me since the day I boarded the steamer at Tianjin.”

“要这样才好。”
“That is how it should be.”

胡雪岩问古应春，
Hu turned to Gu.

“森二爷怎么没有把花想容带来？”
“Why did Second Master Sen not bring Hua Xiangrong?”

“多谢，多谢！”宝森抢着回答，
“Thank you, thank you,” Baosen answered before Gu could speak.

“我到府上来作客，
“I am your guest.

没有把她带来的道理。”
It would hardly be proper to bring her to your house.”

原来花想容是“长三”上的“红倌人”，为宝森所眷。
Hua Xiangrong was a sought-after courtesan of a changsan house, and Baosen was attached to her.

胡雪岩邀他来一赏西湖秋色，原曾在信上写明，不妨挟美以俱，
In inviting him to enjoy the autumn scenery of West Lake, Hu had expressly written that he might bring his beauty along.

而宝森却认为于礼不合，没有带花想容来。
Baosen, however, had considered it improper and left her behind.


按下来便纵谈上海声色与新奇之事，
They went on to talk freely of Shanghai’s pleasures and novelties.

宝森兴味盎然他说他开了多少眼界，看了外国的马戏、东洋女子“天胜娘”的戏法。
Baosen spoke with lively interest of how much he had seen: a foreign circus and the tricks of the Japanese woman Tianshengniang.

一面谈，一面不断有丫头送点心来，
As they talked, maids kept bringing refreshments.

宝森喜欢甜食，最中意又香又软用冰糖煮的桂花栗子。
Baosen had a sweet tooth, and his favorite was the soft, fragrant chestnuts cooked with rock sugar and sweet osmanthus.

“雪岩，”宝森是衷心向往，“我看当皇上都没有你舒服，
“Xueyan,” he said with heartfelt envy, “I doubt even the emperor lives as comfortably as you.

简直是神仙嘛！”
You are simply an immortal!”

他指着窗外，耸起于假山上的那座“百狮楼”，忽然想起一句唐诗，便念了出来，“楼阁玲珑五云起。”
Pointing through the window at the Hundred Lions Tower rising above the rockery, he suddenly recalled a line of Tang poetry and recited it: “Exquisite towers and pavilions rise amid five-colored clouds.”

“森二爷谈诗，我就接不上话了。”
“Once you begin discussing poetry, Second Master Sen, I cannot keep up.”

胡雪岩转脸说道：
Hu turned to Tao.

“厚齐，你看哪一天，把我们杭州城里那几位大诗翁请了来，陪森二爷谈谈。”
“Houzhai, find a day to invite our great Hangzhou poets here to talk with Second Master Sen.”

“不，不！”
“No, no!”

宝森急忙摇手，
Baosen waved his hands hastily.

“我哪里会做诗？
“I know nothing of writing poetry.

千万不必，免得我受窘。”
Please do not embarrass me.”

看他是真心话，胡雪岩一笑置之，不再多说。
Seeing that he meant it, Hu smiled and let the matter drop.

陶敦甫怕场面冷落，便即问说：“森二爷，上海消息灵通，
To keep the conversation from flagging, Tao asked, “News travels quickly in Shanghai, Second Master Sen.

不知道刘制台的参案怎么样了？”
What has come of the impeachment of Governor-General Liu?”

听得这话，宝森突然站了起来：
Baosen suddenly sprang to his feet.

“嘿！”
“Ha!”

他摹地里一拍双掌，声音极大，加以动作近乎粗鲁，倒让大家都吓一跳，
He clapped his hands so loudly, with such a rough gesture, that he startled everyone.

再看他脸上有掩抑不住的笑容，便越发奇怪了。
The irrepressible smile on his face made them more puzzled still.

“森二爷，”胡雪岩说：“请坐下来，
“Sit down, Second Master Sen,” Hu said.

慢慢谈起。”
“Tell us at your leisure.”

“谈起刘岘庄的参案，
“The impeachment of Liu Xianzhuang!

可真是大快人心！”
Now that is something to gladden the heart.”

他摩腹说道：
He rubbed his stomach.

“我肚子里的积滞都消了……”
“It has cleared all the stagnation out of my belly…”

刘岘庄便是两江总督刘坤一。
Liu Xianzhuang was Liu Kunyi, Governor-General of Liangjiang.

自从出了盛宣怀的案子，李鸿章便觉得此人在两江，对他是一大妨碍，而盛宣怀更是耿耿在心，企图中伤。
Since the Sheng Xuanhuai affair, Li Hongzhang had regarded his presence there as a serious obstacle, while Sheng nursed resentment and sought to damage him.

但刘坤一的官声不错，
Liu’s reputation was good, however.

封疆大吏又不比京官，号称“都老爷”的监察御史，见闻不足，无法参他，就上折参劾，慈禧太后亦未必见听。
A great provincial governor was less exposed than a capital official: the investigating censors lacked the information to impeach him, and even a memorial might not persuade Cixi.

几经筹划，认为只有一个人够资格参他，而且一定见效。
After much planning, they concluded that only one man had the standing to denounce him with certain effect.


此人就是“彭郎夺得小姑回”的彭玉鳞，湘军水师的领袖。
That man was Peng Yulin, the Peng associated with the verse “Young Peng carried Maiden Gu away,” and the leader of the Hunan Army’s naval forces.

洪杨失败，彭玉麟淡于名利，
After the Taiping defeat, Peng showed little interest in fame or profit.

外不愿当督抚，内不愿当尚书，
He would accept neither a provincial governorship nor a ministry at court.

于是有人建议，
Someone therefore proposed a different appointment.

长江水师龙蛇混杂，盐枭勾结，为害地方不浅。
The Yangtze naval forces contained men of every description, some colluding with salt smugglers and doing great harm to the districts along the river.

彭玉麟清刚正直，嫉恶如仇，在长江威望素著，
Peng was upright, stern, and fiercely opposed to wrongdoing, with an established reputation throughout the region.

不如仿照旗营“专操大臣”的制度，派他专门巡阅长江水师，
Why not follow the Banner armies’ system of special commissioners for drills and appoint him to inspect the Yangtze naval forces?

得以专折奏事，并颁给“王命旗牌”，遇有不法官吏，得以便宜行事。
He could submit memorials independently and receive the imperial command banner and tablets, authorizing him to act at his discretion against offending officials.

彭玉麟接受了这个差使，
Peng accepted.

一年一次巡阅长江水师，其余的日了，便住在西湖上，与他的孙儿女亲家俞曲园唱酬盘醒，消闲如鹤。
He made one inspection each year and spent the rest of his time at West Lake, exchanging poems and enjoying long visits with Yu Quyuan, whose grandchild had married his own, living as freely as a crane.

不过到得彭玉麟出巡时，威名所播，确能使贪官墨吏，相顾敛迹，
When Peng set out on inspection, however, his reputation made corrupt officials look anxiously at one another and curb their conduct.

他所管的事，亦不限于整顿水师纪律，
He concerned himself with far more than naval discipline.

长江沿岸各地他看下顺眼的事都要管，职权仿佛明朝“代天巡守”的巡按御史。
Anything he found objectionable along the Yangtze came within his attention, much like a Ming censor inspecting on the sovereign’s behalf.

曾经在武昌请王命旗牌立斩不法的水师总兵谭祖纶，
At Wuchang he had invoked the imperial warrant to execute the offending naval brigade commander Tan Zulun on the spot.

至于地方官经他参劾，革职查办的，亦颇不乏人。
Many local officials had also been dismissed and investigated after his denunciations.

总之，只要彭玉麟参谁，准就非倒媚不可。
Whoever Peng impeached was certain to suffer.


盛宣怀想到了这个人，李鸿章亦认为可加利用，
Sheng thought of him, and Li agreed that he could be used.

于是摭拾浮言，激动了彭玉麟的脾气，真个以密折严劾刘坤一，大致是：
They collected circulating allegations and inflamed Peng’s temper until he submitted a severe confidential impeachment of Liu.

第一，鸦片瘾大，又好逸乐，精神不济，无力整顿公事，
First, Liu was heavily addicted to opium and pleasure, leaving him too enervated to put public business in order.

第二，姨太太很多，稀见宾客，又纵容家丁，收受门包，
Second, he kept many concubines, seldom received visitors, and allowed his servants to demand payments at the door.

第三点最厉害，亦是彭玉麟亲眼所见，最感不满而又是他应该管的事，
The third charge was the strongest: something Peng had seen himself, deeply resented, and was responsible for correcting.

“沿江炮台，多不可目，
“Many forts along the river are a disgrace to behold.

每一发炮，烟气迷目，甚或坍毁。”
Whenever a gun is fired, smoke blinds the eyes; some structures even collapse.”

密折到京，慈禧太后召见军机，决定派彭玉麟进一步密查，
On receiving the memorial, Cixi summoned the Grand Council and ordered Peng to investigate further in secret.

同时内召来京觐见，打算不让他回任了。
She also summoned Liu to Beijing for an audience, intending not to send him back.

据说荣王曾经跟李鸿章商量过这件事，
Prince Gong was said to have discussed the matter with Li.

其时陕甘总督改派曾国荃，而曾国荃嫌地方太苦，又怕无法指挥左宗棠的嫡系部队，
Zeng Guoquan had lately been appointed Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu, but considered the region too harsh and feared he could not command Zuo’s own troops.

一直不愿就任，使得朝廷深感为难，
His continuing refusal to take up the post embarrassed the court.

不如乘此机会，改派刘坤一当陕甘总督。
Liu might now be transferred there instead.

至于两江总督则以清望素著的四川总督丁宝祯调补，遗缺由李鸿章的胞兄李瀚章接任。
Ding Baozhen, the well-regarded Governor-General of Sichuan, could take Liangjiang, and Li’s elder brother Li Hanzhang could succeed Ding.


这是李鸿章的一把如意算盘，
This was Li Hongzhang’s carefully calculated plan.

原来清朝的制度，封疆大吏、划疆而治，
Under the Qing system, the great provincial authorities governed defined territories.

总督往往亦仅管得一省，不比明朝的总督、巡抚是有流动性的。
Even a governor-general often administered only one province, unlike the Ming governors and governors-general, whose jurisdictions could shift.

这种制度之形成，当然有许多原因，
Many circumstances had produced this arrangement.

其中之一是，皇帝认为各有专责，易于考查，也就是易于驾驭。
One was the emperor’s belief that separate responsibilities made officials easier to assess and therefore easier to control.

因此，尽管常有“不分畛域”的上谕，实际上限制甚严，不准有越权的行为，
Despite frequent edicts urging them not to confine themselves to territorial divisions, restrictions were severe, and encroachments on another’s authority were forbidden.

及至洪杨兵起，这个相沿两百年而不替的传统被打破了。
The Taiping rising broke a tradition that had endured for two hundred years.

清朝在道光以前，凡有大征伐、调兵遣将，权皆操之于皇帝，军饷亦由国库拨发，
Before the Daoguang reign, the emperor had controlled every major expedition and deployment, and military expenditure had come from the imperial treasury.

统帅功成还朝，缴还兵权，受赏而回本职，
A victorious commander returned to court, surrendered his military authority, accepted his reward, and resumed his substantive post.

并无私有的军队。
He possessed no private army.

但自曾国藩创立湘军，而军饷又需带兵将帅就地白筹以后，整个情况大变，
Once Zeng Guofan created the Hunan Army and commanders had to raise funds locally, everything changed.

变成官不符职、守非其地、财难己用、兵为私有。
Posts no longer matched duties; service extended beyond one’s jurisdiction; locally raised revenue was unavailable for local needs; armies became private property.

曾国荃进围金陵时，他的官衔是浙江按察使，
When Zeng Guoquan advanced to besiege Jinling, his title was Judicial Commissioner of Zhejiang.

一省司法长官，带兵打伏，岂非“官不符职”？
For a province’s chief judicial officer to lead troops into battle was surely a case of office and duty failing to match.

而打仗又非为浙江划守土之责，这就是“守非其地”。
Nor was he fighting to defend Zhejiang: he was serving outside his own territory.

“财难己用”就更微妙了，
The revenue problem was subtler still.

本秦人视越，肥瘠漠不相关，
Normally, one province’s prosperity might seem as remote as Yue’s affairs to a man of Qin.

但在左宗棠西征时，却非希望浙江丰收不可，
Yet during Zuo’s western campaign, good harvests in Zhejiang became essential.

因为浙江按月要交西征协晌十四万银子，而本省修理海塘，反需另筹财源。
Zhejiang had to contribute a hundred and forty thousand taels every month to the western army, while money to repair its own coastal embankments had to be found elsewhere.

至于“兵为私有”，则以湘、淮两军原为子弟兵，爷子兄弟叔侄，递相率领，成为规例，
As for private armies, both the Hunan and Anhui forces had begun as local bands, in which fathers, sons, brothers, uncles, and nephews served under one another.

淮军的这个传统，更是牢不可破。
In the Anhui Army especially, this tradition had become unbreakable.


因为打破了疆域与职守的限制，李鸿章才能运用手腕，伸张其势力于两江——南洋。
Only after the restrictions of territory and office had been broken could Li Hongzhang maneuver to extend his power into Liangjiang, the Southern jurisdiction.

直督兼北洋大臣，江督兼南洋大臣，
The Governor-General of Zhili also served as Beiyang Minister, while the Governor-General of Liangjiang served as Nanyang Minister.

李鸿章一直强调，无论筹办防务或者与外洋通商，南北洋必须联络一致，不分彼此。
Li repeatedly insisted that coastal defense and foreign trade alike required the Northern and Southern administrations to act together without distinction.

话是如此，却只有北冰侵南洋之权，南洋的势力达不到北洋，
In practice, however, the North could encroach on the South, while Southern influence could not reach the North.

因为北洋近在畿辅，得地利之便，可直接与各国驻华公使联络交涉，
Situated close to the capital, the Northern administration could deal directly with foreign ministers in China.

这样，有关南洋的通商事务，自然而然地由北洋代办了。
Southern trading affairs consequently passed quite naturally into its hands.

同时“总理各国国务衙门”，为了在交涉上留有缓冲的余地，往往先委托北洋从事初步谈判，保留着最后的裁决权，
The Zongli Yamen, wishing to retain room for maneuver in negotiations, also often entrusted preliminary talks to the Northern administration while reserving the final decision.

这一来使得李鸿章更易于扩张势力了。
All this made Li’s expansion easier.

如此这般，李鸿章就不能不关心两江总督的人选了。
He therefore had to concern himself with the Liangjiang appointment.

最好是能听他指挥，其次也要能合作。
Ideally its holder would obey him; failing that, he must at least cooperate.

象刘坤一这样，李鸿章就觉得有许多不便，因而希望丁室祯接任江督。
Liu Kunyi presented many inconveniences, so Li wanted Ding Baozhen to replace him.

丁宝祯是他会试的同年，李鸿章一直很拉拢他。
Ding had passed the metropolitan examination in the same year as Li, who had consistently cultivated him.

丁宝祯每次奉召述职时，京中上自王邸军机，下至同乡京官都要打点，
Whenever Ding was summoned to report at court, gifts were needed for everyone from princes and grand councillors to fellow provincials holding capital posts.

无不是由李鸿章预备了整箱的现银，
Li invariably prepared whole chests of ready silver for the purpose.

这样的交情，他相信丁宝祯调任江督，一定能跟他合作无间。
Given such a friendship, he felt certain Ding would cooperate perfectly if transferred to Liangjiang.

至于李瀚章，除了贪默之外，别无他能，
Li Hanzhang, meanwhile, had no talent beyond lining his own pockets.

而四川经丁宝祯整顿以后，是个可以卧治的省份，
After Ding’s reforms, Sichuan could be governed with little effort.

李鸿章是想为他老兄找个奉母养老的好地方。
Li wished to find his elder brother a comfortable place to support their mother and spend his declining years.

这把算盘打得极精，
The calculation was meticulous.

哪知真如俗话所说的“人有千算，天有一算”，
But, as the saying went, “A man may make a thousand plans; Heaven has a plan of its own.”

彭玉麟的复奏到京，大出李鸿章的意外，竞是痛劾李鸿章的至亲赵继元。
Peng’s further memorial arrived in Beijing and, to Li’s astonishment, fiercely denounced his own close relative, Zhao Jiyuan.


赵继元是安徽太湖人，
Zhao came from Taihu in Anhui.

他的祖父叫赵文楷，嘉庆元年的状元。
His grandfather, Zhao Wenkai, had placed first in the palace examination in the first year of Jiaqing.

赵继元本人也是个翰林，但肚子里一团茅草，
Zhao himself had entered the Hanlin Academy, but his head was full of straw.

“散馆”时考列三等，分到部里当司官。
At the examination concluding his probation he ranked in the third class and was assigned to a ministry as a departmental official.

做官要凭本事、讲资格，赵继元倒有自知之明，
Advancement required ability and seniority, and Zhao knew his limitations.

自顾当司官既不能“掌印”，
In a ministry he could not hope to take charge of a departmental seal.

而两榜出身虽可派为考官，却又须先经考试，这一关又是过不去的，
His examination qualifications might make him eligible to serve as an examiner, but that too required a further test he could not pass.

不如当外官为妙。
A provincial career seemed wiser.

于是他加捐了一个道员，走门路分发两江。
He paid an additional contribution for circuit-intendant rank and used influence to secure assignment to Liangjiang.

江督正是李鸿章的老师曾国藩，
The governor-general was then Li Hongzhang’s teacher, Zeng Guofan.

爱屋及乌，所以赵继元一到江宁“禀到”，立即“挂牌”派了他军需总局总办的肥差。
Out of affection for Li, Zeng favored his relative: no sooner had Zhao reported at Jiangning than an official notice appointed him director of the lucrative Liangjiang General Military Supply Bureau.


从此赵继元便把持着两江军需总局，
Zhao had controlled it ever since.

历任总督都看李鸿章的面子，隐忍不言。
Successive governors-general tolerated him for Li’s sake.

这一次到底由彭玉麟无情地揭发了他的劣迹，
Now Peng finally exposed his misconduct without mercy.

复奏中说：“两江军需总局，原系总督札委局员，会同司道主持，
His further memorial stated: “The staff of the Liangjiang General Military Supply Bureau were originally appointed by the governor-general to work under the joint supervision of the provincial commissioners and circuit intendants.

自赵继元入局，恃以庶常散馆，捐升道员出身，又系李鸿章之妻兄，
Since entering the Bureau, Zhao Jiyuan has relied on his background as a former Hanlin probationer promoted by contribution to circuit rank, and on being Li Hongzhang’s wife’s elder brother.

卖弄聪明，妄以知兵自许，
He parades his cleverness and presumptuously claims military expertise.

由是局员营员派往修筑炮台者，皆惟赵继元之言是听。
Consequently, Bureau and army officers assigned to construct forts obey him alone.

赵继元轻前两江总督李宗羲为不知兵，忠厚和平，事多蔑视，
He despised the former Governor-General Li Zongxi as ignorant of military affairs and too gentle and accommodating, treating his authority with contempt in many matters.

甚至督臣有要务札饬总局，赵继元竟敢违抗不遵，直行己意。
Even when the governor-general sent the Bureau directives on important business, Zhao dared disobey and follow his own wishes.

李宗羲旋以病告去，赵继元更大权独揽，目空一切。
After Li retired through illness, Zhao monopolized authority still more completely and recognized no one above him.

炮台坍塌，守台官屡请查看修补，皆为赵继元蒙蔽不行。”
When forts collapsed, their commanders repeatedly requested inspections and repairs; Zhao concealed the facts and prevented action.”

李宗羲字雨亭，四川开县人，道光二十七年进士，是李鸿章的同年。
Li Zongxi, courtesy name Yuting, came from Kai County in Sichuan and had become a jinshi in the twenty-seventh year of Daoguang, the same examination year as Li Hongzhang.

同治十二年曾国藩殁于两江总督任上，由于李鸿章的推荐，李宗羲竟能继任此一要缺。
When Zeng died in the Liangjiang post in the twelfth year of Tongzhi, Li’s recommendation enabled Li Zongxi to succeed to this important appointment.

其人才具平常，李鸿章可以遥制，两江诸般设施，每听北洋指挥。
A man of ordinary abilities, he could be controlled from a distance, and Liangjiang affairs often followed Northern instructions.

盛宣怀以直隶候补道得以派到招商局去当会办，便是李宗轰任内之事。
It was during his term that Sheng Xuanhuai, an expectant intendant assigned to Zhili, obtained the assistant directorship of China Merchants.

这样的一个人，赵继元自然不会将他放在眼里。
Zhao naturally thought little of such a governor-general.


至于对刘坤一，据彭玉麟在复奏中说：“臣恐刘坤一为其所误，力言其人不可用。
As for Liu Kunyi, Peng wrote: “Fearing that Liu would be misled by Zhao, I strongly urged that this man could not be employed.

刘坤一札调出局，改派总理营务，亦可谓优待之矣，
Liu ordered his transfer out of the Bureau and appointed him to oversee army-camp affairs, which was generous treatment indeed.

而赵继元敢于公庭大众向该督力争，仍旧帮理局务。
Yet Zhao dared argue with the governor-general before the assembled officials and continued assisting in the Bureau’s management.

本不知兵，亦无远识，嗜好复深，
He understands neither warfare nor the longer view, and his addiction is deep.

徒恃势榄权，妄自尊大，
Relying on powerful connections to grasp authority, he is conceited and overbearing.

始则炫其长，后则自护其短，专以节省军费为口实，惑众而阻群言。
First he displays his supposed strengths, then conceals his weaknesses, always using economy in military expenditure as his pretext, misleading others and silencing objections.”

“彭玉麟说，在赵继元看，跟洋人如果发生了纠纷，到头来无非归归之于“和”之一字。
According to Peng, Zhao believed that any dispute with foreigners would eventually end in peace.

既然如此，“江防”也好，“海防”也好，都是白费心血，
River defense and coastal defense were therefore wasted effort.

不过朝廷这样交代，不能不敷衍而已。
Since the court demanded them, one merely had to keep up appearances.

但是真的节省经费、粉饰表面，也还罢了，
Genuine economy, even accompanied by a show of compliance, would have been one thing.

实际上浪费甚多，只是当用不用而已，
In fact, expenditure was wasteful; only necessary spending was withheld.

彭玉麟认为赵继元持这种论调，是件极危险的事，
Peng considered this outlook extremely dangerous.

防务废弛，尽属虚文，
Defenses had decayed into empty formalities.

一旦有警，无可倚恃，必至贻误大计。
At the first alarm there would be nothing to rely on, and the greater plans of the state would suffer.

最后又说：“黜陟之柄，操自朝廷，差委之权，归于总督，
He concluded: “The power to dismiss and promote belongs to the throne; the power to assign duties rests with the governor-general.

臣不敢擅便，
I dare not act without authority.

惟既有见闻，不忍瞻徇缄默，
Yet, having witnessed and heard these things, I cannot keep silent out of personal consideration.

恐终掣实心办事者之时，而无以儆局员肆妄之心。”
I fear that those sincerely attending to their work will remain hampered, while the Bureau’s staff receive no warning against their reckless arrogance.”

这意思是很明白的，如果他有权，即时会将赵继元撤差革职。
His meaning was unmistakable: had he possessed the authority, he would have removed Zhao from both his assignment and his rank at once.

此奏一上，慈槽太后震怒，
Cixi was furious.

初揽大权，正想整饬纳纪立威之时，
Having just assumed sole power, she was eager to enforce discipline and establish her authority.

当即批了个“劣迹昭著，
She immediately wrote, “His misconduct is notorious.

即行革职”，
Dismiss him forthwith.”

再一次为彭玉磷显一显威风。
Once again Peng had demonstrated his power.

这一来，李鸿章自亦大伤面子，不便对两江总督的入选，再表示意见，
Li naturally lost a great deal of face and could no longer conveniently express an opinion on the Liangjiang succession.

那把如意算盘，竟完全落空了。
His entire plan had fallen through.


听宝森谈完这段刚出炉的新闻，胡雪岩便即问道：“这么说，刘岘帅还会回任。”
When Baosen had finished this fresh news, Hu asked, “Then Governor-General Liu will return to his post?”

“回任大概不会了。”
“Probably not.”

“那么是准来呢？”
“Who will go, then?”

“当然是曾九帅。”
“Ninth Marshal Zeng, of course.”

“曾九帅”便是曾国荃。
Ninth Marshal Zeng was Zeng Guoquan.

江宁是他在同治三年攻下来的，加以湘军旧部，遍布两江——上江安徽、下江江苏，
He had captured Jiangning in the third year of Tongzhi, and former Hunan Army officers were scattered throughout Liangjiang—Anhui, the Upper River province, and Jiangsu, the Lower.

所以每逢江督出缺，总有人把他列入继任人选。
Whenever the governorship-general fell vacant, someone proposed him.

这一回，看起来真的要轮到“曾九帅”了。
This time his turn seemed finally to have come.

“曾九不相宜。”宝鋆说道：
“Zeng the Ninth would be unsuitable,” Bao Yun said.

“他嫌陕甘太苦不肯去，最后拿富庶的两江给他，
“He refuses Shaanxi and Gansu because conditions are harsh, and we then give him prosperous Liangjiang?

且不说人心不服，而且开挟持之渐，
Quite apart from the resentment it would cause, that would set a precedent for holding the court to ransom.

朝延以后用人就难了。”
Future appointments would become difficult.”

宝鋆是恭王的智囊，
Bao was Prince Gong’s trusted adviser.

听他说得不错，便即问道：“那么，你看是让谁去呢？”
Recognizing the force of his argument, the prince asked, “Whom do you suggest, then?”

“现成有一个人在那里：左季高。”
“There is a man ready to hand: Zuo Jigao.”

“啊，啊！
“Ah, yes.

好。”
Excellent.”

恭王深深点头。
Prince Gong nodded emphatically.

原来左宗棠在军机处，主意太多，而又往往言大而夸，不切实际，宝望一直在排挤他。
Zuo had too many ideas in the Grand Council, often grandiose and impractical, and Bao had continually tried to push him aside.

左宗棠一气之下，上折告病，请开缺回籍养菏，
Angered, Zuo had memorialized that he was ill and requested release from office to recover at home.

朝廷赏了他两个月的假。
The court had granted two months’ leave.

恭王毕竟忠厚，
Prince Gong was fundamentally a decent man.

虽也讨厌左宗棠喋喋不休，但挤得他不安于位，也不免内疚神明，
Though he too disliked Zuo’s incessant talk, he felt guilty that the pressure had made Zuo unable to remain comfortably in office.

如今有两江这个“善地”让他去养老，可以略补歉疚，
Liangjiang would provide a pleasant place for his old age and ease the prince’s conscience.

因而深为赞成。
He therefore approved warmly.


于是九月初六那天，由恭王面奏，
On the sixth day of the ninth lunar month, Prince Gong reported orally to the throne.

说海防之议方兴，势在必行，主其事者是北洋、南洋两大臣，
Coastal defense was now a developing policy that must be carried through, principally by the Beiyang and Nanyang Ministers.

北洋有李鸿章在，可以政心，
With Li Hongzhang in the North there was no cause for anxiety.

南洋需要有威望素著的重臣主持，几经考虑，认为以左宗棠为最适宜。
The South needed a senior minister of established prestige, and careful consideration showed Zuo to be the most suitable.

而且，江南政风疲软，亦需象左宗棠那样有魄力的人去当总督，才能大事整顿。
Jiangnan administration was also lax and required an energetic governor-general like Zuo to undertake thorough reform.

慈禧太后亦很讨厌左宗棠的口没遮拦，什么事想到就说，毫无顾忌，
Cixi too disliked his unguarded tongue: he said whatever came into his head without restraint.

不过她很念旧，总想到左宗棠是艰难百战、立过大功劳的人，
Yet she remembered old service and could not forget the arduous campaigns and great victories he had won.

既然不宜于在朝，应该给他一个好地方让他去养老，
If he was unsuited to court life, he should have a good place in which to grow old.

所以同意了军机的建议，外放左宗棠为两江总督。
She accepted the Council’s recommendation and appointed him Governor-General of Liangjiang.


这个消息传到时，恰好胡雪岩陪着畅游了西湖上六桥三竺之胜的室森回到上海。
The news reached Hu just as he returned to Shanghai with Baosen after showing him the Six Bridges and Three Tianzhu Temples at West Lake.

对他来说，这自然是个喜讯，不由得又在心里激起了好些雄图壮志。
Naturally, it was welcome news, and it stirred fresh ambitions in him.

照例的，胡雪岩每一趟到上海，起码有半个月的工夫，要应付为他接风而日夜排满了的饭局，
Each visit to Shanghai normally meant at least half a month of welcome dinners filling his days and evenings.

第一是官场，第二是商场，最后才轮到至亲好友。
Official circles came first, business circles second, and only then could close friends and relatives have their turn.

古应春和七姑奶奶夫妇是“自己人”，
Gu and the Seventh Lady were family.

挨到他们做主人请客，已经是十月初，将近慈德太后万寿的日子了。
By the time they could entertain him, it was early in the tenth lunar month, near Empress Dowager Cixi’s birthday.

这天请了两桌客，陪客也都是“自己人”，
They invited two tables of guests, all intimates.

其中有刘不才，他如今管着胡庆余堂药店，这一回到上海是要转道北方去采办明年要用的药材，
Among them was Liu Bucai, now managing Hu Qingyu Hall, who had come to Shanghai on his way north to purchase medicinal materials for the coming year.

有宓本常，他是阜康雪记银号上海总号的“大伙”。
There was also Mi Benchang, head manager of the Shanghai headquarters of Fukang Xueji Bank.

此外也都是胡雪岩私人资本开设的丝号，典当的档手。
The others managed silk firms and pawnshops established with Hu’s private capital.

酒阑人散，为时尚早，
When the wine was finished and the guests had gone, the hour was still early.

胡雪岩想趁此机会跟古应春夫妇好好谈一谈自己这几天的见闻与想法，所以决定留宿在古家。
Hu wanted a proper conversation with Gu and his wife about his recent observations and ideas, and decided to stay the night.

古家原替他预备得有宿处，是二楼后房极大的一个套间，一切现成，
A spacious suite at the back of the second floor was always kept ready for him.

便将他的轿夫与跟班都打发了回去，只留下一个贴身的小跟班，名叫阿成的，随他住在古家。
His chair bearers and attendants were sent home; only a young personal attendant named A’cheng stayed with him.


“应春，这回湘阴放两江，等于合肥掼了一大跤，
“Yingchun, sending Xiangyin to Liangjiang amounts to a heavy fall for Hefei.
