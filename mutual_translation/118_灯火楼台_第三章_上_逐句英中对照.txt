胡雪岩
《灯火楼台》第三章（上）
Towers of Light — Chapter Three, Part One
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


CHAPTER THREE: YUANBAO STREET｜第三章 元宝街


Early in the eighth lunar month, when West Lake offered the prospect celebrated in the lines, “Remember the finest scenes of the year: the time when oranges turn yellow and tangerines are green,” Baosen was brought to Hangzhou by Hu Xueyan. He had begun to tire a little of Shanghai’s lights and wine, its perfumes and painted beauties.
八月初，在西湖上正是“一年好景君须记，最是橙黄橘绿时”，在上海已略感厌倦于酒绿灯红，脂香粉腻的宝森：为胡雪岩接到了杭州。

Gu Yingchun accompanied him.
他是由古应春陪着来的。

When their boat reached the landing at Wangxian Bridge, two large sedan chairs, one covered in green woolen cloth and the other in blue, were already waiting.
船到望仙桥埠头上早有一乘绿呢、一乘蓝呢的大轿在等候，

There were also a horse to lead the procession and two to follow it.
另外一匹顶马、两匹跟马，

Four soldiers in the livery of the Provincial Treasury stood apart, holding back passersby and clearing a space for Baosen to land.
四名兵丁，都穿着布政司的号衣，四散排开，挡住了行人，留出一片空地，容宝森登岸。

The boatmen moored the vessel and laid the gangplank.
船家将船泊稳，搭好跳板，

Working with Hu’s servants on shore, they held a stout bamboo pole firmly at either end, waist-high, for Baosen to use as a handrail.
船家与岸上胡家的听差合作，伸出一条粗竹杆，捏稳两端，高及腰际，宝森以竹杆作扶手，

As he stepped ashore, an impeccably dressed middle-aged man came smiling forward.
自跳板登上埠头，立即便有一个穿是极体面的中年人，含笑迎上前来。

Baosen had met him in Shanghai: Tao Dunfu, courtesy name Houzhai, who had purchased the rank of expectant county magistrate and served as a gentleman retainer in Hu’s household, chiefly responsible for receiving guests.
宝森在上海也见过此人，名叫陶敦甫，字厚斋，捐了个候补知县，作胡雪岩的清客，专责是接待宾客。


“You have come at last, Second Master Sen.
“森二爷到底到了，

Mr Hu has been looking forward to your arrival for days.
胡大先生盼望了好几天了。

Was the journey comfortable?”
森二爷路上还舒服？”

“Very comfortable.”
“舒服得很。”

Baosen drew a contented breath and looked about at the throng of people.
宝森舒了口气，游目四顾，看过往辐辏的行人，不由得赞叹：

“They call Hangzhou a blessed retreat of the immortals.
“都说杭州是洞夭福地，

It certainly deserves the name.”
真是名不虚传。”

“You see how lively it is today, Second Master Sen.
“森二爷只看到今天的热闹，

You cannot imagine the desolation sixteen or seventeen years ago.
哪知道十六七年前满目凄凉，

It was unbearable to look at.”
惨不忍睹。”

Hangzhou had suffered especially grievous destruction in two periods of warfare, and Hu deserved foremost credit for its relief and restoration.
杭州经两度战事，毁坏独重，善后复兴之功，推胡雪岩为首。

Besides flattering guests, a retainer must not shrink from praising his employer; the art lay in leaving no obvious trace.
做清客捧宾客以外，亦须不忌捧东主，但以不着痕迹为贵。

Baosen nodded repeatedly.
听得这话，宝森连连点头，

“Xueyan’s good fortune is truly the reward of accumulated virtue.”
“雪岩之有今日、实在是积德之报。”

His friendship with Hu had become close enough for him to use Xueyan without further formality.
他跟胡雪岩的交情已很厚了，所以径以雪岩相称。


Finding a moment to greet Gu, Tao invited Baosen into Hu’s own green-covered chair and Gu into the blue one.
陶敦甫觑空跟古应春招呼过了，请宝森坐上胡雪岩自用的绿呢大轿，古应春坐蓝呢轿，

The lead horse guided them forward, while Tao took a small sedan by a side route to reach Yuanbao Street ahead of them.
由顶马引导前行，陶敦甫乘一顶小轿自间道先赶往“元宝街”等候。

The street was paved throughout with blue stone slabs and wide enough for four horses abreast.
“元宝街”满铺青石板，足容四马并行，

Raised in the middle and lower at either end, it resembled a silver ingot, hence its name, Yuanbao.
街中突起，两头低下，形似元宝，因而得名。

When Hu had first laid it out, he had not intended this rather vulgar name, so appropriate to his own title of God of Wealth.
不过，胡雪岩当初铺这条街时，却并未想到这个能配合他的“财神”之号的俗气的街名，

His purpose had simply been to provide good drainage.
只是为了便于排水，

The surrounding drains had also been carefully constructed and flowed freely.
当然，四周的阴沟经过细心修建，畅通无阻，

After a summer downpour, water might stand two or three chi deep elsewhere, but in Yuanbao Street it disappeared as soon as the rain stopped.
每遇夏日暴雨，它处积水两三尺，元宝街却只要雨停，便即水消。

The journey from Wangxian Bridge took no longer than a cup of tea.
由望仙桥到元宝街，只是一盏茶的工夫，

Through the glass windows on either side of his chair, Baosen saw a vast enclosing wall five or six zhang high, with stone foundations rising above a man’s height.
坐在绿呢轿中的宝森，由左右玻璃窗中望出去，只见五，六丈高的一大圈围墙墙脚基石，竞有一人多高。

His chair passed through a gateway wide enough for two sedans, beneath the gatehouse, and through the second gate before being set down.
大轿抬入可容两乘轿子进出的大门，穿过门楼，抬人二门歇轿，

Hu was waiting beneath the eaves at the front of the main hall.
胡雪岩已站在大厅滴水檐前等候了。

“A good journey, Second Master Sen?”
“森二爷，”胡雪岩拱拱手说：“一路好吧？”

“Very good indeed.”
“很好，很好。”

Resting a hand on Hu’s arm, Baosen tilted his head to study him.
宝森扶着他的手臂，偏着脸细看了一下说：

“It has been over a month, Xueyan, and you have grown stouter again.”
“雪岩，一个多月不见，你又发福了。”

“Thanks to your good wishes.
“托福，托福。

Please come inside.”
请里面坐。”

Baosen nodded, his face already turned upward.
宝森点点头，已把脸仰了起来，

He was not putting on airs or ignoring his host; the hall was so lofty and spacious that he had to look up to take it in.
倒不是他摆架子不理人，而是因为胡家的厅堂过于宏敞，必须仰着脸才能看清楚。


Before examining the hall, he looked back at the courtyard.
未看大厅，先回顾天井，

It was seven bays wide and exceptionally deep, allowing room for a stage on theatrical occasions.
天井有七开间大，而且极深，为的是可以搭台唱戏。

The hall was likewise seven bays across and could, he estimated, hold thirty banquet tables.
大厅当然也是七开间，估计可摆三十桌席。

Its height made even the deep interior bright.
由于高敞之故，堂奥虽深，却很明亮，

In the center stood a great upright plaque, blue and gold with dragons around its edges, bearing four large black characters: “Accumulate Goodness, Extend Blessings.”
正中树一方蓝地金底，四周龙纹的大立匾，案巢大书“积菩衍庆”四个黑字，

A large square impression at the upper center was unmistakably an imperial seal.
正中上端一颗大方印，一望即知是御玺，

The inscriptions above and below were too far away to read, so Baosen could not tell whether the calligraphy was Empress Dowager Cixi’s or the late emperor’s.
上下款却因相距得远，看不清楚，不知是慈禧皇太后，还是先帝的御笔。

On the eastern and western paneled walls hung two red-sandalwood screens, each five chi high and more than a zhang wide.
转眼看去，东西两面板壁上，各悬一方五尺高、丈余宽的紫檀挂屏，

The western one showed a blue-and-green landscape; the eastern one bore the Preface to the Pavilion of Prince Teng, copied by Beizi Yimo.
西面是一幅青绿山水，东面是贝子奕模写的《滕王阁序》，

Beside it were two screen doors, beyond which Baosen supposed the family shrine must lie.
旁有两扇屏门，料想其中当是家词。

Bannermen were always attentive to propriety, and he immediately said that he ought to pay his respects.
旗人向来重礼节，当即表示，理当瞻拜。

Hu protested that he could not presume to accept such an honor.
胡雪岩自然连称“不敢当”。

Baosen was sincerely insistent, however, so the doors were opened and incense and candles lit.
只是宝森意思诚敬，当下唤人开了屏门，点燃香烛，

Before the tablets of the Hu ancestors, Baosen respectfully kowtowed three times.
宝森向神龛中“胡氏列祖神位”恭恭敬敬地磕了三个头，

Hu performed the rites at his side, then kowtowed to Baosen in thanks.
胡雪岩一旁陪礼，最后又向宝森磕头道谢。

“I must also call on your honored mother.”
“还要见见老太太。”

“Another day, perhaps.
“改天吧！”胡雪岩说：

My mother has gone to offer incense at Tianzhu today.”
“家母今天到天竺烧香去了。”

Tao intervened. “Second Master Sen has only just arrived. Let him rest a little first.
“森二爷刚到，先请歇一歇。”陶敦甫插嘴说道：

I shall lead the way.”
“我来引路，”


They left the main hall by the western corridor, turned north, and came to a moon gate in a whitewashed wall, inscribed “Zhijing,” the Path of the Auspicious Herb.
于是出了大厅，由西面走廓绕出去，往北一折，一带粉墙上开着个月洞门，上榜“芝径”二字，

An intricate, openwork rockery faced the entrance.
迎门一座玲珑剔透的假山，

Tao went around its eastern side, and suddenly the view opened out.
陶敦甫由东面绕了过去，豁然开朗，

Baosen saw towers and pavilions at different heights among the trees, their windows of colored glass catching the declining sunlight in a bewildering play of hues.
室森放眼一望，但见树木掩映，楼阁参差，窗子上的五色玻璃，为偏西的日光照耀得光怪陆离，真有目迷五色之感。

“This way across the bridge, please.”
“请过桥来！”

Following Tao over a stone bridge with three turns, Baosen reached a broad terrace of white stone.
宝森跟着陶敦甫经过一道三曲的石桥，踏上一座极大的白石露台，

In its center stood a square nanmu building, three bays wide, known as the Four-Sided Hall.
中间便是三开间大，正方的楠木“四面厅”，

A boxwood plaque above it bore the blue characters “Welcoming Purple.”
上悬一方黄杨木蓝字的匾额，榜书“迎紫”二字。

Inside was a different scene.
进门可是一番光景，

Red-sandalwood partitions enclosed a rectangular room two bays in size, furnished in Western style.
用紫檀隔板，隔出两间大小的一个长方形房间，里面是西式布置，

Red-velvet armchairs stood around it with white-and-gilt lacquer tea tables; in the middle was a large dining table of the same material and design, already set with eight solid-silver footed fruit dishes.
四周红色丝绒的安乐椅，配着白色髹金漆的茶几，中间一张与茶几同一质料式样的大餐台，上面已摆好了八只纯银的高脚果盘。

Once host and guests were seated, two pretty maids brought tea and offered tobacco.
等主客坐定，随即有两个面目姣好的丫头来奉茶敬烟，

Only now could leisurely conversation begin.
至此才是开始寒暄的时候。

“How has your appetite for wine been lately, Second Master Sen?”
“森二爷这一晌的酒兴怎么样？”

“Excellent!” Baosen laughed.
“很好哇！”宝森笑道：

“It has never failed me since the day I boarded the steamer at Tianjin.”
“从天津上船那天起，酒兴就没有坏过。”

“That is how it should be.”
“要这样才好。”

Hu turned to Gu.
胡雪岩问古应春，

“Why did Second Master Sen not bring Hua Xiangrong?”
“森二爷怎么没有把花想容带来？”

“Thank you, thank you,” Baosen answered before Gu could speak.
“多谢，多谢！”宝森抢着回答，

“I am your guest.
“我到府上来作客，

It would hardly be proper to bring her to your house.”
没有把她带来的道理。”

Hua Xiangrong was a sought-after courtesan of a changsan house, and Baosen was attached to her.
原来花想容是“长三”上的“红倌人”，为宝森所眷。

In inviting him to enjoy the autumn scenery of West Lake, Hu had expressly written that he might bring his beauty along.
胡雪岩邀他来一赏西湖秋色，原曾在信上写明，不妨挟美以俱，

Baosen, however, had considered it improper and left her behind.
而宝森却认为于礼不合，没有带花想容来。


They went on to talk freely of Shanghai’s pleasures and novelties.
按下来便纵谈上海声色与新奇之事，

Baosen spoke with lively interest of how much he had seen: a foreign circus and the tricks of the Japanese woman Tianshengniang.
宝森兴味盎然他说他开了多少眼界，看了外国的马戏、东洋女子“天胜娘”的戏法。

As they talked, maids kept bringing refreshments.
一面谈，一面不断有丫头送点心来，

Baosen had a sweet tooth, and his favorite was the soft, fragrant chestnuts cooked with rock sugar and sweet osmanthus.
宝森喜欢甜食，最中意又香又软用冰糖煮的桂花栗子。

“Xueyan,” he said with heartfelt envy, “I doubt even the emperor lives as comfortably as you.
“雪岩，”宝森是衷心向往，“我看当皇上都没有你舒服，

You are simply an immortal!”
简直是神仙嘛！”

Pointing through the window at the Hundred Lions Tower rising above the rockery, he suddenly recalled a line of Tang poetry and recited it: “Exquisite towers and pavilions rise amid five-colored clouds.”
他指着窗外，耸起于假山上的那座“百狮楼”，忽然想起一句唐诗，便念了出来，“楼阁玲珑五云起。”

“Once you begin discussing poetry, Second Master Sen, I cannot keep up.”
“森二爷谈诗，我就接不上话了。”

Hu turned to Tao.
胡雪岩转脸说道：

“Houzhai, find a day to invite our great Hangzhou poets here to talk with Second Master Sen.”
“厚齐，你看哪一天，把我们杭州城里那几位大诗翁请了来，陪森二爷谈谈。”

“No, no!”
“不，不！”

Baosen waved his hands hastily.
宝森急忙摇手，

“I know nothing of writing poetry.
“我哪里会做诗？

Please do not embarrass me.”
千万不必，免得我受窘。”

Seeing that he meant it, Hu smiled and let the matter drop.
看他是真心话，胡雪岩一笑置之，不再多说。

To keep the conversation from flagging, Tao asked, “News travels quickly in Shanghai, Second Master Sen.
陶敦甫怕场面冷落，便即问说：“森二爷，上海消息灵通，

What has come of the impeachment of Governor-General Liu?”
不知道刘制台的参案怎么样了？”

Baosen suddenly sprang to his feet.
听得这话，宝森突然站了起来：

“Ha!”
“嘿！”

He clapped his hands so loudly, with such a rough gesture, that he startled everyone.
他摹地里一拍双掌，声音极大，加以动作近乎粗鲁，倒让大家都吓一跳，

The irrepressible smile on his face made them more puzzled still.
再看他脸上有掩抑不住的笑容，便越发奇怪了。

“Sit down, Second Master Sen,” Hu said.
“森二爷，”胡雪岩说：“请坐下来，

“Tell us at your leisure.”
慢慢谈起。”

“The impeachment of Liu Xianzhuang!
“谈起刘岘庄的参案，

Now that is something to gladden the heart.”
可真是大快人心！”

He rubbed his stomach.
他摩腹说道：

“It has cleared all the stagnation out of my belly…”
“我肚子里的积滞都消了……”

Liu Xianzhuang was Liu Kunyi, Governor-General of Liangjiang.
刘岘庄便是两江总督刘坤一。

Since the Sheng Xuanhuai affair, Li Hongzhang had regarded his presence there as a serious obstacle, while Sheng nursed resentment and sought to damage him.
自从出了盛宣怀的案子，李鸿章便觉得此人在两江，对他是一大妨碍，而盛宣怀更是耿耿在心，企图中伤。

Liu’s reputation was good, however.
但刘坤一的官声不错，

A great provincial governor was less exposed than a capital official: the investigating censors lacked the information to impeach him, and even a memorial might not persuade Cixi.
封疆大吏又不比京官，号称“都老爷”的监察御史，见闻不足，无法参他，就上折参劾，慈禧太后亦未必见听。

After much planning, they concluded that only one man had the standing to denounce him with certain effect.
几经筹划，认为只有一个人够资格参他，而且一定见效。


That man was Peng Yulin, the Peng associated with the verse “Young Peng carried Maiden Gu away,” and the leader of the Hunan Army’s naval forces.
此人就是“彭郎夺得小姑回”的彭玉鳞，湘军水师的领袖。

After the Taiping defeat, Peng showed little interest in fame or profit.
洪杨失败，彭玉麟淡于名利，

He would accept neither a provincial governorship nor a ministry at court.
外不愿当督抚，内不愿当尚书，

Someone therefore proposed a different appointment.
于是有人建议，

The Yangtze naval forces contained men of every description, some colluding with salt smugglers and doing great harm to the districts along the river.
长江水师龙蛇混杂，盐枭勾结，为害地方不浅。

Peng was upright, stern, and fiercely opposed to wrongdoing, with an established reputation throughout the region.
彭玉麟清刚正直，嫉恶如仇，在长江威望素著，

Why not follow the Banner armies’ system of special commissioners for drills and appoint him to inspect the Yangtze naval forces?
不如仿照旗营“专操大臣”的制度，派他专门巡阅长江水师，

He could submit memorials independently and receive the imperial command banner and tablets, authorizing him to act at his discretion against offending officials.
得以专折奏事，并颁给“王命旗牌”，遇有不法官吏，得以便宜行事。

Peng accepted.
彭玉麟接受了这个差使，

He made one inspection each year and spent the rest of his time at West Lake, exchanging poems and enjoying long visits with Yu Quyuan, whose grandchild had married his own, living as freely as a crane.
一年一次巡阅长江水师，其余的日了，便住在西湖上，与他的孙儿女亲家俞曲园唱酬盘醒，消闲如鹤。

When Peng set out on inspection, however, his reputation made corrupt officials look anxiously at one another and curb their conduct.
不过到得彭玉麟出巡时，威名所播，确能使贪官墨吏，相顾敛迹，

He concerned himself with far more than naval discipline.
他所管的事，亦不限于整顿水师纪律，

Anything he found objectionable along the Yangtze came within his attention, much like a Ming censor inspecting on the sovereign’s behalf.
长江沿岸各地他看下顺眼的事都要管，职权仿佛明朝“代天巡守”的巡按御史。

At Wuchang he had invoked the imperial warrant to execute the offending naval brigade commander Tan Zulun on the spot.
曾经在武昌请王命旗牌立斩不法的水师总兵谭祖纶，

Many local officials had also been dismissed and investigated after his denunciations.
至于地方官经他参劾，革职查办的，亦颇不乏人。

Whoever Peng impeached was certain to suffer.
总之，只要彭玉麟参谁，准就非倒媚不可。


Sheng thought of him, and Li agreed that he could be used.
盛宣怀想到了这个人，李鸿章亦认为可加利用，

They collected circulating allegations and inflamed Peng’s temper until he submitted a severe confidential impeachment of Liu.
于是摭拾浮言，激动了彭玉麟的脾气，真个以密折严劾刘坤一，大致是：

First, Liu was heavily addicted to opium and pleasure, leaving him too enervated to put public business in order.
第一，鸦片瘾大，又好逸乐，精神不济，无力整顿公事，

Second, he kept many concubines, seldom received visitors, and allowed his servants to demand payments at the door.
第二，姨太太很多，稀见宾客，又纵容家丁，收受门包，

The third charge was the strongest: something Peng had seen himself, deeply resented, and was responsible for correcting.
第三点最厉害，亦是彭玉麟亲眼所见，最感不满而又是他应该管的事，

“Many forts along the river are a disgrace to behold.
“沿江炮台，多不可目，

Whenever a gun is fired, smoke blinds the eyes; some structures even collapse.”
每一发炮，烟气迷目，甚或坍毁。”

On receiving the memorial, Cixi summoned the Grand Council and ordered Peng to investigate further in secret.
密折到京，慈禧太后召见军机，决定派彭玉麟进一步密查，

She also summoned Liu to Beijing for an audience, intending not to send him back.
同时内召来京觐见，打算不让他回任了。

Prince Gong was said to have discussed the matter with Li.
据说荣王曾经跟李鸿章商量过这件事，

Zeng Guoquan had lately been appointed Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu, but considered the region too harsh and feared he could not command Zuo’s own troops.
其时陕甘总督改派曾国荃，而曾国荃嫌地方太苦，又怕无法指挥左宗棠的嫡系部队，

His continuing refusal to take up the post embarrassed the court.
一直不愿就任，使得朝廷深感为难，

Liu might now be transferred there instead.
不如乘此机会，改派刘坤一当陕甘总督。

Ding Baozhen, the well-regarded Governor-General of Sichuan, could take Liangjiang, and Li’s elder brother Li Hanzhang could succeed Ding.
至于两江总督则以清望素著的四川总督丁宝祯调补，遗缺由李鸿章的胞兄李瀚章接任。
