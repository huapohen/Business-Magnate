胡雪岩
《灯火楼台》第四章（下）
Towers of Light — Chapter Four, Part Two
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


月如收拾了床辅，又洗了手，然后开楼门叫丫头从厨房里将一锅鸭粥端了来。
Yueru straightened the bedding and washed her hands, then opened the door to the stairs and called the maid to bring up the pot of duck congee.

随即遣走丫头，亲手盛了一碗捧给胡雪岩，她自己也盛了半碗，在一旁相陪。
She sent the maid away again, filled a bowl for Hu, and took half a bowl herself to keep him company.

“老爷，”月如闲闲问道：“是不是说二十三家的管总，要来个大扳位？”
“Master,” she asked casually, “is it true that you mean to move all twenty-three managers to different shops?”

“是啊！
“Yes.

老唐到德清就是商量这件事去的。”
That is what Old Tang has gone to Deqing to discuss.”

“你预备把老唐调到哪里？”
“Where do you mean to send him?”

“这还不晓得。”
“I do not know yet.”

“怎么你会不晓得呢？”
“How can you not know?”

“ ‘凭天断’，
“Heaven will decide.

我怎么会晓得？”
How could I know?”

“啥叫‘凭天娄’？”
“What do you mean, Heaven will decide?”

“抽签。”胡雪岩答说：
“We shall draw lots.

“二十三家典当分做大中小三等，分等抽签。
The twenty-three pawnshops will be divided into three classes—large, medium, and small—and the managers will draw within each class.

譬如顶大的有八家，这八家的管总合在一起抽签，抽到哪里是哪里。
Suppose there are eight in the largest class: their eight managers draw together, and each goes wherever his lot sends him.”

““这样说，老唐抽到苏州到苏州，抽到镇江到镇江？”
“Then if Old Tang draws Suzhou he goes to Suzhou, and if he draws Zhenjiang he goes to Zhenjiang?”

“不错。”
“Exactly.”


听得这话，月如将筷子一放，掩着脸踉踉跄跄地奔回卧室。
At this, Yueru put down her chopsticks, covered her face, and stumbled back into the bedroom.

胡雪岩大吃一惊，随即也跟了进去，
Startled, Hu followed.

只见她伏在床上，双肩耸动着在哭。
She lay face down on the bed, her shoulders shaking with sobs.

“月如，
“Yueru!

月如！”
Yueru!”

他尽管推着她的身子，她却不理，
Though he kept nudging her, she paid no attention.

但哭声仿佛止住了。
The sound of her crying did seem to stop.

“你到底为啥？
“What is the matter?

无事端端地哭得好伤心。”
Why are you suddenly so heartbroken?”

“我怎么不要伤心？”月如脸朝里床口发怨言：
Facing the inner side of the bed, she complained, “How could I not be?

“你死没良心！
You heartless creature!

把我骗到手，尝过新鲜了，马上想这么一个法子！
You have tricked me into your arms, had your taste of something new, and immediately thought of this scheme.

叫老唐带着我充军充到外县，你好眼不见为净！”
You will have Old Tang take me into exile in another county, so you need never set eyes on me again!”

“这是从哪里说起？”胡雪岩不由得失笑，
Hu could not help laughing in astonishment. “Where did that come from?

“我做梦也没有想到，你会把毫不相干的两桩事情扯在一起！”
I never dreamed you would connect two things that have nothing to do with each other.”

“哪里是毫不相干？
“Nothing to do with each other?

老唐调到外县，我自然要跟了去，
If Old Tang is sent away, I must go with him.

你好象一点都不在乎，
You do not seem to care in the least.

玩过就算数了。
You have had your pleasure, and that is the end of it.”

“这番指摘，不能说她没有道理，
Her accusation was not wholly unreasonable.

胡雪岩细想了一会说道：“你也不一定要跟老唐去，
After considering it, Hu said, “You need not necessarily go with him.

我替你另外买一幢房子。”
I could buy you another house.”

“做你的小公馆？”
“To be your secret household?”

“也不是啥小公馆……”
“It would not exactly be that…”

胡雪岩有些词穷了，月如却毫不放松。
He was at a loss, but Yueru pressed on.

“不是小公馆是啥呢？”她说：
“What else would it be?

“就算作为是老唐买的房子，我一个人住在杭州，别人问起来，我怎么回复人家？
Even if we said Old Tang had bought it, how should I explain living alone in Hangzhou? People would ask.

而且你要来了，总归有人晓得的，
And someone would be sure to discover your visits.

跟你的人不说，自然会有人到螺蛳太太面前去说，
If your attendants said nothing, somebody else would tell Madam Luosi.

总有一天带了人打上门来。
One day she would come with a crowd to attack me.

那时候我除了投河跳井，没有第二条路好走。
Then I should have no choice but to throw myself into a river or a well.”

“话说得驳不倒，胡雪岩愣了好半晌说：
Unable to refute her, Hu stood speechless for some time.

“月如，你晓得的，二十三家管总调动的事在前，
“You know the transfer plan came first.

我们今天会睡在一床，是我连昨天都没有想到的事。
Even yesterday I had no idea we would share a bed today.

本来是两桩不搭界的事情，现在倒好象扯在一起了。
The two things were entirely separate; now they seem tied together.

你倒说说看，有啥好办法？”
Tell me, then: what can we do?”


月如故意沉吟了一会，方始说道：
Yueru deliberately paused as though thinking it over.

“办法是有。
“There is a way.

先要问你，你是只想今天捡捡便宜呢，还是仍旧要我？”
But first I must ask: did you only want to take advantage of me today, or do you still want me?”

“仍旧要你。”
“I still want you.”

“那就只有一个办法，
“Then there is only one thing to do.

原样不动。”
Leave everything as it is.”

“怎么叫原样不动？”
“What do you mean?”

“别家的管总，你尽管去调动，老唐仍旧管公济，”月如又说：
“Move the other managers wherever you please, but leave Old Tang at Gongji.

“老唐是帮你管典当的头脑，跟别家不同，
He is the head of all your pawnshop managers, different from the others.

他不动是说得过去的。”
There is good reason to leave him where he is.”

“那怎么说得过去？
“How could that be justified?

一有了例外，大家不服。”
Once I make an exception, everyone will object.”

“那就大家不动。”月如又说：
“Then move none of them.

“我是不懂做生意，不过照我想，做生意全靠人头熟，
I know nothing about business, but surely it depends on knowing people.

忽然之间到了陌生地方，两只眼睛墨黑，
Arrive suddenly in a strange place and you are completely in the dark.

等到你看清楚，生意已经让别家抢走了。”
By the time you can see your way, other firms have taken the business.”

胡雪岩心里七上八下，盘算来盘算去，苦无兼顾的善策，
Hu turned the matter over anxiously, unable to find a solution that served both purposes.

最后叹口气说：
At last he sighed.

“只好大家不动。”
“We shall have to leave everyone where he is.”


唐子韶的“美人计”，元宝街的下人很快地都知道了，
The servants in Yuanbao Street soon knew of Tang Zishao’s beauty trap.

不过胡老太太治家极严，
Hu’s mother, however, ruled the household strictly.

将“来说是非者，便是是非人”这句俗语，奉为金科玉律，所以没有人敢到十二楼去说这个秘密。
She treated the saying “Those who bring you gossip are troublemakers themselves” as an inviolable law, so no one dared carry this secret into the Twelve Chambers.

但近处未传，远处却传到了。
It remained unspoken nearby but traveled far away.

古应春以抑郁的语气，将这件事告诉了七姑奶奶，而七姑奶奶不信。
Gu related it gloomily to the Seventh Lady, who refused to believe him.

“小爷叔不是这种人。
“Little Uncle is not that sort of man.

如果为了女人会把生意上商量好的事推翻不算，小爷叔哪里会有今天这种场面，
If a woman could make him overturn a settled business plan, how could he have built up what he has today?

老早败下来了。”
He would have ruined himself long ago.”

“我懒得跟你争。
“I cannot be bothered to argue.

好在他就要来接左大人，
He will soon be here to meet His Excellency Zuo.

你不妨当面问问他。”
Ask him yourself.”

“我当然要当面问他。”七姑奶奶继续为胡雪岩辩护，
“Of course I shall.” She continued defending Hu.

“二十三家典当管总仍然照旧，一走有他的道理。
“He must have his reasons for leaving the twenty-three managers in place.

小爷叔的打算不会错的。”
His judgment will not be wrong.”

第二天，胡雪岩就到了，仍旧住在古家，
Hu arrived the next day and stayed, as before, at Gu’s house.

应酬到半夜十一点多种才跟古应春一起回家。七姑奶奶照例预备了消夜在等他们。
He and Gu returned from their engagements after eleven that night to find the Seventh Lady waiting with the customary late supper.

把杯闲谈之际，七姑奶奶闲闲问道：“小爷叔，你二十三家典当管总调动的计划，听说打消了，
Over wine and conversation, she asked casually, “Little Uncle, I hear you have abandoned your plan to transfer the twenty-three managers.

是为啥？”
Why?”

“嗐，七姐，请你不要问了。”
“Ah, Seventh Sister, please do not ask.”

一听这话，七姑奶奶勃然变色，立即问说：
Her expression changed at once.

“为啥不要问？”
“Why should I not ask?”

“七姐，有趣的事，大家谈谈，
“Pleasant things are worth talking about.

没趣的事谈起来，连带你也不高兴，
Unpleasant things will only upset you as well.

何苦？”
Why bother?”

“这样说，是真的了。
“Then it is true.

真的姓唐的做了圈套，请你胡大先生去钻。
Tang really did lay a trap for you to walk into.

小爷叔，你怎么会做这种糊涂事？”
Little Uncle, how could you do anything so foolish?”


说到“糊涂”二字，嘴已经歪了，眼睛也斜了，脸红如火，
As she said “foolish,” her mouth twisted, her eyes turned askew, and her face became fiery red.

古应春叫声：“不好！”赶紧上前去扶，
“Something is wrong!” Gu cried, rushing to support her.

七姑奶奶已在凳子上坐不住，一头栽在地上，
She could no longer sit upright and fell headlong from her stool.

幸好地上铺了极厚的波斯羊毛地毯，头没有摔破。
Fortunately, the thick Persian wool carpet kept her head from being cut open.

“是中风！”
“A stroke!”

胡雪岩跳起身来喊道：“来人！”
Hu jumped up and shouted for servants.

于是一面叫进人来，扶起七姑奶奶，一面打发人去延医，
While some lifted her, others were sent for doctors.

胡雪岩关照去请在咸丰年间曾入宫“请脉”，号称太医的曹郎中，
Hu ordered them to fetch Doctor Cao, known as an imperial physician because he had been summoned to take pulses in the palace during the Xianfeng reign.

但古应春相信西医，且有一个熟识的医生，名叫艾礼脱，所以另外派人去请。
Gu trusted Western medicine and sent separately for a doctor he knew, Ailituo.

时已夜半，叩门将医生从床上叫起来，自然得费些工夫。
It was past midnight, and knocking up doctors from their beds naturally took time.

古应春倒还沉得住气，反是胡雪岩异样地焦急不安，
Gu remained comparatively composed; Hu was extraordinarily distressed.

望着躺在软榻上，闭着眼“呼噜、呼噜”只在喉间作痰响的七姑奶奶，搓着手蹀躞不停。
He paced and wrung his hands, watching the Seventh Lady on the couch, her eyes closed, a rattling sound of phlegm in her throat.

他知道七姑奶奶是听到他做了没出息的事，气恼过度，致生此变。
He knew that anger at his disgraceful conduct had brought this on.

倘或不治，则“我虽不杀伯仁，伯仁由我而死”，
If she died, it would be like the saying, “I did not kill Boren, but Boren died because of me.”

会一辈子歉疚在心，
Lifelong remorse would be his.

日子还过得下去？
How could he go on living with it?


好不容易将医生等到了，先来的是艾礼脱，
At last Ailituo arrived.

一看七姑奶奶躺在那里，用英语跟古应春说中风的病人，不宜横卧。
Seeing her lying flat, he told Gu in English that a stroke patient should not be left in that position.

古应春随即叫两名仆妇，把七姑奶奶扶了起来，靠在安乐椅上、左右扶持。
Gu immediately had two serving women sit her up in an armchair, supporting her on either side.

西医看病，没有“男女授受不亲”那一套，
Western doctors observed none of the rules against physical contact between the sexes.

艾礼脱打开皮包，取出听诊器挂在耳朵上，关照古应春解开七姑奶奶的衣钮，拿听筒按在她胸前听心跳。
Ailituo opened his bag, put the stethoscope to his ears, and had Gu unfasten her clothes so that he could listen to her heart.

诊断完了，撬开牙关，用温开水设法将他带来的药丸，让她吞了下去。
After examining her, he pried open her clenched teeth and contrived to get her to swallow a pill with warm water.

然后告诉古应春，六小时以后，如能苏醒，性命可保，
If she regained consciousness after six hours, he told Gu, her life could be saved.

他天亮后再来复诊。
He would return after daybreak.

正在谈着，曹郎中到了，
As he was speaking, Doctor Cao arrived.

艾礼脱脸色不大好看，抗议式地对古应春说，看西医就不能看中医。
Ailituo looked displeased and protested that someone receiving Western treatment could not also consult a Chinese physician.

这一下，让古应春为难了，跟胡雪岩商量，应该怎么办。
Gu was embarrassed and asked Hu what to do.

“你相信西医，自然是你作主。
“You trust Western medicine, so naturally you must decide.

曹郎中，病情他照看，方子由他照开，
Let Doctor Cao examine her and write his prescription as usual.

不吃他的药就是了。”
Simply do not give her his medicine.”

“不错，不错！
“Quite right.

这法子好。”
That is the way.”

古应春照他的话办。
Gu followed the suggestion.


艾礼脱的本领不错，
Ailituo was a capable doctor.

到了天亮，七姑奶奶居然张开眼睛了，但胡雪岩去倦得眼不开眼睛。
By daybreak the Seventh Lady had actually opened her eyes, though Hu was now too exhausted to keep his own open.

“小爷叔，你赶紧去睡一觉，
“Little Uncle, go and sleep.

下午还要去接左大人。”古应春说，
You still have to meet His Excellency Zuo this afternoon.

“尽管放心去睡，到时候我会叫你。”
Rest without worrying; I shall wake you in time.”

“能放心睡得着倒好了。”
“If only I could put my mind at ease enough to sleep.”

“小爷叔，死生有命，
“Life and death are ordained.

而且看样子也好转了，
Besides, she seems better.

你不必担心。”
You need not worry.”

话虽如此，胡雪岩如何放心得下？
But how could Hu be easy?

双眼虽涩重得睁不开，睡却睡不好，时时惊醒，
His eyes were sore and heavy, yet his sleep was broken by frequent starts.

不到中午就起身了。
He rose before noon.

“艾礼脱又来看病，说大致不要紧了，不过风瘫恐怕不免。带病延年，活上十几年的也多的是。”古应春说道：
“Ailituo has been again,” Gu told him. “He says the danger has largely passed, though paralysis is probably unavoidable. Many people live another ten years or more with such an illness.

“小爷叔办正事去吧，
Attend to your business, Little Uncle.

可惜我不能陪你，
I am sorry I cannot accompany you.

见了左大人，代我说一声。”
Please explain to His Excellency when you see him.”

“好，好！
“Of course.

我会说。”
I shall.”


左宗棠等过了慈禧太后的万寿，方始出京，奉准回籍扫墓，
Zuo had waited until after Cixi’s birthday before leaving Beijing, with permission to visit his ancestral graves.

十一月二十五日到湖南省城长沙，
He reached Changsha, the Hunan provincial capital, on the twenty-fifth day of the eleventh lunar month.

第一件事是去拜访郭嵩焘。
His first act was to call on Guo Songtao.

郭嵩焘与左宗棠有一段重重纠结的恩怨。
Their relations were tangled with old favors and grievances.

当咸丰八年左宗棠在湖南巡抚骆秉章幕府中时，一切独断独行，
In the eighth year of Xianfeng, when Zuo served on Governor Luo Bingzhang’s staff in Hunan, he decided everything for himself.

一天骆秉章在签押房里看书。忽然听见辕门放铳，
One day Luo was reading in his office when a gun sounded at the outer gate.

看辰光不是每天正午的“午时炮”，便问是怎么回事？
As it was not time for the regular noon gun, he asked what had happened.

听差告诉他：“左师爷拜折。”
“Adviser Zuo is ceremonially dispatching a memorial,” a servant replied.

连上奏折他都不知道，
Luo had not even known that a memorial was being sent.

湖南巡抚等于左宗棠在做，因而得了个外号，叫做“左邵御史”。
Zuo was virtually the governor of Hunan himself and acquired the nickname “Left Censor-in-Chief.”

巡抚照例挂“右副都御史”衔，叫左宗棠为左都御史，意思是说他比“右副部御史”巡抚的权还要重。
Governors normally carried the concurrent title of Right Vice Censor-in-Chief: the nickname implied that Zuo exercised still greater power.


其时有个湖南永州镇总兵樊燮，湖北恩施人，声名不佳，
At that time Fan Xie, brigade commander of Yongzhou in Hunan, was a man of poor reputation from Enshi in Hubei.

有一次去见左宗棠，谈到永州的防务情形，
On one visit, Zuo questioned him about Yongzhou’s defenses.

樊燮一问三不知，而且礼貌上不大周到，
Fan could answer nothing and was less than respectful.

左宗棠大为光火，当时甩了他一个大嘴巴，而且立即办了个奏稿，痛劾樊燮“贪纵不法，声名恶劣”，
Furious, Zuo slapped him hard across the face and immediately drafted a memorial denouncing him as “corrupt, unrestrained, lawless, and notorious.”

其中有“目不识丁”的考语，
Among its judgments was “unable to recognize a single written character.”

也不告诉骆秉章就发出去了。
He sent it without informing Luo.

樊燮是否“贪纵不法”，犹待查明，但“目不识丁”何能当总兵官？
Whether Fan was corrupt and lawless remained to be investigated, but how could a wholly illiterate man command a brigade?

当下光革职，后查办。
He was dismissed first and investigated afterward.

这“目不识丁”四字，在樊燮心里，比烙铁烫出来的还要深刻，
Those words burned more deeply into him than a branding iron.

“解甲归田”以后，好在克扣下来的军饷很不少，当下延聘名师教他的独子读书，
Once retired, he used the considerable military funds he had withheld to engage distinguished teachers for his only son.

书房里“天地君亲师”的木牌旁边，贴一张梅红笺，写的就是“目不识丁”四字。
Beside the study’s tablet honoring Heaven, Earth, the Sovereign, Parents, and Teachers, he pasted red paper bearing the charge of illiteracy.

他告诉他的儿子说：“左宗棠不过是个举子，就这么样的神气，
“Zuo is only a provincial graduate, yet he puts on such airs.

你将来不中进士，不是我的儿子。”
Unless you become a jinshi, you are no son of mine.”

他这个儿子倒也很争气，后来不但中了迸士，而且点了翰林，早年就是名士，
The son justified his hopes: he not only became a jinshi but entered the Hanlin Academy, winning literary distinction early.

此人就是樊增祥。
He was Fan Zengxiang.

一方面教子，一方面还要报仇。
While educating his son, Fan also sought revenge.

樊熨走门路，告到骆秉章的上司，两广总督官文那里，又派人进京，在都察院递呈鸣冤。
Through influence he appealed to Luo’s superior, Guanwen, described as Governor-General of Liangguang, and sent men to present his grievance at the Censorate in Beijing.

官文为此案出奏，有一句很厉害的话，叫做“一官两印”，意思是说有两个人在做湖南巡抚。
Guanwen’s memorial used the formidable phrase “one office, two seals,” meaning that two men were acting as governor of Hunan.

名器不可假人，而况是封疆大吏，
Official authority could not be lent to private hands, least of all the authority of a great provincial governor.

这件事便很严重了。
The matter was serious.


其时郭嵩焘是南书房翰林，
Guo Songtao was then a Hanlin scholar serving in the Southern Study.

他跟左宗棠的胞兄左宗植是儿女亲家，与左宗棠当然很熟，深知他才气过人，
His child had married a child of Zuo’s elder brother Zuo Zongzhi, so he knew Zuo well and understood his exceptional ability.

便跟同为南书房的翰林潘祖荫说：
He approached Pan Zuyin, another Southern Study scholar.

“左季高如果不在湖南，一走保不住，
“Without Zuo Jigao, Hunan cannot possibly hold out.

东南大局，不夏可问。
There would then be no hope for the situation in the southeast.

我跟他同乡，又是姻亲，不便进言，
I am his fellow provincial and a relative by marriage; it would be improper for me to speak.

老兄何妨上个摺子。”
Why not submit a memorial yourself?”

潘祖荫听他的话，果然上了个摺子，
Pan did so.

铺叙他的功绩以后，作了个结论：“国家不可一日无湖南，即湖南不可一日无左宗棠。”
After recounting Zuo’s achievements, he concluded, “The state cannot do without Hunan for a single day, and Hunan cannot do without Zuo Zongtang for a single day.”

咸丰一看，为之动容，当即传旨问曾国藩，左宗棠是仍旧在湖南好呢？还是调到曾国藩大营中，以便尽其所长。
Moved, the Xianfeng Emperor asked Zeng Guofan whether Zuo should remain in Hunan or be transferred to his headquarters, where his abilities could be fully used.

曾国藩回奏，左宗棠“刚明耐苦，晓畅兵机”。
Zeng replied that Zuo was “firm, clear-sighted, able to endure hardship, and thoroughly versed in military affairs.”

于是奉旨随同曾国藩襄办军务。
An edict assigned him to assist Zeng.

左宗棠因祸得福，多亏得藩祖荫、郭嵩焘，但他对潘、郭的态度，大不相同。
Misfortune had become good fortune, thanks largely to Pan and Guo, but Zuo treated the two men very differently.

对潘祖荫除了“三节两寿”必送一份极厚的礼金以外，
For Pan, he sent generous gifts of money on the customary three festivals and two birthdays.

知道潘祖荫好收藏金石碑版，当陕甘总督时，凡是关中有新出土的碑，初拓本一走专差送潘祖荫，
Knowing Pan’s interest in ancient inscriptions and stone monuments, he also sent the first rubbings of newly unearthed Guanzhong steles by special messenger while serving as Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu.

有时甚至连原碑都送到潘家。
Sometimes he sent the original stones themselves.


郭嵩焘是在洪杨失败后，奉旨出任广东巡抚。
After the Taiping defeat, Guo had been appointed Governor of Guangdong.

两广总督名瑞麟，与巡抚同驻广州，
Ruilin, Governor-General of Liangguang, also resided in Guangzhou.

“督抚同城”，常不和睦，
A governor and governor-general in the same city often failed to agree.

瑞麟贪而无能，但为内务府出身，有事可直接诉诸两宫太后，靠山很硬，
Ruilin was greedy and incompetent, but his Imperial Household background gave him direct access to both empress dowagers and powerful protection.

所以郭嵩焘深受其掣肘之苦而无可如何。
Guo suffered badly from his obstruction and could do little about it.

那知处境本已很难的郭嵩焘，万想不到多年好友。且曾加以援手的左宗棠会跟他为难，
Difficult though his position already was, he never expected Zuo, an old friend whom he had once helped, to turn against him as well.

为了协饷，除致函指南以外，且四次上奉祈，指摘郭嵩焘，措施如何不然。
Over supplementary military contributions, Zuo sent reproachful letters and submitted four memorials criticizing Guo’s administration.

郭、左失和的原因，有仲付传说，
Various stories explained their estrangement.

流传最盛的一个说法是，当郭嵩焘放广东巡抚时，湘阴文庙忽产灵芝，
The most widespread was that an auspicious lingzhi fungus had appeared in the Confucian temple at Xiangyin when Guo was appointed to Guangdong.

郭嵩焘的胞弟郭昆焘写信给老兄，以为是他开府的吉兆。
His younger brother Guo Kuntao wrote that this foretold good fortune for his new governorship.

左宗棠得知其事，大为不悦，说“文庙产灵芝。如果是吉兆，亦当应在我封爵一事上面，
Zuo supposedly took offense: “If lingzhi in the temple is an omen, it must refer to my ennoblement.

与郭家何干？“由此生了意见。
What has it to do with the Guo family?”

其实，湘阴文庙产灵芝，是常有之事，左宗棠亦不至于小气到连这种事都要争，
In fact, lingzhi appeared there often, and Zuo was not petty enough to quarrel over such a thing.

真正的原因是，洪杨军兴以后，带兵大员，就地筹饷，真所谓“有土斯有财”。
The real reason lay in military finance. Since the Taiping wars, commanders had raised their own funds: possession of territory meant possession of revenue.

李鸿章最懂得这个道理，所以始终霸住江苏，尤其是上海这个地盘不放，
Li Hongzhang understood this best and never loosened his grip on Jiangsu, especially Shanghai.

左宗棠却只得浙江一省，每苦不足，
Zuo had only Zhejiang, whose resources frequently fell short.

看出广东是大有生发之地。所以狠狠心不顾感情友谊，一再攻讦郭嵩焘。
Seeing Guangdong’s possibilities, he hardened his heart against friendship and repeatedly attacked Guo.

最后终于如愿以偿，由他的大将蒋益澧接了郭嵩焘的手。
At last his own general, Jiang Yili, replaced him.

不过蒋益澧的广东巡抚，干不多久就被调走了。
Jiang’s tenure as Governor of Guangdong, however, was brief; he too was soon transferred.


郭嵩焘因此郁郁不得志。
Guo remained depressed by his frustrated career.

光绪建元，起用在籍大员，他跟曾国荃同被征召至京，
At the beginning of the Guangxu reign, senior officials living in retirement were recalled, and he and Zeng Guoquan were summoned to Beijing.

曾国荃放了陕西巡抚，因为不愿与陕甘总督左宗棠共事，改任河东河道总督，
Zeng was appointed Governor of Shaanxi, but, unwilling to serve with Zuo, was transferred to the governor-generalship of the Hedong River Conservancy.

郭嵩焘则奉派为福建按察史。
Guo became Judicial Commissioner of Fujian.

这在当过巡抚的人来说，是很委屈的，不过他还是接了事。
For a former governor, this was humiliating, but he accepted it.

不久，诏命开缺，以侍郎候补，充任出使英国钦差大臣。
Soon afterward he was released from the post, placed on the expectant list for a vice-ministership, and appointed imperial envoy to Britain.

其时云南发生英国公使翻译马嘉理，越滇缅边境接应英国武装“探路队”，为中国军民所杀，
At that time the British minister’s interpreter, Margary, had crossed the Yunnan–Burma frontier to meet a British armed surveying party and been killed by Chinese troops and civilians.

因而引起的很严重的交涉。
A serious diplomatic dispute followed.

英国公使咸妥玛表示，郭嵩焘出使英国，如果在国书上表明中国认错字样，可即赴任，否则应候云南案结后再赴英国。
The British minister, Wade, said Guo could leave at once if his credentials acknowledged China’s fault; otherwise he must wait until the Yunnan case was settled.

总署诸大臣都认为中国不能认错，郭嵩焘亦就不能出国，
The Zongli Yamen ministers refused such an admission, so Guo could not sail.

奉旨署理兵部侍郎，并在总署行走。
He was made acting Vice-Minister of War and assigned to the Yamen.


郭嵩焘对办洋务，一面主张公平合理，认为非此不足以析服洋人。
Guo believed foreign affairs required fairness and reason, without which foreigners could not be persuaded.

他认为马嘉理被杀一案，云南巡抚岑毓英不能说没有责任，
In his view, Governor Cen Yuying of Yunnan could not disclaim responsibility.

当案发以后，意存掩护，又不查明击杀情由，据实奏报，一味倭罪于深山中的野人。
After the killing, Cen had sought to protect those involved, failing to establish the circumstances and report them truthfully, and simply blaming remote mountain tribes.

而朝中士大夫又因为官兵所杀的是洋人，群起袒护岑毓英，以至于英国更觉不平，态度亦日趋强硬。
Officials at court defended him because the man killed by the troops was a foreigner, making the British feel more wronged and increasingly intransigent.

郭嵩焘以为，这件纠纷固结不解，全由不讲公平、不讲事理之故，
Guo attributed the deadlock entirely to this absence of fairness and reason.

因而奉命入总署之日，便单衔上奏，请旨“将岑毓英先后酿成事端之处，交部严加议处，以为恃虚骄之气，而不务沉心观理、考察详情，以贻累国家者戒。”
On entering the Yamen, he submitted a memorial under his sole name, requesting a strict disciplinary review by the ministry of Cen’s part in provoking the affair, “as a warning to those who rely on empty arrogance instead of calmly examining principles and circumstances, and thereby burden the state.”

郭嵩焘平时讲洋务，本已为守旧的“卫道君子”所不满，
Conservative defenders of orthodoxy already resented Guo’s views.

如今居然参劾杀洋人的岑毓英，在他们看，显然是私通外国，
To impeach a man who had killed foreigners now seemed clear proof of collusion with foreign powers.

因而引起了公愤，
His action provoked general indignation.

连他平素往来密切的朋友、门生，对他亦很不谅解，湖南则有许多人不认他是同乡。
Even his close friends and former pupils could not understand him; many Hunan men would no longer acknowledge him as a fellow provincial.

此外京师有人做了一副对联骂他：“出乎其类，拔乎其萃，不容于尧舜之世。
In Beijing, someone wrote a mocking couplet: “Outstanding among his kind, surpassing all his fellows—yet unwelcome in an age of Yao and Shun.

未能事人，焉能事鬼，
Unable even to serve men, how can he serve devils?

何必去父母之邦？
Why must he leave his parents’ land?”

“到得第二年七月底，中英订立《烟台条约》，“滇案”解决。
At the end of the seventh lunar month of the following year, the Chefoo Convention settled the Yunnan dispute.

郭嵩焘可以启程赴英国了，当时称为“放洋”，而“放洋”以前又发生了一件很不愉快的事。
Guo could finally sail for Britain, but another unhappy incident occurred before his departure.


有个广东人叫刘锡鸿，原任刑部员外郎，此人是郭嵩焘在广东的旧识，谈起洋务来，颇为投机。
Liu Xihong, a Cantonese formerly serving as an assistant departmental director in the Ministry of Punishments, was an old Guangdong acquaintance with whom Guo had enjoyed discussing foreign affairs.

此时希望跟郭嵩焘一起放洋。
He wanted to accompany him.

但谈洋务是一回事，办洋务又是一回事，
Talking was one thing, however, and conducting diplomacy another.

郭嵩焘认为刘锡鸿脾气太刚、好意气用事，而办洋务是“水磨工夫”，颇不相宜。
Guo thought Liu too rigid and impulsive for work requiring patient, delicate effort.

哪知刘锡鸿不死心，托出郭嵩焘的一个好友朱孙治来关说。
Liu persisted, enlisting Guo’s friend Zhu Sunzhi to intercede.

朱孙治向郭嵩焘说：“你批评他不宜办洋务的话，我都跟他说了，他亦很有自知之明，表示一切不问，你只当带一个可以谈谈，以解异国寂寞的朋友好了。”
Zhu said he had repeated Guo’s criticisms to Liu, who recognized his faults and promised not to meddle: Guo should simply take him as a friend with whom to talk and relieve the loneliness of a foreign country.

听得这样说，郭嵩焘可怜刘锡鸿穷困不得意，便上奏保他充任参赞。
Moved by Liu’s poverty and lack of prospects, Guo recommended him as a counselor.

刘锡鸿是个司员，而且只是六品的员外郎，论资格只能当参赞。
A mere sixth-rank departmental official, Liu qualified for no higher diplomatic appointment.

不过上谕下来，竟是“刑部员外郎刘锡鸿着即开缺，以五品京堂候补，并加三品衔，充出使英国副使”。
Yet the edict released him from the Ministry of Punishments, made him an expectant fifth-rank metropolitan court official, added an honorary third-rank title, and appointed him deputy envoy to Britain.

这种例子，殊为少见，
Such a precedent was rare.

其中有个内幕，军机大臣李鸿藻对郭嵩焘的态度，有此怀疑，
Behind it lay Grand Councillor Li Hongzao’s mistrust of Guo.

怕他出使后，处处帮英国人讲话，因而提拔刘锡鸿，以副使的身分去钳制正使。
Fearing that Guo would always take Britain’s side, he had raised Liu to deputy rank to restrain the chief envoy.

这刘锡鸿是个不明事理的人，以为李鸿藻派他去当“打手”，
Liu misunderstood his mission as that of an enforcer.

所以谢恩以后，便去看郭嵩焘，责问他为何不保他当副使而当参赞？
After thanking the throne, he visited Guo and demanded to know why Guo had proposed him only as counselor, rather than deputy envoy.

说他不够朋友，另外还有很难听的话，等于是骂了郭嵩焘一顿。
He accused him of failing a friend and added much abusive language.


郭嵩焘气得半死，总是遇到这种恩将仇报的人，只好自怨命中注定。
Guo was furious, but could only blame fate for continually bringing him people who repaid kindness with hostility.

后来刘锡鸿果然处处跟他为难，而且大吵大闹，不顾体统，
Liu indeed obstructed him at every turn and made disgraceful scenes.

郭嵩焘写信给李鸿章，形容共事为“鬼嗥于室，狐啸于梁”，
In a letter to Li Hongzhang, Guo described their service together as “ghosts howling in the rooms, foxes screeching among the rafters.”

公使馆的上下不安，可想而知。
The unrest throughout the legation was easy to imagine.

其时刘锡鸿已调充驻德公使，可以单衔上奏，
By then Liu had become minister to Germany and could submit memorials independently.

彼此互劾，而由于刘锡鸿有李鸿藻撑腰，占了上风。
They impeached one another, and Liu’s backing from Li Hongzao gave him the advantage.

李鸿藻的门下，赫赫有名的“翰林四谏”之一张佩纶，上奏“请撤回驻英使臣”。
Zhang Peilun, one of the celebrated Four Hanlin Remonstrants and a follower of Li Hongzao, called for the envoy to Britain to be recalled.

郭嵩焘大为泄气，一再求去，
Discouraged, Guo repeatedly asked to leave.

终于在光绪五年七月改派曾国藩的长子曾纪泽接替郭嵩焘，
In the seventh lunar month of Guangxu’s fifth year, Zeng Guofan’s eldest son, Zeng Jize, was appointed to replace him.

不过刘锡鸿亦同时垮台，改派郭嵩焘所欣赏的李凤苞使德。
Liu fell at the same time, however, and Li Fengbao, whom Guo admired, succeeded him in Germany.

这是李鸿章力争的结果。
This was the result of Li Hongzhang’s vigorous intervention.


郭嵩焘在英国博得极好的声望，所以于郭之去，多表惋惜。
Guo had won an excellent reputation in Britain, where his departure was widely regretted.

郭嵩焘原配早死，继室下堂，
His first wife had long been dead, and his second had been put aside.

只带了个姓梁的姨太太赵英，
He had taken only a concubine surnamed Liang to Britain.

照她的身分是不能觐见维多利亚女王的，竟亦破例特许。
Her status should have barred an audience with Queen Victoria, yet an exception had been made for her.

但在英国如此，回国后郭嵩焘自知李鸿藻这班人不会放过他，
However well he had been treated there, Guo knew that Li Hongzao’s party would not leave him in peace at home.

而且已六十二岁，因而决意引退，
Already sixty-two, he resolved to retire.

一到上海即称病，不回京复命，而请开缺，
On reaching Shanghai he pleaded illness rather than returning to Beijing to report, and requested release from office.

终得如愿以偿，回湖南后住在长沙。
His wish was eventually granted, and he settled in Changsha.

身虽在野，并不消极，
Retirement did not mean indifference to public affairs.

关于时政，特别是洋务方面，常跟李鸿章、曾国荃书信往米，细作讨论。
He corresponded frequently with Li Hongzhang and Zeng Guoquan, discussing current policy, especially foreign affairs, in detail.

日子过得也还闲适。
His days were fairly tranquil.

这一年，光绪七年，郭嵩焘年初年尾有两件比较快意之事，
In the seventh year of Guangxu, two events, one early and one late, gave him particular satisfaction.

一件是二月间，调回国充任通政使司参议的刘锡鸿，因为李鸿章敲掉了他的“洋饭碗”记恨在心，奏劾李鸿章跋扈不臣，俨然帝制。
In the second lunar month, Liu Xihong, recalled to serve as a councillor in the Office of Transmission, denounced Li Hongzhang as overbearing, disloyal, and practically assuming imperial powers. He resented Li for having broken his diplomatic rice bowl.

李鸿章正在红的时候，刘锡鸿自不量力，出以此举，自然是自讨没趣，
Li was then at the height of favor, and Liu’s ill-judged attack merely invited humiliation.

上谕斥责其“信口诬蔑，交部议处”。
An edict condemned his “reckless slander” and referred him for disciplinary review.

结果竟落得个革职的处分。
The result was dismissal.


再一件就是左宗棠来拜访。
The other satisfaction was Zuo’s visit.

排场阔极，顶马、跟马高脚牌，前听后拥一顶绿呢大轿，
His procession was magnificent: horses before and behind, lofty boards displaying his titles, and crowds of attendants around a great green-covered chair.

内中坐的是头戴宝石顶、双眼花翎、身穿四开褉袍黄马褂，鼻架一副大墨晶镜的东阁大学士烙靖候。
Within sat the Grand Secretary of the Eastern Pavilion and Marquis Kejing, wearing a jeweled hat button, a double-eyed peacock feather, a four-slit robe, and a yellow riding jacket, with large dark crystal spectacles on his nose.

首府长沙知府及首县长沙县，早就在郭嵩焘家附近，清道等候。
The prefect and county magistrate of Changsha had already cleared the streets near Guo’s house and stood waiting.

湖南省的藩、两司、候补道等等，亦来站班。
Hunan’s two provincial commissioners, expectant intendants, and other officials also attended.

可是郭家双扉紧闭，拒而不纳，
Yet Guo’s double doors remained firmly shut.

左宗棠只好在大门口下轿，由戴红顶子的“材官”上门投帖。
Zuo had to alight outside and send a red-buttoned military aide with his visiting card.

“不敢当，不敢当！”郭家门上到左宗棠面前，打千说道：
Guo’s doorkeeper came out, made a formal obeisance, and said, “We cannot presume to receive such an honor.

“请大人回驾。”
Please have Your Excellency’s chair turn back.”

左宗棠早已料到有此一着。一点都不生气，和颜悦色地答说：
Zuo had expected this and showed no anger.

“你跟你家老爷去回，说我是来看五十年的故人，
“Tell your master that I have come to see a friend of fifty years.

便衣不恭敬，所以穿了官服来的。”
Informal clothes would not show sufficient respect, so I have worn my official robes.”

门上一进去，久无消息。
The servant went in, and no answer came.

首县看“爵相”下不了台，硬闯进去跟郭嵩焘打躬作揖，说是“如果不见，全城文武亦都僵在那里了。”
Seeing the marquis-grand secretary left in this embarrassing position, the county magistrate forced his way inside and pleaded with Guo: unless he admitted Zuo, all the civil and military officials in the city would remain stranded outside.

请他体恤下情。
He begged consideration for their predicament.

总算说动了郭嵩焘，开正门迎接，不过他自己只是站在大厅上等候。
At last Guo agreed to open the main gate, though he himself waited in the hall.

“老哥！”左宗棠见面便说：“宗棠无状，
“My old friend,” Zuo said on meeting him, “I behaved disgracefully.

特来请罪。”
I have come expressly to ask your pardon.”

接着，拂一拂马蹄袖，捞起四开褉袍下摆，跪了下去。
He brushed back his horseshoe cuffs, gathered the skirt of his robe, and knelt.

“不敢，不敢！”
“I dare not accept this!”

郭嵩煮也只好下跪答礼。
Guo had no choice but to kneel in return.

随从官员，将主客二人都搀扶了起来，
The accompanying officials helped both men up.

左宗棠便自责当年的不是，也不解释是为了军饷，“有土斯有财”的缘故，
Zuo reproached himself for his former conduct, making no attempt to explain it by military funding or the need for territory and revenue.

只连声：“是我该死，
He merely repeated, “I deserved to die.

是我荒唐。”
I behaved outrageously.”


左宗棠一向健谈，
Zuo was always talkative.

谈西征、谈边防、谈京里的新闻，又从曾国藩谈起往事，
He discussed the western campaign, frontier defenses, and news from Beijing, then turned from Zeng Guofan to old times.

一直到中午都没有告辞的意思，
Noon arrived with no sign of his leaving.

郭嵩焘也不便象督抚会客那样“端茶碗送客”，便只好留饭。
Guo could hardly dismiss him by lifting a teacup, as governors did with visitors, and had to invite him to lunch.

随从倒是有首县办差，从长沙第一家大馆子玉楼东去叫了酒席来，在附近的关帝庙接待。
The county magistrate provided for the attendants, ordering a banquet from Yuloudong, Changsha’s foremost restaurant, to be served at a nearby temple of Guan Di.

左宗棠却必须是郭嵩煮的家庖，才是待客之道。
Zuo, however, must be fed from Guo’s own kitchen if hospitality were to be properly observed.

好在湘军出身的达官，除了胡林翼以外，都不甚讲究饮食。
Fortunately, senior men from the Hunan Army, apart from Hu Linyi, were undemanding about food.

左宗棠喜欢吃狗肉，称之为“地羊”，
Zuo liked dog meat, which he called “earth mutton.”

有些一味，加上腊味，再炒一盘去骨的东安鸡，在他便是盛馔了。
A dish of that, some cured meats, and a plate of boned Dong’an chicken made a feast for him.

一顿饭吃到未末申初，
The meal lasted until about three in the afternoon.

左宗棠开始兴尽告辞。
Only then did his enthusiasm ebb enough for him to leave.

临行时做个手势，材官递上一个红封套，
At the door he gestured, and a military aide handed him a red envelope.

左宗棠双手奉上，口中说道：“不腆之仪，聊肋卒岁，
Offering it in both hands, he said, “A modest token to help you through the year’s end.

务请赏收。”
I earnestly beg you to accept it.”

郭嵩焘不肯收，左宗棠非送不可。
Guo refused; Zuo insisted.

当着好些湖南的文武官儿，郭嵩焘觉得起了争执，有失体统，便收了下来，不过，心里已经打算好了，
Before so many Hunan officials, Guo considered a dispute undignified and accepted, having already decided what to do.

拆开封套一看，是阜康钱庄所出的一万两银票，
Inside was a Fukang banknote for ten thousand taels.

当即提起笔来批上“注销”二字，拿个信封装了，送到左宗棠的行辕。
He immediately wrote “Canceled” across it, put it in another envelope, and sent it to Zuo’s temporary headquarters.

照道理是要回拜的，郭嵩焘也免了这套俗礼。
Courtesy required a return visit, but Guo dispensed with that convention too.

左宗棠到头来，还是讨了个没趣。
In the end, Zuo had still met with a rebuff.


十二月初二到湘阴，
On the second day of the twelfth lunar month, Zuo reached Xiangyin.

当天晚上，就收到一道由湖南巡抚衙门派专差送来的军机处的“廷寄”。
That evening a special messenger from the governor’s office delivered a confidential dispatch from the Grand Council.

廷寄中说，有人参劾湖广总督李瀚章“任用私人，纵容劣员，该省防军缺额，虚糜帑金，贻害地方，李瀚章本人默货无套，
It reported an impeachment of Li Hanzhang, Governor-General of Huguang, for employing favorites, indulging corrupt officials, maintaining defense units below their authorized strength while wasting public funds, harming the people, and amassing wealth without restraint.

民怨日深。”
Popular resentment was deepening.

原奏肿列了李瀚章许多劣迹，其中情节重大者四款：
Four charges were particularly serious.

一，湖北全省厘金，岁收三四百万，报部则仅四万。
First, Hubei’s likin revenue amounted to three or four million taels annually, but only forty thousand was reported to the ministry.

二，竹木税年收百万，报部仅三万。
Second, bamboo and timber duties yielded a million a year, while only thirty thousand was reported.

湖广总督衙门每日用银七百五十两，即在此中开支，年耗帑银二十七万余两。
The governor-general’s office spent seven hundred and fifty taels a day from this source, consuming over two hundred and seventy thousand annually.

三，以公家轮船，载运私货，公然贩卖。
Third, public steamers carried private merchandise for open sale.

四，李瀚章在扬州、芜湖均设有当铺。
Fourth, Li owned pawnshops in both Yangzhou and Wuhu.

清朝的规制，凡是督抚被参，视情节轻重作不同的处置。
Qing procedure varied according to the gravity of an impeachment against a governor or governor-general.

情节较重者，常由京里特派大员，至少是尚书，且须资格较被参督抚为深的，前往查办。
A serious case usually brought a special investigator from Beijing, at least a minister and senior in standing to the accused.

为了防备被参督抚事先湮灭证据，所以明发上谕中只说派某人往某地出差，所谓“某地”决非被参督抚所管的省份，
To prevent the destruction of evidence, the public edict named another province as his destination.

譬如说派到四川出差，湖北是必经之地，
An assignment to Sichuan, for example, required passage through Hubei.

一到武昌，立即传旨，随带司员马上动手，封库的封库，查帐的查帐、来他一个措手不及。
On reaching Wuchang, the investigator would proclaim the actual order, and his staff would immediately seal the treasury and inspect the books, taking the accused by surprise.

情节轻微，或者有意把案情看得不重，便就近派官阶资格较高音查办或查夏。
In a minor case, or one the court intended to treat lightly, a nearby official of superior rank and seniority would be appointed to investigate formally or simply ascertain the facts and report.

左宗棠奉到的上谕是：“将所奏各节，确切查明，据实具奏。”
Zuo’s order read, “Establish the truth of each allegation and report the facts.”

这是查夏，不是查办，
This was an inquiry for a report, not a formal disciplinary investigation.

可是左宗棠不理这一套。
Zuo disregarded the distinction.


十二月十三到武昌时，李瀚章已经接到李鸿章的通知，知道左宗棠要来查案，
By the time he reached Wuchang on the thirteenth, Li Hanzhang had been warned by Li Hongzhang that Zuo was coming to investigate.

需先示意布政使衔候补道杨宗濂告假回籍。
He had prompted Yang Zonglian, an expectant circuit intendant with the honorary title of provincial treasurer, to take leave and return home.

此人在成丰末年，以户部员外郎在原籍江苏金坛办团练。
In the closing years of Xianfeng, Yang had organized militia in his native Jintan, Jiangsu, while holding rank as an assistant departmental director in the Ministry of Revenue.

同治元年，江苏土绅凑集了十八万银子，雇用英国轮船到安庆，接淮军到上海打太平军时，杨宗濂就是往来奔走接头的人，
In Tongzhi’s first year, when Jiangsu gentry raised a hundred and eighty thousand taels to hire British steamers to bring the Anhui Army from Anqing to fight the Taipings at Shanghai, Yang had gone back and forth making the arrangements.

以此渊源，与李鸿章的关系很深，
This gave him a close connection with Li Hongzhang.

李鸿章镇压捻军那两年，杨宗濂替他管过营务处，以后一直在湖北当道员。
During Li’s two years fighting the Nian, Yang had managed his army administration; afterward he remained a circuit intendant in Hubei.

李氏兄弟相继督鄂，李宗濂由“李二先生”的部属变为“李大先生”的部属，管理汉口“新关”。
As the Li brothers successively governed the province, he passed from the service of “Second Master Li” to that of “Eldest Master Li,” managing Hankou’s New Customs.

“关差”一向是好差使，
A customs assignment was always lucrative.

汉口是长江的第一个大码头，收入以竹木税为大宗。
Hankou was the Yangtze’s foremost commercial port, and bamboo and timber duties formed its principal customs revenue.

西南深山中的木材，以湘西辰州为集散地成“木排”，由阮江入洞庭湖，经岳阳入长江，在汉口交易。
Timber from the southwestern mountains was assembled into rafts at Chenzhou in western Hunan, floated down the Ruan River into Dongting Lake, then through Yueyang to the Yangtze for sale at Hankou.

宗棠早就听湘西的“排客”谈过，汉口“新关”收竹木税的种种弊端，
Zuo had long heard western Hunan raft merchants describe abuses at the New Customs.

所以一到武昌，就要找杨宗濂。
On reaching Wuchang, he immediately sought Yang.


由于奉旨查案，所以左宗棠跟李瀚章不作私人的交往，
Acting under an imperial commission, Zuo avoided private dealings with Li Hanzhang.

在行辕以一角公文咨湖广总督衙门，“请饬杨宗濂到案备询”，
From his temporary headquarters he sent the governor-general’s office a formal request that Yang be ordered to attend for questioning.

而夏文是“该员业已告假回籍，无从传饬”。
The reply stated that Yang had already taken leave and returned home, and could not be summoned.

这一下左宗棠大为光火，
Zuo was furious.

用“札子”下给汉黄德道及武昌府，“催令杨宗濂迅赴江宁问话“。
He issued directives to the Han–Huang–De circuit intendant and the prefect of Wuchang, requiring them to have Yang proceed promptly to Jiangning for questioning.

一面出奏：”臣前次回湘，路过新关，杨宗濂避而未见，
He also memorialized: “On my previous journey home through the New Customs, Yang Zonglian avoided meeting me.

此次又先朗告假回籍，
On this occasion he again obtained leave in advance and returned home.

是否有意规避，虽未可知，
Whether this was deliberate evasion cannot yet be known.

而查询杨宗濂素日声名平常、性情浮动，则众论相同，
Inquiries into his ordinary reputation, however, agree that it is undistinguished and his disposition unstable.

无代其剖白者。
No one speaks in his defense.”

“至于经收竹木税有无弊端，”应俟查取票根底薄，传杨宗濂到到案质询，方昭核实“。
Whether there had been fraud in the bamboo and timber duties must await examination of the receipt counterfoils and original ledgers, together with Yang’s personal interrogation.

接着声明：因为须赴两江接任，所以传杨宗濂到江宁备询，
Zuo explained that, as he had to assume office in Liangjiang, Yang was being summoned to Jiangning.

同时以”贪鄙狄诈“的考语，请旨将杨宗濂”先行革职，听候查办“。
Describing him as “greedy, mean, and deceitful,” he requested Yang’s immediate dismissal pending investigation.

此外汉黄德道何维键、候补知府李谦，都是李瀚章的私人，
He Weijian, the circuit intendant, and Li Qian, an expectant prefect, were also Li Hanzhang’s clients.

左宗棠亦毫不客气，
Zuo spared neither.

对何维键以“庸软无能”四字考语，奏请“开缺送部引见”，意思是请慈禧太后亲自考查，
Calling He “weak and incompetent,” he requested removal from his post and presentation through the ministry for an imperial audience—in other words, examination by Cixi herself.

对李谦则谓之“性善圆通、难期振作”，请旨交湖北巡抚彭祖贤“察看”。
Li Qian was “adept at accommodating himself to others, unlikely to show vigor.” Zuo asked that Governor Peng Zuxian of Hubei keep him under observation.


奏折中还将李瀚章训了一顿，
The memorial also lectured Li Hanzhang.

他说：李瀚章一门，遭逢圣时，功名大显，
The Li family, Zuo wrote, had enjoyed an enlightened reign and risen to great distinction.

亲党交游，能自立的亦颇不乏人。不过依附者亦很多，
Many relatives and associates could support themselves, but many others lived through dependence.

当时随从立功，身致富贵者，又各有其亲友，辗转依附，
Those who had earned wealth and rank in the brothers’ service had their own friends and relations, creating successive chains of patronage.

久而久之，恃势妄为，
In time such people abused their influence.

官府处置为难，不能不作姑息，乡里受其欺凌，亦惟有敢怒而不敢言。
Officials found them difficult to punish and tolerated them; local people endured their bullying, angry but afraid to speak.

由于“贤者不肯规之以正，懦者畏其忌嫉，
“The worthy will not correct them, while the timid fear their resentment.

谣诼纷兴、事端叠起，
Slanders multiply and troubles arise one after another.

洵非家门之福。
This can scarcely benefit the family.

宜以身作则，毋与乡邦人士争势竟利，遇事敛抑，免为怨府。
They should lead by example, refrain from contending with their fellow provincials for power and profit, and exercise restraint, lest they become a focus of resentment.

其李鸿章、李瀚章所难尽言者，臣等忝仕疆圻，亦当尽心化诲，俾知以义为利，
Where Li Hongzhang and Li Hanzhang cannot say everything necessary, we who have the honor to hold provincial office should earnestly instruct these people, so they may understand that righteousness is their true advantage.

如思保世承家，为报国之本，
Preserving the family across generations is the foundation of service to the state.

则李氏亲友之福，亦李鸿章、李瀚章一门之福也”。
Such conduct would benefit both the Li brothers’ relatives and friends and the family itself.”

话说得很不客气，但左宗棠自以为对李瀚章多所开脱，帮了他很大的忙。
The language was far from gentle, yet Zuo believed he had largely exonerated Li and done him a great service.

十二月十九拜发奏折以后，随即坐长江轮船，鼓悼东下，到江宁拜印接任。
After ceremonially dispatching the memorial on the nineteenth day of the twelfth month, he took a Yangtze steamer east to Jiangning to receive the seal and assume office.

因为如此，使得胡雪岩扑了个空。
Thus Hu’s journey to meet him came to nothing.

原来左宗棠原先的计划是：回湖南原籍祭祖扫墓以后，南下由广东至福建，自厦门坐特派的南洋兵舰到上海，再转江宁接任。
Zuo’s original plan had been to visit the ancestral graves, then travel south through Guangdong to Fujian, take a specially dispatched Nanyang warship from Xiamen to Shanghai, and proceed to Jiangning.

这是为了一履旧日百战立功之地，同时还有“南洋大臣”巡海之意。
Besides revisiting the ground of his old campaigns, he intended a coastal inspection in his capacity as Nanyang Minister.

不想一到湘阴，有奉旨查夏李瀚章纵容劣员一案，前后耽误了十一天，不能不走捷径，在年前赶到江宁接任。
The imperial inquiry received at Xiangyin delayed him eleven days, forcing him to take the direct route if he was to assume office before the new year.


“既在如此，小爷叔你回杭州过年吧。”古应春说：
“In that case, Little Uncle, go home to Hangzhou for the new year,” Gu said.

“过了年，我陪小爷叔专程到南京去一趟。”
“Afterward I shall accompany you on a special visit to Nanjing.”

“也只好这样子。
“There seems no other choice.

不过，七姐的病，我实在不放心。”
But I cannot stop worrying about Seventh Sister.”

“不要紧的。
“She will be all right.

人是醒过来了，只要慢慢调养，逐渐会好的。
She has regained consciousness; with patient nursing she will gradually improve.

医生说：中风这种病，全靠调理。
The doctor says a stroke depends above all on aftercare.

将来总归带病延年了。”
She will have to live on with the illness.”

胡雪岩跟七姑奶奶情如兄妹，
Hu and the Seventh Lady were as close as brother and sister.

看她人虽醒了，却还不能说话，
Though awake, she still could not speak.

不过人是认得的，一见双泪交流，嘴唇翕动，不知多少有苦难言，
She recognized him, and tears streamed down her face as her lips moved, full of things she could not say.

胡雪岩忍不住也掉眼泪。
Hu could not hold back his own tears.

“小爷叔，小爷叔，千万不要如此。”古应春劝道：
“Little Uncle, please do not,” Gu urged.

“这样子反让病人心里难过。”
“That only makes her feel worse.”

胡雪岩点点头，抹掉眼泪，强作欢颜，
Hu nodded, wiped his eyes, and forced a cheerful expression.

坐在病榻前向七姑奶奶说道：“七姐，年底下事情太多，我不能不走。
Sitting beside the bed, he said, “Seventh Sister, there is so much business at year’s end that I must leave.

你慢慢调养，
Take time to recover.

我记得你的八字上，说你四十四岁有一关，来势虽凶，凶而不险，
I remember your horoscope said you would meet a crisis at forty-four: fierce in its onset, but not truly dangerous.

过了这一关，寿至七十八。
Once past it, you would live to seventy-eight.

今年年内春，算壬午年，你正好四十四，
This year the Beginning of Spring falls before the lunar year ends, so the reckoning is already the renwu year, when you turn forty-four.

你这一关应过了，
You have passed the crisis.

明年秋天，老太太等你来吃寿酒。”
Next autumn Mother will be waiting for you at her birthday feast.”

七姑奶奶口不能言，却听得懂，只在枕上摆头，表示会意。
Unable to speak but understanding him, she moved her head on the pillow to acknowledge his words.

“还有句，七姐，
“One more thing, Seventh Sister.

那种荒唐事情，偶尔一回，
That foolish conduct was a single lapse.

以后决不会再做了。”
It will never happen again.”

七姑奶奶致疾之由，便是由于气恼胡雪岩的荒唐，所以这句对她是最好的安慰，
Anger at his behavior had brought on her illness, and this was the best comfort he could offer.

居然含着泪笑了。
She even smiled through her tears.


离了病榻，打点回乡。
Leaving her bedside, Hu prepared to return home.

当天晚上，古应春为胡雪岩饯行，
Gu gave him a farewell dinner that evening.

只为七姑奶奶在病中，所以在家由厨娘备了几味精致的肴馔，
With the Seventh Lady ill, they stayed at home, where the cook prepared a few delicate dishes.

也不邀陪客，只是两人对酌。
No other guests were invited; the two men drank alone.

在餐桌上，采运局的司事送来了一封信，是左宗棠自湘阴所发，
During dinner a clerk from the Purchasing and Transport Bureau brought a letter from Zuo, written at Xiangyin.

告诉胡雪岩因为奉旨赴武昌办案，原来的行程取消，
It explained that the imperial order to investigate at Wuchang had canceled his original itinerary.

武昌事毕，径赴江宁，约胡雪岩灯节以后，在江宁相会。
When that business was finished, he would go straight to Jiangning, and wished to meet Hu there after the Lantern Festival.

此外又托胡雪岩查一件事，说是“江苏司关厘局，及鄂湘皖西的督销局，每月均有专拨之饷。
He also requested a discreet inquiry: “The treasury, customs, likin and other bureaus in Jiangsu, together with the salt-sales supervision bureaus in Hubei, Hunan, Anhui and the province abbreviated as Xi, all make earmarked monthly military remittances.

其细数如何，
What are the exact amounts?

乞为密访见示。”
Pray inquire confidentially and let me know.”

胡雪岩看完信，沉吟了好一会说：
Hu considered the letter for some time.

“我看，左大人对李合肥要动手了。”
“I believe His Excellency means to move against Li of Hefei.”

“喔，小爷叔看出苗头来了？”古应春问道：
“You see a sign of it, Little Uncle?

“怎么样动手法？”
How will he act?”

“这还言之过早。而且动手也要看机会，
“It is too early to say, and action will depend on opportunity.

不过左大人现在已经有这个意思了。”
But he already has the intention.”


原来李鸿章的准军有好些部队，驻扎在江苏，
Several units of Li’s Anhui Army were stationed in Jiangsu.

湘淮军都是子弟兵，
Like the Hunan Army, it consisted of men recruited through personal and local ties.

行命将，后招募，募兵成营，即以统率将官之名命名。
A commander was appointed first and then raised his men; the resulting camps took his name.

吴长庆所部名“庆字营”，有一营在江苏，
Wu Changqing’s forces were the Qing camps, one of which was stationed in Jiangsu.

“刘六麻子”刘铭传虽已挟其宦囊，在合肥原籍构筑“大潜山房”，饮酒赋诗，大过儒将的瘾，但“铭字营”的番号依旧，
Although “Sixth Pockmarked Liu,” Liu Mingchuan, had already carried home his official fortune and built the Daqian Mountain Retreat at Hefei, where he drank, wrote poetry, and thoroughly enjoyed playing the scholarly general, the Ming camps retained their designation.

不过由李鸿章拿他们一分为二，
Li had divided them.

一部分由记名提督刘盛休统带，驻山东张秋一带，防守运河要口，
One part, under Liu Shengxiu, a registered candidate for provincial military command, guarded important canal points around Zhangqiu in Shandong.

一部分交福建提督唐定奎率领，驻防江苏、靖江两县，
Another, under Tang Dingkui, the provincial military commander of Fujian, was stationed in two counties named in the text as Jiangsu and Jingjiang.

另有铭字先锋马队之营，驻扎江苏宿迁，主要的任务，亦是防运河沿岸一带有警，可以迅速赴援。
A mounted vanguard camp of the Ming forces stood at Suqian in Jiangsu, chiefly to rush to the aid of places along the canal at the first alarm.

李鸿章的淮军中。亦有原为湘军的将领，此人名叫郭松林，
The Anhui Army also included a former Hunan Army commander, Guo Songlin.

他的旧部名为“武毅军”，
His old forces were known as the Wuyi Army.

有十营为江防军，亦驻江阴、靖江境内，有五营为海防军，驻扎上海、宝山两县境内。
Ten camps formed river-defense units in Jiangyin and Jingjiang, while five served coastal defense in Shanghai and Baoshan.

这些部队，都由江苏发饷。
Jiangsu paid all these troops.

所谓“司关厘局”，司指藩局，关指海关，厘指厘金，局指捐局、税局以及淮盐督销局。
The expression “treasury, customs, likin and bureaus” meant the provincial treasury, customs offices, likin administration, contribution and tax bureaus, and the Huai Salt Sales Supervision Bureau.

两淮出盐，盐课收入为两江一大财源。
Salt from Lianghuai was an important source of revenue for Liangjiang.

但上江安徽、下江江苏两省的人吃不完两淮的盐，所以淮盐有指定的销售地区，称为“引局”，分布在鄂、湘、西、皖四个省份，
Anhui and Jiangsu could not consume the entire output, so designated sales areas, known as salt-certificate districts, extended into Hubei, Hunan, Jiangxi, and Anhui.

西非山西而是江西。
Here Xi meant Jiangxi, not Shanxi.

这四省都有淮盐督销局，收入亦归两江。
Each of the four provinces had a Huai Salt Sales Supervision Bureau, whose revenue also belonged to Liangjiang.


“也不回杭州查，也不叫采运局去办，
“There is no need to investigate in Hangzhou or set the Purchasing and Transport Bureau to work,” Hu said.

我有个极方便的法子叫老宓写信到各处问一问，
“I have a much easier way. Let Old Mi write to the various places and ask.
