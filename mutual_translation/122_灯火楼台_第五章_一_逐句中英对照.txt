第五章 “螺蛳太太”｜CHAPTER FIVE: MADAM LUOSI


胡雪岩这年过年的心境，不如往年，
Hu Xueyan did not enter the New Year in quite the spirits of former years.

自然是由于七姑奶奶中风，使他有一种难以自解的歉疚之故。
The Seventh Lady’s stroke had left him with a sense of guilt he could not shake off.

不过，在表面上是看不出来的，
Nothing of this showed on the surface, however.

胡家的年景，依旧花团锦簇，繁华热闹，其中最忙的要数“螺蛳太太”。
The Hu household celebrated with its customary splendor and bustle, and no one was busier than Madam Luosi.

这个称呼，由来已久，
The name was of long standing.

她本姓罗，行四，
Her surname was Luo, and she was the fourth in her family.

未嫁以前，是个极能干的小家碧玉，
Before her marriage she had been an exceptionally capable young woman of modest background.

认识她的人，不管老少，都叫她“罗四姐”，算是个尊称。
Everyone who knew her, old or young, called her Luo Sijie—Sister Four Luo—as a mark of respect.

这罗四组慧眼识英雄，在胡雪岩潦倒的时候，接济过他。
She had recognized Hu’s promise while he was still down on his luck and had helped him through his difficulties.

可惜胡雪岩已经娶了妻子，
Unfortunately, he already had a wife.

彼此虽都有爱慕之意，却无从结合。
Though they were fond of each other, they could not marry.

不久，太平军起事，他们纷纷逃去，
Before long the Taiping uprising sent them fleeing in different directions.

音信不通，一别九年，方始重逢。
They lost all news of each other and did not meet again for nine years.

胡雪岩记得很清楚，
Hu remembered their reunion clearly.

那年是同治六年，
It was the sixth year of Tongzhi.

他已经奉委主持西征采运局，长驻上海。
He had already been appointed to direct the Purchasing and Transport Bureau for the western campaign and spent most of his time in Shanghai.

清明之后不久，胡雪岩的旧侣张胖子去世，
Shortly after Qingming, his old associate Fat Zhang died.

在静安寺作佛事，他跟古应春夫妇去祭吊时，
Buddhist services were held at Jing’an Temple, and Hu went with Gu Yingchun and the Seventh Lady to pay his respects.

看见有个在烧香的淡妆少妇，
There he saw a young married woman, lightly made up, offering incense.

异常面善，却怎么样也想不起来是在哪里见过。
Her face was extraordinarily familiar, yet he could not remember where he had seen her.

那少妇烧完香，带着个十三四岁的小大姐走了。
When she had finished, she left with a maid of thirteen or fourteen.

胡雪岩不死心，悄悄跟在后面，一路走，一路想，到底是什么人？
Unwilling to let the matter rest, Hu quietly followed, wondering all the way who she could be.


静安寺是上海第一古刹，建于吴大帝赤乌十年，
Jing’an was Shanghai’s foremost ancient Buddhist temple, founded in the tenth year of Chiwu under the Wu emperor.

地方很大，
Its grounds were extensive.

原有“静安八景”之称，但那时已只剩下“涌泉”一景，涌泉又称沸井，
Once celebrated for its Eight Views, it now retained only one, the Gushing Spring, also called the Boiling Well.

井中之水终年翻翻滚滚，有如水沸，上海人说它是个海眼。
The water churned throughout the year as though boiling, and Shanghai people called it an opening into the sea.

初礼静安寺的人，少不得都要去望一望。
A first-time visitor could hardly fail to go and look.

那少妇亦不例外，
The woman was no exception.

胡雪岩亦步亦趋地跟了过去，装作来看沸井的旅客，驻足不行，以观动静。
Hu followed closely, then stopped nearby, pretending to be a traveler examining the well while he watched what she did.

“阿华，当心，当心，
“A’hua, be careful!

跌到井里，把你小命送掉！”
If you fall in, you’ll lose your life!”

原来那小大姐探头下望沸井，走得很近，身子又往前倾，
The maid had gone too close to the edge and was leaning forward to peer down.

这个动作很危险，所以那少妇大声警告，
The danger made her mistress call out loudly.

一口杭州话帮胡雪岩敲开了记忆之门，
Her Hangzhou accent unlocked Hu’s memory.

又惊又喜地在想：这不是罗四姐？
Could this be Luo Sijie? He was astonished and delighted.

本想冒叫一声，证实了再上前招呼。
At first he thought of calling her name to make sure before approaching.

但游客甚多，
But there were many visitors about.

而上海的风气虽然比较开通，也还不到西洋人男女可以在稠人广众间公然招呼的程度，
Shanghai might be comparatively liberal, yet it had not reached the Western custom of allowing men and women to greet each other openly in a crowd.

因而考虑了一下，回头关照书僮桂生，赶快将七姑奶奶所带来的小大姐叫一个来，越快越好。
After a moment’s thought, he turned to his page Guisheng and told him to fetch one of the Seventh Lady’s maids as quickly as possible.

桂生飞奔而去，
Guisheng ran off.

他亦不必先告诉七姑奶奶，在七姑奶奶带来的两个小大姐中，找到跟他比较好的彩凤，说一声：
Without first consulting the Seventh Lady, he found Caifeng, the more friendly toward him of the two maids she had brought.

“跟我来，
“Come with me.

有要紧事，
Something important.

快，快！”
Quick!”

彩凤只当他闯了什么祸，急急忙忙跟在他身后，
Thinking he had got himself into trouble, she hurried after him.

桂生等看到胡雪岩的影子，方始停住脚。
Only when Hu came into sight did Guisheng stop.


“是我们老爷要叫你。”
“It’s our master who wants you.”

“彩凤，”胡雪岩悄悄指点：“你上去问她，是不是杭州的罗四姐？
Hu pointed the woman out discreetly. “Caifeng, go and ask whether she is Sister Luo from Hangzhou.

如果她说是，你就说我们奶奶是胡老爷的亲戚，请她跟你们奶奶去见一见。”
If she says yes, tell her that your mistress is a relative of Mr Hu’s and would like her to come over and meet her.”

彩凤很伶俐，
Caifeng was quick-witted.

想了一下问：“如果她不肯去呢？”
She considered this and asked, “What if she refuses?”

“你就回过头来看我，
“Just turn and look at me.

她就一定肯去了。”
Then she will certainly agree.”

果然，一如胡雪岩的估计，
Events followed his prediction.

只见彩凤上前搭话时，仿佛有难以沟通的情状，
When Caifeng approached the woman, there seemed at first to be some difficulty in making herself understood.

然后是彩凤先回头来看胡雪岩，
Then she looked back at Hu.

接着是那少妇随着她的视线所示来搜索，遥遥望去，显得相当震动似地。
The woman followed her gaze, searching until she found him in the distance, and appeared deeply shaken.

胡雪岩知道成功了，
Hu knew that his plan had worked.

赶紧转身直奔作为堂客休憩之地的一座禅房，
He immediately turned and hurried to a monastic room set aside for the women guests.

找到七姑奶奶的另一个小大姐，关照请她的主母出来叙话。
Finding the Seventh Lady’s other maid, he asked her to bring her mistress out for a word.

“七姐，我同你谈过的罗四姐，你还记得记不得？”
“Seventh Sister, do you remember the Sister Luo I told you about?”

七姑奶奶想了一下，点点头说：
After a moment she nodded.

“记得。”
“I do.”

“她今天在这里，
“She is here today.

我叫彩凤‘假传圣旨’，说你同我是亲戚，请她来见面。
I have had Caifeng issue a false imperial decree in your name: she has told her we are relatives and you want to meet her.

马上就要来了。
She’ll be here any moment.

七姐，你请她到你那里去，仔仔细细问问她，
Invite her home, Seventh Sister, and ask her carefully about her circumstances.

她好象居孀在那里。”
She seems to be living as a widow.”

“好，好！”七姑奶奶连连答应，又问，
“Of course, of course!

“小爷叔，你呢？”
And what about you, Little Uncle?”

“我到钱庄里、
“I must go to the bank.

有桩要紧事情料理好了，马上来。”
As soon as I have dealt with something important, I’ll come straight over.”


等胡雪岩走了好一会，才看到彩凤领着莲步姗姗一个俏括括的素服少妇，
Hu had been gone some time before Caifeng appeared with an attractive young woman in plain mourning clothes.

扶着小大姐的肩头，冉冉而来。
Resting a hand on her maid’s shoulder, she advanced slowly with delicate steps.

七姑奶奶性子急，撇开一双大脚，迎了上去。
The impatient Seventh Lady strode forward on her unbound feet to meet her.

“是不是罗四姐？”
“Are you Sister Luo?”

“不敢当。
“You are too kind.

我姓罗，
My name is Luo.

尊姓？”
May I ask yours?”

“我夫家姓古，娘家姓尤，行七，
“My husband’s name is Gu, my maiden name You, and I am the seventh in my family.

我们小爷叔叫我‘七姐’。
Our Little Uncle calls me Seventh Sister.

罗四姐你也这样叫我好了。”
Please call me that too.”

七姑奶奶是直性子，一古脑儿都说了出来。
Frank as ever, she poured everything out at once.

在罗四姐听，却有些牛头不对马嘴，
To Luo, it did not quite make sense.

既是“小爷叔”，何以又叫她“七姐”？
Why should someone she called Little Uncle call her Seventh Sister?

但这个疑团，还在其次，
That puzzle could wait.

眼前有句最要紧的话先要问清楚，才谈得到其他。
One more pressing question had to be settled first.

“请问，古太太你的‘小爷叔’是哪个？”
“May I ask, Mrs Gu, who is this Little Uncle of yours?”

“还有哪个？不就是你老早认识的胡雪岩，鼎鼎大名阜康钱庄的老板。”
“Who else but your old acquaintance Hu Xueyan, owner of the famous Fukang Bank?”

罗四姐又惊又喜。
Luo was both surprised and delighted.

她也曾听说过，阜康钱庄的老板，就是从前在张胖子那里做伙计的胡雪岩，
She had heard that Fukang’s proprietor was the Hu Xueyan who had once worked for Fat Zhang.

一直想打听，苦无机会。
She had long wanted to inquire but had found no opportunity.

不想真的有这回事。
So it really was true.

“罗四姐，”七姑奶奶说，“你听我叫他小爷叔，就晓得我们是自己人。
“You can tell from my calling him Little Uncle that we are close, Sister Luo.

你一定要请到我那里去坐一歇。
You must come and sit with me at home.

你当年待我们小爷叔的好处，他也跟我说过。
He has told me how kind you were to him in the old days.

等下他也要来的。
He will be coming over shortly.”

“罗四姐心想：胡雪岩倒真是有良心的！
Hu had not forgotten her kindness after all!

就这一转念间，心里顿时七上八下在翻动了。
The thought set Luo’s heart in a turmoil.

“罗四姐，”七姑奶奶催问着：“你肯不肯赏面子。”
“Will you do me the honor, Sister Luo?” the Seventh Lady urged.

“唷，古太太，你的话太客气了。
“Oh, Mrs Gu, you are far too courteous.

真正不敢当。”
I really don’t deserve it.”


于是七姑奶奶向丧家致意告辞，将罗四姐主婢二人带回家。
After taking leave of the bereaved family, the Seventh Lady brought Luo and her maid home.

一看她家的气派，七姑奶奶又热心伉爽，罗四姐决心要结交，
The style of the household, together with her hostess’s warmth and generosity, convinced Luo that she wanted to cultivate this friendship.

因而改了称呼，同时深谈身世。
She adopted the more intimate form of address and began to speak freely of her life.

原来罗四姐当年随父母逃避战乱，转徒千里，流离途中，父母双亡，
She had fled the fighting with her parents, traveling a thousand li in their wanderings, and both had died on the road.

不然一身，不是了局，只有择人而事，
Remaining entirely alone was no solution; she had to choose someone to marry.

结伴同行，一共有三家，
They had been traveling in a party of three families.

其中两家都有个尚未婚娶的二十来岁的儿子，当然亦都时时在找机会向她献殷勤。
Two of the other families had unmarried sons in their twenties, both continually looking for opportunities to show her attentions.

这两家一富一穷，
One family was rich and the other poor.

而罗四姐挑了穷的那家，姓程，是独子。
Luo chose the poor family’s son, an only child named Cheng.

“七姐，我是因为他虽穷，肯上进，
“Seventh Sister, I chose him because, poor as he was, he wanted to make something of himself.

只要他肯上进，我就有把握帮他出头。
So long as he had that ambition, I was confident I could help him rise.

再说，上头只有一个老娘，
Besides, he had only an old mother.

不比另外一家，父母双全，还有三个兄弟，两个妹妹，
The other man had both parents, three brothers, and two sisters.

嫁过去做媳妇，一定象顶石臼做戏，吃力不讨好。”
Marrying into that household would have been like performing a play with a stone mortar on my head—all effort and no thanks.”

“罗四姐，换了我，也会象你一样，宁愿挑这一位。”
“I would have chosen as you did, Sister Luo.”

七姑奶奶早就发现她鬓边戴一朵白头绳结的菊花，却故意问说：“我们程姐夫呢？
The Seventh Lady had already noticed the chrysanthemum made from white hair cord at her temple, but deliberately asked, “And where is Brother-in-Law Cheng?

几时请过来见一见。”
When will you bring him to meet us?”

“不在了。”罗四姐凄然说道：
“He is gone,” Luo answered sadly.

“是前年这个时候去世的。”
“He died at this time the year before last.”

“可怜，
“Poor thing.

可怜！”
Poor thing.”

七姑奶奶紧握着她的手，但有无言的慰藉。
The Seventh Lady clasped her hand, offering the comfort for which there were no words.


“说起来也怪我不好。”罗叫姐说：
“In a way, it was my fault,” Luo said.

“他学的是刻字匠手艺。
“He had learned the woodblock cutter’s trade.

有一回他跟我谈起，说是长毛打到杭州的前两年，乡试考举人，他跟他师父一起到考场里去刻题目纸，
Once he told me that, two years before the Longhairs reached Hangzhou, he and his master went into the examination compound to cut the blocks for the provincial examination papers.

熬夜熬到天亮，心里在想：我也读过书，
He worked through the night until dawn, thinking: ‘I have studied too.

一样是熬夜，为啥不是去考举人，坐在这里当个低三下四的刻字匠，
If I must stay up all night, why shouldn’t I be sitting the examination for a juren degree, instead of working here as a lowly block cutter?

人家举子写借了字，顶多贴出‘蓝榜’，
If a candidate writes a wrong character, at worst there is a notice on blue paper.

我刻错一个字要打手心，
If I cut one wrong character, they beat my palms.

‘吃生活’？我就说：“你果然有心，把招牌收起来，好好儿读书。
I get a thrashing!’ So I told him, ‘If you really mean it, take down your shop sign and devote yourself to your books.

开门七件事都是我管，
Leave all seven daily household necessities to me.

用不着你费心。
You needn’t worry about them.’

‘他真的就听我的话，三更打灯五更鸡，闷倒头读书……”
He took me at my word and studied with his head down, burning his lamp in the third watch and rising with the cock in the fifth…”

“罗四姐”，七姑奶奶打断她的话问：“你这开门七件事，怎么管法？”
“Sister Luo,” the Seventh Lady interrupted, “how did you provide those household necessities?”

“我绣花，
“I embroidered.

不光是绣花，还替绣庄去收件，
And not only that: I collected work for embroidery shops.

到后来做‘小包’，一批绣货包下来，再分给人家去做，
Later I became a small contractor, taking on a whole batch and distributing it among other women.

日子过得很舒服。
We lived very comfortably.

七姐，上海滩繁华地方，
Shanghai is a prosperous place, Seventh Sister.

遍地银子，只要你肯花工夫去捡。
There is silver lying everywhere, if only you are willing to take the trouble to pick it up.

不瞒你说，我就不相信，世界上有饿死的人。
To tell you the truth, I don’t believe anyone need starve.

饿死的人是有，那是因为有钱买不到米，不是没有铜钱买米。
People do starve, of course, but because there is no rice to be bought, even with money—not because they have no cash to buy it.

这不一样的。
That is a different matter.

七姐，你说是不是？
Wouldn’t you say so?”

““怎么不是？”
“Certainly!”

七姑奶奶笑道：
The Seventh Lady smiled.

“你的说法，倒跟小爷叔很象。”她紧接着又问：
“You sound very much like Little Uncle.

“后来呢？”
What happened then?”


“后来杭州光复了。他同我说，考秀才要到杭州去考，将来举人也是杭州考，
“After Hangzhou was recovered, he told me he would have to go back there to take the examination for a xiucai degree, and later the provincial examination as well.

家一搬到杭州，你的这点基础，就要抛掉了。
If we moved, all I had built up here would be lost.

不如捐个监生，下回直接进京去考举人，
It would be better to purchase Imperial Academy student status and go straight to Beijing next time to sit for the juren degree.

头一年秋天考中了，第二年春天再考进士。
If he passed in the autumn, he could take the jinshi examination the following spring.

如果在浙江考中了举人，考进土还是要进京。
Even if he won his juren in Zhejiang, he would still have to go to Beijing for the jinshi.

一番手续两番做，反而不划算。
Doing in two stages what could be done in one would be less economical.

我想想不错，凑了二百两银子，替他捐了个监生，
I thought he was right and scraped together two hundred taels to purchase the qualification for him.

他就更加用功了。
Then he studied harder than ever.

唉！”罗四姐叹口气，说不下去了。
Ah…” She sighed and could not continue.

“用功用出毛病来了？”练达人情的七姑奶奶问说。
“Did the studying damage his health?” asked the Seventh Lady, who understood the ways of the world.

“先是吐血。”罗四奶用低幽但很平静的声音说。
“First he began coughing blood,” Luo said, quietly but calmly.

“他还瞒着我，
“He hid it from me.

吐血吐到手帕里，手帕自己去洗。
He coughed into his handkerchief and washed it himself.

脸色越来越白，到了下半天，颧骨上倒象搽了胭脂，
His face grew paler and paler, yet in the afternoons his cheekbones looked as though he had put rouge on them.

我懵懵懂懂，还不当它一回事。
I was foolish and thought nothing of it.

有一天他有应酬回来，
One day he came home from an engagement.

我替他脱袍子，随手在口袋里一摸，
I helped him take off his robe and happened to put my hand into a pocket.

摸出一条上有血迹的手帕，
There was a bloodstained handkerchief.

才晓得他是痨病。”
Only then did I realize he had consumption.”

“痨病？”
“Consumption?”

七姑奶奶神色紧张，
The Seventh Lady looked alarmed.

“后来呢，照样还是赶考去了？”
“Did he still go to the examinations?”

“没有。
“No.

他这样子怎么能赶考？”
How could he, in that condition?”

“以后呢？”
“What happened afterward?”

以后自然是养病。
Afterward, naturally, he tried to recover.

痨病俗称“馋痨病”，想吃这个，想吃那个，
People called consumption the craving sickness: the sufferer longed for one thing after another.

罗四姐总依着他的性子去办，办来了，却又浅尝即止，
Luo always procured whatever he wanted, but once it arrived, he took only a taste.

剩下来的不仅是食物，还有他的歉疚。
What remained was not merely uneaten food, but his guilt.

“我听人说，痨病只要胃口好，还不要紧，
“I had heard that with a good appetite, consumption was not too serious.

象他那样子，馋是馋得要命，胃口一点都没有。
But he had terrible cravings and no appetite at all.

人一天比一天瘦，
He grew thinner every day.

不过三个月的工夫。
In only three months…

唉！”罗四姐又是一声长叹。
Ah!” She sighed again.


七姑奶奶不必再谈她的丈夫，
There was no need to ask more about her husband.

觉得要关心的是罗四姐，
The Seventh Lady felt it was Luo herself who now needed concern.

“你现在住在哪里？”她问。
“Where are you living?”

“南市。天主教堂后面。”
“In the southern city, behind the Catholic church.”

“日子过得很艰难吧？”
“Life must be very difficult?”

“也还好。”罗四姐淡淡地答说。
“I manage,” Luo answered without emphasis.

“有没有伢儿？”
“Any children?”

“没有。”
“No.”

罗四姐口中干脆，内心不免抱歉。
Her answer was brisk, though inwardly she could not help feeling apologetic.

“既无儿女，年纪也离‘老’字还早……”
“With no children, and still a long way from being old…”

七姑奶奶突然咽住。
The Seventh Lady stopped short.

毕竟还是第一次见面，哪里能谈得那么深。
This was their first meeting, after all; she could hardly go so far.

“Very well.

Sir, it must be nine years since we last met?”

Hu calculated silently.

“Nine years!

杭州光复之后，左大人叫我办善后，

The Seventh Lady thought the account would take some time.

Having heard it already, she need not sit there idly.

“Sister Luo, Little Uncle, you are both staying for dinner.

“Sister Luo,” she heard Hu say, “you helped me so much in the old days.

If there is anything I can do for you, please say so.”

“多谢你。
“Thank you.

你也还混得落，
I can still get along.

到我混不落去的时候，再请你大先生帮忙。”
When I can no longer manage, I shall ask for your help, Sir.”

“But living alone like this is no lasting solution.”

听得这话，七姑奶奶心中一动，
The Seventh Lady’s interest stirred.

悄悄起身，遥遥相望，
She rose quietly and looked in from a distance.

只见胡雪岩与罗四姐四目凝视，
Hu and Luo were gazing into each other’s eyes.

心里在想：他们那一段旧情，又挑起来了。
Their old affection had been rekindled, she thought.

她猜得不错。
She was right.

胡雪岩觉得九年不见，罗四姐变了，
Hu felt that Luo had changed during their nine years apart.

从前是一根长辫子甩来甩去，走路腰扭得很厉害，左顾右盼，见了陌生人不会脸红的小家碧玉，
In the old days she had been a young woman of modest means whose long braid swung to and fro, whose waist swayed briskly as she walked, and whose eyes were always glancing about. Strangers never made her blush.

瓜子形的清水脸上，那一双黑白分明的眼睛，不似从前那么灵活，
The sharply contrasted black and white of her eyes, set in an unpainted oval face, no longer moved about as restlessly as before.

Yet an occasional glance at him seemed charged with countless things she wished to tell.

胡雪岩选色，喜欢年轻孀妇，所以这朵带孝白菊花，最逗人遐思。
Hu had a particular taste for young widows, and this white flower of mourning stirred his imagination more than anything.

“这样好不好，”胡雪岩说：
“What do you think of this?” he asked.

“我帮你在杭州开一家绣庄。”
“Let me help you open an embroidery shop in Hangzhou.”

“不！
“No.

我不想回杭州。”
I don’t want to return to Hangzhou.”

“为啥呢？”
“Why?”

“I am used to living in Shanghai.”

“Let me think it over.”

The Seventh Lady would have liked to listen longer, but Gu had returned.

She had to intercept him, explain briefly who their new guest was, and then bring him into the sitting room.

“Ah, Brother-in-Law Gu.” Luo gathered her jacket neatly and made her greeting with easy grace.

是这样亲近的称呼，使得古应春很快地消失了陌生感，象跟熟人那洋谈了起来。
The intimate form of address soon dispelled Gu’s feeling of unfamiliarity, and he talked to her as he would to an old acquaintance.

Before long the restaurant dishes arrived and they went in to dine.

大家都尊罗四姐上坐，她说什么也不肯，
Everyone urged Luo to take the place of honor, but she would not hear of it.

“And she has an excellent capacity.”

“Then grape wine will be too light.

“罗四姐，你喜欢哪种酒，
What do you prefer, Sister Luo?

烫花雕来好不好？”
Shall we warm some Huadiao?”

“谢谢。
“Thank you.

我现在酒不吃了。”
I no longer drink.”

“为啥要戒酒？”七姑奶奶说：
“Why give it up?

“你一个人，正要吃酒，
Living by yourself, you need a little wine.

一醉解千愁。”
One good bout of drinking dissolves a thousand sorrows.”

“Listen to yourself!” Gu reproached her.

“You sound drunk before you have touched a drop.

人家罗四姐日子过得好好地，
Sister Luo is managing perfectly well.

何必借酒浇愁？”
Why should she drown her sorrows?”

“好！算我说错了。”
“Very well, I spoke out of turn.”

七姑奶奶让步，复又劝客人：
The Seventh Lady yielded, then urged her guest again.

“你为我开戒，
“Break your rule for me.

我陪你吃两杯。”
I’ll have a couple of cups with you.”

“不敢当，不敢当。
“You are too kind.

七姐一定要我吃，我就吃。”

“守和好。
“Then that is settled.

The Seventh Lady fetched a bottle of mint liqueur from a cabinet.

“I’ll have some too,” Hu said, joining in the spirit of the occasion.

“I have been urging Sister Luo to open an embroidery shop.

“Sir, I have considered it,” Luo interposed.

“Thank you for your kindness, but it is beyond my strength.

“There is not much satisfaction in having everything handed to me.”

The conversation was beginning to go awry.

Gu thought it better to leave the matter for the time being and changed the subject.

As host, he naturally chose something familiar or interesting to the guest, and so they came to Gu embroidery.

中国的刺绣分三派，湖南湘绣、苏州苏绣以外，上海独称“顾绣”，
Chinese embroidery fell into three schools: Hunan’s Xiang embroidery, Suzhou’s Su embroidery, and the Shanghai style uniquely called Gu embroidery.

先是明朝道州知府顾名儒所建，本名“万竹山居“。
Gu Mingru, a Ming prefect of Daozhou, had originally built it under the name Retreat of Ten Thousand Bamboos.

顾名儒的胞弟叫顾名世，嘉靖三十八年的进士，官拜尚宝丞，
His younger brother Gu Mingshi became a jinshi in the thirty-eighth year of Jiajing and rose to Assistant Director of the Imperial Seals Office.

告老还乡，宦囊甚丰，看万竹山居东面的空地尚多，于是拓宽来开辟一座池塘，

哪知此地本来就是池，有掘出来的一块石碑为证。
The site proved to have held a pond before: an unearthed stone inscription supplied the evidence.

碑上刻的是”露香池“三字，而且是赵子昂的手笔。
It bore the words “Dew-Fragrance Pond,” in Zhao Zi’ang’s own calligraphy.

Gu Mingshi therefore renamed the retreat Luxiang Garden, the Garden of Dew Fragrance, and naturally restored the pond’s old name.

顾名世的姬妾很多，其中有一个姓缨，
Among his many concubines was one surnamed Ying.

她在京城的时候，学会了刺绣，而且是宫中传出来的诀窍，
While in Beijing she had learned embroidery, including techniques passed down from the palace.

缨姨娘在这方面有天才，更加改良，益见精妙。
Gifted in the craft, she improved on them until her work became still more exquisite.

五色丝线孽，细针密缕，颜色由浅入深，浑然一体，配色之美，更不在话下。
With fine needles and close stitches, her silks of five colors blended seamlessly from light to dark; the beauty of her color combinations went without saying.

最见特色的是，顾绣以针代笔，以丝钱作丹青，以名迹作蓝本，
Above all, Gu embroidery used the needle for a brush and silk thread for pigment, taking famous paintings as models.

山水、人物、花鸟，无不气韵生动，工细无匹，
Landscapes, figures, flowers, and birds all possessed a living spirit and unrivaled delicacy.

当时称为“画绣”。
It was then known as “painted embroidery.”

缨姨娘曾经仿绣赵子昂的《八骏图》，
Mistress Ying once embroidered a version of Zhao Zi’ang’s Eight Steeds.

董其昌认为即使是赵子昂本人用笔，亦未见得能胜过她。
Dong Qichang declared that even Zhao himself, working with a brush, might not surpass her.

又绣过一幅《停针图》，真是穷态极研，而且无法分辨是画，是绣。
She also made a Pausing the Needle, extraordinarily expressive and beautiful, in which painting and embroidery were indistinguishable.

后来由扬州的一位监商，拿一个汉玉连环，及南唐名家周昉作画的一幅美人图交换了去。
A Yangzhou salt merchant later obtained it in exchange for a set of linked jade rings from the Han dynasty and a painting of a beauty by the celebrated Southern Tang artist Zhou Fang.

由于缨姨娘的教导，露香园的女眷，下至丫头，都会刺绣，而且极精，
Under Mistress Ying’s instruction, all the women of Luxiang Garden, down to the maids, became highly accomplished embroiderers.

“画绣”之名大著，顾名世本人的名字，反而不为人所知，以至于顾名世有一次酒后大发牢骚，说他“寄名于汝辈十指之间”。
Their fame overshadowed Gu Mingshi’s own name, until one day, after drinking, he complained that his reputation hung between their ten fingers.


不过称为“顾绣”是入清以后的事。
The name “Gu embroidery,” however, came into use only after the beginning of the Qing.

顾名世有个孙女儿，嫁夫姓张，
Gu Mingshi had a granddaughter who married a man named Zhang.

二十四岁居孀，有个一岁的儿子。抚孤守节，全靠纤纤十指，
Widowed at twenty-four with a year-old son, she supported the orphan and maintained her widowhood entirely through the work of her slender fingers.

绣件不输于缨姨娘，
Her embroidery equaled Mistress Ying’s.

但除绣画以外还绣普通的花样，生意很好，
Besides pictures, she embroidered ordinary designs, and business flourished.

“顾绣”便取“画绣”之名而代之，传遍南北。
“Gu embroidery” replaced “painted embroidery” as the name of the craft and became known throughout the country.

同时“顾绣”也成了上海的一佯名产，家学户习，甚至男子也有学刺绣的。
It was now one of Shanghai’s celebrated products, practiced in household after household; even men learned to embroider.

罗四姐讲得头头是道，胡雪岩与七姑奶奶也听得津津有味。
Luo told the story with such command that Hu and the Seventh Lady listened with keen enjoyment.

不过古应春却有些心不在焉，
Gu was somewhat distracted, however.

他关心的是胡雪岩这天在长三堂子中有六七处应酬，
Hu had six or seven engagements at changsan houses that evening.

每处坐半点钟，连路上的工夫，至少亦要四个钟头，
At half an hour in each, plus travel, they would take at least four hours.

所以等罗四姐谈得告一段落，便提醒他说：“应该去了。”
As soon as Luo reached a pause, he reminded Hu, “We ought to go.”

一听这话，胡雪岩便皱起了眉，
Hu frowned.

“可以不去的，有哪些地方？”他问。
“Which can we miss?”

“最好都去。
“Best to go to all of them.

万不得已，那么，有两处非去不可。”
If you absolutely cannot, there are two you must attend.”

“好吧！
“Very well.

就去这两处。”
We’ll go to those two.

胡雪岩问道：“罗四姐呢？
What about Sister Luo?

应该有人送。”
Someone ought to see her home.”

“不要了。”七姑奶奶说：
“There is no need,” the Seventh Lady said.

“城里这么远，又是晚上。”
“It is such a long way into the city, and at night too.”

七姑奶奶是不由分说要留客过夜了。
Clearly, she meant to keep her guest overnight and would accept no argument.

罗四姐也想留下来，不过家里只有一个老苍头看门，
Luo wanted to stay, but only an old manservant was at home to watch the house.

她一夜不回去，害老苍头着急，亦觉于心不忍。
If she did not return, he would worry, and she could not bear to cause him that anxiety.


“这倒容易。”古应春说：
“That is easily settled,” Gu said.

“请罗四姐把府上的地址告诉我，我派人去通知。”
“Give me your address, Sister Luo, and I’ll send someone to let him know.”

于是胡、古二人先行离席，
The two men left the table first.

七姑奶奶陪着罗四姐吃完饭，领她到专为留堂客的客房，检点了补褥用具，请罗四姐卸了妆，再舒舒服服喝茶闲谈。
When the women had finished, the Seventh Lady took Luo to a room reserved for female guests, checked the bedding and other necessities, and invited her to remove her makeup and settle comfortably over tea and conversation.

一谈谈到午夜，
They talked until midnight.

古家照例每天必有消夜，正在吃粥时，古应春回来了，同行的还有胡雪岩。
A late supper was a daily custom in the Gu household, and while they were eating congee, Gu returned—with Hu beside him.

“小爷叔没有回去？”七姑奶奶信口说了一句。
“You didn’t go home, Little Uncle?” the Seventh Lady remarked casually.

“我想来吃粥。”胡雪岩也信口回答。
“I felt like some congee,” Hu answered just as casually.

其实，大家都明白，他是特为来看罗四姐。
Everyone understood that he had come expressly to see Luo.

卸了妆的她，梳一条松松的大辫子，穿的是散脚裤、小夹袄，
