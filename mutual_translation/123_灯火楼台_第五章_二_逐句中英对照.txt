胡雪岩
《灯火楼台》第五章（二）
Towers of Light — Chapter Five, Part Two
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


“你们去是去，”古应春半真半假地警告：“当心《申报》登你们的新闻。”
“You may go,” Gu warned, half in jest, “but take care that your adventures do not appear in the Shen Bao.”

“喔，”胡雪岩突然提高了声音说：
“Ah!” Hu suddenly raised his voice.

“应春提到《申报》，我倒想起一件事来了。
“That mention of the Shen Bao reminds me of something.

从去年冬天天津到上海的电报通了以后，我看《申报》上有些新闻是打电报回来的。
Since the Tianjin–Shanghai telegraph opened last winter, I have noticed that some of its news arrives by wire.

盛杏荪当电报局总办，消息格外灵通，
With Sheng Xingsun directing the telegraph administration, he is exceptionally well informed.

有些生意上头，我们消息比人家晚，哪怕只不过晚一步，亏就吃得很大了。
In certain kinds of business, if our news comes later than his—even one step later—we can suffer a serious loss.

所以，我有个念头，应春，你看能不能托《申报》的访员帮忙？”
I have been wondering, Yingchun, whether we could obtain help from the newspaper's reporters.”

“是报行情过来？”
“To telegraph market quotations?”

“是啊。”
“Yes.”

“那，我们自己派人在天津，每天用密码发过来好了。”
“Then why not station someone of our own in Tianjin and have him send a daily report in cipher?”

“那没有多少用处。”胡雪岩说：
“That would be of limited use.

“有的行情，只有访员才打听得到。
There are things only reporters can discover.

而且，也不光是市面上的行情，
Besides, I do not mean merely conditions in the market.

还有朝廷里的行情。
I mean conditions at court too.

象去年冬天，李大先生的参案……“
Take the impeachment of the elder Mr Li last winter…”


“李大先生，”是指李瀚章。
The elder Mr Li was Li Hanzhang.

七姑奶奶的性情，外粗内细，
For all her outward bluntness, the Seventh Lady was inwardly perceptive.

一听谈到这些当朝大老的宦海风波，深知有许多有关系的话，不宜为不相干的人听见，
Once the conversation turned to the political fortunes of senior statesmen, she knew that many consequential remarks ought not to be overheard by people unconnected with the matter.

传出去会惹是非，对胡雪岩及古应春都没有好处，
If repeated, they might cause trouble for both Hu and Gu.

所以悄悄拉了罗四姐，同时还做了个示意离席的眼色。
She quietly drew Luo Sijie aside, signalling with her eyes that they should leave the table.

“他们这一谈就谈不完了，
“Once they begin, they will talk for ever.

我们到旁边来谈我们的。”
Let us go over there and have our own conversation.”

罗四姐极其知趣，立刻迎合着七姑奶奶的意向说：
Luo immediately understood and cooperated.

“我也正有些话，不便当着他们谈。
“I have things to say that I would rather not discuss before them.

七姐，我心里头有点发慌。”
Seventh Sister, I feel rather unsettled.”

“为啥？”
“Why?”

罗四姐不即回答，将七姑奶奶拉到一边，在红丝绒的长安乐椅上并排坐了下来，
Instead of answering, Luo led her to a long red-velvet easy chair, where they sat side by side.

一只手执着七姑奶奶的手，一只手只是摸着因酒而现红晕的脸。
With one hand she held the Seventh Lady's; with the other she kept touching her wine-flushed cheeks.

“是不是身子不舒服？”七姑奶奶不安地问：
“Are you unwell?” the Seventh Lady asked anxiously.

“怎么好端端地，心里会发慌？”
“Why should you suddenly feel unsettled?”

“不是身子不舒服。”罗四姐仿佛很吃力他说：
“It is not that.” Luo seemed to find the words difficult.

“我做梦也没有想到，忽然会有象今天这样子一天，又遇见雪岩，又结识了七姐你，
“I never dreamed that a day like this would come: meeting Xueyan again, and getting to know you.

好比买‘把儿柴’的人家，说道有一天中了‘白鸽票’，
I feel like a family that buys its firewood a handful at a time and suddenly wins a prize in the White Pigeon lottery.

不晓得怎么好了。”
I hardly know what to do.”

七姑奶奶虽是松江人，但由于胡雪岩的关系，也憧杭州话，
Though a native of Songjiang, the Seventh Lady understood Hangzhou expressions through her acquaintance with Hu.

罗四姐的意思是，升斗小民突然中了奖券，也就是拿穷儿暴富的譬喻，来形容她自己的心境。
Luo meant that an ordinary poor person had suddenly won a fortune.

七姑奶奶觉得她的话很中听，
The comparison pleased her.

原来就觉得她很好，这下便更对劲了。
She had liked Luo already and now felt still closer to her.

不过要找一句适当的话来回答倒很难，所以她只是笑嘻嘻地说：“怎么会呢？
Finding a suitable reply was difficult, however, so she merely smiled and said, “Surely not!

怎么会呢？”
Surely not!”


“怎么不会？
“Why not?

我一个寡妇，哪里有过这种又说又笑又吃酒的日子。
When has a widow like me ever had a day of talking, laughing, and drinking like this?

他要帮我开绣庄，你要请我逛堂子，
He offers to help me open an embroidery shop, and you offer to take me to a courtesan house.

不要说今生今生，前世都不曾想到过的。”
I could never have imagined such things in this life—or even a previous one.”

踌躇满志之意，溢于言表，七姑奶奶当然看得出来，
Her delight was unmistakable.

抓住她一只手，合拢在那那双只见肉、不见骨的温暖手掌中，悄悄问道：“罗四姐，他要帮你开绣庄，不过一句话的事，你的意思到底怎么样呢？”
Taking one of Luo's hands between her own warm, plump palms, the Seventh Lady quietly asked, “Helping you open a shop would take no more than a word from him. What do you really think of it, Sister Luo?”

罗四姐不答，低垂着眼，仿佛有难言之隐，无法开口似地。
Luo lowered her eyes without replying, as though troubled by something she could not express.

“你说一句嘛！
“Say something.

愿意就愿意，不愿意就不愿意，
If you want it, you want it; if you do not, you do not.

勉强不来的事。”
Nobody can force you.”

“我怎么会。不愿意呢？
“How could I not want it?

不过，七姐，”
But, Seventh Sister…”

罗四姐倏然抬眼，
She suddenly looked up.

“我算啥呢？”
“What would that make me?”

“女老板。”
“The proprietor.”

“出本钱是老板，
“The person who supplies the capital is the proprietor.

本钱又不是我的。”
The capital would not be mine.”

七姑奶奶始而诧异，
At first the Seventh Lady was puzzled.

做现成的老板，一大美事，还有什么好多想的？
Becoming the owner of a ready-made business was a wonderful opportunity; why hesitate?

继而憬然有悟，脱口说道：
Then understanding dawned.

“那么是老板娘？”
“The proprietor's wife, then?”

罗四姐又把头低了下去。
Luo lowered her head again.

幽幽地说：“我就怕人家是这样子想法。”
“That is exactly what I fear people would think.”

不说自己说人家，言外之意就很微妙了。
By speaking of other people rather than herself, she conveyed a subtle implication.

遇到这种时候，七姑奶奶就不会口没遮拦了，
At such moments the Seventh Lady was far from indiscreet.

有分寸的话，她拿把握住分寸，才肯出口。
She would not venture a delicate remark until she had judged its precise effect.


“罗四姐，”她终于开口探问了，“你年纪还轻，
“You are still young, Sister Luo,” she finally said, testing the ground.

又没有儿女，守下去没有意思嘛。”
“And with no children, what is the point of remaining a widow?”

在吃消夜以前，罗四姐原曾谈过身世。当时含含糊糊表示过，没有儿女，
Before supper, Luo had spoken of her past, vaguely implying that she had no children.

此时听七姑奶奶这样说，她觉得应该及时更正。才显得诚实。
Hearing this repeated, she felt she should correct it at once to demonstrate her honesty.

“有个女儿。”她说，
“I have a daughter,” she said.

“在外婆家。”
“She lives with her maternal grandmother.”

“外婆在哪里？”
“Where is her grandmother?”

“杭州。”
“Hangzhou.”

“女儿不比儿子，
“A daughter is different from a son.

总是人家的。
In the end, she belongs to another family.

将来靠女婿，他们小夫妇感情好还好，不然，这碗现成饭也很难吃。
Depending on a son-in-law may be tolerable if the young couple get along well, but otherwise that ready-provided meal can be hard to swallow.

尤其是上有婆婆，亲家太太的脸嘴，实在难看。”
Especially if his mother is still living: the looks a daughter's mother receives from her fellow mother-in-law can be very unpleasant.”

“我是决不会靠女婿的。”罗四姐答说，
“I shall never depend on a son-in-law,” Luo replied.

声音很平淡，但字字清楚，显得很有把握。
Her voice was calm, each word clear and confident.

“那么你靠哪个呢？”
“Whom will you depend on, then?”

“靠自己。”
“Myself.”


“靠自己就更要有一样靠得住的东西了。”
“Then you need something dependable all the more.”

意在言外，是劝她接受胡雪岩的资助，
The Seventh Lady meant that Luo should accept Hu's financial assistance.

但罗四姐就在这一顿消夜前后，浮动在心头的各种杂念，渐渐凝结成一个宗旨：要接受胡雪岩的好处，就不止于一家绣庄，
But during supper, the various thoughts drifting through Luo's mind had gradually crystallized into a resolution: if she accepted anything from Hu, it must be more than an embroidery shop.

否则宁可不受。
Otherwise, she would accept nothing.

因而明知其意，却装作不解。
Understanding perfectly, she pretended not to.

七姑奶奶当然不相信她不懂这话，沉默不答，必是别有盘算，
The Seventh Lady did not believe she had missed the meaning; silence must conceal some further calculation.

便追问着说：“你说我的话是不是？
“Am I not right?

靠自己是有志气的事，不过总也要有一样东西抓在手里。
It is admirable to rely on yourself, but you must have something secure in your hands.

绣花这样本事，全靠年纪轻、眼睛亮、手底下准，
Embroidery depends on youth, clear eyesight, and accurate fingers.

没有几年，你就靠不住了。”
In a few years, you will no longer be able to rely on them.”

靠得住的便是绣庄。
The dependable alternative was plainly a shop.

罗四姐不会再装不懂了，
Luo could not continue pretending incomprehension.

想一想说：“要说开绣庄，我再辛苦两三年，邀一两个姐妹淘合伙，也开得起来。”
After a pause, she said, “As for opening an embroidery shop, if I work hard for another two or three years and bring in one or two women friends as partners, I can manage it myself.”

莫非是嫌胡雪岩的忙帮得不够？还是性情耿介，不愿受人的好处？
Did she consider Hu's offer insufficient, or was she too proud and upright to accept favours?

七姑奶奶一时还看不出来，便也就保持沉默了。
The Seventh Lady could not yet tell and fell silent.

“七姐，”罗四姐忽然问道：“胡家老太太还在？”
Luo suddenly asked whether Hu's mother was still alive.

“健旺得很呢。”
“Very much alive and vigorous.

七姑奶奶问：“你见过？”
Have you met her?”

“见过。”
“Yes.”

“那么，胡太太呢？
“And Mrs Hu?

也见过？”
Have you met her too?”

“也见过。”
“Yes.”

罗四姐忽然幽幽地叹了口气。
Luo gave a soft sigh.

这一下，七姑奶奶恍然大悟。
Now the Seventh Lady understood completely.

胡雪岩未忘旧情，罗四姐旧情未忘。
Neither Hu nor Luo had forgotten their former affection.

胡雪岩那边不会有什么障碍，如果罗四姐这方面肯委屈，倒也未始不是一件美事。
There would be no obstacle on his side; if Luo were willing to accept a less exalted position, the arrangement might prove a happy one.

感情上的事，要两厢情愿。
Affection required willingness on both sides.

七姑奶奶当时便作了个决定，给他们机会，让他们自己去接近。
She decided to give them opportunities to draw closer on their own.

果然有缘，两情相洽，那时看情形，再来做现成媒人，也还不迟。
If their feelings truly accorded, there would still be time to act as matchmaker once matters had developed.


“阿七，”古应春在喊，
“A'qi!” Gu called.

“小爷叔要走了。”
“Little Uncle is leaving.”

七姑奶奶转脸看时，小大姐已在伺候胡雪岩穿马褂了，
Turning, the Seventh Lady saw the maid helping Hu into his riding jacket.

“小爷叔，”她说：“今天不算数，
“Little Uncle, today does not count.

明天晚上我正正式式请罗四姐，
Tomorrow evening I shall formally entertain Sister Luo.

你有没有空？”
Are you free?”

胡雪岩尚未答话，罗四姐抢在前面谦谢，
Before he could answer, Luo protested politely.

“七姐，七姐，”她说，“你太客气了。”
“Seventh Sister, you are much too kind.”

“不是客气，
“It is not excessive kindness.

道理上应该。”七姑奶奶奶又说：
It is only proper.

“就算客气，也是这一回。”
And even if it is a formality, it will be the only time.”

罗四姐不作声了，
Luo fell silent.

胡雪岩便笑着问她说道：
Hu smiled at her.

“你看，七姐就有这点本事，
“That is one of Seventh Sister's talents.

随随便便一句话就能够把你的嘴封住，没话可说。”
A casual sentence, and she closes your mouth, leaving you nothing to say.”

“我话还是有的，”罗四姐说：“恭敬不如从命。”
“I still have something to say: obedience is better than excessive courtesy.”

“你这话。”七姑奶奶说道：“才真的太客气了。”
“Now that,” the Seventh Lady replied, “really is too polite.”

“那么，还有句不客气的话：只此一回，下不为例。”
“Then here is something less polite: just this once, and no precedent.”

“好，好。
“Very well.

下不为例。”
No precedent.”

古应春与胡雪岩互相看了一眼，有同感的默契，
Hu and Gu exchanged a glance of shared understanding.

罗四姐也是个角色，针锋相对，口才上并不逊于七姑奶奶。
Luo was a woman of mettle too, matching the Seventh Lady's wit point for point.


“闲话少说，”七姑奶奶问道：
“Enough talk,” the Seventh Lady said.

“小爷叔，明天晚上你到底有没有空？”
“Are you free tomorrow evening or not, Little Uncle?”

“没有空，也要抽出空来啊！”
“Even if I am not, I shall make time.”

“罗四姐，你看，你多少有面子！”
“See what consideration he shows you, Sister Luo!”

“哪里，我是沾七姐你的光。”
“I merely benefit from your influence.”

“地方呢？”胡雪岩插嘴问说。
“Where shall we dine?” Hu asked.

“你看呢？”七姑奶奶征询丈夫的意见，“我看还是在家里吧！”“也好。”
The Seventh Lady consulted her husband. “At home, I think?” He agreed.

“那就说定了。”七姑奶奶又说：
“Then it is settled.

“小爷叔，还有句话，我要言明在先。
But I must make one thing clear beforehand.

罗四姐今天住在我这里，
Sister Luo is staying here tonight.

明天早晨，我送她回去，下午再去接她，
Tomorrow morning I shall take her home and fetch her again in the afternoon.

不过。晚上送她回家，小爷叔，是你的差使了。
Taking her home tomorrow night, Little Uncle, will be your responsibility.”

“这是试探罗四姐，
This tested Luo's feelings.

如果她对胡雪岸没有意思，一定会推辞，
If she had no interest in Hu, she would certainly object.

一个男人，深夜送单身女子回家，那会在邻居之中引起极多的批评，
A man escorting an unattached woman home late at night would provoke much comment among the neighbours.

罗四姐果真以此为言，七姑奶奶是无法坚持一定要胡雪岩送的。
If she raised that objection, the Seventh Lady could not insist.

推辞也很容易，最简捷的办法，便是说夜深不便，仍旧想住在古家。
Refusal would also be easy: she could simply say that it would be inconvenient to return so late and that she wished to stay at the Gu house again.

可是，她不是这样说，说的是：“胡大先生应酬多，
Instead, she said, “Mr Hu has so many engagements.

不要再耽误他的工夫了。”
We ought not to take up more of his time.”

“没有，没有！”胡雪岩赶紧接口：
“None! None!” Hu answered quickly.

“明天晚上我没有应酬。”
“I have no engagement tomorrow evening.”

七姑奶奶看着罗四姐笑了，这一笑倒使得她有些发窘，将视线避了开去。
The Seventh Lady smiled at Luo, who grew a little embarrassed and looked away.


第二天，七姑奶奶送罗四姐回家，
The next morning the Seventh Lady took Luo home.

她家住南市，一楼一底的古库房子，这条弄堂是小康之家集居之地。
She lived in Nanshi, in a two-storey stone-gated house on a lane inhabited by comfortably situated families.

楼上住家，楼下客厅。
The living quarters were upstairs and the reception room below.

客厅中已坐满了人，大多挟着一个平平扁扁的包裹，
The latter was already crowded, most of the visitors carrying flat parcels.

有个中年妇女首先迎上来埋怨似地说：
A middle-aged woman approached first, sounding reproachful.

“罗四姐，你昨天一天哪里去了？
“Where were you all yesterday, Sister Luo?

我儿子要看病，
My son needs a doctor.

急着要交货等钱用。
I am waiting to deliver my work and get the money.”

““喔。”罗四奶歉然答说：
“Oh!” Luo replied apologetically.

“昨夜我住在我姐姐那里。”
“I stayed at my sister's last night.”

谁也没有听说过罗四姐有个姐姐，所以不免好奇地注视七姑奶奶，
Nobody had heard that she had a sister, and everyone looked curiously at the Seventh Lady.

看她一副富态福相，衣服华丽不说，腕上一双翠镯，指上黄豆大一枚闪光耀眼的金刚钻戒指，便使得大家另眼相看了。
Her prosperous appearance and fine clothes were impressive enough; the pair of jade bracelets at her wrists and the dazzling diamond ring, its stone as large as a soybean, inspired still greater respect.

七姑奶奶却毫无架子，而且极其爽郎，
Yet she was entirely unassuming and cheerful.

“你先不要招呼我，
“Do not attend to me.

大家都在等你。”她对罗四姐说：
Everyone is waiting.

“你赶紧料理，我来帮你。”
Get on with your work, and I shall help.”

“再好没有。”
“Nothing could be better!

罗四姐高叫：“老马，
Old Ma!

老马！”
Old Ma!”

老马是她请的帮手，五十多岁，帮她管帐兼应门，有时也打打杂，
Old Ma was her assistant, a man in his fifties who kept the accounts, answered the door, and occasionally did odd jobs.

人很老实，但语言木讷，行动迟缓。这么多交货领货的人，无以应付，索性在厢房里躲了起来，
Honest but slow in speech and movement, he had been unable to cope with so many people delivering and collecting work and had simply hidden in a side room.

此时听得招呼，方始现身。
Only when called did he emerge.

平时收货发货，只有罗四姐跟他两个人，
Normally Luo and he handled all the receiving and issuing themselves.

这天添了一个帮手，便顺利得多，但也一直到中午，方能毕事。
With another pair of hands, things went more smoothly, but it was still noon before they finished.

“真对不起。”罗四姐说，“累你忙了半天。”
“I am so sorry to have kept you working all morning,” Luo said.

接着便关照老马，到馆子里叫菜，要留七姑奶奶吃饭。
She told Old Ma to order food from a restaurant, intending to keep her guest for lunch.


“你不必客气，
“There is no need.

我来认一认地方，等下再来接你。
I came to learn where you live and shall return to fetch you later.

家里还有事要料理，
There are things to arrange at home.

我索性楼上都不上去了，下半天来了再来看你的卧房。”
I shall not even go upstairs now; I can see your room this afternoon.”

这在罗四姐倒是求之不得，因为卧房中难免有凌乱不宜待客之处。
Luo welcomed this, for her bedroom was bound to contain some untidiness unsuitable for a visitor.

“既然这么说，我也不留七姐了。”她说：
“Then I shall not detain you.

“下半天七姐派车子来好了，自己就不必劳驾了。
Just send a carriage this afternoon; you need not trouble to come yourself.

明天晚上，我请七姐、七姐夫来吃便饭，
Tomorrow evening I should like you and Brother-in-Law Gu to have a simple meal here.

不晓得七姐夫有没有空。”
I wonder whether he is free.”

“等下再说好了。”
“We can discuss that later.”

客人一走，罗四姐便从容了，
Once her guest had gone, Luo could relax.

吃过饭，她有午睡的习惯。
