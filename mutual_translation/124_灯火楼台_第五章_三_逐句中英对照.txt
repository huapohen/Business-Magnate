胡雪岩
《灯火楼台》第五章（三）
Towers of Light — Chapter Five, Part Three
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


罗四姐当然记得，
Of course Luo remembered.

在与胡雪岩重逢那天晚上就回忆过。
She had recalled it on the evening of their reunion.

那天，是七月三十日地藏王菩萨生日，
It had been the thirtieth day of the seventh month, the birthday of the Bodhisattva Kshitigarbha.

插了地藏香，全家都出去看放荷花灯，留她一个人看家，
After setting out the incense, the family had gone to watch lotus lanterns being floated, leaving her alone to mind the house.

胡雪岩忽然闯了进来。
Hu had suddenly appeared.

“你怎么来了？”
“What brings you here?”

“我来送会饯。”
“Your savings-club payment.

胡雪岩说：“今天月底，
Today is the last day of the month.

不送来迟一天就算出月了，信用要紧。
A day's delay would carry it into next month, and credit matters.

你们家人呢？”
Where is everyone?”

“都看荷花灯去了。”
“Watching the lotus lanterns.

罗四姐又说：“其实，你倒还是明天送来的好。
Actually, it would have been better if you brought it tomorrow.

因为我这笔钱转手要还人家的，
I have to pass it straight on to someone else.

左手来，右手去，清清爽爽，
In one hand and out of the other—that would be simple.

你今天晚上送来，过一夜，大钱不会生小钱，说不定晚上来了贼，
Money kept overnight will not produce little coins, and a thief might come.

那一来你的好意反倒害人。
Your kindness could turn into harm.”

““这一层我倒没有想到，
“I had not thought of that.

早知如此，我无论如何要凑齐了，吃过中饭就送来。”
If I had, I would have found some way to gather it all and bring it after lunch.”

胡雪岩想了一下说：
He considered.

“这样子好了，钱我带回去，省得害你担心。
“Let me take it back, then, so you need not worry.

这笔钱你要送给哪个，告诉我，明天一早，我替你去送。
Tell me who is to receive it, and I shall deliver it for you first thing tomorrow.”

““这样太好了。”罗四姐高兴地笑着，
“That would be wonderful!” she said happily.

“你替我赔脚步，
“You are saving me the journey.

我不晓得拿啥谢你？”
I do not know how to thank you.”

“先请我吃杯凉茶。”
“First, give me a cup of cool tea.”

“有，有！”
“Certainly!”

原来是借着插在地上的蜡烛光，在天井中说话，
They had been talking in the courtyard by the light of candles set in the ground.

要喝茶，便需延入堂屋。
For tea, she invited him into the main room.


她倒了茶来，胡雪岩一饮而尽。
She brought tea, and Hu drank it all.

随后他对罗四姐举止不当，她表示拒绝。
He then behaved inappropriately towards Luo, and she objected.

恰好门外有人大喊：“罗四姐，
Just then someone outside called loudly, “Sister Luo!

罗四姐！”
Sister Luo!”

她立刻与他拉开距离，答应一声：“来了！”
She immediately moved away from him and answered, “Coming!”

同时示意胡雪岩躲到一旁。
At the same time she signalled that Hu should step out of sight.

来的是邻居，来问一件小事，罗四姐三言两语，在门外把他打发走了。
The visitor was a neighbour with a small inquiry, whom she answered in a few words at the door and sent away.

回屋后，她站得远远的。
Back inside, she kept well away from Hu.

胡雪岩再想靠近时，她退到方桌对面。
When he tried to approach again, she moved to the other side of the square table.

“你好走了。
“You ought to go.

刚刚那个冒失鬼一叫，我吓得魂灵都要出窍了。
That thoughtless fellow’s sudden shout frightened me nearly out of my senses.

快，快，快点走。”
Quickly, quickly—please go.”


两人又说起那次往事。
They returned to the memory of that incident.

罗四姐的神情有些怅惘与落寞。
Luo’s expression showed sadness and loneliness.

“这件事有十年了吧？”胡雪岩问。
“It must have been ten years ago?” Hu asked.

“十一年。”罗四姐答说：
“Eleven,” Luo replied.

“那年我十六岁。”
“I was sixteen that year.”

胡雪岩还想顺着旧事谈下去，罗四姐却说不愿再谈，至少现在不是时候。
He tried to pursue the old subject, but she said she did not wish to discuss it, at least not yet.

“要到什么时候呢？”胡雪岩问。
“When, then?” he asked.


“我不晓得。”
“I don’t know,” Luo replied.

罗四姐忽然问道：“你看我的本事，就只配开一家绣庄？”
Then she suddenly asked, “Do you think my abilities fit me for nothing more than an embroidery shop?”

问到这句话，胡雪岩的绮念一收，
The question checked Hu's amorous thoughts.

“我们好好来谈一谈。”
“Let us discuss this seriously.

他说，“你的本事，十几岁我就晓得了，
I knew how capable you were when you were still in your teens.

那时候‘摇会’，盘利息，哪个都没有你精明。
Nobody was shrewder at arranging savings clubs or calculating interest.

说一句实话，你如果是男的，我要请你管钱庄。
Frankly, if you were a man, I would ask you to manage a bank.”

““卖高帽子不要本钱的。”
“Compliments cost nothing.”

罗四姐笑道：
She smiled.

“不管你说一定要男的才好管钱庄，这话我倒不大服气。”
“But I am not convinced by your claim that only a man can manage a bank.”

“你不要误会。
“Do not misunderstand.

我不是说你本事不如男的，
I am not saying that you are less capable.

是女人家不大方便，尤其是你这样子漂亮，
It is inconvenient for a woman—especially one as beautiful as you.

下面的伙计为了你争风吃醋，我的钱庄就要倒灶了。”
The clerks would quarrel over you, and my bank would be ruined.”

“要死！”
“How outrageous!”

罗四姐的一双脚虽非三寸金莲，但也是所谓“前面卖生姜，后面卖鸭蛋”裹了又放的半大脚，
Her feet were not tiny three-inch golden lilies, but the larger sort that had been bound and then released, described as having ginger for sale in front and duck eggs behind.

笑得有些立足不稳，伸出一只手去想扶桌沿，却让胡雪岩一把抄住了。
Laughing, she became a little unsteady and reached for the table, only to have Hu catch her hand.

“不要说伙计，”
“Never mind the clerks.

胡雪岩笑道，“就是我，只怕也没心思在生意上头了，一天到晚担心，哪个客人会把你讨了去。”
I might be unable to concentrate on business myself, worrying all day that some customer would marry you and take you away.”

杭州人叫“娶亲”为“讨亲”，
In Hangzhou, the expression he used meant taking a wife.

这最后一句话，又勾起罗四姐的心事，
It touched Luo's old sorrow.

“不要说了！”
“Enough!”

她夺回了手，坐到一旁，幽幽地说：“总怪我自己命苦。”
She drew back her hand, sat aside, and said softly, “I can only blame my unhappy fate.”

“我也难过啊！”胡雪岩以同感表示安慰，
“I feel unhappy too,” Hu offered in sympathy.

“我迟两年讨老婆就好了。”
“If only I had married two years later.”

“哼！”罗四姐微微冷笑，
She gave a faint, cold laugh.

“你嘴里说得好听。”
“Fine words come easily to you.”

“好听不好听，你等着看将来。”
“Whether they are merely fine words, time will show.

胡雪岩说道：“言归正传，你说你的本事不止于开一爿绣庄，
But to return to business: you say your abilities extend beyond an embroidery shop.

那么，还有啥大生意好做？
What larger enterprise have you in mind?

你说来我听听看。”
Tell me.”


罗四姐不作声，低着头看桌面，睫毛不住眨动，盘算得好象出神了。
She lowered her eyes to the tabletop, her lashes moving as though absorbed in calculation.

“明天再说。”罗四姐抬眼说道：
At last she looked up. “Let us talk tomorrow.

“你明天来吃便饭好不好？”
Will you come for a simple meal?”

“怎么不好？
“Gladly.

我明天下半天早一点来，好多谈谈。”
I shall come early in the afternoon so that we can talk at length.”

“不！
“No.

你明天来吃中饭，下半天早一点走。
Come for lunch and leave early in the afternoon.

晚上总不方便。”
Evenings are inconvenient.”

胡雪岩想了一下说：
Hu considered his engagements.

“明天中午我有两个饭局，有一个是要谈公事，不能不到。
He had two invitations for lunch the next day, one involving official business that he could not miss.

这倒麻烦了。”“那么后天呢？”后天中午已有应酬，不过可以推掉的。
The day after, he also had an engagement, but it could be declined.

““那就后天。”胡雪岩无奈，只好答说：“后天就后天。”
Luo settled on that day, and he reluctantly agreed.

“后天我弄两个杭州菜给你吃。”
“I shall make a couple of Hangzhou dishes for you.

罗四姐又说：“现在我代七姑奶奶做主人，请你吃消夜。”
Now let me act as hostess on the Seventh Lady's behalf and offer you supper.”

胡雪岩胃口不太好，本不想吃，但想到第二夭不能会面，便有些不舍之意，
Hu had little appetite, but the thought of not seeing her the next day made him reluctant to leave.

惜吃消夜盘桓一会也好，
Supper would provide an excuse to linger.

便点点头：“不必费事！”
He nodded, asking her not to go to any trouble.

“现在的东西。”
“Everything is ready.

罗四姐说，“到楼下去吃好不好？”
Shall we eat downstairs?”

原要在楼上小酌才够味，但那一来比较费事，变成言行不符，
Drinking a little together upstairs would have been more agreeable, but would involve extra work and contradict his request.

只好站起身来，跟着罗四姐下楼。
He rose and followed her down.

“你吃什么酒？”
“What would you like to drink?”

“随便。”
“Anything.

胡雪岩说：“我又不会吃酒，完全陪你。”
I am no drinker; I shall merely keep you company.”

“谢谢。既然你陪我，就陪我吃我自己泡的药酒。”
“Then join me in some medicinal wine I have infused myself.”

“喔，
“Ah!

我倒想起来了……”
That reminds me…”

“慢点！”罗四姐说：“等我把桌子摆好了再说。”
“Wait until I have laid the table.”


桌子上摆出来四个碟子，火腿、脆鳝、素鸡糟白鲞是七姑奶奶送的。
Four dishes appeared: ham, crisp eel, vegetarian mock chicken, and dried croaker preserved in wine lees, all sent by the Seventh Lady.

罗四姐另外捧来一个白瓷坛，
Luo also brought a white porcelain jar.

倒出来的药酒，颜色不佳，但香味扑鼻，发人酒兴。
The medicinal wine she poured from it had an unappealing colour but an enticing fragrance.

“你这酒看样子不坏，
“This looks promising.

有没有方子？”
Do you have the recipe?”

“有。
“Yes.

名叫周公百岁酒。
It is called Duke Zhou's Hundred-Year Wine.

你要，我抄一个给你。”
I can copy it for you.”

“有这种方子，越多越好。”
“The more recipes of this kind, the better.

胡雪岩说，“我想开一家药店，将来要卖药酒。”
I intend to open a pharmacy and sell medicinal wines.”

罗四诅不由得诧异，“怎么忽然想起来要开药店？”她问。
Surprised, she asked what had suddenly given him that idea.

“其中有好些缘故。
“Several things.

有个缘故是有人要我办各样成药，数量很大，
One is a request for a very large quantity of prepared medicines.

我心里在想，不如自己开一家药店，既方便，又道地。”
I thought that opening a pharmacy myself would be both convenient and a way to ensure proper quality.”

“这个人是哪个？要那许多成药，做啥用场？”
Who had made the request, and why so much medicine?

原来左宗棠的西征将士，已发现有水土不服的现象，
Zuo's soldiers on the western campaign had begun suffering from difficulty adjusting to unfamiliar climates and conditions.

寄信到上海转运局，要采办大批丸散膏丹，
Letters to the Shanghai transshipment bureau requested large quantities of pills, powders, plasters, and other remedies.

因而触发了胡雪岩自己设一座大规模的药铺的构想。
This had prompted Hu's plan for a substantial pharmacy.

目前已请了一道陕甘总督衙门所发、请予免税的公文，派人到生药最大的集散地，直隶安国县采办道地药材去了。
He had already obtained an official communication from the Governor-General of Shaanxi and Gansu requesting tax exemption, and had sent buyers to Anguo County in Zhili, the largest centre for crude medicinal ingredients, to purchase authentic supplies.

对于这个计划，胡雪岩最感兴趣，认为是救世济民、鼓励士气最切实的一件事，
Nothing interested him more than this plan, which he regarded as a practical means of relieving suffering and encouraging the troops.

一谈起来，滔滔不绝。
Once started, he spoke without pause.

罗四姐很用心地倾听着，遇有他说得欠明白之处，会要言不烦地提出疑问。
Luo listened closely and asked concise questions whenever something was unclear.

这表示她不但能够领会他的计划，而且也关心他的事业，胡雪岩便越加兴奋了。
Seeing that she both understood the project and cared about his work, he grew still more enthusiastic.

一谈谈到三更天，
They talked into the third watch.

胡雪岩发现左右邻居看她家半夜里灯火辉煌，门前轿班高声谈笑，都好奇地在张望，
At last he noticed neighbours looking curiously at the brilliant lights in her house and the chair bearers talking and laughing loudly outside.

不免抱愧，也不好意思再作流连。
Ashamed of the disturbance, he could no longer justify staying.

“好了，后天中午再来。”
“The day after tomorrow at noon, then.

胡雪岩站起身来说：“再谈下去，邻居要骂人了。”
If we talk any longer, the neighbours will curse us.”


到得第三天上午，胡雪岩照例先到阜康钱庄办事，
On the third morning, Hu went as usual to Fukang Bank.

有人告诉他说，“维记”来提了九千两银子，开出数目大小不等的十七张庄票。
He was told that Weiji had withdrawn nine thousand taels in seventeen bank drafts of differing amounts.

胡雪岩记在心里，并未多问。
He remembered the information but asked no questions.

由于那天到罗四姐家，自觉太招摇了，这天只带了一个跟班，亦未乘轿，而且坐了一辆“亨斯美”马车，
Feeling that his previous visit to Luo had been too conspicuous, he took only one attendant this time and took a Hengsimei carriage instead of a sedan chair.

在罗家弄口下车，将马车打发回去，步行赴约。
Alighting at the entrance to her lane, he sent the carriage away and walked to the house.

本未过午，罗家客厅里还坐着七、八个客户在等候发落。
It was not yet noon, and seven or eight customers were still waiting in her reception room.

“胡大先生请坐。”罗四姐大大方方地站起来说：
Luo rose without embarrassment. “Please sit down, Mr Hu.

“我马上就好了。”
I shall be finished very soon.”

“不忙，不忙！
“There is no hurry.

你尽管请治公。”
Attend to your business.”

胡雪岩捧着一杯茶，悄悄坐在一边，看罗四姐处事，口讲指画，十分明快。
Holding a cup of tea, he watched quietly as she gave clear, brisk explanations and directions.

她的客户似乎也服她，说如何便如何，绝无争执，
Her customers seemed to accept her authority: what she said was done, without argument.

所以不过一盏茶的工夫，都打发走了。
In scarcely the time needed to drink the tea, everyone had departed.

“佩服，佩服。”
“Admirable!”

胡雪岩笑道：
Hu smiled.

“实在能干。”
“You really are capable.”

“能干不能干还不晓得。
“We do not know that yet.

等我替你买的地皮涨了价，你再恭维我。”
Wait until the land I bought for you rises in value before praising me.”

胡雪岩摸不着头脑，
He was bewildered.

“罗四姐，”他问：“你在说啥？”
“What are you talking about?”

“等等吃饭的时候再同你讲。
“I shall explain over lunch.

你请坐一坐，我要下厨房了。”
Sit a little longer while I go to the kitchen.”


厨房里菜都预备得差不多了，
Most of the meal was ready.

炉子上炖着鱼头豆腐，“件儿肉”在蒸宠里，
Fish head and bean curd simmered on the stove; pieces of pork were in the steamer.

凉菜盐水虾、葱焖鲫鱼和素鸡，是早做好了的，
Brined shrimps, crucian carp braised with spring onions, and vegetarian mock chicken had been prepared earlier.

起油锅炸个“响铃儿”，再炒一个荠菜春笋，就可以开饭了。
She needed only to fry crisp bean-curd-skin rolls and stir-fry shepherd's purse with spring bamboo shoots.

“没有啥好东西请你。”
“Nothing fine to offer you.

罗四姐说：“不过我想，你天天鱼翅海参，大概也吃腻了，
But you eat shark's fin and sea cucumber every day and must be tired of them.

倒不如清清爽爽几样家常菜，或许反例可以多吃一碗饭。”
A few simple, refreshing home dishes might persuade you to eat another bowl of rice.”

“一点不错。”
“Exactly.”

胡雪岩欣然落座，
Hu sat down happily.

“本来没有啥胃口，现在倒真有点饿了。”
“I had little appetite before, but now I am hungry.”

罗四姐笑笑不作声，只替他斟了一杯药酒，然后布菜。
She smiled, poured medicinal wine, and served him.

胡雪岩吃得很起劲，罗四姐当然也很高兴。
He ate with relish, much to her pleasure.

“你刚才说什么地皮不地皮，我没有听懂。
“I did not understand what you said about land.

请你再说一遍。”
Please explain.”

罗四姐点点头，“你给我的折子，我昨天去提了九千两银子。”
“I withdrew nine thousand taels yesterday from the account you gave me.

她问，“你晓得不晓得？”
Did you know?”

“他们告诉我了。”
“They told me.”

“从前年英租界改路名的辰光，我就看出来了，外国人办事按部就班，有把握的，
“When the British concession renamed its roads two years ago, I noticed how methodically the foreigners proceeded.

马路修到哪里，地价涨到哪里，
Wherever they built a road, land prices rose.

可惜我没有闲钱来买地皮。
Unfortunately, I had no spare money to buy any.

前两个月还有人来兜我，说山东路……”
A couple of months ago someone approached me about a plot on Shandong Road…”

“慢点！”
“Wait.

胡雪岩问道：“山东路在啥地方？”
Where is Shandong Road?”

“就是庙街。”
“Temple Street.”


原来英租街新造的马路，最初方便他们自己，起的是英文名字，例如领事馆集中之处，名为Consulate Road，江海关所在地名为Customs Road.上海在战国时，原为楚国春申君黄歇的封邑，
When the British concession's roads were first laid out, they received English names for the foreigners' convenience: Consulate Road where the consulates gathered, and Customs Road where the Jianghaiguan stood. During the Warring States period, Shanghai had belonged to the fief of Lord Chunshen, Huang Xie of Chu.

当时为了松江水患，要导流入海，春申君开了一条浦江，
To relieve flooding in the Songjiang region, he had opened a channel to carry water to the sea.

用好的姓，称为黄浦江，或称黄歇浦，
It was called the Huangpu after his surname, or Huang Xie Pu.

此外春申浦、春申江、申江，种种上海的别称，都由此而来。
Other names for Shanghai—Chunshen Pu, Chunshen Jiang, and Shenjiang—came from the same association.

后人为了崇功报德，曾建了一座春申侯词，又称春申君庙，但年深月久，遗址无处可寻。
Later generations had built a shrine to honour his achievement, also known as Lord Chunshen's temple, but its site could no longer be identified.

相传建于明朝、地在三茅阁桥、供奉“三茅真君”的延真观，原来就是春申君庙，
Tradition held that Yanzhen Temple, established in the Ming near Sanmaoge Bridge to worship the Three Mao Immortals, had originally been that shrine.

英国人便将开在那里的一条马路，称为Temple Street，译成中文便是“庙街”。
The British therefore named a nearby road Temple Street.

英租界的地名很乱，工部局早就想反它统一起来，
Place names in the concession were confused, and the Municipal Council had long wanted a uniform system.

将界内的马路，分为两类，横的一类从东到西，用中国主要的城市命名，纵的自南至北，以中国的省名命名，
East–west roads were to bear the names of major Chinese cities, while north–south roads would take provincial names.

因此领事馆路改名北京路，
Consulate Road became Beijing Road.

而第二个大城市是南京。便将外滩公园向西延伸的马路，改名南京路。
Nanjing supplied the next great city's name, given to the road extending west from the Bund park.

庙街是南北向，改名山东路。
Temple Street ran north–south and became Shandong Road.

那是前两年的事，胡雪岩未尝留意于此，所以罗四姐提起这个新地名，他茫然莫辨。
This had happened two years earlier, but Hu had paid no attention and did not recognize the new name.

庙街他是知道的，
Temple Street he knew.
