胡雪岩
《灯火楼台》第五章（四，至本章结束）
Towers of Light — Chapter Five, Part Four (conclusion)
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第三天下午，七姑奶奶陪了罗四姐去请教吴铁口。
On the afternoon of the third day, the Seventh Lady accompanied Luo Sijie to consult Wu Tiekou.

他住的二马路，英文名字叫做Rope Walk Road，翻译出来是“纤道路”，
He lived on Second Road, whose English name had been Rope Walk Road, translated into Chinese as Towpath Road.

当初洋泾浜还可以通船，不过水浅要拉纤，
In the early days, boats could still use Yangjing Creek, but the shallow water made towing necessary.

这条纤路改成马路，就叫纤道路。
When the towpath was turned into a road, it retained that name.

本地人叫不来英文路名，就拿首先开辟的Garden Lane叫做大马路，
The local people could not manage the English street names, so they called Garden Lane, the first road opened, First Road.

往南第二条便叫二马路，以下三马路、四马路、五马路，一直到洋泾浜，都是东西向。
The next to the south became Second Road, followed by Third, Fourth and Fifth Roads, all running east and west, down to Yangjing Creek.

前两年大马路改名南京路，二马路改名杭州路，
Two years earlier, First Road had been renamed Nanjing Road and Second Road Hangzhou Road.

有人跟洋人说，南京到杭州的水路是两条，一条长江，一条运河，
Someone pointed out to the foreigners that there were two waterways between Nanjing and Hangzhou, the Yangtze and the Grand Canal.

南京是长江下游，要挑个长江上游的大码头当路名，跟南京路才连得起来，
Since Nanjing was on the lower Yangtze, the name of a major port farther upstream would make a more fitting companion to Nanjing Road.

因而改为九江路，三马路也就是“海关路”，自然成为汉口路。
Second Road was therefore renamed Jiujiang Road, and Third Road, formerly Customs Road, naturally became Hankou Road.

不过上海人叫惯了，仍旧称做大马路、二马路。
Shanghai people were accustomed to the old names, however, and continued to say First Road and Second Road.


二马路开辟得早，市面早就繁华了。
Second Road had been opened early, and its shops and businesses had long flourished.

吴铁口“候教”之处在二马路富厚里，进弄堂右首第一家就是，
Wu Tiekou's place for receiving clients was in Fuhou Lane: the first house on the right.

二座石库房子打通，客堂很大，
Two stone-gated houses had been joined together, making a large reception hall.

上面挂满了达官巨商名流送的匾额，
Its walls were covered with inscribed plaques presented by high officials, wealthy merchants and distinguished men.

胡雪岩也送了一块，题的是“子平绝诣”四字，
Hu Xueyan had given one himself, bearing the words “Supreme Mastery of Ziping Astrology.”

挂在北面板壁上，板壁旁边有一道门，里面就是吴铁口设砚之处。
It hung on the north partition, beside a door leading to Wu's consulting room.

那吴铁口生得方面大耳，两撇八字胡子，
Wu had a square face, large ears and a moustache that spread down on either side.

年纪只有三十出头，不过戴了一副大墨晶眼镜，看上去比较老气，
He was only a little over thirty, but his large dark-crystal spectacles made him look older.

身上穿的是枣红缎子夹袍，外套玄色团花马褂，
He wore a lined robe of date-red satin beneath a black jacket patterned with round floral medallions.

头上青缎小帽，帽檐上镶一块极大的玭霞，
An enormous stone called pinxia was set in the band of his blue-satin cap.

手上留着极长的指甲，
His fingernails were very long.

左手大拇指上套一个汉玉扳指，右手无名指上还有一枚方钻白金戒指，马褂上又是黄澄澄横过胸前的一条金表链，
On his left thumb he wore an archer's ring of Han jade, on his right ring finger a platinum ring set with a square diamond; a bright gold watch chain crossed his chest.

打扮得象个花花公子。
He was dressed like a fashionable rake.

“古太太，”
“Mrs Gu!”

吴铁口起身迎接，马褂下面垂着四个大小荷包，
He rose to greet them, four purses of different sizes dangling beneath his jacket.

他摘下眼镜笑道：
Removing his spectacles, he smiled.

“你的气色真好。”
“How well you look.”

“交比劫运了，怎么不好。”
“With a bijie period like this, how could I not?”

七姑奶奶指着罗四姐说：
She indicated Luo.

“这位是我的要好姐妹，姓罗。
“This is a dear friend of mine, surnamed Luo.

吴先生，你叫她罗四姐好了。”
You may call her Sister Luo.”

“是，是！
“Certainly.

罗四姐。
Sister Luo.

两位请坐。”
Please sit down, both of you.”

红木书桌旁边，有两张凳子，一张在对面，一张在左首，
There were two stools beside the rosewood desk, one opposite Wu and one to his left.

七姑奶奶自己坐了对面，示意罗四姐坐在吴铁口身旁，以便交谈。
The Seventh Lady took the former, indicating that Luo should sit beside him for ease of conversation.

吴铁口重新戴上墨晶眼镜，在那张红木太师椅上落坐，挽起衣袖，提笔在手，
Wu put on his spectacles, settled into his rosewood armchair, rolled back his sleeves and took up his brush.

问明罗四姐的年月日时，在水牌上将她的“四柱”排了出来：“已巳、辛未、甲子、甲子”，
Having asked Luo's birth year, month, day and hour, he wrote her Four Pillars on a writing tablet: ji-si, xin-wei, jia-zi, jia-zi.

然后批批点点，搁笔凝神细看。
He added marks and annotations, laid down the brush and studied them intently.


这一看，足足看了一刻钟，
He studied them for a full quarter of an hour.

罗四姐从侧面望去，只见他墨晶镜片后面的眼珠，眨得很厉害，心里不由得有些发毛。
From the side, Luo could see his eyes blinking rapidly behind the dark lenses, and she began to feel uneasy.

“吴先生，”她终于忍不住了，“我的命不好？”
“Mr Wu,” she said at last, unable to contain herself, “is my destiny bad?”

吴铁口摘下眼镜，看着罗四姐说：
He removed his spectacles and looked at her.

“可惜了！”
“What a pity!”

接着望对面的七姑奶奶，加重语气说：“真可惜！”
Then he turned to the Seventh Lady and repeated with emphasis, “A very great pity!”

“怎么？”七姑奶奶说：“吴先生，
“What is it?

请你实说。
Please tell us frankly.

君子问祸不问福，
A sensible person asks about misfortune, not good fortune.

罗四姐很开通的，
Sister Luo is broad-minded.

你用不着有啥忌讳。”
There is no need to spare her feelings.”

吴铁口重重点一点头，将眼镜放在一边，拿笔指点着说。
Wu nodded heavily, set his spectacles aside and pointed with his brush.

“罗四姐，你是木命，
“Sister Luo, yours is a Wood destiny.

‘日元’应下一个‘正印’，时辰上又是甲子，木‘比’‘印’庇，
Beneath your day stem lies a Direct Seal, and the hour is also jia-zi: Wood has a fellow to support it and a Seal to shelter it.

光看日时两柱，就是个逢凶化吉、遇难成祥的‘上造’。”
Judging by the day and hour pillars alone, this is a superior configuration, one that turns misfortune into blessing and danger into good luck.”

罗四姐不懂什么叫“上造”，但听得出命是好命，
Luo did not understand “superior configuration,” but she could tell that her destiny was good.

当即说道：“吴先生，请你再说下去。”
“Please go on.”

“木命生在夏天，又是已火之年，这株树本来很难活，
“Wood born in summer, in a si-Fire year as well: this tree would normally find it hard to survive.

好得有子水滋润，
Fortunately, zi-Water nourishes it.

不但可活，而且是株大树。
Not only can it live; it is a great tree.

金木水火土，五行俱备，‘财’、‘官’、‘印’、‘食’四字全，
Metal, Wood, Water, Fire and Earth are all present, and so are Wealth, Officer, Seal and the Food God.

又是正官正印，
Moreover, these are a Direct Officer and a Direct Seal.

这个八字，如果是男命，就同苏州的潘文荣公一样，状元宰相，寿高八十，儿孙满堂，荣华富贵享不尽。
If this were a man's horoscope, he would be like Lord Pan Wenrong of Suzhou: first in the imperial examination, a chief minister, living to eighty with children and grandchildren all around him, enjoying endless honour and riches.

可惜是女命！”
A pity it is a woman's destiny!”


罗四姐尚未开口，七姑奶奶抗声说道：
Before Luo could speak, the Seventh Lady protested loudly.

“女命又怎么样？
“What is wrong with a woman's destiny?

状元宰相还不是女人生的？”
Are examination champions and chief ministers not born of women?”

“古太太，你不要光火！”吴铁口从从容容地答道：
“Do not be angry, Mrs Gu,” Wu replied calmly.

“我说可惜，不是说罗四姐的命不好。
“When I say it is a pity, I do not mean that Sister Luo's destiny is bad.

这样的八字如果再说不好，天理难容了。”
Heaven would not forgive me if I called a horoscope like this bad.”

听这一说，七姑奶奶才回嗔作喜，
Her anger gave way to pleasure.

“那么，可惜在哪里呢，
“Then what is there to regret?

吴先生，”她说：“千万请你实说。”
Please, Mr Wu, tell us honestly.”

“我本来要就命论命，实话直说的，
“I had intended to judge the horoscope on its merits and speak plainly.

现在倒不敢说了。”
Now I hardly dare.”

“为啥呢？”
“Why not?”

“古太太火气这么大，万一我说了不中听的话，古太太一个耳光劈上来，
“With your temper, Mrs Gu, if I said something unwelcome, you might slap me.

我这个台坍不起。”
I could not afford such a loss of dignity.”

“对不住，对不住！”七姑奶奶笑道道歉，
“I beg your pardon!” She laughed apologetically.

“吴先生，请你放心。
“Rest assured.

话说明白了，我自然不会光火。”说完，吴铁口叫小跟班拿水烟袋来吸水烟，又叫小跟班装果盘招待堂客。
Explain yourself clearly, and of course I shan't lose my temper.” Wu called for his water pipe, then told his young attendant to bring a fruit platter for the ladies.

七姑奶奶一面连声：“不客气，不客气。”一面却又唤小大姐取来她的银水烟袋，
Repeatedly protesting against all this trouble, the Seventh Lady nevertheless summoned her maid to bring her silver water pipe.

点上纸媒，好整以暇地也“呼噜呼噜”地吸将起来。
She lit a paper spill and began smoking with leisurely, bubbling draws.

她跟吴铁口取得极深的默契而扮演的这出双簧，已将罗四姐迷惑住了，
She and Wu were playing their duet with a perfect understanding, and Luo was already taken in.

渴望想听“可惜”些什么，见此光景，心里焦急，而且有些侄七姑奶奶不体谅她的心事，
Desperate to learn what was so regrettable, she grew impatient at this interruption, even resenting the Seventh Lady's failure to consider her anxiety.

却又不便实说，只好假装咳嗽，表示为水烟的烟子呛着了，借以暗示七姑奶奶可以歇手了。
Unable to say so openly, she pretended to cough, as though the pipe smoke were choking her, to hint that her friend might stop.


“把窗户开开。”
“Open the window,” Wu ordered.

吴铁口将水烟袋放下，重新提笔，先看七姑奶奶，将她的注意力吸引过来，方始开口说道：
He put down his pipe and took up his brush again, first looking at the Seventh Lady to secure her attention.

“女命跟男命的看法不同，
“A woman's horoscope must be read differently from a man's.

女命以‘克我’为‘夫星’，
In a woman's destiny, the element that controls her represents the Husband Star.

所以男命的‘正官’、‘偏官’，在女命中都当丈夫来看。
Thus what we call the Direct Officer and Indirect Officer in a man's horoscope both stand for husbands in a woman's.

这是一句‘总经’，
That is a fundamental rule.

要懂这个道理，才晓得罗四姐的八字，为啥可惜？
You must understand it to see why Sister Luo's horoscope is regrettable.”

“七姑奶奶略通命理，听得懂他的话，
The Seventh Lady knew something of astrology and followed him.

罗四姐不十分了了，但为急于听下文，也微微颔首，表示会意。
Luo understood less, but, eager for what came next, nodded faintly too.

“金克木，
“Metal controls Wood.

月上的这个‘辛金’，就是‘甲木’的夫星。
This xin-Metal in the month pillar is the Husband Star for jia-Wood.

坏就坏在时辰上也有个甲，
The trouble is that there is another jia in the hour pillar.

这有个名堂，叫做‘二女争夫’。”
We have a name for that: Two Women Contending for One Husband.”

七姑奶奶与罗四姐不约而同地互看了一眼，
The women exchanged a simultaneous glance.

罗四姐有所示意，七姑奶奶也领会，便代她发言。
Luo's look conveyed a request, and the Seventh Lady spoke for her.

“吴先生，你是说另外有个女人，跟罗四姐争？”
“You mean another woman will compete with Sister Luo?”

“不错。”
“Exactly.”

“那么争得过，争不过呢？”
“Can she win?”

“急得过就不可惜了。”
“If she could, there would be nothing to regret.

吴铁口说：“二女争夫，强者为胜。
When two women contend for one husband, the stronger wins.

照表面看，你是甲子，我也是甲子，子水生甲木，
On the surface, each is jia-zi, with zi-Water generating jia-Wood.

好比小孩打架，这面大人出来帮儿子，那面也有大人出来说话，
It is like children fighting: a parent comes out to help the child on one side, and another parent speaks up on the other.

旗鼓相当扯个直。”
They seem evenly matched.”

“嗯，嗯，”罗四姐这下心领神会，连连说道：“我懂了，我懂了。”
“Yes, yes, I understand!” Luo said repeatedly.

“罗四姐，照规矩说，时上的甲子本来争不过你的，
“By the usual rule, Sister Luo, the jia-zi in the hour pillar ought not to beat you.

为啥呢，你的夫星紧靠在你，近水楼台先得月，
Your Husband Star is right beside you: the pavilion nearest the water catches the moon first.

应该你占上风。
You should have the advantage.

可惜‘庚子望未’，
Unfortunately, there is the configuration called geng-zi wang-wei.

辰戌丑未‘四季土’，土生金，
Chen, xu, chou and wei are the Earth of the four seasons, and Earth generates Metal.

对方就是‘财星官’，
On the other side, Wealth benefits the Officer.

对夫星倒是大吉大利，对你大坏，坏在‘财损印’！
That is excellent for the Husband Star but disastrous for you, because Wealth Damages the Seal.

好比小孩子打架，一方面有父母，一方面父母不在了，是个孤儿。
Imagine children fighting when one has parents and the other has lost theirs and is an orphan.

你想，打得过人家，打不过人家？”
Could the orphan defeat the other child?”


这番解说，听得懂的七姑奶奶觉得妙不可言：
The Seventh Lady, who could follow the explanation, thought it marvellous.

“吴先生，我看看。”
“Let me look, Mr Wu.”

吴铁口将水牌倒了过来，微侧着向罗四姐这面，让她们都能得见，
He turned the tablet round, angling it slightly towards Luo so that both could see.

七姑奶奶细看了一会，指点着向罗四姐说：
After studying it, the Seventh Lady pointed things out to her friend.

“你看，
“Look.

庚下这个未，是土，
This wei beneath geng is Earth.

紧靠着你的那个子，是水，水克土。
It is right beside your zi, which is Water, and Water controls Earth.

水是财，土是印，所以叫做财损印。
Water is Wealth and Earth is the Seal, hence Wealth Damages the Seal.

没有办法，
There is nothing to be done.

你命中注定，争不过人家。”
You are destined to lose to the other woman.”

“争不过人家，怎么样呢？”罗四姐问。
“And what happens if I lose?”

这话当然要吴铁口来回答：
That, of course, was for Wu to answer.

“做小！”
“Become a concubine.”

两字斩钉截铁。
The words were uncompromising.

罗四姐听他语声冷酷无情，大起反感，提高了声音说：
Repelled by his cold, unfeeling tone, Luo raised her voice.

“不愿意做小呢！”
“And if I refuse to become a concubine?”

“克夫。”
“You will bring death to your husband.”

“克过了。”
“I have already lost one.”

“还是要做小！”
“You must still become a concubine.”

“偏要做大！”
“I insist on being a principal wife!”

“做大还是要克，
“Then you will still bring death.

嫁一个克一个。”
Every man you marry will die.”

罗四姐脸都气白了，
Luo's face went white with anger.

“我倒不相信……”
“I don't believe…”

一个铁口，一个硬碰，
Wu's iron certainty met an equally unyielding resistance.

看看要吵架了，七姑奶奶赶紧拉一拉罗四姐的衣服说：
Seeing a quarrel about to break out, the Seventh Lady quickly tugged Luo's clothes.

“宁可同爷强，不可同命强，
“Better defy your father than defy your fate.

你先听吴先生说，
Hear Mr Wu out.

说得没有道理再驳也不迟。”
If he makes no sense, there will be time enough to contradict him.”

“我如果说得没有道理，古太太，罗四姐请我吃耳光不还手。”
“If I make no sense, Mrs Gu, Sister Luo may slap me and I shall not lift a hand.”

吴铁口指着水牌说：
Wu pointed to the tablet.

“罗四姐克过了，八字上也看得出来的，
“Her husband's death can also be seen here.

‘印’是荫覆，
The Seal represents shelter.

在家从父，出嫁从夫，
At home a woman depends on her father; once married, on her husband.

这印是个靠山，
The Seal is her support.

丈夫去世，不就是靠山倒了？”
If her husband dies, has that support not collapsed?”

说着，抬眼去看。
He raised his eyes to observe her reaction.


罗四姐脸色比较缓和了，
Luo's expression had softened somewhat.

七姑奶奶便说：“为啥还是要做小呢？”
The Seventh Lady asked, “But why must she still become a concubine?”

“因为未土克了第一个子水，过去就克第二个子水了，
“Because after wei-Earth has overcome the first zi-Water, it goes on to overcome the second.

逃不掉的。
There is no escaping it.

真的不肯做小，也没有办法，
If she truly refuses to become a concubine, there is nothing to be done.

所谓‘人各有志，不能相强。’
Everyone has their own aspirations; we cannot force them.

不过，这一来，前面的‘财’、‘官’、‘食’就不必再看了。”
But then there is no need to examine the Wealth, Officer and Food God in the earlier part of the horoscope.”

“为啥不必再看？”
“Why not?”

“人都不在了，看它何用？”
“What use are they if the person is no longer alive?”

罗四姐大吃一惊，
Luo was badly startled.

“吴先生，”她问，“你说不肯做小，命就没有了？”
“Mr Wu, do you mean I shall die if I refuse to become a concubine?”

“当然。
“Certainly.

未土连克子水，
Wei-Earth overcomes one zi-Water after another.

甲木不避，要跟它硬上，
Suppose jia-Wood refuses to retreat and confronts it.

好，木克土，甲木有帮手，力量很强，
Very well: Wood controls Earth, and jia-Wood has assistance, so its strength is considerable.

不过你们倒看看未土，
But look at wei-Earth.

年上那个己土是帮手，
The ji-Earth in the year pillar supports it.

这还在其次，最厉害是已火，火生土，源源不绝，
That is not even the most important thing: the greatest power is si-Fire, for Fire generates Earth, supplying it without end.

请问哪方面强？
Which side is stronger?

五行生克，向来克不倒就要被克。
In the interaction of the Five Elements, what fails to overcome its opponent is overcome in turn.

这块未土硬得象块石头一样，草木不生，
This wei-Earth is hard as rock, on which nothing can grow.

甲木要去斗它，就好比拿木头去开山，
If jia-Wood challenges it, it is like trying to split a mountain with a piece of timber.

木头敲断，山还是山。”
The timber breaks; the mountain remains.”

听得这番解说。罗四姐象斗败了的公鸡似地，
Luo looked like a defeated fighting cock.

刚才那种“偏要做大”的倔强之气，消失得无影无踪，但心里却仍不甘做小。
Her fierce insistence on being a principal wife had vanished completely, although inwardly she still resented becoming a concubine.


于是七姑奶奶便要从正面来谈了，
The Seventh Lady now turned to the favourable side.

“那么，做了小就不要紧了。”
“Then she will be all right if she becomes a concubine?”

她问“不是不要紧，
“It is not merely a matter of being all right.

是要做了小，就是说肯拿辛金当夫星，然后才能谈得到前面那四个字的好处。”
Only by becoming a concubine—that is, by accepting xin-Metal as her Husband Star—can we begin to discuss the benefits of those four characters at the beginning.”

“你是说，年上月上那四个字？”
“You mean the four characters of the year and month pillars?”

“是啊！
“Yes.

土生金好比母子，
Earth generating Metal is like a mother and child.

木既嫁了金，就是一家眷属，
Once Wood has married Metal, they belong to the same family.
