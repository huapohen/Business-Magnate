胡雪岩
《灯火楼台》第六章（中）
Towers of Light — Chapter Six, Part Two
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


七姑奶奶亦正有此意。
The Seventh Lady had been thinking the same thing.

找个空隙，打断他们的谈锋，说了两句做女主人应有的门面话，与罗四姐双双席席。
Finding a pause, she interrupted the men's flow of talk with a few of the gracious remarks expected of a hostess, and the two women left the table.

七姑奶奶将她带到楼上卧室。这间卧室一直为罗四姐所欣赏，
She took Luo Sijie upstairs to her bedroom, which Luo had always admired.

因为经过古应春设计，改成西式，有个很宽敞的阳台，装置很大的玻璃门，门上加两层帷幕、一层薄纱、一层丝绒，
Gu Yingchun had redesigned it in the Western style, with a spacious balcony and large glass doors hung with two layers of curtains, one gauze and the other velvet.

白天拉开丝绒那一层，阳光透过薄纱，铺满整个房间，明亮华丽，令人精神一爽。
When the velvet curtains were drawn back by day, sunlight passed through the gauze and filled the room, making it bright, splendid, and wonderfully refreshing.

晚上坐在阳台上看万家灯火，亦别有一番情趣，
At night, the sight of countless lights from the balcony had a charm of its own.

尤其是象这种夏天，在阳台上纳凉闲谈，是最舒服不过的一件事。
In summer, especially, nothing could be more comfortable than sitting there in the cool air and talking.

“你是喝中国茶，还是喝洋茶？”
“Will you have Chinese tea or foreign tea?”

所谓“喝洋茶”是英国式的奶茶。
Foreign tea meant English tea with milk.

七姑奶奶有全套的银茶具，照英国规矩亲自调制，而且亲自为客人倒茶，颇为费事。
The Seventh Lady had a complete silver tea service and followed the English custom of preparing the tea herself and pouring it for her guests, a rather elaborate business.

罗四姐此刻要谈正事，无心欣赏“洋茶”，便即说道：
Luo, intent on discussing serious matters, had no mind to appreciate it.

“我想吃杯菊花茶。”
“I'd like a cup of chrysanthemum tea.”

黄白“杭菊花”可以当茶叶泡来喝，有清心降火之功，
The yellow and white Hangzhou chrysanthemums could be brewed like tea to calm the mind and dispel internal heat.

七姑奶奶笑着问道：“你大概心里很乱？”
“Is your mind rather unsettled?” the Seventh Lady asked with a smile.

“也不晓得啥道理，心里一直烦躁。”
“I don't know why, but I've been feeling restless all this time.”

“我们到阳台上来坐。”
“Let's sit out on the balcony.”

七姑奶奶挑到阳台上去密谈，是替罗四姐设想，
She chose the balcony out of consideration for Luo.

因为谈到自己的终身大事，她难免腼腆，阳台上光线幽暗，可以隐藏妞促的表情，就比较能畅所欲言了。
Discussing her own marriage was bound to make her shy; in the dim light she could conceal her embarrassment and speak more freely.


等小大姐泡了菊花茶来，背光坐着的罗四姐幽幽地叹口气说：
When the maid had brought their chrysanthemum tea, Luo, sitting with her face turned away from the light, gave a soft sigh.

“七姐只怕我真的是命中注定了。”
“Seventh Sister, perhaps it really is my destiny.”

“喔，”
“Oh?

七姑奶奶问道：“胡家托乌先生来作媒了，
The Hu family has asked Mr Wu to act as matchmaker.

他怎么说？”
What did he say?”

“他说的话也不晓得是真是假？
“I don't know whether to believe him.

说胡大先生的意思，要我去替他当家。”
He said Mr Hu wanted me to run his household.”

“不错，
“That's true.

这话应春也听见的。”
Yingchun heard him say it too.”

“这么说，看起来是真的！”
“Then it seems he really means it!”

罗四姐心里更加踏实，但心头的疑虑亦更浓重，
Luo felt more assured, yet her misgivings grew stronger.

“七姐，你说。 我凭啥资格去替他当家？”
“Tell me, Seventh Sister, what standing would I have to run his household?”

七姑奶奶心想，胡雪岩顾虑者在此，罗四姐要争者亦在此，
This, the Seventh Lady reflected, was precisely what troubled Hu Xueyan and what Luo meant to fight for.

足见都是厉害角色，不开口则已，一开口必中要害。
They were both formidable people: once either of them spoke, the words went straight to the heart of the matter.

不过，她虽然已从古应春口中摸透了“行情”，却不愿轻易松口，
Although Gu had already told her exactly how things stood, she was unwilling to commit herself too readily.

因为不知道罗四姐还会开什么条件，不能不谨慎行事。
She did not know what further conditions Luo might put forward, so caution was necessary.

于是她试探地问道：“四姐，你自己倒说呢？
“What do you think yourself?” she asked tentatively.

要啥资格，才好去替他当家。”
“What standing would you need to manage the household?”

“当家人的身分，
“It is the housekeeper's position that matters.

身分不高，下人看不起，
If your rank is low, the servants look down on you.

你说的话他左耳进、右耳出，
Everything you say goes in one ear and out the other.

七姐，你说，这个家我怎么当？”
Tell me, how could I run a household like that?”

“是的。这话很实在。
“Yes, that's a very practical consideration.

我想，我们小爷叔，不会不懂这个道理，
I don't suppose Little Uncle fails to understand it.

他总有让下人敬重你的办法。”
He will find some way to make the servants respect you.”


“啥办法？”罗四姐紧接着问，
“What way?” Luo asked at once.

“七姐夫怎么说？”
“What did Brother-in-Law Gu say?”

“他说，胡老太太托我来做媒。
“He said Old Madam Hu had asked me to act as matchmaker.

不过，我还不敢答应。”
But I haven't dared agree yet.”

罗四姐又惊又喜，
Luo was both startled and delighted.

“原来是胡老太太出面？”她问：
“So Old Madam Hu herself is behind this?

“胡太太呢？”
What about Mrs Hu?”

“他们家一切都是老太太作主。
“The old lady decides everything in their household.

胡太太最贤惠不过，
Mrs Hu is as virtuous as can be.

老太太说啥就是啥，百依百顺的。”
Whatever the old lady says, she accepts; she obeys her in everything.”

听得这一说，罗四姐心头宽松了些，
Luo felt easier on hearing this.

不过七姑奶奶何以不敢答应做媒？
But why had the Seventh Lady not dared accept the commission?

这话她却不好意思问。
She was too embarrassed to ask.

“我为啥不敢答应呢？”七姑奶奶自问自答他说：
“Why haven't I dared agree?” the Seventh Lady went on, answering her own question.

“因为我们虽然一见如故，象同胞姐妹一样，到底这是你的终身大事，
“Though we felt like old friends from the moment we met, as close as real sisters, this is still your marriage, a matter for your whole life.

你没有跟我详详细细谈过，我不晓得你心里的想法，
You haven't talked it over with me in detail, so I don't know exactly what you want.

如果冒冒失失答应下来，万一做不成这个媒，反而伤了我们感情。”
If I rashly agreed and then failed to arrange the match, it might end by hurting our friendship.”

“七姐，这一层你尽管放心。
“Seventh Sister, you can put that fear aside.

不管怎么样，你我的感情是不会伤的。”
Whatever happens, it will never hurt the friendship between us.”

“有你这句话，我的胆就大了。
“That gives me courage.

四姐，除了名分以外，还有啥？
Sister Luo, apart from your formal status, what else is there?

请你一样一样告诉我。
Tell me everything, one thing at a time.

看哪一样是我可以代为答应下来的，哪一样我能替你争的，哪一样是怎么样也办不到的。”
Then we can see what I can agree to on their behalf, what I can try to obtain for you, and what cannot possibly be done.”


“怎么佯办不到的事，我也不会说。”
“I wouldn't ask for anything impossible.”

罗四姐想了一下说：
Luo thought for a moment.

“七姐，我顶为难的是我老娘。”
“My mother is my greatest difficulty.”

她老娘何以会成为难题？
Why should her mother present a difficulty?

七姑奶奶想一想才明白，必是指的当亲戚来往这件事。
After a little thought, the Seventh Lady understood: Luo must mean whether the two families would acknowledge each other as relatives by marriage.

以她的看法，这件事是否为难，主要的是要看罗四姐自己的态度？
In her view, whether this became a problem depended chiefly on Luo's own attitude.

倘或她坚持要胡老太太叫一声“亲家太太”，这就为难了！
If she insisted that Old Madam Hu address her mother as a fellow mother-in-law, that would indeed be difficult.

否则胡家也容易处置。
Otherwise, the Hu family could easily manage it.

谈到这里，话就要明说了，
At this point, they had to speak plainly.

“四姐，你的意思我懂了。”她说：
“I understand what you mean.

“还有啥，
What else is there?

你一股脑儿说出来，我们一样一样来商量。”
Tell me everything, and we'll discuss the points one by one.”

“还有，你晓得的，我有个女儿。”
“Then there's my daughter, as you know.”

“你的女儿当然姓她老子的姓。”七姑奶奶说：
“Your daughter naturally bears her father's surname,” said the Seventh Lady.

“你总不见得肯带到胡家去吧？”
“Surely you wouldn't want to take her into the Hu household?”

“当然，
“Of course not.

那算啥一出？”
What sort of arrangement would that be?”

“既然不带到胡家，那就是你自己的事，
“Since you won't be bringing her into their household, she is your own concern.

不管你怎么安排，胡家都不便过问的。
Whatever arrangements you make, the Hu family would have no business interfering.

这件事可以不必谈，
We needn't discuss that.

还有啥？”
What else?”


“还有，我只能给老太太一个人磕头。”
“One more thing: I can kowtow to the old lady, but to no one else.”

“是不是！”七姑奶奶马上接口，
“There!” the Seventh Lady replied immediately.

“我不敢答应，
“That is just why I was afraid to agree.

就是怕你有这样的话，叫我说都不便去说的。”
I feared you might say something like this, a condition I couldn't even bring myself to put to them.”

罗四姐自己也觉得要求过分了一些，
Luo herself felt she had asked a little too much.

不过话既已出口，亦不便自己收回，因而保持沉默。
Having said it, however, she could hardly withdraw it of her own accord, so she remained silent.

当然，在七姑奶奶看，这就是不再坚持的表示，能商量得通的。
To the Seventh Lady, this naturally meant that she was no longer insisting and could be persuaded.

“四姐，我现在把人家的意思告诉你：
“Now let me tell you what the family proposes.

第一是称呼，下人部叫你太太，
First, the form of address: all the servants will call you Madam.

第二进门磕一个头，以后都是平礼，
Second, you will make the required kowtow when you first enter the household; after that, all greetings will be exchanged as between equals.

第三生了儿子着红裙。
Third, once you have borne a son, you may wear a red skirt.

这三洋，是老太大交代下来的。”
These three instructions come from the old lady herself.”

罗四姐考虑了一会，
Luo considered them for a while.

觉得就此三事而言，再争也争不出什么名堂来，
She felt that, on these three points, no amount of argument would gain her anything further.

不如放漂亮些，换取对方在他处的让步。
Better to concede gracefully and obtain concessions elsewhere.

于是她说：“七姐这么说，我听七姐的。
“If that is what you say, Seventh Sister, I'll accept your advice.

不过，我进他家的门，不晓得是怎么个进法？”
But how exactly am I to enter their household?”


七姑奶奶心想，这是明知故问。
The Seventh Lady thought that Luo knew the answer perfectly well.

妾侍进门，无非一乘一轿抬进门，在红烛高烧之下，一一磕头定称呼。
A concubine was carried into the house in an ordinary sedan chair and, before the blazing red candles, made the kowtows that established her rank and the proper forms of address.

罗四姐问到这话，意思是不是想要坐花轿进门呢？
Was Luo asking because she wanted to arrive in a decorated bridal chair?

当然，照一般的办法，是太委屈了她，但亦决无坐花轿之理。
Certainly the ordinary procedure would be too humiliating for her, but there could be no question of the bridal chair either.

七姑奶奶觉得这才真的遇见难题了。
Here, the Seventh Lady felt, was a real difficulty.

想了又想，七姑奶奶只能这佯回答：“这件事我来想办法，
After much thought, she could only say, “I'll try to find a way.

总归要让你面子上看得过去。
Whatever we do, it must preserve your dignity.

你明天倒问问乌先生，看他有啥好办法？”
Ask Mr Wu tomorrow and see whether he has a good idea.”

正事谈到这里，实在也可以说是很顺利了。
Their discussion had, in fact, gone very smoothly.

做媒本来就要往返磋商，一步一步将双方意见接近来，
Matchmaking always required negotiation back and forth, bringing the two sides closer step by step.

罗四姐也很明白事缓则圆的道理，
Luo understood that patience gave matters time to come right.

因而很泰然地答说：“事情不急，
“There's no hurry,” she answered calmly.

七姐尽管慢慢想。”
“Take your time thinking it over.”

“你是不急，小爷叔恐怕急着要想做新郎官。”
“You may not be in a hurry, but Little Uncle is probably eager to become a bridegroom.”

七姑奶奶笑着将她的脸扳向亮处，
With a smile, the Seventh Lady turned Luo's face toward the light.

“不晓得你装扮成新娘子，是个啥样子？”
“I wonder what you'll look like dressed as a bride.”

这话说得罗四姐心里不知是何滋味？
Luo could hardly have described the feelings this stirred in her.

说一句：“七姐真会寻开心。”一闪站起身来，
“You do love teasing me!” she said, springing to her feet.

“乌先生不知道吃好了没有？”
“I wonder whether Mr Wu has finished his dinner.”

“我们一起下去看看。”
“Let's go down together and see.”


两人携着手夏回楼下，只见古应春陪着乌先生在赏鉴那些西洋小摆设。
Hand in hand, they went downstairs, where Gu Yingchun was showing Mr Wu his little Western ornaments.

七姑奶奶少不得问些吃饭了没有之类的客气话，然后间到乌先生下榻之处。
The Seventh Lady made the customary inquiries about whether their guest had eaten enough, then asked where he was staying.

“客栈已经定好了。”古应春问道：
“His room at the inn has already been booked,” Gu said.

“不知道罗四姐今天晚上，是不是还有事要跟乌先生谈？”
“Does Sister Luo have anything else she wants to discuss with Mr Wu tonight?”

“今天太晚了。”
“It's too late tonight.

罗四姐答说：“有事明天也可以谈。”
Anything we need to discuss can wait until tomorrow.”

“那么，我送乌先生回客栈。
“Then I'll take Mr Wu back to his inn.

明天一早我会派人到客栈陪了乌先主到罗四姐那里。
First thing tomorrow, I'll send someone there to accompany him to your house.

下午我陪乌先生到各处逛逛。”
In the afternoon, I'll show him around.”

等古应春送客回来，七姑奶奶还没有睡，等着要将与罗四姐谈论的情形告诉他，
When Gu returned from seeing their guest home, the Seventh Lady was still awake, waiting to tell him about her conversation with Luo.

最后谈到罗四姐如何“进胡家的门”。
She ended with the question of how Luo was to enter the Hu household.

“一顶小轿抬进门，东也磕头，西也磕头，且不说罗四姐委屈，我们做媒人的也没有面子。”
“To be carried in in a little chair and made to kowtow here, there, and everywhere—quite apart from the humiliation to Sister Luo, it would do us matchmakers no credit.”

“为小爷叔，没有面子也就算了。”古应春说：
“For Little Uncle's sake, we can put up with losing a little face,” Gu said.

“你不要把你的想法也摆进去，
“Don't add your own feelings to the problem.

那一来事情就越发摆不平了。”
That will only make it harder to settle.”

“好！
“Very well!

那么罗四姐，总要让他的面子过得去。”
But at least Sister Luo must be allowed to keep her dignity.”

“这有点难办。
“That's rather difficult.

又有里子，又要面子，世界上恐怕没有那么便宜的事。”
To get both the substance and all the outward honours—I'm afraid there are few bargains as good as that in this world.”

七姑奶奶也觉得丈夫的话不错，不过已经答应罗四姐要让她“面子上过得去。”所以仍在苦苦思索。
The Seventh Lady knew her husband had a point, but she had promised Luo a dignified arrangement and went on searching for a solution.

“睡吧！
“Go to sleep.

我累了。”
I'm tired,” Gu said.

古应春旅途劳顿，一上床，鼾声即起，
Exhausted by his journey, he began to snore as soon as he was in bed.

七姑奶奶却无法合眼，
She could not close her eyes.

最后终于想到了一个办法，而且自己觉得很得意，
At last she hit upon an idea of which she was particularly proud.

很想唤醒古应春来谈，却又不忍，只好闷在心里。
She wanted to wake him and discuss it, but could not bear to disturb him, so she kept it to herself.


第二天一早，古应春正在漱洗时，七姑奶奶醒了，掀开珠罗纱的帐子，探头说道：
Early the next morning, while Gu was washing, the Seventh Lady awoke, drew aside the gauze bed curtains, and poked out her head.

“不要紧了！
“It's all right now!

我有法子了。”
I've found a way.”

没头没脑一句话，说得古应春愣在那里，
Gu stared, bewildered by this announcement without preamble.

好一会才省悟，
After a moment he understood.

“你是说罗四姐？”他问。
“You mean Sister Luo?”

“对。”
“Yes.”

七姑奶奶起床，倦眼惺松，但脸上别有一种兴奋的神情，
She got out of bed, her eyes still heavy with sleep but her face alight with excitement.

“他们的喜事在上海办，照两头大的办法，
“They can hold the wedding in Shanghai, with two wives of equal standing in separate households.

一样可以坐花轿、着红裙。”
Then she can have both the decorated bridal chair and the red skirt.

她问：“你看呢？”
What do you think?”

“小爷叔在杭州有大太大的，无人不知，
“Everyone knows Little Uncle already has a principal wife in Hangzhou.

人家问起来怎么说？”
What will they say when people ask?”

“兼桃！”七姑奶奶脱口回答：
“He can be heir to two family branches!” she answered at once.

“哪个去查他们的家谱？”
“Who's going to investigate their genealogy?”

“这话倒也是。
“There's something in that.

不知道小爷叔肯不肯？”
I wonder whether Little Uncle would agree.”

“肯不肯是他自己的事，
“That's for him to decide.

我们做媒人的，是有交代了。”七姑奶奶又说：
We matchmakers will at least have offered a solution.

“我想他也不会不肯的。”
Besides, I don't think he'll refuse.”

古应春考虑了一会，同意了她的办法，只问：“回到杭州呢？”
Gu considered the idea and agreed to it, asking only, “What happens when she goes back to Hangzhou?”

“照回门的办法，先到祖宗堂磕头，再见老太太磕头。”
“Treat it like a bride's return visit to her parents: first she kowtows in the ancestral hall, then she meets the old lady and kowtows to her.”

“这不是哈回门的办法，
“That isn't a return visit to her parents.

是‘庙见’，
It's the formal presentation at the ancestral shrine.

这就抬举罗四姐的身分了。”
That would raise Sister Luo's standing.”

古应春深深点头：
Gu nodded emphatically.

“可以！”
“It could work.”

“你说可以就定规了。
“If you think so, it's settled.

下半天，你问问乌先生，看他怎么说。”
Ask Mr Wu this afternoon and see what he says.”

“能这样，乌先生还有什么话说？
“If it can be arranged that way, what objection could he have?

至到你说‘走规’，这话是错了，
But you're wrong to say it's settled.

要小爷叔答应了才能定规。”
It can only be settled when Little Uncle agrees.”

“你这么说，那就快写信去问。”
“Then write quickly and ask him.”

古应春觉得不必如此匆促。
Gu thought there was no need for such haste.

不过，这一点他觉得也不必跟爱妻去争，反正是不是写了信，她也不会知道，
He also thought there was no need to argue with his beloved wife over it: she would not know whether he had written or not.

所以答应着说：“我会写。”
“I'll write,” he said.


乌先生上午去看了罗四姐，
Mr Wu visited Luo that morning.

下午由古应春陪着他，坐了马车去观光，一圈兜下来，乌先生自己提出要求，想到古家来吃晚饭，为的是谈罗四姐的亲事。
In the afternoon, Gu took him sightseeing by carriage, and when they had completed their tour, Mr Wu himself asked to dine at the Gu house so that they could discuss the marriage.

“我跟她谈过了，
“I've talked to her.

她说她的意思，七姑奶奶都晓得。
She says the Seventh Lady knows what she wants.

不过，既然我是媒人，她说有些话，要我跟七姑奶奶来商量。
Still, since I'm acting as matchmaker, she has asked me to discuss a few points with you.”

““是的。
“Yes, Mr Wu.

乌先生你说。”
Please go ahead.”
