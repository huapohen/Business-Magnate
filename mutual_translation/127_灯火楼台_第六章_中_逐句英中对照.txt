胡雪岩
《灯火楼台》第六章（中）
Towers of Light — Chapter Six, Part Two
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


The Seventh Lady had been thinking the same thing.
七姑奶奶亦正有此意。

Finding a pause, she interrupted the men's flow of talk with a few of the gracious remarks expected of a hostess, and the two women left the table.
找个空隙，打断他们的谈锋，说了两句做女主人应有的门面话，与罗四姐双双席席。

She took Luo Sijie upstairs to her bedroom, which Luo had always admired.
七姑奶奶将她带到楼上卧室。这间卧室一直为罗四姐所欣赏，

Gu Yingchun had redesigned it in the Western style, with a spacious balcony and large glass doors hung with two layers of curtains, one gauze and the other velvet.
因为经过古应春设计，改成西式，有个很宽敞的阳台，装置很大的玻璃门，门上加两层帷幕、一层薄纱、一层丝绒，

When the velvet curtains were drawn back by day, sunlight passed through the gauze and filled the room, making it bright, splendid, and wonderfully refreshing.
白天拉开丝绒那一层，阳光透过薄纱，铺满整个房间，明亮华丽，令人精神一爽。

At night, the sight of countless lights from the balcony had a charm of its own.
晚上坐在阳台上看万家灯火，亦别有一番情趣，

In summer, especially, nothing could be more comfortable than sitting there in the cool air and talking.
尤其是象这种夏天，在阳台上纳凉闲谈，是最舒服不过的一件事。

“Will you have Chinese tea or foreign tea?”
“你是喝中国茶，还是喝洋茶？”

Foreign tea meant English tea with milk.
所谓“喝洋茶”是英国式的奶茶。

The Seventh Lady had a complete silver tea service and followed the English custom of preparing the tea herself and pouring it for her guests, a rather elaborate business.
七姑奶奶有全套的银茶具，照英国规矩亲自调制，而且亲自为客人倒茶，颇为费事。

Luo, intent on discussing serious matters, had no mind to appreciate it.
罗四姐此刻要谈正事，无心欣赏“洋茶”，便即说道：

“I'd like a cup of chrysanthemum tea.”
“我想吃杯菊花茶。”

The yellow and white Hangzhou chrysanthemums could be brewed like tea to calm the mind and dispel internal heat.
黄白“杭菊花”可以当茶叶泡来喝，有清心降火之功，

“Is your mind rather unsettled?” the Seventh Lady asked with a smile.
七姑奶奶笑着问道：“你大概心里很乱？”

“I don't know why, but I've been feeling restless all this time.”
“也不晓得啥道理，心里一直烦躁。”

“Let's sit out on the balcony.”
“我们到阳台上来坐。”

She chose the balcony out of consideration for Luo.
七姑奶奶挑到阳台上去密谈，是替罗四姐设想，

Discussing her own marriage was bound to make her shy; in the dim light she could conceal her embarrassment and speak more freely.
因为谈到自己的终身大事，她难免腼腆，阳台上光线幽暗，可以隐藏妞促的表情，就比较能畅所欲言了。


When the maid had brought their chrysanthemum tea, Luo, sitting with her face turned away from the light, gave a soft sigh.
等小大姐泡了菊花茶来，背光坐着的罗四姐幽幽地叹口气说：

“Seventh Sister, perhaps it really is my destiny.”
“七姐只怕我真的是命中注定了。”

“Oh?
“喔，”

The Hu family has asked Mr Wu to act as matchmaker.
七姑奶奶问道：“胡家托乌先生来作媒了，

What did he say?”
他怎么说？”

“I don't know whether to believe him.
“他说的话也不晓得是真是假？

He said Mr Hu wanted me to run his household.”
说胡大先生的意思，要我去替他当家。”

“That's true.
“不错，

Yingchun heard him say it too.”
这话应春也听见的。”

“Then it seems he really means it!”
“这么说，看起来是真的！”

Luo felt more assured, yet her misgivings grew stronger.

“Tell me, Seventh Sister, what standing would I have to run his household?”
“七姐，你说。 我凭啥资格去替他当家？”

This, the Seventh Lady reflected, was precisely what troubled Hu Xueyan and what Luo meant to fight for.
七姑奶奶心想，胡雪岩顾虑者在此，罗四姐要争者亦在此，

Although Gu had already told her exactly how things stood, she was unwilling to commit herself too readily.

She did not know what further conditions Luo might put forward, so caution was necessary.

“What do you think yourself?” she asked tentatively.

“What standing would you need to manage the household?”
要啥资格，才好去替他当家。”

“It is the housekeeper's position that matters.
“当家人的身分，

你说的话他左耳进、右耳出，

七姐，你说，这个家我怎么当？”

“Yes, that's a very practical consideration.

我想，我们小爷叔，不会不懂这个道理，

他总有让下人敬重你的办法。”

“啥办法？”罗四姐紧接着问，

“What did Brother-in-Law Gu say?”
“七姐夫怎么说？”

“He said Old Madam Hu had asked me to act as matchmaker.
“他说，胡老太太托我来做媒。

But I haven't dared agree yet.”
不过，我还不敢答应。”

Luo was both startled and delighted.

“So Old Madam Hu herself is behind this?

What about Mrs Hu?”
“胡太太呢？”

“The old lady decides everything in their household.
“他们家一切都是老太太作主。

Mrs Hu is as virtuous as can be.
胡太太最贤惠不过，

Whatever the old lady says, she accepts; she obeys her in everything.”
老太太说啥就是啥，百依百顺的。”

Luo felt easier on hearing this.
听得这一说，罗四姐心头宽松了些，

But why had the Seventh Lady not dared accept the commission?
不过七姑奶奶何以不敢答应做媒？

She was too embarrassed to ask.
这话她却不好意思问。

“我为啥不敢答应呢？”七姑奶奶自问自答他说：

“Seventh Sister, you can put that fear aside.
“七姐，这一层你尽管放心。

不管怎么样，你我的感情是不会伤的。”

“That gives me courage.
“有你这句话，我的胆就大了。

Sister Luo, apart from your formal status, what else is there?

看哪一样是我可以代为答应下来的，哪一样我能替你争的，哪一样是怎么样也办不到的。”


“I wouldn't ask for anything impossible.”
“怎么佯办不到的事，我也不会说。”

Luo thought for a moment.
罗四姐想了一下说：

你一股脑儿说出来，我们一样一样来商量。”

“还有，你晓得的，我有个女儿。”

“你的女儿当然姓她老子的姓。”七姑奶奶说：

“Of course not.
“当然，

What sort of arrangement would that be?”
那算啥一出？”

“Since you won't be bringing her into their household, she is your own concern.

这件事可以不必谈，

What else?”
还有啥？”


“One more thing: I can kowtow to the old lady, but to no one else.”

“There!” the Seventh Lady replied immediately.
“是不是！”七姑奶奶马上接口，

“That is just why I was afraid to agree.
“我不敢答应，

I feared you might say something like this, a condition I couldn't even bring myself to put to them.”
就是怕你有这样的话，叫我说都不便去说的。”

Luo herself felt she had asked a little too much.
罗四姐自己也觉得要求过分了一些，

Having said it, however, she could hardly withdraw it of her own accord, so she remained silent.
不过话既已出口，亦不便自己收回，因而保持沉默。

当然，在七姑奶奶看，这就是不再坚持的表示，能商量得通的。

“Now let me tell you what the family proposes.

第三生了儿子着红裙。

这三洋，是老太大交代下来的。”

Luo considered them for a while.
罗四姐考虑了一会，

She felt that, on these three points, no amount of argument would gain her anything further.
觉得就此三事而言，再争也争不出什么名堂来，

Better to concede gracefully and obtain concessions elsewhere.
不如放漂亮些，换取对方在他处的让步。

“If that is what you say, Seventh Sister, I'll accept your advice.
于是她说：“七姐这么说，我听七姐的。

But how exactly am I to enter their household?”
不过，我进他家的门，不晓得是怎么个进法？”


The Seventh Lady thought that Luo knew the answer perfectly well.
七姑奶奶心想，这是明知故问。

A concubine was carried into the house in an ordinary sedan chair and, before the blazing red candles, made the kowtows that established her rank and the proper forms of address.
妾侍进门，无非一乘一轿抬进门，在红烛高烧之下，一一磕头定称呼。

Was Luo asking because she wanted to arrive in a decorated bridal chair?
罗四姐问到这话，意思是不是想要坐花轿进门呢？

当然，照一般的办法，是太委屈了她，但亦决无坐花轿之理。

七姑奶奶觉得这才真的遇见难题了。

你明天倒问问乌先生，看他有啥好办法？”

Their discussion had, in fact, gone very smoothly.

“Take your time thinking it over.”
七姐尽管慢慢想。”

“你是不急，小爷叔恐怕急着要想做新郎官。”

这话说得罗四姐心里不知是何滋味？

说一句：“七姐真会寻开心。”一闪站起身来，

“It's too late tonight.

“Then I'll take Mr Wu back to his inn.
