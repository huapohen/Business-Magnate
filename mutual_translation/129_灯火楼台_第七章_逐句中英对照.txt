第七章 帮夫运｜CHAPTER SEVEN: A WIFE WHO BRINGS HER HUSBAND PROSPERITY


自从罗四姐嫁到到胡家，真是走了一步帮夫运，
Once Luo Sijie married into the Hu household, she truly entered upon a fortune that advanced her husband.

胡雪岩的事业如《红楼梦》上所形容的“鲜花着锦”般兴旺。
Hu Xueyan's enterprises flourished like “fresh flowers upon brocade,” as Dream of the Red Chamber puts it.

当然，兴旺的由来是他恃左宗棠为靠山，左宗棠视他为股肽，
Their prosperity, of course, rested on Zuo Zongtang's support, while Zuo regarded Hu as his indispensable right hand.

只要左宗棠西征，节节胜利，所请在朝廷无有不准，胡雪岩水涨船高，亦就事事顺手了。
As the western campaign advanced from victory to victory and the court granted every request Zuo made, Hu rose with the tide and found everything going smoothly.

原来从道光年问开始，君暗臣愚，激出内忧外患，
Since the Daoguang reign, benighted rulers and foolish ministers had provoked troubles at home and abroad.

西北的回民起义，亦是贪官污吏激荡而成。
The Muslim uprisings in the northwest had likewise been stirred up by corrupt officials.

其时所谓“甘回”共有西、南北三大支，三大首领，
The forces then known as the Gansu Muslims fell into three main divisions, western, southern and northern, with three principal leaders.

西面的叫马朵之，驻扎在青海的西宁，
In the west was Ma Duozhi, based at Xining in Qinghai.

南面的叫马占鳌，以甘肃与青海的河州，也就是临夏为根据地，
In the south was Ma Zhan'ao, whose base was Hezhou, also called Linxia, in the Gansu–Qinghai region.

北面叫马化龙，是三大首领中最厉害的一个，
The northern leader, Ma Hualong, was the most formidable of the three.

势力范围在宁夏、灵武一带，根据地名为金积堡，
His influence extended over Ningxia and Lingwu, and his stronghold was Jinjibao.

这个地方就是“黄河百害，惟富一套”的河套的起点，
It stood at the beginning of the Hetao region, the great bend of the Yellow River celebrated in the saying, “The Yellow River brings a hundred troubles; only its bend grows rich.”

擅茶、马之利以外，东面有个盐池叫花马池，更是一大财源。
Besides controlling the profits from tea and horses, he had a major source of wealth in Huamachi, a salt lake to the east.

金积堡周围有五百多个寨子，众星拱月般环卫着马化龙的金积堡。
More than five hundred fortified villages encircled Jinjibao like stars surrounding the moon.

此人对宁夏将军穆图善很恭敬。
Ma was extremely respectful towards Mutushan, the general at Ningxia.

左宗棠看到了此人的底蕴，所以西征的第一目标就是攻下金积堡。
Zuo saw what lay beneath this behaviour, and the first objective of his western campaign was to capture Jinjibao.


在攻金积堡之前，先要隔断捻军与甘回的联系。
Before attacking Jinjibao, however, he had to sever the link between the Nian armies and the Gansu Muslim forces.

捻军分为两大股，称为“东捻”、“西捻”。
The Nian were divided into two main groups, the Eastern and Western Nian.

曾国藩解释捻军之捻说：“捻纸燃脂，故谓之捻”，
Explaining their name, Zeng Guofan wrote, “Paper is twisted to burn in oil; hence the name nian.”

凡是用薄纸搓成条状，如吸水烟用的纸煤等等，都叫做捻子。
Anything made by twisting thin paper into a strip, such as the spills used for lighting water pipes, was called a nianzi.

捻军的特性在于易聚易散，
The Nian armies could gather and disperse with equal ease.

但看起来象乌合之众，而流转不定，飘忽千里，令人疲于奔命，亦很厉害。
Though they looked like an undisciplined rabble, their unpredictable movements over a thousand li left pursuers exhausted and made them formidable opponents.

僧格林沁的黑龙江马队，追奔逐北，
Sengge Rinchen's Heilongjiang cavalry chased them relentlessly.

捻军见了就澈，
Whenever they saw it coming, they withdrew.

但一停下来，周围不知如何，就会冒出无数捻军来，
Yet as soon as the cavalry halted, innumerable Nian soldiers would somehow appear all around it.

僧王就是这样阵亡的。
That was how Prince Sengge Rinchen had met his death.

僧王打的是东捻，
He had been fighting the Eastern Nian.

西捻的首领叫张宗禹，自河南至陕西，由河南横渡黄河，直上延安、米脂，
Zhang Zongyu, leader of the Western Nian, moved between Henan and Shaanxi, crossing the Yellow River from Henan and pressing north towards Yan'an and Mizhi.

南北战线拉长到一千多里，
His north–south front stretched over a thousand li.

目的就是希望与马化友由西往东，也有千把里的这条战线交会。
He hoped to join Ma Hualong's front, which extended a similar distance from west to east.

只要一接上头，西捻不复可制，回民起义亦不知何时才能镇压下去，
Once the two linked up, the Western Nian would be beyond control, and there would be no telling when the Muslim uprising could be suppressed.

所以左宗棠西征的初步战略，就在隔离西捻与甘回不让他们“会师”。
Zuo's initial strategy was therefore to keep them apart and prevent a junction.

罗四姐嫁到胡家时，正当西捻初平，
When Luo married into Hu's household, the Western Nian had just been defeated.

两宫太后召见左宗棠，天语褒嘉，左宗棠自陈五年可以镇压回民起义之时。
The two empress dowagers received Zuo with gracious praise, and he declared that he could suppress the Muslim uprising within five years.


左宗棠最初驻军西安，然后往西北逐步推进，
Zuo first stationed his army at Xi'an, then advanced gradually northwest.

大营先移乾州，再移甘肃境内的泾川，
Headquarters moved first to Qianzhou, then to Jingchuan in Gansu.

然后往北打，攻克镇原、庆阳，收容降众及饥民十七万人，行屯垦之法，
Turning north, he captured Zhenyuan and Qingyang, took in 170,000 surrendered people and famine refugees, and established agricultural settlements for them.

种子、农具，都由胡雪岩的转运局采办好了，运到甘肃。
The seeds and farming implements were all procured by Hu's Transport Bureau and shipped to Gansu.

及至左宗棠的前锋逼进灵武，马化龙看根据地有被剿之虞，于是“上书言抚”，
When Zuo's advance troops approached Lingwu, Ma Hualong saw that his base was in danger of destruction and submitted a letter asking for pacification.

抚是安抚，表示愿意投降，部众或者收编为清军、或者遗散、或者为他们谋个生计。
That meant he was willing to surrender: his followers might be absorbed into the Qing army, dispersed, or provided with some means of livelihood.

有此化干戈为玉帛的结果，本来是最理想的办法，
Turning weapons into peaceful relations would have been the ideal outcome.

但造反起义的，就抚而又反复音，不知多少。
But countless rebels had accepted pacification only to rise again.

左宗棠阅历极丰，而马化友又有善于翻覆的名声，
Zuo was deeply experienced, and Ma had a reputation for repeated reversals.

他可以玩弄穆图善，而左宗棠决不会受他的愚，
He might play tricks on Mutushan, but Zuo would not be fooled.

所以置之不理，备妥三月行粮，进攻金积堡。
Ignoring the appeal, he prepared three months' provisions and advanced on Jinjibao.


指挥此役的大将是刘松山。此人是曾国藩的小同乡，
The general commanding the attack was Liu Songshan, a man from Zeng Guofan's own immediate home district.

行伍出身，积功升至总兵。
He had risen from the ranks to become a brigade commander through distinguished service.

咸丰十年，英法内犯，僧格林沁提兵勤王，
In the tenth year of Xianfeng, when British and French forces invaded, Sengge Rinchen had taken his troops to defend the emperor.

东南没有这一支膘悍的马队，战局大受影响。
The removal of that fierce cavalry force from the southeast greatly affected the war there.

那时太平军李秀成，刚开始为洪秀全所重用，在芜湖召集军事会议，分道进兵，
Li Xiucheng, just beginning to enjoy Hong Xiuquan's confidence, convened a military conference at Wuhu and divided the Taiping forces for an advance on several routes.

李秀成本人自率大军，由芜湖南下，攻占皖南黟县，
He himself marched south from Wuhu and captured Yi County in southern Anhui.

另外太平军悍将李世贤、黄文金、李继远等，相继陷宁国、下徽州，又占江西浮梁、都昌、饶州。
Other formidable Taiping commanders, Li Shixian, Huang Wenjin and Li Jiyuan, successively took Ningguo and Huizhou, followed by Fuliang, Duchang and Raozhou in Jiangxi.

驻节祁门的曾国藩，西面来自湖北的接济，因江西粮道中断而绝，
Zeng, based at Qimen, was cut off from his Hubei supplies to the west because the route through Jiangxi had been broken.

东面则有二李亲领的骄兵相逼，
From the east, confident troops led personally by the two Lis pressed upon him.

重重围困，听从幕宾建议，反攻徽州，以期打开通浙江的运道。
Encircled, he accepted his advisers' proposal to counterattack Huizhou and reopen a supply route to Zhejiang.

于是曾国藩侈军祁门以东、徽州以西的休宁；
He therefore moved his army to Xiuning, east of Qimen and west of Huizhou.

有一天太平军夜袭，诸营皆贵，
One night the Taipings attacked, and every camp broke except Liu's.

只有刘松山在月下列队迎敌，太平军不敢相逼，
Liu formed his men in the moonlight to meet the enemy, who dared not approach.

其余溃散各营，月夜看不真切，以为太平军拦截，掉头要逃，
Troops fleeing from the other camps, unable to see clearly, mistook his ranks for a Taiping blocking force and turned to run.

及至刘松山打出真号，大家才知道大营未夫，“老帅”无恙，
Only when Liu displayed the proper recognition signals did they realize that headquarters still held and the old commander was safe.

惊魂始定。
Their terror subsided.

祁门一役，是曾国藩靖港兵败，投水遇救以后，另一次的大危机，
Since his defeat at Jinggang, when he had thrown himself into the water and been rescued, this had been Zeng's next great crisis.

他连遗书都写好了，
He had even written his last letters.

结果转危为安，都由刘松山之功，从此以国士相待。
His escape was entirely due to Liu, whom he thereafter treated as a man of exceptional national worth.


及至左宗棠受命西征，这是一场大战役，非地方性的军务可比，各军理当协力，
When Zuo received command of the western expedition, its scale demanded cooperation from all the armies; this was no merely local operation.

曾国藩将他最重视的刘松山一军，交给左宗棠指挥。
Zeng placed his most valued force, Liu Songshan's, under Zuo's command.

左宗棠本由曾国藩所提携，以后由于争饷而存意见，
Zuo had originally owed his advancement to Zeng, but they had later quarrelled over military funds.

复以曾国荃破金陵，纵容洪秀全之子逃遁，直言讦奏，
When Zeng Guoquan captured Jinling and allowed Hong Xiuquan's son to escape, Zuo openly denounced the matter in a memorial.

因而失和，不通音问已久，
Their relations broke down, and they had long ceased corresponding.

到这时，左宗棠才知道，谋国之忠，知人之明，自愧不如元辅“。
Only now did Zuo acknowledge, “In loyalty to the country's interests and discernment of men, I am ashamed to fall short of the Grand Secretary.”

将刘松山一军交他节制，比作曾国藩”嫁女“，
He compared Zeng's placing Liu under his orders to giving a daughter in marriage.

对刘讼山的重用，自不待言。
Needless to say, he entrusted Liu with important duties.

刘松山真亦不负曾国藩的知遇及左宗棠的期许，打西捻、甘回，几乎战无不胜、攻无不克。
Liu fully justified both Zeng's recognition and Zuo's expectations, winning almost every engagement against the Western Nian and the Gansu Muslim forces.

他从军以前，在家乡就已定下亲事，聘而未娶，
Before enlisting, he had become engaged at home but had not married.

在军中十几年，只因招兵，回过一次家乡，
In more than ten years of service, he returned home only once, to recruit men.

直到西捻既败，方在洛阳成婚，
Not until the Western Nian were defeated did he finally marry at Luoyang.

新郎新娘都三十多岁了。
Bride and groom were both over thirty.

蜜月只得十天，刘松山便即入陕，
After a honeymoon of only ten days, he entered Shaanxi.

肃清榆、延、绥、鄜四州以后，进军灵武，一战而克。
Clearing the four districts of Yu, Yan, Sui and Fu, he advanced on Lingwu and took it in a single battle.

马化龙力战无功，一面再次言抚，一面四处求援，
Unable to defeat him, Ma Hualong again sought pacification while appealing everywhere for help.

但西宁、河州、临洮、靖远各地的回民军，震于刘松山的威名，都不敢妄运，
But the Muslim armies at Xining, Hezhou, Lintao and Jingyuan, overawed by Liu's reputation, dared not move rashly.

于是刘松山大举进攻。
Liu launched a major assault.

同治九年正月，攻金积堡外围一个寨子，中炮坠马，因而阵亡，
In the first lunar month of the ninth year of Tongzhi, he was struck by cannon fire while attacking a fortified village outside Jinjibao, fell from his horse and died.

所部由他的侄子刘锦棠率领，
His nephew Liu Jintang took command.

同年十一月，终于攻克了金积堡。
In the eleventh month of that same year, Jinjibao finally fell.


西征军能够胜多败少，着着进展，是因为器械利、士气旺、纪律好。
The western army won more often than it lost and advanced steadily because its weapons were superior, its morale high and its discipline good.

胡雪岩得古应春之力，西洋凡有新式枪械，以及其他精巧的军事装备，只要能用得上的，不必向左宗棠请示，先就办了来，
With Gu Yingchun's help, Hu bought any new Western firearms or ingenious military equipment that could be useful, without first asking Zuo for instructions.

加以补给适时，从无粮饷不断之虞，
Supplies arrived on time; the troops never feared an interruption in provisions or pay.

士气自然就旺盛了。
Naturally, morale was high.

这是西征军将士都佩服，也感激胡雪岩的，
Officers and men admired Hu for this and were grateful.

但纪律好亦应归功于胡雪岩，就只有左宗棠最明白了。
But only Zuo fully understood that their discipline too owed much to him.

从成丰未年，同治皇后阿鲁待氏的祖父赛尚武丧师失律，浪掷了一笔发自部库的二百万两银子的军饷以后，仗都是地方上自己在打，
Since the closing years of Xianfeng, when Saishang’a, grandfather of the Tongzhi emperor's empress of the Aludai clan, had lost his army and squandered two million taels of military funds from the ministry treasury, the provinces had been fighting their own wars.

因此有楚军、湘军、淮军、浙军、粤军等等名号，都称之为“官军”，
Hence the various Chu, Hunan, Huai, Zhejiang and Guangdong armies, all called government troops.

这些官军，来源不一，
They came from many sources.

“回乡招募”的子弟兵固占多数，但也不个是土匪或者别处投过来的，
Men recruited in their commanders' home districts formed the majority, but there were also bandits and men transferred from elsewhere.

出身不同，队官的作风各异，军纪就大有区别。
Their backgrounds differed, as did their officers' methods, and discipline varied greatly.

湘军中以彭玉鳞部下纪律最严，鲍超一军最糟糕，这就是带兵的看法不同之故，
Within the Hunan Army, Peng Yulin's men were the best disciplined and Bao Chao's the worst, reflecting their commanders' differing views.

不过鲍超骁勇善战，是曾国藩的“爱将”，所以诸事宽容。
But Bao was brave and skilful, a favourite of Zeng's, and was indulged in many things.

左宗棠所部，亦是杂牌军队，但都能格守纪律，
Zuo's forces were equally mixed, yet they all observed discipline.

一半是左宗棠治军较严，一半亦由于心悦诚服，不忍违犯纪律，
Half the credit belonged to his strict command; the other half to their wholehearted devotion, which made them unwilling to disobey.

论心悦诚服之所起，就不能不推服胡雪岩了，
For that devotion, Hu deserved acknowledgement.

“湖湘子弟满天下”而无后顾之忧，都由于胡雪岩靠他广设钱庄、通汇便利，按时得能接济官兵家属。
Hunan men served all over the empire without anxiety for those at home because his extensive network of banks and convenient remittances supported their families regularly.

至于阵亡将士，恤死养生，不用左宗棠关照，他就派人去做了，
When officers or soldiers died, he sent men to provide death benefits and support the survivors without waiting for Zuo's instructions.

大家都道“侯爷”如此爱护部下，何忍犯他的军纪？
Everyone thought: the marquis cares so much for us; how could we bear to violate his discipline?

却不知是胡雪岩在助“候爷”维持纪律。
They did not know that Hu was helping the marquis maintain it.


胡雪岩能够公私兼顾，
Hu managed to attend to public duties and private business alike.

钱庄。典当、丝号一家接一家开张，生意越做越大，“财神”的名气越来越响，
Banks, pawnshops and silk firms opened one after another, his enterprises expanded, and his reputation as the God of Wealth grew ever louder.

从胡老太太起始，都认为是“螺蛳太太”的功劳，
Everyone, beginning with Old Madam Hu, attributed it to Madam Luosi.

原来为了避免用“二太太”之名，却又想不出更合适的称呼，
That title had arisen because they wished to avoid calling her Second Madam but could think of nothing more suitable.

有个通人说：“顺治年间‘江左三大家’之一的龚芝麓，娶了秦淮出身的顾眉生，
A learned man had offered a precedent: “In the Shunzhi reign, Gong Zhilu, one of the Three Great Masters of the Lower Yangtze, married Gu Meisheng, who came from the Qinhuai courtesan quarter.

龚芝麓的元配称她为‘顾太太’，
Gong's principal wife addressed her as Madam Gu.

仿照这个例子，拿‘罗四姐，的’姐，字改为‘太太’，有何不可？
Why not follow that example and replace the ‘Sister’ in ‘Luo Sijie’ with ‘Madam’?”

于是，‘罗四太太，就此叫开了。
Thus she became Madam Luo Si.

下人不明其理，只当她娘家住在螺蛳门外的缘故，叫成’螺蛳太太“。
Servants who did not understand the reasoning assumed the title came from her family home outside Luosi, or Snail, Gate, and it became Madam Luosi.

但最为乡党称道，而且使得胡雪岩自觉对螺蛳太太有愧，既爱且敬的是，她有个“大贤大德”的名声，为胡雪岩娶了十一房姨太太。
What most impressed their neighbours, however, and made Hu feel indebted to her, loving and respecting her all the more, was her reputation for extraordinary virtue: she arranged for him to take eleven more concubines.


约莫嫁后一年，螺蛳太太向到杭州三天竺来烧香的七姑奶奶诉苦。
About a year after her marriage, Madam Luosi confided her troubles to the Seventh Lady, who had come to Hangzhou to offer incense at the Three Tianzhu Temples.

原来胡雪岩精力过人，
Hu had exceptional sexual energy.

只她一个人“当夕”，有些力不从心，因而也就觉得乐不敌苦了。
Being his sole nightly companion had become more than she could manage, and the pleasure no longer outweighed the strain.

于是胡雪岩不免留连花丛，
Hu consequently began lingering among the courtesans.

本来欢场中应酬，在胡雪岩几乎是每天的例课，以前仅止于“吃花酒”，
Entertaining at their houses had long been an almost daily duty for him, but previously he had confined himself to dining and drinking there.

渐渐地以勾栏为行馆，经常整夜不归，其至在“堂子”里接见宾客，料理公事，
Gradually he began treating the brothels as residences, frequently staying all night and even receiving visitors and transacting official business there.

这件事就可忧了。
That was cause for concern.

“七姐，”螺蛳太太说：“他现在正在风头上，
“Seventh Sister, he is riding high now.

这步桃花运走不得，
He cannot afford this run of romantic adventures.

第一，伤身体，
First, it harms his health.

第二，耽误正事，
Second, it delays important business.

第三，名声不好听，
Third, it damages his reputation.

还有第四，伙计们看东家的样，个个狂嫖滥赌，怎么得了？
And fourth, what if every employee follows his example, whoring and gambling without restraint?

就算不学他的样，也会灰心，
Even if they don't imitate him, they will be discouraged.

辛辛苦苦帮他创业，哪知道他是这样子不成材！”
They work so hard to help him build his enterprises, only to discover that he is such a disappointment.”

七姑奶奶知道最后两句话，是她“夫子自道”的牢骚，
The Seventh Lady knew that these last words expressed her friend's own resentment.

不过，她也有些怀疑，
Still, she was puzzled.

“小爷叔对这个‘色，字看不破，是大家都晓得的。
“Everyone knows that Little Uncle has never overcome his weakness for women.

不过，”她问；“又何至于’好‘到这个程度呢？”
But why should it have reached such a degree?”

“喏，”螺蛳太太不免有怨言，
Madam Luosi could not suppress a complaint.

“都是我们那位刘三叔！”
“It is all the fault of our Third Uncle Liu!”


原来胡雪岩决定开办药店，

螺蛳太太也怕逼出人命案子来，约束下人，不准胡来。
Madam Luosi also feared that coercion might lead to a death and restrained the servants from any rash act.

以至于一直到巨宅落成，元室街也重新翻修过，那家剃头店始终存在。
Even after the great mansion was completed and Yuanbao Street repaved, the barbershop remained.


落成之日，大宴宾客，共分三日，
The completion was celebrated with three days of banquets.

第一天是“三大宪”，杭州府、仁和、钱塘两县，以及候补道，邀约在籍的绅士作陪，
On the first day, the guests were the three principal provincial authorities, the prefect of Hangzhou, the magistrates of Renhe and Qiantang, and expectant circuit intendants, with local gentry invited to keep them company.

入席之前，主人亲自引导游园，
Before the meal, Hu personally led them through the garden.

曲曲折折，转过假山，只见东南方树木掩映之中，矗起一座高楼，华丽非凡，
Following its winding paths around a rockery, they saw an extraordinarily splendid tower rising among the trees to the southeast.

令人不解的是，四周雕栏，金光闪耀，
What puzzled them was the gold light flashing from the carved railings all around it.

远远望去，谁也猜不透是何缘故。
At that distance, nobody could account for it.

“雪翁，”巡抚杨昌浚问道：“那是个什么所在？”
“Mr Hu,” Governor Yang Changjun asked, “what is that building?”

“是内人所住的一座楼。”
“The residence of my wife.”

听说是内眷住处，杨昌浚不便再间，私下打听，
Hearing that it belonged to the women's quarters, Yang could not inquire further, but private questions brought the explanation.

才知道那座楼名为“百狮楼”。
It was called the Hundred Lions Tower.

栏杆柱子上，用紫檀打磨出一百个狮子，
One hundred lions carved and polished from zitan stood on the railing posts.

突出的狮目，是用黄金铸就，所以映日耀眼，令人不可逼视。
Their protruding eyes were cast in gold and glittered in the sunlight so brightly that one could scarcely look at them.

“太太们住的地方，怎么叫百狮楼，
“Why call the ladies' residence Hundred Lions Tower?

莫非‘河东狮吼’这句话，他都不懂。”
Does he not know the saying about the lioness of Hedong roaring?”

“不是。
“No, that isn't it.

因为那位太太称为螺蛳太太，所以胡大先生造了这座楼给她住。”杨昌浚再问“螺蛳太太”之名如何而起，是何出身。
The lady is called Madam Luosi, and he built the tower for her.” Yang asked how she had acquired the name and what her origins were.

打听清楚了觉得未免过分，便悄悄写了一封信给在肃州的左宗棠，颇有微词。
Once informed, he felt that Hu had gone rather too far and quietly wrote to Zuo at Suzhou in Gansu, expressing some criticism.


哪知左宗棠对他的看法，颇不以为然，只是不便明言，
Zuo disagreed considerably with Yang but could not conveniently say so outright.

恰巧他的长子来信，亦批评了胡雪岩，正好借题发挥，
As it happened, his eldest son also wrote to criticize Hu, giving him a suitable occasion to express his view.

说一个人的享用，求其相称，
A man's enjoyments, he said, should be proportionate to his deserts.

胡雪岩的功劳，世人不尽了解，他很清楚，
The world did not fully understand Hu's services, but he did.

西征军事之能有今日，全亏得有胡雪岩，享用稍过，自可无愧。
The western campaign owed its present success entirely to Hu; a little excess in his pleasures need give him no shame.

他又提到他的儿女亲家，也是平生第一知己的陶澍，
He cited Tao Shu, his closest lifelong friend and the father of his child's spouse.

在两江总督任上时，他的女婿胡林翼，以翰林在江宁闲住，每天选歌征色，花的都是老丈人的“养廉银”，
When Tao was Governor-General of Liangjiang, his son-in-law Hu Linyi, a Hanlin academician living idly at Jiangning, spent his days seeking singers and beautiful women, all at the expense of his father-in-law's integrity allowance.

内帐房有一次向陶演表示，胡林翼挥霍无度，是否应该稍加节制？
Once Tao's household accountant asked whether Hu Linyi's extravagance ought to be restrained.

陶澎告诉他说：“尽管让他花！
“Let him spend,” Tao replied.

他将来要为国家出力，
“He will serve the country one day.

有钱亦没有工夫去花。”
Then, even if he has money, he will have no time to spend it.”

胡雪岩跟胡林翼的情形虽有不同，但个人的享用，比起为国家所谋的大利来，即令豪奢亦不足道。
Hu Xueyan's circumstances differed, but compared with the great benefits he secured for the country, even extravagant personal pleasures counted for little.

这些活辗转传到浙江，
These remarks eventually reached Zhejiang.

胡雪岩感激在心，对左宗棠自然越发尽忠竭力，
Deeply grateful, Hu served Zuo with still greater loyalty and effort.

但螺狮太太却心生警惕，
Madam Luosi, however, grew uneasy.

与七姑奶奶私下谈起来，都认为“树大招风”，应该要收敛了。
In private talks with the Seventh Lady, both agreed that a tall tree catches the wind: it was time to become less conspicuous.

可是胡雪岩只问一句：“怎么收法？”螺蛳太太却又无词以对。
Yet Hu needed only to ask, “How are we to cut back?” and she had no answer.

因为胡雪岩所凭借的是信用，信用是建立在大家对他的信心上面，而信心是由胡雪岩的场面造成的。
For Hu depended on credit; credit rested on the confidence others placed in him; and that confidence was created by the grandeur of his establishment.

场面只能大，不能小，
The display could only grow, never shrink.

否则只要有人无意间说一句：“胡大先生如今也不比从前了。”立刻就会惹起无数猜测，
Otherwise, a single casual remark—“Mr Hu is no longer what he used to be”—would instantly set off endless speculation.

原来有仇恨的、无怨无仇只是由于妒嫉的，都将推波助澜，大放谣言，
Those who bore him grudges, and those who had no grievance but were merely envious, would fan the rumours.

那一来信用就要动摇，后果不堪设想。
His credit would be shaken, with consequences too terrible to contemplate.
