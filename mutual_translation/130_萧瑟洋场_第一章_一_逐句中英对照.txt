胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement

原著：高阳


第一章
CHAPTER ONE


因为如此，螺蛳太太的心境虽然跟胡雪岩一样，不同往年，还是强打精神，扮出笑脸，热热闹闹地过了一个年。
For this reason, although Madam Luosi, like Hu Xueyan, felt quite unlike her usual self at the turn of the year, she forced herself to be cheerful, put on a smiling face, and saw the household through a lively New Year.

接着便又要为胡老太太的生日，大忙特忙了。
Then came another rush of preparations, this time for Old Madam Hu’s birthday.

生日在三月初八，『浩治桃觞，恭请光临』的请帖，却在年前就发出去了。
The birthday fell on the eighth day of the third month, but invitations announcing that a birthday feast would be laid and respectfully requesting the guests’ presence had gone out before the New Year.

到得二月中旬，京中及各省送礼的专差，络绎来到杭州，
By the middle of the second month, special messengers bringing gifts from the capital and the provinces were arriving in Hangzhou in a steady stream.

胡府上派有专人接待；
Members of the Hu household had been assigned specifically to receive them.

送的礼都是物轻意重，
The gifts were modest in material value but rich in sentiment.

因为胡雪岩既有『财神』之号，送任何贵重之物，都等于『白搭』，
Since Hu Xueyan already bore the title of God of Wealth, anything merely expensive would be so much wasted effort.

惟有具官衔的联幛寿序，才是可使寿堂生色的。
What could truly lend distinction to the birthday halls were congratulatory couplets, embroidered banners, and birthday tributes bearing their donors’ official titles.


寿堂共设七处，
Seven birthday halls had been prepared.

最主要的一处，不在元宝街，而是在灵隐的云林寺。
The most important was not in Yuanbao Street but at Yunlin Temple at Lingyin.

铺设这处寿堂时，胡雪岩带着请客，亲自主持，
Hu Xueyan brought his resident literary companions there and personally supervised its decoration.

正中上方高悬一方红地金书的匾额，『淑德彰闻』，
High in the center hung a plaque with gold lettering on a red ground: “Her Virtuous Conduct Is Widely Known.”

上铭一方御玺∶『慈禧皇太后之宝』，款书∶『赐正一品封典布政使衔东西候补道胡光墉之母朱氏』。
Above the inscription appeared the imperial seal, “Treasure of the Empress Dowager Cixi”; below it was the dedication, “Bestowed upon Lady Zhu, mother of Hu Guangyong, expectant circuit intendant [the province is corrupt in the source], bearing the honorary rank of provincial treasurer and the honors of the senior first rank.”

匾额之下，应该挂谁送的联幛，却费斟酌了。
Whose couplets and banners should hang beneath it required some thought.

原来京中除了王公亲贵，定制向不与品官士庶应酬往来以外，
In the capital, princes and members of the imperial family were ordinarily prohibited by established custom from exchanging such courtesies with officials or private citizens.

自大学士、军机大臣以下，六部九卿，都送了寿礼，
Apart from them, however, everyone from the grand secretaries and grand councilors down through the heads of the Six Ministries and the Nine Courts had sent birthday gifts.

李鸿章与左宗棠一样，也是一联一幛，
Li Hongzhang, like Zuo Zongtang, had sent a pair of couplets and an embroidered banner.

论官位，武英殿大学士李鸿章，久居首辅，百僚之长，应该居中。
In terms of official standing, Li, Grand Secretary of the Wuying Hall, had long been the senior grand secretary and the foremost of all civil officials; his gifts belonged in the center.

但胡雪岩却执意要推尊左宗棠，
Hu Xueyan, however, insisted on giving Zuo pride of place.

便有爱人以德的一个名叫张爱晖的清客，提出规劝。
One of his literary companions, Zhang Aihui, believing that true concern for a friend required guiding him toward proper conduct, ventured to remonstrate.


『大先生，朝廷名器至重，
“Mr Hu, the ranks and honors conferred by the throne are matters of the utmost importance.

李合服是首辅；
Lord Li of Hefei is the senior grand secretary.

左湘阴是东阁大学士，入阁的资格很浅，
Lord Zuo of Xiangyin is Grand Secretary of the Eastern Pavilion and entered the Grand Secretariat only recently.

不能不委屈。
He must accept the lesser position.

这样的大场面，次序弄错了。要受批评；
If the order of precedence is wrong at an occasion as grand as this, people will criticize it.

如果再有好事的言官吹毛疵，说大先生以私情乱纲纪，搞出啥不痛快的事来，也太无谓了。』
And should some meddlesome censor choose to pick faults and accuse you of letting private attachments overturn the established order, whatever unpleasantness followed would be quite needless.”

『你的话不错。
“You are right.

不过「花花轿儿人抬人」，
But a splendid sedan chair needs people to lift it: we all depend on others to help us up.

湘阴这样看得起我，
Xiangyin thinks so highly of me.

遇到这种场面，我不捧他一捧，拿他贬成第二，我自己都觉得良心上说不过去。』
If I fail to uphold him on an occasion like this and put him down in second place, my own conscience will reproach me.”

『话不是这么说。大先生，
“That is not the way to look at it, Mr Hu.

你按规矩办事，湘阴一定也原谅的。』
If you follow the rules, Xiangyin will certainly understand.”


『就算他原谅，我自己没法子原谅，
“Even if he understands, I cannot forgive myself.

张先生，你倒想个理由出来，怎么能拿湘阴居中。』
Mr Zhang, do try to think of a reason that would let us put Xiangyin in the center.”

『没有理由。』张爱晖又说∶『大先生，你也犯不着无缘无故得罪李合肥。』
“There is no such reason,” Zhang Aihui replied. “Besides, Mr Hu, you have no need to offend Lord Li of Hefei for nothing.”

胡雪岩不作声，
Hu Xueyan said nothing.

局面看着要僵了；那常来走动的乌先生忽然说道∶『有办法，
The discussion was on the point of reaching an impasse when Mr Wu, a frequent visitor, suddenly spoke. “There is a way.

只要把下款改一改好了。』
We need only alter the signature at the bottom.”

『怎么改法？』胡雪岩很高兴问。
“How?” Hu asked eagerly.

『加上爵位就可以了。』
“Add his hereditary title.”


原来左宗棠送的寿幛，上款是『胡老伯母六秩晋九荣庆』，
The banner Zuo Zongtang had sent was addressed, “To my honored Aunt Hu, in celebration of her sixty-ninth birthday.”

下款是『秃头』的『左宗棠拜祝』，
At the bottom stood the bare signature, “Zuo Zongtang respectfully offers his congratulations.”

平辈论交，本来是极有面子的事；
For Zuo to treat Hu as a friend of his own generation was in itself a considerable distinction.

乌先生主张加上左宗棠的爵位，变成『恪靖候左宗棠拜祝』；
Mr Wu proposed adding Zuo’s hereditary title, making the signature read, “Zuo Zongtang, Marquis Kejing, respectfully offers his congratulations.”

这一来就可居李之上了，
This would place him above Li.

因为李鸿章的下款上加全衔『武英殿大学士北洋大臣直隶总督部堂肃毅伯』，伯爵次侯爵一等，只好屈居左宗棠之次。
Even if Li’s signature were furnished with all his titles—Grand Secretary of the Wuying Hall, Minister of Beiyang, Governor-General of Zhili, and Earl Suyi—an earl still ranked one grade below a marquis and would have to yield precedence.

那乌先生是个庙祝，
Mr Wu was only a temple keeper.

只为他是螺蛳太太的『娘家人』；胡雪岩爱屋及乌，将他侧于清客之列。
Hu Xueyan had included him among his literary companions because he belonged to Madam Luosi’s own circle: affection for her extended to those connected with her.

一直不大被看得起，
He had never been held in much regard.

此时出此高明的一着，大家不由得刮目相看了。
This masterly suggestion made everyone see him in a new light.


『不过大先生，我倒还要放肆，胡出一个主意。
“Still, Mr Hu, let me presume to offer one more suggestion.

如果左湘阴居中，李合肥的联幛只好挂在东面板壁，
If Lord Zuo of Xiangyin hangs in the center, Lord Li of Hefei’s couplets and banner will have to go on the paneled wall to the east.

未免贬之过甚；
That seems rather too great a slight.

是不是中国挂一幅瑶池祝寿图，拿左、李的联幛分悬上下首，比较合适？』
Would it not be better to hang a picture of the immortals offering birthday greetings at the Jade Pool in the center, with Zuo’s and Li’s gifts on the more and less honored sides?”

胡雪岩看乌先生善持大体，便请他专管灵隐这个最主要的寿堂，
Seeing how carefully Mr Wu considered the whole situation, Hu put him in sole charge of the principal birthday hall at Lingyin.

而且关照他的一个外甥张安明，遇事常找乌先生来商量；
He also instructed his nephew Zhang Anming to consult Mr Wu whenever anything arose.

张安明是胡府做寿揽全局的大总管。
Zhang was the chief steward responsible for the entire birthday celebration.


张安明自然奉命唯谨，
Zhang Anming naturally obeyed to the letter.

当天就请乌先生小酌，诚意请教，
That very day he invited Mr Wu to drink with him and sincerely sought his advice.

『有件事，不晓得乌先生有啥好主意？他说，』
“There is something I should like your opinion on,” he said.

寿堂虽有七处，贺客太多，身分不同，挤在一起，乱得一塌糊涂，
“We have seven birthday halls, but there will be so many guests, of such different standing, that if they all crowd in together it will be chaos.

一定要改良。
We must find a better arrangement.”

『』寿堂是七处，
“There are seven halls.

做寿是不是也做七天？
Will the celebrations last seven days as well?”

『『不错。
“Yes.

大先生说，宫里的规矩「前三后四」，要七天。』
Mr Hu says the palace custom is ‘three days before and four days after,’ making seven days.”

张安明轻声答说∶『不过，这话对外面不便明说；
Zhang lowered his voice. “But we cannot say that openly.

只说老太太生日要「打七」，所以开贺也是七天。』
We shall simply say that the old lady’s birthday calls for a seven-day Buddhist observance, so guests will be received for seven days.”

『打七』便是设一坛水陆道场，是佛门中最隆重的法事，称为『水陆斋仪』，亦名『水陆道场』，俗称『打水陆』。
This observance was a shortened form of the Water and Land rite, the most solemn of Buddhist services, formally called the Water and Land Fasting Ritual and popularly known as “performing the Water and Land service.”

斋仪又有繁简之分，讽经礼忏七七四十九日称为『打水陆』；
The full ritual, with scripture recitation and rites of repentance, lasted seven times seven days, or forty-nine days.

为了祝厘延寿，通常只须七日，叫做『打七』。
For prayers to secure blessings and prolong life, seven days were generally sufficient; hence the name “the seven-day observance.”


『有七处寿堂，又分七天受贺，大可分门别类，拿贺客错开来，
“With seven halls and seven days for receiving congratulations, you can easily divide the guests into categories and keep them apart.

接待容易，而且酒席也不致于糟蹋。』
They will be easier to entertain, and the banquets will not go to waste.”

『这个主意好。
“An excellent idea.

我们来分它一分。』
Let us work out the divisions.”

于是细细商量，决定第一天请官场，
After careful discussion, they decided to invite the official world on the first day.

三品以上文武大员；五品以下文武职官，占了四个寿堂，
Civil and military dignitaries of the third rank and above, together with civil and military officials of the fifth rank and below, would occupy four halls.

此外是现奉差委的佐杂官，与文武候补人员各一；
Minor officials currently holding special assignments would take another, and expectant civil and military officials another.

留下一处专供临时由外地赶到的官员祝寿之用。
The seventh would be kept for officials arriving unexpectedly from out of town.

第二天请商场，丝、茶、盐、典、钱、药、绸各行各业的伙友，分开七处。
The second day would be for the business world, with associates from the silk, tea, salt, pawnbroking, banking, medicine, and silk-fabric trades divided among the seven halls.

第三天是各衙门的司事，以及吏户礼兵刑工六科的书办；
The third would be for functionaries from the various government offices and the clerks of the six departments—Personnel, Revenue, Rites, War, Punishments, and Works.

第四天是出家人的日子；
The fourth would be for members of religious orders.

第五天、第六天请亲戚朋友，一天『官客』，一天『堂客』，一天『堂客』。
Relatives and friends would be invited on the fifth and sixth days, the men on one day and the women on the other.

第七天是寿辰正日，自然是自己人热闹热闹。
The seventh, the birthday itself, would naturally be a cheerful gathering of the family and their closest associates.


这样安排好了，去请示胡雪岩；他不甚满意，
They took the completed arrangements to Hu Xueyan, who was not entirely satisfied.

『自己人热闹热闹，用不着七处寿堂，
“We do not need seven halls merely for a family gathering.

而且光是自己人，也热闹不起来。』
Besides, with no one but our own people it will hardly be lively enough.

他说，『我看还要斟酌。
We must think again.

而且我的洋朋友很多，
And I have many foreign friends.

他们来了，到哪里去拜寿？』
Where are they to offer their congratulations when they come?”

『这样好了，专留一天给洋人。』乌先生说∶『一到三、四月里，来逛逛西湖的很多，
“Then let us set aside a day especially for foreigners,” Mr Wu suggested. “Many come to see West Lake in the third and fourth months.

大先生索性请个客，这一天的洋人，不论识与不识，只要来拜寿的，一律请吃寿酒。』
You might as well make it an open invitation: any foreigner who comes that day to offer birthday greetings, whether you know him or not, shall be invited to the feast.”

『洋人捏不来筷子。』胡雪岩说∶『要请就要请吃大菜。』
“Foreigners cannot handle chopsticks,” Hu said. “If we invite them, we must serve Western food.”

『这要请古先生来商量了。』
“We shall have to consult Mr Gu about that.”

请了古应春来筹划。
Gu Yingchun was summoned to plan it.

由于洋人语言不尽相同；饮食习惯，亦有差异，
The foreigners did not all speak the same language, and their eating habits differed too.

好在有七处寿堂，
Fortunately, there were seven halls.

决定英、法、德、美、日、俄、比七国，各占一处。
One each was assigned to the British, French, Germans, Americans, Japanese, Russians, and Belgians.


『应春，』胡雪岩说∶『这七处接待，归你总其成。
“Yingchun,” Hu said, “you will oversee the reception arrangements at all seven places.

大菜司务，归你到上海去请。』
Go to Shanghai and engage the cooks for the Western meals yourself.”

『好。』古应春说∶『要把日子定下来，我到上海，请字林西报的朋友登条新闻，
“Very well,” Gu replied. “Once we have fixed the day, I shall ask my friends at the North-China Daily News to print a notice when I go to Shanghai.

到时候洋人自然会来。』
The foreigners will then come of their own accord.”

『妙极！』张安明笑道∶『外婆生日，洋人拜寿∶只怕从古以来的老太太，只有外婆有这份福气。』
“Splendid!” Zhang Anming said, laughing. “Foreigners paying their respects on Grandmother’s birthday! I doubt any old lady in all history has enjoyed such good fortune.”

果然，胡老太太听了也很高兴。
Old Madam Hu was indeed delighted when she heard.

胡家的至亲好友，更拿这件事当作新闻去传说，而且都兴致勃勃地要等看见洋人拜寿。
The family’s close relatives and friends spread the story as a remarkable piece of news, all eagerly awaiting the sight of foreigners offering birthday congratulations.


这年杭州的春天，格外热闹，
Spring in Hangzhou was unusually lively that year.

天气暖和，香客船自然就到得多，这还在其次；
The warm weather naturally brought more boats carrying pilgrims, but that was a lesser cause.

主要的是胡老太太做生日，传说如何如何豪华阔气，招引了好些人来看热闹。
The chief attraction was Old Madam Hu’s birthday: accounts of the celebration’s magnificent extravagance had drawn crowds of curious onlookers.

何况光算外地来拜寿的人，起码也增加了好几千人。
Those coming from other places specifically to offer their congratulations must alone have added several thousand people.

到得开贺的第一天，城里四处，城外三处，张灯结彩，
On the first day for receiving guests, four halls inside the city and three outside it were festooned with lanterns and colored decorations.

『清音堂名』细吹细打的寿堂周围，车马喧阗，
Around the halls, where ceremonial ensembles softly played their wind instruments and percussion, carriages and horses made a great clamor.

加上看热闹的闲人、卖熟食的小贩，挤得寸步难行。
Add idle spectators and vendors of cooked food, and the crowds were so dense that one could scarcely move.

只有灵隐是例外，
Lingyin alone was an exception.

因为三大宪要来拜寿，仁钱两县的差役以外，『抚标』亦派出穿了簇新号褂子的兵丁，
Since the three highest provincial authorities were coming, soldiers from the governor’s own command, wearing brand-new uniform jackets, joined the runners of Renhe and Qiantang counties.

自九里松开始，沿路布哨弹压，留下了极宽的一条路，直通灵隐山门。
From Jiulisong onward they posted guards along the road to maintain order, leaving a broad passage clear all the way to the temple gate.


从山门到寿堂，寿联寿幛，沿路挂满；
From the gate to the birthday hall, congratulatory couplets and embroidered banners hung everywhere.

寿堂上除了胡雪岩领着子侄，等在那里，预备答谢以外，
In the hall, Hu Xueyan and the younger men of his family waited to return the visitors’ courtesies.

另外请了四位绅士『知宾』。
Four gentlemen had also been invited to act as hosts.

一位是告假回籍养亲的内阁学士陈怡恭，专陪浙江巡抚刘秉璋∶一位是做过山西臬司，告老回乡的汤仲思；
One was Chen Yigong, a subchancellor of the Grand Secretariat home on leave to care for his parents, who would attend specifically to Liu Bingzhang, Governor of Zhejiang. Another was Tang Zhongsi, a former provincial judicial commissioner of Shanxi who had retired to his native place.

另外两位都是候补道，三品服饰，华丽非凡，是张安明受命派了裁缝，量身现做奉赠的。
The other two were expectant circuit intendants, magnificently dressed in third-rank robes that Zhang Anming, acting on instructions, had sent a tailor to measure, make, and present to them.

近午时分，刘秉璋鸣锣喝道，到了灵隐，
Near noon, Liu Bingzhang arrived at Lingyin to the beating of gongs and the cries of his advance attendants clearing the way.

藩臬两司，早就到了，在寿堂前面迎接；
The provincial treasurer and judicial commissioner, who had arrived earlier, waited before the birthday hall to receive him.

轿子一停，陈怡恭抢上前去，抱拳说道∶『承宪台光临，主人家心感万分。
As soon as his sedan stopped, Chen Yigong stepped forward with his hands clasped. “Your Excellency’s presence fills our host with gratitude.

请，请！』
Please come in.”

肃客上堂，
He ceremoniously conducted the governor into the hall.

行完了礼，刘秉璋抬头先看他送的一堂寿序，
After performing the proper greetings, Liu looked up first for the birthday tribute he himself had sent.

挂在西壁最前端，与大学士宝均金送的一副寿联，遥遥相对；
It hung at the foremost end of the western wall, facing a pair of couplets sent by Grand Secretary Bao Yun.

这是很尊重表示，
This was a marked sign of respect.

他微微点头，表示满意。
He gave a slight, satisfied nod.


这时率领子侄在一旁答礼的胡雪岩，从红毡条上站起身来，
Hu Xueyan, who had been leading the younger men of his family in returning the greetings from one side, rose from the strip of red felt on which he had knelt.

含笑称谢∶『多谢老公祖步，真不敢当。』
Smiling, he offered his thanks. “We are deeply honored that our venerable grandparent has troubled to come.”

这『老公祖』的称呼，也是乌先生想出来的。
The form of address, “venerable grandparent,” was another of Mr Wu’s inventions.

因为胡雪岩是布政使衔的道员，老母又有正一品的封典，
Hu was a circuit intendant with the honorary rank of provincial treasurer, and his mother had been granted the honors of the senior first rank.

自觉地位并不下于巡抚，要叫一声『大人』，于心不甘；
He did not feel that his own standing was lower than the governor’s, and he was unwilling to address him as “Your Excellency.”

如用平辈的称谓，刘秉璋字仲良，叫他『仲翁』，又嫌太亢。
Yet to address him as an equal, calling him “Mr Zhong” from his courtesy name Zhongliang, would be too presumptuous.

这个小小的难题跟乌先生谈起，他建议索性用『老父母』的称呼；
When Hu mentioned this small difficulty to Mr Wu, the latter suggested simply using the language of parental authority.

地方官是所谓父母官，士绅对县官称『老父母』，藩臬两司及巡抚则称『老公祖』，
Local officials were called the people’s father and mother; members of the gentry addressed a magistrate as their “venerable parent,” and the provincial treasurer, judicial commissioner, or governor as their “venerable grandparent.”

这样以部民自居，一方面是尊重巡抚，一方面不亢不卑反而留了身分。
By speaking as one of the people under the governor’s jurisdiction, Hu could show due respect while preserving his own standing, neither arrogant nor servile.


刘秉璋自然称他『雪翁』，说了些恭维胡老太太好福气的话，
Liu Bingzhang naturally addressed him as “Mr Xue” and offered some complimentary remarks about Old Madam Hu’s good fortune.

由陈怡恭请到寿堂东面的客座中待茶，
Chen Yigong then escorted him to the guest seats east of the hall for tea.

十六个簇新的高脚金果盘，映得刘秉璋的脸都黄了。
Sixteen gleaming new gold fruit stands, raised on high stems, cast such a glow that even Liu’s face seemed yellow.

稍坐一坐，请去入席。
After a short rest, the party was invited to the banquet.

寿筵设在方丈之西的青猊轩；这座敞轩高三太六尺，一共六间，
It was held in Qingni Pavilion, west of the abbot’s quarters, an open hall six bays wide and thirty-six chi high.

南面监时搭出极讲究的戏台，
A finely constructed temporary stage stood to the south.

台前约两丈许，并排设下三席，巡抚居中，东西藩臬；
About two zhang in front of it, three tables had been placed in a row, with the governor in the center and the treasurer and judicial commissioner to east and west.

大方桌前面系着平金绣花桌围，
The large square tables had embroidered coverings worked in flat gold thread hanging down their fronts.

贵客面对戏台上坐，陈怡恭与胡雪岩左右相陪；
The honored guests sat in the upper seats facing the stage, with Chen Yigong and Hu Xueyan attending on either side.

后面另有四席，为有差使的候补道而设。
Four more tables behind were for expectant circuit intendants currently holding appointments.

偌大厅堂，只得七桌，连陪客都不超过三十个人，
There were only seven tables in that enormous hall and fewer than thirty diners, even counting those present as companions.

但捧着衣包的随从跟班，在后面却都站满了。
Behind them, however, stood a whole crowd of attendants carrying their masters’ bundles of spare clothing.


等安席既罢，戏台上正在唱着的『鸿鸾禧』暂时停了下来，
Once everyone was seated, the performance of Hongluan Xi, then in progress, stopped briefly.

小锣打上一个红袍乌纱、玉带围腰，口衔面具的『吏部天官』，
To the tap of a small gong, the Celestial Official of the Ministry of Personnel appeared in a red robe, black official hat, and jade belt, holding his mask in place between his teeth.

一步三摆地，步到台前『跳加官』。
With ceremonious swaying steps, he advanced to the front of the stage to perform the Dance of Promotion.

这是颂祝贵客『指日高升』、『一品当朝』，
This conveyed wishes that the honored guests might “rise in office any day now” and “hold first rank at court.”

照例须由在座官位最高的人放赏；
By custom, the highest-ranking official present had to reward the performers.

不过只要刘秉璋交代一声就行了，主人家早备着大量刚出炉的制钱，盛在竹筐中，
Liu Bingzhang needed only to give the order: the host had already prepared a great quantity of newly minted copper cash in a bamboo hamper.

听得一个『赏』字，便有四名健仆，抬着竹筐，疾步上前，合力举起来向台上一泼，
At the word “Reward!” four sturdy servants lifted the hamper, hurried forward, and together heaved it up and poured its contents onto the stage.

只听『哗喇喇』满台钱响，声势惊人。
Coins clattered across the boards in an astonishing display.


接下来便作戏班子的掌班，戴一顶红缨帽，走到筵前，
Next the troupe’s manager, wearing a hat with a red tassel, came before the tables.

一膝屈地，高举着戏折子说道∶『请大人点戏。』
Dropping to one knee, he raised the repertoire list above his head. “May Your Excellency choose the plays.”

『点戏』颇有学问。
Choosing plays required considerable knowledge.

因为戏名吉祥，戏实不祥，这种名实不副的戏文很多，不会点会闹笑话；
Many had auspicious titles but unhappy plots; an ignorant choice could make one a laughingstock.

或者戏中情节，恰恰犯了主人家或者哪一贵宾的忌讳，
Worse, something in a play might touch a sensitive point in the history of the host or one of the distinguished guests.

点到这样的戏，无异公然揭人隐私，因而成不解之仇者，亦时有所闻。
Choosing such a work could amount to publicly exposing someone’s private affairs, and cases of lasting enmity arising from it were not unknown.

刘秉璋对此道外行，决定藏拙；
Liu Bingzhang knew little about the theater and decided not to reveal his ignorance.

好在另有内行在，
Fortunately, an expert was at hand.

当下吩咐∶『请德大人点。』他指的是坐在东面的藩司德馨，
“Ask Lord De to choose,” he said, indicating Dexin, the provincial treasurer, seated to the east.

他是旗人，出身纨绔，最好戏曲；
A bannerman brought up in privileged circumstances, Dexin loved the theater above all else.

当下略略客气了两句，便当仁不让地点了四出不犯忌讳而又热闹的好戏，
After a few modest words, he readily selected four lively and excellent plays that offended no taboos.

第一出是『战宛城』，
The first was The Battle of Wancheng.

饰邹氏的朱韵秋，外号『羊毛笔』，是德馨紧赏识的花旦，
Zhu Yunqiu, who played Lady Zou, was a lively female-role performer known as “the Goat-Hair Brush,” a particular favorite of Dexin’s.

演到『思春』那一段，真如用『羊毛笔』写赵孟钭郑崦耐鹱钊艘庀正当德馨全神贯注在台上时，有个身穿行装的『戈什哈』悄悄走到他身旁，递上一封信说∶『陈师爷派专人送来的。』
In the scene of awakening romantic longing, the performance was likened to calligraphy traced with such a brush [the comparison is partly illegible in the source]. While Dexin was wholly absorbed in the stage, a personal guard in traveling clothes quietly approached and handed him a letter. “Secretary Chen sent a special messenger with this.”

陈师爷是德馨的亲信，
Secretary Chen was Dexin’s trusted aide.

此时派专人送来函件，当然是极紧要的事；
A letter delivered by special messenger at such a time must concern something urgent.

因而当筵拆阅，
Dexin opened it at the table.

只见他面现诧异之色，
Surprise appeared on his face.

挥一挥手遣走『戈什哈』，双眼便不是专注在『羊毛笔』身上，而是不时朝刘秉璋那边望去。
He waved the guard away, and his eyes no longer remained fixed on “the Goat-Hair Brush”: instead, he kept glancing toward Liu Bingzhang.


他是在注意胡雪岩的动静，
He was watching for a movement from Hu Xueyan.

一看他暂时离席，随即走了过去，将那封信递了过去，轻声说道∶『刚从上海来的消息。』
As soon as Hu left the table for a moment, Dexin went over and handed the governor the letter, saying quietly, “News just in from Shanghai.”

刘秉璋看完信，只是眨眼在思索；
Liu read it and sat blinking in thought.

好一会才将原信递给陈怡恭∶『年兄，你看，
Only after some time did he pass the letter to Chen Yigong. “My friend, look at this.

消息不巧；
The timing is unfortunate.

今天这个日子，似乎不宜张扬。』
On a day like this, it hardly seems proper to make it known.”

『是！』陈怡恭看完信说∶『这一来，政局恐不免有一番小小的变动。』
“Quite,” Chen said after reading it. “This may bring about a small change in the political situation.”

『是的。』
“Yes.”

刘秉璋转脸问德馨说∶『请老兄在这里绷住场面，
Liu turned to Dexin. “Please keep things going here.

我得赶紧进城了。』
I must hurry back to the city.”


德馨也想回衙门，听刘秉璋如此交代，只能答应一声∶『是。』
Dexin, too, wanted to return to his office, but after the governor’s request he could only answer, “Certainly.”

于是刘秉璋回身招一招手，
Liu turned and beckoned to his attendant.

唤来他的跟班吩咐∶『提轿。』
“Have the sedan brought up.”

接着向陈怡恭拱一拱手，正待托他代向主人告辞时，胡雪岩回来了。
He then clasped his hands to Chen Yigong and was just about to ask him to make his excuses to the host when Hu Xueyan returned.

『怎么？』他问∶『老公祖是要更衣？』
“What is it?” Hu asked. “Does our venerable grandparent wish to retire for a moment?”

『不是！』刘秉璋歉意地说∶『雪翁，这么好的戏、好的席，我竟无福消受；
“No,” Liu replied apologetically. “Mr Xue, such fine plays and such an excellent banquet, and I am denied the pleasure of enjoying them!

实在是有急事，马上得回城料理。』
An urgent matter requires me to return to the city at once.”

『呃、呃。』
“Ah, of course.”

胡雪岩不便多问；
Hu could hardly press him.

只跟在刘秉璋后面，送上轿后方始问德馨∶『刘中丞何以如此匆匆？
He followed Liu out and saw him into his sedan. Only then did he ask Dexin, “Why is Governor Liu in such a hurry?

到底是什么急事？』
What can be so urgent?”

『此处不便谈。』
“We cannot discuss it here.”

德馨与胡雪岩的交情极厚，以兄弟相称∶『胡大哥，有个消息，不便在今天宣扬，
Dexin and Hu were close enough to address each other as brothers. “Brother Hu, there is some news it would be improper to spread today.

不过，消息不坏。』
But it is not bad news.”


胡雪岩点点头不作声，回到筵前，
Hu nodded without speaking and returned to the banquet.

直待曲终人散∶才邀德馨他借住的一间禅房中，细问究竟。
He waited until the music had ended and the company dispersed before inviting Dexin to the monks’ cell where he was staying and asking for the details.

『为什么今天不便宣扬呢？』德馨说道∶『李太夫人在武昌去世了。』
“Why would it be improper to spread it today?” Dexin said. “Old Madam Li has died in Wuchang.”

去世的是李瀚章、李鸿章兄弟的老母。
The dead woman was the mother of the brothers Li Hanzhang and Li Hongzhang.

胡老太太做生日，自然不便宣布这样一个不吉利的消息。
Naturally, news of a death could not be announced during Old Madam Hu’s birthday celebration.

但这一来，李氏兄弟丁忧守制，
But the Li brothers would now have to retire from office to observe the prescribed mourning.

左宗棠暂时去了一个政敌，
Zuo Zongtang would temporarily be rid of a political opponent.

对胡雪岩来说，当然是有利的，亦可说是喜事，不过只能喜在心里而已。
For Hu Xueyan this was certainly favorable, even a cause for rejoicing, though he could rejoice only inwardly.

『一下子两个总督出缺，
“Two governorships-general vacant at once!

封疆大吏要扳扳位了。
The leading provincial officials will all have to shift their places.

不晓得哪个接直隶，哪个接湖广？』
I wonder who will succeed in Zhili and who in Huguang?”


这一问，恰恰说中德馨的心事。
The question touched precisely on what occupied Dexin’s mind.

总督出缺，大致总是由巡抚调升；巡抚有缺，藩司便可竞争，
A vacant governorship-general would normally be filled by promoting a governor; a vacant governorship would in turn be open to competition among the provincial treasurers.

刘秉璋与德馨，各有所图，
Liu Bingzhang and Dexin each had his own hopes.

所以都急着要赶进城去打听消息。
Both had therefore been eager to hurry back to the city and inquire into the news.

不过德馨既有巡抚嘱咐，又有胡家交情在，不便就此告辞，
But Dexin had the governor’s instructions to fulfill, as well as his friendship with the Hu family to consider, and could not simply take his leave.

心想何不就跟胡雪岩谈谈心事。
Why not discuss his thoughts with Hu?

『湖广，我看十之八九是涂朗轩接，
“For Huguang, I should say Tu Langxuan is almost certain to get it.

直隶就不知道了。』
Zhili I cannot guess.”

涂朗轩就是湖南巡抚涂宗瀛，
Tu Langxuan was Tu Zongying, Governor of Hunan.

他替曾国藩办过粮，与李瀚章昔为同事，今为僚属，
He had once managed grain supplies for Zeng Guofan and had formerly been Li Hanzhang’s colleague; he was now Li’s subordinate.

由他来接湖广总督，倒是顺理成章的事。
His succession to the governorship-general of Huguang would therefore be perfectly natural.

『那末湖南巡抚呢？』胡雪岩笑着掉了句文∶『阁下甚有意乎？』
“Then what about the governorship of Hunan?” Hu asked, smiling and adopting a literary turn of phrase. “Does Your Honor entertain an interest?”

『只怕人家捷足先登了。』
“I fear someone else may get there first.”


『那也说不定。』
“That is by no means certain.”

胡雪岩想了一下说∶『你先要把主意拿定了，
Hu thought for a moment. “First you must make up your mind.

才好想办法，
Then we can see what can be done.

倘或老大哥根本没有这个意思，也就不必去瞎费心思。』
If you have no intention of seeking it, Brother, there is no point in wasting effort.”

『水往低处流，人往高处爬，
“Water flows downward, and men climb upward.

岂能无意。
How could I not want it?

不过鞭长莫及，
But the lash will not reach that far.

徒唤奈何。』
I can only lament my helplessness.”

『谋事在人，成事在天。』胡雪岩说∶『等我来打个电报给汪惟贤，要他去寻森二爷探探「盘口」。』
“It is for men to make the attempt and Heaven to decide the outcome,” Hu said. “Let me send Wang Weixian a telegram asking him to find Second Master Sen and sound out the situation.”

此事不便假手于人，胡雪岩又拿不起笔，
The matter could not safely be entrusted to an outsider, and Hu was hardly able to wield a writing brush himself.

因而由他口述，让德馨执笔，
He therefore dictated while Dexin wrote.

电报中关照汪惟贤立即去觅宝森，托他向宝均金探探口气，藩司想升巡抚，该送多重的礼。
In the telegram, Wang was instructed to seek out Baosen immediately and ask him to sound out Bao Yun: how substantial a gift would be required for a provincial treasurer who wished to become governor?
