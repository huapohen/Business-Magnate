胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement
第一章（本章第2部分，共4部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


Dexin chose each word carefully and couched the message in guarded language.
德馨字斟句酌，用隐语写完，

After rereading it, he said, “Grand Secretary Bao and his brother are not on good terms.
看了一遍说∶『宝中堂他们兄弟不和，

Second Master Sen may have no influence with him.
森二爷或许说不上话。

Should we ask Manager Wang to sound out Saltpeter Li as well?”
是不是请汪掌柜再探探皮硝李的口气。』

“Yes.
『好！

I agree.”
我赞成。』

Dexin revised the draft.
于是德馨改好了电报稿子；

Hu called in A’xi, his young personal attendant, whose particular duty was to look after the Western leather bag that accompanied his master whenever he left home.
胡雪岩叫进贴身小跟班阿喜来，他专替主人保管一个一离家就要带着的西洋皮包，

Inside it was a telegraphic codebook.
内中有个蜜码电报本，

Hu and Dexin encoded the message themselves.
胡雪岩与德馨亲自动手，将蜜码译好，

By the time they had finished, the setting sun was touching the hills.
夕阳已经衔山了。

“I had not intended to go back to the city,” Hu said, “but now I must. The telegram has to be taken to Shanghai to be sent.
『我本来不打算进城，现在非回支一趟不可了。』胡雪岩说∶『电报要送到上海去发，

I shall dispatch someone reliable and have him wait there for the reply.
我派一个妥当的人去，叫他在上海等回电；

If it is a matter of twenty or thirty thousand taels, I shall advance it for you.
如果是两三万银子，我先替你垫。

More than that would hardly be worthwhile.”
多了就犯不上了。』

“Indeed, indeed.
『是，是。

I leave everything in your hands.
一切拜托，

I cannot thank you enough.”
承情不尽。』


Hu Xueyan and Dexin then set off for the city together.
于是胡雪岩与德馨一起进城，

Their official ranks were the same, but Hu had been granted the privilege of wearing the yellow riding jacket, so his ceremonial retinue was more imposing than that of Dexin, the serving provincial treasurer.
两人品秩相同，但胡雪岩曾赏穿黄马褂，所以仪从较现任藩司的德馨更为煊赫；

Hu’s tall official placards were used only for display, however.
只是他的『高脚牌』只作陈列之用，

When he traveled, there was simply one mounted outrider in front and four mounted attendants behind.
出行只是前面一匹顶马、后面四匹跟马、

His eight-man sedan had three teams of bearers, who took turns at the poles. Hu’s bearers were themselves addressed as “master” at home.
八抬大轿的轿班，一共三班，轮流换肩一胡雪岩的班，在家亦是『老爷』；

When one returned, a maid would call, “The master is back!
一回家就会听见丫头在喊∶『老爷回来了，

Hurry and bring water to wash his feet.”
赶快打水洗脚。』

Yet however desirable the position, carrying Hu’s sedan was no easy task.
不过替胡雪岩抬力求虽是好差使，却很难当，

He demanded both speed and steadiness: the men must keep pace with the leading horse, yet never spill the tea resting on the armboard inside.
因为既要快、又要稳，快到能跟着顶马亦步亦趋；稳到轿中靠手板上的茶水不致泼出来。

Thus, although Hu and Dexin started at the same time, Hu’s sedan took the lead from the first step and soon left Dexin far behind.
因此，两人虽是同时动身，胡雪岩的轿于起步就领先，很快地将德馨在身后抛得老远了。


Approaching Yuanbao Street, Hu could see the lanterns, decorations, and brilliant lights from a distance.
回到元宝街，老远就看到张灯结彩，灯烛辉煌；

The birthday hall itself was fairly quiet.
但寿堂中却颇安静，

Since particular days had been assigned to the guests, only a few of the closest relatives would venture to call unannounced.
因为既已排定贺寿的日期，除了极少数的至亲以外，不会有人贸然登堂。

Hu alighted, exchanged a few words in the hall, and immediately turned to the business of securing an office for Dexin.
胡雪岩下了轿，在寿堂中略作寒暄，随即着手处理德馨谋官之事。

He had just summoned a capable household servant and begun giving his instructions when Madam Luosi quietly appeared, leaning on a little maid’s shoulder.
正唤来得力的家人在交代时，只风见螺蛳太太扶着一个小丫头的肩，悄然而至；

Seeing that he was occupied, she seated herself some distance away in a velvet chair.
看到胡雪岩有来，她远远地在一张丝绒椅上坐了下来。

“Leave first thing tomorrow morning and wait in Shanghai for news.
『你明天一大早就动身，在上海等消息，

As soon as the reply from Beijing arrives, come straight back.
等北京的回电一到，马上赶回来，

The sooner the better.”
愈快愈好。』

The servant assented and withdrew.
等家人答应着走了，

Madam Luosi rose and approached.
螺蛳太太一面起身走近来，

“Why have you come back to the city instead of staying at Lingyin with the old lady?”
一面问道∶『你不在灵隐陪老太太、怎么回城来了？』

“Two governors-general’s posts have fallen vacant, which should mean two vacant governorships as well.
『出了两个总督的缺，连带就会出两个巡抚的缺，

De Xiaofeng wants one of them.
德晓峰想弄一个，

I had to come in to make arrangements for him.”
我只好进城来替他料理。』

As he spoke, Hu noticed something unusual in her expression.
说到这里，胡雪岩发觉螺蛳太太神色有异，

Looking at her closely, he asked, “What is this?
定睛看了一下问道∶『怎的，

Have you been crying?”
你哭过了？』

“Do not talk nonsense!
『不要乱说！

Why should I cry on the old lady’s happy day?”
老太太的好日子，我哭什么？』

She immediately changed the subject. “Were there many guests?”
螺蛳太太紧接着问∶『客人来得多不多？』


“Everyone who ought to have come was there,” Hu said. “There are not many officials of the third rank or above to begin with.
『该来的都来了。』胡雪岩说∶『三品以上的官，本来没有多少，

From tomorrow onward, each day will be busier than the last.
从明天起就要一天比一天忙了。

What worries me most is the day after tomorrow.
我最耽心后后天，

Everyone says they are going to watch the fun.
大家都说要去看热闹，

I wonder whether something ridiculous will happen.”
不晓得会不会有啥笑话闹出来？』

The schedule had been rearranged, and the third day was now reserved for the foreign guests.
原来贺寿的日期，已经重新安排，第三天轮到外宾。

The very phrase “foreigners offering birthday greetings” excited curiosity.
『洋人拜寿』这四个字听起来，就会逗人好奇，

People wondered what the foreigners would actually do: kowtow or bow with clasped hands?
都说不知道洋人拜寿是怎么个样子，是磕头还是作揖？

Would they use chopsticks to eat the birthday noodles?
吃寿面会不会用筷子；

If they could not, what would they use?
不会用用啥？

A fork would never hold them; perhaps they would have to scoop them up with their fingers.
叉子叉不住，只怕要用手抓。

All this talk of waiting to enjoy a joke made Hu uneasy.
诸如此类等着看笑话的议论，不免使胡雪不安，

He feared an embarrassing scene that would cost him face.
怕闹出笑话来失面子。

“Oh,” Madam Luosi said, reminded of something, “there is a present here you should look at.” She called through the window for a servant.
『喔，』螺蛳太太倒被提醒了，『有份礼在这里，你倒看看。』说着，例向窗外喊一声∶『来人！』

Ruixiang, her trusted senior maid, entered. Having already heard her mistress, she said at once, “The gifts from the foreign gentleman have been taken to the master’s study.”
进来的是螺蛳太太的亲信大丫头瑞香∶她已经听到了螺蛳太太的话，所以进门便说∶『洋人送的那份礼，送到老爷书房里去了。』

Hu reflected that he had received countless birthday presents over the past month or so.
胡雪岩心想，这个把月来，所收的寿礼，不知凡几？

If these alone had been sent to his study, there must be something special about them.
独独这份礼送到他书房，可知必有来历，

“Who sent them?” he asked.
便即问说∶『是哪个送的？』


“I am not sure either,” Madam Luosi replied. “They came through the customs office at Gongchen Bridge.
『我也清楚。』螺蛳太太说∶『是拱宸桥海关送来的，

I wanted you to have a look.
我想你来看，

There is a formal visiting card with them; that will tell you.”
有份全帖在那里，你一看就晓得了。』

“Very well.
『好！

I shall go to the study.”
我到书房里去看。』

“Yes, do.
『对！

They are about to begin dinner outside, and I must put in an appearance and see to the guests.
外面要开席了，我也要去照个面，敷衍敷衍。

What about you?
你呢？

Where will you eat?”
在哪里吃？』

“I am too tired to eat much.
『太累，吃不下什么，

Some congee will do.”
吃点粥吧。』

“You must at least have some of the old lady’s birthday noodles.”
『老太太的寿面不能不吃。』

Madam Luosi turned to give an order. “Ruixiang, tell the small kitchen to make a bowl of fine noodles in chicken broth.
螺蛳太太转脸吩咐∶『瑞香，你关照小厨房下碗鸡汤银丝面，

If the broth is too rich, have them skim off all the fat.”
鸡汤太浓，要把浮油撇干净。』

The three went their separate ways.
于是主仆三人各散，

Alone, Hu followed the long passage, which even on ordinary nights was lined with lamps and easy to walk along, to his study in Jingjian Pavilion.
胡雪岩一个人穿过平时就沿路置灯、明亮好走的长长的甬道，来到他的书房镜槛阁。


Jingjian Pavilion was one of the garden’s finest sights.
这镜槛阁是园中一胜，

A still pond lay before it and an artificial hill behind.
前临平池，后倚假山，

The stair rail had an iron core sheathed in porcelain cylinders shaped like bamboo joints, made to order at Jingdezhen in alternating crimson and green.
拾级而上时，那扶手是以铁杆为芯，外套在景德镇定烧的，朱翠相间，形如竹节的瓷筒；

Inside stood an immense mirrored panel that drew into its surface the pond outside, the mandarin ducks on the water, the red bridge across it, and the willows along the banks.
阁中有一面极大的镜砖，将阁外平池、池中鸳鸯、池上红桥、池畔重杨，一齐吸入镜中，

The design followed Jian Garden, Prince Gong’s retreat beside Shichahai outside Xuanwu Gate in Beijing, but surpassed it in delicacy.
这是仿北京玄武门外，什刹海畔恭亲王的别墅鉴园的规模所造，而精巧过之。

On entering, Hu stood before the mirror for a while.
胡雪岩进得阁来，在镜砖面前站了一回，

Distant towers and nearby galleries were hung with birthday lanterns and decorations.
看远处楼阁、近处回廊，都挂着寿庆的灯彩，

Their reflections fell into the pond and then appeared anew in the mirror: mirrors within mirrors, images within images, until reality and illusion could no longer be distinguished.
到影入池，复又重生于有镜、镜中有镜、影中有影，疑真疑幻，全不分明了。

As he gazed, absorbed, he heard a young voice. “Master, the room is open.”
正看得出神时，听得有个娇嫩的声音∶『老爷，房门开了。』


Hu looked up.
胡雪岩抬头看时，

He seemed to have seen the little maid before.
这个小丫头仿佛见过，

“What is your name?”
便问∶『你叫什么名字？』

“Xiaomei.”
『我叫小梅。』

“Oh.
『喔，

Have you just been assigned here?”
你是新派过来的吗？』

“No.
『不！

I have been here a long time.”
我老早就在这里了。』

“A long time?
『老早在这里？

Why do I hardly ever see you?”
为啥不常看到你？』

As he spoke, he stepped into the study and caught sight of a great heap of presents.
胡雪岩一面说，一面踏进书房，触目一大堆礼物；

Forgetting Xiaomei for the moment, he looked first for the visiting card.
便顾不得跟小梅说话，先找全帖来看。


The formal card was signed, “He Lubin, your humble friend, honored by your instruction.”
全帖的具名是『教遇弟赫鹭宾』。

The sender was the Englishman Robert Hart, Inspector-General of Customs.
原来是总税务司英国人赫德。

He had lived in China for more than twenty years, spoke excellent Beijing Chinese, and could read Chinese writing.
此人在华二十多年，说得一口极好的京腔，也识汉文；

An admirer of Chinese culture and a foreign servant of the throne entitled to wear an official hat button, he had given himself the Chinese name He Lubin, fashioned from the sound of his English name.
仰慕中华文化，兼且是朝迁的有顶戴的客卿，所以用他的英文名字的发音，自己起了一个中国名字叫做『赫鹭宾』。

A gift list accompanied the card.
全帖以外还有礼单。

There were four birthday presents: a complete set of fine porcelain tea things, a large silver bowl filled with sweets, a whole bolt of woolen cloth, and a purple sable muff suitable for an elderly lady.
寿礼一共四样，全喜精瓷茶具、一个装糖果的大银碗、整匹的呢料，另外一个老年人用的紫貂袖筒。


“Someone come here!”
『来啊！』

Hu had in mind the two senior maids who looked after Jingjian Pavilion, the sisters Qiaozhu and Qiaozhen.
他心目中使唤的是专管镜槛阁的两个大丫头一巧珠、巧珍两姊妹；

But it was Xiaomei who came.
但来的却是小梅。

“Neither of the two Qiaos happens to be handy,” she said with a play on their names.
『两巧一巧都不巧。』小梅答说，

“They have both gone to Lingyin with the old lady.”
∶都跟老太太到灵隐去了。

Her quick speech made Hu think she was quite capable of doing an errand.
『胡雪岩看她语言伶俐，料想也能办事，

“You will do just as well.
便即说道∶』你也一样。

Find two people to carry these four presents outside, and have them sent straight to Lingyin for the old lady to see.
你去寻两个人来，把这四样东西搬到外面，叫人马上送到灵隐给老太太看，

Say that they are from—” He stopped. To explain that He Lubin was Hart, and that this foreign dignitary also wore a red button on his official hat, would be too complicated; the messenger might not convey it clearly.
说是┅┅『这要说赫鹭宾就是赫德，这位』洋大人『戴的也是红顶子，那就太罗嗦了，怕传话的人说不清楚，所以停了下来。』

“What would you like, Master?”
老爷要啥！『


“I want to write something.”
『我要写字。』

Xiaomei immediately went to the desk, lifted the inkstone’s cover, poured in a small spoonful of clean water, and began carefully rubbing the inkstick.
小梅听说，立刻走到书桌前面，掀开砚盖，注了一小杓清水，细细研墨。

Hu sat down, dipped his brush, and with considerable effort wrote six characters on the visiting card: “That is, Hart, Inspector-General of Customs.”
胡雪岩便坐了下来，提笔蘸墨，很吃力地在全帖上批了六个字∶『即总税司赫德』。

As the ink was still wet, Xiaomei picked up the card and pursed her lips to blow on it.
小梅因为墨渖未干，便拿起全粘，嘟起小嘴朝字上吹气，

She was blowing busily when Ruixiang arrived.
正吹得起劲时，瑞香来了。


At the sight, Ruixiang first stared, then snapped at Xiaomei. “Get out!
见此光景，她先是一楞，接着便呵斥小梅∶『出去！

What right have you to be in here?”
这地方也是你来得的？』

The Hu household had adopted the conventions of great aristocratic families, with several ranks even among the servants.
原来胡家也学了一套豪门世家的规矩，下人亦分几等，

A young maid like Xiaomei, employed on rough household work, was not supposed to appear before the master.
象小梅这种『做粗生活』的小丫头，是走不到主子面前的，

To do so was to overstep her station.
否则便是僭越。

Ruixiang ordinarily presumed on her position as Madam Luosi’s confidante and treated the other maids as beneath her.
这瑞香平日自恃是螺蛳太太的心腹，目中无余丫，

She was unpopular, and Xiaomei was not inclined to submit to her.
人缘不好，小梅不大服她；

Blamed without cause, the girl felt deeply wronged.
此时无辜受责，大感委屈，

Young as she was, she had a sharp tongue and retorted at once.
她人小嘴利，当即反唇相讥，

“Qiaozhu is away, and the master came in.
『巧珠不在，老爷来了，

Was I not to attend to him?
莫非我就不伺候？

I did not go looking for a chance to curry favor.
这又不是我瞎巴结差使，

Why are you shouting at me?
何用你来吼我？』

We are all lowly servants.
她说∶『大家都是低三下四的人，

Who do you think you are impressing, putting on the airs of a wealthy young lady?”
摆你千金小姐的威风，摆给哪个看？』

“What!”
『啊！』

Ruixiang’s face went white with anger.
瑞香脸都气白了，

“How dare you answer back!”
『你在嚼什么嘴？』

She rushed forward to strike her.
说着，奔上去就要打。

Xiaomei stood her ground and spoke quickly. “This is the old lady’s happy day.
『小梅毫不示弱，又快又急地说∶』今天老太太的好日子，

Would you dare hit someone?”
你敢打人？『


Ruixiang was checked.
瑞香被吓阻住了，

Only with an effort did she lower her raised hand.
一只手好不容易放了下来，

Grinding her teeth, she cursed. “If it were not for the old lady’s celebration, I would tear you apart, you filthy little—!
咬牙切齿地骂道∶『不看老太太的好日子，看我不撕烂你的小Ｘ！

Just wait.
你等在那里，

I shall deal with you.”
看我不收拾你。』

Now Xiaomei was frightened.
这下小梅害怕了，

She knew only too well how much power Ruixiang wielded.
瑞香的威风，她自然识得，

In desperation she dropped to her knees before Hu Xueyan.
情急之下，向胡雪岩双膝跑倒，

“Master, you see how it is.
『老爷，你看。』

Please be fair to me.”
她说∶『请老爷做主。』

“There, there,” Hu said soothingly. “It was I who told her to grind the ink.
『好了，好了！』胡雪岩解劝着∶『原是我叫她磨墨的。

If you will not excuse her for her own sake, do it for mine.
不看僧面看佛面，

There is no need to tell your mistress.”
不必告诉你太太。

With the master himself pleading for her, Ruixiang had recovered some face and vented her anger.
『主人出面说情，瑞香总算扳回面子，出了口气，

“Still kneeling there in the hope of a reward?” she shouted.
当下喝道Ｌ∶∶你还跪在这里想讨赏是不是，

“I shall reward you with a good beating!
赏你一顿「毛笋炒腊肉」！

Get out!”
滚！

Seeing tears well in Xiaomei’s eyes, she added, “This is the old lady’s happy day.
看见小梅盈盈欲泪，瑞香便又警告∶』今天是老太太的好日子，

Do not dare start crying!”
你敢哭出来！

Xiaomei did not dare.
『小梅果然不敢哭，

She withdrew with tears brimming in both eyes.
噙着两泡眼泪，退了出去。

Hu felt deeply sorry for her but could hardly comfort her in Ruixiang’s presence.
胡雪岩好生不忍，却不便当着瑞香支抚慰小梅。

Fortunately, a ready means of drawing Ruixiang away lay before him: the birthday gifts had to be sent to Lingyin.
不过，眼前恰有一条现成的调虎离山之计，便是安排那份寿礼，送到灵隐。

When Ruixiang went downstairs to summon help, Hu stepped into the gallery and said softly, “Xiaomei, do not be afraid.
等瑞香下阁子去唤人时，胡雪岩便走到廊上，轻声说道∶』小梅，你不要怕，

Do not be upset.
不要难过，

Tomorrow I shall speak to your mistress and have you promoted.”
明天我跟太太说∶提拔你。

When Hu spoke of “your mistress” to the servants, he usually meant Madam Luosi.
『胡雪岩对下人说太太，多半是指螺蛳太太，』

“I do not want that,” Xiaomei replied. “How could I ever have a peaceful day under Ruixiang?”
我不要。『小梅答说∶』在瑞香手下，哪有好日子过？『


Hu was about to ask her more when Ruixiang unexpectedly returned.
胡雪岩正待再问时，不想瑞香来得好快，

On going downstairs, she had met Second Madam Yang, one of the household’s four chief women stewards, whose special charge was to supervise everyone entering or leaving the garden.
原来她一下阁子，就看到胡家四大管家婆之一，专管稽察花园出入的杨二太，

Carrying a palace lantern herself, she was escorting Gu Yingchun to see the master.
亲自打一盏宫灯，领着古应春来见主人。

Ruixiang exchanged tasks with her, and each turned back: one went to find people to deal with Hart’s presents, while the other brought Gu upstairs.
于是瑞香便跟她换了差使，各自回头，一个去找人来料理赫德的礼，一个便领着古应春入阁。

“Why have you come back?” Hu asked.
『你怎么回来了？』胡雪岩问。

Gu had intended to stay at Lingyin in preparation for receiving the British visitors who would offer birthday greetings the next day.
古应春原是预定留在灵隐，预备第二天接待来拜寿的英国人；

But news that Hart had unexpectedly arrived in Hangzhou had brought him back to find out more.
只为得到赫德忽然到了杭州的消息，特为赶了来探问究竟。


“I only discovered it was Hart when I read his card just now,” Hu said, pointing to the four presents. “Look, I was about to send these to Lingyin for the old lady to inspect.”
『我也是刚刚看了拜帖才晓得是赫德，喏，』胡雪岩指着那四样礼物说∶『正预备送到灵隐，请老太太去过目呢。』

Gu examined the gifts appreciatively and nodded. “By foreign standards, this is a very substantial present.”
于是古应春赏玩了礼物，点点头说∶『照洋人来说，这份礼送得很重了。』

It was plainly a sign of the sender’s high regard, and Hu could not help feeling pleased.
这自然是人家看重的缘故，胡雪岩不免得意，

After a moment’s thought, he said, “I wonder where he is staying.
想了一下说∶『他不晓得住在哪里？

It is too late today.
今天晚了，来不及了，

First thing tomorrow morning, you and I should go and call on him.
明天一大早，我同你先去拜访。

That is only proper, since we are the hosts.”
这也是我们做主人该尽的道理。』

“He is staying with Dr Meitenggeng.”
『他住在梅藤更那里。』

Meitenggeng was an English missionary and physician who had come to Hangzhou to preach and established a hospital at Dafangbo in the center of the city.
梅藤更是个英国教士，也是医生，到杭州传教，在中城大方伯开了一家医院；

There was a bridge there that had been called Guangji Bridge in the Song dynasty, and the hospital took the same name, Guangji, which also conveyed the idea of bringing help to all.
大方伯这个地方有一座桥，在宋朝叫广济桥，因此这家医院题名就用了双关的『广济』二字。

Hu had made a large donation when the hospital was founded and could count the doctor as an old friend.
梅藤更开设广济医院时，胡雪岩捐过一大笔钱，所以他跟梅藤更亦算是老朋友，

“Since he is staying there,” he said, “I shall send someone to ask the doctor to tell Hart that we will call tomorrow morning, and to find out what time would suit him.”
当即说道∶『既然是住在梅藤更那里，我派人去通知一声，请他转告赫德，说我们明天一早去看他，请他问一问赫德什么时候方便。』

“There is no need to send anyone.
『不必叫人去。

Calling on a doctor in the evening cannot be considered improper.
好在晚上去看医生，不算冒昧，

I shall go myself; it will be more satisfactory.”
我自己去一趟，比较稳当。』


“Very well.
『也好！

Thank you for the trouble.
辛苦，辛苦。』

Have you eaten?”
胡雪岩问道∶『你吃了饭没有？』

“I have been so busy I forgot to be hungry.
『忙得肚子饿都忘记了。

In fact, I am not hungry now.”
实在也不饿。』

“Neither am I.
『我也不饿，

I shall wait for you, and we can eat together when you return.”
我等你一来一起吃。』

“Good.”
『好！』

“Ruixiang, show Mr Gu down.” Then Hu suddenly asked, “When did these presents arrive?”
『瑞香，你送古才爷下去。』胡雪岩忽又问道∶『这礼是啥辰光送来的？』

“About three in the afternoon,” Ruixiang replied. “Dr Mei sent someone with them.”
『未末申初。』瑞香答说∶『梅院长派人送来的。』


“At that hour!”
『那个时候！』

Hu frowned. “We ought, properly, to have sent over a banquet.”
胡雪岩蹙着眉说∶『照道理要送席。』

“We did not send a banquet,” Ruixiang put in.
『席是没有送。』瑞香接口，

“But we sent a first-class hotpot, four kinds of pastries, and a basket of juicy flat peaches.
『送了个一品锅、四样点心，还有一篓水蜜蟠桃。

Madam had me prepare a gratuity envelope for the messenger [the amount is corrupt in the source].
太太叫我包了一个八封的赏封，打发来人，

She told him to explain to Dr Mei that our master was at Lingyin and therefore did not know who this foreign dignitary was, but that he must in any case be a good friend of our master’s.
请他告诉梅院长，我们老爷在灵隐，所以不晓得这位洋大人的身分，不过总归是我们老爷的好朋友。

Dr Mei was like one of the family, and we asked him to take the trouble to entertain the guest on our behalf.
梅院长是象自己人一样的，请他费心代为款侍，

When our master returned tomorrow, he would thank him in person.”
明天我们老爷回来了，再当面同他道谢。』

Ruixiang rattled it all off in one breath, setting out everything clearly.
瑞香咭咭呱呱一口气说下来，事情交代得清清楚楚；

Hu thought Madam Luosi had handled the matter most suitably.
胡雪岩觉得螺蛳太太处置得颇为得体，

“A good thing I did not have her come to Lingyin,” he said with satisfaction.
很满意地说∶『亏得我不叫她到灵隐去，

“Otherwise there would have been no one capable of attending to it.”
不然，没有人料理得来。』

“A good thing, too, that a strong commander has no weak soldiers.”
『也亏得强将手下无弱兵。』

Ruixiang recognized Gu’s compliment, smiled prettily at him, then turned her head away.
瑞香听出来是在夸赞她，古应春嫣然一笑，随即把头别了开去。

Gu smiled as well, two crow’s-feet showing at the corners of his eyes.
古应春也笑，笑得眼角露出两条鱼尾纹。

When Ruixiang returned from seeing him out, she said to Hu, “I suppose you will not want the noodles now.
等瑞香送了古应春回来，向胡雪岩说道∶『面想来不要了。

I have told the small kitchen to prepare a few delicate, refreshing dishes.
我已经关照小厨房，弄几样精致爽口的菜；

Where would you like dinner served?”
请老爷的示，在哪里开饭？』


“Here will do,” Hu replied. Then he added, “I had no idea you were so vicious!
『就在这里好了。』胡雪岩又说∶『我倒不晓得你这么凶！

A woman may be formidable; that is all very well.
女人厉害，可以；

But viciousness will only hurt her in the end.”
凶，不可以，自己吃亏。』

“Madam runs the household.
『太太当家，

Someone has to play the villain for her.
总要有个人来替她做恶人。

Would you have her be the villain herself while we stand by putting in kind words for everyone?”
莫非倒是太太自己来做恶人，我们在旁边替人家说好话？』

Hu could find no answer to that.
胡雪岩觉得她的话竟无可驳；

After a moment’s thought he said, “Even if you must play the villain, there is no need to behave like a fool.
想了一下说∶『就做恶人也犯不着撒蠢；

Those filthy insults you used—do you not hear how ugly they sound?”
什么小Ｘ不小ＸＸ，难听不难听？』

Ruixiang flushed.
瑞香涨红了脸，

She wanted to defend herself but could think of no excuse, and stood rooted to the spot, at a loss what to do.
欲待分辩，却又实在没有理由，以致于僵在那里有些手足无措的模样。


Hu offered a literary quotation. “‘A person must first demean herself before others will demean her.’
胡雪岩便又掉了一句文∶『「人必自悔而后人侮之。」』

Suppose she had turned your own obscenity back on you,” he went on.
他说∶『如果人家回你一句∶我「小」你「大」！

“How would a young woman like you have held up her head?”
你一个大青娘，脸上挂得住、挂不住？』

In Hangzhou, the expression he used referred to a girl in the bloom of youth, the age at which feelings were most tender and easily stirred.
杭州人叫妙龄女郎为『大青娘』，是最多悉善感的时候；

Ruixiang was ashamed and remorseful.
瑞香又羞又悔，

Her eyes reddened, and she was on the verge of tears.
眼圈红红的，要哭出来了。

“Why, why, why!” Hu exclaimed in astonishment.
『咦，咦，咦！』胡雪岩大为诧异，

“You forbade her to cry, and now you are about to cry yourself.
『你叫人家不准哭，自己倒要哭了，

What is the matter?
为啥？

Have I spoken too harshly?”
莫非我的话说得重了。』

At once Ruixiang checked her tears.
一听这话，瑞香顿时收泪，

She drew from beneath her arm a white silk handkerchief embroidered with a spray of daphne, the flower whose Chinese name was her own, and blew her nose. “Who is crying?”
抽出腋下的一方白纺绸绣一枝瑞香花的手绢，擤一擤鼻子答说∶『哪个哭了。』

“Best not to cry.
『不哭最好，

Bring me the dominoes, then go out front and see how far the dinner has got.”
你把牙牌拿来，再到前面看看，坐席坐到啥光景了？』


Ruixiang fetched a box of ivory dominoes, tipped them onto the square rosewood table, and went downstairs.
瑞香答应着，取出一盒牙牌，倒在红木方桌上，然后下了阁子。

Left alone, Hu played “Passing the Five Gates” to while away the time. After several unsuccessful rounds, he turned from the game to divination, seeking a sign of his future.
胡雪岩一个人拿牙牌『通五关』打发辰光连着几副不通，便换了起数问前程。

Following the rhyme for consulting the domino oracle—“Lay the full set out in line; count the openings that you find; three successive layouts mark, then judge the high, the low, the middle”—he counted sixteen openings in the first layout, twenty-one in the second, but in the third only one matched pair and one unmatched combination, six openings in all.
于是照牙牌神数的歌诀∶『全副牙牌一字开，中间看有几多开，连排三次分明记，上下中平内取裁。』头一次得了十六开，第二次更多，竟有廿一开，第三次却只得一副对子，一副分相，共计六开。


Hu knew the method well.
胡雪岩是弄熟了的，

He worked out the result as “highest auspice, highest auspice, lower middle.”
一算是『上上、上上、中下』。

He could more or less remember the verse, but he needed the book for its “Explanation” and “Judgment.”
诗句也还约略记得，但『解』与『断』，却须找书来看。

Finding the section on the domino oracle in Elegant Pastimes of the Ladies’ Chamber, he opened it and read: “With a fair wind, hoist your sail in time; / Cross safely the whale’s vast waters, ten thousand miles.
找到『兰闺清玩』的『牙牌神数』，翻开来一看，那着诗是『一帆风顺及时扬，稳度鲸川万里航，

/ Should you reach the turn where the sail follows the Xiang, / Rein in the gallant horse before it takes the downward slope.”
若到帆随湘转处，下坡骏马早收缰。』

As he recited it, he thought, “There is something in that.”
一面念，一面心想∶『有点意思。』

He read on: “Explanation: Do not torment yourself over your undertakings.
再往下看，『解曰∶谋为勿忧煎，

Their fulfillment lies before your eyes.
成全在眼前，

Whatever you attempt will prosper, but everywhere you must handle others tactfully.”
施为无不利，到处要周旋。』

At the last phrase, he suddenly slapped the table and exclaimed, “Today’s reading is uncannily accurate!”
看到最后一句，不由得蓦然一拍桌子，大声自语∶『今天这个数起得神了！』

He had scarcely finished when someone asked, “What are you doing?”
语声刚终，有人接口∶『你在作啥？』

Looking up, he saw Madam Luosi entering with a hand on a little maid’s shoulder; Ruixiang followed behind.
抬眼看时，前面螺蛳太太手扶小丫头的肩，正踏进门来，后面跟着瑞香。

“Have the guests gone?”
『客散了？』


“Not yet, but every table has someone to attend to it,” Madam Luosi replied. “I heard that Brother-in-law Gu had come back and gone out again.
『还没有，不过每桌都有人陪。』螺蛳太太说∶『我是听说七姐夫来了又出去了，

I wondered whether something important had happened, so I came specially to see.” “He has gone to Dr Meitenggeng’s.
不知道是不是有啥要紧的事，所以我特别来看看—』『他到梅藤更那里去了，

He will be back as soon as he has had a word with him.”
说一句话就回来的。』

Hu then went on to the “Judgment” that followed the “Explanation.”
胡雪岩接着又往下看『解』了以后的『断』。
