胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement
第一章（本章第3部分，共4部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


『断曰∶黄节晚香，清节可贵，
“Judgment: The yellow bloom gives its fragrance late; unsullied integrity is precious.

逝水回波，急流勇退。』
Flowing water swirls back upon itself: have the courage to withdraw from the swift current.”

最后这四个字，胡雪岩是懂得；
Hu understood that final injunction perfectly.

而且这也正是内则老母、外则良友在一再劝他的。
It was precisely what his mother at home and his good friends outside had repeatedly urged him to do.

此刻不自觉地便仔细想了下去。
Without quite realizing it, he began to ponder it closely.

螺蛳太太也常看他起数，但都不似此刻这么认真，而且是上了心事的模样，
Madam Luosi had often watched him cast a divination, but never with such seriousness or with anything weighing on his mind as it seemed to do now.

当然深感关切。
Naturally, she was concerned.

『瑞香，去调一杯玫瑰薄荷露来，我解解酒。』
“Ruixiang, mix me a glass of rose-and-mint cordial to clear my head after the wine.”

说着，在胡雪岩对面坐了下来问道∶『你起的数，倒讲给我听听。』
Seating herself opposite Hu, she asked, “Tell me about the reading you have had.”

『今天起的这个数，我愈想愈有道理。』胡雪岩说∶『先说我一帆风顺，不过到时候要收篷。
“The more I think about today’s reading, the more sense it makes,” Hu replied. “First it says I am sailing before a fair wind, but that when the time comes I must furl my sail.

啥时候呢？
When?

「帆随湘转处」，
‘Where the sail turns with the Xiang.’

灵就灵在这个「湘」字上，
The uncanny thing is that word Xiang.

是指左大人；
It means Lord Zuo.

到左大人不当两江总督了，我就要「下坡骏马早收缰」了。』
When Lord Zuo ceases to be Governor-General of Liangjiang, I must ‘rein in the gallant horse before it takes the downward slope.’”

『还有呢？』
“And what else?”


『还有这两句，也说得极准∶』施为无不利，到处要周旋。
“These two lines are remarkably accurate too: ‘Whatever you attempt will prosper, but everywhere you must handle others tactfully.’

「拿银子铺路，自然无往不利路路通了。
Pave the way with silver and naturally every road opens before you.”

『』还有呢？
“And what else?”

『『那就是「急流勇退。」』
“‘Have the courage to withdraw from the swift current.’”

螺蛳太太点点头，喝了一大口玫瑰薄荷露说∶『我看只有「急流勇退」四个字说得最好。
Madam Luosi nodded and took a long drink of her rose-and-mint cordial. “I think that last advice is the best of all.

又是「下坡」又是「骏马」，你想收缰都收不住。』
A gallant horse, and a downward slope as well—by then, even if you wanted to pull on the reins, you might not be able to hold it back.”

胡雪岩正要回答，只听外面人在报∶『古老爷回来了。』
Hu was about to answer when someone announced from outside, “Mr Gu is back.”

『瑞香，』螺蛳太太一面站起来，一面说∶『带人来开饭。』
Madam Luosi rose. “Ruixiang, bring the servants in to lay dinner.”


『讲妥当了？』
“Is everything arranged?”

胡雪岩也站了起来，迎上去问。
Hu stood up too and went to meet him.

『讲好了。
“Yes.

明天上午八点钟去看赫德。
We shall call on Hart at eight tomorrow morning.

然后他料理公事完毕中午到灵隐去拜寿。
Then, once he has finished his official business, he will come to Lingyin at noon to offer birthday greetings.”

『』吃饭呢？『螺蛳太太急忙问说。
“And what about his meal?” Madam Luosi asked at once.

『这就要好好商量了。』
“We must discuss that carefully.”

『对，对，好好商量。』
“Yes, yes, let us do so.”

胡雪岩扬一扬手，
Hu waved a hand.

『我们这面来谈。』
“Come over here.”

古应春跟到书桌旁边坐定了说∶『我不但见了梅藤更，还见了赫德，
Gu followed him to the desk and sat down. “I saw not only Dr Meitenggeng but Hart himself.

他说他这一次一则来拜寿；二则还有事要跟小爷叔约谈。』
He says he has come partly to offer birthday greetings and partly because there is something he wishes to discuss with you, Uncle.”

『什么事？
“What is it?

汇丰的款子，应付的本息还早啊！』
The principal and interest due to HSBC will not fall due for some time yet.”

『是茧子的事。』
“It concerns the cocoons.”


『这个，』胡雪岩问∶『怡和的大板怎么不来呢？』
“In that case,” Hu asked, “why has Jardine Matheson’s chief manager not come?”

『已经来了，
“He has.

也住在梅藤更那里。』
He is staying with Dr Meitenggeng too.”

『这样说，是有备而来的。
“Then they have come prepared.

我们倒要好好儿想个应付的办法。』
We had better think carefully about how to deal with them.”

『当然。』古应春又说∶『小爷叔，你哪天有空？』
“Certainly,” Gu said. “What day would suit you, Uncle?”

『要说空，哪一天都不空。』
“If you mean a day when I have nothing to do, none would.

胡雪岩答说∶『他老远从北京到这里，
But he has come all the way from Beijing.

当然主随客便，我们只有看他的意思。』
The host must accommodate the guest; we can only see what suits him.”

『既然小爷叔这么说，明天中午等他到灵隐拜了生日，请他到府上来吃饭，
“Since you feel that way, why not invite him to dine at your house tomorrow after he has offered birthday greetings at Lingyin at noon?

顺便带他逛逛园子。』
You could show him the garden as well.”

『我也是这么想。』
“That is what I had in mind.

胡雪岩问∶『吃西餐，还是中国菜。』
Shall we serve Western food or Chinese?”

『还是西餐吧。』
“Western food, I think.

古应春说∶『我这回带来的六个厨子，其中有一个是法皇的御厨，
Of the six cooks I have brought this time, one formerly cooked for the Emperor of the French.

做出来的东西，不会坍台的。』
His food will not disgrace us.”

『来，来！』螺蛳太太喊道∶『来坐吧！』
“Come, come!” Madam Luosi called. “Sit down!”


『来了！』
“Coming!”

胡雪岩走过来说道∶『明天中午总税务司赫德要来吃饭，
Hu walked over. “Inspector-General Hart will be dining here tomorrow at noon.

吃西餐；
Western food.

厨子应春带来，
Yingchun will provide the cook.

席摆在哪里方便，要预备点啥，顶好趁早交代下去。』
Where it would be convenient to set the table and what else will be needed had best be settled early.”

『有多少人？』
“How many people?”

『主客一共四位。』古应春答说。
“Four altogether, including hosts and guests,” Gu replied.

『应春，』胡雪岩问∶『你是说，怡和的大班也请？』
“Yingchun, do you mean to invite Jardine Matheson’s chief manager too?”

一听这语气，古应春便即反问∶『小爷叔的意思呢？』
At the sound of Hu’s voice, Gu immediately asked in return, “What would you prefer, Uncle?”

『我看「阳春面加重，免免」了！』
“I think we shall have ‘a double helping of plain noodles’—mian, mian: let us leave him out!”

『我看预备还是要预备在那里，』螺蛳太太插进来说∶『说不定赫德倒带了他来呢？』
“I think we should prepare for him anyway,” Madam Luosi put in. “Hart might bring him along.”

洋人没有挟带不速之客的习惯。
Foreigners were not in the habit of arriving with uninvited companions.

螺蛳太太对这方面的应酬规矩不算内行；
Madam Luosi did not fully understand their rules of social intercourse.

不过多预备总不错，
Still, it could do no harm to prepare extra.

或许临时想起还有什么人该请，即不致于捉襟见肘。
They might suddenly think of someone else who ought to be invited, and then they would not be caught short.

因此，胡雪岩点点头说∶『对，
Hu nodded. “Quite right.

多预备几份好了。』
Have a few extra places ready.”

说着，相将落座，
They sat down together.

喝的是红葡萄酒；
Red wine was served.

古应春看着斟在水晶杯中、紫光泛彩的酒说∶『这酒要冰了，味道才出得来。』
Looking at the purple glow of the wine in the crystal glasses, Gu said, “This wine needs chilling to bring out its flavor.”

『那就拿冰来冰。』
“Then bring some ice and chill it.”

原来胡家也跟大内一样，自己有冰窖。
Like the imperial palace, the Hu household had its own ice cellar.

数九寒天，将热水倒在物制的方形木盒中，
In the depths of winter, hot water was poured into specially made square wooden molds.

等表里晶莹，冻结实了，置于掘得极深、下铺草荐的地窖，
Once it had frozen solid, clear through and through, the blocks were laid in a very deep cellar with straw matting underneath.

到来年六月，方始开窖取用。
Ordinarily, it would not be opened until the sixth month of the following year.

此时胡雪岩交代，当然提前开窖。
Hu’s order meant opening it ahead of time.


这一来不免大费手脚，耽误工夫，古应春颇为不安，
This required considerable trouble and caused a delay, which made Gu uneasy.

但已知胡雪岩的脾气愈来愈任性，
But he knew Hu was growing more willful by the day.

劝阴无用，只好听其自然。
Remonstrance would be useless, so he could only let matters take their course.

趁这工夫，胡雪岩与古应春将次日与赫德会谈可能涉及的各方面，细细研究了一番。
They used the interval to examine in detail all the questions that might arise in the next day’s discussion with Hart.

其时螺蛳太太已回到前面，等席散送客；
Madam Luosi had meanwhile gone back to the front of the house to wait for the banquet to end and see the guests off.

镜槛阁中，凿冰冻酒，检点肴馔，都是瑞香主持，
At Jingjian Pavilion, Ruixiang supervised the breaking of the ice, the chilling of the wine, and the checking of the dishes.

只见她来往俏影，翩翩如蝶，不时吸引着古应春的视线移转。
Her graceful figure flitted to and fro like a butterfly, repeatedly drawing Gu’s eyes after her.


胡雪岩看在眼里，
Hu noticed.

愈发觉得刚才胸中所动的一念，应该从速实现。
All the more reason, he thought, to put into effect without delay the idea that had occurred to him earlier.

等入了座，他先看一看桌上的菜，
Once seated, he surveyed the dishes on the table.

问道∶『还有啥？』
“What else is there?”

『还有锦乡长寿面、八仙上寿汤。』瑞香答说∶『古老爷跟老爷还想吃点啥？
“Splendid Longevity Noodles and Eight Immortals Offering Birthday Blessings Soup,” Ruixiang replied. “Would you or Mr Gu like anything else?

我去交代。』
I shall tell the kitchen.”

『够了，够了。』古应春说∶『两个人吃八样菜，已经多了；
“Enough, enough,” Gu said. “Eight dishes for two people are more than sufficient.

再多，反而看饱了吃不下。』
Any more and we should be full just from looking.”

『什么叫八仙上寿汤？』
“What is Eight Immortals Offering Birthday Blessings Soup?” Hu asked.

『就是八珍汤。』瑞香笑道∶『今天是老太太的好日子，所以我拿它改个名字。』
“Eight Delicacies Soup,” Ruixiang answered with a smile. “I gave it a different name because this is the old lady’s happy day.”

『好，
“Good.

晓得了。』
I see.

胡雪岩答说∶『我想吃点甜的，
I should like something sweet.

你到小厨房去看看，
Go to the small kitchen and see what they have.

等弄好了带回来。』
Bring it back when it is ready.”

这是胡雪岩故意遣开瑞香，
Hu was deliberately sending Ruixiang away.

因为他要跟古应春说的话，是一时不便让瑞香知道睥。
What he wanted to discuss with Gu could not yet be allowed to reach her ears.


『老太太说，这回生日样样都好，
“The old lady says everything about her birthday is wonderful this year.

美中不足的，就是七姐没有来。』
The one thing lacking is that Sister Seven has not come.”

『要美中不足才好。』古应春答说∶『曾文正分别号叫「求阙斋」，
“It is better to have something lacking,” Gu replied. “Zeng Guofan called his study ‘Seeking Imperfection.’

特为去求美中不足，那才是持盈保仄之道。
He deliberately sought a flaw in perfection; that is the way to preserve prosperity and keep oneself safe.

醇亲王从儿子做了皇帝以后，置了一样古董，叫做「欹器」，
After his son became emperor, Prince Chun acquired an antique vessel called a tilting bowl.

盛水不能满，一满就翻倒了。』
You cannot fill it right up: when it is full, it overturns.”

胡雪岩并未听出他话中的深意，管自己问道∶『七姐现在身子怎么样？』
Hu missed the deeper meaning of his words and pursued his own question. “How is Sister Seven’s health now?”

『无非带病延年。
“She can only nurse her illness and hope to live on.

西医说∶中风调养比吃药重要；
The Western doctor says that after a stroke, proper care matters more than medicine.

调养第一要心兀，她就是心静不下来，
The first requirement is peace of mind, but she simply cannot be at peace.

我怎么劝也没用。』
Nothing I say will persuade her.”

『为啥呢？』胡雪岩问∶『为啥心静不下来？』
“Why?” Hu asked. “Why can she not settle her mind?”

『小爷叔，你晓得她的，
“You know her, Uncle.

凡事好强。
She insists on doing everything well.

自从她病倒以后，家里当然不比从前那样子有条理了，
Since she fell ill, the house has naturally not been as orderly as before.

她看不惯，自己要指挥，话又说不清楚，丫头老妈子弄来总不如她的意。
She cannot bear to see it and wants to direct things herself, but she cannot speak clearly, and whatever the maids and older servants do never satisfies her.

你想，一个病人一天到晚操心，还要生气，
Think of it: a sick woman worrying all day and getting angry into the bargain.

糟糕不糟糕？』说到这里，古应春叹口气，将酒杯放了下来。
Is that not dreadful?” Gu sighed and set down his glass.


提起不愉快的事，害得他败了酒兴，胡雪岩不免歉然，
Hu felt sorry to have raised an unhappy subject and spoiled his friend’s pleasure in the wine.

但正因为如此，更要往深处去谈。
Yet this was precisely why he had to pursue it.

『还有呢？』
“And what else?”

『还有，就是她总不放心我；
“She is always worrying about me.

常说她对不起，因为她病在床上，没法子照料我的饮食起居。
She often says she has failed me because she is confined to bed and cannot look after my meals and daily needs.

我说，你千万不要这样想，这是没法子的事；再说，有丫头老妈子，我自己会指挥。
I tell her she must not think like that: it cannot be helped, and besides, there are maids and older servants whom I can direct myself.

她说∶没有体己的人，到底不一样。
She says that without someone close to me it is simply not the same.

又说∶』中年丧妻大不幸，
Then she says, ‘To lose your wife in middle age is a great misfortune.

弄个半死不活的老婆在那里，你反而要为我操心，是加倍的大不幸。
To have a wife who is neither quite alive nor dead, and to have to worry about her as well, is twice the misfortune.’

「常时谈得她也哭，我也哭。
Often we talk until she is crying and I am crying too.”

『说着，古应春又泫然欲涕了。
As he spoke, Gu was again close to tears.

『应春，你说得我也想哭了。
“Yingchun, you make me want to cry myself,” Hu said.

你们真正是所谓伉俪情深，
“Yours truly is a marriage of deep affection.

来世也一定是恩爱夫妻。
I am sure you will be a loving couple in the next life as well.

不过，既然七姐是这样子的情形，我的想法倒又改过了。』
Still, if that is how things stand with Sister Seven, it changes my thinking.”

『小爷叔，你有啥想法？』
“What have you been thinking, Uncle?”


『我在想，要替你弄个人。
“I have been thinking of finding someone for you.

这个人当然要你中意，要七姐也中意。
Naturally, she must be someone you like and Sister Seven likes too.

人，我已经有了，
I already have someone in mind.

虽说有把握，你们都会中意，不过，女人家的事情，有时候是很难说的，尤其是讨小纳妾，更加要慎重，
Though I am confident you would both be pleased with her, matters involving women are sometimes difficult to judge, and taking a concubine calls for even greater care.

所以我想过些日子，叫罗四姐到上海去一趟，当面跟七姐商量，
I had therefore thought of sending Sister Luo to Shanghai in a few days to discuss it with Sister Seven in person.

照现在看，我想这件事，可以定局了。』
From what you have told me, however, I think we may be able to settle it.”

一番话说得古应春心乱如麻，
Gu’s thoughts were in turmoil.

不知是喜是惧？
He hardly knew whether he was pleased or afraid.

定定神，理出一个头绪，先要知道，胡雪岩心目中，『已经有了』的那个人是谁？
Composing himself, he picked out the first question to be answered: who was the woman Hu already had in mind?

等他一问出来，胡雪岩答道∶『还有哪个，
When he asked, Hu replied, “Who else?

自然是瑞香。』
Ruixiang, of course.”

古应春又惊又喜，
Gu was startled and delighted.

眼前浮起瑞香的影子，耳边响起瑞香的声音，顿时生出无限的遐想。
Her figure rose before his eyes and her voice sounded in his ears, setting his imagination wandering.

『应春，』胡雪岩问说∶『你看怎么样，
“Yingchun,” Hu asked, “what do you think?

七姐会不会中意她？』
Would Sister Seven be pleased with her?”

『我想，应该会。』
“I think she ought to be.”


『你呢？』
“And you?”

古应春笑笑不答，只顾自己从冰桶中取酒瓶来斟酒。
Gu smiled without answering and busied himself taking the wine bottle from the ice bucket to refill his glass.

『我说得不错吧！
“Was I not right?

这个人你们夫妻俩都会中意。』
Both husband and wife would be pleased with her.”

『话也不能这么说。』
“One cannot quite put it that way.”

古应春将七姑奶奶得病以来说过的话，细细搜索了一遍，
Gu searched his memory for everything the Seventh Lady had said since falling ill.

有些悲伤地说∶『小爷叔，有件事，我不能不提出来。
With some sadness he continued, “Uncle, there is something I cannot leave unmentioned.

阿七从来没有提过要替我弄一个人的话。』
A’qi has never once suggested finding someone for me.”

这使得胡雪岩一楞，
That gave Hu pause.

心中寻思，七姑奶奶既然因为无法亲自照料丈夫的饮食起居而深感抱歉；同时也觉得没有一个得力的帮手替她治家，
The Seventh Lady felt deeply sorry that she could no longer personally care for her husband’s meals and daily needs; at the same time, she plainly lacked a capable assistant to manage the household.

那末以她一向看得广、想得深的性情，一定会转过替古应春提过，
Given her habitual breadth of view and depth of thought, she must surely have considered finding someone for Gu. Yet she had never raised the subject.

这中间就大可玩味了。
There was much to ponder in that.


『应春，』他问∶『你自己有没有讨小的打算？』
“Yingchun,” he asked, “have you yourself thought of taking a concubine?”

古应春仔仔细细地回忆着，而且在重新体认自己曾经有过的感想以后，
Gu searched his memory carefully and reconsidered feelings he had once had.

很慎重地答说∶『如果说没有，我是说假话。
At last he answered with great seriousness. “I should be lying if I said no.

不过，这种念头只要一起，我马我会丢掉，
But whenever the thought arises, I immediately dismiss it.

自己告诉自己∶不要自讨苦吃。』
I tell myself not to go looking for trouble.”

『这种心境，你同七姐谈过没有？』
“Have you ever discussed these feelings with Sister Seven?”

『没有。』
“No.”

『从来没有谈过？』
“Never?”

『从没有。』
“Never.”

『有没有露过这样的口风呢？』
“Have you ever even hinted at them?”

见他这样『打破沙锅问到底』，古应春倒不敢信口回答了，
Hu’s insistence on getting to the bottom of the matter made Gu unwilling to answer casually.

复又想了一下，方始开口∶『没有。』
He thought again before saying, “No.”


『好！
“Good.

我懂了。』胡雪岩说∶『讨小讨得不好，是自讨若吃；
I understand,” Hu said. “A badly chosen concubine does indeed mean looking for trouble.

讨得好，另当别论。
A well-chosen one is another matter.

我料七姐的心事，不是不想替你弄个人，是这个人不容易去觅。
I suspect Sister Seven is not opposed to finding you someone; the difficulty is finding the right person.

又要能干，又要体贴，又要肯听她的话；还要相貌看得过去，
She must be capable and considerate, willing to obey her, and reasonably good-looking as well.

所以心里虽有这样的念头，没有觅着中意的人之前，先不开口。
So although the idea is in her mind, she will say nothing until she has found someone suitable.

七姐做事向来是这样的，
That is how Sister Seven has always done things.

我晓得。』
I know her.”

古应春觉得他的话也不无道理，
Gu thought there was some reason in his words.

倒不防探探妻子的口气。
Perhaps he might sound his wife out.

旋即转念，
But immediately he checked himself.

此事决不能轻发！
This was not a subject to raise lightly.

倘若妻子根本不愿，一说这话，岂非伤了感情？
If she was fundamentally unwilling, would mentioning it not wound her feelings?

『能干、体贴、听话、相貌过得去，这四个条件，顶要紧的是听话。
Hu went on, “Capable, considerate, obedient, and reasonably good-looking: of those four conditions, obedience matters most.

七姐人情、世故熟透，
Sister Seven knows human nature and the ways of the world thoroughly.

世界上总是听话的老实无用；能干调皮捣蛋，
Obedient people tend to be honest but useless; capable ones tend to be unruly and mischievous.

她一个端正人，躺在床上，
She is an upright woman, lying there in bed.

如果叫人到东，偏要到西，拿她有啥法子？
If she tells someone to go east and that person insists on going west, what can she do?

那一来，不是把她活活气死？
It would anger her to death.

七姐顾虑来，顾虑去，就是顾虑这个。
That is the worry she turns over and over in her mind.

应春，你说对不对！』
Is that not so, Yingchun?”

『是的。』古应春不能不承认∶『小爷叔把职七的为人，看得很透。』
“Yes,” Gu had to admit. “You understand A’qi very well, Uncle.”

『闲话少说，
“Enough talk.

我们来谈瑞香。
Let us consider Ruixiang.

四个条件，她占了三个；
She meets three of the four conditions.

体贴或许差一点，不过那也是将来你们感情上的事，
Perhaps she falls a little short in consideration, but that will depend on your feelings for each other later.

感情深了，自然会体贴。』
Once the affection is deep, consideration will come naturally.”

『哪里就谈得到将来了？』古应春笑着喝了口酒说∶『这件事要慢慢商量。』
“How can we already be talking about later?” Gu laughed and took a sip of wine. “This matter needs careful discussion.”


『你说谈不到将来，我说喜事就在眼前。』
“You say we cannot talk about later; I say the happy occasion is just before us.”

胡雪岩略略放低了声音∶『贤慧，瑞香当然还谈不到；
Hu lowered his voice a little. “I cannot yet call Ruixiang a model of womanly virtue.

不过，我同罗四姐两个人一起替你写包票，一定听七姐的话。
But Sister Luo and I will jointly guarantee that she obeys Sister Seven.

你信不信。』
Do you believe us?”

古应春何能不信，亦何能不喜，
How could Gu fail to believe him, or fail to be delighted?

但总顾虑着妻子如果真的有妨意，这件事就弄巧成拙了。
Yet he still feared that if his wife truly resented the idea, their clever plan might produce the opposite of what they intended.

看他脸上忽喜忽忧的神情，胡雪岩当然也能约略猜到他的心事。
Hu could readily guess his thoughts from the way delight and anxiety alternated on his face.

但夫妻之间的这种情形，到底只有同床共枕的人才能判断。
Still, only those who shared a bed could really judge such matters between husband and wife.

所以他不再固劝，让它冷一冷，
He pressed no further, allowing the subject to cool.

看古应春多想一想以后的态度，再作道理。
He would see what attitude Gu took after more thought and decide what to do then.


于是把话题扯了开去，海阔天空地聊了一阵，
He changed the subject, and they chatted freely for a while.

瑞香亲自提来一个细篾金漆圆笼，
Ruixiang herself brought in a round basket of finely woven bamboo coated with gold lacquer.

打开来看，青花瓷盘中，盛着现做的枣泥核花奶酥；是醇亲王厨子传授的。
Inside, on a blue-and-white porcelain plate, lay freshly made cream pastries filled with date paste and walnuts, made from a recipe passed on by Prince Chun’s cook.

接着，小厨房另外送来寿面跟『八仙上寿汤』；
The small kitchen next sent in the birthday noodles and the Eight Immortals Offering Birthday Blessings Soup.

寿面一大盘，炒得十分出色，但胡雪岩与古应春都是应应景，浅尝即止。
There was a great platter of beautifully fried noodles, but both Hu and Gu merely tasted a little for the occasion.

『多吃点嘛！』瑞香劝道∶『这么好的寿面，不吃真可惜。』
“Do have some more,” Ruixiang urged. “Such fine birthday noodles—it would be a pity not to eat them.”

『说得不错。』古应春答说∶『我再来一点。』
“You are right,” Gu replied. “I shall have a little more.”


于是好替他们各自盛了一小碗，
She filled a small bowl for each of them.

古应春努力加餐，算是吃完了。
Gu made an effort and finished his.

胡雪尝了一口说道∶『我再来一点。』
Hu tasted a mouthful and said, “I shall have a little more.”

『糟蹋了实在可惜。』瑞香向外喊道，
“It would be a shame to let them go to waste.” Ruixiang called outside.

『小梅，你们把这盘寿面拿去，分了吃掉∶沾沾老太太的福气。』
“Xiaomei, take this platter of birthday noodles and share it among you. Have a little of the old lady’s good fortune!”

说着，亲自将一盘炒面捧了出去。
She carried the platter out herself.

胡雪岩看在眼里，暗自点头。
Hu watched and nodded inwardly.

等饭罢茶时，螺蛳太太亦已客散稍闲，来到镜槛阁休息；
When they had finished eating and were drinking tea, Madam Luosi came to Jingjian Pavilion to rest, her guests now departed.

当然还有许多杂条要料理，
There were, of course, still many miscellaneous matters to attend to.

走马换将，都交给瑞香了。
The two women exchanged duties, leaving everything to Ruixiang.


『我刚刚跟应春谈了一件大事，
“I have just been discussing an important matter with Yingchun.

现在要同你商量了。』
Now I must consult you.”

商量的便是嫁瑞香之事；
It was the proposed marriage of Ruixiang.

不等胡雪岩话毕，螺蛳太太便即说道∶『我早就有这个意思了。
Before Hu had finished, Madam Luosi said, “I have long had the same idea.

七姐夫，史要七姐一句话，我马上来办。』
Brother-in-law Gu, let Sister Seven say the word, and I shall arrange it at once.”

『就是这句话为难。』古应春答说∶『我自己当然不便提；就是旁人去提，也不大妥当。』
“That one word is precisely the difficulty,” Gu replied. “I can hardly raise the subject myself, and it would not be quite proper even for someone else to do so.”

『何以见得？』
“Why not?”

『人家去说，她表面上说不出不愿意的话来，
“If someone speaks to her, she will be unable to say openly that she objects.

心里有了疙瘩，对她的病，大不相宜。』
But she may harbor a grievance in her heart, which would be very bad for her health.”


『我看七姐不会的。』胡雪岩对螺蛳太太说∶『下个月我到上海，你同我一起去，当面跟七姐谈这件事。』
“I do not think Sister Seven would,” Hu said. He turned to Madam Luosi. “Come with me when I go to Shanghai next month and discuss it with her face to face.”

『那一来，她怎么样不愿意，也得很高兴。』古应春大为摇头∶『不妥，不妥！
Gu shook his head vigorously. “Then, however unwilling she was, she would have to pretend to be delighted. No, no.

她决不肯说真心话的。』
She would never tell you her true feelings.”

『我倒有个办法，
“I have a way,” Madam Luosi said.

我要由七姐自己开口。』
“I shall have Sister Seven herself bring it up.”

此言一出，古应春、胡雪岩一齐倾身注目，倒要听听她是何好办法，能使得七姑奶奶自愿为丈夫纳妾。
At this, both men leaned forward and fixed their eyes on her, eager to hear how she could induce the Seventh Lady voluntarily to arrange a concubine for her husband.

『办法很容易。』
“It is quite simple.

螺蛳太太说∶『我把瑞香带了去。
I shall take Ruixiang along.

只说我不放心她的病，特为叫瑞香去服侍她，帮她理家的。
I shall merely say that I am worried about her illness and have brought Ruixiang specially to wait on her and help her manage the house.

只要瑞香服侍得好，事事听她的话，她自然会想到，要留住瑞香只有一条路，让她也姓古。』
Provided Ruixiang serves her well and obeys her in everything, she will naturally see that there is only one way to keep her: let her become a member of the Gu family.”

『此计大妙！』
“A marvelous plan!”

岩胡雪拍着手说∶『准定这么办。』
Hu clapped his hands. “That is settled, then.”


古应春也觉得这是个很妥当的办法，
Gu, too, thought it a very sound plan.

但螺蛳太太却提出了警告∶『七姐夫，不过我劝你不要心急，
Madam Luosi added a warning, however. “Brother-in-law Gu, do not be impatient.

你最好先疏远瑞香一点。』
You had better keep your distance from Ruixiang at first.”

『人逢喜事精神爽』古应春这一夜只睡了两个辰，一觉醒来，天还没有亮透，
Happiness renews a man’s spirits. Gu slept only four hours that night, and when he woke, it was not yet fully light.

看自鸣钟上一直线，恰好六点钟响。
Looking at the clock, he saw the hands in a straight line just as it struck six.

他住的是胡家花园中的一处客房，名叫锁春院，茶木甚盛，
He was staying in a guest suite in Hu’s garden called Suochun Court, where flowers and trees grew thickly.

揭开重帘，推出窗去，
He lifted the heavy curtains and pushed open the window.

花香鸟语，令人精神一振，
Fragrant blossoms and birdsong refreshed his spirits.

心里寻思，这天洋人拜寿，是他的『重头戏』，
Today’s reception for the foreign birthday visitors would be his great undertaking.

宁可赶早去巡查，看有什么不妥的地方，须先改正，庶几不负至交所托。
He had better begin his inspections early, discover and correct anything amiss, and justify the trust his close friend had placed in him.

于是漱洗早餐，随即带了跟班，坐着胡家替他预备的轿子，
He washed, ate breakfast, and set out with an attendant in the sedan the Hu household had provided.

先巡视了设在城里的六处寿堂，一一检点妥当，
First he inspected the six birthday halls inside the city and checked that all was in order.

然后出钱塘门到灵隐，
Then he went out through Qiantang Gate to Lingyin.

不过九点刚过。
It was only just after nine when he arrived.

这灵隐的寿堂，原规定了是英国人来拜寿的地方，只是洋人闹不清这些细节，
The Lingyin hall had originally been assigned to the British visitors, but foreigners did not understand these fine distinctions.

有的逛了天竺、灵隐，顺便就来拜寿，
Some simply called to offer their congratulations after sightseeing at Tianzhu and Lingyin.

人数不多，倒是看的人多，指指点点，嘻嘻哈哈，乱得很热闹。
There were not many guests, but a great many spectators, pointing, chattering, and laughing in lively disorder.
