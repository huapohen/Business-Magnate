第二章
CHAPTER TWO


江浙的养蚕人家，大部分是产销合一的。
Most silkworm-raising households in Jiangsu and Zhejiang both produced and marketed their own silk.

茧子固然亦可卖给领有『部帖』的茧行，
They could, of course, sell their cocoons to cocoon firms holding licenses from the ministry.

但茧行估价不高，而且同行公议，价格划一，
But those firms offered low prices, and the members of the trade had agreed to a uniform rate.

不卖茧则已，卖茧子一定受剥削；
Once a family decided to sell cocoons, exploitation was unavoidable.

再则收茧有一定的日子，
Moreover, cocoons were bought only on specified days.

或者人等不及，急于要钱用；或者茧子等不及，时间一长蚕蛾会咬破茧子，
Sometimes the growers could not wait because they needed money urgently; sometimes the cocoons could not wait, since in time the moths would bite their way out and ruin them.

所以除非万不得已，或者别有盘算，总是自家养蚕、自家做丝，
Unless driven by necessity or guided by some special calculation, families therefore raised their own silkworms and made their own silk.

这就要养活许多人了，
This provided a livelihood for many people.

因为做丝从煮茧开始，手续繁多，
From boiling the cocoons onward, silk-making involved a multitude of operations.

缫丝以后『捻丝』、『拍丝』，进炼染炼染，纬丝捻成经丝，还有『掉经』、『牵经』等等名目，
After reeling came twisting and beating, scouring and dyeing, twisting weft silk into warp silk, and the various stages of preparing and drawing out the warp.

最后是『接头』，到此方可上机织绸。
Only after the final joining of the threads could the silk go onto a loom to be woven into fabric.

一旦出现了机器缫丝厂，茧子由机器这头进去，丝由那头出来，
Once a mechanized silk-reeling mill appeared, however, cocoons went in at one end and silk emerged at the other.

什么『拍丝』、『牵经』都用不着了，这一行的工人，亦都敲破饭碗了。
The beating and drawing of threads were no longer needed, and the workers who had performed them lost their livelihoods.

更为严重的是，江浙农村，几乎家家户户都有缫丝的纺车，
More serious still, almost every rural household in Jiangsu and Zhejiang had a reeling wheel.

妇女无分老幼，大都恃此为副业；
Women of all ages relied on it for supplementary income.

孤寒寡妇的『棺材本』，小家碧玉的『嫁时装』，出在一部纺车上的，比比皆是，
A poor widow’s savings for her coffin, a girl from a modest household’s dowry—countless such sums came from a single wheel.

如果这部纺车一旦成为废物，那就真要出现『一路哭』的场面了。
If that wheel became useless, there would indeed be weeping all along the road.


因此，早就不断有人向胡雪岩陈情，要求他出面控制机器缫丝厂；
For this reason, people had long been coming to Hu Xueyan with petitions, asking him to intervene and restrain the mechanized mills.

就因为他的力量太大，
His power was immense.

手头经常握有价值三百万两银子的一万包丝在手里，可以垄断市场，
He regularly held ten thousand bales of silk worth three million taels of silver, enough to dominate the market.

所以恰和洋行竟搬动了『二品大员』的赫德来谈条件。
That was why Jardine Matheson had even persuaded Hart, a dignitary of the second rank, to negotiate terms.

条件是很好。
The terms were very good.

所谓『市价以外，另送佣金』，便是两笔收入，
“A commission on top of the market price” meant two sources of income.

因为『市价』中照例每包有二两五钱的佣金，由介绍洋行买丝的中间人与红纵栈对分；
The market price already included the customary commission of two taels and five mace per bale, divided equally between the intermediary who introduced the foreign buyer and the silk warehouse.

如果『另送佣金』，每包至少亦有一两，
An additional commission would be at least another tael per bale.

坐享厚利，在他人求之不得，而胡雪岩却只好放弃。
Here was a handsome profit for no effort, an opportunity others would have coveted; Hu could only let it go.

麻烦的是，赫德的情面不能不顾；
The difficulty was that he could not disregard Hart’s feelings.

至少要想个虽拒绝而不伤赫德面子，让他能向怡和洋行交代的说法。
At the very least, he must find a way to refuse without costing Hart face, so that the latter could still give Jardine Matheson an acceptable account of the meeting.

转了转念头，决定采取拖延的手段。
After turning the matter over, he decided to play for time.


『鹭翁，』他从从容容地答道∶『中国人有句话，叫做「在商言商」，
“Mr Lubin,” he replied calmly, “we Chinese have a saying: ‘In business, speak as a businessman.’

怡和这样好的条件，在我求之不得。
Terms as good as Jardine Matheson’s are more than I could hope for.

不过，鹭翁总也晓得广东的情形，缫丝的机器都打坏了；
But you must know what happened in Guangdong, where the silk-reeling machines were smashed.

如果我同怡和订了合同，起了风潮，不是我一个人的损失，
If I signed a contract with Jardine Matheson and disturbances followed, I should not be the only one to suffer.

地方上亦要受害。
The whole locality would suffer too.

鹭翁，请你想一想，外到我们浙江巡抚，内到军机处、总理衙站，岂不都要怪我？
Consider it: from our governor here in Zhejiang to the Grand Council and Zongli Yamen in the capital, would they not all blame me?

「都老爷」的厉害，鹭翁在京多年，总也晓得，
You have spent many years in Beijing and know how formidable the censors are.

他们会饶得了我？』
Would they let me off?”

看看是水都泼不进去了，
His case seemed watertight.

不道胡雪岩突然一转，
Then he abruptly changed course.

『不过，』他的语声很重，『鹭翁，你不是替怡和做说客，
“But”—he emphasized the word—“you are not merely speaking for Jardine Matheson.

你是为了我们中国富强，
Your concern is the wealth and strength of China.

这件事情，一定要弄它成功，
We must find a way to make this succeed.

等我同各方面筹划出一个妥当办法出来，
Let me work out a suitable arrangement with everyone concerned.

只要不起风潮，不弄坏市面原来靠养蚕缫丝的人家，有条生路，我一定遵鹭翁的吩咐，只跟怡和一家订约。
Provided there is no unrest, the market is not disrupted, and the families who have lived by raising silkworms and reeling silk retain a livelihood, I shall certainly do as you ask and contract with Jardine Matheson alone.

至于额外的佣金，是鹭翁的面子，决不敢领。』
As for the extra commission, out of regard for you I could never accept it.”

这番话说得很漂亮，
It was a handsome speech.

但赫德有名的老奸巨猾，对中国的人情世故，摸得透熟；心想不起风潮，不坏市面，还要养蚕人家有生路，要避免这三点的『妥当办法』，花十年的工夫也未见得能筹划得出来。
But Hart, famously shrewd and thoroughly versed in Chinese social dealings, reflected that a satisfactory plan meeting all three conditions—no unrest, no market disruption, and a living for the silkworm-raising families—might not be devised in ten years.

然则什么『只跟怡和一家订约』，额外佣金『不敢领』，无非是有名无实的『口惠』而已。
The promise to contract exclusively with Jardine Matheson and the refusal of extra commission were thus generous words with nothing behind them.

话虽如此，但仍能体谅胡雪岩的苦心，
Nevertheless, he could appreciate Hu’s care.

明明是办不到；或者说他不肯抹煞良心，不顾利害去做的事，
The thing was plainly impossible, or at least something Hu would not do at the cost of silencing his conscience and disregarding the consequences.

有他则才前半段的话，也就够了，
The first half of his reply would have been sufficient.

而还有后斗段『不过』以下的补充，是一种很尊重客人的表现，其意还是可感的。
Everything after “but” was an additional mark of respect for his guest, and the intention deserved gratitude.


因此，他深深点头，
Hart nodded deeply.

『雪翁真是明理的人，比京中那几位大老，高明得太多了。』
“You are a man who understands reason, Mr Xue—far more enlightened than those venerable gentlemen in Beijing.

他说∶『我总算也是不虚此行。』
My journey has not been in vain.”

『哪里，哪里！』胡雪岩答说∶『都象鹭翁这么样体谅，什么都好谈。』
“You are too kind,” Hu replied. “If everyone were as understanding as you, anything could be discussed.”

侍者上菜，暂时隔断了谈话。
A servant brought in another dish, briefly interrupting the conversation.

这道菜是古应春发明的，名为『炸虾饼』，
It was Gu’s invention, called Fried Shrimp Cakes.

外表看来象炸板鱼，上口才知味道大不相同，
At first sight it resembled fried flatfish; only on tasting it did one discover how different it was.

是用虾仁捣烂，和上鸡胸肉切碎的鸡绒，用豆腐衣包成长方块，沾了面包粉油炸，
Pounded shrimp was mixed with finely minced chicken breast, wrapped in tofu skin in rectangular parcels, coated in breadcrumbs, and fried.

做法仿佛杭州菜中的『炸响铃』，只是材料讲究得太多了。
The method resembled Hangzhou’s fried “ringing bells,” but the ingredients were far more luxurious.

赫德的牙齿不太好，所以特别赞赏这道菜。
Hart’s teeth were poor, and he particularly appreciated the dish.

这就有了个闲谈的话题，
This supplied a lighter topic.

赫德很坦率地说，他舍不得离开中国，口腹之欲是很大的一个原因。
He freely admitted that the pleasures of the table were a major reason for his reluctance to leave China.

『董大人常常请我吃饭。』他不胜神往地说∶『他家的厨子，在我看全世界第一！』
“Lord Dong often invites me to dinner,” he said longingly. “To my mind, his cooks are the finest in the world!”


『董大人？是指户部尚书董恂，在总理衙门』当家『；
Lord Dong was Dong Xun, Minister of Revenue, who managed affairs at the Zongli Yamen.

他是扬州人，善于应酬，用了两个出身于扬州』八大盐商『家的厨子，
A native of Yangzhou and an accomplished host, he employed two cooks who had worked in the households of that city’s Eight Great Salt Merchants.

都有能做』全羊席『、』全鳝席『的本事。
Both could prepare entire banquets made from mutton or from eel.

董恂应酬洋人，还有一套扬州盐商附庸风雅的花样，经常来个』投壶『、』射虎『的雅集。有时拿荷马、拜伦的诗，译成』古内『或』近体『。
In entertaining foreigners, Dong also adopted the salt merchants’ displays of cultivated taste: gatherings for pitch-pot or riddle guessing, sometimes even translating Homer and Byron into ancient-style or regulated Chinese verse.

醉心中国文化的赫德，跟他特别投缘。』
Hart, enamored of Chinese culture, felt a particular affinity with him.

白乐天在贵外杭州做的诗∶『未能抛得杭州去，一半勾留为此湖。
“Bai Letian wrote a poem while in your city of Hangzhou: ‘I cannot bring myself to leave Hangzhou; / Half of what holds me here is this lake.’

「我倒想改一改，」未能抛得中华去，一半勾留是此┅┅「』赫德有点抓瞎，
I should like to alter it: ‘I cannot bring myself to leave China; / Half of what holds me here is this—’” Hart was stuck.

搔着花白头发『此』了好一会，
Scratching his graying hair, he repeated “this” for some time.

突然双眉一掀，
Then his eyebrows suddenly rose.

『肴！
“Fare!

一半勾留是此肴。』
‘Half of what holds me here is this fare.’”


胡雪岩暗中惭愧，不知道他说的什么。
Hu felt secretly ashamed: he had no idea what Hart was talking about.

古应春倒听懂了一半，便即问道∶『听说赫大人常跟董大人一起做诗唱和，
Gu understood at least part of it and asked, “I hear that you and Lord Dong often exchange poems.

真是了不起！』
A remarkable accomplishment!”

『唱和还谈不到，
“I could hardly claim to exchange poems with him.

不过常在一起谈诗、谈词。』赫德又说∶『小犬是从小读汉文，
But we often discuss poetry and lyrics.” Hart continued, “My son has studied Chinese writing from childhood.

老师也是董大人荐来的；
Lord Dong recommended his teacher.

现在已经开手做八股了，将来想在科场里面讨个出身，
He has now begun composing eight-legged essays, and he hopes eventually to gain a degree through the examinations.

董大人答应替我代奏，
Lord Dong has promised to submit a memorial on his behalf.

不知道能准不能准？』
I do not know whether permission will be granted.”

这番话，胡雪岩是听明白了。
This Hu understood.

『洋娃娃』读汉文、做八股『已经是奇事；居然还想赴考，真是闻所未闻了。』
A foreign child reading Chinese and writing eight-legged essays was strange enough; that he should actually hope to sit the examinations was unheard of.

一定会准。『古应春在回答。』
“It will certainly be granted,” Gu answered.

难得贤乔梓这样子仰慕中华，
“Such admiration for China on the part of both father and son is rare.

皇上一定恩出格外。
His Majesty is sure to show exceptional favor.”

『』但原能准。『赫德忽然说道∶』我想起一件，
“I only hope so.” Hart suddenly said, “That reminds me of something.

趁现在谈，免得回头忘记。
Let me mention it now before I forget.

雪翁，有件事，想请你帮忙，
Mr Xue, there is a matter in which I should like your help.

怡和洋行派人到湖州去买丝，定洋已经付出去了；
Jardine Matheson sent someone to Huzhou to buy silk, and the deposits have been paid.

现在有个消息，说到新丝上市，不打算交货了。
Now we hear that when the new silk comes onto the market, the sellers do not intend to deliver.

将来真的这样子，恐怕彼此要破脸了。
If that really happens, I fear there will be an open rupture.”

『胡雪岩隐约听说过这回事，
Hu had heard something vaguely about the affair.

其中还牵涉到一个姓赵的』教民『，但不知其详，更不知谁是谁？
It involved a Christian convert named Zhao, but he knew neither the details nor exactly who was concerned.

不过赫德话中的分量，却是心里已经掂到了。
He had, however, already weighed the significance of Hart’s words.


『鹭翁，』他问∶『你要我怎么帮怡和的忙，请你先说明了，我来想想办法。』
“Mr Lubin,” he asked, “tell me first exactly how you would like me to help Jardine Matheson, and I shall see what can be done.”

『雪翁一言九鼎。
“Your word carries enormous weight.

既然怡和付了定洋，想请雪翁交代一声，能够如期交货。』
Since Jardine Matheson has paid the deposits, I should like you to give instructions that delivery be made on time.”

胡雪岩心想赫德奸滑无比，
Hu reflected that Hart was exceedingly cunning.

他说这话，可能是个陷井，
This might be a trap.

如果一口应承，他回到京里说一句，养蚕做丝的人家，都只凭胡某人一句许，他们的丝，说能卖就卖；说不能卖，谁也不敢卖。
If he agreed too readily, Hart could go back to Beijing and say that all the silkworm growers and silk producers acted solely on Hu’s word: when he said their silk could be sold, they sold it; when he said it could not, nobody dared sell.

那一来总理衙门就可能责成他为了敦睦邦交，一定要让怡和在乡下能直接买丝，
The Zongli Yamen might then order him, in the interests of friendly foreign relations, to ensure that Jardine Matheson could buy silk directly in the countryside.

这不是很大的难题。
What a difficulty that would create!

于是胡雪岩答说∶『一言九鼎这句话，万万不敢当。
“I cannot possibly accept your description of my influence,” he replied.

丝卖不卖，是人家的事，
“Whether people sell their silk is their own affair.

我姓胡的，不能干预；干预了他们亦未必肯听。
I have no right to interfere, and even if I did, they might not listen.

不过交易总要讲公道，
But business must be conducted fairly.

收了定洋不交货，说不过去；
To accept a deposit and then refuse delivery is indefensible.

再有困难，至少要还定洋。
Whatever the difficulty, at the very least the deposit must be returned.

鹭翁特为交代的事，我不能不尽心力去办。
Since you have specially asked me, I must do all I can.

这样，』他沉吟了一下说∶『听说其中牵涉到一个姓赵的，在教堂做事；
Let us put it this way”—he paused to consider—“I hear the affair involves a man named Zhao who works at a church.

我请应春兄下去，专门为鹭翁料理这件事。』
I shall send Brother Yingchun down expressly to deal with it for you.”

『承情之至。』
“I am most grateful.”

赫德拱拱手道谢。
Hart clasped his hands in thanks.


『请问赫大人，』古应春开口问道∶『能不能让怡和派个人跟我来接头。』
“May I ask,” Gu said, “whether Jardine Matheson could send someone to make contact with me?”

『怡和的东主艾力克就在杭州。』赫德用英语问道∶『你们不是很熟吗？』
“Ailike, the proprietor of Jardine Matheson, is here in Hangzhou,” Hart replied in English. “Are you not well acquainted?”

『是的，很熟。
“Yes, very well.

而且听说他也到杭州来了，不知道什么地方可以找得到他。』
I had heard he was here, but I did not know where to find him.”

『你到我这里来好了。』梅藤更插进来说。
“Come to my place,” Dr Meitenggeng put in.


『好。』古应春答说∶『我明天上午到广济医院去。』
“Very well,” Gu replied. “I shall call at Guangji Hospital tomorrow morning.”

送走了客人，胡雪岩跟古应春还有话要谈。
When the guests had gone, Hu still had things to discuss with Gu.

酒阑人散，
The drinking was over and the company dispersed.

加以胡家的内眷，都在灵隐陪侍老太太，少了二、三十个丫头，
Most of the women of the Hu household were at Lingyin attending on the old lady, taking twenty or thirty maids with them.

那份清静简直就有点寂寞了。
The resulting quiet was almost lonely.

『难得，难得！
“A rare opportunity, a rare opportunity!

今天倒真是我们弟兄挖挖心里的话的辰光。
Tonight we brothers can really speak from the heart.

应春！今天很暖和，
Yingchun, it is warm.

我们在外面坐。』
Let us sit outside.”

『外面』指的镜槛阁的前廊，因为要反映阁外的景致，造得格外宽大，
Outside meant the gallery in front of Jingjian Pavilion, built unusually broad to allow the scenery beyond to be reflected inside.

不过凭栏设座，却在西面一角，
The seats by the railing were in a corner to the west.

三月十一的月亮也很大了，
The moon of the eleventh day of the third month was already large.

清光斜照，两人脸上都是幽幽地一种肃散的神色。
Its clear light fell obliquely across the two men, giving their faces a faintly somber and withdrawn look.

『应春，』胡雪岩说∶『我这几天有个很怪的念头，
“Yingchun,” Hu said, “I have had a very strange thought these past few days.

俗语说「人在福中不知福」，
There is a saying: ‘A man surrounded by good fortune does not know how fortunate he is.’

这句话不晓得对不对？』
I wonder whether it is true.”

古应春无从回答，因为根本不知道他为什么有这样一个『很怪的念头』。
Gu could not answer, having no idea what had prompted this strange thought.


『我们老太太常说要惜福，
“My mother is always telling me to cherish my blessings.

福是怎么个惜法？』
But how does one cherish them?”

『这——』
“Well…”

古应春一面想，一面说∶『无非不要太过分的意思福不要享尽。』
Gu thought as he spoke. “I suppose it means not carrying things too far—not using up all one’s good fortune.”

『对，
“Quite.

不过那一来就根本谈不到享福了。
But then one can scarcely speak of enjoying good fortune at all.

你只要有这样子一个念头在心里，喝口茶、吃口饭都要想一想，是不是太过分？
Once that thought is in your mind, every sip of tea and every mouthful of rice makes you wonder whether you are going too far.

做人做到这个地步，还有啥味道？』
What pleasure is there in a life like that?”

古应春觉得他多少是诡辩，但驳不倒他，
Gu felt this was something of a sophistry, but he could not refute it.

只好发问∶『那末，小爷叔，你说应该怎么样呢？』
He could only ask, “What do you suggest, then, Uncle?”

『照我想，反倒是「人在福中不知福」，才真是在享福。』
“I think it is precisely the man who does not know how fortunate he is who truly enjoys his good fortune.”

『小爷叔，你的意思是一个人不必惜福？』
“Do you mean one need not cherish one’s blessings?”

『不是，我不是这个意思。
“No, that is not what I mean.

我是说∶享福归享福，发财归发财，
I mean that enjoying oneself is one thing and making money another.

两桩事情不要混在一起，
Do not confuse the two.

想发财要动脑，要享福就不必去管怎么样发财。』『小爷叔』，古应春笑道∶『你老人家的话，我愈听愈不懂。』
To make money, one must use one’s brains; to enjoy oneself, one must stop thinking about how to make money.” Gu laughed. “Uncle, the more I hear, the less I understand.”


胡雪岩付之一笑，
Hu smiled back.

『不但你愈听愈不懂，我也愈想愈不懂。』
“The more I think about it, the less I understand it myself.”

他急转直下地说∶『我们来想个发财的法子——不对，想个又能发财，又要享福的法子。』
Then he abruptly changed direction. “Let us think of a way to make money—no, a way both to make money and enjoy ourselves.”

古应春想了一会，笑了，
Gu considered for a while, then laughed.

『小爷叔，』他说，『法子倒有一个，只怕做不到；
“There is one way, but I doubt it can be done.

不过，就算能够做到了，恐怕小爷叔，你我也决不肯去做。』
Even if it could, I am afraid neither you nor I would agree to it.”

『说来听听，
“Tell me.

啥法子？』
What is it?”

『「嫖能倒贴，天下营生无双」。
“‘To visit courtesans and have them pay you—there is no business like it under Heaven.’

那就是又发财又享福的法子。』
That is a way to make money and enjoy yourself at once.”

『这也不见得！』
“Not necessarily!”

胡雪岩欲语不语，
Hu seemed about to say more, then stopped.

『好了，
“Enough.

我们还是实实惠惠谈生意。
Let us talk about practical business.

今天我冒冒失失答应赫德了，
I rashly gave Hart my word today.

你总要把我这个面子绷起来。』
You must make sure I do not lose face over it.”

『那还要说！
“That goes without saying.

小爷叔说出去了，我当然要做到，
Once you have spoken, I must see it through.

好在过了今天就没有我的事；
Fortunately, after today my duties here will be over.

明天上半天去看艾力克，下半天来开销我带来的那班人，
Tomorrow morning I shall see Ailike; in the afternoon I shall settle up with the people I brought.

后天就可以动身。』
The day after tomorrow I can leave.”

『要带什么人？』
“Whom will you take with you?”


古应春沉吟一会说∶『带一个丝行里的伙计就够了。
After a little thought, Gu said, “One clerk from the silk firm will be enough.

要人，好在湖州钱庄典当、丝行里都可以调动，
If I need anyone else, the banks, pawnshops, and silk firms in Huzhou can all provide men.

倒是有一样东西不可不带。』
But there is one thing I must take.”

『是啥？』
“What is it?”

『藩司衙门的公事——』
“An official dispatch from the provincial treasurer’s office.”

『为啥？』胡雪岩迫不及待地追问。
“Why?” Hu asked eagerly.

『这道公事给湖州府，要这样说∶风闻湖州教民赵某某仗势欺人，所作所为都是王法所不容，
“It must be addressed to the Huzhou prefecture and say something like this: reports have been received that a Christian convert in Huzhou, one Zhao, abuses his influence to bully others and acts in ways intolerable under the law.

特派古某某下去密查，湖州府应该格外予以方便。』
Gu is therefore being sent specially to investigate in secret, and the prefecture is to give him every assistance.”

『古某某』是古应春自称。
By “Gu,” he meant himself.

他捐了个候补通判的职衔，又在吏部花了钱，分发到浙江。
He had purchased the title of expectant assistant prefect and paid further money at the Ministry of Personnel to be assigned to Zhejiang.

实际上他不想做官，又不想当差，
He did not actually wish to hold office or take an official assignment.

只是有了这样一个头衔，有许多方便；甚至于还可以检便宜，
Such a title merely afforded many conveniences and could even bring opportunities for personal advantage.

这时候就是用得到的时候了。
This was one occasion when it would prove useful.


『我有了这个奉宪命查案的身分，就可以跟赵某人讲斤头了，
“With the status of an investigator acting under the provincial authority’s orders, I can bargain with Zhao.

斤头谈不拢，我再到湖州府去报文，也还不迟。』
If we cannot come to terms, it will still be time enough to present my credentials to the Huzhou prefecture.”

『这个法子不坏！』胡雪岩说∶『明天上午我们一起去见德晓峰。』
“Not a bad plan,” Hu said. “Let us call on De Xiaofeng together tomorrow morning.”

『上午我约好要去看艾力克，
“I have already arranged to see Ailike in the morning.

是不是下午看德藩台？』
Could we call on Treasurer De in the afternoon?”

『只怕公事当天赶不及。』胡雪岩紧接着，『晚一天动身也不要紧。』
“I am only afraid they may not get the dispatch ready that same day.” Hu added at once, “There is no harm in leaving a day later.”

『好，
“Very well.

那就准定后天动身。』
Then we shall definitely leave the day after tomorrow.”


『应春，』胡雪岩换了个话题，『你明天见了艾力克，要问他要帐，
Hu changed the subject. “Yingchun, when you see Ailike tomorrow, ask for his accounts.

他到底放出去多少定洋，
Exactly how much has he advanced in deposits?

放给什么人，数目多少，
To whom, and in what amounts?

一定要他开个花名册。』
He must provide an itemized list of names.”

『这——』
“Well…”

古应春迟疑着，
Gu hesitated.

『只怕他开不出来，
“He may not be able to do that.

帐都在他洋行里。』
His accounts are at the firm.”

『不要紧，
“Never mind.

等他回上海再开。
He can prepare it when he returns to Shanghai.

你告诉他，只要花名册开来，查过没有花帐，一定如数照付，
Tell him that once the list arrives, and we have checked that there are no fictitious entries, we shall pay the whole amount.

叫他放心好了。』
He need not worry.”

『小爷叔，』古应春郑重警告∶『这样做法很危险。』
“Uncle,” Gu warned gravely, “that is dangerous.”

『你是说风险？』
“You mean there is a risk?

胡雪岩问∶『我们不背风险，叫哪个来背？』
If we do not bear it, who will?”

古应春想了一说∶『既然如此，何不索性先把款子付了给他，也买个漂亮。』
Gu thought for a moment. “In that case, why not pay him the money first and make a handsome gesture of it?”

『我正是这个意思，
“That is precisely my intention.

也不光是买个漂亮，
And it is not merely a handsome gesture.

我是要叫他知难而退；
I want him to see the difficulties and retreat.

而且这一来，他的那班客户都转到我手里来了。』『还是小爷叔厉害。』古应春笑道∶『我是一点都没有想到。』
Besides, all his suppliers will then pass into my hands.” Gu laughed. “You are the shrewder man, Uncle. That never occurred to me.”

谈到这里，只见瑞香翩然而至，问宵夜的心开在何处？
At this point Ruixiang appeared, asking where the late supper should be served.

胡雪岩交代∶『就开到这里来！』
“Bring it here,” Hu instructed.

古应春根本就吃不下宵夜，而且也有些疲累，
Gu had no appetite at all and was rather tired.

很想早点归寝，但仿佛这一下会辜负瑞香的一番殷勤之意，怕她会觉得扫兴，
He wanted to go to bed early, but felt that leaving now would slight Ruixiang’s attentiveness and disappoint her.

所以仍旧留了下来。
So he stayed.


不过一开了来，他倒又有食欲了，
Once supper appeared, his appetite returned.

因为宵夜的只是极薄的香粳米粥，六样粥菜，
There was only a very thin gruel of fragrant rice and six accompaniments.

除了醉蟹以外，其他都是凉拌笋尖之类的素肴。
Apart from drunken crab, all were vegetable dishes such as cold dressed bamboo-shoot tips.

连日饱沃肥甘，正思清淡食物，所以停滞的胃口又开了。
After days of rich eating, he had been longing for something plain, and his sluggish appetite revived.

盛粥之先，瑞香问道∶『古老爷要不要来杯酒？』
Before serving the gruel, Ruixiang asked, “Would you like a glass of wine, Mr Gu?”

『好啊！』
“Gladly.

古应春欣然答说∶『我要杯白兰地。』
I should like some brandy.”

『有我们太太用人参泡的白兰地，
“We have some brandy in which Madam has steeped ginseng.

我去拿。』
I shall fetch it.”

说着，先盛了两碗粥，然后去取来浸泡在水晶瓶里的药酒，
She filled two bowls of gruel first, then brought the medicinal liquor in a crystal decanter.

取来的水晶杯也不错，是巨腹矮脚，用来喝白兰地的酒杯。
The crystal glass she chose was right too: a large-bowled, short-stemmed glass designed for brandy.

这就使得古应春想到上个月在家请客，请的法国的一个家有酒窑的巨商，
It reminded Gu of a dinner at home the month before for a wealthy French merchant whose family owned wine cellars.

饭前酒、饭后酒，什么菜配红酒，什么菜配白酒，都有讲究。
There were rules for everything: drinks before and after the meal, red wine with one dish and white with another.

古应春原有全套的酒杯，但女仆不懂这套规矩，预备得不周全；
Gu possessed a full set of glasses, but the maidservants did not understand the rules and had not prepared them properly.

七姑奶奶不知道怎么知道了，在床空着急。
Somehow the Seventh Lady learned of it and could only fret helplessly in bed.

如果有瑞香在，她便可以不必操心了。
With Ruixiang there, she would not have to worry.

这样想着，不自觉抬头去看瑞香，脸上自然是含着笑意∶瑞香正在斟酒，不曾发觉，胡雪岩冷眼旁观，却看得很清楚。
Thinking this, Gu unconsciously looked up at Ruixiang with a smile. She was pouring the brandy and did not notice, but Hu, watching quietly, saw it all.

『湘阴四月里要出巡，
“Xiangyin will be making an inspection tour in the fourth month,” he said.

上海的制造局是一定要去看的，那时候我当然要去等他。
“He is certain to visit the arsenal in Shanghai, and naturally I must be there to wait for him.

应春，我想等老太太的生日一过，让罗四姐先去看七姐；
Yingchun, once Mother’s birthday is over, I think I shall have Sister Luo go ahead to see Sister Seven.

到时候我再跟他换班，
Then I can take her place when I arrive.

那就两头都顾到了。
That way we shall attend to both ends.

你看好不好？』
What do you think?”


『怎么不好？』古应春答说∶『这回罗四姐去，就住在我那里好了。』
“What could be better?” Gu replied. “Sister Luo must stay at my house on this visit.”
