胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement
第二章（本章第3部分，共6部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


『那太好了。』古应春很高兴地说∶『既然替湖州府帮忙办洋务，教会里的情形一定熟悉，
“Excellent!” Gu Yingchun said with pleasure. “If he helps the prefecture with foreign affairs, he must know the church’s circumstances well.

赵宝禄不能不买他的帐。
Zhao Baolu will have to take him seriously.

悟心，你这个忙帮得大了。』
Wuxin, you have done us a great service.”

到了湖州城里，问清楚赵宝禄的教堂在何处，就在附近挑个清静之处泊舟。
On reaching Huzhou, they asked where Zhao’s church was and chose a quiet mooring nearby.

古应春与雷桂卿带着一个跟班上岸；悟心在船上等，
Gu and Lei Guiqing went ashore with an attendant, while Wuxin waited aboard.

她带来一个食盒，现成的素菜，在船上热一下便可食用，
She had brought a food box containing vegetarian dishes that needed only heating.

正整治好了尚未动箸，不道古应春一行已经回船了。
Everything was ready, but she had not yet lifted her chopsticks when the men returned.

『怎么这么快？』
“Back so soon?”

『事情很顺利。
“Things went smoothly.

不过太顺利了。』
Too smoothly.”

『这是怎么说？』悟心又说∶『我总当你们办完事下馆子，我管我自己吃饭了，
“What do you mean?” Wuxin added, “I assumed you would eat at a restaurant after finishing your business, so I was going to eat by myself.

现在看样子，你们也还没有吃，
It seems you have not eaten either.

要不要先将就将就？』
Will you make do with this?”

『我们也还有点船菜，
“We have some food aboard too.

不必再上岸了。
There is no need to go ashore again.

我要把经过情形告诉你，看有什么法子，不让赵宝禄耍花样。』
I must tell you what happened and see whether you can think of a way to prevent Zhao Baolu from playing tricks.”

原来古应春到得教堂，见到赵宝禄，道明来意，
At the church, Gu had found Zhao and explained his purpose.

原以为他必有一番支吾，哪知他绝口否认有任何耍赖的企图。
He had expected evasions, but Zhao flatly denied any intention of defaulting.

『做人要讲信用，对洋人尤其重要，
“A man must keep his word, especially when dealing with foreigners.

我吃了多年的教，当然很明白这层道理。
I have lived within the church for many years and naturally understand that.

两位请放心，
Rest assured, gentlemen.

我收了怡和洋行的定洋，丝也定好了，
I received Jardine Matheson’s deposits, and the silk has been contracted for.

到时候大家照约行家，
When the time comes, everyone will fulfill his agreement.

决无差错。』
Nothing will go wrong.”

『可是，』古应春探询似地说∶『听说赵先生跟教友之间，有些瓜葛？』
“But,” Gu asked tentatively, “I hear there is some difficulty between you and your fellow Christians?”


『什么瓜葛？』
“What difficulty?”

赵宝禄不待古应春回答，自己又说∶『无非说我逼教友捐献。
Without waiting for an answer, Zhao went on, “Merely the claim that I force believers to make donations.

那要自愿，
Donations must be voluntary.

他不肯我不好抢他的；
If someone refuses, I cannot rob him.

总而言之，到时候如果出了差错，两位再来问我，
In short, if anything goes wrong when the time comes, come and question me then.

现在时候还早。』
It is too early now.”

明知道他是敷衍，也明知他将来会耍赖，
Gu knew he was being put off and was equally certain that Zhao would evade his obligations later.

但却什么劲都用不上，
Yet there was nothing he could bring to bear on him.

真叫无可奈何。
It was maddeningly helpless.

古应春从未遇到过这样的对手，所以神色之间，颇为沮丧。
Never having encountered such an opponent, Gu looked thoroughly discouraged.

『你不要烦恼！』悟心劝慰着说∶『一定有办法，
“Do not worry,” Wuxin comforted him. “There is bound to be a way.

你先吃完了饭再说。』
Finish your meal first.”

古应春胃口不开，但经不住悟心殷殷相劝，
Gu had no appetite, but could not resist her repeated urging.

便拿茶泡了饭，就着悟心带来的麻辣油焖笋，匆匆吞了一碗；
He poured tea over his rice and hurriedly swallowed a bowl with the spicy oil-braised bamboo shoots she had brought.

雷桂卿吃得也不多，
Lei ate little too.

两个都搁下筷子，看悟心捏着三镶乌木筷，慢慢在饭中拣稗子，
Both laid down their chopsticks and watched Wuxin slowly pick barnyard-grass grains from her rice with ebony chopsticks bearing three inlays.

拣好半天才吃一口。
Only after a long search would she take a mouthful.


『这米不好，是船老大在这里买的。』古应春歉意地说∶『早知道，自己带米来了』。
“The rice is poor,” Gu said apologetically. “The boatman bought it here. Had I known, I should have brought some.”

悟心也省悟了，
Wuxin realized that she was keeping them waiting.

『对不起，对不起。』
“Forgive me.

她说∶『我吃得慢，
I eat slowly.

两位不必陪我，
Please do not feel obliged to sit with me.

请宽坐用茶。』
Make yourselves comfortable and have some tea.”

雷桂卿却舍不得走，
Lei was reluctant to leave.

尤其是悟心垂着眼皮注视碗中时，是个恣意贪看的好机会，
With her eyes lowered over her bowl, he could gaze at her to his heart’s content.

所以接口说道∶『不要紧，不要紧，
“Not at all, not at all,” he said.

你尽管慢用。』
“Take your time.”

悟心嫣然一笑，对她的饭不再多挑剔，
Wuxin gave a lovely smile and ceased to be so particular about the rice.

吃得就快了。
Her meal went more quickly.


等小玉来收拾了桌子，水也开了。
By the time Xiaoyu had cleared the table, the water was boiling.

沏上一壶茶来，扑鼻一股杏子香，雷桂卿少不得又要动问了。
The tea she brewed gave off a fragrance of apricots, prompting another question from Lei.

『那没有什么诀窍。』悟心答说∶『挑没有熟的杏子，摘下来拿皮纸包好，放在茶叶罐里，
“There is no great secret,” Wuxin answered. “Pick unripe apricots, wrap them in bark paper, and put them in the tea canister.

隔两天便有香味了。
After two days the tea will be fragrant.

不但杏子，别的果子，也可以如法炮制。』
Other fruit can be used in the same way.”

『悟心师太，』雷桂卿笑道∶『你真会享清福。』
“Mistress Wuxin,” Lei said, laughing, “you certainly know how to enjoy a peaceful life.”

悟心笑笑不作声，转脸问古应春∶『你的心事想得怎么样了？』
She smiled without answering, then turned to Gu. “Have you made any progress with what is troubling you?”

古应春确是在想心事，
He had indeed been thinking.

他带着藩司衙门的公文，可以去看湖州知府，请求协助；
With the provincial treasurer’s dispatch, he could call on the Huzhou prefect and ask for assistance.

但如传了赵宝禄到案，他仍旧是这套说法，那就不但于事无补，
But if Zhao were summoned and repeated the same arguments, nothing would be gained.

而且还落一个仗势欺人的名声，
Worse, Gu would acquire a reputation for using official influence to bully others.

太划不来了。
It would scarcely be worth it.


等他说了心事，悟心把脸又转了过去∶『雷先生，要托你办件事。』
When he had explained, Wuxin turned back to Lei. “Mr Lei, I must ask you to do something.”

『是、是。』雷桂卿一叠连声地答应，
“Yes, yes,” he answered eagerly.

『你说，你说。』
“Tell me.”

『我写个地址，
“I shall give you an address.

请你去找一位杨师爷；
Go and find a certain Secretary Yang.

见了面，说我请他来一趟，有事求他。』悟心又加一句∶『他是乌程县的刑名师爷。』
Tell him I should like him to come here; there is something I must ask of him.” She added, “He advises the Wucheng magistrate on criminal and legal affairs.”

做州县官，至少要请两个幕友，一个管刑名、一个管钱谷，
A prefect or magistrate needed at least two private secretaries: one for criminal and legal matters, the other for revenue and grain.

权柄极大。
Their influence was considerable.

请乌程县的刑名师爷来料理此案，不怕赵宝禄不就范。
With Wucheng’s legal secretary brought in to manage the affair, Zhao could surely be made to comply.

雷桂卿很高兴地说∶『悟心师太，你真有办法！
Lei was delighted. “Mistress Wuxin, you truly know how to get things done!

把这位杨师爷请了来对付赵宝禄，比什么都管用。』
Bringing Secretary Yang to deal with Zhao will be more effective than anything.”

『也不见得，
“Not necessarily.

等请来了再商量。』
Let us discuss it once he is here.”

于是悟心口述地址，请古应春写了下来，
She dictated the address and had Gu write it down.

船老大上岸雇来一顶轿子，将欣欣然的雷桂卿抬走了。
The boatman hired a sedan ashore, and Lei was carried off in high spirits.


『你要不要去睡个午觉？』悟心说道∶『雷先生要好半天才会回来。』
“Would you like an afternoon nap?” Wuxin asked. “Mr Lei will not be back for some time.”

『怎么？
“Why?

那杨师爷住得很远，是不是？』
Does Secretary Yang live far away?”

『不但住得远，
“Not only far away.

而且要去两个地方。』
He will have to go to two places.”

『为什么？』
“Why?”

悟心诡秘地一笑说道∶『这位雷先生，心思有点歪，
Wuxin gave a mysterious smile. “This Mr Lei’s thoughts stray a little.

我要他吃点小苦头。』
I mean to make him suffer a small penalty.”

『什么苦头？』
“What penalty?”

古应春有点不安，
Gu was uneasy.

『是我的朋友，
“He is my friend.

弄得他惨兮兮，他会骂我。』
If you make him miserable, he will blame me.”

『他根本不会晓得，是我故意罚他。』
“He will never know I am punishing him deliberately.”

原来这杨师爷住在县衙门，但另外租了一处房子，作为私下接头讼事之用，
Secretary Yang lived at the county yamen, but had rented another house for private consultations about lawsuits.

为了避人耳目，房子租在很荒僻的地方，
To avoid attention, he had chosen a very secluded place.

又因为荒僻之故，养了一条很凶的狗。
Its isolation in turn made it necessary to keep a fierce dog.

雷桂卿找上门去，一定会扑空，而且会受惊。
Lei would certainly fail to find him there and would get a fright into the bargain.


『怎么会扑空呢？
“Why will he fail to find him?”

悟心解释∶』除非杨师爷自己关照，约在哪里见面，不然他就是在那里，下人也会说不在，有事到衙门去接头。
“Unless Secretary Yang himself has arranged where to meet,” Wuxin explained, “even if he is there, the servants will say he is out and direct callers to the yamen.”

『』怎么会倒在其次，
“Missing him is a lesser matter.

让狗咬了怎么办？
What if the dog bites Lei?”

『『不会！
“It will not.

那条狗是教好了的，
The dog is trained.

来势汹汹把人吓走了就好了，从不咬人。』
It rushes at people fiercely and frightens them away, but never bites.”

听这一说，古应春才放下心来；
Only then was Gu reassured.

他知道悟心有午睡的习惯，便即说道∶『我倒不困，
Knowing that Wuxin habitually slept after lunch, he said, “I am not sleepy.

你去打个中觉。』
You should take your nap.”

『好！』
“Very well.

悟心问说∶『哪张是你的铺？』
Which berth is yours?”

『跟我来。』
“Come with me.”


后舱一张大铺，中间用红木隔成两个铺位，
A broad sleeping platform in the aft cabin was divided into two berths by a rosewood partition.

上铺洋式床垫，软硬适度，
Western mattresses, neither too soft nor too hard, covered it.

悟心用手揿一揿床垫，又看一看周围的陈设，
Wuxin pressed one with her hand and surveyed the furnishings.

不由得赞叹∶『财神家的东西，到底不同。』
“Things belonging to the God of Wealth’s household really are different!” she exclaimed.

『这面是我的铺。』
“Mine is this side.”

古应春指着左面说∶『你睡吧，
Gu pointed to the left. “Lie down.

我在外面。
I shall be outside.

有事拉这根绳子。』
Pull this cord if you need anything.”

悟羽将一根红弦绳一拉，前舱的银铃琅琅作响；
Wuxin tugged a red cord, and a silver bell rang in the forward cabin.

小玉恰好进前舱，闻声寻来，一看亦有惊异之色。
Xiaoyu, who had just entered it, followed the sound and looked equally impressed.

『真讲究！』
“How beautifully arranged!”

小玉抚摸着红木～＊子说∶『是可以移动的。』
She ran a hand over the rosewood partition. “It can be moved.”

『索性把它推了过去。』古应春说∶『一人个睡也宽敞些。』
“Push it aside altogether,” Gu said. “There will be more room for one person to sleep.”

小玉便依言将红木～＊子推到一边。古应春也退了出去，在中舱喝茶闲眺，
Xiaoyu did so, and Gu withdrew to the middle cabin to drink tea and look out at the scenery.

心里在盘算，
He considered their plans.

杨师爷来了，如果谈得顺利，还来得及回庵；
If the discussion with Secretary Yang went well, there would still be time to return to the retreat.

倘或需要从长计议，是回庵去谈呢？还是一直谈下去，夜深了上岸觅客栈投宿，让悟心师徒住在船上。
If more thought was needed, should they go back there to continue it, or talk on aboard, however late it grew? They could then find an inn ashore and leave the boat to Wuxin and Xiaoyu.

转念未定，听得帘挂钩响动，
Before he decided, he heard the curtain rings move.

是小玉出来了，
Xiaoyu emerged.

『古老爷，』她说，『你请进去吧，
“Mr Gu, please come in.

我师父有事情商量。』
Teacher wishes to discuss something.”

到得后舱，只见悟心在他的铺位上和衣侧卧，身上半盖着一条绣花丝被，
In the aft cabin, Wuxin lay on her side in his berth, fully clothed, with an embroidered silk quilt half covering her.

长发纷披，遮盖了大半个枕头；
Her long hair spread over most of the pillow.

一手支颐，袖子褪落到肘弯，奇南香手串的香味，俞发馥郁了。
One hand supported her chin; the sleeve had slipped down to her elbow, and the fragrance of her agarwood prayer beads seemed richer than ever.

『你有事？』
“What is it?”

古应春在这一面铺前的一张红木骨牌凳上坐了下来。
Gu sat on a rosewood stool beside the other berth.


『杨师爷很晚才回来。』悟心说道∶『恐怕要留他吃饭，
“Secretary Yang will not be back until quite late,” Wuxin said. “We may have to ask him to dinner.

似乎要预备预备。』
Perhaps something should be prepared.”

『菜倒是有。』
“There is food.

古应春说，『船家一早就上岸去买了菜，
The boatman went ashore to buy it early this morning.

只以为中午是在城里吃了，你又带了素菜来，所以没有弄出来。
We thought we should lunch in the city, and you brought vegetarian dishes as well, so it was not served.

你闻！』
Smell that!”

悟心闻到了，是火腿炖鸡的香味，
She caught the aroma of chicken stewing with ham.

『你引我动凡心了。』她笑着又说∶『酒呢？』
“You are tempting me back to worldly desires,” she said with a laugh. “And wine?”

『那更是现成，
“Ready too.

一坛花雕是上船以后才开的。
We opened a jar of huadiao after coming aboard.

我还有白葡萄酒，你也可以喝。』
I have white wine as well, which you can drink.

古应春又说∶『倒是有件事得早早预备，今晚上你跟小玉睡在船上，我跟雷桂卿住客栈，
There is one thing we ought to arrange early: you and Xiaoyu can sleep aboard tonight while Lei and I stay at an inn.

得早一点去定妥当了它。』
I had better reserve the rooms.”

『不！』
“No.

悟心说道∶『睡在船上不妥当了，
It would not be proper for me to sleep here.

我还是回庵；不过船家多吃一趟辛苦。』
I shall return to the retreat, even if it means another journey for the boatmen.”

『那没有什么。
“That is no trouble.

好了，说妥当了，
Then it is settled.

你睡吧！』
Sleep now.”


『我还不困，
“I am not sleepy yet.

陪我谈谈。』
Stay and talk.”

说着，悟心拍拍空铺位，示意他睡下来。
Wuxin patted the empty berth, inviting him to lie down.

古应春有些踌躇，但终于决定考验自己的定力，
Gu hesitated, then decided to test his self-command.

在雷桂卿的铺位横倒，脸对脸不到一尺的距离。
He stretched out in Lei’s berth, their faces less than a chi apart.

『古太太的病怎么样？
“How is Mrs Gu?

好点了没有？』
Is she any better?”

『还是那样子。
“Much the same.

总归是带病延年了。』
She can only nurse her illness and hope to live on.”

『那末，你呢？』悟心幽幽地说∶『没有一个人在身边，也不方便。』
“And you?” Wuxin asked softly. “It must be inconvenient, with no one beside you.”

古应春想把瑞香的事告诉她，
Gu thought of telling her about Ruixiang.

转念一想，这一来悟心一定寻根究底，追问不休，
But she would be sure to pursue every detail and question him endlessly.

不如不提为妙。
Better not mention it.


『也没有什么不便。
“It is no great inconvenience.

多一事不如少一事。』
One less complication is better than one more.”

『什么事都好省，这件事省不得，除非——』悟心忽然笑了起来。
“You may dispense with anything else, but not this—unless—” Wuxin suddenly laughed.

这一笑实在诡秘，古应春忍不住问∶『话说半句，无缘无故发笑，
There was something so mysterious in the laugh that Gu could not help asking, “You leave your sentence unfinished and laugh for no reason.

是什么花样？
What game is this?

除非什么？』
Unless what?”

『除非你也看破红尘，出家当了和尚，
“Unless you too renounce the world and become a monk.

那件事才可以省，
Only then can you dispense with that need.

不然是省不了的。』
Otherwise you cannot.”

『这话也没有啥好笑啊！』
“There is nothing so funny about that.”

『我笑是笑我自己。』
“I am laughing at myself.”


『在谈我，
“We are discussing me.

何以忽然笑你自己。』
Why suddenly laugh at yourself?”

古应春口滑，想不说的话。还是说了∶『总与我有关吧？』
Gu’s tongue slipped into the very question he had meant not to ask. “It must have something to do with me.”

『不错，与你有关。
“It does.

我在想，你如果出家做了和尚，不晓得是怎么个样子？
I was imagining what you would look like as a monk.

想想就好笑了。』
The thought made me laugh.”

『我要出家，也做头陀，同你一样。』
“If I renounced the world, I would become a toutuo—like you.”

『啥叫头陀？』
“What is a toutuo?”

『亏你还算出家，
“And you call yourself a religious person!

连头陀都不懂。』
You do not even know what a toutuo is.

古应春答说∶『出家而没有剃发，带发修行的叫做头陀；
Someone who renounces the world without shaving his head and practices with his hair intact is called a toutuo.

岂不是跟你一样。』
Is that not just like you?”

『喔，我懂了，
“Oh, I see.

就是满头乱七八糟的头发，弄个铜环，把它箍住，象武松的那种打扮？』
A head of unruly hair held in place with a copper band, dressed like Wu Song?”

『就是。』
“Exactly.”

『那叫「行者」！不叫头陀，
“That is called a xingzhe, not a toutuo!

我那里有本《释氏要贤》说得清清楚楚。』
My copy of Shishi Yaoxian makes it perfectly clear.”

原来她是懂的，有意相谑，
So she had known all along and was deliberately teasing him.

这正是悟心的本性；
It was typical of Wuxin.

古应春苦笑着叹了口气，无话可说。
Gu gave a rueful smile and sighed, with nothing to say.


『应春，我们真希望你是出家的行者。』
“Yingchun, I really wish you were a xingzhe who had renounced the world.”

『为什么？』
“Why?”

『那一来，你不是一个人了吗？』
“Then you would be alone, would you not?”

古应春心一跳，故意问说∶『一个人又怎么样了呢？』
Gu’s heart gave a leap, but he deliberately asked, “And what if I were?”

『你不懂？』
“Do you not understand?”

『我真不懂。』
“I truly do not.”

『不跟你说了。』
“Then I shall say no more.”

悟心突然一翻身，背对古应春。
Wuxin suddenly turned her back on him.

古应春心想，这就是考验自己定力的时候了，
This, Gu thought, was the moment to test his resolve.

心猿意马地几次想伸手去扳她的身子，却始终迟疑不定。
His thoughts raced. Several times he wanted to reach out and turn her toward him, but hesitation held him back.

终于忍不住要伸手了，
At last he could resist no longer.

而且手已快碰到悟心的身子了，突然听得扑通一声，是重物落水的声音，古应春一惊缩手，

I shall not stay to meditate with you.”

“Of course.
