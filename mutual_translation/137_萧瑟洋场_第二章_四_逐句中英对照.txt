胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement
第二章（本章第4部分，共6部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


杨师爷沉吟了一回说道∶『办法是有，不过要按部就班，一步一步都要走到。
Secretary Yang considered for a while. “There is a way, but we must proceed in order and omit none of the steps.

赵宝禄有没有「牙帖」？』
Does Zhao Baolu have a broker’s license?”

交易的介绍人，古称『驵侩』，
Intermediaries in trade had anciently been called zangkui.

后汉与四夷通商，在边境设立『互市』；
When the Later Han traded with the peoples beyond its frontiers, mutual markets were established along the borders.

到唐朝，『互市』扩大，且由边境延伸到长安，特设『互市监』，掌理其事，
Under the Tang, these expanded and extended as far as Chang’an, with special market supervisors to administer them.

『互市』中有些『互郎』，即是『驵侩』，
Some intermediaries were called hulang.

互市之物，孰贵孰贱，孰重孰轻，只凭他一句话，因而得以操纵其间，
Their word determined which goods were dear or cheap, weighty or insignificant, allowing them to manipulate transactions.

是个很容易发财的行业，不过第一、须通番语；第二、要跟互市监拉得上关系。
It was an easy occupation in which to grow rich, but it required both a command of foreign languages and connections with the market supervisors.

所以胡人当互郎的很多，如安禄山就是。
Many foreigners therefore became hulang; An Lushan was one.

不过胡人写汉字，笔划不真切，互字不知如何写成『牙』字，
Their Chinese handwriting, however, was imprecise, and somehow the character hu came to be written as the similar-looking ya.

以论传论，称为『牙郎』；后世简称为『牙』，
The error spread, and the intermediaries became known as yalang, later shortened to ya.

一个字叫起来不便，就加一个字，名之为『牙行』。
Since a single syllable was awkward to use, another character was added, giving yahang, or brokerage.

『牙行』是没本钱生意，
It was a business that required no capital.

黑道中人手里握一杆秤，在他的地盘上强买强卖，两面抽佣，
A man from the underworld could seize a pair of scales and force people within his territory to buy and sell through him, taking commission from both sides.

甚至于右手买进、左手卖出，大『戴帽子』。
He might even buy with one hand and sell with the other, piling on an enormous markup.

所以有句南北通行的谚语∶『车船店脚牙，无罪也该杀』，
Hence a proverb common to north and south: “Carters, boatmen, inn servants, porters, and brokers deserve death even without a crime.”

车案、船老大、店小二、脚案，无非欺侮过往的陌生旅客；只有牙行欺侮的不是旅客而是本地人。
The first four merely preyed on strangers passing through; brokers preyed on the local people themselves.


当然也有适应需要，为买卖双方促成交易、收取定额佣金的正式牙行，
There were, of course, legitimate brokers who met a real need, bringing buyers and sellers together for a fixed commission.

那要官府立案，取得户部或者本省藩司衙门所发的执照，称为『牙帖』，方能从事这个行当。
They had to register with the authorities and obtain a license from the Ministry of Revenue or the provincial treasurer before entering the trade.

赵宝禄不过凭借教会势力，私下在做牙行，
Zhao, however, merely relied on the church’s influence to act as a broker privately.

古应春推测他是不可能领有牙帖的。
Gu supposed it unlikely that he held a license.

『我想他大概也不会有。』杨师爷说∶『怡和洋行想要有保障，要写个禀帖来。
“I suspect he has none either,” Yang said. “If Jardine Matheson wants security, it must submit a petition.

县衙门把赵宝禄传来，问他有没有这回事？
The county yamen can summon Zhao and ask whether the matter is as stated.

他说「有」；好，叫他象牙帖出来看看。
If he says yes, very well: have him produce his broker’s license.

没有牙帖，先就罚他。』
If he has none, begin by fining him.”

『罚过以后呢？』
“And after the fine?”

『要他具结，将来照约行事。』
“Make him give a written undertaking to fulfill the contract.

杨师爷说∶『这是怡和跟他的事，
That concerns him and Jardine Matheson.

将来要打官司，怡和一定赢。』
If a lawsuit follows, the firm is certain to win.”

『赢是赢了，就是留下刚才所说的，不怕讨债的的凶，只怕欠债的穷，
“It may win, but we still face what you said just now: the difficulty is not a fierce creditor but a poor debtor.

他如果既交不出丝，又还不出定洋，莫非封他的教堂？』
If he can neither deliver silk nor repay the deposit, can we seal up his church?”


『虽不能封他的教堂，可以要他交保。
“We cannot seal up the church, but we can require him to provide surety.

那时如果受骗上当的人，进状子告他，就可以办他个「诈伪取财」的罪名。』杨师爷又说∶『总而言之，办法有的是。
Then, if the people he has deceived file complaints, he can be prosecuted for obtaining money by fraud.” Yang continued, “In short, there are plenty of ways.

不过「凡事豫则立」；
But preparation is the foundation of success.

刑名上有所谓「抢原告」，就是要抢先一步，防患未然。
In legal practice there is what we call ‘seizing the plaintiff’s position’: act first and forestall trouble.

你老兄照我的话去做，先叫怡和洋行写禀帖来，
Follow my advice and have Jardine Matheson write its petition.

这是最要紧的一着。』
That is the essential first move.”

『是，是！
“Yes, yes.

多承指点，以后还要请多帮忙。』
I am grateful for your guidance and shall need your help again.”

正事谈得告一段落，酒也差不多了。
The business discussion had reached a stopping point, and they had drunk enough.

杨师爷知道悟心还要赶回庵去，所以不耽误她的工夫，
Knowing that Wuxin still had to return to the retreat, Yang did not detain her.

吃完饭立即告辞；
He took his leave immediately after dinner.

古应春包了个大红包犒赏他的仆从，看着杨师爷上了轿，吩咐解缆回南浔。
Gu prepared a generous gratuity envelope for his attendants, watched him into his sedan, and ordered the boat to cast off for Nanxun.


归寝已是三更时分，
It was the third watch when they went to bed.

雷桂卿头一着枕，突然猛吸鼻子，发出『嗤，嗤』的响声，
The moment his head touched the pillow, Lei began sniffing noisily.

古应春不由得诧异。
Gu was puzzled.

『怎么？』
“What is it?

他问∶『有什么不对？』
Is something wrong?”

我枕头上有气味。
“There is a smell on my pillow.”

『『气味？』古应春更觉不解，
“A smell?

『什么气味？』
What kind?”

『是香气。』
“A fragrance.

雷桂卿说，『好象悟心头发上的香气。
It seems like the scent of Wuxin’s hair.

你没有闻见？』
Did you not notice it?”

『我的鼻子没有你灵。』
“My nose is not as keen as yours.”

古应春心想，这件事实在奇怪，
Gu thought this very strange.

悟心并没有用他的枕头，
Wuxin had not used Lei’s pillow.

何以会沾染香味？
How could her fragrance have reached it?

这样想着，不免侧脸去看，一看看出蹊跷来了。雷桂卿的枕头上，有一根长长的青丝，可以断定是悟心的头发，
Turning to look, he discovered something curious: a long black hair lay on the pillow, unquestionably hers.

然则她真的用过雷桂卿的枕头？
Had she really used it after all?

『不对！』雷桂卿突然又喊∶『这不是我的枕头，
“No!” Lei suddenly exclaimed. “This is not my pillow.

是你的。』
It is yours.”

他仰起身子说∶『我记得很清楚，
He raised himself. “I remember perfectly.

这对鸳鸯枕，你绣的花样的鸳，我的是鸯，
Of these two mandarin-duck pillows, yours had the male embroidered on it and mine the female.

现在换过了。』
They have been exchanged.”

古应春恍然大悟，点点头说∶『不错，换过了。
Gu understood at once and nodded. “Yes, they have.

你知道不知道，是哪个换的？』
Do you know who exchanged them?”


『莫非是悟心？』
“Could it have been Wuxin?”

『不错，一定是她。
“Certainly.

她有打中觉的习惯；
She always naps after lunch.

原来睡的是我的枕头，现在换到你那里了。』
She originally used my pillow, and now it has been put on your side.”

『这——』雷桂卿惊喜交集地，『这，
“Then—” Lei was astonished and delighted.

这是啥意思？』
“What does it mean?”

说着将脸伏下去，细嗅枕上的香气。
He buried his face in the pillow to inhale its fragrance.

古应春本来不想『杀风景』，见此光景不能不扫他的兴了，
Gu had not intended to spoil his pleasure, but at this sight felt obliged to do so.

『「贾氏窥帘韩掾少，宓妃留枕魏王才」，
“‘Lady Jia peered through the curtain at young Han the clerk; / The Goddess of the Luo left her pillow for the gifted Prince of Wei.’

桂卿，』他说∶『你要想一想，两样资格，你有一样没有？』
Guiqing, consider: do you possess either of those two qualifications?”


『我不懂你的意思。』
“I do not understand.”

古应春的意思是说，除非雷桂卿觉得在年轻英俊，或者博学多才这两个条件占有一个，就难望获得悟心的青睐。
Gu meant that unless Lei could claim either handsome youth or exceptional learning and talent, he could hardly expect to win Wuxin’s favor.

而悟心一向好恶作剧，
She had always enjoyed practical jokes.

他去请杨师爷所吃的苦头，就是悟心对他的轻佻所予的惩罚。
The ordeal he had endured in fetching Yang was her punishment for his frivolous behavior.

如今将留有香泽的枕头换给他，是一个陷阱，也是一种考验；
Giving him the pillow scented by her hair was another trap, another test.

雷桂卿倘或再动绮念，后面就还有苦头吃。
If he again indulged amorous thoughts, more suffering lay ahead.

雷桂卿倒抽一口冷气，
Lei drew a sharp breath.

对悟心的感觉当然受过了；不过那只是片刻之间的事，
His feelings toward Wuxin changed, but only briefly.

古应春所说的话，到底不及他脑中『美目盼兮，巧笑倩兮』的印象来得深刻，
Gu’s words could not efface the image of her lovely eyes and enchanting smile.

所以仍为枕上那种非兰非麝、似有似无的香味，搅得大半夜六神不安。
The faint, elusive scent on the pillow, neither orchid nor musk, kept him restless for much of the night.


第二天醒来，已是阳光耀眼，
When he awoke, sunlight dazzled his eyes.

看表上是九点钟，比平时起身，起码晚了两个钟头；
His watch showed nine, at least two hours later than he ordinarily rose.

出舱一看，古应春静静地在看书喝茶。
Coming out of the sleeping cabin, he found Gu quietly reading and drinking tea.

『昨晚上失眠了？』他问。
“Could you not sleep last night?” Gu asked.

雷桂卿不好意思地笑一笑，顾而言他地问∶『我们怎么办？』
Lei smiled awkwardly and changed the subject. “What are we to do?”

『你先洗脸。』
“Wash first.

古应春说∶『悟心一早派人来请我们去吃点心，
Wuxin sent an invitation early this morning for us to take refreshments with her.

我在等你。』
I have been waiting for you.”

雷桂卿有点迟疑，
Lei hesitated.

很想不去，但似乎显得心存芥蒂，气量太小；
He wanted to refuse, yet that might make him seem resentful and small-minded.

如果去了，又怕自己沉不住气，脸上现出悻悻之色，
If he went, however, he feared he might lose his composure and reveal his annoyance.

因而不置可否，慢慢地漱洗完了，
Giving no definite answer, he washed slowly.

只见小玉又来催请了。那就容不得他再多作考虑，
Then Xiaoyu arrived to urge them to come, leaving no more room for hesitation.

相将上岸，到了莲池精舍，仍旧在悟心禅房中的东间坐落，
They went ashore together and returned to the eastern reception room in Wuxin’s quarters.

那只小哈叭狗只往雷桂卿身上扑，
The little lapdog kept jumping at Lei.

他把它抱了起来，居然不吠不动，乖乖地躺在他怀里。
He picked it up, and it lay quietly in his arms without barking or struggling.

『它倒跟你投缘。』
“It has taken to you.”

雷桂卿抬头一看，悟心含笑站在门口；
Lei looked up to see Wuxin smiling in the doorway.

哈叭狗看见主人，从雷桂卿身上跳了下来。转入悟心怀中，用舌头去舐主人的脸。
At the sight of its mistress, the dog sprang from him into her arms and licked her face.


『不要闹！』
“Do not make a fuss!”

悟心将狗放了下来，
She put it down.

『到外面去玩。』
“Go and play outside.”

狗通人性，响着颈下的小金铃，摇摇摆摆地往外走去，
The intelligent creature waddled out, its little gold bell ringing.

雷桂卿笑道∶『这只狗真好玩。』
Lei laughed. “What an amusing dog.”

『你欢喜，送了给你好不好？』
“If you like it, shall I give it to you?”

雷桂卿大感意外，
Lei was taken completely by surprise.

不知道她这话是真是假，更不知道她说这话的用意；
He did not know whether she meant it, still less why she had offered.

由于存着戒心之故，就算她是真话，他亦不敢领受这份好意。
On his guard now, he would not dare accept even if she was sincere.

『谢谢，谢谢！
“Thank you, thank you.

君子不夺人之所好。』
A gentleman does not deprive another of something cherished.”

『我是真的要送你。』
“I really mean to give it to you.”

『真的我也不敢领。』雷桂卿说，
“Even so, I could not accept.

『而且狗也对你有感情了。』
Besides, it is attached to you.”


这时点心已经端出来，有甜有咸，颇为丰盛；
The refreshments had arrived, a plentiful assortment of sweet and savory dishes.

一直未曾开口的古应春便说∶『悟心，我想赶回去办事，
Gu, who had so far said nothing, spoke. “Wuxin, I must hurry back to attend to business.

中午的素斋，下次来叨扰。
Let us leave the vegetarian lunch until next time.

好在吃这顿点心，中饭也可以不必吃了。』
After all these refreshments, we shall hardly need lunch.”

『喔，』
“Oh?

悟心问道∶『你总还要回来，
You will be coming back, then.

哪一天？』
When?”

这就问到古应春为难之处了。
This touched on Gu’s difficulty.

原来他在来到湖州之前就筹划好了的，在湖州的交涉办得有了眉目，未了事宜由雷桂卿接下来办，以便他能脱身赶到上海，安排迎接左宗棠出巡。
Before coming to Huzhou, he had planned that once negotiations were under way, Lei should take over the unfinished business, freeing him to go to Shanghai and arrange Zuo Zongtang’s reception.

如今照原定计划，应该由雷桂卿在怡和洋行与杨师爷之间任联络之责；
Under that plan, Lei should now serve as liaison between Jardine Matheson and Secretary Yang.

可是这一来少不得还是要托悟心居间，
But Wuxin would still have to act as intermediary.

他怕雷桂卿绮念未断，与悟心之间发生纠纷，因而不知如何回答。
Gu feared that Lei’s continuing infatuation might lead to trouble between them and did not know how to answer.

『咦！』悟心问道∶『你怎么不开口？』
“Well?” she asked. “Why are you silent?”

『我在想。』
“I am thinking.”

『怎么到这时候你才来想？』
“Why have you waited until now to think?”

这样咄咄逼人的姿态，使得古应春有些发窘，
Her pressing manner embarrassed him.

只好再想话来搪塞。
He sought an excuse.

『这件事很麻烦，
“The matter is complicated.

我要跟桂卿回去以后，跟怡和商量以后再说。』
Guiqing and I must go back and consult Jardine Matheson before deciding.”


『以我说也不必这么费事。』
“I do not think so much trouble is necessary.”

『你有什么好办法？』
“Have you a better plan?”

『依我说，你回去办怡和洋行的禀帖，
“You should go back to obtain Jardine Matheson’s petition.

雷老爷不妨留下来，
Mr Lei can stay here.

「蚕禁」马上要过了，
The silkworm restrictions will soon be over.

做丝虽忙，说几句话的工夫总有，
Silk-making is busy work, but people can spare time for a few words.

哪个收了赵宝禄多少定洋，大家算算清楚，说说明白，
Let everyone work out and make clear how much, if anything, he received from Zhao in deposits.

如果要进状子告赵宝禄，里面有杨师爷，外面有雷老爷，事情就好办了。』悟心又说∶『这是昨天晚上我跟小玉商量出来的办法。
If they decide to sue him, with Secretary Yang working inside the yamen and Mr Lei outside, matters will be much easier.” Wuxin added, “Xiaoyu and I worked this out last night.

她有好几家亲戚，我也有几个熟人都跟赵宝禄有纠葛；
Several of her relatives and a few people I know have disputes with Zhao.

难得你们替怡和来出面，
It is fortunate that you are acting for Jardine Matheson.

大家是一条线上的。』
We are all pursuing the same end.”

这个意外的变化，不但古应春想不到，雷桂卿更感意外，
This unexpected development surprised Gu, and Lei even more.

心里有好些话要说，但照理应该由古应春先表示意见，所以默然等待。
Lei had much to say but properly left Gu to express his view first.

古应春是完全赞成悟心的办法，但先要说好一个条件，
Gu thoroughly approved, but wanted one condition settled.

『不错，内有杨师爷，外有雷老爷。』
“Yes, Secretary Yang inside and Mr Lei outside.

他说∶『不过，你也不要忘记，中有悟心师太，
But you must not forget Mistress Wuxin in the middle.

都要靠你联络。』
We shall rely on you to keep everyone in touch.”

『那当然。』
“Of course.”


『你怎么联络法？』古应春说∶『雷老爷在这里人生地不熟，
“And how will you do that?” Gu asked. “Mr Lei knows neither the people nor the place.

再遇到那么一条吓坏人的狗，不是生意经。』
Another encounter with a terrifying dog would hardly help the business.”

『不会了。』悟心答说，
“There will not be another,” Wuxin replied.

『我保险不会再遇到。』
“I guarantee it.”

说罢嫣然一笑。
She smiled beautifully.

这一笑又让雷桂卿神魂飘荡了；不过这一回古应春却不再担心，
Lei was captivated once more, but Gu no longer worried.

他担心的是悟心会出花样，
His concern had been Wuxin’s tricks.

既然她如此保证，而且要靠雷桂卿办事，也不敢再恶作剧。
Having given her guarantee and needing Lei’s help, she would hardly play another prank.

至于雷桂卿这面，已经对他下过警告，
As for Lei, he had been warned.

倘或执迷不悟，那是他自己的事。
If he persisted in his delusions, that was his own affair.

转念到此，便向雷桂卿笑道∶『这一来我也放心了。
Gu smiled at him. “Then I can rest easy.

你虽不是曹植、韩寿，不过做了鲁仲连，反而更吃香了。』
You may not be Cao Zhi or Han Shou, but play the mediator Lu Zhonglian and you will be more welcome still.”

悟心不知道他为雷桂卿讲过『贾氏窥帘韩掾少，宓妃留枕魏王才』这两句诗的典故，便叩问说∶『你在打什么哑谜。』
Unaware that he had explained the verse about Lady Jia, Han Shou, the goddess’s pillow, and the Prince of Wei to Lei, Wuxin asked, “What riddle are you speaking in?”

『不错，是个哑谜；
“A riddle, indeed.

你要想知道，等我不在的时候，你问他好了。』
If you want the answer, ask him when I am gone.”

悟心这下大致可以猜到了，这个哑谜与她有关。
Wuxin could now guess that it concerned her.

此时当然不必再问，
There was no need to pursue it.

一笑置之。
She let it pass with a smile.

『我们谈谈正事。』古应春说，
“Let us return to business,” Gu said.

『悟心，我准定你的办法，
“I accept your plan.

今天吃过中饭，我就回杭州，
I shall go back to Hangzhou after lunch today.

桂卿一半帮你们的忙，照应他的责任，都在你身上。』
Guiqing will be working partly on your behalf, and I leave his welfare entirely to you.”

『那当然。
“Naturally.

我庵里不便住，我另外替雷老爷找个好地方借住，
He cannot conveniently lodge in my retreat, but I shall find him a good place elsewhere.

一定称心如意。』
I am sure he will be pleased.”


刚谈到这里，小玉来报，说船老大带了个陌生人来觅古应春。
Just then Xiaoyu reported that the boatman had brought a stranger looking for Gu.

此刻人在大殿上，请去相见。
He was waiting in the main hall.

出去一看，才知道是胡雪岩特遣的急足来投信。
Gu went out and found that Hu Xueyan had sent a special express messenger with a letter.

信上说∶左宗棠已自江宁起程，一路视察防务、水利，
Zuo had already left Jiangning, it said, inspecting defenses and waterworks along the way.

在镇江、常州、苏州都将逗留，大概十天以后，可到上海，
He would stop in Zhenjiang, Changzhou, and Suzhou and reach Shanghai in about ten days.

在杭州所谈之事，希望古应春即速办理，
Hu urged Gu to attend promptly to what they had discussed in Hangzhou.

可由湖州径赴上海，省事得多。
It would save trouble to go straight from Huzhou to Shanghai.

这一来，计划就要重新安排了，
The plan had to be revised again.

古应春吩咐来人回船待命∶随即拿着信报找悟心与雷桂卿去商量。
Gu told the messenger to wait aboard, then took the letter back to consult Wuxin and Lei.

『左大人出巡到上海，胡大先生要替他摆摆威风，
“Lord Zuo is coming to Shanghai on inspection, and Mr Hu wishes to give him an imposing reception.

这件事我要赶紧到上海托洋人去办。
I must hurry there and arrange matters with the foreigners.

桂卿，我看，你要先回一趟杭州，把情形跟胡先生说清楚了再回来。
Guiqing, you had better go to Hangzhou first, explain the situation to Mr Hu, and then return here.”

『』怡和的禀帖呢？『雷桂卿问∶』你在上海办妥了，不如直接寄湖州，似乎比寄到杭州多一个周折来得妥当。
“What about Jardine Matheson’s petition?” Lei asked. “Once you have it ready in Shanghai, it would be better to send it directly to Huzhou, rather than through Hangzhou.”

『』好！
“Good.

湖州寄到哪里，
Where in Huzhou should it be sent?

是——『古应春的话犹未完，悟心抢着说道∶』寄给杨师爷，请他代呈好了。『
To—” Before he finished, Wuxin said, “Send it to Secretary Yang and ask him to submit it.”


『可是信里说些什么，桂卿不知道啊！』
“But Guiqing will not know what it says.”

『杨师爷知道，
“Secretary Yang will.

莫非不能问他？
Can he not ask him?

你如果再不放心，抄个底子寄到我这里转，也可以。
If you are still uneasy, send a copy to me and I can pass it on.

不过，光寄封信，你自己也不好意思吧？』
But surely you will be ashamed to send nothing except a letter?”

『你说，你说，你要啥，我给你寄了来。』
“Tell me what you want, and I shall send it.”

『敲你一个小竹杠，
“I shall levy a small tribute.

到洋房里买一包洋糖给我寄来。』
Buy me a packet of foreign sweets at a foreign shop.”

『还有呢？』
“Anything else?”

『就这一样。』
“That is all.”

『好了，
“Very well.

我知道了。』
I understand.”

古应春对雷桂卿说∶『你坐一会，
Gu turned to Lei. “Stay a moment.

我回船去写了信再来。』
I shall go back to the boat, write a letter, and return.”


『何必回船上去写？
“Why go back to the boat?

我这里莫非连纸墨砚笔都没有？』
Have I no paper, ink, inkstone, or brushes here?”

说着，悟心抬一抬手，将古应春带到后轩，是她抄经做功课的所在。
With a gesture, Wuxin led him to the rear pavilion, where she copied scriptures and performed her devotions.

『到上海往东走，回杭州往南走，
Gu addressed Lei about the journey. “Shanghai is east, Hangzhou south.

船你坐了回去。』古应春向悟心说道；「能不能请你派人打听一下，往上海的船是啥辰光有？
You take the boat back.” Then he asked Wuxin, “Could you send someone to find out when there is a boat for Shanghai?”

『『每天都有。几点钟开，我就不晓得了。
“There is one every day, but I do not know the time.

我去问。』
I shall inquire.”

等悟心一走，古应春向雷桂卿笑道∶『这是意外的机缘。
Once she had gone, Gu smiled at Lei. “This is an unexpected opportunity.

悟心似乎有还俗的意思，
Wuxin seems inclined to return to secular life.

你断弦也有两年了，
You have been a widower for two years.

好自为之。』
Conduct yourself wisely.”

雷桂卿笑笑不作声；不过看得出来，心里非常高兴。
Lei smiled without answering, but his delight was plain.

『我只劝你一句，要顺其自然，
“I have only one piece of advice: let things take their course.

千万不可心急，更不可强求。』
Do not be impatient, and above all do not force anything.”

『我明白，
“I understand.

你放心好了。』
You need not worry.”


胡雪岩替老母做过了生日，第二天就赶往上海，
The day after his mother’s birthday celebrations ended, Hu hurried to Shanghai.

那是在古应春回家的第六天。
It was the sixth day since Gu’s return home.

一到当然先去看七姑奶奶，
Naturally, his first visit was to the Seventh Lady.

絮絮不断地谈了好久，直到吃晚饭时，才能谈正事，
They talked at length, and only at dinner could business be discussed.

『左大人已经到苏州了，预定后天到上海，
“Lord Zuo has reached Suzhou and expects to arrive here the day after tomorrow.

小爷叔来得正是时候。』
You have come at just the right time, Uncle.”

『他来了当然住天后宫。转运局是一定要来的，
“He will stay at Tianhou Temple, of course, and he is certain to visit the Transport Bureau.

你看应该怎么接待？』
How should we receive him?”

『左大人算是自己人，
“He is one of our own people.

来看转运局是视察属下，
Visiting the bureau means inspecting his subordinates.

我看不必弄得太客气，倒好象疏远了。』
There is no need for excessive ceremony; that would only make us seem distant.”

『太客气虽不必，让他高兴高兴是一定要的。』
“Perhaps not excessive ceremony, but we must certainly please him.

胡雪岩说∶『我想挑个日子，请他吃饭陪客除了我们自己官面上的人以外，能不能把洋人的总领事、司令官都请来。』
I should like to choose a day to give him a dinner. Besides our own officials, can we invite the foreign consuls-general and naval commanders?”

『这要先说好。照道理，请他们没有不来的道理。』古应春又说∶『放礼炮的事，已经谈妥当了，
“It must be arranged beforehand, but there is no reason for them to refuse.” Gu continued, “The salutes are arranged.

不过，日子不晓得哪一天？』
What I do not know is the day.”


『何不到道台衙门去问一问？』
“Why not ask at the intendant’s yamen?”

古应春不作声，
Gu said nothing.

胡雪岩看出其中别有蹊跷，便即追问是怎么回事？
Seeing that something lay behind his silence, Hu pressed him.

『「排单」是早已来了，
“The itinerary arrived long ago.

哪天到，哪天看哪个地方，哪天什么人请客，都规定好了，
His arrival, his visits, his hosts for each day—everything has been fixed.

就是我们转运局去要排单，推说没有。』
But when our bureau asks for a copy, they say there is none.”

胡雪岩不由得生气，
Hu was angry.

『他们是什么意思呢？』
“What do they mean by it?

他问∶『我们转动局一问也很敬重他们的。
Our bureau has always treated them with respect.

明天我倒要去看看邵小村，怎么跟我说的。』
I shall see Shao Xiaocun tomorrow and hear what he has to say.”

古应春始而默然，继而低声说道∶『小爷叔，你不要动意气。
Gu was silent at first, then said quietly, “Do not let anger carry you away, Uncle.

我听到一个说法，不晓得是真是假？
I have heard something, though I do not know whether it is true.

据说李合肥已经派人通知邵小村，关照他跟盛杏荪联络，不许左湘阴的势力伸到上海。
They say Li of Hefei has sent word to Shao to work with Sheng Xingsun and prevent Zuo of Xiangyin from extending his influence into Shanghai.

有人在邵小面前献计，说左湘阴容易对付，就是胡某人不大好惹，要防左，先要防胡。』
Someone advised Shao that Zuo would be easy to deal with, but Hu was not a man to provoke lightly: to guard against Zuo, first guard against Hu.”

胡雪岩听完，不大在意这话，
Hu took little notice.

『他们防我也不止今天一天了。』
“They have been on their guard against me for more than a day.

他说∶『见怪不怪，其怪自败，
Ignore a strange trick, and it defeats itself.

你不必把这件事看得太认真。』
Do not take it too seriously.”

看他这种掉以轻心的态度，古应春不免兴起一种隐忧，但此时不便再多说什么，
His dismissive attitude gave Gu an uneasy sense of danger, but he could say no more then.

自己私下打了一个主意，要为胡雪岩作耳目，多方注意李鸿章与左宗棠在两江明争暗头，
Privately, he resolved to act as Hu’s eyes and ears, watching the open and covert rivalry between Li and Zuo in Liangjiang.

倘或有牵涉及于胡雪岩的可能时，更要预先防备，弭祸于无形。
If it threatened to involve Hu, he must take precautions before harm arose.

由于古应春的极力活动，同时也由于左宗棠本身的威望，上海英、法两租界的工部局，以及各国驻沪海军，都以很隆重的礼节致敬；
Through Gu’s strenuous efforts, and because of Zuo’s own prestige, the municipal councils of Shanghai’s British and French settlements and the foreign navies gave Zuo an exceptionally ceremonious reception.

经过租界，派出巡捕站岗、仪队前导，
When he passed through the settlements, police stood guard and an honor escort went before him.

尤其是出吴淞口阅兵时，黄浦江上的各国兵舰，都升起大清朝的黄龙旗，呜放十三响礼炮，
Most strikingly, when he went out through Wusong to review the forces, the foreign warships on the Huangpu hoisted the Qing yellow-dragon flag and fired thirteen-gun salutes.

声彻云霄，震动了整个上海，
The thunder filled the sky and shook all Shanghai.

都知道左宗棠到上海来了。
Everyone knew Zuo Zongtang had arrived.
