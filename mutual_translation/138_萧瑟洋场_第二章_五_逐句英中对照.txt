胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement
第二章（本章第5部分，共6部分）

原著：高阳


Zuo’s temporary headquarters were at Tianhou Temple.
行馆设在天后宫，

Shao Youlian, the Shanghai intendant, led the prefect of Songjiang and the subordinate county magistrates in paying formal respects.
上海道邵友濂率领松江知府及所属各县『庭参』，

Next came the commissioner of the Jiang Customs and directors of the municipal councils, followed by the civil and military officials stationed in Shanghai.
接着是江海关税务司及工部局的董事拜会，在上海的文武官员谒见，

Shao then joined the intendants holding appointments there, including Hu Xueyan and Sheng Xuanhuai, in a banquet honoring the marquis and grand secretary.
然后是邵友濂联合在上海有差使的道员，包括胡雪岩、盛宣怀在内，『恭宴爵相』，

By the time it ended, the first night watch had begun.
散席时，已经起更了。

Hu and Gu naturally stayed until last.
胡雪岩与古应春当然留在最后，

“Your Excellency has had a tiring day,” Hu said. “Please retire early.
『大人今天很累了。』胡雪岩说∶『请早早安置，

We shall return tomorrow to pay our respects.”
明天再来请安。』

“No, no!”
『不、不！』

Zuo waved a hand. “I inspect the arsenal tomorrow and return to Jiangning the day after.
左宗棠摇着手说∶『我明天看了制造局，后天就回江宁了。

There are many things I must discuss with you.
有好些事情跟你谈谈，

Do not hurry away.”
不忙走。』

Hu had offered only the customary polite words.
胡雪岩原是门面话，

Since Zuo was in good spirits and wished him to stay, he could ask for nothing better.
既然左宗堂精神很好，愿意留他相谈，自是求之不得，

He assented and sat down.
答应一声，坐了下来。


“After all that argument over land defense and maritime defense, in the end I have to attend to it myself.
『陆防、海防争了半天，临到头来，还是由我来办，

What tricks fortune plays!”
真是造化弄人。』

Zuo threw back his head and laughed so loudly that the roof seemed to shake.
说罢，左宗棠仰空大笑，声震屋瓦。

Only Hu understood that the laughter was directed at Li Hongzhang.
这一笑只有胡雪岩明白，是笑李鸿章。

In the fifth month of the eleventh year of Tongzhi, Russia had taken advantage of the Muslim rebellion in Xinjiang to send troops into Ili.
原来同治十一年五月，俄国见新疆回乱，有机可乘，出兵伊犁；

In the third month of the thirteenth year, Japan used the incident involving shipwrecked Ryukyuans as a pretext to invade Taiwan.
十三年三月，日本借口琉球难民事件，派军入侵台湾，

Threats on land and at sea followed one another, provoking debate over which kind of defense should take priority.
一时陆防、海防相继告警，因而出现了陆防与海防孰重的争论；

Its principal opponents were Zuo and Li.
相争两方的主角，正就是左宗棠与李鸿章。

Zuo managed affairs in the northwest, Li commanded Beiyang; each had his own responsibilities and convictions.
左宗棠经营西北，李鸿章指挥北洋，各有所司，亦各有所持，

Considering the issue of great importance, the court ordered governors-general and governors throughout the provinces to state their views.
朝迁认为兹事体大，命各省督抚，各抒所见。

Wang Wenshao, then Governor of Hunan, happened to be back in Hangzhou to visit his ancestral graves.
其时湖南巡抚王文韶，正好回杭州扫墓，

Hu asked him whether he favored land or maritime defense.
胡雪岩便问他∶『赞成陆防，还是海防？』


“What do you think?” Wang countered.
王文韶反问一句∶『你看呢？』

“As Governor of Hunan, you should naturally speak for a Hunan man.”
『你当湖南巡抚，自然应该帮湖南人讲话。』

“Quite.
『不错。

A man governing a province must not offend its powerful families.
为政不得罪巨室。』

I have been unable to decide this matter.
王文韶说∶『我为这件事，一直踌躇不决，

Your words have settled it.
现在听老兄一句话，算是定了主意。

My friendship with Lord Li must be put aside for the moment.”
李大先生的交情，暂时要搁一搁了。』

Wang’s ties with Li were deep, but for the sake of governing Hunan smoothly, he decided to support land defense.
原来王文韶跟李鸿章的关系很深，为了在湖南做官顺利，王文韶决定赞成陆防，

In his memorial he wrote: “Defenses on both river and sea urgently require preparation.
复奏说道∶『江海两防，亟宜筹备，

Yet a threat to the coast cannot arise without a cause; the crucial matter lies in the military situation on our western frontier.
然海疆之患，不能无因而至，其关键则在西陲军务，

The Russians occupy our Ili and show every sign of holding it indefinitely.
俄人据我伊犁，强有久假不归之势，

Each day our troops delay gives them another day’s advance.
我师迟一日，则俄人进一日，

Nothing could be more urgent.”
事机之急，莫此为甚。』

This memorial gave the land-defense argument the advantage.
就因为这个奏折，使得陆防论占了上风。

Soon afterward the Tongzhi Emperor died, and the dispute subsided temporarily.
不久同治驾崩，争端暂息。

It revived in the first year of Guangxu, when Empress Dowager Cixi ordered princes, grand secretaries, and the heads of the Six Ministries and Nine Courts to confer on maritime defense.
光绪元年，争议复起，慈禧太后命亲郡王、大学士、六部九卿，会议海防事宜。

Li memorialized for an end to the western expedition; Zuo naturally opposed him.
李鸿章上折请罢西征；左宗棠当然反对，

Finally, with Wenxiang’s support, Zuo was appointed imperial commissioner to direct military affairs in Xinjiang.
最后是由于文祥的支持，派左宗棠以钦差大臣督办新疆军务，

Plainly, the maritime-defense argument had again lost ground.
显然的，海防论又落了下风。


In practice, however, the case for land defense had arisen from the Ili affair.
不过陆防之议，实际上是由伊犁事件而来，

After Zeng Jize’s mission to Russia settled the Sino-Russian dispute, it ceased to be raised.
及至曾纪泽使俄，解决了中俄纠纷，陆防论就不再有人提起。

When Zuo completed his western campaign, he was summoned to the Grand Council, then soon appointed Governor-General of Liangjiang.
到得左宗常西征收功，内召入军机；不久又外放两江，

Li revived the old question and this time won a complete victory.
李鸿章旧事重提，这回大获全胜，

The court accepted his maritime-defense plan in full.
海防的计划，朝廷完全同意，

Three measures were to come first: establishing a base at Yingkou to train a new naval force; raising funds to continue building steel-armored warships, with government loans due for repayment by China Merchants temporarily retained and diverted to the purchase of ironclads; and simultaneously building docks and forts at the important harbors of both Nanyang and Beiyang.
首先要办的是三件事∶一是在营口设营，编练新工海军；二是筹款续造『钢面铁甲』兵轮，招商局原应归还的官款暂缓归还，拨作购铁甲船之用；三是南北洋各紧要海口修船坞、修炮台，同时并举。

Just as the work gathered momentum, Old Madam Li died in Hankou.
哪知正在干得如火如茶之时，李太夫人病殁汉口，

Li Hongzhang returned home to observe mourning, and Zhang Shusheng, Governor-General of Liangguang, was transferred to act in Zhili.
李鸿章丁忧回籍，调两广总督张树声署理直督，

The coastal-defense preparations temporarily came to a halt.
筹设海防一事，便暂时拦下来了。


“If Beiyang can manage maritime defense, why should Nanyang not do so?
『海防，北洋可管，南洋又何尝不可管；

Most of the money comes from Liangjiang anyway, making it still more proper for Nanyang to take charge.
而且经费大部分出在两江，南洋来管，更觉名正言顺。

I mean to begin with docks and forts.
我现在想先从船坞、炮台这两件事着手。

I have already sent for Lord Peng.
已经派人去邀彭宫保了；

The reason I must hurry back to Jiangning is that he is coming down the Yangtze on an inspection and should arrive within days.
我要赶回江宁，就因为他从长江上游巡阅下来，日内可到江宁，

It would hardly be courteous for the host to be absent when his guest arrives.” Having said all this in one breath, Zuo suddenly called, “Xueyan!”
客临主不在，未免失礼。』左宗棠一口气说到这里，突然叫一声∶『雪岩！』

“What are your instructions, Your Excellency?”
『大人有什么吩咐？』

“Is Focke in Shanghai?”
『福克在不在上海？』

“Yes.
『在。』

He was about to return home, but on hearing of your inspection he specially postponed his departure by one steamer.
胡雪岩答说∶『他本来要回国了，因为听说大人巡视上海，特为迟一班轮船走。

He will certainly call on you tomorrow.”
明天一定会来见大人。』

“Will he come back after returning to Germany?”
『喔，他回德国以后，还来不来？』

“Yes, indeed.”
『来，来。』

“Good.
『那好。

Then we can discuss the matter further and take advantage of his journey home to have him purchase any new and effective weapons.”
正好趁他回国之便，我们再商量商量，看有什么新出的利器，托他采办。』


Hu was about to reply when a personal guard lifted the curtain and entered with a document folder.
胡雪岩正待回答，只见一名戈什哈掀帘而入，手里持着一个卷夹，

Without speaking, he approached Zuo and opened it.
走到左宗棠面前，一言不发，只将卷夹打了开来，

There was a sheet inside.
里面张纸；

Zuo read it and immediately passed it to Hu.
左宗棠拿起来看完，随手便递了给胡雪岩。

It was the decoded text of a confidential telegram: “Shanghai office respectfully reports to Marquis Zuo: [confidential] Marshal Yuan to govern Liangguang; public appointment decree imminent.”
接过来一看，是一份密电的译文∶『申局探呈左爵相，（享密）沅帅督粤，即明发。』

The signature was the single character “Yun.”
署名是一个『云』字，

Hu knew the message came from Xu Yongyi.
胡雪岩知道，是徐用仪发来的密电。

Marshal Yuan was, of course, Zeng Guoquan, whose courtesy name was Yuanfu.
这『沅帅』当然是指号沅甫的曾国荃，

Hu smiled. “Liangguang is a good place.
胡雪岩笑道∶『两广是好地方。

This time Ninth Marshal Zeng will not be like last year, when he insisted on leaving the governorship-general of Shaanxi and Gansu after less than six months because conditions were too harsh.”
曾九帅这回不会象去年那样，陕甘总督当不到半年，就因为太苦而一定要求去了。』

Zuo nodded and considered for a while.
左宗棠点点头，沉吟了一会，

Raising his head, he said slowly, “Sending Zeng the Ninth to Liangguang shows that Zhang Zhenxian will not return there.
抬起头来，徐徐说道∶『叫曾老九到两广，可见张振仙是不会回任，

He is to be confirmed in Zhili.
要真除直督了。

Xueyan, I must use this opportunity for a thorough reorganization.
雪岩，我要乘此机会，大加整顿，

Let Nanyang belong to Nanyang and Beiyang to Beiyang.
南洋的归南洋，北洋的归北洋，

We must remove Li Shaoquan’s invisible meddling hand.”
把李少荃那只看不见的「三只手」消除出去。』

“Yes, Your Excellency.”
『是。』

Hu reflected that Li’s influence in Nanyang was deeply rooted and would not be easy to eliminate.
胡雪岩心想李鸿章在南洋的势力，已有根深柢固之势，要清除不容易；

If it could be done, however, an entirely new situation would open before them.
但真的办到了，将来另有一番局面，

It was worth a great effort.
这件事值得出一番大气力。


“I am going to inspect the arsenal tomorrow.
『明天我去看制造局，

You had better come with me and see what can be improved.”
你最好跟我一起去，看看有什么可以改良的地方。』

“Certainly.
『是。

I shall attend you early tomorrow morning.”
我明天一早来伺候。』

When they left the headquarters, it was only a little after nine, and Shanghai’s foreign settlement was at its liveliest.
辞出行辕，不过九点多钟，十里洋场正是热闹的时候；

As they climbed into the carriage, Gu’s driver quietly said, “Master, your appointment with Seventh Miss is tonight.”
上车时，古应春的车案悄悄说道∶『老爷，七小姐那里的约会是今天。』

“You remember it more clearly than I do.
『你倒比我记得还清楚。』

Did Seventh Miss specially ask you to remind me?”
古应春说道∶『是不是七小姐特为关照，要你到时候提醒我。』

The driver merely grinned, cracked his whip, and drove south.
那车案笑嘻嘻地不作声，只扬鞭驱车，往南而去。

“Who is Seventh Miss?” Hu asked.
『七小姐是哪个？』胡雪岩问。


“Seventh Miss Aiyuelou.
『爱月楼老七。』

She has just come from Suzhou.”
古应春答说，『刚从苏州来的。』

“What does she look like?”
『人长得怎么样？』

“Presentable, that is all.
『不过大方而已。

But her skill in entertaining is first-rate.”
应酬工夫可是一等。』

“From the look of things, it is not only her entertaining that is first-rate.” Hu laughed. “She knows how to hold on to a client too.”
『看样子不止于应酬工夫。』胡雪岩笑道∶『扎客人的工夫也是一等。』

“You will see for yourself, Uncle.”
『小爷叔看了就知道了。』

Soon the carriage stopped at Zhaorong Lane off Baoshan Street.
转眼之间，马车在宝善街兆荣里停了下来，

Seventh Miss Aiyuelou’s establishment was the second house on the right.
爱月楼老七家就在进弄堂右首第二家，

An attendant called, “Rear wing!” and a serving woman immediately came to receive them.
相帮高喊一声∶『后厢房。』即时便有一名娘姨迎了出来。


Hu and Gu waited in the courtyard.
古胡二人便站在天进中等，

The woman’s hair was covered with red flowers, her face thick with white powder.
只见那名娘姨插了满头红花，擦一脸白粉，

Ugly and outlandish, she looked like a demoness.
丑而且怪，真是所谓鸠盘荼，

Yet when she spoke, her soft, delicate Wu dialect sounded like that of a girl of seventeen or eighteen: a true “beauty on the other side of the wall,” as Suzhou people called such women.
但开出口来，那一口娇滴滴的吴侬软语，恰如十七八女郎，这就是苏州人所说的『隔壁西施』！

“Oh, Mr Gu, why are you so late?
『喔唷，古老爷，耐那哼故歇才来介？

Seventh Miss has been waiting and waiting!”
七小姐等是等得来。』

On discovering Hu, she made a greater fuss still.
及至发现胡雪岩，愈发大惊小怪，

“Oh, my goodness!
『喔唷唷唷，

What an occasion!
难末事体大格哉！

How have we managed to invite the God of Wealth himself?”
啥叫财神老爷还清得来哉介？

Her cries brought windows opening upstairs and several pretty faces peering down into the courtyard.
她这一喊不打紧，楼上纷纷开窗，探出好几张俊俏面庞，住天井中探望；

One called loudly, “Mr Hu!
其中有一个大声喊道∶』胡老爷，胡老爷，

Do you remember me?
耐阿记得我介？

Fourth Miss Xiangyun!
奴是湘云老四，

Come and sit with me later.”
晏歇到倪搭来坐。

Hu had met so many women in his visits to the pleasure quarters that he could not remember this Fourth Miss Xiangyun.
『胡雪岩涉历花丛，阅人甚多，记不得有么一个湘云老四，

He merely answered, “Certainly!
只连声答应∶』好！

Certainly!”
好！

Following the serving woman upstairs, they found a woman of about twenty-four holding up the curtain at the rear room’s door, smiling as she waited.
『当下随着娘姨上楼，只见后厢房门口，有个花信年华的女子，打起门帘，含笑等待；

As they entered, Gu said, “Seventh Miss, I suppose you have never met Mr Hu?”

“Never met him?
『啥叫见过歇？

Of course I have.”
奴见过格。』

She curtsied.

“Mr Hu, you have grown more prosperous-looking.”
口中说道∶『胡老爷，耐发福哉。』

“Oh?
『喔，』

Where did we meet, Seventh Miss?”
胡雪岩问道∶『七小姐，我们在哪里见过？』

“At Shantang, near the end of the year before the year before last.
『山塘畹？是大前年年脚边浪格事体哉。

Governor Le’s eldest son gave the dinner.
格日子是勒抚台格大少爷请客。

You even called me over to entertain you.
胡老爷还转过奴一个局，

A great man has too much to remember, but I have always treasured it.”
耐末贵人多忘事，奴是一直记好勤心里浪向。』

说着，便上前来替胡雪岩解钮扣，卸马褂。

The scent of her hair stirred his memory.
胡雪岩闻到她头发上的香味，记起有这么一回事，

那年年底路过苏州，江苏巡抚勒方琦的长子，在上海便是稔友；

特地在虎丘一家局书寓中请客，

仿佛是在席间转过局，

面貌依稀，但名字却记不起，

“You were not called Aiyuelou then?”

“I was called Xifang.”

爱月楼老七向古应春瞟了一眼，

“Since Mr Gu began supporting my establishment, things have gradually improved.”

胡雪岩与古应春是听惯了这种奉承话，不以为意；

这时果盘已经摆上来了，

多谢、多谢。』

回顾一班小大姐说∶『你们以后就叫我半胡老爷好了。』

Hu liked her quick wit and ready tongue.
胡雪岩看她心思灵活、口齿便给，颇有好感；

胡雪岩跟他走马章台，已历多年，间或也有这种『让贤』之举；

“That will not do!
