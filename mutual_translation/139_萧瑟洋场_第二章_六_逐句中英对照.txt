胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement
第二章（本章第6部分，共6部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


『啊唷唷！』有个惯在花丛中混，除非大年三十不回家的『洋行小鬼』江罗勃，
“Oh, my!” exclaimed Jiang Luobo, a foreign-firm clerk so constantly at home in the pleasure quarters that only New Year’s Eve brought him back to his own house.

学着苏白说道∶『格是出新闻哉！
Imitating the Suzhou accent, he said, “This is news indeed!

啥叫我倪湘云老四是清倌人畹！』
Since when has our Fourth Miss Xiangyun been a chaste courtesan?”

大家都知道这是故意曲解『完璧』取笑湘云老四；
Everyone understood that he was deliberately twisting “intact jade” into a joke about her virginity.

她不懂这个典故，但知道是在开她的玩笑，却是看得出来，
Xiangyun did not know the allusion, but she could see that she was being teased.

索性老一老面皮，学四马路『野鸡』的口吻，
Brazening it out, she adopted the manner of a streetwalker from Fourth Road.

回敬江罗勃∶『不错，
“Certainly!

阿拉是的的刮刮的清水货。
I am pure, unadulterated fresh goods.

「酱萝卜」，你来啥！』
Pickled Radish, why do you not come and try?” Her nickname for Jiang played on the sound of his name.

就在满座轰笑声中，胡雪岩将湘云老四拉到一边，促膝密语，
Amid general laughter, Hu drew her aside and sat close to speak privately.

『老四，』他说，『我替你做这个媒，你看怎么样？』
“Fourth Miss, what do you think of the match I am making for you?”

『奴那哼好说弗好？
“How can I say it is no good?

耐胡老爷又看我弗起，
You do not think much of me yourself, Mr Hu.

吃仔格碗把势饭来，有啥办法？』
Earning my rice by this trade, what choice have I?”

胡雪岩原来欠了她一个情——有一回答应捧她的场，结果忘掉了；
Hu owed her a favor: he had once promised to support her establishment and forgotten.

这天恰有机会补这个情，也应酬了林茂先，
Here was a chance both to make amends and to entertain Lin.

所以此时开门见山地问∶『林老爷要到福建去上任，
He came straight to the point. “Mr Lin is leaving to take up his appointment in Fujian.

只怕没有工夫到你那里「做花头」，
I fear he has no time for an elaborate courtship at your house.

你能不能陪陪他。』
Could you keep him company?”

『那哼陪法？』
“In what way?”


『这还要说吗？』
“Must I spell it out?”

湘云老四脸一红，
Xiangyun blushed.

『呒拨格号规矩格！』她说，
“That is not how things are done!

『传仔出去末，奴落里还有面孔见人介？』
If word got out, how could I face anyone?”

『当然也不是一个花头都不做，
“Of course there would be some of the usual formalities.

等下翻台过去，是我做主人；
We shall move to your rooms shortly, with me as host.

明天下午，他到你那里碰和，晚上摆个双台，下来「借干铺」。
Tomorrow afternoon he can play mahjong there, followed by a double-table dinner in the evening, and afterward ‘borrow a dry bed.’

你看好不好？』
Would that do?”

『借干铺』是长三中对恩客的一种掩耳盗铃的手法，意思只是客人喝醉了，或者路太远，天时突变，临时借宿一宵，规矩是开销六两银子。当然，到底是干是湿，是没有人问的。湘云不作声，
She said nothing. Among changsan courtesans, “borrowing a dry bed” was a transparent pretense offered to favored clients: the man was supposedly drunk, or lived too far away, or had been caught by a change in the weather, and merely borrowed a bed for one night. The customary charge was six taels. Whether the bed was truly “dry” was nobody’s business.

看意思是有点活动了；胡雪岩便趁机补情，『老四，』他说，
Seeing that Xiangyun was wavering, Hu took the chance to make amends.

『林老爷是我的朋友，
“Mr Lin is my friend.

你就算委屈一回，
Make an exception this once.

林老爷人很爽快的，出手不会太小气。
He is generous and will not be mean with his money.

另外，你到大马路方九霞去挑一副金镯头，算是我送你的。』
Besides, go to Fang Jiuxia’s on First Road and choose a pair of gold bracelets as my gift.”

声色场中，向来黄金能买美人心，
In the pleasure quarters, gold had always been able to win a beauty’s heart.

湘云老四想一想说道∶『胡老爷。耐为朋友，格能操心法子，实头少见笃。
Xiangyun considered. “Few men would take such trouble for a friend.

不过格是耐胡老爷的想法，
But these are your wishes, Mr Hu.

你兴俚到看奴不入眼呐？
What if he does not care for me?

我啊弗能桠上去畹。』
I cannot force myself upon him.”

胡雪岩懂她的意思，是怕万一好呈不成，金镯落空，
Hu understood: she feared that if the affair came to nothing, so would the bracelets.

当即答说∶『总归我是心尽到了，
“I shall have done my best in any case.

只要林老爷今天上船到福建，明天你就到方九霞去挑镯头，
If Mr Lin boards his boat for Fujian today, you may go to Fang Jiuxia’s tomorrow and choose the bracelets.

好了，就这样说定了，』
There, it is settled.”

话宗，胡雪岩先站起来回席。
With that, Hu rose and returned to the table.


其时莺莺燕燕，陆续来到，而且都带了『乌师先生』，
The other courtesans were arriving one after another, all with their professional musicians.

笙歌嗷嘈，热闹非凡。
Music and song filled the rooms with a cheerful clamor.

就在这时候，听得楼下『相帮』高喊∶『后厢房客人。』
Just then the attendant downstairs called, “A guest for the rear room!”

『必是沙一心赶来了。』
“That must be Sha Yixin.”

古应春连忙起身，迎出门外，
Gu rose and went out.

果然就是沙一心。
It was indeed Sha.

『应春兄，』沙一心在楼梯口拉住他说∶『我的行李已经下长江轮船了，天亮就要上船。
At the staircase, Sha took his arm. “Brother Yingchun, my luggage is already aboard the Yangtze steamer, and I must embark at daybreak.

因为你说要替我引见一位朋友，所以特为赶了来，
Your note said you wished to introduce a friend, so I have come specially.

不知道是什么朋友？
Who is it?

倘或本来是住在上海的，等我半个月以后，从广州回来再见面，好不好。』
If he lives in Shanghai, could I meet him when I return from Guangzhou in half a month?

略停一停，他接着又说∶『实不相瞒，我还要回去过瘾。』
To be frank, I still have to go back and satisfy my opium craving.”

古应春考虑了一下说道∶『我要替你引见的这位朋友，就是胡雪岩胡大先生，
Gu considered. “The friend is Mr Hu Xueyan himself.

这样，你进去先见个面，跟大家招呼一下，
Come in, meet him, and greet the company.

然后，我替你说明缘故，放你回长发栈，
Then I shall explain and let you return to Changfa Inn.

等你从广州回来，如果胡大先生还在上海，我们再畅叙如何？』
If Mr Hu is still in Shanghai when you come back from Guangzhou, we can have a proper gathering. How would that suit you?”

『这倒行。』
“That would do.”

于是古应春将他引到筵席，一一介绍，
Gu led him in and made the introductions.

其中一大半是初识。
More than half the company were strangers to him.

这沙一心三十多年纪，丰神俊朗，说一口带川音的京腔，
Sha was in his thirties, handsome and distinguished, speaking clear Beijing Chinese with a Sichuan inflection.

音吐清亮，颇予人好感。胡雪岩很喜欢这个新朋友。
His voice made a favorable impression, and Hu liked his new acquaintance.

他是候补同知的班子，所以彼此以官衔相称，
Sha held the rank of expectant deputy prefect, so they addressed one another by official titles.

『胡观察名满天下，今天才能识荆，
“Intendant Hu is known throughout the world, yet only today do I meet you.

可见孤陋。
How provincial I must be!

不过，到底也拜见了一尊大菩萨，
Still, I have at last paid my respects to a great benefactor.

幸何如之。』
What good fortune!”

他举杯说道∶『借花献佛。』
Raising his cup, he added, “I borrow another’s flowers to offer them to the Buddha.”

说完，一饮而尽照一照杯。
He emptied it and showed the bottom.


『不敢，不敢。』胡雪岩声明∶『第一回，我不能不干。』
“You are too kind,” Hu replied. “On our first meeting, I must drain my cup.”

『胡观察吃花酒是有规矩，向不干杯。』江罗勃说道∶『今天是沙司马的面子。
“Intendant Hu has a rule against draining his cup at courtesan dinners,” Jiang Luobo remarked. “Today he makes an exception for Deputy Prefect Sha.

来，来，大家都干一杯。』
Come, let us all drink a full cup.”

沙一心人本谦和，看面子十足，赶紧站起来说∶『承各位抬爱，实在不敢当，
Naturally modest, Sha rose at once, grateful for the honor. “You are all too kind.

理当我来奉敬。』
It is I who ought to toast you.”

说着，自己满斟一杯，干了酒不断地说∶『谢谢！』
He filled and emptied another cup, repeatedly thanking them.

这时写局票的木盘又端上来了，
The tray for writing summons cards was brought round again.

古应春便看着沙一心问∶『仍旧是小金铃老三，如何？』
Gu looked at Sha. “Third Miss Little Golden Bell, as usual?”

『不，不！应春兄，
“No, no, Brother Yingchun.

我今天豁免了吧！
Excuse me today.

你知道的，我今天的情形不一样。』
You know my circumstances are different tonight.

沙一心又说∶『而且偷此片刻之暇，不向胡观察好好讨教一番，虚耗辰光，也太可惜。』
Besides, having snatched these few moments, it would be a pity not to seek Intendant Hu’s instruction instead of wasting the time.”

『也好。』
“Very well.”

古应春点点头，
Gu nodded.

『回头我另作安排。』
“I shall make another arrangement presently.”

『我已经有安排了。』胡雪岩接口说道∶『等一等我们翻到前厢房，替林太尊、沙司马饯行。』
“I have already made one,” Hu put in. “Shortly we shall move to the front room for a farewell dinner in honor of Prefect Lin and Deputy Prefect Sha.”


『不敢当，不敢当。』
“We could not presume!”

林茂先、沙一心异口同声地说。
Lin and Sha exclaimed together.

古应春已经知道胡雪岩要为林茂先与湘云老四拉拢的本意；
Gu already understood Hu’s intention of bringing Lin and Xiangyun together.

而他的另作安排是看胡雪岩与沙一心颇为投缘，要匀出工夫来让他们能作一次深谈，
His own plan was to make time for a substantial conversation between Hu and Sha, who seemed to have taken to one another.

这一下正好合在一起来办，
The two purposes could now be combined.

当即说道∶『各位听见了。
“You have all heard.

我代胡大先生作主人。
I shall act as host on Mr Hu’s behalf.

老四，你现在就回去预备吧。』
Fourth Miss, go and prepare.”

湘云老四喜孜孜地站起身来，先含笑向胡雪岩说∶『格末奴先转去，拨台面先端整起来。』
Xiangyun rose happily and smiled at Hu. “Then I shall go back and have the table set.”

接着，提高了声音说∶『各位老爷，晏歇才要请过来，
Raising her voice, she added, “Gentlemen, you must all come over shortly.

勿作兴溜格噢！
No slipping away!

江大少，格桩事体末，我拜托仔耐哉畹！』
Mr Jiang, I leave that to you.”

『包拉我身浪，
“Leave it to me.

一个缺。
Not one will be missing.

不过，老四，耐那哼谢谢我呐？』
But how will you thank me?”

『耐讲！』
“You tell me.”

『香个面孔阿好？』
“May I kiss your cheek?”

『瞎三话四，
“Nonsense!

讲讲就呒淘成哉！』
You cannot speak without becoming improper.”

说着白了江罗勃一眼，翩然而去。
She gave him a reproachful glance and went gracefully out.


林茂先久居北方，见惯了亢爽有余、不解蕴藉的北地胭脂，
Having lived long in the north, Lin was accustomed to women whose forthright manner lacked subtlety.

这天领略了娇俏柔媚、妖娆多变的南朝金粉，大为着迷。
The delicate charm and changeable allure of these southern beauties captivated him.

大家都知道，这天的主客的是林沙二人，
Everyone knew that Lin and Sha were the evening’s principal guests.

同时也从古应春『代作主人』的宣布中，意会到胡雪岩与沙一心或许有事要谈，便趁机起哄，都道不如此刻就翻台过去。
Gu’s announcement that he would act as host also suggested that Hu and Sha might have something to discuss, so the company eagerly proposed moving at once.

『这样吧！』古应春正好重新安排，
“Then let us arrange it this way,” Gu said.

『一心兄，你就请在这里过瘾，胡大先生陪你谈谈。
“Brother Yixin, satisfy your craving here while Mr Hu keeps you company.

我先陪大家过去，
I shall take the others over first.

回头过足了瘾再请过来。』
Join us when you have had enough.”

说道，站起身来；
He rose.

客人因为就在前厢房，倒省了一番穿马褂、点灯笼、出门进门的麻烦。
Since the other room was just at the front, the guests were spared the trouble of putting on riding jackets, lighting lanterns, and going out into the street.

爱月楼老七却仍守着她送客的规矩，站在房门口一一招呼；
Aiyuelou nevertheless observed her usual rule of seeing everyone off, standing in the doorway to address each guest.

等该走的客人都走了，回身向胡雪岩说道∶『胡老爷搭沙老爷请过来吧！』
Once those leaving had gone, she turned to Hu. “Mr Hu, Mr Sha, this way, please.”


后面是爱月楼老七的卧室，靠里一张大铜床，
Behind lay her bedroom, with a large brass bed against the inner wall.

已在床中间，横置了一个烟盘，两条绣花湖绉面的被子，叠成长条，上面摆了两只洋式枕头。
An opium tray had been placed across the middle, flanked by two embroidered Huzhou-crepe quilts folded into long bolsters and topped with Western pillows.

胡雪岩虽不抽鸦片，却知道抽烟的人向左侧卧，为的是右手在上，动作方便，
Though Hu did not smoke opium, he knew smokers lay on their left side to leave the right hand uppermost and free to work.

因而道声『请』；让沙一心躺了下来，自己在烟盘对面相陪。
He invited Sha to recline and lay opposite him across the tray.

『沙老爷！』爱月楼老七手上持着一只明角烟盒，走来说道∶『呒拨啥好个烟膏请耐，只有「云土」，
Aiyuelou came with a translucent horn opium box. “I have no fine opium to offer you, Mr Sha, only Yunnan opium.

晓得阿好迁就？』
I hope you can make do with it.”

说着，拖张小凳子在床前坐下来。
She drew a small stool to the bedside.

『蛮好、蛮好。
“Excellent, excellent.

七小姐，我自己来，
Please let me prepare it myself, Seventh Miss.

不敢劳动。』
I could not trouble you.”

『呒拨格号规格畹！』
“That would be against all custom!”

『老七，』胡雪岩便说∶『你就不必客气了，
“Do not stand on ceremony,” Hu said.

我晓得你打烟也不怎么在行。
“I know you are not particularly skilled at preparing it.

既然沙老爷这么说，你就让沙老爷自己来。』
Since Mr Sha asks, let him do it himself.”

『格末奴也只好恭敬勿如从命哉。』
“Then I can only obey.”

说着，将烟盒放下，检点了热茶、糖果，又去削了一盘水果来，然后说道∶『有啥事体末，招呼一声末哉，
She set down the box, checked the hot tea and sweets, cut a plate of fruit, and said, “Call if you need anything.

奴就来浪前头。』
I shall be in the front room.”

等她放下门帘离去时，沙一心已揭开盒盖，自己拿烟签子在水晶『太谷打』上开始打烟泡了，
By the time she had lowered the curtain and gone, Sha had opened the box and begun working the opium on the crystal taiguda.

右手烟签、左手象牙小砧，一面打、一面卷，手法干净利落，
With the opium pick in his right hand and a small ivory anvil in his left, he rolled and shaped it neatly and swiftly.

不一会打成一个『黄、高、松』三字俱全的大烟光，
Soon he had made a large pellet possessing all three desired qualities: golden, tall, and airy.

装在斗门上，又转过来、转过去，一面烘、一面捏，装好了用热烟签在烟泡中间打个到底的眼子，
He fitted it over the pipe bowl, turning, warming, and pressing it into place, then pierced it through the center with the heated pick.

然后抛过来将烟枪伸向胡雪岩。
Turning toward Hu, he offered him the pipe.


『请，请。』
“Please, go ahead.”

胡雪岩急忙摇手，
Hu waved it away.

『我没有享「福寿膏」的福气。』
“I am not fortunate enough to enjoy the ‘Paste of Happiness and Longevity.’”

听此一说，沙一心便不再客套，
Sha made no further attempt at courtesy.

对准了火『沙、沙、沙』地一口气抽完，拿起烫手的山茶壶嘴对嘴喝一口热茶，
He held the pipe to the flame and finished it in one continuous draw, then lifted the hot teapot and drank directly from its spout.

眼睛闭了一下，才从鼻孔中喷出淡白色的烟雾来。
His eyes closed briefly before pale smoke emerged from his nostrils.

这一筒烟下去，沙一心才有谈话的精神——实在是兴致。
Only after this pipe did he have the energy—or rather the inclination—to converse.

谈起胡雪岩很熟的一个人——为人骂作『汉奸』的龚孝拱。
He began with someone Hu knew well: Gong Xiaogong, whom people denounced as a traitor to China.


此人是道光年间大名士龚定庵的儿子。
Gong was the son of Gong Ding’an, the celebrated man of letters of the Daoguang reign.

龚家是杭州世家，
The Gongs were an old Hangzhou family.

龚定庵的父祖都是显宦，
Ding’an’s father and grandfather had held distinguished offices.

他本人才气纵横，做得极好的诗，而又不仅辞章；
He himself possessed prodigious talent and wrote excellent poetry, but his accomplishments were not confined to literary composition.

幼年受他外祖父金坛段玉裁之教，于『小学』——文字之学，亦有极深的造诣；
Taught in childhood by his maternal grandfather Duan Yucai of Jintan, he had also acquired profound learning in philology, the study of written language.

但中举以后，会试不利，几番落第。
After passing the provincial examination, however, he repeatedly failed at the metropolitan level.

原来宣宗的资质性情，很象明朝的末代皇帝思宗，
Emperor Xuanzong’s temperament and abilities resembled those of Sizong, the last Ming emperor.

他倒是有心做个英主，但才具甚短，而又缺乏知人之明，
He wished to be an enlightened ruler, but lacked both ability and judgment of men.

信任的宰相曹振镛，是个妨贤妨能、瞒上期下的庸才，
His trusted chief minister, Cao Zhenyong, was a mediocrity who resented talent, deceived his superiors, and oppressed his inferiors.

专门劝宣宗吹毛求疵，察察为明，
He constantly urged the emperor to mistake fault-finding and petty scrutiny for wisdom.

所以政风文风，两皆不振；
Government and literary life both languished.

试卷中的文章好坏在其次，最要紧的是格式不能错，
In examination papers, the quality of the writing mattered less than observing the prescribed format.

错了就是违犯『功令』，文章再好，亦遭摒弃。
A mistake was a breach of official regulations, and even the finest essay would then be rejected.

龚定庵几次名落孙山，都是为此。
This was why Gong Ding’an repeatedly failed.


好不容易会试中了，大家都说他必点『翰林院庶吉士』，
When he finally passed the metropolitan examination, everyone expected him to be selected as a Hanlin bachelor.

哪知殿试卷子因为书法不佳，不与翰林之选。
But his handwriting in the palace examination was deemed poor, and he was excluded.

龚定庵牢骚满腹，无可发泄，叫他的姨太太、丫头都用『大卷子』练书法，
Full of resentment with no outlet, he had his concubines and maids practice calligraphy on large examination sheets.

真有写得『黑、大、光、圆』四字俱全，极好的『馆阁体』的，
Some achieved the black, large, polished, rounded script admired in the academies and official halls.

每每向人夸耀，说『此举如能赴试，必点翰林』。
He would boast of them: “If this one could sit the examination, she would certainly enter the Hanlin Academy.”

其时有个满洲才女，叫『西林太清春』，做的词与纳兰性德齐名。
At that time, a talented Manchu woman named Xilin Taiqingchun wrote lyrics esteemed alongside those of Nalan Xingde.

她是贝勒奕绘的侧福晋，
She was a secondary consort of Prince Yihui.

住宅在京城西南角的太平湖，就是后来的醇王府，也就是光绪皇帝出生的『潜邸』。
Their residence beside Taiping Lake in the southwestern corner of Beijing later became Prince Chun’s mansion, the birthplace of the Guangxu Emperor before his accession.

龚定庵因为在宗人府当差，
Gong worked in the Imperial Clan Court.

又因为深通文字音韵之学，会说满州话及蒙古话，所以不但为了『回公事』，经常出入亲贵府邸，而且亦颇得若干亲贵的赏识。
His command of philology and phonology enabled him to speak Manchu and Mongolian, so he not only entered princely residences frequently to report on official business but also won the appreciation of several members of the imperial family.

奕绘人很开通，不禁西林太清与朝贵名士唱和，
Yihui was broad-minded and did not forbid Taiqingchun from exchanging poems with eminent officials and scholars.

龚定庵就是与西林太清春诗笺往还最密的一个人。
Gong was among her most frequent correspondents.


龚定庵因为科名晚，到了四十多岁，还只是一个『司官』，
Having gained his degree late, Gong remained only a ministry official into his forties.

前程有限，俸禄微薄，便动了解官之念，
His prospects were limited and his salary small, so he considered resigning.

那里江淮的盐商还很阔，而盐商又多喜附庸风雅，
The salt merchants of the Yangtze and Huai regions were still wealthy and fond of displays of literary culture.

象龚定庵这样名动公卿的人，『打秋风』亦可以过很舒服的日子。
A man of Gong’s fame among the great could live comfortably enough by soliciting their patronage.

主意一定，毅然而行，
Once decided, he acted resolutely.

不道京城里已起了谣言，说他解官是迫不得已，因为与西林太清春之间，有一段不可告人的秘密，倘不辞官出京，便有不测之祸；
Rumors had already arisen in Beijing, however, that he was being forced out: he and Taiqingchun shared a shameful secret, and unless he resigned and left the capital, disaster would befall him.

不幸的是，辞官不久，就了一个书院的山长，一夕暴毙，
Unfortunately, not long after resigning he became the head of an academy and died suddenly one night.

实在是中风，而传说他是被毒死的。
In fact, he suffered a stroke, but rumor claimed that he had been poisoned.

龚孝拱是龚定庵的长子，
Gong Xiaogong was his eldest son.

名字别号甚多，晚年自号『半伦』，
He used many names and literary sobriquets; in later life he called himself “Half a Relationship.”

据说他自己以为君臣、父子、夫妇、兄弟、朋友这五伦之中，无一可取，
It was said he regarded none of the five relationships—ruler and minister, father and son, husband and wife, brothers, and friends—as worth preserving.

不过有一个爱妾，勉强好说尚存『半伦』。
Only a beloved concubine allowed him, with some indulgence, to claim that half a relationship survived.

由这个别号，可以想见是个狂士。
The name alone suggested an eccentric.


龚孝拱天资甚高，
Xiaogong was exceptionally gifted.

由于遗传及家学，亦精通满洲、蒙古文字，比他父亲更胜一筹的是，还会英文。
Through inherited talent and family learning, he too mastered Manchu and Mongolian, and surpassed his father in knowing English as well.

咸丰年间，龚孝拱住在上海，由一个姓曾的广东人介绍，得识英国公使威妥玛；
During the Xianfeng reign he lived in Shanghai, where a Cantonese man named Zeng introduced him to the British envoy, Thomas Wade.

英法联军之役，威妥玛北上，带了龚考拱治文书、备顾问。
When the Anglo-French forces campaigned in the north, Wade took Gong with him to handle documents and serve as an adviser.

及至英法联军破京城，火烧圆明园，传说是龚孝拱领的头，而且趁火打劫，盗取了一批珍宝，
After the allies entered Beijing and burned the Yuanmingyuan, stories claimed that Gong had led them and taken advantage of the destruction to steal treasures.

在上海租界上作富公，挥霍无度，穷困而死，
He was said to have lived as a wealthy man in Shanghai’s foreign settlement, squandered his money, and died in poverty.

这就是他为人骂作『汉奸』的由来。
Hence the denunciations of him as a traitor.

『这是冤枉他的。』胡雪岩答说∶『我同他很熟。
“That is an injustice,” Hu said. “I knew him well.

狂是有的，不过还不致于做汉奸。』
He was eccentric, certainly, but not a traitor.”

『说得是。
“Quite right.

此人很可惜！』沙一心说∶『现在讲究洋务，真正能够摸透洋人性情的并不多，
A great pity!” Sha replied. “Foreign affairs matter so much now, yet few men truly understand foreigners’ ways.

龚孝拱是其中之一；
Gong was one.

他如果不是自暴自弃，在现在可以替那班有心学洋人长处，或者真想做一番事业的督抚，帮许多忙。』
Had he not given himself up to despair, he could have done much for governors and governors-general who sincerely wished to learn from foreign strengths or accomplish something worthwhile.”

『那末照一翁看，当今督抚之中，哪几位是真想做一番事业的？』胡雪岩随口问说。
“Then which of the present governors and governors-general,” Hu asked casually, “do you consider genuinely intent on achieving something?”

『象张振轩就是。』
“Zhang Zhenxuan, for one.”
