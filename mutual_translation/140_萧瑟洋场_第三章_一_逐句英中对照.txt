CHAPTER THREE
第三章


Zhang Zhenxuan was Zhang Shusheng, the acting governor-general of Zhili.
张振岩便是现署直隶总督的张树声。

Hu could scarcely remain indifferent to any mention of him.
提到此人，胡雪岩不能不关心，

Since Zuo Zongtang intended to drive Li Hongzhang’s influence out of Liangjiang, his interests would soon clash directly with Zhang’s.
因为左宗棠既然有意要驱逐李鸿章在两江的势力，眼前就会跟张树声直接发生利害冲突，

Here was a good opportunity to learn something about the man.
有机会倒要打听打听这个人。

“I hear Governor-General Zhang began as a licentiate and rose through military service,” Hu said.
『听说张制军是秀才的底子，由军功起家。

“The moral critics in the capital put on tremendous airs these days.
现在京里一班清流，架子大得不得了，

Can a rough soldier stand up to them?
行伍出身的老粗，能吃得消他们？』

When he was in Guangdong, he could at least say Heaven was high and the emperor far away.
胡雪岩又说∶『以前在广东，还可说是天高皇帝远，

Now that he is stationed in Tianjin, who knows how many Hanlin scholars pass through on their journeys north and south by sea?
现在驻扎天津，南来北往由海道经过那里的翰林不知多少，

Being governor-general must give him a headache.”
他这个总督恐怕很头痛吧？』

“Zhang Zhenxuan is hardly a rough soldier.
『张振轩倒不算老粗。

He was a stipendiary licentiate—”
他是廪生出身——』


“A stipendiary licentiate!”
『原来是禀生。』

Hu felt that he had spoken too hastily in dismissing Zhang as an uncultivated soldier.
胡雪岩觉得说张树声是行伍出身老粗，未免失言，

Among licentiates, he knew, stipendiary scholars ranked just below those selected for tribute scholarships.
因为他知道廪生在秀才之中，仅仅次于拔贡，

They received an allowance from the county treasury; candidates admitted to the county school also required their sponsorship and customarily gave them a fee in thanks.
一县之主，县衙门里可以领一份钱粮，童生进学，亦须廪生作保，照例亦须送一份谢礼，

Such senior licentiates therefore needed both substantial learning and good conduct.
反以资深的秀才，不但要有真才实学，而且品行也要端正，

Otherwise the provincial education commissioner would hardly confer one of these limited, well-remunerated places upon them.
否则学政是不肯将这个有限名额而有丰富收入的廪生，轻易畀予的。

“His standing as a stipendiary licentiate later gave him a considerable advantage,” Sha Yixin continued.
『张振轩这个廪生出身，后来占了很大的便宜。』沙一心继续谈张树声的经历，

“At first, in Li Hongzhang’s Huai Army, his reputation was far below those of Cheng Xueqi, Liu Bingzhang, Guo Songlin, and Liu Mingchuan—even below Pan Dingxin’s.
『他起先在李合肥的淮军中，名气不但比不上程学启、刘秉璋、郭松林、刘铭传，甚至还不及潘鼎新。

But when military merit brought him promotion to the fifth rank, he transferred to a deputy prefect’s post.
可是由军功保到五品，改了同组，

Moving from military to civil office was a great advantage.
由武入文，这就占便宜了。

In the summer of the fourth year of Tongzhi, he became acting intendant of the Huaihai Circuit.
同治四年夏天署理淮海道；

Liu the Sixth Pockmark was governor-general of Zhili, a first-rank official, yet he preferred to give up that first-rank post and retire to an idle life at home in Hefei.
刘六麻子是直隶总督，官拜一品，可是他情愿不要这个一品官员，回合肥老家去吃闲饭。

Do you know why, Mr Xueyan?”
雪翁，你知道不知道，这是什么道理？』


Hu did know.
这道理胡雪岩懂。

“Liu the Sixth Pockmark” was Liu Mingchuan’s nickname, and Hu had heard his story.
『刘六麻子』是刘铭传的外号，他的故事，胡雪岩也听人谈过。

The highest commander of a province’s Green Standard troops was the provincial commander-in-chief, commonly addressed as “General.”
原来一省绿营兵的最高弄官是提督，通称『军门』，

He cut a splendid figure among soldiers, but became a lesser person in the presence of a governor or governor-general.
在军队里很神气；一遇见督抚就矮了半截，

A governor-general held the concurrent title of minister of war, and a governor that of vice-minister; both thus ranked as heads of the ministry, and both were the commander-in-chief’s superiors.
因为总督挂兵部尚书衔；巡抚挂兵部侍郎衔，都算是兵部的『堂官』，也都是提督的上司，

A first-rank commander had to obey a second-rank governor and perform the full ceremonial obeisance when formally received.
一品的提督要受二品的巡抚的节制；而且正式见礼时，要用『堂参』的大礼。

Liu Mingchuan considered himself a scholar-general and had published a collection of verse entitled Poems from the Daqian Mountain Lodge.
刘铭传自命为儒将，刻过一部《大潜山房诗集》，

He felt that even first-rank military office counted for little, so he pleaded illness, relinquished his appointment, and secluded himself in his “mountain lodge.”
认为武官即使一品亦不值钱，所以告病开缺，潜居在他的『山房』中。

“Yes, military rank counts for little.
『是的，武官不值钱。

Zhang Zhenxuan was only a circuit intendant then, but once he became provincial judge of Zhili, his advancement went smoothly.
张振轩那时虽只是一个道员，可是一升直隶臬司，一帆风顺，

By the tenth year of Tongzhi, he was governor-general of Grain Transport and acting governor-general of Liangjiang.
同治十年就以漕运总督署理两江总督。

Li Hongzhang naturally did much to promote him.
他之得意，李合肥自然很提携他，

Their relationship was closer than most.
关系交情不同泛泛，

So when Li left office to mourn his mother and specially recommended Zhang to act in his place, he had a purpose.”
反以这回李合肥丁忧开缺，特保张振轩署理，自然是有作用的。』

“Ah, I see!” Hu exclaimed.
『啊，啊，我懂了。』胡雪岩恍然大悟，

“He was simply leaving Zhang to mind the house for him.”
『原来他是替李合肥暂且看家。』

“Exactly.
『正是。

What Li did not realize was that his former subordinate was no longer the unpolished junior he had once known.
不过，李合肥不知道，昔日部属，已非吴下防蒙，

Zhang had made connections with the moral critics.
张振轩跟清流结交上了，

That began three years ago—” In the eleventh month of the fifth year of Guangxu, Shen Baozhen, governor-general of Liangjiang, died in office.
那是大前年——』大前年——光绪五年十一月，两江总督沈葆桢病殁在任上，

The court transferred Liu Kunyi, governor-general of Guangdong and Guangxi, to Liangjiang, and promoted Zhang Shusheng from governor of Guangxi to fill the vacancy he left.
朝命以两广总督刘坤一调任两江；留下来的缺，由张树声以广西巡抚升任。


Guangzhou had an Eight Banner garrison.
广州是八旗驻防之地，

Its general, Changshan, belonged to the Tatara clan, one of the eight great Manchu noble houses.
广州将军叫长善，出身满洲八大贵族之一的他他拉氏。

He was a cultivated man, eager to encourage younger talent and, particularly, free of prejudice between Manchus and Han Chinese.
此人很风雅，乐予奖掖后进，尤其是没有满汉的畛域之见。

The rear garden of his headquarters was renowned for its flowers and trees, and he often invited the city’s promising young scholars to gather there for literary conversation and wine.
将军署的后花园，颇有花木之胜，长善常常邀请广州的一班少年名士作文酒之会。

At the metropolitan examination in the gengchen year, two years before, Minister of Works Weng Tonghe had presided, allowing men of genuine learning to distinguish themselves.
前年庚辰科会试，闱中由工部尚书翁同齸主持，实学真才多能脱颖而出，

Liang Dingfen of Guangdong and Yu Shimei of Guangxi, both frequent guests of Changshan, had been selected for the Hanlin Academy.
其广东的梁鼎芬、广西的于式枚便常常作长善座上客，而且都点了翰林。

Zhang Shusheng’s son, Zhang Huakui, had also often received Changshan’s invitations while in Guangzhou.
在广州时，张树声的儿子张华奎，亦常受长善的招邀，

He was therefore a close friend of Yu and Liang, and of Wen Tingshi from Jiangxi, whose literary reputation surpassed theirs but who had unfortunately failed the metropolitan examination.
所以跟于式枚、梁鼎芬，还有一个文名盛於于、梁但禀表会试不幸落第的江西人文廷式。都是极熟的朋友。

Having accompanied his father to the governor-generalship of Zhili, Zhang Huakui now frequently visited the capital and spent time with these three friends.
这时张华奎随父到直隶总督任上，便经常进京，与于、梁、文等三人盘桓。


Though old friendships were warm and meeting familiar faces away from home was a pleasure, Zhang Huakui had another object.
虽说他乡遇故，旧雨情深，但张华奎却是另有企图。

In recent years, officials entitled to remonstrate had acquired immense influence.
原来这几年言路的势力极大，

The Hanlin scholars who also lectured to the throne were especially powerful: their words carried such weight that even the Empress Dowager Cixi and Prince Gong could not disregard them.
尤其是一班兼讲官的翰林，一言九鼎，连慈禧太后及恭王都不能不听，

These were the “Pure Stream,” the court’s moral critics; the four most famous were known as the Four Hanlin Remonstrants.
这班人就是『清流』，其中最有名的四个人，号为『翰林四谏』。

Yu Shimei and Liang Dingfen were junior Hanlin members, but their longstanding literary distinction brought them into frequent contact with these men.
于式枚、梁鼎芬虽是翰林后辈，但文名久著，所以亦常与清流有往还；

Through Yu and Liang, Zhang Huakui gained access to such leading critics as Zhang Peilun and Sheng Wu.
而张华奎便是凭借了于、梁的关系，得以上文张佩纶、盛吴这一班响当当大清流。

Zhang Huakui was a provincial graduate, young but capable, with a modest and agreeable disposition.
这张华奎是个举人，年纪虽轻，人很能干，而且赋性廉和可亲，

Moreover, the Beiyang branch office had accumulated substantial “public funds,” against which any expense incurred in entertaining metropolitan officials could be charged.
加以『北洋分所』积存的『公款』很多，凡是应酬京官，无不可以报销，

This gave his talents still greater scope.
使得张华奎愈发长袖善舞，

Wherever the critics met—at Songyun Hermitage, the Shrine of Former Worthies of the Capital Region, such celebrated places as Taoran Pavilion and Chongxiao Temple, or even the lodgings of male entertainers in the Eight Great Lanes—he provided their banquets.
清流们集会，不论是在松筠庵，还是『畿辅先哲寺』，或者陶然亭、崇效寺这些名胜之处，乃至于八大胡同『相公』的下处，筵宴所需，都是他来备办，

Whenever errands or arrangements were required, he willingly undertook them.
有事需要奔走联络，张华奎更是义不容辞，

Thus he acquired the nickname “the Green Ox’s Legs.”
因而得了个『青牛腿』的外号。


“Green ox,” qingniu, sounded like qingliu, “Pure Stream.”
『青牛』是清流的谐音。

Every household had its picture of the spring ox.
民间家家有『春牛图』，

Spring belonged to the east, the east to wood, and wood to the colour green; a green ox was therefore a spring ox.
春为东，东为木，木色青，所以『青牛』也就是春牛。

In these pictures the head, body, horns, ears, belly, tail, legs, and hooves were distinctly marked.
画春牛图时，头、身、角、耳、腹、尾、胫、蹄、部位分明。

Wits accordingly used the various parts to represent members of the Pure Stream.
因而好事者，用青牛的各部分，来形容清流中人，

The head was Li Hongzao, tutor to the Tongzhi emperor; his two followers named Zhang—Zhang Zhidong and Zhang Peilun—were the body and belly.
牛头是同治皇帝的师傅李鸿藻，他门下两张——张之洞、张佩纶是牛身、牛腹。

Others said Li Hongzao was Goumang, the deity driving the ox, and that Zhang Peilun was its head: no one could withstand those two horns, and anyone they struck suffered grievously.
也有人说，李鸿藻是驱牛的勾芒神，张佩纶才是牛头，因为他头上的一对角厉害不过，凡被触及，必受巨创。

Since Zhang Huakui did the running about for the critics, they called him the legs.
张华奎因为替清流效奔走之劳，所以名之为『腿』；

Some, however, said he was not qualified even for that: he was merely the Pure Stream’s boots, serving its legs.
但也有人说，他连『清流腿』都不够资格，只是『清流靴子』为『清流腿』服务而已。


Whether legs or boots, Zhang Huakui certainly attracted attention.
不管是『清流腿』还是『清流靴子』，张华奎很受人瞩目是事实。

He also incurred the hostility of Li Hongzhang’s followers, who accused him of disloyal ambitions.
不过因此而引起了李鸿章门下的敌视，认为他『图谋不轨』，

First, he constantly cultivated Weng Tonghe, who had always been at odds with Li Hongzhang.
第一是因为他常巴结翁同齸，而翁同齸一向是与李鸿章不睦，

Most of the Pure Stream, meanwhile, followed Li Hongzao, leader of the northern faction, whereas Weng was a leading figure of the southern faction; their political views had always differed.
同时清流多为北派领袖李鸿藻门下，而翁同齸是南派巨擘，对政事的见解，一向是有差异的；

Second, Zhang Huakui’s energetic courtship of the critics plainly aimed to build up his father’s reputation so that he could replace Li Hongzhang.
第二，张华奎拼命拉拢清流，显然是在为他父亲培养声名，目的是想取李鸿章而代之。

Embellished accusations of this kind reached Hefei continually.
这些加油添酱的谗言，不断传到合肥，

Secluded there in the ritual observances of mourning, Li himself began to grow suspicious.
在『闭门读礼』的李鸿章不由得也动了疑心。

His followers accordingly started planning to oust Zhang and bring Li back.
他的一班徒党，因而开始谋划逐张迎李之计，

Before long they found their opportunity.
不久便找到了可乘之机。


Zhang Peilun was full of learning and eager to put it to public use.
原来张佩纶满腹经纶，颇有用世之志，

Zhang Huakui therefore advised his father to follow Zuo Zongtang’s earlier example: Zuo had enhanced his own standing by petitioning for Yuan Baoheng’s services.
张华奎便向他父献计，仿照当年左宗棠奏调袁葆恒来提高本人声价的办法，

Why not request Zhang Peilun as assistant commissioner for Beiyang military affairs, with particular responsibility for the navy?
不妨奏调张佩纶『帮办北洋军务』，专门督办水师。

Once his father agreed, Huakui worked hard to persuade Zhang Peilun.
张树声同意以后，张华奎极力向张佩纶游尽；

The Beiyang fleet already possessed several ironclad warships; its scale was impressive and its prospects boundless.
那时产洋的水师，已拥有好几艘铁甲兵轮，规模壮阔，前程无量，

Zhang was greatly tempted and eventually consented.
张佩纶怦然心动，终于同意了。

News soon spread through Tianjin, Baoding, and elsewhere.
于是天津、保定等处，很快地传出消息，

It was also said that, once appointed, Zhang would institute sweeping reforms and impeach all “four intendants and eight brigade commanders.”
还说张佩纶帮办北洋军务后，将大加整顿，『四道八镇』，一律要参。

The governor-general of Zhili had four circuit intendants and eight brigade commanders under him; each brigade’s station was called a zhen.
直隶总督属下，有四名道员，八名总兵，总兵驻防之地称为『镇』；

The phrase thus denoted the province’s regular civil and military establishment.
四道八镇便是直隶文武官员的经制，

All these men had, of course, been appointed by Li Hongzhang.
当然全部都是李鸿章所派的。


At this critical moment, however, Zhang Shusheng and his son made a false move. They were overhasty and unfamiliar with the precedents governing court appointments.
不道在此要紧关头，张树声父子一则操之过急；二则不明京朝掌故，以至于走错了一步。

Provincial governors-general and governors could petition to have metropolitan officials transferred to their provinces, but not members of the Hanlin Academy.
原来封疆大吏，准许奏调京官到省任职，但不准奏调翰林。

The prohibition had been particularly strict under Qianlong.
这个禁例在乾隆年间更为严格。

Hanlin scholars who also served as daily lecturers and recorders of the emperor’s activities could be summoned to his presence at any time and knew his every word and action.
因为翰林如兼日讲起居注官，随传在皇帝身边，一言一动，无不深知；

They also had opportunities to read memorials and share in state secrets.
而且有机会看到各种奏章，参预国家机密，

Transferring them at a provincial chief’s request created a risk of disclosure; hence the strict rule.
如为疆吏所奏调，便有泄密之虞，因而有此厉禁。

Since the Taiping rebellion, enforcement had relaxed, but two questions still mattered: whether the petitioner had sufficient standing, and whether the appointment was truly necessary at that moment.
到得洪杨以后，禁例虽不如以前之严，但第一要看请奏调的人，够不够分量；第二要奏调的时机，是否确有需要。

When Zuo requested Yuan Baoheng to direct his supply administration, he was a distinguished commander honoured with a noble title and high ministerial rank, about to undertake a major western campaign in which Yuan’s assistance was essential.
当年左宗棠是封拜相的勋臣；奏调袁葆恒总理粮台，又有正当大举西征，用兵深资倚赖的理由，

Approval had naturally been easy to obtain.
自然容易照准。

Zhang Shusheng’s standing fell far short of Zuo’s, and there was no pressing military need.
如今张树声的资格远不如左宗棠，且亦非军务所必需，

When his memorial requesting Zhang Peilun reached the Grand Council, it was rejected by imperial order.
因而请奏调张佩纶的折子一到军机处，竟奉旨驳斥。

This sharp rebuff gravely damaged the governor-general’s prestige; for Zhang Peilun, it was still more humiliating.
这一下不但张树声以封疆大吏碰这么个硬钉子，大伤威望，张佩纶的面子更加难看。


Zhang Peilun considered himself worthy of being “the honoured guest of princes.”
照张佩纶的想法，他应该是『诸侯之上客』，

Zhang Shusheng ought to have treated him with the reverence of a pupil towards a master.
张树声应该北面以师礼相事，

Merely agreeing to assist with Beiyang military affairs had already seemed a concession.
如今答应帮办北洋军务，已嫌委屈；

If the governor-general truly wished to recruit him, he should have arranged matters with the Grand Council and secured a special imperial appointment to Beiyang, giving him the dignity he deserved.
张树声果然有心延揽，应该设法疏通军机，用『特旨』派他到北洋，才够面子。

Now the edict rebuked Zhang Shusheng for presumption.
加今上谕中责备张树声『冒昧』，

Presumptuous he certainly had been!
确是太冒昧了。

Li Hongzhang’s officials in Beiyang rejoiced at the rebuff.
李鸿章一系的北洋官僚，看到张树声碰钉子，自然高兴；

Hearing that Zhang Peilun had expressed resentment towards father and son, they were delighted beyond measure.
又听说张佩纶对张家父子有不满的表示，更是大喜过望，

Such an opportunity to divide them must not be lost.
认为挑拨离间的良机，决不可失。

It happened that, on the day Zhang Shusheng submitted his memorial, an examination was held for prospective provincial examiners.
恰好张树声上奏的那天有『考差』——

Metropolitan officials with provincial or metropolitan degrees had to pass it before being sent out to preside over provincial examinations.
两榜出身的京官，须经考试合格，才能放出去当乡试主考；

One such appointment could provide enough income for a year or two, so few men willingly missed the qualifying examination.
一任考官，所得可以维持一两年的生活，所以绝少有人放弃考差；

Zhang Peilun, however, was in mourning and ineligible for appointment; he therefore had no need to sit it.
但张佩纶因为有丧服在身，不能派任考官，考差自然不必参加。

Outsiders did not know this.
这个缘故，外人不会知道，

Malicious people could thus spread a rumour that he had deliberately stayed away, waiting at home for the edict approving Zhang Shusheng’s request so that he could set off for his new post.
因而别有用心者，就可以造他一个谣言，说他故意避考，在家等待准为张树声所请的上谕，以便走马上任。

The slander spread swiftly and widely.
这个中伤的谣言，传布得很快也很广；

His reputation for integrity suffered severely, and his humiliation turned to anger—naturally redirected at the Zhangs.
张佩纶的清誉大损，不免恼羞成怒，自然是迁怒到张家父子身上。


“Everyone knows how narrow-minded the Academician from Fengrun is.
『丰润学士的气量小是大家都知道的，

He will certainly take revenge.
他一定会复仇，

Zhang Zhenxuan has outsmarted himself and cannot possibly keep Zhili,” Sha said. “All that is needed now is a reason for Li Hongzhang to be recalled before completing his mourning.
张振轩弄巧成拙，直督一定保不住。』沙一心说∶『现在只是在一个可以让李合肥夺情回任的理由，

Once they find one, Zhang must hand over the office.”
这个理由一找到，张振轩就要交卸。』

This inside information was useful to Hu.
这段内幕，对胡雪岩很有用；

He had assumed that even if Li returned, it would be only after the twenty-seven months of parental mourning.
原以为李鸿章即会回任，也是父母之丧二十七个月以后的事，

Yet with a suitable reason, he could return at any time.
不过只要有理由，随时可以回。

Zuo ought therefore to accelerate his efforts to expel Li’s influence from Liangjiang.
照此看来，左宗棠想驱逐李鸿章在两江的势力，应该加速进行才是。

By now Sha’s craving for opium had been satisfied.
其时沙一心的瘾已过足，

Hu accompanied him to Fourth Miss Xiangyun’s rooms, where they drank merrily, passing the cups beneath the moon.
便由胡雪岩陪着到湘云老四妆阁中，飞觞醉月地闹了一回酒。

When Sha rose to leave, the other guests remembered that Hu and Gu were to accompany Zuo on an inspection of the arsenal early the next morning and also took their leave.
沙一心起身告辞，余客亦知胡雪岩与古应春第二天一早要左宗棠巡视制造局，都说要走，

Only Lin Maoxian stayed on for a “dry bed” at Fourth Miss Xiangyun’s.
只有林茂先在湘云老四那里『借干铺』。

“Sha Yixin is a useful man,” Hu told Gu on the way home. “You might keep in closer touch with him.
『沙一心这个人很有用，』在归途中，胡雪岩对古应春说∶『你以后不妨跟他多联络联络，

He knows the Huai Army and Beiyang well.
他对淮军及北洋的情形很熟，

We can ask him to make inquiries when necessary.”
有事可以请他打听。』

“That was my intention from the beginning.
『我的原意就是如此。

Leave it to me, Little Uncle.
小爷叔放心好了，

I shall arrange it.”
我会安排。』


The Jiangnan Arsenal stood at Gaochangmiao, on the Huangpu River outside Shanghai’s county town.
江南制造局在上海县城外，濒临黄浦江的高昌庙，

The site had once been wasteland, but since Zeng Guofan petitioned for the arsenal’s establishment, settlement had steadily increased.
本来是一片荒地，自从曾国藩奏请设制造局以后，人烟日起，

A gravel road led east to the town’s south gate.
造一条石子马路，东通县城南门。

That day, however, Zuo boarded a boat in front of his temporary headquarters behind the Temple of the Heavenly Empress and travelled straight down the Huangpu to the arsenal’s own landing.
不过左宗棠这天仍旧是在天前宫后辕前面下船，沿黄浦江直达制造局的专用码头，

Its director, the expectant circuit intendant Li Mianlin, received him in his large green-cloth sedan chair and conveyed him to the main hall.
制造局的总办，候补道李勉林用他的绿呢大轿，将左宗棠接到大堂，

There he presented his subordinate officers, who paid their respects in turn, before asking which section His Excellency wished to see first.
然后引见属员，一一参谒。接下来请示∶先看哪一处？

“The dockyard,” Zuo replied. “When I took leave of the throne last year, particular emphasis was laid upon maritime defence, and I was instructed to give it special attention.
『先看船坞吧』左宗棠说∶『我去年陛辞出京，上头特别交代，洋防要紧，要我分外留意。

Your dockyard may be smaller than Fujian’s, but it is nevertheless China’s second shipyard.
制造局的船坞，规模虽不及福建，到底是中国第二个造船厂，

If its men, grounds, and materials are all put to their fullest use, it can contribute substantially to our coastal defences.”
能人尽其用、地尽其用、物尽其用，对洋防亦颇有裨益。』

This opening address carried a hint of reprimand.
这一段开场白，便有些教训的意义，

Li Mianlin was naturally uncomfortable, and his embarrassment showed.
李勉林听入耳中，当然不很舒服，脸上不免有尴尬之色，

Hu intervened with a few words in Li’s favour and managed to smooth the occasion over.
见此光景，胡雪岩便在一旁替李勉林说好话，总算将场面圆过来了。


The dockyard was in such confusion that Zuo could make little of it.
船坞中乱糟糟一片，看不出一个名堂来，

He had to ask directly. “Grand Guardian Peng spends the year inspecting the Yangtze’s estuary and its river and sea defences.
左宗棠只好问了∶『彭宫保整年巡阅长江海口、江防、洋防的形势，

He has surveyed every aspect and written to me expressing concern that there are too few warships to meet our needs.
周览无遗，写信给我，以兵船不敷调度为虑，

He says at least ten small steamers must be added.
说至少要添造小火轮十号，

I think even ten are insufficient; it would be better to build five vessels on the latest fast-ship model as well.
照我看，十号亦还不够，最好再能仿造新式快船五艘，

Can you construct them here?”
你看你这里能不能造？』

“We can build small steamers.
『小火轮能造，

The new fast vessels are beyond the capacity of our machinery.”
新式快船，限于机器，力所不逮。』

“Then how much would each small steamer cost?”
『那末，造小火轮每一号要多少钱呢？』

“We should have to prepare an estimate.”
『这要估起来看。』

Again the conversation threatened to become awkward.
话又有些碰僵了，

Fortunately Zuo took no offence, asking only how long the estimate would require.
幸好左宗棠没有在意只问∶『要多少日子才能估得出来？』

“For accuracy, we should still have to consult the Fujian Naval Dockyard.
『估价欲求精确，还得找福建船政局，

They have complete plans and fuller information on material prices.
他们那里图说全备，材料的行情也比较准。

If Your Excellency is determined to proceed, we shall send someone to Fujian at once.
大人如果决意要造，局里马上派人到福建，

The detailed figures should be ready in about a month.”
大概有一个月的工夫，细帐就可以出来了。』

“Very well.
『好！

Please see to it immediately.”
请你马上就办。

Beside the dockyard stood the arms factory, which greatly interested Zuo.
『船坞旁边就是枪炮厂，左宗棠对这里很感兴趣，

His western campaigns had owed much to superior equipment, and he was particularly knowledgeable about foreign rifles.
因为西征，得力就在器械精良；尤其是对洋枪，他已经很内行了，

Yet for all his experience of seeing and using them, this was his first opportunity to observe their manufacture.
但看得多，用得多，洋枪如何制成，却还是初次见识，

Beginning with the steelworks, he inspected every department closely.
所以从炼钢厂看起，每一部门都看得很仔细。


At last they reached the inspection section, which had a firing range attached.
最后到了检验处，附设有个靶场，

Shots cracked noisily.
乒乓乒乓地声音很热闹。

As Zuo entered, an old man jumped down from a high stool and retreated to one side.
左宗棠一踏了进去，坐在高凳上的一个老头子跳了下来，躲到一边；

“Master Yao,” Li Mianlin called, “come and pay your respects to His Excellency Zuo!”
李勉林便喊∶『姚司务，见见左大人！』

Yao’s face was red as fire and his hair white as silver.
这姚司务面红似火，发白如银，

His eyes differed in size; the larger, the right, shone brightly.
一双眼一大一小，大的那只右眼，炯炯有神；

His arms were likewise mismatched, one thick and the other thin.
手臂亦是一粗一细，侔不相伦。

Zuo had seen much of the world and encountered many unusual people, but Yao’s singular appearance caught his attention at once.
左宗棠平生阅历甚富，看过不少异人；一看这姚司务形相古怪，不由得便加了几分注意。

When the old man had risen from his kowtow, Li said, “Master Yao is one of the arsenal’s treasures.
等姚司务磕过一个头起身，李勉林便看着左宗棠说∶『这姚司务是制造局一宝，

Any rifle that passes through his hands is certain to shoot true.”
不管什么枪，经他手里出去的，「准头」一定好。』

Ordnance was Zuo’s keenest interest.
『喔，』左宗棠对军械的兴趣最浓，

Looking up, he asked, “Is this where you test the rifles?”
当下抬起头来，看了一下问∶『这就是你验枪的所在？』

“Yes,” Li answered for Yao.
『是。』李勉林代为回答。

“How do you test them?”
『怎么验法？』

“Your Excellency may find it hard to believe.
『说起来大人恐怕不信，

He takes one look down the sights and fires one shot; then he knows.”
他只是瞄一眼、开一枪就知道了。』

“That is extraordinary skill!” Zuo said with pleasure. “I should like to see it.”
『这倒是神乎其技了。』左宗棠欣然说道∶『我倒要见识见识。』

“Certainly.”
『是。』

Li turned to Yao. “Give His Excellency a demonstration.”
李勉林转脸对姚司务说∶『你演练演练给大人看。』


Yao seemed so inarticulate that he could not even say yes.
姚司务似乎很木讷，连一声『是』都不会答应，

He merely nodded and moved the high stool away, intending to test the rifles standing up.
只点一点头去掇开那张高凳，意思是站着验枪。

“No, no!”
『不，不！』

Zuo stopped him hastily.
左宗棠急忙阻止，

“Do as you normally do.
『你照平常一样。

If you usually sit, sit now.”
平常坐着，现在不是坐着。』

Yao dared make no response.
姚司务不敢答应，

Li had to tell him, “Do as His Excellency instructs.”
仍旧须李勉林说一声∶『你照大人的吩咐。』

Only then did Yao replace the stool, climb its attached steps, and sit down.
姚司务这才又将高凳搬回原处，踩着凳上所附的踏级，坐了上去。

Before him, walls enclosed a long, narrow passage.
他面前是用墙砌出来的，狭长的一条弄堂，

At its far end stood a target of six concentric circles, its centre riddled with bullet marks.
尽头处是个六个同心圆的靶子，中心弹痕累累；

Yao called loudly, “A new target!”
姚司务便大声喊道∶『换个靶！』


Someone behind the target attended to it, and a fresh one appeared immediately.
枪靶后面有人在照料，顿时换了新靶。

Zuo noticed two long wooden chests on Yao’s left and two large baskets on his right, with rifles heaped carelessly inside.
左宗棠看他左面摆着两个长木箱，右面又有两个大箩筐，里面乱堆着枪枝，

The long chests held newly repaired rifles, tended by another man.
长木箱中是刚修好的枪，有个人在照管。

“Here!” Yao called.
『来！』听得姚司务这一声，

The attendant took a rifle and tossed it up to him.
那人便取一枝枪，抛了上去，

Yao caught it in his left hand, transferred it to his right, and narrowed his eyes for a glance.
姚司务左手接住，交到右手，眯起眼睛看了一下，

A shot rang out, followed almost immediately by a thump: he had thrown the rifle into the nearer basket.
便即听得『砰』的一声；接着又听得『彭』的一声，那枝枪已被他扔在前面那个箩筐里了。

Zuo had not even seen clearly how he pulled the trigger with one hand.
左宗棠根本没有看清楚，他是如何单手在扣扳机，

The little hole in the exact centre of the new target, however, was plain enough.
不过新靶上正中红心有个小洞，却看得很清楚。
