胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement
第三章（本章第2部分，共4部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


听这时又是『砰砰彭彭』好一阵，
Now came a rapid succession of cracks and thumps.

有的枪丢在外面箩筐，有的枪丢在里面箩筐，不过外面少，里面多。
Some rifles went into the outer basket and others into the inner one, though far fewer went into the outer.

『是这样，』李勉林为左宗棠解释，『丢在外面的，没有修好，拿回去重修；
“Those in the outer basket have not been properly repaired and must go back,” Li Mianlin explained.

丢在里面的，是修好了的。』
“Those in the inner one are ready.”

左宗棠有些不大相信，
Zuo was not entirely convinced.

『就这么看一眼、放一枪，就能听得出来？』
“One glance and one shot, and he can tell by the sound?

他说∶『似乎有点不可思议。』
It seems scarcely credible.”

『是！是有点不可思议，不过确实如此。』
“Indeed it does, but that is how he does it.”

『我倒有点不明白。』
“There is something I do not understand.”

左宗棠便趁空隙喊道∶『姚司务！
During a pause, Zuo called, “Master Yao!

姚司务！』
Master Yao!”

那姚司务纹风不动，恍若未闻，
The old man did not stir, as though he had heard nothing.

李勉林赶紧又解释，
Li hastened to explain.

『他重听，
“He is hard of hearing.

耳鼓让枪声震坏。
Gunfire has damaged his eardrums.

平时说话，只看人的嘴。』
In ordinary conversation he watches people’s lips.”

接着他走上前去，拍一拍姚务的身后，让他下来。
He stepped forward, tapped Yao on the back, and signalled him to come down.


『姚司务，』左宗棠问∶『你今年多大？』
“How old are you, Master Yao?” Zuo asked.

『六十六岁。』
“Sixty-six.”

『你玩枪玩了少年了？』
“How many years have you been handling guns?”

姚司务屈指算了一下∶『四十八年。』
Yao counted on his fingers. “Forty-eight.”

左宗棠也在心里略为算了一下说∶『这么说，你在道光那年就干这一行了？』
Zuo made a quick calculation of his own. “Then you were already in this trade during the Daoguang reign?”

『是。』
“Yes.”

『你跟谁学的？』
“Who taught you?”

『先是德国人，后来是英国人。』
“First a German, then an Englishman.”

『喔！』左宗棠问∶『你说德国的枪好，还是英国的枪好？』
“And which guns do you think better, German or English?”

『德国。』
“German.”


听这一说，左宗棠便回身去看，
Zuo turned to look behind him.

胡雪岩知道是找他，便从一大堆官员中挤上前去。
Knowing whom he sought, Hu pushed forward through the crowd of officials.

『雪岩，』左宗棠问道∶『福克来了没有？』
“Xueyan, has Focke come?”

『没有。』
“No.

胡雪岩问∶『大人有什么吩咐？
Have you any instructions for him, Your Excellency?

我马上告诉他。』
I shall pass them on at once.”

『我是要找一枝「温者斯得」的枪。』
“I want one of those Winchester rifles.”

『呃，』胡雪岩答说∶『我已经分派给新兵，
“I have already issued them to the recruits.

在用了。』
They are using them.”

『好、好！
“Good.

拿一枝来。』
Bring one here.”

这枝枪是交到姚司务手里，问他见过没有？
The rifle was placed in Yao’s hands, and he was asked whether he had seen one before.

答说没有。
He had not.

不过他只略为看了一下，便转开一个螺丝，接着一样一样拆了下来，
Yet after a brief examination, he turned a screw and began removing the parts one by one.

不过几分钟的工夫，一枝新枪成了一堆零件。
In a few minutes, the new rifle lay in pieces.

这显出真工夫来了，左宗棠不能不服他，
This was genuine skill, and Zuo could not withhold his admiration.

当下问道∶『这枪好不好？』
“Is it a good rifle?” he asked.

那姚司务竟不回答，只看着李勉林。
Yao gave no answer, merely looking at Li Mianlin.

左宗棠不知是怎么回事；胡雪岩却看出来了，
Zuo did not understand, but Hu did.

姚司务一说好，左宗棠说不定马上就会交代购买那一种。那一来，岂不断了采购委员的财路。
If Yao pronounced it good, Zuo might immediately order rifles of that kind, cutting off the purchasing officers’ opportunities for profit.

因此，胡雪岩便说一句∶『只怕不见得好。』
Hu therefore remarked, “Perhaps it is not so good.”


谁知李勉林恰好相反，
Unexpectedly, Li took the opposite line.

连连说道∶『好，好，好得很。』
“Good, good—excellent!” he repeated.

表面彼此客气，实际上已等于短兵相接，也是彼此猜忌。
On the surface they were being polite; in reality, they were already crossing swords and regarding one another with suspicion.

本来江南制造局是李鸿章的禁脔，
The Jiangnan Arsenal was Li Hongzhang’s exclusive preserve.

不管自造也好，外购也好，都轮不到胡雪岩来插手，
Whether weapons were manufactured there or purchased abroad, Hu had no part in the business.

所以他之说『怕不见得好』，便有不愿跟制造局『抢生意』的意味在内；
His remark that the rifle might not be so good therefore signalled that he did not intend to compete for the arsenal’s trade.

反过来说，他如果要『抢生意』，唾手可得。
Conversely, if he chose to compete, that trade was within easy reach.

这就使李勉林深深感到，劲敌当前，必须小心了。
Li Mianlin felt keenly that a formidable rival stood before him and that caution was necessary.

这笔买『温者斯得』来福枪的生意，自然还是归胡雪岩，
The purchase of these Winchester rifles did, naturally, come Hu’s way.

但大发利市的却是福克。
The man who profited handsomely, however, was Focke.


原来这种枪的在华代理权，属于福克的洋行，
Focke’s firm held the Chinese agency for these rifles.

第一批进了五百枝，四处兜销，只卖去一百多，
Of the first five hundred imported, barely more than a hundred had been sold despite efforts in every direction.

起初亦并未想到左宗棠，因为他知道西征军中来福枪极多，左宗棠甚至还送了一批给醇王，供神机营使用。
He had not initially thought of Zuo, knowing that the western armies already had many rifles; Zuo had even sent a consignment to Prince Chun for use by the Shenji Battalion.

及至听说胡雪岩要到上海，心想左宗棠的『小队』也许要用这种比较精良的新枪，送了二十枝当样品，
Hearing that Hu was coming to Shanghai, however, Focke thought Zuo’s personal detachment might need these superior new weapons and sent twenty as samples.

估量着，即使能做到这笔生意，充其量也不过百把枝，
Even if this produced an order, he expected no more than a hundred or so.

库存还有一半，不知销场何在？
Half his original stock would still remain, with no buyer in sight.

哪知由胡雪岩转来的消息，说要买两千五百枝，预备分发江南各防营使用。
Then word came through Hu: Zuo wanted two thousand five hundred, to be distributed among the various Jiangnan garrisons.

福克喜出望外，却又发愁，
Focke was delighted, but troubled as well.

因为能够供应的现贷，连个零头都不足。
The goods available for immediate delivery did not amount even to the odd five hundred.

『胡先生，』福克透过古应春的翻译，向胡雪岩说∶『我拿库中存货先交，其余的，准备三个月内交齐；
“Mr Hu,” he said through Gu’s interpretation, “I shall deliver what I have in stock first and supply the rest within three months.

我回国去一趟，专门办这件事。
I shall go home myself expressly to arrange it.”


胡雪碉便跟古应春商量，
Hu discussed this with Gu.

他亦看出李勉林对他深具戒心，
He too had seen how wary Li Mianlin was of him.

认为不宜一开始就树敌，免得以后的障碍愈来愈多。
It would be unwise to make an enemy at the outset and encounter increasing obstacles thereafter.

这笔军火是左宗棠亲自交代，不能不办；正愁着李勉林会『吃味』，
Since Zuo had personally ordered these arms, he could hardly refuse to act; yet he feared Li’s resentment.

难得福克供应不足，恰好打消了这笔生意，避免得罪李勉林。
Focke’s insufficient stock offered a convenient reason to abandon the transaction and avoid offending Li.

他将他的意思告诉了给古应春，
He explained this to Gu.

又说∶『我看就此推掉为妙。
“We had better turn it down now.

你跟他说，马上要用，
Tell him the rifles are needed immediately.

要现货，没有现货就免谈了。』
We must have them in stock, or there is nothing to discuss.”

『这话他不会相认的。』
“He will not accept that explanation.

古应春说∶『小爷叔在左大人面前讲话的分量，他不是不知道，
He knows how much influence you have with His Excellency.

哪一次买军火都是先送样品，看中意了再下定单，
Arms purchases always begin with samples; if they are approved, an order follows.

如今说全部都要现货，不是明明为难他？』
To insist now that the entire quantity be available at once would plainly be making difficulties for him.”

『这话倒也是。』
“That is true.”

胡雪岩踌躇了一会说∶『这样，你叫他自己去看左大人。
Hu hesitated. “Then let him see His Excellency himself.

而且我们要避嫌疑，你叫他先到制造局去看李观察，请李观察带他去见左大人。
To avoid any suspicion, have him first visit Intendant Li at the arsenal and ask Li to take him to His Excellency.

生意成不成，看他自己的运气。』
Whether he secures the business will depend on his luck.”

『这办法！行得通吗？』古应春不免怀疑，
“Will that work?” Gu asked doubtfully.

『我们犯不着把自己的路子，交给人家。』
“We need not hand our own connections over to someone else.”

『不！
“No.

现在他们怕我们防得厉害，犯不着为这点小事，做成个死对头。
They are so afraid of us and so much on their guard that it would be foolish to make a sworn enemy over this small matter.

不如现在大方一点，以后办事反而顺手。』
A little generosity now will make future business easier.”

古应春心想，这是欲取姑予的手法，亦未尝不可用。
Gu reflected that giving now in order to gain later was a legitimate tactic.

两千五百枝枪的佣金，虽至少有五千佣金，别人看来是个大数目，但在胡雪岩眼中，却是小事；
The commission on two thousand five hundred rifles would be at least five thousand taels—a large sum to others, but a trifle to Hu.

既然他要『大方』，就照他的意思办好了。
Since he wished to be generous, Gu would do as he asked.


但胡雪岩的顾虑与打算，福克是怎么样也无从知道的，
Focke could have no knowledge of Hu’s anxieties or calculations.

因此一听古应春的话，大感困惑，
He was bewildered by Gu’s message.

多年合作得好好地，何以有这种见拒的态度？
After so many years of successful cooperation, why this rejection?

莫非胡雪岩在左宗棠面前，说话已经没有力量了，还是另有其他原因？
Had Hu lost his influence with Zuo, or was there some other reason?

当下率直向古应春发问。
He questioned Gu frankly.

古应春当然不能跟他说实话，只说胡雪岩是尊重江南制造局。
Unable to tell him the truth, Gu said only that Hu wished to respect the Jiangnan Arsenal’s position.

这话在福克半信半疑，
Focke half believed him.

他在华多年，官场中的情形，亦相当了解，向来是谁有办法，谁就可以争权夺利；
After many years in China, he understood official life fairly well: whoever had the means could contend for power and profit.

权责并不分明，尊重更是假话。
Authority and responsibility were ill-defined; talk of respect was still less credible.

福克做事很老练，先去打听胡雪岩在左宗棠那里的『行情』，所得到的答复是绝未失宠。
An experienced operator, Focke first inquired into Hu’s standing with Zuo and learned that he had certainly not fallen from favour.

这一来，他就不能不怀疑，另有人在钻军火生意的路子，想取他而代之；胡雪岩是一种让他知难而退的态度。
He therefore began to suspect that someone else was trying to supplant him in the arms trade, and that Hu meant to discourage him into withdrawing.

去问古应春，古应春绝口否认。
Gu denied this absolutely.

这一下，福克释然了，
Focke then felt reassured.

中国官场不足跟外人道的花样很多，不必去多打听。
Chinese officialdom had many complications that could not be explained to foreigners; there was no need to investigate them all.

反正自己仍旧抱定利益均沾的宗旨，将胡雪岩拉紧了，保持了多年合作的关系，总是不错的。
In any event, he could not go wrong by holding to his principle of sharing profits, keeping Hu close, and preserving their longstanding cooperation.


于是福克便带了一名翻译到制造局求见李勉林。
Focke therefore took an interpreter to the arsenal to see Li Mianlin.

那时的官场，对洋人都是另眼看待，何况福克是上海洋商领袖之一，
Officials gave foreigners special treatment in those days, and Focke was one of Shanghai’s leading foreign merchants.

所以名刺一报进去，正在花厅中会客的李勉林，丢下他人，在签押房接见福克。
As soon as his card arrived, Li left the guests he was receiving in the garden reception room and met him in his private office.

动问来意，福克透过翻译说道∶『左大人要买两千五百枝温者斯得来福枪，
Asked his business, Focke explained through the interpreter: “His Excellency Zuo wishes to purchase two thousand five hundred Winchester rifles.

可是我现货只有三百多枝，其余准三个月内交足。
I have only three hundred and some in stock, but can guarantee delivery of the rest within three months.

胡观察说不行，要我来见李观察，请你带我去见左大人当面谈。』
Intendant Hu says that will not do and has sent me to ask you to take me to His Excellency so that I may discuss it with him in person.”

听得这话，李勉林不免诧异，
Li was surprised.

定购西洋军火，向来都是期货；
Foreign arms were normally ordered for future delivery.

目前内外无事，又不是打仗遇到劲敌，急需要精良武器才足以克制，何必一定非现货不可？
With no present disturbance at home or abroad, and no formidable enemy in battle requiring superior weapons urgently, why insist on immediate availability?

仔细想一想，显然是胡雪岩不愿意经手这件事，
Reflection made the answer plain: Hu did not wish to handle the purchase.

但又为什么不愿意呢？
Why not?

唯一的缘故是左宗棠已非西征统帅，而是两江总督、南洋大臣，
There could be only one explanation. Zuo was no longer commander of the western expedition; he was governor-general of Liangjiang and commissioner for the Southern Ports.

两个头衔中一『江』、『南』，就彰明较著地表明了，这一案应该由江南制造局主办。
The “jiang” and “nan” in those titles unmistakably indicated that this business belonged to the Jiangnan Arsenal.

对于胡雪岩的能守分际，李勉林颇为佩服，
Li admired Hu’s respect for the proper boundaries.

胡雪岩的手腕很厉害，但还是『上路的』。
Hu’s methods might be formidable, but he understood how things should be done.


当下欣然答说∶『可以、可以！
“Certainly!” Li answered readily.

左大人明天动身回江宁，
“His Excellency leaves for Jiangning tomorrow.

我本来就要去见他；
I was going to see him in any case.

我们一起去好了。』
We can go together.”

于是约定当天下午三点钟，在天后宫行辕见面。
They arranged to meet at three that afternoon at Zuo’s headquarters in the Temple of the Heavenly Empress.

到时候会齐李勉林先递书本谒见，
Once they had assembled there, Li sent in his card and was received first.

然后找个谈话的空隙，说福克在外，等候接见，有事面禀。
At a suitable pause, he mentioned that Focke was waiting outside and had something to discuss in person.

左宗棠已经接到胡雪岩的报告，认为胡雪岩所说，此案由江南制造局承办，一切签约、付款等等手续，都比较方便的看法不错，所以听得李勉林的话，立即接见福克。
Hu had already reported to Zuo and argued that having the arsenal handle the transaction would make the contract, payments, and other formalities more convenient. Zuo approved this reasoning and immediately admitted Focke.

他跟福克很熟，也很欣赏福克的有条理，温言相接，颇假以词色；
He knew him well and appreciated his orderly way of doing things, so his greeting was warm and gracious.

谈到买枪一事，也很爽快的答应了，先交若干现货，余数立定期限，陆续解交。
He also agreed readily to the proposed arrangement: the available rifles would be delivered first, and the remainder in instalments by a fixed deadline.

价格方面，福克与李勉林细谈。
Focke and Li were to settle the price between them.


『这两千五面枝枪是交绿营用的。』左宗棠交代李勉林∶『你收到枪，马上交给李朝斌好了。』
“These two thousand five hundred rifles are for the Green Standard troops,” Zuo instructed Li. “As soon as you receive them, hand them over to Li Chaobin.”

李朝斌的官衔是江南提督，绿营的最高长官。
Li Chaobin was the Jiangnan commander-in-chief, the highest officer of the Green Standard forces.

『是。』
“Yes, Your Excellency.”

『听说你要回国。』左宗棠转脸问福克∶『什么时候动身，什么时候回来？』
Zuo turned to Focke. “I hear you are going home. When do you leave, and when will you return?”

『十天以后动身，
“In ten days.

两个月就回来。』
I shall be back in two months.”

『我现在要整顿水师；
“I now intend to reorganize the navy.

水师的利器，是鱼雷不是？』
Torpedoes are an effective naval weapon, are they not?”

『是的。』
“They are.”

『我想买一批鱼雷，
“I should like to purchase a consignment.

你有没有？』
Can you supply them?”

『有、有。』
“Certainly.

福克答说∶『左大人知道的，东西洋各国凡有新出的利器，一定把样品跟说明书，送到我洋行里来的。
As Your Excellency knows, whenever a new weapon appears in any country, East or West, samples and instructions are sent to my firm.

尤其是这趟我回国，可以亲自打听到最新式的运了来。』
On this journey home, I can personally inquire into the newest models and bring them out.”

『能不能连技师一起请了来。』
“Could you bring technicians as well?”

『当然。
“Of course.

凡是采购中国从前所没有的新式武器，一定由技师派来，教导如何演放。
Whenever China buys a new weapon it has not used before, technicians are always sent to demonstrate its operation.

这是必有的规矩，不会错的。』
That is an established practice; there will be no difficulty.”

『喔，你没有弄清我的意思，
“You have misunderstood me.

我是说能制造鱼雷的技师。』
I mean technicians who can manufacture torpedoes.”

『那也有。』福克答说∶『不过要先看制造局，有没有能造鱼雷的机械。』
“They can be found too, but we must first see whether the arsenal has suitable machinery.”

『你跟李观察商量。』左宗棠又问∶『还有种「碰雷」，
“Discuss that with Intendant Li.” Zuo continued, “There is also something called a ‘contact mine.’

作何用处？』
What is that used for?”


『是——』福克向翻译弄清楚了『碰雷』二字的意思，
“It is—” Focke first clarified the term with his interpreter.

方始回答∶『那叫水雷，是专门为了防备对方兵舰用的。
“That is a naval mine, used specifically against enemy warships.

譬如一个港口，不愿意对方舰闯进来，就可以在港口海面上布下水雷，
For example, if you wish to keep their vessels out of a harbour, you can lay mines across its entrance.

船一碰到就会爆炸。』
A ship touching one causes it to explode.”

『自己的船呢？』
“What about our own ships?”

『自己的船，一样也会爆炸。』
“They would cause an explosion too.

福克又说∶『水雷的威力很大，麻烦是不长眼睛，
Mines are very powerful, but unfortunately they cannot distinguish friend from foe.

所以非遇到与外国交锋，打算断绝水路交通，不用水雷。』
They are therefore not used unless war with a foreign country makes it necessary to close the waterways.”

『事后呢？』
“And afterwards?”

『事后要清理。
“Afterwards they must be cleared.

专门有种船叫扫雷艇。』
There is a special kind of vessel called a minesweeper.”

『照此说来，这件事牵涉很广，
“Then the matter has wide implications.

暂作罢论，
We shall leave it aside for now.

你只管替中国采购最新式的鱼雷好了。
Simply obtain the latest torpedoes for China.

细节你跟李观察去商议。』
Discuss the details with Intendant Li.”

『是！』
“Yes, Your Excellency.”

看看没有话了，
There seemed nothing more to say.

福七克在翻译示意之下，起身告辞。
Prompted by the interpreter, Focke rose and took his leave.

李勉林虽被留了下来，但从头到底没有能容他说一句话，内心万分不悦。
Li was asked to remain, but he had not been able to get a word into the entire conversation and was deeply displeased.


至于左宗棠将李勉林留了下来，是要谈半公半私的事。
Zuo had kept Li behind to discuss something partly public and partly private.

不过私事倒也不是他的个人之私，是为了曾国藩的小女婿聂规缉。
The private interest was not his own, however; it concerned Zeng Guofan’s youngest son-in-law, Nie Guiji.

原来曾国藩的欧阳夫人，共生三子六女。
Zeng’s wife, Madam Ouyang, had borne three sons and six daughters.

长子及五女，自幼夭折；在世的有两子五女，
The first son and fifth daughter died in childhood, leaving two sons and five daughters.

长子纪泽，文章政事俱是第一流，
The elder surviving son, Jize, excelled both in letters and in public affairs.

而且由自修而通英文，为国藩所看重，
He had also taught himself English, which greatly pleased his father.

后来袭封侯爵，以钦差大臣出使西洋，
Later he inherited the marquisate and went to the West as an imperial envoy.

与郭嵩焘都是真正懂洋务的大才。
He and Guo Songtao were men of exceptional ability who truly understood foreign affairs.

次子纪鸿中举以后，会试一直不利；
The younger son, Jihong, passed the provincial examination but repeatedly failed at the metropolitan level.

曾国藩也知道『场中莫论文』，考试要碰运气，
Zeng knew that literary merit did not guarantee success in the examination hall and that luck played a part.

但功名这念，横亘胸中；期望亦未免过切，总说他的次子不用功。
Yet official distinction remained an obsession, and his expectations were too pressing; he constantly accused his younger son of not studying hard enough.

偏偏运气也真坏，
Jihong’s luck was indeed poor.

直到曾国藩去世，始终是个举人，以后也一直没有能够中进士，
When Zeng died, he was still only a provincial graduate, and he never subsequently obtained the metropolitan degree.

与长兄相较，境遇大不相同，以至于在京郁郁以终，
His circumstances differed so greatly from his elder brother’s that he died despondent in the capital.

身后还是左宗棠替他料理的。
It was Zuo who attended to his affairs after his death.


比起曾纪鸿来，他的姐妹们的境遇，又更不如他了，
Compared with Jihong, his sisters fared worse still.

有的婆婆太凶，有的丈夫没出息。
Some had fierce mothers-in-law; others had worthless husbands.

曾国藩持家极严，
Zeng governed his household strictly.

说他见过许多名门之女，贪恋母家富，往往不肯在夫家尽子妇之道，到后来都无好结果，
He said he had seen many daughters of prominent families who clung to the wealth of their parents’ homes and neglected their duties as wives and daughters-in-law, and that none had come to a good end.

因此他的女儿虽都遇人不淑，但因曾国藩不许她们归宁，
Although his own daughters had made unfortunate marriages, he would not permit them to return home.

只好在夫家受罪，个个都是终日以泪洗面。
They had to endure their misery with their husbands’ families, spending their days in tears.

其中四小姐嫁得不错，偏又青年守寡，
The fourth daughter had married well, only to be widowed young.

所以曾国藩生前常说，他的『坦运不佳』。
Zeng often said that his luck in sons-in-law was poor.

六小姐是取小的女儿，湖南人称为『满小姐』，名叫曾纪芬，
The sixth daughter, his youngest—what Hunan people called the “last little miss”—was Zeng Jifen.

她是曾国藩去世后才嫁的。
She married only after his death.

本来由她叔叔『九帅』作媒，许婚于衡山聂家，定在同治十一年出阁。
Her uncle, Ninth Marshal Zeng, had arranged the match with the Nie family of Hengshan, and the wedding was set for the eleventh year of Tongzhi.

不意就在这年二月初，曾国藩中风殁于两江总督任上；
But early in the second month of that year, Zeng suffered a stroke and died in office as governor-general of Liangjiang.

到得服满已是光绪年间。
By the time mourning ended, the Guangxu reign had begun.


曾纪芬的夫婿聂规缉，字芸台，他家是衡山世家，先世以行善出名。
Jifen’s husband, Nie Guiji, whose courtesy name was Yuntai, belonged to an established Hengshan family long known for charitable works.

但聂规缉却连个举人都没有考上，以致于只能混个小差使；
He had failed even to gain the provincial degree and could obtain only minor employment.

他有个姐夫为先前的两江总督刘坤一委为『筹防局总办』，
One of his brothers-in-law had been made director of the Defence Preparations Bureau by the former governor-general of Liangjiang, Liu Kunyi.

聂规缉单身跟到江宁，在筹防局当差，只得八两银子的车马费，
Nie followed him to Jiangning alone and took a position in the bureau, receiving only eight taels for transport expenses.

但却要接眷；
Yet he wanted to bring his wife there.

原来聂规缉到了江宁，才知道曾国藩真是门生故吏满天下，
Once in Jiangning, he had discovered how widely Zeng’s former pupils and subordinates were dispersed.

将他妻子以『曾文正的满小姐』这个『头衔』搬出来，在裙带上着实能拖出来一点好处，
By presenting his wife under the title “Lord Wenzheng Zeng’s youngest daughter,” he could extract considerable benefit from her connections.

这就是他接眷的打算。
That was why he wished her to join him.

果然，曾纪芬照她丈夫的嘱咐，由湖南坐船经武昌时，特为去拜见湖广总督李瀚章的夫人，
Following his instructions, Jifen made a special call on the wife of Li Hanzhang, governor-general of Huguang, when her boat from Hunan passed through Wuchang.

稍为谈一谈丈夫的境况，
She said a little about her husband’s circumstances.

聂规缉立即被委为湖南督运局驻江宁的委员，月支津贴五十两，
Nie was promptly appointed the Hunan Transport Supervision Bureau’s representative in Jiangning, with a monthly allowance of fifty taels.

日子过得很舒服了。
Life became quite comfortable.


及至左宗棠接刘坤一的手，到了江宁不久，便将曾纪芬接到总督衙门叙旧，
Soon after succeeding Liu Kunyi at Jiangning, Zuo invited Jifen to the governor-general’s residence to talk over old times.

曾国藩生在嘉庆十六年辛未；左宗棠生在壬申小一岁，因而以叔父自居。
Zeng had been born in the xinwei year, the sixteenth of Jiaqing; Zuo, born in the following renshen year, was a year younger and assumed the role of her uncle.

左宗棠在曾国荃克江宁后，与曾国藩失和，有三四年不通音问，
After Zeng Guoquan captured Jiangning, Zuo and Zeng Guofan had quarrelled and ceased corresponding for three or four years.

但当左示棠奉命西征，曾国藩命湘军刘松山相助，
But when Zuo was ordered to campaign in the west, Zeng sent Liu Songshan of the Xiang Army to assist him.

大为得力，这使得左宗棠大为感动，
His help proved invaluable and deeply moved Zuo.

而况平生功名，关键所在是曾国藩知道他的才具，派他独当一面收复浙江，与曾氏兄弟同时封爵。
Moreover, the decisive opportunity in Zuo’s career had come when Zeng recognized his ability and entrusted him with independent command to recover Zhejiang, enabling him to receive a noble title alongside the Zeng brothers.

拜相封侯，位极人臣，饮水思源，亦不能不感激曾国藩；
Having risen to the highest ministerial honours and a marquisate, Zuo could hardly forget the source of his good fortune.

所以表面上倔强如昔，仍旧处处要批评曾国藩，私底下的态度，却已大为改变，
Outwardly he remained as stubborn as ever, criticizing Zeng at every turn, but privately his attitude had changed greatly.

曾国藩殁后，他致送的挽联，道是∶『谋国之忠，知人之明，自愧不如元辅』，
When Zeng died, Zuo sent a funeral couplet whose first line read: “In loyalty to the state and discernment of men, I acknowledge myself inferior to the chief minister.”

这等于认输，以左宗棠的性情来说，是很难得的事。
This amounted to conceding defeat—an uncommon act for a man of Zuo’s temperament.


至于照应曾国藩的后人，是为了要证实他的挽联中的下一句∶『同心若金，攻错若石，相期无负平生』，
His care for Zeng’s descendants was intended to bear out the couplet’s second line: “Our shared purpose was firm as metal; our corrections of each other, a whetstone; may neither betray the hopes of a lifetime.”

与曾国藩是为国事而争，私交丝毫无损。
Their disagreements concerned affairs of state and had never impaired their private friendship.

特别是老年人，往往有一种将朋友的女儿看作自己掌上珠的通性，
Elderly men, moreover, often regarded a friend’s daughter as a precious child of their own.

爱屋及乌，对聂规缉亦就另眼相看，
Zuo’s affection extended to Nie.

派了他营务处的差使，每天中午会食，一定找聂规缉；
He appointed him to the military administration office and invariably summoned him to share the midday meal.

对他的肯说实话、留心西学，颇为赞许，有心要培植他。
He approved of Nie’s willingness to speak honestly and his interest in Western learning, and intended to foster his career.

这回左宗棠出省阅兵，聂规缉作随员，
Nie accompanied him on this tour of military inspection outside the province.

李勉林跟他是熟人，左宗棠故意相问∶『勉林，你跟聂芸台熟不熟？』
Li Mianlin knew the young man, and Zuo deliberately asked, “Mianlin, do you know Nie Yuntai well?”

李勉林各州兴锐，早年曾替曾国藩办过粮台，
Li, whose personal name was Xingrui, had once managed military supplies for Zeng.

当即答道∶『他是曾文正的满女婿，
“He is Lord Wenzheng’s youngest son-in-law,” he replied.

我当然很熟。』
“Of course I know him well.”

『那就再好没有。
“Excellent.

我看你也很忙，
You seem very busy.

我想派他来当你的会办。』
I should like to appoint him your assistant director.”
