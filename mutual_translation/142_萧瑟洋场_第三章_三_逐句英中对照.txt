In fact, Li supposed, they had settled everything privately and merely used him to ferry the business across.
事实上他们暗底下都谈好了，只是利用他来摆个渡而已。


After Wang’s report, Li considered the matter serious enough to consult Shao Youlian, the Shanghai intendant.
因此听到王伯炎的报告以后，认为事态很严重，特意去找上海道邵友濂商量。

“Li Hongzhang’s mourning has come at a most unfortunate time.
『合肥道赵丁忧，实在不凑巧，

In Beiyang, Zhang Zhenxuan plainly intends to replace him.
北洋是张振轩大有取而代之的意思；

Here, Zuo advances at every step.
这里左湘阴着着进逼，

Nie Zhongfang is planted inside, and Hu Xueyan contrives one scheme after another outside.
里面一个聂仲芳卧底；外面一个胡雪岩花样百出。

The arsenal is the foundation of Beiyang’s strength; I fear we shall lose it.”
制造局是北洋的基础，看来要保不住了。』

Deeply anxious, he appealed to Shao. “Brother Xiaocun, you are always resourceful.
李勉林忧心忡忡地说∶『小村兄，你一向足智多谋，

For the sake of our common debt to Lord Wenzheng, you must help me.”
总要看在大家都是曾文正一脉相传这一点的情份上，帮帮我的忙才好。』

“You need not speak so gravely.
『言重，言重。』

Our fortunes are bound together; I could never stand by.
号『小村』的邵友濂说∶『彼此休戚相关，我决无坐视之理。

Hu carries much less weight with Zuo than he once did.
胡雪岩在左湘阴面前的分量，也大不如前了，

Grit your teeth and hold on while I find an opportunity to strike him from behind so hard that he cannot get up again.”
你先咬咬牙撑住，等我找个机会，好好来打他一闷棍，叫他爬不起来。』

“A hundred-footed creature does not stiffen even in death—” “Even if it does not, it can do nothing more,” Shao interrupted. “The first priority, Brother Mianlin, is to keep Nie Zhongfang comfortable.”
『百足之虫，死而不僵。』『即使不僵也不能有什么作为了。』邵友濂打断他的话说∶『勉林兄，目前最要紧的一件事，你要把聂仲芳敷衍好。』

“I understand.”
『我明白。』

“As for Focke’s contract, you had better let Hu sign it with him.”
『至于福克的合约，你最好还是让胡雪岩跟他去订。』

“Is there a particular reason?”
『喔，这，这有什么讲究吗？』

“Naturally.
『自然有讲究。

This money will ultimately have to come from the Shanghai Customs receipts.
这笔经费，将来少不得要在江海关的收入之中开支；

Suppose I cannot arrange the funds—would I not be exposing you to his demands?”
如果我这里调度不开，不是害你受人家的逼？』

After reflection, Li understood.
李勉林沉吟了一会，恍然大悟，

Shao controlled the customs revenue and could make excuses to delay payment.
江海关的税收归邵友濂管，将来该付福克的款子，他可以借故拖延；

If Hu had signed the contract, Focke could only ask Hu to intervene.
如果是胡雪岩跟福克签的约，福克自然只能找胡雪岩去办交涉，

By obstructing Focke, Shao would really be making difficulties for Hu.
所以邵友濂的刁难福克，实际上便是与胡雪岩为难。

“Excellent!” Li agreed.
『好，好！』等想通了，李勉林满口应承，

“I shall attend to it as soon as I return.”
『我回去就办。』


Li instructed Wang to prepare a report to Zuo stating that Focke’s price was too high and negotiations had failed.
李勉林的办法是，命王伯炎备公事禀报左宗棠，说福克索价过高，合约谈不拢，

Since Focke had previously supplied the western expedition and only Hu could make him accept terms, the public interest required that Zuo order Hu directly to conclude the contract.
福克以前承办西征军火，只有胡雪岩能使他就范，所以为了大局着想，请左宗棠径饬胡雪岩与福克签订合约，

The report added that Focke had offered a ten-per-cent rebate, which the Jiangnan Arsenal could not possibly accept; it should therefore be deducted from the price, ensuring that every last fraction went to the public treasury.
同时，福克原拟致送回扣一成，江南制造局决不敢领这笔回扣，请在价款中扣除，庶符涓滴归公之议。

This impeccably proper-sounding report reached the governor-general’s office in Jiangning.
这一份『禀帖』说得冠冕党皇，到得两江总督衙门，

Zuo found it reasonable and sent a copy to Hu with instructions to act.
左宗棠议为言之有理；便将原禀录了一个副本，一并寄交胡雪岩办理。

Thus the affair travelled in a great circle, from Shanghai to Jiangning, from Jiangning to Hangzhou, and from Hangzhou back to Shanghai.
这样由上海而江宁，由江宁而杭州，再由杭州而上海一个大圈子兜下来，

Amid the exchange of letters and telegrams, two months quickly passed.
函电往来，很快地两个月过去，

The transaction remained unsettled, while the wider situation changed profoundly.
事情尚无结果，局势却有了重大变化。


Civil disorder had broken out in Korea to the east.
原来东邻朝鲜发生内乱，

King Yi Hui was weak, and Queen Min wielded power.
国王李熙暗弱，王妃闵氏当权；

The king’s own father, Yi Haeung, known as the Daewongun, had long been struggling with the queen for control.
李熙的本生父叫李癵应，称号是『大院君』，与王妃争权，已非一日，

His followers now attacked the palace, wounded the queen, and killed ministers including Min Gyeom-ho.
这一次的内乱是大院君的党羽进攻王宫，伤及王妃，并杀大臣闵谦镐等人。

Japan seized the opportunity to send troops to Korea.
日本见有机可乘，出兵朝鲜；

Li Shuchang, China’s envoy in Japan, urgently telegraphed acting Governor-General Zhang Shusheng, recommending that Beiyang warships be dispatched immediately to counter the Japanese.
驻日公使黎庶昌急电署直隶总督张树声，建议北洋立派兵舰，与日军抗衡。

Zhang had been eager for a conspicuous achievement.
张树声本就想有声有色地大干一番，

On receiving the warning, he decided to send troops to watch developments while simultaneously reporting to the court.
接到黎庶昌告警的电报，决定一面出兵观变，一面奏报朝廷。

The court commended his prompt response.
朝廷对张树声能够迅速应变，颇为嘉许，

But with France also threatening Vietnam, it feared that the Korean crisis would exceed Zhang’s capacity.
但因法国其时正在图谋越南；朝鲜又有警报，怕张树声无法应付，

Li Hongzhang was therefore ordered to interrupt his mourning at home and return to Tianjin at once.
所以决定命在籍守制的李鸿章夺情复起，即日回津。

Someone advised Zhang that, since the court had appointed Li to direct the matter, he should wait until Li resumed office before sending troops.
因而便有人劝张树声说∶『朝中既已命令他主持此事，出兵似以等合肥回任后再办为宜。』

Zhang refused.
张树声不听，

Speed was essential in war; if the opportunity were lost and Japan acted first, China would be at a disadvantage.
说兵贵神速，时机一误，让日本军着了先鞭，中国要落下风。

Responsible as he was for Beiyang, he could not allow indecision to lead him astray.
他即负北洋重任，不能因循自误。


Thus, when the second telegram urging Li to depart had only just reached Hefei, and Li had replied that he would leave immediately for Tianjin by way of Shanghai, Zhang’s expedition was already crossing the sea eastward.
于是当第二道催李鸿章动身的电报刚到合肥，李鸿章已复奏即行就道，由上海转天津时，张树声所派的军队，已经在『跨海征东』途中了。

Its naval and military commanders were Ding Ruchang, a Beiyang naval officer registered for appointment as commander-in-chief, and Wu Changqing, nominally commander-in-chief of the Guangdong navy but in practice a land commander.
张树声所派水陆两员大将，一个是北洋水师记名提督丁汝昌；一个是广东水师提督吴长庆，此人名在水师，实在是陆军，

Wu was a veteran of the Huai Army stationed at Dengzhou in Shandong.
他是淮军宿将，驻扎山东登州；

He sailed from there in China Merchants’ steamers with six battalions.
随带淮军六营，由登州坐招商局的轮船出海，

His headquarters contained many able men.
幕府中人材济济，

Directing the field military administration was a twenty-four-year-old from an established Jianghuai family: Yuan Shikai, nephew of Yuan Baoheng, the former Hanlin scholar who had risen to vice-minister of revenue and managed supplies for Zuo.
总理前敌营务处的，是一个年方二十四岁的江淮世家子弟，就是翰林出身、官至户部侍郎、曾为左宗棠办过粮台的哀保恒的侄子袁世凯。

Yuan Shikai had disliked study from childhood.
袁世凯从小不喜读书，

Despite his distinguished family, his disreputable behaviour had made him an outcast among his neighbours.
虽是世家子弟，行为无赖，不齿于乡党。

Unable to remain at home, he conceived an extraordinary plan: gathering more than ten unemployed youths, he travelled from Xiangcheng in Henan to Yantai in Shandong.
在家乡存不住身，异想天开，召集了无业少年十余人，由河南项城到山东烟台，

Leaving his companions at an inn, he went alone to see Wu.
将同伴留在旅舍中，只身去见吴长庆。

Wu, then handling military affairs in Shandong under his Guangdong naval title, had been a sworn brother of Yuan’s adoptive father, Yuan Baoqing.
吴长庆当时以广东水师提督办理山东军务，他跟袁世凯的嗣父袁保庆是八拜之交，

He naturally wished to help his old friend’s son and first asked what had brought him there.
对故人之子，当然要照应，首先动问来意。
