胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement
第三章（本章第4部分，共4部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


袁世凯答说∶『身为将门之子，投笔从戎。』
“As the son of a military family, I have laid down the writing brush to take up arms,” Yuan replied.

又说他带来的十几个少年，都是难得的将才，
The dozen or so young men he had brought, he added, were all rare military talents.

『请老伯全数录用。』
“I ask you, Uncle, to employ every one of them.”

吴长庆大为诧异，不好骂他荒唐、斥之为冒昧。
Wu was astonished, but could hardly call him absurd or rebuke his presumption.

当下派了一名军官携带银票，到旅舍里，将他的同伴好言资遣。
Instead, he sent an officer to the inn with banknotes to dismiss his companions kindly and provide their travelling expenses.

当然，袁世凯是被留下来了。
Yuan himself was, of course, kept on.

『你进了学没有？』
“Have you gained admission to the county school?”

『没有。』
“No.”

袁世恺连秀才都不是，
Yuan was not even a licentiate.

不过捐了监生，照例可应北闱顺天乡试；
He had merely purchased the status of an Imperial Academy student, which entitled him to sit the Shuntian provincial examination in the northern capital.

吴长庆便叫他在营读书，拜张謇为师。
Wu told him to study in camp under Zhang Jian.

此人号季直，是南通的名士，
Known by the courtesy name Jizhi, Zhang was a distinguished scholar of Nantong.

他在吴长庆幕府中参赞军务，同时也是吴开庆次子吴保初的业师。
Besides advising Wu on military affairs, he tutored Wu’s second son, Wu Baochu.


既然要应考，张謇当然教他做八股文。
Since Yuan was to take the examination, Zhang naturally taught him eight-legged essays.

袁世恺兴趣缺缺；
Yuan had little interest in them.

但陪着张謇谈谈时事，以及用人驭士的手段，却头头是道，很得张謇的赏识。
But in conversations about current affairs and the management of men, he spoke so cogently that Zhang came to admire him.

吴长庆幕府中，还有个朱铭盘，也是南通人，
Another member of Wu’s staff, Zhu Mingpan, was also from Nantong.

与张謇及另一个诗做得极好的范肯堂，号称为『通州三生』；
He, Zhang, and the gifted poet Fan Kentang were known as the Three Scholars of Tongzhou.

这朱铭盘对袁世恺亦颇有好感，
Zhu too liked Yuan.

因此，当张謇保荐袁世恺时，而朱铭盘在一旁帮腔以后，吴长庆便委袁为营务处帮办，而且派了两名勤务兵给他。
When Zhang recommended him, with Zhu supporting the recommendation, Wu appointed him assistant in the military administration office and assigned him two orderlies.

这是前年光绪六年四月间的事。
This occurred in the fourth month of the sixth year of Guangxu, two years before.

及至朝鲜发生内乱，张树声派丁汝昌特召吴长庆议事。
When the Korean disturbances broke out, Zhang Shusheng sent Ding Ruchang specially to summon Wu for consultation.

吴长庆带同张謇，在天津密商三日，定策平乱。
Wu took Zhang Jian to Tianjin, where three days of secret discussions produced a plan to suppress the disorder.

这年壬午，『子午犯酉，大比之年』，
It was the renwu year—one of the years of the Rat, Horse, Rabbit, or Rooster in which the triennial examinations were held.

袁世恺奉命入京乡试，恰好也在天津；
Ordered to go to the capital for the provincial examination, Yuan happened also to be in Tianjin.

听说要出兵朝鲜，便去见张謇，想弃文就武，不赴乡试而赴朝鲜。
Hearing of the projected Korean expedition, he sought out Zhang Jian and asked to abandon letters for arms, going to Korea instead of the examination.

张謇答应了，为他向吴长庆要求，如愿以偿。
Zhang agreed to intercede with Wu, and Yuan obtained his wish.


到了烟台以后，吴长庆回登州去调兵遣将，
After they reached Yantai, Wu returned to Dengzhou to mobilize his troops.

在烟台派船征粮，须备辎重，由张謇负责，
Zhang Jian was responsible for assigning ships, requisitioning provisions, and assembling supplies at Yantai.

事多且杂，张謇顺理成章的找了袁世凯作帮手，
With so many varied tasks, he naturally turned to Yuan for assistance.

由吴长庆下札子委为『前敌营务处』，居然独当一面了。
Wu issued him a written appointment to the field military administration, and he found himself entrusted with independent responsibility.

七月十二日黄昏，吴长庆带领大队人马，由烟台抵达朝鲜仁川；
At dusk on the twelfth day of the seventh month, Wu’s main force arrived at Incheon from Yantai.

可是日本海陆军已经早一小时到达。
The Japanese land and naval forces, however, had arrived an hour earlier.

只是天色已晚，中日两军都住在船上，预备天亮登陆。
As it was already late, both sides remained aboard, intending to land at dawn.

哪知就夜色苍茫中，闵妃所遣的密使到了。
Then, through the gathering darkness, secret envoys arrived from Queen Min.

原来朝鲜国王李熙，也象光绪皇帝一样，是旁支入继；
Korea’s king, Yi Hui, had succeeded from a collateral branch of the royal house, like the Guangxu emperor.

李熙的生父『大院君』李是应，便等于醇亲王，所不同的是，『大院君』摄政。
His biological father, Yi Haeung, the Daewongun, occupied a position comparable to Prince Chun’s, with the difference that the Daewongun served as regent.


李熙成年以后，『大院君「自然看不过去，便跟闵妃争权。
After Yi Hui came of age, the Daewongun resented the situation and struggled with Queen Min for power.

那闵妃象慈禧太后一样，非常能干，
Like the Empress Dowager Cixi, she was highly capable.

心想朝鲜是中国的藩属，只要倾心结交中国官吏，自然就占上风，
Since Korea was China’s tributary state, she reasoned, wholeheartedly cultivating Chinese officials would give her the advantage.

此时日本的野心日炽，
Japan’s ambitions were growing daily.

看朝鲜两派对立，各不相下，便蓄心要找机会，作为入侵的借口。
Seeing two irreconcilable factions in Korea, it watched for an opportunity that could furnish a pretext for invasion.

机会终于来了。
At last one arose.

朝鲜内政不修，人民困苦，士兵的饷欠了好几个月，
Domestic government was neglected, the people were impoverished, and soldiers had gone unpaid for months.

一再『闹饷』，发又发得不足数，于是便常有造反作乱之事，
Repeated protests over their pay produced only incomplete payments, so mutinies and disturbances were frequent.

日本人便买通乱党，故意让他们抢劫日本领事馆，
The Japanese bought over some of the rebels and deliberately had them plunder the Japanese consulate.

日本便以保护领事馆为名，酝酿出兵朝鲜。
They then prepared to send troops under the pretext of protecting it.

闵妃得到消息，向中国官吏告密；驻日公使亦有急电到北洋，
Queen Min learned of this and secretly informed Chinese officials; China’s envoy in Japan also telegraphed Beiyang urgently.

中日双方军队都想抢个先着，但同时到达，不分先后，
Both countries sought to arrive first, but their forces reached Korea at virtually the same time.

而闵妃的密使一来，情势就不同了。
The queen’s envoys changed the situation.


这些密使谒见吴长庆、丁汝昌，说日本与李癵应已有勾结。
Meeting Wu and Ding, the envoys said that Japan and the Daewongun were already in collusion.

哪一个军队先到朝鲜京城汉城，哪一国便控制了整个局势。
Whichever army reached the capital, Hanseong, first would control the whole situation.

这就象楚汉相争，先入咸阳为胜是一样的道理。
It was like the struggle between Chu and Han, when entry into Xianyang determined the advantage.

『为今之计，我们劝天朝大军，乘黑夜登陆，由间道入汉城，
“We advise the Celestial Empire’s forces to land under cover of darkness and take a side road to Hanseong.

一昼夜可以抵达。
You can reach it in a day and a night.

这条间道捷径是日本人所不知道的。』
The Japanese do not know this shortcut.”

『主意是很好，
“An excellent idea.

可是这一昼夜的供应呢？
But how are the troops to be supplied for that day and night?

士兵不能不吃饭啊！』
They must eat.”

『请放。』
“Do not be concerned.

闵妃的密使说∶『沿途都设备好了。』
Everything has been arranged along the route.”

吴长庆大喜，立即召集张謇及马建忠密议，
Delighted, Wu immediately called Zhang Jian and Ma Jianzhong into a secret conference.

决定接受闵妃的计划，先派五百人连夜登陆；另派一千人在黎明下船，其余守在船上待命。
They decided to accept the queen’s plan: five hundred men would land that night, another thousand at dawn, while the rest remained aboard awaiting orders.

密议既定，吴长庆在招商局轮船的大餐间点兵发令。
Once the decision was made, Wu assembled his officers in the China Merchants’ steamer’s dining saloon and issued his commands.


这本来应该是士气昂扬、踊跃争先的一个场面，
This should have been an occasion of high spirits and eager volunteers.

不过吴长庆下达了命令，肃静无声，约有五分钟之久；
Instead, after Wu gave his orders, silence lasted for about five minutes.

这一下气氛便显得很僵硬了。
The atmosphere grew painfully strained.

终于有个姓刘的帮带，凑到吴长庆面前低声说道∶『本营都是陆军，从来没有出过海，
At last a deputy battalion commander named Liu approached and spoke quietly. “Our men are land soldiers who have never been to sea.

现在轮船刚停下来，弟兄晕船的很多，
The steamer has only just stopped, and many are seasick.

能不能请大帅体谅，让大家休息一夜，到天亮再上岸。』
Might you allow them a night’s rest before they go ashore at dawn, Marshal?”

此言一出，吴长庆即时变色，
Wu’s expression changed instantly.

偏偏另外还有同样的请求，
Worse still, others voiced the same request.

吴长庆勃然大怒，拍桌骂道∶『这是什么时候，什么地方，你敢不遵我的命令，
He flew into a rage and struck the table. “At a time and in a place like this, you dare disobey?

莫非不知道军法？』
Do you not know military law?”

说着，要拔令箭斩那个刘帮带。
He reached for a command token to order Liu’s execution.

张謇在旁，不等他再开口抢着说道∶『大帅，刘帮带不宜再带兵了；
Zhang Jian hastily intervened before he could speak again. “Marshal, Liu is no longer fit to lead troops.

另外派人吧！』
Appoint someone else!”

『派谁呢？』
“Whom?”

『我看袁世凯可以接替。』
“I suggest Yuan Shikai.”

『好！』
“Very well!”

吴长庆向左右说道∶『把姓刘的先看管起来，
Wu turned to his attendants. “Place Liu under guard.

等我办完了大事再来处置。』
I shall deal with him once the important business is done.”


这时袁世凯已得到通知，
Yuan had meanwhile been summoned.

进来行了礼；
He entered and saluted.

张謇说道∶『大帅有差使派给你，
“The Marshal has a duty for you,” Zhang told him.

你仔细听着。』
“Listen carefully.”

吴长庆接口下令∶『刘帮带不遵命令，
Wu took up the command. “Deputy Commander Liu has disobeyed orders.

我已把他革职看管，
I have dismissed him and placed him under arrest.

现在派你为帮带，接管他的队伍；
You are now deputy commander and will take over his men.

即刻预备，
Prepare immediately.

半点钟以后，先领一营人，坐朝鲜派来的船登陆，
In half an hour, lead one battalion ashore in the boats supplied by the Koreans.

由朝鲜向导带领，连夜行军。
Their guides will take you forward on a night march.

袁世凯，这个差使，你担当得下来，担当不下来？』
Yuan Shikai, can you carry out this assignment?”

『能担当。』
“I can.”

『好！
“Good.

你部下如有人不遵命，违反军法，准你先斩后报。』
If any of your men disobey or violate military law, you may execute them first and report afterwards.”

说着，吴长庆将手中的令箭，往前一递。
He held out the command token.

袁世凯接令在手，高声答道∶『遵大帅将令。』
Yuan accepted it and answered loudly, “I obey your orders, Marshal.”

半点钟不到，袁世凯已扎束停当，草鞋短裤，干净利落，
In less than half an hour, he was ready, neatly equipped in short trousers and straw sandals.

进来向吴长庆禀报∶『已经跟朝鲜的译官商量决定，
He returned to report: “I have settled everything with the Korean interpreter.

登陆后连夜急行军，天明到果山早饭，在那里恭候大帅驾临。』
After landing, we shall march rapidly through the night, take breakfast at Guoshan at dawn, and await Your Excellency there.”

辞行既华，立即下船，
Having taken his leave, he immediately went ashore.

到得天亮，吴长庆亲统两营，接续前进，中午抵达果山，
At daybreak, Wu followed with two battalions and reached Guoshan at noon.

袁世凯下马迎谒，
Yuan dismounted to greet him.

说已派先锋五百人，由营官率领先走，他特为在此候驾。
He had sent five hundred men ahead under the battalion commander and had remained expressly to await the Marshal.


『路上怎么样？』
“How was the journey?”

『一路平安，
“Entirely peaceful.

朝鲜的供应很完备，
The Koreans have supplied everything we need.

一切请大帅放心。』
You may be at ease.”

『好！』
“Good.

吴长庆又问∶『还有什么事要报告的？』
Anything else to report?”

『士兵的纪律不大好，
“The men’s discipline was poor.

抢民间的东西，还有对妇女无礼，
They plundered the inhabitants and insulted women.

王师戡乱，这样子会让人家看不起，
For an imperial army sent to restore order, such behaviour would earn contempt.

世凯已遵大帅将令，就地正法了七个人。』
Acting under your orders, I have executed seven men on the spot.”

一听这话，吴长庆放心了。
Wu felt reassured.

原以为他不会带兵，现在看来，倒真不愧将门之后，
He had thought Yuan incapable of commanding troops; now he seemed indeed a worthy descendant of a military family.

当下慰了一番，关照袁世凯继续前进。
Wu commended him and instructed him to continue the advance.


当天深夜，先锋五百人到了汉城，在南门扎营。
Late that night, the five hundred advance troops reached Hanseong and camped at its south gate.

第二天上午十点多钟，吴长庆亲统的一千人亦复疾驰而至，在距汉城七里的屯子山扎下大营，
After ten the following morning, Wu arrived at speed with his thousand men and established his main camp at Tunzishan, seven li from the city.

其时『大院君』李癵应已经得到消息，派了他的儿子大将军李载冕来见吴长庆，表示慰劳。
The Daewongun had learned of their arrival and sent his son, General Yi Jae-myeon, with greetings and gifts for the troops.

吴长庆亦很客气地敷衍了一番，
Wu responded courteously.

等李载冕一走，立刻进城去拜访李癵应，作礼貌上的周旋。
Once Yi had left, he immediately entered the city to pay the Daewongun a formal visit.

出城回大营以后，吴长庆立即召集高级将领及幕僚密商马建忠建议，擒贼擒王，等李是应来回拜时，设法扣留，送往天津，以寒乱党之胆。
Returning to camp, he called his senior officers and advisers into a secret council. Ma Jianzhong proposed seizing the leader to subdue his followers: when the Daewongun returned the visit, they should detain him and send him to Tianjin, intimidating the rebels.

倘或乱党不受安抚，再行进剿。
If the rebels still refused pacification, military suppression could follow.

吴长庆认为此计大妙，其余的人众都同意，
Wu thought the plan excellent, and everyone agreed.

于是密密部署，设下了陷阱，只等李癵应来自投。
They made their preparations in secret and laid the trap, waiting for the Daewongun to enter it.


李癵应来回拜时，是在下午四点钟，带的卫队有数十名之多；
He returned the visit at four in the afternoon, accompanied by several dozen guards.

接入帐内，由张謇与马建忠二人，与李癵应笔谈，这样交换意见，
Received inside the tent, he conversed with Zhang Jian and Ma Jianzhong by exchanging written notes.

即令是泛泛的寒暄，一来一往，亦很费事。
Even commonplace civilities took considerable time in this manner.

等营外李癵应的卫队被隔离开来，看看时候差不多了∶吴长庆便即说道∶『本人奉朝廷之命传旨，着贵藩亲自到北京，面陈乱党的一切。』
Once his guards outside had been separated from him, Wu judged the moment ripe. “By command of the court, I convey an imperial order: Your Highness is to go personally to Beijing and report fully on the rebels.”

说完，也不管李癵应听得懂听不懂，由马建忠扶起李癵应出营；
Without waiting to discover whether the Daewongun understood, Ma helped him to his feet and took him out of camp.

外面有一顶轿子，
A sedan chair stood ready.

将他塞入轿内，抬起便走，
They thrust him inside, lifted it, and hurried away.

健卒百余人前后夹护，
More than a hundred picked soldiers escorted him before and behind.

连夜冒雨急驰一百二十里，第二天一早到南阳港口，
Through the night and the rain they covered a hundred and twenty li, reaching Namyang harbour early the next morning.

登上威远兵轮，李是应才知道是怎么回事。
Only after he was aboard the warship Weiyuan did the Daewongun understand what had happened.


下一个目标是李癵应的长子，亦即韩王李熙的胞兄李载冕，据说，乱党是由他指挥的。
The next target was his eldest son, Yi Jae-myeon, full brother of King Yi Hui, who was said to direct the rebels.

吴长庆派袁世凯领兵入城，逮捕了李载冕，而乱党却已逃散了。
Wu sent Yuan with troops into the city to arrest him, but the rebels themselves had dispersed.

当天晚上，吴长庆接到李熙的密报，乱党是屯驻在两个地方，一个叫利泰院，一个叫枉寻里。
That evening, a secret message from the king told Wu that they were concentrated in two places: Itaewon and Wangxunli.

枉寻里就在吴长庆大营附近，便由他亲自出马；
Wangxunli lay close to Wu’s main camp, so he dealt with it himself.

利泰院的任务派了袁世凯，
Yuan was assigned Itaewon.

乘黑夜奇袭，抓了一百多人，其余的乌合之众纷纷走避；
A surprise attack in the darkness captured more than a hundred men, while the remaining rabble fled in every direction.

枉寻里的情形亦差不多。
Much the same happened at Wangxunli.

等日军三千人沿大路开到汉城，局势已经平定了。
By the time three thousand Japanese troops reached Hanseong along the main road, order had already been restored.

这一来，日军便没有进城的理由，为了避免与清军冲突，驻扎在城外。
They therefore had no grounds for entering the city and camped outside to avoid a clash with the Qing forces.

日本驻韩公使花房义质亦回汉城，向韩国提出赔偿的交涉，
Japan’s envoy to Korea, Hanabusa Yoshitada, also returned to Hanseong and opened negotiations for compensation.

这不是吴长庆的事；
That was not Wu’s concern.

他将大营移驻东门外关帝庙以后，随即行文北洋，奏请论功行赏。
After moving his headquarters to the Temple of Lord Guan outside the east gate, he sent an official report to Beiyang requesting rewards according to merit.
