The Desolate Foreign Settlement

CHAPTER FOUR
第四章


This ought to have been good news, but Yuan Shikai had a guilty secret.
这本来是件好事，但袁世凯却怀着鬼胎，

There was nothing he could do except wait for the flaw to emerge and then find a remedy.
但亦无法，只好等纰漏出来以后再来想办法——

At last Zhou Jialu, a member of Wu Changqing’s secretariat who handled correspondence and had also taught Yuan, summoned him for a question.
终于有一天，为吴长庆办文案的幕僚。而且也教袁世凯读过书的周家禄，将他找了去有话问。

“Weiting, are you a secretary in the Secretariat Office?”
『慰亭，』他问∶『你是中书科中书？』

“What is the matter?”
『怎么样？』

Yuan neither confirmed nor denied it, first seeking to learn what had happened.
袁世凯不置可否，先打听出了什么事。

“Look at this.”
『你看！』

It was an official communication from Beiyang concerning the Qing Army’s recommendations for rewards.
是北洋来的公事，说庆军保奖一案，

Yuan Shikai, secretary in the Secretariat Office, had received imperial approval for promotion to deputy prefect.
中书科中书袁世凯，保升同知，业已奉旨允准。

But after searching its records, the ministry could find no secretary of that name.
惟本部遍查档册，中书科中书并无袁世凯其人；

He was to be instructed to submit an explanation.
请饬该员申复云云。

Yuan felt both delight and anxiety.
袁世凯一则以喜，一则以忧，

His delight was that he had built a tower from level ground and obtained a fifth-rank deputy prefecture.
喜的是平地起楼台，搞了个五品同知，

The post carried military duties—hence its other designation, “sima”—but its holder was a civil official with unlimited prospects, making it more valuable than a second- or third-rank military command.
这个职务是武职，故别称『司马』；但官却是文官，前程无量，比二、三品的副将、参将还值钱；

His anxiety was that the secretaryship in his record was fraudulent.
忧的是资历上的中书科中书原是假冒的，

If that underlying appointment did not exist, his dream of becoming deputy prefect would collapse.
这个底缺如果不存在，升同知的美梦也就落空了。

Inwardly he was in turmoil; outwardly he remained calm.
心里七上八下，表面却很沉着，

“Mr Zhou,” he said with a grin, “why not guess?”
『周先生，』他笑嘻嘻地说∶『你倒猜上一猜。』

“There is no need.
『用不着猜，

The ministry certificate for a purchased appointment that you brought originally had the right surname, Yuan, but the wrong personal name.
你当初拿来的那张捐官的「部照」，姓不错，是袁，名字不是，

You must have borrowed it.”
当然是借来的。』

“Yes, yes.
『是，是，

Nothing escapes you, Mr Zhou.”
周先生明见万里，

Yuan made a formal bow. “Please help me through this.”
这件事，』他打了个千说∶『请周先生成全。』

“That goes without saying.
『成全不用说，

If the truth were reported, even Commander Wu would be punished for failing to detect it.
据实呈复，连庆公都要担个失察的处分。』

There are two ways now, one easy and one troublesome.
周家禄紧接着说∶『现在有两个办法，一个容易，一个麻烦，

You must choose.”
要你自己挑。』

“Please explain them.”
『那请周先生指教，是怎么样的两个办法。』

“The easy one first: use the name on the certificate.
『先说容易的，你改用部照上的名字。』

It is not only easy but convenient.
周家禄说∶『这个办法，不但容易，而且方便。

A single official note stating that Yuan Shikai is a name adopted by Yuan So-and-so, and that you are reverting to the original name, will suffice.”
你方便，只要一角公文，袁世凯为袁某某的改名。恢复原名即可。』

Yuan did not want this easy solution.
袁世凯不愿用这个容易方便的法子，

He was already well known in Korea.
因为他在韩国已是知名人物；

More importantly, everyone at court, from Empress Dowager Cixi and Prince Gong to the secretaries of the Zongli Yamen, knew that a man called Yuan Shikai had distinguished himself there.
尤其有关系的是，朝中自慈禧太后、恭王到总理衙门章京，都知道有个在朝鲜立了功的袁世凯，

With a different name, who would recognise a mere deputy prefect?
一改名字，区区同知，有谁知道。


That, however, was not the reason he gave.
不过他拒绝的理由，却不是这么说，

“To be frank, Mr Zhou, the certificate belonged to a nephew of my clan who has died.
『周先生，实不相瞒，』他说，『原来的部照，是我一个堂侄子的，此人业已去世，

Restoring his name could cause all sorts of unforeseen complications.
恢复原名，有许多意外的纠葛。

Please tell me the difficult method.”
请说难的那个办法吧！』

“You must find someone to approach the Ministry of Personnel on your behalf.
『难的那个办法，就是你自己托人到吏部去活动。

Its clerks have more tricks than one can imagine.
吏部那些书办，花样之多，意想不到，

They will certainly find a way.
他们一定有办法，

But ‘Keep the fire going, and the pig’s head softens; provide the money, and official business gets done.’
不过「火到猪头烂，钱到公事办」，

Your affair may cost a thousand taels.”
你这件事，只怕非千金莫办。』

“Yes, yes.
『是、是。

I shall do as you advise.”
我照周先生的意思去办。』

“Good.
『好！

I shall hold the papers back for now.
我暂且把公事压下来，

Approach the ministry, and we shall decide what to do when we see the result.”
等你到吏部活动，看结果如何，再作道理。』

“Thank you, thank you.”
『是。多谢、多谢。』

Zhou smiled. “Weiting, I have a bit of doggerel for you.”
『慰亭！』周家禄笑道∶『我有一首打油送你。』

Taking up his brush, he wrote at one sweep on a sheet of official draft paper.
说完，拈起笔来，就桌上起公文的稿纸，一挥而就；

Yuan read it: “A crooked Henan scholar was his start; / The borrowed secretaryship is plain.
袁世凯接过来念道∶『本是中州歪秀才，中书借得不须猜。

/ Once he displayed his statesman’s art, / Seven severed heads became his gain.” Zhou roared with laughter.
一时大展经纶手，杀得人头七个来。』等他念完，周家禄哈哈大笑，

Yuan could only join in with a few dry chuckles to show how lightly he took it.
袁世凯也只好陪着干笑几声，以示洒脱。

Back in his tent, he naturally thought of Xu Shichang, a provincial graduate and a thoroughly reliable man.
回到自己营帐，袁世凯自然而然想起了一个人，此人名叫徐世昌，是个举人，办事很扎实，

Entrusting the matter to him would ensure success.
托他去活动，万无一失。

But if, as Zhou said, it would cost a thousand taels, where was the money to come from?
只是照周家禄说，花费须一千两银子，款从何出，却费思量。


After much thought, Yuan could only turn to Zhang Jian.
想来想去，只好去找张謇。

Zhang also managed the supply office and controlled the flow of provisions and pay.
他兼管着支应所，粮饷出入，大权在握，

One nod from him would secure the thousand taels.
只要他点头，一千两银子就有着落了。

Yuan greeted him with “Mr Zhang!”
见面招呼，一声『张先生！』

Zhang was taken aback.
张謇便是一楞，

Yuan called Zhou “Mr Zhou”, but had always addressed Zhang simply as “Teacher”.
原来他称周家禄是『周先生』，叫张謇一向只『老师』二字，

Now he had changed it to “Mr”, and added the surname.
如今不但改了『先生』，而且还加了姓，

Why?
此又何故？

It was awkward to challenge him immediately, so Zhang replied coldly, “What can I do for you?”
一时不便责问，只冷冷地答一声∶『有何见教？』

Yuan realised his mistake too, but would not correct himself.
袁世凯也发觉自己错了，但亦不愿再改口，

He explained his difficulty indirectly and asked Zhang to help him through it.
只婉转地说明了自己的困难，请张謇『成全』。

“I would not presume to call it that.
『成全不敢当，

But as friends, we ought to help one another.
不过既然是朋友，理当相助。

The supply office holds public money, which I cannot lend privately.
支应所的款子是公款，我不便私下借给你；

There is only one course: your official allowance is two hundred taels a month.
如今只有一个办法，你的公费每月二百两，

Give me five signed acknowledgements, and I shall advance you five months.”
你写五张「领结」来，我把你的五个月公费先发给你。』

“Good.
『好！

How should I write them?”
请问领结如何写法？』

In a “sealed acknowledgement”, the word “acknowledgement” signified that a matter was concluded.
本来『印结』之结，当作承认事情已经结束来解释，

The wording was not particularly pleasing, but ordinarily no one troubled about it.
辞句上不大好听，没有人去理会，

Yuan, however, was sensitive.
只是袁世凯心里有病，

When he took the form away and read it, he found it stated that he had received two hundred taels for a specified month, counted in his presence, with no deficiency in purity or weight, and that he would make no subsequent claim for compensation for any shortage.
将张謇所开的印结式样，拿回去一看，上面写的大意是，领到某月份公费二百两，当面点清，成色分两，均未短缺；嗣后倘有短缺，决不致提出任何补偿的要求。

It seemed designed to prevent him from repudiating the receipt, and he was already displeased.
倒象防他会耍赖似的，心里已经不大舒服；

Then he consulted the Expressions of the Six Ministries, a book he used as a guide to official life.
再翻一翻一部他当作作官秘诀来用的『六部成语』，

Under Personnel was an entry for a “voluntary undertaking”: once a case was decided, or property ordered returned, the person concerned signed a document declaring willing obedience.
其中『吏部』有一条常用的成语，叫做『甘结』，注解是∶『凡官府断案既定，或将财物令事主领回者，均命本人作一「情甘遵命」之据，上画花押，谓这甘结』。

Yuan flew into a rage.
顿时大为光火；

So a sealed acknowledgement was this sort of meek submission, merely replacing the signature with a seal!
原来所谓印结是这么一种做低服小的表示，不过画花押改为钤印而已，

He felt that the supply office was treating him intolerably.
他觉得支应所欺人太甚了。

Moreover, in the present recommendations Zhang had merely risen from assistant county magistrate to eligibility for a seventh-rank magistracy.
再一想到，这回的保案中，张謇不过是以县丞保用为七品的知县；

Yuan was a deputy prefect, a “fifth-rank occupant of the yellow hall”.
自己是同知，所谓『五品黄堂』，

Why should he give a sealed acknowledgement to the supply office?
凭什么要向支应所具印结？

He complained furiously, but without the documents he could not obtain the money and had to swallow his anger and comply.
当时大发了一顿牢骚，但不具印结，领不到银子，只好忍气吞声照办。

Zhang heard that he had been cursing behind his back—“What sort of thing is this supply office?”—and found his petty arrogance in success contemptible.
可是张謇虽然听说他背后大骂『何物支应所』，觉得小人得志的那副脸嘴，令人齿冷；

Nevertheless, he continued to help him.
但还是很帮他的忙。


“Weiting, is this money to be used in Beijing?”
『慰亭，』他问，『你这银子是要在京里用？』

“Yes.”
『是的。』

“To whom will you send it?”
『那么你要寄给谁呢？』

“A childhood friend named Xu.
『我的一个总角之交。』袁世凯答说∶『姓徐，

By now he is probably a newly successful provincial graduate.”
大概已经是新科举人了。』

Zhang understood.
张謇懂他的意思，

His friend had sat the northern provincial examination, whose results were now out.
他这姓徐的朋友应北闱乡试，如今已经发榜，

He might have passed, but Yuan, far away in another country, had not received the news; hence “probably”.
可能榜上有名，不过远在异国，未得京师消息，所以用了『大概』二字。

“Very well,” Zhang said. “I cannot give you silver to send, of course.
『好！』张謇说道∶『我当然不能发你现银，

A banknote might be lost in the post, which would cause trouble.
用银票呢，又怕寄递中途失落了，也很麻烦。

I have a method, if you agree.”
我有一个办法，不知道你愿意不愿意。』

“Please explain, Mr Zhang.”
『喔，请张先生说。』

“Mr Zhang” again!
又是『张先生』！

After hearing “Teacher” so often, this second use jarred.
听惯了他口口声声叫『老师』，现在第二回听见这个称呼，实在有些刺耳。

Still, Zhang patiently continued. “Our army’s pay is issued through the Beiyang Office in Tianjin.
不过张謇还是很耐心地说∶『本军的饷银，都是由天津「北洋公所」发的；

I shall give you an order to collect the money.
我现在给你一张领据，

Send it to your friend, and he can draw it there directly.
你寄给你的朋友，由他直接到北洋公所去领，

Would that not be convenient?”
岂不方便。』

“Excellent.
『好，好！

Thank you for your trouble, Mr Zhang.”
费心张先生了。』

“What is your friend’s personal name?
『你贵友的大名是哪两个字？』

It will be safer to name the person authorised to collect it.” Yuan agreed. “Xu Shichang.
张謇又说∶『领据上指明由某人去领，比较保险。』袁世凯觉得这话也不错，点点头说∶『叫徐世昌。

The characters for shichang come from ‘May his descendants prosper for five generations’.”
五世其昌的世昌。』

“Where is he from?”
『哪里人？』

“Must that go on the order too?”
『这也要写在领据上？』

“Not for its own sake.
『不是这意思。

I must specify his status.
我要写明他的身份，

To sit the northern provincial examination, he must be either an Imperial Academy student or a county licentiate.
赴北闱当然不是监生，就是生员，

If the latter, I must state the county.
生员就要写明哪一县的生员，

That is why I ask.”
所以我问他是哪里人。

“He is a licentiate.
『他是生员。』

His family originally came from Ningbo in Zhejiang and moved to Tianjin in the Qianlong reign.
袁世凯说∶『他原来浙江宁波人，乾隆年间迁居天津，

His great-great-grandfather served as magistrate of Nanyang in Henan, died in office and was buried in Ji county.
他高祖是河南南阳知县，殁在任上，葬在河南汲县，

The family has lived there since.
他家以后就一直寄居在那里，

So he can be considered a Zhejiang man, a Zhili man or a Henan man.”
所以他又算浙江人，也算直隶人，或者河南人。』

“Then his licentiate registration must still be in Tianjin.
『这样说，他还是天津的生员，

If he had qualified in Ji county, he would have had to sit the Henan examination.”
如果是汲县进的学，就得在河南乡试。』


Zhang issued the order, naming “Xu Shichang, formerly a licentiate of Tianjin” as the recipient.
张謇开了领据，指明由『原天津生员徐世昌』具领『。

By the time it reached Xu, he had indeed become a provincial graduate.
等这张邻据寄寄到徐世昌手里，他已经是新科举人了。

He and his younger brother Xu Shiguang had taken the examination together.
徐世昌是与他的胞弟徐世光一起下科场的。

After its three sessions, during the month’s wait for the results, his eagerness for a degree and anxiety over success or failure robbed his food of flavour and his sleep of ease.
三场考毕，在等候发榜的那一个月之中，功名心热，得失这念梗在胸中，有些食不甘味、寝不安枕；

A close friend advised him to seek a divination lot.
常常往来的一个好朋友，便劝他去求一支签。

The friend was Ke Shaoban, courtesy name Fengsheng, from Jiaozhou in Shandong.
他这个朋友叫柯绍半，字凤笙，山东胶州人。

“The lots at the shrine of Immortal Lü in Liulichang are extraordinarily accurate,” he said.
告诉徐世昌说∶『琉璃厂的吕祖祠，那里的签，最灵验不过，

“Every question receives an answer, and every answer proves true.
有求必应；有应必中。

Why not try?” Xu readily agreed.
你何妨去求一求看。』徐世昌欣然乐从，

At the shrine he found the worship even more fervent than at the Fire God Temple to its west, which strengthened his faith.
到了琉璃厂吕祖祠，看香火比它西面的火神庙还盛，信心便又添了几分。

He prayed sincerely and drew a lot bearing this verse: “The mystic work has laid its base; / Feed the fire well, make no mistake.
当下虏诚祷祝，抽了一支签出来，上面写的一首诗是∶『八九玄功已有基，频添火候莫差池，

/ When twelvefold towers yield their inner space, / The elixir forms, and cranes awake.”
待看十二重楼透，便是丹成鹤到时。』

“It seems my work is not yet complete,” Xu said. “Perhaps there is no hope in this examination.”
『这好象工夫还不到。』徐世昌说∶『今科恐怕无望。』

“Not so,” Ke replied. “I think it refers to the spring examination.
『不然。』柯绍半说∶『照我看，这是指春闱而言，

The second line, ‘Feed the fire well, make no mistake’, means that after success in the autumn, you must study harder and practise more full examination papers.
第二句「频添火候莫差池」，是说你秋闱得意以后，要加紧用功，多写写「大卷子」，

Next year you will pass the metropolitan examination and gain one of the top three places in the palace examination.
明年会试中式，殿试得鼎甲，

Would that not be breaking through the ‘twelvefold towers’?”
那岂非「十二重楼诱」出？』

Xu was delighted by this interpretation.
徐世昌听这一解，大为高兴。

Then he read the pronouncement beneath the poem: “Bring glory to those before and abundance to those after; make the family flourish greatly.”
再看诗后的『断曰』∶『光前裕后，昌大其门庭』，

His happiness increased still further.
益发满心欢悦了。


On the day the list appeared, he waited from midnight until dawn and from dawn until noon.
到得登榜那天，由半夜等到天亮，由天亮等到日中，

At last a messenger arrived with good news—but it left Xu Shichang acutely embarrassed.
捷报来了，不过徐世昌却格外难堪，

His younger brother, Xu Shiguang, had passed in ninety-fifth place.
原来他的胞弟徐世光中了第九十五名举人。

Rewards were paid and the provincial hostel bustled for a while.
当下开发了喜封，在会馆中乱过一阵，

Once it grew quiet, Shichang could not help shedding miserable tears.
等静下来不由得凄然下泪。

“Elder Brother, I thought your examination essays better than mine,” Shiguang consoled him. “You must be among the top five.
『大哥，我看你的闱墨比我强。』徐世光安慰他说∶『一定是五经魁，

It is too early for that news.”
报来还早呢！』

When the provincial results were posted, the sealed names were opened and written out one by one, a process lasting from the previous midnight until the following evening.
原来乡试发榜，弥封卷子拆一名，写一名，从前一天半夜，一直要写到第二天晚上。

By custom, the list began with sixth place.
向例写榜从第六名开始，

The top five, the “Leaders of the Five Classics”, were disclosed last, by which time it was evening.
前五名称为，『五经魁』，留到最后揭晓，那时已是第二天晚上，

When those scripts were opened, every servant and labourer in the examination compound held a red candle, making the place bright as day.
到拆五经魁的卷子时，闱中仆役杂工，人手一支红蜡烛，光耀如白昼，

This was called “making a stir over the list”.
称为『闹榜』。

Dusk had not yet come, which was why Shiguang said the news was still to arrive.
其时黄昏未到，所以徐世光说是『报来还早呢』。

“News!”
『报！』

There was another commotion outside.
外面又热闹了，

Shichang listened intently.
徐世昌侧耳静听，

“Your household’s eldest young master, Xu Shichang, has passed the Shuntian provincial examination of the Renwu year in one hundred and forty-fifth place!”
报的是∶『贵府徐大少爷郎世昌，高中壬午科顺天乡试第一百四十五名举人。』

Could it be true?
这是真的吗？

Of course it was.
当然是真的，

He might misread the gold-printed announcement, but the messenger, nicknamed “Three Firsts in Succession”, was demanding his reward.
泥金报条上所写的，还怕会眼花看错，报子『连三元』来讨赏，

Even twenty taels did not satisfy him.
赏了二十两还不肯，

“The eldest young master and the second young master—double happiness at your door!
说是∶『大少爷、二少爷，双喜临门，

At least a hundred taels is called for.”
起码得赏个一百两银子。』

Surely this could not be false.
这总不是假的吧！

After bargaining, they finally sent him away with forty.
争多论少，终于以四十两银子打发了『连三元』。

That was only the first announcement; second and third messengers followed, costing a few more taels.
不过这是『头报』，接下来还有『二报』、『三报』，少不得还要破费几两银子。

The hostel was lively that night.
这一夜会馆中很热闹，

With both Xu brothers bringing honour to the family, congratulations flowed especially thickly into their courtyard.
徐氏兄弟棠棣联辉，他们所住的那个院子，更是贺客接踵不断，

Not until after midnight was it quiet.
直到午夜过后，才得清静下来，

Though physically exhausted, Shichang was too excited to feel sleepy.
虽然人已经非常困倦了，但徐世昌的精神亢奋，一点睡意都没有。


“Younger Brother, how accurate it was!”
『二弟，好灵啊！』

Shichang suddenly sprang up and shouted, giving Shiguang a fright.
徐世昌突然跳起来，大声嚷着，倒把徐世光吓一大跳。

“What thing was accurate?”
