The Desolate Foreign Settlement

『何书办说，这件事一进一出，关系极大，
“Clerk He says the movements into and out of office make this a serious matter.

如果袁老爷的中书还不出娘家，不但升同知不必谈，
If Mr Yuan’s secretaryship cannot be traced to its proper origin, promotion to deputy prefect is out of the question.

还要追究他何以资历不符，
There will also be an investigation into the discrepancy in his record.

那就不是吏部的事了。』
Then it will no longer concern the Ministry of Personnel.”

『不是吏部的事，』徐世昌问∶『是哪一部的事呢？』
“Which ministry would it concern?” Xu asked.

『刑部。』
“Punishments.”

『好家伙！』
“Good heavens!”

徐世昌大吃一惊，
Xu was alarmed.

『还要治罪啊！』
“He could be prosecuted?”

『人家是这么说，
“That is what they say.

咱们也不能全听他的。不过，袁老爷正好有这个短处非求他不可，
We need not believe every word, but Mr Yuan has this weakness and must ask their help.

那就只好听他狮子大开口了。』
We can only listen to an extravagant demand.”

『要多少？』
“How much?”

『两千。』
“Two thousand.”

正好差了一半；
Exactly twice the available sum.

徐世昌面有难色，将袁世凯的信，递给了张瑞荫看。
Looking troubled, Xu handed Yuan’s letter to Zhang Ruiyin.

看完信，张瑞荫问道∶『老牛，他跟你说了没有，是怎么个办法？』
After reading it, Zhang asked, “Old Niu, did he tell you how he means to do it?”

『大少爷，你倒想，他肯跟我说吗？
“Would he tell me, Young Master?

我倒是盯着问了好半天，
I pressed him for ages.

他只跟我说了一句话∶「事不难办，不过就告诉了你，你也办不到。』
All he would say was, ‘The matter is not difficult, but even if I told you, you could not do it.’”

『好吧，
“Very well.

跟他讲盘子吧，
Bargain with him.

最多给他一个半数。』
Fifteen hundred at the most.

张瑞荫又说∶『徐老爷的朋友，不是外人。』
Mr Xu’s friend is not an outsider.”

这时是暗示老牛别从中乱戴帽子∶『是！
This hinted that Old Niu must not inflate the price for his own benefit. “Yes.

既然大少爷交代，我尽力去办就是。』
With your instructions, Young Master, I shall do my utmost.

老牛又说∶『我得连夜跟何书办去谈，
But I must negotiate with him tonight.

不然人家天一亮就走人了。』
He leaves at first light.”


连夜折冲，以一千二百两银子成交；先交一半，等办妥了再交一半。
After negotiations that night, they settled at twelve hundred taels: half in advance and half when the business was complete.

徐世昌第二天到天津，去北洋公所将一千两银子领了出来，存在阜康福钱庄，先打了一张六百两的票子，交给张瑞荫。
The next day Xu went to Tianjin, collected the thousand taels from the Beiyang Office, deposited them at Fukangfu Bank and first obtained a note for six hundred, which he handed to Zhang.

张瑞荫办事也很周到，
Zhang was thorough.

将老牛唤了来说道∶『你最好把何书办约出来，大家当面锣、对面鼓说明白。
He summoned Old Niu. “You had better arrange a meeting with Clerk He, so that everyone can speak plainly face to face.

怕的万一出了什么差错，徐老爷对袁老爷也有个交代。』
If anything goes wrong, Mr Xu must have an account to give Mr Yuan.”

『是。』
“Yes.”

老牛便去约好何书办，在一家饭馆见面。
Old Niu arranged for them to meet at a restaurant.

部中胥吏的身份甚低，
Ministry clerks occupied a low social position.

尽管衣着比纨绔子弟还讲究，但见了张瑞荫称『大少爷』，对徐世昌叫『徐老爷』，站着说话，执礼甚恭。
Though He dressed more elegantly than a rich young dandy, he addressed Zhang as “Young Master” and Xu as “Mr Xu”, standing while he spoke and observing every courtesy.

等把银票递了过去，何书办接到手中，摆在桌上，然后请个安说∶『跟大少爷、徐老爷回，事情呢？一举手之劳，不过要经十三道关口，
When the banknote was handed over, he placed it on the table, bowed and said, “To explain to you both: the task itself is no more than lifting a hand, but it must pass thirteen gates.

一关过不去就不成。
Failure at any one of them will stop it.

银票我暂时收着，也不会去兑，等事情办妥了再说。』
I shall keep the note for now without cashing it until the work is done.”

『是的，
“Very well.

你多费心。』张瑞荫问∶『徐老爷应该怎么回复袁老爷？』
Thank you for your trouble,” Zhang said. “How should Mr Xu reply to Mr Yuan?”

『请徐老爷告诉袁老爷，说当初捐中书的名字不假，
“Tell him that the name under which the secretaryship was purchased was genuine.

只为将门之后，投效戎行，所以改名「世凯」。
As a descendant of a military family, he subsequently volunteered for service and adopted the name Shikai.

只要北洋这么咨复吏部，
Let Beiyang send that explanation to Personnel.

一准改名，袁老爷的同知就升定了。』
Once the change of name is approved, his promotion is assured.”

这个诀窍说穿了不稀奇，
The device was simple once explained.

但如果不是在吏部打通了关节，一改名字就可能会把整个前程断送掉，
Without connections smoothed at the ministry, however, a change of name could destroy an entire career.

因为要刁难的话，随便找个理由就可以折磨个一年半载，
If the clerks wished to obstruct it, any excuse could delay the matter for six months or a year.

及至一关通过，又有另一关，
After one barrier was passed, another would arise.

非把钱花够了数，不能领一张俗称为『部照「的任官』文凭『，
Until enough money had been spent, no appointment warrant—the document commonly called a ministry certificate—would be issued.

而在更名未确定以前，不能分发，不能赴任，
While the change remained unresolved, the man could neither be assigned a post nor take one up.

只有闲等，先就是一大损失。
Waiting alone was a substantial loss.

所以考试发榜，吏部铨选，如果姓名弄错，往往情甘委屈，将错就错，
Consequently, when a name was wrong in examination results or Personnel’s appointments, many willingly endured it and continued under the error.

象袁世凯这样顺利的假』更名『，实在很少。
A fraudulent “name change” as smooth as Yuan’s was rare indeed.


即令如此，公文往返，也得半年工夫。
Even so, the exchange of official documents took half a year.

其时局势又已有变化，李鸿章的回任已经定局了——
By then the situation had changed again: Li Hongzhang’s return to his former post was settled.

从张树声父子无意间得罪了张佩纶以后，李鸿章发觉张树声对北洋有『久假不归』之势，
After Zhang Shusheng and his son inadvertently offended Zhang Peilun, Li realised that Zhang Shusheng seemed inclined to borrow Beiyang indefinitely without returning it.

便利用时机，极力拉拢张佩纶，走李鸿藻的路子，搞出来一个与张树声各回本任的结果，
He seized the opportunity, cultivated Zhang Peilun and worked through Li Hongzao to secure a decision that both men should return to their substantive posts.

但李鸿章母丧尚不满一年，而朝鲜的内乱已经平定，
But less than a year had passed since his mother’s death, and the Korean disturbance had been quelled.

不必再动用武力，就没有『墨从军』而且『夺情』的理由，
With no further need for force, there was no justification for summoning him from mourning to military service.

好在洋务上棘手之事甚多，
Fortunately, foreign affairs offered many difficult matters.

以需要李鸿章与各国公使折冲的借口，将李鸿章留了下来，等待适当的时机再颁回任之谕。
On the pretext that he was needed to negotiate with the foreign ministers, Li was retained, awaiting a suitable moment for the formal order restoring him.

当李鸿章自合肥老家入京时，在上海住了好些日子，对左宗棠打算驱逐李鸿章势力出两江的情形，印象深刻。
Travelling from his Hefei home to Beijing, he spent many days in Shanghai and gained a strong impression of Zuo’s intention to expel his influence from Liangjiang.

同时，对洋务、军务的见解，大相径庭，
Their views on foreign and military affairs also differed sharply.

象中国与法国在越南的纠纷，李鸿章认为『彼欲难餍，我饷难支』，
Over the dispute with France in Vietnam, Li believed that “their demands cannot be satisfied, while our military funds cannot bear the strain”.

应该和平了结，决不可用武，
A peaceful settlement was necessary; force must not be used.

而左宗棠主张支持助越拒法的『黑旗军「刘永福，
Zuo, by contrast, advocated supporting Liu Yongfu’s Black Flag Army in helping Vietnam resist France.

不但以军火接济云贵总督岑毓英转以援刘，而且正式致书总理衙门，认为』主战主和，不难一言而决『，
He supplied arms to Cen Yuying, governor-general of Yunnan and Guizhou, for onward delivery to Liu, and formally wrote the Zongli Yamen that “the choice between war and peace can be settled in a word”.

目前的情势，』不但泰西诸邦多以法为不然；逆料其与中国不协，必致事无结束，悔不可追『，
At present, he argued, “not only do many Western countries consider France in the wrong; they foresee that discord with China must leave the affair without a conclusion and bring irretrievable regret”.

就是法国亦何尝不顾虑，真要与中国开战，危险甚大，不过势成骑虎，不能不虚张声势，
France itself feared the great risks of war with China, but, having mounted the tiger, could only bluff.

如果中国动摇，适中其计。
If China wavered, it would fall into the trap.

他说他』默察时局，惟主战于正义有合，而于时势攸宜，
His assessment was that war accorded with justice, suited the circumstances and conformed to opinion both in China and abroad.

即中外人情亦无不顺『就因为他一直有这种论调，所以朝廷特派李鸿章前赴广东督办越南事宜；
Because he kept arguing thus, the court specially ordered Li to Guangdong to supervise Vietnamese affairs.

这是一个名义，实际上李鸿章并不必赴广东，在上海、在天津，都可以跟法国公使宝海和谈。
This was nominal: Li need not actually go there, since he could negotiate peace with the French minister Bourée in Shanghai or Tianjin.

但如左宗棠不断鼓吹武力干预，到最后恐终不免要到广东去指挥对法战事，那一来只怕非身败名裂不可。
But if Zuo continued urging armed intervention, Li might eventually have to command a war against France from Guangdong—a prospect that threatened to ruin him.

当然，总署亦很持重，不会轻信左宗棠的『正义』说，
The Zongli Yamen was cautious and would not readily accept Zuo’s argument from “justice”.

只是李鸿章跟宝海的交涉，因此而愈感困难。
Nevertheless, his advocacy made Li’s negotiations with Bourée more difficult.


对法如此，对英亦常使李鸿章伤脑筋。
Britain, too, gave Li headaches.

英商的海洋电报线希望由吴淞接一条旱线到上海，左宗棠坚持不许；
British merchants wanted to connect their submarine telegraph cable at Wusong to Shanghai by a land line; Zuo firmly refused.

英商希望减轻茧捐，左宗棠亦表反对。
They sought a reduction in the cocoon levy; he opposed that as well.

而最使李鸿章为难的是，左宗棠倡议洋药土烟加厘一事。『洋药土烟』皆指鸦片，『加厘』便是加『通行税』。
Most troublesome was his proposal to raise the likin on imported opium and native-grown opium.

左宗棠认为鸦片流毒无穷，主张寓禁于征，
He regarded opium’s harm as limitless and proposed using taxation as a means of suppression.

奉旨允准后，会同李鸿章与英国公使威妥玛交涉。
After imperial approval, he and Li negotiated with the British minister, Thomas Wade.

威妥玛提出洋药进口增加税，行销内地在各关卡所征厘捐不增加；
Wade proposed increasing the import duty while leaving inland transit levies unchanged.

左宗棠也同意了，但每箱的进口税，中国要一百五十两；英国只愿缴八十两。
Zuo agreed in principle, but China demanded a hundred and fifty taels per chest and Britain offered only eighty.

相差太巨，一直没有成议。
The gap was too wide for agreement.

以后左宗棠外放，交涉由李鸿章接办，
After Zuo took a provincial appointment, Li continued the negotiations.

而威妥玛奉调回国，希望此一交涉能如英国的条件谈成功，增添他回国以后的面子，
Wade, recalled to Britain, hoped to conclude the matter on British terms and return home with enhanced standing.

李鸿章有心帮忙，却以左宗棠的不肯妥协，以江督的地位表示反对，搞得事成僵局。
Li wished to help, but Zuo refused to compromise and opposed the terms as governor-general of Liangjiang, producing a deadlock.


但在事业上最大的冲突是，李鸿章原主『海防』，而张佩纶有个专设『水师衙门』创办新式海军之议，大为李鸿章所欣赏。
Their greatest conflict of policy concerned the navy. Li had originally advocated maritime defence, and greatly admired Zhang Peilun’s proposal for a special Naval Yamen and a modern fleet.

但左宗棠一到两江，巡阅过海口及长江以后，改变了他原来『陆防』的主张，
But after reaching Liangjiang and inspecting the coast and Yangtze, Zuo changed his earlier preference for land defence.

特意将水师出身的彭玉麟请了来，商量造新式兵舰，而且已经开始在办了。
He invited Peng Yulin, whose career had begun in the naval forces, to discuss modern warship construction, and work was already under way.

左宗棠首创福建船政，对此道不能说他是外行；
Having founded the Fujian shipbuilding establishment, Zuo could not be dismissed as an amateur.

因此可以预见的是，将来创办新式海军，左宗棠决不容北洋单独掌权。
It was therefore foreseeable that he would never permit Beiyang sole authority over the future modern navy.

『海防』、『陆防』之争，只要打倒了他的理论，便无他虑；
In a dispute between land and sea defence, defeating his argument would have removed the difficulty.

如今左宗棠亦主张海防，那就变成彼此竞争着办一件事，
Now that he too favoured maritime defence, they would compete to carry out the same enterprise.

权不能独专、事不能由心，是李鸿章最不能容忍的一件事。
Li could not tolerate sharing authority or being unable to act as he wished.

因此，无论看眼前，算将来，李鸿章认为左宗棠是非拔除不可的眼中钉。
Whether he considered the present or the future, Zuo was a thorn in his eye that must be removed.

这得从剪除左宗棠的羽翼着手。
The first step was to strip away his support.

李鸿章手下的谋士，都有这样一种见解；且认为第一个目标，应该是胡雪岩。
Li’s advisers agreed, and thought Hu Xueyan should be the first target.

于是上海道邵友濂便与盛宣怀等人，密密商定了一个打击胡雪岩的办法，在洋债还款这件事上，造成胡雪岩的困窘。
Shanghai Circuit Intendant Shao Youlian accordingly made a secret plan with Sheng Xuanhuai and others: they would place Hu in difficulty over repayment of a foreign loan.


其时胡雪岩经手、尚未清结的借款，还有两笔，
Two loans handled by Hu remained outstanding.

一笔是光绪四年八月所借的商款，华洋各计，总计六百五十万两；
One was merchant funding contracted in the eighth month of the fourth year of Guangxu, with Chinese and foreign portions totalling six and a half million taels.

洋款不借借商款，
Why had merchant funding been used rather than a foreign loan?

其中别有衷曲，
There was a story behind it.

原来光绪三年，由胡雪岩经手，向汇丰银行借款五百万两，借还均用实银，
In Guangxu’s third year, Hu had arranged a five-million-tael loan from HSBC, to be advanced and repaid in actual silver.

条件是月息一分二厘五，期限七年，连本带利分十四期拔还。
Interest was one and a quarter per cent a month, with principal and interest repaid in fourteen instalments over seven years.

每期六个月，
Each instalment fell six months apart.

仍由浙、粤、江海、江汉四关出票，按期偿还。
As before, repayment was secured by bills issued by the Zhejiang, Guangdong, Shanghai and Jianghan customs.

此外有个附带条件，即商定此项条件后，如果借方作罢，三个月内关票不到，则胡雪岩罚银十五万两；
A supplementary condition stipulated that if the borrower abandoned the transaction after agreement, failing to supply the customs bills within three months, Hu would forfeit a hundred and fifty thousand taels.

汇丰如果三个月内不交银，罚款相间。
HSBC would incur the same penalty if it failed to deliver the silver within three months.

这笔借款由于两江总督沈葆桢的介入，一波三折，拖延甚久。
Intervention by Shen Baozhen, governor-general of Liangjiang, caused repeated complications and long delay.

其时西征军事颇为顺手，
The western campaign was then going well.

刘锦棠率军自乌鲁木齐南进，并分兵与陕西提督张曜会功吐鲁番，一举克复，
Liu Jintang had advanced south from Urumqi and sent a force to join Shaanxi provincial commander Zhang Yao in an attack that recovered Turfan at one stroke.

回乱首脑之一的白彦虎仓皇西遁；
Bai Yanhu, one of the Muslim rebel leaders, fled west in haste.

刘锦棠亦推进至吐鲁番盆地西端的托克逊，回众投降者两万余人。
Liu reached Toksun at the western end of the Turfan basin, and more than twenty thousand Muslim followers surrendered.

但回部首脑经和硕、焉耆，出铁门关在库尔勒地方，跟俄国军方搭上了线，
But the leadership passed through Hoxud and Karashahr, emerged from Iron Gate Pass and established contact with the Russian military at Korla.

而西征军却因粮饷困难，无法西进，左宗棠着急得不得了。
Meanwhile, shortages of provisions and pay prevented the expeditionary army from advancing west, to Zuo’s intense anxiety.

好不容易在五月里谈成功了这笔洋债，
At last the foreign loan was negotiated in the fifth month.

至少望梅止渴，军心先是一振；同时在上海、湖北、陕西的三处粮台，借商款应急，亦比较容易措手了。
Even the prospect was like imagining plums to quench thirst: army morale revived, and the supply offices in Shanghai, Hubei and Shaanxi found it easier to borrow from merchants for immediate needs.


哪知在办手续时，起了波折，
Complications arose during the formalities.

原来英商汇丰银行贷款，照例要由总理衙门出面，致英国公使一个照会，叙明借款条件等等，
A loan from the British-owned HSBC ordinarily required the Zongli Yamen to notify the British minister of its terms.

由英国公使再转行总税务司及驻上海领事，转知汇丰银行照办。
The minister then instructed the inspector-general of customs and the Shanghai consul to inform HSBC accordingly.

这一来，如果贷款放出去收不回，便可由英国向中国交涉；
If the loan could not be recovered, Britain could then raise the matter with China.

这通照会实际上是中国政府所出的保证书，
In effect, the note was a Chinese government guarantee.

所以由汇丰银行拟好稿子，交给胡雪岩，再经左宗棠咨请总理衙门办理，
HSBC therefore drafted it and gave it to Hu, who passed it through Zuo with a request that the Zongli Yamen act.

而汇丰的稿子中，说明『息银不得过一分』，
Yet the bank’s draft said that interest must not exceed one per cent.

然则左宗棠的奏折中，何以说是月息一分二厘五？
Why, then, had Zuo’s memorial specified one and a quarter per cent a month?

为此，其中处于关键地位的总税务司赫德，表示这笔借款不能成立。
Robert Hart, the inspector-general of customs whose role was crucial, declared the loan could not proceed.

这当然要查。
An investigation was inevitable.

左宗棠根据胡雪岩的答复回奏，说汇丰的息银，只有一分，诚然不错，
On the basis of Hu’s explanation, Zuo memorialised that HSBC’s interest was indeed only one per cent.

但付款办法是以先令计算，折付银圆；
But the loan was reckoned in shillings and paid in silver dollars.

这种银圆，一向在东南各省通用，称之为『烂番银』，西北向不通用，
Those dollars, commonly called “worn foreign silver”, circulated in the southeastern provinces but not in the northwest.

所以仍旧须借以两为单位的现银。
The funds therefore still had to be obtained in actual silver measured in taels.

但先令的市价，根据伦敦挂牌，早晚不同；
The shilling exchange rate varied with London quotations, even between morning and evening.

到时候如果汇价上涨，胡雪岩便要吃赔帐，
If it rose at the time of conversion, Hu would suffer a loss.

所以接洽德商泰来洋行，『包认先令』，
He had therefore arranged with the German Tailai Trading House to guarantee the shilling conversion.

这要承担相当风险，泰来洋行得息二厘五，并不为多。
This involved substantial risk, for which its additional quarter of a percentage point in interest was not excessive.

左宗棠表示，此案『首尾本属一贯』，只是前次『未经声叙明析』，
Zuo insisted that the arrangement had been consistent throughout and merely had not previously been explained clearly.

又力言胡雪岩『息借洋款，实无别故』。
He strongly asserted that Hu had no ulterior motive in borrowing abroad.

很显然的，这是左宗棠硬顶下来的，朝廷不能不买他的老面子；
Plainly, he was taking responsibility and standing firm; the court could hardly refuse to respect his seniority.

左宗棠心里却觉得很不是味道，
Inwardly, however, he felt badly about it.

从此对胡雪岩的信用便打了一个折扣，可是却不能不用胡雪岩。
His trust in Hu diminished, though he could not do without him.


胡雪岩当然亦想力盖前愆，于是而有借商款的办法，
Hu naturally wished to make amends, and devised the merchant-loan method.

这年——光绪三年年底，左宗棠写给胡雪岩的复信说∶『今岁饷事，拮据殊常，
At the end of that year, Guangxu’s third, Zuo wrote in reply: “This year our finances have been exceptionally strained.

非枢邸严催协饷，筹部款，大局已不可问。

What is to be done after that, I do not know.

What I wished to discuss, you have already considered for me.

『原来胡雪岩早替左宗棠算过了，年底本应发饷；陕甘两省旱灾要赈济，再还了湖北、上海两处借款，到得明年夏秋之间，便又是青黄不接的时期了。

Borrowing for military finance required a year’s preparation.

Why trouble the Zongli Yamen?”

For the first two years only interest was payable.

第三年起每年拔本一百万两，分两期给付；光绪九年四月付第一期、十月付第二期，每期各五十万两。
From the third year, a million taels of principal fell due annually, in two instalments: the first in the fourth month of Guangxu’s ninth year and the second in the tenth, five hundred thousand taels each.

Hu had handled earlier foreign loans without having to worry about repayment.

If provincial bills could not be honoured, he could withhold customs revenue to pay them.

Assuming that future provincial remittances would easily cover it, he had broadly promised that principal would “be repaid on schedule through the Shanghai Transport Office; if Shanghai has no funds, the lender shall be permitted to obtain payment on schedule from the Ministry of Revenue”.

He feared that the Zongli Yamen and Li might prompt Hart to obstruct the loan, and accepted concessions to secure it.

无形之中便将全部风险都加了在胡雪岩的肩头上，
The promise quietly placed the entire risk on Hu’s shoulders.

If the provinces failed to remit, HSBC would certainly approach him.

There was no need for the trouble of asking the British minister to negotiate with Revenue.

With his resources, credit and willingness to shoulder responsibility, Hu would surely meet each five-hundred-thousand-tael principal instalment.


话虽如此，五十万两银子到底不是一个小数目。
Even so, half a million taels was no small sum.

邵友濂与盛宣怀秘密商定，到时候，『挤他一挤』，
Shao Youlian and Sheng Xuanhuai secretly agreed to “give him a squeeze” when the time came.

虽未必能挤倒，至少可以打击打击他的信用。
They might not bring him down, but could at least damage his credit.

That spring, Guangxu’s ninth year, relations with France deteriorated again.

But the French naval and colonial authorities separately told their foreign ministry that they objected to Bourée’s agreement.

海军方面且已增兵越南北部的海防。
The navy had already sent reinforcements to Haiphong in northern Vietnam.

A political change in France then brought a new cabinet.

新内阁的外交部长沙美拉库支持军部的主张，推翻前议，而且将宝海撤任，另派特使德理固专程来华谈判。

With a French minister opposing his own government, Li felt confident of peace once more.

所以仍旧照原定计划，奏请准予给假回籍葬亲。
He therefore followed his original plan and requested leave to return home for his mother’s burial.

李还不肯回任，但为了开始建设旅顺军港，北洋大臣的差使是接下来了，
He was still unwilling to resume his former office, but had accepted the Beiyang commission in order to begin construction of the naval harbour at Lüshun.

既然请假，北洋大臣自然由张树声暂署。
During his leave, Zhang Shusheng would naturally act in that capacity.

They not only captured Nam Dinh but directly infringed Chinese interests.

招商局运米的船，在海防为法军扣押；设在海防及顺安的两处仓库，为法军占领，其中的存粮及其他物品，当然也被没收了。

Besides formally seeking aid through the Ministry of Rites, the Vietnamese government specially sent its “Minister of Punishments”, Fan Shen, to China, emulating Shen Baoxu’s weeping appeal at the court of Qin.

因此，朝中震动，
The court was shaken.

清议昂扬，都主张采取强硬的对策；
Moral criticism rose, and all called for a firm response.

甚至驻英兼驻法公使一等毅勇侯曾纪泽，亦打电报回来，建议派军援越，不可对法国让步。
Even Zeng Jize, the first-rank Marquis Yiyong and minister to Britain and France, telegraphed to urge military aid to Vietnam and no concessions to France.

当时疆臣亦多主战，
Many provincial chiefs also favoured war.

云贵总督岑毓英，备战已有多时，但署理两广总督的曾国荃，却不愿轻启战端，清议深为不满，
Cen Yuying had long prepared for it, but Zeng Guoquan, acting governor-general of Liangguang, would not lightly begin hostilities, to the critics’ great displeasure.

因而主持总署的恭王，一面循外交途径向法国抗议；一面奏准命李鸿章迅回直隶总督本任，
Prince Gong, directing the Zongli Yamen, protested diplomatically to France while obtaining approval to order Li back at once to his substantive post as governor-general of Zhili.

Zuo was simultaneously ordered to prepare troops in the lower Yangtze region for possible dispatch south.

胡雪岩的估计很正确，
Hu’s estimate proved correct.

At the end of the third month, Li arrived in Shanghai by a specially arranged steamer and established headquarters at Tianhou Temple.
