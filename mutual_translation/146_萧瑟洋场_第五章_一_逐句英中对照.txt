CHAPTER FIVE
第五章


The sight of Gu Yingchun gave Hu Xueyan a shock.
一见古应春的面，胡雪岩一吓跳，

He had grown so thin that his whole appearance had changed.
他人都瘦得落形了。

“Yingchun, what—what has happened to you?” Gu sighed deeply.
『应春，你，你怎么弄成这个样子？』『唉！』古应春长长地叹口气，

“Little Uncle, my luck has been terrible.
『小爷叔，我的运气太坏！

I was careless too.”
也怪我自己大意。』

“What happened?
『你出了什么事？

Tell me at once.”
快告诉我。』

“I am going to lose everything.
『我要倾家荡产了。』

All because I listened to Xu Yuzhi.”
古应春说∶『都是听信了徐雨之的话。』

Xu was a wealthy Guangdong merchant whom Hu also knew well.
这徐雨之是广东籍的富商，胡雪岩跟他也很熟。

His personal name was Run.
此人单名一个润字，

Both capable and fortunate, he had learnt business in a Shanghai foreign firm, winning the foreigners’ high regard.
人很能干，运气也很好，在上海一家洋行学生意，深得洋人的器重，

From the age of twenty-two, he had joined partners to establish banks, silk firms and tea warehouses, profiting handsomely in every venture.
从廿二岁开始与人合伙开钱庄，开丝号，开茶栈，无不大发利市。

By the second year of Tongzhi, when he was twenty-six, he had accumulated a hundred thousand taels or more.
同治二年廿六岁，已经积赀十来万，

Through the Jiangnan military supply office he purchased the rank of an assistant departmental director, adding a peacock feather, and became a prominent gentleman of the Shanghai foreign settlement.
在江南粮台报捐员外郎，加捐花翎，俨然上海洋场上有名的绅士了。

In Tongzhi’s tenth year, he consequently received a commission.
因此，同治十年得了个差使。

Zeng Guofan, governor-general of Liangjiang and Nanyang minister, had decided to send young boys abroad to study.
那时两江总督南洋大臣曾国藩决定挑选幼童出洋留学；

Preliminary enquiries suggested selecting them in Guangdong.
事先研究，这批幼童以在广东挑选为宜，

Most Chinese residents in America came from that province, whose people were receptive to new ideas.
因为美国的华侨绝大部分是广东人，广东风气开通，

Fathers and elder brothers would not be unduly alarmed at sending young relatives across ten thousand li of ocean; meanwhile, the boys could be visited in America by older countrymen speaking familiar dialects, easing their homesickness.
作父兄的固不以幼年子弟在万里重洋之外而不放心；而此辈幼童在美国常有乡音亲切的长辈去看他们，亦可以稍慰思乡之苦。

As a leader of Shanghai’s Guangdong merchants, Xu received the task.
由于徐润是上海『广东帮』商人的领袖，所以曾国藩把这个差使交了给他。

He planned carefully, choosing a hundred and twenty promising boys to travel in four groups of thirty.
徐润策划得很周到，挑选了一百二十个资质很不错的幼童，分四批出洋，每批三十人；

The first sailed early in the seventh month of Tongzhi’s eleventh year under Yung Wing.
第一批在同治十一年七月初上船，由容闳带队，

Most were from Guangdong, especially Xiangshan, Xu’s own county.
大部分是广东籍，广东籍中又以香山为最多，因为徐润就是香山人。


There were only five boys from elsewhere: two from Jiangsu, one from Shandong, one from Fujian, and one whose family came from Huizhou, though he had been recruited in Guangdong.
当然，也有其他省份的人，但为数极少，只得五个，两个江苏、一个山东、一个福建、还有一个是徽州人，不过是广东招来的，

The last was twelve, born in the year of the Xinyou coup, and named Zhan Tianyou.
这个十二岁、生在辛酉政变那一年的幼童，叫做詹天佑，

His father, Zhan Zuoping, worked as a machinist at the Fujian shipbuilding establishment, while the family lived in Guangzhou.
他的父亲叫詹作屏，在福建船政局当机器匠，家眷寄居广州。

When Tianyou applied, someone advised his father to let him study law so that he could return and become an official.
詹天佑应募时，有人劝詹作屏让他的儿子学法律，学成回国，可以做官；

Zhan insisted that his son learn technical skills, and the newest ones at that.
但詹屏坚持他的儿子要学技艺，而且要学最新的技艺。

The second group sailed in the fifth month of Tongzhi’s twelfth year, led by Huang Pingfu, whose family was linked to Xu’s by marriage.
第二批是在同治十二年五月放洋的，由徐润的亲家黄平甫领队。

Besides the thirty government-supported boys, seven more from Guangdong went at their parents’ expense.
这回在挑选的官费生三十名以外，另有七名广东少年，由他们的家长自备资斧，

Their families asked Huang to take them to America: attitudes had indeed changed, and privately funded overseas study had begun.
请黄平甫带到美国——风气到底大开了，已经有自费留学的了。

The third group left in the eighth month of Tongzhi’s thirteenth year.
第三批是在同治十三年八月间派遣。

This time, apart from two boys studying technical crafts and one studying machinery, all attended ordinary schools: the older boys at middle schools, the younger at elementary schools.
这回与以前不同的是，除了两个学技艺、一个学机器以外，其余的都念普通学校，年长的念『中馆』；年幼的念『小馆』，

Even the older ones were only thirteen, including Tang Shaoyi of Xiangshan and Zhu Baokui of Changzhou, Jiangsu.
但所谓年长，亦不过十三岁，台广东香山的唐绍仪、江苏常州的朱宝奎；

The youngest were at least ten.
而最年幼的，至少也要十岁。

The fourth group departed in the ninth month of Guangxu’s first year.
第四批放洋在光绪元年九月，

Ten additional places brought the total to forty.
增加了十个名额，一共是四十名，

All would attend ordinary schools and choose a specialisation after completing middle school, according to their aptitudes.
这回一律念普通学校，到中学毕业，再视他们性之所近，决定学什么。

More boys came from other provinces, though these were still confined to Jiangsu, Zhejiang and Anhui.
同时外省籍的幼童也多了，但仍不脱江苏、浙江、安徽三省。

Zeng had initiated the scheme but did not live to see the first departure, dying in office in the second month of Tongzhi’s eleventh year.
幼童放洋是曾国藩所创议，但他不及见第一批幼童放洋，同治十一年二月殁于任上；

Li Hongzhang subsequently supported it.
以后便由李鸿章支持这件事，

Xu thereby gained Li’s appreciation and received a Beiyang appointment as associate director of China Merchants, working alongside Sheng Xuanhuai.
徐润亦由此获得李鸿章的赏识，由北洋札委为招商局的会办，与盛宣怀同事。


During those seven or eight years, Xu’s enterprises had flourished.
在这七八年中，徐润的事业蒸蒸日上，

He remained far below Hu in wealth, but was a substantial Shanghai merchant.
当然还远不及胡雪岩，但亦算是上海『夷场』上的殷商。

Apart from charitable undertakings, Hu had no business dealings with him.
胡雪岩跟他除了作善举以外，别无生意上的往来，

Gu, however, came originally from Guangdong, often encountered him when dealing with foreign firms, and had become close to him.
而古应春因为原籍广东，又以跟洋商打交道时，常会聚在一起，所以跟徐润走得很近，

They shared several ventures, including property speculation.
也有好些合伙的事业，其中之一是做房地产生意。

Xu held more than two thousand nine hundred mu of land, fifty-one foreign-style houses and at least two thousand commercial premises.
徐润的房地产很多，地皮有两千九百多亩，建成的洋房有五十一所，市房更多，不下两千间，

Their book value exceeded two million two hundred thousand taels, but too much capital was tied up.
照帐面上算，值到两百二十几万，但积压的资本太重，

Vacant land produced nothing and still incurred taxes, while rents from the premises were limited.
空地毫无收入，还要付税；市房则只是收租金，为数有限。

A British friend named Gulin, a knight keen on sport, widely connected and knowledgeable about business, proposed a partnership.
于是，他有一个英国朋友，名叫顾林，此人在英国是个爵士，本人热心运动，交游很广，亦很懂生意经，他向徐润建议，彼此合作。

Gulin also knew Gu, so Xu invited him to their discussions.
顾林亦是古应春的朋友，因此，徐润邀他跟顾林一起谈合作，

“Let us form a large company and invest capital to build on all the empty land,” Gulin said. “Build one group of houses and sell it, then use the proceeds for the next.
『我们组织一个大公司，投入资金，在空地上都盖起房子来。』顾林说道∶『造一批，卖一批；卖来的款子造第二批。

Once the vacant land is used, rebuild the old houses and keep renewing them.
空地用完了，把旧房子再来翻造，不断更新，

That is how great foreign cities, especially American ones, have grown.”
外国的大都市，尤其是美国，都是这样建造起来的。』

Xu understood the cycle of building and selling.
这个周而复始盖房子的决窍，徐润也懂，

“But it needs a great deal of ready cash.
『可是，「他问∶』这要大批现金，

Can you invest?”
你能不能投资？

“Of course.
『『当然，

Otherwise I would not propose a partnership.
我没有这个意思，不会跟你谈合作。

But I too must return home to raise subscriptions.
不过，我也是要回国去招股。

Once we agree on the terms and circulate the prospectus in London, I believe we can raise the capital in less than three months.”
我们把合作的办法商量好了，拿章程在伦敦市场上传了出去，相信不到三个月，就能把股本募足。』

“How much capital?”
『股本算多少呢？』

“That depends on you.
『这要看你的意思。

Value your property—at its real value, of course—and I shall raise an equal sum.”
你拿你的房地产作价——当然是实价；看值多少，我就募多少股本。』

Xu nodded and asked Gu’s view.
『徐润点点头问古应春∶』你看呢？

“It is workable and fair.
『『他这个法子可行，也很公平。

But I think our side should hold a larger share.”
不过，我认为我们这方面股份要多占些。』

After consideration, Xu proposed definite terms: capital of four million taels, fifty-five per cent Chinese and forty-five per cent British.
徐润想了一下，提出很明确的办法，这中英合资的公司股本定为四百万两，华方占五成半，英方占四成半；

The Chinese side would contribute property at an agreed real valuation, and the British side remit one million eight hundred thousand taels in cash.
华方以房地产核实作价，英方四成半计一百八十万两，由英国汇来现金。


A lawyer drew up a preliminary agreement, which they signed.
于是，请律师撰文签订了草约，

Xu also gave Gulin ten thousand taels to go home and raise subscriptions.
徐润还送了一万两银子给顾林，让他回国去招股。

But his property’s true value was only one and a half million.
但是徐润的房地产，照实价只值一百五十万两；

Another seven hundred thousand taels’ worth of land had to be bought to make up the Chinese share of two million two hundred thousand.
还要再买价值七十万两的地皮，才能凑足二百二十万两，合足五成半之数。

“Brother Yingchun, good friends should share the profits.
『应春兄，好朋友利益均沾，

Will you subscribe that seven hundred thousand?”
这七十万两，你来入股如何？』

After examining his resources, Gu agreed to contribute five hundred thousand.
古应春筹划了一下，愿意出五十万两银子。

This was at the end of the previous year.
这是去年年底的话；

By the second month of the present one, enough land had been purchased—but disaster overtook Gulin.
到这年二月里，地皮买足数了，可是顾林却出了事。

Soon after returning to London, he attended a royal hunting party.
原来顾林回到伦敦不久，在一次皇室邀请的狩猎会中，

His horse stumbled and threw him headfirst, inflicting a grave brain injury.
马失前蹄、人从马上倒栽出去，头先着地，脑子受了重伤，

Two eminent physicians saved his life, but he developed epilepsy.
请了两位名医诊治，性命虽已保住，但得了个癫痫症，

The great company project quietly died.
合作设大分司的事，就此无疾而终。

Xu and Gu were hit hard.
这一来徐润跟古应春大受打击，

In the Vietnamese dispute, France had repudiated the settlement and prepared for war, sending Admiral Courbet east with a fleet.
因为中法在越南的纠纷，法国政府不惜推翻已经达成和解的协议，准备动武，且已派水师提督孤拔，率舰东来，

Its legislature also voted five million francs for military expenditure.
同时国会通过，拨款五百万法郎，作为战费，

Rumours swept Shanghai.
因此上海谣言纷纷，

The most widespread said that French warships were continually cruising outside Wusong and had decided to attack the arsenal.
流传最盛的一个说法是，法国军舰不断巡弋在吴淞口外，决定要攻制造局。

Timid people were already fleeing.
胆小的人已经开始逃难；

In this atmosphere of panic, no one would even enquire about property.
在这种风声鹤唳的情况之下房地产根本无人问津。


“Of my five hundred thousand taels, three hundred and fifty thousand were borrowed.
『我那五十万银子，其中卅五万是借来的；

Money is now desperately tight.
现在银根紧到极点，

All thirty-odd Shanghai banks are trembling, afraid of another panic.
上海三十几家钱庄，家家心惊肉跳，只怕再来一个风潮，

If everyone withdraws their deposits, any number may collapse together.
大家提存挤兑，一倒就是多少家。

My lenders are pressing hard—very hard.
我借的款子，催得很急；实在是急！

Their clerks come and sit at my door every day, demanding payment.
每天都有钱庄里的伙计上门坐讨，

I can only keep pleading with them.” Gu added, “And I am afraid A’qi will hear and worry, so I must constantly hide it from her.
只好不断同人家说好话。』古应春又说∶『还有一层，我怕阿七晓得了着急，还要时时刻刻留心瞒住她。

Little Uncle, imagine the life I am leading.”
小爷叔，你想，我过的是啥日子？』

Hearing this and seeing his haggard face, Hu felt a sharp pang of pity.
胡雪岩听了他这番话，再看到他憔悴的形容，恻然心伤，

“Do not worry, Yingchun!”
『应春，你放心！』

He struck his chest. “I shall settle it for you.
他拍一拍胸脯说∶『我来替你了；

Leave everything to me.”
都在我身上。』

Gu hesitated without answering.
古应春迟疑未答。

Hu was surprised.
胡雪岩倒奇怪了，

Surely he should be delighted to have someone shoulder the whole burden?
照情理说，现有人替他一肩担承，他应该高兴才是，

Why look so troubled?
何以有此显得困惑的神情？

“What other difficulty is there?
『应春，』他问∶『还有啥难处？

With our friendship, what can you not tell me?”
我们这样的交情，你还有啥在我面前说不出口的话？』

“Little Uncle,” Gu paused, “do you really know nothing of the Shanghai market?”
『小爷叔，』古应春顿了一下问道∶『莫非上海的市面，你真的一点都不晓得？』

“What do you mean?
『怎么？

Markets rise and fall.
市面有好有坏，

That is ordinary enough.”
这也是常有的事。』

Gu stared, then at last spoke. “It seems you truly do not know.
古应春楞住了，好一会方始开口∶『看起来你老人家真的不晓得。

Let me tell you plainly: the man pressing hardest for repayment is Old Mi.”
我现在说实话吧，来催讨欠款，来催得最厉害的，就是老宓。』

Hu’s face burned as though he had been slapped.
此言一出，胡雪岩脸上火辣辣地发烧，真象上海人所说的『吃耳光』一样，

His thoughts tumbled, and he could not speak.
一时心里七上八下，竟开不得口了。

Old Mi was Mi Benchang, the managing clerk of his own Fukang Bank.
原来古应春口中的『老宓』，就是他阜康钱庄的档手宓本常。

“Pressing our own people so hard!
『自己人催欠款催得这么厉害！

Outrageous!”
岂有此理！』

Hu was angry.
胡雪岩非常生气；

Then another thought came: if even a friend was being pursued so urgently, Fukang itself must be in serious difficulty.
但转念一想，连自己人的欠款都催得这么厉害，可见得阜康的境况也很窘。

这一转念间，惊出一身汗，定一定神说道∶『应春，你晓得的，这几年，阜康的事，我都交老宓，难得问一问；

From what you say, its funds seem tighter than anyone’s.
照现在看，阜康的银根好象比哪一家都紧，

Tell me exactly what is happening.”
你倒同我说一说，到底是怎么个情形？』


“You have come from Jiangning, Little Uncle.
『小爷叔，你从江宁来，

Did His Excellency Zuo not discuss the Shanghai market with you?”
莫非没有听左大人跟你谈上海的市面？』

“What?
『怎么？

Is the market—” “It has never been worse.
上海的市面，莫非┅┅』『从来没有这么坏过。

Do you know how much ready silver there is in Shanghai now?”
小爷叔，你晓得现在上海的现银有多少？』

“How much?”
『有多少？』

Gu raised one finger.
『这个。』古应春伸一指相示。

“Ten million?”
『一千万？』

“One million.”
『一百万。』

Hu was shocked.
胡雪岩大吃一惊，

“Really?”
『真的？』他问。

“Ask Mi and you will see.”
『你差别老宓就晓得了。』

Hu still found it hard to believe.
胡雪岩仍旧有点不大相信，

“If the market is so bad, someone should tell His Excellency Zuo.
『市面这么坏，应该有人告诉左大人啊！』他说，

When we discussed Shanghai in Jiangning, he said that French military threats had affected trade somewhat, but it was nothing serious.”
『我在江宁，跟左大人谈起上海他说因为法国称兵，上海市面多少受点影响，不过不要紧。』

Gu gave a cold snort. “Which official nowadays does not deceive those above and bully those below?
『哼！』古应春冷笑一声∶『现在做官的，哪个不是瞒上欺下，

只会做喜鹊，不肯当乌鸦。』

“Come.
『走！

Let us go to Jixian Lane together.”
「胡雪岩说∶』我们一起到集贤里去。

Fukang stood in Jixian Lane in the British settlement, just across a road from Hu’s residence.
『阜康钱庄设在英租界集贤里，与胡雪岩的公馆只隔一条马路，

他经常是安步当车走了去的。

As they were leaving, the housekeeper, Mrs Chen, hurried out. “When will you be back, sir?”
正要出门时，女管家陈嫂赶出来问道∶』老爷，啥辰光回来？『


“I do not know yet.”
『现在还不晓得。』

“Mr Mi has just sent an apprentice to say he knows you have arrived and will come at dinnertime.” She added, “We have found a very fresh shad today, which is rare.
『刚刚宓先生派徒弟来通知，他说晓得老爷已经来了，吃夜饭辰光他会来。』陈嫂又说∶『今夜难得买到一条很新鲜的鲥鱼，

老爷回来吃夜饭吧！』

Hearing that Mi was coming, Hu hesitated.
一听宓本常要来，胡雪岩倒有些踌躇了；

Gu said, “Then perhaps we should wait for him.
古应春便即说道∶『即然如此，不如等老宓来，

Hu took his striking watch from his gauze waistcoat pocket.
胡雪岩听这一说，便从纱背心口袋中掏出打簧表来看，

已经四点半了，

于是重回客厅去密谈。

Hu’s chief concern was the first five-hundred-thousand-tael principal payment to HSBC.
胡雪岩此时最关心的是要还汇丰银行第一期的本银五十万两。

The sealed notes issued by the governor-general’s office in Shaanxi and Gansu were little more than a formality.
陕甘总督衙门出的『印票』，不过是摆个样子，

数目如果不够，他可以代垫，但银根如此之紧，代垫恐有不能，

“Yingchun, the HSBC payment is due at the end of the month.
『应春，』他问，『汇丰的款子，月底要交，

Do you know how much Shao Xiaocun has received?”
你晓不晓得，邵小村那里已经收到多少了？』

“Ten days or so ago I heard that half had arrived.
『前十来天我听说，已经收到半数了。

More should have come in since.
这几天，总还有款子进来。

The shortage should be no more than a million or so.
