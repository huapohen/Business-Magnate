The Desolate Foreign Settlement

Where one bank had a surplus, another had a deficit.
有多就有少，

The association issued a transfer order authorising Fukang to collect silver from a bank short of funds, with payment due before two the following afternoon.
由总会开出『划条』交阜康向欠头寸的钱庄先收现银。时间规定是在第二天下午两点钟以前。

What could the bank in deficit do?
那么，缺头寸的钱庄怎么办呢？

No matter: the next morning it could borrow through the association from another bank with a surplus.
不要紧，第二天上午可以到公会向有头寸多的同行去拆进，

Interest was calculated by the day, the transaction being called a “silver loan”. Such interbank advances for a day or two required no receipt, only an entry in the association’s books.
利息以日计，称为『银拆』这种一两天的同业借款，不必打收据，由公会记一笔帐就可以了。

There were two methods of charging interest.
至于利息的计算，又分两种，

Loans without receipts were “floating advances”, their rates rising and falling with the money market.
不打收据的拆借，称为『活拆』，利息高低视银根松紧而定。

Longer interbank loans were “fixed advances”, supported by written instruments at an agreed rate, unaffected by the daily quotation.
另外一种同业长期的拆借，称为『呆拆』，要立票据，议定利息；在此期间，不受每天挂牌的『银拆』的影响。

“Issuing public clearing certificates is like handing out gambling counters,” Mi explained.
『这种打「公单」的法子，就好象赌钱发筹码，

“There are two differences.
所不同的是，

First, only the house controls a casino’s counters, while every participating bank can issue these certificates.
第一，赌场的筹码，只有头家可以管；公单只要是汇划钱庄，家家可开。

Second, casino counters cannot be spent outside.
第二，赌场的筹码，不能拿到外面去用，

A clearing certificate can be converted into the bank’s own notes and used anywhere.
公单可以化成本号的银票，到处可用。

In plain terms, it creates something from nothing—tens of millions of taels out of thin air.
说实了，无非无中生有，凭空生出几千万银子来；

That is why great transactions continue even though there is only a million in actual silver.
所以现银不过一百万，市面上的大生意照样在做。

That is the clearing trick.”
这就是要汇划的把戏。』

They then turned to silk and cocoons.
接下来便谈到丝茧的情形。

Buyers going into the countryside depended largely on bank loans.
丝茧业下乡收值，多仰赖钱庄放款，

Hu’s control of a bank had helped make him a leader of the silk trade.
胡雪岩也就因为有钱庄在手里，所以成为丝业领袖，

During the past two years, his campaign against modern reeling mills had led him to buy still more cocoons and silk.
这两年因为抵制新式缫丝厂，收的茧子与丝更多。

Mi did not manage the silk business, but could judge the size of its stocks from the consolidated records of deposits and loans at the branches.
宓本常虽非胡雪岩经营丝业方面的档手，但可以从各联号存放款进出的总帐中，看出存货有多少。


“Sir,” Mi said gravely, “there must now be six or seven thousand bales of silk in stock, and still more cocoons.
『大先生，』宓本常神情严肃地说∶『现在存丝总有六七千包，茧子更多，

I do not think we need so much.”
我看用不着这么多存货。』

“You mean too much capital is tied up?”
『你是说吃本太重？』

“Yes.
『是啊。』

At a rough estimate, nearly three million taels are immobilised there.
宓本常说∶『粗估一估差不多有三百万银子的本钱压在那里。

Otherwise I would be ashamed to press Mr Gu for his hundred thousand.”
不是因为这样子，古先生的十万银子，我也不好意思来讨。』

“Ah!” Hu answered at once. “Transfer those hundred thousand to my account.”
『呃！』胡雪岩立即接口∶『这十万银子转到我名下。』

He turned to Gu. “We shall find a way with the rest.
他紧接着又转脸对古应春说∶『另外的，再想办法。

You have the land, after all.
好在你有地皮在那里，

It is only a temporary shortage of cash.”
不过现金一时周转不开而已。』

Gu’s own anxiety vanished, but concern for Hu took its place.
古应春满怀忧虑一扫而空；但自己虽不愁了，又为胡雪岩发愁，

“Little Uncle, all three reeling mills are short of material.
『小爷叔，』他说∶『现在三家缫丝厂都缺货，

Why not release a few thousand bales of cocoons?
你何妨放几千包茧子出去；

The new machines work very quickly.
新式机器，做丝快得很，

Once the silk is made, there will be no shortage of foreign buyers.
一做出来，不愁外洋没有买主，

Would that not get things moving?”
那一来不就活络了？』

“Mr Gu is quite right,” Mi said.
『古先生这话一点不错。』宓本常也说，

“The export buyers have been inactive this year because the foreigners are waiting for machine-reeled silk.
『今年「洋庄」不大动，是外国人都在等，等机器的丝，

In fairness, it is far superior to what our old foot-driven and hand-turned methods produce.”
凭良心说，机器做的丝，比脚踏手摇土法子做的丝，不知道要高明多少。』

“I know,” Hu said quietly. “But a man must have principles, and above all he must keep his word.
『我也晓得。』胡雪岩用低沉的声音说∶『不过，做人总要讲宗旨，更要讲信用，说一句算一句，

I promised not to let modern mills steal the livelihood of country families who raise silkworms and reel silk.
我答应过的，不准新式缫丝厂来抢乡下养蚕做丝人家的饭碗，

I cannot sell them cocoons.
我就不能卖茧子给他们。

If I tighten my grip a little longer and those three mills collapse, the foreigners will have nothing to wait for.
现在我手里再紧一紧，这三家机器缫丝厂一倒，外国人没有想头了，

They will buy my silk, and then I shall name the price.”
自然会买我的丝，那时候价钱就由我开了。

Both Gu and Mi thought this wishful calculation.
『古应春与宓本常，都认为他打的如意算盘。

But Gu was a close friend, Mi an employee; only Gu could continue trying to persuade him.
不过，古应春是好朋友的身分，而宓本常是伙计，所以只有古应春还可以劝他。


“Little Uncle, if the three mills close, foreigners will naturally have to buy our traditionally reeled silk.
『小爷叔，如果那三家新式缫丝厂倒闭了洋商当然只好仍旧买我们土法子做的丝；

But what if they do not close?”
可是那三家厂不倒呢？』

“If they have no material, what difference does staying open make?”
『不倒而没有货色，跟倒了有啥两样？』

“There is something else to remember.
『还有一层，小爷叔要想到，

Dried cocoons still cannot be stored indefinitely.
茧子虽然烘干了，到底也还是摆不长的。

Once they yellow, they lose their value.”
一发黄就卖不起价钱了。』

“True.
『这话是不错。

But you say Shanghai has less than a million taels of silver.
不过，你说上海现银不到一百万，

Even if I release the cocoons, I cannot turn them into cash.”
我就放茧子出去，也换不出现银。』

“Sterling or American notes would do,” Mi put in quickly.
『有英镑、有花旗票就可以了。』宓本常接口来个快，

“For example, you owe HSBC five hundred thousand.
『譬如说，现在要还汇丰五十万，

If the foreign currency from selling cocoons were deposited there, it could be converted and credited against that sum.
如果大先生有卖茧子的外国钱在汇丰，就可以折算给他，

Bringing in five hundred thousand in silver would free up our funds, would it not?”
收进五十万现银，周转不就活络了？』

Hu considered. “If I must sell cocoons to preserve my credit, that is a reason I can stand behind.
胡雪岩沉吟了一会说∶『为了维持我的信用，只好抛茧子，这话我说得响的。

Tomorrow I shall see Shao Xiaocun and find out whether the full five hundred thousand can be collected.
明天我去看邵小村，看看这五十万两银子，到底收得齐收不齐？

If it falls short, I shall follow your proposal and sell cocoons to make up the difference.
如果银数不够，决定照你们的办法，卖茧子来拿它补足。

Otherwise, I have another idea.”
不然，我另有主意。』

“What idea?”
『小爷叔，你是啥主意？』

Hu smiled.
胡雪岩笑笑，

“Heaven’s secrets cannot be revealed.
『天机不可泄漏。』

It is a rather ruthless move.”
他说，『是蛮狠的一着。』


After dinner, Mi took his leave, but Gu stayed.
吃完了饭，宓本常告辞，古应春却留了下来，

Hu had only just arrived in Shanghai and had not yet appeared publicly.
因为胡雪岩刚到上海，尚未露面，

Once the news spread tomorrow, social obligations would leave no time for a long conversation.
到第二天消息一传，应酬就会忙不过来，那时候就没有工夫细谈了。

Hu too wanted to hear his recent news.
当然胡雪岩也要跟他谈谈近况，

His first concern was the Seventh Lady.
第一个关切的是七姑奶奶，

“How is Seventh Sister? Any better?”
『怎么样？』他问∶『七姐好点了？』

“Much better.”
『好得多了。』

Gu’s expression brightened.
古应春的神气不同了，显得很有生气的模样，

“Her whole right side was paralysed.
『本来右半身完全瘫了，

Now some feeling has returned.”
现在有点知觉了。』

“Good!
『那好！

Perhaps she may recover completely.”
说不定还会复原呢！』

This made Gu uneasy, and he had to speak frankly.
这一说，使得古应春很不安，只好老实说了，

“Little Uncle, something troubles me.
『小爷叔，我心里有个疙瘩，

Only a few days after Ruixiang entered our household, the news came that Gulin had fallen from his horse outside the British queen’s country palace and injured his brain.”
从瑞香一进门，没有几天就有消息。顾林在英国女皇的行宫外面，从马上摔下来，把脑子摔坏了。』

He hesitated. “I fear her birth signs may not agree with mine.”
他迟疑着说∶『我怕她跟我八字上不大相配。』

Hu strongly disagreed.
『！』胡雪岩大不以为然，

“You are so Western in your ways.
『你蛮洋派的人，

How can you believe that?
怎么也相信这个。

If necessary, take both sets of birth particulars to Wu Ironmouth and let him compare them.”
要不然，你拿你们两个人的八字，叫吴铁口去合一合看。』

Mention of Wu was enough to provoke a smile.
提到吴铁口，不免令人失笑；

When Madam Luosi had once consulted him about birth signs, it had been a performance secretly arranged with the Seventh Lady.
当初罗四姐去合八字，原是七姑奶奶跟他串好一的出双簧。

Hu knew the trick.
胡雪岩也知道其中的奥妙，

To believe Wu’s pronouncements genuinely infallible now would be deceiving himself and others.
竟真的相信吴铁口是真的铁口，岂非自欺欺人？

“What are you smiling at?” Hu asked. “Do you think me ridiculous?
『你笑点啥？』胡雪岩说∶『你当我荒唐？

Let me say this: when a falsehood is made true, and the truth really is true, that is an infallible Ironmouth.” Gu laughed at the tongue-twister.
实在说一句∶假的说成真的，「真的是真的，那就是不折不扣的铁口。』听他说得象绕口令似的，古应春不由得笑了，

“Very well.
『好，好！

I shall do as you say and ask him to compare her birth signs.
我听小爷叔的话，叫吴铁口去合她的八字，

But I do not know her birth particulars.”
不过，』他说∶『她的八字我不晓得。』

“I shall ask her.”
『我来问她。』

“Wait.
『慢慢，

We must first have some indication from A’qi.”
总要等阿七有了表示以后。』

“Of course.
『当然。』

Once I see Seventh Sister tomorrow, I guarantee good news that very day.”
胡雪岩说∶『我明天看了七姐，包你当天就有好消息。』


“What?
『怎么？』

Do you mean to speak to her directly?”
古应春问∶『小爷叔是打算当面她明说。』

“Face to face, yes; directly, no.
『当面是当面，不是明说。

You will see tomorrow.”
你到明天就晓得了。』

“Complete recovery is impossible.
『复原是办不到，

I only hope she can get out of bed,” Gu said. “For that, we really must thank Ruixiang.”
只望她能够起床就好了。』古应春又说∶『谈到这一点，实在要谢谢瑞香。』

“Exactly!”
『对了！』

Hu came to his second concern.
胡雪岩谈到他第二件关心的事，

“How does Seventh Sister treat her?”
『七姐对瑞香怎么样？』

“There is nothing to complain of.
『那没有话说，

Like her own younger sister.
当她自己妹子一样。

Of course, half of that is out of regard for Madam Luosi.”
当然这也一半是看罗四姐的面子。』

“Then we should follow the ingenious plan she gave us, step by step.
『照这样说，应该是照她的锦囊妙计，一步一步走拢来；

Has Seventh Sister said anything to you?”
七姐对你有没有表示？』

“Yes, but I did not answer.”
『有。不过我没有答腔。』

“Why?” Hu was astonished.
『咦！』胡雪岩大为诧异∶『为啥？』

“With my affairs in such a mess, Little Uncle, how could I think of taking a concubine?”
『小爷叔，你看我现在弄得这样焦头烂额，哪里还有讨小的意恳。』

“True enough.
『这倒也是实话。』

Leaving aside the hundred thousand at Fukang, how much more do you need?”
胡雪岩问∶『阜康的十成旭不必再提了，你还差多少头寸？』

Gu thought. “Another hundred and twenty or thirty thousand.”
应春想了一下答说∶『还差十二、三万。』

“What you lack is cash.
『差点是现款，

Anything you can turn into cash will serve.
能够变现就好。』

I shall lend you five hundred bales of silk.
胡雪岩说∶『我再借五百包丝给你，

With all your friends in foreign firms, you should be able to sell them.”
你洋行里的朋友多，总可以卖得掉。』

This was precisely what Gu had hoped for but had long hesitated to ask.
古应春打的正是这个主意，踌躇好久，难于启齿，

Now Hu had offered it himself.
不想胡雪岩自己说了出来；

Gu’s gratitude and relief were beyond description.
心里的那份感激与痛快，难以形容了。

“Little Uncle, as we say in Hangzhou, you are my life-saving King Bodhisattva.
『小爷叔，你真是杭州人说的，是我的「救命王菩萨」。』

I shall pledge all the title deeds to you.”
他说∶『我把道契都抵给你。』


“No need.
『不必，不必，

Between brothers, why trouble about that?
我们弟兄何在乎此？

But you must not ask too low a price.
不过应春，你开价不能太低，

Once a market quotation is established, I cannot raise mine later.”
不然，有个盘口在那里，以后我就抬不高了。』

“Yes.”
『是的。』

Gu thought carefully. “Then lend me another two hundred bales. I can certainly raise a hundred and forty thousand taels by pledging seven hundred bales.
古应春凝神想了一下说∶『这样，小爷叔，你索性再借两百包给我∶七面百丝抵押十四万银子，一定可以，

That will settle everything.”
那就什么都摆平了。』

“Good.
『好！

Pledging without selling will not depress the market price.
光押不卖，就不算把行情压低。

We shall do that.” Hu added immediately, “Now can you think about Ruixiang?”
准定如此。』胡雪岩紧接着说∶『你现在有心思想瑞香了吧？』

Even so, Gu could not answer freely.
这一点，古应春还是不能爽爽快快地答复；

As he hesitated, Hu pressed him.
沉吟未答之际，胡雪岩少不得要追问了。

“My mother is concerned about this, and Madam Luosi even more so.
『这件事老太太都蛮关心的。罗四姐更不用说，

Remember, Yingchun, it is not only you.

She owes Ruixiang an answer too.”

Early the next morning, Hu went to the Gu household.
第二天一大早，胡雪岩就到了古家。

The Seventh Lady knew he was coming and had Ruixiang comb her hair and help her dress.
七姑奶奶已知道胡雪岩要来，叫瑞香替她栉发梳妆；

She also ordered food prepared so that he could stay for a meal, and was much excited.
又关照预备菜留胡雪岩吃饭，大为兴奋。


Hu was naturally shown straight to her sickbed.
胡雪岩一来，当然请到病榻前面，

“Seventh Sister,” he said gladly, “you do look much stronger.”
『七姐，』他很高兴地说，『看起来精神是好得多了。』

“Yes.
『是啊，

It is all thanks to Fourth Sister.”
都要谢谢四姐。』

“Why?”
『为啥？』

“If she had not sent Ruixiang to help me, I would never have improved.
『不是四姐派了瑞香来帮我的忙，我不会好起来，

Look, Little Uncle!”
小爷叔你看！』

七姑奶奶将右手提高了数寸，

“I can move it now.

Ruixiang massages it so many times every day.”

“Oh!”
『喔！』

Hu glanced at Ruixiang as though about to speak, then stopped, suggesting something difficult to say.
胡雪岩看一看瑞香，想要说话，却又住口，仿佛有难言之隐似的。

七姑奶奶虽在病中，仍旧神智清明，察言辨色的本事一点也不差；

殷殷地从胡老太太起，将胡雪岩全家大小都问到了。

“Yes.
『是的。

“Madam Luosi asked me to bring this message.

『赎回去？』

The Seventh Lady’s expression changed.
七姑奶奶脸色都变了，

“No.
『不是。

Madam Luosi was uncertain, and I could not remember.

七姑奶奶用殷切盼望的眼色看着胡雪岩说∶『她晓得我离不开瑞香，
