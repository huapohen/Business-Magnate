胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement
第六章（本章第1部分，共2部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


CHAPTER SIX
第六章


HSBC’s comprador, Zeng Yousheng, was a snob who liked to bolster his own importance with foreign power.
汇丰银行的买办曾友生，为人很势利，喜欢借洋人的势力以自重。

His attentiveness to Hu arose chiefly from the fact that Hu could walk straight into the bank manager’s office and deal with him directly, whether or not they had met before.
他对胡雪岩很巴结，主要的原因是，胡雪岩跟汇丰银行的『大班』，不论以前是否认识，都可以排闼直入去打交道，

Zeng dared not withhold respect; Hu, however, disliked precisely this snobbery.
所以他不敢不尊敬；但胡雪岩却不大喜欢这个人，就因为他势利之故。

This time Zeng had come on his manager’s instructions.
但这回他是奉了他们大班之命，

The bank had just received five hundred thousand taels in ready silver and needed to put it to work.
来跟胡雪岩商量，刚收到五十万现银，需要『消化』，

Would Hu care to borrow it?
问胡雪岩可有意借用？

“Money is very tight in the market.
『现在市面上头寸很紧，

You could lend it to someone else.
你们这笔款子可以借给别人，

Why ask a banker like me?”
何必来问我这个做钱庄的？』

“Money is tight, certainly, but the situation is unfavourable.
『市面上头寸确是很紧，不过局势不大好；

We must choose our borrowers carefully.
客户要挑一挑。

For credit, your name stands above all others.”
论到信用，你胡大先生是天字第一号的金字招牌。』

Zeng smiled ingratiatingly. “Such an opportunity seldom comes, Mr Hu.
曾友生陪着笑说∶『胡大先生，难得有这么一个机会，

Please give me a helping hand.”
请你挑挑我。』

“You flatter me, Brother Yousheng.
『友生兄，你言重了。

HSBC’s comprador is the one who helps others along.
汇丰的买办，只有挑人家的，

Who is qualified to help you?”
哪个够资格来挑你？』

“You are.
『你胡大先生就够。』

There is no pretending with a man like you.
曾友生说∶『真人面前不说假话，

HSBC dares lend this money to no one else.
除了你，汇丰的款子不敢放给别人，

Only you can help me.”
所以只有你能挑我。』

“Since you put it that way, I shall do whatever a friend can, so far as it lies within my power.
『既然你这么说，做朋友能够帮忙的，只要我办得到，无不如命。

But I do not understand what help you want.”
不过，我不晓得怎么挑法？』


“Only permission to add a little to the interest for myself,” Zeng said frankly. “Could you use the whole five hundred thousand?”
『无非在利息上头，让我稍稍戴顶帽子。』曾友生开门见山地说∶『胡大先生，这五十万你都用了好不好？』

“You fear risks, and so do I.”
『你们怕风险，我也怕风险。』

Hu deliberately turned to Gu. “Minister Wang insists on depositing two hundred thousand taels with us.
胡雪岩故意问古应春∶『正中堂有二十万银子，一定要摆在我们这里，

Can we turn him down?”
能不能回掉他？』

Gu had no idea who this “Minister Wang” was, but understood the intention: Hu wanted to suggest that Fukang had ample funds.
古应春根本不知道他说的『王中堂』是谁？不过他懂胡雪岩的意思，是要表示阜康的头寸很宽裕，

He therefore feigned puzzlement. “Did you not see yesterday’s telegram from Beijing?”
便也故意装困惑地问∶『呀！小爷叔，昨天北京来的电报，你没看到？』

“No.
『没有啊！

What did it say?”
电报上怎么说？』

“Minister Wang’s two hundred thousand has already been deposited: half in Beijing and half in Tianjin.
『王中堂的二十万银子，一半在北京，一半在天津，都存进来了。』

Has Mi not told you?”
古应春又加一句∶『莫非老宓没有告诉你？』

“He has been terribly busy today.
『老宓今天忙得不得了，

He must have forgotten.”
大概忘掉了。』

Hu looked at Zeng. “The silk-buying season is almost over too.
胡雪岩脸看着曾友生说∶『收丝的辰光差不多也过了，

This is rather difficult.”
实在有点为难。』

“With your resources, a few hundred thousand more would scarcely matter.
『胡大先生，以你的实力，手里多个几十万头寸，也不算回事；

Shanghai is full of rumours, but business inland is sound.
上海谣言多，内地市面不坏。

Besides, the lean fifth and sixth months, between the old harvest and the new, are approaching.
加上五荒六月，青黄不接的时候，

If Fukang has funds, it need never fear being unable to lend them.
阜康有款子，不怕放不出去，

Think it over.
你们再多想一想看。

Taking this money can only help you.”
吃进这笔头寸，只有好处，没有坏处。』

Hu nodded, paused, and asked the rate.
胡雪岩点点头停了一下问道∶『利息多少？』


“A round one,” Zeng said. “But I shall report only point eight-five to the bank.
『一个整数。』曾友生说∶『不过我报只报八五。

That is fair enough, surely?”
胡大先生，这算蛮公道吧？』

“Annual or monthly?”
『年息还是月息？』

“Monthly, of course.”
『自然是月息。』

“One per cent a month means twelve per cent a year.
『月息一厘，年息就是一分二。

There is nothing fair about that.”
这个数目，一点都不公道。』

“You cannot compare present conditions with the old days.
『现在的银根，胡大先生，你不能拿从前来比，

Besides, other lenders deduct something at the outset.
而且分家借有扣头，

You will receive this sum in full.”
不比这笔款子你是实收。』

Hu did not readily believe him, but, considered fairly, the loan could hardly be called unattractive.
胡雪岩当然不会轻信他的话，但平心而论，这笔借款实在不能说不划算，

They bargained back and forth and finally agreed upon ten per cent a year, payable half-yearly, for a two-year term with an option to extend for another year.
所以彼此磋磨，最后说定年息一分，半年一付；期限两年，到期得展延一年。

Hu did not ask what additional margin Zeng would arrange in his account with HSBC; he would simply acknowledge the figures Zeng presented.
至于对汇丰银行，曾友生要戴多少帽子，胡雪岩不问，只照曾友生所开的数目承认就是。


Hu had already intended to borrow this money, just as HSBC had intended to lend it to him.
胡雪岩原来就已想到要借汇丰这笔款子，而汇丰亦有意贷放给胡雪岩。

Their wishes coincided.
彼此心思相同，

His handsome gesture of repaying early instead of pursuing a small interest gain had strengthened the manager’s conviction that he was a first-class customer.
加以有胡雪岩不贪小利、提前归还这很漂亮的一着，汇丰的大班，愈发觉得胡雪岩确是第一等的客户，

Zeng therefore arranged the loan without difficulty.
所以曾友生毫不困难地将这笔货款拉成功了，

Six months’ interest was deducted in advance.
利息先扣半年，

Once the proceeds had been transferred to Fukang, Hu personally drew a draft for Zeng’s benefit and had Gu deliver it.
曾友生的好处，等款子划拨到阜康，胡雪岩自己打一张票子，由古应春转交曾友生，

Even Mi knew nothing of this private side of the transaction.
连宓本常都不知道这笔借款另有暗盘。

News travelled quickly among bankers.
司行中的消息很灵通，

At the senior bankers’ gathering in Yuyuan at the City God Temple the next morning, Mi’s table was unusually crowded with men hoping to borrow silver.
第二天上午城隍庙豫园的『大同行』茶会上，宓本常那张桌子上，热闹非凡，都是想来拆借现银的。

But he kept a tight hold on it.
但宓本常的手很紧，

Hu had instructed that, apart from covering Gu’s outstanding bad debts, the money was reserved for another purpose.
因为胡雪岩交代，这笔款子除了弥补古应春的宕帐以外，余款他另有用途。

“Business depends on recognizing opportunities,” Hu said. “A poor market can be an opportunity too.
『做生意看机会。』他说∶『市面不好，也是个机会；

It requires judgment: judge correctly and you make a fortune; judge wrongly and you lose your capital.
当然，这要看眼光，看准了赚大钱，看走眼了血本无归。

With money tight, everyone is selling goods for cash.
现在银根紧，都在脱货求现，

How do you think we should use these funds?”
你们看这笔款子应该怎么用？』

Gu favoured stocking up on tea; Mi suggested land.
古应春主张囤茶叶，宓本常提议买地皮，

Hu rejected both for one reason: either investment would depend on subsequent developments and could not produce an immediate effect.
但胡雪岩都不赞成，唯一的原因是，茶叶也好，地皮也好，投资下去要看局势的演变，不能成上发生作用。


“In an unfavourable situation, nothing will produce an effect,” Mi argued.
『大先生，』宓本常说∶『局势不好，什么作用都不会发生，

“Short-term lending seems best.”
我看还是放拆息最好。』

“We shall not discuss that.
『放拆息不必谈；

We did not open a bank merely to profit from other bankers.
我们开钱庄，本意就不是想赚同行的钱，

As for producing an effect, circumstances matter, but strength matters more.
至于要发生作用，局势固然有关系，主要的是看力量，

If you already have enough, a small addition will make the difference.”
力量够，稍为再加一点，就有作用发生。』

Hu took three teacups and filled one. “These two are only half full.
胡雪岩随手取过三只茶杯，斟满其中的一杯说∶『这两只杯子里的茶只有一半，

They are like tea and land: still a long way from the brim.
那就好比茶叶同地皮，离满的程度还远得很；

Add more to this full cup, however, and it overflows at once.
这满的一杯，只要倒茶下去，马上就会到外面，

That is what happens when your existing strength is sufficient.”
这就是你力量够了，马上能够发生作用。』

Gu understood.
古应春颇有领会了，

“Four ounces moving a thousand jin.
『这是四两拨千斤的道理。』

But you have more than one full cup, Little Uncle.
他说∶『小爷叔，你的满杯茶，不止一杯，

Which do you intend to make overflow?”
你要哪一杯发生作用？』

“What do you think?”
『你倒想呢？』

“Silk?”
『丝？』

“Exactly.”
『不错。』


Gu strongly disagreed.
古应春大不以为然。

Hu already held enormous stocks of silk.
因为胡雪岩囤积的丝很多，

The foreign market was poor that year, and overseas buyers offered low prices.
而这年的『洋庄』并不景气；洋人收丝，出价不高，

Hu refused to sell, tying up much ready silver; there was no reason to invest still more.
胡雪岩不愿脱手，积压的现银已多，没有再投入资金之理。

“No, Yingchun.
『不！应春。』

Their prices are low because they have miscalculated.
胡雪岩说∶『出价不高，是洋人打错了算盘，

They think I want to sell for cash and mean to buy cheaply.
以为我想脱货求现，打算买便宜货，

There is still silk available elsewhere, so they are in no hurry.
而且，市面上也还有货，所以他们还不急。

What about me?
我呢！

Do you think I am in a hurry?”
你们说我急不急？』

The sudden question left Gu and Mi at a loss.
忽然看出这么一句话来，古应春与宓本常都不知如何回答了。

“Well?
『你们倒说说看，

Why do you say nothing?”
怎么不开口。』

“I do not know about you,” Mi said, “but I am certainly anxious.”
『我不晓得大先生怎么样』宓本常说∶『不过我是很急。』

“So am I.
『你急我也急。

How could I not be?
我何尝不急，

But showing anxiety only makes matters worse.
不过愈急愈坏事；

Once people know you are desperate, they wait to take advantage of you.
人家晓得你急，就等着要你的好看了。

With the HSBC loan, I said Minister Wang had deposited a large sum and we had plenty of money.
譬如汇丰的那笔款子，我要说王中堂有大批钱存进来，头寸宽裕得很，

That made Zeng all the more eager to lend, and gave us an advantage over the rate.
曾友生就愈要借给你，利息也讨俏了；

Had I let slip how much I wanted it, you would have seen quite another face.”
只要你一露口风，很想借这笔钱，那时候你们看着，他又是一副脸嘴了。』

“I do not think the principle applies in every case,” Gu said.
『这似乎不可以一概而论。』

He felt Hu’s calculations were wrong but could not find where to begin his objection.
古应春总觉得他的盘算不对，但却不知从何驳起。


“You say we cannot generalize.
『你说不可一概而论，

I say the principle is the same.
我说道理是一样的。

While prices are low, I shall buy up all the silk on the market.
现在我趁市价落的时候，把市面上的丝收光，

Unable to find supplies, the foreigners will naturally return to me.”
洋人买不到丝，自然会回头来寻我。』

“Suppose both sides hold out: one refuses to sell because the price is too low, the other refuses to buy because it is too high.
『万一倒是大家都僵在那里，一个价钱不好不卖；一个价钱太贵，不买。

You must remember, Little Uncle, that you will suffer, not they.”
小爷叔，那时候，你要想想，吃亏的是你，不是他。』

“Why should I?”
『怎么吃亏的是我？』

“Does silk not turn yellow?”
『丝不要发黄吗？』

“Certainly.
『不错，

But it only turns yellow.
丝要发黄。不过也仅止于发黄而已，

Bleaching will take some trouble; it will not become so useless that we must throw it into the sea.
漂白费点事，总不致于一无用处，要掼到汪洋大海。』

If we hold out against each other, I am on my own ground and can find some way through.
胡雪岩又说∶『大家拼下去，我到底是地主，总有办法好想；

The foreigners who come for supplies cannot return empty-handed.
来收货的洋人，一双空手回去，

Without raw materials, their mills must close.
没有原料，他厂要关门。

I do not believe they can outlast me.
我不相信他拼得过我。

If they really unite to force my price down, I still have one last inspired move to turn defeat into survival.”
万一他们真是齐了心杀我的价，我还有最后一记死中求活的仙着。』

Both men wanted to hear it.
大家都想听他说明那死中求活的一着是什么？

Hu merely smiled and said no more, as though the remark had been an offhand evasion intended to conceal a weakness.
但胡雪岩装作只是信口掩饰短处的一句『游词』，笑笑不再说下去了。


Alone with Gu, however, his manner changed.
可是当他只与古应春两个人在一起时，态度便不同了，

“I have considered your objections, Yingchun.”
『应春，你讲的道理我不是没有想过。』

He seemed agitated.
他显得有些激动，

“Abroad, especially in Britain, merchants rank among the foremost people.
『人家外国人，特别是英国，做生意是第一等人。

Here we have scholars, farmers, artisans, and merchants, and merchants are called the lowest of the four classes.
我们这里呢，士农工商，做生意的，叫啥「四民之末」；

Now it is better still: ‘Every merchant is a rogue.’
现在更加好了，叫做「无商不奸」。

If I had not understood how official life works, I should never have reached my present position.
我如果不是懂做官的诀窍，不会有今天。

Is that not the truth?”
你说，我是不是老实话？』

“Not necessarily.
『不见得。』

In business alone, you would still be a first-rate man.”
古应春答说∶『小爷叔光讲做生意，一定也是第一流人物。』

“First-rate among merchants, you mean—not among the four classes.
『你说的第一流，不过是做生意当中的第一流，不是「四民」当中的第一流。

Do not lose your head, Yingchun, and imagine your business ability is so remarkable.
应春，你不要「晕淘淘」，真的当你做生意的本事有多少大！

However great it is, it cannot surpass the foreigners’, especially the British.
我跟你说一句，再大也大不过外国人，尤其是英国人。

Why?
为啥？

Because a whole country is doing business against you.
他是一个国家在同你做生意；

Take a foreign loan.
好比借洋款，

Once the terms are agreed, the British minister intervenes and requires an official undertaking from the Zongli Yamen.
一切都谈好了；英国公使出面了，要总理衙门出公事，

Failing to repay a British merchant becomes the same as failing to repay the queen.
你欠英商的钱不还，就等于欠英国女皇的钱不还。

Try it!
真的不还，你试试看，

Their gentler method is to hold the customs in their hands.
软的，海关捏在人家手里；

Their harsher method is to bring warships off your port and aim their guns at the busiest parts of your city.
硬的，他的兵舰开到你口子外头，大炮瞄准你城里热闹的地方。

It is a debt to the King of Hell.
应春，这同「阎王帐」一样，

Dare you refuse?
你敢不还？

They will take your life!”
不还要你的命！』

Hu normally spoke calmly; Gu had never heard such sharpness.
胡雪岩说话的语气一向平和，从未见他如此锋利过。

He dared neither agree nor contradict, since either response would only drive Hu further.
因此，古应春不敢附和；但也不敢反驳，因为不管附和还是反驳，都只会使得他更为偏激。


Hu paid no attention to the reason for his silence.
胡雪岩却根本不理会他因何沉默，

The words had reached his lips and would not be held back.
只觉得『话到口边留不住』，要说个痛快，

“When Mr Wu described the British government’s opium trade, I had much to reflect upon.
『那天我听吴秀才谈英国政府卖鸦片，心里头感慨不少。

Outwardly it is the East India Company that grows the opium; in reality, the government directs everything.
表面上看起来，种鸦片的，都是东印度公司，其实是英国政府在操纵，

The slightest injury to the company’s interests brings government intervention.
只要对东印度公司稍为有点不利，英国政府就要出面来交涉了。

Its profits go to the government, so that is understandable.
东印度公司的盈余，要归英国政府，这也还罢了。

But silk?
然而，丝呢？

British merchants trade in it entirely on their own account.
完全是英国商人自己在做生意，

Their profits and losses have nothing to do with the government.
盈亏同英国政府毫不相干；

Yet it still intervenes, claiming that our cocoon levies are too high and increase British buyers’ costs, and demanding reductions.
居然也要出面来干预，说你们收的茧捐太高了，英商收丝的成本加重，所以要减低。

Their government speaks for its merchants at every turn.
人家的政府，处处帮商人讲话；

What about ours, Yingchun?”
我们呢？应春，你说！』

“Must I say it?” Gu replied with a bitter smile.
『这还用得着我说？』古应春苦笑着回答。

“As the saying goes, you need not know good goods from bad if you can compare them.
『俗语说∶不怕不识货，只怕货比货。

Governments are the same.
政府也是一样的。

Some say our Qing dynasty is much better than the Ming.
有的人说，我们大清朝比明朝要好得多，

With the absurd conduct of the emperors and eunuchs at the end of the Ming, there would have been no justice in Heaven had the dynasty survived.
照明朝末年，皇帝、太监那种荒唐法子，明朝不亡变成没有天理了。

But you must compare three shops, not two.
但是，货要比三家，所谓货比三家不吃亏，

Granted, the Qing is better than the Ming; we must compare it with other countries as well.
大清朝比明朝高明，固然不错；还要比别的国家，

That is the third shop.
这就是比第三家。

Which can it match?
你说，比得上哪一国，

Not Britain, France, America, or Germany—not even Japan, in my view.”
不但英法美德，照我看比日本都不如。』

“You are straying from the subject, Little Uncle.”
『小爷叔，』古应春插嘴说道∶『你的话扯得远了。』

“Then back to business.
『好！我们回来再谈生意。

I know where the court has helped me.
我，胡某人有今天，朝廷帮我的忙的地方，我晓得；

Official deposits at low interest give my bank an advantage.
象钱庄，有利息轻的官款存进来，就是我比人家有利的地方。

But I earned that by helping the court.
不过，这是我帮朝廷的忙所换来的；

It favours Hu the red-buttoned official who has rendered service, not Hu the great merchant.
朝廷是照应你出了力、戴红顶子的胡某人，不是照应你做大生意的胡某人，

There is a distinction.
这中间是有分别的。

Do you agree?”
你说是不是？』

“Your argument is rather profound today.”
『爷叔，你今天发的议论太深奥了。』

Gu rubbed his temples. “Let me think.”
古应春用拇指揉着太阳穴∶『等我想一想。』

“Do.
『对！

We cannot proceed until you understand.”
你要想通了，我们才谈得下去。』


Gu carefully considered the distinction, then asked, “Do you mean the court should support large-scale business?”
古应春细细分辨了两者之间的区别。以后问道∶『小爷叔的意思是，朝廷应该照应做大生意的？』

“Yes—business that competes with foreigners.
『不错。』胡雪岩说∶『不过，我是指的同外国人一较高下的大生意而言。

It should support everyone selling to the foreign market.
凡是销洋庄的，朝廷都应该照应；

That is warfare with foreigners, though without swords and guns.”
因为这就是同外国人「打仗」，不过不是用真刀真枪而已。』

“Indeed.
『是，是。

胡雪岩始终不愿谈个打算，事实上他也从没有认真去想过，

此时却不能不谈不想了。

此言一出，古应春竟有些不相信自己的耳朵了。

Hu had always opposed modern mills.
胡雪岩一向不赞成新式缫丝厂，

现在的做法完全相反，实在不可思议。

然而稍为多想一想，就觉得这一着实在很高明。

He had always differed from Hu on this question.
古应春在这方面跟胡雪岩的态度一直不同，

Knowing foreign languages and dealing frequently with foreigners, he understood Western developments better.
他懂洋文跟洋人打交道的辰光也多，对西方潮流比较清楚，

Traditional reeling was costly and produced inferior silk; it should long ago have been superseded.
土法做丝，成本既高、品质又差，老早该淘汰了。

Hu’s concern for rural silk households had made him resist modern methods.
只因为胡雪岩一直顾虑乡下丝户的生计，一直排斥新式缫丝，

现在难得他改变想法，不但反对，而且更进一步，自己要下手做，

Gu was both astonished and delighted.
怎不教人既惊且喜。

『小爷叔，就是洋人不跟你打对台，你也应该这样做的。

你倒想——』古应春很起劲地为胡雪岩指陈必须改弦易辙的理由，

First, modern machinery worked several times faster than hand- and foot-operated equipment.
第一是新式缫丝机器，比手摇脚踏的『土机器』要快好几倍，

Cocoons could be bought in any quantity and sent straight to the mills.
茧子不妨尽量收，收了马上运到厂里做成丝，

No warehouses would be needed for dried cocoons, nor would there be fear of living pupae emerging through them.
既不用堆栈来存放干茧，更不怕茧中之蛹未死，咬出头来；

Second, the finished silk’s evenness, cleanliness, and lustre far surpassed the traditional product. Third, buying the cocoons, reeling the silk, and selling it abroad oneself would place the entire operation under one direction.
第二，出口的匀净、光泽远胜于土法所制『第三，自己收茧，自己做丝，自己销洋庄，竞』一条鞭『到底，

Foreign competition need no longer be feared; indeed, foreigners would have no means of competing.
不必怕洋人来竞争，事实上洋人也无法来竟争。

These arguments, especially the last, moved Hu considerably.
这三点理由，尤其是最后一点，颇使胡雪岩动心；

Still unable to decide, he answered only, “We shall see.
但一时也委决不下，只这样答一句∶『再看吧！

There is no great urgency.”
这不是很急的事。』


Gu thought otherwise.
但古应春的想法不同，

Action should begin at once.
他认为这件事应该马上进行。

Hu held large stocks of dried cocoons.
胡雪岩手里有大批干茧，

If he had them reeled by traditional methods and could not agree on a price with foreign buyers, the silk would sit in warehouses and turn yellow.
如果用土法做成丝，跟洋人价钱谈不拢，摆在堆栈里，丝会发黄；

With mills of his own and direct foreign sales, Gu believed, there would be little risk.
如果自己有厂做丝直接外销，就不会有什么风险了。

He therefore actively investigated the modern mills.
因此，他积极奔走，去打听新式缫丝厂的情形，

There were five.
共有五家，

The earliest, Baochang, had been founded by a Frenchman named Brunat.
最早是法国人卜鲁纳开设的宝昌丝厂，

Next came the Qichang mill, attached to the American firm of Russell & Company.
其次是美商旗昌洋行附设的旗昌丝厂。

The third, Gongheyong, had opened only the previous year under Huang Zuoqing of Huzhou.
第三家去年才开，名为公和永，老板是湖州人黄佐卿。

Jardine’s and Gongping Trading House also operated mills, as Russell’s did.
此外怡和、公平两家洋行，跟旗昌洋行一样，也都附设了丝厂。

All five were of roughly similar size and all were unprofitable, for two reasons. Supplies of dried cocoons were unreliable, frequently leaving machinery idle.
这五家丝厂，规模都差不多，也都不赚钱，原因有二∶第一，是干茧的来路不畅，机器常常停工待料。

Nor was the machinery’s capacity fully realized, and the finished silk fell short of expectations.
第二，机器的效用不能充分发挥，成品不如理想之好。

It was said that Gongheyong, Jardine’s, and Gongping intended jointly to engage a renowned Italian technician to supervise operations.
据说，公和永、怡和、公平三家打算联合聘请一名意大利有名的技师来管工程。

The other two seemed ready to abandon the business.
其余两家，已有无意经营之势，

If Hu wished to buy, this was the opportunity.
如果胡雪岩想收买，正是机会。


Gu was enthusiastic.
古应春对这件事非常热中，

He first consulted the Seventh Lady about how best to put the matter to Hu.
先跟七姑奶奶商量，看应该如何向胡雪岩进言。

“I do not know much about modern reeling mills,” she said, “but even an outsider can see that machine-reeled silk is better than the traditional kind.”
『新式缫丝厂的情形，我不在清楚，不过洋丝比土丝好，那是外行都看得出来的。』
