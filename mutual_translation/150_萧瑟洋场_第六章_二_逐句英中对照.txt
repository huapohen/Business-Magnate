“Good products will find buyers,” Gu said. “Little Uncle insists on the best in every business.
『东西好就不怕没有销路。』古应春说∶『小爷叔做什么生意，都要最好的；

现在明明的最好的东西在那里，他偏不要，

The Seventh Lady considered. “I shall speak to him.”
七姑奶奶想了一下说∶『我来跟他说。』

“It is not that I do not want it, Sister Seven,” Hu explained when she did.
『七姐，不是我不要。

“I know machine-reeled silk is at least two grades better.
我也知道洋丝比起土丝来起码要高两档。

不过，七姐，做人总要讲定旨、进信用，

I have always opposed modern reeling.
我一向不赞成新式缫丝，

If I now undertake it myself, will I not be an unprincipled turncoat?
现在反过来自己下手，那不是反复小人？

What could I say when challenged?”
人家要问我，我有啥话好说。』

“Times change.
『小爷叔，所谓此一时也，彼一时也，

The world changes every day.
世界天天在变。

I grew up in Shanghai: how could I ever have imagined the city as it is now?
我是从小生长在上海的，哪里会想到现在的上海会变成这个样子？

Everything advances in the West.
人家西洋，样样进步；

Refuse the benefits, and you suffer.
你不领益，自己吃亏。

Could His Excellency Zuo have succeeded in the west without the foreign weapons you bought him?”
譬如说，左大人西征，不是你替他买西洋的军火，他哪里会成功？』

“You misunderstand.
『七姐，你误会了，

I do not say the silk is inferior—” “I know, and I have not misunderstood.
我不是说洋丝不好——』『我知道，我也没有误会。』

My point is that one must recognize the direction of events.
七姑奶奶抢着说∶『我的意思是，人要识潮流，

Fall behind, and by the time you try to catch up it is too late.
不识潮流，落在人家后面，等你想到要赶上去，已经来不及。

You have helped Yingchun so greatly; our concern for you is entirely sincere.”
小爷叔，承你帮应春这么一个忙，我们夫妇是一片至诚。』

“Sister Seven!” Hu interrupted hastily.
『七姐，七姐，』胡雪岩急忙打断，

“To speak like that makes our friendship sound shallow.”
『你说这种话，就显得我们交情浅了。』


“Very well.
『好！我不说。

But I truly am anxious for you.
不过，小爷叔，我真是替你担足心思。』

The situation is bad.
七姑奶奶说∶『现在局势不好，

I hear the French intend to station warships off Wusong and stop merchant vessels passing.
听说法国人预备拿兵舰拦在吴淞口外，不准商船通行，

If the export trade stops, what becomes of the cocoons and silk in which you have invested several million taels?”
那一来洋庄不动，小爷叔，你垫本几百万银子的茧子跟丝，怎么办？』

“Where did you hear that?”
『这，这消息，你是从哪里来的？』

“From the foreign doctor treating me.”
『是替我看病的洋大夫说的。』

“Really?”
『真的？』

“When have I ever lied to you?”
『我几时同小爷叔说过假话？』

“Of course—my mistake,” Hu apologized hastily.
『喔，喔，』胡雪岩急忙道歉，『七姐，我说错了。』

“There are times to take risks and times to be cautious.
『小爷叔，人，有的时候要冒险，有的时候要稳当，

Forgive a coarse saying used by men about town: ‘Once you possess even a ragged quilt, you must protect what you have.’
小爷叔，我说句很难听的话，白相人说的「有床破棉被，就要保身家」。

How much have you to protect now, Little Uncle?”
小爷叔，你现在啥身家？』


After a long silence, Hu sighed. “Do you think I do not know?
胡雪岩默然半晌，叹口气说∶『七姐，我何尝不晓得？

But sometimes the choice is not mine.”
不过，有的时候，由不得自己。』

“I do not believe that.
『我不相信。』

You built this business yourself.
七姑奶奶说∶『事业是你一手闯出来的，

No one else can decide for you.”
哪个也做不得你的主。』

“You do not quite understand.
『七姐，这你就不大清楚了，

There are many unseen restraints.
无形之中有许多牵制，

If I start operating modern mills, people will come begging: ‘Instead of helping us, Mr Hu, you kick us from behind.
譬如说，我要一做新式缫丝厂，就有多少人来央求我，说「你胡大先生不拉我们一把，反而背后踢一脚，

We silk-reeling families will have nothing to eat.’
我们做丝的人家，没饭吃了。」

Then I cannot harden my heart.”
这一来，你的心就狠不下来了。』

The Seventh Lady had not expected him to forestall her argument so effectively.
七姑奶奶没有料到，他的话会说在前头，等于先发制人，将她的嘴封住了。

But she would not give up; she must find another way to persuade him.
当然，七姑奶奶决不会就此罢休，另外要想话来说服他。

“That is like climbing into a well to rescue someone.
『小爷叔，照你的说法，好比从井救人。

Is it worth risking yourself?
你犯得着，犯不着？

Modern reeling is the way things are moving.
再说新式缫丝是潮流，

At present its silk goes only to the foreign market.
现在光是销洋庄；

As mills multiply, everyone will prefer cloth woven from machine-reeled silk, and no one will want the traditional product.
将来厂多了，大家都喜欢洋机丝织的料子，土法做丝，根本就没人要；

Look at cotton cloth.
只看布好了，

Foreign cloth is fine, white, and light.
洋布又细又白又薄，

Who does not want a shirt of it in summer?
到夏天哪个不想弄件洋布衫穿？

Only country people still wear coarse blue homespun, and in a few years even they will stop.”
毛蓝布只有乡下人穿，再过几年乡下人都不穿了。』

“You cannot apply one rule to everything.”
『这不可以一概而论的。』

“Why not?
『为啥不可以，

The principle is the same.
事情是一样的。』

With a rescue from a well, your own risk is one question; whether the person can usefully be saved is another.
七姑奶奶接着又说∶『从井救人看自己犯得着、犯不着是一桩事；值得不值得救，又是一桩事。

If a healthy, vigorous man drowns, that is a pity.
如果鲜龙活跳一个人，掉在井里淹死了，自然可惜；

But if he is a consumptive, skin and bones, he will have few years left even if rescued.
倘或是个骨瘦如柴的痨病鬼，就救了起来，也没有几年好活，

Frankly, it makes little difference.
老实说，救不救是一样的，

Traditional silk-reeling is like that dying consumptive.”
现在土法做丝，就好比是个去日无多的痨病鬼。』

There seemed something in her comparison.
她这个譬方，似乎也有点道理，

Hu decided that argument alone was useless; he had better confine himself to his own difficulty.
胡雪岩心想，光跟她讲理，没有用处，只说自己的难处好了。


“A man cannot be double-faced, playing both the spirit-medium and the ghost.
『七姐，实在是做人不能「两面三刀」，「又做师娘又做鬼」。

If I behaved like that, I should certainly lose all I have.”
你说，如果我胡某人是这样一个人，身家一定保不住。』

Unable to refute him, the Seventh Lady turned the problem anxiously over in her mind.
七姑奶奶驳不倒他∶心里七上八下转着念头，

Suddenly she had an inspiration.
突然灵机一动，

“Then you do want modern mills, but those restraints prevent you.
便即问道∶『小爷叔，照你刚才的话，你不是不想做新式缫丝厂，是有牵制，不能做，

Is that right?”
是不是？』

“Yes.”
『是的。』

“Without the restraints, you could proceed?”
『那么牵制没有了，你就能做，是不是。』

“You could put it that way.”
『也可以这么说。』

“Then I can guarantee a way to remove them.”
『那好，我有一个法子，包你没有牵制。』

“Let me hear it.”
『你倒说说看。』

“Simple.
『很容易，

Keep your own name out of it.”
小爷叔，你不要出面好了。』

“You mean… be the owner behind the scenes?”
『是┅┅』胡雪岩问∶『是暗底下做老板？』

“Exactly.”
『对！』


Hu was tempted, but the matter was too important to decide without thought.
胡雪岩心有点动了，但兹事体大，必须好好想一想，

Seeing the possibility of a change, the Seventh Lady knew she must keep pressing.
见此光景，七姑奶奶知道事情有转机了，松不得劲，当即又想了一番话说。

“The situation can deteriorate very quickly.
『小爷叔，局势要坏起来是蛮快的，

If you do not prepare now, there may be no remedy when you discover the danger.
现在不趁早想办法，等临时发觉不妙，就来不及补救了。

Several million taels is no small sum.
几百万银子，不是小数目；

Even the God of Wealth could scarcely bear that risk.”
小爷叔，就算你是「财神」，只怕也背不起这个风险。』

Hu could not disregard this.
这话自然是不能当为耳旁风的；

“Who would undertake it?”
胡雪岩不由得问了一句∶『叫哪个来做呢？』

Once they were discussing a representative, the problem became easier.
要谈到委托一个出面的人，事情就好办了，

“I should like Madam Luosi to come,” she said.
七姑奶奶说∶『我在想，最好请罗四姐来；

“My body is paralysed, but my mind is sound.
我的身子风瘫了，脑子没有坏，

I could help her think things through.”
也可以帮她出出主意。』

“And who would manage the household?
『她一来，一家人怎么办？』

It might work if you could get about.”
胡雪岩说∶『除非七姐你能起床，还差不多。』

“I certainly cannot.
『我是决不行的。

Perhaps…” She hesitated.
要么┅┅』她沉吟着。

“Yingchun?
『你是说应春？』

Everyone knows his connection with me.
不过应春同我的关系，大家都晓得的，

His appearance would be much the same as mine.
他出面同我自己出面差不多。

Such self-deception would hardly do.”
这种掩耳盗铃的做法，不大妥当。

“I was not thinking of him, only wondering where to find someone trustworthy.”
『』我不是想到应春，我光是在想，哪里去寻一个靠得住的人。

She paused. “Think of someone yourself first.
『七姑奶奶停了一下说∶』小叔爷，你自己倒想一想，

If there really is no one, I have a person in mind.”
如果真的没有，我倒有个人。

“Tell me.”
『』那么，你说。

“No.
『『不！

You must consider your own people first.”
一定要小爷叔你自己先想。』


Hu reflected that Gu’s participation would be indispensable, yet Gu could not appear openly.
胡雪岩心想，做这件事少不了古应春的参预，而他又不能出面；

If the Seventh Lady nominated someone, it would be almost as reassuring as having Gu act himself.
如果七姑奶奶举荐一个人，就等于古应春下手一样，那才比较能令人放心。

He therefore did not even consider his own people.
这样一转念头，根本就不去考虑自己这方面的人，

“I have no one, Sister Seven.
『七姐，』他说∶『我没有人。

If you do, we can discuss it further with greater confidence.”
如果你有人，我们再谈下去，那才比较能令人放心。

He was making her take responsibility for the recommendation. She understood and reconsidered carefully, satisfying herself that nothing should go wrong before speaking.
这是逼着她荐贤。七姑奶奶明白，这是胡雪岩更加重她的责任；因而重新又考量了一下，确知不会出纰漏，

“Let my fifth brother act for us.”
方始说道∶『由我五哥出面来做好了。』

Brother You had long been retired, and few in Shanghai’s business world knew him.
尤五退隐已久，在上海商场上，知道他的人不多，

His influence in the Grain Transport Brotherhood remained, however.
但他在漕帮中的势力仍在，

With Gu’s assistance, he could undertake the task.
由他出面，加以有古应春做帮手，这件事是可以做的。

“If Brother Five agrees, I have no objection,” Hu said. “We shall discuss it properly when Yingchun returns.”
『如果五哥肯出面，我就没话说了。』胡雪岩说∶『等应春回来，好好商量。』


Gu made a special journey to Songjiang to bring Brother You back for a meeting.
古应春专程到松江去了一趟，将尤五邀了来，当面商谈。

Hu insisted on one condition: everything must be kept secret.
但胡雪岩只有一句话∶事情要做得隐秘，

He would remain entirely behind the scenes to avoid unnecessary trouble.
他完全退居幕后，避免不必要的纷扰。

“If you want nobody to know, the only sure course is not to do it,” Brother You said frankly. “But once the concern is established and the rice cooked, it will not matter if people discover it.”
『若要人不知，除非已莫为。』尤五的话很坦率∶『不过，场面摆出来以后，生米煮成熟饭，就人家晓得了，也不要紧。』

“True enough.
『这也是实话，

Still, I should like something to say by way of an excuse.”
不过到时候，总让我有句话能推托才好。』

“If you deny it, what can they do?” the Seventh Lady asked. “Go to Beijing for a while then, and spare yourself even the noise.”
『小爷叔你不认帐，人家有什么办法？』七姑奶奶说道∶『到时候，你到京里去一趟，索性连耳根都清净了。』

“Exactly.
『对，对！』胡雪岩连连点头，

I shall keep out of the way.”
『到时候我避开好了。』

Hu had thus fully accepted the couple’s advice on this large undertaking.
这就表示胡雪岩在这桩大生意上是完全接受了古应春夫妇的劝告。

Silk-reeling and cocoon purchases ranked third among his businesses, behind banking and pawnbroking.
纺丝收茧子，在胡雪岩全部事业中，规模仅次于钱庄与典当而占第三位，

The banks and pawnshops, however, had affiliated houses and carried on regular operations, each with a principal manager in overall charge.
但钱庄与典当都有联号，而且是经常性的营业，所以在制度上都有一个首脑在『抓总』，

Hu alone directed the silk and cocoon trade, drawing upon banking and pawnshop staff whenever necessary.
惟独丝茧的经营，是胡雪岩自己在指挥调度。钱庄、典当两方面的人，只要是用得着时，他随时可以调用，

Advances for purchasing the future crop required Fukang’s senior clerks in Huzhou and elsewhere; when silk and cocoon warehouses lacked space, the pawnshops had to assist; foreign sales inevitably called for Gu’s services.
譬如放款『买青』，要用到湖州等地阜康的档手；存丝、存茧子的堆栈不够用，他的典当便须协力，销洋庄跟洋人谈生意时，少不了要古应春出面。

The head clerks in individual silk and cocoon firms managed only their own portions of the business.
丝行、茧行的『档手』，只是管他自己的一部分业务，

Their rank was far below that of chief managers such as Mi.
层次较低，地位根本不能跟宓本常这班『大伙』相比。

For years Hu had sought someone capable of overseeing the whole concern, to whom he could entrust it completely.
多年来，胡雪岩总想找一个能够笼罩全局的人，可以将这部分的生意，全盘托付；但一直未能如愿。

Now Gu seemed the natural choice.
如今他认为古应春应该是顺理成章地成为适当的人选了。


“I am doing as you advise, Yingchun, and this business will henceforth be conducted differently.
『应春，现在我都照你们的话做了，以后这方面的做法也跟以前不相同了。

I therefore entrust all the silk and cocoon operations to you.
既然如此，丝跟茧子的事，我都交了给你。』

Nothing hinders business more than having one’s hands tied.
胡雪岩又说∶『做事最怕缚手缚脚，

Whether cooperating or competing with foreigners, one must have prompt information and make immediate decisions.
尤其是同洋人打交道，不管合作也好，竞争也好，贵乎消息灵通，当机立断，

Without full authority, if you still have to consult me at a critical moment, you will inevitably lose a move.”
如果你没有完全作主的权柄，到要紧关头仍旧要同我商量，那就一定输人家一着了。』

His reasoning was clear and his sincerity moving.
他的这番道理说得很透彻；态度之诚恳，更是令人感动，

But Gu felt the responsibility too heavy to accept.
但古应春觉得责任太重，不敢答应；

The Seventh Lady remained silent, apparently sharing his concern, which made him more cautious still.
七姑奶奶却沉默无语，显得跟他的感觉相同，便愈发谨慎了。

Yet he dared not refuse.
但他不敢推托；

To insist on declining would imply that he had no confidence in modern reeling.
因为坚持不允，便表示他对从事新式缫丝，并无把握的事，

What motive, then, had he had for urging someone else into it?
极力劝人家去做，是何居心？

That alone would be impossible to explain.
光在这一点上就说不通了。


“I am grateful for your confidence, Little Uncle.
于是他说∶『小爷叔承你看得起我，我很感激；

After so many years of friendship, I have no reason to refuse.
以我们多少年的交情来说，我亦决无推辞之理。

But this is a business turning over several million a year, with very wide connections.
不过，一年进出几百万的生意，牵涉的范围又很广，

Knowing only its surface, I dare not assume such responsibility before understanding it thoroughly.”
我没有彻底弄清楚，光是懂一点皮毛，是不敢承担这样大的责任的。』

“Naturally.
『这个自然是实话。』

But you are to steer the ship; others will do the work below.
胡雪岩说∶『不过，我是要你来掌舵，下面的事有人做。

Most of the specialists have served me for years.
专门搞这一行的人，多是跟了我多年的，

I shall bring them together to explain things. In a day or two, you will understand the essentials.”
我叫他们会集拢来，跟你谈个一两天，其中的决窍，你马上就都懂了。』

“If I take over, that will certainly be necessary.”
『如果我来接手，当然要这么做。』

Gu adroitly deferred the point. “We must proceed step by step.
古应春很巧妙地宕开一笔∶『凡事要按部就班来做，

Let me first help Brother Five complete the purchase of the two mills.
等我先帮五哥，把收买两个新缫丝厂的事办妥当了，

Then we can discuss the next stage.”
再谈第二步，好不好？』

“That is the proper course,” the Seventh Lady agreed. “The busy silkworm season is over for this year. Apart from the modern mills, everything can follow the established arrangements.
『应该这样子办。』七姑奶奶附和着说∶『而且今年蚕忙时期也过了∶除了新式缫丝厂以外，其余都不妨照常年旧规去办。

The immediate priority is to sell the stock you hold.”
目前最要紧的是，小爷叔手里的货色要赶紧脱手。』

Her last sentence mattered most. Still fearing a worsening political situation and declining trade, she considered conversion of goods into cash the safest course.
她的话，要紧的是最后一句∶她还是怕局势有变，市面愈来愈坏，脱货求现为上上之策。

Hu thought precisely the opposite.
但胡雪岩的想法正好相反，

With modern mills of his own, he need not fear finding an outlet for his cocoons.
他觉得自己办了新式缫丝厂，不愁茧子没有出路，

Secure on that point, why not fight the foreign merchants without restraint?
则有恃无恐，何不与洋商放手一搏？

Before a transaction, Hu consulted widely and willingly sought advice from anyone.
胡雪岩做生意，事先倒是周咨博询，不耻下问，

At the moment of decision, however, he made up his own mind.
但遇到真正要下决断时，是他自己在心里拿主意。

That was his nature; more than ten years of Zuo’s influence had also taught him the meaning of Yue Fei’s saying that the art of applying a method rests in the commander’s own judgment.
他的本性本就是如此，加以这十来年受左宗棠的熏陶，领会到岳飞所说的『运用之妙，存乎一心』的道理，

He gave the Seventh Lady’s suggestion little thought and merely nodded to show he had heard.
所以七姑奶奶的话，并未多想，也不表示意见，只点点头表示听到了而已。

“Let us turn to the immediate arrangements.
『现在我们把话说近来。』

Since Brother Five is to represent the concern, we must make it convincing.
胡雪岩说∶『既然是请五哥出面，样子要做得象，

I think two contracts are needed.”
我想我们要打两张合同。』

“Certainly,” Brother You said. “I should like to see how much work and responsibility I am taking on.
『是的，这应该。』尤五答说∶『我本来也要看看，我要做多少事，负多少责任？

Only a contract will make that clear.”
只有合同上才看得清楚。』

“You misunderstand me.
『五哥，』胡雪岩立即接口∶『你有点误会了，

I am not asking you to bear responsibility.
我不是要你负责任。

With you representing us and Yingchun involved, you need not carry the burden.
请你出来，又有应春在，用不着你负责任；

If the mills prosper, may they bring you good fortune in your later years, and us some reflected benefit.”
但愿厂做发达了，你算交一步老运，我们也沾你的光。

“You have that backwards, Little Uncle—” “Enough politeness!” the Seventh Lady interrupted impatiently. “Tell us about the two contracts.”
