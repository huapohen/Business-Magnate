CHAPTER SEVEN
第七章


The contracts were drafted.
合同稿子是拟好了，

But a cocoon firm could not open without approval from the Ministry of Revenue, and Mi used this as a pretext to suggest waiting for the ministry licence before signing.
但由于设立茧行需要呈请户部核准，方能开张，宓本常便以此为借口，主张等『部照』发下来，再签合同。

Hu and Gu had no suspicion of his malicious intentions.
胡雪岩与古应春哪里知道他心存叵测？

To them the contracts were merely a formality: once the mill purchase was agreed, the money would be available whenever required.
只认为订合同只是一个形式，只要把收买新式缫丝厂这件事说好了，款子随时可以动用，

They therefore consented.
所以都同意了。

With his Shanghai business concluded, Hu hurried back to Hangzhou through the oppressive summer heat.
在上海该办的事都办了，胡雪岩冒着溽暑赶回杭州；

A marriage had been proposed for his third daughter, and his mother had accepted on her behalf: she was to marry the only son of “Wang the Philanthropist.”
原来胡三小姐的红鸾星动，有人做媒，由胡老太太作主，许配了『王善人』的独养儿子。

Wang, whose personal name was Caisheng, had been Hu’s friend for many years.
王善人本名王财生，与胡雪岩是多年的朋友，

In their youth both had been shop assistants, the men Hangzhou people jokingly called “counter monkeys,” though in different trades.
年纪轻的时候，都是杭州人戏称为『柜台猢狲』的商店伙计，所不同的是行业，

Wang had begun as an apprentice in a large sauce-making establishment.
王财生是一家大酱园的『学徒』出身。

When Hu renewed his acquaintance with Wang Youling and rose rapidly in the world, Wang Caisheng remained an assistant.
当胡雪岩重遇王有龄，青云直上时，王财生仍旧在酱园里当伙计，

After the Taiping rebellion was suppressed, however, he reappeared in the guise of a gentleman.
但到洪杨平定以后，王财生摇身一变，以绅士姿态出现，

Some said he had enriched himself by looting during the disorder; others that he had unearthed gold, silver, and jewels buried by the rebels.
有人说他之发财是由于『趁火打劫』；有人说他『掘藏』掘到了『长毛』所埋藏的一批金银珠宝。

Whatever the source of his fortune, one fact was certain: at Hu’s invitation he helped with reconstruction, tending the wounded, saving the dying, and aiding displaced people.
但不管他发财的原因是什么，他受胡雪岩的邀约，同办善后，扶伤救死，抚缉流亡，做了许多好事，

These good works earned him the title “Philanthropist.”
博得个『善人』的美名，却是事实。

In the second year after Hangzhou’s recovery, a son was born to him, and people called it a reward for his charity.
杭州克复的第二年，王财生得了个儿子，都说他是行善的报应。


That was the yichou year, the fourth of Tongzhi.
那年是同治四年乙丑，

The boy, nicknamed A’niu, was now nineteen.
所以王财生的这个独子，小名阿牛，这年十九岁。

Wang had long wanted a marriage connection with Hu, but Hu thought A’niu dull—indeed, as stupid as the ox suggested by his name—and had consistently refused.
王财生早就想跟胡雪岩结亲家，而胡雪岩因为阿牛资质遇鲁，真有其笨如牛之概，一直不肯答应，

Unexpectedly, the young man had now passed into the county school as a licentiate, and the proposal was renewed.
不道这年居然进学成了秀才；因而旧事重提，

The matchmaker argued that his honest, kindly nature would ensure that Hu’s daughter was never mistreated.
做媒的人说∶阿牛天性淳厚，胡三小姐嫁了他一定不会吃亏，

As an only son’s wife, she would also be cherished by her parents-in-law.
而况又是独子；定受翁姑的宠爱。

Wang’s wealth fell far short of Hu’s, but his reputation for benevolence made up the difference; the families could be considered suitable equals.
至于家世，富虽远不敌胡雪岩，但有『善人』的名声弥补，亦可说是门当户对，

The sole deficiency had been A’niu’s lack of a degree.
所欠缺的只不过阿牛是个白丁；

Now a licentiate, he had a promising future: as the saying went, “A licentiate is the seedling of a chief minister.”
如今中了秀才，俗语说『秀才乃宰相之根苗』，前程远大，

It was an excellent match.
实在是头良缘匹配的好亲事。

The argument was reasonable.
这番说词，言之成理，

Hu’s mother particularly valued the fact that, with no brothers-in-law’s wives in the household, her granddaughter would suffer no friction from sisters-in-law.
加以胡老太太认为阿牛是独子，胡三小姐嫁了过去，即无妯娌，就不会受气，

She therefore accepted, merely writing to inform Hu and urge him home: the formal betrothal ceremony was set for the seventh day of the seventh month.
因而作主许婚，只写信告诉胡雪岩有这回事，催他快回杭州，因为择定七月初七『传红』。

On returning, he learned that the wedding too had been fixed, for the fifth day of the eleventh month.
回到杭州，才知道王家迎娶的吉期也定下了，是十一月初五；

Wang’s aged mother was frail and might die at any time; she longed to see her grandson’s bride enter the household.
为的是王善人的老娘，风烛残年，朝不保夕，急于想见孙媳妇进门；

If she died first, the wedding would have to wait three years, an excessive delay.
倘或去世，要三年之后才能办喜事，耽误得太久了。

The reasoning was entirely proper, and Hu’s mother agreed.
这番理由，光明正大，胡老太太深以为是，

Fortunately the dowry had long been prepared.
好在嫁妆是早就备好了的，

Only some fashionable foreign goods remained to be added.
只要再办一批时新的洋货来添妆就是了。


The scale of the celebration, however, awaited Hu’s return.
但办喜事的规模，却要等胡雪岩来商量；

Four people had to decide: Hu, his mother, his wife, and his concubine, Madam Luosi.
这件事要四个人来决定，便是胡雪岩与他的母、妻、妾——螺蛳太太。

All four had conflicting inclinations.
而这四个人都有一正一反的两种想法，

The three women favoured restraint, yet Hu’s mother loved this youngest granddaughter best and feared slighting her.
除了胡雪岩以外，其余三人都觉得场面应该收束，但胡老太太最喜欢这个小孙女儿，怕委屈了她；

His wife wanted equal treatment: the girl should enjoy the same splendour as her two elder sisters.
胡太太则认为应该一视同仁，她的两个姐姐是啥场面，她也应该一样地风光；

Madam Luosi thought of her own daughter.
螺蛳太太则是为自己的女儿设想，

A restrained wedding now would set a precedent limiting the display when her daughter married.
因为开了一个例子在那里，将来自己的女儿出阁，排场也就阔不起来了。

Hu naturally preferred the grandest possible occasion, but feared criticism in a depressed market.
至于胡雪岩当然愈阔愈好，但市面不景气，怕惹了批评。

Two days of discussion produced no decision.
因此谈了两天没有结果；

At last he concluded, “The occasion must be respectable.
最后是胡雪岩自己下了个结论∶『场面总也要过得去，

The difference between a large and small celebration is limited anyway.
是大是小，相差也有限；

There are still four months; we shall see how matters stand.”
好在还有四个月的工夫，到时候再看吧。』

“The celebration is for other people to look at,” Madam Luosi observed. “The dowry brings real benefit.
『场面是摆给人家看的。』螺蛳太太接口说道∶『嫁妆是自己实惠。

Give Third Miss a splendid dowry, and a smaller celebration will do.
三小姐的陪嫁，一定要风光；这样子，到时候场面就小一点，

Publicly, we can explain that business is poor; privately, she will not feel slighted.
对外说起来是市面不好；对内，三小姐也不会觉得委屈，

Even the bridegroom’s family can have no objection.”
就是男家也不会有话说。』

This considered every side.
这番见解，真是面面俱到，

Both Hu’s mother and his wife were pleased, while Hu felt that a magnificent dowry would preserve his dignity even without a lavish ceremony.
胡老太太与胡太太听了都很舒服；胡雪岩则认为惟有如此，就算排场不大，但嫁妆风光，也就不失面子了。


“Sister Luo is right.
『罗四姐的话不错。

We must not stint the dowry.
嫁妆上不能委屈她。

But the additions will have to be bought ready-made.”
不过添妆也只有就现成的备办了。』

“Then someone must go to Shanghai,” Hu’s wife said after her mother-in-law, looking at Madam Luosi.
『那只有到上海去。』胡太太接着她婆婆的话说，同时看着罗四姐。

Madam Luosi wanted to volunteer, but reconsidered and remained silent.
罗四姐很想自告奋勇，但一转念间，决定保持沉默；

There were too many tongues in the household.
因为胡家人多嘴杂，

However hard she tried, someone would talk behind her back, perhaps alleging that she begrudged the expense because Third Miss was not her own daughter.
即使尽力，必定也还有人在背后说闲话，甚至造谣言∶三小姐不是她生的，她哪里舍得花钱替三小姐添妆。

Hu had expected her to respond.
胡雪岩原以为她会接口，

When she did not, he decided for her.
看她不作声，便只好作决定了，

“You know Shanghai.
『上海是你熟，

Go there, and visit the Seventh Lady while you are about it.”
你去一趟。』他说∶『顺便也看看七姑奶奶。』

“Of course I should go for Third Miss’s wedding.
『为三小姐的喜事，我到上海去一趟，是千应万该的。

But expensive jewellery is not necessarily pleasing.
不过，首饰这样东西，贵不一定好；

Naturally I would choose costly pieces, yet their designs might not suit her taste.”
我去当然挑贵的买，只怕买了来，花样款式不中三小姐的意。

She smiled. “Why not let me take her and have her choose for herself at the foreign firms and jewellers?”
我看，』螺蛳太太笑一笑说∶『我陪小姐到上海，请她自己到洋行、银楼里去挑。』

“Quite improper!” the Old Madam exclaimed in broad Hangzhou speech. “An unmarried girl choosing her own dowry!
『不作兴的！』胡老太太用一口道地的杭州话说∶『没有出门的姑娘儿，自己去挑嫁妆，

People would laugh themselves sick if they heard of it.”
传出去把人家笑都笑煞了。』

“You go yourself,” Hu repeated.
『就是你去吧！』胡雪岩重复一句。


Madam Luosi still would not commit herself.
螺蛳太太仍旧不作承诺，

“I wonder whether Third Miss might enjoy a visit,” she murmured, as if to herself.
『不晓得三小姐有没有兴致去走一趟？』她自语似地说。

“There is no need,” Hu’s wife said. “Surely you know what jewellery she likes.”
『不必了。』胡太太∶『三丫头喜欢怎么样的首饰，莫非你还不清楚？』

Finally the Old Madam settled the matter: Madam Luosi would go alone.
最后还是由胡老太太一言而决，由螺师太太一个人到上海去采办。

She would, of course, first consult the girl’s preferences and Hu’s wife’s wishes.
当然，她要先问一问胡三小姐的爱好，还有胡太太的意见，

Most important was a spending limit, which only Hu could set.
同时最要紧的是，一个花费的总数，这是只有胡雪岩才能决定的。

“The dowry has already cost over a hundred thousand taels.
『她这副嫁妆，已经用了十几万银子了。

Spend no more than another fifty thousand on additions.
现在添妆，最多再用五万银子。』

Money is tight in Shanghai, so goods must be cheap.
胡雪岩说∶『上海银根很紧，银根紧，东西一定便宜，

Fifty thousand should buy at least seventy thousand’s worth.”
五万银子起码好当七万用。』


In Shanghai, Gu accompanied her to the German firm Biefa.
到了上海，由古应春陪着，到德商别发洋行里一问，

Their first inquiries showed that the opposite was true.
才知道胡雪岩的话适得其反。

Domestic products were indeed being discounted for ready cash, but imported goods had risen in price.
国内的出产，为了脱货求现，削价出售，固然不错，但舶来品却反而涨价了。

“The situation grows more tense every day, Mr Gu,” the manager explained.
『古先生，』洋行的管事解释∶『局势一天比一天紧，

“France has a new premier, Jules Ferry, a very determined man.
法国的宰相换过了，现在的这个叫茹斐理，手段很强硬，

If China refuses to yield over Vietnam, he means to go to war.
如果中国在越南那方面，不肯让步，他决心跟中国开仗。

Ever since the foreign newspapers reported Admiral Courbet’s arrival in Vietnam, goods at every foreign firm have risen by ten to fifteen per cent.
自从外国报纸登了法国水师提督孤拔到越南的消息以后，各洋行的货色，马上都上涨了一成到一成五；

Some things cannot now be bought at any price.”
现在是有的东西连出价都买不到了。』

“Why?” Madam Luosi asked.
『这是为啥？』螺蛳太太发问。

“If war begins, French warships will close China’s ports and foreign merchant ships will be unable to enter.
『胡太太，战事一起，法国兵舰封住中国的海口，外国商船不能来；

Once supplies stop, sellers can ask whatever they please.
货色断档，那时候的价钱，老实说一句，要多少就是多少，

The question will be whether an article is available, not whether it is dear.
只问有没有，不问贵不贵，

Every item sold now is one less for later, so everyone is putting the best goods aside.”
所以现在卖一样少一样，大家拿好东西都收起来了。』

“No wonder!”
『怪不得！』

She pointed at the jewellery in the glass cabinets. “There is nothing here I like.”
螺蛳太太指着玻璃柜子中的首饰说∶『这里的东西，没有一样是看得上眼的。』

“Your taste is naturally exceptional, Madam Hu.
『胡太太的眼光当然不同。』

We would not risk offending an old customer.
那管事说道，『我们对老主顾，不敢得罪的。

Tell me what you require, and I shall open the safe for you.”
胡太太想置办哪些东西，我开保险箱，请胡太太挑。』

Madam Luosi knew that foreigners in China, whatever their nationality, acted together.
螺蛳太太知道，在中国的洋人，不分国籍，都是很团结的；

They too had agreements among traders.
他们亦有『同行公议』的规矩，

One firm would be much like another, so the maxim about comparing three shops would be little use.
这家如此，另一家亦复如此，『货比三家不吃亏』这句话用不上，

Better to negotiate as a major customer.
倒不如自己用『大主顾』的身分来跟他谈谈条件。


“To be frank, I am buying a dowry for our third daughter.
『我老实跟你说，我是替我们家三小姐来办嫁妆，

If we agree, I shall give you the entire order—several tens of thousands of taels.
谈得拢，几万银子的生意，我都作成了你。

Otherwise, Biefa is hardly Shanghai’s only large foreign firm.”
不然，说老实话，上海滩上的大洋行，不是你别发一家。』

The prospect demanded attention.
听说是几万银子的大生意，那管事不敢怠慢，

“Third Miss’s dowry must be treated seriously.
『办三小姐的嫁妆，马虎不得。

Please come inside.
胡太太，你请里面坐！』

If you have a list, let me take it and assemble the goods for you to examine.”
他说∶『如果胡太太开了单子，先交给我，我照单配齐了，送进来请你看。』

She had prepared a list but would not reveal her hand.
螺蛳太太是开好了一张单子的，但不肯泄漏底细，

“No list.
只说∶『我没有单子。

If the goods are good and the prices reasonable, I shall buy more.
只要东西好，价钱克己，我就多买点。

First show me two pairs of diamond bracelets.”
你先拿两副钻镯我看看。』

Chinese and Western fashions differed.
中外服饰时尚不同，

To Chinese customers, diamond bracelets were the most precious jewellery.
对中国主顾来说，最珍贵的首饰，就是钻镯；

The request confirmed that she really was buying a dowry and that the order might indeed run to tens of thousands.
那管事一听此话，心知嫁妆的话不假，这笔生意做下来，确有好几万银子，

The manager became still more attentive.
是难得一笔大生意，便愈发巴结了。

He escorted her and Gu to the foreign director’s private sitting room and specially brought in a foreign saleswoman who spoke Chinese.
将螺蛳太太与古应春请到他们大班专用的小客厅，还特为找了个会说中国话的外籍女店员招待；

Her name was Aidun; Madam Luosi called her “Miss Ai.”
名叫艾敦，螺蛳太太便叫她『艾小姐。』


“Where are you from, Miss Ai?”
『艾小姐，你是哪里人？』

“I was born in Edinburgh,” she replied while preparing tea with milk.
『我出生在爱丁堡。』艾敦一面调着奶茶，一面答说。

Madam Luosi did not know the place, so Gu explained that she was British.
螺蛳太太不知道这个地名，古应春便即解释∶『她是英国人。』

“Then your country is like ours,” Madam Luosi said.
『喔！』螺蛳太太说道∶『你们英国同我们中国一样的，

“Both are ruled by an old empress dowager.”
都是老太后当权。』

Aidun’s Chinese covered everyday conversation only.
艾敦虽会说中国话，也不过是日常用语，

Talk of an “empress dowager ruling” left her as bewildered as the name Edinburgh had left Madam Luosi.
什么『老太后当权』，就跟螺蛳太太听到『爱丁堡』这个地名一样，瞠目不知所对。

Gu had to explain again. “She means your Queen Victoria and our Empress Dowager Cixi.”
这就少不得又要靠古应春来疏通了∶『她是指你们英国的维多利亚女皇，跟我们中国的慈禧太后。』

Aidun was surprised.
『喔，』艾敦颇为惊异，

She had served many Chinese women—courtesans, and occasionally ladies of rank or respectable young women—but none had mentioned the British queen.
因为她也接待过许多中国的女顾客，除了北里娇娃以外，间或也有贵妇与淑女，但从没有一个人在谈话时会提到英国女皇。

She warmed to Madam Luosi and served her afternoon tea with particular care.
因为如此，便大起好感，招待螺蛳太太用午茶，非常殷勤。

The manager then brought three oblong boxes, all of black leather with gold lettering.
接着，管事的捧来了三个长方盒子，一律黑色真皮，上烫金字，

Inside the first, a pair of brilliantly sparkling platinum bracelets set with diamonds rested on blue velvet.
打开第一个盒子，蓝色鹅绒上，嵌着一双光芒四射的白金钻镯，

The workmanship was exquisite.
镶嵌得非常精致。


On closer inspection, however, the box looked new while the metal’s colour seemed otherwise.
仔细看去，盒子虽新，白金的颜色却似有异，

“These are used?” she asked.
『这是旧的？』她问。

“Yes.
『是的。

They were favourite jewels of Napoleon’s empress.”
这是拿破仑皇后心爱的首饰。』

“I care nothing for empresses.
『我不管什么皇后。』

New things are better for a dowry.”
螺蛳太太说∶『嫁妆总是新的好。』

“Both these other pairs are new.”
『这两副都是新的。』

One was set entirely with diamonds; the other combined diamonds with rubies and sapphires.
另外西副，一副全钻，一副镶了红蓝宝石，

The all-diamond pair was the more valuable.
论贵重是全钻的那副，

Each bracelet held four diamonds the size of soybeans, linked by smaller stones, and glittered even when held still.
每一只有四粒黄豆大的钻石，用碎钻连接，拿在手里不动都会闪耀；

For splendour, however, the coloured stones excelled.
但谈到华丽，却要算镶宝石的那副。

“What are the prices?”
『什么价钱？』

“Thirty-five thousand for these; thirty-two thousand for the pair with coloured stones.
『这副三万五，镶宝石的这副三万二。』

I advise the all-diamond pair, Madam Hu.
管事的说∶『胡太太，我劝你买全钻的这副，

It costs three thousand more but is better value.”
虽然贵三千银子，其实比镶宝的划算。』

Unable to decide, she asked Aidun to put them on.
螺蛳太太委决不下，便即说道∶『艾小姐，请你戴起来我看看。』


Aidun wore one kind on each wrist and held out her arms for inspection.
艾敦便一只手腕戴一样，平伸出来让她仔细鉴赏，

After a long look, Madam Luosi turned to Gu. “What do you think, Brother-in-law?”
螺蛳太太看了半天转眼问道∶『七姐夫，你看呢？』

“The all-diamond pair is better, certainly, but rather too plain in colour.”
『好，当然是全钻的这副好，可惜太素净了。』

This exactly matched her own view, and she decided at once.
这看法跟螺蛳太太的完全一样，顿时作了决定，

“For a bride, with the Old Madam present, the effect should not be too subdued.”
『又是新娘子，又是老太太在，不宜太素净。』

She turned to the manager. “I have chosen.
她向管事说道∶『我东西是挑定了，
