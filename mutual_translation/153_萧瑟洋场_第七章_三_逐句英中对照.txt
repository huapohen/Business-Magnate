胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement
第七章（本章第3部分，共6部分）
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


“To tell you the truth, Seventh Sister, apart from the Old Lady, everyone finds those plain vegetable dumplings pleasant enough once in a while, but too many of them spoil the appetite,” Madam Luosi said. “Even to me, ours taste quite different from the Xu family’s at Sanpailou.”
『七姐，我老实告诉你，那种净素的菜圆子，除了老太太以外，大家都是偶尔吃一回还可以，一多，胃口就倒了。』螺蛳太太又说∶『我自己也觉得完全不是三牌楼徐家的那种味道。』

The wine-lees casserole was an authentic Shanghai dish, one of the so-called local specialities, usually available only at this time.
糟钵头是上海道地的所谓『本帮菜』，通常只有今天才有，

Pork tripe, liver, and other offal were simmered with a plump chicken; when everything was properly cooked, it was poured into an earthenware pot and wine lees were added—hence its name.
用猪肚、猪肝等等内脏，加肥鸡同煮，到够火候了，倾陶钵加糟，所以称之为糟钵头『。

Wine-lees-cured black carp, cut into pieces and cooked with pale-hearted Chinese cabbage as a soupy dish, was called chuanzao.
糟青鱼切块，与黄芽菜同煮作汤菜，即是』川糟『。

“And do you think yours is better or worse than Tao A’da’s?”
『那末，你觉得比陶阿大的是好，还是坏？』

“Worse, of course.
『当然不及陶阿大的。』

Otherwise I should not keep thinking of his.”
螺蛳太太说∶『不然我也不会这么想了。』

“I suspect his would not seem quite as good now as you remember.”
『只怕现在不会象你所想的那样子好。』

“Oh?
『喔，』

Has the shop changed hands?”
螺蛳太太问道∶『莫非换过老板？』

“I have never tried those dumplings, but before I fell ill I used to eat Tao A’da’s casserole, outside the county yamen.
『菜圆子我没有吃过，县衙前陶阿大的糟钵头，我没有得病以前是吃过的。

Last year, when Brother Five came from Songjiang in the twelfth month, he made a special trip there.
去年腊月里五哥从松江来了，还特为去吃过。

Their business is flourishing.
人家做得兴兴旺旺的生意，

Why should it change hands?”
为啥要换老板？』

Madam Luosi was quick to detect a further meaning.
『那末，』螺蛳太太也极机警，知道七姑奶奶刚才的话，别有言外之意，

“Then there must be a reason for what you said.”
便即追问∶『既然这样子，你的话总有啥道理在里头吧？

The Seventh Lady considered. “I speak my mind, and we are as close as sisters.
七姑奶奶想了一下说∶』我是直性子；我们又同姊妹一样。

If I say something mistaken, do not take offence.”
我或者说错了，你不要怪我。

“How could I?
『』哪里会！

Seventh Sister, there is no need to say that.”
七姐，你这话多余。『


“There may indeed be some special knack to making those dumplings.
『我在想，做菜圆子，或者真的有啥诀窍；

But as for the casserole, surely your highly paid master cook is no less skilful than Tao A’da?
至于糟钵头，我在想，你家吃大俸禄的大司务，本事莫非就不及陶阿大？

And as for ingredients, leaving everything else aside, the wine lees you have brought from Shaoxing are better than anything he uses.
说到材料，别的不谈，光是从绍兴办来的酒糟，这一点就比陶阿大那里要高明了。

Your casserole cannot possibly be inferior to his.
所以府上的糟钵头，决不会比陶阿大来得差。

Yet you say it is. Why?”
然而，你说不及陶阿大的糟钵头这是啥道理。』

“Seventh Sister!” Madam Luosi laughed. “That was what I asked you.
『七姐！』螺蛳太太笑道∶『我就是问你，

Why turn the question back on me?”
你怎么反倒问我？』

“The casserole, I think, is still the casserole of those days.
『依我看，糟钵头还是当年的糟钵头，

But Sister Luo is no longer the Sister Luo of those days.”
罗四姐不是当年的罗四姐了。』

She hastened to add, “I do not mean that you have forgotten your beginnings.
七姑奶奶紧接着说∶『四姐，我这话不是说你忘本，

I mean that times and circumstances change.
是说此一时，彼一时，

Nor did I discover this truth for myself.
这番道理，也不是我悟出来的，

It came from a story I heard a storyteller tell.
是说书先生讲的一段故事，

In the Tang dynasty there was a monk called Lancan—” She told the story of the monk roasting taro.
唐朝有个和尚叫懒残——』讲了懒残和尚煨芋的故事，

Madam Luosi certainly felt no suggestion of mockery in it, but she remained silent for a long time, deep in thought.
螺蛳太太当然决不会觉得七姑奶奶有何讽刺之意，但却久久无语，心里想得很深。


Ruixiang now brought a maid to lay the table.
这时瑞香已带了小大姐来铺排餐桌，

They helped the Seventh Lady up and lifted her into a specially made round-backed chair.
然后将七姑奶奶扶了起来，抬坐在一张特制的圈椅上，

It was large, with brocade cushions packed around her to keep her upright without effort.
椅子很大，周围用锦垫塞紧，使得七姑奶奶不必费力便能坐直，

Across the front lay a broad, removable wooden tray for plates and bowls, edged with a little “wall” five fen high to prevent spilled soup from running everywhere.
前面是一块很大的活动木板，以便置放盘碗，木板四周镶嵌五分高的一道『围墙』以防汤汁倾出，以不致流得到处都是。

The chair served much the same purpose as a child’s chair.
那张圈椅跟『小儿车』的作用相同；

When Ruixiang had tied on her bib, the Seventh Lady laughed at herself. “People in Wuxi are always saying that the old become children again.
七姑奶奶等瑞香替她系上『围嘴』以后，自嘲地笑道∶『无锡人常说「老小、老小」，

The older I grow, the more of a child I am.”
我真是愈老愈小了。』

“You scarcely look old,” Madam Luosi said with a smile. “Your skin is so fair and soft that I want to feel it myself.”
『老倒不见得。』螺蛳太太笑道∶『皮肤又白又嫩，我都想摸一把。』

She took her arm and gave it a couple of gentle squeezes.
说着便握住她的手臂，轻轻捏了两下，

The flesh had lost its firmness, after all.
肌肉到底松弛了。

“Dumplings first, or wine?” Ruixiang asked.
『是先吃圆子，还是先吃酒？』瑞香问道。

The dumplings were already cooked, so naturally they came first.
菜圆子，已经煮好了，自然先吃圆子；

They were so large that only two would fit in a rice bowl decorated with yellow flowers.
圆子很大，黄花累瓷饭碗中只放得下两枚，

Ruixiang had taken particular pains, adding a few strips of ham and two or three coriander leaves.
瑞香格外道地加一几条火腿后，两三片芫荽，

The contrast of red and green was appetizing.
红绿相映，动人食欲。


“Let me try one.”
『我来尝一个。』

The Seventh Lady lifted a dumpling in her spoon, blew on it, and took a bite, followed immediately by another: her enjoyment was plain.
七姑奶奶拿汤匙舀了一枚，嘘口气，咬了一口，紧接着便咬第二口，，欣赏之意显然。

Madam Luosi also put one to her lips, then set it down and tasted the soup.
螺蛳太太也舀了一枚送入口中，接着放回圆子舀口汤喝，

“Ruixiang,” she asked doubtfully, “did these come from Widow Xu’s at Sanpailou?”
『瑞香，』她疑惑地问∶『是三牌楼徐寡妇家买的？』

“Yes.”
『是啊！』

Ruixiang’s smile suggested a secret.
瑞香微笑着回答。看她的笑容，便知内有蹊跷，

“What soup did you cook them in?”
『你拿什么汤下的圆子？』她问。

“You noticed, madam!
『太太尝出来了。』

A Cantonese restaurant called Xinghua Lou has just opened.
瑞香笑道∶『新开一家广东杏花楼，

I used their stock.”
用它家的高汤下的。』

“Stock?”
『高汤？』

In a modest eating house, stock was free: bone broth with a spoonful of soy sauce and a sprinkling of chopped scallions.
在小馆子，『高汤』是白送的；肉骨头熬的汤，加一匙酱油，数粒葱花便是。

Madam Luosi naturally wondered how such a broth could make vegetable dumplings taste so delicious.
这样的汤下菜圆子能有这样的鲜味，螺蛳太太自然要诧异了。

“Xinghua Lou’s stock is nothing like that pot-rinsing water.
『杏花楼的高汤，不是同洗锅水差不多的高汤；

They simmer chicken, ham, lean pork, and crucian carp over a low flame.
它是鸡、火腿、精肉、鲫鱼，用文火熬出来的汤，

It is sold by the liang.”
论两卖的。』

“No wonder!” the Seventh Lady laughed. “If Widow Xu’s dumplings tasted like this by themselves, she would have to be an immortal.”
『怪不得！』七姑奶奶笑道∶『如说徐寡妇的菜圆子有这样的味道，除非她是仙人。』

“Ruixiang has gone out of her way to please me,” Madam Luosi said, “but now I cannot recover the taste I remember at all.”
『瑞香倒是特别巴结我，不过我反而吃不出当年的味道来了。』


“Then try the casserole, madam.
『那末太太尝尝糟钵头，

We bought it at Tao A’da’s and have done nothing to it.”
这是陶阿大那里买回来以后，原封没有动过。』

Madam Luosi nodded, picked up a piece of tripe, and chewed it carefully, doing her utmost to recall the flavour of the old days.
螺蛳太太点点头，挟了一块猪肚，细细嚼；同时极力回忆当年吃糟钵头的滋味，

It was no use.
可是没有用，

This tasted less good than her own cook’s.
味道还不如她家厨子做的来得好。

“Seventh Sister, you were right.
『七姐，你的话不错。

I am no longer the Sister Luo I used to be.”
我罗四姐，不是当年的罗四姐了。』

The Seventh Lady said nothing.
七姑奶奶默不作声，

She rather regretted speaking so bluntly and making her friend sad.
心里还颇有悔意，刚才的话不应该说得那么率直，惹起她的伤感。


Ruixiang, who could make nothing of their riddling conversation, stared wide-eyed in bewilderment.
瑞香却不知她们打的什么哑谜，瞪圆了一双大眼睛发楞。

“Ruixiang,” Madam Luosi said, “remember this: only those who endure the bitterest hardships can rise above others.”
罗四姐便又说道∶『瑞香，你总要记牢，吃得苦中苦，方为人上人。』

Still unable to understand what she meant, Ruixiang could only answer, “Yes.”
瑞香仍旧不明她这话的用意，只好答应一声∶『是。』

“Then again, people are not born simply to suffer,” the Seventh Lady said. “If you have the good fortune to enjoy life, you should enjoy it.
『话要说回来，人也不是生来就该吃苦的。』七姑奶奶说道∶『有福能享，还是要享。

Only—” With Ruixiang present, she thought it better not to go too deeply into the subject, and changed her words. “The danger is to live in good fortune without knowing it.”
不过——』她觉得有瑞香在旁，话说得太深了也不好，便改口说道∶『就怕身在福中不知福。』

“Seventh Sister, that goes straight to the heart of it.
『七姐这句话，真正是一针见血。』

Ruixiang, warm a pot of huadiao wine.
螺蛳太太说∶『瑞香，你去烫一壶花雕来，

I feel like a drink today.”
我今天想吃酒。』

Madam Luosi could hold her wine well.
螺蛳太太的酒量很不错，

When it came, she poured for herself and drank rapidly.
烫了来自斟自饮，喝得很猛；

“Sister Luo, wine is for pleasure,” the Seventh Lady reminded her.
七姑奶奶便提了一句∶『四姐，酒要吃得高兴，

“Take it slowly.”
慢慢吃。』

“Do not worry.
『不要紧，

One pot will not make me drunk.”
这一壶酒醉不倒我。』

“Perhaps not, but you may talk as though you were.
『醉虽醉不倒，会说醉话；

And once you do, people will take you even less seriously.”
你一说醉话，人家就更加不当真的了。』

That was a riddle only the two of them understood.
这才真正是哑谜，只有她们两人会意。

Remembering that she had to speak to Gu about Ruixiang, Madam Luosi accepted the warning.
螺蛳太太想到要跟古应春谈瑞香的事，便听七姑奶奶的劝，

She took small, leisurely sips as they talked, finished the pot, and asked for no more.
浅斟低酌，闲谈着将一壶酒喝完，也不想再添，

After she had eaten a bowl of fragrant rice porridge, Gu returned.
要了一碗香粳米粥吃完，古应春也回来了。


At first they chatted in the Seventh Lady’s bedroom.
先是在七姑奶奶卧室中闲话；

When the clock struck nine, Madam Luosi said, “Seventh Sister must be sleepy.
听到钟打九下，螺蛳太太便即说道∶『七姐只怕要困了；

Brother-in-law, I should like you to write a letter for me.”
我请姐夫替我写封信。』

“Certainly.
『好！

Come to my study.”
到我书房里去。』

As soon as they entered, Ruixiang brought tea.
等他们一进书房，瑞香随即将茶端了进来，

She remembered the Hu household rule that servants must withdraw when a member of the family asked someone to write a letter, and closed the door behind her.
胡家的规矩，凡是主人家找人写信，下人是不准在旁边的，她还记着这个规矩，所以带上房门，管自己走了。

“Brother-in-law, the letter is a pretence.
『姐夫，写信是假，

I have really come to settle a matter with you.”
跟你来办交涉是真。』

“What is it?
『什么事？』

Whatever you wish, Sister Luo, just tell me.”
古应春说∶『有什么话，四姐交代就是。』

“Then I shall speak plainly.
『那末，我就直说。

What do you mean by leaving my Ruixiang to one side?”
姐夫。你把我的瑞香搁在一边，是啥意思。』

Faced with this aggressive challenge, Gu was embarrassed.
看她咄咄逼人，看有点办交涉的意味，古应春倒有些窘了。

The matter was hard enough to explain at the best of times; under these circumstances he could scarcely find a word.
本来就是件不容易表达清楚的事，在这样的情况之下，自然更是讷讷然无法出口。

Her manner was deliberate, intended to save argument.
罗四姐原是故意作此姿态，说话比较省力，

Having gained the advantage, she immediately softened her tone. “Brother-in-law, what is really in your mind?
既占上风，急忙收敛，『姐夫，』她的声音放得柔和而恳切，『你心里到底是啥想法？

Tell me freely.
尽管跟我说；

Have you found, after knowing her longer, that Ruixiang’s character is not what it should be?”
是不是日子一长，看出来瑞香的人品不好。』

“No, no!”
『不、不！』

Gu broke in hastily.
古应春急急打断，

“If I thought anything of the kind, I should have no conscience at all.”
『我如果心里有这样的想法，那就算没良心到家了。』


“Then you do like her?”
『照你说，瑞香你是中意的。』

“More than like her…” Gu smiled and left the sentence unfinished.
『不但中意┅┅』古应春笑笑没有再说下法。

“You mean you do not merely like her—you like her very much?”
『意思是不但中意，而且交关中意？』

“That is true too.”
『这也是实话。』

“Then, with Seventh Sister longing for you to begin your married life together, why do you show no enthusiasm?
『即然如此，七姐又巴不得你们早早圆房，你为啥一点都不起劲。

Brother-in-law, give me a reason.”
姐夫，请你说个道理给我听。』

Her voice rose again.
螺蛳太太的调子又拉高了。

Gu frowned slightly and did not answer at once.
古应春微微皱眉，不即作答；

He had recently taken to smoking—not opium, but Manila cigars.
他最近才有了吸烟的嗜好——不是鸦片是吕宋烟；

Opening his silver case, he took out an Old Beauty, clipped its end with a special cutter, struck a red-headed match against his shoe sole, and slowly lit it.
打开银烟盒，取出一支『老美女』用特制的剪刀剪去烟头，用根『红头火柴』在鞋底上划燃了慢慢点烟。

A moment later Madam Luosi could smell the rich tobacco but could no longer see his face through the smoke.
霎时间螺蛳太太只闻到浓郁的烟香，却看不见古应春的脸，因为让烟雾隔断了。


“Sister Luo,” his voice emerged from the cloud, “taking a concubine is, to speak frankly, one of the pleasures of a man’s life.
『四姐，』古应春在烟雾中发声∶『讨小纳妾，说实话，是我们男人家人生一乐。

That being so, it depends on circumstances and on one’s state of mind.
既然这样子，就要看境况、看心情，

If circumstances are bad, one may still find a little pleasure amid one’s troubles.
境况不好做这种事，还可以说是苦中作乐；

But if one’s heart is troubled, there can be no pleasure at all.”
心情不好，就根本谈不到乐趣了。』

The answer was rather unexpected.
这个答复，多少是出人意外的；

Madam Luosi considered it. “The Master told me that you were dragged into trouble over property by that man Xu.
螺蛳太太想了一会说∶『大先生也跟我谈过，说你做房地产受了姓徐的累，

But it is over now.
不过现在事情已经过去了，

Surely your spirits ought to be different?”
心情也应该不同了。』

“Quite the contrary.
『恰恰相反，

Things are different now, too.”
事情也应该不同了。』

“Why?”
『为啥呢？』

“Little Uncle could not have treated me better.
『四姐，小爷叔待我，自然没有话说；

He would never begrudge me a hundred thousand taels.
十万银子，在他也不会计较。

But it weighs on my mind, especially with money so tight in the market.
不过，在我总是一桩心事，尤其现在市面上的银根极紧；

Little Uncle does not mind; other people do not think as he does.”
小爷叔不在乎，旁人跟他的想法不一样。』

The implication in his last words made Madam Luosi both astonished and indignant.
最后这句话，弦外有音，螺蛳太太不但诧异，而且有些气愤，

“Who are these other people?
『这旁人是哪一个？』

What have their opinions to do with the Master?
她问∶『旁人的想法，同大先生啥相干？

Why should you listen to them?”
你为啥要去听？』

Gu did not reply.
古应春不作声，

He drew deeply on his cigar and continued with his own thought. “Little Uncle has helped me so much that I can feel easier only if I do something for him with all the ability and devotion I possess.
深深地吸了口烟，管他自己又说∶『小爷叔帮了我这么大一个忙，我想替小爷叔尽心尽力做点事，心里才比较好过。

Last time, after a great deal of effort, I persuaded him to buy modern silk-reeling mills, produce his own silk, and sell directly to the foreign firms.
上次好不容易说动小爷叔，收买新式缫丝厂，自己做丝直接销洋庄；

Who could have expected obstacles at every turn?
哪晓得处处碰钉子，

To this day I have achieved nothing.
到今朝一事无成。

Brother You has lost heart and gone back to Songjiang.
尤五哥心灰意冷，回松江去了。

Sister Luo, how can I be in the mood to think about Ruixiang?”
四姐，你说我哪里会有心思来想瑞香的事？』

His sincerity deeply moved her, and their conversation naturally shifted from Ruixiang to the mills.
这番话说得非常诚恳，螺蛳太太深为同情；话题亦就自然而然地由瑞香转到新式缫丝厂了。


“Was not everything carefully planned at the start?
『当初不是筹划得好好的？』

Why these obstacles at every turn?
她问∶『处处碰钉子是啥缘故；

What sort of obstacles?”
碰的是啥个钉子？』

“It is a long story.”
『一言难尽。』

Gu shook his head, unwilling to go further.
古应春摇摇头，不愿深谈。

She tried approaching him from several directions but could not draw out what troubled him.
螺蛳太太旁敲侧击，始终不能让古应春将他的难言之隐吐露出来。

She was beginning to feel annoyed and was about to reproach him when an idea occurred to her: she would provoke him into speaking.
以致于螺蛳太太都有些动气了。但正当要说两句埋怨的话时，灵机一动想到了一个激将法。

“Brother-in-law, tell me freely.
『姐夫，你尽管跟我说，

When I go home I shall certainly not make mischief.
我回去决不会搬弄是非；

I shall only put in a good word for you with the Master.”
只会在大先生面前替你说话。』

This made Gu extremely uneasy.
一听这话，古应春大为不安。

To refuse further would be virtually to admit that he feared she would “make mischief.”
如果仍旧不肯说，无异表示真的怕她回去『搬弄是非』。

Her tone also suggested that she suspected him of mismanagement, or even of pursuing some private interest that had left him with nothing accomplished.
同时听她的语气，似乎疑心他处置不善，甚至怀有私心，以致『一事无成』。

He was unwilling to accept such an unfounded misunderstanding in silence.
这份无端而起的误会，亦不甘默然承受。

Suppressing his agitation, he considered for a moment and said, “Sister Luo, I meant to swallow my broken teeth with the blood—to bear my grievance myself.
于是，古应春抑制激动的心情，考虑了一会答说∶『四姐，我本来是「打落牙齿和血吞」，有委屈自己受。

Now it seems I must speak.
现在看样子是非说不可了！

But first let me make one thing clear: I have never suspected you of making mischief between Little Uncle and me.”
不过，四姐，有句话，我先要声明，我决没有疑心四姐会在小爷叔面前搬弄是非的意思。』

“I know, I know.”
『我晓得，我晓得。』

She smiled triumphantly. “If I had not pressed you like that, how could I ever have got it out of you?”
螺蛳太太得意地笑道∶『我不是这样子逼一逼，哪里会把你的话逼出来？』


Only then did Gu realize that he had been caught. “I shall tell you.
听得这话，古应春才知道上当了∶『我说是说。

But now it feels as though I am the one making mischief.”
不过，』他说∶『现在好象是我在搬弄是非了。』

“Brother-in-law,” she said gravely, “I do understand what matters.
『姐夫，』螺蛳太太正色说道∶『我不是不识轻重的人。

Of course I shall consider what may be repeated and what must be kept to myself.
你告诉我的话，哪些能说，哪些不能说，我当然也会想一想。

If you refuse to tell me the truth merely to avoid suspicion, you are not treating me as one of your own.”
为了避嫌疑不肯说实话，就不是自己人了。』

There was an undertone of reproach in her final words, and Gu felt all the more obliged to speak frankly. “Strictly speaking, we cannot blame Old Mi altogether.
最后这句话，隐然有着责备的意思，使得古应春更觉得该据实倾诉∶『说起来也不能怪老宓，

He has his own difficulties—” “So it is he!” she interrupted.
他有他的难处——』『是他！』螺蛳太太插进去说，

“I began to suspect that the other person making remarks might be him.
『我刚就有点疑心，说闲话的旁人，只怕是他，

And sure enough!
果不其然。

How is he doing at Fukang?”
他在阜康怎么样。』

“I know nothing about his affairs at Fukang.
『他在阜康有情形我不清楚，

I can speak only of my own.
我只谈我自己。

I cannot understand where I have offended Old Mi, but he seems bent on making things difficult for me at every turn.”
我也弄不懂是什么地方得罪了老宓，有点处处跟我为难的味道。』

The reason the purchase of the modern mills had failed to go through was that Mi Benchang was obstructing it openly and sabotaging it in secret.
原来，收买新式缫丝厂一事，所以未成，即由于宓本常明处掣肘、暗处破坏之故。

He had spread the word that Hu Xueyan had no intention of running such mills.
他放了风声出去，说胡雪岩并无意办新式缫丝厂，

Gu, he said, had made a tremendous loss in property and was now speculating in purchases and sales he could not finance, hoping to conjure up money to fill the hole.
是古应春在做房地产的生意上扯了一个大窟窿，所以买空卖空，希图无中生有，来弥补他的亏空。

Any mill owner wishing to sell would do better to find another buyer; otherwise he would end up with nothing and miss his opportunity through his own mistake.
如果有缫丝厂想出让，最好另找主顾；否则到头来一场空，自误时机。


These reports left people divided between belief and doubt.
这话使人将信将疑，

In Gu’s favour was the fact that he was no obscure figure in Shanghai business, and his credit was good.
信的是古应春在上海商场上不是无名小卒，信用也很好。

That he had emerged unscathed from his failed partnership with Xu Yuzhai showed that he was a man of more than ordinary resources.
只看他跟徐愚斋合作失败，而居然能安然无事，便见得他不是等闲之辈了。

Against him stood the fact that his circumstances were indeed poor.
疑的是，古应春的境况确实不佳；

Still harder to understand was why Hu Xueyan, who had always opposed modern silk-reeling, should abruptly change course.
而更使人觉得不可思议的是，胡雪岩一向反对新式缫丝，何以忽然改弦易辙？

Everyone knew how highly Hu valued keeping his word.
大家都知道，胡雪岩看重的一件事是∶说话算话。

No one could recall an occasion on which he had gone back on it.
大家都想不起来，他做过什么出尔反尔的事。

Gu’s negotiations consequently became laborious: the other side was merely humouring him.
因为如此，古应春跟人家谈判，便很吃力了，因为对方是抱着虚与委蛇的态度。

Nevertheless, so long as there was no clear reason for an outright rupture, he had to continue, however difficult the talks, and sooner or later they were bound to yield some preliminary result.
当然只要没有明显的决裂的理由，尽管谈判吃力，总还要谈下去，而且迟早会谈出一个初步的结果。


At this time Gu was negotiating with Huang Zuoqing, the owner of Gongheyong.
其时古应春谈判的目标是公和永的东主黄佐卿。

Huang had built his mill at the same time as Jardine Matheson and Gongping Trading House had built theirs.
他跟怡和、公平两洋行，同时建厂，

All three were of much the same size, with over a hundred reeling machines apiece, bought from Italy and France.
规模大小相仿，都有上百部的丝车，买的是意大利跟法国的丝车；

Liu Hefu, Gongping’s comprador, proposed that the three jointly engage an engineer.
公平洋行的买办叫刘和甫，提议三厂共同延请一名工程师，

Huang agreed, and Liu hired an Italian named Maidengsi to supervise operations and train the workers.
黄佐卿同意了，由刘和甫经手，聘请了一个意大利人麦登斯来指导厂务、训练工人，

He was technically capable but a man of very bad character; his chief failing was his appetite for women.
此人技术不错，可是人品甚坏，最大的毛病是好色。

Most mill hands then were women, known as the Silk Sisters.
原来那时的工人，以女工居多，称之为『湖丝阿姐』。

Women from modest families who needed to help support their households had generally gone into domestic service.
小家碧玉为了帮助家计，大多以帮佣为主；

Those who did paid piecework took the materials home: embroidery and the pasting of tinfoil were older trades, while making matchboxes and sewing uniforms were newer ones.
做工是领了材料到家来做，旧式的如绣花、糊锡箔；新式的如糊火柴匣子、缝军服。

But the Silk Sisters, gathering in groups at the sound of the factory whistle and making their conspicuous way through the streets, were something wholly new.
但做『湖丝阿姐』，汽笛一响，成群结队，招摇而过，却是前所未有，

Watching them go to and from work became a public spectacle.
因而看湖丝阿姐上工、放工，成了一景。

These young women grew accustomed to appearing in public and naturally became much freer in speech and manner.
这些年轻妇女，抛头露面惯了，行动言语之间，自然开通得多；

The distinction between an open manner and loose morals, however, was little more than a matter of getting into bed; in time, breaches of convention became common.
而放荡与开通不过上下床之别，久而久之便常有荡检逾闲的情事出现；

The male workers, being nearest at hand, enjoyed the first opportunities, and many formed liaisons, particularly with young widows.
至于男工，『近水楼台先得月』，尤其是『小寡妇』，搭上手的很多。

There were benefits on both sides, of course.
当然这是『互惠』的，

A woman with a male worker to support her would not be bullied; if her protector was a foreman, the advantages were greater still.
女工有个男工作靠山，就不会受人欺侮；倘或靠山是个工头，好处更多，

At the least, she might be transferred to an easier department.
起码可以调到工作轻松的部门。

Conversely, a foreman whose wishes were denied could use his official authority to avenge a private grievance, assigning a woman to the worst work of all in the reeling room.
相对的，工头倘或所欲不遂，便可假公济私来作报复，调到最苦的缫丝间，

There boiling water and steam created a perpetual midsummer.
沸水热汽，终年如盛暑；

Even midsummer had an occasional breeze; the reeling room was both hot and airless.
盛暑偶尔还有风，缫丝间又热又闷，

Before one had been inside for the time it took to eat a meal, one was drenched from head to foot.
一进去要不了一顿饭的工夫，浑身就会湿透，

Men could strip to the waist and wear shorts; women had to remain in their soaking clothes.
男工可以打赤膊，着短裤，女工就只好着一件『湿布衫』，

Once the machinery started, it ran for twelve hours, and they had to wear those scorching, wet garments all day.
机器一开就是十二个钟头，这件火热的『湿布衫』就得穿一整天。

Summer was bearable enough.
夏天还好，

In winter, when the shift ended and the cold wind struck, the wet clothing became an iron shell.
冬天散工，冷风一吹，『湿布衫』变成『铁衣』，

That this should cause illness was hardly surprising.
因而致病，不足为奇，

After visiting a reeling room, one foreign journalist called it “a veritable hell on earth.”
所以有个洋记者参观过缫丝间以后，称之为『名副其实的活地狱』。


If a foreman could wield such power, an engineer could lord it over the workers even more easily.
工头如此，工程师自然更可作威作福，

Maidengsi treated the sexual exploitation of the Silk Sisters as his rightful privilege, using unscrupulous foremen to satisfy his demands.
麦登斯便视蹂躏湖丝阿姐为他应享的权利，利用不肖工头，予取予求，

Huang Zuoqing frequently received complaints and asked Liu Hefu to warn him.
黄佐卿时常接到申诉，要求刘和甫警告麦登斯，

He would improve for a few days, then soon revert to his old behaviour.
稍为好几天，很快地复萌故态，

After several repetitions, Huang could bear it no longer and decided to dismiss him.
如是几次以后，黄佐卿忍无可忍，打算解雇麦登斯，

He then discovered that Liu had signed a deeply disadvantageous contract: dismissal would require a huge payment in compensation.
哪知刘和甫跟人家订了一张非常吃亏的合约，倘或解雇须付出巨额的赔偿。

Huang was thoroughly discouraged.
为此黄佐卿大为沮丧，

With business poor as well, he finally decided to sell Gongheyong to Gu Yingchun.
加以生意又不好做，才决定将公和永盘让给古应春。
