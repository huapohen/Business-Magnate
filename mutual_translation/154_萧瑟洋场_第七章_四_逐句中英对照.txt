胡雪岩 · 萧瑟洋场
The Desolate Foreign Settlement
第七章（本章第4部分，共6部分）
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


条件都谈好了，厂房、生财、存货八万银子『一脚踢』。
All the terms had been settled: the buildings, equipment, and stock were to go for a lump sum of eighty thousand taels.

古应春便通知宓本常，照数开出银票；
Gu Yingchun therefore notified Mi Benchang to issue a bank draft for the full amount.

哪知所得的回答是∶『不便照拨。』
The answer, to his astonishment, was that the funds could not be released.

『怎么？』古应春诧异，『不是有「的款」存在那里的吗？』
“What?” he asked. “Was there not a sum specifically set aside for this?”

当初汇丰借出来的五十万银子，除了左宗棠所借的二十万以外，
Of the original five hundred thousand taels borrowed from HSBC, two hundred thousand had been lent to Zuo Zongtang.

余数由胡雪岩指明，借给尤五出面所办的茧行，作为收买新式缫丝厂之用，
Hu Xueyan had expressly designated the remainder as a loan to the cocoon firm headed by Brother You, to finance the purchase of modern silk-reeling mills.

这一点宓本常并不否认，但他有他的说法。
Mi did not deny this, but he had his own explanation.


『应春兄，「死店活人开」，
“Brother Yingchun, a shop may be dead, but the man who runs it must be alive.

大先生是有那样子一句话，
The Master did say that, certainly.

不过我做档手的，如果只会听他的话，象算盘珠一样，他拨一拨、我动一动，我就不是活人，
But if, as his manager, I can do nothing except obey him—if I am like a bead on an abacus, moving only when he pushes me—then I am hardly a living man.

只不过比死人多口气。
I have only one breath more than a corpse.

你说是不是呢？』
Would you not agree?”

古应春倒抽一口冷气，
Gu drew in a sharp breath.

结结巴巴说∶『你的话不错，大先生的话也要算数。』
“You—you are right,” he stammered, “but the Master’s word must count for something too.”

『我不是说不算数，
“I am not saying it counts for nothing.

是现在没有钱，
I am saying there is no money at present.

有，钱又不是我的，我为啥不给你。』
If there were, why should I withhold it? It is not mine.”

『这钱怎么会没有？
“How can the money be gone?

指明了做这个用途的。』
It was expressly assigned to this purpose.”

『不错，指明了作这个用途的。
“Quite true.

不过，应春兄，你要替我想一想，更要替大先生想一想。
But you must consider my position, Brother Yingchun—and the Master’s even more.

几次谈到缫丝厂的事，你总说「难，难，
Every time we discussed the silk mills, you said, ‘Difficult, difficult!

不晓得啥辰光才会成功？」
Who knows when anything will come of it?’

如果你说∶快谈成功了，十天半个月就要付款，我自然会把你这笔款子留下来。
If you had told me, ‘The agreement is almost settled; payment will be due in ten days or a fortnight,’ naturally I should have kept the money for you.

你自己都没有握，怎么能怪我？』
When you yourself had no certainty, how can you blame me?”

『你不必管我有没有把握，
“My certainty is no concern of yours.

指明了给我的，你就要留下来。』
Money expressly assigned to me ought to have been kept for me.”


这话很不客气；
This was distinctly brusque.

宓本常冷笑一声说道∶『如果那时候你请大先生马上交代，照数拨给你，另外立个折子，算是你的存款，我就没有资格用你这笑钱。
Mi gave a cold laugh. “Had you asked the Master then and there to order the sum transferred, with a separate passbook opened so that it became your deposit, I should have had no right to use it.

没有归到你名下以前，钱是阜康的。
Until it was entered in your name, however, the money belonged to Fukang.

阜康的钱是大先生所有；不过阜康的钱归我宓某所管。
Fukang’s money belongs to the Master, but its management belongs to me.

受人之禄，忠人之事，
A man who takes another’s pay must serve his interests faithfully.

银根这么紧，我不把这笔钱拿来活用；只为远在杭州的大先生的一句话，把这笔钱死死守住，等你不知道哪天来用，
With credit as tight as it is, am I to leave that money idle merely because of a word from the Master, far away in Hangzhou, and guard it until some unknown day when you may want it?

你说有没有这个道理？』
Would that make sense?”

这几句话真是将古应春驳得体无完肤，
These arguments left Gu without a leg to stand on.

他不能跟他辩，也不想跟他辩了。
He could not argue further and no longer wished to.


可是宓本常却还有话∶『你晓得的，大先生的生意愈做愈大，就是因为一个钱要做八个钱、十个钱的生意。
Mi, however, had more to say. “You know how the Master’s business keeps expanding: he makes one coin do the business of eight or ten.

大先生常常说∶』八个坛子七个盖，盖来盖去不穿帮，就是会做生意。
He is always saying, ‘Eight jars and seven lids—keep moving the lids without letting anyone see an uncovered jar, and you know how to do business.’

「以现在市面上的现款来说，岂止八个坛子七个盖？
With the cash available in the market today, we have nothing like seven lids for eight jars.

顶多只有一半，
At most we have half as many lids as jars.

我要把他搞得不穿帮，哪里是件容易的事。
Do you think it is easy to keep everything covered?

老兄，我请问你，
Let me ask you this, my dear fellow.

今天有人来提款，库房里只有那二十几万银子，我不拿来应付，
If a depositor came to withdraw his money today, and the only cash in the vault was that two hundred thousand taels and more, should I refuse to use it?

莫非跟客户说∶那笔银子不能动，是为古先生留在那里收买缫丝厂用的？
Should I tell him, ‘That money cannot be touched; it is being kept for Mr Gu to buy silk mills’?

古先生啊古先生，我老宓跟你，到那时候，不要说本来就是阜康的钱，哪怕是两江总督衙门的官款，明天要提了去给兄弟们关饷，我都要动用。
My dear Mr Gu, at such a moment I tell you I would use not merely Fukang’s own money, but even official funds belonging to the Governor-General of Liangjiang, due to be withdrawn tomorrow to pay the troops.

客户这一关过不去，马上就有挤兑的风潮，
Fail to satisfy our customers and we shall have a run on the bank at once.

大先生就完完大吉了。
That would finish the Master.”

『』四姐，老宓的说法，只要是真的，就算不肯帮我忙，我亦没话说。
“Sister Luo,” Gu went on with his account, “if what Old Mi said was true, then even if he would not help me, I had nothing to complain of.

因为虽然都是为小爷叔办事，各有各的权限，各有各的难处，
We both work for Little Uncle, but each of us has his own authority and his own difficulties.

我不能怪他。
I could not blame him.”

『』那末，『螺蛳太太立即钉一句∶』你现在是怪他罗？
“Then,” she immediately pressed him, “you do blame him now?”

『古应春老实答道∶』是的。
“Yes,” Gu admitted frankly.

有一点。
“A little.”

『』这样说起来，是老宓没有说真话！不然你就不会怪他。
“In that case Old Mi cannot have been telling the truth, or you would not blame him.

『螺蛳太太问道∶』他那几句话不真？『
Which part of what he said was untrue?”


『还不是头寸。』
“The part about the shortage of funds, of course.”

话到此处，古应春如箭在弦，不发不可，
Gu had now drawn the bow and could no longer hold back the arrow.

『他头寸是调得过来的，
“He could have arranged the funds.

而且指定了收买缫丝厂的那笔款子，根本没有动，
Moreover, the money earmarked for purchasing the mills has never been touched.

仍旧在汇丰银行。』
It is still at HSBC.”

一听这话，螺蛳太太动容了，
Madam Luosi’s expression changed.

『姐夫，』她问，『你怎么知道他没有动过？』
“Brother-in-law, how do you know he has not touched it?”

『我听人说的。』
“Someone told me.”

『是哪个？』
“Who?”

『这——』古应春答说∶『四姐，你不必问了。
“Well—Sister Luo, please do not ask.

我的消息很靠得住。』
My information is thoroughly reliable.”

螺蛳太太有些明白了，
She thought she understood.

阜康管总帐的周小棠，跟宓本常不甚和睦，
Zhou Xiaotang, who kept Fukang’s general accounts, was not on particularly good terms with Mi Benchang.

也许是他透露的消息。
Perhaps he had let the information out.


『姐夫要我不问，我就不问。
“If you would rather I did not ask, I shall not.

不过我倒要问姐夫，这件事现在怎么办？』
But there is something else I must ask: what are you going to do now?”

『收买缫丝厂的事，已经不必再谈了。
“There is no longer any point in discussing the purchase.

现在就有八万银子，也买不成功；
Even with eighty thousand taels in hand, I could not buy the mill now.

人家黄佐卿看我拿不出现银，另外寻了个户头，卖了九万五千银子。』
When Huang Zuoqing saw that I could not produce the cash, he found another buyer and sold it for ninety-five thousand.”

古应春说到这里，摇一摇头，脸色非常难看，
Gu shook his head, his face dark with distress.

『四姐，我顶难过的是，在上海滩上混了几十年，听了一句教人要吐血的话。』
“What hurts me most, Sister Luo, is that after all my decades in Shanghai, I had to hear something that could make a man spit blood.”

『噢！』
“Oh!”

螺蛳太太大为同情，
Her sympathy was immediate.

『你说了出来，
“Tell me what he said.

我来替你出气。』
I shall get satisfaction for you.”

『出气？』
“Satisfaction?”

古应春连连摇头，
Gu shook his head repeatedly.

『那一来变成「窝里反」了，
“That would mean fighting among ourselves.

不好「不好。』
No, no, that would never do.”

『就算我不响，你也要说出来；
“Even if I say nothing about it, you must tell me.

心里有委屈，说出来就舒服。』
Speaking about an injustice makes it easier to bear.”

古应春沉吟了说∶『好，
Gu hesitated. “Very well.

我说。
I shall tell you.

那天——』
That day—”


那天——螺蛳太太到上海的前两天，黄佐卿发了个帖子请古应春吃花酒。
That day—two days before Madam Luosi arrived in Shanghai—Huang Zuoqing had sent Gu an invitation to a banquet with courtesans.

买卖不成，朋友还是朋友，古应春准时赴约；
Though their business had fallen through, they were still friends, and Gu arrived punctually.

场面很热闹，黄佐卿请了有近二十位的客，
It was a lively gathering, with nearly twenty guests.

两桌麻将，一桌牌九，打了上千大洋的头。
Two tables of mahjong and one of pai gow yielded over a thousand silver dollars in table fees.

接下来吃花酒，摆的是『双双台』；
The banquet that followed was arranged with courtesans seated alongside the guests.

客人连叫来的局，不下五十人之多，
Counting the women summoned to join them, there were at least fifty people.

须将整楼三个大房间打通，才摆得下四桌酒。
The partitions between three large upstairs rooms had to be opened before there was room for four dining tables.


主客便是收买公和永的潮州帮『鸦片大王』陈和森；
The guest of honour was the purchaser of Gongheyong, Chen Hesen, the Chaozhou merchants’ “Opium King.”

古应春也被邀在这一桌坐。
Gu was seated at the same table.

笙歌嗷嘈之余，黄佐卿举杯向古应春说道∶『应春兄，我特为要敬你一杯酒；
Amid the clamour of music and singing, Huang raised his cup to him. “Brother Yingchun, I particularly want to drink to you.

如果十天之前不是你头寸不便，我就不会跟「陈大王」谈公和永，也就少卖一万五千银子了。
Had you not been short of funds ten days ago, I should never have discussed Gongheyong with King Chen, and I should have received fifteen thousand taels less.

说起来这一万五千两，是你老哥挑我赚的，
In a manner of speaking, it was you who helped me make that extra fifteen thousand.

我是不是应该敬杯酒。』说完哈哈大笑，管自己干了酒。
Ought I not to drink to you?” He laughed heartily and drained his cup.

讲完了这一段，古应春又说∶『四姐，你想，这不是他存心给我难堪？
Having related this, Gu added, “Sister Luo, was he not deliberately trying to humiliate me?

当时，我真正是眼泪往肚子里流。』
I truly had to swallow my tears.”

螺蛳太太亦为他难过，更为他不平，
She grieved for him and felt the injustice even more keenly.

『这件事，大先生晓不晓得？』她问。
“Does the Master know about this?” she asked.


『这件事，我怎么好告诉大先生？
“How could I bring myself to tell him such a thing?

不过收买公和永不成这一节，我已经写信给大先生了。』
But I have written to him about the failure to buy Gongheyong.”

『我在杭州没有听说。』
“I heard nothing of it in Hangzhou.”

古应春想了一下说∶『算起来你从杭州动身的时候，我的信还没有到。』
Gu considered a moment. “My letter probably had not arrived when you left.”


『好！这一节就不去谈它了。
“Very well, we shall say no more about that.

至于老宓勒住银不放，有意跟你作对，这件事我一定要问问他。』
But as for Old Mi holding back the money and deliberately obstructing you, I must certainly question him.”

『不！』古应春说∶『请四姐一定要顾大局，
“No!” Gu exclaimed. “Please think of the larger situation, Sister Luo.

现在局势不大好，全靠大家同心协力，
Things are difficult at present; everything depends on our working together.

你一问他，必生是非，
If you question him, it is bound to stir up trouble.

无论如何请你摆在心里。』
Whatever you do, please keep this to yourself.”

『你晓得的，我也同七姐一样，
“You know I am like Seventh Sister.

有不平的事，摆在心里，饭都吃不下的。』
If I keep an injustice bottled up, I cannot even eat.

螺蛳太太说∶『我只要不「卖原告」，他哪里知道我的消息是哪里来的。
So long as I do not give away the complainant, how can he know where my information came from?”


看她态度非常坚决，古应春知道无法打消她的意向；
Seeing her determination, Gu knew he could not dissuade her.

考虑了一会说∶『四姐，你以为不提我的名字，他就不会疑心到我，那是自己骗自己。
After some thought he said, “Sister Luo, if you think that merely leaving out my name will keep him from suspecting me, you are deceiving yourself.

你总要有个合情理的说法，才可以瞒得过他。』
You need a plausible explanation if you are to put him off the scent.”

『你讲，
“Tell me, then.

应该怎么个说法？』
What explanation should I give?”

『在汇丰银行，你有没有认识的人？』
“Do you know anyone at HSBC?”

螺蛳太太想了一下说道∶『有个张纪通，
She thought for a moment. “There is a Zhang Jitong.

好象是汇丰银行的。』
I believe he works there.”

『不错，张纪通是汇丰银行「二写」。』
“Yes, he is HSBC’s second clerk.

古应春问∶『四姐跟他熟？』
Do you know him well?”

『他太太，我们从前是小姊妹。
“His wife and I were friends as girls.

去年还特为到杭州来看过我。』
She made a special trip to Hangzhou to see me last year.”

『好！
“Good.

那就有说法了。
Then you have your explanation.

四姐，你如果一定问这件事，见了老宓就这样子说∶你说，古应春告诉我，阜康的头寸紧得不得了；
If you insist on questioning Old Mi, say this when you see him: ‘Gu Yingchun tells me that Fukang is desperately short of funds.

可是，我听张政通的太太说∶阜康有廿几万银子，一直存在汇丰没有动过。
Yet I hear from Mrs Zhang Jitong that Fukang has more than two hundred thousand taels sitting untouched at HSBC.’

看他怎么说？』
See what he says to that.”


『我懂了，
“I understand.

我会说得一点不露马脚；
I shall give nothing away.

明天早晨我先去看张太太，做得象真的一样。
Tomorrow morning I shall first call on Mrs Zhang, to make the story look genuine.

我看他一定没话可说；
I am sure he will have no answer.

那时候我再埋怨他几句，替你出气。』
Then I can reproach him a little and get satisfaction for you.”

『出气这两个字，不必谈它。』
“Please leave satisfaction out of it.”

『好，
“Very well.

不谈出气，谈你圆房。』
Let us leave that and discuss your wedding night.”

螺蛳太太急转直下地说∶『这件事就算不为你，也不为瑞香，为了七姐，你也要趁我在这里，请我吃这杯喜酒。』古应春终于答应了。
She changed the subject with startling abruptness. “Even if you will do nothing for your own sake or Ruixiang’s, you must do this for Seventh Sister: hold the wedding while I am here, so that I may drink a cup at your celebration.” Gu finally agreed.

于是螺蛳太太便将与七姑奶奶商量好的计划，一一说知；
Madam Luosi then explained, point by point, the arrangements she had settled with the Seventh Lady.

事到如今，古应春除了唯唯称是以外，别无话说。
Matters had gone so far that Gu could only murmur his assent.


第二天早饭既毕，螺蛳太太便催瑞香瑞得出门。
The next morning, as soon as breakfast was over, Madam Luosi urged Ruixiang to get ready to go out.

这是前一天晚上就说好了；
This had been agreed the previous evening.

但瑞香因为一出门便是一整天，有好些琐屑家务要安排好，因而耽误了工夫，
But since they would be away all day, Ruixiang had a number of small household matters to arrange, and these delayed her.

七姑奶奶帮着一催再催，
The Seventh Lady joined in urging her to hurry.

快到不耐烦时，方始相偕登车，看表上已经十一点了。
They were almost out of patience before the two women finally entered the carriage together; a glance at the watch showed that it was already eleven.

『刚刚当着七姑奶奶，我不好说，我催你是有道理的，
“I could not explain in front of the Seventh Lady just now, but I had a reason for hurrying you.

先要到张太太家去一趟，稍为坐一坐到阜康去开银票。
We were to call briefly on Mrs Zhang, then go to Fukang to get the bank drafts.

现在，辰光不对了，
The timing is all wrong now.

吃中饭的时候去了，一定留住；
If we arrive at lunchtime, she is sure to keep us for the meal.

下半天等去了阜康，就办不成事了。
By the time we have been to Fukang in the afternoon, we shall get nothing done.

看首饰不能心急；不然十之八九要后悔。
One must take one’s time choosing jewellery, or nine times out of ten one regrets it.

现在，没法子，张家只好不去了。』
We shall have to miss the Zhangs.”

『都是我不好。』瑞香陪笑说道∶『太太何不早跟我说一句。』
“It is all my fault,” Ruixiang said with an apologetic smile. “Why did you not tell me earlier, Madam?”


『我也不晓得你这么会磨！
“I did not know you could dawdle so!

摸东摸西，忘记掉辰光。
Fiddling with this and that until you forget the time.

喔！』螺蛳太太特为关照∶『回头我同宓先生说，我们是从张家来，你不要多说什么，免得拆穿西洋镜。』瑞香答应着，随同螺蛳太太坐轿子到了阜康；
Oh—and remember: when I tell Mr Mi that we have come from the Zhangs, do not say anything that will give the game away.” Ruixiang agreed and accompanied her by sedan chair to Fukang.

宓本常自然奉如上宾，
Mi Benchang naturally treated Madam Luosi as an honoured guest.

他的礼貌很周到，从胡老太太起，胡家全家，——问到。
His courtesies were meticulous: beginning with Old Madam Hu, he asked after every member of the family in turn.

接下来又敷衍瑞香，笑嘻嘻地问道∶『瑞姑娘，哪天请我们吃喜酒？』
He then turned his attentions to Ruixiang and asked with a broad smile, “Miss Rui, when are you going to invite us to your wedding feast?”


瑞香红着脸不答；
Ruixiang blushed and said nothing.

螺蛳太太接口∶『快了，快了！』
“Soon, soon!” Madam Luosi answered for her.

她说∶『今天就是为此到钱庄来的，
“That is why we have come to the bank today.

我想支两千银子，
I should like to withdraw two thousand taels.

七姑奶奶也有个折子在这。』
I also have the Seventh Lady’s passbook here.”

取出七姑奶奶的折子来一看，存银四千五百余两，
She took it out and found a balance of more than four thousand five hundred taels.

螺蛳太太作主，也提二千，一共是四千银子，关照宓本常开出数目大小不等的十来张银票，
On her own authority, she withdrew another two thousand from that account, making four thousand in all, and asked Mi to issue a dozen or so bank drafts in assorted amounts.

点收清楚，要谈古应春的事了。
Once she had counted and received them, she was ready to raise Gu’s affairs.

『宓先生，』她闲闲问说∶『这一晌，上海市面怎么样？』
“Mr Mi,” she asked casually, “how has business been in Shanghai lately?”

『不好，不好！
“Bad, bad!

银根愈来愈紧了。』
Money grows tighter every day.”

『我们阜康呢？』
“And at our Fukang?”

『当然也紧。』
“Tight here too, of course.”


『既然紧，』螺蛳太太摆出一脸困惑的神情，『为啥我们有廿几万银子摆在汇丰银行，动都不动？』

Her husband’s name is Zhang Jitong.”

Zhang Jitong was a friend of his; they had played cards together only the previous evening.

“Mr Mi, you must not talk like that!
