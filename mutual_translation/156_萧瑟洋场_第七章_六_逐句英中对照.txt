“It is a happy occasion.
原是一桩喜事，

There is no point in giving people something unpleasant to say about it.”
落了些不中听的闲话，就犯不着了。

Mi’s words reminded Gu of a danger.
『这话倒提醒古应春了。

The Seventh Lady detested gossip.
七姑奶奶是最讨厌闲言闲语的，

If the celebration was too modest, someone might say that Gu dared not make a fuss over taking a concubine because his wife would be jealous.
场面过于俭朴，就可能会有人说∶』古应春不敢铺张；因为讨小老婆的场面太热闹了，大老婆会吃醋。

Should that reach her ears, she would make herself ill with anger.
『倘或有这样的一种说法，传到七姑奶奶耳朵里，她会气得发病。

This was serious.
这是非同小可的一件事，

Grateful for the timely warning, Gu clasped his hands. “You have my interests entirely at heart, Mi, and I must listen.
古应春很感谢宓本常能适时提醒，让他有此警惕。因而拱着手说∶『老宓，你完全是爱护我的意思，我不敢不听，

Still, with only two days to prepare, we must observe some limits.”
不过到底只有两天的工夫预备，也只好适可而止。』

“Certainly.
『当然、当然，

Everything must be ready in time.
一定要来得及。

First we need a guest list.
现在第一件要紧的是，把请客的单子拟出来。

Your acquaintance is wide: at least eight or ten tables.
你的交游一向很广，起码也要请个十桌八桌，

I think we shall need another venue.”
我看要另外借地方。』

“No!
『不，不！

Then there would be no end to it.
那一来就没有止境了。

The number must depend on the space in my house.”
请客多少只能看舍间地方大小而定。』

They calculated carefully.
于是细细估量，

Including the inner and outer reception rooms, study, and sitting room, there was room for about seven tables.
将内外客厅、书房、起坐间都算上，大概只能摆七桌，

Provisionally they allotted five to men and two to women.
初步决定五桌男客，两桌女客。

“A canopy over the courtyard would give us four more,” Mi said, “but then there would be nowhere for the entertainment. Very well, seven tables.
『本来天井里搭篷，还可以摆四桌，那一来「堂会」就没地方了。』宓本常说∶『好，准定七桌，

You make the list; my staff will write the invitations.
名单你开，帖子我叫我那里的人来写，

They must go out by tomorrow afternoon at the latest.
至晚明天下午一定要发出。

Where shall we order the food?”
菜呢，你看用哪里的菜？』


“Choose for me.
请你斟酌，

So long as it is good.”
只要好就好。

“Good and reasonably priced.
『』不但要好，还要便宜。

The guests will arrive around four or five.
『宓本常又问∶』客人是下半天四五点钟前后就来了，

The entertainment must begin at four and finish at nine, so we shall need three acts.
堂会准定四点钟开场，到晚上九点钟歇锣，总要三档节目；

Which would you like?”
应春兄，你看，用哪三档？

“I know little about that.
『』此道我亦是外行，

Please arrange it.”
请你费心提调。

Mi considered. “First, a programme of ballad-storytelling from Suzhou’s Guangyu Society.
『』我看？『宓本常一面想，一面说∶』先来档苏州光裕社的小书；

Then magic: the Japanese conjuror Tianshengniang is back, and I shall book her today.
接下来弄一档魔术，日本的女魔术师天胜娘又来了，我今天就去定好了；

The finale can be Dongxiang opera.
压轴戏是「东乡调大戏」，

That is very lively.” Gu agreed and left everything to him.
蛮热闹的。『古应春称是，都由宓本常作主。

After Mi departed, Gu told the Seventh Lady what they had decided.
等他告辞而去，古应春将所作的决定告诉七姑奶奶，

She had objections.
她却颇有意见。

“I do not think we should invite women.
『我看堂客不要请。』她说，

Even if invited, they may not come.”
『请了，人家也未见得肯来。』

Ordinarily, women were not invited to a celebration of taking a concubine unless the man was an elderly patriarch surrounded by children and grandchildren.
本来纳宠请女客，除非是儿孙满堂的老封翁，

The younger women of his family would then have to offer congratulations and pay their respects from filial duty.
晚辈内眷为了一尽孝心，不能不来贺喜见礼；

Otherwise such invitations were rare.
否则便很少有请女客的。

Shanghai might be more liberal, but jealousy was still regarded as natural to women, and the distinction between wife and concubine remained strict.
上海虽比较开通，但吃醋毕竟是妇人天性，而嫡庶之分，又看得极重；

Women close to the Seventh Lady would certainly stay away in protest.
如果是与七姑奶奶交好的，一定会作抵制。

Gu realized that he had agreed too thoughtlessly.
古应春觉得自己同意请女客，确是有欠思量。


“Besides, I cannot get about to act as hostess, and it would be awkward to trouble Sister Luo to entertain on my behalf.
『再说，我行动不便，没法子作主人；更不便劳动四姐代我应酬。』

If a few women feel comfortable coming, by all means let them.
七姑奶奶又说∶『如果有几位堂客觉得无所谓的，尽管请过来；

We can treat it as an ordinary informal visit, without ceremony, so that everyone feels at ease.
我们亦就象平常来往一样不拘礼数，主客双方都心安，

That is different from issuing special invitations.
这跟特为下帖子是不同的。

Do you agree?”
你说是不是呢？』

“Entirely,” Gu readily conceded. “We shall not invite women formally.”
『完全不错。』古应春从善如流地答说∶『不请堂客。』

“As for some lively entertainment, I approve. It will give Sister Luo a pleasant day too.
『至于堂会热闹热闹；顺便也算请四姐玩一天，我赞成。

But omit the Dongxiang opera.”
不过，东乡调可以免了。』

Dongxiang was a form of flower-drum opera originating in Pudong, hence its name, “eastern district tunes.”
原来东乡调是『花鼓戏』的一种，发源于浦东，所以称为『东乡调』，

It was also called bentan, short for local tanhuang.
又名『本滩』是『本地滩簧』的简称。

Its lyrics were coarse, and the combination of singing and acting was extremely bawdy, which made it popular.
曲词卑俚，但连唱带做，淫治异常，所以颇具号召力，

In rural Pudong, whole villages would turn out for night performances by kerosene light.
浦东乡下，点起火油灯唱东乡调的夜台戏，真有倾村来观之盛。

It was hardly suitable for a refined gathering.
但却难登大雅之堂。

“‘Those two breasts go jiggle-jiggle,’” the Seventh Lady sang in imitation.
『「两只奶奶抖勒抖」，』七姑奶奶学唱了一句东乡调说，

“How could we invite Sister Luo to watch that?”
『这种戏，怎么好请四姐来看？』

Her performance made Gu laugh despite himself; even the servants covered their mouths and giggled.
看她学唱东乡调的样子，不但古应春忍俊不禁，连下人都掩着嘴笑了。


“What shall we have instead?”
『不唱东乡调，唱啥呢？』

“Hangzhou tanhuang.
『杭州滩簧，

It is elegant, with strings and singing.
文文气气，又弹又唱，

They say it dates from the Song dynasty and was even admitted to the palace.
说是宋朝传下来，当时连宫里都准去唱的。

That would be best for Sister Luo.
为了请四姐，杭州滩簧最好；

Inquire tomorrow: if there is a troupe in Shanghai, ask it to perform.”
明天倒去打听打听，如果上海有，叫一班来听听。』

“Very well.”
『好！』

Gu considered. “Even without women guests, you cannot get about, and Sister Luo is a visitor. We ought to ask one or two women to help.”
古应春想了下说∶『堂客虽不请，不过你行动不便，四姐可是作客总要请一两个来帮忙吧！

“Ask Mrs Wang.”
『请王师母好了。』

Her husband, Wang Zhongwen, was Gu’s pupil.
王师母的丈夫王仲文是古应春的学生，

He served as a church clerk and also took pupils in English, so his wife was called shimu, “teacher’s wife.”
在教堂里当司事，也收学生教英文，所以称的他的妻子为『师母』，

The Seventh Lady used that title too.
七姑奶奶也是这样叫她。

Yet as the wife of Wang’s own teacher, the Seventh Lady was in turn Mrs Wang’s shimu.
但七姑奶奶却不折不扣地是王师母的『师母，』

Anyone first hearing their forms of address was apt to be thoroughly confused.
因此，初次听她们彼此的称呼，往往大惑不解。


Madam Luosi was no exception.
螺师太太即是如此，

When Mrs Wang arrived that day and was introduced, she heard the young woman respectfully say, “Shimu, you look better again than you did a little while ago.”
那天王师母来了，七姑奶奶为她引见，又听王师母恭恭敬敬地说∶『师母这两天的气色，比前一晌又好得多了。』

Madam Luosi could not resist asking, “Which of you is whose shimu?”
便忍不住要问。『你们两位到底哪个是哪个的师母？』

“Naturally, Shimu is my shimu.
『自然是师母是我的师母；

I have asked Shimu not to call me Young Mrs Wang, but Shimu will not listen.
我请师母不要叫我小王师母，师母不听，

Once I deliberately failed to answer Shimu, and Shimu was angry, so I had to let Shimu go on calling me Young Mrs Wang.”
有一回我特为不理师母，师母生气了，只好仍旧听师母叫我小王师母。』

The torrent of shimu sounded like a tongue-twister.
一片叽叽喳喳的师母声，倒象在说绕口令；

Madam Luosi found the round-faced woman, about twenty-five or twenty-six, warm and agreeable.
螺蛳太太看她二十五六岁年纪，生就一张圆圆脸，觉得亲切可喜，

They quickly felt as though they had known each other for years.
自然而然地便熟悉得不象初见了。


She was especially relieved by the easy affection between Young Mrs Wang and Ruixiang.
尤其是看到小王师母与瑞香相处融洽的情形，更觉欣慰。

Ruixiang was glad to have her future settled, yet on this auspicious day she felt the anxiety and fear common to a bride.
原来瑞香虽喜终身有托，但在好日子的这一天，跟一般新嫁娘一样，总不免有凄惶恐惧之感，

Madam Luosi and the Seventh Lady treated her well, but one was her former mistress and the other now the principal wife.
更因是螺蛳太太与七姑奶奶虽都待她不坏，但一个是从前的主母，一个是现在的大妇，

She had always been reserved with them, and today dared reveal still less of her feelings, lest they think her frivolous or ungrateful.
平时本就拘谨，这一天更不敢吐露内心的感觉，怕她们在心里会骂她『轻狂不识抬举』。

Fortunately, the familiar and warm-hearted Young Mrs Wang attended to her needs with the care of a sister.
幸而有热心而相熟的小王师母殷勤照料，不时嘘寒问暖，竟如同亲姊妹一般；

Only then did Ruixiang’s anxious heart settle and smiles begin to appear.
瑞香一直悬着的一颗心才能踏实，脸上也开始有笑容了。

Madam Luosi’s feelings were complicated.
在螺蛳太太，心情非常复杂，

Towards Ruixiang she felt something of a mother marrying off her daughter, but her friendship with the Gu household mattered still more.
对瑞香，多少有着嫁妇儿的那种心情；但更重要的是古家的交情。

She understood Ruixiang’s emotions without finding the right words to comfort her.
因此，她虽了解瑞香心里的感觉；却苦于没有适当的话来宽慰她；

Now that Young Mrs Wang could bring out her happiness, Madam Luosi felt almost as though she had gained a second self.
如今有了小王师母能鼓舞起瑞香的一团喜气，等于自己分身有术，

Free of that concern, she could devote herself to the Seventh Lady and help make the celebration a complete success.
可以不必顾虑瑞香，而全力去周旋行动不便的七姑奶奶，将这场喜事办得十分圆满。

Another contributor, of course, was Mi.
当然，这场喜事能办得圆满，另一个『功臣』是宓本常。

His tireless and thoughtful attention won not only Madam Luosi’s praise but a changed opinion from Gu and his wife.
对于他的尽心尽力，殷勤周到，不但螺蛳太太大为嘉许，连古应春夫妇都另眼相看了。


As the Seventh Lady had predicted, few women came—not enough even for one full table.
果如七姑奶奶的估计，堂客到得极少，连一桌都凑不满，

The men, however, arrived eagerly.
但男客却非常踊跃。

By the start of the entertainment, there seemed enough to fill five tables.
当堂会开始时，估计已经可以坐满五桌了。

Since the occasion was the taking of a concubine, the decorations were relatively simple.
由于是纳妾，铺陈比较简单，

There were lanterns and festive hangings, but the centre of the reception hall displayed only a brightly embroidered picture on red satin of the Old Man of the South Pole, the God of Longevity.
虽也张灯结彩，但客堂正中却只挂了一帽大红缎子彩绣的南极寿星图，

An uninformed visitor might have thought it a birthday celebration.
不明就里的，只当古家做寿。

The Seventh Lady and Madam Luosi had agreed upon this.
这是七姑奶奶与螺蛳太太商量定规的，

Taking a concubine ordinarily involved little ceremony: a small sedan arrived, the woman kowtowed to master and mistress, and the matter was complete.
因为纳妾向来没有什么仪节，只是一乘小轿到门，向主人主母磕了头，便算成礼。

Ruixiang was receiving exceptional consideration already.
如今对瑞香是格外优遇，

Lanterns and coloured hangings were unusual; to display the Two Immortals of Harmony as well would make it resemble a formal marriage and exceed the proper rites.
张灯结彩，已非寻常，如果再挂一幅和合二仙图，便象正式结裏，礼数稍嫌过分，

They had therefore chosen the longevity picture instead.
所以改用一幅寿星图。

The two women had also discussed Ruixiang’s clothes.
瑞香的服饰，也是七姑奶有与螺蛳太太商量过的。

A red skirt was what women valued most, but her status did not entitle her to wear one.
妇人最看重的是一条红裙，以瑞香的身份，是没有资格着的；

As compensation she was allowed a plum-red lined jacket.
为了弥补起见，许她着紫红夹袄，

Time was too short even for a tailor working through the night.
时日迫促，找裁缝连夜做亦来不及；

Again Mi found a solution: he borrowed a completely new one from a pawnshop that dealt with Fukang.
仍旧是宓本常有办法，到跟阜康钱庄有往来的当铺中去借了一件全新的来，

It was slightly small, but that only emphasized her slender figure.
略微显得小了些，但却更衬托出她的身材苗条。


At the auspicious hour of five, the storytelling performance of The White Snake ended.
到得五点钟吉时，一档『白蛇传』的小书结束，

Guests streamed from beneath the courtyard awning into the reception hall to watch the ceremony.
宾客纷纷从席棚下进入堂屋观礼。

A maidservant carried the Seventh Lady downstairs on her back.
七姑奶奶由仆妇背下楼来，

She was settled in a ceremonial armchair, which was then carried to the front of the hall.
纳入一张太师椅中，抬到堂前；

An identical chair on her left awaited Gu.
她的左首，另有一张同样的椅子，是古应春的座位。

“Where is the bridegroom? Bridegroom!” someone shouted playfully.
于是便有人起哄地喊道∶『新郎倌呢？新郎倌！』

Gu was pushed forward from the crowd.
『新郎倌』古应春为人从人丛中推了出来，

He wore a lined robe of sapphire-blue tribute satin, a black riding jacket of imported silk faille, black boots with white soles, and a stiff satin cap with a piece of green jade set centrally above its brim.
宝蓝贡缎夹袍，玄色西洋华丝葛马褂，脚踏粉底皂靴，头上一顶硬胎缎帽，帽檐正中镶一块碧玉，

His head had been freshly shaved.
新剃的头；

Following foreign fashion, he wore no beard, which made him look younger.
他是洋派不留胡子，翕显得年轻了。

Once he sat down, everyone’s attention naturally took in the Seventh Lady: a pleated red skirt, a dark-green lined jacket, pearl flowers in her hair, a face round as the full moon and bright with happiness—a truly fortunate appearance.
等他一坐下来，视线集中，自然而然地看到了七姑奶奶，下身百褶红裙，上身墨绿夹袄，头上戴着珠花，面如满月，脸有喜气，真正福相。

Beside her, a middle-aged woman rested a hand on the chair back.
再看到旁边，扶着七姑奶奶的椅背的一个中年妇人，

Her oval face needed no powder or rouge; its grace was natural.
一张瓜子脸，脂粉不施，天然丰韵，

Her dark, bright eyes resembled sunlight falling directly into a cold, deep pool: unfathomable, almost impossible to meet.
一双眼睛，既黑且亮，恍如阳光直射寒潭，只觉得深不可测，令人不敢逼视。

She wore a black satin jacket and, like the Seventh Lady, a red skirt.
她穿的是玄色缎袄，下面也是红裙；

There was little jewellery in her hair, but the diamond ring on the hand resting against the chair flashed dazzlingly.
头上没有什么首饰，但扶着椅背的那只手上戴着一枚钻戒，不时闪出耀眼的光芒，

Its stone must have been at least the size of half a broad bean.
可以想见戒指上镶的钻，至少也有蚕豆瓣那么大。


“Who is she?” someone whispered.
『那是谁？』有人悄悄在问。

“I hear she is Mr Hu’s concubine.”
『听说是胡大先生的妾。』

“Then why is she wearing a red skirt?”
『是妾，怎么着红裙？』

“She is not in her own house.
『又不是在她自己家里，

Who can stop her?”
哪个来管她？』

“No,” another man said. “That is Madam Luosi of the Hu household.
『不！』另有一个人说∶『她就是胡家的螺蛳太太，

The Old Madam has specially permitted her to wear red.”
着红裙是胡老太太特许的。』


The first two men would have continued, but Ruixiang drew their eyes.
那两个人还想谈下去，但视线为瑞香所吸引了。

Her head was lowered beneath a wealth of pearls and jade, hiding her face.
只见她低着头，但见满头珠翠，却看不清脸，

Yet her tall, graceful figure and fair skin already suggested beauty.
不过长身玉立，皮肤雪白，已可想见是个美人。

Supported by Young Mrs Wang, she approached the strip of red felt and stood poised before it.
她是由小王师母扶着出来的，嬝嬝婷婷地走到红毡条前立定；

Old Nanny Wang of the Gu household called out the ritual directions: “The new lady pays her respects to Master and Mistress. First kowtow!
古家的老王妈赞礼∶『新姑娘见老爷、太太磕头∶一叩首、

Second kowtow!
二叩首、

Third kowtow!
三叩首、

Rise!”
兴！』

Young Mrs Wang helped Ruixiang to her feet.
小王师母便将瑞香扶了起来；

The Seventh Lady raised her hand. “Come here.” Old Nanny Wang announced, “Mistress presents the new lady with a welcoming gift.”
七姑奶奶抬抬手喊一声∶『你过来！』老王妈便又高唱∶『太太赏新姑娘见面礼。』

Madam Luosi quietly handed the Seventh Lady a small velvet box.
这时螺蛳太太便将一个小丝绒匣子悄悄递了给七姑奶奶，

She opened it to reveal another diamond ring, took Ruixiang’s hand, and slipped the ring onto her right ring finger.
她打开匣了——也是一枚钻戒，拉起瑞香的手，将戒指套在她右手九名指上。

“Thank you, Madam,” Ruixiang said softly.
『谢谢奶奶！』瑞香低声道谢；

She was about to kneel again, but Madam Luosi stopped her.
还要跪下去，却让螺蛳太太拉住了。


The ceremony was complete—until Gu unexpectedly stood and turned to Madam Luosi. “Sister Luo, come here.
这就算礼成了，不道奇峰突起，古应春站起身来，看着螺蛳太太说道∶『四姐，你请过来，

Ruixiang ought to kowtow to you.”
应该让瑞香给你磕头。』

“There is no such custom.
『没有这个规矩，

What are you doing?”
这算啥一出？』

She tried to withdraw, but the Seventh Lady had already caught her clothing.
说着，便待避开，哪知七姑奶奶早就拉住了她的衣服；

Ruixiang also stepped forward and supported her. “Please sit, Madam.”
适时瑞香竟也走上前来，扶着她说∶『太太请坐。』

Young Mrs Wang and Old Nanny Wang joined in the urging.
小王师母与老王妈亦都上前来劝驾，

Unable to escape, Madam Luosi had to accept Ruixiang’s full obeisance.
螺蛳太太身不由主，只好受了瑞香的大礼。

When the bustle subsided and everyone was about to disperse, another surprise followed.
乱轰轰一阵过去，正要散开，奇峰又起，

This time Mi climbed onto a stool, raised both hands, and called, “Photographs!
这回是宓本常，站到一张凳子上，举双手喊道∶『还要照照相、

We must have photographs!”
照照相。


Everyone stopped and followed his instructions.
这一下大家都了下来，听从他的指挥，

Two photographs were taken: one of Gu and the Seventh Lady seated together, with Ruixiang standing behind her; another of the entire company.
照了两张相，一张是古应春、七姑奶奶并坐，瑞香侍立在七姑奶奶身后；一张是全体合照，

Feeling there was no appropriate place for her, Madam Luosi quietly slipped away.
螺蛳太太觉得自己无可位置，悄悄地溜掉了。

The photography took time.
照相很费事，

The magnesium flash failed for the second picture, and the attempt had to be repeated.
第二张镁光不亮，重新来过；

By the time the meal began, it was dark.
到开席时，已经天黑了。

The women had a single table upstairs, with Madam Luosi in the seat of honour.
女客只有一桌，开在楼上，螺蛳太太首座；

Unable to sit for long or move easily, the Seventh Lady specially instructed Ruixiang to act as hostess.
七姑奶奶因为不耐久坐，行动也不便，特意命瑞香代作主人，

This was plainly intended to honour her.
这自然是抬举她的意思。

Madam Luosi too felt that she had been shown great respect.
螺蛳太太也觉得很有面子，

Once more she thought of Mi.
不由得又想到了宓本常，

His arrangements had enabled her to send Ruixiang into her new life with dignity, settle a longstanding concern, and fulfil the affection between mistress and maid.
都亏他安排，才能风风光光嫁了瑞香，了却一桩心事，成全了主婢之情。
