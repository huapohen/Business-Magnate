Hu Xueyan: Vanishing into Thin Air

CHAPTER ONE: THE CRISIS OF THE JIASHEN YEAR｜第一章 甲申之变


Business in Shanghai had deteriorated further under the impact of France's war in Vietnam.
上海的市面更坏了，是受了法国在越南的战事的影响。

France had long coveted Vietnam.
法国凯觎越南，由来已久。

In the first year of Tongzhi, the French emperor Napoleon II launched a major naval invasion.
同治元年，法皇拿破仑第二，以海军大举侵入越南。

The Qing court, fully occupied with the rebellion of Hong and Yang, had no strength to protect Vietnam, though it was a tributary state.
其时清廷正因洪杨之变自顾不暇，所以越南虽是清王朝的属国，却无力出兵保护，

Vietnam was forced to sign a humiliating treaty and cede the three provinces of Qinghe, Jiading, and Dingxiang.
越南被迫订了城下之盟，割让庆和、嘉定、定祥三省。

Jiading was Saigon, where the French worked energetically to establish a base for further aggression against Vietnam and eventual advances toward China's Yunnan Province.
嘉定省便是西贡，法国人在那里竭力经营，作为进一步侵略越南、进窥中国云南的根据地。


In the eleventh year of Tongzhi, internal disorder broke out in Vietnam.
同治十一年，越南内乱，

Its leader, Huang Chongying, commanded tens of thousands of men under yellow banners, hence the name Yellow Flag Army.
头目叫做黄崇英，拥众数万，用黄旗，号称“黄旗军”。

The French colluded with him to seize Tonkin, crossed the Han River, and captured Lang Son beyond Zhennan Pass on the Guangxi frontier.
法国人勾通了黄崇英，规取“东京”，渡汉江。攻取广西镇南关外的谅山，

Liu Changyou, Governor of Guangxi and a veteran commander of the Hunan Army, sent troops to help Vietnam suppress the disorder and also invited Liu Yongfu to join the campaign.
广西巡抚是湘军宿将刘长佑，派兵助越平乱，同时邀请刘永福助剿——

Liu Yongfu came from Shangsi in Guangxi and had originally been a smuggler.
刘永福是广西上思州人，本是个私枭，

During the Xianfeng reign, when Hong and Yang rose in rebellion, he followed an ambition of his own, leading three hundred sturdy followers through Zhennan Pass into Baosheng in Vietnam.
咸丰年间，洪杨起事，刘永福却另有心胸，率领部下健儿三百人，出镇南关进入越南保胜。

A Guangdong man named He Junchang had occupied the place, but Liu supplanted him.
此地本为一个广东人何均昌所占领，为刘永福起而代之，

His men carried black banners and became known as the Black Flag Army.
所部用黑旗，号称“黑旗军”。

Having accepted Liu Changyou's invitation and the Vietnamese king's offer to bring him into the royal service, he joined the Guangxi troops in attacks on the French from two sides and won a formidable reputation.
既受刘长佑的邀请，复又受越南王的招抚，与广西官兵夹击法军，威震一时，

Opinion within Vietnam was divided, however, and peace negotiations were eventually chosen.
但越南内部意见分歧，最后决定议和，

The three envoys sent to negotiate were detained by the French and compelled to sign the twenty-two-article Treaty of Saigon.
所派遣的大臣三名，为法军拘禁，被迫订了二十二条的《西贡条约》，

Besides ceding territory and opening trade, Vietnam accepted French protection.
割地通商以外，承认受法国的保护。

To placate Liu Yongfu, he was appointed deputy commander of Sanxuan.
为了安抚刘永福，授职为三宣副提督。

He settled in the remote mountain frontier, farming and training his troops.
刘永福便在边境深山中，屯垦练兵，

His followers grew to as many as two hundred thousand, including twenty thousand picked men between seventeen and twenty-four.
部下聚集至二十万之多，其中劲旅两万人，年龄在十七以上，二十四以下，

Tall, dark-faced, and powerfully built, they crossed forests and ravines as nimbly as apes, their morale exceptionally high.
一个个面黑身高，孔武有力，越林超涧，轻捷如猿，士气极其高昂，

The French regarded him as a thorn in their side and offered a large reward for his head.
因而为法军视如眼中钉，曾经悬重金买他的首级。


After the Treaty of Saigon, regret and resentment spread through Vietnam from the highest ranks to the lowest.
自从《西贡条约》订立以后，越南举国上下，无不既悔且愤，

King Ruan Fushi resolved to make full use of the Black Flags, but France struck first, capturing Hanoi with a powerful force.
越南王阮福时，决意重用黑旗兵。不道法国先下手为强，以重兵陷河内。

From Hue, the king ordered the Black Flags to drive the French out.
于是在顺化的阮福时遂予黑旗军驱逐法军的任务。

If Vietnam were lost, Guangxi and Yunnan would be threatened; France had also formally demanded trading relations with China.
越南有失，广西、云南便受威胁，而且法国已正式向中国提出通商的要求。

At court, opinion divided between war and peace.
朝中议论，分为为战、主和两派，

The war party was led by Li Hongzao.
主战派以李鸿藻为首，

Besides supporting Cen Yuying, Governor-General of Yunnan and Guizhou, in his support for Liu Yongfu, it secured the special appointment of Zeng Guoquan as Governor-General of Liangguang to organize coastal defence.
除了支持云贵总督岑毓英支持刘永福以外，且特起曾国荃为两广总督，部署海防。

Zuo Zongtang also urged war, and opinion among the scholar-officials grew more impassioned.
此外左宗棠亦力主作战，清议更为激昂，

Yet the peace party was far from weak.
但主和派的势力亦不小。

Li Hongzhang, naturally, favoured peace, and Zeng Jize, the minister to France, also opposed a rupture.
当然，李鸿章是主和的，驻法公使曾纪泽亦不主张决裂，

The clearest understanding of the advantages and dangers, however, belonged to Guo Songtao, who had previously represented China in France.
但对其中的利害得失，看得最清楚的是曾经使法的郭嵩焘。

In the first lunar month of that year, the ninth of Guangxu, Li and the French minister Bourée had reached an agreement: China would withdraw its troops, and France would not invade Vietnam.
这年光绪九年正月，李鸿章与法国公使宝海，本已达成“中国撤兵、法不侵越”的协议，

But political upheaval in France brought a cabinet reshuffle.
不意法国发生政潮，内阁改组，

The new foreign minister, Challemel-Lacour, was ambitious: he recalled Bourée, repudiated the agreement, and urged the dispatch of more French troops to Vietnam.
新任外务部长拉克尔是个野心家，一面将宝海撤任、推翻成议，一面促使法国增兵越南。

An imperial order then instructed Li, who was observing mourning, to proceed urgently to Guangdong, direct Vietnamese affairs, and command the defence forces of Liangguang and Yunnan.
于是朝旨命丁忧守制之中的李鸿章迅往广东督办越南事宜，节制两广云南防军。

Outwardly, he appeared to be taking charge of a war, but Guo saw otherwise.
就表面看，是派李鸿尊去主持战局，而实际上并非如此。此中消息为郭嵩焘所参透，

From his home at Xiangyin in Hunan, he sent a special messenger with a long letter.
特意从他的家乡、湖南湘阴派专差送了一封长信给李鸿章，

“In dealing with the West, war has never offered a solution,” he argued.
以为“处置西洋，始终无战法”，他说，

The foreigners wanted trade; China should discuss trade with them.
洋人意在通商，就跟他谈通商好了。

Agreeing to negotiations would ease the situation in Vietnam.
只要一答应谈判通商，越南的局势自然就会缓和。

Sending Li to command armies with a great show of force, as if determined to fight to the finish, would compel France to fight.
如今派李鸿章出而督师，大张旗鼓，摆出一决雌雄的阵势，是逼迫法国作战。

France had not originally wished for war.
法国本无意于战，

To force it into one was to fail, in military terms, to understand the enemy.
逼之应战，是兵法上的“不知彼”。


If China actually went to war, Guo continued, it would also be failing to understand itself.
如果真的要战，又是“不知己”，

His words were bitter: “For more than thirty years we have waged war.
他的话说得很沉痛：“用兵三十余年，

Gathered together, men become soldiers; dispersed, they become bandits, spreading across the empire and leaving profound dangers in their wake.
聚而为兵，散而为盗，蔓延天下，隐患方深。

Add repeated floods and droughts, a decaying administration, robbers everywhere, and a population unable to sustain life.
重以水旱频仍，吏治调敝，盗贼满野，民不聊生，

To provoke trouble on the frontier now and recruit still more troops for defence would, as the days and months passed, produce exhaustion beyond endurance.”
而于是时急开边畔，募兵以资防御，旷日逾时，而耗敝不可支矣。”

In terms of military expenditure alone, China could not fight.
这是就军费者言，说中国不能战。

Even if it won, what then?
就算战胜了，又怎么办？

Victory would require demobilizing the newly recruited troops, leaving them “wandering with nowhere to return,” gathering “the hungry and destitute to pursue their designs.”
战胜当然要裁兵，将刚招募的新兵遣散，结果是“游荡无所归”，聚集“饥困之民图逞”，

China would be creating the sources of its own disorder.
是自己制造乱源。

Guo next reported the discussion in Beijing: “The Grand Council has repeatedly criticized the contrast between the Governor-General of Yunnan's preparations for battle and the Governor-General of Guangdong's apparent unconcern.
接下来，他转述京中的议论：“枢府以滇督援甲厉兵，而粤督处之泰然，数有訾议，

That is why Your Excellency has been entrusted with supreme command.”
是以属中堂以专征之任。”

It seemed that Cen Yuying wanted to fight while Zeng Guoquan stood aside, and that a man of Li's seniority and authority was needed to bring the commanders into agreement and direct the whole undertaking.
看起来是因为岑毓英想打，而曾国茎袖手旁观，前方将帅意见不一，需要一个位高权重的李鸿章去笼罩全面，主持一切。

In reality, however, “the capital's purpose in entrusting this to Your Excellency is still to negotiate peace, not to seek war.”
事实上呢“京师议论，所以属之中堂，仍以议和，非求战也”。


Although in mourning, Li was fully informed about the court.
李鸿章虽然在守制之中，但朝中情形，毫不隔膜，

He maintained several resident informants in Beijing, who reported every development to Hefei by the fastest available means.
他在京师有好几个“坐探”，朝中一举一动，无不以最快的方法，报到合肥，

He knew that Prince Gong wavered between war and peace, while the strongest advocates of war were Li Hongzao, leader of the northern faction, and the men of the Pure Stream group, particularly Zhang Peilun, the Left Deputy Censor-in-Chief.
知道恭王子和战之际，游移不决，而主战最力的是“北派”领袖李鸿藻及一班清流，尤其是左副都御史张佩纶。

Even if Li intended to negotiate peace, he dared not say so openly.
因此，李鸿章纵有议和之意，却不敢公然表示，

Scholarly opinion carried great force, and the Black Flags' successes had further strengthened the war party.
因为清议的力量很大，而且刘永福的黑旗军打得很好，更助长了主战派的声势，

To advocate peace now would defy the sentiment of the whole country.
此时主和是冒天下之大不韪，

He therefore delayed his departure.
所以迟迟其行，

On reaching Shanghai, he held several talks with Tricou, the new French minister who had replaced Bourée, adopting a manner neither yielding nor aggressive and relying above all on delay.
到上海以后，与接替宝海的新任法国公使德理固，谈了几次，态度不软亦不硬，掌握了一个“拖”字诀。

What would delay achieve?
“拖”下去会有什么结果呢？

Li himself did not know.
这是李李鸿章自己都不知道的事，

Behind the scenes, however, he worked hard to remove obstacles to negotiation.
不过他在暗中大下功夫，想消除几个议和的障碍，

The first was Zhang Peilun, a pillar of the Pure Stream group.
第一个左副都御史张佩纶，他是清流的中坚，

If Zhang could be won over and the clamour for war quieted, peace would be easier to arrange.
能把他疏通好，主战的高调不是唱得那么响，议和便较易措手。

Another obstacle was Zeng Jize.
另一个是驻法公使曾纪泽，

His opposition to a breakdown in negotiations did not mean that he favoured concessions to France.
他不主张交涉决裂，但并不表示他主张对法让步，

After returning from Russia to Paris, he had observed that French policy was still unsettled and that only a minority wanted war.
尤其是在从俄国回到巴黎以后，眼看法国的政策亦在摇摆之中，主战的只是少数。

He sent secret telegrams to Li and the Zongli Yamen recommending military aid to Vietnam and a firm line with Tricou.
因此特地密电李鸿章及总理衙门，建议军事援越，对德理固的交涉不妨强硬。

Li neither accepted nor rejected his advice, but wrote to Guo, hinting that he should influence Zeng.
李鸿章对曾纪泽的意见，不置可否，但却致书郭嵩焘，暗示希望他能影响曾纪泽。

Guo's ties with Zeng were close; they had also served in succession as minister to France.
郭嵩焘与曾纪泽的关系很深，而且驻法是前后任，

Zeng would certainly respect his views.
他的言论一定能为曾纪泽所尊重。


During these one or two months of delay, developments in both France and Vietnam changed the situation.
就在这“拖”的一两个月中，法国与越南的情势，都起了变化，

French policy grew more unified.
法国的政策已趋一致，

Prime Minister Jules Ferry told parliament that he was determined to intensify military operations in Vietnam.
内阁总理茹斐理向国会声称，决心加强在越南的军事行动，

Nine thousand reinforcements and another twelve warships were sent east, and Admiral Courbet replaced General Bouët as commander of the French forces.
同时派出九千人援越，另遣军舰十二艘东来，水师提督古拔代陆军提督布意为法军统帅。

In Vietnam, King Ruan Fushi died and was succeeded by his younger brother Ruan Fusheng, under the title King Hehe, or “Harmonious Peace.”
越南则国王阮福时去世，由王弟阮福升继位，称号为“合和王”。

The title itself suggested a willingness to submit to France.
由这称号，便知他是愿意屈服于法国的，

Only a month after his accession, he signed the twenty-seven-article Treaty of Hue, formally acknowledging Vietnam as a French protectorate while continuing to recognize China as its suzerain.
即位只有一个月，便与法国订立了二十七条的《顺化条约》，正式承认越南为法国的保护国，而又仍旧尊重中国为宗主国，

The annual tribute, previously carried overland through Zhennan Pass to Beijing, would henceforth travel by sea.
原来每年进贡，取道镇南关循陆路进京，今后改由海道入贡。

This Franco-Vietnamese treaty helped unite French policy and gave the French troops a recognized basis for expelling the Black Flags.
这一法越《顺化条约》，促成了法国政策的一致，同时也赋予了法军名正言顺得以驱逐黑旗军的地位。

The war party within the Vietnamese government was furious.
因此，越南政府中的主战派大为不满。

It killed King Hehe and enthroned Ruan Fuwu as King Jianfu.
弑合和王而另立阮福吴，称号是“建福王”。

Though Li, now back at his post in Tianjin, continued negotiating with the French minister over Vietnamese sovereignty, the two countries were in fact poised for battle, and war seemed almost unavoidable.
尽管已到天津回任的李鸿章仍与法国公使在谈判越南的主权，而事实上中法双方剑拔弩张，开仗几不可免，

News of Peng Yulin's special appointment to direct military affairs in Guangdong deepened the panic in Shanghai.
尤其是特命彭玉鳞办理广东军务，消息一传，上海的人心越发恐慌。

It was now the middle of the ninth lunar month, just when Madam Luosi returned from Shanghai to Hangzhou.
其时在九月中旬，正当螺蛳太太由上海回到杭州时。


The day after her return, a special envoy arrived from Jiangning.
就在她回到杭州的第二夭，江宁派了个专差来，

He wore red and was covered in the dust of travel, but on his hat, fringed all over with red silk, rose a red rank button, with a peacock feather trailing behind.
身穿红装，风尘满面，但头上一顶披满红丝穗的纬帽，高耸一粒红顶子，后面还拖一条花翎，

Four attendants followed, each with an official button of his own.
身后跟着四名从人，亦都有顶戴。

They had come by road on five tall horses, raising clouds of dust to the jingle of bridle bells, so imposing a procession that passers-by turned to stare.
他们是由陆路来的，五匹高头大马，一路沙尘滚滚、辔铃当当、威风凛凛，路人侧目。

Just inside Wulin Gate, the envoy raised his hand and all five reined in.
一进了武林门，那专差将手一扬，都勒了马，

One officer, wearing a dark blue button, rode forward for orders.
其中一个戴暗蓝顶子的武官，走马趋前，听候吩咐。

“Ask the way.”
“问问路！”

“Yes, sir!”
“喳！”

He slipped from the saddle and, holding the reins in one hand, caught a middle-aged man with the other.
那人滚鞍下马，一手执缰，一手抓住一个中年汉子问道：

“Here, my friend, a place I want to ask about: where's Wubao Street?”
“来、来，老兄，打听一个地名，无宝街在哪里？

“What?
““啊！

What did you say?”
你说啥？”

The officer came from Zeng Guofan's own home district.
原来那武官是曾国藩的小同乡，

Of all Hunan speech, the Xiangxiang dialect was the hardest to understand; delivered rapidly by an impatient soldier, it became even more incomprehensible.
湖南话中湘乡话最难懂，加以武夫性急，说得很快，便越发不知他说些什么了。

Fortunately, he recognized the problem.
还好，那武官倒有自知之明，

“Yuan-bao Street,” he repeated, syllable by syllable, cupping both hands to suggest a silver ingot.
一字一句地答道：“元宝街。”说着还双手上捧，作手势示意元宝。

“Ah, Yuanbao Street! That's what you mean, sir.”
“喔、喔、喔，你老人家是说元宝街！”

The man, surnamed Bu, was a clerk in the rites section of the Qiantang county office.
那人姓卜，是钱塘县“礼房”的书办，

Instead of answering, he asked, “May I ask where you have come from?
不作回答，却反问：“请问，你们是从哪里来的？

Jiangning?”
江宁？”

“Yes.”
“不错。”

“Then you are going to Yuanbao Street to see Mr Hu?”
“这样说，到元宝街是去看胡大先生？”

“Mr Hu?”
“胡大先生？”

The officer hesitated, then understood.
那人一愣，旋即想到：

“Yes, yes.
“不错，不错，

Mr Hu is His Honour Hu Xueyan.”
胡大先生就是胡雪岩胡大人。”


Clerk Bu nodded, stepped closer, and pointed discreetly.
卜书办点头，趋前一步，手指着低声问道：

“Who is the gentleman on horseback with the red button?”
“马上那位红顶子的人，是什么人？”

The officer was growing impatient.
那武官有些不耐烦了，

The roads were open to everyone, and asking directions was an ordinary matter.
天下人走天下路，问路应是常事，

Someone who knew the way and felt helpful could explain it; someone who knew but could not be bothered could say he wasn't sure.
知道而热心的，详细指点，知道而懒得回答的，说一声“不清楚”，

A helpful person who did not know could apologize and suggest asking someone else, while an unhelpful one could simply turn and walk away.
真的不知道而又热心的，会表示歉意，请对方另行打听，不知道而又懒得回答的，只字不答，掉臂而去。

But to ask directions and be questioned in return, almost as though under investigation, was a new experience.
象这样问路而反为别人所问，类似盘查，却还是第一次遇见。

Seeing his expression, Bu hastened to explain.
卜书办看那武官的脸色，急忙提出解释：

“Please don't think me tiresome, sir.
“你老人家不要嫌我罗嗦，

The gentleman on that horse is a first-rank military officer.
实在是马上那位大人一品武官，

I dare not neglect him.
我不敢怠慢，

If I know who he is, I can report to our magistrate, and any service he requires can be attended to promptly.”
晓得了身分，好禀报本县大老爷，有啥差遣，不会误事。”


So that was his kindly intention!
原来是这样一番好意！

The officer felt embarrassed, but did not know how to answer.
那武官倒觉得过意不去，但却不知如何回答——

The envoy had originally been called Gao Laosan, Third Son Gao.
那专差本名高老三，

When he joined the Hunan Army, the recruiting official had given him the more elegant name Leshan, playing on the sound of his old name and the saying that the benevolent delight in mountains. Because he was third-born, he also received the courtesy name Renshu.
投效湘军时，招募委员替他改名“乐山”来谐音，“仁者乐山”而又行三，因而又送他一个别号叫“仁叔”。

Gao had originally served under Liu Songshan.
这高乐山原隶刘松山帐下，

When Zuo began his western campaign, Zeng assigned Liu and his battalion to him, an arrangement people jokingly called a dowry.
左宗棠西征，曾国藩特拨刘松山一营隶属于左，时人称为“赠嫁”。

Liu's brilliant service proved invaluable.
刘松山在西征时，战功彪炳，左宗棠大为得力。

Zuo and Zeng did not get on; in the gifted Zuo's eyes, Zeng had otherwise done nothing worthy of admiration.
