胡雪岩
《烟消云散》第一章（二）
Vanishing into Thin Air — Chapter One, Part Two
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


当邵友濂谒见李鸿章，谈妥了以打击胡雪岩作为对左宗棠掣肘的主要手段时，胡雪岩不过刚刚到了江宁。
By the time Shao Youlian had met Li Hongzhang and agreed that striking at Hu Xueyan would be their principal means of obstructing Zuo Zongtang, Hu had only just reached Jiangning.

原来胡雪岩与螺蛳太太商量行程，螺蛳太太力主先到江宁，后到上海。
When he discussed his itinerary with Madam Luosi, she strongly urged him to visit Jiangning before Shanghai.

胡雪岩觉得她的打算很妥当，
He thought her reasoning sound.

因为由于螺蛳太太的夸奖，他才知道宓本常应变的本事很到家，这样就方便了，
Her praise of Mi Benchang had made him realize how well Mi could handle unexpected situations, which simplified matters.

在南京动静要伺候左宗棠，身不由主，
In Nanjing he must attend upon Zuo and could not control his own movements.

到了上海，是宓本常伺候自己，
In Shanghai, Mi would attend upon him.

即令有未了之事，可以交给宓本常去料理，欲去欲留，随心所欲，决不会耽误了为女儿主持嘉礼这一件大事。
Any unfinished business could be left to Mi, allowing him to come and go as he pleased without missing the important duty of presiding over his daughter's wedding.

于是，他一面写信通知宓本常与古应春，一面打点到江宁的行李——行李中大部分是送人的土仪。
He wrote to Mi and Gu Yingchun while preparing his baggage, most of which consisted of local gifts.

江宁候补道最多，有句戏言叫做“群‘道，如毛”。
Jiangning had more expectant circuit intendants than anywhere else, giving rise to a joke that its “circuit men were as thick as hairs.”

这些候补道终年派不到一个差使，但三品大员的排场，不能不摆，
They might go a whole year without an assignment, yet still had to maintain the appearance of third-rank dignitaries.

所以一个个苦不堪言，只盼当肥缺阔差使的朋友到江宁公干，才有稍资沾润的机会。
Struggling to do so, they awaited visits from friends holding lucrative posts, hoping for a little help.

胡雪岩在江宁的熟人很多，又是“财神”，
Hu had many acquaintances there and was the God of Wealth.

这趟去自然东西是东西、银子是银子，个个要应酬到。
Everyone must receive attention, in gifts or silver.

银子还可在江宁阜康支用，土仪却必须从杭州带去，整整装满一船，
The silver could be drawn from Fukang in Jiangning, but the Hangzhou gifts filled an entire boat.

连同胡雪岩专用的座船，由长江水师特为派来的小人轮拖带，经嘉兴、苏州直驶江宁。
A small steamer specially sent by the Yangtze naval forces towed it and Hu's private passenger boat through Jiaxing and Suzhou to Jiangning.

当此时也，李鸿章亦以密电致上海道邵友濂，要他赴津一行，有要事面谈。
Meanwhile, Li secretly telegraphed Shao, the Shanghai circuit intendant, summoning him to Tianjin for an important personal discussion.

上海道是地方官，不能擅离职守，所以在密电中说明，总理衙门另有电报。
As a local official, Shao could not leave his post without authorization, so Li explained that a separate telegram would come from the Zongli Yamen.

关照他先作准备，等总理衙门的公事一到，立即航海北上。
Shao was to prepare and sail north as soon as its official instructions arrived.

公事是胡雪岩从杭州动身以后，才到上海的。
They reached Shanghai only after Hu had left Hangzhou.

但因上海到天津的海道，费时只得两天一夜，所以邵友濂见到李鸿章时，胡雪岩还在路上。
But the sea passage to Tianjin took just two days and a night; when Shao saw Li, Hu was still on his way.


这南北洋两大臣各召亲信，目的恰好相反。
The ministers responsible for the northern and southern maritime administrations were thus each summoning a trusted man, but for opposite purposes.

左宗棠主战，
Zuo favoured war.

积极筹划南洋防务以外，全力支持督办广东军务的钦差大臣彭玉麟。
Besides actively preparing southern coastal defences, he fully supported Peng Yulin, the imperial commissioner supervising military affairs in Guangdong.

李鸿章则表面虽不敢违犯清议，但暗中却用尽了釜底抽薪的手段，削弱主战派的力量及声势。
Li, though unwilling openly to defy respectable public opinion, secretly used every means to remove the war party's support and weaken its power and prestige.

第一个目标是左副都御史张佩纶，因为他是主战派领袖大学士李鸿藻的谋主，
His first target was Zhang Peilun, the Left Deputy Censor-in-Chief, chief strategist for Grand Secretary Li Hongzao, leader of the war party.

制服他亦就是擒贼擒王之意。
Bringing Zhang under control would be like capturing the leader to subdue the band.

就压制主战派这个目的来说，收服张佩纶是治本，打击胡雪岩是治标。
In terms of suppressing the advocates of war, winning Zhang over dealt with the root; attacking Hu dealt with the branches.


可是首当其冲的胡雪岩，却还睡在鼓里，
Hu, who would bear the first blow, knew nothing of this.

到了江宁，先到他自己所置的公馆休息。
On reaching Jiangning, he went first to rest at his own residence.

胡雪岩在通都大邑，都置有公馆，但一年难得一到，
He kept houses in the great cities but rarely visited them even once a year.

江宁因为左宗棠的关系，这年是第二次来往。
Because of Zuo, this was his second stay at Jiangning that year.

这个公馆的“女主人”姓王，
The “mistress” of this house was surnamed Wang.

原是秦淮“旧院”钓鱼巷的者鸨，运气不佳，两个养女，连着出事，一个殉情，一个私奔，
She had formerly run a brothel in Diaoyu Lane in Qinhuai's Old Quarter, but misfortune had struck her two adopted daughters in succession: one died for love, and the other eloped.

私奔的可以不追究，殉情的却连累老鸨吃了人命官司，
The elopement could be left alone, but the death involved Madam Wang in a homicide case.

好不容易才得无罪被释，心灰意懒再不愿意吃这碗“把势饭”了。
She was finally acquitted and released, yet so disheartened that she no longer wished to earn her living by that precarious trade.

既然如此，只有从良之一途。
The only alternative was to leave it and marry.

这个王鸨，就象《板桥杂记》中所写的李香君的假母那样，虽鸨不老，三十出头年纪，丰韵犹存，
Like Li Xiangjun's adoptive mother in Miscellaneous Records of Banqiao, she was a madam without being old, only a little over thirty and still attractive.

要从良亦着实有人愿量珠来聘。
There were men willing to offer riches for her.

但秦淮的勾栏中人，承袭了明末清初“旧院”的遗风，
But Qinhuai courtesans inherited the Old Quarter's traditions from the late Ming and early Qing.

讲究饮食起居，看得骚人墨客，
They cared about refinement in food and daily life and admired poets and cultivated men.

而看中她的，腰有万金之缠，身无一骨之雅；她看中的，温文尔雅，不免寒酸。
Those who wanted her had fortunes but no trace of elegance; those she wanted were gentle and refined but poor.

因而空有从良之志，难得终身之托。
She wished to leave the profession, yet could find no one to entrust her future to.


这是三年前的事，
That was three years earlier.

江宁阜康新换一个档手，名叫江德源，此人是由阜康调过来的，
Jiangning Fukang had just acquired a new manager, Jiang Deyuan, transferred from another Fukang establishment.

深通风月，得知有王鸨这么一个人，延聘她来当“胡公馆”的管家，
Well versed in the world of pleasure, he heard of her and hired her to manage Hu's residence.

平时作为应酬特等客户的处所，等“东家”到江宁，她便是“主持中馈”的“主妇”。
Normally it entertained especially important clients; when the proprietor visited, she became its hostess, presiding over the household.

当然，这“主妇”的责任，也包括房帏之事在内。
Those duties naturally extended to the bedroom.

王鸨为胡公馆的饮食起居舒服，且又不受拘束，欣然同意。
Comfortable living and freedom from restraint made her readily accept.

那年秋天，胡雪岩到江宁，首先就看中了她的裙下双钩，纤如新月，
When Hu came that autumn, the first thing to attract him was the pair of tiny curved feet beneath her skirt, slender as crescent moons.

一夕缱绻，真如袁子才所说的“徐娘风味胜雏年”，
After a night together, he found Yuan Zicai's saying true: “A mature woman's charms surpass those of youth.”

厚赠以外，送了她一个外号叫做“王九妈”，南宋发生在西湖上的，有名的“卖油郎独占花魁女”的故事，其中的老鸨就叫王九妈。
Besides generous gifts, he gave her the name Mother Wang Jiu, after the brothel keeper in the famous Southern Song tale set on West Lake, The Oil Vendor Wins the Flower Queen.

这王九妈已得到江德源的通知，早就迎合胡雪岩的喜好，除饮食方面有预备以外，另外还打听了许多新闻，作为陪伴闲谈的资料。
Now, warned by Jiang, she had prepared Hu's favourite food and gathered plenty of news to entertain him.


这些新闻中，胡雪岩最关切的，自然是有关左宗棠的情形。
Naturally, Hu cared most about news of Zuo.

据说他衰病侵寻，意气更甚，
He was said to be increasingly old and infirm, but more overbearing than ever.

接见僚属宾客，不能谈西征，一谈便开了他的“话匣子”，铺陈西征的勋业，御将如何恩威并用，用兵如何神奇莫测。
Visitors and subordinates must never mention the western campaign: it opened a floodgate of talk about his achievements, his combination of kindness and authority in handling generals, and the miraculous unpredictability of his strategy.

再接下来便要骂人，第一个被骂的是曾国藩，其次是李鸿章，有时兼骂沈葆祯。
Then came the abuse, first of Zeng Guofan, next of Li Hongzhang, and sometimes of Shen Baozhen too.

这三个人都是左宗棠的前任，有好些旧部在江宁，尤其是曾国藩故旧更多，
All three had preceded him in office, and many of their former subordinates remained in Jiangning, especially Zeng's.

而且就人品来说，左宗棠骂李鸿章犹可，骂曾国藩则不免令人不服，
Whatever might be said of Li, people resented attacks on Zeng's character.

因此，曾国藩的旧部，每每大庭广众之间批评他说：“大帅对老帅有意见，
Zeng's old followers often complained openly: “The present commander has grievances against the old commander.

他们之间的恩怨，亦难说得很。
The rights and wrongs between them are hard to judge.

就算老帅不对，人都过去了，也听不见他的骂，
Even if the old commander was at fault, he is dead and cannot hear the abuse.

何必在我们面前罗嗦。
Why must we endure it?

而且道理不直，话亦不圆，
Besides, the arguments are neither just nor convincing.

说来说去，无非老帅把持饷源，处处回护九帅，
It all comes down to the same thing: he monopolized military funds and protected Ninth Marshal.

耳朵里都听得生茧了。”胡雪岩心想，也不过半年未见左宗棠，何以老境颓唐至此？
We have heard it until our ears have grown calluses.” Hu wondered how Zuo could have declined so much in the mere six months since they last met.

便有些不大相信，及至一问江德源，果然如此，他说：
He was doubtful until Jiang confirmed it.

“江宁现在许多事办不通。
“Many things cannot be done in Jiangning now.

为什么呢？左大人先开讲，后开骂，一个人滔滔不绝，
His Excellency begins with a lecture, then starts abusing people, talking without pause.

说到时候差不多了，戈什哈把茶碗交到他手里，外面伺候的人马上喊一声‘送客’。
When the allotted time is nearly over, a guard puts a teacup into his hand and someone outside immediately calls, ‘See the guest out!’

根本就没法子谈公事。”
There is no chance to discuss business.”


“这是难得一次吧？”
“Surely that happens only occasionally?”

“哪里？可说天天如此。”
“Almost every day.

江德源说，“左大人有点‘人来疯’，人越多他越起劲。
He becomes more excited with a larger audience.

大先生亦不必讲究礼节。
You needn't stand on ceremony, sir.

‘上院，去见，不如就此刻在花厅或者签押房里见，
Instead of attending a formal audience, go now and see him in the garden reception room or his office.

倒可以谈点正经。”
Then you might discuss something useful.”

原来督抚接见“两司”——藩司、桌司以及道员以下的僚属，大致五天一次，“衙参”之期定在逢三、逢八的日子居多，
Governors-general and governors generally received the provincial treasurer and judicial commissioner, together with circuit intendants and lesser subordinates, about once every five days, often on dates ending in three or eight.

接见之处，称为“官厅”，而衙参称之为“上院”。
These formal visits, called “going up to the court,” took place in the official reception hall.

胡雪岩到的这天是十月十七，原想第二天“上院”，
Hu had arrived on the seventeenth of the tenth lunar month and intended to attend on the eighteenth.

如今听江德源这一说，决定接受他的建议，当即换了官服，坐轿直闯两江总督的辕门。
Accepting Jiang's advice, he instead put on his official robes at once and went straight by sedan chair to the Liangjiang governor-general's gates.


辕门上一看“胡财神”到了，格外巴结，
Seeing the God of Wealth, the gatekeepers were exceptionally attentive.

擅作主张开正门，让轿子抬到官厅檐前下轿，
On their own authority they opened the main gate and let his sedan be carried to the eaves of the reception hall.

随即通报到上房，传出话来：“请胡大人换了便服，
Word was sent inside, and instructions came back: “Ask His Excellency Hu to change into informal clothes.

在签押房见面。”
We shall meet in the office.”

于是跟班打开衣包，就在官厅上换了便服，引人签押房，左宗棠已经在等了，
His attendant opened the clothes bundle, and Hu changed in the hall before being shown into the office, where Zuo was already waiting.

胡雪岩自然是行大礼请安，
Hu naturally made his full ceremonial greeting.

左宗棠亲手相扶，延入客座，少不得有一番寒暄。
Zuo helped him up himself and led him to the guest seats, where they exchanged the usual courtesies.

胡雪岩一面说话，一面细看左宗棠的眼睛，
As they spoke, Hu studied his eyes.

左眼已长了一层白翳，右眼见风流泪，非常厉害，不时拿一块绸绢擦拭，
A white film covered the left, while the right watered badly in a draught, forcing him to wipe it repeatedly with a silk handkerchief.

于是找一个空隙说道：“听说大人的眼睛不好，我特为配了一副眼药来，
Finding an opening, Hu said, “I heard your eyes were troubling you and had a special medicine prepared.

清凉明目，很有效验，”
It is cooling and clears the sight, with very good results.”

说着，将随手携带的一个小锦袱解开来又说：“还替大人配了一服膏滋药，
Undoing a small brocade bundle he had brought, he added, “I also had a medicinal paste made for you.

如果服得好，请大人交代书启师爷写信来，我再送来。”
If it agrees with you, have your correspondence secretary write, and I shall send more.”

“多谢，多谢！”
“Thank you!

左宗棠说：“我现在多靠几个朋友帮忙，不但私务，连公事都要累你。
Nowadays I depend on a few friends for help, not just with private matters; even official business must trouble you.

上次山东闹水灾，两江派助赈四十万，藩库只拿得出一半，
When Shandong suffered floods, Liangjiang was assigned four hundred thousand taels for relief, and the provincial treasury could provide only half.

多亏你慷慨援手。
Your generous help saved us.

不过，这笔款子，两江还无法奉还。”
But Liangjiang still cannot repay you.”

“大人不必挂齿。”胡雪岩原想再说一句：“有官款在我那里，我是应该效劳的。”但话到口边，又缩了回去。
“Please don't mention it.” Hu nearly added that, with public funds deposited with him, he ought to be of service, but checked the words before speaking.


“这一回越南吃紧，朝命彭雪琴督办广东军务，
“With the situation in Vietnam urgent, the court has ordered Peng Xueqin to supervise military affairs in Guangdong.

我跟他三十年的交情，不能不助他一臂之力，
We have been friends for thirty years, and I must help him.

而况我奉旨筹办南洋防务，
Moreover, I have been commanded to prepare southern maritime defences.

粤闽洋面，亦在我管辖之下，其势更不能兼筹并顾。
The waters off Guangdong and Fujian fall within my jurisdiction; I cannot neglect them.

可恨的是，两江官场，从曾湘乡以来，越搞越坏，
What infuriates me is that, since Zeng of Xiangxiang's time, officialdom in Liangjiang has grown steadily worse.

推拖敷衍，不顾大局，以致于我又要靠老朋友帮忙了。”
Evasion and delay, with no thought for the wider situation—so once again I must rely on old friends.”

“是。”胡雪岩很沉重地答应着。
Hu assented gravely.

“王阆青已经出京回湖南去招兵了，打算招六千人，
“Wang Langqing has left Beijing for Hunan to recruit six thousand men.

总要有四千支枪才够用。
He will need at least four thousand rifles.

江宁的军械局，为李少荃的大舅子搞得一塌糊涂，
Li Shaoquan's elder brother-in-law has made a complete mess of the Jiangning Arsenal.

交上海制造局赶办，第一是经费尚无着落，其次是时间上缓不济急，
If we order the Shanghai Manufacturing Bureau to produce them, first the money is not yet available, and second they cannot arrive in time for the emergency.

所以我想由转运局来想法了。
So I thought the Transport Bureau might find a way.

雪岩，你说呢？
What do you say, Xueyan?”

““转运局库存洋枪，细数我还不知道。
“I don't yet know the exact number of Western rifles in stock.

不过大人既然交代要四千支，我无论如何要想法子办齐。”
But if you require four thousand, I shall find some means of supplying the full number.”

“好！”
“Good!

左宗棠说：“我就知道，跟你商量不过是一句话的事，
I knew that a word to you would settle it.

最痛快不过。”
Nothing could be more satisfying.”

“光塘……”胡雪岩称名谦谢：“承大人栽培，
Referring to himself respectfully by his personal name, Hu replied, “Guangyong owes everything to your patronage.

不敢不尽心尽力伺候。”
I dare not fail to serve you with all my heart and strength.”


“好说，好说。
“You are too modest.

还有件事，
There is something else.

王阆青招来的兵，粮饷自然由户部去筹划，一笔开拔费，数目可观，
The Ministry of Revenue will of course arrange pay and provisions for Wang's recruits, but the cost of mobilizing them is considerable.

两江不能不量力相助。
Liangjiang must contribute what it can.

雪岩，你能不能再帮两江一个忙？”
Could you help us once more?”

如果是过去，胡雪岩一定会问：“要多少？”
In the past, Hu would simply have asked how much.

但目前情形不同，
Circumstances were different now.

他想了一下说：“回大人的话，现在市面上银根紧得不得了，
After a little thought, he replied, “Your Excellency, money is desperately tight in the market.

就是不紧，大人要顾到老部下。
Even if it were not, you must consider your old subordinate.

如今我遵大人的吩咐，要多少筹多少，到了陕甘接济不上时，就变成从井救人了。”
If I raise whatever you ask now and later cannot supply Shaanxi and Gansu, I shall be like a man who jumps into a well to rescue someone.”

所谓“老部下”是指刘锦棠，
The old subordinate was Liu Jintang.

而胡雪岩又是西征转运局的委员，在他的职司有主有从，
Hu held a commission with the western expedition's Transport Bureau, and his responsibilities had an order of priority.

如两江筹饷是额外的差使，行有余力，不妨效劳，
Raising funds for Liangjiang was additional work, which he could undertake if he had capacity to spare.

否则他当然要顾全西征军为主。
Otherwise, the western army must come first.

左宗棠了解到这一点，便不能不有所顾虑，
Zuo understood and could not disregard the concern.

想了一下说道：“这样吧，
“Very well.

明天我再找藩司来想法子。
Tomorrow I shall speak to the provincial treasurer again.

如果真有难处，那就不能不仰赖老兄拔刀相助了。”
If there really is difficulty, we shall have to rely on you to come to our rescue.”

“大人言重。”
“You are too kind.

胡雪岩问道：“不知道什么时候再来请示。”
When should I return for instructions?”

“请示”便是听回音。
By instructions, Hu meant the answer.

左宗棠答说：“很快、很快，
“Soon.

三两天之内，就有信息。”
There will be news within two or three days.”

于是胡雪岩起身说道：
Hu rose.

“我听大人的指挥办理，
“I shall act as you direct.

今天就告辞了。”
For today, I take my leave.”

“嗯，嗯。”左宗棠问：“今天晚上没事吧？”
“Are you free this evening?”

胡雪岩知道要留他吃饭，急说道：“今天晚上有个不能不去的饭局。”
Realizing that Zuo meant to invite him to dinner, Hu quickly said that he had an engagement he could not miss.

“既然如此，我不留你了。
“Then I won't keep you.

我知道你事情多，
I know you are busy.

不必来看我，
