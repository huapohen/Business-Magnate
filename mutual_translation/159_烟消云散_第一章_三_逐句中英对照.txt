胡雪岩
《烟消云散》第一章（三，至本章结束）
Vanishing into Thin Air — Chapter One, Part Three (conclusion)
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第二天中午，古应春带来一个极好的消息，各省协助的“西饷”，已快收齐了，
At noon the next day, Gu Yingchun brought excellent news: almost all the provincial contributions to the western military funds had been collected.

最早的一笔，在十月初便已汇到。
The earliest payment had arrived at the beginning of the tenth month.

“有这样的事！”
“Can that be true?”

胡雪岩大为困惑，
Hu Xueyan was thoroughly puzzled.

“为啥邵筱村同我说一文钱都没有收到？
“Why did Shao Xiaocun tell me he had not received a single cash?

你的消息哪里来的？”
Where did you hear this?”

“我有个同乡晚辈，早年我照应过他，
“From a younger fellow townsman whom I helped years ago.

他现在是上海道衙门电报房的领班。
He is now the head clerk in the telegraph room at the Shanghai circuit office.”

“那就不错了！”
“Then there can be no mistake!”

胡雪岩既喜且怒，
Hu was delighted and angry at once.

“邵筱村不晓得在打什么鬼主意？
“What game is Shao Xiaocun playing?

我要好好问他一问。
I shall have a word with him!”

““小爷叔不必如此。
“There is no need for that, Little Uncle.

我想最好的办法是请左大人打个电报给邵筱村。”
I think the best course is to ask Lord Zuo to send him a telegram.”

原来古应春从他同乡晚辈中，另获有很机密的消息，说是李鸿章正在设法打击左宗棠，
Gu had also obtained highly confidential information from his fellow townsman: Li Hongzhang was looking for ways to strike at Zuo Zongtang.

因而想到，邵友濂对胡雪岩有意留难，是别有用心。
This suggested that Shao Youlian had an ulterior motive for deliberately obstructing Hu.

但这个消息，未经证实，告诉了胡雪岩，反而会生出是非，
But the report had not been confirmed, and telling Hu might only cause trouble.

只有用左宗棠出面，措词严厉些，带着警告的意味，让邵友濂心生顾忌，在期限之前拨出这笔代收的款子，了却胡雪岩的责任，最为上策。
The best course was to have Zuo intervene with a sternly worded warning, making Shao think twice and release the money he had collected before the deadline, thereby discharging Hu’s responsibility.


但胡雪岩又何从去了解他的用心，
Hu, however, had no way of knowing what lay behind Gu’s advice.

他仍旧是抱着在左宗棠面前要保持面子的用心。
His concern was still to save face before Zuo.

在江宁时，左宗棠原曾问过他，有什么事要他出面，意思就是指上海道代收“西饷”这件事，
In Jiangning, Zuo had asked whether there was anything in which he should intervene, meaning the western military contributions collected through the Shanghai circuit office.

当时如说请他写封信催一催邵友濂，是很正常的回答，左宗棠不会想到别的地方去。
Had Hu then asked him to write and hurry Shao along, it would have been a perfectly natural answer; Zuo would have thought nothing of it.

已经回答没有什么事要他费心，
But Hu had already said that there was nothing he needed to trouble him about.

而结果仍旧要他出面，这等于作了垫不出五十万银子的表示是一样的。
To ask for his intervention now would amount to admitting that he could not advance five hundred thousand taels.

因此，他这样答说：“不必劳动他老人家了，
He therefore replied, “There is no need to trouble His Excellency.

既然各省都快到齐了，我去催他。”
Since almost all the provincial payments have arrived, I shall go and press Shao myself.”


胡雪岩一向沉得住气，这一次因为事多心烦，竟失去了耐性，
Hu had always been able to keep his temper, but this time the strain of too many affairs made him lose patience.

气冲冲地去看邵友濂，门上回答：“邵大人视察制造局去了。”
He went angrily to see Shao, only to be told at the door, “His Excellency has gone to inspect the Manufacturing Bureau.”

吃了个闭门羹，心中越发不快，
This fruitless visit made him still more displeased.

回到制造局命文案师爷写信给邵友濂，
Returning to the Manufacturing Bureau, he ordered his correspondence secretary to write to Shao.

措词很不客气，有点打官腔的味道，而且暗示，邵友濂如果不能如期付款，只好请左宗棠自己来料理了。
The letter was decidedly discourteous, with something of an official’s peremptory tone; it also hinted that, if Shao could not pay on time, Hu would have to ask Zuo himself to deal with the matter.

这封信送到江海关，立即转送邵友濂公馆，
Delivered to Jianghaiguan Customs, it was immediately forwarded to Shao’s residence.

他看了自然有些紧张，
Naturally, it made him uneasy.

因为“不怕官，只怕管”，
As the saying went, “It is not an official you need fear, but the man who has authority over you.”

自太平军被平息后，督抚权柄之重，为清朝开国以来所未有，
Since the suppression of the Taiping armies, provincial governors and governors-general had acquired powers unequalled since the founding of the Qing dynasty.

左宗棠是现任的两江总督，
Zuo was the incumbent Governor-General of Liangjiang.

如果指名严参，再有理也无法申诉，
If he submitted a severe impeachment naming Shao, no argument, however sound, could win Shao a hearing.

而况实际上确也收到了好几省的“西饷”，靳而不予，也是件说不过去的事。
Besides, contributions from several provinces had indeed arrived; withholding them was indefensible.

因此，他很不情愿地作了个决定，将已收到的“西饷”开单送交转运局，
Very reluctantly, he decided to draw up a statement and send the money already received to the Transport Bureau.

为数约四十万两，
It came to about four hundred thousand taels.

胡雪岩只需垫十万银子，便可保住他对洋人的信用。
Hu would then need to advance only one hundred thousand to preserve his credit with the foreigners.

但就在写好复信，正待发出之际，来了一个人，使得他的决定整个儿被推翻。
But just as Shao finished his reply and was about to send it, a visitor arrived who overturned his decision completely.


这个人便是盛宣怀，
The visitor was Sheng Xuanhuai.

由于筹办电报局大功告成，不但成了李鸿章面前有数的红人，而且亦马结上了醇亲王的关系。
Having successfully established the telegraph bureau, he had become one of Li Hongzhang’s leading favourites and had also formed a connection with Prince Chun.

此番是衔李鸿章之命，到上海跟邵友濂来商量，如何“救人”？
Li had sent him to Shanghai to discuss with Shao how to “put out the fire.”

“救火”是盛宣怀形容挽救眼前局势的一个譬喻，这也是李鸿章的说法，
This was Sheng’s metaphor for rescuing the present situation, and Li used it too.

他认为由越南危局引起的中法冲突，他有转危为安的办法，
Li believed he had a way to turn the danger of the Sino-French conflict over Vietnam into a peaceful settlement.

但主战派的行动，却如“纵火”，清流的高调，则是火上浇油。
The actions of the war party, however, were like setting fires, while the lofty rhetoric of the Pure Stream group poured oil on the flames.

但如火势已灭，虽有助燃的油料，终无所用。
Once the fire was extinguished, even oil would have nothing to feed.

意思就是打消了主战的行动，清流便不足畏。
In other words, if the moves towards war could be stopped, the Pure Stream group would cease to be a threat.

那么，谁是“纵火”者呢？
Who, then, were the arsonists?

在李鸿章看，第一个就是左宗棠，第二个是彭玉麟。
In Li’s eyes, Zuo came first and Peng Yulin second.

至于西南方面如云贵总督岑毓英等，自有办法可以控制，
As for men in the southwest, such as Cen Yuying, Governor-General of Yunnan and Guizhou, there were ways to keep them under control.

即使是彭玉麟，倘无左宗棠的支持，亦可设法让他知难而退。
Even Peng, deprived of Zuo’s support, could be made to recognise the difficulties and withdraw.

换句话说，擒贼擒玉，只要将左宗棠压制住，李鸿章就能掌握到整个局势，与法国交涉化干戈为玉帛。
Capture the ringleader first: once Zuo had been restrained, Li could control the whole situation and negotiate peace with France.


“筱村兄，你不要看什么‘主战自强’、‘大奋天威’、‘同仇敌忾’，这些慷慨激昂的论调，
“Brother Xiaocun, do not be misled by all the cries of ‘War and self-strengthening,’ ‘Let the empire display its might,’ and ‘Unite against the common enemy.’

高唱人云，这不过是听得见的声音，
Those impassioned declarations ring to the skies, but they are merely the voices you can hear.

其实，听不见的声音，才是真正有力量的声音，
The voices you cannot hear are the ones with real power.

中堂如果不是有这些听不见的声音撑腰，他也犯不着跟湘阴作对一一湘阴老境颓唐，至多还有三、五年的富贵而已，
Without those silent voices behind him, His Excellency would have no reason to oppose Zuo of Xiangyin. Zuo is declining in his old age; at most, he has another three or five years of wealth and honours ahead of him.

何必容不得他？
Why should His Excellency be unable to tolerate him?

反过来说，如果容不得他，就一定有非去他不可的缘故在内。
Conversely, if he cannot tolerate him, there must be a reason why Zuo has to go.

筱村兄，中堂的心事，你先要明白。
Brother Xiaocun, you must first understand what is in His Excellency’s mind.”

“中堂是指李鸿章。
By His Excellency, he meant Li Hongzhang.

盛宣怀的词令最妙，
Sheng was a consummate speaker.

他将李鸿章对左宗棠的态度，说得忠厚平和，一片恕词。
He portrayed Li’s attitude towards Zuo as generous and temperate, all forbearance and charity.

但在邵友濂听来，是非常明白的，李、左之间已成势不两立，非拼个你死我活不可了。
What Shao heard, however, was perfectly clear: Li and Zuo had become irreconcilable enemies, and their struggle could end only with one or the other destroyed.


“是的。”邵友濂矍然警觉，
“Yes,” Shao said, suddenly alert.

“我明白。
“I understand.

不过，我倒要请问，是哪些听不见的声音？”
But may I ask whose voices these are that cannot be heard?”

“第一是当今大权独揽的慈禧皇太后，
“First, Empress Dowager Cixi, who holds all power in her hands.

她辛苦了大半辈子，前两年又生了一场死去活来的大病，
She has laboured for more than half her life, and two years ago she suffered an illness that nearly killed her.

你想，五十岁的老太太，有几个不盼望过几年清闲日子的，
Think of it: how many fifty-year-old ladies do not long for a few peaceful years?

她哪里要打什么仗？”
Why should she want a war?”

“既然大权独揽，她说个‘和’字，
“If all power is hers, she need only say ‘Peace.’

哪个敢不奉懿旨？”
Who would dare disobey her command?”

“苦就苦在她什么话都好说，就是这个字说不出口。
“That is precisely her difficulty. She can say anything but that one word.

为啥呢？
Why?

洪杨勘定大乱，
She put down the great rebellion of Hong and Yang.

从古以来，垂帘的太后，没有她这样的武功，
No empress dowager who ruled from behind the curtain has ever had military achievements to equal hers.

哪里好向廷臣示弱。
How can she show weakness before her ministers?

再说，清流的论调，又是如此嚣张，只好表面上也唱唱高调，
Besides, the Pure Stream group is so clamorous that she has to echo its lofty sentiments in public.

实际上全不是这么回事。”
In reality, her wishes are quite different.”

“我懂了，
“I understand.

这是说不出的苦。”邵友濂又问：“第二个呢？”
It is a difficulty she cannot voice.” Shao asked, “And the second?”


“第二个是当政的恭王，
“The second is Prince Gong, who is in charge of affairs.

他一向主张跟洋人打交道，以和为贵，
He has always believed that, in dealings with foreigners, peace comes first.

如今上了年纪，更谈不上什么雄心壮志了。”
Now that he is older, he has even less appetite for grand ambitions.”

“英法联军内犯，恭王主和，让亲贵骂他是‘汉奸’、
“When the Anglo-French forces invaded, Prince Gong advocated peace and the imperial princes and grandees called him a traitor.

难怪他不敢开口。
No wonder he is afraid to speak.

可是，醇王一向主战，
But Prince Chun has always favoured war.

怎么也不作声呢？
Why is he silent too?”

““这就是关键所在。
“That is the crucial point.

如今的醇王，不是当年的醇王了，
The Prince Chun of today is not the man he was.

这几年洋人的坚甲利兵，”
In recent years, the foreigners’ strong armour and powerful weapons…”

盛宣怀停下来笑一笑说：
Sheng paused and smiled.

“说起来倒是受了湘阴的教，
“Indeed, it was Zuo who taught him that lesson.

西征军事顺手，全靠枪炮厉害，
The western campaign went so smoothly entirely because of the superiority of its firearms.

这一点湘阴在京的时候，跟醇王谈得很详细。
When Zuo was in the capital, he explained this to Prince Chun in detail.

醇王现在完全赞成中堂的主张，‘师夷之长以制夷’，
The prince now fully endorses His Excellency’s policy of learning the foreigners’ strengths in order to counter them.

正在筹划一个辟旅顺为军港，大办海军的办法。
A plan is being prepared to make Lushun a naval port and develop a substantial navy.

醇王对这件事，热中得不得了，
Prince Chun is intensely enthusiastic about it.

自然不愿‘小不忍而乱大谋’。”
Naturally, he does not want a moment’s impatience to ruin a great undertaking.”

“嗯！嗯！
“Quite so!

有这三位，中堂足足可以择善固执。”
With those three behind him, His Excellency has every reason to hold firmly to the course he believes right.”


“提到择善固执，还有个人不能忽略。
“Speaking of holding to the right course, there is another man we must not overlook.

筱村，你是出过洋的，
Xiaocun, you have been abroad.

你倒说说看，当今之世，论洋务人才，哪个是此中翘楚？”
Tell me: among all the men dealing with foreign affairs today, who is the foremost?”

“那当然是玉池老人。
“The Old Man of Yuchi, of course.

连曾侯办洋务都得向他请教。”
Even Marquis Zeng has to seek his advice on foreign affairs.”

“玉池老人”是郭嵩焘自署的别号，“曾侯”指驻法钦差大臣曾纪泽。
The Old Man of Yuchi was a name Guo Songtao had chosen for himself; Marquis Zeng was Zeng Jize, the imperial envoy to France.

事实上不仅曾纪泽，连李鸿章办洋务亦得向他请教，
In fact, not only Zeng but Li Hongzhang himself had to consult Guo.

因为李鸿章虽看得多，却不如郭嵩焘来得透彻，
Li had seen a great deal, but his insight was less penetrating than Guo’s.

同时亦因为李鸿章虽然亦是翰林，而学问毕竟不如郭嵩焘，
Moreover, although Li too had belonged to the Hanlin Academy, his scholarship could not match Guo’s.

发一议，立一论，能够贯通古今中外而无扞格，
Guo could advance an opinion or develop an argument that embraced ancient and modern times, China and foreign lands, without contradiction.

以李鸿章的口才，来解说郭嵩焘的理论，便越觉得动听了。
Expounded with Li’s eloquence, Guo’s ideas sounded more persuasive still.


“现在彭雪琴要请款招兵，王阆青已经在湖南招足了四千人，
“Now Peng Xueqin is asking for money to recruit troops, and Wang Langqing has already enlisted his full four thousand men in Hunan.

这就是湘阴派出去‘纵火’的人，
These are the men Zuo is sending out to set fires.

一旦祸发，立刻就成燎原之势。
Once trouble starts, it will spread like a blaze across the plains.

中堂为此，着急得很，
His Excellency is deeply anxious.

不说别的，只说法国军舰就在吴淞口外好了，
To take just one example, French warships are lying outside the mouth of the Wusong.

人家已经亲口告诉中堂了，随时可以攻制造局，
The French have told him to his face that they can attack the Manufacturing Bureau at any time.

这是北洋的命脉之一，
That is one of the lifelines of the Beiyang establishment.

你想，中堂着急不着急。”
How can he help being anxious?”

听得这话，邵友濂大吃一惊，
Shao was appalled.

他总以为中法如有冲突，不在广西，便在云南，
He had always assumed that any clash with France would occur in Guangxi or Yunnan.

如果进攻高昌庙的制造局，便是在上海作战，
An attack on the Manufacturing Bureau at Gaochangmiao would mean fighting in Shanghai.

他是上海道，守土有责，
As circuit intendant, he was responsible for defending his territory.

岂不是要亲自上阵跟法国军队对垒。
Would he have to go into battle himself against the French?

转念到此心胆俱裂，
The thought terrified him.

结结巴巴地说：“上海也有这样的话，
“People have been saying that in Shanghai too,” he stammered.

我总以为是谣言，
“I always took it for a rumour.

哪知道人家亲口告诉了中堂，是真有这回事！”
But if they told His Excellency themselves, then it is true!”

“你也不要着急。”盛宣怀安慰他说：
“There is no need to be alarmed,” Sheng reassured him.

“人家也不是乱来的，
“They do not act indiscriminately.

只要你不动手，就不会乱挑衅，
If you do not strike, they will not provoke a quarrel.

你要动手了，人家就会先发制人。”
But if you are going to strike, they will forestall you.”


这话说得再明白不过，
The meaning could not have been clearer.

邵友濂立即答说：“无论如何不可让湘阴把这把火烧起来。
Shao immediately replied, “Whatever happens, we cannot let Zuo start this fire.

放火要有放火的材料，
To set a fire, you need materials.

没有美孚牌煤油、没有一划就来的火柴，火就放不起来。
Without Mobil kerosene or matches that light at a stroke, there can be no blaze.

杏荪兄，你说是不是？”
Am I right, Brother Xingsun?”

“一点不错，
“Exactly.

这就叫釜底抽薪。”
That is what they call removing the firewood from under the pot.”

“要釜底抽薪，只有一个办法。”邵友濂说：
“And there is only one way to do it,” Shao said.

“煤油、火柴都在胡雪岩手里，
“Hu Xueyan has all the kerosene and matches.

没有胡雪岩，湘阴想放火也放下成。
Without him, Zuo could not start a fire even if he wanted to.

江宁官场都不大买湘阴的帐，他说出话去，多多少少要打折扣，
The officials in Jiangning pay little heed to Zuo; whatever he tells them to do, they comply only in part.

只有一个人，他说一是一，说二是二，就是胡雪岩，
There is just one man who does exactly what he says, and that is Hu.

譬如……”
For instance…”

譬如山东火灾助赈，江宁藩座无法支应，左宗棠向胡雪岩借银二十万，如响斯应，这一回王德榜募兵援越不但四千杆洋枪由胡雪岩筹划供给，补助路费亦雪岩负责等等，邵友濂举了好些实例。
He offered a number of examples. When relief was needed for the fire in Shandong and the Jiangning provincial treasurer could not provide the money, Zuo had asked Hu for a loan of two hundred thousand taels and received an immediate response. Now Wang Debang was recruiting men to assist Vietnam; Hu was arranging not only the supply of four thousand foreign rifles but also a contribution towards the troops’ travelling expenses.

结论是要使得左宗棠“纵火”不成，非除去胡雪岩不可。
The conclusion was that, to prevent Zuo from setting his fire, Hu Xueyan had to be put out of action.


“本常，”胡雪岩指着邵友濂复他的信说：“你看了这封信就晓得了，
“Benchang,” Hu said, pointing to Shao’s reply, “read this and you will understand.

人家说得很明白，各省的款子收齐了，马上送过来，
He makes it perfectly clear: as soon as all the provincial payments have been collected, he will send them over.

限期以前，一定办妥当，误了期限，一切责任由他来负。
Everything will be settled before the deadline, and if it is missed, he will take full responsibility.

他到底是上海道，
He is the Shanghai circuit intendant, after all.

说话算话，
His word means something.

不要紧的。”
There is nothing to worry about.”

宓本常看完了信问：“洋人的限期是哪一天？”
After reading it, Mi asked, “What deadline have the foreigners set?”

“放宽十天，
“They have allowed another ten days.

只要十一月初十以前付款，就不算违限。”
So long as the money is paid before the tenth of the eleventh month, it will not count as late.”

“呃，”
“Ah.

宓本常说，“大先生预备啥辰光回杭州？”
When do you intend to return to Hangzhou, sir?”

这句话问得胡雪岩大为不悦，
The question greatly displeased Hu.

“十一月初五的好日子。”他说：
“The wedding is on the fifth,” he said.

“今天是十月二十九，
“Today is the twenty-ninth of the tenth month.

你说我应该啥辰光动身回杭州？”
When do you think I ought to leave?”

由水路回杭州，用小火轮拖带，至少也要三天，
Even with a small steamer towing his boat, the journey to Hangzhou would take at least three days.

喜期以前，有许多繁文缛节，即便不必由他来料理主持，但必须由他出面来摆个样子，
Before the wedding there were many elaborate formalities; even if he did not have to arrange or preside over them, he had to put in an appearance.

所以无论如何，第二天——十月底一定要动身。
