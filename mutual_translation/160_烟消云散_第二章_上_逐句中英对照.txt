第二章 变起不测｜CHAPTER TWO: AN UNFORESEEN CRISIS


螺蛳太太已经上床了，丫头红儿来报，中门上传话进来，说旱康的档手谢云青求见。
Madam Luosi had already gone to bed when the maid Hong'er came to report a message from the inner gate: Xie Yunqing, the manager of Fukang Bank, wished to see her.

“这时候？”
“At this hour?”

螺蛳太大的心蓦地里往下一落，
Her heart suddenly sank.

莫非胡雪岩得了急病？
Could Hu Xueyan have fallen gravely ill?

她不敢再想下去了。
She dared not pursue the thought.

“太太！”红儿催问：“是不是叫他明天早上来？”
“Madam,” Hong'er prompted, “shall I ask him to come back tomorrow morning?”

“不，”
“No.

螺蛳太太说：“问问他，有什么事？”
Ask him what it is.”

“只说上海有电报来。”
“He only said there was a telegram from Shanghai.”

“到底什么事呢？
“But what is it about?

去问他。”
Go and ask.”

螺蛳太太转念，
Then she reconsidered.

不是急事，不会此刻求见，既是急事，就不能耽误工夫，
If it were not urgent, he would not be seeking an audience now; if it was, she must not waste time.

当即改口：“开中门，请谢先生进来。”她又加了一句：“不要惊动了老太太。”
“Open the inner gate and invite Mr Xie in,” she said instead, adding, “Don't disturb the old lady.”

红儿一走，别的丫头服侍螺蛳太太起床，穿着整齐，由丫头簇拥着下了楼。
As Hong'er left, the other maids helped her rise and dress properly, then accompanied her downstairs.

她也学会了矫情镇物的功夫，
She too had learned to master appearances and steady others by her bearing.

心里着急，脚步却依旧稳重，走路时裙幅几乎不动——
Though anxious within, she walked with measured steps, her skirt scarcely stirring.

会看相的都说她的“走相”主贵，
Those who read character and fortune from a person's gait had always said that hers promised distinction.

她本人亦颇矜持，所以怎么样也不肯乱了脚步。
Proud of her composure, she would not allow her steps to become disordered.


那谢云青礼数一向周到，
Xie Yunqing was always punctilious.

望见螺蛳太太的影子，老远就垂手肃立，眼观鼻、鼻观心地等候着，
At the first glimpse of her approaching figure, he stood respectfully with his hands at his sides, his gaze lowered in an attitude of complete restraint.

直到一阵香风飘来，闻出是螺蛳太太所用的外国香水，方始抬头作揖，口中说道：
Only when a breath of the foreign perfume she used reached him did he raise his head and bow with clasped hands.

“这样子夜深来打扰，实在过意不去。”
“I am very sorry to disturb you so late.”

“请坐。”
“Please sit.”

螺蛳太太左右看了一下，向站在门口的丫头发话：
She glanced around, then addressed the maids by the door.

“你们越来越没有规矩了，
“Your manners are getting worse.

客人来了，也不倒茶。”
A guest arrives, and you don't even bring tea.”

“不必客气，不必客气。
“Please don't trouble.

我接得一个消息，很有关系，不敢来告诉四太太。”
I've received important news and dared not keep it from you, Fourth Madam.”

“喔，请坐了谈。”
“Then sit down and tell me.”

说着，她摆一摆手，自己先在上首坐了下来。
She waved him to a seat and took the place of honour herself.

“是这样的。”谢云青斜欠着身子落座，声音却有些发抖了，
Xie perched deferentially on his chair, his voice beginning to tremble.

“刚刚接到电报，
“A telegram has just arrived.

上海挤兑，
There has been a run on the Shanghai bank.

下半天三点钟上排门了。”
They put up the shutters at three this afternoon.”

螺蛳太太心头一震，
Madam Luosi's heart jolted.

“没有弄错吧！”她问。
“There can be no mistake?”

“不会弄错的。”
“None.

谢云青又说：“电报上又说：宓本常人面不见，
The telegram also says that Mi Benchang cannot be found.

据说是到宁波去了。”
He is said to have gone to Ningbo.”

“那么，电报是哪个打来的呢？”
“Who sent it?”

“古先生。”
“Mr Gu.”

古应春打来的电报，决不会错。
A telegram from Gu Yingchun could not be mistaken.

螺蛳太太表面镇静，心里乱得头绪都握不住，
She maintained an outward calm, but her thoughts were in such turmoil that she could scarcely gather them.

好一会儿才问：“大先生呢？”
After a long pause, she asked, “Where is Mr Hu?”

“大先生想来是在路上。”
“Presumably on his way home.”

“怎么会有这种事？”螺蛳太太自语似地说：
“How could such a thing happen?” she murmured, almost to herself.

“宓本常这样子能干的人，怎么会撑不住，弄成这种局面？”
“With all Mi Benchang's ability, how could he fail to hold things together and let it come to this?”

谢云青无以为答，
Xie had no answer.

只搓着手说：“事情很麻烦，
Rubbing his hands, he could only say, “It's a very serious matter.

想都想不到的。”
Quite unimaginable.”


螺蛳太太蓦地打了个寒噤，
She suddenly shivered.

力持平静地问：“北京不晓得怎么样？”
Struggling to remain calm, she asked, “What about Beijing?”

“天津当然也有消息了，
“Tianjin must have heard already.

北京要晚一天才晓得。”谢云青说：
Beijing will learn of it a day later,” Xie said.

“牵一发而动全身，
“Pull one hair and the whole body moves.

明天这个关，只怕很难过。”
Tomorrow will be very hard to get through.”

螺蛳太太陡觉双肩有股无可比拟的巨大压力，何止千斤之重！
She felt an immense pressure descend on her shoulders, far heavier than any physical burden.

她想摆脱这股压力，但却不敢，
She longed to escape it but dared not.

因为这副无形中的千斤重担，如果她挑不起来，会伤及全家，
If she could not bear this invisible weight, the entire family would suffer.

而要想挑起来，且不说力有未胜，只一动念，便已气馁，
Yet even the thought of lifting it drained her courage, before any question of whether her strength was sufficient.

可是紧接着便是伤及全家，特别是伤及胡雪岩的警惕，
Then came the warning thought of harm to the family, and above all to Hu.

因而只有咬紧牙关，全力撑持着。
She could only grit her teeth and support it with all her might.

“大先生在路上。”
“Mr Hu is on the road.

她说：“老太太不敢惊动，另外一位太太是拿不出主意的，
We dare not alarm the old lady, and the other madam cannot devise a plan.

谢先生，你有什么好主意？”
What do you suggest, Mr Xie?”

谢云青原是来讨主意的，
He had come to ask her for a decision.

听得这话，只有苦笑，
He could only smile bitterly.

他倒是有个主意，却不敢说出来。
There was an idea in his mind, but he dared not voice it.

沉默了一会，依旧是螺蛳太太开口。
After a silence, she spoke again.


“谢先生，照你看，明天一定会挤兑？”
“In your judgement, there is bound to be a run tomorrow?”

“是的。”
“Yes.”

“大概要多少银子才能应付？”
“How much silver would we need to meet it?”

“这很难说。”
“That is hard to say.

谢云青说：“阜康开出去的票子，光是我这里就有一百四十多万，
Here alone, Fukang has notes outstanding for more than one million four hundred thousand taels.

存款就更加多了。”
The deposits are greater still.”

“那么钱庄里现银有多少呢？”
“How much cash is in the bank?”

“四十万上下。”
“About four hundred thousand.”

螺蛳太太考虑又考虑之后说：
She considered the matter repeatedly.

“有四十万现银，我想撑一两夭总撑得住，
“With four hundred thousand in ready silver, I should think we can hold out for a day or two.

那时候大先生已经回来了。”
By then Mr Hu will be back.”

谢云青心想，照此光景，就胡雪岩回来了，也不见得有办法，
Xie thought that, judging by what had happened, even Hu might have no solution.

否则上海的阜康何至于“上排门”，
Otherwise, why would the Shanghai branch have had to close its doors?

不过这话不便直说，
But he could not say that plainly.

他只问道：“万一撑不住呢？”
He asked only, “And if we cannot hold out?”


这话如能答得圆满，根本就不必谢云青黄夜求见女东家。
Had she possessed a satisfactory answer to that question, he would never have needed to seek out his employer's wife in the middle of the night.

“谢先生，”螺蛳太太反问道：“你说，
“Tell me yourself, Mr Xie.

万一撑不住会怎么样？”
What happens if we cannot?”

“会出事，会伤人。”谢云青说：
“There will be trouble, and people will get hurt.

“譬如说，早来的、手长的，先把现银提走了，后来的一落空，
Suppose those who arrive early, or can reach farther, take away the cash first, while the latecomers get nothing.

四太太你倒设身处地想一想，
Put yourself in their place, Fourth Madam.

心里火不火？”
Wouldn't you be furious?”

这是个不必回答的疑问，螺蛳太太只说：
The question needed no answer.

“请你说下去。”
“Go on.”

“做事情最怕犯众怒，
“The worst thing is to provoke a crowd's anger.

一犯众怒，官府都弹压不住，
Once that happens, even the authorities cannot suppress it.

钱庄打得粉碎不说，只怕还会到府上来吵，
Besides smashing the bank to pieces, they may come here to make trouble.

吵成什么样子，就难说了。”
Where that would end is impossible to say.”

螺蛳太太悚然而惊，
She was horrified.

勉强定一定心，从头细想了一遍说：
Forcing herself to settle her thoughts, she went over the problem again.

“犯众怒是因为有的人有，有的人没有，
“They become angry because some receive their money and some don't.

不公平了！
It is unfair.

索性大家都没有，倒也是一种公平，
If no one receives anything, that is a kind of fairness too.

谢先生，你想呢？”
What do you think?”

“四太太，”谢云青平静地说：“你想通了。”
“Fourth Madam,” Xie said calmly, “you have understood.”

“好！”
“Very well!”

螺蛳太太觉得这副千斤重担，眼前算是挑得起来了，
For the moment, she felt she could shoulder the burden.

“明天不开门，
“We won't open tomorrow.

不过要对客户有个交代。”
But we must give the customers an explanation.”

“当然，
“Naturally.

只说暂时歇业，请客户不必惊慌。”
We shall say the closure is temporary and ask them not to be alarmed.”

“意思是这个意思，话总要说得婉转。” “我明白。”谢云青又说：“听说四太太同德藩台的内眷常有往来的？”
“That is the meaning, but the words must be tactful.” “I understand.” “I hear you are often in touch with Provincial Treasurer De's household?” Xie continued.


德藩台是指浙江藩司德馨，字晓峰，此人在旗，
Treasurer De was Dexin, courtesy name Xiaofeng, a Bannerman serving as provincial treasurer of Zhejiang.

与胡雪岩的交情很深，所以两家内眷，常有往还。
His friendship with Hu was close, and the women of the two households often visited one another.

螺蛳太太跟德馨的一个宠妾且是“拜把子”的姐妹。
Madam Luosi and one of his favourite concubines were sworn sisters.

“不错。”
“Yes.

螺蛳太太问：“怎么样？”
What about it?”

“明天一早，请四太太到藩台衙门去一趟，
“Go to his residence first thing tomorrow, if you would.

最好能见着德藩台，当面托一托他，
It would be best to see him himself and ask for help in person.

有官府出面来维持，就比较容易过关了。”
With the authorities intervening to maintain order, we shall have a better chance of getting through this.”

“好的，
“Very well.

我去。”
I'll go.

螺蛳太太问：“还有什么应该想到，马上要做的？”
Is there anything else we should consider and do immediately?”


一直萦绕在螺蛳太太心头的一个难题是，这样一个从来没有想到过的大变化，要不要跟大太太说？
One question kept troubling her: should she tell the First Madam of this wholly unforeseen upheaval?

胡家中门以内是“一国三公”的局面，
Within the Hu family's inner gate, there were, as the saying went, three rulers in one state.

凡事名义上是老太太主持，好比慈禧太后的“垂帘听政”，
Nominally, the old lady presided over everything, like Empress Dowager Cixi ruling from behind the curtain.

大太太仿佛恭亲王，螺蛳太太就象前两年去世的沈桂芬。
The First Madam resembled Prince Gong, while Madam Luosi was like Shen Guifen, who had died two years earlier.

曾经有个姓吴的翰林，写过一首诗，题目叫做《小姑叹》，将由山西巡抚内调入军机的沈桂芬，比做归宁的小姑，
A Hanlin scholar named Wu had once written a poem called “The Younger Sister-in-Law's Lament,” comparing Shen, who had been summoned from the governorship of Shanxi into the Grand Council, to a daughter returning to her parents' home.

深得母欢，以致当家的媳妇，大权旁落，一切家务都由小姑秉承母命而行。
So beloved by her mother was this daughter that the daughter-in-law in charge of the household lost her authority: all domestic business was conducted by the daughter under her mother's instructions.

如果说天下是满洲人的天下，作为满人的沈桂芬，确似归宁或者居娟的姑奶奶，越粗代庖在娘家主持家务。
If the empire was the Manchus' family property, then the Manchu Shen did resemble a daughter coming home for a visit, or returning as a widow, and taking over the management of her natal household.


胡家的情形最相象的一点是，老太太喜欢螺蛳太太，就象慈禧太后宠信沈桂芬那样，
The closest parallel in the Hu family was the old lady's affection for Madam Luosi, which resembled Cixi's trust in Shen.

每天“上朝”——一早在胡老太太那里商量这夭有什么要紧的事要办，通常都是螺蛳太太先提出来，胡老太太认可，
At the daily morning “audience,” when the day's important business was discussed, Madam Luosi usually raised a matter and the old lady approved it.

或者胡老太太问到，螺蛳太太提出意见来商量，
Alternatively, the old lady would ask and Madam Luosi would propose a solution.

往往言听计从，
Her suggestions were almost always followed.

决定之后才由胡老太太看着大太太问一句：“你看呢？”
Only after a decision had been reached would the old lady turn to the First Madam and ask, “What do you think?”

有时甚至连这句话都不问。
Sometimes even that was omitted.

但是，真正为难的事，是不问胡老太太的，尤其是坏消息，更要瞒住。
Truly difficult matters, however, were not put to the old lady at all, and bad news was particularly to be concealed.

螺蛳太太的做法是，能作主就作主了，不能作主问胡雪岩。
Madam Luosi decided what she could herself and asked Hu about what she could not.

倘或胡雪岩不在而必要作主，这件事又多少有责任，或许会受埋怨时，螺蛳太太就会跟大太太去商量，这样做并不是希望大太太会有什么好办法拿出来，而是要她分担责任。
If he was absent and a decision carrying some responsibility or risk of blame had to be made, she would consult the First Madam, hoping not for a useful idea but for someone to share the responsibility.

不过这晚上谢云青来谈的这件事是太大了，情形也太坏了，
Tonight's news was too serious.

胡老太太如果知道了，会受惊吓，即令是大太太，只怕也会急出病来。
It would terrify the old lady; even the First Madam might fall ill with anxiety.

但如不告诉她，自己单独作了决走，这个责任实在担不起，
Yet to decide alone would be more responsibility than she could bear.

告诉她呢，不能不考虑后果——
Telling her, on the other hand, meant considering the consequences.

谢云青说得不错，如今要把局势稳住，自己先不能乱，
Xie was right: to steady the situation, they must first keep their own composure.

外面谣言满天飞都还不要紧，
Rumours outside were one thing.

倘由胡家的人说一句撑不下去的话，那就一败涂地，无药可救了。
A single admission from within the family that they could not hold on would bring complete and irretrievable ruin.


“太太！”
“Madam!”

螺蛳太太微微一惊，抬眼看去，
She started slightly and looked up.

是大丫头阿云站在门口，
A'yun, her senior maid, stood in the doorway.

她如今代替了瑞香的地位，成为螺蛳太太最信任的心腹，
She had replaced Ruixiang as Madam Luosi's most trusted confidante.

此时穿一件玫瑰紫软缎小套夹，揉一揉惺松的倦眼，顿时面露惊讶之色。
Wearing a soft rose-purple satin lined jacket, she rubbed her sleepy eyes and suddenly looked astonished.

“太太没有睡过？”
“Haven't you slept?”

“嗯！”
“No.

螺蛳太太说：“倒杯茶我喝。”
Pour me some tea.”

阿云去倒了茶，一面递，一面说：“红鬼告诉我，谢先生半夜里来见太太……”“不要多问。”
A'yun brought it and said as she handed it over, “Hong'er told me Mr Xie came in the middle of the night—” “Don't ask questions.”

螺蛳太太略有些不耐烦地挥着手。
Madam Luosi waved her away with some impatience.

就这时更锣又响，晨钟亦动，
Just then, the watch gong sounded again and the morning bell began to ring.

阿云回头望了一眼，失惊地说：
A'yun glanced back in alarm.

“五点钟了，
“It's five o'clock.

太太再不睡，天就要亮了。
If you don't sleep now, it will soon be daylight.

今天‘大冰太太’来吃第十三只鸡，
Madam Matchmaker is coming for her thirteenth chicken today.

老太太特为关照，要太太也陪，
The old lady particularly asked you to be there.

再不睡一息，精神怎么够？”
How will you manage unless you get a little sleep?”

杭州的官宦人家称媒人为“大冰老爷”，女媒便是“大冰太太”，
In Hangzhou's official households, a male matchmaker was respectfully called Master Matchmaker and a woman Madam Matchmaker.

作媒叫做“吃十三只半鸡”，
Arranging a marriage was known as “eating thirteen and a half chickens.”

因为按照六礼的程序，自议婚到嫁娶，媒人往还于乾坤两宅，须十三趟之多，
Under the procedures of the Six Rites, a matchmaker made as many as thirteen journeys between the prospective bride's and groom's homes.

每来应以盛馔相飨，至少也要杀鸡款待，
Each visit required a fine meal, with at least a chicken killed in the visitor's honour.

而笑媒人贪嘴，花轿出发以前，还要来扰一顿，不过匆匆忙忙只来得及吃半只鸡，
The joke was that a greedy matchmaker would come for another meal just before the bridal chair departed, but in the rush could eat only half a chicken.

因而谓之为“吃十三只半鸡”。
Hence thirteen and a half.

这天是胡三小姐的媒人来谈最后的细节，
Today, the matchmaker for Hu's Third Miss was coming to discuss the final details.

下一趟来，便是十一月初五花轿到门之前，吃半只鸡的时候了。
Her next visit would be for the half-chicken meal before the bridal chair arrived on the fifth day of the eleventh lunar month.


螺蛳太太没有接她的话，
Madam Luosi did not answer her point.

只叹口气说：
She only sighed.

“三小姐也命苦。”紧接着又说：“你到梦香楼去看看，那边太太醒了没有？
“Third Miss has a hard fate too.” Then she added, “Go to Mengxiang Tower and see whether the madam there is awake.

如果醒了，说我要去看她。”
If she is, say I want to see her.”

“此刻？”
“Now?”

“当然是此刻。”
“Of course now!”

螺蛳太太有些发怒，
She was growing angry.

“你今天早上怎么了？
“What's the matter with you this morning?

话都听不清楚！”
Can't you understand anything?”

阿云不敢作声，悄悄地走了，
A'yun dared say no more and slipped away.

大太太住的梦香楼很有一段路，
Mengxiang Tower, where the First Madam lived, was some distance off.

所以直到螺蛳太太喝完一杯热茶，阿云方始回来，后面跟着大太太的心腹丫头阿兰。
Madam Luosi had finished a cup of hot tea before A'yun returned, followed by A'lan, the First Madam's trusted maid.

“梦香楼太太正好醒了，
“Madam had just woken up.

叫我到床前问：啥事情？
She called me to her bedside and asked what it was.

我说：不清楚。
I said I didn't know.

她问：是不是急事？
She asked whether it was urgent.

我说：这时候要谈，想来是急事，
I said that, if you wanted to talk at this hour, I supposed it must be.
