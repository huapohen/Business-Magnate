胡雪岩
《烟消云散》第二章（下，至本章结束）
Vanishing into Thin Air — Chapter Two, Part Two (conclusion)
逐句英中对照
原著：高阳


Yueru had seldom entered the Hu residence in recent years.
月如近年来难得进府。

There were several reasons, but chiefly she dreaded meeting her old companions.
原因很多，最主要的是怕见旧日伙伴，

Once a kitchen drudge tending the fires, she had unexpectedly “flown up to a branch and become a phoenix,” and envy was inevitable.
原是烧火丫头，不道“飞上枝头作凤凰”，难免遭人妒嫉，

Some called her Mistress Tang, others Madam Tang; neither sounded as pleasant as her own name.
有的叫她“唐姨太”，有的叫她“唐师母”，总不如听人叫月如来得顺耳。

Since her affair had become common knowledge, the question she dreaded most was, “Has the master been to your place for a meal these last two days?”
尤其是从她出了新闻以后，她最怕听的一句话就是：“老爷这两天有没有到你那里吃饭？”

Today circumstances forced her to brave a visit.
这天情势所逼，只好硬着头皮去走一趟，

She entered through the back door of the main kitchen.
由大厨房后门进府，

Beside it stood an open hall where the maids and serving women from the different households collected boiling water and gathered to talk.
旁边一间敞厅，是各房仆妇丫头到大厨房来提开水、聚会之地，

Today two large baskets stood on a long table, and more than a dozen maids were wrapping osmanthus sweets in pieces of red paper.
这天长条桌上摆着两个大箩筐，十几个丫头用裁好的红纸在包“桂花糖”——

In Hangzhou, people expected these sweets at weddings in households great and small.
杭州大小人家嫁娶都要讨“桂花糖”吃，

White sugar was mixed with osmanthus blossoms, tinted with rose or mint juice, and pressed into small molds of various shapes.
白糖加上桂花，另用玫瑰、薄荷的浆汁染色，用小模子制成各种花样，

Each piece was about the size of a thumb: delicate, delicious, and fun to play with, a delight to children.
每粒拇指大小，玲珑精致，又好吃、又好玩，是孩子们的恩物。

For the Third Miss’s wedding, four hundred jin of specially enriched sweets had been ordered from Fang Yuhe.
胡三小姐出阁，在方裕和定制了四百斤加料的桂花糖，

They had arrived that morning, and maids from the different households had been called in to help.
这夭早晨刚刚送到，找了各房丫头来帮忙。

A’ju, who tended the lamps, candles, and incense for Old Madam Hu, happened to be sitting by the door.
进门之处恰好有个在胡老太太那里管烛火香蜡的丫头阿菊，

A good friend of Yueru’s, she shifted along and patted the table.
与月如一向交好，便往里缩了一下，拍拍长条桌说：

“Just in time to help.”
“正好来帮忙。”

Yueru sat beside her and glanced around.
月如便挨着她坐了下来，先抬眼看一看，

Those she knew greeted her silently with their eyes.
熟识的几个都用眼色默然地打了招呼，

Even the two most talkative girls said nothing today.
平时顶爱讲话的两个，这天亦不开口，

Naturally, there were no smiles.
各人脸上，当然亦不会有什么笑容。


Yueru dared not speak loudly in such an atmosphere.
见此光景，月如亦就不敢高声说话了，

“Will the Third Miss’s wedding have to be postponed?” she asked, putting her chief concern first.
“三小姐的喜事，会不会改日子？”她先问她最关心的一件事。

“Can’t you see we’re still wrapping the sweets?” A’ju answered quietly.
“你不看仍旧在包桂花糖？”阿菊低声答说：

“At first light, the First Madam and Madam Luosi told everyone in the Common Hall that everything was to go on as usual.”
“今朝天朦朦亮，大太太、螺蛳太太在‘公所’交代，一切照常。”

“How could this happen?
“怎么会出这种事？”

How is the Third Miss?
月如问说：“三小姐怎么样？

Has she been crying?”
有没有哭？”

“Crying?
“哭？

What for?
为啥？

What has it to do with her?”
跟三小姐啥相干？”

“Such a thing happening just before her wedding—she must be upset.”
“大喜日子，遇到这种事，心里总难过的。”

“Being upset is one thing, but whoever heard of a bride crying?
“难过归难过，要做新娘子，哪里有哭的道理？

Still,” A’ju added, “she certainly can’t manage a smile.”
不过，”阿菊说道：“笑是笑不出来的！”

Yueru lowered her voice almost to a whisper. “A’ju, do you think it’s serious?”
“你看，阿菊，”月如将声音压得极低，“要紧不要紧？”

“What do you mean, serious?”
“什么要紧不要紧？”

“I mean, could it…”
“我是说会不会……”

“Could the whole thing collapse? Is that what you mean?”
“会不会倒下来是不是？”

A’ju shook her head.
阿菊摇摇头，

“I’m afraid it’s hard to say.”
“恐怕难说。”

“Collapse?” Yueru asked in alarm.
“会倒？”月如吃惊地问：

“Really?”
“真的？”

“Don’t carry on like that!”
“你不要这样子！”

A’ju gave her a reproachful look.
阿菊白了她一眼，

“Madam Luosi hates people making a fuss.”
“螺蛳太太最恨人家大惊小怪。”

Realizing she had lost her composure, Yueru resumed a calmer tone.
月如也自知失态，改用平静的声音说：

“What makes you think it might collapse?”
“你从哪里看出来的，说不定会倒？”

“People are too wicked!”
“人心太坏！”

There was much behind those words, well worth probing, but before Yueru could ask, a serving woman from her own house hurried in.
话中大有文章，值得打听，但是来不及开口，月如家的一个老妈子赶了来通知，

Tang Zishao wanted her home at once.
唐子韶要她赶紧回家。


“Where are those pawn tickets?” Tang Zishao asked.
“那几张当票呢？”唐子韶问。

Yueru opened her jewelry box and took out a stack of them, which he examined one by one.
月如开了首饰箱，取出一叠当票，唐子韶一张一张细看。

She could recognize a few characters, but the wild cursive scrawls on pawn tickets were utterly beyond her.
月如虽也认得几个字，但当票上那笔“鬼画符”的草书，只字不识，

When he selected three and laid them aside, she asked, “What are those for?”
看他捡出三张摆在一边，便即问说：“是些啥东西？”

Tang had resorted to every conceivable form of fraud at Gongji Pawnshop.
原来唐子韶在公济典舞弊的手法，无所不用其极，

Besides tampering with goods whose redemption periods had expired, he would make lists of particularly valuable unredeemed articles and send them to the Hu residence.
除了在满当货上动手脚以外，另外一种是看满当的日期已到，原主未赎，而当头珍贵，开单子送进府里，

If one of the concubines in the Twelve Chambers took a fancy to something and wanted to keep it, a new ticket would be issued on the pretext that the original had been lost.
“十二楼”中的姨太太，或许看中了要留下来，便以“挂失”为名，另开一张当票。

There were also tickets sold by their owners, or acquired from dubious sources—picked from pockets or stolen by thieves—which Tang bought for next to nothing and entrusted to Yueru, to be redeemed when convenient.
此外还有原主出卖，或者来路不明，譬如“扒几手”扒来，甚至小偷偷来的当票，以极低的价钱收了下来，都交给月如保管，看情形取赎。

The three he had selected were to be redeemed now.
这捡出来的三张，便是预备赎取的，

One covered a hat ornament: an exceptionally large, flawless Persian emerald worth two thousand taels at current prices, on which only five hundred had been lent.
一张是一枚帽花，极大极纯的一块波斯祖母绿，时价值两千银子，只当了五百两；

Another covered a set of silver tableware weighing six hundred taels.
一张是一副银台面，重六百两，

That did not mean a loan of six hundred taels, for a deduction had to be made for the loss in remelting; its purity was also pronounced deficient, and interest was deducted.
却当不得六百银子，因为回炉要去掉“火耗”，又说它成色不足，再扣去利息，

After one reduction and another, the six hundred taels of silver had yielded a loan of only three hundred.
七折八扣下来，六百两银子减掉一半，只当三百两，

Yet making another set to match it would cost at least a thousand taels.
可是照样打这么一副，起码要一千银子。


The third article was even more valuable: a pair of diamond bracelets set with twelve large diamonds and sixty-four small ones.
第三张就更贵重了，是一副钻镯，大钻十二、小钻六十四，

Without counting the workmanship, the diamonds alone were worth eight thousand taels, but the loan had been only two thousand.
不算镶工，光是金刚钻就值八千两银子，只当得二千两，

Tang had bought the ticket from a petty thief for eight taels.
是从一个小毛贼那里花八两银子买来的，

The next day the owner’s servant had come rushing in, distraught, to report its loss.
第二天，原主的听差气急败坏来挂失，

Tang had attended to him personally.
唐子韶亲自接待，说一声：

“I’m terribly sorry.
“实在很对不起，

Someone has already redeemed the goods.”
已经有人来赎走了。”

He had produced the ticket, which the owner’s side acknowledged was indeed the right one.
拿出当票来看，原主都说“不错”，

But who had redeemed them?
但问到是什么人来赎的？

Again: “I’m terribly sorry.
又是一声：“实在对不起，

I have no idea.”
不晓得。”

Throughout the empire’s eighteen provinces, pawnshops followed the same rule: they recognized the ticket, not the person presenting it.
天下十八省的当铺，规矩是一样的，认票不认人，

The servant could only return dejectedly to report to his master.
来人只好垂头丧气去回复主人。

“Get these three tickets taken care of at once,” Tang now said.
“这三张票子赶紧料理。”唐子韶说，

“Fukang holds a great deal of public money.
“阜康存了许多公款，

Every authority from the Qiantang and Renhe county offices up to the governor’s yamen has grounds to seal the pawnshops.
从钱塘、仁和两县到抚台衙门，都有权来封典当，

Unless we redeem the goods, we’ll simply lose them.”
不赎出来，白白葬送在里面。”

“If Fukang fails, what has that to do with Gongji Pawnshop?”
“阜康倒了，跟公济典有啥关系？”

“How can you ask such a question?
“亏你问得出这种话！

Anything belonging to Mr Hu can be seized.”
只要是胡大先生的产业都可以封。”

With that, Tang hurried away.
说完，唐子韶匆匆忙忙地去了。


Yueru saw him to the door and took the opportunity to look at the commotion outside.
月如送他到门口，顺便看看热闹。

Her house stood in a back street with little traffic, but the clamor from the main street was clearly audible.
她家住在后街，来往的人不多，但前面大街上人声嘈杂，却听得很清楚，

Among the voices she thought she could hear gongs and calls to clear the way.
其中隐隐有鸣锣喝道之声，

Listening carefully, she found she was right.
凝神静听，果然不错，

Tang’s warning came back to her, and she started in alarm.
月如想起刚才唐子韶说过的话，不由得一惊，

Could officials really be coming to seal Fukang Bank and Gongji Pawnshop?
莫非宫府真的来封阜康钱庄与公济典了？

The truth was precisely the reverse of her guess.
她的猜测恰好相反，

Provincial Treasurer Dexin, accompanied by Wu Yun, the prefect of Hangzhou, was coming to urge Fukang to reopen, rather than to seal its doors.
由杭州府知府吴云陪着来的藩司德馨，不是来封阜康的门，而是劝阜康开门营业。

Earlier that morning, following Xie Yunqing’s suggestion, Madam Luosi had gone by sedan chair to the provincial treasurer’s yamen to visit his favorite concubine.
原来这天上午，螺蛳太太照谢云青的建议，特地坐轿到藩司衙门去看德藩台的宠妾。

The building was said to have been the residence of Qin Hui, the powerful Southern Song chief minister.
相传这座衙门是南宋权相秦桧的住宅，

According to another story, the two great pools enclosed by stone balustrades outside concealed water gates leading to the provincial treasury, guarded by the enormous soft-shelled turtles that Hangzhou people called “scabby-headed turtles.”
又说门前两座石栏围绕的大池，隐藏着藩库的水门，池中所养的大鼋，杭州人称之为“癫头鼋”，便是用来看守藩库水门的，

Such legends were difficult to verify, but watching the turtles outside the treasurer’s yamen had been a familiar city pastime for decades.
这些传说，虽难查证，但“藩司前看癞头鼋”，是杭州城里市井中的一景，却是亘亘数十年不改。

Normally Madam Luosi paid no attention to the people sitting or leaning against the balustrades as her sedan passed.
螺蛳太太每次轿子经过，看池边石栏上，或坐或倚的人群，从未有何感觉，

Today she imagined them pointing and saying, “Look, that’s Mr Hu’s Madam Luosi in the chair.
这天却似乎觉得那些闲人指指点点，都在说她：“喏，那轿子里坐的就是胡大先生的螺蛳太太。

The God of Wealth has fallen and lost even his shoes.
财神跌倒，变成赤脚，

Now she has to show herself in public and beg for help.”
螺蛳太太也要抛头露面来求人家了。”

These imaginings made her heart ache.
这样胡思乱想着，她心里酸酸的，

Suddenly her eyes grew hot; she hastily wiped away the tears and steadied herself.
突然觉得眼眶发热，赶紧拭去眼泪，强自把心定下来，

It would be all right, she told herself.
自己对自己说：不要紧的！

Whatever happened, she must not be the first to show alarm.
无论如何自己不可先摆出着急的样子。

Recalling how she usually behaved on these visits, she resolved to do everything as usual.
于是她将平日来了以后的情形回忆了一下，警惕着一切如常，不能有甚异样的态度。

Her sedan was unusually splendid, and she had always tipped generously.
由于她那乘轿子格外华丽，更由于她平时出手大方，

Before her card bearer could announce her at the side entrance, one of the treasurer’s attendants came out and opened both doors.
所以未进侧门以前，不待执帖家人上前通报，便有德藩台的听差迎了出来，敞开双扉，

The sedan passed along the covered passage west of the main hall, and she alighted at the garden entrance.
容她的轿子沿着正厅西面的雨道，在花园入口处下轿。


The treasurer’s favorite concubine was named Lianzhu.
德藩台的宠妾，名叫莲珠，

Second-born in her family and a year older than Madam Luosi, she had exchanged sworn-sisterhood pledges with her.
在家行二，她们是换帖姐妹，莲珠比螺狮太太大一岁，

Madam Luosi called her Second Sister; Lianzhu called Madam Luosi Fourth Sister.
所以称之为二姐，莲珠唤她四妹，

They greeted one another with their usual affectionate names and exchanged a few pleasantries.
出来迎接时，象平时一样，彼此叫应了略作寒暄，

But once they were inside, even before they had settled into their seats, Lianzhu’s expression changed.
但一进屋尚未坐定，莲珠的神情就不一样了。

“Fourth Sister,” she said, taking Madam Luosi’s hand, “what is happening?
“四妹，”她执着螺蛳太太的手，满腹疑惑地问：“是怎么回事？

Early this morning I heard Fukang hadn’t opened, and I said it couldn’t be true.
一早听人说，阜康不开门，我说没有的事。

Only when the master came in just now and I asked him did I learn that the Shanghai Fukang has failed, and that there are crowds outside the bank here and trouble may break out.
刚刚我们老爷进来，我问起来才知道上海的阜康倒了，这里挤满了人，怕要出事。

He kept sighing, and I’ve been worried too.
我们老爷只是叹气，我也着急，

How serious is it?”
到底要紧不要紧？”

Her words threw Madam Luosi into fresh confusion.
这一番话说得螺蛳太太心里七上八下，

Her face felt hot.
自己觉得脸上有点发烧，

She struggled to remain calm, but it was quite impossible to laugh and talk as she normally did.
但力持镇静，不过要想象平时那样有说有笑，却怎么样也办不到了。

“How could it not be serious?
“怎么不要紧？

A gilded signboard is hard to polish bright and very easy to soil,” she said slowly.
一块金字招牌，擦亮来不容易，要弄脏它很方便。”螺蛳太太慢条斯里地说：

“The trouble is that our master is still on the road, with neither Shanghai nor Hangzhou able to reach him.
“怪只怪我们老爷在路上，上海、杭州两不接头。

Even if a woman like me comes out in public, who will listen?
我一个女人家，就抛头露面，哪个来理我？

I can only ask the treasurer for help.”
说不得只好来求藩台了。”

“Between our two families, there’s no question of asking a favor.” Lianzhu called a maid.
“以我们两家的交情，说不上一个求字。”莲珠唤来一个丫头说：

“Go to the inner gate and send word to A’fu: as soon as the master has finished receiving visitors, ask him to come in.”
“你到中门上传话给阿福，看老爷会客完了，马上请他进来。”


A’fu was Dexin’s personal valet.
阿福是德馨的贴身跟班，

On receiving the message from the inner gate, he took the opportunity of filling his master’s water pipe to whisper, “Madam wishes to see you.”
接到中门上传来的消息，便借装水烟袋之便，悄悄在德馨耳际说了一句：“姨太太请。”

Dexin had several concubines, but “Madam,” without qualification, meant Lianzhu.
德馨有好几个妾，但不加区别仅称“姨太太”便是指莲珠。

What could be so urgent that she could not wait until he returned to the inner apartments for lunch?
心想她有什么要紧事，等不及他回上房吃午饭时谈？

It must be something to do with the Hus.
一定是胡家的事。

He turned to the expectant circuit intendant he was receiving.
这样想着，便对正在会见的一个候补道说：

“My dear fellow, I’m not yet entirely familiar with this case.
“你老哥谈的这件案子，兄弟还不十分清楚，

Let me look into it, and we can discuss it later.”
等我查过了再商量吧！”

Without allowing an answer, he lifted his teacup.
接着不由分说，端一端茶碗，

An attendant in the reception-hall corridor immediately called, “See the gentleman out!”
花厅廊上的听差，便高唱一声：“送客！”

The expectant intendant was thus summarily dismissed.
将那候补道硬生生地撵走了。

Four visiting cards remained: three expectant county magistrates and the serving magistrate of Haining Department.
看“手本”，还有四客要接见，三个是候补知县。一个是现任海宁州知州，

Dexin hesitated, then singled out two.
他踌躇了一回，先剔出两个手本，

“No time for these gentlemen today,” he said, almost to himself.
自语似他说：“这两位，今天没工夫了。”

A’fu took the cards and saw that one belonged to a man named Liu, who had given him a substantial gatekeeper’s tip.
阿福取手本来一看，其中一个姓刘，送过很大的一个门包，便即说道：

“Madam has given instructions about this Mr Liu.”
“这位刘大老爷是姨太太交代过的。”

“What instructions?”
“交代什么？”

“He wants the assignment of escorting next year’s grain shipment.
“刘大老爷想讨个押运明年渣米的差使。

Madam said you must appoint him.”
姨太太交代。老爷一定要派。”

“If I’m bound to appoint him, there’s no need to see him.”
“既然一定要派，就不必见了。”

“What shall I tell him, then?”
“那么，怎么样回他？”

“To wait at home for word.”
“叫他在家听信好了。”

“Yes, sir.”
“是。”

“As for this one…”
“这一位，”

Dexin picked up another card, paused, then cut through the difficulty at a stroke: he pushed that card away together with the Haining magistrate’s.
德馨拿起另一份手本，沉吟了一下，用快刀斩乱麻的手法，连海宁州知州的手本，一起往外一推：

“Say I’m feeling unwell.
“说我人不舒服，

Ask them to come back tomorrow.”
都请他们明天再来。”


He rose, returned to the inner apartments through a side door of the reception hall, and went straight to Lianzhu’s rooms.
说完，起身由花厅角门回到上房，径自到了莲珠那里。

Madam Luosi hastily stood and made a formal curtsy.
螺蛳太太一见急忙起身，裣袄为礼。

Dexin was a close friend of Hu Xueyan, whom he privately called Brother Hu; to Madam Luosi he used the familiar Sister Luo.
德馨跟胡雪岩的交情很厚，私底下管他叫“胡大哥”，对螺蛳太太便叫“罗四姐”，

His first question was, “Sister Luo, when will Xueyan be back?”
他一开口便问：“罗四姐，雪岩什么时候回来？”

“This afternoon.”
“今天下半天。”

“Ah!”
“唉！”

He stamped his foot.
他顿一顿足说：

“Just one day too late.”
“就差这么一天工夫。”

He meant that if Hu had arrived yesterday, today’s crisis would never have arisen.
意思是胡雪岩只要昨天到，今天的局面就不会发生。

Madam Luosi had no idea how he might resolve it, but his willingness to do everything he could was evident.
螺蛳太太不知道他能用什么办法来解消危机？但愿倾全力相助的心意是很明显的。

In adversity, people feel kindness all the more keenly.
