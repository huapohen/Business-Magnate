第三章 仗义执言｜CHAPTER THREE: SPEAKING UP FOR JUSTICE


杭州府知府吴云，一名吴世荣，到任才一个多月，对于杭州的情形还不十分熟悉。
Wu Yun, also known as Wu Shirong, had been Prefect of Hangzhou for little more than a month and was not yet thoroughly familiar with local conditions.

德馨邀他一起去为阜康纾困，觉得有几句话，必须先要交代。
Before taking him along to help Fukang out of its difficulties, Dexin felt there were a few things he must explain.

“世荣兄，”他说：“杭州人名为‘杭铁头’，
“Brother Shirong,” he said, “Hangzhou people are known as ‘Iron Heads.’

吃软不吃硬，
They respond to kindness, but not to force.

硬碰的话，会搞得下不了台，
Confront them head-on and you may find yourself in a humiliating position.

以前巡抚、学政常有在杭州吃了亏的事，你总听说过？”
You must have heard how provincial governors and education commissioners have come to grief here?”

“听说过‘万马无声听号令，一牛独坐看文章’。”
“I have heard the story of ‘Ten thousand horses silently await commands; one ox sits alone appraising compositions.’”

吴世荣是听说有一个浙江学政，赋性刻薄，戏侮士子，
Wu had heard of a harsh Zhejiang education commissioner who delighted in humiliating scholars.

孝试时怕彼此交头接耳，通同作弊，下令每人额上贴一张长纸条，一端黏在桌上，
To prevent candidates from whispering together and cheating during an examination, he ordered a long strip of paper pasted to each man’s forehead, with the other end fixed to his desk.

出了个试帖诗题是：“万马无声听号令，得瘏字。”
Then he set a formal examination poem on the line “Ten thousand horses silently await commands,” prescribing the rhyme tu.

这明明是骂人，
This was plainly an insult.

哪知正当他高坐堂室，顾盼自喜时，有人突然拍案说道：“ ‘万马无声听号令’是上联，
While he sat high in the examination hall, looking about him in self-satisfaction, someone suddenly slapped his desk and declared, “‘Ten thousand horses silently await commands’ is the first line of a couplet.

下联叫做‘一牛独坐看文章’。”
The second is ‘One ox sits alone appraising compositions.’”

顿时哄堂大笑，纸条当然都裂断。
The whole hall roared with laughter, and naturally the paper strips all snapped.

那学政才知道自取其辱，只好隐忍不言。
Realising that he had brought the humiliation on himself, the commissioner could only swallow it in silence.


“老兄知道这个故事就好。
“It is just as well that you know the story.

今天请老兄一起去弹压，话是这么说，可不要把弹压二字，看得太认真了。”
I have asked you to come with me today to keep order, but do not take that expression too literally.”

这话便不易明白了，
This was harder to understand.

吴世荣哈着腰说：
Wu bowed slightly.

“请大人指点。”
“I should be grateful for Your Excellency’s guidance.”

“胡雪岩其人在杭州光复之初，对地方上有过大功德。
“When Hangzhou was first recovered, Hu Xueyan did a great deal for the city.

洪杨之役，杭州受灾最重，可是复原得最快，
During the rebellion of Hong and Yang, Hangzhou suffered the worst devastation, yet it recovered the fastest.

这都是胡雪岩之功。”
That was all Hu’s doing.”

“喔，
“Ah.

大人的意思是杭州人对胡雪岩是有感情的。”
You mean that the people of Hangzhou feel warmly towards him.”

“不错。
“Exactly.

妒嫉他的人，只是少数，
Those who envy him are a minority.

还有靠胡雪岩养家活口的人也很多。”
A great many people also depend on him for their living.”

既是靠胡雪岩养家活口，当然站在他这一边，
Anyone whose livelihood depended on Hu would naturally stand by him.

而更要紧的一种关系是，决不愿见胡雪岩的事业倒闭，
More importantly, such people would have no wish to see his businesses fail.

吴世荣恍然有悟，连边点头。
Understanding dawned on Wu, and he nodded repeatedly.

“照此看来，风潮应该不会大。”
“In that case, the disturbance ought not to be very serious.”

德馨认为吴世荣很开窍，便用嘉许的语气说：“世荣兄目光如炬，明察秋毫，
Dexin thought him quick to grasp the point and said approvingly, “Your eyes are like torches, Brother Shirong, discerning the finest autumn down.

兄弟不胜佩服之至。”
My admiration is boundless.”

话中的成语，用得不甚恰当，不过类此情形吴世荣经过不是第一次，
His choice of literary expressions was not particularly apt, but Wu had encountered this sort of thing before.

也听人说过，德馨虽有能员之称，书却读得不多，
He had also heard that, capable an official though Dexin was reputed to be, he had read little.

对属下好卖弄他腹中那“半瓶醋”的墨水，所以有时候不免酸气，
Before his subordinates, he liked to display his half-filled bottle of learning, which occasionally gave his speech a sour, pedantic flavour.

偶尔还加上些戏词，那就是更酸且腐的一股怪味了。
When he added a line or two from the theatre, the mixture became stranger and staler still.


这样转变念头，便觉得无足为奇了，
Remembering this, Wu ceased to find it surprising.

“大人谬奖了。”
“You are too generous, sir.”

他接着问道：“府里跟大人一起去弹压，虽以安抚为主，
He went on, “When I accompany you to keep order, reassurance will of course be our chief purpose.

但如真有不识轻重、意因鼓动风潮的，请大人明示，究以如何处置，方为恰当？”
But if some people fail to understand the gravity of the situation and deliberately stir up trouble, how should they be dealt with?”

“总以逆来顺受为主。”
“Above all, we must accept provocation with good grace.”

“逆”到如何犹可“顺受”，此中应该有个分寸，
There ought to be some limit to how much provocation could be borne.

“请大人明示！”他问：
“Please instruct me, sir.

“倘有人胆敢冲撞，如之奈何？”
What if someone dares to insult or defy us?”

“这冲撞么，”
“As for that…”

德馨沉吟了一会儿说：
Dexin considered for a moment.

“谅他们也不敢！”
“I do not suppose they would dare!”

吴世荣可以忍受他的语言不当，比拟不伦，
Wu could put up with inappropriate language and ill-chosen comparisons.

但对这种滑头话觉得非打破砂锅问到底不可。
Such an evasive answer, however, required him to get to the bottom of the matter.

“如果真有这样的情形呢？”
“But what if it does happen?”

吴世荣也降低了措词雅饰的层次：
He too abandoned some of the refinements of official speech.

“俗语说不怕一万，只怕万一，
“As the saying goes, it is not the ten thousand ordinary cases we fear, but the one exception.

不能不防。”
We must be prepared.”

“万一冲撞，自然是言语上头的事。
“Any offence would naturally be a matter of words.

你我何必跟小民一般见识？
Why should you and I lower ourselves to the level of the common people?

有道是忍得一时气，保得百年身；
Endure a moment’s anger and preserve yourself for a lifetime.

又道是不痴不聋，不作阿家翁。
And they say no man can be a father-in-law unless he knows how to play the fool and turn a deaf ear.

贵府是首府，
Yours is the prefecture of the provincial capital.

就好象我们浙江的一个当家人一样。”
You are, as it were, the head of Zhejiang’s household.”

能做到这样，需要有极大涵养，吴世荣自恐不易办到，
Such forbearance required immense self-control, and Wu doubted his ability to practise it.

但看德馨的意思，非常清楚，一切以平息风潮为主，
Still, Dexin’s intention was clear: everything must serve the restoration of calm.

至于手段，实在不必听他的，
As for the means, Wu need not really follow his advice.

能迁就则迁就，不能迁就，还是得动用权威，只要大事化小，又不失体统，便算圆满。
Yield where he could, use authority where he could not; if a major disturbance could be reduced to a minor one without loss of official dignity, that would be a satisfactory outcome.


他考虑了一下，觉得有一点不能不先说清楚，
After some thought, Wu decided there was one point he must settle in advance.

“回大人的话，为政之道，宽猛相济。
“Sir, government requires a balance of leniency and severity.

不过何人可宽，何人可猛；何时该宽，何时该猛？一点都乱不得。
But we must be quite clear about whom to treat leniently, whom severely, and when each is called for.

照府里来想，今天的局面，大人作主，该猛应猛，交代严办，
As I see today’s situation, Your Excellency should direct proceedings and order stern measures where they are necessary.

府里好比当家的家妇，少不得代下人求情，请从轻发落，
I shall be like the woman managing the household, pleading on behalf of the servants for lighter punishment.

这样一个红脸、一个白脸，这出戏才唱得下来。
One of us plays the stern red face, the other the kindly white face: that is how we can carry the performance through.

“他接着说：”倘或有那泼妇刁民，非临之以威不足以让他们就范，
But suppose there are shrews or unruly fellows whom only a show of force will bring to heel.

那时候府里派人锁拿，大人倒说要把他们放了，这样子府里就不知道该怎么办了。
If I send men to put them in chains and you then order their release, I shall not know how to proceed.”

““不会，不会！”德馨连连说道：
“That will not happen!” Dexin assured him repeatedly.

“我做红脸，你做白脸，
“I shall be the red face and you the white.

你如果做红脸，我决不做白脸，
If you take the red face, I certainly shall not take the white.

总而言之，你当主角我‘扫边’，
In short, you will play the leading role and I the supporting part.

我一定捧着你把这出戏唱下来。”
I shall back you throughout the performance.”

话很客气，但这一回去平息阜康风潮的主要责任，已轻轻套在他头上了。
Courteous though this was, he had lightly slipped the chief responsibility for settling the Fukang disturbance onto Wu’s shoulders.

吴世荣心想，德馨真是个装傻卖乘的老狐狸！
What an old fox, Wu thought, playing the fool to get his own way!


有此承诺吴世荣才比较放心，
With this assurance, Wu felt easier.

于是起身告辞，同时约好，他先回杭州府，摆齐“导子”先到清和坊阜康钱庄前面“伺候”，
He rose to take his leave, agreeing to return to his prefectural office, assemble his ceremonial escort and go first to “await” Dexin outside Fukang in Qinghefang.

德馨随后动身。
Dexin would follow.

两人拟好辰光，先后来到阜康，
They timed their departures and arrived one after the other.

人群恰如潮汐之有“子午潮”，日中甫过，
As a tide has its midnight and midday surges, so the crowd swelled just after noon.

上午来的未见分晓，坚持不去，得到信息的，在家吃罢午饭，纷纷赶到，
Those who had come in the morning still had no answer and refused to leave; others, hearing the news, finished lunch at home and hurried over.

杭州府与仁和、钱塘两县的差役，看看无从措手，都找相熟的店家吃茶歇脚，
The runners from the Hangzhou prefectural office and the counties of Renhe and Qiantang, seeing no way to manage the situation, had gone to familiar shops for tea and a rest.

及至听得鸣锣喝道之声，听说吴知府到了，随后德藩台也要来，自然不能躲懒，
But when gongs and shouted commands announced that Prefect Wu had arrived and Treasurer De was following, they could no longer shirk.

好在经过休息，精神养足，一个个挺胸凸肚，迎风乱挥皮鞭，一阵阵呼呼作响，即时在人潮中开出一条路来。
Refreshed by their rest, they thrust out their chests and bellies and swung their whips through the air with a succession of swishes, clearing a path through the crowd.

清和坊是一条大街，
Qinghefang was a broad street.

逼退人潮，阜康门前空出来一片空地，足容两乘大轿停放。
Once people had been driven back, there was room outside Fukang for two large sedan chairs.

谢云青是已经得到螺蛳太太的通知，官府会出面来料理，
Xie Yunqing had already been informed by Madam Luosi that the authorities would intervene.

所以尽管门外人声如沸，又叫又骂，让人心惊肉跳，他却如老僧入定般，闭目养神，
Terrifying though the shouting and cursing outside might be, he sat with his eyes closed like an old monk in meditation, conserving his strength.

“心里在一层深一层地盘算，官府出面时，会如何安排，阜康应该如何应付。
Beneath that stillness, he was considering, layer upon layer, what the officials might arrange and how Fukang should respond.

等盘算得差不多了，吴世荣也快到了。
By the time he had thought it through, Wu was nearly there.


这要先迎了出去，
Xie had to go out to meet him first.

如果知府上门，卸排门迎接，主顾一拥而入，就会搞得不可收拾，
If the prefect arrived at the door and the shutters were taken down to admit him, the customers would surge in and all control would be lost.

因此，他关照多派伙计，防守边门，然后悄悄溜了出去，
Xie therefore ordered extra employees to guard the side entrance, then slipped out quietly.

一顶毡帽压到眉际，同时装做怕冷，手捂着嘴跟鼻子，
He pulled a felt hat down to his eyebrows and covered his mouth and nose with his hand, as though against the cold.

幸喜没有人识破，
Fortunately, nobody recognised him.

到得导子近前，他拔脚便冲到轿前，轿子当然停住了。
On reaching the ceremonial escort, he suddenly ran forward to the sedan, which naturally came to a halt.

这叫“冲道”，
This was called breaking into an official’s procession.

差役照例先举鞭子护轿，然后另有人上前，看身分处理，
The runners would first raise their whips to protect the chair; someone else would then come forward to determine the intruder’s status and deal with him accordingly.

倘苦是老百姓，可以请准了当街拖翻打屁股。
An ordinary person might, with permission, be thrown down in the street and beaten on the buttocks.

谢云青衣冠楚楚，自然要客气些，
Xie’s respectable clothes earned him more courtesy.

喝问一声：“你是干什么的？”
“What are you doing?” a runner demanded.

谢云青在轿前屈膝打千，
Xie bent one knee in a formal salute before the sedan.

口中说道：“阜康钱庄档手谢云青，向大人请安。”
“Xie Yunqing, manager of Fukang Bank, pays his respects to Your Honour.”

“喔，”
“Oh.

吴世荣在轿中吩咐，“停轿。”
Halt the chair,” Wu ordered from inside.

“停轿”不是将轿子放下地，
This did not mean setting it on the ground.

轿杠仍在轿夫肩上，不过有根带桠杈的枣木棍，撑住了轿杠，其名叫做“打杆子”。
The poles remained on the bearers’ shoulders, but were supported by forked sticks of jujube wood, a practice known as propping the poles.

这时轿帘自然亦已揭起来了，
The curtain was lifted.

吴世荣问道：“你就是谢云青？”
“You are Xie Yunqing?”

“是。”
“Yes, sir.”

“你们东家什么时候回来？”
“When will your employer return?”

“今天晚上，一定可到。”
“He is certain to arrive this evening.”

吴世荣点点头说：
Wu nodded.

“藩台马上也要来，
“The Treasurer will be here shortly.

我跟他在你店家坐一坐，好商量办法。“
He and I shall sit in your bank and discuss what can be done.”


接着，德馨亦已驾到，
Dexin arrived soon afterwards.

仍旧是由谢云青引领着，由边门进入阜康钱庄的客座。
Xie again led the way, taking them through the side entrance into Fukang’s reception room.

这里的陈设非常讲究，广东酸枝木嵌螺甸的家具，四壁是名人书画，上款差不多都是“雪岩观察大人雅属”，
It was furnished with great elegance: Guangdong rosewood inlaid with mother-of-pearl, and distinguished paintings and calligraphy on all four walls, most dedicated “To His Honour Circuit Intendant Xueyan, at his gracious request.”

最触目的是正中高悬一幅淡彩贡宣的中堂，
The most striking piece was a central scroll, hung high, on delicately tinted tribute-quality xuan paper.

行书一首唐诗，字有碗口那么大，下款是“恭亲王书”，
It bore a Tang poem in running script, each character as large as the mouth of a bowl, signed “Written by Prince Gong.”

下铃一方朱文大印，印文“皇六子”三字，
Below the signature was a large seal impression in red lettering, reading “The Emperor’s Sixth Son.”

左右陪衬的一副对联是左宗棠的亲笔。
A couplet in Zuo Zongtang’s own hand hung on either side.

客座很大，也很高，正中开着玻璃天窗，
The room was spacious and high, with a glass skylight in the centre.

时方过午，阳光直射，照出中间一张极大的大理石面的八仙桌，
Just after noon, sunlight fell directly onto a huge square marble-topped table.

桌上摆了八个大号的高脚盘，尽是精巧的茶食，但只有两碗细瓷银托的盖碗茶，自然是为德馨与吴世荣预备的。
Eight large raised dishes held delicate tea snacks; there were only two covered teacups of fine porcelain on silver stands, plainly prepared for Dexin and Wu.

“赶紧收掉！”德馨一进来便指着桌上说：
“Take those away at once!” Dexin said on entering, pointing at the table.

“让人见了不好。”“德大人说得是，”吴世荣深以为然，向谢云青说道：
“It will look bad if people see them.” Wu thoroughly agreed. “His Excellency is right,” he told Xie.

“德大人跟我今天不是来作客的。”
“We have not come as guests today.”

“是，是。”
“Yes, of course.”

谢云青指挥伙计，收去了高脚盘，请贵客落座，他自己站在两人之间，等候问话。
Xie had the raised dishes removed, invited the visitors to sit and remained standing between them, awaiting their questions.


“不开门，总不是一回事。”德馨问吴世荣：
“Keeping the doors closed will never do,” Dexin said to Wu.

“我看应该照常营业。”
“I think business ought to continue as usual.”

此言一出，晨世荣无以为答，谢云青更是一脸的苦恼。
Wu had no answer, and Xie looked thoroughly distressed.

能够“照常营业”，为何不下排门？
If they could conduct business as usual, why had they not taken down the shutters?

这话是真正的废话。
The remark was utterly pointless.

德馨也发觉自己的话不通，便又补了一句：“不过，应该有个限制。”
Dexin realised this himself and added, “There should, however, be a limit.”

这才象话，
That made more sense.

吴世荣接口说道：
Wu took it up.

“我看怎么限制，
“We must consider what limit to impose.

阜康总不至于库空如洗吧？”
Surely Fukang’s vault is not entirely empty?”

“不错，
“Exactly.

限制要看阜康的库存而定。”德馨问道：“你们库里有多少现银？”
The limit must depend on the reserve.” Dexin asked Xie, “How much silver have you in the vault?”

库存有四十余万，但谢云青不敢说实话，
There were over four hundred thousand taels, but Xie dared not tell the truth.

打一个对折答道：
He halved the amount.

“二十万出头。”
“A little over two hundred thousand.”

“有二十万现银，很可以挡一阵子。”
“Two hundred thousand in silver should hold things off for a while.

德馨又问：“胡观察的事业很多，
Circuit Intendant Hu has many businesses.

他处总还可以接济吧？”
Surely the others can help?”

“回大人的话，我们东家的事业虽多，我只管钱庄，
“Sir, our employer does have many businesses, but I manage only the bank.

别处的情形不大清楚。”
I am not very familiar with the others’ affairs.”

“别处银钱的收解，当然是跟阜康往来，
“Their receipts and payments must pass through Fukang.

你怎么会不清楚？”吴世荣说，语气微有斥责的意味。
How can you be unfamiliar with them?” Wu asked with a note of reproof.

“回大人的话，”谢云青急忙解释，“我之不清楚是，不清楚别处有多少现银，
Xie hurried to explain. “What I do not know, sir, is how much ready silver they have.

不过就有也有限的，
In any case, it can only be a limited amount.

象间壁公济典，存银至多万把两，
Gongji Pawnshop next door, for example, holds at most ten thousand taels or so.

有大笔用途，都是临时到阜康来支。”
Whenever it needs a large sum, it draws it from Fukang at the time.”


“那么，”德馨问道：“你们开出去多少票子，总有帐吧？”
“Then,” Dexin asked, “you must have records of the notes you have issued?”

“当然，当然！
“Certainly, certainly!

哪里好没有帐！”
How could we not?”

“好！
“Good.

我问你，你们开出去的票子，一万两以下的有多少？”
What is the total value of your notes for ten thousand taels or less?”

“这要看帐。”
“I must consult the accounts.”

谢云青告个罪，在旁边的椅子上坐下，叫伙计取帐薄来，
With an apology, Xie sat in a nearby chair and called for the ledgers.

一把算盘打得飞快，
His fingers flew over an abacus.

算好了来回报，“一共三十三万挂零。”
When he had finished, he reported, “A little over three hundred and thirty thousand taels in all.”

“并不多嘛！”
“That is not very much!”

“大人，”谢云青说：“本号开出去的票子虽不多，可是别处地方就不知道了。
“Sir, the notes we have issued here are not so very many, but I cannot say what there may be elsewhere.

譬如上海阜康开出去的票子，我们一样也有照兑的，”
For instance, we also honour notes issued by Shanghai Fukang.”

“啊，啊！”
“Ah!”
