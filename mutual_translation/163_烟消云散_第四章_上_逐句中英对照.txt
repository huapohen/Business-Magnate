Vanishing into Thin Air — Chapter Four, Part One

第四章 夜访藩司｜CHAPTER FOUR: A NIGHT VISIT TO THE PROVINCIAL TREASURER


胡雪岩船到望仙桥，恰正是周少棠舌战黄八麻子，在大开玩笑的时候，
When Hu Xueyan’s boat reached Wangxian Bridge, Zhou Shaotang was in the midst of his verbal battle with Huang Bamazi, making jokes at his opponent’s expense.

螺蛳太太午前便派了亲信，沿运河往北迎了上去，在一处关卡上静候胡雪岩船到，遇船报告消息。
Before noon, Madam Luosi had sent a trusted man north along the canal to wait at a checkpoint and report the news as soon as Hu’s boat arrived.

这个亲信便是乌先生。
That man was Mr Wu.

他在胡家的身分很特殊，即非“师爷”，更非“管事”，但受胡雪岸或螺蛳太太的委托，常有临时的差使。
His position in the Hu household was unusual: he was neither a secretary nor a steward, but Hu or Madam Luosi often entrusted him with particular errands.

这个人当螺蛳太太与胡雪岩之间的“密使”，自然是最适当的人选。
No one could have been better suited to serve as their confidential messenger.

“大先生，”，他说：“起暴风了。”
“Sir,” he said, “a violent storm has broken.”

不说起风波，却说“起暴风”，
He had said a violent storm, not merely a disturbance.

胡雪岩的心一沉，但表面不露声色，
Hu’s heart sank, but his face betrayed nothing.

只说：“你特为赶了来，当然出事了。
“Since you have come all this way, something must have happened.

什么事？
Tell me what it is.

慢慢说。”
Take your time.”

“你在路上，莫非没有听到上海的消息？”
“Have you heard nothing of the news from Shanghai on your journey?”

等乌先生将由谢云青转到螺蛳太太手里的电报，拿了出来，
Mr Wu produced the telegram that Xie Yunqing had passed to Madam Luosi.

胡雪岩一看色变，不过他矫情镇物的功夫过人，
Hu changed colour as he read it, but his ability to command his expression and steady others was exceptional.

立即恢复常态，只问：“杭州城里都晓得了？”
He recovered at once and asked only, “Does everyone in Hangzhou know?”

“当然。”
“Of course.”

“这样说，杭州，亦会挤兑？”
“Then there will be a run here too?”

“罗四姐特为要我来，就是谈这件事……”
“That is exactly why Sister Luo sent me…”

乌先生遂将谢云青深夜报信，决定卑康暂停营业，以及螺蛳太太亲访德馨求援，德馨已答应设法维持的经过，细说了一遍。
Mr Wu gave a full account of Xie’s visit late at night, the decision to suspend business at Fukang, Madam Luosi’s personal appeal to Dexin, and the treasurer’s promise to do what he could to keep matters under control.


胡雪岩静静听完，
Hu listened quietly to the end.

第一句话便问：“老太太晓得不晓得？”
His first question was, “Does the old lady know?”

“当然是瞒牢的。”
“Naturally, it has been kept from her.”

“好！”
“Good!”

胡雪岩放心了，
Hu felt relieved.

“事情已经出来了，
“What has happened has happened.

着急也没有用。
Worrying is no use.

顶要紧的是，自己不要乱。
Above all, we must not lose our heads.

乌先生，喜事照常办，不过，我恐怕没有工夫来多管，
Mr Wu, the wedding is to go ahead as usual, but I am afraid I shall have little time to attend to it.

请你多帮一帮罗四姐。”
Please give Sister Luo all the help you can.”

“我晓得。”
“I understand.”

乌先生突然想起：
Mr Wu suddenly remembered something.

“罗四姐说，大先生最好不要在望仙桥上岸。”
“Sister Luo says it would be better if you did not go ashore at Wangxian Bridge.”

胡雪岩上船下船，一向在介乎元宝街与清河坊之间的望仙桥，
Hu invariably boarded or left his boat there, between Yuanbao Street and Qinghefang.

螺蛳太太怕惹人注目，所以有此劝告。但胡雪岩的想法不同。
Madam Luosi had made the suggestion because she feared attracting attention, but he thought differently.

“既然一切照常，我当然还是在望仙桥上岸。”胡雪岩又问：
“If everything is to proceed as usual, I must go ashore at Wangxian Bridge as usual.

“罗四姐原来要我在啥地方上岸？”
Where did she intend me to land?”

“万安桥。
“At Wan’an Bridge.

轿子等在那里。”乌先生答说：
Your sedan chair is waiting there,” Mr Wu replied.

“这样子，我在万安桥上岸，关照轿子仍旧到望仙桥去接。”
“In that case, I shall get off there and tell the bearers to go on to Wangxian Bridge to meet you.”


胡雪岩的一乘绿呢大轿，华丽是出了名的，
Hu’s large green-cloth sedan chair was famous for its splendour.

抬到望仙桥，虽然已经暮色四合，但一停下来，自有人注目。
Though dusk had fallen by the time it reached Wangxian Bridge, its arrival inevitably drew attention.

加以乌先生了解胡雪岩的用意，关照来接轿的家人，照旧摆出排场，
Understanding Hu’s purpose, Mr Wu told the household men to make their usual display.

身穿簇新棉“号褂子”的护勇，码头上一站，点起官衔灯笼，
Guards in brand-new padded livery took their places on the landing and lit the lanterns bearing his official titles.

顿时吸引了一大批看热闹的行人。
A large crowd of passers-by soon gathered to watch.

见此光景，胡雪岩改了主意。
At this sight, Hu changed his mind.

往时一回杭州，都是先回家看娘，
Whenever he returned to Hangzhou, he normally went home first to see his mother.

这一次怕老娘万一得知沪杭两处钱庄挤兑，急出病来，更加不放心。
Now he was all the more anxious to do so, fearing that news of the runs on the Shanghai and Hangzhou banks might have reached her and made her ill.

但看到这么多人在注视他的行踪，心里不免设身处地想一想，
But with so many people watching his movements, he could not help putting himself in their place.

如果自己是阜康的客户，又会作何想法？
What would he think if he were a Fukang customer?

只要一抛开自己，胡雪岩第一个念头便是：不能先回家！
The moment he set himself aside, his first thought was that he must not go home first.

多少人的血汗钱托付给卓康，
So many people had entrusted their hard-earned money to Fukang.

如今有不保之势，而阜康的老板居然好整以暇地光顾自己家里，不顾别人死活，
Now it was in danger, yet the owner was calmly attending to his own household, regardless of what happened to anyone else.

这口气是咽不下的。
Who could swallow that?

因此船一靠岸，他先就询问：“云青来了没有？”
As soon as the boat touched the bank, he asked, “Has Yunqing come?”

谢云青何能不来？
How could Xie have stayed away?

不过他是故意躲在暗处，此时闪出来疾趋上前，口中叫一声：
He had deliberately kept in the shadows, but now stepped out and hurried forward.

“大先生！”
“Sir!”

“好，好！
“Good, good!

云青，你来了！
You are here, Yunqing.

不要紧，不要紧，
Nothing to worry about!

阜康仍旧是金字招牌。”
Fukang still has its golden reputation.”

他特意提高了声音说，“我先到店里。”
Raising his voice deliberately, Hu added, “I shall go to the bank first.”


店里便是阜康。轿子一到，正好店里开饭，
When his sedan chair reached Fukang, the staff were just sitting down to their meal.

胡雪岩特为去看一看饭桌，
Hu made a point of inspecting the dinner table.

这种情形平时亦曾有过，但在这种时候，他竟有这种闲情逸致，就不能不令人惊异了。
He had done so before, but that he should have leisure for such things at a moment like this was astonishing.

“天气冷了！”胡雪岩问谢云青说：
“The weather has turned cold,” he said to Xie.

“该用火锅了。”
“You ought to be having hotpot.”

“年常旧规，要冬至才用火锅。”谢云青说：
“Our standing rule is to start at the winter solstice.

“今年冬至迟。”
It falls late this year.”

“以后规矩改一改。
“Change the rule from now on.

照外国人的办法，冬天到寒暑表多少度，吃火锅，夏天，则多少度吃西瓜。
Follow the foreigners’ practice: in winter, serve hotpot when the thermometer falls to a certain temperature; in summer, serve watermelon when it rises to a certain temperature.

云青，你记牢。”
Remember that, Yunqing.”

这是稳定“军心”的办法，
This was his way of steadying his troops.

表示阜康倒不下来，还会一年一年开下去。
It told them that Fukang would not fall, but would carry on year after year.

谢云青当然懂得这个奥妙，
Xie understood perfectly.

一叠连声地答应着，交代“饭司务”从第二天起多领一份预备火锅的菜钱。
Agreeing at once, he told the cook to draw an extra allowance for hotpot ingredients from the following day.

“阜康的饭碗敲不破的！”有人这样在说。
“No one is going to smash our rice bowls at Fukang!” someone said.


在听谢云青的细说经过时，胡雪岩一阵阵胃冷中，
As Xie described what had happened, chills ran through Hu.

越觉得侥幸，越感到惭愧。
He felt increasingly fortunate, and increasingly ashamed.

事业不是他一人能创得起来的，所以出现今天这种局面，当然也不是他一个人的过失，
He had not built the business single-handed, and its present predicament could not be his fault alone.

但胡雪岩虽一想起宓本常，就恨不得一口唾沫当面吐在他脸上，
Whenever he thought of Mi Benchang, he wanted to spit in the man’s face.

但是，这种念头一起即消，
Yet the thought vanished almost as soon as it arose.

他告诉自己，不必怨任何人，连自己都不必怨，
He told himself not to blame anyone, not even himself.

最好忘记掉自己是阜康的东家，当自己是胡雪岩的“总管”，颇雪岩已经“不能问事”，委托他全权来处理这一场灾难。
Best of all would be to forget that he owned Fukang and imagine himself as Hu Xueyan’s chief steward, entrusted with full authority to handle this disaster because Hu himself could no longer attend to his affairs.

他只有尽力将得失之心丢开，心思才能比较集中，
Only by setting his personal hopes and fears aside could he concentrate.

当时紧皱双眉，闭上眼睛，通前彻后细想了以后说：
He closed his eyes, his brows tightly knitted, and thought the matter through from beginning to end.

“面子就是招牌，
Then he said, “Our standing is our reputation.

面子保得住，招牌就可以不倒，
Preserve that standing, and the name need not fall.

这是一句总诀。
That is the key to it all.

云青，你记牢！”
Remember it, Yunqing.”

“是，
“Yes.

我懂。”
I understand.”


“你跟螺蛳太太商量定规，今天早晨不开门，
“You and Madam Luosi decided not to open this morning.

这一点对不对，我们不必再谈。
There is no need to discuss now whether that was right or wrong.

不过，你要晓得，拆烂污的事情做不得。”
But you must understand: we cannot simply shirk our responsibilities and leave a mess behind.”

“我不是想拆烂污……”“我晓得。”
“I did not mean to—” “I know.”

胡雪岩摇摇手阻止他说：
Hu raised a hand to stop him.

“你不必分辩，
“You need not defend yourself.

因为我不是说你。
I am not accusing you.

不过，你同螺蛳太太有个想法大错特错，
But you and Madam Luosi have one idea that is utterly mistaken.

你刚才同我说，万一撑不住，手里还有几十万款子，做将来翻身的本钱。
You told me just now that, if we could not hold out, we would at least have several hundred thousand taels left as capital for a fresh start.

不对，
Wrong!

抱了这种想法，就输定了，
Think that way, and we are certain to lose.

永远翻不得身。
We shall never recover.

云青，你要晓得，我好象推牌九，
Yunqing, imagine me keeping the bank at pai gow.

一直推得是‘长庄’，注码不管多少都要，
I have always played with an unlimited bank: whatever the stake, I take it.

你输得起，我赢得进，
If you can afford to lose it, I can accept the winnings.

现在手风不顺，忽然说是改推‘铲庄’，尽多少铜钱赌，自己留起多少，当下次的赌本，
Now my luck turns against me, and I suddenly announce that I am limiting the bank: I shall risk only so much and keep the rest for another game.

云青，没有下次了，
Yunqing, there will be no other game!

赌场里从此进不去了！
I shall never be allowed into the gambling house again.”

“谢云青吸了口冷气，然后紧闭着嘴，
Xie drew a sharp breath, then pressed his lips together.

无从赞一词。
He could not add a word.


“我是一双空手起来的，
“I began with empty hands.

到头来仍旧一双空手，
If I end with empty hands, what have I lost?

不输啥！
Nothing!

不但不输，吃过、用过、阔过，都是赚头。
Everything I have eaten, enjoyed and spent in grand style has been a gain.

只要我不死，你看我照样一双空手再翻起来。”
So long as I live, you will see me rise again with nothing.”

“大先生这样气概，从古到今也没有几个人有。
“Few men in any age have had your spirit, sir.

不过，”
But…”

谢云青迟疑了一下，终于说了出来：
Xie hesitated, then finally said it.

“做生意到底不是推牌九。”
“Business is not quite the same as keeping the bank at pai gow.”

“做生意虽不是推牌九，道理是一样的，‘赌奸赌诈不赌赖’。
“It may not be, but the principle is the same: you may gamble with cunning and deception, but never welsh on a bet.

不卸排门做生意，不讲信用就是赖！”
Keeping the shutters up and refusing to honour your word is welshing!”

“大先生这么说，明天照常。”
“Then, as you say, we open as usual tomorrow.”

“当然照常！”
“Of course!

胡雪岩说：“你今天要做一件事，
There is one thing you must do tonight.

拿存户的帐，好好看一看，
Go carefully through the depositors’ accounts.

有几个户头要连夜去打招呼。”
There are several customers we must approach before the night is out.”

“好。
“Very well.

我马上动手。”
I shall begin at once.”

“对。
“Good.

不过招呼有个打法，
But there is a way of approaching them.

第一，一向初五结息，
First, interest is normally settled on the fifth.

现在提早先把利息结出来，送银票上门。”
Settle it early this time and send notes for the amount to their homes.”

“是。”
“Yes.”

“第二，你要告诉人家年关到了，
“Second, tell them the year’s end is approaching.

或者要提款，
Perhaps they will need to withdraw money.

要多少，请人家交代下来好预备。”
Ask them to let us know how much, so that we can have it ready.”

“嗯、嗯、嗯。”谢云青心领神会地答应着。
Xie nodded repeatedly, understanding exactly what Hu meant.


能将大户稳定下来，零星散户，力能应付，无足为忧。
If the large depositors could be kept steady, the scattered small withdrawals were within their means and gave no cause for fear.

胡雪岩交代清楚了，方始转回元宝街，
Only after giving these instructions did Hu return to Yuanbao Street.

虽已入夜，一条街上依旧停满了轿马，
Night had fallen, but the street was still packed with sedan chairs and horses.

门灯高悬，家人排班，雁行而立，
Lanterns hung high at the doors, and household attendants stood in orderly ranks.

仿佛一切如常，但平时那种喧哗热闹的气氛，却突然消失了。
Everything seemed as usual, except that the customary bustle and cheerful noise had suddenly disappeared.

轿子直接抬到花园门口，
His chair was carried straight to the garden entrance.

下轿一看，胡太太与螺蛳太太在那里迎接，
When he alighted, the First Madam and Madam Luosi were waiting for him.

相见黯然，但只转瞬之间，螺蛳太太便浮起了笑容，
Their meeting was sombre, but within a moment Madam Luosi produced a smile.

“想来还没有吃饭？”
“I suppose you have not eaten?

她问：“饭开在哪里？”
Where shall dinner be served?”

这是没话找话，
She was merely finding something to say.

胡雪岩根本没有听进去，
Hu scarcely heard her.

只说：“到你楼上谈。”他又问：
“Let us talk upstairs in your rooms.

“老太太晓得不晓得，我回来了。”
Does the old lady know I am back?”

“还没有禀告她老人家。”
“She has not been told yet.”

“好！
“Good.

关照中门上，先不要说。”
Tell the people at the inner gate not to say anything for the moment.”

“我晓得。
“I know.

不会的。”
They will not.”

胡家的中门，仿佛大内的乾清门一般，禁制特严，真个外言不入，
The household’s inner gate was guarded by restrictions as strict as those at the Gate of Heavenly Purity in the imperial palace: no word from outside was allowed through.

螺蛳太太早已关照过了，大可放心。
Madam Luosi had already given her orders, and he could be easy on that score.

到得螺蛳太太那里，阿云捧来一碗燕窝汤，一笼现蒸的鸡蛋糕，另外是现沏的龙井茶，
Upstairs, A’yun brought a bowl of bird’s-nest soup, a steamer of freshly made egg cakes and newly brewed Longjing tea.

预备齐全，随即下楼，这是螺蛳太太早就关照好了的。阿云武守在楼梯口，不准任何人上楼。
Once everything was ready, she went downstairs, as Madam Luosi had instructed beforehand, and stood guard at the foot of the stairs to keep everyone else away.


“事情要紧不要紧？”胡太太首先开口。
“Is it serious?” the First Madam asked first.

“说要紧就要紧，说不要紧就不要紧。”胡雪岩说：
“You could call it serious, or you could call it nothing,” Hu replied.

“如今是顶石臼做戏，
“We are performing a play with a stone mortar balanced on our heads.

能把戏做完，大不了落个吃力不讨好，
If we can finish it, the worst that happens is that we work hard and get no thanks.

没有啥要紧，
That is nothing.

这出做不下去，石臼砸下来，非死即伤。”
But if we cannot carry the performance through, the mortar falls, and we are killed or injured.”

“那么这出戏要怎样做呢？”螺蛳太太问说。
“Then how are we to perform it?” Madam Luosi asked.

“要做得台底下看不出我们头上顶了一个石臼，
“So that the audience never sees the mortar on our heads.

那就不要紧了。”
Then we shall be all right.”

“我也是这样关照大家，
“That is what I told everyone too.

一切照常，喜事该怎么办，还是该怎么办。
Everything as usual: the wedding is to be conducted exactly as it should be.

不过，场面可以拿铜钱摆出来的，
But money can provide the grand display.

只怕笑脸摆不出来。”
It may not provide the smiling faces.”

“难就难在这里。
“That is precisely the difficulty.

不过，”胡雪岩加重了语气说：“再难也要做到，
Still”—Hu spoke with added force—“however difficult it is, it must be done.

场面无论如何要好好儿把它绷起来，不管你们用啥法子。
Whatever means you use, you must keep up the display.”

胡太太与螺蛳太太相互看了一眼，
The two women looked at each other.

都将这两句话好好地想了一下，各有会心，不断点头。
Both considered his words carefully, understood them in their own ways and nodded.

“外头的事情有我。”
“Leave the outside affairs to me.

胡雪岩问说：“德晓峰怎么样？”
How has De Xiaofeng behaved?”

“总算不错。”螺蛳太太说：
“Well enough,” Madam Luosi said.

“莲珠一下午都在我这里，
“Lianzhu spent the whole afternoon here.

她说：你最好今天晚上就去看看德藩台。”
She says you would do best to see Treasurer De tonight.”

“晚上，恐怕不方便。”
“Would it not be inconvenient at night?”

“晚上才好细谈。”
“Night is the best time for a proper conversation.”

“好，
“Very well.

我等一下就去。”
I shall go shortly.”


胡雪岩有些踌躇，
Hu hesitated.

因为这时候最要紧的事，并不是去看德馨，
Calling on Dexin was not the most urgent business before him.

第一件是要发电报到各处，第二件是要召集几个重要的助手，商量应变之计。
First he had to send telegrams to various places; then he must bring his principal assistants together to discuss how to meet the emergency.

这两件事非但耽误不得，而且颇费功夫，
Neither task could wait, and both would take time.

实在抽不出空去看德馨。
He really could not spare any to visit Dexin.

“有应春在这里就好了。”
“If only Yingchun were here!”

胡雪岩叹口气，颓然倒在一张安乐椅，头软软地垂了下来。
With a sigh, he sank dejectedly into an armchair, his head drooping.

螺蛳太太大吃一惊，
Madam Luosi was alarmed.

“老爷！
“My dear!

老爷！”
My dear!”

她走上前去，半跪着摇撼着他双肩说：
She hurried over and half knelt before him, shaking his shoulders.

“你要撑起来！
“You must hold yourself together.

不管怎么样要撑牢！”
