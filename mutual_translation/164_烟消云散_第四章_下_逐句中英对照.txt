胡雪岩
《烟消云散》第四章（下，至本章结束）
Vanishing into Thin Air — Chapter Four, Part Two (conclusion)
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


正在谈着，闪出一个梳长辫子的丫头，带着老妈子来摆桌子，预备吃消夜。
While they were talking, a maid with a long braid slipped in, followed by older women who set the table for a late supper.

胡雪岩本想告辞，转念又想，应该不改常度：
Hu Xueyan had been about to take his leave, but on reflection he decided that he ought to keep to his usual ways.

有几次夜间来访，到了时候总是吃消夜，这天也应该照常才是。
On several previous evening visits he had stayed for supper when the time came; he ought to do the same tonight.

“姨太太呢？”德馨问说，
“Where is your mistress?” Dexin asked.

“说我请她。”
“Tell her I should like her to join us.”

“马上出来。”
“She will be out directly.”

原来莲珠是不避胡雪岩的，
Lianzhu did not observe the convention of avoiding Hu Xueyan.

这天原要出来周旋，一则慰问，再则道谢。
She had already intended to come out and entertain him that evening, both to express her concern and to thank him for his gifts.


及至胡雪岩刚刚落座，听得帘钩微响，
Hu Xueyan had scarcely taken his seat when he heard the faint clink of a curtain hook.

扭头看时，莲珠出现在房门口，
Turning, he saw Lianzhu in the doorway.

她穿的是件旗袍，不过自己改良过了，袖子并不太宽，腰身亦比较小，
She was wearing a Manchu gown, though she had altered the design herself: the sleeves were less wide and the waist more closely fitted.

由于她身材颀长，而且生长北方，穿惯了旗装，
She was tall and slender, had grown up in the north, and was accustomed to Manchu dress.

所以在她手握一方绣花手帕，一摇三摆地走了来，一点都看不出她是汉人。
As she approached with an embroidered handkerchief in her hand and a graceful swaying walk, there was nothing to suggest that she was Han Chinese.

“二太太！”
“Second Madam!”

胡雪岩赶紧站起来招呼。
Hu rose at once to greet her.

“请坐，请坐！”
“Please sit down, please!”

莲珠摆一摆手说：
She waved him back to his seat.

“胡大先生，多谢你送的东西，
“Mr Hu, thank you for all those things you sent me.

太破费了。”
You have been far too extravagant.”

“小意思，小意思。”胡雪岩说：
“A mere trifle, a mere trifle,” Hu replied.

“初五那天，二太太你要早点来。”
“You must come early on the fifth, Second Madam.”

“胡大先生，你不用关照，
“There is no need to remind me, Mr Hu.

我扰府上的喜酒，不止一顿，
I shall be enjoying more than one wedding feast at your house.

四姐请我去陪客，
Fourth Sister has asked me to help entertain the guests.

一前一后，起码扰你三顿。”
With the gatherings before and after the wedding, I shall be eating at your expense at least three times.”

原来杭州是南宋故都，婚丧喜庆，有许多繁文褥节，
Hangzhou, the former capital of the Southern Song, retained many elaborate observances for weddings, funerals, and other family occasions.

富家大族办喜事，请亲友执事，前期宴请，名为“请将”，事后款待，称为“谢将”。
When a wealthy family held a wedding and enlisted relatives and friends to help, the preliminary banquet was called “inviting the generals,” and the feast afterwards, “thanking the generals.”

莲珠是螺蛳太太特为邀来陪官眷的“支宾”。
Madam Luosi had specially invited Lianzhu to act as a hostess to the wives of officials.


“雪岩！”德馨问道：“喜事一切照常？”
“Xueyan,” Dexin asked, “is everything for the wedding going ahead as usual?”

胡雪岩尚未答话，莲珠先开口了，
Before Hu could answer, Lianzhu spoke.

“自然照常。”
“Of course it is.

她说：“这还用得着问？”
Do you even need to ask?”

“你看！”
“There, you see!”

德馨为姨太太所抢白，脸上有点挂不住，指着莲珠，自嘲地向胡雪岩说：“管得越严了，
A little embarrassed at being put down by his concubine, Dexin pointed at her and said to Hu with a self-mocking smile, “She keeps me under tighter control every day.

连多说句话都不得。”
I am not even allowed an extra word.”

“只怕没有人管。”胡雪岩答说：
“The thing to fear is having no one to look after you,” Hu replied.

“有人管是好事。”
“Having someone who does is a blessing.”

“我就是爱管闲事，也不光是管你。”莲珠紧接着又说：
“I do like to meddle, and you are not the only person I meddle with,” Lianzhu went on.

“胡大先生的事，我们怎么好不管，
“How could we stand aside when Mr Hu needs us?

有件事我要提醒你，到了好日子那天，要约了刘抚台去道喜！”
There is something I must remind you of: on the wedding day, you must arrange to go with Governor Liu and offer your congratulations.”

这正是胡雪岩想说不便说，关切在心里的一句话，
This was precisely what Hu had been longing to ask but had felt unable to put into words.

所以格外注意德馨的反应，
He watched Dexin's reaction with particular attention.

只听他答了一句：“当然非拉他去不可。”顿觉胸怀一宽。
When Dexin answered, “Of course I must drag him along,” a weight lifted from his heart.


“胡大先生，我特为穿旗袍给你看，
“Mr Hu, I put on this Manchu gown especially to show you.

你送我的哔叽衣料，我照这样子做了来穿，
I want to have the serge you sent me made up in this style.

你说好不好看？”
Do you think it would look well?”

通家之好，到了这样的程度，似乎稍嫌过分，
Even between families on such intimate terms, this seemed a little too familiar.

胡雪岩只好这样答说：“你说好就好。”
Hu could only answer, “If you think it looks well, then it does.”

“好是好，太素了一点儿。
“It would, only it would be a little too plain.

胡大先生，我还要托你，
There is something else I must ask you, Mr Hu.

有没有西洋花边，
Can one get Western lace?

下次得便请你从上海给我带一点来。”
Next time you have an opportunity, would you bring me some from Shanghai?”

“有！有！”胡雪岩一叠连声地答说：
“Certainly, certainly!” Hu answered eagerly.

“不必下一次。
“There is no need to wait until next time.

明天我就叫人送了来。”
I will have some sent over tomorrow.

他接着又说：“西洋花边宽细都有，花式很多，
Western lace comes in broad and narrow widths, and in a great many patterns.

我多送点来，请二太太自己挑。”
I shall send a selection for you to choose from.”

“那就更好了。”
“That would be even better.”

“别老站着。”德馨亲自移开一张凳子，
“Do not keep standing there,” Dexin said, moving a stool himself.

“你也陪我们吃一点儿。”
“Sit down and have something with us.”

于是莲珠坐了下来，为主客二人酌酒布菜，静静地听他们谈话。
Lianzhu sat down, poured wine and served food for host and guest, and quietly listened to their conversation.


“雪岩，我听说你用的人，也不完全靠得住。
“Xueyan, I hear that not all the people you employ can be relied upon.

你自己总知道吧？”
You must know that yourself?”

“过了这个风潮，我要好好整顿了。”胡雪岩答说：
“Once this crisis is over, I shall put things thoroughly in order,” Hu replied.

“晓翁说周少棠值得重用，
“You said Zhou Shaotang deserved to be given greater responsibilities, Mr Xiaofeng.

我一定要重用。”
I shall certainly see that he gets them.”

“你看了人再用。”莲珠忍不住插嘴，
Lianzhu could not resist intervening. “Judge the man for yourself before you employ him.

“不要光看人家的面子，
Do not do it merely out of regard for someone else.

人用得不好，受害的是自己。”
If you choose badly, you are the one who suffers.”

“是，是！
“Yes, yes!

二太太是金玉良言。”
Those are words of gold, Second Madam.”

胡雪岩深为感慨，
Hu spoke with deep feeling.

“这回的风潮，也是我不听一两个好友的话之故。”
“This crisis, too, has come about because I failed to listen to one or two good friends.”

“其实你不必听外头人的话，
“In fact, you need not listen to outsiders.

多听听罗四姐的话就好了。”
You would do well enough if you listened more to Luo Sijie.”

“她对外面的情形不大明白。
“She does not understand very much about affairs outside the household.

这一点，比二太太你差多了。”
In that respect she is a long way behind you, Second Madam.”

听得这话，莲珠颇有知己之感，
Lianzhu felt that here, at least, was someone who appreciated her.

“胡大先生，你是明白的。
“Mr Hu, you understand.

不比我们老爷，
Our master is quite different.

提到外面的事，总说：“你别管‘。
Whenever I mention outside affairs, he says, ‘Keep out of it.’

一个人再聪明，也有当局者迷的时候。
However clever a person may be, there are times when being in the middle of things prevents him from seeing clearly.

刚才你同我们老爷在交谈的情形，我也听到了这一点儿。”
I caught something of what you and our master were discussing just now.”

说到这里，她突然问道：“胡大先生，上海跟杭州两处的风潮，左大人知道不知道？”
At this point she suddenly asked, “Does Lord Zuo know about the trouble in Shanghai and Hangzhou, Mr Hu?”


“恐怕还不晓得。”
“I am afraid he does not yet.”

“你怎么不告诉他？”
“Why have you not told him?”

“告诉他？”
“Tell him?”

胡雪岩有些茫然，
Hu sounded rather bewildered.

多少年来，凡是失面子的事，他从不告诉左宗棠，
For many years he had never reported anything to Zuo Zongtang that would cost him face.

所以阜康的风潮一起，他根本就没有想到过左宗棠。
When the trouble at Fukang broke out, Zuo had therefore never even entered his thoughts.

“为什么不告诉他？”莲珠说道：
“Why should you not tell him?” Lianzhu asked.

“你瞒也瞒不住的。”
“You cannot keep it from him in any case.”

“说得不错。”德馨也说：
“Quite right,” Dexin agreed.

“如果左大人肯出面，到底是两江总督部堂！”
“If Lord Zuo were willing to intervene—he is, after all, the Governor-General of Liangjiang!”

这个衔头在东南半壁，至高无上，
In the southeastern half of the empire, no office stood higher.

但到底能发生什么作用，却很难说。
What practical effect its intervention could have, however, was another question.

哪知道莲珠别有深心，
Lianzhu, as it happened, had a further purpose in mind.

“胡大先生这会心很乱，
“Mr Hu's thoughts must be in turmoil at present.

恐怕不知道该跟左大人说什么好？”
Perhaps he does not know what he ought to say to Lord Zuo?”

她随即提出一个建议：
She promptly made a suggestion.

“是不是请杨师爷来拟个稿子看看？”
“Why not ask Secretary Yang to draw up a draft for us to look at?”

那杨师爷是苏州人，年纪很轻，但笔下很来得，而且能言善道，善体人意，
Secretary Yang was a young man from Suzhou, an excellent writer, an accomplished speaker, and particularly good at understanding other people's wishes.

莲珠对他很欣赏。
Lianzhu thought highly of him.

德馨只要是莲珠说好就好，所以对杨师爷亦颇另眼相看，
Since Dexin was content to approve anyone she approved of, he too treated the young secretary with special favour.

此时便问胡雪岩：“你的意思怎么样？”
“What do you think?” he now asked Hu.

“好是好！不过只怕太缓了。”
“It would be a good idea, only I am afraid it would take too long.”

“怎么缓得了？
“How could it?

发电报出去，明天一早就到了。”
Send a telegram and it will be there first thing tomorrow morning.”

“我密码本不在这里。”
“I have not brought my codebook.”

“用我们的好了。”莲珠接口。
“Use ours,” Lianzhu put in.

“对啊！”德馨说道，
“Of course!” Dexin said.

“请杨师爷拟好了稿子，就请他翻密码好了。
“Once Secretary Yang has drafted it, he can put it into code.

小妾也可以帮忙。”
My concubine can help him.”

“这，怎么好麻烦二太太？”
“But how could I trouble Second Madam with that?”

“怕什么，
“What trouble?

我们两家什么交情！”
Think how close our two families are!”

真是盛情难地，胡雪岩只有感激的分儿。
Faced with such warmth, Hu could only feel grateful.

在请杨师爷的这段时间中，离座踱着方步，将要说的话都想好了。
While they sent for Secretary Yang, he left his seat and paced slowly about, arranging in his mind what he wished to say.


“杨师爷，拜托你起个稿子，要说这样子几点：
“Secretary Yang, may I trouble you to prepare a draft covering these points?

第一，请左大人为了维持人心，打电报给上海道，尽力维持阜康。
First, to preserve public confidence, ask Lord Zuo to telegraph the Shanghai circuit intendant and instruct him to do everything possible to support Fukang.

第二，请两江各衙门，暂时不要提存款。
Second, ask the various government offices in Liangjiang to leave their deposits untouched for the time being.

第三，浙江刘抚台、德馨台很帮忙，请左大人来个电报，客气一番。”
Third, Governor Liu and Treasurer Dexin here in Zhejiang have been most helpful; ask Lord Zuo to send a telegram expressing his appreciation.”

“客气倒不必。”德馨说道：
“There is no need for compliments,” Dexin said.

“要重重托一托刘抚台。”
“But he ought to make a strong personal appeal to Governor Liu.”

“是！是！”
“Certainly, certainly!”

杨师爷鞠躬如也地问：
Secretary Yang bowed deferentially.

“还有什么话？”
“Is there anything else?”

“想到了，再告诉你。”莲珠接口说道：
“If we think of anything, we shall tell you,” Lianzhu interposed.

“杨师爷，你请到外面来写，
“Come and write in the outer room, Secretary Yang.

清静一点儿。”
It will be quieter there.”

莲珠很热心地引领着杨师爷到了外屋，悄悄嘱咐了一番。
She led him out with great solicitude and gave him some instructions in a whisper.

他下笔很快，不到半个钟头，便将稿子送了上来，
He wrote quickly, and in less than half an hour brought back the draft.

除了照胡雪岩所要求的三点陈述以外，前面特为加一段，盛称德馨如何帮忙，得以暂度难关，实在令人感激，
Besides setting out the three points Hu had requested, it opened with a special passage praising Dexin's assistance, which had enabled Hu to weather the immediate difficulty and deserved his profound gratitude.

同时也说了些德馨在浙江的政绩。
It also mentioned some of Dexin's achievements in the administration of Zhejiang.

着墨不多，但措词很有力量，
The passage was short but forcefully worded.

这当然是莲珠悄悄嘱咐的结果。
This was plainly the result of Lianzhu's whispered instructions.


胡雪岩心里雪亮，
Hu understood perfectly.

德馨曾透露过口风，希望更上层楼，由藩司升为巡抚，作一个真正的方面大员，
Dexin had previously let fall a hint that he hoped to rise another step, from provincial treasurer to governor, and become a true provincial chief.

而目标是江西。
The province he had in mind was Jiangxi.

这就需要两江总督的支持了。
For that he needed the support of the Governor-General of Liangjiang.

原来所谓两江是明朝的说法，安徽是上江，江苏是下江，
The expression “Liangjiang,” or “the Two Jiangs,” dated from the Ming: Anhui was the Upper Jiang and Jiangsu the Lower Jiang.

两江总督只管江苏、安徽两省，但江西与苏皖密迩，两江总督亦管得着，犹之乎直未总督，必要时能管山东。
The Governor-General of Liangjiang governed only Jiangsu and Anhui, but because Jiangxi adjoined them, his authority could extend there too, just as the Governor-General of Zhili could, when necessary, intervene in Shandong.

将来江西巡抚出缺，如果左宗棠肯保德馨，便有一言九鼎之力。
If the governorship of Jiangxi should fall vacant and Zuo Zongtang were willing to recommend Dexin, his word would carry decisive weight.

所以电报中由胡雪岩出面，力赞德馨如何帮忙，实际上即是示好于左宗棠，为他自己的前程“烧冷灶”。
By having Hu lavish praise on Dexin's assistance in the telegram, they were in effect making an overture to Zuo and tending a currently cold hearth for the sake of Dexin's future career.

当然胡雪岩是乐于帮这个惠而不费的忙，
Naturally Hu was happy to do a favour that cost him nothing.

而且电报稿既出于杨师爷之手，便等于德馨作了愿全力维持的承诺，
Moreover, since the draft came from Dexin's own secretary, it virtually amounted to a promise by Dexin to give him his fullest support.

更是何乐不为！
Why should he not welcome it?

因此，他看完稿子，口中连声说道：“好极，好极！
Having read it, he said repeatedly, “Excellent, excellent!

杨师爷的一支笔实在佩服。”
I truly admire your pen, Secretary Yang.”

“哪里，哪里？”
“You are too kind.”

杨师爷递过一支毛笔来，
Yang offered him a writing brush.

“有不妥的地方，请胡大先生改正。”
“If anything is unsuitable, please correct it, Mr Hu.”

“只字不改！
“I would not change a single word.

都是我心里的话，
It says exactly what is in my heart.

为啥要改？”
Why should I alter it?”

说着，接过毛笔来，写了个“雪”字，表示同意。
Taking the brush, he wrote the character Xue from his name to signify his approval.


正谈到里，只见阿福掀帘人内，
At that moment A’fu lifted the curtain and came in.

悄悄地走到德馨身边，送上一个卷宗，口中轻声说道，“刚到的。”
Going quietly to Dexin's side, he handed him a folder and murmured, “Just arrived.”

“喔！”
“Oh?”

德馨将卷宗掀开，
Dexin opened it.

内中只有一张纸，
There was only one sheet inside.

胡雪岩遥遥望去，看出是一通电报，字迹却看不清楚。
From where he sat, Hu could see that it was a telegram, but he could not make out the writing.

“我的眼镜呢？”
“Where are my spectacles?”

德馨一面说，一面起身找眼镜，借此走到间壁，
Dexin rose to look for them, using this as an excuse to go into the adjoining room.

杨师爷随即跟了过去。
Secretary Yang immediately followed.

胡雪岩有点心神不定，
Hu began to feel uneasy.

深夜来了电报，是不是有关阜康的消息？
Could a telegram arriving so late concern Fukang?

如果是阜康的消息，德馨应该告诉他才是。
If it did, Dexin ought to tell him.

这样想着，双眼不由得一直注视里间。
As he thought this, his eyes remained fixed on the inner room.

“胡大先生，”莲珠说道：“你不要着急，
“Mr Hu,” Lianzhu said, “do not distress yourself.

有什么为难的事，你不便出面，让罗四姐来跟我说，
If there is any awkward matter you cannot conveniently raise in person, have Luo Sijie come and tell me.

我来告诉我们老爷。”
I will speak to our master.”

“是，是，多谢二太太！”
“Thank you, thank you, Second Madam.”

莲珠还有话要说，但德馨已经出来了，
Lianzhu had more to say, but Dexin had already returned.

她跟胡雪岩都盯着他看，希望他宣布深夜来电报，是何事故。
Both she and Hu looked intently at him, waiting to hear what the late-night telegram concerned.

但德馨却不作声，
Dexin said nothing.

坐了下来，举杯徐饮。
He sat down, raised his cup, and sipped his wine.

“哪里来的电报？”莲珠问说。
“Where was the telegram from?” Lianzhu asked.

“不相干的事。”
“Nothing to do with this.”

只说了这句又没话了。
That was all he said.


原来这个电报是宁波海关监督候补道瑞庆打来的，
The telegram was in fact from Ruiqing, the expectant circuit intendant serving as superintendent of the Ningbo Customs.

说他得到密报，上海阜康钱庄的档手宓本常潜回宁波来筹现银。
He had received confidential information that Mi Benchang, manager of the Shanghai Fukang Bank, had slipped back to Ningbo to raise cash.

阜康在宁波的联号，共有两家，一家叫通泉钱庄，一家叫通裕银号。
Fukang had two affiliated establishments there, Tongquan Bank and Tongyu Banking House.

但因宁波市面亦以越南战事的影响，颇为萧条，通泉、通裕都无从接济阜康。
But business in Ningbo had also been badly depressed by the war in Vietnam, and neither establishment was in a position to assist Fukang.

而且通泉的档手不知避匿何处，通裕银号的档手则自行请求封闭，
Worse still, Tongquan's manager had gone into hiding and could not be found, while Tongyu's manager had himself requested that his establishment be sealed.

因此，瑞庆即命鄞悬知县查封通裕，请德馨转知通泉、通裕的东主，即速清理。
Ruiqing had accordingly instructed the magistrate of Yin County to seal Tongyu, and was asking Dexin to notify the proprietors of both banks to settle their affairs without delay.

德馨对通泉、通裕的情况还不清楚，一时不知如何处置，因而就不便公开这通电报。
Dexin knew too little about their circumstances to decide what to do, and had therefore felt unable to disclose the telegram.

直到胡雪岩告辞以后，才跟莲珠商量。首先问她，这个消息暂且瞒着胡雪岩，是不是做错了？
Only after Hu had left did he discuss it with Lianzhu, first asking whether he had been wrong to withhold the news for the time being.

““当然错了！”莲珠问道：
“Of course you were!” she replied.

“你为什么当时不说？”

是暗示她如果觉得有将财物寄顿他处的必要，她可以效劳。
Lianzhu was hinting that if she felt it necessary to place money and valuables elsewhere for safekeeping, Lianzhu would be willing to help.


莲珠一向言辞爽脆深刻，
Lianzhu usually spoke with briskness and penetration.

隐微难达之情，在她往往三、五句话，便能直透深处。
Feelings too subtle to express could often be laid bare by her in three or five sentences.

唯独这件事如此难于出口，其中的道理，在同样善体人情的螺蛳太太；不难明白，
That this subject alone should prove so difficult was not hard for Madam Luosi, equally sensitive to human feelings, to understand.

正因为交情厚了，才不易措词。
Their very closeness made the words harder to find.

因为，要谈这件事，便有一个不忍出口的前提，就是阜康的风潮，会牵连到许多衙门来提公款，
For to raise the matter was to imply something too cruel to say aloud: the crisis at Fukang might cause numerous government offices to demand their public funds.

倘或无以应付，即可查封财产备抵，而犹不足，不可避免地就会抄家。
If Hu could not pay, his property could be sealed as security; if that still proved insufficient, confiscation of the household would inevitably follow.

莲珠一面说，一面心里就有一种顾忌，是设想螺蛳太太听了她的话以后的想法：什么！
As Lianzhu spoke, she was troubled by the thought of how Madam Luosi might interpret her words: What!

已经看得我们胡家要抄家了。
Had she already decided that the Hu household was going to be confiscated?

照此看来是黄鼠狼给鸡拜年，没有存着好心。
In that case, she was like a weasel paying a New Year's call on a hen—her intentions were far from kind.

如果再谈到寄顿财物，似乎坐实了她没有存着好心，
If she then offered to keep their valuables, would that not seem to confirm the suspicion?

胡家抄家于她有什么好处？
What could she gain from the confiscation of the Hu estate?

不就可以吞没了寄存的财物了吗？
Surely she could simply swallow up whatever they had entrusted to her.

不但抄家，最好充军、杀头，才能永绝后患。
And confiscation alone would not be enough: exile or execution would be better still, leaving no one to trouble her afterwards.

在这佯的顾虑之下，微稍聪明些的人都知道，这不是谈这件事的时候。
With such considerations in mind, anyone of even modest intelligence would know that this was no time to broach the subject.

但象这种寄顿家财，以防籍没的事，时机最要紧，愈早部署愈好。
Yet in arranging safekeeping for family wealth against confiscation, timing was crucial: the earlier the preparations, the better.

莲珠必是想到了这一点，
Lianzhu must have thought of that.

正见得是为好朋友深谋远虑的打算。
Her willingness to speak showed how carefully she was considering her friend's future.


转念到此，螺蛳太太异常感动，
When this occurred to Madam Luosi, she was deeply moved.

“莲姐，不枉我们同烧过一炉香。
“Sister Lian, we did not burn incense together in vain.

真正是急难可以倚靠，比同胞还亲的姐妹。”
You truly are a sister I can rely upon in adversity, closer to me than my own flesh and blood.”

她声音急促他说：
She spoke quickly.

“不过，莲姐，我现在只能作我自己的主，
“But for now I can only dispose of what belongs to me.

我有点首饰，
I have some jewellery.

初五那天还要戴，
I shall still need to wear it on the fifth.

过了这场喜事，我理好了送到你那里去。”
Once the wedding is over, I will put it in order and send it to you.”

这一说莲珠反倒推辞了，
This time Lianzhu held back.

她主要的是要提醒螺蛳太太，应该有最坏的打算。
Her main purpose had been to remind Madam Luosi to prepare for the worst.

如今看她显然已领会到了，那就不必亟亟，
Now that her meaning was clearly understood, there was no need to hurry.

“罗四姐，你懂我的意思就好。”她说，
“It is enough that you understand me, Luo Sijie,” she said.

“现在也还不到那步田地，
“Things have not yet come to that.

不过人无远虑，必有近忧，
Still, without thought for the future, trouble will soon be at one's door.

但愿你们逢凶化吉，遇难成祥，我今天的这番心里的话，完全是多余的。”
May every danger turn to good fortune for you, and may all these words from my heart prove entirely unnecessary.”

“莲姐，算命的都说我命中有‘贵人’，
“Sister Lian, the fortune-tellers always said there would be a benefactor in my life.

你今天就是。
Today, that benefactor is you.

但愿如你金口，
May your kind words come true.

等这场凤潮过了，莲姐，我们到普陀去烧香，
When this crisis is over, let us go to Putuo to burn incense together.

保佑藩台高升抚台，你老来结子，生个白胖儿子。”
We will pray for the treasurer to be promoted to governor, and for you to bear fruit in your later years and have a fine, plump baby boy.”


“不要说笑话了。”
“Do not tease me.”

莲珠的脸一红，嗫嚅了好一会说，“不知道你们胡庆余堂，有没有好的调经种子丸？”
Lianzhu blushed and hesitated for some time before saying, “I wonder whether your Hu Qingyu Hall has any good pills for regulating the monthly courses and promoting conception?”

“有，有！
“Yes, indeed!

我明天叫人送来。”
I shall have some sent over tomorrow.”

“不要，不要！”
“No, no!”

莲珠连连摇手，
Lianzhu waved both hands.

“传出去笑死人了。”
“If word got out, people would laugh themselves to death.”

“那么，改天我亲自带来。”
“Then I shall bring them myself another day.”

于是促膝低语谈了许多房帏间的心得，一直到胡雪岩重新上楼，方始结束。
Sitting close together, they went on to exchange a great many private observations about married intimacy, and stopped only when Hu returned upstairs.

此时此地居然有这样的闲情逸致，且不说螺蛳太太，连莲珠亦觉得是件不可思议的事。
That they could find leisure for such a conversation at such a moment seemed extraordinary even to Lianzhu, let alone Madam Luosi.


“稿子是拟好了，
“The draft is ready,” Hu said.

请二太太看看，有不妥当的地方，再改。”
“Please look it over, Second Madam, and alter anything that is unsuitable.”

“唷！胡大先生，我哪里看得懂。
“Oh, Mr Hu, how could I make sense of it?

你说给我听听好了。”
Tell me what it says instead.”

“大意是……”
“The substance of it is…”

大意是告诉宁波关监督瑞庆，说胡雪岩的态度光明磊落，
In substance, it informed Ruiqing, superintendent of the Ningbo Customs, that Hu Xueyan's intentions were open and honourable.

通泉、通裕的倒闭，虽非始料所及，但一定会负责到底，而且以胡雪岩的实力，亦必能转然为安。
Although the failure of Tongquan and Tongyu had taken him by surprise, he would accept responsibility to the end, and his resources were certainly sufficient to turn danger into safety.

但阜康受时间的影响，事出无奈，
Fukang's position, however, had been affected by the times, and the present difficulty was unavoidable.

为了维持市面，只可尽力协助，不宜逼迫过急，反生事端。
To preserve stability in the market, every assistance should be given; excessive pressure might merely provoke further trouble.

接着提到窗本常在宁彼，希望瑞庆即刻传他到案，责成他料理‘两通’，但所用手段，宜以劝导为主。
The telegram then mentioned that Mi Benchang was in Ningbo and requested that Ruiqing summon him at once and hold him responsible for putting the two Tong banks in order, relying chiefly on persuasion.

语气婉转周至，
Its tone was tactful and considerate throughout.

而且暗示瑞庆，若能费心尽力，料理妥当，德馨会面陈巡抚，今年的年终考绩，必有优异的“考语”。
It also hinted that if Ruiqing would take the necessary trouble and settle the affair satisfactorily, Dexin would report his efforts personally to the governor, ensuring an excellent assessment in that year's official review.

“好！好！”莲珠满口答应，
“Good, very good!” Lianzhu agreed readily.

“我请我们老爷，马上发出去。”
“I shall ask our master to send it at once.”

“是！多谢二太太。”
“Thank you, Second Madam.”

“我要走了。”
“I must be going.”

莲珠起身说道：
Lianzhu rose.

“你们也早点休息，
“You should go to bed early too.

初五办喜事，一定要把精神打起来。
With the wedding on the fifth, you must both keep your spirits up.”
