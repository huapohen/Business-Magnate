胡雪岩本想告辞，转念又想，应该不改常度：

On several previous evening visits he had stayed for supper when the time came; he ought to do the same tonight.
有几次夜间来访，到了时候总是吃消夜，这天也应该照常才是。

“姨太太呢？”德馨问说，

“马上出来。”

Lianzhu did not observe the convention of avoiding Hu Xueyan.

莲珠是螺蛳太太特为邀来陪官眷的“支宾”。

“Of course it is.

连多说句话都不得。”

二太太是金玉良言。”

多听听罗四姐的话就好了。”

“恐怕还不晓得。”

“Why have you not told him?”
“你怎么不告诉他？”

“Tell him?”
“告诉他？”

Hu sounded rather bewildered.
胡雪岩有些茫然，

For many years he had never reported anything to Zuo Zongtang that would cost him face.
多少年来，凡是失面子的事，他从不告诉左宗棠，

When the trouble at Fukang broke out, Zuo had therefore never even entered his thoughts.
所以阜康的风潮一起，他根本就没有想到过左宗棠。

“Why should you not tell him?” Lianzhu asked.
“为什么不告诉他？”莲珠说道：

“You cannot keep it from him in any case.”

In the southeastern half of the empire, no office stood higher.
这个衔头在东南半壁，至高无上，

What practical effect its intervention could have, however, was another question.
但到底能发生什么作用，却很难说。

Lianzhu, as it happened, had a further purpose in mind.
哪知道莲珠别有深心，

“Mr Hu's thoughts must be in turmoil at present.
“胡大先生这会心很乱，

Perhaps he does not know what he ought to say to Lord Zuo?”
恐怕不知道该跟左大人说什么好？”

She promptly made a suggestion.
她随即提出一个建议：

“Why not ask Secretary Yang to draw up a draft for us to look at?”
“是不是请杨师爷来拟个稿子看看？”

Secretary Yang was a young man from Suzhou, an excellent writer, an accomplished speaker, and particularly good at understanding other people's wishes.
那杨师爷是苏州人，年纪很轻，但笔下很来得，而且能言善道，善体人意，

Lianzhu thought highly of him.
莲珠对他很欣赏。

Since Dexin was content to approve anyone she approved of, he too treated the young secretary with special favour.
德馨只要是莲珠说好就好，所以对杨师爷亦颇另眼相看，

此时便问胡雪岩：“你的意思怎么样？”

“It would be a good idea, only I am afraid it would take too long.”

“How could it?
“怎么缓得了？

Send a telegram and it will be there first thing tomorrow morning.”
发电报出去，明天一早就到了。”

“I have not brought my codebook.”
“我密码本不在这里。”

“Use ours,” Lianzhu put in.
“用我们的好了。”莲珠接口。

“Of course!” Dexin said.

小妾也可以帮忙。”

“But how could I trouble Second Madam with that?”
“这，怎么好麻烦二太太？”

“What trouble?
“怕什么，

Think how close our two families are!”
我们两家什么交情！”

真是盛情难地，胡雪岩只有感激的分儿。

While they sent for Secretary Yang, he left his seat and paced slowly about, arranging in his mind what he wished to say.
在请杨师爷的这段时间中，离座踱着方步，将要说的话都想好了。

第三，浙江刘抚台、德馨台很帮忙，请左大人来个电报，客气一番。”

清静一点儿。”

莲珠很热心地引领着杨师爷到了外屋，悄悄嘱咐了一番。

他下笔很快，不到半个钟头，便将稿子送了上来，

除了照胡雪岩所要求的三点陈述以外，前面特为加一段，盛称德馨如何帮忙，得以暂度难关，实在令人感激，

着墨不多，但措词很有力量，

这当然是莲珠悄悄嘱咐的结果。


Hu understood perfectly.
胡雪岩心里雪亮，

德馨曾透露过口风，希望更上层楼，由藩司升为巡抚，作一个真正的方面大员，

Lianzhu was hinting that if she felt it necessary to place money and valuables elsewhere for safekeeping, Lianzhu would be willing to help.
是暗示她如果觉得有将财物寄顿他处的必要，她可以效劳。


Lianzhu usually spoke with briskness and penetration.
莲珠一向言辞爽脆深刻，

Feelings too subtle to express could often be laid bare by her in three or five sentences.
隐微难达之情，在她往往三、五句话，便能直透深处。

That this subject alone should prove so difficult was not hard for Madam Luosi, equally sensitive to human feelings, to understand.
唯独这件事如此难于出口，其中的道理，在同样善体人情的螺蛳太太；不难明白，

Their very closeness made the words harder to find.
正因为交情厚了，才不易措词。

For to raise the matter was to imply something too cruel to say aloud: the crisis at Fukang might cause numerous government offices to demand their public funds.
因为，要谈这件事，便有一个不忍出口的前提，就是阜康的风潮，会牵连到许多衙门来提公款，

If Hu could not pay, his property could be sealed as security; if that still proved insufficient, confiscation of the household would inevitably follow.
倘或无以应付，即可查封财产备抵，而犹不足，不可避免地就会抄家。

As Lianzhu spoke, she was troubled by the thought of how Madam Luosi might interpret her words: What!
莲珠一面说，一面心里就有一种顾忌，是设想螺蛳太太听了她的话以后的想法：什么！

Had she already decided that the Hu household was going to be confiscated?
已经看得我们胡家要抄家了。

In that case, she was like a weasel paying a New Year's call on a hen—her intentions were far from kind.
照此看来是黄鼠狼给鸡拜年，没有存着好心。

If she then offered to keep their valuables, would that not seem to confirm the suspicion?
如果再谈到寄顿财物，似乎坐实了她没有存着好心，

What could she gain from the confiscation of the Hu estate?
胡家抄家于她有什么好处？

Surely she could simply swallow up whatever they had entrusted to her.
不就可以吞没了寄存的财物了吗？

And confiscation alone would not be enough: exile or execution would be better still, leaving no one to trouble her afterwards.
不但抄家，最好充军、杀头，才能永绝后患。

With such considerations in mind, anyone of even modest intelligence would know that this was no time to broach the subject.
在这佯的顾虑之下，微稍聪明些的人都知道，这不是谈这件事的时候。

Yet in arranging safekeeping for family wealth against confiscation, timing was crucial: the earlier the preparations, the better.
但象这种寄顿家财，以防籍没的事，时机最要紧，愈早部署愈好。

Lianzhu must have thought of that.
莲珠必是想到了这一点，

Her willingness to speak showed how carefully she was considering her friend's future.
正见得是为好朋友深谋远虑的打算。


When this occurred to Madam Luosi, she was deeply moved.
转念到此，螺蛳太太异常感动，

“Sister Lian, we did not burn incense together in vain.
“莲姐，不枉我们同烧过一炉香。

You truly are a sister I can rely upon in adversity, closer to me than my own flesh and blood.”
真正是急难可以倚靠，比同胞还亲的姐妹。”

She spoke quickly.
她声音急促他说：

“But for now I can only dispose of what belongs to me.
“不过，莲姐，我现在只能作我自己的主，

I have some jewellery.
我有点首饰，

I shall still need to wear it on the fifth.
初五那天还要戴，

Once the wedding is over, I will put it in order and send it to you.”
过了这场喜事，我理好了送到你那里去。”

This time Lianzhu held back.
这一说莲珠反倒推辞了，

Her main purpose had been to remind Madam Luosi to prepare for the worst.
她主要的是要提醒螺蛳太太，应该有最坏的打算。

Now that her meaning was clearly understood, there was no need to hurry.
如今看她显然已领会到了，那就不必亟亟，

“It is enough that you understand me, Luo Sijie,” she said.
“罗四姐，你懂我的意思就好。”她说，

“Things have not yet come to that.
“现在也还不到那步田地，

Still, without thought for the future, trouble will soon be at one's door.
不过人无远虑，必有近忧，

May every danger turn to good fortune for you, and may all these words from my heart prove entirely unnecessary.”
但愿你们逢凶化吉，遇难成祥，我今天的这番心里的话，完全是多余的。”

“Sister Lian, the fortune-tellers always said there would be a benefactor in my life.
“莲姐，算命的都说我命中有‘贵人’，

Today, that benefactor is you.
你今天就是。

May your kind words come true.
但愿如你金口，

When this crisis is over, let us go to Putuo to burn incense together.
等这场凤潮过了，莲姐，我们到普陀去烧香，

We will pray for the treasurer to be promoted to governor, and for you to bear fruit in your later years and have a fine, plump baby boy.”
保佑藩台高升抚台，你老来结子，生个白胖儿子。”


“Do not tease me.”
“不要说笑话了。”

Lianzhu blushed and hesitated for some time before saying, “I wonder whether your Hu Qingyu Hall has any good pills for regulating the monthly courses and promoting conception?”
莲珠的脸一红，嗫嚅了好一会说，“不知道你们胡庆余堂，有没有好的调经种子丸？”

“Yes, indeed!
“有，有！

I shall have some sent over tomorrow.”
我明天叫人送来。”

“No, no!”
“不要，不要！”

Lianzhu waved both hands.
莲珠连连摇手，

“If word got out, people would laugh themselves to death.”
“传出去笑死人了。”

“Then I shall bring them myself another day.”
“那么，改天我亲自带来。”

Sitting close together, they went on to exchange a great many private observations about married intimacy, and stopped only when Hu returned upstairs.
于是促膝低语谈了许多房帏间的心得，一直到胡雪岩重新上楼，方始结束。

That they could find leisure for such a conversation at such a moment seemed extraordinary even to Lianzhu, let alone Madam Luosi.
此时此地居然有这样的闲情逸致，且不说螺蛳太太，连莲珠亦觉得是件不可思议的事。


“The draft is ready,” Hu said.
“稿子是拟好了，

“Please look it over, Second Madam, and alter anything that is unsuitable.”
请二太太看看，有不妥当的地方，再改。”

“Oh, Mr Hu, how could I make sense of it?
“唷！胡大先生，我哪里看得懂。

Tell me what it says instead.”
你说给我听听好了。”

“The substance of it is…”
“大意是……”

In substance, it informed Ruiqing, superintendent of the Ningbo Customs, that Hu Xueyan's intentions were open and honourable.
大意是告诉宁波关监督瑞庆，说胡雪岩的态度光明磊落，

Although the failure of Tongquan and Tongyu had taken him by surprise, he would accept responsibility to the end, and his resources were certainly sufficient to turn danger into safety.
通泉、通裕的倒闭，虽非始料所及，但一定会负责到底，而且以胡雪岩的实力，亦必能转然为安。

Fukang's position, however, had been affected by the times, and the present difficulty was unavoidable.
但阜康受时间的影响，事出无奈，

To preserve stability in the market, every assistance should be given; excessive pressure might merely provoke further trouble.
为了维持市面，只可尽力协助，不宜逼迫过急，反生事端。

The telegram then mentioned that Mi Benchang was in Ningbo and requested that Ruiqing summon him at once and hold him responsible for putting the two Tong banks in order, relying chiefly on persuasion.
接着提到窗本常在宁彼，希望瑞庆即刻传他到案，责成他料理‘两通’，但所用手段，宜以劝导为主。

Its tone was tactful and considerate throughout.
语气婉转周至，

It also hinted that if Ruiqing would take the necessary trouble and settle the affair satisfactorily, Dexin would report his efforts personally to the governor, ensuring an excellent assessment in that year's official review.
而且暗示瑞庆，若能费心尽力，料理妥当，德馨会面陈巡抚，今年的年终考绩，必有优异的“考语”。

“Good, very good!” Lianzhu agreed readily.
“好！好！”莲珠满口答应，

“I shall ask our master to send it at once.”
“我请我们老爷，马上发出去。”

“Thank you, Second Madam.”
“是！多谢二太太。”

“I must be going.”
“我要走了。”

Lianzhu rose.
莲珠起身说道：

“You should go to bed early too.
“你们也早点休息，

With the wedding on the fifth, you must both keep your spirits up.”
初五办喜事，一定要把精神打起来。
