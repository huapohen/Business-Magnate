胡雪岩
《烟消云散》第六章（上）
Vanishing into Thin Air — Chapter Six, Part One
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第六章 探骊得珠｜CHAPTER SIX: SEIZING THE DRAGON’S PEARL


乌先生却还未睡，所以一请就到，
Mr Wu had not yet gone to bed and came as soon as he was summoned.

他是第一次见德馨，
This was his first meeting with Dexin.

在胡雪岩引见以后，少不得有一番客套，
After Hu Xueyan introduced them, the usual courtesies were exchanged.

德馨又恭维他测字测得妙，接下来便要向他“请教”了。
Dexin complimented him on his ingenious character divination, then proceeded to ask his advice.

“不敢当，不敢当！
“You are too kind!

雕虫小技，不登大雅。”乌先生问：
A trifling skill, quite unworthy of serious company,” Mr Wu replied.

“不知道德大人想问什么？”
“What would Your Excellency like to ask?”

“我在谋一件事，不知道有成功的希望没有？
“I am pursuing a certain ambition and do not know whether there is any hope of success.

想请乌先生费心替我卜一下。”
Would you be kind enough to divine the outcome?”

“是！
“Certainly.

请报一个字。”
Please give me a character.”

德馨略想一想说：“就是谋字吧。”
After a moment’s thought, Dexin said, “Let it be mou: ‘to scheme.’”

一旁有现成的笔砚，
Brush and ink were ready at hand.

乌先生坐下来取张纸，提笔将“谋”字拆写成“言”、“某”两字，
Mr Wu sat down, took a sheet of paper, and separated the character into its two components, yan, meaning “speech,” and mou, meaning “a certain person.”

然后搁笔思考。
Then he laid down the brush to think.

这时德馨与胡雪岩亦都走了过来，手捧水烟袋，静静地站在桌旁观看。
Dexin and Hu came over, each holding a water pipe, and stood quietly beside the table.

“德大人所谋的这件事，要托人进‘言’，
“For what you are planning, Your Excellency, you need someone to speak on your behalf.

这个人心目中已经有了，没有说出来，那就是个‘某，。”
You already have a person in mind but have not named him: hence ‘a certain person.’”

乌先生笑道：
Mr Wu smiled.

“不瞒德大人说，我拆字是’三脚猫，，
“To be honest, I am only a dabbler at this.

也不会江湖诀，
I know none of the professional diviners’ formulas.

不过就字论字，
I simply consider the character itself.

如果说对了，一路拆下去，或许谈言微中，亦未可知。”
If I have begun correctly and continue taking it apart, perhaps I may stumble upon something pertinent.”

“是，是！”
“Yes, yes!

德馨很客气地：“高明之至。”
Most perceptive,” Dexin said politely.

“那么，请问德大人，我刚才一开头说对了没有？
“Then was my opening statement correct?

不对，重新来，
If not, we must begin again.

请德大人不要客气，一定要说实话。”
Please do not spare my feelings; you must tell the truth.”

“是的，我一定说实话，
“I certainly shall.

你老兄一开头就探骊得珠了。”
You have plunged straight to the dragon’s pearl.”

乌先生定睛细看一看他的脸色，直待确定了他是说的实话，
Mr Wu studied his face until he felt sure the answer was sincere.

方始欣慰地又说：“侥幸，侥幸。”燃后拈起笔来说道：
“A fortunate guess!” he said with satisfaction, taking up the brush again.

“人言为信，这个人立在言字旁边，
“‘Person’ beside ‘speech’ makes xin, ‘trust.’

意思是进言的人要钉在旁边，才会有作用。”
The person standing beside the speech means that your spokesman must keep close at hand if he is to be effective.”

“嗯，嗯！”德馨不断点头，而且不断眨眼，似乎一面听，一面在体味。
Dexin nodded repeatedly, blinking as though weighing the words as he listened.


“现在看这个某字，
“Now look at the component mou, ‘a certain person.’

加女为媒，
Add ‘woman,’ and it becomes mei, ‘a go-between.’

中间牵线的要个女人。”
A woman must provide the connection.”

“请教乌先生，这个牵线的女人，牵到哪一面？”
“May I ask which party this woman connects with?”

“问得好！”
“A good question!”

乌先生指着“信”字说，
Mr Wu pointed to xin, “trust.”

“这里有两个人，一个进言，一个纳言，
“There are two people here: one who speaks, and one who listens.

牵线是牵到进言的人身上。”
The woman makes the connection with the one who speaks.”

“意思是，这个为媒的女子，不是立在言子旁边的那个人？”
“You mean that the woman acting as go-between is not the person standing beside ‘speech’?”

“不错。”
“Exactly.”

“我明白了。”
“I understand.

德馨又问：“再要请教，我谋的这件事，什么时候着手？
Now let me ask: when should I begin?

会不会成功，
Will I succeed?

能够成功，是在什么时候？
And if so, when?”

““这就要看某字下面的这个木字了。”
“For that, we must consider the ‘wood’ beneath mou.”

乌先生将“某”下之“木”涂掉，成了“甘”、“言”二字，
Mr Wu blotted out that lower component, leaving gan, “sweet,” and yan, “speech.”

这就不必他解释了，
No explanation was necessary.

德馨便知道他所托的“某”人，满口答应，其实只是饴人的“甘言”。
Dexin understood that the person he relied on might promise everything, yet offer nothing more than soothing “sweet words.”

因此，他问：“要怎么样才会失掉这个木字？”
“How would the ‘wood’ be lost?” he asked.


“金克木。”乌先生答说：
“Metal overcomes Wood,” Mr Wu answered.

“如果这件是在七、八月里着手，已经不行了。”
“If you began in the seventh or eighth month, the venture would already be lost.”

“为什么呢？”
“Why?”

“七月申月，八月西月，
“The seventh month is shen, the eighth you.

都是金。”
Both belong to Metal.”

“现在十一月，”胡雪岩插嘴：
“This is the eleventh month,” Hu put in.

“十一月是不是子月？”
“Isn’t the eleventh month zi?”

“县的”胡雪岩略通五行生克之理，便向德馨说道：
“Yes.” Hu knew something of how the Five Elements generated and overcame one another.

“子是水，水生木，
“Zi is Water, and Water generates Wood.

晓翁，你赶快进行。”
Mr Xiaofeng, you must begin at once.”

“万万来不及。”德馨说道：
“There is no possible way to do it in time.

“今天十一月十六日，
Today is the sixteenth of the eleventh month.

只半个月不到，
Less than half a month remains.

哪来得及？”
How could I manage it?”

“而且水固生木，到下个月是丑月，丑为土，木克土不利。”乌先生接下来说：
“Besides,” Mr Wu continued, “though Water generates Wood, next month is chou, which belongs to Earth. Wood overcomes Earth: an unfavorable relation.

“最好开年正月里着手，
It would be best to begin in the first month of the new year.

正月寅，二月卯，都是木，
The first month is yin, the second mao, both Wood.

三月里有个顿挫，不过到四、五月里就好了，
There will be a setback in the third month, but all will be well by the fourth or fifth.

四月已，五月午都是火……”
The fourth is si, the fifth wu, both Fire…”

“木生火。”胡雪岩接口，
“Wood generates Fire,” Hu supplied.

“大功告成。”
“Complete success!”

“正是这话。”乌先生同意。
“Precisely,” Mr Wu agreed.

“高明，高明！
“Wonderful!

真是心悦诚服。”
I am entirely convinced.”

德馨满面笑容将水烟袋放下，
Beaming, Dexin put down his water pipe.

“这得送润笑，不送就不灵了。”
“I must offer you a fee, or the prediction will not come true.”

一面说，一面掀开“卧龙袋”，里面束着一条蓝绸汗巾作腰带，
He lifted his sleeveless outer garment, revealing a blue silk kerchief serving as a sash about his waist.

旗人在这条带子上的小零碎很多，
Bannermen carried many small accessories on such sashes.

他俯首看了一下，解下一个玉钱，双手递了过去。
Looking down, he detached a jade coin and presented it with both hands.

“不成敬意，
“A trifling token.

留着玩。”
Keep it to amuse yourself.”

乌先生接过来一看，倒是纯净无暇的一块羊脂白玉，上镌“乾隆通宝”四字，制得颇为精致，
Mr Wu found it was a flawless piece of mutton-fat white jade, finely carved with the four characters for “Qianlong circulating treasure.”

虽不甚值会，但确是很好的一样玩物，
It was not particularly valuable, but made a charming object to handle.

便连连拱手，口说：“谢谢，谢谢！”
He repeatedly clasped his hands in thanks.

“这个不算，
“This hardly counts,” Dexin added.

等明年夏天我谋的事成功了，再好好表一表谢意。”
“When my plan succeeds next summer, I shall thank you properly.”


等乌先生告辞退出，胡雪岩虽然自己心事重重，但为了表示关怀好朋友，仍旧兴致盎然地动问，德馨所谋何事？
After Mr Wu withdrew, Hu, despite his own heavy troubles, made a show of lively interest in his friend’s concerns and asked what he was planning.

“还不是想独当一面。
“What else but a post where I can take charge in my own right?

我走的是宝中堂的路子，托他令弟进言。”
I am working through Grand Secretary Bao, asking his younger brother to speak for me.

德馨又说：“前年你不是邀他到南边来玩，我顺便请他逛富春江，约你作陪，你有事不能去。你还记得这回事不？”
You invited the brother south for a visit the year before last, remember? I offered to take him along the Fuchun River and asked you to join us, but you were too busy.”

“嗯嗯。我记得。”
“Yes, I remember.

胡雪岩问说：“逛富春江的时候，你就跟他谈过了？”
Did you discuss it with him on that trip?”

“不！
“No.

那时候我刚升藩司不久，
I had only recently become provincial treasurer.

不能作此非分之想。”
I could hardly aspire beyond my station so soon.

德馨说道：“我们这位宝二爷看中了一个江山船上的船娘，向我示意，想藏诸金屋，
Our Second Master Bao took a fancy to a boatwoman on one of the Jiangshan boats and hinted that he wanted to install her in a secluded love nest.

而且言外之意，自备身价银子，不必我花费分文。
He also let me understand that he would supply the money himself: I need not spend a penny.

不过，我刚刚到任，
But I had just taken office.

怎么能拉这种马，
How could I act as a procurer?

所以装糊涂没有答腔。
I pretended not to understand.

最近，他跟我通信，还没有忘记这段旧情，
He has written to me recently and still hasn’t forgotten her.

而那个船娘，只想择人而事，
As for the woman, she wants to choose a suitable man to attach herself to.

我已经派人跟她娘老子谈过，
I’ve already sent someone to speak to her parents.

只要两千两银子，宝二爷即可如愿。
For just two thousand taels, Second Master Bao can have his wish.

我一直还在犹豫，今晚上听乌先生这一谈，吾志已决。”
I’ve been hesitating, but after listening to Mr Wu tonight, my mind is made up.”

这样去谋方面大员，胡雪岩心里不免菲薄，
Hu could not help thinking little of such a way to seek a major provincial appointment.

而且他觉得德馨的路子亦没有走对。
He also thought Dexin had chosen the wrong channel.

既然是朋友，不能不提出忠告。
As a friend, he ought to warn him.

“晓翁，”他问：“宝中堂跟他老的情形，你清楚不清楚？”
“Mr Xiaofeng, do you know how matters stand between Grand Secretary Bao and his brother?”

“弟兄不甚和睦是不是？”
“They don’t get on very well?”

“是的。”
“Exactly.

胡雪岩又说：“宝中堂见了他很头痛，
The grand secretary finds him a headache.

进言只怕不见得效。”
I doubt his intercession would do much good.”

“不然。”德馨答说：
“On the contrary.

“我跟他们昆仲是世交，他家的情形我知道。
Our families are old friends, and I know their affairs.

宝中堂对他这位令弟，一筹莫展，唯有安抚，
Bao can do nothing with his brother except placate him.

宝二爷只要天天在他老兄面前罗嗦，宝中堂为了躲麻烦，只有听他老弟的活。”
If Second Master Bao nags him every day, he’ll have to listen simply to escape the nuisance.”


听得这一说，胡雪岩只好付之一笑，
Hu could only smile at this.

不过想起一件事，带笑警告着说：
Then something occurred to him, and he added a smiling warning.

“晓翁，这件事你要做得秘密，
“You must keep the business secret, Mr Xiaofeng.

让都老爷晓得了，参上一本，又出江山船的新闻，
If the censors hear and submit an impeachment, there’ll be another Jiangshan boat scandal.

划不来。”
It wouldn’t be worth it.”

所谓“又出江山船的新闻”，是因为一年以前在江山船上出过一件新闻，
He was referring to a scandal of the previous year.

“翰林四谏”之一的宝廷，放了福建的主考，
Baoting, one of the “Four Hanlin Remonstrators,” had been appointed chief examiner in Fujian.

来去经由杭州，坐江山船溯富春江而入上闽，
Passing through Hangzhou on both journeys, he had taken a Jiangshan boat up the Fuchun River and into upper Fujian.

归途中纳江山船的一个船娘为妾，
On his return, he made one of the boatwomen his concubine.

言官打算搏击，宝廷见机，上奏自劾，因而落职。
The censors prepared to attack him, but Baoting anticipated them and submitted a memorial impeaching himself, whereupon he lost office.

在京的大名士李慈铭，做了一首诗咏其事，其中有一联极其工整：“宗室八旗名士草，江山九姓美人麻。”
Li Ciming, a celebrated man of letters in the capital, wrote a poem about it, including a particularly neat couplet: “Eight-Banner royal clansman, a scholar of grass; / Nine-Surname Jiangshan woman, a beauty with pockmarks.”

宝廷是宗室，也是名士，但加一“草”字，自是讥刺。
Baoting belonged to the imperial clan and was indeed a man of letters, but the addition of “grass” was plainly satirical.

下句则别有典故，
The second line contained another allusion.

据说江山船上的船户，共有九姓，皆为元末陈友谅的部将之后，
The families living on Jiangshan boats were said to bear nine surnames, all descended from commanders who had served Chen Youliang at the end of the Yuan.

朱元璋得了天下，为惩罚此辈，不准他们上岸居住，只能讨水上生涯。
After winning the empire, Zhu Yuanzhang punished them by forbidding them to live ashore: they could earn their living only on the water.

而宝廷所眷的船娘，是个俗语所说的“白麻子”，
The woman Baoting admired was what people called a “white-pockmarked woman.”

只以宝廷近视，咫尺之外，不辨人物，竟未发觉，
Being so shortsighted that he could barely distinguish people and things even at close quarters, he had failed to notice.

所以李慈铭有“美人麻”的谐谑。
Hence Li Ciming’s joke about a pockmarked beauty.

这两句诗，亦就因此烩炙人口，腾为笑柄。
The lines became widely quoted, and the affair a laughingstock.

德馨当然也知道这个故事，
Dexin naturally knew the story.

想起言官的气焰，不免心惊肉跳，
Remembering the censors’ ferocity, he felt distinctly uneasy.

所以口中所说“不要紧”，暗地里却接受了胡雪岩的警告，颇持戒心。
Though he said there was nothing to worry about, privately he accepted Hu’s warning and resolved to be cautious.


一夜之隔，情势大变，
Overnight, the situation changed drastically.

浙江巡抚刘秉漳接到直隶总督北洋大臣李鸿章的密电，
Liu Bingzhang, governor of Zhejiang, received a confidential telegram from Li Hongzhang, Governor-General of Zhili and Minister for the Northern Ports.

说有直隶水灾赈款六十万两银子，存在阜康福，被倒无着，
Six hundred thousand taels of Zhili flood-relief funds had been deposited in Fukangfu and were now stranded by its collapse.

电请刘秉漳查封胡雪岩所设的典当，备抵公款。
Li asked Liu to seal Hu Xueyan’s pawnshops as security for the public money.

于是刘秉璋即时将德馨请了去，以电报相示，问他有何意见？
Liu immediately sent for Dexin, showed him the telegram, and asked his opinion.

德馨已估量到会有这种恶劣的情况出现，老早亦想好了最后的办法，
Dexin had anticipated just such an adverse development and long since considered his last resort.

“司里的愚见，总以不影响市面为主。”他说，
“In my humble view, our principal concern must be to avoid disturbing trade,” he replied.

“如果雷厉风行，丝毫不留情面，刺激民心，
“If we act with ruthless speed, showing no consideration, we shall alarm the public.

总非地方之福，
That cannot benefit the province.

至于胡雪岩本人，气概倒还光明磊落，
As for Hu himself, his spirit is honorable and straightforward.

我看不如我去劝一劝他，要他自作处置。”
I suggest that I speak to him and persuade him to make his own arrangements.”

“何以谓之自作处置？”
“What do you mean by that?”

“让他自已把财产目录，公私亏欠帐目开出来，捧交大人，请大人替他作主。”
“Let him draw up an inventory of his property and accounts of his public and private debts, and place them in Your Excellency’s hands, asking you to act for him.”

刘秉璋原以为德馨的所谓“自作处置”，是劝胡雪岩自裁，
Liu had imagined that “make his own arrangements” meant persuading Hu to take his own life.

听了德馨的话，才知道自己误会了，也放心了。
Discovering his mistake, he felt relieved.

“好！
“Very well.

你者哥多费心。”
I must trouble you with this.

刘秉璋问：“什么时候可以听回音。”
When may I expect an answer?”

“总得明儿上午。”
“Tomorrow morning, at the earliest.”

当夜德馨又去看胡雪岩，
That night Dexin called on Hu again.

一见哽咽，居然挤出一副急泪，
At their meeting his voice caught, and he actually squeezed out a few anxious tears.

这就尽在不言中了。
They said everything.

胡雪岩却很但然，说一声：
Hu, however, was quite calm.

“晓翁，说我看不破，不对，说我方寸不乱，也不对。
“It would be wrong to say I cannot face the truth, Mr Xiaofeng, but equally wrong to say I am untroubled.

一切都请晓翁指点。”于是德馨道明来意，
I leave everything to your guidance.” Dexin explained his purpose.

胡雪岩一诺无辞。但提出一个要求，要给他两天的时间，理由是他要处分家务。
Hu agreed without argument, asking only for two days to settle his household affairs.

德馨沉吟了好一会说：
Dexin pondered for some time.

“我跟刘中丞去力争，大不了赔上一顶纱帽，也要把你这两天争了来。
“I shall fight for those two days with Governor Liu, even if it costs me my official cap.

但望两夭以后，能把所有帐目都交了给他。”
But I hope you can hand him all the accounts when they are over.”

“一言为定。”
“Agreed.”


等德馨一走，胡雪岩与螺蛳太太关紧了房门，整整谈了一夜。
After Dexin left, Hu and Madam Luosi shut themselves in and talked all night.

第二天分头采取了几项行动，
The next day they took several steps.

首先是发密电给汉口、镇江、福州、长沙、武昌各地的阜康，即日闭歇清理，
First, they sent confidential telegrams to the Fukang branches in Hankou, Zhenjiang, Fuzhou, Changsha, and Wuchang, instructing them to close immediately and begin liquidation.

其次是托古应春赶紧回上海，觅洋商议价出售存丝，
Next, they asked Gu Yingchun to hurry back to Shanghai and find foreign merchants with whom to negotiate the sale of the stored silk.

第三是集中一批现银，将少数至亲好友的存款付讫，
Third, they gathered a quantity of ready silver and paid off the deposits of a small number of close relatives and friends.

再是检点一批首饰、古玩，约略估价，抵偿德馨经手的一批存款。
They also selected jewelry and antiques, valued them approximately, and used them to settle a group of deposits handled through Dexin.

当然，还有最要紧的一件事是，开列财产目录。
Most important of all, they drew up an inventory of their property.

密密地忙到半夜，方始告一段落，
They worked quietly until midnight before reaching a stopping point.

胡雪岩累不可当，喝一杯人参浸泡的葡萄酒，正待上床时，德馨派专人送来一封信，
Utterly exhausted, Hu drank a glass of wine in which ginseng had been steeped and was about to go to bed when a special messenger brought a letter from Dexin.

信中写的是：“给事中邓承修奏请责令贪吏罚捐巨款，以济要需，
It read: “Supervising Censor Deng Chengxiu has memorialized, requesting that corrupt officials be ordered to contribute large sums as a penalty, to meet urgent needs.

另附一片，
A supplementary memorial was attached.

抄请察觉。”
A copy follows for your information.”

所附的抄件是：“另片奏：闻阜康银号关闭，协部大学士刑部尚书文煜，所存该号银数至七十余万之多，
The enclosure read: “In a supplementary memorial he states: ‘I hear that Fukang Bank has closed, and that the sum deposited there by Wenyu, Assistant Grand Secretary and Minister of Punishments, amounts to more than seven hundred thousand taels.

请旨查明确数，究所从来，等语，
I request an imperial order to ascertain the exact amount and investigate its source.’

着顺天府确查具奏。”
The Shuntian prefectural authorities are hereby ordered to investigate and report.”

这封信及抄件，不是个好消息，但胡雪岩亦想不出对他还有什么更不利之处，
Neither letter nor enclosure brought good news, but Hu could not see how this might make his own position any worse.

因而丢开了睡觉。
He put them aside and went to sleep.


一觉醒来，头脑清醒，自然而然地想到德馨传来的消息，同时也想到了文煜——
He awoke with a clear head and naturally thought of Dexin’s news, and of Wenyu himself.

他是满洲正蓝旗人，与恭王是姻亲，早在咸丰十一年就署理过直隶总督，
A Manchu of the Plain Blue Banner, related by marriage to Prince Gong, Wenyu had served as acting Governor-General of Zhili as early as the eleventh year of Xianfeng.

但发财却是同治七年任福州将军以后的事。
His fortune, however, had begun only after he became Fuzhou Garrison General in the seventh year of Tongzhi.

原来清兵入关，虽代明而得天下，但南明亡后，浙东有鲁王，西南有永历帝，海外有郑成功，
The Qing armies had entered the passes and won the empire from the Ming, but even after the Southern Ming fell, the Prince of Lu remained in eastern Zhejiang, the Yongli Emperor in the southwest, and Zheng Chenggong overseas.

此外还有异姓封王的“三藩”，手握重兵，亦可能成为心腹之患，
There were also the Three Feudatories, princes of other surnames commanding powerful armies who might become a grave internal threat.

因而在各省冲要枢纽之地，派遣旗营驻防，借以防备汉人反清复明。
Banner garrisons were therefore stationed at strategic centers in the provinces to guard against Han attempts to overthrow the Qing and restore the Ming.

统率驻防旗营长官，名为“将军”，上加地名，驻西安即名之为西安将军，驻杭州即名之为杭州将军。
Their commanders were styled generals, with the place name attached: the commander at Xi’an was the Xi’an Garrison General, the one at Hangzhou the Hangzhou Garrison General.


各地将军的权责不一，因地因时制宜，
Their authority and responsibilities varied with local conditions and circumstances.

福建因为先有郑成功父子的海上舟师，后有耿精忠响应吴三桂造反，
Fujian had first faced the fleets of Zheng Chenggong and his son, then Geng Jingzhong’s rebellion in support of Wu Sangui.

是用兵的要地，所以福州将军权柄特重，
As a strategic military region, it gave the Fuzhou Garrison General exceptional power.

他处将军，只管旗营，只有福州将军兼管“绿营”，
Elsewhere, generals commanded only Banner troops; the Fuzhou general alone also controlled Green Standard troops.

此外还有一项差使，兼管闽海关。
He had a further duty: supervision of the Fujian Maritime Customs.

起初只是为了盘查海船，以防偷渡或私运军械，到后来却成了一个专门收税的利薮，
Originally intended to inspect seagoing vessels and prevent illicit crossings or arms smuggling, this had gradually become a lucrative tax-collecting office.

尤其是鸦片战争以后，海禁大开，英、法、美、日各国商人都在福州设有洋行，
Especially after the Opium War, when the maritime restrictions were thrown wide open, British, French, American, and Japanese merchants established trading houses in Fuzhou.

闽海关的税收大增，兼管海关亦就成了有名的美差。
Customs revenue grew enormously, and its supervision became a famously profitable appointment.

文烃从同治七年当福州将军，十年兼署闽浙总督，
Wenyu became Fuzhou Garrison General in the seventh year of Tongzhi and concurrently acted as Governor-General of Fujian and Zhejiang from the tenth year.

直至光绪三年内调，前后在福州九年，
He remained until his transfer to the capital in the third year of Guangxu, spending nine years in Fuzhou.

宦囊丰盈，都存在阜康银号。
His official purse grew fat, and he deposited the money with Fukang.

及至是京以后，先后充任崇文门正监督、内务府总管大臣，亦都是可以搞钱的差使，
In the capital, he served successively as chief superintendent at Chongwen Gate and minister of the Imperial Household Department, both posts offering opportunities to make money.

所以存在阜康的款子，总数不下百万之多，是胡雪岩最大的一个主顾。
His deposits with Fukang totaled no less than a million taels, making him Hu’s largest customer.


这个主顾的存款，要查他的来源如何？虽与胡雪岩无关，
An investigation into the source of this customer’s money had nothing to do with Hu himself.

但因此使得阜康的倒闭更成了大新闻，对他大为不利。
Yet it would make Fukang’s collapse an even greater sensation, which was very bad for him.

但这亦是无可奈何之事，
There was nothing he could do, however.

胡雪岩只有丢开它，细想全盘帐目交出以后的情形。
He put it aside and considered what would happen once he had handed over all the accounts.

帐都交了，清理亦无从清理起。不是吗？
With the books gone, there would be no way for him to conduct the liquidation himself—would there?

胡雪岩这样转着念头，突然精神一振，
As this thought took shape, his spirits suddenly lifted.

不可思议地、竟有一种无债一身轻之感。
Incredible as it seemed, he felt the lightness of a man freed from debt.

这道理是很明白的，交出全部帐目，等于交出全部财务，当然也就交出了全部债务，
The reasoning was plain: surrendering all the accounts meant surrendering all the finances, and therefore all the debts as well.

清理是公家的责任，
Liquidation would become the authorities’ responsibility.

当然，这在良心上还是有亏欠的，但事到如今也顾不得那许多了。
Of course, his conscience would still be burdened, but at this stage he could no longer attend to every consideration.

不过，胡雪岩还存着万一之想，那就是存在上海、天津的大批丝货，能够找到一条出路。来偿还全部债务；
He also retained one faint hope: that some outlet might be found for the great stocks of silk in Shanghai and Tianjin, enabling him to repay every debt.

这件事，虽托了古应春，但他的号召力不够，
He had entrusted the matter to Gu, but Gu lacked sufficient influence.

必得自己到上海，在古应春协助之下，才有希望。
There would be hope only if Hu went to Shanghai himself and worked with Gu’s assistance.

照这个想法来说，他交出全部帐目，债务由公家来替他抵挡一阵，等于获得一段喘息的时间，得以全力在丝货上作一番挣扎。
Seen in that light, handing over the accounts would let the authorities hold off the creditors for a time, giving him breathing space to concentrate all his efforts on the silk.

这样一想，他的多日来的忧烦与萎靡，消失了一半，
Half his recent anxiety and dejection vanished.

级着鞋，悄悄到房里去找螺蛳太太。
Slipping his feet into his shoes, he quietly went to Madam Luosi’s bedroom.


她也忙了半夜，入睡不过一个多时辰。
She too had worked late into the night and had slept for only a little over two hours.

胡雪岩揭开皮帐子，一股暖香，直扑鼻观，
Hu lifted the fur bed curtains, and a warm fragrance met him.

螺蛳太太鼻息微微，睡得正酣，
Madam Luosi was sound asleep, breathing softly.

胡雪岩不忍惊醒她，轻轻揭开丝棉被，侧身睡下，
Unwilling to disturb her, he gently raised the silk-wadded quilt and lay down on his side.

不道惊醒了螺蛳太大，一翻身朝里，口中说道：“你真是不晓得死活，
But she woke, turned inward, and said, “You really have no idea what trouble you’re in!

这里候还有心思来缠我。”
At a time like this, you still have a mind to pester me.”

胡雪岩知道她误会了，忍不住好笑，
Realizing her mistake, Hu could not help smiling.

而且心境不同，也比较有兴来开玩笑了，
His changed mood even gave him some appetite for a joke.

便扳着螺蛳太太的依旧圆润温软的肩头说：“这就叫吃着黄连弹琴，苦中作乐。”
Taking hold of her shoulder, still rounded, soft, and warm, he said, “It’s like playing music while chewing bitter coptis: finding pleasure in suffering.”

“去！去！
“Go away!

哪个同你作乐？”
Who wants to find pleasure with you?”

话虽如此，身子却回过来了，而且握住了胡雪岩的手。
Yet she turned back and took his hand.

“我刚刚想了一想。”胡雪岩开始谈正事，
“I’ve just been thinking,” Hu said, coming to business.

“我见了刘中丞，请他替我一肩担待。
“When I see Governor Liu, I’ll ask him to take the whole burden on his shoulders.

我正好脱室身体到上海去想办法。
That will leave me free to go to Shanghai and try to find a solution.

你看我这个盘算怎么样？”
What do you think?”

听得这话螺蛳太太睁开双眼，坐起身来，
At this, Madam Luosi opened her eyes and sat up.

顺手将里床的一件皮袄披在身上，抱着双膝，细细恩量。
She put on a fur jacket lying on the inner side of the bed, clasped her knees, and considered carefully.

“他肯不肯替你担待呢？”
“Will he be willing to take it on for you?”

“不肯也要肯。”
“He’ll have to.

胡雪岩说：“交帐就是交产，
Handing over the books means handing over the property.

原封不动捧出去，请他看了办。”
I’ll surrender it intact and ask him to deal with it as he sees fit.”

“你说交产？”
“The property?

螺蛳太太问：“我们连安身之处都没有了。”
Then we won’t even have anywhere to live.”

“那当然不是。”
“Of course we will.

胡雪岩说：“我跟你来商量的，就是要弄个界限出来。”
What I’ve come to discuss is where to draw the line.”


“这个界限在哪里？”
“Where is the line?”

“在……”胡雪岩说：“在看这样东西，是不是居家过日子少不了的，
“It depends…on whether something is indispensable to living our everyday lives.

如果是，可以留下来，
If it is, we can keep it.

不然就是财产，要开帐，要交出去。”
Otherwise it is property, and must be listed and handed over.”

“这哪里有一定的界限，
“How can there be a fixed line?

有的人情茶淡饭，吃得蛮好，有的没有肉吃不下饭。
Some people are perfectly happy with plain tea and simple food; others can’t eat a meal without meat.

你说，怎么来分？”
How do you distinguish between them?”

“当然这里的伸缩性，也蛮大的。”
“Of course, there is considerable room for interpretation.”

螺蛳太太沉吟不语。
Madam Luosi considered this in silence.

她原来总以为只是胡雪岩的事业要交出去，私财除了金块、金条、金叶子以及现银以外，其他都能不动。
She had always imagined that only Hu’s businesses would be surrendered; among their private possessions, only gold ingots, bars, and leaf, together with ready silver, would be touched.

照现在看，跟抄家也差不多了。
Now it seemed little different from having their home confiscated.

一想到“抄家”，心里发酸，不过她也是刚强明达一路人，
The thought made her heart ache, but she was strong-minded and clear-sighted.

仍能强忍住眼泪想正经。
Holding back her tears, she tried to consider the practical questions.

只是想来想去，想不出一个头绪来，
Yet she could find no starting point.

因为细软摆饰、动用家具、一切日常什物，诚如胡雪岩所说的伸缩性很大，似乎每一样东西都必须评估一番，才能区分。
Jewelry and fine possessions, ornaments, furniture, everyday utensils—each item seemed to require a separate assessment before it could be classified. As Hu had said, the scope for interpretation was enormous.

“这样一片家业，哪里是即时之刻，开得出帐目来的？”螺蛳太太说：
“How could an estate like this be inventoried in an instant?” she asked.

“我看只有两个办法，
“I can see only two ways.

一是同刘抚台声明，私财的帐目太琐碎，一时没法子开得周全，一个是只开大数，自己估个价，譬如说红木家具几堂，大毛皮统子多少件，每一项下面估个总数。”
Either tell Governor Liu that the private property is too complicated to list completely in such a short time, or give only broad totals with our own valuations: so many sets of hongmu furniture, so many linings of heavy fur, and an estimated total under each heading.”

“我看照第二个办法比较好。”
“The second seems better.”

“不过，估价也很难，
“But valuation is difficult too.

譬如说我们的住身房子，
Take this house we live in.

你倒估估看。”
What would you put on it?”

“这只有把造价开上去。
“We can only enter the cost of construction.

数目也好看些。”
That will make the figures look better.”

为了求帐面好看，不但房子照造价开，其他一切亦都照买进的价钱开列。
To improve the appearance of the accounts, they listed not merely the house at its building cost, but everything else at its purchase price.


第二天又忙了大半天，
They spent most of the following day at work again.

诸事齐备，胡雪岩去看德馨，约期晋见巡抚刘秉璋。
When everything was ready, Hu went to Dexin to arrange an audience with Governor Liu Bingzhang.

“最好是在今天晚上。”他说，
“Tonight would be best,” he said.

“这不是啥有面子的事，
“It’s hardly something to be proud of.

最好少见人，
The fewer people I meet, the better.

而且，晚上可以穿便衣。”
Besides, I can wear informal clothes at night.”

“我看不必，
“I don’t think that is necessary.

这是很光明磊落的事，没有什么见不得人。
This is an entirely honorable proceeding, nothing to hide.

而且，刘中丞是翰袜出身，很讲究这些过节，
Governor Liu comes from the Hanlin Academy and is particular about such conventions.

晚上谈这件事，倒仿佛私相授受似的，他一定不愿意。
Discussing it at night would make it look like a private bargain, which he certainly would not want.

准定明天上午上院吧。”
Let us fix the audience for tomorrow morning.”

“是。好！”
“Yes, very well.”

胡雪岩只得答应。
Hu could only agree.

“穿便衣也不必。
“And there’s no need for informal clothes.

倒象有了什么罪过，青衣小帽负罪辕门似的。
That would make you look like a guilty man arriving at the yamen gate in a plain gown and little cap to confess.

不过，雪岩，你的服饰也不必太华丽。”
Still, Xueyan, you need not dress too splendidly.”

这是暗示，红顶花翎都不必戴。
The hint was that he should leave off the red finial and peacock feather.

胡雪岩当然会意，
Hu understood.

第二天循规蹈矩，只按道员三品眼色穿戴整齐，带着从人上轿到佑圣观巷巡抚衙门。
The next day, observing all the rules, he dressed simply according to the third-rank attire of a circuit intendant and set off by sedan, accompanied by his attendants, for the governor’s yamen in Youshengguan Lane.


其时德馨已先派了人在接应，
Dexin had already sent someone ahead to receive him.

手本一递进去，刘秉璋即时在西花厅延见，
As soon as Hu’s visiting card was presented, Liu Bingzhang admitted him to the western reception hall.

胡雪岩照官场规矩行了礼，刘秉璋很客气地请他“升炕”，
Hu made the proper official salute, and Liu courteously invited him onto the kang.

平时他来看刘秉漳，本是在炕床上并坐的，但这天却再三谦辞，
On ordinary visits they had sat together there, but today Hu repeatedly declined.

因为回头德馨要来，如果他升了炕，德馨只能坐在东面椅子上，未免委屈，
Dexin would be coming shortly; if Hu occupied the kang, Dexin could only take the chair to the east, which would slight him.

所以他只坐在西面椅子上，留着上首的位子给德馨。
Hu therefore sat in the western chair, leaving the place of honor for Dexin.

此时此地，当然不必寒暄，
At such a time there was no need for small talk.

胡雪岩开门见山他说：
Hu came straight to the point.

“职道没有想到今天。
“I never imagined I should reach this position, Your Excellency.

公私债务，无从料理，要请大人成全。
I cannot settle my public or private debts and must ask your help.”

““言重，言重！”刘秉璋说：
“Please, there is no need to put it so strongly.

“如今时局艰难，
These are difficult times.

一切总以维持市面，安定人心为主。
Our first concern must be to sustain trade and reassure the public.

在这个宗旨之下，如果有可为雪翁略效绵薄之处，亦是我分内之事。”
Whatever small service I can render you within that purpose, Mr Xueyan, is part of my duty.”

谈到这里，花厅外面有人高唱：“德大人到。”
Just then a voice outside announced, “His Excellency De!”

于是刘秉璋站了起来，而胡雪岩则到门口相迎。
Liu rose, while Hu went to the doorway to meet him.

听差打开门帘，
An attendant lifted the curtain.

德馨人内，先向刘秉璋行了礼，然后转身道：
Dexin entered, first saluted Liu, then turned to Hu.

“雪翁，你请这面坐！”
“Mr Xueyan, please sit over here.”

说着，他占了胡雪岩原来的位置，将上首留给胡雪岩。
He took Hu’s former seat, leaving the place of honor to him.

“不，不！
“No, no!

晓翁请上坐。”
That is your place, Mr Xiaofeng.”

两人辞让了好一会，刘秉璋忍不住发话：
They exchanged courtesies for some time before Liu intervened.

“细节上不必争了。
“No need to dispute over details.

雪翁就坐在这面，
Sit on this side, Mr Xueyan.

说话比较方便。”
It will be easier to talk.”

听得这话，胡雪岩方始在靠迎刘秉璋的东首椅子上坐了下来，向对面的德馨问道：“我帐国已经带来了，
Hu finally took the eastern chair, nearer Liu, and asked Dexin opposite him, “I have brought the accounts.

是否现在就呈上刘大人？”
Shall I present them to His Excellency now?”

“是，是，
“Yes, yes.

我看现在就上呈吧！”
I think you should.”


胡雪岩便起身将置在一旁的一厚叠帐簿，双手捧起，送上炕床，
Hu rose, lifted the thick stack of account books placed beside him with both hands, and carried them to the kang.

德馨也站起来帮着点交。
Dexin stood too and helped check them as they were handed over.

帐傅一共六本，
There were six books in all.

第一本是阜康钱庄连各地分号的总帐，
The first was the general ledger of Fukang Bank and all its branches.

第二本是二十九家当铺的档手及架本数目清帐，
The second listed the managers and capital of the twenty-nine pawnshops.

第三本是所有田地一万一千亩，座落的地点及田地等则的细帐，
The third gave the locations and grades of all eleven thousand mu of land.

第四本是丝茧存货数量地点的清册，
The fourth inventoried the quantities and locations of the silk and cocoons in stock.

第四本是杂项财产，包括胡庆余堂药店在内的目录，
The fourth listed miscellaneous property, including Hu Qingyu Hall.

第五本是私人财产清单，第六本便是存户名册。
The fifth was an inventory of personal possessions, and the sixth a register of depositors.

但各钱庄所开出的银票，列在第一本之内。
Notes issued by the various banks, however, appeared in the first book.

刘秉璋只略翻一翻，便即搁下，
Liu merely leafed through them and put them down.

等胡雪岩与德馨归座以后，他才问道：“雪翁这六本帐的收支总数如何？”
Once Hu and Dexin had resumed their seats, he asked, “What is the overall balance of these six books?”

“照帐面上来说，收支相抵，绰绰有余，
“On paper, there is far more coming to me than I owe.

不过欠人是实数，人欠就很难说了。”
But what I owe is a definite sum; what is due to me is much less certain.”

“所谓‘人欠’；包括货色在内。”德馨补充着说：
“‘What is due’ includes the goods,” Dexin explained.

“雪翁的丝茧，因为跟洋人斗法的缘故，将来只怕必须出之以‘拍卖，一途，
“Because of his struggle with the foreign merchants, Mr Xueyan may eventually have to sell his silk and cocoons by auction.

能收回多少成本就很难说了。”
How much of their cost he can recover is difficult to say.”


“何谓‘拍卖’？”
“What is an auction?”

“这是外国人的规矩。”胡雪岩说：
“A foreign practice,” Hu replied.

“有意者彼此竞价。
“Those interested bid against one another.

由底价叫起，
The bidding starts at a reserve price.

只要有两个人出价，就一路往上叫，
So long as two people keep offering more, the price rises.

叫到没有人竟价，主持人拍一拍‘惊堂木，，就算敲定了。”
When nobody bids further, the person presiding strikes a block of wood, like a magistrate’s desk block, and the sale is settled.”

“这样说，洋人可以勾通好，故意不竞价。”
“Then the foreigners could agree among themselves and deliberately refrain from competing.”

“不但故意不竟价，甚至不出价，
“They might not merely refuse to compete; they might refuse to bid at all.

那一来就只好把底价再往下压。”
Then the reserve would have to be lowered.”

“照此而言，雪翁的丝茧值多少银子，根本无从估计？”
“In that case, there is no way to estimate what your silk and cocoons are worth?”

“是！”
“Exactly.”

“难。”
“Difficult.”

刘秉璋转脸问道：
Liu turned to Dexin.

“晓翁看，应该如何处理？”
“How do you think we should proceed, Mr Xiaofeng?”

“只有先公后私，一步一步清理。”
“Settle the public claims before the private ones, step by step.”

“也只好如此。”
“There seems no alternative.

刘秉璋说：“现在朝廷的意思还不知道，
We do not yet know the court’s intentions.

我亦暂时只能在‘保管’二字上尽力。”他又问道：“雪翁，一时不会离开杭州吧？”
For now, I can only do my best to hold everything in safekeeping.” He added, “You won’t be leaving Hangzhou for the time being, will you?”


这句话问出来的暗含着有监视他的行踪的意味在内，
The question carried an implication that Hu’s movements were to be watched.
