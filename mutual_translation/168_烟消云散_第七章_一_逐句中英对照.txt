胡雪岩
《烟消云散》第七章（一）
Vanishing into Thin Air — Chapter Seven, Part One
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第七章 大封典铺｜CHAPTER SEVEN: THE MASS SEQUESTRATION OF THE PAWNSHOPS


杨书办惠了帐，带着马逢时穿过两条街，进入一条曲曲折折的小巷，
Clerk Yang paid the bill and led Ma Fengshi across two streets into a winding lane.

在巷底有一家人家，双扉紧闭，
At its end stood a house with both doors shut.

但门旁有一盏油灯，微弱的光焰，照出一张退了色的梅红笺，上写“孙寓”二字。
An oil lamp beside the entrance cast a feeble light on a faded rose-red slip of paper bearing the words “Sun Residence.”

“这是什么地方？”马逢时有些不安地问。
“What is this place?” Ma asked uneasily.

“马……”杨书办赶紧顿住，
“Ma…” Yang checked himself.

“老李，这个地方你不能告诉李大嫂。”
“Old Li, you must not tell Mrs Li about this place.”

一听这话，马逢时不再作声，
Ma said no more.

只见杨书办举手敲门，三急三缓，
Yang knocked three times quickly and three times slowly.

刚刚敲完，大门呀地一声开了，
As he finished, the door creaked open.

一个半老徐娘，高举着“手照”说：
A middle-aged woman, still attractive, held up a hand lantern.

“我道哪个，
“I wondered who it was.

是你。
You!

算算你也应该来了。”
I thought it was about time you came.”

接着，脸上浮满了笑容又问：
Her face filled with smiles.

“这位是……”
“And this gentleman is…?”

“李老板。”杨书办紧接着问：“楼上有没有客人？”
“Mr Li,” Yang answered, then asked at once, “Any guests upstairs?”

“没有。”
“No.”

“楼下呢？”
“Downstairs?”

“庆余堂的老朱同朋友在那里吃酒，
“Old Zhu from Qingyu Hall is drinking with some friends.

就要走的。”
They will be leaving soon.”

“他们东家遭难，他倒还有心思吃花酒。”杨书办又说：
“His employer is in trouble, yet he still has a mind for courtesan parties!

“你不要说我在这里。”
Do not tell him I am here.”

“多关照的。”
“As if I needed reminding!”

那半老徐娘招呼“李老板”说：
The woman turned to “Mr Li.”

“请你跟我来。
“Come with me, please.

走好！”
Mind your step.”

于是一行三人，由堂屋侧面的楼梯上楼，
The three of them went up the stairs beside the main room.

楼上一大两小三个房间，
Upstairs were three rooms, one large and two small.

到了当中大房间，等主人剔亮了灯，杨书办方为马逢时引见。
In the central room, once their hostess had brightened the lamp, Yang introduced her.

“她姓孙。
“Her surname is Sun.

你叫她孙干娘好了。”
You can call her Auntie Sun.”

马逢时已经了然，这里是杭州人所说的“私门头”，而孙干娘便是鸨儿，
Ma now understood: this was what Hangzhou people called a private brothel, and Auntie Sun was its madam.

当即笑嘻嘻地说道：“孙子娘的子女儿一定很多？”
Smiling broadly, he said, “You must have a great many daughters, Auntie Sun?”

“有，有。”
“Indeed I do.”

孙子娘转脸问杨书办：
She turned to Yang.

“先吃茶还是先吃酒？”
“Tea first, or wine?”

“茶也要，酒也要，还要吃饭。”
“Both—and we shall want a meal as well.”

说着，杨书办拉着孙干娘到外房，过了好一会才进来。
He drew her into the outer room and returned some time later.


“这个孙干娘，倒是徐娘半老，丰韵犹存。”马逢时说道。
“Auntie Sun may be past her first youth, but she still has plenty of charm,” Ma remarked.

“怎么？
“What?

你倒看中她了！
Have you taken a fancy to her?

我来做媒。”
I shall be your matchmaker.”

“算了，算了！
“Enough of that!

我们先谈正事。”
Let us discuss our business first.”

这话正好符合杨书办的安排，
This suited Yang’s arrangements perfectly.

他已关照好孙干娘备酒备饭，要讲究，但不妨慢慢来，公便跟马逢时先谈妥了明日之事，再开怀畅饮。
He had instructed Auntie Sun to prepare good wine and a good meal, but to take her time, so he could settle tomorrow’s business with Ma before they drank freely.

“你的事归我来接下半段。
“Leave the rest of your business to me.

我先问你，你年底有多少帐？”
First, how much do you owe at the year’s end?”

马逢时一愣，
Ma was taken aback.

约莫估计了一下说：“总要五六十两银子才能过关。”
After a rough calculation, he said, “I shall need fifty or sixty taels to get through it.”

“我晓得了。”杨书办说：
“I understand.

“明天我陪了你去，
I shall go with you tomorrow.

到了公济典，你看我的眼色行事。”
Once we reach Gongji Pawnshop, take your cue from my eyes.”

何谓看眼色行事？
What exactly did that mean?

马逢时在心里好好想了一会问道：
Ma pondered it for a while.

“杨大哥……”
“Brother Yang…”

“慢点，慢点。”杨书办硬截断了他的话，
“Wait!” Yang cut him short.

“明天在公济典，你可不能这样叫我。”
“You must not address me like that at Gongji tomorrow.”

“我明白。
“I understand.

做此官，行此札，
When holding an office, observe its forms.

到那时候，我自然会官派十足地叫你杨书办，
I shall call you ‘Clerk Yang’ in my grandest official manner.

你可不要生气。”
Do not take offence.”

“不会，不会。
“Of course not.

这不过是唱出戏而已。”
We are only putting on a performance.”

“这出戏你是主角。”马逢时问：
“You are the leading player.

“你认识不认识唐子韶。”
Do you know Tang Zishao?”

“怎么不认识，不过没有什么交情。”
“Naturally, though we are not close.”

“你认识最好，
“Good.

我想明天我做红脸，你做白脸，
Tomorrow I shall play the stern red face and you the kindly white.

遇见有不对的地方，我打官腔，你来转圜，
If anything is wrong, I shall speak with official severity and you can smooth matters over.

唐子韶当然就要找上你了，
Tang will naturally turn to you.

什么事可以马虎，什么事不能马虎，我都听你的语气来办。
Whether something can be overlooked or must not be, I shall follow the tone you take.”

““一点不错。”杨书办很欣慰他说，
“Exactly!” Yang said with satisfaction.

“我们好好儿来唱他一出‘得胜回朝’。”
“We shall give a fine performance of Returning to Court in Triumph.”


谈到这里，楼梯上有响声，
Footsteps sounded on the stairs.

只见帘启处，孙干娘在前，后面跟着女佣，手中端一个大托盘，四样酒菜，两副杯筷。
The curtain lifted, and Auntie Sun entered, followed by a maid carrying a large tray with four dishes and two sets of cups and chopsticks.

“怎么只有两副？”杨书办问。
“Why only two?” Yang asked.

“我怕你们要谈事情，不要旁人来打搅。
“I thought you had business to discuss and would not want to be disturbed.”

“谈好了，
“We have finished.

再去添两副来。”杨书办问：
Bring two more sets.

“巧珍在不在？”
Is Qiaozhen here?”

“今天没有来。”孙干娘说：
“She has not come today.

“阿兰在这里，
A’lan is here, though.

不晓得李老板看得中，看不中？”
I do not know whether Mr Li would care for her.”

杨书办心中一动，因为看到马逢时目不转睛地看着孙干娘，决心成全他们这一段露水姻缘，
Noticing that Ma could not take his eyes off Auntie Sun, Yang decided to bring about a brief liaison between them.

当即说道：“等一等再说。
“We shall see later,” he said.

你先陪我们吃两杯。”
“Have a couple of cups with us first.”

于是又去添了杯筷来，
More cups and chopsticks were brought.

孙干娘为客人布菜斟酒，颇为周到，马逢时不住地夸赞酒好菜好，
Auntie Sun served the guests attentively, and Ma kept praising the wine and food.

杨书办只是微笑不语。
Yang merely smiled.

看看是时候了，他问：“庆余堂的老朱还没有走吧？”
When he thought the time was right, he asked, “Old Zhu from Qingyu Hall has not left yet?”

“还没有？”
“Not yet.”

“我下楼去看一看他。”
“I shall go down and see him.”

杨书办站起身来，对孙干娘说：“你陪李老板多吃几杯，
Rising, Yang told Auntie Sun, “Keep Mr Li company over a few more cups.

我的好朋友，
He is a good friend of mine.

你要另眼相看。”
You must treat him especially well.”

于是杨书办扬长下楼，叫相帮进去通知，
He went downstairs and had an attendant announce him.

床余堂的老朱，满脸通红地迎了出来，
Old Zhu came out, his face flushed scarlet.

“老杨，老杨！”
“Yang!

他拉着他的手说：“请进来吃酒。”
Come in for a drink!” he said, taking his hand.

“方便不方便。”
“Would it be convenient?”

“方便，方便。
“Certainly.

不是你的熟人，就是我的熟人。”
Everyone is either a friend of yours or a friend of mine.”

进去一看，四个人中只有一个不认识，
Inside, Yang knew three of the four men.

请教姓名，才知道是老朱的同事。
On asking the fourth man’s name, he learned that he was Zhu’s colleague.

杨书办之来闯席，一则是故意避开，好让马逢时有跟孙千娘勾搭的机会，再则便是打听庆余堂的情形，
Yang had joined the party partly to leave Ma an opportunity to make advances to Auntie Sun and partly to find out how Qingyu Hall was faring.

尤其使他困惑而又好奇的是，胡雪岩的全盘事业，都在风雨飘摇之中，何以老朱竟还兴高采烈地在这里寻欢作乐。
What especially puzzled him was how Zhu could be enjoying himself so heartily when all Hu Xueyan’s businesses were in peril.


席间一一应酬过了，一巡酒下来有人提起阜康的风波，
After the greetings and a round of drinks, someone mentioned the crisis at Fukang.

这是最近轰动南北的大新闻，凡是应酬场中，几乎无一处不资以为谈助。
It was the great news of the day throughout the country, discussed at almost every social gathering.

杨书办只是静静地听着，等到谈得告一段落时，他开口了。
Yang listened quietly until there was a pause.

“老朱，你在庆余堂是啥职司？”
“Zhu, what exactly is your job at Qingyu Hall?”

“我管查验。”
“I handle inspections.”

“查验？”杨书办问：“查验点啥？
“Inspections of what?

查验货色？
The quality of the goods?

你又不是药材行出身，
You did not start in the medicinal-herb trade.

药材‘路脚’正不正，你又不懂。”
You cannot tell whether the ingredients come from the proper sources.”

“货色好坏不懂，斤两多少还不会看？
“I may not know their quality, but I can read a weight!

等看货的老先生说药材地道，过秤时就要请我了。”
Once the experienced men pronounce the herbs genuine, they call me to supervise the weighing.

老朱又说：“不过，我顶重要的一项职司，是防备货色偷漏。”
My most important duty, though, is preventing goods from being stolen or spirited away.”

“有没有抓到过。”
“Have you ever caught anyone?”

“当然抓到过，不过不多。”
“Of course, but not many.”

“你说不多；
“You say not many.

只怕已经偷漏了的，你不晓得。
Perhaps goods have disappeared without your knowing.”

“不会。”老朱停了一下说：
“No.” Zhu paused.

“老实说，你就叫人偷漏，你们也不肯。
“To be frank, even if you urged the men to steal, they would refuse.

你倒想，饭碗虽不是金的、银的，至少也是铁的，一生一世敲不破；
Think of it: their rice bowls may not be gold or silver, but they are at least iron, unbreakable for a lifetime.

工钱之外有花红，
Besides wages, there are bonuses.

遇到夏天有时疫流行，上门的主顾排长龙等药，另外有津贴。
When summer epidemics bring customers queuing for medicines, there is an extra allowance.

再说家里大人、小伢儿有病痛，用药不管丸散膏丹，再贵重的都是白拿，
If an adult or child at home falls ill, medicines—pills, powders, pastes or pellets—are free, however costly.

至于膏滋药、药酒，收是收钱，不过比成本还要低。
They do pay for tonic pastes and medicinal wines, but at less than cost.

如果贪便宜，偷了一两支人参，这些好处都没有了，
Steal a root or two of ginseng out of greed, and all those benefits vanish.

你想划得来，划不来？“
Would that be worth it?”


“你的话是不错，
“You have a point.

不过这回恐怕要连根铲了！”
But this time the whole thing may be uprooted!”

“你是说胡大先生的生意怕会不保？
“You mean Mr Hu’s businesses may not survive?

别的难说，庆余堂一定保得住。”
I cannot speak for the others, but Qingyu Hall is certain to remain.”

“为啥？”
“Why?”

“有保障。”老朱从从容容地说：
“It has protection,” Zhu replied calmly.

“这回阜康的事情出来，我们的档手同大家说：胡大先生办得顶好的事业，就是我们庆余堂。
“When the trouble at Fukang began, our manager told us that Qingyu Hall was the best of all Mr Hu’s enterprises.

不但挣钱，还替胡大先生挣了名声。
It earns money and has earned him a reputation too.

如果说亏空公款，要拿庆余堂封了抵债，
Suppose they say there is a shortage of public funds and seal Qingyu Hall to cover the debt.

货色生财，都可以入官，庆余堂这块招脾拿不出去的。
They can confiscate the stock and equipment, but they cannot take away its signboard.

庆余堂是简称，
Qingyu Hall is only the short name.

正式的招牌是胡庆余堂，
The full name is Hu Qingyu Hall.

如果老板不姓胡了，怎么还好用庆余堂的招牌。
If the owner is no longer a Hu, how can they keep using that sign?

所以官府一定不会封庆余堂，
So the authorities are certain not to seal it.

仍旧让胡大先生来当老板。
They will let Mr Hu remain the owner.

大家要格外巴结，抓药要道地，对待客人要和气，
We must all work especially hard, dispense genuine medicines and treat customers courteously.

这只饭碗一定捧得实，
Then our rice bowls will be secure.

不必担心。”
There is no need to worry.”


听到这里，杨书办心中浮起浓重的感慨，
Yang was deeply moved by what he heard.

胡雪岩有如此大的事业，培植了不知道多少人材，是可想而知的事，
With enterprises on so vast a scale, Hu must have trained more capable men than anyone could count.

但培植人材之始。如果只是为他自己找个不问手段，只要能替他嫌钱的帮手，结果不是宓本常，就是唐子韶，
But if, at the beginning, he sought only assistants who would make money for him by whatever means, the result would be a Mi Benchang or a Tang Zishao.

因为水涨船高，“徒弟”升伙计，伙计升档手，
As the water rose, so did the boat: an apprentice became an employee, an employee became a manager.

这时候的档手心里就会想：“你做老板，
Then the manager would think, “How did you become the proprietor?

还不是靠我做徒弟的时候，洗尿壶、烫水烟袋，
When I was an apprentice, I emptied your chamber pot and rinsed your water pipe with hot water.

一步一步抬你起来的？
I raised you up step by step.

伙计做到啥时候？
How long must I remain an employee?

我要做老板了。”
I want to be a proprietor myself.”

一动到这个念头，档手就不是档手了，
Once that thought took hold, he ceased to be a true manager.

第一步是“做小货”，
First came private trading on the side.

有好生意，自己来做，
If a profitable transaction presented itself, he took it for himself.

譬如有人上门求售一批货色，明知必赚，却多方挑剔，最后明点暗示，到某处去接头，有成交之望，
Someone might offer a consignment he knew would make money; he would find fault with it in every way, then openly or indirectly suggest another place where a deal might be struck.

其实指点之处就是他私下所设的号子。
That place would be his own secret business.

其次是留意人材，伙计、徒弟中看中了的，私下刻意笼络，一旦能成局面，不愁没有班底，
Next, he would watch for talent, privately cultivating promising employees and apprentices so that, when he could establish himself, he would have his staff ready.

最后是拉拢客户，
Finally came drawing away customers.

其道孔多，但要拉拢客户，一定不会说原来的东家的好话，是一定的道理，
There were many methods, but one thing was certain: to win them over, he would never speak well of his present employer.

否则客户不会“跳槽”。
Otherwise, why would they change firms?

因此，只要有了私心重的档手，一到动了自立门户的念头，就必然损人以利己，侵蚀到东家的利益，
A selfish manager who decided to set up on his own would inevitably profit at his employer’s expense.

即令是东家所一手培植出来的，亦不会觉得自己忘恩负义，
Even if the employer had trained him from the start, he would feel no ingratitude.

因为他替东家赚过钱，自以为已经报答过了。
He had made money for the man and believed that debt was already paid.


庆余堂的档手能够如此通达诚恳，尽力维持庆余堂这块金字招牌，为胡雪岩保住一片事业，这原因是可想而知的，
The reason Qingyu Hall’s manager could be so sincere and understanding, doing everything to preserve its reputation and one part of Hu’s business, was not difficult to imagine.

胡雪岩当初创办庆余堂，虽起于西征将士所需要成药及药材，数量极大，
Hu had founded the medicine shop because the troops on the western campaign required enormous quantities of prepared medicines and herbs.

向外采购不但费用甚巨，而且亦不见得能够及时供应，
Buying them elsewhere was expensive and did not always ensure timely delivery.

他既负责后路粮台，当然要精打细算，
Responsible for the supply administration behind the army, he had to calculate carefully.

自己办一家大药店，有省费、省事、方便三项好处，
A large shop of his own would save money, save trouble and make supply easier.

并没有打算赚钱，
He had not intended to earn a profit.

后来因为药材地道、成药灵验、营业鼎盛，大力赚钱。
Later, genuine ingredients and effective preparations brought flourishing trade and substantial earnings.

但盈余除了转为资本，扩大规模以外，平时对贫民施药施医，历次水旱灾荒、时疫流行，捐出大批成药，亦全由盈余上开支，
But apart from profits reinvested to expand the business, the surplus paid for free medicines and treatment for the poor, and for large donations of prepared remedies during floods, droughts, famines and epidemics.

胡雪岩从来没有用过庆余堂的一文钱。
Hu had never taken a single cash from Qingyu Hall for his own use.


由于当初存心大公无私，物色档手的眼光，当然就不同了，
Because his original purpose was entirely public-spirited, he had sought a different sort of manager.

第一要诚实，
Honesty came first.

庆余堂一进门，供顾客等药休息之处，高悬一幅黑漆金字的对联：“修合虽无人见，存心自有天知。”
Just inside the shop, above the place where customers waited and rested, hung a black-lacquered couplet in gold: “Though none may see your medicines prepared, Heaven knows the intentions in your heart.”

因为不诚实的人卖药，尤其是卖成药，材料欠佳，分量不足，服用了会害人。
A dishonest medicine seller, especially one selling ready-made preparations, could harm people by using poor ingredients or insufficient quantities.

其次要心慈。
Compassion came next.

医家有割股之心，卖药亦是如此，
A physician must have the heart to sacrifice his own flesh for another; a medicine seller should be the same.

时时为病家着想，才能刻刻顾到药的品质。
Only by constantly thinking of the sick could he constantly attend to quality.

最后当然还要能干，否则诚实、心慈，反而成了易于受欺的弱点。
Finally, the man must be capable, or honesty and compassion might merely make him easy to deceive.

这样选中的一个档手，不必在意东家的利润，会全心全力去经营事业，
Such a manager, free from concern for the proprietor’s personal profit, would devote himself wholeheartedly to the business.

东家没有私心，也就引不起他的私心，加以待遇优厚，亦不必起什么私心。
An unselfish employer would not awaken selfishness in him; generous treatment removed any need for it.

庆余堂能不受阜康的影响，细细考查来龙去脉，自有种善因、得善果的颠扑不破之理在内。
Examined closely, Qingyu Hall’s escape from Fukang’s troubles exemplified the enduring truth that good causes bear good fruit.

念头转到这里，不由得对那连姓名都还不知道的余庆堂的档手，油然而起敬慕之心。
Yang felt a spontaneous admiration for its manager, whose name he did not even know.

于是在把杯闲谈之际，杨书办向老朱问起此人的生平，
Over their drinks he asked Zhu about the man’s history.

据说庆余堂的档手姓叶；当初是由胡雪岩的一个姓刘的亲戚去物色来的，
He learned that his surname was Ye and that a relative of Hu’s named Liu had originally found him.

“The New Year is coming.

好在还有以后再商量的话，

He would satisfy Yang first and consider the rest afterwards.
