胡雪岩
《烟消云散》第七章（二）
Vanishing into Thin Air — Chapter Seven, Part Two
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


第二天仍如前一天那样嘴上很客气，眼中不容情，
The next day the inspection continued as before: polite words, unsparing eyes.

将唐子韶的弊端，一样一样，追究到底。
Tang Zishao’s irregularities were pursued one by one to the end.

唐子韶的态度，却跟前一天有异，
His own manner, however, had changed.

仿佛对马逢时及杨书办的作为，不甚在意。只是坐在一边，不断地抽水烟，
He seemed scarcely concerned with what Ma Fengshi and Clerk Yang were doing, merely sitting to one side and smoking his water pipe.

有时将一根纸煤搓了又搓，直到搓断，方始有爽然若失的神情，显得他在肚子里的功夫，做得根深。
Sometimes he rolled a paper spill between his fingers again and again until it broke; only then did he look momentarily lost, revealing how deeply his mind was at work.

约莫刚交午时，公济开出点心来，请马逢时暂时休息。
Shortly after noon began, Gongji served refreshments and invited Ma to rest.

唐子韶便趁此时机，将杨书办邀到一边有话说。
Tang took the opportunity to draw Yang aside.

“杨先生，”他问：“今天查得完查不完？”
“Mr Yang, can you finish the inspection today?”

一想把它查完。
“We mean to try.”

““以后呢？”唐子韶问道：
“And afterward?

“不是说好商量？”
Didn’t you say we could discuss things?”

“不错，好商量。
“Certainly.

你最好去寻周少棠，
You had better find Zhou Shaotang.

只要他那里谈好了，马大老爷这里归我负责。”
Once you have settled matters with him, I’ll answer for His Honour Ma.”

唐子韶迟疑了好一会说：
Tang hesitated for some time.

“本来是谈好了，
“I thought we had settled things already.

哪晓得马大老爷一来，要从头查起。
Who could have known His Honour would come and insist on examining everything from the beginning?”

语气中仿佛在埋怨杨书办跟周少棠彼此串通，有意推来推去，不愿帮忙。
His tone suggested that Yang and Zhou were working together, deliberately passing him back and forth without helping.

杨书办心想，也难怪他误会，其中的关键，不妨点他一句。
Yang thought the misunderstanding natural enough; he might as well give him a hint.

“老兄，你不要一厢情愿！
“My dear fellow, don’t indulge in wishful thinking.

你这里查都还没有查过，无从谈起，更不必说啥谈好了。
Before we’ve even examined the place, there’s nothing to discuss, let alone settle.

你今天晚上去寻他，
Go and see him tonight.

包你有结果。
I guarantee you’ll get a result.”

“唐子韶恍然大悟，
Tang suddenly understood.

原来是要看他在公济典里弄了多少“好处”，然后再来谈“价钱”。
They wanted to see how much he had taken from Gongji before naming their price.

看样子打算用几千银子“摆平”，是一种不切实际的妄想。
Expecting to settle everything for a few thousand taels was an absurd dream.

“树倒猢狲散”，
When the tree fell, the monkeys scattered.

不如带着月如远走高飞，
Better to take Yueru and flee far away.

大不了从此不吃朝奉这一行的饭，后半世应可衣食无忧。
At worst, he would never work as a pawnbroker again; he should still have enough to live comfortably for the rest of his life.

就在这刹那间打定了主意，就更不在乎杨书办与马逢时了，不过表面上仍旧很尊敬，
The decision formed in an instant, leaving him even less concerned with Yang and Ma, though outwardly he remained respectful.

当天查帐完毕，要请他们吃饭。
When the day’s examination ended, he invited them to dinner.

马逢时当然坚辞，杨书办且又暗示，应该早早去觅周少棠“商量”。
Ma firmly declined, while Yang again hinted that he should find Zhou early and “discuss matters.”


唐子韶口头上边声称“是”，其实根本无此打算，
Tang repeatedly agreed, without the least intention of doing so.

他要紧的是赶回家去跟月如商量。
His urgent business was to get home and consult Yueru.

约略说了经过，随即向月如透露了他的决心。
After briefly describing what had happened, he revealed his decision.

“三十六计，走为上让。
“Of the Thirty-Six Stratagems, running away is the best.

你从现在起始，就要预备，
Start preparing now.

最好三、五天之内料理清楚，我们开溜，”
We should have everything ready in three to five days, then slip away.”

月如一愣，
Yueru stared.

“溜到哪里？”她说：
“Where to?

“徽州我是不去。”
I won’t go to Huizhou.”

唐子韶的结发妻子在徽州原籍，
Tang’s first wife remained in his native Huizhou.

要月如去服低做小，亲操井臼，宁死不愿，
Yueru would rather die than go there to take a subordinate place and do the household drudgery herself.

这一层意思表明过不止一次，唐子韶当然明白。
She had made that clear more than once, and he knew it perfectly.

“我怎么会让你到徽州去吃苦？
“How could I send you to suffer in Huizhou?

就算你自己要去，我也舍不得。
Even if you wanted to go, I couldn’t bear it.

我想有三个地方，一个是上海，一个是北京，再有一个是扬州，我在那里有两家亲戚。”
I have three places in mind: Shanghai, Beijing, or Yangzhou, where I have two sets of relatives.”

只要不让她到徽州，他处都不妨从长计议，
Provided Huizhou was excluded, the choice could be discussed at leisure.

但最好是能不走，
Better still, though, would be not to leave at all.

上生上长三十年，从没有出过远门，
Yueru had lived here all her thirty years and had never traveled far.

怕到了他乡水土不服住不惯。
She feared that elsewhere the climate and water would disagree with her and she would not settle.


“不走办不到，除非倾家荡产。”
“We can’t stay, unless we’re prepared to lose everything.”

“有这么厉害？”
“Is it as bad as that?”

“自然。”唐子韶答说：
“Of course.

“这姓周的，良心黑，手段辣，
This Zhou has a black heart and a ruthless hand.

如今一盘帐都抄了去，
They have copied all the accounts.

一笔一笔照算，没有五万银子不能过门。”
Once he adds them up item by item, it will take fifty thousand taels to get past him.”

“你不会赖掉？”
“Can’t you deny it?”

“把柄在人家千里，怎么赖得掉？”
“With the evidence in their hands?”

“不理他呢？”
“What if you ignore him?”

“不理他？你去试试看。”唐子韶说：
“Try it!

“姓马的是候补县，奉了宪谕来查封，
Ma is an expectant magistrate acting under his superior’s orders.

权力大得很呢！
He has enormous powers.

只要他说一句，马上可以送我到仁和县班房，
One word from him, and I’m in the lockup at Renhe.

你来送牢饭吧！”月如叹口气说：
You can bring me my meals there.” Yueru sighed.

“那就只好到上海去了。
“Then it will have to be Shanghai.

只怕到了上海还是保不得平安。”
But even there, we may not be safe.”

“一定可以保！”唐子韶信心十足地说，
“We certainly will!” Tang declared confidently.

“上海市场等于外国地方，
“The foreign settlement is practically foreign territory.

哪怕是道台也不能派差役去抓人，上海县更加不必谈了。
Even the circuit intendant can’t send runners in to arrest people, let alone the Shanghai magistrate.

而且上海市场上五方杂处，各式各样的人都有，
People come there from everywhere, every sort imaginable.

只要有钱，每天大摇大摆，坐马车、逛张园、吃大菜、看京戏，
So long as you have money, you can swagger about every day, ride in carriages, visit Zhang Garden, eat Western meals, and watch Beijing opera.

没有哪个来管你的闲事。”
Nobody will meddle in your affairs.”

听他形容上海的繁华，月如大为动心，
This picture of Shanghai’s pleasures thoroughly tempted Yueru.

满腔离愁，都丢在九霄云外，
All her sorrow at leaving vanished.

细细盘算了一会说道：“好在现款存在汇丰银行，
After careful thought, she said, “Fortunately, the cash is in HSBC.

细软随身带了走，有三天工夫总可以收拾好，
We can take our valuables with us and should be packed within three days.

不动产只好摆在那里再说。
The property we can’t move will have to stay for now.

不过，这三天当中，会不会出事呢？”
But might something happen during those three days?”


“当然要用缓兵之计。
“We must play for time.

杨书办要我今天晚上就去看周少棠。
Yang told me to see Zhou tonight.

他一定会开个价钱出来，
He’s bound to name a price.

漫天讨价，就地还钱，
He’ll ask the earth, and I’ll bargain him down.

一定谈不拢，
Naturally, we won’t agree.

我请他明天晚上来吃饭，你好好下点功夫……”
I’ll invite him to dinner tomorrow evening, and you must make a special effort…”

“又要来这一套了！”月如吼了起来，
“That trick again!” Yueru shouted.

“你当我什么人看。”
“What do you take me for?”

“我当你大慈大悲、救苦救难的观世音菩萨看。”唐子韶说：
“The compassionate Guanyin herself, saving those in distress.

“这姓周的请我吃空心汤圆，
This Zhou fed me an empty dumpling.

你要替我报仇。
You must avenge me.”

““报仇？哼，”月如冷笑，
“Avenge you?” She gave a cold laugh.

“我不来管你的事！
“I’ll have nothing to do with it.

你弄得不好‘赔了夫人又折兵’，我白白里又让人家占一回便宜，
You may end up losing the lady as well as the troops, and I’ll have let another man take advantage of me for nothing.

啥犯着？”
Why should I?”

“你真傻，
“Don’t be foolish.

你不会请他吃个空心汤圆？
Can’t you give him an empty dumpling too?

两三天一拖拖过去，我们人都到上海了，
Drag it out for two or three days, and we’ll be in Shanghai.

他到哪里去占你的便宜？”
How will he take advantage of you then?”

“万一，”月如问说：“万一他来个霸王硬上弓呢？”
“Suppose he forces himself on me?”

“你不会叫？
“Can’t you shout?

一叫，我会来救你。”
I’ll come and rescue you.”

“那不是变成仙人跳了？
“Then it becomes a blackmail set-up.

而且，你做初一，他做初二。
And what you do to him one day, he’ll repay the next.

看起来我一定要去送牢饭了。”
It looks as though I’ll be bringing you prison meals after all.”

唐子韶不作声。
Tang fell silent.

月如不是他的结发妻子，而且当初已经失过一回身，
Yueru was not his first wife, and she had already once slept with another man.

反正不是从一而终了，再让周少棠尝一回甜头，亦无所谓。
Since lifelong fidelity was no longer in question, he thought, it would hardly matter if Zhou enjoyed her favors too.

不过这话不便说得太露骨，只好点她一句。
But he could not say so crudely and could only hint.

“如果你不愿意送牢饭，实在说，你是不忍心我去吃牢饭，那么全在你发个善心了。”
“If you don’t want to bring meals to prison—if, in truth, you can’t bear to see me imprisoned—then everything depends on your showing a little compassion.”

月如亦不作声，
Yueru said nothing either.

不过把烧饭的老妈子唤了来，关照她明天要杀鸡，要多买菜。
She merely called the serving woman who did the cooking and told her to kill a chicken and buy extra food for the following day.


周少棠兴冲冲地到了元宝街，要看胡雪岩，不道一说来意，就碰了一个钉子。
Zhou went eagerly to Yuanbao Street to see Hu Xueyan, only to meet a rebuff as soon as he explained his purpose.

“说实话，周先生，”胡家的门上说：“生病是假，挡驾是真。
“To be frank, Mr Zhou,” the doorkeeper said, “illness is an excuse; the master is refusing visitors.

你老倒想想，我们老爷还有啥心思见客。
Just think—what mood can he be in to receive anyone?

我通报，一定去通报，不过，真的不见，你老也不要见怪。”
I’ll announce you, certainly, but if he won’t see you, please don’t take offense.”

“我是有正事同他谈。”
“I have serious business to discuss with him.”

“正事？”门上大摇其头，
“Business?” The man shook his head vigorously.

“那就一定见不着，
“Then you definitely won’t see him.

我们老爷一提起钱庄、当店、丝行，头就大了。”
Mention the bank, pawnshops, or silk business, and his head begins to ache.”

“那么，你说我来看看他。”
“Then say I’ve come to visit him.”

“也只好这样说。
“That’s all I can say.

不过，”
Still…”

门上一面起步，一面咕哝着，“我看是白说。”
As he started off, the doorkeeper muttered, “I doubt it will do any good.”

见此光景，周少棠的心冷了。
Zhou’s enthusiasm cooled.

默默盘算，自己想帮忙的意思到了，胡雪岩不见，是没法子的事。
He reflected silently that he had done his part in wishing to help; if Hu would not see him, nothing could be done.

唐子韶当然不能便宜他，
Tang must not be let off, of course.

不妨想想看，用什么手段卡住他的喉咙，让他把吞下去的东西吐出来：
He could think of some means of gripping the man by the throat and making him disgorge what he had swallowed.

过年了，
The New Year was coming.

施棉衣、施米、做做好事，也是阴功积德。
Distributing padded clothes and rice would do good and earn merit in the unseen world.

这一落入沉恩，就不觉得时光慢了，忽然听得一声：“周先生！”抬头看时，
Absorbed in these thoughts, he lost track of time, until a sudden “Mr Zhou!” made him look up.

是门上在他面前，
The doorkeeper stood before him.

“我们老爷有请，”
“The master asks you in.”

“喔，”周少棠定定神说：
“Oh!” Zhou collected himself.

“居然见我了？”
“So he will see me after all?”

“原来周先生是我们老爷四十年的老朋友。”门上赔笑说道：“我不晓得！
“I didn’t know you were his friend of forty years!” the man said with an apologetic smile.

周先生你不要见气。”
“Please don’t be offended.”

“哪里，哪里！
“Not at all.

你请领路。”
Lead the way.”


门上领到花园人口处，有个大丫头由一个老妈子陪着，转引客人直上百狮楼。
At the garden entrance the doorkeeper handed him over to a senior maid accompanied by an older serving woman, who led him straight to the Hundred Lions Tower.

“周先生走好！”一上楼便有个中年丽人在迎接，
“Mind your step, Mr Zhou!” A beautiful middle-aged woman greeted him upstairs.

周少棠见过一次，急忙拱手说：
He had met her once before and hastily clasped his hands.

“螺蛳太太，
“Madam Luosi!

不敢当，不敢当！”
You are too kind!”

“大先生在里头等你。”
“Mr Hu is waiting inside.”

说着螺蛳太太亲自揭开门帘，
She lifted the curtain herself.

周少棠是头一回到这里，
This was Zhou’s first visit to these rooms.

探头一望，目迷五色，东也是灯，西也是灯，东也是胡雪岩，西也是胡雪岩。
Looking in, he was dazzled: lamps to the east, lamps to the west; Hu Xueyan to the east, Hu Xueyan to the west.

灯可以有多少盏，胡雪岩不可能分身，
There might be any number of lamps, but Hu could not multiply himself.

周少棠警告自己，这里在镜子很多，不要象刘姥姥进了怕红院那样闹笑话。
He warned himself that there were many mirrors here: he must not make a fool of himself like Granny Liu entering Yihong Court.

因此，进门先站住脚，看清楚了再说。
He stopped just inside the doorway to get his bearings.

“少棠！”胡雪岩在喊：
“Shaotang!

“这面坐。”
Sit over here.”

循声觅人，只见胡雪岩坐在一张红丝绒的安乐椅上，上身穿的小对襟棉袄，下身围着一条花格子的毛毡，
Following the voice, he found Hu in a red-velvet armchair, wearing a short padded jacket buttoned down the front, with a checked woolen blanket around his lower body.

额头上扎一条寸许宽的缎带，大概是头痛的缘故。
A satin band about a cun wide was tied around his forehead, presumably for a headache.

“坐这里！”胡雪岩拍一拍他身旁的绣墩，指着头上笑道：
“Sit here.” Hu patted the embroidered stool beside him and pointed to his head with a smile.

“你看我这副样子，象不象产妇做月子？”
“Don’t I look like a woman resting after childbirth?”

这时候还有心思说笑话，周少棠心怀一宽，看样子他的境况，不如想象中那么坏。
If he could still joke, Zhou thought with relief, perhaps things were not as bad as he had imagined.


于是闲闲谈起查封公济典的事，原原本本、巨细靡遗，
He began casually describing the inspection of Gongji, recounting the whole affair without omitting the smallest detail.

最后谈到从唐子韶那里追出中饱的款子以后，如何分派的办法。
Finally he explained how the money was to be divided once recovered from Tang.

“算了，算了。”胡雪岩说：
“Let it go,” Hu said.

“不必认真。”
“There’s no need to pursue it so seriously.”

此言一出，周少棠愣住了，
Zhou stared.

好半天才说了句：“看起来，倒是我多事了，”
Only after a long silence could he say, “It seems I’ve been meddling unnecessarily.”

“少棠，你这样子一说，我变成半吊子了。
“Shaotang, that makes me sound a halfwit.

事到如今，我同你说老实话，
Let me tell you the truth.

我不是心甘情愿做洋盘瘟生，不分好歹，不识是非，
I’m not willingly playing the gullible fool, unable to distinguish good from bad or right from wrong.

我是为了另外一个人。”
I’m thinking of someone else.”

“为了哪一个？”周少棠当然要追问。
“Who?” Zhou naturally asked.

“唐子韶的姨太太。”
“Tang’s concubine.”

“喔，喔！”周少棠恍然大悟，
“Ah!” Zhou understood at once.

他亦久知胡雪岩有此一段艳闻，此刻正好求证：
He had long known of Hu’s amorous adventure and now had a chance to confirm it.

“我听说，唐子韶设美人局，你上了他的当？”
“I heard Tang laid a beauty trap and you fell into it?”

“也不算上当，
“It wasn’t exactly that.

是我一时糊涂。
I lost my head for a moment.

这话也不必去说它了。”
There’s no need to discuss it now.”

胡雪岩紧接着说：“昨天我同我的几个妾说：我放你们一条生路，
Hu went on, “Yesterday I told my concubines: ‘I’m giving you a way out.

愿意走的自己房间里东西都带走，我另外送五千银子。
Any of you who wish to leave may take everything in your rooms, and I’ll give you five thousand taels besides.’

想想月如总同我好过。
Yueru, after all, once shared my affection.

现在有了这样一个机会，我想放他一马。
Now that I have this opportunity, I’d like to let Tang off.

不过，这是马逢时的公事，又是你出了大力，
But it is Ma Fengshi’s official business, and you have gone to such trouble.

我只好说一声：多谢你！
All I can do is thank you.

到底应该怎么办？我也不敢多干预。”
I wouldn’t presume to interfere too much with how it is handled.”

“原来你是这么一种心思，
“So that is what you have in mind.

倒是我错怪你了。”同少棠又说：
I misjudged you.

“原来是我想替你尽点心，
I only wanted to do something for you.

你不忘记者相好，想这样子办，我当然照你的意思。
If you remember an old lover and want it handled this way, naturally I’ll follow your wishes.

至于论多论少，我要看情形办，
As for how much or how little, I must judge by circumstances.

而且我要告诉人家。”
And I shall tell them.”


“不必，不必！
“No, no!

不必说破。”
Don’t explain it.”

胡雪岩忽然神秘地一笑，
Hu suddenly smiled mysteriously.

“少棠，你记不记得石塔儿头的‘豆腐西施’阿香？”
“Shaotang, do you remember A’xiang, the ‘Tofu Beauty’ at Shita’ertou?”

周少棠愣了一下，从尘封的记忆中，找出阿香的影子来——
Zhou paused, retrieving her image from the dust of memory.

石塔儿头是地名，
Shita’ertou was a place name.

有家豆腐店的女儿，就是阿香，艳声四播。
A’xiang, the daughter of a tofu-shop owner there, had been famous for her beauty.

先是周少棠做了入幕之宾，后来胡雪岩做了他的所谓“同靴弟兄”，周少棠就绝迹不去了。
Zhou had first become her lover; later Hu had become his so-called “brother sharing the same boots,” and Zhou had stopped visiting altogether.

少年春梦，如今回想起来，什么感觉都没有了，
Looking back on that youthful dream of love, he felt nothing now.

只是奇怪胡雪岩何以忽然提了起来？
He only wondered why Hu had suddenly brought it up.

“当初那件事，我心里一直难过，
“I’ve always felt bad about that.

‘兔子不吃窝边草’，
A rabbit shouldn’t eat the grass beside its own burrow.

我不该割你的靴腰子。
I shouldn’t have poached on you.

现在顶好一报还一报。”
Now it would be best to repay you in kind.”

胡雪岩放低了声音说：“月如是匹扬州人所说的‘瘦马，，

语声发自外面那间屋子，清脆而沉着，
The voice came from the outer room, clear and composed.

从语声的韵味中，想象得到月如过了风信年华，正将步入徐娘阶段的年龄。
Its tone suggested that Yueru was past the first bloom of womanhood and approaching the age of mature charm.

这样在咫尺之外，发号施令，指挥丫头，是不是意味着她不会露面？
Giving orders to a maid from only a few steps away—did that mean she would not appear herself?

转念到此，周少棠心头，不免浮起一丝怅惘之感。
A slight disappointment rose in Zhou.

此时丫头进来请示，唐子韶已经交代，饭就开在楼上，理由仍旧是楼上比较暖和。
When the maid came to inquire, Tang ordered dinner served upstairs, again because it was warmer.

接着，门帘启处，周少棠眼前一亮，
Then the curtain lifted, and Zhou’s eyes brightened.

进来的少妇，约可三十上下年纪，长身玉立，鹅蛋形的脸上，长了一双极明亮的杏眼，
The woman who entered was about thirty, tall and graceful, with an oval face and exceptionally bright almond-shaped eyes.

眼风闪处，象有股什么力量，将周少棠从烟榻上弹了起来，
As her glance swept towards him, some force seemed to spring him from the smoking couch.

望着盈盈含笑的月如，不由得也在脸上堆满了笑容。
Looking at her gentle smile, he found his own face wreathed in smiles.

“这是小妾月如。”在烧烟的唐子韶，拿烟签子指点着说：
“This is my concubine, Yueru,” Tang said, indicating her with his opium needle as he prepared another pipe.

“月如，这是周老爷，
“Yueru, this is Mr Zhou.

你见一见。”
Make your greeting.”

“喔，是姨大大！”周少棠先就抱拳作揖。
“Ah, Madam!” Zhou clasped his hands and bowed first.

“不敢当，不敢当！”月如裣作礼，
“You are too kind!” She curtsied.

“周老爷我好象哪里见过。”
“Mr Zhou, I feel I’ve seen you somewhere.”

“你自然见过。”唐子韶说：
“Naturally you have,” Tang said.

“那天阜康门口搭了高台，几句话说得挤兑的人鸦雀无声，
“Remember the platform outside Fukang, and the few words that silenced everyone demanding their money?

就是周老爷。”
That was Mr Zhou.”


“啊！我想起来了。”
“Ah, now I remember!”

月如那双眼睛，闪闪发亮，惊喜交集，
Her eyes sparkled with delighted surprise.

“那天我同邻居去看热闹回来，
“I went with our neighbors to watch.

谈周老爷谈了两三夭。
We talked about you for two or three days afterward.

周老爷的口才，真正没话说。
Your eloquence is beyond praise.

这倒还在其次，
But that is the lesser thing.

大家都说周老爷的义气，真正少见。
Everyone says your loyalty is truly rare.

胡大先生是胡财神，平常捧财神的不晓得多少，
Mr Hu was the God of Wealth; who knows how many people flattered him then?

到了财神落难，好比变了瘟神，哪个不是见了他就躲，
Once misfortune struck, he became a God of Pestilence, and everyone fled at the sight of him.

只有周老爷看不过，出来说公道话。
Only you could not bear it and came forward to speak fairly.

如今一看周老爷的相貌，就晓得是行善积德，得饶人处且饶人，有大福气的厚道君子。
Now that I see you, I can tell you’re a blessed, kindhearted gentleman who does good, earns merit, and shows mercy wherever he can.”

“这番话说得周少棠心上象熨过一样服帖，
Her words soothed Zhou as though his heart had been gently ironed.

当然，他也有数，“得饶人处且饶人”，话中已经递过点子来了。
Naturally, he recognized the hint conveyed by “shows mercy wherever he can.”

“好说，好说！”周少棠说：
“You are too kind.

“我亦久闻唐姨太太贤惠能干，是我们老唐的贤内助。
I too have long heard how capable and virtuous Mistress Tang is—a worthy helpmate to our Old Tang.”

唐子韶一听称呼都改过了，知道周少棠必中圈套，
Hearing this change of address, Tang knew Zhou was certain to fall into the trap.

“随你奸似鬼，要吃老娘洗脚水”，心中暗暗得意，
“Cunning as a devil, yet you’ll drink the water a woman has washed her feet in,” he thought smugly.

一丢烟枪，翻身而起，口中说道：
Tossing aside his pipe, he sprang up.

“好吃酒了。”
“Time for wine!”
