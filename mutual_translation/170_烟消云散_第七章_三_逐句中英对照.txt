胡雪岩
《烟消云散》第七章（三，至本章结束）
Vanishing into Thin Air — Chapter Seven, Part Three (conclusion)
逐句中英对照
原著：高阳


其时方桌已经搭开，
The square table had been set.

自然是请周少棠上座，但只唐子韶侧面相陪。
Naturally Zhou Shaotang was given the seat of honour, but only Tang Zishao sat beside him.

菜并非如何讲究，但颇为人味，
The dishes were not particularly elaborate, yet they were delicious.

周少棠喜爱糟臃之物，所以对糟蒸白鱼、家乡肉、醉蟹这三样肴馔，格外欣赏，
Zhou liked foods preserved in wine or wine lees, and especially enjoyed the whitefish steamed with lees, the country-style cured pork, and the drunken crabs.

听说家乡肉、醉蟹并非市售，而是月如手制，便更赞不绝口了。
On learning that the pork and crabs had been prepared by Yueru herself rather than bought, he praised them still more warmly.

周少棠的谈锋很键，兴致又好，加以唐子韶是刻意奉承，
Zhou was an animated talker and in excellent spirits; Tang was deliberately flattering him.

所以快饮剧谈，相当投机。
They drank freely, talked eagerly, and got on very well.

当然，话题都是轻松有趣的。
Their subjects were naturally light and entertaining.

“老唐，”周少棠间到唐子韶的本行，“天下的朝奉，都是你们徽州人，好比票号都是山西人，
“Old Tang,” Zhou asked, turning to his host’s trade, “all the pawnbrokers in the country seem to be Huizhou men, just as all the remittance banks belong to Shanxi men.

而且听说只有太谷、平遥这两三府的人。
I have heard that even those come from only two or three prefectures, such as Taigu and Pingyao.

这是啥道理？”
Why is that?”

“这话，周先生，别人问我，我就装糊涂，随便敷衍几句，
“Mr Zhou, if anyone else asked, I should pretend not to know and put him off with a few words.

你老哥问到，我不能不跟你谈来历，
But since it is you, I must tell you the history.

不过，说起来不是啥体面的事？”
It is not a very creditable story, though.”

“喔，
“Oh?

怎么呢？”
How so?”


“明朝嘉靖年间，我们徽州有个人，叫汪直，
“During the Jiajing reign of the Ming, there was a man from our Huizhou called Wang Zhi.

你晓得不晓得。”
Have you heard of him?”

“我只晓得嘉靖年间有个‘打严嵩’的邹应龙，
“I know only that there was a Zou Yinglong in the Jiajing reign—the man who attacked Yan Song.

不晓得啥汪直。”
I know nothing about Wang Zhi.”

“你不晓得我告诉你，
“Then I shall tell you.

汪直是个汉奸。”
Wang Zhi was a traitor.”

“汉奸？
“A traitor?

莫非象秦桧一样私通外国。”
You mean he conspired with foreigners, like Qin Hui?”

“一点不错。”唐子韶答说，
“Exactly.

“不过汪直私通的不是金兵，
But Wang Zhi’s allies were not the Jin troops.

是日本人，那时候叫做倭寇。
They were Japanese, what people then called the wokou pirates.

倭寇到我们中国，在江浙沿海地方一登了陆，两眼漆黑，
When they landed on the Jiangsu and Zhejiang coast, they knew nothing of the country.

都是汪直同他的部下做向导，带他们一路奸淫掳掠。
Wang Zhi and his followers guided them as they raped and plundered their way inland.

倭寇很下作，放枪的时候，什么东西都要，
The pirates were a base lot: when looting, they took everything.

不过有的带不走，带走了，到他们日本也未见得有用，
Some things they could not carry away; others would be of little use in Japan even if they could.

所以汪直动了个脑筋，
So Wang Zhi had an idea.

开爿典当，什么东西都好当，
He opened a pawnshop that would accept anything.

老百姓来当东面，不过是个幌子，
Ordinary people bringing in their pledges were merely a cover.

说穿了，不过替日本人销赃而已。
In plain language, he was disposing of stolen goods for the Japanese.”

““怪不得了，你们那笔字象鬼画符，说话用‘切口’，
“No wonder your writing looks like a sorcerer’s charms, and you speak in special jargon.

原来都有讲究的。”周少棠说：
There was a reason for it all,” Zhou said.

“这是犯法的事情，当然要用同乡人。”
“Since it was illegal, you naturally had to use men from your own district.”

“不过，话要说回来，徽州地方苦得很，
“Still, one must remember how poor Huizhou is.

本地出产养不活本地人，
What the district produces cannot support its own people.

只好出外谋生，呼朋招友，同乡照顾同乡，
They have to go elsewhere for a living, calling in friends and helping fellow townsmen.

也是迫不得已。”
They have little choice.”


“你们微州人做生意，实在厉害，
“You Huizhou men certainly know how to do business.

象扬州的大盐商，问起来祖籍一大半是徽州。”周少棠说：
Ask the great salt merchants of Yangzhou where their families came from, and more than half say Huizhou.

“象汪直这样子，做了汉奸，还替日本人销赃，倒不怕公家抓他法办？”
But if Wang Zhi was both a traitor and a receiver of the Japanese pirates’ stolen goods, was he not afraid of being arrested?”

“这也有个原因的，
“There was a reason for that too.

当时的巡按御史，后来做了巡抚的胡宗宪，也是徽州人，
Hu Zongxian, the inspecting censor who later became governor, was also a Huizhou man.

虽不说包庇，念在同乡份上，略为高一高手，事情就过去了。
One need not call it protection; he had only to be a little lenient towards a fellow townsman, and the matter passed.

官司不怕大，只要有交情，总好商量。”
However serious a case may be, if there is friendship, something can always be arranged.”

唐子韶举杯相邀：
Tang raised his cup.

“来，来，周先生干一杯。”
“Come, Mr Zhou, let us drink.”

最后那两句话，加上敬酒的动作，意在言外，的然可见，
The last two sentences, accompanied by the toast, plainly carried another meaning.

但周少棠装作不觉，
Zhou pretended not to notice.

千了酒，将话题扯了开去，
He drained his cup and changed the subject.

“那个胡宗宪，你说他是巡按御史，
“You say this Hu Zongxian was an inspecting censor.

恐怕并没有庇护汪直的权柄。”
I doubt he had the power to protect Wang Zhi.

他又问一句：“真的权柄这么大。”
Was his authority really so great?”

“那只要看三堂会审的王金龙好了。”
“You have only to look at Wang Jinlong in The Trial Before Three Judges.”

“王金龙是小生扮的，好象刚刚出道，
“Wang Jinlong is played as a young man, apparently just beginning his career.

哪有这样子的威风？
How could he have such authority?

戏总是戏。”
A play is only a play.”

谈到这方面，唐子韶比周少棠内行得多了，
Tang knew much more about this than Zhou.

“明朝的进士，同现在不一样。
“Ming jinshi were different from those today.

现在的进士，如果不是点翰林或者到六部去当司官，放出来不过是个‘老虎班’的知县，
Nowadays, unless a jinshi enters the Hanlin Academy or becomes an official in one of the Six Ministries, he is sent out as no more than a county magistrate of the ‘tiger class.’

明朝的进士，一点‘巡按御史’赏上方宝剑，
But a Ming jinshi appointed inspecting censor received an imperial sword of authority.

等于皇上亲自来巡查，
He was as good as the emperor himself on a tour of inspection.

威风得不得了。
His power was tremendous.

我讲个故事，周先生你就晓得巡按御史的权柄了。”
Let me tell you a story, and you will understand.”


据说明朝有个富人，生两个女儿，
There was said to have been a rich man in the Ming with two daughters.

长女嫁武官，次女嫁了个寒士，
The elder married a military officer, the younger a poor scholar.

富人不免有势利之见，所以次婿受了许多委屈。
The father was somewhat snobbish, and the younger son-in-law suffered many slights.

及至次婿两榜及第，点了河南的巡按御史，
Eventually the scholar passed the provincial and metropolitan examinations and was appointed inspecting censor for Henan.

而长婿恰好在河南南阳当总兵。
His fellow son-in-law happened to be brigade commander at Nanyang in that province.

御史七品，总兵二品，
A censor held the seventh rank, a brigade commander the second.

但巡按御史“代天巡狩”，地位不同，
But an inspecting censor toured on the emperor’s behalf, which gave him a different standing.

所以次婿巡按到南阳，第二天五更时分，尚未起身，长婿已来禀请开操阅兵，
When he reached Nanyang, the commander came before he was out of bed, during the fifth watch next morning, to request permission to parade the troops for inspection.

那次婿想到当年岳家待他们连襟二人，炎凉各异，一时感慨，在枕上口占一绝：“黄草坡前万甲兵，碧纱帐里一书主；
Remembering how differently their father-in-law had treated them, the scholar was moved to compose a quatrain on his pillow: “Before the yellow-grass slope, ten thousand armoured men; behind green gauze curtains, a solitary scholar.

于今应识诗文贵，卧听元戎报五更。”
Now you should know the worth of verse and prose: lying in bed, I hear the commander announce the fifth watch.”

既“有诗为证”，周少棠不能不信，
With a poem to prove the story, Zhou could hardly disbelieve it.

而且触类旁通，有所领悟，
He also drew a further conclusion.

“这样说起来，‘三堂会审’左右的红袍、蓝袍，应该是藩司同臬司？”他问：
“Then the men in red and blue robes on either side in The Trial Before Three Judges must be the provincial treasurer and judicial commissioner.

“我猜得对不对？”
Am I right?”

“一点不错。”
“Exactly.”

“藩司、桌司旁坐陪审，那么居中坐的，身分应该是巡抚？”
“If those two sit beside him as fellow judges, the man in the centre ought to have the standing of a governor?”

“胡宗宪就是由巡按浙江的御史，改为浙江巡抚的。”
“Hu Zongxian went from inspecting censor for Zhejiang to governor of Zhejiang.”

“那就是了。”周少棠惋惜他说“胡大先生如果遇到他的本家就好了。”
“That explains it.” Zhou sighed. “If only Mr Hu had encountered that namesake of his!”

这就是说，胡雪岩如果遇见一个能象胡宗宪照顾同乡汪直那样的巡抚，他的典当就不至于会查封。
He meant that, had Hu Xueyan found a governor willing to protect him as Hu Zongxian had protected his fellow townsman Wang Zhi, his pawnshops would not have been taken into official custody.

唐子韶明白他的意思，但不愿意接口。
Tang understood but did not care to respond.


“周先生，”唐子韶忽然说道：“公济有好些满当的东西，
“Mr Zhou,” Tang said suddenly, “Gongji has quite a few unredeemed pledges whose terms have expired.

你要不要来看着？”
Would you like to come and look?”

周少棠不想贪这个小便宜，但亦不愿一口谢绝，便即问说：
Zhou did not want to take such a petty advantage, but neither did he wish to refuse outright.

“有没有啥比较特别、外面少见的东西？
“Is there anything unusual, something one does not often see elsewhere?”

““有，有，多得很。”
“Yes, plenty.”

唐子韶想了一会说：
Tang thought for a moment.

“快要过年了，
“The New Year is approaching.

有一堂灯，我劝周先生买了回去。
There is a set of lanterns I advise you to buy.

到正月十五挂起来，包管出色。”
Hang them up on the fifteenth of the first month, and I guarantee they will be a splendid sight.”

一听这话，周少棠不免诧异，
Zhou was puzzled.

上元的花灯、竹篾彩纸所扎，以新奇为贵，
Lantern Festival lanterns were made of bamboo strips and coloured paper; novelty was their chief attraction.

他想不明白，凭什么可以上当铺？
How could they be accepted as pledges?

因此，他愣了一下问道：“这种灯大概不是纸扎货？”
“These cannot be paper lanterns, I suppose?”

“当然。
“Of course not.

不然怎么好来当？”唐子韶说：
How could they be pawned otherwise?

“灯是绢灯，样子不多，大致照宫灯的式样，以六角形为主。
They are silk lanterns, in a limited number of designs, mostly hexagonal, like palace lanterns.

绢上画人物仕女，各种故事，
The silk is painted with figures, beautiful women, and all sorts of stories.

架子是活动的，
The frames fold.

用过了收拾干净，折起包好，明年再用。
After use, you clean them, fold and wrap them, and bring them out again next year.

海宁一带，通行这种灯。
They are common around Haining.

周先生没有看过？”
Have you never seen them?”

“没有。”
“No.”

“周先生看过了就晓得了。
“You will understand when you do.

这种灯不是哄小讶儿的纸扎走马灯，
They are not revolving paper lanterns for amusing children.

要有身分的人家，请有身分的客人吃春酒，厅上、廊上挂起来，
A respectable family gives a New Year dinner for distinguished guests and hangs them in the hall and corridors.

手里端杯酒，慢慢赏鉴绢上的各家画画。
With a cup in hand, one can study the paintings by different artists at leisure.

当然，也可以做它多少条灯谜，挂在灯上，请客人来打。
One can also hang riddles from them for the guests to solve.

这是文文静静的玩法：
It is a quiet, cultivated amusement.

象周先生现在也够身分了，应该置办这么一堂灯。”
A man of your present standing ought to acquire a set.”

周少棠近年收入不坏，常想在身分上力争上流，
Zhou’s income had been good in recent years, and he was eager to improve his social position.

尤其是最近为阜康的事，跟官府打过交道，已俨然在绪绅先生之列，
Since his dealings with the authorities over Fukang, he was by now virtually counted among the gentry.

所以对唐子韶的话，颇为动心，想了一下间道：
Tang’s suggestion appealed to him.

“办这么一堂灯，不晓得要花多少？”
“How much would such a set cost?”


“多少都花得下去！”唐子韶说：
“You could spend any amount,” Tang replied.

“这种灯，高下相差很大，
“There are enormous differences in quality.

好坏就在画上，要看是不是名家？
It all depends on the paintings—whether they are by famous artists.

就算是名家，未见得肯来画花灯，值钱就在这些地方。
Even a famous artist may not consent to paint lanterns; that is what makes them valuable.

譬如说，当今画仕女的，第一把手的费晓楼，
Take Fei Xiaolou, the foremost painter of beauties today.

你请他画花灯，他就不肯。”
Ask him to paint lanterns, and he will refuse.”

“那么，你那里满当的那一堂灯呢？是哪个画的呢？”
“Then who painted the set of expired pledges you have?”

“提起此人大大的有名，
“An exceedingly famous man.

康熙年间的大人先生，请他画过‘行乐图’的，不晓得多少。
Countless great officials of the Kangxi reign commissioned him to paint portraits of them at leisure.

他是扬州人，
He came from Yangzhou.

姓大禹的禹，名叫禹之鼎，
His surname was Yu, like Yu the Great, and his full name Yu Zhiding.

他也做过官，官名叫鸿胪寺序班。
He also held office as an usher in the Court of State Ceremonial.

这个官、照规矩是要旗人来做的，不晓得他怎么会做这个官……”
By regulation that post was for bannermen; I do not know how he came to hold it...”

“老唐，”周少棠打断他的话说：“我们不要去管他的官，谈他的画好了。”
“Old Tang,” Zhou interrupted, “let us leave his office aside and talk about his paintings.”

于是唐子韶言归正传，
Tang returned to the point.

说禹之鼎所画的那堂绢制花灯，一共二十四盏，六种样式，画的六个故事，西施沼吴、文君当垆、昭君出塞、文姬归汉、宓妃留枕、梅杨争宠，
Yu’s set comprised twenty-four silk lanterns in six designs, illustrating six stories: Xi Shi and the fall of Wu, Wenjun tending the wine shop, Zhaojun going beyond the frontier, Wenji returning to the Han, Fufei leaving her pillow, and Mei and Yang competing for favour.

梅是梅妃，杨是杨玉环，
Mei was Consort Mei, and Yang was Yang Yuhuan.

所以六个故事，却有七大美人。
Thus the six stories contained seven beauties.

“禹之鼎的画，假的很多，不过这堂灯绝不假，
“There are many counterfeit Yu Zhiding paintings, but these lanterns are certainly genuine.

因为来历不同。”唐子韶又说：
Their history is different.

‘康熙年间，有个皇帝面前的大红人，名叫高江村，他原来是杭州人，
In the Kangxi reign there was a great imperial favourite called Gao Jiangcun, originally from Hangzhou.

后来住在嘉兴的平湖县，
Later he lived in Pinghu County, Jiaxing.

到了嘉庆年间，子孙败落下来，
By the Jiaqing reign his descendants had fallen into poverty.

这堂灯就是高江村请禹之鼎画的，所以不假，
Gao commissioned Yu Zhiding to paint this set, so there can be no doubt about it.

周先生，这堂灯，明天我叫人送到府上。
Mr Zhou, I shall have it sent to your house tomorrow.”

““不，不！”周少棠摇着手说：
“No, no!” Zhou waved his hands.

“看看东西，再作道理。”
“Let me see it first; we can decide afterwards.”


唐子韶还要往下说时，只见一个丫头进来说道：
Tang was about to continue when a maid came in.

“公济派人来通知，
“Someone has come from Gongji.

说‘首柜，得了急病，
The chief counter appraiser has been taken suddenly ill.

请老爷马上去。”
They ask you to go at once.”

典当司事，分为“内缺”、“外缺”两种，
Pawnshop clerks held either internal or counter posts.

外缺的头脑，称为“首柜”，照例坐在迎门柜台的最左方，
The head of the latter was called the chief counter appraiser and ordinarily sat at the extreme left of the counter facing the entrance.

珍贵之物送上柜台，必经首柜镜定估价，
Any valuable article brought in had to pass through his hands for examination and valuation.

是个极重要的职司，所以唐子韶得此消息，顿时忧形于色，
It was therefore an important position, and worry immediately appeared on Tang’s face.

周少棠也就坐不住了。
Zhou could no longer remain comfortably seated.

“老唐，你有急事尽管请。
“Old Tang, if you have urgent business, please go.

我也要告辞了。”
I was about to take my leave.”

“不！不！
“No, no!

我去看一看就回来。
I shall go and look, then return.

我们的事也要紧的。”接着便喊：“月如，月如！”
Our business is important too.” He called for Yueru.

等丫头将月如去唤了来，唐子韶吩咐她代为陪客，随即向周少棠拱拱手，道声失陪，下楼而去。
When the maid brought her, he told her to keep their guest company, saluted Zhou with clasped hands, and went downstairs.

面临这样的局面，周少棠自然而然地想起了胡雪岩中美人汁的传说，起了几分戒心。
In this situation Zhou naturally remembered the story of Hu falling into a beauty trap, and became a little wary.

但月如却落落大方地一面布菜斟酒，一面问起周少棠的家庭情形，由周太太问到子女，因话搭话，
But Yueru was entirely at ease. Serving food and pouring wine, she asked about Zhou’s family, moving from his wife to his children as one subject led naturally to another.

谈锋很健，却很自然，完全是熟不拘札的闲话家常。
She talked readily, with the unceremonious familiarity of an old acquaintance discussing household affairs.

在周少棠的感觉中，月如是个能干贤惠的主妇，因而对于她与胡雪岩之间的传说，竟起了不可思议之感。
To Zhou, she seemed an able and virtuous housewife, making the stories about her and Hu almost impossible to believe.

当然也少得谈到胡雪岩的失败，月如更是表现了故主情殷，休戚相关的忠捆。
Inevitably Hu’s failure came up, and she showed deep loyalty to her former master, as though his fortunes were her own.

周少棠倒很想趁机谈一谈公济的事，但终于还是不曾开口。
Zhou was tempted to raise Gongji’s affairs, but in the end said nothing.


“姨太，”丫头又来报了，“老爷叫人回来说，首柜的病很重，
“Madam,” the maid reported again, “the master has sent word that the chief appraiser is very ill.

他还要等在那里看一看，
He must remain there a little longer to see how things develop.

请周老爷不要走，还有要紧事谈。”
Mr Zhou is asked not to leave; there is still important business to discuss.”

“晓得了。
“Very well.

你再去烫一壶酒来。”
Warm another pot of wine.”

“酒够了，酒够了。”周少棠说，
“I have had enough wine,” Zhou said.

不必再烫，
“There is no need for more.

有粥我想吃一碗。
If there is congee, I should like a bowl.”

““预备了香粳米粥在那里，
“We have fragrant rice congee ready.

酒还可以来一点。”
You can still have a little more wine.”

“那就以一壶为度。”
“Then let us stop after one pot.”

喝完了酒喝粥，接着又喝茶，
After the wine they had congee, then tea.

而唐子韶却无回来的消息，周少棠有些踌躇了。
Still there was no word of Tang’s return, and Zhou began to feel uncertain.

“周老爷，”
“Mr Zhou.”

月如从里间走了出来，是重施过脂粉了，
Yueru emerged from the inner room, her powder and rouge freshly applied.

她大大方方地说：“我来打口烟你吃。”
Quite openly she said, “Let me prepare you a pipe.”

“我没有瘾。”
“I have no habit.”

“香一简玩玩。”
