Vanishing into Thin Air — Chapter Eight, Part Two (conclusion)

原著：高阳


“少棠，你我相交一场，
“Shaotang, we have been friends for so long.

我有力量帮你的时候，没有帮你什么……”
When I had the means to help you, I did very little…”

“不，不！”周少棠插嘴拦住，
“No, no!” Zhou interrupted.

“你不要说这话，
“Do not say that.

你帮我的忙，够多了。”
You have helped me more than enough.”

“好！
“Very well.

我现在还要帮你一个忙，替你好好儿物色一个人。”“大先生！”周少棠笑道：
Now I want to do you another service and find a suitable woman for you.” Zhou laughed. “Mr Hu!

“你现在倒还有闲工夫来管这种闲事？”
At a time like this, you still have leisure to concern yourself with such trifles?”

“正事轮不到我管，
“My serious affairs are no longer mine to manage.

有刘抚台、德藩台替我操心，
Governor Liu and Treasurer De worry about them for me.

我就只好管闲事了。”
I can only occupy myself with trifles.”

满腹牢骚，出以自我调侃的语气，正见得他的万般无奈。
His self-mocking tone, beneath which lay so much resentment, revealed his utter helplessness.

周少棠不免兴起一种英雄末路的苍凉之感。
Zhou felt the desolation of a hero brought to the end of his road.

再谈下去，说不定会掉眼泪，
If they talked much longer, he might weep.

因而起身告辞。
He therefore rose to take his leave.

胡雪岩握着他的手臂，仿佛有话要说，
Hu grasped his arm as though he had something to say.

却两次欲言又止，
Twice he almost spoke, then stopped.

终于松开了手说：
At last he released his hold.

“再谈吧！”
“We shall talk again.”


半夜里叩中门，送进来一封信，说是藩台衙门的专差送来的。
At midnight there was a knock at the inner gate, and a letter was brought in, delivered by a special messenger from the provincial treasurer's yamen.

螺蛳太太将胡雪岩唤醒了，拿一盏水晶玻璃罩的“洋灯”，让他看信。
Madam Luosi woke Hu and held a Western lamp with a crystal-glass shade for him to read it.

看不到几行，胡雪岩将信搁下，开口说道：
Before he had gone through more than a few lines, he put it down.

“我要起来。”
“I must get up.”

于是螺蛳太太叫起丫头，点起灯火，拨旺炭盆，服侍胡雪岩起身，
She roused the maids, had the lights lit and the brazier stirred, and helped him dress.

他将德馨的信，置在桌上细看。
He laid Dexin's letter on the table for closer examination.

一张八行笺以外，另有一个抄件，字迹较小，需要戴老花眼镜，才看得清楚。
Besides the sheet of eight-column stationery, there was a copy of another document, written so small that he needed his reading spectacles.


抄件是一道上谕：“谕内阁：
It was an imperial edict: “To the Grand Secretariat.

给事中郎承修奏请，责令贪吏罚捐巨款，以济要需一折，
Supervising Censor Deng Chengxiu has memorialized, requesting that corrupt officials be ordered to make substantial compulsory contributions to meet urgent needs.

据称该给事中所开赃私最著者，如已故总督瑞麟、学政何廷谦、前任粤海关监督崇礼及俊启、学政吴宝恕、水师提督翟国彦、盐运使何兆瀛、肇难道方浚师、广州府知府冯端本、潮州府知府刘湘年、廉州府知府张丙炎、南海县知县杜凤治、顺德县知县林灼之、现任南海县知县卢乐戌，
He lists as particularly notorious for illicit gains the late Governor-General Ruilin; Education Commissioner He Tingqian; former superintendents of the Guangdong Maritime Customs, Chongli and Junqi; Education Commissioner Wu Baoshu; Naval Commander Zhai Guoyan; Salt Transport Commissioner He Zhaoying; Circuit Intendant Fang Junshi of Zhaonan; Prefect Feng Duanben of Guangzhou; Prefect Liu Xiangnian of Chaozhou; Prefect Zhang Bingyan of Lianzhou; Magistrate Du Fengzhi of Nanhai; Magistrate Lin Zhuozhi of Shunde; and the present Magistrate Lu Lexu of Nanhai.

皆自宫广东后，得有巨资，
All, he states, acquired great wealth after serving in Guangdong.

若非民膏，即是国帑等语，
Such wealth must have come either from the people's substance or from the public treasury.

着派彭玉麟将各该员在广东居官声名苦何，确切查明，据实具奏。”
Peng Yulin is hereby directed to ascertain precisely the reputations of these officials during their service in Guangdong and report the facts.”

这跟胡雪岩无关。
This did not concern Hu.


另有一个附片，就大有关系了：“另片奏：闻阜康银号关闭，协办大学士刑部尚书文煜，所存该号银数至七十余万之多，
The supplementary memorial, however, concerned him very closely: “The supplementary memorial states: ‘I hear that Fukang Bank has closed, and that Wenyu, Assistant Grand Secretary and Minister of Punishments, has more than seven hundred thousand taels deposited there.

请即查明确数，究所从来，据实参处等语，
I request an immediate investigation to ascertain the exact sum and its source, followed by a report seeking appropriate disciplinary action.’

着顺天府确查具奏。”
The Shuntian prefectural authorities are hereby ordered to investigate and report.”

接下来再看德馨的亲笔信，只有短短的两行：“事已通天，
Dexin's own letter contained only two short lines: “The affair has reached the throne.

恐尚有严旨，
Further severe orders may follow.

请速为之计。
Please make preparations without delay.

容面谈。”
We must discuss this in person.”


“你看！”
“Look!”

胡雪岩将信递了给螺蛳太太，
Hu handed the letter to Madam Luosi.

“话没有说清楚，
“He has not made himself clear.

‘容面谈’是他来，还是要我去？”
Does ‘discuss this in person’ mean he is coming here, or that I am to go to him?”

“等我来问问看。”螺蛳太太将递信进来的丫头、由镜槛阁调过来的巧珠唤了来，
“Let me find out.” She called Qiaozhu, the maid who had brought in the letter and who had been transferred from Jingjian Pavilion.

关照她到中门上传话，赶到门房去问，藩司衙门来的专差，是否还在？
Qiaozhu was to send word through the inner gate to the gatehouse and ask whether the treasurer's messenger was still there.

如果已经走了，留下什么话没有？
If he had gone, had he left any message?

这得好一陈工夫才会有回话，
It would take some time for an answer to return.

胡雪岩有点沉不住气了，
Hu could no longer sit still.

起身蹀躞，喃喃自语：“严旨，严旨！
He paced about, muttering, “Severe orders, severe orders!

是革职还是抄家？”
Dismissal from office, or confiscation of the household?”

螺蛳太太一听吓坏了，但不敢现诸形色，
Madam Luosi was terrified, but dared not show it.

只将一件大毛皮袍，一件贡缎马褂堆在椅子上，
She merely laid a heavy fur robe and a tribute-satin riding jacket on a chair.

因为不管是德馨来，还是胡雪岩去，都要换衣服，所以早早预备在那里。
Whether Dexin came to Hu or Hu went to Dexin, he would need to change, and she wanted the clothes ready.

“ ‘速为之计’，怎么‘计’法？”
“‘Make preparations without delay’—what preparations?”

胡雪岩突然住足，
Hu suddenly stopped.

“我看我应该到上海去一趟。
“I think I must go to Shanghai.”

“为啥？”
“Why?”

“至少我要把转运局的公事，弄清楚了，作个交代，
“At the very least I must put the Transport Bureau's official business in order and give a proper account.

不要牵涉到左大人，
I cannot let Lord Zuo become involved.

我就太对不起人了。”
I would never forgive myself.”

“光是为这件事，托七姐夫就可以了。”
“If that is all, you can ask Brother-in-Law Gu to do it.”

“不！
“No.

还有宓本常，
There is Mi Benchang as well.

我要当面同他碰个头，看看他把上海的帐目，清理得怎么样了。”
I must meet him face to face and see how far he has got with settling the Shanghai accounts.”

商议未定之际，只见巧珠急急来报，德馨已经微服来访。
Before they had reached a decision, Qiaozhu hurried back: Dexin had arrived in plain clothes.

胡雪岩急忙换了衣服，
Hu changed quickly.

未及下楼，已有四名丫头，持着宫灯，前引后拥地将德馨迎上楼来。
Before he could go downstairs, four maids with palace lanterns were already escorting Dexin up, some leading and others following.


胡雪岩在楼梯口迎着，作了一个揖，口中不安地说：
Hu met him at the head of the stairs and bowed.

“这样深夜，亲自劳步，真正叫我不知道怎么说了！”
“To trouble you to come in person at such an hour—I truly do not know what to say!”

“自己弟兄，
“We are brothers.

不必谈这些。”
There is no need to speak of that.”

德馨进了门，还未坐定，便即说道：“文中堂怕顶不住了。”
Dexin had scarcely entered and had not yet settled into his seat when he said, “I am afraid Lord Wen can no longer hold the line.”

“文中堂”便是文煜，现任协办大学士刑部尚书，所以称之为“中堂”。
Lord Wen was Wenyu, the current Assistant Grand Secretary and Minister of Punishments, and was therefore addressed as a grand secretary.

他是八旗中有名的殷实大户，
He was a famously wealthy man among the Eight Banners.

发财是在福州将军任上。
His fortune had been made as Fuzhou Garrison General.

海内冲要重镇，都有驻防的将军，
Garrison generals were stationed in important strategic cities throughout the empire.

位尊而权不重，亦谈不到什么入息，
Their rank was high, but their power modest, and their income generally unremarkable.

只有福州将军例外，因为兼管闽海关，
Fuzhou was the exception because its general also supervised the Fujian Maritime Customs.

五口通商以后，福州亦是洋商贸易的要地，税收激增，所以成了肥缺，
After the five treaty ports opened, Fuzhou became a major centre of foreign commerce, customs receipts rose sharply, and the appointment grew lucrative.

文煜因为是恭王的亲戚，靠山甚硬，在这个肥缺上盘踞了九年之久，
Related to Prince Gong and backed by powerful protection, Wenyu had occupied this profitable post for nine years.

及至内调进京，又几次派充崇文门监督，这也是一个日进斗金的阔差，
After his transfer to the capital, he had several times been appointed superintendent at Chongwen Gate, another office where wealth poured in by the day.

数十年宦囊所积，不下千万之多。
Over decades in office he had accumulated no less than ten million taels.

在阜康，他是第一个大存户，
At Fukang he was the largest depositor.

一方面是利害相共，休戚相关；一方面他跟胡雪岩的交情很厚，
His interests were bound up with Hu's, and their personal friendship was close.

所以从阜康出事以后，他一直在暗中支持，
Since the crisis began, he had supported Hu behind the scenes.

现在为邓承修一纸“片奏”所参，
Now Deng Chengxiu's supplementary memorial had impeached him.

纸包不住火，
The fire could no longer be wrapped in paper.

自顾不暇，当然不能再替胡雪岩去“顶”了。
Preoccupied with protecting himself, he could naturally no longer shield Hu.

“雪岩，”德馨又问：“文中堂真的有那么多款子，存在你那里？”
“Xueyan,” Dexin asked, “does Lord Wen really have so much deposited with you?”

“没有那么多。”
“Not that much.

胡雪岩答说：“细数我不清楚，大概四五十万是有的。”
I do not know the exact figure, but there must be four or five hundred thousand.”

“这也不少了。”
“That is no small sum either.”


“晓翁，”心乱如麻的胡雪岩，终于找到一句要紧话：“你看，顺天府据实奏报以后，朝廷会怎么办？”
At last Hu's confused thoughts yielded an essential question. “Mr Xiaofeng, once the Shuntian authorities have reported the facts, what do you think the court will do?”

“照定制来说，朝廷就不会听片面之词，
“Under the established procedure, the court will not listen to one side alone.

一定是要文中堂明白回奏。”
It will certainly order Lord Wen to submit a clear explanation.”

“文中堂怎么回奏呢？”
“What explanation can he give?”

“那就不知道了。”
“That I cannot say.

德馨答说：“总不会承认自己的钱，来路不明吧！”
He will hardly admit that his money comes from a dubious source.”

“他历充优差，省吃俭用，利上滚利，积成这么一个数目；
“He has held profitable posts for years, lived frugally, and let the interest accumulate.

似乎也不算多。”
A sum like that hardly seems excessive.”

“好家伙，你真是‘财神’的口吻，
“Good heavens, you really do speak like the God of Wealth!

光是钱庄存款就有四五十万，还不算多吗？”
Four or five hundred thousand in bank deposits alone, and you call it nothing excessive?”

胡雪岩无词以对，
Hu had no answer.

只是在想：文煜究竟会得到怎么一种处分？
He could think only of what punishment Wenyu might receive.

“文中堂这回怕要倒楣。”德馨说道：
“I fear Lord Wen is in for trouble,” Dexin continued.

“现在清流的气焰正盛，
“The Pure Stream group is very much in the ascendant.

朝廷为了尊重言路，只怕要拿文中堂来开刀。”
To show respect for the right of remonstrance, the court may make an example of him.”

胡雪岩一惊，
Hu started.

“怎么？”
“What?

他急急问道：“会治他的罪？”
Will he be convicted of an offence?”

“治罪是不会的。
“Not that.

只怕要罚他。”
But he may be penalized.”

“怎么罚？
“How?

罚款？”
A fine?”

“当然。
“Of course.

现在正在用兵，军需孔急，
We are at war, and military funds are urgently needed.

作兴会罚他报效饷银。
He may be ordered to contribute to the war chest as a penalty.

数目多寡就不知道了。”
I cannot say how much.”

德馨语重心长地警告：
Dexin's warning was earnest.

“雪岩，我所说的早为之计，第一步就是要把这笔款子预备好。”
“When I urged you to prepare early, Xueyan, the first thing I meant was that you must have this money ready.”

“哪笔款子？”胡雪岩茫然地问。
“Which money?” Hu asked blankly.

“文中堂的罚款啊！
“Lord Wen's fine!

只要上谕一下来，罚银多少，自然是在他的存款中提的。
Once the edict arrives, whatever sum he is required to pay will naturally be drawn from his deposit.

到那时你就变成欠官款了，而且是奉特旨所提的官款，
Then it becomes public money that you owe—and public money demanded by a special imperial order.

急如星火，
It will be as urgent as a spark in dry tinder.

想拖一拖不都不成。”
You will not be able to put it off for a moment.”


“喔！”
“Oh!”

胡雪岩心想，要还的公私款项，不下数千万，
Hu thought of the tens of millions he already owed in public and private funds.

又何在乎这一笔？
What difference could this one sum make?

但德馨的好意总是可感的，因而答说：
Still, Dexin's concern deserved gratitude.

“晓翁关爱，我很感激，
“I am very grateful for your care, Mr Xiaofeng.

这笔款子我这回一到上海，首先把它预备好，
As soon as I reach Shanghai this time, I shall make preparing this sum my first task.

上谕一到，当即呈缴。”
When the edict comes, it can be submitted at once.”

“这才是。”德馨问道：
“That is the right approach.

“你预备什么时候动身？”
When do you intend to leave?”

“明天来不及，
“I cannot manage tomorrow.

后天走。”
The day after.”

“哪天回来？”
“When will you return?”

“看事情顺手不顺手。
“That depends on how smoothly things go.

我还想到江宁去一趟，看左大人能不能帮我什么忙？”
I also want to visit Jiangning and see whether Lord Zuo can help.”

“你早就该去了。”德馨紧接着说：
“You should have gone long ago,” Dexin said at once.

“你早点动身吧！
“Leave as soon as you can.

这里反正封典当这件事正在进行，
The sequestration of the pawnshops is proceeding here.

公款也好，私款也好，大家都要看封典当清算的结果，一时不会来催。
Public and private creditors alike are waiting to see what it yields, and will not press you for the moment.

你正好趁这空档，赶紧拿丝茧脱手，
Use the interval to dispose of the silk and cocoons quickly.

‘讲倒帐’就比较容易。”
Then it will be easier to negotiate a reduced settlement.”

“讲倒帐”，便是打折扣来清偿。任何生意失败，都是如此料理。
Such a settlement meant paying debts at a discount, the usual way of dealing with a failed business.

但讲倒帐以前，先要准备好现款，
But cash had to be ready before negotiations could begin.

胡雪岩一直在等待情势比较缓和，存货就比较能卖得比较好的价钱，“讲倒帐”的折扣亦可提高。
Hu had been waiting for conditions to ease so that his stocks might fetch a better price and creditors could be offered a larger proportion of what they were owed.

但照目前的情势看，越逼越紧，
Now the pressure was tightening.

封典当以后，继以文煜这一案，接下来可能会有革职的处分，
After the seizure of the pawnshops came Wenyu's case; dismissal from office might follow.

那时候的身分，一落千丈，处事更加困难，
If Hu's standing collapsed, everything would become harder.

真如德馨所说的，亟应“速为之计”。
As Dexin said, preparations really must be made without delay.


因此，等德馨一走，胡雪岩跟螺蛳太太重作计议，
After Dexin left, Hu discussed their plans afresh with Madam Luosi.

“箭在弦上，
“The arrow is on the bowstring.

不得不发了。”他说：
It must be released,” he said.

“有句话叫做‘壮士断腕’，
“There is a saying about a brave man cutting off his wrist.

我只有自己斩掉一条膀子，
I must cut off one of my own arms.

人虽残废，性命可保。
I shall be crippled, but I shall live.

你看呢？”
What do you think?”

“都随你！”
“Whatever you decide.”

螺蛳太太噙着眼泪说：
Tears stood in her eyes.

“只要你斩膀子，
“Only cut off the arm yourself.

不叫我来动手。”
Do not ask me to do it.”

“虽不叫你来动手，只怕要你在我的刀上加一把劲，
“I shall not ask you to wield the blade, but I may need you to add your strength to mine.

不然斩不下来。
Otherwise I shall never bring myself to cut.

这一点，你一定要答应我。”
You must promise me that.”

螺蛳太太一面流泪，一面点头，然后问道：“ 这回你以上海，预备怎么办？”
Weeping, she nodded, then asked, “What do you intend to do in Shanghai this time?”

“我托应春把丝茧全部出清，款子存在汇丰银行，作为讲倒帐的准备金。
“Ask Yingchun to sell off all the silk and cocoons and put the proceeds in HSBC as a reserve for negotiating settlements.

再要到江宁去一趟。请左大人替我说说话，
Then go to Jiangning and ask Lord Zuo to speak for me.

官款即令不能打折扣，也不要追得那么紧，
Even if the public debts cannot be reduced, they need not be pressed so hard.

到底我也还有赚钱的事业，
I still have profitable businesses.

慢慢儿赚了来还，
Let them earn the money to repay gradually.

一下子都逼倒了，对公家也没有什么好处。“
Driving them all to ruin at once would do the authorities no good either.”


“怎么？”
“Do you mean…”

螺蛳太太忽有意会，定神想了一下说：
A thought struck her, and she considered it carefully.

“你是说，譬如典当，照常开门，到年底下结帐，赚了钱，拿来拉还公款，
“For example, the pawnshops would stay open, and at the year-end reckoning their profits would go towards the public debts?

等还清了，二十几家典当还是我们的？
Once those were paid, the twenty-odd pawnshops would still be ours?”

“胡雪岩失笑了，
Hu could not help laughing.

“你真是一手只如意、一只手算盘，
“You have a wish-granting sceptre in one hand and an abacus in the other!

天下世界哪里有这么好的事？”
Where could one find such a wonderful arrangement?

他说，“所谓‘慢慢儿赚了来还’，意思是赚钱的事业，先照常维持，然后再来估价抵还公款。”
By earning the money gradually, I mean keeping the profitable businesses running for the time being, then valuing them and handing them over against the public debts.”

“这有啥区别呢？
“What difference does that make?

迟早一场空。”
Sooner or later, everything is gone.”

螺蛳太太大失所望，声音非常凄凉。
Her disappointment sounded desolate.

“虽然迟早一场空，还是有区别的。
“There is a difference, even if everything must go in the end.

譬如说：这家典当的架本是二十万两，
Suppose a pawnshop has capital of two hundred thousand taels.

典当照常营业，当头有人来赎，可以照二十万两算；
If it continues trading and customers come to redeem their pledges, it can be valued at two hundred thousand.

倘或关门不做生意了，当头只好照流当价来估价，
If it closes, the pledges have to be valued at the prices they would fetch as unredeemed goods.

三文不值两文，
What should be worth three cash may not fetch two.

决不能算二十万两，不足之数，仍旧要我们来赔，
The shop certainly cannot be counted at two hundred thousand, and we must still make up the shortfall.

这当中出入很大。
That is a considerable difference.

这样子一说，你明白了吧？”
Do you understand now?”

“明白是明白。
“I understand.
