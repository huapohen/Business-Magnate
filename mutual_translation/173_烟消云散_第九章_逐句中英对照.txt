“我有事。
“I have business.

多谢！多谢！”
Thank you all the same.”

“去啊！”阿彩没有理他的话，管自己催促阿利。

Obedient as ever, he said, “You must not stand on ceremony, Master Gu,” and went out.

这是阿彩特意遣开丈夫，有些心里的话要吐露，
She had deliberately sent her husband away so that she could speak her heart.

“古老爷，”她面色深沉地说：“我实在没有想到，今生今世，还会遇见二十几年前的老客人；

更没有想到，当年当了夹袍来吃饭的客人，就是名气这样子大的胡财神。

古老爷，不瞒你说，我昨天晚上一夜没有睡着，

因为这桩事情，想起来想不完。”
There seemed no end to the thoughts it brought me.”

说着，将一双眼睛低了下去，
She lowered her eyes.

眼角微显晶莹，似乎泪水要流出来。
Moisture glimmered at their corners, as though tears were about to fall.

古应春当然能体会她的心情，故意不答，
Gu understood her feelings, but deliberately said nothing.

他觉得既不能问，更不能劝慰，只要有这样一句话，她的眼泪就会忍不住，
He must neither question nor console her: a single word might release her tears.

惟有保持沉默，才能让她静静地自我克制。
Only silence would allow her to regain control in peace.

果然，停了一会，阿彩复又抬眼，平静地说道：
After a while, she raised her eyes and spoke calmly.

“古老爷，请你告诉胡老爷，我决不能收他这笔钱，
“Please tell Master Hu I cannot accept his money.

第一，他现在正是为难的时候，
First, he is in difficulties himself.

我收了他的这笔钱，于心不安；
My conscience would trouble me if I took it.

第二，我收了他的这笔钱，变成我亏欠他了，
Second, if I accepted it, I should become the one indebted to him.

也没有啥好想的了。”
There would be nothing left to cherish in my thoughts.”


古应春觉得事态严重了，比他所想象的还要严重，
Gu felt that matters had become serious, more serious than he had imagined.

这三千两银子，可能会引起他们夫妇之间的裂痕。
The three thousand taels might open a breach between husband and wife.

转念到此，颇为不安，也深悔自己多事。
This troubled him, and he deeply regretted involving himself.

细细想去，要割断她这一缕从云外飘来的情丝，还是得用“泉刀”这样的利器，于是他说：
On reflection, the sharp blade of money remained the best means of severing this thread of affection that had drifted down from distant clouds.

“朱太太，我说一句不怕你见气的话，
“Mrs Zhu, I hope you will not take offense.

如果说，胡老爷现在三千两银子部花不起，你未免太小看他了。
If you think Master Hu can no longer afford three thousand taels, you underestimate him.”

朱太太，”古应春将声音压得低低地，同时两眼逼视着她，“我有两句肺腑之言，
Lowering his voice and looking straight into her eyes, he continued, “There are a couple of things I should like to say from the heart.

不晓得你要不要听？”
Are you willing to hear them?”

“当然要听。”
“Of course.”

“只怕我说得太直。”
“I am afraid I may speak too plainly.”

“不要紧，
“It does not matter.

没有旁人在这里。”
There is nobody else here.”

这表示连阿利不能听的话都能说，
That meant he might say even what A’li must not hear.

古应春便不作任何顾忌了，
Gu put aside his remaining scruples.

“朱太太，”他说：“三千两银子，不是一个小数日，而况是号称财神的胡老爷送你的，更何况人家是为了完当年的一笔人情债，
“Three thousand taels is no small sum, Mrs Zhu, especially when it is a gift from the man known as the God of Wealth, given to repay an old kindness.

送的人光明正大，受的人正大光明。
The giver has nothing to conceal, and neither has the recipient.

朱老板如果问一句：你为啥不收？请问你怎么同他说？
If your husband asks why you will not accept it, what are you going to tell him?”

“阿彩根本没有想到阿利，
A’cai had not considered A’li at all.

如今古应春提出来一问，才发现自己确有难以交代之处。
Only now did she realize that she would have difficulty explaining herself.

见她语塞，古应春知道“攻心”已经生效，便穷追猛打地又钉一句：
Seeing her at a loss for words, Gu knew he had struck home and pressed the point.

“莫非你说，我心里的那段情，万金不换，
“Will you say, ‘All the gold in the world could not buy the feeling I cherish.

三千两算得了什么？”
What are three thousand taels to me?’”

“我当然有我的说法。”
“Naturally I shall have my own explanation.”

这是遁词，
It was an evasion.

古应春觉得不必再追，可以从正面来劝她了。
Gu felt there was no need to press further; he could now appeal to her directly.


“不管你怎么说，朱老板嘴里不敢同你争，心里不会相信的。
“Whatever you say, your husband may not dare argue, but in his heart he will not believe you.

这样子，夫妇之间，就有一道裂痕了。
That will open a breach between you.

二十几年的夫妇，你肚皮里还有个老来子，
You have been married more than twenty years; you are carrying another child in your later years.

有这三千两银子，将老同和老店新开，扩充门面，兴兴旺旺做人家，
With these three thousand taels, you can rebuild Laotonghe, expand the business, and make a prosperous home.

连你们死掉的老老板，在阴世里都会高兴。
Even your late father would be pleased in the world below.

这种好日子不过，要自寻烦恼，害得一家人家可能会拆散，何苦？
Why throw away such good days, seek out trouble, and risk breaking up your family?

Besides, in Master Hu's present circumstances, a few thousand taels do not matter so much, but emotionally he cannot bear another blow.

他因为能先还笔人情债，心里很高兴，
He was delighted that he could settle this old debt of kindness first.

昨天晚上睡了个把月以来从没有睡过的好觉。
Last night he slept better than at any time in the past month or more.

倘或晓得你有这种想法，他心里一定不安，他现在经不起再加什么烦恼了。
If he knew what was in your mind, it would trouble him, and he cannot stand any further worries now.

总而言之，你收了这笔银子，让他了掉一桩心事，就是帮他的忙。
In a word, accepting the money and relieving him of this concern will help him.

Otherwise, to speak bluntly, it is as though you were deliberately trying to hurt him.

朱太太，你不是十七八岁的姑娘了，
You are no longer a girl of seventeen or eighteen, Mrs Zhu.

而且有儿有女，
You have a son and a daughter.

闹出笑话来，不好听。”
It would not sound well if people began talking.”

这长篇大套一番话，将想得到的道理都说尽了，
This long speech exhausted every argument he could think of.

阿彩听得惊心动魄，
A’cai listened, deeply shaken.

终于如梦方醒似地说了一句：“我收！
At last, as though waking from a dream, she said, “I shall take it.

请古老爷替我谢谢胡老爷。”
Please thank Master Hu for me.”

“对啊！”
“That is right!”

古应春大为欣慰，少不得乘机恭维她几句：
Greatly relieved, Gu could not resist adding some compliments.

“我就晓得你是有见识、讲道理、顾大局的人。
“I knew you were a woman of judgment and reason, who could see the larger picture.

“Keep these safely, Mrs Zhu.”

“You could really have given me Fukang drafts instead.”

A’cai knew that Fukang was already settling its affairs and that no one yet knew what proportion of a draft's value would be paid.

所以如此说法，亦依旧是由于一种不愿接受赠款的心理。
Her remark still sprang from a reluctance to accept the gift.

古应春明白这一点，却正好借此道出胡雪岩的心境。
Gu understood, and took the opportunity to explain Hu's feelings.

阿彩爽然若失，
A’cai felt a blank sense of loss.

“Not at all!
