第十一章 人去楼空｜CHAPTER ELEVEN: THE PEOPLE GONE, THE TOWERS EMPTY


两人并坐低声谈了好一会方始结束。
The two sat side by side and talked in low voices for a long time before they finished.

胡雪岩戴了一顶风帽，帽檐压得极低，带了一个叫阿福的伶俐小厮，
Hu put on a hooded winter cap, pulling its brim very low, and took a quick-witted servant called A’fu with him.

打开花园中一道很少开启的便门，出门是一条长巷，
They opened a seldom-used side gate in the garden and stepped into a long lane.

巷子里没有什么行人，就是有，亦因这天冷得格外厉害，而且西北风很大，都是低头疾行，
Few people were about, and those who were kept their heads down and hurried along: the cold was unusually severe, with a strong northwesterly wind.

谁也没有发觉。这位平时出门，前呼后拥的胡财神，竞会踽踽凉凉地只带一个小厮步行上街。
Nobody noticed that the God of Wealth, ordinarily surrounded by attendants whenever he went out, was trudging forlornly through the streets with only a single servant.

“阿福，”胡雪岩问道：“周老爷住在哪里，你晓得不晓得？”
“A’fu, do you know where Mr Zhou lives?”

“怎么不晓得？
“Of course.

他住在龙舌嘴。”
At Longshezui.”

“对！龙知嘴。”胡雪岩说：
“Yes, Longshezui.

“你走快一点，通知他我要去。”
Go ahead and tell him I am coming.”

“是。”
“Yes, sir.

阿福问道：“如果他不在家呢？”
What if he is not at home?”

“这么冷的天，他不会出门的。”
“He will not have gone out in weather like this.

胡雪岩又说：“万一不在，你留句话，回来了到城隍山药王庙旁边的馆子里来寻我。”
But if he has, leave word that when he returns he should come and find me at the restaurant beside Yaowang Temple on City God Hill.”

阿福答应一声，迈开大步往前走。
A’fu strode ahead.

胡雪岩安步当车，缓缓行去。
Hu followed slowly on foot.

刚进了龙舌嘴，只见阿福已经走回头路了，
He had barely entered Longshezui when he saw A’fu coming back.

发现主人，急急迎了上来。
Catching sight of his master, the servant hurried towards him.

“怎么样，
“Well?

不在家？”
Is he out?”

“在！”
“No!”

阿福回头一指：
A’fu pointed behind him.

“那不是！”
“There he is!”

原来周少棠特为赶了来迎接。
Zhou had come out specially to meet him.

见了面，胡雪岩摇摇手，使个眼色。
Hu waved a hand and gave him a warning look.

周少棠会意，他是怕在声招呼，惊动了路人，所以见了面，低声问道，“你怎么会来的？”
Understanding that he feared a loud greeting would attract passers-by, Zhou asked quietly, “What brings you here?”


这话问得胡雪岩无以为答，
Hu could find no answer.

笑笑答说：
He smiled.

“你没有想到吧？”
“You did not expect me, did you?”

“真是没有想到，”
“Certainly not.”

胡雪岩发觉已经有人在注意了，便放快了脚步，反而走在周少棠前面，
Noticing that people were already looking, Hu quickened his pace and went ahead of Zhou.

一直到巷口才停住步，抬头看了一下说：
Only at the mouth of the lane did he stop and look up.

“你府上有二十年没有来过了。
“It is twenty years since I visited your house.

我记得是坐南朝北第五家。”
I remember: the fifth house, facing north.”

“搬到对面去了，
“We moved to the other side.

坐北朝南第四家。”
The fourth house, facing south.”

“不错，不错！
“Of course!

你后来买了你对面的房子，
You bought the house opposite.

不过，我还是头一回来。”
This will be my first visit there.”

“这房子风水不好。”
“The house has bad feng shui.”

何以风水不好？
Why bad?

胡雪岩一时无法追问，因为已到了周家。
Hu had no chance to ask, for they had arrived.

周少棠的妻子，胡雪岩还是二十几年前见过，
He had last seen Mrs Zhou more than twenty years before.

记得很清楚的是，生得非常富态，
What he remembered most clearly was her substantial figure.

如今更加发福，一双小足撑持着水牛般的身躯，行动非常艰难，
She had grown still stouter: a pair of tiny bound feet supported a body as large as a buffalo, making movement difficult.

但因胡雪岩“降尊纡贵”，在她便觉受宠若惊，满脸堆笑，非常殷勤。
Yet she was overwhelmed that Hu should condescend to visit, and attended to him with a face full of smiles.


“不敢当，不敢当！”
“Please, please!”

胡雪岩看亲自来敬茶，摇摇晃晃，脚步不稳，真担心她会摔交，
Watching her sway unsteadily as she brought tea herself, Hu genuinely feared she might fall.

所以老实说道：“周大嫂，不要招呼，
“Sister-in-Law Zhou, do not trouble yourself.

你法身太重，掼一交不是当耍的。”
You are too heavy to risk a tumble. It would be no laughing matter.”

“是不是！
“Exactly!

你真好省省了。
Do sit down,” Zhou said.

胡大先生肯到我们这里来，是当我们自己人看待，
“Mr Hu comes here because he treats us as his own people.

你一客气，反而见外了。”
All this ceremony only makes him an outsider.

周少棠又说：“有事叫阿春、阿秋来做。”
Let A’chun and A’qiu do whatever needs doing.”

原来周少棠从受了胡雪岩的提携，境遇日佳，
Since Hu had helped him advance, Zhou’s circumstances had steadily improved.

他又喜欢讲排场，老夫妇两口，倒有四个佣人，
Fond of display, he kept four servants for the elderly couple alone.

阿春、阿秋是十年前买来的两个丫头，如今都快二十岁了。
A’chun and A’qiu had been bought ten years earlier and were now nearly twenty.

“恭敬不如从命。”
“Better to obey than insist on ceremony.”

周太太气喘吁吁地坐了下来，跟胡雪岩寒暄：
Mrs Zhou sat down, panting, and asked after his family.

“老太太精神倒还健旺？”
“Is the old lady still strong?”

“托福，托福。”
“Very well, thank you.”

“胡太太好？”
“And Mrs Hu?”

“还好。”
“Well enough.”

看样子还要问螺蛳太太跟姨太太。
She seemed about to ask after Madam Luosi and the other concubines.

周少棠已经知道了胡家这天上午发生了什么事，
Zhou already knew what had happened in Hu’s household that morning.

怕她妻子过于罗嗦，再问下去会搞得场面尴尬，所以急忙打岔，
Afraid she would keep questioning him and make matters awkward, he hastily intervened.

“胡大先生在我们这里吃饭。”
“Mr Hu will dine with us.

他说：“自己预备来不及了，
There is no time to prepare everything ourselves.

我看只有叫菜来请客。”
We shall have to order some dishes.”


“少棠，”胡雪岩开口了：“你听我说，
“Shaotang, listen.

你不要费事！
Do not put yourself to any trouble.

说句老实话，山珍海味我也吃厌了，
To tell the truth, I have had my fill of rare delicacies.

尤其是这个时候，你弄好了，我也吃不下。
Especially now, I could not eat them however well you prepared them.

我今天来，是想到我们从前在一起的日子，吃得落，困得着，逍遥自在，真同神仙一样，
I came because I remembered our old days together, when we could eat heartily, sleep soundly and live without a care, like immortals.

所以，此刻我不觉得自己是在做客人，
I do not feel that I am a guest here.

你一客气，就不是我来的本意了。
If you treat me ceremoniously, it defeats the purpose of my visit.

你懂不懂我的意思？”
Do you understand?”

“本来不懂，你一说我自然就懂了。”
“I did not before, but now you have explained, naturally I do.”

周少棠想了一下说：
Zhou thought for a moment.

“可惜，张胖子死掉了，
“What a pity Fat Zhang is dead.

不然邀他来一起吃‘木榔豆腐’，听他说荤笑话，
Otherwise we could invite him for mulang tofu and listen to his dirty jokes.

哪怕外头下大雪，都不觉得冷了。”
Even if it were snowing hard outside, we would not feel cold.”

提起张胖子，胡雪岩不免伤感，怀旧之念，亦就越发炽烈，
The mention of Fat Zhang saddened Hu and sharpened his longing for the past.

“当年的老朋友还有哪几个？”
“Which of our old friends are still around?

他说：“真想邀他们来叙一叙。”
I should love to gather them for a talk.”

“这也是改天的事了。”周少棠说：
“That will have to wait for another day.

“我倒想起一个人，
But I can think of one man.

要不要邀他来吃酒？”
Shall we invite him for a drink?”

“哪个？”
“Who?”

“乌先生。”
“Mr Wu.”


胡雪岩想了一下，欣然同意：
After a moment, Hu gladly agreed.

“好的、好的。”
“Yes, yes.

他说：“我倒又想起一个人，郑俊生。”
And I have thought of another: Zheng Junsheng.”

这郑俊生是安康名家——杭州人称滩簧为“安康”，生旦净末丑，五个人坐着弹唱，而以丑为尊，称之为“小花脸”，
Zheng was a celebrated performer of an’kang, the Hangzhou name for tanhuang: five seated performers sang and played the male, female, painted-face, older-male and comic roles, with the comic performer, known as the Little Painted Face, taking precedence.

郑俊生就是唱小花脸的。
That was Zheng’s role.

此人亦是当年与胡雪岩、周少棠一起凑份子喝酒的朋友。
In the old days he had often pooled money with Hu and Zhou for wine.

只为胡雪岩青云直上，身分悬殊，郑俊生自惭形秽，不愿来往，
But as Hu rose swiftly in the world, the gulf between their positions had made Zheng feel inferior, and he had withdrawn.

胡家有喜庆堂会，他亦从不承应。
He never even accepted engagements at Hu’s household celebrations.

胡雪岩一想起这件事，便觉耿耿于怀，这一天很想弥补这个缺憾。
This had always troubled Hu, and today he wanted to make amends.

周少棠知道他的心事，点点头说：
Understanding his feelings, Zhou nodded.

“好的，我同他有来往，
“I still see him.

等我叫人去请他。”当即将他用了已经十年的佣人贵生叫了来吩咐：
Let me send for him.” He called Guisheng, a servant of ten years’ standing.

“你到安康郑先生家去一趟，
“Go to Mr Zheng, the an’kang performer.

说我请他来要有要紧事谈，
Say I need to speak to him about something important.

回头再去请乌先生来吃酒。
Then invite Mr Wu to drink with us.

喔，你到了郑先生那里，千万不要说家里有客。”
And when you are at Zheng’s, be sure not to mention that I have a guest.”

这是怕郑俊生知道胡雪岩在此不肯来，特意这样叮嘱。
He feared Zheng would refuse to come if he knew Hu was there.


交代完了，周少棠告个罪，又到后面跟周太太略略商量如何款客。
After giving these instructions, Zhou excused himself and went to the back to discuss the meal briefly with his wife.

然后在堂屋里坐定了陪胡雪岩围炉闲话。
Then he returned to sit with Hu by the brazier in the main hall.

“你今天看过《申报》了？”客人先开口。
“Have you read Shenbao today?” his guest asked first.

“大致看了看。”
“Glanced through it.

周少棠说：“八个字的考语：加油添酱，胡说八道。
My verdict is simple: embroidered stories and utter nonsense.

你不要理他们。
Pay no attention.”

““我不在乎。
“I do not care.

你们看是骂我；我自己看，是他们捧我。”
You see it as abuse; I see it as praise.”

“你看得开就好。”
“As long as you can take it lightly.

周少棠说：“有句话，叫做‘百足之虫，死而不僵’。
There is a saying: a hundred-legged insect does not fall stiff even when dead.

你只要看得开，着实还有几年快活日子过。
If you can take things as they come, you still have several comfortable years ahead.”

““看得开，也不过是自己骗自己的话。
“Taking things lightly is only a way of deceiving oneself.

这一个多月，我常常会有个怪念头，哪里去寻一种药，吃了会叫人拿过去忘记掉。”
For more than a month I have often had a strange thought: where could I find a medicine that would make me forget the past?

胡雪岩又说：“当然不能连自己的时辰八字、父母兄弟都忘记掉，

It would be best if I could cut out a stretch of my life.”

“Which stretch?”

“从我认识王有龄起，到今天为止，
“From the day I met Wang Youling until now.

这段日子切掉，回到我们从前在一起的辰光，
Cut that out and return to the days when we were together.

那就象神仙一样了。
That would be like living among immortals.”

“周少棠的心情跟他不同，
Zhou did not share his feelings.

觉得说回到以前过苦日子的辰光象神仙一样，未免言过其实。
To describe a return to their former hardships as an immortal’s life seemed a considerable exaggeration.

所以笑笑不作声。
He merely smiled and said nothing.


“少棠，”胡雪岩又问：“你道我现在这种境况，要做两年什么事，才会觉得做人有点乐趣？”
“Shaotang, in my present circumstances, what could I do over the next couple of years to find some pleasure in being alive?”

周少棠想了好一会儿，而且是很认真地在想，但终于还是苦笑着摇摇头说：
Zhou considered for a long time, quite earnestly, but could only shake his head with a bitter smile.

“说老实话，我想不出，
“Honestly, I cannot think of anything.

只有劝你看开点。”
I can only advise you to take things less to heart.”

“我自己倒想得一样。”
“I have thought of something myself.”

“喔！”
“Oh?”

周少棠倒是出自衷心地想与胡雪岩同甘苦，
Zhou sincerely wished to share Hu’s fortunes, good or bad.

只是身分悬殊，谈不到此，但心情是相同的，
Their unequal positions had put that beyond his reach, but the feeling was there.

““你不要心急，
“Do not hurry me.

我先讲一桩事情你听。”
First let me tell you something that happened.”

他讲的就是在老同和的那一番奇遇。讲完了又谈他的感想：

“Probably because you considered it your duty.

“不错，
“Exactly.

这是钱用我，不是我用钱，

所以不觉得发财之可贵……”

“啊，啊！我懂了。”周少棠插嘴说道：

“You must first want to do something that lack of money prevents.

“That is only half the answer.

机会岂不是很好。
Could there be a better opportunity?

哪知道你把钱送了去，人家不受。

这就是看人。”
That is what I mean.”

“为啥呢？”周少棠说：
“Why would he refuse?

“正在需要的时候，又是好朋友，

There is no reason.”

“不受就是不受，
“Refusal is refusal.

没有道理好讲的。”
Reason has nothing to do with it.”

Zhou shook his head repeatedly. “Then he must have something wrong with him—an eccentric who understands nothing of human kindness.”

“What if it were you?”

“I should certainly accept.”

“好！”
“Good!”

胡雪岩笑着一指，

“这话是你自己说的，
“Those are your own words.

到时候你不要赖！”

周少棠愕然，

“我赖啥？”
“Go back on what?

他说：“胡大先生，你的话说得我莫名其妙。”
Mr Hu, you have completely baffled me.”

胡雪岩笑笑不答，只问：“乌先生不是住得很近吗？”
Hu only smiled and asked, “Does Mr Wu not live quite nearby?”

原来乌先生本来住在螺蛳门外。
Mr Wu had originally lived outside Luosi Gate.

当年螺狮太太进胡家大门，周少棠帮忙办喜事，认识了乌先生，
When Madam Luosi entered Hu’s household, Zhou had helped with the wedding arrangements and met him.

两人气味相投，结成至交。
They had taken to each other and become close friends.

螺蛳太太当乌先生“娘家人”，劝他搬进城来住，有事可以就近商量。
Madam Luosi regarded Wu as one of her own family and urged him to move inside the city, where she could consult him more readily.

乌先生便托周少棠觅屋，在一条有名曲折的十三弯巷买的房子，
He asked Zhou to find him a house and bought one in Shisanwan Lane, famous for its many turns.

两家不远，不时过从，乌太太与周太太还结拜成了姐妹。
Their homes were close, they visited frequently, and Mrs Wu and Mrs Zhou had even become sworn sisters.

胡雪岩是因为周少棠提议邀他来喝酒，触机想起一件事，正好跟他商量，因而有此一问。
Zhou’s suggestion of inviting Wu had reminded Hu of something he could discuss with him, which prompted his question.


“快来了，快来了，”
“He will be here soon.”

果不其然，不多片刻，乌先生来了，
Sure enough, Wu arrived before long.

发现胡雪岩在座，顿感意外，殷勤致候，但却不便深谈。
Surprised to find Hu there, he greeted him warmly, but it was not convenient to talk at length.

“少棠，”胡雪岩说：“我要借你的书房一用，
“Shaotang, may I borrow your study?

跟乌先生说几句话。”
I should like a few words with Mr Wu.”

啊唷，胡大先生，你不要笑我了，
“Oh, Mr Hu, do not make fun of me!

我那个记记帐的地方，哪里好叫书房？
How can my little bookkeeping room be called a study?”

““只要有书，就是书房。”
“Where there are books, there is a study.”

“书是有的，时宪书。”时宪书便是历本。
“There is one book: the imperial almanac.”

虽然周少棠这样自嘲地说，但他的书房却还布置得并不算太俗气，
Despite Zhou’s self-mockery, his study was not badly furnished.

又叫阿春端来一个火盆，也预备了茶，然后亲自将房门关上，好让他们从容密谈？
He told A’chun to bring a brazier and prepare tea, then shut the door himself so that the two could speak privately at ease.

“乌先生，我家里的事，你晓不晓得？”
“Mr Wu, do you know what has happened in my household?”

“啥事情？
“What is it?

我一点都不晓得。”
I know nothing.”

乌先生的神情显得有些紧张不安。
Wu looked rather anxious.

“我把她们都打发走了。”
“I have sent them all away.”

“呃，”
“Ah.”

乌先生想了一下问：“几位？”
After a moment Wu asked, “How many?”

“一共十个人。”
“Ten altogether.”

胡雪岩的花园中，有名的“十二楼”，
Hu’s garden was famous for its Twelve Chambers.

遣走十个，剩下两个，

Wu next selected Twelve Letters of the Su Family, containing letters by Su Laoquan, Dongpo, Ziyou and Dongpo’s youngest son Shudang.

Under Hu’s direction, the maids opened three chests, found all the works intended for Wu and tied them into a bundle.

The ten thousand taels for the charitable hall had been assembled, all in banknotes.

即时点交乌先生收讫，

“Three cups and sets of chopsticks,” Hu told A’yun.

“Sit with us.”

她视乌先生如亲属长辈，不必有礼仪上的男女之别。
She regarded Wu as an elder of her own family, so there was no need for the formal separation of men and women.

胡雪岩仍旧照例喝睡前的药酒。

“At her own request.

“That will not be the case,” Madam Luosi said.

His last night with Mistress Zhu was more than two months ago.

至于……”
As for…”

She had been about to joke that she could not know whether Hu and Mistress Zhu had been intimate in private since.

But nobody was in a joking mood, so she swallowed the words.

“还是小心点的好。

“Very well.

Where will she stay for this month or more?”

“At my house,” Wu said.

“Then we can be still easier in our minds.”

胡雪岩作个切断的手势，
Hu made a decisive gesture.

“这件事就算这样子定规了。”
“That settles it.”

“I understand,” Madam Luosi said.

“I shall arrange everything.”

“Mr Hu, do you believe this fall is final?”

“What can I do but accept it?”

“I do not accept it!”

Madam Luosi put in.

“不然，
“Not so.

If you can stand upright, you can walk,” Wu said.

A comeback depends on human effort.”

你说东山再起，我就不晓得东山在哪里？”

“No.
