第十二章 城狐社鼠｜CHAPTER TWELVE: FOXES IN THE CITY WALL, RATS IN THE SHRINE


胡雪岩讲的是一个掘藏的故事。
The story Hu Xueyan told concerned the digging up of buried treasure.

凡是大乱以后，抚缉流亡，秩序渐定，往往有人突然之间，发了大财，
After a great upheaval, when refugees were being brought home and order was gradually restored, people would often become rich overnight.

十九是掘到了藏宝的缘故。
Nine times out of ten, they had found a hidden hoard.

埋藏金银财定的不外两种人。
There were two sorts of people who buried gold, silver, and valuables.

一种是原为富室，遇到刀兵之灾，举家逃难，
The first were wealthy families fleeing the ravages of war.

只能带些易于变卖的金珠之类，现银古玩，装入坚固不易坏的容器中，找一个难为人所注目的地方，深掘埋藏，等待乱后重回家园，掘取应用。
They could take only readily saleable things such as gold and pearls; silver and antiques were packed into durable containers and buried deep in some inconspicuous place, to be recovered when they returned after the war.

如果这家人家，尽室遇害，或者知道这个秘密的家长、老仆，不在人世而又没有机会留下遗言，这笔财富，便长埋地下，知不多少年以后，为哪个命中该发横财的人所得。
If the whole family was killed, or the head of the household or old servant who knew the secret died without an opportunity to pass it on, the fortune remained underground until, years later, someone destined for a windfall came upon it.

再一种藏宝的，就是已经横财就手之人，只以局势大变，无法安享，
The other sort had already acquired a fortune but could not enjoy it because the situation had suddenly changed.

暂且埋藏，徐图后计。
They buried it for the time being and waited for a better opportunity.

同治初年的太平军，便不知埋藏了多少财宝。
In the early Tongzhi years, the Taiping troops buried untold quantities of treasure.

太平军一据通都大邑，各自找大家巨室以安，名为“打公馆”。
Whenever they occupied an important city, they took over the houses of wealthy families as their quarters, a practice known as “setting up a mansion.”

凡是被打过“公馆”的人家，重归家园后，每每有人登门求见，说“府上”某处有“长毛”埋藏的财物，
When the owners returned, visitors would often arrive claiming that the “Longhairs” had buried valuables somewhere on the premises.

如果主人家信了他的话，接下来便是谈分帐，或者对半，或者四六——主人家拿六成，指点的人拿四成，最少也得三七分帐。
If the owner believed them, the next question was how to divide the proceeds: half each, perhaps, or sixty percent for the owner and forty for the informant, whose share would be at least thirty percent.

掘藏有获的固然也有，但投机的居多，
Some searches did turn up treasure, but most such visitors were opportunists.

反正掘不到无所损，落得根据流言去瞎撞瞎骗了。
They lost nothing if nothing was found and might as well try their luck with rumors and deception.


太平军败走后的杭州城，亦与其他各地一样，人们纷纷掘藏。
Hangzhou was no different after the Taiping troops were driven out: everyone seemed to be digging for treasure.

胡雪岩有个表叔名叫朱宝如，颇热中于此，
Hu Xueyan had a cousin of one of his parents, whom he called Uncle, named Zhu Baoru, who was particularly keen on it.

他的妻子便是螺蛳太太口中的“朱老太太”，
His wife was the “Old Madam Zhu” of whom Madam Luosi had spoken.

相貌忠厚而心计极深，她跟她丈夫说：“掘藏要有路子。
Honest-looking but deeply calculating, she told her husband, “You need a way in if you want to find treasure.

现在有条路子，
There is one now.

你去好好留心，说不定时来运转，会发横财。”
Keep your eyes open, and your luck may turn.”

“你说，路子在哪里？”
“What way?”

“善后局。”
“The Reconstruction Bureau.

她说：“雪岩是你表侄、
Xueyan is your cousin’s son.

你跟他要个善后局的差使，他一定答应。
Ask him for a post there, and he is bound to agree.

不过，你不要怕烦，
But you must be willing to take trouble.

要同难民混在一起，听他们谈天说地，静悄悄在旁边听；
Mix with the refugees and listen quietly while they talk about anything and everything.

一定会听出东西来。”
You are certain to hear something useful.”

朱宝如很服他妻子，
Zhu Baoru had great respect for his wife’s judgment.

当下如教去看胡雪岩，自愿担任照料难民的职司。
He went to Hu Xueyan as instructed and volunteered to look after the refugees.

善后局的职位有好有坏，
Some jobs at the bureau were better than others.

最好的是管认领妇女，有那年轻貌美，而父兄死于干戈流离之中，孤苦伶仃的，有人冒充亲属来领，
The most lucrative was handling claims for women who had lost their fathers and brothers in the fighting and flight. If a young, attractive woman was alone in the world, someone might come to claim her under the pretense of being a relative.

只要跟被领的说通了，一笔谢礼，银子上百。
Once an understanding had been reached with her, the gratuity could run to a hundred taels or more.

其次是管伙食，官采实，亦有极肥的油水。
Next came food supplies and purchasing, both rich sources of illicit profit.

此外，抄抄写写、造造名册，差使亦很轻松。
Even copying documents and compiling registers were easy work.

只有照料难民，琐碎烦杂而一无好处，
Only caring for the refugees was a tangle of troublesome details with nothing to be gained.

没有人肯干。
No one wanted it.

而朱宝如居然自告奋勇，胡雪岩非常高兴。立即照派。
Hu Xueyan was delighted that Zhu Baoru had volunteered and appointed him at once.


朱宝如受妻之教，耐着心跟衣衫褴褛、气味恶浊的难民打交道，应付种种难题，细心听他们在闲谈之中所透露的种种秘闻，
Following his wife’s advice, Zhu Baoru patiently dealt with the ragged, foul-smelling refugees, resolved their various difficulties, and listened carefully to the secrets that slipped out in casual conversation.

感情处得很好。
He established excellent relations with them.

有一天有个三十多岁江西口音的难民，悄悄向朱宝如说：“朱先生，我这半个多月住下来，看你老人家是很忠厚的人，
One day a refugee in his thirties, who spoke with a Jiangxi accent, quietly said to him, “Mr Zhu, I have been here for more than half a month, and I can see that you are an honest, kindly elder.

我想到你府上去谈谈。”
I should like to come to your home for a talk.”

“喔，”
“Oh?”

朱宝如印象中，此人沉默寡言、亦从来没有来麻烦过他，
Zhu Baoru remembered him as a man of few words who had never caused any trouble.

所以连他的姓都不知道，
He did not even know his surname.

当即问说：“贵姓？”
“What is your name?”

“我姓程。”
“Cheng.”

“程老弟，你有啥话，现在这里没有人，你尽管说。”
“Brother Cheng, there is nobody here. Whatever you have to say, go ahead.”

“不！
“No.

话很多，
There is a great deal to say.

要到府上去谈才方便。”
It would be more convenient at your home.”

朱宝如想到了妻子的话。心中一动，便将此人带回家。
Remembering his wife’s words, Zhu Baoru felt a stir of anticipation and took him home.

姓程的进门放下包裹，解下一条腰带，带子里有十几个金戒指。
Cheng put down his bundle, untied his belt, and produced more than a dozen gold rings hidden inside it.


“朱先生、朱太太，”此人说道：“实不相瞒，我做过‘长毛’，
“Mr and Mrs Zhu,” he said, “to tell you the truth, I was a Longhair.

现在弃暗投明，想拜你们两老做干爹、干妈！
I have now left the darkness for the light, and I should like to take you as my adoptive parents.

不知道你们两老，肯不肯收我？
Would you be willing to accept me?”

“这件事来得有些突兀，
The request was rather sudden.

朱宝如还在踌躇，他妻子看出包裹里还有花样，当即慨然答应：“我们有个儿子，年纪同你差不多，如今不在眼前。
Zhu Baoru was still hesitating, but his wife suspected that the bundle held more surprises and readily replied, “We have a son about your age, but he is away.

遇见你也是缘分，
Fate has brought us together.

拜干爹、干妈的话，暂且不提，
We need not discuss becoming your adoptive parents just yet.

你先住下来再说。”
Stay with us first.”

“不！
“No.

两老要收了我，认我当儿子，我有些话才敢说，
Only if you accept me as a son will I dare tell you certain things.

而且拜了两老，我改姓为朱，
And once I become your son, I can take the surname Zhu.

以后一切都方便。”
That will make everything easier.”

于是，朱宝如夫妻悄悄商量了一会，决定收这个干儿子，
The couple conferred in private and agreed to adopt him.

改姓为朱，由于生于午年，起了个名字叫家驹。
His surname was changed to Zhu, and since he had been born in the year of the Horse, they gave him the name Jiaju, “the family’s colt.”

那十几个金戒指，便成了他孝敬义父母的见面礼。
The gold rings became his introductory gift to his new parents.

有了钱，什么事都好办了。
With money, everything was easy.

朱宝如去卖掉两个金戒指，为朱家驹打扮得焕然一新。
Zhu Baoru sold two of the rings and fitted Zhu Jiaju out in new clothes.

同时沽酒买肉，畅叙“天伦”。
He also bought wine and meat, and they enjoyed a convivial celebration of their new family ties.


朱家驹仿佛从来没有过过这样的好日子，
Zhu Jiaju seemed never to have known such comfortable days.

显得非常高兴，一面大块吃肉、大碗喝酒，一面谈他做“长毛”的经过。
Delighted, he ate great chunks of meat, drank bowlfuls of wine, and described his life among the Longhairs.

他是个孤儿，在他江西家乡，为太平军挑辎重，
An orphan, he had carried baggage for the Taiping troops in his native Jiangxi.

到了浙江衙州。太平军放他回家，
When they reached Quzhou in Zhejiang, they released him to go home.

他说无家可归，愿意做“小长毛”。
He said that he had no home and wished to become a “little Longhair.”

这就样由衢州到杭州，但不久便又开拔了。
Thus he went with them from Quzhou to Hangzhou, though they soon moved on again.

那是咸丰十年春天的事，
This was in the spring of the tenth year of Xianfeng.

太平军的忠王李秀成，为解“天京”之围，使了一条围赵救燕之计。
To relieve the siege of the Heavenly Capital, the Taiping Loyal King, Li Xiucheng, used the strategy of besieging Zhao to rescue Yan.

二月初由皖南进攻浙江，目的是要将围金陵的浙军总兵张玉良所部引回来，减轻压力。
Early in the second month he marched from southern Anhui into Zhejiang, intending to draw back the Zhejiang troops under Brigade Commander Zhang Yuliang, then besieging Jinling, and so ease the pressure.

二月二十七李秀成攻入杭州，
Li entered Hangzhou on the twenty-seventh day of that month.

到了三月初三，张玉良的援军赶到，李秀成因为计已得施，又怕张玉良断他的归路，便弃杭州西走，
When Zhang’s reinforcements arrived on the third day of the third month, Li withdrew westward: his purpose had been achieved, and he feared that Zhang would cut off his retreat.

前后只得五天的工夫。
The occupation had lasted only five days.


朱家驹那时便在李秀成部下，转战各地，兵败失散，为另一支太平军所收容。
Zhu Jiaju served under Li and fought in various places until a defeat separated him from his unit and another Taiping force took him in.

他的长官叫吴天德，是他同一个村庄的人，极重乡谊。
His new commander, Wu Tiande, came from the same village and valued such ties.

所以朱家驹跟他的另一个同乡王培利，成了吴天德的贴身“亲兵”，深获信任。
Zhu Jiaju and another fellow villager, Wang Peili, became his trusted personal guards.

以后吴天德在一次战役中受了重伤，临死以前跟朱家驹与王培利说：“忠王第二次攻进杭州，我在那里驻扎了半年，
Later, mortally wounded in battle, Wu told them, “When the Loyal King took Hangzhou the second time, I was stationed there for half a year.

‘公馆’打在东城金洞桥。
My quarters were at Jindong Bridge in the eastern city.

后来调走了，忠王的军令很严，我的东面带不走，
When I was transferred, his orders were so strict that I could not take my things.

埋在那里，以后始终没有机会再到杭州。
I buried them there and have never had a chance to return.

现在我要死了，有这样东西交给你们。”
Now that I am dying, I have something to give you.”

说着，他从贴肉的口袋中，掏出一个油纸包，里面是一张藏宝的图。
From a pocket next to his skin he took an oilpaper packet containing a treasure map.

他关照朱家驹与王培利，设法找机会到杭州去掘藏，如果掘到了，作三股分，一股要送回他江西的老家。
He told them to find an opportunity to dig up the hoard and divide it into three shares, sending one to his old home in Jiangxi.

又叫朱家驹、王培利结为兄弟，对天盟誓，相约不得负义，否则必遭天谴。
He also made them swear brotherhood and vow before Heaven never to betray their trust, on pain of divine punishment.


“后来，我同我那位拜兄商量，把地图一分为二，各拿半张，
“Afterward, my sworn elder brother and I agreed to cut the map in two and keep half each.

我们也一直在一起。
We stayed together all along.

这回左大人克复杭州，机会来了，
Now that Lord Zuo has recovered Hangzhou, our opportunity has come.

因为我到杭州来过，所以由我冒充难民，行来探路，
Since I had been here before, I came ahead disguised as a refugee to find the place.

等找到了地方，再通知王培利，商量怎么下手。”
Once I had located it, I meant to send for Wang Peili and discuss how to proceed.”

“那么，”朱宝如问：“你那姓王的拜把兄弟在哪里？”
“Where is this sworn brother of yours?” Zhu Baoru asked.

“在上海。
“In Shanghai.

只要我一封信去，马上就来。”
One letter from me will bring him at once.”

“你的把兄弟，也是自己人。”朱宝如的老婆说：
“Your sworn brother is one of our own too,” Mrs Zhu said.

“来嘛！
“Send for him!

叫他来嘛！”
Tell him to come!”

“慢，慢！”
“Wait, wait!”

朱宝如摇摇手，
Zhu Baoru waved his hand.

“我们先来商量。
“Let us discuss this first.

你那张图呢？”
Where is your map?”

“图只有半张。”
“I have only half.”

朱家驹也是从贴肉的口袋中，取出一个油纸包，
Zhu Jiaju, too, took an oilpaper packet from a pocket next to his skin.

打开一看，半张地图保存得很好，
The half-map was well preserved.

摊开在桌上抹平一看，是一张图的上半张，下端剪成锯齿形。
Spread flat on the table, it proved to be the upper half, with its bottom edge cut in a zigzag.

想来就是“合符”的意思；另外那半张，上端也是锯齿形，两个半张凑成一起，吻合无间，才是吴天德交来的原因。
Presumably this was intended to work like matching halves of a tally: the top edge of the other half was similarly cut, and only when the two fitted perfectly together could they be identified as Wu Tiande’s original map.


“这半张是地址。”朱家驹说：
“This half gives the location,” Zhu Jiaju said.

“下半张才是埋宝的细图。”
“The lower half shows exactly where the treasure is buried.”

这也可以理解，
That made sense.

朱家驹在杭州住过五天，所以由他带着这有地址的半张，先来寻觅吴天德当初“打公馆”的原址。
Having spent five days in Hangzhou, he had brought the half with the address to find Wu Tiande’s former quarters.

朱宝如细看图上，
Zhu Baoru examined it closely.

注明两个地点，一个是金洞桥，一个是万安桥；
Two places were named, Jindong Bridge and Wan’an Bridge.

另外有两个小方块，其中一个下注“关帝庙”，又画一个箭头，注明：“往南约三十步，坐东朝西。”
There were also two small squares, one labeled “Temple of Lord Guan,” with an arrow and the note: “About thirty paces south; faces west.”

没有任何字样的那一个小方块，不言可知便是藏宝之处。
The unlabeled square was plainly the house containing the treasure.

“这不难找。”朱宝如问：
“That should not be hard to find.

“找到了以后呢？”
And once we find it?”

“或者租，或者买。”
“We rent it or buy it.”

“买？”
“Buy it?”

朱宝如踌躇着，
Zhu Baoru hesitated.

“是你们长毛打过公馆的房子，当然不会小，
“A house used by your Longhairs as a mansion can hardly be small.

买起来恐怕不便宜。”
It may cost a great deal.”

“不要紧。”朱家驹说：
“That does not matter.

“王培利会带钱来。”
Wang Peili will bring money.”

“那好！”朱宝如很高兴地说，
“Good!” Zhu Baoru said happily.

“这件事交给我来办。”
“Leave this to me.”

“家驹！”他老婆问说：“不晓得里面埋了点啥东西？”
His wife asked, “Jiaju, what exactly is buried there?”

“东西很多……”
“A great many things…”

据说，埋藏之物有四五百两金叶子、大批的珠宝首饰。
There were said to be four or five hundred taels’ weight of gold leaf, together with quantities of jewels and ornaments.

埋藏的方法非常讲究，
The method of burial had been elaborate.

珠宝首饰先用绵纸包好，置于瓷坛之中，用油灰封口，
The jewelry was wrapped in soft paper and placed in porcelain jars sealed with putty.

然后装入铁箱，外填石灰，以防潮气，
These went into an iron chest, surrounded by lime to keep out damp.

最后再将铁箱置放于大木箱中，埋入地下。
Finally the iron chest was placed inside a large wooden chest and buried.


朱宝如夫妇听得这些话，满心欢喜。
The couple listened with delight.

当夜秘密商议，
That night they conferred secretly.

怕突然之间收了一个来历不明的干儿子，邻居或许会猜疑，决定第二天搬家，搬到东城去住，为的是便于到金洞桥去觅藏宝之地。
A sudden adoption of a son whose background was unknown might arouse the neighbors’ suspicions, so they decided to move the next day, to the eastern city, where they could more conveniently search around Jindong Bridge.

等迁居己定，朱宝如便命义子写信到上海，通知王培利到杭州，
Once settled, Zhu Baoru told his adopted son to write to Shanghai for Wang Peili.

然后到金洞桥去踏勘。
Then he prepared to inspect the neighborhood.

“家驹，”他说：“你是外乡口音，到那里去查访，变成形迹可疑，诸多不便。
“Jiaju, with your out-of-town accent, you would attract suspicion if you went asking questions.

你留在家里，
Stay here.

我一个人去。”朱家驹欣然从命，由朱宝如一个人去悄俏查访。
I shall go alone.” Zhu Jiaju readily agreed.

万安桥是杭州城内第一座大桥，为漕船所经之地，桥洞极高，
Wan’an Bridge was the foremost bridge within Hangzhou, with a high arch under which grain boats passed.

桥东桥西各有一座关帝庙，
There was a temple to Lord Guan on either side.

依照与金洞桥的方位来看，图上所指的关帝庙，应该是桥东的那一座，
From their positions relative to Jindong Bridge, the one on the map must be the eastern temple.

庙旁就是一家茶馆，
Beside it was a teahouse.

朱宝如泡了一壶茶，从早晨坐到中午，静静地听茶客高谈阔论。
Zhu Baoru ordered a pot and sat from morning until noon, quietly listening to the patrons’ expansive talk.

如是一连三天，终于听到了他想要听的话。当然他想听的便是有关太平军两次攻陷杭州，在这一带活动的情形。
After three days he finally heard what he wanted: accounts of the Taiping troops’ activities during their two occupations.

自万安桥到金洞桥这个范围之内，太平军住过的军宅，一共有五处，
Between Wan’an and Jindong bridges, they had occupied five houses.

其中方位与藏宝图上相合的一处。主人姓严，是个进士。
The one whose position matched the map belonged to a jinshi named Yan.


这就容易找了。
Now it was easy.

朱宝如出了茶店，看关帝庙前面，自北而南两条巷子，一条宽，一条窄，
Leaving the teahouse, Zhu Baoru found two lanes running south from in front of the temple, one broad and one narrow.

进入宽的那条，以平常的脚步走了三十步，看到一块刻有“泰山石敢当”字样的石碑，
He took the broad lane, walked thirty ordinary paces, and came to a stone inscribed with a Mount Tai protective formula.

以此为坐标，细细搜索坐东朝西的房屋，很快地发现有一家人家的门楣上，悬着一块粉底黑字的匾额，赫然大书：“进士第”三字，
Using it as his reference point, he examined the west-facing houses and soon saw a plaque with bold black characters on a pale ground: “Residence of a Jinshi.”

自然就是严进士家了。
This must be Yan’s home.

朱宝如不敢造次，先来回走了两趟，一面走，一面观察环境，
He did not act rashly but walked past twice, studying the surroundings.

这一处“进士第”的房子不是顶讲究，但似乎不小。
