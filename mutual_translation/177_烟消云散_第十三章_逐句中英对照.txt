第十三章 烟消云散｜CHAPTER THIRTEEN: VANISHING INTO THIN AIR


胡雪岩谈朱宝如夫妇的故事，话到此处，忽然看着乌先生问道：
At this point in his story of Zhu Baoru and his wife, Hu Xueyan suddenly looked at Mr Wu.

“你晓不晓得，是哪个抓的朱宝如？”
“Do you know who had Zhu Baoru arrested?”

“不是团练局的巡防队吗？”
“Was it not the militia bureau's patrol?”

“不是。
“No.

是他自己。
He did it himself.

这是一条苦肉计，巡防队的人是串出来的。”
It was a scheme to win sympathy by suffering along with them; the patrolmen were his accomplices.

胡雪岩说，“朱宝如一抓进去，问起来在我善后局做事，巡防队是假模假样不相信。”
When he was taken in and said that he worked at my Reconstruction Bureau, they pretended not to believe him.

“朱宝如就写了张条子给我，我当然派人去保他。
He wrote me a note, and naturally I sent someone to stand surety for him.

等他一保出来，戏就有得他唱了。”
Once he was out, he could stage the rest of his performance.”

据胡雪岩说，他释放之前，向朱家驹、王培利，拍胸担保，全力营救。
According to Hu, before his release Zhu had solemnly promised Jiaju and Wang Peili that he would do everything possible to rescue them.

其时这两个人，已由防巡队私设的“公堂”问过两回，还用了刑，
By then they had been questioned twice in the patrol's unauthorized courtroom and tortured.

虽不是上“夹棍”或者“老虎凳”，但一顿“皮巴掌”打下来，满嘴喷血，牙齿打掉了好几颗，当然出言恫吓，不在话下——
They had not suffered the leg press or the tiger bench, but blows from a leather slapping paddle had left their mouths streaming blood and knocked out several teeth, to say nothing of the threats.

朝廷自平洪杨后，虽有“胁从不问”的恩诏，
After suppressing the Taiping rebellion, the court had issued a merciful decree exempting those coerced into joining it.

但太平军的零散败兵，除非投诚有案，倘为私下潜行各处，地方团练，抓到了仍送官处治。
But unless scattered former soldiers had formally surrendered and been registered, local militias could still arrest them as they slipped secretly from place to place and hand them over for punishment.

因此，朱家驹、王培利惊恐万状，一线生机，都寄托在朱宝如身上，
Terrified, the two men placed their only hope of survival in Zhu Baoru.

朝夕盼望，
They waited day and night.

盼到第三夭盼到了。
On the third day he finally came.


朱宝如告诉他们，全力奔走的结果，可以办个递解回籍的处分，不过要花钱。
Zhu told them that, after all his efforts, he could arrange for them to be sent under escort to their native place, but it would cost money.

朱家驹、王培利原有款子在阜康钱庄，存折还在。
They still had their own funds at Fukang and their passbooks.

朱宝如说，这笔存款不必动，他们回到上海仍可支取。
Those deposits need not be touched; they could withdraw them when they reached Shanghai.

至于刘家的房子，出了这件事以后，眼前已经没有用处，
The Liu house, however, was of no immediate use after what had happened.

不如牺牲定洋，设法退掉，存在阜康的三千银子提出来，
Better to forfeit the deposit, cancel the purchase, and withdraw the three thousand taels held at Fukang for it.

在团练局及钱塘、仁和两县，上下打点，大概也差不多了。
That should be about enough to pay everyone who mattered in the militia bureau and the Qiantang and Renhe county offices.

好在宝藏埋在刘家，地图在他们身边，
Fortunately, the treasure remained buried at the Liu house and they still had the map.

等这场风波过去，再回杭州，仍旧可以发财。
Once the trouble had passed, they could return to Hangzhou and make their fortune.

到此境界，朱家驹、王培利只求脱却螺绁，唯言是从。
Desperate only to escape their bonds, they agreed to everything.

但朱宝如做事，显得十分稳重，
Yet Zhu proceeded with great apparent caution.

带着老婆天天来探监送牢饭，谈到释放一节，总说对方狮子大开口，要慢慢儿磨，
He and his wife visited daily with food, saying that those who could release them were making outrageous demands which must be bargained down.

劝他们耐心等待。
They must wait patiently.

这样，过了有十天工夫，才来问他们两人，说谈妥当了，一切使费在内，两千八百两银子，剩下二百两还可以让他们做路费，
About ten days passed before he announced an agreement: all expenses included, twenty-eight hundred taels, leaving two hundred for their journey.

问他们愿意不愿意。
Would they agree?


“你们想，”胡雪岩说：“岂有不愿之理。
“How could they refuse?” Hu said.

存折的图章在王培利身边，交给朱宝如以后，第二天就‘开笼子’放人了。
“Wang Peili handed over the seal for the bank account, and the next day the cage was opened.

不过，两个人还要具一张甘结，回籍以后，安分守已，做个良民，
But both had to sign an undertaking: after returning home, they would live quietly as law-abiding subjects.

如果再潜行各地，经人告发，甘愿凭官法办。”
If they again traveled about in secret and were reported, they agreed to submit to official punishment.”

“好厉害！”乌先生说，
“Ruthless!” Mr Wu said.

“这是绝了他们两个人的后路，
“That cut off their retreat.

永远不敢再到杭州。”
They would never dare return to Hangzhou.

“手段是很厉害，不过良心还不算太黑。”
Still, his conscience was not entirely black.

乌先生又说：“那两个人，叫天天不应，叫地地不灵，
Those two had nobody to appeal to.

如果要他们把存折拿出来，五千银子全数吞没，亦未尝不可。”
He could have demanded their passbooks and swallowed the entire five thousand taels as well.”

“不然！
“No.

朱宝如非要把那张合约收回不可，否则会吃官司。
He had to recover the purchase contract or risk a lawsuit.

为啥呢？因为从头到底都是骗局，
The whole thing was fraudulent.

那家的房主，根本不姓刘，孙四也不是‘瓦摇头’，
The owner was not named Liu, and Sun Four was no property broker.

完全是朱宝如串出来的。
Zhu had arranged it all.

如果这张合约捏在他们两个人手里，可以转给人家，
With that contract, the two men could transfer their rights to someone else.

到了期限，依约付款营业，西洋镜拆穿，
When the time came to pay and take possession, the fraud would be exposed.

朱宝如不但要吃官司，也不能做人了。”
Zhu would face a lawsuit and lose all standing.”

“啊，啊！”
“Ah!”

乌先生深深点头，
Mr Wu nodded deeply.

“这个人很高明。
“Very clever.

不吞他们的五千银子，放一条路让人家走，才不会出事。”
Leaving them their five thousand and a way to live kept him out of trouble.”

“不但不会出事，
“More than that.

那两个人还一直蒙在鼓里，梦想发财。”
They remained completely in the dark, still dreaming of a fortune.”


“对了！”乌先生问：“严进士家的房子呢？”
“And the jinshi's house?” Mr Wu asked.

“我先讲他骗了多少？”
“Let me first tell you how much Zhu got.”

胡雪岩扳着手指计算：

“房价一共三千四百两，
“The price was thirty-four hundred taels.

付定洋四百两是孙四的好处，
Sun Four took the four-hundred-tael deposit as his reward.

整数三千两听说巡防队分了一千，朱宝如实得二千两，
Of the remaining three thousand, I heard the patrol took one thousand and Zhu kept two thousand.

典严家的房子够了。”
That was enough to take Mr Yan's house in pledge.”

“典了房子开粥厂？”
“And open a soup kitchen there?”

“是啊！
“Yes.

朱宝如来同我说，他看中严家房子的风水，想买下来，不过现在力量不足，
He came to tell me he liked the house's feng shui and wanted to buy it, but could not yet afford to.

只好先典下来，租给善后局办粥厂。
He would take it in pledge first, then rent it to the Reconstruction Bureau as a soup kitchen.

他说：”做事情要讲公道，
‘We must be fair,’ he said.

粥厂从第一年十一月办到第二年二月，一共四个月，
‘The kitchen will operate from the eleventh month through the second month of the following year, four months altogether.

租金亦只收四个月，每个月一百两。
I shall charge rent only for those four months, a hundred taels a month.’

‘我去看了房子，告诉他说，’这样子的房子，租金没有这种行情，
I inspected the house and told him, ‘That is above the going rate.

五十两一个月都勉强。
Even fifty a month would be generous.

善后局的公款，我不能乱做人情。
I cannot use public funds to do you favors.

不过，我私人可以帮你的忙。
But I can help you personally.’

“承他的情，一定不肯用我的钱。
To his credit, he refused my money.

不过办粥厂当然也有好处。”
Of course, running the soup kitchen had advantages of its own.”

“那么，掘藏呢？
“And the treasure?

掘到了没有？”
Did he find it?”

“这就不晓得了。
“That I do not know.

这种事，只有他们夫妇亲自动手，不能让外人插手的。
Only he and his wife could have dug for it; they could not let outsiders take part.

不过，朱宝如后来发了财，是真的。“
But he certainly became rich afterward.”


“大先生！”乌先生提出一大疑问：“这些情形，你是怎么知道的呢？”
“Mr Hu, how did you learn all this?” Mr Wu asked.

“有些情形是孙四告诉我的。
“Some of it came from Sun Four.

他只晓得后半段，严家房子的事，他根本不清楚。”
He knew only the latter part, nothing at all about the Yan house.”

谈到这里，胡雪岩忽然提高了声音说：
Hu suddenly raised his voice.

“若要人不知，除非己莫为。
“If you do not want a thing known, do not do it!

过了有四、五年，有一回我在上海，到堂子里去吃花酒，遇见一个江西人，姓王，
Four or five years later I was at a courtesan banquet in Shanghai when I met a man from Jiangxi named Wang.

他说：胡大先生，我老早就晓得你的大名了，
‘I have known your great name for years,’ he said.

我还是你杭州阜康钱庄的客户。”
‘I was even a customer of your Fukang Bank in Hangzhou.’”

“不用说，这个人就是王培利了？”
“Wang Peili, of course?”

“不错。
“Exactly.

当时他跟我谈起朱宝如，又问起万安桥刘家的房子。
He mentioned Zhu Baoru and asked about the Liu house at Wan’an Bridge.

我同他说：朱宝如，我同他沾点亲，万安桥刘家，我就不清楚了。”
I told him Zhu was a distant relative, but I knew nothing of that house.

胡雪岩接着又说：“堂子里要谈正经事，都是约到小房间里，躺在烟铺上，清清静静私下谈，
In such places, serious matters are discussed privately in a small room, lying on the opium couch.

席面上豁拳闹酒，还要唱戏，哪里好谈正事？
With drinking games, noisy toasts, and singing at the banquet, how can anyone discuss business?

所以我说了一句：有空再变。
So I said, ‘We can talk another time.’

原是敷衍的话。
It was merely a polite way of putting him off.

哪晓得……
Who would have thought...”

““他真的来寻你了？”乌先生接口问说。
“He really came to see you?”

“不是来寻我，是请我在花旗总会吃大菜。
“He invited me to a Western meal at the American Club.

帖子上写得很恳切，说有要紧事情请教，又说并无别客。
His note was very earnest: an important matter on which he sought my advice, and no other guests.

你想想，我应酬再忙，也不能不去……”
Busy as I was with engagements, I could hardly refuse.”

胡雪岩说，他准时赴约，
Hu arrived punctually.

果然只有王培利一个人。开门见山他说他做过太平军，曾经与朱宝如一起被捕。
Wang was indeed alone and began directly by saying that he had served in the Taiping army and been arrested with Zhu Baoru.

这下胡雪岩才想起他保释过朱宝如的往事，顿时起了戒心。
Only then did Hu remember having secured Zhu's release, and immediately grew wary.

王培利似乎知道胡雪岩在浙江官场的势力，要求胡雪岩设法，能让他回杭州。
Wang seemed aware of his influence among Zhejiang officials and asked him to find a way for him to return to Hangzhou.

“你答应他没有呢？”乌先生插嘴发问。
“Did you agree?” Mr Wu interrupted.

“没有。
“No.

事情没有弄清楚，我不好做这种冒失的事。”
I could not act so rashly before understanding the matter.

胡雪岩说，“我同他说，你自己具了结的，
I told him, ‘You signed an undertaking yourself.

我帮不上忙，
I cannot help with that.

不过，你杭州有啥事情，我可以替你办。他叹口气说，这件事非要我自己去办不可。

接下来就把掘藏的事告诉我。
Then he told me about the treasure.

我一面听，一面在想，朱宝如一向花样很多，他老婆更是个厉害角色……”
As I listened, I thought of Zhu's many tricks and his still more formidable wife...”


说到这里，乌先生突然发觉螺蛳太太神色似乎不大对劲，便打断了胡雪岩的话问，
Mr Wu suddenly noticed that Madam Luosi looked unwell and interrupted.

“罗四姐，你怎么样，
“Sister Luo, what is wrong?

人不舒服？”
Are you ill?”

“不是，不是！”
“No, no!”

螺蛳太太摇着手说：
She waved her hands.

“你们谈你们的。”
“Go on talking.”

她看着胡雪岩问：“后来呢？”

“后来，他同我说，如果我能想法子让他回杭州掘了藏，愿意同我平分。
“He offered me half if I could get him back to Hangzhou to dig up the treasure.

这时候我已经想到，朱宝如怎么样发的财，恐怕其中大有文章。
By then I suspected there was a good deal behind Zhu's fortune.

王培利一到杭州，说不定是要去寻朱宝如算帐，
If Wang returned, he might seek a reckoning with Zhu.

““出人命？”
“Dead?”

乌先生想了一下说：
Mr Wu considered.

“你是说，王培利吃了哑巴亏，会跟朱宝如动刀子？”
“You mean that, unable to obtain redress, Wang might take a knife to him?”

“这是可以想得到的事。
“It was easy to imagine.

或者朱宝如先下手为强，先告王培利也说不定。

“Did Wang give up?”

“大概死心了。
“Probably.

胡雪岩说：“你们几时见过生意做得象个样子的人，会去买白鸽票？”

“这倒是很实惠的话。”

“No.”

“I never reopen people's old sores.”

“You do not reopen theirs, but they will reopen yours.”

Madam Luosi, who had listened silently all this time, spoke at last.

“ 如果你同朱宝如谈过就好了。”
“It would have been better if you had mentioned it to Zhu.”

朱宝如的事跟我毫不相干，
Zhu's affairs had nothing to do with me.

怎么跟你谈？

而况，也没有工夫。

“着！”
“Exactly!”

乌先生击案称赏：
Mr Wu struck the table approvingly.

“这句话，我要听。
“That is what I like to hear.

我现在要劝胡大先生的，就是雄心壮志，不可消沉。

你的精力还蛮旺的，
You still have plenty of energy.

东山再起，为时未晚。”
It is not too late to make a fresh start.”

胡雪岩笑笑不作声。
Hu smiled without answering.

就这时听得寺院中晨钟已动，
Just then the temple's morning bell sounded.

看自鸣钟上，短针指着四时，已是寅正时分了。
The clock's short hand pointed to four: the midpoint of the yin watch.

“再不睡要天亮了！”胡雪岩说，
“It will be daylight if we do not go to bed,” Hu said.

“明天再谈吧。”
“We can talk tomorrow.”

收获极丰，现款、金条、珠宝等等，估计不下二三十万银子之多。
The yield was considerable: cash, gold bars, jewels, and other valuables estimated at no less than two or three hundred thousand taels.

她问胡雪岩，这笔款子，作何处置？
What did Hu want done with it?

“我没有意见。”
“I have no opinion.

胡雪岩说：“现在已经轮不到我作主了。”
It is no longer for me to decide.”

这句话听起来象牢骚，不过螺蛳太太明了他的本意，
It sounded like a complaint, but she understood his real meaning.

“你也不要这样说，
“Do not say that.

现在你还可以作主。”
You can still decide now.

她说：“过两三天，就难说了。”
In two or three days, it may be another matter.”

“你说我现在还可以作主，那么，请你替我作个主看。”
“If I still can, make the decision for me.”

“要我作主，我现在就要动手。”
“Then I would act immediately.”

“怎么动法？”
“How?”

“趁天不亮，请乌先生把这些东西带出去。”
“Have Mr Wu take everything away before daylight.”

螺蛳太太指着一口大箱子说：
She pointed to a large chest.

“喏，东西都装在里面。”
“It is all in there.”

“喔！”
“Oh!”

胡雪岩有些茫然，定定神说：
Hu looked bewildered, then collected himself.

“你刚才怎么不提起？”
“Why did you not mention it just now?”

“现在也还不迟。”
“It is not too late.”

胡雪岩重新考虑下来，认为不妥，
Considering the matter again, Hu felt it would be wrong.

此举有欠光明磊落，于心不安，
Such concealment lacked the openness and integrity he valued; his conscience would not allow it.

因而很歉疚地表示不能同意。
Apologetically, he refused.
