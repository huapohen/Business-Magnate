后记｜AFTERWORD


写完《灯火楼台》最后一章，真有如释重负之感。
Having finished the last chapter of Towers of Light, I truly feel as though a great weight has been lifted from me.

《胡雪岩》、《红顶商人》等作自连载未几，即谬承读者奖饰有加；
Almost from the beginning of their serialization, Hu Xueyan and The Red-Topped Merchant received more generous praise from readers than they deserved.

单行本出版后，行销遍及世界各地的华人社会；
Once published in book form, they circulated among Chinese communities throughout the world.

甚至还有许多外国读者，他们不识中文，特为请他们的中国朋友讲解。
There were even many foreign readers who, unable to read Chinese, specially asked Chinese friends to explain the books to them.

但说来让我有些啼笑皆非，这些外国读者是想从拙作中，学得胡雪岩的经商技巧；
Yet something about this leaves me not knowing whether to laugh or cry: these readers hoped to learn Hu Xueyan's business techniques from my modest work.

实力始料所不及。
That was something I had never anticipated.


《胡雪岩》三部曲的写作过程，跟小女的年龄相仿佛。
The writing of the Hu Xueyan trilogy has taken about as long as my daughter has been alive.

在这十余年之间，台湾经济发展的情势，使得我在写作中途，不断产生新的感慨。
Over these ten years and more, the course of Taiwan's economic development has continually prompted new reflections as I wrote.

其中最深刻的是：第一，胡雪岩失败的主要原因是，当英国瓦特发明蒸汽机，导致工业革命后，手工业之将没落是时间的问题。
Of the reflections that have struck me most deeply, the first is this: the principal cause of Hu's failure was that, once Britain's Watt invented the steam engine and set the Industrial Revolution in motion, the decline of handicraft production became only a matter of time.

胡雪岩非见不及此，
Hu was not blind to this.

但为了维持广大江南农村养蚕人家的生计，不愿改弦易辙；
But to preserve the livelihoods of the many silkworm-raising households in the Jiangnan countryside, he refused to change course.

亦不甘屈服于西洋资本主义国家雄厚的经济力量之下，
Nor was he willing to submit to the formidable economic power of the Western capitalist countries.

因而在反垄断的狐军奋斗之下，导致了周转不灵的困境。
His solitary struggle against their monopoly consequently led him into a crisis of cash flow.

胡雪岩是不折不扣的民族资本家；
Hu was a national capitalist in the fullest sense.

如果在现代一定会获得政府的支持，但当时的当政者并无此种意识。
In modern times, he would certainly have received government support; the authorities of his own day had no such understanding.

所以他的失败，可说是时代的悲剧。
His failure may therefore be called a tragedy of his age.


第二，胡雪岩失败后，态度光明磊落，不愧为我乡的“杭铁头”。
Second, after his failure, Hu remained open and honourable, worthy of the name “Hangzhou Ironhead” from my native region.

看到近年来不断发生的经济犯罪事件，我不知道会不会有人从胡雪岩身上记取若干警惕与感化？
Looking at the economic crimes that have occurred so frequently in recent years, I wonder whether anyone might find in Hu Xueyan something to warn them and move them towards a better course.


第三个浓重的感慨，胡雪岩是李鸿章与左宗棠争夺政治权力、争议发展路线下的牺牲者。
My third deeply felt reflection is that Hu was a victim of the struggle between Li Hongzhang and Zuo Zongtang for political power and over the course of development.

从事投资，要看投资环境，而其间最重要的一个因素是：政治稳定。
Anyone making an investment must consider the investment environment, and one of its most important factors is political stability.

这应该是拙作的另一个始料所不及的贡献。
This may be another contribution of my work that I had not foreseen.
